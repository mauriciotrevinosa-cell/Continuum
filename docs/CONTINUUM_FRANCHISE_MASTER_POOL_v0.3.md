- **Kaguya-sama: Love Is War:** keep. Useful social/comedy premise, including the possibility of characters accustomed to elite status entering a world where they are no longer automatically the most important or capable people in the room.
- **My Ridiculous Reincarnation:** `BIG MAYBE`; difficult to balance but not removed yet.
- Several power-fantasy entries may be recontextualized/nerfed rather than discarded if their source trope of being overwhelmingly strongest would damage Continuum's balance.
