## Core rule

For the current manga/manhwa acquisition pass, **official narrative comic material is acquired even when it is not required to understand the main story and even when its canon authority is lower or ambiguous**.

Inclusion in the personal Library and canon authority are separate decisions:

```text
OFFICIAL != MAIN CANON
IN LIBRARY != CANON EQUIVALENCE
ACQUIRE FIRST -> CLASSIFY AUTHORITY LATER
```

Acquire, when officially published and reasonably obtainable:

1. main manga/manhwa;
2. official prequels and sequels;
3. official parallel/alternate adaptations;
4. official spin-offs and side stories;
5. official parody/gag/IF/crossover comics;
6. official comic anthologies and author-created supplemental comics, while tagging them separately from main continuity.

Do **not** mix fanmade/doujin works into this official-source pass. Artbooks, fanbooks, guidebooks, coloring/activity books, model sheets and other non-narrative reference products remain separate acquisition categories. Novels/light novels are also a separate medium pass unless explicitly added later.

## Authority classification

Continuum should eventually classify acquired works explicitly instead of using acquisition status as a canon signal. Suggested labels include:
