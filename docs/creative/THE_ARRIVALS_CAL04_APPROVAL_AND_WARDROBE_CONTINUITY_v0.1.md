## Wardrobe continuity lock

Rimuru may help the household create or adapt new clothing after Rimuru arrives, but this does not create an unlimited wardrobe.

- New garments must enter continuity gradually and have a traceable source: arrival clothing, recovered clothing, repaired clothing, deliberate reuse, trade, salvage, or an explicitly shown Rimuru-assisted creation/adaptation beat.
- A character must not acquire many unrelated outfits between scenes without a documented acquisition or creation event.
- CAL-04 validates the clothing visible in this calibration image for this test only; it does not silently establish a large off-screen wardrobe.
- Future pages must resolve owner, wearer, first appearance, acquisition/creation source, repair state, and reuse before introducing a new recurring garment.

The approved CAL-04 asset must be preserved and must not be overwritten by later candidates.
