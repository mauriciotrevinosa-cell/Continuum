# The Arrivals — Creative Direction v0.2

**Status:** high-confidence creative direction; concept-level canon where explicitly marked  
**Project/app:** `Continuum`  
**Working title of the primary series:** `The Arrivals`  
**Supersedes:** `THE_ARRIVALS_CREATIVE_DIRECTION_v0.1.md` for current creative planning

This document consolidates the creative direction established so far for The Arrivals. It intentionally distinguishes locked concept-level canon from high-confidence direction that still needs polishing. It is not permission to implement future app phases ahead of the approved engineering order.

---

## 1. Naming hierarchy

- **Continuum** is the app/platform/project umbrella.
- **The Arrivals** is the working title for the primary series/story created inside Continuum.
- Continuum may later host side stories, alternate branches, What If projects, or entirely different stories.

---

## 2. Story philosophy

The core creative philosophy remains:

> **Continuum no importa franquicias. Importa personas.**

Characters are not treated as collectible cameos from separate properties. They become people living in one evolving world.

The story should value:

- relationships and ordinary life as much as combat;
- consequences that remain meaningful;
- slow civilization growth;
- culture, work, family, hobbies, food, medicine, science, art and music;
- changes caused by arrivals rather than forcing every arrival to fit a static world;
- long-form character growth rather than immediate power normalization.

Not every episode must advance the main plot directly. An episode can matter because it changes a relationship, establishes a routine, develops infrastructure, advances a craft, reveals a social problem, or gives characters time to process what happened.

---

## 3. Opening Cohort is user-sized

The earlier `10 characters + Avatar` idea was only an example.

```text
OPENING_COHORT_SIZE = USER_DECIDED
```

The opening group may be smaller or larger. The design goal is a group small enough that the audience can learn each person and the early relationships can breathe.

The user chooses the final Opening Cohort visually before the first season is locked. Continuum can recommend balance and narrative load, but the user makes the decision.

---

## 4. Arrivals are a long-running arc

Characters do not all appear at once.

- Some arrive together.
- Some arrive alone.
- Some can remain undiscovered in another region for weeks or months.
- Arrival order does not have to match source-canon chronology.
- New franchises can be added in later seasons without restarting the world.
- A new source volume/season can create a delta review rather than reopening every prior decision.

The world continues developing while new people keep arriving.

A later franchise is allowed to materially change the world. If Season 2 introduces a new monster ecology, energy system, species, technology or social force that does not fit existing categories, Continuum should not force it into an old box. The world adapts: defenses, politics, ecology, economies, institutions and alliances can change in response.

### Pillar rule

```text
PILLARS ARE DESCRIPTIVE, NOT RESTRICTIVE
```

Pillars describe what currently shapes the world. They are not immutable limits. New arrivals may strengthen a pillar, weaken one, split one into several, or create an entirely new pillar.

---

## 5. Opening/world progression

The broad civilization progression remains:

```text
camp
→ settlement
→ town
→ port
→ city
→ autonomous territory / nation
```

The world exists before the Arrivals. It is not an empty stage waiting for protagonists. It has geography, people, work, inequality, institutions, conflicts and local history.

A high-confidence opening structure is:

```text
existing world
→ first isolated arrivals
→ early normalization / curiosity
→ ordinary coexistence begins
→ serious incident proves arrivals can include real threats
→ society divides over Otherworlders
→ fear and conflict create additional consequences
→ settlement/city keeps evolving anyway
```

The pace should remain slow enough that ordinary life is visible. The Arrivals can spend full episodes discussing next steps, building, eating, working, training, exploring, dating, recovering, opening businesses or simply talking after a major event.

This is not filler when it changes people or the world.

---

## 6. Otherworlders and social conflict

**High-confidence direction; exact politics and terminology still need polishing.**

`Otherworlder` can become a social label used by locals for people who arrived from other realities/worlds.

The crucial point is that the public may treat Otherworlders as one homogeneous group even when they come from unrelated worlds, moral systems and factions.

- Some Otherworlders are kind.
- Some are indifferent.
- Some are criminals.
- Some are monsters or villains.
- Some actively fight dangerous Otherworlders.
- Locals may still blame the whole category for the acts of one faction.

This allows the story to explore prejudice, xenophobia and racism-like social dynamics without pretending all Otherworlders are morally equivalent.

A possible reinforcing loop is:

```text
Otherworlders arrive
→ some arrivals cause genuine disasters
→ fear and resentment rise
→ people generalize across unrelated Otherworlders
→ fear can empower or generate phenomena such as curses/devils
→ more incidents occur
→ the public reads those incidents as proof that the prejudice was correct
```

This creates a conflict where some fears have a factual trigger but the resulting collective judgment is still unjust.

Attack on Titan can contribute themes of fear, collective blame and social pressure; Jujutsu Kaisen and Chainsaw Man can contribute supernatural mechanisms in which negative emotion/fear has real consequences. These sources inspire Continuum's own world rather than being copied wholesale.

## 7. Current worldbuilding pillars

The current pillars are a working map, not a closed taxonomy.

### People / ordinary life
Makes the setting feel inhabited rather than existing only for battles.

Examples: Bocchi the Rock!, Komi Can't Communicate, Horimiya, My Dress-Up Darling, Dealing with Mikadono Sisters Is a Breeze, Alya Sometimes Hides Her Feelings in Russian, Food for the Soul, Smoking Behind the Supermarket with You, Haimiya-senpai Is Scary and Cute.

### Family / relationships / belonging
The emotional center of the series.

Examples: SPY×FAMILY, Miss Kobayashi's Dragon Maid, The 100 Girlfriends, Komi, The Angel Next Door.

### Magic / knowledge / craft
How the world learns and develops new systems.

Examples: Frieren, Witch Hat Atelier, Tensura, The Apothecary Diaries, Dr. STONE.

### Supernatural / fear
Curses, devils, spirits, vampires and anomalous phenomena.

Examples: Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, Dandadan, Call of the Night, Tokyo Ghoul.

### Combat / security / underworlds
Threat response, crime, dangerous professions and physical conflict.

Examples: Solo Leveling, Gachiakuta, Sakamoto Days, Kagurabachi, MarriageToxin, Attack on Titan.

### Civilization
Technology, healthcare, economy, infrastructure, government, trade and institutions.

Examples: Tensura, Dr. STONE, Apothecary Diaries, Witch Hat Atelier, Frieren.

### Otherworlder / society
Integration, rejection, law, politics, identity and public reaction.

This pillar eventually touches almost every franchise.

### Comedy / chaos
Relief, absurdity and tonal contrast.

Examples: Bocchi, My Deer Friend Nokotan, KonoSuba, Dragon Maid, The 100 Girlfriends, BOFURI.

### Identity / adaptation
Who someone becomes when their original world no longer defines their social position.

Examples: Tokyo Ghoul, Attack on Titan, Kaguya-sama, The Quintessential Quintuplets, Dr. STONE, and eventually much of the cast.

## 8. Avatar / MC — concept-level canon

**LOCKED AT CONCEPT LEVEL unless deliberately revised later.**

The main character is the **Avatar / MC**.

- The MC will be visually based on the user's real appearance.
- Exact visual design is not locked until the user provides/approves suitable visual reference material.
- The MC arrives with amnesia and no usable memory of their origin.
- The MC may be a new/independent existence derived from a divine being rather than secretly being that original being.
- Identity is earned through lived choices, not inherited from the source entity.
- The MC may develop an internal analysis companion inspired by the relationship type we like in Raphael/Ciel, but the companion must be original and develop its own identity.
- The companion must not conveniently know the MC's full origin.
- Self-analysis may return unknown/restricted results, preserving the mystery.
- Potential can be effectively unbounded without current power being infinite.

The conceptual ability loop is canon direction:

```text
OBSERVE
→ UNDERSTAND
→ EVOLVE / CONSTRUCT
```

Understanding a power is not the same as executing it. Copying a mechanism does not copy years of experience.

## 9. Separate energy systems and construction costs

The MC can begin at zero in systems they do not naturally possess.

Examples:

```text
Cursed Energy = 0
Mana          = 0
```

Different systems remain independent unless a later, earned conversion/unification mechanism is built.

Growth should track at least:

```text
Capacity
Control / Efficiency
Tolerance
Recovery
```

Construction and operation are different costs.

A major body/system upgrade can consume maximum capacity as a structural investment. Example only:

```text
CE max before ocular construction = 100
structural investment             = 35
CE max immediately after          = 65
```

The lost maximum does not simply return after sleeping; the reservoir must be trained upward again. Separately, using an extreme technique may drain current energy or put a subsystem into temporary burnout even when some energy remains.

---

## 10. Limitless / Six Eyes-style perceptual-system example

This example is intentionally preserved because it captures the desired MC progression in detail.

Observing Gojo's Limitless should not produce instant access.

```text
observe Limitless
→ understand part of the mechanism
→ execution fails
→ identify missing perceptual/computational infrastructure
→ train/build an original equivalent architecture
→ pay construction cost
→ learn to sustain it
→ eventually evolve beyond the initial inspiration
```

The MC's eventual ocular/perceptual system should become their own design rather than a literal duplicate of Six Eyes.

The important rule is that the MC can learn from extraordinary systems without turning observation into effortless copying.

---

## 11. Normal characters: everyone can grow, not everyone needs powers

Progression is multidimensional.

The Arrivals should not use a single hierarchy where destructive combat power equals character level.

Possible mastery dimensions include:

```text
Combat
Magic
Physical Ability
Art
Craft
Science
Medicine
Engineering
Music
Social Skill
Leadership
Teaching
Logistics
Cultural Impact
Experience
```

Three paths can coexist:

```text
A) human/professional growth
B) extraordinary mastery of an existing discipline
C) optional later learning of a supernatural system
```

Path C is never automatic. It requires the rules of that system to permit it, plus compatibility, training, time, costs and narrative justification.

Some originally normal characters may eventually become extremely powerful. Others may never want or need to. Neither path is failure.

A person can be crucial to the future nation with almost no combat ability.

Example direction: Bocchi can remain fundamentally a musician while her guitar skill grows to extraordinary levels and may eventually create supportive/resonance-like effects. She does not need to become a generic mage for her progression to matter.

## 12. Source history vs Continuum changes

Continuum must distinguish what happened in the source from what Continuum chooses to change.

A character can enter from a chosen source snapshot, preserve memories from one stage, inhabit a younger/older body, retain or lose particular physical states, or receive a Continuum-specific override.

These decisions happen **after source ingestion and character review**, not before.

Source Canon remains recorded as-is. Continuum does not silently rewrite it.

### Consequence integrity

High-confidence story rule:

> **Changing the future should not casually erase the emotional meaning of what characters already lived.**

Time travel is not absolutely forbidden, but a late reset should not be used as a cheap undo button. A story fundamentally built around loops and causality may justify time travel because the loop is part of the premise; otherwise consequences should remain meaningful.

This principle is especially relevant when adapting sources whose later canon introduces reset/time-travel mechanics that the user does not want imported into Continuum.

### Example overrides under consideration

- **Attack on Titan:** the Curse of Ymir / 13-year lifespan rule should not bind Continuum characters merely because it existed in Source Canon.
- **The 100 Girlfriends:** remove the forced-destiny relationship mechanic as an unquestioned obligation. The protagonist can learn what happened, become angry at the lack of agency, explain it, and the girls can choose for themselves. Some may remain romantic partners; others may choose different lives.
- **BOFURI:** bring the people while preserving meaningful versions of their game identities/avatars; later define how that layer interacts with physical Continuum reality.
- **Dr. STONE:** Senku can arrive aware that his cognition is below his former level. He may begin above average rather than immediately at full source capability and progressively recover/expand his intelligence through experimentation and adaptation to Continuum. This prevents instant civilization-solving while making the nerf itself part of his character experience.
- **Power-fantasy characters:** source claims that someone is 'the strongest' do not automatically make them globally dominant in Continuum. Their abilities can be recontextualized, compatibility-limited, locally powerful, partially nerfed, or moved into a more niche/civilian role if that produces a better world balance.

These are high-confidence directions, not permission to change characters arbitrarily. Every override should have an explicit reason and provenance.

## 13. Frieren / Fern emotional core

High-confidence developing premise:

- Frieren and Fern do not arrive together.
- Circumstances eventually lead Frieren to genuinely believe Fern died.
- This reopens the wound left by Himmel, but in a different form: Frieren had already learned to value Fern while Fern was alive.
- Frieren comes to understand that losing Fern feels less like losing a traveling companion and more like losing family / a daughter.
- While alone, Frieren remains recognizably Frieren but behaves subtly outside her usual emotional pattern.
- Visual storytelling should show Fern's absence through routines, empty space, objects, habits and pauses rather than explaining everything with dialogue.
- When Fern is found alive, Frieren's reaction can be unusually physical and emotional for her: freezing, verifying that Fern is real, embracing her, not immediately letting go.
- The reunion does not instantly reset Frieren. Temporary overprotection and emotional aftereffects can continue afterward.

Exact timing, dialogue and episode placement remain open.

## 14. Character review philosophy

The franchise pool is not a closed census of every character who can ever exist in Continuum.

A narratively identifiable character who was missed should default conceptually to:

```text
UNREVIEWED_RESERVE
```

not `EXCLUDED`.

Future character review should keep these concepts separate:

```text
REVIEW_STATE
SOURCE_PROMINENCE
CONTINUUM_ROLE
ARRIVAL_PRIORITY
```

A minor source character may become important in Continuum; a source protagonist may remain low-focus for long stretches.

Before a franchise receives a real cast lock, Continuum should eventually:

1. ingest the user's available manga/anime/LN/source material;
2. identify narratively distinguishable characters;
3. reconcile aliases/duplicates;
4. ignore anonymous background population;
5. present pending characters visually;
6. show image, name, brief description, source prominence and evidence/source coverage;
7. let the user decide `SÍ / AIRE / NO`;
8. preserve unresolved cases as `UNREVIEWED_RESERVE`.

A closed review can later reopen only for newly detected characters.

## 15. Relationships and romance

Major relationships never auto-canon.

The MC's final romance remains deliberately undecided.

A light or recurring accidental-harem joke is allowed if it emerges naturally, but:

- nobody automatically falls in love with the MC;
- no franchise's original romantic mechanic transfers by default;
- the MC does not receive a passive seduction power;
- multiple crushes do not force a particular end-state;
- strong friendship can remain friendship.

The fun version is social misunderstanding, gradual chemistry, the MC missing obvious signals, or the internal companion trying to model romance too clinically.

---

## 16. Family and slice-of-life characters are not background filler

Franchises without combat are not lesser contributors.

SPY×FAMILY is a useful model: its primary emotional focus can remain the Forger family while individual arcs still allow Yor, Loid, Anya, Bond or others to become involved in danger, politics, espionage or rescue situations. A character's ordinary-life role does not ban them from action.

Similarly, slice-of-life characters can become essential to culture, education, food, art, music, commerce, relationships and social stability.

My Deer Friend Nokotan can function as a controlled absurdist/toon-force wildcard, providing comedy even between serious arcs without requiring the entire world to obey her gag logic at all times.

---

## 17. City and civilization direction

The future city should not become a theme park with one district per franchise.

Its identity emerges from:

- local geography and materials;
- shared survival needs;
- technologies actually developed;
- magic/supernatural systems that can be safely integrated;
- migration;
- trades;
- institutions;
- decades of lived history.

Useful future concepts include `Arrival House`, preservation of the original camp as historic `Old Camp`, civic institutions, healthcare, markets, workshops, port logistics, education and culture.

Civilization growth should be observable over time rather than skipped in montage. Characters such as Senku can materially accelerate development while still facing local constraints, unknown physics/magic, resource limitations and social consequences.

## 18. Visual direction — multistyle

Characters may retain meaningful elements of their original visual grammar while still occupying one physically coherent world.

Do not homogenize every character into one generic anime house style, but also do not make them look like unrelated flat assets pasted on top of a background.

Character/franchise-specific traits that may survive include:

- line weight;
- facial simplification;
- expression timing;
- smear/deformation language;
- texture;
- shading treatment;
- impact FX;
- manga hatching;
- comic onomatopoeia where appropriate;
- apparent animation cadence;
- POV-specific composition.

Shared scene layers should unify:

- perspective;
- contact with the ground;
- key light direction;
- cast shadows;
- occlusion/depth;
- weather;
- environmental particles;
- physical interaction;
- camera and spatial sound.

A useful experiment remains Bocchi + Frieren + Okarun in one scene:

- Bocchi retains softer/flat readability and extreme comedic deformation when her POV dominates;
- Frieren retains calm, clean fantasy composition and restrained motion;
- Okarun can bring aggressive manga hatching, speed and contrast during danger/action;
- the same sunset, floor plane, environment and physical lighting bind all three together.

The reference is the philosophy of mixed visual languages, not imitation of any one existing film's pipeline.

## 19. Source/Vault creative policy

The immediate acquisition goal is intentionally simple. The user should not be expected to hunt every possible artbook, promotional image or production artifact before Continuum becomes useful.

Priority is:

```text
1. primary source material (manga / light novel / novel as applicable)
2. anime/adaptation where it exists
3. fan art / derivative material the user personally wants to preserve
4. additional official extras only when they are easy to obtain or clearly useful
```

The physical Vault remains outside the code repository, intended at:

```text
C:\ContinuumVault\
```

Raw user source files are immutable/read-only once registered. Continuum should generate derivatives elsewhere rather than rewriting source files.

Folder structure should stay simple enough that the user can download a file, choose Save As, select the franchise folder and drop it into the appropriate medium folder without manual renaming/cropping/conversion work.

Language should not block acquisition:

- Japanese audio + subtitles is valid.
- English dub is equally valid when preferred; performance preference matters (for example, a user may prefer a particular English dub voice).
- Dual-audio is desirable when convenient but not mandatory.
- English subtitles/text are useful for later source intelligence but do not need to be hunted separately if already embedded or if obtaining them becomes burdensome.
- English manga/LN translations are valid working sources; Japanese originals can be added later.

Continuum should eventually recognize multiple language versions of the same work as versions of one source, not unrelated works.

Fan material must remain clearly separated from source-canon authority. It can inspire or even be deliberately adopted into Continuum Canon later, but never silently becomes Source Canon.

## 20. Creative curation workflow

The current workflow is:

```text
franchise pool
→ user taste/interest classification
→ source-material acquisition
→ Library registration
→ source/authority review
→ cast review
→ per-character arrival profile
→ Opening Cohort / Season planning
```

Per-character arrival profiles are intentionally deferred until enough source material is available. Later decisions may include:

- source snapshot/cutoff;
- retained memories;
- younger/older body;
- physical state;
- skills/powers;
- Continuum-specific overrides.

This ordering prevents premature decisions made without seeing the character's full trajectory.

This document is creative/product context.
