# The Arrivals — Creative Direction v0.3

**Status:** high-confidence creative direction; concept-level canon where explicitly marked  
**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Project/app:** `Continuum`  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Supersedes:** `THE_ARRIVALS_CREATIVE_DIRECTION_v0.2.md`

This document preserves the current consolidated creative direction for The Arrivals while keeping it explicitly separate from the generic Continuum product.

---

## 1. Product/project separation

```text
CONTINUUM = APP / CREATIVE STUDIO / ENGINE
THE ARRIVALS = ONE PROJECT INSIDE CONTINUUM
```

The Arrivals is not abandoned or downgraded. It remains the flagship personal project. The separation simply means Continuum can also host unrelated continuations, rewrites, What Ifs, crossovers and original stories.

The Arrivals may select from the user's Library, but Library membership never means automatic inclusion in The Arrivals.

---

## 2. Story philosophy

Core principle:

> **Continuum no importa franquicias. Importa personas.**

Characters are not collectible cameos. They become people living in one evolving world.

The story values:

- relationships and ordinary life as much as combat;
- meaningful consequences;
- slow civilization growth;
- family, work, hobbies, food, medicine, science, art, music and culture;
- long-form adaptation to a shared world;
- change caused by arrivals rather than forcing every arrival into a frozen setting.

Not every episode must push the main plot. An episode can matter because it changes a relationship, routine, institution, technology, craft, social problem or emotional state.

---

## 3. Opening Cohort and long-running arrivals

The Opening Cohort has no fixed required size.

```text
OPENING_COHORT_SIZE = USER_DECIDED
```

Some characters arrive together, some alone, and some may remain undiscovered for weeks or months.

Arrival order does not need to match source chronology.

New franchises may enter in later seasons without restarting the world.

A new source should be allowed to **change** the world instead of being forced into old categories.

---

## 4. Dynamic worldbuilding pillars

```text
PILLARS ARE DESCRIPTIVE, NOT RESTRICTIVE
```

Current pillars are a map of what shapes the world at a given moment, not immutable laws.

A future franchise may introduce a new monster ecology, species, energy system, technology or social force. If it does not fit an existing pillar, Continuum should not force it into one.

The world reacts:

```text
new source enters project
→ new force exists
→ balance changes
→ defenses / politics / ecology / economy / institutions change
→ new opportunities and new problems emerge
```

Current high-confidence pillars include:

- People / ordinary life
- Family / relationships / belonging
- Magic / knowledge / craft
- Supernatural / fear
- Combat / security / underworlds
- Civilization
- Otherworlder / society
- Comedy / chaos
- Identity / adaptation

These can split, merge, weaken or gain new companions over time.

---

## 5. Civilization progression

Broad visual/world progression:

```text
camp
→ settlement
→ town
→ port
→ city
→ autonomous territory / nation
```

The world exists before the Arrivals. It has geography, local people, history, work, inequality, institutions and conflicts of its own.

Useful long-term concepts include Arrival House, Old Camp as a preserved historical site, healthcare, markets, workshops, education, ports, logistics, governance and cultural institutions.

---

## 6. Opening structure

High-confidence mini-layout:

```text
existing world
→ first isolated arrivals
→ early curiosity / normalization
→ ordinary coexistence begins
→ serious incident proves some arrivals are dangerous
→ society divides over Otherworlders
→ fear and conflict create additional consequences
→ civilization continues evolving anyway
```

The pace should be allowed to breathe. Full episodes can center on discussions, building, food, training, exploring, dating, family, recovery, business, education or simply processing previous events.

That is not filler when people or the world change.

---

## 7. Otherworlders and social conflict

`Otherworlder` can become a social category locals use for people from other realities/worlds.

The core conflict is that society may treat Otherworlders as one group even when they belong to unrelated universes, species, factions and moral systems.

Some are kind. Some are indifferent. Some are criminals. Some are villains. Some actively fight dangerous Otherworlders. The public may still generalize from one faction to everyone.

This supports racism-like / xenophobia-like prejudice without pretending every fear is invented.

A reinforcing loop can develop:

```text
Otherworlders arrive
→ some genuinely dangerous arrivals cause disasters
→ fear and resentment increase
→ unrelated Otherworlders are collectively blamed
→ fear / negative emotion feeds curses, Devils or analogous phenomena
→ more incidents occur
→ the public treats those incidents as proof of the original prejudice
```

Attack on Titan contributes themes of fear, collective blame and social pressure. Jujutsu Kaisen and Chainsaw Man contribute mechanisms where negative emotion or fear can have literal supernatural consequences. These inspirations are adapted into Continuum rather than copied wholesale.

## 8. MC / Avatar — concept-level canon

**LOCKED AT CONCEPT LEVEL unless deliberately revised later.**

The main character is the Avatar / MC.

- The MC will be visually based on Mauricio's real appearance.
- Exact visual design waits for user-provided and approved reference images.
- The MC arrives with amnesia and no usable memory of their origin.
- The MC may be a new/independent existence derived from a divine being rather than secretly being the original being.
- Identity is earned through lived choices.
- The MC may develop an original internal analysis companion inspired by the relationship type of Raphael/Ciel, but it must become its own character.
- The companion must not conveniently know the full origin.
- Self-analysis may return unknown/restricted results.
- Potential may be effectively unbounded without current power being infinite.

Core progression loop:

```text
OBSERVE
→ UNDERSTAND
→ EVOLVE / CONSTRUCT
```

Understanding is not execution. Copying a mechanism does not copy years of experience.

---

## 9. Separate energy systems and construction costs

The MC can begin with zero access to systems they do not naturally possess.

```text
Cursed Energy = 0
Mana = 0
```

Different systems stay distinct until a later earned conversion/unification mechanism exists.

Growth tracks at least:

```text
Capacity
Control / Efficiency
Tolerance
Recovery
```

Construction cost and operating cost are separate.

A major structural upgrade can permanently invest part of maximum capacity until that reservoir is rebuilt through growth.

Example only:

```text
CE max before construction = 100
structural investment      = 35
CE max after construction  = 65
```

Rest does not simply restore sacrificed maximum capacity.

---

## 10. Limitless / Six Eyes-style example

This example is intentionally preserved because it captures the desired MC progression.

```text
observe Gojo's Limitless
→ understand part of mechanism
→ execution fails
→ identify missing perceptual/computational infrastructure
→ train/build an original equivalent architecture
→ pay structural cost
→ learn to sustain it
→ eventually evolve beyond the inspiration
```

The eventual ocular/perceptual system should become the MC's own design, not a literal Six Eyes copy.

Observation is a starting point, not a cheat code.

---

## 11. Everyone can grow; not everyone needs combat powers

Progression is multidimensional.

Possible mastery dimensions include:

```text
Combat
Magic
Physical Ability
Art
Craft
Science
Medicine
Engineering
Music
Social Skill
Leadership
Teaching
Logistics
Cultural Impact
Experience
```

Three paths may coexist:

```text
A) human / professional growth
B) extraordinary mastery of an existing discipline
C) optional later learning of a supernatural system
```

Path C is never automatic. Compatibility, rules, training, time, costs and narrative justification matter.

A slice-of-life character can become crucial without becoming a fighter.

Bocchi is a model case: she can remain fundamentally a musician while her guitar mastery becomes extraordinary and may eventually create resonance/support-like effects. She does not need to become a generic mage.

---

## 12. Slice of life, family and ordinary roles matter

Non-combat franchises are not background filler.

SPY×FAMILY is a useful model: its emotional center can remain the Forger family while different arcs involve Yor, Loid, Anya, Bond or others in danger, politics, espionage, rescue or conflict.

The point is not to lock Yor into a combat function; her example simply demonstrates that a family-focused source may still enter action when the story naturally calls for it.

Slice-of-life characters can shape education, food, fashion, entertainment, neighborhoods, businesses, relationships and culture.

My Deer Friend Nokotan can function as a controlled absurd/comedic wildcard; toon-force-style humor may provide contrast between serious arcs without requiring the entire world to obey her comedic logic at all times.

---

## 13. Source history vs project continuity

Source Canon must remain recorded as-is.

The Arrivals may later choose, per character:

- source snapshot/cutoff;
- memory cutoff;
- physical/body age;
- health/injury state;
- retained skills/powers;
- Continuum-specific overrides.

A character may have a younger or older body while retaining memories from a later source stage.

These decisions happen after source ingestion and character review, not before.

Continuum must always be able to distinguish:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED IN SOURCE
vs
WHAT THE ARRIVALS CHANGED
```

---

## 14. Consequence integrity and time travel

High-confidence rule:

> **Changing the future should not casually erase the emotional meaning of what characters already lived.**

Time travel is not universally forbidden. A story fundamentally constructed around loops and causality can justify it. What should be avoided is a late reset used as an undo button that makes prior sacrifice or growth feel as if it never mattered.

This is particularly relevant to Dr. STONE: the user enjoyed much of the story but dislikes the emotional effect of the later time-machine direction, especially when it risks undermining experiences such as Suika's. Continuum can preserve Source Canon while choosing not to import that mechanic into The Arrivals.

## 15. Specific adaptation directions already liked

These are strong directions but can still be polished before final character locks.

### Attack on Titan

- primarily valuable for characters and themes;
- the Curse of Ymir / 13-year lifespan rule does not automatically bind characters in The Arrivals;
- themes of fear, collective blame and social conflict can feed the Otherworlder storyline.

### Chainsaw Man

- characters remain important;
- fear and Devils provide strong material for the supernatural feedback loop;
- project continuity can choose different futures without pretending Source Canon did not happen.

### Jujutsu Kaisen

- characters and curses are major contributions;
- curses can reinforce social consequences of widespread fear/hatred toward Otherworlders.

### Tokyo Ghoul

- useful especially for selected characters and themes of identity/humanity;
- The Arrivals does not need to import every part of its source-world structure.

### Solo Leveling

- useful for characters, threats and power systems;
- source power scale does not automatically define global Continuum hierarchy.

### The 100 Girlfriends

- remove the forced-romantic-destiny mechanic as an unquestioned obligation;
- the protagonist can learn the truth, become angry about the lack of agency and explain it;
- each girl can choose her own path;
- some relationships may remain romantic while others become something else.

### BOFURI

- bring the people while preserving meaningful versions of their game identities/avatars;
- later define how game-derived abilities interact with physical Continuum reality.

### Dr. STONE

- Senku may arrive aware that his cognition is below his former level;
- he can begin above average rather than instantly at full source capacity;
- experimentation and adaptation to Continuum allow his intelligence/capability to recover and grow;
- the nerf becomes something Senku himself notices and investigates rather than an invisible author convenience;
- his long-term scientific contribution can help evolve civilization without solving it immediately.

### Power-fantasy sources

A source claim that a character is 'the strongest' does not automatically make them globally dominant in The Arrivals.

Their powers may be:

- compatibility-limited;
- recontextualized by Continuum rules;
- partially nerfed;
- locally/niche powerful;
- redirected toward civilian or specialist roles;
- removed from The Arrivals if they ultimately damage the story more than they contribute.

## 16. Frieren / Fern emotional core

Strong developing premise:

- Frieren and Fern do not arrive together.
- Frieren eventually has reason to genuinely believe Fern died.
- This reopens the wound left by Himmel differently because Frieren had already learned to value Fern while Fern was alive.
- Frieren comes to understand Fern as family / daughter-like rather than merely a traveling companion.
- Fern's absence should be shown visually through routines, empty space, objects, habits and pauses.
- When Fern is found alive, Frieren may react with unusually physical and sustained emotion.
- The reunion does not instantly erase the aftermath; temporary overprotection and emotional residue can continue.

Exact timing and dialogue remain open.

## 17. Character review philosophy

A missed narratively identifiable character defaults conceptually to:

```text
UNREVIEWED_RESERVE
```

not `EXCLUDED`.

Keep separate:

```text
REVIEW_STATE
SOURCE_PROMINENCE
CONTINUUM_ROLE
ARRIVAL_PRIORITY
```

A minor source character may become important in The Arrivals. A source protagonist may remain low-focus for long stretches.

Character locks should happen after Library/source ingestion reveals the actual cast and evidence.

---

## 18. Relationships and romance

Major relationships never auto-canon.

The MC's final romance remains intentionally undecided.

A recurring accidental-harem joke is allowed if it emerges naturally, but:

- nobody automatically falls in love with the MC;
- no franchise romance mechanic transfers by default;
- no passive seduction power exists;
- crushes do not force an end state;
- strong friendship may remain friendship.

Social misunderstanding, gradual chemistry and an overly analytical internal companion can be used for comedy.

---

## 19. Visual direction — coherent multistyle

Characters may retain meaningful elements of their source visual grammar while occupying one physically coherent world.

Potentially preserved traits include line weight, facial simplification, expression timing, deformation language, texture, shading, manga hatching, impact FX and apparent animation cadence.

Shared scenes still unify perspective, ground contact, light direction, cast shadows, occlusion, weather, environmental particles, camera and spatial sound.

A useful test remains Bocchi + Frieren + Okarun in one scene: each retains recognizable expressive grammar while the same physical environment binds them together.

## 20. Library relationship

The current 49-title acquisition pool belongs to Mauricio's **Continuum Library**, not automatically to The Arrivals.

A source may stay Library-only or be used in another independent project such as a single-franchise continuation/rewrite.

The Arrivals source membership will be selected later from the Library and can evolve over time.

This distinction is intentional and should never be collapsed again.

## 21. Current maturity

The creative foundation is considered roughly **90% conceptually settled** by the user: the core MC direction, world growth, arrival philosophy, consequence philosophy, dynamic pillars, ordinary-life pacing, Otherworlder conflict and project/source separation are all strongly liked.

The remaining work is primarily refinement, source ingestion, cast selection, per-character arrival/body/memory decisions, exact opening cohort, detailed season planning and scene-level execution.

Nothing in this document authorizes implementation phases to be skipped.
