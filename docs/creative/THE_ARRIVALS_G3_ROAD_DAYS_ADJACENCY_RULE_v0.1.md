# The Arrivals — G3 / Road Days Adjacency Rule v0.1

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE ROUGH ORDERING DECISION  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

## Decision

For the current late-S1 review, keep the following as **separate conceptual episode units**:

1. `G3 / First Days / Three Roads`
2. `Road Days / Campfire`

Do not merge them yet.

However, they must remain **adjacent in story order** during the upcoming structural review.

## Review goal

During the detailed late-S1 structure pass, decide whether the pair should become:

- one longer-than-usual episode with G3 arrival/road setup followed by travel/campfire material; or
- two consecutive episodes, with the second functioning as a road/travel character episode and passage-of-time bridge.

The merge decision should be based on pacing, character introduction density, and whether the G3 cast receives enough space without feeling rushed.

## Guardrails

- Do not separate `Road Days / Campfire` far away from the G3 introduction.
- Do not force either option to fit a standard runtime/page quota.
- If merged, the resulting episode may be significantly longer than a normal episode/manga unit.
- If kept separate, the second episode must add real character/world/time progression and not exist as filler.
- Final numbering remains open until the late-S1 order review is complete.
