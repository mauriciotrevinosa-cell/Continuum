# The Arrivals — Group 3 Direction v0.1

**Status:** exploratory / high-confidence direction; NOT locked canon unless later promoted  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Scope:** late-Season-1 Group 3 structure, staggered arrivals, merchant contact, and future search/reconnaissance implications

This document preserves the current Group 3 direction without forcing every discussed future character into the same arrival wave.

---

## 1. Group 3 should not be a single synchronized party

A favored direction is for the late-Season-1 "Group 3" label to describe a **macro arrival wave / story block**, not necessarily several people appearing shoulder-to-shoulder at the same instant.

Characters may appear separated by distance and/or hours or days, then converge through local travel, trade routes, coincidence, or native contact.

This reinforces the broader rule that Arrivals do not follow one standardized pattern.

---

## 2. Current strongest Group 3 anchors

The strongest current anchors are:

- **Okarun** — pays off Momo's reversed-memory relationship thread and proves that a person known to an existing Arrival can appear significantly later.
- **Mikasa** — introduces a disciplined survival / reconnaissance perspective and can later influence fortification and exploration culture.

**Eren and Stark are deliberately deferred.**

They remain valuable future arrivals, but they do not need to enter with Group 3.

Possible later timing discussed:

- **Stark:** early Season 2, where his arrival can provide warmth/comedy after the heavier late-S1 material and create another Frieren/Fern-world reunion.
- **Eren:** potentially around mid-Season 2 or later, allowing the Mikasa/Eren divergent-memory plot to receive its own space rather than competing with the Group 3 convergence.

Exact timing remains open.

---

## 3. Native merchant as first bridge to civilization

A strong possible Group 3 beat is that Okarun, Mikasa, and/or other nearby late arrivals are initially scattered and do not know what `Otherworlder` means.

A merchant traveling a known trade route encounters one or more of them and asks, in effect:

> "Are you Otherworlders?"

The newcomers do not recognize the term.

From their perspective, however, the merchant is the first clear sign of organized civilization and therefore worth following / questioning.

The merchant may know rumors that unusual outsiders have settled near an abandoned town or trade hub and can point them toward the existing Groups 1 and 2 without understanding the deeper multiversal mechanism.

This avoids making the convergence feel like fate simply teleported everyone into the same campsite.

---

## 4. Important world implication: the protagonists may not be the only Arrival cluster

The merchant's recognition of the word `Otherworlder` can imply that the concept is already spreading through local rumor / trade networks.

This does **not** have to mean the merchant personally knows many Arrivals.

It can instead establish a larger possibility:

```text
known settlement of Otherworlders
+ rumors from travelers
+ unexplained strangers appearing elsewhere
= locals begin using a shared label
```

The critical realization for the protagonists becomes:

> **There may already be other Arrival groups somewhere in the world who have not found us yet.**

This expands the story beyond "people keep appearing near our camp" into a genuinely regional / global phenomenon.

---

## 5. Group 3 as the seed for an Arrival-search institution

Once Group 3 demonstrates that late arrivals can be found through roads, merchants, rumors, and deliberate travel, the settlement gains a reason to stop waiting passively.

This can become one of the origins of the future exploration / reconnaissance organization inspired in spirit by *Attack on Titan*'s outward-looking expedition culture but adapted to The Arrivals.

Its mission would not be only military exploration.

Possible responsibilities:

- patrol trade routes and wilderness;
- investigate reports of unusual newcomers;
- find isolated Otherworlder groups;
- escort vulnerable arrivals back to safety;
- verify whether a reported Arrival is dangerous, lost, injured, or simply confused;
- map safe routes;
- establish contact points with merchants / villages;
- search for missing loved ones;
- later scout monsters, resources, and hostile forces.

This gives the institution an emotional origin:

> **If someone we love can arrive weeks or months later, we should not make them survive alone until they somehow find us.**

---

## 6. Why delaying Stark and Eren helps

Separating major reunions prevents Season 1 from spending every emotional payoff at once.

A staggered model creates recurring proof that the world is still changing:

```text
late S1: Okarun / Mikasa / Group 3 convergence
→ first undeniable late-arrival proof for existing cast

early S2: possible Stark arrival
→ another source-world reunion with a very different emotional/comedic tone

mid/later S2: possible Eren arrival
→ Mikasa's memories collide with an Eren from an earlier personal timeline
```

This preserves narrative runway and makes arrival itself remain meaningful after Season 1.

---

## 7. Current working conclusion

The current preferred direction is **not** to force a large fixed Group 3 roster yet.

Instead:

```text
Group 3 story block
= Okarun + Mikasa as strongest current anchors
+ possibly a small number of additional characters selected later
+ staggered / nearby arrival positions
+ native merchant contact
+ route toward the existing settlement
+ proof that late known arrivals are possible
+ realization that other scattered Arrival groups may exist
+ seed for a dedicated search / reconnaissance institution
```

Eren and Stark remain intentionally available for later, separately timed arrivals.
