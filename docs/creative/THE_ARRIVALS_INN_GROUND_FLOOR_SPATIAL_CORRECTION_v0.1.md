## Correction

From the approved main-entry / common-room view, the **fireplace/chimney mass is directly adjacent to the stair assembly**. They must read as one connected architectural cluster.

The following interpretations are rejected:

- a window between fireplace and stairs;
- a full wall bay between fireplace and stairs;
- a separate room or passage inserted between them;
- moving the chimney or stairs to solve a generated-image composition.

The latest creator-approved measured shell and program remain authoritative. This correction supersedes any ambiguous diagram spacing or prior generated interior that implies separation. Exact revised coordinates and wall thicknesses should be folded into the next numbered measured-plan/Spatial-Bible release; until then, every CAL-02 / CAL-03 prompt and review must enforce direct adjacency.
