## Volume guardrails

- Mau's birthday is chosen by the household; it is not recovered from memory.
- Frieren's E15 gift is the original S1E1 bucket + original plant, now healthy and accompanied by flowers.
- E15 carries the first favored explicit use of `home`.
- `Otherworlder` is coined as a new, neutral descriptor in E16.
- The forgotten route home becomes spatially legible from the mercantile-town side.
- E17 uses repeated working days and return-home compositions rather than a single escalating mission.
- The dead village contracts visibly as salvage feeds the inn.
- E18 chocolate lowers inhibition only; it is not a love potion, truth serum or romance confirmation.
- Frieren's E18 wardrobe escalation uses Mau's hooded outer layer + cap + Frieren's own comfortable short bottoms.
- No kiss contact occurs in E18.
- G3 groupings remain fixed:
  - Okarun + Umaru + Kita;
  - Coco + Qifrey + Wakana + Marin;
  - Mikasa initially alone.
- Okarun cannot access the missing person's name, face or identity in S1.
- G3 does not reach G1/G2 in Season 1.
- E19 safe-night sequence must genuinely feel safe before the hard cut.
- Current S1-ending Sukuna body state is Meguna, superseding old Heian-body material.
- Sukuna says `So that brat is here.` before `Domain Expansion. Malevolent Shrine.`
- Exact catastrophe radius and casualty count stay unlocked.
- Sukuna suppresses cursed energy only after the catastrophe and wrong-world realization.
- No Yuta reaction in S1 after the hard cut.
- Never return to the inn after the Sukuna cut.
