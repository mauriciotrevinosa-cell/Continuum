## G5 continuity lock

G5 forms accidentally because Diablo is searching for Mau after almost two days of absence.

Active high-confidence G5 set in this volume:
- Senku Ishigami + Suika;
- Mai Zenin;
- Nijika Ijichi + Ryo Yamada;
- Richeh + Tetia;
- Nazuna Nanakusa;
- Vamola + Turbo Granny + Seiko Ayase;
- Jinshi;
- Milim Nava.

Milim is part of the arrival window because she finds/recognizes Diablo and follows him to Rimuru; she is not treated as a true Mau false positive.

The obsolete separate **Frieren Hits First** chapter is not in active reader order. The Frieren/Diablo clash now lives inside **A Demon at the Door**, where it belongs chronologically.

The witch chapters intentionally include a short controlled backtrack:
- G5 / Diablo search reaches the eight-signature lead on Day 3;
- **Witches First** and **Not Their Work** reveal that Mau had already been found by the witches the previous day;
- **Eight, Not One** returns to the Day-3 search-party present.
