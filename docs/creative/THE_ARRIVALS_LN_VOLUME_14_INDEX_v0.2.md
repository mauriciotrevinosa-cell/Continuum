## Creator-revision lock — 2026-10-04

The Move now establishes:
- room choice as chosen closeness, not proof of independence through distance;
- Mau/Frieren's quiet-corner plan losing because family members independently choose nearby rooms;
- Frieren's east-facing window preserved by reorienting the unfinished residential plan rather than separating people;
- Bocchi choosing acoustic privacy without choosing emotional isolation;
- the rejection of franchise/source-world housing enclaves;
- the family core beginning to resemble a future mega-inn organically;
- S3 habitation as an **essential first cluster**, not a finished city or final family-house;
- ordinary common-room life as structural story material.

## Handoff to V15

- the inhabited first cluster is now the default lived home for many ongoing residents, while the permanent family-house remains unfinished;
- old inn remains active, maintained, usable and emotionally protected;
- Rem/Kaneki/Aira and G5 have explicit non-identical statuses;
- settlement remains intentionally incomplete and materially flawed; S3 has not finished the final family-house, atelier, workshop, clinic or civic district;
- Arrival House does not yet exist as a dedicated institution;
- migration/refuge pressure can now arrive at a place people actually inhabit.
