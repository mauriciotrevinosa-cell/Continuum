V17 reveals that repeated sacrifice was only the surface test. The real question is whether Mau can believe people might choose him and Continuum freely. Return/Stay remains morally open. Mau's late failure evolves from unilateral death to unilateral agency, the family intercepts him before any irreversible deal, and S3 ends with individual choices still private and unresolved.
