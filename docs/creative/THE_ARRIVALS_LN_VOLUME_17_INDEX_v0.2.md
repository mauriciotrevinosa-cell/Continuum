## Creator-revision authority / antagonist expansion — 2026-10-04

This volume now makes two distinctions explicit:
- Mau's relational centrality is real and does **not** need to be erased to avoid authoritarianism;
- affection / influence / being the person everyone knows is not the same as owning decisions.

External representation becomes issue-specific and preferably plural.

The anti-Otherworlder pressure also advances from vague rumor into a documented hostile classification frame that:
- assumes every community has a primary authority;
- instructs assessors to choose a central figure even when none is declared;
- recasts Searchers as recruitment, Arrival House as retention and family relationships as command functions;
- increasingly names Mau as the supposed control point.

This is evidence of coordinated framing, not yet proof of one mastermind.

## Handoff to V18

- Eren is integrated by choice, with unlived future guilt explicitly rejected;
- defense is layered, not wall-centered;
- exit rights/emergency review are explicit;
- sanctuary and due process exist from lived cases;
- domestic convergence shows settlement worth defending;
- outside discourse increasingly attaches Mau's name to settlement;
- residents begin treating Mau as default emergency sacrifice;
- repeated maps/phrases support coordination pattern without mastermind reveal;
- Last Defense now has social, infrastructural and ethical stakes.
