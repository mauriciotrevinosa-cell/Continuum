## Trial accumulation lock

The Trial must not read as "Mau dies many times" summarized by a number.

Reader experience should establish a progression:

```text
first deaths are individually terrifying
-> Mau searches for third options
-> other people demand their own agency
-> Mau survives some scenarios and discovers survival can hurt more
-> scenarios expand into long-term regret rather than only death
-> the count becomes cognitively useless
-> the body learns reset / anticipates catastrophe
-> exact total remains unresolved, but scale becomes explicitly hundreds
-> Mau sees contradictory outcomes for the same apparent choice
-> no universal correct answer survives
-> Mau begins to think the mercy may be removing the burden of choosing
-> Trial shifts from "why will you die?" to "can you accept being chosen?"
```

The exact numerical death/loop count remains intentionally unfixed.

## Backdoor-deal motivation lock

The late-S3 backdoor deal is **not** merely:
> Mau's old controlling nature returns.

The old flaw remains relevant, but the new causal engine is Trial trauma.

Mau has subjectively lived:
- hundreds of deaths;
- scenarios in which loved ones die because he survives;
- scenarios in which others choose him;
- scenarios where nobody dies but people regret an irreversible decision years or decades later;
- contradictory scenarios where the same choice produces opposite emotional outcomes;
- long constructed lives in which Mau must keep living after the choice.

He therefore exits the Trial with two simultaneously held beliefs:

1. other people have the right to choose their own lives;
2. irreversible choice can leave a person carrying pain for decades, and Mau desperately wants to spare everyone even a fraction of what he has already experienced.

The backdoor plan grows from belief #2 overwhelming Mau's ability to live belief #1.

His internal logic is:

```text
I do not know what the correct choice is.
The Trial proved there may be no universally correct choice.
They get only one real life / one real Return-or-Stay decision.
I had hundreds of iterations and still could not find a painless answer.
If the burden itself can be removed, perhaps I can spare them the aftermath.
If somebody must carry the moral weight of removing that choice, let it be me.
```

This is compassionate logic transformed into an agency violation.

The family must **not** dismiss Mau's experience as fake or irrelevant.

Their answer is, in substance:

> We believe you.
> What you suffered was real to you.
> Those constructed lives still were not our lives.
> Your suffering does not give you authority over our one real choice.
> Let us decide.

## Ten-day clock / S3-S4 handoff lock

The Return/Stay offer opens a **ten-day decision window**.

Active timing:

```text
Day 0  Mau exits Trial physically intact but conditioned by hundreds of loops
Night 0  almost no sleep / repeated remembered deaths
Day 1  real people's questions trigger Trial consequence-patterns
Night 1  fragmented sleep / wake-reset confusion
Day 2  Mau functions publicly while accumulating future-regret scenarios
Night 2  private modeling -> four versions of note
pre-dawn Day 3  Mau leaves alone to ask for collective-return terms
Day 3  family catches him on the road BEFORE he reaches any Goddess contact site
Day 3  confrontation / full Trial disclosure / Mau Jail Part Two
end S3  seven days remain; no final individual Return/Stay choice has been spoken
S4  remaining decision window + individual choices + Trial aftermath
```

The backdoor attempt is stopped **before divine negotiation begins**:
- Mau never reaches the intended clearing;
- the Goddess does not appear;
- no request is made;
- no collective terms are shown;
- no binding agreement exists.

The only completed violation is Mau choosing secrecy and attempting to carry the decision alone.

## Season-end lock

- Goddess Trial authority is demonstrated as bounded domain authority;
- death looping is temporary and Trial-only;
- physical injuries reset, learned fear / anticipatory reactions do not;
- repeated deaths are rendered selectively with cumulative deterioration;
- the exact count is open, but the scale is hundreds;
- several loops contain no death and instead force Mau to live with long-term consequence / regret;
- "Because they're my family" is explicitly not the final question;
- the real question is free choice and whether Mau can accept being chosen;
- Return and Stay remain morally open;
- individual terms remain private;
- no full Stay/Return list is revealed;
- Mau experiences a visible post-Trial aftermath before the backdoor plan;
- Mau recognizes that real residents have one life each while he received hundreds of iterations;
- Mau's evolved flaw is carrying everyone else's irreversible choice/regret;
- family intercepts Mau on the road before he reaches the intended Goddess contact site or asks any divine question;
- Frieren identifies the agency violation directly;
- the confrontation explicitly acknowledges rather than minimizes Mau's Trial trauma;
- Mau Jail Part Two follows as consequence and immediate safety boundary, not cure;
- a ten-day Return/Stay window is active; S3 ends on Day 3 with seven days remaining;
