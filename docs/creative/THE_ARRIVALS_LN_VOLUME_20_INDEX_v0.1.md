## What this volume covers

S4 opens exactly where S3 ends: the evening of Day 3 of the Goddess's ten-day Return/Stay window, with seven days left. This volume runs from Day 4 to the morning of Day 11.

- **Anger.** Ring Three's and Rings One and Two's, day after day. It is not hate. The S2 questions come back: did he love them, did he feel loved?
- **The talk.** The condition (DH-023) is kept. The number, **437**, is finally said and finally understood: every reset was a death.
- **The two days.** Mau leaves by his own signed contract, to somewhere he never died. Ciel sends one word home each evening.
- **The return**, and the conversations he owes.
- **The last days of the window.** Nobody named leaves. Only the frightened and the complaining go, and that is how Ring Four ends.
- **The coins.** Every stayer gets one: the Trial's prize, what Mau spent millennia to win.

## Handoff to Volume 21

Volume 21 is the month of peace: ugly days, healing days and days of pure fun. **Zero new arrivals.** Coins are held, and "has anyone decided?" becomes a household question. The volume ends with the witches' new contract.
