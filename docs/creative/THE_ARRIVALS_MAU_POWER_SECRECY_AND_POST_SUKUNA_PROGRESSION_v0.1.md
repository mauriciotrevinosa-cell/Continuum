# The Arrivals — Mau Power Secrecy and Post-Sukuna Progression v0.1

Status: **PROVISIONAL CREATOR-DIRECTED POWER/CONTINUITY SEED — NOT FULLY MECHANICS-LOCKED**

Purpose: preserve current direction for how Mau's unknown ability should be revealed, concealed, and evolve around the S2 Sukuna / Second Chance arc without prematurely turning him into a conventional powerhouse.

This note supplements `THE_ARRIVALS_S2_SUKUNA_SECOND_CHANCE_MAJOR_ARC_SEED_v0.1.md` and the Frieren/Himmel/Mau future arc material.

## 1. Frieren's mortality realization should remain a slow-burn consequence

Frieren choosing Mau does **not** require the human-lifespan problem to become an immediate post-confession crisis.

A better possibility is to leave the issue dormant until much later — potentially S3 or beyond — when an ordinary comment about age, lifespan, mortality, or future planning causes the idea to finally click for her.

The important effect is delayed recognition:

- Frieren has already chosen Mau;
- she is already living the relationship rather than debating whether it is real;
- then a seemingly small comment makes her realize that, if Mau is truly human, their time together may be extremely short by her standards;
- the thought can begin turning quietly in her head before becoming its own later arc.

Do not rush this simply because the relationship becomes explicit in S2.

## 2. Hollow Purple is Mau's first undeniable power reveal to most of the cast

Before the Sukuna battle, Mau may have displayed only minor, ambiguous, or easily dismissed abilities.

Current direction:

- Mau may have one or two simple abilities before the battle;
- he may be somewhat tougher or more capable than at the beginning of the story;
- he can participate in limited combat by this point;
- he is still comparatively weak and should not read as a hidden top-tier fighter.

The Hollow Purple analogue is therefore the **first time most witnesses see unmistakable evidence that Mau's unknown ability can do something extraordinary and dangerous**.

That reveal should feel alarming precisely because it is radically out of scale with what they believed Mau could do.

## 3. Information-control pact after the battle

The people who understand the most about what happened should reach a deliberate consensus that Mau's ability is not yet safe to publicize.

Core participants currently envisioned:

- Mau;
- Frieren;
- Yuta;
- Rimuru.

Their reasoning:

- they still do not know what Mau's power actually is;
- they do not know its true ceiling;
- they do not know what triggers it;
- they do not know what it costs;
- they do not know whether it can be reproduced safely;
- revealing too much could frighten the settlement or attract dangerous attention.

Therefore they agree to hide as much of the mechanism as practical.

Yuta and Rimuru may know more of the truth because Mau trusted them during preparation and can later explain how the one-use Hollow Purple analogue was assembled.

People who were not part of the battle/preparation do **not** automatically receive that explanation.

The group may give outsiders a simpler, incomplete account of the final blast rather than disclose Mau's unknown internal system.

This secrecy is protective rather than manipulative: they are containing an unresolved risk.

## 4. Mau does not become overpowered after surviving Sukuna

The Sukuna arc should mark growth, but not a sudden genre shift in Mau's combat level.

After recovery he may plausibly be:

- somewhat stronger;
- somewhat more durable;
- more comfortable under pressure;
- capable of basic fighting rather than requiring protection in every encounter;
- more aware that his internal system can analyze/reconstruct phenomena.

But he should still be far from the strongest members of the cast.

The story should preserve the gap between:

> Mau can now survive and contribute in some fights

and

> Mau is a conventional high-tier combatant.

Those are not the same thing.

## 5. Hollow Purple is consumed / unavailable after the one-use construction

The S2 Hollow Purple analogue is not a permanent unlock.

Current intended rule:

- Mau's system assembled temporary parameters specifically for that crisis;
- those parameters were consumed, collapsed, or invalidated when he used the technique;
- the system later tells him that the same Hollow Purple construction cannot simply be used again;
- new parameters would have to be created from new analysis / understanding before another attempt is possible.

This prevents the Sukuna climax from becoming a repeatable button Mau can press in later battles.

The first use should remain exceptional.

## 6. Possible later reconstruction with Gojo

A later Gojo-focused period may create the conditions for Mau to understand the phenomenon properly enough to attempt a new version.

This should happen through interaction, observation, explanation, and rebuilt requirements — not because the original Sukuna-era copy remained permanently stored.

Gojo's presence can therefore matter mechanically without retroactively making the first reproduction easy.

Exact future rules remain open.

## 7. Long-term retained toolkit should stay small

Current creator preference is that Mau's enduring personal toolkit should remain narrow rather than becoming a collection of every spectacular power he has ever analyzed.

A possible long-term endpoint is that he meaningfully retains only a very small number of abilities, especially:

- the flower-related ability / technique already associated with him;
- RCT / self-healing capability after sufficient analysis and development.

Hollow Purple should not automatically become a permanent signature move.

This reinforces a useful distinction:

- Mau's unknown system may temporarily reconstruct extraordinary things under specific conditions;
- Mau himself does not necessarily permanently own every reconstructed technique.

## 8. RCT and the Second Chance interaction

The goddess's Second Chance and Yuta's RCT should continue to have separate roles.

Second Chance:

- returns / reanchors Mau's soul to his body;
- restores the body only enough to make rescue possible;
- does not fully heal him.

Yuta:

- still does the brutal practical work of keeping Mau alive and repairing what can be repaired;
- remains essential to Mau's survival.

Mau's own later RCT capacity can emerge from analysis during/after this process, but should not erase Yuta's contribution or imply Mau secretly healed himself completely during the three-day recovery.

## 9. Secrecy should create future story pressure

Hiding Mau's true mechanism gives the story future tension without requiring immediate betrayal drama.

Possible consequences later:

- someone realizes the public explanation of the Sukuna blast does not add up;
- a new arrival recognizes what Mau reproduced;
- Gojo immediately understands that the event should have been impossible;
- Mau has to decide who else can safely know;
- settlement leadership eventually has to debate whether such abilities must be disclosed for public safety.

These are future possibilities, not locked beats.

## Guardrails

- Do not make Mau secretly overpowered before the story earns it.
- Do not make Hollow Purple permanently spammable.
- Do not let copying/reconstruction trivialize the original user's mastery.
- Keep the exact nature and ceiling of Mau's system unknown for now.
- Preserve Yuta and Rimuru as trusted early confidants.
- Preserve Frieren as part of the secrecy decision after the battle.
- Do not force the lifespan crisis immediately after the romance becomes explicit.
- Let Mau's permanent toolkit remain much smaller than the set of phenomena he may temporarily reconstruct.
