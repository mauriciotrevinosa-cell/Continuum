# The Arrivals — Mau Visual Reference Pack v0.1

**Status:** APPROVED BASE VISUAL CANON  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

This document defines the production-facing visual baseline for Mau. It is based on creator-provided photo references shared in the production chat. The photo files themselves are not required to be committed to Git for this document to remain useful; Continuum may ingest them separately as reference assets.

---

## Core design target

Mau should read as a stylized anime/manga interpretation of the creator rather than a photorealistic copy.

The goal is:

- clearly recognizable in face, hair, build, smile, and general presence;
- visually compatible with the rest of the crossover cast;
- simple enough to reproduce consistently panel to panel;
- not reduced to a generic anime pretty-boy archetype;
- not made overly muscular, angular, aggressive, or childlike.

---

## Face

Preserve:

- youthful face;
- soft oval-to-slightly-rounded facial structure;
- soft rather than square jaw;
- dark, clearly defined eyebrows;
- expressive eyes;
- moderate/small nose;
- soft mouth shape;
- distinctive warm smile when relaxed;
- a strong contrast between warm smiling expressions and quiet, introspective neutral expressions.

Mau should be able to look kind and approachable when smiling, but slightly distant, observant, or melancholy when silent.

Avoid:

- exaggerated sharp jaw;
- hypermasculine facial planes;
- generic model-like beauty treatment that erases resemblance;
- excessively childlike proportions.

---

## Hair

This is a key identity feature.

- dark brown / deep brown;
- medium length;
- naturally voluminous;
- lightly wavy;
- loose, irregular, slightly messy shape;
- front and side strands may fall naturally around the forehead;
- should not read as rigidly styled, pin-straight, very short, or long-haired.

Hair silhouette should remain recognizable across panels and rendering styles.

---

## Build and silhouette

- slim-athletic build;
- light, agile silhouette rather than bulky or fighter-heavy;
- moderate shoulders;
- relatively lean limbs;
- youthful proportions;
- medium to slightly-above-medium apparent height relative to the cast.

Mau should look physically capable without having a bodybuilder or combat-specialist silhouette.

---

## Expression language

Important recurring expression modes:

1. **Warm / open** — easy smile, friendly, relaxed.
2. **Curious / literal** — attentive eyes, slight head tilt, direct focus.
3. **Quiet / observant** — neutral mouth, calm face, watching rather than performing emotion.
4. **Lost / introspective** — subdued, distant, slightly melancholy without melodrama.
5. **Confused innocence** — genuine lack of understanding rather than comedic stupidity.
6. **Protective / alarmed** — intensity should feel unusual because it contrasts with his normal softness.

The visual performance should preserve Mau's warmth and odd calmness at the same time.

---

## Clothing logic

The creator photo set supports a casual, clean, youthful wardrobe language: hoodies, lightweight shirts, polos/knits, simple fitted casual clothing, occasional sporty pieces, and occasional more dressed-up clothing.

For The Arrivals, translate that energy into the setting rather than copying modern outfits literally.

Production clothing principles:

- simple;
- comfortable;
- functional;
- not flashy;
- clean silhouette;
- limited unnecessary ornamentation;
- should feel like something Mau would accept and keep wearing without caring much about fashion signaling.

Modern creator outfits are reference for taste and silhouette, not literal canon wardrobe unless a scene specifically calls for it.

---

## Stylization rule

Preferred direction: **stylized but faithful**.

Mau should look like the creator translated into the visual language of the manga/anime cast, not like a pasted realistic face and not like a completely redesigned original character.

Preserve, in order of importance:

1. hair silhouette;
2. facial softness / proportions;
3. expressive eyes and eyebrows;
4. smile;
5. slim-athletic body language;
6. quiet/warm overall presence.

# Glasses progression — approved canon note

Mau does **not** begin the story wearing glasses.

Later, Anko notices through ordinary observation that Mau has some difficulty reading small text / seeing fine written detail. This should emerge naturally rather than through a large medical scene.

Possible visual seeds before the payoff:

- Mau brings text slightly closer than expected;
- squints at small writing, maps, forms, or notes;
- takes longer than expected to parse small text;
- leans closer over guild papers or books;
- does not initially treat it as an important problem.

Anko is the person who notices the pattern and acts on it. The beat should fit her practical notebook/ruler/problem-solving energy: she observes, confirms, and helps solve it.

The eventual glasses become a **story-earned accessory and visual progression marker**, not a retroactive starting trait.

### Glasses design direction

Preferred:

- thin frame;
- discreet;
- round to slightly oval shape;
- visually soft;
- light enough not to dominate his face;
- should complement the observant/quiet side of Mau without turning him into a caricatured 'nerd' design.

Initial use can be primarily for:

- reading;
- writing;
- maps;
- notes;
- guild forms;
- quiet study/work scenes.

Whether they later become more frequent everyday wear can evolve naturally from visual production and story use.

---

## Continuity rule

Do not place Mau in glasses in early S1 material unless a later approved continuity pass deliberately seeds the eyesight issue without contradicting existing scenes.

The accessory progression is:

`no glasses → subtle reading-detail difficulty → Anko notices → glasses introduced → optional increased use over time`

This progression should be preserved across scripts, manga panelization, Continuum character manifests, and later model-reference packages.
