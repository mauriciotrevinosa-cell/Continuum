# The Arrivals — Memory Crisis Rescue Trio v0.1

**Status:** STRONG DIRECTION / THIRD RESCUER OPEN  
**Date:** 2026-09-26

This document addresses the external rescue during Mau's later memory/system crisis. It is not the Sukuna rescue.

---

## 1. Preserve Ori's existing function

Ori should remain inside the second-disappearance incident.

Current preserved logic:
- Mau destabilizes / runs or disappears again;
- Ori goes with him because she has become a reliable constant for him;
- she does not hide this in order to steal him from Frieren;
- she goes because she believes otherwise Mau may run alone;
- Ori is therefore already present when the crisis escalates.

Ori is **not** counted as one of the external three rescuers.

That keeps her established emotional payoff intact while allowing another group to discover/recover Mau and Ori from outside.

---

## 2. External rescue trio — current shape

Strong current direction:

1. **Rem**
2. **Ken Kaneki**
3. **one additional character selected primarily for tracking/detection/extraction utility**

The trio should not feel like three interchangeable fighters.

Each should solve a different part of the problem.

---

## 3. Rem — strong first rescuer

Rem is a good fit because her oni nature provides a source-consistent basis for unusual sensitivity to mana/miasma, depending on the exact source snapshot chosen later.

Possible story function:
- detects that something living is present even while Mau's signature is unstable/noisy;
- distinguishes distress/intent from a purely hostile monster signal;
- helps locate the crisis zone;
- physically refuses to abandon Mau/Ori once found.

Do **not** lock exact sensing mechanics until Rem's arrival snapshot/source evidence is finalized.

Her emotional value is also important:
- she understands what it means to attach enormous worth to another person;
- she can respond to Mau as a person in crisis rather than as an anomaly that needs to be destroyed.

---

## 4. Kaneki — strong second rescuer

Kaneki should cover the **biological / ghoul-like** side of the incident.

The memory crisis already favors:
- Mau's internal systems destabilizing;
- shifting biological signatures;
- involuntary kagune-like Construction / morphology;
- fear that observers could misread Mau as becoming a monster.

Kaneki is uniquely useful because:
- ghoul senses are much sharper than ordinary human senses;
- ghoul biology is organized around RC cells/kakuhou/kagune;
- he can recognize that Mau's manifestation has ghoul-like logic without concluding that Mau is simply a hostile ghoul;
- he has lived the human/monster identity boundary personally.

Story function:

> Kaneki can be the person who looks at something horrifying happening to Mau and still says, in effect, **"He's still in there."**

That is more valuable than adding another high-damage fighter.

Exact Tokyo Ghoul biology transferred through Construction remains later source/mechanics work.

---

## 5. Third rescuer — function before identity

Do **not** choose the third person solely because they are another fan-favorite.

The third rescuer should close a capability gap left by Rem + Kaneki.

Preferred functions, in order:

### A. Long-range magical / signature tracking

A character who can:
- detect mana/anomalous energy at range;
- notice Mau's signature changing rather than disappearing;
- triangulate where the crisis is happening;
- guide Rem/Kaneki through a dangerous or distorted area.

### B. Extraction / mobility

If Rem's final source snapshot already gives enough useful mana sensitivity, the third person may be more valuable as:
- high-speed extraction;
- teleportation/spatial movement;
- flight;
- barrier/navigation support.

### C. Stabilization

Another option is someone who cannot solve the crisis but can keep Mau alive / contained long enough to return him to Rimuru/Raphael and the wider household.

The final choice should avoid duplicating Kaneki's ghoul-specific role.

---

## 6. Why the trio works thematically

The rescue should demonstrate the value of the increasingly diverse city.

No one system fully understands Mau.

Instead:

```text
Ori       = emotional constant already beside him
Rem       = mana/miasma/person-intent clue
Kaneki    = biological/kagune clue
Third     = tracking/extraction/stabilization gap
→ combined evidence lets them find and recover Mau
```

This makes the rescue an ensemble/worldbuilding payoff rather than coincidence.

It also reinforces a larger Continuum principle:

> **Mau is becoming too cross-system to be understood by one franchise's rules alone.**

Exact version-specific abilities must still be checked against the final source snapshots selected for The Arrivals.
