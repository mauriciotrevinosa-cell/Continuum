# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 9 — What Goes Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S10E03
**Continuity state:** B+157 morning. Guest yard dispositions voluntary. Independent courier and written accounts go out. No judicial outcome invented. The village keeps its keys and its obligations.

*When: West comes to breakfast, he has carried his own cup back from the road.*

He sets it beside the other cups. Then he takes it away and washes it first. The water runs for quite a long time. The woman who came with the horse watches him from the doorway. She has a folded coat on her arm. When West has finished with the cup, she asks if he wants the coat.

He touches the edge of a sleeve.

“It's yours,” she says. “You left it on the peg when they took you out.” ˣ

West takes it. He checks the pockets and puts it on. He doesn't say thank you. She waits until she can see that he doesn't want to say anything else and goes to get her breakfast.

At the other end of the table Nell is trying to read a page Jinshi has given her. It says what they can verify. Somebody asked that she be held. Nobody brought the order with them, and the messenger who has gone to look for it is carrying her name, rather than her.

She turns it over.

“How long do I have?” she asks. ˣ

“The bed? Tonight at least. Ask me again tomorrow.” ᶠᵉ

Fern has her book shut. She can see that Nell noticed. There is room on the table for another piece of paper if she wants one.

“My sister isn't going to know where to look.” ˣ

“Would you like to write to her?” ᶠᵉ

Nell looks down at the blank side of the sheet.

“Can I tell you what to write?” ˣ

Fern opens the book to get at the clean pages she keeps in the back.

* * *

There are two horses at the marker by the time Mau gets down there. One belongs to the keeper and one belongs to the courier. The cart has been emptied. There are boards at the bottom, dark and clean where three bodies had kept the dust from getting in.

Jinshi has the keeper's account folded once in his hand.

“Read me the last bit,” the keeper says. ᴱᵐ

Jinshi reads it. It says what they did and who stepped out of the cart. It says nobody asked them to surrender. The keeper rubs a place on his coat while he listens.

“It'll be read in the grainhouse?” Jinshi asks. ᴶⁱ

“In the yard. They all fit in the yard.” ᴱᵐ

The courier has a copy. A second copy will go with the keeper. Nell's letter has a different destination. None of them needs Mau's seal.

Perin comes along behind him and asks if he can say something to the keeper. The keeper looks around before he realizes nobody else is going to answer.

Perin says he wants the owner of the goat told that he will pay him if he is allowed to come back and work. He would rather the answer come here in writing. He doesn't want to get in the cart to hear it.

The keeper looks at the boards.

“I'll tell him.” ᴱᵐ

Perin thanks him. He has spent some time working out how to ask the question and very little time working out what to do with his hands once he has. He puts them in his pockets and goes to wait for West at the wicket.

* * *

Mau watches the cart turn. It takes three attempts to get the horse around without running a wheel over the key's little mark in the road. Nobody else can see the mark. He can see exactly where the teeth were.

“Will they believe him?” he asks. ᴹ

“Some will,” Jinshi says. ᴶⁱ

The keeper's horse starts to walk. On the way out, the woman who steadied it yesterday twists around and lifts a hand to West. West puts his hands deeper in the coat. A moment later he lifts one back, when she has almost turned away.

The courier waits to give them the road. There is another pouch at her saddle. It has Nell's letter in it.

Mau stays until both have gone. Then he asks Fern if there's anything else that needs doing before he goes up.

She thinks about it. She has a list. She doesn't look at it.

“Go have breakfast,” she says. “I have the next part.” ᶠᵉ
