# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 10 — Before the Bell

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S10E04
**Continuity state:** B+157 afternoon. Shoko allows brief unpowered footwork with supervision. Mau and Yuka set aside time to go out. No sparring, technique or return to full training.

*When: Gojo puts three marks on the practice floor, Mau asks what happened to the other nine.*

“Your wife made me promise not to move them farther apart while you were doing it.” ᴳ

Yuka is on the other side of the room. She is working the end of a practice blade over a rag with a little oil on it, and when he looks over she keeps on doing it.

“Shoko said three,” she says. “I got him to agree that it meant three.” ʸ

“I read the whole agreement,” Gojo says. “I think we'll all be glad to know there aren't any surprises.” ᴳ

He is holding a kitchen timer. He puts it on the floor where they can both see it. The three marks are close enough together that Mau could reach the last by stepping over the middle one. Gojo shows him the order with the tip of his shoe.

Mau does it once. The last step feels good. He does it again and finds out why. He has been turning his right foot out so that the pull comes later, after he's already put his weight down. He tries the same step without turning it out. A little pain runs up his side, clean and bright, and he has to look down.

Gojo doesn't tell him to start over. He waits where he is until Mau looks up.

“Can I try it smaller?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Sure.” ᴳ

He moves the last mark half a foot closer. Mau steps onto it with the foot straight. He had expected there to be a decision about whether to keep going after that. It turns out that it is enough to stand there for a while and find out what his weight feels like.

* * *

He sits before the timer goes.

Gojo picks it up. There are twenty seconds left. He twists it back to nothing and brings it with him to the bench.

“I thought you'd try to finish out the minute,” he says. ᴳ

“I did finish.” ᴹ

Gojo looks at the mark he moved. He puts the timer in his pocket.

“You did.” ᴳ

There's a little pause. Mau can hear Yuka set the practice blade down. Gojo tips his face toward the practice floor as though there is somebody over there he'd rather be talking about.

“I can come tomorrow, if you want,” he says. ᴳ

“You want to come tomorrow?” ᴹ

“I've been waiting to be asked.” ᴳ

Mau looks at him. Gojo has chalk all over the front of his trousers. He tries to take it off with the hand he used to make the marks and makes it worse.

“Come tomorrow,” Mau says. ᴹ

Gojo nods once, very quickly. Then he goes to find out what Shoko wants on the report. Mau has a feeling he is going to be some time.

* * *

Yuka comes over with the rag folded in her hand.

“I told Mai I'd be here till you were done,” she says. “She said she wants to see what the hinge does with the new pin.” ʸ

“You can go.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

She says it and stays standing in front of him. He starts to wonder if she wants the bench, so he moves a little. She sits. Her shoulder is warm where it touches his.

“Will you come out with me tomorrow?” she asks. “Just us. We can stay close enough to walk back.” ʸ

His first thought is of the road and of a little key somebody set down for him. He has been waiting for that thought to go away since yesterday. He takes a moment and tells her what it is.

“We don't have to go past the marker,” she says. “I just want to go somewhere on purpose.” ʸ

He looks at her. In the middle of her palm there is a line from the work she has been doing all afternoon. He lays his hand across it.

“Can we take something to eat?” ᴹ

She starts to smile.

“I'll let you carry the bread.” ʸ

They stay there until somebody comes in looking for the timer. By then she has picked a place, and he has asked if he can bring cheese, and they are both doing an unusually poor job of pretending that this is only about food.
