# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 14 — Eleven Marks

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S10E06
**Continuity state:** B+159 late afternoon. Boy and uncle arrive with eleven marks. Mau learns what their family took from the quarry. No worship accepted, magical bond or protection extended. Guest rules hold.

*When: the boy at the gate is asked what he brought, he takes the board out of its cloth and counts the marks before he answers.*

“Eleven.” ˣ

The guard asks what they're for. The boy looks back at his uncle. His uncle looks down at the bag he has been carrying since the last place they stopped for water.

“He saw eleven steps,” the uncle says. “He put one on for each.” ˣ

The boy lifts a finger and rests it on the last mark. He doesn't say that they have to be counted from the other end. The guard sees him do it and turns the board in his hands.

* * *

Mau comes down because the boy is willing to talk to him. He brings two stools. Jinshi said there was an uncle and forgot to say the boy had a good deal of pride about how long he could stand. They use one stool for the board.

The boy asks him whether he remembers how far it was.

Mau looks at the marks. The last two are cut so deep they're nearly through. He could tell the boy he doesn't remember. There are some things he'd be glad to let the boy keep to himself. The steps aren't one of them.

“I remember getting to ten,” he says. ᴹ

“There was one after that.” ˣ

“I think you're right.” ᴹ

The boy has something else to ask. He works on it for a while. Mau moves one of the stools out of the sun and sits. Eventually the boy puts his hand on the board.

“Can my sister have it?” ˣ

Mau looks at the uncle.

The uncle's bag is on the ground now. He puts a hand on it. The boy made the board for her. She has been learning to walk again since winter. There are eleven boards in their kitchen floor from the stove to the chair, and when she gets tired they have been counting the boards instead of telling her it isn't far.

The boy wants to know whether it's all right to give her something with Mau's steps on it. A man on the road told them they'd have to bring it here first.

Mau had thought they were bringing it to him. There was already a place in his head where he was going to have to find room for it.

“Does she know you made it?” ᴹ

The boy shakes his head.

“Then I think you should show her.” ᴹ

* * *

He can see the uncle getting ready to stand. He has both hands on his knees and the look of somebody deciding how to ask the other question before he has to take his things and go.

“Would it help if he told her you touched it?” the uncle asks. ˣ

The boy looks down at the board. Mau can see how carefully he has rounded the corners.

“You can tell her I counted it,” he says. “I don't think it'll help her walk.” ᴹ

The uncle lets out a breath. He looks almost disappointed, and then he looks as if something has been taken off his shoulders. The boy puts the cloth back around the board. He does it slowly enough that Mau can see there is a drawing on the back. It's a chair with a very large cushion.

“She has that chair already?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“I put it at the other end.” ˣ

He takes the board under his arm. It has started to get cold and he gets one sleeve up over his fingers. Jinshi shows the uncle which way he'll be able to get back to the road while there is still light. They go out the same way they came in.

* * *

Yuka finds Mau on the stool when she comes looking for a place to sit. There is another one next to it and she takes it without asking. They watch the corner of the road for a while.

“He brought eleven,” Mau says. ᴹ

She knows what he means. She puts her hands in her lap. Her fingers are cold from outside. He gets his own hand under them.

“I wish they'd all come with something that good,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka looks at the other stool, at the little dust-free rectangle the board left on it. She picks it up when they go in, and he carries the one he's been sitting on.
