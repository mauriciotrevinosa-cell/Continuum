# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 15 — The Rooms Beside Hers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S10E06
**Continuity state:** B+159 evening. Bocchi asks Fern to reopen the family coin conversation. Kita accompanies at Bocchi's request. House meeting to follow; no balances assumed, coins pledged or spent, or arrival.

*When: Bocchi comes to ask Fern about the empty rooms, she brings somebody who can ask her to say it again if she whispers.*

Kita takes a chair by the door. She moves it once, so that Bocchi can see her without having to turn her head. Then she sits down and waits.

Fern has a book open. She gets as far as the end of a line and puts her pen down.

“The rooms beside mine,” Bocchi says. “Do they still have that thing on the plan?” ᴮᵒ

Fern looks at her. She knows which rooms those are. She goes to the cabinet anyway and finds the plan. There is a folded corner where the family wing begins. She flattens it on the desk, and there it is. The small pencil mark. It has not been written over.

“Do you want me to change it?” ᶠᵉ

Bocchi shakes her head so hard that her hair falls forward. She puts it behind her ear. The same piece comes down again.

“I want to do the thing that comes after having the rooms,” she says. ᴮᵒ

Fern moves her hand off the plan. Bocchi gets one finger onto the narrow little rectangle next to the stairs. That was going to be the place where her parents could set their things down. She could never quite imagine the things. Her mother would bring something she'd want to put away and then something else she'd decide to leave out. Her father would find one thing he had packed by accident. Futari would know which door was hers and go into the other one.

She can imagine it now. She has been able to imagine it for days. This is one reason she has had such trouble getting the words out.

* * *

“What are you going to need from me?” she asks. ᴮᵒ

Fern leaves the plan out. She says they should start with the people who might already be here, the reports they've actually checked, and what it would mean to ask somebody to come. Then they can get the people who own the coins in a room and hear from them. There's a book for that. Fern doesn't reach for it yet.

“Would it have to be in front of everybody?” Bocchi asks. ᴮᵒ

“We should talk about how to do it together. You can tell me what you want to ask in here.” ᶠᵉ

Bocchi looks over at Kita. She has been hoping Kita will nod for a while now. Kita looks back. She doesn't nod.

“I want my mother and my father,” Bocchi says. “And my sister. I don't know how to ask about the dog.” ᴮᵒ

Fern puts a blank sheet beside the plan. She writes the three down as people to look for, and then she asks for the dog's name.

Bocchi tells her.

Fern writes that down too. The pen sounds the same.

* * *

Outside, someone is trying the new latch with an elbow and saying it opens the wrong way. Someone else tells them to go round and try it from the right side. Kita and Bocchi wait for the person to come out. It turns out to be Ryo, carrying nothing.

“I heard it was for when your hands were full,” she says. ᴿʸ

“They aren't,” Kita says. ᴷⁱ

“They could be.” ᴿʸ

She goes on down the hall. Kita lets the door settle. Then she asks Bocchi if she would like company on the way home.

Bocchi says yes. When they get to the stairs she asks if they can take the longer way round. It goes past the rooms beside hers. She thinks she would like to look at the doors again, while they are still empty and she has time to decide where her father will put the thing he brought by accident.

Kita takes her hand.

They go the longer way.
