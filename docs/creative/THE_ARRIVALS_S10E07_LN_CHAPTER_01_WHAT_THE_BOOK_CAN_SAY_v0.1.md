# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 16 — What the Book Can Say

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S10E07
**Continuity state:** B+160 morning. Fern reopens voluntary coin review. Jinshi retains Gaoshun reservation and checks a specific unverified report. Nell keeps the guest room while awaiting a reply. Perin and West start making their own plans. No collective coin balance or allocation.

*When: Fern brings the guest-yard book out to the table, Nell sets two coins on the cover and keeps her hand over them.*

“I've stayed more than a night,” she says. ˣ

Fern has been out to see the rooms every morning. Nell has asked the same question each time, with her hand on the door so she can close it before she hears the answer if she needs to. This is the first time she's brought the coins.

“Did somebody ask you for those?” Fern asks. ᶠᵉ

“No. The courier left already. I thought if I paid I could ask you to keep the bed while I went to the mill.” ˣ

Fern sets the chair beside her straight. Nell can reach it from where she's standing. She doesn't sit until Fern sits too.

The mill has the letter. That much came back with a driver Hollis knows. Her sister might be working past the bend for another four days, and there is a woman at the mill who puts things by for people. Fern tells her all of it. If Nell goes to the mill herself, she can leave word of where she'll be. If she wants to wait, they can leave the room in her name.

Nell moves her hand far enough to see the coins.

“I think I want to be here when she answers,” she says. “I want to know what it feels like to get a letter that isn't an order.” ˣ

She picks the coins up. Then she asks Fern how to find out whether somebody at the market would let her mend a coat for money, because the woman who brought West his has done both cuffs herself and it will take her all week if she goes on at that rate.

Fern gives her Hollis's hours at the desk. Through the door she can see West and Perin putting a borrowed stool back outside. Perin is talking about the goat again. West tells him to write the name of the man who bought it on a separate bit of paper so he can stop starting from the beginning.

Fern turns the guest book toward herself. She leaves the room in Nell's name.

* * *

When she brings out the book they used before the wedding, Mai remembers which page had the drawing of the kitchens.

It's the page under the one with the names. Somebody pressed so hard on the plan that there's a rectangle you can feel through the back. Mai puts her finger there. The page above it is mostly empty.

“We should leave the date where it is,” she says. “We can write a new one underneath.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Fern looks at the date and agrees. In that much time they built the kitchens, and got married, and had to bring somebody home from the quarry on a board. A name on this paper is allowed to have taken that long.

Jinshi takes out a slip of his own. His handwriting is smaller when he has left himself enough room to write.

“There was a caravan on the eastern road with a set of weights Maomao recognized. I asked Hollis if he could find out where they came from.” ᴶⁱ

Maomao moves her tea off the edge of the slip.

“I recognized the cuts on one weight,” she says. “The seller had bought the rest in a different town. We ought to find out whether anybody who used them came with them.” ᴹᵐ

Fern puts the slip with the inquiries. Jinshi's coin stays where Jinshi keeps it. He thanks her for reading the note before asking him whether he has decided.

* * *

Bocchi had thought the room would be full. There are only seven people there when she comes in, and two of them are carrying cups. Nobody is sitting in the chair beside hers.

She tells Fern what they already tried. The names in Hollis's inquiries. The message that went with the next music shipment, and the answer from a shop that had heard of a girl with a blue guitar. They went to the shop. The girl had red hair and was nineteen and wanted to sell her guitar because the tuning peg was broken. Ryo bought it. That was the only useful thing they found out that day.

“I don't think we missed them there,” Bocchi says. “I think I kept hoping it would be somewhere else, where I hadn't looked yet.” ᴮ

Fern sets the two checked reports on the table. They will keep their eyes open. A report of a family coming through is still worth reading, even if they decide to ask in another way. She asks Bocchi if she would like to tell the room how many people she wants to find. Bocchi says three. Then she says there is a dog too.

Kaneki asks whether any of the checked reports mentioned a dog. He waits while she tells him the story about the broken tuning peg again, in slightly more detail. Nobody has an answer to the dog question, but by the time she's done she can say it without trying to make it sound like a joke.

* * *

They talk about what would happen if somebody arrived and wanted to sleep in the guest rooms first. Rem says the bedding ought to be the same in both places. Maki asks who will sit with a person who says no to every question for the first half hour. Fern writes down that they can leave the water where it can be reached without asking.

Then she closes that part of the book. Nobody is going to put their coin in the middle of the table to prove they were listening.

“If you want to offer yours, come tell me what you've decided. I can write it down privately. A person can still change their mind before anything is given.” ᶠᵉ

Bocchi lifts her cup and finds that she finished it some time ago. On her way out she nearly leaves it there. She goes back for it. It is the only thing she can do just then, and there is a whole table of cups which still need taking down.

Kita takes two and follows her.
