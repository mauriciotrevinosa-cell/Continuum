# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 17 — The Small Compartment

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S10E07
**Continuity state:** B+160 evening. Bocchi's own unspent Stay coin confirmed. Ryo and Kita separately verify and voluntarily transfer their own unspent Stay coins to Bocchi. No invocation. Nijika's prior use for Seika remains.

*When: Bocchi opens the guitar case, the little compartment takes the same two tries it always did.*

Kita is putting the stand away. She hears the clasp and stops. The guitar is still on Bocchi's lap. What she's taken out is smaller than a pick and much heavier than anything she remembers putting there.

“I thought if I kept it near the guitar I'd know where it was,” Bocchi says. “Then I knew so well I didn't have to look.” ᴮ

Kita leans the stand against the wall. Bocchi rolls the coin across her palm. It stops where the little finger starts. There is nothing on it that will tell her which name she ought to say first.

“What do you want to do with yours?” Kita asks. ᴷⁱ

“Futari. If I can ask. She's little and she always goes to bed last if she's waiting for somebody.” ᴮ

“Would you ask her if you could ask all three?” ᴷⁱ

Bocchi closes her hand. She gets it open again.

“Yes. I'd ask my parents too.” ᴮ

Kita sits on the other end of the bench. She has had a thing in her pocket all afternoon and she is aware of it every time she sits down. She doesn't take it out yet. She asks Bocchi whether she wants to try this even if she has to wait for each of them to answer. It takes Bocchi a little time. When she says yes, Kita can tell she has imagined the wait.

* * *

Ryo comes in for a cable. She turns the light on and finds her own coin beside the cable under a folded receipt.

“I told Fern I'd think about it,” she says. “I thought about it.” ᴿʸ

She has also had it with her since the meeting. Bocchi watches her put it on the bench. Ryo takes one hand off it at a time, very slowly, like a person showing that there is nothing underneath.

“I have my reasons. They're mine. If you'd like to use it, you can.” ᴿʸ

Bocchi stares at the coin.

“What do I say?” ᴮ

“Tell me if you'd like to use it.” ᴿʸ

She says she would. Ryo asks if she'll help her get the cable out from behind the amp first. That takes both of them. By the time it's free, Bocchi can look at her and say thank you, and Ryo can tell her to keep her end up or they'll have to do it again.

* * *

Kita waits until they're back at the cabinet with Fern. Ryo has told Fern in her own words. Fern has asked whether she understands that a gift is hers to make only once, and Ryo has said she does. Now Kita takes out the coin she has kept since she stayed.

Bocchi takes a step backward. Kita follows her with her eyes and leaves the coin where Bocchi can see it.

“I asked Fern to give me some time earlier,” Kita says. “I was trying to work out whether I wanted this, or whether I just wanted you to stop having to wait.” ᴷⁱ

Bocchi can't think of anything useful to do with her hands.

“What did you decide?” ᴮ

“That I want to be there when you ask them. If you'd like me there. This is a separate thing.” ᴷⁱ

She puts the coin down. She looks at Fern and says she'd like to give it to Bocchi. Fern asks her the same question she asked Ryo. Kita answers it.

Bocchi wants to say yes in a way that will show she knows the size of what they're giving her. There doesn't seem to be a way. She says yes in the ordinary way and looks down at the book. Fern puts the dates in. There's a line between the two gifts. There is a place for Bocchi to say that she received each one, and she writes her name twice.

* * *

They can go in the morning, if she still wants to.

Before they leave Fern's desk, Bocchi puts a hand on the folded cloth.

“The day,” she says. “We talked about the day Seika-san left. Is it still that day for them?” ᴮ

Fern turns back to the older entry. Bocchi remembers the posters Seika dropped. Four faces. Her own face on a sheet someone had been carrying from station to station while she was learning to live here.

“Let's ask her together,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

* * *

DIO has a chair pulled close to the window in the protected part of the house. She moves a book off the second one when they come in. Mau is already there with Seika; he gets up before Bocchi reaches the door, then sits again when she points at the chair with more alarm than politeness.

“I was only going to move it,” he says. ᴹ

“You can stay in it.” ᴮ

He stays. Kita takes the place beside Bocchi.

Seika tells DIO which day she remembers. Three days of calling, searching and putting up the same four faces. The day she was taken from the club. The day Bocchi had asked them to keep in mind for her family, so she wouldn't spend more years imagining the search going on without an end.

“That day,” Bocchi says. “My parents. Futari. All of them.” ᴮ

“The same day,” DIO says. “The time you've spent here won't be added to their wait.” ᴰⁱ

Bocchi looks down at the cloth. There are things she had been practising saying to a little sister who might not be little anymore. Now she can't remember a single one.

“So they'll think it's been a few days,” she says. ᴮ

Seika's hand closes over her own elbow.

“They won't think it,” she says. “It has been.” ˢᵉ

Mau looks toward her. Seika meets his eyes, then turns back to Bocchi.

“They'll have been frightened,” she says. “Let them look at you.” ˢᵉ

Bocchi nods. It takes her a moment to ask the next thing.

“Will you come?” ᴮ

Seika says yes before she finishes. Bocchi looks at DIO, then at Mau.

“You too. If you're all right. I want you there. I don't know what I'm going to say.” ᴮ

“Then I'll be there while you find out,” Mau says. ᴹ

DIO asks Fern to put a chair near the wall for her. The arrival room is inside the protection; she can stay with them there. She doesn't take the coins or ask Bocchi to hand over the calling. Bocchi keeps them on the cloth in her lap until she is ready to stand.

* * *

In the corridor Kita asks whether she should take the long way home.

Bocchi says that she would like to go straight to her room this time. She would like to sit down somewhere she can put both feet on the floor. And she would like Kita there.

They go straight to her room.

When Bocchi takes the coins out again she puts them on a folded cloth, one at a time. Kita sits beside her and lets her count them as many times as she needs to.
