# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 19 — All the Things at Once

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S10E08
**Continuity state:** B+161 continuous morning. Bocchi uses Ryo's gifted coin for her mother's willing arrival. Futari and dog present. Kita's gifted coin unspent; father still absent at chapter end.

*When: Bocchi takes the second coin out, Futari asks if Mum will get the same chair.*

“She can have it,” Bocchi says. ᴮ

“It looks like you want me to have it.” ˣ

Bocchi looks down at her sister. Futari has already climbed into it and picked the dog up far enough to get his front paws over the edge. She is right. That is what the chair looks like.

Kita brings over one of the straight chairs from the desk. She sets it where there is room to walk around it. Bocchi picks up the cloth from the coins because she doesn't want to be standing here with only the one thing in her hands.

Fern asks her what she wants written. Bocchi says her mother's name. This one came from Ryo. She wants it to say that, too. She's looking at the place on the page where she wrote her name last night. She wishes she'd taken a little more care with the last letter.

* * *

The room goes quiet.

This time the first thing she sees is a hand.

The hand has a towel around it. Her mother is holding the other end of the towel where she's folded it, and she's still holding it when she looks down and realizes she isn't taking something out of the oven. She looks up quickly. Futari shouts before anybody else has time to make a sound.

Their mother goes straight to her. She gets a hand around the back of Futari's neck and draws her close and looks her over at the same time. She asks where the dog is while she can see the dog with her own eyes. Futari tells her everything at once. Most of it is about the chair.

Bocchi is still standing by the desk.

“Mum,” she says. ᴮ

Her mother turns her head. She has one hand on the girl's shoulder. She leaves it there and puts her free hand out toward Bocchi.

The towel falls on the floor.

Bocchi gets as far as her mother's hand. After that she can't quite tell where she is, except that her face is in a shoulder and there's an arm around her. Futari gets up so close that the three of them have to move together to let the dog out.

“You cut your hair,” her mother says. ˣ

Bocchi shakes her head.

“Did it grow, then?” ˣ

Bocchi is laughing by the time she manages to nod. Her mother brushes it back from her cheek, goes to do it again and puts her hand flat against the cheek instead.

* * *

They sit down. The towel is on the back of a chair. Nobody remembers putting it there.

“Is Dad all right?” Bocchi asks. ᴮ

Her mother says she thinks he is. He had gone around the block to look for Futari. She had stayed in case either of them came back. She says it like an answer to an ordinary question. Then she stops. Bocchi has heard her mother stop a sentence like that in the middle before. It is usually when the next thing she wants to say costs more than she thought it would.

“How long have you been here?” ˣ

Bocchi has been trying to decide how to answer that all morning. She tells her mother the truth as well as she knows it. Then she tells her about the band, and Kita, and the rooms she walked past last night. Her mother asks her to go back to the band. Not because the other things can wait. She can tell how much Bocchi wanted to say that part and she wants to hear all of it.

Bocchi looks at Kita. Then she looks down at the last coin.

“Will Dad know it's me who's asking?” ᴮ

Her mother puts her hand next to Bocchi's.

“Say it the way you just said it to me,” she says. ˣ

Bocchi picks it up. She puts it down again to wipe her face. When she reaches for it the second time her mother is still sitting there.
