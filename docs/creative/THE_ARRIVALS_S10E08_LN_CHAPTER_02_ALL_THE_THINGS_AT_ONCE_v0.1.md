# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 19 — All the Things at Once

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S10E08
**Continuity state:** B+161 continuous morning. Bocchi uses Ryo's gifted coin for her mother's willing arrival. Futari and dog present. Kita's gifted coin unspent; father still absent at chapter end. DIO, Mau, Seika and Fern remain at the protected reception. Mother recognizes Kita and Seika, sees Nijika/Ryo alive, and learns the source-world wait was only a few days while Bocchi lived years.

*When: Bocchi takes the second coin out, Futari asks if Mum will get the same chair.*

“She can have it,” Bocchi says. ᴮ

“It looks like you want me to have it.” ˣ

Bocchi looks down at her sister. Futari has already climbed into it and picked the dog up far enough to get his front paws over the edge. She is right. That is what the chair looks like.

Kita brings over one of the straight chairs from the desk. She sets it where there is room to walk around it. Bocchi picks up the cloth from the coins because she doesn't want to be standing here with only the one thing in her hands.

Fern asks her what she wants written. Bocchi says her mother's name. This one came from Ryo. She wants it to say that, too. She's looking at the place on the page where she wrote her name last night. She wishes she'd taken a little more care with the last letter.

* * *

The room goes quiet.

This time the first thing she sees is a hand.

The hand has a towel around it. Her mother is holding the other end of the towel where she's folded it, and she's still holding it when she looks down and realizes she isn't taking something out of the oven. She looks up quickly. Futari shouts before anybody else has time to make a sound.

Their mother goes straight to her. She gets a hand around the back of Futari's neck and draws her close and looks her over at the same time. She asks where the dog is while she can see the dog with her own eyes. Futari tells her everything at once. Most of it is about the chair.

Bocchi is still standing by the desk.

“Mum,” she says. ᴮ

Her mother turns her head. She has one hand on the girl's shoulder. She leaves it there and puts her free hand out toward Bocchi.

The towel falls on the floor.

Bocchi gets as far as her mother's hand. After that she can't quite tell where she is, except that her face is in a shoulder and there's an arm around her. Futari gets up so close that the three of them have to move together to let the dog out.

Her mother keeps saying her name. Once, she tries to lean back to see her face, then pulls her close again without looking.

Mau gets the coin cloth off the chair before the dog can drag it down. Fern puts the next name out of the way. DIO stays seated while the three of them hold one another in the middle of the room.

“You look older,” her mother says. ˣ

Bocchi tries to nod.

“I know it's you,” her mother says quickly. “I know. Let me look.” ˣ

She brushes the hair away from Bocchi's cheek, goes to do it again and puts her hand flat against the cheek instead. Bocchi presses into it. Her mother laughs through a breath which has almost become a sob.

* * *

They sit down. Her mother has the towel on her knee. Bocchi doesn't remember her picking it up.

“Is Dad all right?” Bocchi asks. ᴮ

Her mother says he is. He went out to look for Futari after she and the dog vanished. She stayed in case somebody came back to the house. Then she sees Kita beside the chair, and the sentence stops.

“Ikuyo?” ˣ

Kita comes close enough that Bocchi's mother can take her hand.

“I'm sorry we couldn't call.” ᴷⁱ

Her mother looks her over, all the way from her face to the shoes, as if an answer about a telephone is something she will deal with after she knows the girl is whole. When she looks up again, Seika is standing beside her.

“You're here.” ˣ

Seika nods. The gesture is stiff, the way it was when she was trying to keep Nijika from seeing her cry.

“All four of them,” she says. “I found them.” ˢᵉ

Bocchi's mother gets up. The towel drops off her knee this time. She catches Seika by both forearms, and Seika bends toward her before either woman seems to have decided how to do it.

“We couldn't get hold of you at the club,” her mother says. “They said—” ˣ

“I know.” ˢᵉ

“I didn't know where else to look.” ˣ

Seika pulls her closer. For a moment Bocchi can see only the two of them and the hand her mother still has stretched back toward her.

At the open door Nijika asks whether she can come in. Bocchi nods so hard that she loses sight of her.

Nijika comes first, Ryo just behind her. Bocchi's mother makes a little sound when she sees their faces. Ryo lets herself be pulled into the next embrace without making one joke about it.

Kita has Futari's hand. Bocchi takes the other one.

* * *

When her mother sits down again, she doesn't let go of Bocchi's hand.

“How long have you been here?” ˣ

Bocchi has been trying to decide how to answer that all morning. She tells her mother about the winters. About the first room and the move, the rooms after it, the people she has been learning to call family.

“Years,” she says at last. “For me.” ᴮ

Her mother's thumb stops against hers.

“It's only been a few days.” ˣ

Seika says it is the day she left. The same search, the same posters. DIO answers when Bocchi's mother turns toward her.

“The two worlds haven't kept the same time for you,” she says. “For her, those years have already passed here.” ᴰⁱ

“I wanted to,” Bocchi says. “Find a way. I tried. I just—” ᴮ

Her mother pulls her close enough that the explanation ends against her shoulder.

“You're here,” she says. ˣ

Bocchi stays there until her mother loosens her hold. Then she tells her about playing with the band again, and Kita, and the rooms she walked past last night. This time when she says girlfriend, she gets the whole word out.

Her mother looks at Kita, who hasn't let Futari wander out of the room, then back at Bocchi. She brushes the hair away from her cheek again.

“Tell me,” she says. ˣ

She can hear how much Bocchi wants to say that part. She wants all of it.

Bocchi looks at Kita. Then she looks down at the last coin.

“Will Dad know it's me who's asking?” ᴮ

Her mother puts her hand next to Bocchi's.

“Say it the way you just said it to me,” she says. ˣ

Bocchi picks it up. She puts it down again to wipe her face. When she reaches for it the second time her mother is still sitting there.
