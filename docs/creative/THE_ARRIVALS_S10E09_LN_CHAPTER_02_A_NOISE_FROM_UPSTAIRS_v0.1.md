# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 61
## Chapter 21 — A Noise From Upstairs

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 61
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S10E09
**Continuity state:** B+161 afternoon. Family visits reserved adjacent rooms and chooses to stay the night. Family already knows Kessoku and Seika; the music room is new, their relationship is not. Bocchi names Mau as her chosen brother after his presence at the arrival. No additional coin activity.

*When: Futari discovers the rooms have two keys, she asks which one she's supposed to lose.*

Her father tells her she can have one that doesn't open anything, if she wants to get that out of the way. She goes off to consider the offer. Her mother tries the little window and leaves it open about as far as it will go.

“Is this where we live?” she asks. ˣ

Bocchi had been hoping somebody else would ask it first, and now she finds that she wants the answer to belong to her.

“It's where there's room,” she says. “We can see what you want after tonight.” ᴮ

Her mother looks around. There are two chairs and one bed with a cover that does not quite reach the floor. She sits on the edge of it and the dog sits beside her. Then she tells Bocchi she'd like to sleep here tonight, if Futari does. Futari calls from the other room that she does.

Her father has hung up his coat and is still standing beside it. He asks if the music room is upstairs.

“Downstairs,” says Bocchi. ᴮ

He puts his hand against the wall anyway.

“That's strange,” he says. “There's a noise coming from up there.” ˣ

The noise comes again. It is a very small set of feet on a floor, and then the sound of something that did not have feet when it started to fall.

Futari comes around the doorway holding two towels.

“Nothing broke,” she says. ˣ

For the first time that day it sounds exactly like home.

* * *

Nijika is sitting on a step outside the music room. She stands when she sees them coming. Seika has come with them, still holding the little bag Bocchi's father forgot to take back. Ryo has brought the guitar with the broken peg. The peg turns now. She is waiting to see if anybody will notice.

“There you are,” Bocchi's father says to Nijika, and this time he can smile while he says it. ˣ

Futari goes straight to Ryo to ask about the guitar. Ryo lets her turn the peg once and then moves the instrument out of reach before the experiment becomes expensive.

Bocchi shows her parents where the four of them practise. Her father knows who plays with her; he doesn't know this room, or where Nijika sits, or which corner they keep moving the amp out of. She tells him those things slowly enough to point each one out. Her mother asks if they have somewhere people can listen. Bocchi says yes, but she'd rather have them come in through the little door today, because from there she can see them.

They come in through the little door.

She puts the metronome beside the amp. It has no battery. Nijika gives them four clicks with her sticks, and Bocchi comes in on the third by mistake. It used to be the sort of thing she'd want to go and be alone about. She looks up and sees her father looking straight at her hands. He has got a smile on his face she's seen before, from the other side of the door to her room. She asks for the four clicks again.

They start at the beginning.

* * *

Mau has brought bread. He makes it as far as the bench outside the music room before he finds out there is music in it. He sits with the bread on his knee until somebody comes out.

It's Bocchi. Her mother is behind her, with a hand on the door.

“I was going to ask if you'd come by later,” Bocchi says. ᴮ

He gets up and holds out the bread.

“I can come back later.” ᴹ

She takes it. Then she takes hold of his sleeve before he can go.

“I told them your name earlier,” she says. “I didn't tell them the rest.” ᴮ

Her mother comes a little farther out. Mau has enough time to decide what he should say about himself. In that time Bocchi gets there first.

“This is my brother here,” she says. ᴮ

Mau puts a hand on the back of the bench.

Her mother looks at him. Then she looks at the bread. She asks whether he knows where to find enough butter for that. He tells her he does.

They are halfway down the passage when the dog gets past Futari and follows. Mau turns to see what has caught up to him. Bocchi gets there first. She takes the lead, then looks up at her mother and at Mau and asks if they can eat upstairs with the window open.

Everybody has somewhere to go, now. They go upstairs.
