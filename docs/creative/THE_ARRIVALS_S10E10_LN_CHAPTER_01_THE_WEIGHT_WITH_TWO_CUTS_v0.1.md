# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 1 — The Weight With Two Cuts

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S10E10
**Continuity state:** B+162 morning. Hollis's inquiry into the familiar weights returns. No verified Gaoshun location. Jinshi elects to request Gaoshun with his own reserved Stay coin. No invocation yet.

*When: Hollis puts the weight on the table, it makes less noise than Jinshi has been expecting.*

It's smaller than the one Maomao used to keep for remedies whose names she did not want shouted down a hall. She turns it over. There are two cuts on the underside. She gets a fingernail into the larger one and lifts her hand when the weight goes with it.

“That's the mark,” she says. “They used to check the small ones against a piece kept by the storeroom.” ᴹᵐ

“Who had it?” Jinshi asks. ᴶⁱ

Hollis takes out a scrap of paper. The man who sold it to the caravan had been keeping his stall opposite a roof that arrived in the middle of a field. A stretch of tiled roof, and a little way underneath it a line of shelves. Some of the boxes fell out when the shelves came down. He bought the weights from the person who cleared the rubble.

“Anybody in the building?” Jinshi asks. ᴶⁱ

“Nobody he saw. Some people came through that week. I asked for names. He's going to send me the ones he can get.” ᴴᵒ

Maomao puts the weight back on its flat side. She has been doing her best to remember the storeroom and can still only see the table nearest the door. She had never made a point of looking farther in when she got what she needed.

Jinshi asks Hollis what he owes for the inquiry. Hollis tells him what the weight cost. Jinshi pays him for the weight and the trouble. Neither of them has the heart to argue over the difference for very long.

* * *

The slip stays with them after Hollis leaves. The words do not change. Jinshi reads them again anyway.

“I thought if we found a place, then we could find out where he would go from it,” he says. ᴶⁱ

“Can you see this place?” Maomao asks. ᴹᵐ

“Some shelves. And some boxes.” ᴶⁱ

She nods. She is glad he says the things they know about. There are a great many things he could have put in the field.

He asks if she would keep looking. She says yes. She would keep looking for who came through because they might need somebody to recognize the place where they came from. She isn't sure she would keep doing it instead of something she had been waiting to do for a long time.

He takes the little weight and turns it onto its side.

“I don't want him to arrive thinking I need somebody to hold the room together.” ᴶⁱ

“What do you want him to arrive thinking?” ᴹᵐ

Jinshi considers that. There had been a great many mornings when he could have told somebody else what he wanted, and Gaoshun had been the one who waited until he told the truth. He doesn't know how to put that in an invitation. The weight tips over under his hand.

“That I wanted to see him,” he says. ᴶⁱ

Maomao nudges the paper toward him. It has a blank side. He takes out his pen.

* * *

Fern listens to both of them. She writes down that the source of the marks has been checked and the trail to a person is still open. She asks whether Jinshi wants to put a date on the next inquiry.

“I do,” he says. “And I want to ask him today.” ᴶⁱ

He brings the coin out. It is the first time this morning that he has put something on the desk without trying to decide which side of it he wants to see.

Fern gets out the sheet with the reservation on it. She asks if he would like a private room again, the way Bocchi did. He asks for the little office by the yard. Gaoshun would want to know where the door went before he sat down.

Maomao asks if Jinshi wants her there. He says yes so quickly that she puts her pen away without checking whether he's finished with it.

“Bring the weight?” she asks. ᴹᵐ

He looks back at it.

“Yes,” he says. “I want to know what he thinks it is.” ᴶⁱ
