# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 2 — No One at the Door

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S10E10
**Continuity state:** B+162 midday. Gaoshun willingly arrives on Jinshi's reserved coin, spent once. He recognizes Jinshi and Maomao. Source status and wider political information not asserted beyond their shared history. Voluntary guest.

*When: Jinshi opens his hand, the first thing Gaoshun does is look past him at the door.*

There is no one there. The door is half open and it goes into a yard. Jinshi sees Taihei cross it with a broom without looking in. Gaoshun watches until he gets to the other side, then turns back to Jinshi.

He inclines his head.

“Is there somewhere we need to be?” ᴳᵃ

Jinshi was going to tell him something about the yard. Instead he finds himself looking at the knot on Gaoshun's sleeve. He remembers seeing exactly that knot three times in one morning when he kept sending Gaoshun out to ask about something he could have gone to see for himself.

“Here,” he says. ᴶⁱ

Gaoshun waits. Jinshi has given him answers like that before.

“I asked you to come here,” Jinshi says. “I wanted to see you.” ᴶⁱ

Gaoshun looks at him for quite a long time. Then he says, very simply, that he wanted to see him too.

* * *

Maomao pours tea. Gaoshun thanks her in the same tone he'd use if she had been the one to arrange a meeting and wished to leave before anyone found out. She puts the cup in front of him and sits down. He adjusts to this.

“Can they see you from the yard?” he asks Jinshi. ᴳᵃ

Jinshi looks out. Taihei has come back to collect a bucket. Jinshi lifts his cup in greeting. Taihei lifts a hand and goes on with the bucket.

“Yes,” he says. ᴶⁱ

Gaoshun has a second look at the door. Then he takes up his own cup. He keeps it on the table after he drinks from it, rather than returning it precisely to the ring it left.

Maomao puts the weight beside it. He turns it over. His thumb goes into the cut the way her nail did.

“It's from a storeroom.” ᴳᵃ

“Do you know which one?” ᴶⁱ

“There are six with that mark. Possibly seven. Where did you get it?” ᴳᵃ

Jinshi tells him about the roof in the field. Gaoshun makes him say which part of the roof the witnesses saw and which part they said they saw because someone else told them about it. Maomao takes out the scrap from Hollis. When the three of them have finished, they know rather less about where the weight was kept. They know considerably more about what to ask on the next trip.

Gaoshun folds the scrap and starts to put it away. He stops before it reaches his sleeve.

“Do you want me to keep this?” ᴳᵃ

Jinshi looks at his hand. He had brought Gaoshun a cup of tea and put something in front of him to do before he had finished it.

“Leave it here,” he says. “I can keep it for now.” ᴶⁱ

Gaoshun sets it down. He finishes the tea.

* * *

They go out together. There is no one keeping people back from the yard. Maomao tells Gaoshun where the guest rooms are, then asks whether he'd like to see the kitchen first. He says he would like to see both. He has some questions about where he is, and a good many more about how the two of them are, and it seems sensible to ask some of them over food.

Jinshi takes the weight with him. At the far side of the yard Gaoshun asks if he wants help carrying it.

“It's a weight,” Jinshi says. ᴶⁱ

“I can see that.” ᴳᵃ

He almost offers it to him anyway. Then he realizes what Gaoshun has done with his face and laughs, so suddenly that somebody sitting in the sun looks around.

Gaoshun stands beside him while he finishes. There's room to walk around both of them.
