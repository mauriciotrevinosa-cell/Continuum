# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 3 — Three Without Raisins

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S10E11
**Continuity state:** B+162 late afternoon. Gaoshun eats with Jinshi and Maomao. Rem and Umaru keep their own evening arrangement. Subaru lead only, brought by courier; identity and arrival not yet verified.

*When: Umaru gets to the kitchen, Rem has drawn a line in the flour with one finger.*

“Those are mine,” Umaru says. ᵁ

Rem looks down at the four little buns on the side Umaru is pointing at.

“One of them has raisins.” ᴿᵉ

“That's how I'll know if anybody takes it.” ᵁ

Rem leans a little closer. She can see the corner of Umaru's mouth go up. She pushes the one with the raisins across the line, sets the three others on a clean cloth and folds the cloth over them.

“You wanted to go when I'm done?” ᴿᵉ

“If you still want to.” ᵁ

“I kept three without raisins.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks at the little parcel. One of these days she is going to get used to being answered like that. She doesn't know when and it hasn't happened yet. She reaches around the board and wipes a bit of flour off Rem's wrist with the side of her finger.

“I'll go set the cups out.” ᵁ

Rem gives her the parcel. She has enough left for the people who are having supper in the yard. She takes one of the last ones out to the table where Jinshi is sitting.

* * *

Gaoshun is halfway through asking how the kitchens decide what to put aside for late arrivals. Maomao points out that he has been asking the person sitting farthest from the kitchens. Gaoshun thanks Rem for the bun and starts the question over.

Rem says that they keep something out until the light goes, and then anybody who comes can ask. People have different hours now. She used to think there was an hour after which nobody ought to want food, and then she met Gojo.

Jinshi asks what happens when Gaoshun doesn't want food. Gaoshun takes another bun.

“I did have a question about my room,” Gaoshun says. ᴳᵃ

Jinshi sits up. He has been thinking all afternoon that the room might be on the wrong side of the building. There is a room nearer his, but it overlooks the yard and people go in and out until very late. He begins to explain.

“I like the one you showed me,” Gaoshun says. “I wanted to know if I can open the shutters myself. Somebody had tied them back when I went up.” ᴳᵃ

“You can do what you like with them,” Jinshi says. ᴶⁱ

Maomao puts down the bun she'd been pulling apart.

“You should check that one in the morning,” she says. “He tried to lend me his room for afternoon naps. The birds start before the light comes up, and one of them knows four notes, all of them wrong.” ᴹᵐ

“You could have told me it was the birds,” Jinshi says. ᴶⁱ

“I thought you'd think I was making fun of your singing.” ᴹᵐ

Gaoshun looks down at his bun until he can swallow. He asks where they bought the flour. Rem tells him a little about the people who come from across the river, and that if he means to get his own loaf he'll want to arrive before eight. Jinshi had been about to offer to get it. He breaks a piece off his bun instead.

“I'll come down then,” Gaoshun says. He looks at Jinshi. “Are you usually up?” ᴳᵃ

Jinshi says he is. Gaoshun asks if he'd like to meet him there. Jinshi looks at Maomao, who has already started on the outside of the bun so she can save the middle. She says there is room for her at eight if somebody wants to make tea.

Rem leaves them there. She takes the other bowl in with her and sets it by the window. She can see Umaru and the two cups down the path. One cup is standing on its handle for a reason she will probably be told when she gets there.

* * *

Someone says her name from the doorway. It's Fern. She has the register under one arm and a short letter under the other. Rem puts down the bowl. Fern asks if she can come in.

The letter came with a courier from the way station. Someone had been asking for two sisters. He gave their names, and he asked whether there was a place on this road where people woke up and found they'd come from somewhere else.

Rem reads that part again. She hasn't asked what else the letter says yet. Fern waits until she gets to it.

He had given his own name for the courier to carry. It's written at the bottom, with the family name first.

“Did the courier see him?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“She says she spoke to him. He's waiting at the station. I asked her to stop there before she sent anyone this way.” ᶠᵉ

Rem puts the letter back on the counter.

“Tell Ram as well,” she says. ᴿᵉ

“I will.” ᶠᵉ

Fern stays a moment. She looks down the path and asks if Rem wants her to come back for an answer later.

“Tomorrow,” Rem says. “I want to think about it first.” ᴿᵉ

Fern takes the register and leaves the letter. Rem folds it once and tucks it where she can get it again. The parcel of buns is already outside. Through the window she can see Umaru trying to make the second cup stand the same way as the first.

She goes down the path to ask her why.
