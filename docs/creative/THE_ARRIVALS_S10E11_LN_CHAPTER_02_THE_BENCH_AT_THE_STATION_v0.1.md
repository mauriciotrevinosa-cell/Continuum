# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 4 — The Bench at the Station

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S10E11
**Continuity state:** B+163 morning. Rem and Ram verify Subaru at the way station. He arrived alone by convergence, in the early royal selection period following Julius's duel, prior to later reconciliation and contracts. No coin or Return by Death event. Guest offer contingent on his choice and house terms.

*When: Rem sees the bench outside the way station, she recognizes the person sleeping on it by the way one heel hangs over the side.*

Ram stops at the edge of the shade. There is a coat over him that belongs to the station. The station keeper comes out to say he has been there since before her shift started and asked her to wake him at sunrise. She woke him. He thanked her, walked to the water barrel and came back to the bench.

“Has he eaten?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“He said he had someone coming.” ˣ

Ram takes the stool beside the door. She drags it across the planks, deliberately. Subaru opens his eyes at once. The hand outside the coat comes up across his face. Rem sees a bruise under the cuff and waits for him to lower it.

“Ram?” he says. ˢᵇ

“The very one.” ᴿᵃ

He sits so fast the coat slides off. Rem catches it. The expression on his face when he sees her is something she had been afraid she would have to remember all by herself.

“Rem,” he says. ˢᵇ

She sets the coat back on the bench.

“You can sit. We've come to talk.” ᴿᵉ

He stands anyway. He takes two steps toward her and stops on his own. His hands are up, and he looks down at them as if they belong to somebody who has come to find out what the trouble is. He puts one of them behind his neck.

“You have no idea how good it is to see you.” ˢᵇ

“I think I do,” says Rem. ᴿᵉ

* * *

He tells them where he woke up. A road he didn't know, with a cart on it and somebody wanting him out from under the wheels. He thought he had been taken out of the capital. Then he saw the road markers and found he couldn't read them. He asked people who did know the road until one had heard of two sisters who might answer to their names.

Ram asks what he was doing immediately before that. He says he was resting. He doesn't look at either of them when he says it. Rem asks why his wrist hurts.

“Julius. We had a disagreement.” ˢᵇ

“Did Julius have a disagreement?” Ram asks. ᴿᵃ

He makes a short, embarrassed sound. She knows the answer now. She doesn't need him to put it any better than that.

“Where is Emilia?” he asks. ˢᵇ

They tell him what they know. They know who he means, and they haven't seen her here. There are roads people are still trying to map. There are people who may have come through somewhere nobody has heard from yet. Ram won't say she knows where Emilia is. Subaru asks her again in a different way and gets the same answer.

* * *

Rem tells him they can ask for a room at the mini Inn. If he wants it, he will have a place to sleep while he works out what to do. He asks how long she has been working there. She says she lives there.

He looks straight at her, startled.

“The two of you?” ˢᵇ

“With a good many others,” she says. ᴿᵉ

He wants to ask who. She can see it gathering. He asks instead whether they have anybody who can look at a wrist. He says it so quietly that Ram has to turn a little to hear him. Rem tells him yes. She leaves the coat on the bench for the next person and asks whether he can walk.

“I've been walking for days,” he says. ˢᵇ

“Then tell me if you want to stop.” ᴿᵉ

He looks down at the bench. He sits for another minute, at the very end where his heel doesn't hang off. They sit with him.
