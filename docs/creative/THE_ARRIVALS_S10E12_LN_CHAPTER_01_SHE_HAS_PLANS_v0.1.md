# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 5 — She Has Plans

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S10E12
**Continuity state:** B+163 late morning. Subaru accepts temporary lodging and voluntary exam. Ram and Rem do not promise to leave. Rem chooses to hear him in the guest yard. Umaru already has plans with Rem.

*When: Subaru asks if Ram can put his room beside Rem's, Ram tells him they're in different buildings.*

He looks out of the mini Inn window. From here he can see a shed, a walkway and a roof that looks as if it belongs to a house. There is no corridor he can picture walking down until he finds the right door.

“How far?” ˢᵇ

“She can tell you herself.” ᴿᵃ

Shoko gets the rest of the wrap around his wrist. He has held still for her even while looking out the window. She tells him what she needs him to do if the fingers go numb, and asks him to say it back. He says it back word for word. Then he asks if he can make a fist.

“Tomorrow,” she says. ˢʰ

He rests the hand on his knee.

Ram says she has to go and tells him where he can find someone if he needs anything. Subaru says her name once as she gets to the door. She stops. He looks at the wrist and tries to move it.

“Thanks for coming,” he says. ˢᵇ

“You're welcome.” ᴿᵃ

He looks up. He had been expecting her to make fun of him. She has gone before he thinks of something to say about that.

He goes down by himself. Jinshi has shown him the route to the front desk, and on the way he takes the wrong turning and finds a table with a loaf on it. Somebody has cut one slice out of the middle and left both ends leaning together.

“There's a knife if you want some,” a woman at the table says. She has a pencil behind one ear and two pieces of a music stand laid out in front of her. “You might want somebody else to cut it with that hand.” ˢᵉ

“I was looking for Ram.” ˢᵇ

“You've just missed her. She had a thing she wanted to do before lunch.” ˢᵉ

He almost asks what. The woman moves a piece of the stand clear of the bread. It occurs to him that Ram might have said what she wanted to do to the people she'd arranged to do it with. He can't imagine how many of those people there might be.

“Would you cut me a piece?” he asks. “If that's all right.” ˢᵇ

“Sit down a second.” ˢᵉ

By the time he gets up he's had two pieces. The woman's name is Seika. She has told him that if he's looking for a map he'll have better luck asking at the desk than knocking on doors. He thinks of offering to put the stand together for her. When he takes a proper look there are two little holes that need drilling and nothing he could get a good grip on. He takes his own plate as far as the basin instead.

* * *

Rem is in the yard with Umaru. Umaru has a basket between her feet. She is holding a cup out and asking if the crack goes through. Rem puts water in the cup so they can both see. It comes out against Umaru's thumb.

“We can use mine,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

Subaru stops in the doorway. He has seen Rem at a table with a tray. He has seen her listen to a person speak and wait for her turn. She's changed out of the dress she wore to the station. She is sitting on the ground in a loose shirt and a skirt with a pocket big enough to swallow a whole spoon, and she takes the cup from the other girl's hand before the water can get onto her sleeve.

“Rem,” he calls. “Can I borrow you?” ˢᵇ

Both of them look up.

“For what?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

Subaru keeps one hand on the frame.

“I need to know how to get back to the road. We can look at a map or something.” ˢᵇ

“There is one at the desk. I'll show it to you after lunch.” ᴿᵉ

He opens his mouth. She can see him catch himself and say all right. She is about to thank him for waiting when he asks whether whatever she has to do first can wait instead.

Umaru pours the rest of the water out into the grass.

“She has plans,” she says. ᵁ

Rem gets up with the cup. She keeps hold of it.

“After lunch,” she says again. “I have time to talk to you then.” ᴿᵉ

He lets go of the frame. He tells her he'll be at the desk. It is a perfectly ordinary thing to tell her, and she gives him a perfectly ordinary nod.

* * *

Mau is coming down the path with a parcel for the mini Inn when Umaru calls him over and holds out the cup.

“Do you think Mai could fix this?” ᵁ

He takes it and tips it to see the crack.

“She'll ask you how it got that way.” ᴹ

“Then ask her when I can come tell her.” ᵁ

He puts the cup under his arm. The new guest is watching from beyond the desk. Mau has seen Rem see him. She said after lunch and set the basket back upright with her foot. He doesn't ask her if she needs somebody to be there.

He asks if she wants him to take anything else to Mai.

“That'll do,” she says. ᴿᵉ

Umaru picks up the basket. Rem takes the handle from the other side, and they leave the yard together.
