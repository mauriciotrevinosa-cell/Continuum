# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 6 — Ask Me What I Want

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S10E12
**Continuity state:** B+163 afternoon. Rem chooses to meet Subaru. His declaration is used as pressure; she names it and refuses. Rem's relationships and future stay hers. No imposed exile or accepted journey.

*When: Rem takes the map down from the peg, Subaru gets his hand on one end before she can set it flat.*

“Which way did I come from?” ˢᵇ

She points to the station, then the road he described. He follows it to the edge of the sheet.

“What's over there?” ˢᵇ

“We haven't been that far on that road.” ᴿᵉ

“Then we ought to go.” ˢᵇ

Rem lets go of her end of the map. It starts to curl, then stops against the cup she set down earlier.

“Who do you mean by we?” ᴿᵉ

He looks up. He has been sitting with his wrist on the table exactly the way Shoko asked him to. He moves it now.

“You can come with me. You know how to get that far. Once we're there we can figure out the rest.” ˢᵇ

“I can show you the way to the station. I'm staying here.” ᴿᵉ

He puts both hands on the map. She thinks he might ask her what she wants to stay for. Instead he tells her how much trouble they might be in at home. He says her sister may not know how badly things were going. He says he'd have come for her.

“I did come for you this morning,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

He stops. The silence lasts long enough for her to think that might have reached him.

“I love you, Rem,” he says. “You know that, right? I wouldn't ask you to do this if I didn't.” ˢᵇ

Her first thought is that she would once have liked to hear those first four words, if he'd stopped there. She takes a moment to let herself have that thought. She can have it without letting him finish the sentence for her.

“You just told me what I was going to do,” she says. ᴿᵉ

“I'm asking now.” ˢᵇ

“You haven't asked me what I want.” ᴿᵉ

He looks at her. His mouth moves. She can see that he would like to say he already knows.

* * *

Rem sits down on the other side of the map. Her cup has gone cold. She drinks from it anyway.

She tells him about the first time Mau asked her if she wanted something and waited through the whole silence while she worked out whether she could say yes. She tells him she has a room where she can hear Ram come home. She tells him about Umaru, who left a raisin bun on a plate all afternoon because nobody was allowed to eat the wrong one. She gets to the part where she and Umaru are going to take a walk after supper and finds she can say that too.

Subaru starts to tell her he could make her happy at home.

“I just told you I'm happy,” she says. ᴿᵉ

He goes quiet.

“You're my friend. I came because I heard your name and wanted to see you. If you try to put a leash on that, you're an ass.” ᴿᵉ

She takes another drink. Her hands are shaking a little now. She can see it in the water. She waits until she can put the cup down where she means to put it.

“You can ask me for help. You can tell me you're scared. You can say the rest again when you know what you mean by it. I'm staying.” ᴿᵉ

* * *

He takes his hand off the map.

“Can I keep looking at this?” he asks. ˢᵇ

“Of course.” ᴿᵉ

She goes to the door. She can hear him flatten the curl in the paper behind her. It springs up after he takes his hand away. He'll have to find his own cup to put on that corner.

Outside she stops. She hadn't made plans for the minute after saying all of that. Then she remembers she'd told Umaru after supper, and it isn't supper yet. There is time to go tell Ram what happened. She takes the path toward her sister's door.
