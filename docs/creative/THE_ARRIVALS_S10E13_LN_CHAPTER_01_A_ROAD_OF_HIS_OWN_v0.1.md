# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 7 — A Road of His Own

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S10E13
**Continuity state:** B+163 late afternoon. With Rem's permission Ram addresses the incident. Jinshi and Rimuru enforce guest terms. Subaru can remain while respecting boundaries and requests a route for himself. No closure of his personal arc.

*When: Ram comes to the guest yard, she asks Subaru to pull up his own chair.*

He had been hoping he might have the map to himself for another minute. He puts the cup on the far corner and gets the chair.

Ram sits down opposite. Jinshi has brought the little register. Rimuru comes last and shuts the yard door because the hinge keeps lifting in the wind.

“Did she tell everybody?” Subaru asks. ˢᵇ

“She told me. She asked me to be here if you wanted to discuss leaving, because you tried to speak for me.” ᴿᵃ

He looks at the map. He knows which bit of the sheet he had his hands on when he said it. He looks there before he looks at her.

“I thought you'd want to come too.” ˢᵇ

“I don't. And if you'd spent half as long listening to Rem as you did telling her why she should go, you'd have heard her say the same.” ᴿᵃ

He opens his mouth.

“I know you were scared,” Ram says. “I watched you get off that bench. Then you came here and told her you loved her because you thought she couldn't refuse you after that.” ᴿᵃ

“I didn't think it would hurt her.” ˢᵇ

Ram holds his gaze. He reaches for the cup and leaves it where it is.

“I thought it would work.” ˢᵇ

There is a considerable silence. She has already put the right amount of tea in her cup. While he thinks about that, she drinks it.

* * *

Subaru asks whether he is still allowed to stay. He tries to put the question lightly. It's a bad fit.

“You have the room until the next review,” Jinshi says. “If Rem has declined to go with you, the matter is settled. You can request directions and supplies through the desk.” ᴶⁱ

Subaru asks whether that is what the house does with everybody who has come through.

“If they ask for directions, yes.” ᴶⁱ

He rubs the edge of the bandage with his other thumb. He has spent part of the day finding out how many of the people here know one another's names. Almost nobody knows his. That's one of the reasons he'd thought Rem would come. He could have said it that way this afternoon, if he'd known it was what he meant.

“I don't know where I'd go,” he says. ˢᵇ

Rimuru pulls the map a little closer. He asks Subaru to point to the first place he remembers seeing. Subaru finds it after a while. He had walked all the way around the station before he knew what it was, so he can draw the turn. Jinshi makes room for the paper by turning the register sideways.

Ram watches him start the line twice. She can see the exact moment he finds a place he remembers properly and stops drawing what he thinks ought to be there.

“I'd like to find out whether somebody saw anyone else on that road,” he says. ˢᵇ

“Then put down which day it was,” Jinshi says. ᴶⁱ

Subaru asks for a second piece of paper. Jinshi gives it to him.

* * *

As Ram gets up, Subaru starts to ask her to tell Rem something. He gets as far as her name.

Ram waits.

“Can you tell me when she's usually at the kitchen?” he asks instead. ˢᵇ

“Leave her be for now,” Ram says. “I mean it, Subaru. Let her decide when she wants to see you.” ᴿᵃ

He looks down at the line he drew. He says he can do that.

She has her hand on the door when he asks whether she wants the tea warmed. She turns back. There is enough in her cup for a mouthful. He has reached for the pot with the hand Shoko told him to rest.

“I do,” says Ram. “Use the other hand.” ᴿᵃ

He changes hands. When he pours, she doesn't give him the cup to hold. She leaves it on the table where he can see how much room there is.

* * *

Seika is waiting by the bend with the stand under one arm. Ram had said she'd help carry the rest of it over before supper. Seika has done up the loose screw and has the other pieces in a cloth bag. She holds the bag out, then takes another look at Ram's face and keeps it.

“Do you want me to walk back with you?” ˢᵉ

Ram had meant to go straight to Rem's door. From here she can see the yard where Rem and Umaru are putting the good cups out. Rem has taken her hair down and is holding a spoon up to the light because Umaru has told her there's something on it.

“Yes,” Ram says. “The long way.” ᴿᵃ

Seika puts the stand up on her shoulder. At the next fork she turns the way that goes past the tree, as if that's where she meant to go all along. Ram reaches for the bag this time. Seika lets her take it, and keeps her free hand close enough that Ram can take that too.
