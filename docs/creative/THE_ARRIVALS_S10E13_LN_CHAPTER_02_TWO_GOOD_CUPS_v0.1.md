# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 8 — Two Good Cups

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S10E13
**Continuity state:** B+163 evening. Rem tells Umaru as much as she chooses and keeps their plans. No new relationship label, obligation, or decision about Subaru's future.

*When: Umaru gets to the table, Rem has found the good cups.*

“Those were put away,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“That's how I knew where they were.” ᴿᵉ

She hasn't poured anything yet. There are three buns on the cloth. Umaru counts them before she sits down. She takes the one nearest the edge and splits it, puts half on the other plate and waits for Rem to do whatever she was going to do with the kettle.

Rem pours the tea. She pours it into both cups. Then she sets the kettle down beside her foot.

“I need to tell you something about today,” she says. “Would you like to hear it now, or after the first one?” ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks down at the bun.

“Now. I think.” ᵁ

So Rem tells her. She tells her she used to want to hear something like what Subaru said, and that the words hurt when she finally did. She says that it would have been easier if she didn't care about him. She would have been able to stand up and leave a lot sooner.

Umaru takes a sip from a cup she has forgotten to let cool. It takes a moment before she can speak.

“Did you think you wanted to go?” she asks. ᵁ

“I thought about how I'd have answered before. I thought he was the only person who saw anything in me I hadn't done for him yet. I would have gone with him. I might even have called that being happy.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru runs a thumb along the handle. She has taken hold of it in both hands and is beginning to see how warm it is.

“Do you remember when Mau left that room empty?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“The one with the little window? I tried to annex it.” ᵁ

“Three times.” ᴿᵉ

“It was a good window.” ᵁ

Rem smiles. It goes away slowly enough that she can feel the difference.

“I spent months waiting for him to ask me to move in. I'd made up an answer for when he did. Then one day I asked him myself, and I found out he'd been waiting to see if I wanted it.” ᴿᵉ

She looks down at the cup. There's a little bit of tea in the saucer now. The kettle had been full when she poured and she can remember spilling it. For some reason that makes it easier to keep talking.

“I'm angry,” she says. “I still want him to have somewhere to go. I hate that he said it like he thought it would make me forget I already have somewhere. I hate that for a second I was glad to hear him say it.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru starts to speak, then sets the cup down so she can turn all the way around.

“Did you want me here even before I said I'd bring anything?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“I asked you first,” Umaru says. “I didn't even know there were going to be buns. You told me that two days later.” ᵁ

Rem makes a sound she had been hoping would turn out to be a laugh. Umaru moves her plate out of the way and puts her arms around her. Rem gets hold of the back of her sleeve. She doesn't say anything else until the grip stops hurting her fingers.

“You're getting crumbs in my hair,” Umaru says eventually. ᵁ

Rem draws back just far enough to see. There are no crumbs in Umaru's hair. There's one on her own sleeve, and Umaru picks it off and eats it in front of her.

“I would've missed you,” she says. ᵁ

Rem pulls her chair in. She goes as far as she can without putting a foot on Umaru's.

“I was going to be right here,” she says. “I had plans.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru laughs, though it takes a little while to get there. She puts the cup down and picks up her bun. Rem eats her own half. The first thing either of them has to say after that is that one of these has raisins after all.

* * *

They take the cups back later. They walk with them through the yard where the clay jar stands and catch Taihei coming out for water. He asks if the cups have to go anywhere in particular. Umaru tells him they are going to wash them first.

“I can wash them,” he says. ᵀᵃ

Rem looks at Umaru.

“I said we would,” Umaru says. ᵁ

He tells them the drying cloth is over the handle of the cupboard. He goes to get his water. At the door he remembers to ask if they want the last bun left out, and both of them say yes. It is the first time all day Rem has answered somebody at exactly the same moment as somebody else, and she likes the sound of it.

She finds the cloth. Umaru sets the first cup in the basin with her thumb over the handle. They do the dishes side by side, then go for the walk they'd planned.
