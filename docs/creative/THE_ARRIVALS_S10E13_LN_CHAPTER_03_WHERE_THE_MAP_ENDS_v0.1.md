# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 9 — Where the Map Ends

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S10E13
**Continuity state:** B+164 morning to afternoon. Subaru follows his own map from the Inn to the station. He leaves Rem space and writes a request the reed cutter can answer at his convenience. No new sighting or lead to Emilia. Subaru returns to his temporary lodging.

*When: Subaru gets to the desk in the morning, his copy of the map is on top of a stack of work orders with a clean cup holding it down.*

Gaoshun takes the cup off it when Subaru asks for the map. He'd been waiting to see which of the two places at the table would catch the sun first. Jinshi had told him it was the one closest to the window. Maomao had bet him a cup of tea that the sun would get all the way across the table before Jinshi found out.

“Would you like the cup too?” Gaoshun asks. ᴳᵃ

Subaru has spent half the night writing down questions. At the top of the sheet is Rem's name. He folds the top over so it's under his thumb.

“I should probably find out where I'm going first.” ˢᵇ

Gaoshun puts the cup down. “You can have tea while you do that.” ᴳᵃ

* * *

Hollis comes in from the yard carrying two empty baskets. The handles have caught together, and he sets one down to get them apart. Subaru thinks about asking when Rem will be at breakfast. He knows where the kitchen is. She might come through it any minute.

He moves over until he can't see the kitchen door.

“I came through here,” he tells Hollis. He turns the map to show the little bend he drew with Ram. “I want to find out if anyone saw somebody else there before I did. Would you tell me who I can ask?” ˢᵇ

Hollis gets a pencil out of one of the baskets. The man he usually sees at that bend went east two days ago. There might be a load of reeds at the station tomorrow, if the cutter has enough to bring in. He works the ditch near where Subaru woke up.

“I can wait here for him,” Subaru says. ˢᵇ

“He doesn't come here. He takes the load to the station. I'll pass it on if he wants to speak to you.” ᴴᵒ

Subaru has his mouth open. He wanted to go somewhere this morning. He tells Hollis so. Hollis points out a shorter way to the station, with a roof at the last bend where he can sit out of the weather. Subaru could walk it before dinner and know the way when there's somebody to ask.

He copies the turn onto his own sheet. On his way out he sees Rem coming across the yard. She's wearing a sweater with the sleeves pushed up. She has the end of a long piece of string in her hand and Umaru is at the other end of it, making very determined progress backward.

He stops. Rem sees him stop.

Then he takes the road he just asked for. In the yard, Umaru calls to Rem to hold on tight. He hears Rem laugh before he's too far away to hear what they're doing.

* * *

It looks like the wrong way to the station from the moment he leaves the main road. The path goes down far enough that he can't see any of the houses. He goes back up twice to check the bend Hollis drew. The second time, a woman leading a mule asks if he means to cross before her. He says he doesn't know. She tells him she's going to the station and she's going down.

He lets her go first. At the bottom there's a culvert where water goes under the path. He would have known he was going the right way if Hollis had drawn that. He adds it to his map while the mule decides which side it wants to get its feet wet on. Then he adds the next turn himself.

At the station the bench is occupied by a bundle of kindling. The station keeper sees him standing over it and comes to take the bundle in.

“The women found you, then,” she says. ˣ

He starts to say he found them. He remembers Ram scraping the stool across the boards so she could wake him without putting a hand on him.

“They did.” ˢᵇ

“Good.” ˣ

She lifts the kindling against her chest. His good hand comes up to help. She shifts the bundle so he can get hold of two sticks that would have dropped. He takes them as far as the box inside. She asks why he came back, and for once he has the map open before he tries to tell the story.

The reed cutter hasn't brought a load for a while. The lower ditch is full and he likes to wait for it to go down rather than dry his reeds on somebody else's roof. He might come tomorrow. She wouldn't tell a person to spend a whole day on the bench for that.

Subaru looks out at it. He had sat there the last time until the coat fell off him. He remembers being certain that if he got up, the only people who might know him would arrive while he was gone.

“Can I leave a note for him?” he asks. ˢᵇ

The keeper pushes the kindling down into the box. She'll put a note under the hook where the cutter leaves his gloves. She can't tell him when it will get read.

Subaru takes the sheet with Rem's name on it out of his pocket. He tears off the part he'd folded under, then tears off a little more until the top of the page is clear. He writes which day he woke up and where the cart was when he saw it. At the bottom he starts to write a second name and thinks better of it. He leaves his own, so the reed cutter can ask for him without asking for either sister.

“I'll come back,” he says. ˢᵇ

“Are you staying here?” the keeper asks. ˣ

He looks down at the map. There is a short line coming the other way now, where he drew the turn after the culvert. He can read it without somebody standing beside him.

“I've got a room up the road,” he says. “I'll come back from there.” ˢᵇ

She takes the note. He leaves before he can ask her where she's going to put it a second time.
