# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 9 — An Hour After Dark

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S10E14
**Continuity state:** B+164 morning and evening. Subaru takes a voluntary first step on his own search and leaves Rem the space she asked for. A credible new report concerns Ko. Nazuna and Anko choose to verify it at the old crossing. No invitation, test, or coin yet.

*When: Subaru gets to the desk in the morning, someone has put his copy of the map on top of a stack of work orders so he can find it.*

He's spent half the night writing down questions. At the top of the sheet is Rem's name. He reads it while he waits, then folds the top over so it's under his thumb.

Hollis comes in from the yard carrying two empty baskets. The handles have caught together, and he sets one down to get them apart. Subaru thinks about asking when Rem will be at breakfast. He knows where the kitchen is. He knows she might come through it any minute.

He moves over until he can't see the kitchen door.

“I came through here,” he tells Hollis. He turns the map to show the little bend he drew with Ram. “I want to find out if anyone saw somebody else there before I did. Would you tell me who I can ask?” ˢᵇ

Hollis gets a pencil out of one of the baskets. The man he usually sees at that bend went east two days ago. There'll be someone bringing in a load of reeds tomorrow who might have been working the ditch when Subaru woke up.

“I can wait here for him,” Subaru says. ˢᵇ

“He doesn't come here. He takes the load to the station. I'll pass it on if he wants to speak to you.” ᴴᵒ

Subaru has his mouth open. He wanted to go somewhere this morning. He tells Hollis so. Hollis points out a shorter way to the station, with a roof at the last bend where he can sit out of the weather. Subaru could walk it before dinner and know the way when there's somebody to ask.

He copies the turn onto his own sheet. On his way out he sees Rem coming across the yard. She's wearing a sweater with the sleeves pushed up. She has the end of a long piece of string in her hand and Umaru is at the other end of it, making very determined progress backward.

He stops. Rem sees him stop.

Then he takes the road he just asked for. In the yard, Umaru calls to Rem to hold on tight. He hears Rem laugh before he's too far away to hear what they're doing.

* * *

That evening Anko comes to find Nazuna. She brings a note and waits until Nazuna has finished putting her shoes on.

Nazuna notices the waiting. She goes back and tightens a lace she already did up.

“You can just tell me.” ᴺᵃ

Anko holds the note out. Hollis wrote it on a piece of paper with the price of onions on the other side. She has put a finger over the price so Nazuna will know where to start.

A boy had asked for someone on the road. He'd been working for a ride on one of the carts, and Hollis heard him ask the driver the same question twice because the driver hadn't answered the first time. He'd given a name. Nanakusa. Then he'd changed his mind about the ride and got down at the old crossing. He said he'd wait there an hour after dark the next day. In case somebody came.

“What does he look like?” Nazuna asks. ᴺᵃ

“Hollis didn't think that would be the part you wanted in writing,” Anko says. ᴬⁿ

“Hollis thinks a person is something you describe by how much room they take up on a cart.” ᴺᵃ

Anko nods. Hollis had said that the boy made room for a basket without being asked. She passes that along too.

* * *

Nazuna goes as far as the common room and has to turn around because she has left the note in the wrong pocket. Anko is still by the door. She lets Nazuna come all the way back for her.

“Do you think he meant it?” Nazuna asks. ᴺᵃ

“What part?” ᴬⁿ

“That he'd wait.” ᴺᵃ

Anko looks toward the dark window. She has known enough people to know what a promise to be somewhere at night might be worth. She has known Nazuna long enough to know that she's already worked that out.

“We can go see,” she says. ᴬⁿ

Nazuna puts both hands in her pockets. They come straight back out.

“I'd like that,” she says, very fast. “If you're coming anyway.” ᴺᵃ

Anko goes to tell Jinshi where they'll be. Nazuna takes the note out again while she's gone. The onion price has been crossed out, and written back in underneath, the same number both times. She reads it until she can hear Anko coming back.

* * *

They have time to walk. The moon's just up by the time they get to the old crossing. There is a strip of pale stone across the water where the stepping blocks start. Nazuna sits on the bank on their side. Anko stays back where she can see the road as well.

“How much of an hour?” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃ

“We've got a while.” ᴬⁿ

Nazuna nods. There is nothing to look at on the other bank. She keeps looking anyway. Behind her Anko finds a place to sit too, and for a long time neither says anything about how early they are.
