# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 11 — The Other Bank

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S10E14
**Continuity state:** B+164 evening continuous. Ko verifies himself in person; source baseline late after both recognize their feelings, altered vampire state retained. He crossed by convergence, no coin. No feeding or test.

*When: the boy steps down to the crossing, Nazuna recognizes the way he stops to check if she's coming too.*

He stops on the other bank. There's a stone between them that sticks up farther than the others. He has got one foot on it, ready to come across, and he doesn't.

“You found me,” he says. ᴷᵒ

“You left a note.” ᴺᵃ

He stands there considering the difference. She gets up. The rock under her foot goes into the water a little when she steps on it. She doesn't lose her balance. She looks down all the same.

“You coming over?” he asks. ᴷᵒ

She thinks about saying something clever. The things she thinks of are mostly ways of saying yes without having to say it. She looks at him and says yes.

* * *

They meet two stones short of his bank. There isn't enough room for the two of them on the one she picks. He goes back one and she follows. When they get to the shore he takes his hands out of his pockets. She recognizes those too. She has been able to remember what they looked like very well. She had forgotten that sometimes he didn't know what to do with them.

She takes one.

“You look like you walked here,” she says. ᴺᵃ

He tells her about the road, and the lights he thought would lead to a town he knew, and the ride he got from a woman who wanted somebody to hold her basket while she drove. He had started asking about her because it was the only thing he could think to do after dark. The first time a person said they might know who he meant, he didn't believe it. He asked three times. She believes that part.

“I thought it might just be a name somebody remembered,” he says. ᴷᵒ

“It was my name.” ᴺᵃ

“I wanted to see if I could say it where you'd hear it.” ᴷᵒ

She looks at him. She has his hand in hers and can feel that he's been holding it a little too carefully. She tightens her fingers. He tightens his.

* * *

Anko waits long enough that Ko notices her on his own.

“Did you bring a stake?” he asks. ᴷᵒ

“I brought a clean towel. You're standing in a river.” ᴬⁿ

He looks down. The water is about as far up his shoes as it can get. He comes the rest of the way onto the bank. Anko throws him the towel and asks how he feels. He says he's tired. She asks whether it's the ordinary kind. He gives her a look which she recognizes, and then he says he thinks so.

Nazuna has let go of his hand so he can dry his feet. There is something she had got quite good at pretending she didn't think about. She can feel it in the way he's sitting a little farther from her than he needs to sit. She doesn't want him to think she hasn't noticed.

“We can talk about it when you get somewhere warm,” she says. ᴺᵃ

He looks up at her. She can tell he knows which thing.

“Do we have to talk about it tonight?” he asks. ᴷᵒ

“No.” ᴺᵃ

He sets the towel down and she takes the place beside him, which is wet now, so she gets him to move over a little. Anko says there's a room they can ask for. Ko asks how far away it is. Nazuna tells him it depends how fast he wants to walk.

“Pretty slow,” he says. ᴷᵒ

“Good.” ᴺᵃ

They go back by the bridge. It is farther, and the planks stay dry all the way over. Halfway across, Ko reaches for her hand again.
