# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 13 — Before Anybody Offers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S10E15
**Continuity state:** B+165 afternoon. Nazuna and Ko consent to consultation. Maomao, Kusuri, Shoko and Anko discuss separate questions. Yuka raises Mau's adaptation as a possible model. No sample, exposure or approval yet.

*When: Maomao asks what they want to know, Ko and Nazuna give her two different answers.*

“If it's still dangerous,” says Ko. ᴷᵒ

“What his body's doing,” says Nazuna. ᴺᵃ

They look at each other. Kusuri has brought a sheet with a line down the middle and nobody has known what it was for until now. She writes both things down.

“Start with his body,” Maomao says. “What was the first thing you noticed here?” ᴹᵐ

Ko looks down at his fingers. He had been saving the story about the cut because it is the one he can tell without showing anybody anything. He tells it first. Maomao wants to know what he was doing just before the cut closed the wrong way. He was lying on his back under a cart, holding a board up with one foot because the driver had asked him to. Was he frightened? Yes. Had he been frightened when the cut closed properly? No. He had been eating a plum.

Kusuri writes *plum*. Maomao leans over and adds *afterward*.

“Did you feel it change?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

“I felt it stop,” Ko says. “That was the bit I was waiting for.” ᴷᵒ

She asks whether he would let her see where it was. He shows her the place. There isn't a scar. She takes a note of that and asks if he wants his shoe back. He does.

* * *

Nazuna has her arms folded. There is a cup on the table for her. She has turned it so the handle's facing away.

“Can you find that out without having me bite him?” she asks. ᴺᵃ

Maomao looks at the two columns. She can say quite a lot about how she'd separate the things they already know from what they have guessed. She can't say that an experiment on a table would settle what happens between two people, and she tells them so.

“I can tell you what we see,” she says. “That would be a place to start.” ᴹᵐ

Ko asks if they can start without hurting anybody. Shoko tells him they can start by talking and that they've already done that. He had expected more questions before it counted.

Yuka has been sitting by the window where Anko asked her to wait. She looks at Mau. He is at the end of the bench with the back of his head against the wall, listening with his eyes closed. She waits until he opens them.

“Could you look at what his body does before anybody tries to copy it?” she asks. ʸ

Mau looks at Ko rather than answering for him. Ko looks down at his hands again.

“What would I have to do?” ᴷᵒ

“Tell us what you would let us see,” Mau says. “You could stop whenever you wanted.” ᴹ

Shoko taps the edge of Kusuri's paper. They haven't even decided what a result would look like. She would like them to do that before somebody offers to try anything on himself.

Yuka looks back at Mau. He has been sitting up a little straighter since she spoke.

“That's what I was about to say,” he tells her. ᴹ

“Good.” ʸ

She leaves her hand on the bench beside his. He turns his over under it.

* * *

Kusuri makes a second copy for Ko. She writes the answers they have so far on it, then puts a space underneath for anything he remembers after he goes home.

Nazuna asks if he wants her to stay while he reads it.

“I want you to read yours too,” he says. ᴷᵒ

Kusuri looks at the table. She had thought she'd made two copies. It turns out she made one copy and gave it away. Maomao slides the original toward Nazuna. She has already memorized which side has the plum on it.

They read them beside each other. Nothing on the page tells them what they have to do tomorrow.

Outside the door, Nazuna folds her copy in quarters. She gets as far as putting it in her pocket. Then she takes it out and hands it to Ko.

“I keep getting to the same word and wanting to start again,” she says. “You can hold them for tonight.” ᴺᵃ

Ko takes both. From here he can see through the kitchen window. Kusuri has got there ahead of them and is taking a lid off a jar while someone keeps trying to set a bowl where her elbows are. He hadn't thought anybody here would have a reason to talk to him again if they couldn't do the thing on the page today.

“You want to walk later?” he asks. ᴷᵒ

“I know a place with a roof where they don't lock the gate at night.” ᴺᵃ

“Do you have to fly to get there?” ᴷᵒ

“No. But if we take the stairs you'll have to hear my opinion of all of them.” ᴺᵃ

He tells her he'll bring a drink for the top, if they ever get there. She starts giving him her opinion before they've gone three steps. Somewhere in the first part of it he folds her copy into his so he can give her his arm.
