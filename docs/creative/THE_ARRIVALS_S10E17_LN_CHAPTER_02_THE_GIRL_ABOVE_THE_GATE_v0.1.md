# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 17 — The Girl Above the Gate

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S10E17
**Continuity state:** B+166 afternoon continuous. Momo Nishimiya arrives alone through ordinary convergence from fifteen subjective days after receiving Mai's body in the source. Mistakes Mau for Gojo at a distance. Lands outside the marker. Mai recognizes and comforts her. No admission or copying demonstrated yet.

*When: Mai hears someone call Gojo's name from above the path, she looks at the roof first.*

It's a sensible place to look. Gojo has gone to show Shoko the paper ball. He may have gone up onto the roof on the way. But the voice is coming from farther out, past the road marker, and what she sees when she looks past the fence is a girl sitting astride a broom.

The broom comes down very fast. Then the girl sees where the yard ends and pulls up so sharply her shoes swing out in front of her.

“Gojo!” she calls again. “How did you get out?” ᴺⁱ

Mau turns. Blue goes out in front of him. The ball of paper falls between two cups, a very long way from the cross.

For a moment Mai can't tell which of the two of them the girl is looking at. She's holding one hand up to keep the sun out of her eyes. Then she puts the hand down, and Mai sees her face.

“Momo,” she says. ᴹᵃⁱ

The broom drops the last foot to the road.

* * *

Momo gets off it and puts both hands back on it, in that order. The guard has already gone to the marker. Mai asks her if she can go out and speak to the girl. The guard tells her to use the wicket. Mau picks up the cups so nobody will step on them and comes as far as the gate.

Momo has put one foot forward. She has forgotten the other one.

“I saw you,” she says. “Maki brought you down the steps.” ᴺⁱ

Mai stops where Momo can see her whole face. It's farther away than she'd usually stand to talk to her. Momo looks like she could go back into the air without ever touching the broom.

“I know what she brought,” Mai says. “She told me this morning.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo shakes her head. Her hand slides along the broom handle until it hits the place where the binding starts.

“She put you in my arms.” ᴺⁱ

Mai knows, now, how much of a thing it is to be asked to say what actually happened. She can hear that Momo has got as far as she can by herself.

“I'm here,” Mai says. “I can come closer if you want.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo nods once. Mai comes across the rest of the road. When she reaches for Momo's hand, Momo takes her wrist first. She holds two fingers there for several seconds. Then her whole face goes wrong and she gets both arms around Mai.

Mai puts her hand between her shoulders. Momo tries to tell her that she was supposed to call and that everybody knows this and that it would have taken about a minute. Mai says she knows. She says it a second time when she feels Momo start to shake.

* * *

The broom is lying across the road. Mau considers it. He picks it up just far enough to get it out of the way of the wicket. It pulls gently against his fingers, as if it would like to go a little higher.

He puts it back down, very carefully.

Momo lifts her head.

“Did he just touch my broom?” ᴺⁱ

“Yes,” Mai says. ᴹᵃⁱ

Mau goes back into the yard without waiting for the other question. Mai feels Momo laugh before she hears it. She doesn't let go of her.

“I'll tell you who he is in a minute,” she says. ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo nods against her shoulder. They stay where they are until she wants to lift her head again.
