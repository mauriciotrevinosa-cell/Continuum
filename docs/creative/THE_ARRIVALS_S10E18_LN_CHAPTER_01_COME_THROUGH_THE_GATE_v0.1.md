# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 18 — Come Through the Gate

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S10E18
**Continuity state:** B+166 late afternoon. Mai vouches for Momo Nishimiya; ordinary temporary guest admission. Momo meets Maki and Gojo. Noise observes the broom technique; no activation, ownership change or use.

*When: Momo lets go of Mai's sleeve, she keeps hold of the button at the end.*

“Is it all right if I come with you?” she asks. ᴺⁱ

“Yes.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Mai waits while Momo picks up her broom. Then she asks the guard who has the guest book. The guard has it under her arm. She opens it in the middle, where it will stay open by itself, and asks Momo whether she'd like to spell her name. Momo takes her hand off the button long enough to do it.

“I know her,” Mai says when the guard looks up. “I'll bring her back here if she wants to leave.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo looks at the opening in the fence. It's not much of an opening. Somebody carrying a table would have to take the table over the top.

“I can walk,” she says. ᴺⁱ

Mai looks down at the way she's holding the broom. There are three fingers on it and one still hooked in the air by Mai's sleeve.

“You can walk with me,” she says. ᴹᵃⁱ

They go through together.

* * *

Maki is by the yard with a boot under her arm. When she sees who Mai has brought, she puts the boot down on the wall. Momo sees her do it. For a moment it looks as though she's going to turn around and go back through the gate before it shuts.

“I never got to ask you anything,” Momo says. ᴺⁱ

“You can ask me now.” ᴹᵃ

Momo's chin goes up. Mai has seen her do that before when she was sure she'd be able to ask the next question without her voice going wrong.

“Did you know I went back down there?” ᴺⁱ

Maki shakes her head. Momo looks at her for a while. Then she says that she had gone back down because she didn't know what else to do. She didn't find anybody to ask.

“I'm here,” Mai says. “Would you come see where I live first?” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo looks down at the boot. She asks why Maki brought only one.

“The other one works.” ᴹᵃ

Mai puts a hand over her mouth. Momo looks at both of them, then asks which way they have to go.

* * *

At the turn a man is sitting on the fence with a cup. He has the white hair Momo was looking for earlier and a very familiar way of lifting one hand with all the fingers hanging down.

“Oh,” she says. “There you are.” ᴺⁱ

“Yes,” Gojo says. “I get that quite a lot.” ᴳ

Mai tells him that Momo has had a long afternoon. He looks past Mai at the broom, then looks at Momo properly. He gets down from the fence. He doesn't ask her to tell him the afternoon.

“Do you want a cup?” he asks. ᴳ

“Later.” ᴺⁱ

“I'll have it ready later.” ᴳ

Momo gives a little nod. She walks on beside Mai.

Mau is still in the yard, getting the cups nested. Noise has been following the little correction the broom makes whenever Momo shifts her grip. It has found three things to look at and wants a fourth. Mau puts the last cup in the stack and tells it to wait. Mai is going to show somebody a room, and for once he has thought of something more useful to do than follow. He goes to see how many cups Gojo actually has.
