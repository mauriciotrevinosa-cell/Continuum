# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 19 — What Happened to the King

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S10E18
**Continuity state:** B+166 evening. Mai tells Momo what happened to Sukuna. Mau answers briefly. Momo begins to understand Mai has a life here and accepts company. No archive shared.

*When: Momo asks if she ought to keep the broom by the door, Mai says there's room for it beside the chair.*

Momo puts it where she was told. It sticks up past the top of the chair by rather more than she'd expected. She turns it around. There's the same amount sticking up, but it looks like less.

Mai takes the boot Maki brought and sets it on the bench by her tools.

“You really fix those?” Momo asks. ᴺⁱ

“Mostly I tell her which part she wore out.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo watches her pick up the boot and press the heel with the same thumb Maki used. She has known Mai for a long time. There are things she could put down beside her without asking, and things Mai would find an excuse to push back across the table. She hadn't known a boot could be either of those things. She asks Mai what else she makes here.

Mai shows her the latch from the inside. Momo tries it with her elbow, too high the first time and just right the second. Then she asks how much of this place was here before the fighting.

“Which fighting?” Mai says. ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo looks at her. That hadn't been a question anybody needed to ask where she came from.

“Did you find out where Sukuna is?” ᴺⁱ

Mai lets the latch spring back under her hand.

“He died.” ᴹᵃⁱ

* * *

It takes three attempts to explain it. The first time Momo thinks they mean the vessel died. The second time she thinks somebody has sealed him again. The third time Mau happens to be outside with the tray Gojo promised. Mai asks if he has a minute.

“You're the one who did it?” Momo says. ᴺⁱ

Mau puts the tray down. There is a bit of the old training paper still caught in his sleeve. It comes off when he brushes it this time.

“Yes.” ᴹ

“What did you do to him?” ᴺⁱ

He looks at Mai. Mai doesn't look away, but she moves the tray nearer so he has a place to put his hand if he wants it.

“I stayed up longer than he did,” he says. “Then we talked for a minute. He chose to go.” ᴹ

Momo keeps watching him. The person she had come down to see was someone she thought might know exactly what to do about Sukuna. This one has a scar going into the neck of his shirt and paper on his sleeve and has brought the tea two cups at a time. She looks down at the tray. There are three cups.

“Did you want one?” she asks him. ᴺⁱ

“That one's for Maki if she comes back for her boot.” ᴹ

Mai tells him it isn't fixed yet. He tells her he'll warn Maki. Then he leaves so Momo can ask Mai anything she didn't want to ask with him there.

* * *

Momo sits down at the table.

“I think I want to know what you did yesterday,” she says. ᴺⁱ

Mai looks at the boot. Yesterday she waited until she could tell somebody what she wanted in the right words. She thinks about saying that. Then she looks up at Momo, who has given her the whole afternoon to begin anywhere she likes.

“I helped fix a door,” she says. “Do you want to hear why the first hinge was rubbish?” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo puts both hands around her cup. She says she does.
