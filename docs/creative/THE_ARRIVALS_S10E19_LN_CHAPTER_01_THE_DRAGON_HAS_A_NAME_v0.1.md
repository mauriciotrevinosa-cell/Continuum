# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 20 — The Dragon Has a Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S10E19
**Continuity state:** B+167 morning. Nishimiya takes a bounded practice flight with Mai's knowledge and meets Kamish; first encounter with Momo Ayase. No new technique activation by Mau or romantic commitment.

*When: Momo lands for the third time that morning, she puts the broom down before she's finished getting off it.*

Mai looks up from the heel of the boot. Momo has come in through the door at something faster than a walk and has gone straight past the chair she was supposed to leave the broom beside.

“There's a dragon,” she says. ᴺⁱ

“Which one?” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo puts both hands on the workbench.

“The dragon.” ᴺⁱ

She takes a breath to explain. She had asked if she could go as far as the marked rise. She kept to the line Jinshi showed her, except when she had to move because there was an eye in the air higher than her broom and she couldn't see what the eye belonged to. She had gone a little way down. It turned out it belonged to the other end of the hill.

Mai puts her awl down and goes to the open window.

“That's Kamish.” ᴹᵃⁱ

The eye comes a little nearer. Momo makes a noise behind her.

“The dragon has a name,” Kamish says. ᴷᵃ

Momo looks at Mai.

“It talks.” ᴺⁱ

“It can hear, too,” says Kamish. ᴷᵃ

Mai looks straight down at the window ledge and concentrates on breathing. Momo asks her if the dragon is offended. Mai tells her she could ask the dragon that.

Momo considers it. She has put her hand on Mai's elbow without noticing.

“Are you offended?” ᴺⁱ

Kamish draws back a little, so that his eye is no longer level with the window.

“I had thought I was being quite small about the whole thing.” ᴷᵃ

* * *

Momo Ayase comes up with two cups while Kamish is explaining which path is nearest his wing. She says she heard somebody shout her name and then something about a dragon. Mai looks at the broom in the wrong place. She sees a problem beginning.

“Momo,” she says, and both of them turn.

There's a moment of complete quiet. Kamish, looking over the roof now, seems to find this highly instructive.

“I'm Nishimiya,” the one beside Mai says. ᴺⁱ

“Ayase.” The other sets down both cups. “Do people get your name wrong often?” ᴹᵒ

“No.” ᴺⁱ

“They might start.” ᴹᵒ

Mai laughs into her shoulder. Nishimiya glances at her. By the time Ayase has asked if one of the cups was supposed to be for the dragon, the hand on Mai's elbow has relaxed enough that Mai could move it if she wanted.

She leaves it there until Momo remembers to take it away herself.
