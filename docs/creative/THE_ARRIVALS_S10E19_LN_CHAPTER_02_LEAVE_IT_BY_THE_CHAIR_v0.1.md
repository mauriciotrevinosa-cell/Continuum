# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 62
## Chapter 22 — Leave It by the Chair

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 62
**Reading order:** 22
**Source episode:** S10E19
**Continuity state:** B+167 evening. Mai offers Nishimiya recurring time together and accepts a request for a longer guest stay to be arranged. The reading of Tool Manipulation is a bounded pattern, not a demonstrated ability. Volume closes; S10 continues.

*When: Momo comes back for the broom, the boot is gone.*

“Did you finish it?” she asks. ᴺⁱ

“Maki says it's better than the other one now.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Mai is sitting on the bench with nothing in her hands. There is a chair opposite her. Momo puts one foot against the leg and drags it closer before sitting down.

“I asked how long I could stay,” she says. “They said we could talk about it after breakfast tomorrow.” ᴺⁱ

Mai looks up. She wants to ask how long Momo asked for. She has a feeling she ought to ask the other question first.

“Do you want me there?” ᴹᵃⁱ

“Yes. Unless you've already got something to do.” ᴺⁱ

Mai lifts her hands from her knees. Momo smiles at how empty they are.

“I meant tomorrow.” ᴺⁱ

“I know.” ᴹᵃⁱ

* * *

There is a tap on the door. Mau is on the other side holding the broom with both hands and looking very carefully at the top of it. Momo had left it near the outside step after she finished flying. The air in the passage has been catching the bristles and moving them a little at a time toward the floor.

“I thought I'd bring it in before somebody had an idea.” ᴹ

“Did somebody have one?” Momo asks. ᴺⁱ

He considers telling her how many. Noise has a neat impression now of how she puts cursed energy into the broom and gets a response from a part she isn't touching. It would like to know whether the small adjustments are conscious. It would like to know why the broom resists some of them. It would especially like to know if he would just let go of the bottom for a second.

“I had one,” he says. “Then I decided I liked being allowed in this room.” ᴹ

Mai lifts one eyebrow.

“You are in the passage.” ᴹᵃⁱ

“I was hoping to work up to it.” ᴹ

Momo takes the broom from him. She says she can show him how she puts it down without it rolling, if that's what he wants to know. She lays it on its side, turns it half a turn and takes her hand away. It stays where she put it. Mau watches the end settle. Noise gives him a small, satisfied feeling and leaves it alone.

He thanks her and goes to find Yuka. Mai hears him take the first step too quickly, then take the second at the speed he meant to go.

* * *

Momo asks if she ought to carry the broom back to the guest room. Mai thinks about the space by the chair. It hasn't been in anybody's way since the first day, except when Momo put it in the wrong place on purpose.

“Leave it here,” she says. “You'll need it when you come back.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo looks down at the broom. She moves it a little farther under the chair.

“After breakfast?” ᴺⁱ

“You can come before breakfast. I don't have to have anything to show you first.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo leans back in the chair. Mai thinks she'll go when she hears the kitchen bell. The bell goes, and Momo asks what they have for dinner. Mai gets up to go with her and leaves the door open until they're both through.
