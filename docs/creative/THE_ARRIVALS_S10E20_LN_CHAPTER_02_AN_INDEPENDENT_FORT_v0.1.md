# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 2 — An Independent Fort

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S10E20
**Continuity state:** B+168 afternoon. Mau signs the Fort cupboard proposal as a witness. Yuka marks the places that need specifics. The paper stays at the Fort desk inside the house.

*When: Umaru asks him to sign, Mau asks whether she'd like him to use his good handwriting.*

“Use the usual one,” she says. “We need it to look authentic.” ᵁ

She sets down the sheet next to Ori's map. The map is of the little dungeon they have been planning to ask about. The sheet is of a table at which someone has written *Fort* on one side and *rest of house* on the other. There is a space between the two columns exactly wide enough for a pencil.

“The Fort wants to be independent in the matter of the cupboard,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“Which cupboard?” ᴹ

She shows him the drawing. He recognizes it. It was the first cupboard any of the children could open without borrowing a chair. He tells her that it is excellent to have a cupboard at the height of the people who use it. Umaru nods and folds her hands on the sheet.

“And if somebody takes the sweets from it, we can go through our own process.” ᵁ

“What is your process?” ᴹ

“We count what's left and ask Rem.” ᵁ

He thinks about that. It's about the strongest process he's heard of all day.

“Can I apply for a cupboard?” he asks. ᴹ

“What would you do with one?” ᵁ

“Open it every once in a while. Put things in it so you can find them.” ᴹ

Umaru looks at him over the top of the paper. This sounds dangerously like what he does already. She turns the pencil around and draws him a little space at the end of the table.

“You can bring things to the cupboard,” she says. “We can give you a receipt.” ᵁ

Mau asks her if it can have a hamster on it. She puts the pencil down and looks at him with a new respect. When he offers to sign, she makes sure the hamster will fit beside his name.

* * *

Yuka has gone to put the cups back. She comes to the door in time to see Mau turn the page so Umaru can see his name. Beneath it he has added *witness*, which would normally be the first thing he'd think to ask about. There is a little drawing of a hamster beside the signature.

“What did you sign?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“The cupboard.” ᴹ

Umaru takes the paper and reads the first line back to him. It says that the Fort is independent in matters it manages on its own behalf. The cupboard is pictured in the margin.

Yuka sets both cups on the table.

“Did you write ‘cupboard’ anywhere on it?” ʸ

Mau thinks about the drawing. He thinks it is a very good drawing of a cupboard. He tells her so.

Yuka looks at Umaru.

Umaru folds the paper in half. She puts the cupboard on the outside because it makes a very good cover.

“I can write it in again,” she says. “Once we agree on where the limits go.” ᵁ

Yuka puts a line under *independent*. Umaru hasn't had to ask anybody to let her draw this cupboard, or make a place for somebody at the table. It would be a shame if she had to stop doing either because they couldn't agree on what the first line meant.

“Keep it here,” Yuka says. “Get the people who actually open the cupboard to say what they want.” ʸ

From upstairs comes Mau's voice asking whether somebody would like to see a dragon. Ori says yes loud enough for them to hear through the ceiling.

“He can wait till tomorrow to do that,” Yuka says. She hands the pencil back to Umaru. “And if you put him in charge of the cupboard before then, count the biscuits yourself.” ʸ

She takes the cups back upstairs. By the time she gets to Ori's map, Umaru has written *ask Rem* on the inside of the fold.
