# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 3 — The Assistant's List

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S10E21
**Continuity state:** B+168 afternoon to evening. Ori asks to lead a supervised first dungeon outing. No flight or dungeon entry while chocolate is active. Adults agree to assess and confirm plans in the morning; role boundaries agreed.

*When: Ori finds out the dragon isn't coming until tomorrow, she asks what else he is doing today.*

Mau looks at the map. He has been keeping the corners down for a long time. They stay down when he takes his hands off. He does it very slowly, so as not to startle them.

“He could come and sit in the yard,” he says. ᴹ

“Do you need him to?” Yuka asks. ʸ

He looks up. She has brought back the paper from the desk with a line under the word *independent*. He doesn't remember seeing the line before. He would like to ask her if she drew it. Then he remembers Ori asked something first.

“No,” he says. “I want to show Ori how it feels when a dragon comes up under your feet. Tomorrow would be fine.” ᴹ

“Can I say where we go?” Ori asks. ᴼ

“If the people who look after where we go say yes.” ᴹ

Ori turns the map toward herself. It has been rolled up for three days, because Frieren told her she ought to check what the scale meant before she put any more things on it. She checked. She has also put more things on it.

“We want to go here,” she says. “The Fort hasn't done a dungeon. We've been to the festival one, but that wasn't ours. I want to be the person who says what we do next.” ᴼ

Yuka asks who is going with her. Ori takes out another sheet. She has a list of children who said they wanted to try, and a shorter list of people who hadn't been asked yet because they were supposed to be given a chance to say no. Milim is on both lists. Frieren is on the second. Mau finds his name at the bottom, under a heading he can't read from here.

“What's that?” he asks. ᴹ

“Assistant.” ᴼ

He puts a hand on his chest. He has been given much bigger titles and nothing has ever reached him as quickly as that one.

* * *

Frieren comes to look at the map after dinner. By then Maomao has checked on Mau twice, and he has stopped trying to tell the people who bring her water how good they are at it. He'd still quite like to, but it occurs to him that they probably know.

Frieren asks Ori where she'll take the group if the first path is blocked. Ori shows her. Frieren follows the line twice with one finger. Then she asks about a place on it with two marks together.

“That's where you tell me if you need to do something,” Ori says. ᴼ

“What if I need to do something before then?” ᶠ

Ori looks down at the map. She thinks about it and moves a mark to the edge of the page where it won't get in the way.

“Then you tell me there.” ᴼ

Milim takes the chair behind her and puts her chin on the back of it. She says that an assistant is allowed to stop something which is going to hurt somebody. It goes better if the assistant knows before they go what things they can let the leader work out. She wants to tell Mau that part herself.

Mau asks if she'll show him in the morning. She says yes and gets a pencil to put herself down for it.

Ori rolls the map up. It still curls to one side when she lets go. Mau reaches out to hold it for her and she shakes her head.

“You can let me do that.” ᴼ

He lets go. She puts the map under her arm and asks Frieren to come see if the little bag has room for two lunches. When they leave, Mau sits beside Yuka and puts his hand on the table where the corners were.

“Assistant,” he says again. ᴹ

Yuka smiles. She folds the paper with the line under *independent* and puts it where she'll find it tomorrow.

“That's what they put you down for.” ʸ
