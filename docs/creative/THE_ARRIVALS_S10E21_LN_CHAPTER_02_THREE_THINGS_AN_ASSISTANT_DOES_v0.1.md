# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 4 — Three Things an Assistant Does

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S10E21
**Continuity state:** B+169 morning. Chocolate effects ended. Shoko clears Mau to ride as a passenger and act as an observer only in a checked entry. Frieren, Milim, Gojo and Yuka accept specific roles. Ori leads Umaru, Coco, Suika and Richeh, with the agreed support. No flight or entry yet.

*When: Milim asks Mau what an assistant does, he says he brought three pencils.*

She looks down. He has got them laid across his palm, all pointing the same way. She takes the one with the best point.

“That's one thing,” she says. ᴹⁱ

“Are there three things?” ᴹ

She nods, though she hadn't been counting until he asked. She puts the pencil through the loop on the side of the bag. It doesn't quite go through, so she turns it around and puts it in the other way.

“You carry things. You look where they're not looking. And when they're thinking, you give them time to think.” ᴹⁱ

Mau has brought a little card on which to write whatever she tells him. He writes all three down. Then he shows it to her so she can see that he didn't leave one out.

“What if something comes around the corner while they're thinking?” he asks. ᴹ

“Then you do the second thing properly.” ᴹⁱ

She goes to tell Ori that her assistant knows what to do. Mau puts the card in his pocket. He could have said it back without looking. He has got it in his pocket anyway.

* * *

Shoko takes his pulse and asks what he did before he came.

“I made breakfast.” ᴹ

“Did you sit down and eat any of it?” ˢʰ

“Yuka made me some.” ᴹ

Shoko makes a note. She asks if he's had any of Maomao's chocolate this morning. He says he has been offered two pieces of ordinary toast and turned down one of them, so he thinks he can be trusted. She checks his eyes anyway.

“You can ride. You can walk the short route they checked. If they need another fighter, they come out. Make sure you tell them that before you go.” ˢʰ

He thanks her. She asks him to stop looking surprised every time she says yes to something.

* * *

At the gate, Ori has drawn a line between the names of the leaders and the names of the people who can close the outing. She reads them out. Umaru answers first. Coco and Richeh answer for themselves. Suika has been trying to get the pencil through the loop Milim showed her. When Ori says her name, she gives up on the loop, puts the pencil in her pocket and says yes too.

Gojo has been given the last place in line. He asks whether that's because of his eyesight.

“It's so you don't tell us what's coming,” Ori says. ᴼ

Yuka sits down on the edge of the stone to help Suika tie her boot. Gojo turns to her for help and gets none. Frieren explains that she and Milim can stop the group immediately, and that anybody who wants to turn back can tell them or put a hand up. Ori tests this by putting her own hand up. Everybody stops talking.

Mau asks if she wants the things for lunch in one bag or two.

Ori thinks about it. Nobody answers for her. He can hear the wind getting into one of the paper maps. He waits while she holds it down.

“Two,” she says. “If somebody leaves, they get to take their lunch.” ᴼ

He gets the other bag. Milim looks down at his pocket, where the corner of the card is showing. She gives him a small, approving nod.
