# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 5 — You Tell Him Where

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S10E22
**Continuity state:** B+169 late morning. Ori leads the approved short ride with Mau aboard as a passenger. Kaisel returns to shadow before the gate. Other participants take the checked path. No unsupervised flight.

*When: Kaisel puts his shoulder down, Ori gets one foot up and asks Mau what comes after that.*

Mau shows her with his hand. He has got hold of the strap already, with the other hand, and has to let it go again to make the movement clear. Ori watches and then gets up on her own. Once she's sitting she looks over the side to see if he needs her to show him.

“I do know this bit,” he says. ᴹ

He has to try twice. She doesn't tell him she saw. She waits while he gets his feet into the places they go. Then she takes both hands off the strap, holds them in the air in front of her and puts them straight back.

“How do I tell him?” she asks. ᴼ

“You can use your voice.” ᴹ

She leans forward. She tells Kaisel which part of the marked route Frieren said they could fly. She starts to explain where the markers are, in case he has only seen them from above. Kaisel turns his head just enough that she can see one eye. He waits until she finishes.

“Ready?” Mau asks her. ᴹ

“I already asked him.” ᴼ

The shoulder comes up under them. Mau remembers the little drop in the middle, which takes you just far enough to wonder whether you've let go. He hears Ori take a breath and then find out that it's already happened.

She laughs so loudly that Milim looks up from the path.

* * *

Ori had said she wanted to see the tree from the side that has no windows. Kaisel turns the wide way and gives her time. There is a place where you can see the paths coming out between the roofs. She points them out one by one. She can see the common room door and the little bit of roof where she usually stands when she wants to see if anybody's coming.

She hadn't thought it would all fit. She tells Mau this. He asks if she wants him to look at the map when they get down, and she says she wants to draw it before she looks at what she already drew.

They come in low over the route Frieren checked. Ori asks Kaisel to go farther left so nobody on the path has to move. He goes farther left. She sits very still until all four feet have found the ground.

“Did I tell him the right way?” she asks Mau. ᴼ

“Ask him,” he says. ᴹ

She puts her hand against Kaisel's neck and does.

He lowers the shoulder again. Ori takes that for an answer. She gets down, goes around to the front where he can see her properly and thanks him there.

* * *

The people who came on foot are at the last bend. Gojo is carrying the second bag. He holds it up as if he has discovered the purpose for which he was brought. Ori asks him to keep it a little longer.

Mau lets Kaisel go back into the shadow. He checks that the strap has gone with him. It has. He takes the card from his pocket and reads it once more before Ori gets to the marker.

She has the map open. She has turned it so that the way they came is nearest her. There is room at the top for the thing she hasn't drawn yet. She looks along the line until she finds the people she was looking for.

“Are we ready?” she asks them. ᴼ

Mau puts the card away without answering first.
