# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 6 — Where They Aren't Looking

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S10E22
**Continuity state:** B+169 midday. Supervised Fort dungeon route. Milim intercepts one blind-side monster. Everyone is unharmed. Mau stays in his observer role. Ori's team solves passage and inspects last chamber from safe position.

*When: Ori calls a stop at the first fork, the people behind her stop in exactly the places they would have chosen themselves.*

It leaves the whole width of the passage between the first person and the second, and almost no space between the last three. Ori turns around and asks them to get where they can all see the map. Umaru tells Gojo he is tall enough to see from where he is. He retreats one step without protest.

The left fork is longer. There's a ledge halfway down it where the last pair can wait while the front pair goes to look. The right fork has tracks in it. The tracks look old until Suika puts a finger down next to a heel mark and shows Ori where the dust has gone into it.

“Left, then,” Ori says. ᴼ

She puts the charcoal at the top of the page so she can find it again. Mau watches her take the first step. It is only after she has taken it that he remembers he has something else to look at.

Milim is already looking there.

* * *

Something with four jointed legs comes down from above the turn. It is quick enough that Mau has only time to see the place its feet will land, which is behind Richeh. Milim goes up to meet it.

She catches it at the point where the two bits of stone come together and puts it down on the empty side. There is a little noise and a great deal less monster. Mau has not yet got his hand out of his pocket.

“That one,” she tells him. ᴹⁱ

He looks where the legs stopped. Frieren asks Richeh if she wants to step back. She does, and Coco and Suika go with her. Ori waits until Frieren says she can come over, then brings the map to where Richeh is standing.

“Did it drop from here?” Ori asks her. ᴼ

Richeh looks where she's pointing. She says no, she thinks it came farther around. Ori gives her the charcoal. Together they put a mark where the dark reaches over the path. On the way back somebody will look there first.

Mau buys a cold can from his shop, takes it straight out of inventory and puts it in Milim's hand. She looks at it. She had been expecting him to ask if she was sure she got all of it.

“That's for the Guardian,” he says. “You looked up before any of us did.” ᴹ

“You're supposed to do that for free.” ᴹⁱ

“I know.” ᴹ

She opens it anyway. He takes the first bag off her shoulder while she drinks.

* * *

Halfway along the ledge, Ori puts her hand up again. The line on the scout sketch is supposed to go down to a lower floor with a short drop at the end. She can see the floor. The last part of the ledge has fallen onto it.

“I could carry them,” says Gojo from the back. ᴳ

Ori looks down. It's an easy drop for Gojo. He would have everybody across in less time than it would take her to draw the change. Then he would have to take them all back out the same way.

“If he carries us over,” she asks Richeh, “can you get back from there by yourself?” ᴼ

Richeh looks over the edge once. “I could get down. I don't think I could get up.” ᴿᶜ

Ori backs up. A little stone goes over where her toe was. She hears it hit once and roll. Behind her, Chloe has already turned the map so the entrance is at the top.

“We should go back to the fork,” Ori says. “If the other way's wrong too, we go out and tell them the sketch needs fixing.” ᴼ

Suika holds the charcoal while Ori crosses out the line. They go back far enough to see the tracks again. Coco notices a place where a fresh print covers an older one, with both toes pointing toward the chamber. When Ori asks what would let them walk that way, Richeh points at the high edge of the passage. From there they can look down the whole bend without putting their feet on the track.

Frieren goes as far as the corner to check it. When she comes back she lets Richeh show Ori where to lead them. This time Mau looks up before he follows.

* * *

They find the chamber by listening. There is something in it going round and round, with a pause every time it reaches the same place. Ori wants to know what the pause is. She gets everybody down behind the lower stone and has them count aloud on their fingers.

After three turns, Coco spots the pale line on the floor. Every time the creature comes to it, the plates on its back open and then close again.

Umaru looks down the line to where Gojo is sitting with both bags now, waiting to be told he can put them down.

“I think we know what to do,” she says. ᵁ

Gojo gets to his feet. He leaves the bags against the wall.

“Tell me where to stand.” ᴳ
