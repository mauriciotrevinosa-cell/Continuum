# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 7 — On the Count of Three

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S10E23
**Continuity state:** B+169 continuous. Fort team leads route and timing. Gojo uses one bounded Red from a secured angle with Ori's permission to end chamber guardian. All exit safely; no shadow raised or mechanics added.

*When: Ori shows Gojo the line on the floor, he asks her to tell him when to look at it.*

She looks into the chamber. The creature's got its head down. As it comes around, its front legs spread and its back opens in four separate places. It takes one breath and the opening's gone. Ori holds up three fingers. She takes them down one at a time until Gojo can see the last part for himself.

“There,” she says. ᴼ

Gojo nods. He can see two places he could stand to do this. He points out the closer one and Ori asks if he can stand in the farther one so the line stays open behind him. He goes to the farther one.

Frieren has the children check where they are. There is solid stone in front of them and room to get back if they need it. She waits until each of them has found the person they're going back with. Milim goes where she can see the other opening.

Umaru crawls along the safe side of the wall to where Ori is counting.

“We could ask him,” she whispers. ᵁ

“I already asked him.” ᴼ

“For the good one.” ᵁ

Ori thinks about it. She puts her hand out. Umaru takes it. Then Ori leans just far enough around the stone for Gojo to see her and asks if they can do the power of friendship.

Gojo gives her a perfectly serious nod.

“You count it,” he says. “I'll do the loud part.” ᴳ

* * *

They count another turn. Ori feels Umaru's grip tighten on the second mark. On the third she says it aloud.

Gojo lets the Red go from the far side of the chamber. It gets there in the little space before the plates come down. The creature lifts off the floor and comes down again against the wall on the empty side, in a place so far from the children that Ori has to look past Gojo to see it. The noise goes back around the chamber, and then the creature stops making the noise it made when it was going round and round.

Nobody moves until Frieren says they may. When Ori stands up, her hand is still in Umaru's.

“Did it work?” Ori asks. ᴼ

“You told me exactly when,” says Gojo. ᴳ

Umaru lets go of her hand and straightens the bit of her sleeve she pulled out of place. She is grinning hard enough that it is difficult to sound as official as she wants to when she says they ought to make an entry for it.

Mau gets the charcoal out of the side of the bag. He almost asks what they want him to write. Instead he gives it to Ori. She puts a little circle beside the place they stood and writes the names of the people who counted with her.

Gojo comes over to see if he got a mark. She shows him the place where she wrote *loud part*. He says that will do.

* * *

Outside, Frieren holds the bag while Ori gets the map flat enough to show the people who waited. She points to the corner where the monster dropped first. The girl who marked it asks if the mark will still be there next time.

Ori says the mark will be. She doesn't know if the monster will be.

Then she gets to the last chamber. Everyone wants to know what they did. She tells them which of them stood where and who had the idea to count the turns.

“What did you do?” one of the children asks Mau. ˣ

Mau looks down. He has the second bag in his hand. It is quite a lot lighter than when they came.

“I brought this back,” he says. ᴹ

Ori looks over at him. She waits until he's put it down. Then she asks if he can hold the map while she shows them where they're going to draw Kaisel.

He comes to hold it.
