# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 9 — Who Runs the Workshop

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S10E23
**Continuity state:** B+169 early afternoon. With the second small portion still active, Mau uses a limited reconstruction of Mai’s Construction on his own finite cursed energy for two simple gifts. Mai’s agreement still supplies her own work separately. Momo sees a single authorized lift by Ruler’s Authority. Her broom technique stays hers. Mai describes the two structures Mau tried to sustain against Sukuna’s Shrine and the cost of that attempt. The effect fades at home.

*When: Mai comes looking for her pincers, Yuka has both hands in her hair and Mau is holding something suspiciously flat.*

It started with the clip Yuka had let him see once, in a little memory of her own. She’d worn it while she washed her face. It had a long, shallow curve and two rounded ends that didn’t catch the hair at the back of her neck. The one she’d been using here bent every time she tried to take it off with one hand.

Mau had asked how wide she wanted it. Then he’d checked with Ciel that he could manage two little pieces of steel and stop there. The first one had come out a fraction too tight. Yuka turned it around and put it in her hair herself. It held.

“Look at that,” he said. “You knew exactly what to do with it.” ᴹ

“It’s a hair clip. I had a little training.” ʸ

He had already started on the other piece. A wide folded clip for keeping a bag of biscuits shut, the kind Taihei had once shown him from his kitchen at home. Mau put it over the fold in the paper bag beside Yuka and found that it held even when he turned the bag over. He was so pleased with this that he tried it a second time.

That’s the part Mai comes in for. Momo Nishimiya comes in behind her, holding her broom across both arms. Mai looks at the clip. Then she looks at the stillness in Mau’s empty hand as he lets Construction close down.

She gives him a resounding smack on the back of the head.

“Ow.” ᴹ

“I am the only one who gets to build like that in my workshop.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Mau looks down at the bench. Yuka can see him consider whether he might be able to prove they were on the other side of it.

“That’s fair,” he says instead. ᴹ

“You can keep the clip,” Mai tells Yuka. “I’m talking to him.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka puts her hand over the clip in her hair as if it had been about to leave. Mau turns to see how it sits. He has just enough sense left to keep the next compliment to himself. Yuka reads it on his face anyway.

* * *

“How did he do that?” Momo asks. ᴺⁱ

Mai looks over. Momo has set the broom on the bench. Yesterday she was still watching Mai’s wrist for the shaking to start. Now she is watching Mau’s hands with the same concentration.

“When he gets a clear read on a technique, he can work out a way to build a version of it,” Mai says. “He knows the shape of mine from working on our deal. He paid for those with his own cursed energy. And he has Gojo’s eyes and about thirty other things he could have used to annoy me today. He picked mine.” ᴹᵃⁱ

“It made her smile,” Mau says. ᴹ

Yuka turns her face away and smiles harder.

Momo looks down at the broom.

“Have you got mine?” she asks. ᴺⁱ

Mau follows her gaze. He puts both hands on the bench where she can see them.

“May I lift it a little?” ᴹ

She studies him, then moves her hand off the handle. The broom rises about the height of a cup. It tips toward the bristles for a second before Mau levels it. Momo’s eyes go wide.

“Oh, that’s Authority,” he says. “I’m lifting the broom itself. Here, give me your hands.” ᴹ

Momo holds them out. The weight settles all at once in her palms when he lets go. She flexes her own technique just enough to bring the far end back level. There is the familiar small pull through the handle. It feels as it always has.

“You watched me put it away yesterday,” she says. ᴺⁱ

“I saw you correct the angle. I’d still have to work out how you did it. You could show me someday, if you wanted.” ᴹ

Momo puts the broom down where it won’t roll. She takes her time thinking about that.

“What else can you do?” she asks. ᴺⁱ

“A fair bit. The eyes help me waste less cursed energy and Ciel’s filter makes all the information bearable. Yuta reckons I can reach a bigger reserve than his. Between those I can push some things a long way.” ᴹ

He says it with about as much ceremony as he’d used to explain how the paper bag closes. Momo looks at Mai for the part he has left out.

“At the quarry he tried keeping one domain’s barrier up while he opened a Shrine inside it,” Mai says. “Sukuna answered with his own. For a moment there were three structures in that fight. His second gave him one step. Then his head couldn’t carry both.” ᴹᵃⁱ

“One step?” ᴺⁱ

Mau puts his hand down flat. Yuka puts hers over it, softly enough that he could move if he wanted. He keeps it there.

“It was a good step,” he says. ᴹ

Mai looks at him for a moment. Then she nudges the bag toward Momo.

“And this is a good clip. You can see what he’s chosen to do with himself today.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo takes the clip off, opens the paper, and takes a biscuit. She puts it back on and presses along the fold to see if any of it comes loose.

“It is a good clip,” she says. ᴺⁱ

Mai sighs so loudly that Yuka has to put her forehead on Mau’s shoulder.

* * *

By the time the light gets to the end of the bench, Mau has stopped finding new things to offer everybody. He folds the biscuit bag once more because he likes the little sound the clip makes. Then he puts it away and goes to find Yuka.

She’s on the low step outside, putting her boots back on. The clip is still in her hair.

“Have you run out of things to give me?” she asks. ʸ

He sits beside her and thinks about it. After a moment he puts his hand out, palm up.

“I could stay another minute.” ᴹ

She sets the boot down without doing the lace. Then she puts her hand in his and leans her weight against him. He lets his head come down until it rests against hers.

“You know what I liked best?” she says. ʸ

“Please say it was the clip. I got hit for that.” ᴹ

“You came back when I asked you to sit with me.” ʸ

He looks down at their hands. She can feel the small change in his breath when he hears her. He has gone quiet enough now that she can tell the chocolate is wearing off. She takes her time settling closer, so that when he puts his arm around her he can do it without either of them having to get up again.

“I liked that too,” he says. ᴹ
