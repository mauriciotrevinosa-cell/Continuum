# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 12 — All Hail Queen Yuka

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S10E24
**Continuity state:** B+170 midday through afternoon. The Fort adopts a household agreement. Ori recruits Beru for the coronation. Mau provides a limited celebration stash and plays along with Queen Yuka. No outside notice, binding order, new power or recurring debt.

*When: Ori asks Mau whether she can borrow somebody, he thinks she means him and puts his cup down.*

“Can you ask Beru to come out?” she says. ᴼ

He picks the cup back up. He has spent most of the morning with people telling him which side of a table to sit on, so he is quite happy to bring somebody in who can make up his own mind.

Beru steps out beside the bench. His eyes go to Ori and then to Mau, who takes his cup and moves a little farther down so they can see each other.

“Ori wants to ask you something,” Mau tells him. “You get to decide.” ᴹ

Ori had written what she needed on her hand. It has come off along the crease. She remembers the first part.

“The Fort needs someone loyal, and strong, and who can keep a straight face even if—” ᴼ

Beru has one arm round her before she gets to the end. He waits just long enough to see if she'd like to be picked up, which she would. Then he goes up the stairs with her so fast Mau hears the question from the landing.

“Are you crying?” ᴼ

“Something flew into my eye.” ᴮᵉ

“We haven't gone outside.” ᴼ

Mau takes a drink. By the time he's ready to ask what the Fort needs, they're at the other end of the house.

* * *

There is a chair on the Fort's side of the table, and Beru has managed to make it taller than all the others by balancing it on the box they keep under the desk. Yuka looks at it, puts both hands on the arms and lifts it off the box.

“I'm not sitting on that unless it has four feet on the floor.” ʸ

Beru gets down to check the feet. They do. He lifts the paper crown Ori cut out, which he has carried all this way on a folded tea towel. Umaru calls on the Guardian to provide tape. Milim tears off a piece with her teeth. It takes both of them to stick it where the points will still go all the way around.

“Do I get to hear what I'm being made queen of?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Ori gives the explanation to Beru. He gets as far as *the Fort humbly petitions* before she interrupts him.

“We want Mau to give us more sweets.” ᴼ

Yuka looks at Mau. He is at the far side of the table with Rimuru, whose pencil is moving much faster now that none of this seems to be addressed to him.

“And what do I get?” ʸ

Mau puts the cup down.

“I have to hear what you ask for first.” ᴹ

She takes the crown from Beru. He makes a sound when he thinks she's going to give it back. Then she sets it on her head and puts one finger under the point above her eyebrow.

“This queen would like to know what you have for the cupboard.” ʸ

Mau opens his inventory. He puts two cold cans on the table, a bag of the sweets Ori likes and a smaller bag with the ones she always trades away first. Umaru grabs both bags before Milim can find out which is which. Rem makes her put them down again so they can count.

“There's going to be dinner later,” Mau says. ᴹ

“We know,” Ori says. “That's why you have to put something in the cupboard.” ᴼ

Beru holds out the folded towel while she puts the cans in. When Yuka asks for a knife to open a packet, he and Mau both move. Beru looks down at the towel in his hands and then at Mau, who already has the knife.

“You have been appointed to defend the cupboard,” Yuka tells Beru. ʸ

He looks at Ori to make sure. She gives him a sash made out of the paper left over from the crown. It fits one arm.

“I shall keep it safe until you return,” he tells her. ᴮᵉ

“I'm going to get a plate.” ᴼ

He holds his post.

* * *

It might have ended there. Yuka is halfway to taking the crown off when she notices that Mau has already set her cup on the side she likes.

“Your queen would like you to come outside with her,” she says. ʸ

He goes straight to get her coat. It isn't cold enough for a coat. He brings it anyway, carries it over one shoulder when she laughs at him and follows her as far as the bench by the tree. At her direction he sits on the right, gets up again when she decides she likes that side and moves to the left without asking what's wrong with the right.

“Is there anything else?” he asks. ᴹ

“I want half that biscuit you put in your pocket.” ʸ

He gets it out. It's the one with the broken corner. He gives her that half and she gives it back, because she knows which one he'd have chosen.

“You're really going to do whatever I ask all afternoon?” she says. ʸ

He considers it. “Most of it I'd have done if you'd asked this morning.” ᴹ

She takes his arm. He has it out far enough that she can fit both hands round it and lean into him. The crown slides another inch. When he straightens it, she holds still.

From across the yard comes a shout about a missing biscuit. Beru answers that the person responsible presented an absolutely convincing receipt. Rem says that this time the receipt is for a different biscuit.

Yuka closes her eyes. “You don't have to go,” she says. ʸ

“I wasn't going to.” ᴹ

She leans over and kisses him. He gets a hand to the crown in time to stop it going down the back of the bench. She laughs before she's pulled away and tells him that's quite enough diligence for one afternoon.

He puts it on his own head.

It fits him worse. She lets him keep it until it's time to go in.
