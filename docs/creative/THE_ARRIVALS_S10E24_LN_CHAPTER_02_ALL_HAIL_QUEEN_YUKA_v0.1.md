# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 10 — All Hail Queen Yuka

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S10E24
**Continuity state:** B+170 midday. Fort charter adopted internally and trade copy corrected. Extra agreed biscuits and one limited basket of Mau's supplies. Queen Yuka is Fort play; Mau alone follows the playful commands. No transfer of sovereignty, power or recurring debt.

*When: Hollis brings the biscuits down, he says he needs a receipt before anybody opens the lid.*

Umaru turns to the last page of the book. There's a number in the top corner. She counts the packets through the open end of the wrapping and writes the number down. Rem looks at it and asks if Umaru counted the one she's holding in her other hand. Umaru adds one.

Fern has signed the revised notice for the outer desk. It has the cupboards, outings and account arrangements in words Hollis can use. At the bottom it says that the earlier notice was an internal proposal and has been replaced. Hollis reads as far as the place where he needs to know whom to ask before he loads the next wagon, and says that'll do.

Mau puts a little basket on the table. It has two cans from the things he brought for the Fort and some wrapped sweets. He asks if there's a place in the receipt book for something that only happens once.

“There is now,” Umaru says. ᵁ

She writes it down. Before she puts the pencil away she draws a line through the part of the page that is still empty, because Rimuru can see it from where he's sitting and has already begun to look interested.

* * *

Ori brings out a paper crown from under the map. It is the right size for someone quite a lot smaller than Yuka. She opens the back with her fingers, fits it against Yuka's head and asks Milim for a bit of tape.

“What am I being crowned for?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“We got more biscuits.” ᴼ

“We negotiated.” ʸ

“That's what I said.” ᴼ

Milim gives her the tape. The crown stays on at a slight angle. Ori tells them all to say the words, and for one moment there is nobody in the room who can resist.

“All hail Queen Yuka,” says the table. ˣ

Then Hollis goes back to the wagon and Rem takes the cups to the kitchen and Umaru has to follow her because she has got the receipt in the wrong book. Yuka asks whether anybody has brought a knife for the last packet. Rimuru hears her and continues eating. Milim says there was a knife earlier and holds up the tape as if it can take its place. Yuka straightens her crown.

“The queen would like a knife,” she says. ʸ

Mau gets up. Yuka looks at the others. Rimuru turns his cup around until the handle is perfectly straight.

“She didn't say she was everybody's queen,” he says. ᴿⁱ

“I heard her,” Mau says from the cupboard. ᴹ

He finds her a knife, brings the bread plate while he's at it and makes an elaborate little bow. She asks if he is going to keep doing that.

“Until somebody tells me to stop,” he says. ᴹ

She considers the advantages of having one person who takes the position seriously.

“Your queen would like you to sit down and eat one with her.” ʸ

He sits. She gives him the biscuit with the broken corner because that is the one he will choose if she asks. When he reaches for his cup the crown slips over her left eyebrow. He straightens it for her and tells her it fits.

“It doesn't,” she says. ʸ

He puts both hands around his cup. She doesn't take it off until they're done.
