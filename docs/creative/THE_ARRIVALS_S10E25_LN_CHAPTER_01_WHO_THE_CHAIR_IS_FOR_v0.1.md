# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 11 — Who the Chair Is For

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S10E25
**Continuity state:** B+170 evening. Rimuru receives invitation to Walpurgis as member, with Mau asked to attend. Rimuru and Mau discuss conditions. No title accepted, appointment conferred, or departure yet.

*When: Rimuru brings the letter over, he puts it under Mau's empty plate instead of on top of his hands.*

“Whenever you're done,” he says. ᴿⁱ

Mau looks down. He has been done long enough for the crumbs to cool. There is a seal on the paper where the plate doesn't cover it. He asks Rimuru what it says before he moves the plate.

“Walpurgis. They want to talk about who gets to make certain promises.” ᴿⁱ

“Which promises?” ᴹ

“That's what I asked. They sent another page.” ᴿⁱ

Rimuru sits down. He takes both pages out from under the plate and lays them side by side. One is addressed to him. The other is addressed to Mau with a space after the name where they could have put something else. There is no space like that on Rimuru's.

Mau reads the part about the roads twice. Then he reads the part about people who carry letters in his name. He can see what that part is supposed to mean. He has been at the bench when somebody came to ask which sign on a wall belonged to him.

“Are they asking me who has permission?” he says. ᴹ

“They're asking who can give it. They've asked for a chair for you.” ᴿⁱ

* * *

Yuka gets up to take her plate away and comes back without it. She reads the first letter over Mau's shoulder. At the end she asks Rimuru if he wants Mau there.

Rimuru looks up. He had been thinking so hard about what the others wanted that he hadn't decided what his answer was going to sound like.

“I do. I'd rather not answer their questions about you while you're here and could answer them yourself.” ᴿⁱ

Mau says he can answer them. He says it too quickly. Yuka pulls out the chair that was beside him at dinner and sits down in it again.

“What would make you come home before it was over?” she asks him. ʸ

He considers it. It has been a long day of being able to say yes to things. He hadn't counted on how good that would feel.

“If they ask the same question three ways after I've answered it,” he says. ᴹ

“That's going to take about twenty minutes,” Rimuru says. ᴿⁱ

Yuka looks at the paper, at the blank space after Mau's name.

“If it only takes five, you can come home after five.” ʸ

Mau looks at her. He had been waiting for a question about whether he could promise to stay calm. Now he has a different one to answer.

“I'll tell you when we're going,” he says. ᴹ

“And tell me which way you're coming back.” ʸ

Rimuru pulls the route sheet out from underneath the two pages. He shows them where the appointed road runs between the hall and the next turning. There's a place to wait outside the meeting if somebody leaves first. Mau puts his finger on it.

“Here,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka takes a copy of the route. She doesn't ask him to fill in the space after his name.
