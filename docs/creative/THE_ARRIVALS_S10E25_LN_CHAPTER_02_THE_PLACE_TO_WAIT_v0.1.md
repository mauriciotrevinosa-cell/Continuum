# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 14 — The Place to Wait

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S10E25
**Continuity state:** B+171 morning. Mau and Rimuru leave for Walpurgis by approved route and meet with no delegated authority from the house. Yuka has route and return plan. No commercial passenger transport or change to the ward.

*When: Yuka hands Mau the letter, he finds she has folded the route so the way home is on the outside.*

“You can open it the other way,” she says. ʸ

“Then I'll be going the wrong way.” ᴹ

She looks up. He has been holding the papers out of his coat while she checks that the pocket will close. It closes. She takes both hands off it and gives him a little push toward the path.

He takes one step. Then he turns around and comes back to kiss her. He puts his hand on the side of her face to do it and feels her smile before he has moved close enough.

“If you're going to leave early, leave early,” she says. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Don't sit there for an hour trying to decide if you're allowed.” ʸ

He looks at her. She has said the thing he actually needed to hear. She can see that too, and gives him the time it takes to put the paper in his pocket the right way.

“What are you doing while I'm gone?” he asks. ᴹ

“I'm taking the Fort receipt book back to Umaru. Then Mai and I are going to hang the second door.” ʸ

“Will you need the bucket?” ᴹ

“We own a bucket.” ʸ

He laughs, and she goes with him as far as the path before turning back for the book.

* * *

Rimuru can take a short meeting bag or a long one. He brings the long one. Mau asks why, given the speed with which he thinks the meeting will be over.

“I thought you were leaving early. I have to stay there after you do.” ᴿⁱ

He says it without complaint. Mau knows he has thought about it because the papers in the bag have been put in order. There's a separate note from the people who handle the roads. It has what was actually requested, what the house can agree to discuss and who can give an answer when they come home.

“If they want a decision today, can you make it?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“For Tempest, some of them. For our house, no. If I hear something useful I'll bring it back.” ᴿⁱ

Mau puts the strap of his bag over his wrist. That is about as much of a handhold as he needs for the ride.

* * *

Kaisel sets them down short of the hall. Mau thanks him and calls him back before they go any closer. There is a place where the road bends around a low wall with a covered bench inside it. Mau takes the map out to see if it is the place Rimuru showed him. It is. From the bench he can see the opening at the far side of the wall. He tells Rimuru that if he's not in the hall, this is where he'll be.

Rimuru takes a look for himself. Then he asks if Mau wants him to come out the first time they get a break.

“Stay as long as you need to,” Mau says. “I'll tell you if I change the plan.” ᴹ

Rimuru nods. He takes the papers out of his bag so he won't have to find them in front of a table full of people.

They walk the last stretch together. Someone at the door reads Rimuru's name and looks past him for the second person. Mau puts his map away and says his own name before the man can decide what else to call him.
