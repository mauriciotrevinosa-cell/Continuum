# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 13 — The Empty Line

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S10E26
**Continuity state:** B+171 morning. Walpurgis asks about responsibility for use of Mau's name. He distinguishes actual road safety from accepting office.

*When: Rimuru sits down, the chair on his left has a name in front of it and the chair on his right has a clean piece of paper.*

Mau takes the one on the right. Guy looks at him over the rim of his glass. Nobody asks him to move.

The map has been weighted at its corners with whatever was at hand. One corner is held down by a brass seal. On the road that goes east from the quarry, a line stops at a fork. Mau knows the fork. If you go too far left there, you end up at a mill where a woman dries washing right across the road.

Luminous sets a folded note on the map.

“Three wagons stopped here last week,” she says. “One was carrying lamp oil. Somebody told the drivers you had forbidden them to go any farther.” ᴸᵘ

“Did they give a reason?” ᴹ

“That you could reach the road before any of them got to the next town.” ᴸᵘ

Mau reads the note. Someone has tried to draw the mark the men were wearing. It looks like a black crown if he holds the paper far enough away.

“Those aren't my people,” he says. “I don't collect a fee at that fork. Neither does the house.” ᴹ

“Then the drivers ought to be able to learn that without coming all the way to your house,” Leon says. ᴸᵉ

Mau sets the note down. That, at least, is something he can bring home. Rimuru uncaps his pen.

“The first wagon didn't stop on account of a seal,” Guy says. “It stopped because the driver had seen you fight.” ᴳᵘ

Mau looks at the three marks on the map. Someone has made them all the same length. The driver who saw the quarry still had to get his oil delivered. Mau tries to imagine how far he would have gone before deciding it was worth finding out if the men were bluffing.

“If it happens again, can they get a message to you without giving their names to the men on the road?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Luminous says yes. She brings the note back to her side and writes down who should receive a report. Mau copies the name while she spells it. Rimuru notes that the house could tell its trading partners where to send the same warning.

They finish that much before anyone touches the paper in front of Mau.

* * *

Leon is the one who turns it around.

There is a place for a seal beneath a line that has already been written. It puts the common roads, the settlement and anyone said to be acting on its behalf under one name. Mau reads to the end and then goes back to the beginning. It is more difficult to see what the form means after he has seen how easy it would have been to fix the thing they were actually talking about.

“Who asked you to put the house on there?” ᴹ

“Your word already carries there,” Leon says. “We want to know when it binds.” ᴸᵉ

“To me. When it's my word.” ᴹ

Guy sets his glass down.

“Would it help if we called you something people could recognize?” ᴳᵘ

“What would you call me?” ᴹ

Nobody answers immediately. Mau looks down at the paper. The first word on the line would fit just as well with or without his name underneath.

Rimuru moves the road note away from the form.

“We have something here that people can use,” he says, tapping the note. “Keep that separate.” ᴿⁱ

Luminous draws the note to her again. She puts her hand over the corner so it stays where Rimuru put it. Then she looks directly at Mau.

“And if they aren't wearing a borrowed crown next time?” ᴸᵘ

He waits for the rest of the question.

“If somebody sends people openly, how much of what happens next is yours to decide?” ᴸᵘ
