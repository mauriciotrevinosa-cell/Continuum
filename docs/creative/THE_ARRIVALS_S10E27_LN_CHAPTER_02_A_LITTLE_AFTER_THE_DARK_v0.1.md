# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 16 — A Little After the Dark

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S10E27
**Continuity state:** Day475 late night, immediately after V34#41 in the original history. Mau wakes in color. Rimuru and Ciel have not consented to the proposed intervention. Mau asks to wake Yuka.

*When: Mau comes to, there is a hand on his shoulder and the floor is close enough to touch.*

He thinks he must have fainted. His knees are against the floor. His cheek is pressed into somebody's sleeve. The sleeve moves when he takes a breath.

“Can you hear me?” ᴿⁱ

Mau nods. He has to do it twice before the hand on his shoulder loosens. He puts one palm flat on the boards and sees the lamplight between his fingers.

It is yellow.

He moves his hand. The light stays yellow in the space it leaves. There is a blue thread at the edge of the sleeve he had been lying on. Farther away a stool is on its side. Its legs are facing the bed, with one hooked into the edge of the blanket.

He knows the stool. He had watched it fall.

“Start with her,” he says. ᴹ

Rimuru doesn't move his hand.

“What?” ᴿⁱ

“That's what you said to me.” ᴹ

“I said a lot of things to you.” ᴿⁱ

Mau turns toward the bed. Yuka's hand is close to her face. There is a blade of grass caught against a seam in the blanket. He knows how long it had been there before he saw it.

He starts to stand. His left hand catches on the floor because two fingers are still half closed. Rimuru puts a palm against his back until he has both feet under him.

*Can you see clearly enough to stand?* ᶜ

He stops. The question is what does it. He remembers exactly when she started asking him that way, giving him enough information to check on his own and waiting for him to answer. There have been years between hearing it like that and being asked it now.

“Ciel.” ᴹ

*Yes.* ᶜ

He waits for another sound behind it. He can hear Yuka breathing. Something ticks when the lamp gets hot. He knows the sound of the road by the hall, the way the loose pebbles had come away one by one. There is no road here.

“Did I say yes?” ᴹ

Rimuru takes his hand off him. Mau can feel the space it leaves.

“You said you accepted. Then the light went out and you fell. Are you asking me because you don't remember?” ᴿⁱ

“No. I remember saying it.” ᴹ

Rimuru waits.

“Did anything happen to her?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“She's asleep. She was asleep when you fell. That's what I can tell you.” ᴿⁱ

Mau puts a hand against the frame of the bed. The woman he left that morning would have said his name if he had been on the floor within reach of her. The woman here is wearing the bracelet. There is no ring. He remembers the last time he looked at that hand without expecting there to be one.

Rimuru bends to pick up the stool. Mau touches his sleeve before he can set it right.

“Wait. You told me to wake her,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I did.” ᴿⁱ

“Will you stay while I do it?” ᴹ

Rimuru looks at him for long enough that Mau has time to put his hand back on the bed. Then he sets the stool on its feet.

“Yes.” ᴿⁱ
