# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 16 — A Little After the Dark

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S10E27
**Continuity state:** Day475 original late night after V34#41. Mau panics at the old room and color, wakes Yuka by accident and searches for the attacker. Original Ciel and Rimuru know only the bargain and the collapse. His wife belongs to the life he came from.

*When: Mau opens his eyes, the first thing he sees is Rimuru's sleeve pressed against his mouth.*

He jerks away hard enough to hit the bed frame.

Rimuru catches his shoulder. Mau gets one foot under himself, misses the floor with the other and shoves at the wood instead. Something falls. He hears it strike twice before he can work out that it is the stool.

“Where is she?” ᴹ

“Mau. Stay there a second.” ᴿⁱ

“The woman. Where did she go?” ᴹ

He gets his hand out from under Rimuru's and looks past him. There ought to be a wall low enough to see over. There ought to be a piece of map catching against it, a woman looking up while the road turns wrong under his feet.

There is a bed.

Yuka is in it.

For a second he thinks he has brought the woman all the way home.

“Yuka.” ᴹ

He turns toward her so fast that his knee catches the stool and sends it under the bed. He reaches the mattress. Her hand is near her face. There is a blade of grass in the blanket seam. He sees the bracelet, the bare finger beside it, and every sound in the room goes thin.

“No.” ᴹ

Rimuru is saying his name. Mau takes Yuka's hand, looks at the finger, turns it over as if the ring might be caught underneath it.

“No. No, where—” ᴹ

Her eyes open.

* * *

“Mau?” ʸ

He lets go as if he has hurt her.

She pulls her hand back and looks at him. Then she looks at Rimuru standing beside the bed.

“What happened?” ʸ

Mau can hear her asking. He can see her looking for an answer and still can't make himself give her one. The lamp shines through the little space between her fingers.

The lamp is yellow.

He looks up.

The blanket is blue. The edge of Rimuru's sleeve is blue too, a deeper shade with a thread that has caught where Mau grabbed it. He had spent a long time remembering the blue without being able to see it when he looked at the same coat.

He puts a hand over his eyes.

When he takes it away, the colors are still there.

He starts for the door.

Rimuru steps into the space beside it.

“Tell me where you're going.” ᴿⁱ

“Outside.” ᴹ

“You fell beside the bed. What are you looking for outside?” ᴿⁱ

Mau reaches for the handle. He gets the door open far enough to see the passage beyond. It has the old turn. The beam above it is too low. He knows the pale mark where somebody once scraped the plaster carrying a cupboard upstairs.

He was the one carrying the cupboard.

He looks down the passage again, trying to make the end of it become a window onto the road. It stays exactly as he remembers it.

“I have to find her.” ᴹ

“Who?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“The thing at the wall.” ᴹ

*Can you hear me?* ᶜ

He goes still.

The voice is close in a way the one at the road had been trying to stay close. He had heard the start of Ciel's answer and then the drop had taken the rest.

*Ciel?* ᴹ

*I can hear you. Tell me what you can see.* ᶜ

He says her name out loud. It comes out wrong, caught between a question and a sound he cannot stop.

Rimuru looks at his face.

“She can hear you,” he says. “She's been talking to you.” ᴿⁱ

Mau looks at him. Rimuru's hand is near the door. He hasn't reached for Mau again. There is a small line between his eyes that Mau remembers from when he was trying to ask one more question without making it an accusation.

The last thing this Rimuru heard was an answer Mau gave anyway.

“Did I say yes?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Rimuru's hand closes.

“Yes.” ᴿⁱ

“When?” ᴹ

“Just now.” ᴿⁱ

Yuka has got out of bed. She is standing behind them in the passage, holding the blanket around her shoulders.

“What did you say yes to?” she asks. ʸ

Mau can't look at her hand again.

* * *

The stairs are where they used to be.

He goes down too quickly and finds himself holding the banister with both hands. There is a lamp on the table below. The common room has the old chairs and the old way through to the kitchen. He can see the place he used to leave his boots when he thought nobody was going to trip over them.

Rimuru comes down behind him.

Mau gets the front door open.

Cold air hits his face. He takes a breath so big it hurts and steps onto the path. He looks left, toward the part of the road where the low wall should be if this is another room built around a lie. He looks right.

The roots have the old circle around them. There is no covered path to the house Yuka lives in now. He can see the line of the roof where he'd sat with Rimuru and Yuta, where there had always been room to make somebody move over.

There is nothing where his window should be.

He goes far enough down the path to see if it is hidden behind the tree.

It isn't.

He says Yuka's name. The girl on the step answers him.

He closes his eyes.

* * *

“Let me look,” he tells Ciel. ᴹ

*For what?* ᶜ

“I don't know. Anything that doesn't belong.” ᴹ

He can feel Noise answer the thought before he has put all of it into words. He asks for the nearest places first. The underside of the step. The turn past the yard. The ground at the tree.

A shadow comes up far enough to see along the path. Another takes the edge of the roof. Mau tries to hold both views and his own at once. He knows how to do this. He has done it on ordinary afternoons while somebody spoke to him about what to buy.

Tonight he keeps looking for the line where the wall of a room has been made to look like the sky.

There isn't one.

Rimuru stops at the bottom of the steps.

“You can tell me what you're doing while you do it.” ᴿⁱ

“I was in a meeting. I left. A woman was outside.” ᴹ

“You were in here.” ᴿⁱ

“I know what you're saying.” ᴹ

“Then look at me when you say you know.” ᴿⁱ

Mau turns. Rimuru has put himself where Mau can see him without looking past Yuka. The blanket is still around her. She has put her feet into her boots and hasn't tied them.

Mau looks at the boots and understands that she followed him downstairs because he was afraid.

“I have to get back,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka's face changes.

Rimuru looks at the open door behind him. Upstairs, someone has woken enough to ask why the front door is standing open.

“Back where?” he asks. ᴿⁱ

Mau tries to answer. All he can see is Yuka at the edge of the ditch, the one who hasn't come for him yet, and the place on the path where his home ought to be.

“To my wife,” he says. ᴹ

The sound from upstairs stops.

The girl in the blanket looks down at the hand he held beside the bed.
