# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 17 — What She Was Going to Do

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S10E28
**Continuity state:** Day475 old night. Original Yuka, Maki, Rimuru and the waking house learn what Mau chose and the life that followed. They voice anger and ask to hear his account. No memories shared yet.

*When: Yuka asks whose wife, Mau has to look at her to answer.*

“You,” he says. ᴹ

She looks at him for a long time.

Her blanket has slipped off one shoulder. She catches it with the hand that doesn't have the bracelet. Mau starts to reach for the loose edge. She gets it herself.

“When?” she asks. ʸ

“I can't make the dates line up from here.” ᴹ

“Try.” ʸ

He looks back at the tree. The shadows he sent out have gone as far as he asked them to. He can feel the first one waiting beside the old turn of the fence.

Nothing is coming down the road. Nothing at all.

“After we built the town,” he says. “After I asked you on the mountain. You said yes. We had time to think about it before we did.” ᴹ

“We?” ʸ

He looks at her.

“I know it's you I'm talking to,” he says. “I know which night this is.” ᴹ

“Then tell me what happened on this night.” ʸ

Rimuru asks him to bring the shadows back before he keeps going. Mau tries to listen to the last view for another second. He wants something to move wrong. A seam. A voice from somewhere it can't belong. He wants the search to give him somewhere else to put his feet.

He brings them back.

The cold seems greater when there is only one pair of eyes looking into it.

* * *

Maki comes down before they close the door.

Yuka sees her and goes straight to her. Maki gets one hand under the blanket at her elbow, then looks past her at Mau.

“What happened?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

Rimuru answers the part he knows. Mau agreed to an alteration of the house's memories. Then he fell. Now he says he has come from a life where that night has already happened.

Maki stares at him.

“You agreed to what?” ᴹᵏ

Mau has had years of remembering that sentence as something he would have to say if he ever got the chance. The words don't get any easier to put in front of the person.

“I asked DIO what could be changed. Then I accepted it.” ᴹ

“Whose memories?” ᴹᵏ

“Ours. Yours. Ciel's. I would have kept what I knew.” ᴹ

Yuka steps away from Maki far enough to see him.

“Mine?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“While I was asleep?” ʸ

He nods.

Yuka looks at Rimuru. It takes him only a moment to answer the question on her face.

“I asked him to wake you.” ᴿⁱ

She turns back.

“You were sitting right next to me.” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Why didn't you?” ʸ

Mau tries to tell her about the meals they had arranged and the rooms that would still have been hers. He hears himself start with the details, the same way he'd done before. Yuka is looking at him as if she can see him putting a little more furniture between them.

He stops.

“I wanted you to say yes to something you hadn't heard,” he says. “I knew you could say no.” ᴹ

Maki pulls out a chair. It makes a rough sound all the way across the floor.

“Sit down.” ᴹᵏ

He does.

* * *

Shoko checks his eyes because the first thing Rimuru told her was that he'd fallen. Mau follows her finger. She asks how many of her hands he can see.

“One.” ᴹ

“What color is the lamp?” ˢʰ

“Yellow.” ᴹ

“Then tell me what happened after you fell.” ˢʰ

He tells her about the flowers.

It is the place he always has to start. Waking with someone beside him and no memory of why he ought to be afraid. The way the house grew. Where the life they shared began to take a different shape. Frieren teaching him and Ori going to her first. Yuka arriving. How long he went on thinking the things he didn't have in that life had never existed.

Maki stays standing while he speaks.

At the part where his body starts to change again, Yuta comes in from the passage. He hears his own name and asks Mau to go back to the start of that bit. Mau tells him about the conversation, the blurred edge, the way Yuta caught him before he reached the floor.

Yuta puts one hand on the back of the chair Maki hasn't used.

“What was I saying to you?” he asks. ʸᵗ

“You were trying to understand why I'd kept my relationship with her from you.” ᴹ

“Again?” ʸᵗ

* * *

Frieren comes to the doorway while Mau is explaining how he got his memories back.

She has put a robe over her night clothes. There is an empty place beside her where Ori would be if she had come down too.

“I told her I would come back upstairs,” Frieren says. “She heard you call.” ᶠ

Mau looks at that empty place. He can't keep looking for more than a second.

“She doesn't need to hear all of this yet,” Maki says. ᴹᵏ

“I know.” ᶠ

Frieren stays near the door. He tells her the rest of that first morning, the things he recognized and the ones that had changed. He tells her there is still a compass. When he gets to who carries it, she holds the edge of the robe tighter.

“You made a different life for us,” Maki says. ᴹᵏ

“I didn't know it would do that. I thought it was going to change what you remembered.” ᴹ

“Which you were willing to do.” ᴹᵏ

“Yes.” ᴹ

He makes himself keep looking at her.

Rimuru asks for the part between that morning and the night Mau left to go to a meeting. Mau starts to tell him about the town, the people who found their families, the rooms and the paths, the gates and the new danger.

He can't put it in order.

One thing has to come before another because somebody hadn't arrived yet. Another thing came from a promise they'd made here, and he can't explain why it mattered without going back again.

Yuka pulls her blanket onto her knees.

“You keep saying we're happy,” she says. “You keep saying we've got a life.” ʸ

“I know that doesn't make what I did—” ᴹ

“I want you to tell me about it.” ʸ

“I was going to ask Maki to come get me for breakfast tomorrow,” she says. “I hadn't told you. I was going to come down even if everybody was already here.” ʸ

“I know how bad it was,” Yuka says. “I was still going to go.” ʸ

“Look at me,” she says. ʸ

“I can be angry with you and still want to know where I got to.” ʸ
