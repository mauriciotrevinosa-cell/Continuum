# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 17 — What She Was Going to Do

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S10E28
**Continuity state:** Day475 late night. Old Yuka learns what Mau agreed to and what came of the failed operation. No romance.

*When: Yuka wakes, the first thing she does is feel for a glass of water.*

Mau puts it where she can reach it. She drinks without opening her eyes all the way. Then she sees Rimuru in the chair and pulls herself up against the wall.

“What's happened?” ʸ

Mau moves the glass out of the way before he sits on the edge of the stool. It takes him a moment to tell her why Rimuru is here. By the time he gets to what DIO offered, Yuka has drawn the blanket up to her chest. She lets it down again, deliberately, when she sees him looking at it.

“Did you think I'd say yes?” ʸ

“I wanted you to be able to come down to breakfast without being afraid. I didn't think through what you might ask me to do instead.” ᴹ

Yuka presses her lips together. She looks toward Rimuru.

“He asked me to wake you,” Mau says. “I heard him and I went ahead.” ᴹ

She sets the glass on the floor where she won't have to keep holding it.

“I was going to ask Maki to come get me for breakfast,” she says. “I hadn't told you. I was going to see if she'd do it even if everybody was already downstairs.” ʸ

Mau sits very still.

“I know how bad it was,” Yuka says. “I was still going to go.” ʸ

He had stood in the place where there would have been a breakfast after this and wondered what he had saved her from. He had never imagined Maki coming upstairs with two plates and asking which of them she wanted.

“I'm sorry,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka looks down at the flower on her bracelet. She turns her wrist until the clasp is back where it belongs.

“Do I have to decide now?” ʸ

“No.” ᴹ

“Then tell me why you're looking at my hand like that.” ʸ

* * *

He had thought it would be difficult to find a first thing to tell her. She makes it easier by asking exactly what she wants to know.

“Did you marry me?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Did I know everything when you asked?” ʸ

“You knew what I could tell you. You knew I'd loved you here. You asked me some things again later because you wanted the answer for yourself.” ᴹ

She nods slowly. Mau tells her that they live above a part of the house that wasn't there when she went to sleep. There is a window they argue about leaving open. The two of them decide they want to have a family someday and then decide that someday can wait. He tells her how nervous he was to say that last part. She asks what she did with her hands when she heard it.

“I think you put one on my arm. You were still making up your mind how you were going to say the same thing.” ᴹ

Yuka puts her hands in her lap. They stay there for a while.

“I wanted to know if I'd ever be able to say that to somebody and have them listen to the whole thing,” she says. ʸ

“I listened.” ᴹ

“I'm glad she got to hear you say it too.” ʸ

The lamp burns low enough that Rimuru gets up to turn the wick. Mau looks down at the boards while he's doing it. Rimuru would have sat in the dark with both of them if there was no more oil.

“I'm happy for me,” Yuka says. “I think I can be happy for me and still wish I could have come downstairs tomorrow.” ʸ

Mau manages a nod. She looks at him for a while before she goes on.

“There's something I want you to tell her.” ʸ
