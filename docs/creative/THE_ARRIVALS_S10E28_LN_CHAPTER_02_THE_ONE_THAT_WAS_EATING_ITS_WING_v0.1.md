# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 22 — The One That Was Eating Its Wing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 22
**Source episode:** S10E28
**Continuity state:** Day475 old night after the collective viewing. Yuka's anger and her choice to let Mau return coexist. She gives her present self a message, remembers private confidence only with Mau, and asks him to share the happy hand dragon. No renewed romance.

*When: Yuka sits on the edge of the bed again, she looks for the glass before she remembers she left it downstairs.*

Mau starts toward the door. She catches his sleeve.

“There's water here.” ʸ

The pitcher on the little shelf is half full. He pours with both hands, because he'd tried it with one downstairs and the rim had knocked against his teeth. She takes the glass from him and drinks.

He stays near the stool.

“You can sit where I can see you,” she says. ʸ

He brings it closer and sits down. The blade of grass is still in the blanket seam. Yuka sees him look at it. She pulls it out and lays it on the sill.

“I was saving that,” she says. ʸ

He looks up.

“To see if you'd notice when we put the blanket away.” ʸ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

“Eventually.” ʸ

He smiles. The smile doesn't last, but she sees it.

For a while the room is no more difficult than the two of them trying to work out where to start. The house makes the sounds it made on nights when some people wanted to sleep and others didn't know how. Footsteps move along the landing. A door closes carefully, then opens again because somebody forgot something on the other side.

Yuka looks at the ring Mau is wearing.

“Did you want to marry me?” she asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Or did you remember what you told me tonight and think you had to get there?” ʸ

He has an answer so quickly that it almost comes out before she's finished. He lets her finish.

“I wanted to marry you. I asked your grandparents because I wanted them to know before I asked you. I was so nervous I kept finding other things to do first.” ᴹ

“Like what?” ʸ

“Training. Trying to understand a gate. Talking about anything that wasn't the ring. Mai had to come get me.” ᴹ

Yuka looks down at her knees. Her mouth changes a little at Mai's name.

“That sounds like you.” ʸ

“She knows you better than that too,” he says. “You caught her and Yuta trying to keep it from you and made them work for it.” ᴹ

Yuka lets out a small laugh. It catches before she can take another breath.

* * *

“I was angry before I knew where you'd been,” she says. “I'm still angry about the thing you chose here.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

“I thought maybe when you told me the rest I wouldn't be. Then you showed me all that, and I was happy, and it was still there.” ʸ

Mau puts his hand flat on his knee. He could try to get her to the part where he thinks he'll know how to answer. He could tell her something beautiful from the wedding and hope it covers the feeling she is making herself say.

“You were right to want me to wake you,” he says. ᴹ

She looks at him.

“I don't need you to stop being angry before I hear that.” ᴹ

Yuka presses the glass between both palms.

“I wanted a whole life,” she says. “I told you that. I didn't mean a life where nobody ever heard me complain.” ʸ

He can see her in the kitchen he showed them, arguing over a door because she'd had time to care whether it opened the right way. The little complaints. The ordinary room she takes up. How much of the way he loves her lives there.

“You get to complain,” he tells her. “Quite a lot, sometimes.” ᴹ

She laughs again. This time she gets the second breath.

Then she puts the glass down.

“I asked you if we were going to have a family,” she says. ʸ

He remembers where her head was on the pillow when she asked. He'd been able to see one eye above the fold in the blanket. He'd told her yes before she shut it.

“I did mean that,” he says. ᴹ

“Give me your hand.” ʸ

He puts it out. She turns it just enough to look at the band.

“You are doing it,” she says. “Look.” ʸ

He looks. He can see where Yuka catches the ring with her thumb when she puts his hand back into his own lap. He waits. She has turned her face toward the door again. He can hear Maki moving a little farther away on the other side.

“I wanted to get there from here,” Yuka says. “I thought I had tomorrow to tell you some more about it.” ʸ

Mau thinks about the breakfast she wanted. He'd been thinking of all the mornings he could show her. There was one she had been expecting to get up for.

He stays with her while she cries about that one.

When she reaches for the glass again he puts it close enough that she can take it herself.

* * *

She asks about the body he came home in. He tells her enough to account for the scars she can see and the years she has heard him leave out. The rest stays where it is until she asks for it.

When she touches her own wrist and asks whether he remembers what she told him before she fell asleep, he says yes.

“Did I tell you there too?” ʸ

“You did. You wanted me to hear it from you.” ᴹ

“Did you let me finish?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Yuka nods. She has no reason to ask the next question again. She saw the wedding with her own eyes.

“Good,” she says. ʸ

Mau looks at her hand, at the little flower on the bracelet. He bought it without knowing the promise it carried. She turned it into something he would spend years learning to answer on purpose.

“I wish I knew how to give you the morning you wanted,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka looks toward the door. Maki is somewhere on the other side, leaving them enough room to talk while staying close enough that Yuka can call her.

“I think you showed me some mornings I wanted,” she says. ʸ

Her eyes fill before she gets to the end.

“I wish I could remember the wedding when you tell me about it,” she says. “But I'm glad I got to see her say yes. She looked like she knew.” ʸ

“She did.” ᴹ

Yuka takes her time wiping her face. She wants to be able to say the next thing without having to try twice.

“Tell her I used to think about what we'd have to get through to have a nice time. Tell her we were already having one.” ʸ

Mau starts looking for paper.

“I don't think you'll forget it,” she says. ʸ

He puts his hand back on his knee.

“And tell her thank you. I used to lie here and try to picture what that would be like. Being married to you and having a day where we could talk about breakfast. I hope she has a lot of those.” ʸ

He nods. He can do that much.

“Show her that thing we did with our hands,” Yuka says. “The one that kept eating its wing. I'd like her to have that part.” ʸ

“I still need somebody to make the wing.” ᴹ

“She can do it.” ʸ

He can hear the noise she'd made for the creature on the mountain. He'd started to keep that afternoon beside so many things he regretted that he'd almost forgotten how hard they'd laughed.

Yuka watches him remember it.

“You can tell her the nice things,” she says. “They happened too.” ʸ

* * *

Yuka calls to Rimuru on the landing and asks him to get Maki. He brings her up without coming into the room.

She takes the place at the edge of the bed. Mau gets the glass out of the way. Before he gets up, Yuka asks to see his hand.

He puts it where she can look at the ring. She doesn't touch the band. She holds his wrist lightly with her thumb below the flower on her own.

“I wanted you to get home when you said you had to,” she says. “I didn't understand what you meant yet.” ʸ

He looks at her.

“I understand now. Go home to me.” ʸ

He tries to answer. All he gets is the start of her name.

She lets his wrist go so he can put his hand over his face.

Maki stays beside Yuka while he leaves the room. At the threshold he hears her ask if Yuka wants the blanket back around her shoulders. Yuka says yes, please, and could she get the other side too.

Mau stands there until he can trust his feet to keep moving.
