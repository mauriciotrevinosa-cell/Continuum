# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 18 — The One That Was Eating Its Wing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S10E28
**Continuity state:** Day475 late night. Old Yuka gives Mau words and permission to share the good. Old Ciel and Rimuru hear what happened without being asked to erase their earlier refusal.

*When: Yuka asks for the message to be passed on, Mau starts looking around for something to write it on.*

“I don't think you'll forget it,” she says. ʸ

He looks back at her. She has rested her right wrist on her knee where the bracelet won't dig into it.

“Tell her I used to think about what we'd have to get through to have a nice time. Tell her we were already having one.” ʸ

Mau nods. He expects her to go on immediately. Instead she looks at the bare finger where he had been looking for a ring.

“And tell her thank you,” she says. “I used to lie here and try to picture what that would be like. Being married to you and having a day where we could talk about breakfast. I hope she has a lot of those.” ʸ

He looks up. She gives him a little nod, as if she is making sure he knows which part to take with him.

Mau can hear the tiny sound she'd made for the dragon. He can hear the noise he'd made trying to do it better. He had started to forget that he was allowed to laugh when he thought of that afternoon.

“Show her that thing we did with our hands,” Yuka says. “The one that kept eating its wing. You can skip the rest if she doesn't want it. I'd like her to have that part.” ʸ

Mau lifts his right hand. He folds his thumb inward by about the same amount as he had on the mountain. With the light this low all he can do is put a piece of the shape across his knee.

“I still need somebody to make the wing,” he says. ᴹ

“She can do it.” ʸ

She doesn't put her hand up to meet his. She sits against the wall and watches him take his time letting the shape go.

Mau asks if she wants him to get somebody before he leaves the room. She asks for Maki, and asks whether she'll be angry at being woken. Rimuru says he can wake Maki himself. Yuka takes another drink and pushes the covers off her legs to make a place for somebody to sit.

* * *

Mau waits just inside the doorway while Rimuru steps out. Then he remembers there is somebody else in the room who still has something to say.

*When I came back, you were there,* he tells Ciel. *You hadn't heard what you said to me here. I took a while to let you hear any of it.* ᴹ

*Did I ask?* ᶜ

*Yes. More than once. Eventually I let you decide what you wanted to see.* ᴹ

He can't tell if that answer helps. He can tell it matters that he stop talking long enough for her to say whether it does.

*What do you want me to do now?* she asks. ᶜ

*I don't know. I didn't know you could ask me that again.* ᴹ

Ciel lets him breathe for a little while.

*You heard me say no before. I need to know you can hear me say that and still tell me what happened next.* ᶜ

He tells her. He says which decision was his and which part was the accident. He tells her about the first memory she'd chosen and the one she'd stopped, then about the day she'd gone back with him and asked to see more. He doesn't try to make that day stand in for an answer from her.

Yuka has settled at the edge of the bed by the time he finishes. She gets up for a second to pass him the glass. He hadn't realised his throat was making a sound when he swallowed. She waits until he's holding it firmly and sits back down.

*I'm glad I was able to ask again,* Ciel says. *I want you to go back to a place where I can keep asking you things.* ᶜ

He keeps both hands around the glass.

*Are you sure?* ᴹ

*I was sure before as well, Mau.* ᶜ

He takes a breath and finds that he can answer this time.

*I know.* ᴹ
