# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 21 — The Life That Came After

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.2
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S10E28
**Continuity state:** Day475 old night. The house views Mau's selected public wedding and ordinary-life memories. Anger and grief coexist with knowing his present. Each listener can stop. Old Ciel assists, original memories stay intact. The house begins to let him return.

*When: the image of the yard ends, Gojo is holding the edge of his chair as if it might be the thing keeping him in the room.*

“Show us something with you in it,” he says. ᴳᵒ

Mau looks up. Gojo has lowered the blindfold over one eye. The other stays on him.

“I am in them.” ᴹ

“Doing something you wanted to do.” ᴳᵒ

Mau thinks of the mountain.

It is the first place that comes, with the light caught on the ring box and Yuka looking at him as if she knows what he is trying to ask and still wants to hear him say it. He starts to reach for the memory. Then he remembers how careful he was that day to let her know who could see them.

“The wedding,” he says. “Everyone was there.” ᴹ

The Yuka at the table takes her hands off the blanket.

“There really was one?” she asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

She draws her knees back under the chair until both feet are flat on the floor.

“Let me see.” ʸ

* * *

There is a sound of music ending.

For a moment they can see only Yuka's hands, in color against the gray of Mau's sleeves. Then he remembers lifting his eyes.

She is standing in front of him.

Her hair is dressed for the day. She has a page in her hand and is looking at it with an expression the girl at the table knows so well that she starts to smile before she means to.

*I wrote something,* his wife says. *And now you're here.*

At the table Yuka puts a hand against her mouth.

The people in the memory laugh softly. The sound comes from all around Mau. He can feel how many of them were close enough that he could have turned and looked at them. He stays with the woman who is speaking.

She tells him about the blankets and the cups. She tells him that there are things she wants him to want for himself.

The original Maki is looking at her granddaughter. She doesn't turn when Yuta's hand finds hers under the table. He puts it there awkwardly, from the wrong side of his chair, and leaves it there.

*There are days you carry which I didn't live,* Yuka says in the image. *I'm choosing the life we're making here.*

Mau stops remembering the next word.

He is back at the old table with his throat tight and the wedding gone. The room is waiting for him to find it again.

“I'm sorry,” he says. ᴹ

“For what?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“It's a lot.” ᴹ

She wipes the side of her face with the heel of her hand.

“I want the rest of it.” ʸ

He finds the moment by the way her thumb moved against his hand.

His wife calls him her husband.

The girl watching her lets out a breath she has been holding so long that the sound hurts to hear.

* * *

He gives them a few more pieces of the day.

Mai bringing the rings. Yuta's hand on his shoulder before he turned to Yuka. The first glance toward the people who came, because neither of them could believe how many faces were there.

Rimuru sees himself standing where somebody asked him to stand.

He leans forward and asks Mau to hold the next moment. There is a young woman beside the children. She has turned toward somebody who speaks from outside Mau's view.

“Who is that?” Rimuru asks. ᴿⁱ

Mau looks at him. He had meant to tell him privately. He had meant so many things to happen in an order he could control.

“Shizue,” he says. ᴹ

The room goes quiet around Rimuru.

He looks at the image. There are things Mau could tell him before he asks. Why she looks that age. What she remembers. Whether she has the spirit of fire with her. He could fill the whole room with the explanation and spare Rimuru having to say her name again.

He stays quiet.

“She came?” Rimuru says. ᴿⁱ

“You asked for her.” ᴹ

“Did she know me?” ᴿⁱ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Rimuru has both hands on the table. He takes one off it and covers his mouth. For a long time, nobody moves. Rem holds the pitcher where it is. Kaneki keeps his eyes on the edge of the image so Rimuru has somewhere he isn't being watched.

Mau lets the memory end when Rimuru nods.

* * *

Shoko asks for a glass of water. She gives the first one to Mau and pours another for herself.

“Did you get out of the business of coming home in pieces?” she asks. ˢʰ

He looks into the glass.

“No.” ᴹ

She sits down. Her face has changed, and he knows the look from the next life as well as this one.

“Did we learn how to get you home?” ˢʰ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Did you let us?” ˢʰ

Mau sets the glass down before he can spill it.

“More than I used to.” ᴹ

Gojo makes a small sound. Shoko looks at him, then back at Mau. She says she'd like that answer to keep getting better.

He nods.

Fern asks what she does in the town. Mau tells her about the accounts, the letters people bring, the things she lets somebody else handle when she wants to be with Stark. He can show her a meal where she took a seat and stayed in it. She chooses to see that part. When it ends, she looks at the empty plates on the old table and says somebody ought to move those before they spend the whole night talking around them.

Maki still hasn't let go of Yuta's hand.

* * *

“What happens to us when you go?” Yuta asks. ʸᵗ

Mau has asked himself that every time he tried to picture this room.

“I don't know what it'll feel like from here,” he says. “DIO told me this life became the past of that one. I went back to the flowers. All the way through. Then I came back with both.” ᴹ

“And you remembered us,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“All this time?” ʸᵗ

“Every day.” ᴹ

Mau has kept the answer small for years because people have other things to do than stand in a room he lost. Now Yuta hears it and puts his head down. Maki turns her hand over under his.

Yuka looks at them. Then at Frieren beside the wall.

“I don't want him to stay here because we're afraid to be the ones who tell him to go,” she says. ʸ

Maki turns toward her.

“You don't have to answer that tonight.” ᴹᵏ

“I know. I'm answering the part I know.” ʸ

Mau starts to say her name. She raises one hand. He stops.

“I saw where I got to,” she says. “I want him to get back there.” ʸ

Maki closes her eyes. When she opens them, her anger is still there. He can see it, and can see how much effort it takes her to put it beside the other things she knows.

“I wanted you to hear us before you decided,” she tells him. “I still wish you'd done that.” ᴹᵏ

“I do too.” ᴹ

“Then remember hearing us this time.” ᴹᵏ

He looks around the room. None of them is easy to leave. There is no place in his memory where he will be able to put tonight down as something which didn't hurt.

“I will.” ᴹ

Upstairs, Ori calls for her mother.

Frieren stands so quickly that the chair catches the wall. She looks at Mau and says she wants to tell Ori enough that she can decide whether to come down. Then she goes.

Yuka gets up more slowly. She asks Mau to come back upstairs for a few minutes. There's something she wants to give him to take with the rest.

It takes him two tries to stand.

Rimuru comes around the table and puts his hand on Mau's shoulder, in exactly the place it had been when the room went black.

This time he leaves it there while Mau gets his feet under him.
