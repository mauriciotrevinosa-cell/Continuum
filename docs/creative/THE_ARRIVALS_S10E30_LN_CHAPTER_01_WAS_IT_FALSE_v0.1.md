# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 21 — Was It False

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S10E30
**Continuity state:** Day475 late night. Original Frieren hears how the current Frieren and the house live. She takes responsibility for what she did to Yuka and asks about their love.

*When: Frieren opens the door, Ori lets go of Mau's hand so she can reach for both of hers.*

“I asked him,” she says. ᴼ

Frieren looks at Mau. He nods. Ori tells her about the two windows and the dragon, and then about the paper. She nearly tells her what she wrote. She puts one finger across her mouth to stop herself and looks so pleased to have remembered that Frieren smiles.

Mau sees her smile at Ori before she looks up at him again. It is the first familiar thing in this room that doesn't seem to hurt.

Ori wants to go with Rimuru to see whether there's still hot water downstairs. Frieren asks if she can bear to wait while they talk. Ori says yes and asks her to leave the door open a little. Frieren puts a folded cloth under it to keep it from swinging shut.

There is a book on the chair where Mau could sit. He leaves it there. Frieren sits at the foot of the bed and asks why he looks older when he thinks nobody is looking.

“Because I am,” he says. ᴹ

She draws one leg up beside her and waits.

* * *

Mau tells her that she lives with Ori. He starts to tell her which rooms are closest to theirs. He loses the order halfway around the courtyard and explains instead that he can hear the two of them arguing over which books count as lessons if he comes down early enough.

“Who wins?” Frieren asks. ᶠ

“Ori gets through a fair number of things you tell her are too difficult by pretending she only wants to look at the pictures.” ᴹ

Frieren has heard of this method. She smiles briefly and looks at her hands.

“And Yuka?” she asks. ᶠ

Mau tells her.

Frieren is quiet for a long time. He has enough time to wonder whether the cloth under the door will hold. He isn't looking at her when she finally speaks.

“Does she have to see me at breakfast?” ᶠ

“She sees you there if she wants to. You live in the same place. You can both walk into a room and decide who you'd like to sit with.” ᴹ

“Did she forgive me?” ᶠ

Mau sits down beside the door. It leaves both of them room to look out into the passage.

“She didn't have to forgive you there. What happened here belongs to the people it happened to.” ᴹ

Frieren takes that in. From the room farther along the passage, there is a noise like a cup being set down.

“I wanted to hurt her. I had time to stop and I didn't.” ᶠ

“I know.” ᴹ

“Can I speak to her now?” ᶠ

“You can ask if she wants to speak to you. Maki's with her. Let her have that for a while.” ᴹ

Frieren nods. She puts both feet on the floor. She doesn't get up.

“You're happy,” she says. ᶠ

Mau wants to be careful with that answer. He looks at her and realises she has been trying to ask for it since the door opened.

“Yes.” ᴹ

She takes a breath. Her foot moves against a seam in the boards and comes back to the same place.

“I keep hearing about a life where we never became what we were to each other,” she says. “Was it false?” ᶠ

Mau turns all the way toward her.  The house has grown quiet enough that he can hear what it costs her to keep waiting for him to answer.
