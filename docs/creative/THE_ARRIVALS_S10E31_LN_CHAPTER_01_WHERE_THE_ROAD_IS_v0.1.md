# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 63
## Chapter 23 — Where the Road Is

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 63
**Reading order:** 23
**Source episode:** S10E31
**Continuity state:** B+171 late morning. Mau returns physically at the roadside. Gray except Yuka. Shoko check arranged; no new combat or departure from Walpurgis by compulsion.

*When: Mau puts his foot down, his boot slides along the side of a ditch and he lands on his hip.*

It is shallow. There is grass between him and the road and a strip of paper caught on a stone. He can see how light it is. He cannot see what color the person who drew the roads used on the back.

Somebody says his name. He leaves both hands on the ground. He has to hear it again before he can work out that this time it is coming from above and to his left.

*Are you on the ground?* ᶜ

He puts one palm on the bottom of the ditch.

“Yes.” ᴹ

Ciel is quiet for the length of one full breath. Then she asks if he can move his toes. He does it on both sides. It takes no time at all to do and longer to tell her that he has done it.

There are footsteps on the road. Mau doesn't stand. He puts his hand on his pocket. Something inside it makes the sound of folded paper.

Rimuru stops on the other side of the ditch. Mau can see the long bag at his feet. Rimuru stays there while Mau looks at it.

“You went back in,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I stayed in. Then Ciel said she lost you.” ᴿⁱ

“How long?” ᴹ

“Less than half an hour. I called home when I got to the bench. Yuka knew the turn. She rode Kaisel out and took him along the wall while I checked the ditch.” ᴿⁱ

Mau listens. Somewhere behind the low wall there is a steady sound, a little louder than the wind. He recognizes Kaisel's breathing. Rimuru has got his bag out of the road, where the dragon can wait without being in anybody's way.

Mau hears the end of the sentence when he sees her. Her sleeve catches the light on the wall where the road turns. He looks at her sleeve, then at her hand, and then at the grass beside his knee, which has gone back to being exactly as gray as it had been on the walk out that morning.

Yuka comes down into the ditch with him. She has to put a hand against the slope when her foot slips. Then she sits down so he doesn't have to get any farther off the ground to see her.

“What hurts?” she says. ʸ

“My hip. I think I landed on it.” ᴹ

She looks at him a little longer.

“And what else?” ʸ

Mau puts his hand in hers. For a moment he doesn't say anything. Rimuru picks up his bag and stays where he is.

“I went somewhere I didn't know I could go,” Mau says. “I was trying to figure out if I could come back.” ᴹ

Yuka turns her hand over so his fingers can lie flat. She asks whether he can tell her the rest once he's somewhere he can sit without a root in his back.

He looks down and finds the root. He laughs a little. It hurts the side he fell on, but the laugh keeps going anyway, just long enough for him to have to lean closer to her.

“I think so,” he says. ᴹ

She puts her free hand against the side of his neck.

“Then we'll get you home.” ʸ
