# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 1 — One Thing in His Pocket

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S10E31
**Continuity state:** B+171 midday. Shoko checks Mau after the fall. He tells the people who need to know what happened on the road and what he can personally verify.

*When: Shoko asks Mau to empty his pockets, he puts the folded paper down on the bench with his palm still on it.*

She looks at his hand. Then she moves the basin far enough away that he can leave the paper where it is.

“Anything sharp?” she asks. ˢʰ

“No.” ᴹ

“Then you can keep it there.” ˢʰ

Yuka takes Mau's coat while Shoko examines his hip. The examination is quick enough that he doesn't get cold until he has to stand and wait for Shoko to put his weight on the sore side. He takes two steps. On the second he has to think about where his foot is. He tells her which part hurts instead of taking the step a third time to make it look better.

Ciel can account for the time after she heard him at the wall. She was able to tell Rimuru which direction to take before she lost the connection completely. There was a tug before it broke, as if the place she was speaking into had stopped facing her. She says that part twice when Mau asks. He waits for the distinction between what she saw and what it felt like.

“Was there anybody else at the wall when you got there?” he asks Rimuru. ᴹ

“No. There were loose stones in the road. I could see where you'd been holding on.” ᴿⁱ

Mau describes the woman as far as he remembers seeing her. At the seam in her throat he stops. Rimuru waits with his pen over the page until Mau says that's the end of what he can tell him about her.

“Do you think she sent you there on purpose?” Rimuru asks. ᴿⁱ

“I think she knew what I was afraid to see. I don't think she knew where I'd land.” ᴹ

Rimuru writes that down as Mau's guess. He can send word about the road and ask people to take another way until they can check it together. Yuka asks him to tell Mai she has the latch measurements on the kitchen table and will be back later for the second door. She says it while she puts Mau's coat on the hook closest to the bench.

* * *

When it's quiet again, Mau lifts his hand off the paper.

“I was there,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka comes over from the hook. Shoko has moved on to washing her hands. At the sound of Mau's voice she turns the water down so he doesn't have to raise it.

“In the room from the old life. Right after the light went out.” ᴹ

Yuka pulls the other end of the bench toward herself before she sits. Her knee touches his where there's no bruise.

“Do you want to tell me now?” she asks. ʸ

“I want to tell you while I still know which things I want to say first.” ᴹ

Shoko dries her hands. She tells Mau to stay where he is until he can get up without having to take a breath first. Then she lets herself out. Yuka moves the basin a little farther from the paper before she takes his hand.

Mau had thought he would start with the dark. Instead he tells her how yellow the lamplight was when he opened his eyes.
