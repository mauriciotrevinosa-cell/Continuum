# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 2 — Room for the Wing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S10E32
**Continuity state:** B+171 afternoon. Mau tells Yuka about the old night and gives her the message. They make a hand dragon together, freely and in the present.

*When: Mau tells Yuka there was a glass beside the bed, she asks which one.*

He describes the bottom of it. A ring in the glass thick enough to feel when you ran a finger across it. She knows the one. It used to come upstairs when somebody wanted a glass that wouldn't crack if they put it down hard in the dark.

“So I was there,” she says. ʸ

“Yes. You were thirsty.” ᴹ

Yuka looks down at the hand she has over his. She asks whether he'd told her the truth about the decision. He tells her he did, and what she had said about breakfast. He had expected Yuka to ask how the two of them had said goodbye. Instead she asks if Maki came upstairs.

“Rimuru got her. She sat down with you.” ᴹ

Yuka breathes out. She moves her thumb once across the back of his hand.

“I wish I could remember what she said.” ʸ

“I wasn't in the room for it.” ᴹ

“Good. I had to be able to have some conversations without you even there.” ʸ

Mau looks up. Yuka has a little bit of a smile on her face. He takes his time smiling back.

“She asked about you,” he says. “She wanted to know if you knew what you were doing when you married me.” ᴹ

“I did. She can ask me again if she likes.” ʸ

Mau tells her what the answer had meant to the woman who had asked. Then he gives her the message exactly as he heard it. Yuka keeps looking at him after he finishes.

“I want to know what we were doing,” she says. ʸ

“She said I could show you if you wanted. There's an afternoon I think she'd want you to see first.” ᴹ

“Will you show me without putting it in my head?” ʸ

Mau looks up at the window. The light falling between them is wide enough for a hand.

* * *

Yuka has to lean farther across him to see the shape on the wall.

“That's not a dragon.” ʸ

“This is the mouth. We haven't made the rest of it.” ᴹ

“You've never made shadows like this with me.” ʸ

“I think I was mostly working up to asking if you'd spend the rest of your life with me.” ᴹ

“You could have opened with the dragon. I can't promise it would have helped.” ʸ

Mau tries to keep his thumb in position while he laughs. He fails. Yuka makes him show her where the wing goes so the creature will still have it the next time he tries.

She holds her hand too high on the first pass and too close on the second. On the third, the wing belongs to it. Mau makes the sound he'd made that afternoon, or the closest he can get to it with her looking straight at him instead of at the wall.

Yuka laughs so hard that the wing goes flying over his head. He lowers his hand and puts it around her waist. She lets herself come the rest of the way across the bench.

“Can we do that again?” she asks. ʸ

He puts his hand back into the light.

Yuka gives the dragon a second wing, a little crooked. Mau shifts his wrist to leave enough room for it. She rests her head against him and for a while the two of them let it stay exactly where it is.
