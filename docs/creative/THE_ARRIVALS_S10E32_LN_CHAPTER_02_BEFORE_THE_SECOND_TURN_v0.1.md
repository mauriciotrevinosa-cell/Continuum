# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 3 — Before the Second Turn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S10E32
**Continuity state:** B+171 later afternoon. Ori chooses a quiet local flight with Mau on Kaisel. No dungeon or second physical recovery arc.

*When: Ori finds Mau at the edge of the yard, she asks why he is watching Kaisel's shadow instead of Kaisel.*

Mau looks up. Kaisel is lying where the ground is warm with one wing stretched far enough that the shadow reaches the path.

“I'm trying to work out how much of the house you can see from where his wing ends.” ᴹ

Ori takes three steps to the left and squints upward.

“You can't see the top from here. You have to go up.” ᴼ

“Would you like to?” ᴹ

She turns around. There's a question in her face, so he leaves her time to decide how to ask it.

“Is somebody going to give us another job when we get up there?” ᴼ

“Just me and you. We can go round the tree and come down. Or you can take me somewhere else close by if you like.” ᴹ

Ori considers whether a trip that short deserves to be called a trip. She decides it does, provided Kaisel doesn't mind being asked to go up for almost no time at all. Mau tells her to ask him. Kaisel lifts his head before Ori puts her hand out.

They agree to go after Ori has told Frieren where they are going and Mau has told Shoko he'll be sitting down for the entire thing. Shoko says she'd like to see him manage that for once. He can hear her laughing a little when he promises her he'll be very good at it.

* * *

Kaisel doesn't turn until Ori asks.

She finds her window first. When she tries to find Frieren's, one is hidden by the angle of the roof. Mau waits while she points straight ahead, then a little to the left. Kaisel goes left, slowly enough that she has time to see where the roof starts to leave the other window clear.

“Stay there a second,” she says. ᴼ

The wings beat. From here Mau can see the path from his door, where it passes under one of the windows and bends to come out on the other side. When he was sitting in the passage looking at the drawing, he'd been so glad she was there that he hadn't thought to ask what it was about the two windows that mattered.

“If I leave my door open and Mum leaves hers open, we can hear each other,” Ori says. “I wanted to see if there was a place you could see them both too.” ᴼ

“Can you?” ᴹ

She moves her head a little closer to his arm and points to both in turn.

“Yes. And I can see yours.” ᴼ

He looks where she points. She found it before he asked.

They stay there long enough for Ori to notice that she can see a corner of the Fort as well. When she asks to go round again, Mau turns to make sure the dragon can hear her. Kaisel is already beginning the turn.
