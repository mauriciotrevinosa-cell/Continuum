# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 4 — Yes, Later

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S10E33
**Continuity state:** B+171 evening and night. Mau leaves Sukuna's archive closed by choice. Ori decides to receive her paper later and leaves it folded. Mau sleeps well beside Yuka and the familiar lift carries them both briefly.

*When: Mau tells Frieren that Ori found both windows, Frieren asks whether it was before or after she put a light in hers.*

“Before,” he says. ᴹ

“Then she really does know which one it is.” ᶠ

Frieren is keeping her finger between two pages. She lifts her other hand to turn the book face down. Mau can still see the finger in it.

He tells her why he asked Ori about the flight. He tells her that the first Ori wanted to show him the same thing when there was only the old roof to look at. At the part about the paper, he takes it out and puts it where Frieren could touch it if she wanted to.

“Has anybody read it?” she asks. ᶠ

“No. I watched her write a bit of it with her hand over the paper. She told me Ori could decide when she looked.” ᴹ

Frieren keeps looking at the fold. She doesn't move her hand off the book.

“What did I say?” she asks after a while. ᶠ

Mau hadn't been planning to ask her to listen to that part. He tells her what he can say without asking her to answer for the woman he spoke to. He tells her about the book on the chair and the thing he told her about having time to read a page.

Frieren looks down. She loosens the hand holding her place in the book. The pages come apart on their own and she finds the place again.

“I think I'd have wanted to hear that too,” she says. ᶠ

Mau lets her look for as long as she likes.

“Is it all right if I offer Ori the paper?” ᴹ

“I think you can tell her you have it. Whether she reads it is her decision.” ᶠ

He folds his hand around the paper and puts it back in his pocket.

“Will you be there if she asks for you?” ᴹ

“Of course.” ᶠ

Frieren picks her book up. Mau can see the place where her finger was. He's thought about asking her a question since the first time she told him they'd go slowly with what he'd brought back from the quarry.

“About Sukuna's memories,” he says. “I know you said you'd help me with them.” ᴹ

She looks up.

“I want to leave the archive closed for now,” he says. ᴹ

“All right.” ᶠ

He had brought a reason in case she asked. He finds that he can leave that where it is too.

* * *

He finds Ori on the steps with a pencil across both knees. She has been trying to draw the turn they made around the house. Mau tells her she can ask him to hold the corner of the page down.

She lets him. She redraws the turn a little wider.

When he tells her about the folded paper, she puts the pencil down where it can't roll off. She asks if there's something inside it that he thinks she has to hear.

“No. You can ask me for it whenever you want. You can leave it alone too.” ᴹ

Ori looks at his pocket, then at the drawing between them.

“Yes,” she says. “I want it. But not now.” ᴼ

Mau nods. She makes sure he has understood that she means she'll ask for it later. He promises to keep it folded until then. He doesn't reach for it.

Ori picks her pencil back up and puts a small mark beside the line she's drawn around the house.

“Which window's that?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Yours. I want to remember where we were when I saw it.” ᴼ

He holds the corner until she's done.

* * *

That night, Mau puts Ori's paper away before he gets into bed. Yuka lifts the blanket with one foot so he can get his knee underneath it. She tells him his foot is cold after he's already put it against hers.

“I can move it,” he says. ᴹ

“You can get it warm first.” ʸ

He does. She slides nearer and he puts his right arm around her. He's had a whole day of looking at things he thought he'd never see again. He'd spent so long wondering what he'd say if he got that chance. When he thinks of the room now, he can think of Frieren telling him to go home and of actually getting to put his foot down. All of it led him somewhere he wanted to be. Yuka's foot is still tucked against his when he falls asleep.

* * *

Yuka wakes when the blanket slips off her shoulder. She reaches back to pull it up and her elbow goes farther than she expects. She can see the dip in the pillow below her, just far enough away that she could touch it if Mau let her turn. He has his arm round her waist. They're both above the bed.

She starts to say his name and stops. He's asleep with the beginning of a smile against the back of her shoulder. She knows that lift. He's done it before on a good night. This time he seems to have decided, in his sleep, that she ought to come too.

“Mau,” she whispers. ʸ

He opens his eyes.

“You took me with you,” she says. ʸ

He looks down. They settle into the mattress slowly enough that she feels his laugh before she hears it.

“I was having a good dream,” he says. ᴹ

“About what?” ʸ

He thinks for a second. He can remember how it felt to reach for her and have her right there.

“You were here.” ᴹ

Yuka turns in his arm. “I'm still here. Give me my half of the blanket.” ʸ

He pulls it over both of them. She fits her forehead under his chin and tells him he can keep his arm where it is. He does. By the time she's got her feet warm again, he's asleep.
