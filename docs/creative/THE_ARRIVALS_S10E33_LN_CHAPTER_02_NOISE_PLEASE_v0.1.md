# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 5 — Noise, Please

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S10E33
**Continuity state:** B+172 morning. Noise makes his own choice of name. No new manifestation or upgrade.

*When: Mau goes to look for DIO, he finds her trying to persuade Noise that a list of names is still a list even if none of them has a mark beside it.*

*There can be one mark when you've decided,* she says. ᴰ

*I decided,* Noise says. ᴺ

DIO looks down at the list. She has gone to the trouble of spacing the names so somebody can make quite a large mark without touching another one. At the bottom there's a blank line. Mau had left it for whatever Noise came up with when nobody was waiting for him to think of something.

*Do you want to write it?* Mau asks. ᴹ

There is a pause.

*You can,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau takes the pencil. He holds it above the blank line. In front of him the part of the room that usually only changes when somebody comes in has gone very still.

*Noise,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau thinks he has been called. He looks up.

*That's it,* Noise says. *I want that one.* ᴺ

DIO lowers the edge of the paper. Her thumb settles at the bottom where there is still room to write.

*What about the other ones?* Mau asks. ᴹ

*Some of them were good.* ᴺ

*We could have come up with more,* DIO says. ᴰ

Noise doesn't say anything to that. Mau takes enough time to write the first letter the size Noise deserves. The pencil catches on the paper twice. He keeps going.

*When you call that, I know it's me you want,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau finishes the last letter. He holds it up a little so Noise can see he hasn't put anything before it.

*There,* Mau says. *Noise.* ᴹ

*Yes?* ᴺ

Mau hadn't meant to ask for anything. He sits down anyway. DIO leaves the names where they can all see them and comes to sit with him.

*Will you stay for a while?* Mau says. ᴹ

*Yes.* ᴺ

* * *

Out in the kitchen, Yuka has found an extra mug on the table. She pushes it across to Mau when he takes his place.

“Is this one mine?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes. Somebody made me put it at the end because you use your left hand for the other things.” ʸ

Mau turns the mug so he can get to the handle without closing the two fingers that won't close. Nobody at the table says who it was.

*Noise,* he says quietly to himself. ᴹ

*Yes?* ᴺ

*Thank you.* ᴹ

He can feel Noise stay long enough to hear the whole thing.
