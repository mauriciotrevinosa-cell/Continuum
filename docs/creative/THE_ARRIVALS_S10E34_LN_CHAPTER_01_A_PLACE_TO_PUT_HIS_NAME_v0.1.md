# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 6 — A Place to Put His Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S10E34
**Continuity state:** B+172 morning. King Edran of Vey receives Makima's prompting to target Hollis. He signs his own detention order.

*When: the king finishes reading the copy of the road complaint, he puts it back in front of the woman with the red hair.*

“Three drivers I buy oil from,” he says. “One of them left a letter with my clerk at dusk. She wants the flour shed on the crossing to take complaints from her drivers.” ᴱᵈ

He knows the shed. The same wagon used to go past it when it was time to pay him for another year. He can see the turn where it would have to take the other road.

“Did he ask you first?” Makima asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

King Edran looks at her. He hadn't needed the man to ask him before. The man paid the same rate every year and Edran's men saw him and his wagons through to the lower market. It was a simple arrangement. All he had to do to keep it was be there when there was trouble.

“He should have brought it here.” ᴱᵈ

Makima slides the complaint into the open space between them. She has turned it so he can see the mark the men wore. He has seen that kind of crown chalked on a wagon twice this month. The second time, his men didn't dare ask the driver what it meant.

“Hollis will be at the crossing in the afternoon,” she says. “He can tell you who pays for a wagon that comes in under this sign.” ᴹᵃᵏ

“Does it belong to him?” ᴱᵈ

“I didn't say that. You can ask him yourself.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Edran looks out the narrow window. He can see part of the lower street. At the top of the steps there's already a man waiting with a bucket to get water from the yard. The man puts it down when he has to stand aside for somebody coming the other way.

“They have a right to turn down a load before they accept it,” Edran says. ᴱᵈ

“They can turn down anything you send, then.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He hears the way it sounds when she says it. In the window he can still see the handle of the bucket.

“I can send for Hollis,” he says. “A man who wants to keep trading can spare an hour.” ᴱᵈ

“And if he'd rather leave?” ᴹᵃᵏ

Edran takes a blank sheet off the end of the table. There is a sentence his clerk usually writes for him that allows men on the road to carry out what is asked of them even after the man they've stopped has been told he can go. He knows how to write it himself.

Makima stays until he has brought the seal down on the paper. She looks at the impression while it's still soft. He puts his hand over it because he can see she is about to reach for the sheet.

“That's mine,” he says. ᴱᵈ

“Of course.” ᴹᵃᵏ

She picks up the first complaint and leaves the order in front of him.

The king calls a clerk in after she's gone. He tells him how many men to send and what to do if Hollis refuses to sign. The clerk asks which place to bring him back to. Edran looks down at the seal and tells him to bring Hollis to the castle.
