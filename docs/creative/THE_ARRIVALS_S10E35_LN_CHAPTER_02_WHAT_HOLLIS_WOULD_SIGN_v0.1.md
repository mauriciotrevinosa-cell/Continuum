# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 9 — What Hollis Would Sign

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S10E35
**Continuity state:** B+172 late afternoon. Igris shields the withdrawal. Hollis rides home, gives his account, asks that the route and ordinary business be protected.

*When: Hollis steps onto the sacks, Igris comes out of the shadow at the foot of the wagon.*

The guard at the back turns. His sword comes half out of its sheath. Igris stands where he would have to put his foot to get down. He lets him finish drawing it. The man looks at the distance between himself and the road and calls for the other guard instead.

Mau keeps walking with Hollis. There is a low place beside the road where a ditch has run dry. Hollis takes it without being asked and comes up on the other side far enough ahead that he has to look back for Mau.

Kaisel has flattened himself behind the rise. Rimuru puts his arms out and waits for Hollis to tell him which one to take. Hollis gives him the good one. Mau stays low until they are all on. The last thing Hollis sees of the road is the driver standing clear of both guards, holding his hat against the wind from Kaisel's wings.

* * *

When they get home, the tally keeper is waiting by the door. She starts to tell Hollis that she left the key with the driver.

“He'll know what to do with it,” Hollis says. “Sit down a minute. I can wait for Shoko.” ᴴᵒ

Shoko has already come in behind him. She moves a chair across with her foot and has him put both arms on the table so she can see the difference between them. By the time she wraps the wrist, Hollis has had enough time to listen to what the tally keeper wrote and to ask for a pen.

He writes his account under hers. His handwriting is awkward. He goes back over his name at the end so nobody will have to ask him if the last letters are really there.

Fern takes the two pages to make copies. Rimuru asks if there is anything else Hollis wants on the first letter to Edran.

“Yes. Put in that I'm willing to answer the question about the road. If he wants to ask me, he can come to the counter in daylight.” ᴴᵒ

Mau is standing beside the table. Hollis looks up at him.

“You don't have to go back there tomorrow,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I want to go back when the people I pay to open can do it without watching the road the whole time. Ask him if he's going to let them.” ᴴᵒ

Mau waits while Rimuru writes it down. He can see that Hollis is still holding the pen in the hand that hurts.

“Anything else?” Rimuru asks. ᴿⁱ

Hollis puts the pen down on top of the paper he signed.

“If he agrees, I still want him to say why he did it. I didn't lose a morning because a guard got confused.” ᴴᵒ

Fern waits for Rimuru to finish writing. She takes the page back upstairs with the others. Shoko lets Hollis rest his hand on the table while the ink dries.
