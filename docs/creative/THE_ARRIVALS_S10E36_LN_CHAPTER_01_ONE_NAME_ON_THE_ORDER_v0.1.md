# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 10 — One Name on the Order

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S10E36
**Continuity state:** B+172 evening. Edran refuses to withdraw the detention order and renews it against Hollis and any substitute. The house conditions its response on civilian safety.

*When: Rimuru reads the answer out, Hollis asks him to start again at the part where the king says what he'll do to the next person.*

Rimuru does. Anyone taking Hollis's place at the counter is to be held to the same order. There is no answer to the invitation to come in daylight. Edran has signed this page himself, too.

Fern sets the first accounts alongside it. She hasn't put the two seals on top of each other. She leaves them far enough apart that anybody in the room can see which paper came first.

Mau asks Hollis if he wants to be there for the rest of the discussion. Hollis pulls his chair a little closer to the table.

“Yes. Don't go back to saying what happened to me after I leave the room.” ᴴᵒ

Mau pulls out the chair beside Yuka. He sits down before he says what he came to say.

“I told the people at Walpurgis that I'd make sure there was no trace if somebody raised a hand against the house. If I can get everybody out, I can take down the castle he sends his orders from. I can keep the stone and bring it back if he answers for what he did.” ᴹ

Somebody at the end of the table starts to say something. Mau looks that way and waits.

“Do you know who lives in there?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“No. I know where his men took the wagon. That's about it.” ᴹ

“Then what does everybody out mean?” ᴿᵉ

Mau looks at the map. The old road he knows is there. The little houses along the side of the hill aren't marked.

“It means I have to find out who uses it and where they go first.” ᴹ

Yuka turns the answer from Edran so she can read it the right way round.

“I don't want somebody who works in his kitchen to have to come apologize so her children can drink water,” she says. ʸ

Hollis looks down at the wrap on his wrist.

“There's a well in the first yard. I've bought water there.” ᴴᵒ

“Leave that,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Find out what else they use,” Fern says. “And if you can't find someplace safe to put a person, that person's home stays where it is.” ᶠᵉ

Mau nods. He looks around the table again. A few people have started writing. Gojo hasn't said anything yet. When Mau looks at him, he asks whether Edran gets a way out too.

“Yes. I want him there to read it.” ᴹ

Gojo leans back. He takes his hand away from his mouth.

“I can help people down the hill. If this turns into getting people out at swordpoint, I'm stopping you.” ᴳᵒ

Mau gives him the answer before the room can decide it was meant as a joke.

“Please do.” ᴹ

Hollis picks up the second order with his good hand. He looks at what Edran has written about his replacement. Then he slides it toward Mau.

“I'll help you find the people who use the lower market,” he says. “I know who to ask without sending a stranger to knock.” ᴴᵒ

Mau reaches for the order. Hollis leaves two fingers on it. He hasn't finished.

“There's a woman who bakes in that yard. If you send a shadow to her door at this hour, she'll think you're there for her. She won't hear another word you say.” ᴴᵒ

Mau lets his hand go flat on the table.

“When would she hear you?” ᴹ

“When she's lighting the oven. I can get there before she puts the first bread in. She knows me well enough to tell me to get out of her way.” ᴴᵒ

Fern takes the map and puts it over the order. Hollis shows her the street he'll take. Mau recognizes one of the turns from the view he had from Kaisel's back. It's closer to the wall than he had thought.

“I'll come if she'll let me,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

Hollis looks across at her. “Can you carry a basket?”

“Yes.” ᴿᵉ

“Then bring one. She'll give you something to do while you talk.” ᴴᵒ

Rimuru sets a fresh page beside the map. There is time to send a warning to the people scheduled at the crossing before any of them gets up for work. He starts with the tally keeper. Fern sends somebody to find out whether she has eaten.

Shoko comes back for the cup she left behind. She hears enough about the hill to ask what Mau will actually have to lift.

“The shadows can carry the pieces. I'll put them away,” he says. ᴹ

“You can do that sitting down?” ˢʰ

“I can do it standing up.” ᴹ

“That wasn't the question.” ˢʰ

Yuka pulls a chair away from the wall with the toe of her boot. Mau looks at it and concedes that he can. Igris will bring the pieces to him. If his hip goes stiff, he's to say so before he gets up to show her it hasn't. Shoko takes the cup and leaves the chair where it is.

Mau stays at the table. He'd thought the next thing he did would be to stand up. When Hollis gets to the end of the street, Mau asks him who lives above the steps.
