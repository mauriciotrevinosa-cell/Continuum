# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 11 — The Woman with the Bread Key

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S10E36
**Continuity state:** B+173 morning through evening. The house verifies civilian needs and provides shelter, water and access before removal. King is offered safe egress.

*When: Hollis asks the baker how many people use the castle oven, she gives him a number and then adds two people who don't use it on the day he usually comes.*

Her name is Meret. Hollis has bought bread from her since before the crossing had a roof. She lives below the castle. She lets a girl sleep above the oven during the week because the girl comes from too far down the road to make the first batch otherwise. She wants to know whether the girl will be able to keep working.

“I have an oven at home I can offer her for a few days,” Rem says. “That's if you don't have another one here.” ᴿᵉ

Meret lifts a lid and uses the back of her wrist to push the steam away. Rem has set the basket where it would be if she'd come to pick up bread. Meret looks at it and then at the loose wrap on Hollis's wrist.

“Does the girl have to ask your man every time she wants to put bread in?” she asks. ᴹᵉ

“At my oven she'd have to ask me. So would he.” ᴿᵉ

Meret looks at her for the first time since she set the basket down. Her brother has a small oven with a door that won't shut. He has offered it to her before. She had been making enough bread where she was. She asks who would own a door if they brought her one.

“Your brother, if he wants it,” Rem says. “Can we see the hinge first?” ᴿᵉ

“After I get this batch in. I have to fetch my starter too.” ᴹᵉ

Rem sets the basket down farther from the oven and takes the clean cloth out of it. Meret gives her the first four loaves to wrap. On the third loaf she remembers a boy who buys yesterday's bread on the way back from the well. She asks Hollis if the boy will be able to get there.

Hollis tells her about the path below the yard. She sends him down it to see which gate he's thinking of. When he comes back he tells her there are two gates. He only remembered the one that was open the day he came. Meret shows him where the other key is kept. He asks if the people who use the well can have it.

“I can ask them if they want to mind it,” she says. She puts her oven key down next to his map. “It'll be a different door if you do this. I want to see the other oven hot before I give you this one.” ᴹᵉ

“Keep it until then,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

Hollis marks the oven on the map. The shared grain shed is already marked. There are six beds in the rooms behind the castle laundry. One person there will have to be carried down the steps or wait while somebody gets a chair that can go round the turn. Fern asks which he would rather. She sends someone to ask him in person before she puts a mark against the room.

The lower town has an empty schoolroom, a clean storehouse with a chimney, and neighbors who can take in most of the people from the upper floor. The house sends out blankets and two big water jars by wagon. Nobody has to give a name to collect one. Yuka walks the route from the schoolroom to the well with one of Meret's neighbors. She carries an empty pail to see if it fits through the gap beside the steps. It doesn't. By the time Mau comes, there is a second way through from the lane. The neighbor tells him to keep both.

* * *

By the time Mau goes up the hill, Igris has found all the rooms that share walls with the occupied houses. He shows Mau which stones can't come away before they put a brace in. Mau asks him how he'd go about it if he couldn't fit a shadow between the two walls. Igris takes him round the back and shows him another way to see the seam.

Kaisel is waiting at the bottom of the hill. Gojo stands beside the turn where it's easy to miss the steps. The people coming down mostly ask him if they are allowed to go home when they get to the bottom. He says that anybody who doesn't live inside the castle walls can go directly to their own door.

Edran comes out after his clerk. He has brought a bag that looks too heavy to have paper in it. Mau opens the outer gate so the man can walk down carrying it himself.

“Where are you going to make me go?” Edran says. ᴱᵈ

“There is room in the lower town if you need it. There's also a road.” ᴹ

Edran looks behind him. There are shadows waiting in the passage. One of them has set down the brace he was carrying and is standing well clear of the opening. Edran moves the bag to his other hand.

“I could close that gate,” he says. ᴱᵈ

Mau looks at the key in his hand. “Is there anybody you still need to let out?” ᴹ

Edran looks down the hill. His clerk has stopped where the steps divide. The man's coat is thrown over an elbow and he has a small empty basket at his feet. He hasn't gone any farther without him.

Edran leaves the key in the lock. He picks up his bag with both hands and walks through the gate.

Mau waits for Fern's signal from the bottom of the hill before he lets Igris touch the first stone.
