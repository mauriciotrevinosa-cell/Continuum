# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 14 — The End Piece

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S10E38
**Continuity state:** B+174 midday. Mau and Yuka take Hollis up on lunch. Hollis keeps the inner-window setup and chooses how the crossing works for his staff.

*When: Mau asks Yuka if she wants to go out to lunch, she is holding a wooden wedge between her teeth.*

She takes it out. He has come straight from Shoko, who made him walk the length of the room and asked when he sat down last night. He'd been able to tell her. He still feels it when he turns too fast. He tells Yuka he can get to the crossing if they go at the speed of people going out to eat.

“Where?” ʸ

“The crossing. Hollis said we could try the bread from Meret's new oven.” ᴹ

“Did he invite me or did you?” ʸ

“He invited me. I'm inviting you.” ᴹ

She puts the wedge down on the bench beside Mai. The little plate for the catch is screwed in at last. Mai puts two fingers against it and asks if either of them is planning to check how it opens with a full bucket. Yuka looks at Mau.

“I'll do it when we get back,” she says. ʸ

Mai hands her the wedge again. “Take it with you. It'll give you a reason to come back.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka drops it in Mau's coat pocket on her way past him. She keeps walking, then takes two steps back because he hasn't caught up.

* * *

The crossing smells like something has come out of an oven nearby. Mau can only see two parcels on the inner counter. Yuka points him toward the little one with the broken string.

Hollis is standing at the window with the tally keeper. A driver outside wants to know whether she expects him to open a barrel right there in the lane. She says she expects him to put it where she can look at the seal. Hollis waits until she has finished explaining it. When the man asks Hollis the same question, Hollis points to her.

“She told you where,” he says. ᴴᵒ

The man shifts the barrel. It's easier to hear one another when he isn't knocking it against the sill. The tally keeper makes him stand where he can watch her enter it. She puts the slip on the counter for him to check and lets him find the place to sign by himself.

By the time the window is clear, Yuka has unwrapped the parcel far enough to see the bread. She hasn't taken any.

“Are we waiting for anybody?” she asks Hollis. ʸ

“For me to get the knife out,” he says. ᴴᵒ

The handle catches against his wrap. He changes hands, then puts the knife down in front of her.

“Will you cut it?” ᴴᵒ

She takes it. Mau puts the cloth flat so it won't go under the blade. Hollis tells her Meret sent the loaf with a note asking them to say whether the crust is different. It's thicker at one end. Mau takes that piece before anybody can put it aside for him.

Yuka takes a bite from the middle and looks at him. He has his teeth in the crust and is getting exactly nowhere. He lowers it. She breaks the corner off hers and offers it to him. Then she puts out her other hand, waiting for his piece.

“You're going to have the best bit?” he asks. ᴹ

“You had first pick.” ʸ

She bites down and has to stop and start again, the same as he did. Mau laughs. Yuka keeps hold of the crust with both hands and finishes what she started. Hollis uses the quiet to take a second slice.

* * *

The tally keeper comes back for her lunch after the driver leaves. She asks Hollis how long he thinks they can keep using the little window. Hollis asks whether there's a part of it she wants to change.

“I want the wagon to have a place to wait where the second wagon can go past it,” she says. “And I want to be able to close this side if I see those men again.” ᵂᵏ

Hollis takes the keys out of the drawer and puts them beside her plate.

“You can close it whenever you think you need to,” he says. “We'll find a place for the wagon after lunch.” ᴴᵒ

Mau stays in his seat while they talk about it. When Hollis asks him if he can move a marker later, he says yes. He waits until Hollis has eaten his bread before he asks which one.

Outside, Mau puts his hands in his coat pockets. Yuka reaches into the nearer one. He thinks she's after the wedge until her fingers go past it and she closes her hand around his.

“We have time to go the long way back,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her. “Do we?” ᴹ

“If you stop asking me whether we have time.” ʸ

He turns with her toward the long way.
