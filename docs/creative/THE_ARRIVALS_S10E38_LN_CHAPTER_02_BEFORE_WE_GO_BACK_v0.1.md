# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 15 — Before We Go Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S10E38
**Continuity state:** B+174 early afternoon. Mau and Yuka choose time together on the walk home. They talk about Vey, then finish the door test with Mai.

*When: Yuka sees the low wall above the road, she checks whether there's room for both of them before she puts a hand out to Mau.*

It's an ordinary wall that keeps somebody's beans from sliding into the lane. The owner has put a thin board across the top so people waiting on the road don't crush the first row. Yuka sits on it. Mau puts his coat down beside her and sits on that.

For a while they watch a woman below them trying to get a very old dog to come out from under a wagon. The dog comes out as far as its shoulders. The woman gives it a piece of something out of her pocket. It backs up under the wagon to eat it.

“I wanted to stop here yesterday,” Yuka says. ʸ

“On the way back from the well?” ᴹ

She nods. There had been two trips from Vey after the last bucket, and she had done the second one without him. He asks what she wanted to do.

“This. Except I was too tired to climb up. I thought I'd sit here in the morning when somebody could help me off.” ʸ

He puts his hand on the board beside hers. She takes it a little farther up than the wrist.

“I had enough left to go up the hill again,” he says. “I was going to go and look at it one more time.” ᴹ

“Did you?” ʸ

“No. You put a bucket in my hand.” ᴹ

She smiles. “You looked like somebody ought to put a bucket in your hand.” ʸ

Mau looks down at the road. The woman has gone to sit on the wagon step. The dog puts its nose out to see where she went.

“I'm glad you were there,” he says. “I needed somebody who could tell me what the people at the bottom of the hill were doing.” ᴹ

“I was glad you came down to see,” Yuka says. ʸ

They sit for a little while without talking. Mau had thought she might want to ask him if he'd do the same thing again. It is the question he has been asking himself since he got up. He says so.

“I don't know what'll happen next time,” she says. “I'd like it if you asked me again while there's still time to move things around.” ʸ

“You moved a lot of things around.” ᴹ

“The first pail wouldn't fit. The next one would have, if nobody wanted to get past it.” ʸ

“You're very particular about doors.” ᴹ

“Somebody has to be. You think a hole in a wall is a door.” ʸ

He laughs. Yuka puts her shoulder against his and leans in until he's looking at her. She waits while he gets his hand out from under the coat and puts it around her. She slides closer, her knee warm against his.

They kiss. Somewhere below them the dog makes up its mind and comes out. By the time Mau sees it again, the woman is walking in front and the dog is taking little quick steps to keep up.

“Should we go?” he asks. ᴹ

“In a minute.” ʸ

He puts his chin on her shoulder. She tilts her head far enough that he can stay there comfortably. They use the whole minute.

* * *

Mai is sitting on the floor in front of the second door when they come in. There is an empty bucket beside her. She looks first at Mau's pocket. Yuka reaches in for the wedge and hands it back.

“What did you do while we were gone?” she asks. ʸ

“I ate,” Mai says. “Then I moved the catch a hair. You can tell me if it was the wrong hair.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka fills the bucket. Mau starts to take it from her. She puts one hand over the handle so he can't get a grip.

“You get the next one.” ʸ

She goes through with the full bucket. The door swings back and clicks behind her. Mai looks at the hinge. Mau looks through the opening before it shuts all the way and catches the grin Yuka was trying to save until she got to the other side.
