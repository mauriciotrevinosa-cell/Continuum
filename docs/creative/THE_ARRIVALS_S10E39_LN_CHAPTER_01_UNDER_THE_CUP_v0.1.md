# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 16 — Under the Cup

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S10E39
**Continuity state:** B+174 evening. Cards and dinner at the house. Rem gets Meret's letter and shares it with Mai. Hollis keeps the road question for the morning.

*When: Umaru finds two hands of cards already laid out at the long table, she asks whether the house has decided to play without a rules committee.*

Rem is kneeling beside the bench looking for a card. She gets up with it between two fingers.

“The rules committee can decide whether the dog is allowed to keep the one he licked,” she says. ᴿᵉ

Futari has one hand on her dog's head and the other pressed down on three cards nobody has had the heart to tell her are from a different deck. She looks at Umaru. Umaru asks if the dog wants to play or if he just wants the card. Futari considers it seriously. The dog lies down while she is considering.

“He just wants the card,” she says. ᶠᵘ

Umaru takes one from her own pile and puts it face down in the space he left. Rem puts the damp card on the windowsill to dry. She leaves her hand on the end of the bench until Umaru sits down next to her.

Across the table, Bocchi is showing her father where to hold his cards so Futari can't read them in the window. He moves his whole chair. Bocchi has to get up and move hers with it because she still wants to sit beside him.

* * *

Hollis brings the letters in just before supper. There are three for the outer desk and one addressed to Rem. He puts hers down beside her elbow and tells her she can open it while he's here if she likes. She leaves her cards on the table and opens it.

Meret has written down how long she left the new oven to heat. The first line is about the hinge. Rem reads it twice. The last line is for the girl who used to sleep above the old oven. There is a bed for her with Meret's sister if she still wants to stay in Vey through the week.

“Did you see her when you went back?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“I saw her putting loaves in the cart,” Hollis says. “She said she wanted the bed if she could be the one to carry the key.” ᴴᵒ

Rem folds the letter. She starts to put it away, then unfolds it again and calls for Mai. She thinks Mai will want to know what Meret said about the hinge. Mai asks if she can read it after dinner. Rem brings her another card and puts the letter under the unused plate where it'll keep.

Fern comes in to take the other three letters. One of them asks if it is safe to take the old road. Hollis can answer that one in the morning with the tally keeper. He asks Fern to leave it on top. Fern does. Then she takes a chair. It is the first thing she has picked up all evening that she puts down without checking where anybody else wants it.

* * *

Mau stands at the corner of the table because Yuka has one foot across the gap he usually uses to sit down. She looks up at him.

“You're late.” ʸ

“I was getting plates.” ᴹ

“Did the plates need four trips?” ʸ

He sets the last two down. Gojo takes one of them. He looks at it as if he might be able to tell how many trips it took to get there.

Yuka moves her foot. Mau sits. She deals him in, then lifts his cup far enough for him to see the card she was keeping safe underneath it.

“Is that mine?” ᴹ

“Do you want it?” ʸ

“Very much.” ᴹ

She pushes the cup closer to him. He takes the card. When he sets the cup down, she keeps hold of the handle on her side until their knuckles touch. Across the table Gojo asks who is going to deal him in. Yuka says she will as soon as he gives back the card he put under his plate.

Gojo takes a bite of his supper and has to put the card down to chew. Mau lets him keep it. Yuka reaches across Mau and takes it anyway.
