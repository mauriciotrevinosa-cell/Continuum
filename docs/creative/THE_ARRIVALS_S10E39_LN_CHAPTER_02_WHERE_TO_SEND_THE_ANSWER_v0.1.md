# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 17 — Where to Send the Answer

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S10E39
**Continuity state:** B+175 morning. Luminous answers the practical road question. Hollis and Sela put their names to the notice at the crossing. Walpurgis appoints no representative.

*When: Luminous reads the two accounts from Vey, she puts the one with the names on top.*

The other one is shorter. It says how far away the castle could be seen before it disappeared and gives a number for the people who lived there. Its writer has added that he hasn't seen any of them since. He could have added that he hadn't been down to the schoolhouse. Somebody has written the names of the people sleeping there on the next page.

Leon has come back for the papers they agreed to answer. He is at the far end of the table with the one Mau left behind. It is folded down the middle and he keeps it that way.

“Was this what he meant?” he asks. ᴸᵉ

Luminous looks at the names again. She hadn't wanted to hear him make a threat. She'd wanted to hear what he thought counted as one. It had taken longer than she'd expected to notice that her own question sounded like a plan somebody was asking him to approve.

“I think this is what he did,” she says. “If you want to ask him what he meant, you can ask him without sending somebody to a door with a writ.” ᴸᵘ

Leon rests his thumb on the folded edge. He asks whether she's still going to tell the drivers to send complaints to her.

“One of them sent me one yesterday. There are two places marked on his map where he says men came out asking for money. I can send somebody to find out who they were.” ᴸᵘ

“Is he going to use the road again?” ᴸᵉ

“He wants to. Somebody at the other end will have to tell him where he can unload.” ᴸᵘ

Leon asks if she's sending anybody to the wall outside the hall. Rimuru's account is on a separate sheet. She has left it there because the guard found the loose stones and the torn map, and the road men haven't brought her anything to explain either of them.

“I sent somebody to look at it,” she says. “I haven't sent anybody to say they know who did it.” ᴸᵘ

Luminous asks for another sheet. She writes to the person who can answer that part. Leon reads the question once, adds his name under hers and leaves the other paper folded.

* * *

At the crossing, Hollis puts the letter where his tally keeper can read it too. Sela moves the register to make room. This time there are two signatures at the bottom of the page and only one question. Can a wagon on the east road bring its load to the inside window?

“Depends how wide it is,” Sela says. ˢᵉˡ

Hollis looks at her. She has a piece of chalk on the counter and a line drawn down the side of her sleeve from the last time she reached across it. She wants him to come and see what she did with the waiting marker.

Mau is outside holding it upright. Sela has asked him to hold it there until they can see where a driver will need to look back. The driver who brought the bread stands beside him and points to the place a wheel will go if the wagon comes in too far on the left.

Hollis brings the letter out. He reads the question from the road, where he can see how much space they have.

“Can we ask how much it needs before it comes?” he says. ᴴᵒ

Sela puts the chalk in her pocket. “And tell them who they should ask for when they get here.” ˢᵉˡ

“You?” ᴴᵒ

“Either of us. My name's going on the notice.” ˢᵉˡ

She goes to get the notice out of the window. Mau holds the marker in the place she leaves him. By the time the chalk is back in her hand there is somebody else in the road asking him what his sign is for.

He looks across at Sela. She is still writing her name.

“Ask her,” he says. “It's hers.” ᴹ
