# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 18 — What He Can Tell Them

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S10E40
**Continuity state:** B+175 afternoon. Ko chooses what Mau and Noise can observe about the incomplete vampire state. Team defines limits for any later test. No blood or exposure.

*When: Ko brings back the paper Kusuri gave him, there are things written under the line he was supposed to stop at.*

Nazuna asks whether he wants her to read them aloud. He puts his finger beside the first one. She starts there and reads until he lifts it again.

He has written what his hands feel like when the strength goes away. He has written that sometimes he knows he's about to lose it because his jaw aches and sometimes he only knows because something he was holding comes down harder than he meant. He tried holding the same thing both ways yesterday. He can't tell whether he got it wrong or was just thinking about it too hard.

“What did you hold?” Kusuri asks. ᴷᵘ

“A sack of grain.” ᴷᵒ

“Did you ask the sack?” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃ

Ko looks at her. She looks back, with both hands on the edge of her chair. He has been waiting to laugh at the sack for about a day. Once he starts, it gets easier to tell them which time he dropped it.

Kusuri draws a box around what Ko says happened before his jaw ached. Maomao waits to add what happened afterward until she's finished.

* * *

Mau takes the chair next to him when Ko asks him to. Yuka has come to take some of the notes. She asks Ko what he wants on the page that goes home with him and what he would like her to leave off. He wants all the things he said about his hands. The thing about when he's afraid can stay in the room.

Mau tells him what Noise usually does when he sees something new. Then he tells him the one thing he's actually asking to do.

“I'd like to see where it starts and where it stops,” he says. “You can tell me to look away as soon as you've had enough.” ᴹ

“What if I don't know how to start it?” ᴷᵒ

“Then we can see what it looks like while it isn't happening.” ᴹ

Ko looks at Nazuna. She has been looking down at the page. She looks up when he says her name. Ko asks her to keep reading where she stopped. He wants something to listen to while he holds his hand out.

Mau looks. There is a moment when he can see Ko tense his fingers, then relax the wrong two. Noise waits through it. The thing he notices comes a little later, when Ko stops waiting for somebody to tell him what to do and turns his hand over on the table.

*Some of it is still there,* Noise says. *Different when he's quiet.* ᴺ

Mau says it aloud. Kusuri writes down what Noise saw and the time she saw the hand turn. She leaves a gap between that and Ko's answer about what it felt like.

When Ko puts his hand in his lap, Mau lets the observation go. He asks Noise how much he could hold steady if Mau wanted his own body to do it later. Noise gives him an answer he can pass on without calling it a guarantee. Maomao asks him where the wheel comes in. Mau says he can keep it ready to adapt if the model does something they didn't expect. Shoko says she wants them to know when to stop before he has to find out whether it can.

Ko has put his hands flat on his knees. He looks up to ask his question before they start drawing another box.

“Do I get to use the sign too?” he asks. ᴷᵒ

Kusuri turns the paper so he can read the empty place where she was going to write it.

“Want to pick it?” she asks. ᴷᵘ

* * *

Kusuri brings the spare chair home to show Nano where the other person would be sitting. Nano lets her put it down. Then she sits in it and tells her she can't see anything under the edge of the table.

“That's why he'll move his hand out first,” Kusuri says. ᴷᵘ

“And if that's the part he can't do?” Nano asks. ᴺᵒ

Kusuri looks at her hand. She had wanted Ko to pick something he could do when the room was looking at him. She hadn't thought to ask what would happen if his hand was already under something.

“I'll ask him for a word as well,” she says. ᴷᵘ

Hakari pulls her own chair nearer with both feet. She asks what Kusuri is going to say when somebody knocks in the middle of it. Kusuri opens her mouth. She can already imagine herself trying to answer three people without losing the place on the page.

Shizuka gets up and brings back the card they use when somebody is reading in the little room. It says *please knock* on one side and has a drawing of a book on the other. She turns it over so the blank margin faces out. Karane asks who the sign is supposed to be for.

“People outside,” Hakari says. ᴴᵃ

“People outside here ignore a sign unless it's bigger than the door.” ᴷᵃ

Shizuka looks up at her. She keeps her hand on the card.

Karane sighs. “Fine. I'll be bigger than the door.” ᴷᵃ

“You could sit with me instead,” Hakari says. ᴴᵃ

Karane had already pulled out a second chair for her. She claims she just needed it out of the way. Nobody moves it back. Kusuri borrows the card. Tomorrow she'll ask Ko what he'd like it to say before they hang it up.
