# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 19 — Take Me Out for Nothing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S10E40
**Continuity state:** B+175 evening. Nazuna and Ko take a night walk by choice after the consultation. They keep their commitment and leave feeding and the test open.

*When: Nazuna comes for Ko, he's still trying to fold the paper small enough to fit in his jacket.*

“Put it down,” she says. ᴺᵃ

He looks up. “I'm taking it with me.” ᴷᵒ

“Where do you think we're going?” ᴺᵃ

He doesn't know. He's been carrying the paper since he left the room that afternoon. There is a new mark at the bottom made where he showed Kusuri which way he could turn his hand without feeling like somebody was asking him to raise it in class. He'd tried it on the way out. Everybody had stopped talking. Even Maomao.

Nazuna waits until he puts the paper on the sill. She waits another second while he takes it off the sill and puts it somewhere it won't blow away. Then she takes him out through the door.

* * *

They make it down one flight of steps before Ko asks if there's somewhere she wanted to go.

“I was hoping you'd tell me,” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃ

“You said to put the paper down.” ᴷᵒ

“Yeah. I don't want to go out with your piece of paper.” ᴺᵃ

Ko looks down the path. It is getting dark under the trees. Somebody is shutting the back of a cart with the little lift you have to give a heavy door before the catch will take it. On the other side of the path there's a row of places you can sit without being in the way.

“Do you want to walk as far as the last one?” he asks. ᴷᵒ

“Sure.” ᴺᵃ

They pass the first two at a fair speed. At the third Ko thinks he has caught sight of something moving on the roof, and when they stop it turns out to be a bit of laundry. Nazuna wants to know what he thought it was. He says he doesn't know. He only knows he wanted her to see it too. She stands looking up with him long enough to see the cloth move again.

At the last place to sit, she sits first.

“I didn't say I wanted him to do it tomorrow,” Ko says. ᴷᵒ

“I know. I was there.” ᴺᵃ

“I could want to know and still take a while.” ᴷᵒ

Nazuna pulls one leg up and wraps both arms around it.

“You can go back and tell them that if you want. They seemed like they were listening.” ᴺᵃ

Ko sits down. He leaves a little space. She nudges him with the side of her shoe until he looks up. He moves over.

“I didn't mean I wanted to take a while with you,” he says. ᴷᵒ

“I know that too,” she says. “You want to go out tomorrow even if we got nothing to tell each other?” ᴺᵃ

He looks at her. She is still looking up at the roofs as if there might be something on one worth seeing.

“Yes,” he says. “Take me out for nothing.” ᴷᵒ

She laughs. Her shoe stays against his. They sit there until they can both make out which window the light on the roof is coming from.
