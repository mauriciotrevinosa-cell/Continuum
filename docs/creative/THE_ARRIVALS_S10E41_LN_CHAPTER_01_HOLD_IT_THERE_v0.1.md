# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 20 — Hold It There

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S10E41
**Continuity state:** B+176 afternoon. Ko, Nazuna and Mau each opt in. Noise temporarily models Ko's incomplete vampire state in Mau. Nazuna tastes Mau's modeled blood. Shoko and Yuta stand ready for Nazuna.

*When: Ko comes back to the consultation room, the first thing he does is show Kusuri the sign he picked yesterday.*

She stops in the middle of putting down a cup. She waits.

“Good,” he says. “I wanted to see if you'd remember.” ᴷᵒ

Kusuri sets the cup down.

“Try that on Maomao,” she says. ᴷᵘ

Maomao lifts her eyes from the page. Ko decides he has seen enough. He sits where he sat yesterday and puts a hand on the other chair until Nazuna takes it.

Yuta has come with Shoko. He asks Nazuna where she would like him to wait. She points to the place beside the window, then changes her mind and tells him the end of the table is better. If she has to say something to him, she wants to be able to do it without turning her head. Yuta puts his chair there. Shoko waits until he's settled, then asks Nazuna to tell her again what she expects to feel if something goes wrong.

“I don't know. That's part of why I asked you to come,” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃ

“Then tell me the first thing that's different, even if you're embarrassed.” ˢʰ

Nazuna lifts an eyebrow. Shoko lets her keep it up for a while.

“I'll tell you,” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃ

* * *

Mau rolls his right sleeve back. His other hand stays where Yuka left it, on the flat part of the bench between them. Maomao asks if he's willing to try to hold the state and if he's willing to let Nazuna taste his blood while he does. He says yes to both. When she asks Ko the separate question about whether he still wants Mau to use what Noise observed, Ko takes a moment and says yes too.

“The rule I heard was about drinking from the person you already love,” Nazuna says. “I want to know if the part of you that changed can hurt me on its own.” ᴺᵃ

Ko looks across at Mau. “That part is all you're copying?” ᴷᵒ

“Just what your body does,” Mau says. “The rest is yours.” ᴹ

“What happens if you can't keep it steady?” he asks Mau. ᴷᵒ

“We stop and you get to tell me I looked silly trying.” ᴹ

“I can tell you that right now,” Yuka says. ʸ

Mau smiles. Noise asks him to be still for the first part. His jaw starts to feel too full on one side. He reaches up with the hand that is free and stops before it gets to his face. It feels like part of his body is waiting for something he hasn't decided to do.

“Is that when you dropped the sack?” he asks. ᴹ

Ko leans forward. Mau can tell by the way he looks at his mouth that it is close enough for him to recognize.

“Before,” Ko says. “That was right before.” ᴷᵒ

Kusuri makes a mark on the page. The wheel is waiting. Mau can feel where he would have to let it turn if things go wrong. He tells Shoko that much. She asks Nazuna if she'd rather wait. Nazuna opens the hand she's been holding shut and puts it on the table.

“I used to think I'd find out by being stupid at the wrong moment,” she says. “I'd rather find out this way.” ᴺᵃ

Yuta shifts close enough to help her without having to reach across anybody. Shoko leaves room for Nazuna to back away on her own. Maomao waits for Noise to confirm that the state is still holding before she passes the sample across. Nazuna lifts it herself. There is barely enough to taste.

Her eyes close. Ko's hand starts to come up. He holds it still at the edge of the table.

“What is it?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

“Tastes strange.” ᴺᵃ

“Where?” ˢʰ

“In my mouth.” ᴺᵃ

Nazuna opens her eyes. Shoko is already close. She takes the cup away and checks Nazuna's face while Nazuna looks from her to Yuta and back.

“I can still see both of you,” she says. “I can still taste it, too. Give me a second.” ᴺᵃ

Nobody moves to take the second away from her. Mau holds still until Shoko says he can let the model go. He feels the ache leave his jaw. Yuka rests her thumb over his. The wheel stays where it is.

Kusuri leaves the page open. She has written down what happened up to the point where Nazuna asked for the second. She waits for Nazuna to say what comes after it.
