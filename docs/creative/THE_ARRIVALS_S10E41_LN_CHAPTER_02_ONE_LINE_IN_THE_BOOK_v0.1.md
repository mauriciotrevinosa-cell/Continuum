# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 21 — One Line in the Book

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S10E41
**Continuity state:** B+176 later afternoon. Nazuna shows no toxic reaction in the observed model. Kusuri and Maomao record the bounded result. Ko and Nazuna keep their own decision about feeding.

*When: Nazuna has had time to complain about two different chairs, Shoko asks if anything besides the chairs still feels strange.*

Nazuna considers it. She checks her own hand where she'd pressed it against the table. The marks her nails left there have already faded.

“The taste is gone,” she says. “Nothing hurt. I thought something was about to, and then it didn't.” ᴺᵃ

Yuta has kept the chair where she asked him to put it. He moves it back at last, far enough that she can get up if she wants to. She stays seated. Ko puts his hand near hers and gives her time to notice it.

“Can you write that down?” she asks Kusuri. ᴺᵃ

Kusuri reads back what she's written. On the last line she starts to say that the blood is safe. Maomao puts one finger at the end of the sentence.

“Whose did we try?” she asks. ᴹᵐ

“Mau's. While it was doing the thing Ko's does.” ᴷᵘ

“Write that.” ᴹᵐ

Kusuri thinks for a second, then turns the paper sideways so she has enough space. No toxic response observed in Nazuna. One test. She writes what Noise saw change in Mau and when his body settled again. Ko leans over far enough to see his own name in the sentence. He doesn't have to ask where to put his hand to cover the rest of it. Nazuna has already laid hers across the page to keep it flat.

“I thought the answer was going to tell me what I had to do,” he says. ᴷᵒ

“You wanted it to?” Nazuna asks. ᴺᵃ

“Kind of. I could be scared of what it told me instead of what I wanted.” ᴷᵒ

She looks at him. He had been looking at the page while he said it. He looks back at her now.

“Do you want it to happen today?” she asks. ᴺᵃ

He takes a little while before he shakes his head.

“Me neither,” she says. “I want to spend today knowing you asked me.” ᴺᵃ

Ko puts his hand over hers at the edge of the page. Kusuri waits to turn it. Maomao pulls the other copy toward herself and writes the same line while they are still sitting there.

* * *

Outside the room, Mau gets his sleeve over his hand and misses the cuff the first time. He tries again. Yuka takes his coat off the peg and waits until the button goes through.

“Does your jaw still hurt?” she asks. ʸ

“No. Did I look silly?” ᴹ

“About as much as when you tried to eat that crust.” ʸ

He laughs. She puts the coat over his shoulders without making him lift his arms. Inside, Nazuna is asking Ko what he wants to do now. Mau hears the beginning of Ko's answer, then the door swings shut.

Yuka holds out her hand. He takes it.

“You have an appointment with a bowl of batter,” she says. ʸ

“What did I agree to?” ᴹ

“Having an evening off. We have to make something to eat first.” ʸ

She takes him down the stairs. Halfway down, he remembers to ask what kind of batter it is. By the time she tells him, she has both of his hands and is walking backward so she can watch his face.
