# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 22 — What Fits on the Table

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 22
**Source episode:** S10E42
**Continuity state:** B+176 late afternoon. Yuka and Mau cook for a household evening. Futari and Bocchi agree what music to share. Mai brings something of her own.

*When: Yuka takes the cover off the bowl, Mau can see where somebody put one finger into the batter and tried to smooth it over again.*

“Umaru?” he asks. ᴹ

“Me.” ʸ

She finds him a clean spoon. He takes a little off the edge and watches her face while he tastes it. It turns out she has been waiting to find out what he thinks of the ginger. He tells her she could put more in. She tells him the last time he said that, neither of them could finish what he made.

“Then we'll put more in mine,” he says. ᴹ

“You're cooking them,” she says. ʸ

He has to laugh at that because she already has the pan ready. She hands him the cloth for the handle and tips the bowl back toward herself while he works out where he wants to stand.

They have made two before Rem comes in to ask how many places they want at the table. Yuka says she thought they could put the first plate out and see who comes. Mau turns his cake over. It's a little darker than the first one. He starts to get a plate down to keep it separate. Yuka takes it out of the pan before he can and starts breaking it up between them.

“Leave me the middle,” he says. ᴹ

She does. It is the part that has got most of the ginger in it. He takes another bite and tells her it was just right. She gets him a glass of water.

* * *

Futari has come to ask if a guitar will fit on the bench. Rem takes her to see. It will fit if nobody is sitting next to Bocchi. Futari tries sitting on the floor and discovers she can't see what Bocchi's hands would be doing from there.

Bocchi has stopped in the doorway with the guitar in its bag.

“Do you want me to play something?” she asks. ᴮᵒ

“Yes,” Futari says. “Do you want to?” ᶠᵘ

Bocchi looks back into the passage. There are people bringing plates in from both directions. Her mother has taken the chair that's easy to get out of. She can leave it there for Bocchi if Bocchi wants it. Nobody in the passage has asked when she's going to start.

“One thing,” Bocchi says. “I want Kita and Nijika there. Ryo, if she comes.” ᴮᵒ

“I came already,” Ryo says from behind her. ᴿʸ

Bocchi nearly drops the bag. Ryo takes hold of the bottom. She says she can move it if Bocchi wants. Bocchi keeps the handle and the two of them carry it in that way.

* * *

Mai arrives with a bowl under a clean towel. She puts it on the table just far enough from Mau that he has to ask what is in it. The answer is cherries. She'd gone to see whether there were any left and Momo had seen her come back with them and helped her pull the stems.

“You can have two,” Mai tells him. “One if you ask how much it cost.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Mau takes one. Yuka gets a second one for him and puts it on the edge of his plate where it won't roll. Mai takes the towel away. From where she's standing, she can see Momo trying to find a place to sit in the doorway with the rest of the people who just wanted to listen.

“There are seats,” she calls. ᴹᵃⁱ

Momo sees who called to her and comes over. Mau moves the bowl far enough down the table that they can both reach it. Yuka takes the last cake out of the pan. They have burnt one, eaten two and made enough of the rest that Rem starts to bring in a second plate.
