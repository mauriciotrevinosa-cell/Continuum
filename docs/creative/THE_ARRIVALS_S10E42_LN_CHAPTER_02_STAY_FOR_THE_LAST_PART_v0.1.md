# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 23 — Stay for the Last Part

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 23
**Source episode:** S10E42
**Continuity state:** B+176 evening. The house spends an evening together. Music is by Bocchi's choice. Guests have their own places. A caller knocks at the outer door.

*When: Bocchi lifts one hand off the strings, Futari stops eating long enough to check if that's the end.*

“It goes like that twice,” Bocchi says. ᴮᵒ

Futari puts the cake down. On the second time through, she sits almost too still to chew. When the last note goes away, her father asks what the tune is called. Bocchi looks down at the strings. She tells him she hasn't called it anything yet.

“Can we hear it again when you have?” he asks.

“Yes,” she says. “You can hear it before too.” ᴮᵒ

Kita has been sitting beside her with a cup between both hands. She lets the answer get all the way out before she reaches over to move the edge of Bocchi's sleeve away from the strings. Bocchi looks at her. Then she asks if she wants to do the next one with her.

The people at the back of the room start to move their chairs out of the passage so they can stay.

* * *

Ori has saved a place by Frieren for a letter which is too big to go beside her plate. She opens it on the floor first. It's a note from one of the students who went on the Fort expedition. The note says that the route on the last map looked longer before they had walked it. Ori has been asked if that means the next map should be smaller.

Frieren takes a look at the old map while Ori decides. She turns the page right side up. Ori takes it back and turns it around again. It is easier to see why they missed the little path when she holds it as she was holding it in the dungeon.

“Can you read me that line again?” Ori asks. ᴼ

Frieren does. On the other side of the table Umaru has written down who brought each thing in case she has to remember how to ask for it again. Rem brings her the bowl of cherries and tells her it's going to be too late to count them if she leaves it there.

“Did you have one?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

Rem shakes her head. Umaru picks one out and leaves it on Rem's plate. Rem goes back for her cup and sits down with it beside her.

* * *

Rimuru has brought the long bag. Yuka sees it before Mau does and asks whether there's room in it for anything that can wait until tomorrow. Rimuru looks down. The bag is still closed. He puts it under his chair and asks if she wants to know what he actually brought.

“Is it work?” she asks. ʸ

“Jam.” ᴿⁱ

She looks at him. He gets it out so she can see. There are two jars, one with a loose lid that explains the way he's been carrying the bag. Yuka puts both of them where she can see them and tells Mau to find another spoon before he eats all his cake dry.

Mau brings back two spoons. He sits beside her while she spreads the jam on the last corner of hers. He holds his hand out for the jar. She puts the corner into it instead.

He eats it. Then he takes the spoon she hasn't used and makes one for her, with enough jam on it to keep her from asking whether there was any.

In the other room somebody has found the cards. They call through to ask if Ori is coming. Ori waits for Frieren to reach the end of the letter first. Mau looks over to see if Yuka wants to play. She pushes his knee with hers to get him to move out of the way. He moves just far enough that she has to laugh and push him again.

They hear the knock before they get up. Three taps from the outer door, then enough time for the person to decide whether to try again. No one calls for a weapon. Rimuru puts his cup down and goes to see who is waiting.
