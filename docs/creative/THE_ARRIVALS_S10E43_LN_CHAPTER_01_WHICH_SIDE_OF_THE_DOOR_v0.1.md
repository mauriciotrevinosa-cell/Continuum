# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 24 — Which Side of the Door

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 24
**Source episode:** S10E43
**Continuity state:** B+176 evening. Jin-Woo arrives by a one-time opening and walks the outer road. Rimuru and Mau verify identity before inviting him in. His Beru remains with him.

*When: Rimuru opens the outer door, the man waiting on the step has backed up far enough to give him room.*

His coat is wet around the ankles. He asks if he's come to the right house. Then he looks past Rimuru and sees Mau coming down the path.

“You are here,” he says. ᴶʷ

Mau stops. He remembers what it felt like to hear a man's voice in the middle of something else and have no way to know whether there would be a next word. From the step he can hear the whole sentence. The man stands as if he's been on his feet all day and might have to stay that way for some time yet.

“What did you ask me?” Mau says. ᴹ

“Who you were. I don't think I finished the next one.” ᴶʷ

The man waits a second. He looks at Mau, then down at the space between their feet, where both shadows lie flat and ordinary on the ground.

“How did you get my shadows?” he asks. ᴶʷ

Mau has wanted to hear the end of that question for a long time. He hadn't pictured it being asked in a place where somebody had to remember to shut the door before the supper got cold.

“They weren't yours by the time I could call them,” Mau says. “I can show you. First I'd like to know how you got here.” ᴹ

“Something opened where I was. I knew the trace I'd heard you through. By the time I got my footing there was a road behind me and the opening had closed.” ᴶʷ

He points away from the house, toward the way Rimuru had told the guard to keep clear of wagons. Rimuru asks how far. The man describes the two bends. The guard can check those without leaving the path.

Mau looks again at the man's face.

“Sung Jin-Woo?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴶʷ

* * *

They step into the light under the roof together. Jin-Woo waits there while Rimuru calls Yuta, who wants to hear the voice before he can put it with anything he remembers. Yuta comes out carrying the dish he'd been about to take to the kitchen. He listens to Jin-Woo say his name once.

“That was you,” he says. “A long time ago.” ʸᵗ

Jin-Woo looks from Yuta to Mau. He had come prepared to explain quite a lot before somebody at the door would recognise him. He takes his hands out of his coat pockets.

“I brought someone,” he says. ᴶʷ

A shape rises at his heel, tall enough that the light reaches the edges of its shoulders before it reaches its face. It stops a little behind him. Mau knows how it will bow a second before it does.

There is a movement in his own shadow. He puts his hand down at his side.

*Wait until we're inside,* he tells Beru. ᴹ

He can feel the effort it takes Beru to wait.

Yuka comes to the far side of the path. She has brought the cup he left on the table. She holds it out while he thinks of what to say next. He takes it from her.

“We have room if you'd like to sit down,” Mau tells Jin-Woo. ᴹ

Jin-Woo looks at Yuka. She turns so he can see the light in the common room and the people inside getting another place ready.

“I would,” he says. ᴶʷ

They show him the way in.
