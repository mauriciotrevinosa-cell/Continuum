# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 64
## Chapter 25 — It Still Opens

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S10 v0.1
**Volume:** 64
**Reading order:** 25
**Source episode:** S10E43
**Continuity state:** B+176 evening. Ashborn chooses to see Jin-Woo from his protected guest room. They speak without changing a contract or settling the next season's proposal.

*When: Mau asks Ashborn if he wants to see the man who came to the door, Ashborn puts one hand flat on the sill.*

“Is he coming here to ask me for something?” he asks. ᴬˢ

“He asked if you were here. I told him I'd come and ask whether you wanted to see him.” ᴹ

The window is shut. It gets cold enough at this hour that sometimes Ashborn leaves it shut and listens with his chair a little closer. He looks down at the latch. Then he tells Mau to bring Jin-Woo.

DIO is in the next room. She has heard the two voices and comes to the doorway with a book open in her hand. Ashborn says he wants a little time. She asks if he wants the door closed. He thinks and tells her she can leave it just as it is. She leaves it that way and takes the book back with her.

Jin-Woo stops where he can see Ashborn without going around the chair. Mau has put another one near the window. For a moment both men look at it. Then Jin-Woo sits.

“I didn't think I'd see you again,” he says. ᴶʷ

Ashborn keeps his hand on the sill. “You weren't meant to have to.” ᴬˢ

Jin-Woo looks up at him.

“Do you still want what you asked for?” he says. ᴶʷ

“Yes.” ᴬˢ

He says it with enough ease that Mau, who is still at the door, decides he can go get a third cup. He turns. Ashborn asks him to stay a moment.

“Show him the window,” he says. ᴬˢ

Mau crosses to the chair and turns the latch. It moves easily now. Ashborn takes the frame where Mau has left room for his hand and opens it the rest of the way himself.

There is music in the yard. It takes a while for somebody to get the first part of a tune and then it takes less time on the second try. The light from the common room comes as far as the path but leaves the ground under the window dark.

Ashborn looks at Jin-Woo. “Sometimes I open it just to hear whether they're still there.” ᴬˢ

Jin-Woo listens. Mau has set his cup down on the ledge. Without either of them asking, Ashborn pushes it a little farther from the edge.

“Did you come all this way because you thought I'd call you back?” Jin-Woo asks. ᴶʷ

Ashborn's hand stops. Then he settles it around his own cup.

“No. I came because he offered me somewhere I could stop.” ᴬˢ

Jin-Woo looks toward Mau. Mau can see the question start to form. Jin-Woo gives it the length of the music outside, then sets it aside.

“Then I'm glad you stopped here,” he says. ᴶʷ

Ashborn draws his chair closer to the window. He asks if Jin-Woo wants to keep his coat on. Jin-Woo takes it off. Mau catches the sleeve before it goes into the corner of the chair and puts it over his arm to hang up.

“Take your time,” he says. ᴹ

Ashborn looks at Jin-Woo when Mau says it. Jin-Woo turns his chair far enough that he can see the same part of the yard. They stay there after Mau goes for the cup.
