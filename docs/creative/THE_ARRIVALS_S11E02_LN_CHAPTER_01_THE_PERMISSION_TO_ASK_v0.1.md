# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 3 — The Permission to Ask

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S11E02
**Continuity state:** B+177 midday. Existing guest-room/restricted-model distinction from V51 retained. Ashborn and DIO hear a proposal voluntarily. Mau asks before reuse; no derived model activated, no guest power donation, no route built. Yuka and Jin-Woo remain outside inner meeting this chapter; invitations do not grant access to private spaces.

*When: Mau tells DIO he needs to ask about something ridiculous, she asks whether he has already done it.*

“No.” ᴹ

“That is an excellent beginning.” ᴰ

She puts her book down. Ashborn is at the adjoining doorway, listening without having promised to join. Mau has asked whether both of them are willing to hear a proposal about Jin-Woo. DIO said yes immediately. Ashborn asked whether the proposal needed an answer today.

It didn't. He came to the doorway anyway.

Rimuru stands beside Mau. Ciel is listening through the channel they share; Noise has been attentive since the table with the map. Neither of the guests has been asked to surrender their quiet room for an audience.

“Jin-Woo wants help holding the war on his side,” Mau says. “We haven't agreed to leave. We can't even tell him how we would get there.” ᴹ

Ashborn looks past him toward the outer room.

“He asked you?” ᴬˢ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“What did you say?” ᴬˢ

“That I'd listen.” ᴹ

Ashborn settles his hand against the frame. He stays.

Mau tells them the parts Jin-Woo has agreed he can share. He takes care to distinguish what the man has seen from what he believes may happen next. When DIO asks whether the family has asked to come, he says they have not been reached. When she asks whether he has promised them a way, he says no.

“Good,” she says. “Please keep that answer until we know what we are discussing.” ᴰ

“I was planning to.” ᴹ

Rimuru makes a thoughtful sound.

Mau turns his head.

“You could support me once.” ᴹ

“I walked here with you.” ᴿ

“Without commentary.” ᴹ

“You should have put that in the invitation.” ᴿ

DIO moves her book to her lap and asks to see the drawing.

* * *

The first answer is a limit.

She can recognize the difference between the old, balanced arrivals and a raw connection which pulls at two worlds. The opening Jin-Woo crossed has left something to study. That does not make it a door she can reopen because Mau has asked nicely.

“If there is a path to examine, we examine it,” she says. “Before promising a return, before promising that somebody can be brought through.” ᴰ

“And anyone we speak to gets to decide,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴰ

Ashborn asks what they will do about the war if the family comes. It is almost the question Yuka asked, and Mau likes him for asking it even though it means he has to explain the stupid part sooner.

“They keep sending things because they think Jin-Woo is the thing to get past,” he says. “They know his presence. They have an idea of what he can do. What happens if something else appears?” ᴹ

DIO looks at him over the edge of the paper.

Rimuru folds his arms.

Mau carries on.

“Something that makes them reconsider the place itself. A power that looks like it belongs there. Something they don't already know how to measure.” ᴹ

“A god,” DIO says. ᴰ

“An impression of one.” ᴹ

Ashborn's hand leaves the frame.

For a moment Mau thinks he is going back into his room. Instead he steps close enough to see the drawing properly.

“You want to build a god?” ᴬˢ

“I want to build something they could mistake for one.” ᴹ

“With what?” ᴬˢ

Mau feels Noise attend to the question. He asks it to leave the closed work closed.

Then he tells them.

* * *

There are parts of the explanation he would rather deliver standing somewhere else.

He stays where they can see him and speaks about the models kept apart since the unauthorized work. He doesn't describe them as a gift he has been saving. He reminds DIO and Ashborn where they came from and says exactly what he wants permission to consider.

Ashborn lets him get through the whole sentence.

“I said I did not want what belongs to me used to fight another war.” ᴬˢ

“I remember.” ᴹ

“And you have brought me a war.” ᴬˢ

“I've brought you a plan that might let someone leave one.” ᴹ

Ashborn looks at him. Mau doesn't hurry to fill the space.

“The models stay separate from you,” he says. “You stay in your room. Nobody takes a piece of you and makes you swing it. I'm asking whether we can study the copies we already have, with you watching whatever you want to watch, to see if they can produce a signal.” ᴹ

“A signal of what?” ᴬˢ

“You. Her. Something neither of those attackers expects to find together.” ᴹ

DIO shifts the paper so the blank side faces him.

“Show me what you think they will see.” ᴰ

Mau takes the pencil from Rimuru.

He draws a figure and immediately regrets the shape of its head. Rimuru notices. DIO very kindly decides to ask about the feet instead.

“It doesn't have to look like that,” Mau says. ᴹ

“A relief,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Ashborn has not looked away from the page.

* * *

Mau explains what he means by a performance. There would have to be a witness able to report it. There would have to be a reason for the attackers to believe the presence belonged to the world they were trying to enter. The thing would need enough actual behavior behind the display to survive being looked at twice.

He cannot promise that it could defeat a maker of worlds. He says that before Ashborn asks.

“Then what makes it convincing?” Ashborn says. ᴬˢ

Mau taps the name Jin-Woo wrote beside one of the routes.

“They've been trying to get past him.” ᴹ

Rimuru turns toward him very slowly.

“Mau.” ᴿ

“He would know. He would agree first. We'd build the entire thing around bringing him out safely.” ᴹ

DIO puts the paper flat.

“Say it.” ᴰ

Mau looks at the badly drawn figure, then at the name beneath it.

“They see something kill Sung Jin-Woo.” ᴹ

The room becomes so quiet that he hears someone moving a bucket in the passage outside.

Rimuru rubs his face with one hand.

Ashborn looks at the page for another moment.

Then he laughs.

Mau has heard him amused. He has seen him smile at things other people thought were not jokes. He has never heard this, a low sound which catches once before Ashborn can finish it.

“That is the stupidest idea you have brought through that door.” ᴬˢ

“So?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Ashborn sits down.

“Explain it properly.” ᴬˢ

DIO starts to smile.

“Does that mean we can examine the models?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“It means I want to hear how you intend to avoid actually killing him.” ᴬˢ

“That's a very reasonable place to start,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau turns the page over again. Noise has gone completely still with attention.

* * *

They finish with fewer permissions than Mau's first excited explanation might have suggested.

DIO agrees to examine the record of the crossing. Ashborn agrees to discuss what the old models might reveal to an observer. Neither has authorized activation yet. Jin-Woo has not agreed to die theatrically for anyone, and the house has heard none of the practical questions that would follow if it did work.

It is enough for a next conversation.

When Mau gathers the pages, DIO keeps the drawing.

“Why?” he asks. ᴹ

“I would like to remember the beginning.” ᴰ

Rimuru is looking at the figure again.

“It has six fingers.” ᴿ

“It's a god.” ᴹ

“On one hand.” ᴿ

Ashborn laughs again, quieter this time. Mau leaves them with the drawing and takes the useful page back to Jin-Woo.
