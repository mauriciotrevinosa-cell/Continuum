# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 4 — Something Worth Coming Home To

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S11E02
**Continuity state:** B+177 afternoon. Household hears the false-deity/family proposal and agrees only to local feasibility work. No departure, live imitation, compelled family extraction or activation of retained guest-derived models. Jin-Woo's family names beyond wife/son not enumerated; source history is his account, not combined canon maxima.

*When: Yuka sees the drawing DIO has kept, she asks whether the god is meant to have a hat.*

“That is its head.” ᴹ

“Oh.” ʸ

Mau folds the paper before anybody else can contribute. DIO allowed him to bring it to this meeting on the condition that he return it. He is beginning to suspect that her reasons were less solemn than she made them sound.

They have borrowed the common-room table after lunch. People arrive with the things they were already doing: Fern with accounts, Senku still removing a mark from his wrist, Kaneki carrying the cup Touka told him he had left behind. Yuta comes with Maki. Gojo tries to sit near Jin-Woo and finds Shoko has chosen that place for reasons he understands perfectly.

Mai comes to see what Mau means by a construction which will need help looking convincing. She stays when she hears why.

“Before anyone gets excited,” Rimuru says, “we haven't agreed to build it.” ᴿ

“You invited the wrong people for that condition,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

Senku pulls a sheet toward him.

“Start with what the audience has to believe.” ˢᵉⁿ

Jin-Woo looks around the table. There are more people than he expected. Mau asks whether he wants to explain the war himself or have the account repeated. He chooses to tell it. This time Yuka brings the first drawing out before he needs to ask for paper.

* * *

They get through the history, the present threat and the possibility of bringing his family before anyone touches the drawing with the unfortunate head.

Fern asks whether coming here would leave people without the defenders they have now. Kaneki asks what has already been left in place. Jin-Woo answers both, correcting the impression that every shadow he commands must follow him through a doorway to remain loyal.

“But this connection is different,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“Yes. I can't promise to give orders through a world that isn't answering me.” ᴶʷ

Mau watches Yuta put that distinction beside the question he was preparing to ask. Nobody at the table treats an unproven route as an available rescue just because somebody needs it badly.

Touka comes to stand beside Kaneki's chair.

“When you contact your family, tell them what the place is before you ask whether they want it,” she says. ᵀᵒ

Jin-Woo looks at her.

“I will.” ᴶʷ

“And tell them you're here. They might want that answer first.” ᵀᵒ

His gaze drops to the drawing of the road. He nods once.

Mau asks her to stay. She says she can stay until Ichika finishes what she is making with Ori. From the sound of the discussion near the window, that gives them a little time.

* * *

The proposed god gets a less respectful reception.

Gojo asks whether it needs to stand up. Senku asks whether anyone actually knows how these observers identify a god. Frieren asks which part of the display they intend to conceal from somebody who can examine magic while pretending to be impressed.

“Thank you,” Shoko says to her. ˢʰ

“That was my question,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“You asked whether it could have an entrance.” ˢʰ

“An entrance can be informative.” ᴳ

Jin-Woo answers Frieren first. He describes what has drawn the attention of the forces he has fought: traces recognized across a distance, authority they test by sending something through, the difference between a noise which brings a scout and a threat which makes an army hesitate.

“Then a picture won't do it,” she says. ᶠ

“It wasn't going to be only a picture,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Show us what it has to do.” ᶠ

He takes out a second page. The first one remains folded.

The performance needs a few convincing acts, each with a reason to be seen. It needs the presence the invaders think they recognize, something unexpected in how that presence behaves, and an apparent victory over the defender they know. Mau outlines those parts with Jin-Woo correcting him whenever the enemy would have no reason to witness what he is proposing.

By the third correction, Senku has drawn a box around the smallest version of the plan.

“Try this before making it enormous.” ˢᵉⁿ

Mai studies it.

“That still looks terrible.” ᴹᵃ

“It's a diagram.” ᴹ

“So was the first one, apparently.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka laughs into her hand. Mau looks at her, betrayed.

“I love you,” she says. ʸ

“That felt unrelated.” ᴹ

“It isn't.” ʸ

* * *

Maki stops the conversation at the apparent death.

“Explain that part without saying it'll be fine.” ᴹᵏ

Mau puts the pencil down.

“We find out what they can see first. Then we test the impression here. Jin-Woo isn't hurt for the sake of selling it.” ᴹ

“And the person doing the rescuing?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

“Also isn't hurt for the sake of selling it.” ᴹ

“Good. Keep that sentence.” ˢʰ

Jin-Woo turns toward her.

“I can tell you what wouldn't injure me. I can't tell you yet what that would look like from here.” ᴶʷ

“Then we start there.” ˢʰ

Yuta asks whether there needs to be a real target at all. Mai offers the possibility of a shell. Senku wants to know which of the things they are trying to fool would notice the difference. For several minutes the conversation becomes the useful kind of mess, with two people speaking at once until Fern puts the questions in an order and makes them finish one.

Mau looks at Jin-Woo during a pause.

“Still willing to hear the stupid idea?” ᴹ

“It's getting less stupid.” ᴶʷ

“Give us time,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Jin-Woo actually laughs.

* * *

When the room is beginning to thin, Gojo asks about the other possibility.

“If it works well enough for them to believe it, what stops us from giving them the real fight they're afraid of?” ᴳ

Mau looks toward the doorway that leads back to the guests.

“For one thing, we don't have it. We have pieces we haven't earned the right to use carelessly.” ᴹ

Gojo waits.

“For another, he asked for help because there's always another place to defend,” Mau says, turning toward Jin-Woo. “If we find a way to make them hesitate, I'd like to try that before making a bigger war.” ᴹ

Gojo leans back.

“All right.” ᴳ

It is such an uncomplicated answer that Mau looks at him again. Gojo gestures toward the paper as if to say that he was asking a question, not laying a trap for his student.

Yuta has stayed behind with the cups. He sets one in the tray and asks Jin-Woo what he thinks.

Jin-Woo doesn't answer immediately. He looks past the table, at Ichika trying to carry something she has made without letting Ori see that one part is falling off. Touka goes to help only when Ichika calls her.

“I think I'd like to ask my wife whether she'd come,” he says. ᴶʷ

“That's worth finding out,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

* * *

They leave the meeting with work divided among people who volunteered for it. Senku wants a physical outline and something he can measure. Frieren wants an example of the signal before she discusses concealing its edges. DIO will look at the crossing trace when Mau can take her observations without asking her to step outside safety. Ashborn still has the right to refuse the model, even if everybody else becomes attached to the scheme.

Fern takes the list away before someone adds an estimated date.

“We don't have one,” she says. ᶠᵉ

“I wasn't going to write one.” ᴹ

She looks at Senku.

He raises both hands, enjoying himself.

Yuka stays while Mau stacks the papers. She reaches past him, takes the folded drawing and opens it.

“Six fingers?” ʸ

“Apparently.” ᴹ

“Could we ask Mai to help?” ʸ

“I was hoping you would defend it.” ᴹ

She puts it back in his hand and kisses the side of his face.

“I married you. I don't have to marry your drawing.” ʸ

At the door, Jin-Woo turns back to ask whether they need the table cleared before he goes to look at the road again. Mau tells him the watcher has reported no change.

He can still go if he wants.

Jin-Woo looks at the tray in Yuta's hands instead.

“Where do these go?” he asks. ᴶʷ
