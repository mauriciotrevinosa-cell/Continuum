# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 5 — Where the Other Hand Goes

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S11E03
**Continuity state:** B+177 late afternoon. Shoko reviews both participants; limited non-technique practice with wooden trainers. Permanent physical gains remain active. No attempt at full-output bout or reversion of body stats. Jin-Woo's locally available body is compared through controlled contact, not source-maximum tier math. Movement observations only; learner not constructed yet.

*When: Jin-Woo asks Mau what he does with his empty hand, Mau discovers that he has no answer he trusts enough to demonstrate slowly.*

“Depends what the other person is doing.” ᴹ

“Then start with me.” ᴶʷ

They are in the practice yard, where Shoko has allowed a short lesson on the condition that neither man interpret the word short as permission to finish it violently. She has checked Mau's recovery before agreeing and asked Jin-Woo enough questions about the crossing to make Gojo complain that nobody interviewed him so politely.

“You interrupt,” she said. ˢʰ

Now she is sitting near the edge with Yuka. Yuta has brought two practice knives and a longer wooden blade, then stayed because he wants to see the lesson himself. Gojo is leaning against the wall in the position of a man absolutely prepared to have opinions.

Mau leaves his real daggers and katana on the rack. They remain where everyone can see them. His body doesn't become weaker when he lets go of the weapons; he has to make his movements smaller himself.

Jin-Woo takes one wooden knife.

“Show me how you'd get past that.” ᴶʷ

Mau chooses a step to the outside, catches the wrist and moves into the space beside Jin-Woo's shoulder. He stops before the edge of his own trainer reaches the neck.

“Good?” he asks. ᴹ

“Where is my other hand?” ᴶʷ

Something taps Mau below the ribs.

Jin-Woo is holding the second practice knife, taken from the bench before they began.

Mau looks down at it.

“That feels dishonest.” ᴹ

“You watched me pick it up.” ᴶʷ

Gojo makes an unbearable noise from the wall.

“Thank you for your contribution,” Mau tells him. ᴹ

“Any time.” ᴳ

* * *

They begin again.

This time Mau watches the free hand and gets tagged on the wrist by the first knife. He has seen both motions. His eyes supply more information than he can use in the order he needs it.

Jin-Woo stops before Mau can turn the failed exchange into a faster one.

“What changed?” ᴶʷ

“I followed the other hand.” ᴹ

“With what?” ᴶʷ

Mau almost points to his eyes. Then he looks down at his own stance.

His shoulder has turned with his attention. The movement opened the wrist Jin-Woo touched.

“My whole body, apparently.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo nods.

“You don't need to face everything you know is there.” ᴶʷ

Mau tries the step without an opponent. Then he tries it with Jin-Woo's hand held still. Neither attempt feels like fighting. On the third, Jin-Woo changes the angle a little and Mau makes the same mistake again.

“I saw that.” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴶʷ

“I knew you were going to do it.” ᴹ

“Then you know what you're practising.” ᴶʷ

There is nothing smug in the answer. Mau finds this more annoying than if there were.

Yuta asks to see the step from the other side. Jin-Woo shows him. The first thing Yuta does is move his elbow out of the way of a cut which hasn't happened yet.

“That one,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“I have had help with this mistake,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

Mau turns to Gojo.

“Did you teach me that?” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴳ

“When?” ᴹ

“Apparently at a time when you were admiring my other qualities.” ᴳ

Shoko doesn't look up.

“There aren't that many to divide his attention.” ˢʰ

Yuka laughs so hard she has to put down her cup.

* * *

They take the exchange at walking speed until Mau can finish in balance. Then Jin-Woo lets him try once with a little more pace.

Mau succeeds.

He is pleased enough that the next attempt fails immediately.

Jin-Woo catches the wooden knife before it can hit the ground and gives it back handle-first.

“What happened?” ᴶʷ

“I decided I'd learned it.” ᴹ

“That's a difficult habit.” ᴶʷ

Gojo folds his arms. For once he says nothing.

Mau rolls the trainer between his right fingers. His left hand will not close around the other grip the same way; two fingers keep their old limit. Jin-Woo asks him to show the range he actually has, then changes the hand position so Mau can use the grip without pretending those fingers will provide a catch they cannot make.

“Don't build the movement around a hand you don't have,” he says. ᴶʷ

Mau looks at him.

“I'm used to compensating.” ᴹ

“Then let's make the compensation part of the movement.” ᴶʷ

It takes several attempts to stop thinking of the adjusted grip as the lesser version. When it works, it works because Mau can keep the elbow closer. He loses a little reach and gains a cleaner way back.

Yuka comes over during the pause to look at his hand. She knows what Jin-Woo has changed before either man explains it.

“That looks more comfortable.” ʸ

“It is.” ᴹ

“Are you going to keep doing it the other way because it feels like winning an argument?” ʸ

Mau looks injured.

“You have no faith in me.” ᴹ

She kisses his cheek and goes back to Shoko.

“I have records.” ʸ

* * *

The closest they get to testing strength is an accident of pressure.

Jin-Woo braces Mau's forearm to show where the line should hold. Mau meets it, corrects too far and starts to push through instead of changing the angle. For one breath both men feel the resistance. The practice knife creaks.

They release at once.

Shoko stands up.

“That was your warning.” ˢʰ

“We stopped,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Yes. Keep doing that.” ˢʰ

Jin-Woo looks at the pale mark in the wood, then at his own hand. He has been adjusting since the crossing. Here, for the first time, he has something to compare the answer to.

“Closer to what I had before the larger changes,” he says. ᴶʷ

Gojo steps away from the wall.

“How far before?” ᴳ

“A fight against a hunter named Thomas Andre. I remember what it took to move him.” ᴶʷ

“Is that bad?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Jin-Woo glances at Mau.

“No. It's a great deal to have.” ᴶʷ

Mau feels the place on his arm where the pressure stopped. He has no useful way to turn that feeling into a number, but he recognizes the economy of what Jin-Woo did with it. No wasted reach. No second motion to recover the first.

“Then teach me to stop breaking the wooden ones,” he says. ᴹ

Jin-Woo offers the knife again.

“That was going to be the next part.” ᴶʷ

* * *

They finish when Shoko said they would.

Mau asks for one final repetition after she calls time. She considers him for a moment and allows the slow version. He completes the step, holds the finish and checks where the second hand would have reached him.

Jin-Woo changes that hand.

Mau starts to follow it with his shoulder, catches himself and laughs.

“I hate this.” ᴹ

“You asked.” ᴶʷ

“I know. Terrible decision.” ᴹ

He puts the trainer away instead of finding a reason to stay. Yuta asks Jin-Woo whether he would mind showing the adjusted entry again another day. Gojo says he will remember this public betrayal.

“You can join,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

For a moment Gojo has nothing clever ready. Shoko enjoys that enough to forgive the rest of his afternoon.

As they leave, Mau can feel Noise examining the remembered motion. Ciel is following a different part: the instant between the information Mau received and the choice his body made.

*Could we keep that?* he asks. ᴹ

*The observation is already retained,* Ciel says. ᶜ

*I mean in a way I can use when he's not holding my arm.* ᴹ

Noise's attention turns toward him.

*Explain,* it says. ᴺ

Yuka reaches for Mau's hand on the path. He takes it and tells her he has had an idea for his homework.

“Does it do the homework for you?” she asks. ʸ

“I'm being attacked from all sides today.” ᴹ

She squeezes his hand. He starts explaining it to her before they get to the door.
