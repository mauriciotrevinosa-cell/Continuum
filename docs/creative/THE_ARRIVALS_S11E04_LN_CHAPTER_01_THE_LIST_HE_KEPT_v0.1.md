# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 7 — The List He Kept

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S11E04
**Continuity state:** B+178 morning. Mau keeps promised project review with Noise/Ciel. DIO participates as invited friend; retained guest-derived models remain closed. Narrow movement-review work continues, other concepts reviewed rather than granted. Invitation to Yuka requested, no prior personal meeting retconned.

*When: Mau arrives after breakfast, DIO has a pencil ready and Noise has already arranged the work.*

He knows the difference between the attention which follows him everywhere and the presence waiting in the shared guest space. The dark outline is turned toward the entrance. Beside it, DIO has moved her chair so there is room to see the table.

“Am I late?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“No,” DIO says. ᴰ

Noise checks something only it can see.

“After breakfast.” ᴺ

“I did say that.” ᴹ

He takes off the coat he has been wearing in the cold passage and puts it where he will remember it. Rimuru has gone to speak with Hollis about the next crossing window. Yuka is finishing the morning exercise she wanted with Maki; before parting, she reminded Mau to listen to the answers instead of planning his next question through them.

“An ambitious request,” he told her. ᴹ

She kissed him and sent him on his way.

Now he settles at the threshold, where he can see the work without walking into the space containing its presenter. Ciel takes the local form she has used for their visits, her place supported as before. DIO looks pleased to have the whole group assembled.

“Where would you like to start?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Noise begins with the first thing on its list.

There are considerably more things than Mau expected.

* * *

Some of the work is familiar. A response from Momo's broom, held where it can be examined without pretending Mau has learned to fly one. The distinction between a fold Yuka initiates and one Mau holds for her. Information from Jin-Woo's arrival, including the part which does not match a shape Mau already has.

Others are older.

Noise brings forward a movement from before the wedding, then a correction Ciel kept separate when Mau was recovering. Mau remembers postponing both. He remembers why. Neither reason makes the list shorter.

“You kept all of this ready?” he asks. ᴹ

Noise indicates the work.

“Yes.” ᴺ

“How much of it can you actually try?” ᴹ

The outline moves toward the first set, stops, then points at three different places.

Ciel explains the distinction Noise is struggling to phrase. One has enough structure for a small trial. Another needs an answer from Mau about what he wants it to do. The third might become useful if he lets them compare two existing operations without attempting to run both through him at once.

Mau asks for the first one. Noise starts to bring it closer.

“Explain it first,” Mau adds. ᴹ

The movement stops.

DIO puts her hand on the table.

“He was doing that.” ᴰ

“He was about to show me.” ᴹ

“A respected teaching method.” ᴰ

Mau looks at her. She looks back with the expression of someone whose friend is being treated very unfairly indeed.

Ciel says they can do both.

* * *

The first trial belongs to the learner they discussed yesterday.

Noise has found a way to keep the selected attempt apart from the stream of everything Mau sees. Ciel can call up one comparison, leave it for him to examine and close it without asking him to remember where it belongs in the larger record.

The sample contains the shoulder turn. Nothing else.

Mau tries to add the knife angle.

“One,” Ciel says. ᶜ

“I wanted to check whether you remembered.” ᴹ

“I did.” ᶜ

He can review the motion, pause at the point before he follows the second hand, and see where the mistake begins. When he asks what happens if Jin-Woo moves differently, the presentation offers no prediction. It has recorded one exchange. Ciel tells him they need more actual attempts before there is anything reliable to compare.

“That part I like,” Mau says. ᴹ

Noise turns toward him.

“It tells me what it doesn't know.” ᴹ

The dark head tilts. Ciel looks pleased, though whether at the explanation or at the little sister's first useful behavior, Mau cannot tell.

He agrees to try a few slow repetitions outside later. That authorizes the small review process, not every design on the table. Noise acknowledges the limit and immediately brings forward the second item.

* * *

By the time they have examined four, Mau has asked to stop twice and return to something he didn't understand. Noise has asked to begin construction three times. Ciel has rejected one comparison because the observations were made under conditions too different to say what Mau wanted them to say.

DIO has been supportive throughout.

Suspiciously supportive.

“He has waited a long time,” she says when Mau asks whether the next experiment can wait until after Jin-Woo has explained its source. ᴰ

“I know.” ᴹ

“With great patience.” ᴰ

“You were there for some of the reasons.” ᴹ

“I am here for this reason.” ᴰ

Noise looks from her to Mau, then folds its hands in front of itself in a remarkably similar position.

Mau points at DIO.

“Don't encourage him.” ᴹ

She puts a hand to her chest.

“I am supporting my friend.” ᴰ

Noise watches the gesture.

Then its own hand rises to the same place.

“Friend.” ᴺ

Mau has to look away. When he looks back, Ciel is making no attempt to conceal her satisfaction.

“Three,” he says. ᴹ

“You invited us,” she says. ᶜ

* * *

The thing Noise wants most turns out to be the broom.

It returns to the response recorded when Momo corrected its angle. Mau recognizes the familiar shape and the point where his own Authority held it up. Noise keeps moving past that part to the moment after he released it, when Momo took the balance back for herself.

“You want me to fly it?” he asks. ᴹ

Noise shakes its head.

“Returns,” it says. ᴺ

Ciel lets it try again before helping. Noise is interested in the correction: the broom tilts, the rider shifts, the thing finds a usable balance instead of continuing to fall. Holding it with Authority answers a different question. Noise wants to understand how Momo's own technique receives and answers that changing position.

“You would need her to show you more,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Ask,” Noise says. ᴺ

He looks at the unfinished sequence again. It has a gap at exactly the place where he had assumed he could simply hold the thing straighter.

“You want to understand it even if I don't use it?” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴺ

He thinks of the number of times he has asked what a piece of work will let him do. The question has not always been wrong. It has not always been the whole question either.

“Then put that down too,” he says. “We ask Momo. And work can stay work.” ᴹ

DIO picks up the pencil.

Noise follows the mark she makes on the page.

Mau gives the new line a long enough look to remember it.

* * *

They have a next time before they finish.

This afternoon Mau will test the narrow shoulder review after his practice with Yuka. Tomorrow they will compare what happened, then return to the project which requires Jin-Woo's explanation. The observation of the guest is not an agreement to build his whole power overnight.

Noise accepts this with much better grace than Mau expected.

Then it turns to DIO.

She looks at it, waits, and gives the smallest encouraging nod.

“There was another request,” she tells Mau. ᴰ

He puts his coat back down.

“Okay.” ᴹ

Noise takes longer over this than over the technical explanation.

“Wife.” ᴺ

Mau stares.

“You want to build a wife?” ᴹ

Ciel's expression changes first. DIO closes her eyes with a patience so theatrical that Mau immediately knows he has said something that will be preserved against him.

“Your wife, Mau,” Ciel says. ᶜ

Noise nods.

“He would like to meet her properly,” DIO says. ᴰ

“Oh.” ᴹ

“An excellent beginning,” Ciel says. ᶜ

Mau looks from one to the other.

“You've been talking.” ᴹ

Neither woman denies it.
