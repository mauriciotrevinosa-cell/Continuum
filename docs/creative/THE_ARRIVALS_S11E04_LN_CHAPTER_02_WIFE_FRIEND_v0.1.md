Friendship visit does not change MY marriage, Noise origin, or establish romance among inner friends.

*When: Yuka asks whether she should bring anything, Mau says she has already been invited with more preparation than he received for his wedding.*

“That sounds unlikely.” ʸ

“They have been rehearsing.” ᴹ

“What did you do?” ʸ

“Why would you assume I did something?” ᴹ

She keeps looking at him.

“I misunderstood one word.” ᴹ

“Which word?” ʸ

He tells her. Yuka has to put a hand against the wall while she laughs. When she can speak again, she says she would like to go, provided the invitation isn't an emergency disguised as a visit.

“It isn't.” ᴹ

“Then let me finish putting this away.” ʸ

He helps her return the practice cloth to the basket. Maki asks whether they will still need the yard after lunch. Yuka says yes. Mau is pleased to discover that the part of the afternoon he has been threatened with remains intact.

They go together.

* * *

Yuka knows the guest entrance and who is staying beyond it. She has brought things to its threshold before. Knowing the arrangement does not prepare her for Noise standing beside a chair with one hand resting on its back.

The shadow outline is taller than she expected. Its edges move just enough to make her look twice, seeking a face in the place where it turns toward her.

She doesn't find one in the ordinary way.

“Hello,” she says. ʸ

“Wife.” ᴺ

DIO is seated close by, ready to help and very clearly trying not to do so before being asked. Ciel has taken the other place at the table. Mau remains at the open threshold, where Yuka can see him without looking away from her host.

“Yuka is fine,” she tells Noise. ʸ

It attends to the name.

“Yuka.” ᴺ

“Yes.” ʸ

The chair moves a little. Noise stops, looks at DIO and moves it back the other way. Yuka steps closer.

“Is that for me?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴺ

“Thank you.” ʸ

She sits. DIO settles with the satisfaction of someone whose friend has successfully survived an introduction of considerable importance.

Mau catches Yuka's eye.

She gives him a look which says that he is going to behave.

* * *

Noise begins by showing her the learner.

At first the explanation comes in the form it would have used with Mau: a short phrase, an indication of where the remembered motion belongs and a shift of attention which would have supplied the part between them.

Yuka listens, then glances at Ciel.

“I think I missed something.” ʸ

Ciel starts to answer. Noise raises its hand.

“I can.” ᴺ

She stops immediately.

“Of course.” ᶜ

Noise looks back at the work. DIO gives it time. Mau stays so still at the doorway that Yuka wants to smile at him and doesn't, in case Noise mistakes it for an answer to something it hasn't managed to say yet.

“He sees,” Noise says. ᴺ

“Mau?” ʸ

“Yes. Much.” ᴺ

“Sometimes too much.” ʸ

Noise's head turns toward her more sharply.

She thinks of Mau holding a spoon over supper while trying to explain how his entire shoulder betrayed him.

“He told me about the lesson.” ʸ

Noise brings forward the narrow comparison. DIO offers a spare sheet. With Ciel helping only when invited, the explanation reaches the page: where Mau stood, what he followed, where the second knife could reach him.

Yuka draws a little arrow beside the shoulder.

“Here?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴺ

“And you can keep this much apart so he can study it?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴺ

She looks at the drawing, then up at Noise.

“That sounds useful. I think Grandma has told me the same thing about looking after my whole body when only one hand needs to move.” ʸ

The outline becomes very attentive.

For the next few minutes Yuka is the one explaining. Noise listens to the description of the exercise without reaching through her established connection to inspect something she has not offered. When it wants to compare the two, it asks.

She says they can begin with her drawing.

* * *

DIO brings the next project forward only after Noise has finished with the first.

It is incomplete enough that Yuka asks what the finished thing is meant to do. Noise explains the part which holds a chosen problem apart from the surrounding information. Ciel supplies a word when it pauses. Noise repeats the word, considers it and chooses another.

Mau has heard the same idea this morning. It is different listening while it tries to explain it to someone new.

Yuka asks what it still needs.

“Time,” Noise says. ᴺ

Mau closes his eyes briefly.

“A decision,” Noise adds. ᴺ

“From him?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Together,” Ciel says. “About the scale of the first test.” ᶜ

Yuka nods. She doesn't offer Mau's afternoon or volunteer his body for it. She asks Noise how it would start if the first test could be small.

There is an answer ready for that.

It comes out much more quickly.

DIO picks up her pencil and finds herself with nothing to add. She puts it down, smiling.

* * *

After the third explanation, Yuka asks a question which stops the whole presentation.

“Which one would you be happiest to finish?” ʸ

Noise turns toward DIO.

DIO looks back with equal seriousness, as though this possibility should have been discussed in their preparation and someone has neglected it.

“You can think about it,” Yuka says. ʸ

Noise looks at each piece in turn. The learner is close enough to a test to be satisfying. The larger observation still has places it cannot explain. The response from Momo's broom has been waiting longer and carries no obvious urgency Mau can point to.

Its hand stops beside that one.

“This.” ᴺ

“What do you like about it?” ʸ

Noise begins to answer, then stops trying to make the answer useful to her. With a few marks on the page it shows the broom leaning and Momo changing its angle. Yuka recognizes the movement. The part Noise cannot yet follow is how Momo tells the broom it has gone far enough. Holding it still would be easy. Understanding how it finds a new balance would answer the question Noise actually wants to ask.

“Close,” it says. ᴺ

“You want to see if you're right.” ʸ

The outline goes still.

“Yes.” ᴺ

Mau finds himself looking down at his own hands. He has asked that question of Senku and Gojo. He has asked Yuka what she wants to try with a technique he helped make available to her. Hearing her ask it here reveals a space he had walked past without noticing.

He lets Noise finish.

* * *

Yuka stays long enough that her host stops checking DIO after every question.

She asks whether she may come back when the first review has been tested. Noise says yes so quickly that Ciel turns her face away for a moment. DIO asks whether they should put another chair nearer the table next time.

“This one is good,” Yuka says. ʸ

Noise looks at the chair with visible satisfaction.

When she stands, it starts to move the work aside, as though the visit needs a properly cleared ending. She tells it she enjoyed being invited and asks whether she can keep the little shoulder drawing.

Noise looks toward Mau.

“It's his embarrassing shoulder,” she says. “I should ask you, too.” ʸ

“It has become public property.” ᴹ

She folds the page once and takes it with her.

* * *

Mau stays after Yuka goes to wash her hands before lunch.

Noise has begun arranging the same pieces again. It puts one down, adjusts the distance to the next and stops. Mau recognizes the uncertainty before it asks anything.

“You did excellently,” he says. ᴹ

Noise turns.

“She liked you.” ᴹ

The hand leaves the edge of the work.

“Wife… friend?” ᴺ

“Always.” ᴹ

DIO lets out a small breath. Then she starts with the explanation Noise completed for itself, moves to how carefully it listened and reaches the moment it asked before comparing Yuka's exercise.

“And you chose what you wanted to show her,” she says. ᴰ

Noise listens without moving.

“Thank you, friend.” ᴺ

DIO's next compliment doesn't arrive. She puts her hand on the table beside its own instead.

Ciel says the preparation should also be recorded.

Mau looks at her.

“And the wife question?” ᴹ

“Already recorded.” ᶜ

DIO starts laughing. Noise looks at her, then at Mau. It cannot quite reproduce the sound, but the pleasure reaches him clearly enough that he laughs too.

When he joins Yuka in the passage, she takes his hand before he says anything.

“He was nervous,” she says. ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

“You could have warned me.” ʸ

“Would it have changed anything?” ᴹ

She looks back at the open door.

“I might have been nervous too.” ʸ
