# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 9 — The Wrong Fold

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S11E05
**Continuity state:** B+178 afternoon. Yuka practices her existing Sky access and own choices with Mau as chosen partner. Established contract source/load unchanged. First Movement Learner narrow-review test compares a recorded shoulder error after action, no motor execution or live automatic mastery. No new combat technique unlocked.

*When: Yuka tells Mau that he is allowed to move, he asks whether that is a rule or a threat.*

“It depends how well you move.” ʸ

She has brought two cloth balls, a length of cord and the napkin he used at supper. The yard is free because Maki agreed to let her have it after lunch. Mau puts their water on the bench, leaves his real weapons beside it and asks what she wants him to do first.

“Throw one at me. Here.” ʸ

She indicates her shoulder.

He makes the first throw too gentle. Yuka catches it without using the fold at all and looks at him.

“I am extraordinarily supportive,” he says. ᴹ

“Support the exercise.” ʸ

The second throw is useful. She folds the surface in front of her, sends the ball to the side and turns her hand for the motion Maki has been asking her to keep available. Mau catches it near his hip.

“Again?” he asks. ᴹ

“Wrong side this time.” ʸ

He throws. She starts the fold where she intended, catches her own mistake halfway and tries to use its new angle. The ball comes out lower than she planned and bounces off her boot.

They both look down.

“That one counts as a defense,” Mau says. ᴹ

“That one counts as you picking it up.” ʸ

He bows and retrieves it.

* * *

Yuka doesn't want the finished version of his correction.

He discovers this on the fourth attempt, when he starts explaining how he would move the surface and she raises one finger without looking away from the place where she lost it.

“Let me try this.” ʸ

He closes his mouth.

She lays the cord where the ball passed and uses the napkin to show herself what she thinks happened. The folded surface does not behave exactly like cloth. She knows that. The cloth is still useful for finding the part she keeps turning in the wrong direction.

Mau sits on the ground beside her while she works.

“Do you want me to hold that?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes. There.” ʸ

He holds the corner. She changes the angle and tries to explain what Maki meant by keeping the next action available. Mau recognizes his own empty hand from yesterday's lesson.

“You can know where it should go and still keep putting it somewhere else,” he says. ᴹ

“Exactly.” ʸ

“I have extensive experience.” ᴹ

Yuka bumps his knee with hers.

When they stand again, she asks him to move after the throw. It makes the target less obliging. He discovers that being a supportive husband includes having a cloth ball arrive behind his elbow while his wife looks delighted with herself.

“You did that on purpose.” ᴹ

“You said you would be the target.” ʸ

“I was hoping to be a dignified one.” ᴹ

She holds out her hand for the ball.

“Again.” ʸ

* * *

They stop when she asks to stop. There is no dramatic failure to force the pause. She has completed the motion twice and is beginning to use too much effort to repeat it.

Mau gives her water and asks whether she wants the view of the successful attempts. Yuka agrees to those two and the one immediately before them. Ciel helps present the chosen observations through the connection they already use, leaving the rest of the afternoon alone.

Yuka watches the comparison with her chin against Mau's shoulder.

“The second one was better,” she says. ʸ

“It was.” ᴹ

“I thought I was slower.” ʸ

“You were ready earlier.” ᴹ

She thinks about that while he puts the cord away. Then she catches his sleeve before he can pack the napkin.

“Your turn.” ʸ

“I knew there was a price.” ᴹ

* * *

The learner's first practical test is disappointingly small.

Mau makes the movement once without a knife. Ciel asks him to finish before calling up the comparison. He obeys, then looks at the marked shoulder turn in the presentation Noise has prepared.

He has turned too far again.

The learner can show it. It cannot make the second attempt correct.

He tries to follow the retained shape exactly and finishes with his weight in a place that feels wrong for his own grip. Ciel closes the comparison before he tries to move through discomfort simply because the picture looks authoritative.

*That reference uses the wrong finish for this hand,* she says. ᶜ

Noise checks the two observations. Yesterday Jin-Woo changed Mau's grip after the first sample was taken. The record the new process has been given needs that distinction attached to it.

Mau holds up his left hand and flexes what will flex.

“So the manual can be wrong for me.” ᴹ

*The manual can be incomplete,* Ciel says. *We need to label what it actually demonstrates.* ᶜ

Yuka listens to his explanation and asks whether Jin-Woo would have noticed.

“Immediately.” ᴹ

“Then you still need your teacher.” ʸ

“Cruel.” ᴹ

She smiles and holds out the cloth ball.

They make another attempt with the correct grip kept separate from the early observation. Mau holds the finish. He can feel the difference more clearly this time because he knows which thing he is trying to notice.

On the next one he misses it.

Noise records both.

* * *

Jin-Woo finds them on his way back from the outer road with Rimuru.

The watcher still has nothing new to report. DIO has asked for a few more details of the crossing, which Jin-Woo has tried to reconstruct without inventing certainty where he has none. He looks at the ball in Yuka's hand and the wooden knife Mau has finally taken from the bench.

“Should I ask?” ᴶʷ

“I'm helping him become dignified,” Yuka says. ʸ

Rimuru nods as if she has undertaken an admirable public service.

Mau shows Jin-Woo what the review can do. He describes its first mistake before presenting its first success. Jin-Woo asks him to repeat the movement without looking at it again.

The shoulder stays closer. The foot doesn't.

Jin-Woo points at the ground.

“You kept the part you studied. Now you have somewhere to begin the rest.” ᴶʷ

“That sounded almost encouraging.” ᴹ

“It was.” ᴶʷ

“I am unused to the method.” ᴹ

Rimuru says he is going to tell Gojo. Yuka says Gojo will claim he invented it.

* * *

They go to the kitchen for something to eat before supper because Yuka announces that being praised has made her hungry. Mau offers to obtain an entire meal. She wants the bread already being cut and a little of whatever smells like fruit.

Rem gives them a plate and tells them to leave enough for the people who have not come in yet. Umaru appears behind her to ask whether training is an accepted reason to eat early or whether she needs to demonstrate a skill first.

“You have a skill,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Which one?” ᵁ

“You arrived exactly when the food did.” ᴹ

She accepts this tribute and takes a slice.

Mau and Yuka carry theirs outside. She puts her feet beside his on the step and asks whether he minds when she tells him to stop explaining.

“Occasionally I am unbearable,” he says. ᴹ

“Occasionally?” ʸ

“This was an opportunity for a loving denial.” ᴹ

She tears off a piece of bread and offers it to him. He takes it from her fingers before answering properly.

“I like watching you figure something out. Remind me when I get in the way.” ᴹ

“I will.” ʸ

He rests his shoulder against hers. From somewhere inside, Rimuru laughs at something Jin-Woo has said. Yuka turns toward the sound, pleased by it.

“He looks less like he's waiting to leave,” she says. ʸ

Mau looks through the doorway. Their guest is holding a cup while Rimuru explains why Umaru has carried away two pieces of bread after requesting one. He is listening with an expression Mau is beginning to recognize as amusement.

“Maybe he can stay long enough to decide whether he wants to,” Mau says. ᴹ

Yuka puts her head against his.

They finish the plate before going back inside.
