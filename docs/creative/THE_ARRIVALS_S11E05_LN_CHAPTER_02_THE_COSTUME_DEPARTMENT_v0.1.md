# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 10 — The Costume Department

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S11E05
**Continuity state:** B+178 early evening. Mai/Nishimiya make costumes from existing cloth and small constructed fasteners, using Mai's established support agreement and advance warning habit. Mau's Beru and Jin-Woo's Beru remain separate. No new people, copied CT or model activation. Nishimiya broom demonstration offered for next day, not already transferred.

*When: Mai asks Mau for a piece of dark cloth, he asks whether it is going to become his problem.*

“Very likely.” ᴹᵃ

He is carrying the empty plate back with Yuka. Mai has caught him near the workshop because she wants his help before he disappears into the next conversation. Momo Nishimiya stands behind her holding a length of cord and trying much harder than Mai to look innocent.

“How much cloth?” he asks. ᴹ

Mai shows him. He takes an unused piece from the ordinary supplies he has stored, checks that it is the one she means and hands it over. The inventory closes without requiring a trip anywhere.

“And I'll be making two fasteners. Small ones,” she says. ᴹᵃ

“Okay. You know you can just—” ᴹ

“I'm telling you.” ᴹᵃ

“I have noticed.” ᴹ

Yuka asks what it is for. Mai looks at Momo, then at the direction of the common room.

“Professional equipment.” ᴹᵃ

“For what profession?” ᴹ

“You'll see.” ᴹᵃ

Mau decides that insisting will only give her time to make the answer worse. He lets them return to the workshop and tells Yuka he has a bad feeling.

“You supplied the material,” she says. ʸ

“That never used to be enough evidence.” ᴹ

* * *

The hats require two attempts.

Momo refuses to let Mai blame the first on the size of either head. Mai refuses to let Momo call the shape a useful experiment. They compromise by turning it inside out and discovering that this makes it considerably funnier.

“Keep that one,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“Who for?” ᴹⁿ

Mai gives her a look.

“No,” Momo says. ᴹⁿ

“You didn't even hear the suggestion.” ᴹᵃ

“I have heard your suggestions before.” ᴹⁿ

Mai laughs and reaches for the next piece of cloth. It catches under the cup beside her elbow. Momo moves the cup, smooths the fabric and keeps a hand on the corner while Mai measures.

They have been doing small things together for long enough that the next movement doesn't need to be negotiated aloud. Mai marks. Momo holds. When the scissors reach the place where her fingers are, she moves them before being asked.

The second hat works.

Mai sets it on her own head for a moment and asks for an opinion. Momo looks at her too long before deciding to laugh.

“Terrible,” she says. ᴹⁿ

“Good. Exactly the brief.” ᴹᵃ

* * *

Mau discovers the profession after supper.

Jin-Woo has joined Senku and Frieren for a discussion of what a convincing observer would actually notice. Mau comes in with the pages Ashborn has agreed to examine and stops in the doorway.

Both Beru are seated in the front row.

They have scarves. They have little hats. Someone has supplied lenses in frames which do absolutely nothing the eyes behind them require, and each has a notebook opened to a fresh page.

Mai is sitting far enough away to deny responsibility if anyone is foolish enough to ask.

Mau looks at her.

She smiles into her cup.

“I bought the cloth,” he says. ᴹ

“You provided the cloth,” she corrects. ᴹᵃ

Jin-Woo looks at his Beru.

“Do I need to ask?” ᴶʷ

“My king, we have considered the proposed performance.” ᴮʲ

Mau's Beru inclines its head with grave professional agreement.

“There are weaknesses.” ᴮᵐ

“We haven't performed it,” Mau says. ᴹ

“That is one,” his Beru says. ᴮᵐ

Rimuru has to sit down.

* * *

The criticism is, unfortunately, useful.

Jin-Woo's Beru objects to a staged collapse in which its king would fall before an audience understood he had resisted. Mau's Beru believes the audience should have time to appreciate the supposed opponent before the victory, particularly if the opponent must be seen overcoming somebody they already fear.

“You want him to look strong while losing?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Both Beru look at him as though he has finally caught up.

“Obviously,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Yuka starts laughing. Mai looks deeply pleased with her investment.

Senku asks whether either critic can describe what the observers have actually seen Jin-Woo survive. The tone changes. Jin-Woo's Beru supplies a short answer; Jin-Woo corrects the part which contains an unnecessary tribute to his greatness.

Frieren asks them to identify one visible moment which could carry the result. No speech, no list of powers, simply something an enemy could recognize and misunderstand.

The notebooks become less decorative.

Mau watches Mai listening. She has one elbow on the table and the little failed hat beside her. When Momo leans over to say something, Mai pushes the hat into her hands without taking her eyes off the argument.

Momo puts it on Mai's head.

Neither Beru breaks character.

* * *

Later, when the meeting has dissolved into several smaller ones, Momo asks Mau about the broom.

He glances at Mai.

“She didn't tell you?” ᴹ

“She told me you were interested in the correction. I'd rather you asked me which part you want to see.” ᴹⁿ

He explains Noise's question and the place where its observation stops. Momo thinks about it, then asks whether Mau intends to hold the broom still again.

“Not unless you ask.” ᴹ

“Good. That makes it harder to show what I'm doing.” ᴹⁿ

They arrange a demonstration tomorrow with a little empty space and no demand that she surrender the technique. Mai says she will be there to supervise Mau's behavior. Momo asks who will supervise hers.

“No one qualified,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

Mau hears Noise's unmistakable pleasure at the appointment. It is the same feeling he noticed after the visit with Yuka, directed now toward a question somebody has agreed to help it answer.

He thanks Momo.

She tells him to arrive on time.

* * *

On the way upstairs, Yuka catches him looking back at Mai, who is adjusting one of the scarves while Momo pretends not to help.

“What?” she asks. ʸ

“She looks happy.” ᴹ

Yuka follows his gaze.

“She does.” ʸ

He doesn't try to turn the observation into an intervention. Mai has made something ridiculous for friends who wanted it, spent an evening laughing and arranged to be somewhere tomorrow because she wants to be there.

He knows better than to improve that without being asked.

Yuka slips her arm around his.

Behind them, one Beru asks the other whether the scarf is suitable for a formal rehearsal. The answer takes long enough that Jin-Woo begins to apologize again.

Mau calls back that he has supplied the material and therefore bears some responsibility.

Mai raises her cup to him.
