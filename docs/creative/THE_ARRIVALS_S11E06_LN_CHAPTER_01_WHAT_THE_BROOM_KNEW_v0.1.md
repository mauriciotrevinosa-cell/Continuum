# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 11 — What the Broom Knew

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S11E06
**Continuity state:** B+179 morning. Nishimiya demonstrates her own existing broom technique by consent, with Mai present. Observation improves an incomplete control model, no usable copy awarded to Mau and no inspection of memories. Movement review remains narrow, permanent physical/body gains unchanged.

*When: Momo tells Mau to get on the broom, he asks whether this is revenge.*

“For what?” ᴹⁿ

“I'd like to know before accepting.” ᴹ

Mai is sitting on the low wall with two cups and the expression of someone who has been promised a good morning. She tells him to stop delaying the demonstration. Yuka, who has come to watch before her own practice, offers to hold his coat.

“You could look concerned,” he tells her. ᴹ

“I've seen you on a dragon.” ʸ

“A very different arrangement.” ᴹ

“You had a much smaller passenger telling you what to do.” ʸ

He has no answer to that. Ori's account of the last expedition has been repeated often enough that his role as assistant is a matter of public record.

Momo keeps the broom low. Mau puts his weight where she tells him and immediately discovers that knowing which way something is tilting does not teach him where to put his knee.

The handle rolls a little.

His feet reach the ground before anything dramatic can happen.

Mai claps once.

“Excellent landing.” ᴹᵃ

“Thank you.” ᴹ

* * *

Momo takes the broom back and asks what he felt.

“It moved.” ᴹ

“I noticed.” ᴹⁿ

“I knew which way.” ᴹ

“So did I. What did you do?” ᴹⁿ

He starts to describe the grip, then stops. He had braced as though the handle were a rail fixed to something else. The broom was answering the balance Momo maintained; his correction had simply become another piece of weight for her to account for.

“I tried to hold the thing I was sitting on.” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴹⁿ

“Very dignified theme developing this week.” ᴹ

Momo smiles despite herself and shows him the same turn without a passenger.

Noise follows the change with such attention that Mau asks whether they can see it once more, slower. Momo agrees. She keeps it close enough to the ground that the demonstration remains a demonstration, turning, returning to level and then deliberately letting the handle tilt farther before correcting it.

“There isn't one answer that stays right,” she says. “You keep answering.” ᴹⁿ

Mau repeats that inwardly for Noise.

*Keep answering.* ᴺ

*That's what we're looking at,* he tells it. ᴹ

* * *

They don't need Mau on the broom for the useful part.

Momo shows the response to a small change in weight, then to a movement that starts from the rider. Ciel keeps the observations separate. When Mai asks what the distinction looks like to him, Mau explains it with a cup set on the wall.

“If I hold it here, I can stop it falling.” ᴹ

“A remarkable contribution to drinkware,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

He gives her a look and continues.

“That doesn't tell me how someone else notices it starting to fall and corrects without pinning the whole thing in place.” ᴹ

Yuka moves the cup toward him before it can reach the edge.

“Or you could ask your wife.” ʸ

Noise makes a very pleased sound in the private channel.

Mau laughs.

“He likes you.” ᴹ

“I like him.” ʸ

Momo looks between them. Mai gives her the short explanation of the visit, then insists that she has not been informed of any plan to construct a spouse and is disappointed to have missed the conversation in which that required clarification.

“Nobody is constructing a spouse,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I know. It would need better supervision.” ᴹᵃ

* * *

The last demonstration is the one Noise cannot yet account for completely.

Momo lets the broom begin to turn, changes her own position and then asks it for a different direction before the first movement has finished. Ciel can mark the changes. Noise can follow where the relation alters. Neither has a finished answer for the way the corrections remain hers throughout.

Mau tells Momo that.

“Good,” she says. ᴹⁿ

He looks at her.

“You can ask again. I'd rather you told me than pretended you'd understood it.” ᴹⁿ

Noise retains the offered demonstration. The rest of her remains her own business. Mau asks permission to review that piece later and gets a yes limited to what she has shown.

“Thank you,” he says. ᴹ

Momo dismounts and turns the broom in her hand.

“Tell me when you want another look.” ᴹⁿ

“He will,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“I was speaking to him.” ᴹⁿ

“I know.” ᴹᵃ

They look at each other for a moment. Mai offers the second cup. Momo takes it and comes to sit beside her on the wall.

Yuka takes Mau's coat before he can forget it again. He offers to carry hers on the way back and receives a warning that he will not be allowed to classify it as a heroic rescue.

“What about public service?” ᴹ

“You can have that one.” ʸ

* * *

Noise asks to review the final turn while they are walking.

Mau tells it to keep the record ready until he has put his things down. It agrees without the disappointment he expected. The promised demonstration happened. There is a next thing to examine and someone willing to be asked about it.

When he joins DIO later, she asks whether the visit was useful.

“He didn't learn to ride the broom,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I wasn't asking about you.” ᴰ

Noise brings forward the observation with such obvious satisfaction that DIO puts the book aside.

Mau sits down to hear the explanation too.
