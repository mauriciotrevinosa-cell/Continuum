# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 14 — Someone Else Stood Up

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S11E07
**Continuity state:** B+179 evening. Mau invites Jin-Woo to established roof company with Gojo/Rimuru/Yuta. Discusses duty/rest without source-memory transfer, resignation from house leadership or new body/army access. Yuka knows location; no disappearance, fixed-hour alarm or insomnia relapse.

*When: Jin-Woo asks whether people go to the roof for a reason, Rimuru says it depends who has followed them.*

“Usually someone sensible,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“Usually someone carrying the thing you forgot,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau has brought cups in the inventory and has therefore avoided the accusation for tonight. Yuta brings the drink because he prefers to know where it is before Gojo begins offering to improve it. Jin-Woo comes last, after Mau tells him that the invitation involves sitting down rather than another examination of the proposed god.

Below them, there is enough activity to tell which windows are open. Somebody is laughing near the common room. A door closes; another opens before the sound has quite finished. Jin-Woo sits where he can see the path leading toward his guest room.

“Do you always keep looking that way?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Which way?” ᴶʷ

Mau points at the place an approaching person would first become visible.

Jin-Woo follows the gesture.

“I suppose I do.” ᴶʷ

Gojo passes him a cup.

“At least pick somewhere comfortable to do it.” ᴳ

* * *

Jin-Woo asks what Mau's life was like before the house.

“Before this world, I don't know,” Mau says. “I've told you a little of the rest. The ordinary parts kept changing.” ᴹ

“They still do,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“You are about to accuse me of something.” ᴹ

“I was going to say I like where you live.” ʸᵗ

“Suspicious kindness.” ᴹ

Rimuru explains that this is what Mau does when he has been caught feeling pleased about something. Gojo offers several embarrassing examples and is stopped by Yuta asking whether he would like a few of his own returned.

Jin-Woo listens to them with the same slight smile he wore at breakfast. When he asks about the people who teach here, Gojo answers properly.

“You wanted them to become stronger than you?” Jin-Woo asks. ᴶʷ

“I still do.” ᴳ

“Even now?” ᴶʷ

Gojo looks at Mau.

“Especially when he makes it annoying.” ᴳ

Mau raises his cup.

“An important part of my education.” ᴹ

Gojo starts to smile, then lets the joke finish before saying the next thing.

“There were days I got up because there wasn't another person to send. I wanted them to have more choices than that.” ᴳ

Jin-Woo's hand settles around his cup.

“I know that kind of day.” ᴶʷ

* * *

Mau watches the path for a moment before answering.

“I didn't set out to be the person in front,” he says. “I kept finding out what happened when I wasn't there.” ᴹ

No one on the roof asks him to prove the sentence with the worst memory he can supply.

“Then I could do more. So there were more things I couldn't see happening and leave alone.” ᴹ

Yuta nods. Rimuru watches him without interrupting.

“I like that people can sit here,” Mau says. “I like being able to help keep it that way. Sometimes I don't know how to stop checking whether there's one more thing I should have done first.” ᴹ

Gojo turns his cup between his hands.

“You came up here.” ᴳ

“After checking several things.” ᴹ

“We saw.” ᴿ

Mau laughs, caught.

Jin-Woo looks toward the lit windows below. A figure passes one of them, stops and returns for something left behind.

“My wife would have liked this,” he says. Then he corrects himself. “She might like it.” ᴶʷ

The change is small enough that Mau almost misses it.

“You could find out,” he says. ᴹ

“If there's a way.” ᴶʷ

“We're looking.” ᴹ

* * *

They hear the wagon before they see it.

Jin-Woo is on his feet as the first wheel reaches the turn. He pauses when nobody else rises. Below, one of Mau's soldiers steps out to signal the driver toward the unloading space. The cart carries the empty baskets Hollis asked to have returned, recognizable from the cloth tied to the side.

Mau confirms the report through the watch and tells the others.

Jin-Woo remains standing another moment.

Then he sits down.

No one says anything about it until Gojo notices his cup needs refilling and offers the pot.

“I still went first,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“Habit,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“Yes.” ᴶʷ

Rimuru looks down at the cart, where the soldier has lifted a basket from the hands of someone trying to carry three.

“There are worse things to learn than letting somebody finish the job they're already doing,” he says. ᴿ

Jin-Woo accepts more drink.

* * *

When they go down, Yuka is in the passage with Shizue. She has come to ask whether the cups need collecting before everyone forgets where they left them.

Mau shows her his empty hands.

“Stored.” ᴹ

“Excellent husband.” ʸ

“At last, recognition.” ᴹ

Shizue asks Rimuru whether he intends to keep standing in the doorway while she holds both books. He takes one immediately. Gojo says good night before Shoko can come looking for him, which Yuta considers suspiciously sensible.

Jin-Woo stops at his own turn.

“Tomorrow?” he asks Mau. ᴶʷ

“The lesson?” ᴹ

“That too. If DIO has anything else she needs to ask, I'd like to be there.” ᴶʷ

“I'll tell her.” ᴹ

Yuka waits until Jin-Woo has gone before taking Mau's arm.

“Good evening?” she asks. ʸ

He looks at the closed guest-room door, then toward the room where his wife is taking him.

“Yes.” ᴹ

She leans against him for the first few steps. He tells her the funny parts on the way, saving the rest for when they can sit together and he can ask what her evening was like too.
