# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 17 — The Seat Rem Chose

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S11E09
**Continuity state:** B+180 supper. Subaru still follows own uncertain lead and occupies agreed temporary lodging; no new Emilia sighting, departure or earned reconciliation. Rem/Umaru closeness and Ram/Seika remain voluntary, unlabeled. Casual clothes, no presumed maid duty. No new staff or shadow speaker.

*When: Umaru saves Rem a seat, she leaves a card on it face-down.*

“A warning?” Rem asks. ᴿᵉ

“A diplomatic invitation.” ᵁ

Rem turns it over. It is the card Umaru accused Mau of keeping out of the last game.

“He had it?” ᴿᵉ

“He had several opinions about whether I was allowed to ask.” ᵁ

Across the table, Mau looks up from helping Yuka divide the bread.

“I had the rules.” ᴹ

“A very convenient place to hide.” ᵁ

Yuka takes the bread out of his hands before he can try to argue while cutting it. She passes the basket to Rem, who takes a piece and sits where the card was waiting.

She is wearing the loose sleeves she chose for an afternoon with no kitchen work. Ram has already told her twice to keep one of them out of her cup. This time Umaru catches the cuff without interrupting her explanation of what Mau supposedly did.

Rem looks at the fingers around the cloth.

Umaru lets go when the sleeve is safe.

“Thank you,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

“An important protection of national resources.” ᵁ

Rem smiles into her cup.

* * *

Subaru sees the seat before he finds his own.

He has come back with mud on one boot and a page folded into the map he carries. The road he wanted to ask about is still a road somebody remembers differently depending on which direction they last traveled it. He has a place to try and no new name he can honestly call a lead.

Taihei moves a cup so there is room at the far end of the table. Subaru begins to ask whether anything needs carrying, then sees the space being offered and sits.

He looks toward Rem once.

Ram sees him look.

He keeps the question he was about to ask to himself.

Rem has seen him too. Her smile has gone, though Umaru is still speaking. She listens until the end of the sentence before putting down her cup.

“Can we play somewhere else after we eat?” she asks. ᴿᵉ

Umaru glances toward the other end, then back to her.

“Of course. We need somewhere the accused can't influence the court.” ᵁ

“You invited me to the game,” Mau says. ᴹ

“You may attend under supervision.” ᵁ

Yuka raises her hand.

“I'll do it.” ʸ

Rem laughs, a little late but genuinely. Ram returns to her food after seeing where the next seats will be.

* * *

Subaru leaves before the game begins.

He takes his plate back, asks Taihei where the road notes should be left if Hollis has already finished for the evening, and writes the part he can verify separately from the part he hopes is true. Nobody promises the distinction will make the search easier.

At the door he pauses as though there is one more thing he ought to say.

Rem is already walking with Umaru, carrying the cards in both hands while Umaru explains a new condition she has decided will make the result fairer.

Subaru goes to put the note where he has been told.

* * *

Seika finds Ram with the spare cups.

“Are you coming?” she asks. ˢᵏ

“To watch those two invent justice?” ᴿᵃ

“I was going outside for a little while.” ˢᵏ

Ram looks toward the game. Rem has chosen her place beside Umaru and is already refusing a rule that would let the president inspect everybody's cards.

“That would be cheating,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

“Oversight,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“Cheating,” Yuka says. ʸ

Ram puts the cups down.

“A little while,” she tells Seika. ᴿᵃ

Seika waits while she gets her coat. When they step out, Ram asks how practice went. Seika answers without making her explain what she has just been watching.

Inside, Mau agrees to one last game and accuses his wife of conspiring against him. Yuka tells him to draw his cards.

Rem has not forgotten why she asked to move. She is still glad she did.
