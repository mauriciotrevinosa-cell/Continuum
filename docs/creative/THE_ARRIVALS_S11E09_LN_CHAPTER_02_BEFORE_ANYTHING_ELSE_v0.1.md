## Chapter 18 — Before Anything Else

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S11E09
**Continuity state:** B+181 morning. First bounded two-way voice contact along repaired old communication trace, using Jin-Woo's voluntarily offered source reference and recorded one-time crossing. DIO works within refuge, no person/object route opened. Hae-In and Suho together per creator choice. No family consent/extraction yet, no broad guest mind-reading or original-world combat broadcast.

*When: the first voice comes through, Jin-Woo has been looking at the wrong place for several seconds.*

He is watching the narrow part of the shared space Ciel has set aside for the call. DIO told him that it would not need a face to carry a voice. He knows this. He has still been watching as though a face might appear if he can work out where it belongs.

Mau stays near the entrance with Rimuru. Yuka has asked whether Jin-Woo wants her nearby or outside; he asked her to stay until he knows the connection holds. Ashborn has left his own door closed. This morning belongs to the people Jin-Woo is trying to reach.

They have tested the close twice. The attempt will carry chosen sound only. Noise keeps the observed relations from the last pulse separate from any proposal to enlarge the route. Ciel is ready to stop it; DIO has agreed to tell them before fatigue becomes something she tries to work around.

Jin-Woo gives them a short message.

His name. His wife's name. Where to listen for the return.

The first attempt receives nothing.

On the second, something catches at the end of his name.

Then a woman says it back.

“Jin-Woo?” ᴴᶦ

He turns his head so quickly that Yuka sits forward.

“Hae-In.” ᴶʷ

There is a small sound on the other side, as though someone has put something down too hard. When Hae-In speaks again, her voice has the concentration of a person who refuses to spend the first useful breath on panic.

“Can you hear me?” ᴴᶦ

“Yes.” ᴶʷ

“Where are you?” ᴴᶦ

Jin-Woo looks toward Mau, then at the room around him.

“Somewhere I didn't know existed.” ᴶʷ

“Are you hurt?” ᴴᶦ

“No.” ᴶʷ

He says it again before she has to ask whether he means a different kind of no.

“I'm not hurt.” ᴶʷ

Yuka settles back a little. Mau can feel her hand finding his at the threshold.

Jin-Woo explains the opening and the road. Hae-In asks which side it closed on. He tells her what he knows and leaves the parts he cannot answer as questions. Her next few responses are practical enough that Mau understands how much of their life has required this kind of conversation.

Then she asks who is with him.

“People who gave me a place to stay.” ᴶʷ

“Names, Jin-Woo.” ᴴᶦ

He looks a little embarrassed.

“Mau. His wife, Yuka. Rimuru. There are others helping with this.” ᴶʷ

“Are they listening?” ᴴᶦ

“Yes. Would you like them to leave?” ᴶʷ

“Not yet.” ᴴᶦ

“He came here himself,” he says. “We just didn't send him back out into the cold.” ᴹ

“That can matter more than you think.” ᴴᶦ

Jin-Woo looks down.

The faint distortion returns at the edge of her voice. Ciel marks it for Mau without interrupting the conversation. DIO changes the support just enough to keep the return aligned with what they have already tested.

Then another voice comes through, farther away at first.

“Dad?” ˢᵘ

Jin-Woo stops looking at the floor.

Suho repeats the question closer to whatever place is receiving the call.

“I'm here,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“Mom said she heard you.” ˢᵘ

“She did.” ᴶʷ

There is a pause in which neither of them seems willing to begin with the war.

“You sound different,” Suho says. ˢᵘ

“I'm sitting down,” he says. ᴶʷ

Suho laughs once, surprised by the answer. It reaches the room cleanly. Mau has been waiting for a report about forces moving behind a broken line. He finds himself smiling before he realizes that the sound has made him do it.

“There are chairs here,” Jin-Woo adds. ᴶʷ

“That must be difficult for you,” Hae-In says. ᴴᶦ

Rimuru turns his face toward the wall. Yuka puts a hand over her mouth. Jin-Woo looks at them with a resignation Mau recognizes from men whose families have supplied an inconveniently accurate account.

“I'm learning.” ᴶʷ

They tell him what he asks first.

Hae-In and Suho are together. His sister and parents can be reached. The part of the war they know about has not suddenly ended because Jin-Woo has gone out of sight. There are people still carrying the duties he left them, and questions he will need to answer before anyone treats the interruption as a plan.

He listens without trying to solve all of them in the same breath.

When Suho asks whether he can return, Jin-Woo looks toward DIO.

She shakes her head once, not because the answer must be no forever, but because it would be a lie to make it yes now.

“We don't know how yet,” he says. ᴶʷ

“But you can speak,” she says. ᴴᶦ

“For a little while. We're testing it.” ᴶʷ

“Then tell me the part you were going to save for last.” ᴴᶦ

Jin-Woo looks at her name on the paper.

Mau realizes that his own wife has begun rubbing a thumb over the back of his hand. He holds hers more securely and gives the man at the table time to find his sentence.

“They asked whether you might want to come here,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Hae-In doesn't answer immediately.

Jin-Woo explains that there is no promise of an opening just because the voice works. He says they are studying a way to deter the forces in his world, and that the people helping him will not make his family's departure an unspoken price for that help.

Ciel signals that they are nearing the limit of the attempt. Mau tells Jin-Woo quietly. He repeats the message so the silence won't be mistaken for something happening to him.

“Can we speak again?” Hae-In asks. ᴴᶦ

“We will try after I have rested. I would rather give you another honest short conversation than promise one I cannot hold.” ᴰ

“Please,” Jin-Woo says. “Tell them I'm here. Tell them I want to hear them.” ᴶʷ

For a little while Jin-Woo stays exactly where he is.

DIO takes the water Rimuru brings her. Ciel confirms that the path is closed. Noise keeps the successful relation without building a larger opening around it. Nobody speaks over the last ordinary words the room has heard from another world.

Yuka lets go of Mau's hand and asks Jin-Woo whether he wants company.

He looks at the page again. Five marks, two names answered, more voices still possible.

“A little,” he says. ᴶʷ

Jin-Woo folds the paper along the old crease, opens it and looks at the names again.

Then he puts it down where it can remain in reach.
