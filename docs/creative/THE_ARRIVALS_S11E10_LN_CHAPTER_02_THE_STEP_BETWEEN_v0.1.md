# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 20 — The Step Between

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S11E10
**Continuity state:** B+182 morning. Jin-Woo/Mau resume bounded dagger movement practice, Gojo/Yuta present by invitation. Real equipment acknowledged and safely put aside for partner work, no stat toggle. Movement Learner reviews collected attempts after action; slow improvement through practice, no live automated counter or power gain. Family contact plans continue, no arrivals yet.

*When: Jin-Woo changes the direction of the second knife, Mau nearly congratulates himself for noticing.*

Then the first one touches his sleeve.

“I would like a less educational opponent,” Mau says. ᴹ

“You invited me to teach,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Gojo, standing beside Yuta, laughs with the pleasure of a man who has been waiting to hear somebody else receive that accusation.

They have returned to the practice yard with the corrected grip and the small set of recorded attempts. Ciel can separate the ones made before Jin-Woo changed Mau's hand position from the ones after. Noise has kept the distinction when storing the comparison. Neither supplies Mau with the correct movement while his teacher is coming at him.

He has to make that part happen himself.

The next attempt fails differently.

Jin-Woo looks down at Mau's feet.

“Better shoulder.” ᴶʷ

“Tragic everything else?” ᴹ

“We can work on the next part.” ᴶʷ

“You're very committed to that tone.” ᴹ

* * *

Yuta tries the exchange while Mau takes a drink.

He makes a different decision about the free hand, leaves himself more room and still gets touched when he assumes the first line is finished. Jin-Woo demonstrates the point with the two wooden trainers held apart.

“The turn doesn't end the first attack,” he says. “It makes space for another answer.” ᴶʷ

Yuta asks him to repeat it and looks pleased when he finally understands the part he missed.

Gojo comes off the wall.

Mau is immediately interested.

“You don't have to look so delighted,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“I am supporting my teacher.” ᴹ

“I recognize that phrase.” ᴳ

Jin-Woo asks Gojo to leave Infinity quiet for the touch exercise. Gojo agrees. They begin slowly, with the second knife visible from the start. Gojo keeps his arm closer than Mau did and changes his approach before the second hand becomes a threat.

The first touch misses.

Mau tries to look supportive through disappointment.

On the next attempt Jin-Woo offers him a line he can take very cleanly. Gojo takes it, finds the other knife waiting and stops with an expression that makes Yuta look at the ground.

“A generous offer,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“I saw.” ᴳ

“Eventually,” Mau says. ᴹ

Gojo points the trainer at him.

“Your next lesson will remember this.” ᴳ

* * *

The learner becomes more useful when Mau stops asking it to tell him he improved.

He chooses one attempt, looks at the turn before the touch and asks what part of his own movement has changed since the first day. Ciel shows the retained shoulder position. Noise adds the foot only after Mau has studied the first answer.

His foot is arriving late because he is waiting to confirm the first knife has gone past.

He knows why he does it. He is trying to be certain before committing.

Jin-Woo makes him practise the step without a knife, then with a hand, then with the first line beginning before the second has been disclosed. The range stays small. Speed comes later, after he can stop without falling into the next action.

Mau completes two clean attempts.

The third is too eager.

“I felt that,” he says before Jin-Woo can ask. ᴹ

“Good.” ᴶʷ

For once the word doesn't irritate him. Knowing the mistake before looking at the retained record is a kind of progress his eyes could not simply give him.

* * *

They finish with the real daggers on a stationary target, each man working alone while the others stand clear.

Mau feels the familiar equipment settle into his hands. The weapons' contribution belongs to using them; his own gained physical capacity was already there while they practised with wood. Jin-Woo examines the way he lets the first cut take his balance with it.

“You're giving the blade the next step,” he says. ᴶʷ

Mau slows down and tries the finish they have been practising. The second edge stays available. He does not need to use it to feel the difference.

Noise marks the motion for later study.

Mau asks it to keep the failed version too.

* * *

Yuka arrives with something wrapped in a cloth just as Gojo is declaring the lesson a great success for everybody who was not being unfairly observed.

“I brought food,” she says. ʸ

“You are my favorite person.” ᴹ

“An excellent marriage policy.” ʸ

She opens the cloth and hands Jin-Woo a piece first because Mau already has one in his mouth. Yuta takes the next. Gojo protests the order until Yuka asks whether he intends to file a complaint with Shoko.

He accepts his portion.

Jin-Woo eats while Mau stores the real weapons and puts the trainers on the rack. The latter takes longer because Yuta asks for the adjusted grip once more. Jin-Woo shows him without anyone needing to make another appointment around a battlefield.

“Do you mind?” Mau asks after Yuta has gone to try it. ᴹ

“Teaching?” ᴶʷ

“People asking things while you're waiting for answers of your own.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo looks toward Yuka, who is telling Gojo she has been warned about several of his definitions of fair.

“No,” he says. “I think I'd like to have more days where the question can wait until after breakfast.” ᴶʷ

Mau reaches for the last piece of food. Yuka moves it out of his way and asks whether he wants to split it.

He does.
