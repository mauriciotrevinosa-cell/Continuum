# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 22 — One Hundred

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 22
**Source episode:** S11E11
**Continuity state:** B+182 late afternoon. DIO explains proposed physical passage using old specific trace and Jin-Woo's inaccessible larger source connection. Creator-approved voluntary exchange: local body above National Hunters/Thomas reference, future growth possible, maximum one hundred selected shadow bonds retained in this world, no containers hiding extra active army. Choice made in principle only; no extraction/source surrender performed. Unselected not donated to Mau or destroyed in this chapter.

*When: DIO reaches the number, Jin-Woo asks her to repeat it.*

“One hundred,” she says. ᴰ

He looks at the drawing between them. This one was made by Senku after Mau's first attempt was declared a threat to the dignity of the enterprise. It has a line for the specific connection they have tested, a place for the arrival and a limit which cannot be enlarged by drawing the destination bigger.

“Including those already here?” Jin-Woo asks. ᴶʷ

“Yes.” ᴰ

Beru stands behind him without moving.

Mau sits at the threshold with Yuka and Rimuru. They have asked Jin-Woo whether he wants the terms privately. He asked them to stay. Ashborn is listening by choice from the adjoining doorway; nobody has asked him to decide what a younger king ought to give up.

DIO keeps the number where everyone can see it.

* * *

The call has given them a path narrow enough to study and an answer from the place it reaches. It has not given them an ordinary gate through which a family can walk without changing anything else.

The larger connection Jin-Woo can no longer fully use from this side still belongs to the structure which let him recognize the crossing. DIO explains the possibility they have found there: relinquish the attempt to carry that vast reach intact, use that relation to hold one properly bounded arrival and settle what remains into the world which has already accepted his body.

His present power would remain formidable. He would keep his knowledge, learned techniques and the self who used them. The scale he could call upon would begin near the earlier stage he recognized in the yard, with room to train and grow in the laws of the place where he chose to live.

His army would no longer be something he could reach beyond the limit by deciding he needed it badly enough.

“One hundred bonds kept available here,” Ciel says. “Every selected soldier counts. Carrying the others inside one of them would not preserve the agreement or the structure.” ᶜ

Jin-Woo nods. He had not asked for the loophole. He is glad it has been said before somebody tries to offer it as kindness.

“I choose them?” ᴶʷ

“You choose,” DIO says. ᴰ

* * *

Mau asks what happens to the rest.

Jin-Woo looks at him before DIO can answer.

“They aren't yours to take to make the loss easier.” ᴶʷ

“I wasn't asking for them.” ᴹ

“I know.” ᴶʷ

The older man looks down at his own shadow.

He will have to finish the duties he has left in his world, settle the orders which were holding positions when he crossed and release the part of the great deployment he chooses not to carry into this life. That cannot be accomplished by pretending the soldiers are extra equipment left in a bag.

“I want time to make that choice properly,” he says. ᴶʷ

DIO nods.

“You have it. This is what a possible passage would require. You have not paid for it by listening.” ᴰ

Mau feels Yuka's hand move against his. He doesn't look away from Jin-Woo.

“And they hear this before deciding,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“Yes.” ᴰ

* * *

The next question is the one Rimuru asks.

“What can follow them?” ᴿ

DIO points to the narrow connection. The proposed physical passage would be held to the people named and answering on the other side. Once the arrival is complete, the remaining support for that crossing would be spent and closed. It would not stay as a road an enemy could use after the family.

“The performance would need to happen before that final close,” Mau says. ᴹ

“If we mean to place its signal along this route, yes,” Ciel says. ᶜ

Jin-Woo studies the order.

They need the family ready without forcing them to cross. They need the false presence to reach an actual observing force without letting the same operation expose the house at its other end. They need Jin-Woo's withdrawal to be legible as the thing they mean to show rather than an obvious retreat.

And they need to stop if the test fails before any of those decisions become irreversible.

“A busy god,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau looks at him.

“You can still volunteer to draw the next one.” ᴹ

DIO keeps her hand over the page until they have finished looking amused enough to avoid the difficult part.

* * *

Jin-Woo asks whether the limit means he will eventually be unable to protect the people he brings.

DIO lets the question remain a question about fear rather than answering with a grander promise of power.

“You will be able to do a great deal,” she says. “You will also live among people who can help you.” ᴰ

He looks toward Mau.

Mau doesn't tell him that nobody will ever get past him. He has no such promise to give anybody.

“You helped me learn something today,” he says. “I'd like there to be days you can do that because you want to, with your family waiting for supper.” ᴹ

Beru's hand tightens once behind Jin-Woo's chair.

“My king,” it says. ᴮʲ

Jin-Woo turns.

The ant lowers its head. For all its talent with a notebook, it has no prepared speech for this question.

“I would like to see that supper,” it says. ᴮʲ

* * *

They leave the terms unchanged while Jin-Woo thinks.

Yuka asks whether he wants someone to help explain them to his family. He says he will tell them first, then asks DIO if she would answer the questions they have. She agrees.

Mau puts the diagram beside Jin-Woo's own page. Five people on one. A number on the other. For a moment he hates the simplicity with which paper can make a choice look balanced.

Jin-Woo sees him looking.

“You don't need to apologize for an option I didn't have before,” he says. ᴶʷ

“I won't call it small.” ᴹ

“It isn't.” ᴶʷ

He stands and takes both pages.

“But I didn't want the power because I liked missing them.” ᴶʷ

Mau lets go of the edge he had been keeping flat.

* * *

Ashborn asks Jin-Woo to remain a moment after the others move toward the outer room.

Mau goes far enough to leave the conversation private. He can still see the two men without hearing what has not been offered to him.

Ashborn says something. Jin-Woo looks down at the number, then back at him. There is no gesture of command from either. No power changes hands.

After a while, Jin-Woo draws the spare chair nearer to the window and sits.

Mau goes to find water for DIO. She thanks him, then asks whether he is going to keep looking so grave until the family has answered.

“I'm considering it.” ᴹ

“Then I shall ask Yuka to assign you something.” ᴰ

“You have become very comfortable doing that.” ᴹ

Noise, attentive nearby, puts a hand to its chest.

Mau points at DIO.

“This is your fault.” ᴹ

She accepts the accusation with a smile.
