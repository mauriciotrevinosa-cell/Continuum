# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 65
## Chapter 23 — The Other Kind of Hand

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 65
**Reading order:** 23
**Source episode:** S11E12
**Continuity state:** B+183 morning. First deliberate S11 combination exercise: learned dagger footwork plus existing Ruler's Authority holding/turning a cloth target, then bounded partner resistance with wooden trainer. No new CT, automatic counters, two different CT simultaneously active or neural repair. Movement Learner reviews after attempts. Successful low-output coordination remains practice, not perfected battle combo.

*When: Jin-Woo asks why Mau keeps leaving one of his hands unused, Mau looks down at the two knives.*

“This feels like a cruel return to the first lesson.” ᴹ

“The other kind.” ᴶʷ

Jin-Woo indicates the cup Mau moved off the bench without touching it. Mau has put it beside the water to make room for the trainers and then forgotten he did anything worth mentioning.

“Authority?” ᴹ

“You already know how to use it. Why did you stop thinking about it when you picked these up?” ᴶʷ

Mau considers several answers, most of which would sound better if they were true.

“I was trying to learn the lesson.” ᴹ

“Then learn how the lesson fits the body you have.” ᴶʷ

Yuta, who has come to practise the entry again, turns toward the hanging cloth they have been using to show the lines of the exchange. Yuka is beside it with the cord she brought. She looks at Mau as if to say that this question belongs in more than one exercise.

He gives her a wounded look.

She smiles and holds the cloth out.

* * *

They begin with no opponent.

Mau uses Authority to hold one corner of the cloth away from its hanging position. The pressure is familiar and easy to maintain while he stands still. He makes the short dagger entry they have practised, touches the wooden edge to the marked line and stops.

The corner has drifted.

“Again,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Mau watches the corner more closely this time and loses the clean placement of his foot.

“I've been here before,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka nods with great sympathy and very little pity.

“Now you have another hand to follow with your shoulder.” ʸ

“You could be on my side.” ᴹ

“I'm holding the target.” ʸ

He laughs and gives her a moment to retie the cord where his first attempt shifted it. They lower the pressure, reduce the movement and choose one instant at which the grip needs to change instead of asking him to maintain a perfect invisible hand throughout the whole exchange.

The next attempt is less impressive and much more useful.

* * *

Ciel keeps the record after he finishes. Noise marks the place where the pressure begins to follow the motion of the knife instead of the chosen point on the cloth.

Mau studies it.

“I was turning everything together.” ᴹ

“You wanted the cloth to turn,” Yuka says. “You didn't want the rest to go with it.” ʸ

“That distinction was clear in my imagination.” ᴹ

*An excellent place to begin,* Ciel says privately. *We still need it in the movement.* ᶜ

Mau tells Yuka. She looks delighted to have support from the committee.

Jin-Woo demonstrates with his own hold, making the small external turn meet the step rather than lead it. He lets Mau see the timing without offering to place it into him as a completed reflex.

“You can be strong enough to do both and still choose the wrong moment,” he says. ᴶʷ

Mau tries again.

This time the cloth turns where he intended. The wooden blade reaches the marked line without his weight falling after it. He holds the finish for a breath, then lets the pressure go deliberately.

Yuta looks at the cord.

“That would change where the second edge had to go.” ʸᵗ

“If I can still do it when the second edge objects,” Mau says. ᴹ

“We should probably find out.” ʸᵗ

* * *

Jin-Woo becomes the objecting edge.

They agree on the contact and use a wooden trainer. Mau's Authority will meet the movement at the forearm for one short correction. Jin-Woo will resist enough to show whether Mau has found a useful line, not enough to turn a coordination exercise into a contest of who can throw the other through the wall.

The first try fails before the hold begins. Jin-Woo changes the line and Mau reaches for the place he expected the arm to be.

The second catches too late.

On the third, the invisible grip turns the approach a little and Mau steps into the space it creates. His trainer reaches the chosen mark. He stops there, releases the pressure and steps back.

He looks at Jin-Woo.

“That?” ᴹ

“That.” ᴶʷ

Yuka raises both hands in a small celebration. Mau accepts the tribute with the dignity of a man who has earned one clean attempt after a deeply inconvenient morning.

Jin-Woo changes his grip.

“Now from the other side.” ᴶʷ

Mau closes his eyes.

“Cruel man.” ᴹ

* * *

They end with a combination Mau can describe more honestly than he can yet repeat under every condition.

He knows what it asks of his attention, where it takes pressure and which part fails when he tries to solve the whole exchange by pulling harder. The learner has a useful series of attempts instead of one flattering result. Jin-Woo has pointed out two occasions where ordinary footwork would have been simpler than adding another operation.

“You don't have to use everything because you have it,” he says. ᴶʷ

“People have mentioned that.” ᴹ

Yuka looks at him.

“Occasionally.” ʸ

Mau puts the trainers away and asks whether she would like to try her surface fold against the same entry tomorrow. She says yes, provided he doesn't prepare every possible counter before she has found her first useful one.

He agrees.

Yuta asks to keep the cloth up for his own practice. Jin-Woo stays to show him the change in timing once more.

Mau joins Yuka on the bench and takes the cup he moved without thinking at the start.

“Did you enjoy that?” she asks. ʸ

He looks at the marked cloth, then at his teacher helping someone else see the same mistake.

“Very much.” ᴹ

She rests her shoulder against his while he drinks.
