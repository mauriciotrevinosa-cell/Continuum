He has been telling himself for days that he wants them here. Having her within reach reveals how much of that desire he was still holding at the distance of a plan.

Hae-In gets her arms around him. He bends enough to rest against her without making her work to keep the contact. For a while they stand with the door shut and hear people moving beyond it, ordinary sounds belonging to a place which does not require them to leave the embrace unfinished.

It is a smaller room than the future she could have been offered by a man with the power he gave up. She looks at the closed door, the place for their things and the window they can open themselves.

“I like this,” she says. ᴴᶦ

“We can find something larger when—” ᴶʷ

“I know. I like this.” ᴴᶦ

She asks how the change feels.

He tells her the part he can describe. There are distances he used to reach toward without thinking. The first time he tries now, the answer ends closer. The selected connections are still there. His body remains strong. None of that makes the old habit disappear at once.

“Do you regret it?” she asks. ᴴᶦ

“I miss parts of what I left,” he says. “I don't regret you being here.” ᴶʷ

“You can tell me about both.” ᴴᶦ

He looks at their joined fingers. The first sensation he expected from the final choice was loss. He has felt some of it. He has also spent most of the last hour being touched by people he wanted to see.

“I thought I'd know how to do this once there wasn't another position to check,” he says. ᴶʷ
