# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 2 — A Hand You Can Reach For

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S11E16
**Continuity state:** B+186 afternoon. Mau/Yuka/Ciel/Noise examine a bounded emergency handoff through their existing contract. Yuka knowingly permits receiving a specifically offered defense if injured/unable to request; retains revocation. Low-energy intact-contact test carries one brief resistance to a padded tap, not a damaging spatial-cut trial. Native Ten Shadows receiving path tested; stored adaptation response only shown, no universal immunity/new permanent Yuka adaptation or automatic defensive oracle.

*When: Yuka asks whether he can catch her through the contract, Mau says he would prefer she not demonstrate by falling off something.*

“I wasn't going to.” ʸ

“I know people.” ᴹ

She looks toward Gojo, who has come to hear the question with Yuta. Gojo smiles with the innocence of someone who has considered several more entertaining demonstrations and is willing to keep them to himself for almost a minute.

They stay in the practice room. There is no gate, no opponent and no reason to turn the question into a feat of endurance. Rimuru is there so Ciel can help keep the explanation audible without everybody trying to interpret Mau's silences. The work involves the contract Yuka already owns her side of, not a new right to inspect her because he is worried.

“What happens if I can't ask for the thing I'm already allowed to use?” she says. “Not sleeping. If something actually stops me.” ʸ

Ciel answers through the arrangement they have chosen.

The supply can remain available while Yuka is unconscious; they have tested its independence from a fresh spoken permission from Mau. That isn't the same as selecting a new operation for an injured person without any agreement about what they want.

“Then I'm asking now,” Yuka says. ʸ

* * *

She doesn't ask for Mau to take control of her body.

She asks whether a response he can deliberately offer could reach the existing line when she cannot complete the request herself. Something brief, chosen for what is happening, with the source and strain still where they have always agreed to put them.

Mau looks at her.

“I would have to recognize it.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

“And have something useful to offer.” ᴹ

“I know that too.” ʸ

Yuta asks whether she wants the offer to remain something she can close. She says yes. The part about being unable to ask cannot become a convenient way to pretend she has stopped being allowed to refuse.

Ciel holds those conditions apart while Noise uses the clearer structure Jin-Woo helped them examine.

“One offered answer,” Mau says. “Not whatever I decide your whole body should do.” ᴹ

Yuka nods.

“That is what I'm asking for.” ʸ

* * *

The first test uses a padded stick and so little pressure that Gojo declares the experimental design emotionally unfulfilling.

“You may write a review,” Yuta tells him. ʸᵗ

“I have competition.” ᴳ

Yuka holds her forearm where the padding will meet it. She keeps her ordinary movement out of the exercise and lets Ciel mark the offered path as waiting rather than already supplying a response.

Mau offers the brief resistance.

It arrives late. The padding touches her sleeve before the part intended to meet it settles into place.

Yuka looks down, then at Mau.

“Good thing I wasn't falling.” ʸ

“You can stop enjoying that point.” ᴹ

“It's educational.” ʸ

They inspect the delay. Mau started shaping the response before Noise had separated its arrival from the supply already crossing to the second pattern. Ciel changes the order, has them close it and asks Yuka to reopen the waiting permission herself.

The next attempt meets the touch in time.

Only for an instant. Only the response they offered. The padding stays a harmless piece of padding when the resistance ends.

Yuka closes the line afterward and asks Mau to try again.

Nothing reaches her through the closed offer.

She looks satisfied.

* * *

The path through her native shadow technique is examined separately.

Her Mahoraga is hers. Mau's is his. The two do not acquire every answer either has learned because the names of the techniques resemble one another. What they can inspect is the established route by which a specifically offered operation can borrow a place to arrive without replacing her own pattern.

Mau has Ciel show the retained response from his wheel as a reference, without running the event which first produced it through his conscious memory. It concerns one particular kind of severance. He does not need to reopen Sukuna's private archive to recognize the thing his own body survived.

Gojo studies the relation and stops smiling.

“That held once,” he says. ᴳ

“I know.” ᴹ

“Keep that in the description.” ᴳ

Ciel does. A stored answer may be offered through the line; its match to a future attack, readiness and duration still matter. The existence of the route cannot make an unfamiliar harm become familiar in time to save anyone.

Yuka listens to all of it before agreeing to keep this narrow emergency permission available.

“If you've got an answer and I can't reach for it, you can help me reach,” she says. ʸ

Mau takes her hand.

“Okay.” ᴹ

* * *

They leave with a useful small test rather than a reason to demonstrate a dangerous wound.

Mau checks the difference between the line Yuka can close and the ordinary supply she already owns. She asks him to say it aloud once more, then tells him that sounded very much like a lesson she would like to repeat without the stick.

He offers to carry the stick to Gojo.

“I heard that,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“Excellent hearing.” ᴹ

“Your next lesson is accumulating.” ᴳ

Yuka takes the padding out of Mau's hand before the exchange acquires a practical demonstration.

* * *

On the way back she asks whether he was uncomfortable with the question.

“A little,” he says. ᴹ

“Because it might be needed?” ʸ

“Because I wanted to answer with more than I could honestly offer.” ᴹ

She leans against his arm as they walk.

“I wanted to know what we could do. You told me.” ʸ

He looks at their joined hands. She turns hers a little so the ring catches against his finger, an ordinary contact which doesn't need to carry an instruction.

“I'd still catch you,” he says. ᴹ

“Then stay close.” ʸ

He does.
