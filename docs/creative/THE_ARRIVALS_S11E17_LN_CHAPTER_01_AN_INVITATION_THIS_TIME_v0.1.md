# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 3 — An Invitation, This Time

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S11E17
**Continuity state:** B+187 morning. Makima delivers request at established outer crossing without ward entry; ordinary shadow carries sealed note under standing instructions, Fern/Rimuru handle, no anonymous new resident runner. House evaluates voluntary conversation; no nonaggression accepted or meeting begun yet. New Jin-Woo family present in ordinary life. Makima account remains future claim.

*When: the note reaches Fern, Mau is losing a game to Jin-Woo's mother.*

He considers the arrival an opportunity to escape gracefully. Yuka sees the thought before he has stood and informs the table that unfinished results will remain on the record.

“I wasn't leaving.” ᴹ

“Excellent,” Jin-Woo's mother says. “Then finish your turn.” ᴶᵐ

Umaru, watching a guest achieve what she considers a matter of national importance, looks delighted. Rem takes the cup she has nearly elbowed over while trying to see Mau's cards.

Fern waits until the hand is finished. The ordinary soldier which brought the note remains near the door until Rimuru acknowledges the delivery. It has come from the crossing, where somebody may leave a request without being invited beyond the perimeter.

Mau reads the signature.

His smile goes.

Yuka puts her cards down.

* * *

Makima has asked to speak.

She names the earlier neutral meeting as a precedent for being able to sit in one place without pretending either side has become harmless. She offers the same beginning: a protected conversation, separate departures, no attack or compulsion during the visit.

What she says she wants to discuss is different.

Her nature. The reason for what she did. A position she believes Mau should reconsider now that he has seen more of what the world can make of a person.

Mau reads the final part twice.

*I would rather explain it to you myself than let the next thing you learn about me come from somebody who wants us fighting.*

He puts the page down.

“She's very good at making herself the alternative to a worse person,” he says. ᴹ

“Yes,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“Does that mean we shouldn't hear her?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Neither man answers immediately.

* * *

They move the discussion away from the game without making the whole common room into a response to Makima's timing.

Jin-Woo asks whether there is anything he needs to know. Mau tells him who sent the note and that it concerns an enemy they have spoken to before. He does not make the newly arrived family sit through the worst account of the old gate in order to justify taking a conversation elsewhere.

“If you need me, ask,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“I will.” ᴹ

Yuka reaches for the page. Mau lets her read it again beside him.

Maki joins them after Fern has asked her, with Gojo and Yuta arriving separately. They hear the actual request before being told what anyone thinks the answer should be. Shoko reads the part about the earlier encounter and asks whether anyone is proposing to bring Makima into the clinic again.

“No,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Good. Continue.” ˢʰ

* * *

Mau wants to understand how she can act, what her power needs and which parts of the stories circulating through the world she chooses to claim. He has been collecting warnings from effects. Hearing her describe her own mechanism might give them questions they have not known to ask.

“She can lie,” Maki says. ᴹᵏ

“She will,” Mau says. “Or tell the useful part in a way that makes the rest sound reasonable.” ᴹ

“Then we don't trade for the story while she's telling it,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Yuta asks whether Mau thinks she is offering terms or asking for an audience. He says the note reads as the second. There is no reason to negotiate a power exchange in the clearing just because she knows how to make one sound urgent.

Yuka puts the page down.

“I want to hear what she thinks protecting us means,” she says. ʸ

Mau looks at her.

“You don't have to be there because she mentioned you.” ᴹ

“I know. I want to be there because she keeps using what happens to us as a way to explain what you should do.” ʸ

He holds her gaze, then nods.

* * *

They agree to prepare a reply, not to walk out immediately.

The meeting would be outside the fixed protection. Rimuru would attend with Mau and Yuka. Frieren agrees to come after hearing the request; Gojo and Yuta will remain available on the return side rather than every capable person taking the same chair. The ordinary watch will inspect the approaches and keep its named positions. The nonaggression must be accepted before anyone begins explaining themselves across the table.

There is time to read the terms before choosing to stand under them.

Fern writes the proposed day and time only after the people attending have agreed: B+189, noon. A refusal of the conditions means no meeting. The reply will go to the same outer place where the request was left, through an acknowledged handover rather than a stranger being allowed to follow somebody home.

Mau reads the page beside Yuka.

“Are you sure?” he asks. ᴹ

She takes his hand.

“About listening with you? Yes.” ʸ

He nods and gives the reply back to Fern.
