## Chapter 4 — The Way Back Is Part of It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S11E17
**Continuity state:** B+187 afternoon. Meeting terms/return/field review before ratification. Prospective personal nonaggression forbids fresh initiation, instruction/authorization/triggering, coercion and following from acceptance through separate departure; permitted defense of independent attack. Does not adjudicate earlier completed instigation, a flaw house has not identified. No changed older S9 pact or attack prepared under defeated Sukuna's road threat; new meeting/actors. Yuka keeps permanent own Limitless pattern and narrow revocable emergency consent from V66#2.

*When: Gojo asks where they intend to leave the way back, Mau points at the map instead of the place he expects to sit.*

“Here. Enough space for everybody who comes with us.” ᴹ

Rimuru moves the marker a little. The first position would put the return through the part of the path used by wagons at that hour. Fern confirms the crossing window and says it can be kept clear without pretending the road belongs to them all afternoon.

“A separate line for leaving,” Yuka says. ʸ

Frieren looks at the two marks and asks to see the place before agreeing it is sufficient. They go there while the watch carries out its ordinary work, taking the side path so they do not hold up the unloading at the crossing.

One soldier shows the cleared approach. Another remains where the road becomes the track toward the protection. Igris will coordinate the return from the position he has been given. The ordinary forms signal their acknowledgment, and Mau repeats what he has received so everyone can check the same instructions.

Mau gives the instructions through the existing link and has Rimuru hear them aloud too.

* * *

The place is familiar enough to help and open enough not to hide the nearest approach.

It is still outside the ward. Nobody asks DIO to move the boundary with the table, and no one treats the protection as an invisible floor that will follow Mau wherever he happens to put his feet.

Frieren examines the ground and the visible lines. Yuka checks where a chair would put her reach relative to the return. Mau looks over the positions with the eyes he has learned to keep usable, widening his attention for the inspection instead of leaving it fixed on the central marker.

“No hostile presence in the approaches we're checking,” he tells Rimuru. ᴹ

Rimuru repeats the area covered, leaving the rest of the forest out of the claim.

They will check again on the day. The place is free now. That is a useful fact, not a promise about every creature that can move through a world.

* * *

They read the terms at the kitchen table afterward because it is the first clear surface with enough light.

The promise begins at mutual acceptance and lasts through the conversation and the named interval of separate departure. Neither party will initiate an attack, issue or authorize a hostile instruction, activate a delayed attack or use the meeting to exert compulsion on the other people there. No follower or mark will be placed during the visit to turn leaving into a second confrontation later.

The people taking part bind their own actions. Mau does not sign the minds or lives of the household into anything. Makima does not get to make a future request sound like an order they accepted by agreeing to listen.

They retain the right to defend against an independent attack without turning that defense into permission to assault the other party.

Yuta reads that part again.

“An animal in the woods doesn't become hers because it attacks,” he says. ʸᵗ

“And a signed promise doesn't make a sentence true,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau nods.

“We listen. We ask. Anything else comes home before we decide.” ᴹ

* * *

Makima accepts the proposed wording by the outer handover before evening.

The reply agrees to the time, the site, the absence of companions on her side and the departure interval. Her signature on a proposed page does not begin the spoken undertaking before the people attending have accepted it together. They have used that distinction before. They will do so again in the clearing.

Gojo reads the answer and asks whether they want him at the return marker instead of in the house.

“Near enough to reach it,” Mau says. “With Yuta on the other route.” ᴹ

“And if somebody wants to turn the conversation into a long tour of her suffering?” ᴳ

“We can leave,” Yuka says. ʸ

Gojo nods to her, satisfied with the person who answered.

* * *

Shoko asks which energy paths they intend to keep available while listening.

Mau explains that Yuka's pattern remains her own to use, with the shared supply and combined strain they know. He will not attempt an experimental combination in the middle of the meeting. Ciel stays connected, Rimuru hears her as usual and the watch has a way to relay a problem without needing a new public voice for the ordinary soldiers.

Muster will hold the home-side return anchor near the clinic instead of the usual inner turn. Shoko agrees to have that place kept clear. Yuka explicitly asks them to use the emergency return if she is hurt and cannot answer; the permission is for bringing her to help, not for deciding she has surrendered the right to leave a conversation while she is able to choose.

Yuka asks Shoko whether she wants any change to the emergency permission they tested.

“Tell me what was actually tested,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

They do. The padded contact. The late first response. The successful second. The closed offer which refused the next attempt. The stored wheel reference they examined without pretending a demonstration of a lethal cut had been performed.

Shoko asks what Ciel would do if raw supply started reaching the wrong place.

“Stop that operation before asking him to push harder,” Rimuru relays. ᴿ

“Good,” Shoko says. “Keep that order.” ˢʰ

* * *

Later, Yuka finds Mau looking at the plan once more.

He is not changing the route. He is tracing the part where the people coming back will pass from an agreement they can read into protection they have lived with for years.

She sits beside him.

“I like that we have a way to leave,” she says. ʸ

“So do I.” ᴹ

“I also like that you let me help choose it.” ʸ

He looks at her.

“It's your way home too.” ᴹ

She rests her head against his shoulder, and the plan stays a piece of paper between them until somebody asks for the table back.
