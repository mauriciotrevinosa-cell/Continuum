# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 5 — A Cup Full

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S11E18
**Continuity state:** B+188 dawn, one day BEFORE meeting vow B. Original Severing Devil, no claim of canon CSM identity. Prior independent agreement A under Continuum pledge mechanism: one spatial-severance attack at fixed tomorrow clock time/target if present, no fresh signal/order/trigger from Makima required. Reward one cup Makima blood if devil returns through its believed cycle; identity tied to same named fear, no guaranteed canon memory retention. Self-interested autonomous contractor, not mind-controlled current puppet. No new household knowledge.

*When: Makima tells the devil how much blood, it asks whether she means a cup full.*

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

It looks at the space between her hands as though she has offered it a country.

The body it wears is narrow and badly joined at the wrists. The line where its mouth should end continues farther along the cheek than any face needs. When it opens the mouth, the two sides of a fallen branch beside it part without a blade moving between them.

Makima watches the demonstration.

“One cut,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“One good cut.” ˢᵛ

“At the time I gave you. Tomorrow.” ᴹᵏ

It repeats the time. Makima sets a small clock beside the fallen branch and makes it read the same hour as the one at the crossing. The place has already been named. The woman beside Mau has been described with enough detail that it won't need Makima to point, look at her or say anything different once the conversation begins.

If the target is not there, the task will not be done. No other victim earns the reward. If the target is there, the devil chooses when to spend the single opening within the moment they have fixed.

It asks whether the man will kill it.

“He may try.” ᴹᵏ

The devil thinks about the cup.

It has been hungry for a long time. It knows what a little blood from a powerful devil might mean to a body like its own. What she has offered sounds absurdly generous. Dying during the task is a cost it has already begun to place on the other side of a return it expects to make.

“When I'm back below,” it says. ˢᵛ

“If you return and the task was done. The promise follows the one bearing your fear's name. One cup.” ᴹᵏ

It leans closer.

“Yours.” ˢᵛ

“Mine.” ᴹᵏ

The pledge catches between the terms, the same kind of chosen undertaking this world has made available to beings who arrived with different rules. The devil feels the reward settle into something it believes it can collect.

It grins.

“You don't need to call me tomorrow?” ˢᵛ

“No.” ᴹᵏ

“Or move your hand?” ˢᵛ

“No.” ᴹᵏ

“Then go and talk.” ˢᵛ

Makima leaves it with the time, the place and the most profitable decision it thinks it has ever made.

Behind her, the two halves of the branch finish falling apart.
