# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 6 — The Evening They Had

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S11E18
**Continuity state:** B+188 afternoon/evening. Existing family/relationships remain active before meeting. Mau/Yuka normal chosen evening, no intuition of future wound or forced withdrawal. Readiness checked through established watch/contract. Jin-Woo offers backup; house roles shared. No meeting vow yet, no new construction/power.

*When: Yuka tells Mau to put the map away, he says he already did.*

She looks at the paper beside his elbow.

“That's the other side of the same map.” ʸ

“A technical distinction.” ᴹ

“Put that side away too.” ʸ

He rolls it and gives it to her. She puts it where he will have to ask for it instead of finding it under his hand by habit, then sits beside him with the cloth Mai has asked her to return.

They are in the common room early enough that there is still light for the last seam. Mau asks whether she wants help. She gives him a corner to hold and tells him not to make it so easy that she can't feel what the material is doing.

“An oppressive household,” he says. ᴹ

“You're thriving.” ʸ

He rests his knee against hers and holds the cloth exactly as requested.

* * *

Hae-In comes to ask Yuka where Mai is working.

Jin-Ah has found one of the small fasteners useful and would like to ask about a different size. Hae-In repeats the request carefully enough that Mai will be able to tell whether she can make it without guessing what the wearer intends.

Yuka shows her the place on the cloth they have just repaired.

“She'll ask you how it's going to be used.” ʸ

“Good,” Hae-In says. ᴴᶦ

Mau sees the start of an alliance and wisely keeps his opinion to himself. Hae-In thanks him for holding the corner while she looks and then goes to find Mai.

“You were preparing a remark,” Yuka says. ʸ

“I chose peace.” ᴹ

She laughs and finishes the seam.

* * *

They return the cloth together.

Mai is with Momo near the workshop, where the broom has been leaned against the wall while its owner helps untangle a cord. Noise notices the familiar object and asks whether there might be time to observe the new question tomorrow.

Mau tells it they will arrange a time after the meeting rather than promising something he may not be able to keep at a particular hour.

*Ask later,* Noise agrees. ᴺ

He conveys that to Momo so the request doesn't become something she is supposed to have overheard privately. She says she will be around after lunch, though she wants the question first.

Mai looks at the repaired cloth and asks who held it.

Yuka points at Mau.

“Useful man,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“Tell people.” ᴹ

“I try not to encourage you.” ᴹᵃ

He sees Momo smiling behind her hand and decides to accept the compliment as delivered.

* * *

Jin-Woo finds him on the way back.

He has heard enough about tomorrow to ask what Mau wants from him. Mau explains where Gojo and Yuta will be, what Igris is coordinating and what Rimuru and Frieren have agreed to watch during the conversation.

“Stay with the people here,” he says. “If there is a problem, I'd like someone who knows what they're doing to already be with them.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo considers the request.

“You trust the promise?” ᴶʷ

“I trust that she has kept a binding undertaking when she accepted one before. I don't trust everything she may say under it.” ᴹ

“That is worth keeping separate.” ᴶʷ

“We are.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo nods, then asks whether Mau still wants the lesson the day after. Mau says yes. He is interested in learning how to stop announcing the next step with every part of his body at once.

“A promising subject,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“I have been promised.” ᴹ

* * *

Yuka is waiting with two cups when Mau returns.

He tells her what Jin-Woo asked and what he answered. She says she likes knowing there is someone at home who will already know which people need helping instead of asking a room full of frightened strangers to invent a plan.

“You don't have to like her explanation,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

“And if you want to leave—” ᴹ

“I will say so.” ʸ

He takes the cup she offers.

“I was going to say we leave.” ᴹ

She looks at him, then smiles.

“I know that too.” ʸ

* * *

They spend the rest of the evening with the people already there.

Ori wants someone to settle a disagreement about the expedition map from the last outing. Frieren asks her to explain the part she wants changed before Mau can offer to redraw it. Ori gives a very serious account of why the old marker makes the route look easier than it was.

“An excellent correction,” he says when she finishes. ᴹ

She looks pleased and asks him to hold the page while she makes it.

Yuka sits on his other side, giving the pencil back whenever Ori puts it down and forgets where. Frieren listens to the account without taking it away from her. By the time the correction is complete, Mau has a small mark of graphite on his thumb and no remaining claim that he has spent the whole day looking at his own map.

* * *

At home, Yuka asks him to help undo the catch at the back of her collar.

He manages it on the second try. She turns to kiss him before he can apologize to a piece of clothing, then leaves him to wash while she puts their things away.

When he comes back, she has moved to his side of the bed.

“An invasion,” he says. ᴹ

“Negotiable.” ʸ

He sits beside her and asks what she wants in exchange for the territory.

She lifts his arm, settles beneath it and tells him she will consider the matter there.

He turns the light down when she has finished laughing.
