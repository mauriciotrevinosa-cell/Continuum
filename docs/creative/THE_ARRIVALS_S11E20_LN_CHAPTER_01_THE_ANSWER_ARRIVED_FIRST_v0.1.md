# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 9 — The Answer Arrived First

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S11E20
**Continuity state:** B+189 just before half past noon, continuous. Prior A executes autonomously at fixed time, no new Makima order/gesture triggering attack. Original devil spatial severance reaches Yuka despite neutral Infinity (does not traverse gap). Mau reacts immediately on recognizing formed phenomenon, uses consented emergency native-carrier route to lend one retained matched wheel answer, then withdraws; prevents completion, not already incurred major injury. Makima kills exposed attacker under permitted independent-threat defense, B remains intact. No attribution proved. Yuka unconscious, emergency Exchange to house after dialogue, no new enemy shadow/XP or full immunity.

*When: Yuka begins to lift her hand from his, Mau sees something wrong in the space beside her shoulder.*

There has been no sign from Makima.

Her hands are still on the table. Her mouth has closed after the last sentence. The pledge between the people sitting there remains clear.

The wrongness arrives elsewhere.

It has no flying edge for the watch to see cross the approach. It is a line being made in the place Yuka already occupies. Her Infinity is there. The line is not traveling through the distance it holds apart.

Mau recognizes the kind of answer it needs in the same instant the first part opens her sleeve.

He is already moving.

* * *

His own neutral hold closes as he reaches for the other route. He doesn't try to build a new adaptation while the cut is happening. He has one retained answer to a severance like this, one he has lived through, and a line Yuka chose to keep available if she could not finish asking.

*Send it.* ᴹ

Ciel has seen the same thing. Noise takes the offered response through the carrier they tested. The wheel comes into view above Yuka before she has finished turning toward him.

There is no time for it to learn this from the beginning.

It arrives with the answer.

The line catches.

For one terrible interval, something which should have continued through the body in front of him fails to finish what it began.

Mau gets both arms around her before the chair has fallen.

She looks at him.

“Mau?” ʸ

He has her. He can feel her weight. There is still her hand against his wrist.

Then the blood reaches his fingers.

* * *

“Yuka.” ᴹ

The sound comes out too quietly. He says it again, louder, as he lowers her onto the ground instead of letting her body fold through the injury.

The wheel has spent its answer. He brings it back, closes the operation and reaches for healing with a force so immediate that Ciel has to catch the order before it becomes the wrong kind of help.

*Mau. Slow the supply.* ᶜ

He sends more anyway for the beginning of a breath. Raw mana, positive output, every available route in him trying to arrive at the same place.

Yuka's body gives a small, awful reaction beneath his hands.

*You will hurt her. Listen to me.* ᶜ

He stops the excess.

Not the help. The excess.

He hears Ciel divide the paths and follows the one she names. Positive energy reaches the damaged tissue in a flow he can hold. The other supply is kept where it will not tear through a body that is already struggling to answer itself.

“Okay,” he says. “Okay. Tell me.” ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru is beside them. Frieren has turned toward the source line, following the thing the cut has made visible without taking the route home out of her attention.

Igris reaches the clearing from the return marker. The ordinary soldiers remain where they can keep the approach clear. Gojo's answer comes through Rimuru's call almost before Mau has understood the words being spoken over him.

None of it changes the face in his arms.

Yuka's eyes have closed.

“Stay,” he says. ᴹ

He knows it isn't an instruction her body can obey because he wants it badly enough. He says it again anyway, his hand shaking against the place Ciel tells him to hold.

Something moves beyond the nearest bank.

A narrow figure steps into the open with its mouth too wide and a clock clutched against its side. It looks at the blood, then at Mau, with the pleased disbelief of a creature which thinks the hardest part of an excellent bargain is over.

Mau lifts his head.

The whole clearing seems to become the distance between them.

Makima raises one hand.

* * *

The sound is short.

The devil's head breaks apart before it can say anything. Its body strikes the bank, drops the clock and falls in a shape which has ceased to hold the attack together.

Igris reaches it with the sword already drawn. Frieren checks for the continuing effect. Rimuru keeps himself between the fall of the corpse and the people on the ground.

Makima lowers her hand.

The vow doesn't break.

Mau can feel that with a clarity which will be unbearable later. A woman has been cut open beside him while every assurance he believed he understood remains exactly where he put it.

He looks back down at Yuka.

“Ciel.” ᴹ

*I am here. We can move her if you keep this steady.* ᶜ

“Noise. If you've kept something. If you can finish something that helps her—please.” ᴹ

The answer is attention so complete that it feels like another pair of hands reaching for the same person.

*Helping,* Noise says. ᴺ

* * *

Makima takes one step closer, stops outside the place Rimuru has made clear and speaks as though the conversation has been interrupted by something unfortunate she is willing to accommodate.

“I would have liked to continue. We can do that another day.” ᴹᵏ

Mau keeps the flow Ciel has given him. He doesn't lift the hand supporting Yuka's head. The blood has gone beneath his collar and down the front of his shirt. He can feel the ring on her hand where he has brought it against his own.

“Thank you,” he says. ᴹ

Makima's expression softens by the smallest amount.

“Of course. Postponing is—” ᴹᵏ

“For the devil,” he says. “And for keeping the contract.” ᴹ

She stops.

He looks at her for one moment. There are tears on his face, and there is no useful way for her to tell how much of the sentence belongs to gratitude.

Then he turns away from her entirely.

* * *

“Keep the body,” he tells Igris. ᴹ

“My liege.” ᴵ

Rimuru confirms the house-side place is ready. Shoko knows. Yuta is returning along the route assigned to him; Gojo has reached the edge of the clearing without turning the intact truce into an excuse to strike the woman watching them.

Mau doesn't need to carry Yuka the whole way while every step asks him to let go of a part of the work.

The home anchor answers through the connection already prepared for returning people.

“I'm taking her,” he says. ᴹ

Rimuru tells him the others will follow.

Mau gathers Yuka closer with all the care his body has learned how to give. He keeps his hands where Ciel needs them and makes the Exchange.

* * *

Shoko is there when the place changes.

She sees the injury. She sees the flow Mau is maintaining and asks Ciel's question through Rimuru's still-open link before trying to change it. Yuta reaches the room from the inner side as Mau lays Yuka where Shoko points.

“Tell me what happened,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

Mau tries to answer and gets as far as his wife's name.

“The cut,” Shoko says. “What did it do?” ˢʰ

He looks at her. Hears the question this time. Gives her the answer Ciel can help him make accurate, one piece at a time.

Shoko nods and begins working.

Mau takes Yuka's hand before anyone can ask him to move it out of the way.

He will move it if they need him to.

He doesn't let go.
