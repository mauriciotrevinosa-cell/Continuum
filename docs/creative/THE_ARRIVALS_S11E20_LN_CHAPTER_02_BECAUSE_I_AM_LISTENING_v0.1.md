# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 10 — Because I Am Listening

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S11E20
**Continuity state:** B+189 early afternoon, continuous clinic work. Severe spatially mismatched injury requires coordinated repair, consciousness not restored by closing surface. Mau listens to Ciel/Shoko/Yuta, refuses only leaving. His Beru summoned heals with own reserve, emotions reflected in legion without loss of watch. First stress execution narrow Diablo-derived domestic cleaning: literal blink/no travel/seat unchanged, dirt removed/clothes and linens cleaned, not wound cure, new teleport or conceptual erasure. Noise supports bounded repair work, no unrestricted guest-model activation.

*When: Shoko tells Mau to lower the flow, he does it before she has to say his name twice.*

Yuta is on the other side. The positive energy he can shape reaches a part Mau has been trying to hold too broadly. Ciel helps separate what each is doing. Noise keeps the mismatched relation at the wound visible enough for the healers to work with something more useful than the appearance of closed skin.

The cut has ended.

The places it interrupted have not all found one another again.

Shoko refuses to call the surface the whole injury. She makes Mau repeat the part he is holding, corrects him when his fear starts to turn a precise supply into more force and tells him when a breath she can see is real.

He watches Yuka's face while he listens.

“That. Keep that,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

He keeps it.

* * *

Beru rises beside the bed when Mau calls him.

There is no announcement. No bow performed for a room which would have to turn away from the patient to acknowledge it. The ant sees Yuka, sees its king's hands and comes to the place Shoko indicates after Mau tells it to help.

Its own healing joins the work in the line Ciel keeps clear.

Beru's hand trembles.

It doesn't speak.

Mau looks at the hand for a second, then at the ant's face. He understands the fear there too well to ask it to go away for his comfort.

“With me,” he says quietly. ᴹ

Beru steadies the part it can control and keeps healing.

* * *

Rimuru reaches the room after the separate return is complete. Frieren is with him. Gojo stays in the passage long enough to tell the people gathering there that the door has to remain clear; then he asks what Shoko actually needs rather than what he can hit.

Igris has secured the body outside the protected living space, under the instructions Mau gave before leaving. Makima has departed along the separate route. Nobody has followed her, and the room hasn't acquired a second emergency because somebody decided the first one justified abandoning the terms.

Rimuru gives the account where Mau can hear it without asking him to look away.

“The pledge held,” he says. ᴿ

Mau's jaw tightens.

“I know.” ᴹ

Rimuru doesn't try to make that fact comforting.

He comes to the bedside and asks Ciel what she is seeing.

* * *

The healing takes longer than closing an ordinary wound because there are several different answers to put back in the same place.

Shoko has the body. Yuta can support another part of the repair. Beru's mana is its own contribution, not an excuse to spend endlessly from a source which never tires. Ciel and Noise keep the routes from answering one another wrongly while Mau supplies the part they have told him to hold.

Every time he wants to do more, he asks.

Sometimes Shoko gives him another task.

Sometimes she says no.

He hears both.

It is the no attached to leaving that he cannot accept.

* * *

When the first dangerous part has been stabilized, Yuta asks whether Mau will let him take the hand nearest the supply.

Mau moves his grip to Yuka's fingers. Yuta gets the angle he needs. Nothing in the cooperation requires Mau to stop touching her altogether.

Shoko tells him that they will need shifts.

“You can work around me.” ᴹ

“That isn't always how a shift works.” ˢʰ

“Then tell me where to sit.” ᴹ

She studies him for a moment and tells Rimuru to bring the chair closer to the side they are not using. Mau sits exactly where she points. He adjusts his hand when asked. He answers a question about the line, admits when he cannot tell what part Ciel is asking him to describe and lets her give it through Rimuru instead.

When someone suggests that he go outside while they continue, he says he is fine here.

The second time, he doesn't answer.

His eyes stay on Yuka.

* * *

There is blood drying on his neck.

Shoko has been occupied with more important things. Now she can see the collar sticking where the cloth has become stiff. His hands have been cleaned for the work; the rest of him looks like the place they brought her from.

“Mau. You need to get clean.” ˢʰ

“I'm not leaving her.” ᴹ

“You are not staying over this bed covered in blood.” ˢʰ

He looks at her.

She blinks.

The blood is gone.

* * *

His hand is still around Yuka's. His feet haven't moved. The chair is exactly where Shoko told Rimuru to put it. His collar has loosened from his skin, clean cloth lying where a moment ago there was something dark and stiff.

Shoko looks at his sleeve, then the sheet beneath the arm which held Yuka.

The stained part is clean when she looks back.

Rimuru stops in the middle of the word he was about to say.

Ciel notices the old domestic relation settle into an actual operation under a kind of attention Mau has not been able to reproduce deliberately. Noise helps keep its scope away from the body being healed. Dirt and cloth. The surface of his clothes. The ordinary disorder he has been told he must remove.

Yuka's injury does not disappear with it.

Mau looks at Shoko as though waiting for the next instruction.

“Clean,” he says. ᴹ

She takes a breath.

“I can see that.” ˢʰ

“I heard you.” ᴹ

For a moment the words make her face change.

Then she asks him to keep the next interval steady, and he does.

* * *

Jin-Woo has stayed with the people in the house as asked.

When he comes to the passage, his own Beru is with him. He offers the help rather than assuming a second healer should push into a room where every active route has just been separated carefully enough to keep one person alive.

Shoko asks what the new contribution can sustain. Ciel checks the offered line through the means they already have for speaking with her. Jin-Woo's Beru takes the next support interval when the first needs relief, while Mau's Beru remains near the bed with a hand which still has not quite stopped shaking.

The two kings do not discuss what power remains available in the abstract.

They ask what is useful here.

* * *

At the outer turn, one of Mau's ordinary soldiers makes a small, involuntary motion through the shoulders.

Another does the same near the room.

Shoko notices when the form carrying fresh cloth pauses in the passage, the edges of its gauntlet wavering before it sets the cloth down. It completes the assigned task. It doesn't attack anyone or abandon the post it has been given.

“What's happening with them?” she asks. ˢʰ

Mau looks toward the doorway as though he had forgotten the rest of his shadow was allowed to be seen.

“Me,” he says. ᴹ

Rimuru supplies the fuller answer when Mau cannot find it. The soldiers are answering the emotions carried through the bond. They have their orders. The watch is still there.

Mau looks at Beru again.

The ant looks back, silent and frightened, still making its contribution exactly where it has been asked.

* * *

By late afternoon the body on the bed looks much less like the injury which arrived.

That doesn't make Yuka open her eyes.

Mau asks why. Shoko tells him what they have repaired and what still isn't answering steadily. She refuses to invent a time because he would spend every second between now and it waiting for the promise to prove itself.

He nods as if he can accept that.

Then he turns back to his wife.

“Yuka.” ᴹ

Her hand lies in his. There is warmth in it. He has so many ways to recognize that she is alive, and none yet that gives him the sound he wants.

“I'm here,” he tells her. ᴹ

He bends over the hand and stays.
