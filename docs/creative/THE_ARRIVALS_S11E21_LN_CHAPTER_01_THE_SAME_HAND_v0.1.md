# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 11 — The Same Hand

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S11E21
**Continuity state:** B+189 evening/night. Yuka unconscious but alive, joining of injured body/own energy pattern requires time and supported repair, not more raw force. Mau maintains selected contribution with help, no sleep, listens to care instructions but refuses leaving. Narrow cleaning repeats on linens, not patient injury. Outside family/house respond without blame or instant resolution. No new attack, universal healing, source-memory wipe or fixed-hour sleep habit.

*When: Rimuru brings the cup, Mau asks whether something changed.*

“Only that you haven't drunk anything,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau looks at the water as though it has arrived from very far away.

He takes it with his free hand. Rimuru helps keep the angle while the other remains around Yuka's fingers. Mau drinks, gives it back and looks toward the place where Shoko is checking the latest part of the repair.

“Did that change?” he asks. ᴹ

Shoko tells him exactly what did.

The joining is holding more steadily. The damaged places are no longer being pulled into the wrong relation every time her own pattern tries to answer. There is still work they cannot finish by pressing more power through the same line.

“Her body has to keep the answer,” she says. “We can support it. We can't frighten it into going faster.” ˢʰ

Mau looks down at his hands.

“I'm trying not to.” ᴹ

“I know. Keep listening.” ˢʰ

* * *

The house has learned something happened long before everybody has heard an account of it.

They saw the blood. They saw Gojo come through the passage without his usual voice and Yuta take the shortest route to Shoko. The ordinary soldier bringing cloth stood too still for a moment before completing the task, and Igris returned with instructions for a place outside the living rooms to be kept undisturbed.

Fern asks people to leave the clinic door clear. She names the things that would actually help and takes the rest of the questions away from the room.

Nobody is told to stop caring.

They are given somewhere for the care to go.

Rem brings food which can be eaten without asking Mau to put down both hands. Taihei carries the second bowl. Kaneki asks where the next clean things should be left and gets an answer from Yuta. Touka keeps Ichika with her while answering the question her daughter has understood enough to ask.

Jin-Woo tells his family what he knows and leaves the unknown parts unknown.

His mother puts the extra cup back on the tray before Rem takes it in.

“For him too,” she says. ᴶᵐ

Rem nods.

* * *

Frieren comes to the bedside after Shoko has a moment for her.

She has the first account from the field, checked with the watch rather than turned into a theory that somebody can mistake for a witness. The cut did not enter along an ordinary projectile's path. The thing which made it was killed. The retained response reached Yuka and interrupted the part that would otherwise have completed the severance.

“You did reach her,” Frieren says. ᶠ

Mau looks at the face on the pillow.

“Look at her.” ᴹ

“I am.” ᶠ

His eyes shine suddenly. He blinks and the tears fall anyway.

“I checked it.” ᴹ

Frieren stays where he can see her without looking away from Yuka.

“We checked it,” she says. ᶠ

“I knew what to ask for. I thought I knew.” ᴹ

“We didn't know everything.” ᶠ

He shakes his head once, as though the sentence is too small to contain the body lying in front of him.

“I said I wouldn't let it happen again.” ᴹ

Frieren's hand comes to rest on the back of the chair. She doesn't tell him the promise was foolish. She doesn't make him prove how seriously he meant it.

“The person who hurt her doesn't become you because you wanted to stop it,” she says. ᶠ

He bows his head over Yuka's hand.

The words reach him. They do not make the tears stop.

* * *

When Yuta needs to change the support interval, Mau moves his elbow before being asked a second time.

When Shoko says the angle is wrong, he corrects it. When Ciel asks him to narrow the supply, he follows her count until it stays where they need it.

Then Rimuru asks whether he would sit outside the room for a little while.

Mau doesn't answer.

“I will be here,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau looks at him, then back at Yuka.

“Mau.” ᴿ

“What do you need me to hold?” ᴹ

Rimuru goes quiet.

There is enough of his friend still listening that the practical question cannot be mistaken for a failure to hear. There is also a place the request cannot yet get through.

Rimuru sits beside him instead.

* * *

The night does not bring a new disaster.

That makes it possible for everyone else to notice how much the first one has cost.

Shoko gives Yuta a rest interval he argues about once and then takes. One Beru relieves the other for a part of the work they can share; Mau's remains closest whenever its own reserve allows it. Jin-Woo helps keep the offered contributions from becoming a room full of people trying to save somebody by all doing the same thing at once.

Mau remains the constant beside Yuka's hand, holding the precise small part he has been given even when it no longer feels large enough to deserve the word help.

He asks for more when he can.

He accepts no when it is the answer.

He doesn't sleep.

* * *

Ciel tells him the joining has held through another interval.

“Thank you,” he whispers. ᴹ

*You are doing what we asked.* ᶜ

“I should have seen it.” ᴹ

*You saw it when it became something you could answer. We will examine the rest when she does not need this part of you.* ᶜ

He hears the distinction. He wants to turn back time anyway, to the hand lifting from his, the sleeve intact for the instant before it wasn't.

“Yuka.” ᴹ

There is no answer from the bed.

He puts his mouth against her fingers.

“Please.” ᴹ

* * *

Near the quietest part of the night, a fold beneath the supporting arm becomes stained where the old cloth was missed.

Shoko notices because she is about to replace it.

The stain is gone before she reaches for the clean piece. The fold settles flatter without moving Yuka's body. Mau hasn't looked at it. He has been listening to Ciel's next count with the concentration of someone for whom every number contains the person he wants to keep.

Shoko glances at Rimuru.

He has seen it too.

They leave the useful operation within the narrow scope it is holding and keep working on the injury it cannot clean away.

* * *

Before dawn, Yuka's hand shifts in Mau's.

He is out of the chair at once.

Shoko checks before she speaks. It is a response, but not waking. She says both parts clearly enough that he cannot turn one into the other by needing it too much.

He sits down again.

“She moved,” he says. ᴹ

“She did.” ˢʰ

He holds on to that honest little answer for the rest of the dark.
