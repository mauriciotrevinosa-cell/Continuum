# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 12 — All the Ways They Stay

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S11E21
**Continuity state:** B+190 day/evening, second calendar day after attack. Mau remains awake bedside, sustained contribution/repeated narrow cleaning; helpers take shifts, eat, retain wider household responsibilities. Ori sees real concern with Frieren support, no burden to cure adult. Makima culpability suspected, prior A still unknown to house; pledge intact is not proof of innocence, corpse analysis deferred. No new powers or collapse of protection.

*When: Mai tells Mau that Yuka would be furious with the chair he chose, he looks down as though it has done something wrong.*

“It's too close to that leg. She'd catch her foot every time she got up.” ᴹᵃ

Mau looks at the empty place beside the bed, then moves the chair a little without letting his hand leave Yuka's.

“Better,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

She has brought a soft layer for the hard seat because she knew he would refuse anything described as moving somewhere comfortable. Maki is behind her with the clean things Shoko asked for. Neither asks Mau to leave before putting them where they can be used.

Mai waits until he has accepted the cushion before asking how the joining is doing.

He gives her Ciel's last answer almost word for word.

“And you understand it?” she asks. ᴹᵃ

“Enough to hold my part.” ᴹ

She nods.

“Then do that.” ᴹᵃ

* * *

Maki stays after Mai has gone to bring the next things.

She sits on the other side of her granddaughter, watches the steady breath and asks Shoko two questions whose answers Mau has been collecting all morning.

Then she looks at him.

He cannot bear that look for the first moment. His eyes drop to the hand he is holding.

“I brought her,” he says. ᴹ

“She chose to go.” ᴹᵏ

“I thought—” ᴹ

“We all heard the conditions. We all knew the plan.” ᴹᵏ

His mouth tightens. The distinction is not the one his fear keeps offering him.

Maki leans forward just enough that he has to hear her without her raising her voice.

“I'm angry she's here. I'm angry somebody did this. I'm not going to make her decision disappear so you have one more thing to use against yourself.” ᴹᵏ

He finally looks at her.

She stays until he nods, a small acknowledgment which does not pretend the words have solved the whole of it.

* * *

Outside, the house continues because people need it to.

Fern and Hollis shorten the day's crossing window after explaining why the usual assistance will be elsewhere. Muster remains the home anchor, with the inner watch adjusted around that post rather than duplicated into a second unit with the same name. Igris checks the secured remains outside the living area and keeps the record of the field where it can be examined later.

The little clock is in that record.

It tells them the attacker had a time to keep. It doesn't tell them who gave it to him.

Rimuru puts the fact beside the intact pledge and refuses to let either one become more proof than it is.

“We still suspect her,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“Yes.” ᴿ

“Keeping the promise doesn't wash this clean.” ᴳ

“No.” ᴿ

They agree where to keep the work and go back to the people who need them now.

* * *

Jin-Woo brings the meal himself.

He has asked what Mau can eat while keeping the contribution steady. He has also learned which questions will make Mau stop hearing the useful part because they begin by asking him to go somewhere else.

He sets the bowl within reach.

“Eat while I hold this interval,” he says. ᴶʷ

Mau looks at the work, then at him.

“Ciel?” ᴹ

She confirms the exchange of support is the one they have already checked. Mau lets Jin-Woo's Beru take that part, keeps his hand around Yuka's and eats with the other.

He does not taste much of it.

Jin-Woo stays until the bowl is lighter.

“Your family?” Mau asks at last. ᴹ

“With people who know where they are.” ᴶʷ

Mau's face changes.

Jin-Woo hears the answer he has accidentally touched and gives him a moment before saying the next thing.

“They asked what would help. This helps.” ᴶʷ

He doesn't tell Mau that reaching the house made the world harmless. He sits beside him while he finishes eating.

* * *

Ori comes with Frieren in the afternoon.

She has been told Yuka is hurt and being helped. She has seen enough adults change what they were doing to know the explanation has left something large outside its words. Frieren has answered her questions without making the fear bigger to prove she is being honest.

At the door, Ori takes her mother's hand more tightly.

Mau looks up when he hears them.

“Hi, Ori.” ᴹ

His voice sounds wrong to her. Too rough, as though he has been speaking quietly for a very long time.

She holds up the little folded cloth she wanted to bring. Frieren asks Shoko where it can go before letting Ori come nearer. The answer is the table beside the bed, where it can remain clean and out of the work.

Ori puts it there herself.

“For when she wakes up,” she says. ᴼ

Mau looks at it.

“She'll like that.” ᴹ

Ori nods but keeps watching him. He is sitting differently from the man who held her map. All of him seems turned toward the hand he has not let go of.

Frieren stays with her until she is ready to leave.

* * *

After they go, Mau asks whether he frightened her.

“She's worried about you,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

He closes his eyes.

Rimuru doesn't let the sentence turn into another charge before adding the rest.

“Frieren is with her. She's allowed to bring something and go back to the people looking after her.” ᴿ

Mau nods.

His fingers shift around Yuka's, careful not to hold too hard.

“I wanted them to be able to just be here.” ᴹ

“They are here.” ᴿ

“Like this?” ᴹ

Rimuru looks at Yuka, then at his friend.

“Today, like this. With us helping.” ᴿ

* * *

There is no sudden turn that makes the second evening easy.

There are intervals that hold. A response Shoko can repeat. Less support needed in one part and a careful transfer of attention to another. Yuka's face looks rested enough that Mau keeps expecting her to open her eyes and object to something he has done with the blankets.

When she doesn't, he says her name again.

Beru remains close. Its hands are steadier while it works. The tremor returns when it has no task for a moment and has to stand with the same waiting its king is trying to survive.

Mau reaches toward it once, touches the arm and goes back to Yuka's hand.

Nobody has to explain the gesture.
