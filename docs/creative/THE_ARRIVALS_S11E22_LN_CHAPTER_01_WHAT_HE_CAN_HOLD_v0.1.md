# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 13 — What He Can Hold

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S11E22
**Continuity state:** B+191 day/night. Yuka unconscious >48h, repair stabilizing, Mau's contribution smaller/replaceable rather than sole life support. He remains awake by choice/fear, listens to adjustments. Inner friends support without consuming private guest models or granting universal cure. No continued source attack, no army reset or fixed-hour insomnia recurrence.

*When: Shoko tells him they can hold the next part without him, Mau asks which part she wants him to take instead.*

“You can just hold her hand,” she says. ˢʰ

He looks at the place where his supply has been kept so carefully.

“It will be all right if I stop that?” ᴹ

“We will take it over before you stop. Yes.” ˢʰ

He asks Ciel to confirm what changes. She gives him the order, waits until he has followed it and tells him when the other support is carrying the part he is trying so hard not to drop.

His hand remains around Yuka's.

Nothing terrible happens because that is all it is doing.

Mau looks at her face for a long time.

* * *

The third day has different work in it.

There are fewer moments when the repair needs the whole room's attention at once. Beru can stand back without feeling another contribution falter immediately. Yuta can leave for food and return without running. Shoko can explain a change before she makes it rather than naming it while moving her hands.

Yuka still doesn't wake.

Mau's eyes follow every little response. The shift of a finger. A change in breath. Something Shoko notices which looks to him like the beginning of the answer he has been waiting for, then becomes another honest piece of progress instead.

He asks what it means each time.

Shoko tells him, even when it is the same question again.

* * *

Gojo comes in when the room is quieter.

He has asked Shoko whether there is anything the visit would interrupt. She tells him to leave room at the side and not turn it into an argument about who can stay awake longer.

He pulls up a chair without promising to be amusing.

Mau glances at him, then back to Yuka.

“I'm listening,” he says before Gojo begins. ᴹ

“I know.” ᴳ

“Everyone keeps asking me to—” ᴹ

“I'm sitting here.” ᴳ

Mau stops.

Gojo settles in the chair. For a while he talks about what Suho asked in the yard, the thing Yuta showed him and the point at which Maki told both of them they had not come to demonstrate the entire history of swordsmanship before lunch.

Mau's mouth shifts at one part of it. Almost a smile.

Gojo lets that be enough.

* * *

Inside, Noise keeps finding the edges of the work which can actually help.

There are many things it could build. Some have been waiting. Some are only beginning to make sense after the offered map from Jin-Woo. None becomes the right answer merely because its other half has asked with a voice it has never wanted to hear sound like that.

Ciel helps it keep the repair visible where the healers can use it. The rhythm matters. The distinction between supporting an answer and demanding a new one matters.

DIO joins them in the shared place when she has rested enough to sit there comfortably.

“He asked,” Noise says. ᴺ

“You are helping,” she says. ᴰ

The outline turns toward the incomplete work.

“More.” ᴺ

DIO puts a hand beside its own, as she did after the visit with Yuka.

“Tell Ciel what you see. Let her tell you which part is needed.” ᴰ

Noise stays still for a moment.

Then it does.

* * *

Mau notices the difference before knowing what happened inside.

The information he needs becomes easier to hold without reopening every possible question around it. He asks Ciel what changed. She tells him Noise has kept the next relation separated more cleanly, giving the healers an answer they can actually use.

“Thank you,” he says. ᴹ

*Helping,* Noise answers. ᴺ

He bows his head. There is no additional power to demand in the word. There is somebody staying with him in the part neither can make easy.

* * *

Late in the afternoon, Shoko checks the small remaining line near Yuka's collarbone.

The wound that arrived is no longer there in the same way. Most of the skin has returned without a mark. This place has kept a faint difference, a narrow trace where the repair had to meet its own pattern slowly enough not to pull apart again.

Mau watches Shoko's face.

“Does it mean something's wrong?” ᴹ

“It means it has a mark. I'm checking whether the joining underneath it is holding.” ˢʰ

He nods.

“Is it?” ᴹ

“Yes.” ˢʰ

He looks back at Yuka. The answer should be enough for the moment. He makes himself hear it again instead of filling it with the thing he is afraid she has not said.

* * *

Rimuru offers the chair beside him after evening rather than offering to take his away.

They sit until the room changes into its night arrangement. Shoko's next check. Yuta's return. Beru lowering its hand when the support is no longer needed at the same strength.

“You can sleep,” Rimuru says after a while. “We know how to hold this now.” ᴿ

Mau doesn't answer immediately.

“I know.” ᴹ

It is not the answer Rimuru wants, but it is a different answer from denying the help exists.

He stays beside him a little longer.

* * *

Before midnight, Mau tells Yuka about the card game he lost.

He starts because he doesn't know what else to say and continues because he can imagine the questions she would ask. Whether Umaru behaved. Whether the guest knew the rules. Whether he used the inventory as an excuse to conceal a card, an accusation he would like to deny before it reaches the household record.

His voice roughens halfway through.

“You were going to make me finish,” he says. ᴹ

He bends over her hand.

“You can still make me finish.” ᴹ

The room stays quiet around the words.
