# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 14 — When She Says His Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S11E22
**Continuity state:** B+192 early afternoon, >72h unconscious after B18912:30 attack. Yuka wakes with repaired body/faint collarbone scar, disorientation about lost days but no new powers or lost relationship memory. Mau has not slept during critical vigil; becomes emotionally overwhelmed but hears Shoko. Support withdrawn by healers in checked stages, her hand remains held. No instant psychological recovery or claim of symptom-free life forever.

*When: Yuka says his name, Mau has been telling her she doesn't have to answer quickly.*

He has told her that before. He has also asked her to wake up and apologized for asking. He doesn't know which of the sentences would reach her if one could, and he has become afraid of letting the silence contain nothing he meant her to hear.

“Mau.” ʸ

He stops speaking.

Her eyes are open.

* * *

For an instant he does nothing at all.

Then he is standing, the chair moving behind him, his free hand lifted and stopped halfway because he cannot decide where to touch without interfering with something that has finally happened.

Yuka looks at him with a small, confused crease between her brows.

“Hey.” ʸ

His face breaks.

“Hey,” he says. ᴹ

It is barely a word.

Shoko is at the bedside. She asks Yuka to look at her for a moment. Mau moves when she needs the space, stays close enough that Yuka can keep the hand she has just found and listens while the questions begin.

Her name. Where she thinks she was. What she remembers before the room.

Yuka answers until she reaches the clearing. Her eyes turn toward Mau again.

“We were going home.” ʸ

He nods too quickly.

“We did.” ᴹ

* * *

She asks for water.

It is such an ordinary request that three people begin answering it. Shoko takes the cup from Yuta, helps Yuka sit enough to drink and tells the others to give her the room to do one thing at a time.

Mau is still crying.

He has become very quiet about it, which doesn't make it less obvious. Yuka watches him over the cup and reaches toward his face when Shoko takes it away.

He catches the movement with his cheek instead of catching her wrist.

“What happened?” she asks. ʸ

His mouth moves. He can't give her the whole answer.

“You were hurt,” Shoko says. “We've been repairing the injury. You are awake now, and I want to check the things that need checking before anybody asks you to have a long conversation.” ˢʰ

Yuka nods, still looking at Mau.

“Okay.” ʸ

He draws her hand against his face and bows over it.

* * *

Rimuru gives the news outside only after Shoko has said which news is actually true.

Awake. Speaking. Being checked. Give them time.

The words change the passage. Fern closes her eyes. Mai sits down in the nearest place without noticing she has chosen the floor. Maki puts a hand over her mouth and stays standing because she has been doing it for so long she has forgotten to choose otherwise.

Gojo turns away from the door, one hand pressed against the bridge of his nose. Jin-Woo's Beru lowers its head. The other Beru, inside, takes a step back when Shoko tells it the next support can be reduced.

It does so with extraordinary care.

* * *

Yuka feels better than the room seems prepared for.

There is an unfamiliar lightness after so much enforced stillness, but no great wound waiting for her when she shifts. She tries to look beneath the edge of her collar. Shoko helps before the movement becomes awkward.

The faint line is visible.

Yuka touches near it with a finger, following Shoko's instruction to leave the part being checked alone.

“Oh,” she says. ʸ

Mau goes still.

She notices, looks from the mark to him and tries a small smile.

“You're not the only one with battle scars anymore.” ʸ

The joke doesn't land where she expected.

His eyes fill again.

“I'm sorry,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka's smile changes. She reaches for him with both hands this time.

“Come here.” ʸ

* * *

Shoko lets the embrace happen in the position she has checked.

Mau bends carefully, so carefully that Yuka has to draw him the last little way. She puts one hand behind his head and holds him against her shoulder. His breath catches once. Then again.

She remembers his arms in the clearing.

The moment is only a fragment. Something wrong, his face suddenly close, the world moving while he kept her from falling. She had not had time to know what was happening before knowing he was there.

“You caught me,” she says. ʸ

He shakes his head against her.

“I was late.” ᴹ

“I remember you catching me.” ʸ

He has no answer which can remove that memory from her. He doesn't try.

He holds her until Shoko asks him to change the angle a little.

He hears that too.

* * *

When they can sit apart enough to look at one another, Yuka studies his face properly.

His clothes are clean. His hair is out of his eyes. There is something terribly tired in the way he keeps returning to the fact that she is looking back at him.

“How long?” she asks. ʸ

“A few days,” he says. ᴹ

Shoko gives the clearer answer.

More than three days since the injury. The body repaired much faster than the interrupted pattern could settle safely. Yuka has been supported through the part that would not hold by force alone.

Yuka looks at the hand he hasn't let her stop holding.

“Have you slept?” ʸ

Mau doesn't answer quickly enough.

She understands that part before anybody explains it.

* * *

The next support is withdrawn after Shoko and Ciel check the answer from Yuka's own body.

Mau waits for the instruction, lets the flow go in the order he is given and keeps expecting to feel something wrong when he is no longer contributing to the repair.

Yuka squeezes his hand.

It is such a small amount of pressure. It reaches him more clearly than the last three days of trying to hold everything together.

“Still here,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her.

“I know.” ᴹ

For the first time, the answer begins to feel like something his whole body has heard.

* * *

Shoko gives them a little time before the next check, with Yuta nearby and the room still quiet enough to hear if either needs her.

Mau sits on the edge of the bed where Yuka asks him to. She keeps his arm across her lap and touches the damp place on his cheek with the sleeve of the clean shirt he somehow hasn't left to change.

“You look terrible,” she says softly. ʸ

His mouth moves into the beginning of a smile.

“I was trying to impress you.” ᴹ

“Try resting. I hear it can be very attractive.” ʸ

He laughs, one rough breath which falls apart before becoming a second.

She draws him close again and lets him have the sound of her breathing while he finds his own.
