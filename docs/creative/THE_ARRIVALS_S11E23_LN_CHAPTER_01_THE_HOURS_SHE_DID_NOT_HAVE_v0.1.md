# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 66
## Chapter 15 — The Hours She Did Not Have

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 66
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S11E23
**Continuity state:** B+192 afternoon. Yuka asks about lost days with Mau present and receives account from Shoko/Rimuru/Mai/Maki; no secret campaign to shame husband. Feels physically restored, processes near-death/lost time in own voice. No further inpatient support required after checks, home with ordinary follow-up. McLaren hoodie retained as her existing garment. Mau carries with permission, no agency revocation.

*When: Yuka asks what he means by a few days, Mau looks toward Shoko.*

“I heard how many,” she says. “I want to know what happened in them.” ʸ

They have finished another check. She has drunk more water, eaten a little and answered the questions which tell Shoko she can follow a conversation without trying to make her keep up with every visitor waiting outside.

Mau is still beside her.

He looks at their hands before answering.

“We were healing you.” ᴹ

“Who is we?” ʸ

He begins naming them. Shoko. Yuta. Beru, then the other Beru when the support needed changing. Ciel and Noise keeping the damaged relations clear enough to work on. Rimuru helping them hear one another and keep the rest of the house moving.

He doesn't say much about himself.

Yuka notices.

* * *

Shoko tells her what the first minutes looked like without making her relive the injury in a catalogue of parts.

Mau reached her. The response through their contract interrupted the cut before it could finish. He brought her to the clinic while holding the first useful flow. The healers took the work together and kept adjusting it until her body could hold the repair itself.

Yuka looks at her husband.

He watches her face as though the account might hurt something they have only just put back together.

“And you stayed,” she says. ʸ

He nods.

“Here?” ʸ

“Here.” ᴹ

“The whole time?” ʸ

His throat moves.

Rimuru answers from the other side.

“He moved when we needed the space. He ate when somebody put the food where he could reach it. He did the work Ciel and Shoko asked him to do.” ᴿ

Yuka waits for the rest.

“He didn't leave.” ᴿ

* * *

Mau starts to say that it wasn't important beside the injury.

Yuka turns her hand in his.

“Let me hear it.” ʸ

He closes his mouth.

She asks why his clothes are clean if he hasn't left. Shoko looks at Rimuru, then explains exactly what she saw: one blink, no movement from the chair, the blood gone. Later the cloth beneath the arm cleaned itself while he was listening to something else.

Yuka looks down at his sleeve.

“That's new.” ʸ

“A little,” he says. ᴹ

“It was not a little to watch,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

For a moment Yuka almost smiles. Then she sees what the story means beneath the impossible neatness of his shirt. He found a way to obey without leaving the place where he was afraid something would happen when he wasn't watching.

She brings his hand up to her mouth.

“You must have been so frightened,” she says. ʸ

He looks away.

* * *

Mai comes in with Maki when Yuka asks to see them.

Her aunt has brought the McLaren hoodie from the room Yuka actually lives in. Yuka recognizes it at once. She asks whether anyone tried to steal it while she was sleeping. Mai says only if Mau's old claims of ownership count, which they have never done as far as the family is concerned.

Mau opens his mouth.

Maki raises a finger.

He closes it again.

Yuka laughs, then reaches for them both. The embraces are careful for the first moment because they remember the body that arrived. She holds them firmly enough to tell them which body is here now.

Maki's hand stays against her hair afterward.

“I remember him catching me,” Yuka says. ʸ

“He did,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

Mau bows his head over the side of the bed.

* * *

When Yuka asks about the attacker, they tell her what they actually saw.

A devil. A cut which did not cross the distance her Infinity held apart. Makima killed it. The body was kept. The meeting pledge did not break.

Yuka looks at the people around her.

“That doesn't make her innocent.” ʸ

“No,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“But we don't know how.” ʸ

“Not yet.” ᴿ

She sits with the answer rather than trying to make the room prove it can solve the whole thing before she is allowed to go home.

“I wanted to hear her too,” she says after a while. ʸ

Mau turns toward her immediately.

“That doesn't make this yours.” ᴹ

Yuka holds his gaze.

“I know.” ʸ

The small silence afterward makes him hear what he has just said.

She doesn't force the rest of the lesson into it.

* * *

Shoko has Yuka stand only after the checks she wants are complete.

Mau rises with her. Yuka takes two steps, finds her balance and looks surprised by how ordinary the floor feels. She expected the room to tell her body a story it remembers. Instead it is the faces around her which carry the missing days.

She looks back at Mau.

He has one hand lifted, ready to catch her again.

“I'm steady,” she says softly. ʸ

He nods. The hand lowers a little.

Shoko tells her what to report, when she wants the next visit and that home is close enough for the rest to happen there. It is an actual release from the work which kept her in this bed, not a favor granted because her husband has asked enough times.

Yuka puts on the hoodie with Mai's help.

Mau watches the familiar cloth settle over her shoulders.

* * *

At the door, he asks whether he can carry her.

Yuka looks at the short stretch of passage, then up at him.

“I can walk.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

He doesn't take her off her feet before she has answered. She sees how much effort it costs him to leave the question open.

“You can carry me,” she says. ʸ

He gets an arm beneath her knees and another behind her back, moving with the care she remembers from the moment before the world went away.

This time she is awake to put her arms around him.

“Home,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her for a second.

Then he brings her there.
