*When: Mau wakes, Yuka is downstairs arguing with Mai about the most inconvenient place to put a clasp.*

He hears her before he has finished sitting up.

For a moment his body is ready to move faster than the sound requires. Then the next sentence reaches him. She is laughing. Mai is defending her design with the tone she uses when she knows precisely which part someone has found annoying.

Mau puts his feet on the floor.

Ciel tells him the night stayed steady. He thanks her, washes and goes to find his wife.

* * *

Yuka is wearing the hoodie, holding the disputed piece between two fingers and demonstrating what happens when the sleeve catches.

Mau sees the motion and starts to reach before noticing she is doing it deliberately.

She looks up.

“Good morning.” ʸ

“Good morning.” ᴹ

“You slept.” ʸ

He nods.

She sets the clasp down and opens an arm for him. He bends to kiss her, stays close a moment longer than the greeting needs and asks whether she has eaten.

“I'm waiting for you.” ʸ

Mai points toward the food.

“Please. Before he asks it three more ways.” ᴹᵃ

Mau takes the seat beside Yuka. She moves her cup out of his elbow's path and gives Mai the piece to keep working on.

* * *

The follow-up is uneventful in the best way.

Shoko checks the joining, asks what Yuka has felt since leaving and hears that she has been comfortable. The faint mark remains near the collarbone. The work underneath it is holding without the contributions that kept the clinic room awake.

Mau listens to every answer.

“You can ask her how she feels,” Shoko tells him when his eyes return to the marked line. ˢʰ

“I did.” ᴹ

“Then hear the answer too.” ˢʰ

Yuka takes his hand on the way out.

“I feel good,” she says. ʸ

He nods.

She watches him make the effort to let that remain the answer for more than the first few steps.

* * *

The papers return after lunch.

Mau is not planning a way to strike Makima while she leaves the next meeting. He is looking at the undertaking which held while Yuka was hurt and asking where the action began.

The body was outside the watched approach. The attack appeared at the place it cut. The clock was keeping the same time as the crossing. Makima's new acts during the meeting can be accounted for. None includes a visible instruction to the attacker.

“An old decision,” he says to Rimuru. “Something already moving.” ᴹ

“It could be,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“And the pledge only answered the part after we accepted.” ᴹ

“We need more than could before we call that the whole account.” ᴿ

Mau knows. He also knows how easily being unable to prove the missing part can become the place another person hides.

They write down the question rather than a verdict.

Then Rimuru takes the first copy away to keep the work from depending on Mau staring at the same lines until the next meal.

* * *

Yuka asks him to come sit with her while she finishes the clasp question.

He does. He holds the piece Mai wants, catches the thread Yuka drops and answers a question about whether the new position would catch his left fingers.

Then he goes quiet.

She sees where his eyes have stopped: the edge of her sleeve, the place a harmless fold could resemble the beginning of the shape he saw in the clearing.

She touches his wrist.

He looks up immediately.

“Still with me?” she asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Then tell me which side you think it belongs on.” ʸ

He looks at the actual clasp. Gives her an answer. Lets the next few minutes belong to the thing in her hands instead of the thing he couldn't stop from reaching them.

* * *

That evening, Ori comes to show the corrected map.

Yuka has room beside her and pats the place before Ori needs to ask. Mau holds the lower edge while Ori points out the path she and Richeh checked, the little change Coco wanted and the reason the old mark was misleading.

He tells her the correction is good.

“That was the old one,” she says. ᴼ

He looks down.

She has turned the page over while explaining.

“Sorry. Show me again.” ᴹ

Ori does. He listens this time, asks about the right part and finds an answer which makes her smile.

After she goes back to Frieren, Yuka leans against him.

“You don't have to get every minute right today,” she says. ʸ

He looks at the page Ori has left.

“I wanted to hear her.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

She stays beside him while he finishes looking at the actual map.
