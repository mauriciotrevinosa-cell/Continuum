# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 67
## Chapter 15 — Emergency Supplies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 67
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S11E32
**Continuity state:** B+195 evening after return. Shop expansion rechecked outside, purchase works against existing balance. Fort receives newly available snacks; Cola was already available, not a new unlock. Ordinary carriers finish tasks and return to Mau. Umaru/Rem, Chloe and Ori are existing residents/Fort members. No new arrivals or invented children.

*When: Umaru sees the packet in Ori's hand, she stops in the middle of asking about the guardian.*

“Where did that come from?” ᵁ

Ori holds it up.

“The expedition.” ᴼ

“Specifically.” ᵁ

Mau recognizes the change in tone. He has heard important negotiations conducted with less concentration than Umaru is now giving a bag of crisps.

Ori looks at the packet, then at him.

“The shop got more things,” she explains. ᴼ

Umaru takes the bag with the care of someone being handed a fragile historical document.

“Is this the only one?” ᵁ

“No,” Mau says. ᴹ

She does not appear to hear him.

“If it is, we need to protect it until a proper tasting can be arranged. We can't just open something like this in a corridor.” ᵁ

Mau produces a second packet from the inventory.

Umaru looks at it.

He produces a third.

Rem, standing behind Umaru, begins to smile.

“Those are supplies for the Fort,” Ori says. ᴼ

Mau sets a labelled bundle on the table. It contains the ones Ori selected for that purpose, not every packet they bought. There are other bundles for the common stores and a smaller one he has put aside to try with Yuka.

Umaru stares at the labels.

Then she looks up at him.

“Could you come down here a little?” ᵁ

He bends.

She places a hand on his shoulder with solemn care.

“Thank you.” ᵁ

Before he can answer, she hugs him.

The packet crinkles between them. He puts an arm around her back, laughing softly at the sound, and holds her for as long as she stays.

When she draws back, her hand remains on his shoulder.

“And while you're there, can I have a Coke?” ᵁ

Mau closes his eyes.

Rem turns away with a laugh she does not manage to hide.

“Of course,” he says. ᴹ

* * *

He checks the expanded shop outside the gate before turning the supply moment into a promise.

The listings remain. He buys one small packet they did not already bring out, sees the balance change and catches the item in the ordinary way. No counter, no open Purple frame needed.

Ciel records the successful test.

Umaru watches with an expression which suggests she has just learned the extent of a new diplomatic resource.

“Don't,” Mau tells her. ᴹ

“I haven't said anything.” ᵁ

“You were about to establish a ministry.” ᴹ

“A modest office.” ᵁ

Rem reaches past her to move the packets away from the edge of the table.

“Maybe finish the expedition report first.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks at her, then at Ori's carefully arranged bundles.

“The expedition report has been excellent.” ᵁ

Ori smiles.

Chloe arrives in time to hear that part and asks what happened after the machines. Ori reaches for her map before Mau can begin telling the story himself.

He lets her take it.

* * *

Igris brings the last of the ordinary carriers back when their assigned deliveries are complete.

Mau checks that the materials needing inspection have reached the correct place, that the paper is dry and that none of the mechanical pieces has ended up among the food. The soldiers return to his shadow. Igris stays long enough for Ori to thank him for helping with her list.

“You gave clear instructions,” he tells her. ᴵ

She looks pleased in a way Mau suspects she will remember for longer than the guardian.

After Igris withdraws, Rem asks whether the new meals are complicated to prepare. Mau shows her the description rather than telling her they will replace anything she enjoys making.

“Ori thought you might want one when you don't feel like cooking,” he says. ᴹ

Rem looks at Ori, now bent over the map with Chloe.

For a little while she says nothing.

Then Umaru puts one of the ordinary snack packets beside her cup.

“We can conduct a joint review.” ᵁ

Rem smiles down at it.

“That sounds nice.” ᴿᵉ

Mau leaves them to choose when.

On the way back to Yuka, he buys the Coke Umaru asked for even though there are already familiar drinks in the supplies. It is such a small, old thing to be able to give her. No new technique. No risk to understand. He has credit, she asked, and he can put it in her hand.

When he does, she thanks him again without the performance.

Then she remembers the first bag and calls for Ori to come help protect the contents from a premature tasting.

Ori hurries back, laughing.

Mau finds Yuka watching him from the doorway.

“Successful expedition?” she asks. ʸ

He looks at the map table, at Frieren with the new book beside her and at Mai turning one of the tools over in her fingers while Momo examines the fastening on Ori's case.

“I think so.” ᴹ

Yuka takes his hand.

“Come tell me.” ʸ
