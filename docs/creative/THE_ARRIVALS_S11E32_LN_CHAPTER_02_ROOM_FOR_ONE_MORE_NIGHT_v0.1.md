# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 67
## Chapter 16 — Room for One More Night

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 67
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S11E32
**Continuity state:** B+195 evening before family supper. Ori/Frieren visit Mau and Yuka's shared home while Rimuru/Shizue are there. Ori asks another night near Mau; Frieren's small response is noticed without accusing or burdening Ori. Group sleepover proposed for all six, then open to named willing residents in common room. Temporary bedding, not bedroom reassignment or forced romance. Shizue still has her own choice/guest accommodation.

*When: the knock comes, Mau is trying to explain to Yuka how a man selling a lamp became the most important person in an old travel book.*

“He wasn't in the travel book,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“You weren't there.” ᴹ

“Ciel was.” ᴿ

*He wasn't in the travel book,* Ciel confirms. ᶜ

Shizue looks from one of them to the other and starts smiling before she has worked out the whole disagreement.

Yuka has the map in her lap. Mau has already pointed out the bridge, the wheelhouse and the path they took when it rained. She has asked more questions about the rain than the guardian. It took him a moment to understand that she wanted to hear what they did while nothing dangerous was happening.

Now somebody knocks again, more softly.

Mau gets up.

Ori stands beside Frieren in the passage. Her hat is off, her face washed and the map case finally unfastened from the jacket. Without the expedition equipment she looks ready for an ordinary evening at home.

She sees Yuka, Rimuru and Shizue inside and hesitates.

“Come in,” Yuka says before the hesitation can become a retreat. ʸ

Ori steps over the threshold.

Frieren follows at a pace which leaves the question Ori's to ask.

Mau crouches near her.

“Something left out of the report?” ᴹ

She shakes her head.

“Can I sleep with you one more night?” ᴼ

He looks at her, then past her for an instant.

Frieren hasn't changed her expression very much. It is in the ears first: a small lowering, almost gone as soon as she notices him noticing. She has been waiting while seven days happened for them. She heard the reports. She has the book. None of that is quite the same as having Ori asleep nearby again.

Ori is still looking at Mau, unaware that the question has landed anywhere else.

He doesn't want to make her turn around and apologize for wanting something good.

He thinks of the map in Yuka's lap, the book Frieren brought with her and the two people who have wandered in to hear about their day because this home has begun making room for them almost without anybody noticing.

Then he looks at Yuka.

“What about a sleepover?” ᴹ

She glances at Ori, then Frieren, and understands without needing him to point anything out.

“All of us?” ʸ

“If everyone wants.” ᴹ

Ori turns immediately.

“Mum too?” ᴼ

“Mum too,” Mau says. ᴹ

Frieren's ears lift a little before she answers.

“I'd like that.” ᶠ

Rimuru starts examining the available floor as though somebody has assigned him the first planning meeting.

“Six people here might be—” ᴿ

“The common room,” Shizue suggests. ˢᶻ

They all look at her.

She has spoken quietly, but she doesn't take the idea back.

“There would be room to sit together before sleeping. If it's free.” ˢᶻ

Ori looks at Mau with hope returning to its full, unreasonable strength.

“We could take the book.” ᴼ

He stands.

“Then we should see about the room.” ᴹ

* * *

The first plan is for six.

That lasts until Umaru hears the word sleepover.

She arrives with Rem, takes in the folded bedding and asks whether an event involving the vending citizen in a common space has been properly registered with the Fort.

“It's six blankets,” Mau says. ᴹ

“That's how these things start.” ᵁ

Rem looks at the space beside the ones Yuka has laid out.

“Could we stay too?” ᴿᵉ

Umaru stops performing long enough to look at her.

“Yes,” Yuka says. ʸ

Mau opens his mouth, finds that this has already been settled correctly and goes to get two more sets of bedding.

The invitation spreads by people hearing it, not by an order that every resident must appear. Some want to stay. Some come to sit for a while and plan to go back to their own rooms. The walkways and doors remain clear, the supper space usable. Fern makes sure of that before somebody puts a pillow where a tray will need to pass.

“You can leave this part with me,” Mau tells her as she checks the next gap. ᴹ

“I know where people trip.” ᶠᵉ

“So do I. It's usually wherever I put something.” ᴹ

She looks at him, then at the bundle he has set down exactly where he should not have.

He moves it.

“Evidence received.” ᴹ

Fern smiles despite herself and goes to find somewhere she actually wants to sit.

* * *

Ori chooses a place close to both Mau and Frieren.

No one makes her explain the geometry. She puts the book where they can reach it, lays her blanket down and asks whether this side will be all right. Yuka checks where she intends to lie, then moves her own pillow enough to make the answer yes.

Frieren sets the grimoire out of the bedding area before sitting beside Ori to hear one part of the report again. It is the part where the replacement fitting was too short. Ori has apparently decided that this needs to be shared with her mother in full.

Mau hears his professional reputation being revised while he helps Rimuru carry the last cushions.

“Seven days,” Rimuru says. “And that's what she brings home about you.” ᴿ

“I caught a whole cart.” ᴹ

“With the wrong fitting?” ᴿ

Shizue laughs. Mau points at Rimuru as though he intends to remember this insult, then hands him another cushion.

Across the room, Chloe has joined Ori over the map. Umaru and Rem are settling their things with enough room to keep the packets away from the pillows. Ram stops by, looks at where her sister has chosen to be and tells Seika they can bring their own cushions over after supper if they want to stay a while.

Mai comes to inspect the sleeping arrangements with Momo. Ori proudly shows her that the hat has been placed somewhere it cannot be flattened by a blanket. Mai approves the decision with the gravity it deserves.

By the time someone calls that supper is nearly ready, the room no longer resembles a formal arrangement for six.

It looks like the house has decided to spend the evening together.

Yuka stands beside Mau, watching Ori pull Frieren down to see a detail at the bottom of the map.

“Good idea,” she says softly. ʸ

Mau looks at the two of them.

“I wanted another night too.” ᴹ

Yuka takes his hand and presses it between both of hers.

“Then stay.” ʸ
