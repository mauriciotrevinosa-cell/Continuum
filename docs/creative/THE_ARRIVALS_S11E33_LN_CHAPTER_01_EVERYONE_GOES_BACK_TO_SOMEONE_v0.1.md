# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 67
## Chapter 17 — Everyone Goes Back to Someone

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 67
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S11E33
**Continuity state:** B+195 night. Existing isolated slime form used for play/rest without simultaneous incompatible body builds. No sovereign powers stolen from Rimuru. Shizue freely offers Rimuru a place beside her, no official romance decree. Named residents join common-room sleepover; others return to own rooms. Mau sleeps with Yuka/Ori near Frieren, Rimuru near Shizue. Warm close to approved first part, not S11 season finale.

*When: Yuka looks from Mau to the empty space in her arms, he knows exactly what she is suggesting.*

They have finished supper. The first round of stories has become three conversations, a game at the far end of the bedding and a discussion about whether the new snacks should be opened before anybody has brushed their teeth.

Ori is sitting with Frieren and Chloe, telling them where Igris stood during the guardian fight. Mau is nearby helping Yuka fold the cloth wrappers so none of the little presents will lose their tags.

Yuka looks at him again.

He glances toward Rimuru.

“He's going to complain.” ᴹ

“About what?” Rimuru asks at once. ᴿ

Mau looks back at his wife.

She smiles with absolutely no sympathy for his attempt to blame somebody else.

He lets Ciel confirm the familiar isolated body pattern before making the change. There is no second body being forced into the first, no new structure built because he happens to want a joke. The clothes and things he needs kept safe go into the inventory. The small slime settles onto Yuka's waiting hands.

For a moment the room goes quiet around them.

Then Umaru makes an unmistakably interested sound.

Rimuru points.

“That. That is what I'm going to complain about.” ᴿ

Yuka brings Mau against her chest.

“He's very convenient.” ʸ

“I'm a person,” Mau protests. ᴹ

“A convenient person.” ʸ

Ori has already left the map to come closer.

“Can I?” ᴼ

Yuka looks down at Mau. He gives the little movement which means yes before Ori has to ask again. Yuka helps settle him into her arms, staying close until Ori has the weight properly held.

Frieren watches the handover with a smile so small Mau is glad he is facing her to see it.

Rimuru folds his arms.

“My entire identity has become a household amenity.” ᴿ

Shizue looks up at him from beside the pillows.

“You can sleep beside me if you stop complaining.” ˢᶻ

He turns his head.

For once there is no answer ready.

Mau makes a delighted noise from Ori's arms.

Rimuru looks at him, then at Shizue, who is still waiting to see whether he wants the place she offered.

He becomes a slime.

The room approves the decision so enthusiastically that he is not allowed to reach his chosen cushion without being greeted by several people first.

* * *

The two of them travel farther around the common room than either intended.

Mau goes from Ori to Umaru, who explains that the vending citizen has become portable at last. Rem laughs and makes sure he is passed by agreement rather than suddenly pulled from one set of hands to the next. When Chloe asks to hold him, he tips toward her and lets her settle him carefully against her lap.

“You don't have to hold your breath,” he tells her after a few seconds. ᴹ

She lets it out with a laugh.

At the other end of the room, Rimuru is enduring a formal welcome from the Fort which appears to involve his own bowl being placed beside him. He tells Umaru that this is precisely why he is concerned about institutional overreach.

She asks whether he would like a cushion with it.

He accepts.

Mau laughs so hard his shape wobbles in Chloe's arms.

When Mai gets her turn, she looks down at him for several seconds before speaking.

“About this big,” she says. ᴹᵃ

Mau goes still.

Yuka has to cover her mouth.

“You waited all day,” he says. ᴹ

“Very important commission.” ᴹᵃ

Momo nearly drops the folded cloth she has been holding.

* * *

Later, the room begins to quiet without anyone announcing that the fun is over.

Some people take their cups back and head toward their own rooms. Others stretch out where they have chosen to stay. The game is put away with one disagreement preserved for tomorrow. Fern makes one last check of the clear walkway, then sits down instead of finding another task.

Ori brings Mau back to Yuka herself.

“He's yours again,” she says, a little sleepily. ᴼ

“Thank you,” Yuka answers. ʸ

She settles him beside her pillow long enough for him to return to his usual body in the space they have left clear. He gets his clothes from the inventory and sits with his back against the cushions, Yuka leaning beside him. The unfamiliar pin is still in her hair. He helps her take it out and puts it somewhere it cannot catch on the bedding.

Rimuru has found his way back to Shizue.

She puts a hand against his small round side as he settles near her, neither making a performance of the choice nor taking it back because people have seen it. He stops trying to pretend he only became a slime to defend a principle.

Ori comes between Mau and Frieren with the ordinary travel book.

“The ending?” she asks. ᴼ

Mau opens to the sheet they added to their own notes.

Frieren looks at it, then at him.

“There was a missing part,” he explains. ᴹ

Ori nods.

“They didn't say what happened after they got back.” ᴼ

Frieren moves close enough to read with them.

Mau reads the version Ori chose. They went home. They ate something. He showed her what he had brought back. They slept.

Yuka's head rests against his shoulder before he finishes the last sentence.

“Good ending,” she says. ʸ

Ori looks around the room.

She seems satisfied with it too.

* * *

When the book is put away, Ori lies near Mau with Frieren close on her other side. She reaches across the small space for her mother's hand and finds it without having to sit up.

Mau watches until he realizes that he is allowed to stop watching.

He has protected her. He has also let her choose the longer path, make a mistake on the map, say she was tired and keep a present which might not contain anything new. None of that has required him to stop loving her for one second.

Yuka shifts against him.

“Come here,” she murmurs. ʸ

He lies down beside her. She puts his arm where she wants it, closes her hand over his and lets her eyes fall shut.

He kisses her forehead.

“I love you.” ᴹ

“Love you.” ʸ

Ciel has received the ordinary watch acknowledgment. The soldiers outside have their existing instructions. There is no new sound he needs to investigate before he is allowed to close his eyes.

Across the room, Rem pulls the edge of a blanket back over Umaru's feet. Frieren's hand remains in Ori's. Shizue has left room beside her pillow for a small slime who has finally stopped offering objections.

Mau listens to the people settling around him.

Then he sleeps.
