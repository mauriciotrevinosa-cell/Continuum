# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 6 — The Other End of Breakfast

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S11E36
**Continuity state:** B+197 dawn/morning. Household day begins through separate routines. Ko/Nazuna remain together with feeding decision unchanged. Anko receives a letter Hinata wrote earlier through the existing Tempest correspondence, not instantaneous travel. Proposed visit Luminous/Hinata still to arrange, no arrival. Ram/Seika, Rem/Umaru and other residents retain their own activities. No new anonymous staff, guards or residents.

*When: Nazuna comes in for breakfast, she has already had a day.*

She puts both forearms on the table, lowers her head between them and announces that anyone asking her to be helpful should do it in the next three minutes.

Ko sits down beside her.

“You said five outside.” ᴷ

“Travel time.” ᴺᶻ

Anko, across the table with a cup which looks much more necessary than pleasant, glances at the open door.

“A long and dangerous road from the courtyard.” ᴬⁿ

Nazuna lifts one finger without lifting her head.

“Exactly.” ᴺᶻ

Ko puts the plate where she can reach it. She turns her face toward him, sees what he has brought and sits up enough to eat.

They have been out walking. Nothing in the route required a consultation or a new test. They talked, stopped somewhere Ko liked and stayed long enough that the light began changing before Nazuna had admitted she was tired.

Anko asks whether they saw anyone interesting.

“Us,” Nazuna says. ᴺᶻ

“Reliable report.” ᴬⁿ

Ko laughs into his cup.

* * *

By the time Mau and Yuka arrive, other people have begun bringing their mornings to the table.

Fern has a folded list but hasn't opened it. Jinshi is looking for somewhere he can set down two letters without putting them beneath somebody's food. Maomao takes one look at the space he has chosen and moves a bowl before he can demonstrate why it was a bad decision.

“Thank you,” he says. ᴶⁱ

“You were going to notice eventually.” ᴹᵐ

Mau sits near them while Yuka goes to get her plate. He asks Nazuna how the night was and receives a detailed complaint about morning existing at the end of it.

“I'll mention it to DIO,” he says. ᴹ

“A useful government at last.” ᴺᶻ

Anko's attention has moved to the smaller of Jinshi's letters.

He notices.

“That one's yours. It came with the packet from Tempest.” ᴶⁱ

She reaches for it, pauses to keep the edge away from the tea and turns it over.

The handwriting changes her expression before she opens the fold.

Nazuna sees.

“Oh.” ᴺᶻ

“Eat.” ᴬⁿ

“I'm doing several things.” ᴺᶻ

Anko reads the first line with the determined concentration of someone who has decided not to provide entertainment.

It doesn't work.

* * *

Hinata wrote the letter several days ago.

She gives enough of her schedule for Anko to understand why the next visit isn't as simple as deciding she would like to come. There are people expecting her, an obligation she cannot move and a discussion with Luminous which might make the travel useful for more than one reason.

There is also a question which has nothing to do with duties: whether Anko would like to visit if the dates can be arranged the other way around.

Anko reads that part again.

Nazuna has finished enough of the plate to move closer.

“Is she coming?” ᴺᶻ

“Possibly.” ᴬⁿ

“Are you going?” ᴺᶻ

“Possibly.” ᴬⁿ

“A thrilling amount of possibility.” ᴺᶻ

Anko folds the paper halfway, then opens it again because she hasn't finished the last paragraph.

Mau gives her the time instead of asking to hear the whole thing.

Yuka sits beside him with her plate.

“Good news?” she asks quietly. ʸ

He glances toward Anko.

“I think that's hers to say.” ᴹ

Yuka follows the look, smiles and begins eating.

* * *

The larger letter is meant for Rimuru.

He comes in while Jinshi is explaining which packet brought it. Shizue is behind him with Alice and Ryota, the three of them already involved in a discussion about something they want to examine later. She directs the two toward food before answering Rimuru's question about whether they need anything.

“We'll tell you,” she says. ˢᶻ

He accepts that, takes his letter and sits with the others.

The message concerns a possible visit and work the people in Tempest have been discussing with representatives of Luminous's city. It asks what dates would suit, what the household would like to discuss and whether they can avoid treating the whole matter as a declaration of allegiance somebody forgot to agree to.

Rimuru reads that part aloud.

Mau reaches for his cup.

“An excellent precaution.” ᴹ

Fern opens her list at last.

“We can give them the actual questions about the stores and the road.” ᶠᵉ

“And ask who they're sending before preparing rooms,” Jinshi adds. ᴶⁱ

Rimuru nods.

No one has to decide the future of three communities before breakfast is finished. They agree to sit down later, with the letters and the people who will be affected by what they offer.

Across the table, Anko looks at the line about the possible delegation, then at her own letter.

“I may have a scheduling question,” she says. ᴬⁿ

Nazuna makes a delighted noise.

* * *

By then the room has begun emptying in one direction and filling in another.

Ram stops beside Rem to ask whether she will be using the small table later. Rem shakes her head, then turns to Umaru to check a plan they made yesterday. Umaru draws her closer to see a mark on a sheet without seeming to notice the hand she has kept around hers.

Ram notices. She keeps the smile small enough that her sister can pretend not to have been watched.

Seika is waiting near the doorway.

“Is it free?” she asks when Ram reaches her. ˢᵉ

“Yes. Unless you intend to serve a state banquet on it.” ᴿᵃ

“Two cups.” ˢᵉ

“Manageable.” ᴿᵃ

They leave together, still discussing where it will be quieter.

Mau watches them go, then turns toward Yuka when she taps his knee beneath the table.

“You promised to look at something with me.” ʸ

“I remember.” ᴹ

“After this?” ʸ

He looks at the almost empty plate, takes the last bite and nods.

“After this.” ᴹ

* * *

Nazuna stands before they do.

She has reached the end of what she is willing to call morning. Ko takes his cup back with hers and comes to walk her to the passage.

“You don't have to tiptoe around the whole house,” she tells him. ᴺᶻ

“I wasn't going to.” ᴷ

“Good. Just the parts I'm in.” ᴺᶻ

He laughs, and she catches his hand for a moment before turning toward her room.

Anko has found paper for a reply. She writes the beginning, stops, crosses nothing out and starts a different sentence below it.

Mau passes without trying to read over her shoulder.

At the door he sees her smile at something on Hinata's page.

He goes out with Yuka into the part of the day that is only beginning for them.
