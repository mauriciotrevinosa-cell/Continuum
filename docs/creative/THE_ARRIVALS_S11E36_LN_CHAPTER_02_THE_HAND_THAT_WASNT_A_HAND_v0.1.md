# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 7 — The Hand That Wasn't a Hand

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S11E36
**Continuity state:** B+197 morning/afternoon. Yuka refines an existing Sky operation with Mau/Maki/Yuta observing; no new CT or shikigami granted. Momo Ayase gives a voluntary demonstration and helps Mau revisit his inherited psychic-hand pattern, distinct from Authority and Momo Nishimiya's broom. One small external hold tested, strain remains, not a solved route. No dual CT or concealed construction yet.

*When: Yuka tells him he is looking at the wrong part, Mau has to ask which part she wants him to watch.*

“The edge,” she says. ʸ

“I am watching the edge.” ᴹ

“You're watching where it goes. I want to know what happens here when I let go.” ʸ

She brings two fingers together, showing him the short line where the fold begins to relax.

Mau crouches beside the mark on the ground.

“Again?” ᴹ

Yuka nods.

The small piece of cloth hangs where she put it. There is no person in its path and no reason to turn the exercise into a demonstration of how much force she can borrow. She makes the fold, draws it a little farther than last time and releases it.

The cloth lands well beyond the mark.

Mau looks at the place it left.

“You're closing this part first.” ᴹ

“I know. I want it to stop doing that.” ʸ

Maki, sitting on the low wall with Yuta beside her, points to Yuka's wrist.

“Then start with what you're doing before the release.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuka looks down at her hand.

“I'm keeping it still.” ʸ

“You're keeping it stiff.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Mau begins to say something, catches Yuka looking at him and closes his mouth with considerable dignity.

“You do it too,” she says. ʸ

“I was going to agree.” ᴹ

Yuta laughs.

* * *

They spend the first part of the session making the fold smaller.

It is not the direction Yuka expected to enjoy. The smaller it becomes, the less room there is for her to call a result good because the cloth went somewhere useful. She can see the edge pull unevenly. Maki can see the wrist beginning to lock. Mau can identify the instant at which she starts correcting a movement she hasn't yet allowed to finish.

The third attempt lands closer.

The fourth goes farther than the first.

Yuka stands looking at it with her hands on her hips.

Mau waits beside the ground mark.

“Don't say it,” she tells him. ʸ

“I have many excellent things to say.” ᴹ

“Later.” ʸ

He folds his hands and looks exceptionally patient.

She laughs, annoyed with him and grateful for it at once.

Yuta gets up to retrieve the cloth.

“Try finishing the release before deciding where the next one begins,” he says. ʸᵗ

“That's a lot of trust in a piece of fabric.” ʸ

“It hasn't betrayed us yet.” ʸᵗ

Maki glances at the distant cloth.

“Questionable.” ᴹᵃᵏ

* * *

Gojo arrives while they are taking a break.

He asks what they are working on, listens to Yuka's answer and has her show him once before offering an opinion. Mau moves out of the way when she asks for the space, taking his own advice with the air of a man making a significant concession.

The next release is better.

Gojo watches the beginning instead of following the cloth.

“You're trying to hold every part equally,” he says. ᴳ

Yuka looks at the small distortion still visible near her hand.

“It changes when I don't.” ʸ

“Some change is the movement. You only need to stop the parts which make it stop being yours.” ᴳ

She frowns, considering it.

Mau looks at him across her shoulder. There is a useful idea there for more than the cloth. Ciel keeps the actual explanation with the exercise rather than letting it become an instruction to change everything inside him at once.

Yuka tries again.

This time she lets the fold relax a little while keeping the point she cares about steady. The release isn't perfect. The cloth lands close enough that she turns toward Maki before anyone has said a word.

Maki nods.

“That one.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuka smiles.

Mau likes the smile enough to want to keep looking at it even after she has gone to fetch the cloth herself.

* * *

They encounter Momo on the way back.

She is with Okarun, trying to move a container whose handle has come loose on one side. Okarun has the bottom. Momo is holding the broken handle up with one hand while something neither of her ordinary hands is doing keeps the contents from tipping.

Mau stops.

Momo looks at him.

“What?” ᴹᵒᵃ

“Could you keep doing that for a second?” ᴹ

“He's getting heavy,” Okarun says from beneath the container. ᴼᵏ

“The box?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“The situation.” ᴼᵏ

Mau steps forward to help them put it down before asking a more useful version of his question.

“That grip. The part holding the inside.” ᴹ

Momo looks at the container, then back at him.

“You want to see it?” ᴹᵒᵃ

“If you're all right showing me.” ᴹ

She considers the space, sees Yuka waiting too and points toward the clear patch beside the path.

“Use something less likely to spill.” ᴹᵒᵃ

* * *

The thing she chooses is an empty wooden cup.

Momo lifts it, turns it and lets it sit against the support which has no visible fingers around its sides. Mau knows enough to recognize the pattern he has reached for before. Seeing her use it calmly makes the old emergency version feel rougher than he remembered.

“You're pushing from too far away when you think about it like that,” she says after he tries to describe his version. ᴹᵒᵃ

“I can hold something with Authority. This feels different.” ᴹ

“It is different.” ᴹᵒᵃ

She moves the cup toward herself, then stops.

“If you use it like a hand and only think about squeezing, you're going to squeeze.” ᴹᵒᵃ

Mau looks at the little container.

“I mostly remember catching somebody.” ᴹ

Momo's expression softens a little.

“Then try something you don't have to catch.” ᴹᵒᵃ

He sets the cup on the ground.

The old route answers when he reaches for it. For a moment the sensation is so familiar that he nearly supplies the same force he remembers using over the abyss. Ciel names the increase before he commits to it.

He reduces it.

The cup shifts, lifts unevenly and comes back down with a little click.

Yuka crouches beside it.

“You had it.” ʸ

“The wrong bit,” he says. ᴹ

“Still counts as having it.” ʸ

Momo has him try once more with the support distributed beneath the cup rather than tightened around it. It rises a short distance and stays long enough for him to turn it slightly.

The pressure behind his eyes begins before he has made a full rotation.

He puts it down.

Ciel confirms the strain belongs to that old route, not the ordinary grip he used to help with the box. It is smaller than the crisis he remembers, but it is there.

Momo looks at his face.

“Enough?” ᴹᵒᵃ

“For now.” ᴹ

She nods, takes the cup and puts it back where they found it.

* * *

On the walk home, Yuka catches his hand.

“You remembered something,” she says. ʸ

“Several things.” ᴹ

“A useful one?” ʸ

He considers the uneven lift, the old pressure and how many ordinary things he already has which could make the same task easier.

“I think so. I don't understand why I keep putting more force through it than it needs.” ᴹ

“You told me that this morning.” ʸ

He looks at her.

She smiles and presses her shoulder against his.

“Now we both get to practice.” ʸ

At the courtyard they find Coco, Agott and Richeh looking at a sheet which keeps lifting in the wind. Yuka puts a clean stone on one corner before it can escape. Mau holds the other while Coco finishes the line she was drawing.

No technique is necessary.

He waits until she lifts the pen.
