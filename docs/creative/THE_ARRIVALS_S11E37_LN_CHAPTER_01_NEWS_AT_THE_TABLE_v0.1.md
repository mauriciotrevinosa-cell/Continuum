# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 8 — News at the Table

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S11E37
**Continuity state:** B+197 afternoon/evening. Mau/Rimuru/Fern/Jinshi consider actual incoming correspondence and a future Luminous/Hinata visit, not a treaty already signed. Existing shadow watch remains, no new guard roles or soldiers promised. Anko writes her own reply. Household evening and MY shared time remain real. No covert construction order yet.

*When: Fern asks what they are offering, Rimuru has only got as far as where to put the visitors.*

“A meeting,” he says. ᴿ

“About what we might offer.” ᶠᵉ

“Yes.” ᴿ

“Then we should know which parts we can discuss and which parts belong to somebody who isn't at this table.” ᶠᵉ

Mau looks at the chairs around it.

“We're becoming alarmingly organized.” ᴹ

Jinshi places the second letter beside the first.

“It will pass.” ᴶⁱ

They have chosen a place near enough to the common room that people can find them, but not the table someone needs to lay for supper. Fern has brought the current stores list. Jinshi has the correspondence. Rimuru has brought a cup and the optimistic belief that the conversation can remain short if nobody remembers to ask him about titles.

Mau has brought the copied wheel diagram because he intends to put it where Senku will find it afterwards.

For a few minutes they discuss the practical visit. How many people. Which rooms are free for guests. Whether there is a date that lets Hinata finish the work in her letter without rushing the journey.

Fern writes the questions rather than answering them from what would be convenient.

“And Luminous?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Rimuru looks at the letter again.

“Interested. Not confirmed.” ᴿ

“Does that mean interested in visiting, or interested in what she thinks we're doing?” ᴶⁱ

“I'd assume both.” ᴿ

Mau nods.

That seems a fair beginning.

* * *

The road is where the conversation becomes less tidy.

There are things Hollis can sell, things the house can make reliably and things people ask for because they have heard that somebody here can do almost anything. Those are different lists. Fern has kept them separate.

Mau recognizes one request on the last list.

“That isn't a product.” ᴹ

“I wrote that back,” Jinshi says. ᴶⁱ

“What did they say?” ᴹ

“They asked whether we could make it one.” ᴶⁱ

Rimuru leans back.

“At least they're consistent.” ᴿ

They talk about what the house actually wants from the road. Reliable passage for Hollis. Information which arrives with enough context to be useful. People who know where to leave a message instead of walking toward the perimeter because they assume a familiar name makes it all right.

The existing watch has made ordinary work possible. It has not taught every person outside what a silent soldier means.

Fern asks whether the visitors can explain how their own routes are managed before anyone proposes a single solution for all of them.

“That's worth asking,” Mau says. ᴹ

“It might save us promising help they don't need,” Jinshi adds. ᴶⁱ

“Or missing what they do,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

They leave room for both on the page.

* * *

Anko comes over with her reply folded but not sealed.

She looks at the papers, decides which conversation she has reached and waits until Fern has finished writing.

“If you're sending a packet back, this can go with it.” ᴬⁿ

Jinshi offers a place beside the incoming letters.

“Whenever you're ready.” ᴶⁱ

She keeps it for another moment.

“I asked which dates would actually give her time to visit. Or whether it would be easier if I went.” ᴬⁿ

Mau looks up at her.

“Would you like to?” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴬⁿ

There is no elaborate explanation attached.

Rimuru nods.

“Then we'll keep the two plans separate until she answers. Nobody has to turn your visit into our whole meeting.” ᴿ

Anko's shoulders ease a little.

“Thanks.” ᴬⁿ

She puts the letter down. Jinshi doesn't turn it to read the address until she has moved away.

Fern draws a line between the two lists.

Mau watches the small motion. Several people can be travelling toward the same place for reasons which belong to them. It is an ordinary distinction, useful enough that he wonders how often he has made it harder than it needs to be.

* * *

They stop when the first supper things begin moving past the doorway.

There is more work on the page, but none of it becomes clearer merely because they are hungry. Fern takes the stores list. Jinshi gathers the correspondence. Rimuru promises to ask the people in Tempest the questions he actually agreed to ask, rather than the three additional things Mau thinks of while they stand.

“Tomorrow,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“I hadn't even finished.” ᴹ

“I know how that works.” ᴿ

Mau laughs and takes the wheel diagram to Senku.

Senku looks at it, listens to the short account of the repair and points to the first fitting Mau tried.

“Too short.” ˢᵏ

Mau looks toward the ceiling.

“We did establish that.” ᴹ

“Good. Saves time.” ˢᵏ

* * *

Bocchi is playing when Mau reaches the common room.

Quietly enough that people can keep speaking around her. Kita sits nearby with the page they have been discussing. Neither has arranged a performance. The music stops when Bocchi catches a part she wants to try again.

Mau takes the seat they leave clear.

“New?” he asks. ᴹ

Bocchi nods, looking at the strings.

“Part of it.” ᴮ

“Can I listen?” ᴹ

She glances at Kita, who moves the page where they can both see it.

“Yes.” ᴮ

He listens through the attempt, the little mistake and the second pass. When she stops, he tells her which part made him want to hear it again. He doesn't ask whether she can play it for everyone at the next important event.

Bocchi tries the phrase once more.

Yuka finds him there and sits beside him until the food is ready.

* * *

At home later, she asks whether the cloth will do the same thing tomorrow.

Mau lies on his side, facing her.

“Probably something worse.” ᴹ

“Excellent teacher.” ʸ

“Honest forecast.” ᴹ

She nudges his foot beneath the blanket.

“I'm trying it again.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

“Will you watch?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Her expression softens before she leans closer to kiss him.

He holds her at the waist when she settles against him. The day's conversations are still in his head: hands and folds, routes and letters, the work waiting behind the things he knows how to do. For a little while he lets them remain unfinished.

Yuka rests her hand against his chest.

“Good day?” she asks. ʸ

“Good day.” ᴹ

He means that too.
