# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 9 — What Forbidden Meant

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S11E37
**Continuity state:** B+197 late night. Mau privately authorizes Noise to resume building including forbidden, hidden from everyone. Analyze was continuous; no fresh scan of persons/memories. Sukuna archive selected for deliberate forthcoming review, not fully read here. First work is an isolated routing/pressure prototype for the existing Momo-hand pattern, not external execution or new stable CT. Concealment is requested, not established as perfect. Ciel not disabled, deprived of feelings or made omniscient.

*When: Noise asks whether he wants the account of the cup, Mau says yes.*

Yuka is asleep beside him.

He can feel the steady weight of her arm against his side, the place her fingers have caught lightly in his shirt. The room is dark. Outside, the ordinary watch has given its acknowledgment. There is no immediate danger for him to mistake for a reason to get up.

He has slept well for several nights now.

That should make the thought easier to put down.

Noise brings the afternoon's observation forward. Momo's demonstration. His own two attempts. The moment when he supplied too much, the correction and the pressure which still followed the smaller hold.

Mau studies the transition.

*I know that isn't the same as Authority,* he says. ᴹ

*Different route,* Noise answers. ᴺ

*I keep trying to make it answer like the grip.* ᴹ

The stored work shifts. There are comparisons waiting which did not become available only because he asked tonight. Every observation has given Noise another way to understand things already held. It has continued making sense of them while the next step stayed closed.

Mau looks beyond the afternoon's small error.

*Show me the list.* ᴹ

* * *

It is longer than the one they sat with after breakfast days ago.

Some entries have become clearer without becoming usable. Others have enough structure for a trial and still lack the permission to start one. There are questions which he can answer, questions which need practice, questions he left alone because the last answer frightened somebody he loves.

His body knows more than it did when he first reached for the invisible hands. His reserves and his control are different. The old failure is still an old failure if all he does is remember how much it hurt.

Noise waits at the work he requested.

Mau thinks of the cup turning above the ground. Of a body falling through empty space. Of another body which he caught in time to keep from dying and still could not bring awake by holding more tightly.

Yuka moves in her sleep.

His attention returns to her at once.

She settles with her cheek closer to his shoulder. He adjusts the blanket with the hand which isn't caught under her and waits until her breathing is even again.

Then he returns to Noise.

*We're building again.* ᴹ

The response is immediate, almost eager, then careful.

*Which?* ᴺ

*All the work we can finish. The things you've understood and kept waiting.* ᴹ

Noise moves the afternoon's comparison aside. Several closed branches become visible behind it.

*Forbidden?* ᴺ

Mau looks at them.

The name was useful when it meant he didn't have to decide again each time a possibility came close. A closed place. A reason to leave something alone until he knew more or somebody was ready to discuss it.

He knows more now.

He also knows what happened while he was being careful.

*Yes.* ᴹ

* * *

Noise does not mistake the word for the whole explanation.

It draws two paths close enough to show where their requirements would interfere. Another waits beyond them, too incomplete to give a useful answer yet.

Mau follows the relation.

*You never stopped analyzing,* he says. ᴹ

*No.* ᴺ

*I know. I'm not asking you to start over. I'm asking you to do something with what we already have.* ᴹ

Noise turns toward the retained work.

*Build.* ᴺ

*Build. Separate first. We work out what can fit in me, what we can combine and what stays behind the walls until it can.* ᴹ

The answer seems to give the outline something to stand on. It begins choosing the first branch, then stops again.

*Tell Ciel?* ᴺ

Mau feels the question land in a place the diagrams cannot make simple.

Ciel would understand the work. She might understand the reason too. She would ask which parts he intended to change, what he expected to gain and why he needed the answer tonight.

He can imagine himself explaining the whole thing until it becomes a smaller thing they can try safely with everybody ready.

He can imagine how long he will keep finding another reason to make it smaller.

*Not yet.* ᴹ

Noise remains turned toward him.

*DIO?* ᴺ

*No.* ᴹ

Another pause.

*Yuka?* ᴺ

He looks at the woman asleep beside him.

Her hand is still on him. The faint mark beneath her collar is hidden by the fabric he has helped her take off and put on without managing to stop noticing the place it lies.

*Keep it between us.* ᴹ

* * *

Noise looks at the ordinary paths through which changes become visible.

It doesn't promise that nobody can ever notice. Ciel can compare his condition. Shoko can ask why something is different. The people who train with him will have eyes, memories and questions of their own.

Mau watches the same difficulties.

*Don't let the work show before we've understood what we're doing. No experiments in the middle of breakfast. No reaching for everything because it's there.* ᴹ

*Hidden.* ᴺ

*From everyone.* ᴹ

The quiet after the words is different from an alarm. Nothing has broken in his body. Nobody has said a name which means he is about to lose consciousness.

He has made a decision.

Noise begins with the hand pattern because that is the one they have actually examined today. There is a point where Mau's intention becomes more pressure than the small task requires. Noise can separate a possible regulating path from the rest and construct it without sending the pattern out through his body yet.

The first arrangement catches too late.

Mau watches the stored example pass the point where the pressure began to climb.

*Before that,* he says. ᴹ

Noise changes the relation. The second model holds the selected sample below the old oversupply, then loses the shape which made it a hand in the first place.

They keep that failure too.

On the third attempt the isolated pattern remains coherent through the small interval they are testing.

It is not a result he can carry into a fight. It is something they can now test instead of another possibility kept outside the work.

Mau feels the relief with an intensity he hadn't expected from something so small.

*Keep going.* ᴹ

* * *

Before closing the comparison, he brings one more archive onto the work list.

Sukuna.

He has left it closed by choice. The choice has been real each day he kept it there. Opening it will not mean those days were wasted or that the knowledge has been doing his thinking for him all along.

There are answers in it he hasn't allowed himself to sit with. Ways of arranging a movement, recognizing an opening, understanding what another technique can do before deciding how to meet it. Memories of old opponents, not every technique made his merely because somebody else once survived it.

He will have to read it.

He will have to choose what he can actually use.

*That one too,* he tells Noise. ᴹ

*Now?* ᴺ

He looks at the time and at Yuka's hand.

*When I can give it the time. Keep it ready. We finish this first.* ᴹ

Noise holds the new work apart from the archive. It has learned enough about unfinished things to know they are not all the same kind of unfinished.

Mau closes the comparison.

Yuka stirs when he shifts onto his side. He moves closer, puts his arm where she can settle against it and feels her relax without waking fully.

“Here?” she murmurs. ʸ

“Here.” ᴹ

She finds his hand beneath the blanket.

He stays with her.

The first little construction remains where only he and Noise have chosen to keep it.
