# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 10 — The Smaller Answer

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S11E38
**Continuity state:** B+198 morning. Private first external tests of the regulator constructed V68#9 for inherited psychic hands. One overshoot aborted; revised approach holds a small cup briefly with reduced strain. Repeatable habitual use not established. Existing reserves/walls still checked, no new CT, no power stolen from Momo today. Mau records the result privately; Ciel not blind to physiology and perfect concealment not proven. Yuka's own practice continues.

*When: Mau brings the cup out, Noise wants to begin with the version which worked.*

*The old one first,* he answers. ᴹ

The practice space is empty. He has chosen a low wall, a patch of sound ground and an object whose fall cannot turn a small mistake into somebody else's injury. There is nothing here which requires a dagger.

Yuka knows he wanted to review yesterday's exercise before joining her. She is finishing breakfast with Mai. He has promised to come find her when he is done.

He puts the cup on the wall.

The first reach gives him almost exactly what he remembers: a shape he understands unevenly, answering with more force than the task deserves. The cup lifts, tilts and begins slipping from the place he thought he was holding.

He puts it down before the pressure behind his eyes can become pain.

Noise keeps the attempt beside yesterday's.

There is no need to call the failure a surprise. The point is to see what is actually different when they change the route.

*Now,* Mau says. ᴹ

* * *

The little regulator is not another reserve.

It does not make the old pattern belong to a different power simply because one source would be easier to supply. It sits at the place where his intention becomes a demand, keeping him from sending a larger answer through before he has established what the smaller one needs to hold.

Noise brings the constructed relation into that point.

Mau reaches again.

For a moment nothing moves.

He almost pushes harder.

The cup rises just as he begins to make that decision. He gives too much, feels the support tighten and has to lower it immediately.

He stands looking at it.

*I didn't wait.* ᴹ

*No,* Noise says. ᴺ

*Again. Same amount.* ᴹ

This time he lets the interval happen.

The cup rises a little above the wall. It is not a graceful movement; one side lifts before the other, and Mau corrects it more slowly than he would with Authority. He keeps it there for a short count, turns it a fraction and sets it down.

The pressure is smaller.

Still present. Smaller.

He looks at his own hand as though he expects to find some visible account of the difference there.

*Again?* Noise asks. ᴺ

Mau smiles before he answers.

*After we check.* ᴹ

* * *

His pulse has changed. The supply has changed. None of those facts has become invisible because he asked to keep the work private.

The dangerous part would be pretending a small success makes the readings irrelevant.

He examines the strain with Noise, gives the old route time to settle and makes one more brief attempt. The cup comes up straighter. The correction still takes thought. When he begins to lose the shape, he sets it down instead of buying a few more seconds with a surge.

Three tries with the new relation. One overshoot, two short holds.

There is enough there to practice.

Mau closes the operation and leaves the regulator inactive. He can still lift the cup with his ordinary hand. He does so now, turns it to check the rim and finds no crack.

*It helped,* Noise says. ᴺ

*It did.* ᴹ

The answer is very simple.

For all the questions he could imagine being asked about the work, this one has an object in front of it and a difference he can feel in his own body. Yesterday he had stopped because the route hurt. Today he has found somewhere to begin changing that.

He puts the cup away.

*Keep the failures with it,* he tells Noise. *I don't want the good attempt turning into the whole story.* ᴹ

Noise keeps them.

* * *

Yuka has already reached the yard when he comes to find her.

She is talking with Maki, holding the little piece of cloth they used yesterday. Mai sits on the wall nearby, examining the tool Ori brought her while listening to their disagreement about where the mark should go.

“We started without you,” Yuka says. ʸ

“A dangerous precedent.” ᴹ

“You can appeal after this attempt.” ʸ

He takes his place when she points to it.

The first release is close. The second is worse, and she notices the wrist before Maki has to mention it. She shakes her hand out, laughs at herself and begins again.

Mau watches the point she asked him to watch.

“That was later,” he says after the third. ᴹ

“The bad part?” ʸ

“The part you corrected too soon yesterday.” ᴹ

She looks at the cloth on the ground, then back at him.

“I felt it.” ʸ

“Good?” ᴹ

“Weird.” ʸ

“Also an answer.” ᴹ

Mai looks up from the tool.

“The two of you make learning sound very reassuring.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka tosses the cloth at Mau. He catches it with his ordinary hand, smiling.

* * *

They stop while Yuka still wants another attempt.

Maki has made that part of the exercise too. Keeping the good result useful is easier if they do not insist on finishing the day with a tired mistake and a bruised wrist.

Yuka complains, then asks whether they can come back later in the week.

“Yes,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

Mau folds the cloth and gives it to her.

She takes his hand with the other one.

“You look pleased,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her face.

“You did well.” ᴹ

“So did yesterday's me. I still looked like I wanted to throw you into the sky.” ʸ

“You did throw something at me.” ᴹ

She laughs and rests against his arm as they walk.

He likes being able to hold her without thinking about the new operation at all. It is a small thing he has improved, kept where it can stay until he needs it. Their morning has room for both.

Inside, Noise brings the next question close enough for him to notice.

Mau leaves it waiting until he has taken Yuka to the table.
