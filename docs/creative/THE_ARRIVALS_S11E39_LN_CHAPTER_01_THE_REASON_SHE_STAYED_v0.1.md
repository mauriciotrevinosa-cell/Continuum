# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 12 — The Reason She Stayed

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S11E39
**Continuity state:** B+198 evening. Rem and Umaru choose an evening together after ordinary household work; existing closeness develops through a kiss without declaring a new first or compulsory relationship label. Ram/Seika retain their own evening. Mau/Yuka remain affectionate. Marin proposes a future shared cooking activity, not an event already completed. No new resident, power, guard or time jump.

*When: Umaru comes to find Rem, she is carrying a plate and an excuse.*

“I brought this back,” she says. ᵁ

Rem looks at the plate.

It belongs to the common kitchen. Umaru could have returned it through the open doorway on her left. Instead she has crossed most of the room to present it to the one person who has just finished washing her hands.

“Thank you.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru holds on a moment too long.

Rem takes it before it becomes a contest neither of them knows the rules of.

“Are you busy?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

“I was going to put this away.” ᴿᵉ

“After that.” ᵁ

Rem sets it on the rack behind her without turning around.

“No.” ᴿᵉ

That leaves Umaru with considerably less preparation time than she expected.

* * *

They go outside with something warm to drink.

Rem has a loose cardigan over her clothes. One sleeve keeps slipping down her hand; Umaru watches her push it back three times before offering to hold the cup while she fixes it properly.

“You don't have to rescue me from a sleeve.” ᴿᵉ

“I thought I'd start with something I could beat.” ᵁ

Rem laughs and gives her the cup.

They sit where they can hear the common room through the open doors. Someone inside objects to the amount of seasoning in a bowl. Someone else points out that the objection came after a second helping. There is a short silence, then Gojo says he is capable of evaluating a problem thoroughly.

Umaru settles close enough for their legs to touch.

For a while, that is the evening. No expedition to plan. No person either of them has to become before the next meal. Rem can hear work being finished behind her and remain seated.

Umaru notices the moment she almost gets up.

“Is something wrong?” ᵁ

“No. I thought I heard a tray.” ᴿᵉ

“There are people in there with hands.” ᵁ

“Some of them are Gojo.” ᴿᵉ

“A serious limitation.” ᵁ

Rem puts her cup between both palms. Umaru watches the decision settle in her shoulders as she leans back again.

“You can want to help,” Umaru says. “I just wanted you out here with me.” ᵁ

“I know.” ᴿᵉ

“Not because you looked tired. Or because I thought somebody should make you take a break.” ᵁ

Rem turns toward her.

Umaru studies the cup she has been given to hold. It has become a useful thing to look at while saying something she cannot disguise as Fort business.

“I wanted to be with you,” she says. ᵁ

* * *

The answer doesn't arrive quickly enough for Umaru's courage to remain entirely comfortable.

She glances up.

Rem is looking at her with an expression which makes her wish, briefly, that she had been more articulate. Then Rem reaches for her free hand.

“I used to think I knew what being wanted felt like,” she says. ᴿᵉ

Umaru's fingers close around hers.

“And sometimes I did. My sister loves me. That was real before any of this.” ᴿᵉ

“Of course.” ᵁ

“But there were things I mistook for the only way someone could want me. Being needed. Having the right answer. Being the person who would stay, whatever was said to her.” ᴿᵉ

Her thumb moves over Umaru's knuckles.

“Here, sometimes I put a cup down and nothing terrible happens because I didn't carry the next one.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru doesn't make a joke.

Rem looks toward the doors. Inside, Mai is reaching past Mau for something on a shelf while he argues that if she wanted somebody taller, there are people available who have not already been insulted twice during dinner.

“He left a place for me,” Rem says. “And let me decide whether I wanted it. He didn't ask me to earn it while I was thinking.” ᴿᵉ

She turns back.

“Then you kept finding reasons to come get me.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru lifts the empty plate in her imagination and feels her face grow warm.

“Not all of them were very good.” ᵁ

“I liked them.” ᴿᵉ

“The Fort genuinely needed you.” ᵁ

“I know.” ᴿᵉ

“I also genuinely needed you.” ᵁ

Rem's smile changes.

Umaru realizes what she has said and tries to correct it before the correction becomes another mistake.

“Not needed like— I mean, I could still do things. I wasn't asking you to—” ᵁ

“Umaru.” ᴿᵉ

She stops.

Rem squeezes her hand.

“I understood you.” ᴿᵉ

* * *

The cups go on the step beside them.

Umaru has no memory of deciding which one of them moved first. She remembers Rem looking at her mouth, then looking back up as though offering her time to choose.

She chooses badly in one small, practical respect.

Their noses touch.

Both pull back.

Umaru makes a sound which would have been easier to survive if it had stayed inside her head.

Rem laughs softly, and the sound takes the fear out of the mistake.

“Again?” she asks. ᴿᵉ

“Please.” ᵁ

This time Rem turns her head a little.

The kiss is short. Umaru's hand tightens before she remembers it is already holding something precious. When they separate, she finds Rem still close and smiling with a shyness she has rarely been allowed to see.

“Was that—” Umaru begins. ᵁ

“Yes.” ᴿᵉ

“I hadn't asked the question.” ᵁ

“You can still ask it.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru takes a breath.

“Was that okay?” ᵁ

Rem leans her forehead against hers.

“Very.” ᴿᵉ

* * *

Ram comes out with Seika several minutes later.

She sees the cups, the joined hands and her sister's expression. Her gaze passes over Umaru with enough precision that Umaru sits straighter without knowing whether she has committed an offense.

“Don't leave those out overnight,” Ram says. ᴿᵃ

Rem smiles.

“We won't.” ᴿᵉ

Seika is carrying something folded under one arm. Ram takes it from her, says something too quiet for the others to hear and starts down the path beside her.

Seika answers. Ram's shoulder brushes hers, then stays there.

Umaru waits until they are farther away.

“Am I going to die?” ᵁ

“Not tonight.” ᴿᵉ

“That's a narrow promise.” ᵁ

Rem rests against her shoulder.

“I like tonight.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru forgets the rest of the question.

* * *

When they finally bring the cups inside, Marin is standing near the table with an idea she has already decided is going to be fun.

“Together,” she says, pointing between two imaginary work spaces. “Everybody makes the same thing. Then we see what happens.” ᴹᵃʳ

Wakana looks as though he has begun calculating how many people might ask him whether they are holding a knife correctly.

“A class?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“A dinner. A class. A date, if you want. It's cooking. We can make it all three.” ᴹᵃʳ

“If it's a date, can I make him do the washing?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Mau looks at her.

“I'm right here.” ᴹ

“I know. That's why I'm asking in advance.” ʸ

“An inspiring use of marriage.” ᴹ

She takes his hand under the table and smiles at Marin.

“We're interested.” ʸ

Umaru looks at Rem.

Rem doesn't wait for an empty plate this time.

“We could do it,” she says. ᴿᵉ

“Yes,” Umaru answers, rather quickly. ᵁ

Across the table, Mau catches the look between them and very deliberately returns his attention to his wife.

He has been trusted with enough things to recognize one he doesn't have to ask about tonight.
