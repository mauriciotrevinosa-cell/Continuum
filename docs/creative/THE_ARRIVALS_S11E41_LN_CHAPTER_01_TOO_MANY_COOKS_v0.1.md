## Chapter 16 — Too Many Cooks

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1

*When: Marin asks whether everyone is ready, the courtyard produces six different kinds of yes.*

One is confident. One is worried. One belongs to Gojo and is loud enough that Shoko immediately takes the knife away from him.

“I haven't done anything,” he says. ᴳ

“We're doing prevention today.” ˢʰ

Mai laughs so hard that the flour on her hand becomes a problem for Momo's sleeve.

The tables have been given the extra space. Water and clean cloths sit within reach. The hot pans are at the cooking end, where the people actually using them can see one another move. Wakana has demonstrated the folded bread once already, slowly, and put the finished example beside the ingredients instead of eating the evidence.

Mau is looking at it.

Yuka moves it farther away.

“You can have yours when we make it.” ʸ

“That one already exists.” ᴹ

“So does the task.” ʸ

Marin claps her hands.

“All right. First, dough.” ᴹᵃʳ

* * *

Ori and Chloe have their own place near the end, with Frieren and Himmel working beside them.

It was Ori who asked whether they could stand there. Frieren had said yes before Himmel could ask if she'd like his help, and he has spent the minutes since trying not to look too happy about being handed a bowl.

“Are you going to stir it?” she asks him now. ᶠ

“I was waiting for the next instruction.” ᴴ

“Stir it.” ᶠ

He does.

Ori leans toward Chloe.

“We should be faster than them.” ᴼ

“It isn't a race,” Frieren says. ᶠ

“I didn't say a race.” ᴼ

Chloe presses both hands into the mixture and discovers why Wakana had suggested adding the water gradually.

She looks at the dough attached to her fingers. Then at Ori.

“Don't add more.” ᶜʰ

Ori puts the cup down immediately.

Himmel brings the flour closer without making a speech about helping.

* * *

Mau's first job is measuring.

Yuka gives him the cup, points to the mark and watches him look from it to the bowl as though choosing whether this is a task which deserves his eyes.

“Normally,” she says. ʸ

“My eyes are normally in my face.” ᴹ

“Mau.” ʸ

He measures to the mark.

She is right to stop him. He knows it as soon as he begins mixing. The small uncertainty is part of doing this with her. She touches the dough, decides it needs a little more water and lets him add it while she keeps working.

No calculation makes her fingers less worth watching.

“You've stopped,” she says. ʸ

“I'm supervising.” ᴹ

“You're staring.” ʸ

“I'm married.” ᴹ

She tries to keep a straight face and loses.

He leans in to kiss the corner of her mouth.

“Do that again when my hands aren't covered,” she tells him. ʸ

“A threat?” ᴹ

“A plan.” ʸ

He gives her the water.

* * *

Across the courtyard, Rem is trying very hard not to fix Umaru's work.

Umaru can tell.

She flattens the little disc, looks at its shape and moves the edge which has decided to become part of the table.

Rem's fingers lift.

Then lower.

Umaru turns toward her.

“You can say it.” ᵁ

“A little thicker there,” Rem admits. ᴿᵉ

“Show me?” ᵁ

Rem puts a hand beside hers rather than taking the work away. They press the edge out together. Umaru gets flour on the back of Rem's finger and wipes it with her thumb before remembering the task which made the touch necessary.

Rem looks up.

The dough waits.

At the next table Ram says, without looking over, that somebody ought to tell them food doesn't become more edible if they stare at each other for long enough.

Seika looks at the piece in Ram's own hands.

“Is that why yours is folded like that?” ˢᵉ

Ram examines it.

It has a hole.

Seika turns away just before laughing. Ram considers the damaged bread, the woman beside her and the possibility of pretending none of this happened.

Then she picks up another piece of dough.

“We'll eat that one first.” ᴿᵃ

“We?” ˢᵉ

“I'm not suffering alone.” ᴿᵃ

Seika takes the little ruined piece from her and begins repairing the edge.

* * *

The filling makes the courtyard much louder.

There are bowls to pass, choices to make and Wakana moving between tables to demonstrate how much can fit before the fold refuses to close. Marin has already made one too full. She holds it up as a useful example while Wakana looks at the amount left in the bowl and decides not to ask how much more she had intended to put inside.

Jin-Woo and Hae-In are working a few places down.

He has made a neat row of matching pieces.

She looks at the row, then at him.

“You measured them.” ᴴᶦ

“Approximately.” ᴶ

“All of them.” ᴴᶦ

He doesn't answer quickly enough.

Hae-In adds a little more filling to one and closes it in a shape which is plainly not the same as its neighbors.

“There,” she says. ᴴᶦ

He regards it for a moment.

“That one's yours.” ᴶ

She smiles at him.

“I know.” ᴴᶦ

His mother comes by to see how they're doing, asks why they are making so few and accepts Hae-In's explanation that this is supper, not a supply operation. Jin-Ah hears enough of the exchange to begin laughing before she reaches the table.

Mau catches Jin-Woo's eye across the bowls.

Jin-Woo looks at the flour on Mau's shoulder and wisely offers no criticism.

* * *

Yuka's first fold tears when she tries to move it.

She stops with both hands under the soft shape and makes a face at it.

“I can help,” Mau says. ᴹ

“With your hands?” ʸ

He holds them up.

She lets him take one side. Between them they get it onto a flat board, move the escaped filling back in and press another thin piece across the damaged place.

The result looks as though it has survived a serious disagreement.

Yuka studies it.

“That one's yours.” ʸ

“I see marriage has rules everywhere.” ᴹ

“You offered to help.” ʸ

“And look what it got me.” ᴹ

She touches a clean part of her wrist to his cheek, then kisses him properly, as promised.

For a moment the table, the filling and the badly repaired bread all become somebody else's problem.

Mai clears her throat from the other side.

“If you two are finished, I need that bowl.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka passes it without moving very far away from him.

* * *

At the hot end, Shoko is the one who puts their food in the pan.

Gojo has been allowed the spatula.

He watches the edge brown, lifts it too soon and discovers that being able to describe an operation does not stop oil from making the actual thing difficult to turn.

Shoko catches his wrist before he can try again at the same bad angle.

“Lower,” she says. ˢʰ

“I had it.” ᴳ

“Then have it down here.” ˢʰ

He lowers his hand. She helps him get underneath the bread and lets him complete the turn himself.

The second side lands safely.

He looks far too pleased.

“Excellent teamwork.” ᴳ

“That was supervision.” ˢʰ

“An important part of any relationship.” ᴳ

She tries to take the spatula back. He raises it, leaning away, and she catches the front of his shirt with her free hand.

“Careful,” she says. “There's food here.” ˢʰ

* * *

They eat at the same tables once everything has been moved away from the flour.

Some pieces are beautiful. Some are sealed with the determination of people who had already used all the dough and were not prepared to begin again. Nobody has produced enough disaster to require a replacement meal, though Marin asks Wakana whether he thinks the particularly dark side of hers counts as flavor.

He tries a bite before answering.

She loves him for taking the question seriously enough to check.

Ori and Chloe divide their best piece and put the halves on different plates. Frieren receives one. Mau receives the other.

“You have to say what you think,” Ori tells him. ᴼ

He takes a bite.

The edge is thick. One side has more filling. It is warm enough to make him slow down before swallowing.

He looks at the two waiting faces.

“I'd have another.” ᴹ

Ori's shoulders lift with pleasure.

Chloe points at the remaining plate.

“That one has more filling.” ᶜʰ

“I noticed,” Frieren says. ᶠ

Himmel is laughing into his cup.

* * *

Later, when the plates are mostly empty, Yuka reaches for the piece she assigned to Mau.

He has eaten half and left the rest where they can both find it.

“You don't have to,” he says. ᴹ

“I helped make it.” ʸ

“That isn't usually a defense.” ᴹ

She takes a bite. Chews. Looks at the seam.

“We did all right.” ʸ

“We did.” ᴹ

She rests against his side while he finishes the last piece.

The courtyard empties slowly. Nobody has to turn the good evening into an announcement about how good it was. Rem carries one cup; Umaru carries the other. Ram and Seika leave their damaged example divided between them. Gojo has somehow retained the spatula and is being made to wash it himself.

Mau gets up with Yuka when she says they should help clear their end.

He could take every bowl away in one motion. Instead she hands him one, and he follows her to the water.

Tonight that is what she asked him to do with his hands.
