# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 17 — The Length of a Step

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S11E41
**Continuity state:** B+202 morning. Secret work continues from retained spatial/Diablo-door references, not a newly acquired canon power. Psychic-hand regulator sustains several small-object trials at current low output without escalating pain; reach/load/attention limits remain. Isolated endpoint model transfers a cloth sample across a short gap using Mau's mana. First whole-body Step attempt aborts before transfer when containment cannot follow; no mastered Step, cut through ward, infinite range or double active CT. Noise retains the failure; no Ciel suppression or erased trace.

*When: the cup stops being interesting, Mau makes himself finish the test anyway.*

It has crossed from one mark to another six times. The seventh hold tips when he thinks about the next thing instead of the one he is doing.

He catches it with his real hand.

*Attention,* Noise says. ᴺ

*Apparently still necessary.* ᴹ

There is no building pressure behind his eyes. Not the old, immediate insistence that he has used the wrong reserve at the wrong rate for a task this small. The regulator takes the supply he asks for now, rather than trying to solve a cup with everything the inherited pattern can draw.

That is progress.

He makes a second object move beside the first and finds the limit in a different place. The two holds require more conscious correction. When one drifts, he adjusts both; the object which was behaving begins to tilt.

He lowers them.

*One until I can stop doing that,* he says. ᴹ

Noise keeps the result.

The exercise has become useful enough to be boring. Mau recognizes how quickly he wants to move past that part.

He does two more holds before letting himself ask for the next model.

* * *

The reference comes from a much older construction.

A door which had needed several answers at once. The relationship between places. A hinge which did not behave like an ordinary hinge. The part of Diablo's movement which had made Mau think, years ago and far too recently, that getting across a room should not always require the room's permission about the distance.

He has the memory of seeing it.

Noise has more than a memory. The old construction retains relationships they had not finished putting into a form Mau could use by himself. He had let many of them sit because each new emergency made a different unfinished thing seem more important.

Now Noise brings them forward with the eagerness of someone finally allowed to unpack a box.

*This one,* it says. ᴺ

Mau looks at the two points.

*Not the whole door.* ᴹ

*Step.* ᴺ

*That's the idea. Getting there without turning the entire yard into a project.* ᴹ

The model widens.

He laughs.

*You're negotiating.* ᴹ

*More room.* ᴺ

*Start small.* ᴹ

Noise narrows it again, reluctantly.

* * *

They use a strip of cloth.

Mau sets it over a flat stone. Another stone sits less than an arm's length away. The ground between them is clear; he has no interest in finding out which parts of a living thing can be persuaded to arrive before the others.

The first expression takes power and leaves the cloth where it was.

The second pulls at one end. Mau stops it before the cloth can tear.

*It found the place,* he says. *It didn't take the object.* ᴹ

Noise holds the comparison beside the older door.

Mau understands the mistake only after it shows him how much of the object he has failed to include. He has prepared an answer for the end he can see and allowed the rest to remain ordinary cloth on an ordinary stone.

The operation has done exactly as much as he gave it reason to do.

They begin again.

This time he defines the whole sample before establishing the second point. The supply rises, steadies and falls.

The cloth lies across the other stone.

Mau has not seen it travel between them.

He stares for a moment, then reaches out with his actual hand to make sure he is not looking at a clever expression of cloth which has left the real piece somewhere less convenient.

He turns it over. Finds the same loose thread.

*Again,* he says. ᴹ

* * *

It works twice more within the same prepared distance.

When he moves the receiving stone farther away, the expression fails. He is tempted to put in more power. Noise shows him the relationship which has stopped closing before he can treat mana as an answer to a missing part.

He sits down to work it through.

There are two different problems in front of him now. A way to name the receiving place beyond a distance he has already prepared. A way to make the operation carry something much more complicated than a strip of cloth while preserving every relation it depends on.

His body would be the second problem even if he never tried to go any farther than the next stone.

He thinks of what he carries inside it.

Not passengers waiting on the yard to be transported today. DIO and Ashborn have their own places to spend the morning. But the structures he has made, the working boundaries which let one reserve and one unfinished thing exist beside another without trying to become the whole of him.

He cannot leave those on the first stone.

*All of me,* he tells Noise. ᴹ

*All,* it agrees. ᴺ

* * *

He attempts the body model without releasing it.

Feet on the ground. Receiving point beside the nearer stone. One operation at a time, with no neutral Infinity running to complicate which answer belongs to which construction. A short physical move remains available if he needs to stop and walk away.

The prepared transfer begins to take shape.

For an instant he understands how the old reference makes the distance cease to be the part that matters.

Then something under his ribs pulls in the wrong direction.

*Stop,* he says. ᴹ

Noise closes the attempted release.

Mau's feet have not moved.

He stays still until he can distinguish pain from the expectation of it. The pressure fades. His breathing remains ordinary. Nothing has been severed or left behind. The warning came while the model was still trying to include him.

He sits back down, more carefully this time.

*That boundary,* he says. ᴹ

Noise shows it to him.

The outer transfer would have closed around the visible body. One of the inner containment relationships was still answering to the first position.

He looks at how little disagreement would have been necessary.

*Keep it,* he says. *Don't fix the picture so it looks like what we wanted.* ᴹ

Noise keeps the failure whole.

* * *

By the time he puts the stones back, he has a damp patch under his shirt and no new way to get home.

He walks.

At the path, he pauses to let a pair of his ordinary carriers pass with the timber they were assigned to move. They know where they are going because somebody gave them a destination. They have not been solving every problem in the settlement while he sat in a yard.

One turns its head toward him for the acknowledgment it expects.

He gives it.

Then he looks down at his own hands.

The old cup exercise works better. The cloth has crossed a small distance without crossing the ground between. His body has warned him before the same idea could become a wound.

All three facts belong in the result.

*We can finish it,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau looks toward the roof above the common room.

*Yes,* he answers. *We can.* ᴹ

He has begun to hear that answer as a promise about far more than the next construction.
