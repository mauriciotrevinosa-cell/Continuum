# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 68
## Chapter 18 — A Visit with Two Reasons

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 68
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S11E42
**Continuity state:** B+203 morning. The earlier correspondence becomes a real announced road visit: Luminous/Hinata, with Souei joining from Tempest for the route/security discussion. Arrival checked at ordinary exterior reception, escort/permission into fixed protected area, guest rooms in mini Inn. No civilian teleport network or unannounced entry. Anko/Hinata have private time; official discussion follows rather than consuming their reunion. Luminous notices Mau's containment/scale, not secret meeting contents or omniscient power ledger.

*When: Anko hears that they have reached the outer turn, she finishes buttoning her cuff a second time.*

Nazuna watches from the bench.

“It was done.” ᴺᶻ

“I know.” ᴬⁿ

“Then why—” ᴺᶻ

“Good night.” ᴬⁿ

Nazuna looks toward the bright doorway, then at Ko, who has wisely found something else to examine.

“You'll have to tell me later.” ᴺᶻ

“I'm looking forward to your being asleep.” ᴬⁿ

She goes before the reply can catch up.

* * *

The final confirmation came before the travelers began the last stretch of road. Rooms were set aside in the small guest Inn. Fern has the names, Rimuru has the proposed subjects and Jinshi has asked which part of the party needs to leave first when the work is done.

It is enough preparation that nobody has to turn seeing a familiar face into permission for everyone behind it.

At the reception point Souei waits while the ordinary sentry's report reaches Mau. He could make the distance look less inconvenient. He does not treat an agreed arrival route as something only less capable people are required to use.

Hinata notices Anko before Anko reaches them.

For several steps neither says anything.

Then Anko stops in front of her.

“You came.” ᴬⁿ

“That was the plan.” ᴴⁱ

“You had several other plans.” ᴬⁿ

“I moved some of them.” ᴴⁱ

Luminous looks between them and begins examining the reception arrangements with an interest which is almost certainly exaggerated.

Mau stays near Souei.

He has discovered that the most useful thing he can do for this greeting is not stand in it.

Hinata reaches for Anko's hand.

“Can I put my things down first?” ᴴⁱ

“I was going to offer.” ᴬⁿ

“You were looking at me.” ᴴⁱ

“Several things can be true.” ᴬⁿ

Mau turns away before his expression becomes something either of them will remember to punish later.

* * *

Luminous doesn't need someone to explain that the protected boundary is there.

She feels the distinction as they are admitted. Something settled in the place, with limits which do not shift to flatter the person arriving. The living routines inside it matter as much to her inspection as the force itself: people walking without checking every shadow, a door left open because someone expects to come back through it carrying food.

Then she looks at Mau.

He has learned to keep much more of himself from spilling into the space around him. She can recognize deliberate restraint without mistaking it for emptiness.

“You've been working,” she says. ᴸ

He follows her gaze for half a second before looking back at her.

“Several things.” ᴹ

“That seems to be the local answer.” ᴸ

Yuka comes beside him as they reach the common courtyard. He offers his hand without looking down; she takes it and greets the visitors in her own right.

Luminous sees the difference that small contact makes in his attention.

She doesn't comment on it.

“We have rooms ready,” Yuka tells her. ʸ

“And somewhere to speak afterward?” ᴸ

“Rimuru's pretending he hasn't been arranging it all morning,” Mau says. ᴹ

From the doorway, Rimuru hears enough.

“Somebody had to.” ᴿ

“You live here. You could have asked the responsible people.” ᴹ

“I did. They told me to stop changing where the chairs went.” ᴿ

Luminous looks toward the room.

“Were they right?” ᴸ

Shizue, passing behind him, says yes.

* * *

Hinata has a room of her own.

Anko shows her where the water is, where she can put the things she doesn't want carried into the meeting and which door leads back toward the common room. She manages all the practical information without once mentioning the letter she has read enough times to stop needing the paper.

Hinata sets her bag down.

Then catches Anko's wrist before she can point out another useful feature.

“You're allowed to be pleased,” she says. ᴴⁱ

Anko looks at the hand around her wrist.

“I'm considering it.” ᴬⁿ

“Very carefully.” ᴴⁱ

Anko steps closer.

The kiss answers the question more honestly than the last several sentences. Hinata lets go of her wrist to hold her at the waist. Neither is in a hurry to turn the visit into an item which has been successfully completed.

When Anko draws back, her hand remains on Hinata's shoulder.

“How long before they need you?” she asks. ᴬⁿ

“Not immediately.” ᴴⁱ

“Good.” ᴬⁿ

She closes the door enough to make the room theirs for the moment.

* * *

Outside, Ori is asking Souei whether being able to go somewhere quietly makes every journey more interesting or less.

He considers the question with considerably more care than Mau expects.

“It depends on what I need to notice when I arrive,” Souei says. ˢᵒ

“So you still have to look.” ᴼ

“Yes.” ˢᵒ

Ori nods, satisfied by an answer which leaves an explorer something to do.

Chloe has found Hinata's route on the map and wants to know whether a turn she remembers is still in the same place after the world's changes. Souei shows them which stretch he can account for, then leaves a gap where his own information stops.

Mau watches the empty place on the paper.

It will need somebody to go and see. No amount of power makes a road report into a memory of a place he has never visited.

“Dad,” Ori says, looking up. “Can we keep this out for the meeting?” ᴼ

“Ask Rimuru. It might help.” ᴹ

She takes the map to him with Chloe carrying the other end.

Souei waits until they are out of hearing.

“The ordinary sentries are yours,” he says. ˢᵒ

“Forty assigned here. Muster coordinates that watch. The ones outside don't all speak, if that's what you were checking.” ᴹ

“I was checking how a report reaches the person who can act on it.” ˢᵒ

Mau looks toward the place Ori has just left.

“Then we should put that on the table too.” ᴹ

* * *

They let the guests finish arriving before calling anyone to sit down.

Luminous speaks briefly with Fern about the rooms. Yuka goes to tell Mai how many will stay for the meal. Rimuru is intercepted by Jinshi with a page he actually needs to read.

Anko and Hinata emerge together after enough time that nobody who values their own peace asks whether the directions were difficult.

Mau catches Hinata's eye as she passes.

“Good journey?” he asks. ᴹ

“Good end to it.” ᴴⁱ

He grins.

Anko gives him a look sharp enough to take the grin down by half.

He keeps the surviving half until Yuka comes back and asks what he's done.

“Diplomacy,” he says. ᴹ

“That bad?” ʸ

She takes his hand and leads him toward the room before he can improve the situation further.
