# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 69
## Chapter 3 — Another Cup

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 69
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S11E44
**Continuity state:** B+205 afternoon. Luminous attends an ordinary physical tea with consenting witches, not entry to Mau's inner guest space or automatic inspection of anyone. Request for occasional return stays subject to invitations/schedules. No romantic interest in Mau or new construction. Side chapter intentionally compact; Minerva/Daphne/Sekhmet/Carmilla/Echidna have individual voices.

*When: Luminous asks which chair she should take, Sekhmet points at one she won't have to move for.*

“That one.” ˢᵉᵏ

Echidna smiles.

“A warm welcome.” ᴱᶜ

“It would be warmer if you stopped standing where I need to put my feet.” ˢᵉᵏ

Luminous takes the offered chair. Mau, who has made the introductions, discovers there is another place waiting for him.

“I thought I was bringing a guest.” ᴹ

“You did,” Echidna says. “Now you may sit down.” ᴱᶜ

Daphne wants to know whether the visit includes trying the breads left from yesterday's cooking. Minerva tells her they are not left over if somebody deliberately put them aside before Mau ate everything within reach.

“An ugly accusation,” he says. ᴹ

“An accurate intervention.” ᴹⁱ

Luminous accepts tea and examines the room with the interest of someone who has heard about these people from accounts which never quite agree about which one should be the most alarming.

Carmilla asks whether it was a difficult journey.

Luminous turns toward the quiet question instead of making her repeat it for an audience.

“Longer than it used to be,” she answers. ᴸ

That proves a much better beginning than asking which power belongs to which fear.

* * *

They talk about roads for a little while, then about the inconvenience of having people discover a talent and immediately decide how it ought to be employed.

“He knows about that,” Minerva says, looking at Mau. ᴹⁱ

“I was going to let you have a conversation which wasn't about me.” ᴹ

“A worthy ambition,” Echidna says. ᴱᶜ

Luminous sets her cup down.

“I was interested in the people who agreed to live near him.” ᴸ

“We have interests of our own,” Echidna says. ᴱᶜ

“That's what I hoped.” ᴸ

The answer earns her a longer look.

Daphne describes the growing ground with such enthusiasm that three parts of the account turn into things she would like to eat. Sekhmet objects to the proposed distance between a good chair and the nearest useful shade. Carmilla speaks about a quiet hour which is hers, and Luminous asks what she enjoys doing with it.

Mau drinks his tea while the conversation goes somewhere he has not chosen for it.

He likes that more than he has expected to.

* * *

The questions become sharper by the second cup.

Echidna wants to know how a ruler distinguishes a person who has chosen to serve from one who has merely never been offered a practical way to leave. Luminous asks whether curiosity has ever made Echidna consider an answer more important than the person giving it.

Mau begins reaching for his cup.

Both women look at him.

He stops.

“I am staying hydrated.” ᴹ

Minerva laughs.

The argument does not become a fight. That seems to interest Luminous as much as any answer. She can ask a difficult question here and receive irritation, disagreement or another question without the room deciding that one of them must own the other by the time the cups are empty.

Mau hears Makima say relief again.

He looks down at his tea.

* * *

Before leaving, Luminous asks whether an occasional return would be welcome.

“If the dates suit,” Echidna says. ᴱᶜ

“Every other week?” ᴸ

“A proposal, not a conquest. We can discuss it.” ᴱᶜ

“I have been remarkably restrained.” ᴸ

“So far.” ᴱᶜ

Sekhmet closes her eyes.

“Not mornings.” ˢᵉᵏ

They agree to exchange possible dates instead of pretending the world has already become convenient enough to supply them automatically.

At the doorway, Luminous looks back at Mau.

“You were very quiet.” ᴸ

“I was learning.” ᴹ

“Anything useful?” ᴸ

He thinks of the question Echidna asked. The one Luminous gave back. The way neither woman tried to turn a satisfactory answer into permission for the next demand.

“I hope so.” ᴹ

She leaves him with that answer.
