# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 69
## Chapter 6 — Where the Force Begins

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 69
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S11E46
**Continuity state:** B+206 afternoon, continued visit. Original local Stampede Devil, not a canon identity/power tier. Joint inspection/fight explicitly chosen, not Makima ordering a post-vow attack against Mau/Reze. Mau coordinates existing Authority, neutral and sequential Dismantle; no simultaneous active CT. Reze acts by choice with a bounded local explosive attack. Makima demonstrates her local force emission; Noise observes an incomplete operation, not instant Bang/Control acquisition. Devil destroyed, no Arise. Reze's freedom from Makima is extended beyond visit by Makima's continuing chosen undertaking; no automatic house arrival or owner transfer. Mau knowingly deepens contact while hiding it.

*When: the thing behind the door hits it again, the upper hinge comes out of the wall.*

Mau catches the panel with Authority before it can fall toward Reze.

She has already moved.

Behind the opening, something which has too many legs to fit its own shoulders throws itself against the remaining frame. The nearest pair of hooves beats the earth without finding enough room to build the charge its body wants.

Makima calls it the Stampede Devil.

The name does not make the smell better.

“Does it understand you?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

“Then ask it to stop before we go any farther.” ᴹ

She does.

The creature's head turns.

For a breath the building goes quiet.

Then it drives its horn through the rest of the frame.

* * *

Mau moves the held door sideways instead of throwing it.

The horn scrapes across the panel. Wood peels away. The creature has expected its own weight to carry it through whatever happens in front of it; when Authority takes that weight off the useful line, its legs continue the charge without its shoulders.

It falls sideways through the ruined opening.

Reze is already beyond the wall.

“I can get underneath,” she says. ᴿᶻ

“Wait for it to turn,” Mau answers. ᴹ

It turns toward her voice.

The ground shakes.

It is not only the body making the sound. A rhythm is spreading outward from the hooves, each impact finding a new place for the next one to strike. Broken pieces of stone jump as though another herd is arriving from below the ground.

Mau draws his hold back toward himself and finds the pressure changing beneath it. The force he caught has become several different movements, none willing to stay where he first put the weight.

*The ground,* Noise says. ᴺ

*I see it.* ᴹ

He lifts the nearest falling stone with Authority, places it beyond Reze's path and takes one ordinary step out of the line the hooves are making.

The next impact reaches Infinity and stops short of his body.

The stone under his foot still breaks.

He jumps before the split can take his balance.

* * *

Makima raises a hand.

Mau sees the beginning of the emission with much more attention than he gave her hand the day she first pulled him into a gate.

“Bang,” she says. ᴹᵏ

The creature's flank opens.

Blood hits the damaged wall. The force drives through more than the surface it struck, upsetting the rhythm long enough for the borrowed thunder beneath the ground to falter.

It is a real opportunity.

Mau doesn't waste it trying to finish understanding what made it.

He closes his neutral technique, releases two Dismantles along the joints he can actually see and reestablishes the neutral only after the release has left him. Authority keeps the broken door out of the place Reze needs to cross.

The cuts reach the front legs.

One parts cleanly. The second doesn't; the creature has begun turning the hard outer surface toward him.

Reze takes the opening beneath it.

* * *

The blast is small compared with what Mau could imagine from the explanation of her body.

It is exactly where she wanted it.

The broken foreleg folds inward. A section of the chest gives way. The creature tries to carry its charge forward on limbs which no longer agree where the ground is, and Mau pulls its falling shoulder away from Reze before it can turn her exit into a collision.

She lands hard, rolls and gets up without looking to Makima for the next order.

For a moment her attention catches on Mau's hand still raised toward the creature.

“It isn't holding you,” he says. ᴹ

“I noticed.” ᴿᶻ

She moves past him.

The thing tries to rise again.

Mau lets go of the door.

It falls against the torn frame with a crash. He closes neutral, puts the next cut through the exposed part of the neck and keeps his attention on the impact beneath his feet until he is sure the rhythm has ended with the body.

No triumphant voice announces a level.

There is a ruined building, a dead creature and three people standing in the quiet they have made.

Mau lowers his hand.

* * *

Noise has kept the moment of Makima's emission.

The shape of the beginning is clearer than it was when he only had descriptions. There are relationships it can follow and others hidden behind the way her own power supplies the result.

Mau asks it to keep both kinds marked.

He doesn't have a new attack because he watched somebody use one.

He has something worth examining.

“You were watching,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

He looks at her.

“There was a lot happening.” ᴹ

“Not only the creature.” ᴹᵏ

“No.” ᴹ

She seems pleased by the admission in a way he doesn't find entirely comfortable.

Reze comes back from the far side of the body.

“It isn't getting up,” she says. ᴿᶻ

Mau nods. He has no reason to bring another living pattern out of its death merely because there is room in his legion.

He leaves the shadow alone.

* * *

Before he goes, he asks Makima what happens to Reze when the meeting ends.

The question removes some of her satisfaction.

“She has been outside,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

“Today.” ᴹ

“That's what you asked for.” ᴹᵏ

“And I was clear about not treating her as a thing. Ending the afternoon doesn't make me comfortable with doing it again.” ᴹ

Reze looks from one to the other.

“I can answer,” she says. ᴿᶻ

Mau stops.

She faces Makima.

“I don't want the order back.” ᴿᶻ

Makima's expression is very still.

“You could go somewhere inconvenient.” ᴹᵏ

“Yes.” ᴿᶻ

“You could become a problem.” ᴹᵏ

“I could sit on the chair until you decide what to do with me.” ᴿᶻ

The answer holds the whole room he first found her in.

Mau waits beside her without answering for her.

* * *

The continuing undertaking is Makima's to offer.

No restoration of the revoked control, direct or by a proxy. No forced return because Reze has chosen a road Makima would rather not watch her take. If Reze attacks, defense remains defense; the agreement does not make her incapable of consequences for her own choices.

Reze asks whether anyone expects her to repay Mau.

“No,” he says. ᴹ

“Not you. Her.” ᴿᶻ

He turns toward Makima.

The woman looks at the two people waiting for her answer.

“No,” she says at last. ᴹᵏ

The chosen terms settle when she accepts them. Not a force strong enough to make her kind. A consequence attached to the choice she has made because, for now, keeping Mau interested matters to her more than this particular obedience.

Mau knows the difference.

He still feels relief.

* * *

Reze stays at the doorway when he leaves.

She has not decided where to go. There are things she wants to ask before choosing a destination, and he doesn't offer to solve that uncertainty by putting her in a room he owns.

He tells her how an ordinary message can be left for him at the exterior crossing. No need to approach the protected boundary without notice. No promise of admission before the people living there have heard the question.

“Will they know you met me?” she asks. ᴿᶻ

Mau looks at her.

“They should,” he says. ᴹ

“That wasn't what I asked.” ᴿᶻ

He has no useful answer he is willing to give in front of Makima.

Reze lets him leave with that.

* * *

On the way back, Noise wants to begin on the emission.

Mau asks to see the first observation before deciding what kind of construction might be possible.

The pattern appears.

He is still walking toward the house. The soldier at his return point acknowledges him. Somewhere beyond it, Yuka is having the afternoon he told himself this journey would help preserve.

*Keep the source separate from the release,* he tells Noise. *I want to know which part is force before we try making anything answer to her other rules.* ᴹ

Noise separates the questions.

Mau follows the route home with the careful satisfaction of someone who has learned something dangerous without letting it hurt the people beside him.

He has not yet counted the people he chose not to let stand there at all.
