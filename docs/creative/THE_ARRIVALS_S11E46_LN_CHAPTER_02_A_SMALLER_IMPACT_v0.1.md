# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 69
## Chapter 7 — A Smaller Impact

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 69
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S11E46
**Continuity state:** B+207 morning/day. Noise studies retained local force emission seen from Makima and builds a limited analogue using Mau's own mana, distinct from Control/hierarchy/contracts. Isolated tests then one small directed external impact into an ordinary board at prepared short range, preceded by two failures. Not remote death, naming power, instant internal targeting, limitless Bang or perfected accuracy. Existing Authority would already break the target; this experiment tests a different emission route. No two active CT or stable Step. Secret work remains unshared; Ciel not disabled. Yuka/Mau keep a promised ordinary outing within home grounds.

*When: Noise says it has found a way to begin, Mau asks it to begin somewhere other than his finger.*

The proposed line is very neat.

Point, release, impact.

It contains far fewer of the unpleasant details he expects from a pattern observed only once at close range. He asks Noise to show him what it has left uncertain.

The line becomes considerably less neat.

*There,* he says. *That's the one I want to look at.* ᴹ

Noise keeps it open.

Makima's power had supplied a result through relationships which belonged to her. Mau has no interest in letting a new operation decide that someone standing in front of him is subordinate, indebted or his to direct because pointing at them has become useful.

They separate that question from the narrower one.

A directed force. A place of release. A path through which the supply becomes an impact rather than a hold.

His own mana can feed a model. It cannot make the missing parts true merely because there is enough of it to spend.

* * *

The first isolated expression makes pressure without giving it a useful direction.

Noise closes it.

Mau studies the difference between containing the source and simply squeezing it harder until something happens. The wrong solution would work very impressively in a place he wanted destroyed.

That doesn't make it an answer he wants inside his hand.

The second version finds a direction and loses most of the force along it. The model's target barely changes.

*Too much boundary,* he says. ᴹ

Noise gives him the earlier failure beside it.

Too little. Too much. Both take power. Neither gives him permission to pretend he has discovered an efficient attack.

He sits with the comparison until the middle answer begins to make sense.

Then he asks for one more isolated expression.

* * *

Outside, he chooses a board which is already split at one end and puts it against a low bank of earth.

The space behind it is clear. There are no people passing through the line. His physical hand is empty. He has not brought a dagger to improve the result by accident.

Authority could break the board.

A cut would make it embarrassingly simple. A small magical shot would also solve the practical problem of turning a useful piece of wood into two less useful ones.

He knows all that.

The question is whether this other route can release cleanly without asking his current cursed technique to supply its structure.

Something he could use while a different operation occupied that part of him.

Something he would still have when the next place tried to take his usual answers away.

He looks at the board for a little too long.

*Ready?* Noise asks. ᴺ

Mau raises his hand.

*Small.* ᴹ

* * *

The first external attempt punches dust out of the ground beside the target.

Mau lowers his hand immediately.

Not a miss he can correct by deciding to be better at aiming. The release has pulled toward the line he prepared in the model, while the physical hand offered a slightly different starting point.

He has let a remembered success become the wrong instruction for the place he is standing now.

He moves the target closer.

Sets his feet again.

This time he watches where the operation actually begins instead of trusting the gesture which makes it look familiar.

The impact makes a dull crack.

A short split opens across the middle of the board. The bank behind it catches the displaced pieces.

His finger aches.

He closes the route before Noise can offer a stronger version.

*Keep that,* he says. ᴹ

*Small,* Noise answers. ᴺ

*Small is what we proved.* ᴹ

* * *

He examines the hand before trying anything else.

No open wound. No numbness. A residual pressure which fades when he leaves the route alone. He can move each finger as well as he could before the test; the old stiffness in the left hand has not been solved by an exercise performed with the other one.

The result belongs on a list with its conditions attached.

Prepared distance. Clear line. One weak, directed impact. Supply from mana. Imperfect aim. No evidence that he can reproduce Makima's own reach, potency or the other things she means when she talks about control.

Noise records it.

Then asks a question Mau hasn't been expecting.

*Show Ciel?* ᴺ

He looks up from his hand.

*When it's ready,* he says. ᴹ

Noise holds the image of the cracked board.

*Better first?* ᴺ

Mau thinks of Ciel sitting beside it in the room last night, offering time without making it a test of whether he deserves her patience.

*I want something useful to show her,* he answers. ᴹ

Noise accepts the explanation.

That is not the same thing as making it a good one.

* * *

He returns the board to the scrap pile and washes his hands before finding Yuka.

She is waiting with two drinks and the determined expression she gets when he has already promised something ordinary enough that he might be tempted to fit another task in front of it.

“You made it,” she says. ʸ

“I said I would.” ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

She gives him one of the cups.

They go to the place they chose yesterday, within the familiar grounds, and sit where the shade will reach them before the drinks get warm. He listens while she tells him something Maki said about the way she releases a borrowed fold. She demonstrates with her empty hand, annoyed by the remembered error and already interested in how to fix it.

He asks a question.

She explains it again, more clearly this time, and says she wants to try with him after the guests finish their own plans.

“I'd like that,” he says. ᴹ

“Good. You're the difficult target.” ʸ

“I'm a delightful target.” ᴹ

“You're a moving problem.” ʸ

“You married it.” ᴹ

She puts her drink down and leans across to kiss him.

“I know.” ʸ

He closes his eyes for the kiss.

The new construction waits. For a while he wants nothing from it which he cannot already have by staying where he promised to be.
