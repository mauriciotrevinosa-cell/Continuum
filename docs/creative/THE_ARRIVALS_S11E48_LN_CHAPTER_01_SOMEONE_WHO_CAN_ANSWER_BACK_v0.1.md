# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 69
## Chapter 10 — Someone Who Can Answer Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 69
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S11E48
**Continuity state:** B+208 afternoon. Souei's concrete concern reaches Rimuru/Jin-Woo; no omniscient disclosure. Mau answers directly about his limited double and agrees Ciel will attend its next test. Friends propose a real controlled training duel with Jin-Woo in an already inspected cleared area of the ordinary dungeon next day. No final-trial conversion, death bracelets newly invented or blanket all-technique authorization. Body stats permanent; output can be restrained deliberately. Makima visits/other hidden work not asked/disclosed yet, Ciel neither disabled nor fooled by a fabricated full record.

*When: Jin-Woo asks whether he would like a fight, Mau looks behind him to see who has put him up to it.*

Gojo raises a hand.

“I supported the idea.” ᴳ

“An immediate concern,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Yuta supported it too.” ᴳ

“A more serious concern.” ᴹ

Yuta looks amused despite himself.

They are outside the practice yard, where Yuka has been making Mau occupy a very inconvenient part of the space while she tests the placement of a fold. He is carrying a trainer and wearing the pleased expression of someone who has just made an exercise more difficult on purpose.

Yuka has noticed.

“He could use somebody who won't let him enjoy that so much,” she says. ʸ

“I was helping.” ᴹ

“You were enjoying helping.” ʸ

“A common failing in this family.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo takes the trainer from him and examines the edge.

“Something less like an example,” he says. ᴶ

Mau's attention changes.

“How much less?” ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru gives him the actual concern before the proposal can become a challenge nobody remembers asking for.

Souei has mentioned the double. Not in outrage at being asked a question, and not as a claim that Mau stole his complete technique. A prototype which pulls at the inside of the person using it deserves more than an empty yard and somebody who wants it to work.

Mau's smile fades a little.

“It stopped before anything tore,” he says. ᴹ

“Good,” Rimuru answers. “That means we can discuss it before Shoko has to put something back.” ᴿ

Ciel asks about the warning through their connection.

He tells her. Two positions. One boundary trying to answer to both. No transfer of the guests' rooms, no attempt to make another person out of the shell. She asks to see the failure, and he gives her that working record without changing where it stopped.

For a while she is quiet.

*I would like to be present before the next attempt,* she says. ᶜ

*All right.* ᴹ

*Before, Mau.* ᶜ

He looks down at his hand.

*I heard you.* ᴹ

It is not an account of all the work he has kept. It is a real account of the thing they have asked about. Ciel examines it as that, not as a miraculous complete inventory of everything he has done while she was speaking to somebody else.

Gojo waits until Mau looks up again.

“You're getting better at something,” he says. “I've been watching your feet.” ᴳ

“Terrible hobby.” ᴹ

“There are worse ones. I wanted to see how much of it still works when somebody gets to disagree.” ᴳ

* * *

They choose the cleared space in the ordinary dungeon because the yard is not a reasonable place to discover how far one of them can throw the other.

It has been inspected during the usual work. The approaches and return are available. They will check them again before using it, with people present who can stop the exercise and help if something goes wrong. No one has offered immunity to injury or an opportunity to see whether a fatal wound counts as an interesting result.

Jin-Woo wants to begin with physical technique and weapons they can control.

Mau looks at him.

“You're going to tell me my shoulder is too high while hitting it, aren't you?” ᴹ

“If it is.” ᴶ

“Cruel.” ᴹ

“Then we can add the other tools deliberately.” ᴶ

Shoko, who has arrived in time to hear the last part, asks which tools.

They answer her.

No damaging domain experiment. No True Dragon body. No crowd of shadows attacking outside the agreed field. Two people who can take a serious exchange and still hear the call to stop. The permanent strength of their bodies doesn't switch off; how hard they choose to spend it remains a decision for every contact.

Mau knows the difference. He has caught enough cups badly to be tired of pretending precision is what happens when power goes away.

“I want to actually try,” he says. ᴹ

Jin-Woo nods.

“So do I.” ᴶ

* * *

Yuka walks home with him after the others finish choosing the time.

“You look happy,” she says. ʸ

“Somebody offered to hit me in a room where I don't have to save a city.” ᴹ

“I can hear why Shoko wanted the details.” ʸ

He laughs and puts an arm around her.

For a little while she lets him look pleased. Then her hand settles at his side, over the place where the new prototype had pulled.

“Tell us before something tears,” she says. ʸ

He looks down.

Her expression hasn't become angry. That almost makes the request harder to treat as something he can answer lightly.

“I stopped,” he says. ᴹ

“I know. I want you to be able to say it while stopping is still easy.” ʸ

He covers her hand with his.

“Okay.” ᴹ

She leans into him, and they finish the walk together.

By supper he is thinking about the first exchange. Which hand Jin-Woo will offer him. Which opening will be real. Whether he can get the preparation small enough that the man who taught him the movement won't see the answer coming.

It is a wonderfully selfish thing to want.

He lets himself want it.
