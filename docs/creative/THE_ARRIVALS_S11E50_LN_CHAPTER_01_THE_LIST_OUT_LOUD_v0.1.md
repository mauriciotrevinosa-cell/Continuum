# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 69
## Chapter 14 — The List Out Loud

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 69
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S11E50
**Continuity state:** B+209 afternoon, after return/lunch/initial shoulder care. Requested conversation actually takes place: Mau/Yuka/Rimuru with Ciel and Noise through established shared-room connection; DIO invited before discussing retained guest models. No public household memory broadcast/new physical body. Mau admits blanket hidden-construction authorization and built/tested projects; derived DIO/Ashborn new activation not invented as an offscreen acquisition. Ciel/DIO/Noise have agency and hurt, no instant repair. Direct questions expose three Makima visits and her admitted prior authorization of attacker. No claim Yuka already knew or romance/relationship break.

*When: Ciel asks which part of the archive he opened, Mau doesn't make the answer smaller.*

*All the material we took,* he says. ᴹ

He is sitting beside Yuka after lunch. The shoulder has been examined, the torn shirt changed and the training weapons put away. Rimuru has brought his own chair close enough that they don't need to turn a private conversation into a performance for whoever passes the open common room.

Noise is listening through the shared connection. Mau has given Ciel the part of the practice record she asked to examine, but she hasn't begun pulling him through it frame by frame.

Rimuru repeats the parts of the inward conversation Yuka cannot hear for herself. Nobody has to infer the question from Mau's answer. The relay stays within their small group; the people outside have not been invited into his memories or this room.

*That was your decision to make,* she says. ᶜ

He looks toward Rimuru.

*Yes.* ᴹ

*I am not asking you to close it again.* ᶜ

He had prepared for that question.

Not for the next one.

*What else have you been doing without us?* ᶜ

He looks toward Rimuru before answering.

*I could see effort in the exercises you told us you were practising,* Ciel says. *That didn't tell me you had lifted the boundary we agreed on. You and Noise weren't sharing the working record. Today I could compare more than the effort.* ᶜ

He had counted on that difference: ordinary practice to explain the strain, private work to keep the reason out of the answer. She had not stopped paying attention. He had made use of what she was willing to trust him to say.

* * *

He could answer with the cup.

It would be true. He has done careful work with it. Reduced the oversupply, found the way to make one small grip useful without treating the old pain as a price he should simply learn to tolerate.

He looks at Yuka's hand beside his.

“More than the cup,” he says aloud. ᴹ

She turns toward him.

Rimuru waits.

Mau reaches inward for the work list and brings it into the conversation deliberately. The inherited hands. The short-distance model which moved cloth. The bodily Step which had stopped before a boundary tore. The double Souei has already asked about. The small force emission which has cracked a board once.

No perfected secret army hiding behind the names. No claim that three successful small tests have made the body safe for every answer Noise can imagine.

The real work is enough.

Rimuru looks at the list for a long time.

“When did we decide to start all of this?” ᴿ

“We didn't,” Mau says. ᴹ

* * *

Ciel asks Noise the next question.

*Did you stop using the old construction boundary?* ᶜ

Noise is silent long enough that Mau feels the need to answer for it.

He doesn't.

*Mau said build,* Noise says. ᴺ

*What did he include?* ᶜ

The outline in the shared room turns toward him. Not asking him to supply an acceptable lie. Looking for the person who had agreed with it that they were doing useful work.

“The whole retained list,” Mau says. “Including forbidden.” ᴹ

Yuka's hand becomes still.

Rimuru looks straight at him.

*And you weren't going to tell us,* Ciel says. ᶜ

“I wanted results first.” ᴹ

*I asked whether you intended to keep it from us.* ᶜ

He looks down, then back up.

“Yes.” ᴹ

* * *

They ask DIO to join before the next part.

She has a choice about entering the conversation. Mau tells her why they are asking, without making Ciel carry the first sentence for him. DIO comes to the shared room and takes a chair near Noise, then listens to the list again.

The change in her face is difficult for him to watch.

“My records?” she asks. ᴰ

“Included in what I authorized. The retained models, not a new extraction from you.” ᴹ

“And Ashborn's?” ᴰ

“Included too.” ᴹ

“Have you activated something new from either of us?” ᴰ

“No. The new tests were these. I haven't put a new version of you or him into the field.” ᴹ

“That is an important distinction,” she says. “It isn't an answer to why our agreement stopped being enough for you.” ᴰ

He had wanted the distinction to carry more of the weight.

“I wasn't taking your power.” ᴹ

“I know what you weren't doing.” ᴰ

DIO moves her hand from the table to her lap. The small motion makes Noise turn toward her.

“You asked me before,” she says. “You let me decide what I could offer you. I liked being asked.” ᴰ

* * *

Noise says it thought the work would help.

Not quickly. Not a defensive recitation. The words arrive separately, with long spaces in which it seems to be searching the room for a version of its friends who will understand what it had expected the finished construction to mean.

*Safer,* it says. *Better. Help.* ᴺ

*I believe you,* Ciel answers. ᶜ

The outline lifts slightly.

*I am still hurt,* she continues. ᶜ

It stills again.

Mau puts his hand on the edge of his chair.

“It was my instruction.” ᴹ

“It was also here,” DIO says quietly. “It can tell us what it chose.” ᴰ

Noise looks toward Mau.

Then back to her.

*Build. Help Mau. Show later.* ᴺ

*Did you want to show me?* Ciel asks. ᶜ

*Yes.* ᴺ

*You could have wanted that while we were still working together.* ᶜ

The outline's hand moves across the table and stops before touching hers.

She notices. Leaves her hand where it is.

Nothing in the room becomes a command to take it.

* * *

Rimuru asks Mau which danger he thought required the secrecy.

“Not a danger from you.” ᴹ

“Then from being told to wait?” ᴿ

Mau looks at him.

“From finding out I could have finished something sooner. From being somewhere with the answer still sitting in a model because I decided another week would be fine.” ᴹ

Yuka has been listening without moving her hand away.

Now she turns it over beneath his.

“Since I was hurt,” she says. ʸ

He doesn't answer immediately.

She doesn't make the silence an admission he hasn't chosen to give.

“Yes,” he says at last. “I had ways to do more. Unfinished ones. I wanted to know which could stop being unfinished.” ᴹ

“You could have told me.” ʸ

“You'd just got better.” ᴹ

“And then?” ʸ

He looks at their hands.

“I wanted you to stay better.” ᴹ

The answer does not make her smile.

* * *

Ciel returns to the concrete list after a while.

Not because the feelings have become less important. Because there are actual unknown operations in his body, and loving the person inside it does not make their conditions explain themselves.

Which ones were isolated. Which had been supplied externally. Which warning had come before an actual release and which after it. Mau tells her. Noise provides the failed models rather than only the parts it was proud to have finished.

Yuka listens to the Step warning, then to the double pulling on the same boundaries. Her fingers close around his.

Rimuru asks about the small force release.

“It wasn't Authority,” he says. ᴿ

“No.” ᴹ

“Where did the model come from?” ᴿ

Mau looks up.

There is no useful way to make the answer into a technical term.

“Makima.” ᴹ

* * *

For several breaths nobody says anything.

Then Yuka asks when.

“The first time was nine days ago,” he says. “Again three days ago. Then yesterday morning.” ᴹ

“You went to her,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Alone?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Yuka is looking at his face, not the list now.

“Did you ask her?” ʸ

He knows the question without making her say the rest.

“Yes.” ᴹ

“And?” ʸ

Mau keeps his eyes on hers.

“She arranged it before the meeting. Before the agreement. She told me she sent the devil.” ᴹ

Yuka draws in a breath.

Her hand is still in his when she asks the next question.

“And you went back?” ʸ
