* * *

The rehearsal is almost finished.

Ram waits outside rather than making Seika interrupt it. Through the door she hears Kita count them back into a passage, Bocchi stop and begin again, then the four instruments finding the place together.

Seika comes out with a cloth in her hand.

“You could have come in.” ˢᵉ

“They were playing.” ᴿᵃ

“That does happen in there.” ˢᵉ

Ram looks at her.

Seika's mouth moves at the corner.

“Tea?” she asks. ˢᵉ

“Tomorrow.” ᴿᵃ

Seika pauses.

Ram had intended to make the next part sound effortless. It becomes clear that the effort of sounding effortless will take more time than saying what she came to say.

“I'd like to go somewhere with you tomorrow. Not to carry anything, or wait for someone, or see whether a shelf fits.” ᴿᵃ

Seika looks down at the cloth in her hand, folds it once and looks back up.

“A date?” ˢᵉ

“Yes.” ᴿᵃ

The word leaves Ram with no useful place to hide her hands.

Seika takes one before she has to choose.

“I'd like that.” ˢᵉ

* * *

They remain by the door longer than either intended.

Ram looks at their joined hands.

“We have been doing this for a while,” she says. ᴿᵃ

“I noticed.” ˢᵉ

“I didn't want you to have to keep guessing what I meant by it.” ᴿᵃ

Seika's thumb moves over the side of her hand.

“I had a reasonable idea.” ˢᵉ

“Good.” ᴿᵃ

“I still wanted to hear you say it.” ˢᵉ

Ram looks up.

The teasing has left enough room for the part which matters.

“I want to be with you,” she says. ᴿᵃ

Seika breathes out slowly.

“Me too.” ˢᵉ

Inside, somebody moves a chair. Both women look at the door as though the furniture has been caught listening.

Then Seika laughs, and Ram finds the courage to step closer.

The kiss is quiet and a little awkward only because they are both smiling by the time it ends.

“Girlfriend?” Seika asks softly. ˢᵉ

Ram considers making her wait for the answer.

She doesn't.

“Yes.” ᴿᵃ

Then her sister's expression.

Her own face changes so quickly that Seika points at her before she has spoken.

“Not a word.” ˢᵉ

“I wasn't going to.” ᴺⁱʲ

“You can say congratulations.” ᴿᵃ

“Congratulations.” ᴺⁱʲ

From inside, Kita asks what has happened.

Ram keeps holding her hand.
