## Chapter 20 — All of Him

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 69
**Reading order:** 20

DIO/Ashborn remain physically in protected accommodation, existing inner guest area unoccupied by them. Two rejected isolated models, successful nonliving sample, then first whole-body displacement to prepared visible point less than a pace away. Repeats twice with deliberate recovery pauses; later farther unprepared point rejected before release. Own mana/whole-body containment/eyes/attention costs. No Shadow Exchange anchor, duplicated body, bypass DIO ward, combat mastery, simultaneous twoCT or general travel network. Other body impairments persist. *When: Ciel asks Noise to show the failed version first, it does.*

No improved picture in front of the warning. No clean result held up so everyone will be pleased before they find out what had almost pulled apart.

Mau watches the two positions emerge.

DIO and Ashborn are in their protected accommodation. Neither has been asked to become a passenger in a bodily test. The existing guest area within his structures is empty of them, its relationships still part of what the model must account for.

Yuka sits beside him while the record is examined.

She asks what he felt before he stopped. He tells her, then points to the place so she doesn't have to translate an abstract line into a part of the body she knows.

Her hand settles there briefly.

“Before,” she says. ʸ

He nods.

“This time before.” ᴹ

* * *

The correction is less dramatic than he expected.

Not a second boundary to pull the first one forward. Not enough force to make two incompatible positions stop disagreeing by breaking something between them.

The inner structures have to answer to the body carrying them rather than the patch of ground on which that body began the operation. The change must include the whole relationship before the release, with no part left to decide afterward which position still owns it.

Ciel rejects the first proposed closure.

Noise asks why.

She shows it the lag in the outer reference. The model has made the interior consistent and attached it to an answer which still arrives too late.

Noise goes very still, then brings the comparison closer.

*Again,* it says. ᴺ

They work through the second failure together.

Mau stays in his chair instead of beginning to supply the pattern through his body while the explanation is still being made.

* * *

The next cloth sample moves cleanly.

They check the whole sample, its position and the closure which brought it there. Then change the starting point instead of repeating the same successful picture until confidence begins standing in for evidence.

Another clean result.

Mau wants to stand up immediately.

Shoko puts a hand on the back of his chair.

“Finish the part where you tell us what happens if you need to stop.” ˢʰ

He sits back.

“If the relation doesn't close, no transfer. I stay where I started.” ᴹ

*And I can reject the release before supply begins,* Ciel adds. ᶜ

Rimuru voices the answer for Yuka and Shoko. Noise offers the exact rejection point instead of treating Ciel's participation as an insult to the work it has done.

They test that point with the model.

The sample stays where it was.

Only then does Shoko take her hand from the chair.

* * *

The receiving mark is less than a pace away.

Gojo looks at the distance, then at Mau.

“You could walk.” ᴳ

“That's my backup plan.” ᴹ

“An excellent technique. Very refined.” ᴳ

Mau gives him a look over his shoulder.

Yuka is laughing quietly beside Shoko.

He turns back to the mark before the laugh can make him forget what he intends to do.

No active cursed technique competing for his attention. No blade to bring across late. His own mana feeding a prepared spatial operation, Mini Ciel keeping the perception load within the chosen range, Ciel watching the proposed closure and Noise handling the model it has built.

His feet are on the first mark.

Then on the second.

* * *

He takes an ordinary step afterward because he needs his body to tell him where the ground has become.

The stone is solid.

His knees are still his. The hand he puts against his chest finds no pulling underneath. He can feel the contained structures in the position he occupies, not reaching backward for the place he has left.

*Mau?* Ciel asks. ᶜ

*Here.* ᴹ

The answering interval is short enough that he can hear why she wanted to check it anyway.

He turns toward Yuka.

Her eyes are wide.

He grins.

“I walked very well.” ᴹ

Gojo makes an offended sound.

Shoko reaches him before either can improve the joke.

* * *

They check before repeating.

Not every time a man moves from one stone to another. This time, because it is the first time his body has supplied this particular answer without carrying the error which had nearly made the attempt dangerous.

No lost sensation. No new weakness. The old left fingers remain stiff; the blurred edge of his view has not become a problem a successful spatial trick can claim to have solved.

He is sweating.

The operation has taken attention and flow even though the distance is small. Ciel asks for the pause he is already willing to offer, and he drinks while Noise keeps the successful record beside the rejected ones.

Yuka comes to stand in front of him.

“All of you?” she asks. ʸ

He touches her hand.

“All of me.” ᴹ

* * *

The next two transfers use the same short, visible preparation.

No shadow unit receives him. He isn't exchanging places with somebody waiting on another road. The operation changes his position through the relationship they have built, and its current reliability belongs to this distance, this preparation and this body rested enough to supply it deliberately.

When he asks about a farther point without preparing its relation first, Ciel rejects the release.

He remains where he is.

For a moment disappointment arrives before relief.

Then he looks at the distance and laughs.

“Backup plan.” ᴹ

He walks there.

* * *

They stop with three whole-body successes and one useful refusal.

No sparring immediately afterward to discover what the construction does while his attention is occupied by a dagger. No demonstration at the protected boundary. He has enough of a result to practice without treating it as permission for the largest version he can imagine.

Noise lingers over the records while the others begin putting the small things away.

*Ciel saw,* it says. ᴺ

Mau looks toward the connection.

*She helped.* ᴹ

The outline turns toward her representation.

*Better.* ᴺ

*The model is,* she answers. ᶜ

It waits.

Then puts its hand on the table near hers, in the place it had stopped before.

She doesn't promise that one good review has finished the conversation they still need. She turns her hand toward it.

Noise touches her fingers carefully.

* * *

Mau goes home at the speed of an ordinary person walking beside his wife.

Yuka tells him which part of the small disappearance looked strangest. He asks whether she could see anything in between. She says no, then makes him listen to the more important question: whether it felt as frightening as it looked before they had agreed it was safe enough to try.

He answers honestly.

A little, before the first release. Much less after she took his hand on the other side.

She keeps it now.

He has found a way across a space he used to have to travel.

For the walk home, he chooses the distance with her in it.
