# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 70
## Chapter 1 — Somewhere to Return To

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 70
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S11E53
**Continuity state:** B+212 morning. Luminous/Hinata/Souei remain temporary guests from B203, departure schedules discussed rather than forgotten. Hinata proposes liaison visits/stays partly because she wants Anko nearby, without abandoning duties. Private choice made with Anko; no automatic permanent room or compulsory government appointment. Luminous route proposal advances only to joint survey, no second hundred already deployed. Existing J family/home community present in breakfast rhythm. No season transition/time skip, child or gate incident.

*When: Hinata says she would like to come back for work, Anko asks whether that is the whole reason.*

“No,” Hinata says. ᴴⁱ

Anko looks up from her cup.

The answer has arrived too quickly for the tease she prepared.

“Oh.” ᴬⁿ

Hinata smiles into her own drink.

“You asked.” ᴴⁱ

They are outside the guest Inn, with the morning still early enough that Nazuna can pass on her way to sleep and make an entirely unnecessary observation about how some people are suddenly awake at very civilized hours.

Anko tells her to keep moving.

Ko follows with a quiet greeting and a smile he has considerably more success hiding.

Hinata waits until both are gone.

“Does she always do that?” ᴴⁱ

“When she's enjoying herself.” ᴬⁿ

“Frequently, then.” ᴴⁱ

“An exhausting amount.” ᴬⁿ

* * *

The work is real.

Luminous needs a reliable person who can bring back an account of the road arrangements without treating every report from the house as a rumor to be sorted into the version most convenient to a distant office. Rimuru needs someone at the other end who can ask a practical question before the next letter spends several days answering the wrong one.

Hinata can do some of that.

Not all of it from here. Not without going back when people expect her. She has no intention of discovering that enjoying a place requires pretending the rest of her life has become somebody else's problem.

But visits could become less hurried. Some stays could be planned around the work instead of ending as soon as she has delivered a message.

“Luminous knows you're asking?” Anko says. ᴬⁿ

“She asked whether I had a practical reason to keep looking at the return dates.” ᴴⁱ

“That sounds like her.” ᴬⁿ

“I gave her one.” ᴴⁱ

Anko rests her hand beside Hinata's on the table.

“And the other reason?” ᴬⁿ

Hinata turns her hand over.

“I'm telling her.” ᴴⁱ

* * *

They discuss the visit the other way too.

Anko wants to see where Hinata actually goes when she leaves, not only imagine the road taking her to a set of duties which have no rooms or ordinary meals attached. She has questions about the city. About which places Hinata likes when nobody has required her to be there.

Hinata gives her two answers and refuses to explain the third until they are actually standing in front of it.

“A surprise?” Anko asks. ᴬⁿ

“Something you can judge for yourself.” ᴴⁱ

“That's usually what people call a surprise when they're being difficult.” ᴬⁿ

“Then you understand the plan.” ᴴⁱ

Anko laughs and moves closer.

Their kiss lasts long enough that neither bothers pretending the morning has remained purely administrative.

* * *

At breakfast, Luminous tells Rimuru she will accept a joint survey before asking him to help name the size of an assignment.

Souei has already agreed which road accounts need comparing. Hollis will give them the loading details from the next route he actually intends to use, not a theoretical cart which can fit anywhere because the map is flat.

No second formation rises from Mau's shadow at the table.

He asks which day they are considering, then lets Fern keep the dates while he finishes eating.

Jin-Woo's mother asks whether he would like more. She means the food in front of her, not another responsibility. He accepts, and Jin-Ah tells her she is encouraging a man the rest of the house has already failed to feed into restraint.

“I'm very restrained,” Mau says. ᴹ

Yuka looks at his plate.

“With evidence like that, who could disagree?” ʸ

Luminous watches Hinata come in beside Anko.

“Productive morning?” she asks. ᴸ

Hinata meets her eyes without releasing Anko's hand immediately.

“Yes.” ᴴⁱ

The ruler lets that be enough.

* * *

Before the meal ends, they agree that the guest room remains a guest room.

Hinata will ask for the dates she needs. She won't assume every visit must put her somewhere farther from Anko merely because the work has an official name, and the house won't turn a welcome into a claim that she can no longer leave.

Anko asks Jinshi about the next outward travel arrangements herself.

Mau hears part of the exchange, looks toward Hinata and gets a warning glance which has become familiar enough to be affectionate.

He lifts his cup in surrender.

Yuka nudges his knee beneath the table.

“Diplomacy?” she asks. ʸ

“Improving daily.” ᴹ

She smiles and takes the last piece from his plate before he notices it is the one she has been looking at.

He notices.

He lets her have it.
