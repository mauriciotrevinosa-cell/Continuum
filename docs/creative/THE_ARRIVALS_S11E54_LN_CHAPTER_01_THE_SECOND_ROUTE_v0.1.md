# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 70
## Chapter 2 — The Second Route

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 70
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S11E54
**Continuity state:** B+212 midday/afternoon. Joint design of bounded dual-CT support from retained partial-overlap failure and current body/filtered eyes, not retcon that earlier costs never happened. New silent process keeps one prepared technique cycle supplied while second is prepared, with rejection before bodily activation. Isolated model rejects a pure fast switch and a destabilizing paired version; third model sustains two small independently changing responses briefly. No body test yet, double domain, unlimited CE, new mind, full Six Eyes mastery or stat upgrade. Yuka/Ciel/Gojo/Noise participate before releases. Analyze continues but guests' active powers not used as fuel.

*When: Gojo asks whether they are trying to do two things or trying to make one thing look like two, Mau says that is the first problem.*

He has put the old failure where all of them can examine it.

The brief second effect in the fight with Sukuna. The pressure which had not remained a clean advantage. The brain paying for an answer the rest of the body had wanted badly enough to attempt anyway.

Yuka sits close enough to see the record's boundaries without being made to watch the entire wound again.

“We aren't testing that,” she says. ʸ

“No,” Mau answers. ᴹ

He points to the part before the damage.

“I want to know whether there's a way to keep the preparation from collapsing into the other one. Small. Without a domain trying to make the whole room agree.” ᴹ

Gojo looks toward Ciel's part of the comparison.

“That's a better question.” ᴳ

* * *

Having several patterns available is not what they are trying to add.

Mau already knows how to leave one and use another. He has done it quickly enough in a fight to make an observer care much more about surviving the answer than naming the interval in which it changed.

This asks a different thing of the body.

Keep one technique actually responding while another must also receive a new input. Not a cut continuing after release. Not Sky moving something and neutral returning after the fold closes. Two ongoing operations drawing cursed energy through the structures which let him express them.

Noise offers the first model.

Ciel watches it respond, then makes the two inputs arrive together.

One waits.

*Switch,* she says. ᶜ

Noise holds the failure.

Mau has seen things faster than his eyes wanted to admit. Speed is still not a reason to call a delayed answer simultaneous.

“Again,” he says. ᴹ

* * *

The second model can give two answers.

It begins to feed the correction of the first back into the preparation of the second. The next input arrives with less separation, then the next with none which remains useful.

Ciel stops the expression before it is offered to Mau's body.

Gojo sits forward.

“There. More energy wouldn't have fixed that.” ᴳ

“It would have made the mistake more ambitious,” Mau says. ᴹ

“One of your specialties.” ᴳ

Yuka looks between them.

“Both of you.” ʸ

Gojo pretends to consider objecting.

Mau doesn't bother.

They let Noise take the model apart.

* * *

The useful answer is a support process with a very small job.

Hold the prepared calculation for one chosen cycle. Keep its supply distinct. Reject the attempted pairing if either route begins depending on the correction which should belong to the other.

It will not choose techniques for him. It will not make the cursed energy twice as large or move the burden out of his brain into a place which cannot be hurt. The body's working pattern still has to sustain both. His attention still has to decide what they are for.

Noise can build the support. Ciel can examine the rejection. Mau has to supply an actual pair he understands well enough to stop asking the process to invent the parts he hasn't practiced.

“Neutral and a small patch of Sky,” he says. ᴹ

Gojo nods.

“Two things you've already used separately.” ᴳ

“And one of them should make it very obvious if I turn it off.” ᴹ

Yuka reaches for the little markers they have brought.

“Then we test the answers separately too,” she says. “I don't want one object doing a convincing journey and everybody deciding that's enough.” ʸ

* * *

The third model lasts.

Not forever. Long enough for Ciel to change each input twice while the other response remains active. Noise keeps the two supplies visible in the comparison; they rise separately instead of trading places behind a fast switch.

Then the heat of the work climbs faster than the duration justifies.

Mau sees it before anyone asks whether they should try for more.

“Close.” ᴹ

Noise closes it.

He has not activated either pattern in his body today.

That fact leaves him both disappointed and rather pleased with himself.

Gojo notices the expression.

“Tomorrow,” he says. ᴳ

“You sound like Shoko.” ᴹ

“A devastating review.” ᴳ

* * *

They spend the rest of the session preparing the simple bodily test instead of attempting it with the attention they have left.

Yuka chooses the two approach lines. One will meet a small fold away from the body. The other will pass clear of that patch and test whether neutral is still responding to something new. No sharp object. No person standing in a path they have not already agreed is safe to use.

Rimuru writes down the stop signal after Shoko asks which one both men are least likely to reinterpret as encouragement.

“Stop,” Yuka says. ʸ

“Promising candidate,” Mau answers. ᴹ

She gives him the look which has made several much stronger people remember an unfinished chore.

He lifts both hands.

“Stop it is.” ᴹ

Noise keeps the new support process closed for the night.

Ciel asks it to leave the failed models available beside the successful one. It does so before being asked twice.

Mau puts the markers back in his inventory, takes his wife's offered hand and leaves the next attempt where they have agreed it belongs.
