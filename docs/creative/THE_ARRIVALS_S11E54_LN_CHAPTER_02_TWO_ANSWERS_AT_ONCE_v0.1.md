# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 70
## Chapter 3 — Two Answers at Once

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 70
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S11E54
**Continuity state:** B+213 morning. First controlled body test of new dual-CT support after actual design #2. Own neutral and tiny Sky patch simultaneously accept separate fresh inputs for roughly two heartbeats, then voluntary closure on rising load. Both live supplies witnessed by Gojo/Ciel, not fast switch/projectile continuing/Yuka supplying a second CT. One later repeat at same scale, longer duration not attempted. Finite CE, neural/perception/flow strain; no injury/new permanent stat, double domain, arbitrary pairings or continuous combat mode. Existing gray/blur/fingers intact. New process silent, not independent person or autonomous fighting mind.

*When: the first pebble changes direction, the second is still falling.*

Mau doesn't look at either long enough to forget the other.

The small patch of Sky turns the first path away from his shoulder. The second object approaches from the side where that patch has no part to play, reaches the neutral distance and stops making useful progress toward him.

Both are new inputs.

Both receive an answer.

He feels the two preparations remain distinct inside the support Noise has built. One is not waiting for the other to finish. Neither is a released effect he can abandon while pretending the body is still operating it.

His first heartbeat passes.

Then the next.

The pressure rises behind his eyes.

He closes Sky.

* * *

The change is immediate.

The paired load becomes one operation again. The support returns to its closed preparation instead of trying to recover the lost half by restarting it. Mau keeps neutral only long enough for the testing objects to be moved away, then lets that go too.

His shoulders drop.

Nobody claps yet.

Ciel asks him what he feels. He tells her: warmth, pressure, concentration becoming expensive very quickly. Not a cut inside his head. Not the old blank interval after trying to buy too much from a body already paying for a battle.

Shoko checks what she wants to check before he can decide that a good explanation should count as an examination.

Only then does Gojo speak.

“Both,” he says. ᴳ

Mau looks up.

The man is smiling.

“Not a switch. Not somebody else's technique beside yours. Both.” ᴳ

Mau's own smile arrives slowly, then becomes impossible to remove.

* * *

He sits before being told twice.

The first result is still small enough to describe without pretending the rest of his life has changed overnight. A prepared pair. Very low demands. A support process keeping the calculations apart while his own body and finite cursed energy do the work.

It has cost more than two ordinary uses would have cost separately. Correcting the separation is work too.

He can feel why a domain is not the next sensible experiment.

He can also see why the answer matters.

“I don't have to make the whole defense disappear to change the next line,” he says. ᴹ

“For two heartbeats,” Yuka reminds him. ʸ

He looks at her.

“A beautiful two heartbeats.” ᴹ

She smiles despite herself.

“They were.” ʸ

* * *

Noise asks whether they can make it longer.

Ciel asks which part of the record supports that question today.

The outline brings up the rise in pressure, studies it and leaves it beside the success.

*Not yet,* it says. ᴺ

Mau drinks his water before answering.

“I agree.” ᴹ

Gojo puts a hand against his own chest as though something terrible has happened to him.

“Maturity. In public.” ᴳ

“I'm still prepared to be annoying.” ᴹ

“Thank goodness.” ᴳ

Yuka takes the empty cup from Mau before he can use it to make a point about how easily the next test could be set up.

He notices the removal.

She notices him noticing.

Neither pretends it was accidental.

* * *

The later repeat stays within the same tiny demands.

Two fresh approaches. A fold. The neutral response continuing on the other line. Ciel's observation agrees with Gojo's eyes and with the internal record Noise keeps for them to examine afterward.

Mau closes the pair before the pressure can become a reason to see whether he tolerates pain better than he tolerates stopping.

The process stays available.

So does the warning.

They haven't built a second person inside him to fight while he rests. They haven't made every pair of techniques compatible because one familiar pair has been separated for a moment. Another combination will need its own model, its own proof that the corrections do not collide and a body which can sustain the demand.

Today the right result is two small successes.

He lets it remain two.

* * *

Afterward Yuka asks to hold his hand while he is still sitting.

He gives it to her.

“I liked seeing it,” she says. ʸ

“The part where I was impressive?” ᴹ

“That was nice.” ʸ

“A restrained review.” ᴹ

She runs her thumb over his knuckles.

“The part where I didn't have to find out afterward.” ʸ

His expression changes.

“I liked that too,” he says. ᴹ

She leans in and kisses him.

For a moment his attention belongs entirely to one thing, and he has no complaint about the limitation.
