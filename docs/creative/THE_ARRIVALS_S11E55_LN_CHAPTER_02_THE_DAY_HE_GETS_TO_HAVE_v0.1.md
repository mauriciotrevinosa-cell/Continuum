# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 70
## Chapter 5 — The Day He Gets to Have

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 70
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S11E55
**Continuity state:** B+214, morning through night. Continuous household day centered on Mau while other residents keep real routines. Reze sends an ordinary note through exterior watch, not admitted/new resident or verified safe by handwriting. Kamish/Kaisel summoned for a promised supervised familiar flight, not random permanent dragon at table. Mai's standing support/advance notice, personal inventory/shop, old impairments/gray except Yuka, relationships preserved. Original Ori message remains closed in home drawer; no birthday date/pregnancy/stat acquisition/Final Trial today.

*When: Mau wakes, Yuka has acquired most of the blanket and an entirely unreasonable amount of his pillow.*

He examines the space which remains.

Then the woman occupying it.

“A territorial incident,” he murmurs. ᴹ

She doesn't open her eyes.

“Report it later.” ʸ

“To whom?” ᴹ

“Your wife.” ʸ

“A compromised authority.” ᴹ

She shifts closer instead of giving anything back.

He puts his arm around her and stays there until the noise outside their room becomes recognizable as people going to breakfast rather than an emergency he has failed to hear.

Her hand finds his beneath the blanket.

“Now?” she asks. ʸ

He considers the warm weight against him.

“A little longer.” ᴹ

“Good answer.” ʸ

* * *

In the sitting room, Rimuru is looking at two pages as though one of them might become unnecessary if he dislikes it with sufficient conviction.

Shizue has already eaten.

“You asked them to put the departure arrangements in writing,” she reminds him. ˢᶻ

“I wanted them somewhere I could find them.” ᴿ

“You found them.” ˢᶻ

Mau takes one page long enough to see which part Rimuru is avoiding.

“They want an answer.” ᴹ

“An unreasonable expectation.” ᴿ

“You could claim to be a slime.” ᴹ

Rimuru looks at him.

“That stopped working when everyone discovered I could still understand the question.” ᴿ

Yuka goes past them wearing the McLaren hoodie and asks Shizue whether she wants to walk down together.

Mau loses the thread of the argument.

Rimuru follows his attention.

“You do know she lives here.” ᴿ

“A very good arrangement.” ᴹ

Yuka looks back, catches the expression and holds out her hand.

He returns the page and goes.

* * *

Nazuna is finishing the part of the day which everyone else has just begun.

Ko sits beside her with his own food. She takes one piece from his plate without looking. He moves the next one closer before she can pretend it required a difficult theft.

Anko is awake enough to be suspiciously cheerful and irritated by anybody who notices.

“That bad?” Mau asks her. ᴹ

“Eat.” ᴬⁿ

“Everyone's become very concerned about my nutrition.” ᴹ

“It's easier than listening to you.” ᴬⁿ

He takes a bite in obedience.

Nazuna laughs, finishes her drink and leaves with Ko walking her as far as the stair. Before she goes up, she stops Anko with a touch at her elbow.

The joke she might have made doesn't arrive.

“Tell her bye from me,” she says. ᴺᶻ

Anko's expression softens.

“I will.” ᴬⁿ

Mau looks down at his plate and lets them have the exchange.

* * *

Hinata has packed without making the room look as though she had never stayed in it.

There is a cup to return. A cloth she asks Anko to keep rather than finding room for it in the bag. Something they intend to finish discussing during the next visit, which neither tries to force into a complete answer in the minutes before leaving.

Luminous comes through the common courtyard while Fern is confirming the outward route.

“I expect the survey account,” she tells Rimuru. ᴸ

“You'll have it.” ᴿ

“Not only the part which says the next meeting should include lunch.” ᴸ

Mau looks at him.

“A recurring problem.” ᴹ

“I invite people to a welcoming place and this is how they repay me,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Luminous's farewell to the witches takes longer than her farewell to the paperwork. Echidna supplies a proposed date. Sekhmet objects to the hour. Minerva makes both of them let the guest finish answering before the next correction.

Hinata comes out beside Anko.

Mau checks the anchor only after asking whether she wants it to remain.

She does.

“I'll call if I need you,” she says. ᴴⁱ

“And tell someone nearer if I'm not the useful first answer,” he replies. ᴹ

“You've been listening to the meetings.” ᴴⁱ

“A terrible habit.” ᴹ

She gives him a brief hug before he can hide behind another joke. It has the briskness of someone who has decided affection needn't become a ceremony to be real.

“Look after yourself too,” she says. ᴴⁱ

He nods against her shoulder.

Anko waits until the others have moved ahead before kissing Hinata goodbye.

Mau takes Yuka's hand and turns them both toward the gate arrangements, leaving the last few words to the two people who need to say them.

* * *

The departure is along the road they agreed to use.

No one vanishes through a commercial transfer meant for coins and crystals. Souei has the first route duties to return to, and the hundred assigned soldiers continue the work they were sent to do rather than following his farewell into Mau's shadow.

Muster's house watch remains at its ordinary positions.

By the time the returning report settles, the common room is becoming a different place.

Jin-Woo's father is sitting with his wife and a cup he has let go cool while she tells Suho a story his grandson finds much more entertaining than Jin-Woo appears to think necessary. Jin-Ah contributes a correction which improves it considerably.

Hae-In listens with the serenity of someone who knows the man being embarrassed will survive the experience.

Mau stops in the doorway.

Jin-Woo sees him.

“Don't,” he says. ᴶ

“I haven't heard enough to decide what I'm not doing.” ᴹ

“You'll manage.” ᴶ

His mother waves Mau closer.

Jin-Woo closes his eyes.

* * *

The flight happens because Mau promised it yesterday.

Kaisel comes out first, stretches and lowers his head for Ori's greeting. Kamish follows in the open space chosen for him, large enough to make Jin-Woo stop pretending he was only walking past.

The dragon notices.

“You are looking again,” he observes. ᴷᵃ

Mau looks at Jin-Woo.

Jin-Woo looks at Mau.

“A very impressive dragon,” the older man says. ᴶ

“He's been coping with it for days,” Mau tells Kamish. ᴹ

Kamish appears to consider this a satisfactory tribute.

Ori asks whether he would like to fly beside Kaisel. He lowers his head toward her before answering, the careful choice of a creature who has more than one way to make a small person look up.

“If you can keep up.” ᴷᵃ

Kaisel makes a sound which needs no translation.

Mau laughs and checks the agreed short route with Frieren before they go. Ori has told her mother; Chloe knows where they intend to pass, and nobody has turned a familiar flight into permission to disappear toward an unknown gate.

Yuka comes too.

In the air, she leans against Mau and points out where the common roof vanishes behind the tree. He follows her finger. Ori asks for the slower turn so she can finish counting the windows.

Kaisel gives it to her.

For a while, that is enough journey for everybody.

* * *

Afterward Mai finds Mau near the workshop.

“I'm making the fittings for the second rack,” she tells him. ᴹᵃ

“Good morning to you too.” ᴹ

“It's almost afternoon.” ᴹᵃ

“A devastating correction.” ᴹ

She shows him the size with her hands, points to the prepared pieces and waits until he has actually looked before returning to the work.

“You don't have to announce every fitting.” ᴹ

“I know.” ᴹᵃ

“You'll do it anyway.” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴹᵃ

The familiar pull comes through the standing support a little later. Real cursed energy, his finite available supply, a burden much smaller than it would have been in her own body. Not mana turning every demand into nothing because the Black Heart exists.

Mau notices, checks his own condition and stays where he is.

Momo Nishimiya holds the ordinary rack steady while Mai fits the first piece. She has brought her broom but left it against the wall because not every useful thing has to become a demonstration.

Mau asks whether either would like help carrying the finished frame later.

Mai points to the space where it will stand.

“Later,” she says. ᴹᵃ

He lets them work.

* * *

He finds Bocchi in the music room during the quiet part between rehearsals.

Kita is there too, sitting with her shoes tucked beneath the chair rather than hovering like an audience who might judge the next wrong note. Bocchi plays a passage, stops, makes a face at the instrument and tries the part she wanted again.

Mau waits at the door until she sees him.

“Can I listen?” he asks. ᴹ

Her face changes through several possible answers before finding the one she actually wants.

“Yes.” ᴮ

He sits where she points.

The next passage is not the same one. She has been making something of her own, and showing it still costs her the few seconds between finishing and discovering whether the room remains kind.

Mau does not fill those seconds with a large compliment she will have to survive.

He asks whether the change near the end is meant to bring the earlier part back.

Bocchi looks up, surprised enough to forget being frightened.

“Yes. But it isn't quite—” ᴮ

Kita reaches for the edge of the page.

“Show him where it changes.” ᴷⁱ

She does.

Mau listens to the two versions until he can tell her which difference he hears without pretending he understands all the work inside it.

He stays longer than he intended.

Nobody needs him to justify the time.

* * *

At lunch, Jinshi has news about the outward trade which Maomao is only half listening to because someone has described a plant in a way which makes her doubt the person has actually seen one.

She asks for the description.

Jinshi passes it across before continuing the part meant for Mau and Rimuru.

The requests are changing. More buyers understand that a crystal can run down, that a weapon's usefulness depends on what it was made to strike and that Hollis's route is not permission to approach the house without notice.

Some still ask for impossible things.

“A promise that nothing will damage it,” Jinshi says, tapping one line. ᴶⁱ

“Tell them to leave it in a box,” Mau answers. ᴹ

“They want to use it.” ᴶⁱ

“Ambitious.” ᴹ

Rimuru laughs.

Maomao, without looking up, says the plant description is nonsense and she would like to meet the person who wrote it before somebody sells them a poisonous weed under a flattering name.

Jinshi looks far too pleased to have been given another reason to arrange an outing.

Mau catches the expression.

He decides he has no useful diplomatic contribution to make.

Before they leave the table, Rimuru reminds him about tomorrow's ordinary gate inspection. Mau has the first entry check; Yuta and Gojo will be at the threshold, with Ciel linked through Rimuru and the return route tested before anyone begins the day's work. It is the same practical clearance they have been taking turns doing, not a special invitation addressed to a monarch.

“I have it,” Mau says. ᴹ

Yuka asks whether he expects it to take long.

“The check shouldn't. I'll tell you if the work changes.” ᴹ

She nods and asks whether they can keep the afternoon afterward.

He says yes before Rimuru can think of another page.

* * *

Reze's note arrives during the afternoon.

An ordinary soldier reports a message left at the exterior point. Fern receives it through the usual arrangement while Mau is helping move the completed workshop frame. No new runner has been employed between breakfast and now.

The note waits with the other things intended for him.

He reads it beside Yuka.

Reze has found a place to sleep. She says Makima has not called her back. That is her account, not a reason to stop checking what the continuing undertaking means if she later asks for help. She wants to know whether a future conversation would involve being examined before she is allowed to decide whether she likes the people asking questions.

Mau reads that sentence twice.

“She has reasons to ask,” Yuka says. ʸ

He nods.

They take the question to Shoko instead of promising on her behalf. Shoko says any offered examination gets explained before it happens. Rimuru says the same about a visit. An invitation to speak does not quietly become an agreement to live under somebody else's direction.

They write an answer which Reze can accept, refuse or ask another question about.

Mau leaves it with Fern for the next agreed outward collection.

He doesn't put it away among his own private things.

* * *

The afternoon becomes several ordinary things he can choose between.

Maki asks whether he wants to watch the last part of Yuka's exercise. He does. Yuta has set the target so the answer requires a careful correction rather than more power. Yuka misses once, swears at the same place in the movement she has been trying to fix and gets the next attempt close enough to know what she wants to change.

Mau applauds with unnecessary enthusiasm.

She points at him.

“You're next.” ʸ

“For the exercise?” ᴹ

“For being difficult.” ʸ

Maki tells him to stand where Yuka needs him and stop arguing with a perfectly clear instruction.

He does.

Later, Seiko catches him before he can return a borrowed container without hearing her opinion about the way he put its lid on. Momo Ayase and Okarun arrive halfway through the correction and show no desire to rescue him from it.

“Family,” he says, with profound disappointment. ᴹ

Momo grins.

“Exactly.” ᴹᵒᵃ

* * *

At supper, the place the guests used has become part of the house again.

Kaneki is listening to Ichika explain something whose beginning Touka has already heard twice and whose end she still waits for patiently. Eren passes something to Mikasa before she asks. Rem and Umaru arrive with their hands joined, then discover that taking food is easier if at least one of them agrees to let go for a moment.

Ram takes the place beside Seika without disguising it as a practical necessity.

Across the room, Himmel asks Frieren whether she wants the book he found left where she can reach it after the meal. She asks whether he has finished the part they were discussing.

“Not yet,” he says. ᴴ

“Then bring it with you.” ᶠ

He looks very pleased by the inconvenience.

Ori leans across to tell Chloe they can finish the map tomorrow. Chloe asks which version. Ori begins an explanation which makes Umaru put down her spoon and become involved before either has asked for assistance.

Mau watches them from beside Yuka.

“What?” she asks. ʸ

“Nothing.” ᴹ

She gives him a look.

He smiles.

“I like being here.” ᴹ

Her shoulder rests against his.

“Good.” ʸ

* * *

Before bed, they take a little food to DIO's guest room.

Ashborn has his own portion waiting; he doesn't need the entire evening interrupted to prove they remembered him. Noise and Ciel are choosing when to spend time together again, and DIO is very plainly trying not to turn the choice into an itinerary nobody else had agreed to.

Mau listens long enough to hear Ciel object to one of the proposed arrangements.

Noise agrees with her.

DIO looks wounded.

“Two against one.” ᴰ

“An emerging tradition,” Mau says. ᴹ

She points at him.

“You are not helping.” ᴰ

Yuka starts laughing.

He offers to leave the extra dessert if the dispute can survive without him.

DIO takes it before accepting the terms.

* * *

At home, Yuka puts the hoodie over the chair rather than leaving it on the floor where he will try to invent a careful argument about whose clothes are whose.

He sees it and says nothing.

“You can be taught,” she observes. ʸ

“We've had an excellent day of instruction.” ᴹ

She comes to him with her hair loose.

He reaches for her before deciding whether the conversation needs another sentence. She steps into his arms, rests there and lets the ordinary noises beyond their rooms become quieter around them.

“Was this enough?” she asks. ʸ

He looks down.

“Today?” ᴹ

She nods.

He thinks of the view from Kaisel. The tune Bocchi wanted him to hear. The way Jin-Woo had finally laughed at his mother's story. A note left where someone else could help him answer it. His wife's hand beside his at the table.

“Yes,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka smiles and pulls him toward bed.

He has no reason to make the answer larger than that.
