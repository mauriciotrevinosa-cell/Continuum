*When: DIO asks whether the inspection is today, Mau tells her it should be a short first check.*

She is in her guest room with the little plant by the window. She has been trying to persuade it that the light reaches a perfectly reasonable part of the sill if it will stop insisting on growing toward the inconvenient side.

Ashborn has chosen to spend the morning in his own protected accommodation. Neither is waiting inside Mau's guest space to be carried into the instance. He checks their plans because those remain theirs to make, not because he expects the room he is going to examine to become a prison.

“We can leave the quiet arrangement as it is,” DIO says. “Bring us word if your plans change.” ᴰ

“I will.” ᴹ

She looks at him for a moment.

“You seem distracted.” ᴰ

He touches the doorframe.

“Yuka and I have something to talk about later.” ᴹ

DIO doesn't ask for a private sentence which belongs to his wife too.

“Then don't decide the conversation for both of you before you get there.” ᴰ

He looks back at her.

Then nods and goes to meet the others.

* * *

He takes the daggers out before reaching the gate.

Real weight. Real sheaths. Not something he intends to call from storage only after discovering which rules the next place would prefer him to accept.

The katana remains in his inventory. He has not emptied it or confused a routine inspection with a reason to leave his earned tools behind. He has also learned enough from places which close channels to keep the two blades physically with him.

Gojo notices.

“Planning to argue with the floor?” ᴳ

“Only if it's unreasonable.” ᴹ

“High standards.” ᴳ

Yuta finishes checking the approach and gives Rimuru the result. No unidentified people at the work area. No waiting creature dragged conveniently out of sight. The frame has the ordinary relation they have been using for the current clearance.

Gojo examines the visible space through it.

Mau stands beside him and looks too.

An empty front chamber. The familiar stone. The return face of the threshold where it should be.

*Link,* he asks. ᴹ

*Present,* Ciel answers. ᶜ

They test the response instead of taking the first word as enough.

* * *

The ordinary scout crosses first.

It is one unit from Mau's reserve, not a new person hired to take a risk nobody has explained. Its instructions are narrow: inspect the front room, show the observed approaches, return before anyone begins the work.

Mau watches the record through the bond.

No change of room. No living thing hidden where the frame's angle fails to show. It reaches the agreed mark, turns and comes back through the threshold.

The return works.

Yuta checks the scout's path against the physical line. Gojo examines the room once more after the unit is out. Ciel's chosen signal comes and goes without the delay which would make the others stop the inspection before it begins.

Beru and Igris remain outside.

Mau has asked them to help at the threshold today, not bound them to wait silently if the work goes wrong. They know who is coordinating the return and which call means the people outside need immediate help.

Beru looks as though a first-room inspection does not deserve this much of his king's time.

“A demanding morning,” Mau tells him. ᴹ

“I will bear it, my king.” ᴮᵉ

Gojo turns away too late to hide his smile.

* * *

The first entry belongs to the operator because the instance finishes registering the person responsible for the clearance before admitting the working party to the day's routes.

That part has been ordinary. They have used it before, tested it and refused to proceed when it supplied an answer which didn't match the room they could see.

Mau is taking this turn. He isn't entering a new trial invitation. Nobody has asked him to prove he deserves a title, and no countdown has been threatening to close the house's livelihood unless he obeys.

Rimuru stands within easy reach of the frame.

“First chamber. Call before moving on.” ᴿ

“I know.” ᴹ

“Say it anyway.” ᴿ

Mau looks at him, then nods.

“First chamber. I call before moving on.” ᴹ

He checks the daggers once with his hands.

Yuta has the return line. Gojo is watching the receiving side. Ciel is with him through the link.

Mau steps across.

* * *

The room changes after both his feet are inside.

Not a creature rising from an empty corner. Not a second attack which he could have prevented by drawing a blade more quickly.

The ordinary receiving chamber folds away from the identity it has just admitted.

His return takes one step to become farther than a step.

Then stops being there at all.

*Ciel?* ᴹ

He turns.

Stone moves through the place where the others were visible.

*Ciel.* ᴹ

Noise is with him.

The answer he is waiting for is not.

* * *

Outside, Gojo moves the instant the room shifts.

His hand reaches the frame while there is still the suggestion of an opening. Yuta catches the return line, supplies force through the tested relation and feels the relation cease to point to a place he can pull toward them.

Rimuru calls Mau's name.

Beru is already at the threshold.

The entry rejects him without admitting any part of the body beyond it. Igris pulls him back before another attempt can spend the moment they need to understand what has changed.

“Again,” Beru says, not to anyone in particular. ᴮᵉ

The frame has stopped showing the front chamber.

Words appear over the empty receiving surface.

**THE MONARCH — FINAL TRIAL**

**PARTICIPANT: OPERATOR**

**SUBSTITUTION COMPLETE**

“He did not accept that,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

No voice answers him.

* * *

The next lines are conditions, not a view.

**OUTBOUND COMMUNICATION: CLOSED**

**LEGION / SHIKIGAMI / SECONDARY BODIES: CLOSED**

**EXTERNAL SPATIAL TRANSFER / STORAGE ACCESS: CLOSED**

**PERSONAL DELIVERY: ORDINARY PROVISIONS ONLY**

**HOSTILE DAMAGE MODIFIER: 1.25**

**LOCAL ELAPSED: 00:00:01**

The counter begins moving.

Too fast.

Ciel tries the link again, then follows the last response to the point at which the receiving identity changed. Rimuru relays the useful part aloud while Gojo keeps testing contact with the actual frame rather than attacking the place where he wishes Mau still were.

“The outer room isn't the one he's in,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

The protected ground around them is still protected. No enemy has crossed it. The opening has been made to receive him into a separate place beyond its reach, and that place is the thing refusing the return.

Igris turns toward the path.

“We need Jin-Woo.” ᴵ

Rimuru nods once.

“And Yuka. Tell her what happened. Don't tell her he chose it.” ᴿ

* * *

Inside, in the first moments after the change, Mau stands in a corridor which smells of wet iron.

He doesn't know what the people outside are being shown.

He knows the channel to Ciel is gone. His own body is still here. The daggers are still at his sides. The place beneath his ribs which holds Noise has not been emptied by a line of text he has never seen.

He tries to call a shadow.

The bond answers with presence and no body.

He tries the return.

Nothing opens.

From somewhere farther into the corridor, the Architect speaks.

“Now,” it says, “we can finish.” ᴬʳ

Mau draws both daggers.

“No,” he says. ᴹ
