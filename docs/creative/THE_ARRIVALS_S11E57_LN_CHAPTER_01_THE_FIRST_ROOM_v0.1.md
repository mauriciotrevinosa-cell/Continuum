*When: the first room opens, Mau is still listening for Ciel.*

Not standing motionless. He has checked the vanished return, tried the bond and found the presence of his own army without a way to put a single body in the corridor. He has tried the deer. The wheel. The small receiving relation which should tell him whether an outward exchange can even begin.

Nothing has been erased from him.

Nothing he reaches for is being allowed to arrive.

His daggers remain in his hands. The weight, the edges, the familiar advantage of holding them are real. He flexes the left fingers into the grip Jin-Woo helped him choose and looks at the opening ahead.

*You can hear me,* he tells Noise. ᴹ

*Here.* ᴺ

*Then we find the part that changes when it thinks it's finished a room.* ᴹ

He has escaped through a transition before. Knowing that does not make the same answer exist here, but it gives him something better to do than keep asking a closed wall to be the door it was.

He moves forward.

* * *

The creature is taller than the arch through which he enters.

It has to unfold from its crouch to stand. Four arms. Two legs thick enough that the shifting plates around them make a slow grinding sound. No sword in any hand; the weapons are the hands themselves, long at the fingers, with a blunt ridge along the outside of each forearm.

There are inscriptions beneath the armor.

Mau sees one brighten in a shade the rest of the room scarcely distinguishes from the metal. His eyes give him structure where color will not. The supply moves under the left breastplate, divides and travels toward two of the arms.

“I've been in nicer waiting rooms,” he says. ᴹ

The creature turns its head.

He shifts before it attacks.

* * *

The first forearm strikes the neutral space and slows.

Mau has not dropped his defense because the room looks theatrical. The ridge stops short of his face, the pressure behind it building without buying the last distance.

The creature doesn't lean harder.

Its lower right hand closes toward the floor.

Mau is already off the ground when the slab beneath him tilts.

He knows that answer now. He flies clear of the rising edge and takes the next position before the broken stone can become a trap around his ankle.

“Someone else tried that,” he tells it. ᴹ

The upper hands spread.

Pressure closes around his left arm.

No weapon has crossed Infinity.

The force is being applied where the arm already is.

Mau supplies his own Authority against the hold and moves with the part he cannot immediately stop, turning the wrist out of the worst line before the opposing pressures can use his hand as the place to settle their disagreement.

Pain flashes up to his elbow.

Too much pain.

He lands hard, keeps the body moving and looks at the working inscriptions again.

*Not just the hands,* Noise says. ᴺ

*I saw.* ᴹ

* * *

He closes neutral for the first cut.

Dismantle crosses the near plate where he has chosen the thinnest visible relation. The line bites. Not as deeply as the release should have bought.

Mau is moving before the second arm can take advantage of the open interval. He brings neutral back, catches the physical ridge at its edge and looks at the shallow wound left in the armor.

His own supply was there. The preparation had been right.

Something between release and result has taken part of the answer away.

The creature drives its lower hands apart.

The floor splits beneath him.

He goes up again, then sideways when the pressure catches at his legs instead of the stone. Authority meets it, briefly holds it and loses the convenience of holding anything else at the same time.

The upper left hand comes through the position he was about to take.

He is protected from the fist.

The force already closing around his ankle does not become less real because the fist stops.

Mau turns in the air, gives the pull the direction it has asked for and uses the unwanted movement to reach the creature's flank.

Both daggers strike the exposed seam.

The edges cut.

Dark fluid runs over the lower plate.

He grins for the first time since the room changed.

“Good.” ᴹ

* * *

The creature catches the forearm which has to come close for the second dagger to matter.

It doesn't catch the blade. It closes pressure around the arm behind it, fast enough that Mau feels the trap before the fingers have finished the gesture.

He drops his shoulder and tries to turn through.

The inscription beneath the armor changes.

The pressure changes with it.

Something cracks above his wrist.

His left hand opens without asking him.

The dagger drops.

Mau catches its handle with Authority before it reaches the floor, throws himself away from the body and lands with the injured arm folded against his chest.

For a moment the whole room is the effort of not making a sound which costs another breath.

He makes one anyway.

The wrist is no longer where his forearm expects it to be.

* * *

RCT begins before he has found the next safe position.

It answers.

That is the first relief.

Then he feels how little of the usual repair comes through for the expenditure. The correction is present but constrained, meeting resistance at the place where a functioning body should have been able to put the supply to work.

Mana remains beneath it, vast enough to make the limitation feel obscene.

It does not become the cursed energy the repair needs merely because he wants the two answers to be interchangeable.

*Small,* Noise says. *Line first.* ᴺ

Mau supplies only enough to stabilize what he can while keeping his feet.

The creature comes after him.

* * *

He uses the pillar between them.

Not as a hiding place he expects to survive a direct blow. A line the creature has to choose whether to destroy before it can see the hand moving beneath the armor on the other side.

Mau sends Zoltraak through the narrow gap he has already opened.

The spell reaches the inscription.

Light bursts beneath the plate.

The grip around his legs falters.

He jumps clear of the first falling piece as the pillar breaks. Stone strikes his neutral and slides away. Dust fills the place where he needs a view.

His eyes could give him more.

Mini Ciel keeps the chosen filter where it is until he deliberately opens the useful part rather than demanding the whole room at once. He finds the circulation behind the damaged plate, the two arms still taking their supply from the line he hasn't broken and the moment the next pressure has to form.

*There,* Noise says. ᴺ

He is already moving.

* * *

The familiar desire to solve the problem with more force arrives complete.

Purple.

He can see where he would put it. He can also see how much preparation the creature is refusing to leave him, how much of his current cursed energy is already moving toward the wrist and how little the first cut achieved for the supply he gave it.

He has not lost the technique.

He has lost the conditions in which wasting a large answer would only be an embarrassing choice.

He keeps the dagger in his right hand instead.

Authority brings the other close enough to fasten its handle beneath the strap at his waist. He will not spend the next exchange pretending the left fingers can hold it while the bones are still wrong.

The creature advances through the dust.

Mau meets it at an angle it hasn't yet forced him to take.

* * *

He gives it the small patch of Sky while neutral still answers.

Two prepared operations. The pair they tested. Not a domain, not a new combination made true because the danger has become large enough to flatter it.

The ridge slides along the altered surface.

A second hand hits neutral from the other side.

Both answers hold for the heartbeat he asked of them.

The pressure in his head climbs immediately.

He closes Sky rather than finding out whether the field can turn two heartbeats into a wound before he has counted the second.

But the ridge has passed him.

The opening is real.

He drives the right dagger beneath the damaged plate, turns it and rips the inscription apart with the edge.

The creature makes its first sound.

Mau tears the weapon free before the lower arm can close around his body.

* * *

The next force catches him in the ribs.

Again, no traveling blade for Infinity to refuse. A compression forming against the place where the creature has fixed its attention, using the remaining inscription instead of the arm he expected.

Mau feels his breath go short.

He twists, meets it with Authority and cannot quite get the whole pressure off himself before the nearest forearm takes the stone beside him out of the wall.

The fragments stop at neutral.

The compression doesn't.

He needs the creature to lose its hold for less than a breath.

His injured left hand cannot offer the new release cleanly.

The right one already has a dagger.

He shifts the blade against the grip, raises two fingers beside the handle and asks Noise for the smallest force path they have actually made.

Close. Clear line. The lifted edge of the remaining plate.

The impact lands crooked.

Metal snaps back. Pain tears across his knuckle where the rough release has taken more from the hand than the little test board ever required.

It is enough.

The pressure breaks.

Mau goes in.

* * *

He doesn't try to cut through the whole creature.

He takes the exposed relationship apart.

One edge under the plate. A turn of his body which puts his weight behind a short cut instead of a long heroic swing. Authority holding the torn metal away from the place he needs to see. The neutral closed only when he needs another bounded Dismantle through the mechanism his eyes have finally understood.

The lower hands stop agreeing.

The body drops on one knee.

Mau has no time to feel pleased. The upper arms are still moving, and their physical strength has not become harmless because he has interrupted the part which made it clever.

He takes one glancing ridge across the side while changing techniques.

The hit tears his shirt and opens skin beneath it.

More than it should have.

He feels the blood warm against his waist and uses the next turn to put his right blade through the neck.

Not enough.

The creature's remaining hand closes around his shoulder.

Mau pushes forward instead of pulling away.

He brings the blade out of the first wound, turns its edge against the softer line and cuts again with the strength his body has actually earned.

The head comes free.

* * *

The body keeps moving for one ugly moment.

Then the pressure releases.

Mau falls with it, catches himself on the good hand and gets to his knees before the blood from his side can make the floor harder to use.

He is breathing through his teeth.

The room is quiet.

Nothing opens behind him.

He looks toward the far wall.

Waits.

“Come on.” ᴹ

The wall remains a wall.

* * *

He could try Arise.

The dead pattern is there. Something he might be able to name, hold or store if the field accepts the relation without allowing a body to return from it.

He looks at the wrist.

Then the blood reaching the top of his trousers.

“Not first,” he says. ᴹ

Noise turns its attention to the body he still needs.

He repairs the side until the bleeding slows enough to stop being the next thing which will kill him. Not beautiful. Not the complete answer Shoko would want before letting him stand up and claim it was handled.

It holds.

The wrist takes longer.

He makes himself look at its position instead of asking RCT to bless whatever shape pain has left it in. The alignment comes closer. Not right. He lacks the spare attention to discover the last part while sitting in the middle of the place which has just tried to break him.

He binds it temporarily and gets up.

* * *

The small shop opens when he asks.

For a moment relief is so strong he almost forgets to read what it will actually deliver.

Food. Water. Cloth. Ordinary camping supplies.

The other routes refuse the transfer. Storage does not return the things he has left in it. The catalog still remembers what belongs to him; the room will not admit the deliveries he needs most.

He buys water, a simple meal and material he can use as a cover.

The balance changes.

The objects arrive.

He drinks too quickly, coughs, presses the good hand against the wound at his side and waits until the pain settles before trying again.

There is an alcove beyond the broken pillar. He checks it, drags a piece of ordinary fallen stone into a position which will make the approach inconvenient and sits where he can see the opening without using the whole of his attention to keep it in view.

*Ciel,* he tries once more. ᴹ

No answer.

Noise stays close.

*Here.* ᴺ

*I know.* ᴹ

* * *

He eats with the good hand.

The food is ordinary. He has to chew it, swallow it and stop when the pull in his ribs makes breathing more important than the next mouthful. No recovery bar fills because he has bought something from the correct category.

He keeps a little cursed energy moving through the worst part of the wrist while the rest of him tries to become still enough to recover some of what he has spent.

The first room is dead around him.

There will be another.

He thinks of the promised afternoon. The look on Yuka's face when she asked him to come find her. The sentence before it, still much louder in his head than the small cut which started their morning.

He closes his eyes for one breath.

Then opens them and moves the water where he can reach it without twisting his side.

“We do this properly,” he tells Noise. ᴹ

The outline turns toward the opening.

“Home,” it says. ᴺ

Mau rests his good hand over the ring.

“Home.” ᴹ
