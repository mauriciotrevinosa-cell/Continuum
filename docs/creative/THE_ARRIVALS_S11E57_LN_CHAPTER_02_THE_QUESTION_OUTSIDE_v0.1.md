*When: Yuka arrives, the counter has already passed half a day.*

She hasn't been walking for half a day.

Igris found her, gave the facts without making the trial sound like something Mau had agreed to and brought her to the place where the ordinary receiving chamber no longer appears.

She can still feel the last kiss on her mouth.

The display reads twelve hours.

“How long here?” she asks. ʸ

Rimuru turns toward her.

“A little over two minutes.” ᴿ

The answer makes the ground seem briefly unreliable.

She puts one hand against the frame before deciding whether she needs the support.

“Can he hear us?” ʸ

“Not through the link,” Rimuru says. “We're trying the other routes.” ᴿ

* * *

Gojo has already found three different ways not to reach him.

The visible frame can be touched. The material around it can be broken. A carefully supplied amplification can interfere with the active surface without making that surface become the destination it has stopped showing.

He doesn't keep spending force on the same failure because he wants the next attempt to mean he tried harder.

He tells Yuta where the contact changes.

Yuta follows it with the return line they had tested, gives the line a second angle and finds the relation disappearing into a folded boundary which is no longer addressing this opening as an exit.

“If I pull this part harder, I'm pulling the frame,” he says. ʸᵗ

“Not Mau,” Gojo answers. ᴳ

They keep the line intact anyway.

It is the last useful relation they have to the place which accepted him.

* * *

Yuka tries the carrier before asking anyone else to invent an answer for her.

The agreement is still hers to close. The route inside her own technique responds. The offered connection toward Mau reaches the point where the instance has stopped admitting communication and returns no recipient.

Not a denial from him.

Not permission to force his body to answer.

She releases the attempt and makes herself describe what happened clearly enough for Ciel to compare it with the other failures.

“I could reach my side,” she says. “It didn't get to his.” ʸ

Rimuru repeats the useful part of Ciel's answer. The closed point is the same. Changing the form of the request hasn't made an allowed route appear.

Yuka looks at the ring on her hand.

“Then we keep trying somewhere else.” ʸ

Her voice sounds steadier than her breathing feels.

* * *

Beru does not like being asked to remain outside a door with his king on the other side.

Igris is not enjoying it either.

The difference is in what they do with the same feeling. Igris keeps the space around the frame clear, receives the people arriving to help and stops a second attempt from becoming a crowd in the only place Gojo and Yuta can work.

Beru goes where Rimuru asks him, brings what Shoko needs and comes back too quickly to pretend the errands have distracted him.

“Can you feel him?” Yuka asks. ʸ

She looks at Beru, then catches the missing breath in her own question.

“Not where he is. Him.” ʸ

The ant's hands close.

“The bond has not released us,” he says. ᴮᵉ

It isn't a report of the man's heartbeat.

Yuka accepts it as the fact it is.

* * *

Jin-Woo comes with the memory of his own body answering a system which had once seemed to know what it wanted him to become.

He does not try to solve the receiving field by displaying more soldiers than the other king has been allowed to call.

He puts his hand near the surface and examines the relation it offers him.

There is recognition.

Then refusal.

He alters the expression, uses the narrower shape of the inheritance he actually carries and waits for the point at which the response differs from the one Mau had received before entering.

“It knows the lineage,” he says. ᴶ

Rimuru moves closer.

“But?” ᴿ

“It isn't asking whether another monarch can stand here.” ᴶ

The counter has advanced again while they speak.

Jin-Woo looks at Ciel's comparison of the last acknowledged operator signal.

“It reused something older.” ᴶ

* * *

They work on that difference.

No one gets a view of the fight for finding a more promising question. The surface still gives only the title, closed routes, the conditions and time.

The hostile damage modifier has not changed yet.

That is not reassurance.

Shoko asks how quickly the counter is actually moving. Ciel takes enough samples to avoid turning the first frightening impression into the wrong arithmetic.

Rimuru gives the answer aloud.

“Six minutes there for each second here. Six hours for a minute. Fifteen days if we take an hour.” ᴿ

Yuta closes his eyes briefly.

Then opens them and moves the return line to the next position Gojo has indicated.

“Then we don't waste the hour,” he says. ʸᵗ

* * *

Yuka asks about a message.

Anything small enough that the restricted delivery might admit it. Writing on a container. Words carried with water. An answer he might reach for while trying to understand why none of their voices has come through.

It is a good question.

They examine it rather than comforting her with a promise that somebody will surely find a way. The permitted delivery is the already recognized personal shop route. None of them outside can put an object into its closed catalog by holding that object against the gate. Ciel can recognize the relationship without possessing Mau's internal point of purchase or the stock it will accept.

They keep the possibility on the working list.

It has not delivered anything yet.

Yuka takes a small piece of paper anyway.

She writes slowly enough to make the words clear.

*I love you. I'm staying. Come home so we can finish talking.*

Rimuru sees the second sentence when she folds it.

He doesn't ask the question in front of everyone.

* * *

Frieren has gone to Ori before the news becomes a set of frightening guesses passed between people who are trying not to frighten her.

They sit together away from the frame.

Mau went to do his ordinary check. The gate changed after he entered. The people outside are working to open it. They cannot speak to him yet.

Ori holds the edge of her mother's sleeve while she listens.

“Is Dad by himself?” ᴼ

“Noise is with him.” ᶠ

“And Ciel?” ᴼ

“Here. Helping Rimuru.” ᶠ

Ori looks toward the place where the adults keep moving.

She knows what it means for a day inside to become a different amount of time outside. She has eaten supper beneath another sky while someone at home was still waiting for the afternoon to finish.

“Does he know we're trying?” she asks. ᴼ

Frieren puts an arm around her.

“He knows us,” she says. “We are trying to make the rest reach him.” ᶠ

Ori leans against her and keeps holding the sleeve.

* * *

DIO hears the exact account before anyone asks her for an answer.

The ward remains. The ground they are standing on is hers to protect. An instance beyond it has taken the admitted operator and closed the way he should have been allowed to use to leave.

She cannot make her former authority whole by being frightened enough.

She can help identify which part of the construction still asks a question instead of only imposing a condition.

Jin-Woo shows her the response which recognized his lineage and refused his request.

Ciel adds the remembered distinction from the shared room: signal which resembles a source, and the source answering for itself.

DIO looks toward the protected guest accommodation.

Then back at the two people waiting beside her.

“I will ask him,” she says. ᴰ

* * *

Ashborn is standing by the window when she reaches him.

He has heard enough of the changes outside to know this is not an ordinary delayed inspection. He has not assumed that hearing about trouble makes his own wishes irrelevant.

DIO doesn't assume it either.

She tells him the actual question.

An old framework. A source it may still be required to recognize. A request they cannot safely complete by making a copied likeness say it has authority the living original has not chosen to exercise.

“They are not asking you to go back to war,” she says. ᴰ

“They are asking whether I will answer.” ᴬˢ

“Yes.” ᴰ

He looks toward the path beyond the window.

The house is moving around the absence of one of its people. Nobody has made the noise into a demand that the man in this room earn his welcome by fighting.

“Bring me the question itself,” he says. ᴬˢ

* * *

At the frame, Rimuru finds a moment beside Yuka while Ciel and Jin-Woo compare the route they need to present.

“Do you want to tell me?” he asks quietly. ᴿ

Yuka presses the folded paper between her palms.

“I was trying to tell him he could let me be there when he was frightened,” she says. “I said something about not knowing how to stay in this if he wouldn't. I meant the way he keeps making it sound small. The conversation. I wasn't leaving him.” ʸ

Rimuru listens.

“He said he understood.” ʸ

Her voice changes on the last word.

“He might have,” Rimuru says. “We don't know what he heard.” ᴿ

“What if he didn't?” ʸ

Rimuru looks at the paper.

“Then we get you close enough to tell him.” ᴿ

She nods, once, too sharply.

He stays beside her until she can breathe without pressing both hands together.

Then she asks which part of the work she can do next.
