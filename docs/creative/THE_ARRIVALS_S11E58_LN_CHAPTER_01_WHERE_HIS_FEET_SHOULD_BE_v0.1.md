# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 70
## Chapter 10 — Where His Feet Should Be

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 70
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S11E58
**Continuity state:** Local first night through day3 (outside B215 first~12minutes at360x). RCT/rest/ordinary food only partly repair previous side/wrist; no full reset or external healer. New corridor leads onward, return remains closed. Three original constructed humanoid combatants: one uses local DA-like hollow CE mantle to suppress contacted technique, others coordinate ranged/physical attacks; not real named canon characters. Mau recognizes responses/uses available earned kit, no generic strength bypass. Error under coordination/field constraints leads severe lower-back/spinal injury with lost leg function, hemorrhage/rib trauma, no head loss/death reset. Kills opponents/last body pins him. Uses Authority/blades/ordinary supplies to escape/crawl to cover and close immediate bleeding, collapses alive. Two daggers retained/secured, no new Arise/unit or permanent scar cure. Outsiders see no footage of this.

*When: Mau wakes from the first sleep, his left hand has gone cold.*

He looks at it for a long time before moving anything.

The wrist is straighter than it was. Not straight enough. Something in the repair has become firm around a position he would not have chosen if he had been able to finish the work with the body still and another pair of eyes checking what he couldn't see clearly.

He can move the fingers.

The old two resist in the familiar way. The new pain belongs farther up.

“All right,” he says. ᴹ

Noise gives him the retained comparison.

He looks. Corrects a small part. Stops when the effort begins taking more attention than he has recovered from the sleep.

The side wound has closed enough to hold him together.

He eats before deciding that means he is ready.

* * *

The room has a way onward.

It is beyond the place the guardian fell, through a low opening he had not been willing to crawl into while his blood was still reaching the floor faster than the repair could stop it. Noise has watched the approach through the intervals in which Mau let himself sleep.

Nothing has come through.

He marks the alcove with a small piece of cloth, secures the supplies he can physically carry and takes his daggers with him.

Storage still refuses the extra weight.

He could leave everything except the blades and move more quickly. Instead he buys an ordinary pack and puts the water where the good hand can reach it. A sleeping cover. Food. Enough spare cloth that another wound will not make one torn shirt the whole of his available plan.

The shop balance goes down.

He checks it before buying anything else.

This could take longer than an afternoon.

* * *

The first corridor bends back toward a place he remembers.

Not physically. He knows that before he reaches the end. The door is the wrong distance from the turn, and the light beyond it has the grain of something he has carried in his own head rather than a room which has been waiting here all along.

He hears Yuka ask whether he is done.

Mau stops.

For one terrible fraction of a second, the body believes before the mind finishes objecting.

Then he sees the doorway properly.

No color in a person standing there. No person at all. Only the remembered sound offered at the place where he had wanted the next door to lead.

*Memory,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau makes himself breathe.

“I know.” ᴹ

The voice asks again.

He goes past it without answering.

* * *

The next day brings smaller creatures.

Fast, narrow things with joints which close in the wrong direction and blades grown along the arms. He kills them without allowing every contact to become a test of whether the field will make a scratch larger than it ought to be.

Neutral where it is useful. Movement where holding the defense would keep him in a place the next attack has already chosen. Authority to pull an enemy's footing away rather than spending a large release against the part it has armored for him to strike.

He is still good at this.

The realization comes as a relief so clean that he laughs while ducking the last falling body.

The laugh hurts his side.

He accepts both facts.

No door leads out afterward.

He finds another place to sleep.

* * *

On the third day, the field becomes more expensive.

He does not receive a notice explaining the change. He feels the same repair ask for more work. Sees a cut put less of its usual answer into the material it reaches. Finds the simple flight he uses to clear a broken stair pulling more sharply through the available flow than it did in the first chamber.

His body has not become weaker in the middle of a step.

The place is taking more of what he tries to express through it.

He tests a short receiving point for Step on a sound patch of floor. The model cannot validate the field's added boundary cleanly; he refuses the release before it can ask his body to find out which part arrives.

Walking remains possible.

He walks.

* * *

There are three figures in the next hall.

The nearest is broad enough that its unadorned armor makes Mau expect weight before speed. The other two stand apart, leaving lanes between pillars which would make a very competent archer feel welcome.

None speaks.

Mau looks at the energy around the nearest body.

The thin layer is wrong for ordinary reinforcement. Too empty in its center. A working surface intended to receive something other than the force of the hand carrying it.

He knows the shape well enough to recognize the intention.

“Of course,” he says. ᴹ

The nearest figure moves.

* * *

Infinity catches the first fist.

Then the mantle around the arm reaches the contacted technique and begins taking the defense out of the place where the knuckles want to be.

Mau does not wait to see whether it can finish.

He turns aside, closes neutral and puts the right dagger through the space beneath the raised arm. Armor parts. The body behind it twists before the edge can reach the useful line.

From the left, a narrow release cuts through the pillar at Mau's shoulder.

He drops.

The second distant figure fires through the space the fall will leave him if he makes the ordinary recovery.

He goes forward instead, beneath the broad fighter's arm.

Authority catches one of the fallen stones and drives it across the right-hand lane.

The next attack breaks it.

Stone fragments cut across his cheek before neutral returns.

Small wounds.

Too deep.

Mau tastes blood at the corner of his mouth and changes the next plan.

* * *

He cannot let the broad one decide where he stands while the others choose where he will recover.

He takes the farther shooter first.

Not by pretending the room has forgotten there are three of them. He gives the armored figure a thrown dagger it has to avoid with a body the mantle does not make invulnerable. Authority keeps the weapon moving after the physical throw, changing the return line just enough to buy him the outside angle.

He closes neutral for Sky.

The first ranged release follows a surface which carries its apparent path above his shoulder. Mau steps into the place the shooter believed its own attack would keep empty and puts the remaining dagger through the neck.

He drags the body across the second lane as it falls.

The next release tears through its armor.

Mau is already lower.

He gets his thrown weapon back with Authority and runs along the broken pillar before the broad figure can put its mantle between him and the second shooter.

One of the figures is down.

Two remain.

* * *

The broad one is faster than its armor has suggested.

Mau knew it might be. Knowing doesn't create extra space in a corridor already chosen by another person's attack.

The hollow mantle meets Sky at the edge of the fold and unravels the convenient part of the answer. Mau gives it up before the failing technique can become the place his attention gets caught.

He meets the wrist with his physical blade.

The edge cuts.

The arm keeps coming.

The impact turns his injured left wrist under the grip.

Pain makes the next half-second much larger than he can afford.

He releases the weapon into the small shaped hand rather than dropping it entirely. One light object. Close. The grip holds long enough to put the blade behind the broad fighter's other arm.

It notices.

Mau uses the moment of recognition to drive his right knee against the opened plate at its thigh.

The body gives ground.

The distant shooter changes position.

* * *

He sees the new angle.

He has enough time to choose between two bad answers and not enough to manufacture a good one which wasn't ready.

Hold neutral and let the mantle take it at the contact.

Or turn the line with Sky and carry his own body through the physical opening before the next release reaches it.

He chooses the opening.

The fold catches the first shot.

The broad fighter catches him.

Not with both hands. One arm across his upper body, mantle eating the edge of the technique still trying to keep the space useful. Mau turns the dagger into the joint, feels the cut begin and drives it farther.

The shooter fires again.

He moves his head and shoulder clear.

The blow reaches the lower part of his back.

* * *

There is a sound he feels before he hears.

Something in him folding where no part of a standing person should fold.

His legs stop being a place he can put the next instruction.

The world tilts.

He does not fall clear of the armored body. It has his weight against its chest, and the remaining hand is closing toward the side of his face.

Mau drives the dagger under its jaw.

The mantle doesn't stop steel.

The hand strikes him while the point is already moving upward.

His head snaps sideways. Blood fills his mouth. He keeps pushing until the body has no useful answer left to supply through the grip.

Then both of them fall.

* * *

The armor lands across his legs.

He can see that fact.

He cannot feel where the pressure ends.

The last shooter steps into the lane.

Mau's right hand is still around a dagger. The left blade is lying too far away for the shaped grip to be the simplest answer.

He takes it with Authority.

Sends it low.

The figure avoids the line intended for its chest.

The blade was never going there.

It cuts the front of the knee, turns through the correction Authority supplies and takes the leg out of the position the next release requires.

Mau closes his eyes against the pain beginning to climb from his back into the rest of his body.

Opens them.

The fallen shooter is trying to turn toward him.

He puts Dismantle through the exposed throat.

The first line is shallow.

He supplies the second before the head can find the position it needs.

* * *

Silence arrives badly.

Not relief. The sudden absence of attacks leaves him with enough attention to understand what the last ones have done.

He tries to move his feet.

Nothing.

He tries again before he can stop himself.

Nothing.

“No,” he says. ᴹ

The word barely makes it past the blood in his mouth.

Noise is there, bringing the body comparison close enough for him to see the place which has stopped carrying the instruction.

Broken structure. Pressure where it doesn't belong. Another source of blood lower down than the wound he had already learned to keep closed.

He starts RCT.

The effort makes his vision narrow.

*Small,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau shakes his head once.

“Bleeding first.” ᴹ

* * *

He has to get the armor off himself.

Authority lifts one edge. The body shifts, dragging the leg he cannot feel into a position he can see is wrong.

He lets the weight settle again with a sound which is almost a sob and almost anger.

Too much movement at once.

He uses the good hand to work the dagger against the joined plate, cuts what he can reach and lifts the lighter part instead. Inch by inch, enough room to pull the trapped cloth free without using his own knee as the hinge.

The corpse rolls aside.

Mau is left on the floor.

He could fly in a body which can carry itself as one sound shape. Right now the first small lift makes the broken place shift under a weight his legs cannot help him arrange. He kills the attempt before the pain can take the hand he needs for the next one.

The floor is solid beneath him.

He stays against it.

* * *

The pack has torn.

He buys more cloth because reaching it is easier than admitting he has no useful spare piece left. Buys water. A plain padded cover from the ordinary supplies the shop will still deliver.

They arrive close enough that the right hand can catch one edge.

He drags it toward him.

Then himself toward it.

The first movement costs him a sound he doesn't recognize as his own.

He stops with his face against the stone and lets RCT keep working on the bleeding instead of telling the whole body to become well at once.

*Here,* Noise says. ᴺ

“I know.” ᴹ

*Here.* ᴺ

He moves again.

* * *

The sheltered place is behind the base of the broken pillar.

It is not far.

Distance has become an ugly thing to describe that way.

He reaches it by pulling with the right arm, keeping the rest as flat as he can bear and stopping whenever the dark at the edge of his view becomes more interesting than the floor in front of him.

Both daggers come with him. One at his belt. One pulled along by the smallest useful grip of Authority he can keep separate from the work inside his body.

He gets onto the cover in pieces of effort, not in one motion he will later be able to remember as standing up.

The bleeding slows.

That is the next thing he earns.

He can't make the legs answer yet.

* * *

For a little while he remains awake.

There is water beside his hand. A dead enemy beyond the opening. No sound of another body approaching. Noise has the useful senses it can keep watching while he is too tired to ask the same question again.

Mau touches the ring with his thumb.

He remembers Yuka asking him to find her afterward.

“I'm trying,” he says. ᴹ

No one outside hears it.

When the pain finally becomes farther away, it is not because the body is repaired.

He loses the room with his hand still closed around the edge of the cover.
