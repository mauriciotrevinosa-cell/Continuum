## Chapter 13 — Outside

*When: The counter stops at a little past half past ten in the morning, B+216; nobody in the yard has looked away from it for an hour.*

The frame goes dark first.

Not the dark of the path, the old stone going down. Just dark. The words over the surface go out, all of them at once: the title, the restrictions, the modifier at seven, the counter that has run too fast to be wrong for a whole day. The surface becomes a plain empty frame standing in a yard.

At the edge of the ward, the pale window goes out too.

The three figures beside it are gone. Nobody saw them leave.

Rimuru is on his feet.

“Ciel,” he says. “Ciel, what—” ᴿ

Then the light comes.

* * *

It comes from nowhere. A white flash across the stone of the yard in front of the frame, bright enough that half of them throw up an arm, and a sound like a great breath let out.

When the light goes, there are four people on the ground.

* * *

Jin-Woo is standing.

He is the first thing anybody sees, because he's upright: a dagger in each hand, facing outward, his back to the others, in the stance of a man who doesn't yet know whether the fight is finished. His face is grey with dust. There's blood on his sleeve that might not be his.

He sees the yard. He sees Hae-In.

He lowers the daggers.

Behind him, Gojo is on his knees. Yuta is on his knees beside him. Between them, on the stone, there is somebody lying down, and both of them have their hands on him.

* * *

Beru moves before anyone else.

He is simply there, between one breath and the next. Not walking, not running. The bond that has held him at the edge of the light for a whole day, that he has stood beside without sitting down once, opens, and he goes through it the way water goes through a broken dam, and he is on his knees on the stone with the others before Igris has finished turning his head.

“My king,” Beru says. ᴮᵉ

It comes out of him in a voice nobody in the house has heard him use. Not the drama. Not the complaints. Not *the strongest of the shadows*. Something very small.

“My king. My king.” ᴮᵉ

Igris reaches them a moment later and stands over all of them, sword drawn, facing the empty frame, as though daring it to try anything again.

* * *

Shoko is running.

She was already running when the light went. She doesn't stop for anything. She comes down on her knees on the stone at Mau's head, pushing Beru aside without looking at him, and Beru lets himself be pushed, and Yuta takes his hands off Mau's chest so that Shoko can put hers there.

“Talk to me,” Shoko says to Yuta, without looking up. ˢʰ

“I did the ones that'd kill him,” Yuta says. “Not the arm. He told me not the arm. He said you'd have to break it again first.” ʸᵗ

Shoko's hands go still for one heartbeat.

Then they start moving again, faster.

* * *

The rest of the house sees him.

That's the moment. Afterwards, everybody who was in the yard remembers it, and nobody remembers it the same way, and nobody talks about it for a long time.

He is so thin.

That's the first thing. His clothes hang off him like clothes hung on a chair. His wrists, where they come out of his sleeves, are bone with skin over them. His face has fallen in round the eyes and the cheeks.

His hair is long.

A year of it. It has grown past his collar, brown and white, the white not only at the temple any more but all through it, matted and dull and full of grey dust. Somebody, later, will try to remember when they last saw his hair that long, and won't be able to, because they never have.

His face is scabbed all over, raw in places, as though something has rubbed it with a rasp for a very long time. One eye is swollen shut, purple and black. His mouth is bloody. His jaw sits wrong.

His right arm lies across his chest, bent below the elbow in a place where arms don't bend, held there by nothing anyone can see.

His left hand is crooked. His foot is bound into a split boot at an angle that makes Maki, who has seen a great many broken things, look away.

Where his shirt has torn open, there is a long puckered scar across his belly, from his hip to his ribs. There is a fresh one on his thigh. There is a stiff glossy sheet of burned skin down his left side.

His eye is closed.

He isn't moving.

* * *

Ori makes a sound.

Frieren has her. Frieren had her before the light came, one arm round her from behind, because Frieren has lived a very long time and knows what a person looks like before they see something they shouldn't. Ori pulls against her once, hard, and Frieren holds on, and bends down, and says something into her ear that nobody else hears.

Ori stops pulling.

She doesn't stop looking.

* * *

Hae-In has reached Jin-Woo. She doesn't say anything. She takes the daggers out of his hands, one and then the other, and he lets her, and then she puts her arms round him, and he puts his face down into her shoulder.

DIO is standing very still beside Ashborn. Her hand is over her mouth.

Ashborn is looking at the boy on the ground. At the bones in his wrists and the long hair and the closed eye. His old face doesn't move. But his hand comes up, slowly, and rests flat over his own chest, the way Mau used to rest his over his belly in the dark.

Rimuru is on his knees beside Shoko. Mai is holding Momo's hand so hard that Momo has stopped feeling her fingers. Fern has come out of the kitchen with a cloth still in her hands and stopped in the doorway and not moved since.

Gojo is still on his knees. He hasn't taken his hand from under Mau's head.

He looks up.

He looks across the yard, past Shoko, past Beru, past all of them, to the low wall at the far end, where the lamplight didn't reach last night.

* * *

Yuka is already moving.

She doesn't remember standing up. She doesn't remember crossing the yard. She will never remember it, afterwards, no matter how hard she tries: only that one moment she was at the far end of the wall with her hands pressed together over nothing, because she gave her paper away, and the next she was close enough to see his hair.

She sees all of it.

The thinness. The scars. The arm. The eye. The hair, long, brown and white, full of dust.

She sees his chest move. Once. Then again.

She sees Gojo looking at her, and something in his face that she doesn't understand yet, and Yuta beside him with one hand pressed flat over the inside pocket of his coat.

Then Shoko says, without looking up, *give her room*, and Beru moves, and Rimuru moves, and there is a space on the stone beside Mau's head that wasn't there a moment ago.

Yuka goes down onto her knees in it.
