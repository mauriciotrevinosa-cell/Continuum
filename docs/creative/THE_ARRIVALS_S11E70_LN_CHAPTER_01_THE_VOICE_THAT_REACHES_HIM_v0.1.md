# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 71
## Chapter 14 — The Voice That Reaches Him

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 71
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S11E70
**Continuity state:** B+216, immediately after V71#13 in the yard. Ciel's contact with Mau resumes after the instance closes; she had no access to his interior during captivity. Noise shares an injury sequence for treatment, not the whole memory archive. Yuka reaches Mau, clarifies the morning's words and tells him she is staying. He briefly answers with an automatic reassurance that does not reflect what he believed inside. Shoko directs urgent stabilisation and transfer; wrong repairs remain for correction. Yuta still carries the unread paper. No new powers or units; 6,617 bonds.

*When: Yuka kneels on the stone, and Shoko tells her where she can put her hand.*

“Here. Beside his head. Let him find you.” ˢʰ

Yuka puts her hand on the stone first.

She has been wanting to touch him since the light went out. Now he is in front of her and she cannot find a place that doesn't look hurt.

There is dirt caught in the crease under his ear. His hair lies across it, stiff in places. Yesterday morning she pushed that same hair away from his face to kiss him before he left their room. Yesterday it had stopped above his collar.

Her hand moves off the stone.

She touches the patch of skin behind his ear with two fingers, very lightly, and then cups the back of his head above Gojo's supporting hand.

“Mau.” ʸ

No answer.

Shoko is talking. There are instructions going to Yuta, to Rimuru, to someone behind Yuka who is already bringing something. Beru's hands hover near Mau's chest until Shoko directs them lower, keeping them away from the terrible angle of the right arm.

“Hold there. Don't spread it. Stay with what I'm doing.” ˢʰ

Beru nods.

He doesn't give a speech. He doesn't even say yes.

Yuka strokes one thumb along the uninjured edge of Mau's ear.

“I'm here.” ʸ

His chest rises, catches, comes down.

She watches for the next breath.

* * *

There is a sound he hasn't heard in a long time.

Not quite a sound.

Something reaches a place in him that has been empty so long he has learned to move around it. It is faint at first, interrupted by pain, by the warm pressure trying to get under his ribs, by the feeling that his arm is floating away from him.

*Mau.* ᶜ

He tries to turn towards it.

Something holds his head still.

*Please don't move. I am here.* ᶜ

He knows that voice.

He knows the careful space she leaves between the instructions, the way she says his name when she wants him to listen before she has to ask again.

He tries to answer. His jaw makes a bright, tearing pain. There is a hand near his mouth and someone tells him not to force it.

*You don't need to speak to me out loud.* ᶜ

He has forgotten that.

For a moment, he cannot remember how not to.

“Ciel?” ᴹ

It scarcely gets past his teeth.

*Yes.* ᶜ

He opens his eye.

The light hurts. Everything in reach is grey and moving. Above him is a face he knows, white hair, a cut under the nose. Another face comes into view, and another. He can't keep them in place.

“Ciel.” ᴹ

*Yes, Mau. I'm here.* ᶜ

Her voice breaks on the last word.

He starts crying before he understands why. It pulls at his cheek and hurts. He cannot stop, and then he cannot get enough air, and the warm hands on his chest change what they are doing.

*One thing at a time. Let Shoko help you breathe.* ᶜ

He called her in the dark. He called her beside the dragon when there was something wrong under his hand and he didn't know how to hold it in. He called her after he knew she couldn't answer, because saying her name was something he could still do.

*I called.*

The thought reaches her this time.

There is no need to open his mouth. There is no need to make the sound big enough to cross a room.

*I know you tried. I could not reach you.* ᶜ

*I didn't—*

He loses the rest of it.

*You have reached me now.* ᶜ

Someone takes a breath very close to him. Rimuru, on the other side of Shoko, presses a hand over his mouth for a moment and then lowers it.

“She's here,” Rimuru says. “Both of us. Right here.” ᴿ

Mau tries to nod.

The hand under his head stops him gently.

“You can believe that without demonstrating it,” Gojo says. ᴳ

He sounds terrible. Mau thinks he ought to ask what happened to him.

Then something bright moves into view.

* * *

Yuka feels him notice her.

His eye moves, slowly, past Shoko's sleeve, past the cloth in her other hand, and finds Yuka's face.

He stops trying to turn his head.

“Hi,” she says. ʸ

It comes out so small. She has said his name in the yard, shouted it at a door, spoken it over and over into her own hands all night, and now all she can give him is *hi*.

His lips move.

“Don't. You don't need to.” ʸ

He tries anyway.

She leans down enough that he can see her without lifting his eye, keeping her weight off him, one hand still above Gojo's.

“I'm here. I've been here.” ʸ

Something changes in his face. She cannot tell what. The swollen side barely moves; the other side crumples in a way she will think about later, when she has somewhere to put it.

“You…” ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

His left hand shifts on the stone. It doesn't come far. The wrist sits wrong and his fingers open with a hesitation she knows, made worse by something new.

She lays her hand beside it, palm up. Shoko looks, adjusts Yuka's fingers beneath his so his wrist doesn't have to turn.

He rests there.

Not a grip. Just the weight he can give her.

“I need you to hear something,” Yuka says. “You don't have to answer. Yesterday morning—what I said. I didn't mean I wanted you gone. I don't want you gone.” ʸ

She has practised this in so many ways. Every one of them required more time than Shoko's moving hands will allow.

“I wanted you to let me be with you. When it's bad, too. That's what I meant. I'm sorry I said it like that.” ʸ

His eye closes.

“I'm staying, Mau. I love you. I'm your wife. I'm here.” ʸ

He makes a little sound, almost a word, and she waits because she cannot help it, because she has been waiting for his voice for a whole day and he has been waiting for hers for something she cannot yet comprehend.

“Knew,” he whispers. ᴹ

His mouth barely shapes it.

“First… second. Knew.” ᴹ

Yuta looks up.

Gojo doesn't.

Yuka bends her head until her forehead is close to Mau's, without touching the injured skin.

“Then I'll tell you again later.” ʸ

Her tears hit the back of her own hand. She moves it so they won't fall on him.

“I'll tell you as many times as you want.” ʸ

* * *

“Yuka.” ˢʰ

Shoko waits until she has Yuka's eyes.

“We're moving him inside. I need this space back for a moment. You can walk beside him.” ˢʰ

Yuka nods at once.

She tells Mau before she moves her hand.

“I'm getting up. I'm coming with you.” ʸ

His fingers drag a fraction across her palm.

She stays until Shoko has placed his hand safely against the support beside his body. Then she stands, and Maki is there, steadying her under the elbow without making anything of it.

There is a stretcher. There has been one waiting all morning. Yuka remembers seeing it and refusing to picture him on it.

Shoko gives the count. Gojo, Yuta and Maki move together. Beru keeps his hands exactly where he has been told to keep them, and Rimuru walks with the stretcher, listening to something Yuka cannot hear.

Mau's face tightens during the transfer. A sound gets out of him.

“I know,” Shoko says. “We're going to stop moving in a moment.” ˢʰ

He is still trying to help. Yuka sees it in the useless little brace of his shoulder, in the way his hand searches for an edge to push against.

“Let us,” Maki says, close to him. “We have you.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He gives up the effort.

Yuka takes the place Shoko leaves beside his head.

They pass Ori.

Frieren has loosened her arms, though she hasn't let go. Ori stands inside them with her hands closed on Frieren's sleeves. Her eyes follow Mau's face.

Yuka doesn't tell her he is all right.

“We're taking him to Shoko's room,” she says. “We'll tell you when you can come in.” ʸ

Ori nods, once, with her whole head.

Mau's eye moves towards her voice, misses, finds only Frieren's pale hair. He tries to lift his left hand again.

“She knows,” Frieren tells him. “You don't have to reach.” ᶠ

His hand settles.

* * *

Noise has gone very quiet.

Ciel doesn't ask for everything. There is too much wrong with his body, too little of what she can see making sense against the record from yesterday morning.

*The injuries,* she asks. *Which came first. What you had to hold in place. Can you show me that?* ᶜ

*Not all?* ᴺ

*Only what Shoko needs to treat him. We can stop there.* ᶜ

A pause.

Then a sequence begins.

Not a year of rooms. Not his voice in the dark. The positions of breaks, the failed joins, what he held shut with one hand while trying to heal with the other. Ciel meets each piece and passes the part Shoko needs through Rimuru. The rest stays with Noise.

Shoko asks Rimuru one question.

He answers.

She asks another, and doesn't get the answer she wants.

Her mouth tightens. She tells Yuta where to put his hands when they reach the room.

Mau watches the passage slide past above him.

There are beams he knows. A place in the plaster where someone has marked the height of a shelf. The sound of several people stopping themselves from following all at once.

Nobody asks him to walk.

Yuka is beside him. Every time the light changes, she is still in colour.

He thinks he ought to tell her he loves her. He thinks he might already have said it. He can't find the order of anything.

*Ciel?*

*I'm here.* ᶜ

*Don't go.*

*I'm staying with you.* ᶜ

He looks for Yuka again.

She is already looking back.

“I love you,” she says, before he has to work out how to ask. ʸ

His eye closes before they reach the bed.
