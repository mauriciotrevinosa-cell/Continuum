# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 71
## Chapter 15 — What Has to Be Set Right

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 71
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S11E70
**Continuity state:** B+216, late morning into early afternoon. Clinic stabilisation continues. Shoko leads correction, with Yuta and Beru contributing under her direction; Ciel and Noise provide the requested injury record. Mau consents during a brief waking interval to sleep and receive help. The recently broken right forearm is aligned before repair; the jaw is corrected. The older spine, wrist, foot, burn restrictions and starvation require staged care, not an instant reset. His existing visual and hand sequelae are not cured. Personal belongings, including the tooth, are retained; Ori's original folded message remains safely in the home drawer. Rescuers also receive care. 6,617 bonds.

*When: Shoko shuts the clinic door and asks everybody inside to say what they are doing before they touch him.*

The boot will not come off.

Maki has undone everything that ought to undo. There is a place where the leather has been cut and tied back together, then cut again when the shape underneath changed. The sole no longer sits beneath the foot.

She stops pulling before Shoko has to tell her.

“Cut it,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

Maki cuts the boot away.

Yuka turns her head. She hadn't meant to. One moment she is watching Maki's hands and the next she is looking at the wall with Mau's left fingers resting on her own.

She makes herself look back.

Shoko notices.

“You don't have to watch every part.” ˢʰ

“I want to stay.” ʸ

“I know. That's not what I said.” ˢʰ

Yuka swallows and nods.

The foot is wrapped before she looks down again. Maki lays the ruined boot aside with his clothes.

* * *

They uncover him in sections.

A blanket over what they are not treating. Clean cloth under what they are. There is no moment when the whole room stands looking at everything the dungeon did to his body.

It is worse, somehow, to discover it in pieces.

Shoko lifts an edge of cloth and stops speaking for long enough that Yuka knows before she sees anything. Then Shoko gives another instruction in her ordinary voice.

Yuta follows it. Beru follows it. Rimuru moves the lamp.

Mau's breathing eases a little after the work at his ribs. It is the first improvement Yuka can recognise without somebody explaining it to her.

She watches his chest for three breaths, then four.

“That part's better?” she asks. ʸ

“That part,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

Yuka holds on to the distinction.

* * *

There is something in his pocket.

Maki finds it when she folds the remains of his trousers away from his leg. Small, hard, wrapped in a torn piece of cloth. She puts it in the shallow dish beside his ring and the other things they have taken off him to keep them safe.

Then she unfolds the cloth.

Nobody speaks for a moment.

“He kept it,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

He is listening to Noise.

Maki folds it closed again. She places it beside the ring instead of in the bowl for things to throw away.

Yuka looks at the bundle.

She has seen him come home with crystals, coins, strange weapons that needed a whole discussion before anybody was allowed to touch them. He has pulled ridiculous sweets out of his shop and watched Umaru pretend they were a matter of national security.

This time he has brought back a tooth.

She lowers her face over his hand. Her lips touch the unbroken part of his fingers.

“We're keeping your things,” she tells him. “All right? Nobody's losing anything.” ʸ

His daggers are already laid safely aside. The battered pack sits under Maki's chair. Nothing in it is being opened for curiosity.

Mau doesn't answer.

* * *

He wakes when Shoko examines his right arm.

Not all the way. His eye opens and he makes a movement with his shoulder that hurts before it gets anywhere.

“It's Shoko.” ˢʰ

He stares at her.

She waits until his eye settles on her face.

“Yuta told me what you said about your arm. You were right. I need to set it properly before we finish healing it.” ˢʰ

He tries to look down.

“You don't need to inspect my work in advance.” ˢʰ

His gaze returns to her.

Something almost familiar passes through it. The tiny beginning of an answer. It is gone before it becomes a sound.

“I want to help you sleep while we do this. I don't want you trying to hold it together for me.” ˢʰ

His fingers move against Yuka's palm.

*Let them,* Ciel says. *I have the medical record Noise gave me. You do not have to remember all the positions.* ᶜ

He is trying to remember anyway. There had been a place at the back of the bone where he couldn't quite get the ends to meet. He had found a way to keep the wrist from falling sideways. There was something he mustn't release until—

*Shoko has it,* Ciel tells him. ᶜ

He looks at the arm.

Yuta's hands are already supporting it. Shoko's are beside his. Nothing is waiting for Mau to become strong enough to finish on his own.

“Can you let us help?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

He takes a breath.

“Stay?” ᴹ

Yuka bends close enough to be in view.

“I'm staying.” ʸ

His eye moves to Shoko again. He gives the smallest movement she will accept as a nod.

She touches the uninjured side of his forehead.

“Good. That's your part done for now.” ˢʰ

* * *

Sleep does not look peaceful at first.

Even after what Shoko gives him begins to work, his body keeps trying to brace. His left leg goes rigid. His fingers draw in. There is a moment when Beru looks towards the door as though the thing hurting Mau must be coming through it.

“Here,” Shoko tells him, and brings his attention back. “With me.” ˢʰ

He lowers his head.

Yuta shapes the repair Shoko asks for, stops where she asks, waits while she corrects what has already joined badly. Beru maintains the support they have assigned him. His claws do not shake while he is working.

They shake when Shoko tells him to rest his hands.

Yuka has turned her chair so that she is facing Mau's good side. Maki stands between her and the treatment for a few minutes. Yuka understands what Maki is doing and lets her.

She keeps talking, quietly, about where they are.

The door is shut. Rimuru is here. Gojo has gone to sit where Shoko can see him when he comes back. His ring is beside the bed. She hasn't taken hers off.

She runs out of things that feel small enough to say.

“You were supposed to come find me in the afternoon,” she whispers. “I was going to be on the sofa. I had your side.” ʸ

Maki looks at her.

Yuka shakes her head once. Not now. If Maki touches her shoulder now she will stop being able to make the words.

“It's still yours,” she tells Mau. ʸ

Shoko finishes with the arm.

For the first time since he appeared in the yard, it rests in a shape Yuka recognises.

* * *

The jaw takes more work.

Shoko explains before she begins, even though Mau is asleep. Yuka isn't sure whether she is explaining for him, for her, or because Shoko needs to keep doing things in the order she knows.

Yuta is pale by the time she steps back.

Rimuru has noticed before anybody asks. He puts a chair behind him and tells him to sit.

“I'm not finished,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“Neither am I,” Shoko says. “Sit down so we can both keep going later.” ˢʰ

He sits.

There are more injuries waiting. The wrist. The foot. The place in Mau's back where the record from Noise makes Ciel go silent for several seconds.

And beneath them, a body that has been using almost everything it had to remain a body. Mending the biggest visible breaks will not put back the meals he missed or make all the old repairs safe to pull apart at once.

“We do this in stages,” Shoko says. “He needs rest between them. So do you.” ˢʰ

Yuta looks at the bed, then at his own hands.

He nods.

* * *

Outside, Hae-In has persuaded Jin-Woo to sit.

It took less persuasion than she expected. Now that someone has put a cup into his hand, he is holding it with both of his and watching the water settle.

She has already seen the marks on his wrists, the exhaustion he carried out under all that readiness to fight. Nine days did not pass for her. They did for him.

“Drink,” she says. ᴴᶦ

He does.

Gojo sits two chairs away with a clean cloth under his nose. He hasn't put his blindfold back on. Mai stands in front of him holding a plate he has not taken.

“If you say you don't need it,” she says, “I'm finding Shoko.” ᴹᵃⁱ

“That seems excessive.” ᴳ

“Try me.” ᴹᵃⁱ

He takes the plate.

Mai doesn't leave until he eats something.

Jin-Woo watches him do it, then reaches for the food Hae-In has brought him.

For a little while they sit without trying to tell one another anything useful.

* * *

Rimuru catches Shoko washing her hands.

She is standing over the basin, rubbing a place on one finger that is already clean. He doesn't point that out.

“His arm?” he asks. ᴿ

“Set. It will need checking when he's awake.” ˢʰ

“And the rest?” ᴿ

Shoko looks towards the bed.

Yuka is still in the chair. Mau's face has changed now that the jaw is where it belongs. He looks more like himself. That makes the rest of it harder to look at.

“He spent a long time doing whatever let him keep going,” Shoko says. “Some of it saved his life. Some of it healed in a position he should never have had to live with.” ˢʰ

She dries her hands.

“I can work with that. I need him here, and I need time.” ˢʰ

Rimuru nods.

He doesn't ask her to promise.

* * *

Noise is still waiting for something to go wrong.

The pressure of the field is gone. It has checked. The room under Mau's ribs has not taken him away. The six thousand six hundred and seventeen bonds answer, and Ciel answers, and outside there are people whose hands know where to go.

Every time Shoko pauses, Noise prepares to hold something shut again.

*She's stopping to let him rest,* Ciel tells it. ᶜ

*He stops. It gets worse.* ᴺ

*It did there.* ᶜ

Noise waits.

Shoko changes the cloth under Mau's shoulder. She asks Beru to move his hand, and Beru moves it, and nothing comes through the wall while they do.

*I'll tell you what she is doing,* Ciel says. *You can ask me.* ᶜ

Noise stays close to Mau.

But when the next pause comes, it asks.
