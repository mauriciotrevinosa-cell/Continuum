# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 71
## Chapter 17 — Her Side of the Bed

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 71
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S11E71
**Continuity state:** B+216, late afternoon. Mau wakes for a longer interval after first corrective care. Speech and movement remain limited. Yuka reads her message with his agreement, directly addresses the guest-room assumption and stays physically close without climbing over injured areas. Gojo's disclosure is not hidden from Mau. He begins admitting that he did not know she wanted him back; the whole year and its minimisation are not resolved in this conversation. Ciel remains connected, Noise inside, finite medical recovery continuing. No new power or units.

*When: He wakes with his arm somewhere he doesn't remember putting it.*

His right hand is too far away.

For a moment that is the thing that frightens him most. He has learned the distance between his elbow and his fingers. He can find the weak place without looking, knows where to brace when he has to use the hand, knows how to keep a blade from dragging it out of line.

Now the fingers are somewhere else.

He tries to lift them.

*They have corrected the alignment,* Ciel says. *Please leave the support where it is.* ᶜ

Her voice startles him so badly that his whole body tries to move.

Pain stops most of it.

*It's Ciel. You are in the clinic. Shoko is here.* ᶜ

He remembers her, and then remembers remembering her before. He thinks of the yard, though when he tries to picture it there is only light and someone's hand behind his head.

Something shifts beside the bed.

“Hey.” ʸ

Yuka.

She puts down the cup she has been holding and leans into his view. There is a mark along her cheek where she has rested it against a seam in her sleeve. Her eyes look sore.

“Don't try to sit up. I'll get Shoko.” ʸ

He makes a sound before she can straighten.

She stops.

“She's just there. I'm not leaving the room.” ʸ

He follows her glance.

Shoko is washing a cup at the little basin. She turns before Yuka says her name.

He had not looked far enough to see her.

* * *

The questions are simple.

His name. Where the pain is different. Whether he can feel Shoko touching his foot. She asks for very small movements and stops him when he tries to make them larger.

When she reaches his left hand, he looks down at the familiar stiffness in the two fingers and finds it still there, beneath the new ache of the wrist.

Some part of him had expected the dungeon to have changed that, too.

Shoko notices where he is looking.

“We're working from what was yours before,” she says. “I have the record.” ˢʰ

He looks at her face.

It is difficult to know how long to look at people. In the last room, if he studied anything for long enough, the room learned something back.

Shoko waits without changing the question.

“Can you feel that?” ˢʰ

“Yes.” ᴹ

His voice sounds unfamiliar. The jaw is where it belongs, but speaking pulls at places that have not finished healing.

“Good. Short answers for a while. You don't need to help me by making it sound better.” ˢʰ

He hadn't known he was doing that yet.

* * *

Shoko lets Yuka help him with a little water.

He reaches for the cup first. His fingers miss it. He tries again, and Yuka moves it into a place he can reach instead of putting it back on the table.

The effort makes his hand tremble.

“I can hold it,” she offers. ʸ

He looks at her.

“You can tell me when to stop.” ʸ

He lets her.

The water is ordinary. It doesn't come with a price in the corner of his sight. He swallows carefully, stops because she sees him flinch, then asks for a little more.

Shoko checks him again before she moves to the other end of the room.

Yuka stays where he can see her.

“How long?” he asks. ᴹ

“It's late afternoon.” ʸ

He stares.

“You came out this morning. You slept while Shoko worked.” ʸ

He tries to fit that around the light in the yard, around Gojo's face, around a hand pressing something warm into his chest.

“Yesterday?” ᴹ

She understands after a moment.

“You went in yesterday morning.” ʸ

He closes his eye.

Yuka leaves the cup on the table. She doesn't tell him it is over as though that will make the numbers agree.

* * *

When he looks again, her hand is beside his.

He has been thinking about that hand for so long that the little dent of the ring feels impossible. It is real. Her fingers are warm where they rest against his palm. There is a small mark near her nail that wasn't there when he left.

He wants to ask how it happened.

He wants to put his face into her shoulder and never see another room.

Instead he says, “You can…” ᴹ

She waits.

He looks towards the door.

“Guest room. When…” ᴹ

His throat fails him. He swallows, winces, gives up trying to finish.

Yuka doesn't make him.

“Gojo told me what you asked him to say.” ʸ

His eye moves back to her.

“About me not having to be at the door.” ʸ

He looks ashamed. Not suddenly afraid of her anger. Something smaller and older, as though a thing he tried to put out of sight has fallen where everyone can see it.

She moves her chair closer.

“I was waiting at the door because I wanted you to come out. I wanted to be the person you saw. I was angry I couldn't go in with them.” ʸ

He watches her mouth.

“I don't want the guest room. I want our room. With you. And until we can go there, I want this chair.” ʸ

His lips part.

She lifts her free hand a little, not to stop him but to show him the paper she is taking from her pocket.

“I wrote something while you were inside. Grandpa carried it. He brought it back to me.” ʸ

The folds open between them.

“Can I read it to you?” ʸ

He nods before remembering what that costs.

She smooths the paper against her knee.

“I love you. I'm staying. Come home so we can finish talking.” ʸ

He doesn't move.

She gives him time to look at the words himself. His eye travels slowly across the lines. Once it goes back to the beginning.

“That's what I meant before you went in,” she says. “I said it badly. I wanted to stay with you in the parts you kept trying to do without me.” ʸ

His fingers move a fraction against hers.

“I didn't…” ᴹ

She leans closer so he doesn't have to push the sound.

“Didn't know.” ᴹ

It hurts him more than the movement of his jaw.

Yuka presses the heel of her free hand to her own eye. Just once, hard. When she lowers it, she is still looking at him.

“I know. Gojo told me. You don't have to say you did.” ʸ

He tries to breathe in too much at once.

“I'm sorry,” she says. “For that sentence. For giving you something that could sound like that. I wish I'd found different words.” ʸ

His mouth begins to form the answer he always gives when she looks hurt.

She kisses the part of his hand she can reach safely.

“You don't have to take care of me through this bit.” ʸ

The answer stops.

For a while there is only the effort it takes him to breathe without sobbing too hard.

* * *

He says her name three times.

The first is almost soundless. The second catches. The third she answers with her forehead resting against the uninjured edge of his hand.

“Yes.” ʸ

He had rehearsed things to tell her. Reasonable things. A place he could sleep that wouldn't make it difficult for Ori, a way to give Yuka the space he had finally understood she wanted, a version of being nearby that would not require her to look at him every morning.

None of it fits the chair she has pulled against his bed.

He cannot make himself throw it all away at once. There has been too much time to practise it.

“Still?” he asks. ᴹ

Yuka lifts her head.

“I still want you.” ʸ

She touches her own ring with her thumb.

“I still want the life we were making. I want you to stop hiding the parts that hurt. We'll talk about how. Not all of it today.” ʸ

He looks towards Shoko, as if to find out whether being allowed to stop is something the room can confirm.

Shoko is writing down what she needs to check next. She doesn't pretend not to have heard.

“Not all of it today,” she agrees. ˢʰ

Mau's eye closes again. Tears get past the lashes.

Yuka catches them with the corner of a clean cloth. She is very careful near the swollen side.

“I missed you,” he manages. ᴹ

“I missed you, too.” ʸ

There is no useful way to compare what they mean.

They don't try.

* * *

He tires while she is folding the paper.

She notices because his fingers stop answering hers, then because his eye loses the little point of focus it has been trying to keep on her face.

“Do you want me to put this away?” ʸ

He looks at the paper.

“Here.” ᴹ

She puts it beside his ring, where he can see the folded edge.

“Here.” ʸ

He takes a breath, waits for the pain to settle, then tries one more question.

“You ate?” ᴹ

Yuka laughs. It comes out wet and disbelieving.

“Grandma made sure.” ʸ

His mouth moves at one corner.

“Good.” ᴹ

She kisses his forehead where Shoko has shown her there is unbroken skin.

“You're very difficult to argue with right now.” ʸ

Another little movement at the corner of his mouth. Almost him.

“Advantage.” ᴹ

“I'll remember that.” ʸ

She settles back into the chair without letting go of his hand.

He keeps checking that she is there until sleep makes it impossible.
