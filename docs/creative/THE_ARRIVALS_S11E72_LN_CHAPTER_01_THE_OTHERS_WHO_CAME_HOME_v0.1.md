Shoko stops in the doorway.

His head is against the wall, his empty plate still balanced on his knees. The clean cloth has slipped from his hand. One foot is planted as if he intends to get up at any moment.

She takes the plate before it can slide.

He wakes at the movement.

“I'm awake.” ᴳ

“That wasn't the requirement.” ˢʰ

“He's sleeping. Yuka's with him. Beru's with him. Rimuru knows where to find you.” ˢʰ

Gojo rubs a hand over his face. His fingers stop at the place under his nose that keeps opening when he forgets and touches it.

Shoko sits beside him.

For a moment she is only there, holding his plate. Then she puts it on the floor and takes the hand he has lowered to his knee.

“Nine days,” she says. ˢʰ

“A few of them were quite boring.” ᴳ

“I'm here.” ᴳ

“I can see that.” ˢʰ

“I think you're meant to say something warmer.” ᴳ

“I saw you come out on your knees,” she says. “All I could see was the blood under your nose, and you were bent over him, and for a second I couldn't tell whose it was.” ˢʰ

His fingers close a little more firmly.

“Mine. Mostly. The nose was all mine.” ᴳ

“Satoru.” ˢʰ

“I know.” ᴳ

Then she kisses him, briefly, with one hand against his cheek.

“Lie down,” she says. ˢʰ

“You, too. When Yuta's rested.” ᴳ

“I'll take a turn.” ˢʰ

Yuta has managed to keep moving a little longer.

Maki finds him checking the closure on the inside pocket of his coat. The pocket is empty. He opens it, looks, closes it again.

“You gave it to her,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

“I know.” ʸᵗ

“Then it can survive a wash.” ᴹᵃⁱ

There is a shallow cut along his forearm that he hasn't bothered finishing. Mai sees it. Maki sees it. He realises they have both seen it and looks almost apologetic.

“It didn't matter down there.” ʸᵗ

“We're not down there.” ᴹᵃᵏ

After a while he says, “He knew what was wrong with his arm.” ʸᵗ

“He was barely looking at me. I touched it and he told me not to finish. He knew exactly how he'd put it together wrong.” ʸᵗ

Suho wants to hear about the path.

Jin-Woo can see him trying not to ask. He sits beside his mother with his hands between his knees, watching his father eat the food that has already gone cold once and been warmed again.

“Later,” Hae-In says, without Suho saying a word. ᴴᶦ

“I didn't—” ˢᵘ

He stops. Looks at Jin-Woo.

“Later?” he asks instead. ˢᵘ

“Yes,” Jin-Woo says. “Not tonight.” ᴶ

Suho nods.

From somewhere behind Jin-Woo's chair, his own Beru makes a low, unhappy sound. The other Beru has not left Mau's room. Jin-Woo has felt the attention of his shadow following that closed door all afternoon.

“He's being helped,” Jin-Woo tells him. ᴶ

“I know, my king.” ᴮᵉ

“You can sit.” ᴶ

Hae-In puts her hand over Jin-Woo's where it rests on the table.

Jin-Woo looks at her hand. At his son beside him, at the second cup waiting within reach, at the shadow who has decided to keep his own worried watch from the floor.

He takes another bite before it can get cold again.

“His hand was cold,” Ori says. ᴼ

“Shoko knows. They're keeping him warm.” ᶠ

“He couldn't say it back.” ᴼ

“He meant that.” ᶠ

“I know.” ᴼ

“I wanted him to hear it anyway.” ᴼ

When Umaru comes to ask whether Ori wants company, she brings Rem and two places' worth of food with her. She sees the three of them holding the table together and doesn't announce anything.

DIO waits until Rimuru has finished speaking with Shoko.

“Can you ask whether Noise wants to talk?” she says. “It can say no.” ᴰ

He looks at the bed through the partly open door, listens, then nods for her to come closer.

Mau is sleeping. Yuka has her eyes closed in the chair, though her hand is still beneath his. Beru is beside the far side of the bed. Nobody disturbs them.

Rimuru speaks softly.

“Ciel says it can hear us. And yes.” ᴿ

For once she has not brought a dramatic entrance, or a complaint for Mau, or a joke that gives Noise a new thing to imitate.

“Hello, friend,” she says. ᴰ

“It says, 'Friend.'” ᴿ

“I'm glad you're both here.” ᴰ

For a while it says nothing else.
