# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 71
## Chapter 19 — Until Morning

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S11 v0.1
**Volume:** 71
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S11E72
**Continuity state:** B+216 night, first night after return. Shoko plans further corrective care rather than claiming completion; minor regenerated skin differs from old marks, but major scars and baseline visual/hand sequelae remain. Yuka sleeps beside him in a separate bed pulled within safe reach; proximity is not withheld as punishment and she does not climb over unstable injuries. Ciel and Beru keep the agreed watch; Noise can share responsibility. Mau briefly tells Yuka he loves her and wants her close. No new floating/slime/body test while medically unstable. 6,617 bonds.

*When: He asks what happens if he sleeps too long, and Shoko tells him she has an opinion about that.*

The second time he wakes in the evening, the lamp is on.

He hasn't seen anyone light it. He doesn't remember the daylight getting smaller at the window. For a few seconds he is certain he has missed something he was meant to do.

Then he sees the paper beside his ring.

His eye goes to the chair.

Yuka is there with a bowl in her hands. She notices him looking before he can try to move.

“Hi. Shoko said you'd be hungry.” ʸ

He is. It arrives all at once, a hollow ache so familiar he had stopped thinking of it as a request his body was making.

He looks at the bowl.

“She also said slowly,” Yuka adds. ʸ

“Traitor.” ᴹ

The word is rough and soft. Yuka looks up in surprise, and then he sees the laugh arrive in her face.

It makes him want to cry again.

She gets Shoko to check him before she offers him anything. He resents the delay for one brief, ordinary moment, and is so relieved to feel something that simple that he nearly laughs with her.

* * *

He cannot eat as much as he wants.

Shoko explains. Yuka follows the explanation even when he looks at her in a way that used to get him a second helping before dinner.

“That doesn't work here.” ʸ

“Worked before.” ᴹ

“Different government.” ʸ

He looks past her at Shoko.

Shoko folds her arms.

“Appeal denied.” ˢʰ

He gives up with a small sound that might have been a laugh if it hadn't hurt. Yuka waits for it to pass before offering the next little spoonful.

He swallows. Closes his eye. Opens it to make sure there is still more.

She is holding the bowl in the same hands he has been thinking about for a year.

Once, when the spoon comes too close to his face before he is ready, he goes completely still.

Yuka stops.

“Too much?” ʸ

He doesn't answer immediately.

Her hand stays where it is, not advancing. Shoko looks up from the other end of the room.

“Mau?” ʸ

He looks at Yuka's face, then at the bowl, then at the clean cloth on her knee.

“Just… slow.” ᴹ

“Okay.” ʸ

She waits for him to tell her he is ready.

He doesn't know how to explain what stopped him. There is too much in the way, too many different rooms with her face in them, and the real woman in front of him has not asked for any of those rooms tonight.

He takes the next spoonful when he can.

* * *

Shoko checks the old and new repairs before she lets him settle.

He watches her through the eye that opens. She lifts the blanket only where she needs to. At his side, there is a place where he expects to see a cluster of small old marks and finds different skin instead, pale to him, tight where the newer burn has drawn it together.

His hand starts towards it.

“Some of it was replaced when you healed,” Shoko says. “I've compared it with what we had before.” ˢʰ

He looks farther down. The great old scar is still where he remembers it. The deeper lines he knows by touch have not all gone away because newer damage passed over them.

Neither has the stiffness in his fingers. The edge of his vision is still unreliable when he tries to follow Shoko's hand too far to the left.

She covers him again.

“Your back and foot need more work. We won't do everything in one sitting.” ˢʰ

His eye returns to her face.

“How much?” ᴹ

“I'll tell you before each part.” ˢʰ

He waits for the number anyway. The number doesn't come.

Shoko sits so he doesn't have to look up at her.

“You don't have to work out tonight how you're going to get through all of it. I'll be here when we do the next bit. So will Yuta. Beru's already arguing with the concept of taking a turn.” ˢʰ

From the far side of the bed comes a very quiet objection.

“I am not arguing.” ᴮᵉ

Shoko looks at him.

“You were doing it silently.” ˢʰ

Beru lowers his head.

Mau's mouth moves at the corner.

* * *

Ashborn comes to the doorway before the room is made ready for the night.

He asks Shoko first. A few minutes, no need for Mau to answer anything.

Mau follows him with his eye as he approaches. He remembers a mask. A voice explaining what could stand where he stood. The empty place beneath his ribs, offering him somewhere to put down a weight he could no longer carry.

Ashborn is not standing in that place.

He is standing beside the bed, keeping enough distance that Mau doesn't have to lean back to see him.

“I am glad you returned,” he says. ᴬˢ

Mau looks at his hands. Empty. Quiet at his sides.

“You…?” ᴹ

He cannot find a question that isn't too large.

Ashborn seems to understand some part of it anyway.

“I am where I chose to be.” ᴬˢ

Mau's eye closes for a moment.

“He said…” ᴹ

“You can tell me when you wish.” ᴬˢ

The old man doesn't ask which words. He doesn't require Mau to give him an account before he will remain at rest.

He stays the few minutes Shoko allowed. Before he goes, Mau looks at him again, and Ashborn inclines his head as he has done in rooms where nobody was hurting.

Yuka is still beside the bed when the door closes.

* * *

She isn't in the chair when he next looks.

He makes a sound before he can stop himself.

“Here,” she says at once. ʸ

The chair has moved because Maki is moving another bed into the room. Yuka is standing against the wall to give her space. Mai has pillows under one arm and is trying to stop the blanket dragging along the floor.

Mau watches them turn the bed. Measures the distance between its edge and his.

Shoko moves it a little farther away from his supported arm.

“That side. He can't turn towards you yet.” ˢʰ

“I can move,” Yuka says. ʸ

She goes round the other side without hesitation. The arrangement changes again until she can lie down near his uninjured shoulder, within reach of his fingers, with no weight pressing across him.

Mau watches in silence.

Mai tucks the extra blanket at the end and steps back to look at her work.

“There. Neither of you gets to pretend sitting in a chair counts as a night's sleep.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka looks at Mau.

“That part's mostly for me.” ʸ

“Mostly,” Mai agrees. ᴹᵃⁱ

Maki touches Yuka's shoulder before she leaves. Then she rests a hand briefly on the safe part of Mau's blanket, near his knee.

“See you in the morning.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He looks at her.

Morning. As though it has already been arranged.

He makes himself give the little nod Shoko has told him is enough.

* * *

Yuka changes behind the screen.

He hears the ordinary sounds of it. The little click of something being put on the table, cloth drawn over a head, a muffled complaint when her elbow catches the edge of the screen.

He knows those sounds.

He has fallen asleep listening to them, woken to them, asked what she was looking for without opening his eyes. Hearing them here takes him somewhere that the clean sheets and Shoko's lamp have not been able to reach.

Yuka comes round the screen in clothes he remembers.

For a second he forgets the supports and tries to turn towards her.

Pain runs through his back. He stops with a gasp, frightened by how little it took.

She is beside him at once.

“Don't. I'll come to you.” ʸ

Shoko checks that he has not disturbed anything. Yuka waits, then sits where she is told she can sit.

“You can ask,” she says when Shoko steps back. ʸ

He looks at her without understanding.

“If you want me closer. Tell me. We'll work out where.” ʸ

He is so tired that the words make him cry. Not much, not the way he did in the yard. Just tears he doesn't have the strength to hide.

Yuka wipes them with the cloth she has kept folded beside his pillow.

“Please,” he says. ᴹ

She leans in and kisses the safe place on his forehead. Stays there long enough for him to feel her breath, her hand against his hair.

When she lies down, she faces him.

* * *

He doesn't want the lamp put out.

He cannot say why without beginning too much, so when Shoko asks he only says no. She leaves it low. Beru takes a chair where Mau can see him if he wakes, and Rimuru makes sure Shoko knows who will come to relieve her.

No one is going away without telling him.

It is such a small arrangement. He keeps looking at it as though there must be something he has missed.

*Ciel?*

*Here.* ᶜ

*Noise?*

*Here.* ᴺ

Mau lets his eye close.

For a moment the room is round. For a moment there is no reason to get up, and getting up is the only thing keeping the next room from taking him.

He opens his eye again.

Yuka is watching him. She doesn't ask which room he was in.

“I'm here,” she says. ʸ

His fingers move against hers.

“Love you.” ᴹ

“I love you, too.” ʸ

He has to gather himself for the rest. It feels terribly large to ask after all the things she has already said.

“Want you… close.” ᴹ

Yuka's face changes. Her fingers settle around his without pulling at the wrist.

“Good,” she says, and her voice trembles. “That's where I want to be.” ʸ

He looks at her for a long time.

Not because he is trying to learn anything. Because she is there, and he can.

* * *

He wakes twice before midnight.

Once in pain. Shoko is there before he can decide whether it is bad enough to call her.

Once because the room has been too quiet.

That time it is Yuka's hand he finds first. She is asleep, mouth a little open against the pillow, the paper folded safely on the table beyond her. Her fingers tighten when his move, then loosen again without letting him go.

Beru is still in the chair.

*She sleeps,* Noise tells him. ᴺ

Mau watches her breathe.

He had forgotten how many small movements there were in a sleeping face.

*Stay?* he asks.

Noise comes closer in the place where it has stayed for a year.

*Yes.* ᴺ

Mau shuts his eye again.

When he wakes the third time, there is light at the window.
