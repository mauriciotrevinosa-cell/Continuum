# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 72
## Chapter 2 — The Length of the Room

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 72
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S12E01
**Continuity state:** B+219 through B+220 morning. Staged corrections continue, including the foot; food, rest, assisted sitting and first supported steps are shown as separate efforts. Four days after return he can reach a nearby chair with assistance, not resume combat or walk home unaided. Permanent attributes are retained; malhealing, deconditioning, pain and exhaustion restrict function. Jin-Woo's help is voluntary, not a debt he owes. Mau asks to talk with a small chosen group when rested. Ciel receives consent to check exact chronology if needed, not broadcast the full archive. No power acquisition.

*When: Shoko says he can try sitting, Mau looks at the end of the bed as if she has offered him a road.*

It doesn't happen the way he pictured.

He has been thinking about sitting since he woke. A simple thing. Legs over the side, feet down, hand on the edge. He has done it hurt, done it exhausted, done it with things still open that should have been closed first.

Here there are four people who know better than to let him do it quickly.

Yuta supports him while Shoko guides the movement. Yuka brings the blanket so it won't pull across his foot. Beru is close enough to catch him and trying to look as though he has not built his whole attention around that possibility.

Halfway up, Mau has to stop.

The room tilts. The place in his back that has changed is suddenly the only place he can feel.

“Tell me,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

“Dizzy.” ᴹ

The word comes out before *fine* can find its way in front of it.

They stop.

Nobody looks disappointed. Nobody tells him he has already done worse things than this, so surely he can manage a bed.

He waits until the lamp stops moving.

Then he asks to try the rest.

* * *

His feet touch the floor.

One rests carefully, without weight. The other is set where Shoko has asked him to put it. His spine is upright enough that he can see the room from a height that belongs to him.

He puts his hand on the edge of the mattress.

Yuka kneels in front of him so he doesn't have to look down far to see her face.

“Hi,” she says. ʸ

He makes a little disbelieving sound.

“Hi.” ᴹ

There is a window behind her shoulder. A strip of wall. The corner of the screen where he has heard her change clothes. Nothing important has moved, and from here everything looks different.

He notices how narrow his knee is beneath the blanket.

His fingers close on the mattress.

“How long?” he asks Shoko. ᴹ

“A few minutes this time.” ˢʰ

He wants more.

He has to close his eye by the second minute. When she helps him lie back, his whole body is shaking with the effort of not telling them he could have done another.

Yuka settles the pillow and puts her hand where he can reach.

“Again later?” he asks. ᴹ

Shoko checks him before answering.

“If you rest first.” ˢʰ

He accepts the condition with more relief than he wants them to see.

* * *

There is music outside that afternoon.

Not close to the clinic door. Someone has been careful about that. A guitar, stopping in the middle of a phrase and beginning again. Another voice asks a question, and the guitar tries something a little differently.

Mau turns his head towards it.

Yuka looks up from the things she is sorting beside the bed.

“Bocchi and Kita.” ʸ

He listens.

“Are they…” ᴹ

He doesn't finish. Yuka waits until she realises he is asking whether the sound is allowed to be happening while he is here.

“They're practising. I said we'd tell them if it bothered you.” ʸ

He shakes his head at once.

Then he listens to the phrase again.

It goes wrong in the same place. There is a little burst of talking, and when the guitar starts again it has slowed down.

“Window?” he asks. ᴹ

Yuka opens it a little more.

He lies back with his eye shut.

For a while he can hear people working on a thing that isn't him.

* * *

Jin-Woo comes after the next check.

He carries no weapon in his hands. He has brought two cups and looks at Yuka before offering either to Mau.

“Shoko?” ᴶ

“Said yes to that one.” ʸ

She points. Jin-Woo gives it to her to help with, then takes the chair she has moved close to the bed.

Mau studies his face. The dust is gone. The mark at his wrist is healing. He looks like a man who has slept in his own bed with his own family nearby.

Mau is glad enough of it that the next words almost don't come.

“Sorry.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo puts his cup down.

“For what?” ᴶ

“You just… got them here.” ᴹ

He takes a breath. Talking is easier now, but he still has to decide which parts to spend it on.

“Then you had to come after me.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo looks at him for a moment.

“I chose to go.” ᴶ

Mau's fingers move against the blanket.

“I know you did.” ᴹ

“Then let me have the choice.” ᴶ

He says it without sharpness. Mau can hear the same patience he used when correcting the angle of a dagger.

“You didn't ask me to stop caring about anyone when I came here with my family,” Jin-Woo says. “This is where we live now. You're part of it.” ᴶ

Mau looks at him, then at Yuka.

She hasn't stepped in to make the conversation easier. She is letting him hear it from the person who came down the path.

“Thank you,” he says at last. ᴹ

Jin-Woo nods, accepting that answer.

They sit while Mau drinks a little of what he brought.

Jin-Woo doesn't ask when they can train again.

* * *

The next morning, the chair is closer than Mau wants it to be.

He has asked for the one by the window. Shoko has chosen the one barely beyond the side of the bed.

“That's not walking,” he says. ᴹ

“Show me.” ˢʰ

Yuka looks down at the blanket in her hands. He knows that look. She is trying not to smile while being very serious about something he is doing.

He decides not to give her the satisfaction of noticing.

There is support on both sides. Yuta helps with the rise, Jin-Woo takes the other side when Shoko directs him, and Beru stands beyond the chair as though nothing in the world will be allowed to move it.

Mau gets upright.

It takes almost everything he has been saving since breakfast.

Not the strength he can remember using. Something nearer than that: balance, the shape his back expects, the position of a foot he has spent most of a year putting down wrong. His body tries to use the old answer. The corrected joint objects.

He stops.

“Pain?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

“Wrong place,” he says. “Feels like…” ᴹ

He looks down.

She understands enough to guide the next attempt. Jin-Woo adjusts his support. Yuta waits until Mau is ready rather than lifting him across the gap.

The first step is scarcely a step.

The second leaves him gripping their arms.

By the time he reaches the chair, it is exactly as far away as it needs to be.

He sits with Shoko's help. His breathing is too fast. His face is wet in a way he didn't notice while he was moving.

Yuka kneels beside the chair.

She doesn't say he is better now.

“You got here,” she says. ʸ

He looks back at the bed.

The distance is absurd. A few steps in a room. There is no way to explain what he had thought he would feel when he could move again and what he feels instead.

“Wanted the window,” he admits. ᴹ

She follows his glance.

“I know.” ʸ

He rests his head against the chair and closes his eye.

Her hand stays on his knee, above the support, where he has said she can put it.

“I still do.” ᴹ

“Then we'll keep that one for next time.” ʸ

* * *

Later, when he is back in bed, he asks to talk.

Yuka has been expecting the request and dreading it in ways she hasn't told him. She puts down the cup she is drying.

“With me?” ʸ

“You. Rimuru. Ciel. Shoko.” ᴹ

He thinks for a moment.

“Gojo. If he wants.” ᴹ

“He'll want.” ʸ

He nods. Looks at the blanket, where his hands have begun to disappear into the same crease.

“More later. Not everybody… all at once.” ᴹ

“We can do that.” ʸ

He has been able to tell people what he did with Makima. What he asked Noise to build. He said the facts in the right order and waited for them to tell him what was wrong.

He does not know how to tell them why it kept feeling necessary after he had already promised to stop hiding.

Yuka waits beside the bed.

“I don't know where to start,” he says. ᴹ

“We can sit down first.” ʸ

He looks at her.

“You don't have to finish before we get here,” she adds. ʸ

He makes a small sound, not quite a laugh.

Then he asks Ciel something he hasn't asked while anyone else can hear.

*If I get the order wrong… can you help?*

*Yes,* she says. *I'll ask before I add anything.* ᶜ

He looks at Yuka again.

“After I rest?” ᴹ

She puts the cup down and moves her chair back beside his.

“After you rest.” ʸ
