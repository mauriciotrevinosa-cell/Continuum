# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 72
## Chapter 3 — Before the Gate

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 72
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S12E02
**Continuity state:** B+220 afternoon. A small group chosen by Mau listens: Yuka, Rimuru/Ciel, Shoko, Gojo; he additionally invites DIO. The actions and Makima visits were already disclosed before the Final Trial, and are not falsely introduced as new discoveries. He begins explaining the fear behind the secrecy after Yuka's injury. Nobody blames him or Yuka for the Architect choosing torture; responsibility for secret work and disclosure of Ashborn remains distinct. Noise is not made a scapegoat. No new archive broadcast, romantic separation or immediate restoration of trust.

*When: Gojo asks where to sit, Mau discovers he has been expecting everyone to stand over him.*

“There,” he says. ᴹ

Gojo turns the chair round and sits. Not astride it for a joke, not carefully far away. He sets it beside Shoko's, where Mau pointed.

Rimuru has brought his own from the next room. Yuka is already in hers. The doorway is clear.

Mau looks at it.

“Can DIO…?” ᴹ

Rimuru looks at Yuka, then back at Mau.

“I'll ask her.” ᴿ

When she comes, she pauses at the door until Mau looks at her. She doesn't make him say the invitation twice. She takes the last chair, the one nearest Rimuru.

There isn't an audience outside. Maki has made sure of that. Anyone he wants to talk with later will be told when he asks, and nobody is being required to hear his worst memories in order to prove they love him.

Mau looks at their hands. Resting on knees, round a cup, in Yuka's case close enough to touch his if he wants.

They have all sat down.

He doesn't know what to do with that.

* * *

“I told you,” he begins. ᴹ

He stops, looks at Yuka.

“About Makima. Before. I did tell you.” ᴹ

“You did,” she says. ʸ

He looks round the little group, checking.

Rimuru nods.

“The visits. What she admitted about the attack. What you'd let Noise build. Ashborn's name. We heard you.” ᴿ

Mau lets out a breath.

“I don't want it to sound like I'm… changing it.” ᴹ

“Then start where you can,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

He looks at the hand Yuka has left beside his.

“I thought if I could understand her, I could make it stop.” ᴹ

Nobody answers immediately.

“Not make her good. I knew…” ᴹ

He breaks off. There is a way to explain this in which he sounds reasonable, careful, always one observation from knowing what to do. He has used that version before.

It isn't the part they are asking for.

“She hurt you,” he says to Yuka. “And I couldn't find a way that meant she couldn't do it again.” ᴹ

Yuka's fingers move against the sheet.

He watches the place her injury left, though it is covered by her clothes. He knows exactly where it is.

“I heard her say she wanted my place to be somewhere else. With her. Under her. Whatever she decided it was. And I thought—if she can want something different, if I can show her…” ᴹ

He shakes his head.

“I wanted there to be a way she would choose not to hurt you. So I went to find it.” ᴹ

“Without us,” Yuka says. ʸ

He nods.

It takes him a while to say the next part.

“I knew you'd tell me not to go alone.” ᴹ

Yuka looks down at their hands.

“Yes.” ʸ

“And I wanted to go anyway.” ᴹ

* * *

The words sit between them.

He expects anger. Not because it would be unfair. He has been preparing for it since he asked them to come.

Yuka takes a breath through her nose. Her eyes are wet when she looks at him again.

“That's the bit I couldn't reach,” she says. “You were letting me ask questions after you had already decided the part that mattered.” ʸ

He nods, too quickly, then stops because it hurts.

“I know.” ᴹ

“Do you?” ʸ

He looks at her face.

She isn't asking him to give her the right answer quickly. That makes it harder.

“I thought it was worth being wrong about… asking. If I could make it safe.” ᴹ

Gojo shifts in his chair. Says nothing.

Yuka's hand moves onto Mau's.

“I hate that she hurt me,” she says. “I hate that she did it to get at you. I hate that there's somebody out there who can look at the way you love us and think it's a place to put a knife.” ʸ

Mau closes his eye.

“But you don't get to keep giving her that much of you while I only hear about it afterwards.” ʸ

“I wasn't giving—” ᴹ

He stops himself.

The room waits.

He looks at the ceiling, then back at her.

“I was trying to take something from her,” he says. “Information. A way to stop her. I didn't want to think about what she was getting while I did it.” ᴹ

Yuka rubs her thumb once against his fingers.

She hasn't let go.

* * *

“And the building?” Rimuru asks. ᴿ

Mau looks towards him.

“Same thing.” ᴹ

He thinks of the forbidden shelves Noise had kept waiting. Patterns it had never stopped reading, constructions it had held because he had once agreed that they should wait.

“There was so much we hadn't used. Things behind the walls. Things I hadn't asked permission for. I kept thinking if I'd had one of them ready…” ᴹ

He looks at Yuka again.

She takes a slow breath.

“You saved me.” ʸ

“Almost didn't.” ᴹ

“You saved me.” ʸ

His face changes. For a moment there is something frightened and angry in it, too large for the quiet way he has been trying to speak.

“It was almost the same,” he says. ᴹ

Nobody asks him what he means.

He presses his uninjured fingers into the blanket, holds them there until he can continue.

“I had measures. I checked. I listened to the rules. I had a way back. And it was still…” ᴹ

He cannot find a word that isn't another person's blood on his hands.

Shoko moves her chair a little closer.

“I know you were listening,” she says. “When I told you to wash, you did. When Ciel said you were pushing too much through, you stopped. We saw that.” ˢʰ

He looks at her.

He hadn't expected that part to be remembered.

“You let us help her,” she says. “Then when she was getting better, you started going somewhere we couldn't help you.” ˢʰ

He looks down.

“She was okay.” ᴹ

“She was healing.” ˢʰ

He doesn't answer.

Rimuru waits until he looks up again.

“What did you do with the part of you that wasn't?” ᴿ

* * *

Mau tries to answer.

He had taken Ori to a dungeon. He had kept her safe. He had let her lead and slept with her against his knees, bought a blanket, helped carry the things she wanted to bring home.

He had laughed with Yuka. Played a game. Kept a promise about a flight. Told Noise it could build because there were still things that might get through, and he could not stand in a room full of people he loved knowing that he had left an answer unfinished.

He had done all those things in the same days.

“I thought…” ᴹ

He stops.

“I thought being happy meant it wasn't doing it anymore.” ᴹ

Yuka's hand tightens a little.

“Some of that was real,” he says quickly. “Ori. The night in the common room. You. I wasn't pretending all of it.” ᴹ

“I know,” Yuka says. ʸ

“I wasn't.” ᴹ

“Mau. I was there. I know.” ʸ

He looks at her until he can believe she means it.

Then he begins crying in a way that makes it difficult to speak.

“I didn't know where to put the rest.” ᴹ

Rimuru moves the cup within reach. Doesn't make him drink while he is trying to breathe.

Gojo stays in his chair, his hands loose on his knees, looking at Mau as if he has all the time he needs to finish being a person before anyone asks him to be strong again.

* * *

Ciel asks whether he wants her to speak.

Mau wipes his face with the cloth Yuka puts in his hand. He can't reach every part safely. She helps with what he misses, and he lets her.

Then he nods.

Rimuru gives Ciel's words the room she asks for, repeating them without changing them into an easier explanation.

“She says, 'I was hurt that you and Noise chose to keep me outside it. I wanted to help you understand what you were doing, not only meet you after it went wrong.'” ᴿ

Mau looks down.

“I know. I'm sorry.” ᴹ

Ciel waits until he can hear the rest.

“'I can understand why you were frightened and still need you not to make that choice for me again.'” ᴿ

DIO has her hands folded in her lap. They have stayed there through the whole conversation.

“I wanted to be close to you,” she says. “Both of you. Noise knows that. Being allowed to sit in the room while something important is happening behind a wall isn't the closeness I meant.” ᴰ

Inside him, Noise makes a small movement.

Mau feels it gathering itself to answer.

“I told it to,” he says. ᴹ

DIO looks at him.

“I know you did.” ᴰ

“Don't make it—” ᴹ

“I'm not giving it your blame. I'm talking to my friend.” ᴰ

Mau stops.

Ciel passes the space to Noise without filling it.

*Thought helping,* it says. ᴺ

Rimuru relays it.

DIO's face softens, though the hurt doesn't leave it.

“I know,” she says. “I want us to be able to disagree before somebody gets hurt, too.” ᴰ

Noise takes a long time to answer.

*Need learn,* it says at last. ᴺ

“Yes,” DIO says. “With us.” ᴰ

* * *

Mau tires before anybody is finished.

Shoko sees the way his head has begun to turn into the pillow, the effort it takes him to keep his eye on the person speaking. She asks for a pause.

He looks frightened by it.

“I'm not…” ᴹ

“Nobody's leaving angry because you need to breathe,” she says. “Take a moment.” ˢʰ

He looks at Yuka.

“I'll still be here when you have the rest of the words,” she says. ʸ

He swallows.

“There are more.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

“I don't know how many.” ᴹ

Her face trembles. She keeps her hand beneath his.

“Then don't count them before you start.” ʸ

He lets his eye close for a little while.

The chairs stay where they are.
