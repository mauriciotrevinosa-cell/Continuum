# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 72
## Chapter 4 — The Days He Leaves Out

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 72
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S12E02
**Continuity state:** B+220 late afternoon, after a genuine rest within the chosen listening session. Mau describes parts of the Final Trial without compulsory memory projection. Ciel/Noise help with chronology only after asking; exact duration ~368 local days remains distinct from his minimising language and outside time. The old false-Yuka feeding/knife scenario is identified as the Rulers' earlier trial (V53#12), not real Yuka, a new occurrence in the ultimate dungeon, or Makima's proven work. Noise's own request to stay is shared with its agreement. They pause without pretending the whole archive, trust or rehabilitation is resolved.

*When: Mau says his back was only a problem for a couple of weeks, Shoko puts her cup down.*

“The injury?” she asks. “Or not being able to stand?” ˢʰ

Mau looks at her.

He had thought they were the same answer.

“Standing,” he says after a moment. ᴹ

“What happened after you could stand?” ˢʰ

He shifts his eye towards the window. It is still the same afternoon, though the light has moved along the wall during the rest they made him take.

“I kept going.” ᴹ

“With it like that.” ˢʰ

He nods.

She has touched the repair. She knows more about its shape than he could tell her. He can't work out what is missing from the answer.

“It worked,” he says. “Enough.” ᴹ

Gojo looks down at his cup. Yuka keeps her hand open beside Mau's, waiting for him to choose it.

“How long did it have to work?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

Mau takes a breath.

“I don't know. A few…” ᴹ

He stops at the look on Yuka's face.

He knows there is a number. He said a version of it to Gojo in a room going white. The counter outside said it to everyone who waited, and Noise kept every day he stopped keeping for himself.

“A year,” he says. ᴹ

His mouth has made a mistake. It feels like a mistake even while he knows it hasn't.

“But not all like that. There were quiet bits.” ᴹ

* * *

He tells them about the first place he found to lie down.

Not the whole fight. He can't remember all three bodies in the right order without Noise, and they haven't asked him for a report on how he won. He tells them what he could do after it: pull himself clear, buy the things the shop still allowed, find a place something couldn't easily reach him while he slept.

The blanket. The water. The mistake of putting one thing just beyond his hand.

Yuka looks at his fingers on the sheet.

“You couldn't reach it?” she asks. ʸ

“Moved it after.” ᴹ

He starts to smile, because it sounds stupid when he says it like that.

Nobody smiles back.

The expression dies before it is finished.

“I did move it,” he says. ᴹ

“I believe you,” Yuka says. ʸ

Her eyes are full. She is looking at his face, not at a screen someone has made her watch.

“I'm trying to understand how you got there to move it.” ʸ

He looks at her for a long time.

Then he tells her.

Not every movement. Enough that she understands what *after* had to contain.

* * *

There are parts he can say easily because he has said them to himself so many times.

The rain. Waiting for the lull. The arm. Finding something to brace it against because the first attempt wouldn't stay where he put it.

There are parts he starts and loses.

He has to ask Noise which leg came first. Noise asks Ciel how much to give them. Ciel asks Mau whether he wants the sequence or only the next part.

“Next,” he says. ᴹ

They give him the next part.

The foot.

Shoko watches the blanket over it. She has already had to correct the shape he describes, but hearing how he got from the wrong shape to the next room changes something in her face.

“I had a boot,” Mau says. “Made it fit.” ᴹ

“I saw,” she says. ˢʰ

He remembers Maki taking something off him. A pull that stopped before he could ask it to. He hadn't known that could matter so much.

“She didn't pull,” he says, suddenly. ᴹ

Yuka looks at him.

“The boot. When I came out.” ᴹ

“Grandma cut it.” ʸ

He looks back at the blanket.

“Good.” ᴹ

Shoko wipes a finger beneath one eye before he looks up.

* * *

He doesn't mean to tell them the worst part by accident.

He is trying to explain why the scar across his stomach pulls differently from the other ones. Shoko had asked about that while treating him, and he hadn't been awake enough to answer.

“Couldn't keep all of it in and heal at the same time,” he says. ᴹ

The room goes still.

He looks from Shoko to Rimuru.

“I did. After. Just had to…” ᴹ

He stops.

Yuka's hand has closed on his. Not hard. There is a tremor in it he can't miss.

“Mau,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her.

“What couldn't you keep in?” ʸ

He tells her.

Her face changes so completely that he tries to take it back, not the fact but the way he said it, the ordinary voice he has been using for a thing that has already happened and therefore should be manageable.

“I fixed it,” he says. “Enough to stop—” ᴹ

“You were alone,” she says. ʸ

His eye moves towards Rimuru.

“Noise was…” ᴹ

“I know Noise was there. I mean there wasn't anyone who could hold you. Or put their hands where yours couldn't reach.” ʸ

He swallows.

She is crying now. He watches the tears, frightened by how much larger the room has become than the version he had been preparing to describe.

“I was trying to get back,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka bends her head over their hands.

“I know.” ʸ

* * *

They stop for a while.

Rimuru brings water. Shoko checks the way Mau is breathing, asks about pain, waits until he answers the question instead of nodding at the general idea of being all right.

DIO has not said anything for several minutes. When she moves, it is to bring Yuka a second cloth.

Gojo sets his cup on the floor and moves his chair closer to Mau's bed.

“How long by the dragon?” he asks when Mau can hear him again. ᴳ

Mau thinks.

“A while.” ᴹ

*May I help with the count?* Ciel asks. ᶜ

Mau closes his eye. Opens it.

“Yes.” ᴹ

Rimuru listens.

“From the fight to leaving that area, close to three months,” he says. ᴿ

Mau looks at him.

The number is true. He remembers buying another blanket. Trying a step. Going back because the cough had pulled something apart again. The way the body's scale had become part of the view when he opened his eye.

Three months.

“Didn't feel…” ᴹ

He stops because it had felt like forever, every morning, and now it feels like a place he was in before he came here. Both things cannot fit into the same answer.

“When I come back,” he says slowly, “I start from where I was before. The day before. I know it happened. But I don't… put all the days after it.” ᴹ

Yuka lifts her head.

“After in your life?” she asks. ʸ

He nods.

“It was there. Then I'm here.” ᴹ

He looks down at the place the scar is hidden.

“I keep thinking I should be able to pick up where I left it.” ᴹ

* * *

“Does it happen with the other trials?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

He nods before he thinks about which one.

Yuka feels his fingers change against hers.

Not pulling away. Bracing.

She lowers her hand so he doesn't have to hold it in any particular position.

He watches her do that, then looks at her face with a sudden fear so familiar to him that he begins trying to hide it before he can name it.

“The spoon,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka doesn't move.

“When I stopped. It wasn't you.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

“It looked…” ᴹ

He can't say *like you* while she is holding his hand like this. It feels cruel.

“A likeness,” Gojo says quietly. ᴳ

Mau nods.

He can use that word.

“The older trial. The Rulers. Our table. She asked what I wanted for breakfast.” ᴹ

His breath catches.

Yuka begins to speak, stops when Shoko lifts a hand, waits for Mau to find the next breath.

“She let me eat,” he says. “For a while. Asked about a shelf. Things we…” ᴹ

He looks at the actual woman beside him.

“I answered because it took longer.” ᴹ

Yuka's mouth opens. No words come.

“Then she put it in my hand. The knife. Said I could come home if I…” ᴹ

He cannot finish that sentence here.

They know enough of the earlier trial to finish it without asking him to practise it aloud.

“I knew,” he says, suddenly frantic. “I knew it wasn't you. I kept things. Real things you said. I knew.” ᴹ

Yuka moves closer without touching any new part of him.

“I believe you.” ʸ

“But when you said slowly, at supper…” ᴹ

He is not looking at the room anymore.

He is looking at the moment before a gentle face changed what it was doing. Waiting to see whether he has been good enough to keep the pause.

* * *

Ciel says his name.

Only his name first. Close enough that he can follow it without finding a thought of his own.

He looks towards Rimuru, misses, tries again.

*Yuka has put the cup on the table. Shoko is beside you. Nobody is asking you to finish the memory.* ᶜ

His chest hurts. He knows why. He cannot make knowing change the next breath.

“Mau,” Yuka says. “Can I put my hand against your face?” ʸ

He looks at her fingers.

She waits.

At last he gives the little movement she knows means yes.

Her palm touches the uninjured side of his face. Warm. Still. She doesn't turn his head for him.

“We're in Shoko's room,” she says. “It's afternoon. We stopped talking. We can keep stopping.” ʸ

He looks at her until he can see the cloth she is holding, the ring, the tired crease at the edge of her eye.

“Sorry,” he says. ᴹ

She shakes her head, crying too hard for a moment to answer properly.

Then she gets the words out.

“You didn't do anything to me.” ʸ

* * *

They don't return to the table in that other room.

They sit through the rest of the panic. Shoko watches what needs watching. Gojo moves the cup when Mau keeps following it with his eye, out of sight but not secretly; tells him where it has gone.

Rimuru stays close enough that Ciel doesn't have to repeat anything to be heard.

When Mau begins to cry instead of fight for air, nobody calls it getting worse.

Yuka keeps her hand where he said it could be.

After a while, he whispers, “Don't want you to have it.” ᴹ

“Have what?” she asks. ʸ

“In your head. When you look at me. Look at us.” ᴹ

She closes her eyes for a moment.

Then opens them, because he is watching.

“I don't want you to have it either,” she says. “But you already do. I can't help with the parts you won't let me know are there.” ʸ

He looks at her as if she has asked him to hand over something he doesn't know how to release without making it hurt her.

“We'll choose how much,” she says. “We can stop. You just saw us stop.” ʸ

His fingers move under hers.

There is no promise he can make that will turn the last few minutes into an easy beginning.

He gives her what he has.

“Okay.” ᴹ

* * *

Before the others go, he asks Noise a question.

Not through Ciel, not out loud. In the place where it answered him when nobody else could.

*Can I tell them what you said?*

Noise takes a moment.

*Yes.* ᴺ

Mau looks at Rimuru, then at DIO, then at Yuka.

“I stopped,” he says. ᴹ

Shoko remains beside the bed.

“Near the end. Stopped eating. Stopped trying to learn what the next thing wanted. It got quiet when I did.” ᴹ

His hand has begun to shake again. Yuka doesn't grip it harder.

“Noise asked me to stay. Not for anyone outside. For it.” ᴹ

DIO looks down. Her hands come apart in her lap.

“Said it wanted to be Noise. Wanted me.” ᴹ

He lets himself remember the words instead of moving past them to what he did next.

“So I got up.” ᴹ

The room is quiet for a long time.

Gojo is the one who speaks.

“I'm glad it asked.” ᴳ

Mau looks at him.

There is no question after it. No request that he explain why his wife, his daughter, his family hadn't been enough to make getting up simple. Gojo has heard what he said.

DIO lifts her head.

“Thank you for telling us,” she says. ᴰ

Noise listens through Ciel.

It doesn't offer them a construction.

* * *

They leave in the order Mau can follow.

DIO first, telling him where she will be. Rimuru after making sure he knows Ciel is not leaving with the chair. Gojo at the door, touching the frame once before turning back.

“Later,” he says. ᴳ

Mau believes he means it.

Shoko stays to check him, then goes to speak with Yuta about the next turn of care. There will be no more stories tonight unless Mau asks for them.

Yuka moves her chair back to its familiar place.

For a while he watches her hand resting on his.

“Are you…” ᴹ

He stops.

She waits until he can try again.

“Still angry?” ᴹ

Her face hurts when she answers.

“About the secrets? Yes. And I'm very glad you told me this. Both.” ʸ

He looks at her fingers.

“You don't have to fix how I feel before you sleep,” she says. ʸ

He lets the words settle without trying to find a use for them.

Then he asks her for the thing he has been wanting since she put her hand against his cheek.

“Can you do that again?” ᴹ

She moves her chair closer.

“Yes.” ʸ
