# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 72
## Chapter 5 — The Place They Saved

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 72
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S12E03
**Continuity state:** B+221, late afternoon into supper. Mau asks for a short supervised visit to the common room in a supported chair; being transported is not full walking clearance. He chooses what basic recovery information may be shared with Maki/Yuta/Mai/Frieren; the full account and private memories remain unbroadcast. The house resumes ordinary company around him with food cleared by Shoko. Ori keeps her original paper; no second delivery. Recovering trust, back/foot function, nutrition, the wider listening and the belated birthday remain ongoing. No new crisis, unit or power.

*When: Mau says he wants to go to supper, Yuka asks which part he means.*

“The table,” he says. ᴹ

“I can bring—” ʸ

She stops herself.

He is looking past her towards the door. Not trying to get up, not preparing to tell her he is well enough before she can find out otherwise.

“The table,” she says again, understanding. ʸ

He nods.

They ask Shoko.

It is not a request she answers immediately. She checks how he has managed the afternoon, how much it took to sit, the way his foot responds now that yesterday's effort has had a night to settle.

Then she looks at him.

“A short visit. You're taken there, you stay supported, and you tell me when you've had enough.” ˢʰ

“Okay.” ᴹ

She waits.

He looks faintly offended.

“Actually okay.” ᴹ

“Good. I enjoy those.” ˢʰ

Yuka turns away before he can see the smile she is trying to keep to herself.

He sees it anyway.

* * *

Maki comes to help with the transfer.

The chair is practical, already checked by Shoko, with the support his leg still needs. Nobody suggests carrying him over a shoulder, nobody requires him to make the distance on his own before he is allowed to see the room.

Yuta comes with Maki. He asks before he takes Mau's arm.

Mau says yes.

The movement is easier than the first attempt to sit. It is still enough to leave him breathing carefully while Yuka settles the blanket.

Mai waits until he can look at her before putting his own cardigan within reach.

“This one?” she asks. ᴹᵃⁱ

He touches the sleeve.

“That one.” ᴹ

It hangs loose across him. She doesn't comment. She helps where he needs help and lets him finish the part he can manage.

Then he looks between the three of them.

“Yuka can tell you,” he says. “The things Shoko needs you to know. And… what we talked about. Some of it.” ᴹ

Yuka puts her hand on the back of his chair.

“We'll choose which parts,” she says. “You can be there.” ʸ

He nods.

He had been about to ask that. She knew without deciding the answer for him.

Maki checks that the way to the door is clear.

“No meeting over supper,” she says. “You can come eat.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He looks at her.

“Wasn't going to—” ᴹ

Mai lifts an eyebrow.

He gives up defending a plan he hadn't realised he was already making.

“Fine.” ᴹ

“Try actually okay,” Shoko says from the bed. ˢʰ

He looks back at her, then at Yuka.

Yuka has stopped hiding the smile.

* * *

The common room is noisy.

Not in the way he feared. He had pictured the door opening and everything stopping, all the faces turning at once, the meal waiting for him to decide how much of himself he could show.

There is a pause near the entrance. Fern sees him first, then Frieren. Farther in, a conversation goes on because the people having it haven't noticed yet.

A spoon hits a bowl. Somebody laughs.

Mau has to close his eye for a moment.

Yuka stops the chair.

“Too much?” she asks. ʸ

He shakes his head.

When he opens it again, Frieren is moving a chair out of their way. She leaves the space beside it open. Ori is already there, sitting very straight with her hands in her lap.

“This place?” Frieren asks. ᶠ

Mau looks at the table.

At Ori's bowl. The place Yuka will sit. A cup already waiting within reach, not placed so exactly that he cannot ask to move it.

“Yes.” ᴹ

* * *

Ori doesn't hug him without asking.

She says hello, and when he says it back she looks pleased enough that he wonders what he has missed while learning how to get from a bed to a chair.

Her hand brushes the pocket where she has been keeping something important. Then she puts it on the table, empty.

He remembers the paper going into that pocket. Something in his chest loosens a little.

Umaru comes over with Rem and looks at his bowl.

“That's all?” she asks. ᵁ

Shoko, arriving behind them, answers before Mau can.

“For now.” ˢʰ

Umaru considers the bowl. Looks at him. Looks at Shoko.

“Okay. We can do supplies later.” ᵁ

“Crisis postponed?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Temporarily.” ᵁ

Rem puts a hand against Umaru's back as they move to their places. Umaru keeps glancing over until she sees Mau begin to eat.

He gets the spoon into his hand himself.

Yuka watches without moving to take it. When he needs help with the bowl, she brings it closer.

He eats the first mouthful slowly.

The room keeps talking.

* * *

Beru has taken up a position behind his chair.

Mau notices after the first few spoonfuls. He turns his head as far as is comfortable and finds him standing with his hands together, following the table with all the attention he once gave a battlefield.

“Beru.” ᴹ

“My king.” ᴮᵉ

“Chair.” ᴹ

Beru looks towards the empty one beside the end of the table.

“I am quite able to—” ᴮᵉ

“Sit with us.” ᴹ

He sits.

Ori pushes the little bowl she has moved out of his way a little farther so his hands have somewhere to go. He thanks her with such gravity that Mau nearly laughs into his food.

The laugh hurts less than it did yesterday.

He notices that and tries not to turn it into a test.

* * *

Jin-Woo's family is two places farther down.

His mother is telling his sister something she has heard before, judging by the expression on Jin-Ah's face. Suho leans across to ask his father a question. Hae-In catches the edge of a dish before the elbow beside it can knock it over.

Mau watches them for a while.

Then Jin-Woo looks up, catches his eye, and nods.

Not an assessment. Not an invitation to see what his body can do next.

Mau nods back.

Across the room, Kita is trying to explain a small disaster involving a string. Bocchi begins to answer, stops when too many people look at her, then finds Kita watching patiently enough that she tries again.

There are conversations Mau can't hear from where he sits. People at the other end of the room who do not need to come closer to prove they have noticed he is home.

He likes that more than he expected.

* * *

Mai puts down a dish near him.

He looks at her hand, then at a small piece of work fixed to the handle. Something neat and practical, her doing. He knows the difference between a thing made with Construction and a thing she has shaped by hand after making the material.

“Made that?” he asks. ᴹ

“Fitted it this morning. By hand. The metal was already in the workshop. Before you ask, I haven't charged your reserve for a cupboard while Shoko's trying to fix you.” ᴹᵃⁱ

He looks up at her.

“Wasn't going to ask.” ᴹ

“You were absolutely going to ask.” ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka smiles into her cup.

Mai touches the back of Mau's chair, away from any part that might jar him.

“You're allowed to like it without being responsible for it,” she says more quietly. ᴹᵃⁱ

He looks at the little piece again.

“I like it.” ᴹ

She leaves her hand there another moment.

“Good.” ᴹᵃⁱ

* * *

He lasts less than the whole meal.

Yuka notices before he tells her. So does Shoko. Neither says it first.

Mau takes one more mouthful because he wants it, puts the spoon down because he cannot comfortably hold it anymore, and looks at Yuka.

“Enough,” he says. ᴹ

She puts her cup down.

“Okay.” ʸ

There is no disappointed silence. Maki gets the way clear. Yuta comes to help at the part of the route where Shoko wants another pair of hands. Ori asks whether she can come by tomorrow.

“Ask Shoko,” Mau says, then looks at her. “I want you to.” ᴹ

Ori smiles.

Yuka rests her hand against his shoulder where the cardigan is loose and nothing underneath needs to be held still.

“Ready?” she asks. ʸ

He looks back at the table.

“Tomorrow?” ᴹ

“We'll ask tomorrow.” ʸ

He accepts that.

* * *

Back in the clinic, getting out of the chair takes help he had hoped he wouldn't need.

He tells Shoko where it hurts instead of trying to make the movement faster. Yuta changes his support. Yuka waits until they have finished before she pulls the blanket up.

When everyone else has stepped away, she sits beside him and strokes the hair clear of his forehead.

“Too much?” she asks. ʸ

He thinks about it.

“A little.” ᴹ

She nods, taking the answer as seriously as she would have taken a larger one.

He watches her face.

“Wanted to go.” ᴹ

“I know. I wanted you there.” ʸ

He lets his eye close. Opens it again before she can move her hand.

“Still do,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka bends to kiss him in the place he has begun turning towards her himself.

“Then we'll keep going back.” ʸ

Outside the clinic, somebody starts clearing the table. The sound reaches him through the partly open door, mixed with a conversation that has not finished because he left.

He listens until he falls asleep.
