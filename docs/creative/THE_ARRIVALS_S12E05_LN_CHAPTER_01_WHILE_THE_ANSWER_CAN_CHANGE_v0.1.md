# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 72
## Chapter 12 — While the Answer Can Change

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 72
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S12E05
**Continuity state:** B+224 morning. Mau, Noise, Ciel, DIO and Rimuru make their earlier review agreement usable. Permanent analysis continues; no power is deleted and no blanket permission is issued. Retained personal study, derivative permission, risky bodily construction and medical assistance remain distinct. Mau keeps agency, explicitly accepts disagreement before a decision is executed, and does not resume private Makima visits. Noise owns its part without receiving Mau's blame or a romantic reward. Body still in recovery; prototype status unchanged.

*When: Noise asks whether it may show them the unfinished work, Mau almost says they can look after it is safer.*

He hears the answer before he gives it.

Later, when it is finished. Later, when there is something useful to put between the fear and the questions. Later, when nobody needs to know how close it came to hurting him first.

He looks at Rimuru.

“Now,” he says. “If Ciel's ready.” ᴹ

Rimuru listens, then nods.

DIO has taken the chair nearest him. Yuka is at the little table by the window, fixing a loose stitch in something Ori has left with her. She knows what they are meeting about. When Mau asked whether she wanted to sit with them, she said she wanted to listen while she worked.

He can see her from the bed without having to check the door.

* * *

Noise doesn't begin with the frightening thing.

It brings them the smallest correction it had almost finished: a relationship between two movements, a way to mark where a hand had begun turning before the shoulder followed. A piece of the work they had already tried with Jin-Woo, waiting to be made useful instead of merely understood.

*This one,* it says. ᴺ

Mau feels the familiar interest before the shame can arrive. He can see what Noise liked about it. The clean little fit between what the eyes could notice and what the body still had to learn.

He looks at Rimuru as Ciel asks the question.

“Is it meant to move him?” Rimuru repeats. ᴿ

*No. Show. After.* ᴺ

“Then it stays a review,” Mau says. “No moving for me. Same as we agreed before.” ᴹ

They look at it together.

It is almost embarrassingly ordinary. Mau had spent so long imagining the room that would object to everything that he hasn't pictured the room understanding a thing he wants to make.

Yuka knots the thread and cuts it.

“That one helped you notice the shoulder?” she asks. ʸ

He nods.

“Eventually.” ᴹ

“I remember eventually.” ʸ

Rimuru smiles into his cup.

* * *

The next thing is harder.

The short step across a distance. The work that had put a different answer in reach and then shown how much had to be true before his body could use it safely.

Mau feels Noise wait beside it.

He wants it. Even now, with his foot supported and Shoko's instructions folded beside the cup, he can remember what it felt like to know a point without needing every piece of the path between.

“I still want to finish that,” he says. ᴹ

“I know,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“Not today.” ᴹ

Rimuru waits.

Mau looks towards Yuka. She is watching him now, the sewing set aside.

“I don't mean I'm saying not today so you stop looking, and then doing the other half tonight.” ᴹ

Her expression changes a little. It is not forgiveness arriving all at once. It is recognition.

“Okay,” she says. ʸ

He tells Ciel where he and Noise had stopped. The parts that had been tested, the part that had only fitted in a model, the difference he had wanted to pretend was small enough to cross without another conversation.

Ciel asks a question he doesn't like.

He answers it anyway.

* * *

There is no dramatic destruction of the work.

The patterns Noise has read remain read. Nobody asks it to make its mind stop noticing the world. Nobody tells Mau that his own thoughts must be handed over every time he is afraid.

But when the work would go into his body, or put someone else's likeness into use, or create a new way an enemy might find them, they stop pretending those are choices only two people inside one head will have to live with.

DIO speaks when they reach the pieces shaped from her and Ashborn.

“Ask us for the thing you want to do. Not a yes you can carry into anything you haven't thought of yet.” ᴰ

Mau nods.

“The work we've already done?” ᴹ

“We can talk about that as well.” ᴰ

He looks down at the blanket.

“You're not taking it away.” ᴹ

DIO looks at him for a moment.

“I'm asking you to keep me in it.” ᴰ

He takes a breath, then looks towards Rimuru when Ciel wants to answer, too.

“She says, 'If you only ask whether you can survive a test, I may answer a different question from the one you needed to ask.'” ᴿ

Mau lets that sit.

Then he says what he hadn't meant to say.

“Sometimes I wanted you to answer the smaller question.” ᴹ

Rimuru's cup stops halfway to his mouth.

Yuka puts the sewing on the table.

“Because then you could tell yourself you'd asked?” she says. ʸ

He nods.

It is unpleasant to see her understand that about him. He makes himself stay while she does.

“Then we'll watch for it,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her.

“Together,” she adds. “You know what it feels like before we do.” ʸ

* * *

Noise speaks before Mau can answer for it.

*I helped hide,* it says. ᴺ

Rimuru passes the words into the room.

Mau looks towards him, then stops. DIO is already listening.

*Thought if safe first. Then friends see.* ᴺ

DIO folds her hands together. Her fingers are unsteady for a moment before she settles them.

“I wanted to see you while you were still trying,” she says. ᴰ

Noise is quiet.

*If wrong?* ᴺ

“Especially then. I might have known something useful. Or I might have sat with you while it didn't work.” ᴰ

Ciel waits for Noise's answer without offering one on its behalf.

At last it asks, *Can say no. Still friends?* ᴺ

DIO smiles. Her eyes are wet.

“Yes.” ᴰ

*Both?* ᴺ

Ciel answers before Rimuru needs to speak.

*Yes. I can disagree with you and want to be here.* ᶜ

Noise takes a while to hold those things together.

Mau knows the effort. He has been making it in the bed next to Yuka's every night.

* * *

“And Makima?” Rimuru asks after the review has paused. ᴿ

Mau looks at him.

“I told her she could ask to visit again. With company. Not start the work again.” ᴹ

Rimuru nods. He was there. So was Yuka, who has not become comfortable with the offer merely because they all heard it being made.

“I'm asking what happens when you want more than the visit,” he says. ᴿ

Mau looks at Yuka.

There is no version of that question she can hear without remembering why he went. He doesn't make her ask it again.

“I tell you what I want to find out. Before I make a plan that only works if nobody knows.” ᴹ

“And if we disagree?” ᴿ

“Then we disagree while it can still change what I do.” ᴹ

He looks at Yuka as he says it.

She nods once.

No one asks him to decide the rest of Makima's life this afternoon. They are agreeing on what happens between the first frightened thought and the next secret door.

For now, he can see that distance.

* * *

He gets tired before the list is finished.

He notices, thinks about hiding it until they reach a tidier stopping place, then looks at Rimuru.

“That's enough for today.” ᴹ

Rimuru closes the record.

It is so immediate that Mau almost objects. They were in the middle of something. There are things left to say. If he keeps going a little longer, perhaps—

Yuka is beside the bed, adjusting the pillow where it has slipped.

He lets her.

DIO stands and tells Noise when she will come back. It asks whether she has to leave because the review is over.

She looks at Rimuru, then at Mau.

“No,” she says. “I could stay for a little while.” ᴰ

Noise has no new construction ready to show her.

It asks if she would like to hear about something Ori said before they went into the Purple Gate.

DIO sits back down.

Yuka takes her sewing to the other bed, within reach of Mau's hand, and the room goes on without anyone having to finish themselves first.
