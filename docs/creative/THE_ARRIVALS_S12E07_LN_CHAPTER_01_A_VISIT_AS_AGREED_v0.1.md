# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 72
## Chapter 17 — A Visit, As Agreed

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 72
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S12E07
**Continuity state:** B+228, after the acute recovery arc. Makima's second visit is requested and answered in advance, with renewed visitor terms and Rimuru/Maki accompanying in a mini Inn visitor room inside the established protected area. She receives no access to the couple's bedroom or private archive. Shoko permits the short journey using transport plus brief supported walking; Mau is still in rehabilitation. Yuka chooses not to attend and knows the plan. This is a health visit, not a resumed private research meeting. Makima keeps the limit while testing what kindness means; neither reform nor complete insincerity is certified. Mau reports the encounter to Yuka on returning. No new ability, enemy attack, conception or lost unit.

*When: Makima asks to return, Mau reads the request with Yuka beside him.*

The note is shorter than the first.

She would like to know how he is recovering. She is asking whether a visit would be possible, under the same arrangements. There is no offer of a thing that might improve his body if he lets her bring it.

Yuka reads it after him.

“Do you want to see her?” she asks. ʸ

He thinks before answering.

“Yes. For a little while. I want to know what she does when there isn't a new answer for her to get.” ᴹ

Yuka looks at the paper.

“There might still be one.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

She gives the note back.

“I'm not coming this time.” ʸ

He nods.

“Okay.” ᴹ

She looks at him for a moment, then puts her hand on the back of his.

“Tell me how it goes.” ʸ

“I will.” ᴹ

* * *

The decision takes longer than writing yes.

Shoko checks what he can manage that day. Rimuru agrees to come; Maki says she will be there before anyone asks her to make the offer softer.

They choose a visitor room at the mini Inn. A place meant for people who have permission to visit, with a journey Mau can make mostly seated and a short stretch he can try with help. His own room stays his own.

He tells Yuka the plan before the answer goes back out.

She is brushing something from Ori's sleeve when he finishes. Ori is patiently insisting it isn't what it looks like.

“It looks like crumbs,” Yuka says. ʸ

“It wasn't a biscuit.” ᴼ

Mau looks at the sleeve.

Yuka catches his expression.

“You're not helping.” ʸ

He grins, then looks at her properly.

“Mini Inn. Short visit. Rimuru and Maki.” ᴹ

She nods.

“I'll be here when you come back.” ʸ

He believes she means the place, not a test of whether she has stayed.

* * *

Walking into the visitor room still takes thought.

The last part is not far. That is why Shoko allowed him to try it. Maki gives him support without taking the whole movement away, and he sits before he has to be told that getting there doesn't mean he should remain standing.

Rimuru puts the walking support where he can reach it later.

Makima has not arrived yet.

Mau is glad of that. He wants a moment to let his breathing settle without wondering what she will decide it means.

Maki sets his cup near his hand.

“You tell us when you've had enough,” she says. ᴹᵃᵏ

“I heard Shoko.” ᴹ

“Now you've heard me.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He looks at her, then smiles despite himself.

“Very coordinated.” ᴹ

“We try.” ᴹᵃᵏ

* * *

Makima enters with Rimuru after the terms have been settled again.

She sees the support beside the chair. The clothes that fit better than the loose clinic gown without hiding how thin he still is. The ring kept safely rather than worn where it could slip off his hand.

She looks at his face.

“You're walking.” ᴹᵏ

“A little. With help.” ᴹ

She takes the seat offered to her.

“Does that mean we can discuss the work you wanted to do?” ᴹᵏ

Mau watches her for a moment.

“Not today.” ᴹ

“You said you wanted to walk first.” ᴹᵏ

“I did. I didn't say the first steps would make me ready for everything after them.” ᴹ

Makima's gaze drops briefly to his hand.

“Then a visit,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“A visit.” ᴹ

Rimuru takes the chair beside the table. Maki remains seated nearer the door. Neither is being asked to provide the privacy Makima might prefer.

* * *

For the first few minutes, Makima keeps trying to make a question useful.

What is improving. What he still cannot do. Whether the restrictions were all removed when the instance closed.

Mau answers about the walking and the food and having gone home.

He doesn't answer with a map of his remaining weakness.

When she notices, he lets her notice.

“I can tell you I'm getting better without giving you everything Shoko is treating,” he says. ᴹ

Makima looks at Rimuru, then back at him.

“Of course.” ᴹᵏ

The next quiet is not hostile. It is simply a space she hasn't found a use for yet.

Mau drinks his tea.

After a while, she asks whether he has been able to sleep.

He says some nights are better.

She does not ask him for the dreams.

* * *

“Yuka didn't come,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau sets his cup down.

“She had things she wanted to do.” ᴹ

“Does she mind you seeing me?” ᴹᵏ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Makima studies his face.

He leaves the answer where it is. Doesn't turn his wife's hurt into something smaller to make the visitor feel welcome.

“And you're here.” ᴹᵏ

“After we talked about it. She knows where I am. We agreed where this happens and who comes with me.” ᴹ

He looks at Maki, then back at Makima.

“You don't get her company just because I said yes to giving you some of mine.” ᴹ

Makima lowers her eyes to the table.

“She has no reason to want to sit with me.” ᴹᵏ

“No,” Mau says. ᴹ

There is no comfort waiting after that answer.

Makima accepts the cup Rimuru has put within reach.

* * *

“Did you mean what you said?” she asks after a while. ᴹᵏ

Mau looks up.

“Which part?” ᴹ

She keeps one hand round the cup. The other is flat against her knee.

“That it wasn't my fault.” ᴹᵏ

Maki's attention sharpens beside the door.

Mau takes a breath.

“I meant that he chose what he did to me. I meant he'd wanted something from me long before you met him.” ᴹ

Makima waits.

“You gave him information. You encouraged him to think another room would get you what you wanted. Those are things you chose.” ᴹ

Her fingers tighten round the cup.

“So you take it back.” ᴹᵏ

“I make it clearer.” ᴹ

He looks at her hand, then her face.

“I didn't take your hand to make you innocent. I saw you upset, and I wanted you to know I was still here.” ᴹ

Makima looks down.

For a moment the visitor room is very quiet.

“I didn't ask you to do that,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“I know.” ᴹ

* * *

She lifts the cup, puts it down without drinking, changes the position of her free hand.

Mau recognises the movement because he remembers the shape her palm made beneath his in the clinic.

He doesn't put his hand into it automatically.

He moves the tea closer when she asks for the pot, lets her choose how much she wants, waits while she drinks it.

That is what he is offering now.

She has no command that will turn it into the same moment again.

“I would still like to understand what you meant,” she says at last. “About having a different choice.” ᴹᵏ

“We can talk about it when I'm ready. That doesn't mean you have to bring me something useful every time you ask to visit.” ᴹ

She looks at him.

“You'd rather I brought nothing.” ᴹᵏ

“I'd rather know what you're asking for. Before it turns into a thing I can only discover after I've said yes.” ᴹ

Makima looks at Rimuru, who has not filled the quiet for either of them.

Then at Maki, who has not become less watchful because the conversation sounds gentle.

“I'm asking to see you again,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau nods.

“Then ask. We'll answer.” ᴹ

* * *

He ends the visit himself.

Not because something has happened. Because the effort of sitting has changed, his back wants the support in their room, and he promised to say so before the choice was taken from him by somebody watching more carefully.

“That's enough for today,” he says. ᴹ

Makima looks at the clock.

There would have been a little time left under the limit they agreed outside.

She sets the cup down.

“All right.” ᴹᵏ

Rimuru stands.

She thanks Mau for seeing her. She leaves with the person who accompanied her in. No gift appears, no private message has to be found in a thing she touched.

Maki waits until he can look towards her without having to hold himself upright for the visitor.

“Ready to go back?” she asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

He nods.

“Very.” ᴹ

* * *

Yuka looks up when they reach the apartment.

She is at the table, one hand on the page of a book Ori has abandoned to tell Shizue something else. Mau waits until Maki has helped him settle before trying to speak over the little conversation.

Yuka comes to him instead.

“Tired?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

She puts her hand against his shoulder and asks whether he wants to go to bed or sit there for a little while.

“Here first,” he says. ᴹ

Maki tells them she will be nearby if they need help with the next transfer, then leaves him where he has chosen to be.

Yuka sits beside him.

“How did it go?” ʸ

He tells her.

What Makima asked. What he answered. The question about whose fault it was, and the answer he made clearer this time.

“She asked if you minded,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka looks at him.

“I said yes. I didn't tell her you were fine with it.” ᴹ

She lets out a breath.

“Good.” ʸ

“She stayed within what we'd agreed. That doesn't mean I know what she's going to do with it.” ᴹ

Yuka looks down at his hand on the table.

“Neither do I.” ʸ

He nods.

She puts her hand beside it, and he turns his palm towards hers.

* * *

The short note arrives after supper through the ordinary visiting-request route.

No demand. No new information she expects to buy an answer with.

*Thank you for seeing me.*

Mau reads it, then gives it to Yuka when she asks.

They look at the sentence together.

Neither calls it proof of anything larger.

He puts it with the other correspondence, where it will not become a secret object in a drawer.

Then he asks her to tell him what Ori was explaining to Shizue when he came home.

Yuka pulls her chair closer and begins.
