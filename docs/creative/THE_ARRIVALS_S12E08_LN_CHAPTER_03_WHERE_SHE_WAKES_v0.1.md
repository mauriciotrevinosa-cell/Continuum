# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 3 — Where She Wakes

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S12E08
**Continuity state:** B+236 night through B+237 breakfast. Mau carries Yuka a short distance by choice using familiar support. The adult couple choose intimacy and a first attempt at having a child after their mutual S11 discussions and the bounded review in V73 #1. No pregnancy, timeline or outcome is established. Both remain in their existing room. Ori comes by for breakfast. The house hears about the date by the couple's choice.

*When: Yuka takes out the key, Mau asks if he can carry her the rest of the way.*

She looks down the gallery. There are four steps to go.

“Why?” she asks. ʸ

“Because I want to.” ᴹ

She pockets the key. He smiles when she puts her arms around his neck, as though he'd been prepared to make a longer case for it and is delighted she hasn't made him. There is the familiar lift of his Authority and then there are his arms, warm under the places where he's holding her. He walks the four steps with her and discovers the key is still in her pocket.

“Well?” she says. ʸ

“I'm trying to remember why I asked.” ᴹ

“You wanted to.” ʸ

He looks down at her, laughing. She fishes out the key and gets it into the lock on the second try. He carries her through the door and puts her down only when she asks so she can shut it.

* * *

The lamp is still on. By the time Mau has his sleeping shorts on and has pushed his shoes far enough under the chair that neither of them will trip over them, Yuka has found one of his shirts. She slips it over her head.

“Can I turn around, or are you still—” ᴹ

She kisses him. He makes the beginning of a sound, then sets his bare feet down on the floor properly and kisses her back. When she lets him look at her, he finds the hem of the shirt with his eyes and then looks all the way up to her face.

“Is that how you're going to sleep?” ᴹ

She had thought she might spend all night wondering what to say when the moment came. Instead she takes him by the hand and gets him as far as the bed. She has heard the thing she wanted to know. He loved her all that time. He said it looking her in the face, with their dinner still between them and an ordinary evening left to spend however they liked. When she leans over him now, the only thing she can think about is getting closer to him than she's ever been.

She kisses him again. His hand comes up to her waist. Then he goes still long enough to ask.

“Are you sure?” ᴹ

She draws back so he can see her.

“I've never been more sure of anything.” ʸ

It makes him smile. She can feel it when he kisses the heel of her hand. She keeps it against his cheek.

“Do you remember what we talked about on the island?” she asks. ʸ

His eyes come back to hers.

“About waiting?” ᴹ

“About trying, when we both wanted to.” ʸ

The weight of her on the bed shifts when he sits up. He has to put a hand behind himself, and she moves with him so he can get comfortable. It gives him time to answer. She likes that he takes it.

“I want to try,” he says. “Do you?” ᴹ

“I do.” ʸ

She has said those words to him before. He hears it too. His face changes in a way she wouldn't know how to describe to anyone who hadn't been there that day. She pulls the shirt off over her head and gets her elbow caught in the sleeve. He helps her out of it. They're both laughing by the time it lands on the chair.

They bump noses trying to kiss again. Yuka says it was his fault, with enough conviction to make him laugh right against her mouth. She takes his hand and puts it where she wants it. A sound escapes her, one she hadn't expected, and he stops.

“Are you okay?” ᴹ

“Yes. Give me a second.” ʸ

She catches her breath against his shoulder. He waits. When she's ready, she draws him back to her.

There is a little while when they're both trying so hard to do everything right that Yuka has to put her face against his neck and laugh again. Mau asks what he did this time. She tells him she doesn't know, she's never done it either. That makes it easier. They find their way together, slowly enough to ask and answer, and after a while she stops thinking about what comes next.

* * *

Later she takes up most of his half of the bed.

He moves his hand under the cover to see if there is any more of her he can touch. There isn't much. She has a leg over his and her cheek on his chest. When he puts his hand against the back of her head, she lifts it just enough to kiss him once more.

“I love you,” he says. ᴹ

“Go to sleep.” ʸ

He closes his eyes. There is a little pull on the skin of his chest when she smiles against it.

“I love you more,” she whispers. ʸ

He laughs. Her arm tightens around him. He falls asleep with his mouth still turned up at the corners.

* * *

In the morning, Yuka is in almost exactly the same place. Her hand has moved down his side in the night. She has the edge of the blanket caught between two fingers and a little crease on her cheek from where she'd slept against him.

Mau stays still. He could reach the water if he moved his arm, and in a while he probably will. For now he wants to look at her. In the Trial he'd got so good at imagining how she might let him go that sometimes it had felt like remembering something she'd already done. She shifts now, half asleep, and reaches for him before she's opened her eyes.

*What an idiot,* he thinks, with his cheek in her hair. He had spent all that time trying to decide what she'd do, and here she is, pulling him closer in her sleep.

She stirs a moment later and finds him looking.

“Good morning,” she says. ʸ

“Good morning.” ᴹ

They both laugh a little. She slides up to kiss him, still smiling when she does.

Someone knocks.

“Dad?” ᴼ

Yuka looks at him. The clothes lift off the chair. She blinks and somehow they're both dressed, her feet still bare and a different one of his shirts over her shoulders.

“Convenient.” ʸ

Mau looks down at his own shirt. It's inside out. He decides to let it be.

“Come in.” ᴹ

Ori opens the door enough to put her head around it. Breakfast is ready, she tells them, and people have questions about the anniversary. She says *people* very carefully.

“Go ahead,” Mau says. “We'll be right behind you.” ᴹ

Ori looks as if she might have one of the questions herself. Then she decides it can wait and leaves the door ajar. Yuka waits until the footsteps are far enough away before laughing against Mau's shoulder.

“It's inside out,” she tells him. ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

She helps him fix it.

* * *

They go down holding hands. Yuka thanks Mai for the candle cover, Momo for getting the vegetables right, and Rem for teaching her husband what a lemon is. Mau says he already knew what a lemon was and gets an unexpectedly solemn look from Momo.

Then Yuka leans across the table towards Ori.

“I saw our window,” she says. “The tree went right underneath it.” ʸ

Ori puts her cup down.

“He did it slow?” ᴼ

“Exactly where you said.” ʸ

Ori wants to know if she saw the one next to it. Yuka did. She gets a piece of bread and sets it where the window would be if they were looking down at the village. Ori reaches for another piece for the Fort and Mau moves his plate to make room. Under the table Yuka finds his hand again and holds it while Ori shows her where they should go next time.
