# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 5 — The Line in Chalk

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S12E09
**Continuity state:** B+238 afternoon, after the Fort sleepover. Mau plays a seated Fort game with Umaru, Rem, Ori, Chloe and Milim, then helps with ordinary supper preparation within his current limits. No new Fort membership, purchases, combat clearance or pregnancy update.

*When: Mau puts down the plate, Umaru asks if he can throw a button.*

“Probably,” he says. ᴹ

“That's the entry requirement.” ᵁ

He's come to bring back a plate Rem lent him for the anniversary dinner. He washed it himself. He had also expected to be on his feet for less than five minutes, so he's grateful when she takes the plate and puts a cushion on the bench beside her. At the other end of the table, Ori is working out how wide to make a circle in chalk. Chloe turns a jar lid upside down and gives it to her to draw around.

“Bigger than that,” Milim says. ᴹⁱˡ

“Then everyone will get in,” Chloe says. ᶜʰ

Milim turns that over for a second.

“What's wrong with everyone getting in?” ᴹⁱˡ

“You won't be able to tell who won.” ᶜʰ

Milim sits down. Chloe keeps the lid where it is.

Umaru sets six buttons out in a row. The game is to slide one from behind the line and get it as close to the middle of the circle as possible. You can hit somebody else's button as long as the one you throw stays on the table. There's a cup for anything that falls. Mau takes a thick brown button. It looks like it will go where he puts it. By the time he's chosen it Rem has picked the flattest button on the table.

“You've played before,” he says. ᴹ

“I was here when we were deciding whether there should be a cup.” ᴿᵉ

“Is there a prize?” ᴹ

Umaru looks at Rem.

“I said I'd make something,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

“For everyone,” Chloe adds. ᶜʰ

“The winner gets first choice.” ᵁ

Milim has picked the smallest button. She sets it down a hair behind the chalk and brings one finger against the edge. It shoots the length of the table and lands against the back wall. Nobody has time to move.

“I can do that softer,” she says. ᴹⁱˡ

“Next round,” Ori says. “I'll get it.” ᴼ

Milim watches her go. She looks at the cup and then at the wall.

“It should count if it lands in the cup from over there,” she says. ᴹⁱˡ

“You can try it after we finish,” Rem tells her. ᴿᵉ

She says it with such mild certainty that Milim starts looking forward to it instead of arguing.

* * *

Mau gets his first button into the circle. He'd intended to have it stop just short, since the middle is the easiest place to be knocked out. He looks at Chloe. She looks at his button long enough for him to realise she was waiting for somebody to make exactly this mistake.

“You could go for hers,” he says, pointing at Ori's. ᴹ

“I could,” Chloe says. ᶜʰ

She uses his to stop hers. His button rolls on its edge, turns once and falls flat just outside the circle. Chloe's stays where his was.

“You said you were going to help me,” Ori tells her. ᴼ

“I told you how I was going to do it.” ᶜʰ

Ori thinks back. Then she leans over and asks Chloe where she'd have to hit to get it out. Chloe tells her, in a low voice which is perfectly audible to the rest of the table. Ori gets her button halfway there and almost falls forward when it stops. She says she has an idea for the next round. Chloe goes still for a moment, trying to figure out what it is.

Across from them, Umaru has her elbow on the table and her head close to Rem's.

“I can hit both of theirs if I hit yours first,” she says. ᵁ

“Yes.” ᴿᵉ

“You could tell me where to hit.” ᵁ

“I want first choice too.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru sits up and looks delighted. Mau has seen her get that exact look when Rem makes a small decision everybody else has already made for her. She aims at Rem's button anyway and hits it so gently that it only goes a little deeper into the circle.

“Thank you,” says Rem. ᴿᵉ

Umaru puts both hands over her face. Rem touches her wrist and laughs, and Umaru peeks at her between her fingers.

* * *

Milim wins the third round. She spends most of it watching how far Chloe draws her finger back before she makes contact. When her own turn comes, she uses so little force that Mau thinks she hasn't touched the button at all. It slides the last finger's width into the circle and stops before it can disturb anybody else's.

Milim looks at Ori first.

“Did you see it stop?” ᴹⁱˡ

Ori nods. Milim looks at everybody else to make sure they saw it too. Then she stands up and goes around the table with her button to see if she can get it in the cup from the other end. Chloe goes with her, carrying the cup so they can move it if the first throw goes badly.

Rem starts to gather the chalk. Mau braces his hand on the bench and gets to his feet.

“Are you going?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

“I was going to ask if I could help make the prize.” ᴹ

“She said she's making it.” ᵁ

“I can peel apples,” he says. ᴹ

Rem looks up at him.

“Can you do it sitting down?” ᴿᵉ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Then you can do as many as you feel like.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru wants to be the person who tastes the apples. Rem tells her she can taste the ones she peels. By the time they have all got to the kitchen, Umaru has decided she's also very good at peeling. She sits close enough to Rem that their elbows get in each other's way and refuses the perfectly good chair Mau moves out for her. He puts it back and starts on the first apple.

The peel comes off in one piece until he tries to show Ori. Then it breaks halfway down and drops into his lap. Ori takes the bit that's still on the knife and holds it up to see how long it would have been. Chloe finds her a plate to lay it out on. Milim says she can make one longer and Rem gives her the next apple.

When Yuka comes looking for Mau, he's still sitting in the chair where Rem left him, eating the first apple in quarters because it has become too small to use. He gives her a piece. She takes it and stays to find out who's going to get the longest peel.
