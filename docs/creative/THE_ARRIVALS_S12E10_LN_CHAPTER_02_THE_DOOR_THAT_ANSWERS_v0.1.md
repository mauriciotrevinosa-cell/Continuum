# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 8 — The Door That Answers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S12E10
**Continuity state:** B+241. Ramiris and Beretta continue living in the village while managing their existing Tempest labyrinth responsibilities. They invite Milim and Mau to try an ordinary model for the harder challenge promised in S8E51 CH02. Door and exit tests are paper and wood only. No dungeon established in the village or tactical deployment.

*When: Milim gets to the third wall, she puts her game piece down and knocks.*

Ramiris has put a piece of cloth over the back of the model so nobody can look down into it. Milim can see three short corridors through the hole on her side. One of them turns right. One turns left. The one she picked goes straight for a while and ends at a wall with a little mark in it. She has put her finger on the mark and tried to move it in both directions.

“You have to find the way in,” Ramiris tells her. ᴿᵃᵐ

Milim knocks again. From the other side of the table, Beretta reaches in and moves the wall a little to the left.

“Thank you,” Milim says. ᴹⁱˡ

Ramiris flies around to where she can see what he did.

“It has a person behind it,” he says. “She knocked.” ᴮᵉ

“The person was supposed to stay hidden.” ᴿᵃᵐ

“Then the person should answer very quietly.” ᴮᵉ

Milim moves her game piece through the gap and beams at him. Ramiris looks at the new opening for a long time. She tells Milim to leave the piece right where it is and takes a pencil from beside the cloth.

Mau has been allowed to look at the model from above. He was supposed to say where he would put the first ten shadows, and he's still holding the ten little pieces Beretta gave him in a cloth bag. He picks up one of the others and turns it over. There is a little band of ink around it.

“What's this one?” he asks. ᴹ

“The person everybody came to get.” ᴿᵃᵐ

“Where does that person go if Milim opens the wrong door?” ᴹ

Ramiris looks at the game piece Milim just moved. She puts the pencil down and lifts the cloth herself. On the other side of the model, the marked piece is sitting in a little room with exactly one way in. She has put two bends into the corridor outside it. Mau can see the routes she'd been working on, and that every one of them has somebody going the same way when they get close to the room.

“You could put a second door in,” Milim says. ᴹⁱˡ

“Then his army can use that one,” Ramiris says. ᴿᵃᵐ

“I thought the army was there to get the person.” ᴹⁱˡ

Ramiris opens her mouth to answer. Then she looks at Mau. At Tempest he'd sent enough of the army ahead that she'd been able to spend a considerable part of her afternoon watching the floor count go up instead of getting to see how he'd deal with any one particular floor.

“The army was going to get in,” Mau says. “Getting somebody back out would have been harder.” ᴹ

Ramiris considers that. She takes the bag from him, picks out six pieces and leaves the other four in his hand.

“Well. You can find out. I'll give the person a second door, and you're going to have to be on the right side when it opens.” ᴿᵃᵐ

“Can I knock?” he asks. ᴹ

She gives him a long look.

“You can try.” ᴿᵃᵐ

* * *

They run it again. Beretta keeps the cloth in place with one hand. With the other he notes the turns where somebody on the inside could hear a knock. Milim takes two pieces this time, one for herself and one for the person she's helping, and objects when Mau expects her to wait behind him while he looks for the second door.

“The person can walk,” she says. “I'm going to take them around.” ᴹⁱˡ

“Which way?” ᴹ

She tells him. He looks at the model and sees that it would work if he can persuade Ramiris to open a passage which normally goes the other way. He asks whether he can call ahead from where he is. She makes him point to who could hear him. Then she moves a wall.

They get the marked piece out with one of his still on the wrong side. Ramiris is pleased about that until he asks her to show him the route to the other piece. She looks over all the walls she moved. There is one spot where she can lift two of them together. She does it, and sees that it opens the way in from the outside again.

She puts them back down. For a little while nobody speaks.

“I need a way to open only one,” she says. ᴿᵃᵐ

Beretta reaches for the pencil. Ramiris takes it first and draws a mark on the underside where only she'd be able to see it.

“I have to be able to tell which one I'm opening,” she says. “Even when everybody else is shouting.” ᴿᵃᵐ

Milim stops with a suggestion halfway out of her mouth. She takes the marked piece to the edge of the board and sets it down where it can wait. Ramiris gives her a little nod and finishes the drawing.

On his way out, Mau asks if they can try it next time with a place he's already been. He could draw it for her and she could tell him which of the walls she wants to move. Ramiris says he can bring a drawing, but she's going to choose which way the door opens. He thinks about it, then asks how much of the drawing she wants.

“Enough for me to ruin your plans,” she says. ᴿᵃᵐ

He tells her he can do that. Milim asks if she can come too. Ramiris is already putting a fourth chair beside the table when she says yes.
