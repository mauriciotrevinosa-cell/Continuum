# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 8 — Before He Answers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S12E11
**Continuity state:** B+241. Mau brings the possibility of a work conversation with Makima to Yuka and the house before sending the request. The family understands his motive at different speeds. Rimuru agrees to attend with renewed mini Inn visitor terms. Umaru withholds personal welcome. No access to the household, new pact or transfer of a shadow.

*When: Mau puts the letter in front of Yuka, she sees that he hasn't folded it yet.*

There's an envelope beside his elbow. The letter itself is still flat, with a line drawn through most of the first sentence.

“What are you asking for?” Yuka says. ʸ

“A conversation. About what she wants me to help her with now. At the mini Inn, with Rimuru there if he's willing.” ᴹ

She sits down and pulls the letter a little closer. He has written that they can discuss a time if she is comfortable with the same terms. There's a place where he started to write about something she offered before the gate and then stopped. Yuka puts a finger beside it.

“What's this?” ʸ

“I started asking her about the fear-devil. Then I realised that would give me a reason to see her without having to put the other reason down.” ᴹ

He leaves the pen where it is. She waits.

“I'd like to help her,” he says. ᴹ

The words irritate her as much as she thought they would. She looks at the letter. It helps a little that she can see where he tried to take the easier way and crossed it out himself.

“Why now?” she asks. ʸ

He takes a while over that one. She can hear somebody taking a pan down in the kitchen, and the lighter sound of it being put on the table.

“At the quarry they wanted to know whether I'd fight because they already knew what I'd do if somebody else was going to get hurt. The Architect thought he could build a place around that answer. And sometimes I think there must be a part of me that has to do it because everybody else seems so sure.” ᴹ

He stops with his hand beside the envelope. His thumb goes over the crease once and leaves it alone.

“She talks like that about herself,” he says. “Like there's a thing she'll do every time because of what she is. I think there are times when she could do something else. I'd like her to know what one of those times feels like.” ᴹ

Yuka looks at him. She knows what she could say about his chances. She knows what she could say about Makima making him believe that helping her is the decent thing to do. She has said some of it before and will probably want to say it again. Right now he's waiting to see what she thinks of a letter he hasn't sent.

“What if she just starts doing whatever you want her to do?” she asks. ʸ

He picks up the envelope and turns it over. He hadn't thought that far, or had hoped it wouldn't have to be asked that plainly.

“Then I think we're going to have to talk about it.” ᴹ

Yuka puts her hand on the back of his.

“I'd like you to bring it up early enough that she still has an answer of her own.” ʸ

He nods. She can see he means it, and that he doesn't know yet how difficult it will be.

“And I'd like to come hear what the others think,” she says. ʸ

“Okay.” ᴹ

He leaves the envelope on the table while they go to find Rimuru.

* * *

Umaru finds out because Mau comes to ask if she has time to listen. She says she does and goes to get Rem. When they come back, Mau is still at the table with Yuka, Rimuru and Maki. There is enough room between him and the end for both of them, and he waits for them to sit.

“Is she coming here?” Umaru asks when he's told her. ᵁ

“The visitor room again, if we agree to it,” Mau says. ᴹ

Umaru takes a minute. Then she looks at Yuka.

“Do you want her to?” ᵁ

“I'm still deciding what I want,” Yuka says. “I do know where I'd be all right with them talking.” ʸ

Umaru puts her hands in her lap. Yuka knows she's thinking about a chair near the clinic bed and the days when no one could tell her when she would get to use it. She remembers Umaru saying, after she'd come home, that she wanted to hear the end of a story Yuka had forgotten starting.

“I don't want to have to go see her and be nice,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“I'd tell you myself when she was coming,” Mau says. “You can do whatever you want that afternoon.” ᴹ

“I might want to be angry.” ᵁ

Mau takes her seriously enough to sit with that for a second.

“Okay.” ᴹ

Umaru leans her shoulder into Rem. When Rimuru asks whether there's anything else the rest of them should know, she says she would like the date if they choose one. She has promised Ori an afternoon. She'd like to be able to say yes to Ori without wondering if somebody is going to send for Mau halfway through.

He tells her which afternoon he can keep. Then Rimuru says he can sit in on the meeting, with Maki available at the mini Inn if they need to stop or come back early. They agree on one conversation. They can decide whether to do anything else after they know what it was about.

* * *

Back at the table in their room, Yuka watches Mau fold the letter. He has put the afternoon with Ori on the little paper by the window first. His writing goes out past the edge of the line. He has left space enough below it to put the other appointment on when they get an answer.

“Do you want to hear how I put it?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

He reads it all the way through, including the part about what he hopes the conversation can be. Yuka listens to the last line and asks him to read it again. She tells him the way he said it the first time was better. He sits down and writes it the first way, while she can still remember it with him.
