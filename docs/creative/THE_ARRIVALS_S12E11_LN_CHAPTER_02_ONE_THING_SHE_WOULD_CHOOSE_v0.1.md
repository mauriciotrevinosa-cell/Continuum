# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 10 — One Thing She Would Choose

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S12E11
**Continuity state:** B+244 afternoon and evening. Makima accepts a scheduled work conversation with Mau and Rimuru in the mini Inn visitor room under renewed terms, Maki nearby. Reze's S11 release remains in force. Makima proposes asking one person at the holding site what they want in the presence of a neutral witness; names, access, and timing remain pending. Mau reports to Yuka. No gift, new pact, project, or permission to enter the family home.

*When: Makima sees that Mau can get to the chair by himself, she starts to say something and waits until he's sat down.*

“You're walking farther.” ᴹᵏ

“A little.” ᴹ

She looks at the empty chair beside him. On her first visit he needed somebody to help move a cup. Now he's set three cups out on the table. Rimuru takes the one at the other end. Makima waits for Mau to move the chair nearest her before she sits in it.

“I wasn't sure you'd ask,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“I wasn't sure either.” ᴹ

She waits. He takes a drink while he can still do it without having to interrupt himself.

“Last time we talked about this, you told me you wanted to be close to somebody. You said one way you'd get there would be to control them. I wanted to know whether you'd thought any more about what else you could do.” ᴹ

“Since I came to see you?” ᴹᵏ

“Since you had to go home again.” ᴹ

Makima looks down at her cup. The drink hasn't had time to cool. There is room on the table for her to move it a little farther away.

“I wrote to thank you,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“I read it.” ᴹ

“And did you believe me?” ᴹᵏ

Mau thinks before he answers.

“I think you were glad you came.” ᴹ

She was. It's more disconcerting to hear him say that than it would have been to hear him say he trusted her. She moves the cup back. She can take a little of it now.

“There was a moment when I wanted to ask you to send the others away,” she says. “I thought if I got you on your own, you might say what I came to hear.” ᴹᵏ

Rimuru looks over at her. He doesn't ask what she came to hear. She is glad of it. She'd had an answer for the question before she left and hasn't liked it since.

“Why didn't you ask?” Mau says. ᴹ

“I thought you'd say no. And then I thought you'd be right to.” ᴹᵏ

He doesn't look especially surprised by the first answer. The second makes him put his cup down.

“What did you do with the rest of the afternoon?” he asks. ᴹ

Makima looks at him. For a while she thinks about which answer might be worth telling. Then she tells him she went home, found the thing she'd meant to put away before she left and sat for a little while without putting it away. There'd been nothing else she could do to make the visit go any better. He asks whether she'd have come again if he hadn't written.

“I wanted to. I was deciding whether I could just ask you again.” ᴹᵏ

“You could have asked,” he says. ᴹ

She likes the way he says it. Then she remembers he could have turned her down a second time and finds she likes it a little less.

* * *

Rimuru puts a sheet of paper on the table. He asks whether there is anything they might actually do differently after the conversation. Makima looks at the empty sheet. It takes a little time before she can think of an answer which doesn't begin with Mau offering to do something for her.

“When you asked Reze whether she could go out of the room, I knew what she was going to say,” she tells him. ᴹᵏ

“You knew what she wanted?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“I knew she'd say it if I let her.” ᴹᵏ

“Would you have let her if I hadn't been there?” ᴹ

She sees the answer on his face before she says it.

“No.” ᴹᵏ

He leaves her time to add something. She waits for it too. There isn't anything she wants to add that would make the answer more accurate.

“There are still people who won't tell me what they want while I'm the only person who gets to decide what happens next,” she says at last. “One of them has asked to speak to somebody from outside. I've been saying I need a reason before I agree.” ᴹᵏ

“Sounds like they gave you one,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Makima looks at the blank page. She can think of how to make the request narrower, how to have it happen where she can hear all of it, how to choose a person who will take her side before they get through the door. She would have called all three things sensible a little while ago. She still thinks the first one could be.

“I could ask who they would want there,” she says. “I could ask them again with somebody else in the room so they can say what I got wrong.” ᴹᵏ

Mau looks up at her.

“Is that what you want to do?” ᴹ

She had been waiting for him to tell her she got the answer right. He seems willing to wait as long as it takes for her to give him one.

“I want to see if they'll tell me,” she says. ᴹᵏ

He nods. She would have liked a little more than that. Still, when Rimuru asks what he'd have to know before anybody agrees to go, she turns towards him. She has enough of an answer to make a beginning. The choice of person and place will have to come later, with an answer from the person who asked.

* * *

At home, Yuka is sitting by the open window with a pencil tucked into the cuff of her sleeve. Mau tells her what Makima said about wanting them out of the room, and about asking somebody what they want to do next. He waits until Yuka has asked who might go before he tells her they haven't decided that part.

“What did you say when she suggested it?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“I asked if she wanted to.” ᴹ

Yuka holds his gaze for a second. Then she gets the pencil out of her cuff and sets it down.

“What did she say?” ʸ

He tells her. Yuka sits with it for a little while. In the yard, Chloe calls for Milim, who turns out to be standing right below their window. Yuka looks up in time to see Mau smile at the answer.

“Tell me when the person answers,” she says. ʸ

“I will.” ᴹ

She puts the pencil back into her sleeve, forgets that she's done it and spends the next minute looking for it. Mau watches long enough to see how she'll react before he tells her where it is. She pulls it out and points it at him for another second before she starts to laugh.
