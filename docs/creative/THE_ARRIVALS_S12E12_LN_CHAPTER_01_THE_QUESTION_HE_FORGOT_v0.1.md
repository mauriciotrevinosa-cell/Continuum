## Chapter 11 — The Question He Forgot

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S12E12
**Continuity state:** B+245 morning. Mau speaks to Shoko about wondering whether Yuka could already be pregnant. Shoko explains the uncertainty and offers both of them a place to ask. No pregnancy is found or implied. Creator decision is that the anniversary and the following night do not result in pregnancy. Mau retains his ordinary rehabilitation plan.

*When: Shoko finishes checking his foot, Mau asks if she has time for a question about somebody else's.*

“Foot?” she says. ˢʰ

He looks down at the one she's just put back on the floor.

“No. Sorry. I had a different question.” ᴹ

Shoko waits. He's got both shoes on now. He's quite capable of getting up and coming back tomorrow with something else to put in front of it. She moves the chair nearest her a little farther away from the door and puts her notebook down.

“Is it a question about you?” she asks. ˢʰ

“Sort of.” ᴹ

“Does it need the door shut?” ˢʰ

He gives her a grateful look. She gets up and shuts it.

When she comes back, he's got the laces of one shoe pulled much tighter than the other.

“Do you remember what we talked about with DIO?” he asks. ᴹ

“I remember.” ˢʰ

He nods, mostly to himself. She had expected to be able to help him get to the next bit by saying that. The next bit still takes him a while.

“Yuka and I decided we'd like to try,” he says. ᴹ

Shoko lets him see her smile before she says anything. She knows how long both of them have been thinking about it. She asks how he's feeling about having made the decision.

“Happy,” he says. “Also, I think there might be a baby on the way.” ᴹ

“What makes you think that?” ˢʰ

He looks at her very seriously. Then his ears go red.

“Oh,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

She looks down at the notebook until she can keep a straight face. When she looks up again, he's waiting for an answer with the same expression he wore when he asked her whether he could go outside for the first time.

“Has anybody explained to you how this works?” ˢʰ

“Yes.” ᴹ

She raises an eyebrow. He takes his hands off the edge of the chair.

“I don't remember all of it,” he says. “I remember the part about it being possible.” ᴹ

“I can see why that part stuck.” ˢʰ

He puts one hand over his face. It takes about a second before she hears him start laughing behind it. She lets herself laugh too. He gets his hand down eventually, and looks so relieved that she reaches over and rests hers on the back of his.

“Trying means there's a chance,” she says. “It can take time. There are plenty of things that affect when it happens, and neither of you has to work it out by guessing. If Yuka wants to come in and ask, she can. If she wants you with her, come together.” ˢʰ

He looks at her hand.

“I think I woke up this morning and suddenly wanted to know what to do if it worked,” he says. ᴹ

“Did something happen this morning?” ˢʰ

“She got up before me. I heard her in the kitchen. And I thought about coming out one morning and there being three of us. I wanted that to be true so much I forgot it might take a while.” ᴹ

Shoko sits with that for a moment. He had asked her a question that he thought ought to have a simple answer. She'd had a guess about why he wanted it, and what he's telling her now is a great deal better than the guess.

“You can want it,” she says. “You've got time to get used to the idea too.” ˢʰ

His fingers move beneath hers. After a while he says he'll tell Yuka he came. Shoko asks whether she'd want to know he was this nervous. He says she probably knows already. Yesterday he caught her looking at him while he was moving a stack of books off the low shelf and trying to pretend he needed the space.

Shoko lets go of his hand so he can retie his shoe.

“You could put the books back for now,” she says. ˢʰ

“She already did.” ᴹ

He gets the tension right this time. Then he sits back.

“Have you had breakfast?” he asks. ᴹ

“I had half of it.” ˢʰ

“Where's the other half?” ᴹ

She glances at the shelf behind her. There is a wrapped piece of bread and a cup with about a mouthful left in it. Mau looks at the two things and asks if she wants to go and have something warm. He can wait in the courtyard while she puts her things away.

“You don't have to wait outside,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

She stacks her notes while he tells her about something that happened while he was helping Rem with the apples. He's halfway through explaining why Milim's peel had to be measured twice when she gets the last of the books onto the shelf. She leaves the chair pulled out beside her desk. He finishes the story on the way to the kitchen.
