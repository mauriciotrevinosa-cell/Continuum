# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 13 — Two Lights

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S12E13
**Continuity state:** B+246 afternoon. Ori asks about named shadows in the original life. Mau tells her that her earlier self chose Lantern's name in V30 #11. Lantern is already part of the current legion and responds without speech. Ori has neither been offered custody nor moved a unit into her shadow yet.

*When: Ori asks if all his soldiers came with names, Mau asks what she needs the answer for.*

She opens her notebook so he can see. There are two columns on the page. The first has names he can make out. The other is completely empty, except for a small mark beside Beru which she explains means he answered the question before she'd finished it.

“What did he say?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“That you gave him the name because it was worthy of him.” ᴼ

Mau thinks about answering that. Across the yard, Frieren is putting the things she'd been carrying on a bench so she can come and see what Ori is writing. It takes her a while to get a long piece of paper to stay put in the wind.

“You can leave his answer there,” Mau says. “You might want to put mine underneath it.” ᴹ

Ori gets her pencil out. She has names which came with soldiers, names she assumes came with them because she cannot imagine anyone making them up, and a few she hasn't asked about yet. Mau gives her the ones he knows. They get to Lantern and she turns the book so he can see that his name is by itself at the bottom.

“I wanted to know this one most,” she says. ᴼ

“You gave him that one.” ᴹ

She looks up.

“I did?” ᴼ

“In the other life. He was very large and he'd just come out of my shadow. I think we all stood there trying to get used to how much dragon there was. You looked at his eyes.” ᴹ

Ori draws two little dots beside the name. She hasn't asked what color they were. She's seen them herself.

“What did I say?” she asks. ᴼ

“You thought they looked like lights. You said Lantern, and when I called him that, he answered.” ᴹ

She keeps the pencil in the same place. One of the dots grows a little larger.

“I like it,” she says. ᴼ

“I did too.” ᴹ

Frieren sits beside her by then. Ori looks at her before she asks the next question.

“Can we say hello to him now?” ᴼ

Mau looks out towards the patch of open grass where there is room for a dragon to fold his wings. Frieren asks Ori whether she wants to take the notebook with her. Ori says yes and goes ahead with it under one arm while they follow at a pace Mau can keep.

* * *

Lantern rises with his wings close to him. Ori has to walk a little way round before she can see both lights at once. She holds her notebook in front of herself, considering how she wants to do this.

“Hello,” she says. ᴼ

Lantern lowers his head. Mau keeps his hands at his sides. He'd wondered whether she'd want him to explain anything first, but Ori takes another step on her own and shows Lantern the page. He looks at the notebook, then at her. He gets his head low enough that she can sit down and still be looking straight at him.

“Did you like that name?” Ori asks. ᴼ

One of his eyelids comes down and stays there for a moment. She laughs and sits on the grass. She starts to tell him what Beru said about his own name. Lantern settles the rest of his chin on the ground while she tells it.

Mau finds a place beside Frieren. They let Ori talk as long as she wants. At one point Frieren leans over and asks him if Ori was wearing a coat the first time. He says she was. Frieren asks which coat, and he tells her. By the time Ori has finished, Frieren has thought of two other things to ask him about that afternoon.

On the way home, Ori holds the notebook under one arm so she can take both their hands. She tells Frieren she wants to write the answer herself. Frieren says she can read it to them over supper if she likes. Ori walks the rest of the way thinking about the sentence.
