# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 14 — If It's a Date

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S12E13
**Continuity state:** B+247. Rimuru asks Mau for help finding the words to invite Shizue on a date. He asks her himself. She accepts by choice and picks the day. No change in rooms, duties or relationship agreed for either person beyond the date.

*When: Rimuru asks if there's anything good to eat by the lower path, Mau asks who he's taking.*

Rimuru sits down across from him. That answers most of the question.

“Shizue, if she'd like to go.” ᴿ

Mau puts a finger between the pages of the book he was reading. The lower path reaches a stretch of level grass inside the protection before it turns towards the road. There is room to eat there, and there is a little drop in the bank where you can sit and listen to people walking home above you. Rimuru took him there once when he wanted to talk without using up another cup of tea.

“Would you cook for her?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“I could.” ᴿ

“Would you enjoy it?” ᴹ

“I'd spend all afternoon worrying about the lid.” ᴿ

Mau leaves the book on the table. He has a square of cloth somewhere which would go around two bowls without a lid if they weren't carrying anything liquid. He can offer Rimuru that, and ask which kind of bread he wants. He has got as far as trying to remember where he put the cloth when Rimuru sets both hands on the table.

“I know what I want,” Rimuru says. “I figured it out that night at the Fort. I sat next to her for three stories and every time somebody asked if I was staying for the next one, I looked at her before I answered.” ᴿ

Mau waits. Rimuru keeps looking at his own hands.

“I want to take her out somewhere because I like her. I'd like her to know that's why I'm asking.” ᴿ

“You could say that.” ᴹ

Rimuru looks up at him. For a moment Mau thinks he's going to ask for a better answer. Then he sits back in his chair.

“I was going to ask her if she wanted to look at the bank, because somebody might want to put a bench there.” ᴿ

“Has anybody asked for a bench?” ᴹ

“I was going to.” ᴿ

Mau laughs. Rimuru gives him a hurt look which lasts about as long as it takes him to start laughing too.

“Get her something you want to eat with her,” Mau says. “I'll lend you a cloth for it. You can put a bench out there another day if you still want one.” ᴹ

“And what if she asks whether it's a date?” ᴿ

“I'd say yes.” ᴹ

Rimuru draws a breath. It is the first time he's looked like he's taking any of this as something he could do this afternoon.

“Stay here,” he says. “I might come back and tell you about the bench.” ᴿ

Mau takes his book with him to the window seat. It gives Rimuru a chance to come back without having to walk all the way round the table.

* * *

Shizue is checking which pieces of paper are hers before she goes back to the common room. She has worked her way down to a folded sheet of sums with Alice's name at the bottom. Rimuru asks if she's got a minute and she sets it down for later.

“I'd like to go out with you,” he says. “Somewhere near. We can take something to eat.” ᴿ

“Do you have somewhere in mind?” ˢᶻ

He tells her about the bank. He gets far enough to say there's no seat there and stops. Shizue is waiting, and when she sees the expression on his face she asks if there is something else.

“I'd like it to be a date,” he says. ᴿ

Shizue looks at the paper she just put down. There is nothing on it she needs to finish before she answers him. She keeps it where it is and looks up.

“I would like that,” she says. “Could we go tomorrow?” ˢᶻ

He nods too quickly, then has to ask what time would suit her. She tells him when she expects to finish her lesson. He writes it down, and she takes the pencil back to add that she'd like them to wait until it's cool enough to sit outside for a while.

He looks down at the two things they've written. His smile goes from a small one to one he would have to work to put away.

“Tomorrow, then,” he says. ᴿ

Shizue puts the time on her own paper before she goes. She can hear him folding his behind her, carefully enough that he gets both parts inside.
