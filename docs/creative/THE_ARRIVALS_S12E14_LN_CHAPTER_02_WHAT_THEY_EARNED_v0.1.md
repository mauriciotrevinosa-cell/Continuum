# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 16 — What They Earned

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S12E14
**Continuity state:** B+249. At an ordinary, assessed small Gate, Mau directs an existing shadow crew from outside. After their clear he makes a brief accompanied survey within Shoko's walking limits. Shop credit increases from the crew's earned clear, independently of household salvage. The Trial's spending makes this replenishment incremental. No direct combat clearance, spent new points, new bond, or transfer between personal credits and house accounts.

*When: Jin-Woo asks if he's going to cross, Mau says he'd like to see the map first.*

Jin-Woo lowers the slate to his lap. There are two pockets in the chamber. The smaller one has a turn sharp enough that you wouldn't see the far end until you got to it. The people who've been checking the entrance have marked how much of the first passage they could see from the threshold. He puts a finger on the point where their report ends.

“My two got this far and came back,” he says. “We could send yours the rest of the way.” ᴶʷ

Mau looks at the opening. A while ago it would have been quicker to go and see for himself. He can still think how he'd do it. The rock on the other side of the line is pale and even, and he can imagine putting his foot down on it before anybody has finished asking him to wait.

He takes the slate and has another look at the bend.

“Muster can go in with the crew,” he says. “They can clear it while we stay out here.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo makes room for him on the stone he'd been leaning against. Mau sits while he calls Muster up. The shadow listens to where the survey ends, goes to the lip of the opening and stops long enough for the others to follow it. Mau stays on the stone while they disappear around the turn.

For the first few minutes he has his hands on his knees. When he finds himself leaning forward, he moves one hand to the strap of the bag he's brought to keep what they can bring out. He can see Jin-Woo notice it.

“How often do you get to send them and sit down?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“More often now.” ᴶʷ

Mau looks over at him. Jin-Woo has taken the empty carrier off his shoulder and set it where it can be reached from the stone. He's doing a better job of sitting still than Mau is.

“What do you do with the time?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Depends how much they give me. Yesterday I got through half a story with Suho before somebody needed to know where I'd put something.” ᴶʷ

“What did you do?” ᴹ

“Finished the story. It was one of his things. He knew where it was.” ᴶʷ

Mau laughs. His hand loosens on the strap. He asks which story, and Jin-Woo tells him the beginning while they wait.

* * *

The first sign from inside is Muster at the turn. One of the ordinary shadows brings out a piece of ore and puts it down beside the slate. Muster reports the cleared passage, the shallow last room and a patch of loose stone the humans can step around if they choose to go in. The second group is already bringing its finds back. Mau asks them to leave the pieces they haven't looked over at the mouth, where Jin-Woo can help him sort them.

He checks the shop when the clear comes through. The balance goes up. It goes up by less than he was hoping for and more than he had the last time he looked. He can remember paying for a small loaf in the Trial and wishing it could be one of the things he would never have to count again. He leaves the shop open for another moment and looks at the difference.

“Enough?” Jin-Woo asks. ᴶʷ

“For a start.” ᴹ

Mau closes the window. The ore on the ground is still there for Fern to weigh when they get back. He puts the first piece in the bag while Jin-Woo checks the next.

“I'd like to go as far as the turn,” Mau says. ᴹ

Jin-Woo nods and gets up first. They wait for the last shadow to come out of the passage and step aside before they go through. It takes them a little longer than Mau expects to get to where the passage bends. At the turn he sees the mark Muster left by the loose stone. He can also see the shallow room beyond it. He puts his hand against the wall and stands there long enough to get a good look.

“That'll do,” he says. ᴹ

He turns back with Jin-Woo. For some reason it's the walk out he likes best. He can feel how much ground he has actually covered, and there's enough left in his legs to have a conversation on the way home.

* * *

Fern counts the ore in front of them. Mau holds the end of the bag up so she can get the last little piece without digging around for it. She asks whether he wants one set aside. He says she can use all of it and tells her what the other side of the outing earned him.

“Do you have something you want to buy?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

He thinks about it. In there he'd spent days keeping himself warm enough to sleep. He'd known exactly what he would buy if he could get another little bit of room in the balance.

“I'd like to save some of it,” he says. ᴹ

“For something?” ᶠᵉ

“Just for a little while.” ᴹ

Fern nods and writes down the weight of the ore. She puts the bag aside for him to take next time. They go home with it empty. Mau likes that too.
