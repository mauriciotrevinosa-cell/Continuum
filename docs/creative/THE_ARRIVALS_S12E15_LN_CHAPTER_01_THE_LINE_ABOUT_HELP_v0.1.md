# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 17 — The Line About Help

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S12E15
**Continuity state:** B+250 morning and afternoon. Makima brings a copy of a proposed road declaration to the village by the usual visitor route. The paper identifies a procedure under discussion, not settled law, a named alliance, or proof of an order against Satella. She asks to accompany Mau on his next small Gate visit. He brings the request to Yuka first.

*When: The man at the road table says it won't take long, Makima stops to see which part he means.*

There is a carter in front of him with a bag across one shoulder. The man has asked him where he means to sleep and who will answer for him when he gets there. The carter answers the first one. He's been on this road before. There is a place he can buy supper and a place the horses can drink.

“I'd be happy to have that on the paper,” the man says. “You can sign at the bottom.” ᴬ

The carter puts his bag down so he can get both hands free to look at it. He reads as far as the space left for his name and looks towards the road again.

“I only asked how to get to the crossing.” ᴬᵍ

Makima comes near enough to read the sheet lying sideways on the table. It begins with directions and the name of a person he can ask for help. Three lines from the bottom it says that anyone who receives that help agrees to disclose who has taken them in. There's a blank beneath it for a second name. She can see where the man has pointed, at a place a little farther down.

“Does he need to sign to go on?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

The man looks up. The carter moves his bag out of the way as if he'd been afraid somebody would want to get past.

“It's a proposal,” the man says. ᴬ

“Did you tell him that?” ᴹᵏ

He takes his finger off the line. After a moment he says the road is open. The carter picks up his bag. He asks for directions once more, gets them this time and goes on without the paper.

Makima watches the man put the sheet back on top of the others. He puts it on the table the same way, with the bottom facing the place where the next person will stand.

“May I have one?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“Are you thinking of settling somewhere?” ᴬ

She waits until he looks at her again.

“I'm thinking of reading it.” ᴹᵏ

He gives her a copy. She carries it unfolded as far as the crossing.

* * *

Fern gets the sheet by the usual route. There is a short account of what Makima saw on the back, including the words the carter used and the fact that the man called it a proposal. Fern brings it to Rimuru and Mau before the next visitor hour.

Mau reads the line about help twice. If he were halfway along a road with two hungry people and somebody asked him to put down a name before telling him how to get them fed, he can think of a few names he'd give before he asked to read the rest.

“Does the carter know what they were asking for?” he asks. ᴹ

“He might know now,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Fern points to the note on the back.

“She left the next one on the table.” ᶠᵉ

They go over where the table was, and who they can ask whether anyone has seen one of the sheets elsewhere. Fern sets the original aside and takes a copy for the road correspondence. When Mau asks if she wants to send something back to Makima, she shows him the receipt she's already written.

“She should know we read it,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

He waits while she adds the name of the person who'll be able to answer if there is another sheet. It is Fern's.

* * *

Makima is at the mini Inn for the rest of their arranged hour. The person she'd talked about last time has asked to be told who an outside witness might answer to. Rimuru gives her an answer she can take back. He can offer to ask for someone who works outside the holding site and let the person choose whether they want that witness or another. Makima asks him to write the offer down. He does, and leaves a space for an answer.

Mau thanks her for the paper from the road. She looks at him for a little while after he says it, and then puts the receipt Fern sent beside the other sheet.

“I heard you went to a Gate,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“A small one.” ᴹ

“Would you take me to the next one?” ᴹᵏ

He sets his cup down. He'd been ready to tell her what he went in for. It takes him a moment to get to a useful answer to what she actually asked.

“What would you like to see?” ᴹ

“How you decide what to do with a place after you can get into it.” ᴹᵏ

He thinks of the shallow chamber and the piece of ore Fern weighed at the table. He can imagine showing somebody how little of the work was his to do once he'd agreed to sit down. He can also imagine coming home and having to tell Yuka he decided Makima could come while he was sitting in this chair.

“I'll talk to Yuka about it,” he says. ᴹ

Makima lowers her eyes to the receipt. She puts it away before she gets up.

“You can tell me what she says,” she tells him. ᴹᵏ

“I'll tell you what I decide.” ᴹ

She stops long enough to look at him. Then she takes the letter Rimuru wrote and goes to bring it to the person who asked.
