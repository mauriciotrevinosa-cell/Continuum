# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 18 — The Answer He Brings

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S12E15
**Continuity state:** B+251. Yuka objects to Makima joining Mau's next Gate outing. Mau declines in his own words, keeps the planned afternoon with Ori, and reaffirms that they can consider a different outing later. No outing or permission to enter has been given to Makima.

*When: Mau tells Yuka what Makima asked, she sets her cup down before she gives him an answer.*

“I don't want her to come with you yet.” ʸ

She can see him getting ready to explain something. He stops and pulls a chair out instead. He asks if she'd like to tell him which part bothers her most. She sits down so she can think about it without holding the cup.

“You've been out once,” she says. “You got to the turn and came back. I liked hearing about that when you got home.” ʸ

“I liked doing it.” ᴹ

“And she's already asking for the next one. I want there to be a next one you get to do because you wanted it.” ʸ

Mau looks at her. He'd come in with his walking support in one hand. He puts it against the table and sits across from her.

“I could tell her what I did,” he says. ᴹ

“You could.” ʸ

He waits for more. There is more. Yuka has spent enough time hearing about Makima since the clinic to be able to bring up the whole list of reasons all at once. Right now she wants to give him this one while it still belongs to what they're deciding.

“If you want to take her out another day, we can talk about that day,” she says. “I don't want it to be the one right after she asked.” ʸ

He takes a moment with that. Yuka gets her cup back into her hand. She likes that he hasn't reached for the paper on the table yet.

“Okay,” he says. “I'll tell her.” ᴹ

“What did you want to do next?” ʸ

He looks towards the window as if he can see the Gate from there. Then he tells her he thought he might ask Jin-Woo if he'd bring Suho along as far as the outside of a survey. Suho had wanted to see the slate Jin-Woo drew on. They could draw something without going in. Yuka listens to him until he has the outing he'd wanted before Makima asked him for his next one.

“Would you like to come?” he asks. ᴹ

“Tell me when.” ʸ

He reaches for the paper then. He turns it over to find a clear place, and writes down the question he wants to ask Jin-Woo. Yuka doesn't have to wait for him to send an answer to Makima before he remembers it.

* * *

He takes the reply to the mini Inn at the end of the next visitor interval. Rimuru is with him. Makima waits at the entrance while he reads it to her. He could have left it for somebody else to give her. He wanted her to hear how he meant it.

“I'm going to keep the next outing as it is,” he says. “There might be a different one we can take together later. I'll bring it up when I have something in mind.” ᴹ

Makima looks beyond his shoulder. There are two chairs just inside the door, and there is nobody sitting in either of them.

“Was it because I asked when you were enjoying the idea?” she says. ᴹᵏ

“A little.” ᴹ

She looks back at him. She probably expected him to be less direct about it. He lets her see that he has had time to think.

“There are things the house is still getting used to,” he says. “I'm one of the people getting used to them.” ᴹ

She lowers her eyes. For a second he thinks she might offer a way to make the outing smaller, or say she can wait outside while he does the rest. Instead she folds the paper he gave her and puts it away.

“I would still like you to tell me when you have something in mind,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“All right.” ᴹ

Rimuru gets the door when she goes. Mau waits long enough to see her reach the first turn, then goes back the other way. He has to be home early enough to let Ori choose where she wants to draw.

* * *

Ori wants the steps because there is room beside her for Umaru. They have got as far as deciding whether Lantern needs to fit on one page when Mau comes out. Ori looks over at him, then moves both pencils she had been keeping in the space where he usually sits.

“Can he be on two?” she asks. ᴼ

He looks at the pages. She has already drawn one of his feet nearly as large as her hand. He sits down and tells her she might need three. Ori takes that very seriously. Umaru finds them another sheet and asks what they're going to do about the wings.

Mau has time enough to find out.
