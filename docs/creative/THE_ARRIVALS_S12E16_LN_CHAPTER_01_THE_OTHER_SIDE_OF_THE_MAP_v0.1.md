# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 19 — The Other Side of the Map

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S12E16
**Continuity state:** B+252. Jin-Woo and Cha Hae-In choose a brief outing for Suho at the established outer marker, with Mau and Yuka. Suho stays outside the Gate. A separate shadow clear adds a modest amount of personal credit. Later the house agrees to a specific short, accompanied survey with Makima. No entry approval for her at other Gates or at the family home.

*When: Jin-Woo gives Suho the slate, Suho turns it over to find a side nobody has used.*

“I was going to show you that side,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Suho looks at the map on it. Then he looks back at his father.

“You already know how it goes.” ˢᵘ

Cha Hae-In puts a hand on the corner of the slate before either of them can drop it. She asks Suho where he wants to start. He goes as far as the marker, where Yuka is waiting, and draws it on the back. Then he asks her what should go on the other side.

“Where you want to go from here,” she says. ʸ

He draws a long line that turns away from the Gate. There is a bench on that side which Jin-Woo brought over when he realised they could spend more time looking at the entrance if somebody had a place to sit. Suho draws the bench, then adds the place where he left the carrier with their food in it.

“I don't think we need the carrier on the map,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Suho looks at him. Cha Hae-In takes her hand off the corner of the slate.

“It depends on what we're using it for,” she says. ᴴᶦ

Mau starts laughing before he can help it. He'd have liked to have a map that showed where he could get something to eat today. He asks Suho if he can put an arrow to the place where they have to turn to get back to the house. Suho hands him the chalk and tells him which way up he's holding it. He gets the turn nearly right. Yuka reaches across to move it a little closer to the bench.

They have lunch where Suho drew it. The shadow crew that Mau sent in are still working at first. Later, while he's eating, he gets the clear and sees a little more credit go into the shop. He closes it before Yuka has finished telling him something about the path they took to get there. She's found a place where she thinks she can walk beside him without having to go single file. When they get up to go back, she shows him.

* * *

At home, Fern asks whether he'd like to look at the drawing for the next survey. It's a shallow opening with a straight path, and she has already marked the outside turnout where the people who go with the crew can wait. Mau asks what they want to find out there.

“Whether the marker you saw on the road paper is actually there,” she says. “We can ask the people using the path too. I'd like to hear what they were told it was for.” ᶠᵉ

He puts one finger on the turnout. For a moment he has two separate ideas about what he could say. Then he asks if Fern has time to talk about both.

* * *

Yuka listens to the plan at supper. Mau wants to take a small crew, have them go in while he waits with Fern and Maki, and invite Makima for the outside survey so she can point out the kind of notice she saw. The part she asked to see would be the same part anyone present can see. He leaves the paper on the table while they talk.

“Do you still want to do that part with her?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

She looks at Fern's line around the turnout. There is no turn they need to promise to take afterward. She asks whether the crew can finish even if somebody wants to leave early. Mau says they can, and Maki says she can walk whoever wants to leave back to the post.

“I'd be all right with that,” Yuka says. ʸ

Mau waits while Fern asks what the rest of them think. By the time Fern takes the paper back, the plan is still small enough to fit on one side. Yuka helps Mau clear a space for it on the shelf, in the place where they put things he wants to remember before he leaves.
