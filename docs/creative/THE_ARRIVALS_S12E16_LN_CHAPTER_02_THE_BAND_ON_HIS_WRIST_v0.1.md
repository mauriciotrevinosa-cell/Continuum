# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 73
## Chapter 20 — The Band on His Wrist

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 73
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S12E16
**Continuity state:** B+254–255. In a scheduled mini Inn meeting with Rimuru and Maki present, Makima accepts the limited survey and offers an ordinary bracelet. She asks for an emergency shadow anchor. Mau discusses it at home before agreeing the following day. The bracelet has no active link and is Mau's to keep or remove. One unassigned ordinary shadow is placed with revocable consent and alert-only instructions, no routine reports. The unit remains part of Mau's 6,617. No new promise of automatic rescue or unrestricted travel. The survey still lies ahead.

*When: Makima sets the bracelet down, Mau asks if she can tell him why she brought it.*

They have finished talking about the survey. Fern's paper is between them with the time marked at the top. Makima had asked if she could walk the whole way. Maki showed her where they would wait, and she agreed to the place without asking for more room after it.

There is room now beside the paper for the little cloth she'd brought in. She opens it. The band inside is made of dark metal with a plain hinge. He can see one scratch across the inside where somebody has tested the catch more than once.

“I was hoping you'd wear it,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau looks at it, then at her. She is sitting very still.

“Is there something you want to ask for too?” ᴹ

She has a little smile for that. For a moment he thinks he made the question easier by getting it out of the way. Then she looks at the band again.

“Yes. I wanted to ask for a shadow. So I could call for you if I needed you.” ᴹᵏ

Rimuru sets his cup down. Mau keeps looking at her.

“What would you need me for?” he asks. ᴹ

“I can think of several things,” she says. “I know which one you'd agree to.” ᴹᵏ

He can almost hear Yuka asking what happens if she starts saying whatever he wants to hear. There is time to ask the question while Makima is still deciding how to put her answer.

“Which one do you want to ask me for?” ᴹ

She turns the cloth once. The metal moves a little on it.

“If I were in danger and I couldn't get away on my own, I'd want to be able to call you.” ᴹᵏ

He looks at Rimuru. This is close enough to what they offered Hinata that he can remember how much it mattered to be precise. Maki asks Makima if she understands she could have it taken away whenever she wants. She says she does. Mau tells her what it would report, that it can bring him a call or warn him about danger where it is, and that it won't send him a record of what she does the rest of the day. If there's time to ask before moving her, he'll ask. He can't promise an Exchange from anywhere. He can promise to listen if he gets the call.

Makima waits until he's got to the end.

“If you hear me and you're able to come, will you?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“I'd do what I could.” ᴹ

She seems to consider whether she wants to ask him to say it differently. Then she nods.

“I'd like to take the question home before we do anything with it,” he says. ᴹ

Makima puts the bracelet back in its cloth. Mau doesn't offer to carry it. She takes it with her when she goes.

* * *

Yuka listens to the question that evening. Rimuru tells her what Makima agreed it could report. Yuka asks Mau what he would do if the call came in while the two of them were at home. He puts the pencil down before he answers.

“I'd tell you. If I know anything about what's happening, I'd tell you that too.” ᴹ

“You might be in a hurry.” ʸ

“I'll still know where to find you.” ᴹ

She thinks about it. It's easier to imagine him putting the decision behind him because there's a call he can answer. What he has told her so far has all been a little before it needed telling. She takes his hand and asks him to repeat the part about when he can move a person without asking. He does. Then she tells him she's all right with those terms. She would like them written down where the house can find them if the call ever comes when he's out.

* * *

The following day, Makima leaves the band on the cloth while they go over what a test would look like. She wants to see the soldier before she agrees. Mau calls one from the unassigned part of his legion. It steps out far enough for her to see its hands. It waits in the space Maki has cleared beside the door.

“I'd like to try it,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Mau gives the instructions out loud. The soldier lowers into her shadow. She asks for his attention in the test they'd agreed, and he acknowledges her from across the room. Then she asks to see it again. The shadow comes out. She looks at it for a moment, asks whether it will be able to recognise the same request when she's frightened, and listens to Mau explain what it can do if she doesn't have time to ask. She decides to let it return.

Maki puts her chair back. Rimuru makes a note of the assignment so the house can tell which places are already covered. Mau can still see the bracelet on the table.

“Do you want me to have that if I take the soldier back tomorrow?” he asks. ᴹ

Makima looks at him.

“Would you?” ᴹᵏ

“If you ask me to.” ᴹ

She looks down at the band. Then she moves the cloth nearer to him.

“I'd still like you to have it,” she says. ᴹᵏ

He asks if he can look. She says yes. He checks the catch with his thumb and asks Ciel what she can tell from looking at it. She sees the metal, the hinge and the scratch. Mau closes the catch himself. It fits with room to move when he turns his wrist.

Makima watches him turn it. She'd been looking at his hand while she waited for the soldier to come out, as if what he did with it could tell her which answer he'd give. Now her eyes go straight to the little band.

“You can take it off if it's uncomfortable,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“I know.” ᴹ

He leaves it on while they finish agreeing where to meet.

* * *

At home, Mau tells Yuka about the anchor first. He tells her what the soldier can call him for and what he said when Makima asked whether he'd come. Yuka asks if she could have asked for more. He says he thinks she could have, and they talk for a little while about how difficult it was for her to accept the answer she got.

Then he puts his wrist on the table where Yuka can see it. She works the catch once with a fingernail and asks if he likes it. He says he thinks he does. She can tell he's waiting to find out whether that will make the conversation harder.

“You told me what it was,” she says. “I needed that part.” ʸ

He moves his hand over hers. She gets up to close the window, comes back and settles across his legs so he has to give her the side of the chair she likes. He puts his arm around her and tells her the last thing Makima asked about the survey. Yuka listens until he finishes. Then she asks if he's going to be back in time to eat with her.

He looks at the time on the paper. He tells her yes.
