# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 1 — The Place on the Paper

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S12E17
**Continuity state:** B+256. Makima accompanies Mau, Maki and Fern on the agreed exterior survey. Existing shadows handle the small Gate. The group finds and documents a notice connected with the road declaration, distinguishing what was seen from what is still unknown. Makima respects the agreed turnout and leaves with the group. No entry to the village home, new construction, injury or change in unit count.

*When: Fern stops at the fork, Makima points to a little strip of ground no one has marked.*

“The table was over there,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Fern looks at the fold in the road. Someone coming from the crossing would have to choose whether to go left or right before they'd get close enough to read anything at the side. She had drawn the table a little farther along, where they could stand comfortably while people walked past.

“Could you see it from the other way?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

Makima takes two steps back and shows her. Mau stays where he is. There is room to get to where Makima is standing and he probably could do it. He wants enough left in his foot to walk the last bit with Maki when they go home.

On the inside of the turn there is a hole in the dirt, deeper at one edge than the other. Maki puts a finger on the stone somebody used to fill it. It moves. Fern walks over to look at it from where the person would have been sitting. Then she draws a new little line on her sheet.

“Do you remember how tall the table was?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

Makima shows her. The person who sat there had laid the papers at a height where anyone standing with a bag in their hands had to bend to read them. Fern asks whether the bag the carter put down was all he had with him. Makima thinks for a moment. She knows how much of the space it took up in front of him. She can't say what he'd left outside of it.

“It was the only thing I saw him carrying.” ᴹᵏ

Fern writes that part exactly as she said it.

* * *

The Gate is farther up, past the turnout where they have agreed to stop. Maki goes to the marker with Mau while he sends his crew on. One of the shadows has to wait for another to get around a stone at the mouth. The second one puts a hand out and gives it room.

Makima watches from the turnout. When Mau comes back he puts the walking support against the side of the bench and sits down. There is a place for her at the other end.

“Did you want to ask them to come back and do that differently?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“Which part?” ᴹ

“The one that stopped.” ᴹᵏ

He looks back towards the opening. The shadow is already gone.

“They got around each other.” ᴹ

She thinks about that. There was a time when she would have offered the first one a better instruction as soon as it was close enough to hear. It would have been possible to do. She watches the opening for a little longer before she sits.

“You were right that I wanted to see the next one,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau rests one hand on the bench. She sees the dark band on it when he moves it.

“Did you want to see it because I'd just been?” he asks. ᴹ

“I wanted to see you get up and do something. I'd only been allowed to see you sitting down.” ᴹᵏ

He laughs. She looks at him in surprise.

“I do a lot of sitting down now,” he says. “It's been good for some of the things I wanted to get done.” ᴹ

She looks towards the mouth again. The crew has been gone long enough that she would like to know how far in they've gone. Mau hasn't asked. After a little while, he asks her if she would mind holding the end of the slate so he can get the pencil out. She does. He writes the time the crew went in.

* * *

Fern finds the notice by following the other path around the bend. It has been put up at the height Makima showed her, under a board which still gives perfectly good directions to the crossing. Fern calls for them to come and read it. Mau asks for a hand on the step. Maki gives it to him and waits there with him while Makima goes on ahead.

The notice says that more copies of the declaration will be available at the crossing. It calls them a proposal. The paper Makima brought has a line about the person who offers shelter. The notice says information on shelter will be required when the proposal takes effect. The printed date is blank.

“They left themselves room to decide when,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Or somebody's waiting to be told,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

She makes a copy of the lines and leaves the notice where she found it. Maki asks whether she wants to take the higher path back and have a look at the other side of the crossing. Fern checks the time. They have enough for the walk they agreed on. She says she'd like to ask the people from the crossing to bring a copy back for her instead.

Mau looks at Makima. There's another corner from here and she's looking at it. She brings her attention back to him when he takes his hand off the board.

“I'd like to know who's on the other side,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“So would I.” ᴹ

She waits for him to say whether he thinks they could have a look. He asks Maki for the same hand on the step going down. By the time he has it, Makima is already coming back the way they came.

* * *

The first shadow is out when they reach the turnout. Mau takes the report while sitting down. The second one brings what it has found in its two hands and lets Fern decide where she wants it put. Fern closes the slate over the copy. She asks Makima if she'd like to hear back when they find out who put the notice up.

“I would,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Fern asks where to send it. Makima gives her a place she can find by the same road the carter used. Maki checks the time and they leave together.

Mau gets home in time to hear Yuka from the kitchen. He puts the walking support away and goes to see what she's making. When she asks him if he's hungry, he says yes before he tells her anything he found out on the road.

* * *

Later, in their room, Yuka asks him if he enjoyed the walk. Mau is undoing his cuff. He gets it open and leaves the other one alone.

“Parts of it. Maki found a place I could get back down without turning sideways. I was pretty happy about that.” ᴹ

“And being with her?” ʸ

He looks up. Yuka has set her comb on the bed between them, close enough for her to pick it up if she needs something to do with her hands.

“We laughed about something. I came home and thought about telling you what it was, and then I realised I didn't know if you'd want to hear it.” ᴹ

“I think I do.” She waits a second. “I think I want to be able to hear that you had a good time without having to guess what it means.” ʸ

He shifts towards her. She moves the comb so he can put his hand down.

“Remember what I told you when I was writing her that letter? She thinks she'll always do the same thing because of what she is. I want to help her find out if she can choose something else. That's still what I want from seeing her.” ᴹ

“I remember. I thought if I understood why, it'd stop bothering me that you wanted to.” ʸ

“Some days it still bothers you.” ᴹ

“Today it does.” She touches his loose cuff. “And I hate that I have to ask you for something you already told me.” ʸ

He turns his hand so the cuff drops and she can put her fingers through his.

“You can ask me again. I love you, Yuka. I only have eyes for you.” ᴹ

She studies him a moment. “You know you're the only person who can say that and mean it twice.” ʸ

He laughs. She hears herself do it too, and she puts her forehead down against his shoulder while it passes.

“What do you see right now?” she asks. ʸ

“My wife, who saved me the crispy piece at supper and then ate the corner of it off my plate. And who told me she had a question even though she was afraid I'd think she'd forgotten the answer.” ᴹ

“I was afraid you'd be tired of answering.” ʸ

“I like telling you why I love you. There are days I forget to say it before you ask. Today you asked me how the walk went before you asked me anything about her. I could tell you wanted to hear about my foot. I loved that.” ᴹ

She'd wanted to hear about it. She wants to hear exactly how he got down the step now. He tells her, including the part where Maki made him start again because he tried to look over his shoulder at Fern halfway through it. Then he remembers Yuka's waiting to hear why he laughed, and tells her that too.

By the time he gets up to finish changing, she's lying down with both hands tucked under her cheek. He draws the blanket back where her feet are and slips in beside her. She catches his sleeve before he can reach for the lamp.

“Come a little closer first,” she says. ʸ

He does. She lets the sleeve go and runs her hand up to the back of his neck. She kisses him once, thinks about it, and draws him back for a longer one. When he pulls away far enough to see her face, she looks delighted with herself for having kept him from getting to the lamp.

“Do you want me to put it out?” he asks. ᴹ

“In a minute. I want to see you a little longer.” ʸ

He stays. She asks him if he'd like to spend the rest of the night like this with her. He says yes and puts her hand where she can feel how quickly he means it. When he does reach for the lamp, she keeps hold of him until the room goes dark.

After they make love, he rolls onto his back to give her room to stretch out. She moves with him.

“Am I in your way?” he asks. ᴹ

“You're in exactly the right place.” She gets her feet under the blanket with his. “Can I have five more minutes of you like this?” ʸ

“You can have me till morning.” ᴹ

She gets closer anyway.
