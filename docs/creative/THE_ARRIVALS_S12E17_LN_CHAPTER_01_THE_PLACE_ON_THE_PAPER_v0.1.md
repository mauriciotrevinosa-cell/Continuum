*When: Fern stops at the fork, Makima points to a little strip of ground no one has marked.*

“The table was over there,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Fern looks at the fold in the road. Someone coming from the crossing would have to choose whether to go left or right before they'd get close enough to read anything at the side. She had drawn the table a little farther along, where they could stand comfortably while people walked past.

“Could you see it from the other way?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

Makima takes two steps back and shows her. Mau stays where he is. There is room to get to where Makima is standing and he probably could do it. He wants enough left in his foot to walk the last bit with Maki when they go home.

On the inside of the turn there is a hole in the dirt, deeper at one edge than the other. Maki puts a finger on the stone somebody used to fill it. It moves. Fern walks over to look at it from where the person would have been sitting. Then she draws a new little line on her sheet.

“Do you remember how tall the table was?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

Makima shows her. The person who sat there had laid the papers at a height where anyone standing with a bag in their hands had to bend to read them. Fern asks whether the bag the carter put down was all he had with him. Makima thinks for a moment. She knows how much of the space it took up in front of him. She can't say what he'd left outside of it.

“It was the only thing I saw him carrying.” ᴹᵏ

Fern writes that part exactly as she said it.

* * *

The Gate is farther up, past the turnout where they have agreed to stop. Maki goes to the marker with Mau while he sends his crew on. One of the shadows has to wait for another to get around a stone at the mouth. The second one puts a hand out and gives it room.

Makima watches from the turnout. When Mau comes back he puts the walking support against the side of the bench and sits down. There is a place for her at the other end.

“Did you want to ask them to come back and do that differently?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“Which part?” ᴹ

“The one that stopped.” ᴹᵏ

He looks back towards the opening. The shadow is already gone.

“They got around each other.” ᴹ

She thinks about that. There was a time when she would have offered the first one a better instruction as soon as it was close enough to hear. It would have been possible to do. She watches the opening for a little longer before she sits.

“You were right that I wanted to see the next one,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau rests one hand on the bench. She sees the dark band on it when he moves it.

“Did you want to see it because I'd just been?” he asks. ᴹ

“I wanted to see you get up and do something. I'd only been allowed to see you sitting down.” ᴹᵏ

He laughs. She looks at him in surprise.

“I do a lot of sitting down now,” he says. “It's been good for some of the things I wanted to get done.” ᴹ

She looks towards the mouth again. The crew has been gone long enough that she would like to know how far in they've gone. Mau hasn't asked. After a little while, he asks her if she would mind holding the end of the slate so he can get the pencil out. She does. He writes the time the crew went in.

* * *

Fern finds the notice by following the other path around the bend. It has been put up at the height Makima showed her, under a board which still gives perfectly good directions to the crossing. Fern calls for them to come and read it. Mau asks for a hand on the step. Maki gives it to him and waits there with him while Makima goes on ahead.

The notice says that more copies of the declaration will be available at the crossing. It calls them a proposal. The paper Makima brought has a line about the person who offers shelter. The notice says information on shelter will be required when the proposal takes effect. The printed date is blank.

“They left themselves room to decide when,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Or somebody's waiting to be told,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

She makes a copy of the lines and leaves the notice where she found it. Maki asks whether she wants to take the higher path back and have a look at the other side of the crossing. Fern checks the time. They have enough for the walk they agreed on. She says she'd like to ask the people from the crossing to bring a copy back for her instead.

Mau looks at Makima. There's another corner from here and she's looking at it. She brings her attention back to him when he takes his hand off the board.

“I'd like to know who's on the other side,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“So would I.” ᴹ

She waits for him to say whether he thinks they could have a look. He asks Maki for the same hand on the step going down. By the time he has it, Makima is already coming back the way they came.

* * *

The first shadow is out when they reach the turnout. Mau takes the report while sitting down. The second one brings what it has found in its two hands and lets Fern decide where she wants it put. Fern closes the slate over the copy. She asks Makima if she'd like to hear back when they find out who put the notice up.

“I would,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Fern asks where to send it. Makima gives her a place she can find by the same road the carter used. Maki checks the time and they leave together.

Mau gets home in time to hear Yuka from the kitchen. He puts the walking support away and goes to see what she's making. When she asks him if he's hungry, he says yes before he tells her anything he found out on the road.

* * *

Later, in their room, Yuka asks him if he enjoyed the walk. Mau is undoing his cuff. He gets it open and leaves the other one alone.

“Parts of it. Maki found a place I could get back down without turning sideways. I was pretty happy about that.” ᴹ

“And being with her?” ʸ

He looks up. Yuka has set her comb on the bed between them, close enough for her to pick it up if she needs something to do with her hands.

“We laughed about something. I came home and thought about telling you what it was, and then I realised I didn't know if you'd want to hear it.” ᴹ

“I think I do.” She waits a second. “I think I want to be able to hear that you had a good time without having to guess what it means.” ʸ

He shifts towards her. She moves the comb so he can put his hand down.

“Remember what I told you when I was writing her that letter? She thinks she'll always do the same thing because of what she is. I want to help her find out if she can choose something else. That's still what I want from seeing her.” ᴹ

“I remember. I thought if I understood why, it'd stop bothering me that you wanted to.” ʸ

“Some days it still bothers you.” ᴹ

“Today it does.” She touches his loose cuff. “And I hate that I have to ask you for something you already told me.” ʸ

He turns his hand so the cuff drops and she can put her fingers through his.

She does it one finger at a time. There was a point during supper when she'd almost asked him with her plate still between them. She'd managed to get as far as his name before asking him to pass something she could have reached herself. He'd passed it. She'd spent the rest of the meal wondering how long he'd wait to find out what she'd really wanted.

He has the same hand open for her now. When she looks up, he takes a breath and answers her as carefully as he did the first time she asked him why he was writing that letter.

“You can ask me again. I love you, Yuka. I only have eyes for you.” ᴹ

She studies him a moment. “You know you're the only person who can say that and mean it twice.” ʸ

He laughs. She hears herself do it too, and she puts her forehead down against his shoulder while it passes.

“What do you see right now?” she asks. ʸ

“My wife, who saved me the crispy piece at supper and then ate the corner of it off my plate. And who told me she had a question even though she was afraid I'd think she'd forgotten the answer.” ᴹ

“I was afraid you'd be tired of answering.” ʸ

“I like telling you why I love you. There are days I forget to say it before you ask. Today you asked me how the walk went before you asked me anything about her. I could tell you wanted to hear about my foot. I loved that.” ᴹ

She'd wanted to hear about it. She wants to hear exactly how he got down the step now. He tells her, including the part where Maki made him start again because he tried to look over his shoulder at Fern halfway through it. Then he remembers Yuka's waiting to hear why he laughed, and tells her that too.

She listens to the whole thing. She can hear the laugh in his voice again by the end of it. When he looks at her to see how she took it, she tells him she's glad he told her.

“I might need a minute the next time we talk about her,” she says. “I'd still like you to tell me.” ʸ

“Okay. What do you want me to do with the minute?” ᴹ

The question makes her look down at their hands. He has left enough room for her to draw hers away. She slides it the other way and gets her whole palm against his.

“This is good,” she says. ʸ

He sits with her. A loose thread on his cuff catches on her thumbnail. She winds it around her finger and pulls, and he turns his wrist so she can get the whole thing. It keeps coming out long after both of them thought it would end. When it finally comes free, he reaches past her to leave it on the table.

She kisses the corner of his mouth as he goes by. He puts his hand back on the bed and turns his face towards hers. The kiss is over by then. She can see he's still thinking about it, so she gives him another one.

“Will you sit here a little longer?” she asks. ʸ

He settles back beside her. She takes his hand again because she can.

* * *

Mau puts her comb on the table and leaves his belt on the back of the chair. When he comes to bed, Yuka makes room under the blanket. He draws it up over her feet and reaches for the lamp. She catches his sleeve on the way past.

“Leave it on for a while,” she says. ʸ

He lies down beside her. The cuff she caught is the one he never undid. She works the button through, pushes back the sleeve and kisses the soft skin beside the old scar on the inside of his wrist. He turns his hand over against her cheek.

“Can I leave a mark?” she asks. ʸ

Mau looks down at his shirt. He pulls the collar aside and shows her a place just below his collarbone.

“Here. I want to be able to see it tomorrow.” ᴹ

She'd worked out how to ask and what she could say if it made him laugh. His finger is still resting on the spot he chose. She puts her mouth there while she can feel it beside her lips. At first she takes her time over the kiss. Then his hand comes around her waist and she feels him draw her in. She holds him there until there's a faint mark she can see when she lifts her head.

He looks down at it. She gets to watch him turn red from his neck up.

“Are you going to check it in the morning?” he asks. ᴹ

“I'll check now if you get that shirt off.” ʸ

He gets as far as the last button before she takes it out of his hands. She pulls the shirt off and throws it at the chair. It lands underneath. Mau looks over the edge of the bed to see where it went.

“You can fetch it tomorrow.” ʸ

He turns back. “You want me here all night?” ᴹ

“Yes. And I want you to kiss me here.” She puts two fingers under her ear. “I've been trying to get you to notice that spot for days.” ʸ

“For days?” ᴹ

She brings his face down to hers before he can think of another question. He kisses her mouth first. His hand catches under the hem of her shirt, and she lifts her arms so he can pull it over her head. She leaves it beside the bed. He looks at her for a moment. Then he leans in and kisses exactly where she put her fingers.

She'd wanted to be able to tell him what it felt like when he got it right. His lips settle against that little patch of skin and her fingers go straight into his hair. He feels it. He stays where he is until she loosens her grip, then kisses her there again. This time she has the breath for one word.

“Yes.” ʸ

He comes up to see her face. She kisses him as soon as she can reach him, hard enough that he has to put a hand on the bed to steady himself. When he lies over her, she feels him hard against her through his shorts. She presses her hips a little closer. The sound he makes has her smiling against his mouth.

“I want you, Mau.” ʸ

He keeps one hand at her waist and looks her in the eye. “I want you too.” ᴹ

She'd been trying to work out a way to say something as simple as that all evening. She lets it sit between them for a moment, with his weight warm against her and his hair falling over the side of his face. Then the elbow he's resting on begins to shake. He lifts his head a little.

“Can we turn onto our sides?” he asks. ᴹ

She gives him room to lower himself beside her. He rolls onto his side first. She turns to face him and reaches back for the second pillow. They wedge it behind his shoulder and wait until he's found a place he can stay. She can tell when he finds it because his hand comes back to her waist.

They get the rest of their clothes off under the blanket. One leg of his shorts catches on his heel. She reaches down to free it and gets the blanket twisted around her own foot in the process. He laughs against her forehead while she tugs it loose.

“Very graceful,” he says. ᴹ

“You could've helped.” ʸ

“I liked having your hand on my leg.” ᴹ

She gets the blanket back over them, then puts her hand on his leg again. His skin is warm. She runs her fingers up the side of his thigh and rests them at his hip. He watches her do it with a look that makes her forget what she was going to say about the blanket.

She looks down at the mark she left him. “You can leave one too. Somewhere I can cover it in the morning.” ʸ

He touches a place near her collarbone with the back of one finger. She tips her head to give him room. His mouth is warm there. When he lifts it, she goes after him for a kiss, with her hand still at his hip.

“Come closer,” she says. ʸ

He puts a palm beneath her thigh as she lifts it over his. She takes hold of his waist and brings him towards her. She feels him enter her, slowly, and for a breath all she can feel is how close he is. His hand stays under her thigh. She puts her arm around his back and feels the way he holds himself still when her fingers tighten.

“Stay here for a second.” ʸ

He settles with his forehead against hers. She takes the second. She can feel his breath on her lips, then the effort he makes to let it out quietly when she shifts her leg. She wants to see his face. He lifts his head when she touches his jaw and gives her the time it takes to look.

“That was more than a second,” he says. ᴹ

“I know.” She moves a lock of hair off his forehead. “Do you want me to hurry?” ʸ

“I want to see what you do with the extra one.” ᴹ

She laughs softly. At supper she'd been trying to decide whether she could ask him a question she already knew the answer to. Now she wants to spend an extra second watching him wait, and she can see that he wants it too. She brushes her thumb over his lower lip. He kisses it once and stays where she asked him to.

“Now,” she says. ʸ

He moves inside her again. His hand draws her thigh closer to his hip, and she feels the change as soon as he does it. She'd meant to keep looking at him. He bends to kiss the place under her ear, and her chin lifts into the kiss before she has time to think. The laugh she had ready for him comes out in one short breath.

Her hair has caught between his cheek and the pillow. She starts to pull it out a strand at a time. He lifts his head to let her get it all at once, then helps her sweep it behind her shoulder. His hand ends up by her ear. She kisses the inside of his wrist where she did earlier, now that there's no cuff to work around.

“Do you need me to move?” she asks. ʸ

He moves the pillow a little closer and lies back down. “I want you there.” ᴹ

She'd expected him to ask for a little room after that. Instead he puts his arm back around her, and she feels him let his weight go against the pillow. She can lie just as close as she was a minute ago and feel him relax a little at a time.

After a while he reaches for the corner of the blanket and pulls it up over her shoulder. She finds the mark she left him and traces the skin beside it with a fingertip. Mau laughs before he has a full breath to do it with.

“I said tomorrow,” he says. ᴹ

“I'm checking that I did it right.” ʸ

“You did.” He touches a spot just below her collarbone. “Yours is here, by the way.” ᴹ

She tucks her chin to see it. He picked almost the same place she did. She laughs against him, and the laugh comes back when he tries to tell her how long he'd planned to wait before giving it away.

“You could've waited till morning,” she says. “I'd have been looking at yours.” ʸ

Mau puts his hand over hers when she reaches for it again. She can feel his heart under the two of them. He settles lower into the pillow so he can stay where she has him. She thinks about how many times tonight she's asked him for something twice. There's one more she'd like to ask for.

“Would you tell me again?” she asks. ʸ

“I love you, Yuka.” ᴹ

He knows which words she means. That makes her smile even before he finishes saying them.

“I love you too.” ʸ

She puts her forehead against his and keeps her hand over his heart for another few beats, until she's ready to let him reach for the lamp.

“Now you can put it out.” ʸ

“Am I in your way?” he asks. ᴹ

“You're in exactly the right place.” She tucks her feet under the blanket with his. “Can I have five more minutes of you like this?” ʸ

“You can have me till morning.” ᴹ

She gets closer anyway.
