# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 2 — The Person She Asked For

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S12E17
**Continuity state:** B+257. The woman at Makima's holding site is Ione, who accepted shelter earlier and asks to have her own sister as witness before discussing departure. Makima lets Ione control the letter and route through existing messengers. The sister has not replied. Reze's separate release continues. No use of Control, public disclosure of the site or transfer of responsibility to the village.

*When: Makima brings Rimuru's letter to the table, Ione asks if she's allowed to write on it.*

“It's your copy,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Ione puts it flat. There is a little space underneath where she can say whom she'd like to see. Makima has come prepared to answer whether the person Rimuru offered would be able to come all the way here, and how long it might take to have an answer after that.

Ione writes down two words.

“My sister.” ᴵᵒ

She turns the page. Makima looks at the words. They are much neater than the rest of the letter.

“Is she nearby?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“I'd like to find out.” ᴵᵒ

Ione waits, pencil still in her hand. She came here after someone tore the roof off the last place she had to sleep. Makima had offered the room and asked if she would tell her who had been coming by before it happened. Ione told her. Since then, there have been more rooms, and each time Makima has asked a small thing before she offered the next one. Ione had never said she wanted her sister there to hear any of it.

“I can send somebody to look,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

“I'd rather write to her. And I'd like to put the address on after you're done with the letter.” ᴵᵒ

Makima looks at the space Ione has left underneath the words. If she had it, she could find the sister sooner. She could find out if the sister had somewhere of her own and whether Ione would be safer there. She could think of an answer to almost every question Ione might ask before either one of them got the letter.

“Who do you want to take it?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“The man who brings the bread if he's willing. He gave my sister a ride once.” ᴵᵒ

Makima knows him. He would do it if she asked. Ione seems to know it too. She's looking down at the pencil now.

“I'd like to ask him,” she says. ᴵᵒ

There is a long enough silence for Ione to set the pencil on the table. Makima puts a clean sheet of paper beside it. She opens the door while she can still do it without making Ione ask what she's waiting for.

“He comes in the morning,” she says. “I'll ask him to come this far.” ᴹᵏ

Ione picks up the pencil again. Makima goes out while she decides how to start the letter.

* * *

Later Makima tells Rimuru that Ione picked her sister. She asks whether he would wait for an answer before bringing anyone else. He says he can. Mau is not at the table. She had brought the answer in the hope that he would be there, and now she has a little time to notice how disappointing it is that he isn't.

“Would you like me to tell him?” Rimuru asks. ᴿ

She considers it.

“I'd like to tell him when I see him.” ᴹᵏ

Rimuru folds the record and gives her back Ione's copy. She has left the space below the two words empty. When she gets home, she knocks at Ione's room and returns it to her. Ione shows her where she's put the finished letter, folded so the address is the only part the man with the bread will have to see. Makima asks if she would like another envelope. Ione says this one will do.

For a second Makima wants to know what she wrote. Then Ione thanks her for bringing back her copy, and Makima finds that is an answer she can go away with.
