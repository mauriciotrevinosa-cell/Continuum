# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 3 — One Place for Each

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S12E18
**Continuity state:** B+258 afternoon. Rem and Umaru choose a meal and time together. Umaru tells Rem that she can see some of Makima's help while staying angry about what happened to Yuka. No public position, reconciliation or change in visitor terms.

*When: Umaru asks where Rem has put her plate, Rem points to the empty chair beside hers.*

The little table at the end of the covered walk has room for exactly two plates and the dish Rem is bringing out. Umaru has expected to help carry something. She looks around for what Rem could possibly have left for her to get, and finds a little jar of salt by the window.

“I can bring this,” she says. ᵁ

Rem puts the dish down.

“You could also sit with me.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks at the jar. She puts it on the table, where it takes up about as much space as a thumb. Then she sits. Rem has made enough of the thing Umaru liked last time for both of them to have a second helping. They have come outside early enough that the long table is still empty, and the sound from the kitchen gets fainter when Rem pulls the door partway shut.

“You asked me to spend an afternoon with you,” Rem says. “I thought you might like to see the end of it.” ᴿᵉ

“Am I not usually here at the end?” ᵁ

“You are. Sometimes you get up to see if anybody has asked where you are.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru thinks about who might ask. Ori knows where she is. She came by already to show them what she did with the third piece of paper. Milim is taking the chair by the Fort door out to see if she can fix the leg without help. Mau went to get the book he left with Shoko and told her to tell Rem there was a plate he could wash tomorrow.

“I can sit here a while,” Umaru says. ᵁ

Rem gives her the piece she'd been looking at.

* * *

They are nearly done when Umaru asks if Rem thinks Makima is trying.

Rem sets her fork down.

“What have you seen?” she asks. ᴿᵉ

“She came back when Fern asked her to. And Fern says the thing she brought was something we needed to know.” ᵁ

Rem waits. Umaru takes a little while longer than she thought she would.

“I get mad when I hear it,” she says. “It feels like every good thing she does is supposed to make me less mad about Yuka, and I still get angry if I think about it for more than a minute.” ᵁ

Rem puts her hand on the table. Umaru takes it.

“You can tell me when you've seen her do something good,” Rem says. “You can tell me the other part too.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks down at their hands. She has a thought which she can tell will come out worse if she tries to make it sound reasonable first.

“I didn't like that Mau went to see her again,” she says. “I want to be able to tell him that and still go ask if he wants to play with us.” ᵁ

“I think he'd want you to tell him both.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru leans her shoulder into hers. She has been on the verge of asking for permission to do it the entire afternoon and suddenly that seems a little silly. Rem moves her chair nearer until the edge of it touches Umaru's. They sit that way while Rem finishes eating. When she reaches for the dish again, Umaru asks for the other end and Rem gives it to her. There is enough for two even when they both decide to have more.

* * *

Afterward Umaru brings the plates to the kitchen and leaves them on the counter where Mau can find them tomorrow. Rem thinks they're going to go back the way they came. Then Umaru takes her hand again and asks if she has time to go to the tree.

“You can see it from here,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

“I wanted to take the long way.” ᵁ

Rem looks at the light on the path. She has a little time before the end of the afternoon after all. She tells Umaru she'd like that and lets her pick which way to turn first.
