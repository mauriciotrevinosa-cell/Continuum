# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 5 — Two Pieces of Bread

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S12E19
**Continuity state:** B+259 evening, after Reze follows the lead at the crossing. She finds Denji and they share food by mutual choice. Denji remembers Makima and has his bond with Pochita. Reze confirms Makima is here and that Mau helped secure her release. No agreement to attack, house access or power use.

*When: Reze finds him, Denji is trying to get the last bit of apple off a piece of peel.*

She has two pieces of bread in a bag and an answer to a letter in her pocket. She stands at the end of the low wall with both, watching him turn the peel over to see if it looks any better from the other side. There is a man trying to get a cart out from beside the building. Denji puts his boots on the wall to give him room.

That gives Reze a good look at his face.

“Did you get enough to eat?” she asks. ᴿᶻ

He looks up.

She'd thought that if she found him, she'd know where to put her hands. She holds the bag tighter. He gets one foot down, then the other. For a second he's looking at her in the way you'd look at a person you might have called by the wrong name. Then the peel drops between his boots.

“You're here,” he says. ᴰᵉ

“So are you.” ᴿᶻ

He looks around her, then along the wall where she'd been standing. She can see that he wants to know who got here first. She can't blame him. She'd spent most of the walk wondering the same thing.

“It's me,” she says. “I brought you something.” ᴿᶻ

Denji looks at the bag. He stays where he is. She remembers a time when he would have had it open before he asked what was in it. She goes as far as the other end of the wall and puts one piece of bread down on the paper it came in. The other stays in her hand.

“I bought two,” she says. ᴿᶻ

Denji looks at both pieces.

“And you want one?” he asks. ᴰᵉ

“Yes.” ᴿᶻ

That seems to do more for him than her saying it was her. He picks up the piece nearest him. They eat standing where they are, at first. By the end he has sat down again and made a little room on the wall. She takes it.

* * *

“How'd you get here?” Denji asks. ᴰᵉ

“I don't know the whole of it. I was in a place I knew. Then I was here. Then I was in a room somebody else could decide whether I could leave.” ᴿᶻ

He looks down at his hands. There is a corner of the bread he hasn't eaten.

“Who?” he asks. ᴰᵉ

She takes a breath. She's glad she waited until he'd had most of the bread, even though the bit he has left is going to sit there now.

“Makima.” ᴿᶻ

For a while the cart is the loudest thing on the road. Denji waits until it has turned the corner before he says anything. Reze can see him counting something without moving his mouth. She thinks he might be counting the things he'd have to check before he could believe what she just said.

“You got out,” he says at last. ᴰᵉ

“Yes. A man came to speak with her and asked if I wanted to go outside. Then he kept asking until she let me answer.” ᴿᶻ

“What's his name?” ᴰᵉ

“Mau.” ᴿᶻ

Denji turns the rest of the bread over in his fingers.

“Is he still with her?” ᴰᵉ

Reze thinks about what she can say. Mau came back to a place where Makima could tell him more than anybody else. She remembers the way he spoke to her when there was a door to decide about. She can't fit both things into the answer Denji is looking for.

“He has people he goes home to,” she says. “He told me I could write to him. I did.” ᴿᶻ

She shows him the edge of the letter in her pocket. Denji looks at it long enough that she thinks he might ask to read it. Instead he eats the bit of bread he's been holding.

“Does she know I'm here?” he asks. ᴰᵉ

“She didn't hear it from me.” ᴿᶻ

He breathes out. He looks at his own shirt for a moment with a concentration she remembers. Then he looks back at her.

“I'm glad you found me first,” he says. ᴰᵉ

She had come here with a great many things she might say if he asked whether she had ever meant to come back. It turns out she can sit beside him for a little while longer without making him ask that.

“So am I.” ᴿᶻ
