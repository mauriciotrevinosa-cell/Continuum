# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 8 — She Can Hear It From You

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S12E20
**Continuity state:** B+262. Denji tells Power that Makima is in this world. Power chooses to stand with him. Reze gives the information she knows and agrees to learn what has changed before approaching Makima. No forced reinstatement of Control, completed coalition, or fight.

*When: Denji puts a third bowl on the table, Power asks if he's bought one for the cat.*

“Reze is coming back,” he says. ᴰᵉ

Power looks at the bowl. She has already taken a little more than a third of what he put down between them. She tips a piece back and asks if Reze knows about it. Denji tells her she knows they're eating. It seems to him that if Power wants her to know anything else, she can tell her when she gets here.

Reze brings something to drink. She takes the third place and asks whether she has missed anything. Power says she has missed the best piece. Reze takes a bite, looks at Power and tells her the next one will do. Denji listens to them both for a while. He'd been waiting all day for the part where he has to say what he's been thinking.

He says it before he can start counting the other things he could talk about.

“Makima's here.” ᴰᵉ

Power puts her bowl down hard enough for all three of them to hear it. Denji feels it in his own hand. She looks at Reze first.

“Did she send you?” she asks. ᴾᵒ

“I left,” Reze says. ᴿᶻ

“She lets people leave sometimes.” ᴾᵒ

“I know. This time she agreed I could stay gone.” ᴿᶻ

Power waits. Reze has put both hands on the table now. She tells them how long she's been out, what she asked for before she went, and what Mau did while she was deciding whether she wanted the door open. Denji has heard the short version. He lets Reze finish the rest for Power.

“And he still goes to see her,” Power says. ᴾᵒ

“Last I knew, yes.” ᴿᶻ

“Did he know who she is?” ᴾᵒ

Reze looks down at the bowl she hasn't finished. Denji can see her remember something she doesn't want to put in front of him.

“He knew enough to ask her to let me go,” she says. ᴿᶻ

Power goes quiet. Then she looks at Denji as if there is something he ought to have asked her already.

“What do you want to do?” he says. ᴰᵉ

“I don't want her to know where you are.” ᴾᵒ

He sits still.

“What if she finds out anyway?” ᴰᵉ

Power reaches over and takes the handle of his bowl. She doesn't move it.

“Then I want to be with you when she does.” ᴾᵒ

He looks at her hand. It's still tight around the handle. He puts his hand beside it where she can see it, and she lets go of the bowl long enough to put hers over his.

* * *

Reze goes outside when they finish. Denji finds her by the wall. She has her letter with her and has worked the fold at one corner until it is softer than the rest.

“Are you going to tell him?” he asks. ᴰᵉ

“Mau?” ᴿᶻ

Denji nods.

“What would you want me to tell him?” she asks. ᴿᶻ

He thinks about it. In the past there were days when he could have walked all the way to where Makima was and still hoped she might ask whether he'd eaten before she told him why he'd come. Now he can go back inside and Power will probably ask if he plans to buy enough food tomorrow. He would like to know he can keep doing that.

“I'd want him to know what she'd do if she thought she could get us back,” he says. ᴰᵉ

“Then she can hear it from you if it comes to that,” Reze says. “I'd like to know where she goes these days before any of us decides what comes next.” ᴿᶻ

He listens. There is a way of telling him what to do which always starts by making it sound like he already agreed. He waits for that part, and Reze waits beside him until he comes up with his own answer.

“I know somebody who saw her,” he says. “He'd talk to me again.” ᴰᵉ

“I'd like to come when you ask.” ᴿᶻ

He tells her she can. They go in together. Power has put the good piece she'd taken for herself on his plate, where she can claim it was always there if he says anything about it. He sits down and eats it.
