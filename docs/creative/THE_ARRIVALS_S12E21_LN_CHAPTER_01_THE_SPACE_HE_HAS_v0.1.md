# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 9 — The Space He Has

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S12E21
**Continuity state:** B+263. Yuka tells Mau the first attempts did not result in pregnancy. They take time together. Shoko and Maki see gradual progress in basic footwork, with recovery limits still relevant. Noise has prepared a bounded, unactivated devil-state model based on its sustained observation of Makima. No Control built, full-body trial, combat clearance, combat deployment or general release of the Sukuna archive.

*When: Yuka tells him she has a minute, Mau moves the book before she asks him to.*

“I might want two,” she says. ʸ

He puts it down on the table and makes a place for her. She sits across his legs. He has got good at putting his arm in the place that lets her rest her head against his shoulder. She has got good at taking his hand afterward so he doesn't have to wonder if she wants him to keep it there.

“I don't think it worked this time,” she says. ʸ

He knows what she means. She can tell by the way he gets a little quieter all at once. He looks at the window for a second. She lets him.

“Are you disappointed?” he asks. ᴹ

“A little. I wanted to see your face if I could tell you.” ʸ

“I wanted to see yours too.” ᴹ

She turns in his arms. He looks at her for a while and smiles a little when she does. She had been thinking about a baby all the way here. Sitting down, she finds she still likes what she came into the room for. She settles back against him.

“We can stay right here for a while,” she says. ʸ

“I'd like that.” ᴹ

By the time somebody goes past the window, she's got his book on her knee and is asking him to tell her what was happening before the part she's started reading. He tells her. He has to take one hand off her to find the page he's thinking of. She waits while he finds it, then puts his hand back herself.

* * *

In the yard, Maki makes him move around a line that's half as long as the one he used to practice on. The first time across it he puts his foot down where it needs to go. The second time he gets there before he's got enough weight off the other one.

Maki raises two fingers.

“Which one?” she asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

“Second.” ᴹ

“Tell me what you did.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He tells her. Shoko is sitting close enough to hear him explain exactly where it happened. She asks if he wants another pass. He does, with the part of him that can feel how close he was to getting it right. The other part of him has just begun to feel the little shaking in his leg. He looks back at the line.

“I'd like to stop while I can walk back by myself,” he says. ᴹ

Maki puts a foot across the chalk so he can take the turn out without stepping over it. He takes it. At the bench, Shoko asks how much of the shaking has gone. He tells her. He can stay sitting a while before they go on, and while he does, Maki goes to find water for both of them.

* * *

Noise shows him a drawing while he's waiting.

At first it looks like one of the outlines it uses to keep track of where a change stops. Then he realises whose hand it's using to demonstrate. The gesture comes from Makima. She does it when she has heard enough of a question to know which part she's going to answer.

*It settles this way,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau keeps his attention on the little drawing. It doesn't carry an order. It shows him how an energy he has seen a number of times could sit in a temporary body, the way the slime shape sits on a set of rules that he can step back out of. This one has more things to keep stable at once. There is a place where he can see what Noise would hold shut if it began to move in a way they hadn't chosen.

*Can it make somebody do something?* he asks. ᴹ

*It can move you,* Noise says. ᴺ

He gives that answer time to sink in. He'd like to know what it would feel like. He would also like to get all the way back to his own room today, with both legs still listening to him.

*Show Ciel what you have so far,* he says. ᴹ

*Already did.* ᴺ

He laughs a little. Maki has got back with the water. She sits at the far end of the bench, reaches over to give him a cup and asks what he is laughing at.

“I just had to be told somebody had already asked a second opinion,” he says. ᴹ

“Good. Now you know what it feels like.” ᴹᵃᵏ

He takes the cup. Noise folds the drawing away. For a while, all Mau does is sit next to Maki and drink the water she brought him.

* * *

Yuka finds him in the kitchen waiting for his bread to toast. She came down for the knife Mai asked her to fetch. She finds it, sets it aside and puts both arms around him from behind.

“Is that for me?” she asks. ʸ

“The toast?” ᴹ

“I already decided about the toast. Is the arm free?” ʸ

He puts it over hers and feels her settle. He'd expected her to let go when the bread was ready. Instead she stays where she is and reaches around him to lift it onto his plate. She waits until he's buttered the first piece and steals it out from under his hand.

Mai comes in while she's chewing. She finds the knife where Yuka left it.

“Were you going to bring this back?” she asks. ᴹᵃ

“Eventually.” ʸ

Mai looks from her to Mau. “Do you want me to get you out of there?” ᴹᵃ

“I think I'm doing all right.” He looks down at his plate. “I thought there'd be more toast.” ᴹ

Yuka breaks the last piece in half and holds it up where he can reach it. Mai takes the knife back out with her. She calls from the doorway that Yuka still owes her ten minutes. Yuka says she'll give her fifteen if she can have five more here.

“You'll have to ask him for those,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

Yuka sets her chin on his shoulder. “Can I have five more?” ʸ

He pulls another slice of bread towards him. “I can make more toast.” ᴹ

She kisses his cheek. By the time the second slice is done, he's figured out he can have a piece if he asks for it before he puts it down.
