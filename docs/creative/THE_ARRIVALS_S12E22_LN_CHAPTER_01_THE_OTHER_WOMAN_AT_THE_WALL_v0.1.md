# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 11 — The Other Woman at the Wall

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S12E22
**Continuity state:** B+265 late afternoon. Ione reaches the storeyard to meet her sister with Makima accompanying her as agreed. Denji, Power, Reze and two hybrids confront Makima. Ione and her sister are directed out before the first strike. An unintroduced observer remains beyond the yard. Makima deliberately calls through the emergency shadow.

*When: The woman beside the wall says Ione's name, Makima realises which of them is going to get there first.*

She had thought Ione would wait to see if it was all right to go the rest of the way. Ione leaves her standing in the middle of the yard. She gets to her sister at a walk and then has both arms around her before the other woman has put down what she was carrying. The bag hits the ground. Neither of them bends to get it.

Makima waits.

There is a person in the shade at the other end of the yard who has been waiting longer. He steps far enough out that she can see his hands. One is tucked behind the strap of a bag. The other is empty.

“Do you remember me?” Denji asks. ᴰᵉ

She looks at him. There is nothing on his face to tell her he came because he thought she'd say yes. Beyond him, Reze has stopped where she can see the road and the yard at the same time. Makima looks at her long enough to make sure, then gives her the space to look back.

“Yes,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Denji stands still. She sees the other two men then, one leaning near the cart and one farther back beside the closed part of the store. Both were here when she crossed the yard yesterday. She'd known one of them in another life. She had thought it would be more useful to hear what he wanted before she did anything about it.

“Where are you going?” she asks Ione. ᴹᵏ

Ione draws away from her sister. She has one hand on the other woman's arm.

“We thought we'd go across together. There's a place past the next turn.” ᴵᵒ

Makima nods. She almost tells them to go now. Reze takes a step to clear the road and looks directly at Ione.

“You can go wherever you were going,” Reze says. ᴿᶻ

Ione looks at Makima for a second. Makima takes a step towards the wall, so there will be room for both women to go past. Ione picks up the bag and takes her sister's arm. They walk to the opening together. Denji doesn't look away from Makima until they have turned.

* * *

Power comes around the end of the store.

Makima sees her before Denji does. She has a hand open at her side, held out like she's been trying to decide whether to take hold of something. When she gets close enough, she steps past Denji. He catches her arm for a second. She looks down. He lets go.

“Did you remember me too?” she asks. ᴾᵒ

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

The answer is the same. It sounds very different said to her. Power looks as though she expected a smile with it. Makima finds she can't give her one. She watches her move a little closer and knows suddenly that Power has been imagining this part for a long time.

“We'd like you to stay away from us,” Denji says. ᴰᵉ

Makima looks at him. The man by the cart has drawn himself up against the side of it. The other one has moved where he can see the door to the store. They'd come for something different from what Denji just asked for. Power has too.

“Is that what you all want?” Makima asks. ᴹᵏ

“I want you to remember the door,” Power says. ᴾᵒ

Reze looks at Denji. Makima can tell from that one look that there was a conversation she wasn't part of, and that this is the first moment it has gone differently. She could ask Denji to finish speaking. She can see exactly how to do it.

The man by the cart moves first. Makima gets one hand up and catches the first impact with her forearm. Something in the cart gives behind him. She puts her back against the low wall, far enough from the opening that Ione and her sister can keep going if they hear it. Neither man comes with her. They're waiting for the one who's already crossing the yard.

There is a girl standing on the far side of the broken fence, looking into the yard through the space between two boards. She hasn't put a hand on either of them. Makima meets her eyes for half a second. Then Power comes close enough that she can't afford to look anywhere else.

Makima opens her hand against the wall. In the short shade beneath it, she calls the soldier she'd asked for.

*I need help,* she tells it.

She hears the first answer as a movement at her feet. Power reaches for the blood gathering in her hand.
