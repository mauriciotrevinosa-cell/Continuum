*When: Mau stops reading in the middle of a word, Yuka keeps her finger on the page.*

She'd come in to sit with him after talking to Mai. The book was open to a part she remembered, and he had started reading it aloud while she was deciding how much room she wanted on the chair. She can feel him go still before she knows the sound has stopped.

“What is it?” she asks. ʸ

“Makima's called.” ᴹ

Yuka looks up at him. He has put the book down without getting to his feet. Her hand is still in the crease between the pages. She takes it out.

“What's happening?” ʸ

Mau listens again. The soldier saw the first blow and knows there are others in the yard. Makima made the request herself. The only names he's got so far are the ones she said into it when she could speak. Denji and Reze.

“Denji's the one she told us about,” he says. “Pochita is with him. If that part works the way she remembers it, there's more he can do than hurt me.” ᴹ

She remembers the table where Makima first told them what being eaten could do. She remembers sitting there while Frieren asked about the people who would have to live with a hole in what they remembered. She takes Mau's hand. He's already had things taken from him that people could only explain after he'd come back.

“Do you know how you'd come back if that happened?” she asks. ʸ

“I know how I'd try,” he says. “The wheel can learn. I don't know how much it would get the first time.” ᴹ

He could have left out the second sentence. He's close enough that she can feel him make the effort to say it anyway.

“I can get to her. I'd want to bring her away if I can,” he says. “If you want me to stay, tell me. I'll stay.” ᴹ

Yuka closes her fingers around his. The answer is there before she has time to put it into anything more generous.

“I want you to stay.” ʸ

Mau stays. He doesn't look at the door or at the place on the floor where he'll have to stand if he wants to go. He looks at her. She gets a breath in while she has him there. It's a terrible little relief to see that he means it.

“And I think you should go,” she says. ʸ

His hand tightens around hers. She leans close enough to kiss him before either of them can add anything to the answer.

“Bring her somewhere she can get help,” she says. “Then come home and tell me how it went.” ʸ

“Okay.” ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru is on his way in when Mau gets to the common room. Yuka tells him what's happening while Mau listens for the position the soldier can give him. They have a way back to the outer post if the road stays available. Rimuru says he'll be there. Shoko goes for what she wants to have ready in case he needs help when he gets there.

Noise puts the outline of the temporary form where Mau can see it. Ciel shows him the part they agreed could keep it from running any farther than he asked it to. He asks her how fast he can leave it. She gives him the answer she can give, including the parts they haven't used under pressure.

He looks back once. Yuka is by the doorway, where he can see all of her. She puts her hand up a little. He does the same.

Then he gives the soldier his place.
