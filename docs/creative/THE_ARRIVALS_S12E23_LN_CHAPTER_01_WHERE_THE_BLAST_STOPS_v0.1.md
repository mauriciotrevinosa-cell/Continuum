# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 13 — Where the Blast Stops

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S12E23
**Continuity state:** B+265, continuous. Mau intercepts Power's blood weapon. The continuing blood effect defeats the first RCT closure. A wheel turn corrects that specific effect and permits closure. Noise activates its temporary devil body from its Makima model with Mau's consent and Ciel monitoring. Makima accepts a bounded Infinity contract for this evacuation. Reze's blast stops outside it. No Control, permanent devil power or adaptation to erasure yet.

*When: Power brings her arm down, somebody gets his shoulder between her and Makima.*

The first thing Makima sees is that he hasn't brought anybody with him.

The second is that the blade got through.

Mau moves her behind him with the hand that still works properly. Power pulls her weapon back. A little of it stays where the cut is. He puts his other hand up to it and Makima sees it begin to close. Then Power closes her fist and the wound opens along the same line again.

“They were scared of you?” Power says. ᴾᵒ

He looks at her. She still has her fist shut. There's a ring of dark blood around the knuckles. He looks at it long enough that she can see where he's looking.

“Power,” Denji says. ᴰᵉ

She looks back at him. Mau hears the sound above his head at the same time Makima does. She looks up. The wheel has moved a little, enough for the next spoke to be where the first one was. Mau takes his hand away from his shoulder. The cut pulls closed while Power is still holding her hand shut.

Mau lets out a breath through his nose. For a moment Makima can see how much that first movement hurt.

*Now?* Noise asks. ᴺ

*Now,* Mau answers. ᴹ

The shape settles from his hands inward. He can feel it in the places where he had been using his breath to keep a movement steady. He asks Ciel for the edge of it. She shows him the point beyond which it would begin to take more than it has. He stops it there.

Makima sees the color go through his hair. It is the color she'd have expected to see in the window behind her if she'd turned far enough. Then he looks at her. There are rings in his eyes now, too. At the center of them, where she expects the last of her own color, there is a blue dark enough to make her think of water a long way from the shore.

He tilts his head. It takes her a second to realise why she knows the movement so well.

“Can you still walk?” he asks. ᴹ

She nods. She's looking at his face long enough that he asks her again. This time she says yes out loud.

“I need you to take my Infinity,” he says. “Until I get you somewhere safe. It stays with you while I'm here to hold it. Can you do that?” ᴹ

Reze is moving. Makima sees the change at her hand and wants to tell him about it. Mau looks back once and puts his palm out in front of Makima. He leaves it there where she can reach it.

“I need you to trust me,” he says. ᴹ

Makima puts her hand in his.

“Okay.” ᴹᵏ

There is time for her to notice that he meant it to be a question. Then the blast comes across the yard.

It reaches the space in front of her face and keeps taking time to cross it.

Makima can see the first bright edge of the explosion. She can see the pieces of dirt and a bit of wood from the cart suspended inside it. She can feel how close it should be. The cloth at her wrist doesn't move. Mau gives her hand one squeeze before he lets it go and turns towards the yard.

Reze is looking straight at him. There is dust on one side of her face where she was near enough to feel the beginning of it. On the other side her eyes are clear.

“You came for her,” she says. ᴿᶻ

Mau looks back at Makima once. She's still standing where he left her, with the blast close enough to put light on both of them.

“Yes,” he says. ᴹ

He steps forward.
