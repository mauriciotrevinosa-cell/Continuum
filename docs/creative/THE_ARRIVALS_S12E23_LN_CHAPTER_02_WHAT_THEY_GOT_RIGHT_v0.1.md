# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 14 — What They Got Right

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S12E23
**Continuity state:** B+265, continuous. Denji and Reze land a coordinated exchange and visibly regenerate from nonfatal damage. Mau acknowledges their skill and uses the spatial cut already known from Sukuna's archive. His public combat tools remain Shrine and the wheel. Infinity remains attached to Makima by the temporary agreement. Shared Exchange is pending an opening and a stable link.

*When: Denji asks if he knows what she did, Mau gives him as much of an answer as he can.*

“Some of it,” Mau says. ᴹ

“How much?” ᴰᵉ

He has a hand against his chest. Mau looks at it and then at his face. He could tell him what Makima said about what she wanted. He has already heard how it sounded from her side of a table.

“She told us about you and Pochita,” he says. “I'd hear the rest from you if you'd like to tell it.” ᴹ

Denji looks past him. There is a place where the blast left the wall clear around Makima. She hasn't moved out of it. One of the men has come close enough to see which pieces of the cart fell and which ones stayed up in the air. Denji keeps his hand where it is.

“Can I tell you after you let her go?” he asks. ᴰᵉ

“I'm going to take her out of here.” ᴹ

Denji looks at him again. Reze has moved just far enough that she can see his face. She makes a small movement with one hand. Mau sees it and turns, expecting an attack from where she is.

She uses the blast behind herself.

He'd read the way her weight went forward and assumed she would come along the path she could see. Instead she passes behind Denji. The flash throws both of their shadows at him and only one of them belongs to the person who's about to reach him. A tooth catches his sleeve. He turns with it, exactly as far as he has to, and Reze is already where his foot was about to come down.

The impact sends him back the length of the ruined cart. He gets his feet down. The weaker one slips and he goes as far as one knee before he has enough to get back up.

Behind him, Makima says his name.

“Stay there,” he tells her. ᴹ

She stays. He feels the small steady hold of the contract at the edge of his attention. His shoulder is still closed. He can tell which part of the movement hurt without losing the position of the person he's trying to keep in reach.

Reze gets up on the other side of the yard with one shoulder hanging too low. She must have hit the stone when she used it to turn. He sees her bring the arm back into place before he's got to his feet.

“You did that very well,” he tells Denji. ᴹ

Denji stops for half a breath. The teeth have already started moving again.

“Did what?” ᴰᵉ

“Got me looking the wrong way. I knew she was fast. I didn't think she could do that.” ᴹ

Reze looks at him. For a moment he can see her as clearly as when she was standing by the first door she wanted to walk through. Then she brings both hands up.

The man who was standing by the cart comes around the part of it they haven't broken. There's a sword where his hand was. A second one follows when he twists his arm free of his sleeve. He takes the shorter way around Mau. Mau turns with him far enough to see what he's doing. The first sword slides past his shoulder, and the second is already on its way towards the space Makima needs to cross.

“Behind me,” Mau says. ᴹ

She shifts on the word. The man changes direction after he sees her, and Mau catches him with a cut across the flat of the blade. It goes deep enough to part the steel. The man looks down and jerks his wrist once. The edge starts to grow back as he does it.

The other man is waiting on the other side of the yard. A spear comes out along his arm and stops short of the low wall. He can see Makima. He can see the little piece of the blast still hanging where it couldn't get to her, too. He holds his place long enough for Mau to know he's waiting for something else to happen.

Reze sees him waiting. She looks from his spear to Denji, and Denji brings his hand down at his side. That is all the warning she needs to move.

* * *

They come at him together again. This time he reads the place she wants to get to before he turns to see her. Denji catches a cut across the side of his arm as he comes in. It buys Mau about a breath. When he looks again, the arm is moving, and the cut is closing around the teeth that have pulled it wider.

Mau puts one foot behind himself. He takes in the line from the end of the cart to the stone where there is an opening out of the yard. Ione and her sister went the other way. He can see both people who would be close enough to that line to matter. One is Reze. The other is Denji. Neither is where he needs the full cut to go.

He sets the condition Sukuna used. The shape of it returns as soon as he asks for it, right down to the care Sukuna took over which way it could go. Then he draws the line. The world-cutting slash follows it.

The axle parts first. There is no noise from it until the two ends move. The wall behind it opens at the same height, far enough to let evening through. Reze gets one arm out of the way and turns the other in time to take the very end across it. Denji doesn't get his own weapon free before the part of it that crossed the line comes apart.

For a second he looks down at the pieces. Then he looks at Mau.

“You could do that to the rest of me?” he asks. ᴰᵉ

“I could choose where it goes,” Mau says. ᴹ

Reze is already using her arm again. Denji sees her get her hand up. One of the other men has stopped moving long enough to look through the wall. There are pieces of the cart falling into the empty space where the wheel was.

Mau takes one step towards Makima. She takes one towards him in the opening he has given her. Then Denji looks up, sees how much distance they've got between them, and reaches again for his chest.
