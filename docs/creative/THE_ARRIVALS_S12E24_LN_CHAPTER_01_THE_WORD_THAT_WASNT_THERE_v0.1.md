# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 15 — The Word That Wasn't There

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S12E24
**Continuity state:** B+265, continuous. Denji uses Pochita's presence as a false center to approach through Mau's blind side. Six Eyes and Movement Learner capture the trick, not the ability to separate their bodies. Mau deliberately permits a brief exposure to a localized erasure effect on his manifested state. Portions of Sky and Authority temporarily cease to exist for him. Shrine, the wheel and Makima's already-anchored Infinity survive this exposure. The wheel has not completed the erasure response at the cut.

*When: The sound moves to the far end of the yard, Mau almost looks at it.*

There are two places where Denji could be.

The first has the sound. It is heavy enough to fill the space behind the store, with the familiar rhythm farther inside it. Mau sees Reze turn towards it. One of the men by the wall has done the same thing. He puts his weight on that foot.

The second place is the one Reze hasn't looked at.

Something hits Mau from his left. His foot keeps him up this time, but he gets a hand against the wall before he can choose where to put it. Denji is close enough that Mau can see what it costs him to keep his breathing even. He doesn't have the same sound in him that he had on the other side of the yard. He had found a way to leave it where everybody would think he was.

Mau catches his arm. For a second he can see the entire movement as a thing somebody could have practised. He keeps the order of it, the foot, the attention going where the sound is, the moment Denji chose to be the other person in the room.

“Interesting,” he says. “I'm keeping that.” ᴹ

Denji pulls his arm free.

“You only saw it once.” ᴰᵉ

“I saw where I looked first.” ᴹ

For the first time since the start of the fight, Denji looks back to see what Mau can see. The thing coming around the edge of the store is tall enough that he has to look up. A piece of the building shifts against its shoulder and falls behind it.

Mau looks at the distance to Makima. She's close now. He could get to her in two steps. She could get to him in the same number if there were time to tell her which way.

*The link back is there,* Ciel says. *I need you to hold it while she's close enough.* ᶜ

He keeps his hand near the wall. The thing beside Denji has reached a place from which it can get to Makima before she can take the second step. He has already seen Infinity hold up against what Reze can do. He has no reason to assume it would hold up against what Makima warned him about.

*I need the wheel to see what it does,* he tells Ciel. ᴹ

*It'll have to do it to something.* ᶜ

He looks down at the hand he has against the wall. The body Noise gave him has enough room there for a thing to go wrong before it gets any farther. That is a possibility, and it is the best one he can see from where he is.

* * *

Mau goes in before it reaches Makima. There is a place where he can still make the cut and a place where he would have to let the attack touch him. He lets the first one go by.

Pochita catches the shaped edge of the state at his arm. The pressure lasts a fraction of the time Mau expects.

Then there is a thing he knows he was about to do, and he cannot remember why he thought he could do it. He sees a piece of stone coming towards his face and puts his hand out to move it. His hand does nothing. For a second he doesn't even remember what he was waiting for.

The stone hits his shoulder. He stumbles back with it. Makima has put her hand out to catch him and he goes as far as touching her fingers before he asks her to stay where she is. She puts her hand down. He can still feel the promise sitting where he put it around her. He checks it twice because he can remember every word of making it.

*What did it take?* he asks. ᴹ

Ciel doesn't answer at first. Then she tells him she can see places where the state has had to settle around something missing. There isn't a name in one of the places where she expects to find one.

Mau looks down at his hand. He had put it up with so much certainty. There isn't anything in the room to tell him why.

Over his head, one spoke of the wheel trembles and stops.

Pochita comes towards him again.
