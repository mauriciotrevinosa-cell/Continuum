# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 16 — When the Wheel Turns

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S12E24
**Continuity state:** B+265, continuous. The wheel finishes a response to this local erasure and the portions of Sky and Authority return. Mau keeps them private. He uses a single deliberately narrowed Shrine domain and a controlled Fuga release into the empty side of the yard. Power chooses Denji over another opening at Makima. No death, global immunity, copied erasure, second domain or recovered form of Control.

*When: Mau lifts his hand again, Makima tells him to keep his foot where it is.*

He does. The stone he would have backed onto tips out beneath the broken wall. He hears it hit the ground well below him. Makima must have seen the space under it from where she's standing. He has time to say thank you, but it comes out as half of her name.

Pochita reaches him before he can set both feet. The same edge catches again. Mau feels the shaped part at his arm go light. It has started to come loose from the rest of him when he hears the wheel.

One clear click.

The edge that was coming apart stops. He feels the gap where the first bite went through as if he has just moved a bandage out of the way and looked down at it. There is Sky. There is the reach he had been so sure of when he put his hand up. He remembers the stone that hit him. For an instant he remembers thinking he could reach it before he remembers why.

*They're back,* Ciel says. ᶜ

He closes his hand. He wants to check. There is no time he needs to spend showing everyone else what came back.

“Makima,” he says. “To the end of the wall.” ᴹ

She moves as soon as he asks. Power sees her take the step and starts to bring the blood blade round again. Then she looks past Mau. Denji has one hand down against the stone and the other pressed hard to his chest. Pochita has moved far enough from him that Power is the only one already close.

She turns away from Makima. She gets hold of Denji before the foot he was standing on has a chance to go out from under him.

* * *

The two men have taken different sides of the gap in the wall. Reze is still close enough that she could be through it before Makima crosses the yard. Mau looks along the first line he drew. The part of the store on the other side is empty. Ione and her sister were already gone when he got here. He can see the far passage all the way to the bend.

He waits for Power to get Denji clear of the broken axle. Then he brings his hands together.

“Domain Expansion.” ᴹ

It comes up around a smaller part of the yard than the one he can feel it wanting. He leaves the wall where Makima is standing outside the cut. Inside, the Shrine sets down its lines. The boards beneath the two men part. One tries to move before they fall and has to catch himself on the wall. Reze puts a hand out to reach the next place she can take off from. The line cuts through the stone before she can put her foot on it. She looks down and pulls her hand back.

Power calls Denji's name from the place where the axle was. He gets one knee up. Mau sees the knee before he can see the rest of him. There is enough room in the Shrine for another cut to go straight through the ground on that side. He leaves a strip of it standing, only wide enough for two people if one of them knows where the other is going to put a foot.

Reze is closest to it. He thinks she'll take it to get around him. She puts a hand down, looks across at Power and gets out of the way instead. Power gets Denji as far as the strip. He tries to wave her on without him. She grips the back of his shirt with both hands and makes him put his weight down again.

Mau waits while they go through. The man with the spear tries to use the same place after them. A cut touches the tip of the spear and goes on through the piece of stone beside his boot. He brings the weapon up to see what happened to it. By the time it has grown back, Power and Denji have got to the wall.

Mau can feel every place where the cut would go if he let it have a little more room. He leaves it where he put it. He has to keep himself still to do that.

“What is he doing?” one of the men asks. ᴬ

Denji looks up. He sees the shape above the middle of the yard just as Mau turns the last of the broken space away from them. The cut has left a clear lane past the end of the cart. There is no one in it. Mau opens the part of Shrine he'd kept shut until now.

“Fuga.” ᴹ

The fire takes the empty lane. It runs hot enough that the men both draw back their arms before it reaches the stone on the far side. Reze puts both feet down where she is. There is no forward place for her to go that won't take her through the flame. Mau holds it only long enough for Makima to get to him. She puts her hand on his arm without asking where else she'd be safer.

He lets the Shrine go. The sound it leaves behind is the sound of a few stones finding the ground.

On the far side of the fence, the girl watching takes her hand away from the board. She stays long enough to see what happens to the people he left in the yard.

Power is still with Denji. The others are still on their feet, where there is ground for them to stand on.

Mau takes his next breath carefully. There is enough left in the contract. He can feel it in the distance between his arm and Makima's hand, the small, ordinary pressure of her holding on.
