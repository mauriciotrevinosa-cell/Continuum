# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 17 — Another Day to Tell It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S12E25
**Continuity state:** B+265, continuous. Mau separates Makima from the group, speaks to Reze in the lull and offers a future conversation on her condition that Makima be absent. Mau and Makima leave together by the existing return anchor with her consent. The temporary Infinity ends at safety outside the ward. Mau closes the devil-state before going into the village. All opponents live.

*When: Reze comes through the gap after the light goes out, Mau brings his hands down where she can see them.*

She stops on the first good piece of ground. The cut in her arm has closed. The sleeve still hangs where it tore. She lifts the arm a little as if she has been waiting to see whether it will move again, and the movement turns into nothing when she sees Mau's hands.

Behind him Makima is still holding on. Mau steps aside just enough that both of them can see where he is going. The contract can reach this far. He can feel it pulling at the place he has to keep still.

“I don't want to fight you,” he says. ᴹ

Reze looks down at her sleeve. Then she looks at him again.

“You came a long way to do it.” ᴿᶻ

“Yes.” ᴹ

He gives her a moment to see that he means the answer. She has a hand against the cut in the wall, and he remembers her putting one on a different doorway when she found out she could go all the way through.

“I owe you an explanation,” he says. “I'd like to give it to you when we can sit down long enough to hear each other. You can come by if you ever want it.” ᴹ

Reze keeps her eyes on his. For a while he thinks she's going to ask him to give her the answer here, with Makima close enough to hear it.

“I'll think about it,” she says at last. “She can't be there.” ᴿᶻ

“Okay.” ᴹ

She looks past him at Makima. It is the first time he's seen either of them look directly at the other since he got to the yard. Makima stays very still. Reze takes her hand off the wall.

Denji has got up again. He moves slowly as far as Power, who looks as if she'll follow him wherever he chooses to go as long as she can get her hand free of the place where he's holding it. He stops when he sees Reze still standing. She turns far enough for him to see her face.

“I heard,” he tells her. ᴰᵉ

She nods. Mau waits to see whether she wants to say anything else. He can hear her breathing. She is tired enough that she hasn't moved her feet since he lowered his hands. He leaves her the way back across the yard.

* * *

At the end of the wall, he asks Makima if she's ready.

“Where are we going?” she asks. ᴹᵏ

“The post outside the village. Rimuru knows I'm bringing you there. You can get help.” ᴹ

She looks at his face. He's forgotten what there is to see until she looks at him that long. The form is still sitting where Noise put it, the bright edges of it pressing into things it shouldn't have to hold for much longer.

“All right,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau holds out an arm. She takes it with both hands. Ciel finds the return shadow and tells him when she has the connection still enough for both of them. He waits for Makima to get her feet under her. Then he puts his hand over hers.

The yard goes away.

There is a post at the edge of the village and a bench beside it. He puts her hand on the bench while he still has both feet on the ground. The Infinity lets go. Makima touches the end of the bench again, watching her fingers reach it.

Rimuru is already on his way down the path. Mau gets one hand against the post and tells Noise he wants to come out of the form. For a second the color takes longer to leave his hair than the weight of it takes to come off his hands. Makima watches the last of it disappear. She opens her mouth as if she wants to ask him something. Then she sees how he's standing.

“Sit,” she says. ᴹᵏ

He looks at her. She looks down at the bench.

“If you'd like to,” she adds. ᴹᵏ

He would. He sits beside her until Rimuru reaches them. By then Makima has taken her hand back. She keeps the place clear for somebody else to sit down.
