# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 18 — What He Brings Home

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S12E25
**Continuity state:** B+265 evening. Shoko assesses both people outside the ward. Makima remains at the exterior post while transport is arranged. Mau tells Yuka and then the household what Noise built, the duration of the Infinity contract, the temporary losses and the remaining uncertainties. The state is closed. No spontaneous clearance, restored normal vision, residence for Makima or further use of the state.

*When: Shoko asks which of them needs to sit down, Mau tells her they both already have.*

She puts her things on the bench. Rimuru takes the place at the end where he can see both of them without asking either to move. Makima has a hand over the place where she took the first blow. Shoko asks if she can see it. Makima says yes. Mau looks down at his own hands while they speak.

His hands are the color he expects. That hadn't been true when he let Noise take the shape all the way through his arms, although the first thing he noticed had been how easy it was to hold somebody still enough to ask what they wanted. He can see the small edge of the bracelet where his sleeve has turned. The catch is still shut.

“Do you know where Ione is?” Makima asks. ᴹᵏ

Rimuru says someone at the crossing saw two women walking away from the yard before anything happened. He has asked if they can find out whether both reached the place they meant to go. Makima looks at the path when he tells her. She doesn't say she can go and see for herself. Shoko finishes what she was doing and asks if there's anywhere else she wants looked at.

“His shoulder,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Shoko looks at Mau.

“It's closed,” he says. ᴹ

“I didn't ask if it was open.” ˢʰ

He moves his hand away from it. He can feel the small difference when she puts her fingers on the place where he tried to hold it shut. He tells her about the blood Power left in it and the way it opened while he was already repairing it. Shoko asks whether he can tell which part hurt after the wheel turned. He can. She takes him through one movement. When he tells her that's as far as he wants to go, she puts his arm back where he was holding it.

“Yuka's waiting at the house,” she says. “Do you need a minute before you stand?” ˢʰ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Shoko sits down with him for it.

* * *

Yuka is outside their door when he gets home. She looks at the way he's carrying his arm before she looks at his face. He wants to tell her he can put it down. He can also tell her she'd have to hold him up for a second while he did it, so he leaves it where it is and lets her take his other hand.

“She's safe at the post,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka nods. She waits for the rest.

“I got hit. I let one of them touch the form on purpose because the wheel needed to see it. Some things went away for a little while. They came back.” ᴹ

Her fingers tighten. He can feel it in the knuckle she has got between hers.

“What did it feel like?” she asks. ʸ

He looks at the wall beside their door. He wants to tell her he was too busy to feel it. He'd had plenty of time to notice what his own hand wasn't doing.

“I reached for something with my hand and then I couldn't remember why I'd done it,” he says. “I could still remember asking Makima to take Infinity. I checked that twice.” ᴹ

Yuka's breathing changes. She puts his hand against her side and holds it there, where he can feel the next breath come in.

“And you could find everything again?” she asks. ʸ

“Everything we could check so far.” ᴹ

She stays close to him. He has his back against the door and there is enough room to put his weight on it for a second. She waits with him while he does.

“I need to tell the others what Noise made,” he says. ᴹ

“Do you want to sit down first?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

She opens the door for him. There's a chair on the other side that she pulled out while she was waiting. He sits in it and she sits on the edge of the bed, close enough that he can keep her hand while he tells her what else happened.

* * *

At the table he tells them what he did with the form.

It was a temporary body. Noise worked from what it had observed of Makima, Ciel checked the boundaries with him, and he could get out of it at the post when he chose. It made some of the things he wanted to do possible for long enough to get them both away. It couldn't make somebody obey. He had asked Makima whether she'd take the protection because he wanted her answer.

“What did she say?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

“She said yes.” ᴹ

Umaru looks down at the table for a moment. Rem leaves a hand beside hers. She takes it while she asks the next question.

“And did you have to promise you'd keep doing it after?” ᵁ

“We agreed it would end when I got her somewhere safe. It did.” ᴹ

He gives them the rest. What he'd seen close up. What he expected the wheel might learn, and what he didn't know until it went through him. Ciel corrects one part about how long the missing places were there. He lets her finish. When Fern asks if Reze can be reached, he tells her what he offered and what Reze asked in return.

“I think she might come,” he says. “I'd like to be here if she does.” ᴹ

Yuka shifts nearer on the bench. Mau puts his hand beside hers and finds that she's already turning it over for him. Rimuru has come back from the post. He says Makima has a ride coming and knows how to reach him if they hear from Ione.

Mau looks across the table. There are more questions. They let him eat some supper before he answers the next one.
