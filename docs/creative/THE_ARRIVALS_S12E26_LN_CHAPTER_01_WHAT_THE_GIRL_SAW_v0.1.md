# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 19 — What the Girl Saw

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S12E26
**Continuity state:** B+266 dawn. Reze and Power make sure Denji is still with them after the encounter. The War Devil is identified to the reader as the observer, without revealing a host, arranging a pact or taking part. The observers saw Shrine and the wheel in combat. No one in the storeyard died.

*When: Denji wakes, Power is trying to tell whether he's asleep by pulling on his sleeve.*

He opens one eye.

“You could have asked,” he says. ᴰᵉ

“I did.” ᴾᵒ

She lets go of the sleeve. He can see where she twisted it twice while he was sleeping. For a second he thinks he is still up against the old stone at the edge of the yard. Then he sees Reze by the window, looking at the room on the other side of the glass, and remembers that they all came back to the same place.

“Can you eat?” Power asks. ᴾᵒ

He looks at her. She puts half a roll on the edge of the bed. He sits up slowly and takes it. She watches the first bite go down before she gets up to see what Reze has put away for later.

“Does she have anything else?” Denji asks. ᴰᵉ

“Yes,” says Reze. “I can hear you both.” ᴿᶻ

Power takes two things out anyway. She gives him the bigger one first, then puts a finger against it while she checks to make sure. He looks at her hand. Yesterday there was a moment when she could have gone farther into the yard. He'd heard her come back for him instead.

“Thanks,” he says. ᴰᵉ

She looks at the food to see which piece he means. Then she looks at him. He sees her figure it out and pick a place to sit beside him.

* * *

The girl from the fence is already on the road.

She had seen the blade stop making a wound. She had seen what went out through the yard and what came back after it. The cut went through the cart. The fire went where the cart had been. The distance between where it went and where it could have gone is the part she keeps thinking about.

At the crossing, a man tells somebody that the house has a devil of its own. She stops to hear what else he saw. He didn't see anything. A person from the road told him the sky opened and that everyone who was in the yard was gone. She looks back towards the room she'd watched them reach in the dark.

The War Devil had stayed to count the people walking out.

She starts counting something else now. The cut and the fire had come from the same place. The wheel had been there before either. Somebody could take both away and spend a great deal of time preparing for the man they'd expect to find afterward. She would like to see what he let them think he had left.

The person at the table offers her a sheet. She reads the line nearest the bottom, pushes it a little farther away and asks which road goes north. He tells her. She leaves the space for the name empty.

* * *

Reze takes Mau's letter out of her pocket. It has another crease in it now. She smooths the page on the sill and thinks of the moment he put his hands down.

It would have been easier if he'd asked her to forgive him first. She could have put the answer away again.

She folds the letter along the new crease. Denji asks if she's coming for food when Power finishes what she has.

“Yes,” she says. ᴿᶻ

She puts the letter back in her pocket. She can decide the other thing when she has time to think about it by herself.
