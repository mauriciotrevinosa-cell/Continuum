# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 74
## Chapter 20 — A Chair at the Table

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 74
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S12E26
**Continuity state:** B+270. A formal invitation asks Mau to speak before the representatives considering the road declarations and complaints about his use of force. Testarossa confirms attendance for Tempest. Mau reads it with Yuka and intends to attend on his own terms. No hearing or war preparation yet. Makima's residence remains undecided.

*When: Fern says the invitation came with a chair already assigned, Mau asks if it says what they want him to do in it.*

She hands him the page. There are two seals at the bottom. Testarossa's letter came in the same packet and has fewer words in it. She will be there for Tempest and wants him to know that somebody in the room can tell him when the chair was put there. Fern lays that letter beside the first one.

“They say they want me to answer questions,” Mau says. ᴹ

“About the crossings and what happened at the yard. And there are two other complaints without the names of the people who made them.” ᶠᵉ

He finds those at the bottom of the second page. The writing gets smaller right where it says what he is expected to be ready to explain. He runs a finger down the margin and goes back to the beginning.

“Are there people who think we should answer?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes,” Fern says. “Some of them have been asking what happens to someone who's already signed one of the forms. I would like to hear whether they have an answer.” ᶠᵉ

Rimuru brings the other sheet close enough to see. He tells Mau that he can answer only the questions he chooses and can leave when he chooses. They haven't asked him to agree to be searched, take a different way home or give someone else's name. If those things turn up when he gets there, he can tell them himself what they can do with the chair.

Mau laughs a little. Then he reads the invitation another time.

“I'd like to take it upstairs and talk to Yuka.” ᴹ

Fern gives him Testarossa's letter too.

* * *

Yuka comes in with her sleeves rolled up. She'd been with Mai again and has a short piece of thread on the inside of her elbow. She puts the things she was carrying on the table, sees what Mau is reading and asks if he wants to show her.

“Yes,” he says. ᴹ

She reads it from where she is standing at first. Then she moves his hand off the side of the chair and sits across his legs. He makes room without having to look. She can tell where he wants her to put the letter because he always has to reach a little farther with one arm to hold it steady.

“Do you want to go?” she asks. ʸ

“I'd like to hear what they have to say where I can ask them what they mean.” ᴹ

She reads the last part again. It is possible to see where the people who wrote it expected him to start having to make sense of their questions without being able to look at them.

“Will you come home if they start trying to make you stay?” she asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

He says it very quickly. She puts the letter down and turns far enough to look at him.

“Tell me one other thing,” she says. ʸ

He waits.

“Do you love me forever?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

That one is just as quick. She slips both arms around him and rests her head beside his. He'd been thinking about what he could say if somebody asked about a person he loves. He finds there is a little more room to decide how he'll say it while Yuka is sitting right here, holding on to him. He gets his hand around her side and gives her time to take all of the answer.

She stays there while he picks up Testarossa's letter to show her the line about the chair. It takes him two tries to get it open with one hand. Yuka waits until he's almost got it and then helps.
