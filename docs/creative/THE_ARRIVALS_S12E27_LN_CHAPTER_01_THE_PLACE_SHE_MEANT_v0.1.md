# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 75
## Chapter 1 — The Place She Meant

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 75
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S12E27
**Continuity state:** B+271 afternoon. Ione and her sister are safe together by their choice. Rimuru and Shizue take the second date Shizue chose. They keep their existing living arrangement and privately agree to make regular time for one another. No political promise or change in office.

*When: Shizue says she wants him to see it the way she sees it, Rimuru leaves the things he had been about to bring.*

He had put a cloth on the table and was trying to remember whether Mau had got it back from him the last time. Shizue looks down at it when he takes his hands away. Then she asks if he wants to bring it. He thinks about it, folds it the rest of the way and carries it under one arm.

“What's on the other side of the school?” he asks. ᴿ

“A turn most people go past.” ˢᶻ

They come out while the light is still on the door. The students have already gone home. There's a little stack of stones beside the wall, which Shizue says she's going to have to ask about tomorrow. She steps around it, takes the narrow side of the path where it goes behind the building and holds out a hand when there is room for him to walk beside her again.

* * *

It turns out to be a place where she likes to sit down.

There is room above the low bank for one person and, a little farther along, a place just wide enough for another. Shizue goes to the farther one. Rimuru tries the first and finds he can see down the path where they came from. He also finds there is too much room between them to touch her hand.

“Can I sit there with you?” he asks. ᴿ

She moves a little. The place is wide enough after all, provided they both want to sit close. He gets the cloth out and folds it to fit where the ground slopes down. She waits while he gets it right.

For a while they watch the light go out of the school windows, one by one. The third one stays bright at the top after the other two are dark.

“I wanted to show you that,” she says. ˢᶻ

He looks at the window.

“Why that one?” ᴿ

“I keep thinking of things I might do with the students after they go home. If I sit here until that one goes dark, I can usually remember one thing I wanted to do for myself.” ˢᶻ

Rimuru leans back a little. She looks at him as though she is trying to decide whether she should tell him what today's thing was. Then she takes his hand.

“I thought about you coming with me,” she says. ˢᶻ

He lets a little of his weight go against her shoulder. She waits there with him until he finds an answer he wants to say aloud.

“I'd like us to keep doing this,” he says. ᴿ

“Sitting in the same place?” ˢᶻ

He looks at her. She smiles where he can see it.

“That too,” he says. “I'd like to keep asking you if you have time for me. I think I'd like it if you asked me too.” ᴿ

She watches the light at the top of the third window get very thin. Then she says she can ask him. She already knows one day next week when she'll have time, and she tells him. He asks what part of the day she'd like. She tells him she'd like to find out while they still have tonight to use.

He smiles and stays right where he is.

* * *

On the way home, he tells her what he heard at the post that morning. Ione and her sister made it to the place they had picked. They sent word by the person who brings bread, and Ione is staying there for a little while. Shizue asks if there's anything Ione wanted from her old room. He tells her Ione asked for a cup she liked. Makima sent it over.

Shizue takes his hand again when the path opens.

“Did you want to tell me anything else about that?” she asks. ˢᶻ

He considers it. At home there will be things somebody will have asked him by now. One of them is probably about a chair that has been put out already. He can get back to that chair.

“I'd like to hear what you think about the stones,” he says. ᴿ

Shizue looks over at him. She tells him about the first time she caught one of the students bringing one in. He asks her what she did. By the time she gets to the part where Alice thought she could improve on the whole thing, they're at home with the night still ahead of them. Rimuru opens the door and asks her to finish telling him.
