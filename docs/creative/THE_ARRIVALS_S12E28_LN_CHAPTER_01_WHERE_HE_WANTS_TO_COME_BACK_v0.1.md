# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 75
## Chapter 3 — Where He Wants to Come Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 75
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S12E28
**Continuity state:** B+272 night. Yuka asks about the trial and what Mau heard in her promise. He tells her about the echo of Frieren's words in the previous life. They speak about returning and the terms of the hearing.

*When: When Yuka finds the question, she has to move a little to be able to look him in the eye.*

Mau is trying to read the invitation around her. The part he's trying to see is down near her elbow. She follows his eyes, moves her arm and discovers that he's been looking at the same fold for long enough to have read both sides of the paper.

“Do you want to go?” ʸ

“I want to hear what they say with me in the room.” ᴹ

“Then you can go hear it.” ʸ

He folds the invitation. She takes it and puts it on the floor on her side of the bed, where she knows he won't bother reaching for it. When she sits up again, he draws up one knee to give her room against him. She puts a hand on the inside of his wrist and stays there until he turns it over.

“I thought of something yesterday,” she says. “You told me you spent a long time in there thinking I might never want to be near you again. I keep wanting to ask how long. And I think even if you could tell me, I'd just ask it again.” ʸ

He can feel her thumb on the heel of his palm. “Sometimes I got very good at thinking about the next hour. Then a room would go quiet and I'd be back at what you said. It was hard to imagine you at home going to sleep, because then I'd have to imagine the room without me in it.” ᴹ

“I slept on your side twice.” ʸ

“Did you?” ᴹ

“The first night I was mad and I wanted to be able to see the door from where you do. The second night I found one of your shirts under the pillow and stayed there.” ʸ

“Was it a clean shirt?” ᴹ

“No. I was furious with you for that, too.” ʸ

Her laugh gets as far as one breath. He lifts his hand so she can fit hers more fully in it.

“I don't know how to live without you, Mau,” she says. “You have to come back to me. I know what I'm asking when I say that. Ask me to tell you again when you're home tomorrow. I'll tell you then, too.” ʸ

He looks up at her so quickly that she straightens.

“What happened?” ʸ

“Frieren said something like that to me once.” He waits long enough to find the rest. “Back then. I could feel her talking against my neck. She said she could go on walking for another thousand years and still feel like she'd gone back to the life she had before. I heard her for a second when you said it.” ᴹ

Yuka has one hand on his shoulder now. He can feel her deciding how far to move it and then her fingers are at the base of his neck.

“What did you want to say?” ʸ

He hadn't thought of that. Of all the things he'd tried to remember about the moment, he hadn't allowed for having anything left to say in it.

“I want to come home. I want you to be there. And sometimes I want to come through the door and find a note that you're having a very good time somewhere and won't be back until late.” ᴹ

“You're welcome to come get me if I've been out too long.” ʸ

“I'll ask if you want to be got.” ᴹ

She lowers herself against him far enough that they have to start over with the pillows. Mau wants to ask if she's heard from Mai about tomorrow and remembers he can do that in the morning. For now the only thing either of them moves to reach is the blanket.

“If they tell you you've got to stay?” she asks. ʸ

“You already asked me that. I'm leaving.” ᴹ

“I know.” She draws the blanket up over his shoulder. “I liked hearing you say it again.” ʸ
