# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 75
## Chapter 8 — The Way Out

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S12 v0.1
**Volume:** 75
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S12E30
**Continuity state:** B+273. Testarossa leaves the chamber with Mau's ant. End of Season 12. The house has yet to hear Mau's account.

*When: The ant was given directions from a height quite different from the one at which it has to follow them.*

It makes it to the floor. After that it stays still for a bit. Somebody standing between it and the door moves a shoe and the ant changes direction. It finds its way around the shoe, sees another one and stops again.

Testarossa goes round the table to meet it.

“Would you like help finding the way out?” ᵀᵉ

The ant raises its head. After a moment it gives her a nod. She goes ahead of it as far as the doorway, where the man with the papers is still holding the door. He looks down just in time to keep holding it.

“Your king secured several holidays and acquired several threats,” she tells the ant once they're out on the steps. “In that order.” ᵀᵉ

The ant makes a small sound.

Testarossa laughs. “Yes. He is a curious man.” ᵀᵉ
