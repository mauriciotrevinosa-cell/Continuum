# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 1 — What They Asked Him to Leave

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S13E01
**Continuity state:** B+273, late morning into early afternoon. Mau returns from the hearing, gives the house his account and orders Bellion to reinforce the approaches with ordinary shadows alongside Muster's existing home watch. The ant remains with Testarossa for the moment. No mobilization or known attack date.

*When: At the lodge, Mau makes sure the ant has found the steps.*

He waits on the foot side of the lodge. From there he can see the top of the steps if he stands where the driver would rather put his carriage. He moves for the driver. The next time he looks up, Testarossa is coming down at a pace that would make no sense if she were alone.

The ant comes into view on the step below her. The stairs are wide enough for both of them. It reaches down with its front pair of legs, then brings the rest of its weight forward without scraping its abdomen on the stone. Testarossa says something Mau can't hear from where he's standing. The ant stops to listen. Mau waits long enough to see it choose to go on beside her.

Then he goes home.

* * *

Yuka is out in the common yard, scraping dried mud off the bottom of her shoe with a little stick. Mai has the other shoe and is doing something to the heel. They'd gone out to see whether it held on the lower path. From the look of the mud, Yuka has already found out what the problem was.

Mau stops beside the bench. Yuka looks up at him and takes her foot off the rung.

“Did they try to keep you?” she asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

She puts the stick down. “And you left.” ʸ

“I did.” ᴹ

She holds her hand out. He takes it and sits on the part of the bench she clears with her other foot. Mai keeps the shoe in her lap for a minute. Then she puts it down next to the stick and goes to tell Rimuru that Mau is home.

From the kitchen comes the noise of somebody setting two bowls down too hard. Yuka keeps Mau's hand until he knows what the noise was. She has mud on one thumb. He moves his own thumb over it and finds the part that's still damp.

“Did it go the way you thought?” she asks. ʸ

“For a while. Luminous brought tea.” ᴹ

“Of course she did.” ʸ

“She'll still want the list.” ᴹ

Yuka waits. He can see her looking down at their hands now. He had rehearsed what he would say on the way from the lodge. It began with what they had asked and worked up to what they'd said after he'd answered. If he starts there, she'll be waiting until he gets to the part she's asking about.

“They want my head,” he says. ᴹ

She looks up. He gives her the words the man used. It takes less time than he'd spent trying to decide how to say them.

“Were you scared?” she asks. ʸ

“By then? I mostly wanted to get out of the chair.” He rubs the dried mud off her thumb. “Earlier he said they could take a few people from the house. I thought of you out with Mai this morning. I was scared then.” ᴹ

She lets him finish with her hand. Then she takes his again. The shoe Mai was working on is still beside them. Yuka gets her foot into it. The heel isn't done, but it stays on long enough for her to stand and ask if he wants to tell the others here or inside.

“Here, if they're coming anyway,” he says. ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru comes out with Fern, and Satella is behind them with the cup she'd brought over from the court. Mau moves to get up. Satella takes the place at the far end of the bench so that he can sit where he is. She sets the cup on her knee and asks whether anybody mentioned her.

“They asked me to give you to them,” Mau says. ᴹ

Satella looks down at the cup. There's enough left in it to see it move when she brings her hands together around it.

“Who asked?” she says. ˢᵃ

“The envoy from Lugunica. He said they'd had testimony that you live here. When I asked what the report said you'd done, he said I'd given you a place to stay.” ᴹ

She waits. She looks as if she has left room for a sentence he hasn't said yet.

“I told him you live with your family. I offered to bring you any question he wanted to ask. He wanted custody.” ᴹ

“Did he give you a question?” ˢᵃ

“No.” ᴹ

She brings her cup up and takes a drink. Fern tells her she'll let her know if anything else comes in writing. Satella asks to be told even if Fern thinks she already knows what the letter will say. Fern says she will.

Mau takes out the copy she gave him at breakfast. The fold has come up through the question. He flattens it against his leg before he gives it to her.

“They wrote it down,” he tells her. “Somebody said his office would look for an order about the roads. Nobody told me what happens to a name once they've got it.” ᴹ

“Who said he'd look?” Fern asks. ᶠᵉ

He tells her where the man was sitting and what he remembers of the people next to him. She writes down enough for Testarossa to know who she means if she asks. Rimuru waits while she does it. Then he asks Mau what happened after the envoy asked for Satella.

Mau tells him the rest. He tells him about the sword and the wheel and the woman who had to ask whether the man actually had authority to order anyone off the road. When he gets to the door, he remembers the attendant still standing beside it and has to correct himself. Nobody shut it. The man told him he couldn't go. The attendant kept standing there.

“Was there a vote?” Rimuru asks. ᴿ

“I didn't hear one. Testarossa was still there when I left.” ᴹ

“Then we can ask her what happened after.” ᴿ

From beyond the yard, somebody calls to ask whether the bread cart can come in the same way it did yesterday. Fern looks at Rimuru. He says yes loudly enough for the driver to hear him. The cart comes through at a walk.

Mau watches it go by. He thinks of the three people he'd pictured in the hall. One of them is out right now. He knows when she usually comes back because she brings a particular kind of bread and complains if somebody tries to carry it for her.

“I want more eyes on the roads for a while,” he says. “I'll send Bellion with more of my soldiers. Muster and his forty will keep their usual rounds.” ᴹ

“Tell them who should hear if they see something,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

Mau asks if she'd be willing. She says yes, and asks him to give them Rimuru as well, in case she's out. Rimuru nods. He takes the end of the bench nearest the kitchen and waits there while Mau calls Bellion.

Bellion comes up in the shade beside the wall. Mau shows him which roads he means. Muster's shadows are already at the places people in the house expect to find them. Mau orders Bellion to triple the number on the approaches with soldiers from the reserve. He gives him enough to rotate the extra posts without calling anyone off the gate. If someone comes who looks like trouble, he wants a report. If somebody comes home with bread, let them come home with bread.

Bellion asks whether he wants the roads stopped if he can't get an answer straight away. Mau says to use the watch's existing rules if someone is in immediate danger. For everything else he can send word and keep looking. Bellion repeats Fern's name and Rimuru's. He inclines his head to Mau and gives the order. Along the wall, the first shadows are on their feet before Mau has turned back to the road.

The bread cart stops by the kitchen. The driver takes the top crate off before anybody reaches him. Yuka puts both feet down and tries the heel Mai worked on. It holds.

“Do you want to come help me find out if that was a permanent repair?” she asks. ʸ

Mau gets up with her. “How far do you want to go?” ᴹ

“As far as the bread.” ʸ

They go that far. The driver gives Mau the lighter end of the crate without asking him whether he can take the other. Yuka gets her hand under the middle and walks it in with him.
