# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 2 — A Table by the Water

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S13E01
**Continuity state:** B+273 evening. The house keeps its ordinary supper and activities. Mau shows Yuka Elmesia's reusable invitation. They imagine returning to the island. No date fixed.

*When: Mau still has one thing in his coat to show Yuka. He remembers it while she's deciding whether to hang the coat up.*

By supper Muster's shadows have sent two reports. One is about the bread cart taking the long way back because the lower turn was muddy. The other is about three people with a handcart who asked a shadow at the gate where they could get an axle looked at. Fern tells Mau both of those things while he helps her move a dish from one end of the table to the other.

“Did the people with the axle find somebody?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes. They'd already been here to ask once, and the woman who told them where to go this time knew who they meant.” ᶠᵉ

He sets his end down. The handcart isn't on the road anymore. Somebody's working on it where the three of them can see it. He tries to let that be what the report means.

Across the table, Ori has her paper turned sideways and is looking at a drawing Chloe made in the margin. She has left the space in front of her plate clear, although the paper grows towards it a little every time Chloe wants to show her something. Frieren reaches between them to rescue the corner before Ori puts a cup on it.

“What did you end up calling it?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Ori looks up. “We haven't decided. If I name it, Chloe wants to be the one who decides what goes through it.” ᴼ

“Is it a door?” ᴹ

“It is if we put the hinge on this side,” Chloe says. ᶜʰ

She turns the paper back to show him. Mau holds the bottom so she can point without pulling it across the table. He can follow the first half. By the time he gets to the shape that might be a door or the side of a boat, Umaru is leaning over his shoulder to ask why it opens into the pantry.

“Because people might get hungry on the other side,” Ori tells her. ᴼ

Umaru has no objection to that. She brings her chair around so she can see what else might be on the other side.

Mau stays there until somebody asks him to pass the dish he carried in. He turns to get it and finds Yuka holding the end nearest him. She'd gone to tell Mai the heel had held. Now she's back, and she takes two pieces instead of the one she pointed out to him.

“One's for you,” she says. ʸ

He puts it on his plate. When she reaches across him a few minutes later to take the last corner, he sees her do it in time to stop her. He lets her have it.

* * *

After supper he helps Ori hold her drawing up against the wall while she works out how wide it would have to be if someone were actually going to walk through it. She'd drawn it big enough for Chloe. Then Milim came in and offered to be the person going through. Ori has to make a second mark.

“Do the wings count?” Milim asks. ᴹⁱ

“If you want to bring them through.” ᴼ

Milim says she'll think about it. She stands against the wall while Ori goes to look for a ruler that reaches far enough.

Mau watches her go. He almost calls her back. There is no reason to ask where she's going. He can see the shelf with the ruler from here. She comes back with Frieren holding the far end, and Mau lets them put it against the wall without getting up to help.

Yuka comes for him later, when the drawing has acquired two measurements and Chloe has ruled out the pantry door on the grounds that it already works. She leans over the back of his chair and asks how he's doing. He knows she means his leg this time because she's looking at the way he has it stretched out.

“Better than when I came in,” he says. ᴹ

“Do you want to come upstairs, or sit a little longer?” ʸ

He looks around the table. Frieren has Ori's pencil and is trying to get her to say what a line does before she fixes it. He likes seeing them still at it. He likes the idea of going upstairs too.

“Upstairs,” he says. ᴹ

He walks there with Yuka. When they get to the door, she holds it open for him and asks whether he'd like her to take the coat. He starts to give it to her. Then he feels the card through the lining and puts his hand back into the pocket.

“I got you something,” he says. ᴹ

“What?” ʸ

“I found a way to get my rematch with the bird.” ᴹ

Yuka looks at him for half a second. He gets the card into her hand before she can ask him whether he's brought a bird home.

* * *

She reads Elmesia's name first. Then she turns the card over and goes slowly enough to get all the way through what Elmesia wrote on the back. Mau has time to get his coat off and sit down beside her before she turns it back over again.

“Can we get a room closer to the pool this time?” she asks. ʸ

“I thought you'd ask whether the bird was still there.” ᴹ

“It knows where to find you.” She sets the card on her knee. “I want the room where you can see the water before you get out of bed. Do you remember the ones at the end?” ʸ

He remembers the path to them. He remembers the breakfast table with water around it, and Yuka waiting for him to take the fruit the bird had already picked. He'd been explaining what the bird was looking at right up until it took it.

“I remember where you wanted to sit,” he says. ᴹ

“Good. That's the other thing I want to ask for.” ʸ

He tells her what Elmesia said about bringing everybody if they want. He gets to the part about giving the hotel enough notice, and Yuka's already smiling at the card again. She says she might like to show some of them the island another time. This time she'd like to go with him and have a table where they can leave their plates while they go in the water.

“She said she'd like to see us when we go back,” Mau says. “I thought we could write and find out when she's going.” ᴹ

“I'll write it.” Yuka looks down at the space on the back where the card runs out. “Maybe tomorrow. I might need a whole page.” ʸ

He starts to tell her which corner of the card she could use if she wants to send it back. She folds her hand over it.

“We're keeping this,” she says. ʸ

He had thought of it as something he might put away when they got there. He watches her read the line about how long they can stay again and thinks she might want to have it in her hand all the way to the island.

He takes her hand. She has two fingers on the card so it won't slide off her knee. She keeps them there while she tells him which table she'd ask for and how late she'd like to eat on the first night. When he asks what she'd do if they can't get the room at the end, she turns towards him with a second choice already worked out.

He hears her out. Then he asks whether she's considered that the bird might have learned something since the last time.

“About you?” she says. ʸ

“About me.” ᴹ

“I'm sure it has.” ʸ

He laughs. Yuka puts the card on the table on her side, where she'll see it when she wakes up. Then she comes back to him and puts both feet across his lap, like she's got a third choice of room to tell him about. He shifts a little so she can stay as long as she likes.
