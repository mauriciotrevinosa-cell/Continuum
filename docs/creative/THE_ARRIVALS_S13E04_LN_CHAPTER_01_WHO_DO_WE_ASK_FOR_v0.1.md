# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 7 — Who Do We Ask For

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S13E04
**Continuity state:** B+275 midday. Rimuru invites Makima to planning with Bellion. Ori proposes that aid teams ask injured people who they want contacted. Makima listens and agrees to bring Ori’s wording to Bellion, then tells Mau about it. No deployment begins.

*When: Makima comes to find Mau after hearing Ori out.*

There are four people at the table when Makima comes down from her room. Rimuru has a map spread flat with a saucer on the corner nearest him. Bellion stands where he can see the roads without leaning over Fern. Ori is sharpening the pencil she left in Mau's pocket yesterday. She's made a little pile beside the knife.

Rimuru asks Makima to sit. He tells her what they have managed to confirm since last night and what he'd like her help with. The date is still the same. They would like to be able to get people off the roads before anybody is hurt. So far they've only agreed on where to tell Shoko a person is. They still need to know who will meet her.

“Bellion can tell you which soldiers can stay outside the whole time,” Rimuru says. “I'd like you to help us decide what to ask them to do.” ᴿ

Makima pulls a chair out and asks Bellion if he's looked at the place where the road goes low near the stream. He tells her what his soldiers have seen there. Fern puts a little piece of paper where he points. By the time they come back to the question of Shoko, the piece of paper has moved twice and they're going to have to ask Qifrey about the bank.

Ori looks down at the pencil. She has written something on the small page beside it. She waits for Fern to get the papers straight.

“Can I ask you something?” she says to Makima. ᴼ

Makima takes her finger off the map. “Yes.” ᴹᵏ

“If somebody gets hurt, do we have to tell their captain that we have them?” ᴼ

Makima can think of two reasons to tell the captain and several reasons to find out who the captain is before telling him anything. She'd been about to give Ori the first of them. Ori has her mouth open as though there's a little more to the question, so Makima waits.

“Sometimes when somebody gets hurt here, Shoko asks who they want,” Ori says. “Maybe they'd want somebody else. I would want Mum. I wouldn't want you to ask whoever told me to go out there if I could come home.” ᴼ

Makima looks at the pencil mark on the page. Ori has drawn a line from *ask first* to the space where she was trying to write the rest. She puts the pencil down before Makima can see whether she has finished.

“Ask them who they want us to tell,” Ori says. ᴼ

Makima had thought she'd want to know how the people they'd found were going to be returned. She still does. She can also see Ori waiting to know whether she was heard.

“I'll talk to Bellion about that,” she says. “Can I tell him the way you said it?” ᴹᵏ

Ori looks across the map. Bellion can hear them perfectly well. He sets the bit of paper he was holding down and asks Ori if she'd mind leaving the line there for him to see. She pushes the page over. Makima reads it once. Then she asks Bellion when he has time to work out who the soldiers should call if the person they find can't speak yet.

“In a little while,” he says. ᴮᵉˡ

Ori picks up the knife and brushes the pencil shavings into her hand. She says she's going to find Frieren. Before she goes she tells Makima the step by the common door is loose and that it's easier to take the long way around it until Mai fixes it. Makima thanks her. Ori goes out holding the shavings in a little pile against her shirt.

* * *

Mau is outside with Jin-Woo. They have a shallow crate of cups between them and are checking which ones wobble before they put them out for supper. Mau sets one down on the bench. It rocks. He turns it the other way around, as if there might be a part of the bottom that would be better at the job.

“Could I borrow him for a minute?” Makima asks. ᴹᵏ

Jin-Woo takes the cup. “I think he was about to fix this one by looking at it.” ᴶʷ

Mau gets up. He asks Makima whether she'd like to walk or sit. She goes with him as far as the wall, where they can still see the cups. She's already telling him what Ori asked by the time he sits down.

“That's a good question,” he says when she gets to the captain. ᴹ

“She had another part to it.” ᴹᵏ

He leaves his hand on the wall and listens to that part too. When she finishes, he asks whether Ori wrote it down. Makima tells him she did. She'd like to get Bellion to put the actual question into the instructions rather than trying to come up with a shorter one.

“Is that why you wanted to tell me?” Mau asks. ᴹ

She starts to say yes. She can hear that she was about to end the conversation there.

“I had an answer ready,” she says instead. “As soon as she said captain. And then I wanted to know what she'd say if I let her finish.” ᴹᵏ

Mau looks at her. She thinks she has got to the end this time. Then she catches herself smiling. She puts a hand on the wall beside his. The stone is warmer than she thought it would be.

“Did she surprise you?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes,” she says. She looks down at her hand. “I think I did, too.” ᴹᵏ

He smiles at her. She stays there long enough for him to ask what the words were again. She tells him exactly. Across the yard, Jin-Woo puts the wobbly cup on one side of the bench and gets up to bring the rest inside.

“Do you want a hand with the good ones?” Makima asks. ᴹᵏ

Mau looks over at him. “We can ask.” ᴹ

He gets up. She takes the long way around the loose step when she follows him in.
