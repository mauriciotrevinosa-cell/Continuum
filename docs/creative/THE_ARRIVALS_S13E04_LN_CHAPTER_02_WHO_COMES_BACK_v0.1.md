# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 8 — Who Comes Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S13E04
**Continuity state:** B+275 late afternoon. Rimuru, Bellion and the household consider Makima’s proposal to divide the defence. They replace her proposed expendable living screen with shadows and ask each group what it needs to bring everyone back.

*When: Shoko gets to the table in time to hear Makima name the people she expects not to come back.*

The cups from the yard have got as far as the table. One of them rocks when Shoko sets her bag down. Jin-Woo had taken it out of the crate himself, and Mau remembers putting it back because he thought it might do better on a different piece of wood.

It doesn't. He takes it out of Shoko's way.

Makima has used the middle of the map for the place she expects the first people to come through. There are two lengths of thread from Mai's basket beside it. One runs from the road towards the house. The other stops at the stream.

“A spear and a shield,” Makima says. “Someone who can get past the first people they send. Someone who can hold here while he does it.” ᴹᵏ

She moves the end of the first thread with her nail. Mau can see where she means him to go. She hasn't said his name yet. There is a second place near the stream where somebody would have to stay if the other side came around. Bellion has a thumb on that place so the map won't roll up over it.

“The people here would need to hold until they have been seen,” Makima says. “Once the other side has committed to reaching them, you can afford to lose the position.” ᴹᵏ

Shoko puts her bag down on the floor. “And the people?” ˢʰ

Makima names the people she has in mind. They are adults from the watch who know the road and can answer questions convincingly. Two have been out since morning. One is expected back before supper. She says what she thinks they can get out of the position before they lose it.

Maki reaches down and picks up the thread. “You had room to bring them back until the last thing you said.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Makima looks at the place her nail was. “I don't think we would have room then.” ᴹᵏ

“Then we need a different plan,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

Nobody moves to take the map off the table. Makima watches the thread dangle from Maki's fingers, then reaches out for the loose end so they can both see how much of it is still lying across the road.

“You'll have more people to get out farther down if you give up this part too early,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau can see it too. It bothers him that he can. If they wait until the people at the stream are in trouble, he's going to want to go after them. The whole shape of the plan depends on him being somewhere else when that happens.

“What if the ones at the stream can come back after they're broken?” he asks. ᴹ

Bellion lifts his thumb. The paper curls up to the place where the road begins.

“Mine,” Mau says. “From the legion. They can choose to hold it, and if their forms break they'll come back to me. We can have enough to do that without pulling anybody off the Watch or the roads Tempest asked us to cover.” ᴹ

Makima studies the map for another few seconds. He expects her to ask how many he'll give her. Instead she looks at Bellion.

“Would they understand what I want the position to look like?” ᴹᵏ

“You can tell me what you want them to do,” Bellion says. “I will tell you what they can do, and they will hear the order from me.” ᴮᵉˡ

She takes her finger off the map. “Then I would like to start with what they do if somebody is still on the road.” ᴹᵏ

Rimuru gives her the end of the thread Maki has let go of. Before Makima sets it down, she waits for Fern to show her which side of the stream the people on foot use. The map was drawn for carts. There's a footbridge that isn't marked at all. Fern gets the pencil out and puts it in.

* * *

Mau waits until the discussion has got as far as the house before he says Yuka's name.

“I don't want you at the front,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka looks up from the piece of paper she's been using to keep track of who volunteered for what. “Were you going to ask me?” ʸ

He had intended to. He can see what came out instead. Across the table, Jin-Woo gives the rocking cup a small push so that it stops against the saucer.

“I was going to tell you why,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I'll hear why. Then you can ask.” ʸ

He tells her about being in the yard with Makima and realising something he was sure would work had gone out of his hand. He can imagine seeing Yuka waiting for help and knowing he'd have to get through somebody else first. It doesn't become easier to say just because he's already said how frightened he was at the hearing. He hears himself telling her that part twice.

“I would like you to stay here,” he says at last. ᴹ

Yuka takes a while to answer him. She looks down at the names on her page. Shizue is supposed to be bringing the students down to supper. There's a place next to Frieren's name where somebody put a second mark because Ori will be with her. Yuka draws the page closer.

“What do we have left if the person out front gets somebody to go around him?” she asks. ʸ

“Gojo and Yuta to begin with,” Rimuru says. “And Milim, if we need her. I want to talk to each of them about what that means.” ᴿ

Yuka puts a finger beside the place on the map where the footbridge would lead. Bragas could watch that approach with her. He'd have room to move without coming through the yard. She's spent enough time practising how to call him that she can tell them where she'd want everybody else when she does. She shows Frieren the place. Frieren leans over to see it properly and asks what Yuka wants to be able to see from there.

Yuka gives her two things. They go back and forth until the second is possible.

“I'll stay,” she tells Mau. “I want to be here if somebody gets through. That gives me something to do with Bragas I can actually practise.” ʸ

He starts to tell her how glad he is. She takes his wrist before he can get the words all the way out.

“Ask me next time,” she says. ʸ

“I will.” ᴹ

She lets go of his wrist and takes his hand instead. Nobody at the table is waiting for them to be finished. Bellion is finding out from Shoko how far she'll go if somebody calls. When he asks whether it makes a difference who gave the order to bring the wounded man in, Shoko says she'd like the call to come from the person who's looking at him.

Yuka looks down at the list again. “Can you put me next to the bridge?” she asks Rimuru. ʸ

Rimuru gives her the pencil. She writes it herself.
