# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 10 — The Bank Is Still There

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S13E05
**Continuity state:** B+276 afternoon. Qifrey and Ramiris visit the bank with Fern, Frieren and Shuna. They agree on a reversible change to the ground and test the outside records. No permanent dungeon or ward against every memory effect.

*When: Ramiris goes to look at the stream because Fern drew a bridge that Ramiris cannot find on the old map.*

There is a bridge. It is a little farther down than Fern put it because the person who told her where to find it was standing in the stream when he did. His boots are beside the bridge now. He comes back for them while Ramiris is still trying to look underneath the planks.

“Do you need it?” she asks him. ᴿᵃᵐ

“I use it every day,” he says. “Are you taking it?” ᴴ

“No,” Fern says. She has found the place to put it on the map. “Could we ask you what you'd do if you came down here and found somebody on it?” ᶠᵉ

He thinks about that. If it was somebody he knew he'd wait. If it was somebody with a cart he might go around. If it was somebody who'd told him to get off the road already, he'd be glad of a way around that went somewhere he could get home from.

He puts on his boots. Qifrey has been standing a little farther back to let him do it. When he gets up, Qifrey asks if he'd mind showing them which way he means. He doesn't. He takes them back up the bank and shows them the place the trees open. There is a track there worn by feet, and a little way along it there's a bench that somebody has made from a board left over from the bridge.

“There's a woman who has to sit here on the way up,” he says. “You can leave that where it is, too.” ᴴ

Fern marks it. He looks at the map after she's done. It's upside down from where he stands and he still knows which mark is his bridge.

* * *

Once he's gone, Ramiris goes up to the bank and stands in the track.

“I could give this hill more rooms,” she says. “Which part do you want?” ᴿᵃᵐ

Fern looks down at the bridge. Two people would have to choose who went first to pass each other. A group coming down it would find that out when it was already on the planks. She'd rather that discovery belonged to a group that had somewhere else to go.

“Could you give us room to get past people without making the bridge bigger?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

Ramiris bends to touch the grass. She can make a little more space in a part of the bank that sits within her reach. It won't run the whole way up the hill. She'll need a place to open it and one where people who are inside can find their way back out. She asks Qifrey whether he can mark the second one so she can see it from where she'll be.

Qifrey crouches down and sets one piece of card in the grass. He puts another well back from it, where the ordinary track will still be usable. He asks Ramiris to see what happens if they make a room between them, small enough that it fits under the part of the bank they've already walked.

Frieren waits with Fern above the bench. The first time Ramiris tries it, the grass at the mouth moves. Fern can see the movement quite clearly. From farther down, someone would see the path go a little to one side where it should have gone straight. She points it out to Ramiris. Ramiris has her hands full and asks if somebody can walk through once before she changes it.

Qifrey picks up the second card and goes in. They can see him take three steps. Then they can hear him take several more. When he comes out he is a little farther along the bank than Fern expected. He sets the card down at his feet and asks Ramiris how far from it she thought he was going to be.

She comes down to see for herself. The difference is enough that a man carrying someone could go past the opening altogether. They try it again with Qifrey's mark where Fern can see it from the bench. This time he comes out next to it. Fern walks the track after him. She can see both cards, the bridge and the bench without having to turn around.

“Where does the room go if you stop?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

“I can leave the way back out until everybody's through,” Ramiris says. “You'll have to tell me when everybody's through.” ᴿᵃᵐ

Fern draws a line for that too. She wants the person telling Ramiris to be able to see both exits. She'd like somebody else to count with them. Ramiris watches her put both places down and asks if she can have a copy for herself.

“You usually ask me afterwards,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

“I usually make it bigger afterwards.” ᴿᵃᵐ

Fern gives her a second piece of paper before she can do that.

* * *

Shuna brings the two sheets from the window after lunch. There is a mark on each one where Frieren touched a ward set up in the yard. The mark nearest the touch is darker. They can tell that much in the light at the stream. Shuna makes a third mark when Qifrey tries from the far side of the bank.

“That one showed up first here,” she says. “I watched the other one catch it a little later.” ˢʰᵘ

Fern moves so she can see. She'd expected them to look different. They do, but only because Shuna saw which happened first and had something to write it on. If she'd been talking to somebody, she could have missed it.

“Would you see it from the house?” Fern asks. ᶠᵉ

“If I had somebody here watching this one.” ˢʰᵘ

Frieren puts the papers down side by side. They know that they can mark a touch now. The touch they chose to try was simple enough to make in the yard without anyone getting hurt. She wants to see how the marks behave when somebody comes from farther away and she can't see which side they took.

Fern asks if she'd like to borrow the map. Frieren takes it and goes to the other end of the bridge while Shuna picks the person who'll tell her where the first mark showed up.

By the time they go back, they know they can recognise the direction of the test twice in a row. The third time, Shuna has to ask for it again because somebody walked across the bridge in the middle of it and she was watching his feet. Fern notes that one too. She has room left on the page for a watch that can see the bridge, a way of calling Shuna and the name of the person who knows which woman uses the bench.

Before they leave, Ramiris closes the little room. The grass falls back against the bank. Fern watches a person come down the track, put one hand on the bench to get around it and go on to the bridge. There's nothing he has to ask them before he can cross.
