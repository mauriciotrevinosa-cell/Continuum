# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 11 — Everyone Gets a Turn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S13E06
**Continuity state:** B+277 morning. The household runs a controlled exercise against Mau. The exercise tests decisions under changed conditions and familiar equipment. Shoko ends the session when his movement deteriorates. No new technique or full combat clearance.

*When: Mau is supposed to reach the far door. Maki waits until he has taken three steps to tell him why.*

Shoko gives Mau two things before she lets him go to the yard. One is a cup she expects him to finish. The other is a question about what his leg felt like on the way up from breakfast. He answers the second one while he's working on the first.

“It felt better yesterday,” he says. ᴹ

“Yesterday you took the flat road.” ˢʰ

He thinks about it. She can see him counting the steps he'd forgotten because he'd been talking to Yuka while he took them. He tells her where it started. She asks him to show her how he's putting weight on it now. He does, and has to do it a second time more slowly before he's willing to tell her the truth about the first one.

“You can do the first exercise,” she says. “I want to be able to see you the whole time. If I tell you to stop, you get to ask why after you've done it.” ˢʰ

He finishes the water. “Can I ask why now?” ᴹ

“Because you were about to argue with me about a stair you were standing on.” ˢʰ

He hands her the cup. She takes it to the end of the yard, where Mai has already found a chair that will let her see all the way to both doors.

* * *

At first there's only Maki in front of him. Yuta stands beside the far door. Gojo has gone to a place near the wall where Mau can see exactly as much of him as he wants Mau to see. Jin-Woo is on the other side of the yard. Milim has been told she gets to go last, which is why she has already walked twice around the outside to see whether there will be any yard left by the time it's her turn.

Maki tells Mau to go through the far door. He takes three steps.

“There are people behind you who need to get through it too,” she says. ᴹᵃᵏ

He stops. She has put herself where she'll reach the door before him if he turns around. Behind him, Mai pulls a string and a small panel tips forward. There are two paper figures on it. One has lost its stand.

“Can they walk?” he asks. ᴹ

“You tell me,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

He could get to the door first. He'd see what Yuta was doing from there. He keeps looking at the paper figure that's fallen over. Then he goes back for it. Maki reaches the door while he's picking it up.

When he turns round, Yuta has put a cloth over the handle. Mau knows it means he can't use it. He goes to the place he saw Gojo, and Gojo moves the other way, leaving him exactly enough space to get himself out. There isn't room for what he's carrying unless he lowers his arm. He almost does. He hears Yuka, standing beside Shoko, ask if he remembers the other figure.

He doesn't.

“Go back and do that one again,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

* * *

The second time, he asks Mai which figure she can reach. Mai tells him she isn't there for the exercise unless he gives her something she can do. He asks her to hold up the panel so the one still standing won't fall. She does. He gets the first one under his arm, calls to Jin-Woo to see if the second door is free and waits the breath it takes to hear him say no.

Yuta gets closer. Mau keeps him on the other side of a patch of yard he's allowed to use. He can move the surface between them with Sky. He does it only far enough to take Yuta's hand off his sleeve. Yuta watches what that leaves open, and Maki uses it before Mau finishes thinking about him.

Her practice blade touches his shoulder.

“Who's with Mai?” she asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

He looks. Jin-Woo has got there first. He is holding the panel out of the way so Mai can get around it without having to put it down.

Mau lowers his arm. “You could've told me you were going.” ᴹ

“I could have,” Jin-Woo says. “Would you have let me?” ᴶʷ

Mau looks at where Maki's blade touched. He would have tried to decide which of them was closer. By the time he had done that, Mai would still have been holding the panel.

“Yes,” he says. “If you told me before I started thinking.” ᴹ

Jin-Woo laughs. He puts the second figure in Mau's hand and waits for Maki to tell him where to start next.

* * *

On the third try, Milim gets her turn. She has permission to get to a chalk line in front of Mau and no farther. He can feel her coming when she's still well on the other side of it. He tries to work out which way she'll go when she sees what he's holding. She goes straight at the line and stops on it with enough force to lift the corner of Mai's panel.

He looks at Milim. By the time he looks back at the door, Gojo has gone through it.

“Did you see me go?” Gojo asks from the other side. ᴳ

“I saw her stop.” ᴹ

“Everybody saw her stop,” Gojo says. “That was the idea.” ᴳ

Milim looks pleased enough to offer to do it again for anyone who missed it. Mau is about to ask her to. He gets his foot under himself to start from the other side, and Shoko stands up at the end of the yard.

“Stop,” she says. ˢʰ

He stops. It takes him a second to understand why she said it. His leg is trembling while the rest of him still wants to see what would happen if he let Milim get to the line without looking at her. He puts the figures down on Mai's panel before he asks.

“Was it that obvious?” ᴹ

“Your wife saw it before I did.” ˢʰ

He looks over. Yuka hasn't moved from her place beside the chair. She offers him the cup when he gets there. He takes it and sits down long enough for Shoko to ask him how many times his foot came down harder than he meant it to. He thinks it was twice. Yuka tells him she saw the second one. They get to three between them.

Maki puts her practice blade away. She brings one of the paper figures over to Mau and stands it on the arm of his chair. It doesn't fall this time.

“I'd like you to come back tomorrow and tell us how to get that one out,” she says. ᴹᵃᵏ

He looks at the figure. He'd been looking forward to the part where he found out how to beat everyone in the yard. He can see how long Mai had to stand there waiting for him to find an answer.

“Tomorrow,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka puts one foot against the chair to stop it rocking when he leans back. She leaves it there until he's finished the cup.
