# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 12 — The Turn After Yours

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S13E06
**Continuity state:** B+278 morning. The house rehearses with existing shadows, practices recovering a casualty, and agrees on rotating frontline and relief. Yuka volunteers the exception allowing Mau to intervene in an immediate emergency. The inner reserve and unrehearsed combinations stay private.

*When: Mau wants to know how many times they can let the other side see the same person in the same place.*

Bellion brings ordinary soldiers through the yard one group at a time. They come at the pace people use when they are carrying something heavy, because that's what Shoko wants everybody to be ready for. The last two are carrying a rolled blanket with the weight of a person in it. One of them tries to put it down beside Mai's panel. Mai points to the line where she wants them to stop instead. They take it there.

Mau watches from the other side of the line. Yesterday he'd been given two paper people and lost track of one. Today the soldier at the head of the group has to ask him which way they can carry the blanket without having to turn it on its side.

He looks at the gate. The way through it is blocked by three of Bellion's other soldiers.

“Wait,” he says. ᴹ

They wait. He can see that they could go the other way if they were willing to put the blanket down first. Shoko is watching him think of it. He asks her if they can. She asks what she'd have to do if the person inside it couldn't move on his own. He looks back at the soldiers.

“Can somebody from the other end come and meet them?” he asks. ᴹ

“Ask,” Bellion says. ᴮᵉˡ

Mau asks. Yuta appears at the end he's looking at. He is already holding something else when he gets there and has to give it to Maki before he has his hands free. Maki asks what he wants her to do with it. He tells her. By the time he's there to get one end of the blanket, somebody else has taken the place he left. Mau sees that it is Jin-Woo only after Jin-Woo has been standing there long enough to wave.

They run it a second time. This time Yuta calls to ask if he can leave what he's doing before he comes. Bellion makes him wait for the answer. It takes only a little longer. The extra moment is long enough for the person carrying the blanket to need to shift his grip. Mau goes close enough to ask whether he needs a hand. The soldier makes room for it.

* * *

Gojo draws three places in the dust with the heel of his shoe. He stands in the first, sends Yuta to the second and tells Jin-Woo he's got the third. They all wait there until the people trying to move the blanket have seen where to go.

“What happens if they see us do that on the first day?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Gojo looks down at his shoes. “They'll know where to find me.” ᴳ

“What if they see us again on the second?” ᴹ

“They'll know where to send the part they want me to look at,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

Mau had been thinking about somebody coming for Gojo. Yuta puts it a different way, and Mau can see how the three of them could all be doing their own jobs while the person they needed to see was still going past them.

“We could change who they see,” he says. “Before they get used to it. I can come in with you for a while. Then you go home and let Jin-Woo have the place, and I get to see what's happening from somewhere else.” ᴹ

Makima has been watching from near the map. She asks if he means to do it when somebody is tired. He tells her he'd like to do it before that. She looks at Bellion. He waits for her to finish asking what she wants to ask.

“Then somebody has to know when the person who's coming off still wants to stay,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“I can give them a time,” Rimuru says. “I'd rather give them somebody to say it to as well.” ᴿ

They take a long time over which person that is. Shoko wants whoever comes off to go past her first. Gojo asks what happens if the person taking his place would like him to stay. She says he can explain why while she's seeing whether his hands are steady. He puts both hands on the table for her to see. She makes him pick up the cup next to them and waits until he has a drink before she goes on.

Mau can hold the front by himself for a while. Jin-Woo says he can too. Yuta tells them what he can do if one of the others has to come back early. Milim would like everybody to remember she can stand where one of them was standing and still have something left when they go to ask her to stand somewhere else. Rimuru says he is counting on it. She looks at him to see if he means it. When she sees he does, she moves the mark beside her name a little farther from the others so nobody has to guess where she's meant to wait.

They talk about some things in the yard. They take the rest inside. The soldiers keep to what Bellion has asked them to practise. There is enough in that for them to do until the next person comes to relieve them.

* * *

Yuka finds Mau by the far door, with the paper figure Maki gave him still on the arm of the chair. He has moved it twice. He brings his hand away from it when he hears her coming.

“You're still thinking about the moment I said I'd stay,” she says. ʸ

“A little.” ᴹ

She stands beside him. From here she can see why Maki picked that end of the yard. You can see somebody come through either door before you have to decide where to put the person you're carrying.

“What are you going to do if you're out front and you see something here?” she asks. ʸ

“Tell Rimuru. He can tell Gojo or whoever has it by then.” ᴹ

“And if there isn't time for that?” ʸ

He looks at her. She'd asked it as though it was only the first half of the question. He looks back at the door.

“If there's time, that's what I'll do.” ᴹ

She sits on the arm of the chair. He moves the figure to give her room.

“If you can see that someone's in danger, and you know you can get there in time, I want you to go,” she says. “Even if it isn't your turn. Tell us as soon as you can so somebody knows what you left behind.” ʸ

He hadn't thought she would be the one to say it. He looks at the hand she's put on his knee. She is looking out through the door while she says it.

“I still want you to come back after,” she says. ʸ

“I want that too.” ᴹ

She takes the paper figure off his lap. One of its feet is folded from yesterday. She straightens it with her thumbnail and sets it back where he had it at the beginning. Then she asks if he'd like to come and see where she wants to stand with Mahoraga when there are people on the bridge. He gets up slowly enough to put his foot down where he intends. She stays beside the chair until he has it under him, then goes out at his pace.
