# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 13 — The House Has Supper

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S13E07
**Continuity state:** B+279 through B+280 afternoon. The household allocates rear care, meals, rest, the children's route, and the first front. No attack comes a day early. Satella chooses to stay. The next dusk report confirms the advance.

*When: Shizue has two students at the study table and three who would like to know where they should go if somebody calls them away from it.*

Kenya would like to know who will call. Alice would like to know whether she can take what she's working on. Shizue lets both questions stay in front of her while she moves a book out of the place where Gale wants to set down a cup. Chloe already knows which door Ori will use. She asks Shizue whether she'd like them to take the same one.

“If we're together, yes,” Shizue says. “If I'm with somebody who needs help, I want you to go when Rem or Taihei asks you. We can try it now while we still know where everybody is.” ˢᶻ

They put the books down where they want to find them again and walk out. They go past the rooms where they sleep and down towards the Fort. The first time, Kenya gets all the way through the door before he realises Alice stopped to pick up her cup. He goes back. The next time he asks her whether she's ready before he goes. She tells him she could do it herself. He waits until she has.

At the common room, Rem has cleared the end of a table so there will be space for anybody who comes with work they can do sitting down. She shows them where they can put their things and tells them they can have the space now if they want it. Chloe looks back down the passage. Ori hasn't come out yet. Shizue waits with her while the others go see what's on the table.

Ori comes in a minute later with Frieren and a piece of paper under her arm. She tells Chloe she's found out the place where the wings are meant to go. Chloe asks to see. They go to the table and unroll the drawing themselves.

* * *

That evening, Fern has a man waiting at the outer post who can tell her that some carts came to the tollhouse, left without going through and have asked to come back tomorrow. She asks what was in them. He says he saw blankets. She has a second question ready and then sees he's come all this way with nothing to eat. She asks if he can stay long enough for supper.

At the table, Bellion reports the carts have stopped outside the roads they are watching for the house. Rimuru asks him to keep the existing posts until there is a report of something different. Makima goes over the places they'd like covered if it comes. She has written Ori's question on the small sheet for the people doing the carrying. It is in the words Ori gave her. Shoko has put what to do when the person can't answer on the line underneath.

Satella has brought her own bowl over. She waits for the man from the tollhouse to be shown where he can sit. When there is room to speak, she asks Rimuru if he expects her to be somewhere else tomorrow.

“Do you want to be?” he asks. ᴿ

“No. I wanted to be able to say it before anybody had to ask me to leave.” ˢᵃ

He listens. She tells him she means to be in the court for the part of the day she'd normally be there. If he asks her to help somebody who comes in from the roads, she will hear him. She'll also go to the room they picked if somebody needs the way kept clear. She's thought about it long enough to tell him where they should look for her at the end of the day.

Mau puts her bowl nearer her. She looks at it for a while before she picks up the spoon.

“Thank you,” she says. ˢᵃ

She means more than the bowl. He knows that much. He takes his own spoon and gives her time to start eating before he asks if she'd like someone to come sit with her tomorrow.

“After lunch,” she says. “If you have the time.” ˢᵃ

He tells her he'll come by.

* * *

After lunch the next day, Mau finds her where she said she'd be. Satella has enough hot water for two cups. There's a stack of things beside the pot somebody would have to move if she wanted to put anything else on the table. She tells Mau she could go on putting those things in order all afternoon. He asks her if she'd like him to help. She says she'd like him to take the cup first.

They sit there for most of the time the water stays hot. Mau has already walked the bridge with Yuka and listened to her tell him which side she wants Mahoraga on if he has to come out. He knows where Ori will be. He knows where Shoko will be and what he'll say to her if he needs her. He checks the time once while Satella is talking to him, and then he puts both hands around the cup so he won't do it again.

He goes back when he said he would. Fern has people at the map. Someone from the watch has asked for the spare chair because he'd rather have it near the door than find it after he's had to come back in. Rimuru gets it for him. Yuta comes to tell him he'll be by Gojo until somebody wants them to do something different. Jin-Woo has already taken the walk he wanted to take before dark and is ready to go back out. Milim is eating the end of a piece of bread in the yard because she wants to be able to see the first person who comes for her.

Mau takes a bowl into the kitchen. He sets it down in the place where he found it. When he comes back, Fern has one hand on the map and the other on the message the watch has just sent in.

“How many?” Rimuru asks. ᴿ

“Enough that the first carts have stopped using the road,” Fern says. “They're letting people through on foot.” ᶠᵉ

Mau can see the light at the far window. There is still enough of it for the people coming down to see the bridge. He goes to find Yuka at the door they picked.

“It's time,” he says. ᴹ

She has her hair tied back and a hand on the latch. She takes the hand off it long enough to put it on his cheek.

“I know where I'll be,” she says. “You know where to find me.” ʸ

He kisses her. Then she opens the door for both of them.
