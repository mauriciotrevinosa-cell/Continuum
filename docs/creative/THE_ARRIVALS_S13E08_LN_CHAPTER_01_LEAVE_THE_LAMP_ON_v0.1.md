# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 15 — Leave the Lamp On

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S13E08
**Continuity state:** B+280 evening into B+281 morning. The rescue agreement holds. The house takes its first relief and a shared meal. An injured soldier offers a lead on coerced service after learning his chosen contact may come.

*When: The first person to take Mau's place asks him what he wants for supper.*

Yuta comes down the road with his sword still in its sheath. Mau can see his hands. One is lifted to tell the man in the white helmet that he's seen him. The other is free for the end of the blanket if somebody needs him to take it.

“You can go back,” Yuta says. “I asked Rem what she was making. She said there'd be enough if I got you there before she had to put the lid on.” ʸᵗ

Mau looks up the road. The people who came to ask for Satella have drawn back to the first cart. He can see a man trying to talk to the one whose wrist he took. It looks to him as if they haven't decided whether to send anybody else.

“Did you hear what they asked for?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes. Rimuru told me on the way. I can call you if they have a second question.” ʸᵗ

He has the sense to say it without smiling. Mau looks at him until he does. Then he gives him the places the rescue workers have used so far and goes far enough back to see who is there to take them over from him. Two shadows step up. One has a bit of white cloth from the man with the bag. It holds it where the next person coming down can see it.

Mau gets nearly to the house before he starts thinking of a reason to go back. He can check whether the injured man got to the place Shoko wanted. He can see it from the door when he gets there. The man in the white helmet is sitting on a step with the bag between his feet. Shoko has somebody with her inside. Mau goes up to ask if that is the man they brought.

“Yes,” says the rescuer. “He told us he'd be all right if somebody would stop moving his leg.” ᴴ

Mau remembers the way the man tried to lift it himself. He asks whether Shoko wants anything. The rescuer tells him she wanted a cup of water when he left. Mau gets it and takes it to the threshold. Shoko makes him put it on the table, puts a hand against his shoulder to see what he does and tells him to take his own cup out to supper.

* * *

The door to the common room is open. There are bowls at both ends of the table and a place beside Yuka, who is just putting hers down. He sits there. She lets him look at her for long enough to see where she'd been standing. The knees of her trousers are damp from the bridge. There is nothing on her hands but a bit of flour.

“Did you need Bragas?” he asks. ᴹ

“No. He would've liked me to ask him a second time.” ʸ

“Did you?” ᴹ

“I told him I'd ask when I needed him. He stayed.” ʸ

Mau gets his spoon into the bowl. It has got quiet enough that he can hear the edge tap when he pulls it towards himself. Yuka puts her hand on his knee under the table. She leaves it there while he tells her what the injured man asked.

“He thought we'd keep him,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Did he still think that when they brought him in?” ʸ

“I don't know.” ᴹ

She waits a little. “Then he gets to see what we do next.” ʸ

At the other end, Milim is asking Jin-Woo why nobody came for her. He tells her they could still see her from where they were. She considers whether that makes it better, and decides she wants somebody to ask her to pass the bread at least. Chloe does. Milim gives her half the loaf. Rem reaches over to take a piece for the plate before Milim can decide that the other half belongs to Ori.

Makima comes in with Bellion's next set of notes. She stands near the end of the table until Rimuru has found somebody to take the first half of the watch. There's a chair beside him. Umaru sees her look at it. She moves her plate far enough back for Makima to sit without having to ask. She does not move her own chair.

“I'm still angry,” Umaru tells her. ᵁ

Makima sets the notes down. “I remember.” ᴹᵏ

“Good. Did we have anybody who needed to come in while I was here?” ᵁ

Makima tells her about the man in the white helmet and where the others were when he left. Umaru asks whether that means the people from the other side can come here. Rimuru tells her the injured man can have the person he asked for if they can find him. He tells her where they'll meet him. Umaru listens long enough to know which door he'll come through.

Mau finishes the first half of his bowl before he remembers to ask about the road again. Yuka has eaten half the bread he thought she'd left for him. He takes the remaining piece before she can come back for it. She puts her head against his shoulder until he gives her the crust.

* * *

In the morning the injured man is awake before the person he asked for gets there. The name is his brother's. He makes sure Fern has the right place to send the message, then asks her whether somebody will make his brother sign a paper to get through.

“No,” she says. “We need to know which door to tell him to use. The man who brought you can show him.” ᶠᵉ

The soldier can see her paper from where he lies. He's been looking at it since she came in. He asks if she'll keep what he says next on a different one.

Fern takes a fresh sheet.

“There were men who wanted to leave before we got to the cart,” he says. “They told us the people at home would answer for us if they did.” ᴵⁿ

He stops when he hears somebody pass the door. The person passes. Fern gives him as long as it takes to hear the next set of footsteps go away too.

“Do you know who told you that?” she asks. ᶠᵉ

“I know who read it to us. I know where he went to get the paper.” ᴵⁿ

He puts his head back. Fern doesn't reach for the pencil until he says the place. Then she writes it on the sheet he asked for and shows him exactly what she's put down.
