# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 16 — The Weight Jin-Woo Knows

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S13E08
**Continuity state:** B+281 afternoon. Thomas Andre and a second National Hunter, Rook, reach the front. Jin-Woo holds alone before asking Mau to join him. Both retreat alive; one leaves his employer’s claim to authority in doubt.

*When: Jin-Woo can tell Thomas Andre has recognised him because Thomas stops asking the people behind him where he is.*

The man who comes down with Thomas lets him go first. Jin-Woo watches the distance he leaves. It is the sort of distance somebody leaves when he's fought beside a man who can do a great deal with both arms and wants room to do it.

Thomas stops where the carts have worn a dip in the road. He looks at Jin-Woo. His eyes go past him to where three shadows are carrying a board out of the way. The last soldier looks back at Jin-Woo before it goes round the bank.

“I heard you were here,” Thomas says. ᵀʰ

“You could've come to the house.” ᴶʷ

“I heard about that too.” Thomas lifts his chin towards the carts. “We were told somebody here has people who don't want to stay.” ᵀʰ

“Have you spoken to one of them?” ᴶʷ

Thomas looks at the man behind him. The man tells him the first time they'll get to speak to anybody is when they're across. Thomas rubs one hand over the other. Jin-Woo knows that gesture. He remembers what happened the last time he let it finish before he got his feet under him.

“You can come talk,” Jin-Woo says. “He can stay here.” ᴶʷ

The other man takes another step down the road. The air over Jin-Woo's shoulders bears down. A little stone beside his boot settles into the mud with a sound he can hear over the stream. He moves his foot before the same thing can happen to it.

Thomas moves at the same time.

Jin-Woo gets one arm around the first hit and lets the force carry him sideways. He knows what it costs to stop Thomas where Thomas wants him to stop. He wants to find out what it costs to keep him out of the place where people are still crossing the stream. The next hit takes him as far as the foot of the bank. He catches Thomas at the elbow before he can take a third step and holds him there long enough for two of his own shadows to get somebody behind the bank.

The man who came with Thomas is still walking. The pressure comes with him. It takes two of Jin-Woo's soldiers to hold their forms under it long enough to bring a third one out. When he looks back, Thomas has both feet on the piece of ground he was trying to keep him from.

Jin-Woo remembers being able to put enough bodies around a place that he could forget how many he had left. He has a hundred here. Several are with his family. The rest have things to do besides find out how many times Thomas can break them. He calls the three nearest him back. The first one reaches his shadow. The second goes down to one knee. He gets a hand under it before the third can come back for them both.

“Rook,” Thomas says over his shoulder. “You put that down before I move.” ᵀʰ

The man stops. The weight doesn't go away. Thomas looks back at Jin-Woo, who has got the soldier clear and is trying to find a good place to put his foot.

“You're different,” Thomas says. ᵀʰ

“I made a choice.” ᴶʷ

“Did he make it for you?” ᵀʰ

Jin-Woo waits until he has the breath for the answer. Then he says no. He sends word to Mau that he'd like a hand. He is still looking at Thomas when Mau asks whether he wants him there or behind them.

“Here,” Jin-Woo says. “I'll take Thomas.” ᴶʷ

* * *

Mau comes through a shadow at the bank. He puts his foot down, feels the weight on it and has to catch the edge of the slope with one hand. Jin-Woo sees him look down at the mud before he looks at Rook. There is enough time for Thomas to see it too.

“You want the one farther away?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“I want the one I know,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

Mau goes the other way. The pressure follows him for a moment, and Jin-Woo takes the space it leaves. Thomas had been standing with his weight forward because the ground made it difficult to do anything else. Now he can turn. Jin-Woo is already close enough to make him choose how far.

Rook moves his hand. Mau brings Sky up at the edge of the road. Loose grit goes off to one side. The mud under him doesn't change. He looks at it again. Jin-Woo sees him put his hand down to feel which part of the pressure is still there, and can tell when he understands enough to call for the shadows to move away from him. They come towards Jin-Woo instead. He sends them behind Thomas, out of reach of the bank and close enough to give him another way back when he needs one.

Thomas comes in hard enough for Jin-Woo to feel it in the arm he used to catch the first blow. He gives up the ground. Thomas takes it, then has to turn to follow when Jin-Woo trades places with one of his own. By the time Thomas sees him again, the only way to go straight at him is away from the bridge.

Thomas goes that way. Jin-Woo lets him see that he wanted him to. The first thing Thomas does when he realises it is laugh.

Rook calls for Thomas to come back. The pressure lifts all at once. Mau is standing in the road with his hand open. The man has one knee down and a piece of the cart's broken side laid across the ground in front of him. He looks at it, then looks at how far away the bridge is.

“Do you want to get up?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Rook does. Mau waits while he does it. Jin-Woo keeps his attention on Thomas, whose feet haven't moved since the pressure lifted.

“Ask the people who hired you for a name,” Jin-Woo tells him. “Somebody who's here and wants you to take them away. If they have one, I'll help you ask.” ᴶʷ

Thomas looks at Rook. Rook folds the paper he was carrying and puts it away. Neither of them comes any closer to the bridge.

“Next time I'll come to the house,” Thomas says. ᵀʰ

“Come by the front door,” Jin-Woo tells him. “I'll be glad to see you there.” ᴶʷ

He waits for them to go. His arm has got heavy. Mau comes back and holds out a hand before Jin-Woo has to tell him about it.

“You took your time,” Jin-Woo says. ᴶʷ

“I thought you liked having him to yourself.” ᴹ

“I did.” ᴶʷ

He takes Mau's hand. They go back over the bridge at the speed of the slower one. Neither of them asks which of them that is.
