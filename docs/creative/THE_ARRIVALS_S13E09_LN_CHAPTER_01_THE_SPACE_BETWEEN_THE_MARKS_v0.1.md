# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 17 — The Space Between the Marks

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S13E09
**Continuity state:** B+281 evening. Shuna detects and blocks an attempt to alter the memory of one of Muster's shadow sentries. Frieren and Fern locate the source by their tested ward records. The source withdraws. The effect is not universal and no identity is confirmed.

*When: One of Muster's shadows signals that the bridge is closed.*

The first of Muster's shadows back from the bridge comes in with water running off its gauntlet. It raises the hand so Shuna can move her notes before it puts anything down. She gives it a cloth. It dries its gauntlet, points to the bridge on Fern's map and crosses two fingers over it.

“The bridge is closed?” Shuna asks. ˢʰᵘ

The shadow nods.

“Fern, did Muster send an order to close it?” ˢʰᵘ

Fern shakes her head. Shuna looks down at the map. Water has run out from under the gauntlet towards the paper. She puts the cloth in its way.

“Which side did you come back from?” ˢʰᵘ

The soldier turns toward the window. The light above the bridge is visible from its chair. It belongs to the same woman who asked for her bench to stay where it was. Shuna had gone to see whether she'd managed to get home, and the woman had insisted on putting the light out herself when she was ready.

The sentry traces the road it took back to the house. Its finger follows the route all the way across the bridge. It stops there. After a moment it traces the same stretch again.

Shuna pulls the ward sheet out from under the map. There's a trace coming up along one edge, the way a little ink will move when a pen is put down too close to the end of a line.

“Tell me when you first saw this,” she says to Fern. ˢʰᵘ

Fern has her own sheet under the map. The two women look at their edges and then at the lamp by the door. Fern had seen the beginning of hers when the sentry first put its gauntlet down. Shuna's had started a little later. She asks Fern to tell Frieren which way round it happened, then moves the sentry's chair into the space she prepared against the interior wall.

The trace stops spreading as soon as the soldier sits there. Fern sends word to Muster to cover its next post. Shuna brings the map over and leaves it in reach.

“Can you show me who was still there when you left?” she asks. ˢʰᵘ

The sentry rests its finger on the bridge again. This time it taps the marker Fern put down for Gojo.

Fern confirms with Gojo. He remembers pointing the shadow towards the house so it could dry its gauntlet and make its report. He is still at the bridge. People are still crossing it.

* * *

Frieren brings her sheet in without folding it. She has seen a different edge go dark first. They put all three sheets down and move the map around them until they agree which side each one was on. Shuna takes a minute to look at where their hands all are. It was easier on the day they could ask Qifrey to try again.

“There,” she says. She taps the high side of the stream. “Between the post and the trees. Tell Rimuru it's the far bank.” ˢʰᵘ

Fern calls it in. Frieren leaves long enough to find out whether she can see the place from the bridge. She comes back with a little dirt at the hem of her coat and the account of a light that went out when she turned to look at it.

“Someone was there,” Frieren says. “I couldn't see what they were doing.” ᶠ

“Will they know it failed?” Shuna asks. ˢʰᵘ

“If they saw the sentry come back to report.” ᶠ

Shuna looks back at the sentry. Its gauntlet has gone still on the edge of the map. Fern sets the morning assignment down beside it, open to the order from Muster to keep the bridge clear. The sentry reads the line, follows its route with one finger and touches Gojo's marker again. This time it gives the signal for an open crossing. Shuna watches the edge of the ward sheet. It stays clear.

Muster sends the sentry's relief in by the other door. Shuna shows Fern the route she wants it to use while she checks the first mark. Fern gives the change to Muster, and the soldier moves to the map as soon as Muster signals it. Shuna keeps the three papers in the light so she can see all of them at once.

Mau comes in a little later, wanting to know what she found. Shuna shows him the two routes on the map and tells him what the sentry signaled before and after she moved it inside the ward. Somebody made it remember a closure it never carried out. He asks what she needs.

“Someone out by the post to watch the first mark,” she says. “I want to know which one they reach for next time.” ˢʰᵘ

Mau orders Bellion to put a shadow by the post and have its relief learn the marks too. The first soldier comes in while Shuna is still wiping the water off the table. She gives it a dry corner of the sheet. It waits for her to show it what she saw.
