# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 18 — Something to Look At

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S13E09
**Continuity state:** B+282 morning. Gojo holds the crossing with Yuta and Maki. Ramiris and Qifrey open their reversible bank passage for civilian and medical traffic. A defender discovers the enemy is timing reliefs. Kashimo is not present.

*When: A man on the other bank waits for Gojo to look at the light before he tries to cross.*

Gojo heard about the light at supper. He ate another piece of bread while Shuna told him how they'd found it. When she asked if he wanted to go look in the morning, he told her he'd have a good look at the man carrying it if he came back.

The man comes back before the morning has warmed up. He has something covered with a cloth on a board. Gojo sees him put it down, wait for the cart coming the other way to go past and take the cloth off.

There are two lights now. The first one moves down the bank. The second one sits still until Gojo looks at the first. Then a man with a spear comes out below it and gets one foot on the bridge.

Gojo walks into his way. The spear comes up. It stops. The man looks at where the point stopped as though he's missed a piece of glass somebody put in front of him. He presses harder until his hands are shaking and then gives up the space between them to try from the other side.

“Can you swim?” Gojo asks. ᴳ

The man looks down at the stream. That gives Maki enough time to come up behind him. She takes the spear by the middle, lets him choose which way he'll go without it and waits to see if he needs a hand over the edge of the bank. He puts both hands up. She brings him off the bridge where the people coming down behind him can see him stand.

The light on the far bank goes out. Gojo waits until somebody who was following it moves into the open. He could go and get the light himself. He stays where he is long enough for Yuta to call that he's already got somebody coming towards the path.

There's another one under the bridge.

He sees it in the water before he sees the man carrying it. The man is up to his ribs and holding on with one hand. The light runs from his fist to the first support. Gojo goes over the rail. His foot is still clearing it when he sees what the man has done with the other lights. The one upstream was put where he could reach it. The man underneath is standing just far enough away that if Gojo goes for him he can set off both and catch somebody on the path either way.

Gojo catches the man's coat with Blue. His hand comes loose from the support. So does the light.

He has time to swear once. Yuta's blade is already between the light and the timber when it goes off. The water leaps right past them. Yuta comes back out of it with the bottom of his shirt torn free and a boot in his hand. He throws the boot onto the bank. Then he lets Rika take the man Gojo caught and bring him out of the water where he can breathe.

“Your boot?” Gojo asks. ᴳ

“His.” ʸᵗ

Gojo puts a hand on the timber. It's wet right through. One of Qifrey's marks has stopped moving with the shadow of the bridge and begun to hold steady on the bank. The other end of that mark is still open. Gojo asks Yuta whether anybody's using it. There are two people on the path. Yuta tells him the next light is on his side.

Maki comes over the rail behind them. She has the spear she took. She runs it into the mud where the man has to step to climb out, hooks the flat of her foot behind his wrist and holds it until he lets go of the thing he was reaching for. A shadow comes up beside Rika with a hand free. Rika gives it room to take the man's weight. Maki gets back to the path before the shadow's finished getting him on his feet.

“The two people on your side are through,” she tells Gojo. ᴹᵃᵏ

He waits until Yuta hears it. Then he puts Blue above the water and takes the last light straight up. It goes high enough to be reflected in every window along the bank. He keeps it there until it goes out by itself. The man who brought it watches from beside the shadow. When Gojo comes up he asks him if they were supposed to go after the bridge or after whoever came to stop them.

The man wants his boot. Yuta gets it for him. He works his foot into it, sits there with his palm around the heel and tells them he only knew about the bridge.

* * *

There is a cart just below the bench. The woman who uses it is on the seat next to the driver, telling him which way she usually walks when she wants the road to herself. He can see a corner of it from where he sits. There are two men on that corner now. He doesn't know either of them. He gets off the cart to ask the woman whether she wants him to try the bridge first.

Fern reaches them before he has to go. She asks whether there is anything in the cart they can't take out and carry. There is a box the driver would rather have two people lift. She gets a shadow for the other end. It holds the box while he asks if they're going to leave the cart where it is. Fern says he can come back when he likes and they'll try to have the road clear for him.

Qifrey has set one of his marks in the grass. He waits by it until the woman asks where it goes. He shows her which part she'll be able to see from here, and asks if she wants him to go first. She tells him she'd like to put one hand on his arm if he doesn't mind. He gives her the arm. They step in together. Fern keeps one hand on the side of the cart while she waits for the other mark to come back into view across the bank.

When the woman comes out, she sits on the bench. It's the same board she used yesterday. She gets her breath there while the shadow brings the box up. Fern asks her if the way through was good enough to use again.

“Good enough if I can sit when I get there,” she says. ᵂ

Fern puts a hand on the bench to see how much room there's left. She leaves the room at the end for Qifrey to sit down too. The woman asks what he usually teaches when he isn't taking people through a bank. He tells her what he's been working on with Richeh. By the time the cart driver arrives with the last thing he could carry, they are both looking at one of the marks on his card.

* * *

Yuta takes Gojo's place after the woman has gone. There had been a part of the morning when he'd thought he was going to have to do it earlier. He asks Gojo what the other side did when it saw him get close.

“Somebody got ready to move as soon as I did,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“Did they follow you?” ʸᵗ

“No. I think they were waiting to see who took my place.” ᴳ

Yuta looks back across the stream. There is a person behind the men by the lights who hasn't lifted a weapon all morning. He has been there long enough for Yuta to remember him standing beside the board when the cloth came off. He looks down as Yuta arrives. He doesn't have anything in his hands that Yuta can see from here.

“Can you stay until I tell you who's with me?” Yuta asks. ʸᵗ

Gojo stops before he gets to the end of the bridge. Yuta finds Maki on the other side of the bank. She answers him. He finds Fern and asks whether she can still see the woman from where she is. Fern says the woman is on her way home now. Qifrey has kept the passage open in case the driver wants to make a second trip.

“Okay,” Yuta tells Gojo. “I've got it.” ʸᵗ

Gojo goes. Yuta watches the person by the board look down again. He tells Rimuru what he saw. Rimuru asks him whether he'd like somebody else to look too.

“Yes,” Yuta says. “I'd like them to see whether he does it every time we change.” ʸᵗ

He puts his feet where Gojo had his and waits. The next man to come down to the bridge sees the wrong person there and stops before he's close enough to have to say what he wanted.
