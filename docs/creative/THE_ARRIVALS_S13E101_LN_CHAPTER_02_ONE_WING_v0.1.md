# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 82
## Chapter 26 — One Wing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 82
**Reading order:** 26
**Source episode:** S13E101
**Continuity state:** Whole Fort game begins; ordinary knights nonverbal, marked safe routes and real emergency exception. Three wing tokens needed for treasury. Bell lane first win, no combat damage or new powers. Mau and Yuka active with Ori while on opposite play sides.

*When: B+405, breakfast through the opening of the Hamster Revolution.*

Ori wakes before Frieren does.

For a while she stays beneath the blanket, remembering what is different about the morning. The shell is on the shelf. The little carved bird is beside it, still carrying stolen fruit. Her explorer clothes are folded on the chair instead of put away.

Then she remembers Beru.

She sits up too quickly and has to retrieve the blanket from the floor.

Frieren opens one eye.

“Has the invasion begun?” ᶠ

“Not yet.” ᴼ

“Then we have time to eat.” ᶠ

Ori looks toward the clothes. Frieren lifts the blanket onto the bed, gets up and helps with the fastening Ori cannot quite reach when she is in a hurry. They leave the room together, her mother's hand warm around hers, and find Beru waiting beside the common-room door in the matching jacket.

He has the pencil in the pocket.

Ori looks up at him and grins.

---

Breakfast has acquired two sides.

Shuna has a little paper hamster pinned to her apron. Ori drew it before sitting down, and Shuna bent so she could put it on herself. The tray of cakes is already waiting by the kitchen door, safely out of reach of the military discussion.

Umaru has put the unopened packet from yesterday beside her map. Exhibit One now has its own ribbon.

The seating dispute was not intended. Mau discovers the problem when he sits beside Yuka and Umaru points out that the attaché is hearing classified information.

“She's describing toast.” ᴹ

“You don't know that.” ᵁ

“I can see it.” ᴹ

Yuka offers him the piece she has been buttering. He accepts it, and Umaru looks to Rem for support against a breakdown in the established order.

Rem passes her a cup.

“We haven't started.” ᴿᵉ

“That's how they get you.” ᵁ

Ori arrives before the argument can become a rule. Mau gets up to look at both explorers, then bends when she takes his sleeve and asks him something quietly.

“It'll still be there?” ᴼ

He looks toward the windows. The castle stands across the field, where they left it yesterday. A ribbon moves over the entrance. Kamish's head is visible beyond the low courtyard wall.

“It'll still be there after breakfast.” ᴹ

She sits down between Chloe and Frieren. For the next several minutes she eats with such determination that Yuka has to move the jam away from her sleeve twice.

Mau leaves the last piece of toast where she can reach it. The enemy, it appears, has breakfast too.

---

The banner over the gate reads The Hamster Revolution.

Beneath the words, Bocchi's small hamster has acquired a helmet. Kita insists this has made it look braver. Bocchi is not certain whether the helmet is too large. Suika studies it and gives a firm nod, which appears to settle the matter more effectively than a long explanation would have.

Umaru carries the map. Ori walks beside her with Chloe on the other side and Beru behind them. Milim is trying to persuade Maki that breaking a siege does not necessarily require breaking anything important.

“The castle is important,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

“Only a little corner.” ᴹⁱ

“The corners are holding up the rest.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Milim looks at Mai. Mai gives her exactly the same expression, and the appeal fails before it reaches words.

Marin comes along with the cloth pennants she finished after dinner. Hakari and Karane disagree about who should carry the spare; Shizuka takes it while they are still choosing, solving the problem so quietly that Nano has to point out where it went.

The five students have drawn their own mark on the map. Kenya wants to be first through the gate. Alice says that would require arriving ahead of Ori, and he shortens his next step rather than running past her.

At the entrance, two ordinary knights cross their wooden staffs.

They do not speak.

One points to the board beside the door. Three empty places wait beneath a picture of a bell, a paper boat and a spoon. The treasury opens when all three wing tokens are brought to the courtyard.

Umaru puts her hands on her hips.

“We've come to liberate your supplies,” Umaru announces. “And remove the vending citizen from the service of the oppressor of sweets.” ᵁ

Mau looks up from the water tray inside.

“You assigned me to him.” ᴹ

“The presidency has reviewed the appointment.” ᵁ

Shuna bends toward Ori.

“I was promised an invasion.” ˢʰᵘ

“We have to get in first,” Ori whispers. ᴼ

The knight points lower on the board.

Please.

Ori studies it, then looks up at the helmet.

“Please.” ᴼ

The staffs separate.

Mau waits just inside with a tray of water. Yuka takes a cup as she passes and kisses his mouth before going after the others.

He looks down at the tray.

“That wasn't in the brief.” ᴹ

“Put it in the report,” she tells him. ʸ

---

The Quiet Wing begins with a bell over a narrow marked lane.

The soldier at the far end raises one hand. When it lowers, they may move. When it rises, they must stop. A light cord runs along the lane at different heights, with a few smaller bells waiting to betray a careless sleeve.

Each person carries a paper flower to the basket at the other end. There are sweets waiting on the side table, but the token stays beside the guard until the basket is full.

Umaru examines the lane.

“No flying,” she tells Milim. ᵁ

“I heard.” ᴹⁱ

“No moving the lane.” ᵁ

Milim looks down at the edge of the mat beneath her foot.

“You didn't say that before.” ᴹⁱ

“I saw you thinking.” ᵁ

Ryo takes a flower and asks whether the reward changes if she completes more than one journey. Seika, watching from the doorway with Ram, points out the full tray and the boxes beneath it. Ryo surveys the supply, chooses which packet she intends to collect and enters the lane.

Shuna takes her own flower and tucks the loose edge of her apron into the pocket before joining the line. Ori checks the arrangement with the seriousness of someone who has recruited a person taller than most of the obstacles.

“You have to watch that cord,” she tells her. ᴼ

“I will. I'm being very careful about my first campaign.” ˢʰᵘ

The guard lowers its hand.

They move in an untidy procession. Rem keeps one eye on Umaru's sleeve. Suika takes small steps, looking through the gap between two cords. Maki goes farther in one stride than anybody expected, then holds perfectly still when the hand rises.

Shuna stops beneath the first cord with her free hand holding the apron pocket close. She looks back at Ori, gets a small approving nod and waits for the guard to let them move again.

Milim stops with both feet just above the mat.

The entire lane turns toward her.

“I'm not going up,” she says. ᴹⁱ

Umaru points at the floor.

Milim lands. A bell makes a small, accusing sound.

From the doorway Mau closes his eyes. Yuka looks back at him and finds his shoulders moving.

The knight points Milim back to the start. She goes, keeping her flower intact with immense personal restraint.

---

Ori gets almost to the basket before her hat catches the lowest cord.

The brim lifts it. The bell begins to move.

Beru puts out a hand, stops himself before touching the cord and looks toward the guard. Ori has heard the first tiny note. She lowers her head so quickly that her hat slips over her eyes.

The bell rings properly.

She goes still beneath the brim.

Yuka waits. Beru has turned toward her, plainly hoping that there is an interpretation of the rules which allows this to become somebody else's mistake.

Ori lifts the hat.

“I have to go back?” she asks. ᴼ

The soldier points to the beginning of the lane.

Chloe, already at the basket, keeps Ori's place beside hers empty.

Ori takes off the hat and gives it to Beru. Then she walks back to the start with the flower held carefully upright.

Mau brings her a cup before the next attempt. She drinks, gives it back and looks at him.

“You knew about the bell.” ᴼ

“I helped hang it.” ᴹ

She gives this betrayal serious consideration.

“It's a good bell.” ᴼ

“Thank you.” ᴹ

She hands him the cup and goes back to the lane before the guard lowers its hand again.

---

On the second attempt, she lets the others get farther ahead before starting.

There is room to see the cord this time. She ducks where the hat caught, turns the flower sideways beneath the next strand and reaches the last clear space before the basket.

The hand rises.

She stops.

Chloe is waiting close enough to reach, smiling without waving her over. Ori keeps her knees bent, the flower against her chest, and watches the hand until it lowers.

Then she runs the last two little steps.

The flower lands in the basket. Chloe catches both her hands. Behind them Milim completes a remarkably careful passage, puts her flower beside theirs and turns toward the rest of the wing with the air of someone who has defeated a much larger opponent.

Shuna is waiting by the basket with her flower already inside. She watches Ori and Chloe compare their successful routes, looking delighted with her own part in getting this far.

Beru is the last through. He has to bend beneath every cord. Ori stands beside the basket holding his hat this time, encouraging him in a whisper that grows steadily less quiet.

He reaches her without ringing a bell.

The guard puts the first token in her hand.

She turns it over, tracing the little bell carved into its face. Then she holds it high enough for everyone to see.

From the courtyard Kamish's voice rolls through the open doorway.

“One wing does not constitute a conquest.” ᴷ

Umaru takes the sweet Rem has been keeping for her.

“He sounds worried.” ᵁ

Mau looks toward the enormous head above the wall.

“I'll include that in the report too.” ᴹ
