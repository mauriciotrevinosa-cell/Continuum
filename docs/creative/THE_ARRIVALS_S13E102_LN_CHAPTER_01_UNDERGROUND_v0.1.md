# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 82
## Chapter 27 — Underground

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 82
**Reading order:** 27
**Source episode:** S13E102
**Continuity state:** Ink Wing paper boat challenge solved by actual folds/current observations. Nonverbal ordinary knight gives second token. Entire visible pretend tunnel honoured by silent Kamish after Umaru/Ori explain underground; no real excavation or combat. Fort girls and existing students participate, not new residents.

*When: B+405, late morning through the courtyard infiltration.*

The knight in the Ink Wing has a paper boat balanced on its helmet.

This proves distracting enough that Ori nearly misses the channel running across the room. Water moves from a little reservoir at one end to a shallow tray at the other. The narrow lane between them has three turns and a bridge which is lower than the boat on the knight's head.

Coco studies the bridge. Richeh studies the paper.

Umaru studies the table of sweets beside the exit. There are open tins, several deep bowls and another box waiting beneath the cloth.

“The scale of the occupation grows,” she says. ᵁ

Shuna looks into the nearest bowl.

“I told you there were hundreds.” ˢʰᵘ

Rem gives her an amused look. Shuna takes a sweet and turns back to see what Ori has found in the channel.

The guard takes the boat down and sets it beside a stack of square sheets. On the board, a drawing shows the start, the channel and the far tray. After the boat crosses the starting line, hands stay out of the water.

Milim crouches to see beneath the bridge.

“It won't fit.” ᴹⁱ

Coco is already folding another sheet.

The first boat is beautiful. It has a little raised prow, a broad base and enough room inside that Ori suggests carrying a passenger. The guard points to the picture of a boat with nobody in it.

“For now,” Ori tells Milim, who has begun looking interested. ᴼ

They launch it.

It reaches the first turn, catches sideways and stays there, bowing slightly under the current. Coco bends to look. Richeh points out the place where the base has spread against both sides of the channel.

Mau watches from the doorway with the empty water tray. He does not offer a solution. Coco takes the failed boat out after the knight brings it back and unfolds it to see what the water changed.

That interests him more than a correct first attempt would have.

---

The second boat is narrower.

Tetia holds the unused paper while Coco and Richeh compare two possible folds. Suika follows the channel to the far end, leans close and discovers that the current is faster beside one wall. She comes back to show them, tracing the turn with her finger in the air.

Ori listens, then moves the little starting marker closer to that side.

“Here?” she asks. ᴼ

Suika nods.

This boat clears the first turn. Everyone follows it along the side until the approaching bridge makes them quiet.

The raised prow touches the underside.

For one instant it seems to have stopped. Then the current turns the boat and pins it beneath the bridge exactly as securely as the first one wedged itself in the channel.

Milim lies down on the floor to inspect the failure from water level.

“A very low bridge,” she reports. ᴹⁱ

The guard points toward the paper.

Richeh takes another sheet. She folds the little prow inward instead of leaving it raised. Coco presses the crease flat while Tetia keeps the unfinished edges out of the wet patch on the table.

Ori has been keeping the two failed boats beside her. She gives the first one to Chloe so she can use both hands to carry the new version to the start.

“Everybody ready?” ᴼ

They gather beside the channel. Beru bends low enough to see over Ori's shoulder without making her move. Bocchi comes in from the doorway with Kita, notices the concentration around the water and lowers her voice without needing to be asked.

Ori puts the boat where Suika showed her.

It goes.

Around the first turn. Beneath the bridge, low prow passing with a little room to spare. At the last bend it touches the wall, and Chloe catches Ori's wrist before she can reach toward it.

“Wait.” ᶜʰ

The current lifts the stern. The boat slides free and turns into the receiving tray.

Milim cheers loudly enough to bring people from the next room. Coco laughs with both hands still held in the shape of the fold she no longer needs to keep making.

The knight sets the boat on a dry cloth. Then it gives Ori the second token.

She takes it straight to Coco.

“You carry this one.” ᴼ

Coco looks at the little carved boat, closes her hand around it and goes with the others to the table where the rewards have been waiting.

---

There is a room with no token beyond the water channel.

It has a shelf of small wooden boxes, a knight holding a picture card and a handwritten note warning that the knight is extremely suspicious of biscuits. The boxes have different shapes on their lids. The card shows a key, a moon and a fish.

Shizuka reads the note twice, looks toward the shelf and then points out the fish-shaped shadow cast by the window catch. Nano tests the latch beneath it. A little drawer opens.

It contains wrapped almond sweets.

Karane accuses Hakari of standing close enough to discover this before anyone else. Hakari offers her the first one instead of defending herself. Kusuri looks at the opened drawer, then at the remaining shapes, and begins examining how the light reaches the other catches.

Marin calls the room excellent. Agott looks at the door they came through, then back at the box which was never locked at all, and writes something on the edge of Umaru's map.

“What?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

Agott shows her. Check before declaring a siege.

Umaru objects to the political implication while trying the almond sweet. She takes the map back with the note still on it.

Shuna holds the little drawer open while Shizuka shows Nano the second catch. There are more packets beneath the first layer. Karane takes one for Hakari, who immediately offers to trade for the one she already gave her.

“We should secure the room,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“You can come back after lunch,” Mau tells her from the doorway. ᴹ

She looks at the drawers again, finding the promise much more dangerous than an objection.

---

The courtyard gate is closed when they reach it.

Kamish has put his head above the wall. A narrow opening beside it leads toward the Kitchen Wing, but the broad central gate is barred from the inside. Mau sits beneath the window at a table with cups, the treasury board and the packet Yuka has already noticed him trying to keep separate.

“Two wings,” Kamish observes. ᴷ

Ori holds up the bell. Coco raises the boat.

“I can count them.” ᴷ

“We wanted you to see properly,” Umaru says. ᵁ

The dragon looks down at the map.

“And how do you propose to enter?” ᴷ

Milim lifts a hand.

Maki catches her wrist before the answer becomes architecture.

Momo Ayase looks along the base of the wall.

“Could we go under?” ᴹᵒ

For a moment the whole group examines the ground.

Coco looks at the broad blank space on the map. Then she fetches the chalk from the little box beside the door.

She draws two lines in the grass, beginning outside the gate and curving toward the side opening.

Ori understands before the second line is finished.

“A tunnel.” ᴼ

Chloe goes to help mark the far end. Suika puts a little mound of loose leaves beside the entrance to show where the digging has begun. Richeh folds the corner of the map so that the route fits beneath it rather than crossing an existing room.

Beru watches the operation, visibly ready to offer his claws. Ori gives him the rolled banner to carry instead.

“We have to stay low,” she explains. ᴼ

---

Kamish sees the entire tunnel being made.

He sees Umaru crouch at the chalk entrance. He sees Ori crouch behind her. He sees Chloe, Suika and Coco arrange themselves between the two lines with the seriousness of an expedition about to cross somewhere very dark.

His eye follows them as they begin shuffling toward the side of the gate.

Umaru stops.

“We're underground,” she tells him. ᵁ

The enormous eye remains on her.

Ori looks up from beneath the imaginary ceiling.

“You can't know where we are.” ᴼ

There is a long pause.

Mau puts his cup down very carefully.

Kamish turns his head toward the sky.

He says nothing.

Umaru moves again. Ori follows. At the narrow part of the chalk route, Beru has to lower himself almost onto his hands to keep the banner beneath a ceiling which exists only because the princess has explained it.

Kamish studies a cloud with immense attention.

Shuna follows Beru into the tunnel, gathering her apron clear of the imaginary ceiling. When Kamish's eye begins to turn toward her, she crouches lower with such immediate seriousness that Mau has to look down at his cup.

The dragon returns to the cloud.

Milim arrives at the tunnel mouth, looks at the rest of the invasion disappearing beneath a perfectly visible wall and drops onto her knees with immediate delight. The people behind her begin laughing. She puts a finger to her lips with such ferocity that they have to cover their mouths.

Inside the courtyard, Mau bends over the campaign report.

Yuka can see him through the side opening. She can also see that he has not written anything for some time.

She joins the end of the line.

---

The tunnel comes up beside the Kitchen Wing.

Ori is the first to stand. She puts both feet outside the chalk, brushes entirely imaginary soil from her jacket and checks behind her for the others. Beru rises at her shoulder with the banner still rolled safely beneath his arm.

Umaru examines the courtyard as though they have penetrated a stronghold by means no enemy could have anticipated.

Mau looks up from the blank report.

“How did you get in?” he asks. ᴹ

Ori points toward the two lines.

“You can't tell him. He doesn't know.” ᴼ

Mau looks at the dragon, whose head remains turned in exactly the wrong direction to notice the invading force gathering behind the gate.

“Understood.” ᴹ

He writes that down. Yuka goes past him, touches his shoulder once and sees the word on the page before he can cover it.

Tunnelling.

She starts laughing so hard that Ori has to remind her they are still being secret.
