# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 82
## Chapter 28 — Terms Are Acceptable

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 82
**Reading order:** 28
**Source episode:** S13E102
**Continuity state:** Third wing matched flavours, three existing tokens open treasury as promised; no surprise rule to invalidate win. *When: B+405, noon through the opening of the treasury.*

The Kitchen Wing smells of warm apples.

Umaru notices before anyone reaches the door. She has stopped pretending the invasion is motivated by anything more complicated than access to the shelves. Rem follows her in with the map, and the rest of the expedition gathers around a knight wearing a paper crown over its helmet.

The wooden spoon is still upright in one hand. The shelves behind it are full: biscuit tins, wrapped chocolate, jars with little sugar-coated shapes inside and the cakes Shuna brought before breakfast.

She comes through the door behind Ori and pauses to admire the paper crown.

“I helped supply this officer,” she admits. ˢʰᵘ

Umaru turns toward her.

“Before enlistment,” Shuna adds, smiling. ˢʰᵘ

Rem catches the map before the president can reach for a fresh page.

“A dangerous officer,” Mai observes. ᴹᵃ

Maki looks at the spoon, then at the empty bowls arranged in front of it.

“We've faced worse.” ᴹᵃᵏ

The knight points to three little dishes and three illustrated cards. Apple, orange, ginger. The cards have to go beside the matching filling. There is a basket of plain rice cakes to taste them with, and the token beneath a clear cover beside the spoon.

Milim is first to the basket.

“I can do this one.” ᴹⁱ

“You have to taste them separately,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

Milim considers the arrangement in her hand.

“Now I know.” ᴹⁱ

---

The first filling is easy.

Ori takes a small bite, looks at Chloe and points at the apple. Chloe tries it too before they put the card down. It tastes like the apples Shuna warms when she has more than anyone wants to eat raw, except that this has been cooked until the pieces hold together on the cake.

The next two are both sweet and both sharp.

Umaru tastes one, thinks, then tastes it again. Rem asks whether the second bite was necessary to the investigation. Umaru assures her that it was essential.

Maomao smells the dishes before taking a little of the one nearest her. Kusuri is already doing the same on the other side. They compare the bit of peel caught on the spoon, look at the cards and wait while Suika comes close enough to examine it herself.

Suika points to the orange.

Ori takes another bite. The sharpness leaves a familiar smell at the back of her nose, like the peel in the little packets Yuka brought home. She moves the orange card beside it.

That leaves ginger.

Milim wants to confirm the remaining conclusion. Umaru wants to confirm Milim's. Rem moves the basket between them before confirmation consumes the evidence.

The knight places its spoon flat on the table.

For a second nobody moves. Then the covered dish slides toward Ori.

The third token is a spoon.

Ori takes it with sticky fingers, realizes the problem and gives it to Chloe while she looks for the cloth. Beru has one ready from his new pocket. She stares at it in delighted recognition.

“You used it.” ᴼ

“The equipment is excellent, Princess.” ᴮᵉ

She cleans her hands, takes the token back and puts a rice cake in the pocket which held the cloth. Beru looks down at the change in his supplies as though she has just decorated him for service.

---

Mau is outside with more water when they emerge.

The third token goes up. The cheer travels through the courtyard and brings the people still investigating the smaller rooms to their doors. Kenya and Gale arrive carrying a box which turns out to contain little paper flags instead of sweets. Alice has the sweets. Ryota has understood this arrangement much sooner than the others.

Fern collects the wrappers before they can blow beneath the benches. Mikasa helps Seiko turn one of the benches into somewhere Ichika can see the treasury door. Turbo Granny has acquired a ribbon and the displeased expression of a cat who did not approve the appointment that came with it.

Yuka brings Mau an apple rice cake.

“Contraband,” he says. ᴹ

“We won it.” ʸ

“Then I shouldn't accept it.” ᴹ

She holds it nearer his mouth.

“You should support your wife.” ʸ

He takes the bite. She watches him taste it, smiles and eats the rest herself while leaning against his side.

Across the courtyard Kamish's eye turns toward them.

“My attaché appears to have been compromised.” ᴷ

“Long before the campaign,” Mau answers. ᴹ

Yuka laughs into his shoulder. He kisses her once, tasting the little sweetness she has left at the corner of her mouth, and lets her go when Ori calls for the queen.

She takes his empty cup with her.

---

They gather at the treasury board.

Ori has the spoon. Coco carries the boat. Chloe has been keeping the bell safe while Ori washes the last stickiness from her fingers. Umaru puts the map on the table, straightens the corner with the tunnel on it and looks up at Kamish.

The dragon is watching now. He has apparently exhausted the interesting properties of the cloud.

“We have your three wings,” she says. ᵁ

“You have visited them,” Kamish replies. ᴷ

“We have the tokens.” ᵁ

“An impressive collection of kitchen equipment.” ᴷ

Ori steps forward.

“The door opens when we put them here.” ᴼ

The great head lowers. It leaves Kamish looking at the board beside her instead of down at the whole courtyard.

“Then put them there.” ᴷ

She takes the bell from Chloe and presses it into its place. Coco sets the boat beside it. The spoon goes last, a little crooked until Ori turns it and finds the edge that fits.

Mau lifts the wooden bar inside the door. It is the job he has been waiting to do since the first paper flower reached the basket.

The treasury opens.

For a moment the entire invading force goes quiet.

The shelves run along two walls, full of things they know and things they have not tried: bright little fruit sweets, chocolate in flat stacks, biscuit tins and the packets from the island. Several boxes stand open beneath them, with enough left for the people who want to come back through the rooms later. On the wide table beside the window, trays of Shuna's cakes have a place among the things Mau bought.

A chest holds a different labelled parcel for each person who came. Ori sees Chloe's name before her own. Suika's is beside Coco's. Marin discovers that somebody has tied a miniature version of the banner around hers.

Umaru approaches the shelf with an expression Rem has seen only a few times and generally in the presence of a newly restored game.

“We have won,” she says, very softly. ᵁ

“I noticed,” Rem answers. ᴿᵉ

Shuna comes to stand beside them. Umaru raises the unopened packet from yesterday, compares it with a whole box of the same thing and solemnly sets the evidence among the liberated supplies.

“The information was accurate,” she tells Shuna. ᵁ

“I'm pleased it was useful.” ˢʰᵘ

Umaru opens the packet at last. She offers it to Shuna, takes one herself and, when Mau comes nearer to help carry the parcels, holds it out to him too. He accepts before she remembers he is the accused.

From beside the door, Mau looks at Yuka.

“I could have opened it yesterday.” ᴹ

She slips an arm around his waist.

“Then we wouldn't have had an invasion.” ʸ

He looks back at Ori, who has just found the parcel with Shuna's name and is carrying it toward her with both hands.

“A poor use of a castle,” he agrees. ᴹ

---

Nobody leaves the others outside to watch them eat.

The parcels come out into the courtyard. There are arguments about which should be opened first, two immediate exchanges and a long discussion between Ryo and Nijika about the meaning of a gift with one's own name on it. Kita helps Bocchi loosen a knot before the ribbon can become a permanent part of her hands.

Ori remains by the board.

Mau sees her looking up at Kamish and waits. He has begun to learn when the part after the activity matters more than arranging the next one.

“Did we win properly?” she asks the dragon. ᴼ

Kamish's eye moves toward the gate, then the bell lane, then the narrow opening through which an entire army arrived from beneath the earth.

“You came through my defences. You brought the tokens. You opened the treasury.” ᴷ

“The tunnel was good.” ᴼ

“I saw no tunnel.” ᴷ

Ori's grin arrives so quickly that Mau has to turn his face away.

“Exactly.” ᴼ

Kamish lets the silence remain while she enjoys the victory. Then he asks what the conquerors intend to do with the conquered.

Ori looks at Umaru. Umaru has a biscuit halfway to her mouth. She lowers it, summons a suitable expression and approaches the dragon with Rem beside her.

“He can join the celebration,” she decides. ᵁ

“On what terms?” Kamish asks. ᴷ

Ori holds up the packet with the name Mau wrote for him. It is much larger than hers.

“You have your own.” ᴼ

The dragon studies the parcel. Mau has put aside a portion from the food the household already keeps for him rather than attempting to feed an enormous dragon with six sugared almonds.

“The terms are acceptable,” Kamish says. ᴷ

Milim throws both arms around Ori from behind and lifts her just enough to make her laugh. Frieren looks over from the bench.

“Milim.” ᶠ

Milim lowers her immediately, keeping the embrace.

“She won a castle.” ᴹⁱ

“I saw.” ᶠ

Ori turns within the embrace to hug her back. Frieren lets them finish before coming to straighten the band which has twisted around her daughter's shoulder.

---

Makima has watched the end from beside DIO, near Mau's table.

When Ori comes to show them the three tokens, Makima looks at each one, then at the chalk line on the map.

“You took the entrance he wasn't guarding,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“He was guarding it,” Ori explains. “But he couldn't see us.” ᴼ

Makima follows the line. She has seen exactly how the tunnel worked.

“An important advantage,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Ori nods, pleased to have been understood, and shows DIO the place where Milim nearly moved the lane.

DIO wants to know whether that would have counted. Ori is still explaining when Chloe calls her to see what happened to the paper boat after it dried.

Makima lets the girl finish, looks where she points and does not offer the much shorter route she can see on the page.

Mau catches her looking at him after Ori has gone.

“What?” he asks. ᴹ

“Nothing she needed me to correct.” ᴹᵏ

He follows her gaze back to the children. Then he smiles at her and moves the plate between their cups so she can reach it too.

She chooses a biscuit. For once she does not need to turn the exchange into a reason to remain longer. Everyone is still here.
