# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 82
## Chapter 29 — Excellent Water

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 82
**Reading order:** 29
**Source episode:** S13E103
**Continuity state:** Full Fort castle aftermath, repeated rooms and ordinary family company. Actual play detail recalled by Mau, real watch unchanged. Castle remains checked for afternoon and goes back into storage after use, not new permanent property. Pillow noncombatant hearth beast with long nose. Future explorer outing not scheduled; no new war or forced endgame.

*When: B+405, victory lunch through the family evening.*

The first thing the Fort does with its conquered castle is have lunch.

This takes more organization than the invasion.

The sweets have been distributed, compared and partially traded. The empty cups have migrated to three different rooms. Someone has put the paper boat beside the apple filling, which Ori considers an alarming location for a vessel that survived so much water.

She rescues it while Rem and Fern clear the courtyard table. Shuna arrives with the food she meant them to eat before the rest of the treasury, takes in the wrappers and gives Mau a look of profound familiarity.

“I was outnumbered,” he says. ᴹ

“You supplied them.” ˢʰᵘ

“An administrative oversight.” ᴹ

Shuna puts a plate in his hands and goes to find the place where Milim has decided to sit. Shion follows with the second dish, tells Kamish that his parcel is being brought to the other side of the courtyard and receives such a dignified acknowledgment that she laughs before turning away.

Umaru has kept the chair beside Rem. This becomes a political issue only when Ryo asks whether victorious territories are available for rent.

Seika answers by putting another plate in front of her.

The negotiations pause.

---

Ori brings Mau the map while he is still eating.

She has added the three tokens in the margin, a little boat beneath the bridge and a long dotted line where the tunnel goes. The dotted line passes directly through the place Kamish occupied during most of the game.

Mau examines it.

“A bold route.” ᴹ

“He didn't know,” Ori says. ᴼ

“Of course.” ᴹ

She leans against his knee to see what he is pointing at. He moves his plate, lifts her onto the seat beside him and lets her show him the part where the boat almost stopped. Coco comes over with the real boat before the account has reached the bridge. Richeh corrects which fold changed between the second and third attempts.

Mau listens to both versions. Chloe supplies the bit about waiting at the last turn. Ori agrees that this was essential, although she does not initially remember how far her own hand had been over the water.

Yuka sees him move his cup without being asked so that all three can spread the map between them.

She stays at the other end of the table long enough to hear Mai tell Momo Nishimiya about the knight with the spoon. Momo has seen it. She wants to hear Mai's version anyway. Under the table, Mai's foot rests lightly against hers.

Yuka finishes the last bite on her plate and goes to sit beside her husband.

He puts a hand behind her back before looking away from the drawing.

---

The afternoon refuses to end when the game does.

People go back through rooms they missed during the campaign. Wakana inspects the painted banner with Marin and discovers that the hamster's helmet was a later addition. Kita takes Bocchi to look at it from the gate, where it is easier to see than from beneath the table on which she drew it.

Ram and Seika try the bell lane together. Seika rings a bell, looks at Ram and claims she wanted to know whether the sound changes farther down. Ram does not believe her. She waits at the far end with the basket nevertheless.

Rimuru and Shizue follow the students into the Ink Wing. Kenya is certain he can make a faster boat. Gale wants one which carries something. Alice points out that the narrow turn has already defeated several people who knew more than either of them about folding paper.

They ask Coco for the successful pattern.

She shows them, staying until Ryota has the first crease the right way around. Shizue finds herself holding three unfinished boats while Rimuru tries to explain how to distribute the weight of a hypothetical passenger. She gives him one of the sheets.

“You can demonstrate.” ˢᶻ

He looks down at it, then at the little bridge.

“That may take a while.” ᴿ

“We have time.” ˢᶻ

He looks up. She smiles at him over the paper, and he begins folding.

---

Mau has been released from military service.

Kamish tells him so when the last official report fails to contain a single useful measure for recovering the treasury. Mau accepts the dismissal without contest, puts the ledger on the table and goes to see what Ori wants to show him next.

It turns out to be the suspicious-biscuit room.

She has learned where the moon drawer is. She wants him to find it without pointing directly, which proves difficult enough that her hints become steadily more precise.

“You're looking too high.” ᴼ

Mau lowers his gaze.

“Too low.” ᴼ

He adjusts it again.

“There.” ᴼ

He looks at the catch.

“A difficult case. I couldn't have done it alone.” ᴹ

She nods, entirely pleased with the conclusion, and lets him open the drawer himself.

Yuka watches from the door with Beru. He has the explorer hat tucked beneath one arm and a small collection of paper instructions in the new pocket. Ori has been giving him things to keep since the invasion began. He has kept all of them.

“A successful uniform,” Yuka tells him. ʸ

“Beyond expectation, my queen.” ᴮᵉ

Inside, Ori gives Mau one of the almond sweets. He splits it with her before she can begin demonstrating the other boxes.

---

They leave the castle standing for the last of the afternoon.

The soldiers remain in their play stations while people are still using them. When the rooms empty, Mau recalls the detail himself. The ordinary watch continues its work beyond the field, exactly where it belonged while the castle was being conquered.

Kamish rests in the courtyard until Ori comes to thank him.

She brings the folded map. Beru stands beside her in the matching jacket, and Chloe carries the paper boat in both hands so it will not lose its shape.

“You were a very good enemy,” Ori says. ᴼ

Kamish lowers his head.

“I trust the compliment is specific to the occasion.” ᴷ

Ori thinks about that.

“We can do something else next time.” ᴼ

“Then I shall require a different attaché.” ᴷ

Mau looks up from the last basket.

“I brought you water.” ᴹ

“Excellent water.” ᴷ

Yuka begins laughing. Ori joins her a moment later, discovering the joke in the way the dragon has said it. Kamish lets the noise continue around him, enormous and untroubled beneath the little banner hanging from the window.

---

Before they go in, Maki walks the emptied rooms once more. Mai collects the loose decorations. The work detail comes back at Mau's order, helping move the tables and gather the things which belong to the house rather than the castle.

Ori takes down the sign with the polite request at the gate. She gives it to Yuka to keep while Mau puts the marked sections back into storage. The doorway is the last thing left standing, once again leading from one ordinary stretch of grass to another.

He stores it too.

Beru carries the folded map home beside Ori. She lets him keep it until they reach the common-room table, provided he remembers which side has the tunnel.

By evening the common room has acquired most of the things worth preserving from the battle.

The banner hangs where Umaru can see it from the sofa. The tokens sit on the table beside the map. Ori has decided the paper boat belongs near the shell door, provided it is high enough that nobody mistakes it for something disposable.

Pillow is asleep by the hearth. She sits beside the enormous shaggy body for a while, telling it about the tunnel. The long nose moves when she reaches the part about Kamish looking the wrong way. This appears to be all the response she needs.

Nazuna comes in with Ko as people begin choosing what to watch. She takes in the map and asks whether the battle has ended without her. Umaru offers a comprehensive account. Rem brings Nazuna a portion of what they saved, and the account becomes shorter when she tries to speak around the biscuit she has taken for herself.

Mau sits on the long sofa beside Yuka. Ori comes to him after the story by the hearth and climbs into the space beneath his arm. He moves the cushion to make room.

She smells faintly of paper, apple filling and the grass outside the castle.

“Did you have fun?” she asks him. ᴼ

“Very much.” ᴹ

“Even though you lost?” ᴼ

“My attaché record may never recover.” ᴹ

She presses her face into his shirt, laughing. He holds her closer while she explains that Beru can help him practice for the next time, although Beru will still have to be on her side if they are wearing the outfits.

Yuka puts her hand over Mau's where it rests around Ori's shoulder.

Across the room, Frieren is talking to Himmel. She looks over when her daughter calls Mama, comes to hear the last part of the argument and sits close enough for Ori to keep one hand in her sleeve.

They choose Kiki's Delivery Service after Ori points at the broom on the cover. Momo Nishimiya is obliged to endure Mai looking at her when the flying begins.

“I'm not carrying your shopping all over the city,” she says. ᴹⁿ

“I was going to ask about the cat.” ᴹᵃ

“No, you weren't.” ᴹⁿ

Mai takes her hand beneath the edge of the blanket. Momo looks at their hands, then back at the screen, and lets the accusation remain unanswered.

The room settles around the film in fits and starts, interrupted by someone remembering where they left a cup and Milim announcing that the best part of the campaign was clearly the tunnel.

Mau is still smiling when Yuka lays her head against his shoulder.

She does not ask whether he wants to go somewhere quieter. He is already comfortable enough to let his weight rest against hers, with Ori warm beneath his other arm and the day's map open on the table in front of them.

He stays until the end.
