# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 19 — The First Throw

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S13E10
**Continuity state:** B+282 afternoon. Milim joins Mau in a sanctioned relief. Their first attempt to fight together fails. Mau uses Sky to misdirect a charged attack; Milim trusts the opening and throws Mau to separate the attacker from the column. They reveal no additional technique.

*When: Milim hears the part about going fast and is halfway down the bank before Mau has finished the part about where.*

Rimuru asks Milim for a turn on the far side because somebody is breaking the boards faster than Bellion can have them lifted. They have put the injured from the white helmets on the near side. Mau has been able to see that whole part of the road from where he is. He can see the man who is making it necessary to stand there, too. The man has a bright piece of something in his hands that goes dark each time another board goes down.

Milim gets there first. Of course she does. The man sees her come and turns all the way around. The rest of the people with him go the other way. Mau had meant to put himself between them and the road. Instead they come through the place he'd made for Milim.

He catches the first one by the back of the coat. The next two go round him.

“Milim,” he calls. ᴹ

She looks back from the top of the bank. The man with the light has stopped looking at her. He goes far enough down the other side that Mau loses him altogether.

“Which one?” Milim says. ᴹⁱ

Mau looks at the man whose coat he has. He lets the coat go as soon as he can see the shadows are there to keep him from going where the helmets are working. He could go after the other two. They don't have the light. There is a broken board on the road behind them, and one of the people carrying a wounded man has to put a foot on it to get around.

“The one who can do that again,” Mau says. ᴹ

Milim comes back down. She stops hard enough that he has to close his eyes against the dirt. When he opens them she's looking at the people he'd let through.

“I thought you were going to get ahead of me.” ᴹⁱ

“I thought you were going to wait.” ᴹ

She considers that for the time it takes him to see the light come up again. Then she asks him if he really meant it.

“No,” he says. “I was hoping you'd wait long enough for me to know where you were going.” ᴹ

She looks over her shoulder. “There.” ᴹⁱ

This time he sees which way she means before she goes.

She goes to the bank. Mau sees the next board lift and realises a man with a blue hand on his helmet is about to lose the only firm place to set a foot. He gets Authority under the board. The man stops, holding one end of a litter, and asks whether Mau has it. Mau says he has that end. There is a second where the board can only be in one place at once and he has to get it underneath both of the man's feet. Milim comes down hard on the other end. The board settles. She takes one look at Mau and lets him move his hand away.

“I was going to catch it,” he says. ᴹ

“You did,” she says. “I caught the other end.” ᴹⁱ

The man in the helmet gets across. Milim lifts her foot. The board comes loose in the water and goes off down the bank. She stays where he can see her. Mau points toward the slope where the man with the light went. This time Milim makes sure he's looking that way when she leaves.

* * *

The man on the bank has worked out that he can do more damage if he stands higher. The people below him can see which way he's looking and pull back before he does it. Mau can see how far the bright thing is likely to reach. He'd like him somewhere he can't see the line of helmets from.

The people trying to get to the bridge are watching him just as hard. When Milim goes up, most of them turn so they can see her. One of them keeps going toward the two shadows holding the rail. Mau sees what he's holding and starts to go after him. Milim gets between them before he takes a second step. The man sees her get there and throws. She takes the hit across one palm and closes it. In the quiet afterward the man can hear the two people carrying the litter tell each other to put it down and breathe.

Milim doesn't chase him. She looks back toward Mau, waiting to see which way he wants her to go. He can see the light up the bank even when it goes behind one of the trees. He says her name and looks to the right. She follows his eyes.

He has time to put a hand up and pull Sky across the man's view of Milim. She is coming straight down the side of the bank. From where the man stands, the sky bends low enough that she seems to come from the opposite side. He sends the light where he saw her last. It goes out over the water and lights the surface all the way to the far bend.

Milim comes through on the side he hasn't looked at yet. She gets a hand on the thing he's holding and knocks it free. He runs down the bank before it comes to rest. Mau sees Milim turn to go after him. He pulls the sky up again to show her the people still between her and the man. She comes to a stop beside Mau instead.

The man stops below the next lip of the bank. One of the people he'd been with holds up something that looks like the first light before it started. Milim leans forward. Mau has seen her take off from that angle. This time she puts a hand out in front of him to keep him beside her.

“Which one can hurt them now?” she asks. ᴹⁱ

Mau looks again. The man who's running has something in his left hand the other one keeps watching. They can take the second light and the first man can still get far enough down the road to do it all over again. He tells Milim to watch the one at the lip. She shouts for the helmets to stay where they are. They recognize her from the board. They stop.

“Could you get past him if you were already up there?” she asks. ᴹⁱ

Mau looks up. It's farther than he'd like to make the jump in one go. From the top he'd be able to get between the man and the people he's trying to reach. He can see Milim looking at his arm.

“How far are you thinking?” he asks. ᴹ

She points. It's exactly where he wants to go.

“Tell me when,” he says. ᴹ

She takes hold of him. He puts his feet together because he doesn't know which one she'd rather have in front. She waits just long enough to ask if he's ready. He says yes. The bank is suddenly below him. He puts Sky out just far enough to bend the last of the fall and gets his boots down where he can see the man come up.

From above he can see the second light go up. Milim gets under it with her back to the road. Mau can see her lift one hand to take it and then leave the other hand right where it is so she can get the second man if he tries to follow. For a breath there is light around both of her arms. He can still see the road behind her. The people in the helmets are where she asked them to be. One lifts a hand to show him.

The man has his hands open. He stops when Mau comes into view. On the other side, Milim is standing between him and the place he started. She puts the bright thing down on the ground and steps back from it.

“Would you like somebody to come get you?” Mau asks. ᴹ

The man looks down the road. Someone has just turned back for him. He asks if he can go as far as that person. Mau lets him. Milim comes up beside Mau after the two men are well past them. He thinks she's going to ask him whether he'd do it again. She looks at the place he landed first.

“Was I close?” she asks. ᴹⁱ

“Very.” ᴹ

She smiles so hard he has to look away. Then he has to put a hand on her shoulder, because the place she put him down is farther up the bank than the path and it turns out the way down is difficult on foot. Milim gives him her arm without saying anything about that part.

* * *

At supper Ori hears a version in which Mau gets all the way up the bank because Milim throws him, and is told by Milim herself that she asks his permission first. She gets to the end of it and asks what it felt like. Mau gets as far as telling her he could see the top of the trees before Milim reaches across him for the spoon and tells Ori which trees they were.

“You took the other light while I was up there,” Mau says. ᴹ

Milim stops moving the spoon.

“You saw that?” ᴹⁱ

“Yeah. You stayed on the ground after you threw me. I could see everybody behind you when I looked down.” ᴹ

She lays the spoon down exactly in the middle of the table. Ori takes it while she can. Milim looks at Mau for a little longer, then tells the Fort that part too, with both her feet planted this time.

Yuka sits down beside him. “How much of this were you going to tell me?” ʸ

“The trees were going to be in it.” ᴹ

She puts a hand on his back where Milim took hold of him. He leans into it. Milim has got up to show Ori how far she had to throw him and has to get up a second time because Chloe wants to see which hand she used.

“Does Shoko know?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“She already asked me to walk to her chair and back. Twice.” ᴹ

Yuka laughs. She moves her hand off his back long enough to pick up the bread. He thinks she's going to keep it. She gives him the whole piece and gets another one for herself.

For a while he can hear what Milim sounds like when there is nobody on the other side of the room she has to stop.
