# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 20 — Five Minutes

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S13E10
**Continuity state:** B+283 morning. The house changes its relief schedule after the enemy has watched it. A handoff is marked complete before its relief arrives. Yuta responds to a mistaken call and Yuka exhausts the technique she expects to use on the bridge.

*When: Fern can hear Yuta on two parts of the report. There should only be one.*

The plan for the morning has been on the table since before breakfast. Fern copies it from the one Bellion gave her. She leaves enough room after each place for the person who is there to say who is coming for them. The line by Yuta's name has five minutes on it because he needs to go back to Shoko after what he used before dawn. Gojo has time to get there first. He is coming by the near bank.

Yuta calls before Fern is done copying. The man with the blue hands has somebody who wants to be brought out from the far side. Yuta can reach them from where he is. He asks whether Gojo is coming down from the house.

“On his way,” Fern tells him. ᶠᵉ

She puts it down in the place beside his name. At the same time a shadow comes in with the spare blanket. Bellion points it towards the next door. A sentry from the bank signals that the route is open. Bellion reads the signal back aloud for Fern. The shadow recording the handoffs beside her hears *open*, sees the note beside Gojo's name and draws the little line that usually means the relief is in place.

Fern sees the line when she looks down again. She is still waiting for Gojo to call. Then somebody asks her which report she wants copied first. She gives him the one about the people on the far side. By the time she comes back to the line, the shadow has already sent the confirmation to Yuta.

Yuta leaves.

* * *

Yuka can see the corner of the near bank from where she's standing with Mahoraga. The first five minutes have been exactly what she'd expected. The next one brings somebody running up from the place where Yuta had been. He stops short when he sees the wheel above Mahoraga. He turns to tell somebody behind him where Yuka is.

She calls to Fern and gets two answers. Yuta is with the wounded man. Gojo is still on his way down. Neither one can see the other from where he is.

Yuka asks Mahoraga to hold the crossing. He gets far enough forward that the men on it have to stop and look. There is a second group where the path goes around the end of the rail. She had picked the place she is standing in so that she would be able to see that part. She can see it very well now. She can also see one of the people coming through is carrying the folded blanket from the bank.

“Is there somebody in it?” she asks. ʸ

The man shakes his head and lets the blanket go. Something bright comes out of the fold and hits the path behind her. It was meant to put her on the other side of the rail. She puts up the part of the technique she has left and gets it to go over instead. She can feel the instant it finishes. The shape she was holding is gone from her hand before she can ask it to come back.

Mahoraga can still move. He's standing across the people she'd asked him to keep from coming through. If she calls him back, she can see which of them will go first. She leaves him where he is.

* * *

Out by the road, Mau hears Yuta call twice. The first time he thinks Yuta is telling them he's found the injured man. The second time he hears him say he can't get back to the bridge before Gojo gets there. Mau asks Rimuru whether he can see the place Yuka is standing. Rimuru says he can see Mahoraga.

Mau can see her through the shadow at the edge of the path. She is holding her hand out. The technique she expects to get back isn't there. The man with the blanket has already put his other hand up.

He hears Yuka telling him, beside the yard door, what she wants him to do if he can see someone in danger and knows he can get there. There is nobody between him and the shadow to tell him he ought to wait. He tells Rimuru where he's going. He doesn't wait for the answer.

By the time the man throws the second light, he is looking at Mau.
