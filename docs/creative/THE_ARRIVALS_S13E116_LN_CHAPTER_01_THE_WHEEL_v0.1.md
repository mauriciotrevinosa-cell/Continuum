## Chapter 27 — The Wheel

*When: B+433. Late morning, inside the light. On the ridge, all anyone can see is a dome of white on the quarry floor.*

It's light.

All of it. Everything. The quarry is gone. The ridge, the sky, the broken floor. There's nothing but light, on every side, white and endless and pressing, like being inside the sun. And everywhere in it, in every direction, filling it the way water fills a jar: the wanting.

He feels it on every side.

It reaches for the tap from everywhere at once. Not a hand. A thousand hands. And the Infinity doesn't go thin on one side. It goes thin *everywhere*. All of it, flickering, on and off, on and off, the folded distance round him opening and closing like a lung.

And no shadows.

He notices it a breath later, and it's worse than the light. He looks down, and there's nothing under his feet. Not the floor; the floor is there, white. His *shadow*. There isn't one. Light is coming from every direction at once, from above and below and inside, and nothing in this world casts a shadow, because nothing in this world is allowed to.

*It's the rule*, says Ciel, very fast. *His domain. No shadows. Light from everywhere.* ᶜ

He reaches for the legion. For Beru, home in the yard. For the soldiers in a hundred people's shadows, the anchors, the doors he steps through.

Nothing. There's no shadow for them to be in.

*Everything you keep in the dark is cut off*, says Ciel. *For as long as we're in here.* ᶜ

And somewhere in the light, Dabura, everywhere, nowhere, very close.

“*Now*,” says Dabura, from every direction. “Now let's see what a king is.” ᴰᵇ

The light comes from everywhere.

He moves. He can't move enough. There's nowhere to move *to*. Rika flickers at his back and goes back into the katana like smoke going home; she was made in shadow, and there's none here to hold her. The first line goes through his left leg. The second through his back. The RCT is in after them, always on, filling, filling, but they're coming faster than it can fill, and the tap keeps closing, and the Infinity keeps going out—

*Body withdrawn*, says Noise. *Still too much.* ᴺ

He knows.

He can feel it. The whole world, raw, pouring in. Every grain of light. Every pressure of the wanting. Everything Maki's sense can feel and everything the eyes can see, and under it, coming up, everything else. The plate. The chair. The corridor. *I'll stop bothering you.*

He goes down on one knee in the light.

*Can I—*

Makima.

From the back of him. In the thread. The voice that always asks.

It stops. Starts again. Without the question.

*It's important*, says Makima. *I'm here. Inside.* ᴹᵏ

She isn't beside him.

He realises it all at once. She was at the edge of the quarry floor when the world went white, three steps behind him, in the body Noise holds for her. And she isn't there now. There's no one in the light but him and Dabura.

*Noise pulled me in*, says Makima. *Before it closed. The instant he started to open it.* ᴹᵏ

*Withdrew her*, says Noise. *Before closure. Unknown target rule.* ᴺ

*She is still bound to your life*, Ciel says. *Inside is not immunity. It removes the second body we would have to protect and maintain.* ᶜ

*The interruption is what finds him*, Makima says. *The light still follows it. Let me help reconnect the part it keeps tearing.* ᴹᵏ

“With me,” he says. He gives her the narrow working place they used in the gate, not authority over his choices. ᴹ

He feels her move inside him.

Not in the room with the table. Further in. To the place where the door was in the gate, where everything came through and she stood and held it. Only she isn't holding everything now. She's going to one place. His right side. Where the flow runs out of him into the Infinity, where it's thin, where Dabura keeps getting in.

*I'll hold this part*, she says. *It will still interrupt. I'll help you reconnect it sooner.* ᴹᵏ

He feels her do it.

Something steadies on his right. The tap closes, the thousand hands reaching for it from everywhere, and the right fails too, but reconnects before the next manually aimed line reaches it. Someone is sharing the work from inside. Something that controlled everyone in her world, holding one small thing open for one person, with everything she has.

The Infinity comes back on the right.

The right holds between interruptions. The rest is still flickering. It is a breathing space, not an answer to the whole domain.

And with her body gone back inside, Noise has a little more to spare.

*Got it*, says Noise. *I've got the flood. Some of it. Enough. Go.* ᴺ

He stands up.

In the middle of the light, with Makima inside him holding his right side open, and Noise holding back what it can of the flood, and Ciel counting, and the rest pouring in raw and loud and unbearable.

He stands up anyway.

*Purple won't do it*, says Ciel. *Not from in here. You'd need an edge to aim at, and he's everywhere. You can't outlast this. You can't outrun it.* ᶜ

“I know.” ᴹ

*You have to answer it*, says Ciel. *With your own.* ᶜ

His own.

He has built a territory since the days of the small empty barriers: reconstructed spatial geometry, his Monarch's shadow floor, paths for attacks that do not begin at his hands. A room in which he is strongest.

The room by itself has no sure hit. That does not make it unable to contest another territory. It gives him a place to start; it does not supply the operation that will win this one.

He remembers the diagrams Gojo separated. *Space. Environment. The operation attached to the barrier.* More power poured into an empty room would still leave it empty.

And he remembers the strip he made against Sukuna. Shrine inside the reconstructed boundary. A targeting rule that worked for one step before the two edges asked his brain for incompatible answers.

Before that, the training failure: an unintended third route, blood from his nose and ears, Yuta catching him. It had never been a completed three-domain victory. He has no successful old compression to fall back on.

What he has is every pattern they kept from the failure.

*Spatial boundary. Shrine targeting. Your shadow territory*, Ciel says. *Three functions to make agree. The guarantee comes from Shrine's rule, not the pressure.* ᶜ

“I know.” ᴹ

He is already refreshing his brain with RCT. Keeping it running does not make the reconstruction safe. It gives Ciel a chance to repair damage as it starts instead of reaching it after he has fallen.

*I drop the construction if you stop answering*, she says. *And we stop if repair falls behind. This is not a way to reset a burned-out technique forever.* ᶜ

“Agreed. Three breaths to try.” ᴹ

He quiets the copied light and closes the katana's remaining support route. Noise takes the freed attention, and Makima braces the flow he has actually given her to share.

“During,” he tells Ciel. “Don't wait until after.” ᴹ

*Ready.* ᶜ

He puts his hands together.

“*Domain Expansion*,” he says. ᴹ

The first one opens.

The Dark. The cosmic void the eyes taught him, distance itself folded open: night spreading out from his feet into the white, a stain of it in the middle of Dabura's sun. And all round him the shape of his own territory, pushing against Dabura's.

It isn't enough. The boundary contests a small circle. Within it the guaranteed interference loses its direct purchase for a moment, but Dabura's territory presses from every side. Mau has not yet given his room enough stability to widen.

He opens the second pattern: Shrine, bounded and aimed into the contested floor.

It hurts.

Not the body. The head. A second world opening inside the first, a second set of rules, and his brain trying to hold both at once, and something behind his eyes starting to burn.

He feels the RCT come in.

Not after. *During*. Exactly the way he asked. Ciel steering it, Noise holding the flood off it, the reverse technique pouring into the burning place as fast as it burns, building it back, cell by cell, in the same instant it's destroyed.

It holds.

The dark gets bigger. Two worlds, pushing harder against the white.

Not enough.

He raises his own shadow territory through both, the mana route that gives the floor depth and joins the two sets of coordinates.

Three worlds.

The pain climbs behind the damaged eye and reaches the old fracture's line through his head. Something inside his skull being burned out and rebuilt and burned out and rebuilt, faster than he can count, faster than he can put a name to each returning pain. The eyes, free, costing nothing, pouring everything in. Maki's sense pouring everything in. And three worlds of information on top of that.

He doesn't stop.

He compresses them.

He folds the three worlds into one.

He takes the cosmic dark and the shadow floor and the Shrine targeting rule, and crushes them together. Down. Smaller. The way you'd crush three handfuls of snow into one ball. The pressure builds. Three worlds in the space of one. Rules piling on rules. Territory folding over territory.

The Shrine route stops hunting for a separate floor. It accepts the coordinates held by the shadow territory inside the spatial boundary.

He gives it the narrow rule he once held for a single step. Its cuts acquire a defined target within that strip. His daggers and his other techniques do not become guaranteed attacks.

The sure hit locks.

He feels it lock. Like a key turning. Like the latch of the door in the gate.

The compressed dark, his, three domain patterns compressed into one usable territory, with the copied targeting rule finally seated, presses outward against Dabura's white.

And the white cracks.

Not everywhere. At the place the eyes marked when Dabura closed the barrier: the returning edge where the domain has to agree with itself. Mau seats the narrow targeting strip against that join. The first cuts arrive together instead of chasing a place that has moved.

The join buckles. Dabura tries to hold it, and Mau has to answer the correction rather than simply pour more power into the dark.

“With me,” he says again, through his teeth. ᴹ

Makima keeps the reconnection steady. Noise gives him the changing edge. Ciel counts the last breath he agreed to risk. He presses the strip into its new place before Dabura can settle it.

He hears Dabura make a sound.

From everywhere. From the light. A sound of pure astonishment.

The cracks run through the white the way cracks run through ice. Long and black and branching. Dabura's world, the one he's never opened before today, the one he knew how to make and didn't know where he learned, is coming apart under a dark that shouldn't be able to push it.

Mau's nose is bleeding.

Warm on his lip. The RCT is rebuilding his brain as fast as it burns, and it's keeping up, it's *keeping up*, but only just, and the blood runs down over his mouth and chin.

He pushes.

Dabura's domain breaks.

The quarry comes back.

All at once. The sky, the walls, the broken floor, the ridge. He's standing in the middle of the quarry floor with his hands still together, and as the white goes, he releases the active cuts but keeps the dark territory up for the few seconds he still needs. The effort of keeping it open shakes his hands. Makima stays inside while Noise holds the remaining shape together. His nose is still bleeding. The RCT is still rebuilding behind his eyes, quieter now.

Dabura is on his knees, thirty paces away.

He's staring at the air where his domain was. His face is completely blank.

“That's never happened,” he says. ᴰᵇ

Then he looks at Mau.

And he stands up. The light stutters where his collapsed domain has left its technique burned out; he has not learned a way to repair that on command. He comes in with his body anyway, fast and furious, rather than waiting for his technique to return.

Mau calls the wheel.

He doesn't decide to. He doesn't know where the thought comes from. Except he does. From the reflex Dabura used against the repeated cuts, the guard that answered a change Mau had not yet completed. Mau has seen that kind of answer beside his own wheel. He wants it available now; he does not know what seeing it will do to Dabura.

Mau calls it through the space he is still holding, with Shrine's active rule already quiet. His body takes the wheel's route and the narrow copied light he has prepared; no third innate attack is left firing through him.

It comes up over his head.

The wheel. Not the whole of Mahoraga; just the wheel, the great eight-spoked shape, the thing that turns once for every lesson. It rises over his head in the morning light, enormous, slow.

The visible ring moves once, taking its place above him. Noise has no completed adaptation to report.

The sound it makes is heavy. Like something enormous turning over in the earth.

Dabura stops.

His rush checks for a fraction. His feet touch down too early, his shoulders lift into a guard for an attack that has not come. His momentum is still carrying him forward. His body has answered a sound his mind cannot place.

He's staring at the wheel.

His face—

Mau sees it. With the eyes. With everything. Dabura's face isn't blank. It's a man waking up at the edge of sleep, with his hands up, hearing something heavy turn.

His body remembers.

His mind doesn't.

For one heartbeat, the king of the Simurians misses the step he meant to take, staring at a wheel he cannot place and already knows.

One heartbeat is enough.

Mau is there.

He doesn't remember crossing the distance. The new body crosses it. He's in front of Dabura, under him, and his left hand is on Dabura's chest and his right hand is up, and in it, very small, very bright, the point of light.

At Dabura's throat.

A hair from it.

They go down together.

Dabura onto his back, on the broken floor of the quarry. Mau on one knee over him, left hand flat on his chest, right hand at his throat with the light in it. Not touching. A hair away.

The wheel withdraws. He keeps the last narrow light steady and the territory just long enough to finish the decision.

The quarry is very quiet.

Dabura looks up at him.

He's breathing hard. His face is still doing the thing: waking, and not waking, the edge of a dream. Then it clears. And he looks at the light at his throat, a hair away, and at Mau's face above it.

And he laughs.

Not loud. Breathless. Delighted. The laugh of someone who has finally, after a very long time, lost.

“*Do it*,” says Dabura. ᴰᵇ

Mau doesn't move.

“Do it,” says Dabura again. “You won. That's how it ends. Go on.” ᴰᵇ

Mau looks down at him.

At the king of the Simurians, on his back, laughing, with the light at his throat. At the hands still half raised toward the wheel, as if they are waiting for something he cannot remember. At the RCT he doesn't remember learning, and the domain he'd never opened until today.

He thinks about the first time he stood in this quarry. In pieces. With his shadows holding him together.

He thinks about Sukuna.

He closes his hand.

The light goes out.

“No,” says Mau. ᴹ
