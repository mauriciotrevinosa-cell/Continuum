# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 83
## Chapter 25 — The Wheel

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 83
**Reading order:** 25
**Source episode:** S13E116
**Continuity state:** B+433, the climax. Mau answers with Dabura's own light, copied, and Dabura is overjoyed and furious at once. Pushed, Dabura opens the domain he has never opened: a world of light where the wanting is everywhere and the tap can close from every side. Makima helps without entering the domain: a domain is a sure hit that would break the body Noise holds for her and then go for her soul, so Noise pulls her body back inside Mau the instant it opens. From inside, she holds his thin right side open, the way she held the weight in the Rulers' gate, which also frees Noise to hold back part of the flood. Mau breaks the domain from inside with Purple at full. Then, as Dabura comes back at him, Mau calls the wheel up over his head. Dabura's body remembers what his mind doesn't: the great slow wheel he honed himself against for so long. He freezes for one heartbeat. Mau ends it with the light a hair from Dabura's throat. Dabura, on his back, laughs and says do it. Mau says no.

*When: B+433. Late morning. The sun is high over the quarry. On the ridge, Gojo is still standing.*

The light in his palm is very small.

It's the smallest thing he's ever made. Smaller than the first Blue, the one he threw in the clearing before he knew what it was. A point. A single point of pure bright pressure in the middle of his hand.

The eyes can barely look at it.

Across the quarry, Dabura has stopped smiling.

He's staring at Mau's hand. At the point of light. And his face is doing two things at once: something enormous and delighted, and something else, under it, that Mau has seen before on other faces, on Sukuna's, at the end.

Outrage.

“That's *mine*,” says Dabura. ᴰᵇ

“It was,” says Mau. ᴹ

* * *

He sends it.

He doesn't know how fast it goes. Noise built the sending; Noise knows. It goes the way Dabura's goes, faster than sound, faster than the eyes, and it's in the air between them and it's arriving, and Dabura—

Dabura *moves*.

He's fast. Faster than the light, almost. Not quite. It takes him in the left side, high, under the arm, and goes through, and comes out the back, and goes on into the quarry wall and through it and out the other side, and the stone where it passed stops being there.

Dabura looks down at the hole in his side.

It's closing. His RCT, the one he doesn't remember learning, is already in it, filling it.

He looks up at Mau.

“*Mine*,” he says again. ᴰᵇ

And then he laughs, and comes in, and the light comes out of him in every direction at once.

* * *

It's different now.

Before, it was a wall and a storm, and then a storm and a man dodging. Now it's two storms. Light against light. Every line Dabura sends, Mau sends one back. They cross in the air between them and the air screams. The quarry walls are carved in long smooth grooves from top to bottom. Pieces of the floor go up and don't come down because there's nothing left of them to come down.

And Mau moves.

The new body. Two thirds of everything he saved, in every bone. He moves in the instant the tap closes, every time, before the light can come through the thin place. He's never been this fast. Nobody he's ever fought has made him this fast.

Dabura is faster.

Just. Barely. A hair.

And Dabura is still learning.

* * *

*The interruptions are longer*, says Ciel. *He's holding the tap closed longer each time. In a minute he'll be able to hold it long enough to get through regardless of how fast you are.* ᶜ

“I know.” ᴹ

*You need to end it.* ᶜ

“*I know*.” ᴹ

He sends the light. Dabura isn't there. Dabura is behind him. He turns, and Dabura is above him, and the wanting is reaching for the tap from above and from the right at the same time—

And Dabura stops.

Twenty paces off. Breathing hard. For the first time all morning, breathing hard.

He looks at Mau.

He isn't laughing.

* * *

“I've never done this,” says Dabura. ᴰᵇ

His voice is quiet. Almost wondering.

“I know how,” he says. “I've always known how. I don't know where I learned it. I just know. I've never once needed to.” ᴰᵇ

He lifts his hands. He brings them together in front of his chest, slowly, the way you'd hold something very fragile.

Mau feels it before it happens.

The shape folded very small at the centre of Dabura. The one the eyes found at dawn. The world of his own he's never opened.

It's opening.

* * *

“Domain,” says Ciel. *He's—* ᶜ

The world goes white.

* * *

It's light.

All of it. Everything. The quarry is gone. The ridge, the sky, the broken floor. There's nothing but light, on every side, white and endless and pressing, like being inside the sun. And everywhere in it, in every direction, filling it the way water fills a jar: the wanting.

He feels it on every side.

It reaches for the tap from everywhere at once. Not a hand. A thousand hands. And the Infinity doesn't go thin on the right. It goes thin *everywhere*. All of it, flickering, on and off, on and off, the folded distance round him opening and closing like a lung.

And somewhere in the light, Dabura, everywhere, nowhere, very close.

“*Now*,” says Dabura, from every direction. “Now let's see what a king is.” ᴰᵇ

* * *

The light comes from everywhere.

He moves. He can't move enough. There's nowhere to move *to*. Every direction is light. The first line goes through his left leg. The second through his back. The RCT is in after them, always on, filling, filling, but they're coming faster than it can fill, and the tap keeps closing, and the Infinity keeps going out—

*Mau*, says Noise. *Mau, I can't hold everything. The domain's flooding the eyes, the sense is flooding, Mini Ciel is— I can't keep it down and build at the same time—* ᴺ

He knows.

He can feel it. The whole world, raw, pouring in. Every grain of light. Every pressure of the wanting. Everything Maki's sense can feel and everything the eyes can see, and under it, coming up, everything else. The plate. The chair. The corridor. *I'll stop bothering you.*

He goes down on one knee in the light.

* * *

*Can I—*

Makima.

From the back of him. In the thread. The voice that always asks.

It stops. Starts again. Without the question.

*It's important*, says Makima. *I'm here. Inside.* ᴹᵏ

* * *

She isn't beside him.

He realises it all at once. She was at the edge of the quarry floor when the world went white, three steps behind him, in the body Noise holds for her. And she isn't there now. There's no one in the light but him and Dabura.

*Noise pulled me in*, says Makima. *Before it closed. The instant he started to open it.* ᴹᵏ

*I don't know if his has a sure hit*, says Noise. Strained. Fast. *A first domain, never opened, might not. Yours didn't, until you made one by compressing several into one. But if his does, anything of ours standing out there in it gets hit. Her body would break. And under the body, the thing it would go for next is her. I wasn't going to find out on her.* ᴺ

Her. Not a body Noise can rebuild. The part of her that's bound to him, that gave up coming back for it.

*So I'm in here*, says Makima. *Where the domain can't reach anything but you. It's better. Noise doesn't have to hold a body for me now. And I don't need hands for this.* ᴹᵏ

* * *

He feels her move inside him.

Not in the room with the table. Further in. To the place where the door was in the gate, where everything came through and she stood and held it. Only she isn't holding everything now. She's going to one place. His right side. Where the flow runs out of him into the Infinity, where it's thin, where Dabura keeps getting in.

*I'll hold it*, she says. *The flow. The tap. On this side. From in here. Whatever he closes from out there, I'll hold it open from in here. Like the weight, in the gate. You do the rest.* ᴹᵏ

* * *

He feels her do it.

Something steadies on his right. The tap closes, the thousand hands reaching for it from everywhere, and on the right side it doesn't close. Something is holding it. From the inside. Something that controlled everyone in her world, holding one small thing open for one person, with everything she has.

The Infinity comes back on the right.

Only the right. The rest still flickering. But the right holds.

And with her body gone back inside, Noise has a little more to spare.

*Got it*, says Noise. *I've got the flood. Some of it. Enough. Go.* ᴺ

* * *

He stands up.

In the middle of the light, with Makima inside him holding his right side open, and Noise holding back what it can of the flood, and Ciel counting, and the rest pouring in raw and loud and unbearable.

He stands up anyway.

He brings his hands up.

Blue in the left. Not small. Not careful. The size Blue is when there's nothing holding it back.

Red in the right.

He's made Purple at full once before. At the bottom of a gate built to kill his wife, with Makima holding the weight then too.

He brings his hands together.

* * *

It doesn't make a sound.

It goes out of him in a line, the colour that isn't a colour, through the white, through the light, through the wanting. Not at Dabura. Dabura is everywhere; there's nothing to aim at. At the *edge*. At the place where the domain stops and the world starts, the place the eyes can just about see, a seam in the light.

It goes through the seam.

The domain breaks.

* * *

The quarry comes back.

All at once. The sky, the walls, the broken floor, the ridge. He's standing in the middle of the quarry floor with his hands still together, and as the light goes, Noise lets Makima back out, at his right shoulder, a hand on it, and the line of Purple still going, up and out, over the rim of the quarry, into the sky, gone.

Dabura is on his knees, thirty paces away.

He's staring at the sky where the Purple went. Where his domain was. His face is completely blank.

“That's never happened,” he says. ᴰᵇ

Then he looks at Mau.

And he stands up, and the light comes up in him one more time, all of it, everything left, and he comes in.

* * *

Mau calls the wheel.

He doesn't decide to. He doesn't know where the thought comes from. Except he does. From dawn. From the eyes going into Dabura and finding, all through him, in the way his weight sits and his shoulders turn, the shadow of something that was always just a little stronger, always changing to meet him. The shadow of a great slow wheel.

*He doesn't remember*, Noise said.

Mau calls it.

* * *

It comes up over his head.

The wheel. Not the whole of Mahoraga; just the wheel, the great eight-spoked shape, the thing that turns once for every lesson. It rises over his head in the morning light, enormous, slow.

It turns once.

The sound it makes is heavy. Like something enormous turning over in the earth.

* * *

Dabura stops.

In the middle of the air, coming in at Mau faster than sound, he *stops*. As if he's hit a wall. As if every part of him, all at once, has heard something it knows.

He's staring at the wheel.

His face—

Mau sees it. With the eyes. With everything. Dabura's face isn't blank. It's a man waking up at the edge of sleep, with his hands up, hearing something heavy turn.

His body remembers.

His mind doesn't.

For one heartbeat, the king of the Simurians hangs in the air in the middle of the quarry, frozen, staring at a wheel he has never seen and has always known.

One heartbeat is enough.

* * *

Mau is there.

He doesn't remember crossing the distance. The new body crosses it. He's in front of Dabura, under him, and his left hand is on Dabura's chest and his right hand is up, and in it, very small, very bright, the point of light.

At Dabura's throat.

A hair from it.

* * *

They go down together.

Dabura onto his back, on the broken floor of the quarry. Mau on one knee over him, left hand flat on his chest, right hand at his throat with the light in it. Not touching. A hair away.

The wheel turns once more over Mau's head, slowly. Then it goes, the way smoke goes.

The quarry is very quiet.

* * *

Dabura looks up at him.

He's breathing hard. His face is still doing the thing: waking, and not waking, the edge of a dream. Then it clears. And he looks at the light at his throat, a hair away, and at Mau's face above it.

And he laughs.

Not loud. Breathless. Delighted. The laugh of someone who has finally, after a very long time, lost.

“*Do it*,” says Dabura. ᴰᵇ

Mau doesn't move.

“Do it,” says Dabura again. “You won. That's how it ends. Go on.” ᴰᵇ

* * *

Mau looks down at him.

At the king of the Simurians, on his back, laughing, with the light at his throat. At the shadow of the wheel in his body, that he doesn't remember. At the RCT he doesn't remember learning, and the domain he'd never opened until today.

He thinks about the first time he stood in this quarry. In pieces. With his shadows holding him together.

He thinks about Sukuna.

He closes his hand.

The light goes out.

“No,” says Mau. ᴹ
