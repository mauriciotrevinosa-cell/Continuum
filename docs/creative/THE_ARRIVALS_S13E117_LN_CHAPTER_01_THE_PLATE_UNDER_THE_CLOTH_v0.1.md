# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 83
## Chapter 31 — The Plate Under the Cloth

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 83
**Reading order:** 31
**Source episode:** S13E117
**Continuity state:** B+433, Yuka viewpoint. Cross talks her out of going to the quarry after she has put her boots on. Ciel relays what she can about the duel. Ori tells Yuka she saw Mau waiting at the steps and reminds her what she promised. Shuna tells Yuka what she saw on the night of the walk and helps her set aside something fresh for Mau. Yuka offers the plate and says she had her boots on. Mau asks her to thank Shuna and takes Makima's hand when he needs to stay seated. Yuka finally says she loves him back. Mau believes the meal is a kindness she brought for Shuna and thinks of leaving. He asks Yuta to walk with him to Shoko. Ori invites him to her room later that night.

*When: B+433. While Mau is on his way to the quarry, through the first evening after the duel.*

Her second boot is on when Cross comes to the back door.

She has to put her heel down hard to get it in. He's got his coat on already. She'd thought he might want to see what Dabura could do, so she'd left the latch up for him.

“Did he ask you to come?” Cross says. ᶜʳ

“He asked yesterday. He drew me a map.” She gets the lace through the last eyelet. The map is folded on the sill, under the glove so it won't go out when the door opens. “I want to see him when he looks up.” ʸ

Cross glances at the underside of the glove. He doesn't touch it.

“Did you get to talk about anything else when he brought it?” ᶜʳ

“He asked if I'd come even if we're still angry.” ʸ

The words sound kinder now than they did yesterday, when she had the chalk in one hand and could still see where he'd rubbed an extra line out of the map. She tightens her lace.

“Then he did ask you,” says Cross. He puts his shoulder to the frame and watches her go to finish the bow. “What did he say he'd do if you told him no?” ᶜʳ

“Nothing. He'd go fight Dabura. He's been getting ready for days.” ʸ

“Would he come back here?” ᶜʳ

“Of course he'd come back here.” ʸ

“To the room?” he asks. He looks at the map, and she has to remember which room they're talking about before she can answer.

Yesterday there'd been time to tell Mau what she meant in the yard. They'd got as far as him asking her to come. She goes on thinking about the question Cross gave her until she's remembering the two shirts again, and that tiny pause before Mau said he could give her the room.

“He was worried about me getting there,” she says. “He drew the way around where the rock's broken.” ʸ

“I think he's worried about a lot of things,” says Cross. “If you say yes to this one, he'll have one less thing to ask you.” ᶜʳ

There's enough room to pass him. She gets the lace round her finger and leaves it there, half tied.

“I heard you in the yard,” he says. “You asked him to tell you what he wanted. He went in a gate without telling you and shut off the one thing that lets you find him. We were standing in the grass and you still wanted to go after him. You told me the only reason you could get to your feet after the other gate was you knew he'd already come for you.” ᶜʳ

“I knew he'd *try*,” she says. ʸ

Cross nods. “If you go up there today, you'll spend the whole fight wondering whether he's going to look. And then what are you supposed to do if he doesn't?” ᶜʳ

“Look anyway.” ʸ

She means it when she says it. She pictures the face she would look down at across the quarry, and just as clearly she pictures him walking out of the house with Makima at his shoulder. She would have to stand on the spot he'd marked himself and find out whether he could look up at her with Makima already beside him.

“Do you remember what he said when you walked away?” says Cross. ᶜʳ

Her fingers go still. “He told me he loved me.” ʸ

“Did it make you want to stay?” ᶜʳ

Yes. She can feel precisely how far she'd turned before she decided she would say she knew, instead of what he wanted to hear. She's still waiting to feel as sure about that decision as she did when she made it.

“If you go now,” says Cross, “everyone gets to see you in the place he left for you. He can tell himself it worked. You still haven't heard him say what changes when he gets home.” ᶜʳ

“He could ask me there,” she says. ʸ

“He could come ask you here.” ᶜʳ

Cross puts the glove farther from the wind when it starts to lift, with the map still under it. He's left the path clear. She can see as far as the first turn, where the morning light goes from one side of the stones to the other. He asked her himself. She'd told Cross that as soon as he opened his mouth. Now she's trying to remember why it felt like she'd have to ask first.

Yuka pulls on the lace. With a little more force she could finish tying it.

She loosens it instead.

* * *

Shuna puts her in charge of peeling apples.

There's a knife with a small handle that always turns in your palm at the wrong angle. Shuna has another one and offers it twice. Yuka keeps the first one. It gives her something to do with her left hand.

From the kitchen she can hear Shizue taking names for a game outside. There is a brief dispute about whether Milim qualifies as a whole team even if nobody joins her. Someone asks Rimuru what the rule is. Rimuru says it depends which team has asked him.

Yuka takes the skin off the last apple in one length. She winds it round her finger and almost says Mau's name before she sees how quiet his end of the kitchen is. She'd have done it where he could see her and waited for him to pretend to be impressed.

*Ciel*, she asks. *Can you tell me how it's going? You don't have to ask him to talk to me.* ʸ

There's a pause. She waits, still looking at the apple peel.

*He's fighting. His right side is slower than it should be.* Ciel sounds as if she's also talking to someone else. *He can stand. He knows where he is.* ᶜ

*What happened that you have to tell me that?* ʸ

*He took a hit. It closed. I'll tell you if that changes.* ᶜ

Then Ciel goes quiet. She picks up the knife and starts halving the apples. She wishes she could go back to the boot. She can feel the lace rough round her finger, down to the little hard nub at the end. If she ran now she'd get there after the part she was afraid to see.

Ori comes for an apple piece. She takes one, changes her mind and takes two.

“Did you see him leave?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Dad came to say goodbye to me.” ᴼ

Yuka sets the knife down.

“I was sitting on the steps,” Ori goes on. “He looked up at the door when he got to the end of the path. I thought you were coming out with your boots on.” ᴼ

“I was going to.” ʸ

Ori looks down at her two bits of apple. “You promised.” ᴼ

“He needed to be able to think about the fight. We'd said a lot of things in the yard.” ʸ

Ori eats one of the apple pieces. It takes her a while. “I thought maybe he'd like to see you anyway,” she says. ᴼ

She gives Yuka the second piece and goes back outside. Yuka wants to go after her. She hasn't got a better answer to offer if Ori asks her the same thing again.

By the time she takes the apple outside, Ori is sorting teams with Shizue. Two people put their hands up for Milim's team at once and Ori sends one of them over to the other side. Yuka watches from the step for a while. She eats the piece Ori gave her.

* * *

When the domain goes up, she knows because Ciel answers too fast.

*The floor's gone. Noise is trying to hold the input. Mau's on one knee.* ᶜ

“What can I do?” Yuka asks. She's said it out loud. Only Shuna is there to hear it. ʸ

*Give me a moment.* ᶜ

Yuka grips the edge of the counter. The apple peel is in the bowl with the cores. The water by the stove has just boiled. Ciel stays quiet for long enough that she works out what she'll say if the next thing Ciel tells her is to find Shoko.

*Makima has the right side*, Ciel says. *From inside. Hold on.* ᶜ

Yuka holds on to the counter. She tries to remember what Mau had said about getting enough of the lost flow back to make a difference. She can't. He'd been talking to Gojo at dinner and she'd been close enough to put her hand on his knee. She remembers that she had wanted him to turn toward her. She can't remember what he said.

A little later Ciel tells her both men are alive.

“Both?” Yuka asks, because she needs to hear the word again. ʸ

*Both. Mau's up. He burned more than I wanted him to while we were closing it. I'll ask Shoko to look at him when we get back.* ᶜ

Yuka sits on the stool behind her. She had wanted Mau to ask her to come. She could have spent the morning asking whether he could get up, where he was bleeding, what she could take off his hands. She could have asked from the seat he saved.

For a moment she is angry enough to get up and find Cross. He'd say the same thing he said at the door. She knows that because there's a part of her that would like him to say it again, now that Mau is alive and she can afford to be angry.

Instead she asks Shuna when they should get a plate ready.

* * *

Yuka finds Cross by the pump, washing a little mud off his hands. He looks up when the handle stops squeaking.

“It's over,” she says. “They're both alive.” ʸ

He leans the handle all the way down with his forearm. “Good.” ᶜʳ

She can't tell why it takes her a second to speak. Maybe she was waiting for him to ask who won. She didn't think to ask Ciel herself.

“I wish I'd gone,” she says. ʸ

Cross straightens up. He has water dripping off one cuff. “Next time he'll know to ask you.” ᶜʳ

“He already knew he was saving me a place.” ʸ

There is a short silence. He has heard her say it twice now. The second time she can hear herself, too.

“He still went with the other one,” he says. “Makima. Twice now, if she was with him at the quarry.” ᶜʳ

“She was,” Yuka says. ʸ

She can still feel her fingers gripping the counter while Ciel told her someone inside the domain had a hold on him. Standing right here in the yard, she can think of half a dozen things she'd have given in that moment for a person to be close enough to reach him. She'd have meant it, too. It comes as a shock to find the thought sitting right alongside how angry she still is that he'd gone.

Cross lifts the handle for her. She hadn't come to wash her hands but she puts them under the water anyway. Her fingers are tacky from the apples. He pumps till she says she's done.

“Thanks,” she says. After a moment she adds, “I've got to go help Shuna.” ʸ

He steps out of the way. This time she goes past.

* * *

They start with bread and fish, because Mau can eat that straight away if he wants and it will keep if Shoko makes him sit with her first.

“Where was I sitting the night I came back from the ridge?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Other end of the table.” Shuna is cutting paper to go under the fish. “I could hear you from the stove.” ˢʰᵘ

“Mai said his plate was still there when he left.” ʸ

“It was.”

Shuna waits. Yuka looks down at the clean plate. There is a nick on the edge where it goes a little darker under the glaze. She can remember using the nick to turn the plate when Mau was keeping it out of her reach.

“Did he eat afterward?” she asks. ʸ

“I put it away that night. I emptied it in the morning.” Shuna folds the paper once more before she puts it down. “He'd saved you the pickles. He moved them to the part of the plate you'd have been able to reach.” ˢʰᵘ

Yuka could repeat the joke to Shuna. She'd hear it now the way Mau must have heard it that night. She takes the small plate and gets a cloth to cover it.

“Will you let me take him this?” she asks. ʸ

“Of course.” ˢʰᵘ

When Mau comes through the back door he holds it for Makima. She goes partway in and says something low to him, which makes him put both feet on the floor and stand there a moment longer than he meant to. His shirt has two holes she can see from here. A third one opens under his arm when he reaches for the door handle.

Yuka steps forward with the plate.

“Are you hurt?” she asks. ʸ

“My head hurts,” he says. ᴹ

She'd been prepared for him to say fine. She holds the plate out closer. He looks for somewhere she can put it down. Makima moves a chair out and he sits. Yuka sets the plate in front of him with the edge of the cloth folded up the way he likes to find it when his fingers are cold.

“I got as far as putting my boots on,” she says. ʸ

His eyes move from the cloth to her. For a second she thinks he's going to ask where she'd got the idea she ought to stay home. There are things she has ready in case he does. The Gate. The line. How she'd thought he'd have enough people there. He waits a little while, looking at her as if there's a sentence he wants to let her finish even if he's heard it before.

“Tell Shuna thank you,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka sets both hands on the table. She can see which ring he looked for. He moves his eyes away first. On the back of the chair there's a place where he'd set one hand to hold himself there before he sat down. He's feeling for it again.

“I asked her if I could bring it,” Yuka says. ʸ

He moves the plate toward the middle of the table to give her room to put something else down. There isn't anything else in her hands.

“I'll have some in a minute,” he says. ᴹ

Makima is still standing at the side of his chair. He lifts the hand he was going to put on the chair back and reaches for hers. Makima turns her wrist so he won't have to reach quite as far. He puts his fingers between hers. There's some of the quarry dust in the heel of that hand. Yuka can see it come away against Makima's thumb.

She'd wanted to see him find her when he looked up. By the time he did look up, she'd left him with a place and no one in it. Now she's come down to ask after him where everybody can see her, and the person he wants to hold on to is the one who went with him.

She doesn't know what he got to see before he had to look away from the ledge. She remembers how carefully he erased a line on her map so she'd know exactly which side to take. He did it the day after he watched her take the band off. There's a little pencil mark left where he went over the spot twice.

Yuta comes in at the other door looking for Shoko. He stays there when he sees Mau. Yuka has one hand still on the cloth over the plate. She lets it go.

“I'll leave you to eat,” she says. ʸ

He's got his eyes on her face. She knows how fast he'd said it in the yard, before she had time to take more than a single step. She goes as far as the door on her own.

“I love you, too,” she says. ʸ

The last word comes out while she's looking back at him. He gives her a small nod. He has her whole face in front of him and he's still holding Makima's hand when she lets go of the door.

Upstairs she opens the curtains on his side of the room. She takes the band out of her pocket. She could have worn it down to dinner. The idea had been there while she was getting his plate and she put it off because she'd wanted him to see she could stand in front of him without it. She hadn't expected him to look away so quickly. She hadn't expected to want him to look back just as badly as she wanted him to ask.

She sits on the edge of the bed with it closed in her palm. If she puts it back now, he'll see it when he comes up. She tries to work out what she'd be trying to tell him. In the yard she didn't say it. She made him watch.

* * *

Downstairs Mau looks at the plate. Shuna always covers it this way if she's leaving it for him. He wonders if Yuka had already offered before Shuna asked. He can believe she would. She'd do something kind for him if she thought he was hurt.

He looked up enough times at the quarry to know she'd never come while he'd had his head down. She knew the way up. He'd drawn it for her. She'd stood in the yard and wondered out loud if she'd made a mistake marrying him. The next morning he'd seen the ring between her finger and thumb. Until he looked up for the last time at the quarry, he'd still thought she'd come. Now he tries to put *I love you, too* beside that and finds he's only given himself another thing he can't ask her to explain.

In the small hollow behind his thumb there is still the warmth of Makima's hand. He hasn't let go of it. He starts to say that he could go somewhere else for the night. If he tells her now, she could help him get out before Yuka needs to come back through and say goodnight. He has enough in inventory to stay somewhere well away from the house. He thinks of finding a room near enough that Ori could come by without anybody needing to tell her what happened.

“Do you want me to stay?” Makima asks. ᴹᵏ

He looks down at their hands. His grip has gone tight again. He loosens it before he answers.

“For a minute,” he says. ᴹ

She sits in the empty chair. He lifts the cloth with his free hand. It would have been easier if Shuna had left the plate on the counter. He could eat it without trying to make it mean Yuka wanted to see him.

He puts the cloth down far enough away that it can't fall back on the food.

“Yuta,” he says. The first part of Shoko's name goes out on a breath too thin to put the rest on it. He has to try again. ᴹ

“I'll go get her,” says Yuta. He gets the door and stands with his hand on it. “Or I can walk you over.” ʸᵗ

Mau keeps thinking of the way out of the yard by the training posts. There is a whole house beyond it he doesn't have an answer for. Right now he can tell Yuta he needs help getting down one step.

“Walk with me,” he says. ᴹ

He finishes the fish. Makima waits beside him until he puts his own hand flat on the table to get up.

Much later Ori finds him on the landing. She asks him if he wants to sleep in her room. He asks if she has her pillow ready. She says she has two.
