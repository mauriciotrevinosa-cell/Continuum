# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 21 — What They Marked Done

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S13E11
**Continuity state:** B+283 morning and night. Mau intervenes by Exchange while Yuka’s technique is exhausted; Gojo takes the actual relief. At supper the house traces the mistaken handoff and changes its confirmation. No deaths.

*When: Mau hears the sound behind him first. It's Yuka telling Mahoraga to stay where he is.*

The second light goes up. Mau gets Sky under it and bends it away from the rail. It leaves a white line above them, high enough that a man on the bridge turns his head to see it. Mahoraga has one foot down in front of the man's path. He leaves it there when the man looks back.

Yuka takes a breath behind Mau. He turns enough to see her hand. She's still holding it as though there's something in it. She closes it when she sees him look.

“Can you get him away from the blanket?” she asks. ʸ

Mau looks down. The man has tried to get one foot on the loose end of it. He wants something that's still in the fold. Mau pulls the blanket towards him with Authority. It comes free. Two more pieces of the bright thing fall out. Yuka gets a boot over one and tells the man to step back from the other.

He steps back far enough to meet Gojo.

“Is this where I'm meant to be?” Gojo asks. ᴳ

“Now it is,” Yuka says. ʸ

She tells him what Mahoraga is holding and where the people on the path have stopped. He looks once and says he's got them. Mau waits for him to say it where Rimuru can hear before he takes a breath to ask Yuka how she's doing.

“I thought I'd have enough for two,” she tells him. ʸ

He takes her hand. It closes on his a little sooner than he expects.

“Do you want to walk with me?” he asks. ᴹ

She looks at Mahoraga. She calls him back when Gojo gives her room to do it. He comes far enough to see which way she's going and keeps to that side of her until they're both past the bend.

At the corner they meet Yuta coming back. The man he went for is still being carried. He puts his hand on the rail and looks at where the light went.

“I'm sorry,” he says. ʸᵗ

Yuka keeps walking until she's close enough for him to hear her without the people on the bridge hearing too.

“Come in with us,” she tells him. ʸ

He looks back towards Gojo. Gojo lifts a hand from the other side of the rail. Yuta comes with them.

* * *

At supper, there is a mark through the mistake on Fern's first copy. She puts the copy where all three of them can see it. The little line is still there under the mark. She calls over the shadow who drew it. It stands at the table and looks to Mau. He motions to the place by Fern where it can see the page. It comes round to stand beside her.

Fern asks which report it was working from. The shadow gives the same sign the sentry gave for an open route. Then it taps Gojo's name and the little line beside it. Bellion looks at Fern. She was there when he read the signal out. Both of them heard what the sentry actually reported.

Fern shows them what she wrote. *On his way.* Yuta puts his spoon down when he sees it.

“I heard it was done,” he says. “I wanted to get the man out before his people had to carry him any farther.” ʸᵗ

“Did you hear Gojo?” Maki asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

“No.” ʸᵗ

Maki looks at Fern. Fern leaves the old page on the table and gets the new one. This time there is a place to write who said they had arrived and a different place for somebody saying the road is clear. Yuta asks if they can have the person who's leaving hear it from the person who's there. Mau tells Bellion to make it an order for every shadow handling the reliefs. The soldier who drew the line puts a finger on each of the two places to make sure it has them right. Bellion has it write the first copy for Muster's next rotation.

Yuka tells them how much she thought she had left to use. Shoko asks whether she knew it was gone before the second light came. Yuka says she knew when she reached for it. She can still feel what it's like to make a fist around something that's already stopped being there.

“What did you want us to know?” Shoko asks. ˢʰ

“That Mahoraga could still hold the bridge. And that I needed somebody with me.” ʸ

Mau is listening so hard he doesn't notice that Yuka has put a hand beside his until she moves it nearer. He takes it.

“I knew I could get there,” he says. “I didn't know if I'd got there early enough until I heard you.” ᴹ

She leaves her fingers in his. “I'm glad you went.” ʸ

He thinks of the place where he saw her. He has seen it several times since they sat down, even when he was looking at something else. Yuka turns her hand over against his. He tells her exactly which part he keeps seeing. She waits until he's finished, and then she tells him where she was looking when he came through.

Across the table Yuta has picked his spoon back up. He looks over and asks if they would like to try the new call with him tomorrow before anybody has to use it. Yuka says yes. Mau says he'd like to be the one who hears it from the other end.

Rem brings the bread. There are enough pieces left to have another, and Yuta takes one for himself before he passes it down.
