# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 22 — The Name He Gives Her

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 22
**Source episode:** S13E11
**Continuity state:** B+284 midday. Two soldiers independently describe coercion and identify the officials behind the orders. Fern separates witness consent from medical care. The house chooses transfer to the assembly rather than sentencing its own captives.

*When: The second soldier brings his own page because he wants to know where the first one went.*

Fern sees the first man's brother again in the morning. He has a scarf in his hand and asks whether he can give it back to the man who brought him in. The man in the white helmet is outside putting something dry over the same bag he had at the road. He takes it, asks whether the injured man's foot is still warm, and goes in to look when the brother says it is.

The second soldier is waiting on the other side of the door. Fern knows him by his coat. She saw him wearing it while he was trying to carry somebody on the bridge yesterday. He has a folded page tucked inside it and one sleeve is still wet near the cuff.

“I can show you what I was told,” he says. “I'd like to know who'll see it.” ᴵⁿ²

Fern finds him a place to sit. She tells him what she knows so far. She can take it down for the house and use it to ask somebody else what they were told. If he wants it heard at the hall, she'll have to ask what he wants the clerk to put next to it. She can also just tell Shoko that his sleeve is wet and ask if he needs to get it off.

He looks down at the sleeve. He tells her he'd like to keep the coat on while he talks. She waits until he gives her the page.

The date is the one the woman at the tollhouse gave her. The place on it is farther north than the road she was told to look at first. The name at the bottom isn't the name of the clerk. Fern reads it twice before she asks whether he can tell her who gave it to him.

“He gave it to the man who reads for us,” the soldier says. “We'd asked what happened to the ones who didn't come back after the first night. That's when he read us the part about our families.” ᴵⁿ²

“Did he read from this?” ᶠᵉ

“He had another one with the same name.” ᴵⁿ²

Fern looks at the page again. It has been folded down the middle and unfolded often enough that the name is split by the crease. She asks if the soldier would let her copy the words before she gives him the page back. He nods. She writes until he can see she has got to the place where the name is. Then she asks him to say it out loud.

He says it. He puts a finger on it when she gives the page back. There is a second name he heard and can't spell. Fern writes it the way it sounds and leaves room to correct it if she finds somebody who knows.

* * *

By midday she has three pieces of evidence that can go on the same table. The first injured man named the place where the orders were collected. The second soldier brought the copy and described who delivered it. Testarossa has found a place in the parliamentary record where one of the men asked to be allowed to issue something in the assembly's name. Elmesia's copy records the clerk asking who had authorised it. There is an answer from nobody.

Mau comes in after Shoko has made him sit for a while. He has not come straight from the road. Fern can tell because he has a cup in his hand and is drinking from it while she gets the papers out.

“Is that enough to get them to stop?” he asks. ᴹ

“Enough to know who to ask first. There's somebody on that road who can tell us where they sent the other orders from.” ᶠᵉ

Makima comes up to the table with Bellion. She has heard the second name in another place. She says it before Fern shows her the page. Then she asks whether they have any reason to think the second man is going to stay where he was when she heard him.

They don't. Rimuru asks Bellion to find out if the group can hear its commanders from where they are. Bellion sends an officer from the legion to get the scouts' report while they still have all the pages on the table.

Mau wants to go for both names. He tells them that. Fern asks what he intends to do when he has them. He remembers Maki asking him that at breakfast four days ago. This time he has heard what the men who were given the orders want, and has a place he thinks they can go.

“The hall,” he says. “The people there heard somebody ask to do this. Let them hear who went and did it.” ᴹ

Testarossa can ask for the hall to receive them. Elmesia and Luminous can say whether their people will be there. Rimuru asks Fern to send the question now, while they can still have a place ready before anybody is brought to it.

Mau looks down at the sheet the injured man asked for. It's by itself.

“Does he want this read there?” he asks. ᴹ

“That's what I was about to go and ask him.” ᶠᵉ

She takes it with her. By the door, the second soldier is still waiting. She asks whether he'd like to come with her to see the man in the white helmet. He does. He puts his own page away before he gets up. Fern leaves the copy on the table for Mau, where he can read it without having to ask the man for anything else.
