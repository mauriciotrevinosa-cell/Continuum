He doesn't lift the cloth. He isn't hungry. He's tired in the good way, the way you're tired after a long day of walking somewhere you wanted to go.

He thinks about going upstairs.

The stairs are right there. Their room is at the top. The notebook was on his pillow there, a week ago, with *53* in her handwriting. She's not there. She's been in Ori's room since the night he didn't come up.

He goes out across the yard to the clinic instead, the way he did last night, because he asked Shoko for the cot and she said *all right* and didn't ask why, and because he doesn't know yet how to climb those stairs.

He pushes it open.
