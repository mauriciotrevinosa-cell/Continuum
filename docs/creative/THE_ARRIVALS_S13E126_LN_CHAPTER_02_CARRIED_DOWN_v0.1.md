# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 84
## Chapter 16 — Carried Down

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 84
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S13E126
**Continuity state:** B+440 ("47"), thirty minutes later. Mau goes up. Yuka is in bed with her eyes shut very hard, pretending to be asleep, and failing because she can't stop smiling. He carries her down the stairs the way he said he would. She opens her eyes in the kitchen to everything on the table and the whole house pretending not to watch. Clingy Yuka, reloaded: she won't let go of him, sits in his lap through breakfast, feeds him, and steals from every plate in reach. She kisses him through breakfast, and he gives as good as he gets. She writes "47" in the notebook in whipped cream and then in pencil. The house eats the rejects. Nothing about the last two weeks is said; it doesn't need to be today.

*When: B+440. Twenty-nine minutes after "I'm waking up in thirty". Umaru counted.*

She's pretending to be asleep.

He knows it the moment he opens the door. She's lying on her back in the middle of their bed with the blanket pulled up to her chin and her eyes shut so hard her whole face is scrunched round them, and she's breathing very slowly and very deeply and very loudly, the way Ori breathes when she's pretending.

And the corners of her mouth won't stay down.

He watches them try. He stands in the doorway and watches her mouth try to be asleep, and fail, and try again, and fail, the corners twitching up and being dragged back down and twitching up again, while her heart, down the line, goes like a drum.

*She's very bad at this*, says Ciel. ᶜ

*She's terrible at it*, he agrees. ᴹ

He doesn't laugh. He wants to. He holds it in his chest where it's warm, and goes over to the bed.

* * *

He sits on the edge of the bed. The mattress dips and she rolls an inch toward him, blanket and all.

“Good morning,” he says, quietly enough that it's just for her. ᴹ

One eye opens. “Very slowly,” she reminds him. ʸ

He brushes her hair back and touches his mouth to her forehead first. Then her cheek, where the pillow has left a crease. She catches him by the collar before he can get any further and pulls him down to her mouth. There goes any hope of keeping her face straight. He feels the laugh against his lips and has to put a hand down on the mattress to keep from falling in after her.

“I've got to get you downstairs eventually,” he says. ᴹ

“I know. I'm practising sleeping.” ʸ

She shuts her eyes again and lifts both arms for him.

He slides one arm under her shoulders and one under her knees, the way you'd pick up someone who'd fallen asleep in a chair, and lifts.

She weighs nothing. She never has. She comes up out of the bed with the blanket still half round her, and her head falls against his shoulder in a very good impression of sleep, and her hand, the one nearest him, comes up out of the hoodie sleeve and fists itself in the front of his shirt so hard he can feel her knuckles.

That isn't asleep. Asleep people don't hold on like that.

He doesn't say anything.

He carries her out of the room and down the corridor and down the stairs, slowly, one step at a time, so she won't be jolted. She keeps her eyes shut the whole way. Her face is turned into his shoulder, and he can feel her mouth against his collarbone, and he can feel that it's smiling now, properly, all the way, because nobody can see.

At the bottom of the stairs he stops.

The kitchen door is closed. He can hear them on the other side. Nothing. Absolute silence. The particular silence of about fifteen people holding their breath at once.

*Now*, says Umaru, from the other side of the door, in a whisper so loud it carries through the wood. ᵁ

Somebody shushes her.

He pushes the door open with his shoulder and carries Yuka into the kitchen.

* * *

“You can open your eyes,” he says. ᴹ

She doesn't. Not straight away. She holds on to his shirt a moment longer with her face in his shoulder.

Then she lifts her head and opens them.

And the kitchen is ready.

Everything is on the table. Every cake, every tart, the tower of small round things from the island, finished now, piped and perfect and leaning only very slightly. Emilia's wobbling thing in the place of honour in the middle, wobbling. Fruit arranged on plates. Bread still warm. Fish, the good way, skin on. Rice. Eggs. Pots of jam and honey and plum paste and the cream that Stark whisked for twenty minutes, standing in stiff peaks in a bowl like a mountain range. A hundred things, more than they could ever eat, crowding the long table end to end.

And napkins. Folded into small, very precise shapes. Birds, Mau thinks. Or possibly dragons. Ori's work.

And round the table, the house. All of them who could fit. Pretending very hard not to be watching. Fern pouring tea with enormous concentration. Milim sitting on her hands. Frieren at the end with her cup, not pretending at all, just watching, with that small soft look. Umaru, sitting on the counter by the reject pile, with both fists pressed to her mouth.

Yuka looks at it all.

She doesn't say anything for a long time.

Then she turns her face back into his shoulder and makes a sound he's never heard her make. Muffled. Somewhere between a laugh and a sob and a word.

He can feel it down the line. He doesn't need it to be a word.

* * *

She won't let him put her down.

He tries. He goes to the chair at the head of the table, which someone has pulled out and put a cushion on, and starts to lower her into it, and she makes a small fierce noise and holds on harder. So he sits in the chair himself, with her still in his arms, and she rearranges herself in his lap, sideways, with her back against the arm of the chair and her legs over his and her head against his chest, and the blanket that came down the stairs with her still round her shoulders.

And then she reaches out and takes a cake off the nearest plate and eats it.

The house lets out its breath.

It's like a dam going. Everybody moves at once. Plates going round. Milim falling on the tarts. Umaru jumping down off the counter and announcing that the reject pile is now *officially open*. Stark looking at his mountain of cream with an expression of deep personal pride. Rem, very quietly, pushing the tower of small round things an inch closer to Yuka, and Yuka taking one and putting it in Mau's mouth before he can say anything.

He eats it.

She takes another one and eats it herself.

Then another, for him.

* * *

She's like this all through breakfast.

She doesn't get off his lap. Not once. She eats from every plate she can reach, and the ones she can't reach she points at, and somebody passes them along. She feeds him one bite for every two she eats, and when he tries to feed himself she takes the fork out of his hand and does it for him. She steals the pickles off his plate before he's even had time to set them aside. She puts her cold feet on his leg under the blanket.

And every so often, for no reason he can see, in the middle of a mouthful, she just stops, and turns her face up, and looks at him.

The first time, he thinks she wants him to pass something. He reaches for the plate of cakes and she catches his chin on the way past. Her thumb is cool against his jaw. The kiss leaves a dab of cream on his upper lip.

“You've got something on you,” she says. ʸ

“I wonder how it got there.” ᴹ

She leans in again to get it. He turns his head at the last second and catches her mouth. A little laugh gets out of her. She curls her hand round the back of his neck and keeps him there until the fork in his other hand comes to rest on the plate.

At the far end, Milim takes in a heroic amount of air. Rimuru barely gets there in time.

By the time Mau has slid the tart plate within Stark's reach, Yuka has shifted so he's tucked into the crook of her arm. There's an open place on her cheek just above the edge of the blanket. He puts a kiss there on his own. She stops with a piece of cake pinched between her fingers, puts it back, and presses her face into his neck.

“You can do that again,” she says. ʸ

He does. She tips her head into it so hard he has to catch her at the waist. Her feet slide off his shin, and he gathers the blanket around them before she gets cold. When he looks up again, Stark is holding out the plate with one tart left on it, very solemnly, as if he's offering it to somebody on the other side of a river.

Yuka takes it.

“For him,” she tells Stark. She eats the crisp edge off it and feeds Mau the rest. ʸ

By the time Mau kisses Yuka's wrist on the way to taking a bite, Rimuru has started eating one-handed.

Umaru, by the reject pile, has stopped eating and is just watching them with her chin in her hands and tears running down her face, and isn't doing anything about the tears, and has a fork in one hand that she's forgotten about.

* * *

Mau spots two of the little island cakes left by Rem's elbow. He reaches past Yuka for one, then stops.

“Rem. Which of those has the lemon in it?” ᴹ

“The one on the left.” Rem looks down at the plate and turns it half a circle. “Your left. Now.” ᴿᵉ

He splits the cake on his left, checks the filling, and holds half to Yuka's mouth. She takes a bite. Her back straightens against his arm.

“You could tell?” ʸ

“You ate the lemon ones off my plate first. Every morning we ordered them.” ᴹ

“I did give you one.” ʸ

“It had a tooth mark in it.” ᴹ

She takes a bite out of his half as well, leaving just such a mark. Mau looks down at it, eats the rest, and goes looking for one of the tarts Rem likes before Milim notices there's one left. Rem takes the plate from him when it comes round, still holding the piping cloth in her other hand. She has enough time to take one bite before Umaru notices what it is.

* * *

The notebook is under the bowl of cream.

Yuka finds it halfway through breakfast, when she reaches for the cream and lifts the bowl and there it is, with a ring of cream on the cover. She picks it up. She opens it to the day's page.

It's blank. Nobody has written today's number yet.

She looks at it.

Then she dips her finger in the cream and draws, very carefully, on the page, a **4** and a **7**. In cream. Big. Taking up the whole page.

She looks at it with great satisfaction.

Then she takes the pencil from the spine and writes it properly underneath, small and neat, the way she writes things she means to keep. *47*.

And under that, after a moment, something else he can't see, because she tilts the page away from him and covers it with her hand.

“What did you write?” he says. ᴹ

“Nothing.” ʸ

“You wrote something.” ᴹ

“It's on the list.” She closes the notebook, cream and all. “You'll find out.” ʸ

“When?” ᴹ

She looks at him over the top of the notebook. Her eyes are very bright, and her face is flushed, and there's cream at the corner of her mouth, and she's the only colour in the room, the only colour in the world, and she's looking at him like he's something she nearly lost and got back and intends never to put down again.

“When you do it,” she says. ʸ

* * *

Afterwards, when the table is wrecked and Milim is asleep under it and Stark has been forced to eat his own cream mountain and the reject pile has been entirely consumed, Shuna comes and stands by his chair.

Yuka's asleep. Really asleep this time. In his lap, with her face in his chest and the blanket round her and her hand still fisted in his shirt.

Shuna looks at her for a moment.

“The fire one,” she says. Quietly. “The tray I rejected first thing. It wasn't on fire because you burned it. It was on fire because you were watching the stairs.” ˢʰᵘ

He doesn't answer.

Shuna smiles. She almost never does it where people can see.

“I'll teach you that one properly,” she says. “Another day. When you're not watching the stairs.” ˢʰᵘ

She goes back to the sink.

He sits at the head of the table with his wife asleep in his lap and the kitchen full of the wreckage of the best breakfast this house has ever had, and doesn't move, for a very long time.
