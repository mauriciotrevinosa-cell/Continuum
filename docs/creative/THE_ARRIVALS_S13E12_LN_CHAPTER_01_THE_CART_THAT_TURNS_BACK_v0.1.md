# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 23 — The Cart That Turns Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 23
**Source episode:** S13E12
**Continuity state:** B+284 evening. An order to advance is refused after the wounded speak for themselves. A weapon discharged by an enemy officer injures his own soldier. The house provides care, and the remaining column begins to withdraw.

*When: Bellion sees the first cart turn back before the man behind it knows what the driver has decided.*

The man in the white helmet comes up by the bench and asks to go across with his bag. He has somebody waiting for him on the other side. A captain has told them to get back in the carts before they have had a chance to find out whether all the people they brought are there.

Mau asks him whether he wants someone to come with him.

“One of mine is coming down to meet me,” he says. “I'd like the people on your side to know I asked to go.” ᴴ

Mau sends the word. He sees the two shadows at the bridge move aside as the man goes past them. One stays where it can be seen from the bank. The other goes down to the bench to tell the woman using it that she's welcome to stay a little longer.

There is a cart across the stream. Mau can see a woman with her hand on the side of it. She is asking the driver which way he is going to go. The driver gives her an answer and starts to turn. The man behind him has not understood the answer. He gets a foot up on the board before he realises it is moving the other way.

The man in the helmet looks back at Mau. He leaves one hand up while he takes the last few steps to the cart.

“He's going home,” he says when he comes back to the rail. “His brother says the rest of them ought to go too.” ᴴ

Mau can see the brothers when the cart turns enough for him to see the seat. The man he met on the first night has a blanket up to his waist and one boot beside him because he can't put it on yet. His brother is sitting close enough to keep him where he's got himself. He calls to the man in the helmet to ask where he should send the blanket back.

“Keep it until you get there,” the man says. ᴴ

The cart is almost clear when somebody farther up calls for it to stop.

* * *

Mau hears Yuka before he sees her. She's been watching the far end of the rail with Mahoraga. She calls out that there are people coming down on foot. He looks where she can see them. An officer is coming down with both hands full. The people coming with him are keeping well away from his right side. Mau thinks they're doing that to give him room. Then one of them puts a hand out to keep somebody else from getting into it.

The officer tells the driver to stop a second time. The driver doesn't. The officer turns towards the bridge and raises the thing in his right hand. One of his men catches him by the elbow. The light goes wide. It strikes a man who was waiting beside the ditch with his back to it.

There is a little time when everybody on that part of the road can see him standing. Then he falls. The officer looks down at the hand still on his elbow. The man who caught him lets go and goes to the person on the ground.

The man in the white helmet runs past Mau. Mau follows as far as the rail. He calls Shoko before he has had a chance to see enough to tell her what she wants to know. She asks him for what he can see. The man in the helmet is down on both knees, trying to get the fallen man's coat open without rolling him over. He tells Mau the man is breathing.

“Then ask her to come here,” he says. ᴴ

Mau does. The officer has put his weapon down by then. He reaches for it once. Mahoraga stops between him and the place where he put it. Yuka calls to the men behind him to ask whether any of them want to go with the cart. Two move before they hear the whole question. A third goes far enough to put his hand on the back of the seat.

Mau stays where he can see the man in the ditch. The rescuer asks for a light. Mau makes a little one and gives it to the soldier who took the officer by the elbow. He puts it exactly where the rescuer asks.

* * *

Shoko takes longer with the man in the ditch than Mau expected anybody could take while there are people standing a few steps away who might decide to do the same thing again. She asks him for a second light. He makes it. She leaves it near the man's head and asks the man in the helmet whether he heard him say anything before she got there.

“He asked who was coming,” the rescuer says. ᴴ

Shoko bends nearer to tell him who she is. Mau doesn't hear her get an answer. He watches her listen anyway.

At the end of the bridge, the officer is speaking to Rimuru. He says he was given a time to have the people on the far side through. He says the others will do it if he doesn't. Rimuru asks whether the others are still at the place Fern put on the map. The officer looks down at the weapon he can no longer reach. Somebody behind him answers first.

“Yes. They told us to come back for another page if we couldn't get through.” ᴵⁿ²

Mau recognises the coat before he sees the face. The soldier who gave Fern the folded page is holding the last cart by its side. He tells Rimuru which door the man with the pages uses. Then he asks if they'll let the people on the cart go. Rimuru asks him whether they still want to.

The soldier looks up at the driver. The driver nods. He has waited through everything that happened by the ditch because there was a person in the road and he wanted to know whether anybody was coming for him. He gets back into the seat now. The man in the white helmet goes far enough to tell him that somebody is there.

Shoko looks up at Mau. “He stays here until I can move him. You can make room.” ˢʰ

He goes to do it. The cart passes him at a walk. The people in it are quiet long enough to hear the one behind them start to turn.
