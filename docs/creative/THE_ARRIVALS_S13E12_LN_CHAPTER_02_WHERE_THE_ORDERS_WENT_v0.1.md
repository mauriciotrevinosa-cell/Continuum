# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 24 — Where the Orders Went

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 24
**Source episode:** S13E12
**Continuity state:** B+285 morning. With testimony, records and an assembly commitment to receive detainees, the house reaches the issuing office in the capital and takes the two responsible officials alive. Mau explains the transfer to residents. Reinhard asks about casualties and the grievously injured soldier.

*When: There are people waiting at the office who want to know whether their sons are expected back before dinner.*

Fern gives Mau the assembly's answer before he leaves. Three delegations have agreed to receive the two men if the house brings them. The clerk will take what the witnesses gave permission to share. He has put his own name at the bottom so that somebody who comes to the hall can ask for him.

Mau reads that part again. He puts it away where he put the invitation the last time he went.

Yuta takes the road with him. They have heard the same name from two men and seen it in the same place on two papers. One of the men who brought a paper chose to give his own name as well. The other asked Fern to keep it until his brother had got home. Mau told her he could wait for that one.

At the capital, the door is already open. A woman stands near it with a bag hooked over her arm. She wants to know whether she can go in. The person beside her has told her twice that somebody will come out if they wait.

“Who are you waiting for?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“My son went to the road,” she says. “They told me they'd know today whether he could come back.” ᵂ

Yuta asks her to stay where she can see the door. He says it gently enough that she looks up to find out why he'd ask. Mau has just time to tell her there are people coming back before he sees a man at the end of the passage put several pages into a case. The man sees him too. He closes the case with a finger caught under the flap.

Mau asks him his name. He says it without thinking. Then he looks at who else is close enough to have heard.

“The people at the road gave us copies of your orders,” Mau says. “I'd like you to come with me to the hall and answer for them.” ᴹ

The man pulls his finger out of the flap. He looks past Mau. Yuta is holding the door, leaving the woman room to see what they're doing. Nobody comes in from the other end.

“You don't have the authority to take me,” the man says. ᴰᵉ

“The hall has agreed to receive you. It can hear you say that.” ᴹ

The man starts to look back over his shoulder. Another person in the passage has stopped beside a desk with both hands out where they can see them. Yuta asks that man his name. He says it after looking at the first one, and Yuta asks him to come as well. They stand there long enough for each of the two to see the other decide that he will walk.

When they come out, the woman moves her bag so the first man can go past. She asks what Mau will do with them.

“Take them to the place where the delegations met,” he says. “The people there will hear what happened. They're going to decide what to do with these two.” ᴹ

“Can I go?” she asks. ᵂ

Mau takes the answer out to show her the name at the bottom. Yuta waits beside him while she reads it. She'll go when she has somebody to come with her. She knows a woman who has been at that door almost every day since the carts left. She would like to go and find her first.

* * *

Reinhard meets them at the foot of the steps. He has come out with a man carrying a box he can put the papers in. The man asks the two officials to come up with him. Yuta goes as far as the door with them, waits until he can see that somebody has received them and then gives the box to the clerk himself.

Reinhard stays at the steps with Mau.

“How many have died?” he asks. “I need to know whose family to contact.” ᴿʰ

Mau has asked for that number every time he has come back in. He asked for it once in front of Ori, and the next time she asked if the man who had answered him could stay for supper. He thinks of the cart turning back and the man in the ditch.

“None so far,” he says. “There's a man who was hit when somebody on his side tried to stop an officer from firing. He's in bad shape. Shoko told me he needed to stay where he was until she can move him.” ᴹ

Reinhard looks at the road behind them. “Do you know who he'd want told?” ᴿʰ

“He hasn't been able to tell us. When he can, we'll send word to the person he asks for. If he wants you, I'll tell you where to meet him.” ᴹ

“Thank you.” ᴿʰ

Mau watches him put his hand down by his side. The sword is there. Neither of them looks at it for long. Reinhard asks whether the people in the carts have somewhere to stop before they get back to their villages. Mau tells him about the posts Luminous offered and which road the first cart was using. Reinhard says he'll go down far enough to find out whether they need another place.

“I hope I see you in better circumstances next time,” he says. ᴿʰ

“So do I.” ᴹ

For a moment it looks to Mau as though Reinhard is going to wait for him to go in. Mau tells him he'd like to go home now. Reinhard moves enough to leave the steps clear. Yuta comes out of the door and finds the two of them standing there. He asks if Mau is ready. Mau says yes and this time he goes home by the road.
