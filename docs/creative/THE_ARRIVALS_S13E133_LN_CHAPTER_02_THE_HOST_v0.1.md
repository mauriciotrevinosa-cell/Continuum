# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 84
## Chapter 35 — The Host

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 84
**Reading order:** 35
**Source episode:** S13E133
**Continuity state:** B+456 ("31"). The witches' first visit inside. All seven arrive at tea time the way DIO does: they step into Mau's shadow on the porch and he connects their way in, body and all (only Makima, a construction Noise holds, has to stay near; the witches are real, so they can go in), through the doors Noise built. Noise hosts. It goes about as well as everyone expected: Pride wants a game; Daphne wants to know where the food is (there is none; there is only the cup, and the cup is always warm); Minerva is angry about the chairs; Echidna asks Noise what he is; Sekhmet finds the bed in her room and is not seen again; Carmilla sits in the corner and Noise doesn't notice her until she speaks. Ciel, still offended, refuses to help at first and then can't bear it and helps. DIO arrives late with her plant and is jealous for exactly four minutes. Satella sits at the table among her sisters and drinks from the cup. Noise, at the end, says it was the best day he's had. Mau, outside, can barely walk straight from the noise in his head, and is happy.

*When: B+456. "31." Under it, in Ori's hand: "MAU HAS A HEADACHE (GOOD KIND)".*

They come at tea time.

All of them. All at once. Mau is sitting on the porch with Yuka, not doing anything, when they come across the yard from the north wing in a line, Echidna at the front, Minerva at the back carrying Sekhmet.

They stop at the bottom of the porch steps. His shadow lies down them in the low sun.

“May we?” says Echidna. ᴱᶜ

“Go on,” he says. ᴹ

Echidna steps into his shadow and is gone. Then Pride, with a little jump, the way you'd jump into a pool. Daphne, still chewing. Satella, carefully. Carmilla, so quietly Yuka nearly misses it. Minerva last, with Sekhmet asleep over her shoulder.

It's the same as DIO. They're real; they go in all the way, body and all, and he opens the way for them through the shadows and connects them to the doors on the other side. Not like Makima, whose body is a construction Noise holds out here and who has to stay close. The witches can go in whole.

He feels them arrive: seven small doors opening inside him, one after another along the new corridor, like a row of lamps being lit.

He goes very still.

Yuka looks at the empty steps. Then at him.

“They're in,” he says. ᴹ

“How does it feel?” she says. ʸ

He thinks about it.

“Loud,” he says. ᴹ

* * *

Inside, Noise is standing in the corridor.

Mau goes in to see. He doesn't mean to stay. He just wants to look.

The corridor is longer than it was. Noise built it well. The old part, with DIO's door and Ashborn's that stays closed and the one that's Satella's now. And then the new part, seven doors, each one different, each one with something on it that wasn't there last night, because the witches have already been and claimed them.

A small crown painted on one, slightly crooked. A plate nailed to another. A book wedged in the frame of the third. A bed visible through the fourth, which is already shut, and from behind which comes the sound of very slow breathing.

And Noise, standing in the middle of the corridor with all seven of them coming out of their doors at once and heading for the table, looking, for the first time Mau has ever seen, completely and utterly lost.

*Help*, says Noise. ᴺ

*You'll be fine*, says Mau. ᴹ

*I don't know what to do with my hands*, says Noise. ᴺ

*You haven't got hands*, says Mau. *You're a shadow.* ᴹ

*I know*, says Noise. *That's making it worse.* ᴺ

* * *

Pride gets to the table first.

She climbs onto a chair, then onto the table, and sits down in the middle of it, cross-legged, beside the cup.

“Game,” she says to Noise. “What game do you have?” ᴾʳ

Noise looks at her.

*Games*, he says, inside, to Mau, with rising panic. *What games do I have.* ᴺ

*You don't have any games.* ᴹ

*I know I don't have any games.* ᴺ

“I don't have any games,” says Noise, out loud. ᴺ

Pride looks at him with deep disappointment.

“Then we'll make one up,” she says. “Sit down. You're it.” ᴾʳ

Noise sits down. Mau isn't sure what *it* means. Neither, he suspects, is Noise.

* * *

Daphne comes next, and looks at the table, and looks at the cup, and looks round the room.

“Where's the food?” she says. ᴰᵃ

“There isn't any,” says Noise. ᴺ

Daphne stares at him.

“There's the cup,” says Noise. “It's always warm.” ᴺ

Daphne picks up the cup. She looks into it. She drinks from it.

Her face changes.

“What is this,” she says. ᴰᵃ

“Nobody knows,” says Noise. ᴺ

“It tastes like—” Daphne stops. She drinks again. “It tastes like the first meal I ever ate that I didn't have to fight for.” ᴰᵃ

She puts the cup down, very slowly.

Then she sits down at the table and doesn't say anything else for some time.

* * *

Minerva is angry about the chairs.

“They're too *small*,” she says, standing over the nearest one with her fists on her hips. “Who made these chairs? Who made a chair this small?” ᴹⁿ

“I did,” says Noise. ᴺ

“Well, they're *terrible*.” ᴹⁿ

“I'm sorry.” ᴺ

“Don't *apologise*,” says Minerva. “That makes it worse. Make a *bigger one*.” ᴹⁿ

Noise looks at the chair. The chair gets bigger.

Minerva looks at it for a long time. Then she sits in it.

“Better,” she says, grudgingly. And then, after a moment, much more quietly: “It's a good room. I'm angry about it.” ᴹⁿ

* * *

Echidna sits down opposite Noise and puts her elbows on the table and her chin in her hands.

“What are you?” she says. ᴱᶜ

Noise looks at her.

“I've wanted to ask that for a long time,” says Echidna. “Since the first tea. I've watched you through him. You're not a soul. You're not a guest. You're not a technique, although you began as one. You're not a person, exactly, and you're not a thing. I've read every book I own and three of Frieren's and I don't know what you are. I'd very much like you to tell me.” ᴱᶜ

Noise is quiet for a long moment.

“I'm Noise,” he says. ᴺ

Echidna waits.

“He made me,” says Noise, “to stand beside him. Inside. And I stood there. For a long time. And then I wanted to sit down. At the table. With people. That's all I know.” ᴺ

Echidna looks at him for a long time with that sharp, total, delighted interest.

“That,” she says softly, “is the most interesting answer anyone has ever given me.” ᴱᶜ

* * *

Ciel refuses to help.

She's sitting at the far end of the table with her arms folded and her chin up, still offended from yesterday, pointedly not looking at Noise drowning. Mau can feel her not looking. It's very loud.

*Options*, she says, to nobody. ᶜ

Pride has invented a game. It involves the cup, three spoons, and a set of rules that change every time Noise appears to understand them. Daphne is still sitting very still, looking at the cup. Minerva is complaining about the light. Echidna wants to know whether the cup would stay warm with nobody there to drink from it.

And from the corner, very quietly, a voice says, “...is there more tea?” ᶜᵃ

Noise jumps.

He actually jumps. The whole outline of him goes up an inch and comes down again. He turns round.

Carmilla is in the corner. She's been in the corner the whole time. Sitting on the floor with her knees drawn up, nearly invisible, the way she always is. Nobody saw her come in.

“I'm sorry,” she whispers. “I didn't mean to startle you.” ᶜᵃ

Noise looks at her for a long moment.

Then he gets up from the table, and goes over, and sits down on the floor beside her. Not close. Near.

“You didn't,” he says. “I'm just bad at corners.” ᴺ

Carmilla looks at him.

“So am I,” she says. ᶜᵃ

* * *

Ciel lasts until the spoons.

Pride's game has reached a stage where the spoons are being thrown, and one of them hits Minerva, and Minerva stands up and is angry, and Echidna is asking a question about spoons, and Noise is on the floor in the corner beside Carmilla not knowing what to do, and the cup is being knocked over.

Ciel stands up.

“*No*,” she says. ᶜ

Everyone stops.

Ciel walks down the length of the table. She picks up the cup before it goes over. She takes the spoons out of Pride's hands. She looks at Minerva until Minerva sits down.

“Rules,” says Ciel. ᶜ

And she explains the rules of Pride's game. Properly. In order. So that they make sense and stay the same. Then she sits down in the middle of the table and runs it, like a general, like Ori at the Fort, and in five minutes everybody is playing and nobody is throwing anything.

Noise looks at her from the corner.

*Thank you*, he says. ᴺ

*Don't*, says Ciel. *I'm still offended. I'm just more offended by chaos.* ᶜ

* * *

DIO arrives late.

She comes out of her own door with her watering notebook held against her chest, and stops in the corridor, and looks at the table.

Her table. Hers and Noise's and Ciel's. Full of witches.

Mau feels it go through her. Fast and hot. The old thing. *Mine. Mine. Who are these people at my table.*

It lasts about four minutes.

He counts.

For four minutes, DIO stands in the corridor with her notebook and her chin up and a face like a thundercloud, watching Pride play the spoon game with Ciel and Echidna ask Noise about the room.

Then Satella, at the end of the table, looks up and sees her.

“Is that the notebook?” Satella says. “The one with the leaves?” ˢᵃ

DIO goes still.

“For the plant Elmesia's gardeners admired,” says Satella. “I heard about it. Everyone heard about it. May I see?” ˢᵃ

DIO looks at her for a long moment.

Then she comes to the table, and sits down next to Satella, and opens the notebook between them, at the page with the newest leaf drawn in it, and starts to explain. All of it. The light. The new leaves. The holiday it's going to have in Elmesia's garden. Satella listens to every word.

Four minutes. Mau counted.

* * *

Satella sits at the table among her sisters and drinks from the cup.

He watches her do it. He's back outside by then, on the porch, with Yuka's head on his shoulder and a headache coming on that is entirely the good kind, but he can see her, inside, the way he can always see inside.

She picks up the cup. She looks into it.

She drinks.

He doesn't know what it tastes like for her. He'll never ask. But he sees her face, after, and he sees Echidna see it too, from across the table, and Echidna puts her hand over Satella's for just a moment, and takes it away, and goes back to asking Noise about the room.

* * *

They leave at dusk.

All seven doors closing, one after another, along the new corridor, and then the witches stepping back up out of his shadow onto the porch, one at a time, blinking at the evening. Six of them. Sekhmet's door has been closed since the beginning, and from behind it comes slow, deep, happy breathing. Minerva looks at Mau. He says he'll bring her out when she wakes up.

Noise stands in the corridor after they've gone.

The table is a mess. Spoons everywhere. The chairs all different sizes. The cup, still warm, somehow still full.

*Well?* says Mau. ᴹ

Noise doesn't answer for a while.

*That*, he says at last, *was the best day I've ever had.* ᴺ

Mau sits on the porch with his eyes shut and his head ringing like a bell and Yuka asleep on his shoulder.

*Good*, he says. ᴹ

*They're coming back tomorrow*, says Noise. ᴺ

*I know.* ᴹ

*Pride said so. She said there's a new game.* ᴺ

*I know.* ᴹ

A pause.

*I'm going to need more spoons*, says Noise. ᴺ
