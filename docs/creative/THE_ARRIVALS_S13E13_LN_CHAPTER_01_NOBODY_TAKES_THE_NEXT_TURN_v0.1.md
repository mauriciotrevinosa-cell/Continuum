# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 76
## Chapter 25 — Nobody Takes the Next Turn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 76
**Reading order:** 25
**Source episode:** S13E13
**Continuity state:** B+285 evening. The columns have gone and the assembly has received the organizers. The house celebrates at supper without a return rotation. Umaru invokes the snack agreement. Yuka proposes the trip and writes to Elmesia.

*When: Rem has put an extra bowl on the table and nobody is sure whose turn it used to be.*

Mau comes in carrying the paper the clerk gave Yuta. Fern gets to read it while he takes his shoes off. It says the hall received both men, and the time. She's been waiting for a place to put that time all afternoon. She writes it down before she goes to see whether anyone in the common room is still expecting to be relieved.

Bellion is there, standing by the map. He has heard from the last two groups outside. The carts have gone the way the people driving them wanted to go. He wants to know whether Rimuru would like the additional shadows on the road until morning. Rimuru asks Fern what the posts have said. She reads him their messages, one after the other, until they get to the one that says somebody has reopened the turn where the carts had to stop.

“Keep the extra eyes through tonight,” Rimuru says. “Ask us again in the morning.” ᴿ

Bellion marks the places he can cover without keeping anybody from the meal. Then he takes his thumb off the map and asks if the chair at the end of the table is for him.

“It can be,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

He takes it. Mau has seen him sit at the table before. He had not seen him put both hands down in front of himself as though he has finished everything he means to do for the evening. He asks whether Bellion wants the map put away. Bellion says he thinks it can stay until they're done eating. Mau leaves it where it is.

* * *

Umaru waits until everybody has had enough time to get something into a bowl. Then she comes over to Mau and tells him she has a question for the vending citizen.

“You said that was for the celebration,” he says. ᴹ

“Is this the celebration?” ᵁ

He looks across the table. Milim has caught Yuta trying to stand up to check a door and is pointing at his bowl. Ori has brought her drawing over to Shizue and is showing her what they did with the wings. On the other end of the bench, Yuka has eaten the first part of her supper and has an elbow on the table in exactly the place she couldn't put it while she was watching the bridge.

“I'd like it to be,” Mau says. ᴹ

Umaru brings a chair up to the table. She tells him she had put down everything they might need for it before she knew how long it was going to take. He asks to see the list. She says she's not going to make him read it himself. She'll tell him the first part while he eats.

The first part takes until somebody brings a second dish out. It includes a kind of chip he's never seen in the shop and an argument over whether one bottle counts as a serving if it belongs to Milim. Milim votes that it does. Ori looks at the bottle Mau has just put on the table and asks how many cups it can fill. He gets another out and lets them see.

Makima comes in while the cups are going around. She has brought Shoko's message from the room where the man in the ditch is staying. He is still there. He can answer one question at a time and has asked them to tell somebody his sister knows. Fern takes the name and goes far enough to see whether the watch can find a person to carry it.

“Have you eaten?” Ori asks Makima. ᴼ

“Not yet.” ᴹᵏ

Ori brings the spare bowl over. She puts it down in a place near Bellion and goes back to hear whether Milim is allowed to call a bottle a serving. Makima looks after her a moment. Then she takes the seat.

Umaru watches from where she's sitting beside Mau. She leaves the list down long enough to pass the bread that way. Then she leans over and tells Mau there is a second page.

* * *

Later Yuka finds him with a little pile of cups by the door. He has been waiting for somebody to tell him where he ought to put them. She takes half the pile and goes straight to the kitchen. He follows her.

“You know what I thought about on the bridge today?” she says. ʸ

“Whether you had to get Bragas out?” ᴹ

“After that.” She sets the cups down. “I thought we'd earned a trip.” ʸ

Mau looks at her. She leaves her hand on the top cup. He can see she's been waiting to say it. He asks if she still has the card.

“I have the card and the letter. I finished it when they told me the carts were going home.” ʸ

She takes him upstairs to read it. Elmesia's name is at the top in Mau's hand. Beneath it, Yuka asks if she can tell them when she'll be at the island. She's written one line about wanting the room with the view and one line about wanting to see her when she gets there. He reads the two in order and finds that he likes both. Yuka waits while he adds a small thank-you at the bottom.

“Do we know what day Ori's birthday is?” he asks. ᴹ

“I wrote that into the part about when we could go.” ʸ

He reads it again and sees that she has. She waits until he reaches the bottom. Then she takes the paper back so she can fold it.

Downstairs, someone calls to ask whether he has any more of what was in the second bag. He reaches for his coat and Yuka takes his wrist.

“Tell them to come get you in a minute,” she says. ʸ

He sits back down. “What do you want to do with the minute?” ᴹ

She leans into him with the letter still in her hand. Downstairs, somebody else says they'll come get him if they need to. For the first time in several days, Mau can hear the words and stay where he is.
