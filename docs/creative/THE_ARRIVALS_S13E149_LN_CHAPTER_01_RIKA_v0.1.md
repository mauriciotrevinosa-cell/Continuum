# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 85
## Chapter 22 — Rika

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 85
**Reading order:** 22
**Source episode:** S13E149
**Continuity state:** B+483 ("4"). Yuta asked Rika, as Mau told him to, two things: whether she'd let Noise make the snapshot (a save copy of her, held in a new weapon filled with all the CE Yuta has given her, that takes a killing blow in her place once and then has to be made again), and whether she'd rather live in a second ring or a blade. It took Rika five weeks to answer. Today she answers yes, on one condition: she wants to meet Noise first, properly, the way you meet someone before you let them look closely enough to copy you. Yuta brings her out in the yard, the full ten minutes now that the ring is stretched. Noise steps out as a shadow-outline to meet her after she asks for him by name. They look at each other. Rika asks what he is; Mau says Noise's words for him, since only Mau can hear him. She says she's something that stayed when it shouldn't have, too. She chooses a second ring; Yuta's katana will be raised to the same high grade alongside it. Noise reads her pattern for the snapshot. Not finished yet; a beginning. Yuta cries a little and pretends he doesn't.

*When: B+483. "4." Under it, in Yuta's hand: "Rika said yes". And under that, in a hand nobody has ever seen, very large and very round, as if made by fingers too big for the pencil: "HI NOISE".*

Rika takes five weeks to answer.

Yuta asked her the night after the workshop. Mau knows because Yuta told him. *I asked her*, he said, at breakfast the next morning. *Both things. The copy. And ring or blade.*

*What did she say?*

*She didn't say anything*, said Yuta. *She went quiet. She does that when she's thinking. It might be a while.*

It was five weeks.

Mau didn't ask again. Neither did Noise. Noise, who has built her once already and has had the snapshot half drawn on the table inside since the night of the workshop, waited for five weeks and didn't once say *has she answered yet*.

This morning Yuta comes to find Mau on the porch.

“She said yes,” he says. ʸᵗ

* * *

“On one condition,” says Yuta. ʸᵗ

Mau waits.

“She wants to meet him first,” says Yuta. “Noise. Properly. Not through you. Not inside. She wants to see him. Face to face. The way you'd meet someone before you let them look close enough to make a copy of you.” ʸᵗ

He turns the ring on his finger.

“She says he built her once without asking her,” he says. “For your katana. She doesn't mind. But this time it's a copy that dies for her, and she wants to know who's holding it first.” ʸᵗ

Mau thinks about it.

*Noise*, he says, inside. ᴹ

Noise is at the table. He's been at the table most mornings, these last weeks. He's very still.

*She wants to meet you*, says Mau. *Outside. In the world.* ᴹ

A long pause.

*I've only been outside with DIO and Ciel*, says Noise. *In the shape they made for those days. For them.* ᴺ

*I know.* ᴹ

*Nobody's ever asked for me. Out there. By myself.* ᴺ

*I know*, says Mau. *You don't have to.* ᴹ

Another pause. Longer.

*She asked*, says Noise. *She asked for me. Not for you. For me.* ᴺ

*Yes.* ᴹ

*Then I'll go*, says Noise. ᴺ

* * *

They do it in the yard at noon.

Not many people know. Mau didn't tell the house. It isn't the house's. But the house finds out anyway, the way it always does, and by noon there are faces at every window and Gojo on the orchard wall and Ori on the porch steps with her notebook, very quiet.

Yuta stands in the middle of the yard.

He takes off the ring. He holds it in his palm.

And calls her.

* * *

Rika comes up out of the ring like a flood.

Mau has seen her before. Many times. In fights, mostly. In the domain clash, on Day 133, breaking his hands apart. At the quarry, in the katana. Huge and wrong and grinning, all teeth and arms and that voice that sounds like a child and a storm at the same time.

She isn't like that now.

She comes up out of the ring and stands in the yard behind Yuta, enormous, filling the space between the training posts and the porch, and she's very still. Her great head bent. Her many hands folded in front of her, the way a child folds its hands when it's been told to be good.

She's nervous, Mau realises.

Rika is nervous.

*Ten minutes*, says Ciel, inside. *The ring's stretched. She has ten.* ᶜ

* * *

Noise steps out.

Mau feels him do it. It's the strangest feeling he's ever had. Like something stepping out of his own chest. Not a soldier. Not Makima, whose body Noise holds. Noise himself. The first thing Mau ever made, who stood beside the table inside him longer than anyone knows. He's done it before, a handful of times, with DIO and Ciel. Never because someone outside asked for him by name.

Stepping out into the world.

He appears in the yard beside Mau. A shadow-outline. The shape of someone. Not quite there. A darkness in the noon light, standing on the grass, with the sun going through him a little at the edges.

Mau feels him feel it. Feel the warmth of it, on whatever he has instead of skin. Feel the grass under whatever he has instead of feet.

Noise stands very still.

Then he walks across the yard to Rika.

* * *

They look at each other.

The shadow-outline, small, in the middle of the yard. The enormous curse, folded and nervous, behind Yuta.

Nobody says anything.

At the windows, nobody moves. On the wall, Gojo has taken his blindfold all the way off. On the porch, Ori has her pencil in her mouth.

Rika bends her great head down. Lower. Lower. Until her face, all teeth and eyes, is level with Noise.

“*What are you?*” she says. ʳⁱᵏᵃ

Her voice is very quiet. For Rika.

Noise looks at her.

Nobody outside Mau can hear Noise. So Mau says it for him, from the porch, word for word, as Noise gives it to him.

“*I'm Noise*,” says Mau. ᴺ

Rika waits.

“*He made me*,” says Mau, in Noise's words, “*to stand beside him. Inside. And I stood there. For a long time. And then I wanted to sit down. At the table. With people. That's all I know.*” ᴺ

The same words Noise gave Echidna. Mau recognises them.

Rika is quiet for a long moment.

* * *

“*I stayed*,” she says. ʳⁱᵏᵃ

Noise waits.

“*When I shouldn't have*,” says Rika. “*I was supposed to go. When I died. I didn't. I stayed. Because of him.*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

One of her great hands comes up and very gently touches the top of Yuta's head.

“*I don't know what holds me*,” she says. “*I never knew. I just stayed. And then I was this. And then he let me go, and I went, and then I came back, here, in the ring, and I don't know why. I don't know what I am either.*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

She looks at Noise.

“*You stayed too*,” she says. “*On your feet. When you could have stopped.*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

“*Yes*,” says Mau, for him. ᴺ

“*Why?*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

Noise thinks about it.

Mau feels him think about it. All the way down. The whole long time on his feet beside the table. The Trial. The first Stay. The night he picked up the cup.

“*Because someone needed me there*,” says Mau, for him. “*And then because I wanted to be.*” ᴺ

Rika looks at him for a long time.

Then she smiles. Not the terrible grin. Something smaller. Something that looks, for just a moment, like a girl who died very young and has been waiting a very long time for someone to say something she understands.

“*Me too*,” she says. ʳⁱᵏᵃ

* * *

“*You can look*,” says Rika. ʳⁱᵏᵃ

She sits down. In the yard. Carefully, so as not to crush the training posts. Folds herself up as small as something that size can fold.

“*Make your copy*,” she says. “*The one that breaks so I don't.*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

She looks at Yuta.

“*A ring*,” she says. “*Not a blade. He gave me one ring already. That one was a promise. This one can be too.*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

Yuta makes a sound and doesn't finish it.

Noise looks at Yuta too.

Then he steps forward, and puts one shadow-hand, very gently, on Rika's enormous folded arm.

And begins.

* * *

It isn't finished.

Mau knows that. Noise knows that. It takes nine minutes, and at the end of the nine minutes Noise steps back, and Rika is still Rika, and nothing has changed that anyone can see.

But Mau feels it. Through Noise. Something being read, closer than when he built her for the katana, because this time she's holding still and letting him. Every form. Every line of her. All of it laid down on the table inside like a pattern on the back of one of Mai's drawings.

*Well?* says Mau, inside. ᴹ

*I have her*, says Noise. *The snapshot can be made now. Then the ring, with Mai. Then we move everything he's given her into it, you and me, and she's kept. And his katana goes on the bench beside it. Same grade. She'll want him to have something worthy of standing next to her.* ᴺ

*How long?* ᴹ

*Not long*, says Noise. *I wanted her to say yes before I drew the last line.* ᴺ

* * *

Rika goes back into the ring at ten minutes exactly.

Before she does, she bends down one more time. To Noise. Level.

“*Come back*,” she says. ʳⁱᵏᵃ

Noise looks at her.

“*Next time*,” says Rika. “*Come back out. In the sun. And bring my ring. And we can talk about staying.*” ʳⁱᵏᵃ

“*Yes*,” says Mau, for him. ᴺ

Rika smiles, the small one, and folds herself up, and goes back into the ring in Yuta's palm like water going down a drain.

The yard is just a yard.

* * *

Noise stands in the middle of it, in the sun, for a long moment.

Then he walks back across the grass to Mau, and steps back in. Mau feels him do it. Coming home. Sitting down at the table inside. Very quiet.

*How was it?* says Mau. ᴹ

*Warm*, says Noise. ᴺ

A pause.

*The sun*, he says. *It's different when someone's waiting for you in it.* ᴺ

* * *

Yuta is standing in the middle of the yard with the ring in his hand.

He's looking at it. He's not saying anything.

He's crying a little. Mau can see it from the porch. Quietly. The way Yuta does everything. And pretending he isn't, which he's very bad at.

Maki comes off the workshop step and walks across the yard to him and doesn't say anything either. She takes the ring out of his hand and puts it back on his finger for him, and then stands there with her hand over his.

On the porch steps, Ori takes her pencil out of her mouth.

She writes in her notebook. Under *4*. Under *Rika said yes*.

Then she looks at it, and frowns, and hands the notebook and the pencil to Yuta, as he comes past on his way in.

Yuta looks at it.

Then he looks at the ring.

And something happens that Mau will never be able to explain, and Noise, afterwards, won't be able to either. The pencil moves in Yuta's hand, very slowly, in big round clumsy letters, as if somebody much bigger than Yuta is holding it with fingers far too large for it.

*HI NOISE*

Yuta stares at it.

Ori looks at it for a long time.

“That's going in the official record,” she says. ᴼ
