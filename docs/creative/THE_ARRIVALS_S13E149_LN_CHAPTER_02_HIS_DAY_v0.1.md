# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 85
## Chapter 24 — His Day

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 85
**Reading order:** 24
**Source episode:** S13E149
**Continuity state:** B+484. Yuka and Ori make room for Mau to spend a day at home with company and without taking the watch. Mai asks him and Kaisel for a short flight with Momo, using the harness made with the straps from town. Ori and Umaru induct Makima into the Fort as Agent Control. Makima keeps her plan with Ori when Mau comes to find her, and Mau comes back for her afterward. She asks to hold him on the steps. Three days remain on Yoru's contract. Ori lends Mau her carved dragon.

*When: B+484, before breakfast.*

Yuka has his sleeve trapped under her hip. Mau pulls it once to let her know he's awake. She pulls it back, hard enough that he rolls all the way over into her.

“Have you already talked to Ciel?” ʸ

“Good morning to you too.” ᴹ

“Good morning. Have you already talked to Ciel?” ʸ

He kisses her. She lets him finish, then puts her thumb under his chin and waits. He met Bellion before bed. His eyes are still half shut and he's already thinking he ought to find out whether all the carts made it past the ford overnight.

*The last one got through before dawn,* says Ciel. *Rimuru has the report.* ᶜ

“You asked her,” he says. ᴹ

“I asked her to take it until supper. She said she'd do it while you slept, and that I should really see if I could get you to sleep past sunrise for once.” ʸ

Outside the shutters, somebody is trying to carry a stack of plates through the back door without making two of them touch. Mau listens to the run of careful little steps and the large crash at the end.

“Ori?” ᴹ

“Probably. She's drawing you a map.” ʸ

“Of my own house?” ᴹ

“She told me she'd like it very much if you acted surprised.” ʸ

He looks down at his sleeve. Yuka hasn't moved a millimeter. He could ask about the plates. He could also put his head back down and find out what she's doing with the hand she's just managed to slip under his shoulder.

“Can the map wait for a bit?” ᴹ

She frees enough of his sleeve to let him get his elbow around her.

* * *

At breakfast Ori spreads the map over the table and anchors all four corners with cups. The large shape on the porch is a chair. Mau knows because there is an arrow pointing at it that says *chair*.

“That's yours. The room behind you is still yours too. You don't have to go out this way if you don't want to.” She follows a path around the outside of the house with a finger. “But this is where I think you should eat your bread. It gets sun.” ᴼ

“Can I eat toast in the chair?” ᴹ

“Provided you don't toast it.” ᴼ

At the far end of the table, Gojo quietly pushes his own plate toward Shuna. Milim catches him before the plate clears his elbow.

“Draw your own map.” ᴹⁱ

Ori tells Mau she has Pickles working with her in the yard. He looks for the soldier near the wall. It steps out when Ori calls, the small dent on its head visible as it mirrors her salute with the wrong arm.

“See?” says Ori. She changes arms too. “Now we're both doing it right.” ᴼ

Makima stops at the table to look while Ori gets Pickles to turn. Ori holds the corner down for her so she can see what lies under the cup.

“Where are you eating your bread?” Ori asks. ᴼ

“I'd like to sit out with you for a bit,” says Makima. ᴹᵏ

Ori studies the map. “Then we need another chair. You can bring it from inside, but it can't go on the wet side of the step or two of its feet will be in the way when everybody comes down.” ᴼ

“Can I bring my cup?” asks Makima. ᴹᵏ

“Of course you can bring your cup. I said it's a day off. You can have as much tea as you want.” ᴼ

Mau pushes the butter across so Makima can get a piece of toast before she goes anywhere. On the way out she asks him if he wants the chair beside his or far enough off that Ori can fit in between. He says Ori's going to need more room than that if she's bringing maps.

By the time Mau finishes his toast, the map has become the place where Shuna writes *third loaf out at noon* because it's the nearest piece of paper. Yuka turns a cup over so the others can write underneath it. Then she steals the last bite of his toast while he's still watching Ori teach Pickles to turn around.

* * *

Mai finds him on the porch with a plate between him and Yuka. Shuna brought the warm heel of the loaf and sat long enough to eat the first buttered piece herself. The piece Yuka's tearing off now has both their teeth marks in it.

“Before you tell me nobody's allowed to ask you for anything,” says Mai, “would you like to watch something?” ᴹᵃ

Behind her, Momo has the harness over one shoulder. There's a red strip running the length of the new seat, folded over at the joins. Mau held the strip by both ends for Yuka at the market. He can still remember the twist she put in it while they tried to work out which way it would pull. He can see how much of it she ended up cutting away.

“We hung it up in the workshop,” says Mai. “It carried me. It carried her. It carried her and the entire bin of scrap she insisted was the same weight as you.” ᴹᵃ

“It was approximately his weight.” ᴹᵒ

“It was full of the bits I hadn't sorted yet.” ᴹᵃ

Momo lifts the harness high enough to hide her face. She lets it come back down a moment later. “Would Kaisel let us go as far as the big trees and back?” ᴹᵒ

Mau looks at Yuka. She's already picking up their plate. “I want to see how they get up,” she says. ʸ

They go as far as the grass. Kaisel comes out on the stretch far enough from the yard that a wing won't lift Ori's map off the table. He turns his head toward Mai at the sound of the metal, watches her unroll everything, and folds down until she can reach over him herself. She makes the first loop. Momo walks around to the other side to take the buckle.

“Tell me if it shifts when he stands,” says Mai to Mau. She could have asked him to do the whole thing in a second. He stays where she can see his hands and watches the seam. Kaisel rises a little. The hitch stays straight. ᴹᵃ

“It holds.” ᴹ

Mai checks it anyway. Then she goes up. She sets her knees where she put the marks on the seat and reaches back without looking. For a second Momo stands at Kaisel's side staring up at that hand. Mau saw the same look in the workshop when the two of them tried the buckle together. This time Momo takes it with a small jump that swings her up all the way.

“Did you bring the broom?” asks Mai. ᴹᵃ

“I left it by the stairs.” ᴹᵒ

“You can go get it.” ᴹᵃ

“I know.” Momo slips both arms around her. “We can go.” ᴹᵒ

Kaisel walks out first. At the end of the yard, with room for both wings, he takes them up. Mai's yell at the first drop turns into a laugh so quickly that Yuka laughs too. Momo keeps her face turned into Mai's shoulder until they're clear of the roof, then twists to point out where the tops of the trees are already turning. Mau loses sight of them behind the ridge for the length of time it takes Yuka to put her heel beside his and glance up to see if he's going to put an arm around her. He does.

On the way back Mai asks for another loop. She's close enough that he hears her this time. Kaisel banks gently and carries them around.

* * *

Mai brings him a clasp afterward. She puts it in his hand with the pin folded in so he can work out the hinge for himself. He opens it, closes it, opens it again.

“You used this join up front too.” ᴹ

“You saw that?” ᴹᵃ

“I saw you open it from below, with one hand.” ᴹ

“Oh, good.” Mai pulls a second chair up. “Then you can tell me if you think I could get the catch a little smaller. Momo thinks it'll catch her sleeve when she gets off.” ᴹᵃ

Momo drops onto the step, both elbows resting on the seat between them. “I said it *caught* my sleeve. I can show you the thread.” ᴹᵒ

She finds it. There follows a discussion between the two of them over just how old that snag might be. Mau keeps opening the clasp. It's a lovely thing to do while he listens. Maki comes by with a better look at the torn stitch, slips her thirty-first pin into his free hand and takes her time turning Momo's sleeve inside out so he can get a look at what she's given him before he has to say anything.

Across the yard Ori takes a shortcut over the roots with Pickles. She comes down on both palms. Mau is up before he hears her say she's fine. She gets one foot under herself and holds out her hand. The soldier kneels and offers its palm as she taught it. When Ori sees Mau on the stairs, she shows him both knees.

“Only grass,” she calls. “Come see if you want.” ᴼ

He goes down. He helps pull a piece of bark out of her cuff and hears the rest of the instructions she's been giving Pickles. By the time they come back up together, Gojo has claimed Mau's seat. He is very comfortable, one ankle on the other knee, eating the sweets he brought specifically for Mau to watch him eat.

“Are there any left?” asks Ori. ᴼ

“Two.” Gojo gives her both. ᴳ

She brings one to Mau. They sit on the step to eat them. Ori saves half of hers in the paper, folds it too many times and calls across the yard for Makima. She'd come back from the north wing carrying two empty cups by their handles. When she sees where Ori's hand is, she puts the cups down.

“Are you coming to the Fort later?” Ori asks. ᴼ

“I hadn't been asked.” ᴹᵏ

“I'm asking now.” ᴼ

Makima looks past her at the roof. Umaru is up there barefoot, trying to get a sun-faded banner to hang straight with one of its corners tied round her ankle. “I'm not a member, Ori.” ᴹᵏ

Ori stops folding. She leans far enough forward to see Umaru behind the banner, although the only part of her visible now is the bare foot. “Since when?” ᴼ

“As far as I know, the whole time.” ᴹᵏ

Ori puts the little paper into Makima's open hand and goes up the steps. Mau can hear her asking where they keep the old coats before she's even made it indoors.

Makima opens the paper. It's got half a sweet in it and a great many crumbs. She sits in Gojo's old place to eat them. When Mau asks if she wants the other half of his she says yes and takes it before he can finish measuring where half would be.

* * *

Ori finds her in the north wing an hour later and tells her to come to the orchard. She says it almost casually. There is a high black collar sticking out of her satchel and when she turns to show Makima the way, one of the sleeves trails halfway to the floor.

“I think you've left part of the thing you need to fetch,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

“You aren't allowed to know it's that.” ᴼ

Makima takes the sleeve and feeds it back into the bag. Ori walks more slowly after that, so she won't need to be told twice.

They've cleared the grass beside Kamish's tree. Chloe has put a chair there and set a board across the arms, with two saucers and a pile of squares of paper on it. Suika stands behind the chair. Her coat is fastened at the throat around the helmet somehow. On either side of her are Coco and Bocchi. Coco has ink on her cuffs. Bocchi has her hands inside hers. Rimuru, who thought there would be tea, has come in the little Agent Bird coat. He looks absurdly pleased that it still fits.

Makima stops at the last tree. Milim puts a hand out in front of her.

“Wait until you're called,” says Milim. ᴹⁱ

“Am I in trouble?” asks Makima. ᴹᵏ

Milim thinks about it. Her sleeve is a little short now. The square on it says AGENT DRAGON in ink that has started to run. “Do you like apples?” ᴹⁱ

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

“Then probably not.” ᴹⁱ

Umaru emerges from behind the trunk with the lid from the Fort bell. “Agent Dragon, we agreed you weren't going to tell her there was a snack.” ᵁ

“I didn't say there was a snack.” ᴹⁱ

“You gave her enough information to work it out.” ᵁ

Makima has been in front of people who lined up for her before. At the edge of the little clearing she counts the places by habit. There is one more cup than there are people. When Ori unrolls the coat, it has somebody else's hem and two bright new buttons. One of the sleeves has been repaired at the wrist. She's been standing for a while looking at the stitching by the time Ori asks if she'd like to put it on.

“The President said yes,” says Ori. “We asked Agott too.” ᴼ

“I said yes to the person,” says Agott, from the other side of the board. “The title is still in dispute.” ᴬᵍ

Ori hands Makima the coat. “I wanted Agent Control.” ᴼ

“Red fits on the paper better,” Chloe says. She holds up a square so they can all see the problem. ᶜʰ

Coco gets her pen out. “I can write smaller.” ᶜᵒ

“Control,” says Makima. “If that's what Ori wanted.” ᴹᵏ

Coco sets to work. Agott leans across to see how much of the paper it takes. Ori stands straight long enough to pin it on. Then she goes round beside Makima to get a look at the result from the same angle.

“What do agents do?” Makima asks. ᴹᵏ

For the first time since she got here, all the agents look at each other. Suika raises a hand and says they help the Fort. Rimuru demonstrates that he has held a title for years and been allowed to carry an extraordinary number of bowls. Umaru says that is a very important part of the office. Agott reaches across the board for her apple.

“The title doesn't give you any authority,” she says. ᴬᵍ

Milim nods. “You can help carry the chairs when we're done.” ᴹⁱ

There are only two chairs. Makima puts on the coat. She is halfway through the first sleeve when Bocchi holds the other one up far enough for her to get her hand through. When the cuff comes down it stops exactly at her wrist.

* * *

Mau comes looking for her as the apples run out. He'd finally got his chair back from Gojo and wanted to know if she felt like sitting with him. She sees him first through two branches. He has the little paper from his sweet folded into a triangle in his hand. He gets close enough to show her what he's made out of it.

“Have you got a minute?” he asks. ᴹ

Her mouth is already open when Ori appears from behind the chair. They were supposed to take the coats in together and find a hook for the new one. Ori had asked when Makima first put it on, very quietly, in case Makima would rather carry it straight to her room.

“I said I'd go with Ori,” Makima says. It costs her enough that she can hear the time it takes to finish. “You can come too. We're going to find out who left a drawer full of spoons where the coats should go.” ᴹᵏ

“The spoons have been there since the storm,” says Mau. ᴹ

“You're coming,” says Ori. ᴼ

“I can't. Yuka's waiting to see what I make of Mai's clasp. You can tell me what you find, though.” He looks down at Makima's lapel. He has to turn his head a little to read all the way to the last letter. “That fits.” ᴹ

She thinks he means the name until he smooths the cuff where she hasn't managed to button it. By the time she works out what to say back, Ori has one end of a chair and is waiting for her to take the other. Makima lifts it. They carry it between them to the house, turning farther around the trees than they have to because it keeps Ori's end out of the mud.

* * *

Later, after Gojo leaves and the sun goes behind the roof, Mau finds Makima at the newly cleared hooks. Ori has gone to show Frieren the place on the map where Pickles helped her up. Makima is getting a coat down. It's his. He thought about bringing it with him to find her and left it on the peg because he had both hands in his pockets.

“Are you free now?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” She brings the coat outside with him. “I wanted to sit with you when you came.” ᴹᵏ

He puts one arm in without getting up and has to have her fish the collar out from under him. He has an empty step on either side. He pats the one where the stone's still warm.

They sit there while he does up two of the buttons. The third catches. Makima gets it through from her side, and keeps her hand on the front of the coat after she's finished. She tries to decide from the way he's sitting whether this would be a good time to ask. His eyes are on the hook visible through the open window. He's telling her how he had to read her entire label twice before Ori would let him take his coat down.

When he finishes she says, “Can I hold you for a minute?” ᴹᵏ

He looks round. She holds her breath as he shifts his elbow. He plants it on his knee and turns until he's close enough to lean all the way into her.

“Like this?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

He gets an arm round her too. She lays her cheek against his hair like she did in the clinic. The coat bunches at his shoulder. She starts to put it right, feels him sink farther against her, and gives up on the collar.

“You can ask me again sometime,” he says. He stays where he is to say it. ᴹ

In the yard Pickles still has a piece of grass caught at the elbow. Mau shows her which arm when Shuna comes out to call them in. Makima has to lift her cheek off his head to see it. She puts it back for the length of one more breath before they get up for supper.

* * *

At supper the pear Dabura brought from the valley is too hard to cut. Shuna takes it out of Mau's hand and promises to poach it tomorrow if Dabura will show her where he found the tree.

“With Kamish?” asks Dabura. ᴰᵇ

“Ask him before you go this time,” says Kamish, from beyond the open doors. ᴷ

Mau's laugh comes out through his nose. Yuka puts an arm behind his back, laughing because he is, and leaves it there after he gets his breath. At the other end of the table Ori is explaining the map to Emilia, who would like to know if there's a rule about flying over it. In the middle of it all, Mau tries to thank them. He gets as far as the words *these past few weeks* and puts his hand over his face.

He's still holding Yuka's other hand. She rubs her thumb over his knuckle once. Ori waits for a space in the noise.

“Dad can just eat,” she says. ᴼ

Somebody asks her how soon the pear will be ready. She tells them it belongs to Dad. Then she hears herself say it, looks at Mau, and adds, “Dad can share the pear.” ᴼ

“He'd better,” says Yuka. She's almost in his lap. He moves his plate to let her finish coming over. Tomorrow he wants to be in the kitchen to see what Shuna does with the fruit.

Tonight there's a carved dragon on his pillow. Under it, a piece torn from the edge of the map, with a line in Ori's handwriting.

*A lend. For luck. As long as you need.*
