# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 85
## Chapter 27 — Where It Sits

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 85
**Reading order:** 27
**Source episode:** S13E149
**Continuity state:** B+485 ("2"), between Mau's day off and the house's supper discussion tomorrow. Mai fits the clasp she gave Mau, using Maki's pin to test the fold of his cloak. A passing shower sends Mau, Yuka and the Fort under a canvas that the Fort tests as a shelter for future expeditions. The clasp is ordinary metalwork and the canvas stays at the Fort. Ori's carved dragon is still on loan to Mau.

*When: B+485. "2." Ori has drawn three raindrops, then crossed one out. She maintains that the third one was on the inside.*

The clasp fastens perfectly while Mau stands still.

“Now raise your arm,” says Mai. ᴹᵃ

He raises it. The edge of the cloak comes up under his chin and puts pressure on exactly the place it used to. Mai sees him swallow and clicks her tongue.

“I can wear it like that,” he says. ᴹ

“I know what you can wear. I'm trying to make something you'd keep on.” ᴹᵃ

She unclips it. Across the room, Maki looks up from the vise, where she's trying to get the head on her thirty-second pin as round as the one on the thirty-first. Mau opens his hand. The thirty-first pin is in it. He'd brought it out of his inventory when he saw Maki at the bench, because with the way she gave it to him yesterday he hadn't had time to ask her how she got the end so smooth.

“Can I use it?” asks Mai. ᴹᵃ

He holds it out to her. Maki looks back down at the vise, a little too quickly.

Mai folds the cloth below his shoulder and sets the fold with the pin. One millimetre between where she'd put the clasp the first time and where she puts it now, perhaps two. When he raises his arm, the cloth lifts through the fold and leaves his throat alone.

“Oh,” says Mau. ᴹ

“Now try the other one.” ᴹᵃ

He puts both arms up. Yuka comes in just in time to catch him doing it.

“Do I need to go round the front?” she asks. ʸ

“What for?” ᴹ

She walks into the space he's left open, slips her arms around him from behind, and rests her chin on the shoulder Mai has just finished fitting. He brings both arms down over hers.

“That seemed like an invitation.” ʸ

Mai crouches to look at the way the cloak hangs across both of them. “Pull on it.” ᴹᵃ

Mau feels Yuka's laugh before he hears it. She squeezes him with one arm and tugs the cloak back by the other. The pin keeps the fold. The clasp sits flat. Mau can still turn his head far enough to kiss her, so he does, looking sideways at her until she comes the last inch herself.

“Does the pin have to come out?” he asks Mai when they part. ᴹ

Maki sets the file down.

“You've got a whole shop,” she says. ᴹᵃᵏ

“Yeah. Does the pin have to come out?” ᴹ

Mai looks from him to Maki. Maki gets up and comes round the vise. She lays the tip of her finger against the little head, visible for the moment under the edge of the cloak.

“You've got it going the wrong way,” she tells Mai. “Turn the point in. Then he won't snag it when he takes it off.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Mai gives her the pin and holds the cloth for her.

* * *

By the time they get outside, the Fort has taken up most of the path with a length of canvas.

It is lying flat over Ori, who is somewhere underneath it issuing orders. Umaru has a corner in each hand. Milim has both of the other ones and is attempting to determine whether the whole thing would fly if she let go of only one.

“It won't,” says Umaru. ᵁ

“How do you know?” ᴹⁱ

The canvas rises. Ori's head appears in the new opening.

“That was a good height. Do that again, please.” ᴼ

Mau gets as far as taking a step toward the nearest sagging edge. Yuka catches him around the elbow.

“Do you want help,” she calls to Ori, “or do you want us to see if it works?” ʸ

Ori thinks about this. A drop of water strikes the canvas behind her head. Then another. The stone between their shoes turns dark one speck at a time.

“Both. Get underneath.” ᴼ

Mau ducks in. Yuka follows, one palm pressed to the middle of his back, steering him out of the path of Milim's boot. Beru appears at the far side, sets two crates down for seats, and holds the middle of the canvas up with the heel of his hand. The rain comes harder. It makes so much noise overhead that Mau can't hear what Umaru says to Milim, but he sees them both lower the corners they were holding. Water runs down a fold and tumbles out clear of the crates. Ori watches it. Her boots are dry.

“It's for lunch,” she explains. “Expedition three. We had the tent on two. By the time it's up, there's no point in having lunch.” ᴼ

“A place to sit,” Mau says. ᴹ

“And put the map.” ᴼ

Yuka sits down on the wider of the crates and catches Mau by a fold of his sleeve. He expects her to slide over. She plants both feet against the stones and pulls until he's sitting between her knees, with his back to her. Her arms settle around his ribs and her chin comes down against his shoulder again.

“I'll hold this end,” she says. “You work out lunch.” ʸ

His first thought is the Shop. His second is that Shuna put the rest of yesterday's loaf in his hands on the way out of the kitchen, and he still has it.

He takes the loaf out of inventory. Umaru looks so visibly disappointed by the shape of it that he adds a bag of crisps and sets it on her knee. By then Ori has found the jar of jam that Rem sent out, and Milim has torn off the two ends of the loaf and left them in the wrong people's hands. There is a great deal of traffic below the canvas for something with nowhere to go.

One cold drop lands on Mau's wrist.

“Inside,” he says, showing it to Ori. ᴹ

She comes to see where it fell. While she's working back up to the seam, Yuka takes his wrist, wipes it dry with the corner of her cuff and kisses it.

“Now you have to find another one,” she tells Ori. ʸ

“There are plenty,” says Ori, her head bent so far back she nearly loses her hat. ᴼ

Mau laughs. It shakes Yuka against his back. He sets his head down against the side of hers and she goes quiet, just for a few seconds, with her nose pressed into his hair.

* * *

They get the angles right before the rain lets up. Ori marks the fold with a bit of string, and Beru shows her where he's been holding it so she can mark that too. Umaru insists that lending one more hand would get her wet and makes Milim carry her back over the puddles. She drops the crisp bag on the last jump. Rem, who has come to the door to see what all the noise is, catches the bag cleanly in one hand.

“I meant to do that,” says Umaru. ᵁ

“Of course,” says Rem. She looks inside the bag and takes a crisp. ᴿᵉ

Yuka gets up off the crate. Mau turns when he feels her arms leave him, in time to see her put one foot on the wet path, then pull it back to the dry edge.

“Need a ride?” he asks. ᴹ

“Are you offering?” ʸ

He bends a little. She climbs onto his back without a moment's thought and tucks both feet under his arms. He walks the long way to the door, round the big puddle and past the workshop, so she has time to put her cheek down on his shoulder again.

Mai is still by the window. She takes one look at them and opens it with the heel of her hand.

“How's it sitting?” ᴹᵃ

Mau turns so she can see. The clasp has held. Maki's pin is hidden in the fold, its point tucked away from Yuka's sleeve. She reaches up and feels the top of the clasp with a finger.

“I think we're going to keep it on,” she says. ʸ

“Both of you, apparently,” says Mai. She pulls the window shut against the last of the rain. ᴹᵃ
