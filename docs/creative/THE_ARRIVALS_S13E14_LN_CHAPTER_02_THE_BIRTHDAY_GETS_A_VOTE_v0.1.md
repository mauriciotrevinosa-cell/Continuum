# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 3 — The Birthday Gets a Vote

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S13E14
**Continuity state:** B+292, Ori spends the morning with Frieren, Mau and Yuka before the house presents her with birthday options.

*When: The morning before the island.*

Ori wakes with one foot out of the blanket. She puts it back in and closes her eyes, then opens them again because there's a good strip of sun across the floor and she wants to see if it's warm all the way to the window. It is. She sits in it for a while with her knees pulled up under her nightshirt. Through the glass she can see one person at the tree, standing perfectly still and facing away.

It's Beru. He's holding something behind his back. He keeps turning his head just far enough to find the window and then whipping it forward again. She stands where she is until he notices her. The moment he does, he holds the thing behind his back with both hands and starts examining a branch that is much too high to interest him.

She puts on the first shirt in the drawer, looks down and finds out it's the one with jam on the cuff. She remembers she wanted to wear the one with the deep pockets today. She puts the first shirt back on the chair and goes to find her mother with both hands in the new pockets, checking whether they feel as deep as she remembers.

Frieren is sitting on the edge of her bed with a cup under her nose. She has one sock on. Ori stands in the doorway for a second. Then Frieren sets the cup down and moves her arm, and Ori goes over and fits herself against her side.

“Beru's outside,” Ori says. ᴼ

“He wanted to be the first person you saw.” ᶠ

“I saw him out the window.” ᴼ

Frieren looks down at her bare foot. Ori follows her eyes and spots the other sock hanging from the back of the chair. She fishes it out by the toe and hands it over.

“Good. He was out there when I got up for water.” ᶠ

Ori rests her head on her shoulder. Frieren takes a little longer to get the sock on that way. When she's done, she kisses Ori behind one ear and says happy birthday so quietly that Ori feels it against her skin before she hears it.

Ori has known what day it is since she put her foot back under the blanket. Hearing her mother say it makes her want to stay here a little longer. She slides one foot up beside Frieren's on the bed and asks her to say it again. Frieren does, a little louder this time, and Ori laughs because she says it in exactly the same voice.

They go down the first flight of stairs together. From the landing Ori can see Himmel crossing the courtyard. He has a little pot in one hand and stops to move something off the bench with the other. Frieren leans against the rail and waits until he looks up. He waves with the pot. She waves back with the hand Ori isn't holding.

“I'm going to go say good morning. Do you want to come?” ᶠ

Ori looks down the hall toward Mau and Yuka's door. There's a narrow line of light under it.

“Can I see if they're up first?” ᴼ

“Go ahead. I'll meet you downstairs.” ᶠ

Ori gets halfway down the hall and turns in time to see Frieren reach the courtyard. Himmel makes room on the bench, though Frieren is only going to say good morning. Ori watches her sit down beside him and decides she can take a little time too.

She knocks twice. Yuka says to come in before she gets to the third one. There's a little sand on the mat by the door, which seems odd until she sees the sandals Yuka is trying to fasten. One has a crooked buckle. Mau is kneeling in front of her with one end of the strap in his hand.

“Can you fix it?” Ori asks him. ᴼ

He looks up so fast he lets the strap go. Yuka catches it just before the sandal drops. Ori sees his mouth open and waits to hear what he was going to say.

“Good morning,” he says. ᴹ

“Good morning.” ᴼ

Ori sits on the edge of the bed beside Yuka. She puts a finger through the little loop on the strap while Mau tries the buckle again. Between them they get it through. Yuka tests it with her foot on the floor and decides she can walk as far as the stairs in it. She puts the other pair on anyway. Mau asks Ori if she wants him to leave the crooked one on the mat so she can see if Mai knows what to do with it later. Ori says yes, because she wants to see whether Mai tries the buckle first or already knows to look at the strap.

The three of them head down together. Ori has one hand in her pocket and the other in Mau's. At the bottom of the stairs there's a smell of bread warm enough to make her hurry the last two steps. Yuka catches up with them just before the doorway and bends down so Ori can hear her.

“He was dying to wish you happy birthday before you got downstairs. He got as far as your door and came back because he thought you might still be asleep.” ʸ

Ori looks up at him. He has exactly the face he made when she first walked in on him with the buckle.

“I'm awake now,” she says. ᴼ

“I noticed.” He bends down and holds out his arms. “Happy birthday, Ori.” ᴹ

She lets him hug her in the doorway. Yuka has to step around both of them to get through. She puts a hand on Ori's back as she passes and says happy birthday right into her hair. By the time Ori straightens up, Frieren and Himmel have come in from the courtyard and there's a chair waiting for her beside her mother. She goes to it without taking her hand out of Mau's until the table comes between them.

People start coming down. Chloe has two ribbons over one shoulder. Rem brings bread from the oven and pushes the jar of jam all the way across the table so Ori can put on however much she wants. There's a tray covered with a towel beside it. Shizue has the students behind her, still deciding which one of them gets to bring over the things they made. Beru manages to get back in the house between Kenya and Gale. He is wearing a folded paper hat. The thing he's been hiding is a smaller one. Ori puts it on, though he got the size wrong and she has to move it off her eyes to see who's talking.

Umaru finds her feet at the end of the table.

“We all came up with something to do today. We made plans for it while you were sleeping. You get to pick which one we actually do.” ᵁ

“You already made them?” ᴼ

“They're ready if you want them.” ᵁ

Taihei folds his arms. Ori catches him looking at the towel on the tray.

“Can I still eat that if I don't pick yours?” ᴼ

“You can eat that before you pick anything,” he says. ᵗᵃⁱʰᵉⁱ

They show her the choices as she eats. Chloe and Shizue's students have made a map from the Fort to the tree with clues that have to be read by different people, and a place marked for Ori to add the last clue herself. The kitchen has enough dough to make a long line of cakes and draw on them. Himmel says there is a patch of flat stones by the stream that are good for painting and he will go and sit there for as long as she likes. Mai has thought of building a set of little chairs that fit whoever sits in them. She is willing to call that one a plan if Ori agrees to keep the first chair.

Milim has had her turn next since before Ori put the first piece of bread in her mouth. She's sitting on her hands. Ori asks her to go ahead.

“We could put blankets all the way around the tree. Wherever you say. We'll take food out to each one, and you can draw a way around them, and I'll do all the carrying. And if anybody wants to add a place we have to ask you.” ᴹⁱ

“Can the other plans go on the blankets?” ᴼ

Milim looks across the table at Chloe. Chloe unfolds her map and sets it down between them. The part where she drew the tree is big enough to draw more things around it.

“If you want them there,” Milim says. ᴹⁱ

“Then I want to do yours.” ᴼ

Ori has just enough time to put the bread down. Milim gets an arm around her waist and lifts her straight out of the chair. From up there Ori can see the towel slid half off the tray and the one crooked cake on it with the corner Taihei meant to hide.

“Put her down, Milim.” ᶠ

“Yes. Sorry.” ᴹⁱ

She sets Ori down as carefully as she lifted her quickly, her hands coming off one at a time. Ori leans into her.

“It's all right. Can I have a hug before we go out?” ᴼ

Milim hugs her with her feet still on the floor. Ori holds on until Milim quits being careful about smiling, too.

Mau is in the courtyard when she goes out to pick a place for the first blanket. He has brought two folded ones and he walks where she walks, stopping whenever she checks the branches. When they find a patch that has shade enough for lunch, he kneels and gives her his end.

“Did you have a plan too?” she asks. ᴼ

“I have somebody who wanted to see you.” ᴹ

“After we pick all the places?” ᴼ

“He'd like to help you pick them, if you say he can.” ᴹ

Ori grabs two corners of the blanket. She flaps it once, the way she's seen Rem do, and it nearly covers Mau's head. He catches the edge without getting up.

“Tell him he can come.” ᴼ
