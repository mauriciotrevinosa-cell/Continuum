# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 86
## Chapter 1 — When the Cart Comes Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 86
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S13E151
**Continuity state:** B+494, seven days after *Not Yet*. Asa carries the first notice. The date is B+508 at dawn, fourteen days away. Yoru will give the location herself a week in advance. The promise made in B+487 is still in force. At the northern ford, Mau wants to help Yuka with the work and has trouble trusting the morning after. They make a small plan he can ask for himself.

*When: B+494. Morning at the northern ford, then home.*

Yuka loses two loaves of bread before the cart gets to the ford. The first goes to Ori, who insists she has seen Beru look at it three times. The second goes to the woman who patched the cover. She has brought a knife of her own and cuts it on the end of the cart without undoing the cloth underneath.

“Has the smaller one got nuts in it?” she asks. ᴺᵖ

Mau puts his hand into the basket for it. Yuka catches the heel of his hand before it goes all the way down.

“That one's mine.” ʸ

“I was checking it for nuts.” ᴹ

“I can check it from here.” ʸ

She takes half the crust without pulling the loaf out of its cloth. He holds the basket still while she does it. The last time they brought food out, he spent most of the trip thinking about what she'd meant when she said they'd go home together. This morning he gets to hear her argue over whether bread with nuts in it is still bread. The woman takes Yuka's side because the half that just got broken off is obviously cake.

Maru is down by the water with Rimuru, trying to keep a peg from coming loose under the planks. The stream is up past the marks everybody agreed to use. Somebody is going to have to stand in it to get the ropes back under the first board. When Mau puts the basket down, Yuka gets to the bank beside him at the same time.

“I'm not doing this in my shoes.” She pulls one heel out. “How much of that board can you hold if Rimuru lets it go?” ʸ

“All of it. Can you guide the end by the bank?” ᴹ

“Don't move it till I tell you.” ʸ

She leaves her shoes pointing down the slope. That way the sun can get inside them. He knows what she's doing. She showed him how to put a pair down like that on the island and waited until he'd done it properly before she set off for the water.

He keeps the board level while she wades out. Under the sound of the water there's the tick of a branch caught beneath the far end. Noise puts what he can feel of the current on the edge of Mau's attention. He lifts the board half an inch and the branch comes out. Yuka puts a bare foot down on a stone and calls up to tell him exactly where to stop.

Mau holds.

It's easy enough to do. He can feel the middle of the board and the wet part of the rope she's bringing round it. He looks up to see how much farther she has to go. For an instant he sees the two shoes on the bank and has to know why she's left them there. The board dips. He has it back up again before the rope gets wet.

Yuka lifts her head. “Both feet here. I've got the end.” ʸ

He looks where she puts one foot back against the current. There it is. There's a bit of pale wood stuck to the sole and it stays stuck when she tries to shake it off against the rock.

“Tell me when you need it down.” ᴹ

She tells him. He can listen to the whole direction this time. When she's up on the bank again Rimuru gives her the cloth he was going to dry his hands on. She brings it round both her feet and stands on it like a towel in a bath.

“Do we have another half loaf?” she asks. ʸ

Mau comes over with the basket. While she dries her feet he stands close enough to keep the sun off her eyes and holds both halves of the loaf until she decides which is bigger.

* * *

Asa is at the gate when they return.

She has the pack Mau gave her over one shoulder so she can get it off quickly. A length of green thread goes round the place where the strap is starting to fray. She has tried to mend it from both sides. When Mau puts the cart handle down, she straightens and the repair takes the weight.

“Is that holding?” he asks. ᴹ

“I think so. I had to start again twice.” ᴬˢ

Ori climbs off the back of the cart. She has one hand spread on Beru's shoulder so she can reach the ground without stepping on anything they've brought home.

“Have you come to eat?” she asks. ᴼ

Asa looks at the house. “I came to tell your father when. Is it all right if I tell you the same thing?” ᴬˢ

Ori looks at him. He can see her working out whether she'll have to bring the notebook. He gives her a nod before she's made up her mind about going to get it.

“Fourteen days from today,” says Asa. “At dawn. Yoru's going to come back herself next week to say where it is. I thought you should be able to count all fourteen.” ᴬˢ

Mau waits a second for the small change in her face that means someone else wants to use it. Asa stands very straight and holds her strap. He can still see the green thread.

“Are you coming back with her next week?” he asks. ᴹ

“I want to. She likes having a question only she knows the answer to.” Asa puts the pack down. “If she changes the day again, do you want me to tell you as soon as I know?” ᴬˢ

“Yes, please.” ᴹ

He thinks of looking for somebody who hasn't come to a place he picked out in advance. Asa's bag sits in the dust. She's waiting for an answer to a different question, or somebody to tell her she can put the bag somewhere cleaner. Yuka takes the unused cloth from the bread basket and puts it on the cart bed.

“Shuna was working on a strap just like that this morning. If you've got a few minutes, you can ask her what to do when the thread runs out.” ʸ

“Can I just watch her do it once?” ᴬˢ

“You can ask.” Yuka waits until Asa lifts the pack before she gathers up the cloth to go in with her. ʸ

* * *

Mau finds Ori at the table after lunch. She has fourteen marks down the page and enough space to write next to each of them. The first one is already very dark.

“Do I need to be ready for something tomorrow?” she asks him. ᴼ

Mau gets a chair on the same side of the table. “Tomorrow I told the people at the ford I'd be back with the cart.” ᴹ

“I know. Do you want me to come too?” ᴼ

“I do if you've got time.” ᴹ

She reaches past his elbow to put the pencil right through the last line of four marks. “You can have me till lunch. I'm busy after.” ᴼ

“That's generous.” ᴹ

“It is.” ᴼ

She leaves him the pencil when she goes to find Frieren. He adds *cart* next to the first mark. He gets as far as the little space underneath it before he hears Yuka coming with two cups.

She puts his by the hand he doesn't need for writing. “Keep going. I can sit without you looking at me.” ʸ

He looks at the word he just wrote. There's enough room underneath for a thing he wants, separate from what he has promised.

“Will you come too?” he asks. ᴹ

“Tomorrow?” ʸ

He nods. “I wanted another of those mornings.” ᴹ

Yuka sits down. “I'd like another one too.” ʸ

He writes *Yuka* below *cart*. She presses her palm flat on the table close to his. He takes the time he needs to reach over. Once he's holding her hand she turns the page far enough for both of them to see where Ori has drawn tomorrow.
