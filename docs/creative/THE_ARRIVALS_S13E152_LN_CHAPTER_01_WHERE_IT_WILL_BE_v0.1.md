# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 86
## Chapter 2 — Where It Will Be

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 86
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S13E152
**Continuity state:** B+501. Seven days after Asa's initial notice. Yoru keeps B+508 at dawn and brings the location, a dry lakebed beyond the basin. The terms and the fight remain ahead. Mau and Yuka are living together. He can tell her that he is frightened and still cannot talk through what she said about marrying him.

*When: B+501. Late afternoon.*

The repaired cart comes in without a squeak. There are four people on the porch who want to know if it means Senku has finally found the bad joint or someone bought a new set of wheels and let him take the credit. Rimuru is the one who climbs underneath to find out. He stays there long enough that Milim brings him his drink and asks if he lives down there now.

Mau sets the empty baskets inside. A few weeks ago, he'd have gone up to get clean without anybody having to ask where he was going. Today Shuna hands him a piece of cloth and asks how soon he wants to stop smelling like the river.

“Soon,” he says. ᴹ

“Tell us when you've got a time, then. We can all stand outside until it's safe.” ˢʰᵘ

Yuka is sitting on the edge of the table with one bare foot stuck through the handle of a basket. She puts the basket down when he looks at it. He goes over to see what else she's got caught round her feet. It turns out she set two things aside while they unloaded the cart: a clean towel long enough for both of them and a tiny pear from Dabura's pocket. He gets as far as taking one of them.

“That one's for Ori,” she says. ʸ

He gives her the pear. She gives him the towel. He bends until she can put the pear in his shirt pocket and he doesn't lift his head right away. Her hand comes up behind his neck where there's still a strip of warm sun on it.

“You still have mud past your ear.” ʸ

“I know,” he says. ᴹ

She leaves her hand there. In the yard the new wheel goes round once. It makes a small sound where it meets the gravel, and someone he can almost see moves a boot on the slope above him.

His forehead touches hers. He'd been looking just a little too far over her shoulder. It's a long way back from where he was looking to the hand she has behind his neck. He gets there by the time she asks if she should move.

“Can you hold it a second longer?” he says. ᴹ

He keeps his eyes open while she does. He doesn't have to see the whole room. There's a spot on the wall that gets a little brighter when the cart is out of the way. When he can see the spot change he moves just enough to get his whole face into the side of her hair.

“Are you afraid of next week?” she asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

He says it too close to her ear. She hears it twice, first in the word and then in the breath he hadn't expected to spend on it.

“I wish I'd asked you to do what you just did earlier,” he says. ᴹ

“Today?” ʸ

He turns his head. She has her fingers in the short part of his hair. He can feel the band, cool where it passes over his skin. She did say *maybe* that night. He'll spend the whole afternoon hearing nothing else if he tries to do this with the rest of the kitchen listening over their cups.

“Before all this I used to know which side of the house you'd be on when I came back.” He lets her see him look down. “I'd like to talk about that night. When I can get past the first thing you said.” ᴹ

She uses the clean end of the towel to get the mud off behind his ear. Then she gives him the rest of it and lets him stand where he is while he unfolds it.

* * *

Ciel tells him someone's coming up the hill just as Rimuru gets from under the cart. Mau goes out in his clean shirt. He has buttons in two different holes and a towel thrown over his shoulder. Yuka catches up at the foot of the steps to fix the buttons that matter.

“At least you waited until I had it on,” he says. ᴹ

She does the second one very slowly, in case he has another thing to say.

They hear Beru before they get to the gate. He has Ori's notebook in his hands, flat across his palms, waiting for her to decide how much of the conversation should go in it. Behind him, Gojo looks over the road and leaves the gate shut. Yoru stops on the far side. Asa's pack is slung over one shoulder. The strap has a new patch the colour of the thread Asa had chosen.

Yoru sees where Mau is looking. “She wanted to keep the green.” ʷᵈ

“It's a good green,” says Ori. ᴼ

Yoru looks at the child and then at the thread, as if it has just put an argument together without her.

“Can you still count seven?” she asks Mau. ʷᵈ

“I can. Asa gave us fourteen.” ᴹ

“I know. She was insufferable about it. I thought she was going to stand on my foot all the way up here if I changed the hour.” ʷᵈ

The road is quiet. Nobody has brought anybody else behind her. Makima stands a little farther back than Mau can see without turning. Even so, he can pick out the way she stops alongside the right gatepost. Inside him Noise has a scout's picture of the ridge ready. Ciel waits with it until he needs it.

Yoru rests her hand on the latch without trying it. “There was a lake once, beyond the basin where you met those hungry dogs. You can see the old waterline on the rock if you're coming from the hills. Where it's dry now.” ʷᵈ

Mau has seen it on two of Bellion's maps. He's never needed to go farther than the rim. He takes long enough to see that it's the same lake on both maps, the one with the dark curve at the far end. Rimuru comes up at his shoulder to see if he wants the name on their map.

Yuka asks Yoru, “Is anyone living above it?” ʸ

Yoru gives her a look he remembers very well from the last time she had a reason to talk directly to his wife. Yuka lets her finish taking it. Her hand stays down at her side. She has asked a question she wants answered and has time to wait for it.

“There's nothing at the lake. I picked it because you can tell all these people where it is without having to empty a village to get there,” Yoru says. ʷᵈ

“We'll check,” says Rimuru. ᴿ

“Of course you will.” ʷᵈ

Mau thinks of the name on the map. His head is full of another place he can find now if he asks for it. He only needs the one they're talking about.

“The lakebed,” he says. ᴹ

“At dawn. One week.” Yoru takes her hand off the latch. ʷᵈ

Ori says all four words over before she writes them down.
