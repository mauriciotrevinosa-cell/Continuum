# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 86
## Chapter 4 — The Third Hook

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 86
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S13E153
**Continuity state:** B+503. The house gets the first replies from the old lake road. Makima helps Ori and Chloe sort the Fort's spare coats. Mau comes looking for Makima by choice to talk through something he wants to ask Yoru. Makima holds him as she held him in the clinic. They say they love each other again. The meaning of Mau's feelings remains open.

*When: B+503. The north wing smells of wet wool.*

There are coats on every hook in the north wing except the third. That's the one nobody wants.

Makima takes her own down to see what's underneath it. Ori made a label for the peg, held down on one side by a drawing pin and on the other by a piece of cloth folded over the edge. The label is long enough to reach the floor if someone straightens it. Makima reads *CONTROL* three times with progressively smaller letters. At the bottom somebody has drawn a hand with a cup in it. She thinks the hand is supposed to be hers.

Chloe comes past with two coats on hangers. One drags behind her. She waits for Makima to lift the sleeve before she goes any farther.

“Who gets the hook without a label?” asks Makima. ᴹᵏ

“That one sticks out too far,” says Chloe. “Coco caught her eye on it last winter. We tried hanging a glove over it but people kept taking the glove.” ᶜʰ

At the other end of the hall Ori has her feet braced against a trunk, trying to lift the lid around the coat she's got shut in it. Makima lays her own coat across the clean end and lifts the lid far enough for Ori to get her hand out.

“I thought if I took them all out we'd find the good ones,” Ori says. ᴼ

“We did.” Chloe shows her the one that isn't dragging. “This was under the bottom.” ᶜʰ

Ori recognises it and turns the label up to the light. Agent Pink. There is a little pocket stitched into the lining from a repair Wakana made after Bocchi caught it coming down a ladder. Inside that, Ori finds a glass button and a strip of paper with guitar strings drawn on it. She wants to take it straight to Bocchi. Chloe points out that if they take it this very minute someone will have to stay and watch the trunk while the lid is open.

Makima looks from one of them to the other. “I can watch a trunk.” ᴹᵏ

Ori has the coat up in both hands. She gets it off the hanger and then has to decide whether she'll take the hanger too. “How long will you stay?” she asks. ᴼ

“Until you come back. Leave the hanger. It won't fit around Bocchi with her guitar.” ᴹᵏ

Ori leaves it. Chloe stops long enough to show Makima how to set the lid down on a folded sleeve so it won't fall the rest of the way. Then the two of them go, holding the coat between them like a thing they've found that could still get away.

Makima puts the hanger on the third hook. It sticks out far enough to take the whole weight of it without anyone having to reach behind the coats. She ties a scrap of red cloth around the tip so Coco can see it coming.

* * *

At the other end of the house Mau spends most of breakfast trying to find a reason not to eat the last slice of toast.

Yuka is watching him do it. Her hair is still damp from the basin. She has her knee against his under the table and is arranging a little pile of crumbs around his cup. Every time he moves the cup, she follows it with the crumbs.

“I'm going to have to wash all of that,” he says. ᴹ

“Eventually.” ʸ

“What would you like in exchange for stopping?” ᴹ

She looks at his toast and then at him. He lifts the toast so she can take the end she was after and bends to kiss her before she can use it to bargain for anything else. She laughs into his mouth. When he pulls back she's still smiling and has got the toast out of his hand as well.

“You were saying?” she says. ʸ

“I forget.” ᴹ

“I can give you another minute.” ʸ

He takes it. He's still close enough to feel her laugh a second time when Fern opens the back door and asks if either of them has seen Rimuru. Yuka points to the bench by the stove, where Rimuru has been sitting for several minutes with a letter from the farmer above the lake open on his knee.

The fence is his. He only uses the path twice a month. He knows a woman who collects stone out there and can see if she's planning to go before the week is up. Fern wants to write back and get a location for the stone she collects. Yuka reaches through Mau's arm to get the ink and puts it where Fern can write on the flat part of the table.

“If she's there on the day I want her to know well before she sees me,” says Mau. ᴹ

“Can you tell me how far you expect the boundary to go?” asks Fern. ᶠᵉ

“Once I know what's coming, yes.” ᴹ

He can tell her how far he'd be prepared to take it if there were nobody to think about. He's run that distance in his head enough times that the first steep shelf might as well already be in front of him. He knows he'd take the old waterline from the inside. He wants to know where Yoru would start. At some point on the walk back from the ford he began to look forward to finding that out.

Yuka takes her knee away from his to get up. “Will you come watch me this afternoon? Maki says the second part of the turn gets better if there's somebody there to tell me when I stop looking.” ʸ

“I'll come. I've got a question to ask Makima first.” ᴹ

“Okay.” She stops to get one of the crumbs off his cuff, then kisses the side of his face where there won't be butter. “I'll be the one on the grass.” ʸ

“With Maki?” ᴹ

“If I look up in time.” ʸ

He watches her leave with the last of his toast. Then he picks up both cups and goes looking for Makima.

* * *

He finds Ori first. She's coming back with Chloe and Bocchi, who is wearing her coat over one strap of the guitar case and keeps touching the lining. Ori tells him Agent Control is in her room. She says it like she's giving him the name of a person she expects him to know.

“I know where it is.” ᴹ

“She's probably waiting for me to come back with the good hanger anyway.” Ori looks at the cups. “You can tell her I found it.” ᴼ

Mau knocks. Makima has the door open far enough that he can see she's trying to find a place to put a little piece of cloth. He gives her the cup with the handle that gets hot last. She steps aside. The coat is folded over the back of the chair so the paper won't pull at the pin while it hangs. On one edge of the sill there's a flat green leaf and a scrap of paper with some measurements on it.

“I thought you might know whether Yoru would let Asa answer a question at the lake,” he says. ᴹ

Makima sits down on the near end of the bench. “In front of you?” ᴹᵏ

“I'd like to be able to ask Asa whether I can get her out if she needs it. I could ask before.” ᴹ

“Yoru would want to hear what she says too. Ask her where Asa can hear you. Let her find her own answer.” Makima sets her cup under the sill. “Are you asking because you're worried Asa will agree to something she doesn't want to do?” ᴹᵏ

“I'm asking because if it's Asa standing there I want to know who I'm fighting.” ᴹ

Makima looks up at him. His face changes very slightly while she's looking. It's the look he gives somebody he thinks understood before he was able to finish. He has a hand on the bench between them. She can feel how much he wants to put his weight on it.

She moves back against the wall and leaves him more room. He turns toward her. When she puts a hand against the back of his head, he knows where it's going to be before it touches. He puts his cup down, gets a knee along the bench and leans into her until his cheek is against her chest. He stays half up for the length of one breath. Then her hand comes all the way round his shoulders and he lets it take his weight.

She feels the little stretch of his arm across her back. The first time it happened he was so tired he had to say her name between breaths. He can move nearer now just because he wants to. He does. Her whole face goes warm at the speed of it and a second later she feels him notice.

“I love you,” she says. ᴹᵏ

It reaches him twice. He hears her voice, low and close to his ear. Then there's the other thing he can feel even after his eyes close. He knows she has been waiting to say the words since a long time before he knocked. He wants to hold her closer and he can feel how much she wants him to. It takes him a second to find the smallest thing he's completely sure of.

“I love you too,” he says. He says it into her shoulder and then turns his face enough that she can hear him where he is. ᴹ

She leans her cheek down onto his hair. Neither of them has to move until he does. Out in the corridor somebody hangs a coat up. It takes three tries before the hook takes its weight.

Mau opens his eyes. “I want another minute. Then tell me what you would ask Asa first.” ᴹ

Makima runs her thumb over the back of his head once. “I'd ask whether she wants to come back to the house when it's over.” ᴹᵏ

He stays still. He'd been ready to ask what he could do to get Asa out of the way before something happened. He can see her face when Ori asked about the thread. She may have had a whole different idea of where she was going after the lake.

“I like that question,” he says. ᴹ

His cup is cool by the time he lifts his head. He reaches for hers first, puts it where she can take it with the hand she's got free and finishes asking what he came to ask.

* * *

Yuka goes down in the grass before he gets to the path. Maki has one foot on either side of her and is asking her at what point she thought she'd been going left. Yuka sees Mau from the ground and waves at him with her whole arm.

“Was I looking?” she asks. ʸ

“Not at Maki.” ᴹ

“I was looking where I wanted to go.” ʸ

“I know. That's why I was there,” says Maki. ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuka sits up and catches Mau at the wrist before he has time to offer his hand. He helps her all the way up. She has grass in her hair, high enough that he has to turn her into the light to see how to get it out. He gets it, puts it in her hand and kisses her before he steps back.

“Where should I stand?” he asks. ᴹ

“Right here for another second.” ʸ

He stays. When she lets go and turns for the next try, he stands far enough out that she can see him at the end of the movement, and tells her exactly when her eyes left the person in front of her.
