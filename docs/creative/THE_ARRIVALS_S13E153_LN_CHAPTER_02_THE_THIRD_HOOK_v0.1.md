# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 86
## Chapter 4 — The Third Hook

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 86
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S13E153
**Continuity state:** B+503. The house gets the first replies from the old lake road. Makima helps Ori and Chloe sort the Fort's spare coats. Mau comes looking for Makima by choice to talk through something he wants to ask Yoru. Makima holds him as she held him in the clinic. Mau asks her to stay as she starts to get up. Makima kisses his forehead and he feels her love through the bond. They lean on each other and say they love each other again. They talk about their shared longevity, the first visible change of a form Makima found after the fusion, and ordinary plans. Mau's exact feelings remain open. He finds Yuka for the training he promised to watch.

*When: B+503. The north wing smells of wet wool.*

There are coats on every hook in the north wing except the third. That's the one nobody wants.

Makima takes her own down to see what's underneath it. Ori made a label for the peg, held down on one side by a drawing pin and on the other by a piece of cloth folded over the edge. The label is long enough to reach the floor if someone straightens it. Makima reads *CONTROL* three times with progressively smaller letters. At the bottom somebody has drawn a hand with a cup in it. She thinks the hand is supposed to be hers.

Chloe comes past with two coats on hangers. One drags behind her. She waits for Makima to lift the sleeve before she goes any farther.

“Who gets the hook without a label?” asks Makima. ᴹᵏ

“That one sticks out too far,” says Chloe. “Coco caught her eye on it last winter. We tried hanging a glove over it but people kept taking the glove.” ᶜʰ

At the other end of the hall Ori has her feet braced against a trunk, trying to lift the lid around the coat she's got shut in it. Makima lays her own coat across the clean end and lifts the lid far enough for Ori to get her hand out.

“I thought if I took them all out we'd find the good ones,” Ori says. ᴼ

“We did.” Chloe shows her the one that isn't dragging. “This was under the bottom.” ᶜʰ

Ori recognises it and turns the label up to the light. Agent Pink. There is a little pocket stitched into the lining from a repair Wakana made after Bocchi caught it coming down a ladder. Inside that, Ori finds a glass button and a strip of paper with guitar strings drawn on it. She wants to take it straight to Bocchi. Chloe points out that if they take it this very minute someone will have to stay and watch the trunk while the lid is open.

Makima looks from one of them to the other. “I can watch a trunk.” ᴹᵏ

Ori has the coat up in both hands. She gets it off the hanger and then has to decide whether she'll take the hanger too. “How long will you stay?” she asks. ᴼ

“Until you come back. Leave the hanger. It won't fit around Bocchi with her guitar.” ᴹᵏ

Ori leaves it. Chloe stops long enough to show Makima how to set the lid down on a folded sleeve so it won't fall the rest of the way. Then the two of them go, holding the coat between them like a thing they've found that could still get away.

Makima puts the hanger on the third hook. It sticks out far enough to take the whole weight of it without anyone having to reach behind the coats. She ties a scrap of red cloth around the tip so Coco can see it coming.

* * *

At the other end of the house Mau spends most of breakfast trying to find a reason not to eat the last slice of toast.

Yuka is watching him do it. Her hair is still damp from the basin. She has her knee against his under the table and is arranging a little pile of crumbs around his cup. Every time he moves the cup, she follows it with the crumbs.

“I'm going to have to wash all of that,” he says. ᴹ

“Eventually.” ʸ

“What would you like in exchange for stopping?” ᴹ

She looks at his toast and then at him. He lifts the toast so she can take the end she was after and bends to kiss her before she can use it to bargain for anything else. She laughs into his mouth. When he pulls back she's still smiling and has got the toast out of his hand as well.

“You were saying?” she says. ʸ

“I forget.” ᴹ

“I can give you another minute.” ʸ

He takes it. He's still close enough to feel her laugh a second time when Fern opens the back door and asks if either of them has seen Rimuru. Yuka points to the bench by the stove, where Rimuru has been sitting for several minutes with a letter from the farmer above the lake open on his knee.

The fence is his. He only uses the path twice a month. He knows a woman who collects stone out there and can see if she's planning to go before the week is up. Fern wants to write back and get a location for the stone she collects. Yuka reaches through Mau's arm to get the ink and puts it where Fern can write on the flat part of the table.

“If she's there on the day I want her to know well before she sees me,” says Mau. ᴹ

“Can you tell me how far you expect the boundary to go?” asks Fern. ᶠᵉ

“Once I know what's coming, yes.” ᴹ

He can tell her how far he'd be prepared to take it if there were nobody to think about. He's run that distance in his head enough times that the first steep shelf might as well already be in front of him. He knows he'd take the old waterline from the inside. He wants to know where Yoru would start. At some point on the walk back from the ford he began to look forward to finding that out.

Yuka takes her knee away from his to get up. “Will you come watch me this afternoon? Maki says the second part of the turn gets better if there's somebody there to tell me when I stop looking.” ʸ

“I'll come. I've got a question to ask Makima first.” ᴹ

“Okay.” She stops to get one of the crumbs off his cuff, then kisses the side of his face where there won't be butter. “I'll be the one on the grass.” ʸ

“With Maki?” ᴹ

“If I look up in time.” ʸ

He watches her leave with the last of his toast. Then he picks up both cups and goes looking for Makima.

* * *

He finds Ori first. She's coming back with Chloe and Bocchi, who is wearing her coat over one strap of the guitar case and keeps touching the lining. Ori tells him Agent Control is in her room. She says it like she's giving him the name of a person she expects him to know.

“I know where it is.” ᴹ

“She's probably waiting for me to come back with the good hanger anyway.” Ori looks at the cups. “You can tell her I found it.” ᴼ

Mau knocks. Makima has the door open far enough that he can see she's trying to find a place to put a little piece of cloth. He gives her the cup with the handle that gets hot last. She steps aside. The coat is folded over the back of the chair so the paper won't pull at the pin while it hangs. On one edge of the sill there's a flat green leaf and a scrap of paper with some measurements on it.

“I thought you might know whether Yoru would let Asa answer a question at the lake,” he says. ᴹ

Makima sits down on the near end of the bench. “In front of you?” ᴹᵏ

“I'd like to be able to ask Asa whether I can get her out if she needs it. I could ask before.” ᴹ

“Yoru would want to hear what she says too. Ask her where Asa can hear you. Let her find her own answer.” Makima sets her cup under the sill. “Are you asking because you're worried Asa will agree to something she doesn't want to do?” ᴹᵏ

“I'm asking because if it's Asa standing there I want to know who I'm fighting.” ᴹ

Makima looks up at him. His face changes very slightly while she's looking. It's the look he gives somebody he thinks understood before he was able to finish. He has a hand on the bench between them. She can feel how much he wants to put his weight on it.

She moves back against the wall and leaves him more room. He turns toward her. When she puts a hand against the back of his head, he knows where it's going to be before it touches. He puts his cup down, gets a knee along the bench and leans in until his cheek is against her chest. He stays half up for the length of a breath. Then he lets his elbow slide and puts the rest of his weight in her arms.

She has been thinking about the moment when he would do that again. A dozen times she could have told him she was thinking about it. At breakfast he passed close enough to brush the back of her chair on his way to the stove. She almost asked him to come back before she heard him tell Yuka he wanted another minute. She thinks she could have managed hearing him say no. It was the chance that he'd get up and go because he'd felt how badly she wanted him to stay that kept her quiet.

Now his arm goes round her back. There's a short pull where the cloth at her elbow catches against the bench. She shifts so he'll have room to reach farther. He does. The first time he held on like this she could feel him trying to get enough air to ask her to stay. Today he makes a little sound because she has found the spot at the base of his neck where the effort goes out of him when she holds him properly.

“I was going to ask for this anyway,” he says. He has to shift his mouth before she can hear the last of it. “I kept thinking about it while I was getting the cups.” ᴹ

“Why didn't you?” ᴹᵏ

“I thought you'd know when I got here. I was hoping you would.” He fits his hand farther around her back. “I still check the table inside at night to see if you're there. Most of the time I know exactly where you are. I check anyway.” ᴹ

He says it as easily as he'd tell her where he left the other cup. She has spent whole days trying to decide whether she'd be allowed to feel this glad about it. Now it's going straight across to him while he's warm against her and barely moving. His hand comes up once between her shoulders and settles again. She can feel him wanting to be there for the whole of it.

After a little while she starts to rise. She has one foot under her before he catches her sleeve. She stops while she can still sit down without making him let go.

“Where are you going?” he asks. ᴹ

“I was going to get the cups. I thought you might want to sit up.” ᴹᵏ

“Will you stay like this? I'm good right here.” ᴹ

His fingers have stopped pulling at her sleeve. She can get up from where she is. She puts her foot back down under the edge of the bench and lets her arm go round him again. He feels the answer before she says it.

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

She lowers her head and kisses his forehead where the hair parts against her palm. His eyes shut at the first touch. She can feel the spot on his skin after she has lifted her mouth. The rest comes through hot and quick enough to take his breath, all the wanting she felt when he put his weight down and all the pleasure of being the one he reached for when she moved. For a second he's so glad to be wanted that he can't tell where the gladness began. He can tell when she lifts her mouth. He can still feel what it cost her to lift her head.

Her face is close enough now that she could move a little and reach his mouth. She imagines it. For a second the line she used to keep track of from the other side of a room is very hard to find with him in her arms. Then she sees Yuka standing out by the drying line a few days ago with a piece of wet sheet in both hands, calling across to ask if Makima had eaten, waiting while she finished speaking to Ori to get the answer. She hadn't even been looking for Mau. The memory of it comes with an ache Makima recognises as her own. She'd like to be able to eat with Yuka again tomorrow. The thought is there right along with the other one.

Makima rests her cheek on his hair. Mau feels the warmth of her face where she puts it. What he felt when she kissed his forehead is still there. He thinks of Yuka giving him another minute at breakfast, and of going to find her later because he wants that with her again. He loves her in the same familiar way now, with his cheek against Makima and his hand flat on her back. He lets it stay right where it is. He has been held long enough to want to give some of it back. When he pulls her elbow in a little, the part of her that has been propping her up against the wall goes loose. She puts her weight against him too. He takes it and looks down to find her hand. Her fingers close around his when he finds it.

“I love you,” she says. ᴹᵏ

This time she hears it before she's finished. She knows what she means by it. She lets the part she can say take up its own space in the room. His fingers tighten round hers.

“I love you too,” he says. He says it into her shoulder and then turns his face enough that she can hear him where he is. ᴹ

She breathes out into his hair. He can feel a shiver make it all the way down her arm. She doesn't try to put her elbow back up. He stays where he is and waits for the next breath with her.

* * *

The light has moved as far as the cup when she says, “We're immortal, Mau.” ᴹᵏ

He's still got his head against her. It takes him a second to work out how the conversation got there. “I think I forget that when I'm waiting for something.” ᴹ

“I think about it when I'm waiting.” She makes a small adjustment against his shoulder. “I can still lose you. We can both be hurt. What I mean is that we could be having tea somewhere in a hundred years and the cups would be older than we looked.” ᴹᵏ

“If you're buying the cups.” ᴹ

She laughs against his hair. For a second he can feel it begin in her chest before he hears it. He listens to the whole sound. He'd like to hear it again a good deal sooner than in a hundred years.

“I worked out something else since we joined,” she says. “Will you look at me for a second?” ᴹᵏ

He turns far enough to see her eyes. The rings are spaced as they always are. While she holds his gaze, one of them draws inward and then another follows. They settle around a small clear centre that catches the light differently. It's such a small movement that he would have missed it if he'd been watching for a change in anything else.

“The middle goes blue when I hold it,” she says. “I saw it in the window here two nights ago. It looked like you did when you came for me.” ᴹᵏ

His hand comes up before he's got an idea what to do with it. He leaves it on the bench. “Does it hurt?” ᴹ

“No. I can feel the shape of my face wanting to follow it. This much I can let go again.” ᴹᵏ

He watches the spaces open between the rings. He can't get the shape out of his head. There was a time when looking out of eyes like hers had felt like borrowing an answer. Now she has an answer that arrived for her while she was alone in a room with the light still on. He wants to know how much of it she can keep, and if it will still happen when she wants it instead of when the room and the glass happen to agree.

“Can we tell Noise after the lake?” he asks. “I'd like to see what you can do with it when we've got time.” ᴹ

“I'd like to work it out with you.” ᴹᵏ

He lets his head back down. “That's two things for after, then. You can pick where we get tea first.” ᴹ

She has thought about *after* in terms of years, generally from the middle of the night when he couldn't hear her thinking. He has just handed her something to do the next time they get the chance. She wants both. She turns the thought of a hundred years over once and leaves it where it is.

“There is something I'd like to do tomorrow,” she says. “I've got a book with a whole chapter I think you'll want to argue about. Shuna said the last bread should come out while there's still sun on the porch.” ᴹᵏ

“What part am I going to argue about?” ᴹ

“I think you'll know when we get to the dog.” ᴹᵏ

He laughs. She had almost asked what else he had planned tomorrow and how much of it he'd already given away. She lets herself lie there and have his laugh, and feel that it went across to him again.

* * *

He still has her hand when he asks what she wanted to tell him about Asa. Makima runs her thumb along the side of his finger once before she answers.

“I'd ask whether she wants to come back to the house when it's over.” ᴹᵏ

He stays still. He'd been ready to ask what he could do to get Asa out of the way before something happened. He can see her face when Ori asked about the thread. She may have had a whole different idea of where she was going after the lake.

“I like that question,” he says. ᴹ

They talk long enough to find the order he'd ask the two things in. His cup is cool by the time he finally gets up. He puts hers where she can take it with both hands and tells her not to show him the part about the dog until they've got out to the porch. Then he goes to see Yuka on the grass, carrying his own empty cup and making sure he has time to watch the whole turn she asked him to watch.

* * *

Yuka goes down in the grass before he gets to the path. Maki has one foot on either side of her and is asking her at what point she thought she'd been going left. Yuka sees Mau from the ground and waves at him with her whole arm.

“Was I looking?” she asks. ʸ

“Not at Maki.” ᴹ

“I was looking where I wanted to go.” ʸ

“I know. That's why I was there,” says Maki. ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuka sits up and catches Mau at the wrist before he has time to offer his hand. He helps her all the way up. She has grass in her hair, high enough that he has to turn her into the light to see how to get it out. He gets it, puts it in her hand and kisses her before he steps back.

“Where should I stand?” he asks. ᴹ

“Right here for another second.” ʸ

He stays. When she lets go and turns for the next try, he stands far enough out that she can see him at the end of the movement, and tells her exactly when her eyes left the person in front of her.
