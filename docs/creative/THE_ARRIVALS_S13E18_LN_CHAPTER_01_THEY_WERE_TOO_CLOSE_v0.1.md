# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 11 — They Were Too Close

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S13E18
**Continuity state:** B+296, fourth evening, DIO and Ciel take Noise out with a temporary manifestation.

*When: Around dinner, after a day in which nearly everybody claims to have done nothing.*

Elmesia asks what they did all day. Mau says nothing. Yuka says the same thing at the same time. Then she starts telling Elmesia where the curtains were tied on the daybed and it takes another three minutes to get to the part where they went in the water.

“You can't tell it from there and leave out the drink,” Mau says. ᴹ

“You had one drink.” ʸ

“And you had a third of it.” ᴹ

“You're lucky I left you the other two.” ʸ

Elmesia has to wait to swallow before she can ask how the floating worked. Yuka tells her about that, too. When she gets to the part about the stomach, Mau steals a piece of the bread on her plate and makes her watch him eat it.

DIO has joined them close to the shelter Mau carries, in the same private alcove they used on the first day. She and Elmesia went down the far shore earlier while the pool was close enough to reach, found a stall serving food in paper and stayed there until the wind turned. Elmesia ordered something from the stall because she thought it was a little different from the thing DIO had asked for. They tell the story together and keep disagreeing about whose idea it was to get the second one. DIO picks a crumb off her sleeve as she argues. Mau realizes it's the first time he's seen her have to do that here.

“What are you doing after this?” he asks her. ᴹ

DIO looks at a point just beside his shoulder.

“I hope I'm taking Noise out with Ciel. He asked me a question about how the body would work and I answered a different question. We should try again while he's here.” ᴰⁱᵒ

*I don't want a body of my own.* ᴺ

Mau can tell Noise is saying it to him on purpose, even with DIO there. He puts down the piece of bread he was holding.

“Is that what you thought she offered?” ᴹ

*Yes. I don't want to leave you. I see the things you see. I can feel how cold the steps are when you go into the pool. I like it here.* ᴺ

Mau feels that answer farther down in him than he expected to. He sets his hand on the table. Across from him Yuka pauses with the butter knife held over her plate.

“Stay with me,” Mau says. “I'd still like you to go on a date if you want to.” ᴹ

*I do.* ᴺ

DIO pulls her chair in. She waits a second so Noise can hear her from both places.

“I can give you something to walk around in until we're finished. You'll be here too. The way Ciel can be with Rimuru and still come sit with us. We can keep the whole thing inside Mau's shelter.” ᴰⁱᵒ

*Can I stop if I want to come back before the date is over?* ᴺ

“Yes. I can show you how to let it go yourself.” ᴰⁱᵒ

There is a pause.

*I'd like that for the date. At the end of the day I want to be all the way back with Mau. I want to see both of you. I don't want to get used to being in two places all the time.* ᴺ

Yuka looks up at Mau. She heard his half and DIO's. He tells her the rest. Elmesia offers the alcove for as long as they need the privacy, and DIO says she thinks she can get the far end of it and the light outside the curtain into the shelter where the three of them can sit. She wants Ciel to see the lantern at the far end. Ciel wants to know if anybody saved dessert. Mau puts aside three portions, and when DIO asks him why he didn't give them all the same amount, he tells her Noise gets the biggest one because he agreed to go out with both of them.

*Thank you,* says Noise, before either of the others has time to object. ᴺ

Noise asks him to wait while it tries letting the body go once. Mau keeps a hand on the railing beside the alcove and lets Noise keep the whole of its attention on him as it steps into the room DIO has set up. The release takes a little longer than either of them expected. Noise comes back without losing track of where it is and asks if it can do it again. Mau asks if it needs to. Noise considers it a moment and says it wants to start the date now.

DIO has set three chairs a generous distance apart. By the time Noise gets to the table, Ciel is looking out at the far end of the alcove through the opening DIO left. DIO asks Noise if it wants a look at the lantern too. It sits in the chair closest to the opening and turns its head to see how the light falls on the sleeve it has just acquired. Then it brings its arm down and finds it has a hand flat on the table.

“I want to try the dessert before it gets cold.” ᴺ

They try the dessert. Noise has to ask Ciel how much to take on the spoon if it wants to get a bit of both things in the dish. She shows it with her own spoon and manages to leave a smear across the edge of her plate. Noise tastes its own, lets the spoon rest in its mouth a second, and asks why the smaller part at the bottom is the better part. DIO says she doesn't know. She was going to leave that part until the end. Noise pushes a spoonful of its own across the table to her and asks what they usually talk about when it isn't there.

Ciel wants to answer, then realizes that Noise tends to be with them for everything it would want to know. She tells it about a question she asked Rimuru on a walk, about whether he'd ever gotten a song stuck in his head that he didn't know the words to. He said yes and sang her the only four notes he remembered. The notes were backwards. Ciel had learned the song the other way around. By the time she finishes the story, Noise has slid the chair to her side of the table so it can ask her to sing the notes the way Rimuru had them.

“You can ask him to sing it when we get home,” she says. ᶜ

“I want to hear it the way you remember him singing it.” ᴺ

Ciel sings it very quietly. DIO is close enough to hear it the second time. Noise asks if she has anything she wants the two of them to hear. DIO reaches across for its hand to show it how the rhythm went in a song she heard down the shore with Elmesia, tapping it into the palm in three little groups. Noise asks if she wants to try it on the other hand. She brings her chair over.

Outside, Mau and Yuka stay at the railing. He can still feel Noise listening to the sound of the waves, and every so often the listening gets a little farther away. He asks if Yuka wants him to get them breakfast in the room tomorrow. She says she wants to get up, but only after the first light, and she wants to find somewhere that serves the same potatoes. It takes a while to see how they can do all of those things at once. They get as far as deciding they'll ask when they wake up.

He feels Noise come all the way back while Yuka is still standing beside him. There is a brief new pressure where his left hand rests on the railing, as if it wanted to bring back the feel of something he didn't touch himself. He can tell that the attention which has spent the last hour somewhere else is with him again. He gives it a little time to settle.

“Was it good?” ᴹ

*They were too close.* ᴺ

*You moved your chair,* DIO says immediately. ᴰⁱᵒ

*You took my hand before I sat down,* says Ciel. ᶜ

Mau can feel Noise smiling before it laughs. He laughs with it. Yuka wants to know what's funny and he tells her exactly what the three of them just said. Ciel adds that Noise wanted a hand on each side by the end, which makes Noise laugh again. DIO comes back into the shelter with the patient air of somebody who intends to have a witness to that part. Mau keeps his arm around Yuka while he listens.

“Do you want to go again sometime?” he asks. ᴹ

*Yes.* ᴺ

The answer comes while he still has his mouth open. Ciel and DIO go quiet together. For a little longer there is nowhere else Mau has to be and nobody else Noise wants to be. Yuka leans further into his side and asks if they can take the long way back to the room. They do.
