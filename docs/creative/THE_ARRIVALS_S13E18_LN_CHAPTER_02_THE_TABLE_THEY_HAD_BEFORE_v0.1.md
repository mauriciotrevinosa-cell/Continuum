# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 11 — The Table They Had Before

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S13E18
**Continuity state:** B+297, fifth afternoon and evening, Mau and Yuka make time for their anniversary dinner.

*When: The following afternoon, when Yuka asks where Mau wants to eat.*

Mau has tried to remember the name of the restaurant three times since breakfast. He remembers how he got there and what they ate. He can see the little step at the far end of the room that Yuka almost missed on her way out. If she were to ask him which of the two of them picked it the first time, he could tell her that it was him and that he chose it because he wanted to take her somewhere with the tables far enough apart that they could sit as long as they liked. He can't remember the name.

“Come with me,” he says. “I'll show you.” ᴹ

She's ready before he is. On the way out of the hotel she stops near the stairs and waits. Mau walks back the two steps he took ahead of her. She tells him to look out at the water. It makes him think of the place she had wanted to tell him, and then he realizes they've never stood here looking at it at the same time. He leans his elbows beside hers. He can see a line where the water changes color beyond the beach.

“I still wanted you to see it from here,” she says. ʸ

“It's a good spot.” ᴹ

“The light was worse when I found it. I thought if we waited till evening it would look like this.” ʸ

She gets to spend as long as she likes looking at it. He keeps his elbow against hers. When she finally turns around, she asks him how far it is to the restaurant and slips her hand into the crook of his arm.

He finds it by the step. At first the room looks smaller than it did in his head. Then he sees the old table at the edge of the window and realizes he remembers it as if he'd been sitting down the whole time. He asks the person at the door if it's free later and what time would be best. Yuka keeps hold of his arm through the answer. On the way back to change she makes him tell her what he remembers from the first night. It turns out they remember different parts of what they said to each other. He remembers every word she said about the dessert. She remembers how he asked if there was anything at home she already missed.

“Do you remember what I said?” ʸ

“You missed Ori coming in without knocking.” ᴹ

“I miss it now. In about ten minutes at home I'll start telling her to knock first again.” ʸ

They take the evening steps even more slowly on the way back. Yuka has picked something she can walk in, and when Mau holds the chair out for her she catches the corner of his sleeve before she sits. It's the shirt she chose for him while they were out buying things on the honeymoon. He thought it was too thin at the time. It turns out he likes the way she can warm a spot through it by leaving her hand there. She goes to let go so he can take his own seat. He holds her hand for the extra second it takes him to come around the table.

The first thing the server asks is whether they want the menu or whether they already know. They take the menu. The dessert Mau remembers is on a little card at the back and Yuka sets it against the wall so they won't have to turn all the pages over to find it again. There are three things on the main page she wants to consider. He waits through all three. When she picks, he remembers at the last second that he'd meant to pick something for himself, too. She laughs and folds the bottom of the card straight where he's bent it.

“Happy anniversary,” he says after they order. ᴹ

“Are we having another one?” ʸ

“I wanted to take you here when I saw the table again.” ᴹ

“Happy anniversary, then.” ʸ

He lifts his glass to hers. The sound is small enough he wouldn't have heard it at the big table at home. They put the glasses down and she asks him what surprised him most about being married. He starts to give her an answer about how many things change places in a room when you're both looking for them. Halfway through he sees she really wants to know. He takes a little while over the question.

“I thought I knew what it felt like to come home to you. Then one day I was sitting down with a book and you came over and sat in my lap. You hadn't called me over or asked me to put it down. You started telling me about something Mai had done and went right on with the story while I got my arm out from under you. Now if I'm reading for long enough I start looking up at the door.” ᴹ

She touches the side of her glass and looks right at him.

“Do you ever want me to let you finish the book?” ʸ

“I want you to tell me the end of the thing with Mai.” ᴹ

“That's where I got you. She knew exactly what she was doing.” ʸ

The plates come. Mau cuts a piece off his before she can ask for it. She takes the piece and goes a while without answering her own question. He can see her looking out at where the window now reflects both their faces. She finally puts her fork down.

“I thought I'd always know what I was going to ask from you before I came over. Now sometimes I come sit with you and find out what it was after. When I get my arms around you.” ʸ

“What's the thing you ask for the most?” ᴹ

“Usually it's just if I can stay there.” ʸ

He reaches across and turns his hand over on the table. She puts her wrist in it so she can keep eating. They get through about four more bites this way before he has to ask her to let him cut the rest of his food.

She wants to talk about the future before they order dessert. Her voice goes quieter and he leans on the table to hear her. She'd like to tell Maki and Yuta they're trying. She can say it out loud now and she thinks she'd like to be able to ask them things when they don't know what to do. If anyone else in the house finds out before there's anything to tell, Ori will ask why they said they were trying and exactly what it is they have to try. Yuka is willing to take many questions from Ori. She refuses to take that one.

“Then Maki and Yuta,” Mau says. “I would also like to live.” ᴹ

“I'll protect you.” ʸ

“What if I take your dad's name?” ᴹ

She tests his name with Okkotsu on the end, clearly enough he feels his shoulders go tight hearing it from across the table. It's strange to have it come out of her mouth so easily. She says it a second time and adds that he'll still have to ask Yuta.

“I could use Mai's. See if she lets me borrow it.” ᴹ

“Maki would kill you before she got through asking why.” ʸ

He names Gojo. Yuka says Gojo will have opinions, and from the look on her face she's just heard most of them in her head. They spend half the time it takes to get dessert considering Ieiri. Mau asks what she'd do about hers if he chose something. Yuka shrugs as though it's the easiest decision they've made all night.

“I'd take whichever one you pick.” ʸ

“You don't have to.” ᴹ

“I know. Gojo Okkotsu.” ʸ

He laughs. She starts again with Ieiri Gojo, gets as far as a third name on the next one, and has to stop when the dessert arrives. She waits until the plate is down to tell him that she likes Mau all by itself, too.

He takes the fork she hands him. They each eat from the side closest to them until Yuka reaches the thing she remembers liking best and pushes the plate right into the middle. He holds it steady so she can cut it in two. They stay long enough for the window to turn all the way dark, trying out a few more names between bites and leaving the question exactly where they found it.
