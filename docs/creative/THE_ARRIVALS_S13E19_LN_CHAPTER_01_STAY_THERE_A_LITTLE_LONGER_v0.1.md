# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 13 — Stay There a Little Longer

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S13E19
**Continuity state:** B+297, fifth night, quiet after the anniversary dinner.

*When: After the restaurant closes its windows for the night.*

They could walk back to the room by the path with lamps. Yuka takes the turn that goes down by the sand. Mau slows to take his shoes off when she does. She leaves the bad sandals in his hand along with his own shoes, steps down barefoot and stands where he can come up beside her.

From here the restaurant has one lit window. They're far enough away that she can't see their table anymore. She holds up her empty hand as he comes over and waits for him to give her his.

“Are you still considering Gojo Okkotsu Ieiri?” he asks. ᴹ

“I'm considering asking Shoko what she would do if you showed up with all three and asked her to sign something.” ʸ

“She'd make me sit down and take a nap first.” ᴹ

Yuka laughs at the thought of it. She leans her shoulder against his. The water gets them again when they forget to watch it. They step up out of its reach and spend the rest of the way back walking in the soft sand with no one ahead of them.

At the room Mau puts the sandals by the open door. Yuka stays beside him while he tries to stand up, close enough he has to turn around to see her face. She puts her arms around his neck.

“Can I sit with you again?” ʸ

“Where do you want me?” ᴹ

She nods toward the bed. He goes and sits on the side facing the water, and she climbs into his lap, one knee at a time. He moves his hand out of her way. She takes it and puts it back where it had been, at her waist. There's a little salt left on her temple. She rests it against his cheek.

“What were you going to do if I got the same answer next time?” she asks. ʸ

His hand stays at her waist. She feels him draw a breath before he answers. She's glad he takes the time, even though she wants to hear him right away.

“Listen if you wanted to talk. Let you get in my lap if you didn't.” ᴹ

She tucks her head down and laughs into his collarbone.

“You'd get a break if you learned to put your book down slower.” ʸ

“I like getting to the part where you tell me why you're there.” ᴹ

She moves back enough to look at him. He was careful on the balcony and she was glad he was careful. Right now she wants to kiss him and see how long it takes until he starts laughing because the next thing he wanted to say is gone. She does it. When they break apart he takes a breath to say something and she gets him once more before he can.

“I was going to ask if you wanted to change first,” he says. ᴹ

“Oh. Did you?” ʸ

He shakes his head. Yuka puts both hands on his shoulders and feels him lean in this time. His thumb catches on a seam at her waist. He starts to move it away. She covers his hand with hers and keeps it there while she kisses him, until he works out that she liked exactly where he'd put it.

She shifts her weight to lie back and finds that the edge of the bed is closer than she thought. He gets his arm behind her just as she reaches for him. For a second she's looking straight up at the ceiling with a pillow folded under one elbow and Mau trying to hold her up with the arm she has pinned.

“I think you've got the pillow.” ʸ

He looks down at the corner sticking out between them.

“I was hoping you'd think that was on purpose.” ᴹ

She laughs and lets him have his arm back. He brings the pillow with him as he moves up the bed and sets it by the headboard. She follows and pulls it a little lower before she lies down. From here she can see the curtain lifting off the floor and the line where it comes down again. Mau sits beside her, his knee making a dip in the mattress. She puts one hand on his leg and waits for him to turn.

“Here,” she says. ʸ

He comes down beside her on his elbow. He leaves enough room that she can roll toward him on her own. She likes that he does that. She also likes that when she pulls him the rest of the way in, he comes without making her ask twice. One of his hands settles between her shoulder blades. The wind is cool on the side of her neck he hasn't kissed yet.

At dinner he'd told her about sitting with a book and listening for her at the door. She had pictured him looking up and finding her there. Now she sees the same look from so close she can watch it change. His eyes go from hers to her mouth. She runs a hand behind his neck and feels his breath change before he moves.

“You can kiss me again.” ʸ

He does. The first kiss is quick enough to make her laugh at him. She gets the second one herself and takes her time with it. His hand opens flat on her back. When she pulls away to breathe, he rests his forehead against hers. Neither of them moves for a moment. Somewhere beyond the door a wave goes flat over the sand.

“Still good?” he asks. ᴹ

“Very. I was waiting to see if you'd do that again.” ʸ

“Which part?” ᴹ

She cups the back of his neck and kisses him the way he kissed her before, quick enough that he starts to follow when she pulls away. This time she stays long enough to feel his smile against her mouth. The curtain lifts and a bar of light reaches the foot of the bed. She watches it go and reaches for his hand.

“I want to make love to you,” she tells him. ʸ

Mau looks at her for a second. She can still feel the smile in the corner of his mouth where it rests against her cheek.

“I want that too.” ᴹ

She presses a kiss below his ear. The answer warms her as much as the breath he lets out there. She catches his face between her hands and brings him back to her.

Mau gets a hand around hers before she can take it away from his face. He turns his head and kisses her palm. She expects him to let go after that. Instead he keeps it against his cheek and looks at her from there.

“I want to see you,” he says. “Will you look at me?” ᴹ

She does. He's close enough that when his eyes move from one of hers to the other she can see him doing it. At the restaurant she could say his name a dozen different ways and still look out the window if she got embarrassed. Here he says hers once and she has to lower her face to his shoulder for a breath. She feels him start to laugh. Then he kisses her temple and waits for her to look up again.

“Say it once more,” she says. ʸ

He says it right away. She thought she'd be ready for it the second time. The way he says it is the same, and that's what gets her. She lifts her face and kisses him until she feels his hand tighten around hers.

The wind comes in under the curtain and cools the back of her shoulder. Yuka reaches for the sheet, then changes her mind. She takes the first button of Mau's shirt between her fingers. It's the thin one she picked for him on their honeymoon. The button comes through easily. The next one catches on a thread.

“Leave that one. I'll get it,” he says. ᴹ

She waits for him to sit up. He stays where he is and puts his hand over hers to turn the button sideways. Their fingers are still together when it comes free. She runs her knuckles down the opening. The shirt falls away on one side and she feels him take a quick breath against her temple.

“I like this shirt on you,” she says. ʸ

“I had an idea you might.” ᴹ

“Take it off anyway.” ʸ

He does. She shifts to help him free the sleeve he's lying on and gets a strand of her hair right across his mouth. He lifts his head and starts to back off. She catches his arm.

“Where are you going?” ʸ

“I thought you wanted room.” ᴹ

“I want you to stop getting my hair in your mouth.” ʸ

He laughs. She gathers it off her neck and lays it across the pillow, then puts her hand at the back of his head and brings him down herself. The next kiss lands right. He stays close when it ends. Yuka feels him reach for the side of her dress, stop at the seam and try the other side.

“The zip's at the back.” ʸ

“Will you turn for me?” ᴹ

She turns. He draws it down with one hand flat between her shoulders so the fabric doesn't bunch. When he reaches the end she still feels the heel of his palm there. She stays on her side long enough for him to kiss the place the zipper started, then rolls back to him and gets the dress the rest of the way off. It slides down the side of the bed and lands on the floor without a sound.

She puts a hand on his bare chest. He has one foot tangled in the sheet. She presses her lips together when she sees it, and he catches her looking.

“I'm very good at this part,” he says. ᴹ

“I've been watching.” ʸ

He gets the sheet loose with his toes. They slip out of the rest of their clothes and he draws it up over her back. She catches the edge with one hand and lets him under it with her. There is a moment when she could say any one of the things she has wanted to say since the restaurant. The thing she wants right now is simple enough to tell him in a breath.

“I want you closer. Come over here.” ʸ

He puts his forearm beside her head and comes over her. The mattress gives under his elbow and rolls her a little towards him. Yuka brings her knee up against his side to stay there. He looks down to see where she put it, then at her face. She can feel him against her, warm and hard. He waits there until she puts her hand at his waist and draws him forward.

He enters her slowly. Her heel presses into the mattress as she gets used to the stretch. She had meant to keep looking at him the whole time. When she has to close her eyes, he holds still enough that she can open them again and find him right where she left him.

“Can you come down a little?” she asks. “I want to hold you.” ʸ

He bends his elbow. The change brings his chest against hers and shifts the pressure low inside her. She gets both arms around him before he can lift himself again. Her next breath comes out against his neck, quick and uneven. He keeps his weight on his arm and waits.

“Like that,” she says. ʸ

He moves again. She feels the same pressure when he comes back, and this time she knows what to do with the breath it takes from her. She lets it go where he can hear it. He says her name close to her ear. She turns towards him and catches his mouth while he's still saying it.

She runs her hand up his back until her fingers find the top of his shoulder. He turns his face into hers, his breath warm against her cheek. She feels his mouth move and lifts her head to hear him.

“Your hand,” he says. ᴹ

She loosens her grip on his shoulder. He takes the hand she frees and slides his fingers between hers.

“Here. Keep it here.” ᴹ

She closes her fingers. He exhales as soon as she does. She can feel where his ribs move against hers. When she brings her knee in closer, her heel slips on the sheet. She grips his hand and feels him lean harder on the forearm beside her head to steady them. She gets her foot down flat and keeps hold of him. The next stroke catches her there, braced well enough to meet it, and the breath she'd meant to take comes out against his cheek. He feels her go still and stays close while she catches up.

His arm is shaking a little by the time she lifts her head. She kisses him and draws their hands in between them so he can rest his wrist on the pillow.

“Stay close,” she says. ʸ

Mau nods against her neck. She feels his breathing quicken again. His fingers curl around hers when he comes, and she holds on until the weight on his arm eases.

They stay that way until she can hear the water again. Mau shifts onto his side and brings their hands with him. His cheek comes to rest on a piece of her hair. She tugs it free and drapes it across the pillow before he can offer to move. He traces a little arc over her knuckles with his thumb. She feels him stop after the third pass and waits. The fourth one comes after a wave she can hear all the way up the beach.

“Do you want me to move?” he asks. ᴹ

Yuka draws their joined hands in between them.

“Stay there a little longer.” ʸ

He lets his weight settle beside her. The place where their arms touch is warmer than the rest of the bed. She could go to sleep that way. After another minute she remembers she's thirsty and tries to work out how much she cares about it. Mau hears her swallow. She knows he hears it because he starts to lift his head before he says a word.

“Water?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes. Don't get up.” ʸ

He has one foot on the floor. She draws his hand back toward her. He brings his foot in, opens the shop and buys a bottle he can reach from where he is. It comes cold enough to put a mark on the sheet. He unscrews the lid and hands it to her before he takes any himself.

“You could have ordered some for both of us before you started getting up.” ʸ

“I thought you might want me to come back from the kitchen.” ᴹ

“You can come back from the edge of the bed.” ʸ

He drinks, stretches his arm to put the bottle on the table and rolls in beside her. She scoots over until her head is on his shoulder again. At home someone would have started walking downstairs by now. Here she can lie still long enough to hear how the water comes in at an angle. She remembers him walking the route of their old table today, knowing every turn and still going back to make sure the step was the right one.

“I want to take the kids to the beach sometime,” she says. ʸ

He asks which kids. She says she was thinking about Ori and Chloe, and then she was thinking maybe in a few years they'd have somebody small enough to need help standing up in the water. She thought both things right after each other. Mau doesn't answer immediately. When she looks up, he has that face she pictured on the stairs, the one that wants to hear her say it twice.

“I want to do the first one soon,” he says. “We can look at weekends with Frieren when we're home.” ᴹ

“That's exactly what I want to do.” ʸ

He lifts the sheet higher over her shoulder. A little later he tells her he loves her. She makes a noise she means as an instruction to go to sleep. He kisses her forehead. She waits until he settles beside her to say the thing that kept coming back to her while they sat at their old table.

“I love you more.” ʸ

The laugh he makes is small enough she feels it through his shoulder. She wants to keep feeling it for a while, so when he starts to tell her something else she lets him.
