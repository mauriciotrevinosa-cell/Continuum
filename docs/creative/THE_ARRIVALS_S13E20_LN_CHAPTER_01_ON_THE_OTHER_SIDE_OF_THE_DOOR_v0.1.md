# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 14 — On the Other Side of the Door

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S13E20
**Continuity state:** B+299, morning, six nights complete. Mau and Yuka return by Kaisel.

*When: After the sixth night, before the boats begin to leave.*

Yuka puts the bad sandals back in Mau's hand for the flight home. The sole has come free a little farther. He can push his thumb between it and the rest of the shoe now.

“I think it's had a good life.” ᴹ

“Then it's earned its trip home.” ʸ

He puts the pair away together. There are sweets for the house, a stack of paper Elmesia found for Ori and a sealed packet of coffee that Yuka bought while he was looking at something else. She lets him see the coffee before he stores it. He asks how long she's been planning to do that. She tells him since he asked the man at the pool to tell him what the drink was called a second time.

They have time to walk through the room once more after everything is out of it. She opens the curtain the rest of the way. The wind brings it back across the light. Mau keeps the bags in his inventory and comes up beside her empty-handed. They can see the low place beyond the steps where they stood after dinner. The water is coming in over that part of the sand.

“When we come back I want the same room,” she says. ʸ

He waits a beat. Usually he has about that long before she adds something else.

“And I want to see the other one with the bigger balcony. We can go look at it first.” ʸ

“Get a place for the whole family and take this room for us.” ᴹ

Yuka gives the view one more look.

“How many people would we actually be bringing if we asked everybody?” ʸ

Mau starts doing the arithmetic, counting the people who would want to come and the people they'd have to ask first. Then he remembers that Milim will invite herself the moment she hears the words *the whole family*. Yuka hears him add Milim twice and laughs. It's time to go before they can agree on a number. Mau closes the balcony door, and Yuka goes back to make sure it caught.

Elmesia is down by the landing with two cups of something hot. One of them has already been claimed. DIO holds it near the shelter Mau carries and waits for the steam to clear before she drinks. She and Elmesia have found a long piece of pale glass that they thought was a stone on the last walk. Each tells the story of finding it with the other one as the person who insisted on going back to check. It's coming home with DIO. Elmesia has wrapped it in a cloth and left one corner uncovered so DIO can keep looking at it.

“Next time tell me a month before if you're really bringing everybody,” Elmesia says. “Tell me a week before if you're doing what you did this time.” ᴱˡ

“How early do I have to tell you if it's just for breakfast?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Tell me while you're ordering. I can probably get you a table by the water if you bring me one of those buns.” ᴱˡ

Elmesia takes a folded slip of paper from her pocket. It's the address she wants him to send to if Noise finishes its drawing. Yuka puts her hand out for it while Mau takes the bag of sweets from the low wall. He hears her ask which way the dragon likes to turn when it sees the island. Elmesia shows her with one hand, asks if she wants to see it from above the next time, and gets an answer before she finishes lowering her arm.

Yuka laughs and thanks her. DIO hands Elmesia the cup when she's done. She takes a last look up the shore, asks to be carried inside Mau's shelter again for the flight, and stays talking to Elmesia until Mau calls Kaisel.

Kaisel sets off lower than he flew the first time. They can see the little bay where they snorkeled and a line of chairs close to the pool. Yuka turns in front of Mau to look down between one wing and the other. Once they're clear of the island she stops looking back. He gives her the bag of sweets she saved. She gets it open in the wind and puts a piece in his mouth before the paper can get away from her. Halfway home she goes to sleep against him and the top of the bag stays folded under her hand.

At the house they see a white sheet hanging between two branches and Taihei trying to bring the back of a chair through the door. Mau lands far enough away that the wind from Kaisel's wings won't move the sheet. Ori hears them anyway. She comes down the steps with Chloe, who stops to shut the door behind them. Lantern lifts his head from the grass beside the path. A book is open in front of him. He holds the page down with the tip of a claw as Ori runs past him.

“You came back before lunch!” she calls. ᴼ

Yuka walks the last few paces very quickly. Ori goes into her arms without a word and keeps both feet on the ground for about half the hug. By the time she lets Yuka set her down she remembers she's been working on the answer to a question and goes to show them the page Lantern has saved for her.

Mau waits until Ori is next to him to touch her hair. There is a different knot in it from the one he remembers at the birthday. She tells him about it first, then about a place she tried reading in the afternoon with Lantern, then that they aren't allowed to look in the common room because Senku said he wanted the people getting back to see it when it was done. When she gets to that part, Chloe says it was Ramiris who told them they couldn't look. Senku only said he needed the chairs out of the way.

“Do you want to see what we brought first?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Ori nods, then remembers she has a question for Mau.

“Did you get the bird?” ᴼ

“Technically.” ᴹ

Yuka walks inside with her hand on Ori's back, already starting the story. Mau watches until they turn the corner. Then he calls Bellion and waits on the path.

Bellion comes out where the shade reaches the far edge. He says the man and his daughter went home the day before. The blue hands met them at the turn. The daughter had asked to bring the pillow. Shoko said she could, and sent another one for the long ride. The extra pair came home as agreed. One of them is here if Mau wants to see it.

Mau calls it up beside Bellion. The shadow stands straight at once. Mau thanks it for seeing the family home and tells it to stand down with the rest of the reserve. Bellion inclines his head. He has the report ready for the routes he worked out with Rimuru and the rounds Muster kept while Mau was away.

“Show me where you put them tomorrow. I'll come find you after breakfast.” ᴹ

Bellion inclines his head. Mau can already hear his wife telling Ori what the bird did when she set down the bowl. He opens the door in time to hear Ori ask why he gave the bird its own fruit when it clearly wanted his. He sets the bags out by the stairs, keeps the sweets where he can reach them, and goes in to give her the best answer he has.
