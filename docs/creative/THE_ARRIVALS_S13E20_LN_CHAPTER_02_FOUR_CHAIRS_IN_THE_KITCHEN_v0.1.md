# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 23 — Four Chairs in the Kitchen

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 23
**Source episode:** S13E20
**Continuity state:** B+306, afternoon, Mau and Yuka tell Maki and Yuta they are trying to start a family.

*When: Later that day, while the common room is still closed.*

Yuka tells Maki she has something to ask her and almost loses her to the common room before she gets to the end of the sentence. Two people are arguing about the position of the couch. Maki can hear the leg of it catching on the floor every time they move it. She takes one look at Yuka's face, leaves them to it and comes to the kitchen.

Yuta is already there. Mau asked him to come over early and offered to make tea, so by the time Yuka walks in Yuta has found the cups, poured the water and moved two of the chairs out enough to sit in them. Mau is standing by the kettle wondering how much of his original contribution counts if the person he asked to sit down did most of it.

Yuka takes his hand. Maki sees that, then looks back up at her.

“We're trying to start a family,” Yuka says. “We wanted to tell you two ourselves.” ʸ

There. She did the whole thing before she sat down, and her voice didn't do any of the things she practiced avoiding on the walk down. Mau squeezes her hand. Yuta picks up the cup nearest him and puts it back down without drinking.

“Are you asking us for something now?” he asks. ʸᵗ

“No. I thought I'd like you to know we decided. And if I have a question I'd like to be able to come ask you without having to say that part for the first time.” ʸ

Maki takes one of the chairs and turns it around to sit next to Yuka. She gives her enough time to sit first.

“Come ask us. You can ask me anything that has to do with you.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuka sits. The relief of hearing that is so sudden she wants to lean all the way over and put her head against Maki, the way she does with Mau now. Maki reaches over for the cup Yuta set closest to them. Yuka rests against her shoulder for about as long as it takes to pass her the cup.

“We aren't telling Ori yet,” Yuka says. ʸ

“Are you afraid of her finding out or afraid of answering the next question?” Maki asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

“The second one.” ʸ

That makes all four of them laugh, which helps more than the entire flight home Yuka spent arranging what she meant to say. She sees Mau looking at her with his hands around his cup. She puts her foot across both of his under the table.

Yuta wants to know how long they've been thinking about it. Mau says they first talked about it on their honeymoon and decided then to wait. Yuka takes over from there. She says when they decided they were ready, then asks Maki if she remembers the way she wanted to know about the future as soon as somebody said there would be one. Maki remembers more of it than Yuka thought. She can tell Yuka what time of day the first question came, and whose hand she held out of sight so nobody would see her doing it.

“It still feels like that sometimes,” Yuka says. “I want to have an answer right away. I'm trying to leave room for one to come when it comes.” ʸ

Maki keeps a hand around the cup, giving Yuka somewhere to look while she waits. Then she puts it down.

“Tell me what you'd like me to do on a day when it feels like that.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuka thinks about it. It depends on how far she's got into thinking about it when they see each other. On the balcony she wanted Mau to sit there and let her get to the end. In the morning she wanted something good to eat and a place where she could get in the water. She'd like Maki to ask her if she wants to go do something. She wants to be able to say yes and spend the whole time on it, even if the reason she came by was to say she was disappointed.

“I can do that,” Maki says. ᴹᵃᵏ

Yuta looks across the table at Mau.

“And you?” ʸᵗ

Mau opens his mouth. Yuka knows the first thing he's going to say by the way he starts to sit back. She leaves her foot across his and waits.

“I want to be able to ask you how to do things,” he says after a second. “Some of them might sound a long way off. I've been thinking about them anyway.” ᴹ

“Like what?” ʸᵗ

“How to fit a crib in our room.” ᴹ

Yuta starts asking how much wall they've got. Yuka rests her chin on her hand and lets Mau answer. It's the first time she's heard him tell someone else where he's been thinking of putting it. He explains that the desk chair needs to move. Maki gets up for more water while he talks and puts a fresh cup in front of him without making him stop. They go far enough to know there's room, which is as far as either of them wants to go today.

At the door Maki asks Yuka to bring her along the next time she goes walking, whether she has something to ask her or not. Then she says she's going to find out who's been dragging the couch across the floor. Yuka catches her and gets the rest of the hug before she lets her go. She turns back to find Mau still in his chair with a little bit of tea left. She sits down sideways on his lap and tells him his arithmetic about the crib was off by two inches. He says he knows. He left that much space for her to bring the chair back in if she changes her mind.

She puts her face into the place where his neck meets his shoulder. He moves his cup to the table and holds her while she laughs.
