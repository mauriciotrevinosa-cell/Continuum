# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 16 — A Picture the Whole Room Can See

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S13E21
**Continuity state:** B+299, late afternoon. Senku, Byakuya, Ramiris and Rimuru unveil the library.

*When: A little before supper, once the couch is in place.*

Byakuya is the last one to take a seat. He stands with a hand over the back of the couch and asks everybody at the far end whether they can see the upper left corner of the screen.

“I can see it,” Fern says. “What am I supposed to see there?” ᶠᵉ

“Something the size of my thumb. Senku, go ahead.” ᵇʸᵃᵏᵘʸᵃ

The screen turns from a dull light color to a darker one, starting at the bottom. In the upper left corner something moves. Ori gets up on her knees. Chloe tilts her head the other way. Fern leans back in her chair so she can get the whole square in view. A little white dot moves from the corner to the middle and stops there.

“Yes,” Fern says. “Even when somebody moves in front of me. I can still see around their head.” ᶠᵉ

Senku makes a mark on a folded bit of paper. He wants the curtains open exactly as far as they are now. If they all shut them and watch a picture in a dark room, it proves very little about what the thing is going to do when somebody gets up to leave. He motions to Ramiris to go ahead. She climbs onto the back of the couch in the place Byakuya has cleared and puts both hands on the edge of the screen's little housing.

At first the picture is so bright around the edges that Mau doesn't see what's in it. Then Ramiris turns a dial, tells Senku to take his hand off the other one, and a room comes into focus. There is a little table in it and a woman opens a door and takes a step in. From where Mau is standing he can see the way the light on her dress changes when she crosses the room. Someone beside him leans sideways to see if there is a person behind the screen. It's Milim. She takes two steps and finds Senku already standing there, in the way and quite pleased to be in it.

“Can I go around?” she asks. ᴹⁱ

“Please go around. That's what we want to know.” ˢᵉⁿᵏᵘ

She gets to the other side. There is a wall behind it and a little shallow box about as wide as Senku's outstretched hands. It has warm air coming out the top. She puts her fingers above it and looks at him. He puts his fingers beside hers. It warms both their hands. Byakuya says they can give it room to vent without having to move anybody's chair.

Mau was away for the last part of this. He knew about the test with a single still picture because Rimuru asked if he'd let him use a memory of a movie, and he offered the opening to one he and Umaru had watched more than once. Rimuru asked if he wanted to share the exact part of the room they were sitting in, too. Mau said he'd rather keep what she said halfway through the opening where it was. They made a test file of the movie itself. Mau watches the woman put her bag on the table on the screen now and recognizes the movie. The sound of the buckle touching the wood is something he'd forgotten until he hears it.

“You got the sound,” he says. ᴹ

“Got the sound to come out at the same time,” Senku says. “We already had sound before you left. It was a little like listening to a person shout from a room they hadn't walked into yet.” ˢᵉⁿᵏᵘ

“About a second and a half,” Byakuya says. “Long enough to see how bad it was every single time somebody said something.” ᵇʸᵃᵏᵘʸᵃ

Ramiris clears her throat. She has been waiting since the very first dot to explain what the screen contributes. It takes a picture the size of the inside of the box and lets it be in the whole square in front of them at once. Senku kept finding ways to get one row of the picture in before the one under it. She found the piece that would hold the row where he put it, and it turns out if he sends her the rows fast enough she can keep giving them back to him without a line going down the middle. She wants to be credited for the upper left corner in particular. Fern raises her hand and says that was the first thing she saw. Ramiris decides that counts.

“How many movies?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

Rimuru brings up the library. The screen shows the cards they made while Mau, Yuka and DIO were away. There is one kind for movies and another for music. A section for manga with several shelves' worth of titles goes by so fast that Umaru says wait twice. Rimuru goes back and lets her look. When she asks which ones are from her collection he says her card has a little drawing on the back. Anything she chose to share on it went into the list. Anything that stopped in the middle will stop where the person who provided it stopped. They can finish those when someone knows the rest and asks to contribute it. Umaru gets close enough to read the smallest titles. Rem comes up on her right and points to a book she recognizes from what Umaru told her about it.

“How long did this take you?” Mau asks Rimuru. ᴹ

“The agreeing took most of the time. Byakuya started having people come by with their favorite scene and tell him what they actually wanted everybody else to see. Once we knew which parts we'd been offered, I could help put them where Senku could use them. After that everybody kept coming back with more.” ᴿ

Byakuya has a card in his own hand, with room on it to write several more names. He asks if there is a song Mau remembers well enough to want to bring later. Mau says he can think of a few. Bocchi is close enough to hear him. She asks whether he wants her to play one now so they can see whether it's the same one. He looks over, still in his clothes from the flight, and asks if she wants to play after they eat. She checks the case next to the wall as if it might have an answer already. Then she says yes.

Rimuru switches to a screen with a blank square on one side and a column of short lines on the other. They're parts Senku still has to make. The old race Umaru described had a place where you could push against the grass and come back onto the road if you timed it right. Rimuru could get the picture of that out of a memory she gave him. Senku had to work out how fast the car was going to let it do the same thing.

“We had her show us the turns and made the track from that,” Senku says. “This is the part I could do while the screen was coming along. The next track's missing about a third. If somebody wants to take a shot at drawing that from memory, we'll try it.” ˢᵉⁿᵏᵘ

“Can I make one up?” asks Ori. ᴼ

Senku looks at her, then at Byakuya. Byakuya finds another card and asks Ori how she wants it to start.

“Could I play one first so I know what it's supposed to do?” ᴼ

“That'd be a good idea,” Senku says. “You can still have the card.” ˢᵉⁿᵏᵘ

She takes it. In the meantime Umaru has got far enough down the column of notes to recognize the little signs next to some of the titles. One means the start screen works. One means you can get past that screen, with a little luck. One looks like a check, which Senku explains means somebody actually played the thing all the way to an end. She asks who it was. Byakuya says he has beaten the first race exactly once. He's looking forward to seeing what it looks like when somebody who knows the game does it.

Umaru has found the games. She puts one hand flat on the edge of the table and takes a breath through her nose. The list is longer than the section of the screen she can see. A familiar name goes past. She sits down hard in the chair Taihei finally brought in this morning and asks Senku to go back one. He puts the control on the table in front of her.

“Try it yourself.” ˢᵉⁿᵏᵘ

She looks at him. There is a button where she expected it. She puts her thumb on it, goes back one, and sees the name again. Then she presses a different button. The room hears the first four notes of the game before anybody can ask her which one she picked. Taihei looks at Mau over the back of the chair. Mau reaches into his shop before he finishes turning around.
