# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 77
## Chapter 18 — Do Not Even Think About Leaving

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 77
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S13E21
**Continuity state:** B+299, evening. The Fort tries the new library and the family stays for the first game and movie night.

*When: The same evening, while Umaru is still hearing the start of the game.*

Umaru makes it to the first menu before she starts crying. Mau sees her put the control down, straighten it on the edge of the table and take a little breath. The next breath goes worse. Rem has a hand on the back of the chair by then. She gets down low so Umaru can lean toward her without having to get out of the seat.

“I thought I'd forgotten that sound,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“Do you want to hear it again?” ᴿᵉ

Umaru nods. She uses the back of her hand to clear her eyes enough to find the button. This time when the four notes play she gets all the way to the last one before she cries. Taihei goes to the kitchen and comes back with a clean towel instead of asking her if she needs one. She grabs the end of it and says thanks so quietly that Mau can barely hear her.

He puts chips and a cold Coke down within her reach. The Coke makes the little ring of water that he can recognize on a table from the other side of a room. He pushes it on top of the corner of the towel. Umaru looks at it, then at him. There is room between the couch and the nearest chair for him to get out. He starts that way to leave her to it.

Someone catches his shirt from behind.

“Don't even think about leaving.” ᵁ

He turns. She's looking at him with the most serious expression he has ever seen on her face. He can tell she gave some thought to how to catch him with one hand and still be able to pick up the control. Rem is trying not to laugh beside her. Mau goes back the two steps it took him to get that far and sits down on the couch.

“I'd like some of the chips when you get them open,” he says. ᴹ

“That can be arranged.” ᵁ

She still has hold of his shirt. He has to point at the bag to get her to let go. When she opens it she counts three chips into his hand, eats one herself and passes the bag to Rem. Rem sets it where she can reach it while she watches. Taihei comes around the chair with two glasses. Umaru takes one of them, pours out half the Coke and gives it to him without looking up from the screen.

They get to see her remember how to play. At the first turn she goes into the wall, then tells Rem she wanted to check whether the wall was there. At the next she takes the corner too early and goes into another wall. Ori hears that part of the explanation from the door and wants to know why all the walls are worth checking. Umaru puts the game down long enough to show her the corner and how it looks the first time you come to it. Ori sits on the arm of the couch. Chloe picks a spot where she can see past her. Kenya brings in one more chair and offers it to Alice, who tells him she likes the floor and helps him put the chair where Ryota can get to it.

Yuka comes in when they are debating whether the thing at the edge of the track counts as part of the road. She sees Mau in the middle of the couch with a hand on the bag of chips and three people between him and the door.

“I see how it is,” she says. ʸ

He looks up to see her smiling.

“Can I sit with him?” Yuka asks Umaru. ʸ

“As long as you don't take him away or distract him when I need a refill.” ᵁ

“That seems reasonable. Move your hand a little, Mau.” ʸ

He moves the chips. The couch is about a person short of the room they need. He offers to make himself smaller. Yuka has just enough time to put a cushion against the arm before he changes into a slime and rolls over the place she wants to sit. She stretches out where he was, settles her shoulders and holds both arms out to catch him as he comes back. He lands against her with a little bounce. Umaru looks back to establish that he can still reach the table. A new bag of chips appears there and she turns straight back to the screen.

“Approved.” ᵁ

After the first round Ori wants a turn. Umaru shows her the controls and starts a race at the lowest speed. Ori asks to try it one faster, so Umaru puts it up one and lets her run straight into the grass. Ori figures out how to get back onto the road without giving up the control. Chloe talks her through the last turn from the floor. Milim wants to go next. She can't get over the fact that the chair stays in one place while she can make the thing go all the way around the track. She leans so far on the first bend that Yuka has to catch her with one foot to keep her from sliding onto the floor.

They stop for supper because Shuna gets to the door and tells them how long the food has been waiting. Umaru has to choose where to leave the control. She sets it down in the place she can see from the table. During supper Frieren asks Mau how the spell held up. He gives her the full account, including what happened to the bowl, with an interruption from Yuka whenever she has a detail he left out. Frieren asks him what he'll change for the next bird. He says he's thinking about getting two plates of fruit and waiting to see which one Yuka takes first.

“Get three,” Yuka says. “I'm going to want some from the other plate, too.” ʸ

Makima brings a bowl back from the kitchen and stops to see if the chair beside Bellion is free. Ori tells her she saved it, then wants to know if any of the blue hands wrote back to her. They did. Makima got permission from the woman who answered to bring Ori the little note she'd tucked in with the reply. It says that the man was glad to see his sister when she got home, and asks whether the little one who lent him a pencil could be told he still has it. Makima gives Ori the paper and waits until she has read it to sit down.

“Can I ask him to keep it?” Ori asks. ᴼ

“Would you like me to bring that answer tomorrow?” ᴹᵏ

Ori says yes, then realizes she wants to write it herself and goes to ask Frieren for a pencil she can use. Makima moves the bowl so Ori will have room when she comes back. Mau catches her looking at the little spot she's cleared. For a moment she smiles at it as though she can already see Ori come back to sit there.

Umaru asks if someone can pass the bread from that end of the table. Makima puts the dish where she can reach it. Umaru takes a piece and holds the dish out for Rem. It takes the bread a while to make it all the way around because everybody wants some.

Bocchi plays after they eat. Mau had remembered the melody in the wrong key. She finds it after a few false starts because he can still hum the part that comes after the bit he's trying to get. She plays it once for him, stops to see if he wants another verse, and finishes the whole song while the people who are coming back in stop walking long enough to listen. Rimuru asks her if she wants that version in the library. She looks from her hands to the screen and says she'd like to play it through one more time first.

They put a movie on after the games. It takes them longer to agree what to watch than it took Umaru to teach Ori the controls. Milim has strong opinions about a drawing on one of the cards and knows nothing about what's inside it. Yuta gets a second chair for Maki. Shizue and Rimuru come in from taking cups to the kitchen and sit together. Senku pulls one curtain over to see how much better the picture gets. Byakuya tells him he can move it the rest of the way now if he wants, because people have stopped getting up.

Mau is still on the couch when the lights go down. Yuka has made room for Ori's feet at the other end and puts a hand on him when she thinks he's about to move. He could get her a drink without going anywhere. She asks what he was going to do. He says he was going to turn around so he can see the screen. She helps him do it.

Umaru holds up the empty bowl as the first music starts. Mau gets it full again. Then she passes it to Ori, who takes some and reaches back to give it to him, too. Yuka rests her chin against him. There's a laugh from the floor before the picture has got to the part he thinks is funny, and when it gets there he hears a different person laugh. He lets the bowl stay where it is between him and Yuka. There'll be enough to go around if somebody asks for more.
