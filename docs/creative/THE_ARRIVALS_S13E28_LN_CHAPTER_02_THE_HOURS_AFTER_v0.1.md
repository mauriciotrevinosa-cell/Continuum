# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 78
## Chapter 14 — The Hours After

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 78
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S13E28
**Continuity state:** B+310, afternoon into night. The house waits. Yuka doesn't leave the bed. People come in and out: food nobody eats, Ori at Mau's feet, Gojo in the doorway, Makima at the end of the hall, not coming in. For some hours the house believes there is only waiting left. At night, Mau convulses. The bleeds reopen behind his eyes. Rimuru relays Ciel's updates. Noise holds what it can from inside, and the load keeps building. Shoko says she can close them, but they will keep opening while whatever is in him keeps building.

*When: From early afternoon, when Shoko says he's stable, into the night. Yuka will remember later that she never once looked at the clock.*

The house goes quiet in a way it never does.

Not silent. There are still people in it. But everyone moves softly, as if the floors have become thin, and voices drop when they pass the stairs, and the kitchen, which is never empty, is empty for an hour in the middle of the afternoon while Shuna sits at the table with her hands in her lap and doesn't cook anything.

Yuka sits on the edge of the bed.

She hasn't moved since they put him down. She's still holding his hand. Shoko made her change out of her shirt, which had his blood on it, and she did that without letting go, twisting awkwardly out of one sleeve and then the other while Shoko helped, so that the hand never left his. Somebody brought her a clean one. She doesn't remember who.

His face is very still. His eyes are closed. Every so often she looks at the corners of them to see if anything is coming out. Nothing is.

He's breathing. She counts the breaths. She doesn't mean to. She just finds herself doing it, the way she counted the days, once, in a different room, waiting for a different door.

* * *

People come in.

Rem brings soup. She puts it on the table by the bed and says, “For when you're hungry,” ᴿᵉ and goes away, and the soup gets cold. Later Shuna brings another bowl and takes the cold one away, and the second bowl gets cold too.

Maki comes and stands in the doorway with her arms folded. She doesn't say anything. After a while she comes in and sits on the floor with her back against the wall and stays there for an hour, and then gets up and squeezes Yuka's shoulder once, hard, on her way out.

Gojo doesn't come in. He stands in the doorway. He's put the blindfold back on, but Yuka can feel him looking anyway, at Mau, with the eyes underneath. He stands there for a long time. Then he says, very quietly, “He's still in there. I can see him,” ᴳ and goes away before she can ask what he means.

Ori doesn't leave.

She's at the foot of the bed, sitting cross-legged on top of the blanket with her hand on Mau's ankle, and she's been there since the clinic. Lantern's shape is coiled in her shadow, very still. Every so often Fern comes to the door and asks if she wants to eat, or rest, or come and help with something, and every time Ori shakes her head without taking her eyes off Mau's face.

Yuka doesn't make her go. She thinks about it once and decides she doesn't have the right.

* * *

In the middle of the afternoon, Makima comes to the end of the hall.

Yuka sees her through the open door. She doesn't come any closer. She stands at the top of the stairs with her hands folded in front of her and her face perfectly composed, the way her face always is, and looks down the hall toward the room. Beru is in front of the door. He doesn't move.

Makima doesn't ask to come in.

She just stands there. For a long time. Longer than Gojo did. And Yuka, watching her across the length of the hall, sees something she has never seen on Makima's face before, which is nothing at all. Not the polite little smile. Not the calm. Just a stillness so complete it looks like somebody holding their breath.

Then she turns and goes back down the stairs.

Yuka doesn't think about it. She doesn't have room to think about it.

* * *

Toward evening, Shoko comes back to check him. Rimuru comes with her.

She does everything again, slowly, carefully. His breathing, his eyes. Two fingers at his wrist for the first pulse, her palm flat on his chest for the second. She leaves her hand there through several beats before she moves it to the sides of his head. Rimuru listens to Ciel and relays the numbers quietly. Yuka doesn't understand half of them and listens anyway.

When she's done, Shoko sits back.

“Holding,” she says. ˢʰ

Yuka lets out a breath she didn't know she was holding.

“The bleeds are still closed,” Shoko says. “His brain's quiet. Noise says the load's sitting where it is; it's not building any more, not fast. Ciel agrees.” ˢʰ

“So he's—” ʸ

“So now we wait,” Shoko says. “He sleeps. His body does what it can. We watch. When he wakes up, we see what's left.” ˢʰ

*What's left.* Yuka hears that, and puts it somewhere she doesn't have to look at it.

“But he'll wake up,” she says. ʸ

Shoko looks at her for a moment.

“I think so,” she says. “Yes. I think so.” ˢʰ

It's the most anyone has said all day, and Yuka holds onto it with both hands.

* * *

The news goes through the house the way news does, and the house breathes out.

She can feel it, even from up here. Voices downstairs getting a little louder. Someone in the kitchen, finally, and the sound of a pan. Milim's laugh, once, cut off short as if she's remembered, but there. Shuna brings up a third bowl of soup and this time Yuka eats some of it, because Shoko is standing there watching her until she does.

Rimuru comes back and sits on the other side of the bed for a while. He doesn't say much. He tells her that Shizue has made up a bed for Ori in the room next door if she wants it, and Ori says no without looking up, and Rimuru says that's fine, and puts a blanket around Ori's shoulders instead.

Yuta comes in with Maki. He puts his hand on Mau's chest for a minute with his eyes closed, the way Shoko did, and then nods to himself and takes it away.

“He feels like himself,” Yuta says quietly. “Tired. But like himself.” ʸᵗ

Yuka nods. She doesn't trust her voice.

It's evening, then night. The lamps get lit. The house goes to bed, slowly, in ones and twos, the way it does. Ori falls asleep at the foot of the bed with her hand still on Mau's ankle, and Yuka doesn't move her; she just takes the blanket off her own lap and puts it over her.

And for a few hours, everyone in the house believes that the worst part is over.

* * *

It starts a little after midnight.

Yuka is half asleep, sitting up against the headboard with his hand in hers. She doesn't hear anything. She *feels* it: his hand, in hers, going suddenly rigid.

She opens her eyes.

His whole body has gone stiff. His back is arching, slowly, off the mattress, as if something is pulling it from underneath. His jaw is clenched so hard she can hear his teeth grinding. His eyes are still closed, but behind the lids she can see them moving, fast, too fast, jerking from side to side.

Then he starts to shake.

“Shoko,” Yuka says. Then, much louder: “*Shoko!*” ʸ

Ori wakes up screaming.

* * *

It's worse than the first time.

Shoko gets there in seconds. Yuta right behind her. Rimuru. The room fills with people and light and voices, and Yuka has her arms around Mau's head to keep it from hitting the headboard, and he's shaking so hard the whole bed is moving, and there's blood coming from his nose again. From the corners of his eyes.

“Ciel says they're opening again,” Rimuru tells Shoko. “Both eyes, behind—” ᴿ

“I've got them,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

“She says they're opening *faster than*—” ᴿ

“I know!” ˢʰ

Her hands are on him. Yuta's hands are on him. Yuka can feel their energy going into him, a flood of it, and she can feel it not being enough. Every time they close something, something else opens. It's like trying to hold water in your cupped hands.

Rimuru goes still for a moment, listening. He looks at Mau before he speaks again.

“Noise is holding what it can. There's still more coming.” ᴿ

Yuka has both arms around Mau's head. She can't imagine being close enough to what hurts him to hold it back and still having to ask someone else to help.

* * *

Somewhere in the middle of it, he stops shaking.

Just for a moment. His body goes slack all at once, as if a rope's been cut, and he lies in her arms breathing in short ragged gasps, his face slick with sweat and blood. Shoko doesn't stop working. Yuta doesn't stop.

“Can you close them?” Rimuru asks. ᴿ

“I can close them,” Shoko says. She doesn't look up. Her voice is very steady. “I can keep closing them. As long as I've got anything left. As long as Yuta has.” ˢʰ

“But.” ᴿ

“But they're going to keep opening.” ˢʰ

She finally looks up. Her face is grey with exhaustion. There's blood on her hands to the wrists.

“The domain stopped. This is still building,” she says. “It's slower, but every time it builds far enough, something gives. I can fix what gives. I can't stop it giving.” ˢʰ

“Can we get it out?” Rimuru asks. ᴿ

He waits for Ciel to put the question to Noise. Yuka sees the answer reach him before he says it. His eyes go to her hands, where she's still keeping Mau's head from moving.

“Noise says it's part of him,” Rimuru tells her. “We'd take him with it.” ᴿ

Nobody says anything.

Yuka is holding his head in her lap. She's looking at his face. At the blood under his eyes. At his lips, which are moving, very slightly, as if he's trying to say something, or dreaming that he is.

And then his body goes rigid again, and starts to shake, and the blood comes faster, and there's nothing anyone in that room can do except keep holding on.
