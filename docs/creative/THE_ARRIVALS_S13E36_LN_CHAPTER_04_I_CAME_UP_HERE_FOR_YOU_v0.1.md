He sets his hand on the door when they get to it. Yuka can feel him stop walking. She doesn't let go of his sleeve yet.

“Did you want to come in?” she asks. ʸ

“You are.” ʸ

She waits until he's inside and has closed it behind himself. Then she lets him have his sleeve back. He gives her the tin. She sets it on the low table by the window and lifts the lid. There are two squares in it, one of them broken down the middle just enough that there'll be a little edge for someone to steal.

Mau bends to look, hands on his knees. “Am I still supposed to guess who made them?” ᴹ

“You can finish guessing downstairs. I came up here for you.” ʸ

The words go out just the way she wanted. The colour that goes right up into her cheeks afterwards is altogether less organised. He straightens up. She can tell the moment he takes her seriously because he looks back at her without saying the thing he must have been about to say. It is deeply inconvenient to be looked at that patiently when she was the one who brought him up here.

“I decided downstairs,” she says, because he still hasn't moved and she wants to know if she can say it a second time. “Just now. After we put the cups back.” ʸ

“I was hoping that was what you meant.” ᴹ

“And you still asked about the chocolate?” ʸ

“I didn't know how long you were going to make me guess.” ᴹ

She gets a finger under his chin and turns his head the last little bit so he has to look at her instead of the tin.

“I want you here,” she says. “That's the whole clue.” ʸ

“I can do that.” ᴹ

He sets his hand at her waist, lightly enough that she could forget it was there if she were capable of forgetting. She catches it with her own and takes a step back. He follows. The back of her heel finds the rug where it goes thicker near the bed. They have come about four feet to get here. It has made him look at her like he's been trying to get here since breakfast.

They still eat a little chocolate.

Yuka gives him the whole square and keeps the broken one. It starts melting while they stand there deciding who's going to go first. She takes a bite and closes her eyes to find the other thing underneath the sweetness. Something faintly bitter. Then Mau touches her wrist and she opens them again.

“You got a bit here,” he says. ᴹ

She puts her tongue to one corner of her mouth. He shakes his head. She tries the other. He shakes his head again, despite the fact that this time she knows she got it.

“You're doing this on purpose,” she says. ʸ

“A little.” ᴹ

She tries to tell what he's going to do from the way his hand is holding her. Then she gives up and kisses him while he's still deciding. His lips part in surprise. He has dark chocolate on his tongue even though she gave him the sweeter piece. She takes her time finding out how that happened. When he catches her by the waist with both hands and draws her all the way against him, she has to fold the last of the chocolate into the paper instead of letting it go.

“Put that down,” he says, very softly. ᴹ

“Your arm's in the way.” ʸ

He looks down. One arm loosens enough for her to reach behind herself. She finds the edge of the table with her thumb, leaves the paper just where it is, and takes his arm back for herself. There is a little sound he makes when she pulls him in again. She likes it enough to put her mouth by his ear and tell him so. He turns his face toward her before she quite gets the last word out.

“You know,” he says, “I still had one thing I was going to ask for.” ᴹ

“What?” ʸ

He gets a lock of her hair out from where it's caught in his sleeve. He puts it behind her ear and leaves his hand there, warm against her cheek.

“Could we take a while up here?” ᴹ

It makes her laugh, a small breath across his mouth. “I was going to try to talk you into that myself.” ʸ

“You can still try.” ᴹ

She puts her hands against his chest and gives him one very gentle push. The backs of his knees find the bed. He sits down and has to lift his head to look at her. Now that she has him where she wants him, she has no particular wish to hurry.

* * *

From where Mau's sitting, he has to tip his head back a little to look at her. Yuka likes having that second to see what it does to him when she takes her time. She puts one knee beside him and one hand flat at his shoulder. His hands go to her waist only when the mattress moves under her knee, and she takes the help and settles across his legs.

“You never asked me what I wanted while we were downstairs,” she says. ʸ

“You were pretty good at telling me.” ᴹ

“Mm.” She plucks the loose thread at the edge of his sleeve, tucks it round her finger and lets it go. “You asked if you could stay after tea.” ʸ

“I wanted to hear you say yes.” ᴹ

Her ears get hot. She watches his eyes go straight to the one she tucked her hair behind earlier, then back to her face. He's halfway to a smile. She cups his face with both hands to forestall whatever he's going to say about the ears and kisses him.

He leans into her kiss a little too far. She laughs and goes down with him, spreading her palm across the back of his head so he won't hit the wall. The mattress dips. She waits until it settles to get her hand out from underneath him. By then he's got his hand against her cheek. She turns into it and kisses the pad of his thumb before she comes back to his mouth.

His arm comes round her the way it did in the kitchen. The sleeve catches underneath her, and she props herself up long enough to get it loose and put his hand where she wants it.

“That's better,” she says, barely loud enough for him to hear. ʸ

He keeps the arm where she put it. When she pulls back to breathe, she rests her forehead against his. A strand of hair sticks to her mouth. He goes to move it. She catches his fingers on the way, kisses the tip of each one, and puts his palm back against her cheek when she's done. It takes him another second to remember the hair. She blows it away herself.

“I love you,” he tells her. ᴹ

“I know,” she says, and hears how easily it comes out. There was a time when she would have asked him how long he'd been thinking about that, or whether he'd say it again when they were downstairs. Now she can just reach around him and say, “I love you, too.” ʸ

Yuka starts to sit up and gets stopped by her own elbow. She's been holding herself away from putting any weight on him for so long that when she moves it, her hand shakes against the sheet.

“You can lie down,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Where would you like me to go?” ʸ

He reaches for her arm and she gives it to him. He moves it off the sheet and puts it across his ribs. As soon as she lets herself settle, the breath she's been saving goes out of her all at once. She had been planning what to say next. With her ear here she can hear how fast his heart is going. She stays a little longer to listen.

“Was that what you were going to ask me?” she says. “Just for a while up here?” ʸ

“Part of it.” ᴹ

She waits. His fingers move once in her hair and stop. She lifts her head far enough that he can see her.

“Tell me the rest.” ʸ

“I want to make love to you,” he says. ᴹ

From this close she gets to see him say all of it, including the swallow afterwards. She thought she'd know what he'd look like when he asked. Somehow she hadn't pictured him as the one who'd need a second.

“Yes,” she says, and kisses the corner of his mouth when it starts to pull into a smile. “Could you close the curtain first?” ʸ

He puts a hand up without shifting his head from the pillow. The curtain rings rattle halfway across and jam.

Yuka turns her face into his shoulder. “I can get it.” ʸ

“It's thinking about it,” Mau says, with such misplaced confidence that she starts laughing all over again. ᴹ

He gives up on Authority, rolls over onto his feet, and goes to the window. She takes his place on the pillow while he's gone. When he comes back there is still a thin stripe of light from the gap at the edge. It falls across his wrist as he braces his hand beside her head. She puts her hand on the back of his neck and draws him down to her. He takes a breath against her mouth. She feels him wait there, a hair's breadth away, till she moves the rest of the way herself.

His sleeve brushes her cheek when he lifts his arm. She gets hold of the cuff before it can go past her.

“Wait. I want that hand back.” ʸ

He gives it to her, open. She closes her eyes and traces the old scar with her thumb. Across the middle of his palm, the skin is smooth. It catches near his wrist where the line gets wider. She goes over that spot again, softer this time, and feels his fingers close around hers before she sees him move.

“Were you going to do something with that?” he asks. ᴹ

She opens her eyes. “I was going to put it here.” ʸ

She brings his hand to her waist. The back of his hand has gone cool from being by the window. She can feel the warmth in his palm through the thin cotton at her waist. He lays it there as carefully as he did when they came inside, and when she shifts an inch, the cotton slips under his hand. She'd like him to come with her this time.

“Hold me like you mean it.” ʸ

She feels the little jump in his chest when he laughs. Then his fingers gather the cotton and draw her nearer, firm enough that the pillowcase creases under her cheek. She can feel exactly where each finger rests. She takes a breath to say the clever thing she had ready, and it comes in a short hitch that gives her away first.

“Oh,” she says. ʸ

“That's what I thought you'd say.” ᴹ

She catches the hair at the nape of his neck and kisses him for it. Their noses bump. She turns her head until there's room for both of them and tries again. This time his mouth follows hers when she pulls back for air. He catches himself with his eyes still shut. She could pretend she didn't notice. She comes back and kisses him at the corner of his mouth so he knows she did.

“You can do that again,” she tells him. ʸ

“Which part?” ᴹ

“The bit where you came after me.” ʸ

He studies her face for just long enough that she feels herself start to blush. His thumb moves once against her side. Then he leans in, and this time she holds still to let him come to her. The corner of his mouth pulls up against hers. She tastes the dark chocolate for a second, bitter at the back of the sweetness. When she draws back, the air feels cool on her lower lip. She brushes it against his again before she's even finished breathing in.

Mau stays when she lowers her head. Her hand is still halfway round the back of his neck. If she lets her fingers go slack, the little ends of his hair fall out between them. She does it twice before he notices what she's doing.

“Have you found something?” he asks. ᴹ

“Something I like.” ʸ

She separates another curl with one finger. He bows his head a little more to let her do it. She likes that too. Earlier he'd sat so patiently while she folded back the edge of his collar, as if it really did need her attention. Only now does she think to ask how much of that had been for her.

“Did you like the collar the way I left it?” she asks. ʸ

“I liked that you came back for it.” ᴹ

“I was already sitting next to you.” ʸ

“You kept finding reasons to get nearer. I nearly asked you to stop fixing it so I could kiss you.” ᴹ

Yuka can feel the heat moving up past the collar of her own shirt. He'd been close enough to do it. She'd have had a real fight on her hands pretending it was an inconvenience, with Jinshi and Maomao three feet away.

“Nearly?” ʸ

He brings his hand up and waits with his thumb against her jaw. The contrast between the touch and the look he's giving her is enough to make her reach for his arm. There isn't anything she needs to ask him to do. He leans down of his own accord this time. His mouth is warm from the last kiss, and the breath she was saving for a laugh goes out quietly against his cheek. When he straightens, the place she was holding at his elbow is warm too. She keeps a finger there until he settles again.

The pillow has worked its way up behind her, where it keeps pushing her chin down. She tugs at one corner, and Mau lifts his arm so she can pull it flat. While he's up she draws him closer by the shoulder, until she can rest her other arm over his back without stretching. She can still feel the line the pillow seam left at the back of her shoulder. The linen under her ear is cooler on the side they haven't used. She settles into it and the sound of the room goes muffled on that side. Mau's breath is still close enough to feel on the cheek she's turned up to him.

“Here,” she says. ʸ

A corner of his sleeve has folded in against her wrist. She pulls it loose, then stops at the cuff and looks up at him. He follows her eyes down to the button. When she gets it undone, he works his arm free while she holds the sleeve. The inside of the cotton is warm where his shoulder's been. After he gets the other arm out, she can see the little crease it left across the back of his forearm. She runs a fingertip along it. His hand turns toward hers on the sheet.

“Do you want me to keep going?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” She lets go of the fabric and puts her hand over his. “You can ask me for things too, you know.” ʸ

He looks down at their hands. She waits. There's a trick to waiting with Mau when something's important. If she fills the space because she can guess what he wants, he'll let her. Sometimes she likes guessing. Right now she wants to give him the time to say it.

“Put your arms around me?” he says at last. ᴹ

She puts them around him. She has a second to see the look on his face before he comes nearer. It stays with her even once his face is too close to see all of it. His arm was raised to help her get settled. It comes down slowly now, and she feels the weight of it cross her back.

They make love with the last light coming through the curtain. She sees it cross the back of his hand before her eyes close. The mattress gives under his elbow first. Then comes the warmth of his cheek. She turns enough to meet him, and her ear comes free of the pillow all at once. She can hear her own breathing again. It's quicker than she'd thought. She puts her hand on his shoulder and feels a breath of his catch at the same time hers does. When she opens her eyes, his brow is furrowed the way it gets when he wants to hear her through a room full of people. She sets two fingers between his eyebrows. She can feel the crease release just before he lifts them, and she can't help laughing.

“What?” he asks, smiling now in spite of himself. ᴹ

“I like being this close to you,” she says. “That's all I was thinking.” ʸ

His face changes at that. She feels the change in the hand at her waist, too. His grip eases and she has room to move in closer herself. She turns her cheek against his. A little roughness along his jaw catches when she moves. It makes her want to feel how soft his mouth is again. She turns back and kisses him. She wants another kiss and takes a full breath first. With her palm flat between his shoulders, she can feel him take one with her.

For a little while neither of them says anything. The sheet gathers under her heel. She slides it free just enough to set her foot down, and the mattress gives a small creak that she feels through her back. Mau has turned his head toward the sound. His hair brushes across her cheek with the movement. She smooths it back and catches his eye.

“Were you listening for the door?” she asks. ʸ

“For you.” ᴹ

She hadn't meant to make a sound at all. She couldn't tell him where it came from if he asked. He'd turned toward it so quickly she'd thought he was listening for something down the hall. She brings her thumb to the side of his mouth, where she can feel the shape of the smile he's trying not to give her yet.

“You get a turn,” she says. ʸ

“At what?” ᴹ

“Tell me something you want to see.” ʸ

He looks down at her hand. When he brings his own up, she thinks he's going to move it. He puts it over hers instead.

“I want to see your face when I kiss you,” he says. ᴹ

“You can see my face now.” ʸ

“You close your eyes right before I get there.” ᴹ

“All right. Come back and let me try.” ʸ

She pushes herself up on one elbow so the afternoon light falls across both of them. The sheet pulls tight next to her wrist. “Very. And you?” ʸ

He nods. A loose strand of her hair moves against her temple when he breathes out. She waits, because he's still looking at her.

“I want you to stay right here a little longer,” he says. ᴹ

She slides her hand higher between his shoulders. His back softens under her palm as he settles, a little at a time, until the mattress takes the weight he'd been holding off her. The space beside her neck warms with each breath. He's asked so plainly that for a second all she wants is to keep hearing it. All through tea he was ready before she could finish telling him what she wanted. His face comes down against her neck. She can feel the smile there, even before he says, “That's my line.” ᴹ
