# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 78
## Chapter 31 — I Came Up Here for You

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 78
**Reading order:** 31
**Source episode:** S13E36
**Continuity state:** B+324, late afternoon and before supper. Maomao's tasting has worn off. Yuka brings normal chocolate upstairs and tells Mau she wants time alone with him. They make love at her invitation, then linger in bed talking and teasing before supper. Later Yuka puts on her anthracite and papaya 2021 McLaren hoodie and black shorts, and Mau his red Ferrari zip hoodie and gray sweatpants. She asks him to bring the good blanket later for their movie.

*When: Later the same day. Mau carries the two ordinary squares in their tin. Yuka has kept his sleeve all the way up the stairs.*

He sets his hand on the door when they get to it. Yuka can feel him stop walking. She doesn't let go of his sleeve yet.

“Did you want to come in?” she asks. ʸ

He looks from her hand to the door. “I thought I was coming in.” ᴹ

“You are.” ʸ

She waits until he's inside and has closed it behind himself. Then she lets him have his sleeve back. He gives her the tin. She sets it on the low table by the window and lifts the lid. There are two squares in it, one of them broken down the middle just enough that there'll be a little edge for someone to steal.

Mau bends to look, hands on his knees. “Am I still supposed to guess who made them?” ᴹ

“You can finish guessing downstairs. I came up here for you.” ʸ

The words go out just the way she wanted. The colour that goes right up into her cheeks afterwards is altogether less organised. He straightens up. She can tell the moment he takes her seriously because he looks back at her without saying the thing he must have been about to say. It is deeply inconvenient to be looked at that patiently when she was the one who brought him up here.

“I decided downstairs,” she says, because he still hasn't moved and she wants to know if she can say it a second time. “Just now. After we put the cups back.” ʸ

“I was hoping that was what you meant.” ᴹ

“And you still asked about the chocolate?” ʸ

“I didn't know how long you were going to make me guess.” ᴹ

She gets a finger under his chin and turns his head the last little bit so he has to look at her instead of the tin.

“I want you here,” she says. “That's the whole clue.” ʸ

“I can do that.” ᴹ

He sets his hand at her waist, lightly enough that she could forget it was there if she were capable of forgetting. She catches it with her own and takes a step back. He follows. The back of her heel finds the rug where it goes thicker near the bed. They have come about four feet to get here. It has made him look at her like he's been trying to get here since breakfast.

* * *

They still eat a little chocolate.

Yuka gives him the whole square and keeps the broken one. It starts melting while they stand there deciding who's going to go first. She takes a bite and closes her eyes to find the other thing underneath the sweetness. Something faintly bitter. Then Mau touches her wrist and she opens them again.

“You got a bit here,” he says. ᴹ

She puts her tongue to one corner of her mouth. He shakes his head. She tries the other. He shakes his head again, despite the fact that this time she knows she got it.

“You're doing this on purpose,” she says. ʸ

“A little.” ᴹ

She tries to tell what he's going to do from the way his hand is holding her. Then she gives up and kisses him while he's still deciding. His lips part in surprise. He has dark chocolate on his tongue even though she gave him the sweeter piece. She takes her time finding out how that happened. When he catches her by the waist with both hands and draws her all the way against him, she has to fold the last of the chocolate into the paper instead of letting it go.

“Put that down,” he says, very softly. ᴹ

“Your arm's in the way.” ʸ

He looks down. One arm loosens enough for her to reach behind herself. She finds the edge of the table with her thumb, leaves the paper just where it is, and takes his arm back for herself. There is a little sound he makes when she pulls him in again. She likes it enough to put her mouth by his ear and tell him so. He turns his face toward her before she quite gets the last word out.

“You know,” he says, “I still had one thing I was going to ask for.” ᴹ

“What?” ʸ

He gets a lock of her hair out from where it's caught in his sleeve. He puts it behind her ear and leaves his hand there, warm against her cheek.

“Could we take a while up here?” ᴹ

It makes her laugh, a small breath across his mouth. “I was going to try to talk you into that myself.” ʸ

“You can still try.” ᴹ

She puts her hands against his chest and gives him one very gentle push. The backs of his knees find the bed. He sits down and has to lift his head to look at her. Now that she has him where she wants him, she has no particular wish to hurry.

* * *

From where Mau's sitting, he has to tip his head back a little to look at her. Yuka likes having that second to see what it does to him when she takes her time. She puts one knee beside him and one hand flat at his shoulder. His hands go to her waist only when the mattress moves under her knee, and she takes the help and settles across his legs.

“You never asked me what I wanted while we were downstairs,” she says. ʸ

“You were pretty good at telling me.” ᴹ

“Mm.” She plucks the loose thread at the edge of his sleeve, tucks it round her finger and lets it go. “You asked if you could stay after tea.” ʸ

“I wanted to hear you say yes.” ᴹ

Her ears get hot. She watches his eyes go straight to the one she tucked her hair behind earlier, then back to her face. He's halfway to a smile. She cups his face with both hands to forestall whatever he's going to say about the ears and kisses him.

He leans into her kiss a little too far. She laughs and goes down with him, spreading her palm across the back of his head so he won't hit the wall. The mattress dips. She waits until it settles to get her hand out from underneath him. By then he's got his hand against her cheek. She turns into it and kisses the pad of his thumb before she comes back to his mouth.

His arm comes round her the way it did in the kitchen. The sleeve catches underneath her, and she props herself up long enough to get it loose and put his hand where she wants it.

“That's better,” she says, barely loud enough for him to hear. ʸ

He keeps the arm where she put it. When she pulls back to breathe, she rests her forehead against his. A strand of hair sticks to her mouth. He goes to move it. She catches his fingers on the way, kisses the tip of each one, and puts his palm back against her cheek when she's done. It takes him another second to remember the hair. She blows it away herself.

“I love you,” he tells her. ᴹ

“I know,” she says, and hears how easily it comes out. There was a time when she would have asked him how long he'd been thinking about that, or whether he'd say it again when they were downstairs. Now she can just reach around him and say, “I love you, too.” ʸ

Yuka starts to sit up and gets stopped by her own elbow. She's been holding herself away from putting any weight on him for so long that when she moves it, her hand shakes against the sheet.

“You can lie down,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Where would you like me to go?” ʸ

He reaches for her arm and she gives it to him. He moves it off the sheet and puts it across his ribs. As soon as she lets herself settle, the breath she's been saving goes out of her all at once. She had been planning what to say next. With her ear here she can hear how fast his heart is going. She stays a little longer to listen.

“Was that what you were going to ask me?” she says. “Just for a while up here?” ʸ

“Part of it.” ᴹ

She waits. His fingers move once in her hair and stop. She lifts her head far enough that he can see her.

“Tell me the rest.” ʸ

“I want to make love to you,” he says. ᴹ

From this close she gets to see him say all of it, including the swallow afterwards. She thought she'd know what he'd look like when he asked. Somehow she hadn't pictured him as the one who'd need a second.

“Yes,” she says, and kisses the corner of his mouth when it starts to pull into a smile. “Could you close the curtain first?” ʸ

He puts a hand up without shifting his head from the pillow. The curtain rings rattle halfway across and jam.

Yuka turns her face into his shoulder. “I can get it.” ʸ

“It's thinking about it,” Mau says, with such misplaced confidence that she starts laughing all over again. ᴹ

He gives up on Authority, rolls over onto his feet, and goes to the window. She takes his place on the pillow while he's gone. When he comes back there is still a thin stripe of light from the gap at the edge. It falls across his wrist as he braces his hand beside her head. She puts her hand on the back of his neck and draws him down to her. He takes a breath against her mouth. She feels him wait there, a hair's breadth away, till she moves the rest of the way herself.

His sleeve brushes her cheek when he lifts his arm. She gets hold of the cuff before it can go past her.

“Wait. I want that hand back.” ʸ

He gives it to her, open. There's an old scar through the palm. She knows it well enough to find it with a finger in the dark. She runs her thumb across it once and brings the hand back to her waist.

“There?” he asks. ᴹ

“There. Hold me like you mean it.” ʸ

He lets out a little laugh. She would ask what was funny, but then his fingers close, warm and sure, and she has to take a breath before she can say anything at all.

“Oh,” she says. ʸ

“That's what I thought you'd say.” ᴹ

She gets a hand into his hair and kisses him for it. A minute ago he was watching her face to see if she'd make up her mind. Now he has his eyes shut, and when she breaks their kiss his mouth follows hers a little before he catches himself. She likes seeing that. She comes back just far enough to kiss him at the corner of his mouth and feels his smile against hers.

They make love with the last light coming through the curtain. Yuka loses sight of the bright line when she closes her eyes. She keeps her hand on Mau's back, where she can feel the quick rise of his breath against her palm. When he goes still, she opens her eyes and finds him looking down at her.

“Still good?” he asks. ᴹ

“Very. And you?” ʸ

He nods. She waits, close enough that his next breath lifts a hair at her temple.

“I want you to stay right here a little longer,” he says. ᴹ

She moves her hand up between his shoulders and holds him where he is. It's the way he's asked that makes her take her time answering. All through tea he was ready before she could finish telling him what she wanted. Right now she has one thing to do for him, and it's exactly what she wants to do.

“I can do that,” she says. ʸ

His face comes down against her neck. She can feel the smile there, even before he says, “That's my line.” ᴹ

She lets her laugh come out against his hair. His hand stays at her waist. She keeps hers against his back until she feels it rise with a slower breath, and when he lifts his head she puts her lips to his forehead first. He turns toward her kiss, and she gives him another one where he can give it back.

Her shoulder starts to ache from reaching around him. She gives it a little roll and Mau shifts his weight to give her room. She turns onto her side and he comes down beside her on the pillow. The edge of the sheet slides over her shoulder. She reaches down to get it back around both of them and discovers that the whole corner has come loose at his feet. Mau tries to pull it straight with his heel. She tucks her corner under her chin and holds on when she feels him tug.

“I can get cold, too,” he says. ᴹ

“You can come over here.” ʸ

He does. She lifts the sheet for him and rests her hand on his chest while he gets comfortable. His heartbeat is slower now. She settles her palm flat and stays there until she can feel the rhythm through her fingers. It makes her smile to remember how fast it was when she asked him to tell her the rest.

“Was I supposed to go back down for the chocolate?” he asks. ᴹ

“You left it on the table.” ʸ

“I meant when we were in the kitchen. Were you actually offering me any?” ᴹ

“I offered you the edge. You can still have it if you want.” ʸ

He looks toward the table. Neither of them moves.

Yuka lies on her back and watches the narrow line of light slide off the foot of the bed. Mau has his head beside hers. She could turn and kiss him without either of them sitting up. She does it once, to see if he's as close as she thinks.

He is.

“Do you think we missed supper?” she asks. ʸ

“I can hear plates going out.” ᴹ

“How many?” ʸ

He waits a moment. There is another clatter from down the hall. “That could've been one plate or all the plates at once.” ᴹ

Yuka rolls toward him again. He puts his arm round her automatically. This was supposed to be how she finally gets up. She puts her face against the warm part of his neck and starts laughing where he can't see her do it. It doesn't work. She feels the laugh go out of him at the same time.

She gets out from under his arm eventually, then comes back for his hand because she'd like him to get up too. The window is cold enough now that she's going to want something soft for downstairs. She gets into her black shorts and pulls the old McLaren hoodie over her head, getting one of the sleeves tangled until Mau catches the end of it for her. She loves the papaya panel across the dark shoulders even when the rest of the room goes dim enough that she can barely see it. Mau finds his gray sweatpants and his red Ferrari hoodie, slides both arms into it and does the zipper up halfway. She could have told him the red was brighter in this light. Instead she reaches over and straightens the fold on one side of the zipper where it wants to curl in.

“You've been looking at that all afternoon?” he asks. ᴹ

“You weren't wearing this all afternoon.” ʸ

“I meant the fold.” ᴹ

“I can find another one if you like.” ʸ

He looks at her hand. She gets two fingers around one of his again and takes him as far as the door.

“The good blanket,” she says. “Could you get it for later? I want the one big enough for both of us.” ʸ

“I know which one.” ᴹ

“And I might want popcorn.” ʸ

“I know which bowl.” ᴹ

“What if I change my mind?” ʸ

“Then I'll find out what you want.” ᴹ

She looks at him. He's got his hand ready on the handle and he is perfectly prepared to wait until she's had time to say anything else she has in mind. The chocolate wore off hours ago. She opens the door herself and lets him walk with her all the way to supper.
