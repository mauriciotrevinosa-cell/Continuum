She takes his hand. The fingers that were round the glass are cool against hers. She tucks them under the cover with her own until they warm. Then she nudges the lamp a little nearer, turns it off and lies back with him.

* * *

Much later she wakes long enough to find his weight against her again. He still has one hand over hers. Their backs are on the bed and the lamp is out, but he's put his head in almost exactly the same place it was earlier.

“You comfortable?” she whispers. ʸ

“Very,” he says. ᴹ

He sounds far too awake. She works one arm out from under the cover and puts it around his shoulders. His mouth is warm against her skin when he kisses her once, very gently, and she feels him smile when she does the same to his forehead.

“You'll have to tell me if he ever gets the horse,” she says. ʸ

He laughs against her. She feels it more than she hears it. When her eyes shut again, his hand is moving slowly on her back. She can tell when it gets still, and knows he's fallen asleep before she has.

She leaves him his half of the pillow. She doesn't move far enough to give him any reason to use it.
