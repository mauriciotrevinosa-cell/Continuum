# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 78
## Chapter 36 — The Chair by the Lamp

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 78
**Reading order:** 36
**Source episode:** S13E39
**Continuity state:** B+330, evening. Yuka comes to Mau in their room. In the chair by the lamp, she rests his head against her chest and says she will stay. He relaxes, then tells her how much he wants her close. They spend the night together.

*When: Later that evening. Yuka has finished telling Mai about the thread she pinched for Anko's plant. Mau has brought two glasses of water upstairs and remembered the book he left open that morning.*

Yuka can tell how much progress he's made with the book by where he's holding it. Same page, same corner folded down. He's pushed one foot against the leg of the chair to tip himself back, and he's watching the door with the book open on his lap.

“You waiting for somebody?” she asks. ʸ

“I thought I'd see what you did when you came in.” ᴹ

“I was going to take my shoes off.” ʸ

“I'd like to see that.” ᴹ

She turns to give him a look over her shoulder. He has just enough time to pretend to be absorbed in whatever's on the page. She takes her shoes off slowly, all on the same side of the rug, and leaves them where he'll have to see them in the morning. Then she comes back for him.

“Where were you?” she asks, looking down at the book. ʸ

“He was going out to see about a horse.” ᴹ

“That's what he was doing at breakfast.” ʸ

“It's taking a while.” ᴹ

She slips a finger between the pages and closes the book around it. He reaches for the piece of paper on the little table beside him. She keeps her finger there until he's found the place again, then puts the book down.

“Would you make me some room?” she asks. ʸ

He moves one arm. She settles across him with both legs over the side of the chair, then shifts until she can see his face straight on. He'd already had his hands up to help her balance. She takes one of them and sets it on her back, exactly where she wants it, and waits for him to get comfortable.

He doesn't quite. He stays sitting up, head high enough to pass for paying attention. His eyes go to the little table when the glass catches the light.

“You're allowed to lean,” she says. ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

She puts her palm behind his head and draws him down to her chest. He goes easily the moment he knows where she's putting him. His cheek settles against the soft cotton at her collar. Yuka slides the heel of her hand up under the hair at the back of his neck and lets it stay there.

“Rest,” she tells him. “I'll be here. You can just relax.” ʸ

For the first few breaths she feels the places where he's still holding his own weight. Then his arm gets heavier across her back. His knees go loose under hers. He lets out a breath that seems to undo each place his shirt was pulled tight.

She puts her mouth against his hair. She likes that he can do this awake now. The first time after the quarry, the only way she'd known how tired he was had been how fast he fell asleep when he let her put him here. He'd have another thing to do as soon as he woke up. Tonight the only thing waiting for him is a man who still hasn't got his horse.

* * *

From where Mau has his head, he can hear her breathe and feel it a moment before he hears it.

She gives him enough time to forget about the chair under them. He notices the cuff of her sleeve against his ear when she raises her hand to touch his hair. He puts his hand over hers to keep it there. Yuka shifts one leg, worried perhaps that she's been on him too long, and his arm tightens around her waist before he thinks about it. She stops. He feels her look down at the place his hand has caught her.

“Did you think I was getting up?” she asks. ʸ

“A little.” He starts to let go. ᴹ

She catches his wrist and brings his arm back around her. She twists far enough to get both her knees onto the chair, one on either side of his, and takes his hand with her when she settles. His fingers close against her back. She pauses there, watching his face, then puts her weight against him again. He'd have moved one of the cushions if she hadn't already found the place where she wanted to be.

“I'll stay,” she says softly. ʸ

He knows. This is what he asked for without finding the words for it. He turns his face just enough to kiss the edge of her collar, then raises his head. Yuka is looking back at him, far too close for him to have any excuse for taking this long to tell her.

“I want you closer,” he says. ᴹ

Her eyes go down to where she's sitting. “I don't think there's actually any chair left.” ʸ

He laughs a little. “I know where you are. I was lying there thinking how good it feels to know you'll hold me if I let go. And all I wanted was more of you. I've wanted you before. I keep finding out I can want you even closer than that.” ᴹ

She goes red where her throat meets the collar of her shirt. He sees it travel all the way to her cheeks. He has time to see the whole thing because she doesn't look away. She tucks her chin down once, trying it, and then lifts it again.

“You can say things like that to me whenever you want,” she says. ʸ

“I was trying to tell you earlier. You were trying to get bread into my mouth.” ᴹ

Her laugh is small and pleased. She slides her hand down to his waist. When her fingers come up beneath the hem of his shirt, she feels his breath catch against her palm. He tips his face toward her before she's halfway to him. She brushes her mouth against his once, then stops close enough that he can feel her smile.

“More of me?” she asks. ʸ

“Please.” ᴹ

She draws him to her with the hand at his waist. His back comes away from the chair and he brings both arms around her. The first kiss goes a little crooked. She feels him smile, puts one hand against his jaw to hold him still, and tries it again exactly the way she wanted the first time.

When she takes her hand away from his face, he kisses her just beneath her collarbone. Then he lowers his head to her chest again, all on his own. Yuka feels him turn his ear toward her heart. She'd put him there earlier because she could feel him holding himself up. This time she hasn't had to move him an inch.

“I thought you'd had your rest,” she says. ʸ

“I did.” He lifts his head enough to see her. “I'd still like to stay here a little longer.” ᴹ

“Here?” She puts a finger to the place his cheek was. ʸ

He kisses her where she's pointing. His hand comes around her ribs and slides up along the curve of her breast, slowly enough that she can feel the warmth of his palm through the cotton. Yuka lays her hand over his and presses it closer. He looks up at her then. She was going to say something clever about how much he likes it there, but he lowers his mouth to the skin just above her neckline and she lets the breath go without saying it.

“Again,” she says, once she's found her voice. ʸ

He smiles where she's warmest and kisses her again, slower this time. Her fingers loosen in his hair. She tips her head back to give him room and feels his hand firm up against her when she moves. By the time he looks up, she's pulled him close enough that the chair creaks under both of them.

“You can tell, can't you?” she asks. ʸ

He tips his head. “That your heart's going faster?” ᴹ

“I thought you were going to be kind and pretend you couldn't.” ʸ

He lifts his face all the way this time. His eyes go to hers and he holds himself there, close enough for her to see the crease his cheek left in her top. She reaches down with the idea of smoothing it. He takes her hand before she gets very far and brings it to his chest.

“Feel mine,” he says. ᴹ

It's going just as quickly. She leaves her palm where he put it. He was up here waiting for her in the chair with two glasses of water and a book he couldn't read, and he'd wanted her badly enough to go quiet while she took off her shoes. The thought makes her grin. Then he pulls her closer by the waist, quite deliberately this time, and she has to catch herself with an arm round his shoulders. She'd meant to tease him about needing more chair. All she can manage is his name.

He turns his head to look up at her.

“Are you sure you want to get up?” she asks. ʸ

“If you're coming with me.” ᴹ

* * *

Yuka gets her feet on the rug and turns back for his hand.

She tugs before he's quite found his balance. He overshoots and she has to catch him. His knuckles skim the little table and she gets the glass with her free hand just as the base clears the edge.

For a second they stand like that: Mau gripping her shoulder, Yuka holding the glass out at arm's length, both of them trying very hard to keep straight faces.

“Set it down,” he says. ᴹ

“Where?” ʸ

There is plenty of space. They are standing in their own room, which is full of places she can put a glass. He takes it out of her hand, drinks half the water and leaves the rest for her on the table by the bed. She watches him do it and covers her smile with the back of her hand. Then she walks over and drinks the rest, slowly, because he's looking at her and she can take as long as she likes.

He sits beside her when she puts the glass down. She catches his sleeve and he waits while she rolls it back past his wrist. The fabric has gone soft along the seam. She likes this shirt on him. She likes even more that he wears it until she has to steal it off the back of the chair to get it washed.

“You could've asked me to come up earlier,” she says. ʸ

“You were talking to Mai.” ᴹ

“I would've finished talking to Mai.” ʸ

“I like listening for you on the stairs, too.” ᴹ

That one gets her worse than the thing he said in the chair. She pushes the sleeve up a little further than she meant to, then has to roll one side back to make it even. He lets her fuss. When she finally looks at him again he's still waiting right where she left him, with the beginnings of a smile and his hand open on the bed between them. She guides it to her hip and keeps hers over it until she feels him take hold.

He brings his fingers up under her chin.

“Look at me for a second,” he says. ᴹ

She does.

He looks at her long enough that she almost asks what he's looking for. Then he says, with the same simple certainty he used in the chair, “I'm glad I asked.” ᴹ

She kisses him before she can get embarrassed all over again. He makes room for her, and she pulls him with her until they are laughing into each other's mouths at how hard it is to do that without getting tangled in the cover. He gets the edge out from under her elbow. Once she has her arm back, she puts it across him so he'll stay where he is for a second.

“Lie down,” she tells him. ʸ

He does, looking up at her the whole time. She slips a hand behind his neck again and leans across him. This time she has room to feel him reach round her and enough time to see his eyes close when she kisses his throat. She stays there for one more breath. Then she pulls back a little, and he opens his eyes, puts his hand to her cheek and draws her down just as slowly as she'd come the first time.

Yuka rolls onto her side and takes him with her. His arm settles around her waist. The cover slips down when she reaches to smooth his hair, and he follows the opening at her collar with a kiss. She feels him start to come back up.

“You can stay there,” she says. ʸ

He looks up, and she catches the smile before he can hide it against her. She moves his hand from her waist to her breast. The cotton is warm where his palm rests, and when he draws her nearer she can feel the pressure of his fingers change. He takes his time kissing the skin above his hand. Yuka closes her eyes. This time she doesn't have to hold herself upright or get her knee around the arm of a chair. She can stretch out against him and put both hands in his hair. When she opens her eyes he's still looking at her, waiting to see where she'll bring him next.

She slides a hand to his jaw and brings his mouth back to hers.

The edge of the light goes all the way across his pillow. Yuka looks at it for a moment, deciding whether she wants to see his face or be close enough that she can't. He makes a little questioning sound under his breath. She smiles against his mouth, pulls the cover up around them both and lets the lamp burn a while longer.

* * *

Much later she wakes long enough to find his weight against her again. He still has one hand over hers. Their backs are on the bed and the lamp is out, but he's put his head in almost exactly the same place it was earlier.

“You comfortable?” she whispers. ʸ

“Very,” he says. ᴹ

He sounds far too awake. She works one arm out from under the cover and puts it around his shoulders. His mouth is warm against her skin when he kisses her once, very gently, and she feels him smile when she does the same to his forehead.

“You'll have to tell me if he ever gets the horse,” she says. ʸ

He laughs against her. She feels it more than she hears it. When her eyes shut again, his hand is moving slowly on her back. She can tell when it gets still, and knows he's fallen asleep before she has.

She leaves him his half of the pillow. She doesn't move far enough to give him any reason to use it.
