# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 3 — The People on the Other Page

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S13E74
**Continuity state:** Consent-noticed invitations bring Hinata, Luminous and Testarossa with full-size nonverbal Antonio via existing anchors. Longer unloaded flight verification postpones house departure to B+380. Mau/Yuka/Frieren/Ori advance B+379 on Kaisel; Mau returns via established home anchor for test and shared main-house flight B+380. DIO/Makima inner guests accompany Mau. Mai/Momo request Kamish; passenger harness not yet used. Anko chooses platform after seeing stored Architect construct dragon; its flight/load remains untested.

*When: B+379, early afternoon.*

Anko finds the first letter underneath a boarding diagram.

She picks it up by one corner, sees the seal and looks at Mau.

“You've been inviting people.”

“Yes.”

“People I don't have on this page.”

“That's why there are two pages.”

She lifts the sheet beneath her list. He has put the replies in a folded packet under it, with the agreed windows written on the outside. Anko reads the first one, looks back at him and then down at the writing again.

“You could have told me.”

“I wanted you to find that one.”

“While I'm working?”

He manages to look apologetic without giving up the smile. It makes her more suspicious.

She has reached for the second reply when somebody puts both arms around her from behind.

Anko goes stiff. A mouth comes close to her ear.

“I missed you.”

She drops the second letter.

Hinata catches it before it reaches the ground. She stays against Anko's back while she hands it to her, and Anko looks at the paper with an expression that suggests she has temporarily lost the ability to read.

“You said you had work,” she says.

“I finished the part I had to do.”

“You never finish.”

“I left the rest with people who can do it.”

Anko turns inside the embrace. She looks at Hinata's travelling clothes, then the bag set beside her feet, then the small smile that she can never quite prepare for.

“You're coming.”

“If you still have room on your page.”

Anko takes the pencil out of her hair and writes something without looking. Hinata watches the point travel straight off the line.

“I'm going to be difficult to count there.”

“Stay close. I'll keep track.”

Mau retrieves the fallen diagram, makes certain they don't need him and goes to find Yuka before Anko remembers who arranged it.

* * *

Testarossa steps out of the agreed shadow in the orchard with her own hat and Antonio at her shoulder.

The ant straightens to his full height before turning toward Mau. His carapace catches the light along the shoulders. A claw rests lightly against the luggage Testarossa has brought; he waits until she takes her hand off the handle, then lifts it.

Ori, who has been crossing the orchard with Chloe, stops.

“He's coming too?”

“Naturally,” says Testarossa.

Antonio inclines his head toward Mau. Mau returns it, then looks at the narrow band around the ant's forearm. Testarossa has tied a piece of fabric there, the same pale weave as her hat.

“Is that so you can find him?” he asks.

“So people know he's with me.”

Mau considers the enormous shadow soldier beside her.

“I think they'll ask.”

“Then he'll show them.”

Antonio raises the banded arm obligingly. Ori raises a hand back.

Testarossa puts her luggage beside the other labelled things, gives Anko a polite greeting and tactfully does not ask why Hinata is holding the pencil while Anko holds her hand.

Luminous arrives during the discussion of the revised departure. Mau has sent the agreed notice before opening the route. She comes through already looking at the field, stops at the sight of the shikigami and then notices the passenger structure being checked by Senku.

“You built one.”

“Noise did.”

“And the other part?”

“Several people who are still deciding whether they regret it.”

She watches Mai make a mark on a brace. Then her gaze moves past him.

Makima has stepped out onto the path.

Luminous looks at her. Makima looks back, still close enough to Mau that he can feel the change in her attention through their bond.

“That,” Luminous says, “is new.”

“There's a longer explanation.”

“I imagine there is.”

She leaves it there. Yuka comes up beside Mau with two pieces of bread and gives him the one she has already bitten. Makima watches the exchange, then puts her attention back on Luminous.

“You brought the tea,” Mau says.

“I brought what you wrote down.”

“All of it?”

“And several things you didn't. You can explain those if someone dislikes them.”

He laughs. Yuka leans across him to look at the package.

“Are we allowed to come?”

“To a part of it,” Luminous says. “I'd like to hear what the others have been doing first.”

“They'll like that,” Mau says. “There's a place near the water. We thought you could have the first hour to yourselves.”

From the porch, Daphne is already asking whether the place has plants she can look at. Echidna has heard enough of the conversation to begin approaching. Sekhmet remains where she is and watches the distance she will eventually be required to walk.

“Can we reach it sitting down?” she asks.

“Yes,” Mau says.

She closes her eyes, satisfied.

* * *

The revised departure is decided over the cleared lunch table.

The platform passed its short test. It still has to pass the longer one before anyone brings their family aboard. Senku has made his opinion on skipping that stage sufficiently clear that even Gojo is no longer pretending to disagree.

“Take the time,” Shoko says.

“We will,” Mau answers.

She takes the sealed itinerary out of her pocket. Yuka straightens immediately.

“Am I allowed to see that now?”

“Your part.”

There are separate sheets inside, each folded around its own instructions. Shoko gives Yuka the smallest one. Elmesia's writing begins with where the room is and ends with the permission to change their minds.

Between those two points there is very little.

Yuka reads it twice. Mau tilts his head toward her shoulder.

“Is it blank?”

“Almost.”

He smiles under the blindfold.

“Good itinerary.”

Shoko puts her own sheet away.

“The rooms will be there tomorrow too. You four can go ahead, if Frieren still wants her early morning by the water. The rest of us will follow after the checks.”

Frieren looks up from the book she has been reading while the others talk.

“Ori wanted to see what comes in with the tide.”

Ori nods very hard. Then she looks at the platform.

“But I wanted to ride with everyone.”

“We'll meet them when they come,” Mau says. “I have to bring this one across anyway. I'll go back for the last test and come with them.”

“You'll come back?”

“Tomorrow afternoon. We can have the morning together before that.”

Ori thinks about it. Chloe leans close enough to say something into her ear. Ori's expression changes at once.

“I'll find you a shell.”

“A little one,” Chloe says.

“A good one.”

Mau sends the change to Elmesia through the arrangements already made for the visit. Her answer comes while he is putting the afternoon test materials away: the room is ready, the smaller neighbouring one can take Frieren and Ori tonight, and she would like them to leave her some dinner if they beat her to it.

He reads the last part aloud. DIO asks what she was planning to order.

* * *

Mai and Momo find him beside the shikigami.

“Tomorrow,” Mai says, “could we go on Kamish?”

Momo's hand is in hers. Mau glances down, then back at their faces. Momo draws herself a little straighter on her broom.

“Flying on this would be fine,” she says.

“You asked about the view,” Mai tells her.

“I was making conversation.”

“Then we can make conversation on Kamish.”

Mau turns toward the shadow beneath the trees. Kamish raises his head when called, the shape of his jaws passing out of the dark into daylight. Momo's fingers tighten. Mai looks at her.

“We can sit where I can hold on to you,” she says quietly.

Momo looks down at their joined hands as if this is information she has not yet processed.

Mau asks Kamish. The dragon considers them with an old, measuring eye, then lowers his head enough to let Mai stand by his cheek.

“That's a yes,” Mau says. “We still check the harness. No showing off over the sea.”

“Tell him,” Mai says, pointing to Kamish.

Kamish exhales through his nose. It lifts the edge of Momo's enormous brim.

Anko arrives with Hinata and the remaining notices. She sees where Mai is standing and immediately gets an idea.

“Which one would I get?”

Mau thinks for a moment.

“I have another big one. We haven't checked what he can carry.”

Hinata looks at him.

“Checked?”

“He was in storage.”

He clears the space, has the others stand back and calls the shape that has waited there since the Architect's gate. The construct dragon opens across the far end of the field, its head rising on a neck Mau remembers from much too close. The dark edge of a forelimb settles into the grass.

His abdomen tightens before he can stop it. Yuka's fingers meet his wrist. He keeps his attention on the creature until its movement stills, then lets the breath go.

Anko looks from the head to the enormous body and back to Mau.

“I like the bus.”

“You haven't seen the seats.”

“I'm going to love the seats.”

Hinata touches her elbow. Anko lets herself be turned back toward the platform without another word.

Mau returns the untested dragon to shadow. Its outline closes over the grass. Yuka keeps hold of his wrist until he turns toward her.

“You all right?”

“I remembered where I met him.”

She nods. He moves his hand so their fingers fit, then walks with her back to the table.

Behind them Mai is still talking to Momo about where they would sit. Momo's hand has not left hers.
