# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 3 — The People on the Other Page

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S13E74
**Continuity state:** Consent-noticed invitations bring Hinata, Luminous and Testarossa with full-size nonverbal Antonio via existing anchors. Everyone waits for the longer weighted flight check and will depart together B+379. Four-person beach outing is the first morning after the shared arrival, not an advance trip. Mai/Momo request Kamish; passenger harness not yet used. Anko chooses platform after seeing stored Architect construct dragon; its flight/load remains untested.

*When: B+379, early afternoon.*

Anko finds the first letter underneath a boarding diagram.

She picks it up by one corner, sees the seal and looks at Mau.

“You've been inviting people.” ᴬⁿ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“People I don't have on this page.” ᴬⁿ

“That's why there are two pages.” ᴹ

She lifts the sheet beneath her list. He has put the replies in a folded packet under it, with the agreed windows written on the outside. Anko reads the first one, looks back at him and then down at the writing again.

“You could have told me.” ᴬⁿ

“I wanted you to find that one.” ᴹ

“While I'm working?” ᴬⁿ

He manages to look apologetic without giving up the smile. It makes her more suspicious.

She has reached for the second reply when somebody puts both arms around her from behind.

Anko goes stiff. A mouth comes close to her ear.

“I missed you.” ᴴⁱ

She drops the second letter.

Hinata catches it before it reaches the ground. She stays against Anko's back while she hands it to her, and Anko looks at the paper with an expression that suggests she has temporarily lost the ability to read.

“You said you had work,” she says. ᴬⁿ

“I finished the part I had to do.” ᴴⁱ

“You never finish.” ᴬⁿ

“I left the rest with people who can do it.” ᴴⁱ

Anko turns inside the embrace. She looks at Hinata's travelling clothes, then the bag set beside her feet, then the small smile that she can never quite prepare for.

“You're coming.” ᴬⁿ

“If you still have room on your page.” ᴴⁱ

Anko takes the pencil out of her hair and writes something without looking. Hinata watches the point travel straight off the line.

“I'm going to be difficult to count there.” ᴴⁱ

“Stay close. I'll keep track.” ᴬⁿ

Mau retrieves the fallen diagram, makes certain they don't need him and goes to find Yuka before Anko remembers who arranged it.

---

DIO and Makima have returned inside for the collection trips. Mau is back from the agreed collection point by the time Anko has finished showing Hinata the revised list. Testarossa arrives with him in the orchard, her own hat already on and Antonio at her shoulder. The soldier exchanged to the other end will stay there until Mau's return interval has passed.

The ant straightens to his full height before turning toward Mau. His carapace catches the light along the shoulders. A claw rests lightly against the luggage Testarossa has brought; he waits until she takes her hand off the handle, then lifts it.

Ori, who has been crossing the orchard with Chloe, stops.

“He's coming too?” ᴼ

“Naturally,” says Testarossa. ᵀᵉ

Antonio inclines his head toward Mau. Mau returns it, then looks at the narrow band around the ant's forearm. Testarossa has tied a piece of fabric there, the same pale weave as her hat.

“Is that so you can find him?” he asks. ᴹ

“So people know he's with me.” ᵀᵉ

Mau considers the enormous shadow soldier beside her.

“I think they'll ask.” ᴹ

“Then he'll show them.” ᵀᵉ

Antonio raises the banded arm obligingly. Ori raises a hand back.

Testarossa puts her luggage beside the other labelled things, gives Anko a polite greeting and tactfully does not ask why Hinata is holding the pencil while Anko holds her hand.

Mau makes the remaining collection after the interval and the agreed notice. He returns with Luminous during the discussion of the revised departure, bringing her to the familiar soldier in the orchard. She is already looking at the field when they arrive. She stops at the sight of the shikigami, then notices the passenger structure being checked by Senku.

“You built one.” ᴸᵘ

“Noise did.” ᴹ

“And the other part?” ᴸᵘ

“Several people who are still deciding whether they regret it.” ᴹ

She watches Mai make a mark on a brace. Then her gaze moves past him.

Makima has stepped out onto the path.

Luminous looks at her. Makima looks back, still close enough to Mau that he can feel the change in her attention through their bond.

“That,” Luminous says, “is new.” ᴸᵘ

“There's a longer explanation.” ᴹ

“I imagine there is.” ᴸᵘ

She leaves it there. Yuka comes up beside Mau with two pieces of bread and gives him the one she has already bitten. Makima watches the exchange, then puts her attention back on Luminous.

“You brought the tea,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I brought what you wrote down.” ᴸᵘ

“All of it?” ᴹ

“And several things you didn't. You can explain those if someone dislikes them.” ᴸᵘ

He laughs. Yuka leans across him to look at the package.

“Are we allowed to come?” ʸ

“To a part of it,” Luminous says. “I'd like to hear what the others have been doing first.” ᴸᵘ

“They'll like that,” Mau says. “There's a place near the water. We thought you could have the first hour to yourselves.” ᴹ

From the porch, Daphne is already asking whether the place has plants she can look at. Echidna has heard enough of the conversation to begin approaching. Sekhmet remains where she is and watches the distance she will eventually be required to walk.

“Can we reach it sitting down?” she asks. ˢᵉᵏ

“Yes,” Mau says. ᴹ

She closes her eyes, satisfied.

---

The departure waits for the longer test.

The platform passed its short flight. Senku still wants the full circuit over empty land before anyone brings their family aboard. Nobody argues once Mai lays the marked fastening from the failed version on the lunch table.

“Take the time,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

“We will,” Mau answers. ᴹ

She takes the sealed itinerary out of her pocket. Yuka straightens immediately.

“Am I allowed to see that now?” ʸ

“Your part.” ˢʰ

There are separate sheets inside, each folded around its own instructions. Shoko gives Yuka the smallest one. Elmesia's writing begins with where the room is and ends with the permission to change their minds.

Between those two points there is very little.

Yuka reads it twice. Mau tilts his head toward her shoulder.

“Is it blank?” ᴹ

“Almost.” ʸ

He smiles under the blindfold.

“Good itinerary.” ᴹ

Shoko puts her own sheet away.

“We leave when it's ready. Together.” ˢʰ

Frieren looks up from her book.

“Then we can have the early tide tomorrow.” ᶠ

Ori turns toward her.

“All of us?” ᴼ

“You, me, Mau and Yuka. Before the others decide what they want to do.” ᶠ

Ori looks at Yuka, who nods.

“I want to see what you find.” ʸ

Chloe leans close enough to say something into Ori's ear. Ori's expression changes at once.

“I'll find you a shell.” ᴼ

“A little one,” Chloe says. ᶜʰ

“A good one.” ᴼ

Mau sends their expected arrival window to Elmesia through the arrangements already made. Her answer reaches him while he is putting the test materials away: the rooms are ready for everyone, and she would like them to leave her some dinner if they beat her to it.

He reads the last part aloud. DIO asks what she was planning to order.

---

Mai catches Mau while he is putting away the last of the test weights.

“If we wanted a different ride, could we take Kamish?” ᴹᵃ

“You can ask him.” ᴹ

She looks toward Momo Nishimiya, who has stopped beside her broom with the exaggerated attention of somebody who has not been listening.

“Us?” Momo asks. ᴹⁿ

“Unless you were planning to leave me on the ground.” ᴹᵃ

Mai takes her hand.

She does it before asking whether the broom needs carrying. Momo looks down at the contact, then up at Mai, and forgets the entirely sensible answer she had prepared about not needing a dragon to fly.

“It can stay,” she says at last. ᴹⁿ

“The broom?” ᴹᵃ

“Yes, the broom.” ᴹⁿ

Mai smiles and leads her toward the trees. Mau turns enough to give them room, calling Kamish into the clear space beyond the fence.

The dragon's head rises through the shade. Momo stops. Mai stops with her instead of using their joined hands to pull her closer.

“Would you take us on the flight?” Mai asks him. “Together. If we make somewhere secure to sit.” ᴹᵃ

Kamish lowers his head until he can examine both of them.

“How many seats?” ᴷ

“Two.” ᴹᵃ

“There is room.” ᴷ

“And something to hold,” Momo adds, finding her voice. ᴹⁿ

“There is no virtue in falling off.” ᴷ

Mai laughs. The sound helps Momo look past the teeth to the dragon answering them. She asks where he bends when he turns. Kamish moves one shoulder to show her. Mai watches the changing angle, already thinking about a fastening.

Mau leaves them with the drawing board when Mai waves him away. By the time he finishes checking Anko's second list, they are still discussing the view.

Anko arrives with Hinata and the remaining notices. She sees where Mai is standing and immediately gets an idea.

“Which one would I get?” ᴬⁿ

Mau thinks for a moment.

“I have another big one. We haven't checked what he can carry.” ᴹ

Hinata looks at him.

“Checked?” ᴴⁱ

“He was in storage.” ᴹ

He clears the space, has the others stand back and calls the shape that has waited there since the Architect's gate. The construct dragon opens across the far end of the field, its head rising on a neck Mau remembers from much too close. The dark edge of a forelimb settles into the grass.

His abdomen tightens before he can stop it. Yuka's fingers meet his wrist. He keeps his attention on the creature until its movement stills, then lets the breath go.

Anko looks from the head to the enormous body and back to Mau.

“I like the bus.” ᴬⁿ

“You haven't seen the seats.” ᴹ

“I'm going to love the seats.” ᴬⁿ

Hinata touches her elbow. Anko lets herself be turned back toward the platform without another word.

Mau returns the untested dragon to shadow. Its outline closes over the grass. Yuka keeps hold of his wrist until he turns toward her.

“You all right?” ʸ

“I remembered where I met him.” ᴹ

She nods. He moves his hand so their fingers fit, then walks with her back to the table.

They are checking the platform when Mai returns.

Momo walks beside her rather than floating on the broom. Her hand is still in Mai's. She has pushed the huge brim back, exposing a face which becomes considerably pinker when Mau looks up.

“We're going to wait on the Kamish flight,” Mai announces. “Momo got frightened.” ᴹᵃ

“I did not.” ᴹⁿ

“She had concerns.” ᴹᵃ

“I asked about the straps. You asked about the straps.” ᴹⁿ

Mai looks down at their joined hands, then back at Mau.

“Serious concerns.” ᴹᵃ

Momo begins answering, notices Mai smiling at her and loses the argument somewhere between the two expressions. She does not take her hand away.

Mau makes himself look at the drawing.

He knows that face. He has made enough versions of it around Yuka to recognize someone discovering that a perfectly ordinary hand can make the rest of the yard difficult to use. Momo will want to ask him something sooner or later. He is not going to make her do it in front of Mai.

“Another day, then,” he says. ᴹ

“If that's still all right,” Momo says. ᴹⁿ

Kamish answers from beyond the fence.

“I do not become smaller while you wait.” ᴷ

“That's reassuring,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

Momo laughs before she can help it. Mai turns toward the sound, pleased, and Mau suddenly finds something extremely absorbing about the boarding rail.
