The last test takes them beyond the fields.

Mau sits alone on the platform with the weighted sacks secured around him. Senku has given him a route with two broad turns and a place to land if a support starts moving. Mai stands beside the boarding step until he repeats it back to her.

“And if I get an idea?” he asks. ᴹ

“You land.” ᴹᵃ

“I tell you.” ᴹ

“After you land.” ᴹᵃ

He promises. She steps away, and the shikigami rises with its first passenger.

It carries him over the empty stretch of ground south of the town. The platform moves under him in a way the drawing couldn't show. At the first turn he can feel the load shifting before the bracing receives it; he slows the turn, takes it wider and watches the marked fastenings. The altered front joint moves and holds.

On the way back he chooses the second planned course, low enough that the others can follow his progress from the field. He lands where Byakuya has put the marker and stays seated while Mai checks the points that matter.

She finally raises her head.

“All right.” ᴹᵃ

At the fence, Milim throws up both arms. Ori and Chloe start running before Mai can add the rest.

“In order,” she calls. “Using the steps!” ᴹᵃ

They slow down just enough to demonstrate that they heard.

Everybody has advice about boarding.

Anko's advice is to wait until she finishes counting. Gojo's advice is that he can get himself up there. Shoko's advice is that he can use the stairs like somebody who plans to remain welcome.

Mau stands at the bottom with Yuka and the notebook. Ori has already checked the labels. He puts the larger cases into his inventory as people hand them over, repeating the names when two look alike. Bocchi stops him before he stores her guitar.

“That one with me. Please.” ᴮ

“Of course.” ᴹ

Kita takes the smaller case from her so she can keep both hands on it. Ryo gives Mau a bag, remembers something and takes it back before he can store it. Nijika looks inside, removes the snacks and returns the bag herself.

“She meant to share those,” she tells him. ᴺⁱ

“I hadn't decided,” Ryo says. ᴿʸ

“Now you have.” ᴺⁱ

On the platform, Jin-Woo is helping his mother find a seat with something she can hold during the climb. Hae-In takes the one beside her while Suho tries every opening through which he might see the wings. His grandfather quietly chooses the best view before Suho has finished comparing them.

Rem sits with Umaru's cardigan in her lap. Umaru looks at it, sighs and takes it from her. On the next bench Ram has already persuaded Seika to move far enough over that she can sit close without anyone needing to discuss why.

Ramiris has reserved a stretch of rail by declaring that Beretta needs the view. Beretta waits until she finishes explaining how good it is before sitting where she has chosen. She settles on the seat back beside him, pleased that someone has understood the arrangements.

Antonio folds his arms carefully within the space Testarossa selects. He lowers his head beneath the upper brace, settles his weight and watches her adjust the band on his forearm. Across from him, the perching rail has temporarily acquired a very suspicious cat.

“I'm not luggage,” Turbo Granny says. ᵀᴳ

“Then stop sitting on the bags,” Seiko answers. ˢᵉⁱᵏᵒ

Mai and Momo take a seat together halfway along the platform. Mai puts the drawing board between their knees, leaving it there only until Momo has stopped looking for somewhere to put her hand.

Mau calls Kaisel beside the boarding step.

Yuka has the little bag she wanted to keep out of the inventory. They were going to fly alongside the bus, close enough to hear the others and far enough out that she could show him the coast as it came into view.

“Are you going over there?” Ori asks from the rail. ᴼ

Mau looks up. She is between Chloe and Suika, holding her brim back with one hand.

“We thought we'd take Kaisel. You can see us from—” ᴹ

“But it's the first trip. Can't you come with us?” ᴼ

“Yes,” Yuka says. ʸ

Mau turns toward her. She is already handing him the bag to store.

“We can take Kaisel another time.” ʸ

Ori's face opens. She disappears from the rail to arrange something about their seats before Mau has found a useful objection to a decision he is not particularly interested in opposing.

Kaisel lowers his head. Mau strokes the place above his nose.

“Apparently we've been reassigned.” ᴹ

“The princess is persuasive,” Kamish observes from the field. ᴷ

“You could have helped.” ᴹ

“Against her and the queen?” ᴷ

Yuka pats the nearest dark scale as she passes.

“Good judgment.” ʸ

Kaisel lets Ori hurry back down for one last touch before returning to shadow. Kamish follows once Mau has checked that no one is standing within his outline.

The new shikigami will carry them together.

Mau checks the home arrangement before he boards.

Bellion has the permissions and the guard routes. Muster is at the entrance; his ordinary soldiers already occupy their posts. The ones assigned to the common rooms wait for the last departure before beginning the work Fern and Jinshi marked. DIO's plant stays on its familiar sill, with the measured cup and Ori's copy of the instructions beside it.

Mau gives the final orders himself. Bellion bows. Diablo is still on the veranda, having chosen the quiet house over the crowded flight. Fern has left him the domestic arrangements as well as Bellion's copy; anything requiring a conversation with a resident has a resident to answer it. He lifts a hand when Mau looks back, then returns to the book he has been waiting to read without interruption.

The private rooms remain private. Ashborn's peace remains his own. If anything requires a decision, Bellion knows how to reach him through the established connection; the verified home anchor stays ready for a return.

When Mau climbs the boarding step, Yuka is waiting halfway up.

“I saved you a seat.” ʸ

“Is it a good one?” ᴹ

“I plan to sit on it.” ʸ

He looks past her at the bench. She waits until he understands, then turns and takes his hand to bring him the rest of the way.

Frieren has a book open in her lap. Ori sits beside her, holding the edge of Chloe's sleeve so she will lean close enough to hear something. Milim is across from them, already arguing with Richeh about whether a person can see a cloud from inside it.

“We'll find out,” says Richeh. ᴿⁱ

“That's what I said.” ᴹⁱ

“Then wait.” ᴿⁱ

Mau sits at the forward position where he can direct the shikigami. Yuka settles against his side first, then takes one look at how far he has left the blanket from her and moves onto his lap to reach it herself.

“Everything secured?” Byakuya calls. ᵇʸᵃᵏᵘʸᵃ

Yuka raises the blanket in reply.

“The important things,” Mau says. ᴹ

Mai walks down the centre one last time. She checks a loose strap at Okarun's knee, hands Momo Ayase the end and makes them both demonstrate that they know which buckle releases it. She gets to Mau, sees where Yuka is sitting and points at the safer position for the climb.

Yuka moves without arguing.

“After we level out,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“I know.” ʸ

“I was talking to him.” ᴹᵃ

“He knows too.” ʸ

Mau nods solemnly. Mai does not believe him until he has checked Yuka's strap himself.

The takeoff makes the whole platform quiet.

The first movement is a deep gathering beneath the seats. Then the fields tilt away, the workshop roof comes into view and Ori's fingers close around Frieren's hand. Her other hand finds Chloe's.

Mau keeps the rise gentle. He can feel the creature respond, immense beneath him, following each deliberate change of direction. The support that failed moves within the space they gave it. A cord tightens and stops where it should.

Behind him somebody laughs in surprise.

It spreads. Milim is the first to point out the courtyard from above, but she is soon talking over three other people. Shizue leans forward to see the path between the houses. Rimuru looks where she points and starts explaining which roof he can recognize before she tells him that she knows; she wants him to look at the people waving.

Mau looks back once.

Bellion stands at the gate. Beside him, a soldier raises one hand.

Yuka touches Mau's wrist and waves with the other.

They settle into the journey by degrees.

Once the shikigami is flying level, people begin talking again. Byakuya watches the nearest fastening until Senku tells him that staring won't improve it. Senku then spends several minutes watching the same fastening.

Shuna brings out something wrapped from the kitchen. Shion hands the first portion toward Rimuru and discovers that Milim has intercepted it.

“I was passing it,” Milim says. ᴹⁱ

“Through your mouth?” ˢʰⁱ

Milim considers the question while chewing.

Mau hears Shuna tell them to stop fighting before they drop anything over the side. Yuka leans against his shoulder, laughing, and accepts a piece when the parcel reaches them.

The blindfold cuts the incoming detail to something he can live with. Through the rest he follows the flight and the people near him: Frieren turning a page, Ori asking where the sea starts, DIO making a pleased sound inside when he gives her the view she requests.

Makima remains within while they travel. She asks whether there is room beside him when she comes out later.

“We'll find somewhere at dinner,” he tells her through their agreed channel. ᴹ

There is a pause before she answers.

“All right.” ᴹᵏ

Yuka has her cheek against his upper arm. He tells her what Makima asked. She thinks about it, then nods.

“Elmesia should sit with us too.” ʸ

“I'll ask.” ᴹ

Yuka reaches for the blanket. He brings it closer before she has to get up, and she tucks it over both their knees.

The sea arrives first as a change in the distance.

Mau lets the cloth rise far enough to see the horizon, then lowers it again before the moving water resolves into more detail than he wants. Ori stands at the viewing rail with Frieren holding the back of her dress. Around them the family begins choosing which distant shapes must be boats.

“That one's a rock,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

“It could be a very slow boat,” Stark answers. ˢᵗ

“With a tree?” ᶠᵉ

“Someone could bring one.” ˢᵗ

Behind them Daphne asks whether they can stop there on the way back. Rimuru says they should find out who owns it first.

Yuka puts her chin on Mau's shoulder.

“I know where I want breakfast.” ʸ

“You haven't seen the island yet.” ᴹ

“I remember.” ʸ

“What are you ordering?” ᴹ

She tells him. It takes long enough that he has to ask if they are discussing one breakfast or several. She presses her smile into his sleeve.

“We'll have time.” ʸ

He turns his head toward her and leaves a kiss at her temple, just beneath the enormous brim.

Yuka finds Ori looking back from Frieren's seat. She waves her over once the flight is level. Ori arrives with a question about the water, stays for the answer and ends up tucked between them while Chloe points out another very slow boat.

This one has two trees.
