# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 5 — The Next Nap

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S13E74
**Continuity state:** Whole group arrival; luggage delivered first, Elmesia receives Mai hat, Ori sleeps in Mau arms then Frieren, couple unpack and nap together. No advance group or sexual escalation.

*When: B+379, sunset arrival into early evening.*

Elmesia meets them on the broad landing as the last light reaches the water.

She looks up at the shikigami, then at the structure along its back. Mau sees her begin examining the supports rather than the height. She waits until the creature has settled and Mai has secured the boarding step before approaching.

“Noise finished it,” she says. ᴱˡ

“He wanted you to see.” ᴹ

Inside, Noise goes very quiet. The quiet gives him away.

Elmesia puts a hand against the smooth side of the creature, where it has settled low enough for her to reach. Mau told her what they were studying before the trip. Seeing the result makes her smile in a different way.

“There you are,” she says. ᴱˡ

“Not him,” Noise tells Mau. “New.” ᴺ

Mau passes that on. Elmesia looks up toward him.

“I can see that. Would he mind if I looked more closely while you're here?” ᴱˡ

“He'd mind if you didn't.” ᴹ

Noise objects to this description with a sound that does very little to disprove it.

Mai comes down behind Mau, carrying the board. Senku points at Byakuya when Elmesia asks who made the platform, and Byakuya points straight back.

“All of you, then,” she says. “One morning when you've had enough rest to start explaining it to me.” ᴱˡ

“How much explaining?” Senku asks. ˢᵉⁿᵏᵘ

“I have questions about the front joint.” ᴱˡ

Mai looks at the board and begins smiling.

“You'll need breakfast first.” ᴹᵃ

“I thought I might.” ᴱˡ

Behind them the landing fills with people finding their feet. Anko has her list out; Hinata takes the pencil while she counts. Testarossa steps down with Antonio and introduces herself to the attendant watching him with increasingly professional restraint.

“He's with me.” ᵀᵉ

Antonio raises the band on his forearm. The attendant inclines his head to both of them and moves a luggage trolley out of the ant's path.

Elmesia catches Mau before he follows Yuka toward the guest paths.

“All the bags out first, please. Then nobody has to find you before they can change.” ᴱˡ

Mau looks at the people already turning toward the rooms.

“Right. Yes. Everyone with a label, come collect your person.” ᴹ

Ori appears at his elbow.

“The bags have labels.” ᴼ

“Then this will be much easier.” ᴹ

She takes the notebook from him with a patience which makes Yuka turn away to hide her laugh.

The cases arrive in groups, clear of the boarding step. Ori reads a name and waits until somebody answers before giving the next one. Maki catches hers without difficulty. Ryo looks at the case Mau produces, checks the label and attempts to tell Nijika that the absence of snacks is a storage error.

“It happened before storage,” Nijika says. ᴺⁱ

“A difficult case.” ᴹ

“Solved,” Kita tells him. ᴷⁱ

Bocchi is still holding her guitar. Mau gives her the bag that belongs beside it; she thanks him and then, seeing nobody is trying to take the guitar now, lowers one hand from its neck.

Frieren waits with Ori's things and her own. The room keys come to them on little wooden tags. Suika compares hers with Ori's and discovers that the doors are along the same path. Chloe leans over to read both numbers.

Ori starts explaining which one is nearer the beach before they have seen either door.

“We should inspect,” Chloe says. ᶜʰ

“A preliminary inspection,” Umaru agrees, arriving at exactly the right moment to acquire authority over it. ᵁ

Frieren watches the three faces turn toward her.

“You can inspect the path. Stay where I can see you.” ᶠ

They go as far as the next planter and spend several minutes debating which way the beach must be. Milim joins them, announces she can solve this from above and is told by all three that they are doing it properly.

She crouches beside them instead.

---

DIO comes out once the bags are clear.

She has her hat against her chest. At Elmesia's greeting she puts it on to demonstrate the effect and nearly brushes the brim against Mau's face.

He tilts back. Elmesia catches the edge before it folds.

“That,” she says, “is wonderful.” ᴱˡ

DIO straightens.

“Mai made it.” ᴰ

Elmesia turns at once.

“Could I have one?” ᴱˡ

Mai looks at DIO, then at Elmesia's hair and the space occupied by the existing brim.

“For shade, or for being seen from the other end of the island?” ᴹᵃ

“Can I have both?” ᴱˡ

“Good answer.” ᴹᵃ

She sets the board down. Momo Nishimiya takes it before the corner reaches the paving, keeps it beneath her arm and watches Mai begin measuring with her hands.

“This one needs a higher crown,” Mai explains. “If I just widen yours, it'll press here. Then she'll keep moving it, and I'll have made an expensive nuisance.” ᴹᵃ

Elmesia bends slightly when Mai asks. Mai checks where the band should sit, then asks whether she wants a fastening for the wind.

“Please. I intend to keep it.” ᴱˡ

The hat takes shape between Mai's hands. The brim curves upward at the sides instead of continuing flat; a narrow band follows the crown, and the fastening stays where Elmesia can loosen it herself. Mai turns it over before offering it, checks the underside with her thumb and removes one little rough place.

Mau is watching.

She looks at him.

“Yes, you could make one. I'm making this one.” ᴹᵃ

“I was admiring your customer service.” ᴹ

“You get the version you've earned.” ᴹᵃ

Momo makes a small sound behind the board. Mai glances at her, and whatever she sees makes her smile before she turns back to Elmesia.

The new hat fits. Elmesia lowers her chin, looks up at DIO beneath the brim and waits for judgment.

DIO moves beside her to compare them in the glass of the nearest door.

“We need another opinion,” she says. ᴰ

“You have two,” Mau answers. ᴹ

“A useful one.” ᴰ

Yuka joins them with both hands over her mouth. Elmesia sees her reflection, takes one look at Mau's face and begins laughing too.

---

The rooms are ready. That does not make everyone go to them immediately.

Ori comes back with a report on the path, which turns out to require Mau to lift her so she can point over the planter rather than around it. Milim has found the sea. This is apparently useful information despite the sea occupying most of the view.

“We knew where,” Ori says. “We were finding how.” ᴼ

“An important distinction.” ᴹ

He listens to the rest with her on his arm. She shows him Chloe's room key, gives it back when Chloe reminds her whose hand it belongs in and begins telling Yuka about a place where all of them might sit tomorrow.

Yuka stands close enough for Ori to touch the ribbon on her hat. The explanation becomes slower. Ori forgets the name of the thing beside the thing she has been describing.

“A bench?” Yuka suggests. ʸ

“No. Behind it.” ᴼ

“Another bench?” ᴹ

Ori shakes her head against his shoulder. He adjusts his arm beneath her legs. Her hand, which has been showing him how far, comes to rest on his collar.

Frieren closes the conversation with the attendant and turns toward them.

“She's done a great deal of preliminary inspecting,” Mau tells her. ᴹ

“I can see that.” ᶠ

Ori opens her eyes long enough to say that she is not finished. Nobody challenges the claim. Mau keeps the back of her hat from pressing against her neck, then takes it off when she has stopped trying to look at the path.

Frieren accepts the hat. Her daughter sleeps with one cheek against Mau's shoulder, trusting him to keep holding her through the parts of the evening she has decided to miss.

For a little while they all stay there.

“The next nap is mine,” Frieren says. ᶠ

Mau looks at her. She folds Ori's light cardigan over one arm, holding out the other for her daughter, and offers no clarification.

Yuka's mouth begins to curve. Mau gives her a warning look which arrives too late to be effective.

He passes Ori to Frieren slowly, keeping one hand behind her head until she has settled. Ori stirs, finds her mother's familiar sleeve and goes still again.

“Tea will wait a little,” Elmesia tells them. “Get comfortable. The market stalls with lamps stay open into the evening.” ᴱˡ

Yuka touches Mau's wrist.

“Come and see.” ʸ

He follows her down the guest path. At Frieren's door she turns to wave; Frieren lifts Ori's little hat in reply without waking the person who owns it.

---

They find their room while the others find theirs.

The room opens onto the balcony Yuka remembers. She is still holding Mau by the wrist when she crosses it, pulling him after her to the railing.

The pool is where she remembers it. Beyond it is the path to the water, and farther down the shore she can see the tables she was talking about on the flight.

“There,” she says. ʸ

He comes to stand behind her. She takes his hand and points with it until he has found the place.

“Breakfast?” ᴹ

“Breakfast.” ʸ

They unpack enough to stop looking for things. He brings their clothes out of the inventory while she names the drawers. The hats go on a wide shelf that was probably intended for something more modest. He watches her press a palm against the brim of his to keep it from springing back.

“It fits,” she announces. ʸ

“You're holding it.” ᴹ

“Then I'll hold it.” ʸ

He puts an arm around her waist and lifts her away from the shelf. She turns toward him, already laughing, and lets him carry her the short distance to the seat by the balcony.

She settles across his lap facing him. He starts to say something about dinner, but she places a hand at the back of his head and draws him toward her chest.

“A minute,” she says. ʸ

He lets her arrange him. His arms come around her back. She leans into the support of the chair and begins telling him what she wants to do tomorrow: breakfast first, then the shallow pools with Ori and Frieren, and after that they can see what everyone else has decided.

He answers the first few things. She feels the last answer move against her shirt more than she hears it.

Her hand travels slowly through the shorter hair above his collar.

“And I want to go back to that stall,” she says. “The one you said we'd come back to when I wasn't hungry.” ʸ

“You were always hungry.” ᴹ

“That's why we have to go back.” ʸ

She waits for his answer. His arms stay around her, his cheek settled against her. The next breath goes deeper.

Yuka keeps talking for a little while, quieter now, until she forgets which things she has already told him. Outside, someone laughs on the path. She closes her eyes and rests her cheek against the top of his head.
