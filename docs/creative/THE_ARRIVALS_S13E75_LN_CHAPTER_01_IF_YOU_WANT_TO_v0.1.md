# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 5 — If You Want To

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S13E75
**Continuity state:** Entire house already arrived together. Couple wake from ordinary affectionate nap. Brief reconnecting tea, Daphne seed market with Elmesia/Rimuru before communal dinner. Optional activities, no compulsory nightly report; Makima included from first night and considers independent preferences. Elmesia/DIO/Testarossa plan time together. Four-person early beach morning planned B+380.

*When: B+379, sunset into the first shared evening.*

Yuka wakes because her leg has gone to sleep.

For a few seconds she is reluctant to admit it. Mau is warm against her, his cheek resting where she put it, his arms still loosely around her back. She can hear voices on the path below the balcony and the scrape of somebody moving a chair. The room has grown dimmer while they were asleep.

She tries moving one foot. The rest of her leg answers with a thousand tiny complaints.

“Mau.”

He makes a sound against her shirt.

“I need my leg.”

His hand moves first. Then he raises his head, sees where she is sitting and lifts her just enough to let her shift without putting her weight on the tingling foot.

She breathes out in relief.

“You could have said,” he murmurs.

“I just did.”

He smiles, still sleepy. She runs her fingers over the mark the seam of her shirt has left on his cheek and begins laughing.

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“That means something.”

“It means you slept on me.”

“You put me there.”

She kisses the seam-marked cheek. He accepts it with his eyes half closed, then catches her before she can get up.

“Dinner?”

“Dinner.”

“Will you walk?”

She flexes her recovering foot and considers the question with the gravity it deserves.

“Eventually.”

* * *

They find Luminous and the witches beneath an awning beside the water.

The tea has already arrived. Pride is explaining to Elmesia why the chairs on one side of the table are better, while Sekhmet has managed to occupy the chair everyone else is discussing without contributing anything to the argument. Minerva sees Mau carrying Yuka and starts to rise.

“What happened?”

Yuka looks down at herself.

“My leg fell asleep.”

Minerva sits back. Mau lowers Yuka into the chair she indicates and takes the one beside her. Her recovering foot finds his ankle under the table.

“We came to see if you wanted dinner,” he says.

“We're discussing the next one,” Luminous replies.

“Dinner?”

“Tea.”

“We haven't had this one.”

Echidna lifts her cup.

“You haven't.”

Elmesia passes him a clean cup. Yuka takes one of the small things from the dish between them, puts half in Mau's hand and begins explaining that she intends to get to the pool early tomorrow. Daphne asks if she saw the path beyond it.

“The one with the stalls?”

“They sell plants there.”

“We saw it from the room.”

“I'd like to look before they close.”

Mau takes a sip, sets the cup down and looks toward the lamps beginning to appear along the promenade.

“We can walk through on the way.”

Daphne has the market bag in her lap already.

Luminous notices. So does Elmesia. Neither of them asks how long it has been ready.

* * *

The plant stall is closing when they reach it.

The owner has folded one side of the canvas and is bringing the smaller pots back under cover. Daphne stops in front of a shallow tray. She asks a question so specific that he puts the pot down to answer it.

Mau stands beside Yuka while she examines a packet of seed that looks like every other packet to him. Elmesia joins Daphne at the tray. Within a minute she is asking the stallholder for something to write on.

“Do they travel well?” Rimuru asks from behind them.

Mau turns. Rimuru and Shizue have come up the other path with a little paper bag. Shizue offers Yuka one of the things inside it. Rimuru immediately begins explaining how much space the plants would need when they grow.

“We should start with a few,” he says.

Daphne holds up one packet.

“A few of each?”

He looks at the remaining trays.

“Let's read what they are first.”

Yuka keeps the piece Shizue gave her between two fingers while she leans over the writing. Mau waits beside her with their empty cups in one hand. He has no idea why they brought them as far as the market.

“How much garden do we have left?” he asks.

Rimuru looks at Daphne, who is now describing a particular kind of shade to Elmesia. The stallholder has begun taking out packets he had already put away.

“We can talk about it after dinner.”

“We're going to bring back a forest.”

Yuka feeds him the last of Shizue's purchase.

“You have the inventory.”

“I know what I'll be carrying. I mean where we'll put it when it comes out.”

She glances toward Rimuru, smiles and leaves the question with him.

* * *

Dinner spreads across two adjoining tables.

People keep moving between them. Byakuya gets up to show Elmesia the corrected brace on the board he somehow brought to dinner. Senku takes it away from him long enough to make him eat. Coco sits beside Tetia until Ori needs to show her the room key, then moves over and finds that Agott has taken the empty chair.

Shizuka has the menu open beside her plate. Nano turns it slightly so they can both see the small print. Karane is arguing with Hakari about whether ordering something she hasn't tried counts as an adventure. Kusuri asks Maomao what she thinks is in the sauce. Maomao tastes it, thinks and asks the attendant a different question.

Mau gets Yuka's chair back before someone else sits in it. She puts a hand on his shoulder on the way down and leaves it there while Elmesia comes around the table.

DIO appears beside him with her hat held carefully against her chest. Elmesia turns the chair next to hers so it faces the water as well as the table.

“You'll want to see that before it's gone,” she says, pointing toward the last clear stretch of sky.

DIO sits. She puts the hat on her knees and watches the light with Elmesia, occasionally asking a question too quiet for Mau to hear over the other table.

Makima comes out near the path. Mau shifts his attention to her and points to the place they kept.

She stops behind the chair.

“Would you prefer me there?” she asks Yuka.

Yuka follows the direction of her gaze. There is another empty chair at the end of the table.

“Either's fine.”

Makima looks from one to the other. Mau can almost see her trying to find the consequence of choosing incorrectly.

“This one has the bread,” he says.

She sits down beside the basket.

* * *

Elmesia waits until most people have food before she speaks.

“Shoko and I arranged some possibilities,” she says. “If you want to go out on the boats, talk to the desk. If you'd rather find things yourselves, someone can give you a map that will spare you a very long walk to a locked gate. Some activities need a name beforehand. Some don't. You can decide tomorrow.”

“What time do we all meet?” Mau asks.

Yuka turns toward him.

Elmesia's smile gets wider.

“When you want to.”

“For dinner.”

“If you want to have dinner together.”

“Oh.”

Shoko puts her cup down.

“You can go off and do something without reporting back at six.”

From halfway down the table Gojo points at him.

“That one's for you, son.”

Mau looks at Elmesia.

“Can we throw him out yet?”

She laughs before she can pretend to consider it. Yuka bends her head against Mau's shoulder. Gojo puts a hand to his chest and turns toward Shoko, who is already passing his empty plate to an attendant.

“I was helping.”

“Then keep doing that.”

* * *

The plans begin changing before anyone has finished proposing them.

Luminous wants a longer afternoon with the witches, but first she asks Hinata whether she would like to look at a particular walk along the old wall. Hinata looks at Anko. Anko asks whether the wall has stairs. Luminous gives her an unamused look that makes Hinata smile into her glass.

DIO mentions a workshop Elmesia described on the flight notices. Elmesia offers to take her, and Testarossa asks whether they can pass the gardens on their way. Antonio turns his head at the sound of his name when Ori asks if he will need a room too.

“He'll be with me,” Testarossa says.

“He takes up a lot of bed.”

“We'll work something out.”

Mau watches the ant very carefully move a bread basket so an attendant can set down another dish. Testarossa says thank you to him. The ant inclines his head.

At Mau's side, Makima asks what the activity sheets are for. He tells her she can put her name down for something she wants to try.

“With you?”

“Sometimes. Or you could ask somebody else.”

She looks along the table at Elmesia and DIO.

“I haven't chosen yet,” he adds. “You can look first.”

Makima takes one of the sheets. She reads it while the others continue making plans around her. When Elmesia asks whether she would like to see the workshop too, she looks up more quickly than Mau expects.

“I'd like to,” she says.

Then, after a pause:

“If there's room.”

“We'll walk,” Elmesia answers. “There will be.”

* * *

Ori reaches the sleepy part of the evening halfway through a story.

She has been explaining to Suika how she intends to recognize the best shell. She gets as far as the colour, which she has not yet decided, and rests her cheek against Frieren's sleeve.

Frieren looks down. Ori sits up immediately.

“I'm listening.”

“You were talking.”

“I'm doing both.”

Mau takes the little room key out of the path of her elbow. Yuka notices, brings her own chair closer and holds a piece of fruit where Ori can reach it without having to lean across the plate.

Ori takes it, eats slowly and puts her head back down.

“Early tomorrow,” she reminds them.

“We'll be there,” Yuka says.

“Before everybody goes places.”

“Yes.”

Frieren closes the small market bag, keeping the packet Ori chose separate from Daphne's much more ambitious purchases. She stands, and Ori turns toward her before being asked.

Mau rises to help with the hat and the bag. Frieren has Ori's hand by the time he has found the right side of the brim.

“Breakfast?” he asks.

Ori nods through a yawn.

Yuka leans close to say good night. Ori catches her around the shoulders and stays there for a second before letting Frieren lead her down the path.

Mau sits again. Yuka turns her chair toward his and puts both legs across his lap beneath the tablecloth. He rests a hand over her ankle.

“Did you have a good day?” she asks.

He thinks of the enormous shadow in the field, Mai's hand held up at the failed test, all the voices behind him over the sea. He looks toward the tables, where Shion is defending the last piece of something from Milim with a serving spoon.

“Very.”

Yuka takes a piece from his plate and settles farther into her chair.
