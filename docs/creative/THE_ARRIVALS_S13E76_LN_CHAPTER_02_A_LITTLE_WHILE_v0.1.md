## Chapter 9 — A Little While

*When: B+380, midday through dusk.*

Milim is waiting beside the path with a ball.

She has arranged herself under a palm tree, one foot against its trunk, looking in a direction that happens to bring Mau and Yuka into view as soon as they reach the top of the steps. She looks away immediately.

Mau slows down.

Yuka follows his attention. Ori sees Milim, releases her hand and runs over to show her the shell before anyone can decide what the encounter means.

“Did you find that?” Milim asks. ᴹⁱ

Ori nods. She unfolds the cloth and puts the shell in her palm. Milim bends close enough to cast a shadow over the entire present.

“It's for Chloe.” ᴼ

“She's down there.” ᴹⁱ

Milim points beyond the palms. Voices reach them from a broad patch of beach where someone has marked a court in the sand. Ori turns to Frieren, who nods toward the path. The girl folds her parcel again and hurries off.

Milim stays where she is.

Mau looks at the ball.

“Is that yours?” ᴹ

“I'm holding it.” ᴹⁱ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

“It's a very good ball.” ᴹⁱ

Yuka puts the stack of hats down on a nearby bench and folds her arms.

“Does it do anything?” ʸ

“Lots of things.” ᴹⁱ

Milim demonstrates a small bounce. She catches it at her waist and looks away again.

Mau waits. The ball bounces once more.

“Nobody's playing?” he asks. ᴹ

“They're playing. I said I was going to get the ball.” ᴹⁱ

Yuka looks at it.

“You got it.” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹⁱ

Another pause. Milim's fingers tighten around the soft surface.

“There are rules,” she says at last. ᴹⁱ

Mau can hear where this is going and decides to make her get there.

“For the game?” ᴹ

“For bothering people.” ᴹⁱ

Yuka's mouth twitches.

“Are you bothering us?” ʸ

“No. That's why I'm standing here.” ᴹⁱ

Frieren sits on the bench beside the hats. She takes out her book with the air of someone who has recognized a situation that will resolve more quickly without her help.

Mau looks from Milim to Yuka. Yuka tilts her head, giving the decision back to him.

He holds out a hand for the ball.

“Do you want us to play?” ᴹ

Milim's whole face changes.

“You asked.” ᴹⁱ

“I did.” ᴹ

“Both of you?” ᴹⁱ

“You'd better ask her.” ᴹ

Milim turns to Yuka with such concentrated hope that Yuka laughs before answering.

“For a little while.” ʸ

Milim takes the ball back and starts running toward the beach.

“THEY'RE COMING!” ᴹⁱ

Mau watches her go.

“A little while,” he repeats. ᴹ

Yuka puts her hand in his.

“That's what I said.” ʸ

The game has changed twice by the time they reach it.

Umaru originally proposed teams. Nano pointed out the imbalance. Someone suggested rotating people after each point, and now Rem is trying to record which side she is supposed to be on while Suika moves a marker that no longer appears to mean anything.

Kenya has been placed on both teams. He considers this a promotion.

“You have to choose,” Shizue tells him. ˢᶻ

“I can help whichever one's losing.” ᴷᵉ

“Then you'll have to stop changing the score.” ˢᶻ

Chloe is sitting beside Ori just outside the court, examining her present. She holds it up to the light, turns it over and puts it in her own pocket before Ori can worry about whether she likes it. The little move makes Ori smile.

Milim brings Mau and Yuka directly to the centre.

“New teams.” ᴹⁱ

“We've just made them,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“Now they're better.” ᴹⁱ

Umaru looks at Mau. He looks at the lines in the sand, at the rope pretending to be a net and at Yuta trying to retrieve a marker from where Rika's smaller presence has decided to sit near him.

“What are we playing?” ᴹ

Everybody gives him a slightly different answer.

---

They settle on getting the ball across the rope without letting it touch the sand.

No techniques. No strengthening. No deciding that the other team's half of the beach has become a different jurisdiction in the middle of a point.

The last rule is added after Umaru asks a question.

Yuka gives Mau her sandals and stands across from him. He puts them beside his own, moves his hat into the shade and rolls his shoulders slowly before joining Yuta and Rem.

“You could have picked me,” Yuka says. ʸ

“I was assigned.” ᴹ

“You'll regret it.” ʸ

“I'm looking forward to that.” ᴹ

Milim serves.

The first ball goes so far past the court that everybody turns to watch it.

There is a small silence.

“No strengthening,” Shizue reminds her. ˢᶻ

“I didn't.” ᴹⁱ

Maki, standing at the edge with an unopened drink, lowers her head into one hand.

“Less than that, then.” ᴹᵃᵏ

Milim looks offended. Then she goes to get the ball.

---

The next serve stays on the beach.

Yuta gets underneath it, makes a careful return and has to move out of Rem's way when she changes direction. Mau reads the space between them and steps back instead of crowding the ball. Yuka spots the gap immediately.

She puts it right where he has stopped standing.

“I told you.” ʸ

He looks at the little mark it leaves in the sand.

“You did.” ᴹ

Rem apologizes. He shakes his head and points to the place she had been trying to reach.

“Go for it. I'll leave you room.” ᴹ

The next time she does. He moves before she has to look for him, Yuta covers the other side and they manage three returns before Milim becomes impatient and jumps too early.

The ball falls behind her.

Umaru sits down laughing. Milim turns toward the sound and discovers the point is over.

“I was going to get that.” ᴹⁱ

“From the air?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“I was coming down.” ᴹⁱ

Mau retrieves the ball and hands it through the rope to his wife. She takes it with both hands and holds his gaze for a second before she turns away.

He spends the next point trying not to smile.

---

People drift into the shade between rounds.

Hae-In joins for one game and discovers that Jin-Woo has been watching carefully enough to identify where her next return will go. She changes it at the last moment. The look she gives him when it lands is so pleased that he puts down his drink and gets up to play the next one.

Mau sits with Yuka between his knees while she finds a knot in the string around her shell. She leans back against him, hands raised in front of them, and tells him to hold one end very still.

“You're pulling.” ʸ

“I'm holding.” ᴹ

“Then hold more gently.” ʸ

He eases his grip. The knot gives way. She makes a triumphant sound, ties it the way she wants and puts it in her bag before turning to rest her forehead against his collar.

His hand settles at the back of her head. The game continues in front of them. He hears Milim demand another team change and Ori propose that Lantern count as a referee.

“He can't see the line from there,” Chloe tells her. ᶜʰ

Ori studies the dragon, who has settled under the far palms.

“We can move the line.” ᴼ

Yuka laughs against Mau's shirt.

“Your daughter.” ʸ

“Her mother's decision-making.” ᴹ

On the bench behind them Frieren turns a page.

“I can hear you.” ᶠ

“I know,” he says. ᴹ

Lunch finds them before they go looking for it.

Elmesia comes down the path with DIO and Testarossa. They have been looking at a garden beyond the rooms and have apparently acquired enough opinions about it to continue the conversation all the way to the beach. DIO points out a detail on the terrace with both hands, forgetting that one of them is supposed to keep her hat in place. Elmesia catches the brim before the wind takes it.

DIO stops talking.

“Keep going,” Elmesia says, holding it for her. ᴱˡ

“I negotiated poorly.” ʸ

“Then we'll put some chairs together.” ᴱˡ

Mau gets up, offers Yuka a hand and is rewarded with both arms lifted toward him instead.

“You said you'd walk eventually.” ᴹ

“This is still the little while.” ʸ

He bends and picks her up. She catches the hats before he can leave them behind, stacking them against his chest.

“The little while's getting longer,” he says. ᴹ

“You can complain after lunch.” ʸ

After lunch, Shion challenges someone to a game she has misunderstood.

Then Maki decides the rules can be improved. Senku asks what the original rules were. Anko says she would like to see that written down before they improve anything.

Mau and Yuka look at each other across the end of the table.

Neither gets up to leave.

When the lamps along the path begin coming on, Yuka has a little sand dried against her heel and Mau has lost track of which towel belongs to him. Ori is asleep against Frieren's side on the bench. The ball is resting beneath Milim's hand, ready for an argument tomorrow.

Yuka hooks two fingers into Mau's sleeve.

“We were going to have a quiet afternoon.” ʸ

“We can still have one.” ᴹ

“It's night.” ʸ

“Tomorrow, then.” ᴹ

“Tomorrow.” ʸ
