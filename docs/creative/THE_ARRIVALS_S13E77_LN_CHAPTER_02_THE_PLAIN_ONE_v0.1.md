# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 11 — The Plain One

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S13E77
**Continuity state:** DIO/Elmesia/Testarossa/Makima outing within agreed near-Mau range; no unlimited roaming or mind surveillance. Makima experiences choosing and accompanying first friend without goal, competent and obsessive. DIO natural affinity with Elmesia grows, not declared replacement for Noise. Dinner joins couple; Makima privately re-evaluates Pochita relative to Mau surviving erasure. This perception creates no new universal immunity. Pause intact.

*When: B+381, afternoon alongside the couple day, then dinner.*

Elmesia has been to the glass workshop before.

DIO can tell by the way the woman at the entrance greets her: pleased to see her, unsurprised that she has brought people, and entirely willing to tell her that she cannot stand in the doorway while a tray is being carried through it.

Elmesia moves immediately.

Testarossa steps aside with her. Antonio turns sideways to clear the rest of the path. Makima watches the tray pass before following DIO into the cooler part of the room.

The pieces on the table are small. Glass leaves, shallow dishes, things meant to hold water or catch the light. DIO reaches toward a little blue shape, stops before touching it and looks at the woman.

“You can pick it up,” she says. “Those are finished.” ᵃᵗᵗ

DIO lifts it with both hands. It resembles a leaf folded around an empty place, one end curved high enough to make a handle. She turns it slowly.

Elmesia stands close enough to see the change in its surface.

“That one's for a cutting,” she says. “A plant you want to root.” ᴱˡ

DIO looks down into the empty place.

“How do you keep it from getting too much water?” ᴰ

“You start by asking which plant you're putting in it.” ᴱˡ

“A very important one.” ᴰ

Elmesia's smile appears before she can help it.

“Then we'll ask carefully.” ᴱˡ

---

Makima follows the conversation without taking part.

She knows how to ask for information. The woman in the workshop has prices, time constraints, materials that will fail if they are handled badly. Elmesia knows which questions produce useful answers. Testarossa is looking at the supports beneath a shelf with the practical attention of someone who has already decided how Antonio will get past it again.

DIO asks whether the little vessel can travel. She describes a room and the direction of the light. Her description becomes more detailed as she goes. Elmesia listens until she has finished, then asks about the plant itself.

Makima stands beside a table of unfinished pieces and waits for an opening that nobody has required her to find.

The woman notices.

“Would you like to try one?” ᵃᵗᵗ

She points to the shallow forms ready for a simple surface finish. Makima looks at her hands, then at the table.

“What would I make?” ᴹᵏ

“A dish, unless you want to argue with the mould.” ᵃᵗᵗ

Testarossa laughs softly.

Makima takes the seat she is offered.

---

They are allowed to choose the finish.

DIO picks a colour, changes her mind when she sees it beside another and asks Elmesia to hold both toward the window. Elmesia does. They spend long enough comparing them that the woman brings over the little vessel again and shows them how each will look against it.

“This one,” DIO says at last. ᴰ

Elmesia keeps it in her hand while DIO studies it once more.

“You're sure?” ᴱˡ

“I think so.” ᴰ

“Then you can come back for the other if you decide you need two.” ᴱˡ

DIO looks at her. For a moment she has nothing to answer.

Makima chooses a pale finish almost immediately. The woman asks whether she wants a pattern at the edge.

“What would you suggest?” ᴹᵏ

“Depends what you like.” ᵃᵗᵗ

Makima looks down at the small dish. She can think of several answers that would keep the conversation moving. She can think of a question that would give her the woman's preference and let her agree with it.

Elmesia places two sample pieces beside her without speaking. One has a raised edge. The other is plain.

Makima touches both.

“This one,” she says, indicating the plain piece. ᴹᵏ

The woman takes away the other. Nothing further is required.

---

Outside, they stop where DIO can sit within the distance she has arranged with Mau.

He is near the quiet part of the pool with Yuka. He knows where they have gone and has agreed to stay in that area while DIO is out. The agreement is simple enough that she has asked about it only once since they left him.

Now she puts her hat on the bench beside her and looks at the small wrapped vessel in Elmesia's hands. It will stay at the workshop until the finish is ready. The woman let them carry this finished example outside to compare its size before they return it.

“Ori knows how much water,” DIO says. ᴰ

“You trust her.” ᴱˡ

“Very much.” ᴰ

Elmesia turns the vessel so the light catches the rim.

“Then tell me about her.” ᴱˡ

DIO begins with the plant. She reaches the instructions and the two new leaves before remembering that Elmesia has already heard that part. She stops.

“You told me some of it,” Elmesia says. “What did she say when you left?” ᴱˡ

DIO remembers the girl's hands on the pot, the concentration with which she read both pages. She tells Elmesia about the exact promise, the way Ori checked the line on the watering cup that morning.

Elmesia listens with the glass resting between her palms. When she smiles, DIO starts thinking of the next thing she wants to tell her.

There are a surprising number of them.

---

Testarossa goes back inside to ask about having a shallow tray wrapped for travel. Antonio follows her, paying careful attention to the shelf he nearly touched on the way in.

Makima stays near the bench.

She has not tried to direct the afternoon. A practical consequence of this is that she cannot say how long they intend to remain here, whether the next stop is food or the garden, or where she ought to stand when they begin walking again.

She can feel that Mau is alive in the ordinary way of the bond. The rest of what he is doing is his own unless he shares it. She looks toward the pool without reaching further.

DIO notices.

“You can ask him,” she says. ᴰ

“I know.” ᴹᵏ

“You could also stay here.” ᴰ

Makima looks at her.

DIO moves her hat enough to clear the space at the end of the bench. Makima sits. Elmesia asks if she liked the dish she chose.

“I haven't seen it finished.” ᴹᵏ

“Did you like choosing it?” ᴱˡ

Makima considers the question. The answer takes longer than she expects.

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

Elmesia nods, as if that is sufficient.

---

They find Mau and Yuka at dinner later, still in the quiet corner they chose in the morning.

Yuka's hair has dried in an untidy curve against one cheek. Mau has a hand resting over hers on the table and is trying to finish a point she appears determined to contest. Both turn when Elmesia asks whether they would mind company.

“Please,” Yuka says, moving the menus. ʸ

DIO takes the chair beside Elmesia. Testarossa sits where Antonio can stand clear of the path. Makima waits until Mau gestures toward the remaining place, then sits down with the memory of the last time she spent too long choosing a chair.

“What did you do?” Yuka asks. ʸ

DIO has barely begun before Elmesia interrupts to correct which of them insisted on holding the samples toward the window. DIO protests. Testarossa settles it by explaining that they both did it and made the woman bring a third sample.

Mau listens, amused, while Yuka asks about the little vessel. When DIO tells her what it is for, Yuka offers to help find something safe to put it in for the trip home.

“I can store it,” Mau says. ᴹ

“We know,” she answers. “We're going to wrap it before you do.” ʸ

He puts his free hand up in surrender.

Makima watches their hands settle together again.

He looks toward her.

“Did you make something?” ᴹ

“A dish.” ᴹᵏ

“For the room?” ᴹ

She had imagined it near a place where he might sit. She had not decided whether to tell him that.

“Maybe,” she says. ᴹᵏ

“I'd like to see.” ᴹ

She feels herself smile and keeps her voice level when she answers.

“When it's finished.” ᴹᵏ

---

The conversation turns to the things they didn't do.

Elmesia asks whether they found the boat they wanted. Yuka tells her that they got as far as considering it over breakfast. She asks about the far beach. Mau says he saw it through the curtains.

“The curtains?” ᴱˡ

“We found a very good place to sit.” ᴹ

DIO begins to laugh. Yuka looks pleased rather than defensive and takes another piece of bread.

Makima tries to place an entire day inside what they have described. Breakfast. Water. A place to lie down. Talking about a book. Waiting until the next meal.

Mau has spent that day without testing a technique, answering a challenge or preparing to endure something. She saw him survive what she believed could take a being out of existence. She has felt the disorder he carries and what it costs to hold it. Pochita once occupied a height in her understanding that she had no reason to question.

Now the man beside her is negotiating over the last piece of bread with his wife.

He gives it to Yuka. She breaks it in two and returns half.

Makima finds that she wants to remain at the table long after the food is gone.
