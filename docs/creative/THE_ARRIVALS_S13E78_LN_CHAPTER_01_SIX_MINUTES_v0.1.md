## Chapter 12 — Six Minutes

*When: B+382, late morning and lunch.*

Shoko has found the only table on the terrace that Gojo cannot reach without passing three people who will ask him where he is going.

She explains this to Mau when he asks if the chair is taken.

“You studied the route,” he says. ᴹ

“I know my subject.” ˢʰ

He sits with his cup. Yuka has gone down the path with Mai and Momo Nishimiya to look at something in a shop window. She told him not to follow because he has opinions and might express them before she has made up her mind.

He has been dismissed with a kiss and instructions to remain findable.

Shoko looks over the top of her cup.

“Where's your supervisor?” ˢʰ

“Shopping.” ᴹ

“Did you offer to help?” ˢʰ

“That's why I'm here.” ᴹ

She laughs. He settles farther back in the chair, putting his right elbow where the old chest seam won't pull. She notices the adjustment and lets it pass. He has already told her it catches when he stretches. She will ask again if he starts protecting the arm instead of using it.

For now she drinks her coffee while he watches two people on the pier argue about a rope.

“You promised me somewhere like this,” she says. ˢʰ

“I did.” ᴹ

“You left out the stairs.” ˢʰ

“I can carry you.” ᴹ

“Don't let Satoru hear you. He'll demand equal treatment.” ˢʰ

“He can walk on the air.” ᴹ

“He can also complain while doing it.” ˢʰ

Mau smiles into his cup.

---

Anko finds them by accident and makes it look like she intended to.

She puts a small packet on the table, takes the empty chair and asks Mau what he knows about the path beyond the old wall. Shoko leans back with immediate interest.

“Did you take the short stairs?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“They became long.” ᴬⁿ

“How?” ᴹ

“Luminous found another thing to look at.” ᴬⁿ

“That happens,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

Anko opens the packet. There is a leaf wrapped in damp cloth inside it, a label tied around its stem.

Mau looks at it.

“Is this for the plant?” ᴹ

“The woman said it would do well near a window. I asked three questions so that you couldn't complain I brought you something difficult.” ᴬⁿ

“I don't complain.” ᴹ

Both women look at him.

“About plants,” he adds. ᴹ

Anko slides the packet toward him. He lifts the corner of the damp cloth, reads the label and stores the cutting as it is. It will come out exactly as she has wrapped it; he can keep the instructions with it instead of relying on remembering which leaf belongs beside which window.

“Thank you.” ᴹ

“You can show me where you put it when we get home.” ᴬⁿ

“Our windowsill?” ᴹ

“Depends what your wife has done to it by then.” ᴬⁿ

Mau glances toward the path Yuka took.

“We might need another window.” ᴹ

---

They talk about the walk while the terrace changes around them.

Anko tells them about the narrow part where Hinata offered a hand before she needed it. She leaves out how long she kept holding it afterward. Shoko notices the omission with visible enjoyment and does not help her hide it.

Mau asks about the things they saw from the wall. He wants the route written down for another day. Anko turns a napkin over, takes out a pencil and begins drawing a map so detailed that she has to move her cup to make room.

“This part's steep,” she says. ᴬⁿ

“You said it was the short walk.” ᴹ

“Short and steep can coexist.” ᴬⁿ

He studies the little line.

“Yuka will want me to carry her.” ᴹ

“Then stop advertising the service.” ᴬⁿ

Shoko puts her empty cup down.

“He won't.” ˢʰ

“I know.” ᴬⁿ

They discuss him for a few minutes as if he has gone somewhere else. He stays in his chair, finishes the last of his drink and offers a correction when they accuse him of agreeing to something before he heard the question.

“I heard most of it.” ᴹ

“There,” says Anko. ᴬⁿ

Shoko nods.

---

Hinata comes to find Anko with a different packet and an apologetic explanation about an errand that took longer than expected. Anko gets up before she finishes. She leaves the map beside Mau's cup and tells him which part he should not attempt to improve.

Hinata thanks him for including her in the trip.

“She was getting tired of threatening the calendar,” Mau says. ᴹ

Anko points at him.

“Confidential.” ᴬⁿ

“There was a whole room there.” ᴹ

“They can all be confidential too.” ᴬⁿ

Hinata smiles and takes her hand. They walk away together, Anko still looking back to make certain he understands.

Mau waits until they are out of earshot.

“She brought me a plant.” ᴹ

“I saw.” ˢʰ

“I'll have to put it somewhere good.” ᴹ

Shoko looks at the empty chair Anko left.

“She'll tell you if you don't.” ˢʰ

---

Gojo appears at the far end of the terrace.

Shoko watches him greet the first person on her predicted route. He stops, answers a question, notices somebody farther along and changes direction. She makes a small, satisfied sound.

“How long do you think?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Five minutes if he gets distracted.” ˢʰ

“And if he doesn't?” ᴹ

“Three.” ˢʰ

He watches Gojo begin a conversation with Jinshi. Shoko reaches for the menu.

“We can get something before then.” ˢʰ

Mau turns toward her.

“You're allowed to tell him you want a quiet cup of coffee.” ᴹ

“I know.” ˢʰ

She says it without irritation. It is a statement of what she has chosen for the morning. He finds himself smiling at the answer, and she catches it.

“What?” ˢʰ

“Nothing.” ᴹ

“You learned that answer from somebody.” ˢʰ

“Several people. It seems popular.” ᴹ

She gives him the menu first.

---

He asks about the name while she is choosing something sweet.

He has been using it in his own head, putting it beside the words he already knows: Mau Ieiri. Ori Ieiri. There are moments when the shape of it still surprises him.

“Does it bother you?” he asks. ᴹ

Shoko looks up.

“Which part?” ˢʰ

“That it's mine too.” ᴹ

She puts the menu down between them.

“I said you could have it.” ˢʰ

“I know. I meant when other people say it.” ᴹ

Her expression changes slightly. He watches her consider the question instead of answering it quickly for him.

“I like hearing it,” she says. “It takes me a second sometimes. Then I like it.” ˢʰ

He nods.

She reaches across, smooths a piece of hair that has caught beneath the knot of his blindfold and rests her hand at the back of his head for a moment.

“You don't have to keep checking whether I meant it.” ˢʰ

“All right.” ᴹ

“Especially when you brought me somewhere with coffee and someone else doing the washing up.” ˢʰ

He laughs. She keeps her hand there just long enough for him to lean into it before letting go.

---

Yuka returns with a bag and the expression of someone who has made a good decision.

She comes up behind Mau, puts both arms around his shoulders and rests her chin beside his hat. He tilts his head toward her immediately.

“You found me.” ᴹ

“I told you to stay findable.” ʸ

“Excellent instructions.” ᴹ

She turns his face toward her and kisses him, leaning over his shoulder until he lifts a hand to steady her. When she draws back, he follows far enough to steal another.

“I was only gone a little while,” she says, delighted. ʸ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

She puts the bag on the table and comes around his chair. Mau shifts his knees before she asks. Yuka sits sideways in his lap, makes herself comfortable against his chest and opens the bag for Shoko, who asks a question that proves she understands the purchase better than Mau would have.

“See?” Yuka tells him. ʸ

“I haven't expressed an opinion.” ᴹ

“You were going to.” ʸ

Yuka moves the bag to her unused chair once Shoko has finished looking inside. Mau catches her hand on its way back and kisses her fingers. Shoko asks what they intend to do after lunch. Yuka tells her about Anko's map, resting the napkin on Mau's forearm so they can both see the route.

“That's steep,” she says. ʸ

He closes his eyes beneath the cloth.

“I know.” ᴹ

Shoko starts laughing before Yuka can lift her arms to demonstrate the solution.

---

Gojo reaches the table after the food arrives.

He looks at the three places, at the dish Shoko has chosen and at Yuka occupying the same chair as Mau.

“You started without me.” ᴳ

Shoko moves the unused chair beside hers. Yuka reaches for the bag on its seat and gives it to Mau to store before Gojo can sit on it.

“You took six minutes.” ˢʰ

“Was there a time limit?” ᴳ

“There is now.” ˢʰ

He sits beside her and immediately tries what she has ordered. She hits his wrist with the handle of the spoon, but she is smiling when she does it.

Mau catches Yuka's hand before she reaches his own plate. She looks offended until he puts a piece into it for her.

“Thank you,” she says sweetly. ʸ

Gojo watches them for a moment and then looks at Shoko.

“We did a good job.” ᴳ

Mau looks up.

“With what?” ᴹ

Shoko takes another spoonful, gives the question every appearance of serious consideration and declines to answer.
