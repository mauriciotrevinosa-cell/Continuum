# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 13 — The Unhappy Face

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S13E78
**Continuity state:** Couple follow Anko map on optional walk, playful mistake at closed gate and own choice lunch/shop. World outsider recognizes Mau without new attack; Yuka keeps ordinary conversation. Island pass covers participating vendors. Inventory for bulk, shop not free. Shirt chosen for comfort across established scar. Physical strain distinct from unlimited reserves. All quoted dialogue has superscript marks.

*When: B+382, afternoon walk and late lunch.*

Anko's map is accurate about the stairs.

This is the first thing Mau says when he sees them. The second is that she has failed to convey the full experience of looking up from the bottom.

Yuka stands beside him with the folded napkin in her hands.

“She drew every turn.” ʸ

“She made them very small.” ᴹ

“It was a napkin.” ʸ

He looks at the first landing. It has a bench, some shade and a view that seems designed to persuade people to forgive what comes after it.

Yuka puts the map away.

“We don't have to go.” ʸ

“Do you want to?” ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

He offers his hand.

She takes it and begins walking before he can offer the other service.

---

They reach the first landing under their own power.

Yuka looks satisfied. She drinks some water, gives the bottle to Mau and leans on the wall to see the buildings below. He joins her. From here the terrace where they had lunch is hidden by a roof, and the pool becomes a clear shape between the palms.

“We were there all day,” she says. ʸ

“You chose well.” ᴹ

“I did.” ʸ

He lets her look until she is ready to move. At the next flight she asks whether he has been counting the steps. He says no. She tells him the number she has reached and immediately regrets it when he asks whether she is certain.

“Don't make me start again.” ʸ

“I'm interested in accurate records.” ᴹ

She presses his hand against the small of her back and resumes climbing.

“Record this.” ʸ

---

At the third landing a woman is opening a stall.

She has put cloth over the table and is arranging little bundles tied with string. Yuka stops to look. The woman greets her, asks whether they have come from the beach and begins explaining what the bundles are before Mau has found somewhere to stand that leaves the path clear.

They contain dried leaves and peel for hot water. Yuka asks about the smell of one. The woman unties it and lets her choose a piece to crush between her fingers.

“Here,” Yuka says, holding her hand up to Mau. ʸ

He leans down. Something sharp and clean rises off her fingertips.

“That one's good.” ᴹ

“For the room?” ʸ

“For wherever you intend to put it.” ᴹ

The woman has been looking at him since he bent closer. She takes in the cloth over his eyes, the white in his shorter hair and the old mark running beside the edge of the blindfold. Her fingers stop on the string.

Mau notices the pause. He straightens slowly, keeping his hands where she can see them.

“Two of those, please,” Yuka says. ʸ

The woman looks back at her.

Yuka is still holding the little crushed leaf. She asks which other one would taste different enough to make it worth trying both. The woman begins answering, uncertain at first, then more steadily when Yuka asks a follow-up question about how long to leave it in the water.

Mau listens. When the woman has finished wrapping the second bundle, he asks how she keeps the packets from getting damp beside the sea.

She turns and shows him the box beneath the table, lifting the lid so he can see the raised bottom. He thanks her. Yuka presents Elmesia's pass, and the woman writes down the seal for the bill.

They leave with two bundles and instructions about the kettle.

At the next turn Yuka puts her hand back in his.

“We can try them tonight.” ʸ

“Both?” ᴹ

“One each. Then switch.” ʸ

He nods. They keep walking.

---

The wrong turn is Mau's fault.

Anko has put a mark beside the narrow path. Mau interprets it as a direction to take the path. Yuka says it might mean that they should avoid it. He points out that the line continues. She points out that Anko also drew a small unhappy face.

“It could be an encouragement.” ᴹ

“For whom?” ʸ

They try it far enough to discover that it ends at a closed service gate. There is another bench beside it. Yuka sits down, folds her hands in her lap and waits for his assessment.

He examines the gate.

“This is an excellent unhappy face.” ᴹ

She laughs so suddenly that he turns toward her before he can finish pretending to read the notice. She keeps laughing while he comes to sit beside her, and she leans across to catch his shirt when he starts laughing too.

“We could have asked,” she says eventually. ʸ

“We can ask on the way back.” ᴹ

“Will you tell her?” ʸ

“She'll know.” ᴹ

Yuka rests her head against his shoulder.

“She will.” ʸ

They sit in the little patch of shade long enough to drink the rest of the water in the first bottle. Mau brings the spare out of his inventory when she holds up the empty one. She takes it, unscrews the cap and passes him the first drink.

---

They find the correct turn without further interpretation.

Beyond it the stairs level out along the old wall. There is a long view down toward the harbour and the smaller boats tied beyond the pier. Yuka stops at the first opening to look. Mau comes up beside her and shifts the cloth just enough to follow where she points.

The water offers too much detail if he lets it. He chooses the outline of the boat she likes, the space beneath its awning and the way she holds his wrist while describing where she wants to sit.

“Lunch,” he says. ᴹ

“Obviously.” ʸ

“Something soft.” ᴹ

“You remember.” ʸ

He kisses her hair.

“I listen when the instructions matter.” ᴹ

She turns toward him, catches the front of his shirt and draws him down until she can kiss his mouth instead.

“All my instructions matter.” ʸ

“That sounds expensive.” ᴹ

“You have a shop.” ʸ

“It charges money.” ᴹ

“Then we'd better enjoy the free view.” ʸ

She stays with both hands on his shirt for a moment after she says it. He puts his hands at her waist and lets her keep him there.

---

They go down by a different route.

It reaches a small row of shops and a place serving lunch beneath a patched awning. Yuka chooses it because she likes the smell. Mau likes that she says so without looking for the place on anyone's suggested list.

The owner offers a table by the edge. He recognizes the seal, tells them which dishes take longer and lets Yuka ask whether one of the sauces can come separately. Mau orders something different so they can try both.

“You planned that,” she says when the plates arrive. ʸ

“I've been learning.” ᴹ

She pushes her plate into the middle before taking anything from his. He tries what she recommends and changes his opinion of the sauce. She notices, makes him admit it and looks pleased enough that he asks whether she intends to write that down too.

“I have witnesses,” she says. ʸ

The owner, passing with a jug, looks politely away.

---

They are full enough after lunch that the shop with the clothes becomes a place to look rather than an urgent destination.

Yuka nevertheless finds a shirt for him almost immediately.

She holds it up, compares it to the one he is wearing and asks the woman behind the counter if he can try it. Mau is given no official opportunity to vote. He is, however, allowed to identify the size.

“This one's better at the shoulder,” Yuka says when he comes out. ʸ

He moves his arm, feeling where the cloth gives rather than dragging across the old seam.

“It is.” ᴹ

“See?” ʸ

He looks toward her through the lowered edge of the blindfold.

“You chose it because it's comfortable?” ᴹ

She steps closer and smooths the front down with both palms. Then she looks up at his face.

“Several reasons.” ʸ

He stays in the shirt while they choose the rest. She finds something for herself and asks his opinion before he has to guess whether she wants it. He tells her what he likes. She tries another one, tells him why she prefers the first and buys that instead.

Outside, he stores the wrapped parcel in the inventory. She keeps the little packet of leaves in her hand because she likes its smell.

---

On the way back, Yuka starts looking at the stairs with less enthusiasm.

Mau notices at the same moment she notices him noticing. She gives him a look of exaggerated patience.

“You can offer now.” ʸ

“Would you like—” ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

He bends, picks her up and settles her against the shoulder where the new shirt lets him move freely. She puts one arm around his neck and keeps the packet in the other hand.

“How will I know where we're going?” he asks. ᴹ

“I'll read the map.” ʸ

“The unhappy face?” ᴹ

“We're going to avoid that one.” ʸ

She kisses his cheek, unfolds the napkin and gives him the first direction.

He follows it.
