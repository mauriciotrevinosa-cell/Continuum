# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 13 — Things You Did Not Hear

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S13E79
**Continuity state:** Couple deliberately transmit chosen messages through existing private mind link all morning, not passive access. Other household conversations remain spoken; friends present and mixed groups. Yuta notices unusual silence but physical affection contradicts assumed quarrel; payoff pending next scene. Maki/Mai existing distinct relationships. No new telepathy ability, no memory intrusion.

*When: B+383, morning and market through lunch.*

The first message arrives while Mau is brushing his teeth.

*Don't put that shirt in the inventory yet.* ʸ

He looks toward the doorway. Yuka is standing beside the wardrobe, holding yesterday's purchase against the one she originally packed for him.

*Which one?* ᴹ

She raises it.

*That one. I want you in it.* ʸ

He lifts a hand in acknowledgment, finishes what he is doing and comes out to find she has put the shirt on his side of the bed. She has also found something for herself and is holding it by the shoulders while she looks at the weather beyond the balcony.

He goes behind her and puts both arms around her waist.

*That one,* he tells her. ᴹ

She leans back against him.

*I haven't asked.* ʸ

*You were looking at the other one.* ᴹ

*I might still wear it.* ʸ

*Then I'll be pleasantly surprised.* ᴹ

She turns in his arms to kiss him, smiling before she gets there.

By breakfast they have discussed the stall from yesterday, a plan to meet the others at the market and whether either of them intends to admit to Anko that they found her unhappy face.

Neither has said very much aloud.

* * *

They meet Mai at the path with Momo Nishimiya and Marin.

Mai is studying the front of Mau's new shirt with the suspicious satisfaction of someone whose advice has been followed without being credited. Momo looks at Yuka's dress and asks where she found it. Yuka tells her, then points toward the street they mean to take.

Mau reaches for Yuka's hand. She puts hers in it without turning.

*Your aunt is inspecting the stitching.* ᴹ

*She'll tell you if she hates it.* ʸ

*She hasn't said anything.* ᴹ

*Then you passed.* ʸ

He smiles. Mai looks up at his face.

“What?” ᴹᵃ

“Nothing.” ᴹ

“Never reassuring.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka leans her shoulder into his arm, takes the paper bag Marin offers and looks at what she has brought. Marin begins explaining the order in which she wants to visit the shops. It changes when they see the first window.

* * *

The market is busy enough that they keep finding people from the house between the visitors.

Coco and her mother have stopped at a stall with woven bands. Tetia holds two beside each other while Agott explains that they have already spent too long deciding. Richeh has found the one she wants and is waiting with admirable patience until someone asks her why she didn't say so.

“You didn't ask,” she answers. ᴿⁱ

Mai looks directly at Mau.

He raises both hands before she says anything.

Yuka's laugh arrives privately a moment before she manages to conceal the rest of it.

*She has a point.* ʸ

*She usually does. It hurts more when she demonstrates.* ᴹ

Yuka moves his hand down so she can keep holding it. Her thumb passes over the old finger damage, tracing the familiar crooked line lightly before she lets their arms hang between them.

At the next stall, Nijika is trying to stop Ryo from committing the entire group's spending money to a single instrument neither of them knows how to play. Bocchi is listening to the owner demonstrate it. Kita sees Yuka and waves them over, then looks at Mau as if another responsible person might be useful.

He leans toward the instrument, listens and asks what the smallest version costs.

Kita looks betrayed.

*You were supposed to stop them,* Yuka tells him. ʸ

*I wanted to know.* ᴹ

*That's how it starts.* ʸ

*You're holding three bags.* ᴹ

She passes him two of them. He stores them after checking that she has taken out what she wants to keep looking at.

The instrument stays at the stall. Bocchi leaves with the owner's permission to come listen again, which appears to satisfy her more than carrying it home would have.

* * *

They stop for something cold beneath a wide awning.

Mau stands at the counter with Yuta while the others find seats. Yuka points to a place beside hers, then raises two fingers to show him what she wants. He has already heard the full order through the link, including which piece of fruit she intends to take out and give him.

*You could order it without that,* he says. ᴹ

*Then you wouldn't get any.* ʸ

*Thoughtful.* ᴹ

*I try.* ʸ

He brings the drinks over, puts hers exactly where she has cleared a space and accepts the piece she gives him before she has to ask. She taps the chair beside her. He sits, their knees fitting beneath the table without either looking down.

Yuta watches from the other side.

Maki takes her drink from him and follows his attention.

“What?” she asks. ᴹᵃᵏ

“Nothing.” ʸᵗ

She examines him for a moment, then decides her drink matters more.

Mau and Yuka continue discussing the instrument stall. She thinks he was genuinely tempted. He says he would have needed to ask whether the instrument tolerates being stored. She sends him an image of Ryo's face when he asked the price, a small deliberate memory she chooses to share rather than the rest of what she was thinking.

He laughs into his cup.

Yuka touches his wrist to keep him from spilling it.

Across the table, Mai puts her chin on one hand and watches them both.

* * *

The group divides at the square.

Marin wants to look at a tailor's work. Mai and Momo go with her. Maki chooses to follow a little farther while Yuta stops at a stall showing ordinary knives and cooking tools. Mau joins him. Yuka looks at the clothes, tells Mau where she is going through the link and kisses his cheek before following the others.

He watches her turn the corner, then puts his attention on the objects laid out before him.

Yuta picks up a small kitchen knife with permission and tests the balance.

“Shuna would like this handle.” ʸᵗ

“She'd want to try it.” ᴹ

“We could ask if she can come back.” ʸᵗ

They do. The seller gives them the hours and puts the knife aside when Yuta says he will bring the person who actually knows what she wants.

*Maki is pretending not to like a hat,* Yuka says. ʸ

Mau pauses with another utensil in his hand.

*Is it enormous?* ᴹ

*Normal. That's how I know it's serious.* ʸ

He laughs.

Yuta glances at him, then down the street where the others went. He appears to consider something and puts it aside.

* * *

They meet again near the lower path.

Yuka comes straight to Mau, hooks her hand around his elbow and rests her head against his arm while Mai finishes telling Marin what can be altered on the garment she has chosen. Mau looks down at his wife.

*Hungry?* ᴹ

*Always.* ʸ

*I found a place.* ᴹ

*Does it have somewhere to sit?* ʸ

*I learned my lesson.* ᴹ

She squeezes his elbow.

“This way,” Mau tells the others. ᴹ

He leads them down the path. Yuka stays close while privately informing him which of their purchases she intends to show Shoko first. He objects to one of her descriptions. She makes it worse until he has to stop pretending he isn't laughing.

Behind them Yuta slows enough to walk beside Mai.

“Have they said anything to each other today?” he asks quietly. ʸᵗ

Mai looks at the couple ahead. Yuka has just caught Mau's sleeve and made him stop so she can brush something off his shoulder. He waits, smiling down at her.

“Something,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“I haven't heard it.” ʸᵗ

“Then perhaps it wasn't for you.” ᴹᵃ

Yuta thinks about that. Mai leaves him with it and goes to join Momo before Marin can volunteer her for another fitting.

* * *

By the time they reach the lunch table, Mau knows what Yuka has seen in every shop, which part she wants to revisit and why she has decided not to buy the thing she originally went to find.

She knows what Yuta picked for Shuna, what Mau thought of the instrument and that he has been carrying an extra cold bottle for her since the square.

He sets it in front of her when they sit.

She smiles, presses her knee against his and sends him a thank-you that arrives without a sound.

Yuta reaches for his own glass, misses the edge of it and has to catch it before it tips.

Maki looks at him.

“Just ask them,” she says. ᴹᵃᵏ
