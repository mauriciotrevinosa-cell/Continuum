The afternoon brings a little rain.

It starts while they are choosing whether to walk back by the water or the shops. Marin notices the first drops on the fabric she has brought out to show Wakana. He gets it covered before she finishes the warning. Everyone moves beneath the nearest awning and discovers that it belongs to a place with enough tables for most of them to sit down.

They stay for something warm.

Other members of the house arrive by chance. Kaneki comes in with Touka and Ichika, who has decided the rain is something she should be allowed to go back out and touch. Touka keeps one hand on the back of her little shirt while Kaneki orders. Ori notices them from the far end of the table and brings the shell she has already shown at least six people.

Mau turns his chair so Yuka can lean back against him while talking to Touka. He is still listening when a little hand appears at his knee.

Ichika has brought him something wrapped in a cloth.

He looks to Touka before opening it. She nods, smiling.

It is an extremely ordinary stone.

“Good choice,” he tells Ichika. ᴹ

Mau stops far enough back that Bocchi won't feel he has come to inspect her. Yuka stays beside him, her arm around his waist.

They listen until the piece ends.
