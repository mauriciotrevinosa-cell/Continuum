# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 17 — Enough Chairs

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S13E80
**Continuity state:** Luminous reconnects at long tea with all seven resident witches; prior friendship respected. DIO/Elmesia/Testarossa and Makima participate naturally; no automatic forgiveness or emotional cure. Tea begins after couple return from boat; Mau participates from its start, familiar bonds and practical gardening discussion. DIO attraction emerges in choices not declaration. Gift from Elmesia remains unspecified. No new magic or plant relocation executed.

*When: B+384, afternoon tea after the couple return from the boat, before dinner.*

Mau puts both hats on the empty bench while Luminous lays three unopened tins beside the pot.

Yuka still has wind in her hair. She pushes it behind one ear, takes the place between Mau and Satella and lets Mau move her chair nearer the shade before sitting down himself.

“Which one first?” he asks, examining the labels. ᴹ

“You wrote the list,” Luminous says. ᴸᵘ

“This one wasn't on the list.” ᴹ

“It is now.” ᴸᵘ

Echidna reaches for the nearest tin. Mau opens it for her and holds it between them while they consider the unfamiliar scent. Yuka leans across his arm, decides it smells more interesting than the one she recognizes and points to the empty pot.

They begin there.

Luminous knows she has chosen the right table when Sekhmet sits down without asking how long the meeting will last.

There is shade, a broad seat at one end and enough distance from the main path that Daphne can examine the planted border without repeatedly moving her chair. Elmesia has sent the tea things they asked for. She has also sent a tray of things nobody asked for, which Pride has begun inspecting.

“Those are for everyone,” Minerva says. ᴹⁱⁿ

“I'm checking.” ᴾʳ

Luminous moves her remaining packages toward the centre of the table.

“You can try these next. I was told somebody would take responsibility if they were unpopular.” ᴸᵘ

Echidna looks up.

“Mau?” ᴱᶜ

“Who else writes instructions for tea as if he's arranging a border crossing?” ᴸᵘ

Carmilla takes the packet nearest her, reads the label and smiles into the edge of her sleeve. Satella moves the empty dish toward Pride before the girl has to reach across anyone's cup.

The familiar blends come out beside the new one. Mau passes each tin on after reading the label, and Luminous tells them which she would pair with the biscuits.

An hour passes before somebody mentions the hour.

Echidna has a question about the way the island keeps its kitchens supplied. Luminous answers the part she knows, points out what Elmesia would know better and refuses to have three unrelated questions treated as though they were one.

“You said I could ask,” Echidna reminds her. ᴱᶜ

“You can. I can also finish answering.” ᴸᵘ

Sekhmet makes a small sound of approval without opening her eyes. Mau passes Yuka the biscuits before putting in his own question.

“Were those three separate questions when you started?” ᴹ

“They became relevant,” Echidna says. ᴱᶜ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

Luminous looks grateful for his assistance until he asks whether the first answer changes what they ought to order for dinner. She puts her cup down and tells him he can wait with the others.

Daphne has already shown Luminous the packets she bought at the market. The exchange grows practical: light, soil, how much of the garden remains unclaimed and whether Rimuru understands how many seedlings she considers a modest beginning.

“He said we'd see what grew,” Daphne says. ᴰᵃ

“That can be an expensive sentence.” ᴸᵘ

“He looked happy.” ᴰᵃ

“Then perhaps he deserves to learn.” ᴸᵘ

Minerva objects to the delight with which Luminous says it. Luminous points out that she is offering gardening advice, not an injury. Pride laughs so hard that she has to put down the thing she has finally selected from the tray.

---

The others approach when the conversation has made room for them.

Elmesia comes with Testarossa, carrying a pot she was told they would want to inspect. Antonio pauses at the outer edge until Testarossa indicates a clear place for him. A second parcel rests beneath Elmesia's arm: DIO's finished vessel from the workshop, which she has been keeping until they can compare it with the plants.

“I thought this might save you an argument,” Elmesia says, putting the pot beside Daphne. ᴱˡ

Daphne looks at it. Then at her packets.

“It starts another one.” ᴰᵃ

Elmesia laughs and takes the chair Luminous offers.

Elmesia unwraps one corner to show Daphne the shape. She leaves the rest for DIO to open when she joins them.

“Has she decided what to put in it?” Daphne asks. ᴰᵃ

“She's decided Anko should help her choose. Anko may not know this yet.” ᴱˡ

“Then she should come to the next tea,” Luminous says. ᴸᵘ

“Anko?” ᴱˡ

“Anko and her plant. Hinata will come if I tell her it's an invitation with practical consequences.” ᴸᵘ

Minerva gives her a look.

“You could just invite both of them.” ᴹⁱⁿ

“I have. The practical consequences are what will persuade Anko she isn't interrupting.” ᴸᵘ

Satella sets down the spoon she has been turning between her fingers.

“We can tell her ourselves. She already comes past the garden.” ˢᵃ

“With the notebook,” Daphne says. ᴰᵃ

“And the wrong shoes,” Minerva adds. ᴹⁱⁿ

They begin telling Luminous about the afternoon Anko stepped into the wet bed while looking at a leaf and spent the next ten minutes trying to prove she had intended to examine the soil. Satella corrects the story when it reaches the part about whose shoes had to be left outside. The fact that she was there, and remembers Anko laughing rather than apologizing all afternoon, makes Luminous sit back with her cup.

“I would have liked to see that,” she says. ᴸᵘ

“Come when you can stay long enough for something unplanned,” Echidna tells her. ᴱᶜ

Luminous looks toward the water, considering the days she has already spent here.

“I'm attempting it.” ᴸᵘ

“You're doing very well,” Sekhmet says without moving. ˢᵉᵏ

Pride wants to know whether this means Hinata can join their next game. Luminous asks which game before making promises on anyone's behalf. The answer involves teams, several disputed rules and a ball Milim has been told she must not hit through anything.

“And does she obey?” ᴸᵘ

“She moves the things first,” Minerva says. ᴹⁱⁿ

Testarossa laughs into her tea. Antonio inclines his head when she touches his forearm; he was one of the things asked to move.

---

Pride catches Mau considering the plate nearest her.

“Those little ones are the best,” she tells him. ᴾʳ

“All of them?” ᴹ

“I checked.” ᴾʳ

Satella moves a small plate toward him. There are two pieces on it.

“These aren't as sweet,” she says. ˢᵃ

Yuka thanks her before Mau can and moves the plate between them. Beside him DIO comes out after he relays Elmesia's invitation. She accepts the parcel Elmesia has kept for her and begins unwrapping it when Carmilla asks what she made. Elmesia gets her a pencil to show the leaves of the plant she has in mind.

Luminous has finished her account of the visitors who kept her busy. Now Echidna wants to know which part of the coast Mau and Yuka chose that morning.

Mau and Yuka give the account of the boat under questioning that becomes less orderly as it goes.

Echidna wants to know the route. Daphne asks what grows on the beach where they stopped. Pride wants to know whether they saw anything big enough to fight. Yuka tells her that Mau lost to a stone.

He lowers his head.

“It was the wrong shape.” ᴹ

“I taught him,” Yuka says. ʸ

“She did.” ᴹ

“He improved.” ʸ

“One skip.” ᴹ

Luminous looks at him.

“A productive expedition.” ᴸᵘ

He accepts the verdict. Yuka puts one of the pieces in his hand and takes the other for herself.

---

Daphne turns her chair toward the planting border beside the awning. Mau follows the line of her hand from his place at the table while she points out where the afternoon shade reaches first and explains why that changes what she wants to try at home. His questions are ordinary ones: whether she has somewhere to put the packets, whether she wants them stored, whether she has told Rimuru about the ones she went back for.

She looks at him.

“I was going to.” ᴰᵃ

“I'll wait here while you do it.” ᴹ

“I don't need permission.” ᴰᵃ

“You might need another garden.” ᴹ

She gives the problem serious consideration. He stays with her long enough to hear the first version of the plan, then the second, in which the path between two beds becomes narrower in a way he suspects she will have to explain to someone else.

At the table Yuka has begun looking at DIO's drawing. It is recognizable as a plant. Elmesia identifies the part she thinks DIO has made too round. DIO objects that the leaf she remembers from the pot at home is exactly that round.

“We'll ask Ori,” Yuka says. ʸ

DIO considers it, then nods.

Elmesia folds the paper when DIO has finished and asks whether she can keep it until they find a better place to put it. DIO has the answer ready more quickly than she expects.

“Yes.” ᴰ

She watches Elmesia put it safely inside the cover of her notebook.

When Mau turns back from Daphne's plan, Yuka has refilled his cup.

She moves it clear of the seed packet beside his hand. He takes a careful sip and looks toward Luminous.

“Did the list work?” ᴹ

“We haven't exhausted it.” ᴸᵘ

“That's diplomatic.” ᴹ

“You'll hear complaints directly.” ᴸᵘ

“I usually do.” ᴹ

Sekhmet opens one eye.

“Does that boat require getting out again?” ˢᵉᵏ

“Only at the end,” Yuka says. ʸ

“Promising.” ˢᵉᵏ

Makima comes out near Mau after he checks with her. Carmilla moves a cup to clear a place. Makima thanks her, sits and finds Pride explaining the wrong way to throw the ball they have been discussing.

“There's a wrong way?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Milim's,” Pride says. ᴾʳ

“She scored,” Yuka points out. ʸ

“On the other beach.” ᴾʳ

Makima has seen the ball return under protest. She gives the distance with enough accuracy that Pride looks vindicated and Yuka has to admit this complicates the case for counting it.

“You should have moved the boundary,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Minerva turns toward her.

“That's what she said.” ᴹⁱⁿ

For a moment Makima considers the resemblance. Then she looks at Mau's expression and smiles before he can make the comparison aloud.

Yuka takes the last biscuit from his plate while he is distracted. He watches it go, then moves the empty plate closer to her.

“For your next acquisition.” ᴹ

She places a little piece back on it.

“We share here.” ʸ

“I had heard that.” ᴹ

The conversation finds its way to the next game without anybody agreeing whether Pride's team lost the previous one. Sekhmet has closed her eye again, but she is smiling when Luminous asks whether being referee can be done from this particular chair.

---

They remain until the tea has become a reason to stay rather than something anyone still needs.

Mau brings the bags Daphne wants stored and leaves the packets she intends to show Rimuru in her lap. Yuka gets up to stretch, comes behind his chair and rests her forearms over his shoulders while waiting for him to finish the last conversation.

“Dinner?” she asks quietly. ʸ

“Soon.” ᴹ

“I'm going to hold you to that.” ʸ

He takes one of her hands and keeps it against his shoulder.

“You can.” ᴹ

On the other side of the table, Elmesia says something that makes DIO laugh before she can stop herself. DIO puts a hand over her mouth and looks surprised by the sound. Elmesia smiles back at her without asking for an explanation.

Mau feels Yuka notice too. Neither of them interrupts.
