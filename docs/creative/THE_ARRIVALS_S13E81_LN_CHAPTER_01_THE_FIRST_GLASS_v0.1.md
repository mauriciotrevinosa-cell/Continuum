# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 17 — The First Glass

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S13E81
**Continuity state:** Mau already knows and loves carajillos, not first discovery. Consensual optional alcohol; Anko/Gojo talkative after first glass without sickness or drunken sexual escalation. Couples and adult household convivence; Shizue students resident nearby, never outsiders at school. Inner DIO/Noise/Ciel privacy retained and Noise concise. Makima participates from beginning, still capable of directing but chooses ordinary moment. No handholding permission for Makima yet.

*When: B+384, after tea and dinner through the evening.*

Mau recognizes the drink before it reaches the table.

He has been watching the tray with enough interest that Yuka follows his gaze, looks at the glasses and begins smiling.

“There it is,” she says. ʸ

“I didn't order it loudly.” ᴹ

“You asked three questions.” ʸ

“I wanted to make sure it was the one.” ᴹ

The attendant sets it down in front of him. Mau thanks him, takes the first sip and closes his eyes for a second beneath the cloth.

Yuka leans closer.

“Still?” ʸ

“Very much.” ᴹ

She takes the sip he offers and makes a thoughtful face before returning the glass.

“I like mine better.” ʸ

“Then this is an excellent arrangement.” ᴹ

She holds her own drink up for him to try. It is colder and sweeter than he expects. She watches him recognize the difference and looks pleased when he takes another little taste before giving it back.

“Now you know why,” she says. ʸ

He puts his hand over hers for a moment around the glass.

“I had a theory.” ᴹ

* * *

They have chosen a table near the end of the terrace, after dinner, while the younger ones are occupied with dessert and an argument about a game further along.

Nobody has been required to drink. Frieren has something warm without alcohol. Yuta has chosen the same, though he spends a while defending the choice when Gojo suggests that the evening might be an opportunity to expand his education.

“I've learned enough from you,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

Maki turns her face away too late to hide the smile.

Shoko takes her glass from the tray and looks at Gojo.

“That's a good answer.” ˢʰ

“He's become very disrespectful.” ᴳ

“You worked hard on him.” ˢʰ

He considers accepting this as praise and decides to do so.

Anko sits down beside Hinata with a drink she selected because she liked the description. Hinata asks if she has tried it before. Anko says that is part of the point. Luminous makes an observation about responsible experimentation that is lost when Shion asks whether anyone intends to finish the dish at the other end of the table.

“I am,” Milim says immediately. ᴹⁱ

“Then finish it.” ˢʰⁱ

Milim takes the plate, delighted that the argument has produced such a useful outcome.

* * *

The first glass makes Anko talkative.

It takes Mau a little while to identify the change because she is perfectly coherent. She simply has more to say about every part of the walk than she did over coffee. Hinata listens with an expression that gets softer the more Anko tries to explain why the stairs should have been mentioned differently on the map.

“I drew the face,” Anko says, looking at Mau. ᴬⁿ

“I appreciated it.” ᴹ

“After you went up there.” ᴬⁿ

Yuka puts her head against his shoulder and laughs. Mau looks at her with the dignified calm of a man who has been betrayed by a napkin.

“We gained experience,” he says. ᴹ

“I told you not to improve the route.” ᴬⁿ

“He didn't,” Yuka says. “He believed in it.” ʸ

Hinata laughs before she can help it. Anko looks at her, loses the rest of her accusation and begins smiling too.

Mau takes another sip of his carajillo. Yuka reaches for his hand under the table and settles it on her knee.

* * *

Gojo's first glass has a similar effect, with a different subject.

He has decided that Yuta's reluctance to try something new requires an account of all the times Yuta was once reluctant to do other things. Yuta objects to the entire category. Maki asks which particular story he intends to tell, and Gojo becomes distracted by the fact that she knows the first one already.

Shoko listens for a while, then puts her hand on his forearm.

“Let him eat.” ˢʰ

“He can listen while eating.” ᴳ

“So can you.” ˢʰ

He opens his mouth, recognizes the trap and takes the food she puts in his hand.

Mau has to put his glass down before laughing. Yuka presses her fingers into his knee.

“That's familiar,” she says. ʸ

“I follow instructions beautifully.” ᴹ

“Sometimes.” ʸ

“The important ones.” ᴹ

She lifts his hand and kisses the damaged knuckles, quick and affectionate, then leaves it between both of hers while the others continue talking.

* * *

DIO is describing the glass workshop to Noise.

She has chosen to go inside again for a while rather than trying to make the range of her manifested body fit every chair arrangement. Elmesia knows where she has gone. They finished their discussion before DIO stepped back, leaving the wrapped vessel with Yuka for safekeeping.

Mau hears only what they choose to include him in. The rest remains theirs.

*You should see it,* DIO tells Noise when she opens the conversation to him too. *We could go back.* ᴰ

*With you?* ᴺ

*Of course.* ᴰ

*Yes.* ᴺ

She starts explaining which part Elmesia particularly liked. Noise listens. Ciel asks one precise question about the finish, which makes DIO begin again with much more attention to the details.

Mau looks across the table at Elmesia. She catches the glance and raises one eyebrow.

“She's making plans for the workshop,” he tells her. ᴹ

“Good. Tell her they can keep the piece until we go.” ᴱˡ

He relays it. DIO's pleased answer comes back so quickly that his smile reaches Elmesia before the words do.

* * *

Makima has a chair at the corner of the table.

She has asked what is in the drinks and selected something she can keep tasting slowly. It remains nearly full while she listens to Anko describe a second part of the walk that Hinata remembers differently.

There are several ways Makima could guide the conversation. She recognizes them as they arrive: a question that would expose a preference, a comparison that would make someone defend a choice, a silence long enough to invite an explanation.

Mau laughs at something Yuka says, and Makima looks toward him before deciding whether to use any of them.

He catches her gaze.

“Did you like it?” he asks, indicating her glass. ᴹ

She looks down as if remembering it is there.

“It's sweeter than I expected.” ᴹᵏ

“You can try something else.” ᴹ

“I haven't decided whether that's bad.” ᴹᵏ

Yuka looks over.

“Give it a little longer,” she says. ʸ

Makima takes another small sip. This time she attends to what it tastes like rather than what somebody might infer from her choice.

* * *

The evening lengthens without an announcement.

Rimuru and Shizue move over from the other table after making sure the students know where they are going. Shizue sits beside Shoko, and the conversation acquires another person who can tell when Gojo is improving a story.

Anko's hand rests on Hinata's arm. She has stopped pretending it is there by accident. Hinata covers it with her own while answering a question from Luminous, and Anko falls quiet for the first time in several minutes.

Mau notices. She notices him noticing and points a warning finger at him with her free hand.

He raises his glass in a promise to behave.

Yuka sees the whole exchange and leans close to his ear.

“You like seeing her happy.” ʸ

“I do.” ᴹ

She stays close enough that the next thing she says is for him alone.

“Me too.” ʸ

He turns toward her. For a few seconds the conversation keeps going without them, and they let it.

* * *

They walk home rather than letting Yuka put her argument for carrying to the table.

She has her arm around his waist. He has hers around his shoulders. Neither appears interested in reaching the room quickly, and twice they stop so she can point out a patch of light on the water that he looks at through the small gap in the cloth.

“You liked your drink,” she says. ʸ

“I did.” ᴹ

“We can have another evening like that.” ʸ

“I'd like to.” ᴹ

She looks up at him, smiling, then squeezes his waist and keeps walking.

At their door she gives him the key. He opens it, waits while she gets the wrapped glass vessel safely onto the table and reaches for her as she comes back.

Yuka rests both hands on his shoulders.

“Bed,” she says. ʸ

“I thought we were walking tonight.” ᴹ

“We did. I'm very proud of us.” ʸ

He laughs, bends and carries her the rest of the way.
