*When: B+385, afternoon recollection and the following quiet night.*

“I thought we could try again.” ʸ

Mau is halfway through folding the shirt she asked him to leave out. He looks up at her, then puts it on the chair before answering.

“For a baby?” ᴹ

Yuka nods. She is standing beside the open balcony door with her hands at her sides, looking as if she has come to say something simple and discovered that the words have more weight once they are out.

He stays seated on the bed, leaving her the space between them.

“When were you thinking?” ᴹ

“I don't know. Soon. I thought…” ʸ

She looks toward the balcony, then back at him.

“I thought I was ready to say it.” ʸ

He holds out one hand. She comes to him and takes it, but does not sit down yet.

“You can say it,” he tells her. ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

---

She had spent part of the afternoon with Mai.

They were choosing something for the little sitting area, an ordinary decision made difficult because Momo had found a third possibility after Yuka had finally compared the first two. Mai had put both down, told them to eat first and taken them somewhere they could sit with their purchases at their feet.

Yuka told her about the beach with Ori. About Frieren. About the title Ori tried and promptly rejected.

Mai laughed at the part about the steps, then grew quieter while Yuka tried to explain the rest.

“It was good,” Yuka said. ʸ

“It sounds good.” ᴹᵃ

“It was.” ʸ

She had been turning the spoon around between her fingers. Mai let her do it until she put it down herself.

“I keep thinking I want everything,” Yuka said. ʸ

Mai looked at her.

“Which part?” ᴹᵃ

“All of it. More days like that. Going home. Going away again. A family. I know we have one. I mean…” ʸ

“I know what you mean.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka stopped trying to make the sentence perfect. She looked down at the little spoon, then at the shade moving across the table.

“I'm happy,” she said. “And sometimes I remember what I thought we were going to have, and it still hurts.” ʸ

Mai nodded slowly.

“Have you told him that part?” ᴹᵃ

“Some of it.” ʸ

“Then tell him the rest when you want to.” ᴹᵃ

She said it simply, without giving Yuka a better version of what she should feel. They finished eating. Momo returned from the counter with the drink she forgot to order, and the conversation went somewhere else until Yuka was ready to pick up the purchases again.

---

Now Mau's hand is warm around hers.

She wants to put the afternoon into words for him. She wants to say that she has spent days enjoying herself so thoroughly that she forgot to examine the hurt, and then discovered it was still there when she looked.

Instead she moves between his knees and puts her arms around his neck.

He receives her without pulling her down before she has chosen where to settle. She rests her cheek against his hair, standing close enough that she can feel him breathe.

“I keep picturing it,” she says. ʸ

“Tell me.” ᴹ

“A little person with your hair. Or mine. And everyone having opinions. Maki pretending she doesn't, and then having the most.” ʸ

He laughs softly against her shoulder.

“She will.” ᴹ

“Yuta too.” ʸ

“Quieter opinions.” ᴹ

She smiles for a moment. Then it fades, and she lets it fade where he can feel it.

“I pictured it before,” she says. “When I was late. I didn't know I was already doing that until I had to stop.” ʸ

His arms close around her back. He takes a breath, lets it out and waits.

“And today?” he asks. ᴹ

“Today I thought maybe I could picture it without…” ʸ

She stops. He lifts his face enough to look at her.

She brings both hands to his cheeks, looking at the familiar mark by his eye and the shorter strands of white near his temple. Her thumb moves once across the edge of the old injury and stops.

“I still want it with you.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

She kisses him. It is a long, quiet kiss, one she has chosen without needing to explain the rest of the night. When she moves back, he keeps a hand against her waist.

“I thought if I said I wanted to try, I'd know what to do with the rest of it,” she says. ʸ

Mau looks at her for a while.

“What do you want right now?” ᴹ

She knows that answer.

“You.” ʸ

His hand moves slowly along her back.

“I'm here.” ᴹ

She nods, then presses her forehead to his.

“And I want a little more time.” ʸ

---

He draws her down into his lap when she starts bending her knees.

She settles facing him, her arms still around his neck. The room is quiet enough that she can hear the sea when neither of them speaks. She looks at the open door and thinks of all the good things that happened today without fixing the thing she wanted fixed.

“I thought today I'd be able to,” she says. ʸ

“You don't have to turn a good day into a test.” ᴹ

She looks back at him.

“I wasn't trying to.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

He rubs his thumb lightly over the side of her waist. She can feel the patience of the gesture without having to guess whether he has something more to ask.

“I'm not ready yet,” she says at last. ʸ

He brings her closer and puts his face against her shoulder.

“Then we aren't ready yet.” ᴹ

She stays very still for a moment.

Then her arms tighten around him.

“You can still want it,” she says after a while. ʸ

He lifts his head enough to see her face.

“I do.” ᴹ

She nods. The answer hurts a little and is better than having him hide it from her.

“I wanted to tell everybody,” he admits. “When we thought it might be happening. I kept thinking of things I was going to have to learn.” ᴹ

“You already know a lot.” ʸ

“About other things.” ᴹ

She smiles faintly.

“You'd ask Shoko.” ʸ

“And Maki. And the people who have actually held a baby when it doesn't want to sleep. I'd probably ask all the wrong questions.” ᴹ

“I'd be asking with you.” ʸ

He nods.

“That's the part I want. With you. When we get there.” ᴹ

She studies his face, then moves her hand from his cheek to the back of his neck.

“I want that too.” ʸ

“Then we can keep wanting it. We don't have to make tonight do everything.” ᴹ

She puts her face against him again. This time she does not try to explain the pause before her next breath.

---

The tears come slowly enough that she almost thinks they won't.

She feels the first one against her own cheek, wipes it away with the side of her hand and discovers that the next is already there. Mau lifts his head. She shakes hers before he can reach for the cloth on the table.

“Just stay.” ʸ

He stays. She puts her face against his neck and lets herself be held without making the feeling smaller for him. His hand rests between her shoulder blades. The other remains around her waist, keeping her balanced while she shifts closer.

After a while she can breathe without catching halfway through.

“I really was happy today,” she says. ʸ

“So was I.” ᴹ

“I don't want you to think all of this was…” ʸ

She tries to find the end of the sentence. He waits until she gives up on it and looks at him.

“I was with you,” he says. “I remember the day.” ᴹ

She looks at his face, then laughs once through the last of the tears.

“Even the part where I changed my mind?” ʸ

“Several times. About several things.” ᴹ

“You were very patient.” ʸ

“I had food.” ᴹ

That gets a real laugh out of her. She hits his shoulder lightly, careful of the place that pulls, and puts her arms back around him.

---

They go to bed with the balcony door still open a little.

Mau sets the things she wants within reach, puts the rest away and lies down beside her. She turns toward him immediately, slipping one leg between his and drawing his hand to the place on her back where she wants it.

He lets her settle before moving any closer.

“Tomorrow,” she says, “I'd like to do something easy.” ʸ

“We can.” ᴹ

“And I don't want anyone making a plan about this.” ʸ

“We haven't told anyone to.” ᴹ

She nods. He can feel it against his collar.

“I might tell Mai what I said.” ʸ

“You can.” ᴹ

She turns her face up enough to look at him.

“You're very easy to talk to when you stop trying to solve the whole world before breakfast.” ʸ

He smiles at her.

“I'll put that off.” ᴹ

“Good.” ʸ

She kisses him once more, then puts her head down and lets his arm be the thing she falls asleep holding.
