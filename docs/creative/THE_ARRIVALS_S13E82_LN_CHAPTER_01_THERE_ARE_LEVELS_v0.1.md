# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 20 — There Are Levels

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S13E82
**Continuity state:** Elmesia knowingly inspects previously consented new Ten Shadows transport and speaks with builders/Noise via relay. No surprised copying accusation, free deific upgrade or new mass capacity. Yuka dragon request, Ori levels joke, Mau escapes behind Mai and both chase briefly; Kaisel existing familiar bond, no transfer of shadow ownership. Lantern remains Ori chosen guardian. DIO/Elmesia common interest grows.

*When: B+386, morning inspection and family walk to lunch.*

Elmesia comes to inspect the transport on a morning when the wind is mild.

Mau has arranged for the broad landing to stay clear. He asks the shikigami to emerge far enough from the buildings that its folded wings will not shade every window. It settles under his direction, the platform still secured where they left it after arrival.

Elmesia looks up, then walks toward the boarding step with Mai, Senku and Byakuya.

“You gave the front room to move,” she says. ᴱˡ

Mai nods.

“The first one didn't.” ᴹᵃ

“What happened?” ᴱˡ

“It told us.” ᴹᵃ

She shows her the marked joint, explaining the failed test with enough detail to make Byakuya contribute the angle and Senku question whether they need the entire history before breakfast.

“I find the history useful,” Elmesia says. ᴱˡ

Mai gives Senku a look of triumph.

Mau stands at the bottom of the steps with Yuka. She has put one hand beneath his elbow, which he suspects is partly affection and partly a way of knowing if he intends to join the discussion without eating first.

“She likes it,” Yuka says. ʸ

“Noise will be insufferable.” ᴹ

*Already good,* Noise tells him. ᴺ

Mau laughs. Yuka has heard the reply through the channel they opened for showing the design. She puts her forehead against his arm for a moment before looking back up at the platform.

---

Ori comes with Lantern after Frieren finishes breakfast.

She walks beside the dragon, one hand held up where she can touch him when the path is wide enough. Chloe joins her at the last turn. Together they come to the edge of the landing and wait for Mau to tell them where they may stand.

Yuka spots them first.

“Your competitors have arrived.” ʸ

Mau turns toward the path.

“He's not competing with anyone.” ᴹ

Ori hears the last part and looks up at Lantern.

“He could.” ᴼ

The dragon lowers his head until she can put a hand against it. She does so with considerable pride.

Elmesia comes down from the platform and greets them. Ori shows her the shell tied on the string, explains that Chloe has the other one and immediately begins asking whether the large dragon would be able to stand in the water.

“We haven't tried,” Mau says. ᴹ

“You should try.” ᴼ

“Another day.” ᴹ

She accepts this as a future item of business and turns to Lantern to tell him what they have decided.

---

Yuka watches the exchange with one eyebrow raised.

Mau recognizes the expression a moment too late.

“When do I get a dragon?” she asks. ʸ

He looks at her.

Ori turns around.

“There are levels,” she says. ᴼ

Yuka looks down at her.

For a second Mau hopes one of them will laugh. Instead they both turn their faces toward him at exactly the same time.

Mai is standing just beyond the steps with Elmesia's notes in her hand.

Mau moves before either his wife or his daughter can put the next question into words.

He gets behind Mai with an efficiency he has spent years acquiring for considerably less important situations.

Mai looks over her shoulder.

“Absolutely not.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka and Ori are already coming toward them.

“You were part of the hat decision,” Mau tells her. ᴹ

“That was a gift.” ᴹᵃ

“You know how these negotiations go.” ᴹ

“Yes. You're losing.” ᴹᵃ

Ori goes around one side. Yuka goes around the other. Mai raises both hands and steps out, leaving Mau with nowhere respectable to stand.

Elmesia starts laughing first.

---

The chase is very short.

Mau gets as far as the low wall before Yuka catches his shirt. Ori arrives at his other side, out of breath from laughing. He lets them turn him around and rests both hands on the top of the wall while the charges are read.

“You have several,” Yuka says. ʸ

“They have occupations.” ᴹ

“So do I.” ʸ

“I respect your work.” ᴹ

“And I want a dragon.” ʸ

Ori looks between them, then lifts a hand.

“Lantern's mine.” ᴼ

“Nobody's taking Lantern,” Mau says at once. ᴹ

She relaxes, satisfied that the central issue has been addressed.

Yuka folds her arms.

“Kaisel likes me.” ʸ

“He does.” ᴹ

“Then I'm going to ask him.” ʸ

Mau tilts his head.

“You can.” ᴹ

“When we want to go somewhere. Just us. You can be very politely invited.” ʸ

Ori considers the new arrangement, then nods as if Yuka has proposed a perfectly valid level of dragon association.

Mau looks from one to the other.

“May I attend the first meeting?” ᴹ

Yuka smiles.

“If you behave.” ʸ

She takes his hand, and Ori takes the other. They escort him back to the steps while Elmesia tells Mai that the passenger platform appears to have much less demanding politics.

---

They go to see Kaisel after the transport has been returned to the technique.

Mau chooses a clear place, asks the dragon out and lets Yuka approach first. She puts her palm against his cheek and tells him what she has decided. Kaisel lowers his head a little further, making it easy for her to scratch the place near his jaw.

Mau watches the betrayal with great restraint.

“He said yes,” Yuka announces. ʸ

“He likes having his jaw scratched.” ᴹ

“We have common interests.” ʸ

Ori pats Lantern as if reassuring him that their arrangement remains superior. Frieren stands beside her, looking amused enough that Mau suspects she has been enjoying this for longer than he realized.

“You could have helped,” he tells her. ᴹ

“They were making progress.” ᶠ

Yuka reaches back for his hand without taking hers off Kaisel. He steps closer until she can put his palm beside hers on the dragon's neck.

“We'll go together,” she says more quietly. ʸ

He looks down at her.

“I'd like that.” ᴹ

She leans into him, and Kaisel settles his head to a height where all three can remain comfortable.

---

Later, when the others have begun discussing where to eat, Elmesia asks Mau to stay for a moment.

Yuka remains beside him. DIO comes out near the landing, her hat in both hands, and Elmesia waits until she has found a place in the shade before continuing.

“You asked if I wanted to see what Noise made,” Elmesia says. ᴱˡ

Mau nods.

“I'm glad you did.” ᴱˡ

“I promised you.” ᴹ

“You did. You also told me before you brought it. That matters.” ᴱˡ

He thinks of the first conversation beside her own dragon, the permission to observe it and the long work afterward. Noise is listening through the channel he agreed to share for this purpose.

*Liked it?* Noise asks. ᴺ

“He wants to know if you liked it,” Mau says. ᴹ

Elmesia looks directly at him, making the answer easy to pass along.

“Very much. And I would like to hear what he found most difficult.” ᴱˡ

Noise offers a shape, then a short explanation. Mau translates the part Elmesia cannot receive directly. It is about the movement where the wings meet the body, and about finding a way for the technique to hold a creature this large without making the rest of it pretend to be the original.

Elmesia asks a careful question. Noise takes a little time before answering.

DIO moves closer to see the drawing Mai has left them. Elmesia turns it toward her and lets her keep one hand on the lower edge while they compare it with what they just saw.

Yuka slips her fingers between Mau's and watches him listen. He is happy in a way she recognizes from the workshop: absorbed, pleased by a problem somebody else understands, willing to let the answer take time.

When they finally leave for lunch, Elmesia takes the drawing and DIO takes the little wrapped vessel she brought to compare with a planter nearby. They are still talking as they go.

Yuka walks close to Mau and leans up to speak near his ear.

“We should let them choose the next place.” ʸ

He looks after them.

“I think they already have.” ᴹ
