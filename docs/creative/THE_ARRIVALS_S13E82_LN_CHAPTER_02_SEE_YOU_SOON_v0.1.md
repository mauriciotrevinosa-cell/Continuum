# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 21 — See You Soon

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S13E82
**Continuity state:** Luminous/Hinata leave for bounded urgent border dispute, not new island attack; Anko independently chooses to accompany partner with practical help. Existing consented anchors and recovery interval, confirmed safe arrival. Witches future tea and Anko/Mau plant friendship maintained. First natural staggered return, everyone had initially traveled together. Household adapts seating and continues holiday.

*When: B+386, afternoon emergency departure and dinner.*

Luminous reads the message twice.

The first time she is still standing beneath the awning with the cup she brought back from lunch. The second time she puts it down. Hinata, who has been talking to Anko by the path, sees the change and comes over before she is called.

Mau and Yuka are returning from the landing. He slows when he notices them, then lets Luminous decide whether she wants him to approach.

She folds the message.

“We have to go back.” ᴸᵘ

Hinata takes the page she offers. Anko stays a little behind her, close enough to read her face.

“Now?” Hinata asks. ᴴⁱ

“As soon as we confirm the return.” ᴸᵘ

Mau comes closer.

“I can help with that.” ᴹ

“I'd appreciate it.” ᴸᵘ

Yuka looks toward Anko, whose attention has remained on Hinata rather than the page. The holiday keeps making its ordinary sounds around them: somebody calling from the pool, a cart moving down the path, a little burst of laughter from the shaded terrace.

Hinata finishes reading and hands the message back.

“I'll get my things.” ᴴⁱ

Anko catches her hand before she turns.

“I'm coming with you.” ᴬⁿ

---

They discuss it in Hinata's room while Mau confirms the existing return route.

Yuka stays outside with him, holding the small bag Anko left on the chair. The door remains open. They can hear the practical part of the conversation without trying: whether Anko has everything she needs, how much of the journey will involve waiting, whether anyone there knows she is coming.

“I can tell them,” Hinata says. ᴴⁱ

“Then tell them.” ᴬⁿ

A pause.

“You can stay here. I know you wanted more time.” ᴴⁱ

“I do want more time.” ᴬⁿ

The next part is quieter. Mau looks away toward the path, leaving them the rest of it.

Yuka adjusts the strap of Anko's bag and rests it over her own arm until someone needs it.

---

Luminous explains enough of the emergency for them to understand the urgency.

Two conflicting accounts from a border dispute have reached people who are prepared to act on the one they prefer. Hinata needs to establish what happened before an accusation becomes a confrontation. Luminous needs to be there when the interested parties discover that they will have to answer questions in person.

“If I let them wait, they'll spend the time making themselves certain,” she says. ᴸᵘ

Mau nods.

“Do you need anything else?” ᴹ

“A holiday after the holiday.” ᴸᵘ

He smiles a little.

“You know who owns the place.” ᴹ

“I intend to remind her.” ᴸᵘ

Elmesia arrives while they are checking the final notice. She has heard enough from the attendant who brought the message to ask the right practical question. Luminous answers it, then thanks her for the days she did have.

“We can continue the conversation,” Elmesia says. ᴱˡ

“I'd like that.” ᴸᵘ

They arrange where the next reply should go. Nobody calls the visit wasted.

---

The witches come down to say goodbye.

It takes Sekhmet longer than the others, and Luminous waits for her without remarking on it. Daphne has brought a packet she wants Luminous to try at home. Echidna offers a question she can take with her, which Minerva informs her is a terrible farewell present.

“It can wait,” Luminous says. ᴸᵘ

“That's why I'm asking now.” ᴱᶜ

Luminous looks at her. Echidna smiles and lets the question remain unspoken for once.

Satella thanks her for coming. Pride asks whether she will be at the next tea. Luminous tells her that somebody had better write to her before they begin choosing things she is supposed to bring.

“He'll write,” Pride says, pointing at Mau. ᴾʳ

“Apparently,” he answers. ᴹ

Yuka puts a hand at his back, amused.

---

Anko comes out carrying considerably less than she arrived with.

The rest is labelled for Mau to send home or give back when she returns. She checks the packet with the little cutting he stored for her and makes him promise he knows which one it is.

“I do.” ᴹ

“It has a label.” ᴬⁿ

“Several people have improved my system.” ᴹ

“Good. Keep it that way.” ᴬⁿ

He puts the bag where she can see it disappear. She watches the empty space for a second, then looks up at him.

“I'll see you soon.” ᴬⁿ

“You will.” ᴹ

“And you'll tell me if you move the plant.” ᴬⁿ

“You can inspect it when you get home.” ᴹ

She smiles. He opens his arms when she steps closer, and she hugs him with one hand still holding the folded map. He waits until she lets go.

Yuka hugs her next. Anko says something into her ear that makes Yuka laugh and glance at Mau. Neither offers him the explanation.

Hinata waits beside the cleared place in the path.

Anko goes to her, takes the bag she was carrying and redistributes it between them before Hinata can object.

---

Mau checks the destination response once more.

DIO and Makima return inside before the journeys home, as they did while he collected the guests. He brings Luminous to the confirmed anchor first. Yuka waits beside Hinata and Anko while the exchanged soldier holds the island end of the route. After the interval Mau comes back, confirms the landing was clear and takes the other two with him.

Anko is still holding Hinata's hand when they disappear. The soldier returns in their place, inclines its head toward Yuka and waits for Mau's next exchange.

He returns alone once the route is ready again. The time between journeys has been spent with people thanking him and telling him which message to give the others. He gets the names straight before giving Yuka the first one.

Yuka stands beside him with the empty label sheet.

“They're there?” ʸ

“They're there.” ᴹ

She folds the sheet and puts it away.

---

The table feels different at dinner.

Someone has left a little more room between the chairs, and the gap makes the whole arrangement unfamiliar until Rem moves over to hear what Seika is saying. Then the others adjust around her, and the table acquires a new shape.

Mau sits beside Yuka with Ori leaning across him to explain something she didn't finish telling him earlier. Frieren pulls the girl's plate closer so she won't forget it while talking. Shuna asks how much food they should keep for anyone still out, and Jinshi checks the plans people actually gave him rather than guessing that everyone will return.

“Anko took the map,” Yuka tells Mau. ʸ

He looks at her.

“Our map?” ᴹ

“She said we'd learned the important part.” ʸ

He begins laughing, and she leans into him while Ori waits with a patient expression that suggests the adults have wandered off her subject again.

“Sorry,” Mau tells her. “Go on.” ᴹ

Ori does. Beyond the table, the water keeps moving against the steps. Somebody at the other end asks for a dish to be passed, and a hand reaches across the place where Anko was sitting yesterday.
