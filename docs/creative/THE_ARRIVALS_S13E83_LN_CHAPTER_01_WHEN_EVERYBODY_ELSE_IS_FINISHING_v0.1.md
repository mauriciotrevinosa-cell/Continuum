# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 21 — When Everybody Else Is Finishing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 21
**Source episode:** S13E83
**Continuity state:** Nazuna chooses return because daylight confinement limits own holiday, still genuinely enjoyed nights. Ko chooses accompany; Anko absence acknowledged without erasing their bond. Existing consented home route and known acknowledgment, no new vampire cure or power claim. Mau/Yuka share night walk and friendship, no separation. All characters who stay continue holiday.

*When: B+386 late night; brief return scene B+387 evening.*

Nazuna finds them after most of the terrace has emptied.

Mau is sitting on the low wall while Yuka puts her sandals back on. She has decided that she wants to walk along the pier before bed. He has decided that the part where she makes this announcement with both feet in his lap is one of the advantages of being married.

“You're still awake,” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃᶻ

Yuka looks up.

“So are you.” ʸ

“I'm starting.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Ko comes up beside her with his hands in his pockets, looking out toward the water. Nazuna looks at the empty tables and then at the windows where lights are going out.

“Everybody's finishing.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Mau fastens Yuka's last buckle, puts her foot down and stands.

“Walk with us?” he asks. ᴹ

Nazuna looks at the pier.

“Where?” ᴺᵃᶻ

“We haven't decided.” ʸ

She appears to consider this the first sensible plan anyone has offered all day.

* * *

The pier is mostly quiet.

They pass a crew securing a boat and a man carrying a coil of rope toward the far shed. Yuka stops to see whether the boat from their trip is there. Ko finds it first, pointing out the little awning that has been folded for the night.

“That's the one,” she says. ʸ

“Lunch?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“Very good lunch.” ʸ

Nazuna leans over the rail.

“What happens out here in the day?” ᴺᵃᶻ

Yuka begins answering. Nazuna listens, asks one question about the market and makes a dissatisfied sound at the description of the open square.

“No shade?” ᴺᵃᶻ

“Some. Not all the way.” ʸ

“Of course.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Ko turns toward her.

“We could go later.” ᴷᵒ

“And find the nice things being put away.” ᴺᵃᶻ

She says it lightly, but Mau hears the irritation underneath. He rests his forearms on the rail beside her, giving her the space to decide whether she wants to say more.

Yuka stays at his side. Her hand slips into the back of his shirt, settling there while she looks down at the water.

* * *

Nazuna tells them what she has liked.

The night air. The view from the path when nobody is hurrying along it. A part of the beach where she and Ko sat long enough for the people in the nearest building to turn out every light. The way the water looks when it reaches the line below the pier.

She has liked those things. Mau can hear that too.

“But?” he asks. ᴹ

She tilts her head toward the sleeping rooms.

“I'm tired of that part.” ᴺᵃᶻ

“The hotel?” ʸ

“Waiting in it. Everyone goes somewhere. I come out and hear about it. Then they start yawning when I'm ready to do something.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Ko leans against the rail beside her.

“We did things.” ᴷᵒ

“We did. I'm keeping you.” ᴺᵃᶻ

He loses the answer he was about to give. Yuka puts her face against Mau's arm for a moment, smiling.

Nazuna looks back out at the water.

“I think I want to go home.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Mau waits long enough to hear that she means it.

“When?” ᴹ

“Tomorrow. Before I start complaining about curtains to somebody who can't do anything about the sun.” ᴺᵃᶻ

“You can tell Elmesia,” Yuka says. ʸ

Nazuna looks at her.

“She might take it personally and build something.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Mau starts laughing. After a moment Ko does too.

* * *

They walk beyond the pier to the little stretch of sand open at night.

There are benches under the trees and enough light from the path to find somewhere to sit. Nazuna takes the place with the clearest view. Ko sits on the wall in front of her until she nudges his leg and points to the space beside her.

Mau sits with Yuka at the other end of the bench. She draws his arm over her shoulders and makes herself comfortable against him.

For a while they talk about the people who are away and the things that will be different at home. Anko's departure comes up naturally. Nazuna complains that she has taken somebody who would have understood how annoying the sleeping arrangements have become.

“She went where she wanted,” Ko says. ᴷᵒ

“I know. That's annoying too.” ᴺᵃᶻ

She is smiling when she says it. Yuka recognizes the shape of the complaint and leaves it alone.

Mau asks Ko whether he intends to return with her. Ko looks at Nazuna, who has suddenly become very interested in the end of her own sleeve.

“Yes,” he says. ᴷᵒ

“Then we'll arrange it together.” ᴹ

Nazuna's foot bumps lightly against Ko's ankle. He leaves his where it is.

* * *

Yuka begins yawning after they have found their way back from the end of the beach.

She tries to conceal the first one. Nazuna catches the second and points at her as though this proves the entire argument.

“See?” ᴺᵃᶻ

Yuka laughs, then gives up hiding the third.

“I'm sorry.” ʸ

“Don't apologize. It makes it worse.” ᴺᵃᶻ

Mau looks down at his wife. She has leaned more of her weight against him, her cheek close to his shoulder.

“We can walk back slowly,” he says. ᴹ

“I'll survive.” ʸ

“I didn't doubt it.” ᴹ

She squeezes his waist and looks at Nazuna.

“I'm glad you came with us.” ʸ

Nazuna looks away toward the pier for a moment.

“It was good,” she says. ᴺᵃᶻ

She lets the sentence remain as it is. Ko gets up beside her, and they walk the first part of the path together before dividing at the rooms.

* * *

They leave the following evening, after Nazuna has slept through one last sunny day.

Mau checks the home acknowledgment, gathers the labelled things they want sent and waits while Nazuna remembers an item she put in the wrong pocket. Yuka is there, with Rem and Umaru coming down from the terrace to say goodbye.

Umaru gives Nazuna instructions about something at home. Rem listens, corrects one detail and asks her to leave the rest for when they return.

“I'll tell you if anybody takes it,” Nazuna says. ᴺᵃᶻ

“That's the important part.” ᵁ

Ko thanks them for the trip. Yuka tells him to come tell her about the next night walk he finds at home. He nods, a little surprised by how directly she asks.

Nazuna looks at the path, the hotel and the little opening between the buildings where the sea is still visible.

“Enjoy the curtains,” she tells Mau. ᴺᵃᶻ

“We'll try.” ᴹ

She goes through with Ko after the route is confirmed.

When the acknowledgment comes back, Mau turns toward Yuka. She holds out a hand and points toward the water with the other.

“One more walk?” ʸ

He takes her hand.

“While we're still awake.” ᴹ
