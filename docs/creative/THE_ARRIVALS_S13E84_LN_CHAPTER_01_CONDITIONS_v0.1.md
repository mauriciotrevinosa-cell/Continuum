# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 23 — Conditions

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 23
**Source episode:** S13E84
**Continuity state:** Fort household cards with couple, Rem/Umaru, Ram/Seika and Kessoku; relationships active, no characters newly invented as residents. Mau genuinely plays/wins/loses, no technique-based cheating or forced self-denial. Plant friendship practical continuation. Shuna chooses involvement, not reduced to duty. No compulsory daily all-house gathering.

*When: B+388, late morning and lunch.*

Umaru has discovered a serious defect in the holiday.

Mau learns this when she puts a pack of cards on the table in front of him and waits until he has stopped talking to Yuka.

“You haven't played with us today,” she says. ᵁ

He looks at the cards.

“It's not lunchtime.” ᴹ

“Exactly. You're falling behind.” ᵁ

Yuka leans back against him, her shoulder under his chin. He has been sitting with her by the open doors of the common terrace, halfway through a very good plan to do nothing until somebody needs food.

He looks at her.

“Am I falling behind?” ᴹ

“Terribly,” she says. ʸ

“You're supposed to be enjoying this with me.” ᴹ

She puts one hand over his wrist and raises the other for Umaru to deal her in.

“I am.” ʸ

* * *

The table acquires players in stages.

Rem comes first, carrying a little bowl she sets where Umaru can reach it without leaning across the cards. Seika sits down when she hears what they are playing. Ram follows her, moves the nearest chair an inch and occupies it as though the position was settled in advance.

Nijika brings Bocchi, who asks whether it matters if she isn't good at the game. Umaru assures her that they are about to provide instruction. Ryo asks whether the instruction includes prizes.

“It includes losing honestly,” Seika says. ˢᵉⁱ

Ryo looks thoughtful.

Mau puts Yuka's feet down gently so she can turn her chair toward the table. She keeps one ankle hooked behind his while arranging her cards.

“No helping each other,” Umaru announces. ᵁ

Yuka looks at Mau.

“We haven't started.” ʸ

“I'm establishing the conditions.” ᵁ

Rem touches Umaru's wrist, and Umaru forgets her next condition long enough for Seika to start dealing.

* * *

Mau loses the first round because he trusts Ryo's expression.

The expression has clearly taken years to develop. It contains just enough uncertainty to encourage him and just enough reluctance to make him feel the encouragement was his own idea.

Ryo lays down the cards.

Seika laughs before he has finished looking at them.

“You believed her?” ˢᵉⁱ

“I respect my family,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Expensive,” Ryo observes. ᴿʸ

Yuka has been trying to keep a straight face. She loses it when he turns toward her.

“Did you know?” he asks. ᴹ

“I suspected.” ʸ

“You could have warned me.” ᴹ

Umaru points at the table.

“Conditions.” ᵁ

He accepts this with considerable dignity and asks Seika to deal again.

* * *

Rem wins the next round so quietly that Umaru has to ask her to show the cards again.

She does. Umaru looks at her, then at the table, then back at her.

“You let me say all of that.” ᵁ

“You were having a good time.” ᴿᵉ

Ram lifts her cup to hide a smile. Seika sees it anyway and touches her knee beneath the table. Ram's expression becomes very composed, which makes Seika smile more.

Mau takes the cards he has been given and waits while Umaru explains that this does not count as a betrayal. Rem agrees with her. The agreement seems to please Umaru until she notices the amusement in Rem's face.

“You can laugh,” Umaru says. ᵁ

Rem laughs.

It is enough to make the others start, and for a little while the round cannot begin because nobody has put all their cards back down.

* * *

They change seats after someone suggests that the game has become unfairly arranged.

Nobody explains how. Umaru moves beside Rem. Ram remains beside Seika, who moves only her cup. Bocchi finds herself between Kita, who has arrived from the path, and Mau, who offers her the little dish that has worked its way across the table.

“You don't have to explain what you're doing,” he tells her quietly. ᴹ

She looks down at her cards.

“I wasn't going to.” ᴮ

He waits.

“I might have,” she adds. ᴮ

“I know.” ᴹ

Kita leans closer to see whether Bocchi wants help understanding the rule. Bocchi shakes her head and keeps the cards near her chest, concentrating.

She wins the round.

For a second she looks so alarmed by the attention that Mau applauds badly on purpose. Yuka tells him to stop being embarrassing. Bocchi laughs, and the moment becomes small enough for her to stay in it.

* * *

During the break, Ram asks Mau about the plant Anko sent home.

He shows her the labelled packet, still protected as instructed, and tells her where he intends to put it. Seika suggests another place near the window in the common room, where people will see it even when Anko is out.

“She'll want to choose,” Ram says. ᴿᵃ

“Then she can move it,” Mau answers. ᴹ

“You make that sound easy.” ˢᵉⁱ

“I live with several people who have strong opinions about windows.” ᴹ

Seika gives Ram a look. Ram ignores the implication, sets her cup down and tells him the practical part of what Anko will need to keep it out of the wrong light.

Yuka listens from the chair she has pulled beside his. Her hand comes to rest on the back of his neck while she looks at the packet. He leans into her fingers for a moment, then turns his attention back to Ram's explanation.

* * *

Mau wins a round after lunch has already become overdue.

He does it without announcement, putting his cards down while Umaru is explaining why she has chosen a particularly clever move. She stops. Rem examines his hand, then her own.

“Oh,” she says. ᴿᵉ

Yuka starts smiling.

Umaru points at the cloth over his eyes.

“Was that assistance?” ᵁ

“That was remembering what you played.” ᴹ

“Suspicious.” ᵁ

“You announced it.” ᴹ

Seika laughs hard enough that Ram has to move the cup from her hand.

Mau takes the little marker they have been using to record wins and puts it in front of him with enormous care. Yuka kisses his cheek, which Umaru immediately declares an unfair reward structure.

“Win your own,” Yuka tells her. ʸ

Umaru looks at Rem. Rem's expression softens before she can pretend she hasn't understood.

Mau looks down at his cards and gives them the moment.

* * *

The call for lunch comes from Shuna, who has found the group and discovered they have been treating snacks as a meal plan.

“You can finish the round,” she says. ˢʰᵘ

Everyone looks at the cleared table.

“We've finished,” Mau tells her. ᴹ

“Then get up.” ˢʰᵘ

They do, with a great deal of discussion about who is actually winning overall. Umaru collects the cards. Rem collects the abandoned napkins. Seika takes the little marker Mau won, shows it to Ram and sets it beside him again before he notices it was gone.

Yuka puts her arms around him from behind while he gets the packets stored.

“You looked very pleased with yourself.” ʸ

“I earned it.” ᴹ

“You did.” ʸ

She lets him turn within the embrace, then keeps hold of his hand as they go to join the others.

At the end of the path, Umaru looks back at them.

“Tomorrow,” she warns. ᵁ

Mau lifts his free hand in acknowledgment.

Yuka leans against his arm.

“You have commitments.” ʸ

“Apparently.” ᴹ

“I'll help you prepare.” ʸ

He looks down at her.

“Is that allowed?” ᴹ

She smiles and draws him toward lunch before Umaru can overhear and add another condition.
