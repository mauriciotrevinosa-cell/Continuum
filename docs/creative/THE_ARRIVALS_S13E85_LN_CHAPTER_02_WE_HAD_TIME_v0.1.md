Rimuru gets through most of the message before looking up at Shizue.

She has been watching him read. He had tried to make the first part look like something he could finish before breakfast. The second part required another page. Ciel supplied the clarification he asked for, and he stopped pretending the cup in front of him would still be warm when he reached the end.

Mau finds him later near the path that leads to the gardens.

Rimuru has finished answering the immediate questions. Rigurd knows he is returning. The visiting delegations have been told to wait for him before treating their preferred arrangement as an agreement. Benimaru has sent a reply that is short enough to suggest he has been anticipating this outcome for days.

“I did,” he admits. ᴿ

“You too?” ʸ

Shizue wants to walk to the little place with the sweets she found on the first evening.

Rimuru asks her after lunch, when she has finished checking what the students want to do. Kenya, Ryota, Gale and Alice will return with her. Chloe has asked for a little longer with Ori, and Frieren has agreed to take responsibility until they return. Shizue has spoken with both girls and knows exactly what they intend to do with the extra days.

The answer is apparently several things, all urgent.

“We can go before dinner,” Shizue tells Rimuru. ˢᶻ

“I thought you might already have plans.” ᴿ

Mau and Yuka spend the afternoon on the quiet side of the pool.

She sits facing him in the shallow water with her hands on his shoulders. He has his back against the edge, supporting her easily while she describes what she wants to do when the group gets smaller. Some mornings with no decisions. Another visit to the market. A day with just whichever people happen to find them.

“Those sound like several decisions,” he says. ᴹ

“I'm making them in advance so I don't have to make them then.” ʸ

He considers the logic. She waits with a pleased little smile, certain he will find it excellent once he gives it time.

“We should stay another week,” he says. ᴹ

Her expression changes.

“Another?” ʸ

“If you want.” ᴹ

She puts both arms around his neck and draws him close enough to rest her cheek against his.

“I want.” ʸ

He keeps his hands at her back while she thinks of what the extra time can hold. They can tell the people at home. They can arrange the rooms with Elmesia. Nothing has to be moved from a fixed page to make space for the decision.

Yuka pulls back enough to see his face.

“You asked,” she says. ʸ

“I did.” ᴹ

“You want to stay too.” ʸ

“Very much.” ᴹ

* * *

They find Rimuru and Shizue after dinner with a little paper parcel between them.

Shizue opens it when Yuka asks whether they found the shop. They have brought enough for the people still sitting nearby, though Rimuru appears to have a strong opinion about which one they should save until last.

Mau accepts a piece and waits to hear how the walk went.

Rimuru begins with the shop. Shizue adds the part about the path. He corrects which of them wanted to go a little farther. She turns toward him, smiling, and asks whether he intends to deny the very good reason he gave at the time.

“What reason?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Rimuru looks at the parcel.

“We had time.” ᴿ

Shizue takes his hand beneath the edge of the table, visible to anyone who happens to look. He turns his palm up to fit hers.

Mau looks at Yuka. She has seen it too.

They take the pieces they are offered and leave the rest of the story with the people who went on the walk.

* * *

The return the following morning takes longer than the actual journey.

There are bags to label, things the students suddenly remember they have promised to bring home and a long explanation from Ori about where Chloe will put the shell so it will stay safe. Frieren takes the instructions seriously. Shizue waits until both girls have finished.

Rimuru thanks Elmesia. She tells him the outstanding conversation will wait until the delegations stop trying to borrow him. He laughs with the resignation of someone who knows they will find other reasons.

“Come back when you can,” she says. ᴱˡ

“We will.” ᴿ
