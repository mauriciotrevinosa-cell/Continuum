# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 30 — More Letters

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 30
**Source episode:** S13E87
**Continuity state:** All seven witches choose home after satisfied holiday; gardening packets and Rimuru reply practical, no instant forest. Invite Elmesia for future house tea; Luminous to receive notice. Satella gift modest shell dish, not Elmesia promised undefined gift. Established scars not healed by Minerva touching collar. Known return anchors/intervals and acknowledgments, no new powers. Couple remain with DIO/Elmesia/Testa/Makima and remaining family.

*When: B+392, breakfast tea and witches return.*

Daphne brings the packets to Mau before she brings the suitcase.

He is sitting with Yuka at breakfast when she appears beside the table with the little cloth bag pressed against her. He moves his cup, clears a place and waits while she begins arranging the contents by the order in which she wants them taken out at home.

“These first,” she says. ᴰᵃ

“You've told Rimuru?” ᴹ

“I wrote.” ᴰᵃ

“What did he say?” ᴹ

She produces the reply. Yuka reads it over Mau's shoulder and begins smiling before he reaches the end.

“He wants to see how much you brought,” she says. ʸ

“He'll see,” Daphne answers. ᴰᵃ

Mau looks at the packets still in the bag.

“He will.” ᴹ

---

The decision to go home has apparently spread over the previous evening.

Echidna heard that Daphne intended to start the garden and asked when. Sekhmet observed that the chairs at home were also comfortable. Minerva said they had gone to the places she most wanted to see. Carmilla had something she wanted to put in her own room before its wrapping became more complicated. Satella was ready when the others asked her.

Pride said she would go if they finished the things they had already ordered for breakfast.

This final condition is being fulfilled at the next table when Mau and Yuka join them.

Elmesia comes with DIO, carrying the folded reply from Rimuru because Daphne left it beside the tea tray after showing everyone. She hands it back, looks at the packets and asks whether they have a way to keep the names attached.

“We do now,” Yuka says, holding up the labels. ʸ

Mau gives her an innocent look. She ignores it and begins reading them to him.

---

They have one last pot of tea in the shade.

The conversation begins with which of the things on the table should go into the luggage and becomes a discussion of the next meeting before anybody can call it a farewell. Luminous will need a letter. Somebody should tell Hinata which part Anko can actually reach without another argument about stairs.

Elmesia listens until Pride points at her.

“You come too.” ᴾʳ

Elmesia lifts her eyebrows.

“To the house?” ᴱˡ

“For tea.” ᴾʳ

“I'd like that.” ᴱˡ

Echidna takes the invitation up properly, asking when Elmesia could manage it and whether she would prefer a day when the market is open. Daphne wants her to see the garden once they have decided where everything belongs. Carmilla says the garden may require a separate visit if they wait for Daphne to stop adding things.

Elmesia laughs.

“Then we'll start with tea.” ᴱˡ

DIO looks at her, pleased before she has quite worked out why. Mau sees the look and returns his attention to the bag he is closing, leaving her the feeling without a question attached to it.

---

Minerva asks Mau to stand still while she fixes the edge of his collar.

He does, surprised by the request until she gets the folded cloth out from beneath the strap of the little bag Yuka gave him to hold. She smooths it once and examines the way the seam lies over his shoulder.

“Better?” she asks. ᴹⁱⁿ

He moves the arm.

“Better.” ᴹ

She nods. Her fingers pause over the place the old scar pulls, but there is no open wound to close, no new hurt for her to take away. She lets the shirt lie where it is and asks whether he is actually eating enough while away from home.

Yuka turns toward them.

“I'm supervising.” ʸ

Mau looks at her.

“You're sampling.” ᴹ

“Closely.” ʸ

Minerva appears satisfied with the general arrangement. She tells Yuka to continue, which Yuka accepts as a formal endorsement.

---

Satella waits until Mau has finished the last label before handing him a small wrapped thing.

He asks where she wants it stored. She shakes her head and points toward Yuka.

“For you both,” she says. ˢᵃ

It is a little dish from one of the market stalls, shallow enough for shells or things they keep putting beside the bed. Mau turns it over in his hands. Yuka touches the edge, then looks up to thank her.

“We kept finding things,” Satella says. “I thought…” ˢᵃ

She leaves the sentence there. Yuka understands well enough to put a hand over hers for a moment before helping Mau wrap the dish again.

“We'll use it,” she promises. ʸ

Mau puts it in a separate place from the garden packets. He makes certain Satella sees where it goes.

---

Echidna walks with him to the return point.

She has a final question about the completed shikigami and has chosen the moment when he cannot reasonably pretend to be busy with something else. He answers the part he can answer without asking Noise to open the entire pattern. She notices the boundary and asks a better question.

Yuka walks beside them with Carmilla. Their discussion concerns a piece of cloth and what it will look like in different light. Mau listens to both conversations until the paths narrow, then lets the practical work of getting people home take his full attention.

The destination has acknowledged the notice. The place is clear. The labelled packets have been set apart so Daphne can take them out with Rimuru present rather than losing them among the rest of the luggage.

Mau tells them how he will bring them home in two groups, with time to make the return journeys between. Sekhmet chooses the second so that she can remain seated while the first says goodbye. Mau lets her keep the chair until the moment he actually needs her on her feet.

Pride makes him promise not to let the next tea begin without the things she liked today.

“I'll write it down,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka lifts the notebook.

“Already done.” ʸ

Pride looks pleased with both of them.

---

Elmesia stays until the last departure.

The thanks are personal and untidy. Daphne remembers a question about the plant seller while she is halfway toward the shadow. Minerva stops to hug DIO. Carmilla asks Yuka to send word if the fabric behaves differently after washing. Echidna says she will continue the other conversation in writing, and Mau pretends to be surprised.

Satella says goodbye quietly. Pride does it at enough volume for several people.

DIO returns inside with Makima before he leaves. Mau brings the first group to the clear home anchor and returns after the interval to find Sekhmet comfortably finishing a biscuit. She has kept her seat until the last possible moment and regards the arrangement as a success. She brushes the crumbs from her fingers before joining the second group.

When Mau is back from the last journey, DIO comes out again beside Elmesia. He has delivered Daphne's warning about the seed packets and left the smaller dish where Satella asked. His next journey will be for the people still finishing the holiday here.

Yuka puts the empty bag over her shoulder.

“Fewer chairs,” she says. ʸ

“More letters.” ᴹ

She smiles and takes his hand while Elmesia begins telling DIO which day she could set aside for the promised tea.

---

They go back by the table the witches have just left.

The cups are being collected. The chairs remain in the irregular positions people found comfortable, including the one Sekhmet chose as soon as she saw it. Yuka pauses, looks at the shade and then toward Mau.

“We could sit here.” ʸ

“We could.” ᴹ

She sets the bag down and takes the chair beside his. He rests an arm along the back of it while she turns toward the water.

They stay long enough for the table to become theirs for a little while, without having to pretend it hasn't just belonged to somebody else.
