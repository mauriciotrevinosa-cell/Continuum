# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 31 — One Thing at a Time

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 31
**Source episode:** S13E87
**Continuity state:** Ciel repairs usable partial Mini Ciel filter after weeks, tested gradually with Mau consent/Yuka chosen channel/Shoko check/Gojo practical advice. Not a complete neurological cure, no increase of mana/CE, no scar/eye/finger/color reset; old blur and grey except Yuka remain. Cloth retained for rest/overload. Control can narrow and release ordinary input, no perfect omniscience. No new overload injury or hidden forbidden build.

*When: B+392, afternoon controlled filter trials and evening beach.*

*I would like to try the repaired filter,* Ciel says. ᶜ

Mau stops with the cloth halfway off his head.

He had intended to retie it more comfortably before going out. Now he stands beside the chair with one hand raised, listening while she explains exactly what she is offering.

Yuka looks up from the little dish Satella gave them.

“Ciel?” she asks. ʸ

He nods.

“Mini Ciel.” ᴹ

Yuka sets the dish down.

“She made it?” ʸ

*She is not a person requiring resuscitation,* Ciel says, with enough dryness to confirm that she heard the chosen relay. ᶜ

Mau puts a hand over his chest.

*We were very worried.* ᴹ

Noise laughs once, delighted by something it has had weeks to anticipate.

*You can both be worried quietly while I explain the test,* Ciel tells them. ᶜ

---

They use the room with the curtains partly drawn.

Mau sits with Yuka beside him. She has agreed to hear the practical channel for the test; their private thoughts remain their own. He lets Ciel place the first simple marker at the edge of his attention, waits until she says the reconstructed filter is holding and removes the cloth.

The room arrives in too many pieces.

For an instant he can distinguish every fold in the curtain and the little changes in the light where the window catches it. His gaze tries to follow all of them. Then the filter gives him the familiar choice of what to bring forward, more deliberate than the old automatic response.

He chooses Yuka's hand on the blanket.

The background settles.

*How is the pressure?* Ciel asks. ᶜ

He takes a breath before answering.

*Present. Manageable.* ᴹ

*Keep the focus narrow.* ᶜ

He does. Yuka turns her palm up on the blanket. He follows the movement, then the crease beside her thumb. She waits until he looks at her face before smiling.

“Enough?” she asks aloud. ʸ

“For now.” ᴹ

She stays still while he chooses the next thing he wants to see.

---

The first trial lasts less than a minute.

He puts the cloth back on when Ciel asks, lets the information fall away and sits with his elbows on his knees until the remaining pressure has settled. Yuka has her hand at the back of his neck, moving one thumb in a slow line below the shorter hair.

“Headache?” she asks. ʸ

“The beginning of one.” ᴹ

“Want to stop?” ʸ

He listens to the practical account Ciel is giving him, compares it with what he actually felt and nods.

“For a bit.” ᴹ

Yuka does not make the answer an event. She gets his water, puts it in his hand and sits back down beside him. He drinks while Ciel and Noise compare the part they are allowed to compare, leaving him the quiet they promised.

---

Shoko arrives because he asks her to look at the result before they try again.

She listens to his description, asks where the discomfort began and has him describe the difference between seeing the room and choosing the smaller part. He answers without making it sound better than it was.

“Did you have to keep it going?” she asks. ˢʰ

“No.” ᴹ

“Did you stop when you were told?” ˢʰ

Yuka makes a small approving noise from the other side of the bed.

Mau looks toward her.

“I did.” ᴹ

“Good,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

She checks the practical things she came to check, then asks whether Ciel intends the next trial to be longer. Mau relays Ciel's answer: only after the shorter one becomes comfortable, with breaks and a way to reduce the input at once.

Shoko nods.

“Then you have a plan. Follow it.” ˢʰ

“I will.” ᴹ

She looks at him for a moment, sees that he means it and reaches up to put the edge of the cloth back where it lies comfortably beside the damaged eye.

“And come outside when you're finished sitting in the dark. You brought me here to make sure I got out.” ˢʰ

He smiles.

“I remember.” ᴹ

---

The second trial is simpler because he knows what to expect.

Yuka has moved one of the cups to a different place on the table. He chooses it, follows her finger when she points to the handle and lets the rest of the room remain quiet around it. Ciel asks him to release the detail rather than adding another. He does.

The pressure stays below the level of the first attempt.

*Better,* Noise says. ᴺ

*Yes,* Mau answers. ᴹ

There is no new reserve arriving, no sudden change in what the old wounds look like. The same blurred part of the damaged eye remains. The world outside Yuka stays grey. The improvement is in how much of it he has to receive at once, and whether he can keep one useful thing in front of the rest.

He is pleased enough by that to ask Ciel to show him the place where the rebuilt filter is working.

She does, briefly, through the same practical channel. He looks at the repaired pattern with the attention he gives something somebody has spent a long time making for him.

*Thank you,* he says. ᴹ

Ciel lets the diagram stay a little longer before putting it away.

*Break,* Noise says. ᴺ

*For me?* Mau asks. ᴹ

*Us.* ᴺ

Ciel laughs softly. *We have finished this part. Use the cloth outside. Tell me if the pressure changes.* ᶜ

*I'll tell you,* he promises. ᴹ

Noise is already asking her to come see something when the private conversation closes.

---

They go outside after a proper break.

The cloth comes with him. Ciel has asked him to use it when he is tired or when the view begins arriving faster than he can choose. He ties it loosely enough to raise and lower without repeatedly catching the knot in his hair.

Yuka carries the bag. He offers to take it. She hands him one of the two things inside and keeps the other so he cannot claim to have done all the carrying.

They find Shoko on the shaded path with Gojo. She sees the arrangement, asks one question and accepts the answer. Gojo looks at the cloth, then at Mau's expression.

“Getting used to it?” he asks. ᴳ

“Again. Differently.” ᴹ

Gojo nods and points toward the people crossing the far end of the path.

“Don't make every moving thing a new problem just because you can follow it,” he says. “You know they're there. You don't need to count the stitches in their clothes to keep knowing.” ᴳ

“The filter is separating that part again.” ᴹ

“Then let it. The temptation is checking whether it's done it correctly every half-second. That's another task you've given yourself.” ᴳ

Mau hears Ciel's small sound of agreement through the channel he has open. He looks toward a palm, feels his attention begin to climb every edge of its leaves and stops trying to verify the whole tree.

The pressure stays where it was.

“You can trust her work without auditing it while you're using it,” Gojo adds. ᴳ

Mau turns toward him, caught by the part of the advice which has nothing to do with whether he understands the eyes.

“I'll try that part,” he says. ᴹ

“Slowly,” Shoko adds. ˢʰ

Gojo and Mau answer together, which makes Yuka laugh before either can claim innocence.

---

Later, at the edge of the beach, Mau raises the cloth for a little while.

He chooses the ordinary line where the path becomes sand. Then the shade beneath a palm. Then Yuka, standing a few steps away with both hats in her hands.

She looks up.

“Can I?” he asks. ᴹ

She smiles, understanding what he means only after he has already begun looking at her.

“You can.” ʸ

He keeps his attention on her face, the clear colour that has never belonged to the rest of the view. He knows where the old blur interrupts it. He knows when the next layer of detail begins pressing at the edge. This time he notices early enough to lower the cloth before it becomes something he has to endure.

Yuka comes closer and lifts his hat onto his head. He catches her around the waist before she can step away.

“Good?” she asks. ʸ

“Very.” ᴹ

She runs a finger beneath the brim to straighten the cloth, then leaves her hand against his cheek. He turns into her palm and kisses it.

“You're making that face,” she tells him. ʸ

“Which one?” ᴹ

“The one where I'm going to have to ask three times what you're pleased about.” ʸ

He draws her closer.

“You. And it didn't hurt to keep looking.” ᴹ

Her smile changes. She kisses him slowly, keeping her hand clear of the knot when he leans down to meet her.

“You can tell me the first time,” she says. ʸ

“I'm discovering that.” ᴹ

She keeps one arm around him as they turn toward the water, beginning to describe where she wants to walk. He holds her at the waist and follows the direction she has chosen, getting used to hearing that one voice without losing the rest of the day.
