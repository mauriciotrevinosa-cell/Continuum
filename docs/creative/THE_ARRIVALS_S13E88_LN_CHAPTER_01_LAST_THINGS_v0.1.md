The last large breakfast begins without anyone calling it that.

Mau finds the tables already pulled together, somebody arguing about which dishes belong in the middle and Gojo holding a chair for Shoko with an exaggerated courtesy she chooses to reward by sitting down.

Yuka has come beside him with one hand on his back. She looks at the arrangement, then at the stack of wrapped parcels near the path.

“Tomorrow?” she asks. ʸ

“Most of us,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

“You're ready?” ᴹ

“I have things waiting at home. And Satoru has begun suggesting another week as if nobody would notice.” ˢʰ

Gojo turns toward her.

“People have noticed.” ᴳ

“Yes. That's the problem.” ˢʰ

Yuka laughs and takes the chair beside Mau's. He moves the little dish away from her elbow before she can reach for something farther across the table.

* * *

The reasons emerge over breakfast in the ordinary way of people making plans.

Seika wants to get back to the music space before the things she has thought of doing there lose their shape. Ram intends to go with her. Marin and Wakana have bought material they would rather work with at home. Maomao has been listing things she wants to compare with the ones she already has; Jinshi asks if she has finished the list and receives a look that answers more than the question.

Jin-Woo's family has chosen the shared journey back. His mother is ready for her own room. Suho wants to know which dragon will carry them. Jin-Woo tells him it is the same one, then catches Mau's eye across the table and points at the parcel his son has put beside the luggage.

“That one's fragile.” ᴶʷ

“Then don't let him pack it under the other one,” Hae-In says. ᴴᵃᵉ

Suho immediately moves it.

Mau smiles and puts the little note beside the returning luggage page.

* * *

Elmesia comes down after finishing the messages she has been answering since dawn.

There is room beside DIO. DIO clears the unused cup from the place before Elmesia has to ask, then looks surprised by how quickly she noticed what was needed.

“Thank you,” Elmesia says. ᴱˡ

DIO nods and tells her what she has missed at breakfast. The story becomes longer when Elmesia asks which part Gojo is pretending was his idea.

Makima sits near Mau and Yuka, choosing the chair with the view she has begun preferring. She no longer asks whether the bread is meant for her before taking a piece. She does ask Yuka which of the little dishes on the table is the one she liked yesterday.

Yuka points it out, then passes it closer.

Mau watches the movement and returns his attention to the conversation before it can become something either woman feels required to explain.

* * *

They agree on the flight after the tables have cleared.

Mau will take the main group back on the new shikigami, the same way they came. Mai wants to examine the platform once more before they put people on it. Senku and Byakuya have a short list of things to check after it has spent two weeks standing by the coast.

“Then we check them,” Mau says. ᴹ

“And you come back afterward?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Through the anchor here. When everybody's home and the things are out.” ᴹ

She nods. Testarossa offers to spend the afternoon with her. Elmesia says she has a table she wants them to see if they are staying for dinner.

Yuka looks at Mau.

“Don't be late.” ʸ

He leans down to kiss her.

“I know where dinner is.” ᴹ

“You know where I am.” ʸ

“That too.” ᴹ

She keeps one hand at his collar until he has finished smiling.

* * *

The rest of the day is a collection of last things.

People return to stalls to collect what they ordered. Someone remembers a place they meant to show somebody else. Bocchi and Kita go back to hear the unfamiliar instrument one more time, with Nijika promising Ryo that there is still no place in the luggage for a purchase she intends to justify later.

Mau and Yuka walk with Ori, Chloe and Frieren to the small beach where they built the town.

Nothing recognizable remains of the road. Ori looks at the place, then at the bag in Frieren's hand.

“Did you keep the door?” ᴼ

Frieren takes out the shell. Ori studies it, pleased, then returns it to her.

“For the next one,” she says. ᴼ

“For the next one,” Frieren agrees. ᶠ

Yuka sits on the low wall while Mau helps Chloe untangle the cord of her bag. Ori goes down to the water and back again without looking for a new town to build. She appears content to know that the part she wanted to keep has survived.

* * *

They eat something small near the beach before returning.

Mau has his hand around Yuka's wrist while she holds a cup. She has begun describing the few days she wants after the house leaves: mornings without a time to meet, maybe another boat, maybe going nowhere at all. He adds a place they saw from the wall, and she remembers the exact turn.

“We can stay longer if we haven't done it,” he says. ᴹ

“I intend to use that,” she tells him. ʸ

He draws her hand toward him and kisses the inside of her wrist before letting her have the cup back.

* * *

Ori watches them from Frieren's side.

She has been happy all day. She found another little thing to show Chloe, remembered the shell door and got to sit close enough to Mau that he listened while she explained why the water had changed the beach.

Now Yuka is talking about a morning with just him, and Mau looks so pleased that Ori keeps the question she almost asked inside her mouth.

Frieren touches her shoulder.

“Ready to go back?” ᶠ

Ori nods. She takes her mother's hand and lets the walk begin before the question can become something anybody has to answer.

* * *

That evening the common terrace is full of wrapping.

Mau stores the things people have labelled and leaves the ones they still need before morning. Beretta helps Byakuya move a box while Ramiris gives several opinions about how it should have been packed. Byakuya listens to the useful part and thanks her without accepting the rest. She appears satisfied enough to continue.

Ori brings the shell parcel she promised to look after for Chloe. Mau puts it where she can see it go, separate from the large cases.

“We'll take it out together,” he says. ᴹ

She nods.

Yuka comes over and puts her hat briefly on Ori's head, comparing the two enormous brims. Ori laughs when it comes down too far. Yuka lifts it again so she can kiss her forehead.

“Good trip?” she asks. ʸ

“Very good,” Ori says. ᴼ

She means it. That makes the other thing harder to explain, so she hugs Yuka instead and lets the answer remain complete enough for tonight.
