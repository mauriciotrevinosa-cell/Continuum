# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 81
## Chapter 34 — The Way Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 81
**Reading order:** 34
**Source episode:** S13E88

The second boarding takes less explanation and more conversation.

Everyone knows where the steps are. Everyone also knows somebody who has put a final parcel down somewhere inconvenient. Mau stands beside the labelled cases while Yuka reads the names to him, the same way they did before leaving home.

Ori brings Chloe's shell herself.

He stores it in the place he promised and waits while she makes sure it has gone separately from the larger things.

“I'll show you when we get there,” he says. ᴹ

She nods and goes to find Frieren.

Yuka hands him the last case, checks the page and closes the notebook.

“That should be everything.” ʸ

He looks at the little bag beside her feet.

“That's staying.” ʸ

“I was checking.” ᴹ

“You can check me when you get back.” ʸ

He kisses her cheek. She catches his shirt and keeps him close long enough to kiss his mouth too.

“Dinner,” she reminds him. ʸ

“I remember.” ᴹ

---

Mai has finished her inspection.

The coastal air has worked at one fastening enough to justify replacing it before they load the platform. She shows Mau the piece, makes him follow the corrected fit and accepts the replacement only after Byakuya has checked how it sits beneath the load.

Senku calls the test good. Mai calls it good after him, as if the final word matters most when it comes from the person who first drew the support.

Mau lets her have it.

He has left the island return anchor in the place Elmesia agreed to keep clear. Testarossa knows when to expect his notice. Yuka knows he will send it after everyone and the luggage have reached home. DIO and Makima remain with him for the journey, their outward presence set aside until the arrangements allow it again.

Elmesia comes down to say goodbye to the departing group. She asks the builders to write when they find something they want to improve about the platform. Senku says he already has a list. Mai says she will read it before he sends it.

“Good,” Elmesia says. ᴱˡ

She greets each group without making the whole landing wait for one conversation. Shoko thanks her for the quiet mornings. Gojo asks whether he is welcome back. Elmesia says she will decide when she reads the incident report, and Shoko laughs before he can protest that there isn't one.

“There is if you keep asking,” she tells him. ˢʰ

---

Yuka stays by the lower step until Mau has checked the last strap.

Ori comes back down for one more hug. Yuka bends to meet her, then keeps her arms around the girl while she asks whether the hat has been packed where it won't get bent.

“Frieren knows,” Ori says. ᴼ

“Then it's safe.” ʸ

Ori nods against her shoulder. For a moment she keeps holding on after the question has been answered.

Yuka rubs her back and lets her stay.

“I'll see you soon,” she says. ʸ

“Soon,” Ori repeats. ᴼ

She lets go, takes Frieren's hand and climbs back aboard.

Mau has watched the hug from the top of the steps, smiling. When Yuka looks up, he lifts a hand. She returns the little gesture and goes to stand with Elmesia and Testarossa while the platform rises.

---

The flight home is full of the things people did not finish telling each other on the island.

Bocchi has the instrument owner's explanation written down. Kita has added the part she forgot while concentrating on the sound. Nijika wants to know whether the rhythm will work with something they already play. Ryo appears to be calculating how long she can continue regretting an instrument she was never allowed to buy.

Across the aisle, Marin and Wakana compare the materials they kept out of storage for the journey. Seika listens until Ram puts a hand on her forearm and asks which of the new things she wants to begin with when they get home.

Mau sits at the forward place with the blindfold loose enough to adjust. The repaired filter helps him choose what matters when he raises it briefly: the course, the support marks, the shape of the coast ahead. When the information begins growing beyond the useful part, he lowers the cloth again.

Ciel checks in at the intervals they agreed. Noise keeps the construction quiet and familiar within the technique, offering only what Mau asks for. The journey requires attention. It doesn't require him to give every passing detail the same amount of it.

Frieren sits a little behind him with Ori asleep against her side.

Chloe watches the sea until it becomes land, then leans close to tell Ori. The girl wakes, looks and stays awake for the last part.

---

Home is warm with the afternoon by the time they arrive.

The platform lands in the field where they first built it. Bellion is waiting with the clear path and the soldiers Mau assigned to the luggage. He bows when Mau steps down, then receives the practical orders before the returning passengers begin telling everyone at once where their things should go.

Shuna is already on the path. Shion follows with something covered that smells as if their return time has been taken very seriously.

Mau hears several people remember how hungry they are.

He gets the cases out by their labels, one household at a time. The soldiers carry what they are directed to carry and leave the private doors for the people who live behind them. Bellion directs the next pair of soldiers toward the cases Yuta has already set aside.

Yuta takes the larger case for Maki. She lets him, choosing the one with the fragile things herself. Hae-In takes Suho's parcel before he can set it beneath another bag. Jin-Woo has already thanked Bellion and started listening to the answer about the courtyard.

Ori stays beside Mau until he brings out the shell packet.

He puts it in her hands.

“There.” ᴹ

She checks the corners, then smiles up at him.

“Safe.” ᴼ

“Safe.” ᴹ

She carries it to Chloe, who opens it with both of them watching.

---

Mau has something to eat before leaving again.

Shuna insists only by putting the plate in front of him and remaining where she can see what he does with it. He sits, accepts the company and tastes the thing she wanted to try with the ingredient from the island.

He looks up.

She looks pleased before he has said anything.

“Yuka needs to try this,” he tells her. ᴹ

Shuna puts some aside.

Gojo passes behind him with a cup and asks whether he intends to remain at the table until the next holiday. Shoko catches the back of Gojo's shirt and redirects him toward their own things before he can become part of the arrangement.

Mau finishes what he has been given. He listens to the report Bellion kept for him, answers the two questions that require a decision and leaves the rest of the standing instructions intact.

Frieren comes to the path with Ori when he is ready.

“Have a good time,” she says. ᶠ

“You too. Back here.” ᴹ

She nods. Ori reaches for him, and he bends to take her into his arms.

“Write?” she asks. ᴼ

“We will.” ᴹ

“About the bird.” ᴼ

“I'll report accurately.” ᴹ

She laughs into his shoulder. He puts her down beside her mother, waits until she has both feet under her and sends the notice to the island.

Testarossa's acknowledgment comes back promptly.

He goes when the route is ready.

---

Yuka is waiting in the shade beside the agreed anchor.

She has changed into the dress he liked at the market. When he steps out, she puts both arms around him before asking whether everything went well. He holds her close and answers into her hair.

“Everyone home. Things delivered. Shuna sent you food.” ᴹ

Yuka moves back just enough to see his face.

“Excellent report.” ʸ

He shows her the wrapped portion. She takes it with considerable interest, considers him over the top of it and holds it safely out to one side.

“You forgot something.” ʸ

He looks down at himself, then at the packet.

“Did I?” ᴹ

She catches his shirt and pulls him down. He meets her kiss with both arms around her, smiling when she tries to say something before she has let him go.

“I was getting to that,” he tells her. ᴹ

“You were reporting on luggage.” ʸ

“Very important luggage.” ᴹ

She kisses him again. When she finally takes his hand, she keeps his arm tucked against her all the way to the sheltered path.

DIO comes out when they reach the sheltered part of the path. Elmesia is there, having left time in her own work to join them. Testarossa has Antonio beside the table they chose. Makima steps out near Mau and waits until Yuka points to the place they kept.

The table is smaller than the first night's. There is still enough room for the stories they have brought back with them.

Yuka sits close to Mau and puts her hand in his lap beneath the cloth. He covers it with his, rubbing his thumb over her knuckles while Testarossa describes the table Elmesia nearly chose for them.

“Now,” Yuka says quietly, “we can decide what tomorrow is.” ʸ

He looks toward the water, then back at her.

“Tomorrow.” ᴹ

She leans against him, satisfied to leave the decision there until they have finished dinner. When the first dishes arrive, Mau tells her which one Shuna wanted her to try. She insists on feeding him a bite too, despite his claim to have completed the tasting at home.

“Different company,” she tells him. ʸ

“An improvement.” ᴹ

“I'm telling Shuna you said that.” ʸ

“She'll understand.” ᴹ

Yuka laughs and steals the next bite before he can take it. Elmesia asks about the flight, and Mau answers with his arm around his wife, who contributes the part of the departure he has tried to leave out.
