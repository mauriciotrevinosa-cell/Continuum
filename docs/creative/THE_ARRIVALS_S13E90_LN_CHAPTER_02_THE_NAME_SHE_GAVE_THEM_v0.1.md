# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 82
## Chapter 4 — The Name She Gave Them

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 82
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S13E90
**Continuity state:** Yoru visits by ordinary boat and requests a meeting; Asa is the shared host, not a separate body. No home barrier protects the island. Makima recognizes War; the meeting is conditional.

*When: B+397, early afternoon; Island III, day nineteen.*

The message reaches them while Yuka is deciding whether the water is cold enough to justify keeping Mau between her and the deeper part of the pool.

He has already told her she can stand there.

“I know.” ʸ

“You're taller than the water.” ᴹ

“I noticed.” ʸ

She tightens her arms around his neck. He stops trying to explain and walks her another step along the submerged ledge. She smiles, victorious, and is about to ask him something when he looks toward the path.

An attendant waits there with a folded note.

Mau brings Yuka back to the steps before going to take it. She climbs out after him, squeezing water from the ends of her hair. He reads the short message once, then gives it to her.

A visitor has asked to speak with him. The woman has come by boat, given a name and declined the offer to leave a written message. She says he knows the person she wants to discuss.

“Do you know her?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“I don't know the name.” ᴹ

The attendant offers to tell the visitor that he is unavailable.

“Where is she now?” he asks. ᴹ

“At reception. She agreed to wait.” ᵃᵗᵗ

“Can you describe her?” ᴹ

“Young woman. Dark hair. She had marks across her face when she asked for you. I thought they were scars, but when I came back with the paper I couldn't see them.” ᵃᵗᵗ

Mau looks at Yuka. Neither recognizes the visitor from the name, and the changing face is reason enough to ask someone who might.

“Tell her I'll answer shortly. Please don't bring her down here.” ᴹ

The attendant nods and goes back the way she came. Mau watches until she is well along the path, then looks at Yuka.

“Let's find out who she is.” ᴹ

“After you put a shirt on.” ʸ

He looks down.

“That would help.” ᴹ

---

Makima recognizes the description before they finish giving it.

She has been with Testarossa near the covered terrace. DIO and Elmesia are looking over something at the far end of the table; both stop when Makima sets down her cup.

“War,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Mau looks at her.

“You're sure?” ᴹ

“I would like to see her before I answer completely. But yes.” ᴹᵏ

Yuka puts the folded note on the table.

“Then why use this name?” ʸ

Makima studies it.

“Perhaps it isn't hers.” ᴹᵏ

Mau's attention returns to the path. The people walking along it have no reason to be concerned. One carries a tray of drinks. Another has stopped to fix the strap on a child's sandal.

“Not here,” he says. ᴹ

Elmesia rises.

“I'll have the staff keep her at reception.” ᴱˡ

“Has she threatened anybody?” ᴹ

“Not according to this.” ᴱˡ

“Then let me talk to her first. Somewhere clear.” ᴹ

Elmesia looks toward the pool, considering the people between it and the path.

“There's a terrace beyond the old landing. Nobody is using it this afternoon.” ᴱˡ

“Can we use that?” ᴹ

“You can. If I believe the conversation puts my guests in danger, it ends.” ᴱˡ

He nods without arguing.

Testarossa puts her book away. Antonio straightens behind her chair, responding to the change in her posture. She gives him a small motion to stay beside her.

“I'll go with you as far as reception,” she tells Elmesia. ᵀᵉ

“Thank you.” ᴱˡ

DIO looks at Mau. He understands the question before she puts it into words.

“Inside for now,” he says. “I want to know what she came for.” ᴹ

DIO nods, although she plainly dislikes the instruction. She waits for Elmesia to look back at her, then says, “I'll still be here.” ᴰ

Elmesia answers with a brief inclination of her head. DIO goes in.

Mau opens the practical channel inside.

*Ciel? Noise? Stay with me for this.* ᴹ

*I'm following,* Ciel answers. ᶜ

*Here,* Noise says. ᴺ

Their answers arrive without a delay. Mau gives them the description and what Makima has recognized before returning his attention to the route.

Makima remains outside beside him while he sends two ordinary soldiers ahead to watch the approaches. They move without a sound, obeying the places he gives them. A third stays near the hotel, instructed to warn him of immediate danger rather than follow guests into rooms.

Yuka has gone to change. She returns before he has finished, hair still damp, expression set.

He opens his mouth.

“I'm coming,” she says. ʸ

“I know. I was going to ask if you wanted shoes.” ᴹ

She looks down at the sandals in her hand.

“Oh.” ʸ

He takes them from her and kneels while she finds his shoulder for balance.

---

They wait beneath the roof at the old landing.

The sea is calm. A long stretch of bare stone separates the terrace from the nearest occupied building, and Mau has checked the route back twice by the time he stops moving. The return anchor is where he left it. The soldiers have taken their places. He does not mistake the empty space for the protection surrounding home.

Makima stands at his left. Yuka sits on the edge of the low wall, close enough to touch his back.

“What should we expect?” he asks. ᴹ

“Pride,” Makima says. “Impatience. She can be more direct than is useful to her.” ᴹᵏ

“Useful how?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Makima looks at her.

“If she wants a reaction from you, she may choose something personal. She may approach him, ask for something she knows you won't like. I would prefer that she finish speaking before either of you gives her what she wants.” ᴹᵏ

“That depends on what she's asking.” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

Mau turns toward Makima.

“I'm not agreeing to something because she'll get angry.” ᴹ

“I didn't expect you to.” ᴹᵏ

“You looked hopeful.” ᴹ

“I would rather the island remained here.” ᴹᵏ

The answer is so level that Yuka looks from one of them to the other before deciding Makima has not made a joke.

Mau rests his hand over Yuka's on the wall.

“Then we talk,” he says. ᴹ

---

The visitor comes down the path with Elmesia.

For a moment, before she notices who is waiting, she looks like somebody trying to remember instructions she resents having been given. Her shoulders are tight. Her eyes move from the terrace to the water, then to Mau.

She stops when she sees Makima.

Mau sees the change before the first word: the way her posture settles, the expression taking possession of the face. The marks across it become clear.

“So you really are here,” she says. ʷᵈ

Makima regards her calmly.

“You asked for him.” ᴹᵏ

“I didn't think you'd be far away.” ʷᵈ

Elmesia stops at the edge of the terrace.

“You've been given the conditions,” she says. ᴱˡ

The visitor glances at her, irritated by the reminder.

“I heard you.” ʷᵈ

“Then you know where to find me when you're ready to leave.” ᴱˡ

She does not go far. Testarossa waits on the path above, visible between the trees. Neither woman pretends to have forgotten the meeting merely because she has allowed it.

Mau faces the visitor.

“You can call me Yoru,” she says. ʷᵈ

“Is that the name you gave them?” ᴹ

“No.” ʷᵈ

“Why not?” ᴹ

“Would you have spoken to me if I'd written War?” ʷᵈ

“Probably.” ᴹ

She studies him, apparently deciding whether she finds that answer annoying.

Yuka gets down from the wall and stands beside him. Yoru's eyes move to the place where their hands meet.

“And she stays?” ʷᵈ

“Yes,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I didn't ask you.” ʷᵈ

“Yes,” Yuka says. ʸ

Something like amusement crosses Yoru's face.

“Good. I wanted to see what you were like.” ʷᵈ

“You could have asked,” Yuka says. ʸ

“I'm asking now.” ʷᵈ

Mau draws the empty chair back from the table. Yoru looks at it, then sits as though she has decided to humour him.

Makima takes the place opposite. Yuka chooses the one beside Mau, leaving her hand on his knee once they have sat down.

“What did you want to discuss?” he asks. ᴹ

Yoru leans forward, elbows on the table.

“You.” ʷᵈ

Mau waits.

“The yard,” she says. “The kings. The things you do when you think nobody has time to see them properly.” ʷᵈ

His gaze changes. She notices and smiles.

“I had time,” she says. ʷᵈ

Makima has become very still opposite her.

Yoru looks at her once, satisfied to have her attention, then turns back to Mau.

“I think we should stop pretending we haven't noticed each other.” ʷᵈ
