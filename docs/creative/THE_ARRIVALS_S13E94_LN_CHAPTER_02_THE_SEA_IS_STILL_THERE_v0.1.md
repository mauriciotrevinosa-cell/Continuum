# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 82
## Chapter 12 — The Sea Is Still There

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S13 v0.1
**Volume:** 82
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S13E94
**Continuity state:** Memory ends without showing a self-harm method or Trial reset count to Yoru. No present self-harm intent or new injury. Yoru/Asa leave with ninety-day terms intact; origin remains undisclosed. Mau is responsive but disoriented; recovery continues.

*When: B+397, near sunset; return from the selected memory.*

He does not know how long the quiet lasts.

There is no road left which means home. He tries to find the direction anyway, turning toward the place he believes the window should have been. His mind keeps supplying it: the bench, the book, the small movement of Frieren making room for him.

He has stopped looking for somebody to be angry.

He would accept anything now. A voice telling him to go away. A person who can look at what happened and decide he is not somebody they want near them. Somebody with enough of a life left to refuse him a place in it.

He listens.

The answer is the same quiet.

What follows is the part he learned to stop before remembering.

He has turned everything around him into something he cannot return to. In the end, he turns the violence inward too.

There is no relief waiting inside that choice. No reunion. No word which makes the things before it mean something kinder.

The memory breaks.

---

The sea is still there.

Mau hears it before he can see anything. Water against the landing, a little loose sound beneath the stone. Something touches his hand. He looks down to find fingers around his, a sleeve beside them, the edge of a chair which does not belong in the inn.

“Mau.” ʸ

He looks toward the voice.

Yuka sits close enough that he can see the little place where her hair is still damp from the pool. He knows her. The knowing comes without the rest of the room.

“It's finished,” she says. ʸ

He tries to answer. His breath catches before the word has formed.

Across the table, Asa has both hands pressed against the edge. Her face is white. She looks at him, then down, then at Yuka, as though she is looking for the person who can explain why the terrace has continued to exist.

“I don't want more,” she says. ᴬˢ

“There isn't more being shared,” Yuka tells her. ʸ

Mau hears that too. There is a conversation happening close enough that he ought to be able to join it. He cannot find the place to begin.

Asa wipes at her mouth with the back of her hand. She does not appear to notice the movement until she has made it.

“Those people—” ᴬˢ

She stops.

Makima puts the unused water nearer her, slowly enough that Asa can see the movement coming.

“You can take a moment,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Asa looks at her hand with open distrust. She drinks anyway.

Mau's gaze has gone past them to the broken line of the horizon.

There is nothing broken there. He knows that when he looks directly at it. The knowledge does not stop him from looking again.

---

Yuka says his name a second time.

This time he turns toward her before she has finished it. The little improvement is enough to make her move closer.

“Can you feel my hand?” ʸ

He looks down. His thumb moves against her fingers.

“Yes.” ᴹ

His voice sounds strange to him. It belongs to somebody who has not spoken for a long time.

“Good. Stay here with me.” ʸ

He nods.

It is a very small promise. He can make it while she is holding his hand. He is not yet sure he can understand the distance between here and the place she means.

Yuka looks toward Makima. The other woman has stopped attending to Asa. Her eyes are on Mau's face, with a concentration Yuka has learned to recognize since the accident.

“Is he hurt?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Not in the way you're asking.” ᴹᵏ

*No new injury detected,* Ciel tells Mau privately. *You are on the island. Your body has not undergone the memory's events.* ᶜ

He hears the words. He can understand each of them separately.

*Still here,* Noise says through the same channel. ᴺ

Mau tries to follow the voice. For a moment he can feel Yuka's thumb against his, distinct from the things he has just remembered. Then he looks at his hands again, searching for something which has already been absent from them for a long time.

Yuka puts her other hand around his wrist.

“Don't make yourself answer everything.” ʸ

He looks up at her again.

For a moment he seems about to say something. Then his attention slips, and the words remain where she cannot reach them.

---

Elmesia comes down when Makima signals to her.

She takes in the table without asking for an explanation in front of the visitor. Testarossa follows, stopping where Asa can see her rather than appearing behind the chair.

“Are you ready to leave?” Elmesia asks. ᴱˡ

Asa nods. Then her expression changes, the marks returning before she has got out of the chair.

Yoru looks at Mau for a long time.

Yuka rises a little, placing herself in the line of that attention.

“The memory's over.” ʸ

“I know.” ʷᵈ

“Then you're done here.” ʸ

Yoru's eyes move to her face. Whatever she was going to say meets something she recognizes well enough not to mistake for uncertainty.

She stands.

The pin is no longer on the table. She has put it away without using it. Mau sees that small fact and cannot explain why his mind has chosen to give it to him now.

“That isn't how you move anymore,” Yoru says to him. ʷᵈ

He looks toward her, but does not answer.

She waits a moment. When nothing follows, she looks at Makima instead.

“You knew?” ʷᵈ

“You received what you asked for,” Makima says. ᴹᵏ

Yoru's face closes around the unanswered question.

The record has given her more than she expected and less than she wants to be certain of. She has seen how the man breaks. She has not been given a way to date the pieces, measure them against the person in the chair or decide how much of the world around him had been what it appeared to be.

She looks at his wife one last time.

“Ninety days,” she says. ʷᵈ

“We have the date,” Yuka answers. ʸ

Elmesia gestures toward the path. Yoru goes with her, carrying her own changed piece of metal and no invitation to return without asking.

Testarossa follows far enough to make sure the departure continues. Antonio remains visible at the turn, immobile until she calls him onward.

---

When they are gone, Yuka kneels in front of Mau.

He looks at her at once. For a second the speed of it frightens her more than the delay did. He has been waiting for something close enough to respond to, and she does not know what his mind is doing with the distance.

“It's me,” she says. ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

His fingers close around hers. He says it with enough effort that she believes him without mistaking it for an end to the problem.

“We're still at the landing. We can stay here if you want. Or we can go back to the room.” ʸ

He looks beyond her shoulder.

The window should be there.

It isn't.

“I left,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka feels the words land in the little space beneath her ribs.

“I know what you showed her.” ʸ

“I thought—” ᴹ

He stops. His breath has begun to go too fast.

She does not finish the old sentence for him. She moves her hand from his wrist to his cheek, waiting until he looks at her again.

“Look at me for a little while.” ʸ

He does.

“We read Ori's letter yesterday. Remember the roof you drew?” ʸ

He stares at her.

“The laundry,” she says. “You laughed so much you couldn't walk.” ʸ

Something changes in his face. It is not happiness. It is the painful effort of trying to make one picture fit beside another without losing either.

Yuka stays where he can see her.

Behind them, DIO appears beside Makima. She has asked before coming out, and now she looks at the man in the chair with an expression Yuka does not have the room to understand yet.

“The old Trial,” Yuka says quietly. “He opened that memory. He's having trouble getting back out of it.” ʸ

DIO's hand moves toward him and stops.

Yuka sees the movement. She cannot manage anybody else's guilt right now. She looks back at Mau and lets the sentence be enough.

He has begun rubbing his thumb against her hand again.

She holds it steady and says his name.
