# S1E10 — `Four Strangers / G2 Arrival`

## Scene 1 — Strange Zoltraak

Late morning outside the inn.

The weather has cleared enough that eating outside is easier than carrying everything in and out of the kitchen.

Frieren and Mau sit near the front of the inn with bowls in hand.

Bocchi's guitar is audible from an upstairs room. Rimuru is somewhere around the back working on storage. Yuta trains beyond the nearest houses, far enough not to break another wall by accident.

Frieren watches one of Yuta's smaller cursed-energy strikes.

Mau keeps eating.

**FRIEREN**  
Can you do a stronger one?

Yuta looks over.

**YUTA**  
How much stronger?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
A lot.

Yuta looks toward the settlement.

Then the sky.

**YUTA**  
Up?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Preferably.

Mau stops eating now.

Yuta moves farther out, braces, and releases a high-output cursed-energy beam into open sky.

The force punches through cloud cover without touching the settlement.

Light floods the lane for an instant.

Mau looks up with his spoon halfway to his mouth.

Frieren studies the residual energy with genuine interest.

**FRIEREN**  
What a strange Zoltraak.

Yuta, still recovering from the output:

**YUTA**  
...It's not Zoltraak.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
I know.

Mau looks between them.

Hard cut.

---

## Scene 2 — Fern

Fern wakes on cold ground beneath unfamiliar trees.

Her first thought is not another world.

It is distance.

The explosion.

Mistress Frieren firing.

Being thrown somewhere she cannot immediately identify.

Fern sits up and checks herself.

No serious injury.

Staff present.

Clothes wrong for where she remembers being only moments ago, but intact.

She looks around for a mana signature she knows better than almost any other.

Nothing.

Her face tightens.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren is going to be mad at me.

She stands.

Then notices she is not alone.

---

## Scene 3 — Three More

Anko is already awake several meters away.

She has checked her pulse, pockets, immediate injuries, and the ground around where she appeared.

When Fern notices her, Anko is looking at footprints that begin nowhere useful.

A third person is crouched near an unfamiliar plant.

Maomao has the leaf between two fingers and is considering it with uncomfortable seriousness.

Momo, newly awake and much less impressed by the plant, sees Maomao move it toward her face.

**MOMO**  
Don't eat that.

Maomao looks at her.

**MAOMAO**  
I wasn't going to eat it.

Momo relaxes slightly.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
I was going to taste it.

**MOMO**  
That's the same problem.

Maomao studies the underside of the leaf.

**MOMO**  
It could be poisonous.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
That's the point.

Momo stares at her.

Fern approaches with staff in hand.

**FERN**  
Who are you?

Anko stands first.

**ANKO**  
Anko Uguisu.

She gestures toward the others.

**ANKO**  
We all woke up here within the last few minutes. None of us know where `here` is.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
And nobody knows what this is.

She still means the plant.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Please stop making the plant part of the emergency.

Fern looks at them one by one.

They are strangers.

But at least they are strangers with the same immediate problem.

**FERN**  
Fern.

Anko notices Fern did not give a family name but does not push.

---

## Scene 4 — The Beam

They are still comparing only the facts that matter when the sky changes.

A distant line of light tears upward beyond the trees.

Even at this distance, the scale is obvious.

All four look toward it.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Was that normal here?

Anko:

**ANKO**  
We don't know what normal is here.

Maomao, finally letting go of the plant:

**MAOMAO**  
It was useful.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
How?

Fern has already oriented toward the beam.

She cannot sense Frieren.

The beam does not feel like Frieren's mana.

She does not pretend otherwise.

**FERN**  
Someone made it.

Anko:

**ANKO**  
Someone powerful.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Or someone near people who are.

She looks through the forest in the beam's direction.

**FERN**  
If Mistress Frieren is anywhere nearby, she may have seen it too.

That is enough of a lead.

Anko agrees because a visible event is better than random walking.

Momo agrees because the alternative is staying in an unknown forest.

Maomao pockets a small sample of the leaf.

Momo sees.

**MOMO**  
I saw that.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Good.

They start walking.

---

## Scene 5 — Four People Walking

The beam was visible from far away.

Far enough that reaching its source takes hours rather than one convenient scene transition.

The terrain is not difficult for Fern, but it is uneven enough to make everyone else's limits relevant.

Anko walks while constantly reading the environment.

Maomao gets distracted by plants and soil whenever the group slows.

Momo keeps trying to keep Maomao from testing every unknown substance with her mouth.

Fern sets the pace at first and then realizes she is leaving them behind.

She slows without commenting.

Anko notices.

**ANKO**  
You can move faster than this.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Yes.

Anko:

**ANKO**  
Thanks.

Fern gives the smallest nod.

The group crosses a stream.

Maomao checks the water before drinking.

Momo watches her suspiciously.

**MOMO**  
Are you checking if it's safe?

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Yes.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
For once?

Maomao looks offended by the phrasing.

Fern almost smiles.

---

## Scene 6 — What Everyone Knows

Their conversation becomes less formal as they move.

Momo talks more than the others because silence makes the situation worse.

She explains enough about herself that `spirits` and `aliens` both become plausible categories in her worldview.

Anko takes that information seriously enough to be polite and skeptically enough to remain Anko.

Maomao is more interested in what local medicine might exist than in deciding whether the transfer was spiritual.

Fern mostly listens.

At one point Momo asks what Fern was doing before waking here.

**FERN**  
Fighting.

Momo waits.

Fern does not add more.

Anko:

**ANKO**  
With the person you're looking for?

Fern:

**FERN**  
Yes.

A few steps later:

**FERN**  
I think the attack threw me away from her.

That is still the only explanation Fern has.

Her subjective separation feels like hours at most.

Nobody at this point has any reason to challenge it.

---

## Scene 7 — Night on the Road

They do not reach the settlement before dark.

Fern could probably continue.

The others should not.

They make a small camp where the terrain gives shelter from wind.

Fern handles the fire with efficient magic.

Momo watches.

**MOMO**  
Okay. That's magic.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Yes.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Just checking.

Anko sits with her back to a tree, mapping the day's direction from memory.

Maomao examines the plant sample by firelight.

Momo notices again.

**MOMO**  
You're still carrying that?

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Of course.

Momo looks at Fern as if asking for support.

Fern looks at Maomao.

**FERN**  
Don't eat it.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
I wasn't going to.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
She means taste it too.

Maomao is now outnumbered.

She puts the sample away.

The four share food as best they can.

No one calls themselves a group.

But when Anko takes first watch, Fern takes the next without discussion.

That is close enough.

---

## Scene 8 — G1 After the Beam

Back at the settlement, the beam has become an inside joke almost immediately.

Rimuru heard the blast from inside and came running out too late to see the start of it.

Bocchi complains that nobody warned her before turning the sky into a flashbang.

Yuta apologizes.

Frieren remains delighted by the comparison she refuses to retract.

**YUTA**  
You know it's cursed energy.

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

**YUTA**  
Then stop calling it Zoltraak.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
It looks like Zoltraak.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
It doesn't.

Mau, from the table:

**MAU**  
It did from where I was sitting.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
You're not helping.

The day continues.

None of them realizes the beam gave four strangers a direction.

---

## Scene 9 — Morning, Closer

G2 moves again at first light.

Travel has changed the spacing between them.

Fern no longer walks ten meters ahead.

Momo and Maomao have developed a pattern where Momo notices every time Maomao drifts toward a suspicious plant.

Anko no longer needs to ask Fern before using her magic as part of route decisions; she just points out an obstacle and Fern responds.

At a rise in the terrain, they find the first evidence of a settlement.

An old cut stump.

Then a faint track.

Then smoke.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Please be normal people.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
What would count as normal now?

Momo:

**MOMO**  
People who know where they are.

Anko:

**ANKO**  
Low standard.

They continue.

Fern still senses nothing familiar.

That worries her more than she says.

---

## Scene 10 — Ordinary Afternoon

Frieren and Mau are outside behind the inn dealing with something mundane: moving dried herbs and arguing about whether one container is actually sealed.

Mau presses the lid.

**MAU**  
It's sealed.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
It's crooked.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Those can both be true.

Frieren takes it, straightens it, gives it back.

Mau checks.

**MAU**  
Still sealed.

Frieren ignores him.

Then she stops.

Completely.

The expression is different from the lake with Yuta.

Not caution.

Recognition so sudden it empties everything else from her face.

Mau sees it.

**MAU**  
Frieren?

Frieren turns toward the forest.

There is almost nothing to sense.

A tiny signature, heavily suppressed by habit, barely there at all.

But she knows it.

She has known it for years.

Her hand rises toward the ornament in her hair without touching it.

At the tree line, four figures appear.

Fern is first.

She sees the white hair before Frieren moves.

Her shoulders loosen in immediate relief.

To Fern, this is the end of an inconvenient separation.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren.

Frieren cannot answer yet.

Fern steps forward.

**FERN**  
There you are.

Mau looks from Fern to Frieren.

He understands at once.

This is Fern.

The person whose absence existed in the room before any of them did.

Frieren takes one step.

**CUT.**
