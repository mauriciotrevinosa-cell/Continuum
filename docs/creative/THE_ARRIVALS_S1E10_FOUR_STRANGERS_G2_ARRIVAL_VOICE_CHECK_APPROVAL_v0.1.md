## Approved E10 voice / structure

E10 is green-lit with these locked voice-check decisions:

- Opening stays at the inn exterior: Frieren and Mau are eating / relaxing outside while Yuta trains nearby.
- Frieren asks Yuta to show a stronger cursed-energy attack.
- Yuta fires a large high-output cursed-energy beam into the sky, visually inspired by his known beam against Ryu, so the signal is huge and visible without destroying the settlement.
- Frieren's observational joke remains in the direction of: `What a strange Zoltraak.` Yuta objects that it is not Zoltraak.
- Hard cut from the beam to G2 elsewhere.

### G2 arrival / road sequence

Restore and preserve the already-approved rough roadmap rather than replacing it with a new dynamic:

- Fern wakes elsewhere in the forest and initially assumes the E1 explosion threw her away from the battlefield.
- Fern's first concern can be: `Mistress Frieren is going to be mad at me.` Her subjective separation is extremely short compared with Frieren's much longer grief/search interval.
- Anko is already checking pulse / condition / surroundings with a detective mindset.
- Maomao is inspecting or considering tasting an unfamiliar plant.
- Momo warns Maomao that it might be poisonous.
- Maomao answers essentially: `That's the point.`
- Fern asks who the others are; Anko helps establish that all four have only just awakened there and none knows where they are.
- Fern does not detect Frieren and does not assume the distant beam belongs to her.
- Fern's logic for following the beam is that an attack of that scale is an unusually strong lead: whoever produced it may be powerful, may know the area, or may be near other people; if Frieren is nearby, she could plausibly have encountered the same source.
- G2 spends meaningful travel time together before reaching G1 so Fern, Anko, Maomao, and Momo already function as a recognizable group before the merge.
- Do not force exposition-circle introductions or artificial interpersonal conflict; their chemistry develops through practical travel, competence, and personality friction.

### Ending

- Return to Frieren and Mau rather than staging all of G1 in a dramatic waiting line.
- Frieren and Mau are talking / doing something ordinary when Frieren suddenly detects an extremely small, heavily suppressed but unmistakably familiar mana signature.
- Mau notices Frieren's expression and asks: `Frieren?`
- Fern reaches the edge of the forest / settlement and says: `Mistress Frieren. There you are.`
- Frieren sees Fern.
- Mau understands that this is the Fern whose name and loss have shaped Frieren since he met her.
- Cut before the reunion itself so E11 owns the embrace, crying, time-gap confusion, hair ornament beat, and full G1+G2 emotional merge.
