<!-- scene-id: s1e10-ln01-strange-zoltraak -->

Late morning was clear enough to eat outside.

Frieren and Mau sat near the front of the inn with bowls in hand.

Bocchi's guitar came faintly from upstairs.

Rimuru was somewhere around the back reorganizing storage.

Yuta trained beyond the nearest houses, far enough away that nobody had to worry about another wall becoming a casualty.

Frieren watched one of his smaller cursed-energy strikes.

"Can you do a stronger one?"

Yuta looked over.

"How much stronger?"

"A lot."

He looked at the settlement.

Then the sky.

"Up?"

"Preferably."

Mau stopped eating.

That answer had been too casual.

Yuta moved farther out, planted his feet, and released a high-output beam into open sky.

Light tore upward.

Cloud cover split around it.

For one instant the lane, the roofs and Mau's half-raised spoon all flashed white.

Frieren watched the residue with real interest.

"What a strange Zoltraak."

Yuta was still recovering from the output.

"...It's not Zoltraak."

"I know."

Mau looked between them.

The beam disappeared into the sky.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e10-ln01-fern-wakes -->

Far away, beneath the same sky, Fern opened her eyes.

Cold ground.

Trees she did not recognize.

Her first thought was not another world.

It was the explosion.

Mistress Frieren firing.

Distance.

Fern sat up and checked herself.

No serious injury.

Staff present.

Clothing intact, though not arranged exactly as she remembered before the blast.

She reached for the mana signature she knew better than almost any other.

Nothing.

Her mouth tightened.

"Mistress Frieren is going to be mad at me."

She stood.

Then noticed the others.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e10-ln01-three-more -->

Anko Uguisu was already awake several meters away.

She had checked herself, her pockets, the nearby ground and a set of footprints that did not begin anywhere useful.

A third person crouched near an unfamiliar plant.

Maomao held a leaf between two fingers with the concentration of someone considering a very interesting bad idea.

Momo Ayase saw the leaf moving toward Maomao's face.

"Don't eat that."

Maomao looked over.

"I wasn't going to eat it."

Momo relaxed one degree.

"I was going to taste it."

"That's the same problem."

Maomao looked back at the underside of the leaf.

"It could be poisonous," Momo said.

"That's the point."

Momo stared at her.

Fern approached with her staff in hand.

"Who are you?"

Anko stood first.

"Anko Uguisu."

She gestured toward the others.

"We all woke up here within the last few minutes. None of us know where here is."

Maomao lifted the leaf.

"And nobody knows what this is."

"Please stop making the plant part of the emergency," Momo said.

Fern looked between them.

Strangers.

But strangers sharing the same impossible problem.

"Fern."

Anko noticed the missing family name and did not press.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e10-ln01-the-beam -->

The sky changed.

A line of light climbed beyond the trees.

All four looked toward it.

Momo pointed.

"Was that normal here?"

"We don't know what normal is here," Anko said.

Maomao finally lowered the leaf.

"It was useful."

"How?"

Fern had already turned toward the beam.

She could not sense Frieren.

The light did not feel like Frieren's mana.

She did not pretend otherwise.

"Someone made it."

"Someone powerful," Anko said.

"Or someone near people who are."

Fern had already taken several steps before anyone formally agreed they were following it.

Momo noticed.

She did not tease her.

"If Mistress Frieren is anywhere nearby," Fern continued, "she may have seen it too."

That was enough.

A visible event was better than random walking.

Anko agreed.

Momo agreed because staying put solved nothing.

Maomao slipped a sample of the leaf into a pocket.

Momo saw.

"I saw that."

"Good."

They started walking.
