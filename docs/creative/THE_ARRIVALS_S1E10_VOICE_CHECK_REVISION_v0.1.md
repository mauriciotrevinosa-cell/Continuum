## Correction to E10 opening and closing structure

These notes supersede the earlier E10 voice-check staging where they conflict. All other G2 arrival beats remain intact unless separately revised.

### 1. Yuta is already training outside the inn

The stronger cursed-energy demonstration should not begin with Frieren taking Yuta away from the house for a formal test.

Instead:

- Yuta is already outside training at a safe distance / angle from the inn.
- Mau and Frieren are nearby eating outside together.
- Frieren watches Yuta train and becomes curious about cursed energy again.
- She asks Yuta to demonstrate more of it there.
- Mau is already beside Frieren, so the beat feels domestic and spontaneous rather than staged.

Representative flow:

```text
Yuta is training outside
→ Mau + Frieren are eating nearby
→ Frieren watches
→ Frieren asks Yuta to show her more
→ Yuta gives a stronger demonstration
```

### 2. Yuta fires upward, not into terrain

Yuta's demonstration should be a large cursed-energy beam fired into the sky, visually comparable in spirit to the large beam he used against Ryu Ishigori.

The beam should:

- be clearly much larger than the E4 clash;
- be fired upward so it does not damage the newly repaired home or surrounding settlement;
- be visible from very far away;
- serve as the visual signal later seen by G2.

Frieren can still react with the approved line:

> **Frieren:** `What a strange Zoltraak.`

Yuta can still object that it is not Zoltraak.

### 3. Immediate cut to G2

After the beam is fired into the sky, cut directly to Fern / Anko / Maomao / Momo seeing that distant signal.

This preserves the logic:

```text
Yuta beam into sky
→ cut to G2 seeing it from far away
→ G2 concludes that someone caused it
→ therefore there are people in that direction
→ they travel toward the signal / settlement
```

Fern does not identify the signal as Frieren and does not follow it because she expects Frieren. It is simply proof of people.

### 4. Return to Mau + Frieren for the emotional ending

The episode should not end with all of G1 waiting at the door for G2.

Instead, after the G2 travel material, return to Mau and Frieren together near the end of the episode.

The emotional focus should again be on the pair who began the story.

They are talking naturally / sharing a quiet domestic moment when Frieren suddenly notices something.

Mau notices Frieren's reaction first.

> **Mau:** `Frieren?`

Frieren becomes still because she senses extremely familiar, suppressed mana in the distance.

She does not immediately explain it because she is not ready to trust what she is sensing.

The important closing progression is:

```text
Mau + Frieren together
→ ordinary conversation / quiet domestic beat
→ Frieren suddenly detects familiar suppressed mana
→ Mau notices the change in her
→ Frieren realizes it may be Fern
→ emotional cut / transition into E11 reunion
```

The actual full reunion, embrace, hair-ornament payoff, and Fern's subjective-time confusion remain reserved for E11.

## Continuity principle

E10 should structurally mirror its emotional priorities:

- open on the established home / G1 life;
- use Yuta's skyward beam as the causal bridge to G2;
- spend the body of the episode with G2 forming and traveling;
- return to **Mau + Frieren** for the final emotional beat;
- end on Frieren detecting Fern before the reunion is consumed.
