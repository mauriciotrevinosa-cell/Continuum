## Approval

S1E11 is approved as presented in the Voice Check, with one explicit domestic-continuity correction governing the start of the Frieren/Mau separation arc.

## Locked correction — start of Frieren/Mau separation

Up to Fern's return, Mau and Frieren have effectively been living together in Frieren's room even though Mau technically has an assigned room of his own.

When Fern returns in E11, Mau immediately begins giving Frieren and Fern space. He quietly removes / relocates his everyday belongings from Frieren's room and reduces how much time he sleeps / stays there. This is not a fight and not a mutual breakup of routine; from Mau's perspective he is doing something considerate because Fern is Frieren's person from her old world and they need time together.

Frieren brings Fern to her own room and notices that Mau's things are gone. She does not verbalize the hurt or ask Mau about it. When Fern questions the arrangement, Frieren simply tells her:

> **Frieren:** "Stay."

The line carries two simultaneous emotional pressures:

1. Frieren is terrified of losing Fern again after believing her dead.
2. Frieren has just noticed that Mau's presence has disappeared from the shared room and does not yet understand why.

This is the true beginning of the Frieren/Mau distance arc. E12 continues and deepens it rather than initiating it from zero.

> Mau and Frieren had an established shared domestic routine before Fern's return. E11 begins the disruption of that routine because Mau gives space and Frieren misreads the absence.

This physical separation should later strengthen the emotional misunderstanding in E12-E14.
