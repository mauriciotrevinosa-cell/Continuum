<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln01-reunion -->

Fern started toward Frieren at her normal pace.

Relief had already reached her face.

"Mistress Frieren. There you are."

Frieren crossed the rest of the distance first.

Quickly.

Not quickly for anyone else.

Quickly for Frieren.

Fern had time to look surprised before Frieren wrapped both arms around her.

Fern froze.

Frieren's grip tightened.

Then Fern felt her shaking.

"Mistress Frieren?"

Frieren did not answer.

Fern brought her arms around her.

Carefully at first.

Then with more certainty when she realized Frieren was crying.

Behind them, Mau had taken a step forward.

He stopped.

This was the person whose name had existed in the inn before Bocchi.

Before Yuta.

Before Rimuru.

The person Frieren had waited for through windows and sounds in the night.

Something small had fallen near Fern's feet during the first movement of the reunion.

Mau picked it up.

He placed it within reach near them rather than interrupting.

Then he backed away.

This moment was not his.

Yuta, Bocchi and Rimuru were coming from the inn.

Mau turned toward the three strangers behind Fern instead.

"Hi."

Momo, caught between staring at the crying reunion and pretending not to, looked at him.

"...Hi."

Anko was already studying the settlement.

Maomao had noticed a plant near the wall.

Mau noticed Maomao noticing it.

"Don't eat that."

Maomao looked at him.

Momo exhaled.

"Thank you."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln01-seven-days -->

Frieren eventually loosened her hold.

Not much.

She looked at Fern's face.

Then shoulder.

Arm.

Hair.

Small checks disguised badly as affection.

"I'm fine," Fern said.

Frieren touched her shoulder again.

"The explosion threw me pretty far away. I was trying to get back."

Frieren stopped moving.

"I looked for you for seven days."

Fern blinked.

"...Seven days?"

"Yes."

"But I was only gone for..."

Fern stopped.

She did not know the exact amount anymore.

A stretch of confusion.

Walking.

One night.

Nothing close to seven days.

Frieren answered the unfinished sentence.

"Not for me."

Fern understood then.

Not the mechanism.

The experience.

Her face changed.

She pulled Frieren into another embrace.

This one Fern initiated.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln01-stark -->

After the first wave passed, Fern looked around.

The question arrived behind the relief.

"Where's Stark?"

Frieren's expression answered first.

"Not here."

"Have you seen him?"

"No."

Fern looked toward the forest.

Then the inn.

One impossible fact became two.

Time had not matched.

Their world might not be here at all.

Frieren explained only what she could support.

She had awakened here.

Others had appeared.

Nobody understood how.

Fern's eyes moved across Yuta, Bocchi, Rimuru.

Then Mau.

Mau gave an awkward small wave.

Fern's attention stayed on him a fraction longer.

He belonged to none of the faces she knew.

Yet he moved through Frieren's new life as if he had already been there long enough to learn its shape.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln01-ornament -->

Fern's gaze lifted.

Her expression changed completely.

"That's where that was."

Frieren touched the ornament in her hair.

For the first time since the embrace, the tension cracked.

"It came with me."

"Apparently."

Frieren began to remove it.

Fern caught her hand.

"Later."

Frieren looked at her.

Fern did not explain.

"Okay."

The ornament stayed.
