Mau handled the practical things while Frieren stayed close to Fern.

Water.

Heat.

Food.

Somewhere to put bags.

Momo looked around the common room.

"How long have you been living here?"

Mau thought.

"Long enough that the roof mostly works."

Rimuru passed with cups.

"Don't give me credit for mostly."

"It doesn't leak on the table anymore."

"That is not the same as finished."

Maomao accepted water.

"What medicines do you have?"

"Frieren has plants."

"What kind?"

Mau pointed toward Frieren.

"Ask her later."

Momo looked at Rimuru.

She had already been told enough about the slime situation to remain unconvinced.

"So you're actually a slime."

"Yes."

"Like a spirit?"

"No."

"Alien?"

Rimuru paused.

"Also no."

Momo looked as though she did not consider him an authority on the matter.

Anko said almost nothing.

She was learning the building.

The people.

Who answered questions.

Who avoided them.

Where everyone looked when something unfamiliar happened.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln02-nine-voices -->

Dinner proved the table was too small.

Nine bowls.

Nine bodies.

Too many elbows.

Yuta shifted his chair.

Bocchi made herself smaller automatically.

Mau moved dishes toward the center.

Rimuru said, "We need a bigger table."

Nobody answered because three conversations had already begun.

Anko's eyes moved past Yuta.

"Who's behind you?"

Yuta stopped chewing.

Mau answered without looking up.

"The angel."

"She's not an angel," Yuta said.

Anko's attention sharpened.

"There is someone there."

"Sometimes."

"That's not an answer."

"It's the answer you're getting during dinner."

Across the table, Momo was still investigating Rimuru.

Maomao leaned toward him.

"Do you have blood?"

Rimuru stopped mid-sentence.

"Why?"

"I want to know."

Momo turned.

"Do not offer her blood."

"I wasn't going to."

Bocchi tried to follow everything.

Failed.

Looked down at her food and decided eating was at least one conversation she understood.

Fern noticed something else.

Mau reached across Frieren for a dish.

Frieren moved her book before he asked.

A few minutes later Frieren made a dry comment about one of Rimuru's repairs.

Mau answered with a callback Fern did not know.

Frieren's response came immediately.

No delay.

No long pause while she decided whether a human joke was worth the effort.

Rhythm.

Fern had spent years learning Frieren's silences.

This was a new one.

She did not assign meaning to it.

She remembered it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln02-shared-room-statement -->

The room question came up because there were too many people and not enough prepared spaces.

Momo looked toward the stairs.

"So who sleeps where?"

Rimuru started to answer.

Frieren did first.

"Fern can stay near me."

Then, almost as an afterthought:

"I share a room with Mau."

Fern looked at her.

Momo looked at Mau.

Mau was across the room helping Yuta move a bench.

He had not heard.

The sentence passed through the household without reaching the one person it named.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln02-overprotection -->

The next morning Fern came downstairs with her staff.

Frieren saw it.

"No."

Fern stopped.

"No?"

"Stay here."

"I'm only checking the area."

"No."

"I can protect myself."

"I know."

That made the refusal worse.

"Then why can't I go?"

Frieren had an answer.

Seven days.

A crater.

An ornament in her hand.

She did not have a sentence she could say in front of everyone.

"Stay here."

Fern's mouth tightened.

She turned away before the frustration became an argument.

Yuta had already found another task.

Mau watched both of them hurt each other with true statements that omitted the important part.

He did not interfere.

Not yet.
