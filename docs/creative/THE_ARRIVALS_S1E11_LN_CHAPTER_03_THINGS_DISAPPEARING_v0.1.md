<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-mau-explains -->

Fern stood in the yard inspecting supplies with slightly more force than the task required.

Mau brought out a repaired container and set it near her.

Fern looked at him.

He did not pretend he had come outside by accident.

"She thought you were dead."

Fern stopped.

"For seven days."

"I heard her."

"Hearing it isn't the same."

Fern looked through the window.

Frieren was visible inside.

"Give her a little time."

Fern studied Mau.

"You know her well."

Mau considered the phrasing.

"I know her now."

Not better.

Not instead.

Now.

Fern looked back toward the inn.

That answer mattered.

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-rooms -->

Temporary rooms became necessary before anyone was ready to think about permanent arrangements.

Anko chose daylight and a surface to write on.

Maomao chose proximity to water and storage.

Momo wanted a clear view of the sky and refused to elaborate.

Bocchi carried blankets because she knew where clean ones were.

Yuta fixed a latch.

Frieren discovered Fern's window did not close correctly.

"This needs to be fixed tonight."

Rimuru examined it.

"It's not going to fall out."

"Tonight."

Rimuru looked at Fern.

Fern gave him the smallest apologetic expression.

"Tonight."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-own-room -->

Later, Momo caught Mau carrying books upstairs.

"Which one's yours?"

Mau pointed down the corridor.

"Technically that one."

Momo looked toward the room.

Then toward the room she had seen him use with Frieren.

"You have your own room?"

Mau smiled.

"Apparently."

There was something almost pleased in the discovery.

A place for his books.

A room assigned to him.

Another ordinary thing people had.

Frieren was close enough to hear.

Her eyes lifted from what she was carrying.

Mau did not notice.

He continued down the hall with Momo.

Frieren remained still one second longer.

*He has his own room.*

The fact should have been neutral.

It did not feel neutral.

If Mau liked having the room...

If he wanted independence...

Frieren did what she had always believed people deserved when they wanted space.

She tried not to take it away.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-fern-stays -->

That evening, Fern and Frieren stood together near the room Frieren had functionally shared with Mau.

Fern looked toward her own assigned room.

"Where should I put this?"

Frieren answered while still carrying the thought from the hallway.

"You can stay with me for now."

Mau appeared at the far end of the corridor.

He heard that sentence.

Not the thoughts before it.

Not the statement at dinner that Frieren shared a room with him.

Only:

*Fern can stay with me.*

Mau slowed.

This made sense.

Fern was back.

Frieren had missed her.

He had a room of his own.

Everything was working the way it should.

Something still dropped inside his chest.

He kept walking.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-mixed-belongings -->

When Mau later began moving things, the task was harder than expected.

His belongings and Frieren's had mixed.

Not ceremonially.

Practically.

His cup near her books.

The black-and-orange McLaren hoodie Frieren had borrowed often enough that neither of them reliably returned it to a particular side.

The red Ferrari team shirt over the back of the chair where Frieren had last left it.

His McLaren cap beside a stack of her books.

Grimoires nobody could immediately remember buying for whom.

A tool beside her travel case.

The real flower at the window belonged to Mau by choice and to the room by habit.

He started only with the objects unquestionably his.

Cup.

McLaren cap.

Ferrari shirt.

Hoodie.

His notes.

Two grimoires.

He left anything ambiguous.

He did not want to take something Frieren considered hers.

He also did not want to make Fern feel like she was entering a room already occupied by someone else.

Considerate.

Reasonable.

Painful anyway.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-ornament -->

Before bed, Frieren touched the ornament.

"This is yours."

Fern looked at it.

"I know."

Frieren started to remove it.

"Keep it a little longer."

Frieren's hand stopped.

"Okay."

Fern had begun to understand what the ornament had done while she was gone.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e11-ln03-final-room -->

Frieren entered the room with Fern.

She noticed the missing things immediately.

The cup.

The McLaren cap was no longer beside her books.

The red Ferrari shirt was gone from the chair.

The black-and-orange hoodie was gone too.

Books.

The small repair tool that normally sat beside one of her stacks.

The flower remained.

For now.

Across the corridor, Mau carried another small bundle into his nominal room.

He saw them.

"Good night."

Frieren looked at the things in his arms.

"Good night."

Mau closed his door.

Fern looked around.

"Was he staying here?"

"Yes."

Fern waited.

Frieren looked at Mau's closed door.

"Where am I sleeping?"

Frieren's attention snapped back.

"Stay."

The answer came too quickly.

Fern paused.

Frieren's eyes moved once toward the other door.

Fern noticed.

She did not ask.

Fern was here.

Safe.

Real.

And the room had begun losing someone else while Frieren was still learning how to be relieved.
