## Correction

S1E11 is where the Frieren/Mau separation arc visibly begins, rather than waiting until E12 to introduce it from scratch.

After the Frieren/Fern reunion, Frieren takes Fern to the room Frieren has been sharing in practice with Mau. By this point Mau has already understood the emotional importance of Fern's return and has started quietly giving them space.

When Frieren enters the room with Fern, she notices that Mau's belongings are no longer there / have already been moved out or removed from the shared space. The change should be visually obvious to Frieren because Mau had effectively been living with her despite technically having his own assigned room.

Frieren does **not** confront Mau or explain her reaction. She keeps the hurt to herself.

Fern may not yet understand the significance of the missing belongings.

Frieren then asks Fern to stay with her, using a simple line such as:

> **Frieren:** "Stay."

This beat runs on two emotional tracks at once:

1. Frieren's post-loss overprotectiveness toward Fern: she cannot tolerate the thought of Fern disappearing again.
2. The first physical sign of the Frieren/Mau misunderstanding: Mau is intentionally giving Frieren and Fern space, while Frieren interprets his absence as the beginning of Mau no longer needing / choosing her.

The arc should therefore progress as:

```text
E11: Mau quietly starts removing himself from Frieren's shared space after Fern returns; Frieren notices but says nothing.
E12: the distance becomes more established in daily life and room use.
E13: Frieren increasingly interprets Mau's independence and other relationships as proof he is fine without her.
E14: the misunderstanding is finally verbalized and resolved.
```

Important continuity:
- Mau technically has his own assigned room, but before Fern's return he mostly lives with Frieren and keeps his everyday belongings in Frieren's room.
- His move away is therefore emotionally meaningful and visually legible.
- Mau's intent is considerate, not rejecting: he believes Fern was Frieren's world and wants to give them space.
- Frieren does not know that intent yet.
