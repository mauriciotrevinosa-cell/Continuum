E11 owns the reunion itself. The closing domestic beat begins the Frieren/Mau separation arc through physical space rather than argument.

# S1E11 — `There You Are`

## Scene 1 — Frieren Moves First

Fern stands at the edge of the settlement.

Behind her are Anko, Maomao and Momo, travel-worn from the road.

In front of her, Frieren has stopped in the middle of an ordinary task.

For Fern, the relief is immediate and almost mundane.

She starts toward Frieren at her normal pace.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren. There you are.

Frieren crosses the remaining distance first.

Not quickly by human standards.

Quickly for Frieren.

Fern has just enough time to look surprised before Frieren wraps both arms around her.

Fern freezes.

Frieren's grip tightens.

Then Fern realizes Frieren is crying.

Not silently enough to hide it.

Fern's own expression changes.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren?

Frieren does not answer.

Fern carefully brings her arms around her.

Mau stands several steps back.

He has heard Fern's name almost every day since he woke in this world.

He has seen Frieren cry over her.

He has watched her stop herself from hoping too much after Yuta arrived.

This moment is not his.

He backs away before anyone asks him to.

Yuta, Bocchi and Rimuru begin emerging from the inn as the commotion reaches them.

Mau turns toward G2 instead.

**MAU**  
Hi.

Momo, who was trying not to stare at the crying elf reunion, looks at him.

**MOMO**  
Hi.

Anko scans the settlement, the inn, the people, and immediately starts filing questions away.

Maomao notices a plant beside the wall.

Mau notices Maomao noticing the plant.

**MAU**  
Don't eat that.

Maomao looks at him.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Thank you.

---

## Scene 2 — Seven Days

Frieren finally loosens her grip enough to look at Fern's face.

She checks her visually as if Fern might still disappear if she stops verifying.

**FERN**  
I'm fine.

Frieren touches Fern's shoulder, arm, hair—small checks disguised badly as affection.

Fern lets her.

**FERN**  
The explosion threw me pretty far away.

Frieren goes still.

**FERN**  
I was trying to get back.

Frieren's face changes again.

**FRIEREN**  
I looked for you for seven days.

Fern blinks.

**FERN**  
...Seven days?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Fern looks genuinely confused.

**FERN**  
But I was only gone for...

She stops because she does not know the exact number of hours.

Frieren answers the unfinished sentence.

**FRIEREN**  
Not for me.

That is the first moment Fern understands the reunion from Frieren's side.

She does not argue chronology yet.

She just holds Frieren again.

A little tighter this time.

---

## Scene 3 — Stark

When the first wave settles, Fern asks the question she had been carrying behind the immediate need to find Frieren.

**FERN**  
Where's Stark?

Frieren's expression tells her before the answer does.

**FRIEREN**  
Not here.

Fern absorbs that.

**FERN**  
Have you seen him?

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Fern looks toward the forest, then at the settlement.

She has now learned two impossible things at once: Frieren experienced far more time than she did, and their world may not be where they are anymore.

Frieren keeps the explanation small because small is all they actually know.

They woke here.

Other people from other places have appeared.

Nobody understands why.

Fern looks at Yuta, Bocchi, Rimuru, then Mau.

Mau gives a tiny awkward wave when she reaches him.

Fern's attention lingers on him because he is the only face that seems already woven into Frieren's immediate life without belonging to her old one.

---

## Scene 4 — The Ornament

Fern's gaze moves upward.

She notices the hair ornament.

The one that should have been with her.

Her expression changes from emotional overload to pure puzzled recognition.

**FERN**  
That's where that was.

Frieren instinctively reaches toward it.

The line breaks the tension enough that Fern almost smiles.

**FRIEREN**  
It came with me.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Apparently.

Frieren starts to remove it.

Fern stops her hand.

**FERN**  
Later.

Frieren lets it remain.

---

## Scene 5 — Mau Meets G2

While Frieren keeps Fern close enough to touch, Mau helps the other three with practical arrival needs.

Water first.

Warm room.

Food.

Momo asks how long they have been living here.

Mau thinks.

**MAU**  
Long enough that the roof mostly works.

Rimuru, passing with cups:

**RIMURU**  
Don't give me credit for `mostly.`

Mau:

**MAU**  
It doesn't leak on the table anymore.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That is not the same as finished.

Maomao accepts water, then immediately asks what medicines they have.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Frieren has plants.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
What kind?

Mau points toward Frieren.

**MAU**  
Ask her later.

Momo looks at Rimuru.

She saw slime residue / heard Yuta's explanation on the way in.

**MOMO**  
So you're actually a slime.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
Yes.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Like a spirit?

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
No.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Alien?

Rimuru pauses.

**RIMURU**  
Also no.

Momo looks unconvinced that he is qualified to know.

---

## Scene 6 — First Meal, Nine Voices

Dinner is the first time all nine sit in one room.

The table is not big enough comfortably.

That problem becomes obvious before anyone says it.

Yuta shifts his chair.

Bocchi makes herself smaller out of habit.

Mau moves a bowl to clear space.

Rimuru says they need a bigger table.

No one responds because three conversations have already started.

Anko notices the way Yuta occasionally reacts to a presence no one else can see.

**ANKO**  
Who's behind you?

Yuta stops chewing.

Mau, without looking up:

**MAU**  
The angel.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
She's not an angel.

Anko's attention sharpens instantly.

**ANKO**  
There is someone there.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Sometimes.

**ANKO**  
That's not an answer.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
It's the answer you're getting during dinner.

Momo is still trying to classify Rimuru.

Maomao leans across the table toward him.

**MAOMAO**  
Do you have blood?

Rimuru stops mid-sentence.

**RIMURU**  
Why?

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
I want to know.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Do not offer her blood.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
I wasn't going to.

Bocchi is trying very hard to follow all conversations and succeeding at none.

Fern, however, keeps noticing something else.

Frieren responds to Mau without the small delays Fern remembers from so many ordinary interactions.

Mau reaches past Frieren for something; Frieren moves before he asks.

Frieren makes a dry comment; Mau answers immediately with a familiar callback.

They have rhythm.

Fern does not interpret it yet.

She simply notices that Frieren is more spontaneous around him than she remembers her being with almost anyone.

---

## Scene 7 — Too Far

The next morning, Fern wakes early and prepares to inspect the surrounding area.

Normal behavior.

She comes downstairs with staff.

Frieren sees it.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Fern stops.

**FERN**  
No?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Stay here.

Fern looks toward the window.

**FERN**  
I'm only checking the area.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Fern:

**FERN**  
I can protect myself.

Frieren's answer is immediate.

**FRIEREN**  
I know.

Fern's frustration rises because that makes the refusal less logical.

**FERN**  
Then why can't I go?

Frieren has no concise answer she can say without saying *because I buried you in my head for seven days.*

**FRIEREN**  
Stay here.

Fern's mouth tightens.

She turns away rather than argue in front of everyone.

Mau sees both sides badly hurting and neither saying the relevant sentence.

---

## Scene 8 — Mau Explains

Fern is outside but only within the inn yard, inspecting supplies with more force than necessary.

Mau brings out a repaired container and sets it nearby.

Fern looks at him.

He does not pretend he wandered out accidentally.

**MAU**  
She thought you were dead.

Fern goes still.

Mau continues arranging containers rather than staring at her.

**MAU**  
For seven days.

Fern:

**FERN**  
I heard her.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Hearing it isn't the same.

Fern looks toward the inn window where Frieren is visible inside.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Give her a little time.

Fern studies him.

**FERN**  
You know her well.

Mau considers.

**MAU**  
I know her now.

The phrasing matters.

Not better than Fern.

Not instead of Fern.

Now.

Fern looks back at Frieren.

Some of what she noticed at dinner begins to connect.

---

## Scene 9 — Provisional Rooms

Rimuru starts allocating temporary sleeping space before E12's larger room project.

Anko chooses something simple with a window and enough surface to write on.

Maomao chooses the room closest to whatever currently passes for medicine storage.

Momo wants a window with a clear view of the sky and gives no explanation beyond:

**MOMO**  
I like looking out.

Bocchi helps carry blankets because she knows where the clean ones are now.

Yuta helps fix a latch.

Frieren notices Fern's room window does not close correctly.

Everything else stops mattering.

**FRIEREN**  
This needs to be fixed tonight.

Rimuru checks the frame.

**RIMURU**  
It's not going to fall out.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Tonight.

Rimuru looks at Fern.

Fern gives him a tiny apologetic look that says this is not negotiable.

**RIMURU**  
Tonight.

---

## Scene 10 — Keep It a Little Longer

Later, alone for a moment, Frieren reaches for the ornament in her hair.

**FRIEREN**  
This is yours.

Fern looks at it.

**FERN**  
I know.

Frieren begins removing it.

Fern stops her.

**FERN**  
Keep it a little longer.

Frieren's hand falls.

**FRIEREN**  
Okay.

Fern does not explain why.

She has begun to understand what the object was doing while she was gone.

---

## Scene 11 — Things Disappearing

Night.

Frieren takes Fern to the room that has functionally been Frieren and Mau's shared room.

Fern notices this before Frieren can explain it.

There are traces of two people everywhere.

Two patterns of books.

Two sets of everyday objects.

Except some of Mau's things are missing.

Frieren notices at the same time.

A space on the shelf where his cup usually sits.

Clothes gone from the chair.

Two grimoires missing from the bedside stack.

The small repair tool he leaves by her books is gone.

The real flower remains by the window for now.

Frieren looks toward the doorway.

Across the corridor, Mau is quietly carrying another small bundle into his nominal room.

He sees them.

He gives Fern a polite nod.

**MAU**  
Good night.

Frieren looks at the things in his arms.

**FRIEREN**  
Good night.

Mau closes his door.

Fern looks around Frieren's room again.

**FERN**  
Was he staying here?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Fern waits for more.

Frieren looks at the empty space his belongings left behind.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Where am I sleeping?

Frieren looks at her immediately.

The answer comes with more force than the question requires.

**FRIEREN**  
Stay.

Fern pauses.

Frieren's eyes move, only briefly, toward Mau's closed door.

Fern notices.

She does not ask.

The room now contains the person Frieren feared losing and the absence of someone she did not realize she was afraid to lose from the space beside her.

**END S1E11.**
