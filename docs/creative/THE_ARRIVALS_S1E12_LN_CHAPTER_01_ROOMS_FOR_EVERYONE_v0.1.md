<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln01-crowded -->

Morning after the first full nine-person night, the common room had become a storage problem with breakfast.

Nine bowls.

Nine pairs of boots.

Blankets drying where maps had once hung.

Maomao had occupied one end of a table with herbs.

Anko was writing in the corner Rimuru usually used for planning.

Momo stood on a chair because crates blocked the lower window.

Rimuru entered.

Stopped.

"We need more rooms."

Mau looked toward the stairs.

"We have rooms."

"We need rooms people can actually use."

"That's different."

From behind a book, Frieren said, "Mostly."

Fern looked at her.

Bocchi bit the inside of her cheek.

She had clearly missed a joke with history.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln01-needs -->

Rimuru abandoned the idea of a meeting before anyone suggested one.

He caught people separately.

Anko wanted a desk, light, a working window and, if possible, a door that closed.

Maomao had already chosen a room near storage and water.

Mau was carrying containers for her when Rimuru entered.

"That side needs to stay dry," Maomao said.

Mau moved a box.

"Why?"

"Poisons."

He stopped.

Maomao pointed toward another shelf.

"Those go there."

Mau set the box down.

"What are those?"

"Different poisons."

Mau looked at the exit.

Maomao handed him another container.

He took it.

Rimuru stood in the doorway.

"Do I want to know?"

Mau and Maomao answered together.

"No."

They looked at each other.

Maomao almost smiled.

Momo's request arrived differently.

"Can we make the common room bigger?"

"Eventually."

"And somewhere high enough to see the sky clearly."

Rimuru looked at her.

"For what?"

"I'm looking for someone."

The answer was quieter than the rest of Momo usually was.

Rimuru did not ask who.

"There's part of the roof we can make safe later."

Momo nodded.

Enough.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln01-fern-wood -->

Fern saw construction beginning and reached for the simplest useful task.

"I'll get more wood."

"No," Frieren said.

Fern stopped.

"Mistress Frieren."

"There's enough for today."

"Then I'll get tomorrow's."

"No."

Yuta, standing nearby, changed direction and found something else to do.

Fern closed her eyes for one second.

Mau crossed the room carrying material.

He slowed.

Did not intervene.

That argument belonged to them.

Knowing when not to help was another skill he had learned.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln01-habitual-door -->

Later, Mau carried a stack of folded cloth upstairs.

He turned toward Frieren's room without thinking.

Reached the doorway.

Frieren and Fern were inside.

Fern sat near the bed sorting supplies.

Frieren had a book open.

Mau stopped.

Not long.

Long enough to remember that he had a room across the corridor now.

He changed direction.

Frieren looked up at the sound of his footsteps moving away.

By the time she reached the doorway, Mau was already gone.

She stood there for a moment.

Then went back inside.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln01-whose-room -->

By afternoon, the phrase *your room* had become unreliable.

Momo asked where Mau slept.

Bocchi pointed toward Frieren's room, then hesitated.

Rimuru pointed toward Mau's nominal room.

Yuta said, "Depends what you mean."

Momo stared.

"That is not how rooms work."

"It's how these rooms work," Bocchi said.

Fern said nothing.

She was beginning to understand that architecture and habit had stopped matching a while ago.

The house was gaining rooms for everyone.

Nobody had decided what those rooms meant.
