<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln02-mau-everywhere -->

Mau spent the day everywhere except where habit used to put him.

Maomao needed containers moved.

Rimuru needed a frame held.

Bocchi needed something heavy removed from the music room.

Yuta called him to inspect a support.

Momo asked him to reach a shelf after deciding the chair was not worth trusting twice.

None of it was avoidance.

There were simply more people.

More tasks.

More directions a day could pull him.

At lunch Fern sat beside Frieren.

Mau ended up across the room between Yuta and Maomao because those were the available places.

Yuta said something.

Mau laughed.

Frieren looked up.

Fern saw her eyes move.

Neither said anything.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln02-book -->

Later Fern found a book on a low shelf in Frieren's room.

Rough notes were tucked between several pages.

Not Frieren's handwriting.

"Is this Mau's?"

Frieren looked.

"Yes."

"I'll take it to him."

"...Leave it."

Too fast.

Fern paused with the book in her hand.

Frieren had already lowered her eyes.

Fern returned the book exactly where it had been.

The shelf held fewer of Mau's things than yesterday.

Apparently not all of them were allowed to disappear.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln02-object -->

That evening Mau stood at the doorway.

He did not enter automatically.

"Sorry."

Frieren looked up.

"For what?"

Mau pointed toward a small everyday object beside the bed.

"I left this."

He stepped inside.

Took it.

Turned to leave.

Frieren watched.

"...You can leave things here."

Mau looked back.

The sentence reached him as courtesy.

You don't have to remove every trace immediately.

"It's okay."

He left with the object.

Frieren continued looking at the doorway.

What she had meant was closer to:

*You belong here.*

She had not said that.

Mau could not hear it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln02-cap -->

The next morning Frieren dressed without thinking about the order.

Book.

Hair.

Outer layer.

Her hand reached toward the familiar place beside a stack of clothes.

Nothing.

Frieren stopped.

Mau's cap usually rested there.

She had taken it often enough that her hand knew the location before she did.

The spot was empty.

Her fingers stayed in the air for one beat.

Then lowered.

No dialogue.

She chose something else.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln02-own-room -->

Rimuru found Mau later in his nominal room.

For the first time, the bed looked usable.

Blanket.

Clothes.

Cup.

The room had stopped being a library with a bed frame.

Rimuru leaned against the doorway.

"I thought this was the library you sleep next to."

"I use it now."

Rimuru heard the word.

*Now.*

He did not ask.

Mau looked at the wall.

"I need more shelves."

"That part hasn't changed."

They measured space.

A room becoming habitable should have felt entirely positive.

It did not.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln02-replacement-fear -->

Mau crossed the common room later with a book.

Frieren and Fern sat close enough to share the same light.

Fern was telling a story from before the Arrival.

A place.

A meal.

Something Heiter had done, perhaps.

Frieren looked settled.

Mau stopped.

*This is what she was missing.*

He was glad.

That part was simple.

He started toward them.

Then the next thought arrived.

*Maybe this is what she needed me for.*

The book stayed in his hand.

Mau turned.

Went upstairs.

Not angry.

Not hurt at Fern.

Afraid of the possibility that his place had existed because hers was empty.
