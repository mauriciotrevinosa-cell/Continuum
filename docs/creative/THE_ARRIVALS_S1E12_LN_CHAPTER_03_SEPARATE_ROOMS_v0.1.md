<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln03-frieren-wants -->

Rimuru caught Frieren alone.

"Everyone has asked for something except you."

"No."

"That's not an answer to what I asked."

Frieren turned a page.

"I don't need anything."

Rimuru looked around.

The room had more empty space than it used to.

Mau's cup was gone.

Clothes gone.

Fewer books.

The flower remained near the window.

Rimuru understood enough not to say Mau's name.

"Okay."

Frieren kept reading.

Her eyes did not move for several seconds.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln03-table-corner -->

Downstairs, Momo attempted architecture by moving furniture.

"This table should go here."

Rimuru entered.

"That blocks the path."

"Only if you walk into the corner."

Bocchi came through carrying folded cloth.

Walked directly into the corner.

"Ow."

Silence.

Momo pointed at her.

"Why?"

Bocchi held her hip.

"I didn't do it on purpose."

Rimuru moved the table.

The common room grew through trial, error and actual bodies using it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln03-good-night -->

Night narrowed the inn.

The common room emptied.

The hallway quieted.

Mau and Frieren reached the stairs at roughly the same time.

Before, they would have walked the same direction.

Tonight Mau stopped at his door.

"Good night."

Frieren took half a beat too long.

"...Good night."

Mau reached for the handle.

Frieren was still looking at him.

He noticed.

"What?"

She knew what she wanted to ask.

Why had he left?

Did he want to?

Would he come back if she asked?

The questions were too large for the hallway.

"Nothing."

Mau nodded.

He went inside.

Fern stood farther down the corridor.

She had seen the entire exchange.

"Mistress Frieren?"

Frieren turned.

"Hm?"

Fern looked at Mau's closed door.

Then at Frieren.

"Nothing."

Frieren narrowed her eyes.

Fern continued toward her room.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e12-ln03-parallel -->

By the end of the day, the house was objectively better.

Anko had a desk.

Maomao had a workroom.

Momo had begun clearing a route toward a future lookout.

Fern's window closed properly.

Bocchi's room worked.

Yuta's room remained sparse because he preferred it that way.

Rimuru could finally find supplies without asking three people.

Mau's room looked lived in.

He sat on the bed with books around him, cup on the shelf.

Across the corridor, Frieren lay awake.

Fern was safe.

Fern was here.

Relief should have filled the room.

Frieren looked toward the empty patch where Mau's things had been.

The flower remained near the window.

In the other room, Mau stared at a page without reading.

He thought Frieren finally had what she had been missing.

Frieren thought Mau had discovered he no longer needed the place beside her.

The house had made room for everyone.

They had used that room to move apart.
