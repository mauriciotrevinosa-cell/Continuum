# S1E12 — `Rooms for Everyone`

## Scene 1 — More People Than Rooms

Morning after the first full G1+G2 night.

The common room is crowded in ways the building was never prepared for.

Nine bowls.

Nine sets of boots.

Blankets drying where maps used to hang.

Maomao has already occupied one end of a table with herbs she found yesterday.

Anko is writing on the corner Rimuru uses for planning.

Momo is standing on a chair trying to see through a high window because somebody put crates in front of the lower one.

Rimuru enters, stops, and takes in the room.

**RIMURU**  
We need more rooms.

Mau looks toward the staircase.

**MAU**  
We have rooms.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
We need rooms people can actually use.

Mau:

**MAU**  
That's different.

Frieren, from her book:

**FRIEREN**  
Mostly.

Fern looks at her.

She has clearly missed several household jokes.

Bocchi tries not to laugh.

---

## Scene 2 — Rimuru Does Not Hold a Meeting

Rimuru learns quickly that a formal planning meeting with nine people would take longer than repairing the inn himself.

Instead he catches people throughout the day.

Anko first.

She is examining one of the rooms with decent daylight.

**RIMURU**  
What do you need?

**ANKO**  
A desk. Light. A window that opens.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That's it?

Anko:

**ANKO**  
A door that closes would be nice.

Rimuru writes that down.

No `office.` No `detective room.` Just a place she can think and write.

---

## Scene 3 — Maomao's Workroom

Maomao has chosen a room close to storage and water access.

She is already mentally dividing the space before Rimuru arrives.

Mau is helping carry containers because he happened to be nearby and Maomao told him where to put them with enough confidence that he obeyed.

**MAOMAO**  
That side needs to stay dry.

Mau moves a box away from the wall.

**MAU**  
Why?

**MAOMAO**  
Poisons.

Mau stops.

Maomao points to another shelf.

**MAOMAO**  
Those go there.

Mau sets the box down.

**MAU**  
What are those?

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Different poisons.

Mau looks toward the door.

For one second he appears to consider leaving the entire room forever.

Maomao hands him another container.

He takes it.

Rimuru enters in time to see this.

**RIMURU**  
Do I want to know?

Mau and Maomao together:

**MAU / MAOMAO**  
No.

They look at each other.

Maomao almost smiles.

---

## Scene 4 — Momo Wants Sky

Momo's request is less practical on paper and more important in context.

**MOMO**  
Can we make the common room bigger?

Rimuru looks around.

**RIMURU**  
Eventually.

Momo points upward.

**MOMO**  
And somewhere high enough to see the sky clearly.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
For what?

Momo's answer comes more quietly than the rest of her personality usually does.

**MOMO**  
I'm looking for someone.

Rimuru waits, but Momo does not explain who.

He does not ask again.

**RIMURU**  
There's part of the roof we can make safe enough to use later.

Momo nods.

That is enough for now.

---

## Scene 5 — Fern Wants to Get Wood

Fern sees the construction starting and tries to make herself useful.

**FERN**  
I'll get more wood.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Yuta, standing nearby, immediately changes direction and finds another task.

Fern watches him retreat.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
There's enough for today.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Then I'll get tomorrow's.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Fern closes her eyes for a second.

Mau, carrying material past them, slows but does not intervene this time.

The disagreement belongs to them.

That is another kind of learning.

---

## Scene 6 — Mau Is Everywhere Else

The day keeps moving.

Mau helps Maomao.

Then Rimuru asks him to hold a frame.

Then Bocchi needs help moving something heavy out of the music room.

Yuta calls him to check a support beam.

Momo asks whether he can reach the upper shelf because she does not want to climb the chair again.

Mau is not avoiding Frieren.

He simply no longer automatically ends up beside her because there are eight other people and a building full of tasks.

Frieren notices this without deciding she has noticed it.

At lunch, Fern sits beside her.

Mau sits across the room between Yuta and Maomao because those were the available seats.

He laughs at something Yuta says.

Frieren looks up from her bowl.

Fern notices Frieren looking.

She says nothing.

---

## Scene 7 — The Book

Later Fern is in Frieren's room—the room whose physical history she is only beginning to understand.

A book on a low shelf has Mau's rough notes tucked between pages.

Fern recognizes the handwriting is not Frieren's.

**FERN**  
Is this Mau's?

Frieren looks.

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Fern picks it up.

**FERN**  
I'll take it to him.

Frieren answers too quickly.

**FRIEREN**  
...Leave it.

Fern pauses with the book in her hand.

Frieren has already looked back down at what she was doing.

Fern returns the book exactly where it was.

She remembers the missing things from the night before.

Another piece connects.

---

## Scene 8 — Mau Comes Back for Something

That evening Mau appears at the room doorway.

He does not enter automatically anymore.

**MAU**  
Sorry.

Frieren looks up.

**FRIEREN**  
For what?

Mau points to a small everyday object still by the bed.

**MAU**  
I left this.

He steps in, takes it, and turns to leave.

Frieren watches the movement.

**FRIEREN**  
...You can leave things here.

Mau looks back.

The sentence means one thing to Frieren and a smaller thing to Mau.

He interprets it as courtesy: *you don't need to clean every trace of yourself out immediately.*

**MAU**  
It's okay.

He leaves with the object.

Frieren remains looking at the doorway.

What she meant was closer to *you belong here.*

She did not say that.

Mau did not hear it.

---

## Scene 9 — Mau's Room Becomes Real

Rimuru finds Mau in his nominal room later, actually making the bed usable.

Not just books now.

A blanket.

Clothes.

A place for the cup he removed from Frieren's room.

Rimuru leans on the doorframe.

**RIMURU**  
I thought this was the library you sleep next to.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I use it now.

Rimuru hears the `now.`

He does not ask why.

Mau looks at the walls.

**MAU**  
I need more shelves.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That part hasn't changed.

They plan another shelf.

The room becoming habitable should be a positive thing.

It feels slightly sad anyway.

---

## Scene 10 — Maybe She Doesn't Need Me

The insecurity does not arrive as a clean thought all at once.

It accumulates.

Mau sees Frieren and Fern at the table, reading separately but close enough to share the light.

Frieren looks calmer than she has since he met her.

Fern says something; Frieren answers with the old familiar ease Mau has only heard described.

Mau is genuinely glad.

*This is what she was missing.*

He starts toward them with a book.

Stops before they notice.

Another thought attaches itself to the first.

*Maybe this is what she needed me for.*

He looks at the book in his hand.

Then turns toward his own room.

No resentment toward Fern.

No anger at Frieren.

Only a quiet fear that his place existed because hers was empty.

---

## Scene 11 — What Does Frieren Want?

Rimuru eventually catches Frieren alone.

**RIMURU**  
Everyone has asked for something except you.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That's not an answer to what I asked.

Frieren turns a page.

**FRIEREN**  
I don't need anything.

Rimuru looks around the room.

There is noticeably more empty space than there was before Fern returned.

Mau's cup gone.

Clothes gone.

Fewer grimoires.

The flower remains by the window.

Rimuru understands some of it but wisely does not say `Mau.`

**RIMURU**  
Okay.

Frieren keeps reading.

Her eyes do not move for several seconds.

---

## Scene 12 — The Table Corner

Momo pushes for a larger common-room layout by moving furniture herself before anyone approves the plan.

**MOMO**  
This table should go here.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That blocks the path.

Momo:

**MOMO**  
Only if you walk into the corner.

Bocchi enters carrying folded cloth.

She walks directly into the corner.

**BOCCHI**  
Ow.

Silence.

Momo points at her as if Bocchi has betrayed the argument.

**MOMO**  
Why?

Bocchi, holding her hip:

**BOCCHI**  
I didn't do it on purpose.

Rimuru moves the table.

The common room grows through trial, error, and people actually living in it.

---

## Scene 13 — Good Night

Night.

The hallway is quieter than the common room ever is now.

Mau and Frieren reach the stairs at roughly the same time.

For several episodes this would have meant walking to the same room without thinking.

Tonight Mau stops at his own door.

**MAU**  
Good night.

Frieren takes half a beat too long.

**FRIEREN**  
...Good night.

Mau reaches for his door.

Frieren is still looking at him.

Mau notices.

**MAU**  
What?

Frieren has an answer.

She does not know how to say it.

**FRIEREN**  
Nothing.

Mau nods.

He goes inside.

Fern is farther down the hallway.

She saw the whole exchange.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren?

Frieren turns.

**FRIEREN**  
Hm?

Fern looks at Mau's closed door, then at Frieren.

**FERN**  
Nothing.

Frieren recognizes her own answer coming back at her.

Fern continues to her room.

---

## Scene 14 — Rooms for Everyone

The inn is objectively better by the end of the episode.

Anko has a desk and light.

Maomao has a workroom that can safely separate medicines from dangerous materials.

Momo has begun clearing a route toward a future high lookout and the common space is less cramped.

Fern has a repaired window.

Bocchi's room works.

Yuta's room remains sparse because that is what he chooses.

Rimuru has organized storage enough that he can find things.

Mau's own room is truly occupied for the first time.

He sits on the bed with grimoires around him, his cup on the new shelf, the room looking less like overflow.

Across the corridor, Frieren lies awake with Fern nearby.

Fern is safe.

Fern is here.

Frieren should feel only relief.

Her eyes move to the empty patch where Mau's things used to be.

The flower by the window is one of the few remaining pieces of him in the room.

Mau, in his room, thinks Frieren has what she was missing.

Frieren, in hers, thinks Mau has discovered he does not need to come back.

The house has made room for everyone.

They have accidentally used that room to move apart.

**END S1E12.**
