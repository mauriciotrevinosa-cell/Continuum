## Approval

S1E12 `Rooms for Everyone` is approved as presented, with one additional emotional layer for Mau.

## Mau / Frieren distance arc clarification

The episode still begins from Mau sincerely giving Frieren and Fern space after Fern's return. However, as E12 progresses, that choice begins to mix with insecurity and early jealousy.

Mau starts wondering whether Fern's return means Frieren no longer needs him in the way she did before. He does not fully understand or verbalize this feeling yet. The jealousy is not locked as romantic; it can be read as attachment insecurity, fear of replacement, or the beginnings of romantic jealousy depending on later development.

This means Mau's distance evolves from:

`I should give them space.`

into something closer to:

`Maybe she doesn't need me anymore now that Fern is back.`

He continues behaving as if he is doing the right thing, and he does not blame Fern or Frieren.

Frieren simultaneously interprets Mau's growing independence and absence as evidence that he may no longer need her.

This creates the central mutual misunderstanding that E13 and E14 will deepen and resolve.
