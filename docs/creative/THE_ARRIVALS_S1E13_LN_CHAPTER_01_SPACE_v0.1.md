<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln01-morning -->

Mau came downstairs with Yuta.

They were discussing a loose support near the rear wall.

Frieren sat with Fern.

Mau looked over.

Frieren did too.

"Morning."

"Morning."

Mau kept walking.

Nothing about the exchange was wrong.

That was the problem.

Weeks earlier he would have stopped beside her without deciding to.

Fern watched Frieren's eyes follow him.

Frieren noticed.

"What?"

"Nothing."

Frieren narrowed her eyes.

Fern had become dangerous with that word.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln01-different-with-him -->

Later, Frieren realized she had left a small item outside.

Mau found it first.

"You left this again."

"I knew where it was."

"It was outside."

"I knew it was outside."

Frieren reached for it.

Mau lifted it just beyond her hand.

Her expression changed immediately.

"Mau."

He raised it slightly higher.

"Give it back."

Fern, carrying folded cloth nearby, stopped.

Mau finally returned the item.

Frieren took it with a look that promised some small revenge.

He walked away smiling.

Fern looked at her.

"...You're different with him."

Frieren turned.

"No, I'm not."

Fern did not argue.

She did not need to.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln01-this-is-what-she-missed -->

Mau entered later with a book under one arm.

Frieren and Fern sat together.

Fern was telling her something from before.

A place.

Food.

A travel complaint that had survived long enough to become funny.

Frieren looked calm.

Mau stopped.

*This is what she was missing.*

He was glad.

Then:

*Maybe this is what she needed me for.*

The book in his hand suddenly had no destination.

He turned before either noticed.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln01-overprotection -->

Fern prepared to inspect a nearby road.

Frieren closed her book.

"I'll come."

"You don't need to."

"I know."

Fern looked at her.

"Then why are you coming?"

"I want to."

Technically true.

Not complete.

Mau crossed the room while they gathered things.

He heard enough to know Frieren was leaving with Fern.

He did not ask to join.

He did not joke.

He kept walking.

Frieren noticed.

Another piece joined the conclusion she was building.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln01-old-habits -->

Later, Frieren reached for a second cup while preparing something warm.

Her hand stopped.

Mau was not coming to this room.

She returned the cup.

Across the corridor that evening, Mau opened a drawer looking for something he had once kept near Frieren's books.

He remembered.

Closed the drawer.

Both habits ended before either person saw them.

The hallway kept the evidence.
