<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln02-borrow-the-angel -->

Yuta repaired a strap outside when Mau found him.

Mau sat on the low wall.

"Can I borrow the angel?"

Yuta stopped.

"...You cannot borrow Rika."

"Can she come with me?"

"That's different."

"Okay. Can she?"

Yuta studied him.

"Where are you going?"

"My plant."

Yuta understood enough.

"Fine. But stop calling her an angel."

Mau looked past him toward where he believed Rika sometimes stood.

"Okay."

"You don't mean that."

"No."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln02-failed-flower -->

The clearing remained ordinary.

Mau knelt near the surviving flower.

Rika stayed far enough away not to intrude, close enough that Yuta's promise meant something.

Mau studied the flower.

Shape.

Petals.

Stem.

He closed his eyes.

Tried again.

Nothing.

Changed the position of his hand.

Nothing.

Frustration came faster this time.

`...form...`

Mau opened his eyes.

"That isn't helpful."

He did not know who he was addressing.

No answer.

One last attempt.

Grass remained grass.

The flower remained the only flower.

Mau sat back.

He still could not explain why failure here bothered him more than failing to repair a table or identify a plant.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln02-monster -->

On the return route, something small moved through brush.

Weak.

Local.

The kind of creature Rika did not immediately interpret as meaningful danger.

Mau saw it late.

His instincts were still bad.

Instead of moving away, he stepped sideways into its path.

The creature lunged.

Mau raised his arm.

Claws caught his forearm.

A shallow tear.

Real.

Mau jerked back.

Rika moved.

The creature never received a second chance.

Silence returned too quickly.

Mau looked at the blood running toward his wrist.

"...Ow."

Rika's presence moved closer.

Offended.

"I moved."

Silence.

"The wrong way."

He started home.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln02-maomao -->

Mau tried the side door.

Maomao crossed the hall carrying a basket.

She saw blood before his face.

"What happened to your arm?"

Mau looked down as if he had forgotten the problem was visible.

"Something scratched me."

Maomao set the basket down.

"Sit."

"It's fine."

"Sit."

Mau sat.

She cleaned the wound with the efficient lack of sympathy reserved for people who had already been told to sit.

Mau winced.

"That hurts more than the scratch."

"Good."

"How is that good?"

"Now you'll remember not to get scratched."

Mau laughed once.

Maomao looked up.

"What?"

"Nothing."

"You people say that too much."

That made him laugh again.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln02-blood-cloth -->

After Mau left, Maomao did not immediately discard the bloodied cloth.

She looked at it.

Then toward the door.

Later she found Yuta.

"The creature was weak?"

Yuta looked up.

"Rika said it was."

Maomao held the cloth by one clean corner.

"The wound is small."

"Yeah."

"The amount and appearance of this bothered me."

Yuta's expression changed.

"Why?"

Maomao shook her head.

"I don't know yet."

No diagnosis.

No invented disease.

Only a mismatch.

A weak source.

A minor wound.

A result that looked more concerning than Maomao expected from a healthy young adult.

Yuta looked toward the stairs.

"We watch it."

"Obviously."

The question remained private.
