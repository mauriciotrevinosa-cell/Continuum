<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-wrong-image-frieren -->

Frieren returned with Fern.

The first thing she noticed was that Mau was not in the common room.

She disliked noticing that.

She walked toward the side rooms.

Through a partly open door she saw Maomao and Mau.

Maomao held his forearm with both hands while finishing the wrap.

The injury itself was hidden from Frieren's angle.

Mau was laughing.

Relaxed.

Cared for.

Fine.

Frieren stopped.

*He's fine.*

Then:

*He really doesn't need me.*

The thought hurt more than it had any right to.

Frieren turned before either saw her.

Fern, behind her, looked through the doorway.

Caught only part of the scene.

Then watched Frieren change direction.

Another piece.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-wrong-image-mau -->

Later, Mau crossed the common room.

The wound was hidden beneath his sleeve.

Frieren stood near a window with Fern.

Fern showed her something found along the road.

Frieren smiled.

Mau stopped.

He thought about going over.

*She looks happier now.*

A good thing.

Then:

*Maybe I was temporary.*

He turned and went upstairs before Frieren saw him.

The two wrong conclusions had become almost perfect mirrors.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-old-door -->

That evening Mau walked toward Frieren's room by habit.

He reached the door.

Stopped.

Across the wood, Frieren heard footsteps.

Then silence.

She looked up.

Mau did not knock.

A few seconds later, the footsteps moved away.

Frieren sat still.

She knew the rhythm well enough to know who it had been.

She did not follow.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-frieren-at-mau-door -->

Later still, Frieren crossed the corridor.

This time deliberately.

Mau's door was closed.

She raised a hand.

Voices came from inside.

Yuta.

Mau.

Natural conversation.

Something about a shelf.

Then Mau laughed.

Frieren's hand remained near the door.

He was fine.

Talking.

Comfortable.

She lowered it.

Walked away.

Mau never knew she had come.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-night-hallway -->

Near bedtime, Mau reached his room.

Frieren stood behind him.

"Good night."

"Good night."

His hand touched the door.

"Mau."

He turned.

"Hm?"

Frieren had too many questions.

Where had he gone?

Why Maomao?

Why had he stopped coming back?

Did he want to?

She remembered him laughing.

"...Nothing."

Mau waited half a beat.

"Okay."

He went inside.

To Mau, the question had not mattered.

To Frieren, his leaving confirmed where he wanted to be.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-fern-asks -->

Fern found Frieren in the hallway.

They returned to Frieren's room.

Only then did Fern ask:

"Did Mau always sleep somewhere else?"

Frieren looked at her.

"No."

"He has his own room."

"Yes."

Fern held her gaze.

"That wasn't what I asked."

Frieren did not answer.

Fern finally understood.

The arrangement had changed because she returned.

Not because Frieren wanted it to.

And neither Frieren nor Mau had apparently said the relevant thing out loud.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e13-ln03-parallel-rooms -->

Mau sat alone in his now-functional room.

Books.

Blanket.

Cup.

A room that looked permanent.

He stared at a grimoire without reading.

Across the corridor, Frieren lay awake.

The real flower remained by the window.

Her hand reached once toward the place where Mau's cap used to be.

Stopped.

Both rooms were comfortable.

Both people were safe.

Both believed they were respecting the other's preference.

The hallway had never seemed longer.
