# S1E13 — `Space`

## Scene 1 — Morning

The common room is already active when Mau comes downstairs with Yuta.

They are discussing a loose support near the back wall.

Frieren is at the table with Fern.

Mau looks over.

Frieren does too.

**MAU**  
Morning.

**FRIEREN**  
Morning.

Mau keeps walking with Yuta.

Nothing is wrong with the exchange.

That is what makes it feel wrong.

A few weeks earlier he would have stopped beside her without thinking.

Fern watches Frieren's eyes follow Mau across the room.

Frieren notices Fern watching her.

**FRIEREN**  
What?

Fern:

**FERN**  
Nothing.

Frieren narrows her eyes slightly.

Fern has become dangerous with that word.

---

## Scene 2 — Different With Him

Later, Frieren realizes she left a small item outside near the work area.

Mau finds it first.

He holds it up as she approaches.

**MAU**  
You left this again.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
I knew where it was.

Mau:

**MAU**  
It was outside.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
I knew it was outside.

Mau moves it just out of reach when she reaches for it.

Frieren's response is immediate and far more expressive than the detached elf Fern remembers from older travels.

**FRIEREN**  
Mau.

He raises it a little higher.

**FRIEREN**  
Give it back.

Fern, carrying folded cloth nearby, stops.

Mau finally gives it to her.

Frieren takes it with a look that promises a minor revenge later.

Mau leaves smiling.

Fern looks at Frieren.

**FERN**  
...You're different with him.

Frieren turns.

**FRIEREN**  
No, I'm not.

Fern says nothing.

The denial is not persuasive.

---

## Scene 3 — This Is What She Was Missing

Mau enters the common room later with a book under one arm.

Frieren and Fern are sitting together.

Fern is talking about something from before the Arrival—a place, a meal, a habit from their travels.

Frieren's expression is quiet but settled.

Mau stops at the edge of the room.

*This is what she was missing.*

He is glad Fern is here.

That truth is not complicated.

The next thought is.

*Maybe this is what she needed me for.*

He looks at the book he had intended to show Frieren.

He turns away before either sees him.

---

## Scene 4 — Still Too Protective

Fern prepares to travel to a nearby section of road to inspect tracks and gather information.

Frieren closes her book immediately.

**FRIEREN**  
I'll come.

Fern:

**FERN**  
You don't need to.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
I know.

Fern looks at her.

**FERN**  
Then why are you coming?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
I want to.

Fern recognizes the answer as technically honest and practically incomplete.

Mau passes through the room while they are gathering things.

He hears enough to know Frieren is going with Fern.

He does not make a joke or ask to join.

He simply keeps walking.

Frieren notices.

That absence becomes another piece of her own wrong conclusion.

---

## Scene 5 — Borrow the Angel

Yuta is outside repairing a strap when Mau finds him.

Mau sits on the low wall nearby.

**MAU**  
Can I borrow the angel?

Yuta stops working.

**YUTA**  
...You cannot borrow Rika.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Can she come with me?

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
That's different.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Okay. Can she?

Yuta studies him.

**YUTA**  
Where are you going?

Mau:

**MAU**  
My plant.

Yuta now understands why Mau wants something strong nearby without Frieren.

He does not ask why Frieren is not going.

**YUTA**  
Fine. But stop calling her an angel.

Mau looks past him, toward an empty patch of air where he believes Rika sometimes is.

**MAU**  
Okay.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
You don't mean that.

Mau:

**MAU**  
No.

---

## Scene 6 — Another Failed Flower

The E1 clearing is quiet.

Mau kneels in the same area as before.

Rika is present at a distance—protective, unobtrusive, not interacting unless needed.

Mau studies one surviving flower.

He watches its shape.

Closes his eyes.

Tries again.

Nothing.

His frustration is more visible this time.

He changes hand position.

Nothing.

A fragment scratches through his awareness.

`...form...`

No instruction follows.

Mau opens his eyes.

**MAU**  
That isn't helpful.

He is not even sure whom he is speaking to.

He tries one final time.

The grass remains grass.

The flower remains the only flower.

Mau exhales and sits back.

He still does not understand why this matters enough to keep returning.

---

## Scene 7 — The Scratch

On the way back, a weak local monster moves out of brush near the road.

It is small enough that Rika does not immediately treat it as a serious threat.

Mau sees it.

Too late.

His instincts are still poor.

He steps the wrong direction instead of away.

The creature lunges.

Mau raises an arm reflexively.

Claws catch his forearm.

A shallow but real tear.

Mau jerks back.

Rika is there immediately afterward.

The monster does not get a second attack.

Silence returns almost absurdly fast.

Mau looks at his arm.

Blood runs down toward his wrist.

**MAU**  
...Ow.

Rika's presence shifts close, almost offended at the entire concept of him being injured by something so weak.

Mau looks toward her.

**MAU**  
I moved.

Rika's silence communicates enough.

**MAU**  
The wrong way.

He starts home.

---

## Scene 8 — Maomao Catches Him

Mau tries to enter through the side door quietly.

This would work if Maomao were almost anyone else.

She is crossing the hall with a basket when she sees the blood before she sees his face.

**MAOMAO**  
What happened to your arm?

Mau looks down as if he forgot it was visible.

**MAU**  
Something scratched me.

Maomao sets the basket down.

**MAOMAO**  
Sit.

Mau:

**MAU**  
It's fine.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Sit.

Mau sits.

She cleans the wound with the brisk focus of someone who does not care whether the patient thinks it hurts enough to count.

Mau winces.

**MAU**  
That hurts more than the scratch.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Good.

Mau:

**MAU**  
How is that good?

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
Now you'll remember not to get scratched.

Mau laughs once despite himself.

Maomao looks up.

**MAOMAO**  
What?

Mau:

**MAU**  
Nothing.

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
You people say that too much.

That makes him laugh again.

---

## Scene 9 — Frieren Sees the Wrong Image

Frieren returns from the road with Fern.

She notices Mau is not in the common room.

The realization that she was looking for him bothers her enough that she does not examine it.

She walks toward the side rooms.

From the end of the hall she sees Maomao and Mau through a partially open doorway.

Maomao is holding his forearm with both hands while wrapping it.

From Frieren's angle, the wound is hidden.

Mau is laughing.

He looks relaxed.

Cared for.

Fine.

Frieren stops.

The immediate thought is not *Maomao.*

It is:

*He's fine.*

Then:

*He really doesn't need me.*

Frieren turns away before they notice her.

Fern, behind her, sees Frieren change direction.

She looks toward the doorway and catches only enough to know Mau is inside with Maomao.

Fern looks back at Frieren.

Another piece.

---

## Scene 10 — Mau Sees the Wrong Image Too

Later, with the wound covered, Mau crosses the common room.

Frieren and Fern are by the window.

Fern is showing Frieren something she found on the road.

Frieren smiles faintly.

Mau stops.

He considers going over.

His wrapped arm is hidden under his sleeve.

*She looks happier now.*

He turns away.

*Maybe I was temporary.*

He goes upstairs before Frieren sees him.

The two wrong conclusions now mirror each other almost perfectly.

---

## Scene 11 — Night Hallway

Night.

Mau is heading toward his room.

Frieren is behind him.

**MAU**  
Good night.

**FRIEREN**  
Good night.

Mau reaches his door.

**FRIEREN**  
Mau.

He turns.

**MAU**  
Hm?

Frieren has several possible questions.

Where were you?

Why were you with Maomao?

Why did you stop coming back?

Do you want to come back?

Instead she remembers him laughing in the side room.

**FRIEREN**  
...Nothing.

Mau waits half a beat.

**MAU**  
Okay.

He enters his room.

To Mau, she changed her mind because the question was not important.

To Frieren, he left because that was where he wanted to be.

Both are wrong.

---

## Scene 12 — Fern Finally Asks

Fern finds Frieren still in the hallway after Mau's door closes.

They walk back toward Frieren's room.

Fern waits until they are inside.

Then:

**FERN**  
Did Mau always sleep somewhere else?

Frieren looks at her.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Fern:

**FERN**  
He has his own room.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Fern looks directly at her.

**FERN**  
That wasn't what I asked.

Frieren does not answer.

Fern finally understands that the arrangement changed because she returned, but not because Frieren wanted it to.

She also understands that neither of them is saying what they mean.

---

## Scene 13 — Space

Mau sits alone in his now-functional room.

Books on shelves.

Blanket on bed.

His cup.

The room no longer looks temporary.

That should feel like progress.

He looks at his bandaged arm.

Then at a grimoire open to a page he is not reading.

Across the corridor, Frieren lies in her room with Fern nearby.

The real flower from E9 remains by the window.

The space where Mau's everyday things once mixed with hers is still visible if one knows where to look.

Both rooms are comfortable.

Both people are safe.

Both believe they are respecting what the other wants.

The hallway between them has never seemed longer.

**END S1E13.**
