## Core approved emotional logic

- The Frieren/Mau separation seeded in E11 and deepened in E12 is now actively hurting both of them.
- Mau’s original intention was to give Frieren and Fern space after Fern returned. By E13, that has evolved into insecurity and jealousy: he starts wondering whether Frieren only needed him while Fern was gone and whether he was temporary.
- Mau is not angry at Fern and does not blame her. His jealousy is about the place Fern has in Frieren’s life and his fear of being replaced or no longer needed.
- Frieren simultaneously interprets Mau’s distance as evidence that he no longer needs her.
- Fern begins noticing that Frieren is more spontaneous/reactive around Mau than she remembers, and starts realizing there is a deeper bond she does not yet fully understand.

## Approved beats

1. Mau and Frieren’s ordinary rhythm is visibly different; interactions are still warm, but Mau no longer automatically stays with her.
2. Fern notices Frieren reacts differently around Mau.
3. Mau sees Frieren and Fern together and begins wondering whether Frieren’s old need for him has ended now that Fern is back.
4. Frieren remains overprotective of Fern after believing her dead.
5. Mau privately returns to the flower site and asks Yuta if he can “borrow the angel,” meaning Rika.
6. Mau’s reconstruction attempt still fails; no successful spell or power reveal.
7. A weak monster scratches Mau because Rika initially reads the situation as trivial and expects Mau to react; intervention comes slightly late.
8. Maomao treats Mau’s minor but real injury; they laugh/talk naturally while she works.
9. Frieren sees only part of this from a distance and misreads the image emotionally: Mau looks fine, cared for, happy, and therefore perhaps no longer needs her.
10. Mau later sees Frieren with Fern and similarly decides not to approach, thinking Frieren looks happier now and perhaps he was temporary.
11. A tiny hallway exchange fails: Frieren calls “Mau,” then says “Nothing,” and Mau leaves. Both interpret the moment incorrectly.
12. Fern finally asks whether Mau always slept elsewhere. Frieren answers “No,” giving Fern a clearer sense of the rupture.
13. Closing image: Mau alone in his now-functional room; Frieren in hers looking at the absence where his things used to be. Both believe separation is the correct thing to do.

## Canon guardrails

- Do not simplify this into possessive romance.
- Mau’s jealousy is layered with attachment, replacement fear, dependency, and uncertainty about his place in Frieren’s life.
- Frieren’s reaction to Maomao is not “Maomao stole him”; it is “he is fine without me.”
- The flower reconstruction remains unsuccessful in E13.
- This episode directly sets up E14 “The Lake,” where the mismatched assumptions finally get spoken aloud and corrected.
