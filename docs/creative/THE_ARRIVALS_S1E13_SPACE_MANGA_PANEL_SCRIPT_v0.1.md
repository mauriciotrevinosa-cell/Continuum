## Production rule

This document does not add new story beats. It translates the approved E13 continuous draft into pages and panels. Page/panel counts, exact compositions and silent beats can still expand or contract during rough-manga review if visual pacing demands it.

The story determines manga length. There is no target quota.

## Approved natural chapter cuts

- **Chapter 1 — pp. 1–18:** ordinary distance → Frieren/Mau habitual chemistry still exists → Fern notices → Frieren remains overprotective and Mau quietly steps out of the pattern.
- **Chapter 2 — pp. 19–38:** Mau borrows Rika → flower reconstruction fails → weak monster scratches him → Maomao treats him.
- **Chapter 3 — pp. 39–56:** Frieren and Mau each see the wrong image → failed hallway conversation → Fern identifies the real sleeping-arrangement problem → parallel rooms / title image.

## Visual continuity / QC guardrails

- Mau is not angry with Fern. His insecurity is about being temporary/replaced, not resentment.
- Frieren is not jealous of Maomao as a romantic rival. The Maomao image communicates `he is fine / cared for without me`, not possessiveness.
- The scratch is shallow and medically minor. It matters because Mau reacted late, not because the monster was dangerous.
- Rika kills the weak monster immediately after the hit; this is not a fight scene.
- `...form...` is optional visible Noise text and must remain fragmentary, non-UI, non-instructional.
- Mau still cannot reconstruct the E1 flower magic.
- Frieren/Fern overprotection remains unresolved here; E14 repairs the Mau/Frieren misunderstanding, not this episode.
- Mau's wrapped arm should be hidden from Frieren's initial hallway angle in Scene 9.
- Mau/Frieren physical distance should be visualized repeatedly through doorframes, hallways, table positions and interrupted habits.
- E9 real flower remains in Frieren's room and is visible in the final parallel-room sequence.
- No romance label/confession is introduced.
