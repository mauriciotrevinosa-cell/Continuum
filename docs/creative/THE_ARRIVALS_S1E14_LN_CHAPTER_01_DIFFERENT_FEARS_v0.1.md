<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln01-leaving -->

Mau came downstairs prepared to leave.

Small bag.

Water.

The old folded cloth he used when he went toward the first clearing.

Frieren noticed immediately.

"I'll be back later."

"Where are you going?"

Mau hesitated only because the answer had become a household joke.

"To check on my plant."

Frieren closed her book.

Mau waited.

No question followed.

He left.

The door closed.

Frieren opened the book again.

Read one line.

Turned a page.

Stopped.

She had no idea what she had just read.

A small irritation moved through her face.

At the book.

At Mau.

At herself.

Frieren closed it.

Fern noticed.

"Are you going somewhere?"

"No."

Frieren stood and walked toward the door.

Fern looked at the book.

Then at the door Mau had used.

She waited three seconds.

Then followed Frieren.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln01-clearing -->

Mau reached the clearing.

The place had become ordinary again.

That was part of why he returned.

No catastrophe.

No answer.

No successful magic.

Just grass, trees and the surviving flower.

He crouched.

A branch shifted behind him.

"You followed me."

Frieren stepped into the clearing.

"Yes."

Mau looked back.

"...Why?"

Frieren looked around instead of answering.

"I'm surprised you remembered this place."

"Yeah."

He looked toward the flower.

"It's peaceful here."

Farther back, Fern stopped.

She could see enough.

Frieren had not followed because she thought Mau was in danger.

That distinction mattered.

Frieren watched Mau for another moment.

"We need to talk."

"Okay."

The clearing held too much beginning inside it.

Frieren turned toward the path.

"Come on."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln01-lake -->

They walked to the lake.

Fern followed far enough behind that Mau never noticed her.

Frieren probably could have, if she had tried.

She did not.

At the shore Mau sat.

Frieren remained standing one second longer.

Then sat beside him.

A gap between them.

Neither knew where to begin.

Mau tried first.

"Sorry."

"It's okay."

Too fast.

Mau looked at her.

"...It is?"

"Things change."

He turned toward the water.

The sentence confirmed exactly the wrong thing.

"I think I'm a little jealous."

Frieren went still.

Not romance.

Dependence.

That was the shape she gave the word.

"It's okay not to depend on one person forever."

Mau turned slowly.

"What?"

Frieren frowned.

"What?"

"What are you talking about?"

"...What are you talking about?"

For the first time in days, the misunderstanding became visible enough that neither could continue pretending the conversation made sense.
