<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln02-jealous -->

Mau rubbed the back of his neck.

"Maybe jealous isn't the right word."

Frieren waited.

"I'm happy Fern is here."

"I know."

"I know how much you missed her."

Frieren looked down.

"That's why I left."

Her eyes returned to him.

"Left?"

Mau realized the word was too large.

"Your room."

Pause.

"I noticed."

That landed harder than he expected.

"At first I was just giving you space."

Frieren said nothing.

"You had Fern back. I thought you should have time with her."

Mau watched the lake.

Then said the part he had avoided even in his own head.

"Then I started thinking maybe you didn't need me anymore."

Frieren's expression changed.

"Fern came back. You were happy."

The next sentence took longer.

"Maybe I was just there because she wasn't."

Silence expanded around it.

Water moved against the shore.

Frieren did not answer quickly.

That helped.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln02-frieren-fear -->

Finally:

"I thought you didn't need me."

Mau turned fully.

"Why?"

Frieren almost looked annoyed by the question.

"You stopped coming back."

"Because Fern was there."

"I know that now."

He waited.

Frieren looked away.

"You were with Yuta. Bocchi. Rimuru. Maomao."

"...Yes?"

"You were fine."

"I was fine."

Her voice became very small.

"Exactly."

Mau stopped.

The shape finally appeared.

"You thought because I was okay without you, I didn't want you around?"

Frieren did not answer.

She did not need to.

Mau looked at the lake.

"That's dumb."

Frieren looked at him.

"You thought Fern replacing you made sense."

Mau considered.

"...Okay. We're both dumb."

For half a second neither moved.

Then Frieren laughed.

Very quietly.

Mau did too.

The pressure broke just enough to breathe.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln02-maomao -->

After the laugh disappeared, Frieren remembered one image.

"Maomao was holding your hand."

Mau turned.

Then laughed again.

Frieren's eyes narrowed.

"What?"

Still smiling, Mau pulled up his sleeve.

The healing scratch showed beneath the edge.

"She wasn't holding my hand."

Frieren's embarrassment disappeared.

"Wait."

She caught his arm.

"You were hurt?"

"A little."

"How?"

"Monster."

Her face hardened.

"What monster?"

"It's dead."

"That wasn't the question."

Mau smiled despite himself.

There it was.

The old rhythm.

Not completely old.

Recovered.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln02-most-important -->

Frieren was still holding his arm when Mau became serious.

"Frieren."

She looked at him.

He did not search for a larger word.

"I hope you know you're the most important person to me."

Frieren went completely still.

No category followed.

No confession.

Mau was not defining what she was.

He was correcting the fear that becoming capable had made her unnecessary.

Frieren released his arm slowly.

Then shifted closer.

Shoulder against his.

A moment later her head rested on him.

Mau looked surprised for one second.

Then relaxed.

The sun had dropped lower.

Gold spread across the lake.

Frieren's retained ornament caught the light near her hair.

Fern watched from the path.

The object belonged to Fern's return.

The person Frieren leaned against belonged to a different part of her life.

Neither replaced the other.

Fern understood enough.

She turned back toward the inn.

At the lake, Frieren and Mau stayed quiet.

For once neither tried to fix the silence.
