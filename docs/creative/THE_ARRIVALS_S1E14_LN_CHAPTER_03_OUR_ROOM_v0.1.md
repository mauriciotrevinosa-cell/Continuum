<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln03-fern-bocchi -->

Bocchi lingered in the hallway that evening.

Fern found her.

"Are you looking for something?"

Bocchi reacted as though she had been caught breaking into a vault.

"No."

Fern waited.

Bocchi folded.

"I mean—maybe. Not something. I was just wondering if everyone was sleeping in the same places tonight. Not because I care. I mean I care normally. Not in a dangerous way."

Fern let the sentence finish.

"Do you have trouble sleeping alone?"

Bocchi froze.

"Who told you?"

"No one."

Bocchi looked personally offended by observation.

Fern opened her room.

"You can stay with me."

Bocchi stared.

"...Really?"

"Yes."

She attempted a normal response.

Failed.

"Okay."

Pause.

"Thank you."

Fern nodded.

No ceremony.

A new relationship began because there was space in a room.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln03-return-walk -->

Mau and Frieren returned near dusk.

Nothing dramatic in their posture announced the repair.

The difference lived in small things.

Mau said Frieren was walking too slowly.

Frieren handed him her bag.

"If you have that much energy."

He took it without arguing.

At the inn, most people noticed only that they were talking naturally again.

Fern noticed more.

She said nothing.

After dinner Mau followed the habit built during the separation.

He started toward his own room.

Frieren watched.

Did not stop him.

Not yet.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln03-kicked-out -->

Frieren opened the room she expected to share with Fern.

Bocchi sat on the bed with a blanket.

Fern looked up.

Frieren stopped.

"What are you doing?"

"Bocchi is staying with me tonight."

Frieren looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi looked ready to apologize for occupying oxygen.

Frieren spared her.

"Where am I sleeping?"

Fern remained neutral.

"You have a room."

"...Yes."

"Then I don't understand the question."

Frieren stared.

Fern got into bed.

"Good night, Mistress Frieren."

Bocchi recognized strategy happening above her pay grade and stayed silent.

Frieren closed the door.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln03-fern-kicked-me-out -->

Mau had a book open when someone knocked.

He opened the door.

Frieren stood outside.

"Fern kicked me out."

Mau blinked.

"...Of your room?"

Frieren looked away.

"...Apparently."

Mau stepped aside.

She entered.

The nominal room looked real now.

His books.

Cup.

Blanket.

The belongings he had moved during the misunderstanding.

Frieren looked around.

The black-and-orange McLaren hoodie was folded near the foot of the bed.

She picked it up.

"What are you doing?"

"Taking this."

"That's mine."

"I know."

She walked into the corridor.

Mau followed.

"Then why are you taking it?"

"Because it belongs in my room."

Mau slowed.

The object did not matter nearly as much as the correction inside the sentence.

"...Our room?"

Frieren stopped.

A beat.

"...Yes."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln03-restoration -->

They moved only a few things that night.

Cup.

Books.

The McLaren cap returned to the place beside Frieren's books.

The red Ferrari shirt went back over the familiar chair.

The McLaren hoodie never made it onto a shelf. Frieren kept it.

Enough.

The real flower remained near the window.

Mau set another small object beside it.

Frieren watched.

Pretended she had not.

Mau noticed.

"You know this could have been easier."

"You could have said something."

"You could have said something."

They looked at each other.

No defense existed.

Mau sat beside her.

No embrace.

No declaration.

The room became shared again because both finally understood that the other wanted it shared.

Across the hall, Fern turned out the light while Bocchi thanked her for the fourth time.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e14-ln03-next-morning -->

The next morning, Frieren came downstairs wearing Mau's black-and-orange McLaren hoodie.

The household noticed.

Bocchi looked up.

Fern looked up.

Yuta looked up.

Rimuru looked up.

Momo, who had no context for the full history but enough social awareness to understand she had missed something, looked at everyone else looking.

Nobody said anything.

Frieren sat down.

Mau entered a moment later.

He looked at her.

Then the garment.

"Nice hoodie."

Silence.

Yuta lowered his eyes.

Bocchi made a small choking sound into her breakfast.

Frieren looked down at the hooded outer layer she had taken from Mau's things.

Then back at him.

"Thanks."

She did not correct him.

Mau sat beside her.

The flower remained upstairs in the place they had both finally stopped moving it.

The room was theirs again.

No one needed to explain what that meant.
