# The Arrivals — S1E15 Green Light v0.1

**Episode:** S1E15 — A Birthday for Someone Who Never Had One  
**Status:** GREEN LIGHT / Stage 2 approved  
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

## Approved core

E15 is approved as a family-payoff episode after the E14 reconciliation. It does not need to advance the central plot.

Key approved beats:

- Fern realizes that because Mau remembers nothing from before, they do not know whether he has ever had a birthday or when he was born.
- Fern proposes that they give him one anyway.
- Rimuru immediately turns the idea into a real household project.
- The group accepts quickly because Mau has quietly become someone everyone relies on: he helps, listens, notices things, goes along with others' requests, and rarely asks for anything for himself.
- Everyone plans or makes gifts that reflect both themselves and what they think Mau would like.
- Mau unexpectedly walks in during planning. Everyone abruptly hides what they are doing.
- Mau notices that everyone knows something he does not. He does not interpret it as betrayal, but feels a small, real disappointment because belonging now matters to him.
- Frieren volunteers to keep Mau away from the inn and takes him on a small day-and-night trip under the excuse of looking for a plant used in food.
- The Frieren/Mau trip is deliberately ordinary and post-E14: walking, teasing, camping, conversation, normal companionship rather than crisis.
- During the trip, Mau asks what a birthday is. Frieren explains simply that people celebrate that someone was born because they are glad that person is here.
- Mau does not yet realize that explanation is about him.
- While they are away, the household prepares decorations, gifts, and a cake with Rimuru helping coordinate and fabricate what is needed.
- Frieren brings Mau back when the celebration is ready.
- Mau initially does not understand what is happening and asks whose birthday it is.
- The household tells him it is his.
- Mau objects that he does not know his real birthday; Fern says they picked one.
- Frieren explains that they are glad he is here. This is the emotional hinge that lets Mau understand the celebration.
- Mau experiences cake and gifts as things done specifically for him without his asking.
- He is genuinely excited/overwhelmed in a positive way and does not necessarily need to cry.
- Closing: Mau and Frieren are again in their shared room, surrounded by gifts and the flower from E9. Mau asks whether the chosen date is really his birthday now. Frieren says it can be if he wants. Mau says he likes it.

## Core meaning

This episode gives Mau something that belongs to this life rather than to a recovered past. The birthday is symbolic, chosen by the family he found here. It makes the household feel real and establishes what the cast has to lose when the world later becomes more dangerous.

No romance label is required or implied by the episode. The focus is family, belonging, and chosen continuity.
