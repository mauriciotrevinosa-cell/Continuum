<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln01-blank -->

Fern found the blank while moving papers.

One of Rimuru's household sheets had names, medicine notes, rough ages and practical details written in increasingly crowded lines.

Mau's line had holes.

Fern pointed at one.

"When is your birthday?"

Mau looked up from a book.

"I don't know."

"The day you were born."

"I know what the words mean."

He searched.

Fern recognized the look.

Nothing arrived.

"I don't remember it."

She looked at the blank.

"Do you remember ever having one?"

Mau searched again.

"No."

Neutral.

That bothered Fern more than sadness would have.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln01-give-him-one -->

Fern found Rimuru at the table.

"Mau doesn't have a birthday."

"He doesn't know his birthday."

"He doesn't remember ever having one either."

Rimuru set down the paper he was holding.

Fern had already solved the problem.

"Then we give him one."

Rimuru smiled.

"Okay."

Fern blinked.

"That's all?"

"Did you expect an argument?"

The idea spread faster than any construction plan.

Momo agreed immediately.

Bocchi agreed, then realized birthdays involved gifts and entered a private emergency.

Yuta said yes without adding ceremony.

Anko asked what date they were choosing.

Maomao wanted to know what local ingredients could become cake.

Frieren listened.

Rimuru looked at her.

"You're helping."

Frieren looked mildly offended.

"Obviously."

The date itself became the next problem.

No one wanted the day Frieren found Mau.

Too much fear belonged to it already.

Today meant no preparation.

Rimuru looked at the calendar they had built from local timekeeping.

"Tomorrow."

"Why tomorrow?" Fern asked.

"Because today we prepare, and tomorrow he has a birthday."

Anko accepted the operational logic.

Momo liked that the date would belong entirely to this world.

Frieren quietly did too.

No destiny.

No recovered memory.

A date chosen because people wanted Mau to have one.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln01-caught-planning -->

Mau walked in carrying a bucket and a broken hinge.

Every conversation stopped.

Rimuru covered the calendar.

Bocchi hid ribbon behind her back badly.

Momo turned a bowl upside down over something.

Maomao moved ingredients with the confidence of someone absolutely concealing evidence.

Mau looked around.

"What are you doing?"

Answers collided.

"Nothing."

"Normal things."

"Planning."

Everyone looked at Rimuru.

"Not planning."

Mau looked at Frieren.

Frieren did not attempt deception.

"You should go somewhere else."

Mau blinked.

"...Okay."

He left.

Silence.

Bocchi looked horrified.

"That sounded terrible."

Frieren looked at the door.

It had.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln01-outside-circle -->

Mau did not think they were plotting against him.

He knew them too well now.

That was almost the problem.

They were sharing something he was not part of.

Early in the season, that fact would have meant very little.

Now it stung.

He sat outside with the broken hinge and worked on it longer than the repair required.

Yuta passed.

Almost continued.

Mau looked up.

"You know what they're doing?"

Yuta was not a good liar.

"Yes."

Mau nodded.

"Okay."

"It's not bad."

A small smile.

"I figured."

That helped.

It did not remove the feeling.

Belonging made exclusion possible.

Even temporary exclusion.

Yuta seemed to understand enough not to add anything else.
