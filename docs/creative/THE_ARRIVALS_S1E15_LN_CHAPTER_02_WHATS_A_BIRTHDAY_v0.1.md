<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln02-distraction -->

Frieren found Mau before dinner.

"I need your help tomorrow."

"With what?"

"A plant."

Mau narrowed his eyes.

"Is this revenge for my plant?"

"No."

It sounded exactly like revenge.

She explained there was an edible plant several hours away.

"Why isn't Maomao going?"

"She's busy."

"Momo?"

"Busy."

Mau looked toward the inn.

"Everyone is busy."

"Yes."

He looked at Frieren.

"Okay."

He trusted her enough to go without understanding.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln02-ordinary-trip -->

They left early.

The trip was deliberately uneventful.

They argued over which trail was shorter.

Frieren chose one.

It was not shorter.

She refused to acknowledge the evidence.

A bird made a ridiculous call.

Mau imitated it.

Badly.

"Stop."

He did it again.

They crossed a shallow stream.

Mau nearly slipped and caught Frieren's shoulder.

Frieren looked at his hand.

Then at him.

She could have said something.

She did not.

They stopped for food.

Mau had packed too much bread and almost nothing else.

Frieren ate some anyway.

By midday, Mau had noticed several facts.

The plant was not urgent.

Frieren had no convincing explanation for why only he could help.

Everyone at the inn had been strangely busy.

He looked at her walking ahead.

He could ask.

Instead he smiled.

The excuse was weak.

The day was good.

He decided not to ruin either.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln02-meaning -->

They rested beneath a tree while Frieren inspected the plant she supposedly needed.

"Frieren."

"Hm?"

"What's a birthday?"

She looked up.

"I know it's the day someone was born. But why do people do things for it?"

Frieren thought.

"People celebrate that you were born."

"Why?"

She looked at him.

The answer was too simple to hide.

"Because they're glad you're here."

Mau absorbed that.

Looked through the trees.

"That sounds nice."

"It is."

He still did not understand she was talking about him.

Frieren went back to the plant.

A little later, she stood.

"We should go home."

Mau looked at her.

The word landed differently from inn.

From settlement.

From place we're staying.

Frieren had already started walking.

Mau followed.

He did not ask her to repeat it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln02-preparation -->

At home, everyone worked.

Rimuru organized without taking over.

Momo handled food and decorations.

Maomao had to be reminded repeatedly which ingredients were cake ingredients and which were not experiments.

Fern made something practical.

Yuta spent too long deciding what counted as a normal birthday gift before giving up on normal and choosing something connected to what they actually did together.

Anko drafted a note, crossed out half of it, then rewrote it shorter.

Bocchi overthought everything.

The wrapping.

The object.

Whether giving a gift was presumptuous.

Whether not giving enough was worse.

Eventually she chose something small and personal.

The decorations did not match.

The cake was not perfect.

The house looked like several people had all tried at once.

That was better.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e15-ln02-return -->

Frieren timed the return after dark.

Mau carried the plant from their fake errand.

"We were gone a long time for this."

"Yes."

"Was it worth it?"

Frieren looked at the plant.

"Probably not."

Mau laughed.

The inn was darker than usual.

"Did everyone go to sleep?"

Frieren opened the door.

Light.

Voices.

Decorations.

Everyone there.

"Happy birthday!"

Mau stopped.

Looked behind himself.

"...Whose?"

Momo pointed.

"Yours."

Mau looked at Frieren.

Then the room.

"I don't know when my birthday is."

"Neither do we," Fern said.

"Then how is it my birthday?"

"We picked one."

"You can do that?"

Rimuru leaned over the table.

"We just did."

Laughter.

Mau remained confused.

Frieren stepped beside him.

"We're glad you're here."

The sentence from under the tree returned with a different subject.

Mau understood.
