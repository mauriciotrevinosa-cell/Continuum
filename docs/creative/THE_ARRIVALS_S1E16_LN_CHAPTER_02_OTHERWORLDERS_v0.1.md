<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln02-guild -->

The guild was practical.

Jobs posted.

Workers returning.

Hunters carrying equipment.

Merchants looking for escorts.

Clerks recording things nobody wanted to remember themselves.

The group drew attention.

Not enough to stop business.

A clerk asked where they came from.

The answers failed one after another.

Frieren named a region absent from every local map.

Fern's answer belonged to the same impossible geography.

Several people produced versions of Japan.

Rimuru's origin was no easier.

Then Mau.

"And you?"

"I don't know."

The clerk stared at the form.

Then at the collection of impossible origins.

There was no registry to consult.

No old category waiting.

The clerk improvised.

"...Otherworlders, then."

Momo repeated it.

"Otherworlders?"

The clerk gestured at the map.

"You keep naming places that aren't in this world."

That was all the word meant.

Today.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln02-work -->

The clerk explained legal work.

Monster clearing.

Escort.

Gathering.

Missing persons.

Route checks.

Mapping.

Known dungeon work.

Repairs.

Support contracts.

Strengths began finding uses.

Yuta and Frieren were obvious combat assets.

Anko asked better questions than most new workers.

Maomao's knowledge had value even inside town.

Rimuru could organize and fabricate within limits.

Momo handled people and logistics.

Bocchi looked at the crowded hall and briefly considered whether poverty might be preferable.

Fern stayed near her without announcing why.

Mau asked, "People get paid for finding monsters?"

"Yes."

Mau looked at Yuta.

"Not alone," Yuta said.

"I didn't say anything."

"I know."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln02-caution -->

The clerk asked where they were staying.

Rimuru gave region and road direction.

Nothing about every strange structure near home.

Anko understood immediately.

So did the rest.

Not conspiracy.

Caution.

They did not know this world well enough to advertise everything unusual around the place they slept.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln02-first-work -->

They took modest work.

Not enough to become a mission anthology.

Enough to prove legitimacy.

Yuta cleared a dangerous creature from a route with witnesses.

Frieren solved a magical problem so quickly the client looked cheated until the result held.

Anko found an inconsistency in a delivery request.

Rimuru handled repair work without displaying everything he could do.

Maomao prepared useful medicinal material.

Momo worked supply labor.

Fern became reliable enough that a clerk started speaking to her directly rather than through Frieren.

Bocchi took support work that did not require performing socially for a room.

Mau carried, sorted, watched, asked.

By late afternoon, coins sat in their hands.

Not many.

Real.

Earned.

The concept Mau had studied that morning now belonged to him too.
