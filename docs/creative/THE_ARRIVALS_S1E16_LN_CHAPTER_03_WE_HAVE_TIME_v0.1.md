<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln03-earnings -->

Rimuru divided the earnings.

Shared needs first.

Then small personal portions.

Food.

Lamp oil.

Cloth.

Cookware.

Repair hardware.

Medicine containers.

For the first time, they could buy solutions instead of inventing every one from ruins.

Mau looked at his share.

He knew what it was worth now.

He simply did not want anything.

He pocketed it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln03-bocchi-item -->

Bocchi found the music-related item in a small stall.

Not a replacement for her guitar.

Something useful.

She checked the price.

Then her remaining money.

Put it back.

Mau noticed.

"You want that."

Bocchi reacted as if accused.

"No. I mean yes, but not enough to—I can wait."

Mau looked at the item.

Then his coins.

He already knew what those coins represented.

Meals.

Oil.

Work.

He held them out.

Bocchi stared.

"What?"

"I don't need anything."

"But it's yours."

"Yes."

She waited for an explanation.

Mau did not have one.

He placed the coins in her hand.

"Buy it."

Bocchi started objecting again.

Then looked at the item.

Then the money.

She bought it.

Carried it for the rest of the walk as though it might break from being too important.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln03-forgotten-road -->

The return route taught them something the settlement side had hidden.

Near the mercantile town, an old track branched toward their region.

Maintained poorly.

Still readable.

Farther from town it narrowed.

Vegetation swallowed the edges.

Old markers disappeared behind growth.

By the time it approached their abandoned village, it no longer looked like a road unless someone already knew what to look for.

Mau stopped where the route became familiar.

"This goes home."

Anko looked back along the track.

"Apparently."

The road had always existed.

The abandoned side had simply forgotten how to look like one.

E2's isolation remained real.

E16 gave it history.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln03-proof -->

They returned carrying proof the world was real.

Lamp oil.

A better pan.

Cloth.

Food.

Hardware.

Containers.

Bocchi's item.

A map.

Leftover coins.

The inn changed by inches.

Brighter lamp.

Stronger hinge.

Better cookware.

Participation.

Not wealth.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e16-ln03-map-home -->

That night the regional map covered the common table.

People gathered and separated around it.

Their settlement was not printed.

Anko marked it by hand.

Mau pointed.

"We're here."

"Yes," Frieren said.

Roads spread outward.

Towns.

Mountains.

Coast.

Islands.

"That's a lot."

"It is."

Mau traced a route without touching the paper.

"Do you think we'll see it?"

Frieren looked at the map.

"Probably."

Mau turned.

"Probably?"

Frieren met his eyes.

"We have time."

The common room stretched around the map.

The world was larger than the road outside.

For the first time, they knew how much larger.
