# The Arrivals — S1E16 — The World Opens — GREEN LIGHT v0.1

**Status:** GREEN LIGHT / Stage 2 approved
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

E16 is approved with the following final clarifications layered onto the previously reviewed Voice Check.

## Otherworlder terminology

Frieren and Mau / the current arrival wave are treated as the first known cases in this world, not members of a pre-existing recognized category with established records.

During guild registration, the clerk hears multiple places that do not exist on any local map, including incompatible references to Japan and other regions from the arrivals' source worlds. Mau cannot name an origin at all.

The clerk improvises the descriptive label:

> "Otherworlders, then."

When asked what that means, the explanation is simply that they keep naming places that do not belong to this world.

Do **not** imply that Otherworlders are already a known recurring phenomenon, documented administrative category, or established historical pattern. The term begins here as a mostly neutral description and can gain heavier social meaning later after Sukuna's catastrophe.

## Bocchi purchase beat

The group's initial earnings are deliberately modest and do not cover every desired purchase.

Bocchi finds a small music-related item she wants, but the remaining shared money is not enough for it after the practical household purchases.

Mau has not spent his own portion of the first earnings. He gives his money to Bocchi so she can buy the item.

The moment should stay character-sized rather than sentimentalized: Mau sees that she wants it, realizes he has no immediate use for his money, and gives it to her naturally. Bocchi then buys the item.

This replaces the earlier version where Bocchi puts the item back and simply waits for a later trip.

## Everything else

All other approved E16 Voice Check material remains intact:
- Yuta discovers the mercantile town and returns with "I found people.";
- G1+G2 enter functioning native society together;
- the world expands through roads, maps, coastline, islands, trade routes, guilds, dungeons, and other settlements;
- the group registers for legitimate work and local money;
- they do not advertise the structure/dungeon near their home yet;
- first earnings create material progression at the inn;
- the episode ends with the regional map and the sense that their home is one small point inside a much larger world.
