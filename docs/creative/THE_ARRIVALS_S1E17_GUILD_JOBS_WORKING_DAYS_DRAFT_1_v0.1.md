# S1E17 — `Guild Jobs / Working Days`

## Scene 1 — Morning jobs

Rimuru has turned one corner of the common table into a job board.

Guild notices, copied routes, notes and coins share space with breakfast.

Nobody formally appointed him household manager.

It happened anyway.

**RIMURU**  
We have five reasonable jobs and three stupid ones.

Momo looks over his shoulder.

**MOMO**  
How stupid?

Rimuru taps one notice.

**RIMURU**  
Pays less than the travel food costs.

Anko:

**ANKO**  
That's not a job. That's volunteering with paperwork.

Across the table Maomao reaches for a page.

Rimuru moves it away.

**MAOMAO**  
What was that?

**RIMURU**  
Nothing.

Mau, eating:

**MAU**  
You people say that too much.

Maomao points at him approvingly because he stole her complaint.

The assignments begin.

---

## Scene 2 — Mau + Yuta

Mau and Yuta take a small monster-clearing request along a farm road.

It is not a training session disguised as work.

Yuta treats Mau like someone he already expects beside him.

**YUTA**  
Stay left.

**MAU**  
Why?

**YUTA**  
Because I said so.

**MAU**  
That's not an explanation.

A creature bursts from brush on the right.

Yuta removes it before Mau finishes turning.

**YUTA**  
That's the explanation.

Mau stares at the empty brush.

**MAU**  
Okay. That was a good explanation.

Later Mau helps locate tracks and notices signs Yuta skipped because he was focused on cursed energy rather than ordinary movement.

Yuta acknowledges it without turning the episode into a lesson.

They work like brothers who have learned each other's blind spots.

---

## Scene 3 — Frieren + Anko

Anko and Frieren take a missing-person search.

The person is an elderly trader who failed to return from a short route.

Frieren can cover ground and sense magic.

Anko can read what people did.

At a fork Frieren points one direction.

Anko points another.

**FRIEREN**  
There was mana that way.

**ANKO**  
There are broken reeds this way.

Frieren looks.

Anko kneels.

**ANKO**  
And one boot dragged.

Frieren changes direction immediately.

No pride problem.

They find the trader with an injured ankle in a shallow ravine.

On the walk back Anko asks Frieren questions not about magic, but about how she estimates danger when someone she cares about is involved.

Frieren does not give a clean answer.

Anko does not force one.

---

## Scene 4 — Home workers

Not everyone leaves.

Maomao has turned her workroom into something that increasingly resembles a clinic whether she admits it or not.

Momo helps sort food and prepare meals around the unpredictable return times of field groups.

Rimuru handles money, repairs and job schedules.

Bocchi helps with quiet repetitive work while using the music-related item she bought with Mau's money in E16.

The callback is visual: she uses it naturally now.

Nobody points it out.

---

## Scene 5 — First return / pooled earnings

By evening the field groups return dirty in different ways.

Mau has mud on one side of his clothes because he slipped while pretending he did not need Yuta's warning.

Yuta has monster residue on one sleeve.

Anko has leaves in her hair.

Frieren looks exactly as clean as when she left, which annoys Anko on principle.

Coins land on the common table.

Rimuru starts dividing them.

Momo looks at the growing shared pile.

**MOMO**  
Are we doing individual money or house money?

Several answers happen at once.

The conversation overlaps.

Eventually the practical answer emerges: shared needs first, personal portions after.

Nobody makes a ceremony out of it.

They eat.

The first working day becomes routine by surviving once.

---

## Scene 6 — Day two pairings

The next day changes combinations.

Momo goes with Anko on a local supply/checking job where observation and talking to merchants matter more than powers.

Momo is better with people than Anko expects.

Anko is better at noticing lies than Momo expects.

**MOMO**  
He was lying.

**ANKO**  
Yes.

**MOMO**  
You knew before I did.

**ANKO**  
You got him to keep talking.

They look at each other.

The pairing works.

Elsewhere Rimuru and Yuta help with a damaged cart and route obstruction. Rimuru could solve everything instantly, but deliberately works within what he is willing to reveal. Yuta does the heavy lifting without complaining until Rimuru points out he is lifting the wrong side.

---

## Scene 7 — Frieren still goes with Fern

Fern takes a straightforward gathering/route job.

Frieren starts preparing to leave too.

Fern notices.

**FERN**  
You don't have to come.

**FRIEREN**  
I know.

Fern waits.

Frieren puts on her outer layer.

Fern sighs.

This time she does not argue further.

The important difference from E11 is that Frieren is accompanying rather than forbidding.

They leave together.

On the job Frieren keeps a little too close.

Fern completes the work anyway.

---

## Scene 8 — Another night

The second-night dinner is louder.

People talk across one another because they now know enough about each other's day to interrupt intelligently.

Momo tells a story about Anko scaring a merchant by knowing he lied.

Anko says Momo made him confess by smiling at him.

Momo objects to the word confess.

Yuta complains that Rimuru kept correcting how he held a cart.

Rimuru says the cart was more grateful than Yuta.

Bocchi quietly plays with/uses her new music item at the edge of the conversation.

Mau notices and smiles.

Frieren steals food from Mau's plate.

Mau steals it back.

Fern sees it and does not comment.

Family familiarity is now rhythm rather than exposition.

---

## Scene 9 — Better things

A montage over several days.

Coins become:

better bedding;

new cookware;

glass in a repaired window;

a stronger lamp;

fresh cloth;

more reliable food stores;

proper storage jars;

new tools.

The inn remains rough.

It simply stops feeling one broken object away from collapse.

Rimuru's budget ledger gains categories.

Food.

Repairs.

Guild fees.

Medical.

Then, separately:

`MAOMAO`.

Maomao sees it.

**MAOMAO**  
Why am I my own category?

Rimuru does not look up.

**RIMURU**  
Historical data.

---

## Scene 10 — Fern with Bocchi

A safe nearby errand/job needs two people.

Fern volunteers.

Bocchi gets volunteered by circumstance and looks betrayed by the concept of outside work.

Frieren opens her mouth.

Stops.

Looks at Fern.

Looks at Bocchi.

Bocchi is not strong, but Fern is.

Fern is not alone.

Frieren closes her mouth.

**FRIEREN**  
Before dark.

Fern:

**FERN**  
We will be.

Bocchi quietly:

**BOCCHI**  
We will?

Fern takes her with her.

Frieren watches the door close.

She does not follow.

That matters.

---

## Scene 11 — Frieren + Mau job

On another day Frieren and Mau take a simple magical pest / route-check job.

Mau spends too much time asking what each plant does.

Frieren knows fewer answers than he expects.

**MAU**  
You've lived a long time.

**FRIEREN**  
That doesn't mean I memorized every plant.

**MAU**  
Maomao would know.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Then you should have gone with Maomao.

Mau looks at her.

**MAU**  
No. You're more fun when you don't know things.

Frieren stares at him.

Mau keeps walking.

The job itself is easy.

The value is seeing them outside together again with no separation tension.

---

## Scene 12 — Fern with a stronger companion

Fern later takes a farther job with Yuta.

Frieren checks the posting twice.

Yuta notices.

**YUTA**  
I'll bring her back.

Fern looks at him.

**FERN**  
I can bring myself back.

Yuta corrects immediately.

**YUTA**  
Right.

Frieren almost smiles.

**FRIEREN**  
Before dark.

Fern:

**FERN**  
I know.

This time Frieren returns to her book after they leave.

She still looks at the door once.

Only once.

---

## Scene 13 — Mau spends almost nothing

Rimuru notices Mau's personal money keeps accumulating in a small envelope or pouch.

**RIMURU**  
You know you can spend that.

**MAU**  
I know.

**RIMURU**  
Do you want anything?

Mau thinks.

Looks around the inn.

**MAU**  
Not really.

A beat.

He spots Bocchi using the item from E16.

**MAU**  
That was worth buying.

Rimuru follows his gaze.

He understands enough not to argue.

---

## Scene 14 — Fern asks for a solo job

Eventually Fern chooses a safe nearby assignment that genuinely needs only one person.

She tells Frieren rather than asks permission.

**FERN**  
I'm taking the east-road delivery check.

Frieren looks up.

Old instinct arrives visibly.

Go with her.

Stop her.

Ask someone else to go.

Frieren looks at Fern's prepared bag.

At her staff.

At the door.

Then:

**FRIEREN**  
Before dark.

Fern's expression softens.

**FERN**  
I will.

She leaves alone.

Frieren remains seated.

Mau, nearby, glances at her.

Frieren notices.

**FRIEREN**  
Don't.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I didn't say anything.

**FRIEREN**  
You were going to.

Mau:

**MAU**  
...Maybe.

He does not tease further.

---

## Scene 15 — Waiting without waiting

The afternoon continues.

Frieren reads.

Helps with something.

Eats.

Does not sit at the door.

Does not patrol the road.

When the light begins to change, she looks toward the window once.

Then Fern opens the door.

Mud on boots.

Work done.

Safe.

Frieren looks at her.

**FRIEREN**  
Welcome back.

Fern:

**FERN**  
I'm back.

Nothing else is needed.

---

## Scene 16 — Final working-night dinner

That night everybody returns at different times.

Boots pile by the door.

Guild papers cover part of the table.

Coins clink into the shared bowl.

Someone bought fruit.

Someone complains about a client.

Maomao tries to justify an expense before Rimuru even asks.

Momo tells two people to sit down because dinner is getting cold.

Bocchi is talking to Fern while still half-listening to Yuta.

Anko interrupts Mau to correct a detail of his story.

Frieren eats from Mau's plate without asking.

Mau catches her hand halfway to the second piece.

**MAU**  
That's mine.

**FRIEREN**  
You weren't eating it.

**MAU**  
I was going to.

**FRIEREN**  
Eventually?

Fern hides a smile.

The conversation continues over them.

No one says this is home.

The image does.

---

## Scene 17 — The place they return to

After dinner the house settles.

A map dries near the wall.

Mud-streaked boots wait by the entrance.

A repaired window holds.

New lamps burn steadily.

Bocchi's music item sits where she uses it.

Maomao's jars occupy too much shelf space.

Guild papers are weighted under a cup.

Mau's things remain mixed with Frieren's upstairs.

Fern's bag is back from the solo job.

The final exterior shows the once-abandoned inn lit from several windows.

Not because something extraordinary happened.

Because everyone came back.

**END S1E17**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- Repeated work rhythm is the episode's structure, not a single mission plot.
- Frieren/Fern progression must be behavioral: accompanies → allows trusted companion → allows strong companion → allows safe solo → `Welcome back.`
- No trauma speech needed.
- Bocchi's E16 purchase appears naturally in use.
- Home upgrades remain modest and earned.
- Rimuru `Maomao` budget category stays a gag, not hostility.
- Mau/Yuta feel brother-like and comfortable.
- Dinner dialogue must overlap and callback; avoid early-season interview rhythm.
