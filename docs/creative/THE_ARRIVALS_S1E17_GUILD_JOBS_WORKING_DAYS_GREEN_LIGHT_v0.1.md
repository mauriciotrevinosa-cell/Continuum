# The Arrivals — S1E17 Guild Jobs / Working Days — GREEN LIGHT v0.1

**Status:** GREEN LIGHT / Stage 2 approved
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

## Approval summary

E17 is approved as a working-days / family-routine episode built around the repeated rhythm:

**leave for jobs → work in changing combinations → return to the inn → pool earnings → eat together.**

The episode should prove that E16 changed daily life: the household now participates in the native guild/economy, earns money, develops work routines, and returns each night to a shared home.

## Approved voice / structural beats

- Rimuru informally coordinates jobs and increasingly becomes the household budget manager.
- Mau + Yuta take a monster job; their relationship is comfortable and brother-like rather than instructor/student-only.
- Anko + Frieren take a missing-person/search job and complement each other through detective observation + magical capability.
- Maomao often stays at the inn working on medicine; Momo helps with cooking/supplies/household operations while still without powers; Rimuru sometimes stays for organization/budgeting.
- First-night earnings naturally start becoming a pooled household fund.
- Fern/Frieren overprotection heals gradually and mostly through behavior:
  1. Frieren initially insists on accompanying Fern.
  2. Later Fern leaves with Bocchi / a trusted companion and Frieren allows it.
  3. Later Frieren lets Fern go with another trusted strong companion.
  4. Finally Fern takes a safe nearby job alone and Frieren consciously does not stop her.
- Fern returns safely; Frieren simply says, **“Welcome back.”** No trauma speech is needed.
- Fern + Bocchi continue their sister-like dynamic.
- Additional job pairings may include Momo + Anko, Rimuru + Yuta, Frieren + Mau, and other less-used combinations.
- Money visually improves the inn over repeated days: better bedding, cookware, glass, tools, lamps, cloth, food, etc. The group is not rich; they can simply replace/improve things without survival panic.
- Rimuru budget gag can include a separate `Maomao` expense category.
- Mau does not buy much for himself, with a visual callback to Bocchi using the item he helped her buy in E16.
- Group dinner dialogue should now overlap and flow naturally rather than feeling like early-season Q&A. Characters interrupt, correct, callback, and talk across one another like a family.
- Closing image: the inn as the place everyone returns to after work—dirty boots, guild papers, maps, bought goods, dishes, lights in the windows.

## Core emotional payoff

Frieren moves from refusing to let Fern leave her sight to allowing Fern to walk out the door alone and trusting that she will come back.

The episode should feel like ordinary life finally existing inside the fantasy world.
