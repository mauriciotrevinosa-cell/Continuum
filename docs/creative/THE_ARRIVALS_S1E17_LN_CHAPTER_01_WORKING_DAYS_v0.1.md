<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln01-job-board -->

One corner of the common table became a job board.

Guild notices.

Copied routes.

Coins.

Breakfast.

Rimuru looked over the options.

"We have five reasonable jobs and three stupid ones."

Momo leaned over his shoulder.

"How stupid?"

He tapped one.

"Pays less than the travel food costs."

Anko glanced at it.

"That's not a job. That's volunteering with paperwork."

Maomao reached for another notice.

Rimuru moved it.

"What was that?"

"Nothing."

Mau kept eating.

"You people say that too much."

Maomao pointed at him in approval.

The assignments began.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln01-mau-yuta -->

Mau and Yuta took a small monster-clearing request along a farm road.

Not training.

Work.

Yuta expected Mau beside him now.

"Stay left."

"Why?"

"Because I said so."

"That's not an explanation."

Something burst from brush on the right.

Yuta removed it before Mau finished turning.

"That's the explanation."

Mau stared at the empty brush.

"...Okay. That was a good explanation."

Later Mau noticed tracks Yuta had ignored because Yuta was reading cursed energy more than ordinary ground.

Yuta stopped.

Looked.

"Good catch."

Mau smiled.

No lesson followed.

They simply adjusted.

Two people learning each other's blind spots.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln01-frieren-anko -->

Frieren and Anko took a missing-person search.

An elderly trader had failed to return from a short route.

At a fork, Frieren pointed one direction.

"There was mana that way."

Anko pointed another.

"Broken reeds."

Frieren looked.

Anko crouched.

"And one boot dragged."

Frieren changed direction immediately.

No pride.

They found the trader in a shallow ravine with an injured ankle.

On the way back Anko asked, "How do you estimate danger when someone you care about is involved?"

Frieren looked ahead.

"Poorly."

Anko waited.

Nothing else came.

"Fair."

That was enough.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln01-home-workers -->

Not everyone left.

Maomao's workroom had become a clinic through use.

Momo prepared meals around unpredictable return times.

Rimuru handled schedules, repairs and money.

Bocchi performed repetitive household work while naturally using the music item Mau had helped her buy.

No one pointed out the callback.

The object had already become hers.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln01-return-one -->

The first groups returned dirty in different ways.

Mud streaked one side of Mau's clothes from a slip he had pretended did not need Yuta's warning.

Monster residue marked one sleeve of Yuta's clothes.

Leaves had caught in Anko's hair.

Frieren looked as clean as when she left.

Anko disliked this on principle.

Coins landed on the table.

Momo looked at the pile.

"Individual money or house money?"

Several answers happened at once.

Eventually:

shared needs first.

Personal portions after.

No ceremony.

They ate.

The first working day became routine by surviving once.
