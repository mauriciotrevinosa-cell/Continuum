<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln02-new-pairings -->

The next day changed combinations.

Momo went with Anko on a supply and verification job.

Momo was better with people than Anko expected.

Anko was better at noticing lies than Momo expected.

"He was lying," Momo said after they left a merchant.

"Yes."

"You knew before I did."

"You got him to keep talking."

They looked at each other.

The pairing worked.

Elsewhere Rimuru and Yuta helped with a damaged cart.

Rimuru refused to reveal enough ability to turn himself into a walking miracle.

Yuta lifted the wrong side.

Rimuru corrected him.

Yuta complained.

The cart benefited.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln02-frieren-fern -->

Fern took a route-gathering job.

Frieren prepared to leave.

"You don't have to come."

"I know."

Fern waited.

Frieren put on her outer layer.

Fern sighed.

This time she did not fight it.

The important difference was not that Frieren stayed home.

It was that Frieren accompanied Fern instead of forbidding Fern from going.

On the job, Frieren stayed slightly too close.

Fern completed the work anyway.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln02-night-two -->

The second dinner was louder.

Momo told everyone how Anko had frightened a merchant by knowing he lied.

Anko said Momo made him keep talking by smiling.

Momo objected to the word confession even though nobody had used it.

Yuta complained that Rimuru kept correcting the way he held a cart.

"The cart was more grateful than you," Rimuru said.

Bocchi used her music item near the edge of the conversation.

Mau noticed.

Smiled.

Frieren took food from his plate.

Mau took it back.

Fern saw.

Did not comment.

Familiarity had become rhythm.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln02-earned-things -->

More days.

Coins became bedding.

Cookware.

Glass.

Lamp oil.

Cloth.

Storage jars.

Tools.

Nothing luxurious.

Each object removed one small daily inconvenience.

Rimuru's ledger gained categories.

Food.

Repairs.

Guild fees.

Medical.

Then:

MAOMAO.

Maomao found it.

"Why am I my own category?"

Rimuru did not look up.

"Historical data."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln02-village-contraction -->

The inn improved by consuming the dead village around it.

One ruined house lost usable roof beams.

A week later those beams reinforced the inn.

Boards disappeared from another collapsed structure.

They became shelving.

Stone from a broken wall became part of a repaired boundary.

No one narrated the transformation.

The exterior did.

Each repeated walk home showed one nearby ruin becoming more skeletal while the inn gained something solid.

The abandoned village contracted.

The living part became denser.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln02-fern-bocchi -->

A safe nearby errand needed two people.

Fern volunteered.

Bocchi became the second person through a sequence of circumstances she considered betrayal.

Frieren opened her mouth.

Stopped.

Fern was strong.

Bocchi was not alone.

"Before dark."

"We will be," Fern said.

Bocchi looked at her.

"We will?"

Fern took her outside.

Frieren watched the door close.

She did not follow.

That mattered more than saying she trusted Fern.
