<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-frieren-mau-job -->

On another day, Frieren and Mau took a simple route-check job.

Mau asked what every plant did.

Frieren knew fewer answers than he expected.

"You've lived a long time."

"That doesn't mean I memorized every plant."

"Maomao would know."

"Then you should have gone with Maomao."

Mau looked at her.

"No. You're more fun when you don't know things."

Frieren stared.

Mau kept walking.

The job was easy.

That was the point.

They could exist outside together again without repairing anything between them.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-fern-yuta -->

Fern later took a farther job with Yuta.

Frieren checked the posting twice.

Yuta noticed.

"I'll bring her back."

Fern looked at him.

"I can bring myself back."

Yuta corrected immediately.

"Right."

Frieren almost smiled.

"Before dark."

"I know."

When they left, Frieren returned to her book.

She looked at the door once.

Only once.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-mau-money -->

Rimuru noticed Mau's personal money accumulating.

"You know you can spend that."

"I know."

"Do you want anything?"

Mau thought.

Looked around the inn.

"Not really."

Bocchi passed while using the item from E16.

Mau watched.

"That was worth buying."

Rimuru followed his eyes.

No argument.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-solo -->

Eventually Fern prepared a safe nearby assignment genuinely meant for one person.

She informed Frieren.

Did not ask.

"I'm taking the east-road delivery check."

Frieren looked up.

Old instinct arrived.

Go.

Stop her.

Find someone else.

Frieren looked at Fern's bag.

Her staff.

The door.

"Before dark."

Fern's expression softened.

"I will."

She left alone.

Frieren stayed seated.

Mau looked at her.

"Don't."

"I didn't say anything."

"You were going to."

"...Maybe."

He did not tease further.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-waiting -->

The afternoon continued.

Frieren read.

Helped with something.

Ate.

Did not sit at the door.

Did not patrol the road.

When the light changed, she looked through the window once.

The door opened.

Fern.

Mud on boots.

Work complete.

Safe.

"Welcome back."

"I'm back."

Nothing else.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-final-dinner -->

That evening everyone returned at different times.

Boots gathered.

Papers covered part of the table.

Coins entered the shared bowl.

Someone had bought fruit.

Someone complained about a client.

Maomao began justifying an expense before Rimuru asked.

Momo told people to sit before dinner cooled.

Bocchi talked to Fern while half-listening to Yuta.

Anko interrupted Mau to correct a detail.

Frieren reached toward Mau's plate.

He caught her hand before the second piece.

"That's mine."

"You weren't eating it."

"I was going to."

"Eventually?"

Fern hid a smile.

Conversation continued around them.

Nobody declared home.

Outside, one nearby ruined house had fewer boards than last week.

The inn had another repaired window.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e17-ln03-lit-inn -->

After dinner the place settled.

Map near the wall.

Mud-streaked boots.

Steady lamps.

Bocchi's music item where she used it.

Maomao's jars occupying too much shelf.

Guild papers weighted under a cup.

Mau's things mixed with Frieren's upstairs.

Fern's bag returned from the solo job.

From outside, the once-empty inn glowed through several windows.

Not because something extraordinary had happened.

Because everyone came back.
