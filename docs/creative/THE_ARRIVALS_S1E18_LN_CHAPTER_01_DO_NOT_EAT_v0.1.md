<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln01-morning -->

Not everyone worked that day.

Some jobs existed.

None required the entire house.

The inn spent a morning behaving like a house instead of a staging area.

Momo stood in the kitchen managing too many things.

Mau passed.

"Mau. I need you."

He turned immediately.

"Okay."

Momo blinked.

"...You don't even know what for."

"You said you needed me."

She stared.

Then pointed toward the bowl.

"Fine. Come here."

A few minutes later:

"A little."

Mau added an amount so small it was nearly insulting.

Momo stared into the bowl.

Then at him.

"A little more than that."

Mau added one more pinch.

Momo closed her eyes.

"I walked into that."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln01-experiment -->

In the workroom, Maomao had cacao, chocolate and a local medicinal ingredient laid out with the kind of organization that made everyone else more nervous, not less.

The intended effect was mild.

Relaxation.

Reduced social inhibition.

Easier expression of impulses someone might normally filter.

Not desire creation.

Not compelled honesty.

Dose calibration remained a problem.

Bocchi helped with labels.

Maomao held out a small piece.

"Taste this."

Bocchi looked at it as though asked to sign a contract.

"Is it poisonous?"

"No."

"Will it make me sick?"

Maomao considered.

"Probably not."

Bocchi looked less reassured.

She tasted the smallest possible amount.

Nothing dramatic happened.

"It's good."

Maomao wrote that down.

Bocchi had apparently contributed to science.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln01-yuta-anko -->

Yuta and Anko took a small town errand.

On the road, Anko waited until a question would not feel like an interrogation.

"So Rika was a person before?"

Yuta took several steps before answering.

"Yes."

Anko let him choose the amount.

"And she's still with you."

"Yes."

"Complicated."

Yuta almost laughed.

"Yeah."

That was enough.

Anko did not turn grief into an interview simply because she was good at questions.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln01-fern-solo -->

Fern prepared for a safe mundane errand.

Frieren looked up.

"Before dark."

"I will."

Fern left.

Frieren returned to her book.

No pause.

No hidden following.

The progression from E17 had become behavior.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln01-label -->

Bocchi started a label.

DO NOT—

Momo called for help.

Bocchi set it down unfinished.

Maomao was pulled away by a medicinal task.

The plate remained on the common table among ordinary food.

Everybody assumed someone else knew what it was.

Several minutes later Frieren crossed the room.

Saw chocolate.

Took one.

Mau looked up.

"Is that yours?"

Frieren was already eating it.

"It was on the table."

"That doesn't mean it's yours."

She finished.

"It does now."

Mau watched her.

Nothing happened.

For several minutes.

Then Frieren went upstairs as part of her normal routine.

The unfinished label stayed where Bocchi had left it.
