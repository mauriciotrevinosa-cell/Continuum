<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln02-wardrobe -->

When Frieren came downstairs again, conversation paused.

She wore Mau's black-and-orange 2021 McLaren Team Hoodie.

His McLaren Triple Crown Edition cap.

Her own comfortable short bottoms.

None of the individual choices were unprecedented except the combination.

Frieren had borrowed his clothing before.

The difference was how little she appeared to have considered whether wearing several of his things at once might look unusual.

Mau looked up.

Double-took.

"...Huh?"

Frieren sat.

"What?"

Mau looked at the familiar McLaren cap.

Then at his own black-and-orange hoodie.

Then Frieren.

"Nothing."

Rimuru suddenly found his notes fascinating.

Momo looked between Mau and Frieren.

Bocchi looked at Fern's empty chair and visibly wished Fern were home.

Frieren behaved as though nothing had changed.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln02-closer -->

Mau sat to read.

Frieren sat beside him.

There were other seats.

She chose the closest one.

Mau noticed.

Did not object.

He stood to move a cup.

Frieren stood too.

Mau stopped.

Frieren stopped.

"What are you doing?"

"Nothing."

Mau moved.

Frieren followed.

"You're following me."

"No, I'm not."

She was.

Mau looked at Momo for help.

Momo refused eye contact because this was suddenly much funnier from a safe distance.

A little later Mau said something ordinary.

Frieren laughed more openly than the sentence deserved.

He looked at her.

Concern replaced confusion by degrees.

"Are you sick?"

"No."

"Are you sure?"

"Yes."

She moved closer.

Mau moved his book slightly so she could see it.

The gesture was so automatic neither noticed.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln02-fern-return -->

Fern returned before dark.

She stepped into the inn.

Stopped.

Mistress Frieren wore Mau's black-and-orange McLaren hoodie.

His McLaren Triple Crown cap.

Sat almost against him.

"Mistress Frieren?"

Frieren looked up.

"Welcome back."

Fern stared one beat longer.

"Are you feeling well?"

"Yes."

Mau looked relieved to have reinforcements.

"She's been acting weird."

Frieren turned.

"No, I haven't."

Fern's eyes narrowed.

Investigation began.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln02-outside -->

Mau went outside to bring something in.

Frieren followed.

Evening air cooled the yard.

He caught her staring.

"What?"

"Nothing."

"You're staring."

"You stare at me."

"Not like that."

"How do you know?"

Mau opened his mouth.

No answer.

Frieren stepped closer.

He remained still because he did not understand what she was doing.

Not because he was inviting it.

Frieren leaned toward him.

Fern arrived between them with timing sharpened by years of dealing with Frieren's worst decisions.

Contact never happened.

Frieren stopped inches away.

"...Fern."

"No."

"Move."

"No."

Mau looked between them.

"What was she doing?"

Fern kept her attention on Frieren.

"...Nothing."

"That didn't look like nothing."

"Later."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln02-investigation -->

Fern retraced the day.

Common table.

Plate.

Unfinished label.

Maomao's workroom.

Bocchi reached the same conclusion at the same time.

Her face emptied.

"Oh no."

Fern looked at her.

"What?"

Bocchi pointed at the label.

"I was going to finish that."

Fern closed her eyes.

The handwriting stopped after two words.

DO NOT—

The rest no longer needed writing to be understood.
