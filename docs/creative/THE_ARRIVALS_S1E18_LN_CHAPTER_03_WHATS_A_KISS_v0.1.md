<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln03-maomao-returns -->

Maomao returned to a very specific silence.

Frieren.

Fern.

Mau.

Plate.

Unfinished label.

Understanding arrived instantly.

Then Maomao started laughing.

Fern did not.

"She tried to kiss Mau."

Maomao laughed harder.

From nearby:

"I can still hear you."

Momo entered halfway through.

"What happened?"

Nobody answered quickly enough.

Her suspicion increased.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln03-explanation -->

Mau remained behind the central vocabulary.

Maomao explained.

"It lowers inhibition."

"What does that mean?"

She simplified.

"It makes it easier to do things you'd normally stop yourself from doing."

Mau thought.

"So it makes you want things?"

"No."

Maomao became more precise.

"It doesn't make you want things you don't already want."

Fern looked at Frieren.

Frieren looked away.

Maomao continued before anyone could turn one sentence into a verdict.

"It also doesn't tell you what the impulse means."

Fern's eyes returned to her.

Maomao pointed toward her notes.

"Affection. Curiosity. Comfort. Playfulness. Physical closeness. Something a person hasn't categorized. The medicine doesn't translate any of it for us."

Mau frowned.

"So we don't know why she did that."

"Correct."

Frieren looked relieved by the scientific ambiguity.

Mau looked only more confused.

Good.

The incident remained what it actually was: an impulse with lowered filtering, not a confession machine.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln03-consequences -->

Maomao received consequences.

Storage rules.

Labels.

Experimental food kept away from common food.

Bocchi created a replacement label large enough to be seen from another room.

DO NOT EAT.

Maomao looked at it.

"That's excessive."

Everyone else answered no.

Momo finally received the missing detail.

"WAIT. SHE DID WHAT?"

From upstairs:

"NOTHING HAPPENED."

Momo looked toward the ceiling.

"...I didn't say your name."

Silence.

The room broke.

Even Fern laughed eventually.

Frieren did not return.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e18-ln03-shared-room -->

Later, the inn was quiet.

Mau and Frieren were in their shared room.

Frieren read with the concentration of someone attempting to erase a day through force.

Mau sat nearby thinking.

"Frieren."

"No."

"I didn't ask anything."

"You were going to."

Mau waited.

Then sincerely:

"...What's a kiss?"

Frieren's book lowered one inch.

She stared at him.

Mau waited for an answer.

None arrived before the moment ended.
