## Locked structure

- E19 is one intentionally longer finale episode.
- G1+G2 and G3 are intercut throughout; do not structure it as a sequential `19A` then `19B`.
- G3 grouping remains exactly:
  - Okarun + Umaru + Kita;
  - Coco + Qifrey + Wakana Gojo + Marin;
  - Mikasa initially alone.
- G3 remains several days of medieval travel from the mercantile region; they do not reach G1+G2 in S1.
- `Otherworlder` is a recently coined regional descriptor that began circulating after G1+G2 appeared. It is not an ancient or established category.

## Okarun memory-hole rule

Okarun knows with certainty that there is an important person he should remember / is looking for, but he cannot recover who that person is.

He does not merely have a vague feeling that he forgot something.

He can recognize the absence itself and the importance attached to it, but not the person's name, face, or identity.

That missing person is Momo, but E19 must not reveal or name her in Okarun's thread.

Representative approved exchange:

> Kita: "Is there someone you're looking for?"  
> Okarun: "Yes."  
> Kita: "Who?"  
> Okarun: "...I don't know."  
> Umaru: "How do you know you're looking for someone if you don't know who?"  
> Okarun: "That's the problem."

## Sukuna final sting

Hard cut from peaceful nighttime inn to **middle of the day** in a large regional city.

Sukuna senses Yuta and recognizes him immediately:

> **"So that brat is here."**

Sukuna is still mentally operating from the conflict context he came from. He does not first understand that he is in another world.

He reacts impulsively:

> **"Domain Expansion."**  
> **"Malevolent Shrine."**

The result is catastrophic on the city scale, but exact radius / statistics remain intentionally unlocked.

After the destruction, Sukuna finally studies the environment and realizes the architecture, people, landscape, and context are wrong. He understands he is not on the same battlefield / likely not in the same world.

He then deliberately suppresses his cursed energy because if he sensed Yuta, Yuta may have sensed him.

Do not return to the inn after this.
Do not show Yuta's reaction in E19.
Yuta's recognition / terror / disappearing signal opens S2E1.

## Final tonal progression

`G3 lost but moving toward possible help → G1+G2 fully at home → everyone asleep and safe → hard cut to daylight Sukuna → Yuta recognition → Malevolent Shrine → world realization → deliberate concealment → CUT TO BLACK.`
