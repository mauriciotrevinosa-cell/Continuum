<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln01-home-morning -->

The inn began the morning in noise.

Momo was making breakfast.

Maomao was looking for something somebody had moved.

Rimuru had guild papers open beside a page of numbers.

Bocchi was awake in the technical sense.

Fern had already prepared to leave.

Mau sat beside Frieren.

He had not forgotten the question from the night before.

"I still don't know what a kiss is."

Frieren did not look up.

"Good."

Mau turned.

"How is that good?"

Fern passed behind them.

"I can explain it later."

Frieren finally looked up.

"No."

"Mistress Frieren."

"No."

Mau looked between them.

"...Why is everyone making this complicated?"

The answer did not arrive.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln01-okarun-umaru-kita -->

Several days away, three other people walked beside a rough cart track.

Ken Takakura—Okarun—had already been traveling with Umaru and Kita long enough for silence to have patterns.

Kita noticed when he fell half a step behind.

"Takakura-kun?"

Okarun blinked.

"...Sorry."

Umaru looked back.

"You keep doing that."

"Doing what?"

"Spacing out."

Okarun had no useful defense.

They stopped for water later.

Kita waited until Umaru was occupied with food.

"Are you okay?"

Okarun nearly said yes.

Then did not.

"...I think I'm forgetting someone."

Kita's expression changed.

Umaru heard anyway.

"Someone?"

Okarun nodded.

"Who?"

He looked down.

"...I don't know."

Umaru frowned.

"How do you know you're forgetting someone if you don't know who?"

Okarun looked at her.

"That's the problem."

No face.

No name.

No relationship he could state.

Only certainty that someone important should exist in the empty place.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln01-home-departures -->

Back home, the morning emptied in pieces.

Yuta and Anko left for a guild job.

Fern prepared for a safe solo errand.

Frieren looked up.

"Before dark."

"I know."

Mau smiled.

Frieren caught him.

"Don't."

"I didn't say anything."

"You were going to."

"...Maybe."

Fern left.

Nobody followed.

The sentence had become ordinary enough to be comforting.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln01-caravan -->

Okarun's group heard wheels before seeing the caravan.

Carts.

Pack animals.

Guards.

Travelers.

The lead merchant looked at their clothing.

"You three lost?"

Umaru answered immediately.

"Extremely."

Kita nodded.

"We don't know where we are."

Okarun added, "...Same."

The merchant asked where they came from.

Japan meant nothing to him.

After enough confused questions, he allowed them to travel with the caravan toward the next major junction.

Three visibly lost strangers on a road were not his responsibility.

Leaving them there still felt wrong.

That small choice gave them direction.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln01-coco-group -->

On another road, Coco, Qifrey, Wakana Gojo and Marin Kitagawa had formed a separate traveling group.

Coco and Qifrey looked far more compatible with the fantasy landscape than Wakana and Marin did.

Marin had attempted several genre explanations.

"Okay, so either this is another world—"

"Kitagawa-san."

"—or somebody built the most detailed historical village ever."

"Kitagawa-san."

Marin turned.

"What?"

Wakana pointed ahead.

Road.

Cart.

People.

Coco lit up.

"People!"

Qifrey put out a hand before she ran.

"Let's speak to them before deciding they're friendly."

Coco slowed.

"...Right."

Marin looked at Wakana.

"I like him."

"Please don't make this strange."

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln01-mikasa -->

Mikasa Ackerman traveled alone.

No conversation.

No wasted movement.

Higher ground first.

Water second.

Tracks.

Edible material.

A distant road.

She counted wheel marks.

Hoofprints.

Recent human passage.

Then followed from terrain where she could see travelers before they could see her.

Lost did not mean helpless.

Three separate threads moved through the same world.

None knew the others existed.
