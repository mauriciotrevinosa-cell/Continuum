<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln02-road-humor -->

The caravan merchant explained the route.

"A few more days if the road stays clear."

Umaru stopped.

"...Days?"

"Yes."

"Like... whole days?"

The merchant looked at the road.

"Usually."

Umaru pointed toward a cart.

"In this?"

"Sometimes you'll walk."

Umaru looked personally betrayed by transportation.

Kita tried not to laugh.

Okarun did laugh.

Quietly.

Umaru turned.

"You think this is funny?"

"...A little."

The first easy rhythm appeared between them.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln02-recent-word -->

Kita asked whether the merchant had seen other people dressed strangely.

Not personally.

But merchants talked.

A large group near a mercantile town.

People giving place names absent from maps.

Kita leaned closer.

"Japan?"

The merchant thought.

"That was one of the names."

Umaru straightened.

"There are Japanese people here?"

"Apparently."

"Who?"

He did not know.

Only the new term traveling guild routes.

"Guild people started calling them Otherworlders."

The word sounded fresh in his mouth.

Nickname.

Description.

Nothing ancient behind it.

For Okarun, it became direction.

If other people from Japan were here, the missing shape in his memory might have a road attached to it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln02-known-town -->

At the mercantile town, G1 and G2 no longer moved like newcomers.

They knew which street reached the guild.

Which vendor tolerated Maomao.

Which stall was cheaper.

A guild worker saw Rimuru and Mau.

"More work for the Otherworlders?"

"Apparently," Rimuru said.

Mau looked at him.

"That name stayed."

Anko, nearby, answered.

"You expected them to rename us?"

Mau considered.

"...Maybe."

The term remained light.

Useful.

No fear attached.

At a market stall, a merchant moved several plants as Maomao approached.

She saw.

"Why did you move those?"

"No reason."

"You think they're poisonous."

The merchant sighed.

"I think you're going to ask if they're poisonous."

"Are they?"

Momo reached them.

"Don't tell her."

They had been here often enough to acquire reputations.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln02-quartet-direction -->

Coco's group met merchants on their own road.

Japan failed against local geography.

So did Qifrey and Coco's places.

But now the failure pattern was recognizable.

"There are more people like you that way," a merchant said.

Marin's eyes lit when he explained the term.

"Otherworlders? That sounds kind of cool."

"Kitagawa-san."

"It does."

Qifrey cared less about the name.

More about the fact that unrelated worlds appeared to be converging.

He asked better questions.

Road.

Town.

Distance.

Guild.

A direction emerged.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln02-looking -->

Okarun went quiet again.

Kita noticed.

"You're thinking about them."

"...Yeah."

"The person you can't remember."

He nodded.

"Do you think they might be here?"

"I don't know. But if people from Japan are here..."

He could not complete the logic.

Kita completed the practical part.

"Then we look."

Okarun turned.

"Until you remember."

"...Thanks."

Umaru heard.

For once, she did not make a joke.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln02-wakana-coco -->

That evening Coco watched Wakana repair a torn strap.

"You sew?"

"Yes."

"What do you make?"

Wakana hesitated.

"Hina dolls."

"What are those?"

He began explaining.

Carefully.

Marin joined with more enthusiasm than necessary.

"They're beautiful. And Gojo-kun is insanely good at making them."

Wakana became embarrassed.

Coco took the problem seriously.

"Can you make one here?"

"...Not without materials."

Coco nodded.

"Then when we find materials."

Qifrey smiled.

Marin pointed at Coco.

"See? She gets it."
