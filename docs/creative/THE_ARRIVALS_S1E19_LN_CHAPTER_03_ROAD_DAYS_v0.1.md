<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln03-days -->

The region did not collapse to make travel convenient.

Days accumulated.

Rain.

Mud.

A wheel sank.

Okarun and the merchant pushed while Umaru complained from the dry side until Kita made her help.

A pack animal refused to move.

Marin's group sheltered under cover that stopped only some of the rain.

Coco found the storm interesting until her clothes were soaked through.

Qifrey made fire later.

Wakana repaired another piece of gear.

Mikasa crossed a stone bridge a day or more apart from the others.

The same mountain appeared behind different roads.

The same trade symbol on different markers.

The audience could see a network forming.

The travelers could not.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln03-home-life -->

While G3 struggled through roads, home continued.

Fern returned from a solo errand.

No one made an event of it.

Frieren was not waiting at the door.

Bocchi practiced.

Momo cooked.

Rimuru fought the budget.

Yuta returned tired.

Anko wrote.

Maomao ground medicine.

Mau brought home a small useful object from market.

In their shared room, Frieren read while Mau talked from across the space.

"Do you think this goes here?"

"No."

"Why?"

"Because you put everything there."

"That's why it goes there."

Frieren turned a page.

Mau moved the object anyway.

Ordinary life existed at the same time as strangers searched for it.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln03-stew -->

At the caravan camp, Umaru stared into a bowl.

"...What is this?"

"Stew," the merchant said.

"Of what?"

He thought too long.

"...Food."

Umaru held it toward Okarun.

"You try it."

"Why me?"

"Because you laughed at me earlier."

Kita nodded.

"That seems fair."

Okarun tasted.

Umaru waited.

"Well?"

He considered.

"...It's warm."

"That is not what I asked."

Kita nearly spilled her own bowl laughing.

For a few minutes, they were three people eating bad road food.

Nothing more.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln03-mikasa-network -->

Mikasa saw a caravan from a ridge.

Not close enough to identify anyone.

The same traffic system.

She counted guards.

Weapons.

Animals.

Direction.

At a marker she found the same trade symbol seen elsewhere.

She memorized it.

Continued.

The convergence became unmistakable to the reader.

Still invisible to her.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln03-forget-more -->

That night Okarun tried again.

He forced at the missing memory.

No face.

No name.

Only emotional edges.

He had argued with this person.

Wanted to protect this person.

Believed this person would look for him too.

Nothing visual arrived.

Umaru, not fully asleep, heard him shift.

"Still nothing?"

"...Nothing."

Kita spoke from the other side of the fire.

"Then don't force it."

Okarun stared upward.

"What if I forget more?"

Kita answered without drama.

"Then we remind you that you were looking for someone."

He turned toward her.

"...Okay."

Enough for tonight.
