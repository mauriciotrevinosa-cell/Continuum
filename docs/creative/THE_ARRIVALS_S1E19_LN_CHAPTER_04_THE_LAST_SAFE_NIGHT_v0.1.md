The three G3 threads slept separately.

Mikasa rested in a tree, body arranged for shallow sleep, gear ready.

At the caravan, Umaru occupied more blanket than seemed physically possible.

Kita looked up at the sky.

Okarun remained awake.

"You should sleep."

"In a minute."

Kita knew that was false.

She let him have it.

At another campfire, Marin fell asleep while still talking.

Wakana adjusted her blanket.

Coco was already gone to sleep.

Qifrey watched the fire and thought about worlds converging into roads.

This fire belonged to them.

Frieren was nowhere near it.

None of G3 reached the inn that night.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln04-return -->

At home, dusk gathered people back in pieces.

Door.

Boots.

Bags.

Guild papers.

Coins.

Small purchases.

Complaints.

Someone forgot something and returned to the entrance.

Someone else started eating before Momo officially announced dinner.

The repetition had become invisible to the people performing it.

That was what routine was.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln04-dinner -->

The table was crowded.

Mau reached toward food on Frieren's side.

She stopped him too late.

"That was mine."

"You weren't eating it."

"I was going to."

"Eventually?"

Fern suppressed a smile.

Momo and Maomao argued over whether one ingredient was food or medicine.

Bocchi asked Fern something while listening to Anko with half an ear.

Rimuru moved budget papers away from a dangerous cup.

Yuta mostly listened.

Tired.

Comfortable.

Nobody performed family.

They ate.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln04-house-closes -->

The house separated into its nighttime patterns.

Fern and Bocchi talked quietly before sleep.

Momo and Maomao shared space and continued a small argument.

Yuta slept alone.

Anko slept alone.

Rimuru finished one last note.

Mau and Frieren returned to their shared room without discussion.

Mau set a small object near the plant and flowers.

Frieren read.

"Good night."

"Good night."

Lights disappeared.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln04-home-motifs -->

Before the last light went out, the inn held the season in objects.

The E1 bucket near the window.

The plant alive inside it.

Flowers around it.

The ordinary E9 flower nearby.

Mau's books mixed with Frieren's.

One of his borrowed garments where Frieren could reach it without asking.

Bocchi's guitar and music item.

Fern's things beside a room that no longer frightened Bocchi.

Guild papers on the common table.

Improved lamps.

Maomao's crowded work area.

Boots at the entrance.

A repaired hinge.

A window that held cold out better than it used to.

Beyond the inn, neighboring ruins stood barer because their useful material had become this.

One window remained lit.

Then it went dark.

The abandoned settlement slept with people inside it.

Safe.

Reasonably.

Completely.

* * *
