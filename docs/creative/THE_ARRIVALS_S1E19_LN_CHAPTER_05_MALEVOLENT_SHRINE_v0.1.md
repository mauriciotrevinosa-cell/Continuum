<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln05-midday -->

Full daylight.

No sunrise.

No bridge from the sleeping inn.

A large regional city moved through an ordinary midday.

Stone and timber packed more densely than the mercantile town.

Market voices.

Carts.

Workers.

Children.

Guards.

A world busy with itself.

Sukuna stood inside it.

Black hair.

A stolen face carried into a world where the name Megumi Fushiguro meant nothing.

Only Sukuna was present behind it.

He did not yet understand where he was.

That did not concern him first.

Something else did.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln05-recognition -->

Cursed energy.

Far away.

Familiar.

Yuta Okkotsu.

Recognition arrived without uncertainty.

A small smile touched Sukuna's face.

"So that brat is here."

He did not investigate the street.

Did not ask why Yuta was here.

The context in his head still belonged to the conflict he had come from.

He had defeated Gojo.

Yuta existed somewhere within reach.

Arrogance required no additional geography.

People nearby noticed his hands move.

They had no framework for what they were seeing.

"Domain Expansion."

A beat.

"Malevolent Shrine."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln05-catastrophe -->

The city received no warning.

A storefront separated along a line too clean to understand.

Stone split.

Roof beams failed.

A cart became pieces before the animal pulling it knew to panic.

Then the scale widened.

Walls.

Streets.

Structures.

People caught inside an event from a power system this world had never learned to fear.

There was no opponent.

No exchange.

No battle.

Only an attack.

The destruction did not need gore to explain itself.

An alley that had held people became empty.

A market awning fell into dust.

A bell tower lost the structure that made it a tower.

Noise became collapse.

Collapse became distance.

Then, abruptly, the domain ended.

Silence arrived in pieces.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln05-wrong-world -->

Sukuna stood in the aftermath.

Only now did he properly look.

The architecture was wrong.

Weapons wrong.

Clothing wrong.

Road design wrong.

Nothing matched the place he expected.

He turned slowly.

Curiosity replaced the first impulse.

"...Hm."

Not fear.

Not regret.

A new fact.

Possibly a new world.

Then another thought.

If he had sensed Yuta from far away, the enormous burst of cursed energy might have worked both directions.

Sukuna had already announced himself once.

There was no reason to continue.

His cursed energy dropped.

From immense and unmistakable to deliberately suppressed.

Controlled.

Almost absent at distance.

The world was unfamiliar.

Yuta was somewhere inside it.

That was enough information for now.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e19-ln05-season-end -->

Sukuna walked through the ruined street.

Behind him, the city held evidence of something it had no name for yet.

He did not look back.

His cursed energy remained suppressed.

A small smile.

Season One ended without anyone at the inn knowing what had just entered their world.
