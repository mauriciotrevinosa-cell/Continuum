## Okarun memory-gap nuance

Okarun does **not** begin from a vague idea that he may have forgotten someone.

He clearly remembers that there is **someone important he should remember / be looking for**, but he cannot recover that person's identity, name, face, or enough specific context to identify them.

The emotional shape is therefore:

> There is someone.
> I know there is someone.
> I just don't know who.

This is specifically the Momo-shaped memory hole, but Momo is not named or revealed in S1E19.

Representative dialogue direction:

Kita: "Is there someone you're looking for?"

Okarun: "Yes."

Kita: "Who?"

Okarun hesitates, visibly disturbed.

Okarun: "I don't know."

Umaru: "How can you know you're looking for someone if you don't know who they are?"

Okarun: "That's the problem."

He can know the absence is real while being unable to retrieve the person attached to it.
