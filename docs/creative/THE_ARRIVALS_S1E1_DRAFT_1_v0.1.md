# S1E1 — `Not This Time`

## Scene 1 — Frieren & Fern

The fight is already in progress.

No long establishing sequence. No sense that this is a farewell.

Magic tears through the clearing in short, practiced exchanges. Frieren and Fern move with the economy of people who have fought beside each other for years. They do not call out every action. They do not need to.

Fern watches the enemy through the fading edge of one spell and sees a narrow opening.

Her eyes sharpen.

She moves.

Frieren sees it too.

For the smallest fraction of a second, the choice makes sense.

Then Frieren sees what Fern does not: the enemy is giving ground too cleanly. The opening is real, but the space behind it is not safe.

Fern has already committed.

Frieren's expression changes.

**FRIEREN**  
Fern, wait—

Too late.

The enemy turns into the attack instead of away from it.

Fern's path closes.

Frieren does not hesitate.

She fires.

A violent flash consumes everything.

---

## Scene 2 — The Shot

White.

Then silence.

Shapes return slowly.

The enemy is down.

Frieren's shot worked.

She looks immediately to where Fern was.

Nothing.

Her eyes search the ground first, then the air, then the edges of the clearing.

**FRIEREN**  
Fern?

No answer.

She takes a few steps.

**FRIEREN**  
Fern.

Where Fern stood, there is a small crater.

Frieren kneels beside it.

She feels for mana.

Nothing she can identify.

She checks the debris, the broken ground, the direction of the blast. Still nothing.

Then she sees it.

Fern's hair ornament.

Frieren picks it up carefully.

For a moment, she only stares at it.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

She looks around again, faster this time.

**FRIEREN**  
No...

She begins moving pieces of debris aside with magic.

Nothing.

She moves more.

Her rhythm becomes less precise.

**FRIEREN**  
No. No, no...

At some point she stops using magic efficiently and starts moving smaller pieces with her hands.

There is no body.

There is no answer.

Only the ornament in her palm.

---

## Scene 3 — Day One

By the time Stark reaches the clearing, Frieren is still searching.

He sees the crater.

He sees the ornament in her hand.

He does not ask the obvious question.

Frieren is already studying the ground beyond the blast radius.

She starts walking.

**STARK**  
Where are you going?

**FRIEREN**  
If the blast threw her clear, she would've landed this way.

Stark follows.

They search the slope below the clearing.

Nothing.

They check broken branches, disturbed soil, shallow depressions, anything that could mark a fall.

Nothing.

Later, Frieren is back near the crater, kneeling over the soil again.

Stark waits behind her.

**STARK**  
Teleportation?

Frieren studies the residue for a long moment.

**FRIEREN**  
Maybe.

That single possibility is enough to keep her moving.

They continue until the light is nearly gone.

Stark slows.

Frieren does not.

He watches her disappear a little farther into the dark and follows.

---

## Scene 4 — The Search

Morning.

Frieren leaves the inn first.

Stark follows with supplies.

They search.

Another morning.

Same door.

Same road.

A different patch of forest.

Frieren kneels beside a mark in the dirt.

Stark looks at it, then at her.

**STARK**  
That's from yesterday.

Frieren stops.

She looks at the mark again.

She remembers.

No reply.

She stands and keeps walking.

Another day.

Stark places food near her while she studies a rough map and a collection of notes.

**STARK**  
Eat.

**FRIEREN**  
Later.

Time passes.

The food is still there.

Stark moves it directly in front of her.

**STARK**  
You said that yesterday.

Frieren finally looks at him.

He does not move the plate.

She eats a little.

Stark looks away while she does.

Another night.

They return exhausted.

Stark barely makes it through the door before dropping his things.

Frieren begins organizing supplies for the next search.

Another map.

More marks.

More crossed areas.

More ground already covered.

Stark looks at the map.

Then at Frieren.

He is starting to understand something she refuses to name.

---

## Scene 5 — Stark Says It

Day six.

Frieren is preparing to leave again.

Stark recognizes the direction before she even reaches the door.

**STARK**  
We already searched there.

Frieren keeps packing.

**FRIEREN**  
I know.

Stark stares at her.

**STARK**  
Then why—

**FRIEREN**  
I might've missed something.

Stark looks at the dark circles under her eyes. The dirt still on her clothes. The way her hands move from habit rather than energy.

**STARK**  
Frieren...

She keeps going.

**STARK**  
It's been six days.

Frieren pauses, but only for a second.

**STARK**  
If Fern was hurt...

He struggles with the next words.

**STARK**  
...she would've answered us.

**FRIEREN**  
Not necessarily.

**STARK**  
We searched everywhere.

**FRIEREN**  
Then we search again.

Silence.

Stark looks down.

When he speaks again, his voice is lower.

**STARK**  
She's probably dead.

Frieren stops completely.

Stark regrets the sentence as soon as it exists.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

**STARK**  
Frieren—

She turns toward him.

There is no anger in her face.

Only certainty she cannot afford to lose.

**FRIEREN**  
Fern isn't dead.

She leaves.

The door closes.

Stark stands alone in the room.

For several seconds, he tries to breathe normally.

He cannot.

He lowers his head.

The tears come quietly.

Outside, Frieren walks alone down the road, growing smaller against the landscape.

Inside, Stark finally lets himself grieve.

---

## Scene 6 — The Last Search

Day seven.

Frieren returns to the crater alone.

For a while, she only stands there.

Then she begins again.

She checks the ground.

Nothing.

She looks for traces of mana.

Nothing.

She walks the perimeter.

Nothing.

She studies the direction of the blast.

Nothing.

No new clue appears.

No hidden answer waits beneath the soil.

No body.

No teleportation trace she can identify.

No explanation.

Frieren kneels where she found the ornament.

She takes it out and holds it in her hand.

Her voice is barely above a whisper.

**FRIEREN**  
Fern?

Silence.

Frieren stays there for a long time.

Eventually, she stands.

And for the first time all week, she walks away from the crater without choosing another place to search.

The crater remains behind her.

Empty.

---

## Scene 7 — I'm Sorry

Night.

The inn door opens.

Stark is still awake in the common room.

He looks up immediately.

Frieren steps inside.

She looks exhausted beyond speech.

For a moment, Stark seems ready to say something.

He doesn't.

He watches her walk past him toward her room.

Frieren closes the door behind her.

She removes only what she has to.

No routine. No notes. No preparation for tomorrow.

She sits on the bed.

Then lies down.

The hair ornament rests in her hand.

She brings it against her chest.

A long silence.

**FRIEREN**  
I'm sorry, Fern.

Another silence.

Longer.

**FRIEREN**  
I'm sorry, Heiter.

Her eyes close.

She does not choose to sleep.

Her body simply gives out.

Her hand remains around the ornament.

Darkness.

---

## Scene 8 — Another Sky

Wind through leaves.

A distant birdcall Frieren does not recognize.

Her eyes open.

Sky.

For one half-awake second, she does not move.

Then she realizes she is not in the inn.

Grass beneath her.

Different air.

Different light.

Frieren slowly sits up.

She studies the trees, the plants, the horizon she can see between them.

She reaches outward with her senses.

There is mana in the world.

But nothing about this place tells her where she is.

No familiar spell structure.

No recognizable teleportation residue.

No explanation.

She looks down at herself.

Her expression shifts slightly.

She did not fall asleep dressed like this.

She is wearing her normal travel clothes.

Her staff lies nearby.

Her travel case / luggage is there as well.

Frieren looks from the staff to the case, then back to herself.

Another unanswered question.

Her hand closes around something.

The ornament.

It came with her.

Frieren looks at it for a long moment.

Then she places it in her own hair.

She adjusts her position and turns to take in the rest of the clearing.

A person is lying roughly a meter away from her.

Frieren freezes for the smallest beat.

She had not sensed him.

A young adult man. Early-to-mid twenties in appearance. Taller than her by a clear margin — around 176 cm — but motionless in the grass.

Frieren looks at him.

Then at the tiny distance between them.

Then back at him.

She moves closer and kneels.

He is breathing.

Alive.

Frieren's expression becomes quietly, genuinely curious.

---

## Scene 9 — Mau

Blurred light.

Mau's point of view.

A shape above him.

He blinks.

The world begins to focus.

White hair.

Green eyes.

Long ears.

Frieren is leaning over him, watching with calm curiosity.

The image sharpens fully.

So does the sound.

**FRIEREN**  
Can you hear me?

Mau stares for a beat.

He gives the smallest response — enough for her to know he understood.

Frieren waits.

**FRIEREN**  
What's your name?

Mau opens his mouth.

Nothing comes immediately.

A tiny pause.

Not long enough to be dramatic.

Just long enough for the question to become frightening.

What was his name?

Then it comes.

**MAU**  
...Mau.

Frieren registers the hesitation.

**FRIEREN**  
Where are you from?

Mau searches for the answer the same way.

This time, nothing arrives.

His eyes shift, as if the missing information might be somewhere around him.

**MAU**  
...I don't know.

**FRIEREN**  
Do you remember how you got here?

Mau looks at the grass, the trees, the sky.

Nothing means anything to him.

**MAU**  
No.

A pause.

**FRIEREN**  
Your home?

Mau searches.

Nothing.

He shakes his head slightly.

Frieren waits before the next question.

**FRIEREN**  
Family?

Mau tries again.

No face appears.

No voice.

No room.

No name.

His expression changes.

**MAU**  
...No.

Frieren stops asking questions.

Mau looks down at his hands.

His breathing is a little less steady now.

He knows his name.

That may be all he knows about himself.

Frieren studies him again.

Now that he is awake, the absence is even stranger.

Alive. Speaking. Human-looking.

And almost nothing detectable from him in mana.

She does not comment on it.

Mau raises his eyes toward her.

For him, she is the only person in the world he can currently place anywhere at all.

---

## Scene 10 — The Noise

Mau sits in the grass, still processing the emptiness behind his own name.

Then something catches inside his thoughts.

Not pain.

Not exactly sound.

A fragment.

`...unk...`

Mau blinks.

Another broken piece.

`...known...`

His hand rises toward his head.

Frieren notices immediately.

**FRIEREN**  
What's wrong?

Mau looks around, trying to find where the sound came from.

**MAU**  
I... heard something.

Frieren's attention shifts to the trees around them.

**FRIEREN**  
What?

Mau listens.

Nothing.

He looks at her, unsettled.

**MAU**  
Didn't you hear it?

Frieren gives a small shake of her head.

Mau looks around again.

Nothing moves except the leaves.

**MAU**  
...I don't know.

Frieren studies him.

For once, she has no useful explanation to offer.

---

## Scene 11 — Flowers

The clearing is quiet again.

Mau still looks uneasy.

Frieren listens to the surroundings once more.

No threat.

Nothing she can identify.

She looks at Mau.

He has awakened without a past, in a place he does not know, beside a stranger he has no reason to trust.

Now he has heard something she did not.

Frieren sits near him.

**FRIEREN**  
I didn't hear anything.

Mau looks down.

Frieren raises one hand.

A small glow of magic gathers.

One flower appears in the grass.

Then another.

Then several more.

The ordinary clearing slowly becomes a small field of flowers around them.

Mau forgets to look afraid.

He reaches toward one.

Touches it.

Real.

He looks at Frieren.

She is not showing off.

To her, this is simply something she can do.

**FRIEREN**  
I don't know what's happening to you.

Mau watches her.

A beat.

**FRIEREN**  
But I'll help.

Mau does not answer.

He looks back at the flowers.

His shoulders loosen for the first time since waking.

From behind them, Frieren and Mau sit surrounded by the flowers.

Fern's ornament is visible in Frieren's hair.

No dialogue.

Just the two of them in the first peaceful moment of the new world.

---

## Scene 12 — The Bucket

Later.

Frieren has begun turning the clearing into something survivable.

She hands Mau a bucket.

**FRIEREN**  
We need water.

She lets go of the handle.

**FRIEREN**  
Help me.

Mau looks at the bucket with the seriousness of someone being entrusted with an important task.

He leaves.

Frieren continues arranging the camp.

Some time later, footsteps approach.

Mau returns.

He has the bucket.

It is empty.

In his other hand is a small plant he found nearby.

He seems genuinely pleased with himself.

**MAU**  
I found this.

Frieren looks at the plant.

Then the empty bucket.

Then Mau.

A tiny smile escapes her.

The first real one since Fern disappeared.

Mau catches it.

**MAU**  
What?

Frieren's expression settles again.

**FRIEREN**  
Nothing.

She takes the bucket.

**FRIEREN**  
Come on.

They walk through the trees together.

Ahead, water becomes visible between the trunks — a river widening toward a lake-like bend.

Frieren carries the bucket.

Mau still carries the plant.

Neither comments on this.

---

## Scene 13 — Learning

At the water, Frieren fills the bucket properly.

Mau watches.

On the way back, Frieren pauses to inspect a piece of fallen wood.

Mau watches that too.

Later, Frieren points toward a pile of branches.

**FRIEREN**  
We'll need firewood.

Mau gathers several pieces.

One is damp and half-rotted.

Frieren removes it from the pile.

**FRIEREN**  
Not that one.

She picks up a dry branch instead.

**FRIEREN**  
This one.

Mau studies it.

Later, he brings back another dry branch without being told.

Frieren notices.

No comment.

A small animal moves through the brush nearby.

Both Frieren and Mau turn at exactly the same time.

The animal pauses.

Frieren tilts her head slightly.

Mau makes almost the same gesture without realizing it.

Neither notices the other doing it.

The animal runs off.

They both return to what they were doing.

Later, Frieren opens her travel case.

For the first time all day, there is the faintest hint of pride in her voice.

**FRIEREN**  
This is my secret stash.

Mau looks interested.

Frieren reveals the rock-hard bread.

A beat.

Frieren bites into a piece.

Her face immediately shows the familiar, tiny suffering of someone who knows exactly how hard this bread is and is eating it anyway.

Mau tries his piece.

His expression develops into the same quiet misery.

They chew in silence.

Neither admits defeat.

By sunset, the small camp has taken shape.

A modest shelter.

The bucket nearby.

Mau's plant set off to one side.

Frieren and Mau work together to get the fire going.

Through the trees behind them, the river catches the last light.

The sun drops lower.

The fire finally takes.

---

## Scene 14 — By the Fire

Night settles slowly.

Frieren and Mau sit near the fire.

Not facing each other like an interview.

Just sharing the same warmth.

For a while, neither speaks.

Mau watches the flames.

**MAU**  
Do you remember where you came from?

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Mau is quiet for a moment.

That answer came too easily compared with every question Frieren asked him.

**MAU**  
Do you remember your family?

Frieren keeps her eyes on the fire.

**FRIEREN**  
I did.

Mau looks toward her.

He hears the past tense.

**MAU**  
Did they die?

A pause.

Frieren's hand moves unconsciously toward the ornament in her hair.

Fern.

Heiter.

People separated from her by far more than distance.

**FRIEREN**  
...Yes.

Mau lowers his eyes.

**MAU**  
I'm sorry.

Frieren looks into the fire.

**FRIEREN**  
Me too.

The flames crack softly between them.

After another stretch of silence:

**MAU**  
What are you going to do?

Frieren answers without much hesitation.

**FRIEREN**  
Figure out where we are.

A pause.

She looks toward him.

**FRIEREN**  
And what happened to you.

Mau looks back at her.

He does not say thank you.

He does not need to.

When he returns his eyes to the fire, his posture is slightly easier than before.

The ornament catches a little of the firelight.

For the first time, they do not look like two strangers who happened to wake near each other.

They look like two people sharing a camp.

---

## Scene 15 — The Blanket

The fire burns lower.

The temperature drops with it.

Frieren settles beneath the small shelter near the tree.

Mau chooses a spot a short distance away.

He tries to lie down without the blanket.

Frieren looks over.

**FRIEREN**  
You'll be cold over there.

**MAU**  
I'm okay.

Frieren watches him for another second.

He is visibly shivering.

**FRIEREN**  
You're shivering.

Mau looks down at himself.

A small beat of surrender.

Frieren lifts the edge of the blanket enough to make room.

No invitation beyond that.

Mau moves closer.

He leaves a careful amount of space between them at first.

Physically, he is a grown young man and clearly larger than Frieren.

Nothing about the composition should make him look like a child.

But right now he is completely new to this world and completely dependent on the one person beside him.

They settle in.

Mau looks at the fading fire.

Then the dark sky.

Then, briefly, Frieren.

Frieren is still awake.

Mau closes his eyes.

It does not take long.

His breathing evens out.

He falls asleep first.

Frieren turns her eyes toward him.

A faint surprise.

Already?

Then she understands what it means.

He knows almost nothing about her.

He has nowhere else to go.

And his body decided that beside her was safe enough to sleep.

The blanket has slipped slightly away from him.

Frieren reaches over and adjusts it, covering him properly.

Mau does not wake.

The firelight moves softly across his sleeping face.

Frieren remains awake beside him.

---

## Scene 16 — Not This Time

Frieren keeps watching Mau for another moment.

The forest is quiet.

The fire is almost gone.

Her hand rises to the ornament in her hair.

She touches it lightly.

A flash of memory.

Not the crater.

Not the explosion.

The young Fern Frieren first met — still a child, at the beginning of everything between them.

The image is gone almost as soon as it appears.

Frieren looks back at Mau.

Sleeping without fear beside someone he met only hours ago.

She adjusts the blanket one last time.

A single thought.

**FRIEREN (THOUGHT)**  
Not this time.

Frieren closes her eyes.

Wide view.

The small camp beneath the tree.

Frieren and Mau under the same blanket.

A weak fire still glowing nearby.

The river only faintly visible through the trees.

Beyond them, a world neither of them understands.

For tonight, they are alive.

And they are not alone.

**END — S1E1**
