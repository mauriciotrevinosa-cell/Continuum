## Production rule

This document does not add or rewrite story beats. It fragments the approved episode into pages and panels so the manga can be produced. Page counts, panel counts, choreography and exact composition remain adjustable during rough manga if the visual result needs more or less space.

The story determines the manga length; the manga is not forced into chapter/page quotas.

## Approved chapter cuts for rough

The chapter structure below is approved as the current rough-manga structure. Exact page numbers may shift if rough pacing expands or contracts a scene.

- **Chapter 1 — approx. pages 1–40:** fight with Fern through Frieren awakening in the new world and the visual reveal of Mau lying about one metre away. **End on Frieren seeing Mau.**
- **Chapter 2 — approx. pages 41–55:** Frieren approaches Mau; name/amnesia questions; the internal noise; flower magic; Frieren decides to help him. **End on a large back shot of Frieren and Mau together among the flowers after `But I'll help.`**
- **Chapter 3 — approx. pages 56–79:** bucket, first-day learning, campfire conversation, shared blanket, Fern-child memory and `Not this time.`

These cuts are story-driven, not equal-length targets. Chapter 1 may be much longer than Chapter 2 or Chapter 3.

# SCENE 1 — FRIEREN & FERN
**Pages 1–7**  
**Rough flexibility:** may naturally expand to 8 pages if choreography needs more room.

### Page 1 — already inside the fight
- Large opening panel: damaged forest clearing; Frieren and Fern already moving against the enemy. No calm establishing introduction.
- Fern attacks.
- Enemy evades and counters.
- Frieren intercepts the counter from another angle.
- No dialogue.

### Page 2 — synchronization
- Fern maintains frontal pressure.
- Enemy attempts a lateral escape.
- Frieren already covers that lane.
- Enemy changes direction.
- Fern has already repositioned without needing to look at Frieren.
- End on a readable triangular composition showing that they are containing the enemy together.
- No dialogue.

### Page 3 — the fight begins to tilt
- Combined pressure forces a more serious block/retreat.
- Detail of the enemy giving ground.
- Fern studies the movement.
- Frieren continues pressure without concern.
- A clean corridor begins to open in front of Fern.
- Small change in Fern's eyes: she has seen it.

### Page 4 — Fern commits
- Fern's read of the battlefield makes the opening look legitimate.
- She adjusts stance/grip and starts forward.
- Frieren sees the same opening and is not yet alarmed.
- Fern crosses into the space confidently.
- No `I see it.` line; the decision is communicated visually.

### Page 5 — Frieren sees the hidden problem
- Fern continues into the opening.
- Enemy continues to retreat.
- Small detail reveals the retreat is too clean: posture/weight/hand/defensive angle shows preparation.
- Frieren's eyes change.
- Fern is already too committed.
- Frieren moves suddenly.
- **FRIEREN:** `Fern, wait—`

### Page 6 — too late
- Enemy stops retreating and turns into Fern's attack.
- Fern immediately understands the trap; no exaggerated terror.
- Her route closes.
- Frieren raises her staff.
- Attack lines converge.
- Large lower panel: Frieren fires; light begins consuming the drawing.

### Page 7 — the shot
- Near full-page magical discharge consuming enemy, terrain and silhouettes.
- Detail and screentone disappear toward white.
- Tiny final panel or white space: silence.
- Direct transition into Scene 2.

## Rough-manga review questions

The rough is the first stage where pacing becomes a visual fact rather than a text estimate. Review should focus on:

- whether Scene 1 choreography needs 7 or 8+ pages;
- whether silence/negative space in Scenes 2, 5, 6, 7 and 16 reads correctly;
- whether Frieren/Fern identity and acting remain source-faithful;
- whether Mau consistently reads as an adult early/mid-20s character and not a child;
- whether Chapter 1's Mau reveal is strong enough to end there;
- whether Chapter 2's flower-field back shot lands as the intended emotional close;
- whether the daily-life material in Chapter 3 earns `Not this time.` without feeling rushed or padded;
- whether any page turn should move after seeing actual layouts.

## Production reference principle

When Continuum produces rough pages, references should be selected per need rather than asking one source to determine the whole look. Character/canon references, technique/composition references, continuity references and mood references remain separate. The Arrivals should develop its own coherent visual language while learning useful techniques from the user's Library.
