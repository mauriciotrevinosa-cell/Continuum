## Approval

S1E2 `First Home` has received human GREEN LIGHT for Stage 2 Voice Check.

Approved voice direction:

- **Frieren** should remain concise, observant, dry, and emotionally restrained. When emotional subjects surface, she generally says less rather than more.
- **Mau** should sound direct, curious, and literal without becoming cartoonishly naive. He asks questions because he genuinely lacks context, not because he is unintelligent.
- Their early trust should be evident in behavior and short exchanges rather than repeated declarations.
- Exposition that can be shown visually during manga panelization should be reduced or removed.

Representative approved interaction types include:

- Mau asking whether Frieren knows where they are going; Frieren plainly admitting that she does not.
- Mau noticing the abandoned settlement and Frieren identifying visual signs that nobody lives there.
- Mau questioning whether the damaged inn is really suitable; Frieren valuing the practical fact that it is mostly dry and usable.
- Mau asking whether the inn is now their home; Frieren distinguishing between `somewhere to stay` and `home` without overexplaining.
- At night, Mau asking whether Frieren is waiting for someone; Frieren quietly acknowledging the possibility of Fern.
- The episode ending when Bocchi's scream interrupts the quiet night conversation.
