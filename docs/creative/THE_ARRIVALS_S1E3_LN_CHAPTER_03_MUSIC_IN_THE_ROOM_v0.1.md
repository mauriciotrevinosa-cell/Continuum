<!-- scene-id: s1e3-ln03-morning -->

Frieren woke against Mau.

She noticed that first.

Then Bocchi asleep on the other side of the fire.

Then Mau awake beneath her.

"How long have you been awake?"

"I don't know."

Frieren looked at him.

"You say that a lot."

Mau looked back.

Frieren's mouth moved by almost nothing.

Bocchi woke at exactly the wrong moment.

Her eyes opened.

She saw them.

She immediately closed her eyes again.

Mau watched.

"You're awake."

Bocchi opened them.

"...Yeah."

Nobody mentioned the night before.

The mercy was mutual.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e3-ln03-contribution -->

Bocchi did not become comfortable the next day.

She became useful.

Useful was easier.

Frieren and Mau prepared to search nearby houses for salvage and food.

Bocchi looked toward the snowy lane.

Then at the common-room mess.

"I can... stay here and clean."

The sentence sounded like an apology.

Frieren looked around.

"That would help."

Bocchi blinked.

No lecture about facing fears.

No suggestion that staying inside meant failure.

After they left, the inn became too quiet almost immediately.

Bocchi nearly followed them.

Instead she picked up the broom.

When Frieren and Mau returned, one corner of the common room was noticeably cleaner.

Not because Bocchi possessed secret housekeeping talent.

Because she had cleaned the same area three times while thinking about everything that could happen outside.

Mau stopped near the doorway.

"It looks better."

Bocchi tried not to look pleased.

She failed a little.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e3-ln03-exit-line -->

Over the next several days, Bocchi developed a pattern.

Inside.

Near a doorway.

Outside for one task.

Back inside.

Then, one afternoon, Frieren and Mau left together.

Bocchi remained near the entrance after the door closed.

She needed visual access to the exit.

Mau had noticed that about her by then.

Later, when the three of them sat near the fire, Bocchi chose a place that let her see the door.

Mau shifted a basket that partially blocked the line without saying anything.

Bocchi noticed.

She did not thank him.

That would require acknowledging why it mattered.

She simply relaxed by one degree.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e3-ln03-guitar -->

Mau stopped asking about the guitar.

That created a new problem.

Bocchi noticed him looking at the case one evening.

He did not say anything.

Now she had to wonder if he wanted to hear it.

Which meant she had to decide whether *not* playing would be rude.

Which meant she had created an obligation that nobody else had created.

After several minutes of losing the argument with herself, Bocchi opened the case.

Mau looked over.

Frieren sat near the fire with a book.

Bocchi placed the guitar across her lap.

Her hands knew this.

That helped.

The first notes were quiet.

Mau stopped what he was doing.

Frieren lowered the book slightly.

Bocchi saw both reactions.

Her fingers nearly stopped.

Mau did not praise her.

Did not say *wow.*

Did not turn it into a performance.

He just listened.

Frieren turned a page.

She did not raise the book enough to hide behind it again.

Bocchi kept playing.

For the first time since waking in this world, something sounded exactly like itself.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e3-ln03-several-days -->

Routine grew around the music.

Morning.

Frieren and Mau left with packs.

Bocchi watched from the doorway.

They returned with wood, food, seeds, and salvage.

Another day.

Bocchi and Mau sorted nails.

Neither knew enough to teach the other. They created two piles, argued silently with the shape of several bent pieces, and eventually asked Frieren.

Another afternoon.

Frieren worked near the field.

Bocchi stood at the inn entrance.

One step outside.

Then more.

She stopped when she realized how far she had gone.

Frieren saw.

She did not comment.

Another evening.

Mau carried water.

Bocchi held the door open with one foot while holding kindling.

Frieren entered behind them with something edible.

Three people moved around one another without planning the movement.

There were three bowls on the table now.

Three blankets near the fire.

Three sets of belongings upstairs.

The building remained broken.

It no longer looked empty.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e3-ln03-closing -->

One night, Bocchi finished a song fragment.

The last note disappeared into the common room.

Immediately, uncertainty returned.

Had it been too much?

Too long?

Mau looked up.

"Do another one."

Bocchi stared.

Frieren did not lower her book.

"If you want."

The exact phrase Mau had used at dinner.

Bocchi noticed.

She adjusted her fingers.

"...Okay."

Outside, the settlement remained dark.

Only the inn held light.

Inside, music began again.

The house was not fixed.

None of them were.

The room was safe enough anyway.
