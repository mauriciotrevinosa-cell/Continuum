## Voice Check approval

S1E3 receives **GREEN LIGHT** for Stage 2.

Approved voice / relationship direction:

- Bocchi is socially anxious, catastrophizes neutral situations, and can be funny, but must not be reduced to constant screaming or meme behavior.
- Her sensitivity and observational nature should remain visible underneath the anxiety.
- Mau is direct, patient, literal, and kind without producing polished motivational speeches.
- Frieren remains restrained and economical, especially when emotionally distressed.
- The night scene should show Frieren's grief in fragments rather than a perfect monologue.
- Mau primarily supports Frieren through presence rather than trying to solve grief with words.
- Bocchi seeing Frieren cry is important because it reveals that the people helping her are also hurting.

## Required dialogue seed — DO NOT REMOVE

The episode must preserve an explicit Mau-to-Bocchi promise equivalent to:

> **Mau: “I’ll help you.”**

This matters because Mau is echoing the care Frieren gave him earlier, and the promise becomes a future emotional callback when Bocchi later tells Mau:

> **“We’re here to help you too.”**

The intended emotional chain is:

```text
Frieren -> Mau -> Bocchi -> Mau
```

This is a meaningful thematic continuity beat and should survive full episode construction and manga adaptation unless the exact wording changes while preserving the unmistakable callback.
