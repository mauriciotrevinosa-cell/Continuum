<!-- scene-id: s1e4-ln03-night -->

Yuta did not ask for a permanent place.

"Can I stay tonight?"

There were more rooms than people.

Frieren showed him the corridor.

He chose one without ceremony.

Bocchi watched him set his sword within reach of the bed.

"Do you sleep with that there?"

"Usually."

Bocchi glanced toward her own room.

"I sleep with mine there too."

Yuta looked at the guitar case.

"Probably for different reasons."

Bocchi considered it.

"...Mostly."

Yuta laughed softly.

The next morning, he was still there.

No one asked whether he had joined.

<!-- scene-id: s1e4-ln03-beam -->

Mau was trying to move a beam across the common room when Yuta came downstairs.

It was not especially heavy.

It was awkward enough to make Mau lose the argument with it.

Yuta took the other end.

Mau adjusted his grip.

"Thanks."

"Where does it go?"

Mau looked around.

"I was hoping you knew."

Yuta looked at him.

Then the beam.

"Okay. Put it down."

They did.

Frieren passed behind them.

"Storage."

Both men looked toward the storage area.

Yuta nodded.

"We were close."

They were not.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e4-ln03-hunting-seed -->

Later, Yuta returned with food.

Bocchi stared at what he carried.

"You found all that today?"

"Yeah."

Mau came closer.

"Can you teach me?"

"To hunt?"

"To do that."

He gestured at the useful result rather than Yuta's weapon.

Yuta understood.

"Sure."

Across the room, Frieren looked up.

Not threatened.

Just noticing.

Mau had asked somebody else to teach him something.

New.

Bocchi took one of the supplies toward the kitchen area.

Yuta reached for another.

Mau followed.

Frieren returned to her book.

The room held four people moving through it.

At dinner, there were four bowls.

No one announced what that meant.
