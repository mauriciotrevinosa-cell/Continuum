## Dialogue-flow progression rule

A small amount of question-and-answer rhythm is acceptable here because these characters are still unfamiliar with one another.

However, as Season 1 progresses, dialogue should become noticeably more fluid and less mechanically turn-based.

Later conversations should increasingly use:

- interruptions;
- shared context;
- callbacks;
- unfinished thoughts;
- internal jokes;
- reactions that do not require explicit questions;
- overlapping emotional knowledge;
- less obvious `question -> answer -> question -> answer` construction.

The growing naturalness of conversation should itself show that the household is becoming familiar and close.
