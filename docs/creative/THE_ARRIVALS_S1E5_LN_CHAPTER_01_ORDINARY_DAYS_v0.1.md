<!-- scene-id: s1e5-ln01-morning-noise -->

The inn woke in layers.

Old wood contracted in the cold.

Someone rebuilt the fire.

From upstairs came the same quiet phrase on Bocchi's guitar three times in a row because she apparently believed playing softly meant nobody could hear her.

Everyone could hear her.

Mau sat at the table wrapping salvaged wire around the handle of a pot.

Yuta sharpened his sword nearby.

Frieren came downstairs with a book.

She looked at the pot.

"That's going to fall off."

"It hasn't yet."

Yuta glanced over.

"She's right."

Mau looked at him.

"You were supposed to be on my side."

"I didn't know there were sides."

The handle came off in Mau's hand.

No one spoke.

Mau stared at it.

Frieren walked past.

"Mostly fixed."

Yuta laughed.

Mau watched her go.

The phrase had officially become household property.

Outside the window, a deer moved between the distant trees.

No one noticed.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e5-ln01-hunting -->

Yuta gathered gear later that morning.

Mau noticed.

"Can I come?"

Yuta looked toward Frieren automatically.

She was reading.

Without lifting her eyes:

"Don't get him killed."

Mau looked at her.

"Why did you answer for me?"

Yuta adjusted a strap.

"Because I was going to say yes."

Mau considered this.

"Okay."

They left.

The forest still held patches of snow.

Yuta taught without calling it teaching.

Tracks.

Compressed ground.

Broken branches.

Where to place a foot.

How not to step on every loud object available.

Mau stepped on a branch.

Yuta looked back.

Mau looked down.

"That one was hidden."

"It was on top of the snow."

They kept moving.

A deer appeared once between trees, far ahead.

It saw them and disappeared before either could do anything.

Mau watched the empty space.

Later another shape moved through the brush.

Same deer, perhaps.

Or another.

By the time Yuta finally lowered himself and pointed, Mau was ready.

A deer stood between two trees.

It had already seen them.

"It knows we're here," Mau whispered.

"Yeah."

The deer watched.

Mau watched back.

"I think it's planning something."

Yuta slowly turned his head.

"It's a deer."

"That doesn't mean it's not planning something."

The deer bolted.

Mau pointed after it.

"See?"

Yuta covered his mouth.

They did not catch that deer.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e5-ln01-bocchi-outside -->

Back at the inn, Bocchi helped Frieren sort dried plants and food.

Frieren took a basket and went toward the field.

Usually Bocchi found another task inside.

Today she picked up a second basket.

Doorway.

Stop.

Frieren was already halfway across the lane.

Bocchi stepped outside.

Then again.

Cold air.

Nothing happened.

Frieren looked back.

She did not praise her.

She simply waited long enough for Bocchi to catch up.

They worked near the field.

Silence was comfortable for Frieren.

For Bocchi, silence slowly became louder than speech.

"Do you think the snow will come back?"

"Yes."

Bocchi waited.

No elaboration.

A faint smile appeared.

She was beginning to understand how Frieren worked.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e5-ln01-return -->

Yuta and Mau returned later.

Yuta carried most of the useful result.

Mau carried kindling, two edible plants he had recognized correctly, and a story.

"The deer escaped."

"Most deer do," Frieren said.

"It was suspicious."

Bocchi looked up.

"Suspicious?"

Yuta entered behind him.

"Don't encourage him."

"It was watching us."

Frieren looked at Mau.

"You were hunting it."

Mau paused.

"That explains some of it."

Bocchi laughed.

Everyone looked at her.

She immediately regretted producing sound in public.

Yuta kept unloading supplies.

The conversation moved on before she could apologize.

That helped.
