# SCENE 1 — MORNING NOISE
**Pages 1–5**

### Page 1
- Inn dawn; old wood, fire, faint guitar upstairs.
- Mau at table fixing pot handle; Yuta sharpening sword; Frieren descends with book.

### Page 2
- Frieren checks repair.
- **FRIEREN:** `That's going to fall off.`
- **MAU:** `It hasn't yet.`
- **YUTA:** `She's right.`

### Page 3
- **MAU:** `You were supposed to be on my side.`
- **YUTA:** `I didn't know there were sides.`
- Handle comes off.
- Silent beat.

### Page 4
- Frieren walks past: **`Mostly fixed.`**
- Yuta laughs.
- Mau watches Frieren leave, mildly betrayed.

### Page 5
- Bocchi appears with broom / guitar sound stops.
- Four-person morning composition establishing routine rather than exposition.

# SCENE 2 — HUNTING
**Pages 6–14**

### Page 6
- Yuta gathering hunting gear.
- **MAU:** `Can I come?`
- Yuta glances toward Frieren.
- **FRIEREN (reading):** `Don't get him killed.`

### Page 7
- **MAU:** `Why did you answer for me?`
- **YUTA:** `Because I was going to say yes.`
- Mau: `Okay.`
- Departure.

### Page 8
- Forest lesson montage: tracks, branches, snow compression.
- Yuta points, Mau observes.

### Page 9
- Mau steps on obvious branch.
- Yuta looks back.
- **MAU:** `That one was hidden.`
- **YUTA:** `It was on top of the snow.`

### Page 10
- Yuta stops / lowers stance.
- Mau copies.
- Deer reveal through trees.

### Page 11
- Deer looking directly at them.
- **MAU (whisper):** `It knows we're here.`
- **YUTA:** `Yeah.`

### Page 12
- Long eye-contact gag deer↔Mau.
- **MAU:** `I think it's planning something.`
- Yuta slow look.
- **YUTA:** `It's a deer.`

### Page 13
- **MAU:** `That doesn't mean it's not planning something.`
- Deer bolts.
- Mau points after it.
- **MAU:** `See?`

### Page 14
- Yuta covers mouth / laughing despite himself.
- Forest quiet again.
- They continue without deer.

# SCENE 3 — BOCCHI GOES OUTSIDE
**Pages 15–20**

### Page 15
- Bocchi and Frieren sorting plants/food inside.
- Frieren picks basket and heads out.
- Bocchi watches.

### Page 16
- Bocchi takes second basket.
- Door threshold pause.
- Frieren already across lane.

### Page 17
- Bocchi steps outside.
- Frieren looks back but says nothing.
- Waits subtly.

### Page 18
- Both walk to field together.
- Bocchi works near wall, Frieren checks soil.
- Silence stretches.

### Page 19
- Bocchi unable to tolerate silence:
- **BOCCHI:** `Do you think the snow will come back?`
- **FRIEREN:** `Yes.`
- Bocchi waits for more.

### Page 20
- None comes.
- Bocchi faint smile: she is beginning to know Frieren.
- Quiet field composition.

# SCENE 4 — RETURN WITH FOOD
**Pages 21–25**

### Page 21
- Yuta/Mau return with practical supplies.
- Mau sets kindling down.
- **MAU:** `The deer escaped.`
- **FRIEREN:** `Most deer do.`

### Page 22
- **MAU:** `It was suspicious.`
- **BOCCHI:** `Suspicious?`
- Yuta enters: **`Don't encourage him.`**

### Page 23
- Mau explains it was watching them.
- Frieren: **`You were hunting it.`**
- Mau pauses.
- **MAU:** `That explains some of it.`

### Page 24
- Bocchi laughs unexpectedly.
- Everyone glances at her.
- Bocchi immediate regret.
- Yuta continues unloading without making it a moment.

### Page 25 — CHAPTER 1 END
- Conversation moves on naturally around supplies.
- Bocchi relaxes again because nobody spotlighted her.
- Wide household work composition.
- **End Chapter 1.**

# SCENE 7 — LUNCH OUTSIDE
**Pages 35–38**

### Page 35
