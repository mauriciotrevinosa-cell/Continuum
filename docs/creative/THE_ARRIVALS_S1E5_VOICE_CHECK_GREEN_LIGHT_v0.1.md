## Episode

**S1E5 — Ordinary Days**

## Approval

The user approved the Voice Check for S1E5 and gave GREEN LIGHT.

Approved voice / interaction direction:

- the four-person household should already feel less like strangers interviewing each other and more like people developing shared routines;
- small jokes and callbacks are welcome when they emerge naturally from character interaction;
- preserve canon character identity rather than turning the cast into generic sitcom versions;
- Yuta + Mau can continue developing an older-brother-like rhythm through practical behavior rather than explicit labels;
- Bocchi's progress should remain incremental and understated rather than becoming sudden confidence;
- Frieren notices Mau becoming more independent without this yet becoming a dramatic conflict;
- ordinary household sounds, work, food, guitar, repairs, and repeated routines should carry much of the episode visually.

The representative dialogue shown during Voice Check was approved, including the deer callback, Bocchi's tiny outside-the-inn progress, Yuta teaching Mau practical work, Frieren noticing Mau and Yuta together, Bocchi playing guitar, and the dinner callback.

## Human feedback

The user specifically noted that the character combinations worked, some moments were genuinely funny, and the characters still felt like themselves.
