<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln01-wrong-world -->

Rimuru Tempest woke in grass.

For several seconds, this did not qualify as alarming.

His life had contained stranger transitions.

"Raphael. Where are we?"

The answer arrived with the same calm precision as always.

`Unknown.`

Rimuru waited.

"Unknown as in you haven't checked yet?"

`Unknown as in insufficient matching information.`

He rotated in place.

Forest.

Cold air.

Winter terrain in the distance.

Nothing remotely Tempest-shaped.

"So... not Tempest."

No correction came.

Rimuru went quiet.

Then did what he had always done when reality became inconvenient.

"Okay. We find people."

Raphael identified distant signs consistent with habitation.

Altered terrain.

Repeated movement.

Smoke.

Rimuru brightened.

"People. Finally."

He headed toward them in slime form.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln01-yuta-patrol -->

Yuta saw the blue shape between trees before it reached the lane.

Small.

Moving deliberately.

Not an animal he recognized.

His hand settled on his sword.

Inside Rimuru:

`Recommendation: assume humanoid form before approach.`

"Because I'm a terrifying slime?"

`Because the target has already identified you as a possible threat.`

Rimuru finally noticed the young man ahead.

Sword partly drawn.

"Fair."

His body changed.

Yuta stopped.

The threat he had prepared to intercept had become a person.

"...Okay."

Rimuru raised both hands.

"Hi."

Yuta did not draw the rest of the sword.

"Were you the slime?"

"Yeah."

Yuta looked at the place where the slime had been.

Then at Rimuru.

"Okay."

Rimuru waited for more.

Nothing.

"You took that better than most people would."

"It's been a strange week."

That sounded fair.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln01-people-technically -->

Yuta brought him toward the inn.

Rimuru's optimism weakened when the "settlement" came into view.

Ruined houses.

Snow.

One damaged inn with a few repaired windows.

"This is the town?"

"No."

"Good."

Yuta paused.

"There isn't a town."

Rimuru looked at him.

"Less good."

Inside, Frieren read near the fire.

Bocchi tuned her guitar.

Mau was repairing a wooden stool that had already survived at least one failed repair.

Frieren looked up first.

Her gaze sharpened.

Bocchi looked up second and immediately tried not to look as interested as she was.

Mau looked up last.

Then kept looking.

"I'm Rimuru Tempest."

Yuta pointed lightly.

"Frieren. Mau. Bocchi."

Bocchi twitched.

"Hitori Gotoh."

Rimuru turned.

"Sorry. Hitori."

"Bocchi," Mau said.

Bocchi looked at him as if betrayed.

"Both are fine."

Frieren turned a page.

"You said it was complicated."

Bocchi lowered her face into her hands.

Rimuru laughed before deciding whether he had earned permission.

Bocchi heard it.

A second later, she laughed too.

The room loosened.

Only slightly.

Enough.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln01-first-table -->

Rimuru sat near the fire.

No one demanded a power demonstration.

The slime transformation had already answered enough questions.

Where had he woken?

Some distance away.

Did he know the world?

No.

Did the word Tempest mean anything to anyone?

No.

Rimuru leaned back.

"Of course it doesn't."

Bocchi looked sympathetic.

Mau was still watching him.

Not suspiciously.

Curiously.

Rimuru noticed.

"Something on my face?"

"No."

Rimuru waited.

Mau continued looking.

"...Okay."

Yuta hid a smile.

Frieren did not.

She had seen Mau study people before.

This was different.

Mau seemed to be waiting for recognition to arrive.

It never quite did.

Rimuru got up to help move a crate.

Mau's eyes followed him.

Rimuru tilted his head at something Yuta said.

A few seconds later Mau tilted his head in almost the same way.

Rimuru noticed.

He said nothing.

Inside him, Raphael did.

`Notice. The individual known as Mau appears to be analyzing us.`

Visible behavior.

Nothing more.

Rimuru glanced at Mau.

*Us?*

Raphael offered no speculation beyond the observation.

Rimuru smiled instead.

"So you're Mau?"

Mau looked directly at him.

For half a second, something in his expression emptied.

Then—

"Raphael."

Rimuru froze.
