<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln03-wake -->

Mau woke to the ceiling.

For several seconds, that was all.

Then guitar.

Quiet.

A progression he recognized.

Bocchi sat against the wall.

Frieren sat beside the bed with a grimoire open.

Mau watched her.

"What are you reading?"

Frieren did not react immediately.

Then her eyes moved from the page to his face.

The book closed.

She stared.

Mau had enough time to wonder whether he had said something wrong.

Then Frieren leaned forward and hugged him.

His arms stayed at his sides from surprise.

Bocchi stopped playing.

Mau looked over Frieren's shoulder.

Bocchi's eyes were wet.

"How long was I asleep?"

Frieren did not let go.

"Two days."

Mau processed that.

"Oh."

Another second.

"Can I have water?"

Frieren finally pulled back.

"Yes."

Bocchi stood too fast to get it.

Her foot caught the guitar strap.

She recovered before falling.

Mau watched.

Some things remained reassuringly normal.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln03-missing-time -->

Yuta arrived after Bocchi called him.

Rimuru did not.

Not yet.

Questions first.

Headache?

No.

Dizzy?

A little.

Memory?

Mau thought.

"Rimuru came in."

Frieren watched carefully.

"He said hello."

Pause.

"Then I woke up."

Nothing else.

No name.

No collapse.

No internal pressure.

Frieren looked at Yuta.

Yuta saw the same gap.

Neither filled it for Mau.

Later, Rimuru appeared in the doorway.

He stopped there.

Mau saw him.

"Hi."

Rimuru almost laughed from relief.

"Hi."

No suspicion in Mau's face.

That made Rimuru feel worse.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln03-downstairs -->

By evening Mau was downstairs.

Against Frieren's judgment.

Against Bocchi's.

Mostly against Yuta's.

Entirely according to Mau.

Frieren watched him between bites.

Bocchi watched more obviously.

Yuta had chosen a seat close enough to catch him if necessary and had not explained why.

Rimuru sat farther away.

Five bowls.

Conversation struggled to begin.

Mau looked around.

"Why is everyone quiet?"

Bocchi focused on her food.

Yuta answered.

"You were unconscious for two days."

"I'm awake now."

"We noticed."

Bocchi laughed.

Small.

Enough to break the pressure.

Mau started eating.

After a while he looked at Rimuru's bowl.

"You can eat that?"

Rimuru blinked.

"Yeah."

"Even though you're a slime?"

"It's complicated."

Bocchi looked up.

"I understand that answer."

Rimuru smiled.

Frieren did not.

Not yet.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e6-ln03-volume-end -->

Five people sat at the same table.

Mau ate.

Bocchi slowly stopped watching him after every movement.

Yuta relaxed enough to lean back.

Rimuru answered another question.

Frieren listened.

Physically, there was no empty place left between them.

Emotionally, the distance around Rimuru remained.

No one pretended otherwise.

The fire burned beside the table.

Outside, the settlement was quiet.

Inside, five bowls needed washing.

That was where they began.
