## Non-negotiable E6 causality

- Raphael does **not** consciously choose to analyze The Noise.
- Raphael cannot detect, identify, perceive, or read The Noise itself.
- Mau does not consciously activate The Noise.
- Mau is a classification / ontology failure from Raphael's perspective.
- Mau surfaces the word `Raphael` without knowing why.
- Only Rimuru clearly hears the word.
- Mau's collapse is an internal conceptual overload, not an explosion, attack, or power contest.
- Mau later remembers greeting Rimuru and then waking up; no one tells him he said `Raphael`.

# S1E6

## Scene 1 — A Slime in the Wrong World

Rimuru wakes in grass.

For a few seconds he assumes this is merely one more strange transition in a life that has already included several.

Then Raphael answers his first question with an answer he does not like.

**RIMURU**  
Raphael. Where are we?

A calm internal response.

**RAPHAEL**  
Unknown.

Rimuru waits.

**RIMURU**  
Unknown as in you haven't checked yet?

**RAPHAEL**  
Unknown as in insufficient matching information.

Rimuru rotates in place, taking in forest and distant winter terrain.

**RIMURU**  
So... not Tempest.

No answer changes that conclusion.

He is quiet for a moment.

Then he does what Rimuru does when the alternative is panic.

**RIMURU**  
Okay. Then we find people.

Raphael identifies faint signs consistent with habitation at a distance: smoke, altered terrain, repeated movement.

Rimuru brightens immediately.

**RIMURU**  
People. Finally.

He heads toward it in slime form.

---

## Scene 2 — Yuta's Patrol

Yuta is checking the edge of the settlement alone.

The four-person household has grown comfortable enough that not every movement requires everyone together.

He notices something moving between trees.

Small.

Blue.

Not an animal he recognizes.

His hand goes to his sword.

Raphael notices the armed human before Rimuru reaches the lane.

**RAPHAEL**  
Recommendation: assume humanoid form before approach.

**RIMURU**  
Because I'm a terrifying slime?

**RAPHAEL**  
Because the target has already identified you as a possible threat.

Rimuru sees Yuta now.

Sword half-drawn.

**RIMURU**  
Fair.

He transforms.

Yuta stops.

The threat he had prepared to intercept has just turned into a person.

**YUTA**  
...Okay.

Rimuru raises both hands.

**RIMURU**  
Hi.

Yuta does not draw the rest of the sword.

**YUTA**  
Were you the slime?

**RIMURU**  
Yeah.

Yuta looks back toward where the slime was.

Then at Rimuru.

**YUTA**  
Okay.

Rimuru waits for more.

There is none.

**RIMURU**  
You took that better than most people would.

**YUTA**  
It's been a strange week.

That is enough common ground to avoid fighting.

---

## Scene 3 — People, Technically

Yuta brings Rimuru toward the inn rather than letting a shapeshifting stranger wander through the settlement alone.

Rimuru's optimism dims slightly when he sees what `habitation` meant.

One damaged inn.

A handful of repaired windows.

Broken houses.

Snow.

**RIMURU**  
This is the town?

**YUTA**  
No.

**RIMURU**  
Good.

Beat.

**YUTA**  
There isn't a town.

Rimuru looks at him.

**RIMURU**  
Less good.

Inside, Frieren is reading near the fire, Bocchi is tuning her guitar, and Mau is trying to repair a wooden stool that has already been repaired twice.

Frieren looks up first.

Her eyes immediately assess Rimuru.

Bocchi looks up second and tries not to stare.

Mau looks up last and keeps looking.

Introductions begin.

**RIMURU**  
I'm Rimuru Tempest.

Yuta points lightly around the room.

**YUTA**  
Frieren. Mau. Bocchi.

Bocchi twitches.

**BOCCHI**  
Hitori Gotoh.

Rimuru turns toward her.

**RIMURU**  
Sorry. Hitori.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Bocchi.

Bocchi looks at him in betrayal.

**BOCCHI**  
Both are fine.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
You said it was complicated.

Bocchi lowers her face into her hands.

Rimuru laughs before realizing he may not be allowed to yet.

Bocchi hears it and, despite herself, laughs too.

The room loosens by one degree.

---

## Scene 4 — Raphael's Background Work

Rimuru sits with them near the fire.

No one asks for a demonstration of his abilities.

No one needs one; they watched him arrive as a slime and become human.

Conversation moves around practical questions.

Where did he wake?

Does he know the world?

No.

Does `Tempest` mean anything to anyone?

No.

Rimuru is disappointed but not shocked.

While he talks, Raphael's automatic evaluative functions continue in the background the way they always do.

Frieren is strange by this world's standards but coherent: mana, body, skill patterns.

Yuta is foreign in a different direction: cursed energy does not map neatly to known magic, but it behaves consistently enough to recognize structure.

Bocchi is comparatively ordinary.

Then Mau.

No clean answer.

Not `immense power`.

Not `concealed god`.

The problem is more basic.

Expected categories refuse to settle.

Raphael attempts ordinary passive comparisons and structural resolution.

The result remains incomplete.

Rimuru hears a brief internal notification equivalent to a classification failure.

He does not visibly react much.

He has encountered strange things before.

**RIMURU (thought)**  
Later.

Raphael does not announce danger.

Raphael does not detect The Noise.

There is nothing called The Noise inside Raphael's perception at all.

---

## Scene 5 — Mau Watches Rimuru

Mau has been watching Rimuru since he entered.

Not suspiciously.

Curiously.

Rimuru notices after a while.

**RIMURU**  
Something on my face?

Mau:

**MAU**  
No.

Rimuru waits.

Mau continues looking.

**RIMURU**  
Okay.

Yuta hides a smile.

Frieren notices the exchange from the other side of the table.

Mau's attention is different from how he watched Yuta when Yuta arrived. He seems to be trying to recognize a shape he cannot name.

Rimuru eventually gets up to help move a crate near the wall.

Mau's eyes follow the movement.

Something inside Mau catches.

Not a voice Raphael can hear.

Not a signal Raphael can identify.

Fragments grind across Mau's own awareness.

No full words.

No interface.

No instruction.

Only broken conceptual pressure.

Rimuru approaches him afterward with a friendly smile.

**RIMURU**  
So you're Mau?

Mau looks directly at him.

His expression empties for half a second.

Very quietly:

**MAU**  
Raphael.

Rimuru freezes.

Frieren is close enough to see Mau speak but not to clearly catch the word.

Yuta hears only that Mau said something.

Bocchi looks up from the guitar.

Inside Rimuru, Raphael also registers the external utterance of her name.

She has no explanation for how Mau knows it.

Before Rimuru can ask—

Mau's hand goes to his head.

His breath catches.

The internal pressure tears across him faster than thought.

His knees give out.

There is no flash.

No shockwave.

No aura.

No attack travelling between Rimuru and Mau.

Frieren reaches him before he hits the floor.

---

## Scene 6 — After the Collapse

**FRIEREN**  
Mau.

No response.

Frieren checks breathing, pulse, obvious injury.

Alive.

Unconscious.

Yuta moves to her side.

Bocchi is already standing but does not know where to put herself.

Rimuru has not moved.

He looks horrified.

**RIMURU**  
I didn't—

Frieren looks at him.

The sentence dies under the look.

Yuta's voice stays calm.

**YUTA**  
Did you do something?

**RIMURU**  
No.

Rimuru means it.

Frieren does not know him well enough for that to matter.

She lifts Mau with Yuta's help.

**FRIEREN**  
Move.

Rimuru moves immediately.

They carry Mau upstairs.

Bocchi follows with blankets.

Rimuru remains in the common room alone for several seconds.

The room was joking with him minutes ago.

Now he is the reason everyone is upstairs, whether or not he actually caused anything.

---

## Scene 7 — Two Days

Mau does not wake that night.

Or the next morning.

The household changes around the absence.

Frieren spends most of her time in the room beside him.

Yuta checks food, water, perimeter, then checks Mau.

Bocchi brings her guitar upstairs and plays softly enough that the sound reaches the bed without filling the whole building.

Rimuru helps where he can and is refused by Frieren whenever helping would bring him too close to Mau.

She is not openly cruel.

She simply does not trust him.

On the first night, Rimuru sits alone downstairs.

Internally:

**RIMURU**  
Raphael.

**RAPHAEL**  
Yes.

**RIMURU**  
How did he know your name?

Pause.

**RAPHAEL**  
Unknown.

Rimuru's expression tightens.

**RIMURU**  
Did you do something to him?

**RAPHAEL**  
No deliberate action was initiated.

Raphael explains only what she actually knows: ordinary automatic observation encountered a subject that would not resolve cleanly. No intentional attack. No successful classification. No detected mechanism explaining the collapse.

Rimuru presses.

**RIMURU**  
Could trying again tell us?

A brief pause.

**RAPHAEL**  
Not recommended.

**RIMURU**  
Because it might happen again.

**RAPHAEL**  
Yes.

Rimuru leans back against the wall.

For once, `unknown` is not interesting.

It is frightening because someone else paid for it.

**RIMURU**  
Then we don't.

Raphael agrees.

Neither of them mentions The Noise because neither of them knows it exists.

---

## Scene 8 — What Are You Reading?

Near the end of the second day, Mau wakes.

The first thing he sees is the ceiling.

The second is Bocchi sitting against the wall with the guitar, fingers moving through the same quiet progression she has repeated for hours.

The third is Frieren beside the bed with a grimoire open.

Mau watches her for several seconds.

**MAU**  
What are you reading?

Frieren does not react immediately.

Then her eyes move from the page to his face.

The book closes.

She stares as if making sure he is actually awake.

Then she leans forward and hugs him.

Mau's arms stay at his sides from surprise.

Bocchi stops playing.

Mau looks over Frieren's shoulder at her.

Bocchi's eyes are wet.

**MAU**  
How long was I asleep?

Frieren does not let go yet.

**FRIEREN**  
Two days.

Mau processes that.

**MAU**  
Oh.

After another second:

**MAU**  
Can I have water?

Frieren finally pulls back.

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Bocchi stands too quickly to get it and almost trips over the guitar strap.

Mau watches her catch herself.

Some things are reassuringly unchanged.

---

## Scene 9 — Missing Time

Yuta comes in after Bocchi calls him.

Rimuru does not.

Not until later.

Mau answers simple questions.

Head hurt?

Not now.

Dizzy?

A little.

What does he remember?

Mau thinks.

**MAU**  
Rimuru came in.

Frieren watches him carefully.

**MAU**  
He said hello.

A pause.

**MAU**  
Then I woke up.

Nothing about `Raphael`.

Nothing about the pressure.

Nothing about the fall.

Frieren looks at Yuta.

Yuta understands that she noticed the gap too.

Neither tells Mau what he said.

When Rimuru finally appears in the doorway, he stops there rather than walking in.

Mau sees him.

**MAU**  
Hi.

Rimuru almost laughs from relief.

**RIMURU**  
Hi.

Mau has no suspicion in his face.

That somehow makes Rimuru feel worse.

---

## Scene 10 — Five at the Table

By evening Mau is downstairs, against everyone's better judgment except his own.

Frieren keeps watching him between bites.

Bocchi does too, less subtly.

Yuta has positioned himself close enough to catch Mau if he collapses again without announcing that this is why.

Rimuru sits at the far side of the table.

There are five bowls now.

Conversation is restrained at first.

Mau notices.

**MAU**  
Why is everyone quiet?

Bocchi immediately looks at her bowl.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
You were unconscious for two days.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I'm awake now.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
We noticed.

A small laugh from Bocchi breaks the pressure.

Mau starts eating.

Rimuru remains careful.

At some point Mau looks at him.

**MAU**  
You can eat that?

Rimuru blinks.

**RIMURU**  
Yeah.

**MAU**  
Even though you're a slime?

**RIMURU**  
It's complicated.

Bocchi looks up.

**BOCCHI**  
I understand that answer.

Rimuru smiles.

Frieren does not.

Not yet.

The five share the same table.

Physically, G1 is assembled.

Emotionally, one chair still sits outside the circle.

**END S1E6.**

## Stage 3 continuity / canon notes

- Raphael never detects The Noise. Any Mau-internal fragments are shown only from Mau's subjective experience, not as Raphael-readable data.
- Raphael's interaction is passive/automatic and unresolved; there is no deliberate `analyze Mau harder` experiment.
- Mau's collapse has no physical blast or outward power display.
- Only Rimuru clearly hears `Raphael.` Frieren sees Mau speak but cannot confirm the word.
- Mau is unconscious about two days.
- Mau remembers only Rimuru's arrival/greeting before the memory gap.
- No one tells Mau he said `Raphael` in E6.
- Frieren distrusts Rimuru after the collapse; substantive explanation and first trust step remain reserved for E8.
- Rimuru/Raphael choose not to repeat any analysis/provocation.
- E6 ends with five at the table but Rimuru still socially outside the established household.
