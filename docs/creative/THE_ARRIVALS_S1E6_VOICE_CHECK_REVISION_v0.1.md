## User-approved corrections to the E6 voice-check direction

These notes supersede the initial E6 voice-check presentation where they conflict.

### 1. Rimuru / Yuta first contact

- Yuta does not know what Rimuru is.
- Yuta sees the approaching slime and is close to attacking / reacting defensively.
- Raphael detects that hostility risk and recommends Rimuru shift to humanoid form before Yuta acts.
- Rimuru transforms as a preventative response to Yuta's imminent defensive action, not merely because appearing humanoid is generally more polite.

### 2. Bocchi reaction beat

Keep the comedic Bocchi reaction where Yuta reveals her actual name after she introduces herself as Bocchi, and Bocchi turns to look at him as if betrayed.

### 3. Mau must already be studying Rimuru before direct contact

Critical causal correction:

- Raphael has already been passively analyzing the group before Rimuru directly approaches Mau.
- Frieren is observing Mau and notices that something about his behavior / focus is strange before the collapse.
- Mau is staring at / studying Rimuru in a way that reads as if he is processing something.
- Rimuru approaches normally and says something in the direction of:
  - `Hi, I'm Rimuru.`
  - `You're supposed to be Mau?`
- Mau's only meaningful response is:
  - **`Raphael.`**
- Mau then overloads / collapses.

This preserves the intended implication that the overload was already being triggered by the prior analytical contact / conceptual interaction, rather than by a normal conversation with Rimuru.

### 4. Do not resolve Frieren / Rimuru here

Remove the more explicit post-collapse Frieren/Rimuru explanatory exchange from E6.

E6 should preserve suspicion and discomfort without giving Frieren a full explanation yet.

The more substantive Frieren/Rimuru discussion belongs later in the planned trust progression, especially E8.

In E6:
- Frieren can visibly become guarded toward Rimuru;
- Rimuru can understand that she does not trust him;
- the episode should not resolve that tension.

### 5. Reorder Raphael / Rimuru discussion while Mau is unconscious

The private Rimuru/Raphael discussion should happen **while Mau is still asleep**.

Direction:

- Rimuru privately asks Raphael whether she understands what happened.
- Raphael does not know.
- Raphael advises against repeating the analysis because it may reproduce the harmful event.
- Raphael also advises Rimuru not to reveal / push the `Raphael` detail yet while causality is unknown.
- Rimuru accepts this rather than forcing another attempt.

This beat should occur before any later interaction with awakened Mau.

### 6. Mau wake-up comes before any attempt to question him

Keep the wake-up scene:

- Frieren is beside Mau reading / watching him;
- Bocchi can be nearby with guitar;
- Mau wakes and asks something ordinary such as what Frieren is reading;
- Frieren hugs him;
- Mau is confused by the intensity;
- he learns he was unconscious for roughly two days.

### 7. Remove the direct interrogation of Mau

Do **not** use the earlier version where Rimuru asks Mau:
- whether he remembers talking to him;
- whether he remembers saying a name;
- `What name?`

Instead, after Mau wakes, cut later to the group talking naturally.

Mau may establish that he remembers meeting / greeting Rimuru and then waking later, but no one should explicitly tell him yet that he said `Raphael`.

Raphael privately reminds Rimuru to leave the detail alone for now.

This preserves the mystery for Mau himself and avoids over-explaining E6.
