Mau sat near the water with his knees drawn up.

The lake reflected a person he recognized visually and could not place anywhere else.

Yuta approached without hiding his footsteps.

Mau did not turn.

Yuta sat a short distance away.

Neither spoke.

The silence lasted long enough to stop feeling like a pause.

"Bocchi told me you don't remember anything."

Mau looked at the water.

"I remember things."

Yuta waited.

"I know what a tree is. I know what snow is. I know how to talk."

The next words were harder.

"I don't remember me."

Yuta nodded once.

"I don't know what that feels like."

Mau looked at him.

No lie.

No claim of shared pain because both situations were strange.

Yuta picked up a stone.

"I remember too much sometimes."

He turned it once between his fingers.

"That doesn't mean I know what you should do with nothing."

Mau returned his eyes to the lake.

"Everybody knows who they were."

"Mostly."

Mau looked at him.

Yuta's mouth moved faintly.

The household joke was small enough to survive here.

Then Yuta said, "Remembering a whole life doesn't always tell you who you are now."

Mau thought about that.

"Does it help?"

"Sometimes."

No lesson came after.

Wind moved over the lake.

"When you were asleep," Yuta said, "Frieren barely left the room."

Mau looked at him.

"Bocchi kept bringing her guitar upstairs because she thought you'd hear it."

Mau said nothing.

"You don't have a story to tell them yet."

Yuta let the sentence sit.

"That doesn't mean you're not in theirs."

He did not ask whether it helped.

<!-- scene-id: s1e7-ln02-approach -->

Frieren waited longer than she wanted.

Then she and Bocchi followed.

Bocchi had chosen to come.

That mattered.

Yuta saw them first.

Mau turned.

Frieren stopped several steps away.

Every instinct told her to cross the distance immediately.

She did not.

Bocchi continued past her.

She reached Mau and discovered that whatever sentence she had prepared during the walk had disappeared.

"Um."

Mau waited.

Bocchi swallowed.

"We're here to help you too."

The line returned to him from another direction.

The same simple promise he had given her.

Mau looked at Bocchi.

"Okay."

That was all.

Bocchi sat.

Yuta shifted to make room.

Frieren remained standing one beat longer.

Mau looked back at her.

She came closer.

Not first.

Not absent.
