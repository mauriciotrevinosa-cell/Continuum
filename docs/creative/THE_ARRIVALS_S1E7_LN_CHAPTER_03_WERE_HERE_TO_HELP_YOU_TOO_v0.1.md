They returned after dark.

Rimuru had reheated the food.

He stood before Mau could sit.

"I wasn't thinking."

Mau looked at him.

"I know."

"That doesn't make it better."

Mau considered.

"No."

Rimuru accepted that.

No defense.

No attempt to force forgiveness before dinner.

Mau's chair had shifted slightly while he was gone.

So had his bowl.

Not removed.

Reopened.

Bocchi moved her own chair enough to make the space obvious without announcing what she was doing.

Mau sat.

Conversation resumed around food instead of memory.

The table repaired itself that way.

<!-- scene-id: s1e7-ln03-room -->

Later, in the room that had become shared through repetition rather than declaration, Mau sat near the window.

Frieren put away a book she had barely read downstairs.

Silence.

"Do you feel better?" she asked.

Mau thought.

Then looked at her.

"Do you?"

Frieren paused.

Fern's ornament remained in her hair.

"I'm getting there."

Mau nodded.

"Okay."

Nothing was fixed.

Mau still had no past.

Frieren still carried Fern's absence even while refusing to name the possibility of reunion.

Rimuru still occupied a careful distance in the household.

But Mau no longer depended on one person for every form of care.

Frieren knew that now.

This time, knowing it did not feel like losing him.

Not yet.
