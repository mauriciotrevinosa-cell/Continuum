<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln01-rain -->

Rain replaced snow for two days.

The inn discovered new ways to leak.

Water appeared above storage.

A rear timber softened.

The well outside contained debris no one wanted to investigate by hand.

Rimuru looked around.

"We need better materials."

Frieren turned a page.

"Yes."

"There was stonework east of here. Cave, old structure, something."

Frieren looked up.

Yuta was out.

Bocchi repaired cloth near the fire.

Mau moved salvage outside.

Rimuru realized she was deciding whether his suggestion was an excuse to isolate her.

"You can bring someone else."

"No."

Rimuru blinked.

Frieren closed the book.

"I'll go."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln01-stone-structure -->

The site was half worked stone, half hillside.

Old supports opened into natural cave.

Useful material.

Dangerous material.

Frieren checked residue and stability.

Rimuru checked what could be removed without bringing the ceiling down.

They worked well together.

That was inconvenient.

Rimuru reached toward a fractured support.

Frieren caught his wrist.

"Not that one."

He looked up.

Hairline cracks disappeared into the ceiling.

"Right."

She released him.

They moved.

Eventually silence stopped being useful.

"What did Mau say?"

Rimuru stopped.

"Before he collapsed?"

"Yes."

Inside him:

`Recommendation: answer truthfully.`

Rimuru exhaled.

"He said 'Raphael.'"

Frieren's expression changed by almost nothing.

"Who is Raphael?"

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln01-disclosure -->

Rimuru sat on a fallen stone block.

"She talks to me."

Frieren waited.

"Inside my head. She thinks with me. Helps me understand things. Other people can't hear her."

"And Mau knew her name."

"Yes."

"Because she was doing something to him."

Rimuru started to answer.

Raphael corrected the framing first.

No hostile action.

Ordinary passive processing.

No known mechanism linking it to the collapse.

Rimuru repeated the uncomfortable version instead of polishing it.

"Not exactly. Raphael says she wasn't attacking him. She was doing what she normally does in the background. Trying to understand what was around me."

"Trying to analyze him."

"Passively. But yes."

"It didn't work."

"Why?"

"We don't know."

Frieren heard the plural.

Rimuru continued.

"She can't classify him properly. She doesn't know why he knew her name. She doesn't know why he collapsed."

Pause.

"And we stopped."

"Stopped what?"

"Anything that might trigger it again."

Rimuru met her eyes.

"I'm curious. Raphael is curious. That doesn't matter more than Mau getting hurt."

Frieren watched him.

Words were evidence.

Not proof.

"I don't know if I believe you."

Rimuru nodded.

"But I'll try."

That stopped him.

"Okay."

No answer about The Noise appeared.

Raphael had none to give.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln01-carrying-stone -->

The return trip remained awkward until the stone became heavier than the awkwardness.

Rimuru prepared to use his abilities.

Frieren watched.

"What are you doing?"

"Making this easier."

"That usually means something strange with you."

Rimuru laughed.

"You're learning."

He showed only what was useful.

Storage.

Material manipulation.

Enough to move stone.

Frieren stared.

"You could've done that with the first load."

"You didn't trust me with the first load."

Frieren considered.

"Fair."

The exchange was easy.

Both noticed after it happened.

Neither commented.
