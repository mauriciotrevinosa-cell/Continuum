<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln02-question -->

Everyone noticed the amount of material Frieren and Rimuru brought back.

Mau circled the pile.

"That's a lot."

"And it still isn't enough if we patch one leak forever," Rimuru said.

A drip landed in the familiar bucket.

Bocchi looked at it.

"It has personality now."

Rimuru pointed.

"It has to go."

"The bucket?"

"The leak."

Mau nodded.

Rimuru looked around the room.

"What do we actually need?"

Yuta said storage.

Frieren said roof and insulation.

Mau looked down at the floor.

"Somewhere the floor doesn't move when I step."

Bocchi said nothing.

Rimuru looked at her.

Immediate regret appeared on her face.

"What about you?"

"Maybe... somewhere I can play without bothering everyone?"

"You don't bother everyone," Mau said.

"That's not the point."

Yuta nodded.

"A room would be good."

"Music room," Rimuru decided.

Bocchi blinked.

"A whole room?"

"We have ten rooms and five people."

"Mostly usable rooms," Mau corrected.

Rimuru pointed at him.

"We're fixing that too."

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln02-bath -->

The list expanded.

Kitchen.

Dry storage.

Heating.

A bath.

Everyone stopped.

Bocchi looked at Rimuru as if civilization had personally returned.

"A bath?"

"Yes."

"A real one?" Yuta asked.

"That was the idea."

Mau looked between them.

"What's wrong with the basin?"

Everyone looked at him.

"What?"

Bocchi turned away.

"Nothing."

Yuta stood.

"We'll show you."

Frieren had already accepted the bath proposal as obvious.

Perimeter warnings came next.

Threads.

Bells.

Clear sight lines.

Not a fortress.

A house that told them when something approached.

The list had stopped being about not dying.

It had become about how they wanted to live.

* * *

<!-- scene-id: s1e8-ln02-well -->

The well became Rimuru's first argument with the old settlement itself.

He inspected the water.

Paused.

"No."

Yuta stood nearby.

"No what?"

"We're not drinking that without fixing this."

Rimuru disappeared into the well.

Yuta leaned over the edge.

Sounds came from below.

Water moved.

Something scraped.

Then silence.

"Rimuru?"

A blue shape shot out of the well with far more speed than dignity.

Yuta stepped back.

Rimuru landed.

Human form followed a beat later.

He adjusted himself as though nothing ridiculous had happened.

"The well is clean."

Yuta stared.

"...Good."

Rimuru looked away.

Useful first.

Funny second.

The water no longer smelled wrong.
