## Purpose

This revision replaces the initial E8 opening direction while preserving the later home-building material unless user feedback changes it.

## Core correction: Rimuru must visibly open up

The first version made Rimuru sound as if he already knew exactly what happened with Mau and was comfortable explaining Raphael. That is not the intended trust progression.

E8 should instead show Rimuru gradually revealing something private and secret about himself to Frieren. This disclosure is part of how he earns trust.

## Revised Frieren / Rimuru trust conversation

- Frieren and Rimuru end up investigating a nearby structure / dungeon together because they are the safest pair for an uncertain magical environment.
- Frieren remains guarded and clipped; Rimuru does not begin by volunteering a polished explanation.
- After some shared work, Frieren directly brings up `Raphael`.
- Rimuru hesitates because explaining Raphael means revealing something deeply private about how his mind / abilities work.
- He eventually admits that Raphael is an internal intelligence / partner integrated with him and that she can communicate with him directly.
- When Frieren says in effect, `And she was doing that to Mau`, Raphael internally corrects Rimuru.
- Raphael's correction must preserve E6 continuity:
  - she did not attack Mau;
  - she did not intentionally target him with a hostile action;
  - her analytical processes were functioning on him as they do on others / the environment;
  - she does not understand why Mau was harmed;
  - **Raphael cannot detect, perceive, or identify Mau's `The Noise`.**
- Rimuru chooses to relay this internal correction to Frieren instead of hiding it.
- This is an important trust beat because Frieren realizes Rimuru is exposing something secret and unverifiable about himself rather than giving a convenient external excuse.
- Frieren may notice Rimuru reacting to Raphael in real time and understand that Raphael is not merely a story he invented afterward.
- Rimuru should be honest that neither he nor Raphael understands what happened.
- He should also admit that Raphael's analysis can happen automatically / passively rather than as a conscious attack command.
- Rimuru does not use this as an excuse. He accepts that Mau still got hurt while connected to something inside him.

### Intended trust payoff

The conversation should land near:

- Frieren: `I don't know if I believe you.`
- Rimuru: `That's fair.`
- Frieren: `But I'll try.`

The emotional reason Frieren is willing to try is not that Rimuru produced proof. It is that:

1. he admits what he does not know;
2. he takes responsibility without falsely claiming intent;
3. he reveals Raphael's existence / nature despite it being private;
4. he relays Raphael's correction rather than filtering it into a more convenient story;
5. he does not pressure Frieren to forgive or trust him immediately.

## Home-building half remains

Preserve the previously presented E8 home-building direction unless later user notes change it:

- Rimuru sees how improvised the inn still is and decides they are fixing it properly.
- upgrades can include bath, kitchen, storage, workshop, farm support, defenses, heating, better common space, and Bocchi music-room / soundproofing direction;
- Mau immediately wants to help;
- Bocchi is delighted / overwhelmed by the possibility of a proper place to play music;
- Yuta naturally joins work and starts coordinating with Rimuru;
- Frieren contributes magical solutions and slowly works with Rimuru instead of around him;
- Frieren and Mau still get meaningful work time together;
- keep the beat where Mau mentions Yuta does something differently and Frieren learns to let Mau try both methods rather than monopolizing how he learns;
- the construction gradually becomes group work rather than `Rimuru fixes everything`;
- Rimuru integrates socially through useful shared labor rather than a friendship declaration;
- final dinner / closing imagery should make the abandoned inn visibly become a deliberately chosen home;
- preserve the E2 -> E8 thematic payoff: E2 says the inn is merely `somewhere to stay`; E8 visually answers that it has become home.

## Continuity locks

- Raphael cannot detect `The Noise`.
- Rimuru and Raphael do not know the true cause of Mau's E6 overload.
- Frieren's distrust began in E6, was left unresolved in E7, and receives its first real opening here.
- This is not instant total trust; `But I'll try` is the correct scale.
