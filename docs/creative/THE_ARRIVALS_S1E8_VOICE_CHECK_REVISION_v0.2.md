## Critical correction to the Frieren / Rimuru trust conversation

This revision supersedes the earlier S1E8 voice-check opening where Frieren already knew Mau said `Raphael`.

### What Frieren actually witnessed in S1E6

- Frieren noticed Mau behaving strangely before the collapse.
- Rimuru approached Mau.
- Mau said something too quietly / unclearly for Frieren to hear.
- Mau collapsed.
- Therefore Frieren does **not** know the word `Raphael` from the collapse scene.

### E8 conversation trigger

During the later private Frieren / Rimuru exploration:

1. Frieren asks Rimuru what Mau said immediately before collapsing.
2. Rimuru hesitates because answering truthfully would reveal something extremely private.
3. Raphael privately advises Rimuru to tell the truth.
4. Rimuru chooses to trust Frieren and says:
   - **`He said Raphael.`**
5. Frieren asks who / what Raphael is.
6. Rimuru then reveals that Raphael is an internal intelligence / presence that thinks with him, talks to him, and acts as part of his own system.

The trust beat comes from **Rimuru voluntarily exposing a secret he normally would not explain** rather than Frieren already knowing the name.

### Raphael correction beat

Later, when Frieren frames the incident as Raphael having `done something` to Mau, Raphael corrects Rimuru internally:

- she did not attack Mau;
- her analysis was the same kind of passive / automatic analytical process she applies normally;
- she still cannot explain the collapse;
- she cannot detect Mau's `The Noise` and does not know that such an internal voice exists.

Rimuru chooses to repeat that correction honestly to Frieren instead of simplifying the story.

### Preserved continuity

Everything else from the approved E6 causality remains intact:

- Raphael cannot detect `The Noise`;
- neither Raphael nor Mau consciously initiated the key hidden interaction;
- Mau alone surfaced the name `Raphael` during the collapse;
- Frieren's distrust was seeded in E6 and is processed here in E8;
- Rimuru does not claim knowledge he does not have;
- Frieren's eventual `I don't know if I believe you. But I'll try.` remains the trust milestone;
- the back half of E8 remains the communal rebuilding / deliberate-home episode.
