## Locked voice / continuity notes

- E9 remains a quiet domestic episode about the inn becoming personally inhabited rather than merely functional.
- Mau technically has an assigned room, but in practice **lives with Frieren**.
- Mau's everyday belongings are largely in Frieren's room; Frieren's room visibly contains Mau's presence and routines.
- Mau's nominal room functions more as overflow / grimoires / study / storage than as his emotional or practical home base.
- This domestic reality is important setup for the later Frieren–Mau misunderstanding: when Mau starts giving Frieren space, his physical absence from a room Frieren has come to treat as shared should carry emotional weight.
- When Mau leaves alone in E9, he says he is going **"to check on my plant"** rather than saying he is returning to where they woke up.
- Frieren understands the reference and smiles; the others do not. If they react / look for an explanation, Frieren dismisses it with something like **"Nothing."**
- Frieren allows Mau to go alone and only asks him to return before dark.
- Mau's observation / reconstruction attempts remain **private**.
- Mau does **not** tell Frieren that he tried to create a flower or copy her spell.
- Mau is somewhat embarrassed / uncertain about these private attempts and does not yet understand why he keeps closely observing magic.
- When Frieren notices him watching a spell too carefully, Mau evades rather than explaining himself. Do not use a confident line such as `I always watch`; he does not yet understand the pattern himself.
- Frieren gradually notices small unexplained disappearances and unusually focused observation because she lives with Mau and knows his routines best.
- The Noise may appear only as a tiny incomplete fragment during Mau's private attempt; it must not become an instructional system or explicit explanation.
- No training arc begins here. No successful spell, spark, skill unlock, Raphael analysis, or explicit power explanation.
- Mau brings back one real flower and places it in the room he effectively shares with Frieren.
- The closing flower beat remains quiet and domestic: the flower sits among both Frieren's and Mau's things, representing something Mau chose to bring into their shared life.
- Long-term payoff remains that Mau's first important successful construction should be creation rather than destruction, with the flower-field magic carrying emotional weight because it was the first beautiful thing Frieren did for him when he was frightened.

## Voice / tone approval

The user approved the episode's humor, domestic texture, private mystery around Mau's observation, and the revised shared-room continuity.
