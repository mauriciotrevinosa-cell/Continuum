## Scope

This note changes only the room-continuity beat in S1E9. All other E9 voice-check material remains intact.

## Correction

By this point in Season 1, Mau and Frieren are effectively living together emotionally and practically. Their belongings should already bleed naturally into each other's spaces.

Therefore Mau's room should **not** read as an isolated, nearly empty guest room that contains only his own few possessions.

Instead:

- Mau still has very few truly personal possessions from before arrival because he arrived with essentially nothing;
- however, his room can already contain Frieren's grimoires / books, borrowed reading material, shared practical objects, and other traces of Frieren's presence;
- Frieren's room can likewise contain Mau's things: a tool he left there, clothing or a blanket, something he was working on, an improvised object, or other small belongings that migrated there naturally;
- neither character needs to have formally discussed this arrangement;
- the visual point is that their spaces already overlap because they themselves are deeply intertwined;
- the emotional contrast remains that Mau has little that is **truly his by choice**, which makes the later flower he brings back especially meaningful.

The scene should communicate:

```text
Mau does not have much from a past life.
But he is no longer living like someone alone.
Frieren is already present in his space, and he is already present in hers.
```

This preserves the E9 theme while aligning the room staging with the established Frieren–Mau bond.
