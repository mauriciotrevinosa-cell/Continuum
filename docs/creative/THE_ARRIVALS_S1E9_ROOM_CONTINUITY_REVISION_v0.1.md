## Locked continuity correction for Mau + Frieren living arrangement

This note changes only the room/living-space details in the current S1E9 Voice Check. All other E9 beats, dialogue direction, flower-field callback, failed reconstruction attempt, The Noise seed, home-making tone, and pacing remain intact.

### Core rule

By S1E9, Mau technically has an assigned room, but **he functionally lives with Frieren**.

Their living arrangement should read visually and naturally rather than be explained with labels:

- Mau sleeps in Frieren's room most of the time.
- Most of Mau's actual everyday belongings are in Frieren's room.
- Frieren's room visibly contains Mau's clothes, small objects, tools, or other personal traces mixed into her own space.
- Mau's nominal room exists, but it is not emotionally his primary room.
- Mau's nominal room can contain grimoires, books, overflow storage, study materials, or things that do not fit in Frieren's room.
- This does not require the story to label or define their relationship romantically; it is simply the established domestic reality by this point in S1.

### E9 visual implication

The earlier idea that Mau's assigned room is almost empty because he has no belongings should be replaced.

Instead, the emotional observation is:

> Mau technically has a room, but the things that actually feel like his are mostly somewhere else — with Frieren.

This creates a stronger contrast with the episode's theme of `Making It Ours`:

- Frieren's room has gradually become a shared lived-in space without either of them formally declaring it.
- Mau's assigned room is useful, but reads almost like shared overflow / study space.
- The first flower Mau brings back can still become one of the first objects he chooses for himself; it may be placed in the shared Frieren/Mau room, which makes the gesture even more meaningful.
