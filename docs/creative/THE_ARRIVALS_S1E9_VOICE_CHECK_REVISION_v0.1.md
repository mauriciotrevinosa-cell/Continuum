These notes revise the E9 Voice Check while leaving all other approved/reviewed beats intact.

## Locked continuity corrections

### 1. Mau technically has a room, but practically lives with Frieren

By E9, Mau still has an assigned room, but it is not his primary lived-in space.

In practice:
- Mau sleeps with Frieren most of the time;
- most of Mau's daily-use belongings are in Frieren's room;
- Frieren's room visibly contains Mau's things mixed naturally with hers;
- Mau's nominal room is better treated as overflow / study / grimoire space / storage / secondary room;
- this domestic arrangement is normal to both of them and should not be explained with forced romance dialogue.

This continuity should make the later Frieren/Mau distance drama more physically meaningful because separation changes their actual shared domestic routine, not only their emotional state.

### 2. Mau's reason for revisiting the E1 location

Replace the direct explanation `Where we woke up.`

Mau says he is going:

> `To check on my plant.`

This refers back to the plant he brought back during E1.

Frieren understands immediately and smiles.

Other nearby characters may look confused because they do not understand the reference.

When they look to Frieren for explanation, she only says something like:

> `Nothing.`

The scene should preserve the private shared history between Frieren and Mau rather than explaining it to the group.

### 3. Mau does NOT tell Frieren he is trying to recreate the flower spell

Remove the beat where Mau returns with a flower and explicitly says:

> `I tried to make one.`

At this stage Mau is slightly embarrassed / private about what he is doing and does not properly understand why he keeps studying Frieren's magic.

His reconstruction attempts happen in private.

Frieren does not yet know exactly what he is attempting.

### 4. Frieren notices Mau's observation pattern indirectly

Keep the idea that Mau starts watching Frieren's ordinary magic differently, but change his response.

Frieren notices he is studying the details of a spell and asks:

> `What?`

or

> `You were watching.`

Mau does **not** confidently answer:

> `I always watch.`

Instead he evades because even he does not fully understand the impulse.

Possible direction:
- `Nothing.`
- `I was just looking.`
- a shrug / subject change / leaving to do something else.

The important character read is that Mau himself does not yet possess a clean explanation for why he observes magical processes so intensely.

### 5. Frieren notices the private disappearances / attempts over time

Mau's early reconstruction behavior should remain private.

He may occasionally slip away, revisit the E1 location, observe spells closely, or experiment where no one is watching.

Frieren is the person most likely to notice these small disappearances / changes in attention because of how closely they already live together.

She still does not fully understand what Mau is doing yet.

This preserves the later reveal and keeps E9 from becoming an explicit training episode.

## E9 thematic rule

Mau's early ability seed should read as:

> observation before understanding
> private experimentation before explanation
> attachment before power

His eventual flower-field reconstruction remains meaningful because he is trying to recreate the first beautiful thing he remembers someone doing to make him feel safe, not because he wants to prove strength.
