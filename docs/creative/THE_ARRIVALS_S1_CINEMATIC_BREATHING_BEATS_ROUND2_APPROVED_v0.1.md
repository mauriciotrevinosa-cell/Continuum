## Rule

These beats are approved because they improve visual causality, character interiority, transition, atmosphere, relationship depth, recurring motifs, or page-turn rhythm. They are not page-count filler. Exact page growth remains story-driven and can be resolved during panel-script pacing revision / rough manga composition.

All 24 Round-2 beats are approved. Creator-targeted refinements are incorporated below.

---

17. **E1 — Night-watch trust.** During the first camp night, Mau wakes and sees Frieren still awake keeping watch. He sits near her for a moment without asking anything, then falls asleep again closer than before. Frieren quietly shifts the blanket so it covers him better. No dialogue.

18. **E1 — Flower residue.** After Frieren's calming flower-field magic, a small petal remains caught on Mau's sleeve. Much later, Frieren notices it and brushes it away almost absentmindedly. The visual lets the flower motif physically follow them out of the crisis.

19. **E2 — Learning the inn.** Frieren systematically checks exits, windows, roof damage and unsafe rooms. Mau starts by trailing her, then gradually begins opening doors or checking corners ahead of her. Without discussion, he changes from rescued stranger to someone helping inspect the place they may live in.

20. **E2 — First clean window → home question.** Mau wipes one filthy window until evening light enters the abandoned inn properly for the first time. The warm rectangle crosses the floor where they later sleep. Let the visual breathe long enough for the ruin to look, for one moment, like a place someone could live. This becomes the visual lead-in to Mau's important question about whether this is their home now. The image should motivate the conversation rather than merely decorate it.

21. **E3 — Bocchi needs the door.** At bedtime Bocchi keeps choosing a position where she can see the exit. Mau notices and quietly swaps places / moves his things so she can have that spot without making her explain why. She notices after he has already moved.

22. **E4 — An ordinary meal after death.** Once Yuta is accepted enough to sit with them, someone passes him simple food. He hesitates for a beat because eating an ordinary meal in a young body after remembering an old death feels unreal. Then he accepts it and joins the conversation.

23. **E5 — Household choreography.** A tool or small household object passes through several characters over one day: Mau uses it, Bocchi moves it out of the way, Yuta needs it, Frieren returns it, Mau finds it again. No one narrates the coordination; the house begins developing unconscious shared systems.

24. **E5 — Frieren watches Mau learn.** Yuta shows Mau something practical. Frieren looks up from her book, watches longer than necessary, sees Mau get it right, then returns to reading with a tiny satisfied expression. Mau never notices.

25. **E6 — Unconscious mimicry + Raphael notices.** While listening to Rimuru, Mau briefly mirrors a small head tilt, cadence or hand placement. It is subtle enough to read as ordinary social mirroring on first viewing. Raphael notices only the visible behavioral pattern and says one concise line in the spirit of: `Notice. The individual known as Mau appears to be analyzing us.` Rimuru glances at Mau and, rather than interrogating him, casually closes the distance / engages him a little more. This helps motivate Rimuru's attention toward Mau before the later `Raphael` moment.

**Guardrail:** Raphael does not detect, perceive, identify, read or infer The Noise. Raphael does not know why Mau is observing/mirroring. This is a behavioral observation only.

26. **E6 — Two-day wait shown through light.** Mau's post-overload unconscious period gets a small silent time-lapse: daylight across the room, lamp at night, another morning, different people taking turns nearby, Frieren repeatedly ending up closest even when she is doing something else. This makes the two days feel lived rather than stated.

27. **E7 — A place reopens at the table.** After Mau's conversation outside, he returns while dinner is still winding down. Nobody gives a speech. Someone simply shifts a chair / bowl to make room. Mau hesitates, then sits. The group resumes the conversation around him.

28. **E8 — Chain of hands.** During the home-building sequence, use a visual run of materials and tools passing from character to character. The final handoff is Mau to Frieren or Frieren to Mau without either needing to look. It turns trust into physical choreography.

29. **E8 — Leaving something unattended / silent trust recognition.** Mau leaves an object he values in the common room, realizes later he forgot it, comes back and finds it untouched exactly where he left it. Before he returns, Rimuru and Yuta independently notice the unattended object. They exchange a small glance and leave it exactly where it is, saying nothing. Both understand at least one important thing: Mau now trusts them / this household enough to leave something personally important unguarded. When Mau later returns and simply picks it up, the moment confirms that expectation of safety.

30. **E9 — Soundproofing test.** The soundproofing gag becomes a visual experiment: Bocchi plays behind the wall while the others stand outside and react as successive fixes make the sound go from painfully obvious to muffled to nearly gone. Bocchi eventually opens the door because she cannot tell whether they can hear her anymore.

31. **E10 — Fern reacts to the beam before anyone says it.** G2 sees the beam through the sky. Fern's walking pace subtly increases before the others have agreed what it means. Momo notices but does not tease her. The group follows because it is evidence of people, not because Fern already knows Frieren is there.

32. **E11 — Shared-room misunderstanding / sleeping arrangements.** Expand the room material beyond objects. The household's sleeping arrangements are still informal: some characters prefer private rooms; some spaces are shared by convenience, habit or comfort. In a casual room discussion, Frieren states that she shares a room with Mau, but Mau does not hear that answer. Separately, Mau tells Momo with genuine, almost cheerful pride that he has his own room, referring to the nominal room that technically belongs to him. Frieren hears this and is quietly stung—not devastated, but enough to conclude that Mau may now prefer independence. She then adjusts the current sleeping-arrangement description toward Fern staying/sharing with her, trying to respect what she thinks Mau wants. Mau hears that revision and is quietly hurt in return. Neither realizes that each statement is a reaction to the other's previous statement.

This becomes an additional causal bridge into Mau moving belongings and the E12–E14 separation arc. Nobody is being rejected; both are trying to be considerate using incomplete information.

33. **E12 — `Whose room is whose?`** Momo casually asks where everyone actually sleeps / which room belongs to whom. Different people point in inconsistent directions or answer with caveats. It is light comedy, but it reinforces that room boundaries have historically been informal and makes the Mau/Frieren separation feel like a real behavioral change.

34. **E13 — The extra cup / mirrored desire for the old routine.** Frieren unconsciously sets out / reaches for an extra cup or portion for Mau, then notices he is already occupied elsewhere and quietly puts it back. Later Mau independently begins an equivalent old habit meant for Frieren and stops himself. The key meaning is now explicit in staging: both still want the old closeness, but neither knows how to return to it because each believes respecting distance is what the other wants. Their habits reveal desire for the previous routine while their conscious choices suppress it.

35. **E13 — Maomao notices disproportionate vulnerability.** Mau jokes through treatment and leaves once bandaged. Maomao studies the bloodied cloth / wound evidence after he is gone. Later she quietly raises the issue with Yuta: the creature was weak and the injury source should not have produced such a concerning result in an otherwise healthy person. On Mau, the bleeding / tissue response / apparent severity seems disproportionate to the attack. Yuta understands the implication and becomes quietly concerned. No diagnosis or answer is reached.

**Guardrail:** the injury remains survivable and not a crisis; this beat introduces a question about Mau's physical resilience, not a secret lethal condition or solved medical mystery.

36. **E14 — First shared laugh after the misunderstanding.** After `...Okay. We're both dumb.`, let the almost-smile become one brief, genuine laugh between them. It is the first pressure release after several episodes of misreading each other and should not undercut the emotional conversation.

37. **E15 — Gifts reveal the giver.** Expand preparation into micro-beats: Fern favors something practical, Bocchi overthinks whether her idea is embarrassing, Yuta is surprisingly uncertain about what counts as a normal gift, Maomao argues usefulness, Rimuru tries to keep the entire project organized. The gifts characterize the household before Mau ever sees them.

38. **E16 — Everyone sees a different town.** On first entering the mercantile town, give each character a different visual focus: Maomao spots herbs/medicine, Bocchi notices music, Momo watches people, Anko studies signs and behavior, Rimuru clocks prices/logistics, Yuta scans danger, Frieren notices magic, Mau tries to absorb all of it. The world opens through character POV rather than exposition alone.

39. **E16 — Mau studies money before giving it away.** After receiving his first coins, Mau turns one over, weighs it, watches other people spend and asks a small practical question about value. Later, when Bocchi wants the music item, him giving his untouched share feels even more character-specific: he understands what the money can do but still values her wanting something more than owning it himself.

40. **E19 — Season-ending safety contrast.** Before the Sukuna cut, the sleeping-inn sequence briefly revisits season motifs in silence: the E9 flower, Mau/Frieren shared objects, Bocchi/Fern sleeping arrangement echo, guild papers, improved lamps, Maomao's workspace, shoes by doors. One final lamp/window goes dark. PAGE TURN: full bright daylight regional city before Sukuna acts. The visual contrast makes the catastrophe feel like an invasion of a world that had just learned how to feel safe.
