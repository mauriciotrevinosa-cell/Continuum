**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE ROUGH CORRECTION

**Project:** `The Arrivals`

This note supersedes only the **placement assumption** in `THE_ARRIVALS_S1_FINAL_DAY_IN_THE_LIFE_EPISODE_v0.1.md`.

The day-in-the-life / Maomao chocolate episode is an **approved S1 episode-sized concept**, but it is **not currently locked as the final full-length episode of Season 1**.

Its content remains approved rough direction:

- domestic OVA-like / day-in-the-life structure;
- multiple household interactions rather than a major plot arc;
- Maomao experiments with chocolate and a mild aphrodisiac-style effect;
- Frieren accidentally eats one and becomes unusually affectionate / cuddly with Mau;
- Fern becomes jealous / alarmed and stops an attempted kiss because Mau does not understand what is happening;
- Maomao returns, realizes what happened, laughs, and gets scolded by Fern;
- later Mau asks whether Frieren is back to normal;
- Frieren, embarrassed, insists: `That never happened.`

Important guardrail: the gag does **not** force a romance label onto Frieren / Mau. It exaggerates existing affection by lowering inhibition; it does not create a new feeling from nothing.

## Placement rule

Final placement remains open until the late-S1 episode-order pass.

It may appear:

- shortly before the finale;
- earlier in the late-S1 stability stretch;
- between guild / world-integration episodes;
- or in another position that best supports pacing.

Do **not** assume Sukuna's final sting must be attached to this episode merely because an earlier draft suggested that configuration.

Sukuna remains the final **season beat**, but the full-length episode immediately preceding that beat has not yet been locked.

This correction does not reopen the episode's content; it only keeps the episode ordering flexible.
