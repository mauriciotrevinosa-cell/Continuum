## E2 — First Home

Frieren and Mau continue exploring during their first days in the new world and find a tiny abandoned settlement.

Current scale:
- small inn with roughly ten rooms;
- damaged houses nearby;
- broken common area / infrastructure;
- cultivable land;
- cold season / first snow as favored atmosphere.

Frieren decides they can stay there for now. They begin only the most basic cleanup and survival work.

At night Frieren and Mau are talking in the damaged common area when they hear Bocchi scream nearby.

**End E2.**

---

## E3 — Bocchi

Open from Bocchi's perspective. She wakes disoriented near the settlement with a bird on top of her and screams. The scream is ordinary and character-driven, not caused by a major monster.

Frieren and Mau approach. Bocchi is frightened and struggles to speak because of her social anxiety. Mau helps her up; Frieren gathers her belongings. Frieren notices that it is strange another person appeared so close to their new base.

At the inn they explain that they are not from this world either and have only been there a few days. Mau reassures Bocchi that they will help her, gives her a room, and tells her to get comfortable.

### Night beat

Bocchi is cold and notices light / warmth from the common area. She approaches and hears Frieren crying.

Shift to Frieren and Mau. Frieren talks about Fern and her sense of failure. Mau's behavior shows this is not the first time they have gone through this at night: he simply holds her and lets her cry until she sleeps.

Bocchi realizes that the people helping her are also hurting.

Frieren eventually sleeps near Mau, possibly lightly holding his shirt. Bocchi approaches because she is cold and currently afraid to be alone. Mau gives her a blanket and lets her stay near the fire. Bocchi quietly thanks him before sleeping.

### Closing movement

A short multi-day montage shows the first household routine:
- Bocchi helps mainly inside the inn;
- Frieren and Mau explore nearby and return with food / seeds / supplies;
- basic repairs progress;
- Bocchi sometimes plays guitar;
- the three begin functioning as a small household.

**End E3 on routine / safety rather than another arrival cliffhanger.**

## E4 — Yuta

By now Bocchi has been with Frieren and Mau for several days. Around the fourth day after Bocchi's arrival, Frieren suggests fishing at the nearby lake.

Frieren detects cursed energy / an unfamiliar energy signature. Yuta detects Frieren's mana and danger. They briefly misread each other as possible threats.

A short clash:
- Frieren fires Zoltraak defensively / as a warning;
- Yuta cuts, deflects, or survives it through sword work and cursed-energy reinforcement;
- Yuta prepares to respond;
- Mau looks at him and says, roughly: **"You're not from here either."**

Yuta lowers his weapon and the conflict ends before becoming a real fight.

Back at the inn, Yuta can laugh / apologize about the misunderstanding.

### Arrival chronology revelation

Yuta explains that according to his memories he lived far beyond the age of the body he now has and believes he died at old age before arriving here.

This becomes the first strong on-screen evidence that:

```text
source exit state != arrival body state
source chronology != arrival chronology
```

Frieren connects this with her own arrival anomalies: belongings / important things may be reconstructed and bodies may not match their source-world endpoint.

The implication gives Frieren a tiny spark of hope. She begins:

> "Then maybe Fern can..."

and stops herself because she does not want to return to the cycle of desperate hope she experienced during the week of searching.

Mau notices both the hope and the fact that she suppresses it. He does not force her to continue. This is an intentional small beat showing that Frieren and Mau are already watching each other's emotional weak points.

Yuta joins the practical work. The inn remains damaged but is visibly cleaner, stocked, and more functional.

## E5 — Ordinary Days

Deliberate breathing episode / short story unit. Do not introduce another major character immediately.

Cover roughly two or three ordinary days with the four-person household:
- Yuta hunts / secures food / does perimeter work;
- Frieren gathers, cultivates, studies plants and checks the area;
- Bocchi repairs / cleans inside and plays guitar;
- Mau helps everyone, learns simple work, and starts discovering preferences;
- meals, patched windows, small repairs;
- Bocchi gradually ventures farther outside;
- small Yuta–Mau interactions seed their future relationship;
- Frieren sees Mau becoming more independent, without making it a conflict yet.

Narrative purpose:

> Before Rimuru arrives, the audience understands what these four look like when nothing extraordinary is happening.

## E6 — Rimuru / Raphael / Mau overload

Open away from the group, with Rimuru and Raphael.

Rimuru is disoriented and asks if Raphael is really sure she knows nothing about the world. Raphael does not know. Rimuru stays optimistic and assumes he may eventually find Tempest or familiar people.

Raphael detects signs of people / fire.

Rimuru reacts essentially:

> **"People! Finally."**

He approaches happily and finds only the tiny half-broken settlement.

Yuta is on patrol and detects a slime approaching. Raphael recommends Rimuru take humanoid form before the armed stranger reacts. Rimuru transforms; Yuta is confused rather than immediately hostile.

### Raphael analyzes Mau

Raphael passively examines the group.

Frieren is unusual but still analyzable. Yuta's cursed energy is foreign but presents coherent structure. Bocchi is comparatively ordinary.

Mau does not resolve.

The issue is **not that Raphael concludes Mau is overwhelmingly powerful**. The problem is classification / ontology failure: repeated analysis does not tell Raphael what he is.

Raphael may use a Tensura-like analytical method — construct or copy a known pattern / ability / conceptual structure and offer or use it as a comparison / tribute to force a read — and still fail.

Rimuru himself remains friendly and approaches Mau normally:

> "So you're Mau?"

Mau studies him and says very quietly, low enough that only Rimuru hears:

> **"Raphael."**

Raphael is shocked. Rimuru freezes because Mau should not know that name.

Before Rimuru can ask how, Mau suffers an internal conceptual overload / violent mental noise, not a physical explosion, and collapses. Frieren reaches him first.

### Recovery

Small jump of roughly one or two days.

Mau wakes later in his room. Frieren is beside him reading a grimoire while watching him. Bocchi is also nearby, worried, quietly playing guitar.

Mau's first ordinary question can be roughly:

> "What are you reading?"

Frieren takes a moment to realize he is awake, puts the book down, and hugs him. Mau is confused by the intensity of her reaction.

Later Mau explains that he remembers greeting Rimuru and then waking up. He does not remember recognizing Raphael or the overload.

Raphael privately advises Rimuru not to push the issue. She does not understand what happened and another attempt may harm Mau.

End with all five eating together. The inn is improved but remains clearly a repaired ruin.

**E6 completes the G1 arrival arc, not the G1 family arc.**

## E7 — Stories / The Lake

Do not begin immediately with the story-sharing scene. A couple of days have passed since Mau woke.

Frieren is still cautious around Rimuru because, from her perspective, Rimuru approached Mau and Mau collapsed for one or two days. She is not openly hostile, but she rejects help and keeps an eye on him.

Yuta is naturally beginning to act like an older-brother figure toward Mau and Bocchi. Rimuru notices that he is currently the outsider in an already-forming household.

After a normal day of work, the five eat together. Rimuru tries to connect and casually says something like:

> "This reminds me of when my city was still just a village."

That organically becomes a conversation about their worlds and people.

Rimuru shares. Yuta shares. Bocchi eventually talks about Kessoku Band. Frieren opens up a little about her people / travels / Fern / Heiter / Himmel.

Across the scene, Mau becomes increasingly isolated despite sitting at the same table because everyone else has memories and he does not.

Rimuru, trying to include him rather than hurt him, asks:

> "What about you, Mau?"

The question breaks the pressure Mau has been holding. He leaves the inn and runs toward the lake.

Frieren immediately tries to follow. Yuta stops her calmly. Unknown to Frieren, Yuta has already sent Rika to track / watch Mau discreetly.

Yuta tells Frieren he knows where Mau went and asks her to let him bring Mau back.

### Yuta + Mau at the lake

Mau sits at the water's edge. Yuta joins him without pretending to fully understand.

Important ideas for the conversation:
- while Mau was unconscious, Frieren barely left his side;
- Bocchi had told Yuta that Mau has no memories;
- Yuta explicitly admits he does not know what that feels like;
- Yuta can speak about how remembering a whole life does not automatically tell a person who they are now.

After some time Frieren and Bocchi arrive.

Bocchi approaches Mau and mirrors what Mau told her when she first arrived:

> **"We're here to help you too."**

This is a key proof of Bocchi's growth and of reciprocity inside the group.

Frieren initially stays a little behind, allowing Mau to receive care from others rather than assuming she must be the only person who can help him.

### Night closing beat

Later, Frieren and Mau are together in their room.

Frieren asks whether he feels better.

Mau avoids answering directly and asks:

> "And you?"

Frieren answers simply, approximately:

> **"I'm getting there."**

**End E7.**

## E8 — Trust / Building a Home

Continue the unresolved Frieren–Rimuru tension instead of erasing it in one conversation.

Frieren and Rimuru travel together to investigate a nearby dungeon / cave / structure for practical reasons such as materials, resources, mapping, or security.

At the base:
- Bocchi teaches Mau basic guitar;
- Yuta gets a rare period of actual rest / low-stakes work.

During the expedition Rimuru tells Frieren what happened from his perspective:
- Mau said **"Raphael"**, a name he should not know;
- Raphael had been trying to analyze Mau but could not classify him;
- Rimuru did not knowingly attack Mau;
- neither Rimuru nor Raphael understands why Mau collapsed;
- they do not want to force another attempt while it may hurt him.

Frieren does not instantly trust him. Her response direction is:

> **"I don't know if I believe you. But I'll try."**

This is the first intentional step toward trust, not the completion of it.

### Rimuru earns belonging through quality of life

Raphael can advise Rimuru that controlled disclosure of some capabilities may be more effective than waiting for suspicion to disappear.

Rimuru therefore demonstrates useful abilities in a bounded way and begins helping the group organize the settlement.

Crucially, Rimuru does **not** turn the settlement into Tempest overnight and does not automatically become leader.

Instead he asks what the place actually needs and what each person wants.

First coordinated home projects may include:
- a hot bath;
- better kitchen / common room;
- workshop / storage;
- repaired rooms and insulation;
- organized farm plots;
- a small music room / practice space for Bocchi, with room for future instruments such as drums;
- reclaimed materials from nearby houses that are beyond repair;
- modest expansion of the inn;
- a small wooden perimeter / basic fence or wall;
- simple watch / alert points;
- a low-tech warning system using threads / bells around vulnerable approaches.

The key is that Rimuru plans / coordinates while **everyone builds**.

This is the first large physical expansion of the inn from shelter into a deliberate shared home.

It also demonstrates something important about Rimuru: he understands that survival is not enough; people need comfort, hobbies, dignity, and reasons to enjoy living there.

## Early-block emotional progression

```text
E1 = loss / trust seed
E2 = shelter
E3 = first new person / reciprocal vulnerability begins
E4 = Yuta / proof arrival can break chronology / Frieren's dangerous hope
E5 = ordinary life with four
E6 = G1 physically complete / first Mau mystery escalation
E7 = memories / exclusion / emotional reciprocity / family begins to become explicit
E8 = Frieren–Rimuru trust begins / coordinated home expansion
```

## Important guardrails

- G1 being physically assembled does not mean G1 is already emotionally complete.
- Frieren's suspicion of Rimuru after Mau's collapse is reasonable and should resolve gradually.
- Raphael's failure to read Mau is a classification failure, **not** proof that Mau is secretly stronger than Raphael / Rimuru.
- Mau and Frieren's care is reciprocal; neither exists only to emotionally support the other.
- Yuta's older-brother role should emerge through behavior rather than be declared.
- Bocchi's growth should be visible through increasingly voluntary participation and her ability to return the reassurance Mau once gave her.
- Rimuru earns trust partly by improving quality of life and coordinating others, not by replacing everyone else's contribution.
- The first settlement must remain modest enough that losing / leaving it in Season 2 can feel like abandoning a real first home rather than discarding a temporary set.
