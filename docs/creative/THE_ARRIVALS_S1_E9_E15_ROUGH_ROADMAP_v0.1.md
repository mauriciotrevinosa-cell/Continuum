## E9 — Making It Ours

Purpose: give the group another construction / ordinary-life episode before G2, deepen attachment to the first home, and seed Mau's flower-system interest without paying it off yet.

The group continues turning the abandoned inn into a real home. Rimuru uses his pre-reincarnation engineering experience to organize work, but the result remains collective rather than an instant Rimuru solution.

Possible additions:
- terrace / outdoor eating area;
- dedicated training area;
- larger / more functional common room;
- improved kitchen and storage;
- better weatherproofing;
- early shared-work infrastructure;
- continued cultivation;
- small practical upgrades using salvaged material from nearby ruined houses.

The design principle becomes clearer: each resident has a private room, while the compound also gains communal rooms and specialized spaces that can be used by others when appropriate.

### Mau / flowers seed

Mau disappears alone for roughly an hour or two. The audience follows him to the place where Frieren used flower magic in E1. The flowers are still there.

Mau observes them quietly and tries to understand what Frieren did. This is an early `OBSERVE` beat, not a successful reconstruction. No flower-field payoff in S1.

When he returns, Frieren asks where he went. Mau gives a simple answer such as:

> "I went to check on my plant."

The flower system remains largely unknown to the others. Frieren is the person most likely to suspect that Mau is studying something unusual, but she does not expose him or turn it into a group interrogation.

## E10 — Four Strangers / G2 Arrival

Purpose: let G2 exist as a group before merging with G1 and establish Fern's subjective time gap.

### G1 opening beat

On a pleasant day at the inn, Frieren and Mau are eating / relaxing outside while Yuta trains nearby. Frieren, curious about cursed energy, asks Yuta to show a stronger attack.

Yuta fires a large beam / output attack into the sky, inspired by his known high-output use against Ryu.

Frieren may react with something like:

> "What a strange Zoltraak."

The line is observational / comedic rather than technically literal.

### Cut to G2

Fern wakes elsewhere in the forest and initially assumes she was thrown away from the battlefield by the explosion from E1.

Her first thought can be:

> "Mistress Frieren is going to be mad at me."

For Fern, almost no subjective time has passed compared with Frieren's much longer grief/search interval.

Nearby:
- Anko is checking pulse / condition / surroundings like a detective;
- Maomao is already inspecting or trying to taste an unfamiliar plant;
- Momo warns her not to eat it because it might be poisonous;
- Maomao answers, essentially: **"That's the point."**

Fern asks who they are. Anko introduces herself and indicates Maomao and Momo. They have all just awakened there and do not know where they are.

### Why Fern follows the beam

Fern does **not** assume the distant beam came from Frieren.

Her logic is instead:
- attacks of that apparent scale should not be common;
- whoever produced it may be powerful, may know the area, or may be near other people;
- Frieren could plausibly have encountered the same source if she is nearby.

Fern proposes heading toward it.

G2 spends meaningful screen time traveling together — roughly a couple hours of walking is a current rough possibility, exact distance open — so Anko, Maomao, Momo, and Fern develop a recognizable dynamic before the reunion.

### Ending

Back at the inn, Frieren is talking with Mau when she suddenly goes still.

She detects an extremely small, heavily suppressed but unmistakably familiar mana signature.

Mau notices her expression:

> "Frieren?"

From the edge of the forest / settlement:

> **"Mistress Frieren. There you are."**

Frieren looks up and sees Fern.

**Cut.**

## E11 — There You Are

Purpose: deliver the Frieren/Fern reunion, begin G1+G2 merge, and establish Frieren's post-loss overprotectiveness toward Fern.

Frieren immediately approaches Fern, embraces her, and cries. The crying is real but does not need to last long.

Fern is confused because from her perspective the separation was brief. She then notices the hair ornament and can break the tension with something like:

> "Oh. That's where that was."

Frieren can smile / laugh slightly through the tears.

### Hair ornament

Later, Frieren attempts to return Fern's hair ornament.

Fern tells her to keep it a little longer. Fern had already been thinking about giving Frieren something in return for the trouble / worry, and this moment begins an idea that may later connect to Mau's birthday episode.

### Mau gives the reunion space

Mau understands what the reunion means to Frieren and does not insert himself into the moment. He goes to greet / help Anko, Maomao, and Momo instead.

### First large meal / interpretation mismatch comedy

The first G1+G2 meal allows everyone to interpret unfamiliar phenomena through the rules of their own worlds:
- Anko hears about Rika / an "angel" and begins asking Yuta many questions;
- Momo is fascinated by Rimuru and initially tries to fit him into her paranormal framework;
- Maomao is interested in unfamiliar plants, toxins, medicines, food, and bodies;
- Fern observes that Frieren is more expressive / reactive around Mau than she remembers.

### Frieren will not let Fern leave

A recurring comedic/emotional beat begins here.

Fern tries to go explore, scout, or simply walk off to do something useful.

Frieren stops her.

Possible rhythm:

> Fern: "I'll go check the—"  
> Frieren: "No."  
> Fern: "Mistress Frieren..."  
> Frieren: "No."

This is not because Frieren thinks Fern is incapable. She spent a week believing Fern was dead and then additional time in the new world carrying that grief. Her overprotectiveness is a trauma response she has not yet processed.

Fern gradually realizes how deeply her disappearance affected Frieren.

G2 receives provisional rooms by the end, but their long-term specialized spaces are not all built yet.

## E12 — Rooms for Everyone

Purpose: second major home-expansion episode, integrate G2 physically into the settlement, and begin the Frieren/Mau distance misunderstanding.

The inn already has a private-room section. The new work focuses on **specialized rooms and communal additions**, not simply more bedrooms.

Rimuru asks each person what the home still needs.

Examples:
- **Maomao:** a controlled workroom for medicines, poisons, plants, preparation, and storage. Others may enter when she is present / permits it, but it remains primarily her laboratory.
- **Momo:** a warm home-like communal dining / gathering area and eventually a small skywatch / UFO-searching spot because she still hopes Okarun may arrive.
- **Anko:** a practical work / writing / case-notes / observation space.
- **Bocchi:** music space continues developing.
- **Yuta:** training / practical storage areas remain useful.
- **Frieren:** shelves / study / cultivation / practical magical work areas.
- **Rimuru:** coordinated infrastructure rather than a throne-room / leadership space.

### Communal addition

Rimuru proposes a small bathhouse / hot-bath area as a quality-of-life project. This reinforces that the settlement is becoming somewhere worth living, not merely a defensive shelter.

### Start of Frieren/Mau distance arc

Fern is back.

Mau thinks:

> Fern was Frieren's world. She finally has her back. I should give them space.

He begins spending more time elsewhere and may move his belongings into another room, possibly near Bocchi because Bocchi still struggles to sleep alone.

Frieren sees Mau becoming more independent, surrounded by Yuta, Bocchi, Rimuru, and now G2, and interprets it differently:

> He does not need me anymore.

Neither says this aloud.

Fern begins noticing that something between them has changed.

## E13 — Space

Purpose: let the misunderstanding grow through normal life instead of a contrived argument; deepen Fern's observation of Frieren; stage Mau's injury / Maomao misunderstanding.

### Frieren around Mau vs everyone else

Frieren remains recognizably Frieren with everyone, but Mau brings out more visible reactions:
- she smiles more readily;
- she is more reactive when he teases / annoys her;
- she responds faster emotionally;
- she behaves less detached and more spontaneous.

Fern is surprised by this and may become mildly jealous / unsettled at first — not necessarily romantically, but because Mau has access to a side of Frieren Fern did not know.

A small comedic beat can show Mau deliberately saying something to provoke a reaction, Frieren reacting immediately, and Fern watching with a silent **what?** expression.

### Frieren still overprotective with Fern

Fern again tries to explore / help somewhere farther away.

Frieren physically stops or clings to her despite acknowledging Fern is capable.

This is funny on the surface, but it continues showing how strongly the loss affected Frieren.

### Mau studies the flowers again

Mau wants to return to the flower site and asks Yuta if he can "borrow the angel."

Yuta realizes Mau means Rika. Mau had detected / noticed her during earlier events even before he knew what she was.

Yuta sends Rika as a discreet protector while Mau practices / observes.

The flower reconstruction still fails.

### Monster injury / why Rika is late

On the way back, Mau encounters a relatively weak monster.

Rika does **not** ignore an obvious fatal threat. The delay happens because the creature initially appears trivial enough that Rika / Yuta expects Mau to react or move on his own. When it becomes clear Mau is not responding effectively, intervention comes slightly late.

The monster manages to scratch / tear Mau's arm before being stopped. The injury is not severe but is real and reinforces how physically vulnerable Mau still is.

### Maomao misunderstanding

Mau returns late. Maomao notices the injury immediately, makes him sit down, cleans it, and bandages him.

They end up laughing / talking while she works.

Frieren comes down looking for Mau and sees only part of the scene from a distance: Maomao holding Mau's arm while Mau laughs.

She does not clearly see the injury.

Already feeling unnecessary, Frieren interprets the image emotionally rather than accurately:

> He is fine without me.

She withdraws instead of interrupting.

This is not simple romantic jealousy and should not be played as possessiveness. It is layered on top of the existing fear that Mau no longer needs her.

## E14 — The Lake

Purpose: resolve the Frieren/Mau distance arc, clarify the nature of their dependence without locking romance, and seed Fern/Bocchi as an older-sister / younger-sister pair.

Mau leaves again for the flower site.

Frieren, tired of not understanding what is happening, follows him secretly.

Fern notices Frieren acting strangely and follows Frieren at a distance.

### Flower site

Frieren finds Mau looking at the still-existing flowers.

Possible exchange:

> Frieren: "I'm surprised you remembered this place."  
> Mau: "Yeah. It's peaceful here."

Frieren says they need to talk somewhere else.

Without consciously choosing it for symbolism, Mau leads her to the same lake where Yuta previously helped him after the stories incident.

### The mismatched conversation

They sit together.

Mau, uncertain:

> "Sorry."

Frieren thinks she understands what he means and answers calmly:

> "It's okay. Things happen."

Mau:

> "I think I'm a little jealous."

Frieren, still assuming the problem is dependency / growing apart, responds with something like:

> "It's okay not to depend on one person forever."

They both stop.

They realize they are **not talking about the same thing**.

Frieren asks what Mau is jealous of.

Mau explains that he is not sure `jealous` is exactly the right word. He understands how important Fern is to Frieren. He was genuinely happy when they reunited and intentionally tried to give them space. But since Fern returned, it feels to him like Frieren barely notices he exists.

Frieren realizes she interpreted his distance completely backwards.

### Maomao clarification

Frieren brings up what she saw with Maomao.

Mau laughs:

> "She wasn't holding my hand."

He explains that a monster scratched him and Maomao was treating the wound.

Frieren immediately switches concern:

> "Wait. You were hurt?"

Before the conversation derails, Mau tells her the central truth:

> **"I hope you know you're the most important person to me."**

This is deliberately **not** labeled romance. Their bond may later become romantic or may remain another form of profound attachment; S1 does not force that answer.

Frieren does not need a speech. She stays beside him and rests her head on his shoulder.

### Fern understands

Fern hears enough from a distance to understand that while she was gone, Frieren and Mau became emotional pillars for each other.

She leaves before they notice her.

Fern later talks with Bocchi. Bocchi admits she still struggles to sleep alone and often gravitates toward Mau / Frieren at night.

Fern offers to let Bocchi sleep with her.

This begins a sister-like dynamic:
- Fern as a more stable older-sister figure;
- Bocchi as someone Fern can protect without treating her like a child;
- Bocchi as a younger-sister-like relationship for Fern.

### Night resolution

Mau and Frieren return.

Mau starts heading toward his separate room.

Frieren goes to her room expecting Fern.

Fern makes it clear that she and Bocchi will be fine and that Frieren should go to Mau.

A small chibi Frieren beat can show her embarrassed / slightly red-faced without turning the moment into a romance declaration.

Frieren opens Mau's door. Mau is looking out the window.

Possible line:

> "Fern kicked me out."

Cut to Frieren and Mau talking quietly in the room.

Visual payoff: Mau's belongings are once again in the room / shared space where they had been before the misunderstanding.

This resolves the immediate rupture without defining the ultimate nature of their relationship.

## E15 — A Birthday for Someone Who Never Had One

Purpose: a deliberate non-main-plot family episode after the emotional reconciliation. It demonstrates how loved Mau has become and gives the household a reason to celebrate him even though he has no past memories or known birthday.

### Origin of the idea

After E14, Mau goes to sleep / leaves the room and Frieren and Fern have time to talk privately.

Frieren explains more of how she met Mau, his amnesia, how little he knows about himself, and what happened during their first days.

Frieren again offers Fern the hair ornament.

Fern tells her to keep it a little longer.

While thinking about Mau's lack of memories, Fern suddenly asks:

> "Wait. If he doesn't remember anything... has he ever had a birthday?"

Frieren realizes they do not know.

They bring the idea to Rimuru.

Rimuru says he can probably make / fabricate some simple things with his abilities. The quality may not be perfect, but it is enough to create something meaningful.

### Why everyone wants to do it

By this point Mau has quietly become the person everyone goes to:
- he helps when asked;
- listens seriously;
- goes along with other people's odd requests;
- pays attention;
- rarely asks for anything himself;
- is consistently happy to help.

Mau has found a family without fully understanding the concept of family yet.

The others decide to create a birthday for him even if the date is invented / symbolic.

### Secret planning

During one of Mau's absences, Frieren gathers everyone and explains the idea.

Everyone likes it.

Each person chooses or makes something that represents both themselves and what they think Mau might like.

Examples remain flexible, but Anko may give him a notebook, while others choose gifts tied to their own identities / relationship with him.

Rimuru makes a master list of materials and fabrication needs. Yuta and others collect supplies.

Mau unexpectedly walks in while they are discussing gifts. Everyone abruptly changes topic.

Mau notices but does not confront them. He privately assumes it is simply something they do not want to tell him and feels a small, ordinary disappointment rather than a dramatic betrayal.

### Frieren's distraction mission

Frieren already knows what she wants to give Mau and volunteers to keep him away from the inn.

She proposes a small overnight / day-and-night trip for just the two of them, claiming she wants to look for a plant used in one of her favorite dishes.

This gives them a gentle post-E14 episode together:
- walking;
- camping;
- sleeping in the forest;
- teasing / conversation;
- normal companionship rather than crisis.

Meanwhile the inn goes into full-speed preparation mode.

Rimuru helps everyone fabricate / assemble gifts and somehow manages to produce a cake with the available ingredients and abilities.

### Celebration

Frieren brings Mau back in the afternoon / evening.

The inn is decorated as well as the group can manage.

Mau initially does not understand what is happening.

Frieren explains what a birthday is and why they decided he deserved one even if they do not know his original date.

Mau is genuinely excited / overwhelmed in a positive way.

The episode is intentionally not required to advance the central plot. Its purpose is to make the family real and give Mau something new that belongs to **this life**, not a recovered past.

This episode works best **after** the E14 reconciliation so the celebration feels like a family payoff rather than a bandage over unresolved tension.

## Relationship / tone notes preserved across E9–E15

- Frieren is still Frieren. Her greater expressiveness is most visible specifically around Mau rather than becoming a global personality rewrite.
- Fern can feel mild jealousy / confusion when she realizes Mau brings out reactions from Frieren she has rarely seen. This is emotional displacement / surprise first, not automatically romance rivalry.
- Frieren's refusal to let Fern wander off is rooted in grief and fear after believing her dead.
- Mau's distance from Frieren after Fern's arrival is intended as consideration, not rejection.
- Frieren's distance from Mau is intended as a mistaken attempt to respect his apparent independence, not punishment.
- The Maomao scene should remain a misunderstanding layered on top of that preexisting wound, not a possessive-romance cliché.
- Fern ultimately helps repair Frieren/Mau rather than becoming the reason they separate.
- Bocchi/Fern can grow into a meaningful sister-like bond after E14.
- Anko and Momo are introduced here but do not need their major character-focused episodes immediately. Future S1 units can foreground them without requiring every episode to center the same cast.
- The successful Mau flower-field reconstruction is deferred beyond this S1 rough arc, favored for S2 after more system demonstrations and earned growth.
