## Purpose

Single review checklist of every post-Level-4 addition currently intended for Season 1 manga production, before the creator gives the final lock for rough-manga construction.

This checklist summarizes the authoritative cinematic overlay and adds one new creator refinement to the salvage motif: **the abandoned-village contraction must begin as soon as Rimuru commits to repairing the inn, not only in E17.**

The salvage progression is visual/background continuity and does not require new exposition. It should normally fit inside already-added construction / establishing / return-home pages, so the current provisional Season-1 count can remain **1,304 pages** unless rough composition proves extra space is needed.

## E1 — Not This Time
- Mau wakes during the first night watch, sees Frieren still awake, moves closer without asking, falls asleep; Frieren adjusts the blanket.
- A lingering petal from Frieren's calming flower-field magic remains on Mau and is brushed from his sleeve later.

## E2 — First Home
- Mau's blanket subtly shifts closer to Frieren/fire during the first night.
- Frieren and Mau inspect the inn together; Mau slowly starts checking ahead instead of only following.
- Short settlement inventory: well, neglected cultivation, five ruined nearby houses, hill/tree, lake trail, nearly erased old route.
- Mau cleans one window enough to let warm light into the ruin before `Is this our home now?`

## E3 — title pending
- Bocchi studies how naturally Frieren and Mau communicate with little dialogue before accepting help.
- Bocchi repeatedly chooses sleeping positions with sight of the exit; Mau notices and quietly preserves that option for her.

## E4 — title pending
- Yuta studies his young reflection/hands against memories of having grown old and died.
- After trust is sufficient, an ordinary shared meal gets a quiet beat so eating again in a young body lands emotionally.
- Lake clash remains brief; added space belongs to aftermath/interiority.

## E5 — Ordinary Days
- Deer appears in backgrounds before the eventual `plotting` gag.
- One household tool/object passes Mau → Bocchi → Yuta → Frieren → Mau across ordinary work.
- Yuta teaches Mau something practical; Frieren notices Mau succeed from behind her book and returns to reading before he sees her reaction.

## E6 — Rimuru / Raphael / Mau Overload
- Mau watches Rimuru unusually closely after humanoid transformation.
- Mau unconsciously mimics a small tilt/cadence/gesture.
- Raphael may make one visible-behavior observation only: Mau appears to be analyzing them.
- Rimuru casually engages instead of interrogating.
- Mau's two-day unconscious period is shown through daylight/lamp changes, changing visitors and Frieren repeatedly ending up nearby.
- Guardrail: Raphael still cannot detect, perceive, identify, read or infer The Noise.

## E7 — Stories / The Lake
- Mau hears laughter from the warm dinner outside, smiles because the others are happy, then feels the absence of his own past/story.
- After the support/lake beat, his place at the table reopens without explanation: chair/bowl shifts, he sits, conversation resumes.

## E8 — Trust / Building a Home
- Construction `chain of hands` montage ending in a Mau/Frieren handoff without eye contact.
- Mau leaves a personally meaningful object unattended in common space; Rimuru/Yuta deliberately leave it alone; Mau later retrieves it without concern.
- Rimuru reclaims the well: discovers water is not adequately potable, cleans it using his abilities, then comedically launches himself out while Yuta witnesses it; Rimuru is briefly embarrassed.
- **NEW CREATOR REFINEMENT — salvage progression starts here:** when Rimuru says/commits that they are going to fix the place, the visual repair strategy becomes legible. The group begins reclaiming usable boards, beams, stone, fittings, etc. from structures that are already beyond realistic repair. At least one nearby ruin should already look subtly more stripped by the end of the episode while the inn looks correspondingly healthier.
- No dialogue should explain `we are shrinking the dead village`; viewers infer the material transfer.

## E9 — Making It Ours
- Mau and Frieren repeatedly move the real flower until it settles in a mutually accepted spot.
- Soundproofing becomes a readable test sequence rather than one compressed gag.
- Pre-separation clothing baseline: Frieren sometimes takes Mau's cap / hooded outer layer as if ordinary; Mau barely questions it; others notice but do not explain.
- **Salvage continuation:** background repair work can show reclaimed wood/stone becoming shelves, repairs or practical inn improvements; one or more nearby ruins are now visibly missing a little more usable material than in E8.

## E10 — Four Strangers / G2 Arrival
- Beam disappears into the sky on a silent page → page turn to the same sky from G2's side.
- Fern speeds up before anyone has agreed what the beam means; Momo notices and does not tease her.
- Salvage continuity may remain visible in establishing shots of the home but should not distract from G2.

## E11 — There You Are
- Mau starts to approach Frieren/Fern's reunion, recognizes the intimacy, leaves a dropped object near them and gives them space.
- When Mau begins separating belongings, show how thoroughly his and Frieren's things have mixed; he initially removes only objects unquestionably his.
- Mutual accidental hurt sequence: Frieren says she shares a room with Mau but he doesn't hear; Mau later happily tells Momo he technically has his own room; Frieren hears and assumes he wants independence; she adjusts toward Fern staying with her; Mau later hears that and thinks Frieren prefers Fern; neither understands the other's intent.
- Salvaged repairs can be visible in the environment as quiet evidence the household has continued rebuilding while relationships change.

## E12 — Rooms for Everyone
- Mau enters Frieren's room by habit, sees Frieren/Fern, remembers his nominal room and changes direction.
- `Whose room is whose?` comedy: architecture and actual sleeping habits produce inconsistent answers.
- Frieren reaches by habit for where Mau usually leaves his cap and finds empty space.
- New rooms/partitions/furniture should visibly use the same reclaimed-material language seeded in E8–E9, making the repair history spatially coherent.

## E13 — Space
- Mau reaches Frieren's door by habit, stops and returns to his nominal room; Frieren hears the pause/steps without seeing him.
- Extra-cup mirror: Frieren starts preparing for Mau and stops; later Mau makes an equivalent old-habit gesture for Frieren and stops.
- Frieren actually goes to Mau's nominal door intending to speak, hears him talking naturally with Yuta, raises her hand, chooses not to interrupt and leaves; Mau never knows she came.
- Maomao studies Mau's bloodied cloth and privately tells Yuta that such a weak creature/minor wound should not have produced such a concerning-looking result in a normal healthy person; Yuta becomes quietly concerned; no diagnosis.
- Repeat empty-cap / absent-belongings imagery where useful.

## E14 — The Lake
- Mau leaves for the flower site; Frieren tries to keep reading, realizes she isn't absorbing anything, gets slightly restless/irritated, closes the book and deliberately follows; Fern notices and follows later.
- After `...Okay. We're both dumb.`, brief shared laugh/pressure release.
- More silence after `Maybe I was just there because she wasn't.` and around `Exactly.`
- `I hope you know you're the most important person to me.` gets low-panel-count space.
- Sunset visibly progresses while they sit together; Frieren rests her head against Mau; Fern's retained reunion ornament/brooch is visible and Fern notices it before giving them space.
- Return walk: Mau carries Frieren's bag without being asked.
- After `Our room? / ...Yes.`, next morning Frieren comes downstairs wearing Mau's hoodie/outer layer; everyone notices; Mau sincerely says `Nice hoodie.` without realizing it is his; Frieren does not correct him.
- No romance label is added.

## E15 — A Birthday for Someone Who Never Had One
- During Frieren's fake plant-search distraction, Mau gradually realizes her excuse is weak but doesn't press because he is enjoying the day.
- Gift preparation reveals personality: Fern practical, Bocchi overthinks, Yuta unsure what counts as normal, Maomao utility-minded, Rimuru organizing.
- Mau gets more visual time to process the surprise and individual gifts.
- Birthday remains chosen, never recovered from memory.

## E16 — The World Opens
- First town entry through character POV: Maomao herbs, Bocchi music, Momo people, Anko signs/behavior, Rimuru prices/logistics, Yuta danger, Frieren magic, Mau trying to absorb all of it.
- Mau studies local money before the Bocchi purchase so his later gift of his share has clear informed value.
- Old-road logic resolves: from home the route was swallowed by vegetation; from the mercantile-town side they find surviving track/directional logic and realize it is the forgotten route back to their region.

## E17 — Guild Jobs / Working Days
- Repeated exterior return-home composition across workdays, with gradual material changes.
- Fern safe solo-return / `Welcome back.` progression remains.
- **Salvage motif payoff, now explicitly the continuation of E8 onward rather than a new idea:** repeated backgrounds make it obvious that nearby unsalvageable houses have gradually lost roof beams, boards, stone, shutters/fittings, etc., while those exact material types appear in the inn, fences and household structures. By E17 the contrast is strong enough to read clearly: the dead village has physically contracted while the home has become more complete.
- No speech explains this motif and no usable/safe home is casually destroyed just for materials.

## E18 — Day in the Life / Maomao Chocolate
- Chocolate escalation is gradual: Frieren eats unlabeled experimental chocolate, acts normal at first, later sees Mau's belongings upstairs and chooses Mau's hoodie/outer layer + his cap/hat + her own comfortable short bottoms.
- She comes downstairs visibly more `Mau-coded`; Mau double-takes with a small `...Huh?`; Frieren treats it as normal.
- Escalation continues through sitting beside Mau despite other seats, following him with weak excuse, laughing too easily, staring too long, then near-kiss and Fern interruption.
- Guardrails: no kiss contact; chocolate is not a love potion/truth serum; no romance confirmation; Mau still ends with `What's a kiss?`

## E19 — G3 / Road Days / Season Finale
- Two extra silent pages at the safe-night close revisit home motifs: E9 flower, Mau/Frieren shared objects/clothing, Bocchi/Fern shared room, guild papers, improved lamps, Maomao work area, shoes/boots, repaired household details.
- One final window/lamp goes dark.
- Page turn to bright daytime regional city and Sukuna.
- Sukuna chapter itself remains sharp and unchanged: sensing Yuta → `So that brat is here.` → `Domain Expansion. Malevolent Shrine.` → catastrophe → realization that the world/environment is wrong → deliberate cursed-energy suppression → END.
- Never return to the inn after the hard cut.
