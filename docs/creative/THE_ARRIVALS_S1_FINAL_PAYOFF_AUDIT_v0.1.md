# The Arrivals — Season 1 Final Payoff Audit v0.1

**Status:** POINT 9 RESOLVED / APPROVED ROUGH AUDIT  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

This audit answers Season Board item 9: **what S1 promises must pay inside S1, what has already been paid by the rough roadmap, and what is intentionally deferred to S2 or later.**

It does **not** convert E2+ into locked scripts. S1E1 remains the only episode with approved detailed scene-card / manga-panel structure. The rest remains approved rough roadmap material until each episode receives its own detailed pass.

---

## 1. Audit conclusion

The current S1 rough architecture is narratively complete enough to begin manga production from S1E1.

No new major S1 arc is required before production starts.

Three small continuity/payoff beats should be preserved somewhere before the Sukuna finale, but none requires a new standalone episode:

1. **Show at least one real guild job / official world-integration success after E16.**  
   The guild should not be introduced only as exposition. The household should complete at least one legitimate hunt, route-clear, resource, escort, mapping, or official dungeon job and return with earned money / goods. This proves that the group has begun functioning inside the native world before that coexistence later deteriorates.

2. **Give Frieren one visible beat of letting Fern go do something without stopping her.**  
   Frieren's post-reunion overprotectiveness can remain part of her, but S1 should show at least one small sign that she is healing. No speech is needed. A simple moment where Fern leaves on a normal task and Frieren consciously allows it is enough.

3. **Seed Okarun's Momo-specific amnesia during G3's late-S1 material.**  
   S2E6's `Momo: "Okarun!" / Okarun: "Who are you?"` should feel earned rather than retroactively invented. S1 does not need to reveal the whole answer, but Okarun should show that a meaningful part of his personal memory is missing / inaccessible. Momo's side is already seeded because she remembers Okarun and actively hopes to find him.

With those guardrails, Point 9 is considered **resolved**.

## 2. Major promises already paid inside S1

### E1 loss / `Not This Time`

**Promise:** Frieren believes she failed Fern, then chooses to protect Mau in the new world.

**S1 payoff:** Fern returns through G2, proving Frieren's hope was not foolish while also forcing Frieren to process what the loss did to her. Mau is no longer merely someone Frieren protects; by E14 they explicitly recognize how important they became to each other.

**Status:** PAID.

### Fern's ornament

**Promise:** the ornament is physical proof of Fern's disappearance and a grief object for Frieren.

**S1 payoff:** Fern returns, sees the ornament, and understands something happened to Frieren during the time gap. Fern later tells Frieren to keep it longer.

The ornament does not need to be permanently returned in S1.

**Status:** PAID FOR S1 / LONG-TERM OBJECT REMAINS OPEN.

### Arrival chronology can break

**Promise:** Yuta's arrival demonstrates that source exit state, arrival body state, and arrival chronology are not the same thing.

**S1 payoff:** Fern arrives after Frieren has lived through a much longer subjective interval; G2 further proves that people can arrive with different memory/body/time relationships.

**Status:** PAID.

### Frieren's dangerous hope that Fern could return

**Promise:** after Yuta's explanation Frieren almost says `Then maybe Fern can...` and stops herself.

**S1 payoff:** Fern actually returns.

**Status:** PAID.

### Bocchi receives help and later learns to return it

**Promise:** Mau tells Bocchi they will help her when she first arrives.

**S1 payoff:** during the E7 lake beat Bocchi returns the reassurance to Mau with the direction `We're here to help you too.`

**Status:** PAID.

### Yuta becomes more than a combat protector

**Promise:** Yuta begins behaving like an older-brother figure rather than simply another powerful fighter.

**S1 payoff:** he recognizes Mau's distress, sends Rika to watch him, asks Frieren to let him handle the conversation, and speaks honestly with Mau at the lake without pretending to understand his amnesia.

**Status:** PAID.

### Rimuru begins as the outsider

**Promise:** Mau collapses immediately after Rimuru's arrival; Frieren therefore has a rational reason not to trust Rimuru.

**S1 payoff:** Rimuru explains what happened, refuses to push Mau again, earns trust through useful bounded disclosure and engineering, and helps everyone build rather than taking control.

**Status:** PAID.

### G1 becomes family rather than merely five arrivals

**Promise:** physical assembly is not enough.

**S1 payoff:** stories, Mau's lake episode, reciprocal care, ordinary days, coordinated work, rooms, shared meals, conflict/reconciliation, and the later birthday episode make the group a household.

**Status:** PAID.

### The abandoned inn becomes home

**Promise:** the first settlement must become emotionally expensive to lose.

**S1 payoff:** repeated repair / expansion, specialized spaces, bathhouse, music area, Maomao workroom, communal spaces, material upgrades, birthday, and the first emotionally explicit use of **home** in E15.

**Status:** PAID.

### Mau's E1 plant matters

**Promise:** the plant / bucket from E1 remains a small continuity object rather than a disposable gag.

**S1 payoff:** Frieren gives Mau the original plant in the original bucket, now accompanied by flowers, as his birthday gift. It remains in their room afterward.

**Status:** PAID.

### Frieren and Mau's early dependence becomes reciprocal rather than one-way

**Promise:** E1–E3 establish an unusually deep survival/grief bond.

**S1 payoff:** Mau notices Frieren's hope and grief; Frieren stays beside Mau after his overload; E14 resolves their mutual misreading after Fern returns; Mau tells Frieren she is the most important person to him, and Frieren responds through presence rather than an explanatory speech.

**Status:** PAID FOR S1 / ULTIMATE RELATIONSHIP LABEL DELIBERATELY OPEN.

### G2 must feel like a real arrival group, not just Fern delivery

**Promise:** Fern, Maomao, Momo, and Anko should exist together before merging into the main household.

**S1 payoff:** G2 receives its own arrival / travel material, establishes its internal personality mix, then merges into the household with continuing individual roles and spaces.

**Status:** PAID.

### Fern's return should not erase the family built without her

**Promise:** Fern's reunion must expand the family rather than reset Frieren to her pre-arrival life.

**S1 payoff:** Fern notices Frieren's changed relationship with Mau, later understands it, helps repair the Frieren/Mau distance, and begins a sister-like relationship with Bocchi.

**Status:** PAID.

### The world must open beyond the forest / lake / inn

**Promise:** the story cannot remain an isolated crossover bubble forever.

**S1 payoff:** E16 introduces the medieval mercantile town, guild, roads, trade, coast, islands, money, maps, native society, and the neutral `Otherworlder` label.

**Status:** PAID, with one required follow-through beat: complete at least one real guild job before the finale.

### `Otherworlder` must mean something neutral before it becomes frightening

**Promise:** S2 prejudice works better if the label existed before Sukuna corrupts its meaning.

**S1 payoff:** E16 establishes the word as descriptive / curious rather than hateful; Sukuna later hears / recognizes the framing during the finale.

**Status:** PAID.

### Later arrivals remain possible

**Promise:** after Fern returns, the household has reason to hope other missing people may also appear.

**S1 payoff:** Momo continues hoping for Okarun, the world contains more arrival evidence, and G3 begins appearing during the final stretch.

**Status:** PAID.

### Dangerous arrivals are possible too

**Promise:** S1's hopeful found-family trajectory must eventually reveal the darker half of the Arrival phenomenon.

**S1 payoff:** Sukuna appears in the nearest major regional city, recognizes Yuta's presence / the Otherworlder framing, and uses Malevolent Shrine as the final season beat.

**Status:** PAID BY S1 FINALE.

## 3. Promises intentionally deferred beyond S1

These are **not missing S1 payoffs** and should not be forced into the season merely to close loops.

### Mau successfully reconstructing Frieren's flower magic

S1 only needs:

```text
OBSERVE
→ repeated private attempts
→ failure / insufficient capability
→ plant + flowers birthday symbol
```

The first successful large flower reconstruction is favored for S2 after more system growth is demonstrated.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED.

### What Mau actually is / why Raphael cannot classify him

Raphael's failure and Mau's accidental recognition of her are mystery escalation, not a question S1 must answer.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED.

### Full power / weakness disclosure pact

The writers need author knowledge before combat-sensitive scripting, but the characters do not need a formal disclosure meeting in S1.

The in-story pact remains favored for S2 after the Bocchi social attack makes hidden capabilities / vulnerabilities a practical danger.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED.

### The islands becoming the future secure territory

S1 only needs to establish that the coast and islands exist.

The characters should not already know that one island will matter later.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED.

### G3 merging with the main household

S1 only needs to begin G3's arrival / grouping / merchant-road thread. Their actual arrival at the first home remains S2 material after the Bocchi incident.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED.

### Momo / Okarun reunion

Momo remembering Okarun and Okarun not remembering Momo is a S2 payoff.

S1 must only seed both sides honestly enough that the later reunion is earned.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED AFTER REQUIRED S1 AMNESIA SEED.

### Bocchi attack / relocation / city founding

These are S2 consequences of Sukuna's catastrophe, not S1 obligations.

**Status:** INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED.

## 4. Small character threads that do not need full S1 closure

- Fern can remain somewhat protective / attached after her reunion; only one visible step toward mutual independence is needed.
- Anko and Momo do not need major spotlight arcs immediately after joining; they need recognizable roles, relationships, and future hooks.
- Maomao does not need a complete medical storyline in S1; her competence / curiosity / workroom already establishes her function.
- Rimuru does not need to recreate Tempest or become leader; his S1 payoff is belonging and coordinated quality-of-life improvement.
- Bocchi does not need to become socially confident; S1 only needs clear growth from terrified dependence toward voluntary participation / care.
- Mau does not need to understand the concept of family intellectually before E15; the birthday lets the audience recognize that he has one before he fully names it himself.

## 5. Required late-S1 texture before Sukuna

The finale works best only if the audience has had enough time to believe the hopeful version of the story.

Therefore, after the world opens, preserve some ordinary success before Sukuna:

```text
mercantile town discovered
→ guild registration
→ at least one successful legitimate job / dungeon / route task
→ earned money / refined goods improve home
→ G3 begins appearing elsewhere
→ household feels stable enough that the audience can imagine this life continuing
→ Sukuna destroys that assumption
```

This can occupy one episode or several enrichment episodes. The audit does **not** impose a fixed count.

## 7. Point 9 final status

**Season Board Point 9 — Mandatory S1 character / story payoffs: RESOLVED.**

The season has all major required emotional and structural payoffs identified.

Remaining work is no longer `find the missing S1 story` work. It is:

- preserve the three small required beats listed in Section 1;
- perform author/source-snapshot calibration before combat-sensitive later scripts;
- do a light calendar consistency pass before locking exact late-S1 episode numbering;
- then move episode-by-episode from approved rough roadmap into detailed scene / manga planning.

This audit does not justify reopening the approved S1E1 scene structure.
