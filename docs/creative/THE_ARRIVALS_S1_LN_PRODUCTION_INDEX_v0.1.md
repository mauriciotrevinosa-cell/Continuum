## What this pass intentionally does

The LN is not a screenplay transcription.

Allowed expansion used here includes:
- interiority where it does not reveal information early;
- sensory / spatial prose;
- connective tissue;
- prose-native scene transitions;
- ordinary-life breathing room;
- dialogue needed to make visual acting legible in text;
- pacing expansion already approved by the S1 audit.

This pass does **not** silently alter:
- chronology;
- relationships;
- power mechanics;
- arrival timing;
- wardrobe continuity;
- established motivations;
- locked callbacks/payoffs.

## Season-wide continuity locks carried into the manuscript

### E1–E6 / foundation
- E1 macro story remains intact.
- E2 uses the approved longer Frieren/Mau two-person phase before Bocchi.
- Bocchi is written as Bocchi, not generic timid-girl shorthand.
- Yuta/Frieren clash stays brief.
- E5 is a real breather.
- Raphael never detects The Noise.
- Mau does not remember saying `Raphael`.
- E6 does not resolve Frieren/Rimuru trust early.

### E7–E14 / household + G2 + room arc
- Mau's identity wound gains more support without being solved.
- Frieren/Rimuru trust improves through truth and repeated work.
- Mau's flower reconstruction remains unsuccessful in S1.
- G2 shares exactly one night on the road before reaching the household.
- Frieren initiates the Fern reunion.
- The seven-day time mismatch remains central.
- Mau never competes with or resents Fern.
- E11–E14 separation is mutual accidental hurt expressed through room use, objects and habits.
- Frieren's Maomao misread is `he is cared for / fine without me`, not romantic rivalry.
- `I hope you know you're the most important person to me.` is not a romance declaration.
- `Our room?` / `...Yes.` restores domestic reality without defining romance.
- borrowed-clothing callbacks remain domestic continuity.

### E15–E19 / world opening + finale
- Mau's birthday is chosen, not recovered.
- Frieren's E15 gift is the original E1 bucket + Mau's original plant, now healthy and accompanied by flowers.
- E15 carries the first favored emotionally explicit use of `home`.
- `Otherworlder` is coined in E16 as a recent neutral descriptor.
- E16 resolves the forgotten-road geography from the mercantile-town side.
- E17 works through repeated working days, returns and modest earned improvements.
- nearby ruins physically contract as salvage feeds the inn.
- E18 chocolate lowers inhibition only: no love potion, truth serum or romance confirmation.
- E18 near-kiss has no contact.
- fixed G3 travel groups remain unchanged.
- Okarun's missing person remains inaccessible by identity/name/face.
- G3 does not reach G1/G2 in S1.
- E19 ends the safe-night sequence before a hard cut to a distant midday city.
- Sukuna is in the current **Meguna** body state; old Heian-body material is superseded.
- Sukuna says exactly `So that brat is here.`
- then `Domain Expansion.` / `Malevolent Shrine.`
- exact attack radius/casualty count remains unlocked.
- Sukuna suppresses cursed energy only after the catastrophe and wrong-world realization.
- no Yuta reaction occurs before S2E1.
- after the E19 hard cut, the manuscript never returns to the inn.
