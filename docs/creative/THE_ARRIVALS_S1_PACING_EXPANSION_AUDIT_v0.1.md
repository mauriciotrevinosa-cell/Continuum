> Historical note: this was the first pacing audit, produced before the creator-approved 40 cinematic beats, clothing motif, Spatial Bible v0.2 and final spatial review were fully integrated. Its `~1,261` estimate is no longer the current production expectation. The current integrated provisional Season-1 total is **1,304 pages**.

## Purpose

Review the completed Level-4 S1E1–S1E19 manga corpus for places where visual storytelling may need slightly more breathing room than the first provisional panelization allowed.

This pass does **not** add plot, reopen GREEN LIGHT decisions, or impose chapter/page quotas. It identifies modest visual expansions for silence, establishing shots, reactions, transitions, page turns, emotional holds, and other manga-native pacing needs.

The current Level-4 panel scripts remain valid production baselines. These expansion numbers were **soft rough-stage targets** later superseded by the integrated revision overlay.

## Season-level result — HISTORICAL

Original provisional total: **1,227 pages**.  
First soft pacing estimate: **~1,261 pages**.  
First estimated net change: **+34 pages (~2.8%)**.  
Chapter-count structure remained unchanged.

The point was not to make the manga longer for its own sake. The extra pages were reserved for scenes whose emotional or spatial meaning is stronger when the reader is allowed to stay in the image.

# Detailed pacing directives from first pass

## E2 — First Home (+4 first-pass estimate)
Use added space for:
- walking/travel panels that establish Frieren and Mau as a two-person rhythm;
- exterior/interior reveal of the abandoned settlement and inn;
- tactile inspection of the place before it becomes `somewhere to stay`;
- a quieter first-night sequence before the Bocchi scream cliffhanger.

Do not add new exposition or danger.

## E3 — Bocchi (+2)
Use extra pages for:
- Bocchi trying to read Frieren/Mau's relationship from a distance;
- pauses where Bocchi wants to speak and cannot;
- the weight after `Who's helping you?` without resolving it.

## E4 — Yuta (+4)
Use extra pages for:
- spatial clarity before/within the short lake clash;
- reaction space after Mau says `You're not from here either.`;
- Yuta explaining he remembers growing old/dying despite arriving younger;
- Frieren's private hope that Fern might also have arrived, then suppression of that hope.

The fight itself should stay brief.

## E7 — Stories / The Lake (+2)
Use extra pages primarily for the transition from warm group stories into Mau's quiet isolation, so the emotional shift does not feel mechanical.

## E9 — Making It Ours (+4)
Use extra pages for:
- household personalization/details;
- evidence that Mau functionally lives in Frieren's room even though he technically has his own;
- quiet approach to the E1 flower site;
- failed reconstruction attempts with visual concentration rather than extra dialogue.

## E13 — Space (+4)
Use extra pages for:
- repeated negative-space compositions between Mau and Frieren;
- the flower-site attempt / `...form...` fragment;
- Mau's minor wound and Maomao's treatment as mundane care;
- final parallel-room imagery.

No romantic-rival framing.

## E14 — The Lake (+6)
This was the highest-priority breathing expansion in the first audit.

Reserve added pages for:
- larger water/shore compositions during the misunderstanding reveal;
- silent reactions after `Maybe I was just there because she wasn't.`;
- silent reaction after `I thought you didn't need me.` / `Exactly.`;
- one or more low-panel-count pages around `I hope you know you're the most important person to me.`;
- sunset progression as Frieren rests her head against Mau;
- clear but non-expository visibility of Fern's retained brooch/hair ornament;
- Fern noticing the brooch before leaving them alone;
- shared-room restoration montage with fewer objects per panel and more spatial payoff.

The added pages must not turn the scene into a romance confession.

## E15 — Birthday (+4 first-pass estimate)
Use extra pages for:
- ordinary companionship during Frieren's distraction trip;
- a quiet hold after the birthday definition;
- the dark-inn return before the surprise;
- Mau processing cake/gifts individually rather than compressing all reactions;
- final shared-room stillness with gifts + E9 flower.

## E18 — Maomao Chocolate (+4 first-pass estimate)
Use extra pages before the attempted kiss, not after it.

Give visual progression to:
- Frieren sitting slightly closer;
- following Mau when he moves;
- more open laughter;
- staring;
- Fern noticing inconsistencies;
- pauses where Mau understands only that `she's acting weird`.

The extra room should make the near-kiss feel like the end of an escalation, not a sudden genre jump.
