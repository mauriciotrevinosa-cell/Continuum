# The Arrivals — S1→S2 G3 / Sukuna / Bocchi Handoff v0.1

**Status:** APPROVED ROUGH / HIGH-CONFIDENCE DIRECTION — not locked at S1E1 level  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Branch:** `creative/s1-season-board-v0.1`

This addendum supersedes the older rough assumption that G3 must spend many weeks traveling before S2. It keeps the S1 finale intact while allowing G3 to arrive early enough in S2 without an implausibly long unsupported survival period.

---

## 1. Core timing correction

G3 does **not** need to have arrived at the same world-time as Frieren / Mau.

Favored rough timing:

```text
G3 begins appearing during the final stretch of S1
→ first 2–3 days: separate arrivals / small groups / first merchant contact
→ Sukuna catastrophe occurs near the end of that first week
→ S1 ends on Sukuna / Malevolent Shrine
→ S2 covers roughly the next two weeks as both G3 and the news of Sukuna move toward the same mercantile region
```

The major city attacked by Sukuna is now favored to be the **nearest large regional city connected to the protagonists' mercantile town**, not a city on the opposite side of the continent. This makes a roughly two-week rumor / survivor / merchant propagation window much more believable in a medieval world.

Exact days remain open until the season-calendar pass.

## 2. G3 first-week structure

Current rough roster remains:

```text
Mikasa
Okarun
Umaru Doma / Himouto Umaru
Kita
Marin
Wakana Gojo
Qifrey
Coco
```

Favored early groupings remain flexible rather than mandatory:

```text
Okarun + Umaru + Kita
Coco + Qifrey + Wakana + Marin
Mikasa initially alone
```

The more vulnerable group is found first by a merchant / caravan traveling the regional road. Characters more capable of surviving outdoors can remain separate longer before intersecting the same trade network.

The point of the first G3 material is not to rush them toward the main cast. It is to show them surviving, grouping, camping, learning the road, and gradually being gathered by a decent merchant / caravan operator.

S1 may show only the first part of this process before cutting to the Sukuna finale.

## 3. S1 finale remains Sukuna

The final beat of S1 remains short and separate from the protagonists:

- Sukuna appears in the nearest major regional city;
- detects Yuta's cursed energy;
- reacts in the spirit of `So that brat is here.`;
- hears / understands the local `Otherworlder` framing;
- smiles;
- **Domain Expansion: Malevolent Shrine**;
- cut to season end.

G3 does not need to know this happened.

## 4. S2 first-six-episode rough bridge

### S2E1 — Yuta knows

Yuta briefly reacts with genuine terror when he senses Sukuna.

Sukuna suppresses / hides his cursed energy almost immediately.

Yuta therefore knows only:

> Sukuna is here.

He does not know exactly where Sukuna is now or what he intends to do.

G3 continues traveling / being gathered in parallel.

### S2E2 — normal life under hidden threat

G1+G2 continue guild work, home life, jobs, errands, and ordinary routines while the audience knows Sukuna exists somewhere in the same world.

G3 continues road / caravan travel.

### S2E3 — rumors

The first inconsistent reports from the destroyed major city reach the mercantile network through survivors, travelers, merchants, and roads.

The word `Otherworlder`, previously mostly descriptive, begins acquiring fear.

### S2E4 — social deterioration

The mercantile town begins connecting the catastrophe with the known group living in the abandoned settlement near the lake.

Suspicion grows even though there is no evidence that G1/G2 caused anything.

Frieren favors reducing unnecessary friction. Rimuru can argue that they should not abandon a working relationship immediately and that coexistence may still be repaired.

### S2E5 — Bocchi incident

After a period where the group believes relations may have stabilized somewhat, Mau and Bocchi make an ordinary trip into the mercantile town to buy ingredients / supplies and help calm the atmosphere at home.

The situation escalates socially.

Bocchi receives a small but visible cut to the forehead. It bleeds enough to look frightening even if medically minor. She may become faint or briefly lose consciousness from fear / shock; exact severity remains open.

Mau panics and brings her home.

The family sees the blood and understands the real problem:

> some people in the group cannot defend themselves, and the stronger members will not always be standing beside them.

This becomes the emotional / practical breaking point that makes relocation necessary rather than optional.

### S2E6 — G3 arrives

The merchant / caravan carrying G3 reaches the region while anti-Otherworlder tension is already active.

Rather than bringing them through the mercantile town, the merchant directs them toward the abandoned settlement near the lake after hearing / knowing that several other Otherworlders live there.

G3 therefore can reach the main cast without first entering the hostile mercantile town.

The arrival occurs roughly a day after the Bocchi incident, once Bocchi is already medically okay but visibly bandaged.

Kita sees Bocchi with the bandage before the full group has time to settle into introductions and immediately realizes something happened.

Other early interactions can begin around this moment:

- Umaru meeting Mau;
- Marin and Wakana relaxing somewhat when they see peers / familiar social energy;
- Qifrey trying to understand Frieren / Rimuru / the world's magic structure;
- Coco reacting with fascination;
- Mikasa evaluating the new group and environment pragmatically.

## 5. Okarun / Momo asymmetrical memory reunion — IMPORTANT

Okarun's current project state includes **amnesia regarding Momo**.

Momo's transfer had the opposite effect: after arriving in this world, her memories of Okarun returned / became accessible again.

This asymmetry is intentional.

Therefore do **not** write Okarun as recognizing Momo when G3 arrives.

Favored S2E6 closing beat:

- the new arrivals are being brought inside / introduced;
- Momo comes downstairs after hearing the commotion;
- she sees Okarun;
- for an instant she thinks she may still be dreaming / misperceiving what she sees;
- then recognition hits completely;
- she calls out, emotionally: **"Okarun!"**
- Okarun turns toward her, confused;
- his response is simply:

> **"Who are you?"**

Cut to black / episode end.

This is not a happy reunion yet. It is a deliberately asymmetric reunion:

```text
Momo remembers Okarun
Okarun does not remember Momo
```

It also reinforces the broader Arrival rule that memory effects are not guaranteed to be symmetric or identical between people.

---

## 6. Why this handoff works

The S1→S2 bridge now uses two simultaneous medieval-speed movements:

```text
Sukuna catastrophe
→ survivors / rumors / merchants
→ mercantile town

G3 arrivals
→ road / merchant / caravan
→ abandoned settlement near the lake
```

These two currents converge over roughly the first two weeks of S2.

This preserves:

- S1 ending on Sukuna;
- medieval information speed;
- G3 not surviving alone for an implausibly long period;
- the mercantile town social arc;
- Bocchi as the breaking point;
- G3 arriving at the emotionally worst / most interesting moment;
- the future relocation / island-search arc;
- Okarun / Momo's amnesia asymmetry as its own major payoff rather than an automatic reunion.
