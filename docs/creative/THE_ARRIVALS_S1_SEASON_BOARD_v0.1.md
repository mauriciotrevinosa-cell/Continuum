# The Arrivals — Season 1 Story Board v0.1

**Status:** ROUGH PLANNING — not locked canon  
**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Purpose:** compact season-level board used to discover the minimum episode count before adding optional/bonding/adventure episodes.

This document does not replace the approved S1E1 story or manga panel script. It collects current high-confidence directions, rough episode ideas, open questions, and the known bridge into Season 2.

---

## 1. Planning rule

Do not choose a fixed episode count first.

Workflow:

```text
required S1 story beats
→ define character/world/power facts needed to support them
→ split only plot-essential beats into the minimum number of episodes
→ then add bonding / slice-of-life / training / dungeon / exploration / comedy episodes where they improve the story
```

Episode runtime is flexible. A story unit may be shorter, normal-length, or ~30 minutes if that is what it needs.

## 4. Episode 2 — high-confidence rough

Frieren and Mau continue exploring and find a very small abandoned settlement.

Initial scale idea:
- one inn with roughly ten rooms;
- several damaged / abandoned houses;
- a small common area;
- usable land for cultivation;
- visibly incomplete / broken infrastructure.

Frieren decides this is enough for now: a temporary home rather than a permanent city.

Favored opening-season weather direction: first snow / cold season, which makes shelter matter immediately.

Night ending:
- Frieren and Mau talk in the damaged common area;
- they hear Bocchi scream;
- cut to end credits.

Exact wording, timing, and whether Bocchi is found before or after the scream remain open until E2 is scripted.

## 5. Frieren + Mau recurring nighttime thread — rough / high-value

A possible recurring E2–E3+ behavior:

- Mau continues sleeping in Frieren's room rather than taking a separate room.
- Practical reason: cold / first snow / damaged inn.
- Emotional reason: after E1, Frieren and Mau have quickly developed an implicit or explicit `100% trust in the new world` bond.
- After finally opening up to Mau, Frieren sometimes cries at night over Fern / Heiter / loss.
- Mau comforts her and helps her fall asleep.
- Frieren's friendlier / more open presentation may partly function as a defense mechanism while grief is still active.

Bocchi can understand that Mau being there at night is not romantic; he is helping Frieren through grief.

Possible early Bocchi beat:
- Bocchi hears / realizes Frieren has fallen asleep;
- Bocchi is frightened and does not know what to do with being alone in a strange world;
- she quietly enters the room because she currently cannot handle sleeping alone.

This can become an early found-family image without literal parent/child framing and without forcing romance.

Potential later emotional plot:
- Frieren sees Mau becoming more capable and starts focusing on other responsibilities because she assumes he no longer needs her as much;
- Mau experiences that distance more strongly than she expects;
- the issue exposes how central their early trust became for him.

Not locked yet; preserve for later character-arc placement.

## 6. G1 formation

G1 remains:

```text
Frieren
Mau
Hitori Gotoh / Bocchi
Yuta Okkotsu
Rimuru Tempest
```

Important correction to earlier shorthand: **G1 does not need to be complete by Episode 6.** The formation arc may take longer if the character work benefits.

The goal is not merely to place all five in one location. Each arrival needs enough first conflict, first trust, and first bonding that the group feels earned.

### Required / favored G1 bonding beat

At some point after several members have arrived, the group talks about their past lives / worlds / people they remember.

Mau realizes everyone else has memories and he does not.

He leaves for a while.

Frieren wants to follow him, but Yuta tells her to stay / keep watch over the others and says he will speak with Mau.

This gives Yuta and Mau a real relationship beat and lets Mau's amnesia hurt emotionally rather than exist only as lore.

Placement remains open; likely after Yuta and before or around Rimuru's integration.

## 7. First-home / world-expansion progression

The abandoned mini-settlement is the first shared base.

It should improve visibly as the cast grows.

Early progression can include:
- making the inn weather-safe;
- cleaning rooms;
- repairing roofs / doors / common space;
- securing water;
- beginning cultivation;
- basic storage / food systems;
- mapping nearby roads and resources;
- establishing routines.

Rimuru's arrival can accelerate organization and development, but the home must still feel collectively built rather than instantly solved by one character.

## 12. Known Season 2 bridge — preserve during S1 planning

Existing S2 rough remains active:

```text

→ delayed news of Sukuna catastrophe
→ Otherworlder fear / blame
→ trust / power disclosure
→ social rupture
→ attack on Bocchi
→ current mini-settlement no longer feels sufficiently safe
→ relocation / search for defensible territory
```

After the Bocchi incident, relocation should create its own small exploration / cartography arc rather than teleporting directly to the final answer.

Current rough location idea:
- while mapping / exploring around the broader mercantile region, the group discovers an island separated from the large mainland;
- the first version can be relatively small but defensible and expandable;
- rough capacity target to test against world scale: enough room for on the order of ~1,000 residents / future good-aligned arrivals and locals, not a giant continent;
- Rimuru or later infrastructure may allow expansion / reclamation / more intensive development in the future;
- exact geography, distance from trade routes, water / farmland, harbor potential, monsters, ownership, native politics, and expansion mechanism are OPEN.

This is a candidate secure-territory direction, not locked geography yet.

## 13. Ten things to define before calculating the minimum S1 episode count

Resolve these one at a time:

1. **G1 arrival order and arrival situations** — Bocchi, Yuta, Rimuru.
2. **G1 integration requirements** — what each needs before they truly feel part of the family.
3. **First-home progression** — what changes in the mini-settlement and when.
4. **Mau early emotional arc** — amnesia, dependence / independence, Frieren distance, Yuta conversation, first major growth.
5. **G2 arrival / reunion structure** — exact circumstances and route back to Frieren.
6. **G3 S1 seeds** — who the audience sees, where, and in what recurring thread.
7. **World scale** — geography, travel time, settlement sizes, danger frequency, economy / trade, magic rarity.
8. **Power / source snapshot framework** — what version of each character arrives and how capabilities are compared without a single-number ranking.
9. **Mandatory S1 character / story payoffs** — flowers, home moments, trust, bonding, discoveries, conflicts.
10. **Sukuna finale information state** — what the audience knows, what the protagonists know, and exactly what changes at season close.

Only after these ten are stable do we estimate the **minimum plot-essential episode count**.

Then we deliberately add episodes that improve the series, such as:
- slice-of-life;
- training;
- dungeon exploration;
- mapping / exploration;
- comedy;
- relationship / bonding;
- ordinary work / hobbies;
- character-focused days.

Example planning logic:

```text
plot-essential minimum = X episodes
+ chosen enrichment episodes = Y
→ actual season length = X + Y
```

No quota is assumed in advance.
