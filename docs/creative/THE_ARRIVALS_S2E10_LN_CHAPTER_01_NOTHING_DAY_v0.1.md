<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln01-cooking -->

Nothing important happened on the first day.

Dinner nearly failed.

This was different.

Too many people tried to help.

Marin wanted to improve presentation.

Momo wanted to improve quantity.

Maomao wanted everyone to stop touching ingredients.

Coco wanted to know why Maomao objected to one specific root.

Qifrey wanted Coco to stop asking while Maomao held a knife.

Umaru wanted fried food.

Rimuru wanted order.

Nobody got what they wanted.

The pot survived.

Dinner happened.

It was too salty.

Everyone ate it anyway.

Staring at the table, Mau said, "This is bad."

Frieren tasted it.

"Mostly."

Mau looked at her.

She was smiling into the spoon.

He smiled too.

<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln01-music -->

After dinner, Bocchi played.

Kita sat close.

Marin sang one line badly on purpose.

Then one line well.

Momo joined.

Coco tried to follow.

Umaru refused, then joined when she thought no one was listening.

Mikasa stayed near the edge of the room.

Anko pulled her closer by moving the chair rather than asking.

Yuta tapped the table in time.

Rimuru became a slime and sat on Frieren's lap because Frieren was reading and therefore least likely to move.

Frieren turned a page over him.

"This is inconvenient."

"You haven't moved."

"Still inconvenient."

Mau sat on the floor beside the chair.

Coco leaned against his back.

Umaru leaned against Coco.

The arrangement looked structurally unsound.

It held.

Nothing important happened.

<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln01-late -->

Later, Momo and Maomao investigated Rimuru.

This began because Momo claimed slime-Rimuru had moved three centimeters while pretending to sleep.

Maomao said that was not an investigation.

Anko asked what would make it an investigation.

Maomao made the mistake of answering.

Ten minutes later, Rimuru had chalk marks around him and Momo was whispering about motive.

"I hate all of you," Rimuru said.

Momo gasped.

"The suspect speaks."

Maomao looked down at him.

"Can slimes perspire?"

Rimuru changed back into human form immediately.

The chalk circle was now around his feet.

Anko laughed so hard she dropped the notebook.

Frieren looked up from her book.

"What happened?"

Mau tried to explain.

Halfway through, Frieren laughed.

Quiet.

One sound.

Then returned to reading.

Mau stopped talking.

Fern looked at him.

"Continue."

"I forgot."

Fern rolled her eyes.

Nothing important happened.

That was the point.
