<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln02-lazy -->

On another day, almost everyone was tired.

No battle.

No mission.

Work.

Repairs.

Scouting.

Seventeen people doing the work of keeping seventeen people fed, warm and not buried under their own belongings.

The common room filled slowly after lunch.

Mau sat on the floor with his back against the bench.

Frieren sat on the bench and leaned against him while reading.

This had become normal enough that Kita no longer stared.

Mostly.

Fern and Anko were arguing about whether a rumor counted as useful information.

Mau joined.

Frieren joined without lifting her eyes from the book.

"That's not evidence," she said.

Anko looked at her.

"You weren't listening."

"I was."

"What did I say?"

Frieren repeated the last three sentences exactly.

Anko stared.

Mau said, "She does this."

Frieren turned a page.

"Mostly."

Anko pointed.

"That word again."

Fern sighed.

"You don't want to know."

<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln02-study -->

At the other end of the room, Qifrey had turned one table into a lesson.

Coco was drawing.

Mau was staring.

These were not the same activity.

"You are doing it again," Coco said.

Mau looked at her.

"What?"

"Trying to understand the whole thing before drawing one line."

Qifrey smiled.

"She is correct."

Mau looked betrayed.

"You taught her."

"I teach both of you."

Frieren lowered her book from the bench.

"He's overthinking the first stroke."

Mau looked at her too.

"You aren't in the lesson."

"I can see it."

Fern passed with folded cloth.

"She's right."

"This has become hostile."

Coco laughed.

Then she pushed a piece of bread toward him.

Mau looked down.

"What's this?"

"You forgot lunch."

"I did not."

Qifrey looked at him.

Mau looked at the bread.

"...I delayed lunch."

Coco pushed it closer.

"Eat and then overthink."

Mau took it.

Rimuru entered carrying a ledger, saw the spell geometry on the table, and stopped.

"Why does that line do that?"

Qifrey answered.

Frieren disagreed with one word.

Fern disagreed with Frieren's correction.

Coco started drawing again.

Within five minutes, the lesson had become six people arguing around one sheet of paper.

Mau ate the bread.

Coco finished her first clean line before he did.

She looked unbearably pleased.

Mau pointed at it.

"Again."

Coco grinned.

The table had stopped being a classroom.

It was just another part of the house.


<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln02-wakana -->

Wakana brought down a finished winter layer for Coco.

Not elaborate.

Better fitted than the improvised one she had been using.

Coco put it on.

Spun once.

Then stopped because Qifrey was looking at her with the expression adults got when they tried not to become emotional in public.

"It fits."

"I can adjust it."

"It fits."

Wakana smiled.

Marin immediately asked what he could do with her coat.

Wakana's face changed.

"You have three coats."

"Exactly."

Rimuru looked up from the ledger.

"No."

Marin looked betrayed.

The wardrobe of the house continued to grow.

Not from nowhere.

From hands.

Repairs.

Trade cloth.

Pieces reused.

People learning what other people liked.

Mau's original backpack no longer contained all of his clothes.

Some were on his body.

Some in the shared room.

Some being repaired.

Some currently worn by Frieren.

The backpack itself stayed under a chair.

An artifact from a life he still could not remember.

It no longer looked like luggage.

It looked like storage.

<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln02-bocchi -->

Someone asked Bocchi a direct question at dinner.

She froze.

Kita waited.

Nobody rescued her.

That was new too.

Not cruelty.

Trust.

Bocchi took three seconds.

Then five.

Then answered.

Quietly.

The conversation continued.

Kita smiled into her food.

Bocchi saw.

Became red.

Mau looked away before she caught him looking.

Frieren noticed.

"Why did you turn?"

"Nothing."

"You were watching Bocchi."

"She answered."

Frieren looked at Bocchi.

"Oh."

That was all.

Bocchi did not need applause every time she survived being perceived.

The household was learning that growth could be ordinary.

<!-- scene-id: s2e10-ln02-night -->

That night, the common room emptied in pieces.

Mikasa went upstairs without checking the front latch twice.

Umaru took a snack and said goodnight to Coco.

Okarun and Mau left a broken tool on the table with a note that said TOMORROW.

Momo added MAYBE.

Anko added DEFINITELY.

Rimuru crossed out all three and wrote AFTER BREAKFAST.

Frieren left her book on Mau's side of the table.

Mau's watch lay beside it.

Nobody moved either.

Nothing happened.

The house kept becoming theirs.
