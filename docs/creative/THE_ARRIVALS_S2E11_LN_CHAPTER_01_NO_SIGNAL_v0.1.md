Marin had checked her phone every day since arriving.

This was not because she expected service.

It was because human beings were irrational.

On the ninth day, she checked again.

No signal.

No messages.

No internet.

No new photos.

No world behind the glass except the things already inside it.

She stared at the screen.

Then threw herself backward onto the common-room rug.

"I hate this."

Momo looked over from the table.

"What happened?"

"My phone is decorative."

Umaru, from the couch, said, "I understand your pain."

That was the beginning.

Momo moved closer.

Marin showed her the dead network icon.

Momo showed her an old device that had become equally useless.

Umaru produced something from under a blanket that should not have survived road travel.

Coco became interested immediately.

Maomao did not.

"This is not medicine."

"No one said it was," Marin said.

"Then why am I here?"

Momo smiled.

Maomao narrowed her eyes.

"No."

"You don't know what I'm asking."

"No."

"We're going outside."

"No."

"See? You knew."

Maomao returned to her notes.

Momo looked at Marin.

Marin understood.

They each took one arm.

Maomao became dangerous.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln01-pack -->

The outing grew by accident.

Bocchi became involved because Kita was involved.

Fern joined because someone needed to ensure Momo returned with the same number of people she left with.

Umaru announced she would remain home.

Mau looked at her.

"You should go."

Umaru looked betrayed.

"Why?"

"Because you were complaining about being inside."

"I complain about everything."

"That's true."

"Then my complaint is not evidence."

Mau considered.

"Go anyway."

Umaru looked at the group.

Then at him.

He understood the hesitation.

"You don't have to stay where I am to know this is home."

Umaru stopped.

Mau realized the sentence was more serious than he intended.

He tried to fix it.

"Also Momo cheats at games, so you should supervise her."

Umaru stood.

"That part is true."

Mikasa volunteered to stay.

Momo refused.

"We have Yuta."

"I can help with patrol."

"You always help with patrol."

"Yes."

"Today you're helping us."

"With what?"

Momo looked around.

No one had actually decided.

"Outside."

Mikasa stared.

Marin took her sleeve.

"Perfect."

Mikasa could have stopped her.

She did not.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln01-objective -->

They eventually invented a reason.

Maomao needed plants.

Wakana had mentioned a local plant that could produce a useful dye if Marin wanted color not imported from an impossible world.

Fern wanted to inspect a short route along the lake that had become passable again.

Three practical reasons became one social outing.

Mau watched them leave.

Seventeen people had changed the shape of departure.

Once, when someone left the inn, he tracked the absence.

Now a group could disappear down the road and the house remained full.

Frieren appeared beside him.

"You're watching."

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Umaru went."

Frieren looked at the road.

"Good."

Mau nodded.

Then looked at her.

"You didn't go."

"I wasn't kidnapped."

"That's fair."

Frieren opened her book.

Mau looked toward the road one more time.

Then went inside.

The people he cared about had lives that did not require him in the frame.

He was learning to like that.
