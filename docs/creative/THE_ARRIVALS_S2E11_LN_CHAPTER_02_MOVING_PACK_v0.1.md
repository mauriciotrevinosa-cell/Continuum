<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln02-road -->

The group moved badly.

Not slowly.

Badly.

Marin walked wherever conversation took her.

Momo walked backward while talking until Fern threatened to tie her to the path.

Coco stopped for anything interesting.

Maomao stopped only for plants and became annoyed when everyone else stopped for anything else.

Umaru wanted breaks at mathematically unreasonable intervals.

Bocchi and Kita stayed close.

Mikasa automatically took the rear.

Fern noticed.

"Walk in the middle."

Mikasa looked at her.

"Why?"

"Because you're not escorting us."

"Someone should watch behind."

Fern looked at Momo walking backward.

"She is."

Momo waved.

Mikasa's mouth moved.

Almost a smile.

She moved into the group.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln02-plants -->

The first useful plant was ugly.

Marin objected.

"That's the dye?"

Maomao crouched beside it.

"Part of it."

"It's brown."

"The extracted color is not."

"What color?"

Maomao explained.

Marin's entire posture changed.

Wakana would be able to use it.

That mattered more than appearance.

Soon everyone was gathering.

Even Umaru.

Not efficiently.

Enough.

Bocchi found a cluster first and called Kita before remembering other people could hear her.

"Bocchi-chan, that's a lot."

Bocchi looked proud.

Then realized Marin and Momo were also looking.

She began to disappear.

Kita did not rescue her.

She simply said, "Show me which ones are right."

Bocchi focused on the plants.

The attention became about something she could do.

The panic passed.

Fern noticed.

Stored it.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln02-water -->

At the lake route, they stopped for food.

No threat appeared.

No monster.

No stranger.

Nothing required Mikasa to draw a weapon.

She sat on a rock with bread in her hands and seemed vaguely suspicious of the entire concept.

Marin sat beside her.

"Do you ever relax?"

"Yes."

"When?"

Mikasa thought.

"Sleeping."

Umaru looked over.

"That doesn't count."

"Why?"

"Because you're unconscious."

Mikasa looked at her bread.

"I am not always unconscious when I sleep."

Everyone stopped.

Momo said, "That is the saddest sentence I've heard today."

Mikasa looked confused.

Then Marin offered her a piece of something sweet.

Mikasa took it.

No one asked about war.

They talked about food instead.

That was better.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln02-maomao -->

Maomao attempted to leave early.

Momo caught her.

"We're not done."

"We collected the plants."

"That was the excuse."

Maomao stared.

"You admit it?"

"Of course."

Marin nodded.

"You're socializing."

"I refuse."

"You've been here four hours."

"Against my will."

Fern looked at Maomao.

"You stayed after we finished."

Maomao became quiet.

Momo smiled slowly.

Maomao pointed at her.

"If you say anything, I'll poison you."

Momo looked delighted.

"She likes us."

Maomao walked away.

Everyone followed.

Moving pack.

No leader.

No formation.

A social animal that had not existed a month ago.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln02-home -->

They returned before dinner.

Mau was helping Wakana repair a storage rack.

Umaru entered first.

Mau looked over.

"Good?"

Umaru shrugged.

"Fine."

Then she immediately went to tell Coco everything.

Mau smiled.

Frieren, sitting nearby, said:

"You look pleased."

"She didn't tell me first."

Frieren turned a page.

"That's why?"

"Yes."

Frieren thought.

"That makes sense."

Mau sat beside her.

The house received the returning group in pieces.

Plants went to Maomao.

Dye material went to Wakana.

Food went missing.

Stories spread.

No one announced that a girls' social network had formed.

By bedtime, it already had.
