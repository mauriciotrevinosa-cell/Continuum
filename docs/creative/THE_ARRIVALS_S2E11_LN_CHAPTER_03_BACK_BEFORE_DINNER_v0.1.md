<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln03-dye -->

Wakana tested the dye two days later.

Everyone had opinions.

The color was not the color Marin expected.

This became a crisis.

"It's better," Kita said.

Marin stared.

"...You're right."

Crisis ended.

Wakana used the first successful batch on repaired cloth, not a dramatic outfit.

A lining.

A scarf.

A patch that no longer looked like a patch.

The house learned a new color.

Frieren watched the process with more interest than she admitted.

Mau noticed.

"Do you want something?"

"No."

Wakana looked up.

"Are you sure?"

Frieren looked at Mau's hoodie hanging nearby.

Mau followed her gaze.

"No."

Frieren looked at him.

"I didn't say anything."

"You were going to."

"You don't know that."

"I do."

Wakana quietly continued working.

Marin quietly did not.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln03-kitabocchi -->

Kita and Bocchi took a walk alone.

This was the first time Mau realized they had left after they were already gone.

He looked toward the door.

Fern was reading.

"You knew?"

"Yes."

"Where?"

"Lake path."

"When are they back?"

Fern looked at him.

Mau stopped.

"Right."

He returned to what he was doing.

At the lake, Kita sat on the dock.

Bocchi sat beside her.

Not touching.

Close.

Kita looked at the water.

"You've changed."

Bocchi immediately panicked.

"Bad?"

"No."

"Okay."

"And not completely."

Bocchi waited.

"You still look like you're going to die when someone asks you a question."

"...Yes."

"You still hide."

"Yes."

"You also left home with a guy you barely knew, lived in a ruined inn, made a family, got hurt, and then invited me inside like you owned the place."

Bocchi's face became red.

"I don't own it."

"You know what I mean."

Bocchi looked at the water.

Kita's voice softened.

"I'm glad."

Bocchi looked at her.

Kita smiled.

"I'm glad you weren't waiting for us to come save you."

Bocchi's eyes filled.

Kita immediately panicked.

"Not crying. No crying."

"You made me."

"I know, sorry."

Bocchi laughed through it.

Their shoulders touched.

Neither moved away.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln03-mikasa -->

Mikasa's next patrol was optional.

She did not go.

Yuta noticed.

Said nothing.

She stayed in the common room while Momo taught a game badly.

Mikasa learned the rules faster than Momo explained them.

Then lost because Momo cheated.

Mikasa stared at the table.

Momo smiled.

"Welcome."

Mikasa looked at Yuta.

"Is this allowed?"

Yuta said, "No."

Momo pointed.

"He's bitter."

Mikasa played again.

This time she caught Momo cheating.

She cheated back.

Anko saw.

Said nothing.

War had taught Mikasa to adapt.

Apparently so could cards.

<!-- scene-id: s2e11-ln03-evening -->

By evening, the moving pack had dissolved into smaller groups.

That was healthier.

Marin and Wakana argued about color.

Fern helped Coco with a local-language book.

Umaru played with Momo.

Maomao hid from everyone and was found anyway.

Kita sat with Bocchi near the stairs.

Mikasa helped Anko carry paper.

Nobody needed Mau to connect them.

He stood in the kitchen holding two cups.

Frieren took one.

"You're staring again."

"I know."

"At everyone."

"Yes."

Frieren drank.

"Stop counting."

Mau looked at her.

Fern's old instruction.

He smiled.

"Okay."

Seventeen was no longer a number he had to hold in his head.

They were people with places to be.
