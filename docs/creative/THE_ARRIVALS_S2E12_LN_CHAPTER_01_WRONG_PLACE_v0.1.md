<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln01-route -->

The first place everyone wanted to like was beautiful.

That was suspicious.

A valley opened north of the lake route.

Forest on two sides.

Rock rise on a third.

Enough flat ground for more than one building.

A stream.

Old stone foundations from something that had existed before.

Marin saw it and said, "Yes."

Rimuru said, "No."

They had been there twelve seconds.

Marin turned.

"Why?"

Rimuru pointed at the stream.

"Maybe."

"That's not no."

"It's enough to not say yes."

The scouting group spread out.

Rimuru.

Mau.

Yuta.

Mikasa.

Qifrey.

Coco.

Fern.

Kita.

Not a combat party.

Not source groups.

People who noticed different problems.

<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln01-water -->

The stream failed first.

Not poisoned.

Seasonal.

Fern found the high-water line.

Mikasa found debris caught much higher than the current bank.

Rimuru measured the slope.

"In heavy rain, this entire section floods."

Marin was not there to be disappointed.

Mau was.

"We could build higher."

"Some of it."

"Drainage?"

"Possible."

Qifrey examined the ground.

"The soil holds water."

Coco crouched beside him.

"So it stays wet."

"Yes."

Mau looked toward the rock rise.

"Could build there."

Yuta walked the approach.

"Then everything comes through one narrow route."

"Defensible."

"Also easy to trap."

Mikasa nodded.

"And hard to evacuate."

Another mark against it.

Not impossible.

Wrong.

<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln01-winter -->

The final problem was sunlight.

Fern noticed because Frieren was not there.

That amused her.

The rock and trees blocked more of the low afternoon light than the map suggested.

"In winter," Fern said, "this side will be cold."

Rimuru looked at the line of shadow.

"Very."

Mau stared at the foundations.

The place had looked like an answer from the ridge.

Up close, it became work.

Too much work in the wrong categories.

They could solve drainage.

Build roads.

Reinforce.

But every solution fought the site instead of using it.

Mau sat on one old stone.

"So no."

Rimuru sat beside him.

"No."

Coco looked disappointed.

Qifrey smiled.

"Finding the wrong place is useful."

"How?"

"We know more precisely what the right one needs."

Mau looked around.

Beautiful valley.

Wrong home.

"That's annoying."

"Often."

They took notes anyway.

Nothing was wasted if it made the next question better.
