<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln02-report -->

The report-back took longer than the scouting trip.

Everyone had opinions.

This was new.

Before G3, a scouting report involved the people who had gone and the people who needed the information.

Now Coco had notes.

Marin cared about light.

Wakana cared about damp because damp destroyed cloth.

Maomao cared about water.

Mikasa cared about routes.

Umaru cared whether the place was farther from town.

Bocchi cared whether there was a room far enough from everyone to play.

Momo cared about whether anyone had seen something interesting.

Rimuru cared that Momo's category was not useful.

The rejected valley became a lesson the whole house absorbed.

No one mourned it.

They had not been looking for a fantasy landscape.

They were looking for somewhere seventeen people—and potentially more—could actually live.

<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln02-work -->

That afternoon, the inn needed firewood.

The future could wait.

Yuta and Mikasa handled heavier pieces.

Okarun and Mau repaired a cart.

Wakana showed Fern a better way to reinforce a torn strap.

Coco followed Maomao until Maomao threatened to assign her work.

Coco asked what work.

Maomao made the mistake of answering.

An hour later Coco was sorting dried plants.

Qifrey passed the doorway.

Stopped.

Smiled.

Kept walking.

Integration did not look like friendship speeches.

It looked like people knowing which shelf to use.

<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln02-kita-mau -->

Kita found Mau alone near the well.

He was cleaning mud from his shoes.

She stood there long enough that he looked up.

"Hi."

"Hi."

Silence.

Mau waited.

Kita realized Bocchi's people had learned how to wait.

That was probably important.

"I want to ask Bocchi something."

"Okay."

Kita stared.

"You're not going to ask what?"

"You'll tell me if you want."

This was frustratingly reasonable.

Kita sat on the low stone edge.

"How do you ask her things without making her panic?"

Mau thought.

"I make her panic."

Kita laughed.

"Great."

"Less than before."

"How?"

Mau rinsed one shoe.

"One question."

Kita waited.

"Then stop."

"Stop what?"

"Talking."

Kita looked at him.

Mau continued.

"She needs time. If you explain the question for her, she starts answering the explanation instead."

Kita thought about every time she had filled Bocchi's silence in the old world.

"Oh."

"Also don't ask in front of everyone."

"I know that."

"Good."

Kita stood.

"Thanks."

Mau nodded.

"Kita."

She looked back.

"Bocchi already wants you here."

Kita's expression softened.

Mau returned to the shoe.

He had not told her what to say.

That was enough help.
