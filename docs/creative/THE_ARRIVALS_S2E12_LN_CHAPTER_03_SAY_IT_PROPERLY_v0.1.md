<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln03-music-room -->

Kita asked in the music room.

Not at the lake.

Not somewhere dramatic.

The music room.

Bocchi was replacing a string.

Kita sat on the floor.

She had rehearsed.

This was a mistake.

The rehearsed version was six sentences long.

Mau had said one question.

Then stop.

Kita hated that he was right.

"Bocchi-chan."

Bocchi looked up.

Kita's heart became impossible.

"Do you want to go out with me?"

Bocchi stared.

Kita stopped talking.

It was the longest silence of her life.

Bocchi's mouth opened.

Closed.

Opened.

Kita kept not talking.

This was torture.

"...Like outside?"

Kita nearly died.

"No."

Bocchi became red.

"Oh."

Kita waited.

Bocchi looked at the guitar.

Then Kita.

Then the guitar.

Then Kita.

"Like..."

"Yes."

More silence.

Kita's soul attempted to leave.

Bocchi whispered:

"I thought we already were."

Kita stared.

Bocchi immediately panicked.

"No, not— I mean not officially. I mean sometimes. I mean I wanted—"

Kita started laughing.

Bocchi stopped.

"Why are you laughing?"

"Because I was terrified."

"You?"

"Yes!"

Bocchi looked genuinely shocked.

That made Kita laugh harder.

Then Bocchi laughed too.

The tension broke.

Kita reached out her hand.

Bocchi looked at it.

Then placed her hand in it.

"Can we say it properly?" Kita asked.

Bocchi swallowed.

Nodded.

Kita squeezed her fingers.

"I like you. Romantically. I want to be your girlfriend."

Bocchi's eyes widened.

The word mattered.

A category.

A choice.

She took several seconds.

Kita waited.

"I like you too."

Kita's chest hurt.

Bocchi continued.

"Romantically."

The second word almost killed both of them.

Kita smiled.

"So?"

Bocchi squeezed her hand.

"Yes."

No one applauded.

No one was hiding outside.

No one needed to be.

<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln03-after -->

They came downstairs holding hands.

Then stopped holding hands because seventeen people were downstairs.

Then started again because letting go for that reason felt worse.

Marin noticed first.

She made a sound.

Kita pointed at her.

"No."

Marin covered her mouth with both hands.

Momo noticed Marin.

Then the hands.

Then Bocchi.

Momo made the same sound.

"No," Kita repeated.

Umaru looked over.

Coco looked over.

Fern smiled.

Mau looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi was red enough to be medically interesting.

He did not tease her.

He simply moved one chair so Kita could sit beside her.

Bocchi noticed.

Looked at him.

Mau nodded once.

Bocchi nodded back.

Frieren watched the exchange.

"What happened?" she asked.

Fern looked at her.

"They're together."

Frieren looked at Bocchi and Kita.

"They were already together."

Kita's face changed.

Mau stared at Frieren.

Fern closed her eyes.

Kita started laughing again.

Bocchi hid behind her hair.

Frieren looked confused.

"What?"

Mau said, "Nothing."

That night, Bocchi and Kita sat together during dinner.

Nothing else changed.

That was exactly the point.

<!-- scene-id: s2e12-ln03-volume-close -->

After everyone went upstairs, the rejected-site map remained on the table.

Someone had marked it with a large X.

Beside it sat new criteria.

Better water.

Better winter light.

More than one viable route.

Room to grow.

Mau passed the table on the way upstairs.

He stopped.

Added one line.

Enough rooms.

Then thought.

Crossed out rooms.

Wrote:

Enough room.

He went upstairs.

Volume 5 ended with the house full and the future still larger than it.
