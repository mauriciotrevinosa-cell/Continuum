# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 1 — The Rumor
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S2E13 — `Maki`

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln01-rumor -->
Maki had been in Continuum for four days when somebody described Yuta badly enough to find him.

Dark hair. Sword. Polite. Stronger than he looked. Lived near a lake with the Otherworlders.

It should not have been enough. It was.

Information here arrived damaged. Distances were measured by carts. Names changed between villages. People remembered weapons better than faces. The word Otherworlder now made some conversations shorter.

Maki asked the questions that mattered. Which road? How long? Was he hurt?

Nobody knew the last one.

She started walking anyway.

Her journey was not epic. She was fast, practical, and uninterested in turning four bad days into legend. She slept when necessary, ate what she could buy, helped free a wagon from mud because leaving it there would block the road, and ignored a merchant who doubled a price after realizing she was foreign.

Near the lake, the rumors improved.

A restored inn. A mixed household. A black-haired swordsman who sometimes took guild work.

That was enough.

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln01-bell -->
At the inn, one perimeter bell rang.

Yuta looked up. Mikasa did too.

Nobody panicked anymore when a bell rang once. That was progress.

They walked outside with Mau.

A woman stood beyond the line.

Glasses. Weapon. Posture Yuta knew before his mind accepted the face.

He stopped.

Maki stopped too.

Mau looked between them and stepped away.

Yuta said her name.

"Maki."

Maki exhaled.

"You're younger."

Yuta laughed once. Then the laugh broke.

Maki crossed the distance.

They did not kiss first.

They held each other like people verifying weight. Existence. Heat.

Yuta's hands shook.

"You look terrible," Maki said.

"You look exactly the same."

"Wrong answer."

"I know."

Mau and Mikasa looked away.

The reunion did not need witnesses.
