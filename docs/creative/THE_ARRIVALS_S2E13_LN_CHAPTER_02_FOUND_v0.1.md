# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 2 — Found
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S2E13 — `Maki`

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln02-private -->
Maki learned the impossible parts from Yuta before she learned where the towels were.

They sat outside until sunset.

He told her enough: his younger body, the years he remembered, the people they had lost, the fact that he had already grieved her.

Maki listened.

When he finished, she touched his face.

"You really are you."

Yuta closed his eyes.

"I hoped so."

"Idiot."

"Yeah."

They had already lived a lifetime. Continuum had not reset them to teenagers meeting again. It had given two people who had reached an ending more time.

Neither knew what to do with that yet.

So Maki asked what time dinner was.

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln02-house -->
The wider introduction happened the next morning because seventeen people were too many to process after dark.

Maki met them in waves.

Fern first. Mikasa again without a perimeter between them. Momo and Anko. Maomao while Maomao was measuring something and refused to stop. Bocchi and Kita together. Coco asked a question. Qifrey apologized. Umaru asked whether Maki liked games. Marin asked about clothes. Wakana noticed road damage before Marin finished.

Maki looked at Yuta.

"This is your life now?"

"Apparently."

Mau entered carrying bread.

Frieren followed wearing the navy polo that had once arrived in Mau's backpack.

Maki looked at the shirt. Then Mau. Then Frieren.

Mau sighed.

"Yes, it's mine."

"Didn't ask."

"You were going to."

"I wasn't."

Five minutes later Maki and Frieren were arguing about whether Yuta overworked.

Yuta left the room.

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln02-night -->
That night Maki woke because Yuta was not beside her.

She found him checking the front latch.

"You do this every night?"

"No."

Maki stared.

"Not every night."

"Better."

They sat.

No reunion speech. No promise.

Yuta put his hand on the table.

Maki put hers over it.

The house creaked around them. Someone upstairs complained in their sleep. Rimuru, in slime form, rolled off a chair somewhere in the dark.

Maki looked toward the sound.

Yuta said, "You'll get used to it."

"That's what I'm afraid of."

She stayed.
