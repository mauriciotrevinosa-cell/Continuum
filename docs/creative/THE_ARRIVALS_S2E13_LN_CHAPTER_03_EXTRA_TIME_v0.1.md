<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln03-routine -->
Maki's first week at the inn was not dramatic.

That was the gift.

She learned Yuta now helped with breakfast. Mau and Okarun could spend an hour repairing something that should have been discarded. Mikasa took patrol too seriously until somebody tricked her into a day off. Frieren could read through arguments, music and construction until Fern physically removed the book.

Yuta learned Maki was real by bumping into her in doorways.

They did not cling constantly. They did not need to.

At breakfast, Maki passed him something before he asked. Yuta moved a cup before she reached for it.

A lifetime refusing to disappear just because their bodies had changed.

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln03-frieren -->
Frieren and Maki ended up preparing vegetables together because everyone else escaped.

"This is not preparing," Frieren said.

"Cooking."

"Fern usually does this."

"Yuta usually does this badly."

From the other room:

"I can hear you."

Maki called back, "Good."

Frieren almost smiled.

Maki noticed the McLaren hoodie hanging over a chair.

"You wear his clothes."

"Yes."

"Why?"

"They're comfortable."

"That's it?"

Frieren looked at her.

"No."

Maki waited.

Frieren did not elaborate.

Maki nodded. Enough for today.

<!-- scene-id: s2e13-ln03-mau -->
Later Mau asked Maki, "Does it feel strange?"

"What?"

"Yuta. He looks different from the person you spent most of your life with."

Maki looked across the yard.

"Yes."

Mau waited.

"It also doesn't matter."

"Why?"

"Because I'm not trying to prove he matches a photograph in my head. I know how he avoids a question. How he gets quiet when he's afraid. How he thinks being useful counts as rest."

Yuta shouted from across the yard.

"I can hear this one too."

Maki ignored him.

"The body matters. History matters. But choosing someone isn't an archaeological test."

Mau absorbed that.

"Okay."

"You asking for a reason?"

"I don't know."

"Good answer."

It annoyed him that everyone said that.

He remembered it anyway.
