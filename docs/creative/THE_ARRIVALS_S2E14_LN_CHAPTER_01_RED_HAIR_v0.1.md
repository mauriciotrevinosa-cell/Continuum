# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 4 — Red Hair
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 4  
**Source episode:** S2E14 — `Hamburg Steak`

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln01-arrival -->
Stark arrived on a day nobody expected another person.

Mau and Fern were outside splitting kindling badly.

Fern was doing it correctly.

Mau was learning.

Frieren sat nearby reading and contributing criticism.

"Too shallow."

"You aren't helping."

"I am."

"No."

A bell rang.

Then another.

Rimuru appeared at the door.

"North line."

Yuta and Maki were already moving.

A young man with red hair stood beyond the perimeter, dirty, tired and staring at the inn like it had personally offended him by existing.

Fern stopped.

The axe slipped.

Frieren's book closed.

Stark saw them.

"Fern?"

Fern reached him first.

She hugged him hard enough to make him step back.

"Ow."

"Idiot."

"Ow."

Frieren reached them more slowly.

Stark looked at her over Fern's shoulder.

"Frieren."

Frieren stared, then touched his arm.

Real.

"You're late," she said.

Stark laughed and cried at once.

"So are you."

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln01-mau -->
Mau stayed back because this belonged to them first.

Maki stood beside him.

"You okay?"

"Yes."

"Sure?"

"Someone important to Frieren came back."

"Not an answer."

Mau watched Fern refuse to let go.

"I'm happy."

Maki believed him.

Later Stark met Mau.

Frieren was wearing Mau's cap. Mau held Frieren's book because she had handed it to him while helping Fern.

Stark pointed.

"Is that yours?"

Mau said, "Originally."

Frieren said, "Currently."

Stark looked at Fern.

Fern said, "Don't ask."

Stark nodded.

He understood the house faster than most.
