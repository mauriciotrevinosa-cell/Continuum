# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 5 — Hamburg Steak
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Source episode:** S2E14 — `Hamburg Steak`

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln02-dinner -->
Stark asked for hamburg steak as if saying it aloud could make the old world appear.

Fern looked at him.

Frieren looked at him.

Mau looked at the ingredients.

"We can approximate."

Stark brightened.

Maomao said, "That word is doing dangerous work."

Dinner became a project.

Wakana handled knives. Rimuru handled proportions. Mau handled whatever people handed him. Fern corrected seasoning.

Frieren was assigned one task.

She abandoned it to read.

Stark found her.

"You haven't changed."

Frieren looked up.

"I changed clothes."

"That isn't what I meant."

"I know."

He sat nearby.

Old family did not require constant narration.

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln02-table -->
The kitchen was too crowded, so dinner split across two connected tables.

Yuta and Maki sat together without noticing they were doing it.

Stark sat between Fern and Frieren.

Mau moved between both because he forgot to sit.

Frieren caught his sleeve on the third pass.

"Sit."

"I'm getting—"

"Sit."

He sat beside her.

Stark noticed the ease.

Mau moved Frieren's cup away from an elbow. Frieren put food on Mau's plate because he forgot to serve himself. Fern treated all of this as ordinary.

Stark looked at Fern.

Fern's face said do not start.

He did not.

The hamburg steak was not exact.

Stark ate it anyway.

His eyes watered.

Momo noticed.

"Too salty?"

"No."

Maomao tasted it.

"It is slightly salty."

"That's not why."

Nobody asked him to explain.

Something from Stark's old life had returned imperfectly enough to belong here.
