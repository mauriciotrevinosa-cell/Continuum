# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 6 — Old Family, New House
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Source episode:** S2E14 — `Hamburg Steak`

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln03-morning -->
Stark woke early.

The house was already awake.

Fern had tea. Frieren had not moved. Mau and Yuta carried something outside. Bocchi and Kita quietly shared breakfast. Umaru was not awake in any meaningful sense.

A plate appeared in front of Stark.

He looked away.

When he looked back, half the food was gone.

He froze.

Momo sat across from him.

"Problem?"

"My food."

"What about it?"

"There was more."

Momo looked innocent.

Stark pulled the plate closer.

Adaptation.

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln03-starkmau -->
Stark and Mau were awkward for exactly one afternoon.

They worked on a wall.

Stark pretended a beam was heavy.

Mau stared.

"You can just carry it."

Stark stopped pretending.

"Right."

He lifted it alone.

The problem was gone.

They talked while working.

Not about Frieren.

Repairs. Being useful. The way everyone assigned you a job if you stood still.

Stark looked toward the inn.

"She seems happy."

"Yes."

"You're not bothered I'm here?"

Mau looked at him.

"Why would I be?"

Stark had no good answer.

Mau added, "She missed people she couldn't reach. I'm glad you're here."

No rivalry. No ownership.

Stark nodded.

"Me too."

<!-- scene-id: s2e14-ln03-night -->
That evening Frieren, Fern and Stark sat together long after dinner.

Mau worked nearby, close enough to belong, far enough not to occupy their past.

Frieren laughed at something Stark said.

The sound was different from her laugh with Mau.

Not better.

Older.

It belonged to a history he had not lived.

He liked hearing it.

Later Frieren came to bed.

"Stark thinks you're strange."

"Fair."

"He likes you."

"Concerning."

She lay down.

Mau closed the book.

"Good night?"

Frieren thought.

"Yes."

"Good."

Old family had returned.

Nothing in the new family had to disappear for that to be true.
