# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 7 — The Ball
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Source episode:** S2E15 — `New Routines`

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln01-origin -->
The baseball game began because Momo found something round enough to call a ball.

This was a poor reason.

Marin made it worse by asking whether anyone knew baseball.

Umaru did.

Kita did.

Okarun knew enough.

Mau knew rules without knowing why, which had stopped surprising people months ago.

Stark had never played.

Frieren said she had no interest.

Maki said she would win.

Yuta sighed before there were teams.

"What?"

"I know that voice."

"What voice?"

"The one right before something becomes competitive."

"You married it."

Yuta stopped.

Maki smiled.

The game became inevitable.

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln01-rules -->
Rules took longer than equipment.

No magic.

No cursed-energy reinforcement.

No psychic interference.

No transformation.

No Turbo Granny.

No slime advantages.

Maki raised a hand.

"My physical ability isn't cursed energy."

Everyone stared.

Stark raised his hand too.

"I'm just strong."

Rimuru looked at both of them.

Momo said, "This is going to be incredible."

The first ball lasted one hit.

Stark connected.

The ball vanished into the forest.

Silence.

Stark lowered the improvised bat.

"I thought I was being careful."

Fern said, "You weren't."

Wakana went inside and returned with cloth.

"Give me ten minutes."

The second ball survived three hits.

The third involved Qifrey.

By the fourth, nobody could explain what material the ball technically was.

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln01-frieren -->
Frieren read from a chair near the field.

A ball came toward her.

Without looking up, she moved one finger.

The ball changed direction and dropped into her hand.

Everyone shouted.

Frieren looked up.

"What?"

"Magic!" Momo yelled.

"I'm not playing."

Maki folded her arms.

"Then stop interfering."

"It was going to hit my book."

"Then play."

Frieren looked at Maki.

Then closed the book.

Mau leaned toward Yuta.

"This is going to get worse."

"Yes."

They were right.
