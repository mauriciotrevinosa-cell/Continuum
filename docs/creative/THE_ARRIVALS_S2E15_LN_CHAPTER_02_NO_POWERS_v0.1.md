<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln02-teams -->
The teams were mixed badly enough that nobody could accuse the house of respecting source groups.

Maki and Frieren ended up opposite each other.

Yuta called this dangerous.

Mau and Okarun argued about a rule.

Coco changed sides twice.

Mikasa treated the field like a tactical map until Marin told her she was making baseball frightening.

"I am trying to win."

"Why?"

Mikasa stopped.

Nobody had asked her that about an objective in a long time.

She looked at the bases.

"I don't know."

"Perfect."

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln02-bocchi -->
Bocchi struck the ball cleanly by accident.

Everyone cheered.

Bocchi froze halfway to first.

Kita screamed louder.

"Bocchi-chan! Run!"

Bocchi remembered movement.

Too late.

Out.

Kita celebrated anyway.

Bocchi returned red-faced.

"We lost."

"You hit it!"

"That is not the same."

"It is to me."

Bocchi hid behind her hair.

She was smiling.

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln02-mikasa -->
Maki caught something she should not have been able to catch.

Rimuru objected.

"No enhancement!"

"There wasn't any."

"That is the problem."

Stark threw too hard again.

Fern took the ball away.

Frieren used no magic for almost twenty minutes.

Then Maki hit toward her.

Frieren caught it cleanly.

Maki narrowed her eyes.

"No magic?"

"No."

"Good."

They smiled.

Not a feelings conversation.

Better.

By late afternoon nobody knew the score.

Umaru claimed her team was winning.

Nobody knew which team she meant.

Mikasa missed an easy catch.

The ball rolled through grass.

She stared at it.

Then laughed.

No one died because she missed.

No mission collapsed.

Marin ran past her after the ball.

Mikasa ran too.
