They returned tired, hungry, and still arguing about rules nobody intended to use again.

Maki carried a broken bat.

Stark carried two replacement balls because everyone had stopped trusting him with throwing.

Frieren had reopened her book before reaching the door.

G3 residents explained where everything went without being asked.

That was the actual change.

Kita showed Stark the tool shelf.

Wakana gave Maki a place for damaged clothing.

Umaru told both of them which chair not to take because Maomao became territorial.

Maomao denied this.

Everyone ignored her.

Maki did not feel like Yuta's guest.

Stark did not feel like Fern and Frieren's guest.

They had jobs.

Chairs.

People who complained when they did something wrong.

That was residence.

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln03-dinner -->
At dinner, the game continued through accusation.

"Frieren cheated."

"I did not."

"You redirected the first ball."

"I wasn't playing."

"That is worse."

Maki laughed.

Frieren looked annoyed.

Mau sat between arguments eating quietly.

Stark looked down at his plate.

Full.

He blinked.

Looked away.

Looked back.

One piece was gone.

He slowly turned toward Momo.

Momo was speaking to Marin.

Toward Fern.

Eating normally.

Toward Frieren.

Reading.

Stark pulled the plate closer.

No one explained.

The house had absorbed him enough to become dangerous.

<!-- scene-id: s2e15-ln03-home -->
Later, Maki volunteered for a short inspection route the next morning.

Yuta said he would go.

Maki said he had another job.

"I can do both."

"No."

Yuta looked at her.

That tone again.

He surrendered.

Maki looked toward the sleeping inn.

A week ago, finding people on a road meant finding strangers.

Now if she found someone lost, she knew where she would bring them.

Not where Yuta was.

Home.
