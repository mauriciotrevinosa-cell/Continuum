# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 10 — Inspection Route
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S2E16 — `Four New Faces`

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln01-route -->
Maki found four girls because one of them was arguing loudly enough to carry through the trees.

"How was I supposed to know the road ended?"

"It did not end! You left it!"

"Same difference!"

"It is not remotely the same difference!"

Maki followed the voices.

Four young women stood near a shallow ravine.

Hakari looked tired but composed.

Karane looked angry enough to substitute for fire.

Shizuka held a book to her chest.

Nano studied the terrain like it had failed an examination.

They all turned.

Maki looked at their clothing.

Their confusion.

The absence of local gear.

"Otherworlders."

Karane's expression sharpened.

"You too?"

"Yeah."

Nano asked, "Do you know where we are?"

"Approximately."

"Do you know a safe location?"

Maki thought of the inn.

Too many beds.

Rimuru measuring walls.

Momo cheating.

Yuta asleep with his sword finally farther away.

"Yes."

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln01-return -->
The walk back took longer because fresh Arrivals asked fresh questions.

Do phones work?

No.

Is there a government?

Several.

Are they friendly?

Depends.

Can we go home?

Nobody knows.

Are there monsters?

Yes.

Karane stopped.

"What kind of answer is that?"

"The honest kind."

Shizuka wrote something in the margin of her book.

Hakari asked if the place was safe.

Maki answered more carefully.

"Safer than being alone."

Nano accepted the precision.

When the inn appeared, all four slowed.

Laundry.

Repairs.

Perimeter lines.

Smoke.

Sound.

Life.

Maki looked at them.

"Welcome to the problem."

Then she rang the bell herself so nobody came out armed.
