G3 handled the arrival.

That was new.

Kita brought water.

Wakana found spare clothes.

Umaru explained the no-internet catastrophe with the solemnity of a survivor.

Coco explained too much.

Qifrey corrected Coco.

Marin immediately liked Hakari.

Karane immediately distrusted how much Marin liked Hakari.

Momo appeared and made the equation worse.

Rimuru watched from the table.

Mau leaned toward him.

"We don't have to do anything."

Rimuru looked emotional.

"Don't say it too loudly."

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln02-different -->
The four girls did not react the same.

Hakari adapted socially first. She watched relationships and understood almost immediately that the house contained enough romantic confusion to sustain agriculture.

Karane distrusted anything that looked too easy.

Shizuka found the quietest corner and was eventually joined by Bocchi, who understood sitting near someone without forcing speech.

Nano wanted systems.

Rooms. Rules. Food. Water. Why the perimeter looked improvised.

Rimuru liked her immediately.

Momo called this alarming.

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln02-night -->
Tech loss hit hardest at night.

During the day there was too much happening.

At night, screens became mirrors.

No messages.

No contacts.

No normal world beyond stored data.

Umaru found Nano staring at a dead signal icon.

"Don't."

Nano looked over.

"It is inefficient to repeatedly verify an unchanged state."

"Correct."

"You still check."

"Correct."

Nano put the device down.

Umaru sat beside her.

No speech about loss.

She showed Nano where people kept snacks.

Across the room Shizuka read. Bocchi played quietly. Karane argued with Hakari about a blanket. Marin inserted herself.

The house had four more voices.

It adjusted.
