# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 12 — First Days
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S2E16 — `Four New Faces`

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln03-days -->
First days were measured by mistakes.

Hakari used the wrong storage shelf.

Karane opened a door that stuck and assumed it was locked.

Shizuka learned which room was quiet only at certain hours.

Nano reorganized tools and earned a five-minute lecture from Mau because the old arrangement had a logic nobody had written down.

She asked him to write it down.

He did.

The tool shelf improved.

This annoyed him.

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln03-clothes -->
Wakana became essential again.

Four fresh arrivals meant four wardrobes poorly suited to weather, work, and washing without old-world replacements.

He did not redesign them overnight.

He measured.

Patched.

Adjusted.

Asked what each person wanted.

Hakari had opinions.

Karane had more opinions about Hakari's opinions.

Nano cared about function.

Shizuka cared whether she could move and read comfortably.

Frieren watched from a chair.

Mau's McLaren hoodie was back on her.

Wakana glanced at it.

"That repair held."

Mau looked over.

"Your repair."

Frieren said, "My hoodie."

Mau stared.

The newcomers watched.

Karane whispered, "What's happening?"

Umaru said, "Don't ask. This is normal."

Mau heard.

Frieren looked pleased.

The callback had become household culture.

<!-- scene-id: s2e16-ln03-dream -->
That night, Mau dreamed a cup broke.

Nothing else.

He woke.

Remembered the exact angle.

The exact sound.

Frieren slept beside him.

Mau looked at the cup on the table.

Same cup.

He almost laughed.

Dreams were allowed to be stupid.

Three days later, Momo knocked it from the table.

It broke at the exact angle.

Mau stared.

Momo stared.

"Why do you look offended?"

"No reason."

He told himself coincidence.

That was the first one.
