Hakari became dangerous at dinner.

Karane learned where everyone hid from Hakari.

Shizuka and Bocchi developed conversations made mostly of books, guitar and silence.

Momo resisted.

Progress continued.

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln01-dream2 -->
Mau dreamed it would rain during an outing.

The sky in the dream had been clear until noon.

He remembered Yuta complaining about wet sleeves.

He said nothing that morning because one coincidence was still a coincidence.

At noon it rained.

Yuta returned wet and irritated.

Mau stopped smiling.

Frieren noticed.

"What?"

"I dreamed that."

"Before?"

"Yes."

"Tell Rimuru."

Mau hesitated.

Then nodded.

He did.

No secrecy.

No conclusion.

Rimuru wrote it down.

One dream was coincidence.

Two were data.

Frieren carried one branch and a book.

"You could help."

"I am."

"You're holding one branch."

"It counts."

Then noticed Frieren looking toward the inn.

Toward Mau.

Mau was helping Coco hang a line.

"You do that a lot."

"Do what?"

"Check where he is."

Frieren looked at Maki.

Maki kept walking.

No diagnosis.

No conclusion.

Just observation.
