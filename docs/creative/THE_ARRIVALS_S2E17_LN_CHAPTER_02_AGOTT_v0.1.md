# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 14 — Agott
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S2E17 — `Settling In / Agott`

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln02-search -->
Agott arrived looking for Qifrey and Coco.

That made her different from almost every other Arrival.

She had a destination before she had a home.

Rumors brought her close.

Magic brought her closer.

The perimeter bell brought everyone else outside.

Coco saw her.

"Agott!"

Coco ran.

Agott caught her badly because she had not prepared to be hit by a child at speed.

Qifrey reached them second.

For several minutes, the household ceased to matter.

That was correct.

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln02-changed -->
Later, Agott began noticing what had changed.

Coco knew where things were.

Coco had chores.

Coco asked Mau for help with a shelf without asking Qifrey first.

Then argued with Mau.

Mau argued back.

Neither treated Qifrey as mediator.

Agott watched.

Not jealous.

Protective in the way people become protective of histories.

"You trust him," she said later.

Coco looked toward Mau.

"Yes."

"Why?"

Coco thought.

"He answers when he doesn't know."

Agott frowned.

"That's your reason?"

"It's one."

Agott stored the answer.

She built her own opinion more slowly.

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln02-contract -->
Agott's first real disagreement with the household was about the Contract.

She did not like magic used to enforce speech boundaries.

Qifrey agreed enough to make the argument uncomfortable.

Rimuru explained scope.

Fern explained practice.

Mau said, "You don't have to accept protected information."

Agott looked at him.

"And if I refuse?"

"You still live here."

That answer changed the room.

No coerced belonging.

Agott did not sign that night.

Nobody punished her.

Two days later, after understanding the terms, she chose to.

That mattered more.
