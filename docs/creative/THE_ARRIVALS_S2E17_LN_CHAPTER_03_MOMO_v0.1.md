# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 15 — Momo
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S2E17 — `Settling In / Agott`

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln03-trigger -->
Okarun remembered Momo because she called him stupid.

Not during danger.

They were carrying water.

Okarun took the heavier container.

Momo said he was doing it wrong.

He said there was no wrong way to carry water.

"That's exactly the kind of stupid thing you'd say, Okarun."

Something moved.

Not a vision.

A shape inside a locked room.

Her voice.

A hallway.

His own embarrassment.

A name spoken differently.

Okarun stopped.

Water spilled.

Momo turned.

"What?"

He looked at her.

"Momo."

She froze.

He had used the name before because she had told him.

This sounded different.

"What did you call me?"

"Momo."

She dropped the container.

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln03-pieces -->
The memory did not return as a movie.

It returned as habits.

The way she tilted her head before arguing.

The rhythm of her footsteps.

An expression he knew he had seen a thousand times without knowing where.

He remembered being afraid beside her.

Being annoyed.

Being grateful.

A hand.

A hallway.

A fight.

Nothing complete.

Momo cried anyway.

Okarun apologized.

Momo hit his arm.

"Stop apologizing."

He laughed.

That laugh brought another fragment.

Momo laughed too.

Anko found them sitting on the floor beside spilled water.

She understood and sat down.

No one rushed to retrieve the whole story.

Recognition could return in pieces.

<!-- scene-id: s2e17-ln03-natural -->
That evening Momo entered the common room.

Okarun looked up.

"Momo."

Natural.

Momo stopped.

Smiled.

Kept walking.

The problem was not solved.

The distance had changed.
