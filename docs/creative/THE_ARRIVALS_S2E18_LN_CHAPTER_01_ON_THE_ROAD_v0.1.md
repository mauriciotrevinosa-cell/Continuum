# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 16 — On the Road
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S2E18 — `Himmel`

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln01-himmel -->
Himmel heard about the Otherworlder settlement from three people who disagreed about everything else.

One said it was dangerous.

One said it was kind.

One said there were too many strange people living in an old inn and they bought unreasonable amounts of lamp oil.

Himmel believed the third.

He helped a farmer repair a wheel on the way.

Refused payment.

Asked for directions.

He helped a child retrieve something from a tree.

Asked again.

He slept in a village where the innkeeper warned him not to go near the lake settlement.

"Why?"

"Otherworlders."

Himmel looked at himself.

"That may be a problem."

The innkeeper stared.

Himmel smiled.

The next morning he continued.

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln01-house -->
At the inn, life continued.

Maki and Yuta argued about training.

Stark and Fern argued about whether he had broken something.

Frieren read.

Mau dreamed small things.

A phrase.

A plate in the wrong seat.

A scratch on Okarun's hand.

Then they happened.

The household began keeping a notebook.

Nobody called it prophecy.

Mau especially did not.

The dreams were mundane enough to be insulting.

Then one night they stopped being mundane.
