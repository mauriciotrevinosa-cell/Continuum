# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 17 — First Warning
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S2E18 — `Himmel`

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln02-dream -->
Mau stood somewhere he had never been.

The ground looked prepared.

Not naturally flat.

Chosen.

Blood on stone.

A wheel turning where no wheel should exist.

A shape behind it.

Meguna.

Mau did not know why he knew that face belonged to Sukuna.

He did.

Frieren moved somewhere beyond him.

Mau tried to reach her.

The dream cut.

He woke hard enough to wake Frieren.

"Mau?"

He stared at the dark.

His heart refused to understand the room was safe.

Frieren touched his shoulder.

"Sukuna."

Her hand tightened.

This time Mau got up.

They woke Yuta and Rimuru.

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln02-tell -->
Yuta listened.

His face changed at the name.

The presence from the beginning of the season returned to him.

"I felt him."

Rimuru looked at him.

"When?"

"Right after the city."

Yuta explained the moment.

Too brief.

No location.

Gone immediately.

He had not told the house because he had not trusted the evidence.

Mau understood uncertainty too well to accuse him.

Rimuru wrote everything.

Meguna.

Prepared terrain.

Frieren.

A wheel.

No causal order.

No complete fight.

No fatal branch.

Not yet.

"We tell everyone?" Mau asked.

Rimuru thought.

"Enough to update the threat. Not a nightmare we don't understand."

Mau nodded.

Shared.

Not solved.

Better.

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln02-road -->
The next day Himmel reached the final road.

Smoke rose above the trees.

A roof.

Movement.

He smiled.

He had no idea who waited there.

That was about to change.
