# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 6
## Chapter 18 — Himmel
**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Volume:** 6  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Source episode:** S2E18 — `Himmel`

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln03-bell -->
The bell rang at noon.

Stark was closest to the door.

Fern followed.

Frieren looked up.

Mau saw her expression before he saw the road.

Himmel stood beyond the line.

For once, nobody moved quickly.

Recognition was too large.

Stark whispered his name.

Fern covered her mouth.

Frieren stood.

Himmel smiled.

"Hello, Frieren."

The book fell.

Frieren walked.

Then faster.

Then ran.

Himmel opened his arms.

She reached him.

Held him.

The world allowed an old grief to become a person again.

Fern arrived next.

Stark.

No one said the right thing.

There was no right thing.

Himmel cried.

Stark cried harder and denied it.

Fern tried not to and failed.

Frieren held on longer than Himmel expected.

Mau stayed near the inn.

Maki stood beside him again.

This time she did not ask if he was okay.

She knew he was.

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln03-newlife -->
Himmel entered Frieren's life as it existed now.

Not as it had waited.

Fern older in ways that had nothing to do with age.

Stark at home in a house he had never seen.

Maki beside Yuta.

G3 acting like residents.

Four girls arguing in the kitchen.

Agott with Qifrey and Coco.

Bocchi and Kita holding hands under the table.

Mau bringing Frieren tea without asking.

Frieren taking it without looking.

Himmel noticed that last one.

He noticed the hoodie too.

"That's not yours."

Frieren looked down.

"It is Mau's."

Mau said, "Originally."

Himmel looked at him.

Mau looked back.

Himmel smiled.

"I've heard enough strange things today. I'll save that one."

At dinner Himmel sat between Stark and Fern.

Frieren sat across.

Mau beside her.

Nobody adjudicated history.

The old party was not restored.

Something larger had made room for it.

<!-- scene-id: s2e18-ln03-close -->
Late that night Mau stood outside alone.

The Sukuna dream waited in his mind.

Inside, Himmel laughed at something Stark said.

Frieren answered.

A person from her past had returned.

A threat from Yuta's past had returned.

Continuum kept giving back what people had lost.

It did not distinguish between gifts and disasters.
