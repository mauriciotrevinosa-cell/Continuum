# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 2 — An Old Question

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S2E19 — `Ghosts and Choices`

Himmel asked Frieren outside, close enough that the inn remained visible.

"Can I ask you something selfish?"

"You usually do."

Himmel laughed.

"If things had been different—if I had understood you better, if you had understood me sooner—do you think there could have been a future for us?"

Frieren did not answer immediately.

Himmel did not rescue her from the silence.

"I loved you," she said.

"I know."

"I still do."

"I know."

She frowned. The sentence had not solved anything.

"That's not exactly what I asked."

"No."

Frieren looked toward the inn.

Mau was visible through a window, carrying something with Okarun.

"I don't know."

Himmel followed her gaze.

That was an answer too.

He did not ask her to choose.

He said, "Then you should find out."

Later, Frieren found Maki sharpening a blade.

"Himmel asked if we could have had a romantic future."

"What did you say?"

"I don't know."

"Good."

Frieren looked irritated. "Why does everyone say that?"

"Because it's better than lying."

"I loved him."

"Okay."

"I love Mau."

"Okay."

"Those feel different."

"Probably because they are."

"How?"

Maki set the blade down.

"You're asking me to name your feelings."

"Yes."

"No."

Frieren stared.

Maki stood.

"You get to do that part."

Not helpful.

Exactly helpful enough.
