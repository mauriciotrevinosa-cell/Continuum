# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 4 — Frieren Does Not Die

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S2E19 — `Ghosts and Choices`

Himmel found Mau awake before dawn.

"You sleep?"

"Sometimes."

"Bad dreams?"

Mau looked at him.

Instead of explaining, he asked, "If Frieren wanted someone else, would you still stay?"

Himmel looked toward the dark inn.

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because my love for her isn't a contract she has to satisfy."

Mau absorbed that.

Himmel smiled faintly.

"Also because leaving every time life hurts would make me a terrible hero."

Later, Frieren and Mau walked the lake path.

Frieren talked about Himmel.

Not confession.

History.

Things she had understood too late.

Things she still treasured.

Mau listened.

He never asked her to make Himmel smaller so he could feel larger.

At the dock, Frieren leaned against him.

"Do you want him here?" Mau asked.

"Yes."

"Good."

"That doesn't bother you?"

"No."

"Why?"

Mau thought.

"Because you look happy."

Frieren's chest hurt.

Different from Himmel.

Again.

She still could not name it.

That night the battlefield dream returned twice.

Mau woke the second time and did not go back to bed.

He dressed quietly.

Frieren moved but did not wake.

Mau almost told her.

Did not.

He hated that.

Yuta was awake in the common room.

Rimuru was reviewing supplies.

Both looked up.

Mau closed the door.

"I need to tell you something."

Yuta saw his face.

Rimuru put the papers down.

Mau sat.

"Frieren does not die."

Neither understood.

Not yet.

Mau began from the first dream.
