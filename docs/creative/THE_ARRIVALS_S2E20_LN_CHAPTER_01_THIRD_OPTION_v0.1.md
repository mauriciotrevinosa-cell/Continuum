# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 5 — Third Option

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S2E20 — `Third Option`

Mau told them everything.

The harmless predictions.

The first image of Sukuna.

The repeated battlefield.

The two endings.

Yuta interrupted only once.

"Describe him again."

Mau did.

Meguna.

Yuta went still.

"The presence I felt."

Rimuru looked between them.

Yuta explained the instant from the season's beginning.

For the first time the two pieces became one thing.

Sukuna was here.

The table stopped feeling like a table.

It became a battlefield months early.

Mau finished.

"If I don't move, Frieren dies."

"No," Yuta said.

Mau looked at him.

"No to which part?"

"Your conclusion."

"The dreams—"

"I heard them."

Rimuru leaned forward.

"We accept the information. We reject the binary."

Mau's face tightened.

"How?"

"We find a third option."

The answer irritated Mau because it sounded like optimism.

Then Yuta began reconstructing Shinjuku from memory.

Sukuna's body.

Ten Shadows.

Mahoraga.

What he remembered about adaptation.

What he did not know.

Rimuru asked questions.

Raphael refined probabilities without pretending certainty.

They talked until sunrise.

No miracle appeared.

But the problem became something three people could work on instead of one person could obey.
