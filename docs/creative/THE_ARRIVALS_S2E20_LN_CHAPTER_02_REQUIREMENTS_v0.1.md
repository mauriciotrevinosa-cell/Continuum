# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 6 — Requirements

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S2E20 — `Third Option`

Construction refused to become convenient.

Mau could not simply imagine an attack and make it real.

That would have solved too much.

They tested knowledge.

Structure.

Cost.

Similarity.

What Mau understood versus what he merely remembered as information.

Yuta explained cursed techniques until his voice went dry.

Rimuru translated concepts into other frameworks.

Mau attempted to build.

Failed.

Again.

Failed differently.

Then, for less than a second, blue distortion formed around Mau's hand.

It collapsed.

Mau stared.

Yuta stared harder.

"Do that again."

"I can't."

"Of course."

The path existed.

Not mastery.

A pathway.

One-use at best.

Dangerous.

Incomplete.

The second component was worse.

Red required another set of conditions Mau did not yet satisfy.

And neither mattered unless they could force convergence.

"Hollow Purple," Yuta said quietly.

Mau knew the name.

Not from memory.

From Yuta.

The fact that he now understood enough to attempt it frightened him.

Construction updated the list.

Not words exactly.

Requirements becoming possible.

Output.

Control.

Opposition.

Convergence.

A final warning-shaped absence where survivability should have been.

Mau understood something Yuta had not meant to teach him:

every minute of cursed-energy control practice could move the list closer to completion.

He did not say that part aloud.

Yuta believed the training was making Mau more likely to survive.

That was true.

It was also helping Mau build the thing most likely to kill him if the predicted moment arrived.

Rimuru said, "Even if you construct it, we do not assume you survive using it."

Mau looked at the table.

Yuta hit it with his palm.

"No."

Mau looked up.

"We are not designing a prettier version of your dream."

Silence.

Rimuru nodded.

"Third option."

They kept working.
