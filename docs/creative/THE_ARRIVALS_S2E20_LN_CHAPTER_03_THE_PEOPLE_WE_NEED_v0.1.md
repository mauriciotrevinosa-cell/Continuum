# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 7 — The People We Need

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S2E20 — `Third Option`

By noon, the third option had names.

Qifrey.

Okarun.

Not because they were strongest.

Because the plan needed geometry and speed.

Qifrey received only what he needed: a predicted location, a dangerous opponent, a spatial trigger that had to be controlled within fractions of seconds.

He listened without interrupting.

When they finished, he asked, "And what are you not telling me?"

Mau looked at Yuta.

Yuta looked at Rimuru.

Qifrey sighed.

"Good. At least you know there is something you're not telling me."

He agreed to help anyway.

Okarun's part was simpler to describe and harder to execute.

"Get someone out," Yuta said.

"Who?"

"We may not know until it happens."

"How fast?"

"As fast as you can be."

Okarun looked at Mau.

"This feels like one of those plans where the answer gets worse if I keep asking."

"Yes."

"Okay."

Mau hated how easily he said yes.

Later Maki found Yuta.

"What are you doing?"

"Training."

"Wrong answer."

Yuta tried another.

Maki waited him out.

Eventually she learned the bounded truth: Sukuna, approximate timing, a plan that depended on specific positions.

Not the fatal binary.

Not Mau's expected outcome.

Maki's expression hardened.

"And Frieren?"

"Doesn't know."

"Why?"

Mau answered.

"Because if she changes the predicted geometry too early, we lose the only advantage we have."

Maki looked at him for a long time.

"I don't like this."

"Neither do I."

"Good."

She agreed to help keep the plan from collapsing.

Not to imprison Frieren.

Not to decide for her.

Only to buy the others the chance to try.

That night, the household ate dinner around them.

No one realized five people had begun rehearsing a future the rest of the house did not know existed.
