# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 8 — One Week

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S2E21 — `One Week`

The week did not look like preparation for disaster.

That was the problem.

Breakfast still happened.

Laundry still failed to dry when it rained.

Stark still guarded his plate.

Hakari and Karane still argued.

Bocchi still practiced.

Ori did not exist in their lives yet.

The future-home map still occupied one end of the table.

Between ordinary things, Mau trained with Yuta.

Yuta believed he was teaching Mau cursed-energy control to improve his survival odds.

That was true.

It was not the whole truth.

Mau was also learning the shape required for Blue.

The first successful attempt lasted less than a second.

It tore skin across his palm.

Maomao saw the bandage at dinner.

"What happened?"

"Training."

"With what?"

Mau looked at Yuta.

Yuta said, "Bad judgment."

Maomao accepted this because it described both of them.

Frieren did not.

She watched Mau's hand through the entire meal.

That night she took it and inspected the injury herself.

"You've been doing too much."

"Probably."

"Stop."

"I can't."

Frieren looked at him.

Mau almost told her.

Instead he said, "I need a few days."

Frieren's expression changed.

Not agreement.

Trust offered despite discomfort.

"A few."

Mau nodded.

The lie was not in the words.

It was in what he did not say.
