# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 9 — Training and Breakfast

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S2E21 — `One Week`

Yuta practiced RCT until Maki forced food into his hands.

Rimuru tested barriers until Nano asked why the same section of ground had been measured seven times.

Qifrey disappeared on short trips and returned with dirt on his clothes.

Okarun trained bursts so violent that the first one ended with him throwing up behind a tree.

Life continued around all of it.

Momo and Marin planned decorations for a household festival that had been discussed for weeks.

Wakana repaired old cloth into banners.

Coco wanted to add magic.

Qifrey said no.

Then added a little.

At breakfast, Himmel noticed Mau staring at everyone.

"You're doing it again."

Mau looked at him.

"Doing what?"

"Looking like you're memorizing the room."

Mau picked up his cup.

"I'm tired."

Himmel did not believe him.

He did not press.

That made it worse.

Later, Maki cornered Yuta behind the inn.

"How bad?"

Yuta looked exhausted.

"We have a plan."

"That was not my question."

Yuta looked toward Mau, who was training with Okarun.

"Bad."

Maki folded her arms.

"You planning to tell me more?"

"No."

Maki's jaw tightened.

Then she nodded once.

Not acceptance.

A promise that this conversation was not over.
