# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 10 — The Third Outcome

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S2E21 — `One Week`

On the fifth day, Mau formed Blue long enough for everyone involved to stop breathing.

It appeared around his left hand like space had forgotten how to stay where it was.

Unstable.

Hungry.

Wrong.

Mau released it before it consumed more than the air around him.

His hand shook.

Yuta smiled despite everything.

"Again tomorrow."

Mau looked at him.

"Not now?"

"No."

"Why?"

"Because I'm trying to keep you alive."

The sentence landed between them.

Mau looked away first.

Red remained theoretical until evening.

Raphael had helped Rimuru isolate a pattern Mau could understand.

Not reproduce.

Understand.

Sometimes that was enough for Construction to notice.

The requirement list grew.

Knowledge.

Output.

Opposing vectors.

A moment of convergence.

An external target large enough to justify the cost.

One use.

Maybe.

"Plan C," Rimuru said.

Yuta hated the name.

Mau liked that it sounded ordinary.

They still did not know whether it would save him.

That was why Yuta kept training RCT.

That was why Rimuru had begun drafting a Contract he hoped never to use.

That was why the festival happening two nights later suddenly mattered too much.

Mau returned inside.

Frieren was asleep in the common room with a book open on her chest.

He took the book away and covered her with his hoodie.

Halfway upstairs, Frieren woke and looked down.

"This is yours."

Mau smiled.

"Currently."

Frieren stared at him.

Then smiled too.

The joke had traveled far enough to come home.
