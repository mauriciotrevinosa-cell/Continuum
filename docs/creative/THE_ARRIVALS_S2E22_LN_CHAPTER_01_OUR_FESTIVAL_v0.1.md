# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 11 — Our Festival

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S2E22 — `Our Festival`

The outside world was not ready to celebrate with them.

So they celebrated one another.

Nobody said that aloud.

They simply transformed the inn.

Wakana's repaired fabric became banners.

Coco and Qifrey added small lights that floated only where Rimuru approved.

Momo ignored several approvals.

Marin turned food presentation into a threat.

Maomao protected the kitchen with increasing hostility.

Bocchi tuned her guitar.

Kita decorated the music corner.

Umaru volunteered for quality control and was removed from the snack table.

Himmel helped Stark carry benches.

Mikasa was explicitly forbidden from being security.

"What if something happens?"

"Then you can become security," Anko said. "Until then, sit down."

Mikasa sat.

Yuta and Maki moved through the room with the ease of people who had done celebrations together in another life.

Mau helped wherever someone called his name.

That happened constantly.

He said yes every time.

Frieren noticed.

"You usually say no to Momo."

"Sometimes."

"You said yes three times."

"Festival."

Frieren accepted this.

Almost.

Rimuru watched from across the room.

Yuta did too.

Three people knew the night might be the last ordinary one before the plan.

None of them allowed that knowledge to own it.
