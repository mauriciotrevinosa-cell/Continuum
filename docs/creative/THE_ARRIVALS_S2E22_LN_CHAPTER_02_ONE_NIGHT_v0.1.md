# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 12 — One Night

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S2E22 — `Our Festival`

The festival had no single tradition.

That became the tradition.

Marin insisted on photographs even though nobody could send them anywhere.

Umaru approved because stored photos still counted.

Bocchi played.

Kita stood close enough that Bocchi stopped noticing the crowd for entire songs.

Momo and Okarun argued like people rediscovering an old language.

Sometimes he remembered the shape of her irritation before the reason.

Sometimes Momo caught him looking at her and became angry that he looked familiar.

Then they both laughed.

Yuta stayed near Maki more than usual.

Maki noticed.

She did not expose him.

Rimuru allowed himself to become slime and was immediately stolen by three people as furniture.

"This is why leadership is impossible," he said.

Nobody returned him.

Mikasa played a game badly.

Lost.

Asked to play again.

Himmel told a story about Stark.

Stark denied everything.

Fern confirmed enough to destroy the denial.

Frieren laughed.

Mau watched.

Then Coco grabbed his sleeve.

"You're coming."

"Where?"

"Game."

"I was—"

Umaru took his other arm.

Bocchi appeared behind them with the expression of someone who had been recruited against her will and was now committed.

Mau looked at Frieren.

Frieren waved him away.

"Go."

The three dragged him across the room.

For the next hour, Mau did things he would not have chosen alone.

A guessing game.

A ridiculous relay.

Something Momo claimed was a traditional festival challenge and everyone else claimed she had invented thirty seconds earlier.

Mau laughed until his side hurt.

Yuta watched him once.

Rimuru watched too.

Neither said what they were thinking.

Tomorrow still did not own tonight.
