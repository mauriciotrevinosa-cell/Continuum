# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 13 — Next Year

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S2E22 — `Our Festival`

Late in the night, the festival thinned.

Not ended.

Softened.

People carried food outside.

Someone restarted music quietly.

Mau found Frieren sitting on the low wall beyond the inn.

He sat beside her.

For a while they watched light through the windows.

"You disappeared," Frieren said.

"Coco."

"That explains nothing."

"Umaru."

"More."

"Bocchi."

Frieren nodded.

"Now it makes sense."

Mau laughed.

Frieren leaned against him.

They had spent parts of the night together without calling it a date.

Food.

A walk around the decorated yard.

Frieren showing him a tiny grimoire somebody had found for her.

Mau giving her a repaired bookmark Wakana had helped make from a strip of old fabric.

Nothing large enough to deserve ceremony.

That was why it mattered.

"Do you like this?" Frieren asked.

"The festival?"

"All of it."

Mau looked at the house.

At people still awake.

"Yes."

Frieren watched the lights.

"We should do it again next year."

Mau's chest tightened.

The dream offered no next year.

He did not let the pause grow.

"Yes."

Frieren looked at him.

Mau smiled.

She accepted the answer.

Later, when everyone finally slept, Mau stayed awake long enough to memorize the sound of the inn.

Then he stopped himself.

He had promised Yuta and Rimuru a third option.

Memorizing a goodbye was not the same as accepting one.

Before dawn, he slept beside Frieren.

For less than an hour.

It was enough.
