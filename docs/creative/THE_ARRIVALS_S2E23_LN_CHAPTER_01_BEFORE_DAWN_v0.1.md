# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 14 — Before Dawn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

They left before most of the house woke.

Mau.

Yuta.

Rimuru.

Qifrey.

Okarun.

A low-risk work trip was the explanation left behind.

Maki was awake.

She knew enough to hate them.

Not enough to understand why Mau hugged her before leaving.

It was brief.

Tactical-looking.

"Don't let Frieren come if she senses something," Mau said.

Maki's eyes narrowed.

"I can delay her. I'm not imprisoning her."

"I know."

"You better."

Himmel heard the last part from the stairs.

He waited until the others reached the road.

Then caught up.

Yuta turned.

"No."

Himmel kept walking.

"That sounded serious."

"It is."

"Then I'm coming."

Mau stared.

Himmel smiled.

"You said reality can still surprise you, didn't you?"

Mau had not said that to him.

Apparently his face had.

The plan changed.

Mau chose to treat the change as hope.

Not proof.

Hope.

At the prepared field, Qifrey checked the last marks.

Rimuru confirmed barriers.

Okarun stretched once and became very still.

Yuta stood behind cover.

Himmel took position.

Mau walked forward alone.

The dream had placed him there.

So did the plan.

He waited.

Sukuna arrived without announcing himself.

Meguna.

The face from the dream.

The presence Yuta remembered.

Sukuna looked at Mau.

Then at the empty field.

"You."

Mau's heartbeat slowed.

"We have unfinished business."

Sukuna smiled.

"This was entertaining once."

He stepped closer.

"But it ends here."

Mau looked at him.

"Then give me the first attack."

Sukuna laughed.

"What?"

"Any attack I choose. You don't move until it lands."

Sukuna's amusement sharpened.

He sensed almost nothing meaningful from Mau.

Arrogance did the rest.

"Any attack."

The terms settled.

Mau smiled.

"I never said I came alone."

Rika hit Sukuna from the blind side.
