# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 15 — First Hit

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

The first hit moved Sukuna farther than Mau expected.

Not enough.

Never enough.

Yuta arrived with Rika.

Rimuru came from the opposite side.

Qifrey changed the ground before Sukuna could establish the first clean line of attack.

Okarun crossed the field in a blur and moved Mau out of a cut he never saw.

Himmel entered with a sword and the calm expression of someone who had already decided being afraid was not useful.

For several seconds, the plan looked good.

That was dangerous.

Sukuna smiled wider.

Mau could not follow every exchange.

He contributed where he could.

A constructed barrier fragment.

A change of angle.

One attack Yuta had drilled into him until his muscles hated the motion.

He was not suddenly equal to the monsters on the field.

That was not his role.

Rimuru's telepathic relay kept everyone connected.

Left.

Down.

Move.

Now.

Qifrey raised stone where there had been open ground.

Okarun pulled Himmel clear.

Yuta and Rika forced Sukuna backward.

Hope became visible.

Sukuna noticed.

"Good."

Yuta's expression changed.

He knew that tone.

Sukuna lifted one hand.

"This is fun."

The shadow moved.

"Let's raise the stakes."

The wheel appeared.

Yuta shouted before the name finished leaving Sukuna's mouth.

"Mahoraga!"

The battlefield gained a second disaster.
