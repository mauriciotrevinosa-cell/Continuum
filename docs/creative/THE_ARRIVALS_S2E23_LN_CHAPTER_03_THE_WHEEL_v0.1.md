# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 16 — The Wheel

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

Mahoraga looked manageable for almost a minute.

That was the trap.

Rimuru severed an arm.

The wound changed.

The wheel turned.

Rimuru attacked again.

The same approach failed.

Raphael's message entered the relay.

**Slash-type attacks are no longer producing equivalent effect. Adaptive response confirmed.**

Yuta felt cold.

"Don't repeat attacks!"

Himmel changed his angle.

Qifrey changed terrain.

Okarun stopped thinking of the creature as a target and started thinking of it as a moving rule.

Rika struck.

Mahoraga answered.

The five-minute manifestation window was already bleeding away.

Then Mahoraga landed a massive hit on Rika at almost the exact moment the window expired.

Rika vanished from full manifestation.

Yuta staggered.

The field became smaller.

Sukuna laughed.

"Now."

Mau heard Raphael through Rimuru.

**Reliable elimination requires overwhelming destruction before adaptation or reconstruction closes the interval.**

Yuta answered aloud.

"That's how it works."

Mau looked between Sukuna and Mahoraga.

Two threats feeding each other.

"Separate them."

Nobody knew who said it first.

It did not matter.

Rimuru moved to Mahoraga.

Himmel followed.

Qifrey reshaped the field.

Okarun became the line between both fronts.

Yuta stayed on Sukuna.

Mau stayed with Yuta.

The battle split without becoming two battles.

Warnings crossed constantly.

Positions changed.

Mahoraga learned.

Sukuna watched.

And somewhere far away, Frieren looked up from a book because for the first time in days she allowed herself to listen to the sense she had stopped trusting.
