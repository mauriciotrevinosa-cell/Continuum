# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 17 — The Thing She Knew

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

Frieren had stopped trusting the feeling on purpose.

That was the part nobody else fully understood.

For days, Himmel had been beside her again.

Mau had been beside her too.

Maki kept asking questions Frieren did not want to answer for someone else.

The old love and the new one were not competing.

Frieren was.

Against language.

Against the habit of understanding people only after time had made the answer useless.

So she stopped listening to the internal signal that had become tangled with fear.

Now, sitting at the inn, she chose to listen again.

The world opened.

Far away—

Sukuna.

Something else enormous.

Yuta.

Rimuru.

Mau.

Frieren stood so fast the chair fell.

Maki was already in the doorway.

"Mau asked me to stop you."

Frieren's face changed.

"Move."

"He said there's a plan."

"Move."

Maki held the doorway for one second longer.

Not because she intended to imprison Frieren.

Because Mau had trusted her with one job.

"Changing the variables might make it worse."

Frieren looked at her.

"He lied to me."

Maki's expression tightened.

"He hid something."

"He is there."

"I know."

Frieren's staff was already in her hand.

Maki saw the answer.

She stepped aside.

"Bring him back."

Frieren left.

At the field, Mahoraga had adapted to enough of Rimuru's pressure that every second became negotiation.

Then Zoltraak cut across the battlefield.

It tore open Mahoraga's body.

Everyone looked.

Frieren landed.

Mau's face emptied.

Not relief.

Fear.

"Why are you here?"

Frieren stared at him.

"You are."

There was no time for the rest.

Yuta moved immediately.

Rika returned only partially—enough.

They struck the opening Frieren had made.

Mahoraga's head separated.

Its body collapsed.

The wheel fell.

For one breath, even Sukuna looked interested.

Frieren saw the apparent victory.

A lethal opening.

Reasonable.

Real.

She moved to finish it.

Yuta shouted.

The wheel turned.
