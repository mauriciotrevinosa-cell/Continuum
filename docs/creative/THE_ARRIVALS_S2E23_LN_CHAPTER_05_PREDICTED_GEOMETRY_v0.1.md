# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 18 — Predicted Geometry

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

Mahoraga had not stopped learning because its head was gone.

The rule underneath the battlefield changed.

Frieren saw Sukuna's counter too late.

Mau did not.

He had seen it for weeks.

The angle.

The distance.

Frieren's position.

His own.

The dream finally became the present.

Qifrey's trap fired.

The symbol they had buried in the field reacted to the geometry Mau had described days earlier.

Signal.

Terrain folded.

Mau moved.

Rimuru moved too.

Not toward the barrier.

Toward Mau.

The Contract caught him first.

Not pain.

Resistance.

A line through his body and magic that refused the action he had voluntarily promised not to take.

Raphael's warning hit the relay.

**Direct physical interference constitutes breach. Contract enforcement escalation probable. Severe functional impairment possible. Barrier continuity at risk.**

Rimuru pushed once anyway.

The resistance sharpened hard enough to make his vision flash.

"Mau!"

Mau did not look back.

Rimuru had one decision left that the Contract did not make for him.

Break against it, possibly lose himself and the barrier, and still fail to reach Mau.

Or do the other job Mau had asked him to do.

Rimuru hated both.

He chose the one that kept everyone else alive.

The barrier closed.

Yuta committed to Frieren.

Okarun moved into extraction position.

And Mau stepped into the line.

Frieren saw him.

"No."

Sukuna's hand drove through Mau's torso.

Everything stopped.

Not physically.

Inside Mau.

The Noise reacted.

A structure it had never encountered touched cursed energy, soul and vessel at once.

Something inside him recognized compatibility where none should have existed.

No one on the field understood the words that surfaced.

**merging began**

Sukuna's expression changed.

For the first time, not amusement.

His awareness flickered.

His body failed to detach cleanly.

Mau remained caught against him, impossibly tethered.

The battlefield had gained seconds.

Only seconds.

Frieren tried to move.

Yuta reached her.

The barrier and extraction timing held.

The Contract held Rimuru to the role he had chosen days earlier.

Rimuru screamed through the relay.

Mau looked at Frieren.

She was crying already.

He smiled because he had no better tool left.

"Everything will be okay."

Frieren shook her head.

"No."

"Mau—"

He looked past her.

At Himmel.

Himmel understood before he wanted to.

Mau's voice was thin.

"Take care of her for me."

Himmel's face broke.

Not a transfer.

Not permission.

Trust from one person who loved Frieren to another.

Himmel answered anyway.

"Tell her yourself."

Yuta's attack hit Mahoraga again.

Catastrophically.

For perhaps fifteen seconds, reconstruction failed.

That was the window.

"Now!" Rimuru shouted.

Yuta pulled Frieren clear.

Okarun took Rimuru.

Qifrey released the field.

Everyone who could leave did.

Mau stayed.

So did Sukuna.

The tether would not let either pretend otherwise.
