# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 19 — Purple

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 19
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

Mau's left hand disappeared inside Blue.

Not burned.

Not cut.

Engulfed by a force he only partly understood.

Pain stopped meaning anything useful.

Blue existed.

Unstable.

Hungry.

The requirement was satisfied.

On the other side, Red formed.

For one second it looked almost gentle.

Sukuna understood before Mau completed the convergence.

He tried to tear away.

The tether held.

Then Mahoraga moved.

Not dead.

Reconstructing.

The wheel turned above ruined flesh.

Mau had expected that too.

He redirected the opposing forces.

Mahoraga entered the space between Red and Blue because adaptation demanded an answer to the new phenomena.

It did not have enough time.

The two met.

The wheel shattered in the visual center of the convergence.

Mahoraga disappeared inside annihilation faster than it could become accustomed to it.

For an instant, Mau remembered Frieren smiling.

Not a grand memory.

A morning.

Bad hair.

His hoodie.

A cup in her hands.

Then Hollow Purple detonated.

The field became light and absence.

Far outside the center, Yuta held Frieren down.

Rimuru's barrier screamed.

Okarun could not hear anything.

Himmel stared into white.

When the force ended, Mahoraga was gone.

Mau was still.

Sukuna was not.

Barely.

Something terrible had been exchanged in the final instant.

A binding vow.

A Contract of his own.

Sukuna abandoned the future pathway to the World Cutting Slash—the world-targeting configuration he had been rebuilding toward—in exchange for survival now.

He escaped maimed.

Yuta saw him go.

Could pursue.

Then looked at Mau.

One choice.

Yuta chose Mau.

He was already moving before Sukuna vanished from sight.

RCT began immediately.

Mau gave no sign that there was anything left to heal.
