# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 7
## Chapter 20 — The Hand

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 7
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S2E23 — `Sukuna`

Yuta did not stop.

Rika returned enough to help carry, brace, hold, anything.

Rimuru's coat covered Mau because there was too much the others did not need to see.

Frieren walked only because Rimuru supported her.

She did not speak.

Himmel carried a weapon he no longer remembered picking up.

Okarun reached the inn first.

Then Qifrey.

People came outside.

Questions started immediately.

Where is Mau?

Where is Yuta?

What happened?

Okarun could not answer.

Rimuru arrived with Frieren.

Fern ran to them.

Stark saw Frieren's face and stopped.

"Mau?"

No answer.

Yuta appeared last.

Carrying someone under a coat.

Still using RCT.

Still moving.

Still refusing the meaning of the weight in his arms.

The household went silent in waves.

Umaru stood.

Coco dropped something.

Bocchi's hand found Kita's.

Momo looked at Rimuru.

"Where is Mau?"

Yuta crossed the yard.

The coat shifted.

One hand slipped out.

Mau's watch was still on the wrist.

No one asked again.

Frieren reached him before Yuta reached the door.

The house that had learned to receive strangers made room for one of its own.

Apparently dead.

Volume 7 ended before anyone knew whether there would be another morning with him in it.
