# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 1 — The Room

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S2E24 — `The Aftermath`

The inn had never been quieter with so many people inside it.

Yuta took Mau upstairs.

Maomao cleared the room before anyone finished asking what she needed.

Rika remained close enough to be felt.

Frieren followed and did not leave.

Yuta's RCT continued.

It had already continued too long.

There was damage he could repair.

Damage he could only stabilize.

And something else.

Something that felt less like a wound than an absence refusing to become permanent.

"Again," Yuta said.

Maomao looked at him.

"You have been doing it."

"Again."

She did not tell him to stop.

Not yet.

Frieren sat beside the bed.

Mau's hand lay still beneath hers.

The watch was gone; Maomao had removed it with everything else that interfered with treatment.

Frieren kept looking at the bare wrist.

Wrong.

Everything about the room was wrong.

Fern stood behind her.

"Frieren."

No response.

"Drink."

"No."

"Drink."

Frieren did.

Because Fern put the cup into her hand and waited.

Downstairs, nobody knew what to do with dinner.

They made it anyway.

Food existed.

People did not eat it.

Umaru sat on the stairs.

Coco sat beside her.

Bocchi sat one step below with Kita.

Nobody had planned the arrangement.

Momo paced until Anko told her she was making everyone worse.

Momo stopped.

Then started again more quietly.

Stark stood near the wall with both hands clenched.

Himmel sat at the table.

Mikasa checked the door once, then realized there was nothing outside to defend against.

Agott stayed near Qifrey.

Karane cried angrily.

Hakari cried openly.

Shizuka kept a book closed in her lap.

Nano asked Rimuru what the probability was.

Rimuru did not answer.

He could not.

Raphael would give numbers.

Numbers would not help.

Rimuru looked upstairs.

He had agreed to the Contract.

He had followed the plan.

He had helped keep Frieren out of the fatal geometry until extraction.

Every successful part of the plan felt like evidence against him.

No one said that yet.

They would.
