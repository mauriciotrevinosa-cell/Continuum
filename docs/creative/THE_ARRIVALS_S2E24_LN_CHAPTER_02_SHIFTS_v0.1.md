# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 2 — Shifts

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S2E24 — `The Aftermath`

The first day became shifts.

Not because anyone organized them.

Because bodies failed.

Yuta worked until Maki physically pulled him away.

"I can keep going."

"No."

"Maki—"

"No."

Rika remained.

Yuta slept for forty minutes.

Returned.

Frieren never left.

Fern brought food.

Frieren ate three bites because Fern watched.

Maomao changed dressings.

Stark carried water.

Himmel sat in the hallway when there was nowhere else useful to be.

Downstairs, people began breaking in different directions.

Umaru became silent.

Coco asked questions nobody could answer.

Bocchi tried to play and stopped after one chord.

Momo became furious at objects.

Anko wrote nothing.

Mikasa volunteered for patrol.

Maki told her no.

"Why?"

"Because you're trying to find a job that makes this not hurt."

Mikasa looked at her.

Then sat.

Later someone finally said what several people were thinking.

Rimuru was supposed to protect him.

The words came from Karane.

She regretted them instantly.

Rimuru did not defend himself.

"Yes."

That made it worse.

Mau's voice would have interrupted.

He would have said the responsibility was shared.

He was not there to say it.

Yuta heard from the stairs.

"He chose the Contract too."

Everyone looked at him.

Yuta's face was gray with exhaustion.

"So did I."

No absolution.

Only distribution.

The anger did not vanish.

It found more places to go.

On the second night, Maomao said the sentence nobody wanted.

"We need to consider that his body may not be able to come back from this."

Frieren looked at her.

"No."

Maomao held the gaze.

"I am not saying we stop."

"No."

"I am saying—"

"He told me everything would be okay."

The room went still.

Frieren's voice was not loud.

It was absolute because the alternative could not be held yet.

"He told me."

Maomao looked down.

"Then we keep trying."

It was not medicine.

It was love making a medical decision for one more night.

Yuta returned to work.
