# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 3 — When Frieren Sleeps

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S2E24 — `The Aftermath`

By the third night, Frieren had slept in fragments measured in minutes.

Fern stopped asking.

She brought a blanket.

Then Maomao brought something bitter.

Frieren smelled it.

"No."

Fern sat beside her.

"You have to sleep."

"No."

"You are not helping him by collapsing."

Frieren's hand remained around Mau's.

"I'll wake up and—"

Fern understood.

The sentence ended before it needed words.

What if he changed while she slept?

What if the room became final without her permission?

Fern's eyes burned.

"Then I'll wake you."

Frieren looked at her.

"Immediately."

"Yes."

Frieren finally lay down beside Mau, careful of every bandage.

She wore his McLaren hoodie because at some point on the first day she had gone upstairs, found it on the chair, and put it on without thinking.

No one asked her to remove it.

Her hand stayed on his.

Fern remained in the chair.

Frieren slept.

Only then did Fern allow herself to break.

Silently at first.

Then not.

Stark found her in the hallway.

Fern pushed him away once.

Then stopped.

He held her.

Neither said Mau's name.

They did not need to.

Downstairs, Yuta collapsed at the table.

Not metaphorically.

Maki caught him before his face hit the wood.

Rika's presence flickered.

Yuta woke minutes later and tried to stand.

Maki put him back.

"You can't heal him if you die too."

Yuta stared at the ceiling.

"He chose her."

Maki looked at him.

"What?"

"He knew."

The words came apart.

"He knew what would happen."

Maki went still.

Yuta realized too late how much he had revealed.

Maki's face changed.

Not now.

Anger had to wait.

Yuta covered his eyes.

"I found a third option."

Maki held his wrist.

"Then keep believing in it."

Upstairs, something inside Mau finally found the edge of the path Yuta had been building toward.

Not enough to wake.

Enough not to leave.
