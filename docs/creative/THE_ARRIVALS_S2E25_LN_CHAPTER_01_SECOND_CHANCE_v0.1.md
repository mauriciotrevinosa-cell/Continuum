# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 4 — Second Chance

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S2E25 — `I Told You Everything Would Be Okay`

There was no battlefield.

No body.

No pain.

Only the sense that an answer had already been recorded.

**Accept fate completed.**

Mau did not know who had written it.

A presence asked him a question.

Why did you not let Frieren die?

Mau had no speech prepared.

No philosophy.

"She was my first real memory."

The words came easily here.

"The first person I knew after I woke up."

Another question.

Was that enough?

Mau thought of the inn.

Fern.

Bocchi.

Umaru.

Coco.

Yuta.

Rimuru.

Everyone.

Then Frieren.

"She was alive."

That was the answer.

He had accepted the result if she was safe.

The presence did not praise him.

That mattered.

This was not a reward for dying well.

Something changed anyway.

**Second Chance.**

Not resurrection.

Not repair.

An anchor.

A soul that had come loose caught again.

A body stabilized just enough that another person's effort could matter.

Somewhere far away, Yuta's RCT found resistance where there had been none.

A pattern passed through Mau's system as it happened.

Reverse cursed technique.

Pathway.

Not mastery.

Not a gift he could use.

A shape now known.

Mau did not remember any of this.

By morning, his hand moved.
