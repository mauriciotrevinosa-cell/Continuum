# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 5 — The Hoodie

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S2E25 — `I Told You Everything Would Be Okay`

Mau woke to white hair.

Again.

For several seconds, that was all the world contained.

Frieren slept against him, one hand closed around his.

She wore the McLaren hoodie.

His hoodie.

The one that had stopped being only his long ago.

Mau's body hurt in too many places to organize.

He did not move much.

Only his free hand.

He touched Frieren's hair.

Once.

Again.

Frieren woke.

Her eyes opened without understanding.

Then they did.

She stopped breathing.

Mau smiled badly.

"Hi."

Frieren made a sound he had never heard from her.

Then she was holding him.

Carefully and not carefully at the same time.

Mau hissed.

She pulled back.

"Sorry."

"It's okay."

"No."

She touched his face.

His hair.

His shoulder.

Verifying.

Mau watched tears reach her chin.

"Frieren."

She kissed him.

No warning.

No hesitation.

When she pulled away, her forehead stayed against his.

"I love you."

Mau held her.

The words reached somewhere he still did not have language for.

He knew what he could say.

What had remained true even when everything else had not.

"I told you everything was gonna be okay."

Frieren laughed through tears.

Hit his shoulder weakly.

Immediately regretted it.

Mau winced.

"Sorry."

"It's okay."

"No."

They laughed because there was no better thing to do with surviving.

After a while, Mau looked down at the hoodie.

"You know."

Frieren followed his gaze.

"It looks good on you."

She stared at him.

Three days of terror.

A return from death.

And this idiot was talking about the hoodie.

Frieren laughed again.

This time she buried her face against him.

Mau closed his eyes.

The first thing after death was not prophecy.

It was her wearing his clothes.

That felt correct.
