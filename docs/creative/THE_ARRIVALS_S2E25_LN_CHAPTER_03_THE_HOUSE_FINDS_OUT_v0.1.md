# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 6 — The House Finds Out

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S2E25 — `I Told You Everything Would Be Okay`

Yuta expected to find another medical crisis.

He opened the door.

Mau was awake.

Frieren was beside him.

They were discussing whether a grimoire on the chair belonged upstairs or downstairs.

Yuta stopped.

Mau looked over.

"Hi."

Yuta stared.

Frieren looked at him.

"He's awake."

"I can see that."

The words broke halfway through.

Yuta sat down because his knees had stopped cooperating.

Mau's expression changed.

"You did it."

Yuta covered his face.

"No."

Mau waited.

Yuta's shoulders shook.

"I didn't know if I did."

Mau reached toward him and immediately regretted moving.

Frieren helped reposition him without comment.

Yuta laughed through tears.

"Don't move."

"Everyone keeps saying that."

"Good."

Maki entered because Yuta had been gone too long.

She saw Mau.

Stopped.

Then crossed the room and hit Yuta in the shoulder.

Yuta looked offended.

"Why me?"

"I have several reasons."

Mau smiled.

Maki looked at him.

Her face softened.

"Later."

That meant anger too.

Mau understood.

The news spread in waves.

Fern arrived and froze.

Stark behind her.

Bocchi.

Kita.

Umaru ran and then stopped at the door because suddenly touching him felt dangerous.

Coco asked if she could come closer.

Mau said yes.

Momo cried while insisting she was not crying.

Anko told her nobody believed her.

Rimuru stood outside the room longer than anyone else.

Mau saw him.

"Rimuru."

Rimuru entered.

Mau smiled.

Rimuru looked like he had been forgiven for something nobody had yet discussed.

That was not the same as being forgiven.

Today did not need to solve it.

Today he was alive.
