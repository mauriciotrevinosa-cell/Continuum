# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 7 — Love and Anger

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S2E25 — `I Told You Everything Would Be Okay`

The celebration meal happened before anyone was emotionally ready.

That was why it worked.

Mau sat at the table with more cushions than dignity.

Maomao controlled what he ate.

Yuta looked nearly as bad as Mau and was also being controlled by Maki.

Frieren held Mau's hand under the table.

People laughed while crying.

Coco touched Mau's sleeve twice as if checking.

Umaru sat against his chair.

Bocchi played one song after dinner.

Nobody asked whether everything was fixed.

He was there.

That was enough for one night.

Afterward, the room emptied.

Frieren stayed.

Mau knew from the way she closed the door that the celebration was over.

"You knew."

He did not pretend.

"Yes."

"You knew what you thought would happen."

"Yes."

"And you didn't tell me."

Mau looked down.

"Because if you knew—"

"I know why."

That stopped him.

Frieren's voice remained quiet.

"I know exactly why you did it."

She was crying again.

Not from relief this time.

"That doesn't make it okay."

Mau nodded.

"You decided I would survive losing you."

He had no defense.

"You decided for me."

"I thought—"

"I know."

Frieren sat beside him.

Her hand found his anyway.

Love did not cancel anger.

Anger did not cancel love.

Mau squeezed her fingers.

"I can't promise I won't protect you."

"I don't want that promise."

"I can promise I won't hide something that large and decide everything alone again."

Frieren looked at him.

"Me too."

Mau blinked.

She added, "If I know something that changes your life, I tell you."

The promise became mutual.

Not safety.

Participation.

Frieren leaned against him carefully.

She was still furious.

Mau rested his head against hers.

They stayed like that until sleep became stronger than the argument.
