# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 8 — You Were One of Us Too

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S2E26 — `You Were One of Us Too`

The intervention happened the next morning because too many people had been polite the night before.

Mau sat.

Yuta sat.

Rimuru sat.

Everyone else arranged themselves around the common room.

Mau looked at the configuration.

"Is this a trial?"

Anko said, "No."

Momo said, "Yes."

Rimuru said, "Please don't make it worse."

Nobody laughed enough.

Fern spoke first.

"You thought we could recover from losing you."

Mau looked at her.

He had never said that aloud.

Yuta had.

During grief.

The arithmetic was now public.

Bocchi had Kita.

Coco had Qifrey and Agott.

Yuta had Maki.

Frieren had Fern, Stark and Himmel.

The household had each other.

Therefore Mau could be the cost.

Umaru's face hardened.

"That's stupid."

Mau did not argue.

Coco said, "You were one of us too."

Simple.

That was the sentence.

Not the fighter.

Not the person who could be sacrificed because everyone else had support.

One of the people the plan was supposed to protect.

Rimuru tried to take responsibility for the Contract.

Mau stopped him.

"You agreed because I asked."

"I should have refused."

"Then I would have tried without it."

Rimuru looked furious.

"That is not helping."

"It's true."

Yuta spoke.

"And I helped."

Maki's hand tightened around his.

The blame refused to become singular.

That made it harder.

Also healthier.

The intervention did not end with everyone satisfied.

It ended with a rule.

No more secret fatal plans decided by three people because they thought everyone else would be safer not knowing.

Not every tactical detail had to become public.

But the people whose lives were being chosen for them got a voice.

Mau agreed.

Yuta agreed.

Rimuru agreed.

Frieren did not say thank you.

Not yet.
