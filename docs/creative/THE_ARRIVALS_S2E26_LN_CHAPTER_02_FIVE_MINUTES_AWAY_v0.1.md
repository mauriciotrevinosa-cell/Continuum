# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 9 — Five Minutes Away

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S2E26 — `You Were One of Us Too`

Three days later, Maomao allowed Mau to walk outside alone.

Technically.

Mau stood near the door.

"I'm going to get the tool from the east shed."

Frieren stood.

"So am I."

Umaru stood.

"Me too."

Coco looked up.

"I need air."

Fern closed her book.

"I need to make sure all of you come back."

Bocchi appeared from the hallway wearing shoes.

Mau stared.

"It's five minutes away."

Nobody cared.

Okarun walked past.

"Where are you all going?"

"Apparently an expedition."

Okarun joined because the absurdity had momentum.

Mau opened the door.

"I survived Sukuna and lost to errands."

Frieren took his hand.

"Correct."

The walk was ridiculous.

It was also the first time several of them had watched Mau leave the inn since watching him return under Rimuru's coat.

Nobody said that.

At the shed, Mau found the tool.

On the way back, Umaru stopped clinging to his sleeve.

Coco walked ahead with Bocchi.

Fern complained about mud.

The entourage became a joke.

That was the beginning of healing.

Weeks did not pass all at once.

Small things loosened.

Movie night returned.

Mau was assigned insulting chores.

He sat through a whole common-room evening while people looked at him less and less.

Yuta stopped checking his pulse when he thought Mau wasn't noticing.

Frieren did not stop holding his hand.

Mau did not ask her to.
