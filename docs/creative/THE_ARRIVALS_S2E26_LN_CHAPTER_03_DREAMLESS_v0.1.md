# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 10 — Dreamless

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S2E26 — `You Were One of Us Too`

For several nights, Mau dreamed nothing.

That became strange.

The predictive system had occupied so much of his sleep that silence felt like a missing sound.

He woke normally.

Again.

Again.

No battlefield.

No cups breaking tomorrow.

No impossible certainty.

The Sukuna dreams were over.

Mau began to trust sleep.

Then a girl appeared.

Not as prediction.

As place.

Trees.

Soft light.

A figure alone.

No event waiting to happen.

No branch.

Mau woke.

Frieren was awake beside him.

He told her immediately.

Every detail he remembered.

No hesitation.

Frieren listened.

The next morning he told Yuta and Rimuru.

They started a page.

Girl.

Forest.

Unknown location.

Unknown reality.

No assumption that she needed rescue.

No assumption she did not.

The dream returned two nights later.

The girl was in the same general place.

The trees matched.

A sound of water.

The angle of light.

Mau added details.

Frieren watched him write.

"This time you told me."

Mau looked at her.

"Yes."

Frieren nodded.

No speech.

That was enough.

The strange thing had entered the household before it became a secret.
