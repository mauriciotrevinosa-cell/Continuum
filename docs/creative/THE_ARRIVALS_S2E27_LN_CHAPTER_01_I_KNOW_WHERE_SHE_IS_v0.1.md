# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 11 — I Know Where She Is

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S2E27 — `The Girl in the Forest`

The future-home scouting team left without Mau.

This was controversial only to Mau.

"I can walk."

"No," Fern said.

"I can scout."

"No," Maomao said.

"I can sit in a cart."

"No," six people said.

Mau looked at Frieren.

Frieren looked at him.

"No."

He sighed.

The team left anyway: Rimuru, Mikasa, Maki, Qifrey, Agott, Stark and Nano, with enough different priorities to make any promising site difficult to romanticize.

Mau stayed at the inn.

He helped with light work.

Was told to stop helping with light work.

Sat on the sofa.

Frieren sat beside him reading.

Mau fell asleep.

The dream did not feel like a dream.

He stood in the forest.

Wind.

Wet earth.

Water somewhere close.

The same girl.

Closer.

A broken stone marker he recognized from a real route map.

A ridge shape.

A stream bending east.

Mau knew the place.

Not interpreted.

Knew.

He woke.

Frieren lowered the book.

Mau stood too quickly and immediately regretted it.

"I know where she is."

Frieren was already standing.

"Let's go."

No committee.

No hidden plan.

Mau told Fern.

Frieren told Yuta.

They took what they needed and left.

The lesson from Sukuna had not been never move quickly.

It had been not to make love wait outside the decision.
