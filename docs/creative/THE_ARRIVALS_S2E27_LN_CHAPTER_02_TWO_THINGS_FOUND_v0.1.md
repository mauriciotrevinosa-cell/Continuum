# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 12 — Two Things Found

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S2E27 — `The Girl in the Forest`

Two groups searched for different futures on the same afternoon.

The scouts reached a valley beyond the earlier rejected sites.

Nobody said yes immediately.

That was a good sign.

Fresh water.

A river feeding toward a larger lake system.

Higher ground that did not flood.

Natural barriers without becoming a trap.

More than one approach.

Enough buildable land for several connected roofs and more later.

Maomao was not there to test soil, so Maki took samples.

Wakana was not there to judge workshops, so Nano measured usable space.

Qifrey studied magical conditions.

Stark checked stone.

Mikasa checked exits.

Rimuru checked everything twice.

The weaknesses looked solvable.

That was new.

"No one call it the place yet," Rimuru said.

Maki looked around.

"It might be."

"That's different."

They began notes.

Elsewhere, Mau and Frieren followed the stream from the dream.

Mau tired faster than he admitted.

Frieren noticed.

They stopped.

Mau did not argue.

That was new too.

At the broken marker, the dream became geography.

Mau pointed.

"That way."

Frieren looked at him.

"Certain?"

"Yes."

"How?"

"I don't know."

Frieren accepted the limit.

They continued.

They found the girl beneath trees near the water.

Alone.

Exactly where the dream had placed her.

Mau stopped.

Recognition moved through him without memory.

Not because he knew her.

Because he had been looking at her in sleep.

The girl looked up.

Fear first.

Then confusion.

Mau stayed where he was.

"My name is Mau."

Frieren remained beside him.

"We're not going to hurt you."

The girl's eyes moved between them.

Mau swallowed.

"I've been dreaming about you."

Frieren looked at him.

Mau realized how that sounded.

"Not—"

The girl almost laughed.

Almost.

That was enough to break the terror.

Mau crouched at a distance.

"Do you have a name?"

She hesitated.

"Ori."

Mau nodded.

"Ori."

The dream had become a person.
