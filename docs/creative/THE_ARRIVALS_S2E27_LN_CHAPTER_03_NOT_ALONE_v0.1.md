# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 8
## Chapter 13 — Not Alone

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 8
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S2E27 — `The Girl in the Forest`

Ori followed Mau first.

That was unavoidable.

He had arrived already knowing she existed.

From her perspective, a stranger had crossed a world to find the exact place where she was alone.

Frieren prevented that fact from becoming the whole story.

She walked beside Ori too.

Answered questions Mau could not.

Gave her water.

Did not force closeness.

By the time the inn appeared, Ori understood at least one thing.

Mau had not come from nowhere alone.

He had come from somewhere full.

The bell rang.

People came outside.

Mau saw the future-home scouts returning from another direction at almost the same time, carrying maps and samples.

For several seconds the yard contained two discoveries.

Rimuru stared at Mau.

"Mau."

"I know."

"You were supposed to stay home."

"I didn't go alone."

Everyone looked at him.

Frieren stepped out from behind him.

"He didn't."

Momo looked between them.

"That defense is technically correct."

Fern closed her eyes.

"Who is she?"

Ori moved closer to Mau.

Mau felt it.

Did not make it permanent by reaching for her.

"This is Ori."

Rimuru looked at the maps in his own hands.

Then at Ori.

Then at the house.

"Of course it is."

The scouts had found the strongest possible place for the family to live next.

Mau and Frieren had found another person who might live there with them.

Neither future was chosen yet.

Both had entered the house.

Ori slept in a borrowed room with a borrowed blanket.

Mau stayed nearby until she asked if he was leaving.

"Only downstairs."

She nodded.

Frieren remained after he left.

Ori looked at her.

"You came too."

"Yes."

"Why?"

Frieren thought.

"Because he shouldn't go alone."

Ori absorbed that.

Then asked, "Are you always with him?"

Frieren looked toward the door.

"Mostly."

The word had traveled again.

Volume 8 ended with Ori hearing it for the first time.
