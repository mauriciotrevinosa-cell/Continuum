# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 1 — First Days

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S2E28 — `Somewhere to Belong`

Ori followed Mau for the first two days.

If Mau went to the kitchen, Ori appeared there later. If he sat outside, she found a place nearby. If he left the room, her eyes tracked the door.

Nobody called it romance. The household knew what displaced attachment looked like.

Mau did not encourage dependence. He also did not punish it.

"I'm going upstairs," he told Ori once. "I'll be back after I get the book."

Location. Reason. Return.

The behavior he learned after Sukuna now became someone else's stability.

When Mau returned, Ori was talking to Fern.

That was the first success.

Frieren became Ori's second anchor almost by accident. She had gone to the forest too. Ori could ask her questions about the rescue Mau could not answer.

"Was he sure I was real?"

"No."

"He still went."

"Yes."

"Why?"

Frieren thought.

"Because there was enough chance that you were."

One afternoon Ori sat in the same room as Frieren for nearly an hour without Mau there.

Frieren read. Ori watched rain.

At the end, Ori asked, "Can I sit here again tomorrow?"

"Yes."

That was the second success.

After that, the house began catching her.

Bocchi showed her the music room. Umaru showed her snacks. Coco showed her too many things. Maomao checked her health. Anko made her laugh. Momo and Marin pulled her into conversations. Kita explained people. Yuta made tea. Rimuru found space. Agott showed her how to tell Coco no.

By the end of the week, Mau left for a short walk with Okarun.

Ori noticed.

She did not follow.

She stayed at the table with Frieren and Bocchi.

The house had become somewhere to belong even when her first safe person was elsewhere.
