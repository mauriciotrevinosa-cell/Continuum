# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 2 — The Site Report

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S2E28 — `Somewhere to Belong`

The future-home maps returned to the table.

Not because Ori's story had paused. Life did not wait for one emotional thread to finish before another continued.

Rimuru spread the scout notes.

Water. Elevation. Routes. Stone. Buildable ground. Winter light. Possible access toward larger trade corridors.

Weaknesses.

The weaknesses were the important part.

They could name them.

They could solve most of them.

"This is the first site where the problems sound like construction problems," Nano said.

"Instead of reasons not to live there," Mau added.

Nobody declared victory.

They assigned verification.

Maomao wanted soil. Wakana wanted workshop orientation and dry storage. Qifrey wanted another magical survey. Mikasa wanted to walk every approach. Marin wanted to know which direction sunsets happened.

Rimuru stared at her.

"That is not irrelevant," Marin said.

Frieren agreed.

Rimuru wrote it down.

Ori watched.

"You're building a town?"

Mau shook his head.

"A house."

Rimuru said, "A very large house."

Momo said, "A town."

"No."

"Future city."

"No."

Ori looked at the map.

"Can I come?"

The room changed for less than a second.

Not because anyone objected.

Because the word assumed a future.

Mau and Frieren answered at the same time.

"Yes."

Ori smiled.

Later Wakana repaired Ori's borrowed clothes before making anything new. He asked what she liked. Ori did not know.

That answer was allowed.

So he started with fit: a sleeve that stopped catching, a warmer layer, a pocket where her hand naturally reached.

Frieren sat nearby wearing Mau's Ferrari shirt.

Ori looked at it.

"Is that his too?"

"Yes."

Mau passed the doorway.

"It's becoming a museum."

Wakana looked at him.

"I can make you another."

Mau thought about the original backpack and the shrinking number of things from before.

Then smiled.

"Yeah. Maybe."

Wardrobe could expand without erasing origin.
