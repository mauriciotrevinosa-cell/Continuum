# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 3 — What's a Girlfriend?

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S2E28 — `Somewhere to Belong`

The conversation began after midnight because good decisions rarely did.

Kita was telling Ori how she and Bocchi became official.

Maki corrected a story about Yuta despite Yuta not being present.

Marin talked about Wakana.

Momo talked about Okarun and accidentally revealed three things she had sworn not to tell anyone.

Fern refused to discuss Stark.

This guaranteed everyone wanted to discuss Stark.

Hakari, Karane, Shizuka and Nano contributed incompatible definitions of romance.

Ori listened because she was trying to understand why attachment had so many shapes.

Frieren was upstairs reading.

Mau came down for water.

He saw the group.

Stopped.

Tried to reverse.

Momo pointed.

"Too late."

Mau sat because resistance would take longer.

Eventually someone asked how he and Frieren happened.

"After Sukuna, I woke up. She kissed me. Then she said she loved me."

The room went silent.

Hakari leaned forward.

"And you?"

"I hugged her."

"No. What did you say?"

Mau thought.

"I told her, 'I told you everything was gonna be okay.'"

Several people made sounds.

Mau looked around.

"What?"

Marin covered her face.

Maki was smiling.

Ori watched him carefully.

Kita asked, "Then when did you ask her?"

"Ask her what?"

"When she became your girlfriend."

Mau looked genuinely confused.

"What's a girlfriend?"

Silence became catastrophic.

Fern closed her eyes.

Momo fell sideways.

Karane shouted.

Hakari looked delighted.

Nano began to explain and was stopped by three people.

Kita recovered first.

"Remember when you helped me figure out how to ask Bocchi?"

"Yes."

"We didn't just know how we felt. We said what relationship we were choosing. We made it explicit."

Mau stared toward the stairs.

"Frieren and I never did that."

Fern said, "No."

"She told me she loves me."

"Yes."

Mau looked more confused.

"So why would I need to ask?"

Kita smiled.

"Because choosing it out loud can matter."

Mau went quiet.

Ori did too.

For Mau, the revelation was a missing social category.

For Ori, it sounded like a possibility.

Neither knew yet what that would cost.
