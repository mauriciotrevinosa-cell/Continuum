# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 4 — A Possibility

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S2E29 — `Trying to Understand Love`

Ori did not confess the next morning.

Instead, she wondered.

She watched Mau and Frieren.

Mau made room for Frieren without looking.

Frieren took his cap.

Mau complained.

Frieren ignored him.

They argued about books.

Sat together.

Held hands when neither needed comfort.

Ori had never needed a label to see closeness.

But now she knew they had never spoken one.

And Mau had never said the words back.

That mattered because Ori wanted it to matter.

She hated that.

Ori began asking Mau for time more deliberately.

A walk.

Help with a task she could probably have done alone.

A place beside him at dinner.

Mau said yes often because he cared about her.

That made interpretation worse.

When Ori asked to walk to the lake, Mau went.

They talked about the forest.

The dreams.

How strange it was to be found by someone who had never met her.

Ori asked, "If you hadn't dreamed about me, would you have cared if you met me normally?"

Mau looked at her.

"Yes."

"How do you know?"

"Because you're you now."

The answer hurt in several directions.

Ori smiled anyway.

Frieren watched them return.

She felt something unpleasant.

Small.

Embarrassing.

Maki found her later reading the same page for ten minutes.

"Jealous?"

"No."

Maki waited.

Frieren looked up.

"Maybe."

"Good."

Frieren glared.

Maki smiled.

"Means you noticed."

Frieren looked back at the page.

"He never told me he loved me."

Maki's smile disappeared.

"He didn't?"

"Not in words."

"And what did he do?"

Frieren thought of the battlefield. The promise. The room. Everything.

"That's not the same."

Maki did not tell her she was wrong.

Sometimes words mattered precisely when actions were already obvious.
