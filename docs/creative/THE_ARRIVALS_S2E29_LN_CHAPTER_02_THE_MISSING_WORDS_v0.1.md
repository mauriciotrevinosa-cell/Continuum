# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 5 — The Missing Words

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S2E29 — `Trying to Understand Love`

Frieren became strange.

Mau noticed after two days.

Not distant.

That would have been easier.

She still sat beside him.

Still reached for his hand.

Still wore the hoodie.

She was simply irritated by things that had not previously been problems.

Ori sat beside Mau.

Frieren looked up.

Mau helped Ori with a route map.

Frieren turned a page too hard.

Mau asked if she wanted tea.

"No."

He made tea anyway.

She drank it.

Mau became more confused.

Fern refused to explain.

"You need to ask her."

"Ask what?"

Fern looked at him with profound disappointment.

Mau tried.

"Are you angry?"

Frieren thought.

"Yes."

"At me?"

"Maybe."

"What did I do?"

Frieren stared.

That was the problem.

He did not know.

"I told you I loved you."

Mau's expression softened.

"I know."

"You didn't."

He stopped.

There it was.

Not an accusation that he did not care.

A gap.

Mau searched for the correct answer.

"I didn't know what the words meant for me."

Frieren looked hurt anyway.

"I know what they meant for me."

Mau sat beside her.

"I know."

"Do you?"

He could not lie.

"I think I'm learning."

Frieren looked away.

Mau did not solve it that night.

He held her hand.

She let him.

The missing words remained missing.

The next afternoon, Ori found Frieren alone.

Ori almost left.

Frieren looked up.

"Sit."

Ori did.

For several minutes neither spoke.

Then Ori said, "I think I like Mau."

Frieren's page stopped turning.

Ori's face went red.

"I don't know how. I don't know if it's because he found me. Or because he makes me feel safe. Or because I actually—"

"I know."

Ori looked at her.

Frieren did not look angry.

That almost made it worse.

"Does that bother you?"

"Yes."

Ori flinched.

Frieren continued.

"But you didn't do anything wrong."

Ori stared.

Frieren looked at the book.

"I am trying to understand the difference."

"Between what?"

Frieren thought of Himmel.

Mau.

A century of grief.

A few months of ordinary mornings.

"Everything."

Ori understood less.

She also trusted Frieren more.
