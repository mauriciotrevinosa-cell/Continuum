# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 7 — Ori Says It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 7
**Source episode:** S2E30 — `You Win`

Ori chose the lake because it was where she had first managed to be alone with Mau without feeling alone.

That made sense to her.

It also made the walk terrible.

Mau noticed she was quiet.

He did not fill it.

At the dock, Ori stopped.

"I need to tell you something."

Mau looked at her.

She had practiced versions.

All disappeared.

"I like you."

Mau waited.

Ori forced herself to continue.

"Not just because you found me. I know that's part of it. I know I depended on you. I know I still do sometimes."

Her hands shook.

"But I want things I don't want from everyone else."

Mau understood before she finished.

Ori saw it.

That almost made her stop.

She did not.

"I want to be with you. Romantically."

The word landed because the household had taught her what it meant.

Mau looked at the water.

Then at Ori.

He did not pretend confusion to make the rejection easier.

"I love you."

Ori's breath caught.

Mau continued before hope could turn the sentence into something else.

"But not like that."

The pain reached her face.

Mau hated causing it.

He did not retract the truth.

"I want you here. I want you safe. I want you to have a life that doesn't depend on whether I'm in the room."

Ori looked away.

"That's family."

"I think so."

"You think?"

Mau almost smiled.

"I'm still learning."

Ori did not.

Mau's voice became quieter.

"What I feel for Frieren is different."

There.

The missing category.

He heard himself say it and knew.

"I love her romantically."

Ori closed her eyes.

The answer she had asked for existed now.

It simply was not hers.

Mau stayed.

He did not touch her without permission.

After a while, Ori said, "I want to go back."

They walked home with space between them.

Not abandonment.

Space.
