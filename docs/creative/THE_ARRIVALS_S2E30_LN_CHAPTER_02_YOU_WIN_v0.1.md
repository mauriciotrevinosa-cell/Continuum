# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 8 — You Win

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S2E30 — `You Win`

Ori avoided Mau the next day.

Not completely.

The inn made complete avoidance almost impossible.

She chose different seats.

Different chores.

Different routes through rooms.

Mau noticed and let her.

Frieren noticed too.

That afternoon, Ori found Frieren alone near the drying line.

She stopped.

Frieren looked up.

Ori had not planned to say anything.

"You win."

Frieren stared.

"What?"

Ori regretted the sentence instantly.

"Nothing."

"You said I win."

Ori looked away.

Frieren looked genuinely confused.

Then Mau came through the yard carrying folded cloth.

He saw them.

Stopped.

Ori's face changed.

Frieren understood.

Not everything.

Enough.

"Oh."

Ori laughed once without humor.

"Yeah."

Frieren set the cloth down.

"I wasn't competing."

"I know."

"Then why did you say that?"

"Because it feels like I lost."

Frieren thought for a long time.

"You did."

Ori looked at her sharply.

Frieren continued.

"The thing you wanted."

No false comfort.

"You didn't lose your place here."

Ori's eyes filled.

"That's easy for you to say."

"Probably."

Frieren sat beside the line.

Ori remained standing.

Frieren looked toward the inn.

"I loved Himmel."

Ori did not expect the name.

"I still do."

"And Mau?"

Frieren's face softened.

"Also."

"That's different."

"Yes."

Ori sat.

Frieren continued.

"Different doesn't mean the other love was fake."

Ori looked down.

"So I'm supposed to be happy being family?"

"No."

Frieren's answer came immediately.

"You can be hurt."

That was the first thing anyone said that helped.

Mau did not enter the conversation.

He had learned something.

Care did not always mean approaching.

He took the folded cloth inside and left them alone.

Frieren noticed.

Ori did too.

Neither called him back.
