# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 9 — Not Erased

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S2E30 — `You Win`

Nothing became normal immediately.

Ori still avoided Mau sometimes.

Mau still noticed.

Frieren still felt jealousy and then felt embarrassed about feeling it.

The house refused to stop needing all three of them anyway.

Maomao needed Ori to carry something.

Wakana needed Mau to hold fabric.

Fern needed Frieren to stop pretending a book counted as a chore.

At dinner, there were only so many seats.

Ori ended up across from Mau.

For the first several minutes they did not look at each other.

Then Momo said something ridiculous.

Ori laughed.

Mau laughed too.

Their eyes met.

The world did not end.

That was the first repair.

Later, Mau found Ori in the hallway.

"I don't want you to think I care less."

Ori looked at him.

"That's not how this works."

"I know."

"You can't say one correct sentence and fix it."

"I know."

"Stop saying you know."

Mau stopped.

Ori almost smiled.

"Better."

He nodded.

"Do you want me to leave you alone?"

"Sometimes."

"Okay."

"Not always."

Mau waited.

Ori looked toward the common room where voices overlapped.

"I still want to be here."

Mau's face softened.

"Good."

That was all.

No new label.

No forced sibling declaration.

No instant maturity.

Only proof that rejection had not erased belonging.

A question about the room she might want.
