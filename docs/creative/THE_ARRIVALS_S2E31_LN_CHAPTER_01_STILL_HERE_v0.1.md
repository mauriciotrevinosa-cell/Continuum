# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 9
## Chapter 10 — Still Here

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT
**Volume:** 9
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S2E31 — `Still Here`

Ori's hurt did not disappear.

It became less central.

That was different.

She spent an afternoon with Frieren without Mau.

Not accidentally.

Frieren asked if she wanted to walk.

Ori almost said no because accepting felt like admitting something.

Then she went.

They took the lake path.

For a while, neither mentioned Mau.

Frieren talked about a grimoire.

Ori asked why anyone would spend money on magic that removed stains from bronze statues.

Frieren defended it badly.

Ori laughed.

At the dock, silence settled.

Ori said, "I still like him."

"I know."

"Does that bother you?"

"Less."

Ori looked at her.

Frieren looked at the water.

"I know what he chose now."

"You always knew."

"Not the words."

That distinction mattered to both of them.

Ori asked, "Are you going to ask him what you are?"

Frieren thought.

"He knows now."

"That isn't an answer."

Frieren looked mildly offended.

Ori smiled.

For once, she had caught Frieren using Mau's style of avoidance.

They returned together.

Mau was outside.

He saw them.

Ori felt the old ache.

Then Frieren handed Ori the book she had carried the entire walk.

"Tomorrow."

Ori took it.

A plan that did not include Mau.

That was another repair.

Ordinary life reclaimed more space.

Mau and Okarun repaired a cart.

Ori helped Wakana choose a fastener.

Coco argued with Agott.

Bocchi and Kita disappeared for a walk without anyone tracking when they would return.

Maki and Yuta spent an afternoon away.

Himmel and Stark helped Rimuru with roof work and turned it into an argument.

Mikasa played cards voluntarily.

The house stopped organizing itself around Ori's rejection.

Ori noticed.

It hurt less than being treated carefully forever.

She was still here.

That was the point.
