<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln01-missing-step -->

Mau understood the problem three days after everyone else had.

That was generous.

Kita had explained what a girlfriend was.

Ori had forced him to understand the difference between loving someone and loving someone romantically.

Mau had finally said the words aloud.

I love Frieren romantically.

The remaining problem was simple enough to be embarrassing.

He had never told Frieren.

Not like that.

Not deliberately.

Frieren had kissed him.

Frieren had said `I love you.`

Mau had held her and answered with the promise he knew how to make.

Then life happened.

Death stopped being immediate.

Anger happened.

Ori happened.

Jealousy happened.

Understanding happened.

And somehow Mau and Frieren had continued living exactly as they had before while everyone around them apparently knew there was a missing social step.

Mau found Rimuru at the table.

"I need help."

Rimuru looked up from a ledger.

"With what?"

"I want to ask Frieren to be my girlfriend."

Rimuru stared at him.

Mau waited.

Rimuru put the pen down.

"You are serious."

"Yes."

"You sleep in the same room."

"Yes."

"You share clothes."

"She shares my clothes."

"That is not better."

"I know."

"You almost died for her."

"Yes."

"She kissed you."

"Yes."

"She told you she loved you."

"Yes."

Rimuru covered his face.

Mau waited.

From the stairs, Momo shouted:

"FINALLY."

Mau looked up.

Half the household was apparently listening.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln01-plan -->

The meeting formed in under four minutes.

Mau did not call it.

The house did.

Bocchi arrived with Umaru.

Coco arrived because she heard the word girlfriend.

Marin appeared from nowhere.

Momo dragged Anko.

Wakana followed Marin.

Hakari, Karane, Shizuka and Nano occupied one side of the table before Mau understood how they had been informed.

Yuta and Maki sat together.

Stark stood behind Fern.

Himmel took a chair near Qifrey.

Ori came last.

Mau saw her.

The room changed inside him.

Not because she was a problem.

Because he cared whether this hurt.

Ori noticed.

She sat anyway.

"I'm okay."

Mau looked at her.

Ori added, "Not perfectly."

He nodded.

That was more honest.

Rimuru stood.

"We need Frieren out of the house."

"Why?" Bocchi asked.

"So we can prepare."

Umaru raised a hand.

"I have an idea."

Everyone became concerned.

Rimuru pointed at Mau.

"What does Frieren want enough to leave immediately?"

Mau answered.

"A grimoire."

Fern answered at the same time.

"A grimoire."

Rimuru looked between them.

"Good."

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln01-grimoire -->

The lie was barely a lie.

Rimuru had discretionary money.

There was a bookseller.

Frieren wanted books.

He told Frieren there was enough surplus to buy one grimoire she had been looking at.

Frieren closed her book.

"When?"

"Now, if you want."

She stood.

Mau watched.

Frieren looked at him.

"Do you need anything?"

"No."

She narrowed her eyes.

Mau had become better at lying since Sukuna.

That was unfortunately suspicious.

"I need... soap."

Frieren stared.

Fern stepped in.

"I need paper."

Frieren looked at Fern.

Then Mau.

Then Rimuru.

"Fine."

She took the money and left.

The front door closed.

Silence.

Then Marin stood.

"Okay."

Momo stood too.

"Operation Girlfriend."

Nano said, "That name is inefficient."

Karane said, "The name is not the problem!"

Hakari clasped her hands.

"This is so romantic."

Mau looked at Yuta.

Yuta smiled.

"You're on your own."

Maki hit his shoulder.

"No, he's not."

That was the point.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln01-where -->

Ideas became worse quickly.

Candles.

Too many flowers.

A formal dinner before the question.

A dramatic entrance.

Music.

Fireworks, somehow.

Mau listened until Rimuru asked:

"What do you actually want?"

The room quieted.

Mau thought.

There was one answer.

"Where I woke up."

Frieren's face above him.

White hair against another sky.

The first person in his remembered life.

"The clearing."

Momo stopped designing an arch with her hands.

Marin's expression softened.

Mau continued.

"I don't want it to look different."

"Then why decorate?" Nano asked.

Mau looked at her.

"I don't know."

"Excellent. We have eliminated unnecessary requirements."

Marin looked wounded.

Momo looked more wounded.

Mau added, "Some flowers would be okay."

Both recovered instantly.

Himmel smiled.

"The place should still be the place."

Mau nodded.

That was exactly it.

Where his remembered life began.

Where he would choose what came next.
