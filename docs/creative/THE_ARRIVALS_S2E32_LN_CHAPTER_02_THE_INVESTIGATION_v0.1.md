<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln02-advice -->

Mau learned that asking twenty people for romantic advice was worse than asking none.

Hakari wanted escalation.

"Tell her everything."

Karane's face was red.

"Not everything!"

"What does that mean?" Mau asked.

Karane became more red.

Nano intervened.

"State three things clearly. Feeling. Intention. Question."

Shizuka held up a small book and pointed to a marked line.

Mau read it.

Then looked at her.

"That's good."

Shizuka smiled.

Yuta leaned back in his chair.

"You don't need a speech."

Maki nodded.

"Especially not one someone else wrote."

Mau looked at the notes Momo had already produced.

Momo covered them.

"These are backup."

Himmel had been quiet.

Mau looked at him.

"What do you think?"

The room became subtly more attentive.

Himmel smiled.

"Ask the question you actually want answered."

Simple.

No bitterness.

No hidden claim.

Mau nodded.

"Okay."

Stark looked between them and exhaled like he had expected the room to become much worse.

It did not.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln02-detectives -->

Frieren returned with a grimoire.

This was when the detectives struck.

Bocchi wore a dark improvised coat Wakana had altered.

Umaru wore a cap with the brim low.

Coco had somehow acquired a notebook she did not need.

They blocked the hallway.

Frieren stopped.

"What are you doing?"

Umaru lowered her voice.

"We have questions."

Bocchi tried to lower hers.

It disappeared completely.

Coco said, "We're investigating whether you're good enough for Mau."

Frieren looked at all three.

Then past them.

Fern stood farther down the hall with the expression of someone who had agreed to supervise a natural disaster.

Frieren looked back.

"Okay."

Umaru had not expected cooperation.

She checked her list.

"What's Mau's worst habit?"

Frieren answered immediately.

"He decides something is his responsibility before anyone agrees."

The three detectives became quieter.

Coco wrote it down.

Bocchi asked the next question.

"What... do you like about him?"

Frieren looked at her.

Bocchi almost escaped.

Frieren thought.

"He notices things."

Bocchi stayed.

"He doesn't always understand them."

Umaru nodded.

"True."

"He tries anyway."

Coco wrote that too.

Umaru asked, "What do you want to do with him in the future?"

Frieren's eyes shifted.

For once, the question caught her.

She looked toward the window.

The future-home site existed on a map downstairs.

There were roads they had not walked.

Books she had not found.

Meals they had not ruined.

People who had not arrived yet.

"Everything we're already doing."

Umaru waited.

Frieren added:

"Longer."

Nobody joked.

Bocchi's eyes went wet.

Umaru saw and immediately returned to the bit.

"Final question."

Frieren looked at her.

"Do you know we love him too?"

Frieren's expression changed.

"Yes."

"Good."

Umaru closed the notebook.

"Investigation complete."

Coco raised a hand.

"What's the result?"

Umaru stared at Frieren.

Then at Bocchi.

"Acceptable."

Frieren nodded.

"Okay."

She walked past them.

Bocchi collapsed against the wall.

"That was awful."

"You did great," Coco said.

"I almost died."

"That's not great," Coco corrected.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln02-fern -->

Fern intercepted Frieren before she could disappear into the grimoire.

"Come with me."

Frieren looked at the book.

"No."

"Yes."

"I just bought this."

"I know."

Fern took the book.

Frieren stared.

"Fern."

"You can have it back later."

"Why?"

Fern looked at her.

Because Mau wants to choose you properly.

Because everyone downstairs is losing their minds.

Because I once thought I lost you and now I get to help you walk into a future I never imagined.

Fern said:

"Your hair."

Frieren touched it.

"What about it?"

"Come upstairs."

Frieren followed, suspicious.

That was enough.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln02-clothes -->

Wakana had not made Frieren a costume.

That would have been wrong.

He had adjusted one of her existing nicer outfits.

Small things.

Fit.

A repaired edge.

A cleaner fall to the fabric.

Something that still looked entirely like Frieren.

Fern helped with her hair.

Frieren watched in the mirror.

"Is this necessary?"

"No."

"Then why?"

Fern's hands slowed.

"Because I want to."

Frieren looked at her reflection.

Then at Fern.

"Okay."

Fern continued.

No speech.

No tears.

Not yet.

When Frieren went downstairs, the grimoire was waiting on the table.

Beside it sat Mau's McLaren cap.

She picked up the cap.

Fern said, "No."

Frieren looked at her.

"Why?"

"Not tonight."

Frieren considered rebellion.

Then left it.

Mau would later learn this had been the hardest part of the entire plan.
