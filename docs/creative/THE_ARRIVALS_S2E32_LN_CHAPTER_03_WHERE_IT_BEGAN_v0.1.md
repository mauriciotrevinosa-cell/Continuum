<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln03-clearing -->

The clearing was smaller than Mau remembered.

That happened to places that became important.

In memory, the sky had filled everything.

Frieren had filled the rest.

Now there were trees he recognized.

A stone he had sat on later.

A path worn faintly by people returning to a place that had once been random.

Marin and Momo had listened.

Mostly.

There were flowers.

Not enough to hide the grass.

Soft light from Qifrey's work sat low between branches and looked almost natural.

Nothing blocked the view of the sky.

Mau stood where he thought he had first opened his eyes.

He was wrong by several feet.

Frieren corrected him.

"It was there."

Mau looked.

"Are you sure?"

"Yes."

"You remember exactly?"

Frieren stared at him.

"You were unconscious."

"That's fair."

They moved.

Behind the trees, an unreasonable number of people attempted not to exist.

Mau heard someone whisper.

Then someone else hit them.

He looked toward the sound.

Frieren said, "Don't."

Mau looked at her.

"You know they're there."

"Yes."

"How many?"

Frieren glanced toward three separate hiding places.

"Most of them."

Mau closed his eyes.

"I asked for privacy."

"They're far away."

"They're behind that tree."

"That's farther away than here."

Mau laughed.

The tension broke.

Good.

He needed that.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln03-words -->

Frieren looked at the flowers.

"Why here?"

Mau looked at her.

The answer was easy.

"This is where I started."

Frieren's expression softened.

"My remembered life."

He touched his chest once.

"I don't know what came before."

The Noise existed.

The blank history existed.

Questions remained.

Tonight was not about solving them.

"But I know this."

Frieren waited.

Mau had received advice from too many people.

None of it mattered now.

Feeling.

Intention.

Question.

Nano had been right.

"I love you."

Frieren stopped breathing.

The words were different when they came from him.

Not inferred from a battlefield.

Not contained in a promise.

Not obvious from a room they shared.

Said.

"I love you romantically."

Frieren's eyes became wet.

Mau continued.

"I want to be with you."

She was already smiling.

Barely.

"Will you be my girlfriend?"

Frieren stared.

Then:

"I thought I already was."

Behind one tree, someone made a strangled noise.

Mau ignored it.

"I know."

Frieren tilted her head.

"Then why are you asking?"

"Because I wanted to ask you."

That answer reached her.

Not because the relationship needed permission to become real.

Because the choice deserved language.

Frieren stepped closer.

"Then yes."

Mau swallowed.

Frieren repeated it.

"Yes. I want to be your girlfriend."

There.

Mutual.

Explicit.

Chosen.

Mau smiled.

Frieren touched his face.

Then kissed him.

This time neither of them was dying.

That difference contained an entire season.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln03-family -->

The forest exploded.

Not literally.

Rimuru had forbidden that.

People emerged from impossible places.

Momo first.

Marin immediately behind her.

Bocchi and Umaru from the same tree despite there not being enough tree.

Coco ran past both.

Stark appeared from somewhere Mau was certain had no cover.

Himmel walked normally because he had given up pretending.

Fern came last.

Frieren looked at her.

Fern looked at Frieren.

For one second the rest of the family blurred.

Fern smiled.

Not big.

Enough.

Frieren crossed the distance and hugged her.

Fern froze.

Then hugged back.

Mau watched.

Himmel stood beside him.

"Good question," Himmel said.

Mau looked at him.

"Thanks."

No rivalry.

No transfer.

A man who loved Frieren had helped another man ask Frieren what she wanted.

That was all.

And it was enormous.
