The walk home took twice as long because nobody stopped talking.

Momo claimed credit.

Marin claimed aesthetic credit.

Nano disputed both claims on the basis that the actual question had contained none of their suggestions.

Hakari argued that emotional atmosphere was a measurable contribution.

Karane argued with Hakari for reasons that changed three times.

Coco asked Frieren what being a girlfriend felt like.

Frieren said:

"Mostly the same."

Mau looked at her.

"Mostly?"

Frieren looked back.

Mau smiled.

"Okay."

Bocchi walked beside Kita holding hands.

Kita leaned toward her.

"They really weren't official."

Bocchi whispered, "I told you."

"I thought you were confused."

"I was."

"Fair."

Ori walked with Anko.

Not hidden.

Not at the back.

When Mau looked toward her, Ori saw.

The ache came.

Then passed.

She raised one hand.

Mau raised his.

No rescue required.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln04-table -->

Dinner was too large.

Of course it was.

The table could not hold everyone cleanly anymore, so they extended it with smaller tables and one surface Rimuru insisted was not technically furniture.

Food came from everyone.

Good food.

Bad food.

Something Stark made that disappeared too quickly for him to get enough.

He looked down at his plate.

Blink.

Empty.

Stark stared.

Momo was chewing.

Stark pointed.

Momo looked innocent.

Old joke.

New room.

Same family.

Frieren sat beside Mau.

Officially.

This changed nothing about the chair arrangement.

Umaru complained.

"That's disappointing."

"What?" Mau asked.

"I expected girlfriend status to have visible effects."

Frieren took food from Mau's plate.

Umaru pointed.

"There."

Mau looked down.

"She already did that."

"Then you were pre-girlfriending."

Mau considered the term.

"No."

"Yes."

Fern covered her face.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln04-ori -->

Ori nearly left early.

Frieren noticed.

She reached across the table.

Not to stop her.

To pass a dish.

"Ori."

Ori looked.

Frieren held it out.

"Do you want this?"

Ordinary.

No special treatment.

Ori took the dish.

"Yes."

She stayed.

Later Coco pulled Ori into an argument.

Then Wakana asked her something.

Then Maomao needed help.

By the time Ori remembered she had considered leaving, dessert had started.

That was enough.

<!-- scene-id: s2e32-ln04-after -->

The house quieted late.

Mau and Frieren ended up alone in their room.

Not arranged.

Normal.

Frieren sat on the bed removing one small hair piece Fern had used.

Mau put his watch on the table.

Frieren looked at him.

"My boyfriend."

Mau stopped.

The word sounded strange.

"Yes."

Frieren smiled.

Mau sat beside her.

"My girlfriend."

Frieren leaned against him.

"Yes."

They stayed like that.

No new revelation.

No crisis.

No promise larger than the ones already made.

Downstairs, the future-home map remained on the table.

Tomorrow the family would wake up in the same inn.

For one more day.
