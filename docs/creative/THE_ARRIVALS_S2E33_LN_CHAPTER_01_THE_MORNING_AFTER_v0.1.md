<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln01-breakfast -->

Being official changed breakfast in three measurable ways.

First, Momo would not stop saying girlfriend.

Second, Umaru had begun saying boyfriend whenever Mau entered a room.

Third, Frieren seemed to enjoy both enough not to stop them.

Nothing else changed.

Mau woke with Frieren's hair in his face.

Frieren wore his McLaren hoodie downstairs.

Mau put on the Ferrari shirt.

Fern saw them.

Paused.

"Of course."

Mau looked at his clothes.

"What?"

"Nothing."

At breakfast, Frieren stole food from his plate.

Umaru pointed.

"Girlfriend behavior."

Mau said, "She did that yesterday."

"Retroactive girlfriend behavior."

"That's not how time works."

Frieren continued eating.

Momo nodded solemnly.

"It is now."

The first hour of their official relationship was indistinguishable from the previous months except that everyone had vocabulary for annoying them.

Mau liked it.

He did not say so.

Frieren knew anyway.

<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln01-leftovers -->

The house spent the morning cleaning up the celebration.

Lanterns came down.

Extra tables moved back.

Flowers from the clearing were not brought home; Mau had insisted they remain where they were.

Marin objected to taking down one decoration because it looked good.

Rimuru objected to keeping it because it blocked storage.

Marin won.

Temporarily.

Wakana repaired a sleeve.

Qifrey studied.

Coco interrupted.

Agott interrupted Coco's interruption.

Maomao sorted leftovers according to whether they would remain edible.

Stark guarded his plate.

Momo failed to steal from it.

Stark looked proud.

Himmel stole one piece instead.

Stark stared at him.

Himmel smiled.

"Always watch the hero."

Stark looked betrayed.

Fern laughed.

Old family moved through new family without needing separate scenes anymore.

<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln01-ori -->

Ori spent part of the morning with Frieren.

They read.

Different books.

Same room.

Mau entered once to find something.

Ori's body still reacted before her thoughts did.

A small tightening.

Mau saw.

Did not turn it into a conversation.

He found the item.

Frieren said, "We're using this room."

Mau looked at her.

"It's our room."

Frieren looked at Ori.

Ori looked at Frieren.

Then laughed.

Mau understood he had lost whatever argument existed.

He left.

Ori looked down at the page.

"That still hurts a little."

Frieren turned a page.

"I know."

Ori waited.

Frieren added:

"You can still stay."

Ori's eyes softened.

She stayed.

<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln01-afternoon -->

In the afternoon, almost everyone did something unnecessary.

Cards.

Music.

Reading.

A walk.

Sewing.

A game whose rules had stopped resembling the original rules.

Mikasa lost deliberately once.

Anko noticed.

Said nothing.

Bocchi played while Kita sat nearby.

Momo and Okarun argued naturally enough that nobody tracked whether the familiarity came from recovered memory or new time anymore.

Yuta and Maki disappeared for a while.

Coco fell asleep against Qifrey.

Umaru fell asleep somewhere else and denied it afterward.

Mau repaired nothing.

That was unusual.

Frieren found him outside sitting on the steps.

"No work?"

"No."

"Why?"

Mau looked at the house.

"One more day."

Frieren sat beside him.

Neither explained.

The inn had earned their attention.
