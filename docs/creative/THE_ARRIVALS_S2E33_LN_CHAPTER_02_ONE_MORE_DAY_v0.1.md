<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln02-evening -->

Evening arrived without permission.

Mau noticed because the common room changed color.

Sun through old glass.

Dust.

The table full of things people had not put away.

A book.

Thread.

A half-finished drawing.

Three cups.

The future-home map.

It had been there long enough to stop looking like a decision.

Now it looked like work waiting for them.

Road.

Water.

First structures.

Storage.

Workshop.

Medical room.

Music room.

Arrival space.

Room.

Enough room.

Mau read the words.

A year ago—if the calendar they had built meant anything—he had woken with none of it.

No name.

No memory.

No proof that anyone in the world knew him.

Now the map was difficult to see because someone's mug sat on top of the eastern edge.

Mau moved the mug.

Then stopped.

Why?

The map did not need to be visible tonight.

He put the mug back.

<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln02-frieren -->

Frieren entered.

She carried two books.

One hers.

One Ori's.

Mau looked at them.

"Borrowing books now?"

"She borrowed mine first."

"So you borrowed hers."

"Yes."

"That's good."

Frieren put them down.

Her eyes moved to the map.

"We should make the bedroom window face east."

Mau looked at her.

"You said you didn't care."

"I changed my mind."

"Why east?"

"Morning light."

"You don't like waking up early."

"I didn't say I'd be awake."

Mau laughed.

Frieren stood beside him.

Their shoulders touched.

"Do you think it'll work?" Mau asked.

"The house?"

"Everything."

Frieren thought.

"No."

Mau looked at her.

She continued.

"Not all of it."

That answer was better.

"Some things will go wrong."

"Yes."

"People will keep arriving."

"Probably."

"We might have to leave again someday."

"Maybe."

Mau looked at the map.

Frieren took his hand.

"We'll still be there."

Not the building.

Them.

Mau squeezed her hand.

"Okay."

<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln02-call -->

From the other room, Momo shouted:

"WE'RE STARTING."

Mau looked toward the sound.

"What?"

Umaru shouted back:

"YOU SAID YOU'D WATCH WITH US."

"I didn't."

"YOU WERE PRESENT WHEN WE DECIDED."

"That's not agreement."

Coco shouted:

"THAT COUNTS."

Frieren picked up one of the books.

Mau looked at her.

"Are you coming?"

"Yes."

"You were going to read."

"I can do both."

"Right."

They left the table.

The map remained.

The mug remained on top of it.

No one moved either.

<!-- scene-id: s2e33-ln02-home -->

The common room was too full.

Still.

People sat on chairs.

Benches.

Floor.

Each other, occasionally.

Rimuru became a slime because it solved geometry.

Stark guarded his food.

Mikasa did not choose the seat nearest an exit.

Ori sat with Frieren on one side and Wakana on the other.

Bocchi sat with Kita.

Momo sat close enough to Okarun that neither seemed to notice.

Yuta leaned against Maki.

Fern complained about everyone blocking the view.

Himmel moved.

Fern said she had not meant him specifically.

He moved back.

The argument began.

Mau stopped in the doorway.

Seventeen became more.

Then more again.

The number had stopped being the point a long time ago.

Frieren tugged his hand.

"Sit."

Mau sat.

Someone complained.

Someone laughed.

Something started playing.

Outside, the old settlement remained broken.

Beyond it, somewhere they had chosen, there was land waiting for foundations.

That was tomorrow.

Tonight the future stayed on paper.

Mau looked around the room.

Home had never been the building.

He knew that now.

Season Two ended before anyone stood up.
