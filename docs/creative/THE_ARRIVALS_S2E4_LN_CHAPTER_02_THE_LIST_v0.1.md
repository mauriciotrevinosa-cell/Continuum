When the rain stopped, everyone wanted something.

This became Mau's problem because he made the mistake of asking.

"Do we need anything from town?"

Eight voices answered.

Mau put a blank page on the table.

"One at a time."

Momo raised both hands.

"No."

"You don't even know what I want."

"That's why."

Anko went first.

Ink.

Paper.

Binding thread if the price was reasonable.

Maomao added two ingredients Mau could pronounce and one he could not.

Momo added sweets.

Maomao crossed them out.

Momo added them again.

Fern wanted lamp oil and soap.

Yuta needed replacement wrapping for a sword grip.

Rimuru wanted three things that were technically household supplies and therefore did not count as personal requests.

Frieren put four grimoire titles on the page.

Mau stared.

"No."

Frieren stared back.

"You asked what we need."

"We don't need four grimoires."

"You don't know that."

"Pick one."

Frieren looked offended.

"Two."

"One."

"Two."

"One and if it's cheap, maybe a second."

Frieren considered the victory conditions.

"Fine."

Bocchi sat at the corner of the table.

Mau looked at her.

"Anything?"

She shook her head.

"Strings?"

Bocchi looked at the guitar.

Then at him.

"...Maybe."

Mau added them.

<!-- scene-id: s2e4-ln02-bocchi-volunteers -->

The list was longer than the trip should have been.

Mau folded it.

"I'll go tomorrow."

"I can come."

Everyone looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi immediately regretted existing.

Her face changed color.

She looked at the table.

Then, somehow, kept going.

"You carry... a lot."

Mau looked at the paper.

"It's not that much."

Fern looked at him.

"It is."

Bocchi swallowed.

"I can help."

Mau did not answer immediately.

Not because he did not want her there.

Because he knew what saying yes meant.

Bocchi had arrived at the inn barely able to ask for space at a table.

Now she was volunteering for a town trip because she had noticed someone else carrying too much.

"No pressure," Mau said.

Bocchi nodded too fast.

"I know."

"You can change your mind tomorrow."

"I know."

"We can turn around."

"I know."

Momo leaned toward Anko.

"He's doing the thing."

Anko whispered back.

"What thing?"

"The careful thing."

"I can hear you," Mau said.

"Then it's working."

Bocchi laughed.

Small.

Real.

Fern watched her.

Then looked at Mau.

"Take the shorter road."

"I was going to."

"Don't stay after you finish."

"I wasn't going to."

"Keep the list."

Mau held it up.

Fern's expression did not change.

Mau lowered it.

"Anything else?"

"Bring her back."

Bocchi went still.

Fern looked at her.

The words were simple.

The meaning was not.

Bocchi's shoulders softened.

Mau nodded.

"I will."

<!-- scene-id: s2e4-ln02-night-before -->

That night, the inn dried out.

Windows opened.

Blankets aired.

The temporary rack stayed because nobody wanted to admit Rimuru had been right about it.

Mau packed the empty bags for town.

His McLaren cap ended up on Bocchi's head because Momo put it there while Bocchi was trying to decide whether she needed a hood.

Bocchi froze.

Momo stepped back.

"Actually, that's kind of good."

Mau looked over.

"That's mine."

Frieren turned a page.

"You say that often."

Bocchi reached for the cap.

"Sorry—"

Mau stopped her.

"You can use it."

Bocchi looked at him.

"Really?"

"Yes."

Momo nodded with the authority of someone who had arranged the entire matter.

"Perfect."

Mau looked at Frieren.

"This is spreading."

Frieren did not look up.

"Mostly."

The next morning would be ordinary.

Everyone believed that.

There was no reason not to.
