<!-- scene-id: s2e5-ln03-treatment -->

Maomao was terrifying when the injury was minor.

Mau learned this because she was calm.

"Sit."

Bocchi sat.

"Not you."

Mau stopped beside the chair.

Maomao pointed at the other side of the room.

"Over there."

"I'm helping."

"You're vibrating."

Mau looked down.

"I am not."

Fern took his arm.

"You are."

"I don't think that's possible."

"Move."

Mau moved.

Bocchi sat at the table while Maomao cleaned the cut.

The blood looked worse once mixed with water.

Mau hated this.

Bocchi hated that he hated it.

"It doesn't hurt that much."

Maomao glanced at her.

"Do not perform reassurance during medical assessment."

Bocchi became silent.

Momo hovered behind Anko.

Anko hovered behind nobody and therefore had no defense when Momo grabbed her sleeve.

Yuta stood near the doorway.

Rimuru had already gone outside to make sure no one had followed them.

Frieren entered from upstairs.

She saw Bocchi.

Stopped.

Then looked at Mau's blood-stained shoulder.

Her eyes changed.

"Mau."

"It's hers."

That was the wrong first sentence.

Frieren crossed the room.

Not to Mau.

To Bocchi.

She crouched beside the chair.

Bocchi looked at her.

"I'm okay."

Frieren's gaze moved to Maomao.

"Is she?"

"Probably."

Mau's breath finally moved.

Maomao looked at him.

"I said probably."

It stopped again.

"Head wounds bleed dramatically. The cut is not deep. I am checking everything else."

She did.

Pupils.

Balance.

Questions.

Pain.

Nausea.

Memory.

Bocchi knew her name.

Knew where she was.

Knew who everyone was.

Knew Mau had lost his cap.

Mau closed his eyes.

"That isn't medically relevant."

"It might be to him," Bocchi said.

Maomao wrapped the injury.

The bandage crossed Bocchi's forehead.

White against pink hair.

Too visible.

"She rests tonight," Maomao said. "Wake her if anything changes. No music until tomorrow."

Bocchi looked more upset by the final instruction than the injury.

"Tomorrow?"

"If you remain boring."

Bocchi nodded.

Maomao stepped back.

"Medically, I am not worried."

The room breathed.

No one said how afraid they had been until after it was safe to say they should not have been.

<!-- scene-id: s2e5-ln03-mau -->

Mau washed Bocchi's blood out of the Ferrari shirt at the well.

Frieren found him there after dark.

The red fabric was submerged in a basin.

Mau had been rubbing the same section long after the stain stopped changing.

Frieren crouched beside him.

"Maomao said she's fine."

"I know."

"You don't."

Mau stopped.

Frieren looked at the shirt.

Then at him.

"You heard her."

"Yes."

"That's not the same thing."

"No."

He squeezed water from the fabric.

"I should've left when the first person said something."

Frieren's expression flattened.

"Maybe."

Mau looked at her.

He had expected disagreement.

Frieren continued.

"You also could've left before anyone spoke."

"Yes."

"You could stop going anywhere."

Mau looked back at the basin.

"That would be safer."

"For some things."

He waited.

Frieren touched the wet sleeve.

"If you make every decision from the worst thing that happened once, you stop living before anything else happens."

Mau looked at her hand.

"That's something you learned?"

Frieren was quiet.

"Maybe."

The answer was honest enough.

Mau rinsed the shirt again.

Frieren took it from him.

"The stain is gone."

"I know."

"Then stop."

He let go.

Frieren wrung the fabric once and carried it inside.

Mau followed.

<!-- scene-id: s2e5-ln03-sisters -->

Fern stayed with Bocchi that night.

Not because Maomao required it.

Because Bocchi asked without asking.

Fern brought a book.

Bocchi lay under the blanket.

The room was dark except for one lamp.

"You don't have to stay."

"I know."

"I'm fine."

"I know."

Bocchi looked at the ceiling.

Fern turned a page.

Several minutes passed.

"Kita would've yelled."

Fern looked up.

Bocchi's voice was small.

"If she saw me like this."

"Probably."

"She'd say I should've run."

"Would she be right?"

Bocchi thought about it.

"...Maybe."

Fern returned to the book.

Bocchi turned her head.

"Do you think she's okay?"

Fern knew who she meant.

"I don't know."

Bocchi's fingers tightened on the blanket.

Fern closed the book.

"But if she is here somewhere, she's looking for you too."

Bocchi's eyes moved to her.

"You think so?"

"Yes."

The certainty was a gift Fern could give without proof.

Bocchi looked away before her face changed too much.

Fern opened the book again.

After a while, Bocchi slept.

Fern stayed.

Downstairs, the others did not hold a meeting.

They sat.

Nobody wanted to be the first person to turn what happened into policy.

So they waited until morning.
