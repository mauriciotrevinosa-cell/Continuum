<!-- scene-id: s2e6-ln01-three-days -->

Three days after Bocchi came home bleeding, the settlement had strings in the trees.

Not many.

Enough.

Rimuru stood beneath one of them with the expression of someone who knew this looked ridiculous and intended to defend it anyway.

A small metal piece hung from the line.

"If someone walks through here without knowing the route, that moves."

Momo stared.

"And?"

"The bell rings."

"That's it?"

Rimuru looked at her.

"What were you expecting?"

"Lasers."

"No."

"Explosion?"

"No."

"Slime ambush?"

"No."

Momo sighed.

"We've lost ambition."

Yuta tested the line.

It gave.

Not enough to catch anyone.

Enough to warn.

Mau walked the perimeter with Anko, marking which approaches were visible from the inn.

Frieren followed for half of it and then sat on a stone to read.

Mau looked back.

"You're helping."

"I am."

"How?"

"I can see the north path from here."

"You're reading."

"I can do both."

A branch snapped somewhere behind her.

Frieren did not look up.

"Rabbit."

Mau waited.

A rabbit emerged.

He nodded.

"Okay."

<!-- scene-id: s2e6-ln01-logistics -->

The other changes were less visible.

Rimuru bought more at once when trusted merchants came through.

Yuta and Fern took alternating guild routes so the same face did not appear in town every few days.

Maomao increased what could be grown or gathered near the settlement.

Momo complained that foraging was a conspiracy against snacks.

Anko discovered a trader willing to bring ink to the old road junction for a fee.

Bocchi did not go back to town.

No one told her she was forbidden.

That mattered.

The first morning she stood near the door while Yuta packed for an errand, Mau expected her to look relieved when no one asked her to come.

Instead she looked angry with herself.

Fern noticed too.

"Not today," Fern said.

Bocchi looked at her.

"Not never."

Bocchi's shoulders loosened.

Mau stored that sentence.

Not today.

Not never.

The household was learning the difference.

<!-- scene-id: s2e6-ln01-scouting -->

Scouting began without ceremony.

A route north.

A route along the lake.

An old road that became useless after heavy rain.

A ridge with good visibility and terrible water.

A valley that looked defensible until Maomao pointed out everything growing there suggested the soil stayed wet too long.

The future-home map gained marks.

Most were crossed out.

Nobody was discouraged.

That was what searching looked like.

Mau joined one short route with Yuta.

At a rise overlooking the forest, Yuta stopped.

"Still nothing."

Mau knew what he meant.

"Sukuna."

"Yeah."

"Do you want to feel him?"

Yuta's expression changed.

"No."

"Do you want not to?"

Yuta looked out over the trees.

"Also no."

Mau understood.

Information could be terrifying and still be better than absence.

Yuta glanced at him.

"How's your hand?"

Mau flexed the fingers he had used during the Construction event.

"Fine."

"Head?"

"Fine."

"Can you do it again?"

"No."

"Good."

Mau looked at him.

"Why is that good?"

"Because if you said yes, you'd try it on the hill."

"That's fair."

They continued walking.

<!-- scene-id: s2e6-ln01-bocchi -->

Back at the inn, Bocchi's bandage had become smaller.

Maomao had reduced it that morning.

The cut would likely leave a faint mark.

Bocchi looked at it in a mirror for too long.

Momo leaned into the reflection beside her.

"It makes you look dangerous."

Bocchi nearly dropped the mirror.

"No."

"Yes."

"No."

"Like a musician with a tragic past."

"I have a tragic past."

Momo stopped.

Bocchi stopped.

Then, unexpectedly, Bocchi laughed.

Momo laughed too.

Fern heard from the hallway and smiled before entering.

The mark was not a symbol yet.

It was healing skin.

That was enough.
