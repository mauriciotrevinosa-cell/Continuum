<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln01-kita -->

Kita reached Bocchi and stopped half a step too close.

For one second neither of them knew what to do.

Then Kita hugged her.

Bocchi made a noise that contained surprise, relief and immediate concern about where to put her arms.

Eventually she put them around Kita.

That was enough.

"What happened to your head?"

The question came out into Bocchi's shoulder.

"I'm okay."

"That is not what I asked."

"I'm really okay."

Kita pulled back just enough to inspect the bandage.

Her eyes were already wet.

Bocchi saw that and panicked.

"No, no, don't cry, it's not—"

"I'm not crying."

"You are."

"I'm relieved!"

"That still counts."

Kita laughed through it.

Then noticed the bucket hat.

She stared.

Bocchi touched the brim.

"It's Mau's."

Kita looked past her.

Mau raised one hand.

"Hi."

Kita's expression changed again.

Not hostile.

Assessment.

Bocchi noticed.

Mau noticed Bocchi noticing.

He stepped away.

"Fern's inside."

Bocchi understood what he was doing.

Space.

She looked back at Kita.

"Come in."

It was the first time Kita heard Bocchi say those words about this place like she had the right to.

Come in.

Not:

They let me stay here.

Not:

I think it's safe.

Come in.

Kita did.

<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln01-everyone -->

Eight new people crossed the threshold and the inn lost any remaining argument that it was large.

Marin entered talking.

Wakana entered carrying more than Marin because this had apparently been established on the road.

Coco crossed the doorway, stopped dead at the sight of Rimuru in slime form, and made the mistake of inhaling before speaking.

Qifrey caught her gently by the shoulders.

"Later."

"But—"

"Later."

Umaru looked around the common room.

"Is there a private room with a door and no responsibilities?"

Mau looked at her.

"There are doors."

"That was not the whole question."

"Then no."

Umaru stared.

Mau stared back.

Something about his answer satisfied her.

"You're honest."

"Usually."

Mikasa entered last.

She looked at the exits.

The windows.

The stairs.

The distance between strangers.

Yuta noticed.

"You can stop doing that."

Mikasa looked at him.

"No."

Yuta nodded.

"Fair."

They understood each other enough for the moment.

Marin saw Bocchi.

Saw the bandage.

Saw Kita holding Bocchi's sleeve like letting go was not yet an option.

Her voice softened.

"Oh."

Wakana followed her gaze.

Qifrey did too.

The room reoriented around an injury none of them had known existed.

Mau hated that the bandage had become part of the house's first impression.

Bocchi adjusted the bucket hat.

"It's fine."

Maomao appeared from the side room.

"Stop saying that."

Bocchi looked betrayed.

"You said I'm fine."

"I said the injury is healing correctly. Those are different statements."

Coco whispered to Qifrey, "I like her."

Maomao heard.

"That may pass."

<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln01-food -->

The merchant stayed long enough to accept food and refuse money twice.

Rimuru made him take money on the third attempt.

"You're rerouting because of us."

"I'm rerouting because I don't want eight frightened people delivered into a frightened town."

"Same result."

The merchant took the coins.

At the table, seating stopped making sense.

There had been nine.

Now there were seventeen.

They found stools.

Boxes.

One repaired chair nobody trusted.

A section of floor.

Mau sat on the floor voluntarily and Umaru immediately decided that made him safe enough to sit near.

Coco sat close to Qifrey.

Marin sat where she could talk to everyone.

Wakana sat where Marin had left space.

Mikasa chose the seat with the best view of the front door.

Yuta noticed.

Did not comment.

Kita remained beside Bocchi.

Fern sat on Bocchi's other side.

For the first several minutes, conversation failed because there were too many conversations trying to happen.

Then Momo asked Marin about her clothes.

Marin answered.

Coco asked Frieren what kind of magic she used.

Qifrey apologized before Frieren could decide whether she minded.

Umaru asked Mau whether the house had snacks.

Mau pointed at Momo.

"She hides them."

Momo gasped.

"Traitor."

Anko said, "He learned from the best."

Somewhere between one question and the next, the room stopped being silent about the fact that eight strangers had arrived.

It became loud.

That was easier.

<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln01-night-question -->

After dinner, Rimuru stood.

"Sleeping arrangements."

Everyone looked at him.

Rimuru looked at the ceiling.

Then at the seventeen people.

Then back at the ceiling.

"We have a problem."

Momo raised a hand.

"I have a floor."

"Yes."

"Several, actually."

"Thank you."

Mau stood too.

"They can use my room."

Frieren looked at him.

Fern looked at Frieren.

Mau did not notice either look.

"My nominal room," he clarified. "It's mostly books now."

"Where will you sleep?" Marin asked.

Mau pointed upstairs.

"Where I sleep."

Kita followed the gesture.

Then looked at Frieren.

Then at Bocchi.

Bocchi, unfortunately, understood the question on her face.

Her entire face turned red.

Kita whispered, "...Oh."

Bocchi whispered back, "It's not—"

Then stopped because she had no idea what she was trying to deny.

Frieren had already returned to her book.

Mau was discussing floor space with Rimuru.

For everyone who actually lived here, nothing unusual had happened.

Kita looked at Bocchi again.

Bocchi hid under the bucket hat.

The inn had been full for less than an hour.

It already had secrets that were not secrets.
