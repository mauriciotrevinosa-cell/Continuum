<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-aftershock -->

The house did not stop because Momo's world had.

It became quieter for a while.

That was different.

Momo stayed close to Anko.

Okarun stayed far enough away not to turn distance into another question.

Nobody tried to solve them before dinner.

Eight exhausted people still needed water, food, baths, blankets and somewhere to put their things.

The practical needs were almost a mercy.

<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-umarumau -->

Umaru learned Mau's name because Coco asked it first.

She learned Frieren's because Frieren was wearing his hoodie.

This required clarification.

"Wait."

Umaru pointed.

"That's his?"

Frieren looked down.

"Yes."

"Why are you wearing it?"

"It's comfortable."

Umaru looked at Mau.

Mau nodded.

"That's the answer."

Umaru stared at both of them.

"Are you two—"

"No one knows," Momo said.

Fern looked at her.

Momo smiled.

"What?"

Umaru decided the house was interesting.

That helped.

Later, when Mau showed her where blankets were stored, she asked:

"Do you have games?"

"Some."

"Movies?"

"Sort of."

"Internet?"

"No."

Umaru stopped walking.

"No."

Mau looked back.

"No."

"Like no signal?"

"No internet."

Umaru put both hands on her head.

"This world is evil."

Mau waited.

She lowered her hands.

"Do you at least have snacks?"

"Yes."

"Okay."

Crisis postponed.


<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-umaru-cloak -->

Umaru's first problem was not the internet.

It was dirt.

Road dust had worked into everything.

Her hair.

Her sleeves.

The orange hamster cloak she kept pulling closer whenever someone suggested taking it off.

Mau noticed before he knew enough about her to know why it mattered.

Marin found temporary clothes that would fit.

Wakana adjusted one sleeve.

Umaru looked at the bath.

Then at the cloak.

Then at the bath again.

"You can leave it here," Mau said.

Umaru looked at him.

"You're going to touch it?"

"Only if you want me to."

That answer took some of the suspicion out of her face.

She handed it over slowly.

"If something happens to it—"

"I'll be afraid of you."

"Correct."

She went to bathe.

The cloak was road-worn enough that Mau did not need expertise to understand the problem.

He fetched a basin.

Used less soap than Momo suggested.

Ignored Momo when she suggested more.

Rinsed the cloth until the water stopped turning brown.

Then he hung it where the late light and air could reach it without scorching the fabric.

When Umaru came back in borrowed clothes, hair still damp, she stopped.

The cloak was clean.

Not new.

Not magically restored.

Just cared for.

Mau was already carrying blankets somewhere else.

Umaru touched one dry edge.

She looked after him.

Nobody explained the moment to her.

Nobody needed to.


<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-wakana -->

Wakana's first household contribution happened before anyone assigned him one.

A blanket tore.

He fixed it.

Marin found him doing it and immediately told three people.

By midnight, the torn blanket had become:

two blankets;

one coat cuff;

a shirt seam;

Bocchi's guitar strap;

and a request from Rimuru to inspect the bedding they were about to distribute.

Wakana looked at the pile.

Marin looked delighted.

"See? You're already needed."

Wakana became red enough that Maomao glanced over to confirm this was not fever.

Rimuru noticed the quality of the repair.

"Can you teach me what material is worth saving?"

Wakana blinked.

"Of course."

That conversation lasted longer than the introductions had.

Mau passed by carrying two bedrolls.

Wakana stopped him.

"Your sleeve."

Mau looked down.

The Ferrari shirt cuff had a loose thread.

"It's fine."

Three people in the room reacted.

Mau sighed.

"Right."

He took it off later and gave it to Wakana.

Frieren found it on the repair pile the next morning and looked mildly offended that it was unavailable.

Wakana did not understand why.

Marin did.

Marin said nothing.

For almost thirty seconds.

Then she told Momo.


<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-qifrey -->

Qifrey waited until the second cup of tea before asking Frieren about magic.

Coco had waited approximately eight seconds.

"What is mana suppression?"

Frieren looked at her.

Qifrey closed his eyes.

"Coco."

"What?"

"We discussed not interrogating people the first night."

"I asked one question."

"You asked nine before dinner."

Coco looked at Frieren.

"Was that too many?"

Frieren thought.

"No."

Qifrey opened his eyes.

Frieren continued.

"That one was."

Coco deflated.

Mau laughed.

Coco looked at him.

"You do something weird too."

"Yes."

"What?"

"I don't know."

Coco stared.

Mau stared back.

Qifrey's interest sharpened.

"That answer appears to be causing you more trouble than hers."

"It usually does."

Frieren's book lowered slightly.

Qifrey noticed the way she watched Mau.

Not possessive.

Not protective in the simple sense.

Attentive.

A person used to monitoring a mystery because the mystery had become part of her daily life.

Qifrey knew that look.

He did not ask more tonight.


<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-food -->

The merchant stayed long enough to accept food and refuse money twice.

Rimuru made him take money on the third attempt.

"You're rerouting because of us."

"I'm rerouting because I don't want eight frightened people delivered into a frightened town."

"Same result."

The merchant took the coins.

At the table, seating stopped making sense.

There had been nine.

Now there were seventeen.

They found stools.

Boxes.

One repaired chair nobody trusted.

A section of floor.

Mau sat on the floor voluntarily and Umaru immediately decided that made him safe enough to sit near.

Coco sat close to Qifrey.

Marin sat where she could talk to everyone.

Wakana sat where Marin had left space.

Mikasa chose the seat with the best view of the front door.

Yuta noticed.

Did not comment.

Kita remained beside Bocchi.

Fern sat on Bocchi's other side.

For the first several minutes, conversation failed because there were too many conversations trying to happen.

Then Momo asked Marin about her clothes.

Marin answered.

Coco asked Frieren what kind of magic she used.

Qifrey apologized before Frieren could decide whether she minded.

Umaru asked Mau whether the house had snacks.

Mau pointed at Momo.

"She hides them."

Momo gasped.

"Traitor."

Anko said, "He learned from the best."

Somewhere between one question and the next, the room stopped being silent about the fact that eight strangers had arrived.

It became loud.

That was easier.


<!-- scene-id: s2e7-ln03-night-question -->

After dinner, Rimuru stood.

"Sleeping arrangements."

Everyone looked at him.

Rimuru looked at the ceiling.

Then at the seventeen people.

Then back at the ceiling.

"We have a problem."

Momo raised a hand.

"I have a floor."

"Yes."

"Several, actually."

"Thank you."

Mau stood too.

"They can use my room."

Frieren looked at him.

Fern looked at Frieren.

Mau did not notice either look.

"My nominal room," he clarified. "It's mostly books now."

"Where will you sleep?" Marin asked.

Mau pointed upstairs.

"Where I sleep."

Kita followed the gesture.

Then looked at Frieren.

Then at Bocchi.

Bocchi, unfortunately, understood the question on her face.

Her entire face turned red.

Kita whispered, "...Oh."

Bocchi whispered back, "It's not—"

Then stopped because she had no idea what she was trying to deny.

Frieren had already returned to her book.

Mau was discussing floor space with Rimuru.

For everyone who actually lived here, nothing unusual had happened.

Kita looked at Bocchi again.

Bocchi hid under the bucket hat.

The inn had been full for less than an hour.

It already had secrets that were not secrets.
