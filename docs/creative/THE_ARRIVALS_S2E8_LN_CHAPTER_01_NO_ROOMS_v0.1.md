<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln01-morning -->

Seventeen people required seventeen mornings.

The inn had one.

At dawn, the bath became a political institution.

The kitchen became worse.

Rimuru discovered three people attempting breakfast, two attempting tea, Maomao attempting medicine and Coco asking why a pan was shaped differently from the ones in her world.

"Out."

Nobody moved.

Rimuru pointed.

"Half of you. Out."

Marin looked offended.

"I'm helping."

"You're standing in the doorway."

"Emotional support."

"Out."

Mau entered.

Rimuru pointed at him too.

"I just got here."

"Good. You have less invested."

Mau left.

In the hallway, Umaru was sitting on the floor wrapped in a blanket.

"Kitchen rejected you?"

"Yes."

"Same."

They waited.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln01-room-count -->

By noon, Rimuru had a measuring cord again.

Momo objected on principle.

"No."

"We did this before."

"Exactly."

"This time there are seventeen people."

"Still feels hostile."

They counted usable rooms.

Then counted people.

Then stopped pretending arithmetic might improve.

Mau's nominal room went to Wakana and Qifrey's overflow materials because it was dry and had enough floor space for two bedrolls.

Mau did not hesitate.

"Use it."

Qifrey looked at him.

"Where will you work?"

"Somewhere else."

"Where will you sleep?"

Mau looked toward the room he already shared with Frieren.

Qifrey followed his gaze.

"Ah."

Frieren walked past.

"Mau's things are already in my room."

Mau looked at her.

"Most of them."

Frieren looked at him.

"Mostly."

Qifrey decided not to ask.

The phrase apparently had history.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln01-coco -->

Coco found Mau half-asleep against the wall that afternoon.

Not fully asleep.

The kind where his head dropped once and he woke immediately.

"You should sleep."

Mau looked at her.

"That's good advice."

"Are you going to do it?"

"No."

Coco frowned.

"Then it's bad advice."

Mau considered this.

"That's not how advice works."

"It should be."

She sat beside him.

After a minute, she leaned against his shoulder.

Mau looked down.

Coco was already asleep.

He did not move for forty minutes.

Umaru found them.

Sat on the other side.

Mau looked at her.

"Really?"

"You're already trapped."

That was apparently the beginning of something.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln01-giving-space -->

Fern offered her room next.

Bocchi did too, though the words became difficult halfway through and Kita had to wait patiently while she finished.

"You don't have to," Kita said.

"I know."

Bocchi stared at her hands.

"But there are... too many people."

Kita smiled.

"That's true."

Bocchi's room became temporary space for Kita and Umaru.

Bocchi moved her guitar carefully.

Umaru watched.

"You're leaving because we're here."

"No."

Bocchi stopped.

"Yes. But not—bad."

Umaru understood enough.

"I can sleep in the common room."

Bocchi shook her head.

"No."

The answer came faster than the explanation.

Umaru looked at her.

Bocchi looked away.

"You should have a door."

That was all.

Umaru did not joke.

"Thanks."

Bocchi carried her guitar out.

The room was no longer hers for a while.

It still felt like hers.

That distinction was new.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln01-frieren -->

At night, Mau moved the last stack of notes out of his nominal room.

Frieren watched him bring them into theirs.

He put the notes beside her books.

Then stood.

"Too much?"

Frieren looked at the stack.

Then at him.

"No."

"You didn't check."

"I know what's already in here."

Mau looked around.

The room had stopped having sides months ago.

Her books.

His clothes.

Her staff.

His watch.

The plant by the window.

The Ferrari shirt Wakana had repaired.

The McLaren hoodie currently on Frieren.

Everything mixed.

"Okay."

Mau put his backpack under the chair.

Frieren returned to reading.

For both of them, accepting that Mau would sleep here full-time again was not a decision.

It was logistics.

That made it more intimate than a decision would have been.
