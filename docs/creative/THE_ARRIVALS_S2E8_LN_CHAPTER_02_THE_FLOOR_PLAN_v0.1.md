<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln02-measuring -->

The inn changed because seventeen people kept walking into the same corners.

Rimuru measured.

Wakana measured fabric.

Marin measured nothing and had opinions anyway.

"This room is depressing."

"It's storage," Rimuru said.

"It can be storage and depressing."

"That is not a construction problem."

"It is absolutely a construction problem."

Mikasa carried a damaged beam past them.

Marin stepped aside.

"See? She agrees."

Mikasa did not.

Yuta followed with the other end.

"We need the hallway clear."

Marin moved.

The argument continued from farther away.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln02-wakana -->

Wakana discovered the household's wardrobe the way an archaeologist might discover a civilization after a storm.

Layers.

Repairs.

Borrowed pieces.

Things that clearly belonged to one person and were consistently worn by another.

Clothes from several worlds.

Local replacements.

Patches made by Rimuru.

A coat Bocchi had repaired badly enough that the repair itself had become part of it.

Mau's modern shirts.

Frieren's travel clothing.

Winter pieces.

Sleepwear.

The McLaren hoodie.

Wakana held it up.

"Whose is this?"

Mau answered.

"Mine."

Frieren answered at the same time.

"Mau's."

Wakana looked between them.

Frieren was wearing the Ferrari shirt.

Marin put both hands over her mouth.

Wakana slowly lowered the hoodie.

"I see."

"You don't," Mau said.

"I think he does," Marin said.

Frieren looked down at the Ferrari shirt as if checking whether there was hidden information.

"There isn't a problem."

"No," Wakana said quickly. "I can reinforce the cuff."

Mau looked at Frieren.

"See?"

Frieren handed over the hoodie.

Mau watched it leave.

"You're going to give it back after?"

Frieren looked at him.

"No."

Wakana's face became red.

Marin had to leave the room.

The first new clothing pieces Wakana produced were not fashion.

They were practical.

Liners.

Adjusted sleeves.

Repaired closures.

Warm layers cut from salvaged fabric.

A better-fitting shirt for someone whose old clothes did not match this world's weather.

The wardrobe of the house began expanding because someone finally knew how to make cloth behave on purpose.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln02-first-room -->

The first additional usable sleeping room took four days.

Not because Rimuru was slow.

Because the wall was worse than it looked.

The floor had to be reinforced.

The window needed a frame.

The roof leaked at one corner.

Every solved problem found another problem behind it.

G3 helped.

That mattered.

Mikasa and Yuta moved heavy material.

Okarun worked with Mau on smaller repairs.

Qifrey and Coco became interested in why local plaster behaved differently.

Rimuru banned magical experimentation inside the wall.

Coco asked why.

"Because I enjoy having a wall."

Wakana repaired bedding.

Marin made the room look less like punishment.

Kita painted nothing, because paint was expensive, but found a way to arrange the existing cloth and furniture so the space looked intentional.

Bocchi sat in the doorway with her guitar and played while everyone worked.

No one asked her to stop.

The first night the room became usable, nobody gave a speech.

Rimuru crossed one item off the list.

That was enough.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln02-storage -->

Storage became the next crisis.

Seventeen people owned objects.

This was apparently a surprise.

Coco had materials.

Qifrey had materials.

Wakana had tools.

Marin had clothes.

Umaru had fewer things but somehow occupied more surface area.

Mikasa owned almost nothing and still managed to keep each item positioned with military precision.

Mau's belongings remained distributed across three rooms despite theoretically belonging to one person.

Rimuru looked at the hallway.

"Tomorrow, shelves."

Momo groaned.

"We just finished a room."

"Tomorrow, shelves."

"Dictator."

"Yes."

The house stretched.

Not elegantly.

Not quickly.

Enough.
