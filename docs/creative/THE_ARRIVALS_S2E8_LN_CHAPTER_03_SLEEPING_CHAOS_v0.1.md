<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln03-night-one -->

The first night of temporary sleeping arrangements lasted until three in the morning.

Nobody slept.

This was not entirely because of overcrowding.

Marin talked.

Umaru complained.

Coco asked questions.

Momo answered questions she had not been asked.

Rimuru became a slime in self-defense.

The largest usable upstairs room held:

Frieren.

Mau.

Fern.

Bocchi.

Umaru.

And, eventually, Rimuru.

This arrangement had happened because everyone involved had surrendered their rooms first.

Nobody had planned the result.

Mau lay near the wall.

Frieren lay beside him because that was where she normally slept.

Fern occupied the other side with the calm of someone who had decided embarrassment was inefficient.

Bocchi was near Fern.

Umaru had taken one look at the arrangement and said:

"This is insane."

Then joined.

Rimuru entered last.

"I take almost no space."

Umaru looked at slime-Rimuru.

"Correct."

Five minutes later, she was using him as a cushion.

Rimuru objected.

Umaru was already asleep.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln03-kita -->

Kita found them in the morning.

She had come to wake Bocchi.

She opened the door.

Stopped.

Bocchi was asleep with one arm over her face.

Fern was reading.

Mau was awake but trapped because Frieren's head rested against his shoulder.

Umaru had somehow rotated ninety degrees.

Rimuru remained a pillow.

Kita stared.

Fern looked up.

"Morning."

Kita looked at Bocchi.

Then Mau.

Then Frieren.

Then slime-Rimuru.

"...Morning."

Bocchi woke.

Saw Kita.

Saw where everyone was.

Bocchi's soul left her body.

"Kita-chan."

"It's okay."

"It's not—"

"I didn't say anything."

"Your face did."

Kita tried to fix her face.

This made it worse.

Mau whispered to Frieren, "Can you move?"

Frieren, eyes closed, said, "No."

"You are awake."

"No."

Kita began laughing.

Bocchi covered her head with the blanket.

The room became unbearable.

It was the first morning Kita laughed in the inn without thinking about Bocchi's bandage.

That mattered more than the embarrassment.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln03-umaru -->

Umaru's attachment to Mau began as convenience.

He knew where food was.

He answered directly.

He did not act surprised when she wanted to stay inside.

Then she noticed that everyone else trusted him.

Bocchi did.

Fern did.

Frieren obviously did.

Coco had begun following him into simple chores just to ask questions.

Yuta argued with him like an older brother who had given up pretending not to worry.

Rimuru gave him responsibility without ceremony.

Umaru tested the network one person at a time.

She asked Fern where clean towels were instead of asking Mau.

She let Bocchi show her the music room.

She sat beside Coco during lunch.

She accepted food from Maomao after confirming twice that it was food.

She played a game with Momo and discovered Momo cheated with enough confidence to make cheating part of the game.

One evening, Mau entered the common room and found Umaru asleep on the couch with Bocchi at one end and Coco at the other.

He stopped.

Frieren walked into his back.

"What?"

Mau pointed.

Frieren looked.

"They're sleeping."

"I know."

"Then why are you standing here?"

Mau thought.

"No reason."

They went around them.

Umaru did not wake.

The bridge had worked.

<!-- scene-id: s2e8-ln03-mikasa -->

Mikasa was the last person to stop sleeping near an exit.

Not because anyone told her to stop.

Because one night she returned from patrol with Yuta and discovered there was no empty chair near the door.

Marin had taken it.

Mikasa stood.

Marin looked up.

"Oh. Did you want this?"

Mikasa looked at the room.

There was space beside Anko.

Space near Maomao.

Space on the floor near the fire.

No tactical advantage.

No reason.

She sat beside Anko.

Anko moved her notebook.

"Good patrol?"

"Nothing happened."

"Excellent."

Mikasa watched Momo accuse Rimuru of being an unreliable pillow.

Rimuru said he had never agreed to be furniture.

Umaru said furniture did not get consent.

Mikasa laughed.

Small.

Once.

Anko looked at her.

Mikasa's face became neutral.

Anko returned to the notebook.

Nothing happened.

That was excellent too.
