# The Arrivals — S2 Mau/Frieren Formalization v0.1

**Status:** STRONG DIRECTION / MACRO RELATIONSHIP GAP RESOLVED
**Date:** 2026-09-26

This beat is not a marriage proposal.

It is Mau consciously and explicitly asking Frieren to formalize what they have already been living as: girlfriend / boyfriend / chosen romantic partners.

Exact final English dialogue remains later script work.

---

## 1. Why the moment is communal rather than private

By this point in S2:
- G1, G2, G3 and G4 are living around the inn / expanded temporary settlement;
- Mau and Frieren are among the emotional nuclei of the community;
- practically everyone has watched their relationship grow;
- many residents love both of them independently.

Therefore the preparation should not be a secret two-person romance scene disconnected from the household.

> The whole family helps Mau ask for one private moment.

The comedy comes from everyone inserting themselves into the plan. The emotional payoff comes from the fact that, when Mau finally speaks to Frieren, the words are still his.

---

## 2. Getting Frieren out of the inn

Rimuru creates the opening by telling Frieren that there is some surplus/discretionary money to distribute and that her share is enough for a new grimoire / book she would immediately want to inspect.

Frieren does not overthink it. She takes the money and leaves quickly to go look for the grimoire.

This is part of the plan.

---

## 3. Mau calls the family meeting

The moment Frieren is gone, everyone gathers because Mau asked for help.

Mau says, in essence: I need your help. I want to ask Frieren to be my girlfriend.

Immediate reactions can overlap: finally; wait, you weren't already?; excitement; disbelief; contradictory advice.

The teasing stays affectionate. Mau is earnest and slightly overwhelmed.

---

## 4. Bocchi + Umaru + Coco: fake protective-sibling investigation

Bocchi and Umaru decide that, because Mau is effectively their older brother, they must determine whether Frieren is the right woman for him. Everyone already knows the answer; that is the joke.

Umaru is the stronger instigator and convinces Bocchi to commit to the bit. Coco can join because the absurd seriousness of the operation is funny and gives her another family/social beat.

With Wakana's help they obtain improvised detective/interrogator outfits.

The operation has two functions:
- comedy;
- keep Frieren occupied / buy preparation time after she returns.

The questions can accidentally become sincere: what Frieren likes about Mau, his worst habit, what she wants to do with him in the future, and whether she understands how much Bocchi/Umaru care about him.

Important guardrail: Bocchi/Umaru are performing protective-sibling comedy, not exercising actual veto power over Mau's relationship.

---

## 5. Fern's job

Fern helps manage Frieren after the fake investigation.

She ensures Frieren gets back at the right time, does not disappear into another grimoire tangent, and changes / cleans up / wears something a little nicer for the evening.

Fern does not need to tell her why.

This gives Fern a beautiful role: she is helping prepare Frieren for a future relationship rather than protecting her from one.

---

## 6. Advice committee for Mau

Hakari Hanazono, Karane Inda, Shizuka Yoshimoto and Nano Eiai become a chaotic advice panel.

- Hakari: maximal romantic enthusiasm / escalation.
- Karane: embarrassed denial followed by sincere advice.
- Nano: efficiency / clarity / structure.
- Shizuka: quiet, emotionally precise or book-mediated contribution.

Other characters may drift in and out.

The point is that Mau realizes he does not need a clever speech; he needs to say clearly what he feels.

Yuta and Maki can reinforce this from the perspective of a relationship that already lived an entire lifetime.

---

## 7. Marin + Momo lead decorations

Marin and Momo become highly invested in preparing the location and likely propose more decoration than Mau originally imagined.

Mau explains the one thing he cares about:

> He wants to ask Frieren where they first met.

That becomes the design constraint.

The place must remain recognizable as the original meeting location. Flowers, lanterns / soft light and restrained callbacks can enhance it without turning it into an unrelated spectacle.

> The same place where Mau's remembered life began becomes the place where he consciously chooses his future.

---

## 8. Wider family contributions

Everyone can contribute according to personality without needing equal screen time.

Possible examples:
- Rimuru: logistics / getting Frieren out / timing.
- Fern: Frieren management and clothing.
- Wakana: detective costumes and/or outfit help.
- Bocchi/Umaru/Coco: fake investigation.
- Marin/Momo: decorations.
- Hakari/Karane/Shizuka/Nano: romance advice.
- Yuta/Maki: grounded long-term relationship advice.
- Qifrey: subtle magical/lighting support if useful.
- Stark: physical setup / carrying things.
- Himmel: quiet practical help without romantic drama.
- others: food, flowers, transport, distraction, cleanup.

Himmel helping can be especially valuable because he loves Frieren deeply while respecting her actual romantic choice.

---

## 9. The family gives them privacy

Mau explicitly asks everyone to give him and Frieren space for the actual question.

Everyone agrees. Technically.

In practice many hide behind trees, rocks, paths or anything they believe is subtle. They are not subtle.

Mau may or may not realize. Frieren may notice several immediately and simply choose not to expose them.

---

## 10. Mau asks Frieren

Mau has already nearly died for Frieren, heard her say I love you, reciprocated emotionally, gone through the Ori arc, identified his own feeling as romantic love, and realized that he never actually asked what they were choosing together.

So the final emotional logic stays simple.

Core meaning:

> Frieren, I love you. I want to be with you. Will you be my girlfriend?

Exact English later.

A strong Frieren response shape is: I thought I already was.

Mau can answer approximately: I know. But I wanted to ask you.

Then Frieren explicitly says yes.

Important: Frieren chooses him out loud.

The scene can end in embrace, kiss, quiet physical closeness, or the combination that best fits final staging.

---

## 11. Everyone reveals themselves

Once Frieren says yes / they embrace, the hiding family erupts.

People emerge from everywhere. Mau may be horrified that they heard everything. Frieren may be much less surprised because she already knew they were there.

---

## 12. Celebration dinner

The day ends with a major communal dinner at the inn.

It is not a wedding-style event. It is a joyful family celebration with good food, teasing, people arguing over who helped most, and Mau/Frieren sitting together.

This can function as a late-S2 ordinary-life payoff before the final future-home search / site choice.

---

## 13. Macro closure

The beat resolves both realizations seeded by Ori:
1. Mau recognizes that he and Frieren never consciously formalized their relationship.
2. Mau finally recognizes/names his feeling as romantic love.

He acts on both.

> Mau and Frieren become explicitly, mutually, verbally chosen romantic partners before S2 ends.

Later engagement, marriage and longer-term domestic milestones remain open future material.

Still later-layer work: exact dialogue, exact grimoire, exact detective questions, outfit designs, decoration, kiss/embrace choreography, dinner comedy and exact episode placement.
