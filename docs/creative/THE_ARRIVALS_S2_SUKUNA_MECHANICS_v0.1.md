# The Arrivals — S2 Sukuna Mechanics v0.1

**Status:** STRONG DIRECTION / MACRO MECHANICS RESOLVED, EXACT NUMBERS PROVISIONAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-26

This document resolves the S2 Sukuna macro-logic that previously blocked closure of the season.

It deliberately separates:
- source-memory endpoint;
- body state;
- imported/reconstructed power state;
- Continuum Synchronization.

---

## 1. Sukuna arrival snapshot

Favored project state:

> **Sukuna remembers through his victory over Gojo, but arrives before the Kashimo fight / before fully resuming his Heian incarnation.**

Therefore:

- Sukuna remembers defeating Gojo;
- Sukuna understands the World Cutting Slash / expanded-target Dismantle concept;
- Sukuna is still in **Meguna's body** rather than the four-armed Heian body;
- Megumi is **not** imported as a second trapped soul;
- only Sukuna is an active consciousness in the Continuum body.

This is a project-canon body/memory split, not a claim about literal source chronology.

---

## 2. Ten Shadows reconstruction

In source canon:
- Agito is destroyed by Gojo;
- Mahoraga is destroyed by Gojo's Hollow Purple;
- Sukuna is unable to continue using Ten Shadows afterward.

For The Arrivals, Continuum's Synchronization reconstruction is allowed to rebuild access from the selected **Meguna power/body template** even though Sukuna's memory endpoint extends through Gojo's death.

This is an explicit project-canon reconstruction rule.

It does **not** rewrite source canon.

Favored progression:

```text
low Synchronization
→ Shrine fundamentals / CE / physical baseline
→ ~mid Synchronization: Ten Shadows becomes viable again
→ Sukuna rebuilds/re-establishes Mahoraga + Agito access
→ high Synchronization: Malevolent Shrine becomes safely usable again
→ full/near-full Synchronization: World Cutting Slash would become executable
```

Exact thresholds remain provisional.

Useful current planning numbers:
- Ten Shadows: roughly **50%**
- Malevolent Shrine: roughly **90%**
- World Cutting Slash: **100% / full reconstruction**

The ordering matters more than the exact percentages.

---

## 3. Why S1 Malevolent Shrine happens once

Sukuna's S1 city-destruction Domain remains canon to The Arrivals.

Favored explanation:

- Sukuna arrives with unusually high / unstable access to his source-state output;
- he immediately acts with the arrogance of someone who has just defeated Gojo;
- he uses Malevolent Shrine before he has properly tested how Continuum handles his power;
- the Domain works;
- the use catastrophically destabilizes / crashes his Synchronization and/or the reconstructed Domain pathway;
- afterward he cannot simply reopen Malevolent Shrine;
- safe/reliable access now requires rebuilding to roughly the high-Synchronization threshold (~90%).

This turns the S1 catastrophe into both:
- a demonstration of Sukuna's threat;
- and the mistake that forces him to spend months rebuilding in the shadows.

He does not become harmless.

He retains enough Shrine fundamentals / CE / combat intelligence to remain dangerous while recovering.

---

## 4. Why Sukuna stays hidden between S1 and the S2 fight

He is not idle.

He is:
- learning Continuum;
- rebuilding Synchronization;
- recovering stable Ten Shadows access;
- re-establishing Mahoraga/Agito;
- observing rumors and the Otherworlder settlement;
- updating his model of Yuta and the growing cast.

This explains why he does not immediately repeat Malevolent Shrine after S1.

---

## 5. Why Sukuna attacks before reaching 90–100%

Around the point where Ten Shadows is back online (~50–60% as a useful planning band), Sukuna believes he has enough.

Psychology:

> **He has just come from defeating Gojo.**

He knows:
- how strong Mahoraga can be;
- how to coordinate Mahoraga + Agito;
- how much value Ten Shadows gave him against the strongest opponent he remembers;
- Yuta is dangerous, but Sukuna does not initially understand the full Continuum team as one integrated multi-system combat machine.

He also sees that waiting has a cost:
- Yuta is improving;
- the household is sharing power-system information;
- more Otherworlders are arriving;
- the settlement is becoming more organized.

Therefore Sukuna chooses to attack **before** full reconstruction.

His mistake is not stupidity.

It is a rationally arrogant extrapolation:

> `If this arsenal was enough to defeat Gojo, it is enough for them.`

That confidence is exactly what the S2 team punishes.

---

## 6. World Cutting Slash exists in knowledge but never gets used in The Arrivals S2

Source logic preserved:

World Cutting Slash is not a separate innate technique.

It is a modified application of Dismantle in which Sukuna expands the slash's target to space/existence/world after using Mahoraga's adaptation as a model.

Project rule:

- Sukuna **remembers how to do it**;
- Continuum has not reconstructed enough of his power/control to execute it;
- it sits behind full/near-full Synchronization;
- therefore he never uses it during S1 or S2.

This allows the audience to know Sukuna possesses the terrifying knowledge without importing the attack into the actual fight.

## 7. Mau/Sukuna merging

At the fatal Frieren moment:

- Sukuna's hand/arm penetrates Mau;
- Sukuna's CE/soul/incarnation structure crosses directly into Mau's body/system boundary;
- The Noise detects a foreign structure that resembles something capable of being housed/incarnated inside another vessel;
- The Noise begins attempting to interpret/absorb/merge that structure.

Private internal signal to Mau:

> **`merging begun` / `merger process beginning`**

Exact wording later.

Important:
- The Noise is not correctly concluding "Sukuna is a cursed spirit";
- Sukuna was a sorcerer whose soul was preserved through cursed-object incarnation;
- The Noise is reacting to the **foreign incarnated/vessel-compatible structure**, not a simplistic species label.

---

## 8. Why Sukuna cannot detach from Mau

The restraint is not primarily physical.

Once The Noise initiates the merge:
- Sukuna's CE/soul structure becomes system-tethered to Mau;
- Sukuna can move physically, but cannot cleanly withdraw without dragging against that tether;
- he realizes something inside Mau is attempting to incorporate/analyze him.

This buys Mau the otherwise implausible time needed to complete Red + Blue.

Sukuna does try to escape.

He is not standing still out of plot convenience.

---

## 9. Mahoraga's final intervention

Sukuna responds to the impossible tether with the tool he trusts for impossible phenomena:

> **Mahoraga.**

Mahoraga attempts to:
- adapt to the Mau/Sukuna tether;
- break/separate the connection;
- interfere with Red/Blue before completion.

It is too late.

Mahoraga is annihilated in the resulting Hollow Purple.

The falling wheel remains a favored visual.

Agito's exact destruction point can be set during choreography; it does not need to survive the battle.

---

## 10. Sukuna's survival price: permanent loss of World Cutting Slash

Sukuna survives Mau's Hollow Purple through a catastrophic Binding Vow / Continuum-recognized equivalent.

The price is:

> **Sukuna permanently gives up the ability to execute the World Cutting Slash / world-targeting Dismantle configuration.**

This is meaningful even though he never used it in Continuum.

He:
- remembers possessing/learning it;
- knows it is one of the most valuable weapons he ever developed;
- expected it to return at full Synchronization;
- permanently cashes out that future access to survive now.

Mechanically, the vow should prohibit the **expanded-target/world-targeting application of Dismantle**, rather than pretending World Cutting Slash is an unrelated innate technique.

Exact vow wording is later scene/system work.
