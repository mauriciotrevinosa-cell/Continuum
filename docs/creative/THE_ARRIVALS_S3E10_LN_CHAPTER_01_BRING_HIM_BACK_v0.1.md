# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 10 — Bring Him Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S3E10 — `First Return`

Bringing Mau home required no battle.

That felt wrong after the search.

He walked.

Frieren beside him.

Ori on the other side.

Nobody carried him.

Nobody restrained him.

Nobody celebrated.

At the inn, Bocchi opened the door and immediately cried.

Mau froze.

Bocchi tried to apologize for crying.

That made it worse.

Kita hugged Bocchi before Bocchi could decide whether hugging Mau would be too much.

Umaru stood behind them pretending her eyes were dry.

They were not.

Mau looked at everyone.

Too many faces.

Too much relief.

A memory surfaced:

`They are relieved because the crisis is over.`

Another:

`They are relieved because you're useful again.`

Mau looked down.

Frieren noticed.

"Which one?"

He looked at her.

She had learned to ask.

"Bad one."

"Do you believe it?"

"I don't know."

Frieren nodded.

No reassurance.

No correction.

Just information.

That helped.

The first night back, nobody let Mau sleep alone.

Not because they thought he would run.

Because they did not know whether he would wake believing he already had.

Frieren stayed.

Ori slept on the floor despite three available beds elsewhere.

Umaru appeared at the door with snacks and claimed she was only delivering supplies.

Bocchi appeared later with no excuse at all.

Mau looked at the room.

"You know this is weird."

Frieren opened her book.

"Yes."

Ori said:

"Good."

Nobody left.
