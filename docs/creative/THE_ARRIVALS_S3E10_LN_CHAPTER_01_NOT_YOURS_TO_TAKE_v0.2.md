# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 12 — Not Yours to Take

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Continuity state:** Day 2 — afternoon | Mau: semi-conscious | Frieren reaches him | Transfer not yet agreed

Frieren entered alone.

Mau looked smaller under a blanket than he ever had in battle.

No blood.

No obvious catastrophic wound.

Somehow that made it worse.

She sat beside him.

His eyes opened after several seconds.

Focused slowly.

"Hi."

Frieren almost broke.

Instead she touched his face.

Warm.

Too warm.

Mau leaned into her hand before thinking.

That nearly broke her worse.

"You left."

His eyes moved away.

"I know."

"Do you?"

"I think so."

Wrong answer.

Honest answer.

Frieren lowered her forehead to his.

No speech.

No accusation.

Relief first.

Mau whispered:

"I remembered you telling me to go."

"I didn't."

"I know."

Pause.

"I think."

Frieren closed her eyes.

That was the whole problem.

Outside, the argument began.

Rimuru wanted Mau moved to the inn as soon as Yuta said transport was possible.

The witches refused.

Not because they wanted to keep him.

Because they wanted terms.

"He came to us."

"By manipulated memory," Yuta said.

"Possibly."

"He belongs with his family."

A witch's expression sharpened.

"He belongs to himself."

Rimuru stopped.

Correct.

Annoying.

Important.

Diablo watched.

Ori too.

One witch said:

"You may take him when his condition permits."

Rimuru exhaled.

"Then what are we arguing about?"

"Access."

"To what?"

"Him."

Every person on the household side became colder.

The witch corrected herself.

"Questions."

"No," Frieren said from the doorway.

Everyone turned.

Mau remained inside.

Frieren stood between rooms.

The witch looked at her.

"We kept him alive."

Frieren's mana rose.

Rimuru said:

"Don't."

To both sides.

The witch continued.

"We are not asking to own him."

"Sounded like it."

"We want to speak with him when he is stable."

"Why?"

"Because something used memory in a way several of us recognize partially and none of us understand completely."

Qifrey looked interested despite himself.

The witch pointed toward the room.

"And because he is an anomaly with no before."

Frieren's face became dangerous.

"Don't call him—"

"Treasure?"

Silence.

The witch sighed.

"Fine. Mau."

Better.

"We help return Mau. We remain close enough to verify the interference. When he can answer for himself, you allow us to ask."

Rimuru said:

"He can refuse."

The witch considered.

"Questions, not answers."

"One session at a time."

"Acceptable."

"No memory alteration."

Several witches looked offended.

Rimuru did not care.

"No dreams inserted."

More offense.

"Nothing without explicit consent."

The room became very still.

The witch finally said:

"Accepted."

Frieren did not like the bargain.

Neither did Rimuru.

That was not the same as coercion.

Mau needed to go home.

The witches wanted access to a mystery.

The family wanted their help without surrendering Mau's agency.

Terms existed.

Now transport could begin.
