# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 11 — Do You Remember It That Way Too?

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Source episode:** S3E10 — `First Return`

Frieren chose the first day.

Not the clearing.

The camp.

Fire.

Bad bread.

One blanket.

She told it slowly.

Mau listened.

"You asked if I remembered where I came from," Frieren said.

"Yes."

"You asked about my family."

Mau swallowed.

"Yes."

"I said I did."

Pause.

"Then you asked if they died."

"Yes."

Frieren stopped there.

Mau looked at her.

"You touched your hair ornament."

Frieren froze.

She had not said that.

Mau continued.

"Before you answered."

Frieren stared.

"You looked at the fire after."

Silence.

Mau's expression changed.

"Did that actually happen?"

Frieren smiled.

Small.

Immediate.

"Yes."

Mau looked down.

The relief was disproportionate.

One tiny gesture.

One movement of a hand.

Real.

His.

Not supplied by anyone in the room.

Frieren took his hand.

Mau asked:

"Do you remember it that way too?"

"Yes."

The question had changed.

It no longer meant only fear.

It could also mean hope.
