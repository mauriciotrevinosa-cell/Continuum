# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 14 — Carry Him Home

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 2 — night | Mau: unconscious in transit | Destination: old inn

Mau did not walk home.

That mattered.

Yuta and Himmel carried the stretcher through the first rough stretch.

Rimuru took over later.

Diablo cleared route hazards without turning the return into a parade of power.

Milim flew ahead only when asked.

Two witches followed openly.

Others did not.

Eight signatures became fewer visible bodies without becoming fewer presences in anyone's memory.

Frieren walked beside Mau.

Not touching the stretcher.

Touching him.

Two fingers around his wrist where she could feel pulse.

Every few minutes Yuta said:

"Still stable."

Frieren knew.

She wanted him to keep saying it.

The return took hours because speed was not automatically safety.

Once, Mau woke.

Only enough to pull against the blanket.

Frieren leaned close.

"We're going home."

His eyes found her.

"The inn?"

"Yes."

He stopped fighting.

Then unconscious again.

That single question settled location continuity more effectively than any map.

The old inn was still home.

The new site was still future.

When the route lanterns finally appeared, the front door opened before anyone knocked.

Fern.

Stark behind her.

Bocchi farther back.

Kita.

Umaru.

Coco.

Agott.

Then faces Mau had never seen.

The G5 cohort.

They had spent a night hearing about the missing man.

Now the missing man arrived horizontal.

Milim stood near the doorway and looked at Mau for the first time.

Her face said:

That's him?

Not contempt.

Confusion.

Mau did not look powerful.

He looked exhausted.

Frieren heard someone whisper:

"That's Mau?"

She did not turn.

Good.

Let them ask.

The inn became movement.

Maomao had prepared a room.

Not Mau and Frieren's normal arrangement.

A quieter one with space for people to work.

Water heated.

Clean cloth.

Food for everyone returning.

Overflow guests moved their blankets without complaint.

G5 did not vanish because plot focus changed.

They made room.

One newcomer carried the search maps off the dining table.

Another helped Stark bring more water.

Milim tried to follow into the medical room.

Fern stopped her.

"Too many people."

Milim pointed at Frieren.

"She's going."

"Yes."

"Why?"

Fern looked at her.

Milim realized before the answer came.

"Oh."

She stepped back.

Diablo remained downstairs with Rimuru.

The reunion between them had still not happened properly.

Neither complained.

Upstairs, Frieren sat beside Mau.

The witches waited outside the room because that was the agreement.

Questions later.

Stability first.

Near midnight, Mau's fever finally began to drop.

Frieren did not sleep.

The old inn had held the family through too many impossible nights.

It held one more.
