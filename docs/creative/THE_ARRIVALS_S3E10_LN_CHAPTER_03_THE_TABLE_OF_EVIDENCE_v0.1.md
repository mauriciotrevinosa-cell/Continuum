# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 12 — The Table of Evidence

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E10 — `First Return`

They did not call it therapy.

Maomao would have objected to pretending they knew enough.

They called it the table.

One end of the dining table became evidence.

Not proof of identity.

Evidence.

Rimuru brought dated notes.

Yuta brought memories of shared travel and combat.

Bocchi brought domestic details no one had thought worth recording.

Okarun brought jokes.

Coco brought drawings.

Frieren brought earliest continuity.

Ori brought the strange fact that Mau's memories of her seemed cleaner than memories of almost anything else.

The witches contributed categories.

Diablo contributed objections.

Raphael contributed confidence ranges and refused to label memories `TRUE` merely because records matched.

That refusal made Mau trust the process more.

The categories became:

`PROVABLY FALSE`

`REAL / ALTERED`

`DREAM-DERIVED`

`IMPLANTED OR WRONG-OWNERSHIP`

`AUTHENTIC / EMOTIONAL WEIGHT SUSPECT`

`RECOVERED`

`UNCERTAIN`

The last column grew fastest.

Mau hated it.

Then hated the others more.

At least `UNCERTAIN` was honest.

Coco placed one drawing down.

The old inn from early S2.

Mau pointed at a crooked window.

"You drew that wrong."

Coco gasped.

"I did not."

"You did."

Qifrey looked.

"You did."

Coco stared at him.

"Whose side are you on?"

Mau laughed.

Nobody marked the memory.

For five seconds the table was only a table.

That counted too.
