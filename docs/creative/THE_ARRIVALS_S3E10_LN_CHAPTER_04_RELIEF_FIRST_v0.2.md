# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 24 — Relief First

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 24  
**Continuity state:** Day 3 — late night | Mau wakes after return | Frieren relief precedes anger

Mau woke to Frieren holding his hand.

Not ceiling.

Not witches.

Not a false house his memory insisted existed.

Frieren.

He stared long enough that she noticed.

"Mau?"

He squeezed her fingers.

She stood so fast the chair moved backward.

Then hugged him.

No warning.

Mau's body hurt in small places.

Scratches.

Muscles.

Headache.

Nothing that made the embrace impossible.

He put his arms around her.

Frieren held too hard.

Good.

Real enough to hurt.

He whispered:

"Sorry."

That changed the hug.

Not ended.

Changed.

Frieren pulled back.

Her face was wet.

"You left."

Mau looked down.

"I know."

"You left while I was sleeping."

"I thought—"

"I know what you thought."

Her voice cracked.

That sentence was older than S3.

Sukuna lived inside it.

Mau stopped.

Frieren sat again because standing made anger harder to control.

"I am relieved first."

Mau looked at her.

"What?"

"I need you to understand the order."

Frieren wiped her face angrily.

"I am happy you're alive."

Pause.

"I want to hit you."

"Okay."

"No."

She looked offended.

"Don't say okay."

Mau almost smiled.

Bad instinct.

Frieren saw.

Her expression became worse.

"Do not."

He stopped.

Then both laughed once.

Accident.

Relief leaking through anger.

Frieren touched his cheek.

"Why did you go?"

Mau tried to explain the cabin.

The certainty.

The memory that Rimuru or Qifrey or someone had told him where to go.

Halfway through, the source changed again.

He stopped speaking.

Frieren saw it happen.

"Which version now?"

Mau looked at her.

"Qifrey."

"Before?"

"Rimuru."

"And before that?"

"I don't know."

Frieren took a breath.

Not:

You're wrong.

Not:

Trust me.

"What do you know right now?"

Mau looked at their hands.

"I'm at the inn."

"Yes."

"You're here."

"Yes."

"You were angry before I left."

Frieren considered.

"About the false memory?"

"Yes."

"Correct."

Mau swallowed.

"You love me."

Frieren did not hesitate.

"Yes."

He closed his eyes.

The answer helped.

It did not erase the memories that contradicted it.

That frightened both of them.

Frieren leaned forward again.

Smaller hug.

More careful.

"Questions tomorrow."

"Witches?"

"You remember them?"

"Some."

"They made a deal."

Mau opened one eye.

"Of course they did."

Frieren's mouth almost moved.

"Sleep."

"Are you staying?"

"Yes."

Mau let himself believe that one without verification.

For tonight.
