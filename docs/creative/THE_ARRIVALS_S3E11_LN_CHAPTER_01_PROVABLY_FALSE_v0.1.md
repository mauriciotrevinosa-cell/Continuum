# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 13 — Provably False

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 13  
**Source episode:** S3E11 — `Reconstruction`

The easiest category hurt most.

Provably false.

Mau remembered a morning where Bocchi had left the inn after telling him:

`You don't need me anymore.`

Bocchi had not.

There was no missing morning.

Kita had been there.

Momo had been there.

A dated supply list had Bocchi's handwriting.

Bocchi herself looked horrified.

Mau knew she was telling the truth.

The memory remained.

"Can we delete it?" Bocchi asked.

Raphael answered through Rimuru.

"No safe method established."

Bocchi looked angry.

At the memory.

At the world.

At herself for not being able to fight either.

Mau said:

"You don't have to fix it."

Bocchi looked at him.

He almost said `I'm fine.`

Stopped.

"I hate it."

"Me too."

That was better.

They marked the memory false.

Mau still remembered it.

Classification was not erasure.
