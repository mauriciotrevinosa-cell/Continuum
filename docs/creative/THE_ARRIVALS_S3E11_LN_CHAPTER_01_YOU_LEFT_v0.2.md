# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 25 — You Left

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 25  
**Continuity state:** Day 4 — morning | Mau: awake / weak | Named G5: temporary/undecided statuses | Witches waiting under bargain

Morning made the argument less merciful.

Mau could sit.

Drink.

Eat half of what Maomao gave him.

Therefore Frieren could be properly angry.

They stayed in the room.

No audience.

The witches had not asked yet.

Rimuru had checked once.

G5 had breakfast downstairs.

The worksite sent a note that the stone delivery had finally been moved under cover.

Life existed outside the room.

Frieren said:

"You knew I would stop you."

Mau looked at the blanket.

"Yes."

"And you left because of that."

"Partly."

"That is deciding alone again."

Sukuna.

The word never needed saying.

Mau's shoulders tightened.

"I wasn't trying to die."

"I know."

"I wasn't protecting you from a fight."

"I know."

"I needed to check something real."

Frieren looked at him.

"And why did you have to check it without me?"

Mau had an answer.

It failed before reaching his mouth.

Because if Frieren came, her presence would influence him.

Because he loved her.

Because if she contradicted the memory, he might believe love instead of evidence.

Because maybe love itself was one of the things being manipulated.

All of those sounded terrible.

Some were true.

"I didn't know how to use you as evidence."

Frieren went still.

Mau winced.

"That sounded worse."

"It sounded accurate."

He looked at her.

Frieren sat beside him.

"I am not evidence."

"I know."

"I am a person."

"I know."

"You can ask me what I remember."

Mau nodded.

"You can still disagree."

Another nod.

"You can still leave a conversation."

Mau nodded.

"You cannot decide that my love makes me unusable."

That one landed.

Mau looked at her.

Frieren's voice softened.

"I can't fix your memory."

"I know."

"I can still come with you."

Mau had no answer.

Frieren did not demand one.

The argument ended because Maomao entered with more food and said:

"If either of you raises his temperature through emotional stupidity, I am removing one of you."

Frieren looked at Mau.

Mau looked at Frieren.

"Which one?"

Maomao did not blink.

"The louder."

Neither knew who that was.

Argument postponed.

Not resolved.
