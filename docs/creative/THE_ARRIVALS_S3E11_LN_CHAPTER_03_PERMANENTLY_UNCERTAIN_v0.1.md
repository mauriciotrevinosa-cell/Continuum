# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 15 — Permanently Uncertain

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E11 — `Reconstruction`

No one liked the final pile.

Too small for records.

Too private for witnesses.

Too ordinary to have left evidence.

Mau remembered Frieren saying goodnight in a doorway.

Could have happened.

He remembered Momo stealing half a piece of fruit and denying it while chewing.

Almost certainly happened at some point.

He remembered sitting with Yuta in silence after a bad day.

Yuta remembered several possible bad days.

Which one?

Unclear.

Mau wanted certainty anyway.

Raphael could not provide it.

Diablo could not.

The witches could not.

Frieren would not pretend.

"Maybe."

Mau hated that word.

She knew.

"Do you want me to say yes?"

"No."

"Then maybe."

Mau looked at the uncertain pile.

"If I can't prove half of this, how do I know I'm me?"

Nobody answered immediately.

Good.

An instant answer would have been insulting.

Ori sat across from him.

"You don't remember making me breakfast wrong?"

Mau looked up.

"What?"

"You put too much salt."

"I did not."

"You did."

Frieren said:

"He did."

Mau stared.

Ori smiled.

"You can be wrong and still be you."

Mau looked at the pile again.

That was not enough.

But it was something.
