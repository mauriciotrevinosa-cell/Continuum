## Chapter 18 — Questions Without Answers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Continuity state:** Day 3 — afternoon | First witch questioning complete | No cure / no The Noise diagnosis

The questioning produced six pages of notes and no cure.

Good.

Nobody said good.

But it was.

A false answer would have been worse.

Qifrey sorted observations by framework.

Dream structure.

Perception.

Memory.

Emotional valence.

Ownership.

Rimuru added system observations he actually possessed.

Historical classification failure around Mau.

The S1 collapse after Mau said Raphael's name.

No cause detected then.

No cause detected now.

Yuta added physical facts.

No evidence of ordinary head trauma in the first disappearance.

No toxin they had identified.

Exhaustion.

Sleep deprivation.

Stress.

The witch who stayed said:

"You keep looking for one mechanism."

Rimuru looked at her.

"Because one mechanism would be convenient."

"Reality dislikes convenience."

"People keep telling me that."

Qifrey smiled.

Mau slept upstairs.

Frieren stayed with him.

The discussion continued without turning Mau into a meeting object.

That was another change.

One witch suggested the false memories might be assembled from real material.

Qifrey agreed partially.

Yuta asked whose real material.

No answer.

Rimuru asked Raphael whether any hidden process had become visible.

Her answer:

"No additional internal process can be confirmed."

Not The Noise.

Not foreign structure.

Nothing she could actually support.

Rimuru rubbed his face.

"So we know less than the witches."

The witch corrected:

"We know different things."

Diablo entered.

First time he had been inside the inn long enough to participate in analysis rather than search.

He listened.

Then said:

"Your categories assume the inconsistency lives only in memory."

Everyone looked at him.

"What else?"

Diablo did not answer yet.

He had noticed something in the field.

He wanted one more observation before saying it.

That annoyed everyone.

Especially Rimuru.

Dinner would provide it.
