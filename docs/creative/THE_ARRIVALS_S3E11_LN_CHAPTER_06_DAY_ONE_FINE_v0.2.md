# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 30 — Day One Fine

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 30  
**Continuity state:** Day 5 | Lived base: old inn | Mau improving / supervised | Named G5 present | Worksite active

The first day Mau looked fine, nobody trusted it.

Good.

Maomao checked temperature before breakfast.

Yuta checked balance.

Frieren asked:

"Any wrong memories?"

Mau thought.

"No."

Then corrected:

"None I noticed."

Better.

He was allowed to walk to the worksite.

With people.

Not because he was imprisoned.

Because everyone going there went together anyway.

G5 split for the day.

Senku and Suika were already going to the worksite.

Kusuri went because Maomao had forbidden her from reorganizing another shelf without seeing where future medical storage would live.

Vamola joined Momo and Okarun on the route.

Richeh and Tetia went with Coco and Agott.

Mai stayed at the inn with Maki and claimed that had nothing to do with Maki.

Nijika and Ryo stayed because Bocchi was not ready to spend the whole day away from the old inn.

Jinshi went to the nearby native market with Fern to understand supply routes without pretending he represented the settlement.

Nazuna slept.

Reasonable.

Seiko and Turbo Granny followed a road report.

Milim went wherever Rimuru was until somebody found her actual work.

Nano wrote all of it down.

No cohort evaporated.

At the site, Mau found work had continued while he was gone.

Not much.

Enough.

Stone stacked under cover.

The north shelter repaired.

A drainage marker moved.

One proposed road abandoned.

He felt something ugly.

They did not need him.

Then another feeling.

Good.

The two existed together.

Mau stood beside the water plan.

Rimuru asked:

"Want to help?"

Mau almost said yes automatically.

Instead:

"What needs doing?"

Rimuru pointed at a crate.

"Nothing you specifically need to do."

Mau looked at him.

Rimuru smiled.

"You can still stay."

The phrase had not become a lesson yet.

Only a small irritation.

Mau stayed.

Kusuri passed carrying an empty crate.

"You Mau?"

He looked at her.

"Yes."

"Kusuri."

Mau searched his memory.

Then smiled apologetically.

"First time?"

"First time."

Relief.

He had not forgotten her.

There was simply nothing to remember yet.

Kusuri pointed at him.

"Maomao says I can't test anything on you."

Mau stared.

"Good?"

"Yep."

"Great first conversation."

"Also Senku says you're statistically weird."

"Less good."

Kusuri walked away.

Mau looked at Rimuru.

"Where did all these people come from?"

Rimuru:

"Technically your disappearance."

"That feels unfair."

"Very."

Agott watched Mau do nothing useful for seven minutes.

This improved her opinion of him more than she would admit.

That evening G5 returned to the inn with more questions than answers.

One newcomer said the future site looked terrible.

Momo said:

"Correct."

Another said:

"It looks like people are actually building it."

Rimuru liked that better.

Mau slept through the night.

No false memory woke him.

Day one fine.
