# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 21 — Day One Fine

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 21  
**Continuity state:** Day 4 | Lived base: old inn | Mau: improving / supervised | G5: temporary guests | Future site: work resumes

The first day Mau looked fine, nobody trusted it.

Good.

Maomao checked temperature before breakfast.

Yuta checked balance.

Frieren asked:

"Any wrong memories?"

Mau thought.

"No."

Then corrected:

"None I noticed."

Better.

He was allowed to walk to the worksite.

With people.

Not because he was imprisoned.

Because everyone going there went together anyway.

G5 split for the day.

Two newcomers wanted to see the construction site.

One wanted a native town instead.

Another stayed at the inn to rest.

Nano wrote all of it down.

No cohort evaporated.

At the site, Mau found work had continued while he was gone.

Not much.

Enough.

Stone stacked under cover.

The north shelter repaired.

A drainage marker moved.

One proposed road abandoned.

He felt something ugly.

They did not need him.

Then another feeling.

Good.

The two existed together.

Mau stood beside the water plan.

Rimuru asked:

"Want to help?"

Mau almost said yes automatically.

Instead:

"What needs doing?"

Rimuru pointed at a crate.

"Nothing you specifically need to do."

Mau looked at him.

Rimuru smiled.

"You can still stay."

The phrase had not become a lesson yet.

Only a small irritation.

Mau stayed.

Agott watched him do nothing useful for seven minutes.

This improved her opinion of him more than she would admit.

That evening G5 returned to the inn with more questions than answers.

One newcomer said the future site looked terrible.

Momo said:

"Correct."

Another said:

"It looks like people are actually building it."

Rimuru liked that better.

Mau slept through the night.

No false memory woke him.

Day one fine.
