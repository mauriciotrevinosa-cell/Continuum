# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 26 — The Third Day Breaks

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 26  
**Continuity state:** Day 6 — evening | Apparent stability ends | Mau has not left again yet

The third day broke at dinner.

Not dramatically.

Himmel said something about the roof.

Mau looked at Frieren.

The memory arrived complete.

Old inn.

Late afternoon.

Himmel and Frieren behind the building.

Frieren saying:

"If you had come before Mau, maybe I would have chosen differently."

Mau stopped eating.

Frieren noticed first.

"What?"

Mau looked at Himmel.

Himmel's expression changed.

He knew the shape.

"No."

Mau had not said anything.

That was answer enough.

"I remember it."

Frieren put down her cup.

"Which conversation?"

Mau told them.

Location.

Weather.

Roof tiles.

Himmel remembered the real conversation.

So did Frieren.

Real place.

Real emotional topic.

Wrong sentence.

Again.

Except this time Mau knew the sentence was wrong.

That should have saved him.

It didn't.

Because another thought followed:

What if the sentence was false but the conclusion true?

What if Frieren would have chosen Himmel if timing changed?

What if love was real and still contingent?

Frieren said:

"Mau."

He looked at her.

"I did not say that."

"I know."

"Do you?"

"Yes."

His voice was too fast.

Frieren heard it.

So did Ori.

Mau stood.

Maomao looked ready to object.

"I'm going outside."

Frieren stood too.

Mau flinched.

Tiny.

Fatal.

He saw her see it.

"I just need air."

Frieren stayed still.

"Okay."

Mau walked outside.

Ori waited ten seconds.

Then followed.

Not hidden.

Not yet.

The first false stability had lasted three days.

Long enough for everyone to begin hoping.

Long enough for the second fall to hurt more.

Volume Eleven ended with Mau sitting behind the old inn and Ori beside him, both looking toward a future settlement nobody lived in yet.
