# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 1 — Don't Tell Frieren

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Continuity state:** Day 6 — late night | Lived base: old inn | Mau/Ori still present

Mau did not decide to leave at dinner.

That would have been simpler.

He sat behind the inn with Ori until the lamps downstairs went out one by one.

The false conversation remained intact.

He knew Frieren had denied it.

He believed her.

That was the problem.

Belief and memory no longer occupied the same layer.

"I think there's a place connected to this."

Ori looked at him.

"Remember or think?"

Mau exhaled.

"Both."

"Bad."

"Yes."

South.

Toward the river system below the mercantile route.

Not a cabin this time.

A pressure.

A sense that the repeated wrongness pointed somewhere.

Ori said:

"We tell Frieren."

"No."

Immediate.

Ori stared.

Mau looked away.

"She'll stop me."

"Good."

"Because she loves me."

Ori stopped.

No resentment.

No romantic jealousy.

Only the horror of hearing love described as an obstacle by someone who knew it was love.

"Then let her stop you."

"I don't know if I can."

"Why?"

Mau looked at the dark window of the room he shared with Frieren.

"Because when she says a memory is wrong, I believe her."

"That's good."

"And when my head says the opposite, I believe that too."

Ori understood.

Not agreed.

Understood.

"If I stay here, every answer comes from someone who loves me."

Mau's voice tightened.

"I need one thing I can find without asking someone else who I am."

Ori stood.

"That is the stupidest intelligent thing you've said."

"Thank you."

"Not a compliment."

"I know."

She walked toward the door.

Mau caught her sleeve.

"Don't."

Ori looked at his hand.

Then at him.

"You promised Frieren."

"I know."

"After Sukuna."

"I know."

"You're doing it again."

"No."

Mau meant it.

That frightened her.

"I'm not deciding to die. I'm trying to investigate."

"Alone."

Mau said nothing.

There.

Ori saw the decision had already happened.

Not fully.

Enough.

She pulled her sleeve free.

"If I tell her now, will you wait?"

Mau hesitated.

One second.

Then another.

Ori closed her eyes.

Answer.

She did not go upstairs.
