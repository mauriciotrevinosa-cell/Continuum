# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 16 — Do You Need Help?

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S3E12 — `First Return`

Mau returned to the future site four days after coming home.

Not alone.

Not under guard.

With half the household pretending not to watch him.

He hated that.

Then understood it.

Then hated understanding.

The temporary shelter had changed.

A new brace.

Dryer storage.

A different path around the mud.

Things happened while he was gone.

That hurt too.

Not because he wanted the place to fail.

Because some part of him had begun equating being needed with existing.

He watched Wakana carry cloth.

"Do you need help?"

Wakana looked at what she carried.

Then at him.

"No."

The answer hit harder than it should have.

Wakana continued:

"But you can stay."

Mau stared.

She had already turned away.

No speech.

No rescue.

No task invented to make him useful.

Mau sat on an overturned crate.

For ten minutes he did nothing.

Then Coco sat beside him.

Then Agott, because Coco had stopped helping.

Then Qifrey because both of them had stopped helping.

Work slowed.

Nobody died.

Mau smiled despite himself.
