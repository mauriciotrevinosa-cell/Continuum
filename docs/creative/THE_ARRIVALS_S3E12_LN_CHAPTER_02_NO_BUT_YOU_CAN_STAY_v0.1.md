## Chapter 17 — No. But You Can Stay.

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S3E12 — `First Return`

The sentence spread.

Not intentionally.

Mau asked Fern if she needed help with records.

"No. But you can sit there."

He asked Maomao if she needed help sorting samples.

"No. Don't touch those. You can stay."

He asked Bocchi if she needed him to carry equipment.

"No."

Bocchi panicked.

"I mean— you can— I just— Kita has it—"

Kita rescued her.

"He can stay."

Mau looked betrayed.

"You people planned this."

Nobody answered.

That confirmed it.

Frieren did not participate.

That was worse.

At night Mau asked:

"Do you need anything?"

Frieren looked at him from the bed.

"Yes."

He waited.

"Come here."

Mau sat beside her.

Frieren leaned against him.

No task.

No crisis.

No proof of usefulness.

Just requested presence.

Mau closed his eyes.

The memory of the moment felt real.

That no longer guaranteed anything.

He chose to stay anyway.
