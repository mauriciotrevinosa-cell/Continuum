# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 2 — Then I'm Coming

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Continuity state:** Day 6 — before dawn | Mau/Ori preparing second departure

"If you go," Ori said, "I'm coming."

"No."

"Good. Then we agree you're not going."

Mau looked at her.

Ori folded her arms.

He had no clean route out of the logic.

"You could get hurt."

"So could you."

"That's different."

"Why?"

Mau opened his mouth.

Ori pointed.

"Don't say because you're you."

He closed it.

She knew him too well.

That was another reason he trusted her memories.

Ori packed food.

More than Mau had taken the first time.

Two blankets.

Water.

Basic medicine.

The evidence notebook.

Mau objected.

"No."

"Why?"

"If we lose it—"

"We copy the important pages."

She did.

Not all of them.

Only categories and route notes.

The original stayed at the inn.

Better continuity.

Ori wrote the letter.

Mau tried.

His version became apology instead of information.

Ori took the page.

"We need them to know we're together."

Mau looked at her.

"They'll follow."

"Yes."

"I don't want them to."

"I don't care."

She wrote:

Mau is with me.

We are going south.

He believes something there connects to the memory problem.

We have food and water.

Do not assume we are safe.

Frieren, I'm sorry.

Mau stared at the last line.

Ori did not erase it.

"You're telling them direction."

"Yes."

"That defeats the point."

"The point is you're not alone. Not that everyone else becomes blind."

Mau hated how reasonable she was.

Before leaving, Ori stood outside Frieren's door.

Hand raised.

She almost knocked.

If she knocked, Frieren would wake.

Mau would run.

Maybe.

Probably.

Ori lowered her hand.

"I'm sorry," she whispered.

Then she went downstairs.

Mau waited by the door.

Ori looked at him.

"This is not me choosing you over her."

"I know."

"If you say otherwise later, I'll hit you."

"Fair."

They left together.

The old inn disappeared behind trees.

Neither called the road freedom.
