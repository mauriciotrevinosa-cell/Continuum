# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 18 — Mud Under the Door

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Source episode:** S3E12 — `First Return`

Relief lasted three days before the outside world entered through the door.

Literally.

Mud first.

Then two strangers.

Otherworlders, according to their own account.

Neither could fight.

One had been turned away from a village after a local order required registration.

The other had left before anyone could decide what registration meant.

They had heard rumors about a place where Otherworlders were living together.

They found an old inn.

Not a city.

Not a refuge center.

An old inn with too many people and a future map still on the table.

Fern gave them water.

Maomao checked injuries.

Nano wrote names.

Rimuru explained the confidentiality Contract only when one of them asked what happened to sensitive information here.

No one called the process Arrival House.

Not yet.

Mau watched from the stairs.

Nobody needed him.

That thought arrived.

Then another:

`Good.`

For once, he chose the second one.

Outside, rain pushed mud beneath the door.

At the future site, no one had fixed drainage yet.

The world continued.
