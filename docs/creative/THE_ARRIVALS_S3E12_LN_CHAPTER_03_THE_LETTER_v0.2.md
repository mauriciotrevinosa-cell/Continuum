# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 3 — The Letter

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Continuity state:** Day 7 — morning | Mau/Ori have several-hour lead | Search begins from old inn

Frieren found Ori's handwriting first.

That made the anger arrive in the wrong order.

Mau gone.

Ori gone.

Letter.

South.

Together.

The line:

Do not assume we are safe.

Frieren read it twice.

Then the apology.

She stopped.

Rimuru came downstairs already dressed because the household had learned the sound of emergency silence.

Frieren handed him the page.

He read.

Closed his eyes.

"Second search."

No debate.

This time they had procedures.

That did not make the feeling better.

Verified facts:
- Mau and Ori left together;
- south;
- food for several days missing;
- two blankets;
- water;
- copied evidence pages;
- neither took a horse;
- Ori wrote the letter;
- Mau likely believed the destination mattered.

Frieren asked:

"How long?"

Nano checked the kitchen.

The bread had been taken before dawn.

Tracks began near the back path.

"Four hours. Maybe five."

Enough.

Not impossible.

G5 woke into another search.

This time they knew Mau.

A little.

Some volunteered.

Others did not.

Good.

Temporary guests were not conscripted.

Milim volunteered immediately.

Diablo did too.

One convoy traveler who knew southern roads offered route information but refused field work.

Useful.

Another agreed to cover worksite transport while core people searched.

Also useful.

Population became function rather than background.

Rimuru divided three search lanes.

Frieren, Yuta, Himmel: likely route.

Diablo, Qifrey, Agott: anomaly/environment.

Mikasa, Okarun, Anko: roads/witnesses.

Milim: distance checks with mandatory reporting intervals.

Bocchi tried to volunteer.

Kita took her hand.

Bocchi looked at Frieren.

Frieren almost said no.

Then remembered agency.

"Can you navigate south?"

Bocchi swallowed.

"No."

"Can you help here?"

"Yes."

"Then stay."

Bocchi nodded.

Not rejected.

Assigned.

The future site lost more work.

Again.

One delivery continued because G5 covered it.

That difference mattered.

The community had more people now.

It also had more people to feed.

Search cost changed shape.

Frieren folded Ori's letter and put it in her pocket.

Anger could wait.

Mau had tried to leave a trail without admitting he wanted to be followed.

Ori had made the trail clearer.

Frieren loved her for that.

She was furious with her for that.

Both could wait.

They went south.
