# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 7 — The Thing in the Trees

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** Day 9 — morning | Mau/Ori third travel day | Monster encounter begins

The monster announced itself by making the forest quiet.

Ori heard that.

Mau did too.

No birds.

No insects.

Water still moving.

Mau stopped.

"Do you remember a monster here?"

Ori asked.

"No."

"Good?"

"No."

The first impact broke a tree.

Not aimed at them.

Close enough.

The creature moved low despite its size.

Too many limbs for the body plan Mau expected.

Armor-like plates along one side.

One eye damaged.

Hungry.

Not supernatural in a way anyone could name from sight.

Ori stepped back.

Mau stepped in front.

She grabbed his coat.

"No."

"It's coming."

"That doesn't mean you become the wall."

Good sentence.

No time.

The monster lunged.

Mau moved Ori sideways instead of taking the hit directly.

Progress.

The claws caught his shoulder.

Pain.

He stayed standing.

Ori attacked with what she had.

The monster reacted.

Mau's mind did the dangerous thing.

Observe.

Analyze.

Not enough time.

Understand.

Not enough information.

Construction tried anyway.

He shut it down.

No.

Use what exists.

Stone.

Terrain.

The riverbank.

The creature favored its armored side.

Damaged eye meant blind angle.

Mau shouted:

"Left!"

Ori trusted him.

They moved.

The fight became ugly rather than elegant.

Mau was not the strongest person in the story.

There was no Frieren.

No Yuta.

No Rimuru.

No Milim.

Only two people trying not to die.

That made every mistake expensive.

The monster turned faster than Mau expected.

Its forelimb struck the side of his head.

Sound vanished.

Ground arrived.

Ori screamed his name.

Mau did not hear the first one.
