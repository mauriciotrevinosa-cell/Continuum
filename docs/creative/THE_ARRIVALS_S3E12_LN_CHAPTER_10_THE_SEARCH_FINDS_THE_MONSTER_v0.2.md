# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 10 — The Search Finds the Monster

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Continuity state:** Day 10 — morning | Search one day behind | Evidence of fight found

Frieren found blood before the monster.

Yuta found vomit.

Diablo found the body.

All three discoveries happened within two minutes.

Ori's marker remained on the tree.

A strip of cloth tied twice.

Not subtle.

Deliberate.

Frieren touched it.

"She wanted us to find this."

Yuta examined the ground.

Two human track patterns.

One unstable after the fight.

Blood quantity concerning but not obviously fatal.

The monster's injuries told a story.

Repeated close-range attacks.

Broken knife tip lodged under plated tissue.

Improvised branch wound.

Yuta looked at Frieren.

"Mau fought it."

Frieren's face went empty.

Diablo examined impact marks.

"Someone was struck here."

Yuta moved.

Found blood on stone at head height.

Then another spot lower.

His expression changed.

"Head."

Frieren looked at him.

"How bad?"

"I don't know."

"Guess."

"No."

Correct answer.

Frieren hated him for it.

Then loved him for it.

Ori had left enough evidence to reconstruct:
- monster attacked;
- Mau killed it;
- Mau was injured;
- they rested;
- they moved south afterward.

No mystery later about where the head injury came from.

The search changed priority.

Not missing-person verification.

Medical race.

Milim received the message.

So did Rimuru.

At the old inn, Maomao packed for field travel before anyone finished telling her why.

G5 covered the worksite again.

The future settlement lost another day of expert attention.

But not all labor.

More people existed now.

The community bent.

Did not stop.

Frieren looked south.

"Mau."

No answer.

They ran.
