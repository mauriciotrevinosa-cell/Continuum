# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 1 — Fine for Three Days

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

For three days, Mau looked better.

That sentence became dangerous later.

He ate.

He slept.

He went to the site.

He asked before helping.

Sometimes.

He laughed when Momo moved a stake nobody had authorized.

He kissed Frieren once behind the storage shelter and then discovered Umaru had been on the other side of it looking for snacks.

Umaru said nothing.

For six hours.

Then dinner happened.

"Storage-shed boyfriend behavior."

Mau nearly choked.

Ordinary embarrassment felt miraculous.

The table of evidence remained at one end of the inn.

They used it less.

Not never.

Less.

Mau woke once unsure whether he had already eaten breakfast.

He asked.

Bocchi answered.

He believed her.

Progress.

The future site gained a second temporary shelter.

The northern route gained a safer crossing.

Two travelers stayed one night at the inn and left the next morning.

Mau watched them go without wondering whether they loved him.

Progress.

Frieren began to unclench.

Not completely.

Enough to read for an entire hour without checking where Mau was.

That was the third day.

That evening Mau remembered a conversation with Ori.

It was correct.

Every word.

He checked anyway.

Ori stared at him.

"Yes. That's exactly what I said."

Mau laughed from relief.

Ori did not.

She had noticed something before he did.

Memories involving her were almost never the ones that broke.
