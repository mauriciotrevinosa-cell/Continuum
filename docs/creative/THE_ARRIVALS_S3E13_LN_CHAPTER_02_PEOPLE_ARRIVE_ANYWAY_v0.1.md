# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 2 — People Arrive Anyway

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

The road did not care that the household was recovering.

A Searcher team returned with three people.

Not together originally.

Together now because the road had made them so.

The first was a blue-haired young woman who had spent most of the last two days helping an injured traveler she did not know.

"Rem," she said.

Nothing more until asked.

The second kept his hood up despite the weather.

"Kaneki."

Maomao noticed immediately that he avoided food.

She noticed more when he thought she was not looking.

She did not confront him in the doorway.

The third reached the inn, saw Okarun, and stopped.

Okarun stopped too.

"Aira?"

Aira looked from him to Momo.

Then back.

"Oh, thank God. At least two people here are real."

Momo pointed.

"Excuse me?"

The reunion began as an argument.

That was useful.

It gave everyone else something normal to do.

No one knew yet that the three new arrivals would matter to Mau's second disappearance.

They were just people who had arrived anyway.
