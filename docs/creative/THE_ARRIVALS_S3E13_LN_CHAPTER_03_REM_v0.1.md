# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 3 — Rem

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

Rem did not ask to meet Mau.

That helped.

She helped Maomao clean the medical corner.

Then the kitchen.

Then the blankets.

By noon, Fern had to tell her:

"You are allowed to sit."

Rem looked genuinely confused.

"So are you."

Fern stared.

They both continued working.

Mau met her carrying water.

"Do you need help?"

Rem looked at the bucket.

Then at him.

"I can carry it."

Mau nodded.

Good.

He started to leave.

Rem added:

"But you can take the door."

Mau opened the door.

That was their first collaboration.

Later, Rem passed the evidence table.

She did not read the cards.

Privacy mattered even when curiosity existed.

Mau noticed.

"Thank you."

Rem looked back.

"For what?"

He gestured vaguely.

She understood enough.

"You don't owe me an explanation."

Mau almost laughed.

"People keep saying healthy things to me. It's getting suspicious."

Rem smiled.

"Then I won't make a habit of it."

No instant bond.

No dramatic recognition.

Just one new person who did not treat him like the center of the room.
