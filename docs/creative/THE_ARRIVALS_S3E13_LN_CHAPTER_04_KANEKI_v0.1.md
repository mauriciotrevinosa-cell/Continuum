# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 4 — Kaneki

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 4  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

Kaneki's problem became obvious at dinner.

He did not eat.

Not preference.

Not nerves.

His body rejected what everyone else called food.

Maomao did not ask in front of the table.

Mau did not either.

Later Kaneki found him outside.

"You noticed."

"Everyone noticed."

Kaneki looked tired.

"Great."

"We have weird food problems already."

Kaneki looked at him.

Mau pointed toward the inn.

"Rimuru is occasionally food-shaped."

From inside:

"I HEARD THAT."

Kaneki laughed before he meant to.

Good.

The actual conversation came later.

Biology.

A body built around requirements this world might or might not support.

Hunger that carried moral fear with it.

Mau listened.

Did not offer himself.

Did not say they would solve everything.

"We'll figure out what Continuum lets us do."

Kaneki looked at him carefully.

"You're very calm about monstrous biology."

Mau thought about Sukuna.

The Noise.

Things that had tried to merge with him.

"Bad sample size."

Kaneki accepted that answer.

Neither knew how literal the connection would become.
