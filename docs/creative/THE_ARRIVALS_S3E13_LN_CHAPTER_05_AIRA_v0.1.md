# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 5 — Aira

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

Aira learned three things about the inn in one afternoon.

One: Momo and Okarun were complicated.

Two: nobody here found that surprising.

Three: there were too many shoes by the door.

She adapted to the third problem first.

Aira's reunion with Okarun did not consume her arrival.

She met Bocchi.

Argued with Momo.

Asked Frieren if elves actually slept.

Frieren said yes.

Aira looked disappointed.

"That was less mysterious than I wanted."

She helped at the site the next day because standing around made her restless.

Mikasa gave her a simple job.

Aira finished it.

Asked for another.

By evening she had learned the evacuation route better than people who had lived there longer.

"You always do that?" Mau asked.

"What?"

"Act like you know what you're doing until you do."

Aira pointed at him.

"That's called confidence."

"Is it?"

"No. But it works."

Mau smiled.

Aira did not ask about the evidence table.

She had already heard enough to know it was not her business.

Not yet.
