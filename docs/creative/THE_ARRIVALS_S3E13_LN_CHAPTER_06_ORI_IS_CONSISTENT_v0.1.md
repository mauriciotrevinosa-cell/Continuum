# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 6 — Ori Is Consistent

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

Mau tested it carefully.

He hated himself for testing a person.

Ori hated the wording too.

"Ask."

Breakfast she ruined.

Correct.

The first time she wore one of his shirts.

Correct.

Her confession.

Correct enough to hurt both of them.

The rejection.

Correct.

Frieren sitting with Ori afterward.

Correct.

The strange glitches.

Correct.

Mau tried tiny details.

Where had Ori been standing when Coco interrupted them three weeks earlier?

Ori pointed.

Mau remembered the same place.

What had she said when Mau burned dinner?

"You said it was edible."

"No. You said that."

Ori stared.

Then laughed.

"Right."

One mistake.

A normal one.

Mau almost cried from relief.

They wrote nothing magical around Ori.

No claim that goddess-origin made her immune.

Only the observation:

`ORI-RELATED AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL RECALL CURRENTLY SHOWS LOWER ERROR RATE.`

Rimuru underlined `currently.`

Mau did too.

Ori looked at the line.

Once, she had feared being less real than everyone else.

Now Mau's damaged mind treated her like one of the most stable things in the world.

She did not know whether to find that beautiful or terrifying.

So she chose both.
