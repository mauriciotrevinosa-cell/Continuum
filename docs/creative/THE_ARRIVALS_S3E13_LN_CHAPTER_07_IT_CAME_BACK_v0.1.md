# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 7 — It Came Back

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Source episode:** S3E13 — `False Recovery`

The wrongness returned with a spoon.

Mau remembered Frieren throwing it at him.

Not hard.

Playfully.

During breakfast.

He could hear Momo laughing.

He could see the spoon hit his shoulder.

Mau looked at the actual spoon.

Frieren had never thrown cutlery at him.

Probably.

He asked.

She said no.

Momo said:

"I would remember that."

Umaru said:

"I wish it happened."

The room tried to make the moment small.

Mau couldn't.

By afternoon he had six new inconsistencies.

By night, eleven.

Two involved Frieren.

One involved Yuta.

None involved Ori.

Mau stared at the pattern.

Then at the settlement.

People were building systems around him.

Records.

Checks.

Protection.

He loved them for it.

And suddenly remembered a conversation where Frieren told him she was exhausted by taking care of him.

He knew it was probably false.

Probably.

That word became a door.
