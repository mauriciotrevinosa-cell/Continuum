# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 8 — Don't Tell Frieren

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Source episode:** S3E14 — `Second Departure`

Mau told Ori at two in the morning.

Not because he trusted her more than Frieren.

Because that was exactly the problem.

"I think I know where it's coming from."

Ori sat up.

They were in the common room.

Everyone else asleep.

"Know or remember?"

Mau looked at her.

"Good question."

"That wasn't a compliment."

"I know."

He had a place.

South this time.

Near the river system below the mercantile route.

A pressure in the memory pattern.

Not a voice.

Not a command.

A sense that if he reached the place, the contradictions would become legible.

Ori's face tightened.

"We tell Frieren."

"No."

"Yes."

"No."

Ori stood.

Mau whispered:

"She'll stop me."

"Good."

"Because she loves me."

Ori stopped.

There was no resentment in how he said it.

That made it worse.

Mau continued.

"I know she loves me."

"Then why—"

"Because I don't know whether every memory proving it is mine."

Ori stared.

The sentence was impossible.

And internally coherent.

Love known.

Evidence distrusted.

Mau looked toward the stairs.

"If I ask her, she'll choose safety for me."

"Maybe she should."

"Maybe."

That answer frightened Ori more than refusal.

He had already decided to leave.
