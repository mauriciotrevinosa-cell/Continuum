# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 9 — Then I'm Coming

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E14 — `Second Departure`

Ori went upstairs.

Three steps.

Stopped.

If she woke Frieren, Mau would know.

If Mau knew, he might leave immediately.

Alone.

Ori hated that her options had become shaped like betrayals.

She came back down.

Mau was already putting on his coat.

"You're not going alone."

He looked at her.

"Ori—"

"Then I'm coming."

"No."

"That wasn't a question."

Mau almost smiled.

Wrong time.

Wrong person to make it easier.

"Ori, if something happens—"

"If something happens, then it happens with someone there."

He looked away.

"You should tell Frieren."

"You should tell Frieren."

Silence.

Ori won because the argument was symmetrical and she was more stubborn.

She packed food.

Enough this time.

Water.

Blankets.

The evidence notebook.

Mau objected to that.

Ori ignored him.

Before leaving, she stood outside Frieren's door.

Hand raised.

Did not knock.

"I'm sorry," she whispered.

Not because she was taking Mau.

Because she was helping him leave.

The distinction would matter later.

It did not make the guilt smaller.
