# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 5 — The Road Home Is Slow

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Continuity state:** Day 13–14 | Mau transported north | Field rescue transitions to travel

They moved at noon.

Slowly.

Mau on a reinforced litter.

Yuta and Rimuru alternating medical checks.

Frieren beside him.

Ori walking when she should have been riding.

Rem forced Ori onto the supply wagon after two hours.

Ori hated her.

Then fell asleep immediately.

Aira walked rear security with Mikasa.

Kaneki stayed close enough to recognize biological warning signs.

No one assigned these positions permanently.

They emerged from competence.

The road home took two days.

That mattered.

Night one, Mau woke and asked whether the monster was dead.

Yes.

Asked whether Ori was alive.

Yes.

Asked whether Frieren hated him.

Frieren answered:

"I'm angry."

Mau looked relieved.

That surprised her.

"Why?"

"That's not hate."

Correct.

He slept again.

At the old inn, G5 received updates.

Fern converted one room into recovery overflow.

The new arrival from the previous day stayed.

One G5 member left for the native village as planned.

Another postponed departure to help with meals.

The future site continued work under reduced leadership.

Drainage tests happened.

One path was rerouted.

No one moved there.

The city did not secretly advance into habitation.

Day fourteen, the rescue caravan reached the last ridge.

Mau woke enough to see the inn roof through trees.

He cried.

Quietly.

Frieren pretended not to notice until he reached for her hand.

Then she held it.
