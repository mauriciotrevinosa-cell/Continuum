# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 6 — Home Again

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Continuity state:** Day 14 — evening | Mau physically returned to old inn | Rem/Kaneki/Aira guests, not residents yet

The second return was quieter.

No giant doorway crowd.

They had learned.

Fern controlled access.

Maomao controlled the room.

Frieren controlled nothing and hated that.

Mau was moved upstairs.

Not absorbed.

Not magically solved.

Bed.

Water.

Dark room.

Physical medicine first.

Rem entered the old inn for the first time carrying the cap.

Kaneki entered and immediately noticed food smells he could not use.

Aira entered and said:

"This place has too many shoes."

Momo, from the stairs:

"THANK YOU."

First bond.

Nano recorded their status:

GUEST / RECOVERY.

Not resident.

Not Searcher.

Not rescue squad.

Guest.

Ori went to her room.

Closed the door.

Frieren watched.

Did not follow.

Later.

The cap sat on a table downstairs.

Bocchi saw it.

Stopped.

Momo saw Bocchi stop.

Then everyone nearby went quiet for a different reason.

"Where did you find that?"

Rem explained.

River.

No magic.

No certainty it had anything to do with Mau's route.

Bocchi touched the brim.

Dirty.

Warped.

Mau had lost it while getting her home.

Now it had returned while strangers tried to get Mau home.

Bocchi cried.

Rem did not know the whole history.

Momo told her.

Too loudly.

Halfway through, Rem understood enough.

She looked upstairs.

Object mattered because person had not chosen it over someone he loved.

That was all.

Bocchi cleaned the cap.

Not fully.

She left one water stain.

History should not always disappear when washed.
