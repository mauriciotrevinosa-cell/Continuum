# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 12 — By the River

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E15 — `Second Search`

Rem stopped first.

Mikasa had already passed the bend.

Kaneki turned when he noticed Rem was no longer behind him.

Aira nearly walked into Kaneki.

"What?"

Rem looked toward the river.

Not at a footprint.

Not a broken branch.

The air.

"Something is wrong there."

Mikasa returned.

"What kind?"

Rem shook her head.

"I don't know."

That answer would have been useless from someone pretending expertise.

Rem wasn't.

They moved carefully.

The river widened beneath an eroded bank.

Mud.

Branches.

Trash carried from farther upstream.

A strip of black fabric caught between roots.

Aira pulled it free.

Not fabric.

A cap.

Dirty.

Water-worn.

Orange detail almost hidden beneath mud.

Rem took it.

No reaction.

She did not know it.

Kaneki didn't either.

Aira looked closer.

"Wait."

She had seen Mau wearing other motorsport clothes.

Not this cap.

Still—

"That looks like his."

Rem held it.

"His?"

"Maybe."

Not proof.

A clue.

They marked the location.

Then Rem looked downstream.

The wrongness continued.
