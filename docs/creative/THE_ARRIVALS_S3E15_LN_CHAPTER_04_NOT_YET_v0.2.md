# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 10 — Not Yet

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Continuity state:** Day 16 — night | Mau and Frieren discuss containment privately

"You said no."

Mau's voice was weak.

Frieren sat beside him.

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because I don't want you inside Rimuru."

Mau almost smiled.

"Understandable."

"Not funny."

"No."

Silence.

Mau asked:

"Is it dangerous?"

"We don't know."

"Is staying like this dangerous?"

Frieren looked at him.

Mau understood.

"Yes."

She hated that.

He continued.

"I don't want to disappear."

Frieren's hand found his.

"You're not."

"I mean inside."

Memory.

Self.

The fear Memory Arc had been building.

Mau looked at her.

"If I say yes now, do you trust it?"

Frieren stopped.

He had identified the consent problem himself.

"Do you?"

"I don't know."

Honest.

Mau squeezed her hand.

"Then not yet."

Frieren exhaled.

Relief.

Fear.

Mau said:

"If it gets worse, ask me again when I'm clear."

"How do I know you're clear?"

He smiled without humor.

"You don't."

Both sat with that.

Then Mau said:

"Ask Ori too."

Frieren looked at him.

"Why?"

"She catches me when my memories shift."

Frieren nodded.

No jealousy.

Pain, maybe.

Trust anyway.

"And Yuta."

"Yes."

"Maomao."

"Yes."

Mau looked at her.

"Not just you."

Frieren understood.

No single person owns the decision.

Even when love is strongest.

"Okay."

Mau closed his eyes.

"Not yet."

"Not yet."

They waited.
