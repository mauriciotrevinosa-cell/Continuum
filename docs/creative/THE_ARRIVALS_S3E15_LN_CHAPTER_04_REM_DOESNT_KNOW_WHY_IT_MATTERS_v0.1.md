# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 14 — Rem Doesn't Know Why It Matters

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S3E15 — `Second Search`

Rem met Frieren at the river.

Frieren still had the cap.

Rem looked at it.

"So it is his."

"Yes."

"Was it important?"

Frieren considered.

"Not enough."

Rem waited.

Frieren looked at the ruined brim.

"He lost it while getting Bocchi home."

"Who is Bocchi?"

"My family."

That answer covered enough.

Rem looked downstream.

"So the object mattered less than the person."

"Yes."

"And now the object came back while you're looking for him."

Frieren looked at Rem.

Rem realized how the sentence sounded.

"I don't mean fate."

"Good."

"I mean..."

She stopped.

The cap had a history she did not share.

That was the point.

People around Mau carried pieces of him she had not lived.

Rem had found one by accident.

Now she was part of its next story.

"I don't know why it matters," Rem said.

Frieren looked at the cap.

"You don't have to."

They kept searching.
