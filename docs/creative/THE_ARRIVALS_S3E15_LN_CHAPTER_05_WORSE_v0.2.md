Correct angle.

Good.

Bad.

"Where's the river?"

"No."

"Mau."

"The monster—"

"Dead."

"The inn."

Good.

"Ori."

Good.

"Who is she?"

Then fear.

"Frieren."

Good.

"Yes."

"Did she?"

"No."

Good.

"Maybe."

"How?"

"Containment."
