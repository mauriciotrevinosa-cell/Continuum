# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 17 — He Comes Out

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Continuity state:** Day 18 | Mau exits containment after stabilization | Not cured

Mau emerged the next morning.

Human.

Exhausted.

Still concussed.

No kagune visible.

Frieren touched him before anyone asked a question.

He leaned into her.

Good.

Rimuru sat down immediately afterward.

Also exhausted.

Containment had not been free.

Raphael's active monitoring load had consumed attention Rimuru could have used elsewhere.

The worksite had gone another day without him.

A route report waited unopened.

G5 had managed breakfast.

The world had continued.

Mau looked at Rimuru.

"Did we solve it?"

"No."

"Good."

Rimuru glared.

Mau smiled weakly.

"Consistent answer."

They now had a working label.

The Noise.

Not villain.

Not diagnosis.

Not origin.

An endogenous process Raphael could partially observe under containment conditions.

Separate enough from the memory manipulation that nobody should conflate them.

Connected enough to Mau's overload that ignoring it would be stupid.

The witches hated the name.

One said it lacked poetry.

Mau said:

"Good."

Maomao ended the conversation.

"Bed."

Mau looked at Frieren.

"Whose?"

"Yours."

"The inn?"

Frieren stared.

"Yes."

Location check.

Correct.

He slept.
