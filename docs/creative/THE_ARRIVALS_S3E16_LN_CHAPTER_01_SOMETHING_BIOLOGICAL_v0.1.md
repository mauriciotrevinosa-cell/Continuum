# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 15 — Something Biological

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E16 — `Body Noise`

Ori knew Mau had a fever before Mau did.

"You are shaking."

"I'm cold."

"It's warm."

"Then I'm efficiently cold."

"That's not a thing."

They had reached an abandoned riverside structure before sunset.

Mau had stopped being able to walk straight an hour later.

The Noise no longer felt like memory.

It felt like processing.

Too much.

Systems touching systems that had no reason to know one another.

His back hurt.

Then his shoulders.

Then something moved beneath the skin.

Ori froze.

"Mau."

"Don't."

"You don't know what I'm going to say."

"I know."

He did.

That memory was correct.

Something unfolded behind him.

Not cleanly.

Dark organic structure.

Wrong enough that Ori moved backward before she could stop herself.

Mau saw.

His face collapsed.

Ori moved forward again.

Immediately.

"I moved because it surprised me."

"Sure."

"I'm serious."

The structure twitched.

Mau gasped.

No control.

No understanding.

A biological expression the Noise had observed somewhere else and had no business constructing now.

Ori sat beside him anyway.

"You're still Mau."

He closed his eyes.

"I don't know if that's comforting from the person my memories never get wrong."

Ori took his hand.

"Then don't use the memory."

"What?"

"Use now."

Mau looked at her.

Ori squeezed.

"Now I'm here."
