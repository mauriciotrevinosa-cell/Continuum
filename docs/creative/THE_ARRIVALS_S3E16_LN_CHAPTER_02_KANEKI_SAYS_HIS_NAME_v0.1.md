# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 16 — Kaneki Says His Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S3E16 — `Body Noise`

Kaneki saw the structure through the broken wall.

Everyone else saw danger first.

Kaneki saw biology.

Not his biology exactly.

Close enough to be terrible.

"Don't attack it."

Mikasa stopped.

Aira stopped because Mikasa did.

Rem had already stopped.

Kaneki moved closer.

The organic structure flexed behind Mau.

Kagune-like.

Incomplete.

Unstable.

Mau looked at him with fever-bright eyes.

"You know what this is."

"Part of it."

"Then tell them."

Kaneki looked at the others.

"This doesn't mean he's becoming a different person."

Mau laughed.

Bad sound.

"You don't know that."

"No."

Kaneki came closer anyway.

"I know bodies can become evidence people use against your identity."

Mau stopped laughing.

Kaneki looked at the structure.

"This is a body problem."

Then at Mau.

"His name is Mau."

Simple.

Not a diagnosis.

Not metaphysics.

A refusal to let visible monstrosity make the philosophical decision for them.

Ori started crying.

She looked angry about it.

Kaneki pretended not to notice.
