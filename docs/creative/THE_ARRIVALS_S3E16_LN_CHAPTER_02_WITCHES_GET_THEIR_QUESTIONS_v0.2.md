# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 18 — Witches Get Their Questions

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Continuity state:** Day 19 | Mau physically recovering | Witch bargain continues after containment

The witches returned.

Mau almost admired their consistency.

Almost.

This time he chose a shorter session.

Thirty minutes.

Frieren not present.

That surprised her.

Mau explained:

"I need to know I can do this without you in the room."

Frieren hated it.

Accepted.

Ori stayed.

Qifrey stayed.

One witch asked whether Mau remembered containment.

Yes.

Raphael's voice.

Partial structure.

The name.

The Noise.

"Does the name feel old?"

Mau thought.

"No."

Good question.

"Does the process?"

Pause.

"Yes."

Bad answer.

Or useful.

Mau could not distinguish.

Another question:

"Did the memory manipulation ever speak in the same form?"

"No."

"Did The Noise tell you to leave?"

Mau stopped.

"No."

Important.

The memories did.

The certainty did.

The Noise, as far as he could support, had not issued the command.

The group wrote:

DO NOT CONFLATE MEMORY MANIPULATOR WITH THE NOISE.

Capital letters.

Qifrey underlined.

Question:

"Do you think The Noise is you?"

Mau looked at Ori.

Then away.

"I don't know."

No one answered for him.

Thirty minutes ended.

Mau ended it himself.

The witch stopped immediately.

Agreement still intact.

Outside, Frieren waited but did not ask for every word.

Mau told her what he wanted to.

Agency repeated until it became habit.
