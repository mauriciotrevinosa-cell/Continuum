# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 18 — Extraction

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Source episode:** S3E17 — `Extraction`

Frieren arrived during the worst part.

Mau was conscious.

That was not the same as lucid.

Aira and Kaneki had moved him out of the structure.

Rem had one hand on Ori because Ori kept trying to help from angles that made the biological growth react.

Mikasa held the perimeter.

Frieren landed.

Mau looked at her.

Everything in him changed.

Relief.

Fear.

Guilt.

The kagune-like structure snapped open.

Frieren did not raise her staff.

That choice happened before thought.

"Mau."

He shook his head.

"You shouldn't be here."

Frieren's face hardened.

"Again?"

The word reached him.

Not now.

Not the argument.

Mau tried to stand.

Failed.

Ori moved.

Frieren saw her.

For one second the second disappearance became personal.

"You knew."

Ori's face broke.

"Yes."

Frieren could have asked why.

She did not.

Not while Mau was burning with fever on the ground.

Later.

That restraint was love too.

Rimuru arrived as the structure convulsed.

Raphael's assessment came fast.

Containment.

Isolation.

Reduce external stimuli.

Prevent further cross-system cascade.

Rimuru looked at Mau.

"I need permission."

Mau laughed weakly.

"For what?"

"To put you inside me."

Aira stared.

"That sentence needs work."

Nobody laughed.

Mau looked at Frieren.

Then Ori.

Then Kaneki.

"Will I still be me?"

Rimuru did not lie.

"I don't know what happens to the Noise."

Mau's breathing hitched.

"But I know I'm not eating your identity."

That was enough.

"Okay."

Rimuru absorbed him.

Frieren watched Mau disappear into slime.

This time, nobody called it death.
