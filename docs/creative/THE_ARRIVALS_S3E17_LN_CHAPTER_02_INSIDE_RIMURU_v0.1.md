# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 19 — Inside Rimuru

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 19  
**Source episode:** S3E17 — `Extraction`

Inside Rimuru was not darkness.

Not exactly.

Mau experienced pieces.

Pressure reduced.

Noise filtered.

Biological output slowed.

Foreign-seeming structures compartmentalized enough to stop touching everything at once.

Raphael spoke through categories Mau could barely follow.

No certainty.

No revelation.

No secret file labeled `MAU TRUE SELF.`

That disappointed him.

Then relieved him.

Outside, Rimuru sat on the riverbank and did not move.

Milim arrived.

Then Diablo.

Then Yuta.

Frieren stayed closest.

Ori stayed farther away because guilt had become physical distance.

Rem held the ruined cap.

Nobody had remembered to take it from her.

Rimuru's eyes opened.

"He's stable."

Frieren asked:

"Fixed?"

"No."

Good answer.

Terrible answer.

"Can he come out?"

"Not yet."

Frieren sat.

"Then we wait."

Diablo looked at Rimuru.

"Do you understand the structure?"

"Parts."

"Source?"

"No."

"Architect?"

"No."

"Can it happen again?"

Rimuru closed his eyes.

"Yes."

Nobody liked that.

The rescue had succeeded.

The problem had survived.
