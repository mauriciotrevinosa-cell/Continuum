# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 24 — No. But You Can Stay.

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 24  
**Continuity state:** Day 23 | Mau allowed limited ordinary activity at old inn/worksite

Mau returned to the worksite for two hours.

No lifting.

No Construction.

No solo wandering.

He accepted all three restrictions with visible suffering.

The site had changed.

Still not home.

More framing.

Covered storage.

A better path around mud.

One water line marked for later.

People had kept building while he broke.

That hurt.

Then Wakana walked past carrying cloth.

Mau asked:

"Need help?"

Wakana checked the bundle.

"No."

The word hit deeper than it should have.

Wakana continued:

"But you can stay."

Mau froze.

No invented job.

No courtesy task.

Presence without usefulness.

He sat on an overturned crate.

Coco joined him after ten minutes.

Agott joined because Coco had stopped working.

Qifrey joined because both students were now not working.

Productivity declined.

Nobody died.

Mau laughed.

Later he asked Fern if she needed help with route records.

"No. Sit."

Maomao:

"No. Don't touch that."

Bocchi:

"No. Kita has it."

Every answer accidentally repeated the same lesson.

By evening it became a joke.

Mau entered the inn.

Umaru pointed at a chair.

"Nobody needs you. Sit."

"You're all awful."

Frieren looked up from his hoodie.

"Stay."

Mau did.
