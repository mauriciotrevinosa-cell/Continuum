# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 20 — Raphael Cannot Tell You Who You Are

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 20  
**Source episode:** S3E17 — `Extraction`

Mau emerged exhausted.

Human-shaped.

The biological structure gone.

For now.

Maomao checked him.

Kaneki checked what Maomao could not.

Frieren checked nothing.

She touched his face.

That was enough.

Later, Mau asked Rimuru privately.

"Can Raphael tell which memories are real now?"

Rimuru's silence answered first.

"No."

"Can she tell where they came from?"

"Some signatures. Not origin."

"Can she tell what I was before Continuum?"

"No."

Mau laughed.

No humor.

"Useful."

Rimuru got angry.

"Yes."

Mau looked up.

Rimuru continued.

"Useful. Not god."

Raphael did not object.

"She kept your body from cascading."

"I know."

"She separated enough cross-system activity for you to come back out."

"I know."

"She cannot tell you who you are."

Mau looked away.

Rimuru's voice softened.

"Neither can I."

That was the point.

Everyone could help him survive.

Nobody could own the answer.
