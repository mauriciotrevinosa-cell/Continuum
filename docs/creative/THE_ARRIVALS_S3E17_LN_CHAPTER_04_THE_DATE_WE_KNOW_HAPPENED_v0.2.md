# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 26 — The Date We Know Happened

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 26  
**Continuity state:** Day 25 | Mau cleared for short outing | Deliberately new present memory

Frieren asked Mau on a date.

Explicitly.

"Tomorrow."

Mau looked at her over breakfast.

"Why?"

Frieren stared.

"Because I want to."

The answer stopped him.

Not:
because you need normality.

Not:
because recovery requires exposure.

Not:
because we should test your memory.

Want.

Mau nodded.

"Okay."

Maomao ruined the romance fifteen minutes later.

"Two hours."

Mau looked offended.

"Dates have time limits?"

"Yours does."

"No alcohol."

"We don't drink that much."

"No strenuous magic."

Frieren looked at Mau.

"We weren't planning strenuous magic."

Maomao looked unconvinced by both of them.

They left after lunch.

Known road.

Nearby village.

No search route.

No hidden purpose.

Mau still checked the first three landmarks.

Frieren noticed.

At the fourth he stopped.

"What?"

she asked.

"I'm checking."

"I know."

"Annoying?"

"No."

That helped.

The village had changed almost not at all since their last visit.

One stall moved.

A dog had grown.

A roof repair looked worse.

Mau liked that.

Reality did not need to become cinematic because he was recovering.

They bought bread.

Bad bread.

Mau bit once.

Looked at Frieren.

"You chose this."

Frieren ate another piece.

"Yes."

"Because our early food was terrible?"

"No."

"Then why?"

"It was closest."

Mau stared.

"That's less romantic."

"Good."

He laughed.

They sat near water.

Not the river from the second disappearance.

Different water.

Important enough that Mau noticed.

Frieren stole part of his bread.

Mau objected.

"You have your own."

"Yours is better."

"They are from the same loaf."

"Yes."

"That makes no sense."

Frieren ate it anyway.

Mau watched.

A thought came.

Is this real?

Not a memory.

Present.

He looked around.

Stone wall.

Water.

Blue cloth hanging from a stall.

Frieren chewing stolen bread.

He asked:

"Do you remember this?"

Frieren stopped.

Then understood.

"It is happening."

Mau smiled.

"Right."

The sentence loosened something.

"What if I forget again?"

Frieren looked at him.

Not at the thread.

Not at the water.

At him.

"Then I'll remind you."

Mau swallowed.

"How many times?"

"As many as it takes."

He looked away because that was harder to receive than a guarantee.

It was not:
I can prove every memory for you.

It was:
I will still be here when remembering gets difficult.

Different.

Better.

They walked.

Frieren stopped at a thread seller.

Blue.

She liked one shade.

No symbolic reason.

Mau asked:

"Why that one?"

"I like it."

"That's it?"

"Yes."

He bought it.

Frieren looked at him.

"I didn't ask."

"I know."

"Then why?"

"Because I wanted to."

Her own answer returned.

Fair.

He put the thread in his pocket.

Object.

Today's object.

Not evidence yet.

Just thread.

Later they sat under shade.

Mau's head had started hurting.

He did not hide it.

"Break."

Frieren nodded.

No disappointment.

They sat.

Mau looked at the water.

"What if I remember today wrong later?"

"We can check the thread."

"And if I lose it?"

"I remember."

Mau looked at her.

"And if I don't trust your memory?"

Frieren thought.

Not offended.

Good.

"Then we decide what to do that day."

No eternal guarantee.

No:
trust me forever.

No:
this date will fix you.

Future problem left to future versions of them.

Mau leaned closer.

"Do you know how healthy that sounds?"

"Unfortunately."

He kissed her.

Frieren kissed back.

Not cure.

Not proof.

Not reward after trauma.

A date.

On the walk home, Mau misremembered which stall had sold the thread.

Frieren corrected him.

He laughed.

"Already?"

"Apparently."

"Maybe the seller moved."

"In ten minutes?"

"Fast business."

The mistake did not become crisis.

At the inn, Mau put the blue thread in a small dish.

Not shrine.

Not evidence bag.

A place he could find it.

The date created something Memory Arc had not been able to touch yet:

a memory chosen after the damage.

Its value did not depend on proving every yesterday first.
