# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 26 — The Date We Know Happened

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 26  
**Continuity state:** Day 25 | Mau medically cleared for short outing | Memory Arc recovery beat

Frieren asked Mau on a date.

Explicitly.

"Tomorrow."

Mau looked suspicious.

"Why?"

"Because I want to."

Better answer than because recovery.

They walked to a nearby village.

Short route.

Known.

No search.

No witch questions.

No table.

They bought bread.

Bad bread.

Mau accused Frieren of choosing it because their first meals together had been terrible.

Frieren did not deny it.

They sat by water.

Mau checked himself.

"Do you remember this?"

Frieren looked at him.

"It is happening."

He smiled.

Right.

Present tense.

Frieren stole his bread.

Mau complained.

They bought blue thread because Frieren liked the color and needed no reason.

Mau put it in his pocket.

He asked:

"What if I remember today wrong later?"

"We can check the thread."

"And if I lose it?"

"I remember."

"And if I don't trust your memory?"

Frieren thought.

"Then we'll decide what to do that day."

No eternal guarantee.

No romance cure.

One future problem left for future people.

Mau leaned closer.

"Do you know how healthy that sounds?"

"Unfortunately."

He kissed her.

The date did not repair his autobiography.

It created something else:

a day chosen after the damage.

A memory whose value did not depend on proving every yesterday first.
