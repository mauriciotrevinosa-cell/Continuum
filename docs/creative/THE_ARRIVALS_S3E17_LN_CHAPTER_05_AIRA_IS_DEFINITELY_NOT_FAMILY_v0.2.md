# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 36 — Aira Is Definitely Not Family

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 36  
**Continuity state:** Day 27 | Aira reconnects with Momo/Okarun and begins protector/family integration without admitting it

Aira was definitely not family.

She announced this at breakfast.

Nobody had asked.

Momo looked up.

"Okay."

Aira narrowed her eyes.

"Don't say it like that."

"Like what?"

"Like you don't believe me."

"I said okay."

"Exactly."

Okarun kept eating.

This was wisdom.

Mau had learned similar techniques around Frieren.

Aira pointed at him.

"And you."

Mau froze.

"What did I do?"

"Nothing."

"Great."

"Stop looking grateful when I help."

Mau considered.

"No."

Aira looked offended.

"Why?"

"Because I am grateful."

"That's manipulative."

"How?"

"I don't know yet."

Anko, at the other end of the table:

"Strong argument."

Aira ignored her.

The issue had started because Fern asked where Aira wanted her things stored while Mau recovered.

Not permanently.

Just:
your boots cannot live in the hallway forever.

Aira had heard:
residency.

Then:
family.

Then:
trap.

Nobody else had made those jumps.

Momo peeled fruit.

"You can live here without signing adoption papers."

"There are no adoption papers."

"Exactly."

Aira pointed.

"That's suspicious."

Okarun finally looked up.

"You've slept here for almost two weeks."

"Temporary."

"You know which floorboard outside the bathroom squeaks."

"Security."

"You complain when someone uses your cup."

"Hygiene."

Momo smiled.

Aira:

"Stop."

Momo did not.

The argument should have annoyed Mau.

Instead it made something in his chest loosen.

Dandadan relationships were returning to themselves.

Aira was not only one of the people who found him.

Momo was not only someone waiting at home.

Okarun was not only a name on a search board.

They were three people with their own impossible geometry.

Good.

Mikasa entered.

"Aira."

Aira turned immediately.

"Yes?"

"Route drill."

Aira stood.

Momo:

"You didn't finish eating."

"I'll eat after."

Mikasa looked at the plate.

"Finish."

Aira stared.

Then sat.

Momo covered her mouth.

Mau failed to.

Aira glared at both.

Mikasa did not understand why this was funny.

Better.

After breakfast, Mau walked outside because Maomao had approved exactly one lap around the inn.

Not two.

Aira and Mikasa were marking routes.

Aira saw him.

"You're too far."

Mau looked behind himself.

"I'm twenty meters from the door."

"Twenty-one."

Mikasa:

"He's within limit."

Aira looked betrayed.

Mau:

"Thank you."

Mikasa:

"Do not make me regret it."

Family support remained emotionally hostile.

Excellent.

Aira returned to the marker.

Mau watched.

She was good at this.

Not because she had become Mikasa.

Aira talked more.
Complained more.
Argued with the route.
Wanted to know why every instruction existed.

Mikasa answered when the question was real.

Ignored dramatics when it wasn't.

They worked.

Protector energy finding structure without becoming a cage.

Momo came outside later with Okarun.

She saw Aira halfway up a post.

"Why are you like this?"

Aira:

"Because somebody needs to fix your terrible marker."

"It isn't mine."

"Emotionally yours."

That made no sense.

Okarun accepted it immediately.

Momo did too.

Mau smiled.

Aira saw.

"Stop."

"What?"

"That face."

"Everyone keeps saying that."

"Because your face is annoying."

Momo:

"True."

Mau looked at Okarun.

"Help."

Okarun:

"I've made a choice."

"Which?"

"Survival."

Coward.

That afternoon Aira helped move supplies.

Then route notes.

Then found Vamola trying to understand a storage label and explained it badly but enthusiastically.

At dinner she sat between Momo and Okarun without thinking.

Halfway through, Momo noticed.

Did not say anything.

Growth.

Mau noticed too.

Also did not say anything.

More growth.

Then Aira said:

"I am still temporary."

Nano, across the table:

"Current status: temporary resident."

"Thank you."

Nano continued:

"Social behavior: increasingly integrated."

Aira looked horrified.

"Delete that."

"There is no social-behavior field."

"Then why did you say it?"

"Observation."

Momo collapsed against Okarun laughing.

Okarun tried not to.

Failed.

Aira threw bread.

Mikasa caught it.

Ate it.

The table lost control.

Later, when people separated for the night, Aira stayed behind to help Momo clean.

Momo handed her a cloth.

No:
thank you for saving Mau.

No:
I'm glad you're here.

Not yet.

Aira wiped the table.

Momo bumped her shoulder once.

Aira bumped back harder.

Best friends.

Obviously.

Neither said it.

Aira was definitely not family.

The family had heard worse lies.
