# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 27 — Looks Fine Is Not Fine

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 27  
**Continuity state:** Day 26+ | Dominant Memory crisis de-escalated | Long-term protocols begin

Mau looked fine.

Nobody used that as evidence anymore.

They asked:
- sleep;
- headaches;
- memory mismatches;
- biological pain;
- new Noise fragments;
- whether he wanted company;
- whether he wanted none.

The last question mattered.

Care could become surveillance.

They had nearly done that.

So the protocol included privacy.

Raphael tracked only what Mau had consented to share outside emergencies.

Maomao tracked physical recovery.

Kaneki biological changes.

Ori continuity when asked.

Frieren stopped thinking of herself as tracker.

She lived with him.

Different.

The witches' access became scheduled rather than intrusive.

Diablo remained useful for field anomalies, not internal truth.

The evidence table stayed available.

Not permanent centerpiece.

The worksite continued.

Slowly.

One cluster was approaching actual habitability.

Not ready.

Approaching.

Rimuru brought the latest plan to dinner.

"If we finish water, latrines, covered food storage and two more rooms, we can move a first group."

The table changed.

Move.

Real word.

Mau looked around the old inn.

Too many shoes.

G5 temporary bedding.

Rem's bag near one wall.

Kaneki avoiding dinner smells.

Aira arguing with Momo.

Bocchi's guitar.

Frieren in his clothing.

Ori's cup.

Home.

Then he looked at the map.

Future.

No one said they had already moved.

No one called the site home yet.

Rimuru asked:

"Do we start planning the move?"

This time Mau did not answer first.

Fern did.

"Yes."

Volume Thirteen ended with the household choosing to prepare to leave the old inn without pretending leaving meant abandoning it.
