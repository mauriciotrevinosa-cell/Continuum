# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 30 — Nobody Is Doing Anything

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 30  
**Continuity state:** Post-Memory recovery | Move planning exists but is not allowed to consume the household

For once, nobody was doing anything.

This was not technically true.

Fern was mending a sleeve.

Anko was writing.

Maomao was reading something with enough concentration that everyone had collectively decided not to ask whether it involved poison.

Momo was cutting fruit.

Wakana was repairing a strap because he had failed to understand the assignment.

Marin took the strap out of his hands.

"No."

Wakana blinked.

"It is broken."

"Tomorrow."

"But—"

"Nobody is doing anything."

"You're cutting fruit."

Momo pointed the knife at him.

"That's food."

Apparently food did not count as activity.

Mau sat in the old common-room chair because everyone had independently agreed he should not spend the afternoon standing.

That was how they phrased it.

Independently.

He suspected conspiracy.

Frieren had occupied the couch beside him for approximately eleven minutes before deciding sitting upright was inefficient.

Now her head rested across his thigh.

She had one of his shirts on.

Again.

Mau looked down.

"You have your own clothes."

Frieren did not open her eyes.

"Yes."

"Good talk."

Rimuru, in slime form, dropped from the back of the couch onto Frieren's stomach.

Frieren opened one eye.

Rimuru bounced once.

"Comfortable."

"Move."

"No."

Frieren closed her eye again.

Mau stared.

"How are you alive?"

"Political immunity."

"You don't have political immunity."

"I have slime immunity."

Across the room, Yuta laughed into his cup.

Maki kicked his foot because he was blocking the walkway.

Not hard.

He moved anyway.

Umaru had begun the afternoon sitting on the floor with a game board nobody understood.

Twenty minutes later she was asleep against the side of Mau's chair.

One hand still held a piece.

Coco arrived with three books.

She took one look at the available space around Mau.

Then looked offended.

"There is nowhere to sit."

Bocchi, from the wall:

"There is floor."

Coco considered.

"True."

She sat between Bocchi and the chair.

Bocchi shifted closer by approximately four centimeters.

No one commented.

Important.

Ori entered from the kitchen with two cups.

One for herself.

One for Mau.

She set his down within reach.

"Water."

"Thank you."

"Did you drink the last one?"

Mau hesitated.

Ori looked at Fern.

Fern looked at Mau.

Mau picked up the cup.

"I am being oppressed by accountability."

Anko did not look up from her writing.

"Put that on the family crest."

Mau drank.

Ori sat on the arm of the couch near Frieren's feet.

Not clinging.

Not proving she could be somewhere else.

Just choosing there.

Mau noticed.

Then deliberately did not turn it into a lesson.

That was harder than it sounded.

Marin dropped onto the floor beside Coco.

"Okay. Important question."

Everyone ignored her.

"Extremely important question."

Momo:

"If this is clothes—"

"It is not clothes."

Wakana relaxed.

"It is curtains."

Wakana stopped relaxing.

"The new place has terrible curtains."

"It has temporary cloth over openings," Fern said.

"Exactly."

"Because the walls are not finished."

"Excuses."

Mau looked toward the planning board near the door.

New site.

Sleeping cluster.

Water.

Drainage.

Storage.

Move sequence.

His eyes stayed there too long.

Frieren reached up without looking and put two fingers against his chin.

Turned his face away from the board.

"No."

Mau looked down at her.

"You don't even know what I was thinking."

"Construction."

"Maybe."

"No."

He smiled.

"Okay."

That was when Nijika and Ryo came in carrying nothing.

A remarkable contribution.

Kita immediately moved.

Bocchi immediately did not.

Then reconsidered and shifted enough for Nijika to sit near her.

Ryo chose the floor next to the fruit.

Momo slapped her hand away.

"Ask."

"Can I steal fruit?"

"No."

Ryo stole fruit.

Momo looked at Nijika.

Nijika:

"I've been trying for years."

G5 had been in the inn long enough that this no longer felt like an introduction scene.

That mattered.

Mai came through next.

Saw Maki.

Saw the lack of useful activity.

"Gross."

Maki pointed at an empty chair.

Mai sat.

Also important.

Seiko arrived last among the people who claimed they were not gathering.

She looked around the room.

At the overcrowded furniture.

At the people on the floor.

At Frieren using Mau as furniture.

At Rimuru using Frieren as furniture.

At Umaru asleep against Mau's leg.

At Bocchi sitting close enough that her shoulder almost touched Coco's.

At Anko writing without needing silence.

Seiko snorted.

"You people need a bigger house."

Mau looked at the planning board again.

This time everyone did.

Marin immediately pointed.

"Not today."

Mau raised both hands.

"Not today."

Good.

The future could wait until after dinner.

For three hours, nobody did anything.

Later, Mau would remember more of that afternoon than some days in which they saved lives.

He did not say that.

He did not want to make the moment perform.

So he sat.

Frieren slept.

Rimuru eventually slid off her.

Umaru stole Mau's blanket without waking.

Bocchi played nothing.

Anko wrote three pages.

Momo fed everyone.

Nothing happened.

The household remained alive anyway.
