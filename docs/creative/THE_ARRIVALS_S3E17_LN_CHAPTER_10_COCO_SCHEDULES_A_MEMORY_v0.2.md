# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 34 — Coco Schedules a Memory

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 34  
**Continuity state:** Post-Memory recovery | Witch Hat circle creates deliberate ordinary time with Mau

Coco arrived with a schedule.

This was dangerous.

Mau looked at the paper.

"No."

"You haven't read it."

"It has colors."

"That means it's organized."

"That means somebody cared too much."

Agott, behind her:

"I told her not to color it."

Richeh:

"I liked the colors."

Tetia:

"I added the stars."

Mau looked at Qifrey.

"Help."

Qifrey smiled.

"No."

Betrayal.

Coco put the schedule on the table.

TODAY:
- WALK
- MATERIAL SHOP
- LUNCH
- ONE SAFE SPELL TEST
- NO MEMORY TESTS
- NO MEDICAL QUESTIONS
- NO SETTLEMENT MEETING
- DESSERT

Mau read the list again.

"Why is dessert separate from lunch?"

Tetia looked horrified.

"Because dessert is important."

Correct.

Mau pointed at the third line from the bottom.

"Why does this exist?"

Coco's expression softened.

Because the joke was not the whole reason.

"You and Frieren made a memory on purpose."

Mau looked at her.

The date.

The promise.

If I forget again.

Then I'll remind you.

How many times?

As many as it takes.

Coco tapped the page.

"I want one too."

Mau's chest tightened.

Coco immediately panicked.

"Not like— I don't mean I need a special— I mean with us. All of us. Because the last time we did things you were—"

"Breaking?"

Agott:

"Coco."

"What? He was."

Mau smiled.

"Accurate."

Coco folded her arms.

"We already have memories. I know. That's not the point."

Qifrey spoke from the doorway.

"The point is that she wants one whose purpose is not recovery."

Coco looked at him.

"Yes."

Mau looked at the schedule again.

"One safe spell test?"

Coco brightened.

"One."

"Define safe."

Agott:

"I will."

"Good."

They left after breakfast.

Frieren did not come.

That surprised Coco.

Mau noticed.

Coco tried not to ask.

Mau asked for her.

"You're not coming?"

Frieren looked up from her book.

"No."

"Why?"

"Coco asked you."

Mau looked at Coco.

Coco looked suddenly unsure.

Frieren added:

"Bring him back before dark."

Agott:

"We know."

Frieren:

"I was talking to Coco."

Coco straightened.

"I will."

Mau pointed between them.

"Why do I feel like cargo?"

"Because you are slow right now," Agott said.

Fair.

The walk was ordinary.

That became its own challenge.

Mau kept waiting for his head to produce something false.

A road remembered wrong.

A conversation that had not happened.

A person in the wrong place.

Nothing.

Coco talked enough to occupy every available cognitive channel.

Useful.

Richeh stopped for a plant.

Agott told her not to touch it.

Maomao was not there, so Mau had no idea who was correct.

Tetia collected a stone because it looked like a frog.

Qifrey asked Mau about a spell structure.

Mau answered until the edge of understanding became uncomfortable.

Then stopped.

Qifrey stopped too.

No pressure.

At the material stall, Coco argued over paper.

Not price.

Texture.

Mau watched her hands move when she explained.

Familiar.

Real.

Not because someone else confirmed it.

Because he was here now.

At lunch, Richeh asked:

"Do you remember the first time Coco made you help with practice?"

Mau froze.

Coco froze harder.

Richeh immediately understood.

"Sorry."

Mau looked at the table.

Then at Coco.

"Tell me what you remember."

Coco did.

Not to overwrite him.

Just her version.

Mau listened.

Some matched.

Some he could not retrieve.

One detail felt wrong.

He said so.

Coco nodded.

No one tried to settle it.

Then Tetia pointed at his food.

"You're not eating."

Mau ate.

The world continued.

The spell test happened in an empty patch outside town.

Coco drew.

Agott corrected.

Richeh suggested.

Tetia made something unnecessarily pretty.

Qifrey let them solve it.

Mau contributed one observation.

Then deliberately stepped back.

Coco finished the pattern without him.

When it worked, she turned toward Mau first.

Then caught herself.

Looked at Agott.

Richeh.

Tetia.

Qifrey.

Then back at Mau.

Everyone.

Better.

On the road home, Frieren was waiting at the turn.

Mau saw her and smiled.

Coco saw him smile.

She saw Frieren's face change by almost nothing.

Still enough.

Coco ran ahead.

"We returned him."

Frieren:

"Acceptable condition?"

Agott:

"Debatable."

Mau:

"I am standing right here."

Frieren reached for his hand.

Coco reached for his other one.

Mau looked down.

Then at Frieren.

Frieren looked at Coco.

No jealousy.

Just assessment.

Then Frieren took Coco's wrist with her free hand when the path narrowed and pulled her away from a muddy edge.

Coco blinked.

Walked between them for six steps.

Mau noticed.

Frieren noticed him noticing.

"Don't make it weird."

"I said nothing."

"You were going to."

"Maybe."

Behind them, Agott groaned.

The schedule ended with dessert.

They ate it at the inn.

Mau wrote one line in his recovery notes before bed:

COCO MADE A SCHEDULE. IT WAS TERRIBLE.

Then underneath:

It happened.

No verification column.

No witness signatures.

Just a new memory allowed to be ordinary.
