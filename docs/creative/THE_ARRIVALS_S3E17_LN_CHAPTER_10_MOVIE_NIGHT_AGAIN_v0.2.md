# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 32 — Movie Night, Again

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 32  
**Continuity state:** Post-Memory recovery | Household ritual resumes before The Move

Movie night failed before it began.

Too many people.

That was the first problem.

The second problem was Milim.

"Why is the hero not fighting?"

"Because this is not that kind of movie," Rimuru said.

"Bad movie."

"It started thirty seconds ago."

"Enough evidence."

The third problem was seating.

The inn had once held movie night.

The inn had not been designed for:
G1,
G2,
G3,
G4,
the people who had arrived since,
G5,
and several people who technically claimed they were only visiting.

The distinction stopped mattering when everyone wanted the same floor space.

Marin had prepared cushions.

Not enough.

Wakana had prepared additional cushions because he knew Marin.

Still not enough.

Rimuru eventually became one.

Umaru claimed him first.

Milim objected on territorial grounds.

Diablo offered his seat to Rimuru.

Rimuru stared.

"I am the seat."

"Then I have failed."

"Sit down."

Diablo sat.

This earned him a place beside Seiko.

Seiko looked at him once.

"You always like this?"

Diablo smiled.

"Often."

"Unfortunate."

Momo laughed loud enough that Okarun looked over.

Aira stole popcorn from both of them.

Vamola copied Aira.

Turbo Granny called the movie stupid before understanding the premise.

Nazuna fell asleep during the opening credits.

Anko put a blanket over her without interrupting her own conversation with Mau.

Mau noticed the gesture.

Anko caught him noticing.

"Don't."

"I didn't say anything."

"You had a face."

Apparently his face was a recurring legal liability.

Bocchi sat with Kita.

Then Nijika.

Then Ryo appeared and somehow the arrangement became four people occupying space intended for two.

Bocchi looked compressed.

Happy.

Different from trapped.

Mau knew the difference now.

Coco sat near Qifrey at first.

Halfway through, she migrated toward Mau, Frieren and Fern because Umaru had found snacks.

Agott followed five minutes later while claiming she had not followed.

Richeh and Tetia followed Agott.

There went the floor.

Hakari, Karane, Shizuka and Nano occupied a blanket together.

Kusuri arrived late because Maomao had made her relabel something.

"What did I miss?"

Nano:

"Thirty-seven minutes."

Karane:

"She means the movie!"

"I answered the question."

Mau smiled.

Kusuri sat.

Maomao entered behind her.

"I am only here because the workroom is louder."

Momo:

"Liar."

Maomao sat beside Jinshi anyway.

Maki and Yuta shared a wall.

Stark sat near Fern until Fern told him he was blocking someone.

He moved too far.

Fern looked annoyed.

He moved halfway back.

Better.

Himmel watched this with the expression of a man recognizing an ancient and unsolvable problem.

Frieren sat beside Mau.

No dramatic placement.

She simply did.

Her shoulder touched his.

Ori arrived after everyone else.

She stopped at the doorway.

No space.

Mau began to move.

Ori shook her head.

Then climbed over two cushions and sat on the floor directly in front of Mau's knees.

Her back rested lightly against the couch.

Choice.

No one explained it.

Rem had said she was leaving before the movie.

She did.

Then returned twenty minutes later because she had forgotten something.

Momo handed her a bowl.

Rem stayed.

The forgotten thing remained forgotten.

Senku claimed he was only observing whatever mechanism Rimuru was using to reproduce the movie.

Suika knew this was false.

She sat beside him.

Halfway through, Umaru pulled Suika into the younger group because Senku would not share snacks correctly.

Senku said nothing.

Did not retrieve her.

Watched the movie.

At one point everyone laughed.

Not everyone at the same thing.

That made it worse.

Mau looked around.

He should not have.

The room did something dangerous to him.

Too many people.

Too many remembered arrivals.

Too many versions of home.

A thought surfaced:

What if this isn't real?

Small.

Not crisis.

Still there.

Frieren felt his hand change under hers.

She did not say:
this is real.

Instead:

"Do you want to go outside?"

Mau listened to the room.

Milim complaining.

Karane yelling.

Anko laughing.

Bocchi making a noise when Ryo whispered something.

Momo telling Aira to stop.

Nazuna snoring.

"No."

Frieren nodded.

That was all.

Mau looked toward Rem.

She was explaining something quietly to Ori.

Toward Mai.

Arguing with Maki.

Toward Nijika and Ryo.

Already part of Bocchi's noise.

Toward Senku.

Pretending not to be comfortable.

Toward Seiko.

Watching everyone with the expression of someone who had accidentally acquired too many grandchildren.

G5 had arrived asking:

Who is Mau?

Movie night did not answer the question cleanly.

Good.

There was no clean answer.

Mau was not the room.

The room functioned when he was quiet.

People loved one another in directions that did not pass through him.

And still, when the movie ended, Marin looked at the crowd and said:

"The new common room is too small."

Fern:

"It has not even opened."

"Too small."

Rimuru returned to human form.

"She's probably right."

Mau groaned.

"We haven't moved yet."

Coco raised her hand.

"Can my room be near yours?"

Silence.

Not because the request was strange.

Because it was first.

Mau looked at her.

"Why?"

Coco shrugged.

"Because I want it there."

Simple.

Mau felt an argument forming in his head about independence.

Frieren squeezed his fingers.

Not warning.

Reminder.

Coco had answered.

Mau smiled.

"Okay."

Umaru, immediately:

"Mine too."

Bocchi looked at the floor.

Then:

"Um."

Everyone looked at her.

Terrible.

She almost disappeared.

Kita rescued nothing.

Waited.

Bocchi forced the words out.

"Not... directly. Because music. But... close."

Mau swallowed.

"Okay."

Ori did not ask.

She only looked at the planning board.

Mau followed her eyes.

The quiet corner he and Frieren had tentatively marked no longer looked quiet.

Frieren saw it too.

Mau whispered:

"I think we're losing."

Frieren looked at the crowd.

Then at the plan.

"Yes."

Movie night ended.

The Move had just become a family argument.
