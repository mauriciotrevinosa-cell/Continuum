# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 21 — Reconstruction

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 21  
**Source episode:** S3E18 — `Reconstruction`

The second reconstruction was less optimistic.

Better.

They knew the categories were tools.

Not salvation.

Mau sat at the table.

Frieren on one side.

Ori on the other.

That arrangement happened without planning.

The conversation with Ori came first.

Frieren asked:

"Why didn't you tell me?"

Ori looked down.

"Because he would have left without me."

Frieren's jaw tightened.

"You don't know that."

"I do."

Mau interrupted.

"She's right."

Frieren turned.

"That does not help."

Mau stopped.

Ori continued.

"I thought if I told you, you would stop him."

"Yes."

"And then next time he would leave without telling me."

Frieren hated the logic because she could see it.

"Then you come to me anyway."

Ori's eyes filled.

"And if he runs while I do?"

Frieren had no clean answer.

That was the wound.

Not betrayal.

Damaged options.

She reached across Mau and took Ori's hand.

"I'm still angry."

Ori nodded.

"Okay."

"Very."

"Okay."

"I'm not asking you to disappear because of it."

Ori looked up.

That mattered more than forgiveness.

They returned to the evidence.
