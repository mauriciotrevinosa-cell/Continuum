# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 22 — The Date We Know Happened

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 22  
**Source episode:** S3E18 — `Reconstruction`

Frieren asked Mau on a date.

Explicitly.

That was unusual enough to become evidence by itself.

"Tomorrow."

Mau looked at her.

"Why?"

"Because."

"Strong argument."

"Yes."

They did nothing important.

That was the plan.

Walked to a nearby village that still served them without asking for registration.

Bought bread.

Bad bread.

Mau accused Frieren of choosing it for nostalgia.

She did not deny it.

They sat by water.

No magical analysis.

No evidence notebook.

No one else.

Mau checked himself twice.

"Do you remember this?"

Frieren answered:

"It is happening."

He looked at her.

Right.

Present tense.

They ate.

Frieren stole part of his bread.

Mau complained.

She leaned against him.

Later they bought a small piece of blue thread because Frieren liked the color and had no use for it.

Mau put it in his pocket.

At sunset Frieren said:

"Tomorrow, if you remember this wrong, we can check the thread."

Mau touched his pocket.

"And if I lose it?"

"I'll remember."

"And if I don't trust you?"

Frieren looked at him.

"Then we'll decide what to do that day."

Not forever.

Not certainty.

One day at a time.

Mau kissed her.

This memory did not cure anything.

That was why it mattered.
