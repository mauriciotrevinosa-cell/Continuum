# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 4 — Bocchi's Room

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 4  
**Continuity state:** Packing Day 2 | Music space not yet finished

Bocchi had three options.

Room near common space.

Room farther away.

Small room beside the workshop shell.

She hated all three.

Too loud.

Too isolated.

Too close to tools.

Kita asked:

"What do you actually want?"

Bocchi looked at the unfinished building.

Not bedroom.

Music.

That was the problem.

At the inn, music had adapted around everyone else.

Common room.

Corners.

Outside.

Now they could design for it.

That possibility was worse than compromise because it required preference.

"I want..."

Everyone waited.

Bocchi nearly died.

Then:

"A room where sound can happen without everyone hearing."

Mau, standing too far away to rescue her socially, did nothing.

Good.

Kita smiled.

"Then we build that."

Not today.

Not before moving.

But the plan changed.

One wall gained extra material.

Door placement shifted.

Storage for instruments.

A small sitting place.

Bocchi's future room became the first time the settlement changed because a quiet person admitted what she wanted.

She chose to move on the second wave.

Not first.

That way the space would be closer to usable.

No one called her difficult.

No one said everyone else could tolerate noise.

The town bent around a resident.

That was the point.
