# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 5 — Wakana's Workshop

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Continuity state:** Packing Day 2 | Workshop among first resident-specific spaces

Wakana's workshop existed before his room felt finished.

He objected.

Everyone ignored him.

Light mattered.

Table height mattered.

Storage mattered.

Ventilation mattered.

Clean and dirty work zones mattered.

Marin cared about mirrors.

Wakana cared about cutting space.

Both were right for different reasons.

At the inn, he had adapted.

Here, the room adapted first.

That embarrassed him.

Mau understood.

"Feels selfish?"

Wakana looked over.

"Yes."

"Apparently we're not allowed to use that word for having a chair that fits."

Wakana smiled.

"Who told you?"

"Everyone."

The workshop became one of the first spaces that made the new settlement look inhabited before anyone slept there.

Fabric.

Tools.

Thread.

Patterns.

A mannequin shape covered until proper storage arrived.

Wakana moved some equipment early.

Not himself.

That created a strange state:

part of his life lived at the future settlement before he did.

The move was already happening through objects.
