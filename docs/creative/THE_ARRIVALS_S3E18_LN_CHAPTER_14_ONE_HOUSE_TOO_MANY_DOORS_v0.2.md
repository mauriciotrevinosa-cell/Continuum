# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 14 — One House, Too Many Doors

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Continuity state:** Move planning Day 3 | Household rejects source-world enclave logic

The efficient plan had five buildings.

This was why everyone hated it.

Nano had not designed it.

Important.

She was only presenting the version that minimized covered connectors, duplicated storage and traffic overlap.

One sleeping block for this cluster.
One for that.
Workshop people here.
Magic people there.
Guests farther out.

Nobody said franchise.

They did not need to.

The shape did.

Mau stared at it.

"No."

Nano nodded.

"Expected."

"Why did you make it?"

"To know what we are rejecting."

Reasonable.

Annoying.

Nijika leaned over the board.

"If Bocchi is there and we're here, that's stupid."

Ryo:

"We could visit."

Nijika looked at her.

Ryo:

"I agree it's stupid."

Kita pointed between the blocks.

"We just got everybody back."

Nobody needed clarification.

Coco looked at the Witch Hat rooms.

Then at Mau/Frieren's crowded courtyard.

Then at Qifrey.

"I don't want to live in the magic building."

Agott:

"There is no magic building."

"Good."

Richeh:

"I want the atelier."

Tetia:

"Me too."

Coco nodded quickly.

"Yes. Atelier. Not separate house."

Different.

Momo looked at the Dandadan-side block.

"No."

Okarun:

"I didn't say anything."

"You were thinking maybe the route is practical."

"It is practical."

Aira:

"Traitor."

Seiko leaned back.

"Build the damn route practical. Don't build the family stupid."

Silence.

Mau looked at her.

Seiko shrugged.

"You're all making this harder than it is."

Frieren, beside her:

"Yes."

They had become friends alarmingly quickly.

Maybe because both had low tolerance for unnecessary explanation.

Senku tapped the board.

"Shared central services are also more efficient if your actual behavior is shared."

Nano:

"Correct."

"Then this isn't the efficient plan."

"It is the efficient plan for groups that behave separately."

Everyone looked around.

Kessoku tangled with the common room.

Witch Hat students at Mau's table.

Momo feeding half the building.

Milim wherever Rimuru was.

Suika already being kidnapped by Umaru and Coco for activities Senku had not approved.

Maomao's work intersecting with Kusuri, Senku, Momo, Jinshi, Qifrey and anyone reckless enough to get injured.

Anko appearing in every conversation whether invited or not.

No one behaved separately.

Wakana took the pencil.

"What if the sleeping wings connect to one central common core?"

Momo:

"Kitchen too."

Fern:

"Not directly. Smell."

Kaneki, from the back:

"Please not directly."

Good point.

Bocchi pointed at the music room.

"Buffer."

Wakana nodded.

Marin pointed at a future courtyard.

"Pretty."

Wakana:

"That is not a technical requirement."

"It is now."

Rimuru laughed.

The five-building plan began dying.

Not replaced by one giant box.

That would be worse.

Instead:
wings,
courtyards,
covered walks,
shared center,
specialized spaces attached where noise, smell, safety or magic required separation.

One organism.

Many doors.

Mau watched the plan spread.

"This is going to become another inn."

Fern:

"No."

Everyone looked at her.

Fern studied the increasingly ridiculous footprint.

Then corrected herself.

"Not yet."

Marin grinned.

Mau groaned.

The family had outgrown one inn.

Apparently the solution was to accidentally invent a larger one.
