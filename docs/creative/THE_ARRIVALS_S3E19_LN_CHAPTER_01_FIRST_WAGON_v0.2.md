# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 7 — First Wagon

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 1 | First staged cargo run | Old inn remains occupied

The first wagon contained almost no people.

Beds.

Blankets.

Pots.

Tools.

Food bins.

Medical supplies.

Clothes.

A chair everyone argued should stay.

The chair went.

Fern stood at the inn door with a list.

Rimuru stood by the wagon with another.

They disagreed on three objects.

Mau carried boxes until Maomao saw him.

Then he carried lighter boxes.

Progress.

Yuta and Maki moved furniture with the calm of people for whom changing residences did not automatically mean changing identity.

Mau noticed.

"You two are weirdly fine."

Maki looked at him.

"It's a building."

Mau looked at the inn.

Maki followed his eyes.

"Okay. Not just a building."

Yuta smiled.

"But taking the table doesn't take the room."

Important.

They did not take the old dining table.

That decision had already been made.

New settlement got a new table.

The old one stayed.

Origin object.

Mau liked that more than expected.

The first wagon left.

Halfway down the road, everyone realized the kettle they needed for first night was still at the inn.

Momo had predicted this.

Nobody had listened.

The move became real through forgotten kitchen equipment.
