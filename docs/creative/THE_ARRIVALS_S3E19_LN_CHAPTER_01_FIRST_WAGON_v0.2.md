# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 15 — First Wagon

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 1 | First staged cargo run | Old inn remains occupied

The first wagon carried almost no people.

That made it feel more important.

Beds.

Blankets.

Pots.

Tools.

Food bins.

Medical supplies.

Clothes.

One chair everyone argued should stay.

The chair went.

Fern stood at the inn door with a list.

Rimuru stood beside the wagon with another.

They disagreed immediately.

"Why are there six pots?"

"Because we cook."

"We do not need six tonight."

"What if one breaks?"

"Then we have five."

Fern looked at him.

Rimuru conceded the sixth pot.

Mau carried boxes until Maomao appeared.

She looked at the box.

Then at him.

Mau put it down.

"Light boxes?"

"Very."

She inspected.

"That one."

It contained cloth.

Mau looked humiliated.

Progress.

The move did not begin with possessions anyone would call precious.

It began with things people would need at 7 p.m. when hungry and tired.

That was why the argument over the old dining table surprised Mau.

Yuta had one end.

Maki the other.

Mau saw.

"Wait."

They stopped.

No one had formally decided the table was moving.

It had simply ended up on the list under FURNITURE.

Mau looked at it.

Evidence table.

Dinner table.

First plans.

Arguments.

S1.

S2.

Too much.

Maki looked at him.

"It's a table."

Mau looked at the burn marks.

"Yeah."

Maki followed his eyes.

"Okay. Not just a table."

Yuta set his side down.

"Does it need to be at the new place?"

Mau almost answered yes.

The new home should have the important things.

Then realized that logic would slowly hollow the inn into a shell.

He looked at Frieren.

She understood.

"Leave it."

Mau nodded.

New settlement would get a new table.

The old one stayed.

Not because furniture contained souls.

Because preserving a place required leaving something enough to remain itself.

The chair still went.

Nobody could explain why the chair had less emotional protection.

Momo claimed discrimination.

The wagon filled.

They checked the list.

Then checked again.

Forgot rope.

Found rope.

Lost one box.

Found it already under the wagon.

Milim offered to carry the wagon.

Everyone said no.

"Why?"

"Because the road needs testing under normal load."

Milim looked insulted by normality.

The first wagon left around midday.

People walked beside it.

No music.

No ceremonial goodbye.

Halfway down the road, Momo asked:

"Who packed the kettle?"

Silence.

Fern was not there.

Rimuru looked at Mau.

Mau looked at Wakana.

Wakana looked at Maomao.

Maomao:

"Not medical."

Therefore not her problem.

Momo smiled.

"I said."

They had left the kettle at the inn.

The first supply run to a new settlement required sending someone back for the kettle.

Mau laughed until his head hurt.

Frieren told him to stop.

He laughed more carefully.

The move became real through forgotten kitchen equipment.

By the time the wagon reached the site, the old inn already held objects that belonged there and objects accidentally left there.

The new site held things that belonged to people who were not sleeping there yet.

Nothing had one home anymore.

Neither did the family.

Not yet.
