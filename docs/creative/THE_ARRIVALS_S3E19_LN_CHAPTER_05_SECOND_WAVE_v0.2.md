# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 11 — Second Wave

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 2 | Second resident wave transfers from old inn

The second wave left after lunch.

Bocchi.

Kita.

Kaneki.

Two G5 temporary residents.

Wakana's bedframe.

Most of the kitchen equipment.

Too many boxes labeled MISCELLANEOUS.

Fern stayed behind again.

So did Rem.

Not everyone had to move on the same emotional schedule.

Bocchi stood in the old common room with her guitar.

"Do I say goodbye?"

Kita looked around.

"We're coming back."

Right.

Not death.

Not disappearance.

Move.

Bocchi still touched the table before leaving.

The table stayed.

The mug stayed too.

Mau had almost packed it.

Then stopped.

Some objects belonged to the inn.

Not because no one could use them elsewhere.

Because geography could carry memory on purpose.

The second wave reached the settlement before dark.

Bocchi saw the modified music room.

Not finished.

Better.

She stood inside.

Clapped once.

Listened.

Too much echo.

"Bad."

Wakana smiled.

"Fixable."

That word described most of the settlement.

Kaneki checked food storage before choosing his room.

Good.

Kita chose a space near Bocchi without making it identical.

Good.

The G5 residents chose one shared temporary room rather than take permanent spaces they had not decided they wanted.

Good.

By sunset the new site held enough people that dinner could no longer pretend to be a test night.

It was becoming population.
