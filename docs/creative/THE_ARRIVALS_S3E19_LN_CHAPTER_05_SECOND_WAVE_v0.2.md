# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 11 — Second Wave

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 2 | Second resident wave transfers from old inn

The second wave left after lunch.

Bocchi.

Kita.

Nijika.

Ryo.

Kaneki.

Wakana's bedframe.

Most of the kitchen equipment.

Too many boxes labeled MISCELLANEOUS.

Fern stayed behind again.

So did Rem.

Not everyone had to move on the same emotional schedule.

Bocchi stood in the old common room with her guitar case in both hands.

Everyone else had already taken one box outside.

She had not moved.

Kita came back in.

"You okay?"

Bocchi looked around.

Table.

Chair.

Door.

The corner where she had hidden from too many conversations.

The place where she had played quietly while other people slept.

"Do I say goodbye?"

Kita followed her eyes.

"We're coming back."

Right.

Not death.

Not disappearance.

Move.

Memory Arc had made leaving places feel more permanent than they were.

Bocchi set the guitar down long enough to touch the old table.

Not ceremonial.

Fingers against one worn edge.

Then she picked the case up again.

"Okay."

Outside, Mau was securing boxes badly.

Maki fixed them without comment.

Mau noticed Bocchi.

"Ready?"

Bocchi's entire body objected to being perceived.

"Probably."

Mau nodded.

No reassurance speech.

No:
you'll love it.

No:
this is good.

Just:
"Okay."

That helped.

The mug stayed at the inn.

Mau had almost packed it earlier.

Then stopped.

The table stayed.

Several chairs.

A shelf.

Some objects belonged to the place now.

Not because they could never move.

Because emptying the inn completely would turn preservation into memory theater.

They wanted a place people could still enter and recognize.

The second wave took the road.

Kaneki walked near the food cart.

Not symbolic.

Practical.

His decision to move depended partly on knowing the storage system he needed had actually arrived.

One of the G5 residents asked:

"Are we residents now?"

Nano, riding on the back of the wagon with the ledger:

"You were already temporary residents."

"Then what changed?"

"Your sleeping location."

The G5 resident frowned.

"That sounds less dramatic."

"Correct."

Good.

The new settlement appeared before sunset.

Bocchi heard it before seeing it.

Hammer.

Voices.

Someone shouting about a missing bucket.

Different acoustic map.

She hated it.

Then saw the music room.

Wall changed.

Door moved.

Additional storage framing.

Not finished.

But altered because she had asked.

Bocchi entered.

Clapped once.

Echo.

Too much.

She grimaced.

"Bad."

Wakana, behind her:

"Fixable."

He walked the room.

Listened.

Pointed.

"More material here."

Bocchi looked at him.

"More work."

"Yes."

"Sorry."

Wakana turned.

"No."

Kita smiled.

Bocchi did not understand.

Wakana continued:

"You told us what the room is for. Now we build for that."

Simple.

Bocchi set the guitar case down.

The room became slightly less threatening.

Kaneki checked food storage before his own room.

Maomao had expected that.

The arrangement worked.

Not perfectly.

Enough.

Kaneki chose his room afterward.

Kita picked a space near Bocchi.

Not beside.

Near.

Relationship without architectural possession.

Nijika chose the room nearest the music space without claiming it was permanent.

Ryo chose the room with the best afternoon shade and then insisted this was a financial decision somehow.

No one forced a symbolic bedroom choice onto uncertainty.

By sunset, the site had enough bodies that movement changed.

People crossing paths.

Calling names.

Asking who had scissors.

Someone borrowing bedding.

Dinner smell.

Not test night anymore.

Population.

Bocchi stood outside her room and listened.

Too much noise.

Then somewhere farther away someone tuned an instrument badly.

She smiled.

Maybe this place would learn her too.
