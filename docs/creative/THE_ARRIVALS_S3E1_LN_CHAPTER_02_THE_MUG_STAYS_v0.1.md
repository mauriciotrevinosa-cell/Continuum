## Chapter 2 — The Mug Stays

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT

**Source episode:** S3E1 — `Tomorrow Is Terrain`

<!-- scene-id: s3e1-ln02-camp -->

They built one shelter.

Temporary.

That word was repeated enough times to become defensive.

"Temporary storage," Rimuru said.

"Temporary roof," Wakana said.

"Temporary cooking area," Maomao said.

"Temporary place to sleep if weather traps us," Fern added.

Momo looked around.

"So a house."

"No," five people said.

Momo smiled.

The shelter stood above the lowest ground because even without an engineer everyone agreed water moved downhill.

Nobody agreed yet where downhill became a problem.

Qifrey marked the supports.

Wakana checked cloth strain.

Mikasa checked sightlines.

Himmel carried timber.

Stark carried more timber and complained that Himmel was making it look easy.

Yuta helped until Maki told him he was holding three things badly instead of one thing correctly.

Mau carried what people handed him.

This was surprisingly efficient.

<!-- scene-id: s3e1-ln02-back -->

By late afternoon, the site had:
- one shelter;
- one covered stack of materials;
- two questionable paths;
- nineteen stakes;
- one stake everyone hated;
- no houses.

They went home.

That was the part Momo kept forgetting.

The wagon creaked back toward the old settlement.

Coco slept against Qifrey.

Umaru slept against a sack and denied it before she was awake.

Ori watched the new site disappear between trees.

"Will we still come back to the inn after we move?"

Mau looked at her.

The question was not really about travel.

"Yes."

"How often?"

"I don't know."

Ori frowned.

Frieren answered from Mau's other side.

"As often as we want."

Ori looked at her.

Frieren continued.

"It's not going anywhere."

Mau thought about ruined buildings.

Weather.

People.

Things going somewhere without permission.

"We'll keep it," he said.

Rimuru, ahead of them, heard.

He turned.

"Keep what?"

"The inn."

Rimuru looked back down the road.

No argument.

"Yeah."

Not a civic decision.

Not yet.

Just a promise made by people riding home from bare ground.

<!-- scene-id: s3e1-ln02-mug -->

That night the map returned to the table.

The mug returned too.

Nobody planned that.

Mau found it covering the eastern edge again.

He left it there.

The future did not need the whole table yet.
