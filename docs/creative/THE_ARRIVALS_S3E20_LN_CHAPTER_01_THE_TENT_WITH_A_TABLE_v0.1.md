# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 1 — The Tent With a Table

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E20 — `People Who Heard About Us`

The first Arrival House was not a house.

It was canvas.

One table.

Three chairs that did not match.

A crate turned sideways because they needed a fourth seat.

The tent stood near the future site's temporary storage, far enough from construction noise that frightened people could hear questions clearly and close enough to the medical shelter that Maomao could get there quickly.

Nobody named it.

People did anyway.

"Take them to the Arrival tent."

Then:

"Arrival table."

Then:

"Arrival House."

Fern objected the first time.

"It isn't a house."

Momo looked at the tent.

"It has a roof."

"That is not the definition."

"Debatable."

Nano wrote `ARRIVAL HOUSE — TEMPORARY` on the intake sheet.

Fern stared at her.

Nano said:

"Terminological drift has already occurred."

Fern hated accurate betrayal.

The first morning, nobody arrived.

That was good.

It let them realize they had forgotten water.

The second morning, one person arrived before the water did.

That was less good.

He had heard there was a place where people from other worlds were not automatically detained.

He did not want to stay.

That became the first important thing the table learned.

Arrival did not mean residency.

Safety did not mean belonging.

Help did not require adoption.

They gave him food, a route map, and a place to sleep one night.

He left before sunrise.

The empty bed afterward felt like success.
