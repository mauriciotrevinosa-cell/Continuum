## Chapter 2 — Name, World, Need

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S3E20 — `People Who Heard About Us`

The intake form began with too many questions.

Nano made it.

That was not the problem.

The problem was everybody else adding one.

Name.

World.

Last remembered place.

Known dangerous people.

Known loved ones.

Abilities.

Medical conditions.

Food restrictions.

Languages.

Threats.

Enemies.

Preferred sleeping arrangement.

Whether they wanted to be searched for if they left.

Momo added:

`DO YOU LIKE SPICY FOOD?`

Nano removed it.

Momo added it again on the back.

The first terrified arrival looked at the page and stopped breathing correctly.

Fern took it away.

New form.

Three lines.

`NAME — if you want us to use one.`

`WHAT DO YOU NEED TODAY?`

`IS ANYONE FOLLOWING YOU?`

Everything else could wait.

That changed the tone immediately.

A woman asked for shoes.

Not citizenship.

Shoes.

Her feet were bleeding.

Wakana found a temporary pair.

A man asked whether anyone had seen his brother.

The missing-person board gained a card.

A child asked whether monsters could enter the tent.

Mikasa showed them where the alarm line was.

The answers became specific.

Need first.

History later.

The city learned one person at a time.
