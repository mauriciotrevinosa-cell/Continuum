# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 17 — First Full Night

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 4 — night | Most ongoing residents now sleep at settlement | Inn remains active satellite

The first full night did not mean every person.

It meant enough.

Enough that the settlement sounded inhabited after midnight.

Doors.

Footsteps.

Someone laughing too loudly.

Water.

A baby from one guest family waking.

Milim being told to stop training.

Again.

Bocchi playing softly in her unfinished music room.

Wakana still working too late.

Fern telling Wakana to stop.

Mau lay beside Frieren.

Could not sleep.

Not fear.

Sound.

Too many lives.

The old inn had sound too.

Different density.

He got up.

Walked to the common room.

Ori was there.

So was Kaneki.

Neither asked why.

Mau sat.

After a while:

"Does this feel like home?"

Ori answered first.

"Some of it."

Kaneki:

"Not yet."

Mau nodded.

"Same."

No pressure to name the feeling early.

Outside, the first settlement night watch changed shifts.

Mikasa signed the handoff.

Aira complained the north path needed another marker.

Someone wrote it down.

The town was already producing maintenance debt.

That was more convincing than beauty.
