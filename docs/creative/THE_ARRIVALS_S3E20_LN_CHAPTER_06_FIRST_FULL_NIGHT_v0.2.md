# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 27 — First Full Night

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 27  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 4 — night | Most ongoing residents now sleep at settlement | Inn remains active satellite

The first full night did not mean everyone.

It meant enough.

Enough that the settlement no longer quieted when work stopped.

Doors.

Footsteps.

Someone laughing too loudly.

Water moving through containers.

A child crying in one temporary room.

Milim being told to stop training.

Again.

Bocchi playing softly in the unfinished music room.

Nijika listening from the doorway.

Ryo tuning badly on purpose until Bocchi threw a cloth at her.

Kita laughing too loudly and then apologizing to nobody in particular.

Momo refusing to let Okarun call dried food "dinner" while Aira stole pieces from the cutting board.

Marin announcing that after the next ten minutes nobody was allowed to improve the building until morning.

Wakana, already working too late, pretending not to hear her.

Anko writing at one end of the common room while Nazuna slept across two chairs she had absolutely not been assigned.

Seiko drinking something hot beside Frieren and criticizing the night route markers without once using the word "grandchildren."

Yuta and Maki on the half-built step outside, not talking, watching the dark as if it owed them something.

Himmel at the window that faced the road.

Karane announcing through a wall that Hakari's snoring was structural, and Hakari announcing back that Karane's complaining was load-bearing.

Milim being told to stop training.

Again.

Fern finding Wakana.

"Wakana."

He froze.

"Bed."

"I was just—"

"Bed."

He went.

That was when Mau decided Fern moving had changed the settlement more than walls.

He lay beside Frieren.

Could not sleep.

Not fear exactly.

Sound.

Too many lives.

At the inn, sounds had history.

Stark's steps.

Bocchi's guitar.

The door.

The roof.

Momo opening something she should not.

Here, every sound was unclassified.

Mau's brain disliked unclassified things now.

Frieren noticed him listening.

"Wrong memory?"

"No."

"Head?"

"No."

"Then?"

"Everyone."

Frieren listened.

A baby.

Laughter.

Bucket.

Distant string.

"Yes."

Mau smiled.

After another ten minutes, he got up.

Frieren did not ask where.

Good.

The common room had thinned but not emptied.

Ori, Kaneki and Suika remained near the center.

Anko was still writing.

Nazuna was still asleep.

Nobody understood how.

Seiko had left one cup beside the door for whoever took next watch.

Momo had covered the last plate of food.

The room looked less like a camp and more like people had expected one another to come back.

Ori drinking something warm.

Kaneki seated where the remaining food smell bothered him least.

Suika making a small map of which night paths felt safe at her height instead of everyone else's.

Neither asked Mau why he was awake.

He sat.

Silence.

Not uncomfortable.

Then:

"Does this feel like home?"

Ori answered first.

"Some of it."

Kaneki:

"Not yet."

Mau nodded.

"Same."

He expected disappointment.

None.

No deadline.

Ori looked around.

"I like the courtyard."

Kaneki:

"I like knowing where the kitchen smell goes."

Mau:

"Romantic."

Kaneki stared.

Mau held up both hands.

Outside, night watch changed.

Mikasa signed handoff.

Aira complained the north marker was still too low.

Someone wrote it down.

Maintenance debt.

Proof of use.

Mau looked through the doorway.

Lights.

Paths.

Half-finished roofs.

The settlement looked worse at night because unfinished edges disappeared and people filled the gaps.

More alive.

Ori asked:

"Do you miss the inn?"

"Yes."

Kaneki:

"That's allowed."

Mau looked at him.

"I know."

Then laughed.

"No. I understand that sentence."

Important distinction.

They sat until Mau felt tired enough to try again.

On the walk back, he passed Bocchi's room.

Music stopped.

Door opened.

Bocchi looked out.

"Too loud?"

"No."

She froze.

Mau added:

"It's good."

Bocchi looked like he had handed her a live explosive.

Then:

"Okay."

Door closed.

Music returned slightly louder.

In bed, Frieren was awake.

"You found everyone?"

Mau lay down.

"Enough."

She moved closer.

Outside, the settlement continued making mistakes after midnight.

That was more convincing than beauty.

The first full night did not make the place home.

It gave home enough repeated sounds to begin.
