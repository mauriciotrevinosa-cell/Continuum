# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 21 — The Common Room Refuses to Empty

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 21  
**Continuity state:** First full week | New common room begins repeating old-home family rhythms

The new common room had a problem.

Nobody left.

This was not a construction defect.

Fern considered it one anyway.

"There are beds."

No response.

Mau sat on the new couch.

Technically it was not new.

Wakana had rebuilt the frame from salvaged wood and Marin had insisted the cushions not look like emergency supplies.

Therefore:
new enough.

Frieren sat beside Mau for six minutes.

Then slowly lowered herself until her head rested in his lap.

Mau looked down.

"You know we have a room."

"Yes."

"With a bed."

"Yes."

"Okay."

No further questions.

Rimuru arrived in slime form.

Saw Frieren.

Saw available stomach.

Mau:

"Don't."

Rimuru jumped.

Frieren caught him with one hand without opening her eyes.

Placed him on her stomach.

Rimuru settled.

Mau stared.

"You encourage him."

"No."

Rimuru:

"She does."

Umaru had taken the floor beside the couch.

She was explaining something to Suika with increasing intensity.

Nobody understood the rules.

Suika did.

That was enough.

Twenty minutes later Umaru was asleep against Mau's leg.

Suika covered her with a small blanket.

Then got pulled into Coco's argument about whether a spell drawing could be improved by making it cuter.

Agott said no.

Tetia said yes.

Richeh was trying both.

Bocchi entered.

Stopped.

Too many people.

Mau saw the calculation happen.

Old Bocchi might have vanished.

Current Bocchi stood there long enough for Kita to notice.

Kita did not call her across the room.

She only lifted one corner of a blanket.

Space available.

Bocchi crossed.

Sat.

Nijika arrived later.

Ryo after her.

Kessoku formed without requiring anyone else to leave.

At the table, Anko wrote.

Fern sorted a list beside her.

Mau could hear pieces of their conversation.

Rumors.
Ink.
Someone in town lying badly.
Whether the new storage room needed another lock.

Nothing required him.

Then Anko looked up.

"Mau."

"Yeah?"

"Do you remember if the merchant with the green cart had a scar?"

Mau thought.

"I remember one."

"Confident?"

He checked himself.

"No."

Anko nodded.

Wrote:

Mau remembers one / uncertain.

No drama.

Memory protocol absorbed into normal life.

Momo came in carrying food.

Okarun behind her with more.

Aira behind him carrying nothing.

Momo:

"Why are you here?"

"Emotional support."

"You ate two pieces on the way."

"Physical support."

Seiko entered last.

Saw the room.

Saw Aira.

Smacked the back of her head lightly.

Aira complained.

Vamola copied the complaint without being hit.

Turbo Granny laughed from somewhere she was not supposed to be.

Nazuna had been asleep in a chair by the window for at least an hour.

No one remembered seeing her enter.

Seiko looked at Frieren.

"You letting slime boy sleep there?"

Frieren:

"He's warm."

Rimuru sounded deeply pleased.

Mau:

"I have learned something upsetting."

Frieren:

"Be quiet."

Seiko sat across from them.

Two grandmothers in completely incompatible forms of life looked at each other.

Then at Mau.

Seiko:

"He eating enough?"

Frieren:

"Usually."

Mau:

"I am here."

Neither acknowledged him.

Mau looked toward Fern.

"Help."

Fern:

"No."

Family.

Marin arrived at sunset.

Clapped once.

"Nobody is allowed to work for the rest of the night."

Wakana, entering behind her with tools:

"I only need—"

"No."

He looked at Fern.

Fern:

"She's right."

Wakana surrendered.

The room got louder.

Mau leaned back.

Frieren's hair across his leg.
Rimuru on Frieren.
Umaru asleep against him.
Bocchi close enough to be part of the same conversation without speaking.
Anko and Fern arguing over wording.
Momo distributing food.
Coco pulling Suika into something.
Maki and Yuta arriving together.
Himmel carrying a chair because there were no chairs left.
Stark being told by Fern to stop hovering.
Hakari, Karane, Shizuka, Nano and Kusuri entering as a group and immediately breaking whatever volume level remained.

Mau looked around.

The first cluster was unfinished.

The permanent house barely existed on paper.

The drainage still needed work.

Half the doors did not close correctly.

And somehow the common room already knew what it was for.

Nothing happened that night.

That was the achievement.
