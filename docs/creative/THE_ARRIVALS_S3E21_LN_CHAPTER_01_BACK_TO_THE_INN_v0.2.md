# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 31 — Back to the Inn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 31  
**Continuity state:** Move Day 5 | First deliberate return trip after majority move

Mau returned to the inn the next morning.

No emergency.

That mattered enough that he noticed before leaving.

Frieren came.

Bocchi too.

Different reasons.

Mau wanted to see the place.

Frieren wanted two books she had definitely not forgotten and absolutely did not need immediately.

Bocchi claimed she had left a cable.

Kita knew she had packed three.

No one challenged the excuse.

The road felt different.

Same trees.

Same rut.

Same small stream.

But the destination had changed category.

Yesterday:
home.

Today:
the inn.

Mau hated that thought.

Then corrected it.

Also home.

Maybe.

Language lagged.

The building appeared through trees.

Mau expected grief.

Instead:

relief.

Door still there.

Roof still ugly.

One shutter still crooked.

The door stuck.

Mau pushed harder.

Stark had not fixed it that morning because Stark had slept elsewhere.

Mau laughed.

The absence produced evidence.

Inside, Rem sat at the old table with a guest.

Not empty.

Not museum.

Good.

Rem looked up.

"Forgot something?"

Mau thought.

"No."

That was also good.

Bocchi went upstairs.

Frieren went directly to books.

Mau wandered.

Common room.

Evidence-table box now moved off the dining surface.

Marks.

Old mug.

Richeh's folded guest blanket in one corner.

Mai's spare cup where she had left it.

One of Nazuna's things on the windowsill despite Fern insisting the roof was not a storage system.

Nothing preserved perfectly.

Living place.

He went upstairs.

The room he had shared with Frieren looked mostly empty.

Not stripped.

One blanket.

Lamp.

A few objects deliberately left.

Mau sat on the bed.

Frieren appeared in the doorway with four books.

"You said two."

"I found more."

"Of course."

Then quiet.

Mau looked around.

"This is weird."

"Yes."

He touched the mattress.

"Do you remember it this way too?"

Frieren understood immediately.

"Yes."

Mau smiled.

Question no longer emergency every time.

Habit.

They packed nothing else.

That became the purpose of the trip.

Not every return had to extract another box until history became bare walls.

Bocchi came downstairs without the cable.

Kita had been right.

She sat at the table.

Rem joined.

One guest, who had never lived here during S1 or S2, watched them.

"Why are all of you sentimental about this place?"

Silence.

Too many possible answers.

Mau looked at Frieren.

Frieren looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi answered from the stairs.

"This is where we became us."

No one improved it.

The guest nodded.

Maybe understood.

Maybe not.

Lunch happened.

At the old table.

Bread.

Tea.

Argument over whether the settlement kitchen had better storage.

Mau noticed that conversation assumed going back.

Not leaving.

Going back.

The road reversed meaning without permission.

Before they left, Mau touched the doorframe.

No goodbye.

Rem remained.

Guests remained.

Tonight someone else would sleep here.

Mau walked back toward the settlement carrying nothing.

That was the first time he returned to the inn without treating it as a place being emptied.

It had survived the move.

So had they.
