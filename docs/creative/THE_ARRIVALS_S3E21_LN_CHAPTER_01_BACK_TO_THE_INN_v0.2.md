# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 18 — Back to the Inn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 18
**Continuity state:** Move Day 5 | First deliberate return trip after majority move

Mau returned to the inn the next morning.

Frieren came.

Bocchi too.

No emergency.

That mattered.

The road felt different when it was not commute home.

Grass near one wheel rut.

Dust.

Trees.

The inn appeared.

Still same building.

Mau expected grief.

Instead:

relief.

The door stuck.

Stark had not fixed it today because Stark slept elsewhere.

Mau laughed.

Inside, Rem sat at the table with a guest.

Alive.

Occupied.

Not museum.

Good.

Bocchi went upstairs.

Frieren checked books.

Mau went to the room he had shared with Frieren.

Mostly empty.

Not stripped.

A blanket remained.

One lamp.

Objects deliberately left.

He sat on the bed.

Frieren stood in the doorway.

"This is weird."

"Yes."

"Do you remember it that way too?"

Frieren looked around.

"Yes."

Mau smiled.

They packed nothing.

That was the purpose of the trip.

Not every return needed to extract more.

They ate lunch at the old table.

Rem joined.

A guest who had never lived here asked:

"Why is everyone sentimental about this place?"

Bocchi answered from the stairs.

"This is where we became us."

No one improved it.
