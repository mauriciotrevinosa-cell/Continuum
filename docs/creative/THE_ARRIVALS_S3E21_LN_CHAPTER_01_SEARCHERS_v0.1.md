**Source episode:** S3E21 — `Searchers`

The name came from someone outside the group.

A merchant arrived with a rumor.

"Are the Searchers here?"

Fern looked around.

"The what?"

"The people who go looking."

That was specific enough to be dangerous.

Mikasa was away.

Okarun was not.

Anko was asleep after returning late.

Maomao was in the medical shelter.

Nobody had ever voted on a name.

The merchant waited.

Okarun raised one hand.

"I guess?"

By dinner, everyone knew.

Searchers.

Momo hated it because it sounded too cool for people who mostly walked.

Mikasa hated it because names created expectations.

Anko liked it because expectations could be written down.

So they did.

Searchers did not mean fighters.

It meant people sent to verify, locate, escort, negotiate, retrieve, map, or observe.

Different mission.

Different team.

The first written rule was:

`DO NOT SEND THE SAME PEOPLE BECAUSE THEY WERE GOOD LAST TIME.`

The second was:

`BRING SOMEONE WHO CAN ACTUALLY TALK TO LOCALS.`

The third, added by Maomao:

`BRING WATER.`

Nobody argued.
