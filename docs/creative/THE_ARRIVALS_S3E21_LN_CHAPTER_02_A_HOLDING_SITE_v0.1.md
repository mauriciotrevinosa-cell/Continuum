The rumor was simple.

Three Otherworlders held in a converted toll station.

The truth was not.

One had volunteered to remain while officials checked identity.

One had tried to leave and been stopped.

One did not understand the local language well enough to explain anything.

That changed the mission.

Mikasa wanted a small team.

Anko wanted documentation.

Himmel wanted someone locals recognized as non-hostile.

Diablo volunteered.

Everyone said no.

Diablo looked offended.

"You are visibly a demon," Rimuru said.

"I can be diplomatic."

"You are visibly a diplomatic demon."

Milim volunteered.

The room rejected that faster.

The team became Anko, Mikasa, Okarun, a native guide, and Himmel.

Not maximum force.

Maximum chance of bringing people back without creating a battle.

Mau stayed.

He noticed less this time.

Progress.
