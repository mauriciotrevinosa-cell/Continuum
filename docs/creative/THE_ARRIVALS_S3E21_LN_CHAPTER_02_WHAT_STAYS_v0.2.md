# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 19 — What Stays

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 19
**Continuity state:** Move Day 5 | Old inn preservation decisions

They made the list at the old table.

What stays.

Not because objects could never move.

Because preserving a place required intentional incompleteness.

The table stayed.

The mug stayed.

Several chairs.

One shelf of books.

The old route board.

Marks on the wall nobody could explain anymore.

A repaired section of roof.

The room where Bocchi first slept after one of the worst days.

The doorway everyone had argued in.

No plaques.

No glass cases.

Not yet.

The inn remained usable.

Guest rooms.

Emergency overflow.

A place for someone who needed distance from settlement noise.

A stop for travelers.

Historical and alive.

Rimuru arrived late.

Read the list.

"Protected site?"

Mau made a face.

"Sounds official."

Fern:

"It needs maintenance responsibility."

Correct.

A rotating team.

Repair budget.

Stored water.

Fire checks.

No demolition for materials without group approval.

There.

The first protected historical/home site existed through chores.

Later residents could hear:

where the first family started.

The building would not become sacred because nobody touched it.

It would remain sacred because people kept fixing it.
