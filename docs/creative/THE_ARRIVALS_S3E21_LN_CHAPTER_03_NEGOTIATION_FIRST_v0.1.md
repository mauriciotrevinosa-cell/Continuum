# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 6 — Negotiation First

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Source episode:** S3E21 — `Searchers`

The toll station had six guards.

Mikasa could have solved that problem quickly.

That was why she did not speak first.

Anko did.

She asked what law authorized detention.

The guard captain used a word that meant registration, not detention.

Anko pointed at the locked rear door.

The captain disliked that.

Himmel asked whether the three had been accused of a crime.

No.

Okarun translated one prisoner's explanation badly.

The native guide corrected him.

Nobody died.

This counted as a good mission.

The first traveler chose to finish the registration process.

The Searchers respected that.

The second wanted out.

The guard captain eventually opened the door after Anko made it clear how many witnesses now knew exactly who was holding whom.

The third simply wanted someone to explain what had been happening.

They did.

Two left with the Searchers.

One stayed.

Mikasa looked back twice.

Not because she thought staying was wrong.

Because the entire point of rescue was choice.

At the future site, the two travelers were given bunks.

Not citizenship.

Not loyalty.

A place to sleep.

Negotiation first had worked.

That did not mean it always would.
