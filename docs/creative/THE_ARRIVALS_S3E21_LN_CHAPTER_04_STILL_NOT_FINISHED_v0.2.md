# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 25 — Still Not Finished

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 25  
**Continuity state:** End of first Move week | Habitation achieved; final family-house remains unfinished

Rimuru made the mistake again.

"So are we done moving?"

Fern looked at him.

Rimuru corrected himself.

"Done moving enough?"

"Better."

They stood over the plan.

Not the old plan.

That one was already obsolete.

This version had:
the inhabited first cluster;
the common room;
the temporary kitchen;
basic medical storage;
the first working sanitation line;
the route to the old inn;
the revised east-facing residential wing;
Bocchi's sound buffer;
Wakana's temporary workshop;
empty shapes where later buildings might go.

And string.

Too much string.

The permanent atelier was not built.

The permanent workshop was not built.

Arrival House was not built.

The final clinic was not built.

Several family rooms were provisional.

The central family-house footprint had been redrawn enough times that Senku had started dating the corners.

Mau stared.

"We moved into a draft."

Nano:

"Accurate."

Marin:

"Depressing wording."

"Also accurate."

Outside, someone yelled about water.

Maomao yelled back.

Normal.

Mau looked at Rimuru.

"I thought we were building the new place."

"We are."

"This looks like we built the minimum required to discover all the ways the plan was wrong."

Senku, passing:

"That's called testing."

"Thank you. I hate it."

Senku kept walking.

Frieren stood near the east window.

Actual window now.

Not final frame.
Not final wall.
But glass enough.

Morning light hit her book.

Mau looked at her.

"At least one thing worked."

Frieren:

"Yes."

Coco appeared in the doorway without knocking.

"Can Mau come outside?"

Mau:

"I live here."

Coco:

"That's why I came here."

Umaru shouted from the corridor:

"After Coco!"

Bocchi's guitar started somewhere nearby.

Not loud.

Present.

Fern sighed.

"Doors."

Mau looked at Rimuru.

"This is going to keep growing."

"Yes."

"Into what?"

Rimuru looked at the plan.

Then at the people moving through it.

"Ask me after they stop changing it."

Everyone knew that would never happen.

The old inn had become home by accident.

This place was becoming home through argument.

Maybe the eventual answer would be another inn.

Bigger.
Stranger.
Built around courtyards and workshops and rooms added because someone needed somewhere to be.

But that was not S3's problem to finish.

Right now they had:
water that mostly worked;
drainage that failed visibly instead of catastrophically;
enough beds;
food;
medicine;
one common room that refused to empty;
a route back to the first home;
and people waking up here because they had chosen to.

Mau crossed to Frieren.

Looked through her east-facing window.

The sun was already high.

"Home?"

Frieren considered.

"Getting there."

Mau smiled.

Volume Fourteen did not end with a finished settlement.

It ended with a family finally giving the unfinished place enough life to tell them what to build next.
