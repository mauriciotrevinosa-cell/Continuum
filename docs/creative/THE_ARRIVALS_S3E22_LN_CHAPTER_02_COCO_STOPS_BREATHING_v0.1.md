# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 8 — Coco Stops Breathing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Source episode:** S3E22 — `Richeh`

Later, Coco admitted she had stopped breathing.

Richeh said:

"I noticed."

"I was surprised."

"You screamed."

"Quietly."

Agott looked over.

"No."

Coco ignored her.

The reunion became difficult only after the relief.

Richeh asked where they lived.

Coco pointed toward the old inn.

Then toward the future site.

"Both."

Richeh asked how long.

Coco talked for twenty minutes.

Mau.

Frieren.

The city that was not a city.

Sukuna.

She stopped before details that were not hers to share.

The Memory crisis.

The atelier planning.

Agott arriving.

The board.

Richeh listened.

Then asked:

"You built all that without me?"

Coco's face fell.

Wrong interpretation.

Richeh smiled.

"Good."

Coco stared.

"You were supposed to wait?"

"No!"

"Then why are you making that face?"

Coco hugged her again.

Because reunion did not restore the time between.

It connected two lives that had continued separately.

That was harder.

And better.
