# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 2 — Marin Cancels Construction

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary Week 1 | Rest is treated as household maintenance, not lost progress

Marin cancelled construction.

She did not have this authority.

Nobody stopped her.

At breakfast she stood on a chair.

Fern looked up.

"Get down."

Marin got down.

Then continued.

"Nobody builds anything today."

Senku did not look away from his notes.

"No."

"Yes."

"No."

Wakana quietly stopped eating.

He knew where this was going.

Marin pointed at him.

"Exhibit one."

"I am eating."

"You fell asleep holding a measuring cord yesterday."

"I was resting my eyes."

"While standing."

Wakana looked at his food.

Betrayed by evidence.

Marin pointed at Maomao.

"Exhibit two."

Maomao:

"No."

"You were in medical storage after midnight."

"That is not construction."

"It counts."

"Kusuri was there too."

Kusuri:

"Don't involve me."

Too late.

Momo joined immediately.

"Day off."

Fern:

"Half day."

Marin:

"Full."

Fern:

"Half."

Momo:

"Full."

Frieren:

"Full."

Everyone looked at her.

Frieren continued eating.

Motion carried.

The old lake became the destination because nobody had to invent it.

First home.

Old path.

Water.

Shade.

A place connected to too many earlier versions of them to feel like an excursion.

Mau walked slower than he used to.

Nobody commented.

Bocchi carried her guitar.

Kita carried too much food because Momo had given her too much food.

Nijika corrected the balance.

Ryo contributed nothing.

Umaru complained until Rimuru became a slime and let her carry him for approximately four minutes.

Then Milim wanted a turn.

Rimuru returned to human form.

"Absolutely not."

At the lake, the family broke apart naturally.

Not into franchises.

Into whoever happened to want the same thing.

Momo, Aira, Vamola and Marin near the water.

Bocchi, Kita, Nijika and Ryo with the guitar.

Coco, Richeh, Tetia and Suika looking for things that could become craft materials until Qifrey told them not everything needed to become a project.

Maomao and Kusuri immediately found a plant.

Marin yelled at them from fifty meters away.

"NO WORK."

Maomao held up the plant.

"It was already here."

Apparently this counted as a defense.

Mau and Frieren ended up near the old spot where the lake had already held too many conversations.

They sat.

No agenda.

For seven minutes.

Mau failed first.

"The permanent room."

Frieren looked at him.

Marin, somehow hearing from impossible distance:

"NO."

Mau laughed.

Frieren almost did.

They waited another ten minutes.

Then Frieren said:

"The window stays east."

Mau looked at her.

"That's planning."

"Observation."

"Right."

They looked at the water.

Mau imagined the unfinished family-house.

Everyone's markers crowding them.
Bocchi's sound buffer.
Ori across the courtyard.
Coco too close to knock.
Umaru definitely not knocking.
Fern positioned for maximum judgment.

"I thought we'd have more space."

Frieren:

"We will."

"Not from people."

"No."

Mau smiled.

Across the lake, Coco shouted because Suika had found something.

Mau started to rise.

Frieren caught his sleeve.

"They have Qifrey."

Right.

He sat back down.

That was part of it too.

Being central did not mean answering every sound.

Later Marin forced everyone to eat.

Anko and Nazuna arrived late because Nazuna had not been awake when everyone left.

Anko looked at the entire group.

"You started a family outing before noon?"

Marin:

"Yes."

"Monsters."

Nazuna lay down in the shade.

Within minutes she was asleep again.

Seiko threw a folded cloth over her face.

By sunset, nobody had improved drainage.

Nobody had completed a wall.

Nobody had written policy.

Wakana had laughed enough that Marin stopped checking whether he was resting.

Mau had not looked at the construction board once.

On the road back, Senku said:

"We lost a day."

Marin looked horrified.

"No."

Senku waited.

Marin pointed at the group ahead.

"We used one."

Senku looked.

Suika walking with Coco and Umaru.
Rimuru arguing with Milim.
Momo yelling at Aira.
Frieren beside Mau.
People tired for reasons unrelated to survival.

"Hm."

Marin:

"That means I won."

"I didn't say that."

"You thought it."

Senku regretted having a face.

Construction resumed the next morning.

Nobody had fallen behind.

The house had gained one more reason to exist.
