# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 3 — Breakfast Comes in Waves

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary Week 1 | The household stops pretending one table can schedule everyone

Breakfast used to be a meal.

Now it was infrastructure.

Momo resisted this conclusion.

"People should eat together."

Fern:

"They do."

"At the same time."

"No."

Momo looked personally offended by clocks.

The first wave belonged to people awake too early.

Mikasa.

Yuta.

Maki.

Seiko.

Sometimes Senku, depending on whether he had slept.

Suika if Senku had.

Anko if she had not.

Nazuna never.

The second wave was ordinary people.

A dangerous phrase.

Mau, when his sleep cooperated.

Frieren when Mau physically removed the blanket.

Bocchi when Kita made enough noise nearby that waking became less frightening than continuing to listen.

Wakana.

Marin.

Coco.

Qifrey.

Fern somehow appeared in every wave and therefore did not count.

The third wave was chaos.

Umaru.

Milim.

Ryo.

Hakari.

Kusuri.

Anyone who had gone to sleep at an irresponsible hour.

Nazuna remained not included because breakfast happened during her conceptual night.

Momo began cooking for all of them anyway.

Not alone.

Fern cut.

Mau carried.

Okarun washed.

Aira stole.

Vamola copied useful actions more often than Aira's.

Progress.

One morning Jinshi entered during first wave and stayed through second because Maomao had not appeared.

"Does she always do this?"

Momo:

"Do what?"

"Forget meals."

Everyone at the table:

"Yes."

Jinshi closed his eyes.

Momo slid a plate toward him.

"You'll fit in."

He looked uncertain whether that was a threat.

Another morning Nijika arrived during first wave because Bocchi had fallen asleep late after playing.

She helped without being asked.

Ryo arrived forty minutes later and sat exactly where breakfast had already been cleared.

"Food?"

Nijika stared.

Momo produced food anyway.

Ryo:

"I love this place."

Karane:

"Don't say it like that! You're freeloading!"

Hakari:

"Karane, you ate twice yesterday."

"THAT'S DIFFERENT."

Shizuka hid a smile behind her book.

Nano recorded nothing.

Character growth.

Rem appeared irregularly.

Sometimes old inn.
Sometimes new cluster.
Sometimes route work.

Momo stopped asking whether she was staying.

Instead:

"Eggs?"

Rem:

"Yes."

This was more effective.

Mau learned the household by who he missed.

If Anko was not there, she was probably writing or following a rumor.

If Marin was not there, Wakana was also suspiciously absent.

If Momo was not there, breakfast collapsed.

If Frieren was not there, she was probably still in bed.

If Fern was not there—

Mau stopped.

Looked around.

"Where's Fern?"

Everyone stopped.

Then Fern entered carrying bread.

Mau exhaled.

Fern noticed.

Said nothing.

The bad weeks did not disappear.

They got smaller, and became part of ordinary reactions.

At the end of the second week Momo finally surrendered.

She put up a board.

BREAKFAST:
WHEN YOU WAKE UP.

BE NICE TO WHOEVER COOKED.

CLEAN YOUR OWN CUP.

Aira added:

UNLESS YOU ARE VERY IMPORTANT.

Momo crossed it out.

Aira added it again.

Seiko smacked her lightly.

The board survived.

So did breakfast.

The family had become too large for one perfect table.

It had not become too large to feed one another.
