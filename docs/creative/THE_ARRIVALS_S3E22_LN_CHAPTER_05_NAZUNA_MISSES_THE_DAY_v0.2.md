# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 5 — Nazuna Misses the Day

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary Week 2 | Nazuna/Anko integrate through ordinary rhythm rather than plot utility

Nazuna missed an important day.

Not historically important.

Worse.

They chose the common-room curtains.

Nazuna slept through all of it.

Marin brought samples.

Umaru had opinions.

Coco had more opinions.

Frieren claimed not to care and then rejected one because it made the east light ugly.

Mau accused her of caring.

Frieren denied it.

Karane yelled.

Hakari suggested something nobody allowed to reach a vote.

Nano developed a scoring system.

Shizuka quietly chose the option everyone eventually liked.

Nazuna slept in a chair by the window.

Through all of it.

At one point Marin laid a fabric sample across her.

Nazuna did not move.

"Useful," Marin said.

Anko:

"She's going to kill you."

"Later."

"Yes."

They chose the curtains.

Installed one.

Argued about length.

Ate lunch.

Moved a shelf.

Mau had a brief headache.

Ori noticed.

They sat outside for ten minutes.

It passed.

Nobody turned it into crisis.

Nazuna slept.

Anko wrote two pages.

Then stopped.

Looked at Nazuna.

Mau happened to be nearby.

"How long does she do this?"

Anko:

"Yes."

"That's not a time."

"It is for her."

Mau sat on the floor beside Anko.

They watched Nazuna breathe.

Anko's relationship with her held too much history for Mau to summarize.

He knew enough.

Conflict.
Obsession.
Pain.
Affection shaped badly.
Things that did not fit cleanly into sister or enemy or friend.

Continuum had not solved any of that by putting them under one roof.

Good.

Anko leaned back.

"She's annoying."

Nazuna, asleep:

"Mmh."

Mau looked at Anko.

"Did she hear you?"

"Probably."

Nazuna:

"Mean."

Still eyes closed.

Mau laughed.

Anko threw a scrap of paper at her.

Nazuna caught it without waking.

Impossible.

That evening, everyone left for dinner.

Nazuna remained.

Anko came back.

Put a plate on the table.

Then kicked the leg of Nazuna's chair.

"Night creature."

Nazuna opened one eye.

"What year?"

"Same one."

"Tragic."

She sat up.

Looked at the curtains.

Paused.

"What the hell are those?"

Anko smiled.

"You missed democracy."

Nazuna stared.

"You chose curtains without me?"

"You were present."

"I was unconscious."

"Skill issue."

Nazuna stood.

Went directly to Mau.

He was eating.

"Why did you allow this?"

Mau:

"I have no authority."

"Useless."

"Correct."

She took food from his plate.

Frieren watched.

Mau:

"Why does everyone steal from me?"

Frieren:

"Because you allow it."

Nazuna looked at Frieren.

Then stole one thing from her plate too.

Frieren's eyes narrowed.

Danger.

Nazuna slowly put it back.

Seiko laughed from across the room.

Nazuna sat beside Anko.

Complained for twelve minutes.

Then ate the dinner Anko had saved.

The important day ended.

No blood crisis.
No mystery.
No confrontation.

Just proof that Nazuna could sleep through the family's life and still wake inside it.
