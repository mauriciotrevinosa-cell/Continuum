# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 9 — The Seating Chart Dies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary Week 3 | G5 stops behaving like an arrival cohort

Nobody made a seating chart.

That was why it died.

At the first G5 meal, people had clustered naturally around the person they knew.

Reasonable.

Mai near Maki.

Nijika and Ryo near Bocchi/Kita.

Richeh and Tetia near Coco/Agott/Qifrey.

Vamola, Seiko and Turbo Granny in the Dandadan disaster radius.

Jinshi near Maomao.

Milim near Rimuru.

Senku and Suika together.

Kusuri with Hakari, Karane, Shizuka and Nano.

Nazuna near Anko when awake.

The pattern had made sense.

Three weeks later it had become impossible.

Suika sat with Umaru and Coco.

Senku was at the other end of the table arguing with Maomao and Kusuri about storage temperature.

Kusuri:

"It's stable enough."

Maomao:

"That sentence should be illegal."

Senku:

"It is measurably stable enough for the labeled compounds."

Maomao:

"Which labels?"

Kusuri became very interested in her food.

Jinshi was helping Fern with an intake problem.

Not because Maomao asked him.

Mai sat beside Anko.

Nobody knew when that started.

Anko said something.

Mai laughed once.

Maki saw.

Did not comment.

Nijika was helping Momo distribute plates.

Ryo had been banned from helping after eating ingredients.

Bocchi sat between Kita and Shizuka because Shizuka's quiet did not require Bocchi to perform.

Nano had discovered Rimuru was useful for systems questions and therefore kept asking him things during dinner.

Rimuru regretted being competent.

Milim sat with Mau.

This was new.

She had originally followed Rimuru everywhere.

Now she had discovered Mau was easier to bother.

"Mau."

"No."

"I didn't ask yet."

"Probability."

"What if we make the training area bigger?"

"No."

"See? You didn't listen."

"Was I wrong?"

Milim considered.

"No."

She stole his food.

Family resemblance acquired through crime.

Seiko watched.

"She's got you trained."

Mau:

"Why is that what everyone says?"

Frieren:

"Evidence."

Mau looked betrayed.

Across from him, Nazuna woke up halfway through dinner.

"Morning."

Anko:

"It's night."

"Exactly."

G5 no longer looked like G5.

Not because the old relationships disappeared.

They had multiplied.

Mai still looked for Maki when something truly mattered.

Nijika still knew Bocchi in ways newer friends did not.

Richeh and Tetia still shared Witch Hat history.

Milim still loved Rimuru.

Suika still trusted Senku first.

Kusuri's reunion group remained hers.

Those bonds were roots.

The new relationships were branches.

Mau looked around.

Some people at the table were already family.

Some were becoming family.

Some might remain deeply close without ever using that word.

That was fine.

He caught Senku looking at the table too.

Mau:

"What?"

Senku:

"Nothing."

"You had a face."

"I was calculating food throughput."

Mau smiled.

"Sure."

Senku ignored him.

Suika called from the other end:

"Senku! Coco says I can help tomorrow!"

Senku answered immediately.

"With what?"

Coco:

"Art."

Senku looked alarmed.

The table laughed.

Not at him.

Mostly.

The arrival wave had ended.

People remained.
