# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 15 — Rem Has Two Cups

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary Week 3 | Rem's belonging grows through habit without forced declaration

Rem discovered the second cup by accident.

Momo handed it to her.

Blue rim.

Not the same cup.

Similar.

Rem stared.

Momo:

"What?"

"Nothing."

"Tea?"

"Yes."

At the old inn, Rem had left one cup on the shelf.

Deliberately.

No declaration.

Just easier.

At the new settlement, someone had apparently decided she also needed a cup.

Rem looked at Momo.

"Did you buy this for me?"

Momo:

"No."

Fern, passing:

"It came in a set."

Rem relaxed.

Then, irrationally, felt disappointed.

She disliked that.

Mau arrived.

Saw the cup.

Saw her face.

Made the worst possible choice.

"Symbolism?"

Rem glared.

He raised both hands.

"Sorry."

Progress had limits.

Ori entered behind him.

"That's yours."

Rem looked at her.

"Apparently."

Frieren came in last.

Looked at cup one time.

"Good."

Rem:

"What does that mean?"

Frieren:

"We kept running out."

Practical.

Perfect.

They drank tea.

Mau was supposed to be reviewing route notes.

He lasted eight minutes.

Then his attention slid.

Rem noticed before he did.

"Head?"

Mau blinked.

"Maybe."

Frieren looked up.

Ori put down her cup.

Old emergency reflex tried to fill the room.

Nobody moved yet.

Mau closed his eyes.

Waited.

"Just tired."

Rem watched.

This was different from the field.

There, every change had meant:
could he die?
could he disappear?
was this body still his?

Here:
tired could be tired.

Frieren reached over.

Touched two fingers to his wrist.

Not diagnostic.

Habit.

Mau leaned into her shoulder.

Ori resumed drinking.

Rem did too.

Five minutes passed.

No crisis.

That was perhaps the strangest part of recovery.

Ordinary explanations returned.

Later Rem helped Ori carry linens.

They passed one of the still-provisional rooms.

Ori stopped.

"That one is empty."

Rem:

"I know."

"You could use it."

There it was.

The question again.

Rem looked inside.

She expected pressure.

Ori only continued holding linens.

Rem said:

"Not yet."

Ori nodded.

They walked.

After three steps, Rem stopped.

Turned back.

Put one folded blanket on the room's shelf.

Ori looked.

Rem:

"For guests."

Ori:

"Okay."

Neither believed the label completely.

Neither challenged it.

That night Rem slept at the old inn.

The next night she slept at the new settlement because returning in rain was stupid.

Two nights later, old inn again.

Then three at the settlement.

No schedule.

No announcement.

One morning she reached for a cup without looking.

Blue rim.

New place.

She drank.

Only afterward did she realize she had known exactly where it would be.

She did not move it.
