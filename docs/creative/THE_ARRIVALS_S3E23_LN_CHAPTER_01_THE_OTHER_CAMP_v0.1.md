# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 10 — The Other Camp

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S3E23 — `The Other Camp`

The other community was smaller than rumor.

That mattered.

No walls.

No fighters standing dramatically at the entrance.

Laundry.

Cooking smoke.

Children.

Old people.

People who looked tired in ways combat did not explain.

The Searchers had found them by accident while verifying disappearances.

The camp had been built inside the edge of a native village that tolerated them because most of them caused no trouble and had learned quickly.

Their spokesperson did not look impressed when Rimuru arrived.

Good.

"We know who you are."

Rimuru smiled carefully.

"That sounded threatening."

"It wasn't meant to."

"Also good."

Mau had not come.

Deliberately.

The meeting was about whether the two communities should exchange routes, warnings and emergency signals.

Not about Mau.

That choice made the conversation possible.

The other community did not want to move.

They had reasons.

Real ones.

The first was simple:

"You gather power in one place."

Rimuru waited.

"So people aim at you."

No one at the table could prove that wrong.
