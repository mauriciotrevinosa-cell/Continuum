# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 5 — The Table Gets a Roof

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Continuity state:** Arrival House formalizes as temporary structure

Rain solved the naming argument.

The intake table got wet.

Forms ruined.

Maps curled.

Fern stared at the sky.

"Build a roof."

Two days later the table had canvas.

Then a second table.

Three mismatched chairs.

A crate.

A small privacy screen.

Arrival House.

Still not a house.

Now no one cared.

Functions grew:
- food;
- one-night emergency bedding;
- medical check;
- maps;
- missing-person board access;
- Contract/privacy explanation;
- local-language help;
- status discussion.

Not:
- interrogation;
- automatic registration;
- permanent oath;
- power inventory on arrival.

The process changed when frightened people used it.

One woman asked only for shoes.

Wakana found temporary ones.

One child asked whether monsters could enter.

Mikasa showed the alarm line.

One traveler asked if leaving after one night would offend them.

Fern said:

"No."

The answer surprised him.

People began calling the structure Arrival House because what happened there mattered more than architecture.

The settlement now had an institution before it had a proper building for it.
