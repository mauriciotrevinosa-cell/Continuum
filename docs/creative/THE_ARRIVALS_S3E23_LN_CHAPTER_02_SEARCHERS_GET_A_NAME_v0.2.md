# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 6 — Searchers Get a Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Continuity state:** Proto-search work becomes explicit social function

The Searchers existed before the name.

Everyone knew that afterward.

Nobody noticed before.

A merchant arrived at Arrival House and asked:

"Where are the Searchers?"

Fern looked at Okarun.

Okarun looked behind himself.

"The who?"

"The people who go looking."

There.

Name.

The merchant had heard:
if someone vanished on a road,
if an Arrival was held somewhere,
if strange light fell beyond farms,
tell the Searchers.

No one had published that.

Function created reputation.

Okarun looked uncomfortable.

Momo looked delighted.

"We have a faction."

"No," Fern said.

"Organization?"

"No."

"Cool jackets?"

Okarun:

"Maybe."

Fern:

"No."

The meeting happened that afternoon because pretending the function did not exist would only make it less accountable.

What did Searchers actually do?

Verify sightings.

Map routes.

Find missing Arrivals.

Escort vulnerable travelers.

Contact native settlements.

Check holding-site rumors.

Carry messages.

Document evidence.

Distinguish trap from distress when possible.

Extract when negotiation failed or immediate harm made waiting worse.

The last part produced argument.

Aira wanted faster extraction authority.

Anko wanted documentation.

Mikasa wanted role clarity.

Himmel wanted someone who could speak before someone who could fight.

Everyone agreed that rule should be written embarrassingly large.

BRING SOMEONE WHO CAN TALK.

Maomao added:

WATER.

Okarun:

"That's not a rule."

"It is now."

Then the most important rule:

DO NOT SEND THE SAME PEOPLE JUST BECAUSE THEY WERE GOOD LAST TIME.

Mau looked at it.

Memory Arc.

Witches.

Diablo.

Frieren.

Search had nearly become:
send strongest people who care most.

Unsustainable.

Searchers needed composition by need.

Navigation.

Diplomacy.

Medicine.

Tracking.

Stealth.

Translation.

Combat.

Logistics.

Anomaly sensing.

Not one superhero squad.

Rem joined some missions.

Not all.

Kaneki joined where frightening biology might trigger bad assumptions.

Not all.

Aira joined routes and extraction.

Not all.

Himmel went where conversation mattered.

Mikasa where terrain and security mattered.

Mau did not become automatic leader.

First real proof arrived two days later.

A team left following the brother sighting.

Frieren on it.

Okarun.

Himmel.

Rem.

One native guide.

No Mau.

Mau stood near the route post.

Everything in him wanted to go.

He was physically capable.

That mattered.

Frieren adjusted her pack.

"You don't have to come."

Mau almost heard rejection.

Memory Arc reflex.

Then corrected.

She meant the sentence.

"You've got enough people?"

Okarun nodded.

"Yes."

Mau looked at Rem.

She nodded too.

Trust distributed.

Mau stepped back.

"Okay."

The team left.

Mau stayed.

No one died because he was not present.

The settlement kept functioning.

Hours later, Searchers returned with information, not a rescued brother.

Partial success.

Nobody treated the trip as failure.

That night the merchant asked again:

"Where are the Searchers?"

Fern pointed at a board containing names, routes and current availability.

The function had become real enough not to require one face.
