# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 11 — We Survived by Staying Small

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Source episode:** S3E23 — `The Other Camp`

The spokesperson listed what had worked.

No displays of power.

No permanent armed patrols visible from roads.

Small groups.

Native trade.

No public names.

No famous fighters.

No one person becoming a symbol.

Rimuru heard the last one.

Did not react.

Mikasa did.

The spokesperson continued.

"We survived because nobody cared enough to hate us."

That sentence stayed in the room.

One of their residents asked:

"How many attacks have you had?"

Nobody answered quickly.

Sukuna was not the same kind of attack.

The Bocchi incident was.

Registration pressure.

Rumors.

Holding sites.

The number depended on definition.

The other community had fewer.

Their model had worked.

So far.

Mikasa asked:

"What happens when one of you disappears?"

The spokesperson looked at her.

"We look."

"With what?"

"People."

"How many routes?"

Silence.

Their weakness was visible too.

Small meant unnoticed.

Small also meant fewer eyes.

Less medical depth.

Less defense.

Less capacity to absorb one bad week.

No one won the argument.

They exchanged signal codes anyway.
