## Chapter 12 — The People Who Cannot Fight

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E24 — `Refuge`

The first group from the other camp came after a checkpoint closed the road behind them.

Not warriors.

A teacher.

Two teenagers.

A woman with an injured knee.

An old man who apologized every time someone helped him stand.

They did not call themselves refugees.

Neither did the settlement.

Not at first.

They needed three nights.

That was the plan.

The Arrival tent became full by noon.

Bunks did not exist yet.

The old inn took six people.

The temporary shelter took four.

Mau and Frieren lost their room for one night because it was one of the few spaces with a door that closed properly.

Frieren looked at Mau.

Mau looked at the floor.

"Old times?"

"No."

"Fair."

They slept in the common room.

Nobody died from inconvenience.

The visitors watched powerful people step around them.

Milim carrying blankets.

Diablo boiling water because Rimuru told him to.

Frieren giving up privacy.

Stark moving beds.

Mau carrying nothing because Maomao had told him his back still needed monitoring after the Memory Arc.

One teenager asked:

"Are all of you fighters?"

Umaru answered from a pile of bedding.

"No."

That was the correct answer.
