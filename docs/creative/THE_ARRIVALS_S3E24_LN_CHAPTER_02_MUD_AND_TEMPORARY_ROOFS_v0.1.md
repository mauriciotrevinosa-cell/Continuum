# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 13 — Mud and Temporary Roofs

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 13  
**Source episode:** S3E24 — `Refuge`

Three nights became a week.

Not because anyone voted.

The road remained unsafe.

Then another family came.

Then two people from a holding site.

The future settlement became inhabited before it was ready.

That offended every plan.

Mud reached the medical shelter.

Temporary roofs leaked.

Cooking shifted twice because smoke blew through sleeping areas.

A water queue formed.

Maomao threatened bodily harm if anyone used the clean intake to wash boots again.

No one asked whether she meant it.

The place was ugly.

Alive.

Safe enough.

At dusk Mau stood above the site and tried to compare it to the old map.

The map had clean spaces.

Housing.

Storage.

Arrival House.

Medical.

Workshop.

Reality had blankets hanging between stakes and one child drawing on a crate because there was nowhere else dry.

Frieren joined him.

"We did it wrong."

"Yes."

Mau looked at her.

She continued.

"We built for the people we expected."

Below them, someone laughed.

Frieren looked toward the sound.

"More came."

Mau nodded.

The city changed before it existed.
