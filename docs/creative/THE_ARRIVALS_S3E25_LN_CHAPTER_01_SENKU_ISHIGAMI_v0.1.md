**Source episode:** S3E25 — `Senku`

Senku arrived annoyed.

Not at them.

At himself.

The Searchers found him three days south with measurements written on bark because he had not had paper.

He asked for the date.

Then latitude.

Nobody had a reliable latitude.

That annoyed him more.

By the time he reached the future site, he had already identified one problem he could not explain:

his thinking was slower than he expected.

Not slow.

Never that.

Wrong relative to himself.

He knew what it felt like to hold more variables at once.

The capacity was reduced.

He hated it.

He also trusted measurement more than pride.

So he started keeping tests.

Memory span.

Calculation time.

Error rate.

Problem decomposition.

No grand declaration that he had become stupid.

Data.

The settlement saw a young man with impossible confidence and a stack of questions.

Senku saw:
- wet ground;
- improvised drainage;
- inconsistent road grade;
- mixed sanitation zones;
- useful magical infrastructure;
- people trying very hard.

He smiled.

Mau disliked the smile immediately.

"What's wrong?"

Senku looked at the entire site.

"How much time do you have?"
