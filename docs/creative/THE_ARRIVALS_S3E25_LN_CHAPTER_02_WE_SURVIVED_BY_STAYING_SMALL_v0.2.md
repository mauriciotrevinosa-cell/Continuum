# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 14 — We Survived by Staying Small

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 14

The second community's argument had survived reality.

No giant power displays.

No famous residents.

No public claim to territory.

No concentration large enough to become easy political shorthand.

"We survived because nobody cared enough to fear us."

The sentence hurt because it was not cowardice.

It was strategy.

Mikasa asked:

"What happens if someone disappears?"

The spokesperson answered:

"We look."

"How far?"

"As far as we can."

There.

The weakness.

Small meant less visible.

Also less capacity.

Fewer medics.

Fewer scouts.

Fewer safe beds.

Less ability to absorb one catastrophe.

Rimuru said:

"We're becoming a target."

"Yes."

"We can also find people you can't."

"Yes."

No winner.

The second community had avoided dangers the main settlement had invited.

The main settlement had built capacities the second could not match.

They agreed on one thing:

warning signals.

If a road became unsafe, both would know.

No merger.

Cooperation.

Enough.
