# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 15 — Not Our City

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 15

Some residents of the second community visited.

Most did not.

One looked at the main settlement and said:

"I couldn't live here."

Momo looked offended.

"Why?"

"Too many powerful people."

Milim was visible in the distance.

Fair.

Another disliked the intake table.

Another liked it.

One teenager loved Bocchi's music room.

An older resident hated the noise.

Good.

No universal appeal.

At dinner, someone from the main settlement asked:

"Wouldn't you be safer if you moved here?"

The second-community visitor answered:

"Safer from what?"

Silence.

Monster attack?

Probably.

Political targeting?

Maybe not.

Social absorption?

Definitely not, if they refused.

Mau listened.

Did not defend his home as objectively superior.

That was harder than expected.

The visitor left the next morning.

Took copies of route warnings.

Left a recipe.

No conversion.

The two communities became more real by remaining different.
