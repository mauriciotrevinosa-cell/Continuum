## Chapter 1 — Water Is Public

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E27 — `Water Is Public`

The first civic argument was about a pipe that did not exist.

Senku wanted measurements.

Qifrey wanted maintainable spell geometry.

Rimuru wanted redundancy.

Maomao wanted everyone to stop saying `clean enough.`

Mau wanted to know why six people were yelling near breakfast.

Nobody answered him.

The question was where public water ended and private water began.

If every house drew directly from one magical line, one bad modification could contaminate everyone.

If every house managed its own treatment, maintenance quality would vary.

If the system remained fully centralized, one failure could stop the settlement.

No elegant answer.

That was encouraging.

Senku drew three loops.

Primary intake.

Treatment.

Distribution.

Qifrey added magical lift points.

Agott added access marks.

Coco complained the marks looked ugly.

Wakana asked whether they could be integrated into surfaces people already touched.

That changed the design.

Public infrastructure became something people could see.

Inspect.

Report.

Understand.

Mau looked at the diagram.

"Who owns it?"

Everyone stopped.

Rimuru answered first.

"Nobody."

Senku shook his head.

"Then everybody neglects it."

Fern looked up.

"Or everybody is responsible."

"Same sentence if nobody's assigned."

They created maintenance shifts.

Not ownership.

Not glamorous.

Water became public before the town had a name.
