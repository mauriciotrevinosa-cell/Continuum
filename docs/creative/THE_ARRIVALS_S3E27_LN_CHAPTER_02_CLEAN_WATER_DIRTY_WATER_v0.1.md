# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 2 — Clean Water, Dirty Water

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S3E27 — `Water Is Public`

The settlement's most important line was not defensive.

It was blue.

Clean water.

The second most important was brown.

Dirty water.

Coco objected to brown.

"Why?"

"It's ugly."

Maomao pointed at the line.

"Good."

Coco stared.

"You want it ugly?"

"I want nobody to confuse it."

That ended the aesthetic objection.

Mostly.

Waste separated early.

Human waste.

Kitchen waste.

Medical contamination.

Workshop runoff.

Storm water.

Wash water.

Not everything required the same treatment.

Senku loved that.

"Finally."

Maomao looked at him.

"What?"

"Specific categories."

"You say that like it is rare."

Senku looked at the settlement.

Maomao followed his gaze.

"Fair."

Qifrey designed magical stages that could redirect and isolate flow without requiring a mage to stand there every hour.

But every stage had a manual bypass.

Senku insisted.

Qifrey agreed.

Magic could fail.

Mechanical pieces could fail.

People could misunderstand both.

A robust system assumed failure before failure became tragedy.

Rimuru looked at the layered channels.

"Looks complicated."

Senku smiled.

"Reality usually is."

The town became safer by refusing simple answers.
