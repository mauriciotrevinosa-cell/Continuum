# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 20 — The Draft

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 20

The first draft was terrible.

Too long.

Too defensive.

Too much "we."

The second community crossed out half of it.

A native representative crossed out the phrase:

peaceful Otherworlders.

"Why?"

"Because rights shouldn't depend on being harmless."

Anko underlined that.

Aira liked it.

Mikasa did too.

The third draft separated:
- sanctuary;
- accountability;
- movement;
- detention;
- cooperation.

No demand that towns abolish all checks.

No acceptance of indefinite detention.

No promise that the main settlement would shelter someone from consequences for current harm.

No threat.

One line survived every revision:

"No person should disappear because fear found a category easier than a name."

Mau liked it.

So did people who did not particularly like Mau.

Good.

The statement gained signatures from:
- main settlement delegates;
- second-community delegates;
- willing native partners;
- individual witnesses.

Not from "all Otherworlders."

No false mandate.
