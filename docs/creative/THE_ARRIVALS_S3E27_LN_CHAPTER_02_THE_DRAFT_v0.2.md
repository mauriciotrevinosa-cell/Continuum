# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 29 — The Draft

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 29

The first complete draft was terrible.

Too long.

Too defensive.

Too much "we."

The second community crossed out half.

A native representative crossed out another quarter.

Nano stared at surviving page count.

"Statistically destructive."

Momo:

"Emotionally healing."

Nobody asked.

The central fight happened over one paragraph.

Main settlement version:

> We provide sanctuary to those unfairly targeted because of origin.

Second community:

"Sounds like you're appointing yourselves gatekeepers."

Mau frowned.

"How?"

"If sanctuary is yours to grant, you become center."

Fair.

Arrival House representative suggested:

> People should be able to seek safety across multiple communities without being forced into one political identity.

That survived.

Another fight:

registration.

Anko wanted:
registration cannot become disappearance.

Native representative wanted:
communities may reasonably ask who enters protected facilities.

Both true.

Draft became:

> Identification requirements must be specific, reviewable and tied to a concrete safety purpose; they must not become indefinite detention or collective suspicion.

Momo read.

"Nobody chants that."

"Good," Anko said.

The statement refused slogans.

Mau kept trying to make lines shorter.

Sometimes improved.

Sometimes worse.

One phrase:

"Origin is not guilt."

Everyone liked it.

Then a native delegate added:

"Origin is also not innocence."

Mau looked up.

The room quieted.

Yes.

Put both.

> Origin is neither guilt nor innocence. Actions require evidence.

That line stayed.

Aira asked why current harm still needed a separate clause.

Because sanctuary could not equal immunity.

They wrote:

> Seeking protection does not erase responsibility for harm someone actually committed.

Then:

> Accusation is not proof.

Both.

The statement slowly became less emotionally satisfying and more robust.

Good.

Near evening, a young refugee who had not attended the meeting asked to read it.

Fern handed over the paper.

The refugee read all of it.

Then:

"Where does it say I don't have to join you?"

Silence.

They had implied.

Not said.

The second-community representative smiled without humor.

"Exactly."

New line:

> Temporary refuge, travel assistance or rescue does not create an obligation to become resident, soldier, ally or political member.

Mau stared.

Memory Arc.

Rescue not ownership.

Everything recurring.

They added it.

Final problem:

signature block.

Momo wanted names.

Anko wanted groups.

Second community wanted both.

So:
community signatures.
native partners.
individual witnesses.

Mau's name appeared only if he signed as an individual.

He did.

Near the bottom.

Not first.

Rimuru same.

Frieren signed after reading twice.

Bocchi stared at the line for a full minute before signing as an individual resident.

No requirement.

Her hand shook anyway.

The statement existed.

Not perfect.

Not universal.

No mandate beyond signers.

That was why it could honestly travel.
