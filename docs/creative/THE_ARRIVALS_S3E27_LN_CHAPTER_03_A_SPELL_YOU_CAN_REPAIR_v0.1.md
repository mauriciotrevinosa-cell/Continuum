# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 3 — A Spell You Can Repair

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E27 — `Water Is Public`

Qifrey rejected a beautiful circle.

Coco took it personally.

"It works."

"Yes."

"Then why?"

"Because you are the only person here who can read the correction layer quickly."

Coco stopped.

The circle was efficient.

Elegant.

Compact.

Bad civic infrastructure.

Agott understood first.

"If Coco leaves, nobody repairs it."

Coco looked at the design.

That hurt.

Not because she wanted to be irreplaceable.

Because she had designed like an artist solving one problem, not like a city surviving its creators.

Qifrey redrew it.

Larger.

Clearer.

Less clever.

Redundant symbols.

Physical markings around the magical layer.

An inspection path wide enough for a person carrying tools.

Senku approved.

"Ugly."

Coco glared.

"Maintainable."

Better word.

Weeks later, someone who had not designed the system repaired a damaged section using the embedded instructions.

Coco watched.

Pride arrived differently.

Not:

`Only I can do this.`

But:

`It works without me.`

Mau understood that feeling better than he wanted to.
