# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 22 — The First Reply

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 22

The first reply arrived two days later.

Not support.

A warning.

One local authority declared the statement proof that Otherworlders were organizing politically.

Momo read it.

"So we said we're not one state and they heard we're one state."

Anko:

"Yes."

"Great."

The notice quoted three lines accurately.

Then one inaccurately.

Searchers kept both copies.

Evidence.

The second reply came from a native village.

Different.

They would keep the east road open to travelers carrying no active warrant for a named crime.

Not full alliance.

Safe corridor.

Material consequence.

The third reply was silence.

Also information.

The fourth came from someone imprisoned at a holding station:

We heard you exist.

That changed Arrival House.

The message had not united the world.

It had made the settlement legible.

To friends.

Enemies.

People in between.

No one could pretend the chapter ended when the paper left their hands.
