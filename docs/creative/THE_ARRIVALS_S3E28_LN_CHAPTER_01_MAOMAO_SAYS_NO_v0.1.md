# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 4 — Maomao Says No

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 4  
**Source episode:** S3E28 — `A City That Isn't One`

The proposed bath system was beautiful.

Warm water.

Reused heat.

Drainage separated.

Privacy screens.

Wakana had already started thinking about textiles.

Marin had opinions.

Maomao walked through the plan.

"No."

Everyone groaned.

"Why?"

"Steam."

Silence.

She pointed.

Ventilation inadequate.

Condensation near structural wood.

Medical wash station too close to ordinary bathing.

One cleaning basin drained into the wrong treatment stage.

Senku looked delighted.

Maomao looked annoyed by his delight.

"We fix those."

"Obviously."

Momo whispered:

"They flirt by disagreeing."

Both turned.

"No," they said.

Momo grinned.

The bath design changed.

Less pretty on paper.

Better in air.

When it finally opened weeks later, nobody praised the vent spacing.

They praised hot water.

That was correct.

Infrastructure succeeded when users forgot how many arguments made comfort possible.
