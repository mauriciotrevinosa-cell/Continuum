## Chapter 5 — Roads Are Not Sacred

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Source episode:** S3E28 — `A City That Isn't One`

Senku erased a road.

Momo objected on historical grounds.

"It has a name."

"Old Road has a name too."

"Exactly."

"It was wrong."

"Still historic."

Senku looked at Rimuru.

"Is she always like this?"

"Yes."

The new settlement refused a grid for practical reasons before anyone called it design.

Terrain shaped movement.

Water access shaped movement.

Spell-maintenance corridors shaped movement.

Evacuation shaped movement.

Shade shaped movement.

Existing trees shaped movement.

Some streets curved.

Some narrowed.

Some widened into shared working courts.

One path passed around a rock because moving the rock cost more than accepting the bend.

Marin later declared the rock intentional.

Nobody corrected her.

The city began accumulating decisions that looked aesthetic only after necessity stopped being visible.

Mau walked the route with Frieren.

"No straight line."

"Good."

"You like this?"

Frieren looked at the trees.

"Yes."

Mau smiled.

"Why?"

"It feels difficult to conquer."

That was romantic enough for Frieren.
