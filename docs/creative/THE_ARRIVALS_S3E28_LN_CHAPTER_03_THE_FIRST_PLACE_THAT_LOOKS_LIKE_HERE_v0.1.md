# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 6 — The First Place That Looks Like Here

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Source episode:** S3E28 — `A City That Isn't One`

The first place that looked finished was not a house.

It was a public washing court.

That disappointed Mau.

Then he understood.

Water channels ran beneath open stone.

Maintenance marks remained visible.

A Witch Hat circle lifted clean water to a higher basin.

Mechanical overflow prevented the magic from becoming the only path.

Drainage separated before leaving the court.

Wakana had designed hooks and privacy cloth.

Marin had somehow made functional storage look intentional.

Maomao approved the cleaning schedule.

Senku had measured flow.

Rimuru had argued about capacity.

Coco painted a tiny symbol near one maintenance cover.

Agott pretended not to notice.

People used it.

Children splashed where they were not supposed to.

Someone complained.

Someone else cleaned.

Mau stood at the edge.

"This looks like nowhere I've seen."

Rimuru joined him.

"Same."

That was the point.

Not Earth.

Not Tempest.

Not a fantasy village.

Not Witch Hat.

Not any one home.

Here.

The first civic structure looked like Continuum before any residence did.
