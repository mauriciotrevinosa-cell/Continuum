# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 43 — The Rumor Reaches Sukuna

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 43  
**Continuity state:** Parallel hostile-world interlude | Sukuna remains active while anti-Otherworlder narratives harden

Sukuna heard about the settlement from a frightened man.

This was disappointing.

The man did not know he was speaking to Sukuna.

That made it better.

Roadside fire.

Cheap food.

Four travelers.

One story repeated badly.

"Otherworlders are gathering east."

Sukuna looked bored.

The speaker continued because silence frightened him.

"They built a fortress."

Another traveler:

"Refuge."

"Same thing."

"No."

"They have armed patrols."

"Search parties."

"Same thing."

"No."

Sukuna smiled.

The argument had already learned the shape of the rumor.

A third traveler lowered his voice.

"They say there's a man at the center."

That earned attention.

Not visibly.

"Strong?" Sukuna asked.

The traveler shrugged.

"Nobody knows."

Interesting.

"He doesn't have a world."

Less interesting.

"People follow him anyway."

Better.

The first man added:

"They're calling him the Worldless Man."

Sukuna laughed.

Everyone at the fire went quiet.

"What?"

"Nothing."

He had heard enough names for weak men who wanted importance.

King.
Saint.
Chosen.
Hero.

Worldless was new.

Still stupid.

Then the traveler said:

"Somebody said Yuta Okkotsu lives there."

The fire cracked.

Sukuna stopped smiling for half a second.

"Maki Zenin too."

There it was.

The world became less boring.

He asked where the rumor came from.

Nobody knew.

A copied notice.

A checkpoint officer.

A caravan.

Someone who knew someone.

Perfect.

Fear without ownership.

Sukuna had spent the season moving.

Eating.
Testing.
Learning which rules of this world resembled his and which only pretended to.

He had not become quiet because he had become harmless.

He had become selective because random destruction answered fewer questions now.

The world had more interesting things in it.

A city that feared Otherworlders.

A refuge accused of recruiting them.

Yuta.

Maki.

A man with no world people apparently chose to orbit.

Sukuna looked toward the east.

The travelers noticed.

The frightened man said:

"Don't go there."

Sukuna looked at him.

"Why?"

"They say if one of them is threatened, all of them answer."

That was almost certainly exaggerated.

Sukuna liked it anyway.

Another traveler unfolded a copied sheet.

The wording was familiar from road walls:

UNREGISTERED OTHERWORLDERS CONCENTRATING UNDER CENTRAL AUTHORITY.

SEARCH TEAMS OPERATING BEYOND LOCAL JURISDICTION.

The seal at the bottom belonged to nobody Sukuna recognized.

Could be real.

Could be fake.

Could be copied from somewhere that had become useful.

He did not care.

Somebody, or several somebodies, had discovered that fear became more efficient when written down.

Sukuna held the paper over the fire.

The traveler grabbed his wrist.

"Hey—"

Sukuna looked at the hand.

The traveler released him.

Smart.

The paper burned.

Sukuna stood.

"Are you going east?" someone asked.

He looked east again.

Then turned west.

"No."

Not yet.

The disappointment in the answer confused them.

Good.

Yuta being alive did not mean Sukuna had to run toward him like a dog hearing a bell.

Maki being there did not mean the settlement belonged to them.

And whoever was teaching the roads to fear that place had their own game.

Sukuna did not serve other people's games.

He might ruin one later.

For now, he walked.

Behind him, the travelers rebuilt the story before the ash cooled.

By the next town it would become:

Sukuna is heading for the Otherworlder fortress.

False.

For the moment.

That was the useful thing about rumors.

They did not need permission from anyone they described.
