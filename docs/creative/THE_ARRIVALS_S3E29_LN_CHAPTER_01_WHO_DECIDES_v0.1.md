The emergency grain store needed a location.

Three options.

All bad differently.

Near housing: accessible, fire risk.

Near the outer route: easy deliveries, vulnerable.

Near the water system: protected, contamination concern.

Everyone had an opinion.

Nobody had authority.

That became the problem.

Rimuru could decide.

Mau could persuade people.

Mikasa had defense expertise.

Senku had storage concerns.

Maomao had contamination concerns.

Native builders knew the land.

Residents would live beside it.

"Vote?" Momo suggested.

Nano asked:

"On technical safety?"

"Fine. Bad vote."

They separated the question.

Experts eliminated options that violated hard constraints.

Residents chose among viable options.

Rimuru coordinated implementation.

Emergency disputes could be appealed to a small rotating group.

Not a constitution.

A solution to grain.

But afterward, the method remained.

Governance did not arrive wearing ceremonial clothes.

It arrived because food needed somewhere to go.
