# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 3 — The Lie Brings People Too

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Continuity state:** Hostile propaganda paradoxically drives refuge traffic

Not everyone who arrived believed the settlement was safe.

One man came because propaganda said it was dangerous.

That sounded impossible.

Then he explained.

"If every official is scared of you, the people after me probably won't follow me inside."

Arrival House hated the logic.

Could not deny it.

He was fleeing a local group.

Not official detention.

Personal threat.

Temporary protection.

Fern wrote status.

The man looked around.

"Where's Mau?"

Mau, carrying linens nearby, stopped.

Of course.

"Here."

The man stared.

"You don't look like—"

Mau held up hand.

"Whatever story you heard, probably no."

The man considered.

"Can you erase registration records?"

"No."

"Can Rimuru command every monster around the city?"

Rimuru, entering at exact wrong time:

"No."

Complicated silence.

Momo started laughing.

Another newcomer arrived convinced witches lived under streets.

Coco looked offended.

"Why under?"

Agott:

"Do not engage."

Another thought Arrival House gave permanent residency automatically.

False.

Another believed old inn had been abandoned after "capital" moved.

Wrong.

Fern finally approved Momo's joke.

They made a board.

COMMON THINGS THAT ARE NOT TRUE.

Number one:

MAU DOES NOT OWN THE SETTLEMENT.

Mau insisted bold.

Number two:

RIMURU DOES NOT EAT APPLICANTS.

Rimuru stared.

"Why is this necessary?"

"Because three people asked."

"I hate this place."

Number three:

ARRIVAL HOUSE CANNOT GUARANTEE PERMANENT RESIDENCE.

Number four:

THE OLD INN IS NOT ABANDONED.

Number five:

MILIM SHOULD NOT BE CHALLENGED TO PROVE ANYTHING.

Milim objected immediately.

"Why?"

Everyone else:

"No."

Number six:

SEARCHERS ARE NOT A KIDNAPPING SERVICE.

That one appeared after someone asked whether they could "retrieve" an unwilling relative.

No.

Important.

Number seven:

SECOND COMMUNITY IS NOT A DISTRICT OF THIS SETTLEMENT.

Second community representative approved remotely.

Board became popular.

People added questions beneath.

Can gods be contacted here?

Unknown / not public service.

Can powers be restored?

Sometimes adaptation changes; no guarantees.

Does Mau remember every source world?

No.

Mau circled that one.

No one knew why rumor claimed otherwise.

The propaganda that meant to isolate the settlement had accidentally made it mythic.

Dangerous fortress.

Miracle refuge.

Secret kingdom.

Monster city.

Every label attracted somebody different.

The settlement spent increasing time correcting mythology before helping the person beneath it.

Mau looked at board one evening.

"At least number one is first."

Fern:

"Because it's the one people get wrong most."

Mau sighed.

Public identity had become infrastructure too.

You maintained it.

You never finished it.
