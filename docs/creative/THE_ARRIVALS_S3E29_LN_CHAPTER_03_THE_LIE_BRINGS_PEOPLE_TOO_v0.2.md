# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 3 — The Lie Brings People Too

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 3
**Continuity state:** Hostile propaganda paradoxically drives refuge traffic

Not everyone who arrived believed the settlement was safe.

One man came because propaganda said it was dangerous.

That sounded impossible.

Then he explained.

"If every official is scared of you, they probably won't follow me inside."

Arrival House hated the logic.

Could not deny it.

He was fleeing a local group.

Not law enforcement.

Personal threat.

Temporary protection.

Another newcomer arrived convinced Mau could erase registration records.

False.

Another thought Rimuru controlled monsters.

Complicated.

Another thought witches lived under the streets.

Also false.

Mostly.

The settlement spent more time correcting its own mythology.

Momo suggested printing a list.

COMMON THINGS THAT ARE NOT TRUE.

Fern approved.

Momo regretted joking.

The list became popular.

Number one:

Mau does not own the settlement.

Number two:

Rimuru does not eat applicants.

Rimuru objected to the need for clarification.

Number three:

Arrival House cannot guarantee permanent residence.

Number four:

The old inn is not abandoned.

Number five:

Milim should not be challenged to prove anything.

Milim objected.

Everyone else did not.
