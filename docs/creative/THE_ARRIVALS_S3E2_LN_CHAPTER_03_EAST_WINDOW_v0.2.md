## Chapter 6 — East Window

<!-- scene-id: s3e2-ln03-walk -->

Mau and Frieren stayed after everyone else left.

Not secretly.

Rimuru had said:

"Last wagon in twenty minutes."

Frieren had said:

"We'll walk."

Rimuru had looked at Mau.

Mau had looked at Frieren.

"We'll walk."

Official boyfriend status had not increased Mau's authority.

That was probably healthy.

They crossed the proposed residential slope.

No stakes there yet.

Only grass.

Frieren stopped.

"Here."

Mau looked around.

"Here what?"

"Bedroom."

He laughed.

"You picked a bedroom before the house."

"East window."

He remembered.

The final night of S2.

Morning light she did not intend to be awake for.

"You were serious."

"Yes."

<!-- scene-id: s3e2-ln03-notours -->

Mau looked across the slope.

"How much space do we take?"

Frieren looked at him.

"For the house?"

"For us."

That question had become harder.

The old inn solved space by accident.

Rooms existed.

People filled them.

This place required choosing who got what before walls existed.

Frieren said, "Enough."

"Extremely precise."

"You ask bad questions."

Mau sat in the grass.

Frieren sat beside him.

"Private rooms?" he asked.

"Yes."

"Shared core?"

"Yes."

"Do we put Ori near us?"

Frieren looked at him.

"Ask Ori."

Mau paused.

Right.

They did.

Later.

Ori listened to the question, then looked at both of them suspiciously.

"Near how?"

Mau:

"Same building?"

Frieren:

"Or next building."

Ori thought longer than Mau expected.

"I want close."

Mau smiled.

Ori held up one finger.

"But not because you put me there."

His smile changed.

"Okay."

"I pick."

"Okay."

She pointed somewhere downhill.

Mau looked.

"That's drainage reserve."

Ori lowered her hand.

"Then not there."

Frieren almost smiled.

The point survived the joke.

Ori would choose again after the site had walls.

Right.

"Coco?"

"Ask Coco."

"Fern?"

"Ask Fern."

Mau leaned back.

"This is going badly."

"No."

Frieren touched his shoulder.

"It's going correctly."

The city could not be a physical diagram of Mau's relationships.

People got to choose where they lived.

Obvious.

Still worth learning.

<!-- scene-id: s3e2-ln03-kiss -->

On the walk home, Frieren took his hand.

No emergency.

No audience they knew about.

Mau looked at her.

"What?"

"Nothing."

"You're looking."

"Boyfriend behavior."

Frieren stopped.

Mau immediately regretted borrowing Umaru's language.

Frieren smiled.

Then kissed him once.

"Yes."

She kept walking.

Mau stood still long enough that she had to pull him forward.

The future bedroom remained grass behind them.

That was fine.

They had time.
