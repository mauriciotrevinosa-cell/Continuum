# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 4 — Senku Ishigami

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 4
**Continuity state:** Senku arrives after settlement inhabited and flawed

The Searchers found Senku with numbers written on bark.

That was enough to identify personality before name.

He asked:
- date;
- distance to nearest reliable settlement;
- elevation;
- latitude.

Nobody had a reliable latitude.

He looked offended by the world.

Then asked for paper.

On the walk back, Senku tested himself.

Mental multiplication.

Memory span.

Reaction to multiple variables.

Something was wrong.

Not intelligence gone.

Capacity reduced relative to himself.

He noticed because of course he did.

At the settlement, he did not announce:

"I'm dumber here."

He measured.

How many simultaneous constraints before error rose?

How long to solve known classes of problems?

What changed with rest?

What changed after exposure to local magic?

Data before ego.

Then he looked at the settlement.

Occupied.

Wet.

Alive.

Messy.

Water line.

Temporary drainage.

Mixed building quality.

Canvas Arrival House beside real structures.

Bridge-repair supply pile blocking half a service route.

Children running where a cart wanted to go.

Senku smiled.

Mau saw.

"What's wrong?"

Senku looked around.

"How much time do you have?"

Mau hated him immediately.

Probably healthy.
