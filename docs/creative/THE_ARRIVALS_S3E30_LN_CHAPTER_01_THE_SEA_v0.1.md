# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 9 — The Sea

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E30 — `The Sea`

The Searchers found Eren near salt water.

That was the first thing in the report Mikasa understood.

The second was the name.

She stopped reading.

Anko watched her.

No one touched her.

Mikasa read again.

`Eren Yeager.`

Alive.

Confused.

Last stable lived memory consistent with reaching the sea with his friends.

Mikasa sat down.

The room became too small.

Mau was there.

He did not say:

`Isn't this what you wanted?`

Nobody who had watched reunions carefully said that anymore.

Wanting someone back did not guarantee which version came.

Mikasa remembered a future this Eren had not lived.

That fact had existed as theory for months.

Now it was walking toward them.

She stood.

"I'm going."

Mau nodded.

"Do you want anyone—"

"Yes."

He waited.

Mikasa looked at Armin's empty space on the missing board.

Then at the Searchers list.

"Anko."

Anko stood.

No maximum-force reunion.

No audience.

Mikasa left to meet a boy whose face belonged to several different kinds of grief.
