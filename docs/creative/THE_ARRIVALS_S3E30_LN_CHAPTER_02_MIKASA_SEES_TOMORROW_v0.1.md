# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 10 — Mikasa Sees Tomorrow

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S3E30 — `The Sea`

Eren recognized Mikasa immediately.

That was the problem.

Relief crossed his face.

Then confusion when it did not cross hers the same way.

"Mikasa?"

She stopped three steps away.

Same voice.

Younger context.

A version of him still carrying questions he had not answered yet.

Eren looked at Anko.

Then back.

"What happened?"

Mikasa had rehearsed nothing.

How do you tell someone:

I remember what you become.

I remember things you chose.

I remember losing you after years you have not lived.

She said:

"We remember different amounts."

Eren frowned.

"Of what?"

"Your life."

That was a terrible sentence.

Accurate.

Anko stayed silent.

Eren looked toward the sea.

Then at Mikasa.

"Did I die?"

Mikasa's throat closed.

Not the first question she expected.

"Eventually."

"Everyone does."

Mikasa almost laughed.

Almost.

Then Eren saw her face.

"Oh."

The future entered the reunion without being described.

Not yet.

Mikasa stepped forward.

Eren did too.

They did not hug immediately.

That hurt.

Then Mikasa chose present over memory for one second and pulled him close.

Eren froze.

Held her back.

Neither version of history disappeared.
