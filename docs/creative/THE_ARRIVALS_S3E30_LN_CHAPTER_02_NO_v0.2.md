# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 5 — No

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 5
**Continuity state:** Senku first infrastructure review

Senku's first contribution was:

"No."

Rimuru pointed at the drainage plan.

"Which part?"

"Yes."

Coco frowned.

Agott leaned closer.

Senku crouched.

The waste channel crossed a future expansion line.

One raised road trapped runoff against housing.

Food storage ventilation assumed lower occupancy than Arrival House had already created.

The workshop position was convenient and terrible if fire spread.

The clean-water access design was clever.

Senku liked it.

Then asked:

"What happens if the magic fails during heavy rain?"

Silence.

Not because nobody had imagined failure.

Because they had imagined failures separately.

Magic failure.

Rain.

Population overflow.

Not all together.

"No."

Coco crossed her arms.

"We worked hard on this."

"I can tell."

"That sounded mean."

"It wasn't."

"That sounded mean too."

Maomao arrived halfway through.

Agreed with Senku on sanitation.

That made everyone more upset.

The settlement had been built enough that being wrong cost something.

Good.

Otherwise redesign would be free.
