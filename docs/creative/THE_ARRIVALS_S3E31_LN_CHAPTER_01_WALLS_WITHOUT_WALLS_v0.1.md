# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 12 — Walls Without Walls

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E31 — `Walls Without Walls`

Eren expected walls.

He said so.

Mikasa did not.

The settlement had mountains, water, controlled approaches and too many reasons not to box itself in.

Senku drew layers.

Observation posts.

Route beacons.

Magical sensing points.

Physical trip indicators where magic failed.

Alarm levels.

Evacuation corridors.

Hardened refuge structures.

Inner safe zones.

Mobile response.

Last-defense points.

Eren stared.

"No wall."

Rimuru nodded.

"Some barriers when needed."

"That's not the same."

"No."

Eren walked the map.

"So people can still leave."

Mikasa looked at him.

"Yes."

He noticed.

"That sounded important."

"It is."

Eren did not know why yet.

The defense system assumed danger could come.

It did not assume every resident had to become a prisoner to survive it.

That distinction became part of the architecture.
