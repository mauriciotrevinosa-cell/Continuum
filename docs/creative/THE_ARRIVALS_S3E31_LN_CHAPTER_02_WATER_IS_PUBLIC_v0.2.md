# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 9 — Water Is Public

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Continuity state:** Hybrid civic-water redesign begins

The most political object in settlement was a pipe.

Not even installed.

It sat across two crates while seven people argued around it.

Question:

Where did public water responsibility end?

If every residence connected directly to one magical main line, one bad modification risked everyone.

If each residence treated its own water, quality varied.

If everything remained centralized, one failure became settlement-wide.

Senku drew loops.

Intake.

Treatment.

Storage.

Distribution.

Isolation.

Overflow.

Qifrey added magical lift points.

Not endless pumping.

Specific elevation transitions where spellwork reduced mechanical load.

Coco suggested visible maintenance marks.

Agott asked:

"Visible to who?"

Coco stopped.

Good.

Wakana asked whether someone carrying tools could physically reach the access point.

Better.

Maomao asked whether clean service route crossed contaminated route during repairs.

Best.

Rimuru asked capacity at refuge load.

Not current population.

V15 had taught them normal population was fiction.

The design changed again.

Mau listened for half an hour.

Then asked:

"Who owns it?"

Rimuru answered automatically:

"Nobody."

Senku looked at him.

"Then nobody maintains it."

Rimuru frowned.

Fern, sitting with ledger:

"Assign responsibility without private ownership."

There.

Maintenance could be public obligation without turning water into someone's property.

They wrote:
inspection shifts.
named crews.
training.
replacement stores.
repair authority.
emergency shutoff authority.
public usage rights.

Then the difficult part:

What if someone refuses maintenance duty?

Momo:

"Water jail."

No.

What if one district uses far more than expected?

What if Arrival House surge doubles demand?

What if a workshop contaminates a line?

What if magic user who knows lift circle is away?

The pipe became governance because shared systems made individual choices touch strangers.

Qifrey proposed redundant magical isolation.

Senku added manual valves.

Coco wanted symbols.

Agott wanted text and symbols.

Native builder wanted shapes that remained readable under mud.

Maomao wanted no ambiguity between clean and waste access.

All right.

Mau looked at page.

"Are we building plumbing or constitution?"

Fern:

"Both, apparently."

No one laughed much.

At first test, they connected one public wash point.

Magic lifted water into header tank.

Gravity carried rest.

Simple where possible.

Spell where useful.

Manual shutoff.

Overflow channel.

Coco stood beside circle like proud parent.

Senku opened one valve.

Nothing.

Everyone froze.

Then Wakana:

"Wrong valve."

Senku looked.

Wakana was right.

Momo laughed for full minute.

Good.

Second attempt worked.

Water came.

Not dramatically.

Clear stream into basin.

A child immediately put both hands under it.

Maomao slapped hands away because basin had not been cleaned after installation.

Civilization.

The settlement began becoming a city through boring shared obligations.

No one person's water.

Everyone's responsibility.

That distinction would eventually matter as much as walls.
