# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 8 — Water Is Public

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 8
**Continuity state:** Hybrid civic-water redesign begins

The most political object in the settlement was a pipe.

Not even installed.

Question:

Where did public water responsibility end?

If every residence connected directly to one magical line, one bad modification risked everyone.

If each residence treated its own water, quality varied.

If everything stayed centralized, one failure became settlement-wide.

Senku drew loops.

Intake.

Treatment.

Distribution.

Qifrey added magical lift and isolation points.

Coco proposed visible maintenance marks.

Agott asked who could read them.

Good.

Wakana asked whether people could reach them while carrying tools.

Better.

Maomao asked whether clean and dirty service routes crossed.

Best.

Rimuru asked capacity at refuge load, not normal load.

The design changed again.

Mau asked:

"Who owns it?"

Rimuru:

"Nobody."

Senku:

"Then nobody maintains it."

Fern:

"Assign responsibility without private ownership."

There.

Maintenance shifts.

Inspection logs.

Public access.

No one person's water.

The settlement began becoming a city through boring shared obligations.
