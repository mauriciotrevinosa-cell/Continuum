## Chapter 14 — Exit Rights

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S3E31 — `Walls Without Walls`

The debate started with one gate.

Not a wall.

A checkpoint on the safest night route.

Someone proposed logging everyone who left after dark.

Reasonable.

Eren asked:

"Why?"

"For rescue."

Mikasa answered before anyone else.

"If someone doesn't return, we know where they went."

Eren nodded.

Then:

"Mandatory?"

Silence.

A refugee resident said:

"If it's optional, the people who need rescue most won't use it."

Eren looked at them.

"If it's mandatory, when does it stop being a rescue log and become permission to leave?"

Nobody accused him of being anti-safety.

That would have been easy.

They wrote limits instead.

No permission required to exit.

Route declaration strongly recommended during elevated alerts.

Children/non-independent minors followed guardian rules.

Certain emergency zones could close temporarily with review.

Records auto-expired unless attached to an active search or incident.

Mikasa supported every line.

Eren noticed.

Later he asked:

"Why do you agree with me?"

Mikasa looked at the road.

"Because I remember what fear can justify."

Eren did not know whether she meant him.

She did not clarify.

Some truths needed time.
