# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 10 — A Spell You Can Repair

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 10

Coco created a beautiful circle.

Qifrey rejected it.

"It works."

"Yes."

"Then why?"

"Because only you can repair the correction layer quickly."

Coco stopped.

Agott understood first.

"If Coco leaves, the infrastructure becomes dependent on Coco."

Mau, nearby, winced for reasons unrelated to magic.

Qifrey redrew.

Larger.

Less elegant.

Redundant symbols.

Physical labels around magical geometry.

Inspection access.

Manual shutoff.

Senku approved.

"Ugly."

Coco glared.

"Maintainable."

Better word.

Weeks later someone who had not designed the spell repaired a damaged section using the embedded instructions.

Coco watched.

Different pride.

Not:

Only I can do this.

But:

It works without me.

Mau understood.

This time the feeling hurt less.
