# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 10 — A Spell You Can Repair

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 10

Coco created a beautiful circle.

Qifrey rejected it.

That hurt more because it worked perfectly.

Water rose.

Separated.

Flow stabilized.

No visible flaw.

Coco stared.

"It works."

"Yes."

"Then why?"

Qifrey pointed to correction layer.

"If this segment is damaged?"

"I repair it."

"How quickly?"

"Very."

"Agott?"

Agott looked closer.

"Slower."

"Richeh?"

Richeh frowned.

"I'd need Coco's notes."

Qifrey looked at Coco.

"There."

Coco crossed arms.

"That's not failure."

"No."

"It's dependence."

Different.

Mau, nearby, winced for reasons unrelated to magic.

Senku noticed.

Said nothing.

Good.

Qifrey asked Coco to imagine:
she is away;
storm damages mark;
someone else reaches it first;
water has fifteen minutes before overflow.

Can they understand what matters?

Coco looked at circle.

Beautiful compact geometry.

Dense.

Elegant because she knew it.

Hostile because others didn't.

Agott said:

"You optimized for caster."

Coco:

"I optimized for space."

"Same result here."

Annoying.

Correct.

They redrew.

Larger.

Less elegant.

Redundant symbols.

Clear separation between functional layers.

Physical labels around magical geometry.

Inspection arrows.

A safe zone where non-mage maintenance could stand without disturbing active line.

Manual shutoff nearby.

Senku looked.

"Ugly."

Coco glared.

"Maintainable."

Qifrey:

"Better word."

Coco hated both.

They built mock panel.

Then deliberately hid Coco.

Aira volunteered as "unqualified civilian."

Nobody trusted that phrase.

Test person instead:
Wakana.

He knew tools.

Not spellwork.

Instructions:
do not repair magic.
identify fault.
shut down safely.
call correct person.

He succeeded.

Then Richeh attempted actual repair using notes.

Slow.

But correct.

Agott verified.

Qifrey did not intervene.

Coco hovered so visibly everyone told her to leave room.

She left.

Listened outside.

Richeh emerged.

"Done."

Coco rushed in.

Circle worked.

No catastrophe.

Different pride arrived.

Not:

Only I can do this.

But:

It works without me.

Mau stood beside her.

"Feels weird?"

Coco looked.

"Yes."

He nodded.

"I know."

She knew what he meant.

Memory Arc.

Usefulness.

Necessary versus wanted.

Coco looked back at circle.

"It being repairable by someone else doesn't make my design less mine."

Mau smiled faintly.

"No."

"You need to hear that too."

Mau stared.

Betrayed.

Coco walked away before he could reply.

Weeks later, a real maintenance fault happened.

Nothing dramatic.

One line scraped during material move.

The first person there was not Coco.

They closed water section.

Read instructions.

Called Richeh and Agott.

Repair complete before Coco even returned from old inn.

She heard afterward.

For half second:
left out.

Then:
system worked.

The city had learned one of its most important magical rules.

A civic spell was not good because a brilliant person could cast it.

It was good because ordinary future people could understand how not to die when the brilliant person was somewhere else.
