# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 15 — The Enemy of One Person

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E32 — `At the Door`

The man at Arrival House had a knife wound.

That was ordinary.

The person who recognized him was not.

A resident stepped into the tent.

Stopped.

"You."

The injured man went pale.

Everyone else became still.

They were from the same source world.

Not friends.

Not neutral.

The resident called him a collaborator.

The injured man did not deny it.

That made everything worse.

"He helped them find people."

"I carried messages."

"You knew what the messages did."

"Yes."

No false accusation.

No convenient innocence.

Real harm.

The injured man had also arrived two days earlier in territory where another armed group was hunting him for reasons nobody yet understood.

He asked for one night.

The resident said:

"No."

Arrival House had finally reached the question architecture warned about:

Enemy of one resident.

Enemy of settlement?

Not automatically.

But not irrelevant either.
