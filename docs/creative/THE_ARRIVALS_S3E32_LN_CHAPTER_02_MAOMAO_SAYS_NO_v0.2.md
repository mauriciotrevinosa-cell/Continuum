# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 11 — Maomao Says No

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 11

The bath design was beautiful.

Warm water.

Heat reuse.

Privacy.

Wakana already planned textiles.

Marin had opinions.

Maomao walked through the layout.

"No."

Collective pain.

"Why?"

"Steam."

Ventilation insufficient.

Condensation near wood.

Medical wash point too close.

One basin drained into wrong treatment stage.

Senku looked delighted.

Maomao looked annoyed by delight.

"We fix those."

"Obviously."

Momo whispered:

"They flirt by disagreeing."

Both turned.

"No."

Momo grinned.

The bath changed.

Less pretty on paper.

Better in air.

When it opened later, nobody praised ventilation.

They praised hot water.

Infrastructure succeeded when comfort hid argument.
