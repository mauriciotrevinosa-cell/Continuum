# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 16 — Sanctuary Is Not Forgiveness

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S3E32 — `At the Door`

The hearing lasted two hours.

Nobody called it a trial.

The harmed resident spoke first.

The injured man spoke second.

Anko wrote.

Mikasa watched.

Rimuru asked questions.

Mau did not chair.

That mattered.

The facts they could establish were ugly.

The man had participated.

Under pressure.

Not total coercion.

Not total freedom.

People were harmed.

He had later helped two others escape.

Neither fact erased the other.

The question was not whether he deserved forgiveness.

The harmed resident did not forgive him.

Nobody asked them to.

The question was whether the settlement would hand him to people likely to execute him without process.

No.

That answer also did not make him trusted.

He received:
- temporary sanctuary;
- medical care;
- movement limits around sensitive areas;
- no access to records;
- a review after more information arrived.

The harmed resident left the meeting furious.

Still protected by the same rules.

Mau found them outside later.

Did not say the decision was right.

Asked:

"Do you want someone with you?"

They said no.

Mau left.

Sanctuary had protected two people who hated each other.

That was the point.
