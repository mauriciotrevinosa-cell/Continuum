# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 17 — The Meeting That Became a Council

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S3E33 — `Names and Authority`

They had too many meetings.

That was the first sign governance existed.

Water meeting.

Food meeting.

Defense meeting.

Arrival House review.

Searcher route review.

Medical capacity.

Construction.

Sanctuary case.

Emergency authority.

Everyone important could not attend everything.

That was the second sign.

So they created a coordinating table.

Rotating.

Representatives by function, not permanent nobility.

Infrastructure.

Medical.

Arrival House/refuge.

Searchers.

Defense.

Food/logistics.

Resident concerns.

At-large seats that rotated.

Rimuru coordinated early because he already connected systems.

Mau was included.

Not as king.

Not as owner.

One voice with unusual cross-group trust.

He hated how many people looked at him before speaking.

Frieren noticed.

After the meeting:

"You don't have to answer first."

"I know."

"You keep answering first."

Mau looked at her.

She raised an eyebrow.

He sighed.

Next meeting, someone asked a hard question.

Mau waited.

It took twelve uncomfortable seconds.

Then Fern answered.

The world continued.
