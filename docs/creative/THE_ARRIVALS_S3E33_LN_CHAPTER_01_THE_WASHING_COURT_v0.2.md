# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 14 — The Washing Court

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 14

The first place that looked distinctly finished was a public washing court.

Mau found this anticlimactic.

Then he watched people use it.

That changed his mind.

It sat between housing and workshop routes without belonging fully to either.

Magic lifted clean water into elevated channel.

Gravity handled most movement after that.

Mechanical overflow protected against spell failure.

Drainage split before rejoining treatment.

Hooks.

Benches.

Privacy cloth.

Maintenance access.

Visible safety markings.

One tree deliberately preserved because shade mattered more than symmetry.

Nothing heroic.

People kept using it.

That made it important.

First morning, someone hung too many towels on one rail.

It bent.

Wakana replaced it.

"Why this height?"

Mau asked.

"Because whoever chose the first one was tall."

Mau looked at Rimuru.

Rimuru:

"Not me."

Milim looked away.

Everyone stared.

Milim:

"What?"

New rail lower.

Children immediately splashed in a channel clearly not intended for play.

Maomao corrected them.

Five minutes later they returned.

Maomao corrected again.

Senku:

"Behavioral persistence."

Maomao:

"Children."

Same data.

Coco painted tiny decorative symbol near one maintenance cover.

Agott pretended not to see.

Then later corrected one line so it would weather better.

Coco noticed.

Did not comment.

Rimuru measured peak use.

Morning overloaded one basin.

So they changed flow.

Not built once.

Adjusted.

A resident with limited mobility found one edge too high.

Native builder modified step.

No council meeting.

No pride.

Obvious once seen.

Mau sat nearby one afternoon with Frieren.

People washed:
clothes;
tools;
hands;
vegetables in designated area;
one object nobody could identify.

Mau watched magical glyph brighten faintly when tank level changed.

"This looks like nowhere."

Frieren:

"Yes."

"You like that?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

Frieren watched Coco explain a mark to a child.

Wakana carrying repaired rail.

A native woman showing Momo how to wring fabric without wasting water.

Senku checking one gauge while Maomao told him not to put dirty fingers near clean side.

"It belongs here."

Mau looked again.

Not Earth.

Not Tempest.

Not Witch Hat.

Not generic fantasy city with magical faucet.

Here.

That mattered.

The court became meeting place accidentally.

People waited for clothes.

Talked.

Exchanged news.

Someone started leaving lost items on one hook.

Then another person made a tiny sign.

LOST / FOUND.

Fern objected to informal system.

Then used it.

Bocchi once practiced nearby because water noise hid mistakes.

Someone from second community heard and sat.

No event.

Culture.

At night, low magical maintenance marks remained visible enough for repair crew.

No bright streets copied from modern city.

No torch-only fantasy.

Something else.

The first civic space looked like the world becoming its own place.

And Mau finally understood why the washing court felt more finished than the common room.

People had stopped asking how to use it.

They had begun assuming it was theirs.
