# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 14 — Culture Happens While No One Is Looking

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 14

Nobody scheduled culture.

It happened.

Food changed because ingredients crossed kitchens.

Slang crossed source worlds.

Children learned words without knowing origin.

Wakana copied a useful fastening method from native clothes.

A native carpenter adopted a magical maintenance symbol because it saved time.

Bocchi played with someone from the second community.

Different tuning.

They adjusted.

Richeh and Coco argued over a decorative motif that later appeared on three unrelated doors.

Milim invented a game by misunderstanding another game.

It spread.

Mau heard someone use a phrase he remembered from Momo.

The speaker had never met Momo.

That frightened him for half a second.

Then he laughed.

Not memory corruption.

Culture.

Memory could move socially without becoming his.

After Memory Arc, that distinction mattered.

Identity was not only what survived from before.

It was what people repeated together now.
