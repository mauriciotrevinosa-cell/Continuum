# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 18 — Mau's Settlement

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Source episode:** S3E33 — `Names and Authority`

The phrase came from outside.

A trader.

"Mau's settlement."

Mau corrected him.

"No."

The trader laughed.

"Whatever you call it."

"We don't."

"What?"

"We don't have a name."

The trader looked toward the buildings.

Temporary and permanent mixed together.

Water court.

Storage.

Tents.

Shelters.

Arrival House still partly canvas.

People everywhere.

"Then everyone else will name it for you."

That was true.

Other versions already existed.

Otherworlder camp.

Refuge.

Fort.

Rimuru's place.

The monster town.

The witch settlement.

Mau's settlement.

Each name carried a story.

Some flattering.

Some dangerous.

The coordinating table discussed choosing an official name.

No agreement.

Momo suggested six terrible ones.

Umaru suggested one worse.

Senku refused to participate.

Milim suggested naming it after herself.

Diablo suggested Rimuru.

Rimuru ended the session.

No name.

Outside, rumor kept using Mau's.

That changed something.

New arrivals began looking at him differently.

Not everyone.

Enough.

Mau heard the phrase again a week later.

This time followed by:

"If something goes wrong there, Mau handles it."

Frieren heard too.

She looked at him.

Mau made a joke.

Frieren did not laugh.

Volume Fourteen ended with the town still unnamed and one man's name beginning to stick to problems larger than him.
