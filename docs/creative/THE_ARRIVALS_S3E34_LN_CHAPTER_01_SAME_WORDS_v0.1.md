# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 1 — Same Words

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E34 — `The Pattern`

The first phrase appeared in a registration order two valleys west.

`UNCONTROLLED CONCENTRATION OF FOREIGN CAPABILITY.`

Anko copied it.

Weeks later, a Searcher returned with a notice from a different region.

Different seal.

Different official.

Same phrase.

Not similar.

Same.

`UNCONTROLLED CONCENTRATION OF FOREIGN CAPABILITY.`

Nano compared letter spacing.

Not useful.

Then syntax.

Useful.

A third notice arrived.

Same phrase.

This one called the settlement a `destabilizing foreign enclave.`

The second had too.

Anko laid all three on the table.

Rimuru looked at the dates.

"Could be copying."

"From what?"

Nobody knew.

The orders claimed different local sources.

One cited a magistrate.

One cited an advisory office that did not appear to exist on any map they had.

One cited "regional security guidance."

No author.

Same words.

Mau stared.

"So somebody's writing fear efficiently."

Anko looked at him.

"Or distributing templates."

"That's worse."

"Probably."

They did not write `mastermind` on the board.

They wrote:

`COMMON SOURCE LIKELY.`

Evidence first.

Names later.
