# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 2 — Same Map

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S3E34 — `The Pattern`

The map was worse.

Two captured notices from distant roads marked dangerous Otherworlder zones.

Both maps contained the same mistake.

A river bend drawn too far east.

Senku saw it first.

"Copy."

Rimuru looked.

"Could be common source cartography."

Senku shook his head.

"The error isn't natural."

He explained.

A surveyor could miss a bend.

Two surveyors could make different mistakes.

This one preserved the same false angle and the same missing tributary.

Someone copied someone.

The maps also marked the settlement.

Not by official name.

They still had none.

One label:

`MAU ENCLAVE.`

Mau stopped reading.

Momo said:

"That sounds terrible."

"Thank you."

"It sounds like you're a tax problem."

"Better."

Frieren took the map.

The humor died.

The marked radius included the other camp too.

People who had deliberately stayed small.

Avoided concentration.

Avoided association.

They were being pulled into the same story anyway.

The fear system was not merely reacting.

It was spreading a category.
