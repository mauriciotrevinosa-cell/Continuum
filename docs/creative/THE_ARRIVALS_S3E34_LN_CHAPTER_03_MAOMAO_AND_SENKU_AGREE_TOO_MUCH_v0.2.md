# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 18 — Maomao and Senku Agree Too Much

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 18

Maomao and Senku agreed on:
- clean storage;
- measurement;
- controlled contamination;
- labeling;
- testing;
- people being idiots around unlabeled substances.

They disagreed on:
- acceptable taste;
- whether curiosity justified touching unknown compounds;
- whether Senku's optimism counted as a symptom.

Momo watched them argue.

"This is definitely flirting."

"No."

Again.

The useful result was a preservation room.

Cooler.

Drier.

Measured.

Not modern refrigeration.

Better storage.

Food lasted longer.

Medicinal stock stopped sitting beside ordinary ingredients.

Arrival House gained safer emergency reserves.

Nothing looked revolutionary.

Three weeks later, waste dropped.

That was revolution at settlement scale.

Senku wrote the numbers.

Maomao said:

"Obviously."

Senku liked her slightly less.

Respectfully.
