Outside hostility closed the roads for one day.

Not officially.

Practically.

Three checkpoints.

One missing merchant.

Bad weather.

Searchers recalled.

No nonessential travel.

For the first time in months, almost everyone was home.

At the new settlement.

That phrase still felt strange.

Home.

The old inn remained protected and visited.

But breakfast happened here now more often than there.

The result of everyone being home was immediate disaster.

Too many cooks.

Too many opinions.

Not enough bathrooms despite all the planning.

Momo accused infrastructure of betrayal.

Senku said:

"Usage exceeded design assumptions."

"Same thing."

Milim wanted to train.

Mikasa said no near housing.

Milim asked where.

Mikasa pointed very far away.

Diablo tried to serve Rimuru.

Rimuru made him sit.

Richeh and Coco occupied a table with enough materials to endanger lunch.

Bocchi tuned a guitar.

Kita listened.

Umaru claimed the best corner.

Aira challenged the claim.

No emergency briefing.

For four hours, nobody discussed the pattern board.

The refuge sounded alive.

That was strategic too.
