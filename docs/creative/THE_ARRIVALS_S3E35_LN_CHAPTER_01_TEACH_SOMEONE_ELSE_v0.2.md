# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 19 — Teach Someone Else

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 19

The maintenance rule became:

If only one person knows, it is not finished.

Qifrey hated how effective the sentence was.

Senku loved it.

Every major system needed:
- primary maintainer;
- secondary maintainer;
- written/visual guide;
- manual shutdown;
- known failure signs.

Coco taught two people her water symbols.

Agott taught one person inspection order.

Senku trained native builders in measurement standards.

Native builders trained Senku in local materials.

Maomao taught sanitation checks.

Wakana taught repair of protective cloth structures.

Rimuru documented capacity assumptions.

Mau was assigned nothing unique.

He noticed.

Then understood why.

A city should not become helpless if one resident disappeared.

Memory Arc had nearly done that socially.

Infrastructure would not repeat it physically.

Mau helped write guides.

That role felt different from being indispensable.

Better.
