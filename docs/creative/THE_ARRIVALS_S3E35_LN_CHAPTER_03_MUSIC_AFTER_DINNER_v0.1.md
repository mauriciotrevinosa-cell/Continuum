## Chapter 6 — Music After Dinner

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Source episode:** S3E35 — `Everyone Home`

Music started because Bocchi was already holding the guitar.

No performance announced.

No stage.

She played.

Someone from the second community joined with a local string instrument.

The tuning did not match.

They adjusted.

A child started clapping wrong.

Milim clapped louder.

That fixed nothing.

Kita sang softly at first.

Then less softly.

Momo knew part of something from another world and none of the words correctly.

Umaru objected.

Then joined.

Mau sat near Frieren.

Did not play.

Did not lead.

Did not create the moment.

That mattered.

Culture happened without him.

Frieren leaned against him.

"Do you remember this?"

Mau looked at her.

Memory Arc reflex.

Then smiled.

"It's happening."

Frieren smiled back.

Across the room Ori heard.

Did not interrupt.

New language had become family habit.
