# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 23 — Not One Reason

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 23  
**Continuity state:** G5 no longer behaves as one cohort | Ring Two develops direct reasons to stay close

The question came back.

Not from G5.

From someone newer.

A man at Arrival House watched Mau carry two boxes into the common storage and asked Seiko:

"Who is he?"

Seiko looked at Mau.

Then at the man.

"Which answer do you want?"

The man frowned.

"The normal one."

"Good luck."

Mau, overhearing:

"Thank you."

Seiko waved him away.

The man had arrived three days earlier.

He knew the rumors.

Mau's settlement.
Worldless man.
Founder.
Leader.
Monster.
Refuge builder.

None helped.

"He owns this place?"

"No."

"He runs it?"

"Sometimes he runs into things."

Mau:

"Seiko."

She ignored him.

The question should have been easy.

It had not been easy when G5 arrived either.

Who is Mau?

At first, the answer had mostly been other people's faces.

Frieren not sleeping.

Bocchi trying not to panic.

Ori holding herself together.

Rimuru pretending logistics could protect him from fear.

Yuta moving like an older brother who had already lost too much once.

People asking for Mau out of habit and remembering halfway through that he was gone.

That had told G5 something.

Not enough.

Now months of ordinary life had added different answers.

Mai's answer was still not sentimental.

She was sitting near Maki, repairing a strap badly enough that Wakana kept looking over from across the room.

Maki finally took it from her.

Mai:

"I was doing it."

"No."

"Asshole."

Maki fixed it.

Mai looked toward Mau.

"He doesn't treat her like she needs permission to be angry."

Mau stopped.

Mai looked annoyed.

"Don't make it a thing."

"I wasn't."

"You were about to."

Fair.

That was her answer.

Not:
Mau saved me.

Not:
Mau is my brother.

He was one part of a place where Maki had become more than what the Zenin clan had decided she was.

Nijika's answer came later.

The same Arrival House resident asked her after hearing Kessoku play.

"Is Mau your manager?"

Nijika laughed so hard she had to sit down.

"No."

"What does he do with the band?"

Ryo, from the floor:

"Gets in the way aesthetically."

Bocchi made a betrayed sound.

Mau, nearby:

"I don't even know what that means."

Ryo:

"Exactly."

Nijika smiled.

Then looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi had once struggled to ask for a room.

Now she could say:
too loud.
not there.
I want this one.

Still Bocchi.

Still difficult.

But possible.

Nijika's expression changed.

"He made it easier for her to need things."

Mau heard.

Looked away.

Not because he disagreed.

Because he did not know what to do with the sentence.

Ryo added:

"And he gives good emergency snacks."

Nijika:

"That too."

Richeh's answer was different.

Mau had not restored her old world.

He had not replaced Qifrey.

He had not become the center of Atelier life.

Good.

Her reason was smaller.

When she asked a question that sounded foolish, Mau usually answered as if the useful part mattered more than the embarrassment.

Tetia's reason was that Mau laughed at things he did not understand instead of pretending he did.

Agott's reason, when cornered into answering, was:

"He is less stupid than he looks."

Mau:

"That is not affection."

Agott:

"I didn't say it was."

Coco looked scandalized.

Everyone else understood that Agott had practically written a love letter.

Jinshi's answer was Maomao.

Not possession.

Observation.

Maomao still chose silence.
Still disappeared into work.
Still rejected nonsense.

And somehow this house knew how to drag her into dinner without demanding she become a different woman first.

Jinshi had spent enough of his life around people who wanted Maomao to perform a role.

Here, Mau sometimes asked her medical opinion.

Sometimes stole food from her plate.

Sometimes got told to shut up.

There was no hierarchy in which proximity to Mau made Maomao less herself.

Jinshi found that difficult to name.

So he did not.

Nazuna's answer happened at noon.

Of course she was asleep.

The Arrival House man had given up asking.

Anko had not.

She kicked the leg of Nazuna's chair.

Nazuna opened one eye.

"What."

"Who is Mau?"

Nazuna closed her eye again.

"Warm."

Mau:

"What?"

Anko burst out laughing.

Nazuna, still asleep:

"The house. Idiot."

"That did not clarify anything."

"Not my problem."

Anko looked at Mau.

"You heard the vampire."

"I wish I hadn't."

Seiko's answer was the least subtle.

She watched Mau forget lunch while helping with a route board.

Walked over.

Hit the top of his head with folded paper.

"Eat."

"I was going to."

"No."

"I'm an adult."

"Debatable."

Frieren, nearby:

"Very."

Mau looked between them.

"This alliance is dangerous."

Seiko shoved food at him.

The Arrival House man watched.

"You treat him like your grandson."

Seiko paused.

Mau paused harder.

The room got very interested.

Seiko looked at the man.

Then at Mau.

"He's got enough problems."

That was not a denial.

Mau decided survival required silence.

Milim's answer was easier.

"He's Rimuru's brother."

Rimuru looked up.

Mau did too.

Milim continued eating.

Neither corrected her.

That was new.

Suika's answer:

"Mau helps when Senku is being impossible."

Senku, from across the room:

"I heard that."

"Then be less impossible!"

Umaru cheered.

Coco joined.

Senku looked at Mau.

"This is your fault."

Mau:

"How?"

"Social contagion."

"That's not how—"

Senku walked away.

Probably joking.

Probably.

Vamola's answer was a sequence of people.

Momo.
Okarun.
Aira.
Seiko.
Then Mau.

Not center.

Part of one shape.

Turbo Granny refused the question.

"Nosy bastard."

Nobody knew whether she meant the resident or Mau.

Maybe both.

Kusuri's answer took longer.

She had the 100 Girlfriends group.
Maomao.
Senku.
Her lab arguments.
Her own reasons to stay.

Mau was not required to make those things real.

That mattered.

When she finally answered, she said:

"He doesn't make being close to him the price of belonging here."

Mau went very still.

The Arrival House man looked at all of them.

"So there's no one reason."

Seiko:

"Congratulations."

"Then why does everyone talk about him?"

That was harder.

Mau started to answer.

Anko beat him.

"Because stories like one name."

The man waited.

Anko continued.

"Families don't."

Silence.

Then Ryo:

"That was disgustingly profound."

Anko threw a cushion at her.

The room broke.

Good.

Later, Mau found Seiko outside.

"Grandson?"

She looked at him.

"You survived."

"That is not an answer."

"It is the only one you're getting."

Mau smiled.

G5 had arrived asking:

Who is Mau?

By now most of them had stopped asking.

Not because they had discovered one hidden truth.

Because each had accumulated a different answer.

And none of those answers required everyone else to have the same one.
