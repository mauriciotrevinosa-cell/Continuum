# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 21 — Senku Gets Faster

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 21

Senku's test times improved.

Slowly.

Not magically.

The gap narrowed.

Working memory under local-system load improved most.

Reaction to mixed magic/engineering problems improved less.

He updated his notebook.

Mau looked.

"Happy?"

"No."

"You're impossible."

"Accuracy is not happiness."

The reduced cognition remained.

So did adaptation.

Senku had already changed behavior because of it.

More delegation.

More written process.

More peer checking.

He hated needing those.

The settlement benefited.

Mau pointed that out.

Senku looked offended.

"Don't romanticize impairment."

Mau stopped.

Correct.

"Adaptation can produce useful habits without making the limitation good."

Mau nodded.

That sentence applied to too many people here.

No one said it.
