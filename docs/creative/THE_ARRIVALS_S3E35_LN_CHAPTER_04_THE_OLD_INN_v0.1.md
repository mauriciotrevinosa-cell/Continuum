## Chapter 7 — The Old Inn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Source episode:** S3E35 — `Everyone Home`

They visited the old inn the next morning.

Not everyone.

Enough.

The road felt shorter now.

Maybe because they no longer traveled it daily.

Dust had changed.

Grass had grown near one wheel mark.

The door stuck.

Stark complained.

Then fixed it.

Inside, rooms looked too small.

Mau hated that.

The common room table remained.

Not the same arrangement.

The future map had moved to the new settlement.

The mug had not.

Someone had left it.

Mau found it in the cupboard.

He put it on the old table.

Frieren saw.

Said nothing.

They cleaned.

Repaired a roof section.

Covered books.

Marked what needed preservation.

No museum plaque.

No ceremonial rope.

Just maintenance.

A newcomer asked:

"Why keep this?"

Bocchi answered before Mau.

"This is where we became us."

Simple.

Nobody improved it.

The old inn did not need to remain inhabited to remain home.
