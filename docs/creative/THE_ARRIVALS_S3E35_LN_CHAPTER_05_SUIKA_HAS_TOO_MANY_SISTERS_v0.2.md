# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 27 — Suika Has Too Many Sisters

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 27  
**Same time as:** V16 #8–#23 (an ordinary day inside the redesign weeks)  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary-life interlude | Suika branches beyond Senku without leaving him behind

Suika had a problem.

Too many sisters.

None official.

That made it worse.

Umaru found her first.

"Come on."

Suika looked at Senku.

Senku did not look up from the measurement sheet.

"Why are you looking at me?"

"Suika is helping."

"You're done with the north markers."

"There's more work."

"There's always more work."

Umaru pointed at him.

"See? This is why we're taking her."

Senku finally looked up.

"Taking her where?"

"Important sister business."

Coco appeared behind Umaru.

"Extremely important."

Milim appeared behind Coco.

"FOOD."

That clarified nothing.

Suika looked between them.

Then at Senku.

Old habit.

Not obedience exactly.

Reference point.

Senku saw it.

"Do you want to go?"

Suika hesitated.

Then nodded.

"Yeah."

"Then go."

Simple.

No:
I don't need you.

No:
be independent.

Just:
your choice exists.

Umaru grabbed one hand.

Coco grabbed the other.

Milim attempted to lift all three.

Fern stopped that from across the courtyard with one word.

"Milim."

Milim lowered them.

"Fine."

The sister business turned out to be lunch near the washing court because Marin had declared the main common room "emotionally overbooked."

Nobody understood.

Everyone obeyed.

Bocchi was already there with Kita.

Shizuka read quietly near the wall.

Ori had brought paper.

Vamola had brought something edible.

Probably.

Momo inspected it.

Declared it food enough.

Suika sat in the middle.

This was a mistake.

Questions came from every direction.

What did she like?
Had she tried this?
Did she want to see the music room?
Did she know how to play?
Could she swim?
Why was Senku so annoying?
Did she want ribbons?

"One at a time," Fern said.

She had somehow become supervisor without agreeing to attend.

Suika laughed.

Nobody had asked her to explain Dr. Stone.

Good.

They knew Senku.

They wanted Suika.

Different.

Coco showed her a spell diagram.

Not to teach magic.

Because it looked like a flower.

Umaru explained a game.

Suika understood half.

Milim made up the other half.

Rules collapsed.

Bocchi said almost nothing for twenty minutes.

Then Suika asked about the guitar.

Bocchi froze.

Kita waited.

Bocchi managed:

"I can... show you."

Suika moved closer.

Bocchi demonstrated one string.

Badly, because explaining while observed remained Bocchi's natural predator.

Suika copied.

Sound awful.

Both laughed.

Across the courtyard, Senku passed carrying tools.

He slowed.

Suika saw.

Waved.

Senku lifted two fingers.

Kept walking.

Umaru watched him.

"He wants to come over."

"No he doesn't," Suika said.

"He does."

"Senku likes working."

"Those are not opposites."

Coco nodded seriously.

Suika considered.

Later, the lunch became a walk.

The walk became looking at future rooms.

Not assignments.

Ideas.

Umaru pointed at one.

"I want mine there."

Coco:

"Mine near that."

Ori:

"I already chose."

Bocchi:

"Mine has buffer wall."

Suika looked at the rough plan.

"Where does Senku go?"

Everyone paused.

Good question.

Senku had spoken endlessly about lab/work areas.

Almost never about bedroom.

Momo:

"Probably sleeps in the workshop if we let him."

Fern:

"We will not."

Suika smiled.

"Then Suika wants somewhere he can find."

Umaru misunderstood.

"Next to him?"

Suika thought.

"No."

Then:

"Close enough."

That difference mattered.

Umaru understood that one immediately.

"With me," she said. "He walks past my door every day to get to the atelier. He'll always know where you are without asking anybody."

Suika looked at her for a long moment.

"You'd share?"

"I've never been the big sister," said Umaru, very casually, the way she said things that mattered. "I want to try it."

Coco nodded so hard she nearly fell over.

Suika did not need a room of her own. She needed one Senku could find without asking.

Umaru's was both.

When they returned, Senku was exactly where they had left him.

Almost.

The measurements had moved.

Two boards replaced.

One problem solved.

Suika dropped a small wrapped snack beside his hand.

Senku looked.

"What is it?"

"Sister business."

"That doesn't answer the question."

"Good."

Mau, passing, nearly walked into a post because he was laughing.

Senku pointed at him.

"This is your culture."

Mau:

"I deny jurisdiction."

Senku unwrapped the snack.

Ate it.

Suika smiled.

"I'm moving in with Umaru," she said.

Senku did not look up from his notes for almost ten seconds.

"Go," he said.

Suika went to get her things from the cot beside his bench, where she had slept every night since the Move because she had not wanted to choose a room without him.

As she reached the door, still without looking up, Senku said:

"Take the good blanket."

It was the closest Senku ever came to saying anything.

Umaru carried the cot herself.

That evening, Senku appeared at dinner before anyone called him.

Not early.

Not late.

Just there.

Suika was sitting between Umaru and Coco.

There was one open place across from them.

Senku took it.

No announcement.

Milim immediately asked him whether science could make food explode safely.

"No."

Kusuri:

"Depends what you mean by—"

Maomao:

"No."

Senku closed his eyes.

Mau sat farther down the table.

Watched.

Did not make it meaningful out loud.

He was learning.

Suika had arrived attached closely to one person.

The family had not cut that attachment to prove growth.

It had simply given her more directions to move.

And, because relationships were unfair like that, Senku had acquired those directions too.
