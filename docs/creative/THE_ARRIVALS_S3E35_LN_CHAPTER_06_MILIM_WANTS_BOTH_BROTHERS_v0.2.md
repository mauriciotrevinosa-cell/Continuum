# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 24 — Milim Wants Both Brothers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 24  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary-life day | Milim's chaotic younger-sister bond expands around Mau and Rimuru

Milim wanted both brothers.

This was how she phrased it.

Rimuru dropped his spoon.

Mau choked.

Milim looked between them.

"What?"

Rimuru:

"Don't say it like that."

"Like what?"

Mau was still coughing.

Umaru patted his back with no useful force.

Coco patted too.

Now he was being assaulted rhythmically.

Frieren kept eating.

"He's fine."

Mau raised one finger.

Correct.

Milim pointed at Rimuru.

"You."

Then Mau.

"You."

Then herself.

"Today."

Rimuru narrowed his eyes.

"What are we doing?"

"Nothing."

Everyone at the table stopped.

Marin looked proud.

"She's learning."

Milim slammed both hands down.

"NO WORK."

Marin's pride doubled.

Apparently the anti-productivity movement had recruited a Demon Lord.

Rimuru looked at Mau.

Mau looked at Rimuru.

Both knew resistance was useless.

The day began near the water.

Not the old lake.

A smaller usable stretch near the new settlement where nobody had historical expectations.

That helped.

Milim brought food.

Too much.

Umaru brought games.

Coco brought paper.

Suika brought something she wanted to show everyone.

Momo and Okarun came because Milim had shouted the invitation across half the residential cluster.

Aira came because Momo did.

Seiko came because someone needed to prevent all of them from dying of stupidity.

Frieren came twenty minutes later carrying a book.

Mau looked at her.

"I thought this was brother day."

Frieren sat under a tree.

"Then ignore me."

Impossible.

Rimuru changed to slime form the moment the sun became annoying.

Milim grabbed him.

"Hey!"

"Portable Rimuru."

"I am not portable furniture."

Umaru:

"Debatable."

Mau sat in the grass.

Milim dropped Rimuru into his lap.

Mau looked down.

Rimuru looked up.

"This is humiliating."

"You can transform."

"I am making a point."

"What point?"

Rimuru did not know.

Milim laughed.

Then she dragged Mau into a game with rules she had altered since breakfast.

Mau lost.

Rimuru lost.

Suika won.

Nobody understood how.

Senku arrived halfway through, allegedly only to retrieve Suika.

Stayed because Suika was winning.

Coco celebrated every round regardless of result.

Aira accused the scoring system of corruption.

Milim agreed even though she had invented it.

At one point Mau lay back in the grass.

No medical reason.

No collapse.

Just tired.

Frieren looked over from her book.

He lifted a hand.

Fine.

She returned to reading.

Milim dropped down beside him.

Rimuru, human again, sat on the other side.

For almost a minute nobody spoke.

Then Milim asked:

"Are you brothers?"

Mau turned his head.

Rimuru looked at the sky.

"We're not related."

Milim:

"That's not what I asked."

Rimuru looked at Mau.

Mau looked back.

There had never been a ceremony.

No declaration.

Rimuru had been there for:
the beginning;
the house;
Sukuna;
death;
return;
Memory;
containment;
the new settlement;
every terrible plan Mau thought was reasonable until someone stopped him.

Mau had been there while Rimuru learned how different Continuum felt without Tempest underneath him.

Brother was not inaccurate.

Mau said:

"Yeah."

Rimuru's face changed.

Tiny.

Then:

"Yeah."

Milim beamed.

"GOOD."

She stood.

Announcement complete.

Mau sat up.

"Was this entire day just to make us say that?"

Milim thought.

"No."

Pause.

"Maybe."

Rimuru covered his face.

Later, Milim joined Umaru, Coco and Suika in an argument about snacks.

Mau watched.

"You know she's getting adopted by the sister group."

Rimuru:

"I know."

"You sound worried."

"I know Milim."

"Fair."

From across the grass:

"MAU!"

He looked.

Milim held up something dangerous-looking.

"No."

"I didn't ask yet!"

"No."

Umaru:

"Let her explain."

"No."

Coco:

"It might be safe."

"That made it worse."

Rimuru started laughing.

Mau looked at him.

Rimuru tried to stop.

Failed.

That was the part Diablo noticed from farther up the path.

Rimuru laughed more here.

Not because his old life had been empty.

Because this life asked different pieces of him to exist at once.

Leader sometimes.

Slime pillow sometimes.

Brother.

Friend.

Person being kidnapped by Milim.

Good.

At sunset, Milim walked between Mau and Rimuru with one arm hooked through each of theirs.

No one called it childish.

She was older and stranger than half the assumptions people made about age.

The relationship still felt younger-sister-shaped.

Chosen.

Chaotic.

Loud.

Milim looked at Mau.

"You better not disappear again."

Mau's smile weakened.

Rimuru noticed.

Milim did too.

She squeezed both arms.

Not apology.

Not delicate.

"Seriously."

Mau nodded.

"I'll try."

Milim:

"Bad answer."

Rimuru:

"She's right."

Mau sighed.

Family was exhausting.

They kept ruining all his evasions.
