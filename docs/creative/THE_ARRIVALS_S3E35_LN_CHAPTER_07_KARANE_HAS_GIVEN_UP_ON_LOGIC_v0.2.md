# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 28 — Karane Has Given Up on Logic

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 28  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary-life ensemble | 100 Girlfriends-side group remains visible as family texture rather than cameo cast

Karane had given up on logic before Continuum.

This gave her an advantage.

Nano had not given up.

She had adapted.

Different.

The argument began because Hakari wanted curtains.

Not curtains generally.

Specific curtains.

For the shared sitting room near their rooms.

Karane objected to the pattern.

Shizuka objected by silently showing everyone an illustration in her book that looked exactly like the pattern.

Kusuri wanted fabric that could survive chemical accidents.

Everyone:

"No."

Kusuri:

"I didn't say I was planning one."

Maomao, from the hallway:

"That is not reassuring."

Mau had made the mistake of walking past.

Hakari saw him.

"Mau!"

He stopped.

Old survival instinct:
run.

Too late.

"Which one?"

Two fabric samples appeared in front of him.

Mau looked.

"They're both... fabric."

Karane:

"USELESS."

"Correct."

Hakari:

"Frieren would have an opinion."

Mau:

"Frieren claims not to have opinions about rooms."

Everyone stared.

Mau:

"I know. I know."

Nano held up a third sample.

"Decision can be deferred until the room receives final light conditions."

Karane pointed.

"See? This is why I keep her."

Nano:

"You do not keep me."

"That was a joke!"

"Statistically likely."

Shizuka smiled behind her book.

Mau tried to leave.

Kusuri blocked him accidentally while entering with a box.

"Hey, Mau."

"Hi."

"Do you know where Senku is?"

"No."

"Maomao?"

"Behind you."

Kusuri turned.

Maomao stared.

"Oh."

Mau tried again.

Hakari:

"Wait."

He closed his eyes.

"What?"

"Sit."

"Why?"

"Because we're arguing."

"That is usually a reason to leave."

Karane:

"He's learning."

He sat.

The room became ordinary.

That was the thing that had gone missing when these four first arrived.

They had source-world chemistry so strong it could consume every scene.

Easy.

Hakari teased.
Karane exploded.
Shizuka softened.
Nano analyzed.

The trick was letting that chemistry exist inside the larger household instead of in a sealed franchise bubble.

Momo entered looking for string.

Joined the curtain argument.

Marin arrived because she sensed aesthetics from two buildings away.

Disaster.

Wakana followed because Marin had taken his measuring tape.

Fern arrived because too many people were now discussing attaching things to an unfinished wall.

Rimuru appeared because Fern said structural.

By the time Frieren entered, the original question had become:
curtain,
window angle,
future wall color,
whether the sitting room needed another shelf,
and why Milim was under the table.

Milim:

"Secret."

No one had asked.

Frieren looked at the fabric.

Pointed at one.

"That."

Hakari smiled triumphantly.

Karane:

"WHY?"

"Less ugly."

Argument over.

Mau stared.

"You denied caring about curtains."

"Different curtains."

Of course.

Shizuka opened her book.

The displayed line read:

Consistency is the last refuge of the unimaginative.

Karane read it.

"THANK YOU."

Nano:

"The quote does not prove your position."

"Nobody asked logic."

Nano considered.

"Correct."

That was adaptation.

Karane looked at her.

"You just surrendered."

"I updated."

"Same thing."

"No."

Mau laughed.

Later, when the room finally emptied, Karane stayed behind with Mau for half a minute.

Not intentionally.

They both happened to be carrying fabric in the same direction.

Karane said:

"This place is stupid."

Mau:

"Yes."

"Everybody just walks into everybody else's stuff."

"Also yes."

"It's annoying."

"Yes."

She waited.

Then:

"I like it."

Mau looked at her.

Karane immediately:

"Don't."

"I didn't say anything."

"Your face."

Everyone had discovered the face.

Terrible.

She continued:

"In our world, absurd was normal."

Mau:

"I've heard."

"Here it's different."

"How?"

Karane thought.

"Here everyone knows it's absurd."

Mau laughed.

That was fair.

Hakari called from the corridor.

"Karane!"

Karane answered immediately.

"COMING!"

Then quieter, to Mau:

"Kusuri staying doesn't mean we all have to."

Mau stopped.

The Return/Stay offer did not exist yet.

She meant staying in the settlement.

Current life.

Still important.

"Right."

Karane looked at him.

"And us being together doesn't mean we have to do everything together."

"Right."

"But if somebody makes us live in separate buildings because we're from the same source or because they think independence means distance, I'll kill them."

Mau stared.

Karane flushed.

"Not literally!"

"Good."

"Probably."

"Karane."

"Shut up."

She left.

Mau stood with the fabric.

Family did not mean sameness.

Independence did not mean separation.

Karane, somehow, had summarized half the season while complaining about curtains.

He would never tell her.
