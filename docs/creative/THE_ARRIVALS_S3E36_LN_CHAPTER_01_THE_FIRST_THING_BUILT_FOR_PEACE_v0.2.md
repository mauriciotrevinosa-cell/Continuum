# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 22 — The First Thing Built for Peace

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 22

Most early construction existed because something bad could happen.

Water.

Shelter.

Medical.

Defense.

Arrival House.

Storage.

Drainage.

Then they built a small plaza.

No emergency function.

That made everyone suspicious.

Rimuru presented plan.

Momo looked.

"What's it for?"

"People."

"Doing what?"

"Being there."

Silence.

Aira:

"That's inefficient."

Frieren:

"Good."

The site sat between common room, washing court and one route toward workshops.

Not central enough to control movement.

Close enough to encounter people.

Shade.

Benches.

Open ground.

One section with softer surface.

Coco wanted decoration.

Agott wanted durable surfaces.

Wakana wanted shade cloth.

Bocchi wanted one corner not directly visible from every approach.

Mikasa asked whether open space interfered evacuation.

No.

Maomao asked whether food stalls would ruin sanitation.

If unmanaged.

So utilities planned at edge before stalls existed.

Senku approved.

"Future-proof peace."

Momo:

"That is the worst phrase you've ever said."

Children cared about none of this.

They occupied center before finishing.

Workers had put down flat boards.

Children used them as game.

Construction paused around them.

Fern considered moving them.

Rimuru:

"Isn't that the point?"

Fern hated being caught.

Left them.

The plaza opened without opening.

One day benches were there.

Next day someone ate lunch.

Someone else repaired clothes.

Searchers returned and debriefed outside because weather good.

Bocchi sat in protected corner.

Played quietly.

Nobody asked for concert.

Milim challenged children to game she had invented badly.

Lost.

Demanded rematch.

A native merchant began meeting clients there because Arrival House felt too formal.

Second-community visitor waited under shade.

People argued.

Rested.

Met.

No schedule.

Mau sat with Frieren one evening.

"No survival purpose."

Frieren:

"Rest is a survival purpose."

"Cheating."

"Yes."

Mau looked around.

No alarm.

No intake.

No one sick.

No construction emergency.

People still using space.

That felt stranger than fortress.

A city choosing to spend material on nothing urgent.

Proof it expected tomorrow.

A week later someone proposed mounting one defense signal near plaza because visibility good.

Mikasa agreed.

Coco objected to making plaza feel military.

They compromised:
signal integrated but not dominant.

Safety present.

Not identity.

Important.

Another week:
one couple argued publicly.
One child fell.
One traveler slept on bench.
Someone left flowers by tree for reason nobody knew.

The plaza collected life faster than plans.

The first thing built primarily for peace became one of settlement's most used spaces.

That said more about what city hoped to become than any defensive wall could.
