# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 22 — The First Thing Built for Peace

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 22

Most early construction existed because something bad could happen.

Water.

Shelter.

Medical.

Defense.

Arrival House.

Then they built a small plaza.

No emergency function.

That made everyone suspicious.

Shade.

Benches.

Open space.

A place to eat.

Talk.

Play.

Argue.

Coco wanted decoration.

Agott wanted durable surfaces.

Wakana wanted shade cloth.

Bocchi wanted one corner not directly visible from everywhere.

Children wanted nothing planners proposed.

They occupied the middle.

The plaza became useful precisely because no one needed it to be.

Mau sat with Frieren.

"No survival purpose."

Frieren:

"Rest is a survival purpose."

"That's cheating."

"Yes."

The first thing built for peace became one of the most used spaces in the settlement.

That said something about what the city was becoming.
