## Chapter 8 — The Person in Front

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Source episode:** S3E36 — `The Person in Front`

The expectation appeared in small language.

"If something happens, Mau will know."

"Mau can handle weird system stuff."

"Mau survived Sukuna."

"Mau's been through worse."

None were insults.

That was the danger.

A new resident asked where the emergency shelter was.

Someone pointed.

Then added:

"If the alarms get really bad, find Mau."

Frieren heard.

"So he can do what?"

The resident froze.

They had no answer.

Mau did.

"Probably get lost too."

Joke.

People laughed.

Frieren did not.

Later she asked:

"Why did you make that easier?"

Mau knew what she meant.

"They were scared."

"That doesn't answer me."

He looked toward the defense map.

"I didn't want them embarrassed."

Frieren's expression softened.

Then hardened again.

"Do not make being volunteered for danger socially comfortable."

Mau stared.

That sentence stayed.
