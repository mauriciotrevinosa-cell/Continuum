# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 9 — One Person for Hundreds

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E36 — `The Person in Front`

The resident who said it was not cruel.

That mattered.

They were discussing rumors about a higher entity interested in Mau.

Nothing proven.

Just enough strange events around him to create fear.

The resident asked:

"If something comes here for one person... what are we supposed to do?"

Silence.

They continued.

"I have two kids."

Nobody interrupted.

"If the choice becomes one person or hundreds, what do we do?"

Frieren went still.

Ori stopped breathing.

Bocchi looked sick.

Mau understood the question.

That was the worst part.

He could imagine asking it too.

Not about himself.

About someone else.

The resident looked at Mau.

"I'm sorry."

Mau said:

"Don't be."

Frieren turned to him.

He saw the warning.

Too late.

The room had already transformed Mau's life into a public variable.

Not hatred.

Arithmetic.

One.

Hundreds.

The horror was how reasonable fear could sound.
