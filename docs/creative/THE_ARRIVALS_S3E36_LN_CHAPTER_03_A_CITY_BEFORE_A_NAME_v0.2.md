## Chapter 24 — A City Before a Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 24

The settlement had:
- public water;
- wastewater treatment;
- Arrival House;
- Searchers;
- refuge bunks;
- shared maintenance;
- a safe corridor partnership;
- a plaza;
- too many arguments.

Still no agreed name.

Outsiders had names.

Otherworlder city.

Mau's settlement.

The refuge.

The fort.

The witch place.

Rimuru's town.

None correct.

The people inside kept saying:

home.

That was enough until it wasn't.

Mikasa brought a defense proposal to the coordinating table.

Not walls.

Observation.

Alarm layers.

Evacuation routes.

Safe zones.

The need had become obvious as the settlement grew.

At the same meeting, Anko placed three registration orders side by side.

Different regions.

Same phrase.

Not similar.

Same.

"Uncontrolled concentration of foreign capability."

Senku looked at the wording.

Then the maps.

Pattern.

Not proof of author.

Enough to worry.

The city had become visible before it had named itself.

Volume Sixteen ended with two questions arriving together:

How do we defend a place without turning it into a cage?

And who keeps teaching distant governments to fear us using the same words?
