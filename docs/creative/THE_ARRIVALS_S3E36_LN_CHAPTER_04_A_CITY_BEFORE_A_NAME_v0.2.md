# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 32 — A City Before a Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 32

The settlement had first working versions of:
shared water points;
a wastewater / drainage treatment route;
an enclosed Arrival House shell;
Searchers;
refuge bunks;
shared maintenance;
safe-corridor partnership;
a rough commons people called the plaza;
temporary bath facilities;
workshop shells;
Bocchi's usable-but-unfinished music room;
too many arguments.

Outsiders increasingly called that a city.

The people building it usually looked at the unfinished foundations and laughed.

Still no agreed name.

Outsiders had plenty.

Otherworlder city.

Mau's settlement.

The refuge.

The fort.

The witch place.

Rimuru's town.

Monster town.

None correct.

Inside, people mostly said:

home.

That was enough until it wasn't.

The naming meeting lasted sixteen minutes.

Momo proposed seven.

Rejected seven.

Milim proposed one involving herself.

Rejected immediately.

Senku asked whether name mattered operationally.

Fern:

"Yes. Routes."

Anko:

"Yes. Documents."

Coco:

"Yes. Identity."

Senku:

"Fine."

No answer.

Meeting postponed.

The idea of a city existed before the buildings did.

Socially, the place was already doing city-shaped things.

Physically, half of its future was still string in dirt.

Mau walked through after sunset.

He stepped over one future foundation line on the way.

Washing court marks glowed faintly.

Not bright enough to erase night.

Plaza occupied.

Arrival House lamp.

A Searcher map being updated.

Someone carrying laundry.

Someone arguing about bath queue.

No cinematic reveal.

Life.

Then Mikasa placed defense proposal on coordination table.

Not walls.

Observation layers.

Alarm levels.

Route beacons.

Evacuation corridors.

Hardened refuge points.

Inner safe zones.

Rapid response.

Last-defense positions.

Mau read.

"Looks like we're planning defenses for a city we haven't finished building."

Mikasa:

"Threats don't wait for finished walls."

Fair.

Eren had not arrived yet.

The question would sharpen.

At same meeting, Anko placed three registration orders side by side.

Different regions.

Same phrase.

Not similar.

Same.

> uncontrolled concentration of foreign capability

Senku looked.

Then another.

Same punctuation.

Momo:

"Copying?"

Anko:

"Maybe."

"Same author?"

"Maybe."

No mastermind proof.

Pattern.

Enough to worry.

Rimuru looked at map.

Rules spreading.

Rumors spreading.

Settlement becoming visible.

Power concentrating.

A place not yet physically a city already had enemies who had named it as one for themselves.

Mau looked at phrase.

Then defense plan.

Two pressures arriving together:

outside world learning to describe them as threat;

inside city learning to defend without becoming the threat described.

Frieren sat beside him.

"You're thinking too loudly."

Mau:

"How do we protect a place without turning it into a cage?"

Mikasa heard.

"That's the question."

The settlement went on without a name.

It did not need one.

The place was still unfinished enough that rain could expose a bad route in one afternoon.

But it had become real enough socially that the next problem was no longer only how to build.

It was what kind of community they were willing to become while the world already treated them like a finished power.
