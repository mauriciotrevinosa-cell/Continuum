The first alarm came at dusk.

`HUMAN-LIKE / MULTIPLE / WEST.`

Then:

`ANIMAL-LIKE / LARGE / NORTHWEST.`

Then:

`UNKNOWN / FAST.`

Three directions.

Rimuru stood before anyone asked.

Mikasa was already moving.

No panic bell.

Alarm level two.

Not evacuation.

Prepare.

Arrival House stopped intake.

Children moved toward designated inner spaces.

Medical supplies opened.

Water reserve valves checked.

Searchers recalled.

Outer observers confirmed lights west.

Too many.

Not a merchant caravan.

The northern movement turned out to be animals.

Running.

Not attacking.

Fleeing something.

That made the unknown signal worse.

Mau reached for a weapon.

Frieren touched his wrist.

"Role?"

He looked at the plan.

Not `front.`

Specific.

"Inner rapid response."

"Good."

He hated that she sounded relieved.

Then understood why.
