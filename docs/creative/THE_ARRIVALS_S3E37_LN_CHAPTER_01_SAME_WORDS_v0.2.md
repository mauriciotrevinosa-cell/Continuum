# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 1 — Same Words

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 1

The phrase appeared in three regions.

"Uncontrolled concentration of foreign capability."

Same order.

Same adjective.

Same bureaucratic rhythm.

Different authorities.

Anko laid the documents side by side.

Nobody said mastermind.

Not yet.

A second repeated phrase:

"Temporary relocation pending capability assessment."

Five documents.

Four seals.

One office that could not be verified.

Senku compared copied maps.

Two shared the same wrong river angle.

Not coincidence.

Could still be common source material.

Could still be ordinary copying.

Could be intentional coordination.

The important conclusion was narrower:

the fear was learning to reproduce itself.

Searchers began collecting provenance.

Who issued?

Who delivered?

What packet?

What map?

Which rumor arrived first?

No villain reveal.

Evidence network.

The city had grown enough that its enemies—or merely frightened institutions—could describe it using shared language before it had a name.

That made defense a political problem before it became a military one.
