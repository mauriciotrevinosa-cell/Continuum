# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 12 — Unknown

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E37 — `First Alarm`

The unknown signature crossed one beacon.

Then vanished.

Diablo disliked that.

Senku disliked the timing.

Frieren disliked everything.

The western group carried copied notices.

Not uniforms.

Some local militia.

Some hired people.

Some frightened civilians moving with them.

No clean enemy block.

Their stated demand reached the outer post:

Submit all unregistered Otherworlders for relocation and inspection.

Nobody at the settlement had agreed to either category.

Rimuru sent negotiation.

Anko.

Himmel.

A native representative.

Diablo remained out of sight because diplomacy did not improve when the negotiator looked like a threat from somebody's religion.

The northern animals kept running.

Then the ground shook.

Not an army.

A monster wave displaced from farther northwest.

Coincidence?

Maybe.

Timing?

Bad enough to matter.

The settlement now faced two problems that could have been unrelated.

That was exactly why the defense plan had layers.
