# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 3 — He Doesn't Want the City

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 3

Eren's objection was not:

I hate them.

It was:

"I don't want to walk into a place built to keep everyone safe and discover I'm not allowed to leave."

Anko almost laughed.

Not because it was funny.

Because the settlement was already arguing about that exact fear.

"You can leave."

Eren looked at her.

"Now."

"Yes."

"And during an attack?"

"Depends."

There.

Honesty.

He asked about gates.

No walls.

Checkpoints.

Some.

Registration.

Arrival House asks needs first, not mandatory identity inventory.

Contracts.

Voluntary unless tied to a specific service or risk.

Leadership.

Messy.

Eren listened.

The city sounded less like rumor.

Still dangerous conceptually.

Anko said:

"You don't have to come."

That decided more than pressure would have.

Eren looked toward the sea.

"Mikasa can come here?"

"Yes."

"I'll wait."

Searchers sent word.

No extraction.

No escort by force.

A person who did not want home had been found.

The first task was not bringing him home.

It was asking what home meant to him.
