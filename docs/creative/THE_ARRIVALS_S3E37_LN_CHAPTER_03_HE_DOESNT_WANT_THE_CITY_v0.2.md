# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 3 — He Doesn't Want the City

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 3

Eren's objection was not:

I hate them.

It was:

"I don't want to walk into a place built to keep everyone safe and discover I'm not allowed to leave."

Anko almost laughed.

Not because funny.

Because settlement had spent weeks arguing exactly that.

"You can leave."

Eren looked.

"Now."

"Yes."

"At night?"

"Yes."

"During an alarm?"

"Depends."

There.

Honesty.

Eren's expression sharpened.

"On what?"

"If road is actively under attack, walking into it can endanger you and people sent after you."

"So no."

"Temporary restriction during specific danger."

"Same thing with better words."

Anko accepted hit.

"Sometimes words hide cages."

Eren looked at her.

"And sometimes distinctions matter."

He did not like balanced answer.

Good.

He asked:
gates?
No walls.
checkpoints?
Some route watches.
registration?
Arrival House asks immediate need first; no mandatory full power inventory.
contracts?
Voluntary unless tied to specific service or risk.
leadership?
Messy.
army?
No unified permanent army, but defense groups.
surveillance?
Limited route/alarm systems under debate.

Eren listened.

The place sounded less like rumor.

Still dangerous conceptually.

"Who decides during emergency?"

"That is one of the arguments."

He almost smiled.

At least they admitted incompleteness.

Anko sat several feet away.

"You don't have to come."

That sentence did more than brochure.

Eren looked toward sea.

"Why are you telling me all flaws?"

"Because if you arrive and discover them later, that isn't consent."

He looked back.

Mikasa had chosen to live there.

That fact pulled hardest.

He hated that love could influence movement.

He also understood it.

"What if I come for her and hate the place?"

"Leave."

"What if she stays?"

"That's between you and her."

Eren laughed without humor.

"Everything sounds easy when you say it."

"No."

Anko looked at water.

"It's just your choice until it touches other people."

That sentence stayed.

Eren stood.

Not ready to enter.

Not ready to reject.

"I'll wait for Mikasa."

Searchers sent word.

They did not mark him RESCUED.

Board status changed:

FOUND / SAFE ENOUGH / CHOICE PENDING.

Important.

A person could be found without being returned to group.

The city had to survive someone saying:
not yet.

Eren waited at sea.

For once, a wall existed only in rumor.

He still needed proof.
