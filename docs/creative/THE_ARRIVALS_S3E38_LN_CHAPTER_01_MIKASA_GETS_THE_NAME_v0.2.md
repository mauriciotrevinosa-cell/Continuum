# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 4 — Mikasa Gets the Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 4

Mikasa read the message once.

Then again.

EREN YEAGER.

Location: coast.

Status: safe enough.

Will not enter settlement yet.

Requests Mikasa.

Her hands did not shake.

Everything else did.

The common room continued around her.

Someone carrying water.

Fern answering question.

A child asking where Okarun had gone.

Ordinary life refusing to understand one name had changed gravity.

Mau was nearby.

He saw Mikasa's face.

Did not ask:

Isn't this what you wanted?

He had learned reunions better.

Mikasa looked up.

"I need a team."

Not:
I'm going alone.

Good.

Anko already at coast.

Mikasa chose:
one guide;
one transport option;
medical kit because unknown;
nobody else.

Mau almost volunteered.

Body shifted forward.

Stopped.

This was not his reunion.

Frieren noticed.

Small nod.

Mikasa folded note.

Unfolded.

Read again.

FOUND.

Not:
same.

Not:
future Eren.

The boy at coast had memory around sea.

She knew because Anko included snapshot clues carefully.

Mikasa sat.

For one minute.

Maybe two.

Fern stood nearby.

"Do you need anything?"

Mikasa looked at her.

"Time."

Fern nodded.

Gave it.

Mikasa's memories refused.

Eren laughing as child.

Eren angry.

Eren older.

Eren walking away.

Eren asking terrible things.

Eren dying by her hand.

All attached to one name on paper.

But the person waiting had not lived those years.

That mattered intellectually.

Emotionally, her body did not sort chronology so cleanly.

She went to pack.

Scarf.

Stopped with hand on it.

Took it.

No symbolic decision explained.

Just wanted it.

At route post, Rimuru asked:

"Want us to tell Eren anything before you arrive?"

"No."

Good.

No mediated reunion.

Mau stood a distance away.

"Bring him if he wants."

Mikasa looked.

Mau corrected:

"Come back if you want. Both of you. Either."

Better.

Mikasa nodded.

Left within hour.

The settlement continued.

Water maintenance.

Arrival intake.

Senku measurement.

Searcher debrief.

No one suspended city for one relationship.

That made relationship more real, not less.

On road, Mikasa asked guide to slow once.

Not fatigue.

Breathing.

Then continued.

At coast, Eren waited near salt water.

Mikasa carried years he had not lived toward a boy who still thought the sea was an ending rather than a beginning.

She did not know whether seeing him would feel like reunion, grief, accusation or miracle.

Probably all.

The road did not care.

It ended at sea anyway.
