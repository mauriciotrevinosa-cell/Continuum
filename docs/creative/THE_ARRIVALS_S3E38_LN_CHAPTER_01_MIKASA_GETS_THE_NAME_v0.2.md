# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 4 — Mikasa Gets the Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 4

Mikasa read the message once.

Then again.

EREN YEAGER.

Location: coast.

Status: safe enough.

Will not enter settlement yet.

Requests Mikasa.

Her hands did not shake.

Everything else did.

Mau was nearby.

He saw.

Did not ask:

Isn't this what you wanted?

He had learned reunions better.

Mikasa looked up.

"I need a team."

Not alone.

Good.

Anko already there.

Mikasa chose:
- one guide;
- one transport option;
- nobody else.

Mau almost volunteered.

Stopped.

This was not his reunion.

Frieren noticed.

Small nod.

Mikasa left within the hour.

The settlement continued.

Water maintenance.

Arrival intake.

Senku measurement.

A Searcher debrief.

No one suspended the city for one relationship.

That made the relationship more real, not less.

At the coast, Eren waited.

Mikasa carried years he had not lived toward a boy who still thought the sea was an ending rather than a beginning.
