# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 15 — Outer Line

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E38 — `Last Defense Front`

The outer line did not look heroic.

Mud.

Signals.

Short commands.

People falling back on purpose.

Searchers guiding civilians through routes they had mapped months earlier.

Okarun running messages when magical communication stuttered.

Anko carrying evidence even during retreat because she refused to let the attack become rumor later.

Himmel pulled three frightened militia members out of the path of a monster after they had been part of the force threatening the settlement.

One stared.

"Why?"

Himmel looked at him.

"Because you're there."

No speech.

He moved.

Diablo disarmed rather than killed when surrender was possible.

Frieren did not restrain herself when it was not.

Milim diverted the largest creatures away from housing instead of chasing the biggest target for fun.

Growth looked different on everyone.

The outer line bent.

Did not break.

Then the water alarm sounded.
