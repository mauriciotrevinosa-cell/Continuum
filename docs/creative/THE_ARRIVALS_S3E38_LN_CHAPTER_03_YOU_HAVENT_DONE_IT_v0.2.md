# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 7 — You Haven't Done It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 7

The first future conversation happened on the coast.

Not in hearing.

Not before council.

Eren asked because silence became worse.

"Did I do something?"

Mikasa said nothing.

Answer.

"Something bad?"

Silence.

Anko looked at sea.

Not her history to tell.

Eren kicked sand.

"You remember me doing things I haven't done."

"Yes."

"Do you hate me for them?"

Mikasa's eyes closed.

"No."

Not simple.

True enough.

Eren looked angry.

"Do you think I would do them again?"

Wrong question.

Mikasa opened eyes.

"I don't know what you will do here."

"Convenient."

"It's the only honest answer."

"Then what's point of remembering?"

That hurt.

Mikasa looked toward water.

"To know what I lived."

Not:
to predict him.

Important.

Eren sat again.

"If I ask what I did, will you tell me?"

"Some."

"Why not all?"

"Because some information changes choices."

Eren laughed bitterly.

"So now you decide what future I'm allowed to know?"

Mikasa flinched.

Agency problem.

Anko finally spoke.

"You can ask. She can refuse. Neither makes you guilty."

Eren turned.

Anko continued.

"You haven't done it."

Obvious sentence.

Necessary.

Mikasa flinched anyway.

Source memory could inform caution.

Not guilt.

Eren stared.

"You know what I become."

Anko:

"We know what another continuity's later Eren did."

"That's me."

"Maybe."

He hated maybe.

Good.

Mikasa said softly:

"It was you."

Anko looked.

Mikasa continued.

"I won't pretend it wasn't."

Eren's face hardened.

Then:

"But it isn't something this version of you has done."

Both.

No easy multiverse absolution.

No predestination.

Eren looked at Mikasa.

"If you think I'm going that direction?"

"I tell you."

"You stop me?"

Mikasa went still.

Too many memories.

Eren saw.

"Right."

No need to state future death.

Not yet.

He looked away.

Anko said:

"The settlement has rules for current conduct and current risk."

Eren:

"And future risk."

"Preparation. Not punishment."

"Difference until someone gets scared."

There.

That concern would follow him.

Mikasa answered:

"Then you help us keep the difference."

Eren looked at her.

Not ask:
trust us.

Ask:
participate.

He breathed.

Then question that mattered more:

"Do you want me there?"

Mikasa hesitated.

Every instinct wanted:
yes.

Every lesson wanted:
choice.

"I want you where you choose to be."

Hardest possible answer.

Eren looked at inland road.

Sea behind.

Mikasa ahead but not pulling.

"Show me the place."

Choice.

Not forgiveness.

Not fate.

A first current action everyone could actually judge.
