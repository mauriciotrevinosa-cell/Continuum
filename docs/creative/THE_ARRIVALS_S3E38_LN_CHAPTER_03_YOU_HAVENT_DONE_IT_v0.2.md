# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 6 — You Haven't Done It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 6

The first future conversation happened on the coast.

Not in a hearing.

Eren asked:

"Did I do something?"

Mikasa said nothing.

Answer.

"Something bad?"

Silence.

Anko looked at the sea.

Not her history to tell.

Eren kicked sand.

"You remember me doing things I haven't done."

"Yes."

"Do you hate me for them?"

Mikasa's eyes closed.

"No."

Not simple.

True enough.

"Do you think I would do them again?"

Wrong question.

Mikasa opened her eyes.

"I don't know what you will do here."

Eren looked angry.

"That's convenient."

"It's the only honest answer."

Anko finally spoke.

"You haven't done it."

Eren turned.

Obvious sentence.

Necessary.

Mikasa flinched anyway.

Source memory could inform caution.

Not guilt.

That principle started before the settlement ever discussed Eren officially.

He looked at Mikasa.

"Do you want me there?"

Mikasa hesitated.

Then:

"I want you where you choose to be."

Hardest possible answer.

Eren looked toward inland roads.

"Show me the place."

Choice.
