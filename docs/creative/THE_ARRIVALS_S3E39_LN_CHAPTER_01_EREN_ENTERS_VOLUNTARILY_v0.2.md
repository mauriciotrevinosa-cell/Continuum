# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 7 — Eren Enters Voluntarily

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 7

Eren entered the settlement through an ordinary road.

No escort formation.

No chains.

No ceremony.

Arrival House offered orientation.

He laughed once.

Mikasa looked at him.

"What's funny?"

"A building for arriving."

"Useful."

"Maybe."

He was told:
- no one required permanent residence;
- current dangerous conduct mattered;
- source-world information could inform risk planning but not become automatic guilt;
- he could leave.

Eren asked:

"Now?"

"Yes."

"Tonight?"

"Yes."

"During an alarm?"

"Depends on the alarm."

Same honest answer.

He noticed.

Mau met him later.

No instant philosophical duel.

Mau was carrying maintenance forms.

Eren looked disappointed.

"You're Mau?"

Mau sighed.

"Why does everyone say it like that?"

Rumor.

Again.

Eren had heard Mau controlled the city.

The forms in his hands suggested otherwise.

Good first contradiction.
