## Chapter 17 — When Systems Break

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S3E39 — `Systems Break`

A monster struck the upper diversion channel.

Magic failed first.

Not completely.

Enough.

The maintenance circle fractured.

Clean-water pressure dropped.

Dirty overflow rose.

Exactly the combined failure Senku had asked about months earlier.

Coco saw the warning.

Agott reached the manual bypass.

Senku shouted numbers.

Qifrey stabilized only the sections that needed magic.

Native builders opened the mechanical spill route.

Maomao closed medical intake from that branch before contamination reached it.

Nobody improvised a miracle.

They executed the ugly redundant system everyone had argued over.

Water flooded one unfinished street.

Not the refuge.

The washing court lost supply.

Not the clinic.

A storage shed filled ankle-deep.

Not the clean reserve.

Senku laughed once.

Coco yelled:

"WHY ARE YOU HAPPY?"

"Because it failed correctly!"

Coco looked at the water.

Then laughed too.

Infrastructure had become a combat character.

It survived by being designed to break in pieces.
