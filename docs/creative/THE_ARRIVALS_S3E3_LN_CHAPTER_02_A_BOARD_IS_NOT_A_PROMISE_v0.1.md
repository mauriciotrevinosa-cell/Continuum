<!-- scene-id: s3e3-ln02-priority -->

The first argument happened the next morning.

Not about a name.

About priority.

A merchant had heard of a silver-haired girl traveling north.

Rimuru looked at Frieren.

Frieren looked unimpressed.

"There are many."

Coco was already halfway to the door.

"Richeh could—"

Agott caught her sleeve.

"Silver hair."

Coco stopped.

Richeh did not have silver hair.

Hope had moved before evidence.

That became the lesson.

The board needed rules.

Not laws.

Rules.

A rumor did not become a rescue mission because someone wanted it badly.

They asked:
- how specific was the description?
- how recent?
- was the person in immediate danger?
- how far?
- who else needed help on the same route?
- could a small team verify before everyone moved?

Coco hated the questions.

Then answered them.

The rumor was weak.

Coco read the report three times anyway.

Silver hair.

Young woman.

North road.

No hat.

No known magic.

No Atelier symbols.

Seen from a wagon at distance.

Agott stood beside her.

"Still want to go?"

"Yes."

"Me too."

Coco looked at her.

Agott continued:

"That doesn't make the report better."

The sentence did what the rules were supposed to do.

It separated desire from evidence without insulting either.

Coco put the card back.

"Verify through the next northern team."

Rimuru marked it.

VERIFY.

They did not go.

<!-- scene-id: s3e3-ln02-notfavorites -->

Later Bocchi stood near the board with Kita.

"If someone said Nijika was somewhere..."

Kita looked at her.

Bocchi's voice got smaller.

"I'd want everyone to go."

"Yeah."

"But if somebody else was trapped..."

"Yeah."

Bocchi looked at the cards.

"This is horrible."

Kita took her hand.

"Probably means you're thinking about it right."

Mau heard from the table.

He did not join.

Not every hard conversation needed him.

That was new enough to notice.

<!-- scene-id: s3e3-ln02-rimuru -->

Rimuru created three marks.

Verify.

Urgent.

Known danger.

Momo asked where `I really miss them` went.

Rimuru pointed at the board.

"Every card."

Momo stopped joking.

The first verification came back that evening.

The silver-haired traveler existed.

Native.

Different person.

No crisis.

Coco thanked the team.

Then went outside for ten minutes alone.

Nobody called the process a success in front of her.

It was a success.

It also hurt.

Those facts could coexist.

The system remained imperfect.

Good.

A board that pretended grief could be optimized would have been worse.

At the bottom, Nano added one sentence:

> A name means we remember to ask.

Nobody erased that one.
