## Chapter 9 — Searchers Without a Name

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 10  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E3 — `Names on the Wall`

<!-- scene-id: s3e3-ln03-route -->

The first team that would later be called Searchers left to check a broken bridge.

That was all.

No missing person.

No dramatic rescue.

A bridge.

The route mattered because wagons using the northern valley could reach the future site without passing through the mercantile town where Bocchi had been attacked.

The team was:
- Mikasa;
- Okarun;
- Anko;
- Maomao;
- a native guide who knew the valley.

Mau was not on it.

He noticed.

Nobody had excluded him.

That was worse.

Or better.

He had been working beside Rimuru when Mikasa said:

"We have enough."

Mau looked at the list.

Navigation.

Speed.

Medical judgment.

Local knowledge.

Someone who would write down what actually happened.

They did have enough.

"Okay."

Mikasa waited.

"For what?"

"Nothing."

"You look like you're about to add yourself."

"I was."

"Are you?"

Mau looked toward the unfinished water notes.

"No."

Mikasa nodded.

Then left.

<!-- scene-id: s3e3-ln03-stay -->

Mau spent the day at the site.

That should not have felt like character development.

It did.

He helped Qifrey test marker spacing.

Carried samples for Maomao even though Maomao was not there to tell him he carried them wrong.

Listened to Rimuru explain why storage had to exist before anyone had enough things to justify storage.

Frieren arrived after noon.

"Where is Okarun?"

"Bridge."

"You didn't go?"

"No."

Frieren looked at him.

Mau looked back.

"What?"

"Nothing."

She smiled.

Mau knew that smile.

"I can stay home."

"This isn't home yet."

"You know what I mean."

"Yes."

<!-- scene-id: s3e3-ln03-return -->

The bridge was bad.

Not collapsed.

Worse enough to matter.

The team returned with measurements, two alternate crossings, and news that a family farther north had seen strangers sleeping near an abandoned mill.

No descriptions.

No names.

The board gained a small note.

`VERIFY.`

Nobody called the group Searchers.

Not yet.

They were just the people who had gone to look and come back with something useful.

That was how institutions started in Continuum.

Usually before anyone admitted they were building one.
