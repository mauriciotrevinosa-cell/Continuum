# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 19 — The Rule That Bent

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 19  
**Source episode:** S3E40 — `The Rule That Bent`

The thing under the refuge was not a person.

Not exactly.

A foreign suppression construct had been buried in material delivered weeks earlier.

Who placed it remained unknown.

Its effect was simple enough:

reduce access to imported power systems inside a radius.

Frieren felt it first from outside.

Yuta felt it.

Rimuru felt the edge.

Inside, several residents lost abilities abruptly.

Panic started.

Mau reached the device.

The field touched him.

Stopped.

The warning lattice around him lit with contradictory classifications.

`OTHERWORLDER.`

`LOCAL.`

`UNKNOWN.`

`SYSTEM ERROR.`

People saw.

Too many.

The suppression field tried to classify what it was supposed to suppress.

The Noise reacted.

Not by overpowering it.

By attempting to understand it.

The construct hesitated.

One second.

Senku yelled:

"Don't let it learn!"

Mau almost laughed.

Wrong entity.

He used the hesitation to expose the device physically.

No Purple.

No impossible attack.

Just enough Construction understanding to identify a joint.

Aira broke it.

Literally.

With a tool.

The field collapsed.

That was all.

The important part was what everyone had seen.

The world had not known what category Mau belonged to.

Rumor would not be kind.
