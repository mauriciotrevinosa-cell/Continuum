# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 12 — Walls Without Walls

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 12

Eren expected a wall meeting.

There were no walls.

That was the first thing Mikasa showed him.

Not plans.

Ground.

They walked outer routes.

Observation post.

Beacon.

A slope deliberately kept clear.

One hardened shelter half buried into terrain.

Then back toward housing.

Mikasa explained layers.

Observation.

Route warnings.

Magical sensors.

Physical indicators.

Alarm levels.

Evacuation corridors.

Hardened refuge points.

Inner safe zones.

Mobile response.

Last-defense positions.

Eren stopped.

"So people can leave."

"Yes."

"No wall."

"Some barriers can be raised temporarily."

"Not the same."

"No."

He looked at her.

"You care about that."

Mikasa did not answer immediately.

"I remember what protection can become."

Eren looked away.

Did she mean walls?

Him?

Both?

No need to ask yet.

They returned to planning table.

Senku had failure modes.

"If sensor network fails?"

Human watch.

"If magic interference?"

Physical markers.

"If route blocked?"

Secondary.

Qifrey added magical blind spots.

Searchers added real terrain:

one western approach looked clear on map but flooded after rain.

Native partners added livestock paths sensors should not treat as invasion lanes.

No franchise's defense logic dominated.

Eren studied map.

"Why not build one perimeter and control crossings?"

Rimuru:

"Cheaper in some ways."

Mikasa:

"Worse in others."

Eren:

"Like?"

Mikasa pointed:
trade.
escape.
growth.
psychology.
single breach.
internal control.

Senku added:
maintenance concentration.

Qifrey:
magical target.

Anko:
political symbol.

Eren looked.

Exactly.

A wall did not merely stop enemies.

It changed who controlled movement.

He asked:

"Who can activate temporary barriers?"

Silence too long.

Bad.

They wrote names.

Then wrote conditions.

Alarm level.

Threat type.

Duration.

Review.

Who can lower them.

Eren watched rules appear because he asked suspicious questions.

Useful.

Momo came in late.

Looked map.

"No giant wall?"

"No."

"Can we have cool gate?"

Everyone:

"No."

Defense drill happened that afternoon.

No enemy.

Alarm yellow.

People moved toward refuge points.

Some confused.

One route bottlenecked.

Children ignored ideal path.

One older resident could not reach planned shelter quickly.

Defense plan failed politely.

Good.

They changed.

Eren helped carry marker.

Mau noticed.

"Thought you hated city."

"I hate cages."

"Different."

"Maybe."

At end, Mikasa stood with Eren overlooking settlement.

No wall horizon.

Lights spreading irregularly.

"Safer?"

Eren asked.

Mikasa:

"Hopefully."

"Freer?"

She looked at open roads.

"That's something we have to keep choosing."

Defense shaped around freedom of movement rather than replacing it.

No Attack on Titan clone.

A city that tried to remember every protective structure also created power.

And power required exits.
