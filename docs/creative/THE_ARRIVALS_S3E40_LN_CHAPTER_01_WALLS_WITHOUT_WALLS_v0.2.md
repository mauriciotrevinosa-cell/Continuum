# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 9 — Walls Without Walls

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 9

Eren expected a wall meeting.

There were no walls.

Mikasa's defense plan used layers.

Observation points.

Route beacons.

Magical sensors.

Physical indicators.

Alarm levels.

Evacuation corridors.

Hardened refuge points.

Inner safe zones.

Mobile response.

Last-defense positions.

Eren stared.

"So people can leave."

Mikasa:

"Yes."

"No wall."

"Some temporary barriers."

"Not the same."

"No."

He looked at her.

"You care about that."

Mikasa did not answer immediately.

"I remember what protection can become."

Eren did not know whether she meant him.

Good.

Senku added failure modes.

Qifrey added magical blind spots.

Searchers added route reality.

Native partners added local approach behavior.

No Attack on Titan city clone.

Defense shaped around freedom of movement rather than replacing it.
