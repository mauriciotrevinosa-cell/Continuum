# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 20 — Morning After

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 20  
**Source episode:** S3E40 — `The Rule That Bent`

Morning smelled like wet ash.

The settlement stood.

That was not the same as winning cleanly.

Three structures damaged beyond quick repair.

Water system bruised but safe.

Medical stores low.

Outer warning posts destroyed.

Dozens injured.

No mass-casualty collapse.

The western force had broken apart after realizing the monster wave did not distinguish sides.

Some fled.

Some surrendered.

Some helped.

The monster pressure receded.

No mastermind revealed.

No clean explanation for coordination.

The suppression device remained in pieces on a table.

Anko cataloged it.

Senku measured it.

Diablo hated it.

Mau avoided looking.

People looked at Mau instead.

Not everyone.

Enough.

One whispered:

"The system couldn't classify him."

Another:

"It stopped for him."

Another:

"He broke it."

Aira shouted from across the room:

"I broke it."

Nobody listened correctly.

Mau sat outside the clinic.

Frieren joined him.

"You stayed in your role."

"Mostly."

"You called before moving."

"Yes."

She took his hand.

Progress.

Then a child passed and said:

"That's him."

Mau looked up.

The child smiled.

Admiration.

Frieren's hand tightened.

Victory had created a new problem.
