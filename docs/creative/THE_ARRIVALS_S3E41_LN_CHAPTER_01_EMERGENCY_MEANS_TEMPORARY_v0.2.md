# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 16 — Emergency Means Temporary

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 16

The first real movement restriction lasted one night.

Hostile patrol reported west.

Not rumor.

Searcher visual confirmation.

Unknown intent.

Enough to justify caution.

No nonessential civilian travel after dark on west routes.

Specific.

Reasonable.

Eren hated it anyway.

Mikasa looked at him.

"You think we shouldn't?"

"I think it should end."

"It hasn't started."

"That's when you decide how it ends."

Everyone looked.

Annoying.

Correct.

They wrote:
start at sunset.
review at dawn.
west only.
medical/search emergency exceptions.
no automatic extension.

The night passed.

No attack.

Patrol moved south.

At dawn, Searchers confirmed route clear enough.

Meeting.

Someone proposed keeping restriction one more night.

"Just in case."

Mikasa:

"No."

The room looked.

"What if they come back?"

"Then we reinstate it."

"Why remove something that makes us safer?"

Eren said nothing.

Mikasa answered:

"Because emergency rules that never end stop being emergency rules."

Rimuru wrote it down.

One resident argued:
people slept better with restriction.

Another:
their work requires night travel.

Another:
children safer.

Another:
road closure pushes traffic through worse path.

No universal safety.

They lifted restriction.

That evening, nothing happened.

Good.

Three days later another threat emerged east.

New restriction.

Different route.

Different reason.

Different review.

People complained inconsistency.

Anko:

"Specificity looks inconsistent because threats differ."

Useful.

Then hard test.

One emergency restriction reached review time while threat not fully resolved.

Searchers had incomplete information.

Extend?

Eren expected automatic yes.

Instead:
thirty-minute review.
evidence.
scope narrowed.

Only one corridor remained closed.

Rest reopened.

Better.

Every emergency measure gained:
reason.
start.
review.
responsible person/group.
end condition.
appeal path where practical.

Not constitutional perfection.

A habit against permanent fear.

One afternoon Eren found Mikasa updating board.

"You really believe rules stop people with power?"

Mikasa:

"No."

"Then?"

"They make it easier for other people to say we're breaking our own promise."

Eren looked.

That was different.

Rules not magic.

Evidence.

Pressure.

Memory.

Mikasa continued:

"And they remind us after we're scared why we made them before."

Eren thought about walls built by fear becoming world.

The settlement had more power than he liked.

It was also building mechanisms to stop itself.

That did not make him trust it.

It made distrust more specific.

Specific distrust was something people could work with.
