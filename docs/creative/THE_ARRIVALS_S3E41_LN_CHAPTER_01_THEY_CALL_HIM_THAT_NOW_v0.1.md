# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 1 — They Call Him That Now

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E41 — `After the Front`

Mau learned about the new title from a child.

"The one the world can't name."

Mau stopped.

"What?"

The child looked embarrassed.

"My brother said it."

Mau looked at Frieren.

Frieren looked murderous in a very calm way.

The story had spread.

Not accurately.

The suppression device had failed to classify Mau.

That became:
- the world rejected him;
- the world protected him;
- Mau was native to Continuum;
- Mau owned Continuum;
- Mau could ignore anti-Otherworlder effects;
- Mau was the reason Arrivals existed.

All false.

Or unsupported.

That distinction mattered.

Arrival House started correcting rumors when correction could help.

Not every rumor.

Impossible.

Mau refused a public speech.

Then reconsidered when a frightened new arrival asked whether touching him was dangerous.

He stood in the washing court.

Thirty people.

Not hundreds.

Good.

"I don't know why the device reacted differently to me."

Truth.

"I don't control Continuum."

Truth.

"I can't guarantee weird things won't happen around me."

Unfortunately true.

"I am not a safety system."

Frieren watched from the edge.

That line mattered most.

Afterward someone still called him the worldless man.

Rumor survived information.

But information existed too.
