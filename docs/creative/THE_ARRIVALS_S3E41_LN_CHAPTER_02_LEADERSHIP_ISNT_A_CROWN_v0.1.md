# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 2 — Leadership Isn't a Crown

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S3E41 — `After the Front`

After the assault, people wanted certainty.

Certainty looked like one person.

That was dangerous.

A petition appeared asking Rimuru and Mau to become permanent co-leaders.

Rimuru laughed.

Mau did not.

"Why me?"

The answer was exactly the problem.

"You connect everyone."

"You were there from the beginning."

"People listen to you."

"The world reacts to you."

Mau hated the last one.

The coordinating table rejected permanent personal rule.

Not because leadership was bad.

Because the settlement had already learned too much about dependency.

Roles became clearer.

Terms.

Review.

Emergency powers.

Removal.

Specialists retained authority over technical safety inside defined scopes.

Residents retained voice over lived consequences.

Mau accepted a larger coordination role.

Not king.

Not mayor.

Not owner.

A person responsible for more decisions than before.

Frieren asked him later:

"Do you want it?"

Mau thought.

"Some of it."

"Which part?"

"Helping groups understand each other."

"And the rest?"

"Terrifying."

"Good."

He looked at her.

"Good?"

"If you enjoyed deciding everyone's life, I'd worry."

Fair.
