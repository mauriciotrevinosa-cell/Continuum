# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 14 — Sanctuary Is Not Forgiveness

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 14

The hearing lasted two hours.

Nobody called it trial.

The harmed resident spoke.

The injured man spoke.

Anko wrote.

Rimuru asked.

Mau did not chair.

Important.

Facts:
- he participated;
- not under total coercion;
- not fully free either;
- people were harmed;
- he later helped two people escape;
- his pursuers intended execution.

No fact erased another.

Question:

Would the settlement hand him over for summary death?

No.

That answer did not equal trust.

Temporary sanctuary.

Medical care.

Restricted access to sensitive areas.

No record access.

Review after more evidence.

The harmed resident did not forgive him.

Nobody asked them to.

They left furious.

Protected by the same rules.

Sanctuary protected two people who hated each other.

That was the point.
