# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 3 — The Pattern Behind Fear

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E41 — `After the Front`

The pattern board moved into a locked room.

Not because knowledge should be secret.

Because evidence should not become rumor while unfinished.

Anko arranged:
- notices;
- maps;
- dates;
- testimony;
- supply records;
- holding-site orders;
- attack timing;
- the suppression device delivery chain.

Diablo added observations about intent where he could.

Senku labeled those:

`NON-MEASURABLE / STILL USEFUL.`

Diablo took offense.

Then admitted the label was accurate.

The same administrative language appeared in six regions.

Maps shared copied errors.

False stories often preceded restrictions.

Restrictions often preceded disappearances.

Some officials clearly acted from genuine fear.

Others repeated instructions they could not source.

The mastermind—if there was one—did not need to control everyone.

Only seed enough templates for ordinary institutions to reproduce them.

That was worse than a single villain giving orders.

A system could continue after the person who started it disappeared.

Mau looked at the board.

"Can we prove intent?"

Anko shook her head.

"No."

"Can we prove coordination?"

"Some."

"Can we prove one source?"

"No."

Good.

No premature reveal.

They kept collecting.
