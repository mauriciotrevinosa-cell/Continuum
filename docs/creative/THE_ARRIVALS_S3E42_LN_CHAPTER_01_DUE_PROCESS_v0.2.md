# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 15 — Due Process

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 15

Due process sounded boring until someone hated the person receiving it.

Then it sounded offensive.

A later applicant made the problem harder.

Dangerous here.

Current harm.

Documented.

Also threatened with execution elsewhere.

Arrival House accepted temporary protection under confinement.

Eren asked:

"How long?"

"Until review."

"When?"

The room paused.

Bad.

They wrote the date.

Mikasa looked at Eren.

He noticed.

"Don't."

"I didn't say anything."

"You looked."

The process became more specific:
- allegations written;
- evidence disclosed where safe;
- accused can respond;
- victims can speak without forgiveness;
- restrictions reviewed;
- emergency confinement has clock;
- external enemies do not decide internal guilt.

The first decision satisfied nobody.

Good sign.

The settlement was becoming a place where people could hate outcomes and still know how they were made.
