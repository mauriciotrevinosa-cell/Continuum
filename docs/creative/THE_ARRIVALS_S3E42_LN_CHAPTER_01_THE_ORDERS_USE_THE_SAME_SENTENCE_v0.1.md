# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 4 — The Orders Use the Same Sentence

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 4  
**Source episode:** S3E42 — `Protection Without Trust`

The sentence was:

`TEMPORARY RELOCATION PENDING CAPABILITY ASSESSMENT.`

Seven documents.

Five regions.

Three different legal traditions.

Same sentence.

One official swore he had written his order independently.

Anko believed he believed that.

He showed them the advisory packet.

No author.

No seal.

Professionally formatted.

It had arrived through an intermediary office.

That office existed.

The person who supposedly sent it did not.

Or no longer did.

The chain ended in paperwork.

Diablo called it elegant.

Everyone glared.

"I did not say good."

"Sounded like admiration."

"It was technical."

Senku nodded.

"Unfortunately, he's right."

Rumor spread socially.

Policy spread administratively.

Same pressure.

Different channels.

The settlement stopped treating every hostile official as mastermind collaborator.

Some were.

Some were scared.

Some lazy.

Some opportunistic.

Some convinced they were protecting people.

Complexity made response harder.

Also more honest.
