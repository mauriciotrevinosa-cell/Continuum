## Chapter 5 — Protection Without Trust

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Source episode:** S3E42 — `Protection Without Trust`

The second sanctuary case was harder.

The applicant was dangerous.

Nobody disputed that.

They had injured people after arriving in Continuum.

Not source-world history.

Here.

Current.

Documented.

They also claimed the group pursuing them intended summary execution.

Both could be true.

Arrival House accepted them under guard.

Several residents objected.

Correctly.

"Why protect someone who hurt people?"

Anko answered:

"From what?"

"Death."

"Then yes."

"Why?"

"Because protection from execution is not a declaration of innocence."

That line entered the settlement vocabulary.

The applicant received treatment.

Then confinement in a secure room designed for actual risk, not humiliation.

Access to counsel-like representation from a resident advocate.

A clear list of allegations.

A review date.

No one pretended this was freedom.

No one pretended danger erased personhood either.

Mau watched from outside the process.

Good.

The town's ethics could not depend on his personal mercy.
