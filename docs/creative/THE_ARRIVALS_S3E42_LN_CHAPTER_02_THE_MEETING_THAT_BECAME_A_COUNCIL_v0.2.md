# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 16 — The Meeting That Became a Council

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 16

They had too many meetings.

Water.

Food.

Defense.

Arrival House.

Searchers.

Medical.

Construction.

Sanctuary.

Emergency authority.

Nobody capable of making good decisions could attend all of them.

So one coordinating table formed.

Rotating.

Function-based.

Infrastructure.

Medical.

Arrival/refuge.

Searchers.

Defense.

Food/logistics.

Resident concerns.

At-large seats.

No permanent nobility.

Rimuru coordinated early because he already connected systems.

Mau sat there.

Not king.

One voice with too much cross-group trust.

He noticed people looking at him before speaking.

Frieren noticed him noticing.

Afterward:

"You don't have to answer first."

"I know."

"You do."

Next meeting a hard question arrived.

Mau waited.

Twelve awful seconds.

Fern answered.

The world continued.

Leadership became less about who could answer and more about making sure someone else could.
