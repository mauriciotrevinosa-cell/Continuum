# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 21 — The Meeting That Became a Council

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 21

They had too many meetings.

That was the founding crisis.

Water.

Food.

Defense.

Arrival House.

Searchers.

Medical.

Construction.

Sanctuary.

Emergency authority.

Bridge.

Old inn.

Nobody capable of making good decisions could attend all of them.

Rimuru tried.

Failed by missing lunch twice and one water review.

Fern declared intervention.

"We need one coordinating table."

Momo:

"Council."

Fern:

"No."

Three days later everyone called it council.

Defeat.

The structure stayed simple.

Rotating.

Function-based.

Infrastructure.

Medical.

Arrival/refuge.

Searchers.

Defense.

Food/logistics.

Resident concerns.

At-large seats.

No permanent nobility.

No seat called:
Mau.

He noticed.

Good.

Rimuru coordinated early because he already connected systems.

Not ruler.

Scheduler with too much context.

Fern controlled agendas enough to terrify everyone.

Anko kept decision records.

No one person held all three roles permanently.

First council meeting:
water maintenance conflict with Searcher labor.

Second:
refuge beds.

Third:
emergency restriction review.

Real reason mattered.

Not institution porn.

People needed place where one subsystem could not quietly consume all others.

Mau sat there.

One voice.

Too much cross-group trust.

That created its own gravity.

First hard question:
Do we extend west-route restriction after partial threat report?

Everyone looked at Mau.

He felt it.

Old instinct:
answer.

Frieren, observing from wall, saw.

Mau waited.

One second.

Three.

Five.

Twelve awful seconds.

Fern spoke.

"No. Narrow it."

Discussion moved.

World continued.

After meeting Frieren said:

"You don't have to answer first."

"I know."

"You do."

Mau sighed.

"Apparently."

Next meeting he deliberately asked:
"Who hasn't spoken?"

Agott rolled eyes.

But resident representative answered.

Better.

One council seat went to someone who openly said they did not particularly trust Mau.

Momo asked why.

Fern:

"That's why."

Good.

Eren attended one meeting as guest.

Watched emergency powers.

Asked:
"Who can end an alarm?"

They had activation authority written.

Not termination.

Gap.

Fixed.

Council existed partly so power had a place to return.

No king.

No perfect democracy.

No final constitution.

A coordinating habit.

Minutes posted except private safety details.

Decisions dated.

Temporary rules expired.

Residents could bring issue.

Second community not represented automatically because independent.

Native partners invited when shared infrastructure involved.

Boundaries.

At end of month, Momo finally made small sign:

COUNCIL.

Fern removed.

Next day another appeared.

Fern gave up.

Mau looked at table after everyone left.

Not throne.

Scratches.

Paper.

Half cup tea.

He smiled.

Leadership had become less about who could answer and more about making sure the place still worked when the person everyone looked at chose not to.
