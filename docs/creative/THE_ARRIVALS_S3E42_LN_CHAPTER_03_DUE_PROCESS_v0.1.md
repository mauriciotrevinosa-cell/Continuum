# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 6 — Due Process

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Source episode:** S3E42 — `Protection Without Trust`

Due process sounded like bureaucracy until someone you hated needed it.

Then it sounded offensive.

The harmed residents wanted immediate removal.

Others wanted indefinite confinement.

Eren asked:

"Indefinite based on what?"

Mikasa looked at him.

He noticed.

"Don't."

"I didn't say anything."

"You looked."

Mikasa looked away.

The hearing structure formed from discomfort.

Evidence disclosed unless disclosure endangered someone.

Accused person could respond.

Victims could speak without being forced into forgiveness.

Temporary restrictions required review.

Emergency confinement required a reason and clock.

Outside threats did not decide internal guilt.

Nobody called it a court.

Not yet.

The first decision satisfied nobody.

That was probably a sign it was real.

The dangerous resident remained confined.

Not forever.

Not free.

Protected from execution.

Accountable for current harm.

Mau read the written decision.

Then set it down.

"What?"

Frieren asked.

"We're becoming a place where people can hate a decision and still know how it was made."

Frieren considered.

"That's good?"

"I think so."

"Maybe."

After Memory Arc, Mau respected maybe more.
