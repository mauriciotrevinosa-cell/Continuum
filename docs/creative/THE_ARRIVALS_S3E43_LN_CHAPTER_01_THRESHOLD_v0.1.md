**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Source episode:** S3E43 — `Threshold`

The Noise overload announced itself with numbers.

That was new.

Mau was helping Senku compare pressure readings.

A term appeared in his mind.

`INTEGRATION LOAD.`

He stopped.

Senku noticed.

"What?"

Mau said the phrase.

Senku did not ask what it meant.

He wrote it down.

"Any physical symptoms?"

"Headache."

"Memory?"

Mau checked.

"No mismatch yet."

Good.

Protocol.

He called Rimuru.

Not after collapse.

Before.

Raphael compared current markers to post-Memory records.

Elevated.

Not catastrophic.

Maomao checked temperature.

Kaneki checked for biological changes.

Ori sat near Mau and asked three continuity questions they had chosen in advance.

Frieren did not ask if he loved her.

That was never part of the protocol.

Love was not a diagnostic test.

Mau answered the questions.

All consistent.

The Noise produced another term.

`CROSS-SYSTEM CONTENTION.`

Rimuru swore.

Mau almost laughed.

The danger felt real.

The room did not become chaos.

That difference contained two volumes of learning.
