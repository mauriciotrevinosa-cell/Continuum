# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 19 — Music After Dinner

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 19

Bocchi played because the guitar was already there.

No stage.

Someone from the second community joined.

Different tuning.

First attempt collided.

Both stopped.

Stared.

Then adjusted.

A child clapped wrong.

Milim clapped louder.

Worse.

Kita sang.

Momo knew half of something and invented rest.

Richeh laughed at Coco for knowing none of it and singing anyway.

Agott claimed she was not participating.

She stayed entire time.

Eren sat near doorway.

Not hidden.

Not central.

Mikasa two seats away.

No need to glue reunion into constant closeness.

Mau sat beside Frieren.

Did nothing.

That mattered.

Culture happened without him.

Bocchi shifted song.

Someone recognized rhythm from another world.

Another person didn't.

No one explained every reference.

World felt larger.

Halfway, Frieren leaned against Mau.

"Do you remember this?"

Memory Arc reflex.

Mau looked.

For half second, old panic.

Then room.

Present.

"It's happening."

Frieren smiled.

Across room Ori heard.

No intervention needed.

A question born from fear had become family language.

Bocchi heard too.

Changed chord.

Mau looked over.

She pretended not.

Music continued.

Eren watched.

He had expected:
fortified enclave.
political center.
power concentration.

It sounded like bad music.

That was harder to hate.

One song ended.

Silence.

Then applause.

Bocchi nearly died.

Kita caught her emotionally.

Second-community musician laughed.

"Again?"

Bocchi looked at everyone.

Too many eyes.

Then at room she had once asked to be built around sound.

Door.

Wall.

Corner.

Choice.

"Okay."

Second song.

Louder.

Outside rain hit roof.

Water systems handled.

Defense markers quiet.

Arrival House lamp stayed on.

No emergency.

The city had built all those systems so one evening could contain nothing more important than whether Bocchi played another song.

Mau listened.

The thought scared him because it made place worth losing.

Frieren squeezed his hand.

Not promise.

Present.

Eren noticed people around room:
fighters;
nonfighters;
refugees;
guests;
residents;
someone he knew would be called dangerous in another context.

All sharing terrible rhythm.

Maybe freedom looked less impressive than sea.

Maybe it sounded like this.

No one said.

They played until Fern told them people were sleeping.

Culture obeyed noise rules eventually.
