# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 19 — Music After Dinner

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 19

Bocchi played because the guitar was already there.

No stage.

Someone from the second community joined.

Different tuning.

They adjusted.

A child clapped wrong.

Milim clapped louder.

Worse.

Kita sang.

Momo knew half of something and invented the rest.

Richeh laughed at Coco for knowing none of it and singing anyway.

Mau sat beside Frieren.

Did nothing.

Culture happened without him.

Then Frieren leaned against him.

"Do you remember this?"

Memory Arc reflex.

Mau looked at her.

"It's happening."

Frieren smiled.

Across the room Ori heard.

No intervention needed.

A question born from fear had become family language.

Eren listened from near a doorway.

He had expected a fortified enclave.

It sounded like bad music.

That was harder to hate.
