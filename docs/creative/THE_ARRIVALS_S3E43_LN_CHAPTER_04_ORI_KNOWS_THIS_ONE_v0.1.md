## Chapter 10 — Ori Knows This One

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S3E43 — `Threshold`

Ori came back when Mau asked.

Not before.

That mattered to both of them.

The overload had shifted from pain to confusion.

Not autobiographical.

Spatial.

Mau looked at the room and said:

"The door is farther away."

It was not.

Ori sat where he could see her.

"How far?"

"Ten meters."

"Actual?"

"Three."

Mau closed his eyes.

"Okay."

She asked the anchor sequence.

Where are you?

Settlement medical room.

Who chose to stay with you?

Frieren.

Who left when you asked?

Ori.

Mau opened his eyes.

"You."

"Yes."

"Then came back."

"Because you asked."

"Yes."

Correct.

Ori did not become proof of reality.

She became one stable comparison point inside distorted perception.

That distinction protected both of them.

Mau looked at her.

"You know this one."

Ori nodded.

"Unfortunately."

Once she had wanted to be the person Mau chose over Frieren.

Now she was proud to help him stay connected to a world larger than her.

Growth hurt less when it had somewhere to go.
