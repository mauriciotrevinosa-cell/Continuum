# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 12 — It Passes

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E44 — `It Passes`

The overload lasted eleven hours.

No disappearance.

No rescue mission.

No cabin.

No river.

Mau slept for four hours afterward.

When he woke, Frieren was reading.

Same chair.

Different book.

"Did I leave?"

"No."

"Did I try?"

"No."

"Did I become weird?"

Frieren looked at him.

"More than usual."

Mau laughed.

Then checked.

"Do you remember it that way too?"

Frieren smiled.

"Yes."

The protocol had not cured the Noise.

It had prevented a threshold event from becoming a season of terror.

That difference mattered.

Rimuru updated procedures.

Maomao changed one monitoring interval.

Kaneki added a biological warning sign.

Senku marked a correlation provisional.

Ori removed one anchor question because it annoyed Mau too much.

Frieren added nothing.

Mau asked why.

"I know what you look like before you ask for help."

He looked at her.

"That's not measurable."

"No."

"Senku won't like it."

"I don't care."

Correct.
