# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 20 — The Old Inn Day

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 20

The next morning, a mixed group visited the inn.

Old residents.

New residents.

One person from the second community.

Eren.

He asked why.

Bocchi answered:

"Because this is where we became us."

Same sentence.

Still enough.

They repaired roof.

Cleaned.

Checked stored water.

No museum ceremony.

Eren walked through rooms.

"So you left."

Mikasa:

"Yes."

"And kept it."

"Yes."

"Why?"

Mau looked at the table.

"Leaving isn't the same as erasing."

Eren looked at him.

That line landed somewhere source memory did not explain.

They ate lunch there.

Then returned to settlement.

No one was trapped by honoring old home.

Another freedom lesson hidden in chores.
