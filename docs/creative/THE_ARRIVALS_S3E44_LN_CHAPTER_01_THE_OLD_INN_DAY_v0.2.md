# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 24 — The Old Inn Day

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 24

The next morning, a mixed group went to the old inn.

Not for nostalgia.

Roof maintenance.

Stored water check.

One window latch.

Inventory.

The fact that nostalgia happened anyway was nobody's fault.

Old residents:
Mau.
Frieren.
Bocchi.
Fern.

Newer residents:
Eren.
one Searcher.
one Arrival House volunteer.

One person from second community came because they were already on the route and wanted to see why people kept talking about a building that apparently refused to die.

The door stuck.

Mau pushed.

Nothing.

He pushed harder.

It opened with the exact unpleasant sound it had made for months.

Eren stared.

"This is the famous place?"

Mau:

"Unfortunately."

Bocchi:

"Yes."

Both answered at once.

Good.

Inside, no museum.

Dust.

Stored blankets.

Two guest rooms recently used.

A mug somebody had left on wrong shelf.

The old table.

That table still carried cuts, ink marks and one repaired edge.

Eren ran a finger over a scar in wood.

"What happened?"

Mau looked.

"No idea."

Frieren:

"You dropped a knife."

Mau:

"I did not."

Fern:

"You did."

Bocchi nodded.

Three witnesses.

Memory reconstruction, domestic edition.

Mau sighed.

"Fine."

Eren noticed.

After everything he'd heard about Mau's memory problems, correction happened without fear.

Ordinary.

Good.

They split work.

Roof first.

Mau climbed.

Frieren told him not to step on one board.

He stepped near it.

It cracked.

Frieren:

"I said—"

"I heard you."

"Then why?"

"Different board."

It was not.

Bocchi laughed so suddenly she nearly dropped nails.

No one died.

Progress.

The second-community visitor looked around.

"So you really left."

Mikasa had joined later and answered Eren before Mau could.

"Yes."

"And kept it."

"Yes."

Eren:

"Why?"

Mau sat on roof edge for a moment.

Looked down through doorway toward table.

"Because leaving isn't the same as erasing."

Eren looked up.

The sentence landed harder than Mau intended.

Source world:
walls.
island.
sea.
places abandoned.
places destroyed.
people impossible to return to.

Mikasa heard it too.

No one explained.

They kept working.

At lunch everyone crowded old table.

Too many chairs.

Exactly like early days.

Except not same people.

That mattered.

The second-community visitor asked:

"Do people need permission to stay here?"

Fern:

"Coordination. Not permission from Mau."

Mau pointed with bread.

"Important distinction."

"Who decides?"

"Whoever is handling inn capacity."

"Not historical residents?"

"No."

The visitor looked around.

"So this place is yours emotionally, but not socially."

Mau stopped chewing.

"That's... annoyingly accurate."

Good.

The inn had moved from private home to protected shared place.

History without private ownership of meaning.

After lunch, Eren found one old room with marks where furniture used to sit.

"You moved everything."

"Most."

"Did it feel wrong?"

Mau thought.

"Yes."

"Then why?"

"Because we needed somewhere bigger."

Simple.

Eren looked toward road back to settlement.

"And if city becomes wrong?"

Mau:

"We change it."

"If people don't want to?"

"Then we argue."

"That's not an answer."

"It's the one we have."

Mikasa stood in doorway.

Eren asked:

"Would you leave?"

Mikasa answered honestly.

"If I needed to."

No:
never.

No:
always.

Freedom.

They finished roof by afternoon.

The old inn looked almost exactly same from road.

Maintenance invisible.

Inside:
safer.

Before leaving, Bocchi paused at table.

Someone from newer generation had carved a tiny symbol under edge.

Not old memory.

New.

She smiled.

Mau saw.

"Do we remove it?"

Fern from doorway:

"Yes."

Everyone groaned.

Fern sighed.

"No."

Victory.

They walked back before dark.

No one was trapped by honoring old home.

No one had to pretend leaving it meant betrayal.

The old inn remained origin without becoming destination everyone must return to.

Another freedom lesson hidden in chores.
