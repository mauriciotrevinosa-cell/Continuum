# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 21 — Mau's Settlement

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 21

A trader called it:

"Mau's settlement."

Mau corrected:

"No."

The trader laughed.

"Whatever you call it."

"We don't."

The trader looked around.

Arrival House.

Water court.

Workshops.

Refuge bunks.

Defense markers.

People.

"Then everyone else names it for you."

True.

Outside names:
Mau's settlement.
Otherworlder city.
Refuge.
Fort.
Monster town.
Rimuru's place.

Each carried politics.

The coordinating table discussed an official name.

Failed.

Milim suggested hers.

Diablo suggested Rimuru.

Rimuru ended the session.

Later Mau heard:

"If something goes wrong there, Mau handles it."

Frieren heard too.

Mau joked.

Frieren did not laugh.

The naming problem was becoming a sacrifice problem.
