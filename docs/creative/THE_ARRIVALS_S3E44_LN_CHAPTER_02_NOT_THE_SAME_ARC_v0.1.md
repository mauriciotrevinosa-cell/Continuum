Mau expected recovery to feel profound.

It felt boring.

Good.

Breakfast.

Medication Maomao insisted was not optional.

No Construction work.

Light planning.

A walk.

People did not surround him.

That was the protocol too.

After Memory Arc, care had briefly become surveillance.

They had learned from that.

Mau spent part of the afternoon alone.

Actually alone.

Frieren knew.

Ori knew.

Nobody followed.

He sat near the water court.

Listened.

People used infrastructure.

Argued.

Lived.

The Noise remained inside him.

Not resolved.

Not gone.

Not the story's only problem.

Mau touched the blue thread from his date with Frieren.

Still carried it sometimes.

Present evidence.

Then put it away.

He did not need to prove the day again.

Not today.
