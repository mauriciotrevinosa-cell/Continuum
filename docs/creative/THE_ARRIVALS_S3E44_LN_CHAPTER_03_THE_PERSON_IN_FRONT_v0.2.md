# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 22 — The Person in Front

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 22

Language shifted.

"If things get bad, find Mau."

"Mau knows weird system things."

"Mau survived Sukuna."

"He'll be at the front."

Compliments.

Dangerous.

A new resident asked where to go during highest alarm.

Someone answered:

"Find Mau."

Frieren turned.

"So he can do what?"

Silence.

No one knew.

Habit had volunteered him.

Mau made a joke.

"Probably get lost too."

People laughed.

Frieren waited until later.

"Why did you make that easier?"

"They were embarrassed."

"That doesn't answer."

Mau looked away.

Frieren said:

"Do not make being volunteered for danger socially comfortable."

Sentence worth keeping.

Mau did.
