# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 27 — The Person in Front

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 27

Language shifted slowly enough that nobody could identify first sentence.

"If things get bad, find Mau."

"Mau knows weird system things."

"Mau survived Sukuna."

"He'll be at the front."

Compliments.

Mostly.

That made them dangerous.

A defense orientation for new residents exposed it.

Mikasa showed alarm levels.

Yellow:
prepare.

Orange:
move vulnerable people toward refuge points.

Red:
follow assigned routes.

Someone raised hand.

"At highest alarm, where do we go if route fails?"

Before Mikasa answered, another resident said:

"Find Mau."

Several people nodded.

Frieren turned.

"So he can do what?"

Silence.

Nobody knew.

One person tried:

"He usually knows."

"Knows what?"

"How to..."

No answer.

Habit had volunteered him.

Mau felt room tighten.

People embarrassed.

He made a joke.

"Probably get lost too."

Laughter.

Tension broke.

Mikasa corrected process.

If route fails:
nearest secondary marker.
designated mobile guide.
not Mau.

Orientation continued.

Later Frieren cornered him outside.

"Why did you do that?"

"Do what?"

"Make it easier."

"They were embarrassed."

"That doesn't answer."

Mau looked away.

Frieren:

"Do not make being volunteered for danger socially comfortable."

He went still.

Sentence sharp enough to keep.

"I wasn't volunteering."

"You were forgiving the assumption before anyone had to examine it."

Mau frowned.

"That's not same."

"It can become same."

He wanted to argue.

Then remembered Sukuna.

Memory Arc.

Times people had learned:
Mau will go.

Because Mau always went.

The community had learned from evidence.

That was not their fault.

But habit could become expectation.

Expectation could become entitlement.

Mau sighed.

"Okay."

Frieren:

"Okay what?"

"Next time I won't joke."

"Good."

Next time arrived two days later.

A Searcher route discussion.

One dangerous anomaly.

Someone said:

"Mau can check it."

Mau asked:

"Why me?"

Room paused.

Good.

"Because Construction?"

"Does that help here?"

Unknown.

"Because Noise?"

"Do we know that helps?"

No.

"Because you can fight?"

Others can.

The suggestion collapsed under questions.

Team became:
Qifrey for anomaly magic;
Kaneki for biological signs;
Searcher guide;
Mikasa.

No Mau.

Mau felt:
relief.
hurt.
pride.
worry.

All.

Frieren said nothing.

He did not joke.

Later, a different problem actually needed him.

Unknown system interaction matching something only he had seen.

Team asked.

Mau went.

Voluntary.

Specific.

Not ritual.

Difference.

At dinner, one resident apologized.

"I think I assumed you'd always be the one."

Mau looked.

"Sometimes I will be."

"That makes it confusing."

"Yeah."

No perfect boundary.

The goal was not removing Mau from front.

It was removing:
of course.

A city could ask courage.

It could not quietly turn one person's history of stepping forward into public infrastructure.

That distinction would become brutally important soon.
