# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 14 — The Voice Around Ori

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S3E45 — `The Voice`

Ori glitched during dinner.

That happened sometimes.

Color.

Edges.

A fraction of a second where her body seemed uncertain what rules it preferred.

Usually it passed.

This time the room went quiet with her.

Not socially.

Physically.

A spoon stopped before hitting the floor.

Steam stopped above soup.

Sound disappeared.

Ori's eyes widened.

She had not done it.

A voice came from near her.

Not her mouth.

Not exactly the room.

Mau knew it before memory could interfere.

The same presence he did not remember meeting.

His body did.

Ori grabbed the table.

"No."

The voice continued.

One word.

`Soon.`

Then sound returned.

The spoon hit the floor.

Steam moved.

Milim stood.

Diablo moved.

Frieren had her staff.

Rimuru was already checking Raphael.

Ori shook.

"I didn't say that."

Mau went to her.

"I know."

She looked at him.

"Do you?"

"Yes."

For once certainty was immediate.

Not because he understood the goddess.

Because he understood Ori.
