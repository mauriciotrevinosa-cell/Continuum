# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 15 — She Is Not Your Mouth

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E45 — `The Voice`

The second intrusion happened outside.

Ori was prepared.

That did not help.

Stillness spread around her.

The voice formed.

`Child—`

Ori shouted:

"No."

The word cracked through the stillness.

Impossible.

The voice paused.

Frieren stepped between Ori and empty air.

"She is not your mouth."

No response.

Mau stood beside Ori.

Not in front.

Important.

Rimuru tried to analyze.

Raphael returned only bounded anomalies.

No source.

No control path.

No evidence Ori had consented.

The voice came again.

`She is mine.`

Ori went white.

Mau answered:

"No."

Ori grabbed his sleeve.

Then corrected him.

"I answer."

Mau stopped.

Ori looked into the still air.

"I came from you."

Her voice shook.

"I am not yours."

The stillness ended.

No explosion.

No punishment.

That was almost worse.

The goddess had heard.

Ori remained standing.

Afterward Mau apologized.

"For what?"

"Answering first."

Ori leaned against him.

"You stopped."

Small lesson.

Large stakes.
