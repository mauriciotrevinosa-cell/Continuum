# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 16 — Stillness

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S3E46 — `Trial`

Stillness came to the whole settlement at noon.

This time everyone saw.

Water froze mid-channel.

Not ice.

Motion stopped.

Birds held in air.

People could move.

Some.

Not all.

The world outside the settlement boundary dimmed.

Not gone.

Unavailable.

Raphael reported no ordinary barrier.

Qifrey found no circle.

Senku found no measurable transition point.

Diablo tried to step beyond the affected zone.

Returned to the same spot.

Milim tried upward.

Same result.

No damage.

No progress.

Then a surface appeared above the central court.

Not screen.

Not sky.

Something between.

Words formed.

`TRIAL DOMAIN ASSERTED.`

Mau's stomach dropped.

Frieren took his hand.

The next line appeared.

`PARTICIPANT: MAU.`

Nobody liked that.

The final line took longer.

`REFUSAL CONDITION: SETTLEMENT PROTECTION WITHDRAWN.`

Not death stated directly.

Worse.

The layered defenses began shutting down one by one.

Alarm beacons.

Barriers.

Imported protections.

Not destroyed.

Suspended.

The family had learned not to accept false binaries.

So they refused anyway.
