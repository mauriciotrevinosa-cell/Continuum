# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 17 — Participate or Lose Them

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S3E46 — `Trial`

Rimuru said no.

Frieren said no.

Ori said no first.

Mau said nothing.

Good.

Milim attacked the boundary.

Nothing.

Diablo tried a different framework.

Nothing.

Qifrey looked for rule-writing.

Senku looked for physical inconsistency.

Eren asked whether anyone had actually confirmed the settlement would die without the defenses.

Good question.

Then the outer warning posts showed movement.

A monster group already in the region.

Normally manageable.

With defenses suspended and movement locked inside the Trial boundary, not manageable enough.

The condition was not a hypothetical.

The goddess had timed it.

That made everyone angrier.

Mau looked at the people around him.

Old instinct:

`Agree.`

He did not.

"Options."

One word.

Progress.

They tried seven.

None worked.

Not because everyone was weak.

Because the Trial domain controlled the conditions it had asserted.

Finally the voice returned.

`PARTICIPATE.`

Frieren gripped Mau's hand.

"We're still looking."

Mau nodded.

The monster group crossed the first dead warning line.

Mau looked at Rimuru.

Then Frieren.

Then Ori.

"I participate."

Frieren's face broke.

Mau continued before anyone could misread it.

"Because we checked."

Not alone.

Not immediately.

Not secret.

Different from Sukuna.

Still terrible.

The ground vanished under him.

Volume Sixteen ended with Mau falling into a world no one else could follow.
