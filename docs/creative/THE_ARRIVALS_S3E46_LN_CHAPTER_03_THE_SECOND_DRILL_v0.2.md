The second drill went better.

That exposed different problems.

Too much compliance.

People waited for instructions even when obvious routes were blocked.

Mikasa hated that.

"Use judgment."

A resident answered:

"Then why have a plan?"

"Because judgment needs a starting point."

Eren listened.

Useful tension.

The Last Defense positions were explained.

Not heroic title.

Map language.

If outer observation failed:
fall back.

If mobile line failed:
move civilians inward.

If inner routes failed:
protect people who could not move farther and the infrastructure they needed to survive.

Bocchi asked:

"What do I do?"

Not fighter.

Still role.

Calm-space support.

Children.

Communication.

Kita with her.

Rem medical/logistics.

Kaneki biological emergency.

Aira rapid runner/extraction.

Nobody became useless because they were not front-line combat.

That was the actual purpose of the drill.
