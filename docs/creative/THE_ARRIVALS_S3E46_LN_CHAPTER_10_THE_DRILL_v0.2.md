# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 10 — The Drill

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 10

The first full evacuation drill failed in six ways before breakfast.

Excellent.

Mikasa did not look pleased.

Alarm level two sounded.

Not loud enough.

Half the workshops heard.

The washing court did not.

Failure one.

Coco wrote that down.

A guest at Arrival House followed an outdated sign toward a path removed during Senku redesign.

Failure two.

Wakana tore the sign down with personal anger.

Children thought the alarm meant a game.

Three of them ran toward the observation platform because it looked exciting.

Failure three.

Milim arrived at the wrong response point.

Not because she ignored orders.

Because nobody had explained "mobile reserve" in a way that survived Milim interpretation.

"So I go where the biggest problem is."

Mikasa:

"No."

"That is what mobile means."

"Not by yourself."

Milim looked betrayed by language.

Failure four.

Diablo skipped a route entirely because he could cross terrain ordinary people could not.

His section looked wonderfully fast on paper.

Then Mikasa made him repeat at walking speed.

It failed.

Failure five.

Rimuru attempted to coordinate every subsystem at once.

Arrival House.

Outer watch.

Medical.

Water.

Searchers.

Milim.

Diablo.

He froze for half a second.

Ciel supplied more information.

That made the bottleneck worse.

Rimuru stopped.

Delegated.

Failure six discovered.

One person cannot be the city.

Not even Rimuru.

Mau's card read:

INNER MOBILE RESPONSE.

Underneath, in Maomao's handwriting:

*Inner. Not one step further. — M.*

He stared.

Not front.

Not "wherever danger looks worst."

Inner junction.

Support failed routes.

Respond to abnormal system interactions if requested.

Frieren looked over his shoulder.

Approved.

Mau pretended not to notice.

Drill started again from halfway.

This time Mau heard alarm and moved toward inner junction.

Outer marker flashed.

His body wanted to turn.

He did not.

Someone else handled it.

That was part of exercise too.

At refuge point Bocchi asked:

"What do I do?"

She hated standing in a defensive briefing while fighters received obvious tasks.

Mikasa showed her card.

CALM SPACE / CHILDREN / COMMUNICATION SUPPORT.

Bocchi stared.

"That counts?"

Kita:

"Obviously."

Bocchi did not think obviously.

During drill, one child cried.

Bocchi froze.

Then sat nearby and played three repeated notes.

Not song.

Pattern.

Breathing slowed.

Kita handled talking.

Useful.

Rem ran supplies.

Aira carried route messages where markers failed.

Kaneki stayed near medical because smell and biological sensing could identify injuries before visual triage caught them.

Wakana handled cloth barriers and route marking.

Umaru somehow became blanket logistics.

She complained.

Still efficient.

At old inn, alarm arrived late.

Separate signal protocol had not been tested.

Failure seven.

Nobody complained number exceeded six.

Good.

By noon everyone was irritated.

Mikasa gathered notes.

No inspirational speech.

"Again next week."

Collective groan.

Senku:

"Successful."

Aira:

"How?"

"We discovered failures without corpses."

Hard to argue.

Fourteen arguments.

No injuries.

Seven system defects.

One crucial emotional result:

when people asked where to go, nobody answered:

Find Mau.

They answered with routes.

The city had practiced surviving without turning one person into its emergency plan.

That was the real drill.
