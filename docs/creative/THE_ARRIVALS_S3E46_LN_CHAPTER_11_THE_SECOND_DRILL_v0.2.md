The second drill went better.

Mikasa hated it.

Alarm.

Routes activated.

Signs worked.

Milim went to actual assigned reserve.

Excellent.

Then Mikasa blocked one corridor without telling anyone why.

People stopped.

Waited.

And waited.

Mikasa stared.

"Move."

A resident pointed at sign.

"It says this route."

"Route is blocked."

"Then what?"

"Use judgment."

The resident looked offended.

"Then why have a plan?"

Eren, standing near secondary marker, looked at Mikasa.

Good question.

Mikasa answered:

"Because judgment needs a starting point."

Not:
orders replace thinking.

The exercise changed.

Mikasa created failures deliberately.

One beacon off.

One corridor blocked.

One message delayed.

One team lead "injured."

People had to choose.

Some chose badly.

Good.

A guest tried returning to room for bag.

Arrival House staff stopped them.

Then staff asked:
what if bag contains medicine?

New exception.

Not all personal property equal.

An old resident refused nearest refuge because their partner assigned elsewhere.

No rule covered.

They created reunification procedure.

Not:
everyone run against flow.

Message point.

Name.

Location.

Someone searches when safe.

Painful.

Necessary.

The Last Defense positions were explained after lunch.

Not heroic title.

Map language.

If outer observation fails:
fall back.

If mobile response cannot hold:
do not die proving it.

If evacuation routes fail:
secondary corridors.

If people cannot move farther:
Last Defense protects those people and infrastructure they need to remain alive.

Eren looked at map.

"Last defense sounds like no retreat."

Mikasa:

"It means civilians cannot retreat farther."

Important.

Fighters could rotate.

Fallback still existed inside.

No glorious death line.

Mau's role remained inner mobile.

He asked:

"What if outer line needs me?"

Mikasa:

"They request you if your capabilities matter."

"And if they don't have time?"

"Then the lead there decides with what they have."

Mau hated that.

Accepted.

Second drill tested communication loss.

Physical boards.

Magic.

Redundancy.

No perfect network.

Bocchi's calm space did better.

One child asked why she played same notes.

Bocchi:

"Because I know them."

Enough.

Umaru had food and blankets arranged before people arrived.

She looked smug.

Deserved.

At debrief, Senku asked:

"What failed?"

People listed less.

Then Mikasa:

"Who waited for permission?"

Too many hands.

Eren looked at her.

She knew.

Third drill not scheduled yet.
