# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 1 — Zero

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E47 — `The Trial`

Mau landed standing.

That was the first impossible thing.

The second was seeing the settlement below him while also knowing he was no longer inside it.

A copy.

No.

A representation.

No word fit.

The goddess stood nowhere visible.

Her voice came from the domain.

Outside, everyone remained in the frozen central court.

They could see Mau through the suspended surface above them.

He could not see them.

Milim moved first.

Of course.

She struck the boundary.

The domain answered.

Not with force.

With information.

A line appeared beside Milim.

`RELEVANT TRIAL-DOMAIN OUTPUT: 0.`

Milim stopped in mid-motion.

Not paralyzed.

Power absent.

For one heartbeat, the strongest thing she attempted to access simply was not available.

Her eyes widened.

Then the line changed.

`RESTORED.`

Power returned.

Milim nearly overcorrected.

Rimuru caught her.

The goddess spoke.

`PROOF COMPLETE.`

Diablo's face became terrifying.

Senku's became focused.

Frieren's became nothing at all.

Inside the Trial, Mau understood the message.

Not:

`I am omnipotent.`

Specific.

`Inside this domain, these are my conditions.`

That was bad enough.

Mau looked at the false settlement.

"Then state them."

The goddess sounded pleased.

He hated that.
