# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 2 — The Trial Domain

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Source episode:** S3E47 — `The Trial`

The rules appeared one at a time.

`SCENARIOS ARE CONSTRUCTED WITHIN TRIAL DOMAIN.`

`EXTERNAL RESIDENTS ARE NOT PHYSICALLY TRANSFERRED INTO SCENARIOS.`

Mau exhaled.

Then stopped.

The next line:

`PARTICIPANT WILL NOT BE INFORMED DURING SCENARIO WHETHER A PERSON IS CONSTRUCTED OR EXTERNAL.`

"That's contradictory."

`IT IS PRECISE.`

Mau hated her more.

The goddess continued.

`DEATH OF PARTICIPANT WITHIN TRIAL DOMAIN RETURNS PARTICIPANT TO TRIAL ANCHOR.`

Mau stared.

"How many times?"

No answer.

"Do I keep memories?"

`YES.`

"Do they?"

`CONSTRUCTS RESET AS REQUIRED.`

Outside observers saw the text.

Frieren's hand tightened around nothing.

The final operational rule appeared.

`IN EACH SCENARIO, LOSS WILL OCCUR.`

`PARTICIPANT MAY ACCEPT PERSONAL LOSS OR ALLOW DESIGNATED OTHER LOSS.`

Mau understood too quickly.

"No."

`BEGIN.`

The false settlement vanished.

Mau stood in a road.

Frieren was bleeding ten meters away.

She looked real.

That was the point.
