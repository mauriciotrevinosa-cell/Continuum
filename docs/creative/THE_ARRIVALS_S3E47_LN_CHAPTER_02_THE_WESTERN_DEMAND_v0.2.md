# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 5 — The Western Demand

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 5

The demand arrived on paper.

Submit unregistered Otherworlders for temporary relocation and capability assessment.

The same sentence.

Again.

Different authority.

The messenger looked embarrassed.

Good.

Not villain.

Anko asked who authored it.

The messenger did not know.

Packet from regional office.

Who instructed that office?

Unknown.

The main settlement refused blanket transfer.

Offered:
- individual allegations;
- documented warrants;
- inspection delegation under agreed rules;
- neutral observers.

No response immediately.

The second community received a similar demand.

They refused separately.

Important.

Not one central command.

Two communities reaching parallel conclusions.

The unity message had become part of the justification against them.

Exactly the feared consequence.

No one withdrew it.

That mattered too.
