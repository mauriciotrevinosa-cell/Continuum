## Chapter 23 — The Western Demand

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 23

The demand arrived on paper.

That somehow made it worse.

A mounted messenger came under white cloth.

Mikasa allowed approach.

Anko received.

The messenger looked embarrassed before anyone read.

Good.

Not villain.

The text:

> Submit all unregistered Otherworlders for temporary relocation and capability assessment.

Same sentence.

Again.

Different authority.

Same rhythm.

Same euphemism.

Temporary relocation.

No duration.

Capability assessment.

No limits.

All unregistered.

No named crimes.

Anko looked up.

"Who authored this?"

"Regional office."

"Which person?"

The messenger did not know.

"Who instructed the office?"

"I don't know."

"Who defined assessment?"

Silence.

The messenger swallowed.

"I carry notices."

Not enemy.

Anko softened tone.

"Did you receive a packet?"

"Yes."

"Map?"

"Yes."

She asked to see copy.

Messenger hesitated.

Allowed.

Same wrong river bend.

There.

Pattern Room gained another line.

Not proof of mastermind.

Proof demand and earlier packets likely shared source material.

Council met immediately.

Not closed.

Second-community observer attended remotely through messenger exchange? No magic call. Their own courier arrived later with parallel document.

Same demand.

Different addressee.

They had received it separately.

Important.

No central command.

The two communities considered separately.

Main settlement response:

No blanket transfer.

Provide named allegations.

Documented warrants.

Defined process.

Inspection delegation possible under agreed rules.

Neutral observers possible.

No involuntary mass relocation.

Second community sent its own response:

No blanket transfer.

Their language shorter.

More hostile.

Independent.

Momo:

"I like theirs better."

Fern:

"Not relevant."

The unity statement was cited in western demand as evidence of coordinated political organization.

Exactly feared consequence.

Someone at council said:

"We should never have sent it."

Silence.

Reasonable regret.

Anko:

"Maybe."

No defensive pride.

Then second-community representative's reply arrived:

> We do not withdraw our signature because someone chose to misuse it.

That settled enough.

Mau read western demand twice.

"They want people because of what they might be able to do."

Eren looked at him.

Familiar.

Mau noticed.

"Yeah."

Source-future debate scaled to populations.

Capability as pre-crime.

Danger.

The messenger waited for reply.

Mau offered food.

They accepted.

One resident objected.

"They're carrying threat."

Mau:

"They're carrying paper."

Difference.

The messenger ate under watch.

Left before dark.

No insult.

No detention.

No dramatic challenge.

Anko watched road.

"What happens if they reject counteroffer?"

Mikasa:

"We prepare."

Rimuru:

"And keep talking if they keep talking."

No response that night.

Instead, west road reports stopped entirely.

Then the sensors started.
