ANIMAL-LIKE / LARGE / MULTIPLE.

UNKNOWN / FAST.

Gone.

Three directions.

"Current guests first."

"Role?"

"Inner mobile."

Not front.

Mixed.

Militia insignia.

Officials.

Unknown.

Could be pressure.

Deer.

Livestock.

Small monsters.

From something.

Open movement lane away from housing.

"Can I scare them farther?"

"Gently."

Large.

Deliberate.

Every instinct:
go.

Inner mobile.

"North line says monsters confirmed."

"Not yet."

She kept running.

Good.

Not yet.

DEMAND REPEATED.
