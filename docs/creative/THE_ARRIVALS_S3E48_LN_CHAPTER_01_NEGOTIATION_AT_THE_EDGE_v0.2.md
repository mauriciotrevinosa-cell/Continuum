# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 7 — Negotiation at the Edge

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 7

Anko went west.

Himmel.

Native representative.

No Diablo visible.

No Milim.

Deliberate.

The western group was mixed.

Local militia.

Hired fighters.

Officials.

Frightened civilians behind them.

No clean enemy army.

Their captain repeated the demand.

Anko asked:

"Specific names?"

"No."

"Specific crimes?"

"Capability risk."

"That's not a crime."

The captain knew.

That was visible.

Orders above.

Fear below.

Negotiation stretched.

Meanwhile the northwest animals kept running.

Then the first actual monster signal appeared behind them.

Large.

Multiple.

The captain looked north.

Anko looked too.

Two problems converging.

Maybe planned.

Maybe not.

Nobody had time to prove it before survival mattered.
