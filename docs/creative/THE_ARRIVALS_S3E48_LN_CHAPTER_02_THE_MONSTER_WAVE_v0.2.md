## Chapter 8 — The Monster Wave

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 8

The monster wave hit the northern observation line first.

Not one giant boss.

Worse.

Many creatures displaced from farther northwest.

Something had driven them.

Searchers on observation fell back.

Correctly.

Milim wanted to meet the largest group head-on.

Mikasa redirected her farther from housing.

"Do not erase geography."

Milim looked offended.

Then obeyed.

Growth.

Diablo moved west when negotiation failed.

Not to massacre.

Contain.

Disarm.

Create retreat lanes.

Frieren moved where pressure rose.

Yuta held mobile reserve.

Rimuru coordinated.

Immediately discovered coordination at settlement scale was too large for one mind even with Raphael.

He delegated.

Function leads took over sectors.

Good.

The defense system began doing what it had been designed to do:

replace heroics with structure.
