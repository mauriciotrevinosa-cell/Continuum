# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 8 — The Monster Wave

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 8

The monster wave hit the northern observation line first.

Not one giant boss.

Worse.

Many.

Different species.

Different sizes.

Some normally hostile to each other.

All moving the same direction.

Away from something farther northwest.

That mattered.

This was displacement before invasion.

Searchers on outer observation did exactly what drill demanded.

They fell back.

No heroic stand.

No attempt to prove line could hold.

Markers changed.

First bell.

Second.

North route closed.

Secondary evacuation activated.

Milim reached mobile reserve point.

For three seconds.

Then saw largest creature.

"I can stop that."

Mikasa:

"Where?"

Milim pointed directly along line toward housing.

"No."

"What?"

"Redirect."

"That is slower."

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because if you erase half the hill, we still live under the hill."

Milim looked offended by terrain constraints.

Then grinned.

"Fine."

She moved wide.

Hit ground far north hard enough to change creature direction without collapsing settlement-facing slope.

Power used as steering, not deletion.

Good.

Frieren took eastern side where flying creatures broke formation.

Precision.

No spectacular waste.

Yuta held mobile reserve with medics close.

Maki rotated through breaches instead of chasing kills.

Himmel covered retreat.

Diablo moved west.

Not massacre.

Containment.

Disarm when surrender.

Disable when not.

Create retreat lanes for militia who no longer wanted fight.

Rimuru stood over central map.

Raphael fed:
positions.
alarms.
water.
people.
energy spikes.
western negotiation.
medical capacity.

Too much.

For one moment Rimuru tried to keep all of it.

Then remembered first drill.

"Sector leads."

He delegated.

Mikasa:
defense movement.

Fern:
civilian/refuge flow.

Maomao/Yuta:
medical.

Senku/Qifrey/Coco:
infrastructure.

Searchers:
routes.

Anko:
west contact.

Rimuru:
cross-system coordination only.

The map became less his.

Better.

At inner junction, Mau heard requests.

North:
not needed.

West:
not needed.

Water:
stand by.

Aira sprinted through.

"Inner route three blocked by cart."

Mau:

"I can move it."

"Do."

Specific.

He went.

Not front.

A cart axle had broken across corridor.

Three people already trying.

Mau used Construction-assisted leverage to stabilize rather than brute destroy.

Wakana got wheel clear.

Route reopened.

Then Mau returned to inner junction.

That mattered.

No mission creep.

Okarun went past the other way.

Faster than anything on two legs had a right to be.

A wounded Searcher over his shoulder.

Turbo Granny shrieking directions in his ear.

Medical, then north again.

Nobody had assigned him a number of trips.

He was not counting.

Ori relayed message:

"North creatures are turning east."

Mau looked at map.

"Toward safe corridor?"

"Maybe."

Second community.

Independent.

They had their own warnings.

Searchers sent signal.

No assumption:
main settlement commands them.

A reply came:
received / sheltering locally / no assistance requested yet.

Good.

Then one creature broke past northern line.

Alarm changed.

Aira:

"Need you now."

Mau moved.

The system requested him.

Different.

He met creature near inner agricultural edge.

Not alone.

Maki already there.

Kaneki on flank.

Mau did not turn it into solo duel.

They contained.

Killed only when necessary.

Behind them, civilians moved.

That was what mattered.

The defense system began doing exactly what it had been designed to do:

replace the fantasy of one hero with enough structure that powerful people could be useful without becoming the whole plan.
