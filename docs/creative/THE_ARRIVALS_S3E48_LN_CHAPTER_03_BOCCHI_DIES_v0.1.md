# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 6 — Bocchi Dies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Source episode:** S3E48 — `Again`

Bocchi was not in a battle.

That made it worse.

A building fire.

Smoke.

One exit blocked.

Mau on one side.

Bocchi on the other.

A mechanism would open one route and seal the other.

Bocchi understood before Mau did.

"Open yours."

"No."

"Mau."

"No."

She started crying.

Not because she wanted to die.

Because she knew him.

"If you do the thing—"

"What thing?"

"The thing where you decide you're easier."

Mau froze.

The construct had learned family language.

Bocchi hit the barrier between them.

"I'm not letting you call this love."

Mau's hand shook over the control.

Time.

Smoke.

Bocchi coughed.

Mau did not choose himself quickly enough.

The mechanism sealed.

Bocchi disappeared into smoke.

Again the Trial delayed reset.

Mau screamed at the goddess.

The anchor returned.

Outside, Bocchi had both hands over her mouth.

Kita held her.

The goddess asked:

`WOULD YOU HAVE OBEYED HER?`

Mau looked sick.

"No."

That answer hurt Bocchi more than the scenario.
