## Chapter 7 — Ori Dies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Source episode:** S3E48 — `Again`

Ori's scenario had no monster.

Only a choice.

A door for Mau.

A door for Ori.

One could open.

Ori looked at the two doors.

Then laughed.

Of course.

The goddess's daughter.

Creation.

Extension.

Person.

The Trial aimed directly at the oldest wound.

Ori looked at Mau.

"Choose yours."

"No."

"You're very predictable."

"You're not dying."

"Neither are you."

"Pick yours."

Ori walked to Mau's door.

Pressed her hand against it.

"If you choose my door, you're deciding my life for me."

Mau stopped.

The late-S3 lesson arrived too early.

He did not understand it yet.

Ori smiled.

"Let me choose."

She opened Mau's door.

Her own vanished.

Mau survived.

Ori died.

The Trial reset.

Mau came back furious.

"That wasn't my choice."

The goddess answered:

`CORRECT.`

Mau went still.

For the first time, the Trial had forced him to survive because someone else chose him.

He hated it.

That mattered more than dying.
