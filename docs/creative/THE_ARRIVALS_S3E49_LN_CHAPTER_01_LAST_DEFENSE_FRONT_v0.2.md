# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 10 — Last Defense Front

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 10

The western line broke first.

Not collapsed.

Bent.

Some militia attacked.

Some refused.

Some retreated when monsters reached them too.

Nobody could later agree who fired the first spell.

Last Defense activated when two evacuation routes became unreliable simultaneously.

Not because every outer line failed.

Because people who could not move farther needed a protected endpoint.

Inside:
- injured;
- children;
- elderly;
- a pregnant resident;
- two people whose powers reacted dangerously to panic;
- guests who had arrived that morning;
- the sanctuary collaborator;
- the resident who hated him.

Same room.

No symbolism requested.

Reality did it anyway.

The harmed resident told the collaborator:

"If you run—"

"I can't."

"Good."

They held opposite sides of a support during an impact.

Not forgiveness.

Function.

The Last Defense Front was a room full of people keeping each other alive while stronger people fought somewhere else.
