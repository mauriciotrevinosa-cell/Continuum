# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 8 — The Number Stops Helping

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Source episode:** S3E49 — `Beyond Counting`

Mau counted.

Twenty-one.

Twenty-two.

Twenty-three.

At thirty, he started marking the anchor floor.

At forty, the marks disappeared.

He counted in his head.

At sixty-something, the goddess changed scenario rhythm.

Sometimes Mau died in seconds.

Sometimes he lived hours.

Sometimes the designated other was someone he loved.

Sometimes someone he barely knew.

Sometimes an enemy under sanctuary.

Sometimes a stranger.

Sometimes the choice was physical.

Sometimes social.

Sometimes he could save himself only by ordering someone else into danger.

Sometimes the other person begged him to let them choose.

The number became useless.

Was sleep part of a loop?

Did long scenarios count as one?

Had he repeated one?

He stopped knowing.

Outside, the domain no longer showed every scenario.

Only fragments.

Enough.

Mau dying.

Mau surviving and breaking.

Mau arguing.

Mau refusing.

The family lost count too.

Senku tried.

Then stopped.

Not because numbers did not matter.

Because this number no longer helped them intervene.

The Trial wanted accumulation, not arithmetic.
