## Chapter 9 — A Different Door

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E49 — `Beyond Counting`

One scenario gave Mau no sacrificial option.

That frightened him most.

Frieren and Mau stood before one door.

The condition:

`ONE LEAVES.`

No death stated.

No punishment.

Just one.

Frieren said:

"I'll stay."

Mau said:

"No."

"Why?"

"Because."

"That's not an answer."

"It is today."

She stared.

Then walked through the door before he could stop her.

Mau lunged.

Too late.

The door closed.

He survived.

Anchor.

Another scenario.

Rimuru chose Mau.

Anchor.

Another.

Bocchi chose Mau.

Another.

Ori.

Another.

Yuta.

Another.

People kept making the decision he refused to imagine them making.

The goddess was no longer testing whether Mau would die.

She was testing whether he could survive being selected by someone else.

He failed almost every version.

Sometimes survival itself became the punishment.
