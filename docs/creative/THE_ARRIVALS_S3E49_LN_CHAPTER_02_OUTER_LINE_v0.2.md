## Chapter 20 — Outer Line

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2

The outer line looked terrible.

Mud.

Smoke.

Short commands.

People retreating on purpose and looking like they were losing because retreat never looked heroic from far away.

Mikasa preferred it that way.

Heroic lines got people killed.

"Marker four. Back."

A Searcher repeated the command.

"Marker four!"

Three defenders moved.

One did not.

Maki grabbed the back of his coat and dragged him two steps before the creature's forelimb came down where he had been standing.

He looked at her.

"I had it."

"No."

Maki left before he could argue.

Good.

On the western approach, Himmel pulled three militia fighters away from a monster.

The same three had arrived under a demand that Otherworlders surrender for "assessment."

One stared at him after they reached cover.

"Why?"

Himmel looked genuinely confused by the question.

"You're there."

Enough.

The fighter opened his mouth.

Closed it.

No conversion.

No speech.

He picked up his weapon and turned north.

Diablo handled surrender differently.

A militia squad dropped weapons.

Diablo stopped advancing.

Another did not.

He crossed the distance before their second volley.

Weapons left hands.

Bodies stayed attached to owners.

Mostly.

Anko's voice cut over relay.

"DIABLO."

He looked at the man currently suspended by his collar.

"I am exercising restraint."

"Use more."

He set the man down.

The man ran.

Reasonable.

Frieren held the eastern sky.

Flying creatures had learned that the streets below contained movement.

She did not erase them in one bright display.

Too much debris.

Too much risk.

One shot.

Wing joint.

Second.

Turn.

Third.

Ground outside housing.

Precision as mercy.

Not necessarily mercy toward the creature.

Toward everyone under it.

Yuta moved between field treatment and reserve.

He wanted the line.

Maomao wanted him where he could heal the people the line produced.

He hated that she was right.

Then a breach opened near north orchard.

Yuta looked at the map.

Mikasa:

"Reserve request."

There.

Specific.

He went.

No mission creep.

Milim was farther west than anyone comfortable with scale wanted her to be.

The largest creature had tried to turn toward roofs.

Milim hit the ground to its left.

Not the creature.

Ground.

The impact made it change direction.

Again.

A moving wall of force without erasing the hill.

She laughed once.

Then remembered why everyone had yelled at her during drills.

"Oh."

Rimuru's voice through relay:

"That 'oh' scares me."

"I'm doing it right!"

"Keep doing it right."

"BORING."

But she did.

Eren worked one barrier line under Mikasa's requests.

No command aura.

No wall mythology.

Lift this.

Brace that.

Move when told.

A militia fighter recognized him only as another frightening Otherworlder.

Good.

Source history did not arrive as public briefing material.

At east edge, a Searcher team found a family from the safe corridor moving the wrong direction.

"Main refuge is full."

The father panicked.

"Then where?"

"Secondary."

"We were told central."

"Changed."

The father looked toward smoke.

Then at the Searcher.

"Are you sure?"

"No."

Bad answer.

The Searcher corrected.

"I'm sure secondary is the route we have now."

Better.

They moved.

The second community sent warning support from its own perimeter.

Not fighters emptied into Mau's settlement.

Information.

Two scouts.

A cart of treated water.

Independent cooperation.

No merger because crisis made unity emotionally convenient.

Senku would have approved if he were not currently screaming at a water diagram.

Then the ground alarm chimed.

Not battle.

Infrastructure.

Mikasa heard the tone.

One long.

Two short.

She looked toward the settlement.

"Upper diversion."

Rainwater plus damaged channel.

Wrong time.

Exactly when systems liked to fail.

Mau's voice came through inner relay.

"Water team active."

Mikasa answered:

"Copy."

Nothing else.

Trust by role.

At western edge, the militia captain from negotiation reappeared with blood across one sleeve.

Anko saw him.

"You alive?"

"Unfortunately."

"Your demand?"

He looked at the monster line.

"Still exists."

"Terrible timing."

"Agreed."

Then one of his officers pointed east.

"Your settlement's flooding."

Anko looked.

Water rising along unfinished street.

Not houses.

Not yet.

The outer line bent farther.

The defense did not become cleaner.

It became more honest.

Some people who had threatened the city were now defending civilians.

Some still tried to exploit the battle.

Some monsters were attacking everything.

Some were only fleeing something worse.

Some defenders were too powerful to use full force safely.

Some were exhausted.

Some were afraid.

No clean enemy.

No clean front.

Only choices under pressure.

Then the upper diversion failed.

Not catastrophically.

Correctly.
