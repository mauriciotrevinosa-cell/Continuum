# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 11 — Outer Line

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 11

The outer line looked terrible.

Mud.

Smoke.

Short commands.

People retreating on purpose.

Himmel pulled three militia fighters away from a monster even though they had arrived threatening the settlement.

One stared.

"Why?"

"You're there."

Enough.

Diablo disarmed where surrender happened.

Did not where it didn't.

Frieren used force with precision and no interest in theatrics.

Milim redirected the largest creatures away from housing.

Mikasa coordinated fallback.

Eren reinforced where asked.

Not command.

Not wall speech.

A Searcher team guided civilians from the safe corridor into secondary refuge because the main route was full.

The second community sent warning support but did not empty itself into the main battle.

Independent.

Cooperating.

The outer line bent farther.

Then water alarms triggered.
