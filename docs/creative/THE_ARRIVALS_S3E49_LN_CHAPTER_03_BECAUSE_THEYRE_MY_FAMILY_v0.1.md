# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 10 — Because They're My Family

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S3E49 — `Beyond Counting`

The goddess stopped the scenarios.

Mau stood at the anchor.

He did not know how many times he had died.

Enough that death itself no longer made him shake.

Survival did.

The goddess asked:

`WHY DO YOU CONTINUE TO ACCEPT PERSONAL LOSS?`

Mau said nothing.

`WHY NOT ALLOW THEM TO DIE?`

Nothing.

`WHY?`

Mau looked up.

Anger remained after everything else thinned.

"Because they're my family."

Silence.

Outside, nobody moved.

Mau continued.

"I don't need a better answer."

The domain shifted.

For one second he thought it was over.

Then the goddess said:

`CORRECT.`

Mau closed his eyes.

Relief.

One breath.

The goddess continued.

`THAT WAS NOT THE QUESTION.`

Mau opened his eyes.

The real Trial began.
