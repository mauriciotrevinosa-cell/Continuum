# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 12 — It Fails Correctly

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 12

A creature struck the upper diversion channel.

Magic failed.

Partially.

Exactly the combined failure Senku had asked about.

Heavy use.

Damage.

Rainwater still moving.

Refuge load.

Coco saw warning marks.

Agott reached manual bypass.

Senku shouted flow numbers.

Qifrey stabilized only the magical section that needed him.

Native builders opened mechanical spill route.

Maomao isolated medical intake.

Water flooded an unfinished street.

Not refuge.

The washing court lost supply.

Not clinic.

One storage shed filled ankle-deep.

Not clean reserve.

Senku laughed.

Coco screamed:

"WHY ARE YOU HAPPY?"

"IT FAILED CORRECTLY."

She looked at the damage.

Then laughed too.

Weeks of ugly redundancy became survival.

Infrastructure had entered the battle without throwing a punch.
