# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 11 — Not the Question

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Source episode:** S3E49 — `Beyond Counting`

The anchor disappeared.

Mau did not fall.

No new scenario formed.

Only white space.

The goddess asked:

`DO YOU BELIEVE THEY LOVE YOU?`

Mau almost laughed.

After everything?

"Yes."

No hesitation.

That surprised him.

Memory Arc had damaged evidence.

Not this.

`DO YOU BELIEVE THEY NEED YOU?`

Mau looked down.

"Some."

`DO YOU BELIEVE THEY WOULD REMAIN IF THEY DID NOT NEED YOU?`

Silence.

There.

The wound.

Mau saw the future settlement.

Water running without him.

Searchers leaving without him.

Arrival House receiving people he had never met.

Coco learning.

Senku rebuilding.

Fern deciding.

Rimuru coordinating.

Frieren reading in a house whose walls did not require Mau's hands.

All good things.

His chest hurt anyway.

The goddess asked:

`IF USEFULNESS ENDS, WHAT REMAINS?`

Mau remembered Wakana.

`No. But you can stay.`

He knew the lesson.

Knowing was not believing.

The goddess understood.

That was why the Trial had started with death.

Sacrifice was easy for Mau compared with being unnecessary.
