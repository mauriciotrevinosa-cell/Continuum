## Chapter 11 — You Already Told Me

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT

**Volume:** 10  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Source episode:** S3E4 — `Small Wrong Things`

<!-- scene-id: s3e4-ln02-water -->

Rimuru changed the water plan.

Mau objected.

"We already decided not to put the maintenance channel there."

Rimuru looked up.

"We didn't."

"Yesterday."

"Yesterday we measured it."

"And then you said the slope made the north line safer."

Rimuru stopped.

Qifrey stopped too.

They were both there.

That should have helped.

Rimuru said, "I said the north line was safer for the clean intake."

"Right."

"Not maintenance."

Mau waited.

Rimuru waited back.

Mau could see the conversation.

Same table.

Same map.

Rimuru pointing.

Qifrey saying the public access point needed to remain visible.

Mau saying children would climb on it.

Coco objecting to being used as an example.

He remembered Coco's face.

"Coco was there."

Coco looked up from across the shelter.

"For what?"

Mau's stomach tightened.

"The conversation."

"What conversation?"

<!-- scene-id: s3e4-ln02-dream -->

Nobody made it dramatic.

That helped.

Rimuru asked whether Mau had dreamed it.

Mau said maybe.

Frieren had been told about the predictive dreams long ago.

Dreams becoming familiar enough to masquerade as memory was unpleasant but not impossible.

They wrote the event down.

Date.

Details.

What Mau remembered.

What everyone else remembered.

Mau joked that Continuum had finally invented minutes from meetings.

Nano approved.

The joke worked.

Later, walking home, Frieren asked:

"Are you worried?"

"No."

She looked at him.

Mau corrected himself.

"A little."

Frieren took his hand.

That was the first time he wondered whether he had already answered that question yesterday too.
