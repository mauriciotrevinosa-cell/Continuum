# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 13 — Return

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 13  
**Source episode:** S3E50 — `The Offer`

The option appeared above the court.

`RETURN.`

No one spoke.

The goddess explained only what she was willing to bind herself to publicly.

A person could choose to return to their original continuity.

Not a simulation.

Not a copy.

Return.

What happened to Continuum memory was less clean.

`INTEGRATION MAY RENDER THIS LIFE DREAMLIKE IN RECOLLECTION.`

Mau stared at that line.

Frieren did too.

Momo asked:

"May?"

No answer.

Nano asked whether memories could remain fully accessible.

No answer.

Anko asked whether subjective time changed.

No answer.

Eren asked whether returning meant the exact moment of departure.

No answer.

The goddess finally responded:

`NO IRREVERSIBLE CHOICE WILL BE ACCEPTED BEFORE RELEVANT TERMS ARE SHOWN TO THE CHOOSER.`

That was useful.

Also infuriating.

Return was real.

Its exact personal consequences would be disclosed before each person's final choice.

No one had to decide now.

The option still changed the world immediately.

Home was no longer only memory.

It had become a door.
