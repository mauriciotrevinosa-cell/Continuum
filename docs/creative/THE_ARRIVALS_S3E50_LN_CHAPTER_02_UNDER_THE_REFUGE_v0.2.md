The unknown signature came from below.

Not a person.

Device.

Buried inside material delivered weeks earlier.

That fact would matter later.

Now it mattered because the field expanded.

Imported power systems weakened inside the radius.

Frieren felt it from outside.

Yuta did.

Rimuru.

Several residents inside the refuge lost abilities abruptly.

Panic.

Mau reached the access cavity.

The construct classified.

OTHERWORLDER.

LOCAL.

UNKNOWN.

ERROR.

The sequence repeated.

Mau froze.

Not because it hurt.

Because the world-facing device could not decide what category he belonged to.

The Noise reacted.

Observe.

Analyze.

The construct hesitated while trying to resolve him.

Senku shouted through relay:

"Don't let it finish!"

Mau laughed despite crisis.

"Which one?"

No time.

He used the hesitation to expose a mechanical joint.

Not destroy.

Expose.

Aira arrived.

Looked at the tool.

Looked at Mau.

"That?"

"That."

She hit it.

Hard.

The field collapsed.

No Hollow Purple.

No divine counter.

Aira with a tool.

Important.

Too many people had still seen the classification error.
