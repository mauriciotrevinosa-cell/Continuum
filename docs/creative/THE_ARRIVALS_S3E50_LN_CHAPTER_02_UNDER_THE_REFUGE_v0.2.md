# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 27 — Under the Refuge

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 27  
**Continuity state:** Last Defense active | Suppression device discovered beneath central refuge | Cross-system weakening spreads

The unknown signature was not a person.

That should have been comforting.

It was worse.

Aira found the seam first.

Newer mortar behind an older access plate.

"That wasn't on the plan."

Senku answered through relay.

"It is now."

"Helpful."

Mau crouched.

No touching.

They had learned.

The plate came free mechanically.

Behind it sat a dark object packed inside the wall cavity.

Small enough to hide in building material.

Large enough to have its own geometry.

No familiar circle.

No modern electronics.

No cursed markings Yuta recognized.

No one system claimed it.

Mau looked.

The construct looked back in the only language it seemed to have.

Classification.

The relay sensor above began repeating:

OTHERWORLDER.

LOCAL.

UNKNOWN.

ERROR.

OTHERWORLDER.

LOCAL.

UNKNOWN.

ERROR.

Then the field expanded.

Mau felt pressure flatten.

Aira cursed.

Above them, Frieren's mana control stuttered.

Yuta's reinforcement dipped.

Rimuru went silent for one impossible fraction of a second.

Across the refuge, several imported abilities weakened at once.

Not gone.

Suppressed unevenly.

Panic hit faster than the field.

Fern's voice snapped over refuge relay.

"Stay on current instructions. No one changes route."

Good.

Inside medical overflow, Kusuri reached for a cabinet as one guest's transformed biology began destabilizing under suppression.

Maomao stopped her hand.

"Which?"

"Supportive only."

"Name."

Kusuri named it.

Dose.

Known risk.

Approved.

"Use."

No improvisation because magic failed.

At the cavity, Mau's knees nearly gave.

Aira caught his shoulder.

"Power?"

"No."

"Then what?"

"The Noise."

It was suddenly loud.

Not voice.

Process.

Observe.

Analyze.

Understand.

Construct.

The hidden device was giving it a problem shaped exactly like temptation.

Mau stared at the casing.

Classification continued.

OTHERWORLDER.

LOCAL.

UNKNOWN.

ERROR.

When the sequence reached him, it hesitated.

Longer each time.

Rimuru came back on relay.

"Mau?"

"Here."

"Status."

"It can't classify me."

Silence.

Aira looked at him.

Mau laughed once.

Wrong time.

"Apparently I'm administratively offensive."

"Focus."

"Right."

The device shifted.

The field pulsed harder.

Above, Frieren grabbed the wall.

Rimuru's voice sharpened.

"Do not let it resolve you if you can avoid it."

Mau stared.

"How?"

Good question.

No answer.

The Noise flooded him with possible structures.

Layer.

Joint.

Flow.

The device had mechanical seams beneath whatever system powered it.

That was useful.

Dangerous.

He could feel the old pattern:
understand;
take responsibility;
solve.

He spoke aloud instead.

"I see a joint."

Aira:

"Where?"

"Lower left. Behind the second plate."

"Can I reach it?"

"Yes."

"Can you?"

"Also yes."

She looked at him.

"No."

Mau almost argued.

Then stopped.

Good.

Aira put the tool into the seam.

Nothing.

"Angle?"

"Five degrees down."

She changed.

Metal shifted.

The field spiked.

Mau saw too much.

Magic as pressure maps.

Cursed energy as tension.

Rimuru's presence as layered system.

Aira's body as biomechanics.

For half a second people stopped looking like people.

He recoiled.

"Aira."

"What?"

"Don't let me touch it."

She did not ask why.

"Okay."

The construct hesitated again around his classification.

Senku heard the pause in sensor output.

"That's the opening."

Mau looked at the joint.

"No. That's the distraction."

"Explain."

"It keeps trying to decide what I am."

"Can we use that?"

Mau hated the question because yes arrived too fast.

"Maybe."

Rimuru:

"Maybe is not permission."

Mau closed his eyes.

Good.

"Then Aira uses it."

Aira grinned.

"Finally."

He described the timing.

Not certainty.

Pattern.

When ERROR lingered, the lower brace unloaded for a fraction.

Aira waited.

OTHERWORLDER.

LOCAL.

UNKNOWN.

ERROR—

"Now."

She hit the exposed mechanical joint.

Once.

It bent.

Field staggered.

Again.

The tool slipped.

Her knuckles hit stone.

Blood.

"Again?"

Mau looked.

"Again."

She hit.

The brace snapped.

The field collapsed.

Not dramatically.

No divine light.

No massive explosion.

Pressure simply left.

Above them people inhaled at the same time.

Frieren's mana returned.

Yuta's reinforcement stabilized.

Rimuru came fully back into the relay.

The refuge lights brightened.

Aira stared at the broken joint.

Then at the tool in her hand.

"That's it?"

Mau sat down hard against the wall.

"I hope."

Frieren was already coming down the stairs.

Mau heard her.

"Aira broke it."

Aira looked at him.

"What?"

"You're getting credit."

She laughed.

Then saw his face.

The Noise had not gone quiet.

It had simply lost the device.

"You're not okay."

"No."

Frieren reached the landing.

Looked at Mau.

Then at Aira's bleeding hand.

Then at the construct.

No one got to turn the moment into Mau solving the field.

Not even Mau.

Aira broke it.

The device had only shown them something worse:

a world-facing system had looked at Mau and failed to decide which side of its categories he belonged on.

Too many people above had seen the same sensor readout.

That would not stay technical for long.
