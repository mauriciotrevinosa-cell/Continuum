# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 24 — Aira Broke It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 24  
**Continuity state:** Suppression field down | Western battle resolving unevenly | Evidence recovery begins before victory mythology hardens

By the time the story reached the western line, Mau had destroyed the suppression field.

Aira heard this while Maomao was wrapping her hand.

She stood up.

"No."

Maomao pulled her back down by the wrist.

"Sit."

"But—"

"Sit."

Aira sat.

Furious.

Momo came through the medical area carrying water.

"What?"

"They're saying Mau broke it."

Momo stopped.

"Didn't you?"

"YES."

Mau, from the next cot:

"Aira broke it."

Someone at the door said:

"But you found the weak point."

Mau did not look up.

"Aira broke it."

Aira pointed at him with the unbandaged hand.

"Thank you."

Then honesty ruined the simplicity.

"He found the joint."

Mau:

"She broke it."

"Yes."

Shared action.

Different roles.

No protagonist inflation.

Maomao tied the bandage.

"Tighter?"

Aira flexed.

"Fine."

"That was not the question."

"No."

Maomao redid it.

Outside, the western pressure changed.

The field collapse returned enough power to defenders that several militia units reconsidered the shape of the fight.

Some surrendered.

Some ran.

Some turned on creatures that had started tearing through their own rear.

One officer still tried to push the original demand.

The militia captain looked at him.

Then at the wounded civilians behind their line.

"Not now."

It was not alliance.

It was triage.

Diablo accepted surrender where it happened.

Himmel helped move injured people from both sides.

Frieren returned to the east line only after looking at Mau long enough to make him promise, again, not to leave medical without telling anyone.

"Yes."

"Say it."

"I will not leave medical without telling anyone."

Aira looked over.

"Growth."

Mau gave her a rude gesture.

Good.

The construct came out of the wall under chain-of-custody rules Anko invented in real time.

Nobody touched it bare-handed.

Senku wanted measurements before transport.

Qifrey wanted magical isolation.

Kusuri wanted a chemical swab of the packing material.

Anko wanted all of them to stop talking over each other.

"Sequence."

They looked at her.

"Photograph."

No camera system everyone trusted.

Nano sketched.

Senku marked dimensions.

Qifrey recorded field residue without activating anything.

Kusuri took a sealed material sample from the mortar only after Maomao confirmed it did not belong in medical.

Diablo wanted to know who had placed it.

"Later," Rimuru said.

Diablo's smile disappeared.

Correct order.

People alive first.

Object second.

At the refuge, the lights had become ordinary again.

That was the strangest part.

Children resumed asking for water.

Someone needed a toilet.

A guest vomited from stress.

The collaborator and harmed resident stopped holding the same support and returned to opposite sides of the room.

No transformation into friends.

Good.

The Last Defense had done its job.

Then the north alarm dropped from red to amber.

Then west.

Then east.

Not silence.

Lower threat.

Mikasa did not declare victory.

"Maintain positions."

Milim complained.

"Can I hit the last big one now?"

"Direction?"

"Away."

"Terrain?"

"Clear."

"Civilians?"

"None."

Mikasa looked at Rimuru.

Rimuru looked at the map.

"Yes."

Milim disappeared.

The distant impact made dust fall from one ceiling beam.

Umaru looked up.

"She did that on purpose."

Probably.

At sunset, the outer line still held prisoners, injured militia, injured residents, damaged roads and several dead monsters nobody wanted near water.

No clean victory photograph.

No single finishing blow.

The city survived.

That was enough.

Aira left medical after being cleared.

On the way to the evidence room she heard two people talking.

"The Worldless guy—"

She stopped.

"What?"

They looked at her.

"Who?"

"The one the device couldn't classify."

Aira's irritation changed.

This was new.

Worse than stolen credit.

Mau had already become a story.

She found him outside triage.

"You have a title now."

Mau looked exhausted.

"Please tell me it's handsome."

"Worldless."

He stared.

"That's terrible."

"Yes."

"Can I resign?"

"From being weird?"

"Preferably."

Aira sat beside him.

For a minute neither said anything.

Then she held up the bandaged hand.

"I still broke it."

Mau nodded.

"You absolutely broke it."

Good.

The construct remained intact enough to study.

The mastermind remained unknown.

The battle ended without solving the season's mystery.

It only gave the mystery a physical object.

And gave rumor a new name for Mau.

Both would survive the night.
