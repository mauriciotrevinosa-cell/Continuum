## Chapter 14 — Stay

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S3E50 — `The Offer`

The second word appeared.

`STAY.`

That sounded simpler.

It was not.

Choosing Continuum meant remaining.

Not being trapped by default.

Choosing.

The goddess added one provision:

`A PERSON WHO STAYS MAY RECEIVE ONE BOUNDED PETITION CONCERNING A SPECIFIC LOVED PERSON.`

The court erupted.

Questions.

Bring them here?

Restore them?

Find them?

Alive?

Dead?

Healthy?

Same memory state?

Same age?

Guaranteed?

The goddess answered only:

`NOT UNLIMITED.`

Then:

`NOT GUARANTEED IN ALL CASES.`

Then:

`TERMS WILL BE SHOWN BEFORE FINAL CHOICE.`

Coco had stopped breathing again.

Richeh noticed.

Coco whispered a word too quietly for most people to hear.

Someone she loved.

A possibility.

Not a promise.

That was enough to change staying from abandonment into a different equation.

Rimuru asked whether multiple people could petition for the same person.

No answer yet.

Mau watched everyone.

Return reopened old worlds.

Stay could reopen old relationships.

Neither option was clean.

Good.

If one had been obviously better, it would not have been a choice.
