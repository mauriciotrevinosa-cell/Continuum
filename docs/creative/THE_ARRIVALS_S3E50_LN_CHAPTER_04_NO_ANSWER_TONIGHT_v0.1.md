# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 15 — No Answer Tonight

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E50 — `The Offer`

The Trial ended without asking Mau to die again.

That felt suspicious.

He returned to the central court.

Physically intact.

Then Frieren reached him.

He flinched.

Not from her.

From remembering seventeen different versions of her dying.

Frieren saw.

Stopped before touching.

"Mau?"

He looked at her.

"Can you—"

He could not finish.

Frieren understood enough.

"Yes."

She opened her arms.

Mau stepped into them.

Not collapsed.

Chose.

The Trial boundary disappeared.

Power returned everywhere.

The monsters outside the settlement were again manageable.

Milim left with enough anger to solve that problem quickly without breaking the mountain.

The goddess's final public statement remained overhead:

`NO ANSWER REQUIRED TONIGHT.`

Then vanished.

Nobody celebrated.

Nobody convened immediately.

The coordinating table tried.

Fern said no.

For once, everyone listened.

People went home.

Different homes.

Some to the old inn because they needed old walls.

Some to new rooms.

Some slept beside people they had not planned to choose anything with.

Mau and Frieren lay awake.

Neither asked the question yet.

That restraint cost both of them.
