# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 16 — Morning After

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 16

Morning smelled like wet ash.

Three structures beyond quick repair.

Outer posts damaged.

Water bruised but safe.

Medical stores low.

Road blocked in two places.

Dozens injured.

No mass-casualty collapse.

That last line existed because hundreds of small decisions had worked.

The second community sent aid.

Not merger.

Aid.

The east village sent carpenters.

Partnership.

Arrival House reopened only for emergency intake.

Old inn became overflow again.

History functioning.

Mau sat outside medical.

Frieren joined.

"You stayed in role."

"Mostly."

"You called."

"Yes."

"You didn't chase the unknown alone."

"Yes."

She took his hand.

Progress.

Then a child walked past.

Pointed.

"That's him."

Mau looked up.

"The one the system couldn't name."

There.

New problem.

Victory had created mythology.
