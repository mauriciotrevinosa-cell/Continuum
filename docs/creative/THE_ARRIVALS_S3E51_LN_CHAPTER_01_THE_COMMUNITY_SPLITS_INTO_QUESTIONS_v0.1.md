# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 16 — The Community Splits Into Questions

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S3E51 — `Questions`

The settlement did not split into sides.

It split into questions.

Can couples choose separately?

Can children return without guardians?

What happens to a family formed from different worlds?

Can someone stay now and choose return later?

Does the petition happen before or after commitment?

What if the loved person refuses?

What if the source world is dangerous?

What if returning restores obligations you escaped?

What if your home no longer exists at the point you return?

What if Continuum feels like a dream and you forget someone who stays?

No answers yet.

That prevented campaigning.

Mostly.

Some people spoke as if returning were obvious.

Others as if staying were.

Arguments started.

Then stopped when someone remembered the choice was individual.

Arrival House added a new room.

Not for arrivals.

For private choice consultations once terms became available.

No one wanted a public board labeled:

`STAY / RETURN.`

Good.

A person's answer was not settlement property.
