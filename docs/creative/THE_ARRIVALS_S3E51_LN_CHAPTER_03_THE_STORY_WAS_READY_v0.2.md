# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 32 — The Story Was Ready

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — CREATOR REVISION 2026-10-04  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 32  
**Continuity state:** Post-defense Day 2 | Propaganda timeline proves some hostile narrative was prepared in advance

The first outside account of the battle arrived before the settlement had finished counting the injured.

That was possible.

Fast rider.
Short route.
Rumor.

The wording was not.

Anko read it once.

Then again.

Momo watched her face.

"What?"

Anko handed her the paper.

Momo read aloud:

> The Mau-controlled armed enclave refused lawful capability assessment and initiated organized resistance against regional stabilization forces.

Silence.

Aira:

"We initiated what?"

Mikasa:

"Don't."

Aira stopped.

Barely.

The account continued.

It described:
a fortified central command;
organized Otherworlder combat units;
civilian dependents held inside;
a coordinated counterattack under Mau's direction.

Mau stared.

"I wasn't directing anything."

Fern:

"We know."

"I was barely in the same place for half of that."

"We know."

The paper did not.

Or did not care.

More important:
the notice carried a date.

Anko put it beside the battle timeline.

Negotiation at the edge began after sunrise.

The monster wave hit later.

The suppression device was discovered much later.

The hostile account had been posted in a town far enough west that a truthful battle report could not have reached it before the date written on the copy.

Senku checked travel assumptions.

Horse.
Relay.
Magic courier possibilities.

No known route made the timeline comfortable.

Rimuru asked:

"Could the date be wrong?"

"Yes," Anko said.

Good.

No easy proof.

Then a second copy arrived.

Different town.

Same wording.

Same date.

A native merchant brought it because he thought they should see.

He had watched it go up before sunset on the day of the battle.

"Before you knew who won?" Mau asked.

The merchant frowned.

"Before I knew there was a battle."

The room changed.

Not proof of one mastermind.

Proof of something narrower.

The story existed before the outcome.

Anko wrote on the Pattern Room board:

HOSTILE NARRATIVE PREPARED IN ADVANCE — HIGH CONFIDENCE.

Below:

ATTACK COORDINATION BY SAME ACTOR — NOT PROVEN.

Momo stared.

"How is that not basically the same thing?"

"Because somebody can prepare propaganda around an expected confrontation without controlling every monster, soldier or device."

Senku nodded.

"Or multiple groups could be using a shared packet."

Aira:

"Still evil."

Anko:

"Potentially."

"Anko."

"What?"

"Let me have one uncomplicated emotion."

Anko almost smiled.

"Fine."

Aira looked at the paper.

"Fuck them."

There.

Uncomplicated.

The worst part was how much truth the lie used.

There had been defenses.

There had been armed Otherworlders.

There had been a council.

Mau had been visible.

Arrival House held civilians because it was a refuge.

Searchers had moved people.

Milim existed.

Diablo existed.

Frieren existed.

A hostile writer did not need to invent an army.

Only rename relationships.

Family became command.

Shelter became detention.

Search became recruitment.

Defense became aggression.

Mau's cross-group trust became control.

The same model from the earlier assessment packet.

Now weaponized after blood.

Jinshi asked:

"Do we respond?"

Mau said:

"Immediately."

Everyone looked at him.

He corrected himself.

"Sorry. I mean— I think we should."

Better.

Fern:

"Yes."

Not because Mau said it.

Because silence would leave the first surviving version unchallenged.

They drafted facts.

The western demand arrived first.

Main settlement refused blanket relocation.

Negotiation remained active.

Attack followed.

Monster wave struck civilian routes.

Suppression device was found inside settlement infrastructure.

Aira broke the device mechanism.

Defense involved residents, native partners and even people who had arrived under hostile orders.

No claim about who caused the entire attack.

No claim about mastermind.

No heroic Mau mythology.

Then came the hard question.

What about the line:
Mau-controlled?

Mau said:

"Put the representation rules."

Anko:

"Too long."

"Then say no sole ruler."

"Done."

Momo:

"And 'we're not his followers.'"

Bocchi, from the doorway:

"Yes."

Everyone turned.

Bocchi immediately regretted being audible.

Still:

"Yes."

Nijika stood behind her.
Ryo beside her.
Kita too.

Not one source group defending its own.

Family correcting the frame.

Seiko read the hostile notice.

Snorted.

"They think this idiot controls me?"

Mau:

"Thank you?"

"No."

Frieren took the page.

Her eyes stopped on:

PERSONAL ENFORCEMENT.

Same category as before.

She folded the paper once.

Very neatly.

Mau watched.

"Don't destroy the evidence."

"I know."

She handed it back.

Growth.

The correction left that evening.

So did the hostile story.

Both would travel.

Neither could erase the other.

The settlement had survived an attack.

Now it had to survive somebody explaining the attack before it happened.
