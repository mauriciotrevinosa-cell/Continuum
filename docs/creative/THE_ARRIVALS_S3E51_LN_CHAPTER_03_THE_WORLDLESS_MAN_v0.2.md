# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 18 — The Worldless Man

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Continuity state:** Post-defense Day 3 | Mau classification rumor spreads | Public correction without mythic counterclaim

The title appeared on a wall before anyone admitted it was a title.

WORLDLESS.

Charcoal.

Not malicious.

Worse.

Admiring.

Mau stood in front of it with a bucket and brush.

Momo found him.

"That is kind of cool."

Mau turned.

"Leave."

"I'm just saying aesthetically—"

"Leave."

She left laughing.

He scrubbed.

The word remained faintly visible in the mortar.

Of course.

Rumor worked like that.

By the third day, Arrival House had collected seven versions.

Continuum could not classify Mau.

True enough.

Therefore Continuum protected Mau.

Unsupported.

Therefore Mau belonged to Continuum more than anyone else.

Unsupported and gross.

Therefore Mau owned part of Continuum.

Absurd.

Therefore anti-Otherworlder systems could never affect him.

Demonstrably false.

Therefore Mau caused Arrivals.

No evidence.

Therefore Mau was not from any world.

Maybe.

Also no evidence.

Nano built a correction sheet.

CLAIM.

KNOWN.

UNKNOWN.

DO NOT INFER.

Senku loved it.

Mau hated that he needed it.

Jinshi read the draft.

"You need to speak."

Mau looked at him.

"Why me?"

"Because people are attaching claims to your silence."

"That's not fair."

"No."

Good answer.

No pretending public life was fair because it was understandable.

Anko advised against a grand address.

"Plaza. Small. Questions after."

Mau:

"Why do I feel like I'm running for office?"

"Because rumor made you political without consent."

Worse.

Frieren did not volunteer to stand beside him.

Mau noticed.

"You don't want to come?"

"I'll be there."

"Where?"

"In the crowd."

He looked at her.

"Why?"

"Because if I stand beside you, people will watch me instead of hearing you."

Mau stared.

"That's annoyingly smart."

Frieren:

"I know."

The plaza was not full.

Good.

Thirty people.

Then forty.

Residents.

Guests.

Native partners.

Two reporters from regional towns.

One militia representative under escort.

Children who were there because everyone else was.

Mau stood on no stage.

Important.

Same ground.

He started badly.

"So apparently I'm Worldless now."

A few laughs.

Good.

"I hate the name."

More.

Then serious.

"The suppression device could not classify me consistently."

True.

"It alternated between Otherworlder, local, unknown and error."

True.

"I do not know why."

A person called:

"Because you're native to Continuum."

Mau shook his head.

"I don't know that."

"Do you remember another world?"

"No."

Murmur.

Mau held up one hand.

"That is not proof I never had one."

Good.

Another:

"Did the device fail because of The Noise?"

"I don't know."

"Can The Noise disable those devices?"

"I don't know."

"Can you control Arrivals?"

"No."

That one he knew.

"Can you sense them?"

"Sometimes I sense weird things. So do other people, differently."

"Can you stop anti-Otherworlder weapons?"

"Aira stopped that one."

Aira, in the crowd:

"THANK YOU."

Laughter.

The tension broke.

Mau pointed.

"She broke the joint. I found where."

There.

Again.

Credit repeated until rumor had competition.

A native man asked:

"Are you immune to suppression?"

Mau looked at Frieren.

Not for answer.

Habit.

Then back.

"No. I almost collapsed under it."

Good.

A younger resident:

"Then what are you?"

Silence.

Hardest question.

Mau could have said:
I am Mau.

Too neat.

He tried anyway.

"I don't know what category you mean."

Species?

Origin?

Legal status?

World?

The resident shrugged.

"All of it."

Mau laughed softly.

"Yeah. I don't know."

Some people looked disappointed.

Honesty often did.

He continued.

"What I do know is I'm not the settlement's emergency system."

That sentence changed the room.

Maybe because people had heard the opposite in stories.

"I helped. Aira helped. The water system helped. The refuge staff helped. Militia people who came here against us helped civilians. The old inn helped because we still had it. The second community helped without becoming us."

He looked around.

"If you turn that into 'Worldless man saved city,' you erase the actual reason we're alive."

No applause.

Good.

Applause would have made it another hero speech.

Questions continued.

One reporter asked:

"Do you reject the name entirely?"

Mau looked at the faint charcoal still visible on his sleeve from scrubbing the wall.

"Yes."

Then:

"People will probably use it anyway."

Realistic.

Afterward, some believed the corrections.

Some did not.

One child asked Aira to sign a piece of broken tool handle because she had "broken the machine."

Aira looked at Mau across the plaza.

He grinned.

She signed.

Better rumor.

Frieren joined him only after the crowd thinned.

"Did I sound convincing?"

"No."

Mau stared.

She continued.

"You sounded honest."

He thought.

"Better?"

"Yes."

They walked home.

Past the wall.

WORLDLESS still faint under wet mortar.

Mau stopped.

Frieren looked.

"Again?"

"Tomorrow."

Good.

Information existed.

Rumor survived.

So did he.

Neither had to become destiny.
