# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 18 — The Worldless Man

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 18

The title appeared three days later.

Worldless man.

Mau hated it instantly.

Stories:
- Continuum could not classify him;
- Continuum protected him;
- Mau belonged to the world;
- Mau owned the world;
- anti-Otherworlder systems failed near him;
- Mau caused Arrivals.

Unsupported.

Some absurd.

All sticky.

Arrival House added corrections.

Mau eventually spoke publicly in the plaza.

Small crowd.

"I don't control Continuum."

True.

"I don't know why the device failed to classify me."

True.

"I am not immune to weird effects."

Very true.

"I am not the settlement's emergency system."

Most important.

Some believed.

Some preferred myth.

Rumor survived information.

Information existed anyway.

Frieren stood in the crowd.

Not beside him.

This was a civic clarification, not romantic shield.

Afterward:

"Did I sound convincing?"

"No."

Mau stared.

Frieren continued.

"You sounded honest."

Better.
