# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 19 — The Note

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 19  
**Source episode:** S3E51 — `Questions`

Mau did not decide in one moment.

That would have made him easier to forgive.

The idea grew through small acts of listening.

Someone crying after saying they wanted home.

Someone else saying they would stay only because their partner was afraid to leave.

A resident asking whether choosing wrong would follow them forever.

Mau helped none of them.

Correctly.

He told himself that meant he had learned.

Then he started writing possibilities at night.

Not decisions.

Scenarios.

If everyone returned voluntarily.

If half stayed.

If couples split.

If children remembered Continuum as a dream.

If Frieren returned and remembered him only as something that once felt real.

He stopped on that line.

Could not breathe.

Then continued writing.

That should have warned him.

Instead he interpreted the pain as evidence he was willing to pay the cost.

Old mistake.

New shape.

The note took four drafts.

The first sounded like suicide.

He destroyed it.

The second sounded like permission.

It wasn't.

Destroyed.

The third said too much about Frieren.

Destroyed.

The fourth was short.

`I'm going to ask whether there is a way to return everyone without making each person carry the decision.`

`I am not doing anything irreversible without hearing the terms.`

That line made him feel responsible.

It was technically true.

`I know this looks like the thing I promised not to do.`

Mau stopped.

Then wrote:

`I don't think it is.`

The lie was sincere.

He left before dawn.
