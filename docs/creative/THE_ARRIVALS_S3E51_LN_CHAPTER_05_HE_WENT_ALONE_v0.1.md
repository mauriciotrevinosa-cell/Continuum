Frieren found the note.

Ori felt the stillness first.

Rimuru found the central-court access record.

Senku found Mau's route because Mau had moved one maintenance marker to avoid waking a work crew.

Bocchi found nothing.

She simply saw Frieren running and knew.

Different evidence.

Same conclusion.

He went alone.

Again.

Not into battle.

Worse.

Into a decision.

The pursuit assembled without meeting.

Frieren first.

Ori behind her.

Rimuru.

Bocchi and Kita because Bocchi refused to be left.

Yuta and Maki.

Momo and Okarun.

Senku because someone needed to ask whether Mau's plan had any actual mechanism behind it.

Milim because distance mattered.

Diablo because a goddess negotiation was exactly the kind of phrase that made everyone regret not bringing him.

Himmel came last out of the settlement.

Not because he was slow.

Because he stopped at Arrival House long enough to make sure the coordinating table knew who had left and why.

Someone had to keep the town from emptying itself every time Mau did something catastrophic.

Growth.

The route ended at the edge of the old first clearing.

Where Mau's remembered life had begun.

Of course.

Frieren hated the symmetry before she saw him.
