# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 21 — Interception

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 21  
**Source episode:** S3E52 — `Interception`

Mau stood alone in the clearing.

No goddess visible.

No Contract active.

Good.

They were in time.

Frieren did not slow.

"Mau."

He turned.

His expression contained the exact wrong emotion.

Relief.

He was glad they had found him.

That meant part of him already knew.

Frieren reached him first.

"Did you make a deal?"

"No."

Truth.

"Did you ask?"

"Not yet."

Truth.

"Why are you here?"

Mau looked at everyone behind her.

Too many people.

Too much proof against his argument.

He tried anyway.

"I wanted to see if there's a collective return option."

Rimuru said:

"Without asking us."

"I was going to ask for terms."

Senku cut in.

"That's not the same as asking us."

"I know."

Ori stared.

"No, you don't."

Mau looked at her.

Ori's voice broke.

"You literally taught me that being created by someone doesn't mean they own my choices."

That landed.

Mau looked away.

Bocchi stepped forward.

"Would you have come back before agreeing?"

Mau hesitated.

One second.

Enough.

Bocchi started crying.

Kita held her hand.

Yuta swore quietly.

Frieren's face went blank.

The clearing became the worst place in the world to repeat an old mistake.
