# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 19 — Leadership After the Front

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 19

After crisis, people wanted permanent certainty.

A petition proposed Rimuru and Mau as fixed leaders.

Rimuru laughed.

Mau did not.

Reasons:
- both connect groups;
- both were present from beginning;
- people listen;
- outside world already names them.

Dangerous logic.

The council rejected permanent personal rule.

Not anti-leadership.

Anti-dependency.

Terms.

Review.

Defined emergency authority.

Removal.

Technical scopes.

Resident input.

Mau accepted a larger coordination role.

Not king.

Not owner.

Frieren asked:

"Do you want it?"

"Part of it."

"Which?"

"Translation between people."

"And deciding?"

"Terrifying."

"Good."

Mau frowned.

"If you liked deciding everyone's lives, I'd worry."

Fair.

The flaw remained possible precisely because he did not enjoy power.

He enjoyed relieving others of pain.

That distinction would matter later.
