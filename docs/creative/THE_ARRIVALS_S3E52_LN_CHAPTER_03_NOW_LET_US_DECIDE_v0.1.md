# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 23 — Now Let Us Decide

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 23  
**Source episode:** S3E52 — `Interception`

Mau finally said the whole thing.

"I don't want any of you carrying it."

Rimuru asked:

"Carrying what?"

"The question."

Mau looked at the ground.

"Ten years from now. Fifty. Whatever. Wondering if you chose wrong."

Nobody interrupted.

"If you stay and something happens to the person you could have gone back to."

His voice tightened.

"If you leave and remember enough to know you abandoned people here."

Bocchi cried harder.

"If Frieren stays because she loves me and one day wishes she'd gone home—"

Frieren said:

"Stop deciding my future regret."

Mau stopped.

Ori stepped closer.

"We already know what that weight is."

Mau looked at her.

Ori continued.

"Every choice makes another choice disappear."

Rimuru nodded.

"Letting you decide for us doesn't remove that."

Yuta added:

"It just means you carry it alone."

Mau closed his eyes.

Sukuna.

Different battlefield.

Same shape.

`I carry the fatal consequence.`

Now:

`I carry the irreversible choice.`

Frieren took his hand.

Still angry.

Still chose contact.

"Love doesn't give you ownership of my agency."

Mau whispered:

"I know."

"No."

Frieren squeezed hard.

"You understand the sentence."

Memory Arc language.

Knowing was not believing.

Mau looked at her.

Frieren said:

"Now let us decide."

The clearing stayed quiet.

No goddess answered.

She did not need to.

The Trial had found its ending outside the Trial.
