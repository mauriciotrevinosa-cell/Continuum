# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 24 — Mau Jail, Part Two

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 24  
**Source episode:** S3E52 — `Interception`

Nobody trusted Mau alone for the next twenty-four hours.

Reasonable.

Mau objected anyway.

"This is imprisonment."

Umaru looked up from the chair beside the door.

"You tried to dimensionally deport us without asking."

Mau stared.

"When you say it like that, it sounds worse."

"It is worse."

Bocchi, on the other side of the room, nodded.

Mau looked betrayed.

"You too?"

Bocchi wiped her eyes.

"Especially me."

The joke arrived late enough not to erase the wound.

Five Minutes Away had once been overprotective recovery.

This was different.

Rules:

Mau did not approach any residual Trial site alone.

Mau did not enter a Contract involving Return/Stay without counsel and the affected person's explicit consent.

Mau did not leave a note and vanish.

The third rule received unanimous support.

Mau argued it was overly specific.

Everyone stared.

He withdrew the objection.

Shifts formed.

Frieren took first.

Ori second.

Rimuru wanted third.

Diablo volunteered every shift.

Rejected.

Milim volunteered to sit on Mau.

Rejected more forcefully.

Senku brought paperwork.

Mau asked if that was punishment.

"Yes."

Good.

Later, when the room quieted, Frieren sat beside him.

Mau touched his cheek.

"That was the biggest slap of my life."

Frieren looked at him.

"Good."

Pause.

Then:

"I'm sorry I hit you."

Mau looked over.

"I'm sorry I tried to decide your life."

Neither apology solved the other.

That was fine.

Frieren leaned against him.

Supervision continued.
