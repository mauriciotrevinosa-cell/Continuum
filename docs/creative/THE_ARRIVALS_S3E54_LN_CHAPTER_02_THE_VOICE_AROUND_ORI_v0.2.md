# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 26 — The Voice Around Ori

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 26

Dinner.

Ordinary.

Ori glitched.

Normal enough.

Then the spoon falling from Momo's hand stopped in air.

Steam stopped.

Sound stopped.

People could still think.

Move, barely.

Ori's eyes widened.

Not her.

A voice came from around her.

Not mouth.

Not room.

Mau's body recognized something his memory did not.

One word.

"Soon."

Then motion returned.

Spoon hit floor.

Steam moved.

Frieren had staff in hand.

Diablo standing.

Milim halfway upright.

Rimuru checking Raphael.

Ori shaking.

"I didn't say that."

Mau went to her.

"I know."

Immediate certainty.

Not because he understood Goddess.

Because he understood Ori.

The attack had taught the settlement how to defend systems.

This was not a system it knew how to defend.
