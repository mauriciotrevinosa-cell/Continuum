# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 41 — The Voice Around Ori

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 41  
**Continuity state:** Ordinary dinner after recovery | First undeniable external intrusion associated with Ori | Goddess not yet fully identified

Dinner was ordinary enough that nobody wrote it down.

That was why everyone remembered it.

Ryo complaining about food portion sizes.

Nijika correcting her math.

Kusuri trying to convince Maomao one approved dessert counted as medical recovery.

Maomao saying no.

Milim asking for thirds.

Fern saying no.

Milim getting thirds from Rimuru anyway.

Frieren stealing Mau's food.

Mau watching her do it.

"That's mine."

"You have more."

"That's not the point."

Ori sat across from them.

She glitched once.

Small.

A skip at the edge of her outline.

Normal enough now that only Mau and Nano noticed.

Nano marked time.

Ori rolled her eyes.

Then Momo dropped a spoon.

It stopped halfway to the floor.

No clatter.

Steam stopped above soup.

A candle flame froze without going out.

Ryo's mouth was open in the middle of a word.

No sound.

Mau could still think.

Move?

Barely.

His fingers obeyed like they were underwater.

Frieren's staff was already half raised.

How?

Habit outrunning stopped time.

Diablo stood.

Milim was halfway out of her chair.

Rimuru's face changed.

Ciel active.

No answer.

Ori's eyes widened.

She could move more than the rest.

Not much.

Enough to shake.

"No."

Her voice worked.

One word.

The frozen room heard it.

Then the other voice arrived.

Not from Ori's mouth.

Around her.

Through distance.

From no direction Mau could point to.

"Soon."

Nothing theatrical.

No echo.

No divine choir.

One word.

Mau's body recognized something.

His memory did not.

Cold moved through him.

The Noise reacted.

Not with analysis.

With stillness.

That scared him more.

Ori looked at Mau.

"I didn't—"

Motion returned.

The spoon hit the floor.

Soup splashed.

Sound came back all at once.

Ryo finished the word she had been saying and then screamed because everyone else moved wrong.

Frieren completed the staff motion.

Diablo crossed half the room.

Milim stood.

Momo looked at the spoon.

"What the hell?"

Rimuru:

"Nobody move."

Everyone was already moving.

Fern shouted:

"Sit down!"

More effective.

They sat.

Mostly.

Ori did not.

She was shaking.

"I didn't say that."

Mau went to her.

Not touching first.

"I know."

Immediate.

No investigation required for that part.

Ori's eyes filled.

"How?"

"Because I know your voice."

Dangerous sentence.

Could become ownership.

He corrected.

"And because you said you didn't."

Better.

Trust plus testimony.

Not mystical certainty.

Rimuru checked with Ciel.

"Bounded anomaly. Source unknown."

Qifrey checked for circle.

None.

Senku looked at the spoon, steam, candle, clock.

"Time-stop?"

Rimuru:

"Don't name it yet."

Senku grimaced.

Correct.

Anko was already asking:
who moved;
who thought;
who heard;
did anyone outside experience it?

Jinshi told three people not to leave before statements.

Not detention.

Evidence preservation.

Kusuri looked at Maomao.

"Any exposure?"

"No."

"Good."

No drug explanation.

No easy answer.

Mau remained near Ori.

Frieren came to the other side.

Ori looked between them.

Not triangulated.

Held.

Mau asked:

"Did you recognize it?"

Ori's face went blank.

Then fear.

"Not exactly."

"Almost?"

"I don't know."

Good.

Unknown allowed.

The voice had used her presence.

Maybe.

The anomaly had centered around her.

Maybe.

Her glitch preceded it.

Fact.

No leap to:
Ori caused it.

No leap to:
Ori is conduit.

No leap to:
Goddess.

Not yet.

Outside, people had experienced a blink.

Some noticed missing time.

Some did not.

Inside the room, everyone knew something impossible had happened.

After Last Defense, the settlement had procedures for:
attack;
suppression;
evacuation;
infrastructure failure;
Noise overload.

This fit none.

Mau looked at the spoon on the floor.

One ordinary object.

Evidence that dinner had been interrupted by something no wall could keep out.

Ori whispered:

"I don't want it to use me."

Mau answered:

"Then we start there."

Not:
I won't let it.

Too large.

Not:
you're safe.

Unknown.

Only:
we start with your consent.

The word soon remained in everyone's head.

No one knew what it promised.
