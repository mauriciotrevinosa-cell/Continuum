# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 18
## Chapter 27 — She Is Not Your Mouth

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 18  
**Reading order:** 27

The second intrusion came the next day.

Outside.

Stillness around Ori.

Voice:

"Child—"

Ori shouted:

"No."

The word broke through.

Frieren stepped between Ori and empty air.

"She is not your mouth."

Mau moved beside Ori.

Not in front.

Important.

Rimuru could not identify source.

Raphael detected bounded anomaly.

Qifrey no circle.

Senku no measurable emitter.

The voice:

"She is mine."

Ori went white.

Mau answered:

"No."

Ori grabbed his sleeve.

Then:

"I answer."

Mau stopped.

Good.

Ori looked into nothing.

"I came from you."

Her voice shook.

"I am not yours."

Stillness ended.

No punishment.

Worse.

The Goddess had heard.

Afterward Mau apologized.

"For answering first."

Ori leaned against him.

"You stopped."

Volume Eighteen ended with the settlement recovering from a human-scale assault while something far less human finally addressed them directly.

The next threat would not be solved by better walls.
