## Chapter 1 — The Day After the Voice

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19

Nothing divine happened the next morning.

That made everyone worse.

Ori ate breakfast.

Mau watched her.

Ori put the spoon down.

"Stop."

Mau looked away immediately.

"Sorry."

"You're doing it again."

"I know."

Frieren sat beside Mau and did not defend him.

Good.

Mau picked up his own spoon.

Forgot to eat.

Ori sighed.

"I'm still here."

Mau looked at her.

"That's what I'm checking."

"By staring?"

"Badly."

"Very."

He ate.

Progress through humiliation.

The courtyard looked ordinary.

Workers crossing toward the western road.

Water repair crews leaving early.

A child chasing something too small to identify.

Arrival House intake already open.

The world had the indecency to continue after a voice in stopped time had claimed Ori.

That continuity helped.

Also made everyone feel insane.

Rimuru called a small meeting.

Small mattered.

Ori.

Mau.

Frieren.

Anko.

Qifrey.

Senku.

Nano recording.

No whole-house panic summit.

No turning Ori into public exhibit.

Question:

What can the Goddess actually do?

Anko wrote two headings.

KNOWN.

UNKNOWN.

Known was short.

Localized stillness.

Twice.

Speech originating around Ori rather than through her mouth.

A claim of ownership.

Connection to Ori's origin likely enough to take seriously.

Mau's body recognizing the presence somehow.

Not memory.

Recognition.

Raphael unable to locate source.

Qifrey seeing no ordinary circle.

Senku detecting no ordinary emitter.

Unknown filled the rest of the board.

Can it enter whenever it wants?

Can it stop the whole settlement?

Can it hurt Ori directly?

Can it affect memory?

Can it affect people outside the local field?

Can it create matter?

Can it alter rules?

Can it be refused?

Does it need Ori?

Does it need Mau?

Why "soon"?

What does it want?

Mau stared at the UNKNOWN side.

"That's comforting."

Senku:

"It's accurate."

"Not the same thing."

"Usually better."

Ori looked at the board.

Then at Anko.

"Take off 'does it need Ori.'"

Anko paused.

"Why?"

"I don't want every question phrased like I'm equipment."

Silence.

Good silence.

Anko erased it.

Rewrote:

IS ORI NECESSARY TO FUTURE CONTACT?

Ori considered.

"Better."

Mau noticed the difference.

Not semantic.

Agency.

The room adjusted around the person most affected.

That was the point.

Qifrey asked Ori whether she remembered anything from before her first appearance.

"No."

Any sensation during the stillness?

"Pressure."

Where?

"Everywhere."

Did the voice feel inside her?

"No."

Good.

Important.

It had surrounded her.

Not inhabited.

Maybe.

They marked it as testimony, not fact.

Mau said:

"If she comes through you again, we don't answer for you."

Ori looked at him.

"We?"

Mau stopped.

Corrected.

"I don't."

Better.

Frieren's eyes flicked toward him.

She had seen the repair.

No praise.

Just notice.

Outside, the settlement kept rebuilding.

That mattered enough that Anko ended the meeting on time.

No divine emergency ate the entire day.

Mau went to carry boards.

Fern redirected him to inventory.

His head still had post-Noise restrictions.

He argued once.

Lost.

Kusuri and Maomao ran low-stock review.

Senku checked the damaged diversion.

Jinshi negotiated repayment expectations with a native partner so emergency aid would not silently become debt.

Bocchi played with children near the inn because some still slept better there.

Ori spent part of the afternoon with Coco.

No observer.

No Mau.

No special protection bubble.

Fern looked at him.

He stopped himself.

"Never mind."

"Good."

Painful.

Necessary.

At dinner, nothing happened.

At night, nothing happened.

The second day, nothing.

The third, nothing.

People relaxed badly.

Not actually.

They only became tired of being afraid.

Then, just after noon, every bell in the settlement rang once.

No hand touched them.

Old inn bell.

Arrival House.

Workshop.

East route.

Refuge.

Even the small warning bell near the repaired water gate.

One note.

Together.

Everyone stopped.

Mau looked toward Ori.

Ori looked toward him.

Neither said soon.

They did not need to.

The divine silence had ended.
