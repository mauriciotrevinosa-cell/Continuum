# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 2 — Boundaries

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 2

The bells were followed by lines.

Not physical.

Visible in air at the settlement perimeter.

Senku marked coordinates.

Qifrey marked magical absence.

Rimuru tested crossing.

Normal.

Milim crossed.

Normal.

Diablo.

Normal.

Then the lines vanished.

No trap.

No explanation.

The Goddess was showing she could define space before she used it.

That was Senku's interpretation.

Not truth.

Ori hated hearing people discuss the entity that made her like an engineering problem.

She said so.

The meeting changed.

Ori chose what she wanted to know.

Not everything.

Her origin belonged partly to her even if someone else created it.

Mau said:

"If she comes through you again, we don't answer for you."

Ori looked at him.

"We?"

Mau corrected.

"I don't."

Better.

The family started preparing for contact without turning Ori into equipment.
