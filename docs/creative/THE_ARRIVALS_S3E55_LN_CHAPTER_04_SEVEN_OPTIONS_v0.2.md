# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 4 — Seven Options

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 4

They tried seven options.

Rimuru attacked the boundary structurally.

Failed.

Qifrey searched for rule-writing.

Failed.

Diablo tried soul-space logic.

Failed.

Milim tried force.

Failed.

Senku searched for physical discontinuity.

Found none he could use.

Eren asked whether "protection withdrawn" meant attack guaranteed.

Good question.

No answer.

Searchers checked outside conditions through what limited observation remained.

Monster movement already existed beyond the perimeter.

Not created necessarily.

Timed.

Worse.

Frieren tried to reach the Goddess through Ori.

Ori said:

"No."

Correct.

Not a channel on demand.

Seventh option:

do nothing and see whether domain weakened.

It did not.

Hours passed.

Outside monster pressure approached dead warning zones.

The binary became less hypothetical.

Mau did not volunteer immediately.

That mattered.

He waited until everyone had exhausted what they could actually try.

Then asked:

"Anything else?"

Silence.

Rimuru hated silence.

Frieren hated it more.

Mau said:

"I participate."

Not secret.

Not first impulse.

Still terrible.
