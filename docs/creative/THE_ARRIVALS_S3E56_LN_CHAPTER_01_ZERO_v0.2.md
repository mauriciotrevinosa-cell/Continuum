Before taking Mau, the Goddess proved one rule.

Milim moved.

Power gathered.

A line appeared beside her.

TRIAL-RELEVANT OUTPUT: 0.

For one heartbeat, the power was unavailable.

Not suppressed by combat.

Not drained.

Defined absent within the asserted domain.

Milim's eyes widened.

Then:

RESTORED.

Power returned.

The Goddess spoke:

"Proof complete."

Diablo became very still.

Senku focused.

Frieren looked empty.

The demonstration did not prove omnipotence.

It proved something narrower:

inside this domain, the Goddess controlled at least some relevant rule access.

That was enough.

Mau looked at the sky.

"State the rules."

The Goddess sounded pleased.

He hated that.
