# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 5 — Zero

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Continuity state:** Seconds before Trial entry | Goddess demonstrates bounded domain authority | Omnipotence explicitly not established

Before taking Mau, the Goddess proved one rule.

Milim moved first.

Not attack.

Reflex.

Power gathered around her.

The domain wrote beside her:

TRIAL-RELEVANT OUTPUT: 0.

For one heartbeat, Milim had nothing.

Not exhaustion.

Not suppression pressure.

Not sealed energy.

Nothing available to the action the domain had classified as trial-relevant.

Her eyes widened.

Everyone noticed.

Then:

RESTORED.

Power returned so fast the ground cracked under one foot.

Milim stared at her hands.

The Goddess spoke.

"Proof complete."

Diablo became very still.

The kind of stillness that meant his anger had stopped needing expression.

Senku looked at the words.

"Relevant."

Rimuru heard.

"Yes."

Not:
all output zero.

Not:
Milim powerless.

TRIAL-RELEVANT.

Bounded.

The domain had scope.

That was information.

Mau looked up.

"Can you do that outside this boundary?"

No answer.

"Can you do it to everyone?"

No answer.

"Can you kill them?"

No answer.

Frieren:

"Can you force his answer?"

The Goddess replied.

"No."

Everyone looked at Mau.

Important.

The coercion was environmental.

Not mind control.

"Can he leave after beginning?" Rimuru asked.

"Under stated rules."

"Which you haven't stated."

"Correct."

Senku swore.

The demonstration did not prove omnipotence.

It proved something narrower and bad enough:

inside the asserted domain, the Goddess could define access to at least some relevant capabilities.

Power scale had become the wrong question.

Milim could be stronger in every ordinary sense and still have an action defined as zero under a rule she had not authored.

Eren understood first.

"Freedom inside someone else's conditions."

No one liked that either.

Mau looked at the boundary.

Then at Ori.

She was pale.

"This is what made you?"

Ori shook her head.

"I don't know."

Good.

No invented memory.

Mau looked at the sky.

"State the rules."

Ori grabbed his sleeve.

Not to stop him.

To make sure he looked at her first.

Mau did.

"You don't owe it obedience because it made me."

"I know."

"Say it."

"I know."

Ori let go.

The Goddess sounded pleased.

He hated that.

Text began to form.

Frieren caught Mau's hand before the first line finished.

Not to stop him.

To be there while he read.
