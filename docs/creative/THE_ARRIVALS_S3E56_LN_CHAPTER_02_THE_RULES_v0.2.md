## Chapter 6 — The Rules

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 6

Rules appeared.

SCENARIOS ARE CONSTRUCTED WITHIN TRIAL DOMAIN.

EXTERNAL RESIDENTS ARE NOT PHYSICALLY TRANSFERRED INTO SCENARIOS.

Mau exhaled.

Then:

PARTICIPANT WILL NOT BE INFORMED DURING SCENARIO WHETHER A PERSON IS CONSTRUCTED OR EXTERNAL.

Contradiction in feeling.

Precision in wording.

DEATH OF PARTICIPANT RETURNS PARTICIPANT TO TRIAL ANCHOR.

MEMORY OF PRIOR LOOPS REMAINS.

CONSTRUCTS MAY RESET.

Mau asked:

"How many?"

No answer.

Then:

EACH SCENARIO CONTAINS LOSS.

PARTICIPANT MAY ACCEPT PERSONAL LOSS OR ALLOW DESIGNATED OTHER LOSS.

Mau understood too fast.

"No."

BEGIN.

The plaza vanished.

Road.

Frieren bleeding ahead.

She looked real.

That was the point.
