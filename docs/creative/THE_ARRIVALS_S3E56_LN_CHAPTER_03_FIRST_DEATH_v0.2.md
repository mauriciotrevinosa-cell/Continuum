# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 7 — First Death

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Continuity state:** Trial loop 1 | Mau does not yet understand what reset preserves

Frieren was trapped under debris.

A broken street.

A beam across one leg.

Dust in her hair.

Blood near her temple.

Too detailed.

Mau knew the Goddess had said scenarios were constructed.

He also knew the rules deliberately refused to tell him whether the person inside any scenario was merely constructed.

That uncertainty was the weapon.

A marked exit opened behind him.

Words appeared.

EXIT:
MAU SURVIVES.
FRIEREN DIES.

INTERVENE:
MAU DIES.
FRIEREN SURVIVES.

Mau stared.

"No."

Frieren looked at the words too.

"Mau."

"No."

Threat moved at the far end of the street.

Not enough time to philosophize.

Mau tried the beam first.

Construction.

Leverage.

Move debris without entering the marked danger.

The Trial reconfigured load.

Same topology.

He attacked the threat.

It dispersed.

Reappeared.

Rule, not enemy.

Frieren understood sooner.

"Leave."

Mau looked at her.

"Fuck you."

"That's not an argument."

"Good."

He tried to break the exit.

Nothing.

Tried the road.

Boundary.

Tried lifting Frieren directly.

The danger accelerated.

Time.

Five seconds.

Frieren shouted his name.

Mau ran toward her.

No speech.

No noble pause.

One hand reached the beam.

Then impact.

Pain arrived so completely that thought stopped.

Chest.

Air gone.

Ground.

Frieren above him.

Then nothing.

Anchor.

Mau came back standing.

Whole.

No wound.

His hands went to his chest anyway.

Nothing.

He could still feel exactly where the impact had been.

His knees failed.

He dropped.

Outside, real Frieren took one step toward the boundary.

It did not let her through.

"Mau."

He could not hear her.

Or the Trial chose not to let him.

Mau looked at his hands.

No blood.

His body had reset.

His nervous system had not agreed.

The Goddess asked:

"Why?"

Mau looked up.

First death.

He still believed one death could be singular.

"Fuck you."

Outside, Momo whispered:

"Good."

Nobody laughed.

The next scenario began before Mau stopped shaking.
