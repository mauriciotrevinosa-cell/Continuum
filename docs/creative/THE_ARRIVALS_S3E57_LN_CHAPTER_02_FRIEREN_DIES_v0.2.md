## Chapter 10 — Frieren Dies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 10

Loop seventeen—or near enough.

Mau stopped counting accurately later.

Bridge.

Frieren.

Two routes.

Mau refused both.

Searched for third.

Under.

Above.

Construction.

Noise.

Anything.

Frieren understood.

"Mau."

"No."

"You can't solve every—"

"Don't."

Time expired.

Her bridge section failed.

Frieren fell.

Water far below.

Impact sound.

Silence.

No immediate reset.

Cruelty.

Fifteen seconds.

Mau alive.

Frieren gone.

The Goddess:

"Why did you not choose?"

Mau could not answer.

Anchor returned.

He vomited despite an empty stomach.

Outside, real Frieren watched herself die.

Fern touched her only after Frieren reached first.

The Trial had found something worse than killing Mau.

Letting him survive.
