# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 11 — Bocchi Dies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 11

No battlefield.

Building fire.

Smoke.

Mau one side.

Bocchi other.

One mechanism.

Open one route.

Seal the other.

Bocchi understood first.

"Open yours."

"No."

"Mau."

"No."

She started crying.

Not because she wanted to die.

Because she knew him.

"Don't do the thing."

"What thing?"

"The thing where you decide you're easier."

Mau froze.

The construct knew family language.

Bocchi hit the barrier.

"I'm not letting you call this love."

Time.

Smoke.

Mau's hand hovered.

He still could not choose himself.

Mechanism sealed.

Bocchi vanished into smoke.

Again, delayed reset.

Mau screamed at empty air.

Anchor.

Outside, real Bocchi had both hands over her mouth.

Kita held her.

The Goddess asked:

"Would you have obeyed her?"

Mau looked sick.

"No."

That answer hurt Bocchi more than the scenario.

Because it was honest.
