# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 12 — Ori Dies

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 12

Ori's scenario had no monster.

Two doors.

One for Mau.

One for Ori.

One could open.

Ori looked at both.

Then laughed.

"Of course."

Mau hated the sound.

"Choose yours."

"No."

"You're predictable."

"So are you."

Ori walked to Mau's door.

Pressed her hand against it.

"If you choose my door, you're deciding my life for me."

Mau stopped.

The sentence reached beyond the Trial.

Ori smiled.

"Let me choose."

She opened Mau's door.

Her own vanished.

Mau survived.

Ori died.

Anchor.

Mau came back furious.

"That wasn't my choice."

The Goddess answered:

"Correct."

Silence.

For the first time, Mau had been forced to survive because someone else chose him.

He hated it.

The Trial noticed.
