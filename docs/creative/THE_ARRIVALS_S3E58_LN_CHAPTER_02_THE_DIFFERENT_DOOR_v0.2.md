## Chapter 14 — The Different Door

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2

One scenario offered no death.

Frieren and Mau.

One door.

Condition:

ONE LEAVES.

Frieren said:

"I'll stay."

Mau:

"No."

"Why?"

"Because."

"That's not an answer."

"It is today."

Frieren stared.

Then walked through before he could stop her.

The door closed.

Mau survived.

Anchor.

Next:

Rimuru chose Mau.

Next:

Bocchi.

Next:

Ori.

Yuta.

People kept selecting him.

No sacrifice available.

Only acceptance.

Mau failed almost every version.

Sometimes he tried to follow.

Sometimes broke the scenario.

Sometimes shouted.

Sometimes begged them to choose themselves.

The Goddess did not ask why he died anymore.

She watched what happened when he lived.

That was the deeper test.
