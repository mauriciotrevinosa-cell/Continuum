# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 15 — Because They're My Family

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 15

The scenarios stopped.

Mau stood at anchor.

Death itself no longer made him shake.

Survival did.

The Goddess asked:

"Why do you continue to accept personal loss?"

Silence.

"Why not allow them to die?"

Mau looked up.

"Because they're my family."

No hesitation.

"I don't need a better answer."

Outside, everyone heard.

The domain shifted.

Mau thought:

done.

Then:

"Correct."

Relief.

One breath.

"That was not the question."

Mau opened his eyes.

The real Trial began.
