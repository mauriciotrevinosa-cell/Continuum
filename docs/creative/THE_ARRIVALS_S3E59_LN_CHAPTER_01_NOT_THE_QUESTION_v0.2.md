# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 19 — Not the Question

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 19

White space.

No scenario.

"Do you believe they love you?"

Mau answered:

"Yes."

That surprised him.

Memory Arc had damaged evidence.

Not this.

"Do you believe they need you?"

"Some."

"Do you believe they would remain if they did not need you?"

There.

Silence.

The Goddess showed him settlement life.

Water maintained without him.

Searchers leaving without him.

Arrival House receiving strangers he never met.

Coco teaching.

Senku rebuilding.

Fern deciding.

Frieren reading in a room whose walls did not require Mau.

All good.

His chest hurt anyway.

"If usefulness ends, what remains?"

Mau remembered:

No. But you can stay.

He understood the lesson.

Believing it was harder.

Sacrifice had been easier than unnecessary existence.

The Trial knew.
