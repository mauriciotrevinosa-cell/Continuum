# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 20 — Would They Choose You?

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 20

The settlement appeared.

Real?

Constructed?

Mau could not tell.

Everyone in plaza.

Goddess:

"Would they choose this place if they could leave?"

Mau:

"Some."

Good.

"Would Frieren?"

His throat tightened.

He wanted yes.

Not same as knowing.

The Goddess showed:
- Himmel;
- her world;
- history;
- landscapes;
- people;
- centuries Mau had not shared.

"Would she choose you if her world were open?"

Mau looked at Frieren.

Or construct.

"I don't know."

Honest.

The Goddess sounded almost gentle.

"Then let them answer."

The Trial opened enough that everyone outside heard.

Not ended.

Opened.

The question stopped belonging to Mau.
