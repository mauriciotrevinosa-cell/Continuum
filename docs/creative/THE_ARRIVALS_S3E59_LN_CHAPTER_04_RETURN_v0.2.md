# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 22 — Return

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 22  
**Continuity state:** Trial opens to settlement | Return/Stay offer begins publicly | Individual terms remain private and incomplete

One word appeared above the plaza.

RETURN.

No one cheered.

That surprised Mau.

He had expected somebody to.

Home had been a wound for so many people.

Now it was a word in the sky and everyone looked afraid.

The Goddess spoke.

"A chooser may return to their original continuity."

Anko immediately:

"Original point?"

"Personal terms."

Nano:

"Same body?"

"Personal terms."

Eren:

"Same moment?"

"Personal terms."

Momo:

"Can you stop saying personal terms?"

No answer.

Good.

The offer did not become a FAQ because people were scared.

Mau stood at the edge of the Trial space.

Not fully released.

Able to hear.

Not able to decide anything for anyone.

Correct.

The Goddess continued.

"Return is not simulation."

Several people looked at Senku.

He did not ask why.

Maybe because everyone else would.

"Not copy."

Rimuru:

"Continuity of self?"

"Personal terms."

Rimuru grimaced.

Then memory.

"Integration may render Continuum dreamlike in recollection."

The plaza changed.

Not visibly.

Emotionally.

May.

Hakari:

"May?"

No answer.

Karane looked furious.

"How dreamlike?"

Personal terms.

Bocchi's face went white.

Kita reached for her hand.

Bocchi accepted.

What did it mean to return if this life became something like waking from a dream?

Would love remain?

Would skills?

Would grief?

Would the name Mau become a person or a feeling?

No public answer.

Good and terrible.

Nano asked:

"Can memory remain fully accessible?"

"Personal terms."

Eren:

"Can return restore obligations a person escaped?"

"Personal terms."

Anko:

"Can someone return to a dangerous circumstance?"

"Yes."

That answer hit differently.

Return was not rescue by definition.

Richeh asked whether return could place someone before a tragedy.

"Personal terms."

Coco stopped breathing for one second.

Richeh saw.

Did not ask further.

Not publicly.

Jinshi:

"Can a chooser review terms without committing?"

"Yes."

There.

Important.

"Can they refuse after seeing them?"

"Yes."

"Can the terms change afterward?"

"Before final commitment, material changes will be disclosed."

Not comforting.

Useful.

Then the clearest guarantee yet:

"No irreversible choice will be accepted before relevant terms are shown to the chooser."

Fern repeated it aloud.

Not because she trusted the Goddess.

Because everyone needed to hear the same language.

No surprise binding.

No accidental yes.

No crowd vote.

Mau closed his eyes.

He felt relief.

Then Trial scenarios arrived.

A person returns.

Forgets enough.

Regrets.

Another remembers.

Returns anyway.

Another returns to danger and calls it home.

Another discovers the world moved.

Constructed lives.

Not these people.

His body reacted as if warning.

He said nothing.

Good.

The Goddess had finally given people an option Mau could not evaluate for them.

Return became real before it became clear.

Home changed from memory into choice.

That was enough to fracture every simple sentence anyone had used about belonging.
