## Chapter 14 — The Second Cup

**Source episode:** S3E5 — `Ordinary Work`

<!-- scene-id: s3e5-ln02-cup -->

The cup was chipped.

Mau knew when it happened.

Momo had dropped it during S2.

Not broken.

Chipped.

He remembered Rimuru catching it before the second impact.

Momo complaining that slime cheating should not count.

The cup sat in front of him now.

No chip.

Mau rotated it.

Smooth rim.

Same paint.

Same faded mark near the handle.

Different history.

"Where's the chipped one?"

Momo looked over.

"What chipped one?"

"This one."

"That one's not chipped."

"I can see that."

"Then why—"

She stopped.

Everyone had learned the shape of the question.

<!-- scene-id: s3e5-ln02-two -->

Umaru found another cup.

Same color.

Different handle.

Chipped.

"There."

Mau stared.

The memory shifted without changing.

Momo dropping the first cup.

Rimuru catching it.

A crack near the handle.

No.

The second cup.

He could see both.

Two mutually exclusive events.

Both carried the emotional weight of remembering.

Momo sat down.

"Which one do you remember?"

Mau laughed once.

"Yes."

Nobody laughed with him.

<!-- scene-id: s3e5-ln02-record -->

They added a new column to the notebook.

`Confidence before checking.`

Mau hated it.

Rimuru insisted.

Not because confidence proved truth.

Because they needed to know whether wrong memories felt weak.

They did not.

The chipped cup had felt as real as Frieren beside him.

That night Mau touched the watch on his wrist.

Then checked the table because for one terrible second he remembered Maomao still having it from after Sukuna.

The watch was on him.

He had been wearing it all day.

Mau went to bed without mentioning that one.

The omission was small.

That was how larger mistakes usually began.
