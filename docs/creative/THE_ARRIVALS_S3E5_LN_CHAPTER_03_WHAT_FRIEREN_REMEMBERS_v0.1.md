# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 10
## Chapter 15 — What Frieren Remembers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 10  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Source episode:** S3E5 — `Ordinary Work`

<!-- scene-id: s3e5-ln03-lake -->

Mau remembered the lake correctly.

That made the wrong part worse.

Same dock.

Same wind.

Same evening.

S2.

Before Sukuna.

Frieren leaning against him.

They had talked about Himmel.

Mau remembered asking:

"Do you think you would have gone with him if he arrived sooner?"

Frieren remembered no such question.

Mau stared at her.

"We were there."

"Yes."

"You leaned against me."

"Yes."

"I asked."

"No."

"You answered."

Frieren's face changed.

"What did I say?"

Mau did not want to tell her.

That became its own answer.

<!-- scene-id: s3e5-ln03-words -->

"You said you didn't know."

Frieren was quiet.

Mau continued.

"Then you said maybe."

"No."

The certainty in her voice frightened him more than anger would have.

"I remember it."

"I know."

"Same dock."

"I know."

"Same night."

"Probably."

Mau looked away.

Frieren moved closer.

"I remember talking about Himmel."

"Right."

"I remember you asking if I wanted him here."

Mau looked at her.

That part was real.

She had said yes.

Mau had said good.

Frieren had asked if it bothered him.

He had said no because she looked happy.

The real memory and the wrong memory occupied the same evening.

Like someone had used an existing room and changed one piece of furniture.

<!-- scene-id: s3e5-ln03-firstproof -->

Frieren put her hand over his.

"Mau."

He looked at her.

"I did not say maybe."

His throat tightened.

He believed her.

That did not make the memory disappear.

For the first time, the problem stopped feeling like bad sleep.

Something had entered an autobiographical memory that contained enough truth to defend itself.

Mau squeezed Frieren's hand.

"Do you remember it that way too?"

"No."

The question would survive long after the answer.
