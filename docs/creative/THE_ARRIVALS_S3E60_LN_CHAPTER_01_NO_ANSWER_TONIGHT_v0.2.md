# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 23 — No Answer Tonight

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 23

Mau returned to plaza.

Physically intact.

Then Frieren reached him.

He flinched.

Not from her.

From too many versions of her dying.

Frieren stopped before touching.

"Mau?"

He could not explain.

"Can you—"

No sentence.

Frieren understood enough.

Opened her arms.

Mau stepped in.

Chose.

The Trial boundary vanished.

Power returned.

Monster pressure outside became ordinary problem again.

Milim handled it with visible fury and strict instructions not to destroy a mountain.

Final public statement:

"NO ANSWER REQUIRED TONIGHT."

Then gone.

Council tried to meet.

Fern said no.

Everyone listened.

People went home.

Some to settlement.

Some old inn.

Some second community.

Some slept next to people they had not planned to choose anything with.

Mau and Frieren lay awake.

Neither asked:

Will you stay?

That restraint cost both of them.
