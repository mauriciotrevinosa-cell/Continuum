# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 23 — No Answer Tonight

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 23  
**Continuity state:** Day 0 after Trial | Return/Stay window opens | Ten-day decision period begins

Mau returned to the plaza physically intact.

That was the first lie his body told everyone.

No blood.

No broken bones.

No burn.

No missing tissue.

Then Frieren reached him.

Mau flinched.

Not from her.

From the bridge.

From smoke.

From a room where Frieren had chosen him and another where she had not.

From a version of her decades older asking whether staying had been worth it.

Frieren stopped before touching him.

"Mau?"

He could not answer immediately.

The plaza remained real around them.

People breathing.

People waiting.

The Goddess's presence still pressing against the edges of the world.

Then the final public condition appeared.

TEN DAYS.

No one spoke.

Another line followed.

NO FINAL ANSWER REQUIRED TODAY.

Ten days to request personal terms.

Ten days to ask questions.

Ten days before whatever came next.

The Goddess did not publicly define every edge of the deadline.

Not whether silence at the end counted as Stay.

Not whether an unresolved petition extended anything.

Those details remained personal-term questions.

Good.

The season did not need an omniscient FAQ.

It needed a clock.

Momo stared upward.

"Ten?"

Nano:

"Days."

"That is not enough."

"For some people, perhaps."

"For me."

"Definitely."

The words vanished.

The Goddess did too.

Trial pressure broke.

Ordinary sound returned all at once.

Wind.

Water.

Someone crying.

Milim shouting at a monster outside the boundary with enough fury that Rimuru immediately yelled:

"NOT THE MOUNTAIN."

Normal.

Almost.

Frieren looked back at Mau.

"Can I?"

She opened her arms rather than deciding for him.

Mau stared.

Chose.

Stepped forward.

Frieren held him.

His body locked for half a second because another Frieren had died in his arms in a loop he could not place in sequence.

Then the real one breathed against his shoulder.

Present.

Alive.

No reset.

Mau held tighter.

Too tight.

Frieren did not complain.

Council tried to call an emergency meeting.

Fern said:

"No."

Rimuru started to object.

Fern looked at Mau.

Then at everyone else.

"No one is making life decisions tonight."

That ended it.

Arrival House stayed open only for immediate questions.

No final choices.

No pressure.

People went home.

Some to the settlement.

Some to the old inn.

Some to the second community.

Some slept beside people they had not planned to choose anything with.

Mau and Frieren returned to their room.

The ten-day clock sat nowhere visible.

It still entered with them.

Day zero.

Nine nights after this one.

Maybe enough time to decide a life.

Mau lay down.

Frieren did not ask:

Will you stay?

Mau did not ask:

Will you choose me?

That restraint cost both of them.

For Mau, sleep cost more.
