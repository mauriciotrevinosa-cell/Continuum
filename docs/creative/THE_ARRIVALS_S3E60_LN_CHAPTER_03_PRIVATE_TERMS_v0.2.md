# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 22 — Private Terms

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 22

Personal terms began appearing only when requested.

One person entered Arrival House.

Door closed.

Stillness localized.

Words only they could see.

They came out twenty minutes later.

Nobody asked.

That was harder than expected.

Another requested terms with a partner present.

Allowed after explicit consent.

Another asked alone.

Another refused to request anything yet.

All valid.

The settlement created procedures around divine choice because people still needed privacy.

Witness optional.

Written copy optional.

Cooling-off period before final decision.

No immediate choice accepted after first viewing unless external circumstances made delay impossible.

Not because the Goddess required it.

Because people did.

Senku approved the cooling period.

Eren approved optional witnesses.

Frieren approved nothing aloud.

Mau requested no terms.

Not yet.

He told himself that was restraint.

Mostly true.
