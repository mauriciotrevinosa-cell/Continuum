# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 26 — The Community Splits Into Questions

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 26  
**Continuity state:** Day 1 after Trial | Return/Stay publicly known | Settlement must protect choice from becoming faction

The settlement did not split into camps.

That would have been easier.

It split into questions.

Can couples choose differently?

That one arrived before breakfast ended.

Can children return without guardians?

Fern wrote it down.

What happens to a family formed from different worlds?

Nobody had one definition of family broad enough and precise enough.

Can someone Stay now and Return later?

Personal terms.

Does the petition happen before commitment?

Personal terms.

Can the loved person refuse?

Yes.

Good.

What if source world is dangerous?

What if Continuum becomes dreamlike?

What if it doesn't?

What if returning restores obligations you escaped?

What if staying means accepting you may never see someone again?

What if a partner knows already and you don't?

What if somebody asks for your answer because their answer depends on yours?

That question caused the first shouting match.

Arrival House had become choice infrastructure overnight.

Jinshi hated that phrase.

Senku liked it.

Fern banned both from naming departments.

They created one private room.

Then two.

No public board.

No tally.

No:
STAY — 37
RETURN — 19
UNDECIDED — 84.

A person's future was not settlement census data.

Nano built the intake form.

Then deleted half of it.

"Why?" Mau asked.

She looked at him.

"Because I do not need to know."

That hit harder than expected.

The original form had:
source world;
current residence;
relationship dependencies;
anticipated choice.

Useful.

Also invasive.

New form:

Do you want information?

Do you want private terms?

Do you want a witness?

Do you need language / accessibility support?

Do you want no follow-up unless you request it?

Better.

Mau watched.

The whole city was learning not to turn care into data collection.

Useful lesson.

Some people argued anyway.

"Staying is obvious."

Someone across the common room flinched.

"Returning is obvious."

Someone else left.

Both sentences had turned another person's life into evidence for their own comfort.

Arrival House staff intervened.

Not:
stop having opinions.

Stop campaigning here.

The council issued the rule that afternoon.

NO CAMPAIGNING INSIDE ARRIVAL HOUSE.

Information:
yes.

Personal testimony:
if asked.

Pressure:
no.

Momo read the sign.

"What counts as campaigning?"

Anko:

"If you're trying to make another person choose your answer."

"What if I say my answer is better for me?"

"Fine."

"What if I say their answer will make me sad?"

Silence.

Harder.

Jinshi:

"Truth is not automatically pressure-free."

Good.

They added:
relationship conversations are allowed;
mediators available;
no one owes immediate disclosure.

Still incomplete.

Human.

At one table, a couple argued quietly.

One said:
home.

Meaning source world.

The other heard:
you are not home.

A mediator stopped them.

"Define the word."

They did.

The argument changed.

Not solved.

Better.

Mau passed the room three times.

Did not enter.

Fourth time Fern caught him.

"Do you need something?"

"No."

"Then stop orbiting."

Mau stared.

"I am not—"

"You are."

He looked through the doorway.

People scared.

Questions he had seen in the Trial.

Scenarios already generating.

He wanted to help.

Fern saw.

"Not every question is yours."

Mau looked away.

"I know."

"Understanding?"

He laughed without humor.

"Not living it."

The old words had infected everyone.

Good.

He left.

Outside, Kusuri was carrying inventory toward the medical room.

"Why do you look like you lost an argument?"

"Fern."

"Ah."

No more explanation necessary.

The Goddess had created a choice.

The settlement's first responsibility was not to produce the right answer.

It was to stop fear from turning choice into loyalty test.

Stay could not become:
prove you love us.

Return could not become:
prove your old life mattered.

Undecided could not become cowardice.

Privacy could not become betrayal.

And caring deeply about someone did not entitle you to their answer before they were ready.

Mau understood every sentence.

That night, his mind still generated futures for people whose choices he did not know.

The real test had left the Trial domain.

Now everyone had to practice it.
