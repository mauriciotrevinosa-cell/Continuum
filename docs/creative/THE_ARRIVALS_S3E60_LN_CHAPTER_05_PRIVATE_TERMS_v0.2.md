# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 28 — Private Terms

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 28  
**Continuity state:** Day 1–2 after Trial | Personal divine terms begin | Settlement creates human privacy and cooling-off procedures around irreversible choice

The first person to request terms did not tell anyone why.

That became the precedent.

Arrival House closed one room.

The chooser entered.

Door shut.

Stillness localized.

Not whole-settlement.

Good.

Twenty minutes later they came out.

Fern looked up.

The person looked like they had aged.

Not physically.

Nobody asked:

Return or Stay?

Nobody asked:

What did she offer?

Nobody asked:

Who can you petition for?

It was harder than expected.

Curiosity had become moral pressure overnight.

The second chooser requested a witness.

Their partner.

Arrival House staff asked both separately whether they wanted that.

Yes.

Then together.

Still yes.

Only then did they enter.

The third went alone.

The fourth came to the door and changed their mind.

Fern marked nothing except:

NO FOLLOW-UP REQUESTED.

Good.

The settlement started making rules around a divine process it did not control.

Witness optional.

Written copy optional.

Translation support.

Cooling-off period before final commitment.

No irreversible choice accepted immediately after first terms review unless external circumstances made delay impossible.

The Goddess had not required the cooling period.

People did.

Senku approved.

"Decision under novelty stress."

Maomao:

"Also panic."

Jinshi:

"Also coercion from relationships."

Eren:

"Also the fact that someone powerful set a deadline."

Good.

No one pretended the choice existed in pure freedom.

They could still protect whatever agency remained.

Kusuri requested information on chemical notes before requesting her own terms.

Maomao looked at her.

"Why?"

"If I leave, somebody needs the quarantine system."

"If?"

Kusuri stopped.

Good.

She had an answer forming.

Not public yet.

She finished the handoff notes anyway.

A real choice should not require making yourself indispensable first.

Elsewhere, Seiko requested terms with no witness.

Returned.

Went directly to Momo.

Did not tell her what they said.

Momo wanted to ask.

Stopped.

Growth.

Jinshi requested procedural clarification without requesting personal terms.

The Goddess allowed some general questions and refused others.

He took notes.

No one mistook them for everyone's contract.

Nano requested terms.

No witness.

Came out with an expression nobody could read.

Ryo tried.

Failed.

"Terrifying."

Nano:

"Correct."

Mau requested nothing.

Day one.

Then day two.

He told himself restraint.

Mostly true.

Partly fear.

If he saw his own terms, the Trial might attach more futures.

If he asked for Stay:
Frieren.

Ori.

Home.

If he asked for Return:
to where?

Mau had no remembered source world.

Maybe Return did not exist for him.

Maybe it did.

He did not want to know yet.

Frieren noticed.

Did not push.

That kindness hurt too.

Arrival House added one more rule after a resident came out of terms shaking and immediately tried to announce a decision to the whole room.

Fern stopped them gently.

"Sleep first."

"I know."

"You know words."

The resident laughed despite tears.

"Okay."

They slept.

Changed nothing the next morning.

Good.

The cooling period had not changed the answer.

It had changed the state in which the answer would eventually be made.

Important.

A different resident slept and changed their mind.

Also good.

No shame.

The process was doing its job.

Mau watched from outside one afternoon.

Jinshi joined him.

"You're orbiting again."

"Everyone says that."

"Because you are."

Mau looked through the window.

"Would it help if I knew the terms?"

"No."

Mau turned.

Jinshi continued.

"It would help you imagine more accurately."

"Isn't that help?"

"Not if imagining is the problem."

There.

Mau stared.

Jinshi had no idea how precisely that hit.

Or maybe he did.

Mau stepped away from the window.

Private terms existed because a future did not become more ethical when everyone who loved you had access to it.

The Goddess had created individual choice.

The settlement's job was to keep "individual" from becoming theoretical.
