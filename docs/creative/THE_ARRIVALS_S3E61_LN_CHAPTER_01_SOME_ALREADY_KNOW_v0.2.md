# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 28 — Some Already Know

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 28

Some people knew without terms.

Kusuri knew.

Not because the Goddess had made the choice easy.

Because the choice finally gave a formal name to something she had been choosing since G5.

Stay.

She did not write it on a public board.

There was no public board.

She told Hakari, Karane, Shizuka and Nano in the small medicine room after inventory.

"I think I'm staying."

Karane looked up sharply.

"Think?"

Kusuri corrected herself.

"I'm staying."

Different sentence.

Hakari's face changed first.

Not anger.

Fear of what a different answer might mean.

Kusuri saw.

"This isn't me asking you to."

Good.

Shizuka held her book against her chest.

Nano asked:

"Primary reason?"

Kusuri thought.

That was harder.

Not one person.

Not one laboratory.

Not one romance.

"I chose things here."

They waited.

Kusuri looked at the shelves.

The quarantine labels.

The storage rules Maomao had forced her to hate and then respect.

The notes Senku had challenged.

The work that would continue tomorrow even if nobody praised it.

"I can do work here that matters. I have people here I love. And I want the next version of this place."

Karane:

"That's three reasons."

"Yep."

Hakari asked quietly:

"What if we choose differently?"

Kusuri answered immediately.

"Then you choose differently."

No forced destiny.

No source mechanic turning affection into obligation.

No:
if you love me, stay.

Kusuri's choice did not become a vote the others had to match.

Nano nodded once.

"Consistent."

Shizuka opened her book.

The line she showed was short.

I'm glad you know.

Kusuri cried anyway.

Then laughed because crying near medicine labels felt stupid.

Karane hugged her while claiming it was only because Hakari had started crying first.

Hakari denied everything.

Nano stayed close enough that the denial became irrelevant.

Kusuri did not announce the answer at dinner.

She requested her formal terms privately later.

Her intended choice remained:

STAY.

The story did not require anyone else in the room to know yet.

One resident started packing.

Not because departure was immediate.

Because knowing changed objects.

Another wrote a packing list.

Destroyed it.

A couple argued because one said "home" and meant source world while the other meant Continuum.

A second-community resident said openly:

"If my terms allow it, I'm returning."

No shame.

A young Arrival said:

"I think I'm staying."

Then cried.

No contradiction.

Rem did not announce anything.

Kaneki did not.

Aira said:

"Ask me in a week."

Good.

Milim refused to discuss it until Rimuru requested terms.

Diablo said nothing at all.

Mau heard.

Did not record.

No private answer became governance data.

The story knew less than the characters.

That was intentional.
