# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 29 — Some Don't Want to Say

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 29

The first privacy conflict came from love.

One partner requested terms.

The other asked:

"What did they say?"

"No."

Not cruel.

Boundary.

The answer caused panic anyway.

"If we're deciding a future together—"

"I'm not ready to decide together."

There.

Arrival House mediator did not solve.

Helped words become specific.

You are allowed private information.

Your choice may affect someone else.

Those facts can coexist painfully.

Mau heard about the dispute later.

His instinct:

help.

Better instinct:

not his relationship.

He stayed out.

That success would matter because later he would fail on a larger version of the same principle.

Frieren requested terms.

Mau learned because she told him she was going.

He did not ask to come.

Progress.

She returned an hour later.

Mau wanted to ask everything.

Instead:

"Are you okay?"

Frieren considered.

"Not exactly."

"Do you want to tell me?"

"No."

Mau nodded.

That was one of the hardest things he did all season.
