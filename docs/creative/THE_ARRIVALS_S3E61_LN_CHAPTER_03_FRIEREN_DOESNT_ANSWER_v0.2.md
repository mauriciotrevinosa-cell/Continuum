# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 25 — Frieren Doesn't Answer

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 25

At night Mau almost asked.

Frieren lay beside him.

Awake.

He knew.

She knew he knew.

"What?"

Mau looked at ceiling.

"Nothing."

"Lie."

"Small one."

Frieren waited.

Mau:

"I want to know."

"I know."

"I don't want you to think you can't tell me if it's return."

Frieren turned toward him.

"Good."

"I also don't want you to return."

There.

Truth.

No manipulation.

No noble denial.

Frieren's face softened.

"Good."

Mau looked at her.

"Good?"

"I don't want you pretending you don't want me."

Fair.

She took his hand.

"I am not answering tonight."

"I know."

"You can be unhappy about that."

"I am."

"Good."

Mau laughed.

Wanting someone to choose you was not the same as choosing for them.

He understood.

For now.
