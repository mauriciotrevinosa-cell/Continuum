# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 31 — Mau Hears Everyone

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 31

Mau heard the dangerous sentences over a week.

Not one conversation.

Many.

"I never asked to come."

"My mother is there."

"If I return, maybe I can fix what I left."

"I want to stay but I don't know if guilt is deciding."

"I love people here."

"I miss home every day."

"I don't know if my partner will choose me."

"I don't know if I'll remember enough to miss them."

"I'd go if I knew I wouldn't regret it."

There.

Regret.

Again.

Another person:

"What if I stay and spend fifty years wondering?"

Another:

"What if I leave and dream this place forever?"

The Goddess had asked Mau whether people would choose him.

Mau's mind built a different problem.

What if choosing itself was cruelty?

He knew that conclusion was dangerous.

So he did nothing.

Day one.

Then day two.

Then day three.

The thought did not leave.

That mattered.

It was not an impulse.

It became reasoning.
