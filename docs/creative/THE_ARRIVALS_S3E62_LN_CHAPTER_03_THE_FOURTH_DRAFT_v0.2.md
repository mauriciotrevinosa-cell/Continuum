# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 28 — The Fourth Draft

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 28

Mau wrote a note.

Destroyed it.

The first sounded like suicide.

Wrong.

Second sounded like permission.

Also wrong.

Third talked too much about Frieren.

Destroyed.

Fourth:

"I'm going to ask whether a collective-return option exists that removes the burden of individual decision."

He stopped.

Read it.

Bad.

Added:

"I will not agree to anything irreversible without hearing the terms."

That made him feel responsible.

Still bad.

Added:

"I know this looks like what I promised not to do."

He stared.

Then:

"I don't think it is."

Sincere.

That was the problem.

Mau was not repeating Sukuna exactly.

He was not choosing death.

He was choosing to carry decision architecture.

Different flaw.

Same ownership.

He folded the note.

Did not leave yet.

One more night passed.

Frieren slept beside him.

Mau stayed.

In the morning, he almost tore the note.

Almost.
