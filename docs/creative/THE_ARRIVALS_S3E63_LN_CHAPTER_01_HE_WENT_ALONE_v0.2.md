# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 29 — He Went Alone

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 29

Mau left before dawn.

Again.

Different reason.

Different failure.

He did not take combat gear.

He took:
- note;
- water;
- coat;
- personal terms notebook blank;
- one route map.

No intention to die.

That distinction mattered to him.

Not enough.

He left the note where Frieren would see it.

Walked toward the old first clearing.

The place where remembered life began.

Symmetry he hated and still chose.

On the road, Mau rehearsed what he would ask.

Not:
send them home.

At least not first.

Ask:
Does a collective option exist?

Can regret be reduced?

Can memory loss be chosen?

Can people opt out?

He told himself questions were not decisions.

True.

He also knew he was going alone because everyone else would challenge the premise before he reached the Goddess.

Also true.

That was the betrayal.

Not destination.

Secrecy.

Mau reached the clearing.

No Goddess.

He waited.
