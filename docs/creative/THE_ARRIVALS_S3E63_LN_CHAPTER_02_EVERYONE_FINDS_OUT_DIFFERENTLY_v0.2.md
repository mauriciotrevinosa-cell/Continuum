Frieren woke because Mau was not in bed.

Not unusual by itself.

The room was too quiet.

She saw the note.

Everything in her body changed.

No screaming.

First line:

collective-return option.

Second:

remove the burden of individual decision.

Frieren was moving before she finished.

Then stopped.

Read all of it.

Important.

Not:
Mau plans to die.

Not:
Goddess deal complete.

Not:
everyone already sent home.

He was going to ask.

Alone.

Before dawn.

Day Three.

Frieren looked at time.

He could not be far.

That mattered.

She did not repeat Sukuna mistake from other side.

No blind panic without information.

Ori felt something else.

One maintenance marker near south path had gone unnaturally still for a few seconds.

Not Goddess confirmation.

Oddity.

She went to Mau's room.

Found Frieren already gone.

Note copy on table.

Rimuru saw central-route access log.

One person out before normal shift.

Mau.

Senku found a route marker nudged sideways.

Tiny.

Someone had passed carrying a bag and corrected marker after bumping it.

Mau behavior.

Okarun checked south route.

Fresh tracks.

No need for divine detection.

People.

Systems.

Network.

Bocchi only knew something was wrong because Frieren ran through common room wearing wrong shoes.

She followed to Arrival House.

"What happened?"

Ori handed her the note.

Bocchi read.

Face changed.

"He did it again."

Ori shook head.

"Not yet."

Important.

"He hasn't made anything."

Bocchi looked up.

"Then we can stop him."

Yes.

Not whole settlement.

Growth mattered.

Love.

Family.

Responsibility.

Anger.

Fear.

Knowledge of Mau.

Refusal to let one traumatized person become everyone's sole decision-maker.

First clearing.

First memory.

"If he left when note says, we can catch him."
