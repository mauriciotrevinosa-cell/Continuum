# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 31 — Interception

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 31

Mau stood in the clearing.

No Goddess visible.

No Contract.

Good.

Frieren arrived first.

"Mau."

He turned.

Relief crossed his face.

Wrong emotion.

He was glad they found him.

Meaning some part already knew.

"Did you make a deal?"

"No."

Truth.

"Ask for one?"

"Not yet."

Truth.

"Why are you here?"

Mau looked at everyone behind her.

"I wanted to know whether there's a collective return option."

Rimuru:

"Without us."

"I was going to ask for terms."

Senku:

"Not the same as asking us."

"I know."

Ori stepped forward.

"No. You understand those words."

Mau looked at her.

Ori's voice broke.

"You taught me that being created by someone doesn't mean they own my choices."

Direct hit.

Bocchi asked:

"Would you have come back before agreeing?"

Mau hesitated.

One second.

Enough.

Bocchi started crying.

Kita held her.

Frieren went still.

The clearing where Mau first remembered existence had become the place where everyone refused to let him author theirs.
