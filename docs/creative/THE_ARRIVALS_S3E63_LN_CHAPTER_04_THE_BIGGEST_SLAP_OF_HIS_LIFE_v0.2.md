# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 19
## Chapter 37 — The Biggest Slap of His Life

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 19  
**Reading order:** 37

Frieren asked:

"How could you decide my life without me again?"

Mau answered too quickly.

"Because you're going to believe this was a dream."

Silence.

Wrong.

He knew immediately.

Frieren:

"What?"

Mau tried to explain.

"The terms said Continuum may become dreamlike. If you returned and—"

"May."

"What?"

"May."

She stepped closer.

"You do not know."

"I would know before agreeing."

"For me?"

Mau stopped.

Frieren's voice shook.

"You would learn my terms and then decide?"

"No. I mean—"

"You mean you would carry the answer."

Mau tried again.

"If you believed this was a dream, losing it wouldn't hurt the same."

Frieren hit him.

One slap.

Hard.

No laughter.

No cheering.

Physical full stop because his logic kept moving past her agency.

Mau stared.

Frieren's hand shook more than his face hurt.

"Do not tell me what I am allowed to remember."

Tears.

"Do not tell me which life will hurt less."

Mau's expression collapsed.

Frieren continued:

"Maybe we came here against our will."

Breath.

"You still owe us the chance to choose whether we leave."

Mau whispered:

"I don't want anyone to regret staying because of me."

"That is also our choice."

"I don't want you to carry that weight."

Frieren laughed once through tears.

"You don't get to remove regret from my life by removing me from the decision."

No one made the slap funny yet.

It needed to hurt first.
