# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 1 — No One Waits

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Source episode:** S3E7 — `The Search`

<!-- scene-id: s3e7-ln01-firstten -->

The first ten minutes were bad.

Not because nobody knew what to do.

Because too many people did.

Frieren wanted north.

Rimuru wanted the room untouched until they knew what Mau took.

Mikasa wanted routes closed.

Okarun wanted to run every route at once.

Bocchi wanted shoes and could not get her hands to cooperate.

Kita helped.

Ori stood in the middle of the common room and looked more frightened than she had during her own first days.

"He didn't tell me."

Nobody accused her.

That almost made it worse.

Rimuru found the missing flask.

Coat.

Boots.

Watch.

No pack.

Not enough food for a planned trip.

"Confused departure," Maomao said.

Frieren's face became still.

"North."

Rimuru looked at her.

"Why?"

"He mentioned a cabin."

"When?"

"Last night."

No.

Frieren stopped.

"He didn't."

The Memory problem had already contaminated the investigation.

That realization changed the room.

<!-- scene-id: s3e7-ln01-search -->

They wrote down only things they could verify.

Door opened before dawn.

Boot prints outside.

North for the first stretch.

No horse.

No wagon.

Mau alone.

Rimuru divided routes.

Frieren took the direct north line.

Yuta went with her because someone needed to tell her when destroying a forest would not improve search quality.

She told him she knew that.

Yuta did not answer.

Okarun took the eastern cut.

Mikasa the ridge.

Himmel the west track.

Momo and Anko checked people who might have seen him.

Qifrey and Agott studied whether anything magical remained around the room or path.

Coco stayed with them.

Ori refused to stay.

Rimuru looked at her.

"I know him."

Frieren stopped at the door.

Then nodded.

Ori went with Frieren.

Not because she was strongest.

Because she knew which silences belonged to Mau.

<!-- scene-id: s3e7-ln01-world -->

At the future site, work did not begin.

For two hours.

Then Nano opened the planning book.

Wakana repaired the shelter.

Maomao sent someone to check the water samples.

Fern organized the people who had stayed.

Stark asked if they should really be doing this.

Fern looked north.

"No."

Then she looked at the leaking storage roof.

"But if he comes back and everything fell apart because he was gone, he'll apologize."

Stark hated how accurate that was.

They worked.

No one waited.

Everyone waited.
