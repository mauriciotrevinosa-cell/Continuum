# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 1 — No One Waits

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 1  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 1 — morning | Lived base: old inn | Mau: missing | Future site: provisional worksite

Breakfast lasted six seconds after Frieren realized Mau was not there.

Not six quiet seconds.

Six normal ones.

Stark complained about the bread.

Momo reached for something that was not hers.

Umaru had not fully opened her eyes.

Then Ori asked:

"Where's Mau?"

Frieren was already standing.

The chair hit the floor behind her.

She checked upstairs again even though she had just come from there.

Bedroom.

Empty.

Bathroom.

Empty.

Storage.

His boots were gone.

His coat.

One flask.

Not enough food.

No proper pack.

The room changed shape around those facts.

Rimuru arrived at the doorway.

"What happened?"

Frieren held up the flask that was not there.

"He left."

Nobody asked whether she was sure.

Not after the weeks of wrong memories.

They verified.

Door latch had been used before dawn.

One set of boot marks until the ground hardened.

No horse.

No wagon.

No sign of a struggle.

Mau had walked out.

Alone.

That was worse than kidnapping in a way nobody wanted to name.

Rimuru put a blank page on the table.

"Only verified facts."

Momo started to speak.

Rimuru raised one hand.

"Not what we think he said. Not what we remember unless somebody else can confirm it."

Frieren hated him for being right.

Ori looked at the stairs.

"He didn't tell me."

Nobody blamed her.

Her voice made it clear she had already done that herself.

The search split fast.

Frieren north.

Yuta with her.

Ori too.

Mikasa took the ridge route because height mattered more than speed there.

Okarun took the eastern cut and promised not to outrun his ability to return.

Anko and Momo went toward the roads where somebody might have seen a lone man before sunrise.

Himmel took the west approach.

Qifrey and Agott checked the room, path and first stretch for anything magical that did not belong.

Rimuru stayed for exactly four minutes.

Long enough to assign what he could.

Then he left too.

Fern stood in the common room after half of the household disappeared through different doors.

Stark looked at her.

"What do we do?"

Every instinct said search.

Every responsibility said something else.

At the future site, materials had been scheduled to arrive.

A water test needed checking before the sample became useless.

A merchant expected payment.

Two travelers were supposed to return for route information.

The world had not agreed to pause.

Fern picked up the planning ledger.

"We do both."

Stark stared.

"How?"

"Badly."

That was the first honest logistical doctrine of the search.

At the worksite, Nano crossed Mau's name out of three tasks.

Not because he no longer mattered.

Because he was not there.

Someone else took them.

The water sample was collected.

The merchant waited anyway because nobody wanted to decide where to unload the stone without Rimuru.

The north shelter leaked.

A route rumor went unverified.

By noon the community had already paid for Mau being missing in five different currencies.

Time.

People.

Attention.

Work.

Fear.

Frieren did not know any of that.

She was moving north through trees with Yuta behind her and Ori to the left.

Every footprint became possible.

Every broken branch became meaningful.

Every minute became an accusation.

Yuta finally said:

"Frieren."

She stopped.

He pointed backward.

"You've checked that track twice."

"No."

Ori looked at her.

Frieren closed her eyes.

Memory.

Again.

She opened them.

"Mark it."

Yuta tied cloth to the branch.

Verified route.

Not remembered route.

They continued.

Nobody waited.

Everyone waited.
