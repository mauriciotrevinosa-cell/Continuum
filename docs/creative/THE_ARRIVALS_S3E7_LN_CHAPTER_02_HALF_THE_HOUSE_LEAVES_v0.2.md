# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 2 — Half the House Leaves

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 2  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 1 — afternoon | Lived base: old inn | Search coverage expanding

By afternoon, the missing-person search had stopped looking like concern and started looking like an operation.

That frightened Agott.

Not because it was disorganized.

Because it wasn't.

Maps covered the old dining table.

Search lines were marked in charcoal.

Known paths.

Unverified paths.

Areas where Mau might have followed a memory rather than a real road.

Rimuru had returned once, changed three assignments, taken new information, and left again.

Frieren had not returned at all.

Neither had Yuta or Ori.

Militia rumors from farther west remained unchecked.

The future site lost its main survey team.

One Searcher candidate mission was cancelled before the group had ever officially been called Searchers.

A native family came to the inn asking whether the western checkpoint could be avoided.

Fern answered them because everyone who usually handled roads was outside looking for Mau.

The mother listened.

Then looked at the maps.

"Someone's missing?"

Fern nodded.

"Family."

The woman looked toward the door as if expecting someone more official to answer.

No one did.

Fern gave her an alternate route.

Not guaranteed safe.

Just better.

After they left, Nano added the encounter to the ledger.

External pressure.

Still happening.

The search consumed:
- two wagons;
- most available route maps;
- Yuta;
- Frieren;
- Ori;
- Mikasa;
- Okarun;
- Himmel;
- Momo;
- Anko;
- parts of Qifrey and Agott's day;
- most of Rimuru's attention;
- every conversation at the inn.

Stark stared at the list.

"That's a lot."

Fern did not answer.

It was.

That fact was beginning to become visible to people who had not known Mau from the first clearing.

Agott returned from checking a false trail with Qifrey near sunset.

Nothing.

Again.

She dropped her gloves on the table.

"This is inefficient."

Coco looked up sharply.

Agott immediately regretted the wording.

Qifrey sat.

"What part?"

Agott gestured at everything.

"All of it."

Coco's face hardened.

Agott continued before the argument became something else.

"I'm not saying stop."

"Sounds like it."

"I'm saying look."

She pointed at the empty places around the table.

"Frieren. Rimuru. Yuta. Mikasa. Half the people who make decisions at the site."

Coco crossed her arms.

"So?"

"So if anything else happens today, we're weaker."

Silence.

That was true.

Agott hated that truth did not make the question kind.

Fern said:

"Yes."

Coco looked at her.

Fern continued.

"The search costs something."

No defense.

No moral speech.

Just fact.

Agott looked down.

"Then why this much?"

Nobody answered immediately.

That answer deserved more than one person's love.

Qifrey said:

"Tomorrow, if we still haven't found him, ask me again."

Agott frowned.

"Why tomorrow?"

"Because today you're tired enough to confuse the question with an accusation."

Agott hated that too.

Also true.

Outside, someone lit the inn's route lantern.

The future site went dark earlier than planned because the people assigned to install the second lamp line were still in the forest.

The old inn remained home.

For tonight, it was also search headquarters.

Mau's chair stayed empty.
