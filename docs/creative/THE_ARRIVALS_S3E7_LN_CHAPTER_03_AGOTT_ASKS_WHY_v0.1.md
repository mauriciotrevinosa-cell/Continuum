## Chapter 3 — Agott Asks Why

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E7 — `The Search`

<!-- scene-id: s3e7-ln03-question -->

Agott asked Qifrey on the ridge.

Not cruelly.

That mattered.

They had spent two hours checking a path Mau probably had not used.

Frieren was somewhere north burning through mana on search spells that still had to respect terrain and range.

Rimuru was splitting himself between analysis and logistics.

Yuta had not eaten.

Okarun had run until his legs shook and then run again.

Agott looked down toward the old inn.

"Why are we spending this much?"

Qifrey knew what she meant.

"Mau?"

"Yes."

She was irritated by how guilty the word felt.

Agott continued.

"Frieren, I understand."

Qifrey raised an eyebrow.

"She loves him."

"Yes."

"Rimuru?"

"Friend."

"Yuta?"

"Friend."

"Okarun?"

"Friend."

Agott frowned.

"That can't be the answer for everyone."

"It isn't."

<!-- scene-id: s3e7-ln03-many -->

Qifrey sat on a rock.

Agott stayed standing.

"Coco is looking because she thinks of him as family."

Agott glanced away.

"Anko is looking because she doesn't trust a disappearance connected to altered memory."

"Mikasa?"

"Because a missing resident is a missing resident."

"Himmel?"

Qifrey smiled slightly.

"Because Frieren would tear the mountain apart alone if nobody went with her."

Agott almost smiled.

Almost.

"And you?"

Qifrey looked toward the trees.

"Because something interfered with one of ours."

Agott caught the word.

`Ours.`

Qifrey continued.

"Also because I don't know what caused it. If it can do this to Mau, I would like to know whether it can do it to Coco."

That landed.

Different reason.

Same direction.

<!-- scene-id: s3e7-ln03-answer -->

Agott looked at the search lines again.

Mau was not the strongest person in the settlement.

Not the oldest.

Not the wisest.

Not the best mage.

Not the best fighter.

He had no source-world nation.

No famous title.

No remembered family.

From a distance, he could look like the least important person everyone was treating as the most important.

"Does everyone love him?"

"No," Qifrey said.

Agott looked at him.

"Good."

Qifrey laughed.

"Some barely know him. Some are here because they trust the people who do. Some think the anomaly is dangerous. Some would do the same for you."

Agott looked offended.

"That would be inefficient."

"Probably."

They kept searching.

Agott did not suddenly understand Mau.

She understood the network better.

That was enough.
