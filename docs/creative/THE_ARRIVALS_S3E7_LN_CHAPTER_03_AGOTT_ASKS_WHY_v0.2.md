# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 3 — Agott Asks Why

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 1 — evening | Lived base: old inn | Search cost publicly visible

Agott asked the next question before tomorrow.

She and Qifrey were walking back from one last ridge check when they saw Frieren's spell flare far north.

Not an attack.

A search light.

Huge.

Brief.

Expensive.

Agott stopped.

Frieren was using enough magic to be visible across kilometers because one man was missing.

Below, Rimuru moved between routes.

Okarun crossed the east line again.

Himmel had not eaten since morning.

Agott said:

"Why are all of you spending this much on him?"

Qifrey did not pretend not to understand.

"Mau."

"Yes."

She looked embarrassed by her own question.

That made it better.

"Frieren, I understand."

"Because?"

"She loves him."

"Correct."

"Rimuru?"

"Friend."

"Yuta?"

"Friend."

"Okarun?"

"Friend."

Agott threw up one hand.

"That cannot be the answer for everybody."

"It isn't."

Qifrey sat on a rock.

Agott stayed standing because sitting would make the conversation feel too comfortable.

"Coco is here because she thinks of him as family."

Agott glanced away.

"Anko?"

"Because a disappearance tied to altered memory is dangerous even if she barely liked him."

"Mikasa?"

"Because a resident is missing."

"Himmel?"

Qifrey's mouth moved slightly.

"Because Frieren would search until she collapsed if nobody made her eat."

Agott looked north.

"And you?"

Qifrey took longer.

"Because somebody interfered with one of ours."

The word caught.

`Ours.`

Agott disliked how warm it sounded.

Qifrey continued.

"And because if something can alter Mau's memory that deeply, I would prefer to know whether it can do the same to Coco."

Different reason.

Same direction.

Agott looked at the distant light.

Mau was not the strongest person in the household.

Not the oldest.

Not the most experienced.

Not the best mage.

Not the best fighter.

He had no source-world title.

No remembered nation.

No famous bloodline.

Beside Frieren, Rimuru, Yuta, Milim—if rumors of people like her were even true—Mau looked almost aggressively normal.

"Does everyone love him?"

Qifrey laughed.

"No."

Agott relaxed enough to be annoyed at herself.

"Good."

"Some people barely know him."

"Then why are they searching?"

"Some trust people who do know him."

Qifrey counted on his fingers.

"Some owe him. Some care about someone who cares about him. Some think the anomaly is a threat to everyone if we ignore it. Some would search for any missing resident. Some are simply already on the route."

Agott looked at him.

"And some?"

Qifrey smiled.

"Some are curious why all the serious people are this worried."

Agott scowled.

"I am not—"

"You came back for a third route."

She had.

Qifrey stood.

"Mau has a lot of relationships."

"That's not the same as being important."

"No."

He started walking.

"Sometimes it is more useful."

Agott followed.

She did not come away loving Mau.

That would have ruined the point.

She came away understanding why a community could spend irrational-looking resources for rationally different reasons.

At the ridge, they found nothing.

At the inn, dinner had gone cold.

They ate anyway.

Tomorrow still looked expensive.
