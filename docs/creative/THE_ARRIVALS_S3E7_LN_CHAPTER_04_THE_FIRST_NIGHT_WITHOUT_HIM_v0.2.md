# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 4 — The First Night Without Him

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 4  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Night 1 | Old inn = search headquarters | Mau: missing

Frieren came back after midnight because Yuta made her.

Not persuaded.

Made.

"You've been walking for sixteen hours."

"So has Mau."

"We don't know that."

Wrong answer.

Frieren's face changed.

Yuta corrected immediately.

"We don't know anything. That's why you need enough sleep to notice what is real tomorrow."

Memory.

That word again.

Frieren hated him for using the correct argument.

The inn door opened.

Everyone looked.

One second.

Hope.

Then they saw only Frieren, Yuta and Ori.

The room changed back.

Mau had already become a sound people expected to hear at the door.

Frieren noticed his chair.

Did not sit.

Fern put food in front of her.

"I ate."

"When?"

Frieren tried to remember.

Bad sign.

Fern did not ask again.

She pushed bowl closer.

Frieren ate four bites.

Then unfolded the map.

"No."

Fern put hand over it.

Frieren looked up.

"No what?"

"No map while eating."

"This isn't the time."

"Exactly."

Frieren's eyes sharpened.

Fern did not move hand.

For a second the room remembered that Frieren could erase the table, wall and probably the argument.

Then Frieren let go.

Ate.

Afterward she went upstairs.

Mau's room.

Their room.

His side looked wrong because nothing was missing except the person.

No struggle.

No blood.

No obvious clue.

That made absence feel voluntary.

That hurt differently.

Frieren sat on bed.

Did not lie down.

Ori stood at door.

"He didn't tell me either."

Frieren looked.

Ori's hands were shaking.

"I know."

No blame.

They sat.

At 1:17, Frieren got up.

At 1:19, Ori stopped her at stairs.

"Where?"

"North."

"You already checked north."

"Not enough."

Ori swallowed.

"Then I'm coming."

Frieren looked at her.

Too tired to argue.

Yuta was waiting downstairs because he had predicted this.

"Three hours."

Frieren:

"No."

"Two."

"No."

"One."

Frieren stared.

Yuta stared back.

Finally:

"Forty minutes."

Yuta sighed.

"Terrible negotiation."

Frieren lay on couch because going back upstairs felt impossible.

Closed her eyes.

Saw Mau walking away.

Opened.

Not memory.

Possibility.

Closed again.

Saw him bleeding.

Opened.

Not memory.

Possibility.

Closed.

A voice that sounded like Mau said something she could not verify.

She sat up.

Twenty-three minutes.

That was all.

Yuta saw.

Did not call it sleep.

Before dawn Frieren was outside again.

The first night without Mau ended without rest.

The second day began before anyone was ready.
