# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 6 — Day Two Without Mau

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 6  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 2 | Mau missing >24h | Frieren severe sleep deprivation begins to affect judgment

By noon on Day Two, Frieren had slept less than an hour.

Nobody knew exact amount.

Frieren included.

That mattered.

She checked the same stream twice.

Not same bank.

Same stream.

Yuta recognized the cloth marker.

"Frieren."

She looked.

Then marker.

Blue.

They had tied blue this morning.

Or yesterday.

She could not immediately place it.

Her stomach dropped.

Memory Arc had weaponized uncertainty so efficiently that ordinary exhaustion now resembled attack.

"I remember this."

Yuta said:

"Good."

"Do you?"

"Yes."

Ori nodded.

Shared verification.

Frieren breathed.

Then hated needing it.

They moved.

At the inn, search costs accumulated.

The future worksite still needed hands.

One drainage trench had been left open after rain.

Two people went to close it.

Not because construction mattered more than Mau.

Because somebody falling into it would create another emergency.

Agott went.

Coco went too.

They worked in silence for twenty minutes.

Then Coco said:

"Do you think they'll find him today?"

Agott kept shoveling.

"I don't know."

Coco hated answer.

Agott hated lying more.

A traveler arrived.

Asked for road south.

Fern gave route.

A child needed food.

Stark cooked.

Nothing waited for grief.

Late afternoon, Frieren returned because one search line needed remapping.

She stood at table.

Pointed to three routes.

"This one is wrong."

Nano looked.

"Why?"

"I checked it."

"When?"

Frieren stopped.

No answer.

Rimuru, just returned from another route, saw.

"Frieren."

"No."

He had not said anything yet.

"No what?"

"I'm going back."

"You need sleep."

"Mau needs—"

She stopped.

No one knew what Mau needed.

Alive?

Injured?

Lost?

Hiding?

Frieren's face broke for half second.

Then hardened.

Rimuru did not order.

"Thirty minutes. Eat. Then choose."

Frieren looked like she might attack the concept of time.

Instead she sat.

A newcomer would have thought the terrifying elf was calm.

Everyone who knew her saw:
hands too still;
eyes unfocused between sentences;
magic held too close to surface.

Qifrey noticed too.

"She's going to make a mistake."

Rimuru:

"I know."

"We stop her?"

"How?"

No answer.

Love did not give them ownership either.

They could only keep making rest available.

Frieren ate.

Did not sleep.

At dusk, a pressure appeared on southern edge of perception.

Not Mau.

Something powerful.

Demonic.

Frieren's head lifted.

Rimuru felt it a second later.

He knew the presence.

Frieren did not.

Diablo was walking toward the old inn.

Frieren had spent almost two days failing to find Mau.

Then a demon strong enough to bend the air around him arrived at her home.

Context never had a chance.
