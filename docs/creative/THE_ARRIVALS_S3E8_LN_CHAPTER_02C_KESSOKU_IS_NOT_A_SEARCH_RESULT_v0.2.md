# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 9 — Kessoku Is Not a Search Result

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 2 — late night | Mau still missing | Diablo false-positive search continues

The third return was louder.

Not because Diablo announced himself.

Fern had forbidden that.

Because one of the people behind him said:

"Why is there a crater outside?"

Bocchi knew the voice.

Her spoon hit the bowl.

Kita turned before Bocchi did.

"Nijika?"

The door opened fully.

Nijika Ijichi stood on the threshold with one hand still on a travel bag she did not remember packing.

Beside her, Ryo Yamada looked at the inn, the crater, Diablo, the number of armed people inside, and said:

"This seems expensive."

Kita made a sound that did not belong to language.

Then crossed the room.

Nijika caught her.

Impact.

Laugh.

Crying immediately after.

Bocchi did not move.

That hurt worse than moving.

Ryo saw her first.

"Bocchi."

Bocchi's entire body locked.

She had imagined this.

Not here.

Not like this.

Not after months of building a life without knowing whether any of them still existed.

Nijika turned.

Her face changed.

"Hitori."

That did it.

Bocchi took one step.

Stopped.

Took another.

Then Nijika came to her.

Not running.

Not making Bocchi survive the distance alone.

Bocchi held herself together until arms closed around her.

Then failed.

Badly.

Words arrived in pieces.

"I—"

"Yeah."

"I thought—"

"Yeah."

"Kita was—"

"I know."

"You don't know."

"Then tell me later."

Bocchi cried into Nijika's shoulder.

Ryo stood nearby.

Kita looked at her.

"You too."

Ryo:

"I don't do group hugs."

Kita grabbed her anyway.

Ryo tolerated four seconds.

Five.

Then:

"My ribs."

Kita let go.

Ryo adjusted her shirt.

"Acceptable."

That was how Kessoku Band became whole in Continuum.

Not on a stage.

In an overcrowded inn while someone else was missing.

The joy did not erase the room.

Bocchi pulled back and remembered.

Her eyes went immediately to Frieren.

Nijika noticed.

"What's wrong?"

Bocchi wiped her face too fast.

"Someone's missing."

Ryo looked at Diablo.

"Us?"

"No."

"Then who?"

Bocchi swallowed.

"Mau."

The name meant nothing to them.

Yet.

Kita sat Nijika down.

Started explaining too much.

Arrival.

Household.

Mau.

Construction.

Memory problems.

Search.

Frieren.

She made it three minutes before Fern interrupted.

"Breathe."

Kita breathed.

Ryo had already found the worksite notes.

Senku looked up.

"Don't touch those."

Ryo touched them.

"What is this?"

"Drainage."

"Looks wet."

"It is drainage."

"Then accurate."

Senku stared.

Nijika laughed through red eyes.

The sound made Bocchi flinch and smile at once.

Home had acquired an old sound.

That night did not become celebration.

There was too much fear for that.

But practical life changed.

Nijika saw dishes stacked beside people too tired to wash them and started washing.

Not heroic.

Useful.

Kita joined.

Bocchi tried.

Dropped one bowl.

It survived.

Ryo found the least useful task possible and somehow made it useful by sitting near the door with a lantern and keeping track of when searchers came back.

Every return:

head up.

Check.

Not Mau.

Write time.

Head down.

At the fourth entry, Bocchi realized Ryo had been doing it for an hour.

"You care."

Ryo looked offended.

"I like records."

"Right."

"Also if he is important enough to make you look like that, I would prefer he not be dead."

Bocchi's mouth opened.

Ryo returned to the page.

That was the first thing Ryo ever said about Mau.

It was enough.

Near midnight, Nijika found Bocchi alone beside the stairs.

"You okay?"

No.

Bocchi nodded.

Nijika waited.

Bocchi corrected:

"No."

"Better."

Bocchi looked at the floor.

"I'm happy you're here."

"I know."

"And I feel awful being happy because he's still gone."

Nijika sat beside her.

"Those can both be true."

Bocchi's shoulders loosened.

Not much.

Enough.

Outside, Diablo crossed the dark again.

Inside, Kessoku Band existed in one room for the first time since arrival had split them.

No one played music.

Not tonight.

But Ryo asked where the bass was.

Bocchi laughed.

Small.

Real.

For one minute the inn held joy without treating it as betrayal.

Then the door opened again.

Everyone looked.
