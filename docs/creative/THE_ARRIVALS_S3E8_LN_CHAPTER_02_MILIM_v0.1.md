<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln02-impact -->

Milim arrived loudly.

Not by choice.

The first warning was Okarun yelling:

"SOMETHING'S COMING."

The second was the sky becoming briefly wrong.

The third was Milim stopping above the road hard enough that leaves moved in a circle below her.

Rimuru stared.

Milim stared back.

"RIMURU!"

She hit him.

Hugged.

Technically.

Rimuru became slime because geometry gave up.

Milim lifted him.

"You were here! I knew it!"

Rimuru made several sounds.

Diablo stood nearby with the expression of someone choosing not to comment because history would punish him later.

Milim noticed him.

"Oh. You too."

Diablo's eye twitched.

Perfect reunion.

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln02-notnow -->

Rimuru eventually regained shape.

Milim asked seventeen questions without waiting for answers.

Then Frieren landed nearby.

Milim stopped.

Frieren's face did not invite interruption.

"Did you find him?"

Rimuru shook his head.

Milim looked between them.

"Find who?"

"Mau," Rimuru said.

"Who's Mau?"

Nobody had time to explain correctly.

Frieren said:

"My boyfriend."

Milim blinked.

Then looked at Rimuru.

"Your friend's boyfriend is missing?"

"My friend."

"Your boyfriend?"

"No."

"Rimuru's friend," Diablo said.

Milim nodded.

"Okay."

She pointed north.

"How far can I search?"

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln02-limit -->

Milim was faster than almost everyone there.

That did not make the forest transparent.

Continuum did not hand her perfect knowledge because she was powerful somewhere else.

Her range felt wrong.

Some outputs came easily.

Others did not.

The world resisted assumptions.

Milim hated that for three minutes.

Then adapted.

She searched ridges others would take hours to reach.

Checked clearings.

Scared several animals.

Found no Mau.

When she returned, Agott watched her land.

Then looked at the map.

Then at all the people still searching.

Another absurdly powerful person.

For one ordinary-looking missing man.

Agott understood her own question less.

Good.
