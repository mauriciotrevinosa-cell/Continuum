# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 8 — Mai Doesn't Hug First

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, Day 2 — late night | Diablo search active | Mau still missing | G5 Wave B begins

The second time Diablo opened the door, nobody stood up as quickly.

That was the beginning of learning.

Frieren still did.

Halfway.

Enough that the chair scraped.

Then she saw the person behind him.

Not Mau.

A girl with dark hair, a weapon held low but ready, and the expression of someone who had already decided Diablo was intolerable.

Diablo said:

"I found another—"

"No," Senku called from the table.

Diablo looked at him.

"You do not know what I was going to say."

"You were going to be wrong again."

Suika hid a smile in the edge of the blanket Fern had given her.

The newcomer looked around.

Old inn.

Too many strangers.

An elf who looked like she had forgotten what sleep was.

A blue-haired slime-person.

A girl in glasses staring at her as if the world had just struck a match inside her chest.

Maki stood.

The room changed.

Not because of power.

Because Maki stopped breathing.

The newcomer did too.

Mai.

No one said her name first.

That mattered.

Maki took one step.

Mai raised the weapon.

Not at Maki.

Not quite.

"Don't."

Maki stopped.

Every instinct in the room wanted to turn reunion into motion.

Hug.

Question.

Proof.

Maki did none of it.

"Okay."

Mai stared at her.

"That's it?"

"What do you want me to do?"

"I don't know."

"Me neither."

Good.

The silence that followed belonged to them.

Diablo, for once, had enough sense not to fill it.

Mai looked Maki up and down.

Older in some ways.

Different clothes.

Different scars.

Standing in a room with people Mai did not know.

"You look terrible."

Maki laughed once.

It came out wrong.

"So do you."

"Good."

Another silence.

Then Mai's eyes flicked toward Yuta.

Recognition arrived differently there.

Yuta's face softened.

"Mai."

She looked away first.

"Don't make it weird."

"I wasn't—"

"You were about to."

"I wasn't."

"You have the face."

Maki almost smiled.

Almost.

Frieren sat back down.

Not because she stopped caring about the door.

Because this person was not hers.

That distinction remained possible even now.

Fern took over intake.

Not interview.

Immediate safety.

"Are you hurt?"

Mai looked at Diablo.

"Only psychologically."

Diablo seemed pleased.

Fern ignored him.

"Anyone following you?"

"Not that I know."

"Do you know where you are?"

"No."

"Do you want the long explanation now or food first?"

Mai looked at Maki again.

"Food."

Reasonable.

Maki did not sit beside her automatically.

Mai noticed.

"You're not going to hover?"

"I can if you want."

"Absolutely not."

"Then no."

That answer did more than a hug would have.

At the table, Mai saw the maps.

Search routes.

Names.

Markers.

One name repeated.

MAU.

She pointed.

"Who?"

Senku answered without looking up.

"The missing guy."

Suika corrected softly.

"The person they're looking for."

Mai looked toward Frieren.

Then Maki.

"Friend?"

Maki took too long.

Not because she did not know.

Because Mau was several relationships at once.

"Family."

Mai's eyes narrowed.

"Yours?"

"Mine too."

Interesting.

Not enough to create care.

Enough to create context.

Mai ate.

Maki sat across from her.

Not beside.

Questions arrived sideways.

"How long have you been here?"

"Long enough."

"Helpful."

"You?"

"Long enough to hate it."

"That sounds right."

No reconciliation speech.

No source-world summary.

No immediate forgiveness for anything.

Just two sisters sharing bad soup in another world while a search continued outside.

At one point Mai asked:

"So this Mau guy is why that demon keeps kidnapping people?"

Diablo objected from the doorway.

"I am not kidnapping anyone."

Senku:

"You absolutely kidnapped me."

Suika raised one hand.

"Me too."

Diablo looked offended by the word.

Mai looked at Maki.

"Your new life is stupid."

Maki finally smiled.

"Yeah."

Diablo turned to leave again.

The door opened.

Cold air came in.

Everyone looked.

Even after learning.

Even knowing.

Hope still moved faster than reason.

Diablo saw it this time.

His expression changed before he stepped outside.

He shut the door gently.

Mai watched Frieren stare at the wood after he was gone.

Then looked at Maki.

"How bad?"

Maki's smile disappeared.

"Bad."

Mai did not know Mau.

She still moved her plate aside and reached for the nearest route sheet.

Not love.

Not loyalty.

A sister she had not expected to see again lived here.

That was enough to ask:

"What can I do while we wait?"
