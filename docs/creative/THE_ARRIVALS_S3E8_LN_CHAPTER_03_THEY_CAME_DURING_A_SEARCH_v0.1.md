## Chapter 6 — They Came During a Search

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Source episode:** S3E8 — `Bad Timing for a Reunion`

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln03-inn -->

Milim's first meal at the inn was cold.

Diablo's too.

Nobody apologized.

That told them more about the place than a welcome speech would have.

Umaru pointed at the food.

"Usually better."

Milim ate it anyway.

"This is good."

"It's cold."

"I've eaten worse."

Stark looked offended on behalf of the kitchen.

Diablo did not sit until Rimuru did.

Rimuru did not sit.

Nano handed him a new route summary.

The reunion kept waiting.

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln03-jobs -->

Milim asked why they did not simply search everyone at once.

Fern answered.

"Because someone has to be here."

Milim looked around.

At people who had stayed.

At the missing-person board.

At the future-site plans.

At two native travelers sleeping in a side room because the road west had become unsafe after dark.

The settlement did not exist yet.

Its responsibilities already did.

Diablo studied the board.

"Thirty-four."

Nano corrected him.

"Thirty-three. One card is a route witness, not a possible Arrival."

Diablo looked at the wall again.

"Organized."

Momo laughed from the kitchen.

"No."

Nano said, "Increasingly."

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln03-different -->

Diablo searched because Rimuru asked.

Milim searched because Rimuru was worried and because somebody missing sounded unacceptable.

Frieren searched because she loved Mau.

Ori searched because she could not understand how he left without telling her after everything they had promised about belonging.

Agott searched because by then stopping would have meant pretending she had not started caring whether the answer existed.

Yuta searched because he had already watched Mau die once.

Himmel searched because Frieren had once taught him how much fifty years could cost when you assumed time would wait.

No single reason held the search together.

That made it stronger.

And messier.

At sunset, a signal came from the northern forest.

Not from Frieren.

Not Rimuru.

A witch-mark nobody in the inn recognized.

Qifrey looked at it.

Diablo looked too.

Both said, at nearly the same time:

"That's deliberate."

Frieren was already moving.
