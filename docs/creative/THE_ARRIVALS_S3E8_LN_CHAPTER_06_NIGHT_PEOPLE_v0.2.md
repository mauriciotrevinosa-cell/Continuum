# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 11 — Night People

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, deep night | Mau still missing | Nazuna enters as Diablo false positive

Anko knew the smell before the door opened.

She stood.

Not quickly.

That would have admitted too much.

Diablo came inside first.

Behind him:

pink hair.

Barely contained irritation.

A girl who looked at the room and immediately decided the room was too sober.

Nazuna Nanakusa.

Anko's cigarette fell from her fingers.

Nazuna saw her.

"Oh."

That one syllable carried enough history to make everyone else irrelevant.

Anko did not move.

Nazuna did not either.

Then Nazuna looked at Diablo.

"You said there was a guy with a weird body."

"There is."

"Where?"

"Missing."

Nazuna stared.

"You brought me to the place where the guy isn't?"

Diablo:

"Your signature matched several unstable criteria."

"That's the stupidest sentence I've heard tonight."

Anko laughed.

It escaped.

Nazuna's eyes returned to her.

"Still smoking."

Anko bent to pick up the cigarette.

"Still annoying."

"Good."

No hug.

Good.

Their reunion was not built for hugging.

Nazuna crossed the room until only a few steps remained.

Anko watched every one.

"How long?" Nazuna asked.

Anko answered with the Continuum version.

"Long enough."

"That's not a number."

"I don't have one you would like."

Nazuna looked around again.

At Maomao.

At medical supplies.

At windows covered for privacy.

At the overcrowded floor.

Then at the maps.

"Who's Mau?"

There it was again.

The question.

Anko pointed with the unlit cigarette toward Frieren.

"Her person."

Frieren looked over.

Not offended by the simplification.

Too tired.

Nazuna followed the line.

"Missing?"

"Yes."

"Dead?"

"No," Frieren said.

Too fast.

Nazuna understood enough not to push.

Fern asked the intake questions.

Nazuna answered most with jokes until Maomao asked:

"Do you require blood?"

The room stopped.

Nazuna blinked.

"Direct."

"Do you?"

"Sometimes."

"How often?"

"Depends."

"On?"

Nazuna smiled.

"Lots."

Maomao did not smile back.

"Then tomorrow we define safe supply."

Anko looked at her sharply.

Nazuna too.

No disgust.

No monster speech.

Logistics.

That landed harder than acceptance would have.

Nazuna leaned back.

"Huh."

Maomao wrote a note.

"Also no biting strangers."

Nazuna:

"That was already implied."

Anko coughed.

Nazuna looked at her.

The look lasted too long.

Future problem.

Not tonight.

Important.

Continuum had already changed enough rules that nobody should assume source conditions still worked exactly the same.

No experiment by accident.

No casual bite as comedy.

Anko read the room and knew exactly where that could go wrong.

"She's serious," Anko said.

Nazuna's expression softened by one degree.

"I know."

Later, Nazuna refused a bed.

"Night."

Fern:

"You still need somewhere to put your stuff."

Nazuna:

"Roof?"

Fern stared.

"Not the roof."

"Why?"

"People live under it."

"Exactly."

No.

She got a corner in the provisional outside shelter with the option to move.

Anko found her there before dawn.

Neither admitted it was deliberate.

Nazuna sat on a crate.

"You live here?"

"Sort of."

"You like it?"

Anko lit another cigarette.

"Sort of."

"Helpful."

"You're welcome."

From the road came the faint sound of someone returning.

Both looked automatically.

Nazuna noticed Anko's reaction.

"You care about the missing guy?"

Anko took a drag.

"Yes."

"Why?"

Anko could have told a story.

Did not.

"He shows up."

Nazuna frowned.

"He's currently not."

"That's the problem."

For once Nazuna had no joke.

Diablo appeared without Mau.

Everyone inside would look again.

Nazuna saw the pattern from outside.

Hope.

Recognition.

Drop.

She had known Mau for zero minutes.

By sunrise she understood one fact about him:

his absence had a shape.
