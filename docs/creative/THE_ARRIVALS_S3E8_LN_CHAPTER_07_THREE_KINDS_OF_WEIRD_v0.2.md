# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 13 — Three Kinds of Weird

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 13  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, pre-dawn | Mau still missing | Dandadan-side G5 cluster enters

Aira heard Seiko before she saw her.

"WHO LET THE DEMON PICK PEOPLE UP?"

Diablo entered first.

His dignity was under sustained attack.

Seiko Ayase followed with the expression of a woman prepared to exorcise the building if the explanation was bad.

Vamola came behind her, exhausted and alert.

Turbo Granny came last and insulted the threshold.

Momo stood.

Okarun stood.

Aira stood faster than both and immediately pretended she had not.

"Grandma?"

Momo's voice broke.

Seiko looked at her.

All the anger left her face.

"Momo."

Then Momo crossed the room.

Seiko caught her.

No hesitation.

Okarun stopped several steps away until Seiko reached one hand toward him without letting go of Momo.

He came too.

Aira looked at Vamola.

Vamola looked back.

Recognition took longer.

Then both moved.

Not dramatic.

Just relief becoming physical.

Turbo Granny:

"Pathetic."

Her voice was suspiciously less sharp.

Momo, still holding Seiko:

"Shut up."

"Make me."

Good.

The inn had no capacity for another reunion.

It made capacity anyway.

Chairs moved.

A bedroll rolled tighter.

Someone found water.

Vamola ate before explanation.

Seiko demanded explanation before water.

Fern forced water first.

Seiko respected Fern immediately.

"Good."

Fern looked alarmed.

Momo tried to explain Continuum.

Failed.

Okarun tried.

Failed differently.

Aira interrupted both.

"People from other worlds appear. Rules don't transfer perfectly. Mau is missing. Diablo is trying to find him and keeps bringing everyone else."

Seiko looked at Diablo.

"You're bad at this."

Diablo's eye twitched.

Turbo Granny laughed so hard she nearly fell off the chair.

Vamola asked:

"Mau?"

Again.

The word had become ritual.

Momo answered this time.

"Friend."

Okarun added:

"Family."

Aira:

"Idiot."

Momo looked at her.

Aira crossed her arms.

"He's an idiot. That's relevant."

Seiko watched all three.

Then looked toward Frieren.

She did not need spiritual expertise to recognize someone held together by one task.

"How long?"

"Almost two days," Rimuru said.

Seiko's expression sharpened.

"Sleep?"

Frieren did not answer.

Fern answered for her.

"Almost none."

Seiko walked over.

Frieren raised her eyes.

For a moment the room expected conflict.

Seiko said:

"If you fall down, you stop helping."

Frieren:

"I know."

"You know like a person who will sleep?"

"No."

Seiko clicked her tongue.

"Annoying."

Frieren almost smiled.

Enough.

At the worksite before dawn, Seiko refused to let Momo stay idle.

"If you're waiting, move something."

Momo:

"That's your wisdom?"

"Yes."

Turbo Granny inspected the road and complained about several spiritual impressions that nobody else had named yet.

Not answers.

Signals.

Vamola noticed marks on a storage tarp that looked like damage from something none of the builders recognized.

Okarun wrote it down.

Aira asked:

"Yokai?"

Seiko shook her head.

"Maybe. Maybe not. Don't name things before they earn it."

Good rule for Continuum.

The world was already producing phenomena that resembled known categories without promising to obey them.

Suika helped Vamola with a stack too tall for either alone.

Ryo watched and refused to help until Nijika stared at her.

Then helped.

The worksite continued.

That was important.

Mau missing did not freeze weather.

Did not stop drainage.

Did not stop people needing shelter.

Near dawn, Turbo Granny went still.

Everyone nearby noticed.

"What?" Okarun asked.

She looked toward the north.

"Something ugly."

Diablo, returning at the edge of the road, looked the same direction.

Not Mau.

Not yet.

But the search had just found a different kind of signal.

The Dandadan group had arrived as false positives.

Their first contribution was not combat.

It was knowing enough about strange things to say:

do not assume you already know what strange means here.
