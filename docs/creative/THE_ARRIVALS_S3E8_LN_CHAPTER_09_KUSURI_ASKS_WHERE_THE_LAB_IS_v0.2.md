# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 15 — Kusuri Asks Where the Lab Is

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 15  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, pre-dawn | Mau still missing | Kusuri Yakuzen joins G5 | Existing 100 Girlfriends-side relationships reconnect

Diablo returned with a girl carrying too many bottles.

That was the first problem.

The second was that four people in the inn recognized her at once.

Hakari stood.

Karane stood faster.

Shizuka's book slipped out of her hands.

Nano stopped writing in the middle of a line.

"Kusuri?"

The newcomer froze in the doorway.

Then her whole face changed.

"Hakari! Karane! Shizuka! Nano!"

She ran.

Diablo moved one step aside with the timing of a man learning that reunions were safer if he did not stand between them.

Hakari caught Kusuri first.

Karane reached them half a second later while insisting she was not joining a group hug.

She joined the group hug.

Shizuka came next.

Nano last.

Not because she cared less.

Because she had taken the time to put the pen down properly.

Kusuri squeezed all of them at once.

For several seconds the inn stopped being a search headquarters.

It became five people proving one another existed.

Kusuri pulled back.

Looked at each face.

Touched Hakari's sleeve.

Karane's shoulder.

Shizuka's hands.

Nano's cheek.

"Real."

Nano answered:

"Current evidence strongly supports that."

Karane's eyes were wet.

"Could you say something normal?"

Nano:

"I am pleased to see you."

Karane cried harder.

"That's worse."

Hakari laughed.

Shizuka opened her book with shaking hands and showed a line she had prepared for impossible reunions without knowing she had prepared it.

Kusuri read.

Her mouth folded.

She hugged Shizuka again.

No one translated for the room.

They did not need to.

This reunion did not belong to Mau.

Good.

Then a small glass bottle slipped from Kusuri's bag.

Maomao caught it before it hit the floor.

Everyone looked at her.

Maomao looked at the liquid.

Then at Kusuri.

"What is this?"

Kusuri brightened.

"Oh, that's—"

"No."

Kusuri stopped.

Maomao held the bottle away from herself.

"No explanation while I am holding an unknown liquid."

Senku looked up from the table.

"What concentration?"

Maomao:

"No."

"I didn't touch it."

"You were about to."

"I was asking."

"With your face."

Momo, from three seats away:

"This is going to be incredible."

Maomao ignored her.

Kusuri looked between Maomao and Senku.

Recognition of a different kind.

Not source-world.

Professional danger.

"You're a pharmacist?"

"Apothecary."

"Close enough."

"No."

Senku:

"What's in the bottle?"

Maomao:

"Still no."

Kusuri took the bottle back carefully.

"Okay, okay."

She put it inside the bag.

Maomao pointed.

"Do not open that bag again until we have a table, labels, ventilation and a list."

Kusuri stared.

Then smiled.

"I like you."

Maomao looked immediately suspicious.

Reasonable.

Fern stepped in before chemistry became the new emergency.

"Name."

"Kusuri Yakuzen."

"Are you hurt?"

"Nope."

"Anyone following you?"

"Not that I know."

"Do you need food?"

"Yes."

"Water?"

"Yes."

"Medicine?"

Kusuri looked offended.

"I have medicine."

Maomao:

"That was not the question."

Kusuri reconsidered.

"No."

Better.

Fern pointed to a chair.

Kusuri sat.

Then looked around properly.

The inn was too full.

Maps covered one wall.

Route times another.

People she knew were here.

People she did not know looked exhausted.

One elf looked like sleep had become theoretical.

Kusuri's smile faded.

"What's happening?"

Karane sat beside her.

"Someone's missing."

Kusuri looked at the board.

The same name appeared everywhere.

MAU.

"Who is Mau?"

The room took the question differently now.

It had been asked too many times.

Hakari answered first.

"Someone important here."

Nano added:

"Missing for nearly two days."

Shizuka turned her book and showed a shorter line.

Friend.

Karane looked toward Frieren's empty chair.

"Family."

Kusuri followed the look.

"Can I help find him?"

The instinct was immediate.

Good.

The answer was still no.

Rimuru crouched by the map.

"Not by giving people anything."

Kusuri blinked.

"I didn't say—"

Maomao:

"You were going to."

Kusuri:

"Maybe."

Senku laughed.

Rimuru continued.

"We don't know what is happening to Mau's body or memory. No stimulant. No sedative. No experimental compound. Nothing meant to make tracking easier."

Kusuri's expression became serious.

Fast.

Not childish.

Scientist.

"Because changing his state could change what you're trying to measure."

Senku looked over.

"Exactly."

Maomao:

"And because he can't consent to a drug while missing and cognitively unstable."

Kusuri looked at her.

Then nodded.

"Exactly."

That was the first rule they all agreed on.

No experiment because a crisis made experimentation convenient.

Kusuri asked to see the medical notes instead.

Maomao said no.

Kusuri asked why.

"Privacy."

"Can I see the supply list?"

"Yes."

Different question.

Good.

The supply list was ugly.

Bandages.

Clean cloth.

Water storage.

Basic medicines.

Ingredients from worlds that did not share names.

Things Maomao understood by effect but not source.

Things Yuta understood by magic but not chemistry.

Things nobody had tested enough.

Kusuri read.

"Your labels are terrible."

Maomao's eyes narrowed.

Senku said:

"They are."

Maomao turned on him.

"You arrived tonight."

"And I can read."

Kusuri laughed.

For the first time since arriving, Karane looked normal enough to get annoyed.

"Don't encourage her."

"Why not?"

"Because there will be five explosions by breakfast."

Kusuri looked genuinely offended.

"Five?"

Hakari:

"Minimum."

Shizuka hid a smile behind the book.

Nano had already started a new page.

KUSURI — MATERIAL INVENTORY / DO NOT DISTRIBUTE WITHOUT REVIEW.

Kusuri read over her shoulder.

"That's strict."

Nano:

"Necessary."

"Fair."

That answer made Nano look at her longer.

Continuum had already changed the group.

Not personalities erased.

Priorities widened.

Kusuri noticed.

She would ask later.

Not tonight.

At the worksite, Maomao permitted one box from Kusuri's bag to be opened.

One.

On a clean cloth.

Senku present.

Nano recording.

No tasting.

No testing on anyone.

No "I know what this does in my world" treated as sufficient evidence.

Kusuri objected to the last rule.

"It's my formula."

Senku:

"Different world."

Maomao:

"Different bodies."

Kusuri looked at the bottle.

Then at Vamola.

Nazuna.

Rimuru.

Kaneki in the distance.

People whose biology made the word human useless as a universal assumption.

"Okay."

No tantrum.

Revision.

That mattered more than expertise.

She relabeled the first bottle with:
source purpose;
known source effects;
unknown Continuum effects;
do not administer.

Senku added:
storage conditions unknown.

Maomao added:
keep away from food.

Kusuri added:
probably.

Maomao crossed out probably.

They argued for six minutes.

Momo watched.

"This is definitely flirting too."

Three voices answered at once.

"No."

Momo smiled.

"Science triangle."

Maomao threatened her.

Kusuri asked Senku what he did.

"Science."

"That's not a field."

"Everything is a field if you're good enough."

Kusuri stared.

"I might hate you."

Senku grinned.

"Promising."

Suika, carrying a stack of cloth nearby, sighed with the exhausted familiarity of someone who understood this type of person.

Kusuri helped with supplies until dawn.

Not because she loved Mau.

She had never met him.

Because people she loved had built lives here.

Because the inn had a missing person and too few hands.

Because the medical shelf was a crime.

Because the worksite needed safer storage.

Because doing something concrete was better than watching the door.

When Diablo returned again, every head turned.

Kusuri turned too.

She had been in the inn less than two hours.

Already learned the reflex.

Diablo was alone.

Not Mau.

The disappointment moved through the room.

Kusuri looked at the map.

Then at Hakari, Karane, Shizuka and Nano.

"You all live here?"

Hakari:

"Yes."

Karane:

"Sort of."

Nano:

"At present."

Shizuka nodded.

Kusuri looked toward the worksite notes.

Toward Maomao's rewritten labels.

Toward Senku timing his own calculations.

Toward the overfull room.

"Can I stay?"

Fern answered carefully.

"Tonight?"

Kusuri thought.

"No."

Everyone looked.

She corrected:

"I mean... not only tonight."

Fern did not promise permanent anything.

Good.

"You can stay while you decide what that means."

Kusuri smiled.

"Yep."

No destiny mechanic.

No automatic family status.

No scientist conscription.

A choice.

Small enough to be real.

Later, when Return and Stay became actual words with consequences, Kusuri would still have to choose again.

For now, she chose the next morning.

That was enough.
