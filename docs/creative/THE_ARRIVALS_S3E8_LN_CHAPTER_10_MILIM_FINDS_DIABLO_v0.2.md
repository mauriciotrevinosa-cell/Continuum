# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 16 — Milim Finds Diablo

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Continuity state:** First disappearance, dawn approaching | Mau still missing | Milim joins G5 window by recognizing Diablo

Milim was the first person Diablo brought back who had not been a mistake.

Technically.

She found him.

Diablo was moving north when a pressure he knew very well appeared behind him.

"DIABLO!"

He stopped.

Milim hit the ground hard enough to make birds evacuate three trees.

She pointed.

"You!"

"Milim-sama."

"Where's Rimuru?"

Direct.

Good.

Diablo considered the search.

Considered the inn.

Considered telling Milim to wait.

That option died immediately.

"Rimuru-sama is at a nearby residence."

"Take me."

"I am currently conducting a search."

Milim leaned closer.

"For Rimuru?"

"For a person important to him."

"Oh."

That slowed her.

Not much.

"Then I'll help."

Diablo imagined the search radius after Milim helped without rules.

"No."

Milim's eyes narrowed.

"No?"

"I require you to meet Rimuru-sama first."

"Why?"

"Because the person we are searching for is unstable enough that indiscriminate detection may create more false positives."

Milim thought.

"Like you?"

Diablo decided not to answer.

She followed him anyway.

At the inn, the door opened.

Everyone looked.

Diablo entered alone for exactly half a second.

Hope moved.

Then Milim came through behind him.

Rimuru stood.

"Milim?"

"RIMURU!"

Impact.

Rimuru survived.

Barely.

The room relaxed in a way it had not for any other arrival.

Not because Mau was found.

Because somebody, finally, had been expected by someone.

Milim pulled back.

Looked around.

Then pointed at the crater.

"Did you do that?"

Rimuru:

"No."

Frieren:

"Yes."

Milim looked at Frieren with immediate admiration.

"Cool."

Fern:

"No."

Milim looked confused.

Rimuru rubbed his face.

"Long story."

"Where's the missing person?"

The room tightened again.

Milim noticed.

She looked at Diablo.

"You didn't find him?"

"Not yet."

"Who is he?"

Several people answered.

Not together this time.

Rimuru:

"Mau."

Frieren:

"My boyfriend."

Bocchi:

"Our friend."

Maki:

"Family."

Aira:

"Idiot."

Senku, from the table:

"Apparently a statistical nightmare."

Suika frowned at him.

"Senku."

"What? True."

Milim looked from person to person.

"Okay."

Then:

"What can I smash?"

Everyone:

"Nothing."

Milim deflated.

Senku pointed at a pile of covered material.

"You can move that."

Milim looked at him.

"Who are you?"

"Senku."

"You're bossy."

"You're standing in a drainage route."

She looked down.

Moved.

Good.

The first thing Milim did for the settlement was carry lumber under supervision.

That was intentional.

No mountain removal.

No instant road.

No power-scale shortcut.

Just:
pick this up,
put it there,
not there,
there.

Suika guided from the side.

Milim listened to Suika more readily than Senku.

Senku pretended not to notice.

At the inn, Rimuru finally explained enough.

Mau.

Memory fracture.

Gone nearly two days.

Signature unreliable.

Diablo searching.

Milim's energy changed.

Less playful.

"I'll search."

Rimuru shook his head.

"Not alone."

"I can cover more ground."

"I know."

"Then why—"

"Because we don't know what reacting to him does."

Milim stopped.

Good.

The rule had survived meeting power.

She sat.

Hated it.

But sat.

Diablo prepared to leave again.

Frieren stood.

This time Rimuru did not immediately tell her no.

He looked at her.

"How long have you slept?"

"Enough."

Fern:

"Lie."

Frieren glared.

Fern did not care.

Seiko:

"She's going anyway."

Correct.

Rimuru looked at Diablo.

"Stay with her."

Diablo almost laughed.

Then saw Frieren's face.

Did not.

They left together.

Milim moved toward the door.

Rimuru caught her sleeve.

"No."

She looked at him.

"Why do they get to go?"

"Because this search doesn't need maximum force. It needs the right information."

Milim frowned.

That sentence would matter later.

She stayed.

At dawn, G5 was no longer a vague pile of strangers.

It was a web of reunions, wrong identifications, temporary choices and people who had arrived because one demon kept trying to find one missing man.

Mau still wasn't there.

That remained the center of the room.

Not Milim.

Not Senku.

Not any new arrival.

The empty place.
