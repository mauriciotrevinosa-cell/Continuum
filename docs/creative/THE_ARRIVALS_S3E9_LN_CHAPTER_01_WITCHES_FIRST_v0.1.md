# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 7 — Witches First

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Source episode:** S3E9 — `Witches First`

The cabin existed.

That was the first bad thing.

Mau had followed a memory toward a place he had never visited.

North. Past the split cedar. Across a stream narrow enough to step over. Up through black rock.

The cabin stood exactly where his mind said it would.

Not identical.

The roof angle was wrong.

The door opened inward instead of out.

The memory had known enough to get him there.

Mau stood outside until someone opened the door.

A woman looked at him.

Then at the forest behind him.

"You took your time."

Mau's blood went cold.

"Do I know you?"

She smiled.

"No."

That answer saved him from running.

Barely.

The witches did not capture Mau.

That fact would become important later.

They found him already unraveling.

He tried to explain why he was there.

The explanation changed halfway through.

He remembered Rimuru telling him.

Then Qifrey.

Then Frieren.

Then nobody.

He stopped speaking.

One of the women moved closer.

Mau flinched.

She stopped immediately.

Another watched him with intense curiosity that would have been offensive under better circumstances.

"Interesting."

"Don't call him that," the first said.

"I didn't call him treasure."

"You were going to."

Mau looked between them.

"Please don't."

That silenced both.

They gave him water.

A chair.

No questions for several minutes.

Then one asked:

"Do you know your name?"

"Mau."

"Do you know where you live?"

He pictured the old inn.

Then a house at the future site that did not exist.

Then the cabin.

Mau closed his eyes.

"I know where I want to live."

The witch looked at the others.

That was not the question.

It was still an answer worth keeping.

Outside, one of them drew a mark into the air.

A signal.

Not invitation.

Not surrender.

A message to whoever was tearing through the forest badly enough to be noticed.

`We found him first.`
