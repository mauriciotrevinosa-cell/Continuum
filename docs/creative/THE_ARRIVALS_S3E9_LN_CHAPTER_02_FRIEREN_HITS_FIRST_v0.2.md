# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 8 — Frieren Hits First

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Continuity state:** Day 2 — morning | Frieren: sleep-deprived / searching | Diablo reports before direct witch encounter

Frieren saw Diablo before Rimuru did.

A black figure moving fast from the north.

Powerful.

Unknown to her.

Milim behind him.

Mau still missing.

Two hours of sleep across two days.

The equation completed before context.

Frieren's spell formed.

Diablo saw it.

Moved.

The beam cut through the place his head had been.

Milim shouted:

"HEY!"

Diablo landed thirty meters away.

Frieren raised her staff again.

"Where is he?"

Diablo understood immediately.

That helped.

It also offended him.

"Alive, possibly."

Frieren's second spell stopped half-formed.

Rimuru arrived in slime form, hit the ground, became human and stared at the destroyed tree.

"What happened?"

"Your friend attacked me."

Frieren did not look at Rimuru.

"Where."

Diablo's irritation disappeared.

"North valley. Eight significant presences clustered in one zone."

Yuta reached them.

Ori behind him.

"Eight what?"

"I don't know."

Frieren's staff tightened.

Diablo continued.

"I also detected a weaker human pattern within or near the same area."

"Mau."

"Possibly."

Frieren took one step north.

Rimuru blocked her.

She looked at him.

"Move."

"Ten seconds."

"Rimuru."

"Ten."

He pointed at Diablo.

"You came back instead of engaging."

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because eight unknown powers around a missing friend is not a problem improved by surprise."

Frieren's eyes closed for half a second.

The attack on Diablo replayed inside her.

Too fast.

Too tired.

Too afraid.

She looked at him.

"I thought—"

"I know."

That answer made apology worse.

Frieren said it anyway.

"Sorry."

Diablo blinked.

Milim whispered loudly:

"She almost took your head off."

"I noticed."

Rimuru spread the map.

Routes.

Approach points.

Places to stop before entering the cluster.

Qifrey arrived.

Agott too.

The search reorganized in minutes.

No mass charge.

Frieren.

Rimuru.

Yuta.

Ori.

Diablo.

Milim.

Qifrey far enough back to read environmental magic.

Himmel on secondary extraction route.

Everyone else held lines or stayed home.

The decision to bring fewer people felt wrong.

That was why it was probably correct.

Before they moved, Diablo looked at Frieren.

"I did not see him."

Frieren nodded.

"I know."

"But I believe the weaker pattern was alive when I left."

Another nod.

That was the closest thing to hope she had received in two days.

They went north.
