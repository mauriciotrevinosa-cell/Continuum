# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 18 — Not Their Work

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Continuity state:** Earlier — Day 2 midday | Mau: briefly lucid / stabilized by witches | External interference suspected, not identified

Mau woke once before the others arrived.

Barely.

A ceiling he did not know.

Eight presences he could not place.

That count entered him as fear before vision.

He tried to sit.

Failed.

A witch pushed him down with two fingers.

"No."

"Where—"

"Safe enough."

Bad answer.

Mau looked toward the door.

"Frieren."

"Coming, probably."

His eyes sharpened.

"How do you know?"

"Because the forest has been shouting about people looking for you."

Mau tried to remember leaving.

He could.

That was worse.

He remembered Frieren telling him not to come back until he could be useful.

False.

Probably.

He remembered her sleeping when he left.

Real.

Probably.

Two versions.

Same emotional weight.

His breathing changed.

The witch nearest him said:

"Don't choose one right now."

Mau stared.

"What?"

"You're doing it."

"Doing what?"

"Trying to decide which memory gets to be reality while half-conscious."

Another witch laughed.

"Humans love doing difficult work when least qualified."

Mau's eyes closed.

"Did you do this?"

The laughter stopped.

One answer came immediately.

"No."

Mau opened his eyes.

He did not believe it.

The witch noticed.

"Reasonable."

Another stepped closer.

"Something is attached to your recollection."

"Attached?"

"Not physically."

"Helpful."

"No."

She sounded annoyed at herself.

"Some parts feel authored. Some inherited. Some yours. Some perhaps dreams. The boundaries are wrong."

Mau looked from face to face.

"Can you fix it?"

Silence.

That answer was more useful than confidence would have been.

"No."

One witch added:

"Not without knowing what you were before."

Mau laughed weakly.

"I don't know."

"We noticed."

He turned his face away.

The first witch touched nothing.

"That is why this is interesting."

"Treasure."

This time the word came from someone else.

Mau's eyes opened.

"No."

The witch who had said it tilted her head.

Mau forced the words.

"I'm not."

Something in the room shifted.

Not power.

Social understanding.

The witch nodded.

"Then not ours."

The correction mattered.

Outside, a distant magical flare moved through the trees.

Frieren.

Mau knew the mana.

Or thought he did.

His eyes closed again before anyone could ask.

The witches felt the approaching group.

They also felt Diablo.

Eight signatures noticed one more dangerous thing noticing them.

The room became less peaceful.

One witch sighed.

"They're going to assume this is our fault."

"Is it?"

"No."

"Then they'll be annoying."

Mau slipped unconscious again.

The witch nearest him pulled the blanket higher.

"Do not let them take him before he can survive the trip."

Not mercy exactly.

Not ownership either.

A condition.

The first of several.
