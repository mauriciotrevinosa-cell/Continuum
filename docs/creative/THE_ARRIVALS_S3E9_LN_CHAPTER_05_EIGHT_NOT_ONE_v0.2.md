# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 19 — Eight, Not One

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — REBUILD v0.2  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 19  
**Continuity state:** Day 3 — morning, present resumes | Search party reaches witch zone | Eight-signature fact confirmed; Mau alive inside

The next morning, the story caught back up with the search.\n\nDiablo stopped before the boundary.

"Eight."

Milim counted nothing visible.

"I see three."

"That is not what I said."

Frieren ignored them.

She could feel Mau now.

Weak.

Alive.

That fact overrode caution until Ori grabbed her sleeve.

"Frieren."

Frieren looked down.

Ori's face said:

If you run in, I run in.

That was enough.

They entered together.

The zone did not have a wall.

It had social pressure.

A sense of being observed by more perspectives than bodies visible at once.

Qifrey remained outside the deepest point.

Rimuru kept Raphael on passive observation only.

No invasive attempt.

No classification of Mau.

They had learned that lesson in S1.

Diablo tracked the eight.

Some distant.

Some close.

Some impossible to localize cleanly.

The exact identities remained unspoken.

That was safer than pretending the story knew more than it did.

A woman stepped into the path.

Frieren raised her staff.

The witch raised empty hands.

"He's alive."

Frieren did not lower the staff.

"Where."

"Inside."

"Move."

"Not until we decide whether moving him kills him."

Yuta stepped forward.

Medical question.

Not dominance.

"Condition?"

"Exhaustion. Fever beginning. No major wound we found. Mind unstable."

Frieren's expression barely changed.

Yuta saw the change anyway.

The witch continued.

"He collapsed before we touched him."

Diablo asked:

"Did you alter his memory?"

"No."

Another voice from somewhere unseen said:

"Not this time."

Rimuru hated that answer.

"What does that mean?"

"It means you are asking whether witches do witch things. Yes. It means we did not do this."

Milim whispered:

"They're annoying."

Diablo almost agreed.

Frieren moved one step.

The witch moved too.

Not threatening.

Blocking.

"He wakes frightened. If eight strangers and six more strangers all crowd him, he will get worse."

Frieren's mana spiked.

Ori spoke before Rimuru.

"She's right."

Frieren looked at her.

Ori swallowed.

"I hate it too."

The room held.

Then Mau coughed inside.

Everything changed.

Frieren lowered the staff first.

"Take me to him."

One witch nodded.

"One person."

Yuta started to object.

Frieren was already moving.

Diablo counted eight signatures again.

Still eight.

None attacked.

That did not make them safe.

It made restraint possible.
