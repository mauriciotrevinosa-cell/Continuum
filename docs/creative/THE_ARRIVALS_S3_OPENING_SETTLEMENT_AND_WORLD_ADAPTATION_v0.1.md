# The Arrivals — S3 Opening: Settlement Governance + World Adaptation v0.1

**Status:** STRONG DIRECTION / DETAILS OPEN  
**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Scope:** S3 opening after the S2 future-home site choice.

This document does **not** lock exact episode order, final government structure, exact monster roster, or exact source mechanics.

---

## 1. S3 starts with a place, not a finished city

S2 ends after the household finds and chooses the region that can become its long-term home.

S3 begins with the practical work:

```text
chosen site
→ safety-first planning
→ bunker / civilian refuge
→ water / sanitation / food / storage
→ barriers / evacuation routes / access
→ debate over common core + residences + specialist buildings
→ G5 arrives while the plan is still flexible
→ first meaningful settlement systems
→ memory/perception arc interrupts the build
```

The city should feel collectively made rather than instantly generated.

---

## 2. Leadership: Rimuru/Tempest-like community feeling without copying Tempest hierarchy

The settlement can have clear leaders/coordinators while still making major decisions communally.

Principle:

> **Leadership coordinates; it does not erase everyone else's voice.**

The family/community can discuss major questions together:
- safety priorities;
- where important buildings go;
- resource allocation;
- new arrivals;
- external relations;
- rules that affect everyone.

As population grows, this can gradually evolve into more formal representation/governance, but S3 should still feel close enough that important decisions can visibly involve the people who live there.

Roles emerge from contribution rather than arbitrary titles.

Examples:
- **Wakana Gojo:** clothing/textiles/repair/craft; remains present in wider community decisions.
- **Mikasa:** strong candidate to organize or lead scouts/reconnaissance as the world becomes more dangerous.
- **Qifrey / Coco / Agott:** Witch Hat atelier, magical study/craft and related safety requirements.
- **Maomao:** medicine/pharmacology/clinical experimentation.
- **Senku:** science/engineering/process scaling as his cognition recovers.
- other residents similarly develop useful responsibilities without losing political/social agency.

A person's job is not their whole identity.

## 3. Scouts become more important because the world itself is changing

Reconnaissance should grow from "map the wilderness" into a real institution/function.

Possible responsibilities:
- map routes and resources;
- monitor monsters;
- investigate newly altered zones;
- escort civilians;
- search for arrivals;
- make first contact;
- identify safe/unsafe corridors;
- warn the settlement before ecological threats reach inhabited areas.

Mikasa is a strong fit for this function because her source experience makes vigilance, mobility, terrain and civilian survival emotionally credible rather than just a cool assignment.

Exact chain of command remains open.

## 4. World Adaptation / Retrointegration — STRONG NEW DIRECTION

A new Otherworlder/source does not have to arrive into a world that remains biologically and ecologically unchanged.

Strong direction:

> **Continuum can adapt the world around new arrivals so their existence, biology, threats and necessary support systems become physically compatible with the shared world.**

This may include:
- new monster ecologies;
- new species/biological niches;
- compatible materials/plants/resources;
- new supernatural phenomena;
- newly discoverable regions;
- medical/technical affordances needed to support altered biology.

### 4.1 "As if it had always been there" — bounded version

The favored version is **retrointegration of compatibility**, not universal memory replacement.

The world can behave as though the new condition/ecology has a legitimate place in it without rewriting every person's full personal history.

Possible manifestations:
- old-looking records/books now contain entries relevant to the new biology;
- native healers may recognize a condition or know stabilization protocols without knowing its source franchise;
- compatible plants/materials may already exist or become discoverable;
- local folklore may suddenly have a counterpart that maps imperfectly onto the new phenomenon;
- geography/ecology can gain zones that feel established rather than freshly spawned.

This preserves the uncanny idea that Continuum is **making room for what arrived** while keeping mystery.

Do not automatically give everyone detailed source-world knowledge.

## 5. Source-linked danger clustering — STRONG DIRECTION, NOT ABSOLUTE RULE YET

A particularly useful rule candidate:

> **New source-linked monsters/anomalies preferentially appear near Otherworlders or regions influenced by their arrival.**

This creates a reason the world gets more dangerous as more Otherworlders appear.

Important guardrails:
- not every Otherworlder literally spawns monsters;
- proximity should be a tendency / ecological correlation, not a guaranteed personal curse;
- some threats can migrate away from their point of origin;
- native monsters and pre-existing dangers continue to exist independently;
- the rule should create investigation and uncertainty rather than perfect tracking.

This gives scouts a growing strategic role.

## 6. Titans — candidate S3 escalation

Titans are a strong **candidate**, not locked.

Potential value:
- recognizable large-scale threat;
- immediately validates why fortifications, evacuation routes and scouts matter;
- gives Mikasa emotional/source-specific weight;
- demonstrates that world adaptation can import ecological danger rather than only people/powers.

If used, do not make Titans appear merely for spectacle.

They should teach the cast something about:
- source-linked danger;
- world retrointegration;
- perimeter design;
- evacuation;
- the emotional cost of Otherworlder-associated threats.

Exact Titan type, frequency and Attack on Titan source snapshot remain open.

---

## 7. Ghouls — candidate example of support-side adaptation

Ghouls are a particularly good example of adaptation that is **not only about monsters attacking**.

If ghoul characters enter:
- their biology must function in Continuum;
- the world may contain medical/biological knowledge that can help stabilize or understand them;
- some compatible resources/processes may exist or become discoverable;
- native specialists might recognize symptoms as a rare established condition without knowing the source-world narrative.

This allows the world to say, in effect:

> **You are alien to this world, but the world has already begun making a place where your body can exist.**

This can be compassionate, eerie and useful at the same time.

## 8. Senku + Maomao / waterfall example

The chosen site having a river/lake/waterfall becomes more than visual beauty.

Rimuru may initially see:
- defensible terrain;
- water;
- terrain-shaping possibilities.

Senku can see:
- mechanical energy;
- later hydroelectric potential;
- flow control;
- storage/distribution implications.

Maomao can intersect with Senku through:
- medicines;
- compounds;
- extraction;
- repeatable preparation;
- sanitation;
- testing.

Senku is **not** full-source-genius on arrival.

He can still be immediately useful because he recognizes systems and opportunities others overlook, while his larger capability rebuilds through experimentation.

---

## 9. S3 memory-arc interruption

The settlement thread should be active but unfinished when Mau's memory/perception crisis escalates.

That interruption is thematically strong:

> everyone is trying to decide what their new home will become while Mau's own internal sense of self/home begins destabilizing.

The build can pause, fragment or continue imperfectly around the crisis rather than waiting for the city to be completed first.
