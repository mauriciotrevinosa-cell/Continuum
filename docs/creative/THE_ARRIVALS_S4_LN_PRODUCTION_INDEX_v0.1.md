## Standing S4 rules
- **Nobody named leaves.** Nobody from G1–G5, and nobody in Rings One, Two or Three. Only frightened or complaining Arrival House and Ring Four people Return.
- **Zero new arrivals** in the first month. The coins are held.
- **Coins never expire.** They are the Trial's prize, what Mau spent millennia to win.
- If Claude is not sure a person belongs somewhere, Claude asks the creator.
