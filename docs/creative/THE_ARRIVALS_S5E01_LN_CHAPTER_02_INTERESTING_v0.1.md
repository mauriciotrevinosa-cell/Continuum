# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 2 — Interesting

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 2
**Continuity state:** The Goddess privately asks about Ori and the door; no answer about Mau's nature

*When: Day 262, later that evening.*

The voice came while Rimuru was talking to Shoko.

It did not interrupt either of them. Shoko went on describing the pulse she had counted. Rimuru asked a question. Somewhere behind Mau a mosquito settled on the bench.

*What is Ori to you?*

He stopped rubbing warmth back into Frieren's fingers.

She noticed immediately.

"What?"

He raised one finger. Wait. He did not want the gesture to mean anything else.

*Why do you want to know?*

There was a tremor under his soles. Small enough that he might have blamed his legs, except that water moved against the inside of the cup.

Rimuru turned.

*Would you go through that door?* the voice asked.

Mau looked past the rope. The flowers had not moved since he last looked. Or perhaps they had, and the movement was too familiar to separate from memory.

*No.*

*At any point?*

*Not because you left it open.*

Frieren's hand tightened in his.

*And if Ori's life depended on it?*

*Yes.*

There was no interval in which he could have made a better answer. He had said it before the picture arrived: Ori's room, the little connecting door, somebody going in and finding it empty.

He looked toward the house instead.

She was still by Fern. She had put both hands into her sleeves. He knew that posture. She used it when she wanted to ask for something and did not want anybody to see how much.

*Interesting.*

*Wait.*

Nothing answered.

He tried again, without forming another question he might be made to regret. *I'm talking to you.*

The mosquito lifted from the bench.

"Mau," Frieren said.

He could hear the difference now. Ciel had a place in his attention, exact even when she did not have an answer. The other voice had left no place to speak toward.

"She asked me something."

Rimuru was beside the bench before he finished.

"Ciel?"

"No."

Shoko put down her cup.

For one second he wanted to say that it was nothing yet. A question was not an act. Ori was standing there. He could see her breathing. Nothing had been done to her.

He knew how that sentence would sound if somebody else had to say it later.

"I need Ori," he said. "I need to tell her before we call everyone."

Frieren studied his face. He saw her beginning to put the words together, and had to look away before he tried to spare her by leaving one out.

"Do you need to stay on this bench?" Shoko asked.

*Ciel?*

*The pull remains. It has not increased during the conversation. I cannot identify where that voice was coming from.*

"I can walk if someone stays beside me," he said. "I don't want to walk toward the opening."

"Then we won't," Frieren said.

He got up with her. His knees complained, and Shoko took the cup from the ground before his attempt to retrieve it could become a second examination.

Ori had already started down the path.

Fern let her go when Mau nodded.

"Are you going somewhere?" Ori asked.

He kept looking at her hands hidden in her sleeves.

"I haven't agreed to."

"That wasn't what I asked."

"I know."

He had to swallow before he could continue.

"Can we go into the fort? Just for a minute. I have something to tell you, and you get to hear it first."

Ori's eyes went to the door. Then to Frieren.

"Both of you?"

Frieren moved her hand so Ori could take the other one.

"Both of us."
