# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 15 — The Clearing

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 15
**Continuity state:** Arrival clearing imagery; Mau encounters the fragmented representation of Noise; no outside link is assumed

*When: Day 263, inside the door; subjective duration unmeasured.*

He was lying in the grass.

It was such an exact beginning that he did not move at first.

There should have been a face above him. Light hair, a controlled voice, somebody trying to decide whether he was dangerous or hurt or simply very inconvenient.

There was sky.

Mau sat up.

The flowers were there. Close enough that he could see the uneven place where one stem had bent. He knew that bend. Or he thought he did. He had spent so long returning to the beginning in his head that he no longer trusted recognition to tell him which part had been there the first time.

"Frieren?"

He said it before he could help it.

No answer.

*Ciel.*

Nothing he could recognize as her reply.

He waited and tried once more. Then he stopped before he could turn the silence into certainty that something had happened to her.

His feet were bare in the grass.

He looked down at them. He had crossed in his shoes. He remembered tying them badly and Yuta telling him he had another few minutes to do it properly.

This was not reliable ground for an inventory.

There was a table a little way off.

He had not seen it arrive. It sat where it could not have been hidden by anything, with two chairs and a shape in one of them.

Mau got up slowly.

The shape had shoulders because he was looking for shoulders. Its edges did not stay still when he stopped walking. A head was bent over the table, or something occupying the place a head ought to have been.

He thought of the dark in his own shadow and did not decide they were the same thing merely because both were dark.

The empty chair was on the near side.

He touched its back. Wood under his palm. He could feel a small splinter that should not have mattered here.

He sat.

`let—me—out`

The words did not come from a mouth.

Mau's hands went cold on the table.

He had heard that before.

"Who are you?"

The shape moved. Its edges dragged after it, refusing to arrive at the same time.

`what—you—call`

It stopped so long that he thought that was all he would get.

`noise`

He looked at the table between them. Then past it, at the flowers and the place where he had been lying.

He waited for fear to tell him what to do. There was plenty of it. None of it was new enough to be useful.

"Then we're inside me," he said.

The shape did not answer.

"Where the things are. What the walls are holding."

Its head lifted.

For the first time he had the uncomfortable impression that it was trying to see him and did not know which of the things in front of it counted as his face.

`protect—you`

It said the two words with something like anger.

Mau kept his hands on the table.

"We should talk about that."
