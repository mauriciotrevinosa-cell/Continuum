# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 23 — The Loud End

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 23
**Continuity state:** Kessoku, Seika and visiting friends continue their own musical argument while Mau rests; no new powers or duties

*When: Day 264, afternoon; a separate household scene.*

Ryo had said the passage was simple, and now everyone expected her to prove it at a speed they had agreed upon.

This was unfair.

"You said slower," she reminded Nijika.

"Slower than the first time. Not slower than weather."

Kita put down her cup before she laughed. Bocchi kept her eyes on the strings, which was not convincing anyone. Ryo could see her shoulders moving.

They were in the music room with the door shut. The afternoon had been theirs since before anybody fell out of the sky. Seika had said that changing every plan whenever the house became peculiar would mean never having plans again.

She had then come to sit at the back, apparently to prevent them enjoying that statement too much.

Ryo played the passage again.

Nijika tapped the rhythm against her leg.

"There. That. Keep that."

"I was keeping that."

"You were charging rent on it."

Kita lost the battle with her cup. Bocchi made a small sound, looked surprised it had come from her, and then laughed properly.

The next attempt went beautifully until somebody knocked on the door in a different rhythm.

They all stopped.

"That wasn't me," Ryo said.

Umaru came in with Yuka. Both had the expressions of people who intended to claim they had been passing rather than listening outside.

"We have a request," Umaru announced.

"Requests cost money," Ryo said.

"You owe the fort money."

"That's a complex historical issue."

Yuka sat cross-legged near Kita. "Could you play the fast version?"

Nijika looked at Ryo.

Ryo looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi began putting her hand over her mouth, discovered it was needed for the guitar, and looked at the floor instead.

"We can play a fast version," Ryo said.

"Excellent," Seika said from the back. "There's room to improve the distinction."

They played it. At first they stayed together out of mutual suspicion. Then Kita found the right place to come in, and the shape of the thing was there, large and lively enough that Ryo forgot to be offended.

Bocchi took the little turn in the middle and gave it back so neatly that Nijika grinned at her without missing the beat.

Umaru clapped too early at the end.

That destroyed the ending.

Yuka began laughing. Kita turned away from the others and tried to finish the last note with dignity. Bocchi's hand slipped and supplied a sound that nobody would ever be allowed to forget.

For a while there was no music at all.

Seika waited until they could hear her again.

"You've found an audience. Now practice stopping before they applaud."

Ryo looked at Umaru. "That will incur an additional fee."

"Write it off against your debt."

"Which debt?"

"Do you want the list?"

They tried one last time. This time Yuka caught Umaru's wrists just before the last bar, and the ending landed exactly where it was meant to.

Everyone waited.

Umaru looked at the hands restraining her, then at Ryo.

"Now?"

Ryo considered it.

"For a small charge."

The cushion missed her and hit Nijika, who had done nothing to deserve being the most convenient target.
