# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 25 — Something to Bring

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 25
**Continuity state:** Ori asks about privacy; Bocchi shares music by choice; care and ordinary relationships continue after the briefing

*When: Day 265, later in the afternoon.*

Ori waited until the cups had gone before she asked.

"When did she stop?"

Mau was still in the chair. Frieren had moved enough that he could drink without navigating around her hair, but not enough to let him get up unnoticed.

He looked at Ori.

"Seeing through me."

"She didn't say when."

Ori looked down at the cup she had not returned with the others.

"I keep trying to remember what she might have seen."

Frieren put a hand over hers.

"You don't owe her those things."

"I know. It still feels—"

She stopped. Mau waited, without moving into the place where the next word ought to be.

"Like I should have noticed," she said.

He shook his head.

"I didn't. None of us did. You weren't doing it to us."

She turned the cup in her hands. "I know that part."

"Then we can leave the other part without an answer for a while."

Ori looked at him, doubtful that he had become qualified to offer that advice.

He smiled a little. "I'm trying it myself."

That was better. She took a breath and put the cup on the table.

"I'm going to the music room. Yuka says Umaru was almost arrested."

"Again?" Frieren asked.

"For clapping."

"A serious offence."

Ori laughed and went to find out how serious.

Mau watched her go. Frieren's hand remained on his arm.

"You don't have to watch the whole way," she said.

"I'm practicing stopping."

"At what?"

"The door."

She followed his gaze. Ori had already gone through it. After a moment he looked back at Frieren and asked whether she would mind letting him go as far as the bench.

She considered his face rather than the request.

"Together."

"That was the plan."

* * *

Bocchi found them with her guitar case.

Mau immediately began moving to make room. She shook her head so violently that he stopped.

"I wasn't bringing a concert."

"What were you bringing?"

"A question."

Frieren moved her book off the other end of the bench. Bocchi sat there and took out the guitar with the carefulness of somebody who was comfortable doing a task in front of people and less comfortable being herself while doing it.

"The rhythm from the other night."

Mau raised a finger. "Before the unfortunate journey."

"Yes."

"I practiced it. In my head."

"Please don't demonstrate yet."

He lowered his hand.

She played the short passage slowly enough to hear where it turned. Then again a little faster. He recognized the place she had moved his finger on the chair arm and pointed before he could ask whether that was helpful.

"There."

"Yes. That. I wanted to know if it sounds different when I do this."

She changed something. Small. He could hear it, though he could not have named it.

Frieren looked up from her page.

"The second one waits."

Bocchi looked at her, then down at her own hand.

"Yes. That's what I meant."

Mau tapped the first rhythm on his knee and stopped exactly where Bocchi had stopped him last time. She smiled before she remembered to be self-conscious about smiling.

They tried it with both of them listening. She chose the second version. Not because somebody had called it better for everyone. Because when she played it the third time, it was the one she kept wanting to play again.

She put the guitar away after a while.

"I wanted to bring you something," she said. Then, quickly, "But not a thing you had to keep."

"You did."

She looked at him.

"A rhythm I'm going to get wrong for years."

"You were nearly right."

"Then you'll have to keep checking."

"That's a lot of checking."

"I know."

Frieren turned a page beside them. Bocchi sat with the closed case against her leg and did not rush back inside.
