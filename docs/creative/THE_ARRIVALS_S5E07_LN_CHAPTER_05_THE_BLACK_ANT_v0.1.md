# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 39 — The Black Ant

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 39
**Continuity state:** Physical contact with a stray shadow precedes an unintended activation during Noise analysis; the party does not knowingly plan an Exchange

*When: Day 268, midday.*

It was an ant in the way a shadow could be an ant when a person made it with their hands.

Mau could see the shape. Legs, feelers, a narrow middle. The edges came apart if he stared at them, and gathered again when he looked a little to the side. No blue seam. No glow from inside it. It rested in the basin where the ceiling roots broke the light.

Yuta came back to the fountain.

"Don't move toward it."

Mau stayed seated.

The ant turned. Its feelers touched the stone. For a moment its whole shape flattened into the shadow beneath it, then rose again with one of its legs caught oddly under the rest.

Milim stopped chewing.

"That isn't like the first one."

"No," Mau said.

Something behind his thoughts had become very attentive.

He did not feel the old forward pull. That mattered enough for him to say it aloud.

"It's looking. Not making me move."

*Keep the boundaries where they are,* Ciel said. *I do not have a complete reading.*

He repeated that for the others. Rimuru's voice came from the floor.

"I can see it. I can't see what it's connected to."

Yuta moved to the side, keeping himself between Yuka and the basin. His blade stayed low. Nothing attacked. The ant dragged its caught leg over the stone and failed to lift it.

Mau held out his empty hand above the rim.

"Could it be somebody's?"

"Could be," Yuta said. "Wait."

They waited. The ant turned again. It had a torn edge near its middle; Mau could see pale stone through the place a body ought to have continued. When it tried to move, the tear widened.

He set his hand flat on the rim instead of reaching into the basin.

"I'm not going to pick it up."

Yuta nodded once. "Tell me if that changes."

The ant came to him.

It crossed the shallow slope in short, uneven movements. Yuka crouched by Milim, close enough to look without blocking Yuta's arm. Nobody spoke. Mau felt something brush the heel of his palm, cold through a point that should have been too small to feel.

The little body leaned against his skin.

Information moved.

It was not a thought in words, nor the sensation of a technique coming up through his hand. It was the awful impression of looking through a keyhole and finding another eye looking back. Lines led away from the ant, farther than the basin, farther than the room. One of those lines opened into a structure he could not hold all at once.

*Mau, stop the read—*

The Noise had found a name in what it was analysing.

It brought the word forward before Mau knew it was a command as well as a description. He had not asked it to use anything. He was still trying to withdraw his hand when the recognition completed itself inside him.

`Exchange.`

Not his chosen word. Not a place he had chosen to go.

The fountain dropped away.

For a fraction of a moment he still saw Yuta. One hand had come toward him; the other had lifted his blade. Yuka was beginning to say his name. Milim's food parcel hung open in the air.

Then there was no floor.

Then there was a floor made of broken black ground, and something vast falling through the space where his head would have been if he had remained standing.

Mau threw himself sideways.

The impact lifted him before he landed.

He hit stone hard enough to lose his next breath. His bag struck his ribs. He rolled because something in him knew staying still would kill him, and a blade wider than the fountain cut the place where he had fallen.

He could not hear Rimuru.

He could not hear Yuta.

He got one knee beneath himself and looked up.

An army was fighting under a sky that had no stars.

"Oh," he said.

The thing above him raised its weapon again.
