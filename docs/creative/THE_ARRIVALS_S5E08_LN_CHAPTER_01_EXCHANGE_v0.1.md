# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 35 — Exchange

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 35
**Continuity state:** Mau survives an involuntary swap; the loss of world restrictions exposes his body rather than granting safe mastery

*When: Day 268, Mau's first minutes outside Continuum.*

The first thing that stopped working was the limit.

Mau had learned its pressure so well that its absence felt, for one stupid instant, like relief. The next blow reached for him. Space caught it short of his face, and the effort went through his skull like a nail driven too far into wood.

He screamed without meaning to.

The weapon hung where it should have broken him. Beyond it, a huge face without any expression bent to look at the interruption.

Mau crawled backward.

"Close it."

The pressure inside him faltered.

"Not that. Keep that. The rest."

There was too much rest. Patterns uncoiled into one another, answering a place that did not know how to tell them no. He saw a shape for fire and the place it would borrow heat, a line that wanted to cut, the beginning of something wider than any room in the inn. They crowded behind his teeth, ready to become actions if he so much as reached for them.

He bit his tongue. The taste of blood helped.

"One at a time. Help me."

Something withdrew. Not everything. Enough for him to find his own next breath.

The weapon moved toward him again, slowly, fighting the distance in front of his skin. He could not hold it and crawl forever.

He pushed.

Repulsion tore out of his palm in a broken, crimson-bright knot. The thing above him slid backward through black stone. Its weapon broke in two. Mau's hand opened where skin ought to have stayed closed.

He staggered up before it could rise.

The ground ahead was full of soldiers. Some were shaped like people, others like beasts, all of them made from the same impossible dark as the little ant. They fought creatures whose edges did not stay in one place when Mau tried to look at them. A sword entered a body; the body became something else around it. Light spilled upward from a wound and hardened into a spear.

He had no idea which direction was home.

"Ciel?"

Nothing answered.

He tried Rimuru. Then Yuta, aloud, because there was no sensible reason to expect it and he needed to do something he could understand.

A dark soldier turned toward his voice.

The thing behind it killed it before it could move.

The soldier came back from the ground.

Mau stopped.

It rose with the same weapon, the same broken edge of a helm. It went back into the fight without looking at him again. All around him the army broke and reformed, gaining ground only to lose it when something larger passed through the ranks.

They were holding a place for someone.

He knew that without knowing how he knew. He had stood in enough rooms where one missing person changed the shape of everything.

The opening in front of him widened. Too many eyes turned toward it.

Mau moved before he understood that the opening was where he stood.

A white-hot line crossed the battlefield. He got behind a shelf of stone and felt it come apart against his back. Something entered the skin below his shoulder. He couldn't tell whether it was a shard or the heat itself.

He couldn't stay there.

He put his bleeding hand on the ground and made it push him sideways. Too much. He slid past the shelter he had wanted and nearly broke his knee against a ridge. The soldiers flowed around him, untroubled by the fall.

"Small," he said through his teeth. "I need small."

The next pressure was smaller.

It was still enough to make his vision split at the edges.

He got up again. Somewhere under all the things his body wanted to do, there was a boy who had left home with a pencil map, a sandwich, and a very limited job.

He held on to that job.

Come back.
