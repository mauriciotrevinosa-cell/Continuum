# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 46 — Sorry

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 46
**Continuity state:** Noise acknowledges an accidental activation; Mau's trust continues with the boundary agreement intact; the new capacity structure is unfinished

*When: Day 269, late morning.*

The room became quiet after Ori left to correct the map.

Frieren had gone to eat something Shoko could verify. Mau had been given water, a second blanket he did not need, and instructions to call before trying the stairs.

He lay with his eyes closed.

He could feel the place the new thing occupied. Not another room full of creatures. More like scaffolding erected against a wall he had stopped expecting to change. It stood beside other structures without yet knowing how to join them.

The Noise was working there.

He did not reach into it. He had touched enough things for one week.

`Sorry.`

The word was small enough that he almost took it for his own thought.

He opened his eyes.

Nothing in the room had moved. The beam still had its stupid face. Downstairs somebody dropped a pan and said a word that made Milim laugh.

"The word did it," Mau said quietly. "Didn't it? You were finding out what it was, and the name was also how to use it."

A tightening behind his thoughts. Recognition, perhaps. He had learned to be careful about treating everything he felt as an answer.

"I didn't think you'd chosen to take me."

The work inside him paused.

He let the silence stand for a while.

"Don't worry," he said at last, in the voice he would have used for somebody sitting on the floor beside the bed. "We came home. We'll work out how not to do that again."

He looked at his hands.

"You got more tools, though. Didn't you?"

Nothing answered.

He was beginning to think that was all when the word came.

`Yes.`

Mau smiled despite the ache in his shoulders.

"All right. We can start with looking at the labels before we say them."

No laugh. No second voice making a joke at his expense. The work resumed, cautious at its edge, and he lay listening to the ordinary room around it.

After a while Ciel spoke.

*May I ask what happened?*

"You heard?"

*The words spoken aloud. I have no complete record of your time away.*

He told her the rest. He described the first impact and the absence of the world pressing back, the way the patterns had crowded him, the choices he had managed to keep making. He asked her to stop him when the account stopped being useful.

She did, when his breathing began to change.

*That is enough for now.*

"Was it twice?"

*Your account supports the provisional comparison. Twice the longest you have held an open state. Not twice a recommended limit. There was no recommended limit for what happened there.*

"I didn't hold it very gracefully."

*I did not claim that you did.*

He laughed, and the laugh hurt enough to end itself.

"There's something new."

*Yes. A structure for physical capacity, still being assembled from what was read. It is not the whole of the person you exchanged with. Nor is it an instruction to open everything else.*

"Can you see what it's for?"

*Growth. Conditions remain unclear. The name of a field is not proof of what it can supply.*

He thought about the soldiers getting up from the ground. He could not do that. Nothing in the scaffolding gave him the certainty that he could.

"Tell Rimuru this much," he said. "And Shoko. Not everybody at once. I want to be awake when they ask."

*Understood.*

He closed his eyes again.

The walls were still there. Behind them the Noise kept building. It was not healed into silence, and he was not a different person because he had managed to talk to it.

But when the work pressed close to the edge, it stopped there.

He slept before Frieren returned with the cup she had stolen from his breakfast tray.
