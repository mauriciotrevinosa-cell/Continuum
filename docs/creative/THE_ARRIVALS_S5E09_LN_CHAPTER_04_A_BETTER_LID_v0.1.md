# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 43 — A Better Lid

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 43
**Continuity state:** Ordinary household work and band life continue independently; Yuka and Mau recover their teasing sibling rhythm without erasing the fright

*When: Day 269, late afternoon.*

The first vote on the new kitchen lid was against it.

"It's too heavy," Momo said.

Wakana took it from her and frowned at the rim. Rem held the pot steady. Ram, who had come to inspect a smell, said that perhaps the soup had offended him.

"It isn't for this pot," he said.

"Then why did you bring it to this pot?"

"Because somebody moved the other pot."

Okarun, carrying the other pot, stopped in the doorway.

For a moment nobody helped him.

Momo started laughing first. Rem took the pot from him before he could apologize to the wrong person. Wakana put the lid where it belonged, where it fitted very well, and had the satisfaction of saying nothing at all.

Marin arrived in time to miss his victory and announce that he was leaving with her in half an hour.

"Am I?"

"You said we could go when the lid was done."

He looked at it as though it might need another fitting.

Momo picked it up, put it on the correct pot, lifted it again, and handed it back.

"Done. Go."

They left to change, still arguing about whether testing a thing five times was work or a hobby. Rem turned the soup. Ram stole a taste from the spoon and gave a verdict sufficiently restrained to make her sister smile.

Beyond the courtyard, somebody was playing the same four bars repeatedly. The fourth attempt changed the ending. The fifth put the old ending back. Kita shouted something through a window, and a second voice shouted back that she had meant it that time.

Momo listened while she cut vegetables. Then she found herself cutting to the wrong ending.

"They need to choose," she said.

"You could go tell them," Okarun offered.

"And be wrong in front of four musicians? No thanks."

***

Yuka found Mau on the upstairs landing with a blanket over his shoulders and no convincing explanation for why he was standing there.

"Bathroom," he said before she asked.

"It's behind you."

"I successfully found it."

"Shoko said call for the stairs."

"These are not the stairs. This is me looking at them."

She looked at him. He looked at her.

"All right," he said. "I was going to ask whether I could eat downstairs. I wanted to see if standing still made me tired first."

She accepted that and went to ask. When she came back with permission and a requirement that somebody walk with him, he already had the blanket folded over one arm.

"Excellent porter service," he said.

"I charge rope."

"Your grandfather pays for everything."

"He says that too."

They went slowly. Yuka stayed one step below him and tried not to look like she was doing it on purpose. He let her.

At the bottom she stopped him before they reached the common room.

"I wanted to go after you."

He did not make a joke.

"I know."

"I didn't. There wasn't anywhere."

He waited.

"Next time, don't do that."

"I'm going to try very hard not to."

It was an answer she could use. She gave him the folded blanket as though it had been her job to carry it all along.

"Also, Milim ate your lunch. After."

"I lost my lunch."

"Your spare lunch."

Mau stopped at the common-room door.

"I had a spare lunch?"

"Rem packed it."

"That's the most upsetting thing you've told me."

Yuka pushed him gently through the doorway. Milim looked up from the table, saw them, and moved a plate toward the empty place beside her with the air of somebody establishing an alibi.
