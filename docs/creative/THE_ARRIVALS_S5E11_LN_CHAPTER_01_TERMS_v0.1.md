# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 27
## Chapter 57 — Terms

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 27
**Reading order:** 57
**Continuity state:** Fern agrees to expedition coverage and offers a practical window after the weekly reset; Frieren's leadership gift does not remove retreat or medical conditions

*When: Day 275, afternoon.*

Fern made Mau wait until she had finished the sum.

He waited. Stark, sitting on the far side of the table with a whetstone, did not look sympathetic.

"She'll start again if you breathe too loudly," he said.

"I heard that," Fern said.

"I know."

Mau rested his elbows on the table. "I'm trying to arrange a present."

Fern drew a line beneath the total.

"That sounds expensive."

"Mostly time."

"That sounds more expensive."

He explained. Not simply another outing with Frieren in the party. One she planned and led, with enough food and enough people that she could look at every stupid corner without being pulled back because somebody else was bored. Starting after the next change, when the map was new for everyone.

Fern listened without interrupting. Stark stopped working on the blade.

"And you?" she asked.

"Go along. Do my job. Let her have hers."

"You don't assign her a job."

"I know. I mean—I want to make room for the one she keeps wanting. Not give her something she needs permission to want."

Fern considered that.

"She'll take provisions for three days and complain there's no room for a fourth book."

"That's why I'm asking early."

"And you'll need agreed coverage here. Your medical conditions don't change because it's an anniversary. Nor does the rule about leaving."

"I know. The present isn't a promise to stay inside if somebody's hurt."

"Good."

She opened a different page. Stark quietly put the whetstone away before she could add his name to something without asking.

They worked through it. Food that would keep. Water carried rather than assumed. Enough people for the roles without taking every person who could defend the house. Mau and Rimuru's arrangement. An unhurried day when the next gate opened, with the actual depth and return plan settled after they saw what it was.

"Day two hundred and eighty," Fern said. "That gives her time after your dinner."

"Three days."

"She may use all of them."

"I'm counting on it."

Fern looked at him for a moment longer, then put a small mark beside the date.

"The supplies and coverage can be arranged. Tell her that part is agreed. Don't tell her I've approved every person she might decide to recruit."

"I have learned some things."

"You can demonstrate them."

He got up before she could give him an opportunity immediately.

At the doorway, he stopped.

"You could come. If you want. I think she'd like that."

Fern looked down at the open page.

"She can ask me."

It was not a refusal. He knew better than to improve it for her.

After he left, Stark resumed the small, unhurried movement of the stone. Fern looked at the mark beside the day for longer than the supplies required.

"Would you?" she asked.

"Go?"

She nodded.

"Probably. Do you want to?"

"She hasn't asked."

"I know."

Fern closed the ledger.

"Come with me to put this away."

Stark looked at the blade in his lap, then at her hand on the book.

"That dangerous a job?"

"Extremely."

He put his things down and went with her. She did not let him carry the ledger. She did take his hand once they were out of sight of the long table.
