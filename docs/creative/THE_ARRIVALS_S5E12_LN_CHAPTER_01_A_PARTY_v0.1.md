# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 1 — A Party

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 1
**Continuity state:** Frieren begins recruiting for her own expedition; Yuka chooses to join and Yuta/Maki choose time together rather than being automatically assigned

*When: Day 278, morning.*

Frieren had a list by breakfast.

By the end of breakfast she had a different list, because Milim had put a thumb in the butter and then on the first one.

"That was the important corner," she said.

"You should have kept it away from breakfast."

Mau moved the butter. Frieren moved the list. Neither of them moved Milim, who had established herself between the two problems and was enjoying the arrangement.

Yuka came around the end of the table before Frieren could get up.

"You're really going?"

"After the change."

"Can I come?"

Frieren looked at her over the paper.

"You don't know where we're going."

"Neither do you."

"A fair point."

Yuka sat down, taking the place Mau had just vacated to fetch more tea. When he returned she pointed him toward the other side of Frieren without apologizing.

"Oppa said you're going to look at everything."

"Everything worth looking at."

"So everything."

Frieren was beginning to like this application.

She asked Yuka what she wanted to do besides come. The answer arrived quickly, then became more thoughtful when Frieren did not hurry her. Maps. Routes. Helping carry the things they found. If it was appropriate, a short use of her white dog for scouting, not a new taming attempt or a promise to keep her out all day.

"And listen when I ask you to wait," Frieren said.

"Yes."

"Even if Milim says something interesting is ahead."

"Hey," Milim said.

Yuka agreed to that too.

Frieren put her name on the list. Yuka tried to hide how pleased she was and failed so thoroughly that Mau had to look into his tea.

"Don't," she told him.

"I haven't said anything."

"You're going to."

"I'll write it down for later."

She kicked his boot under the table, gently enough to be affectionate and accurately enough to be a warning.

Yuta and Maki were by the window, sharing the last piece of bread. Yuka went to tell them, taking the list as evidence before Frieren could object.

"You're not coming, right?"

Yuta looked at Maki.

Maki looked at him.

Mau, watching from the table, recognized the look too late to keep himself from smiling.

"We thought we'd stay," Yuta said.

"You're allowed to do that," Yuka said. "I didn't mean—I'm not saying you have to."

"We know," Maki said.

She handed Yuka the remaining crust, which the girl accepted without noticing the bribe.

"We have plans."

"What plans?"

"Not telling you all our plans."

Yuka looked from one of them to the other and then away so quickly that Yuta began to laugh.

"Gross. Fine. Wonderful. I'm going with the elf."

"The elf has a name," Frieren said.

"The elf has a list. Put me on it twice."

After she left, Maki leaned against Yuta's shoulder.

"We haven't made a plan," he said quietly.

"Exactly."

He turned toward her.

The breakfast room went on around them. For a little while they did not offer to help it.
