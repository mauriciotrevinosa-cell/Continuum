# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 3 — High Expectations

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 3
**Continuity state:** Milim joins under Frieren's leadership; Gojo's teasing prompts the chosen Shoko date invitation, without completing their future date or giving him new powers

*When: Day 279, morning.*

Milim applied by announcing that she was going.

Frieren put down her cup.

"Are you?"

The small pause after the question made Milim reconsider the method rather than the outcome.

"Can I?"

"Will you listen?"

"Yes."

"Stay with the party?"

"Yes."

"Leave a thing alone when I say to leave it?"

Milim looked at Mau.

He raised both hands. "I passed this part."

"You didn't have to pass all these parts."

"I had a medical examination. We can swap."

She turned back to Frieren very quickly.

"I'll listen."

Frieren made her wait long enough to understand that the answer mattered. Then she put the last name on the page.

Six. Frieren, Fern, Stark, Yuka, Mau, Milim.

Gojo appeared beside the table with the ease of a man who had heard a plan forming and decided it would improve with his presence.

"I should come. Somebody has to keep an eye on the lovebirds."

The Zoltraak stopped short of his face.

It did not quite touch the space that would not let it touch him. The light stretched, faded, and left him holding his cup at the same careful angle as before.

Mau looked at the ceiling.

Shoko, who had been swallowing tea, put her cup down before trying to finish.

"I don't need watching," Frieren said. "Go spend some time with Shoko."

For once, Gojo did not answer immediately.

Shoko looked at him. That was apparently all the encouragement he needed to recover.

"You heard the elf. You're going on a date with me."

"Am I?"

"An important household contribution."

"I've seen what Mau did for Frieren. I have high expectations."

Mau felt the attention arrive at his end of the table.

"I am unavailable for blame," he said.

"Advice, then," Gojo said.

"Ask her what she likes."

Shoko's mouth moved at one corner.

"A challenging opening."

Gojo looked at her for a moment longer than the joke needed. "Will you?"

The room had gone just quiet enough that everybody who was pretending not to listen became obvious.

Shoko picked up her cup.

"Ask me when you've thought of something."

He smiled. A smaller smile than the one he had arrived with.

"All right."

Frieren put the list away before anybody else could invite themselves.

Later, Mau found Gojo by the open window, turning his cup between his hands.

"Don't make it into a performance," Mau said.

"That bad?"

"I didn't say that."

Gojo looked out into the courtyard. Shoko was speaking with Maomao beside the clinic door, smiling at something neither man could hear.

"I used to know what she'd say," he said.

Mau stayed beside him.

"You can find out again."

Gojo was quiet. Then he looked at the inner pocket Mau had touched without noticing.

"How's the compass?"

Mau took it out.

The needle pointed across the room, where Frieren had stopped to discuss bags with Fern.

Gojo looked at it and laughed softly.

"I thought she'd keep that forever."

"So did I."

He gave it back with more care than he had taken it.

"Good dinner, then."

Mau put it away. "Very good."

They watched the courtyard a little longer before Gojo asked a sensible first question about where Hollis got the wine.
