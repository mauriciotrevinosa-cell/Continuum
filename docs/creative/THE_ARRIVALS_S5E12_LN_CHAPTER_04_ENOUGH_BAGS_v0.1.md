# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 4 — Enough Bags

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 4
**Continuity state:** Provisions and camping equipment are physically packed; a very short monitored defensive access test precedes the trip; no taming or broad kit clearance

*When: Day 279, afternoon and evening.*

Frieren had packed for longer than they expected to stay.

Fern approved of that until she tried to lift the bag.

"How many empty bags are in this bag?"

"Several."

"How many is several?"

"Enough."

Stark took it from her, tested the weight, and suggested that several could travel in two places. Yuka produced the rope she had been waiting to justify. Milim produced food nobody had assigned her and refused to explain where it had come from until Rem arrived to confirm she had packed it.

Mau sorted the cloth parcels at the end of the table. A week's dry provisions if carefully used, more than the ordinary plan required; water for the first stretch, pots and a way to boil more, sleeping rolls, warm layers, spare socks. Frieren had included things they had learned to want on journeys before this world. Fern removed two duplicates and added a missing practical item without making a speech about it.

Nobody packed a convenient supply of healing draughts they did not have. The scarce medical supplies were counted, described, and put where somebody besides their carrier could find them.

In the afternoon Mau left the packing for a short check by the clear practice space.

Shoko came with Maomao. Gojo watched from the side. Frieren stood close enough to see his face when Ciel asked the first question.

They had worked toward a narrow answer: could he request one defensive opening, close it promptly, and remain himself on both sides of the operation?

Not an attack. Not a new trial of the elf route. A small object, moving slowly, stopped in the space just before his palm.

He asked.

The Noise made room for the pattern without pushing the others forward. Ciel kept his attention on the limits they had chosen. The object slowed, stopped, hung.

One breath.

"Close," Mau said.

It fell into the cloth waiting below.

He sat down while they checked him. His head ached a little. Not enough to hide, which was why he said it before somebody asked.

They did not repeat it.

"One operation," Shoko said. "We've established one operation under these conditions."

"I know."

"Emergency use is a judgment, not a reason to practise until it becomes one."

He agreed. Frieren put the little object back in its box.

"You did it," she said when the others stepped away.

He looked at her.

"I did."

Her hand found his. They stayed that way until Yuka called from the table that somebody had hidden three bags inside a fourth.

"A serious allegation," Frieren said.

She went back to defend herself.

Before bedtime they carried the loads across the courtyard, each person with what they would actually take. Milim's was heavier. Stark's fitted better after Wakana adjusted a strap. Yuka's rope survived every inspection and came out looking rather smug for an object with no face.

Ori stood beside Mau while the others put things by the door.

"A long day?"

"We don't know yet. We'll see what the place is. Rimuru has the plan."

She looked at the packs.

"That's a lot of plan."

He laughed. "Your mother is in charge."

Frieren, passing behind them with one final folded cloth, said that was the first sensible thing he had said all evening.

Ori hugged him, then Frieren. Milim demanded one too and received it, after agreeing that Ori should not have to carry her while she was awake.

They went upstairs early.

For once, Frieren put the book away before Mau asked.
