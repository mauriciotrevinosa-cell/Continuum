# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 5 — Normal

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 5
**Continuity state:** All six enter the new blue normal gate before it turns red and seals; the planned ordinary trip becomes a trapped expedition without prior knowledge

*When: Day 280, dawn and entry.*

The new week called itself normal.

Frieren stood before the window at the stake and read it twice. Week twenty-six. Normal. Seven days until the next change.

"Satisfied?" Mau asked.

"With the writing. We'll see about the contents."

Behind them, Yuta and Maki were sharing a cup while Yuka tightened her bag one final time. Gojo had come out to offer unhelpful advice and found Fern sufficiently unimpressed that he had kept most of it to himself. Rimuru checked the relay threads from the safe side of the marker.

Mau touched the compass in his inner pocket.

Frieren saw.

"I am here."

"I'm being thorough."

She looked at him for a moment, then leaned closer.

"If I find another grimoire, prepare to be my subject again."

"Two," he said. "Two books, two days."

Her ears lifted.

"An interesting offer."

"It needs to be a challenge."

"You'll regret that."

He smiled. She caught herself smiling back and turned toward the gate before anybody could comment.

They confirmed the return conditions. Nobody would wait out a failing relay to satisfy the plan. No new summons just because the place looked inviting. Mau and Milim would help as guardians; Frieren chose the route, Fern kept the second line, Stark handled the front when she asked him to.

"Still my adventure," Frieren reminded Mau quietly.

"I know."

"Good."

She went through first.

Cold air met her face.

She stepped onto leaf litter under tall white-barked trees. The sky beyond them was clear and hard, a pale morning sky with a sun low over a dark ridge. No stone alcove. No corridor.

Stark came through behind her, then Fern. Yuka stopped at the threshold long enough to look back, called that she could hear Rimuru, and stepped fully inside. Mau followed. Milim came last with the largest bag.

The light changed.

Frieren turned before the first warning had finished moving through her.

The blue surface drew inward, darkening. Red spread from the middle to the rim. Rimuru's voice broke in half.

Mau reached the entrance before she did, because he had been standing nearer. He did not put a hand through it. He stopped with his palm just short of a surface that no longer moved like an opening.

"Rimuru?"

Nothing answered from the shadows.

Fern was already counting them. All six. No one had been caught halfway. Yuka moved away from the gate when Frieren told her to.

Stark found a long fallen branch and offered its end to the red surface. It stopped hard enough to bend.

They tried the ordinary way back once. Carefully. Frieren tested the surface with a restrained spell from an angle that kept everyone out of its path. The red did not open. Her magic spread thin across it and vanished.

She lowered her staff.

The cold in the trees had begun reaching them through their travel clothes.

"Layers first," she said. "Then we find shelter and work out what changed."

Mau looked at her. She could see the old instinct in his face, the need to make a thing his responsibility before it hurt anyone else.

"Six of us," she said, before he could speak. "We start with six."

He breathed out.

"Where do you want the bags?"

She pointed to dry ground beneath the nearest trees.

They went there together.
