# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 7 — The First Shelter

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 7
**Continuity state:** Frieren organizes shelter, water and rationing; local Ciel cannot relay outside; reduced world restriction does not open Mau's internal walls

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate day one, late morning.*

The stream was real water.

That was the first useful thing they learned. Fern checked upstream as far as the bend; Stark checked the bank for tracks while Yuka marked the way back to the red entrance. Milim found a shallow overhang beyond the roots of a fallen tree, dry enough for their bags and tall enough for everyone except Stark to stand comfortably.

He accepted this with the face of a man who had met the problem before.

Frieren chose it for the first stop.

Not home. A place to stop getting colder while they thought.

They put on the warm layers. They filled the pot and boiled the water. They counted the food before opening anything simply because it was lunchtime.

Milim watched the counting with increasing suspicion.

"We packed plenty."

"We did," Fern said. "Now we're finding out how much plenty is."

A week if they used it carefully. The reserve was part of that week, not a second invisible store to discover after the first ran out. Water would be their first recurring task. Fuel their second. They would not eat things they could not identify just because the shapes were familiar.

Frieren knelt by the map and drew the stream as Yuka described it. She put the red entrance behind them, the low ridge to the east, the darker trees ahead.

"Something is farther in," she said. "A large concentration. I can't tell you what from here."

"The way out?" Yuka asked.

"Possibly. We won't assume. We check the ground as we go."

Mau had been quiet during the work. Now he came to sit where she could see his face.

"The pressure's different."

She put the pencil down.

"The world's," he said. "Not the walls. Ciel says that part is still ours to manage."

He could hear her here, where he had not heard her during the Exchange. She was with the host who had entered. Reaching Rimuru outside was another matter. They had tried; the answer had stayed silence.

"Can you keep them closed?" Frieren asked.

"Yes. Nothing has to open because this place is letting it."

He said that to himself as much as to her. She heard it and did not make him say it again.

They agreed on the same arrangement: he could tell her what he noticed, request help, use the narrowly tested defence if needed and if Ciel could keep it bounded. No exploring an open pattern because they were frightened enough to want a larger answer.

Mau took the pot off the heat with a folded cloth.

"Where next?"

She pointed along the stream.

"We follow it while the ground stays passable. High bank, not the watercourse. We'll see more from there."

He nodded and began packing.

Milim held up the food parcel she had been permitted to open.

"This isn't a lot."

Stark broke off a little of his and held it out.

She looked at it. Then at him.

"I'm not stealing yours."

"I'm offering."

She took it after one more careful look. Frieren watched her tuck half away for later and decided not to say anything about it.

They left the overhang with dry clothes, filled water, and a marked place to come back to.

It was enough to begin.
