# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 10 — First Watch

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 10
**Continuity state:** The party shares watches and sleep; Mau and Milim guard without taking over the expedition or giving Mau an inexhaustible night shift

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate night one.*

Milim had promised to wake him.

Mau opened his eyes to find her crouching beside the sleeping rolls, looking guilty about something that had not happened yet.

"What?"

"You were asleep."

"That's generally the idea before a watch."

"You looked very asleep."

He sat up carefully. Frieren's hand caught the back of his shirt before she was properly awake.

"Our turn," he whispered.

She let go after a moment. He put the warm fold of the blanket back against her shoulder and went to sit beside Milim near the mouth of the shelter.

The fire was low. He fed it a dry piece from the little stack Stark had left within reach.

They had divided the night into pairs. Nobody kept the entire dark because they were strong, or because they had once stayed awake too long and learned to call it helpful. He and Milim had the middle stretch. Fern and Stark would take the last one.

He could see their bags against the stone. Six sleeping places. The little pot with the remaining water. It looked more like a travelling camp than anything they had done through the gate before.

"Do you think Rimuru's angry?" Milim asked.

"Probably worried."

"That's worse."

"A little."

She pulled her knees up.

"We didn't do anything wrong."

"I know."

She needed him to know it enough that he said it again without making a joke.

They listened to the forest. Somewhere far off, something called once and was answered from the ridge. Mau did not open his eyes wider to try to turn the whole dark into information. He watched what the fire let him watch and relied on Milim for the rest.

"Ori ties the ribbon badly," she said after a while.

He looked at her.

"For sleeping. One time I was still tied when I woke up."

"Did you tell her?"

"She said it worked, then."

Mau laughed softly enough not to wake the shelter.

Milim looked pleased.

"It did," he said.

She pushed his shoulder with hers. He pushed back with considerably less effect.

Later she asked whether he had liked flying. He told her about getting two fingers off the blanket and feeling as if somebody ought to applaud. She said that was not very high, then seemed to realize that it might have been a poor answer.

"It was higher than before," she added.

"It was."

"I'll show you properly when you can."

"You can show me now. I can't follow yet."

She thought about the distinction.

"All right."

When their watch ended, he woke Fern as agreed. She rose slowly enough not to disturb Stark, then disturbed him deliberately with a hand on his shoulder.

Mau went back to the sleeping roll. Frieren made a space for him without opening her eyes.

His shirt was cold. She objected, quietly.

He apologized. She kept him anyway.

Outside, Milim's voice told Stark he had put the wrong piece of wood on the fire again.
