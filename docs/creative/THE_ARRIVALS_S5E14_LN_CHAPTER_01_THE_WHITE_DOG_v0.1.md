# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 11 — The White Dog

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 11
**Continuity state:** Yuka uses her already-tamed white Divine Dog for a short scouting task with real cost; she is distinct from Mau's Ear and no new ritual occurs

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate day two, morning.*

The white dog came out of Yuka's shadow shaking snow from a coat that had not been in the snow.

Mau looked at her. Then at Yuka.

"She does that too?"

"Apparently."

The dog inspected his boots, accepted one hand briefly against her head, and went back to her summoner. White fur, bright attention, a weight in the ground that was not the same as Ear's familiar heavy presence.

Mau kept the distinction in his mind. His own dog was resting where he belonged. He was not going to bring him into a new problem because somebody else had done something that looked close enough.

Frieren knelt beside Yuka's map.

"The bend and the near bank. We want the path, not whatever is farthest away. Call her back before you get tired."

Yuka nodded. She had asked for the task after they saw the tracks, and waited while they agreed on its limits. Now the dog nosed the frozen ground, found a scent the others could not separate, and went ahead at a trot.

They followed while keeping her in sight.

The trail around the stream forked beneath a stand of low branches. The dog went left, stopped, and came back to Yuka with a soft, urgent sound. Yuka looked beyond her and saw the fine white line between two trees.

"Don't," she said when Milim reached for it.

Milim froze with one finger extended.

Stark circled the edge. Above them, a bundle of sharpened branches waited under snow, held up by the same pale cord. He cut the anchored side from cover. The bundle dropped into the place they would have been walking.

Nobody made a joke for a moment.

Frieren looked along the trees. No archer showed himself.

"They know this path," she said. "We don't use it."

Yuka recalled the dog before the next bend. The shape dissolved back into her shadow; she sat down afterward, breathing harder than the short walk explained.

Mau offered water.

"I stopped in time," she said.

"I know. That's why you're drinking it here instead of being carried."

She accepted the cup.

Fern gave them a few minutes while Stark checked the alternative route. Frieren marked the trap and the dog’s warning on the map. Milim inspected the fallen branches and announced that she had never liked that path anyway.

"You wanted to pull the cord," Yuka said.

"To improve the path."

Mau laughed. Yuka tried not to and lost halfway through the first breath.

When they moved again, she did not summon the dog a second time just to prove the first had gone well. She walked beside Mau, watching the ground with her own eyes.

At the next clear rise Frieren paused and looked back.

"That helped," she told her.

Yuka went pink above the scarf.

"She did most of it."

"You asked her the right question."

Mau looked at the sky very politely until Yuka elbowed him for it.
