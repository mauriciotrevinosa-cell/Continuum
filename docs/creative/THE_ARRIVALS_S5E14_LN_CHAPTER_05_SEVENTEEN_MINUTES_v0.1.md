# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 15 — Seventeen Minutes

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 15
**Continuity state:** The outside household gathers at the safe boundary; nobody treats the interior week as exterior time or assumes an unlimited Ciel connection

*When: Day 280, exterior minute seventeen.*

Anko said the time again.

Seventeen minutes.

Shoko had brought the emergency kit and set it just inside the protected side of the stake. Maomao checked what she had brought against what might actually be useful. Senku measured the red surface without putting a wire into it. Gojo stood farther out, near Yuta, looking at the place where the entrance met the air.

Ori arrived with Umaru and stopped beside Rimuru.

"All six?"

"All six went in," he said. "Nobody was caught outside halfway."

"But you can't hear them."

"Not since the change."

She nodded with an effort that made him want to offer something better. He kept the answer true instead.

Umaru put an arm around her shoulders. Neither of them went past the red stake. Anko made sure the path behind them stayed clear; if somebody came out hurt, there would be space for more than everyone trying to reach them first.

Maki stood beside Yuta.

"We weren't going to do anything useful today," she said quietly.

He looked at her.

"That's why we were staying."

"I know."

She took his hand, holding it low where he did not have to stop watching the gate to return the pressure.

Gojo came back to the stake.

"No accessible route around it," he said. "I can tell you where the barrier is. That's not the same as knowing how to open it."

Senku nodded, less surprised than pleased to have a useful limit stated.

They compared it with the shop window. The label had not changed to match what they could see. No new instruction appeared when they approached. No price for opening it. No helpful countdown.

Rimuru checked his threads again. Nothing.

Shoko came to stand near Gojo while Maomao took a reading from the cold air at the boundary.

"This isn't a date," she said.

His mouth moved without becoming a smile.

"I had a better location in mind."

"Good. Keep thinking about it."

He looked at her. She was watching the gate, tired and steady, with a hand already resting near the kit she would need if it opened badly.

"I will."

It was all the conversation they had room for. He stayed beside her for a little while before going back to Yuta.

Ori sat down on the grass. Umaru sat with her. Rimuru moved so his shadow did not cover the little place they had chosen.

No voice came from the sky.

No one was relieved by that enough to call it a good sign.

Anko turned her page and kept the time.
