# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 16 — Fern's Turn

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 16
**Continuity state:** Fern chooses a small part of the route and shares discovery with Frieren; their closeness is not reduced to accounts or supervising Mau

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate day four, morning.*

Fern found the side path while looking for dry ground.

It ran behind a low stand of dark trees, sheltered from the wind by the rise they had spent yesterday circling. There was no obvious reward at the end of it. Only a place where the frost lay in rings rather than on the grass, each ring growing around a thin stone spike.

She called Frieren before walking into it.

For a while they stood side by side and looked.

"Magic?" Fern asked.

"Some. Weak. Repeating."

"A trap?"

"Possibly."

Frieren looked unusually pleased by the possibility. Fern sighed and crouched to examine the nearest ring without touching it.

The others stopped on the path behind them. Mau put the bag down. Stark went to look at the edge of the hollow. Milim asked whether they were about to watch grass for a long time.

"Yes," Mau said.

She accepted the answer and sat beside him.

Fern traced the flow with her eyes. Cold collected at the spikes, then eased outward in a regular pulse. The grass between the rings was dry. Nothing there had moved to attack them; nothing needed to for the arrangement to be interesting.

"It's keeping the spaces clear," she said.

Frieren nodded.

"A boundary that moves the frost, rather than warming everything inside."

Fern looked at her.

"You knew."

"I wanted to see what you'd make of it."

That could have sounded like a lesson she had not asked for. Frieren seemed to hear the possibility too, because she added, "I hadn't noticed the dry spaces until you pointed them out."

Fern looked back at the hollow.

They spent a little longer there. She sketched the placement on the back of a spare page, keeping it separate from the travel map. A thing seen was not yet a spell learned, but it was something she wanted to understand after they left.

"Can we go this way?" she asked when the sketch was done. "The slope is easier, and it joins the high path beyond those rocks."

Frieren checked the ground she meant.

"Lead us."

Fern stood up. She did not ask whether Frieren was certain.

On the easier slope, Stark fell into step beside her.

"Grass worth looking at?"

"Yes."

"Good. I was going to be very disappointed if it wasn't."

She showed him the sketch while they paused at the next turn. He asked about the spaces rather than pretending to understand the whole arrangement. She explained it and found that she wanted to.

Behind them, Frieren was telling Mau why the small discovery might matter more than an impressive room full of magical light.

Fern could hear the beginning of his answer.

"I thought you liked rooms full of magical light."

"I can like two things."

Fern kept walking with Stark, smiling where neither of them could see.
