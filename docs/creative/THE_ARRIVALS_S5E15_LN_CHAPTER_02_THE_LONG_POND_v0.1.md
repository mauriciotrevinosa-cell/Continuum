# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 19 — The Long Pond

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 19
**Continuity state:** A shoreline encounter requires coordinated defence; Mau chooses one bounded ordinary CE strike and closes it, with no Red or new named technique

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate day four, afternoon.*

The pond was narrower than the map made it look and much longer than anybody wanted.

Ice covered most of it. Dark water showed near the bank, moving where nothing above the surface moved. Frieren chose the shore anyway. The other route went straight through the trees the archers had used, and she preferred a danger she could keep to one side.

"Nobody on the ice," she said.

Milim looked down at her own feet, already safely on the bank, and decided that did not count as being told twice.

They went in single file through the narrow stretch. Stark first, following Frieren's direction from the wider ground ahead. Fern in the middle. Yuka beside Mau where the bank broadened briefly. Milim last.

Something struck the underside of the ice.

Stark stopped.

A crack ran toward the edge, paused, and branched.

"Away from the water," Fern said.

The first creature came through before Yuka could finish stepping back. Long forelimbs, a narrow armoured head, teeth that closed where her hand had been a moment earlier.

Mau pulled her toward the root behind them.

A second shape came over the bank beside his knee.

There was no room for Milim's full movement. No clean line for Fern with him and Yuka between her and the creature.

*One strike. Close immediately,* Ciel said, answering the choice he was already asking her to help him make.

He let the familiar energy reach his fist, thin and hard enough to matter, and hit the creature where the plates separated beneath its jaw.

The impact hurt.

It knocked the head away from his leg. Milim took it from there, catching the body before it could fall against Yuka and driving it back into the open ground. Mau shut the flow and moved clear.

He did not stay to admire having done it.

Fern fired across the water at the third emerging shape. Stark held the first back with the axe until Frieren had a clean angle. The shot broke the ice behind it and drove the creature into the exposed water, where Milim's thrown stone ended its next attempt to rise.

Then they were still.

Nobody went forward to collect anything until Frieren had checked the dark surface again.

Mau sat on the root with his hand open. His knuckles were bruised. His arm ached. No new crack spreading up the skin, no crowd of other answers demanding the same opening.

Yuka knelt in front of him.

"Show me."

"It's a bruise."

"Then it'll survive being shown."

He showed her. Frieren checked after her, and Ciel compared the operation with the one they had intended rather than with the result Mau wished he had achieved.

"One strike," he said.

"I saw," Frieren answered.

Milim brought back the crystals with a sleeve full of dirty snow. Stark retrieved the rope from the bank. Fern found a clear way up to the dry ground above them.

They left the pond behind before stopping for a proper rest.

Only then did the little window appear.

Mau stared at it for so long that Frieren put a hand on his shoulder.

"What?"

He swallowed.

"Level two."
