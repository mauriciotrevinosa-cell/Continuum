# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 29 — The Opening

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 29
**Continuity state:** Mau's one small Red hits the eye after an earned opening; Stark, Milim, Fern and Frieren finish together; the System's credit does not make the victory solo

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate day seven, the coordinated finish.*

The shape was familiar.

That was not the same thing as easy.

Mau kept his hand low while he asked. No huge convergence. No reaching for the thing that had once taken his chest apart. One repulsive knot, held only long enough to put it where the plan needed it.

*I can help you bound it. I cannot remove the cost,* Ciel said.

"Do it with me."

The Noise separated the pattern from the rest. He felt it waiting, not taking his arm away from him, not deciding what he meant to strike.

Above, Frieren forced the dragon's head down.

Fern's shot took the last easy line away from its right wing. Milim struck the claw that would have caught the pillar. For a moment the beast had to look straight through the gap.

Its eye found Mau.

He let the little Red go.

It was ugly. The edge tore at his fingers before he closed behind it. No perfect sphere, no great shining answer to everything. A dense, violent point, just large enough to do what it had been made to do.

It struck the eye.

The dragon convulsed. Its wing missed the angle that would have carried it clear of the pillars. Stone and scale collided. The whole body came down sideways into the broken ground, one foreleg trapped beneath it.

Mau fell back against the pillar.

"Closed," he said before anybody could ask.

His fingers burned. He curled them carefully and kept the hand against his chest.

Stark was already moving.

Yuka hauled the last loose length of rope away from his feet and shouted the clear line. Milim took the tail's first sweep on the slope rather than letting it reach him. Stark reached the dragon's neck and struck where the fall had opened the plates.

The beast tried to rise.

Frieren and Fern fired into the same exposed line.

The light went through.

For a moment Mau could hear nothing but his own breathing.

Then the dragon stopped moving.

They did not celebrate immediately. Frieren landed near the head and waited for the last movement that might not be the last. Fern kept her angle. Stark backed away with the axe up. Milim stood between Mau and the great body until he told her he could see past her boot and would like to continue doing so.

She sat down instead, which was only a partial improvement.

The window appeared.

*Dragon defeated.*

Below it, the level changed. Three. More available points. The bruised hand stayed bruised; the burned skin stayed sore.

Mau dismissed the window long enough to answer Frieren.

"One. Closed. My hand hurts. Head's clear enough to tell you that."

She knelt and took his wrist, looking at the damage without squeezing it.

"We clean it before doing anything else."

He nodded.

Milim announced that the dragon had been rude. Stark sat in the snow, axe across his knees, and began to laugh in the exhausted way people sometimes laughed when the work was finished and their bodies had not yet believed it.

Fern landed beside him. She touched his shoulder. He looked up, still laughing a little, and caught her hand.

Yuka counted all six aloud before taking off her bag.

Only then did the first blue light run along the fallen body.

The dragon's shadow remained dark beneath it.

Inside Mau, something new reached the edge of a structure it had spent many days learning how to name.
