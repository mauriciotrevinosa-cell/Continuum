# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 30 — Arise

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 30
**Continuity state:** Ciel helps find the confirmation word; the first Arise fails before Mau focuses as with Ear; one dragon shadow is registered with finite separate reserve

*When: Day 280 outside / Red Gate day seven, after the dragon.*

The body was going away.

Blue light ran through the fallen scales, taking the dragon apart in long, quiet pieces. Under it, the shadow stayed. Not a stain on the stone. A depth the morning light should not have allowed.

Mau looked at it.

The window opened again.

*Shadow extraction available.*

He read the line aloud before doing anything with it.

Frieren stopped tying the clean cloth around his fingers.

"Do you know what that means?"

"Not completely."

*Give me a moment,* Ciel said.

He repeated that too. They waited while she compared the offered function with the unfinished structure the Noise had been building since the Exchange. It was not another complete set of patterns behind the old walls. It belonged to the new branch, with a small reserve of its own, already moving in response to the shadow.

Small was the important part.

*There is a confirmation word. I can help identify it. Stop if you feel the other boundaries move.*

Mau nodded, then looked at Frieren's hand still holding the end of his bandage. He had answered somebody she couldn't hear and begun preparing to stand without explaining why.

"Ciel thinks it belongs to the new branch. Not the part we just closed. There's a way to call it, and a storage function beside it. I want to see whether I can use those."

Frieren finished the knot before letting go of his wrist.

"Now?"

"The shape is still here. I don't know if it will be after we leave."

"We don't have to keep every thing we find."

He looked at the depth under the vanishing body. He wanted it badly enough to have already started turning an opportunity into an obligation.

"I know. I want to try this one."

He asked Ciel whether she could distinguish the new reserve's movement from the openings that had hurt his hand. She could observe that much. She could not tell him how a creature he hadn't called would behave.

He repeated both parts.

"Then don't put it between us and the pass back to camp," Fern said from the edge of the hollow.

Mau looked around properly. Yuka and Milim moved to the sound stone on his other side. Stark stayed clear of the place where wings might rise. Frieren waited until he had pointed out the return function he meant to use.

"Briefly," she said. "Then we find out what it cost before trying again."

"Briefly," he agreed.

He stood without using the bandaged hand to push himself up. Frieren stayed beside him. Milim moved closer to Yuka. Fern watched the last of the body vanish without letting the view draw everyone away from the surrounding hollow.

"Rise," Mau said, trying the first candidate.

Nothing happened.

Ciel waited through the response before offering the next.

"Awaken."

The dark remained dark.

He could feel the Noise studying the little structure, separating a label from a command more carefully than it had at the fountain. No word escaped ahead of his choice.

*Arise,* Ciel said at last.

Mau faced the shadow.

"Arise."

Something lifted.

A head, incomplete, long enough to make Milim's hands close. Then the shape folded back into itself, as though it had reached for a place that was not firmly there.

Mau stepped away.

"It didn't work."

*The word was correct. Do you remember Ear?*

He did. The weight of the dog, the moment asking had stopped being a substitute for meaning it.

*You are not asking whether it would like to stand. Establish that you can command what you are bringing into your army.*

He looked at the fading place where the body had lain.

They had beaten it together. He could not turn that into a lie about having stood alone against the dragon. He could stand in the truth of it, though. He had faced it, chosen a blow, stopped. He was still here.

"We defeated you," he said quietly. "Now stand with me."

He held the intention steady rather than reaching for a louder voice.

"Arise."

The shadow rose.

Not the great living weight that had struck the pillars. A dark shape made from its outline, wings drawing themselves out of the pooled black, a head lowering toward the boy who had called it. The eye he had struck was whole in the new shape.

He felt the cost at once.

A separate, shallow reserve falling as the dragon took form. Not his little supply of mana, not the cursed energy he had just closed. Something new, and nowhere near deep enough to mistake for endless.

*That is enough for now,* Ciel said.

He had time to meet the dragon's gaze before asking it back.

It folded into his shadow.

The relief was immediate. Not because the cost returned; it did not. Because the drain stopped.

The window recorded one registered shadow, space for more than he could presently afford to call. Ten places. That number did not mean ten dragons standing beside him.

Mau sat down on the nearest sound stone.

Yuka looked at his feet.

"Is it there?"

"I think so."

"Beside Ear?"

He listened inwardly, carefully.

"Different place. Same direction if you're looking from outside."

She considered that and decided not to put her foot into his shadow to check.

Frieren checked that the knot had held.

"We tell Shoko all of that," she said.

"Yes."

"Before somebody calls it a pet."

Milim closed her mouth.
