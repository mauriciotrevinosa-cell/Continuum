# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 30
## Chapter 1 — The Extension

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 30
**Reading order:** 1
**Continuity state:** Queen2 begins after rest with the existing oak crown and a plainly playful amended pass; its reach remains Mau only, not the fort or household authority

*When: Day 281, morning.*

The crown had survived in better condition than its first flowers.

Frieren found it among the things she had kept, oak beneath old gold paint, Agott's careful lettering still on the inside. She held it at an angle to examine a worn place near the rim.

Mau watched from the bed.

"A constitutional problem?"

"It says one day."

"It also said one day last time."

"Then it has experience being wrong."

She put it on. It sat slightly crooked until he reached up with his better hand and adjusted it.

"Two days," he said. "Beginning today."

She looked at him for a long, pleased moment.

"Breakfast. Subject."

"A formidable first decree."

"I can make it worse."

He got up.

Shoko checked his hand before breakfast, which Frieren accepted as a practical limitation rather than an insult to the throne. The skin was improving. No experiments, no new gate trip, normal easy use if it did not pull the tender places. He took the instructions down with him.

Umaru saw the crown from the other end of the common room and came over with the expression of an official discovering a rival government had reopened without filing notice.

"How long?"

"Two days," Frieren said.

Umaru looked at Mau.

"Two books," he explained.

"You need to stop negotiating unsupervised."

She found a scrap of clean paper and wrote an extension beneath the old arrangement, then read it aloud with increasing severity. Two days. One subject. Mau only. Not valid on the fort. Not valid on Rimuru's loft. No claim over the food budget.

Frieren watched the list grow.

"You're inventing restrictions."

"I'm preserving the nation."

Rimuru, eating at the end of the table, lifted his cup in support of the loft clause.

Mau put the pass where Frieren could see it. He did not sign it; nobody needed a real binding contract to understand a game they had chosen to play.

Diablo inclined his head as he passed.

"Your Majesty."

Frieren accepted the greeting with perfect composure. Then she noticed Mau smiling at her and lost a little of it.

"My tea."

He moved the cup the last few inches into her reach.

"At once."

Fern watched this over breakfast.

"You're carrying your own cup when he's finished eating."

Frieren looked at the pass, discovered that it supplied no useful answer, and decided to save her authority for a better opportunity.

After breakfast she took Mau's hand and led him toward the courtyard bench.

The crown caught the morning light. Nobody had repainted it to make the second time look like the first.

Mau thought it looked better that way.
