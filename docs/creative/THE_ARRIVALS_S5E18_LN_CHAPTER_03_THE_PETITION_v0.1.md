# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 33 — The Petition

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 33
**Continuity state:** The fort fixes its separate owed day for Day284; Bocchi and Umaru exercise their own agency and Queen2 does not consume the fort's promise

*When: Day 281, afternoon.*

The petition arrived folded into a shape that did not improve its legibility.

Umaru put it on Mau's knees. Frieren moved the book just far enough to allow this intrusion.

"Official business," Umaru said.

"With the queen?" Mau asked.

"With the subject. Her jurisdiction is disputed."

Frieren touched the crown.

"One of my subjects can hear you."

"You have one."

"Then all of them."

Mau unfolded the page before either government could decide this required an amendment.

The fort wanted its day. Not a visit squeezed between other things. Not the family day already spent with Ori and Frieren. One of the owed days, planned by the people who lived and gathered there, with Mau in attendance and no right to redesign it into helping everyone else finish work.

He read the proposed date.

"Day two hundred and eighty-four?"

"After your two days and another day in case you need it," Umaru said. "No gate. No being suddenly required to inspect a new sword."

"The sword may file an appeal."

"Denied."

Bocchi stood just behind her with a smaller sheet. Mau held out his hand for it; she hesitated, then gave it to him.

A few choices for the afternoon. A game, music if people wanted it, a place to sit where nobody had to perform. Two alternatives crossed out rather than silently disappearing.

"Which one do you want?" he asked her.

She looked at Umaru, then back at him.

"The last. For part of it."

He read it again.

"Then leave room for it."

"It isn't very—"

"I'll like it if you like it. You still get to decide whether it's good after we do it."

She nodded slowly, taking back the little page.

Frieren leaned over his shoulder.

"I'm invited. Ori said."

Umaru considered her.

"As a person. The crown stays out."

"A harsh frontier."

"A sovereign territory."

They agreed on the day. Mau asked that Shoko's check stay first if it was still needed, and that the whole day include enough sitting to remain enjoyable instead of becoming a test nobody had intended.

Umaru wrote that in without objection.

When they left, Bocchi turned back long enough to ask whether he remembered the two versions of the passage from the shade bench.

"The one I sang badly?"

She nodded, trying very hard not to smile.

"I remember."

"I changed it."

"Good changed or winter changed?"

She laughed then. It lasted only a moment, but it was enough to make her stop hiding behind the page.

"You can hear it that day."

"I'll be there."

Frieren settled against his shoulder after the footsteps were gone.

"You still have royal business today."

He put the appointment safely beside the book.

"I'm entirely at your service."

"Good. My page weight wandered."
