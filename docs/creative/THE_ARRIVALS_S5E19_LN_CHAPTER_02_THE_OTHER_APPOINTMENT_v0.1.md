# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 37 — The Other Appointment

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 37
**Continuity state:** The owed fort day begins after the agreed check; Mau attends a day organized by the fort, with Frieren invited as herself and no new initiation

*When: Day 284, breakfast and morning.*

The fort's day began before Mau reached the stairs.

Ori stood in the connecting doorway with a folded page and a look he recognized from both her mother and the president of the Ministry.

"You're expected."

"I've been expected since yesterday."

"Today it's official."

Frieren looked up from fastening her clothes.

"Am I expected?"

"You're invited. No crown."

Mau turned away too late to hide his laugh.

They went down for the agreed check first. Shoko found the hand improving, checked the rest without discovering an exciting reason to spoil breakfast, and sent him on with a warning about turning games into physical trials. Ori heard the warning and included it in the page.

Umaru met them after they ate.

"No gate," she said.

"I left the key upstairs."

"No surprise jobs."

"I have none scheduled."

"No spending all day making tea for everyone instead of sitting down."

Mau looked at Frieren.

She had no sympathy to offer. "A serious offence."

They found places among the cushions without holding an admission ceremony for people who already belonged. Suika had something under a cloth. Coco had ordinary pencils and several pages, all spread just far enough apart that somebody was going to sit on them if she did not move them soon. Yuka had brought a game. Milim had brought herself and considered the supplies complete.

Bocchi sat beside Kita, quietly comparing two little sheets. When Mau looked over, she lifted one in acknowledgement and went back to the conversation rather than waiting for his attention to make it important.

Fern arrived with Stark, stayed long enough to find out what they were beginning, and took the place offered to her. No one asked her to accept a little-sister title before she was allowed to enjoy being there.

Vamola handed Mau something from the breakfast she had kept and said it was for later. He thanked her and did not eat it immediately, which appeared to satisfy the condition she had intended.

Umaru called the first order of business.

"Choosing what we actually want to do."

"You haven't already chosen?" Mau asked.

"We have several excellent options. We are allowing democracy to discover which one is excellent first."

Frieren inspected the page over his shoulder.

"I vote for the seated part."

"There are several seated parts."

"An excellent administration."

Suika removed the cloth.

Nobody, including the president, had expected the object beneath it to be a sock.

Mau looked at the room full of people looking at the sock and settled more comfortably into his cushion.

He thought he was going to like the day.
