# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 38 — A Very Poor Map

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 38
**Continuity state:** A fort-organized observation game gives Coco and Suika authority and the others playful reciprocal roles; no ink or new magic is exposed to Mau

*When: Day 284, late morning.*

The sock was the first clue.

"It wasn't meant to be," Suika admitted.

"Was it meant to be hidden?" Yuka asked.

"Something was. The sock got put on top of it."

Milim lifted the sock. Beneath it lay a small wooden piece marked with a perfectly ordinary pencil arrow.

The room considered the arrow.

"An ingenious disguise," Mau said.

Coco looked at Suika. Suika looked back. Then both of them started laughing, and the game improved enormously before anybody understood the rules.

They were to find several marked objects using descriptions and a map that deliberately left out the easy parts. No magic for locating them. No moving furniture without asking. No making someone else find your object because you were stronger or more important.

Umaru called that last rule unnecessary. Everybody looked at her until she accepted its potential value.

Mau and Bocchi received one page between them.

Frieren and Yuka got another. This immediately produced a dispute about whether the book beside the cushions counted as furniture.

"It takes up space," Yuka said.

"So do you," Frieren answered.

Stark laughed and tried to hide it when Fern turned toward him. She was smiling too, which made hiding it seem less important.

Mau looked at his map.

"This bend can't be right."

Bocchi turned the page over.

"It's the other side."

He looked again.

"A sophisticated first obstacle."

She laughed under her breath and pointed to the actual first clue. They began there. She noticed the small repeated marks before he did; he found the place where the description depended on looking from the cushion rather than standing over it.

They disagreed about one clue and tested both answers instead of letting the louder person choose. Neither answer was right. Coco enjoyed that so much that Mau accused her of designing the game specifically to embarrass them.

"Only a little," she said.

He put a hand over his heart.

"Betrayed by scholarship."

At the far end, Milim announced a discovery. It turned out to be one of the objects Suika had not hidden yet. The president issued a temporary ruling, amended it under protest, and eventually accepted that discovery had occurred without deserving all the points.

They played long enough for the original teams to stop being useful. People joined the problem nearest them. Kita helped Bocchi compare the last two marks; Yuka and Frieren found their answer only after asking Vamola what she had noticed while everyone else was standing in the way.

Mau finished with fewer points than Milim, which she intended to remember forever.

"These points are not transferable," Umaru told him.

"Tragic. I finally knew where I wanted to spend them."

Coco collected the pages afterward. Not to erase the mistakes. She liked the marks where people had changed their minds.

Suika put the sock away somewhere it would cause a different problem later.
