# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 45 — The First Reading

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 45
**Continuity state:** The bracelets distinguish source recovery, learned skill and Mau's acquired branch; sharing is chosen and unknown calibration is not replaced by invented percentages

*When: Day 285, morning.*

Rem took the bracelet off before asking whether anybody wanted to see.

It was such a small movement that most of the table missed it. Mau did not. She had found the clasp, tested that it came away without a struggle, and put the band down where she could decide to pick it up again.

"It says the reference matches," she told Shoko.

"The whole reference?"

"The part it has classified. My restored state. One hundred percent."

Ram looked at it from the other side of the table.

"How comforting. Perhaps it will now tell you to stop doing everybody else's washing."

Rem smiled and put it on again. She selected the recovery field and showed that much. The rest stayed angled toward her.

They had brought only a few bands out of the case. There was no reason to hold a public inspection of sixty people simply because they had enough objects for it. Shoko wanted to know what could be measured, how the wearer controlled the display, and whether removing it stopped whatever it was doing.

So far, removing it did.

Bocchi tried another after Kita had tested the catch. Her recovery field matched too. She looked from the little notice to her own hands.

"It doesn't mean I can play anything," she said.

"No," Kita answered. "We'll still have to practise."

That pleased her more than the number.

Mau's band behaved differently. The familiar level appeared when he called it. Three. Ten points waiting. One registered shadow. Other sections stopped at the edge of what the new structure had actually named.

The species field stayed empty.

When he asked for the whole of what was inside him, the display did not oblige by turning a blank into a confident answer.

"It's showing the branch," he said. "Not all of me."

Rimuru looked at the field Mau chose to share. "Good to know where the edge is."

Gojo leaned over the table. His own band had a short list of familiar operations and several unavailable ones. He hid most of it again before anyone could mistake interest for an invitation.

"My Blue still isn't bigger because I asked nicely," he said.

"A devastating flaw in the equipment," Shoko said.

He turned the band on his wrist, thinking now. Infinity, the eyes, the small pull he could produce: related, not identical. The device offered distinctions where a single number would have made him look either falsely whole or falsely absent.

Some fields needed a better reference. Some needed a test. They wrote those down instead of guessing.

Yuta showed only the operations he was willing to discuss. Healing was available within its costs. The rest did not become usable because a screen knew a name for it. The band did not confuse his young body with every year he remembered living.

By the time they stopped, Senku had filled a page with questions and Shoko had crossed out two proposed experiments.

"Questions are cheap," he protested.

"Then keep asking them without spending a patient."

Mau closed his display. The quiet afterward was ordinary. His shadow did not produce a dragon because somebody had counted it.

Rem took her band off again and slipped it into a safe pocket before helping carry the cups away.

Ram took half the cups from her.

"An excellent reading," she said. "Already useful."
