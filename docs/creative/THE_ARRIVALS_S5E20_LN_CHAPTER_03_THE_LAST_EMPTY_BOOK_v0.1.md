# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 30
## Chapter 13 — The Last Empty Book

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 30
**Reading order:** 13
**Continuity state:** Arrival House daily reception ceases because no guests remain; Jinshi chooses bounded domestic records under Fern and keeps his own life with Maomao

*When: Day 285, afternoon.*

Jinshi opened the arrivals book and found the same empty space he had found yesterday.

He did not add a mark simply to prove he had looked.

The rooms were empty. The keys were accounted for. The last people who had needed a place on the road had gone on to the lives and work they had chosen. There was no new family waiting because he failed to open the book often enough.

Maomao stood in the doorway with a small packet under one arm.

"Still empty?"

"A remarkably successful inspection."

"Then you can stop doing it every day."

He looked at the page.

It was not that simple, which was generally what he thought when she made a thing sound very simple.

"If someone comes—"

"Someone will tell the house. You don't need to sit in an empty reception to make that possible."

She came over and looked at the key list. Not touching anything he had kept in order, just reading enough to understand why he had stayed with it.

"You did the job," she said. "That doesn't mean the job has to continue forever."

He closed the book.

The sound was disappointingly small for a decision he had been avoiding.

They took the keys and the record to Fern together. The building would stay usable. Its rooms would not become something else merely because they stopped treating an empty desk as a daily duty. Future use could be decided when there was a reason to decide it.

Fern agreed.

Then she looked at Jinshi over a different pile of papers.

"Would you like a job that has people in it?"

"That depends on the people."

Maomao looked pleased with the answer.

Fern explained what she meant. Daily receipts, simple entries, a short record of what came in and went out. Anko still kept the gate loot register. Fern kept the budget, priorities and review. Nano had offered to check arithmetic at agreed times, not to be summoned whenever somebody lost a number.

Jinshi asked how long the routine should take.

They agreed on one daily session and a place to leave later entries, so nobody had to spend all afternoon chasing the next person with a receipt. He would flag questions for Fern rather than decide a new household policy because a line had nowhere obvious to go.

He chose to try it for a week.

"Not the whole day," Maomao said as they left.

"You heard the arrangement."

"I heard you calculating how much else you could put inside it."

He looked at her packet.

"And you?"

She shifted it behind her back with the speed of somebody who had forgotten she was carrying evidence.

"Different."

"Of course."

They went toward the courtyard, arguing about which of them had a more convincing reason to neglect an afternoon.

The arrivals book stayed closed on its proper shelf.

No one was assigned to sit beside it.
