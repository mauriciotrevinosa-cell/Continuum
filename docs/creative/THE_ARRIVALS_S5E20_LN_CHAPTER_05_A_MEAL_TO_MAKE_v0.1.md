# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 45 — A Meal To Make

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 45
**Continuity state:** Rem directs a shared meal with Ram, Mau, Frieren and Fern helping; Momo takes actual chosen time off and the trial does not make Rem permanent substitute chef

*When: Day 285, supper.*

Rem had a menu, a clean work surface, and four people waiting to be told where they belonged.

She looked at them with considerable satisfaction.

"Ram, the sauce. Fern, will you check the grain? Frieren, these. Mau—"

"The glamorous job?"

"The peels."

He took the bowl.

"At last."

Momo had left before they began. Not hiding in the doorway waiting for the first error, not preparing another meal in case they failed. She and Okarun had gone out with Vamola to see something the girls wanted to show them, and she had said she would be back when it was time to eat.

Rem intended that to work.

She did not intend to inherit every supper in exchange.

The new arrangement was a trial. Tonight hers, with the help actually in front of her. Another evening would belong to other hands. Momo kept the parts she liked, chose what she could leave, and would have a real say after trying it rather than thanking them politely for an arrangement made over her head.

Ram tasted the sauce and added a little more water.

"Your kingdom is becoming productive," she told Frieren.

"The kingdom is over."

"A pity. The vegetables respected your authority."

Frieren looked down at the pieces she had cut. They were not very even. Fern reached past her and moved the smaller ones into a separate dish so they would cook together.

"They need different times," she explained.

Frieren accepted the correction with a nod. Mau watched the exchange and kept his sensible thoughts to himself.

Rem adjusted the sequence. Asked for the water. Changed her mind about which pan to use and said so without treating a revision as a failure of command.

The meal took longer than Momo would have taken alone on a familiar menu. It still reached the table hot.

Mau carried the light dishes with his better hand. Ram took the next load. Frieren set the places Fern pointed out, then moved one because Ori asked to sit where she could tell Yuka something before it escaped her memory.

When Momo came back, she stopped in the doorway and looked at the table.

"How bad?"

"We lost a vegetable," Mau said.

"He peeled it into nothing," Ram said.

"A technical disagreement."

Momo laughed. She sat. Okarun put her cup down beside the place she chose instead of asking what needed doing in the kitchen.

Rem watched her take the first mouthful.

Momo looked up.

"Good. Really good."

It mattered. Rem let it matter without promising the next seven days in the glow of the compliment.

After eating, they divided the clearing. A few different people joined. Nobody needed Fern to stand beside the basin to make the dishes stay washed.

She went out with Frieren while the light was still in the courtyard.

Ram found Rem starting to check the kitchen one last time and handed her a cup instead.

"You've finished."

Rem looked at the work, then at her sister.

"So have you."

They sat down together before either could find another useful reason not to.
