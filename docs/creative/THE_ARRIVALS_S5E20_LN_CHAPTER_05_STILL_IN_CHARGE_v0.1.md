# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 49 — Still In Charge

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 49
**Continuity state:** Fern chooses shared execution without surrendering authority; Frieren offers specific affection and time; the brief two-Zoltraak Gojo gag follows the emotional conversation

*When: Day 285, late afternoon.*

Fern had not meant to keep the ledger on her knees.

She had brought it out to finish one entry, and then Frieren sat beside her, and there it remained between the cup and the part of the afternoon neither of them had yet named.

"Jinshi will do the daily entries," Fern said.

"He told me."

"I'll still review them."

"Yes."

"And spending still needs the same agreement."

"I know."

Frieren waited. It made continuing the practical explanation feel increasingly like talking around something she could already see.

Fern closed the book, but did not put it away.

"I like doing it."

"I know that too."

"Not all of it. Some of it."

Frieren looked toward the courtyard path, where Mau had stopped to help Rem lift one end of a light basket. He asked before taking it. She gave him only the end she wanted him to have.

"He's learned to wake you," Fern said.

Frieren turned back.

"Mostly."

"I'm grateful. I don't want to do everything I used to do."

She could say that part easily. The other part sat under it, not a reasonable request for a new rota, not something Stark or the girls could supply simply by being people she loved.

"I still want you to ask for me sometimes. When you don't need anything recorded."

Frieren's face changed.

"Fern."

"I know you do. I'm not saying you never—"

"I know."

She put a hand over Fern's on the closed cover. No condition attached. No agreement that Fern must first become better at leisure before being allowed to want this.

"I wanted you with me in the gate," Frieren said. "Not because somebody had to keep the food list. I liked what you found. I liked being there with you."

Fern looked down at their hands.

"You could say it a little more often."

"I can."

They sat without adding anything for a while. Mau came past with the empty basket, saw where the afternoon had gone, and put it away without joining them. Fern was grateful for that too.

At last she moved the ledger to the space beside her.

"But I'm still in charge."

Frieren smiled.

"Yes. Let somebody else carry the entries sometimes. You can enjoy your life without losing your place in it."

"I enjoy my life."

"Then have more of the parts you enjoy. There are people who want you with them because you're Fern. Not because of what you can do for them."

Fern's answer took a moment.

"Including you."

"Including me."

Frieren drew her closer. Fern let her, leaning against the shoulder she had known in so many different places that accepting it here felt less like asking for something new than finding an old thing still hers.

The quiet lasted long enough to be theirs.

Then Gojo, passing beyond the clear stretch of courtyard, sighed theatrically.

"Beautiful. I almost feel—"

Two thin lines of light stopped short of his face.

He froze with one hand on his chest. Infinity held both harmlessly away from him. Fern and Frieren lowered their hands together, and the last of the light faded with no mark on him or anything behind him.

Shoko laughed from the clinic doorway.

"You earned that."

Gojo considered appealing. Saw both women still looking at him. Decided to carry on walking instead.

Fern put the ledger on the table where Jinshi could find it.

"Tea tomorrow?" Frieren asked.

"When?"

"When we're both awake. A flexible appointment."

Fern laughed softly, and began negotiating a more useful time.
