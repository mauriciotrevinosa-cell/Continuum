# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 30
## Chapter 16 — Dry Ground

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 30
**Reading order:** 16
**Continuity state:** The household chooses a relocation site thirty paces inside the existing boundary, clear of roots, house exits and the interface's public approach range

*When: Day 286, morning.*

Anko left the red stake where it was.

Mau had expected that, but was glad she said it before anybody began measuring from a marker somebody might later move for convenience.

"This is the protection line," she said. "The gate is a different thing. We keep them different."

Thirty paces on the house side, the ground rose into a dry, clear patch. Beyond it, the established path went back toward the yard without crossing the tree's root circle or either cellar exit. It was far enough inside that a person standing outside the boundary would not simply wander into the shop window's ordinary twenty-pace range.

That mattered more than making it look impressive.

Wakana checked the ground where the first screen could stand. Senku checked drainage. Frieren checked the clear approach and asked what they would do if a whole party came out needing to sit down at once.

They moved one proposed panel.

Nobody proposed putting the entrance in a bedroom wall because it would save walking.

Rimuru held the sketch against a flat board while Fern compared it with the existing paths. The house remained around the tree's root circle. This was open ground beyond the service path, not permission to build through the courtyard or cover the roots.

"Three sides," Wakana said, indicating the simple timber screen. "Open approach. I can use the sound spare panels. A building would take longer."

"We don't need a building today," Rimuru said.

The screen would hide the daily activity from the outer path. It would not be advertised as a magical wall or a replacement for keeping watch. There would be room beside it for the key's second entrance when it actually appeared, without forcing one party through another.

Mau listened to all of it with the unused relocation claim before him.

Once.

The notice did not offer to let them try a location and move it again after discovering that carrying water there was annoying.

"Here?" he asked when the final mark was down.

Anko checked the distance to her stake one more time. Fern checked the approach. Wakana set a plain peg in the dry ground and looked along the line of the panels that were not there yet.

They agreed.

Mau closed the notice without accepting it immediately.

"We tell the next watch first. Make sure nobody is inside. Then move it."

Rimuru nodded.

At the edge of the patch, Milim was explaining to Ori that she had carried much heavier things than a timber panel. Ori asked whether she could carry it without hitting the other panel.

Milim looked at the space.

"Yes."

"The other other panel?"

"I can be careful."

Wakana handed her one end and kept the other. She matched his pace so seriously that Mau stopped worrying about the promise before she had finished proving it.

They carried the first panel onto dry ground.

The red stake stayed in the field behind them.
