# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 50 — One Drink

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 50
**Continuity state:** The food expansion begins with one chosen drink and one food proposal, not an instant complete catalogue; Umaru's wider supply plan remains active and purchases still cost shared crystals

*When: Day 286, afternoon.*

Umaru discovered the food proposal before anyone had finished being pleased about torches.

Her response made Milim come running.

"What happened?"

"Civilization."

Mau looked at the window.

It offered a limited beginning: one drink candidate and one food candidate for assessment, with later additions dependent on the catalogue's own expansion. A proposal was not stock. Stock would still require payment. Nothing offered free meals because somebody had had a good idea while hungry.

Umaru read it three times.

Then she began making a list faster than Jinshi could have recorded it if this had been his job.

Cola. Drinks for long walks and heat. Gels. Better travelling rations. Protein bars. Shakes. Desserts.

"That's more than one drink," Mau said.

"It's a plan."

"A large plan."

"We have learned the value of large pages."

She put a firm mark beside cola.

Fern looked at it, then at her.

"Convince us about the food too."

Umaru did. Not by claiming a sweet bar could replace every meal, but by comparing what they actually carried in the gate: weight, keeping quality, a portion someone could eat during a short stop, ingredients the buyer could check. A practical bar with oats, nuts and protein became the first candidate. Not a miracle. Not a healing item. Food.

Momo joined the discussion and chose not to volunteer to manufacture every recipe on the list. Rem asked about portions. Shoko asked whether the catalogue would actually name what was in the result before anyone fed it to people.

They got a limited description and an assessment notice. Later today if accepted, not a promise that imagining something created it instantly in their hands.

Umaru supplied her own chosen description of the drink she wanted. She did not have to offer another person's memories to do it.

The notice became two pending entries.

She stared at the cola line as though attention might shorten the wait.

"It won't be cold," Senku said.

The silence was immediate.

Umaru turned toward him very slowly.

"Why would you say that?"

"Because something has to take the heat out."

"Then take the heat out."

He grinned, already looking at the new materials page.

"Now that's a useful question."

Mau saw the expression and took a quiet step away from the equipment before he was recruited as a power source.

Maomao saw the step, looked at Senku, and moved the empty drinking jar off the table where he had begun imagining a very different sort of experiment.
