# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 51 — The Cold Jar

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 51
**Continuity state:** One drink and food become paid stock; a limited gate cooling component demonstrates finite heat transfer, not a restored electrical grid; Coco's mother and Nazuna have chosen domestic and leisure time

*When: Day 286, late afternoon.*

The first bottle was not cold.

Umaru looked at Senku as though he had arranged this personally.

He looked far too pleased to be innocent.

The two proposals had been accepted into a very small catalogue. Cola. The practical bar, with its contents and portion shown before purchase. Later candidates remained candidates. Nobody got a free crate, and the shared balance changed by the ordinary price when they bought the samples.

Umaru opened the bottle, tasted it, and closed her eyes.

For a moment she was simply happy.

Then she opened them and pointed at Senku.

"Cold."

He had a cooling insert from the new utility stock, purchased under the same small trial allowance as the samples. A gate component, not the ordinary crystal he had once tried to connect to a bulb. Its casing had an inlet, a contact face and a charge limit.

They put the contact face against a metal vessel and the bottle in water inside it. Maomao kept a thermometer where she could actually compare the change. Senku fed the insert a small measured crystal and opened the shortest setting.

The water cooled.

The crystal dimmed.

He closed the setting, waited, and checked both results before grinning.

"There. A specific tool doing a specific job. We haven't persuaded a raw crystal to become a power station."

The charge was partly spent. The cooling stopped when they stopped it. Keeping a room cold would require a great deal more than placing the jar in the corner and believing in it.

Umaru found those limitations acceptable as long as nobody spent the next hour discussing them with her drink trapped in the experiment.

She took a second taste and looked so relieved that Mau decided not to ask whether civilization had now been completed.

Across the courtyard, Coco's mother finished the cleaning she had agreed to share and hung the cloth to dry. Another pair of hands took the next section; she did not silently inherit the whole house because she had done one part well.

Marin called her over to look at a piece she was altering for herself. She went, sat, and soon had an opinion that made Wakana take a second look rather than merely nod politely.

Nazuna appeared after waking for the evening. She helped Anko carry the light stores into their proper place, then stole neither a night shift nor the rest of the afternoon from herself. Anko brought a cup to the bench. They sat watching the cooling experiment with the lazy interest of people who were not required to solve it.

"They're going to argue about the jar," Nazuna said.

"They already are."

"Good. I haven't missed anything."

Mau shared the food sample with Yuka and Milim. It tasted like travel food trying rather hard to be pleasant. That was still an improvement on several alternatives they had eaten in the cold.

Nobody's wounds closed. Nobody's display awarded a point.

Umaru drank the last of her cola and began planning how to make sure that ordinary pleasure survived the practical list she was already writing.
