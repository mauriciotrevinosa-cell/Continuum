# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 61 — Three Favourite Answers

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 61
**Continuity state:** The group develops hypotheses from observed spatial phenomena; Senku's provocation is not an astrophysical claim or an immediate new power unlock

*When: Day 287, morning.*

"You keep showing people the answers you already like," Senku said.

Gojo leaned back.

"People usually enjoy surviving them."

"Distance that doesn't finish closing. Attraction. Repulsion. A particularly violent combination. What part of the operation are you actually varying?"

Gojo did not answer by pretending he had never thought about the question. He explained the way he understood the convergence, the imposed absence, the behaviour he could create by changing how he supplied it. Mathematics was part of the explanation, not a mysterious word someone else had to rescue him with.

Senku listened closely enough to stop preparing his next insult.

Then he prepared a better one.

"And you've spent all that effort polishing three favourite answers."

"Four. If you're going to be offensive, count properly."

Shoko laughed into her tea.

Mau watched Gojo's expression sharpen with something much more lively than last night's anger.

"Give me an example," Gojo said.

"Your little Blue is a very expensive attempt to make things behave as though there's somewhere they have to fall."

"An attempt?"

"Compared with the possibilities you're claiming? A black-hole imitation with excellent marketing."

"It isn't an astrophysical black hole."

"Good. Now we're past naming two different things the same and assuming we understand either. Show me which observable effects you can control."

Gojo was silent for a moment.

Not defeated. Interested.

"I can't run a large test with what I have now."

"Then don't. We start small enough to measure and survive."

Frieren joined them with the duplicate book under her arm. She listened until the discussion reached the dark centre Mau had made during his fight with Gojo.

"He constructed a partial version of something I use," she said. "It isn't his ordinary flight."

"I remember," Gojo said. "I interrupted it rather emphatically."

Mau rubbed the back of his neck.

"I remember that too."

Frieren described the phenomenon she had used against Diablo, before Gojo arrived in this world. That part he learned from her now, not from a memory he could not have had.

Senku separated the descriptions on his page. Attraction. Curved light. What moved, what didn't, what happened when the operation stopped. No horizon, infinite gravity or new weapon supplied by the mere act of writing them beside one another.

They chose an initial comparison: small marked objects, fixed distances, one operation at a time, no attempt to copy an effect until they knew what fuel and control it required.

Shoko added the body to the plan.

"A model isn't another brain. Understanding an idea doesn't make the tissue tolerate it."

Mau nodded before she looked at him.

Ciel offered a private distinction about the different flows she could observe. He asked if she wanted it repeated, then gave the table the useful part without pretending everyone had heard her directly.

Gojo looked at the space near him where there was no visible person.

"She's laughing, isn't she?"

"A little," Mau admitted.

"Tell her I intend to be very difficult to disappoint."

The pencil began moving again.

They had gained questions, a test they could actually describe, and an argument Gojo wanted to continue. None of them had gained a new attack before breakfast.
