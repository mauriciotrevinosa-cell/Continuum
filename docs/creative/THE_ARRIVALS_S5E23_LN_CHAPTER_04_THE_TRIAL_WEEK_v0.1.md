# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 66 — The Trial Week

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 66
**Continuity state:** The new division of work is actually tested; Fern keeps budget authority, Jinshi and Nano have bounded roles, and Momo's chosen time away continues without transferring all work to Rem

*When: Day 287, afternoon.*

Jinshi left one question open.

Fern found it where they had agreed he would put such things, clearly marked rather than hidden inside a total that almost worked.

She read the receipt. Checked Anko's separate entry. Found the difference between a crystal counted as found and the amount actually deposited.

An ordinary discrepancy, repaired before it became a household theory about missing money.

Nano checked the arithmetic at the agreed time. She did not stay to become a new permanent answer to every person who had ever disliked addition.

Fern reviewed the finished page.

It was not exactly how she would have laid it out.

It was clear, correct, and did not need copying in her own hand to become so.

She put it away.

Stark, waiting nearby with a small wrapped parcel, saw the movement and wisely said nothing about its importance.

"Ready?" he asked.

"What is that?"

"Something to eat. I thought we'd walk."

"Where?"

"I have a direction. I thought we could choose the rest."

She considered him for a moment, then took her hand off the ledger shelf.

"A promising beginning."

In the kitchen, Seiko was giving Mau an opinion about how he held a knife. He was giving her the opinion that he had several witnesses to past vegetables surviving him.

"The vegetables aren't here to testify," she said.

He changed the grip.

This was the second agreed meal coverage, not Rem appearing to rescue everyone who had escaped the kitchen. Rem came through, answered one question, and went back to her sister. She did not remain because being needed was easier than leaving.

Momo had chosen a quiet activity with the others. She had not been forced out of a kitchen she loved; she had walked away from part of the work she was willing to share, trusting that food could arrive without her supervising every cut.

The trial still had rough places. A dish ran late. Someone put the clean bowls where their neighbour did not expect them. They wrote down what needed changing rather than declaring that only the old arrangement could ever work.

At supper Momo asked how the coverage had gone. Mau told her about the vegetable trial. Seiko gave a much more convincing account.

Momo laughed and suggested a place for the bowls that would suit both of them.

Fern listened without taking over the entire conversation.

When Jinshi asked whether a new proposal needed her review, she said yes, after supper. Then returned to the meal and the piece of bread Stark had already moved into her reach.
