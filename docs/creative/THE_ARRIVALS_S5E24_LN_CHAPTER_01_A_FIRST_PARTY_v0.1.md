# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 68 — A First Party

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 68
**Continuity state:** Mau chooses a limited first visit with Yuta, Gojo and Kaneki; Rimuru covers home, Frieren is consulted, and later rotations remain open

*When: Day 287, evening.*

Yuka wanted to know whether they had decided without her.

"No," Mau said. "I've decided what I want the first trip to do."

She folded her arms. It was not an unreasonable distinction, which made it much less useful to argue with.

"One room?"

"A look at the entrance. Whether we can leave. How long it takes. Maybe the first room, if all that works."

"That sounds deliberately boring."

"I'm hoping."

She considered the two frames through the gap between the panels, then looked back at him.

"You said that before the ant."

Mau stopped trying to make his answer light.

"I remember."

She let the silence hold for a moment, then asked who he wanted with him.

Yuta, for an unfamiliar place where somebody might need bringing out rather than winning against. Gojo, who wanted to see what happened at the boundary between the two spaces. Kaneki, because he had offered to listen ahead and mark the route while the others watched the things that looked dangerous.

"I can also look at the dangerous things," Kaneki said from beside the water barrel.

"That would be a welcome addition."

Rimuru would stay home. They had discussed the coverage before either of them volunteered to be somewhere else. Frieren had heard Mau's proposal before it became the list on Anko's board.

She had asked why four.

He had answered. She had changed the medical return signal to something they could recognize without hearing a shouted sentence, then said she wanted the first ordinary-gate expedition after this one to include her. There was still a great deal of summer left to investigate.

No one had given away her place at the front of her own adventure.

Yuka looked at Yuta.

"You're going to bring him home?"

"That's the plan."

"It wasn't a very difficult question."

He smiled, a little sadly, because she knew the difference between an answer and a promise no person could make.

"We'll be careful."

She accepted that without looking pleased about accepting it.

"Then you owe me the card game when you're back. Both of you. No saying you're too old to understand the rules."

"I have never said that," Yuta said.

"You think it with your face."

Kaneki turned away to hide his smile. Mau did not bother.

Later, when Yuka went to find Maki, she bumped Mau's shoulder on the way past. Not hard. He bumped her back.

The first party stayed four.

The next one was still a conversation they could have.
