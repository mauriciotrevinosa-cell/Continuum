# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 70 — The Question At The Table

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 70
**Continuity state:** The house begins choosing observable recovery studies without declaring a universal training shortcut or removing noncombat residents from ordinary life

*When: Day 287, evening.*

Byakuya asked whether the new place would restore people faster.

Gojo had been waiting for someone to ask the question properly.

"Which part of which person?"

"A useful objection. An irritating one."

"I contain multitudes."

The page on the table was still mostly empty. Senku had left room for a comparison outside, another inside, and the conditions that would make the two worth comparing at all.

Not everybody needed to go into a dungeon to provide an answer. Fern could show Frieren the sequence she had discussed over tea without fighting for it. A clearer result, or doing the same work with less strain, would matter even if no bracelet announced a level.

Stark listened from the bench, turning a small piece of wood between his fingers.

"And if somebody just gets better at doing it?"

"Then we write that down," Byakuya said.

"Even if it isn't getting the old strength back?"

"Especially then. Otherwise we won't know what we're congratulating you for."

Stark looked rather relieved by this. Mau understood why.

Across the room, Coco was trying to persuade Agott that an ordinary drawing could be interesting without becoming a spell. Agott pointed out that she had never said it couldn't. They argued over a line until Richeh came to look, and the argument changed shape without requiring anyone at the table to settle it.

Mau watched them for a moment.

He did not want the new doorway to become the only place the house could imagine a future.

Gojo brought him back by tapping the page.

"You, for instance. We already know you can make a very complicated mess. I'd like to see you do one small thing twice without borrowing from the part you haven't measured."

"That sounded almost encouraging."

"I'm developing a style."

Shoko, passing behind his chair, advised him to keep developing it.

They chose a quiet test for another day. One position marked on the ground. One small object. Mau would watch first, and would not need to reproduce the result to justify coming.

Then the cups had to move because someone wanted the table for a game.

Senku complained. He moved them anyway.
