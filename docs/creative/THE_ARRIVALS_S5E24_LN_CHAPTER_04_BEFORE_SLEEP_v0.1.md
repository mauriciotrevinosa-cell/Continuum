# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 64 — Before Sleep

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 64
**Continuity state:** The couple keeps ordinary closeness before the first tower visit; the anniversary compass stays Mau's existing gift and Ori receives the agreed notice

*When: Day 287, night.*

Ori wanted the answer before the explanation.

"Tomorrow?"

"After breakfast," Mau said. "If Shoko is happy with the check."

"Is she ever?"

Frieren made a very quiet sound beside him.

"With the check," he repeated, "not with me as a general subject."

Ori sat on the end of the bed and listened to who was going, who was staying, how soon they meant to come back. When he reached the part about the two sandglasses, she asked if one could be broken.

"Yes," he said. "We'll look at more than one thing."

She thought about that, then nodded.

Milim called her from the next room. Something had fallen, and Milim was explaining that it had been a very small fall. Ori looked toward the connecting door with the resignation of a person who knew she would be asked for an opinion.

"Show me when you're back," she told Mau.

"The sand?"

"Whatever it is."

He agreed. She hugged him before she went to investigate the fall.

Later Frieren set her book down, and Mau put the compass on the bedside surface rather than leaving it in a pocket he would have to search in the morning.

The cracked face caught the lamplight. The needle made a small adjustment as she moved.

"It's very pleased with itself," he said.

"It has an easy job."

She lay down beside him. The house still had voices in it: someone on the stairs, a laugh below, Rimuru coming to the closet before going up to his loft. None of it sounded like an assembly waiting for Mau to do something terrible.

Frieren took his hand and looked at the healing fingers.

"You don't need a present from this one."

"Good. I've already packed the pan."

"If there is a book, you may bring it."

"I thought I didn't need a present."

"I can be reasonable about different circumstances."

He laughed. She stayed close enough to feel it.

They spoke for a while about something she wanted to try with the bread spell. Not a battle plan, not a second goodbye. When she began to lose the end of a sentence, Mau drew the blanket up over her shoulder and waited until her breathing settled.
