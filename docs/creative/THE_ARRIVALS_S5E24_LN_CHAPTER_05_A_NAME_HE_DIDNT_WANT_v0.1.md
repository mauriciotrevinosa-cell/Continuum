# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 28
## Chapter 72 — A Name He Didn't Want

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 28
**Reading order:** 72
**Continuity state:** Mau privately wants greater strength because enemies can recover and evolve too; it is a fearful ambition, not a title achieved or a secret plan to replace his family

*When: Day 287, later that night.*

Mau was almost asleep when he remembered Sukuna's voice.

Not the threat. That would have been easier to put away with the rest of the night.

The pleasure.

Sukuna had been delighted by the thought of a higher ceiling. Mau had heard it beneath the insults, beneath the courtesy neither of them believed in. A man who had nearly died, looking at the thing that had almost killed him and finding a reason to look forward to tomorrow.

There would be other people looking up too.

The new page under Mau's thoughts was not the only place in the world where something could be recovering.

Frieren shifted against him. Her forehead found the familiar place below his chin. He put his hand over hers before the thought could become a place he went alone.

He liked tomorrow.

The game Yuka would accuse him of losing incorrectly. Ori asking to see the results. The book Frieren had decided not to expect. Gojo being impossible about a lesson. People at the table who could spend an afternoon choosing cloth because somebody else had taken a share of the work.

He wanted more of it.

That desire was uncomplicated until he tried to imagine what would be enough to keep it.

The strongest.

He disliked the words the moment they formed. They sounded lonely. Like a position everyone else had to look at from somewhere behind it. Gojo had already shown him what that could cost, even without telling him all of it.

Mau did not want to become a wall that forgot how to sit at the table.

He still wanted to know that, if the things outside grew worse, the answer would not always have to be somebody he loved giving up the next ordinary day.

There was no plan in that yet. No promised victory. Only a frightened, stubborn thought he had not decided how to say aloud.

*You are still awake,* Ciel said gently.

"I know."

The word barely left his mouth. Frieren stirred, and he stroked her hair until she settled again.

*Do you need something?*

He considered the unfinished page, the entrance waiting in the clearing, the unfamiliar reserve beneath it all.

"Not tonight."

He did not open the page.

For a little while he listened to the house. Then, with Frieren's hand still under his, he went to sleep.
