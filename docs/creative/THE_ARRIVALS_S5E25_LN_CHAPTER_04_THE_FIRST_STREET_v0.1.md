# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 31
## Chapter 4 — The First Street

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 31
**Reading order:** 4
**Continuity state:** The party clears one nearby sentry post with an open retreat; Gojo uses only current small Blue and Infinity, while Mau's objective reward affects his acquired branch alone

*When: Day 288, the first floor.*

The sentry had no face behind its visor.

That was the first useful thing Mau learned by waiting. A turn of the plated head showed a space where firelight passed straight through. Below it, the armour moved as if there were shoulders inside, keeping a long hooked blade clear of the ground.

Two smaller figures stood beneath the ruined signal. One held a broad shield. The other's empty hands opened and closed around something Mau could not see.

"Three," Kaneki said.

"That we can see," Yuta added.

Gojo leaned a little farther to look, then stepped back beside them.

"I'm not seeing an answer to that which I'd call all clear."

He turned one hand over, flexing the fingers as if checking the fit of a glove. Mau knew the small movement well enough to ask.

"Different?"

"Less resistance in the place. Same person trying to work in it."

He made a tiny point of attraction above the tiled floor. Dust shifted toward it, then settled when he closed the operation.

"Still small," he said. "And I'm not finding the rest of my old bag under the welcome mat."

Yuta looked down the street once more. The sentries had not advanced beyond the broad post marked on Mau's map.

"We can look from the corner," he said. "Keep the vestibule behind us. If there's another approach we haven't seen, back inside."

He asked Mau whether that was still the visit he wanted.

Mau looked at the doorway they had already used to return. Then at the broken curb between it and the corner.

"The post. Not the next floor."

They went out in pairs, close enough to hear one another without shouting. Heat touched Mau's face. The buildings burned without the wind carrying flames from one to the next; he could not tell whether that was a rule of the place or simply something he had not watched for long enough.

Kaneki stopped him before the corner.

"Above."

A metal edge trembled against brick. One of the smaller figures had climbed onto the low wall without making the footsteps Mau had expected. Its fingers opened.

A net flashed across the street.

"Back," Yuta said.

Mau moved. The net struck the empty space where he had been, caught the curb and pulled tight. The larger sentry advanced into the opening its companion had made.

There was room to retreat. Mau saw it. Yuta was keeping it there deliberately.

The hook came down.

Gojo stepped into its reach, and the blade slowed before his sleeve. The sentry pulled against the distance, committing its weight to an edge that would not finish arriving.

"Now would be quite good," Gojo said.

Cursed energy tightened along Yuta's blade. He cut across the exposed knee joint. The armour tilted. Kaneki caught the haft from the side, outside the hooked edge, and dragged it down far enough to stop the thing turning toward Yuta's back.

Yuta changed his grip and cut through the joint below the empty visor. The head came free. The plates folded inward, losing the force that had held them around the darkness. Kaneki kept the haft pinned until the hooked end had fallen clear of Yuta's legs.

The shield carrier charged the gap between them.

Mau saw the net line under his heel before he saw where the shield was going. He caught Kaneki's sleeve and pulled once.

"Line. Left."

Kaneki changed his step. The charge went past his hip, and a small Blue took hold behind the shield instead of trying to crush it head-on.

For an instant the figure had no agreement between the direction of its feet and the direction of its chest.

Yuta used the instant.

The shield hit the paving. The empty armour followed it in pieces.

Up on the wall, the third sentry drew its hand back for another cast. Mau ducked behind the broken corner as the new line hissed past his face. It had been aimed where he was going to stand if he came straight back out.

He stayed down.

"It watches the corner," he called.

Yuta changed sides. Kaneki released the falling haft and moved with him. Gojo stepped into view first, drew the cast, and let the line find the same impossible distance as the blade.

Yuta reached the wall before the net could be withdrawn.

One cut through the hand. Another through the narrow joint below the empty helm.

The last sentry fell on the street side, away from the route they had kept clear.

Mau waited for another movement.

So did the others.

Only when Kaneki had listened and Gojo had looked again did Yuta lower his sword. The first guardian's pieces began to dissolve, leaving the hooked weapon behind for a moment before that too became light.

Mau's notice changed.

*Sentry post cleared. Route to Floor 2 opened.*

The objective reward followed. His level rose to four. Fifteen points waiting now, none of them spent. The notice belonged to the branch carrying the quest; it did not light up three matching levels over his companions' heads.

He looked at the hand that had caught Kaneki's sleeve. A small scrape stung across the knuckle. It remained there after the number changed.

"Any injuries?" Yuta asked.

Mau showed him.

Gojo was looking at his own hand with a much less theatrical interest than usual.

"The pull came more easily," he said. "Once. Before anyone writes a celebration."

Kaneki glanced toward the vestibule, then the now-empty post.

"We can write it outside."

They stayed on the first street.
