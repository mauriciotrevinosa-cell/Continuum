# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 5 — A Command He Already Knew

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 5
**Continuity state:** The interface initially rejects a shadow function Mau already possesses; Noise binds the existing operation to the new branch without granting a class, unlimited reserve or the rest of the System

*When: Day 288, after the sentry post.*

The smaller sentries had vanished completely.

Under the first guardian, a darker outline remained between the broken plates. Mau knew the depth this time. He stopped looking for a face in the visor and watched what the light was failing to reach.

The notice offered extraction.

Then crossed the offer out.

*Unavailable to current class.*

Mau read it twice.

"That's annoying."

"A deeply specialized report," Gojo said.

Mau showed them the line. He had already called the dragon. It was resting in the separate part of his shadow, not waiting to be invented because this page had finally found a name for the command.

"So why does it think I can't?"

*The offered control and the operation you possess aren't fully connected,* Ciel said. *That is what I can distinguish. It isn't evidence the dragon is gone.*

He repeated her answer. Yuta looked at the outline under the armour.

"Do you need this one today?"

Mau checked the time Ciel had counted since entry. They had room before the return limit. Not room to forget there was one.

"I'd like to understand why a thing I can do has become a thing a page says I can't. A few minutes. If we can't find it, we leave it."

Yuta put his sword away far enough that he would not stand over Mau with an answer already drawn. Kaneki stayed where he could watch the street; Gojo took the other corner.

Mau crouched, keeping the scraped hand clear of the paving.

He thought about the old doorway in his shadow, Ear's place beside it, the new depth that had answered the dragon. All of them appeared to be in the same direction when somebody looked down at his feet. Inside, they did not lead to the same thing.

The page was showing him one of them and behaving as though its missing connections described the whole body.

"Can we use what we already have?" he asked inwardly. "Without opening the other places to make the label happy."

The Noise became attentive.

He waited rather than supplying the word himself. The last time it had recognized a command faster than either of them understood its consequences, the fountain had disappeared beneath his feet.

Here there was time to notice the work. A pressure following the existing line, stopping where the offered field failed to recognize it, coming back to compare again.

Nothing moved in his arms.

*The shadow operation is still separate,* Ciel said. *If the other boundaries change, we stop.*

Mau repeated that. Then waited through an uncomfortable minute in which Gojo made no jokes and the fallen plates continued to lose their edges.

A short part of the offered field changed.

Not a new class. Not a title.

The existing command appeared where the rejection had been. Its companion storage operation answered the same shallow reserve he had used before.

"Just that part?" Mau asked.

The rest of the page stayed as it was.

He let out his breath.

"All right."

He stood where the guardian's reach had ended and called it.

"Arise."

The outline lifted less dramatically than the dragon had. A shoulder first, then an angular helm and the shape of the long weapon, all of it drawing together from a depth too small to contain it.

Mau kept the intention steady. He did not need to be angrier than the thing had been. He needed to know what he was asking it to follow.

The sentry completed itself and lowered its weapon.

Two registered shapes now. Ten places still, fifteen points still, a little less in the reserve that was maintaining the new arrival. The page did not buy him a deeper supply for having won an argument about its wording.

Mau asked the sentry to turn toward the empty post.

It turned.

He called it back almost immediately, before the curiosity could become another series of tests. The drain stopped when the shape folded into storage. Nothing replenished the amount already used.

Yuta came beside him.

"Enough?"

Mau looked toward the newly opened passage. Farther down, something was making a slow, deliberate sound against stone. Floor two had begun existing before he was ready to see it.

"Enough."

Kaneki picked up the little bundle of crystals the post had left. Gojo took the cord that had come loose from Yuta's pack. They went back along the street, with no one pretending the new passage had stopped being interesting.

At the vestibule, Mau turned once more to look.

The burning buildings had not changed their minds about him leaving.

He went through the return frame.
