# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 31
## Chapter 10 — A Lamp With A Price

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 31
**Reading order:** 10
**Continuity state:** The found lantern is registered as an eligible ordinary utility item; stock costs crystals and charges remain consumable, while Frieren's own outing receives attention at home

*When: Day 289, afternoon.*

Mau knew she had not found a book before she put the parcel down.

He was willing to admire the lantern anyway.

Frieren took the cloth off with a little too much precision and set it in front of him. Fern put the smaller bundle of crystals beside it. Stark left the route board where Anko could find it and went to wash his hands.

"That is a very fine lantern," Mau said.

"It isn't a grimoire."

"I was going to begin with what it is."

"We checked."

He looked up. Fern's mouth was moving at one corner.

"Twice," she said.

Frieren sat beside him and turned the control. Warm light gathered under the housing. Mau bent to look at the base without putting his fingers against anything that might have opinions about being touched.

The fitted charge had a small used section. Fern had wrapped a separate spent fragment to show Senku how the casing held it. They had not pushed a raw crystal into a household lamp and declared the problem of power solved.

Anko came over after checking the route.

"The shop has the registration field now. Want to see whether it accepts this?"

Frieren looked at the lantern with renewed interest.

"Does that give us another one?"

"It offers another one at a price, if it's eligible."

"Less interesting."

"Still useful," Fern said.

They carried it to the screened clearing. Both entrances stood where they had been left, the ordinary blue and Mau's separate darker frame. Nothing had moved because a new item was being shown to the shop.

The utility field recognized the housing. A price appeared for an empty lantern, with the compatible fitted charges listed separately. Not the rest of the case's loot. Not a recipe for copying anything Frieren put in front of it.

Fern read the cost, compared the charge option, and asked Anko to enter it in the ordinary stock list for the next spending discussion.

She did not have to buy one to prove the new page worked.

They brought the found one home.

Mau carried it while Frieren told him about the draught. He asked which way she had wanted to go. She pointed over his arm, and he nearly followed the gesture into a chair before realizing it described a corridor inside the gate.

"It isn't here," she said.

"I was following the expert."

She looked rather pleased with that and began again, this time using the table edge as the false wall. He listened until he could find the side opening without being told where to look.

Then she handed the account to Fern, because the dust had been her discovery.

They spent most of the afternoon on a trip Mau had not taken.

At dusk Frieren put the lantern beside her chair, outside the path around the shelf. She kept the light low enough for the first test, no brighter than the page required.

Mau lay on the bed and watched her decide which book ought to receive the benefit.

"That one?"

"I haven't chosen."

"You've opened it."

"An important part of choosing."

He left her to it. The small light stayed steady, spending its charge honestly.
