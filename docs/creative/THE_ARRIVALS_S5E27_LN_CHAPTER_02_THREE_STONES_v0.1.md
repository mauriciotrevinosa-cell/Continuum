# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 31
## Chapter 12 — Three Stones

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 31
**Reading order:** 12
**Continuity state:** Shoko has her own plans; Mau and Gojo share a short attention-and-footwork practice without techniques, XP or restored kit

*When: Day 290, after breakfast.*

Shoko's first question was how he had slept.

Mau sat on the edge of the examination bed and thought about the trapped hand, the ribbon, the long interval between hearing Ori and finally getting up.

"Pretty well."

She looked at him, then wrote it down.

"That sounded almost unqualified."

"I can try again."

"Don't."

She took his wrist. Her fingers were cool, her attention exact. The window stood open enough to let the air move through the clinic. On the desk, a page had been turned face down under a cup.

"Any dizziness?"

"No."

"Pain?"

"The scrape is better. Nothing new."

"Anything you're planning to call insignificant until I ask the same question a different way?"

He considered this.

"Gojo's waiting outside."

"Unfortunately, that's chronic."

Mau laughed. Shoko kept her fingers on his wrist until he had finished.

She checked the things she always checked, asked him to follow her finger, and made him repeat an answer when he gave it too quickly. When she was satisfied, she let go.

"The short practice you agreed on. Not the short practice Satoru will describe afterward."

"I heard that," Gojo called from outside.

"Good. Saves me explaining twice."

Mau pulled his sleeve down. "What are you doing after this?"

"Finishing these. Then going out."

"Where?"

"I haven't decided. That's part of the appeal."

He smiled. "Sounds nice."

"I thought so."

She reached past him for the next sheet on the desk. He took that for the dismissal it was and got off the bed.

At the door he almost collided with Gojo, who had been leaning on the frame.

"How am I?" Gojo asked Shoko.

"In the way."

"Excellent. Stable condition."

Mau followed him out before he could ask for a second opinion.

* * *

Gojo put three small stones on the ground.

"That's the whole lesson?"

"Do you want more stones?"

"I'm trying to understand the budget."

"Walk through. Don't look down."

Mau looked at the stones, then at him.

Gojo smiled.

"You already did. Start again."

They worked in the shade, far enough from the door that nobody needed to duck around them. No techniques. No attempt to force his body into something it was not ready to hold. Gojo changed where he stood, lifted a hand, asked Mau a question at exactly the point where the answer made him forget his feet.

The second stone caught his heel.

"Again," said Gojo.

"You moved it."

"I moved me. You followed."

Mau went back. This time he kept his eyes up and shortened the step without thinking about it.

Gojo tapped his shoulder as he passed.

"Better."

After a while the three stones became an absurdly difficult arrangement for three stones. Mau stopped trying to anticipate what Gojo wanted and began to notice what he was actually doing. The small shift of weight. The shoulder that lied. The foot that didn't.

He got through once.

"There," Gojo said.

Mau turned, pleased, and kicked the last stone on his way back.

Gojo bent over laughing.

"You could've waited," Mau said.

"I really couldn't."

They sat down when the agreed time was up. Gojo sat first, which made it easier for Mau to follow without either of them having to announce anything about stopping.

For a few minutes they watched a procession of people going about things neither of them was responsible for. Coco passed with Tetia and a sheet she was protecting from the wind. Qifrey, coming after them, told them to walk rather than run. They slowed until they were out of sight.

Gojo held out his water.

Mau took a drink and passed it back.

"Doing anything later?" he asked.

"Several extremely impressive things."

"Such as?"

Gojo watched the doorway to the clinic. "Undecided."

Mau looked at him.

"Don't," Gojo said.

"I didn't say anything."

"You have an apprentice face. It's insufferable."

Mau smiled into his own shoulder.

They stayed until Gojo had finished the water.
