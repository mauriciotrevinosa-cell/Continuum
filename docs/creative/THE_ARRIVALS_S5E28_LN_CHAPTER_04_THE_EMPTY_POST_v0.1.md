# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 29
## Chapter 19 — The Empty Post

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S5 v0.1
**Volume:** 29
**Reading order:** 19
**Continuity state:** Mau registers Yuka, Yuta and Milim for a new limited expedition; the cleared first street persists, and the second floor must be reached on foot

*When: Day 291, morning; the second tower visit.*

Yuka stopped beside the place where the first guardian had fallen.

"Here?"

Mau nodded. The post was empty. There were no crystals waiting to be collected again, no neatly reassembled enemies. The buildings still burned above them, but the net that had scraped his hand was gone.

He showed her the corner where he had ducked.

She looked from it to the low wall.

"That was a terrible place to stand."

"I learned that."

"Efficiently," Yuta said.

Mau turned toward him. Yuta looked almost apologetic, which did not stop Milim laughing.

They had agreed on two hours outside, including the walk back. Anko had watched them cross after the four names appeared, a new expedition rather than Yuka and Milim borrowing the permission of the previous party. Rimuru was staying home. Frieren had asked Mau to bring back something about the actual rooms, rather than four different accounts of how tall a monster had been.

He had brought paper.

This was already proving optimistic. The first turn he drew made Yuka lean over his shoulder.

"We went left."

"I know."

"Your left is going the other way."

He turned the page around.

"Now the entrance is upside down," she said.

Yuta held out his hand. Mau gave him the pencil.

The passage beyond the post descended through a building with its doors burned away. At the bottom was another opening, marked with a pale two. No return frame stood beside it. Behind them lay the first street and the vestibule; they would have to walk back through both.

Mau asked whether everyone still wanted to go farther.

Milim leaned around Yuta, inspecting the darkness. "Yes."

"You haven't seen it yet."

"That's why."

Yuka put her hand against the wall and listened. Then she asked for room.

The white dog came up beside her, shook herself once, and put her nose near the bottom of the opening. Yuka waited until the ears turned forward before sending her a little way in.

Not racing out of sight. To the first doorway and back.

"Nothing moving there," Yuka said when she returned. "She doesn't like something farther down."

Milim crouched to meet the dog's eyes.

"What is it?"

The dog looked past her toward Yuka.

"You could tell both of us," Milim said.

Yuka smiled, but kept her attention on the doorway. "I'll tell you when I know."

They crossed together.

The air changed before the street did. Less smoke, more cold stone. What had looked like the side of a building opened into a covered market, stalls stripped to metal frames and a broad lane running between them. Pale hooks hung under the roof.

Something struck stone at the far end.

Then again.

The sound Mau had heard before leaving three days ago.

A heavy shape dragged a length of chain across the lane. Two smaller shapes moved among the empty stalls, each stopping when the chain stopped.

The dog pressed against Yuka's leg.

Yuta raised his hand, keeping them inside the entrance's shadow.

"Let's see where it goes."

Milim had already shifted onto the front of her feet. She looked at his hand, then deliberately put her heels down.

They waited through one complete circuit.

The chain carrier walked the middle. The smaller figures changed sides through gaps too narrow for its shoulders. On the second circuit one of them climbed a stall frame, making the hooks tremble overhead.

Mau looked up.

A second chain ran through them, disappearing toward the carrier's back.

"That one isn't decoration," he said.

Yuta followed the line. His expression lost the small amusement it had kept through the argument about the map.

"No."

Milim tipped her head, tracing it too.

"So I shouldn't pull it?"

"Please don't find out what it brings down on us," Yuka said.

Milim considered this a more useful answer than *be careful*. She pointed to the empty crossing behind the carrier.

"Then I can make it look there. You take the little ones."

Yuta asked her to show him where she intended to stand.

She did.

Mau watched her change the position after Yuka pointed out a low hook near her shoulder. Then he checked the passage behind them once more and folded the map where he could reach it.
