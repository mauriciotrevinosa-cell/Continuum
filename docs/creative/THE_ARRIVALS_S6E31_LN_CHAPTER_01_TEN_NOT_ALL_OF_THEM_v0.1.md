# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 33
## Chapter 43 — Ten, Not All of Them

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-22)
**Volume:** 33
**Reading order:** 43
**Source episode:** S6E31
**Continuity state:** Day453, afternoon. After the capability review and small shared-pull tests, Mau chooses to spend ten of the67 held points: six Endurance, four Agility. Umaru records the increments without inventing the prior total of either field;57 remain. Shoko and Gojo compare ordinary movement and monitor the interval afterward. No fever, memory restoration or healing of residual damage occurs. Later, with no loan active, Mau checks one small use of his own Red against an isolated target. No shared Red, Purple, new technique, numerical recovery percentage or automatic full kit follows.

*When: Day453, afternoon. The clear ground beyond the workshops. Shoko has brought her watch, Gojo has brought an apple, and Umaru has brought enough paper for both of them.*

[U] "Ten."

She put the page flat on the board so Mau could read it without anybody leaning over his shoulder.

[U] "Not ten now and the rest while I go to find more paper. Not ten in each place. Ten."

[M] "I understood it before the examples."

[U] "The examples are for the record."

Shoko sat down on the low wall beside them and took the watch out of her pocket. She had finished in the clinic before coming out. Nobody had been sent running to fetch her.

[Sh] "Do you want to do it?"

Mau looked at the page. At the two entries Umaru had discussed with him that morning, and the number left at the bottom if they used both.

[M] "Yes. That much. I don't want to leave everything waiting because the last change was difficult."

[Sh] "Good. Then we find out what this change does. We don't make it do something to justify coming outside."

Gojo bit into the apple.

[G] "I came outside for the apple."

[Sh] "Then you're already ahead."

Umaru tapped the six with the blunt end of her pencil.

[U] "Tell her why these. In your words."

Mau looked from the page to the low pole on the ground.

[M] "I keep finding out I can hold a thing longer than I can comfortably stand there holding it. And I notice where something's going before I've got my feet back under me. Not always. Enough that I notice afterward."

Gojo lowered the apple.

[G] "So you want the body closer to the part doing the noticing."

[M] "A little closer. I don't want to put everything into making a bigger pull and still trip on the way over to use it."

[Sh] "That doesn't mean six and four will solve it."

[M] "I know. It means we can see what ten does before I decide about the others."

Umaru turned the page so Shoko could read the notes underneath. The practice at the trestles. The two short uses at the bridge. A line for the last survey, with no fever beside it and a large question where the third route stayed closed.

[U] "Those are different things. We haven't added them together into a score."

Shoko read them. Then gave the page back.

[Sh] "All right. Show me the walking."

* * *

First they walked.

It was not an impressive test. That was what made it irritating. Mau went from the wall to the old stump and back, turned twice round the marked patch and stepped over the low pole Gojo had laid on two short blocks.

The pole was not high enough to jump.

He jumped it anyway the first time.

[G] "Over. Not over there."

Mau stopped on the far side.

[M] "You said over."

[G] "I also pointed at it with my hand. You watched."

Shoko made him do the same route again at a pace they could repeat. He did not have to prove he could run. Everyone there had seen him run. They wanted a movement small enough that he could notice a difference without falling over something at speed.

On the third circuit he stopped laughing about the pole and paid attention to where his weight landed.

Umaru wrote the time down. Not a score. A time.

Shoko asked how his head felt, then his chest, then whether the left eye had changed since morning.

[M] "No."

[Sh] "Hand?"

He opened it and closed it as far as it went.

[M] "Same."

She nodded. Did not make him try harder.

* * *

The window looked ordinary.

That surprised him a little. The last great change had arrived before anyone was ready for it, filling the room faster than his body could hold it. This time he had sat with the words for days, looked at them with other people, chosen two of them and brought the page out into the afternoon.

He still wanted to check each one before touching it.

[M] *These fields. They aren't moving while I do this?*

[C] *No new change that I can detect. Six in the first. Four in the second. I'll watch what follows, too.*

He selected the two entries for Umaru to see through the bracelet. She checked the increments on her sheet against the ones he meant to apply.

[U] "Six Endurance. Four Agility."

[M] "Yes."

[G] "And nothing else because it turns out you've got another fifty-seven sitting there."

Mau looked at him.

[M] "I was going to say that."

[G] "I got there first. That's agility."

Umaru put a line beneath the two entries.

Mau made the first change.

* * *

He felt six go into Endurance.

Not six little bolts of something. Not a wound closing. A settling through the body he already had, faint enough that he would have missed part of it if he had been watching for light.

He waited.

Ciel waited with him. Shoko did not ask a question until he looked up.

[Sh] "Anything wrong?"

[M] "No. Different. Not wrong."

[Sh] "Different where?"

He thought before answering. He could feel that she wanted his words, not a reassurance he had prepared for her.

[M] "Here. Under my ribs. It's not fuller. More like I can hold the breath without the rest of me wanting to move."

She wrote down *he says* before the description.

Then four into Agility.

His right foot moved before he meant it to. Not far. The heel came in a little, answering a small shift in his balance more readily than he had expected. He looked down at it and corrected the step.

Gojo had stopped eating.

[G] "Slowly when you stand. Don't let the first easy movement choose the second."

Mau stood slowly.

The ground was still in the right place.

* * *

Fifty-seven remained.

Umaru checked the number, checked the two changes and wrote *spent10* beside the date. Not the whole amount ever put into either field. They had not recovered every detail of the gate's spending just by making a better page.

Then she closed her book.

[U] "That's it."

[M] "That is it."

She stayed beside him as he walked back to the wall, not helping, watching whether she needed to. She did not.

They sat for a while. A little breeze moved the dust on the practice ground. Gojo finished the apple. Shoko asked him not to throw the core at the target before they had used the target.

[G] "It would be a useful baseline."

[Sh] "It would be an apple core."

He put it in the little paper she gave him.

Mau looked into the ordinary distance between the wall and the stump. The edges through his left eye were still a little softened. The stiff fingers were still stiff when he spread his hand on his knee.

He could feel the change without finding a cure in it.

[M] "Ready?"

Shoko looked at the watch. Then at him.

[Sh] "Walk first."

* * *

He nearly took the pole with his shin.

Not because he could not step over it. Because he started the turn a little sooner and gave the foot less room than before. The wood rolled on its short blocks, tapped his boot and stopped.

Gojo went to put it back.

[G] "There. That's useful."

[M] "For you?"

[G] "For the pole. It's discovered a weakness."

Mau went through the route again. No jump. No burst of the rough speed pattern behind the walls. Just the same walking pace, the same turn and the low pole in its proper place.

This time he gave his foot the space it needed.

The movement felt easier. Not faster by any number Umaru could responsibly put on the page. Easier to gather again after the turn. Easier to stop with his weight where he meant it to be.

At the wall his breathing was quieter than he had expected.

He told Shoko that. She put it beside the first description instead of drawing a conclusion that would have taken them a week to test.

[Sh] "We'll look again tomorrow. Before you do anything clever."

[M] "Can I do something small and clever today?"

Gojo turned to him.

[G] "How small?"

* * *

The target stood by itself.

A thick wooden offcut on the bare patch beyond the stump, with nobody behind it and nothing fragile on either side. Gojo moved it twice until he was satisfied with the line. Umaru put the papers farther back. Shoko stayed beside her with the watch in her hand.

Mau had closed the loan route before they started. Yuka was elsewhere. There was no second outlet to balance or person whose demand he could confuse with his own.

[C] *Your own small push,* Ciel said. *Not the one you can make when you stop caring what it costs. Not a combination.*

[M] *I know.*

He held his right hand low.

Red came as the familiar pressure turned outward. Small enough to gather without the tremor he remembered from holding more than he could comfortably keep. He did not keep making it bigger until it looked impressive. He put it where Gojo had shown him the line and let the little pulse go.

The offcut toppled backward.

It did not explode. It hit the earth, rolled once and lay still.

Mau lowered his hand.

[G] "Again?"

Mau felt for the answer in his body before looking at the target.

[M] "Not yet."

Gojo nodded.

[G] "Good. That was the question."

* * *

They let the answer stay small.

No second pulse because the first had worked. No other colour beside it. Ciel followed the little demand until it settled; Shoko checked his face and hands again and said they could put the equipment away.

Umaru opened the book a last time.

[U] "Ten spent. Fifty-seven held. No new problem observed this afternoon. Follow-up tomorrow."

[M] "You can write the pole got me."

[U] "I already did."

He laughed. She showed him the line. It had a small diagram of his foot beside it, which seemed excessive until Gojo leaned over and said it needed a larger arrow.

Mau went to get the offcut. The push had left a dent where the old knot in the wood had struck the ground. He turned that side inward so nobody would scrape a thumb on it while carrying it back.

At the practice wall, Shoko held out the wrapped apple core.

[Sh] "Yours."

Gojo put a hand on his chest.

[G] "I thought you'd saved it for me."

[Sh] "I did. You forgot to take it."

He accepted it. Umaru put the watch time beside the final entry. Mau took the pole and the two blocks, and they went back toward the workshops before the light left the clear ground.
