# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 33
## Chapter 55 — The Wrong Voice

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 33
**Reading order:** 55
**Source episode:** S6E37
**Continuity state:** Day459 after dinner, the regular roof night one week after Day452. Mau, Rimuru, Yuta, Gojo and Taihei drift into an ordinary game of household impressions. No techniques or shape changes are used. Rimuru retains private knowledge and unresolved anger; the others acquire no romantic knowledge. Participation and recognition concern present household habits, not a test or restoration of Mau's autobiographical memory. Crackers take place concurrently elsewhere.

*When: Day459, night. The safe, flatter part of the roof, reached through the east-corridor hatch. Five cups on a cloth, blankets round shoulders, and Gojo saying something he has apparently said too often downstairs.*

[G] "I was only helping."

Yuta put down his cup.

[Yt] "That's the problem."

[G] "Not usually the response to help."

[T] "It was the response when you put the spoons in a different drawer."

Gojo looked at Taihei, who was handing the last cup to Rimuru and seemed entirely unaware that he had taken a side.

[G] "They were nearer the bowls."

[T] "The bowls move."

[G] "So do the spoons. Apparently."

Mau was still settling the blanket around his legs. He looked from Gojo to the cloth beneath the cups and back again.

[M] "Did you tell anybody?"

[G] "I thought they'd find them."

[R] "They found you first."

Yuta laughed into his cup. Gojo leaned back on his hands and looked up at the stars, appealing to a less prejudiced audience.

* * *

Taihei demonstrated Momo's face.

He did not mean to. He was explaining that she had come to the drawer, opened it, looked into the empty space and then looked very slowly round the kitchen. At that point he looked round the roof himself, with the same patient, dangerous expression, and the other four began laughing.

Taihei stopped.

[T] "What?"

[Yt] "Do that again."

[T] "Open the drawer?"

[M] "The bit after."

He tried. It was less like her this time because he knew they were watching. His eyes went too wide. Rimuru shook his head.

[R] "Not surprised. Disappointed in the entire room."

Taihei lowered his eyebrows.

Yuta put his cup down before he spilled anything.

[G] "She didn't look like that."

[T] "You weren't there yet."

Gojo turned toward him.

[T] "You were the part after."

Mau had to take the blanket away from his mouth to breathe. Taihei looked pleased, then tried not to, which did not improve matters.

* * *

The game acquired a rule when Gojo tried to be Ram.

He sat very straight, folded his hands and looked at Yuta as though Yuta had arrived with an unacceptable explanation for existing.

[G] "Ram has seen better."

[Yt] "You can't say the name."

[G] "She says her name."

[Yt] "Then leave out that bit."

[G] "It's an important part."

Rimuru sat forward a little, pulling the blanket tighter around his shoulders.

[R] "Try without it. We might still manage."

Gojo looked at the four of them. He had wanted them watching; now that they were, he seemed to find the conditions unreasonably strict.

He began again.

[G] "Adequate. Barely."

There was a brief silence.

[T] "That's still her."

[Yt] "Or Maki, looking at something I did."

[M] "Or Agott."

Rimuru looked at him. Mau was smiling at Gojo, waiting to see whether the objection would be allowed. There was no question in his face about the people they had named. He knew the voices they heard now. That was enough for this game.

[R] "You need a gesture."

Gojo lifted one hand to the side of his head, considering.

[Yt] "Don't do a technique."

[G] "I wasn't going to."

[Yt] "I'm saying it before."

Gojo lowered the hand with dignity and selected a different victim.

* * *

His Wakana was unfairly good.

There was barely anything to it. He bent his head, looked at an invisible piece of work and drew one thumb very carefully along an invisible edge. Then he looked up at a point beside Taihei's shoulder.

[G] "It isn't quite finished."

Taihei answered immediately.

[T] "Wakana."

Gojo spread both hands.

[G] "Thank you."

[M] "You said it too loudly."

[G] "He was outdoors."

[Yt] "He'd say it to the wood first."

Gojo looked back at the imaginary edge and apologised to it.

Rimuru laughed. It came out abruptly, and he covered his mouth with the side of his hand as though that might stop the second laugh from following. It did not.

Gojo watched him for a moment, then returned to the imaginary object instead of making anything of it.

[G] "I'm sorry about the delay."

Taihei leaned forward until his forehead was nearly on his knees.

[T] "Stop."

[G] "Would you like the corner slightly rounder?"

[T] "Please. Stop."

Mau had set his cup down safely and was laughing with both hands over his face.

* * *

Yuta refused to take a turn until Gojo accused him of having no ear for character.

Then he put his own cup squarely in the centre of the cloth, sat back and looked at it with the solemn attention of somebody considering a very difficult question.

Nobody spoke.

He glanced toward the hatch, then at the cup again. He rubbed one thumb against the other and frowned a little.

Gojo opened his mouth.

Yuta held up a finger without looking at him.

Mau made a small, uncertain sound.

[M] "Is he doing someone?"

[R] "I think so."

Yuta leaned forward and moved the cup half an inch. He looked at it from the new angle.

Taihei's expression changed.

[T] "That's not—"

Yuta reached for the pot, stopped with his hand just above it and looked toward the hatch once more.

Rimuru turned to Taihei.

[R] "It's you."

[T] "I don't do that."

[Yt] "Someone might want another cup."

[T] "They might."

[Yt] "But someone might want the hot water downstairs."

Taihei looked at the pot with genuine concern, realised what he was doing and made a despairing noise.

Gojo applauded quietly.

[G] "Excellent. The tiny crisis."

[T] "You put the spoons in the wrong drawer."

[G] "And you've brought us back to the subject. Wonderful chairing."

* * *

Mau did Anko.

He did not announce it. He picked up the empty bowl from the cloth, held it at an angle and examined the bottom for a moment. Then he looked at Gojo.

[M] "Was it like that when it came in?"

Gojo's grin broadened.

[M] "No, don't tell me what you meant to do. Tell me which bit you broke."

Yuta pointed at him, delighted.

[Yt] "Anko."

Taihei nodded so vigorously that Mau broke the expression and had to start again. He turned the bowl, considered the unbroken rim and set it down with a little final tap.

[M] "Right. I'm writing that differently."

Even Gojo could not maintain the face of an innocent man after that.

Rimuru watched the bowl come back to the cloth. Mau had heard Anko use that tone three times this week.

He reached for the bowl and tried the expression himself.

Mau looked at him.

[R] "Who's paying for the other one?"

They both laughed, then Taihei took the bowl away before it could become the evening's main casualty.

* * *

After that the guesses got worse.

Gojo's version of Kaneki was apparently a man holding a cup and looking into the distance. Yuta pointed out that everyone on the roof had been doing that for most of the evening. Gojo added a troubled examination of the contents.

[G] "I made it badly on purpose."

[T] "Now you're giving the answer."

[G] "You asked for clarity."

Yuta did not believe that impersonation was how Maomao folded her arms. Rimuru could hear the difference but could not explain it without standing up, which he refused to do in the cold. Taihei tried again to reproduce Momo's look and got it exactly right when Gojo reached across his cup for the last warm water.

Everyone stopped him at once.

Taihei held the expression while Gojo withdrew his hand.

[T] "That?"

[M] "That."

They were quieter after the laughing. Someone below shut a door. The hatch creaked when the wind reached it, then settled. The cups were empty now, and the cold had got into the corner of the cloth where the pot stood.

Yuta moved his legs and asked whether anybody could still feel their toes.

[R] "Some of them."

[G] "A majority?"

[Yt] "Enough."

They began gathering the cups. Mau folded the blanket which had slipped from Rimuru's shoulder and held it while he freed the corner trapped beneath him. Rimuru took it without making Mau ask what he was allowed to carry.

For most of the evening Rimuru had been able to attend to the people on the roof instead of choosing each sentence for the secret it might reveal, and he was tired enough to be grateful for that much.

* * *

At the hatch, Gojo attempted one last impression.

He held the tray against his chest, regarded the narrow opening with concern and turned toward Taihei.

[G] "Your face is where the tray needs to go."

Taihei looked at him.

[T] "It is. Move."

Yuta started laughing halfway down the ladder, stopped so he could keep hold properly and called up for the cups.

Mau handed them through one at a time. Rimuru held the edge of the tray steady until Gojo had stopped trying to help with the same hand he was using to hold the hatch.

Taihei went last. Before climbing down, he looked at the cleared roof, the folded cloth and the safe empty place where the pot had been.

[T] "We should have brought more hot water."

From below came four voices telling him not to go back for it.
