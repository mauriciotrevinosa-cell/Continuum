# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 33
## Chapter 59 — The Other Angle

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 33
**Reading order:** 59
**Source episode:** S6E39
**Continuity state:** Day463, morning. In a clear practice area, Mau and Yuka test a small sequence using her loaned Blue and his own Red under Gojo's and Ciel's supervision. The two actions redirect a moving target from different angles, with Blue fully released before Red is formed; the phenomena never meet. Ciel caps their common permitted demand, allowing less margin because Red costs Mau more. This is controlled coordination, not a second free supply, shared Red, Purple, a new technique or a large output increase. They stop after a useful small result without a medical crisis.57 held points,14/40 named registrations and98 soldiers remain unchanged; hand and eye damage persist. No public romance is disclosed.

*When: Day463, morning. Beyond the workshops. A weighted sack hangs from the crossbeam, and Gojo is pushing it gently with one finger.*

[G] "That would have hit you."

[M] "I'm not there."

[G] "That's why we're using a sack."

The sack went past the first chalk mark, turned at the end of the rope and came back. Gojo caught it before it could make another journey and looked at the two clear lanes marked on the ground.

Yuka had drawn one straight. The other bent away toward a patch of soft earth, wide enough to leave room for the sack to swing without reaching a person, a window or anything they intended to keep.

Mau checked the knot at the top of the sack.

[M] "It'll hold."

[Yk] "I tied it."

[M] "I know. That's why I'm looking at the other end."

She looked up at the beam.

[Yk] "I tied that one too."

He lowered his hand.

* * *

The idea had been hers.

Not the colours. They had talked about those before, on the white plain, in a place where they could make something enormous because there was nobody else to stand beneath it. This was about the moment after a thing changed direction and before it found somewhere else to go.

Yesterday the plated creature had slipped off the lamp platform. Yuta had used the opening. Yuka had kept thinking about what would have happened if they had needed it farther from the path before he could go in.

Pull one way. Push another. No need to put the two together.

Gojo had listened, looked at Mau and said they would draw the paths first.

Now he stood beyond both marked lanes, where he could see the target and their hands without anybody having to fire past him.

[G] "Tell me where yours goes."

Yuka pointed to the first mark beside the sack's swing.

[Yk] "Here. Just off the line. To turn it."

[G] "And yours?"

Mau showed him the second place, farther along the new direction, with his open hand.

[M] "After she's let go. From this side."

[G] "Not with a Blue still sitting there because you've stopped looking at it. Gone. Then the other one. No bringing them close to save a step."

They both nodded.

* * *

Ciel checked the loan before they moved the sack.

The route was familiar now. Thin, directed, not a door through which everything he had could wander. Mau could feel the place where Yuka would reach for the little pull and the weight that letting her reach put across his own body.

[C] *A short use. Then close that output. Your own push needs more of the permitted margin than another small pull would.*

[M] *So not the two we did at the trestles, with one changed.*

[C] *No. The second shape costs you more. Don't treat the same size as the same demand.*

He told Yuka. She looked at the first mark again and moved it a little nearer the ordinary swing, so the target would need less help to turn.

[Yk] "I don't have to drag it all the way across."

[M] "Just start it."

[G] "And let the target finish moving while neither of you is doing anything. That's allowed."

Mau looked at him.

[M] "You sound as though that's aimed at somebody."

[G] "I am looking directly at you."

* * *

The first attempt went wrong in a very small way.

Gojo sent the sack through the straight lane. Yuka placed the little Blue beyond its side. The rope slanted, the sack began to turn, and she let the pull go where they had agreed.

It was moving sideways now.

Not far enough sideways.

Mau watched it pass the place where he had meant to put the push and did not use Red.

Gojo caught the sack on its return.

[Yk] "I stopped early."

[M] "A little."

[G] "Good that he didn't fire to make up for it."

[Yk] "I know where it went."

[G] "Then use that. You don't need to be cross with it. It's full of sand."

She looked at the sack. At the slightly different line in the earth where it had turned.

[Yk] "I wasn't cross with the sack."

[G] "Even better. It can't apologise."

* * *

They changed the first mark rather than making the pull larger.

Yuka put her boot where she had stood, checked the angle from there and drew the new centre with the blunt end of the chalk. Mau walked to his own place, keeping the clear space between them.

The second turn was better.

Blue took the sack as it crossed the mark. Not enough to stop its motion, just enough to change where the motion went. Yuka lowered her hand as she let the little centre collapse.

[Yk] "Clear."

[C] *Clear,* Ciel confirmed inside Mau.

He gathered the small outward pressure he had tested alone. Red, his own, not lent through the route to her.

The pulse caught the sack along its side.

It went away from the straight lane. Too high. The rope pulled taut with a hard little sound, and the sack swung above the soft patch instead of toward it.

Mau lowered his hand at once.

Gojo caught the rope where it was safe to catch it and brought the weight down without standing in front of it.

For a moment they all looked at the sack.

[M] "Too much."

[G] "Wrong angle first. We can talk about how much after the direction stops being wrong."

Yuka looked at the place where she had put the pull.

[Yk] "Mine was gone? All of it?"

[G] "Yes. You closed it cleanly."

She glanced at Mau. He waited for Ciel's answer before nodding.

[M] "She says the same. The route stopped carrying it. That part worked."

Yuka let out a breath, not a frightened one. The breath of someone finding the mistake on the right side of a page instead of trying to correct every line at once.

[Yk] "All right. Then don't move my mark yet."

Gojo looked at the two chalk centres.

[G] "I wasn't going to. You gave him the turn he asked for."

She left the mark where it was and went to get the water.

* * *

They rested while Gojo reset the rope.

Mau sat on the stump with his right hand open on his knee. The left fingers had stiffened while he watched the two routes and kept his balance. He loosened what would loosen and left the others alone.

Yuka handed him the water. Drank after he passed it back.

[Yk] "You didn't aim there."

[M] "I thought lower. My hand didn't do lower."

[Yk] "You were looking at the sack instead of the bit you wanted to hit."

He looked up at her.

[Yk] "You do that with the sword too. If the whole thing matters, you try to look at all of it."

Gojo had been checking the knot at the beam. He came down and looked at Mau's open hand.

[G] "She's right. You can see where it's going without asking the push to go everywhere you're seeing."

Mau made the movement once with nothing in it. Wrist lower. The line out across the ground instead of up toward the turning weight.

He stopped before making it a shape.

[M] "That?"

[G] "That direction. Now leave it there while you drink."

* * *

They did not start the third attempt until the small pressure behind Mau's eyes had settled.

It had not been pain. He told Ciel that before she asked. It had also not been nothing, and there was no reason to make the next trial carry the last one before it began.

Yuka held her own hand low while Gojo sent the sack along the first lane.

The little pull came. The target turned. She let the centre go.

[Yk] "Clear."

The sack kept moving through the ordinary air between the two marks.

Mau let it.

Then the small push, lower, from the side. One outward pulse. No blue waiting in front of it, no attempt to hold the red place after the work was done.

The weighted sack went past the second mark and swung over the soft ground, low enough this time that its bottom brushed the earth before the rope carried it back.

Mau closed his hand on nothing.

Yuka looked at the new track in the dirt.

Then at him.

[Yk] "There."

He grinned.

* * *

Gojo did not catch the sack immediately.

He let it lose the rest of its swing by itself, staying clear while it passed the first mark in a new direction and came to a slower turn. Then he stopped it and put a hand against the rope to keep it still.

[G] "Tell me what worked."

Yuka pointed at the track.

[Yk] "I didn't keep pulling until it was all mine. I let it turn."

[M] "I waited for it to reach the new line. I didn't push at the place where she'd been pulling."

[G] "And?"

Mau looked at the point where the sack had brushed the dirt.

[M] "It went somewhere we had room for it."

Gojo nodded.

The result was small. It was also something they had chosen before it happened, instead of a direction they had been pleased to explain afterward.

[Yk] "Again?"

Mau felt for the answer before looking at her face.

[M] "Not now."

She looked at him properly. At his breathing, his hand resting open again, the way he had not started gathering anything for the next attempt.

[Yk] "All right."

* * *

They took the sack down together.

Yuka held the weight while Mau loosened the lower knot with his right hand. Gojo reached the beam and took care of the rope above it. There was no need to send up a shadow or pull anything through the yard when three people could carry the equipment back.

[C] *The sequence stayed within what we agreed,* Ciel told Mau. *Not because the two shapes became cheaper. You used less time holding them and left space between the demands.*

He passed that on. Yuka nodded, remembering the empty moment when the sack had kept moving and neither of them had needed to own its whole path.

Gojo rolled the remaining rope over his arm.

[G] "You can put that in the report. Don't call it a new Purple."

[M] "I wasn't going to."

[G] "It's two people putting something where they meant it to go. Much more difficult to give a dramatic name."

[Yk] "Go left, then left again."

[M] "Those weren't both left."

[Yk] "They were from where I stood."

Gojo watched them carry the sack between them, disagreeing over the two chalk arrows they had just left on the ground.

He went back and scuffed the arrows away with his shoe before somebody tried to follow them to the workshop.
