# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 33
## Chapter 62 — The Place Beside Her

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 33
**Reading order:** 62
**Source episode:** S6E40
**Continuity state:** Day466 late morning, overlapping the beginning of Mau and Yuka's flight. The household responds immediately to Ori's distress. Frieren, Fern, Shoko and Umaru stay with her; Maki and Yuta are concerned for both people who left. Ciel relays Mau's location and request through Rimuru; no public access to her private knowledge. Milim regrets the intercepted punch and leaves to apologise with her hands down. No one in this scene discovers the kisses or romance; she has not yet reached Kaisel or seen anything there. Ori remains a person who can choose company and place, not an object to move out of the way.

*When: Day466, late morning. The long room, immediately after Mau and Yuka have gone into the courtyard. The sound of Kaisel's wings reaches the open door.*

Ori was still looking at the place where he had been.

Frieren's hand was still against her back. Ori turned into the support without seeming to see who had given it, caught the front of Frieren's clothes and held on.

[O] "I didn't finish."

[F] "I'm here."

[O] "I hadn't—"

The rest disappeared into a breath that would not come properly. Frieren brought her closer. There was no room to do it neatly; Ori's shoulder struck the edge of the chair, and Fern moved it before either of them had to think about it.

[Fe] "This way. Just a little."

Frieren sat on the bench with Ori beside her, still gripping her clothes. She kept one arm round her. Ori bent forward with the other hand pressed against her mouth.

Shoko was already on the other side.

[Sh] "Ori. Look at me for a moment."

Ori shook her head.

[Sh] "All right. You don't have to. Is anything hurting?"

She shook her head again, harder. Then she held up one hand as though the answer had changed while she was giving it.

Shoko took it lightly and turned it. There was no broken skin, only fingers clenched so tightly that Ori seemed to have forgotten how to open them.

[Sh] "Open it."

Ori tried. Her fingers opened halfway and closed again.

[Sh] "Don't force it. Leave it with me a second."

Umaru came back with the cloth Shoko had asked for. She had wet it so thoroughly that water ran from one corner onto her own wrist. Shoko took it without comment, folded the dripping end inside and laid it over Ori's hand.

Ori stared at it.

[O] "I hit him."

Frieren's arm tightened.

[O] "I didn't mean to start doing that. I was talking."

[F] "I heard you."

[O] "Then I couldn't stop."

Shoko kept the cloth steady while Ori tried to draw her hand away.

[Sh] "You can take it. I'm not holding you here."

Ori stopped pulling. After a moment she took the cloth herself and pressed it between both palms.

Across the room, Milim had not moved from the place Yuta had brought her back to.

There was nothing in front of her any more. No hand she could reach, no Infinity, no brother to shout at. She stood with both hands over her mouth, looking at the open doorway.

Yuta came back from the courtyard and stopped in her line of sight.

[Yt] "Milim. Your hand."

She looked at him. Then down, as if she had not noticed where her hands had gone.

She let them drop. One closed against the other.

[Mi] "It stopped."

[Yt] "I saw."

[Mi] "I didn't hit him."

Yuta did not move aside.

She looked past him at Ori. At the wet cloth between her hands. At Frieren's face, bent close to hers.

[Mi] "I was going to."

The words came out much smaller.

Yuta waited while she rubbed the fist against her other palm and opened it.

Maki had gone as far as the door. From there she could see the shadow passing over the courtyard, broad enough to cross the whole space and be gone again. She did not call Yuka's name. Her mouth opened, then closed as Kaisel rose beyond the angle of the roof.

Rimuru was beside her when she turned back.

[Mk] "Where are they going?"

He had been watching the doorway too. His attention shifted in the small, unmistakable way Maki had learned to recognise.

She stopped herself asking again.

Then he spoke.

[R] "Ciel has word from him. They're staying inside the protection. He says they're coming back. No search."

[Mk] "Both of them?"

[R] "Both of them. It's not an emergency."

She looked out once more.

[Mk] "It's something."

[R] "Yes."

Maki waited for him to say when. He had no time to give her.

Ori made a sound behind them. Maki turned back into the room.

Fern had brought water, but she did not try to put it into Ori's hands on top of the cloth. She set the cup on the table within reach, then moved the abandoned chair another few inches so nobody had to squeeze past.

[Fe] "They know where he is," she told Frieren quietly. "He's inside the boundary."

Frieren nodded.

Her eyes went to the door anyway.

The sound of wings had grown distant. She had watched him leave enough times to know how little a distance meant when he was on the other side of it. Her hand moved up Ori's back and stopped between her shoulders.

Ori felt it stop.

[O] "Are you going?"

[F] "No."

It came out at once.

Ori's face crumpled again. She leaned into Frieren so abruptly that Frieren had to put a hand on the bench to keep them both from slipping sideways.

Fern sat close enough for her knee to touch Frieren's. She did not reach between them or ask Ori to make room.

Frieren's free hand was on the edge of the bench. Fern put her own beside it, near enough that Frieren could take it without being asked.

For a while she did not.

Her eyes kept moving from Ori to the open door, then back. Fern could remember the book put down that morning, the unremarkable breakfast they had almost finished. She had not known until now how much she had been expecting from another unremarkable morning.

[Fe] "I'm staying too," she told Ori.

Ori nodded against Frieren's shoulder.

Only then did Frieren move her hand. Two fingers found Fern's, tightened once and stayed.

Umaru stayed in front of the table, squeezing the damp spot on her sleeve. She had not put her hood up. For once there was nothing she could make into a Ministry matter, no form she could bring that would give anybody a next step.

Ori looked at her.

[O] "He said sorry."

Umaru nodded.

[O] "I know he meant it."

Her voice broke on the last word. That seemed to make her angrier than anything else. She took a breath through her mouth and tried again.

[O] "I know."

[U] "You don't have to keep telling us."

Ori stared at her.

Umaru wiped her wet wrist against the other sleeve, which only spread the water.

[U] "I know you heard him."

She pulled the chair round and sat. Not opposite Ori like somebody waiting for an answer. Beside the table, where she could put the cup nearer if Ori wanted it.

For a while the only words were Shoko asking whether Ori could loosen her fingers a little more. Ori managed it. Nothing in the room was repaired by the small success. Shoko looked at her palm, gave the cloth back and stayed where she was.

At the doorway, Maki caught Yuta's sleeve.

He looked at her hand before her face. She had taken a stronger hold than she meant to.

[Mk] "Yuka was crying too."

[Yt] "I know."

[Mk] "She went with him."

[Yt] "I saw."

Maki let the sleeve go, annoyed with herself. He had seen everything she had. There was no reason to tell him in pieces as if the right order might explain it.

[Mk] "I want to know she's all right."

[Yt] "So do I."

He glanced toward the courtyard. The little crease between his brows had not gone away since Mau had spoken. Maki knew that face too. It meant he was holding too many people in the same thought and could not find a place to set any of them down.

[Mk] "And him."

Yuta looked back at her.

[Mk] "I want to know he's all right as well."

He nodded. She took his hand this time instead of his sleeve.

[Mk] "I didn't want him marched back in here."

[Yt] "I know."

[Mk] "But he could have said where. To us. Before he—"

She stopped, looking at the doorway again. At the ordinary distance he had crossed without walking through it.

Yuta had come out in time to see Yuka seated behind him, her arms round his middle. He had almost called for her to look back. By then Kaisel was lifting, and a shout would only have sent another frightened face after the two already leaving.

[Yt] "We needed more than that."

Maki nodded. Neither of them had found a better thing to do with their hands, so they kept holding on.

Rimuru remained near enough to hear, without turning their two sentences into a meeting. When Yuta looked at him, he answered the question he could answer.

[R] "I'll get word if that changes."

Maki's fingers tightened round Yuta's once. Then she let go and went to fetch a bowl for the dripping cloth.

Ori had not touched the water.

She had begun taking smaller breaths, but every time the long room fell quiet she could hear her own question again. She could hear how loud she had made it. The things she had said after, which everyone had already known and which had changed nothing when she said them herself.

[O] "You said I could ask."

Frieren looked down at her.

[F] "Yes."

[O] "I should have waited."

Frieren closed her eyes for a moment.

Fern's knee remained against hers.

[F] "Until when?"

Ori rubbed the cloth between her palms. She had no answer that did not mean another morning, another game, another week of listening for him to say her name the right way.

[O] "I don't know."

Frieren moved the hair off Ori's damp cheek. She had to do it twice because the first strand stuck to her fingers.

[F] "You weren't wrong to need an answer."

[O] "I made him go."

[F] "He needed to leave the room. That's what he told us."

[O] "After I—"

She stopped.

Frieren looked toward the same door.

[F] "I wish he'd stayed too."

Ori's face turned up to hers. For a moment she seemed almost surprised that they could want the same thing without either of them having made it happen.

[O] "I don't want to be very good at this any more."

Frieren brought her closer.

[F] "Then don't be. Stay here and be angry with me."

Ori made a sound against her shoulder that might have been a protest. Her hand caught the back of Frieren's sleeve. She held on for several breaths, then loosened it enough to find the cup.

Umaru lifted it off the table and waited. Ori took it in both hands.

The first sip was too big. She swallowed awkwardly, looked furious with the water and gave it back.

[U] "It's ordinary water. I didn't do anything to it."

Ori looked at her. For an instant a laugh almost got through. It did not make it all the way, but her shoulders moved differently.

[O] "I know."

Umaru held the cup while Ori wiped her face.

Milim had reached the doorway again.

She stopped there herself this time. Yuta was beside her, not in her way. Beyond them the courtyard lay bright and empty, the tree's shadow moving over the stones.

[Mi] "I have to tell him."

[Yt] "What?"

[Mi] "That I'm sorry."

She looked at the hand she had tried to hit him with. It was open now. She turned it over, then put it flat against her side.

[Mi] "I wanted him to make her stop crying."

Yuta looked toward the bench. Ori had the cup again. Frieren was holding her free hand.

[Yt] "I wanted that too."

Milim looked up at him.

[Yt] "You can go and apologise. Hands down. If he can't talk to you now, you come back. You don't make him answer."

[Mi] "I won't hit him."

[Yt] "Or shout until he says what you want."

She looked away, and for a moment he thought she would argue. Instead she wiped her cheek with the heel of her hand.

[Mi] "I'm still angry."

[Yt] "I know. You can be angry and keep your hands down."

Milim looked at him again. He was not telling her to find a nicer feeling before she could leave. That seemed to give her something she could actually do.

[Mi] "I'll just tell him."

Rimuru came to the door. He gave her the direction Ciel had passed him and made her wait until he had finished explaining that they were remaining inside the protected space.

Milim listened. She did not rise off the ground halfway through his sentence.

Before she left, she looked back.

[Mi] "Ori?"

Ori lifted her head.

Milim's mouth opened. The question in it was plain enough: was there something she should take with her, something she could say for both of them?

Ori shook her head.

[O] "Just don't hit him."

Milim nodded.

Then Ori looked up at Frieren.

[O] "Can we go upstairs?"

[F] "Yes."

[O] "Your room."

[F] "Of course."

She stood when Ori stood. Shoko took the cup before they had to decide who would carry it, then handed it to Fern.

[Sh] "Take that with you. I'll come up in a little while."

Ori kept the cloth. Umaru picked up nothing, looked at the stair, and waited until Ori caught her sleeve.

They went up slowly. Frieren stayed on Ori's other side, close enough for their shoulders to touch. At the turn, Ori stopped and looked down into the long room.

Milim was still at the door.

She lifted one hand, palm out. Ori nodded once and went on.

Only when the last of them had reached the upper corridor did Milim step into the courtyard. Yuta went with her as far as the shade of the doorway.

She rose without the crack of a launch. Her feet left the stones, her hair stirred, and she turned toward the place Rimuru had indicated.

He watched until she was clear of the house.

Upstairs, Fern opened the east-room door. Ori went to the bed and sat on its edge, still holding Frieren's hand. There was room for Umaru on her other side.

[O] "Leave it open."

Fern did.
