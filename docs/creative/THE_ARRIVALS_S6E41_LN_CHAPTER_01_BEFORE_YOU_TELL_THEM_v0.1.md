# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 33
## Chapter 63 — Before You Tell Them

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 33
**Reading order:** 63
**Source episode:** S6E41
**Continuity state:** Day466 late morning, concurrent with the household care in62. Mau/Yuka on Kaisel inside the western boundary, low enough to keep visible clear ground within a short step. They talk about Ori and responsibility, without discovering a guaranteed cure. Milim approaches to apologise and witnesses a real kiss. She objects and flies home to reveal it. Mau cannot outrun her by short steps: he reaches from the dragon, fails to catch her already-departing arm, then takes a short step to clear visible ground; no Infinity cushion or unexplained fall. He orders Kaisel to carry Yuka safely back while he follows. No new injury or relationship secret is public until64. Ciel privately knows and relays their return, not magically broadcasting the kiss to the house.

*When: Day466, late morning. Kaisel circles above the sheltered ground inside the western boundary. The house is still visible through the gaps between the trees.*

At first neither of them spoke.

Kaisel turned once above the low rise. Mau asked him to go lower, then slower, until the ground was moving beneath them at a pace he could follow without thinking. The dragon's wings worked in long, quiet strokes. Each pass brought the same pale stone into sight, then the scrub beside it, then a strip of grass the wind had flattened toward the wall.

They had sat there a week ago.

Yuka remembered the bread sticking to the cloth. Him remembering to put his cup down before kissing her.

His back was rigid against her arms now.

She loosened them a little.

[M] "You aren't hurting me."

She nodded against his shoulder, then realised he could not see it.

[Yk] "I know."

He turned his head enough to look at her.

She did not know what expression she had brought with her. His face changed when he saw it.

[M] "I'm sorry I took you out like that."

[Yk] "I came."

[M] "I know you did. I didn't give you much time to think."

She looked down at her hand against his coat.

[Yk] "I was thinking."

* * *

He asked Kaisel to hold a slow course over the clear grass.

There was room to turn on the broad back without changing places entirely. Mau shifted sideways, keeping his weight low and his right hand on the firm ridge beside him. Yuka moved with him until she could see his face without leaning past his shoulder.

They had flown together before. That part was easy.

She wished the things which were easy between them did not keep arriving beside the ones that weren't.

[Yk] "She didn't do anything wrong."

[M] "Ori?"

Yuka nodded.

[M] "I know."

His breathing was steadier now. He had not noticed when it changed. The space between one breath and the next had stopped feeling like somewhere he might get caught.

He looked back toward the house.

[M] "I could have said yes."

Yuka's mouth moved.

He knew she had an answer. He waited for it.

[Yk] "Not like that. Not just because she needed you to."

[M] "She needed it because of me."

The dragon's shadow passed across the stone. A little bird lifted out of the scrub, thought better of the open air and vanished into it again.

Yuka watched where it had gone.

[Yk] "She needed you before any of this happened."

Mau closed his eyes.

For a moment there was the door again. Not the whole day. The conviction of something, more solid than the image: a person he could not leave on the other side of a threat. He could almost feel the way he had stood when he made the choice.

It was gone before he could turn toward the face inside it.

He opened his eyes.

[M] "I saw a bit of it. When she said it."

[Yk] "The door?"

He nodded.

[M] "I wanted to reach it. I could feel myself trying, and she was right there waiting to see if I had."

Yuka looked at him.

[M] "I couldn't make it be enough."

* * *

Yuka wiped her face with the heel of her hand.

It did not help. The wind had dried the first tears and made the new ones sting at the corners of her eyes. Mau took the clean cloth out of the little bag at his side and passed it across.

She laughed once, miserably.

[Yk] "We keep bringing this."

[M] "We keep needing it."

She pressed it beneath her eyes, then folded the wet part inward before giving it back.

He did not put it away.

[Yk] "Do you want to go back?"

He looked toward the house again.

[M] "Yes."

She felt the answer in her stomach before she understood why it frightened her.

[M] "Not to pretend the last ten minutes didn't happen. I don't want her sitting there wondering whether asking made me leave for good."

Yuka lowered her head.

That was what it would look like. She had known it while putting her hand in his. She had still wanted the cold air and the distance and the place where nobody would look at her before looking at his face.

[Yk] "I should have stayed."

Mau did not tell her that she should have.

He also did not tell her that it had been the only thing she could do.

[M] "We can go back."

She nodded, and neither of them asked the dragon to turn yet.

* * *

There was something else Mau needed to say before the house came close enough to hear.

He looked at Yuka's hands. One held the edge beside her knee. The other was closed around a fold of his coat which he was not sure she knew she had taken.

[M] "I don't want to lose this either."

She looked up.

[M] "I know what it sounded like in there. I know leaving with you—"

He stopped. Tried again without preparing the sentence to make it hurt less.

[M] "I love you. That isn't something I said because I didn't know what else to say."

Her grip tightened on the cloth.

[Yk] "I know."

[M] "And I have to do something about the rest. I can't keep making her wait while I find a way to be certain nothing will change for us."

She stared at him.

[Yk] "What are you going to do?"

[M] "I don't know yet. Talk. Let people ask me the questions instead of leaving them to guess. Try to find the part I keep almost reaching."

Yuka looked away toward the ground.

He waited until she looked back.

[M] "I'm not telling you that I've decided to stop loving you."

The relief was so sharp that it hurt. She hated that it could still come first.

[Yk] "I don't want that."

He nodded.

[Yk] "I keep trying to want the right thing. Then you say that and all I can think is that I don't want you to go."

Her voice broke. She looked down at the fold in her hand and let it go, as if she had been caught holding him there.

He put his right hand over hers.

[M] "We need to go back. It doesn't mean we can't go back together."

* * *

She kissed him because she did not have another answer she could trust.

He had leaned toward her to hear the last words. She only had to close the little distance that remained. The first touch was awkward, salt on her upper lip and his breath caught halfway to a sentence. She drew back enough to look at him.

[Yk] "I love you."

He moved the cloth out from between their hands.

[M] "I know."

This time he was the one who came closer. His hand settled against her cheek. She leaned into it, crying a little again, and kissed him properly.

For a few seconds she could attend to that.

The warmth of his mouth. The hand which could still be gentle while everything in his body was trying to hold itself ready. The fact that when she stopped to breathe, he stayed close enough that she could keep her forehead against his.

Then she heard the voice.

[Mi] "That's wrong."

Mau turned.

Milim was in the air beside Kaisel's shoulder.

* * *

Her hands were open.

That was the first thing Mau saw, and later the detail he would not be able to stop thinking about. She had come with her hands open. Without anything gathered in them, without even the fist she had brought across the table.

She had stopped herself at a distance from the dragon.

Now she was looking from one face to the other.

[Mi] "That's so wrong."

Yuka had gone still. Her hand remained beneath Mau's until she seemed to notice it and pulled it back.

Milim saw that too.

[M] "Milim."

[Mi] "You kissed her."

He could not answer quickly enough to prevent her saying the next part.

[Mi] "You said you didn't want to lie."

The dragon moved one wing lower to hold them level. Mau asked him to stay steady and turned as far as he could without standing.

[M] "I need to tell them."

[Mi] "Then why didn't you?"

Yuka made a small sound. Milim looked at her, and for the first time there was something in the look that Yuka had never had directed at her by this girl.

[Mi] "He's your Oppa."

[Yk] "Milim—"

[Mi] "Ori's in there."

She pointed toward the house without looking away from them.

[Mi] "Frieren's in there."

Mau put one hand on the dragon's ridge.

[M] "I know. Let me talk to you first."

[Mi] "You had time."

* * *

She turned in the air.

Mau reached for her before he had decided what he would do if he caught her. A stupid, frightened movement, his body following his hand while the thought was still forming.

Milim was already moving away.

His fingers passed through the place her wrist had been. Kaisel tipped a wing to steady the back beneath him, and Yuka caught the ridge with both hands.

Mau stopped himself at the edge.

Below, clear grass. A few paces away, the same pale stone. Close enough to see exactly where his feet would arrive.

He did not throw a pull at Milim's back.

[M] "Kaisel. Take her home. Slowly enough that she's safe."

Yuka reached for his sleeve.

[Yk] "Mau."

[M] "I'm going there too."

He took the short step down to the visible ground.

His boots landed on the slope. One knee bent farther than he expected; he put his right hand out, caught himself in the grass and stood before trying to run.

Above him, the dragon turned with Yuka still seated securely on his back.

Milim was a bright, fast shape beyond the first line of scrub.

* * *

He could shorten a piece of the ground he could see.

He could not make himself faster than Milim by wanting to get there first.

He took one short step to the clear place beyond the scrub, then ran along the path toward the house. His left eye did not improve because he was frightened. He kept the turns on the side he could see most clearly and did not make a second blind step through a wall of leaves.

[M] *Tell Rimuru we're coming back now.*

[C] *I have. Milim is already ahead of you.*

He knew.

The kitchen roof appeared above the slope. A figure moved in the open doorway. Milim dropped lower, the speed turning her into a little flash of movement against the ordinary yard.

Mau tried to call her name.

He did not have enough breath left for it to carry.

Behind him, Yuka was coming home more slowly on Kaisel, with nothing to do but watch the distance between them close.

He had wanted to be the one to say it.

He could already hear Milim reaching the house.
