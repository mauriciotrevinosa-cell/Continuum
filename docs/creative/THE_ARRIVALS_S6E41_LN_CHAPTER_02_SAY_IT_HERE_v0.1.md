*When: Day466, near noon. The courtyard, then the long room. The door is still open from when Milim went out to apologise.*

Yuta saw her come back.

Too fast at first. A bright shape above the low path, falling toward the yard with her hair streaming behind her. He stepped out of the doorway.

She slowed before the stones.

Not neatly. Her feet came down apart, one skidding through the grit, and she caught her balance with both arms out. Nothing broke. The little cloud of dust reached Yuta before she did.

[Yt] "Milim?"

She was looking past him into the house.

[Mi] "I saw them."

He put one hand out, not holding her, asking her to stop long enough for the words to reach him.

[Yt] "Saw who?"

She went round the hand.

* * *

Maki had brought back the bowl for the wet cloth.

She was standing beside the long table with it in her hands when Milim came in. Rimuru was near the door of his office. Shoko had stayed downstairs to put the things she wanted to take up to Ori together on a tray.

All three looked round.

[Mi] "Mau and Yuka."

Her voice was too loud for the distance between them. She had meant to say it while flying, had held the sentence all the way home, and now she could not make it come out at the size of an ordinary room.

[Mi] "They were kissing. I saw them. On Kaisel. Both of them."

The bowl tipped in Maki's hands.

She caught it before it fell. Set it on the table with such care that the little sound seemed louder than a crash would have been.

[Mk] "What did you say?"

Milim turned toward her.

[Mi] "I went to say sorry. I wasn't going to hit him. My hands were down. I went like you said, and they were—"

She stopped to breathe and could not get the breath past the crying.

Yuta had followed her in. He stood between the door and the end of the table, watching her face as if he had heard a word in the wrong language and was trying to put it back into the sentence.

[Mi] "That's why. He says he can't love us, but he can do that. With her."

[Sh] "Milim. That's something you saw. The rest isn't something you can know from seeing it."

[Mi] "I know what I saw."

[Sh] "I heard you."

Shoko set the tray down. She did not go upstairs yet.

* * *

Mau came through the door before anybody asked Milim to say it a third time.

He had dirt along his right palm. His breathing was still too fast. He stopped where he could see Milim and Maki and the bowl on the table, and knew from their faces that he had not got there before the words.

[Mi] "I told them."

He nodded once.

[M] "I know."

Maki moved toward him.

Yuta turned at the same moment. Not drawing anything. Coming close enough that Mau could not look past either of them and pretend he was speaking to a room instead of to the people whose granddaughter was still outside.

[Mk] "Where is she?"

[M] "Coming back. Kaisel's bringing her. I asked him to go slowly. She's safe."

[Mk] "You left her there?"

[M] "On him. Inside the boundary. I'm not asking you to be all right with that."

Maki took his upper arm.

Not hard enough to hurt. Hard enough that he felt exactly how much she had chosen not to use. He did not raise the space between her hand and his sleeve.

[Mk] "Is it true?"

He looked at her hand. Then at her face.

[M] "Yes."

Her fingers stopped moving.

[M] "We kissed. Milim didn't mistake what she saw."

Yuta looked toward the open door.

For a moment Mau thought he was leaving to find Yuka. Then the sound of wings came down over the yard, and Maki let go of Mau's arm as if the sleeve had become something she could not bear to hold.

She went outside.

* * *

Yuka had both hands on the ridge of Kaisel's back.

He lowered himself in the clear part of the yard and waited until she could get down without jumping. She was already looking toward the door. Toward her grandmother coming across the stones.

[Yk] "Grandma."

Maki caught her by both forearms when her boots reached the ground.

[Mk] "Hurt?"

Yuka shook her head.

[Mk] "Look at me."

She did.

Maki saw that it was true. No blood. No new tear in the coat. No injury she could find a place to put her fear around.

Behind her, Yuta had reached the doorway again. He stayed there until Yuka saw him.

She looked away first.

Maki's hand moved from her arm to the back of her shoulder.

[Mk] "Inside."

She did not let Yuka go in alone.

* * *

Fern had come as far as the upper landing.

She had heard the raised voices from the corridor, not the words. Ori had heard them too and asked whether Mau was back. Fern had said she would look, leaving her with Frieren and Umaru instead of making a guess into an answer.

Now she could see him below. Yuka coming in with Maki. Milim with both hands pressed against her own chest.

[Fe] "What's happened?"

Shoko looked up.

Nobody answered for a moment.

Mau put his right hand on the end of the table, then took it away when he saw the mark the dirt left behind.

[M] "Milim saw me kiss Yuka."

Fern did not move.

[M] "She's telling the truth."

He saw her face change before she turned back up the stair.

* * *

Ori was sitting where Fern had left her.

On the edge of the bed, with the damp cloth folded between her hands. Frieren was beside her. Umaru had moved the cup onto the small table so Ori would not have to keep deciding whether to take another sip.

[O] "He's back?"

[Fe] "Yes."

Frieren looked at Fern's face.

She stood before Fern found the next sentence.

[F] "What is it?"

Fern came into the room. She did not close the door on Ori or ask her to put her hands over her ears.

[Fe] "Milim saw him kiss Yuka. He says it's true."

Frieren's hand had still been resting against Ori's shoulder.

It stayed there.

Ori looked from one face to the other. The folded cloth moved between her palms.

[O] "Yuka?"

Fern nodded.

For a moment the room had the same furniture and nobody seemed to know how to stand among it.

Then Ori rose.

[F] "You don't have to come down."

[O] "I want to."

Frieren looked at her. At the anger coming through the tired face, and the effort of making the decision when she had only just stopped shaking.

[F] "Then with us."

They went together. Fern ahead, Frieren beside Ori, Umaru at the other shoulder. Nobody sent her downstairs to find the answer by herself.

* * *

Yuta had asked one question while they were upstairs.

[Yt] "Was today the first time?"

Mau had said no.

The answer was still there when Frieren reached the last stair.

Yuka stood beside her grandmother, very still, one hand closed around the edge of her coat. Maki was looking at Mau. Yuta stood nearer the end of the table than the door now.

Milim saw Ori and made a movement toward her.

Ori stopped beside Frieren before Milim could reach them. She looked at Mau first.

Shoko moved the chair nearest them without making anyone ask. There was somewhere to sit if Ori needed it. She remained standing.

Frieren looked at Yuka.

Then at Mau.

[F] "Say it."

He could have looked at the dirt on his hand. At Yuta. At Ciel waiting silently behind the thoughts he could not make safe for this room.

He looked at Frieren.

[M] "Yuka and I have been together. In the gate. Since before we came back."

Her mouth moved without a sound.

[M] "I kissed her. I wanted to. This isn't something she made me do."

Yuka lifted her head.

[Yk] "I kissed him too."

Maki turned toward her.

[Yk] "I know what he doesn't remember. I knew."

She tried to say the next part and had to stop. Her hand opened against the coat, then closed again. Maki put a hand on her back without answering for her.

[Yk] "I'm not saying that makes it all right."

* * *

Frieren sat down.

Not in the chair Shoko had moved for Ori. On the bench close to the stair, the one she could reach without going past anybody else. Her hand left Ori's shoulder only when Ori sat beside her.

Fern stayed on the other side.

[F] "When you spoke to me. In the garden."

Mau understood the question.

[M] "Yes. Already."

She looked down at her own hand.

The string on the post. The small bird. Him asking what she was reading after giving her back permission to sit in a room with him.

She had told Fern there might be a possibility.

[F] "You said you were trying to understand."

[M] "I was. I am. I remember pieces. I didn't lie about that."

She looked up.

[F] "I thought you were trying to find your way back."

The words did not rise. He could hear every one of them.

[M] "I don't know how to do that without pretending the things I feel now don't exist."

Yuta moved then, one step toward him.

[Yt] "And you thought keeping this from us would make it kinder?"

[M] "No."

[Yt] "You sat here. You let us ask how she was. You let us think—"

He stopped with his hand on the back of the chair. The wood creaked under it.

Mau did not try to finish the sentence for him.

[M] "I was afraid of what would happen when you knew. I kept putting it off."

[Mk] "While we kept trusting you with her."

He looked at Yuka. Not to ask her to help him answer.

[M] "Yes."

* * *

Ori's voice was quieter than any of theirs.

[O] "You love her?"

Mau turned back toward the bench.

He could not answer by asking her a different question. Could not make the word smaller without making her hear him do it.

[M] "Yes."

Ori looked at his mouth after he had finished saying it, as if something else might still come out.

[O] "You can say it to her."

Frieren put her arm round her again.

Mau took a step toward them. Stopped before his need to make the sentence better became another thing they had to move for.

[M] "Ori. What happened to my memories isn't because of her."

[O] "I know."

She said it angrily. She knew someone had taken him. She knew Yuka had not reached into his head. She had been listening every time the adults explained what they thought they knew.

[O] "I know that."

He nodded. Let her have said it instead of explaining it again.

Milim had begun crying harder by the end of the table.

[Mi] "I went to say sorry."

Shoko came to her side.

[Mi] "I was going to tell him I shouldn't have tried to hit him."

[Sh] "You still shouldn't have."

Milim looked at her.

[Sh] "This doesn't change that."

She gave Milim the clean cloth she had taken from the tray. Milim pressed it against her eyes without making it into another fist.

* * *

The room had not emptied around them.

That was part of what made it impossible. People at the kitchen side had stopped what they were doing. A cup sat halfway onto the clean shelf. Gojo stood by the clear path to the door, making space when somebody behind him tried to see past his shoulder.

[G] "Not there. Give them room."

He did not say it loudly. The person moved back.

Rimuru had stayed beside the office door. He had not looked surprised when Mau answered Milim, and now he looked older than he had while staring into his tea on the morning after the gate.

He came forward enough for Mau to see him.

[R] "Nobody has to answer every question in front of everyone at once. But we aren't going to call this a misunderstanding."

Mau shook his head.

[M] "It isn't."

Yuka looked at Frieren.

[Yk] "I'm sorry."

Frieren's eyes moved to her face.

For one moment Yuka wanted her to say something sharp, something she could at least answer or fail to answer. Frieren only looked at her, so still that Maki's hand settled more firmly between Yuka's shoulders.

Ori turned her face away from Yuka.

Umaru saw it. Her own hands had closed on the back of the chair Fern had not sat in. She opened them one at a time and went to put the cup where Ori could reach it again.

No one told Yuka it was all right.

* * *

Yuta looked at Maki.

He had not finished being angry. She could see it in the way he was holding his shoulders, and he could see the same thing in her. Yuka stood between all the things they needed to ask and the first thing they had needed to know: that she was home, alive, still someone they could put a hand on.

Maki drew a chair out at the other end of the table.

[Mk] "Sit."

Yuka looked at Mau.

Then sat beside her grandmother.

Yuta stayed near them. He did not turn the chair toward Mau like the first seat of a hearing. He put his hand on Yuka's shoulder for a moment and took it away before she could mistake the touch for an answer.

At the bench, Fern asked Ori whether she wanted the water. Ori shook her head. Frieren kept her arm where it was.

Mau remained in the space between the two places.

He wanted to go to Yuka. He wanted to say something to Frieren that would change what his answer had done. He wanted to crouch in front of Ori and find the word she had asked for without borrowing it from the wrong part of himself.

There was no sentence he could put between all three that would make it come out kind.

Ori looked up at him.

[O] "Are you going again?"

He understood which part she meant.

The door was open behind him. The yard still there. The short step still something his body could do before he had finished thinking about why.

[M] "No."

He moved away from the doorway. Took the empty chair near the end of the bench, leaving room between himself and the people who had not asked him to come closer.

[M] "I'm staying."

Ori did not thank him.

Across the room, Yuka kept both feet beneath her chair and her hands in her grandmother's. Frieren looked at the ordinary floor between her bench and Mau's chair, the little stretch he had finally stayed to cross.

Nobody moved the chairs back.
