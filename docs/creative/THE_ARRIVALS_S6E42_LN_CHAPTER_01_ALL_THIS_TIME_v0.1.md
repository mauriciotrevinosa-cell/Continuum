# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 1 — All This Time

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S6E42
**Continuity state:** Day466 near noon, immediately after V33#64 with everyone in the places established there. Questions continue without repeating the revelation. The group learns the first kiss was inside the second Red Gate, not today's flight; Yuka knew the true history while Mau carried the theft's false story. Their technical borrowing agreement is distinguished from control of feelings. When directly asked who knew, Rimuru admits he knew after the return and chose not to speak; Ciel told him, Mau later spoke with him, and Rimuru never promised a lie. No one acquires the future-family conversation, the nonsexual intimate stop or cancer. Maki/Yuta take Yuka to talk privately; Frieren/Ori/Fern go upstairs while Shoko stays briefly with Milim before following. Mau remains with Rimuru. No forgiveness, attack, cure or ultimatum.

*When: Day466, near noon. The long room. Mau has sat down. For several breaths nobody knows what to do with the fact that he has stayed.*

Shoko picked up the cup beside Ori.

It had been moved so many times that the cloth beneath it was wet in a broad ring. She dried the bottom before putting it back, not because the table needed protecting but because her hands had reached for something they could finish.

Ori watched her.

[Sh] "You don't have to drink it."

Ori nodded without taking the cup.

Across the room Yuka was still sitting beside Maki. Yuta had pulled out the chair on her other side but had not sat in it. He kept looking at Mau, then at her, as though he could not decide which answer would let him ask the next question.

He finally sat.

[Yt] "How long?"

Mau looked at Yuka.

She lifted her head before he could make that into a request to answer for him.

[Yk] "In the gate."

[Yt] "This last one."

[Yk] "Yes."

Yuta's hand moved along the edge of the table.

[Yt] "Before you came home."

[M] "Yes. The first kiss was there."

He could hear how little a sentence that was beside everything it left out. The stars. The warmth of her hand when the world outside the gate had felt like something he might never be able to return to properly.

Those things belonged to them.

Keeping them private had become tangled with keeping the fact of them secret, and he no longer knew how to separate the two without hurting somebody else.

[Yt] "And you knew?"

He was looking at his granddaughter now.

[Yk] "About Frieren? Yes."

Frieren's eyes moved to her.

Yuka made herself keep her head up.

[Yk] "I told him about the tower. I told him why he went. I wasn't telling him she hadn't happened."

[Mk] "That wasn't the only way to keep something from him."

Yuka went quiet.

* * *

Gojo moved away from the doorway once the people standing behind him had gone back toward the kitchen.

He did not join the table. He stood beside the clear end, where he could speak without making either side of the room turn toward him.

[G] "The agreement you two have. Is any of this part of it?"

Yuka looked at him, then at Mau.

[M] "No."

[G] "Tell them what I'm asking."

Mau rested his hands on his knees.

[M] "The borrowing. What we tested with you. It lets her use a little of a technique through me. It doesn't tell her what to feel. It doesn't tell either of us who to love."

Yuka shook her head.

[Yk] "There isn't a promise like that."

Maki looked at Gojo.

[Mk] "You knew about the borrowing."

[G] "I watched the tests."

[Mk] "Did you know about this?"

His gaze moved briefly to Mau.

[G] "No."

Maki sat back. Not relieved, exactly. One possibility put down without making the thing in front of her any easier to hold.

Yuka's fingers moved against the sleeve where Mai had mended it. The seam was sound. It did not give her anything to pull free.

[Yk] "I wanted to kiss him."

Her voice was steady enough that everyone heard.

[Yk] "It wasn't something a contract made me do."

Yuta closed his eyes for a moment.

[Yt] "I heard you."

When he opened them again, he looked at her properly.

[Yt] "I believe you. That doesn't make this simple."

* * *

Frieren had kept her arm round Ori.

It had begun to ache at the shoulder. She did not move it. The small discomfort was useful in a room where the rest of what she felt had no edge she could find with her hands.

[F] "When we talked. You already knew you loved her."

Mau turned toward her.

He had answered the fact of it once. That was not what she was asking now.

[M] "Yes."

She looked at him for a long time.

[F] "I went upstairs and told Fern."

Fern's hand moved against the back of the bench.

[F] "I didn't tell her you'd promised. I knew you hadn't. I told her you were looking for the pieces. That you might—"

She stopped.

Mau could see the moment she decided not to finish in front of everyone. He had not earned the privacy of watching her make that decision, and there was nowhere he could put his eyes that would undo it.

[M] "I should have told you."

[F] "Then why didn't you?"

[M] "I was afraid that saying it would make everyone decide what I had to do before I understood it myself."

[F] "Did I do that? In the garden?"

He remembered her waiting. The book in her lap. The question about the cold foot, and the way she had told him not to hurt himself trying to supply the rest of the sentence.

[M] "No."

Frieren looked down at the hand on Ori's shoulder.

[M] "You didn't. I was afraid anyway. I made that your problem."

The apology had nowhere to land. He could see it in the way her face remained still.

It had been a true afternoon. He had meant the questions he asked and the apology he gave. He had wanted to stay on the bench after the difficult part was finished.

He had let her carry it home without the other part.

* * *

Maki looked toward Rimuru.

He had not retreated to the office. He stood near the end of the long table, close enough to take the empty chair if somebody offered it and far enough away that he had not made himself part of the questioning.

[Mk] "Did anyone know?"

He looked at her.

Mau began to move in his chair.

[R] "I did."

The movement stopped.

Frieren looked up.

Yuta stared at Rimuru. Maki's expression did not change quickly; the change arrived in pieces, the mouth first, then a little drawing back of her head.

[Mk] "Since when?"

[R] "After they came back. Ciel told me. Mau spoke to me the next day."

The room was quiet enough for the sound of the shell under the eaves to reach them. One low note, almost lost behind a door someone was closing near the kitchen.

[F] "All this time."

Rimuru looked at her.

[R] "Yes."

[F] "You knew what I was waiting for."

[R] "Yes."

He did not try to add how difficult the knowledge had been for him. He had spent enough time with that difficulty. She had not been given the same chance.

Mau leaned forward.

[M] "He told me he wouldn't lie. I didn't expect him to."

[Mk] "That isn't the question I asked."

Mau stopped.

Rimuru's hand had closed against his own sleeve.

[R] "He didn't ask me to invent a story for him. I decided not to tell you. I wanted him to be the one who said it, and I kept thinking I could get him there without doing it for him."

[Yt] "While she sat here."

Rimuru nodded.

[R] "I know."

The words sounded very poor now that he had to put them where Frieren could hear them.

He looked at her, not at Mau.

[R] "I should have spoken to you. About what I knew. Not about what I hoped he might do."

Frieren turned her face toward Ori.

He did not receive an answer.

* * *

Ori had been listening to the questions.

She could follow them. Who knew. When. Which thing had been a choice. The part about the borrowing, which she had watched them practise without thinking it was the important thing they were giving each other.

None of it changed the small, clear word Mau had been able to say when she asked about Yuka.

She looked at him.

[O] "But you do remember her."

[M] "The things we did after—yes."

[O] "And the things before."

He took a breath.

[M] "Some of what I thought happened before isn't right. You know that. I know it now. What we did in the gate happened. The things since coming home happened."

Ori's fingers moved in the damp cloth.

[O] "So those get to stay."

He could not make himself answer that as if it were a question about the mechanics of memory.

[M] "I want what I had with you to be there too."

She looked at him, angry in a new, exhausted way.

[O] "I don't know what I'm supposed to do with that."

He nodded.

[M] "I don't either. You don't have to make something out of it for me."

She pressed the cloth against her mouth and looked away.

Mau stayed in the chair.

* * *

Shoko was the one who finally moved the water.

Not to Ori this time. She took it to the kitchen shelf, exchanged it for a fresh cup and came back to the bench.

[Sh] "Do you want to stay here?"

She asked Ori, then looked at Frieren so she would know the question could be hers as well.

Ori shook her head.

Frieren began to get up. Fern stood with her, and this time there was no need to think about whose arm went where. Ori stayed close enough that the back of her hand kept touching Frieren's sleeve.

Maki turned toward her granddaughter.

[Mk] "I'd like to talk to you. Upstairs. With your grandfather."

Yuka looked at Mau.

Maki saw.

[Mk] "I want your answer, Yuka."

Yuka looked back at her.

[Yk] "Yes."

She stood. Her legs were stiff from how she had kept them tucked beneath the chair. Yuta moved it clear, then waited for her to find her balance without touching her.

Mau stood too, half a movement before he knew what he meant it to be.

Yuka looked at him across the space.

[Yk] "I'll see you after."

[M] "Yes."

She went with her grandparents.

The two groups reached the stair at different times. Fern let Maki and Yuta pass first instead of making everyone find a way around one another. Yuka looked back once from the turn. Mau was still beside the chair.

Frieren did not look at her as she passed.

* * *

Milim stayed downstairs with Shoko until Ori was out of sight.

She had been holding the clean cloth without using it for several minutes. The tears had slowed. Her face looked sore where she kept rubbing it with the same corner.

[Mi] "I was right about what I saw."

[Sh] "Yes."

[Mi] "Then why does it feel—"

She stopped. Looked at the stair.

Shoko took the used corner out of her grip and gave her the dry one.

[Sh] "Because seeing a thing doesn't make you able to fix it."

Milim looked down at the cloth, then wiped her face properly.

Gojo had picked up one of the breakfast bowls which had been forgotten when Ori stood. He looked at it, decided against making anything out of the absurdity of still needing to clear breakfast and took it to the kitchen.

Mau watched him go.

Rimuru was still there when he looked back.

[R] "Do you want to sit outside?"

Mau looked toward the open door.

[M] "I don't want her to come back and think I've gone again."

[R] "Then here."

He pulled out the chair opposite, with enough space between them for the things neither had said properly yet.

Mau sat.

From upstairs came the sound of a door opening, then another farther along. Ordinary sounds. He could tell which way Yuka had gone without asking.

He did not follow her.
