# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 8 — Somewhere to Breathe

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S6E45
**Continuity state:** Day467 after dinner. Kessoku rehearse in the established music room. Bocchi asked Rem after lunch about the relationship and received only confirmed public facts, then told her bandmates; no retroactive presence at the revelation or private knowledge. The concrete musical problem concerns Kita hurrying and holding her breath while negotiating a guitar change, followed by a small arrangement adjustment. The stool and ordinary wooden shim from V33#32 remain in use. Bocchi/Kita remain girlfriends; Nijika/Ryo are bandmates and friends. Bocchi's hurt and continuing sibling affection for Mau are not resolved into approval or a compulsory performance. No commercial song, new equipment or scheduled show.

*When: Day467, after dinner. The music room. Kita has reached the end of the same line twice without enough breath to finish it, and Ryo has begun looking at the window.*

[Kt] "What?"

[Ry] "Making sure there's air."

Kita put her picking hand flat across the strings.

[Kt] "There is air."

[Ry] "Then the problem's closer."

Nijika reached across and tapped the top of Ryo's head with the thick end of a stick.

[Nj] "Useful suggestions."

[Ry] "Breathe."

[Kt] "Thank you. I had considered it."

Bocchi looked down at the page before Kita saw her smiling.

It was one of the quieter songs. That had seemed a reasonable choice after dinner. There were no fast runs to chase across the neck, no long ending for Ryo to escape into, and Nijika could keep the rhythm light enough that the instruments did not fill every corner of the room.

Kita had sung it before.

The guitar part was different now. She had suggested the higher chord shape herself when they tried it earlier in the week. It made the verse brighter without making it louder, and she had liked the way Bocchi's little phrase sat above it. Practising the shape on its own had seemed easy enough. She had not tested it while trying to put all the words in their proper places.

Tonight, halfway through the verse, she began hurrying. By the last phrase her voice had gone small, her shoulders were up and Bocchi could hear the breath she had not managed to take trying to get into the note with everything else.

They stopped again.

[Kt] "I know where the words go."

[Nj] "I know."

[Kt] "I sang it all the way here."

[Ry] "It went past me in the corridor. Sounded fine."

Nijika pointed the stick at her without looking.

[Ry] "That was useful."

Bocchi leaned forward to look at Kita's left hand.

The stool stayed quiet beneath her.

She noticed that sometimes. There was still a little pale edge beneath the leg by the window, and two pencil marks on the boards showing where it belonged. One mark had begun to fade beneath their feet. Kita had gone over it once, very carefully, without moving the stool to see where it was supposed to be.

Mau had laughed when they put the first marks down.

Bocchi moved her foot and looked at the page again.

What she knew had come from Rem.

After lunch she had found her by the clean shelves and asked whether the things people were saying were true. Rem had put down the cup she was holding. Told her about the kiss Milim saw, about Mau and Yuka confirming that they were together, about the first kiss having happened inside the last Red Gate.

She had not given Bocchi a story about what either of them must secretly have meant.

Bocchi had asked one more question, about whether Frieren knew now. Rem had answered yes.

She had told the other three before they began playing. Not much. The few things she actually knew, instead of trying to make the gaps join neatly.

Then they had taken out their instruments.

Kita lifted her hand off the strings.

[Kt] "Could we start just before it?"

Bocchi nodded.

They tried the passage again.

This time Bocchi watched instead of following the page. Kita's hand reached for the new shape. Her smallest finger missed the string, came back, found it. Her mouth was still moving through the line, but the breath between the words had disappeared.

She caught up with the guitar by going faster.

Then faster again.

Nijika brought them to a stop with one lifted stick.

[Nj] "We gained half a song."

[Kt] "We did not."

[Ry] "A small song."

Bocchi put her pick on her knee.

[B] "Could you sing it without playing?"

Kita looked at her hand, then at the page.

[Kt] "All right."

She laid the guitar safely beside the stool. Nijika counted them in, and Kita sang.

The verse went where it belonged. The little breath came between the two phrases, barely noticeable unless someone was looking for it. Her voice reached the last note without having to squeeze itself smaller.

Nijika lowered the sticks.

Ryo looked toward the window again.

[Kt] "Don't."

[Ry] "I didn't say anything."

[Kt] "Your head said it."

Bocchi took her own guitar off her lap.

[B] "It's the change. You look for where the finger goes, and then you stop breathing until it's there."

Kita considered this with the expression of somebody being told that she had been forgetting an elementary part of being alive.

[Kt] "That's ridiculous."

[Nj] "You did look very determined."

[Kt] "I was concentrating."

[Ry] "On being dead for a beat."

Nijika made a small threatening movement with the stick. Ryo leaned neatly out of reach.

They tried the guitar part without the voice.

Kita could do that too. It was not smooth at first, because she was thinking about the finger now, but after a few attempts her hand found the shape without her shoulder helping.

Then she sang with it.

The shoulder went up.

Kita stopped herself.

[Kt] "Oh."

Ryo sat forward.

[Ry] "At least you've caught yourself doing it."

The absence of a joke made Kita look at her for a moment. Then she put her fingers back where they had begun.

[Kt] "Again."

They made it smaller.

Two changes, no whole verse. Nijika tapped the pulse against the rim. Bocchi played Kita's line quietly beneath it while Kita made the chord with her left hand and took the breath where it belonged.

The first time, the breath was so loud that Nijika lost the beat laughing.

[Kt] "You told me to take one."

[Nj] "A breath. Not the whole room."

Kita did it again, glaring at her.

This time Bocchi laughed and missed her own change.

[Kt] "You're making it very difficult to improve."

[Ry] "We can all leave."

[Nj] "You stay. You've got the easy part."

Ryo put a hand over the strings of her bass.

[Ry] "That was hurtful."

Nijika kept tapping.

After a while the two movements stopped behaving like opponents. Kita reached the chord, breathed and began the next phrase without hurrying to prove that she had got there.

She looked at Bocchi.

Bocchi nodded before she could ask.

Kita's smile lasted through the next attempt, which was not quite as good but was close enough that they did not stop.

When Nijika lowered her sticks, Ryo asked whether they had meant the space before the next phrase to sound so empty.

Kita slumped.

[Kt] "I have only just acquired that space."

[Ry] "I'm not taking it away. I could put something under it."

She played the bass line by itself, then added two small notes before the next phrase. Not busy. A movement which arrived beneath the breath and gave it somewhere to go.

Bocchi listened twice.

[B] "Could you start the second one a little later?"

Ryo did.

Kita hummed over it. Nijika joined them quietly, and the little gap stopped sounding as if somebody had forgotten a part.

They played it once more before taking a drink.

Kita set her guitar down and reached for the cups on the side table. She held Bocchi's out while Bocchi tried to persuade her pick out of a fold in her trousers without losing it again.

[Kt] "Here."

Bocchi took the cup. Their fingers stayed together a moment after the weight had changed hands.

Across the room, Ryo was explaining to Nijika that her two notes were now the most important part of the verse. Nijika offered to remove one and see whether the other could manage alone.

Bocchi looked toward the stool.

[B] "I don't want to stop speaking to him."

Kita followed her eyes.

[Kt] "Mau?"

Bocchi nodded.

She held the cup with both hands. The water had gone neither hot nor cold. She drank anyway, because she had reached the point in the sentence where drinking might give the rest of it time to arrive.

[B] "I'm angry. I keep thinking about Frieren. And Ori. And I still want to tell him when something happens."

Kita sat beside her, close enough that their shoulders touched.

[B] "It feels like if I go and ask him to listen to something, everyone will think—"

She stopped.

[Kt] "What would you want him to hear?"

That was not the question she had been preparing for.

Bocchi looked at the page.

[B] "This. When we get it right."

[Kt] "Then say that."

Bocchi rubbed one thumb down the cup.

[B] "It isn't the same as thinking he's done nothing wrong."

[Kt] "I heard you."

Kita did not tell her she would find a way to make everybody else hear it too. Bocchi was grateful for that, and annoyed with the part of herself that had wanted the assurance anyway.

[B] "He's my brother. He doesn't have to remember it for me to—"

She stopped again, breathing out through her nose.

Kita moved her hand off the cup and held it.

[Kt] "Do you want me to come with you when you talk to him?"

Bocchi thought about it.

[B] "Maybe just to the door."

Kita nodded.

For a while they sat listening to the argument on the other side of the room. Ryo now claimed she had always intended the second note to arrive late and had only played it early to see whether anybody would notice.

Nijika did not believe her.

Bocchi leaned her temple against Kita's for a moment, then put her cup down.

[B] "We should try it with the voice."

Kita's hand tightened once before letting go.

[Kt] "We should. Before I forget how to breathe again."

They started halfway through the verse.

Kita made the change. Took the breath. Ryo's two notes moved beneath it, the second just late enough to lean toward the voice coming back. Bocchi left them alone instead of putting her own little answer in the same place.

That was the part she had needed to change.

They stopped after the next line, all four of them listening to where it had gone.

[Nj] "That's better."

[Ry] "My contribution."

[B] "Two notes."

Ryo looked at her.

Bocchi looked back, surprised by herself and unwilling to surrender the observation.

[B] "They are good notes."

[Ry] "Thank you."

[Nj] "We might need to ration the praise."

Kita was laughing now. Her left hand had stayed loosely round the guitar neck, no shoulder lifting to help it, and she kept trying to explain that this was making the breathing more difficult rather than less.

Nijika counted them in while she was still saying it.

Kita missed the first word.

[Kt] "Don't forget to breathe—"

She sang it in place of the rest of the line, fitting the words very badly and looking straight at Nijika.

Ryo immediately supplied a lower version beneath her.

Bocchi tried to keep playing. Managed half the phrase. Then Kita turned toward her, still singing the instruction with unbearable seriousness, and Bocchi had to take her hand off the strings.

[Nj] "This is now a different song."

[Ry] "An educational one."

Kita sang the instruction again, more elaborately.

Nijika made a long, dreadful breath on the final beat.

They had to wait some time before any of them could count without laughing.
