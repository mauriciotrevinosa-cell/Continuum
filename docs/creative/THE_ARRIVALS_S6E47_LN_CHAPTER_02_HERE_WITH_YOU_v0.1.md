# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 12 — Here With You

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S6E47
**Continuity state:** Day468, afternoon, immediately following the entry at the end of chapter10 and overlapping the first outdoor aftermath. Yuta did not witness the discharge and learns its concrete sequence from Maki and Mau; Yuka explains the lotus exchange. Nobody was hit. Mau's brief left-side head pressure is easing and Ciel monitors privately without a new diagnosis or cure. Yuka chooses her own room and asks Mau to accompany her; her grandparents' care and anger coexist. Maki leaves Frieren's staff temporarily in Rimuru's downstairs office with his express agreement, not in a newly invented armoury, and does not mistake possession of it for removing Frieren's magic. No battle, retaliation, forgiveness, ultimatum or Goddess intervention.

*When: Day468, afternoon. The common-room doorway. Yuka has just crossed the stone step with Mau beside her and Maki carrying the staff.*

Yuta looked at the staff first.

It was on the wrong side of Maki. Held low, with the upper end kept clear of Yuka's sleeve, as if it were something awkward they needed to take through the door without touching anybody.

Then he saw Yuka's face.

He put down the length of cord he had been trying to untangle. It fell across the seat instead of the table. He did not look to see where.

[Yt] "What happened?"

Yuka opened her mouth.

Nothing came out. She had been able to say no to Shoko outside. Now her grandfather was coming toward her, and the little space beneath her ribs kept closing before she could use it for words.

Maki answered.

[Mk] "Frieren fired at her. Mau stopped it."

Yuta stopped walking.

[Yt] "Fired what?"

[Mk] "Zoltraak. One. At her arm. Here."

She indicated her own upper arm without touching Yuka's. Her granddaughter already had one hand pressed over the place where she had understood the light was going.

Yuta looked there.

No torn cloth. No blood working through it. Mau's right hand was holding Yuka's other hand, and Yuka's fingers were closed so tightly that the colour had gone out of the tips.

[Yt] "Did it reach you?"

She shook her head.

[Yt] "Yuka."

[Yk] "No."

He breathed once. Too slowly to be an ordinary breath.

[Yt] "Where is she?"

[Mk] "Outside. Fern and Shoko are with her. Gojo too."

His eyes moved to the door.

Maki watched him see the path beyond it.

[Mk] "Stay with us."

He looked back at Yuka. At the hand still covering her shoulder.

He came the rest of the way to her instead.

* * *

Yuta did not pull her out of Mau's grip.

He put his hand against her back, below Maki's, and asked her whether she wanted to sit. She nodded, then looked toward the long table and shook her head.

There were people at the kitchen side. Not crowding them. Already trying not to be people who were looking.

[Yk] "My room."

[Yt] "All right."

She looked at Mau.

[Yk] "Come with me?"

He nodded.

Yuta saw the question. He moved the chair beside the stair out of their way before either of them could try to turn sideways around it.

Rimuru had come out of the office. His eyes moved between their faces, then to the thing Maki was carrying.

[Mk] "Can this stay in there for now?"

She lifted the staff only enough to make the question clear.

Rimuru opened the office door farther.

[R] "Yes. Beside the desk. Behind this side. Nobody will catch it walking in."

Maki carried it in herself. Leaned it in the corner he had indicated, with the lower end flat on the floor, and checked that it would not slide.

Her hand remained on it for a moment.

[Mk] "I took it out of her hand. That isn't the same as making her unable to cast."

Rimuru looked at her.

[R] "I know."

[Mk] "Don't give it back while we're upstairs. We need to speak first."

[R] "I won't."

She came out empty-handed.

Mau had waited with Yuka. His left eye kept wanting to close against the light from the yard. He made himself stop pressing his teeth together.

[C] *Has the pressure changed?*

[M] *Less. Still at the side.*

[C] *Tell me if it sharpens. Don't keep testing it by reaching for the route.*

He had not meant to do that. He let go of the small inward attention which had been circling the place where the spell had stopped.

Rimuru looked at him once, aware of the thread, but did not ask the room to wait while they discussed his head.

Yuka began toward the stair. Mau went at her pace.

* * *

The coat was still on its hook.

Yuka noticed that before anything else when they entered her room. She had not worn it to the garden. There had been no reason to put on the gate coat to walk a short way through her own house and try to apologise.

She sat on the bed. Released Mau's hand only when he had to move the stool out of the door's path.

Yuta closed the door after asking her. Not all the way at first; he waited for her answer and then brought it gently into the frame.

Maki sat beside her granddaughter.

Mau stayed by the stool.

[Yt] "Tell me what happened. What you saw."

He was looking at Maki, not asking Yuka to begin with the thing her breath still would not let her say.

Maki rested both feet against the floor.

[Mk] "I was coming from the workshop side. I saw the staff come up. It was pointed at Yuka. Mau moved between them before the light came out. He held it off. I reached Frieren and stopped her hand moving. She stopped casting."

Yuta looked at Mau.

[M] "I saw where it was gathering. Took a short step into the line. The ground was clear."

[Yt] "And your head?"

Mau had hoped not to have to become another person somebody checked before Yuka had finished shaking.

Yuta's expression told him he had seen the hope.

[M] "Pressure from holding it. Left side. It's easing. Shoko asked outside. Ciel's watching it. Nothing hit me."

[Yt] "Did you fall?"

[M] "No."

Yuta nodded. Put the answers somewhere separate from the rest.

[Yt] "It wasn't a warning shot."

Maki's face changed.

[Mk] "No."

She looked at Yuka's hand before continuing.

[Mk] "She aimed where Yuka's arm was. I was close enough to see."

Yuka began to lift the hand from her sleeve. Stopped halfway. Put it back.

Yuta sat on the other side of her, leaving enough room that she did not have to tuck her knees out of anybody's way.

[Yt] "I wasn't there. I'm asking so I don't make up what I missed."

Yuka nodded.

She was grateful for the question. She hated having to be grateful for anything which made her picture the staff again.

* * *

[Yk] "The flower."

All three looked at her.

She took her hand off her shoulder properly. Turned the wrist so the bracelet lay upward, the little metal shape against her skin.

[Yk] "She saw this."

Yuta looked at the mirrored petals.

[Yk] "She asked where it came from. I said my birthday. Then she asked whether I knew what it meant."

Her thumb caught the edge of the flower. She moved it away instead of working at it.

[Yk] "Eternal love. In her world."

Yuta's eyes lifted to Mau.

[M] "I didn't know. I bought it because I liked it for her. Neither of us knew its name."

Maki's fingers curled against the blanket.

[Mk] "You said that outside."

[M] "Yes. After."

Yuka kept looking at the bracelet.

[Yk] "I tried to tell her he hadn't asked the seller. She told me not to say what he thought. Then she said I had somewhere to stand with everyone. That I didn't have to take hers."

She stopped.

Mau sat on the stool. It put him where she could see him without looking past either grandparent. He did not reach for the flower or hide it beneath her cuff.

[Yk] "I wanted to say sorry."

[Mk] "I know."

[Yk] "I knew she might not want to hear it. I wasn't expecting her to be pleased I came. I just—"

Her voice caught. She tried the next breath, and it did not help.

[Yk] "I wasn't ready for her to do that."

Yuta put one hand on the blanket, palm upward.

Yuka looked at it. Put her hand into his with such care that he could feel the effort of not gripping as hard as she had held Mau.

[Yt] "You shouldn't have needed to be."

Her face crumpled.

She bent forward, trying to cry quietly, and Maki's arm came round her before she had finished finding somewhere to put her head.

* * *

It took a long time for the sentences to come apart enough that she could speak between them.

Maki kept one hand against the back of her head. Yuta let her hold his. Mau looked at the edge of the bed instead of making her see a fourth frightened face every time she lifted her own.

[Yk] "It was here."

She moved the shoulder beneath Maki's arm.

[Yk] "I knew where it was going. I saw him get there and I still thought—"

Her fingers closed on Yuta's.

[Yk] "I didn't move."

[Yt] "You didn't have time to work out a move."

[Yk] "I can fight."

[Yt] "Yes."

She looked at him.

[Yt] "This wasn't a practice where you knew somebody was going to attack."

She tried to swallow. Her throat hurt.

[Yk] "If I hadn't come over—"

[Mk] "She wouldn't have had you in front of her."

Yuka went still.

Maki moved enough to look at her face.

[Mk] "That is not the same as you making her fire."

Yuka's mouth opened, then closed.

[Mk] "You went to speak. You didn't agree to be hurt if she didn't like what you said."

Yuka looked down at the metal flower again.

[Yk] "Should I take it off?"

Mau's hand moved against his knee. He kept it there.

Maki looked at him, then back at her granddaughter.

[Mk] "Do you want to?"

[Yk] "I don't know."

Her eyes filled again.

[Yk] "It was a good day. I keep thinking about the good bit, and now I can see her looking at it."

Nobody told her which part ought to win.

Yuta moved his thumb away from the flower so that his hand was not pressing it into her wrist.

[Yt] "You can leave it where it is while you don't know."

She nodded. The little cord remained fastened.

* * *

Maki had been holding her jaw still for several minutes.

Yuta saw the movement when she finally loosened it. The anger had not left when she sat beside Yuka. It had become something she had to keep putting back behind the next useful thing.

[Yt] "What did you say to her?"

Maki looked at him.

[Mk] "That if she tried again, I'd kill her."

Yuka lifted her head.

She had heard it outside. Hearing her grandmother repeat it in her room made the words belong to a person she could reach, not only the voice beside the bench.

Yuta looked down at their joined hands.

He did not ask Maki to turn it into something she had not said. He did not get up and go looking for Frieren either.

[Yt] "Gojo's with her."

[Mk] "He was when we came in."

He nodded.

[Yt] "Then I'm here."

Yuka's fingers moved in his hand.

[Yk] "Are you going to fight?"

[Mk] "Not now."

Yuka looked frightened by the shape of the answer.

Maki saw it.

[Mk] "I'm not going out to attack her. I'm staying with you."

Yuka nodded once. Needed another moment before she could do it again.

[Yk] "I don't want to see her today."

[Yt] "You don't have to go and find her. We'll speak before you need to come down."

[Yk] "I don't want everybody stopping what they're doing when I come in either."

Yuta glanced at the door.

[Yt] "We'll start with this afternoon. You can tell me where you want to be."

She rubbed her cheek against Maki's sleeve, then sat up enough to look toward the window. It showed the workshop roof. Not the garden bench.

[Yk] "Here. For a while."

[Mk] "All right."

* * *

Mau stood when Yuta went to fetch water from the little pitcher.

For a moment he thought he should offer to do it. Then Yuta was already carrying the cup, and there was no reason to turn having hands into another argument about who ought to be allowed to care for her.

He sat again.

Yuta put the water within Yuka's reach. She took a little, held it in her mouth until she could swallow without hurting, then passed the cup back.

[Yt] "Thank you."

Mau looked up.

[Yt] "For being there. For stopping it."

Mau nodded. His own hand had started shaking a little now that he was not using it. He laid it flat against his knee.

[M] "I saw her lift the staff."

[Yt] "I'm glad you did."

Maki's eyes had closed for a moment. When she opened them, she looked at Mau too.

[Mk] "So am I."

There was nothing in either face which told him yesterday had been taken back. He was grateful they had not made him work out whether that was what they meant.

Yuka saw him press his palm harder against his knee.

[Yk] "Your head?"

[M] "Better."

He waited a moment, checked the ordinary sensation instead of reaching for the technique, and gave her the rest.

[M] "Still a little pressure. It's going."

[C] *It is easing. Keep the route closed.*

He did. No demonstration to prove the answer.

* * *

Yuka asked him whether he would stay.

Mau looked at her grandparents before he could stop himself. Yuta saw, reached for the spare cup and set it beside the first.

[Yt] "She asked you."

[M] "Yes."

Yuka let a breath go.

[Yk] "Not talk for a bit. Just stay."

[M] "Okay."

He moved the stool nearer when she held out her hand. Close enough to reach, leaving her where she had chosen beside Maki.

Yuta stayed on the other side. After a while he got up to open the window a little, because the room had become warm with four people keeping still in it. The curtain lifted and settled without reaching anybody's face.

Maki's sleeve was wet where Yuka had cried against it. She noticed her granddaughter looking at the mark and drew the dry part nearer without commenting.

The bracelet touched Mau's thumb when Yuka moved her hand.

He did not turn it over to show the flower. She did not hide it.

Outside the room someone came along the corridor and slowed near the door. Yuta went to answer before they needed to knock. He spoke too quietly for Yuka to hear the words, then came back and set the cup within reach again.

Yuka leaned against her grandmother. Her hand stayed in Mau's.

For now she could look at the window without seeing the end of the staff.
