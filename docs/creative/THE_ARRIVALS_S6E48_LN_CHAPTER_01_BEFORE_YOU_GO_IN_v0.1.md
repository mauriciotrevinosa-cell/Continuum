# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 13 — Before You Go In

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 13
**Source episode:** S6E48
**Continuity state:** Day468 evening. Fern tells Ori plainly what Frieren herself admitted in chapter11; Ori did not witness the attack. Shoko has checked inside after leaving the bench and reports no wound, with fear and temporary strain still taken seriously. Frieren has been accompanied back to the east room without crossing Yuka's chosen space. The staff remains in Rimuru's downstairs office under the temporary arrangement in12. Ori can be hurt, angry and attached to her mother at once; she is not recruited to excuse the attack or repair the adults. Fern has support outside the maternal role. No magic, new injury, recovered memory, reconciliation or ultimatum.

*When: Day468, evening. The east corridor. Fern has come to fetch Ori from Rem, and stops before the last turn toward the room.*

Ori knew something had happened before Fern told her.

Rem had been trying to finish a game. Not the way she did when she wanted to win; the way she did when a person had begun listening to the door and wanted to pretend there was another reason for taking so long to throw.

Then Shoko had come by. Rem had stood up. Ori had heard her own name, and after that the two women had spoken farther away.

She had waited until Fern came.

[O] "Is she sick?"

Fern stopped beside the narrow stretch of wall before the turn.

[Fe] "She's upset. There is something I need to tell you before you go in."

Ori looked past her.

[O] "Mau?"

[Fe] "He's all right. Shoko went to see them. Nobody has a wound."

Ori looked back sharply.

[O] "Why would they?"

Fern put the little bundle of Ori's things against her own side. Rem had sent her with a sock which had come off and a piece from the game which Ori kept forgetting was in her hand.

[Fe] "Frieren cast a spell at Yuka this afternoon. She aimed at her arm. Mau got between them and stopped it."

Ori stared at her.

Fern waited for the words to finish arriving. She did not know how long that took. Only that the face in front of her had become very still.

[O] "Why?"

[Fe] "They were talking. Frieren was hurt and angry. She chose to do something she shouldn't have."

[O] "At Yuka?"

[Fe] "Yes."

Ori looked toward the turn again.

[O] "Did Yuka try to do something?"

[Fe] "She came to apologise."

The silence after that answer was harder than the first one.

* * *

Somebody came up the stair carrying a folded blanket. Fern moved nearer the wall to give them room. Ori followed without looking at who passed.

[O] "Did you see it?"

[Fe] "No. I came outside afterward. I asked Frieren what she had done. She told me."

[O] "And she meant—"

Ori could not finish the question.

Fern crouched enough to put their eyes nearer the same height. Her knees were tired. She rested one hand against the wall instead of pretending they were not.

[Fe] "Yes. She told me she meant to hurt her."

Ori's mouth moved. Closed.

She had been angry with Yuka for two days. It had been possible to be angry with her all morning while Rem explained that the little bone had not fallen where Ori wanted it to, and while Umaru argued that it had fallen exactly there, thank you.

She had wanted Yuka to leave them alone.

Now there was a different thing beside that wish, and she did not know whether it had come from the same place.

[O] "We didn't want her in the Fort."

Fern nodded.

[O] "We said she couldn't come."

[Fe] "Rem told me about that."

Ori looked at her hands.

[O] "I didn't want—"

Her voice stopped.

[Fe] "You didn't make Frieren cast."

[O] "But I was angry too."

[Fe] "So was I. We still didn't choose for her."

Ori leaned back against the wall. She did it carefully, as though the corridor might make an unexpected movement if she let herself touch it too hard.

[O] "Is Yuka scared?"

[Fe] "Yes."

She swallowed.

[O] "Of Mama?"

Fern's hand tightened against the plaster.

[Fe] "Yes."

There was no smaller answer she could give without leaving Ori to find out how small it had been later.

* * *

The door at the end of the corridor was partly open.

Ori could see the edge of a chair. Frieren's hand lay on its arm, fingers turned slightly inward. There was no book in it.

Fern followed her gaze.

[Fe] "You don't have to go in because she's upset. You can stay with Rem, or come with me for a little while."

[O] "Does she want me?"

[Fe] "Yes. I asked before I came. That doesn't decide what you want."

Ori looked at the game piece in Fern's hand.

It was the wrong shape for where she had left it. Something she ought to have put back on the cloth so they could finish the game when she returned.

[O] "Can you give that to Rem?"

[Fe] "I can."

[O] "Tell her I didn't take it. I forgot."

Fern nodded.

Ori went toward the room. At the threshold she stopped, looked back and held out one hand.

Fern went in with her.

* * *

Frieren was looking at the window.

She turned when she heard them. For a moment her expression did the familiar thing which meant she had recognised a small interruption and was making room for it. Then she saw Ori's face.

[F] "You know."

Ori nodded.

Fern put the sock on the bed. The book from the garden lay on the little table beside the chair, its cloth folded beneath it. Somebody had brought it in while they were elsewhere. The staff had not come with it.

[O] "Why did you do it?"

Frieren looked at Fern.

There was no answer waiting there for her to borrow.

[F] "I wanted to hurt her."

Ori's fingers tightened round Fern's.

[O] "I know that part."

Frieren looked down.

For a long while the room contained only the window moving a little in its frame and a sound from below which might have been a chair being pulled out for supper.

[F] "I saw something he gave her. It made me think about the things I thought we'd have. I was very angry."

Ori watched her mouth as she spoke.

[F] "She was standing there, and I wanted to make her feel something because I was hurting. I knew what I was doing."

[O] "Did it make it stop?"

Frieren closed her eyes.

[F] "No."

Ori let go of Fern's hand.

She went to the end of the bed and sat there, leaving the whole space between herself and the chair. Her feet did not settle at once. She looked down at them until they did.

[O] "I don't like her right now."

[F] "I know."

[O] "I still don't want her hurt."

Frieren's hand moved on the arm of the chair.

[F] "You shouldn't."

[O] "I wasn't asking."

The answer left Frieren silent.

Fern sat on the other end of the bed. Not between them. Near enough that Ori could look at her if she needed to.

* * *

Ori had questions about Mau too.

Whether the spell had hurt his head. Whether he was still with Yuka. Whether he had been angry when it happened.

Fern answered the first with what Shoko had actually told her: the pressure had been easing when she checked, and he had not been struck. The other questions were not hers to fill in.

[Fe] "He went upstairs with her. I haven't spoken to him since."

Ori looked at Frieren.

[O] "Will he be scared of you?"

[F] "I don't know."

She looked at the place beside the shelf. The absence of the staff had begun to change the room in ways no one had moved any furniture to arrange.

[F] "I gave him a reason to be."

Ori's eyes filled.

She wiped them with the heel of one hand, annoyed by the movement before she had even finished it.

[O] "I want him to come back."

Frieren put her hand over her mouth.

Fern looked at the window. The sky had not changed enough to justify how long they seemed to have been in this room.

[O] "I don't know what to want now."

Frieren lowered her hand.

[F] "You don't have to decide it for me."

Ori stared at her.

[F] "You can want him here. You can be angry with me."

[O] "I don't want to go away from you."

Frieren's face changed so quickly that Fern had to look down.

[F] "You don't have to."

Ori slid off the bed. Came halfway across the room and stopped.

[O] "Are you going to do it again?"

Frieren shook her head.

[O] "Say it."

[F] "I'm not going to hurt Yuka."

Ori watched her for another moment before coming the rest of the way.

* * *

The chair was too narrow for the position Ori wanted.

Frieren moved anyway. Ori fitted herself against her side, knees bent, one foot finding the edge of the cushion and the other left dangling until Fern pushed the little stool nearer with her heel.

Neither thanked her. It was one less sentence to find.

Frieren put her arm round Ori. After a while the child's forehead came against her shoulder.

[O] "I don't know how to stop being angry."

Frieren looked over her head at Fern.

[F] "Neither do I."

Ori made a very small movement against her.

[O] "I don't want you to tell me something that isn't true."

[F] "Then I won't."

They stayed that way while the corridor grew louder with people coming up and going down. When somebody knocked, Fern went to answer before either of them had to move.

Stark stood outside with a plate covered by another plate.

[St] "Rem said this was for here."

He looked past Fern only far enough to see they were both sitting down.

[St] "And I thought you might not have had anything either."

Fern took it. There was more on the plate than two people who had lost their appetites were likely to eat.

[Fe] "Thank you."

[St] "I'll be down at the end. If you want."

She watched him go before closing the door most of the way.

* * *

Ori ate first.

Not because anybody persuaded her. Frieren broke a piece of the bread and held it while looking at the plate, and after a while Ori took it out of her hand.

The movement made Frieren look down.

[O] "You too."

Frieren took another piece. She chewed slowly, as if there were instructions she had to remember between one ordinary act and the next.

Fern put the plate on the table beside the closed book.

[Fe] "I'm going to sit with Stark for a little while. I'll come back. You can call me before that."

Frieren nodded. Ori did too, her cheek against the same place on Frieren's shoulder.

Fern picked up the game piece from where she had set it beside the sock.

At the door she looked back once.

Ori was not asleep. Frieren was not reading. They were both looking at the window, close enough that the chair no longer seemed to have an empty side.

Fern went down the corridor to give Rem back the piece.

Then she went to find Stark.
