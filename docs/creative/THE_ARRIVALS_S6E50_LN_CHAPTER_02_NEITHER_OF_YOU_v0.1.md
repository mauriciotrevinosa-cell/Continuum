# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 18 — Neither of You

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 18
**Source episode:** S6E50
**Continuity state:** Day471 morning. Mau/Yuka practise two existing small Blue outlets against two swinging targets, observed by Gojo and Umaru with Ciel monitoring privately. Shared permitted demand remains capped; no second supply or full output per person. The initial moving trial fails because the two demand changes overlap. They stop, rest, shorten the starting swings and use less correction earlier, completing a bounded simultaneous task. This develops the static split from V33#27 rather than repeating the separate Blue-then-Red result59 or inventing Purple practice. Gojo knows about the garden through his arrival there; he was not a witness to its beginning. No stat purchase, new technique, injury or restored memories.57 points held,14/40 named and98 soldiers. Existing eye/hand limits remain.

*When: Day471, morning. The clear practice ground beyond the workshops. Two sacks hang from the crossbeam, and Gojo is making them look harder than they ought to be.*

He pushed one.

Waited.

Pushed the other.

The sacks passed each other without touching. One travelled toward the place where Mau stood outside the marked lane. The other went away from Yuka, came back and passed the little hoop she had laid on the ground.

[G] "There."

[Yk] "There what?"

[G] "Neither of you got it."

[M] "We haven't started."

[G] "A very promising explanation."

Umaru had brought the page from the first two-outlet trial. She put it on the flat stump and weighted the edge with the water flask. Nothing on it said a second person received a second reserve. She had underlined that part long before anybody knew why Mau and Yuka liked practising together so much.

She looked from the page to them.

[U] "You're both doing the same thing as before?"

[M] "The two small pulls."

[Yk] "With something moving this time."

Umaru watched Gojo send one sack back through its lane.

[U] "I can see that part."

* * *

The hoops were not targets to hit.

They showed where the sacks needed to pass above the ground before the ropes took them back. Each was a little to the side of the ordinary swing. A small correction would be enough if it began in the right place.

Yuka checked the clear distance beyond hers. Mau did the same at the other end. Neither lane pointed toward the buildings, and nobody stood inside them to make the exercise feel urgent.

Gojo waited while they chose where to put their feet.

[G] "What happens if hers needs more?"

Mau looked at the two sacks.

[M] "Mine gets less."

[G] "What happens if yours needs more at the same time?"

Yuka looked at him.

[Yk] "We don't both get it."

[G] "Good. Try remembering that while the sack is making an unanswerable argument for being the most important thing in the world."

He went back to the crossbeam.

[G] "It's a sack. We can survive disappointing it."

* * *

Ciel checked the narrow loan.

Mau kept his attention on the familiar outlet instead of reaching toward the new common shadow threshold. The two pieces of work were different; finding a soldier more easily yesterday had not changed the demand of a Blue today.

[C] *The same combined limit. I'll tell you if either correction begins taking more than you've agreed.*

He told Yuka.

She flexed her hand once. Ordinary fingers, ordinary movement, and then the little change in the space she could reach through the loan.

[Yk] "I'll say when mine starts."

[M] "Me too."

[G] "I'm about to give you a much faster way to say that. Watch the other person's hand."

They both looked at him.

[G] "And still say it. You can use two sources of information. It's extravagant, I know."

Umaru put her face behind the raised corner of the page for a moment.

* * *

The first sack crossed its mark.

Mau made the small pull beyond its side. The target turned. He could feel that the centre was weaker than when he worked alone, exactly as it had been at the trestles, and began to move it nearer the sack to keep the turn going.

Yuka's target came back from its far swing.

[Yk] "On."

Her Blue caught it late.

The sack was already going past the point she wanted. She drew the centre farther along the new line, trying to ask for a little more while Mau was doing the same thing on his side.

The route tightened across him.

[C] *Both increasing. Stop.*

[M] "Off."

He let his centre go. Yuka heard the change in his voice and closed hers instead of using the margin his release had just left.

The sacks swung past both hoops.

Gojo let them finish travelling before catching the ropes from outside their paths.

Nobody had hit the ground. Nothing had become large.

Mau lowered his hand and discovered how tightly he had been holding the other one.

* * *

Umaru came over only after Gojo had stopped the targets.

[U] "What happened?"

[M] "We both asked for more."

[Yk] "I started late. I tried to pull it back into the turn."

[M] "I saw mine wasn't coming far enough and tried to keep it."

Gojo looked down at the nearest hoop.

[G] "Both quite reasonable things to want."

Mau watched his face.

[M] "But?"

[G] "No but. You wanted more. You didn't have more together. Now tell me which part you can change without growing a second you."

Yuka crouched beside her hoop. Mau stayed where he was, letting the brief tightness behind his eyes settle before he tried to look along another line.

[Yk] "Start before it gets there."

[M] "Make it travel less."

[G] "There you are. Both of those exist."

Umaru moved the flask off the page to write. The paper lifted at once. She caught it with her elbow and gave Gojo an irritated look which he had done nothing to deserve.

He smiled as if he had.

* * *

They rested before changing the trial.

Yuka sat beside Mau, leaving his left hand where it had settled against his leg. She handed him the water with her right, waited while he drank and took it back without turning the exchange into a check he had to pass for her.

Umaru stood by the stump.

[U] "I wrote *both increased*. Not *you used too much*."

Mau looked up.

[M] "I did use too much for what we had together."

[U] "Yes. So did she."

Yuka nodded.

[Yk] "I know."

Umaru looked down at the page again. There were several other things she might have meant by making the distinction. None of them had to become a conversation here with Gojo holding two sack ropes and waiting for her to finish.

[U] "I'm recording the bit that happened."

She put the flask back on the corner.

Mau thanked her. She did not tell him not to.

* * *

Gojo shortened the first swing.

Not the rope. He simply sent the sack from a nearer starting point. He made Yuka choose the place where hers would begin too, then asked them both to stand back and watch the targets travel with no technique in either hand.

The motion was less impressive.

It was also easier to see where a small correction would matter.

Mau moved his Blue mark earlier along the path. Yuka moved hers nearer the first half of the return rather than the place where she had tried to rescue the turn.

[Yk] "I don't need it to come all the way back if I start there."

[M] "And I can let mine travel after it's turned."

[G] "You're both becoming shockingly willing to allow ordinary movement."

Mau made the gesture once without forming a centre. Low hand. A small place beyond the target's side. He turned his head enough to put the marked section comfortably inside the part of his sight which stayed clear.

Gojo watched him make that adjustment. Did not ask him to pretend the other edge was improving.

* * *

On the next attempt, Yuka began first.

Her little Blue drew the sack toward the start of the turn. Mau saw her raised hand before he heard the word, and made his own centre smaller than the one he had tried to hold against the target's weight.

For a short moment both outlets worked.

Different places. Different angles. The demand met in the body which was letting both happen.

Mau did not try to make his centre look like a whole Blue merely because Gojo was watching it.

His sack turned.

Yuka's passed over the outer edge of her hoop. She let go at once.

[Yk] "Clear."

The spare margin returned. Mau could feel it.

He left it spare.

His target was already moving where it needed to go. He closed his own centre before it reached the hoop and watched the bottom of the sack cross above it on the motion he had given it.

[M] "Clear."

Gojo caught neither rope until both had begun to come back.

* * *

[G] "That one."

Yuka looked at Mau. His face had the uncertain expression of somebody who knew he had done the thing and was waiting to discover which entirely different thing his teacher had actually been asking for.

[M] "Both?"

[G] "Both. Less help, earlier. You stopped trying to win the last half of the swing."

Umaru went to look at the ground beneath the targets, then at the two little marks where the centres had been placed.

[U] "They crossed. I saw them."

Yuka put a hand against her own mouth. She was smiling, unexpectedly and without having to coax it there.

[Yk] "I saw yours."

[M] "I saw yours too."

[G] "A touching account of two bags passing above some string."

Mau laughed.

For a moment Umaru looked as if she was going to join him. Instead she bent to pick up the nearer hoop and began coiling it round her fingers.

He let the moment be what it had been.

* * *

They did not add Red to the exercise.

Gojo said it before either pupil asked, which made Yuka look offended and Mau look too innocent to be believed. The small push had a different cost. It did not belong in a trial simply because two pulls had finally obeyed them while something moved.

Ciel agreed. Privately, and then in the part Mau repeated aloud.

[C] *The warning arrived before you forced the route past the cap. Keep that result too.*

Umaru wrote it beneath the failed attempt.

Gojo took one sack down. Yuka reached for the other rope, then looked at Mau.

[Yk] "Can you hold this end?"

He took it with his right hand while she untied the knot above.

The weight settled into his palm. Completely ordinary. More than enough to make him glad she warned him before letting it drop.

When they had put everything away, Umaru carried the page back herself.

Mau and Yuka followed with one hoop each.
