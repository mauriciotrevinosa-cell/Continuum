# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 20 — A Smaller Fold

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S6E51
**Continuity state:** Day472 late morning. In the clear practice field, Mau/Gojo with Shoko present and private Ciel/Noise test a narrow release of the rough sky-grab already built and used in S6E08 and disastrously broad when loaned in RedGate2. This is refinement of an existing construction, not acquisition of Uro's whole kit or domain. A loose strip is dropped from a support outside the affected patch, showing a small bend and return; no projectile is fired at a person. A brief pressure warning ends the test. No injury, continuous RCT, unfiltered eyes, healed fingers, new stat total or Ryu unlock.57 points held,14 named/40 and98 soldiers unchanged. *When: Day472, late morning. The practice field. Gojo is holding up a strip of pale cloth and refusing to call it impressive.*

[G] "It falls down."

[M] "That is generally the plan."

[G] "You said I was going to see something."

[M] "When we start."

Shoko took the strip from Gojo before the conversation could acquire another turn. She tied its upper end loosely round the small support Mau had set beyond the marked patch. The free end hung where the wind could reach it without a person needing to put a hand inside the part they intended to change.

[Sh] "There. A very advanced instrument."

Gojo looked at her.

[G] "I was holding it."

[Sh] "You were talking to it."

Mau checked the ground beyond the strip. Clear. Nothing to bring sideways except a little hanging cloth and the patch of ordinary air they meant to ask it to fall through.

He had left the katana at home.

* * *

The old notes had been clear about one thing.

He had done this before.

There was a movement in his hands which belonged to the moment on the frozen field. The woman reaching above herself, a piece of the sky coming down as if somebody had taken hold of a cloth too large for the table beneath it.

He could find the copied motion more easily than the face of the person who had taught it to Noise by trying to use it on him.

Later, inside the gate, Yuka had borrowed it once. Rough enough that he had closed the loan before the desert could become whatever her hand had nearly made it.

Those were not reasons to open the whole thing here.

[C] *The construction exists. We need to know whether its beginning can be released without the rest following the gesture.*

[M] *And if it can't?*

[C] *We close it. That is an answer too.*

Mau told the others.

Shoko looked at the marked patch, then at the places they had chosen to stand.

[Sh] "And we don't prove the answer twice because the first time was disappointing."

[M] "That sounds very specific."

[Sh] "It's a growing collection."

* * *

Gojo asked him to make the movement with no technique in it.

Mau reached up with his right hand. Closed it. Drew down and across, the motion he remembered making when there had been bolts coming at him and no time to ask whether an untidy copy would agree to help.

Gojo watched.

[G] "You begin with the whole field."

[M] "I'm not trying to."

[G] "Your hand is. Look at where you tell it to end."

Mau looked along his own arm.

The gesture had gone past the cloth. Past the small patch. Finished where the old movement expected the attack to have been turned away.

He let his hand fall.

[M] "I don't need the end of that one."

[G] "Not unless you want to rearrange a much larger piece of this morning."

Mau tried a smaller movement. Not a weak version of hauling the whole sky down. A little hold, close to the place he meant to reach.

Gojo watched him do it again before nodding.

* * *

Noise found the pattern behind the wall.

Mau felt the familiar impulse at once. Not a memory of being loved or of coming home. A built thing waiting for the body which had once needed it badly enough to reach without asking.

There was more of it than he wanted.

[M] *Only this part.*

He showed the little reach he had just practised. Ciel followed the boundary rather than asking Noise to send the whole construction up and hoping Mau could stop it once it arrived.

The answer took time.

[N] *This?*

A narrow beginning. Still awkward, the intention behind it larger than the motion he was trying to make.

[C] *That edge. Hold the rest there.*

Mau kept his ordinary eyes on the cloth.

The left edge stayed thin. He did not open his sight farther to compensate. Turned his head until the support and the patch sat comfortably in front of him.

[M] "Ready. Small part."

Gojo moved farther outside the marked patch. Shoko remained beside him.

* * *

The first attempt moved the cloth.

It fluttered. Turned toward Mau's hand. Fell back against the support.

He let go.

For a moment he could have decided that the motion was proof. His fingers had closed, something had answered, and the pale strip had moved where all of them could see it.

Gojo waited.

[M] "Wind?"

[G] "Maybe."

Shoko looked toward the grass outside the patch. It was moving too.

[Sh] "Bad instrument?"

[G] "Very advanced. Needs an advanced question."

Mau lowered his hand completely.

[M] "We need to see the line change. Not just the cloth move."

Gojo nodded.

They waited for the breeze to ease instead of making the next attempt larger so it would become easier to claim a result.

* * *

Shoko untied the loose end and folded the strip over the support.

One end on each side. A small movement from outside the patch would let it slide down through the place Mau intended to hold. She stood back and tested it once while nothing was open.

The cloth fell straight.

Gojo picked it up.

[G] "Still its best trick."

He laid it over the support again. Moved away.

Mau looked at the middle of the patch rather than following the whole length of the cloth. Reached toward the beginning Noise had kept ready, and put the short hold where his hand meant it to be.

Shoko freed the strip from beyond the affected edge.

It fell.

Then the middle of its path bent sideways.

Not swept away on a gust. For an instant the little length which should have hung beneath the support appeared along a different angle, as if the space it was crossing had been creased. Its lower end followed the turn.

Mau loosened his hand.

The line became ordinary again. The strip finished its fall in a small untidy heap.

He could hear his own breathing.

[G] "That wasn't wind."

* * *

Mau did not reach for another fold.

There was a small weight behind his left eye. Not the sharp edge from the garden, not blood or a new loss of sight, but a demand which had not been there before he asked for the copied construction.

He told Ciel before she finished asking.

[C] *Leave it closed.*

Noise did not try to complete the larger gesture.

The pattern eased back. Mau opened his right hand, looked at the marked patch and waited until the field stopped seeming as if it might have another direction concealed under it.

[M] "That's enough."

Shoko came closer only after he said the route was closed.

[Sh] "Which part?"

[M] "Pressure. Small. Same side. Not getting sharper."

She had him sit on the low stump. Asked him to look at ordinary things in the part of his sight which was already difficult before they came out.

No improvement to claim. No new change he could describe.

Gojo picked up the strip while they talked.

* * *

They left the support standing until Mau could look at it without wanting to test the motion again.

Shoko asked what had made him want this one today.

He could have said it was on the page. That they needed to know which of the things Noise had built could fit through the body he had now, and which had only worked when an emergency made refusing to try more dangerous than trying.

Those things were true.

[M] "It turned something away without needing me in front of it."

Gojo looked up from the knot in the cloth.

Mau watched him understand which afternoon had made the difference important.

[M] "I know this doesn't mean I can use it like that yet."

[G] "Good. Because I'd like you to know before somebody's arm is the next place in the line."

The words were blunt. Mau was grateful they had not become a joke.

[Sh] "Today's result is a strip of cloth. And a warning you listened to."

He nodded.

[M] "It can stay that size today."

* * *

Before they went back, Gojo asked him to describe what the fold had felt like.

Mau tried twice. The first answer sounded too much like pushing something. The second was closer to taking hold of the path a thing was falling through and giving that path a different angle.

Gojo listened without saying the two techniques were the same merely because both men had done something to space.

[G] "Do you know how much of what she did you've got?"

[M] "The rough piece that did that. Not the rest of her life."

[G] "Good answer. Keep it when you get a better piece."

Shoko folded the strip round the little support so neither could be left behind. Mau picked up the two ground markers.

His left fingers would not close round both. He moved one to the other hand instead of waiting for a morning which had not happened.

At the edge of the field, Gojo asked whether the whole sky had survived.

Shoko looked up with extravagant care.

[Sh] "So far."

Mau laughed, and carried the markers home.
