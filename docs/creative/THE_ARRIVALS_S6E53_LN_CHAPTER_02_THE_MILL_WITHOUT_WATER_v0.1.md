*When: Day474, morning. Inside the normal gate. There is a mill wheel, a channel beneath it, and no water to explain either.*

[A] "I preferred it when the strange part waited until we went farther in."

Yuka looked along the empty channel.

The stones were dry. Not damp with something they could not see. Dust had collected in the joints, and a little heap of it stood beneath the wheel where one blade had broken off and fallen years before anybody came here to ask what the place was for.

[M] "Maybe it used to work."

[A] "That's the optimistic version of many things."

Igris stood a few paces behind them, clear of the doorway. Mau had called him after entry. An extra guard, not a way to avoid deciding how far three people intended to go before lunch.

They had told Rimuru before leaving. The first mill building, the yard if it was clear, then back. No deeper branch simply because somebody found a path which looked interesting.

Yuka had repeated the last part herself.

Now she looked at the wheel.

[Yk] "That side's less broken."

Anko looked where she pointed.

[A] "Of course it is."

* * *

The first guardian came out of the dust beside the channel.

Not from under the wheel. From the heap which Mau had been trying to understand as a piece of old machinery. It opened along one side, put three short legs against the stone and turned a smooth plated head toward them.

Yuka's rope was already in her hand.

The thing folded.

Its legs disappeared under the plates. The body became a low, heavy curve, and then it was moving down the shallow channel far faster than its short steps had suggested it could move.

[Yk] "Rolling."

[A] "Noted."

Anko stepped clear of the channel. Mau and Yuka moved onto the wide raised edge rather than waiting to find out how much stone the body could break on its way past.

It struck the curve at the far end, rolled up the bank and came back.

Behind it, a second heap unfolded.

Igris moved to keep the return path clear.

* * *

There was room to fight.

There was also a broken floor beyond the wheel, and no reason to let a good opening persuade them to step onto it. Anko pointed it out before either pupil had to discover the loose stones under a moving foot.

[A] "Stay this side."

Yuka nodded. Mau watched the first guardian come through the turn again.

It wanted the channel. Used the bend to come back toward whatever still stood nearest its original heap. On the straight section it could not change direction without opening the little legs beneath the plates.

[M] "The outside of the turn."

Yuka looked along the bank.

[Yk] "Start it early."

It was not an invitation to make something larger. The words had a field, two sacks and a shared mistake behind them now.

Mau reached for the narrow loan.

[C] *Same combined margin. Don't increase to rescue the turn.*

He passed that part aloud. Anko looked at the two guardians rather than at his invisible conversation, waiting for the useful instruction she could actually hear.

[A] "What do you need me to do?"

[M] "Keep the loose bit clear. We'll bring one up that bank."

She moved to where the broken floor began. Not into the line of the rolling bodies. Put her foot against a loose piece which would otherwise have tipped when they came back toward the doorway.

* * *

Yuka caught the first guardian before the bend.

Her little Blue sat beyond its outside edge, drawing enough to change the beginning of the turn. The plated body rose where the bank was lower, short legs appearing in surprise as it lost the channel it had been using to make its decisions.

She let the centre close.

[Yk] "Clear."

It had more weight than the practice sack. She had not tried to make that weight disappear. It rolled partway up the bank, opened the legs and scrabbled for something to push against.

Igris was there when it exposed the narrow line beneath its head.

One clean strike.

The body unfolded into plates and dust. Something small and hard struck the stone beneath it.

Mau had watched the second guardian while she worked.

It came through sooner than he wanted.

He left it in the channel.

* * *

[A] "Didn't like that one?"

[M] "Wrong place."

Anko watched the guardian take the bend without finding anything to hit.

[A] "It's willing to come back. Very considerate."

Yuka stood beside the raised stone, breathing a little faster. The first body had made a sharp crack when its plates came apart. She had looked toward the sound too quickly and needed another moment before the room became only the room again.

Mau saw her hand against her sleeve.

[M] "Do you want to stop?"

She looked at the empty stretch beyond the channel.

[Yk] "One more turn. If we don't get it, we leave it to Igris."

[Ig] "As you wish, my lady."

The knight had already chosen where to stand if their plan failed.

Nobody needed to make a speech about why that was reassuring.

* * *

The second attempt needed both outlets.

Mau used the earlier part of the straight to start the body away from the deepest groove. A small correction, then no correction, letting the weight continue toward the place where Yuka waited with room beside her.

Her centre came up while his was still doing the last of its work.

For a brief moment they shared the demand.

Neither centre grew to a whole separate pull. Neither had to. The guardian was already coming toward the higher side of the bend.

Mau let his close.

[M] "Clear."

Yuka kept hers only until the nearest plates began to tilt. Then she let it go too, moved back from the bank and cast the rope round the short leg which came out looking for a foothold.

She did not try to hold its whole weight by herself.

Mau caught the slack with his right hand and moved with her, enough to keep the exposed side turned toward Igris instead of hauling the creature across the floor.

The knight stepped in.

Afterward the channel was empty again.

* * *

They waited before collecting anything.

Mau closed the loan. Told Ciel the ordinary sensations first, without reaching back to test whether he could have taken more. Work behind the eyes, but no sharp pressure. Left hand stiff from balancing, not a new injury.

Yuka put the rope down while she loosened her grip.

Anko looked at the broken floor, then at the deeper opening behind the mill wheel.

[A] "Not today."

Mau nodded.

[M] "We said the first building."

[A] "I enjoy discovering that people meant what they said before an entrance."

They found two crystals in the remains. Not a hidden chest, not an army to raise. Two hard pieces which went into the little cloth bag with the other things they intended to carry home, keeping dust away from the food.

Yuka wanted a better look at the wheel.

Anko inspected the standing edge first. Let her look from there, without anybody climbing onto the broken parts.

[Yk] "It really did turn. Look at the groove."

Mau came beside her.

The lower blade had worn a line into the stone. Something had moved here for a long time before the channel became dry.

[M] "Maybe you're right."

[A] "A historic moment. We've established the purpose of a wheel."

* * *

They took ten minutes in the yard before leaving.

Igris kept the entrance behind them in view. Anko sat on a low stone with her food and made no pretence that her looking the other way would make the yard into a private world for the other two.

Yuka did not need it to.

She sat beside Mau, close enough for their shoulders to touch, and untied the cloth round their portions. There was dust on his sleeve. She brushed it off, then stopped when another little line appeared from beneath the fold.

[Yk] "You're bringing the mill."

[M] "Only the loose parts."

She laughed. Handed him the food before he could find another explanation.

For a while they ate without discussing what it would feel like to go home.

It was not that she had forgotten. She could remember the staff whenever a hard sound came too suddenly. She could picture the corridor, her grandparents looking up when she entered, the cushion outside the Fort curtain.

The yard still had sunlight on one side. Mau had eaten too quickly and was trying to deny it while Anko looked directly at the crumbs on his knee.

Yuka leaned her head briefly against his shoulder.

[Yk] "I liked doing that with you."

He turned toward her.

[M] "Me too."

She kissed the side of his mouth. Briefly, without making him turn away from the person who was sitting nearby.

Anko kept eating.

* * *

They were back before lunch.

Rimuru met them near the table. Anko gave the short version while Mau untied the crystal bag where all three could see it.

Dry mill. Two guardians. Broken floor beyond the wheel. Deeper way not explored. No injury. Two crystals.

Yuka added the part about the turn: both outlets for a short overlap, no attempt to increase when the second guardian came at the wrong angle. Igris had finished both.

Rimuru looked at the knight before Mau sent him back.

[R] "Thank you."

Igris inclined his head.

The room had begun filling with people who needed things from the kitchen. Yuka looked at the place she meant to sit, then at Mau.

He did not have to pretend he had forgotten what coming back asked of her.

[M] "I'll wash first. Then there."

She nodded. Put the rope beside her coat rather than leaving it tangled round the bag.

When he came back, she had kept the place beside her clear.
