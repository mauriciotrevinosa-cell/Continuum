# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 28 — Twenty-Four Hours

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 28
**Source episode:** S6E55

Frieren/Fern/Ori have not attended and must still be asked about arrangements affecting them. The Goddess possibility is shared only as25–27 stated it; full terms remain unknown. Yuka's continuing fear, strained meals and friendship obligations prompt a temporary separation-of-routines proposal, not expulsion or forced treatment. If no workable consensual answer, the household will proceed with separate common-room/meal arrangements after asking the affected people, instead of waiting indefinitely. Immediate care and separation already in place do not lapse during the24 hours. No order to manufacture love, stop loving, surrender autonomy or accept divine intervention. Mau's agreement in26 to bring back terms stands. No deal, rewrite, new injury or power occurs.

*When: Day475, early afternoon. One end of the long table. The lunch things have been cleared, and nobody has put a new pot down to make this look like an ordinary visit.*

Yuka chose the seat beside Mau.

She had hesitated before doing it. Not because she wanted a different place, but because she could see her grandmother notice the moment of hesitation.

Maki did not comment. Sat across from them with Yuta beside her.

Rimuru waited until Shoko came back from the kitchen end. Gojo leaned against the wall rather than taking the chair which would have blocked the way out. Anko sat where she could see both doors without making it seem as if she intended to stop anyone using them.

Mau looked at the empty middle of the table.

For a moment he wished they had left the lunch things there.

[R] "This is about how we live while we find out what happens next. It isn't a vote on what either of you is allowed to feel."

Yuka's hand moved toward her wrist, stopped and settled beside Mau's instead.

[R] "And the people who aren't here don't lose their say because we're the ones sitting down first."

Maki nodded once.

Then she looked at her granddaughter.

* * *

[Mk] "Tell us the part you told me this morning."

Yuka looked at the table.

Mau had not heard it. She could see him turn slightly toward her without asking why she had gone to someone else first.

[Yk] "I keep listening before I leave my room."

The words came out smaller here than they had beside her grandmother.

[Yk] "I want to know who's in the corridor. Sometimes I can hear somebody and I wait until they've gone before opening the door. Even when it isn't her."

Shoko sat down slowly.

[Yk] "I know she hasn't come looking for me. I know you've been with her. It doesn't stop me doing it."

Mau looked at the doorway behind her.

He had walked along that corridor beside her, watched her pause and waited for her to decide whether she wanted him to take her hand. He had thought giving her time was the part he knew how to do.

He had not known how many times the pause came before he arrived.

[Yk] "I don't want everyone taking turns checking a room before I go in. I don't want to ask whether I can have lunch."

[Yt] "You don't have to ask that."

[Yk] "I know. That's the part I keep knowing."

Yuta stopped.

She was grateful he did not repeat it more gently as if the gentler version would become something her body could believe.

* * *

[Sh] "She told us she won't hurt you again."

Maki's expression did not soften.

[Sh] "I'm telling you what she said. I'm not asking that to be enough for you to stop being frightened."

Yuka nodded.

[G] "And she hasn't been left to work this out alone. That continues. Whatever we decide about the next day."

Mau looked at him.

Gojo's face was unusually plain. No joking accusation that his pupils kept finding more difficult ways to make him useful.

[Mk] "Good."

She looked back at Yuka.

[Mk] "But Yuka doesn't get to become smaller in the house until everybody else feels better."

The sentence made Yuka's eyes fill before she could prepare for it.

[Mk] "You can need distance. You can choose it. It isn't something you're being sent away to provide."

[Yk] "I don't want to leave."

[Mk] "Then that's where we start."

Yuta put his hand on the table beside hers. Did not take her away from the other hand she was already holding.

* * *

Anko had been listening without interrupting.

Now she leaned forward, elbows on the table, and looked at Rimuru.

[A] "We can't keep improvising every meal. Someone asks whether somebody else has come down. Someone carries a plate up and says not to worry about it. Everyone worries about what happens if they stop asking."

Rimuru nodded.

[A] "The people bringing the plates have lives too. They don't want to stop helping. That doesn't mean there should be no end to the thing they're helping us postpone."

Shoko glanced at the stair.

[Sh] "Fern needs to be able to leave the room without thinking she has abandoned somebody. Ori needs somewhere to be a daughter without having to check which parent the room is protecting."

Mau's hand tightened round Yuka's.

She pressed back once. Not to stop the words.

[R] "So we make arrangements people can understand. For a few days at first. Different times in the shared rooms if that's what they want. Meals which don't depend on someone finding out at the last moment who's afraid to come down. Company by agreement, not whoever happens to be left standing in the corridor."

[Yk] "Do we get asked?"

[R] "Yes. Both sides. And the people doing the work. This is the proposal, not the announcement that you've all agreed."

She looked at Maki. Her grandmother nodded.

* * *

Mau had been trying not to interrupt with the answer which had seemed so large at the stone.

Now he heard how small it would sound laid beside what Yuka had just told them.

[M] "I asked her because I don't want this to be what we become."

Maki looked at him.

[M] "The Goddess. I didn't ask because I wanted to find a way to make everyone stop telling me they're hurt. I want to remember. I want to find something which doesn't keep making the same people wait."

[Mk] "Do you have the conditions?"

[M] "Not yet."

[Mk] "Then we still need to eat tonight."

He looked down.

The words were not cruel. There was a part of him which would have preferred it if they had been, because then he could have put them somewhere other than beside his own hope.

[M] "I know."

[Mk] "I know you do. I'm saying we can't turn the possibility into another thing which means nobody makes a decision."

Rimuru looked at Mau before adding anything.

[R] "We can keep asking what restoration would mean and still arrange tomorrow. Those aren't opposite choices."

* * *

Yuta spoke quietly.

[Yt] "I don't want a rota which looks like the house has two families who aren't allowed to meet."

Maki looked at him.

[Yt] "I also don't want to tell her we won't do it because it would make me sad to see it on paper."

Yuka covered her mouth with her free hand.

Mau watched Yuta wait for her to put it down again.

The man had loved him through years he could not find properly. Had thanked him for standing in front of the spell without taking back the anger about what came before it. Now he was discussing which parts of the day needed separating so his granddaughter could stop listening at her own door.

There was no accusation in Yuta's face which Mau could argue with.

[M] "Give me a day."

Rimuru went still.

Maki looked directly at him.

[Mk] "To do what?"

* * *

[M] "Get an actual answer. What she can return. What she'd ask for. Whether there's something we can decide instead of waiting with a word somebody said in a field."

[R] "We talked about bringing the terms back."

[M] "I know."

[R] "That still stands."

Mau nodded.

Yuka looked at his face. He did not look like someone who already knew what he would be asked to carry. He looked tired of watching the space between all the people he knew become the thing they had to make plans for.

[M] "I'm not asking you to leave things unsafe for a day. Keep doing what you're doing now. I just want a chance to come back with more than yes."

Shoko looked at Maki, then at Rimuru.

Nobody needed to make the time mysterious. An ordinary day. Tonight, a morning, this hour again.

[Mk] "Twenty-four hours."

Mau nodded once.

[Mk] "Until this time tomorrow. If there isn't something we can actually decide together, we go ahead with the arrangements. We stop putting off how we live because you might find an answer next."

She held his gaze.

[Mk] "Not making you love somebody. Not making you stop. Taking care of the people who are here while you work out what can come back."

* * *

Yuka wiped beneath her eyes.

[Yk] "I don't want everybody doing this because I'm frightened."

Maki's expression changed.

[Mk] "You're allowed to be frightened after someone tries to hurt you."

[Yk] "I know."

The answer broke halfway through.

Mau turned his hand beneath hers so she could hold it without having to press her fingers between his.

Maki watched them. When she spoke again, she looked at him.

[Mk] "What Frieren did was her choice. You didn't put the spell in her hand."

He did not look away.

[Mk] "But you did let us keep waiting for a thing you weren't telling us you couldn't give. Both of you did. Don't make the answer to that more waiting without words."

Yuka nodded with her face turned down.

[M] "I won't tell you this is fixed when it isn't."

[A] "Good. That's a useful place to begin."

* * *

They still had to arrange tonight.

Shoko offered to speak with Frieren and Fern about the shared spaces, without presenting the discussion as something they had voted for in their absence. Rimuru would make sure Rem and Momo had a say in any meal arrangement, rather than receiving a plan somebody else expected them to carry out.

Yuta asked Yuka whether she wanted to eat downstairs. She did. Maki said they would be with her if she wanted that too.

Gojo did not volunteer Fern for the rest of the evening. He asked Shoko when she wanted him to come by instead.

Small decisions. None of them gave the house back the shape it had before.

Mau remained seated when the others began to move. Yuka stayed beside him.

Rimuru stopped at the end of the table.

[R] "A day to bring back an answer. Not permission to take the price before we've heard it."

Mau looked up at him.

[M] "I heard you."

Rimuru waited a moment longer before going to find Rem.

* * *

The middle of the table was empty again.

Yuka kept her hand in Mau's. Her face was dry now, though she could feel the places where tears had cooled against her skin.

[Yk] "I wanted one day where we didn't have to think about what coming home would do to everybody."

Mau looked at her.

[Yk] "I know that isn't what you asked for."

He looked toward the stair. At the clear way through the room. The ordinary afternoon continuing after they had put a time beside something which had felt endless.

[M] "We still have the afternoon."

She did not smile.

[Yk] "What would you want to do?"

He turned back to her. For the first time since they sat down, he let himself think about the answer without trying to make it useful to everybody else.
