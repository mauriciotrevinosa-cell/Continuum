# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 29 — Two Cups and a Mountain

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 29
**Source episode:** S6E56
**Continuity state:** Day475 early afternoon, immediately after28. Mau proposes an outing to a relatively nearby low northern mountainside in the real world, and Yuka chooses to go. Rimuru recognises the intended visible shoulder but does not invent a previously walked route. They prepare ordinary food, water, cups, coats and a blanket; report the destination and return that same night before midnight directly to Rimuru and Maki/Yuta. They will return earlier for worsening cold or visibility. Rimuru remains home. Igris is an existing registered guard to be called upon landing; Kaisel provides both journeys. Ciel may relay arrival privately by Mau's choice, not new omniscient surveillance or a portal. Mau explicitly intends to ask for the Goddess's terms after returning; no terms, bargain, acceptance, cure or guaranteed resolution is known. The24h deadline remains Day476 at the same early-afternoon hour. No gate entry/reset, power gain, point spending, cancer disclosure or sex. This chapter ends at takeoff from the agreed clear ground by the house.

*When: Day475, early afternoon. The long table, after the others have begun to leave.*

[M] "Would you like to go up a mountain?"

Yuka looked at him.

Then at the doorway through which her grandmother had just gone.

[Yk] "Now?"

[M] "A small part of one. I wasn't planning to climb it."

She turned back. He had meant the question, though he was beginning to hear how it sounded after asking for a day in which to bring everyone a useful answer.

[Yk] "Which part?"

He pointed north, through a wall which made the gesture very little help.

[M] "The low ridge you can see from upstairs. There's a shoulder below the bare part. Kaisel could take us. We could sit somewhere that isn't a gate."

She looked in the direction of his hand anyway.

[Yk] "And do what?"

[M] "I thought we might get there before deciding."

Her mouth moved. Not quite a smile yet.

[Yk] "That is a very bad expedition plan."

[M] "Then we shouldn't call it one."

He let her have time to think. Her fingers were still between his, warm where their hands rested on the table. She could say she wanted to stay. He was prepared for that answer until he realised how much he wanted the other one.

[Yk] "I'd like to see it."

He looked at her properly.

[Yk] "But I'm bringing food. I don't want you pointing at a mountain again when I ask what there is to eat."

This time she smiled.

* * *

They had to let go to pack.

Yuka went up for her coat while Mau went to ask what could be taken from the kitchen without borrowing somebody else's supper. He came back with a cloth bundle of savoury cakes, two apples and a warning to keep the bundle flat.

He put it down carefully.

Then set the water flask on it.

[Yk] "That's not keeping it flat. That's making it flat."

He lifted the flask again.

She had returned with her coat over one arm and a blanket over the other. Mau tried to take both before discovering he still had the flask in his right hand and had caught the blanket with fingers which did not want to close properly.

The folded edge slid through them.

Yuka caught it against her hip.

[Yk] "I'll put it in."

[M] "I can carry something."

[Yk] "You can carry the water. You were doing very well with that before you tried to put it through dinner."

He moved the cakes to the other end of the table.

She opened the bag and made a place for the blanket first. Food above it, cups wrapped separately so they would not knock together all the way there. When she tried to fit her coat in too, the opening refused to close.

Mau watched her press it down.

[M] "You could wear it."

[Yk] "I know."

She pressed once more, then pulled the coat out and put it on with an expression which discouraged him from saying anything else.

He found his own coat upstairs.

For a moment, alone with one arm in the sleeve, he thought of the paper on Rimuru's desk. The questions beneath another page. They had not become less important because Yuka had smiled at him.

He finished dressing and went back down.

She was tying the top of the bag. The flower at her wrist turned inward against the cloth.

[Yk] "Are you going to ask her up there?"

He stopped beside the table.

[M] "No. When we come back."

Yuka pulled the knot snug.

[M] "I want to go with you. Then I want to ask what the conditions actually are. I don't know them yet."

She nodded, but kept her eyes on the bag.

[Yk] "I wasn't asking you to put it off."

[M] "I know."

He waited until she looked at him.

[M] "Do you still want to come?"

She took his right hand.

[Yk] "Yes. I want an afternoon with you. I can want that and still be frightened about after it."

He lifted her hand and kissed the knuckles. She kept hold of him when he lowered it again.

* * *

Rimuru was near the kitchen end, speaking to Rem.

Mau waited instead of trying to turn their conversation into something which could be interrupted because he had finally decided where to go. Yuka leaned against the table beside him. The bag rested between her feet.

Rem finished what she was saying. Rimuru answered her, then came over.

His eyes went from the bag to their coats.

[R] "Where?"

Mau explained about the ridge.

Rimuru took them as far as a window from which the low northern shoulder could actually be identified. There was more than one bare patch among the slopes; Mau had been looking at the one below and to the west of the place Rimuru first indicated.

[M] "That one. Below the higher rock."

Rimuru leaned to see past the frame.

[R] "I know where you mean. The broad shoulder. Don't mistake seeing it from here for knowing what the ground behind it is like."

[Yk] "We're not trying to get over the top."

[M] "We'll look before we land. Igris can check the ground while we stay by Kaisel. If it isn't somewhere we can sit safely, we'll choose somewhere nearer or come back."

Rimuru considered that. Asked about the return.

[M] "Tonight. Before midnight at the latest. Earlier if the weather changes or we can't see the route clearly."

[R] "You have to turn around while you can still see it. Not after you've proved you can't."

Mau nodded.

[Yk] "We won't be here for supper. I know I just said I'd eat downstairs. Please don't have anyone keep plates waiting."

[R] "Tell Maki and Yuta yourselves. I haven't had the meal conversation yet. I'll tell Rem which part has changed."

Yuka shifted the strap on her shoulder.

Rimuru looked at Mau again.

[R] "And this isn't the walk to ask your questions."

[M] "No. I'll do that when I'm back."

[R] "Then bring back what she says."

[M] "I will."

Rimuru did not tell him to have a wonderful time. Mau could see that he wanted them to have something better than the table had given them, and that wanting it had not made any of the other things easy to say.

[M] "You're staying here?"

Rimuru glanced at the bag.

[R] "I wasn't expecting to fit in there."

Yuka made a small sound into her sleeve.

[R] "Yes. I'll be here. Let us know when you've landed."

Mau asked Ciel privately whether she would relay their arrival to Rimuru.

[C] *Yes. Tell him if you decide on a different place as well.*

[M] *I will.*

He repeated the practical part aloud so Yuka would not be left agreeing to a plan which had only happened in his head.

* * *

They found Maki and Yuta by the stairs.

Yuka told them before Mau could begin. Where, with whom, how they would get back. She had her own reasons for going; she wanted her grandparents to hear those in her voice.

Maki listened without looking toward Rimuru to see whether he had given the correct answer first.

[Mk] "Outside the protection."

[Yk] "Yes. Not far into the mountains. The low shoulder north of here. Rimuru knows which one."

[Yt] "And if you get up there and don't like the look of it?"

[Yk] "We don't have to land."

Yuta nodded, then asked Mau whether he was taking a guard.

[M] "Igris. I'll call him when we land. Kaisel stays available for the way back. I'm not going to try to fly us myself."

Yuka looked sideways at him.

[Yk] "You'd have to carry the bag too."

[M] "That's the part that ruled it out."

Yuta's mouth almost moved. He looked at the bag, checking an ordinary thing because there were still ordinary things he could help with.

[Yt] "Water?"

She tipped it enough for him to see the flask.

[Yt] "All right."

Maki caught the edge of Yuka's collar where it had folded under the strap. Smoothed it out, then stopped with her fingers still on the cloth.

[Mk] "You're coming back tonight."

[Yk] "Before midnight. Earlier if it's colder or the route isn't clear. We told Rimuru."

[Mk] "Tell us when you're home. Don't decide everyone must be asleep and go straight upstairs."

[Yk] "I won't."

Maki let the collar go.

[Yk] "I do want to come home."

Her grandmother looked at her.

[Yk] "I'm not going because I don't."

[Mk] "I heard what you said at the table."

Yuka nodded too quickly. Looked down. Maki waited until she looked up again.

[Mk] "You can go somewhere you want to go. That doesn't change where your room is."

Yuta rested a hand briefly against her upper arm.

[Yt] "Take the cups out before you put the bag down hard."

She laughed, unexpectedly and a little wetly.

[Yk] "They're wrapped."

[Yt] "Then you're doing better than I did."

Maki looked at him.

[Mk] "Three."

[Yt] "I remember."

Yuka wanted to ask. Her grandmother was already beginning to walk away.

[Mk] "He'll tell you when you get back."

* * *

The clear ground beside the house had enough room for Kaisel to open both wings.

Mau checked it before calling him. Waited while someone carried a bucket across the path, then gave the dragon the space instead of expecting everyone to step around him.

Kaisel rose from the shadow and settled with his head turned toward the light.

Yuka took two steps closer.

[Yk] "You're taking us somewhere nice."

The dragon lowered his head. She touched him, smiling, then turned to discover Mau watching her.

[Yk] "I was talking to him."

[M] "I noticed."

He checked the bag's fastening once more. She moved his hand away before he could tighten a knot which was already tight enough to become tomorrow's problem.

Getting seated took an unhurried minute. Mau used his right hand for the awkward part; Yuka held the bag clear, then passed it over rather than trying to climb with it knocking against her knees.

When she settled behind him, she tugged his coat free from beneath her leg.

[M] "Thank you."

[Yk] "I was sitting on it. It wasn't a favour."

Her arms came round his waist.

He covered one hand with his. Beyond Kaisel's shoulder he could still see the open door, the path to the workshop, somebody turning back because they had forgotten what they came out to fetch.

Then Yuka leaned nearer his ear.

[Yk] "If we don't like the mountain, can we still eat the cakes?"

[M] "That depends. Is it a bad expedition or a bad lunch?"

She tightened her arms as Kaisel gathered himself beneath them.

[Yk] "It's a date. You have to feed me either way."

The first wingbeat lifted the loose end of her coat. She caught it against his side, laughing, and the ground fell gently away.
