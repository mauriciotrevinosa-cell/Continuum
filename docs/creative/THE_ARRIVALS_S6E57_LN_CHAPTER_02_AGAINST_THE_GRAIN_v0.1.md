*When: Day475, afternoon. The workshop side of the house. Yuta has brought in the wooden sword which has been lying apart from the others.*

The loose curl was gone. The place it had lifted from was not.

Yuta turned the grip toward the window. A shallow rough patch caught the light just above the worn edge of the wrapping. He had taken the loose wood off with his thumbnail eight days ago and told Mau they would smooth it before anybody used it again.

Then he had put it down.

Maki set a folded piece of canvas on the other end of the bench.

[Mk] "Is that the one?"

[Yt] "Yes."

She leaned closer without reaching across his hands.

[Mk] "How far?"

[Yt] "I'm looking."

He wiped the dust off first. Examined the wood where the wrapping had rubbed back, then the grain above and below the rough place. There was no long dark line running into the body of the grip. He turned it again to be sure.

Maki unfolded the canvas. Two wooden bars rolled out of it and nearly took the file with them.

Yuta caught the file.

[Yt] "Did you need this?"

[Mk] "Apparently. You hadn't picked it up."

She took one bar before he could offer to catch that too.

* * *

It was meant to carry firewood.

Maki had seen the pieces coming out of the kitchen basket through a gap in the weave. The basket was still useful for plenty of things. It was less useful when Rem had to go back along the path picking up the smallest pieces after carrying it inside.

The canvas would fold beneath a bundle. A bar at each end would make handles, and the whole thing could lie flat when nobody needed it.

That was the plan.

The first bar would not go into the fold.

Yuta kept his eyes on the sword.

[Yt] "Other way round."

[Mk] "I'm trying that."

[Yt] "I meant the cloth."

She stopped. Looked at the canvas. Unfolded the little section which had doubled back underneath itself.

The bar went through.

Yuta discovered that it was difficult to look completely occupied by a piece of wood while his wife looked at him from that close.

[Yt] "I didn't say anything."

[Mk] "You were about to."

[Yt] "I thought better of it."

[Mk] "Keep doing that."

She put the bar down and began securing the fold with a length of cord. Yuta laid a scrap of cloth beneath the sword and took up the fine file.

He made the first pass lightly.

Not across the whole grip. Just the edge which stood up when he turned it beneath the light. A little pale dust settled on the cloth.

He stopped to look again.

* * *

For several minutes their work made the conversation.

The scrape of the file. Cord being drawn through a hole in the canvas. Maki asking him to move his elbow, then keeping the space after he had moved it because she needed both hands to pull the knot tight.

Outside, somebody carried a bucket past the open door. Water struck the side twice, a familiar uneven rhythm.

Yuta looked up at the second sound.

Maki saw him look.

[Mk] "They've only just gone."

[Yt] "I know."

[Mk] "You can finish that before you start listening for them."

He drew the file away from the wood.

[Yt] "Are you going to?"

Maki's hands stopped against the knot.

Then she pulled it tight.

[Mk] "Probably not."

He looked down before she could see too much of his smile. She saw enough.

[Mk] "It's a mountain. I'm allowed."

[Yt] "A small part of one."

[Mk] "You sounded very pleased with his answer when he said that."

[Yt] "I liked the part where they didn't have to land."

Maki breathed out through her nose. Shifted the canvas toward him so he could keep one end still while she worked on the other.

He put the file down before helping.

She waited until his hands were clear of it. Neither needed to mention why.

* * *

The second knot sat badly.

Maki loosened it rather than pulling harder. Yuta watched the cord come free, then went back to the rough patch beside the wrapping.

[Yt] "I thought she'd ask to stay."

Maki did not look up.

[Mk] "At the table?"

[Yt] "When I asked about tonight. She said downstairs. I thought we'd eat together."

The cord lay between Maki's fingers.

[Mk] "So did I."

He checked the wood with his thumb, lightly, from the smooth side toward the rough.

[Yt] "I'm glad she wanted to go somewhere."

[Mk] "You can be disappointed about dinner."

[Yt] "I am."

The admission made her glance over.

[Yt] "I wanted to ask whether she'd rather do it tomorrow."

[Mk] "Tomorrow she might be waiting for something else."

He nodded. The file remained in his hand without moving.

Neither of them knew what Mau would bring back from the next conversation with the Goddess. Only that he had agreed to bring the words before taking what they asked of him.

Yuta looked at the open door.

[Yt] "I keep trying to get to the difficult bit before she does. Have an answer ready."

Maki's expression changed.

[Mk] "You had water ready."

He looked at her.

[Mk] "When we brought her in. You asked where she wanted to sit. Then you got the water. She needed both."

Yuta rested the wooden sword across his knees.

He remembered her hand over the shoulder where nothing had struck. The terrible care with which she had put her other hand into his, trying not to squeeze him too hard while she was shaking.

[Yt] "I wanted something larger to give her."

[Mk] "I know."

She put the cord down.

For a little while she sat beside him with nothing to pull tight.

* * *

[Yt] "How are you?"

Maki looked at him.

[Mk] "You were here this morning."

[Yt] "I was."

She waited for him to ask a better question. He let the one he had asked stay where it was.

Eventually she leaned back against the bench.

[Mk] "I want to stop seeing it every time I see her sleeve."

Yuta's face tightened.

[Mk] "Not forget it. I want to look at my granddaughter and see what she's actually doing. Not keep checking the place it would have hit."

He set the sword on the cloth.

[Yt] "When she showed me the water flask, I looked at her arm first."

Maki nodded once.

She had been close enough to see where the spell was aimed. He had received that part from her afterward. Neither position had spared them the picture.

[Mk] "I won't let it happen again."

[Yt] "I know what you meant."

She looked at him, then away toward the doorway.

[Mk] "I wish I hadn't needed to mean it."

Yuta moved nearer on the bench. Not far enough to crowd the work beneath her hands. Their shoulders touched.

Maki let hers stay there.

After a while she reached across his knee for the cord and carried on with the knot.

* * *

The carrier needed a test.

They took it outside to the split wood. Maki put too much in it on purpose. Yuta watched the ends of the canvas rather than telling her what she could plainly see.

[Mk] "Take that side."

They lifted together.

The canvas held. The wood held. The lower bar began sliding out of the fold.

Yuta put his end down first.

[Yt] "That part doesn't."

[Mk] "Helpful."

She lowered hers without dropping the bundle. Examined the end where the cord had dragged through the fold, then took three pieces of wood off the top.

[Yt] "That wasn't the problem."

[Mk] "I want them out of the way."

He picked up the loose bar. Held it while she changed the fastening, keeping the cord away from the worn edge this time.

When they lifted again, it stayed where she had put it.

Maki took both handles in one hand and walked a few paces. The weight swung against her leg. She stopped, looked at Yuta and shortened the hanging part before he could offer a comment.

[Yt] "I didn't say anything."

[Mk] "You're getting good at that."

She handed him the carrier.

He took it inside to set beside the kitchen wood, then returned with it empty. Maki was already putting the spare cord back through the canvas so nobody would have to search for it next time.

* * *

Yuta tried a clean piece of cloth across the grip.

One direction. Then the other, where the grain had caught Mau's thumb. A thread lifted.

He brought it back to the window and found the small edge he had missed.

Maki watched him make two short passes with the file.

[Mk] "Will you take him out tomorrow?"

He stopped without looking up.

[Yt] "I don't know yet."

[Mk] "All right."

She did not ask him whether tomorrow's answer from the Goddess would decide it.

He was grateful. He had been trying not to decide exactly that since picking up the sword.

[Yt] "I told him I'd say when I could."

[Mk] "Then tell him when you can."

Yuta wiped the dust away.

[Yt] "I still want to teach him."

[Mk] "I know."

[Yt] "I'm still angry."

She reached for the clean cloth and offered it back with the roughened corner folded out of the way.

[Mk] "I know that too."

She sat back, folding the spare canvas into a smaller square.

[Mk] "I'd like her to tell us she had a good time when she comes back."

Yuta looked at her.

[Mk] "Without watching my face first to see whether she should say it."

[Yt] "You can tell her that."

[Mk] "I intend to. She might still watch."

He nodded. There were things a sentence from them could not settle, even when Yuka believed every word.

[Yt] "And if she didn't?"

Maki put the canvas down.

[Mk] "Then I want her to tell us that too. I don't want her keeping it to herself because we might say we knew better."

[Yt] "We won't."

[Mk] "You might look as though you do."

[Yt] "I can't put my face on a shelf before she talks."

[Mk] "Practise. You've had years."

[Yt] "With you, it wouldn't have helped."

[Mk] "No."

He was wise enough to stop there.

He tested the wood again. This time the cloth passed without catching.

He checked the grip, turned the sword under the light and looked once more along the grain. Then he closed both hands round it. Changed the pressure gently. Nothing lifted, shifted or opened.

Maki moved back to give him room.

He made one slow turn of the wrist, keeping the wooden point low and away from her, and lowered it again.

* * *

He put the repaired sword beside the sound one.

Not outside in the practice space. Back against the wall, ready to be taken when somebody actually intended to use it.

Maki shook the pale dust off the cloth over the scrap box.

[Mk] "We could eat before they get back."

Yuta looked at her.

[Mk] "They told us not to wait."

[Yt] "I wasn't going to wait until midnight."

[Mk] "You were going to keep being nearly hungry until you heard them."

He considered denying it. She had known him for too long to make that a promising use of the afternoon.

[Yt] "What would you like?"

[Mk] "Food. Sitting down. You there."

His mouth softened.

[Yt] "Very demanding."

She picked a pale shaving off his sleeve. Flicked it into the box with the others.

[Mk] "And you're telling her the story about the cups."

[Yt] "I remember."

[Mk] "Properly."

He offered her his hand before opening the door. She took it, then pulled him back just far enough to kiss him.

When she let go, he was smiling at her.

[Mk] "Bring the carrier. I want to know whether Rem can use it."

Yuta went back for it.

She waited in the doorway, holding out her empty hand.
