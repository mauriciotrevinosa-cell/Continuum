## Chapter 33 — A Very Small Dragon

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 33
**Source episode:** S6E58

Mau/Yuka play with ordinary hand silhouettes in the low sunlight, enjoy closeness and imagine a next date which Yuka would arrange, without fixing a day or promising what restoration will mean. No cloud game/Tsurugi conversation repeated, no cancer, sex, terms, acceptance, complete memory return or new power. *When: Day475, late afternoon. The short stretch beyond the pool. The sheltered blanket is still within sight, and Yuka has reached the patch of sunlight first.*

Her shadow climbed the rock before Mau caught up.

It was a much larger Yuka, with the edge of her coat stretched into something which appeared to have a tail. When she turned to look for him, the shape turned too.

Mau stopped.

[M] "You've grown."

She looked at the rock.

Lifted one arm experimentally.

[Yk] "Finally."

He came nearer. His shadow crossed hers and swallowed most of the tail.

[Yk] "You broke it."

[M] "I'm sorry. I didn't see where it ended."

She took his wrist and moved him a step to the side. Then another, until he was standing in a small gap between two stones, looking at her with the patience of somebody who had not yet been told what the task was.

[Yk] "There."

She raised her hands.

A shape appeared against the pale face of the rock. Two pointed ears, a snout, something which might have been a very unfortunate lower jaw.

Mau regarded it.

[M] "Is it ill?"

She opened the jaw at him.

* * *

Yuka had made it successfully before.

This became important after Mau asked what it was. She changed the angle of her wrist, then moved one finger, and the thing became much more definitely something else.

[Yk] "A dog."

[M] "Oh."

[Yk] "Don't say it like that."

[M] "I didn't want to frighten it."

The dog bit the edge of his shadow.

He offered his right hand to the sunlight. Spread two fingers and turned his wrist. The result looked less like an animal than something left in a kitchen which everyone had agreed not to identify.

Yuka stared.

[Yk] "What is that?"

[M] "I'm waiting to see."

She began laughing. Tried to put her own hands into a different arrangement and failed because she could not hold them still.

Mau changed the angle again.

A beak emerged.

[M] "A bird."

[Yk] "With what?"

He looked at the spare projection below it.

[M] "Supplies."

She folded forward, laughing into the front of his coat. The bird disappeared when he put his arm round her instead.

He could feel her trying to stop. Each time she drew a proper breath, she looked at his hand and began again.

[Yk] "You can't take it up a mountain without supplies."

[M] "Exactly."

He kept his face straight until she looked up.

Then neither of them could.

* * *

When they tried again, she decided they needed to cooperate.

She stood in front of him, close enough that he had to look past her shoulder, and put her hands where he could see them. His left fingers would not fold into the shape she first suggested. He tried the movement once, felt the familiar resistance and let them rest against his coat instead.

[M] "You'll have to do that part."

Yuka changed places with him. Took the more complicated shape herself and held it above his right hand.

For a moment they had a broad head and a collection of wings which did not quite join.

[Yk] "Move nearer."

He moved his hand nearer hers.

[Yk] "Not that. You."

He stepped in behind her.

The head swallowed one wing.

She made an indignant sound and leaned back against his chest to see what had happened. Mau looked over her other shoulder, turning his head enough to keep the whole shape clear on his right.

[M] "It's eating your wing."

[Yk] "Stop feeding it."

He turned his hand edge-on to the light while she spread hers flatter. This time the shapes almost fitted.

They adjusted a little more. Yuka lowered the two fingers which had become horns; Mau bent his thumb until there was something which could reasonably be called a mouth.

A dragon appeared.

Small, considering the trouble they had taken. One wing was larger than the other, and its neck would not have held the head up in any world they had visited.

Yuka made the wings move.

[Yk] "There."

Mau opened the mouth.

She made a little noise for it.

He looked down at her.

[M] "That?"

She did it again. A very fierce sound, if the dragon were the size of a cup and had found something objectionable inside it.

He laughed against her hair. The mouth fell apart.

[Yk] "You're making it worse."

[M] "I'm trying to respect it."

She turned in his arm and made the sound at him instead.

He kissed the tip of her nose before she could do it a third time.

The dragon lost both wings.

Yuka turned back to repair it. Mau kept his hand where it was, though the mouth had become a little doubtful about whether it belonged above or below her wrist.

[Yk] "Wait. Keep it there."

She made the wings again. Slowly this time, one above the other, and sent the shape across the lit part of the stone without moving her feet.

Mau tried to follow with the head.

It lagged behind.

She looked at him. He looked at the creature, now apparently flying backward to collect a part of itself.

[M] "A difficult wind."

Her shoulder shook against his.

He managed the next pass. Head and wings moved together, nearly all the way across the rock. At the far edge she tipped one wrist and he lowered his hand too quickly; the dragon landed on its face.

Yuka made a small, outraged squeak for it.

[M] "I think it needs a more impressive voice."

[Yk] "Then you do it."

He tried.

The noise began deep in his throat and ended somewhere he had not intended. She took a sharp breath through her nose. He heard it and looked down.

[M] "What?"

She put a hand over her mouth.

[M] "It was better than yours."

She shook her head. He tried again, which was a mistake. She caught his sleeve and bent against him, laughing so hard that the dragon became two unrelated hands and vanished from the rock.

* * *

The sunlight moved while they were playing.

They noticed when the lower half of the rock went grey. Yuka tried one last shape higher up, but keeping her arms there became less interesting than having Mau's hand round hers again.

They went back the way they had come.

The wet edge of the pool was darker now. Mau stayed on the dry side and watched his footing instead of trying to keep looking at Yuka while he walked. She waited where the ground widened and took his hand when he reached her.

Igris was at the turn of the path. He looked toward them, then resumed watching the slope beyond their small sheltered place.

Yuka lifted a hand to him.

The knight inclined his head.

She came nearer to Mau before speaking.

[Yk] "We aren't showing him the dragon."

[M] "I wasn't going to."

[Yk] "You had that look."

Mau glanced back at the rock, where the creature had entirely ceased to exist without needing to be dismissed.

[M] "He might have recognised it."

She pulled him toward the blanket.

* * *

They fastened their coats properly before sitting.

The hollow still kept the worst of the wind above them, but the warm part of the stone had begun to cool. Yuka sat close enough that their shoulders touched, and Mau drew his right arm behind her where the rock would not press it awkwardly.

She took a moment to settle.

Then another, apparently necessary, moment to move his hand to her waist.

[M] "There?"

[Yk] "Yes."

She rested against him, looking at the open view beyond the rise.

The farthest slopes had lost their blue and become dark shapes with pale edges. Nearer, a strip of trees still held a little light. She watched it move off the tops of them until one tree remained brighter than the others.

[Yk] "Next time, I choose."

He turned his face toward her.

[M] "The mountain?"

[Yk] "The date."

[M] "All right."

She lifted her head.

[Yk] "You agreed very quickly."

[M] "I can be more suspicious if it helps."

Yuka considered that.

[Yk] "Dinner. Somewhere with a table. Not on our knees."

He looked at the food parcel, still safely wrapped beside the bag.

[M] "An attack on the arrangements."

[Yk] "We pick something for each other. You don't get to order what you always have."

[M] "What do I always have?"

She opened her mouth.

Stopped.

Then laughed at herself, without drawing away.

[Yk] "I was hoping you wouldn't ask that part. I'll find out first."

He let her imagine it. The table, the food she might choose because she thought he would like it, or because she wanted to see his face when he discovered what she had done.

[M] "And you eat what I pick?"

[Yk] "Yes."

[M] "Now who's agreeing too quickly?"

She put her forehead against his shoulder.

[Yk] "Nothing still moving."

[M] "You've taken a lot of the mystery out of it."

[Yk] "You can be surprising after dinner."

He laughed. Her hand tightened on his coat, pleased with the result.

They did not put a day beside it. For a while she told him what she would make him wear, rejecting his suggestion that the bag had done enough work today to be invited too. He asked whether he would be allowed to carry the water.

She gave him permission on conditions which became increasingly difficult to meet.

Then she reconsidered the food.

[Yk] "If one of us chooses something horrible, we can swap."

[M] "Then I'll have picked for myself."

She thought about that, her head still against his shoulder.

[Yk] "Only if we both agree."

[M] "What happens if you like yours and I don't like mine?"

She lifted her face, visibly struggling to make the answer generous.

[Yk] "You can have some of mine."

[M] "How much?"

[Yk] "I haven't seen it yet."

He laughed. She tucked herself back against his side before he could see how pleased she was to have made him do it again.

* * *

The first little point of light appeared before the ground was dark.

Yuka saw it when she lifted her head from his shoulder. She pointed, then looked toward the place where Kaisel waited, checking how much of the path she could still see.

Mau followed the movement.

[M] "We should start putting things away."

She nodded, but did not get up at once.

[Yk] "Can I have a minute?"

He left his arm where it was.

She turned toward him. Put one gloved hand against his cheek, then slid it to the back of his neck where the coat would not catch it.

The kiss was warm after the cold tip of her nose.

It made him smile against her mouth. She felt it and paused just far enough away to ask what was funny.

[M] "Nothing."

She looked unconvinced.

[M] "I'm enjoying myself."

Her expression changed. She leaned back in and kissed him once, firmly, before standing.

[Yk] "Then help me with the blanket."

He got up more slowly. Looked at the remaining light on the way back to the landing. Ciel spoke privately while he checked the ground.

[C] *Can you still see where you're putting your feet?*

[M] *Yes. The dry part is clear. We're packing now.*

He told Yuka they should go while the footing was clear. She had lifted the bag from its corner of the blanket and was holding the mouth open, with the two wrapped cups still inside. The flask stood beside it; the food cloth remained closed, the apple core tucked separately where she had put it.

Two dry stones still held the other edges down.

Mau bent to lift the nearer one. Yuka stopped him with a little sound.

[Yk] "Before you do that."

He looked up.

She opened her mouth and gave him the tiny dragon's furious cry.

He nearly dropped the stone on his boot.
