# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 35 — At the Ends First

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 35
**Source episode:** S6E59
**Continuity state:** Day475 evening, after the return34 and before the couple's private conversation. Rimuru tells Shoko they have returned; she brings that limited news to Frieren/Ori/Fern in the east room. Mau's announced intention to ask for the Goddess's terms tonight is not an accepted bargain, appointment or promised cure. Nobody here knows the date's private conversation or future confidences. Ordinary hair care gives mother/daughter warmth without erasing the attack, restoring memory or making Ori responsible for Frieren's survival. Fern chooses time with Stark; Shoko offers her own company. The established comb comes from the east-room shelf, not a magical object; no new diary or written account. The staff remains in the downstairs office, Rimuru's sleeping loft remains above the closet in this room. At the end Ori has gone into her connected room and Frieren is preparing for bed, with Shoko still present. No sleep intervention, terms, new power or injury.

*When: Day475, evening. The east room. Ori is trying to see the back of her own head without the aid of a mirror.*

Turning farther did not help.

She tried anyway. Caught a strand between her fingers and pulled it round toward her shoulder, only to lose the part she had been looking for when the rest came with it.

Fern looked up from the book on her knees.

[Fe] "What are you doing?"

[O] "There's something in it."

She turned the other way.

Frieren watched from the chair. Ori had been sitting on the end of the bed, doing nothing which required all this movement until one hand went behind her neck.

[F] "A knot."

[O] "I know."

[F] "You said something."

Ori looked at her.

Then held out the comb.

[O] "Can you get it?"

Frieren put down the book she had not been following and came to sit behind her.

* * *

The knot had gathered low down, just above Ori's collar.

Frieren lifted the hair clear and found the smaller tangles beneath it. She began at the ends. Ori sat still through three careful passes, then moved her head to watch Fern turning a page.

The comb stopped.

[F] "You can move your eyes."

Ori looked forward again.

[O] "I was."

Fern hid her mouth behind the book.

Frieren separated another little section. The comb had a missing tooth; she turned it to use the sound part rather than letting the rough gap find a place to catch.

She had done this before. The knowledge was in the small adjustments her fingers made, the way she held the loose hair above a tangle so the pull would not reach Ori's scalp.

Tonight she had to think about continuing when she heard steps outside.

They passed.

She loosened her grip before Ori had to tell her it was too tight.

Fern looked toward the door too, then down at her book. Neither tried to say who it had been.

* * *

Shoko knocked while Frieren was working at the largest knot.

Fern went to let her in. Ori began turning, remembered the comb and stopped halfway.

[O] "Who is it?"

[Sh] "Someone who can wait until you're facing the right way."

She came far enough into the room for them all to see her.

[Sh] "They've come back. Rimuru told me downstairs. They're both home."

Frieren's hands stopped.

The comb remained below the knot, with nothing pulling against it.

[O] "Already?"

[Sh] "They came back while they could still see the way. They said they'd be home tonight."

Ori looked at the window. The glass had begun reflecting the room more clearly than the yard beyond it.

[O] "Did he ask her?"

Shoko moved the stool nearer before sitting.

[Sh] "Not that he's reported to us. Before he went out, he said he meant to ask for the conditions when he got back. That was his plan for tonight. It isn't an answer yet."

Frieren looked at her.

[F] "So he hasn't agreed to anything."

[Sh] "He hasn't told us he has. There were no terms before he went."

Frieren nodded slowly.

It was possible to hear the words and still have a part of herself trying to run ahead of them. A morning in which he knew her. A voice in the doorway. She let the comb rest against her leg until she could hear the smaller account Shoko had actually brought.

They had come home. He still had a question to ask.

* * *

[O] "Will you come and tell us?"

[Sh] "When there's something I can tell you. I don't have a time."

[O] "If we're asleep?"

Shoko looked at Frieren, then back at her.

[Sh] "I don't want you sitting up because you think you might miss the moment something happens. Nothing has been arranged for you to attend."

Ori picked at a fold in her sleeve.

Frieren lifted the loose hair off her collar again.

[F] "We'll get ready for bed."

Ori was quiet for a moment.

[O] "I wasn't going to stand in the corridor."

[F] "I didn't say you were."

She put the comb back against the ends. Began moving it carefully through the section she had already loosened.

Ori watched the dark window for a little while, then turned her eyes toward Fern without moving her head.

[O] "What are you reading?"

* * *

Fern held the book up.

It was the one she had brought that morning. Frieren recognised the place marked with a narrow scrap and the edge of Fern's own writing beside the passage they had discussed.

[Fe] "I was checking the next part."

[F] "The order?"

[Fe] "Yes."

Frieren looked over Ori's shoulder.

[F] "You were right to change it."

Fern's hand stopped at the edge of the page.

Then she lowered the book enough that it no longer hid her mouth.

[Fe] "I'd like to show you the rest tomorrow."

[F] "All right."

She was not certain how much she would be able to follow. Fern had not asked for a different teacher, or an answer tonight which Frieren could only give by pretending to have read the whole page.

Ori made a small complaining sound.

Frieren looked down. She had held the same section of hair in the air for too long.

[F] "Sorry."

She lowered it. Started again from where the comb moved easily.

* * *

Fern closed her book.

[Fe] "I'm going to sit with Stark for a while. In the courtyard, if it isn't too cold."

Frieren looked up.

There was a question behind the movement which she had begun allowing other people to answer before she had to speak it. Who would stay. Whether she could bear the quiet. Whether needing somebody meant the person nearest the door should put everything down again.

Shoko leaned back on the stool.

[Sh] "I've got time to sit here."

Fern looked at her, then at Frieren.

[F] "Take your coat."

Fern lifted the arm on which it was already folded.

Frieren regarded it.

[F] "That one will do."

Ori made a sound which might have been a laugh. Fern looked at her, and it became one properly.

[Fe] "Thank you for the inspection."

She took the book with her. At the door she stopped to straighten the part of her sleeve caught beneath the coat, not to wait for a task somebody had forgotten to give her.

[Fe] "I'll see you before I turn in."

Frieren nodded.

The door closed gently behind her.

* * *

The large knot began to come apart.

Not all at once. A few strands loosened, then the little tight place beneath them became something Frieren could separate with her fingertips. She held the comb across her knee while she did it.

Ori breathed out.

[O] "That was it."

[F] "There are two."

Ori made a disappointed noise.

[Sh] "Very poor arrangements."

[O] "I didn't put them there."

[F] "Your hair did."

Ori considered this. Reached behind herself before Frieren could tell her not to and touched the part which was already smooth.

[O] "This bit knows how."

Shoko smiled at the floor.

Frieren worked on the other side. Ori let her, looking down at her hands instead of trying to see the knot this time.

Outside, somebody called a name from the courtyard. Not Mau's. The answer came back muffled by the closed window, followed by laughter she could not identify.

Frieren kept the comb moving.

The knowledge that he was home did not put him in this room. She knew why she could not turn the wish into an errand for somebody else.

Ori did not ask her to explain the look on her face.

She waited until Frieren lowered the comb, then asked whether there was still anything caught.

[F] "No."

[O] "All of it?"

Frieren passed the comb gently through once more. From the place above the old knot to the ends, with no stop between.

[F] "All of it."

She gathered the two loose sides together behind Ori's neck.

[O] "Not up."

Frieren paused.

[O] "I want it loose."

[F] "It will be in your mouth again."

[O] "I'll move it."

Frieren let the hair fall. Ori ran one hand along the smooth ends, testing them for herself.

* * *

Ori turned round.

The movement brought a strand across her mouth. She pushed it away, offended that the work had not persuaded her hair to stay behind her shoulders by itself.

Frieren tucked the strand clear.

[O] "Thank you."

She leaned forward and put her arms round Frieren. Not suddenly enough to make her catch a falling weight. Close enough that Frieren could put both hands against her back without holding the comb between them.

After a little while Ori moved first.

[O] "I'm going to my room."

[F] "All right."

[O] "Leave it open a bit."

She meant the connecting door. Frieren nodded.

Ori took the comb from beside her knee and carried it back to the shelf. Went round the end instead of trying to step over the low corner in the way.

[O] "It's still there."

Shoko followed her glance.

[Sh] "The shelf?"

[O] "It catches your foot."

[F] "Only if you walk into it."

Ori looked at her mother for a long moment.

Then went through her own doorway, leaving it open to the width she wanted.

* * *

Shoko stayed while Frieren got up.

She moved slowly because she had been sitting too long. Put the closed book on the table beside the chair, then turned back to the bed.

The cover had pulled crooked while Ori was on it. Frieren took the corner and drew it straight. One fold remained beneath the pillow. She lifted the pillow, freed it and set everything down again.

Shoko did not take the work out of her hands.

[F] "You don't have to wait until I'm asleep."

[Sh] "I wasn't planning to time you."

Frieren looked at her.

[Sh] "I can stay a little longer. You can get ready without entertaining me."

Frieren's hand remained on the cover.

Then she nodded. Went to put away the things she had left on the chair, making room for her clothes instead of leaving them on the floor beside it.

From the connected room came a small rustling sound. Ori moving about in her own space. A drawer opened, closed, opened again.

[O] "Mama?"

Frieren turned at once.

[F] "Yes?"

[O] "Was my other sock in here?"

Shoko lifted one eyebrow.

Frieren looked at the bed, then bent to see beneath it.

The sock was under the far edge, just beyond her fingers. She knelt, reached farther and caught it.

[F] "I've found it."

Ori's hand appeared round the door.

Frieren put the sock into it. The hand withdrew, and after a moment the drawer closed for the last time.
