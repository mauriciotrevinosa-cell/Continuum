## Chapter 36 — The Word I Used Before

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)

*When: Day475, night. Outside Yuka's room. Mau has washed the last trace of the mountain off his hands and can hear her laughing before he knocks.*

The laugh stopped at the sound.

For a moment he wished he had waited. Then Yuka called for him to come in, and the feeling changed into something he did not have to interpret before opening the door.

Her grandmother sat on the bed. Yuta had the stool. The blanket from the outing lay across Yuka's knees, dry and folded double, one corner still carrying a blade of pale grass they had missed while packing it.

[Yk] "He told it again."

Mau looked at Yuta.

[M] "Did the pot survive this time?"

[Yt] "It survived every time."

[Mk] "He thinks that improves it."

Yuka laughed again. It sounded different without the cold air carrying half of it away.

Mau stopped beside the door rather than turning the small room into somewhere everybody else had to leave to make space for him.

Yuka looked toward her grandparents.

[Yk] "Could I talk to him for a while? Just us."

Maki put her hand briefly over the blanket on Yuka's knee.

[Mk] "Of course."

* * *

They did not go at once.

Yuta moved the stool out of the door's way. Maki asked whether the window needed closing more tightly; Yuka said it was fine, then stood to hug her before the answer could become the end of the visit.

Her grandmother held her for a little while.

No warning about what the evening must not become. No last attempt to change the choice she had made while everybody listened downstairs.

[Mk] "Good night."

[Yk] "Good night."

Yuta touched her hair when she turned toward him. She went into his arms too, catching the edge of his sleeve with one hand as she had when she was smaller.

Mau looked at the doorframe until she let go.

Yuta passed him without asking him to make a promise in front of her. He met his eyes once. The look was tired, protective and not difficult to understand.

Mau nodded.

When the door closed, Yuka remained standing beside the bed.

[Yk] "You can shut it properly."

He did. Then turned back.

* * *

She looked at his coat.

[Yk] "Are you staying in that?"

[M] "I hadn't got to that part."

[Yk] "You're going to be very warm."

He began undoing the fastening. The stiff fingers on his left hand made the second part awkward; he changed hands before she needed to pretend she had not noticed.

Yuka took the coat when he freed his arm. Put it on the spare hook beside her own, smoothing the shoulder once so it would not sit twisted overnight.

He had taken his gloves off downstairs. One remained tucked into the other in the pocket, where neither could be lost beneath somebody's blanket.

[Yk] "There. You look less prepared to leave."

He looked at her.

She had meant it lightly. The sentence stayed in the room after the smile which had carried it began to fade.

[M] "I'm not leaving yet."

Yuka nodded. Sat down, drew her feet onto the bed and made room beside her.

* * *

The blanket was colder on the outside.

She turned the folded part toward them while Mau took his boots off. He put them beneath the stool instead of leaving them where her grandparents would trip if they came back to ask something.

Then he sat beside her.

For a while they talked about the mountain.

Whether the creature on the rock had genuinely resembled a dragon, or whether both of them had been too polite to explain the problem. Whether Igris had heard the noise, and whether it would be possible to look him in the face tomorrow if he had.

[Yk] "He'd say it was very impressive."

[M] "He has excellent manners."

[Yk] "That's why I wouldn't believe him."

Mau laughed. She leaned against his shoulder and let the sound reach her where the quiet room could keep all of it.

She had wanted to bring that part home.

For a few minutes she had.

* * *

[Yk] "I keep thinking I should be able to decide."

Mau turned toward her.

Her hand lay on the blanket. The metal flower had slipped free of the cuff again. She did not hide it, but neither did she look at it while she spoke.

[Yk] "That I can tell myself what I know and then stop wanting the thing which keeps making everybody hurt."

He waited.

[Yk] "I know what was true before. I knew when I first kissed you. I knew when you came back from talking to Frieren and I let myself have another day before saying anything."

Her voice began to change.

[Yk] "I can't make that into not knowing just because I was frightened."

Mau put his right hand beside hers.

[M] "I kept letting it be later too."

She nodded, looking down.

[Yk] "It's my fault."

[M] "Some of what we did was wrong. That doesn't make everything which happened afterward something you chose."

She shook her head once. Not because she disagreed with the words. Because the words had not reached the part of her which kept picturing the corridor before she opened her door.

* * *

He did not make her look at him.

Put his arm round her when she moved nearer, left the other hand where she could take it, and waited while her breathing stopped being something she could hold very neatly between sentences.

She cried against his shirt.

Quietly at first. Then with a little angry sound which made her draw back and wipe her face, as if she had discovered another thing she ought to be able to control better.

[Yk] "We had a good day."

[M] "We did."

[Yk] "I want to keep that without having to think about what it means for everybody else."

Mau looked at the blanket between them. It had been grass and food and her shoulder against his beneath an ordinary sky. None of that had felt like a thing he needed to excuse while he was there.

[M] "Me too."

Yuka took the hand he had left on the blanket.

[Yk] "I used to say Oppa and just mean you."

He looked at her.

[Yk] "Someone I could bother. Play with. Go and find when I wanted something. I didn't have to work out what it did to anyone before I said it."

Her thumb stopped against his.

[Yk] "I miss that being easy."

* * *

He knew which history she meant.

Not the seamless version which had been put in the empty places, the one where she had always been the person nearest the beginning. The life other people had been helping him understand, in which he had chosen relationships he could still describe more easily than feel.

Yuka had lived that life without having it taken from her.

She remembered what she missed.

[Yk] "Sometimes I wish I could go back to wanting only that. You could be Oppa and I could be happy because you came to play."

She looked up before he could find a reply which would make the sentence kinder to him.

[Yk] "Then you come in and I want you to sit here. Not across the table. Here. And I don't know how to be the person who wishes the other thing again."

Mau rested his forehead against hers.

He could not make either wish a command which she had failed to obey. Could not take the love she was trying to describe and call it wrong merely because it did not fit the only version of tomorrow the house knew how to make room for.

[M] "You don't have to pretend you can switch it off."

[Yk] "Neither do you."

Her voice was very small.

He shook his head without moving away.

[M] "I can't."

* * *

Ciel remained near enough for him to reach.

He knew she was there without needing to ask her to answer in the middle of what Yuka was saying. The questions still existed downstairs. The promise to bring back the words had not changed because he was sitting under a blanket with somebody he did not want to lose.

He would ask tonight.

He wanted to finish hearing Yuka before he heard what the answer would cost.

[Yk] "Are you going to talk to her now?"

[M] "In a little while. I wanted to be here first."

She looked at him, then at their hands.

[Yk] "I don't want her to make you into somebody who has to be sorry for caring about us."

He drew back enough to see her face.

[M] "I'll ask what she actually means. I'm not going to tell you I know before I do."

Yuka nodded.

The answer was smaller than everything he wished he could give her. He kept it that size instead of using her tears as a reason to make it sound certain.

* * *

She kissed him first.

Not to stop him asking. Not to prove the afternoon still belonged to them. A slow, tired movement toward the person she had asked to sit beside her.

He kissed her back. Put his hand against her cheek after she leaned into it, thumb staying beside the place where she had wiped the tears badly rather than trying to make the whole face stop showing them.

For a while she kept one hand in the front of his shirt.

When she let go, the cloth had a small crease in it.

[Yk] "Can you stay a bit longer?"

[M] "I want to."

She settled against him again. He drew the blanket round her shoulder where it had slipped, keeping the part beneath her knees from pulling loose.

The room grew quiet around them.

Outside, somebody went past carrying water. A board answered under the footsteps, then the sound moved down the corridor and was gone.

Yuka listened until she could recognise that she had been listening.

Then she turned her face into his shoulder, and he stayed.
