*When: Day475, late night. Yuka's room. She has stopped crying, but the sleeve she used to wipe her face has not quite dried.*

Mau's shoulder moved when he tried to ease his arm.

Yuka lifted her head at once.

[Yk] "Am I hurting you?"

[M] "No. I've been sitting like that too long."

She looked at the small space he had left himself between her and the end of the bed.

[Yk] "You could have said."

[M] "You were talking."

She sat away from him enough for him to move. Waited while he stretched his arm, then began shifting the blanket so he would have somewhere to put his legs without sitting half across her knees.

The folded edge caught beneath her.

She pulled once. Nothing happened. Looked down and discovered the problem.

[Yk] "I can do this."

He kept his mouth shut.

[Yk] "You're allowed to help. Just don't say anything."

He lifted the corner she could not reach. She moved off it, freed the fold and spread the blanket across the bed again.

When she looked up, he was smiling.

[Yk] "That counts."

* * *

They lay down without making it graceful.

Yuka went first, drawing her knees toward the pillow and then straightening them beneath the blanket. Mau waited until she had found a place for her head before lowering himself beside her.

There was less room than he had expected.

He put one arm in the wrong place, moved it and found that he had made the blanket pull off her shoulder. She caught the loose edge and brought it back before taking his wrist.

[Yk] "Here."

He let her put his hand where she wanted it, over the cloth at her waist.

[M] "You seem to have a plan."

[Yk] "I have a pillow. You're making it complicated."

He moved his head enough for her to have the part she was defending.

She looked at the space he had given her.

Then moved nearer anyway.

[M] "Was that the plan?"

She put her forehead against his chest instead of answering. He felt the small movement of her face against his shirt, and left his arm round her.

Their clothes kept them from settling quite as neatly as they wanted. Her sleeve twisted beneath one elbow. He waited while she pulled it straight, then shifted with her until neither had a fastening pressed into the wrong place.

Afterward she sighed.

He could hear how tired she was without needing her to call it anything.

* * *

For a while they listened to the house.

A door closed down the corridor. Somebody spoke beyond it, too quietly for either to make out the words. Water moved somewhere farther away, then stopped.

Yuka's eyes went toward their door.

He waited until they came back.

[Yk] "I thought I'd be better at coming home after today."

Mau did not try to sit up again to answer properly.

[M] "You told them you liked it."

She looked at his shirt.

[Yk] "Grandma wanted to know."

[M] "I think she did."

Yuka nodded a little.

[Yk] "I didn't want to give her the part she'd like best and keep the rest. I did like it."

[M] "So did I."

Her hand moved over the crease she had left earlier. Smoothed it, although it would have to be washed before the crease mattered to anybody else.

[Yk] "Your shirt's terrible now."

[M] "I was hoping you'd blame the mountain."

[Yk] "It was very damp up there."

[M] "Everywhere."

She laughed quietly against him.

The sound had almost no breath left in it. He touched the back of her shoulder and let her keep her face where it was.

* * *

She began to say something twice before finding the beginning she wanted.

[Yk] "Will you ask her tonight?"

He looked down.

[M] "Yes."

[Yk] "Even if I fall asleep?"

[M] "You don't have to stay awake for me to ask a question."

She moved her head so that she could see him.

[Yk] "I know. I mean don't wait because you think I'll be upset that you did it while I wasn't looking."

Mau took his left hand off her waist and put it where she could reach it.

She did, fitting her fingers between his without making the stiff ones close farther than they wanted to.

[Yk] "I want you to find out what she means. I keep trying to imagine it, and then I get frightened of something nobody's even said."

[M] "I've been doing that too."

She watched his mouth while he spoke.

[M] "I'll ask what she'd return. What happens to what came after. What she'd want."

Those were the questions waiting downstairs. He could remember the paper without having it in the bed between them. Rimuru's hand making a separate line for each thing they had been trying not to mistake for an answer already given.

Yuka's thumb moved once against his.

[Yk] "Please."

He nodded.

* * *

[Yk] "Do you have to go back outside?"

[M] "I don't know. I can try asking from here first."

She looked toward the window, then back at him.

[Yk] "You don't have to leave just to make it easier to hear her?"

[M] "Yesterday I kept thinking about doing it with people at the table. Calling out, and then having everybody waiting for a voice that might not answer."

The explanation made her mouth move a little.

[Yk] "You could have asked her to pass something first."

He pictured it before he could help himself.

The empty place beside a bowl. Everybody turning.

[M] "She might have."

Yuka smiled properly for a moment.

[Yk] "Then you'd have had to explain why you thought that was the important question."

[M] "I was hungry."

She put her face against his chest again. Her hand stayed in his, between them beneath the blanket.

He had not told her there was a way of calling which guaranteed an answer. She had not asked him to promise one.

After a while she moved her head enough to see the coats beside the door.

[Yk] "If you do have to go out, tell me first."

[M] "I will."

[Yk] "Even if I'm asleep. I don't want to wake up and find the coat gone and have to work out what that means."

He looked toward the hooks too.

His coat hung beside hers, one sleeve folded against the other. She had straightened the shoulder before sitting down. Nothing about it looked ready for an urgent departure.

[M] "I won't leave without telling you."

She turned back to him.

[Yk] "You don't have to keep looking at it. I know where it is."

He looked down at her.

[M] "I'm trying to remember which pocket has the gloves."

She watched him for a moment, then realised he was making the face on purpose.

[Yk] "The mountain ruined those too."

[M] "A very difficult place."

She brought his hand nearer beneath the blanket. Her smile faded slowly this time, with no new sentence needed to hold it in place.

* * *

When she spoke next, her voice was quieter.

[Yk] "If it all comes back, this might feel different."

He looked at the parting in her hair.

[M] "I don't know how it will feel."

[Yk] "I know."

She lifted her head. Kept looking at him even when her eyes began filling again, tired enough now that she seemed more annoyed by the tears than surprised.

[Yk] "If our story gets far away. The way the other one is now."

He stayed still.

[Yk] "You don't have to pretend you remember it the way I do."

[M] "I haven't asked her to take this from me."

[Yk] "I know. I'm not asking you to."

She held his hand a little more tightly.

[Yk] "But if you forget it, I'll remember for both of us."

Mau could feel where her fingers rested against his. A small ordinary pressure. She had said it as if keeping the days they had lived was something she could offer him, a part which would remain hers while he tried to find the part stolen from him.

She did not know what the answer would be.

Neither did he.

[Yk] "I'll still know what happened. You won't have to make something up so I don't get sad."

His throat tightened.

[M] "I love you."

Her face changed before she could prepare an answer for it.

[Yk] "I know."

He watched her blink, then move nearer.

[Yk] "I love you too."

* * *

She kept looking at him.

There was something else in her face now, a question which needed more of her than the small offer she had just made.

[Yk] "Can I ask you something before you get your answer?"

[M] "Ask."

Her hand tightened around his.

[Yk] "What we talked about. Having a family. I don't want that to turn into something we could only say when nobody else was there."

He stayed close enough for her to see him listening.

[Yk] "I want us to keep going. Even with all of this. You and me. I want to have children with you, Mau. A life we don't have to stop imagining every time we come home."

His breath moved against her forehead.

She did not lower her eyes.

[Yk] "Will we?"

He thought of the ordinary things they had begun putting into a future. A table. Her brother. The small people they had imagined before knowing how difficult it would be to sit together in the house which was already theirs.

He wanted that life.

[M] "Yes."

Her mouth trembled.

[Yk] "You don't have to say it because I'm crying."

[M] "I want a family with you. I want to have children with you. I mean it."

She watched his face for a long moment.

[Yk] "Even after everything?"

[M] "Yes."

She came closer, stopping with her forehead against his.

[Yk] "Promise me."

He could hear the question without mistaking it for a request to be comforted until morning.

[M] "I promise."

Yuka closed her eyes. Let the breath she had been holding leave her slowly, with both hands still keeping his near her.

When she looked at him again she was smiling through the tears.

[Yk] "Okay."

* * *

The kiss did not ask them to get up or go anywhere.

Yuka tilted her face. Mau met her, his right hand coming up beside her cheek when she moved close enough for it. She let go of the hand beneath the blanket to touch his shirt instead.

They kissed again after the first pause.

Then she rested with her forehead beside his mouth, breathing slowly while he kept his hand against the side of her face.

[Yk] "Stay until I'm asleep?"

[M] "Yes."

She settled lower on the pillow. Waited for him to move with her rather than pulling his arm where she wanted it this time.

He did.

The blanket had slipped from her shoulder again. He drew it up with his right hand, taking care not to pull the lower part out from beneath her feet.

She caught his hand before he could put it away.

[Yk] "You can leave that here."

He laid it on the bed between them. She put hers over it, then turned her face toward him and closed her eyes.

* * *

For some time she was not asleep.

He could tell by the small changes in her grip, the way she opened her eyes once when another door closed and looked at him before shutting them again.

He did not tell her to hurry.

Her breathing slowed. A finger moved against the edge of his palm and stopped. Her face lost the effort of deciding what to do with every feeling which crossed it.

When she spoke, the words were not quite together.

[Yk] "The table."

He looked down.

[M] "What?"

[Yk] "For dinner. Next time."

He brushed a strand away from the corner of her mouth.

[M] "I heard you."

She made a small satisfied sound. Her hand remained on his, warm through the place where their fingers met.

He waited while the next breath came, then the next. No unfamiliar stillness had entered the room. Only the ordinary tiredness of a long day finally reaching her where she had chosen to rest.

After a while he could have moved his hand without her knowing.

He left it there.

The coats hung beside the door. His boots were beneath the stool. Behind the closed window the night continued without giving him an answer merely because he had reached the end of the questions he could ask himself.

Ciel was still within reach.

[M] *I'm going to ask her now.*

[C] *I'm here.*

Mau kept Yuka's hand beneath the blanket and raised his eyes toward the window.
