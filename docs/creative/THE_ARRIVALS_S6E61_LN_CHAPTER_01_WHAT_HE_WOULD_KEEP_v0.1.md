# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 34
## Chapter 39 — What He Would Keep

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (Codex, 2026-10-23)
**Volume:** 34
**Reading order:** 39
**Source episode:** S6E61
**Continuity state:** Day475 late night, directly after38. Mau remains beside sleeping Yuka, clothed, right hand under hers; door closed, coats/hooks, boots/stool and blanket unchanged. He asks the Goddess from the room; she answers through the same private voice only he hears, with Ciel connected and initially receiving his deliberate relay. The answer distinguishes restored original history/affect from false implanted history and the life actually lived after theft. It proposes anchoring his returned love for Frieren in his native heart and his real love for Yuka in the existing Black Heart; one person, no retrospective cause of falling in love, no infinite reserve or bodily healing. Mau voluntarily tells the Goddess the limited fact of Yuka's historical illness/confidence; Ciel objects to disclosing what was entrusted, without spreading it to Rimuru. No completed terms, other-person rewrite, consent, sleep intervention, recovered history, payment or coin yet. The Goddess announces a further proposal at the end.

*When: Day475, late night. Yuka's room. Her right hand has gone slack over his. Mau has told Ciel he is going to ask.*

He did not look for an empty place in the room.

The window held the dim shape of the hooks beside the door, turned backward in the glass. His coat hung beside Yuka's. He could see a little of the cuff she had straightened before asking him to sit down.

[M] "Are you listening?"

He spoke quietly. Yuka's breathing changed at the small sound, then settled again against his shirt.

There was time for him to wonder whether he ought to have gone somewhere else.

[Dio] *Yes.*

He closed his eyes briefly.

The room remained a room. No outline appeared in the glass. Nothing moved beneath the blanket except her next breath.

[M] *She's answered.*

[C] *I heard your question. I can't hear her.*

[M] *I know.*

He opened his eyes again.

[M] "I came back. I want to know what you meant yesterday. All of it, before anything happens."

* * *

[Dio] *You want the history that was taken from you.*

[M] "And what I felt in it."

[Dio] *Yes.*

He waited. The answer was not smaller for coming quietly, but he had spent enough time giving other people the wrong sort of certainty to know that yes was still a beginning.

[M] "What happens to the one I have now?"

Yuka shifted a little nearer in her sleep. He kept his hand where she had left it.

[Dio] *Which one?*

He looked down.

There were days he remembered which other people said had never happened. A beginning which fitted neatly until he tried to put it beside somebody else's. Rooms belonging to the wrong stage of the house. The easy conviction that he had arrived after the girl sleeping beside him.

Then there were things he had done.

The floor giving way beneath a fight. A frightened animal's patience. His own mouth finding hers for the first time beneath a sky which had not belonged to the world outside.

[M] "The things somebody put there. And the things I've actually lived since. They're different. I want to know what you'd do to both."

The Goddess let him finish.

[Dio] *You would know the difference.*

* * *

He thought at first that she had answered too little.

Then she continued.

[Dio] *The beginning which was made for you would no longer pass for the beginning you lived. You would remember having believed it. You would also have the life which lets you see where it is false.*

[M] "And afterward?"

[Dio] *Afterward happened.*

His right thumb moved beneath Yuka's hand.

[Dio] *The person who chose to go with her was you. The person who let the others wait was you. Returning something stolen does not make those days belong to somebody else.*

He could not make the sentence into an accusation which would be easier to argue with. It left his choices where he had put them.

[M] "I'd keep them."

[Dio] *Yes.*

He passed that much to Ciel. Slowly, deliberately, marking the difference between the fabricated past and what they had actually done after the field.

She did not improve the answer for him.

[C] *That answers one of the questions. It doesn't answer how the returned feelings would coexist with the ones you have.*

Mau looked toward the window again.

[M] "What about loving them?"

* * *

There was a little warm place beneath Yuka's cheek where his shirt had stopped feeling like separate cloth.

He had been trying not to think about what she had offered him. *I'll remember for both of us.* He still did not know how to answer the kindness of it.

[Dio] *You have been afraid that one will push the other out.*

[M] "I don't know what remembering her will do to me."

Frieren's name remained unspoken for a moment.

He knew its sound. Knew the shape of her face in the morning, the exact place she had stood when she had asked him something he could not give back. Could recall accounts of an affection which had once made a different answer effortless.

He did not want to come back and discover that the person under his hand had become only a mistake.

He did not want to say that to the woman waiting for the first love to return.

[Dio] *I can make room for both.*

His breathing stopped long enough for Ciel to notice.

[C] *Mau?*

[M] *She's answering the other question.*

He swallowed.

[M] "How?"

* * *

[Dio] *There are two hearts already.*

Her hand did not move.

He became aware of the other beating inside him, not because it had changed, but because he had been asked to think of it while holding her.

The body had continued with it for long enough that ordinary movement no longer made him stop and count. It could become ordinary without becoming understood.

[Dio] *What comes back can be held in the one you began with. What you have lived with her can be held in the other.*

[M] "Held?"

[Dio] *Your love for Frieren in your own heart. Your love for Yuka in the Black Heart. Literally, Mau. Neither would have to become faint to make the other bearable.*

He looked at the sleeping girl.

At the small place where her mouth had parted, at the damp line on a sleeve which would be dry by morning.

[M] "That heart didn't choose her for me."

[Dio] *No.*

The answer came without teasing him.

[Dio] *You did. I am describing what I would do now, not inventing a cause for what you have already chosen.*

He lay still while he understood it.

Two loves, with a place in the body for each. No second person to receive the blame when he hurt somebody. No division in which he could say the one who had done it was out of reach of the one being asked to answer.

[M] "Still me."

[Dio] *Yes.*

* * *

Ciel heard that answer from him too.

The description took longer to pass on than the Goddess had taken to give it. Mau went back once and corrected a word. He did not want *held* to become *controlled* merely because he was frightened.

[C] *That would be an intervention. It isn't what I have observed the Black Heart doing by itself.*

[M] *I understand.*

[C] *It doesn't establish anything about the rest of your body. Or what you are. Or how long you can live.*

He knew why she had said it before he could turn a new answer into an explanation for every old question.

[M] "Would anything else change? My hand. The eye."

[Dio] *Those are not what was taken from your memory.*

His left hand rested beyond Yuka's waist, two fingers bent only as far as they would bend.

[M] "And it isn't more power."

[Dio] *Not the thing I'm offering you.*

He relayed those answers as well.

No reserve grew beneath his skin because a use had been named for a heart. No pain disappeared from the old places. The ordinary difficulty of lying still with an arm beneath somebody remained.

Nothing had been accepted.

* * *

[Dio] *You could love them both without losing either love.*

He almost asked whether she thought that would make anything easier.

Yuka moved before he spoke. A small, sleeping adjustment, her fingers finding his and then failing to hold them because she was too far inside sleep to finish the movement.

He put his hand back beneath hers.

[M] "That isn't the only thing she's afraid of."

The Goddess waited.

[M] "She told me something tonight. About being ill before she came here."

Ciel spoke at once.

[C] *Mau. That was her confidence.*

He closed his eyes.

He had not forgotten that. He had begun telling it anyway, to the person whose next answer he wanted badly enough to give her a reason for it.

[M] *I know.*

It did not undo the sentence already spoken.

He kept names and details which were not needed out of the next one. Could not make that into having kept all of it private.

[M] "She's well now. It was before this world. But there was a time when she thought she wouldn't have another birthday. She wanted one. She wanted a tomorrow she could put something in."

His voice had become very quiet.

[M] "She trusted me with that. I don't want to answer it by taking the rest of her life away from her."

He looked down at her again.

He could remember the afternoon very clearly. The little table she had begun to make in a future where they could choose each other's food. The way she had laughed when he had given the dragon a voice.

There was room in that future for her grandparents, for her brother if he came, for the other people she had said she loved. Wanting him had not made those people stop existing for her.

He had not found a way of making the same room in the house.

* * *

The Goddess did not answer with a promise about Yuka's health.

For that, at least, he was grateful.

[Dio] *Returning what was taken would not choose how you live afterward.*

[M] "I know."

[Dio] *Nor would it make the others forget what has happened.*

He could picture the chair beside the bed in the east room without needing to see it. A book put down in the wrong place. Ori looking at his face before deciding whether to ask him another question. Yuta setting the wooden practice sword aside.

He knew how to put a hand safely on every one of those objects.

He did not know what to say when he got there.

[Dio] *That is one answer to what you asked me yesterday.*

He waited for the price.

Instead, the voice changed slightly, becoming more attentive to the part he had not asked her to solve aloud.

[Dio] *There is another part we could discuss.*

Mau kept his eyes on the sleeping girl.

The coat on the hook. The blanket under her knees. All the things which would still be here when the answer finished.

[M] "Tell me."
