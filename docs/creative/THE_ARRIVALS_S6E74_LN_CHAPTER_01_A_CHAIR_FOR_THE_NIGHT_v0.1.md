*When: Day475, night. Yuka has discovered that the chair makes a noise every time she tries to sit more quietly.*

She moved her foot.

The chair scraped.

Mau's eyelids shifted and she stopped breathing for a moment, waiting to see whether she had woken him or hurt him or done nothing at all.

He remained where he was.

Shoko looked over from the other side of the bed.

"You can move," she said. ˢʰ

Yuka nodded and did not move.

Shoko finished what she was checking, put the cloth down, and came around to the chair.

"Stand up for a minute." ˢʰ

Yuka looked at Mau's hand.

"A minute," Shoko repeated. ˢʰ

She let go carefully and stood. Shoko moved the chair a little farther from the side of the bed, put a folded cloth under the leg which had been catching, and tested it once with her hand.

It made no sound.

"There." ˢʰ

Yuka looked at the cloth, surprised by the size of the relief it gave her.

"Thank you." ʸᵏ

"Now get some water before you sit down again." ˢʰ

She did.

At the door, Yuta was accepting a cup from Rem. They both looked up when Yuka came out, and she had to shake her head before either could ask the question their faces had already made.

Nothing different. Not awake properly. Still warm.

Rem handed her another cup without requiring her to explain.

Yuka drank some while they stood in the corridor. The water tasted unexpectedly strong, as if she had forgotten there were things she could feel without first checking whether Mau could feel them too.

"Have you eaten since dinner?" Rem asked. ᴿᵉ

Yuka nodded.

Rem looked at Yuta.

He nodded too.

"Good," she said, accepting the answers without inviting either of them to prove it by eating in front of her. "There's something kept if that changes." ᴿᵉ

She went back toward the kitchen.

Yuka stayed beside her grandfather.

* * *

"I didn't think you would hurt him," she said after a while. ʸᵏ

Yuta watched the doorway.

"I thought it when I said it. For a second. Then I saw your face." ʸᵏ

She hated the correction, but the first version had not been true enough to give him.

Yuta looked down at his cup.

"I was frightened too." ʸᵗ

"I know." ʸᵏ

She folded her free hand around the one holding the water. Her fingers were cold. She had been keeping them still beside the bed so long that every movement felt too large.

"I keep thinking if we hadn't waited. If we'd told you when we came back—" ʸᵏ

"He had the second heartbeat before we knew," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

She looked up.

"Shoko checked him this morning. Before any of us left. This started before the conversation." ʸᵗ

Yuka heard the care in the answer. It did not let either of them change the facts of the other problem. It kept her from turning this one into something she could undo by regretting the right day.

"You're still angry," she said. ʸᵏ

Yuta closed his eyes briefly.

"Yes." ʸᵗ

She nodded.

"And I want him to wake up," he added. ʸᵗ

Yuka looked toward the clinic. The two things had become much easier for her to understand when he put them side by side instead of making her choose which one ought to be true.

She finished the water.

"Can I go back in?" ʸᵏ

"Ask Shoko. You don't need to ask me." ʸᵗ

She looked at him once more before going.

He stayed in the corridor with the cup between his hands.

* * *

Mau spoke just after midnight.

Yuka had been looking at the seam of the blanket, trying to remember whether the little repair near his shoulder had always been there. She heard the beginning of a breath and looked up.

His eyes were half open.

"Mau?" ʸᵏ

He looked toward her voice. For a second she thought he had found her. Then his gaze shifted past her, searching the wall.

"The door," he said. ᴹ

She turned. It stood open a little way, enough for light from the corridor to reach the floor.

"It's open. Do you want it closed?" ʸᵏ

His fingers tightened weakly around hers.

"Don't—" ᴹ

The rest was lost in a breath.

Shoko came to the bed. She asked him his name, whether he knew where he was, whether he could look toward her hand. Yuka waited, trying not to supply the answers quickly enough that they could mistake hers for his.

Mau looked at Shoko.

His brow drew together.

"Cold," he said. ᴹ

Shoko checked him before changing the blanket. Yuka helped settle it where she was asked, careful of the hand she wanted so badly to keep holding.

"You're in the clinic," Shoko told him. "I'm here. Yuka's here." ˢʰ

He looked toward Yuka again.

"You can sleep," she said softly. "I'll be here." ʸᵏ

His eyes closed.

She waited until his fingers loosened before letting herself look away.

* * *

Ciel called him through the quiet.

She had repeated his name at intervals, adjusted the questions, reduced what she asked of him when asking seemed to draw no response. The link remained. The measurements she could follow changed slowly enough to compare. She could not turn either fact into a report that he was choosing not to answer.

"Mau. It is Ciel." ᶜ

No reply.

In Rimuru, she kept the information separate from the fear of what it might mean. He listened to both. She had learned enough of him to know that giving only the measurements would not keep him from hearing the rest.

"Can I do something?" he asked privately. ᴿ

"Stay available. I may need you to repeat a change to Shoko. She is comparing the physical signs." ᶜ

Rimuru looked across the room.

Yuka had one hand over Mau's again. Shoko was writing down the time of the brief waking. The movement of her pen was unhurried by effort rather than ease.

"And you?" Rimuru asked. ᴿ

Ciel took longer with that answer.

"I would like him to answer." ᶜ

Rimuru rested his elbows on his knees.

"I know." ᴿ

He stayed where she could reach him without having to explain the need again.

* * *

Before the next check, Shoko made them arrange the rest of the night.

There would be somebody nearby. There did not need to be everybody. She was staying in the clinic; Maomao would return at the time they had agreed; Rimuru could sleep close enough to be reached through Ciel. Yuka could remain for now if she rested instead of trying to keep Mau awake by watching him.

Yuta went to find Maki, who had been collecting the things Shoko asked for rather than standing helplessly at the bedside.

They met at the end of the corridor.

Maki looked at his face and put down the folded cloths.

For a moment he tried to tell her the medical part. Two rhythms, still breathing, a few words, no cause. She let him finish it before putting a hand against his arm.

He looked down at the hand.

"I was asking him why he didn't tell me," he said. ʸᵗ

"I was there." ᴹᵏ

"I know. I just keep hearing where I was when he stopped answering." ʸᵗ

Maki moved nearer.

"You were talking to him. You were allowed to talk to him." ᴹᵏ

He closed his eyes.

She stayed until he could open them again without immediately looking toward the clinic.

Inside, the repaired chair no longer made a sound when Yuka shifted. She sat back at last, her hand still near Mau's, and dozed in short intervals between the ordinary noises of people trying to get him through the night.
