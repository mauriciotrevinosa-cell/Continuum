*When: Day477, afternoon. Shoko asks him to take the cup with both hands.*

Yuta was standing at the foot of the bed when Mau tried.

His right hand found the cup first. The left came up more slowly, fingers spreading before they reached the warm side. Shoko kept her own hand beneath it, taking the weight while he worked out the position.

Two fingers did not close.

Mau looked down.

At first Yuta thought he had simply missed the grip. It was the sort of thing a person did when tired, when the body had not caught up with the instruction. Then Mau tried again and the same two fingers stayed farther out than they should.

Shoko took the cup before it could tip.

"That's all right. We'll do the water another way. Put your hand down for a moment." ˢʰ

Mau obeyed.

Yuka stood up from the chair.

Shoko glanced at her, a quiet request to wait until she had finished asking.

"Does it hurt?" she asked Mau. ˢʰ

He considered his hand with an attention which made Yuta think he was trying to place it in a memory rather than answer the question.

"Stiff," he said. ᴹ

"Show me what it will do without forcing it." ˢʰ

He moved the other fingers. The two left behind made small efforts, then stopped. Shoko supported the hand, checked carefully, and did not attempt to force the movement he could not make himself.

Mau watched her hands.

"Don't pull," he said. ᴹ

She looked up at once.

"I'm not going to. Did someone pull them?" ˢʰ

He frowned.

For several seconds he seemed to be searching the room for a person who might explain the question. Yuta took one step nearer, then stopped before he could add another face to the work of looking.

"No," Mau said at last. ᴹ

He did not sound certain of which part he was answering.

Shoko let the hand rest again.

"We're not going to force it," she repeated. ˢʰ

Mau closed his eyes. His right hand remained half lifted, still expecting to hold the cup.

Yuka sat beside him and helped him drink the little amount Shoko allowed. He managed that, then lay back exhausted by the effort of something which had required no thought a week earlier.

* * *

Shoko checked the hand again when he slept.

Yuta stayed because she asked him what he remembered from practice. He described the last morning: the blade, the sanding, the way Mau had held the handle and turned it in his fingers. Nothing had made him favor the hand. Nothing had looked stiff.

"That was the morning before I went to her door," he said. ʸᵗ

He heard himself add the detail and wished it had not become the easiest way to place the day.

Shoko did not comment on that part.

"And before the collapse?" ˢʰ

"He had both hands on the chair. I wasn't checking the fingers. I didn't see him hurt them." ʸᵗ

"I don't think you missed an injury in the room." ˢʰ

Yuta looked at her.

"I'm asking what you saw. You don't need to defend catching him." ˢʰ

He nodded, and gave her only what he knew.

She compared it with her own examination from the day the second rhythm appeared. Then she examined the little marks along the forearm which Yuka had noticed while helping with the cup.

They were closed.

No new blood. No place where a bandage had stuck. The skin carried a pale, uneven line which looked as if it had been healed for a long time, though Shoko had been looking at that same arm through days in which it had not been there.

Yuka stood beside the bed, trying to keep her own hands from touching every new thing to prove it existed.

"It can't be from the gate," she said. ʸᵏ

Shoko looked toward her.

"We saw the hand. Both times. I helped wrap the knuckles. It wasn't like this." ʸᵏ

"I agree," Shoko said. ˢʰ

Yuka seemed to lose a small amount of tension at being believed without having to build a whole argument.

Then she looked back at the marks, and the relief had nowhere to go.

* * *

Gojo came after the next rest.

He listened to Shoko's description, looked where she asked him to, and stayed for less time than he wanted because looking harder was making his own habits unhelpful.

Yuta followed him as far as the table outside.

"You see it too," he said. ʸᵗ

Gojo sat down.

"I see that there's a lot more to look at than there was." ᴳ

"That's not an answer." ʸᵗ

"It's the one I have." ᴳ

Yuta looked at him, caught by the absence of the usual ease.

Gojo turned the blindfold in his hand.

"I can recognize an information problem when it behaves enough like one I've had. That doesn't make every part behind it mine to understand." ᴳ

Yuta sat on the other chair.

He had spent years carrying the knowledge that his teacher had been a person other people expected to know what to do. Sitting across from him now, in a house neither had been born into, he could see how quickly the expectation returned when he was frightened.

"Sorry," he said. ʸᵗ

Gojo glanced up.

"For wanting an answer?" ᴳ

"For asking like you were keeping it somewhere." ʸᵗ

Gojo looked toward the clinic door.

"I'd like it very much if I were." ᴳ

They stayed at the table until Shoko came out to get something from the shelf. She looked at the two of them, took the thing she needed, and told Gojo to eat before returning to ask another question.

He complained about her tone.

It was a weak complaint, but Yuta was glad to hear it.

* * *

Mau recognized him at the next waking.

Yuta knew because the gaze settled. Mau looked at the chair first, then followed the person in it all the way to his face.

"Yuta," he said. ᴹ

Yuta leaned forward.

"I'm here." ʸᵗ

Mau's right hand moved on the blanket. Yuta put his own near enough that he could reach it without having to lift the arm.

For a moment Mau looked at the hand instead of taking it.

"You caught—" ᴹ

"I caught you. You didn't hit anything." ʸᵗ

Mau nodded a little.

His gaze shifted past Yuta's shoulder. The focus began to loosen again, carrying him toward something he could not keep in the room.

Yuta wanted to ask him a dozen questions. Which parts he remembered. Whether he knew his eyes had changed. Whether the pain had a shape he could name. Whether anything anyone said before he fell had reached him as anger rather than love trying to speak through anger.

He asked none of them while Mau was losing the ability to answer.

"You can rest," he said. ʸᵗ

Mau's fingers touched his for a second.

Then the hand settled again.

Yuta stayed until Shoko came to check him, and left the chair ready for whoever would sit there next.
