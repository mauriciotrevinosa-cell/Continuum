*When: Day488, afternoon. Shoko puts a cup into Rimuru's hand and closes his fingers round it when he does not notice.*

"Sit or stand," she said. "Pick one. You're trying to do both." ˢʰ

He sat on a low stone beside the path.

Anko had stopped the next soldier at the top of the steps. It waited with a bundle of limbs held in both arms while she pointed at a clear patch farther from the house. When it did not understand the first gesture, she walked there and made it follow.

Gojo stood where Rimuru could see him.

"Is he alive?" he asked. ᴳ

"Yes," Rimuru said. "He's in the inner chamber. I couldn't get through the seal. The things carrying—" He stopped, swallowed. "They can. They belong to the challenger. The frame lets them pass." ᴿ

"Did you hear him?" ˢʰ

"No. Not his voice. I heard—" ᴿ

He looked at the cargo and could not finish that either.

Shoko waited. He hated how grateful he was that she knew to.

*Rimuru,* Ciel said privately. *Tell me what I need to know. You don't have to show me.* ᶜ

He shut his eyes.

*The garrison's made. Not people from outside. A lot of them are dead. He's still in there.* ᴿ

*Can you reach him through the knight?* ᶜ

He opened his eyes on Igris.

"You can go to him," he said. "You can carry a message." ᴿ

The knight bowed once.

"*I can ask him to answer.*" ᴵᵍ

"Then ask." ᴿ

* * *

Beru had been watching the emerging soldiers.

He stood beside the frame, tall and black, the long arms folded before him. His antennae moved with each load. When a soldier passed with a crystal so large it needed both hands, the insect's head rose a little higher.

"*My King was showing his grandeur,*" he said. ᴮᵉʳᵘ

Anko stopped beside the empty patch.

Shoko looked up from Rimuru's hand.

The voice had come from the insect. Precise. Almost tender. It made the creature a person in the space between one breath and the next, and the words it chose made that discovery worse.

"*He put a false king in his place until he lost the will to fight.*" Beru looked toward the frame. Something shone below the long dark eyes. "*My King. So powerful. So great.*" ᴮᵉʳᵘ

He was crying.

Not pretending. Not laughing at them. Tears gathered and fell with the wonder of a subject who had seen his king do something magnificent and could not understand why the people waiting outside had not begun to praise him.

"*Beru,*" Igris said. ᴵᵍ

The insect turned.

He looked genuinely puzzled.

Behind him the dragon lowered his immense head.

"*Beru.*" ᴷᵃ

This time the insect went quiet.

* * *

Kamish looked at Rimuru.

The great head came down slowly, without forcing him to retreat. The voice had weight enough that he felt it in the stone beneath him.

"*Slime. The small monarch needs you.*" ᴷᵃ

Rimuru looked up.

"*These are monsters that are made,*" the dragon said. "*Not real people.*" ᴷᵃ

"I saw that." Rimuru's voice came out sharper than he intended. "I know what they are. That isn't—" ᴿ

He stopped. Found the words this time.

"He did that. Mau did it. And you know what I'm asking you when I ask why." ᴿ

The old dragon held his gaze.

"*I know.*" ᴷᵃ

"Then help me get to him." ᴿ

"*The knight will take your words. I will hold the outside. We are not keeping you from him.*" ᴷᵃ

Rimuru stood.

He gave Shoko the cup before he dropped it and went to Igris.

* * *

They went back inside together.

The lamps were burning steadily now. The head had not moved from the ironwork. Rimuru made himself look away before he reached the place where he had bent over. He knew where the inner door was. He could walk there without studying what lay on either side of the route.

Igris walked beside him.

At the sealed doors the knight stopped, bent his head and went very still. Rimuru could feel no conversation to join. Only the sense of something listening farther inward than he could reach.

"Tell him I'm here," Rimuru said. "Tell him I don't need him to finish anything for me." ᴿ

The helm stayed bowed.

A dark soldier stepped through the light carrying the top half of an armored torso. It had to turn sideways to pass them. Rimuru moved without looking at the open end of it.

Igris raised his head.

"*He says he can finish. He says he will come out.*" ᴵᵍ

"Tell him I want him now." ᴿ

The knight waited again.

This time there was no answer he could pass on.

Rimuru put his hand against the seal.

He could feel pressure on its other side. Not a person trapped with no air. A place keeping its own condition, waiting for the person inside to end the encounter.

"He can end it," Rimuru said. ᴿ

"*Yes.*" ᴵᵍ

The answer hurt more than another barrier would have.

* * *

Outside, Gojo listened to what Rimuru had found, then inspected the frame himself.

He did not throw a technique at Mahoraga. The pale shikigami watched him move past and stayed where Mau had put it. Gojo looked once at the wheel, and then at the active pattern across the doorway.

"The hallway's open," he said after a while. "The encounter isn't. Pulling stone out of the wall won't change that. I can make a large mess here. It won't tell us where to put our hands around him." ᴳ

He sounded angry. Not because he had been challenged to show how strong he was. Because there was someone behind a rule he could read and could not usefully threaten.

"We keep asking," Rimuru said. ᴿ

Igris bowed.

Anko came back up the path with gloves and two people carrying an empty board between them. Yuta was one of them. He saw Rimuru's face and did not ask a question that would have to wait for an answer anyway.

"Where?" he said. ʸᵗ

Anko showed him.

* * *

The ground changed around the frame as the afternoon went on.

There were places to set the cargo now. Clear lanes where nobody stood while a soldier came through with too much weight to stop quickly. A place for crystals and another for the bodies which needed covering before anybody else walked down the path without knowing what they would see.

Anko counted loads instead of pretending she could count what was inside every load. She marked the unusual ones. She did not let anyone take a crystal away to see whether it would light a lamp.

Shoko had a clear space ready for Mau and kept telling people it was for Mau, not for more cargo. Maki brought cloth. Yuta brought water and the steadiness of someone who was frightened enough to have become very precise about where he put his feet.

When Yuka came, Maki met her before the first covered board.

"Rimuru's trying to get him," she said. ᴹᵏ

Yuka looked at the black soldiers.

She had joked about asking an army to build a sandcastle. She could still hear herself saying it.

"Where is he?" ʸᵏ

Maki answered the question she asked. Then stayed beside her while she looked at the frame.

* * *

Frieren came as far as the turn with Ori in her arms.

She stopped when she saw the covered shapes and the blade above the pale shikigami's arm. Ori had been asking something. The child went quiet when her mother did.

Rimuru looked up from Igris's latest silence.

Frieren met his eye. Took in what he could not put into a sentence at that distance. Then turned before Ori could look round her shoulder at the ground.

"We're going back inside," she told her. ᶠ

"But Mau—" ᴼ

"Rimuru's bringing him. We're making somewhere for him to come back to." ᶠ

Ori held the back of her coat. Frieren carried her away without pretending she had not heard the fear in her voice.

At the frame Beru watched them go. His antennae had lowered.

He did not say anything about grandeur again.

* * *

By the time the light lowered, Rimuru had gone to the inner boundary enough times to recognise a new scrape in the stone beside it.

He had left it himself, trying another edge, another angle, a way to make an opening narrow enough to reach through. Every attempt came back to the same active condition. He could accompany the knight to the barrier. Could send words beyond it. Could not make Mau choose the end.

He came out again with Igris beside him and a line of soldiers carrying the next load.

Two hours, Anko's page said. Since the first one emerged. It did not feel like a measure of time so much as something the whole path had begun carrying.

Yuka stood with Maki. Gojo had not put his blindfold down. Shoko's cup had gone cold without being touched.

Then Igris stopped.

His hand moved to the hilt of his sword, not to draw it. A gesture of attention. The place where he received a command had changed.

The light inside the frame shifted.

"*Clear,*" he said. ᴵᵍ

Rimuru was already turning.

"*The inner doors are open. My liege has one new servant with him. He asks for you.*" ᴵᵍ

*Rimuru,* Ciel said, hearing the words through him. *He's finished. You can go to him now.* ᶜ

He handed nobody a cup this time. He had put it down long ago.

He went in.
