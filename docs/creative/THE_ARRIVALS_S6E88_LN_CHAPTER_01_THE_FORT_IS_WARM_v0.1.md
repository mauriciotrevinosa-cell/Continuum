*When: Day488, evening. Yuta brings a clean shirt to the little space Shoko has made beside the path. He has brought the one with the loose cuff because it will go over the hand without pulling.*

Mau recognised it.

That nearly undid him again. Of all the things Yuta could have brought, the plain practicality of a shirt which would not hurt to put on left him looking at the cloth for too long to answer when Shoko asked him to lift his arm.

She asked once more.

He obeyed.

Yuta held the clean sleeve open. They did the rest without making Mau speak until he could.

"Thank you," he said. ᴹ

Yuta nodded. Took the ruined outer layer away with the same care he had used to bring the new one.

* * *

The four waited until Mau could stand.

He looked at them from beside Rimuru. At the knight, the insect, the old dragon with his head low enough to listen, and the pale shikigami whose wheel held still above him.

"Back," he said. "All of you. Thank you. I don't need you to keep anybody out now." ᴹ

Igris bowed and passed the sheathed katana to Yuta before going down. Yuta took it up to the shelf in Mau's east room and came back while Shoko was still helping Mau stand. The blade would not have to travel through the common room beside him.

Beru looked at his king for a moment. The proud brightness had gone from the insect's face. He lowered his head and sank after the knight without speaking.

Kamish took longer, because everything about him took longer. His great shape folded into dark, and the grass behind him appeared a patch at a time.

Mahoraga disappeared last from the other access, the wheel going with him. There was no new registration and no rush of borrowed fuel. Only the return of the space he had occupied.

Mau felt for the new one inside and told him to rest too.

Then he went home with Rimuru's arm supporting most of what he would not admit was weight.

* * *

He meant to sit for a minute.

The common room was warmer than the path. Someone had moved the nearest low table away so there was room to get him to the seat without turning sideways. There were familiar voices farther off, carefully ordinary. No one had begun asking questions across the room.

Mau sat down.

Yuka knelt to take his boots off before he could decide he ought to do it himself. One had something dark stuck in the seam. She set it farther away, where Ori would not trip over it if she came down.

He looked at the top of Yuka's head.

"I need to tell you," he said. ᴹ

She looked up at once.

"I know. Not like this." ʸᵏ

"I keep saying—" ᴹ

Rimuru sat beside him. Shoko moved the lamp nearer, then farther away when Mau flinched at the change in brightness.

"One question," she said. "Are you cold?" ˢʰ

"Yes." ᴹ

She gave Maki a look. A blanket arrived before he could track where it came from.

He took a breath, put his right hand on the seat beside him, and tried to find the first sentence.

When the breath left, his head went down against Rimuru's shoulder.

He did not find the sentence.

* * *

Yuka reached him before anyone said anything.

Not to shake him. To put a hand against the side of his face and wait for his eyes to open.

They did not.

"Mau?" ʸᵏ

Shoko took the wrist. Rimuru kept the weight where it was instead of making him slide farther into the seat.

Ciel was already speaking privately.

*His answers slowed before he slept. They didn't disappear mid-answer. His rhythms are steady. Rimuru, this is different from the fever. He's exhausted.* ᶜ

He passed it on. Carefully, as an observation, not a reason for Shoko to stop examining him.

Mau made an annoyed sound when she moved his hand. Tried to draw it back beneath the blanket.

Shoko looked at Yuka.

"He's asleep. We're still watching him. But he's asleep." ˢʰ

Yuka sat back on her heels.

She had not known how much she had been holding upright until she no longer needed to.

* * *

They moved him to the Fort because it was warm and already there.

The big structure at the north end of the common room, with blankets layered in places that made no architectural sense and perfect sense to the people who slept in it. They made room near an edge Shoko could reach easily. Someone took out a cushion; someone else put it back and was told why it had been removed.

Umaru untied a length of cloth from the opening and rolled it clear.

"That side," she said, pointing. "It holds." ᵁ

Rimuru carried the last part of Mau's weight. Yuta steadied the blanket. They got him down without making the old hand bear anything.

He woke enough to object to the cold air, then curled toward the warmth and stopped.

Yuka sat outside the opening until Shoko said she could move in beside him. Beside, not underneath his weight, not holding herself awake because everybody else intended to leave.

Rimuru stayed close. Shoko had brought her things. Maki sat where she could see both the opening and the room.

Yuka took Mau's right hand and found it warmer now.

* * *

Anko came in after the last covered load had been set down.

She stopped well short of the Fort. Waited until Shoko looked up.

"The castle's marked exhausted," she said. "Its collection's done. The frame's still there. It's not a door back to that room anymore." ᴬ

"Everything out?" Rimuru asked. ᴿ

"Everything we accepted from them. Samples separate. Nothing in the kitchen. Nothing near a child." Her mouth tightened. "Most of it's on his collection list. The soldiers brought the tags with the loads. I want him to explain the list when he can read it." ᴬ

Rimuru nodded.

"Later," Shoko said. ˢʰ

"Later," Anko agreed. "I'm not asking him now." ᴬ

Nazuna was waiting near the door with another folded cloth. She passed it to Anko when Anko noticed that her own sleeve was stained and had been for some time.

They went back out together. The ordinary night work had not stopped because the day had become terrible. It had acquired things nobody wanted to add to it.

* * *

For a while Mau slept quietly.

Yuka's hand rested beside his instead of round it once she was sure he would not wake without it. She watched his breathing until the watching began turning into her own sleep. When she caught herself going, Shoko told her to lie down properly or go to bed.

She lay down.

There was room. That was what the Fort was for. Her shoulder touched a blanket, not his injured hand. Somewhere beyond the opening Rimuru spoke softly to Yuta and got a short answer. She could not hear the words and did not try.

Mau moved closer to the warmth in his sleep.

She closed her eyes.

* * *

He dreamed of a hand he could not open.

Not one battle clearly enough to call it a memory. A pressure against his palm. Something he needed to pick up before it fell. He saw a small bright field, the familiar place where an answer waited if he made the right movement.

His fingers moved.

In the dark behind his closed eyes, a page opened which he had never deliberately opened awake.

*Personal equipment.*

Two narrow shapes. A weight which ought to fit one hand each. A space beneath them where the day had been added up as something he could spend.

He was trying to stop something falling.

He accepted.

* * *

Yuka woke because there was metal between them.

Not touching her. Present where a moment before there had been his empty hands.

Two daggers lay along his palms, their edges angled out from the blanket. One of his hands was trying to close around the grip in his sleep. The other was failing to do the same thing, the two stiff fingers leaving a gap.

She stopped breathing.

"Mau." ʸᵏ

He did not answer.

She drew back slowly, keeping the blanket from catching on either point.

"Shoko." ʸᵏ

*Mau,* Ciel was saying privately. *Wake up. Open your eyes. There's something in your hands.* ᶜ

The movement had reached her too late to stop what it had already accepted. Now she could feel the weight and the waking body trying to understand it.

Shoko was at the opening.

"Leave space," she said. "Don't pull the cloth." ˢʰ

Yuka moved back another little way. Mau opened his eyes.

* * *

He looked at the blades without recognising them.

Then at Yuka, who was sitting very still beyond them.

"Sorry," he said, voice thick with sleep. "Bad dream. I—" ᴹ

He looked down again.

The grips were real. The weight was real. He knew how to hold a blade and knew he had not had these in the room where he last shut his eyes.

A small field waited beside the right one.

*Store.*

He did it with the smallest movement he could make.

Both weapons disappeared.

Yuka looked at his empty hands.

"That's new," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau stared at them too.

Shoko took each one before he could hide them under the blanket. Checked the skin. Found no cut to explain what had happened.

"Do you know where those came from?" she asked. ˢʰ

He shook his head.

"Then we find out when you're awake enough to answer properly. Nothing else gets opened tonight." ˢʰ

He nodded.

Yuka came back only after he had opened both empty palms for her to see. She sat beside him, took the right one, and waited until he looked at her rather than the place where the blades had been.

"I'm here," she said. "Go slowly." ʸᵏ

He tried to answer. Fell asleep before the whole answer came out.

She did not mistake that for knowing everything was all right.
