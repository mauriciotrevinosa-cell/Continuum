# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 36
## Chapter 29 — What She Wanted

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S6 v0.1 (2026-10-25)
**Volume:** 36
**Reading order:** 29
**Source episode:** S6E92
**Continuity state:** Day490 evening. Yuka chooses a private conversation with Yuta, Maki and Mai; Mau is absent. They address the secret and their own failure to make talking easy, affirm her agency and love, and reconcile instead of demanding breakup. Family approval rests on Mau's character and observed care as well as capacity to protect, not ownership/permission or imported original promises. Her original medical confidence stays private. They choose together to request Tsurugi with the sole existing Goddess coin; consent/arrival and actual coin use remain for later. Yuta subsequently tells Mau directly their friendship survives and the anger over concealment need not be kept alive as a test. A second ordinary evening gives family processing lived time.

*When: Day490, after dinner. Mai brings her sewing and discovers that nobody has invited her to sew.*

"You could have said," she told Yuka. ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka watched her put the little bundle on the table.

"Would you have come without it?" ʸᵏ

"I'd have brought something else to do with my hands." ᴹᵃⁱ

She sat beside Maki. Yuta had already pulled out a fourth chair. He had chosen a place where nobody would be between Yuka and the door, an arrangement so deliberate that she noticed it and loved him for trying.

Then she wished he had not needed to think about it.

"You don't have to make it a meeting," she said. ʸᵏ

Yuta looked at the chairs, caught himself, and moved his closer to the table.

"All right." ʸᵗ

That helped more than a speech about how she was free to leave.

* * *

For a moment Yuka forgot the first sentence she had spent the afternoon making.

Maki waited. Mai wound the loose thread round her sewing bundle without opening it. Yuta rested his hands on his knees.

"I really love him," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

There it was. Much shorter than all the versions she had practised.

She put her hands on the table before she could hide them.

"I know I should have told you. I know finding out like that hurt. I kept thinking if we had one more day when it was just ours, I'd be ready. Then I wanted another one." ʸᵏ

Maki's eyes moved over her face.

"Were you afraid we'd tell you to end it?" ᴹᵏ

Yuka nodded.

"And I didn't want every time we sat together to become people looking at us, trying to decide whether it was serious yet. I wanted to find out myself." ʸᵏ

Mai let the thread go.

"You could have told us that too." ᴹᵃⁱ

"I know now." ʸᵏ

"Good." Mai paused. "I don't know if I made it sound very possible before." ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka looked at her.

"You weren't wrong to expect me to have an opinion," Mai said. "I have several. They escape." ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuta's mouth moved. Maki looked at her sister with the tired familiarity of someone who could have supplied examples for an hour.

Yuka laughed, and the first breath after it was easier.

* * *

"Yesterday scared me," Yuka said. "The size of what I didn't know. I don't think I can pretend it didn't just because I love him." ʸᵏ

"You don't need to," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

She looked at him carefully.

"But I talked to him. And he listened. He didn't tell me it was all right because he could protect me. He let me say what I needed to say." She rubbed her thumb over the repaired edge of her cuff. "I can see that he loves me. I want to see where this goes." ʸᵏ

Nobody interrupted with how young she was, how old he might be, or how much of the story remained difficult to understand.

She had been ready to answer all three. Having room to continue made her eyes sting.

"Not because I know what the other me wanted. I don't. This is what I want." ʸᵏ

Maki reached across the table and put her hand over Yuka's.

"I believe you." ᴹᵏ

Yuka looked down at the hand.

It had been years since she had seen it so young in her first life. Long enough here that she sometimes forgot to be astonished, and then a small familiar touch brought the whole impossible kindness back.

"I watched him listen yesterday when you said you wanted to ask him something later," Maki said. "He didn't reach for you to make later mean right now. He told us he remembered a future you hadn't asked him for here. He still left you room." ᴹᵏ

Yuka's mouth trembled.

"That matters to me," Maki continued. "More than hearing how much stone he can break." ᴹᵏ

"He can break a lot of stone," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

Maki glanced at him.

"I'm getting to the reassuring part." ʸᵗ

Mai folded her arms.

"Do try." ᴹᵃⁱ

* * *

Yuta moved his chair a little closer.

"I trust him to stand beside you when something goes wrong," he said. "He can protect you. He has done it. But I also trust him to be the person who asks whether you still want to go swimming when everything he's planned has already taken half the morning." ʸᵗ

Yuka looked up in surprise.

"He told you that?" ʸᵏ

"He told me three different reasons it might have been too cold. I think he wanted someone to confirm you'd have said so if it was." ʸᵗ

She put a hand over her face.

Mai made a sound which was almost a laugh.

"Yes. That sounds like your choice." ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka lowered her hand.

"You think I chose badly?" ʸᵏ

Mai's expression changed at once.

"No. I think you chose someone who will worry about the temperature of the ocean after doing something nobody else can explain." She reached across to touch Yuka's wrist. "I think you chose well." ᴹᵃⁱ

Maki squeezed her hand once.

"So do I." ᴹᵏ

Yuka looked at Yuta last.

He nodded.

"If either of you needs help, come to us before it turns into another secret you have to protect. That's what I want from you. I don't want you to have to earn every happy afternoon by being afraid we'll take it away." ʸᵗ

She could not make an answer come out properly.

Maki got up before she had to try again and came round the table.

Yuka stood into her arms.

Mai put a hand against her back. Yuta remained sitting until Yuka reached out for his sleeve, then rose quickly enough to knock his chair against the table.

"Graceful," Mai said through the small thickness in her voice. ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka laughed against Maki's shoulder and held all of them as well as she could.

* * *

The coin came out after they sat down again.

Yuka unwrapped it on the table. It had stayed warm through everything, patient in a way none of them had managed to be.

"I'd like him here," she said. ʸᵏ

Nobody needed to ask whom she meant.

Yuta looked at the coin, then at the ring on the cord against her chest.

"Tsurugi will have questions," he said. ʸᵗ

"Tsurugi always has questions. He pretends they're statements." ʸᵏ

Maki laughed at that. Not because she had known this young man through all the years Yuka remembered. Because she had known enough of the boy to hear the family resemblance in the complaint.

"He should have a bed ready before we ask," she said. ᴹᵏ

Mai picked up her sewing at last.

"And someone should warn him that looking older than me won't make him my elder." ᴹᵃⁱ

"I'll make that the first thing I say," Yuka promised. ʸᵏ

They spent the next few minutes arranging a room and a meal, discussing what they could tell a person before he decided whether to stay. Yuka folded the coin back into its cloth. It remained on her side of the table.

* * *

Yuta found Mau beside the tree with an empty basket and a look which suggested he had taken far too long to decide where it belonged.

"Kitchen," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

Mau looked at the basket.

"I know." ᴹ

"You weren't going there very quickly." ʸᵗ

Mau made a small, guilty movement with his shoulder.

Yuta took the basket and set it on the step.

"We talked." ʸᵗ

"She said she wanted to." ᴹ

"She does. Want to be with you." Yuta paused. "I know you're not waiting for me to choose her feelings. I want you to hear what I'm choosing about mine." ʸᵗ

Mau grew still.

"I'm not keeping you angry at yourself so I can keep being angry at you," Yuta said. "You should have told me. You've said that. We've talked about why it hurt. I still want my friend." ʸᵗ

Mau looked away toward the roots.

"I'm here," he said, rather roughly. ᴹ

Yuta put a hand on his shoulder.

"I know. Come inside. Your girlfriend has plans." ʸᵗ

"Should I be frightened?" ᴹ

"Probably. She's related to Maki." ʸᵗ

"I heard that," Maki called from the doorway. ᴹᵏ

Yuta picked up the basket very promptly.
