Umaru inspected the army from beneath the hamster flag.

Igris had tied a strip of cloth around his arm. The soldiers waiting behind him wore the same mark. Bellion stood at her right with the plan, which had acquired a second secret passage during lunch.

Across the field, Beru guarded Yuka and the royal reserve.

Kamish's head rested above the low wall. Mau sat behind it, looking comfortable enough to annoy her.

"He's an observer," Ori reminded her. ᴼ

"I know." ᵁ

"You said he could choose." ᴼ

"I know that too." ᵁ

Beside the wall, Lantern opened one wing.

The route which had looked perfectly direct a moment ago now went round a dragon.

Umaru turned to Bellion.

"Did you know about that?" ᵁ

"The enemy is improving its position, Commander." ᴮˡ

"We need a better plan." ᵁ

He held out the pencil.

* * *

The rules were explained one last time.

A defender touched a cloth band, the attacker went back to the flag and counted to ten. An attacker got past the baskets, the defender had to let them try for the tin. Nobody grabbed, pushed or used anything sharper than Umaru's pencil, which Maki took from her before the whistle.

"For after," Maki said. ᴹᵏ

Umaru surrendered it.

Milim raised her hand.

"Can I fly?" ᴹⁱ

"No." ᵁ

"A little?" ᴹⁱ

"You can run a little." ᵁ

Milim considered whether that was a fair exchange.

Ori took her hand before the consideration could become an argument.

"Come with me. We know where the passage is." ᴼ

Bellion looked carefully in the other direction.

From the edge of the field, Nijika tested a short roll on the borrowed surface she had chosen for the occasion. Ryo supplied a low, ominous answer. Bocchi looked up from her strings to discover that everyone was waiting for her.

She managed a chord.

Kamish raised his head.

"Warned!" Ori called. "That counts as warned!" ᴼ

The roar was still enough to make Umaru's shoulders jump.

Beside her, Bellion lowered his hand.

The army advanced.

* * *

Igris reached the first line with a seriousness which would have suited a much less ridiculous engagement.

Beru stepped in front of him.

The two looked at each other.

Igris moved left.

Beru moved left.

Igris looked past him toward the treasure.

Beru turned just far enough to check that Yuka was comfortable, then returned his attention to the knight.

"You shall not pass." ᴮᵉ

Yuka pressed her lips together.

Igris reached for his cloth band. Beru touched it first.

The knight stopped.

Then he turned and walked all the way back to the hamster flag.

Umaru watched him arrive.

"You have to count." ᵁ

He began.

"Loud enough for us to hear." ᵁ

Mau bent forward on the dragon. Yuka put a hand against his arm before his laughter could make sitting there difficult.

"You can't help him," she reminded him. ʸᵏ

"I wasn't going to." ᴹ

"You're thinking very supportively." ʸᵏ

Below them, Igris reached ten.

Beru waited at the same place.

* * *

The secret passage worked better.

Ori and Milim went round the baskets on hands and knees. Suika followed, trying not to laugh whenever Milim remembered she was supposed to be crawling rather than floating.

They emerged behind Lantern's folded wing.

The great head turned.

Ori held up one finger.

"We're being quiet." ᴼ

Lantern lowered his jaw until it rested beside the passage.

They looked at him.

Then at one another.

Milim put her hand over her own mouth.

The dragon's eyes followed them as they crept around his nose.

At the far side, Yuka was waiting with the tin.

"Hello." ʸᵏ

Ori sat back on her heels.

"You weren't supposed to know." ᴼ

"I live near you." ʸᵏ

Yuka touched their bands, one after another. Milim fell over sideways with a sound sufficiently tragic that Kamish stopped watching the front line to look down at her.

Ori caught the side of her face before it reached the grass.

"You don't die. You go back to the flag." ᴼ

"Oh." ᴹⁱ

They went back together.

Lantern kept his chin beside the passage.

* * *

Bellion received the reports without looking discouraged.

He sent two soldiers toward the wall. They made it far enough for Yuka to stand up, hand the tin to Beru and intercept one herself.

The other got round her.

Kamish looked down.

The soldier looked up.

For a moment they remained that way.

Then the soldier turned toward headquarters and pointed at the dragon with both hands.

"Yes," Umaru called. "We know he's big!" ᵁ

Beside her, Bellion made a small adjustment to the plan.

"The defender is giving the treasure his full attention," he said. ᴮˡ

Beru had indeed tucked the tin against his chest. He stood close enough to Yuka that she could reach him without taking a step.

"Could we distract him?" Ori asked. ᴼ

Bellion considered the distance.

Then the observer on the dragon.

Mau noticed where he was looking and narrowed his eyes.

Bellion bowed.

Very politely.

* * *

"My king is in danger!" Igris announced. ᴵ

Beru turned.

Igris stood in the open, pointing upward.

Mau looked down at himself.

"Am I?" ᴹ

"No," Umaru shouted. "You're observing!" ᵁ

Beru moved anyway.

Only two steps. Then he stopped so suddenly that Yuka nearly walked into him.

He looked at the knight.

Looked at the tin in his own hands.

Looked back at Mau, who had both hands carefully raised to demonstrate that nothing was happening to him.

"A deception," Beru said. ᴮᵉ

From beside the flag came Bellion's laugh.

Beru drew himself up.

"I shall remember it." ᴮᵉ

Yuka took the tin before he could make his point by fastening it somewhere inaccessible.

"You're doing very well," she told him. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"Thank you, Little Rope." ᴮᵉ

Mau turned his face toward Kamish's neck and lost the argument with his laughter.

* * *

Gojo found the two commanders beside the flag.

"Trouble?" ᴳ

"A dragon," Umaru said. ᵁ

"I noticed." ᴳ

Ori looked past him. Shoko had come to the edge of the field with Maki. Neither looked inclined to offer a complicated military solution.

Gojo crouched beside the plan.

"You haven't tried the strongest attack." ᴳ

Umaru put her hands on her hips.

"We're not allowed attacks." ᵁ

"Friendship," he said. ᴳ

She stared at him.

Ori looked interested.

Across the field, Mau saw Gojo raise one finger beside his knee. A very small red light formed there. It pressed a loose fold of the flagcloth away before he closed his hand again.

Gojo looked up at him, then at the clear patch in front of Kamish's foreclaws.

Mau understood.

He leaned forward to tell the dragon.

Kamish's head turned toward the approaching commanders.

Behind them, Bellion began arranging the army into an audience.

* * *

Umaru and Ori stood together at the front line.

"Hands together," Gojo said. ᴳ

They joined hands.

"Eyes closed." ᴳ

Umaru closed one. Ori closed both.

"You too," Ori whispered. ᴼ

The other eye shut.

"Now," Gojo said. ᴳ

"Friendship!" Ori shouted. ᴼ

Umaru threw their joined hands forward with her.

Gojo's small Red passed beside them and struck the empty ground before the dragon's claws. Dust jumped. A few loose blades of grass lifted.

Kamish recoiled as though the sky had fallen on him.

Mau grabbed a ridge.

Yuka grabbed Mau.

The dragon gave a terrible groan. One wing rose, trembled and folded dramatically against his side. He lowered himself onto the patch they had left clear, eased his passengers close to the ground and let his enormous head sink into the grass.

The music stopped.

Ori opened her eyes.

Umaru opened hers.

Kamish's voice carried across the silent field.

"Defeated," he said. "By two noble citizens." ᴷᵃ

Beru looked at his fallen commander.

Then at Yuka.

With immense reluctance, he stepped aside.

Umaru ran for the tin. Ori went with her, laughing before they reached the wall.

Bellion saluted beneath the enormous hamster.

* * *

The treasure was recovered.

Lantern let the small army pass. Igris surrendered his position, then helped a soldier recover a basket which had lost its balance during Kamish's death.

Mau climbed down.

Umaru pointed at him.

"Prisoner." ᵁ

He looked behind himself.

"Me?" ᴹ

"You were in the enemy castle." ᵁ

"Observing." ᴹ

"You can observe from captivity." ᵁ

Yuka stepped down beside him.

Her shoulders were already shaking.

"What happens to the prisoner?" ʸᵏ

Ori consulted the rules.

This part had not been written down.

From the doorway, Momo called that anyone who wanted something sweet could help carry the cups.

Umaru thought for a moment.

Then she handed Mau two empty baskets.

"Community service." ᵁ
