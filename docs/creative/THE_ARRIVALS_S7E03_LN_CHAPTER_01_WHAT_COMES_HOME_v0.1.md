## Chapter 5 — What Comes Home

*When: Day497, morning. Just inside the gate, Mau waits for the answer he was told to check.*

*Still here,* Ciel said privately, once he was across. ᶜ

He let his breath out.

"She can reach Rimuru," he told the others. ᴹ

Anko marked the check on her page. Then they went down the passage together.

* * *

The first room held nothing that wanted to fight them.

Stark disliked that almost as much as the alternative.

He looked into the shallow recess beside the doorway, checked the broken channel along the floor and waited while Anko compared the turn to her notes. Mau called Igris behind them before they went farther.

The knight bowed.

"Our way back," Mau said. "And anything we miss. Let us do the rest." ᴹ

Igris took the rear without drawing attention to it.

Yuka's white dog moved ahead of her, stopped at the next opening and raised its head.

Stark tightened his grip on the axe.

Something scraped beyond the wall.

Mau moved far enough to see it. Low to the ground, scales bunched across its shoulders, a short horn scraping stone as it pushed around the corner. A second shape came behind it.

Stark made a small, unhappy sound.

Then the first creature rushed him and he stepped into its path.

The axe met it before the horn could. Its front legs folded, but the weight kept coming. Stark turned with the impact instead of letting it drive him straight back. The haft struck the edge of the doorway.

Yuka's rope snapped across the second creature's path.

"Left," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau was already moving.

He caught the raised edge of a scale with his left forearm rather than trying to close that hand round it. The push carried more force than he had intended. The creature struck the wall and tried to turn.

Yuka pulled the rope clear of his feet.

The dagger was in his right hand.

One short movement finished it. He stopped the blade before it reached the stone and kept it still while he found where everyone else was.

Stark had planted a boot against the first creature's shoulder. He drew the axe free and stepped away.

Yuka's dog watched the opening behind them.

Anko waited until nothing moved.

"Everyone?" ᴬⁿ

They answered her.

Mau stored the dagger only after checking it. Stark inspected the place on his haft which had met the wall.

"It's all right," he said, before Anko had asked. ˢᵗ

"Good. I wasn't going to carry you and the axe." ᴬⁿ

He gave her a wounded look.

She waited.

He checked it again.

* * *

There were two crystals.

Anko put each in its own wrapping before either could vanish into a pocket. Yuka marked where the creatures had fallen. Mau looked at the shadows beneath the still bodies, then back at Igris.

He did not ask the word to happen.

"What?" Yuka asked quietly. ʸᵏ

"Thinking." ᴹ

"About bringing one back?" ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She looked toward the knight at the door.

"Then tell us when you want to try. We can make time for it." ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

She was already putting her charcoal away.

"It doesn't have to be the thing you do while we're all looking somewhere else." ʸᵏ

"All right," he said. ᴹ

Anko had found a storage niche beyond the next arch. Its door was already hanging open. She let Stark check the recess and the hinges before crouching beside it.

"Not treasure enough to bite," she said. "Still worth looking." ᴬⁿ

Inside lay a small bar of dull metal and two bent straps. One strap had a broken fastening still attached.

Stark turned the bar over.

"Iron?" ˢᵗ

"Something for Wakana to be annoyed about if we guess," Anko said. ᴬⁿ

Mau held the gathering bag open.

"Not with the bread," he said. ᴹ

Yuka smiled at him.

* * *

They stopped in the clear first room on the way back.

Stark sat with the axe across his knees. He rubbed the small mark in the haft with his thumb, then stopped when Yuka offered him bread.

"You weren't waiting for me," he said to Mau. ˢᵗ

Mau looked up.

"At the doorway. You left it to me." ˢᵗ

"You were there first." ᴹ

Stark considered that.

"It was heavier than it looked." ˢᵗ

"I saw." ᴹ

"I thought it would stop." ˢᵗ

"So did it, probably," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

Stark laughed with his mouth full and nearly regretted it.

Mau took the water when it reached him. Yuka sat close enough that their knees touched. She had put the rope down between her feet, carefully coiled, and was watching the dog at the far side of the room.

"I want to do more with it," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau followed her gaze.

"The technique?" ᴹ

"Mine. The shapes I haven't learned to call yet." ʸᵏ

He nodded slowly.

"When we get back, we can talk about how." ᴹ

"You said there was something you could explain. Let's start with that, and ask the people who can help." ʸᵏ

He glanced toward Anko.

She had heard and made no attempt to pretend otherwise.

"That would be refreshing," she said. ᴬⁿ

* * *

They found the moss near the exit.

It grew in a seam where water had collected behind the stone. A few pale roots ran beneath it. Anko stopped Mau before he could gather it with the cloth he had used for the metal.

"Separate." ᴬⁿ

"I know." ᴹ

"You keep saying that while reaching for the same bag." ᴬⁿ

Yuka held out the little spare container.

Mau took a small sample, enough to leave the rest undisturbed. He could already imagine Daphne wanting to know what it had been growing in, rather than whether it looked interesting.

He took a little of that too.

Outside, the light was brighter than he had expected. Rimuru was still by the marker when they came through.

Stark set down the gathering bag with a sound which made Anko point at him.

"Carefully." ᴬⁿ

"That was carefully." ˢᵗ

"Then we have work to do." ᴬⁿ

Rimuru looked from the metal to the wrapped crystals.

"Successful?" ᴿ

Yuka held up the bread cloth. There was one piece left.

"We even brought this home." ʸᵏ

Mau took it from her before she could give it away.

"That's my lunch." ᴹ

"Then don't put it beside the soil." ʸᵏ

* * *

At the house, Fern cleared a place on the table.

Anko laid down the two crystals. Stark put the bar and straps beside them; Yuka added the notes. Mau kept the soil container in his hand until Daphne came to look.

"What stays?" Fern asked. ᶠᵉ

They looked at what they had brought.

For a moment nobody reached for the crystals.

Wakana picked up the broken fastening.

"Can I see what this used to hold?" ᵂ

Daphne took the sample from Mau.

"And tell me where you found this." ᴰᵃ

Fern turned to a clean part of the page.

"All right," she said. "We start there." ᶠᵉ
