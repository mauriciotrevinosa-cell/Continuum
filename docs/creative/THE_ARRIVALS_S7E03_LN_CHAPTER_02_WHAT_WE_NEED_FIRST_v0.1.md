*When: Day497, after lunch. The things on the table are worth more arguments than their size suggests.*

Wakana wanted to bend the strap.

Fern wanted to know whether that would make it impossible to sell.

"Probably," he said. ᵂ

She looked at him.

"Then tell me what we'd learn first." ᶠᵉ

He set the strap beside the bar, turned it over and pointed to the fastening.

"Whether the weak part is the metal or the way it was put together. If it's the fastening, I can do something with that. If it's all brittle—" ᵂ

He stopped, searching for a useful comparison.

Stark held up the part of his axe haft which had caught the wall.

"I'd rather find out here." ˢᵗ

Fern nodded and wrote a word beside the strap.

*Test.*

Jinshi, standing with the second sheet, copied the rest of the items while she asked Wakana what he needed. He waited until she looked over before adding the soil container to a separate column.

"That isn't a sale item," Daphne said. ᴰᵃ

"Which is why it isn't with the sale items." ᴶ

She leaned over to check.

"Good." ᴰᵃ

* * *

Mau had expected to explain what they had fought.

He did that. Then Stark explained the impact at the doorway, Yuka showed how her rope had gone round the corner, and Anko asked which damaged objects people would actually want the party to look for next time.

That took longer than the fight.

Momo wanted a container which closed properly and could be cleaned. Not a box with an interesting curse, she specified. A box.

Wakana wanted fastenings which did not pull apart when the weight changed.

Daphne wanted to know whether the roots had reached the water, or whether the damp stayed in the seam long enough for them to grow without it.

"I didn't check that part," Mau admitted. ᴹ

She put the lid back on the sample.

"Then next time, look before you dig it up." ᴰᵃ

He nodded.

Umaru had come with the folded hamster flag. She settled it on the bench before joining the examination.

"Cold storage," she said. ᵁ

Momo turned toward her.

Umaru held both hands up.

"For food. Also drinks. Mostly food." ᵁ

"Start with mostly," Momo said. ᴹᵒ

Fern made another note.

Rimuru sat at the corner of the table, listening while people moved things into the space in front of him. He passed the bar to Wakana, the container back to Daphne and the untouched crystal wrapping away from Umaru's elbow.

"The shop has materials," he said. "But buying every piece before we learn to use the ones we find doesn't help much." ᴿ

"And finding everything ourselves doesn't make it free," Anko said. ᴬⁿ

She put down the rope they had carried.

One section had been rubbed thin.

"I want this on the page too." ᴬⁿ

Jinshi added it.

* * *

Yuka waited until the argument moved from supplies to training.

Then she put her own rope on the table.

"I want to work on what I can do with this," she said. "And the technique behind it." ʸᵏ

Mau looked at her.

She looked back without asking him to say it for her.

"I know there's more. I don't want to find out what I'm missing halfway through another gate." ʸᵏ

Yuta had been examining the worn section of the ordinary rope. He set it down.

"Then we make time for that." ʸᵗ

"Mau said there might be a way to help," she said. "A connection. We haven't talked about how it works yet. I want to understand that before we try anything." ʸᵏ

Rimuru leaned forward.

"Then we begin with the explanation. Nothing is connected just because we've put it on a list." ᴿ

Mau waited until she had finished before speaking.

"I want to finish things too," he said. "The ones I've already started. Some work, some only work when everything else stays out of the way. I don't want the next thing I see to mean we leave all of those half done again." ᴹ

"Good," Anko said. ᴬⁿ

It was so immediate that he looked at her.

"What?" she asked. "You thought I'd object to finishing something?" ᴬⁿ

Umaru laughed. Yuta did too, more quietly.

Fern pulled the page toward the middle so everyone could see the words already there.

There were more needs than things on the table.

That was useful to know before they spent any of them.

* * *

Afterward, Mau sat with Rimuru while the table returned to being a table.

Jinshi had taken the copied list to check the quantities with Anko. Fern went to find Stark before he could put the nicked haft away. Yuka carried her rope outside with Yuta, talking as they went.

*You heard?* Mau asked privately. ᴹ

*I heard,* Ciel said. *Daphne would like you to observe the ground before arriving with the answer in a jar.* ᶜ

He laughed.

*I meant the patterns.* ᴹ

*I know.* ᶜ

He waited for the part of himself which built in the dark.

*Keep what you find,* he told it. *I don't want you to stop looking. But help me finish what we already need. The things she wants to learn. The things that make this easier to live with.* ᴹ

For a moment there was only the familiar sense of work continuing beyond his attention.

Then a word.

*Finish.*

Ciel was quiet long enough that he could feel her listening too.

*Yes,* Mau said. *That.* ᴹ

Rimuru looked at him.

Mau passed the answer on.

"That's a start," Rimuru said. ᴿ
