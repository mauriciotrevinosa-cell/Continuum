*When: Day498, morning. Yuka has brought her rope, a pencil and questions which are not in Mau's notes.*

"When I reach for it," she said, "do I have to ask you every time?" ʸᵏ

Mau looked from the page to her.

Rimuru stopped turning the little wooden marker in his fingers.

"No," Mau said. "That isn't what I want this to be." ᴹ

"Then how do I know what you're doing? If I need it while you're doing something else?" ʸᵏ

He considered the question before answering.

"We need to be able to feel the demand. Both of us. And stop if there's a problem." ᴹ

"Stop the technique," she said. ʸᵏ

"Yes." ᴹ

"Not stop me talking until I agree with you." ʸᵏ

Rimuru put the marker down.

"Definitely not that." ᴿ

She nodded and wrote something beside the first question.

Mau watched her do it. He had brought the memory of knowing how a connection might hold. She had brought the life she expected to have while using it.

He moved his page so she had more room.

* * *

They chose the pull first.

Not the large one Mau could remember at the edge of terrible things. The small knot he had made and released in practice, with a place to put it and time to let it go.

Ciel asked to see that operation.

He offered it without opening the memories around it.

Rimuru listened privately, then explained aloud what the others needed.

"A path she can use," he said. "The supply still comes through you. That doesn't mean she has to call you to open it every time." ᴿ

Yuka laid the rope on her knees.

"And you won't be doing it for me?" ʸᵏ

"You choose where," Mau said. "You have to hold the shape. I don't get to move your hand because I think I've seen a better place." ᴹ

"You can tell me." ʸᵏ

"I'll probably tell you too much." ᴹ

She smiled.

"I can tell you that too." ʸᵏ

Gojo arrived with Yuta while they were still working through the practical parts. He read the short list, asked which part meant he would have to stop Mau doing something foolish, and found that Yuka had already underlined it.

"Excellent work." ᴳ

"It's mutual," she said. ʸᵏ

"Less convenient. Still excellent." ᴳ

Yuta sat beside her rather than taking the page away.

"What happens if either of you wants to stop the arrangement?" ʸᵗ

Rimuru answered that one carefully. Future access could close. They would check the operation had ended before releasing the path. There was no reason to build punishment into either decision.

Yuka looked at Mau.

He nodded.

"I want you to be able to use it," he said. "Not owe me for using it." ᴹ

She let the end of the rope slip through her fingers.

"All right." ʸᵏ

* * *

They went outside after eating.

The field no longer had a castle. There was still a flattened patch where Kamish had lain and a little piece of thread which Yuta picked up on the way past.

Yuka chose a place with room in front of her. She laid the loose rope end on the ground.

Mau sat close enough that they did not have to raise their voices.

*Can we make this one hold?* he asked beneath the work Ciel was showing him. *Only this one. Only where we agreed.* ᴹ

He felt the attention turn toward it.

Waited.

*One.*

Ciel followed the response at once.

*One pattern. I can keep its boundary clear.* ᶜ

He repeated that aloud.

Rimuru asked Yuka once more whether she wanted to begin. This time she answered by setting her pencil down, putting both feet where she meant them to be and holding out her hand.

"Yes." ʸᵏ

Mau offered the shape.

At first nothing moved.

Then he felt the difference between holding something near another person and letting them take its weight.

His shoulders tightened.

Yuka looked at him.

"Did I do that?" ʸᵏ

"A little. I'm all right." ᴹ

She waited until he let his shoulders settle before looking back toward the rope.

* * *

Her first knot was too close.

The rope end twitched toward her shoe. She tried to correct it and dragged the whole coil several inches instead.

"Let it go," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

She did.

The sudden absence made Mau blink.

Yuka stared at the displaced coil.

"I was looking at the end." ʸᵏ

"You placed the pull where your hand wanted to bring it," Gojo said. "Not where the rope was." ᴳ

He crouched and pointed without touching her wrist.

"Here is a place. There is a place. They don't become the same place because you want the rope to come to you." ᴳ

Yuka looked from his finger to the rope.

"You're enjoying this." ʸᵏ

"A great deal." ᴳ

She breathed out, annoyed enough to stop being frightened of the mistake.

Mau shifted his hands onto his knees.

He had nearly reached out to make the adjustment himself.

She noticed.

"Tell me," she said. ʸᵏ

He pointed to the spot beside the rope end.

"A little farther from you. Let it travel there first." ᴹ

She nodded.

"I can try that." ʸᵏ

* * *

They checked the release before another attempt.

Yuka reached for nothing and felt nothing. Then reached for the offered operation deliberately.

Mau felt it answer before she spoke.

He looked up.

Her attention stayed on the rope.

A tiny pull formed at the place she had chosen.

The end slid across the grass.

Only a short distance. Enough to leave a clear line where it had passed. She held it for a breath, then released it herself.

The coil stopped.

Mau waited until the weight behind his eyes settled.

Yuka was still looking at the line.

"I didn't ask you that time." ʸᵏ

"I know." ᴹ

"And you didn't put it there." ʸᵏ

"No." ᴹ

She looked at her hand.

Then at him.

"I reached for it." ʸᵏ

He could not help smiling.

"You did." ᴹ

* * *

Ciel wanted a pause.

Mau passed that on before Yuka could begin the next attempt. She looked at his face, then put both hands down.

"I'm stopping," she said. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"We can keep the path and leave the operation alone," Rimuru said. "See how it feels after you've both rested." ᴿ

Yuka leaned over to retrieve the rope.

"It's still there?" she asked. ʸᵏ

Mau listened to the quiet, offered part of himself.

"Yes." ᴹ

She wound the rope slowly. Yuta watched her hands for a moment, then took the wooden marker back to the bench. Gojo went with him, already explaining a distinction which Yuta disputed before they had reached it.

Yuka waited until Mau could look at her without squinting.

"I still want to work on mine," she said. ʸᵏ

He nodded toward the familiar place where her dog had stood the day before.

"The others." ᴹ

"And what comes after. I don't want this to be the only way I get better." ʸᵏ

"It won't be." ᴹ

She regarded him for a moment.

"That's a very confident promise." ʸᵏ

He thought about it.

"Then I'll make a smaller one. I'll help you work on it. And listen when you tell me how you want to." ᴹ

Her smile came back.

She moved beside him on the grass and leaned against his unoccupied shoulder.

"That one I like." ʸᵏ
