*When: Day498, after lunch. Yuka brings the page they wrote that morning; nobody treats it as something already done.*

They read it once more before leaving the bench.

Rimuru asked Yuka whether she still wanted the connection they had described, not a larger one which might be easier to make by accident.

"The small pull," she said. "I choose where. I can release it. We can both ask to stop." ʸᵏ

"And no access to anything else just because the path holds," he said. ᴿ

She nodded.

Mau gave his answer too.

Then he asked Ciel to begin.

* * *

The work started on his side.

Ciel helped him find the pattern he had offered that morning. He did not open everything beside it. Did not go looking through an old fight for the feeling he wanted to show her.

Only the small knot, its beginning and its release, the part he knew how to hold deliberately.

*This one,* he told the attention waiting beneath it. *For her to reach. Only what we agreed.* ᴹ

He waited instead of forcing the shape against the rest of himself.

The answer came as a word.

*One.*

*One pattern,* Ciel said. *Keep the others where they are. Rimuru, the outside end first.* ᶜ

Rimuru moved closer.

He explained what Ciel was asking before touching either of their hands. Yuka turned her wrist upward. Mau offered his right hand, palm open, and felt the beginning of a path where there had only been the intention to make one.

It was not the pull itself.

It was the way to it.

"I can feel something," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

Mau looked up at once.

"Like it's waiting on the other side of my hand." ʸᵏ

Rimuru asked whether she wanted them to continue.

She looked at Mau before answering.

"Yes." ʸᵏ

* * *

Mau did not try to put Blue into her fingers.

He kept the pattern available while she reached toward the new path herself. The first time, nothing moved. The second, he felt a faint demand travel through the connection and return when she withdrew.

Her shoulders rose.

"That was me?" ʸᵏ

"Yes," he said. ᴹ

"You didn't do it?" ʸᵏ

"I held the path. You reached through it." ᴹ

She tried once more without shaping the pull. This time she knew which feeling was hers.

Rimuru asked them to separate their hands.

The connection stayed.

Yuka looked at the space between them, then back at her own palm.

"It's still there." ʸᵏ

"That's the part we've built," Rimuru said. "The contract holds the access you both agreed to. It doesn't need you to keep holding hands, and it doesn't decide what she does with the technique." ᴿ

Mau let out a breath he had been keeping while they worked.

Ciel checked the path again before telling him he could relax his hand.

Yuka smiled then, small and unmistakable.

There was no attack in the air. Nothing had been pulled across the grass. They had made the thing which would let her choose to try.

* * *

Gojo and Yuta joined them for that part.

Yuta checked where Yuka had laid the rope and asked what she meant to move. Gojo listened to her answer before pointing out the difference between making the pull beside the rope and trying to put it in her own hand.

"Show me the place," he said. ᴳ

She pointed.

"Then keep that place when you start wanting the result." ᴳ

She gave him a look.

"You make it sound very simple." ʸᵏ

"It's an excellent beginning. You can be angry with the explanation after you've tried it." ᴳ

Mau sat where they had agreed he would. Close enough to speak without shouting, not in the place where Yuka needed to stand.

She looked at him once.

He nodded.

She reached for the offered pattern.

* * *

The first pull was too close.

The rope end twitched toward her shoe. She tried to correct it and dragged the loose coil instead.

"Release," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

She did.

The demand vanished from Mau before she turned toward him.

He touched the place behind his eye, then lowered his hand when Ciel asked him to describe it rather than hide it.

"Weight. Briefly. It's going." ᴹ

Yuka watched his face until he could look back comfortably.

"I put it where I wanted the rope to end up," she said. ʸᵏ

Gojo nodded.

"Now you know what that feels like when it isn't what you meant." ᴳ

She looked down at the displaced coil.

"Wonderful." ʸᵏ

Mau almost reached to fix the position for her.

She noticed.

"Tell me," she said. ʸᵏ

He pointed to the ground farther from her foot.

She moved to see it from the angle he meant, considered it and put her hand down until they had both rested.

* * *

For the second attempt, Mau said nothing.

They had already agreed to it. She did not need a new permission each time she chose to use the path.

He felt her reach.

The little pull formed beside the rope end.

It slid across the grass, not far, but toward the place she had chosen. A loose blade of grass went with it. She held for a breath, then let the operation end before the whole coil could follow.

Mau blinked when the weight withdrew.

Yuka stared at the short line left in the grass.

Then at her hand.

"I asked for it," she said softly. "And it was there." ʸᵏ

"Yes." ᴹ

She looked up at him.

"That's different from watching you do it." ʸᵏ

He smiled.

"I know." ᴹ

* * *

They stopped there.

Not because the path had failed. Ciel wanted to see how it settled while neither of them used the operation; Yuka wanted to be able to tell tomorrow's attempt apart from today's excitement.

She gathered the rope with her ordinary hands.

The connection was quiet, still available.

"I want to keep working on it," she said. "And on mine." ʸᵏ

Mau glanced toward her shadow, where her own technique waited.

"Both." ᴹ

She sat beside him rather than across from him now. Gojo and Yuta were arguing about the best way to describe her first mistake without making it sound like a lesson nobody would understand until afterward.

Yuka leaned against Mau's shoulder.

"Thank you for explaining it first." ʸᵏ

He looked down at her.

"You asked me to." ᴹ

"I know. Keep doing that." ʸᵏ

He took her hand, carefully, and let the new path rest.
