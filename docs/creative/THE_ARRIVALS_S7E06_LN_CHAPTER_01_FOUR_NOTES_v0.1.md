*When: Day499, late afternoon. Bocchi has played the beginning six times and stopped in the same place.*

On the seventh, Mau knocked.

She hit the wrong string.

"Sorry," he said from outside. ᴹ

"N-no. Come in." ᴮᵒ

He opened the door with his free hand. In the other he held a cup.

"I was going to ask whether you wanted this. Then I heard the beginning again." ᴹ

Bocchi looked down at the guitar.

"It's not finished." ᴮᵒ

"Can I hear the part that is?" ᴹ

She moved a page off the chair.

He sat without looking at the unfinished lines on it. That helped more than she had expected.

This time she got past the place where she had stopped.

Not far past. Far enough to hear what she disliked about what came next.

Her hands stopped.

Mau waited.

"That wasn't the end," she said. ᴮᵒ

"I thought it might not be." ᴹ

"You can say it's wrong." ᴮᵒ

"I don't know enough to say that." ᴹ

She looked at him.

He put the cup down.

"I liked the first part. Then it felt as if you were trying to make it go somewhere before you wanted it to." ᴹ

Bocchi looked at the page.

"Oh." ᴮᵒ

He waited while she crossed something out.

* * *

She asked him to play the small part they had tried before.

He moved the chair closer. His left hand still would not make the shape he wanted; she showed him where two strings would do instead.

"Just those?" he asked. ᴹ

She nodded.

He played them too quickly.

She tapped the beat on her knee.

He tried again.

That time she came in over him.

Only a little. Enough to hear the beginning with somewhere to rest beneath it.

Yuka appeared at the open door and stopped when she heard them.

Bocchi's hand nearly stopped too.

"Can I listen?" Yuka asked. ʸᵏ

There was room on the bench. Bocchi moved the page herself.

Mau kept the two notes going until he lost the beat. Then all three laughed, and the awful need to have the piece ready before anybody heard it loosened a little.

Her brother asked whether she wanted to try once more.

She did.

This time, when they reached the place where the melody should turn, she let it wait.

It sounded better.
