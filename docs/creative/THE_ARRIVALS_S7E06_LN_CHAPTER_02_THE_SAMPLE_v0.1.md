*When: Day500, morning. Hollis has the cart nearly ready, and Wakana has not wrapped the bar yet.*

He wanted Fern to see it first.

The bar lay beside the two straps. One strap was straighter than it had been; the broken fastening had been set apart so nobody would mistake a useful test for a repaired object.

"It bends before that fastening gives," Wakana said. "The fastening is still wrong. I haven't learned what heat does to the bar, and I don't want somebody else answering a different question while we think they're answering mine." ᵂ

Hollis looked at the little marks on the page.

"What can they do with it?" ᴴᵒ

Wakana showed him the surface they could test and the end he wanted left intact. A small mark he could compare when it returned would be useful. Cutting the whole thing apart would not.

Jinshi wrote that down before asking whether the workshop would understand the distinction from the drawing alone.

Wakana added another line.

* * *

Mau came out with the wrapped broken tool.

"This goes back too?" ᴹ

"It's theirs," Hollis said. ᴴᵒ

Mau set it beside the bar.

The contrast was easier to see here than it had been in the evening. A tool somebody had expected to use, and a piece of material nobody had turned into a tool yet.

Fern read the note.

"Borrowed sample," she said. "Not stock sent for sale." ᶠᵉ

Hollis nodded.

"If they ask what you'd charge?" ᴴᵒ

"Tell them we need their results before we can answer that," Wakana said. ᵂ

He glanced at Fern to make sure he had not answered for her.

"And what exactly they'd want made," she added. ᶠᵉ

Jinshi left a space for that question on the second page.

* * *

Hollis wrapped the bar where all of them could see him do it.

He tucked the note beside it and kept his own copy. Jinshi retained the drawing of the piece, the marks already on it and the list of things they expected to come back.

There were no coins in either direction.

Anko checked which stops he meant to make before returning. The conversation about the road was shorter than the one about the bar and just as practical. Hollis did not promise good weather. Nobody asked him to make a special trip alone because the test sounded interesting.

When the bundle went into the cart, Mau still knew what the house owned.

Two crystals, wrapped and kept. Two straps, one used for the local test. A bar which would be away for a while and should return with an answer.

It was a very small beginning.

Hollis took the reins.

"Anything else you want me to ask?" ᴴᵒ

Wakana opened his mouth, thought better of the length of the answer and gave him the last sheet instead.

Hollis laughed.

"I'll bring the questions back too." ᴴᵒ
