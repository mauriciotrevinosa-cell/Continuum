*When: Day509, later the same morning. Yuka has asked them to look, and Mau is waiting for Ciel to tell him what she can actually see.*

At first he could feel only the effort Yuka had shown them.

A beginning. The shape of the call. Then a turn which led back toward the place she already knew how to reach.

She tried to follow it once and frowned.

"That's her," she said. ʸᵏ

"The white dog?" Mau asked. ᴹ

Yuka nodded.

He repeated it to Ciel. The pressure eased at once.

*Don't keep pushing there,* Ciel said. *That part already works.* ᶜ

Mau told Yuka to let the call go.

She did, then looked down at the empty place by the step.

"Did I lose it?" ʸᵏ

"No. Ciel found the same turn. She's checking the other side of it." ᴹ

Rimuru had gone very still beside them. Mau could feel him listening too.

* * *

The Noise found something before Mau did.

*Here.*

He almost followed the word alone. Ciel stopped him at the edge of it, not sharply, but with the precision she used when moving one of his hands away from something he had not noticed.

*Wait. Let me see it first.* ᶜ

He waited.

Yuka touched his sleeve.

"Oppa?" ʸᵏ

"It found something. We're looking before we touch it." ᴹ

She watched his face. He made himself keep looking at her instead of closing his eyes and leaving her beside a conversation she could not hear.

After a while, Ciel answered.

*There is a second response. The call doesn't reach it cleanly. It folds back into the first.* ᶜ

Mau explained it to Yuka as well as he could. Two places for her to reach, one working route taking her back to the dog she could already summon.

"You aren't missing the other one," he said. "We can feel him there." ᴹ

Her fingers tightened on his sleeve.

Maki leaned a little closer.

"Can you change where it turns?" Yuka asked. ʸᵏ

He waited for the answer rather than nodding because he wanted to.

*With her showing us the call again. The Noise can open the access. I will keep it to this part,* Ciel said. ᶜ

Rimuru relayed that sentence aloud, giving Mau a moment to breathe.

Yuka looked down at her hands.

"Then I'd like to try." ʸᵏ

* * *

She began the sign again.

This time she stopped where Rimuru asked her to, keeping the beginning steady instead of driving it toward the familiar answer. Mau felt the Noise move along the path she had offered.

Something resisted.

His instinct was to put more behind it. Ciel asked him to hold what was already there.

Yuka's brow drew together.

"It feels different." ʸᵏ

"Where?" Rimuru asked. ᴿ

"Farther. I can feel—" ʸᵏ

She stopped talking.

Mau felt the folded part give. No great rush came through him. No creature moved from his shadow into hers.

Yuka's hands opened.

She stared at the ground.

"He's there." ʸᵏ

* * *

The black dog came up nose first.

Yuka drew in a breath so quickly that he paused with his front paws still in the dark. One ear turned toward her voice.

She said nothing else.

The dog stepped out.

For a moment he stood between her boots, looking up at her. She held her hand a little way above his head, not quite touching, as if the last inch might be the thing that made him disappear.

He closed it himself.

His forehead pressed into her palm.

Yuka made a small sound and bent over him. He put his paws against her knees, trying to bring her hand back when she covered her mouth with it.

"Hi," she managed. ʸᵏ

He pushed against her again.

Mau moved his arm out of the way so she could slide down from the step. The black dog followed her to the grass and leaned into the embrace as though he had known exactly where she meant him to go.

Maki rested a hand against Yuka's shoulder. Her fingers stayed there when Yuka bent farther over the dog.

"There you are," she said. ᴹᵃ

Yuka laughed through the breath she was trying to steady.

"He knows me." ʸᵏ

"Yes," Mau said. ᴹ

The dog had put his muzzle under her chin. That seemed to settle any question about how long they ought to wait before believing it.

* * *

They let her stay there.

Yuta found them when the rope still had not reached the practice markers. He stopped beside Maki, looked at the dog and then at Yuka's face.

She looked up.

"Both," she told him. ʸᵏ

He sat on the step she had left.

"I can see that," he said, very gently. ʸᵗ

She looked around for the white dog, remembered she had dismissed her and laughed at herself again.

"Not yet. I mean—he's here. I can feel where she is too." ʸᵏ

"Take your time." ʸᵗ

Mau leaned back against the step while Ciel finished checking the connection. The examination ended; the small Blue access they had already made remained quiet, separate from what Yuka had just called.

*She should try without us holding this open,* Ciel said. *After she's ready to let him go for a moment.* ᶜ

He did not interrupt the dog pushing his head beneath Yuka's hand to ask immediately.

When she looked up at him, he told her.

Her hand went still.

"He'll come back?" ʸᵏ

Mau looked at the dog.

"That's what we need to find out." ᴹ

* * *

Yuka stroked him once more before dismissing him.

The dark closed around his paws and then his shoulders. She kept her hand against his head until he was gone.

Mau waited with his own hands resting on his knees.

She looked at him.

He nodded toward the place beside her, not reaching into the connection.

Yuka made the sign.

The black dog answered.

She laughed properly that time, caught his face between her hands and had to lean away when he tried to lick her.

"All right. All right, I saw you." ʸᵏ

He did not look convinced she had seen enough.

* * *

She called the white dog next.

The familiar effort followed the second summon. Yuka felt it in her chest, noticed how carefully she had been breathing and stayed seated while the white dog stepped over her leg to investigate the newcomer.

They sniffed each other.

The black dog moved first. The white one followed, nudged him aside and reclaimed the space nearest Yuka's hand.

"There are two hands," Yuka told her. ʸᵏ

The dog settled against her anyway.

Yuta passed her the water Maki had fetched. She took it, drank and spent a while trying to keep both muzzles away from the rim.

They stayed only long enough for her to be sure. Then she sent them to rest, one after the other, and sat looking at the grass with her hands empty.

Mau shifted closer.

"How does it feel?" he asked. ᴹ

She leaned against his shoulder.

"Like I want to call him again." ʸᵏ

He smiled.

"Later?" ᴹ

"Later," she agreed. ʸᵏ

Maki picked up the rope. Yuta took the water back before Yuka could forget she was holding it.

They had come out to begin working toward something new.

Yuka sat a little longer with what should have been there from the beginning, and let it be enough for the morning.
