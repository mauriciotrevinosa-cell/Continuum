*When: Day510, late morning. There is a patch of open floor near the gate entrance, and Yuka has asked Mau to stand inside the line with her.*

Ciel had answered Mau when they crossed. Rimuru was staying home; the private thread had held as far as the room Yuta and Gojo had checked for the lesson.

Now Gojo waited for her to explain why.

He had heard what Mau remembered. The tower, the contract, the two of them doing something which should have failed if another person performed the subjugation. He had also heard Yuka ask whether the gate they were entering would give them the same notice.

Mau had told her he did not know.

"We won't have a sentence telling us it's allowed," she said now. "But if he has to catch something before it gets to my face, I want him close enough to do it." ʸᵏ

"And if that ruins the attempt?" Gojo asked. ᴳ

"Then we find that out. I don't want him standing there wondering whether I meant it when I asked him to help." ʸᵏ

Mau looked at her.

She pointed at the floor beside her.

"Here." ʸᵏ

Yuta checked the room once more before putting their bags beyond the line. The dogs had already gone to rest. Igris remained outside the room, watching the route back; nobody would mistake him for another participant.

"If either of us comes in," Yuta told her, "we're ending it. We'll worry about trying again after you're safe." ʸᵗ

She nodded.

Gojo moved to stand beside him.

"Rabbits," he said. "A modest beginning." ᴳ

Mau looked away just long enough for Yuka to notice.

"What?" she asked. ʸᵏ

"Don't assume modest means slow." ᴹ

* * *

She had practised the shape of the call without completing it.

Now she completed it.

The shadow opened beneath her feet, and something white shot past her ankle.

Another followed. Then three together, spreading over the stone so quickly that Yuka turned toward the wrong one before she had finished seeing the first.

She crouched.

The nearest rabbit sprang at her face.

It met something a finger's width from her cheek, bounced away and landed running.

Mau dropped his hand.

"Still here," he said. ᴹ

Yuka had felt the interruption. The ritual had not dissolved with it.

That was all the answer she had time for.

She reached for the rabbit which had turned toward her shoe. It slipped beneath her palm, went between her feet and left her holding nothing.

From beyond the line, Gojo made a thoughtful sound.

"I withdraw modest." ᴳ

* * *

At first she tried to be faster.

Mau watched her chase the same three shapes across the room, miss the one at her knee because she was looking at the one near her hand, then stop in the middle with her rope hanging uselessly from her fingers.

She breathed out hard.

"They keep going behind me." ʸᵏ

"You keep giving them a behind," he said. ᴹ

She looked at him.

He pointed toward the curve of the wall.

"Move from there. Make them come round where you can see them." ᴹ

She took two steps back. A rabbit rushed through the place her foot had left.

This time she caught it.

It was not like holding her dog. It twisted hard enough to make her change her grip, hind feet scrabbling against her sleeve. She held it against the stone until the resistance eased.

The little shape went dark under her hands.

She stared at the place it had been.

"One," she said. ʸᵏ

Another hit the side of her boot.

She caught that one with the loop of rope before it could turn.

* * *

There were more than she wanted to count.

She stopped counting.

The rope helped until it gave the rabbits another thing to run beneath. Her hands helped until she grew too eager and reached past her own balance. Once Mau caught her elbow before her chin reached the floor.

She righted herself, caught the rabbit at the end of the rope and held it still.

The ritual continued.

No pale letters appeared to explain why.

Yuta crouched beyond the line, watching where her feet went. He only spoke when she began to turn the same weak ankle inward again.

She changed her stance.

Mau moved with her, keeping the open part of the room in front of them. The little patch of space he had used at the beginning was gone. When she looked toward him and then at the far end of the rope, he understood what she wanted to try.

"Yours," he said. ᴹ

She reached through the connection.

The small pull formed where she placed it. Two rabbits skidded sideways; the loose rope end went with them.

She released before she went after them.

Mau felt the weight withdraw. Yuka had caught one rabbit beneath the fold of rope and the other between her palms.

For a moment she looked almost offended.

"They could have done that twenty minutes ago." ʸᵏ

"You put it in a different place twenty minutes ago," he said. ᴹ

She made a sound which might have been agreement if she had not been busy.

* * *

By the time the room began to empty, Yuka was sitting more than crouching.

Her palms were warm. Dust had worked into one knee of her trousers. A rabbit had discovered the back of her coat and was trying to use it to reach her shoulder.

Mau pointed.

She reached behind herself, missed, swore quietly and tried with the other hand.

Yuta looked down at the floor.

Gojo did not bother hiding his smile.

"Your grandfather heard that," he said. ᴳ

"He can help me remember it afterward." ʸᵏ

She caught the rabbit.

Mau laughed just as the last two came toward him from opposite sides. He kept his hands out of their way.

Yuka watched them pass him, waited until they had crossed in front of her and placed the pull beside the nearer one.

This time the rope stayed where she had left it.

The first rabbit lost its footing. She caught it against her knee. The second doubled back, ran straight over her wrist and stopped beneath the hand she had already put there.

She held them both.

The resistance lasted longer than she expected.

Her shoulders began to shake. She braced her elbows, breathed and kept them there until the small bodies softened under her grip.

Then there was nothing between her hands.

* * *

Yuka stayed where she was.

She could hear Gojo moving outside the line. Somebody had put a cup down. Mau was close enough that she could see the dust on his shoe.

Beneath her, something waited.

Many small presences, gathered where there had been only the untried shape of a call.

She let her hands fall to her lap.

"I can feel them." ʸᵏ

Mau sat beside her.

"Do you want a minute?" ᴹ

She nodded.

He gave her several.

Yuta passed the water across the line without stepping over it. She drank, tried to smooth the knee of her trousers and gave up when she saw the other one.

"I thought the rabbits would be easier than that," she said. ʸᵏ

"They are extremely rude rabbits," Gojo said. ᴳ

She laughed, tired enough that it took a moment to stop.

* * *

Mau left the marked area before she tried the call again.

He told her what he was doing. The borrowed pull was quiet. He would not shape anything beside her or keep a defensive operation ready inside her attempt.

She waited until he had sat with the others.

Then she made the sign.

A white rabbit rose by her knee.

This one stayed.

Another came beside it. Then several more, looking around the room instead of trying to escape her.

Yuka touched the nearest pair of ears.

The rabbit lowered its head beneath her hand.

She looked at Mau.

He was smiling before she could say anything.

"You got them," he said. ᴹ

* * *

They ate before walking back.

Yuka had put the rabbits away, but she kept looking at the floor between bites. Yuta asked how the last pull had felt; she showed him with the bread in her hand until Gojo pointed out that she was dropping her explanation into her lap.

Mau helped her collect the crumbs.

"So we can do the others," she said quietly. ʸᵏ

He glanced toward the chalk line.

"We know this worked. We still look at what the next one needs." ᴹ

She considered that, then nodded.

"Together?" ʸᵏ

"Together." ᴹ

On the way out, she asked him to stop.

Mau turned. Yuka rose onto her toes, brushed a last bit of dust from his shoulder and kissed him.

Gojo looked toward Yuta with great interest.

Yuta picked up the food bag.

"They've been doing that for a while, Gojo-sensei." ʸᵗ

"I know. I was checking whether you'd noticed." ᴳ

Yuka took Mau's hand before either of them could answer.

They went home with the rope rolled properly and one more thing she knew how to call.
