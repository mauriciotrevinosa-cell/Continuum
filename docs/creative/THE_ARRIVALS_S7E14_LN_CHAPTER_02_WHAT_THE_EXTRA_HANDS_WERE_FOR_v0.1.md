*When: Day521, late morning. Kaneki has stopped a load of timber from becoming everybody's problem, and Igris is waiting for Mau to explain what he wants the new guardian to learn.*

"Putting it down," Mau said. ᴹ

Igris looked at the long pieces of wood.

"Without breaking the people or the wood," Mau added. ᴹ

"A sound beginning, my liege." ᴵ

Kaneki lowered the tilted end onto the support and stepped away. His coffee had gone cold while he was doing something he had meant to finish before drinking it.

Mau knew that sequence too well to comment.

He asked them to clear a little more room before calling Rampart.

* * *

The guardian rose with its head below the height of the nearest roof.

It was still enormous.

Six hands settled against the ground as it looked toward Mau. The old movement was there, the inclination to find the center of a space and make the rest answer to it.

The yard stayed a yard.

A loose chip beside one hand shifted, then stopped. No great stone platform came out from under the laundry.

Mau waited until Rampart had settled before pointing to the load.

"Those. Hold them while we change the support." ᴹ

The guardian reached.

"From underneath," he said quickly. ᴹ

It stopped with two hands above the timber.

Igris stepped round to the open end and showed the angle with his sword still sheathed.

Rampart moved one hand beneath the load.

Then another.

The wood rose just far enough to clear the slipping support.

Kaneki looked at Mau.

"That's considerably less shouting than yesterday sounded like." ᴷᵃⁿ

"Yesterday I was trying to stop it doing this to me." ᴹ

* * *

They replaced the support.

Rampart held the load without deciding that holding it meant carrying it somewhere else. Mau made himself leave the space clear instead of stepping in to do the work faster.

His shoulder helped him remember.

"Down slowly," Kaneki said when the new support was ready. ᴷᵃⁿ

The guardian looked toward Mau.

"Listen to him for this job." ᴹ

It lowered the wood.

Kaneki checked the weight before stepping away.

"Good. Leave it there." ᴷᵃⁿ

All six hands withdrew.

For a moment the guardian remained bent over the timber, waiting for the next part of the battle.

Mau smiled.

"That's the whole thing." ᴹ

* * *

Touka came along the path with Ichika beside her.

She stopped when she saw what was in the yard, then looked at Kaneki rather than walking the child closer to an untested hand.

"Is this the reason you're still here?" she asked. ᵀᵒ

"Part of it." ᴷᵃⁿ

Ichika leaned round her mother's leg.

"It's big." ᴵᶜ

"We noticed," Mau said. ᴹ

She considered the hands.

"Does it need all of them?" ᴵᶜ

Mau looked back at Rampart.

"We used two. The others were very patient." ᴹ

Kaneki laughed into the coffee he had finally picked up.

* * *

There was another load to move.

Kaneki put his cup down, looked at it and paused.

Mau was showing Igris where the smaller soldiers could stand without passing beneath Rampart's reach. Touka had taken Ichika a little farther along the path, where she could watch without anybody having to keep checking behind themselves.

"Could I leave you with the next one?" Kaneki asked. ᴷᵃⁿ

Mau turned.

"Yes. We'll call if we need something we haven't understood." ᴹ

Kaneki pointed out the split in one piece, the place to set it and the thing which would roll if somebody lifted both ends without noticing it.

Igris listened.

Then Kaneki took his cup.

"You wanted them to give us time for other things," he said. ᴷᵃⁿ

Mau looked toward Touka and Ichika.

"Yes." ᴹ

"Then I'm going to use some." ᴷᵃⁿ

He went to join them.

* * *

Rampart lifted the next load after Igris had shown it where to stand.

Mau stayed near enough to stop the movement if something went wrong. He did not need to hold any part of it himself.

Along the path, Ichika had got her father to put down the coffee so she could show him something with both hands. Touka waited beside them without looking back to see whether the work was still calling him.

Mau watched until Igris asked about the split piece.

He pointed it out.

The wood reached the right place intact.

Kaneki did not come back to check it.
