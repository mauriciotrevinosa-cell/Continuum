She pointed at the open floor.

"If it comes down here, I go underneath. Not back toward you." ʸᵏ

Mau looked at the space, then at her.

"All right." ᴹ

"And if I miss, don't pull it straight onto me." ʸᵏ

"Also a good instruction." ᴹ

Maki checked the loop Yuka had made in the rope. Yuta looked upward before stepping beyond the marked area.

No dog waited beside them this time.

Yuka wanted room to see what she was doing with the call.

She put her hands together.

* * *

The bird came out with its wings already opening.

Mau saw the mask first. Then the span, larger than Yuka's raised arms, and the movement of the feathers as the air changed round them.

It rose faster than she had expected.

Yuka turned with it. A crackle ran along the near wing; she felt the little hairs on her arm lift before the first strike reached the stone.

She went under the dive.

Too early.

The wing clipped the rope and carried its free end upward. She let the line run rather than allow it to take her shoulder with it.

Mau stayed out of her path.

Nue banked wide across the room.

Yuka looked after it, breathing hard.

"That was early," she said. ʸᵏ

"Yes." ᴹ

She glanced at him.

He waited.

* * *

She called the rabbits before the next pass.

They spread across the floor beneath the bird. Nue turned toward the movement, struck and went through the place three small bodies had been. The decoys broke apart; the others kept moving.

Yuka watched the turn instead of the strike.

Her fingers tightened on the rope.

"There." ʸᵏ

The loop went up.

It caught the edge of the lower wing and slid off before she could set it.

Nue wheeled toward her.

Mau caught the loose rope with the Ruler's grip long enough to keep it from whipping round her ankles, then let it fall where she could reach it.

She took it without looking away from the bird.

"Thank you." ʸᵏ

He moved with her toward the wall.

* * *

The third dive came lower.

Yuka waited until the feathers filled the space above the nearest rabbit. Then she stepped beneath the wing instead of running from its span.

Her pull formed at the end of the turn.

Not strong enough to drag Nue out of the air. Enough to make the bird correct where she needed it to correct.

The rope caught.

She released Blue before putting both hands behind the line.

Nue struck the floor with one claw, pushed off and nearly took her with it.

Mau moved.

"The far side," she said through her teeth. ʸᵏ

He saw what she meant.

The brief grip caught the wing joint beyond her, drawing it away from the direction she was already holding. Not pinning the whole bird. Not replacing the strength she was struggling to keep in her hands.

Nue came down crookedly.

Yuka went with it.

* * *

She got one knee beneath her before the wing could throw her clear.

The rope moved against her gloves. She changed her hold, felt it beginning to slip and stopped trying to make her arms do all the work.

Her weight went lower.

The bird fought the new angle. A crackle passed across its feathers; Yuka drew the loose loop clear of the charged edge and shifted with the shoulder beneath it.

Mau released his grip when she had the position.

For a moment her whole body shook with the effort of keeping it.

She stayed.

"Not yet," she whispered. ʸᵏ

Nue tried to rise again.

She let it move far enough to lose the leverage it had been using, then put it down where she could hold it.

The resistance changed.

The next beat of the wing did not reach the floor.

Then there was only the rope between her hands and the dark settling beneath her knees.

* * *

Mau waited until she had let go before crouching beside her.

"Did I—" she began. ʸᵏ

She stopped and looked down.

Something in her shadow had answered the question before he could.

Yuta passed the water in from outside. Maki took the loose end of the rope when Yuka pushed it toward her, then left it where she could see the places it had rubbed.

Nobody put another sign in front of her hands.

Yuka drank.

After a while she looked at Mau.

"You listened." ʸᵏ

"You had a plan." ᴹ

"You could have pulled the whole thing down." ʸᵏ

"You asked for the far side." ᴹ

She smiled, still short of breath.

"It was a good plan." ʸᵏ

He smiled back.

"Eventually." ᴹ

She knocked her shoulder lightly against his good one.

* * *

The later call was quieter.

Mau had gone to sit with Yuta. Yuka stood alone where there was space for the wings and made the sign again.

Nue rose.

It banked over the open floor, came round toward her and landed too close to the rope.

The wind from its wings carried the end across Maki's shoe.

Maki looked down.

Then at her granddaughter.

Yuka put a hand against the bird's side.

"We're going to work on that," she said. ʸᵏ

Nue folded one wing. The other took a moment longer.

Mau laughed before he could help it.

* * *

She asked about riding it while they were packing.

Yuta glanced at the place where Nue had landed.

"First make sure it comes down where you ask it to." ʸᵗ

"I meant eventually." ʸᵏ

"Then eventually, we find out." ʸᵗ

She accepted that answer without pretending she was no longer excited.

On the way back, she told Mau exactly what she would change next time. The pull later. Less rope left beneath her feet. Rabbits spread toward the side where she wanted Nue to look, instead of everywhere because they could.

He listened.

When she asked what he thought, he pointed out the turn she had understood before he had finished deciding how to help her.

Yuka went quiet for a moment.

Then she took his hand.

"We should tell Grandma that part too." ʸᵏ

Maki, walking ahead of them, did not turn around.

"I saw it," she said. ᴹᵃ
