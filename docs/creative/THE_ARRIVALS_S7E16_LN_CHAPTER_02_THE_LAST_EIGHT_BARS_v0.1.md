*When: Day527, late afternoon. This time Bocchi gets past the turn without stopping.*

Mau heard it from the doorway.

He waited until the others stopped before knocking against the open frame. Nijika waved him in with a drumstick; Ryo moved a page off the spare chair without asking whether he planned to stay.

Bocchi looked down at her hands.

"The last part is different," she said. ᴮᵒ

"I heard." ᴹ

"Not just longer. Different." ᴮᵒ

He sat.

"Can I hear it with the beginning?" ᴹ

Kita smiled before turning back toward the others.

"Good excuse. Again?" ᴷᶦ

* * *

They played it again.

The little part Mau remembered was still there. It simply went somewhere now instead of trying to prove it already knew where the song had to finish.

He watched Bocchi's hand make the change.

She did not look toward him until they had reached the end.

"That," he said. ᴹ

Her shoulders rose.

"What?" ᴮᵒ

"The part I was waiting for." ᴹ

She looked at the page as though checking that he meant the same part she did.

Ryo leaned back.

"He has discovered endings. We can begin charging more." ᴿʸ

"We're not charging him," Nijika said. ᴺᶦ

"Family discount. Still more." ᴿʸ

Mau looked at Bocchi.

"You didn't warn me there was a subscription." ᴹ

She laughed before she could decide whether that was the answer she meant to give.

* * *

Nijika showed him the beat against the edge of the empty chair.

He tried it with his right hand. Missed the pause, found it, then lost it again when the others came in.

Bocchi stopped on a note she had not intended to hold.

Mau put his hand in his lap.

"That was me." ᴹ

"We know," Ryo said. ᴿʸ

Kita covered her mouth.

Nijika made him try the little part alone once more, slower this time. When it held, she let the others come back around it.

Only a few bars.

Enough for Mau to feel the moment when he belonged in the sound instead of chasing it.

Then he missed the pause again.

This time Bocchi kept going.

* * *

He stayed while they argued about the next song.

Kita wanted to try it once before deciding the beginning was too high. Ryo wanted to change the part nobody had asked her about. Nijika made them eat the little food they had brought before everybody forgot it was there.

Bocchi folded the finished page and set it beside the one she had begun that morning.

Her brother looked at the blank half of it.

"Already?" he asked. ᴹ

She nodded.

"I had an idea." ᴮᵒ

"Can I hear it when there's something to hear?" ᴹ

She looked up.

This time the answer did not need several attempts.

"Yes." ᴮᵒ

Mau picked up the spare cup when he left. Behind him, the new beginning arrived once, stopped, and began again with Kita trying something over it.
