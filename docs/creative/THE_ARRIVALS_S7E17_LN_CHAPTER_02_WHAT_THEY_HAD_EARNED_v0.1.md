*When: Day535, afternoon. The cart is back, and Momo is checking whether the lids fit before anybody calls the new containers a success.*

One did.

She turned the other, set it down and tried again without the cloth which somebody had left across the rim.

That one fitted too.

"These," she said. "These I can use." ᴹᵒ

Hollis looked more relieved by that than by the pouch he had brought back.

Stark took the containers inside while the guards finished with the cart. Mau stayed with Wakana until the rolls and wrapped tools were down; the cutter which had filled the space in the bundle did not come out with them.

"She kept it?" he asked. ᴹ

Wakana nodded.

Marin answered the part his face had not caught up with yet.

"Bought it. After using it long enough to make him miserable." ᴹᵃʳ

"I wasn't miserable." ᵂ

"Not after." ᴹᵃʳ

* * *

They let Hollis eat before opening the account.

The list beside it was familiar. Things people had asked for before the trip, when the first outing's little pile of materials had turned into a conversation about what the house actually needed.

Some of those things had come back on the cart.

Others still had nothing beside them but the question.

Jinshi laid the old four-three-three note next to the costs. The guards' part followed the agreement they had left with.

Hollis checked that line first.

Fern asked about the used material and the attempts before the working blade.

Wakana told her.

"Then that goes back into the work," she said, marking the replacement provision. "Before we spend it as though the next piece makes itself." ᶠᵉ

Jinshi copied the figure.

There was something left after it.

Not enough for everything on the page.

Enough to choose something.

* * *

Umaru came to see that part.

"One thing we don't have to call useful," she said. ᵁ

Fern looked up.

"We can choose a small thing to enjoy without pretending we don't need anything else." ᶠᵉ

Umaru looked almost offended that the argument had been won before she had properly begun.

Hollis reached for the little packet he had kept apart from the tools.

It held candied nuts. They were on the agreed list too, farther down, with a note beside them about what not to buy if the price was absurd.

"It wasn't absurd," he said. ᴴᵒ

Umaru took it with both hands.

Momo told her to bring it back at dinner rather than deciding that doing the inspection gave her ownership.

She considered arguing.

Then she saw Milim approaching and put it behind her back.

* * *

Mai found Maki by the kitchen door.

Her sister had heard most of the account. She had not interrupted it to ask questions already being answered.

"You look pleased," Maki said. ᴹᵃ

"I went outside, did something useful and nobody tried to kill anybody. I recommend it." ᴹᵃᶦ

Maki smiled.

"Will you do more?" ᴹᵃ

Mai looked at Wakana and Marin carrying the notes back toward the workshop.

"If there's something I want to help with." ᴹᵃᶦ

"Good." ᴹᵃ

She made room when Rem came through with a bowl, and Mai took the second one without turning it into another promise about what she would do for the rest of her life.

* * *

The containers reached the table empty.

Momo demonstrated the lids once more to Rem, who asked which one could go with a returning work party without making everybody carry the meal in separate cloths. Taihei looked at the smaller one and suggested a different use before either could become permanently assigned by accident.

Mau stood listening until Yuka handed him the stack of clean bowls.

"You can think while you put these out." ʸᵏ

He took them.

"Efficient." ᴹ

"I'm learning." ʸᵏ

* * *

At dinner, the little packet went into the middle where people could reach it.

Umaru watched the first hand take a share with the expression of somebody making a significant contribution to household harmony.

Milim noticed.

"You get the same amount," she said. ᴹᶦ

"I know." ᵁ

Mau passed the dish toward the end of the table. Across from him, Wakana was explaining the cutter's last change to Yuta, using the ordinary knife in his hand until Marin took it away and gave him a spoon for the demonstration.

The money had bought two good lids and room to try again.

What the people at the table wanted to do with that room was already becoming a longer conversation.
