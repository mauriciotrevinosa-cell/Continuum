*When: Day539, afternoon. Ciel has asked him to sit by the same window, but this time the cup is waiting somewhere else.*

Mau noticed that before taking his seat.

"Is that part of it?" he asked. ᴹ

Rimuru looked at the cup.

"It can be." ᴿ

Gojo stood near the open door. Yuka put the rope down where she had put it on the first afternoon, then came to sit beside Mau instead of remaining across the room like another object they meant to measure.

There had been smaller attempts since the flood.

One had held the answers so well that getting any of them back took longer than simply looking. Another had decided every change mattered equally, which had made a very quiet room feel like a list of emergencies.

Ciel had kept both failures.

She sounded pleased with what she wanted to try now.

*Begin when you're ready.* ᶜ

* * *

Mau opened the intake.

He did not begin by narrowing it around the rope. That had been the old answer: make the rest small enough that choosing one thing did not hurt.

This time the room arrived whole.

He felt the familiar reflex to close it before it grew too loud. Ciel stayed close while the work they had built took the flow instead.

The leaf at the window moved.

The table stood beneath it.

He could find either of them without carrying the grain of one against every vein in the other.

*Ask for the rope,* Ciel said. ᶜ

He did.

The little line on the floor became clear in the way he wanted. Position, movement, the place where its fibers twisted out from under the knot. He let that detail go.

The processing did not stop when his attention moved away.

He waited for the rest to rush after him.

It stayed where he could reach it.

* * *

Yuka began the small pull.

Mau watched it without losing her hand beside it. When she released, he followed the end and then let the room widen again.

Gojo tried the next operation after hers was gone.

One familiar Blue. A change of position. A release Mau knew how to recognize, made by a person whose control was different from his own.

He could follow that difference.

He could also hear Yuka ask whether he wanted the cup before she moved it closer.

"Yes, please." ᴹ

Her hand stopped for a moment.

He looked at her.

"What?" ᴹ

"You answered." ʸᵏ

He laughed softly.

"I do that sometimes." ᴹ

"Not last time." ʸᵏ

* * *

Rimuru moved the little marker at the far side of the table while they talked.

It remained within the room Mau could see. He simply had not chosen to follow that hand instead of Yuka's face.

Ciel waited until the conversation had reached a pause.

*What changed on the table?* ᶜ

His first impulse was to look again.

Instead he reached toward the work which had continued while he was listening.

The answer came without dragging the rest of the room into the question.

"The marker. To your left," he told Rimuru. ᴹ

Rimuru opened his hand.

"And the cup?" ᴿ

"Yuka brought it closer. You left it alone." ᴹ

Gojo's expression changed.

Not surprise at a trick he could already understand. Attention to the fact that Mau had not needed to stop being present at the table to do it.

"Again," he said. ᴳ

They tried again.

* * *

Mau asked for more detail.

The room opened in layers he could move through. Flow, surfaces, little movements. The quiet work beneath it had already done enough that asking to see more did not mean beginning the whole burden again inside his head.

He reduced what he wanted to attend to.

The input continued.

He increased it once more, found the operation Gojo was preparing and heard Yuka speaking beside him without having to abandon one for the other.

He put his cup down very carefully.

*The old restriction is still in the path,* Ciel said. *We can take it out now.* ᶜ

He considered the familiar pressure of it.

The thing which had let him look at the world at all when closing parts of it had been the only way he knew to stay inside himself.

*Yes,* he said. ᴹ

Ciel helped the Noise remove it from the incoming flow.

The new work stayed.

Mau waited.

Nothing broke open behind his eyes.

* * *

The leaves were still gray.

He looked at them, then at the light on the wood. Gray too. The soft place at the edge of his left vision remained soft when he turned his head to test it.

Yuka was beside him, in color.

He had known what they were changing.

He had hoped anyway.

*I know,* Ciel said quietly. ᶜ

Mau rested his fingers against the cup.

*You didn't promise that part.* ᴹ

*No.* ᶜ

He looked at Yuka again. She was waiting, not reaching for an explanation before he had found one.

"It worked," he said. ᴹ

She glanced toward the window.

"And?" ʸᵏ

"And the other thing is still the other thing." ᴹ

She put her hand beside his.

He took it without closing the room.

* * *

Ciel let the quiet last until he was smiling again.

Then she said, with considerable satisfaction:

*Mini me.* ᶜ

Mau looked up.

Rimuru had already begun to smile.

*Yes,* Ciel continued. *Mini Ciel. That is the name of your new little system. I will not accept another.* ᶜ

Mau laughed.

"She's named it," he told Yuka. ᴹ

*Her. Mini Ciel.* ᶜ

He corrected himself, still laughing.

"Mini Ciel." ᴹ

*If you won't say it properly, I'm taking her with me.* ᶜ

Rimuru put down his cup before he spilled it.

"You've been warned," he said. ᴿ

"I was going to say thank you." ᴹ

*An excellent beginning.* ᶜ

Mau glanced toward the quiet work which had kept processing while the person who made it enjoyed herself.

*Thank you, Mini Ciel.* ᴹ

There was no second voice answering him.

Only Ciel, very pleased.

*She's busy.* ᶜ

* * *

They went outside after the tests.

Not to find a harder thing to put in front of him immediately. Yuka wanted to walk, and Mau wanted to find out what it felt like to walk beside her without making the courtyard smaller first.

The old blur remained. So did the gray. But he could ask for the little movement near the workbench, recognize a carrier turning at the corner and return to what she was saying without losing the beginning of her sentence.

Behind his attention, something kept doing its job.

Ciel stayed with the conversation instead of having to be every part of the labor herself.

Yuka stopped near the tree's clear root circle.

"Tomorrow," she said, "I'd like to try something else we can do together." ʸᵏ

Mau turned toward her.

"Tell me." ᴹ

"The part you used to turn the falling stone," she said. "Yuta says he can help us with it. I'd like to learn that one too." ʸᵏ

Mau looked at the space beside her hand.

"Then let's ask him what we're doing badly before we make it bigger." ᴹ

She smiled and took his hand for the rest of the walk.
