*When: Day540, morning. Yuta has brought a square of ordinary cloth, and Yuka has asked whether that means he intends to make the explanation easier or worse.*

"Easier," he said. "I hope." ʸᵗ

He held it between two fingers and turned one edge toward her.

"This part. You aren't trying to make the air blow the thing somewhere else." ʸᵗ

Mau looked at the curve in the cloth.

"Which is where mine gets ugly," he said. ᴹ

Yuta nodded.

"Show me." ʸᵗ

* * *

Mau reached for the surface.

The pattern was familiar. Still rough at the place where his hand wanted a result before the shape could settle. With the room no longer crowding every little adjustment into his attention at once, he could keep that place clear long enough for Yuta to point at it.

"There. You keep pulling past what you have." ʸᵗ

Mau let the fold become smaller.

It held.

Yuka stepped to one side. The marker behind his hand seemed to move somewhere the grass beneath it had not.

She looked at the real marker, then back at him.

"That is very strange." ʸᵏ

"Yes," Yuta said. "Remember that before you decide it's just a different push." ʸᵗ

Mau let the surface settle flat.

* * *

They sat on the low support before adding it to the connection.

Not another contract in place of the one they already had. Another operation they had chosen to make available through it.

Mau told her which part he was offering: the small fold he had just held, its release, the way to begin without trying to turn the whole space around them.

Yuka asked what he would feel when she used it.

"The demand. And if it starts dragging at the edges because we're doing it badly." ᴹ

"Then tell me." ʸᵏ

"I will." ᴹ

She looked toward the marker.

"I'd like to try." ʸᵏ

Ciel helped him set the offered pattern apart from the pull already waiting there. The Noise kept the construction steady while Yuka found the new reach for herself.

She tried without forming the fold, withdrew and tried again.

Mau felt the second attempt arrive more cleanly.

"There," he said. ᴹ

Yuka looked at her hand.

"I can find it." ʸᵏ

"And you can choose it again," he said. "You don't have to ask me to start every attempt. We keep Blue quiet while we're learning what this needs." ᴹ

She nodded and went to stand beside Yuta.

* * *

Her first fold was mostly a hand moving through empty air.

She looked at the marker.

It looked back from exactly where it had been.

"Wonderful," she said. ʸᵏ

Yuta changed the angle of the cloth in his hand.

"Start here. Before you try to make it reach there." ʸᵗ

She watched him.

Then she tried again.

The edge of the marker shifted.

Yuka turned toward Mau too quickly and lost the hold.

He smiled before she could apologize for it.

"I saw." ᴹ

She looked pleased enough to forget the irritation for a moment.

* * *

The little ball of knotted cloth went straight past her first attempt.

It had not been thrown hard. Yuta caught it behind the marker and brought it back while Yuka inspected the place where she had meant the fold to be.

"You moved your hand toward it," he said. "Then the place you were holding moved too." ʸᵗ

She tried the motion without the operation.

Mau watched the difference she was finding between reaching and chasing.

She set her feet.

"Again." ʸᵏ

The second pass twitched sideways, then carried on.

The third went into the grass beside the marker instead of through it.

Yuka released at once.

She stood with her hand still raised.

Mau felt the demand go quiet and let his shoulders relax.

"That went where I asked," she said. ʸᵏ

"Not very far," Yuta said. "Far enough to tell the difference." ʸᵗ

She picked up the cloth ball herself and turned it over in her hands.

* * *

They stopped before the excitement became an argument for one more attempt made badly.

Yuta showed her the part he wanted her to practise without activating anything. Mau listened too; there was work in it for both of them.

When Yuta went to find Maki, Yuka stayed beside the marker.

"How did it feel?" she asked Mau. ʸᵏ

"Uneven at the beginning. Better when you let the fold stay small. It's quiet now." ᴹ

She looked at him properly before nodding.

"I don't want it to be something you hurt yourself handing me." ʸᵏ

He put the cloth ball on the support.

"Then we keep telling each other what it's doing. You did the part I couldn't do for you." ᴹ

"I know." ʸᵏ

The answer came without her looking away.

He liked that.

* * *

She called Nue after they had rested.

The bird came round over the clear patch, lowered itself and landed beside the marker. This time the rope did not have to be rescued from under its claws.

Yuka stroked the folded wing.

"That's better." ʸᵏ

Mau watched her send it to rest again.

"Where would you go first?" he asked. ᴹ

"When I can ride?" ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She looked up at the open sky.

"Somewhere we haven't been. Something I get to show you." ʸᵏ

He came to stand beside her.

"I'd like that." ᴹ

She took his hand.

"For today, I'd like lunch." ʸᵏ

He laughed.

"Also a good plan." ᴹ

They collected the rope and the knotted cloth before walking back. Yuka carried the little marker; Mau brought the cup neither had remembered to drink from while the fold was working.

At the door she stopped, turned and kissed him before going inside.

He took the seat beside her while she told Rem what they'd managed that morning.
