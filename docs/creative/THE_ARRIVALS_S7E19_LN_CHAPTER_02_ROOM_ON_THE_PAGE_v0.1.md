*When: Day542, morning. Jinshi has brought the old plan back to the table, and the first thing Byakuya does is put a cup beside it rather than on it.*

Mau recognized the circle around the tree.

The space they had left clear. The house built around it, the paths people actually used, the places where something had been drawn as a possibility and had not become a room merely because the pencil was confident.

Rimuru sat beside him.

"Before we decide what to add," he said, "I'd like to know what people want to have time for." ᴿ

Fern looked at the two lists Jinshi had brought.

One was about things.

The other had names.

* * *

Momo began with the kitchen door.

People came through it carrying work, questions, food and occasionally something which should have been left at the workshop until somebody identified it. She wanted deliveries to have a place before they became part of preparing dinner.

"A receiving table near the path," Jinshi said. "Appointments there. The things which need checking can stay there while we decide where they belong." ᴶ

Byakuya marked a question beside the approach rather than drawing a building.

"Covered eventually. First we see what space we have and what crosses it." ᴮʸ

Coco's mother leaned over to look.

"And somewhere a visiting family can put things without feeling they've taken somebody else's chair for the whole day." ᶜᵐ

Momo nodded.

Jinshi added that to the first list.

* * *

The second list took longer.

Some people knew exactly whom they wanted to ask about. Others had not decided whether reaching someone meant asking them to leave the life they had there.

Richeh came to put her brother's name where she could see it. Qifrey stood with her while Fern asked what she would want a message to say if they found a way to send one.

Rimuru looked at the space left on his own page.

"I have people I'd like to talk to," he said. "I want to think about what I'm asking before I decide that bringing someone here is the same as giving them what they want." ᴿ

Mau nodded.

"And the road might find somebody before a coin does. If they want to come, we should have an answer better than we'll work it out when you're already at the door." ᴹ

Fern wrote the question down.

Nobody spent a coin by putting a name on paper.

* * *

They did not revive the old promise of Arrival House and wait for a crowd to fill it.

There were people here already, people they wanted to look for, and a home whose rooms needed to keep making sense as those lives changed.

Byakuya asked about water and the route to the washrooms before anybody drew an extra floor. Momo asked who would want to cook in another family space and who would rather keep coming to the common table.

"Both should be possible," Fern said. ᶠᵉ

Coco was drawing a chair beside the place her mother had pointed to.

She looked up.

"Can it be a place where people sit even if they aren't waiting for something?" ᶜᵒ

Jinshi considered the little chair.

Then he put the question beside the others.

* * *

The meeting loosened when people had said the part they came to say.

Wakana took the measurements he needed for the covered table. Momo went to check the food with Rem. Qifrey asked Richeh which details she wanted kept private while they worked on the message.

Fern remained long enough to choose what actually needed doing first.

Then she gave Jinshi the copied tasks and went to find Stark.

Mau watched her leave without carrying every page away with her.

Rimuru folded his own list.

"We have enough to begin," he said. ᴿ

"Not everything?" Mau asked. ᴹ

Rimuru smiled.

"Enough." ᴿ

* * *

Yuka stayed to help Mau clear the pencils.

She had listened more than spoken, putting a mark beside the place where people would come back from the path with bags and want somewhere to set them before being asked six questions.

Now she looked at the blank space beside the current rooms.

"I'd like good light," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau followed her glance.

"For your practice?" ᴹ

"For a room. One day." ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

She turned the pencil between her fingers.

"I'm not moving my things this afternoon. I'm allowed to think about it." ʸᵏ

"Yes," he said. ᴹ

She waited.

"I'd like that too." ᴹ

Her smile came slowly, then stayed.

"Then when we're ready, we talk about it properly." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She put the pencil down without drawing a room which belonged to them before either had spoken to the people it would affect.

* * *

Outside, Rampart was learning the difference between where a beam could fit and where somebody would need to walk after it had been put down. Igris stood beside the work without making the guardian feel that the whole future depended on getting one piece right the first time.

Farther along the path, Kaneki had stopped to listen to Ichika. From the reading room came Frieren's voice, then Ori's, answering a question which apparently required considerable thought.

Mau could hear them without losing Yuka beside him.

The key was in his inner pocket. The next page had room left on it.

Yuka took the stack of clean cups and handed him the plan.

"Come on," she said. "They'll eat without us." ʸᵏ

He went with her.
