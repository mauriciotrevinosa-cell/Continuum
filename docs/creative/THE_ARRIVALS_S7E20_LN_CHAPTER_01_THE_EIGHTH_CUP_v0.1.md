*When: Day549, late morning. Mau has brought eight cups, and Daphne has already found the bread.*

He put the pot where she would not have to move it to reach the second piece.

Sekhmet had taken the bench. Carmilla sat beside Minerva, listening while the two disagreed about whether a small tear in a sleeve was something to repair now or something to leave until the person wearing it had finished eating.

Echidna watched the new receiving table through the window.

It was only a table, covered when it rained, placed where a delivery would no longer have to arrive in the middle of preparing dinner.

People had begun using it before they had finished deciding what to call it.

Pride waited until Mau sat down.

"You came without something to ask us to solve," she said. ᴾ

He looked at the cups.

"I can go and find a problem if that helps." ᴹ

Her small smile came.

"I was making an observation." ᴾ

* * *

Satella asked how the flying had gone.

He showed her with his hand, not getting up to turn the tea into a demonstration. The height. The slow movement. The part where landing had stopped feeling like somebody had removed the floor and changed their mind.

"And tomorrow?" Minerva asked. ᴹᶰ

"More practice. The parts that already worked, before I decide that means I can carry somebody over the roof." ᴹ

Carmilla smiled into her cup.

"That sounds like somebody has already asked." ᶜᵃ

"She asked about carrying. I added the roof myself." ᴹ

Minerva looked at him.

He took a drink before the question became a practical examination.

* * *

Echidna asked about the work beneath his attention.

Mau told her it stayed busy when he stopped giving it a new thing to do. He could choose a detail without losing everything beside it, then return to the conversation without asking people to begin again.

"Then what will you do with the room?" she asked. ᴱ

He began answering with the things he meant to practise.

There were plenty. Ways of holding a shape without wasting the beginning. A landing. Another operation he and Yuka could learn to use together. The things he wanted ready before he needed them badly enough to improvise with his body.

Echidna listened.

"Those too," she said when he paused. ᴱ

Mau looked at her.

On the bench, Sekhmet opened one eye.

"She asked about the room," she said slowly. "You started filling it before she'd finished." ˢᵉ

He leaned back.

Then he laughed.

* * *

Daphne wanted to talk about seeds.

Not only which ones somebody might bring back. What the people selling them expected the ground to do, how long they had to wait, what failed when the season changed.

"Ask them that part," she said. "Not just what color the fruit is in the drawing." ᴰᵃ

Mau nodded.

He let her finish before offering the answer he did not have yet.

Minerva wanted more of the people who said they were taking a quiet afternoon to actually take one. Carmilla had a question about the cloth Marin had brought back. Satella listened, then asked whether the new bench by the path would be somewhere people could sit after the deliveries were gone.

"Coco asked the same thing," Mau said. ᴹ

Satella looked pleased.

Pride rested her hands beside her cup.

"A useful distinction," she said. "A place is not only the thing it was first made to do." ᴾ

* * *

The pot grew lighter.

Nobody tried to earn the last part of the hour by making it important enough to record. Sekhmet closed her eye again. Daphne divided the remaining bread with considerably less reluctance than she pretended.

Mau reached for the pot, checked whether anybody else wanted it and filled the eighth cup again.

Echidna noticed.

"A decision?" she asked. ᴱ

"The next thing I'm doing with the room." ᴹ

He lifted the cup.

Pride smiled without making a mark anywhere.

Mau stayed until the hour was finished.
