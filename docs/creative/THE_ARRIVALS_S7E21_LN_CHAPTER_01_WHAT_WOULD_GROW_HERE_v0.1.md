Daphne took the page out of Mau's hands before he finished offering it.

She read it standing beside the greenhouse, with her thumb holding down the corner which wanted to lift in the breeze. Inside, the trays took up most of the available light. This was not a place where an exciting packet could simply be put down until somebody remembered it.

"This one," she said, tapping a description. ᴰᵃ

"You know it?" ᴹ

"No. I know what I want to ask about it." ᴰᵃ

He waited.

"Whether the part they sell is the part we would plant. How long it keeps after digging. Whether they mean it survives a frost or grows through one. People say the first as if it means the second." ᴰᵃ

She turned the page over.

"I would like to go." ᴰᵃ

Mau smiled.

"Hollis is asking when they can have us. I told him I'd ask you before making the visit bigger." ᴹ

"Good. Tell him I want to see the ground, not only the sacks." ᴰᵃ

Momo added two questions and crossed out one of Mau's.

"We can ask that too," she said. "After we know whether we can cook it." ᴹᵒ

* * *

Three mornings later, Yuka put a small empty sack inside the larger one and let Mau struggle with the tie until he asked her to hold the other end.

It was not a dungeon bag. The food in it was for the road, and there was space left for things they had not bought yet. Maomao's separate wrapping was marked for the clinic. The clean jars were for samples, not a promise that every interesting leaf belonged in somebody's dinner.

Daphne arrived with her questions folded small enough to fit in a pocket.

Hollis counted the people before the bags. He had a place for her beside the cart and an agreed stop before they turned off the road.

Rimuru stood by the receiving table with Jinshi.

"We're going to talk to a farmer," Mau said. ᴹ

"I remember. You told me yesterday." ᴿ

"I'm reassuring myself." ᴹ

Rimuru looked at the empty sack.

"Then try bringing something back which doesn't require us to build a new room before dinner." ᴿ

Yuka took Mau's hand.

"We didn't promise that." ʸᵏ

Rimuru pointed at her.

"You were supposed to help." ᴿ

She smiled and went with Mau toward the cart.

Ciel checked the private contact before they left. A quiet answer from Rimuru, her acknowledgement, and then the road belonged to Hollis again.

* * *

They did not fly over the places Hollis wanted them to see.

At the narrow bend, one of his guards went ahead while the other waited with the cart. Hollis pointed out the rut which looked shallow from the wrong side and had once cost him a wheel.

Yuka looked down at it as they passed.

"A wheel?" ʸᵏ

"And most of an afternoon. The man who sold me the next wheel heard about both." ᴴᵒ

Mau laughed.

Past the bend, the ground opened into worked strips. There were stakes at the ends of some, pale against the darker soil. Beyond them, a fence had been repaired with several kinds of wood which had no interest in matching.

Mau had expected to recognize the farm by the fields.

Daphne recognized it by the questions she stopped asking.

She watched the line where a darker bed met a pale, stony patch, then leaned forward in her seat.

"There," she said. ᴰᵃ

Hollis followed her glance.

"That's the place." ᴴᵒ

* * *

"Ilen," she said. "Hollis said you'd ask before touching anything." ᴵˡ

"All of you?" ᴵˡ
