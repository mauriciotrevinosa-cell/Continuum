*When: Day553, afternoon into evening. Mau has moved the pots. One of them is still in front of him.*

The first time, he made the little stem tremble.

The second, he held the flow so carefully that it never quite reached the place Ilen had shown him.

She let him stop before correcting that one.

"Did you feel where it ended?" she asked. ᴵˡ

He nodded.

"Then why did you leave it there?" ᴵˡ

Mau looked at the plant.

Because it had been a very long time since *more* had been a comfortable answer to a question about something fragile in his hands.

"I didn't want to pull it apart." ᴹ

Ilen turned the pot a little.

"Good. Don't. You don't need to go through the root." ᴵˡ

She laid one fingertip beside his, leaving a space between them.

"Here. Let it come this far. Then stop making it come." ᴵˡ

* * *

Daphne had taken Yuka to compare the things they might actually carry home.

He heard them at the next table: one name being checked against a split root, then a question about how much of the leaf was supposed to be removed before cooking.

Their voices did not drown in the work of looking.

Ciel waited until Ilen went to fetch water.

*The release is changing before the edge reaches the soil.*

*I can feel it.*

*Would you like the comparison?*

He looked at the little space beside the stem.

*Yes. What I did, beside what she showed us. Not through her.*

*Only the demonstration she allowed.*

He watched the difference without losing the table beneath his hands.

It would have been easy to mistake steadiness for stillness. Ilen's pattern had not been motionless. It had kept making tiny room for something which was still alive inside it.

Mau breathed out.

When Ilen returned, he asked her to watch the next attempt.

* * *

This time the trace reached the new soil.

He almost followed it.

Ilen made a small sound, not quite a word, and he stopped pushing before the little shape collapsed under his interest in it.

For a moment nothing appeared to happen.

The pot remained on the table. The stem did not stand up. Yuka did not begin applauding, which Mau appreciated until he looked up and saw how hard she was trying not to.

Ilen moved closer.

"Let go," she said. ᴵˡ

He took his hands away.

A thin, uneven trace remained.

He waited for it to fail.

It stayed.

Ilen touched the outside of the pot, considered it, then nodded once.

"Keep that one in the shade on the way back." ᴵˡ

"It worked?" ᴹ

"You left something where I asked. We'll see what the plant thinks." ᴵˡ

Yuka rested her chin against his shoulder.

"Congratulations. Your pot stayed on the table." ʸᵏ

He laughed and turned his head enough to kiss her cheek.

She smiled without moving away.

Ilen began putting the other pots back in their tray.

"Do the two of you want this lesson or a different table?" ᴵˡ

Yuka straightened so quickly that Mau laughed again.

* * *

They bought less than the empty sack could have held.

Daphne left two kinds behind after hearing how long they needed before the first useful crop. She took a small amount of another and told Ilen exactly how she meant to try it under glass before giving it a bed outside.

Hollis counted the cost with her, then with Ilen. There was still ordinary supply money left when they finished. Nobody opened a second purse and made the decision for Daphne because a little packet did not look expensive enough to matter.

Yuka copied the descriptions onto the wrappings.

She held up one of them.

"This one is the pale flower when it's ready?" ʸᵏ

"Pale center. Green around it," Ilen said. ᴵˡ

Yuka looked at Mau for a moment, then put the packet down beside her own hand rather than asking him to remember the shade.

"Can I draw which part?" she asked. ʸᵏ

Ilen turned over the paper.

"Please do. Half my nephew's first harvest would have benefited from that question." ᴵˡ

Yuka drew the flower badly, crossed it out and began again.

Mau waited beside her.

The line of her wrist was warm with color. The little drawing was not. She added a mark at the center, held it against Ilen's description and let him look.

"That part," she said quietly. ʸᵏ

"I've got it." ᴹ

She squeezed his fingers once beneath the edge of the table, then went back to the packet.

* * *

At the gate, Ilen looked at the seedling Mau was carrying.

"Don't keep taking it out to see whether the roots understood you." ᴵˡ

"I won't." ᴹ

"And tell me if it dies." ᴵˡ

He looked up.

"Not only if it grows," she added. ᴵˡ

"I'll tell you." ᴹ

Daphne shook her hand and repeated what she could bring on Hollis's next agreed trip. A small exchange. Enough to test before either of them gave away something they would need themselves.

Ilen watched them make a place in the cart where the pot could not roll.

Then she handed Yuka a folded piece of cloth.

"Under it. The road gets rough past the bend." ᴵˡ

* * *

Mau spent part of the journey with the pot between his feet and part with it in his lap.

Yuka leaned against him where the cart moved most gently. She had the seed papers in her own bag. Daphne sat on the other side with the purchased packets and a list which had become longer while they were buying fewer things.

"You look happy," Mau said to her. ᴹ

"I found something I can use." ᴰᵃ

He glanced at the pot.

"We haven't proved this part yet." ᴹ

"I know. The roots we bought will store. Momo can cook them. The leaves can be tried in the small house. And the woman who grows them will tell us when we've done something wrong." ᴰᵃ

She folded the page.

"I found several things I can use." ᴰᵃ

Mau settled the pot more firmly between his hands.

Yuka's head rested on his shoulder.

For a while, all he needed to watch was the road.

* * *

They reached home before the evening meal.

Momo took the cooking description, Maomao checked her separate wrapping and Daphne carried the packets toward the barn before anybody could turn *we brought seeds* into *we have new vegetables tomorrow*.

Rimuru looked at the plant.

"No new room?" ᴿ

"One pot," Mau said. ᴹ

"I'm proud of you." ᴿ

"I tried to pick it up without using my hands." ᴹ

Yuka began laughing.

"He was supposed to be teaching it to stay in the ground." ʸᵏ

Then he took the bag from Mau's shoulder so Mau could carry the pot properly.

"Come on. Show me where it goes." ᴿ
