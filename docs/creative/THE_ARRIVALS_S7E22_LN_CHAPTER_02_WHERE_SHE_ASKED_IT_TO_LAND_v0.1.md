*When: Day556, morning. Yuta has put the sack down where Yuka asked him to put it.*

Nue landed beside it.

Not on it. Not on the rope. Its nearest claw settled beyond the mark Yuka had scratched into the earth, and the folded wing missed Mau's head by enough that Maki did not have to say anything.

Yuka looked at her grandmother anyway.

Maki lifted her chin toward the sack.

"Again with the weight." ᴹᵃ

"You saw that landing." ʸᵏ

"I did. Now I want to see the next one." ᴹᵃ

Yuka sighed, went to check the fastening and gave Nue the order.

It took the bundled load where she had shown it.

The first beat of its wings dragged one corner through the grass.

She stopped it before the rope could catch beneath a claw.

Mau picked up the empty end and held it out to her.

"I would have called that a landing." ᴹ

"You don't get a vote." ʸᵏ

"Apparently neither does the sack." ᴹ

She took the rope and tried again.

* * *

By the time Yuka put her own hands against the bird, she was glad nobody had agreed to let her begin there.

Weight changed the turn.

The first time Nue had lifted the bundle, it had tried to climb before the load was underneath it. She had felt the change through her technique without understanding quickly enough what needed correcting.

Now she knew where she would be when it happened.

Yuta held the broad loop they had been using to distribute the load. He did not put it on her until she had decided where it should sit.

Maki checked the knot, then Yuka checked it herself.

Mau stood beyond the first landing mark.

"Not past you," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"Down if I lift this hand." ʸᵏ

Nue shifted its claws.

She made herself look at the bird rather than at Mau.

This was the part she had wanted.

It was considerably easier to want it while both feet were still on the ground.

* * *

Her toes left first.

Then the grass went away beneath them.

Yuka tightened every muscle she had and immediately understood why Yuta had told her not to fight the lift with her shoulders.

Nue rose crookedly.

She raised her hand.

It came down.

Both feet hit the ground, one before the other. She stumbled into the slack of the loop and caught herself before Mau reached her.

He stopped when he saw that she had her balance.

Yuka stayed bent over for a moment, laughing without quite meaning to.

"I hate that," she said. ʸᵏ

Maki looked at Yuta.

Yuta wisely said nothing.

Yuka straightened.

"Again." ʸᵏ

* * *

The next lift was smaller.

She let her feet hang instead of reaching for a floor which had not gone far enough away to need rescuing.

Nue held her.

Yuka looked along the field to the first mark. Mau stood to one side of it now, leaving the route clear. He looked pleased in a way which made her want to show off and sensible in a way which made that much harder.

She asked the bird to move.

It began too quickly.

"Easy," she said, then again, more clearly. ʸᵏ

Not a prayer for something to understand her excitement. An order, given where she could feel it take hold.

The movement slowed.

She passed over the grass, low enough to see where the stems bent beneath the wings. The first marker came toward her. She told Nue to stop before she reached it.

For a moment they hung there.

Then she asked it to go down.

* * *

Mau held the bird's loose strap while she stepped out.

Yuka put both hands on the feathers and rested her forehead against them.

She could feel her technique still working. This was not the same as asking Nue to turn over an empty field while she watched. There had been her weight, her fear, the way she had almost pulled against her own order as soon as the ground moved.

She wanted to do it again immediately.

She also wanted to sit down.

"One more?" Mau asked. ᴹ

Yuka considered him.

"Not yet." ʸᵏ

"All right." ᴹ

She dismissed Nue after checking that nothing remained tangled in the loop. Its shape went back into the shadow beneath her.

The field grew quiet.

Maki handed her the water.

"You stopped it," she said. ᴹᵃ

Yuka drank before answering.

"I know. Before I got there." ʸᵏ

"I saw." ᴹᵃ

This time Yuka smiled.

* * *

She sat with Mau after Yuta and Maki went to put away the load.

Yuka turned the cup in her hands.

"Could you see what I was doing wrong?" ʸᵏ

"Some of it." ᴹ

"You didn't say." ʸᵏ

"You'd already come down before I could." ᴹ

She thought about that.

"What about the second time?" ʸᵏ

"You were doing it right." ᴹ

Yuka looked toward the little mark in the grass.

It had seemed much farther away with her feet hanging above it.

"I want to go farther." ʸᵏ

"We'll find somewhere to try it." ᴹ

She leaned into him, warm from the work.

"Me. I want to take you somewhere when I can do it properly." ʸᵏ

He turned to look at her.

"I know you have a dragon," she added. ʸᵏ

"I wasn't going to say that." ᴹ

"You looked like you might." ʸᵏ

He smiled.

"Where are we going?" ᴹ

She put the cup down and kissed him before answering.

"I'll tell you when I can get us there." ʸᵏ
