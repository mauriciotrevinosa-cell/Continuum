*When: Day568, afternoon. Hollis returns with the drawing still folded inside his coat.*

Anko asked him where he had stood.

He put his finger on the little mark before answering the questions about the blue.

Mau knew what it resembled. That was not enough to tell him whether everything beyond it would resemble the places he knew.

"You didn't see anything come through," he said. ᴹ

"No. The man who went closer hadn't either while he was there." ᴴᵒ

Mikasa looked at the gap between the cutting and the fence.

"Who was watching after you left?" ᴹⁱᵏ

"The same guards. I haven't heard from them since." ᴴᵒ

She nodded and left that part of the paper empty.

* * *

Jinshi had asked Hollis about the undelivered load first.

It had gone another way. Later than promised, with enough trouble to make the cost real even though no one had been attacked.

That did not make it a request for the house to replace the road.

It did mean the next person might ask.

"Can they get somebody else to look?" Momo asked. ᴹᵒ

"They may have by now," Hollis said. "I wasn't there to stop them." ᴴᵒ

Mau looked at Rimuru across the table.

There was a moment in which he could have offered everything he wanted ready at once. Cleared roads. People who did not look toward the nearest weapon when they saw his hair. A reason for the world to leave the people here alone.

Rimuru waited.

Mau looked back at the small mark Hollis had drawn.

"We should see it," he said. ᴹ

"Yes," Anko said. "With something better than a person pointing at the wrong bend." ᴬⁿ

Hollis tapped his drawing.

"I take my work seriously." ᴴᵒ

"Then you can show us." ᴬⁿ

* * *

They began with the four soldiers who already knew how to leave a road and come back with a report.

Mikasa wanted them placed far enough apart to notice somebody approaching without turning every person into an alarm. Anko wanted one meeting point which could not be confused with the cutting itself.

Igris listened beside the table.

"And if there are people there," Mau said, "we ask what they've done before sending anybody across it. Including a shadow." ᴹ

Hollis glanced up.

Mau saw the question.

"I want to know more than whether something can survive on the other side." ᴹ

Igris inclined his head.

Fern put a small list beside the map.

Food, water, the ordinary things which could be packed tonight. Not enough to turn a survey into a week-long expedition without anybody being told.

"If that changes," she said, "so does the plan." ᶠᵉ

"We come back or speak to you before deciding to stay," Mau said. ᴹ

Rimuru nodded.

Ciel would carry what he actually told her. That did not put the people at home beside him, or make the whole road visible to them.

* * *

Hollis stayed after Mikasa took the map.

"If somebody asks you to make it go away," he said, "what do you want me to tell them?" ᴴᵒ

"That we haven't seen it yet," Rimuru said. ᴿ

"And after that?" ᴴᵒ

Mau put his cup down.

"Then we tell them what we can do. Not everything they hope we can." ᴹ

Hollis looked pleased with that answer and unconvinced that it would remain easy.

Mau understood both parts.

"I'd like you and me to keep speaking for the house when it needs that," he said to Rimuru. "Together when we can. But this isn't a reason to leave everybody else's work waiting for us." ᴹ

Rimuru looked toward the workshop note beneath Jinshi's hand.

"That was already happening without us." ᴿ

"Good." ᴹ

Jinshi turned the page toward Hollis.

There were still questions about the second cutter. Material. Shape. When the people making it could actually do the work. No one had promised to deliver it tomorrow because a new blue opening had appeared on a road.

"If they want help from people here," Mau said, "they have to let those people arrive as themselves. I want that in the conversation before the next time someone asks Hollis to bring a tool and leave the person who made it behind." ᴹ

Hollis rested a hand on the drawing.

"I can introduce you to the people at the rope. I can't make them everybody on the road." ᴴᵒ

"Then we start with them," Mau said. ᴹ

* * *

Yuka found him after the food had been packed.

She had heard the plan. He knew because she pointed at the smaller bag and asked which part was supposed to be his lunch.

He showed her.

She moved it away from the things he would not need until they stopped walking.

"Yuta said he'd help me try the longer turn tomorrow," she said. ʸᵏ

"With Nue?" ᴹ

She nodded.

"I wanted to ask him before everybody found something else for him to do." ʸᵏ

Mau smiled.

"Tell me how it goes." ᴹ

"You too." ʸᵏ

He nodded toward the map.

"You already know the interesting part." ᴹ

"I know what Hollis saw. You haven't gone yet." ʸᵏ

She came closer and put a hand against his chest, not checking anything. Just finding the place she liked to stand when they had finished putting things between them on a table.

"Come back and tell me yours." ʸᵏ

He put his arms around her.

"I will." ᴹ

She stayed there until he stopped looking over her shoulder at the bag.

Then she kissed him and went to find her grandfather before he could agree to spend the morning doing somebody else's sensible thing.
