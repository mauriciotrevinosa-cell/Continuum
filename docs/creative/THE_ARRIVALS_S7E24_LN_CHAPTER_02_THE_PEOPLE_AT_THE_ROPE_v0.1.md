*When: Day569, late morning. This time Hollis comes to the rope with the people he meant to ask.*

The guard recognized him before recognizing Mau.

Hollis watched the moment change.

The man's hand went toward his belt, stopped and remained there while he counted the people coming down the road behind the cart.

Mikasa did not reach for anything.

Anko stopped beside Hollis.

Mau waited a little farther back, with Igris and the four soldiers out of the way of the horses.

"You asked whether I knew somebody," Hollis said. ᴴᵒ

"You didn't say—" The guard looked past him again. ᵍ

"I said I meant to ask. I did." ᴴᵒ

For a moment, neither man moved the rope.

Then Anko pointed toward the cutting.

"What changed since he was here?" ᴬⁿ

The guard looked at her, grateful or irritated to have been given a question he could answer.

"Three people went in." ᵍ

* * *

They had gone after Hollis left.

Not the same day. Not a group sent by anybody from the house. Men who worked the road and had decided that if nothing was coming out, somebody ought to find out whether the road could be used again.

There had been a line tied between the first man and the people waiting outside.

It had come back without him.

The second and third had followed before anyone had agreed on what that meant.

The guard who told them had not seen the first man's knot. He said so when Mikasa asked.

She crouched beside the rope which had been recovered and looked at the end without pretending it would tell her everything about the hand which had tied it.

"When?" she asked. ᴹⁱᵏ

"Two days ago." ᵍ

Mau looked toward the blue.

It moved between the rocks with the same indifference as the first drawing had suggested.

"Who knows them?" he asked. ᴹ

* * *

A woman came forward from beside the nearest cart.

Her brother was the second man. She described his coat, the things he had carried and the old injury in his left hand before Mau finished asking what would help them recognize him.

Mikasa wrote down the part about the hand.

Anko asked what the men had meant to find and whether any of them knew how to fight.

One did, according to the guard. The woman said her brother had helped defend a cart once, which was not quite the same answer.

Mau listened to both.

"Can you get them out?" the woman asked him. ʷ

He wanted to say yes quickly enough to remove the look from her face.

He had not seen the other side.

"I'll look for them," he said. "I can't tell you where they are yet." ᴹ

Her mouth tightened.

Then she nodded.

"He'll say he can walk. Look at the leg before you believe him." ʷ

Anko put that beside the hand.

* * *

The guard lifted the rope.

He did not offer them the road beyond every town he had never visited. He offered them the stretch he had been told to keep people off, and sent a runner to the place which would have to receive anyone they brought back hurt.

Hollis stayed on the near side with the cart.

Mikasa found a place where three people could be put down without blocking the way. Anko asked for clean water and got it from the woman's own supply before the guard had finished considering which permission he needed.

Mau looked back at Igris.

The knight had kept the soldiers still through the whole conversation.

"Two here," Mau said. "They help if you ask. They don't decide to follow somebody because the person is upset." ᴹ

Hollis nodded.

The guard looked at the nearest soldier.

It did not move toward him.

After a while he looked away first.

* * *

Mau walked up to the opening with Anko and Mikasa.

Close to it, the blue did not explain itself any better.

The road narrowed into bare stone beside the place where the light interrupted it. There was no safe width left for a loaded cart. On foot, they could have gone around it and continued walking.

That would not bring the three men back.

Mau checked the contact with Rimuru before stepping through.

*One step first. If it cuts, I come back and we decide from there.*

Ciel acknowledged him.

He crossed.

* * *

Stone under his boots.

A different air. Cooler, still, smelling faintly of water which had not moved far enough.

He could hear something beyond the first bend.

Mau stopped with room behind him for the others, then asked Ciel whether the contact held.

*It holds.*

*Tell Rimuru I'm going back to bring them in.*

He turned and stepped outside again.

Anko looked at his empty hands, then his face.

"Room?" ᴬⁿ

"A passage. Water somewhere ahead. I heard something. I don't know what yet." ᴹ

Mikasa tightened the fastening on the small bag she meant to carry.

Igris came forward with two soldiers.

Mau looked once toward the woman waiting by the rope.

She was watching for a person rather than for the light.

He went back through before deciding he knew what he would find.
