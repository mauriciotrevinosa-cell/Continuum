*When: Day569, early afternoon. The sound past the bend comes again before anyone speaks.*

Three blows.

A pause.

Then two, less evenly spaced.

Anko raised a hand.

Mau stopped beside her, leaving Mikasa room to look beyond his shoulder. Igris waited behind the second soldier rather than letting his armor fill the part of the passage they might need to come back through.

The sound came once more.

Metal against stone.

"That isn't water," Anko said. ᴬⁿ

* * *

The passage widened at the bend.

Mau saw the broken jar first. Then the shaft of a tool propped between two pieces of stone and the hand holding its end where it had been pressed across a narrower opening.

Something hit the other side.

The hand slipped.

Mau moved before the shaft reached the floor.

The first creature came through shoulders first, dragging a crude shield into the gap. Its mouth opened above a neck ringed with hard scales.

He caught the shield with his left hand and drove the dagger through the space its raised arm had left unprotected.

The blow stopped the creature. His grip kept the shield from striking the person beneath it.

Mau lowered both before looking through the gap.

Three men.

One had a coat wrapped around his leg. One held the other end of the broken tool with a left hand which would not straighten completely.

The third lifted an empty metal cup as if he had been waiting to show somebody where the sound had come from.

"Don't stand up yet," Mau said. ᴹ

* * *

Mikasa saw the second attacker above the opening.

Its clawed hand caught the stone at the same moment the tool came free. It pushed up and over, reaching toward the injured men instead of toward Mau.

Mikasa stepped onto the low ledge.

Her blade met the reaching arm and continued through the neck below it. The body struck the outer face of the barricade and fell away from the people behind it.

Anko was already kneeling beside the wrapped leg.

"Look at me," she told its owner. "Did you fall on this?" ᴬⁿ

He began to answer, but the man with the injured hand spoke over him.

"I told him not to stand." ʷ

"Then you can help me keep him from doing it again." ᴬⁿ

Mau turned toward the far passage.

A spear came out of it.

* * *

He could have stopped it against Infinity.

It would still have been moving through a room with Anko's back toward it.

Mau caught a short fold in the space to his right instead. The surface resisted his hand, then gave just enough to change where the spear was arriving.

It struck the shield he had put down.

He let the rough shape go before trying to do anything else with the technique.

The thrower came after its weapon.

Mau met it halfway, stepped inside its second reach and put it down with a short strike beneath the jaw followed by the dagger through the opening it made.

Behind him, Igris's sword sounded against stone.

A fourth creature had tried the side of the chamber while Mau looked toward the spear. It did not reach Anko.

"How many ways in?" Mikasa asked. ᴹⁱᵏ

"Two I can see," Mau said. ᴹ

She pointed toward the low opening.

"Three. That one behind you." ᴹⁱᵏ

He turned.

* * *

Two more bodies forced their way into the room.

Mau waited until the first had moved clear of the stone lip above it. The cut was narrow and low, placed through flesh rather than through the ceiling behind it.

The second tried to climb over the falling body.

He changed the line before releasing the next Dismantle.

Both stopped moving.

The room was quiet long enough for him to hear Anko asking somebody to hold the splint still.

He looked back.

The two ordinary soldiers had put down what they were carrying and waited until she showed them where their hands would help. The injured man had stopped insisting that he could stand.

Mikasa crouched beside the man with the cup.

"Are there more of you?" ᴹⁱᵏ

He shook his head.

"Three. Just us." ʷ

Mau listened down the passage.

Not quiet yet.

* * *

The last two came separately.

That did not make them separate fights.

One threw itself toward the narrow gap as Igris turned. The other stayed back long enough to see whether Mau would follow it into the darker passage.

Mau did not.

He put himself between the opening and the man Anko was helping to sit up.

The knight's sword caught the nearer creature before it could gain the ledge. Mau waited until the other committed to the throw it had been preparing, moved inside the weapon's path and ended the motion with its arm still raised.

This time, when he listened, he heard only water and people.

He kept listening.

* * *

Anko had three answers and wanted one more.

"Anything hit your head?" she asked the man with the cup. ᴬⁿ

"No." ʷ

"All right. A little water. Slowly." ᴬⁿ

The man took it with both hands.

Mau asked Ciel to tell Rimuru they had found all three alive. One could not walk out safely. They were bringing him back toward the opening, not beginning a search for the far end of the dungeon.

*Understood.*

He crouched where the injured man could see him.

"We're going to lift you. She'll tell us how. Is that all right?" ᴹ

The man looked at the two silent soldiers.

Then at Anko's hands on the splint.

"Can they do it?" ʷ

"They can hold still better than I can," Mau said. ᴹ

That got a short, disbelieving laugh from the man with the bad hand.

The injured man nodded.

* * *

They left the chamber more slowly than they had entered it.

Mikasa went ahead with the man who had used the cup. Anko walked beside the makeshift carry. Mau stayed where the last turn would not hide the people moving in front of him.

Igris held the rear.

At the threshold, the man with the injured hand stopped.

The light made a line across his face. For a moment he stared at it without taking the next step.

Mau did not push him.

"She told us about your hand," he said. ᴹ

The man turned.

"Your sister. She's waiting by the cart." ᴹ

The man's mouth moved once before he found an answer.

"She's going to kill me." ʷ

"Not while she's holding the water," Anko said. ᴬⁿ

He laughed in a way which shook halfway apart, then walked through the blue.

* * *

The woman reached him before he had finished looking at the road.

She did not ask what Mau could do. She put a hand against her brother's face, looked him over and began asking him questions too quickly for any of the answers.

Behind them, the carry reached the place Mikasa had cleared.

Hollis had the cart ready.

The guard at the rope moved to help without waiting for an order about whose hands were allowed on it.

Mau stood aside until all three men were out of the way of the opening.

Then he turned back toward the blue, where Igris was still watching the empty threshold.
