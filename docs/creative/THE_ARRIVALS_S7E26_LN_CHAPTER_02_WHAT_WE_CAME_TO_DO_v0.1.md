*When: The night of Day569, then the morning of Day570. Stark finds the work waiting beside his axe, not inside a promise somebody has made for him.*

Wakana had put the replacement binding where Stark would see it before taking the weapon out again.

It was ordinary work. A place which had begun to rub, a better strip to keep it from becoming a problem at the wrong moment. Stark turned the handle in his hands while Mau explained what he had found on the road.

Yuta waited beside the bench.

"You want to go back in tomorrow," Stark said. ˢᵗ

"We shouldn't leave it because the first room was enough to bring people out," Mau said. ᴹ

"And if it's bigger than that?" ˢᵗ

"Then we find out before pretending we've finished it." ᴹ

Stark checked the new binding with his thumb.

"I'll come." ˢᵗ

* * *

They did not finish the plan at the bench.

Anko brought the account of the injured men. Mikasa laid out the way from the road to the first bend. Momo and Rem agreed what could be packed without leaving breakfast half-prepared for everyone else.

Hollis looked at the route and offered to come.

Jinshi looked at the time he had finally returned.

"We know where to go," Mau said before Hollis could make another day's driving sound like a small thing. "You gave us that yesterday." ᴹ

Hollis sat down again.

Yuka put herself beside the route, not beside the food list.

"I'd like to go. Nue doesn't have to carry me to be useful." ʸᵏ

Yuta nodded.

"I'll come with you. We'll see the turn before asking it to fly through one." ʸᵗ

Rimuru kept the copied drawing.

He would be at home. Anko would make sure the people who had gone out of the way to help three strangers did not become invisible now that there was an interesting gate to talk about.

Fern asked what they would do if the way back changed.

They answered that before discussing what might be at the end.

* * *

The guard at the road looked tired when they arrived.

He recognized Mau and let him finish introducing the others instead of leaving the rope up until everyone had become annoyed with it.

The man with the injured leg had been seen. The woman had taken her brother away after being told where to return if the people who had found him came back with more questions.

Nothing had come out of the light in the night.

The guard did not say that made it safe.

"We're going farther this time," Mau told him. "If we have to turn back, we'll tell you why. We aren't promising the road before we've seen the rest." ᴹ

The man glanced at the handcart and the soldiers waiting beside it.

"And those?" ᵍ

"To carry what we need. And what we bring out. They stay clear of your carts unless you ask them to help." ᴹ

The guard moved the rope.

* * *

Stark went into the first chamber expecting the part which would make yesterday's story different.

What he found was the low space where three people had waited.

It was smaller than he had imagined.

Yuka crouched beside the place where the tool had been braced, then stood up without touching it.

Yuta checked the passage on the left. Igris checked the other. Two soldiers remained with the cart while the rest waited for instructions about which places would need holding.

Mau gave them those instructions before moving deeper.

Stark tightened his grip on the axe.

The new binding did not rub.

* * *

The next six creatures came through a turn too narrow for all of them to reach the party at once.

Yuka's black dog caught the first low movement before the spear behind it could use the gap. Yuta stepped past her shoulder, turned the shaft aside and cut through the exposed line beneath the scales.

Stark stopped the second with the flat of his axe, drove it back into the one behind it and changed his grip for the cut which finished both.

Mau held the farther turn long enough for Yuka to move clear.

Three more bodies struck the stone. One beneath Igris's sword. Two before Mau took his hand away from the line he had chosen.

Stark looked back.

Yuka was checking her dog, not waiting to be told whether she had been useful.

* * *

The second group had learned to stay above the passage.

Or something had put them there.

Stark heard the first claw against the stone and moved before he had decided which answer he preferred. A spear struck where his shoulder had been.

Yuka's rabbits broke across the floor below the ledge.

The creature above him reached toward the wrong movement.

His axe caught it at the edge.

Mau shifted a short pull into the far corner, not dragging the whole ceiling down, making two of the six lose the foothold they had chosen. Igris and Yuta met them before they could recover.

Nue passed beneath the high opening with its wings held tighter than it had in the field. A crackle crossed the lip of the ledge. Two attackers fell into the place Stark had left open for the next swing.

The last dropped behind Yuka.

Her dog turned it before it could strike. Mau ended the movement while she pulled the bird back into the passage she could still see.

Then there was room to breathe.

Yuta looked along the ledge before looking at her.

"That turn," he said. ʸᵗ

"I saw it." ʸᵏ

He smiled.

"I know." ʸᵗ

* * *

They stopped before the passage widened again.

Mau told Rimuru where they had reached. Twelve more hostile bodies, no one hurt, first route still open. They had not found the end.

Stark took the water Yuka passed him.

Something tapped beneath his boots.

He stopped swallowing.

The sound was not behind them. Not from the men left with the cart.

It ran through the stone beneath his feet and reached the wide opening ahead at the same moment a line of dust fell from its top.

Mau turned toward it.

Stark gave Yuka the water back.

"Stay off that part," he said, pointing with the axe. ˢᵗ

The floor moved before anyone had time to ask how much of it he meant.
