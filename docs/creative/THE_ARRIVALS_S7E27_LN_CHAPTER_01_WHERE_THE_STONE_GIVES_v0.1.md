*When: Day570, midday. The first warning reaches Stark through the soles of his boots.*

He jumped sideways.

The floor broke where he had stood.

Not a slab falling into emptiness. Something rising through it, pushing the stone up on a broad plated back before the pieces could finish splitting.

Stark landed near Yuta and saw the head come through.

It had no neck he could use. The front narrowed into a hard, low wedge. Two forelimbs opened beneath it, the claws wide enough to catch a body at the waist and carry it under the shell.

Mau caught Yuka's elbow as the ground tilted beneath her.

She stepped where he pulled, then let go and called Nue out of the falling dust.

The creature turned toward the beat of the wings.

"Not over us," Stark said. ˢᵗ

Yuka sent the bird toward the high side of the chamber.

The creature followed it into open ground.

* * *

Stark's first blow struck the shell above the nearer forelimb.

It landed hard enough to make the sound change.

It did not go through.

The limb swept back before he could lift the axe for a second strike. He went with the movement, let it carry him clear of the head and planted a foot against the stone behind him.

Yuta crossed the space Stark had left.

His blade found the narrow seam beneath the lifted plate. The edge went in, then stopped as the creature folded its limb against the shell.

Yuta let the sword slide free before the turn could take it out of his hands.

"Underneath," he said. ʸᵗ

"I saw." ˢᵗ

Stark did not quite like how much that had sounded like something he had heard other people tell him.

He moved before the claws came down.

* * *

The second turn took it toward the passage.

Igris stood in the opening while the soldiers moved the handcart farther back. One held the line of the path clear. Another took the falling pieces away from the place the wheels would need.

Mau saw a crack reach above the doorway.

He did not try to hold the mountain.

The loosened stone slowed inside the brief grip he put beneath it. Igris moved clear; two soldiers dragged the nearest piece aside when Mau lowered it far enough to touch the floor.

Beyond them, Ciel acknowledged the message he had sent home.

*The route remains open.*

He checked it himself before turning back.

The creature had already begun to dig.

* * *

"It's going under," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

Her rabbits spread toward the far side, away from the passage and the people in it.

The head vanished first. The shell followed, leaving a line through the broken floor where the tail had been.

Stark watched the dust.

It moved near the rabbits.

Then stopped.

He felt the next tap beneath his heel.

"Back." ˢᵗ

Yuta moved with him.

The head came up between the place they had stood and the opening Igris was holding.

Mau's pull caught it from the side before the whole body could surface there.

Not the gentle tug Yuka had been practising on cloth. The Blue took hold hard enough to turn the line of the rise, tearing open the stone beside the head instead of letting it emerge beneath the doorway.

The creature came out facing the wrong way.

Stark hit the exposed forelimb.

* * *

This time he felt the difference through the handle.

The joint gave before the plate above it could catch the blade.

The creature struck the floor with the other limb and swung its weight around the wound. Stark let go with one hand to clear the edge of the shell. Yuta caught the next claw against his blade long enough for him to get his footing back.

Nue crossed the chamber.

Lightning struck along the wet line beneath the lifted plates.

The creature shook, then brought its head toward Yuka with a speed its size had not promised.

She called the rabbits back across its path and moved beyond them, pulling her dog out of the line of the claws.

Mau stepped into the turn.

His hand met the side of the head rather than the point of it. The impact cracked the floor beneath his forward foot. He gave ground on purpose, changing the angle until the body had to follow him away from her.

"Stark." ᴹ

Stark was already moving.

* * *

The opening lasted less time than he wanted.

Enough to drive the axe below the damaged plate. Not enough to take it out before the creature folded itself around the wound.

The blade stuck.

Stark pulled once and felt the shell closing harder.

He let the handle move with it instead of planting his feet and being taken underneath.

Yuta came from the other side.

"Let it lift," he said. ʸᵗ

Stark looked at him, then at the line where his own blade had disappeared.

He understood.

The creature wanted the ground. It wanted the narrow world beneath it, where nobody could reach the open seams from two directions at once.

They had been trying to keep it still.

Stark changed his grip.

"Mau. Up, not back." ˢᵗ

* * *

Mau checked where Yuka was before making the force.

She had Nue low on the far side. Her dog was clear. Igris had the opening and nobody stood above the place the shell would reach.

The Red formed beneath the forward edge, small enough to place where a limb had left room, hard enough that the first movement was the whole front of the body leaving the floor.

Stone scattered away from the passage.

The creature rose crookedly.

Stark rose with the handle.

For an instant the weight changed from something closing on his axe to something hanging beyond it.

He tore the blade free.

Yuta's next cut entered below the lifted shell. Igris reached the wounded limb from the passage side and stopped it finding the ground first.

Yuka's short pull caught the loose plate Stark had already split.

Not the whole creature. Only the piece which needed to move a little farther before he had the line.

It opened.

Stark put both hands behind the axe.

* * *

The blow went through the place Yuta had shown him.

Through the seam, not the shell.

The creature hit the stone with its head turned aside and the damaged forelimb folded beneath it. Stark landed clear of the last movement, took one step back and waited with the axe raised.

Yuta stayed beside him.

Nue went quiet above the floor.

Mau kept his eyes on the passage until the dust stopped falling from its top.

Then the chamber changed.

The hard shimmer across the far wall broke apart. A space which had looked like more rock opened behind it, shallow and dry, with a long box resting above the floor.

Stark lowered the axe.

He was breathing hard enough to hear it.

Yuka put one hand against her knee and laughed once, mostly air.

"I got the plate," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau looked at her.

"You got it." ᴹ

Stark turned the handle in his palms.

The new binding had stayed where Wakana put it.

He meant to tell him that before telling anybody about the box.
