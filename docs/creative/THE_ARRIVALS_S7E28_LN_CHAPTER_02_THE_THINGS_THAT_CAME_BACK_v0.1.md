*When: Day580, late afternoon. Hollis puts an empty wrapping on the receiving table, and Daphne knows which question to ask first.*

"She planted them?" ᴰᵃ

"Half. Kept half, like you asked. She says if they fail she'll have enough to blame the first attempt properly." ᴴᵒ

Daphne unfolded the paper inside the cloth.

Ilen's little sketch showed which part of the home sample had interested her. Beside it, somebody had written the day she put it in the ground. She had thanked them for returning the padding before her nephew decided it had become a towel.

Mau leaned over Daphne's shoulder.

"She got the part about the roots?" ᴹ

"She got all of it. Including the part where you didn't grow dinner out of a stone." ᴴᵒ

Daphne made a satisfied sound and carried the reply toward the barn.

Hollis watched her go.

"I think she likes the woman." ᴴᵒ

"She answered the questions," Mau said. ᴹ

"That would do it." ᴴᵒ

* * *

The second paper went to Wakana.

The buyer had agreed to the changed shape, the price and the wait. One cutter. The smaller drawing attached to the page showed where their fingers had to fit while the tool was being used against something set close to a wall.

Wakana put the drawing beside the piece of remaining bar he had already marked for the proposal.

Marin reached for the stiff wrapping they had used to test the first grip.

"That corner again," she said. ᴹᵃʳ

"Different hand," Wakana said. ᵂ

"Then we make the hand." ᴹᵃʳ

She began folding the wrapping into the approximate shape on the page. He changed the place he had meant to begin the cut.

Jinshi left the order with them and took the delivery agreement to Fern.

Nobody called Mau back to approve the corner.

* * *

Yuka found him behind the work area, trying to carry three empty trays without letting the top one slide into his elbow.

She took it.

"I had that," he said. ᴹ

"You still have two." ʸᵏ

They returned them to the place Daphne had cleared, then sat on the low wall where the light stayed longest.

Yuka had put the tray between them. Mau moved it to the other side of his feet.

She took the space it had occupied.

"You went quiet at practice again," she said. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"I didn't mean the part where Gojo was speaking. Everybody did that." ʸᵏ

Mau laughed, but it left him quickly.

She waited.

"Sometimes doing something better reminds me of what came with it before," he said. ᴹ

"The tower?" ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She knew there had been one. Knew it had given him people who knelt in his shadow and something which beat beneath her hand if she stayed close enough. She had not been there.

He did not turn that into a reason she could not ask.

"Is this because of what we're doing?" she asked. ʸᵏ

"The practice?" ᴹ

"Me asking for more." ʸᵏ

He turned toward her fully.

"No. I want you to ask." ᴹ

"Then tell me when it's something else. I can't tell which part you're looking at if you disappear while you're sitting beside me." ʸᵏ

Mau looked down at their hands.

There were words he could give her. Exact words, kept too neatly for how little he understood them. He did not want to place a cold, waiting thing between them and watch her begin measuring every ordinary day against it.

That was not the same as knowing she would.

"I don't understand all of it yet," he said. "But I can tell you when I'm remembering instead of making you guess." ᴹ

Yuka considered that.

"You don't have to give me the whole thing every time." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"You do have to let me decide whether I want to sit here." ʸᵏ

He moved his hand toward hers, leaving the last part for her to cross.

She did.

* * *

They stayed until the edge of the light reached their shoes.

From the workshop came the sound of Marin dropping something light, then accusing it of doing that on purpose. Wakana's answer was too quiet to hear; her laugh was not.

Yuka leaned against Mau.

"I'm practising the fold tomorrow. You can watch if you promise not to look disappointed when I miss." ʸᵏ

"I wasn't disappointed last time." ᴹ

"You looked very thoughtful." ʸᵏ

"I was trying to work out where the cloth went." ᴹ

She covered her face with one hand.

He kissed the fingers which were still holding his.

"I'll come." ᴹ
