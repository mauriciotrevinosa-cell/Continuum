*When: Day596. Yuka has put the question at the top of the page before Mau arrives.*

He read it, then read it again.

"You could have asked me." ᴹ

"I did. You said we should ask Yuta and Gojo. They said we should find a time. This is the time." ʸᵏ

Gojo looked offended by how accurately that represented him.

Yuta sat down before he could improve the explanation.

Rimuru moved a cup out of the space Yuka had left for the next page.

At the top, in her handwriting: *What do I need to make a domain?*

* * *

"Not only more energy," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

"I knew you'd start there." ʸᵏ

"Did you?" ʸᵗ

She looked at the second line, where she had crossed something out.

Gojo leaned over to see it.

She covered it with her hand.

He smiled and sat back.

"The technique isn't the whole room," he said. "You can have the thing you want to put in it and still make a very poor place for it to stand." ᴳ

Yuka looked at Mau.

"You have it." ʸᵏ

"The technique. Some ways to build a domain. Not a finished room of yours." ᴹ

"Could you build one of yours first?" ʸᵏ

He considered the question instead of answering with what he wanted to promise her.

"I can try to construct the part we'd need to understand. Then we find out whether that gives you something useful, or just gives me something I know how to do." ᴹ

Yuka nodded slowly.

"I want to understand the difference." ʸᵏ

* * *

They took the next part outside.

Not because the table had become dangerous. Yuka wanted to begin somewhere she could stand and feel the edges instead of trying to picture them between the cups.

She called the black dog, let it walk round her and dismissed it again.

The shadow beneath her closed.

Gojo asked what she felt at the moment it opened. Yuta asked what she did when she wanted it to stop. Mau waited until she had answered both before asking whether she wanted him to look through the connection.

"At the technique," he said. "The part we're working on. Not everything you are because there's a way between us." ᴹ

"Can I tell when you're there?" ʸᵏ

"We'll make sure you can. If you don't like it, we stop." ᴹ

She held out her hand.

"Then first tell me what you're asking it to do." ʸᵏ

* * *

Ciel took the agreement one part at a time.

The living technique. The route already built by the contract. A signal from Yuka to end the examination, separate from her letting go of Mau's hand by accident.

Rimuru stood where she could speak to him without looking past Mau.

When the little contact changed, Yuka lifted her free hand.

Mau stopped.

She waited, feeling the absence where the inquiry had been.

Then she lowered her hand.

"Again." ʸᵏ

* * *

Mau did not find a finished future hidden behind the part she had learned to use.

He found shapes she could already reach, others folded out of her present control, and the work of a technique which knew more ways to become a space than her hands knew how to ask for.

Noise went still around the boundary Ciel kept for it.

Then a word moved through the private contact.

*Unfinished.*

Mau felt Yuka's fingers tighten.

"What?" she asked. ʸᵏ

He told her what had been said, then told her what he was actually seeing before the word could become a verdict about her.

"There isn't a finished domain sitting there waiting for me to press it," he said. "There are parts we can work with." ᴹ

"So work with them," she said. "Not the rest." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

Ciel kept the boundary where they had agreed to put it.

* * *

The first attempt belonged to Mau.

Yuka watched from beyond the place he had marked, with Yuta beside her and Gojo telling him when the edge of the barrier had begun disagreeing with the thing he was putting inside it.

It looked unimpressive.

A darkening beneath Mau's feet. A little reach which went farther on one side than the other. Then a ripple, a bad join and the return of ordinary ground.

Mau breathed out.

Gojo crouched beside the failed edge.

"That part," he said. ᴳ

"I felt it." ᴹ

"Good. Do it again without making the same apology in advance." ᴳ

Yuka laughed.

Mau looked at her.

She was still holding the page. There was a mark on it now where the first answer had become something more useful than *get stronger*.

"Mine," she said, tapping the question. "When you understand that part, you show me how to make it mine." ʸᵏ

"I will." ᴹ

* * *

They stopped for food before trying the next join.

Yuka took Mau's hand on the way back, without asking the contract for anything.

He felt the two rhythms inside his chest, then the small pressure of her fingers against his.

For a moment the word from the stone room came near enough to hear.

*Vessel.*

He kept walking.

He had asked her before entering. Stopped when she asked. Left the rest of her where it belonged.

He could remember a hand which had not done any of those things without becoming that hand.

Yuka nudged him toward the kitchen.

"You can think while you carry plates." ʸᵏ

He took the stack she gave him.
