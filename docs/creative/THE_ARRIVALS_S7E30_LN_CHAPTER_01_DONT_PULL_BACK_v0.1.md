*When: Day605. Yuka has asked them to stay beyond the line this time.*

Yuta checked the place where she meant to begin.

Mau checked the place where he had promised to stand.

They were not the same place.

Yuka noticed him measuring the distance and raised an eyebrow.

"I heard you," he said. ᴹ

"Good." ʸᵏ

She put the loose end of her rope beside her knee, not round her wrist. The last bird had taught her something about letting a line decide where her shoulder went.

This time the dog waited behind her.

She made the sign.

* * *

The tongue came before she had finished deciding how large the mouth was.

Yuka went sideways.

Too far. The wet line struck the floor beside her foot, pulled taut and turned her first step into the second one she had not meant to take.

She hit the ground on one knee.

The toad opened its mouth again.

Yuka rolled beneath the next reach and came up with the rope still in her hand.

Beyond the mark, Mau had stopped moving.

She could feel the impulse to look at him for an answer.

She kept her eyes on the mouth.

* * *

The rabbits went wide.

Not toward the toad all at once. Across its view, changing where the next small movement would be before it had finished drawing the tongue back.

Yuka let the first pass go.

The second caught a rabbit which broke apart before it could be taken. The tongue returned with nothing held on it.

She saw the moment before the mouth closed.

Not long enough to run straight at it.

Long enough to move the dog.

The black shape crossed behind the toad and struck low at the hind leg as Yuka went forward from the other side.

Its body turned faster than she expected.

The tongue caught her rope.

* * *

She pulled back.

The toad pulled harder.

Yuka's boot dragged across the floor, and for one miserable second she understood that she was about to be defeated by the part of the plan she had brought in herself.

Then she stopped pulling back.

She went forward instead.

The sudden slack brought her under the tongue's line. Her rope fell across the lower part of the open jaw where she could reach it from the side.

The dog moved when she told it to.

The hind leg folded. The body tilted.

Yuka went with it, bringing her weight down where the head could not swing back toward her without meeting the line she had put across it.

Her arms shook.

She stopped trying to win with only her arms.

One knee against the floor. Her shoulder lower. Her own technique holding the dog where it needed to stay.

"Here," she said through her teeth. "Stay here." ʸᵏ

The toad pushed once more.

Then the resistance went out of the rope.

* * *

For a moment she remained exactly where she was.

Mau did too.

Yuka looked down at the shadow beneath her hand.

It answered before she tried the sign again.

She sat back, breathing hard, and began laughing.

Yuta waited for her to look up before crossing the mark with the water.

"You let it pull you," he said. ʸᵗ

"It was going to anyway." ʸᵏ

"You changed what that meant." ʸᵗ

She accepted the cup with both hands.

Mau crouched beside her.

"You stayed," she said. ʸᵏ

"You asked me to." ᴹ

She leaned over and kissed him before he could decide whether there was a more useful thing to say.

* * *

The later call belonged to her too.

Toad appeared with its mouth closed. Yuka let it turn toward her, dismissed it and called it once more after Yuta had finished moving the loose rope out of the way.

It stayed.

"All right," she said. "Now we find out what I can ask you to pick up." ʸᵏ

Mau put a small empty bag on the floor.

Yuka gave the order.

The tongue went out, caught the bag and brought it back much faster than she had wanted.

It struck her chest hard enough to make her take a step backward.

Mau caught the falling bag.

Yuta covered his mouth.

"Not a person," Yuka said immediately. ʸᵏ

Mau shook his head, smiling now.

"Not yet." ᴹ

She pointed at him.

"Especially not you, Oppa." ʸᵏ

His laugh followed them all the way back to the door.
