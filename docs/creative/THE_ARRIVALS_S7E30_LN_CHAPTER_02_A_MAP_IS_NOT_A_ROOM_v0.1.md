*When: Day614. The first thing that works is much smaller than the thing Mau had been trying to make.*

Gojo stood outside the chalk and counted without enthusiasm.

At four, the edge wavered.

At five, Mau corrected the wrong part and almost lost the whole thing.

At six, he stopped trying to make the center answer for the boundary.

The dark surface beneath his feet settled.

Gojo stopped counting.

Yuka leaned forward.

"It stayed." ʸᵏ

Mau let it go before trying to find out what else he could put in it.

Ordinary ground came back beneath him.

Yuta looked at the place the edge had occupied.

"That join. Show her that." ʸᵗ

* * *

They sat down before opening the connection.

Yuka kept her free hand where Rimuru could see it. She had asked for that part herself after the first examination, and nobody had called it unnecessary because the first time had gone well.

Ciel repeated the scope.

Mau gave Noise the constructed map, not an instruction to take whatever it could reach and make it better.

Yuka felt the inquiry arrive.

She held still.

Then she shook her head.

"Too much at once." ʸᵏ

They stopped.

* * *

Mau had been about to apologize when Yuta spoke.

"Which part?" ʸᵗ

Yuka looked at him, then at the hands resting in her lap.

"I can feel how he holds the edge. I can't find where I begin doing it. It's like someone moved my feet while I wasn't looking." ʸᵏ

Mau understood before he liked understanding.

He had wanted to give her a way through. He had brought the shape with himself still in it.

"We take my part out," he said. ᴹ

Gojo nodded.

"Now you're teaching." ᴳ

* * *

The second attempt was slower.

Ciel kept the framework available while Yuka found her own beginning. The route within her technique no longer doubled back at the place Noise had marked as unfinished; it led somewhere she could actually feel from the shadow beneath her.

She did not walk all the way down it.

She asked for one part. Where her energy went when the edge began. Where it was supposed to stop reaching outward and begin enclosing.

Mau watched her face.

Behind his own ribs, the cold beat seemed suddenly too loud.

*He left his door in you.*

For an instant the hands between them were not hers.

He withdrew before the thought became a movement through the connection.

* * *

Yuka opened her eyes.

"Did I stop it?" ʸᵏ

"No. I did." ᴹ

She watched him take his next breath.

He put both feet flat against the floor.

"A memory," he said, as he had promised her. "I need a minute." ᴹ

Rimuru sat beside him without turning the minute into a question asked in front of everyone.

Gojo stood up to get the water.

Yuka left her hand open on her own knee.

Nobody had to keep holding him for him to remain there.

* * *

They finished the lesson with Yuka standing alone.

No thread moving through the contract now. The map was something she could return to, not a hand which had to stay inside her to make the technique exist.

She reached for the shadow beneath her.

It widened.

Not a domain. The edge broke before it could become the other side of a boundary. Yuka felt where it failed and lowered her hands before somebody else had to stop her.

She looked at Mau.

"That's mine," she said. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She tried it again.

* * *

On the way back, Gojo walked beside Mau until the others were far enough ahead that he did not have to raise his voice.

"You stopped," he said. ᴳ

Mau looked at him.

"I noticed." ᴹ

"I meant before you had to make somebody else do it." ᴳ

Mau was quiet for a few steps.

"It doesn't always feel like getting better." ᴹ

Gojo looked toward Yuka, who was showing Yuta the place her hands had been when the little edge broke.

"No," he said. "Still counts." ᴳ

He went ahead to interrupt Yuta's explanation with a much better one.

Yuka told him to wait until the first explanation had finished.

Mau laughed.

The cold had not gone. Neither had the road beneath his feet.
