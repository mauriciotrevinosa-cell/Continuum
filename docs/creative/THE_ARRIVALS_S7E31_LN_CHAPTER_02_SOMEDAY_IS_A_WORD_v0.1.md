*When: Day625, evening. Yuka asks the second question after they have eaten, while Mau is drying the cup she has just washed.*

"You said the things I wanted afterward." ʸᵏ

He looked up.

"Earlier. The other conversation. What was that part?" ʸᵏ

Mau put the dry cup where it would not need to be rescued if his hands forgot it.

"We talked about a future. A family. Not something already happening." ᴹ

Yuka leaned against the edge of the counter.

"Children?" ʸᵏ

He nodded.

Her face warmed before she found the next sentence.

"Did I ask?" ʸᵏ

"We both talked about it." ᴹ

"Did you promise?" ʸᵏ

That answer took him longer, not because he did not know it.

"Yes." ᴹ

* * *

She looked down at the water on her hands.

Mau gave her the cloth instead of reaching for an explanation before she had finished hearing the answer.

Yuka dried one finger at a time.

"And if I hadn't wanted that?" ʸᵏ

"Then we'd need to talk about what we wanted here. Not make you keep an answer because I heard it before." ᴹ

"Would you have been disappointed?" ʸᵏ

He sat on the stool beside the counter.

"Maybe. If we wanted different lives. That wouldn't make yours wrong." ᴹ

She watched him.

"You want it?" ʸᵏ

"I want to be able to talk about it without either of us being afraid the next day is our last chance." ᴹ

Yuka's fingers stopped on the cloth.

He looked at her.

"And yes. I can imagine wanting children with you. Not only because I once said yes." ᴹ

* * *

They took the conversation to the bench near the common room, where the window let enough light fall behind them to see the cups without making everyone inside part of it.

Yuka tucked one foot beneath herself and faced him.

"I do want to be a mother one day," she said. "Not tomorrow." ʸᵏ

Mau smiled.

"Tomorrow's busy." ᴹ

She nudged his knee with hers.

"I'm being serious." ʸᵏ

"I heard you." ᴹ

"I don't need you to know everything now. I needed to ask before I started making a whole life in my head with somebody who was only being kind when he listened." ʸᵏ

Mau turned his hand over on the bench between them.

She took it.

"I'm not only being kind." ᴹ

* * *

There were things they could not promise.

He said that part before she had to ask it for him. They did not know enough about his body to answer every question about the life he could make with someone else. Getting stronger did not turn an unknown into a certainty merely because he wanted the certainty badly.

Yuka listened without taking her hand away.

"Then we find out," she said. "When we need to. We can still talk about what we'd like." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She looked toward the common-room window.

Inside, Ori was leaning over Frieren's open book. Frieren held the page against a small hand which had wanted to turn it too early. The answer she gave was too quiet to hear.

Yuka smiled at the sight, then returned to him.

"I don't have a sensible version of this yet," she said. ʸᵏ

"I was hoping you did. I was going to copy it." ᴹ

She laughed.

The ordinary sound loosened something in him which the afternoon had not asked her to fix.

* * *

They became less sensible as they went.

Where a small bed would fit in a room they did not yet have. Whether either of them would be useful when somebody cried at a time which did not care how much sleep they had planned. What a child might think of a house where going to find an uncle could produce half a dozen different people before breakfast.

"Uncle Gojo would teach them something awful," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

"He'd tell them it was advanced." ᴹ

"Then Shoko would teach them what to say back." ʸᵏ

Mau began laughing.

Yuka leaned against him, pleased with the whole imaginary disaster.

After a while she asked, quite casually, what they would call a boy.

Behind them, something struck the window frame.

* * *

Yuta stood in the doorway with the expression of a man who had heard one word too clearly and everything before it not at all.

"A boy?" ʸᵗ

Yuka straightened.

Mau's hand remained in hers, which suddenly seemed like a piece of evidence nobody had asked him to prepare.

"Hypothetically," he said. ᴹ

Yuta looked at him.

"That's a very long word for this hour." ʸᵗ

Yuka covered her face with the hand which was free.

"We're talking about someday." ʸᵏ

"Someday how far?" ʸᵗ

"Not because anything happened," she said, peering at him between her fingers. ʸᵏ

Yuta looked from her to Mau.

Mau shook his head.

"Nothing happened. We were talking." ᴹ

The change in Yuta's shoulders was so complete that Yuka began laughing before he had finished breathing out.

"I thought I'd skipped a whole conversation," he said. ʸᵗ

"You did," she said. "You came for the names." ʸᵏ

* * *

He stood there for another moment, caught between leaving them their privacy and checking that he had not misunderstood the correction too.

Yuka got up and hugged him.

"I want a future," she said into his shoulder. "I'm allowed to talk about one." ʸᵏ

Yuta put his hand against her back.

"You are." ʸᵗ

Mau looked away long enough for them to have that without him watching it like a verdict on whether he was welcome.

When Yuka came back to the bench, Yuta pointed at him.

"Tell Gojo about that part at your own risk." ʸᵗ

Mau nodded solemnly.

Yuta went inside.

They waited until the door had closed before laughing again.

Yuka put her head against Mau's shoulder.

"We still didn't choose a name." ʸᵏ

He kissed her hair.

"We have time." ᴹ
