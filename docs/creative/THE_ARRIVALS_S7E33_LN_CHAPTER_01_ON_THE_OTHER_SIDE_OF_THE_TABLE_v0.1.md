*When: Day654. Momo comes to the kitchen and finds Rem standing in the place where she meant to begin.*

"I was going to cut those," Momo said. ᴹᵒ

"I know." ᴿᵉ

Ram took the empty bowl out of her hand before she could find a different job for it.

"You're early for dinner." ᴿᵃ

"I live here." ᴹᵒ

"Then you should know where the chairs are." ᴿᵃ

Momo looked past them.

Taihei was checking the pot. Coco's mother had the washing in a system which did not require four people to reach for the same cloth. Mau and Fern were working at the far table, with Frieren watching the size of the pieces in front of her as if there might be an interesting spell for avoiding the next half of the work.

Fern handed her another vegetable.

Frieren accepted it.

Momo looked at Rem again.

"You really don't need—" ᴹᵒ

"Sit down," Rem said, smiling. "Tell me after you've tasted it." ᴿᵉ

* * *

She sat outside the new covered stretch first.

It was not a new house. A little more room to arrive with something in both hands without standing in the rain, and space for a table where the old path had wanted people to move quickly.

The posts stood clear of the tree's root circle. Byakuya had made them move one before the first beam was lifted, and Muster had held the replacement where he was told while the people with the plan checked the angle again.

Now the beam stayed without anyone holding it.

Two benches waited beneath it.

Momo sat on one and found that nothing happened because she had stopped being useful for a moment.

* * *

Jinshi came past with the accepted copy from the second cutter's delivery.

The buyer had the tool. The agreed money had come back through Hollis after the fit was checked. Part went where the earlier costs had told them it needed to go; the little covered place had used some of the ordinary material they could now buy without postponing every other repair.

Not the price of a town.

Enough to stop making everybody choose between carrying the food in dry and leaving the same table dry for someone else.

Momo moved her feet so he could put a folded cloth on the bench.

"What's that for?" she asked. ᴹᵒ

"People sitting here," he said. ᴶ

She looked at him.

He smiled and went back inside.

* * *

Daphne brought the leaves herself.

A small bowl, rinsed and ready. Not every new thing they had planted. The first little bed had done well enough to give up this much without eating the next attempt before it began.

She set it where Rem asked.

Momo stood up to come and look, then stopped halfway between the bench and the door.

"Am I allowed to ask what you're doing with those?" ᴹᵒ

Ram looked over.

"Questions are included. Taking the knife isn't." ᴿᵃ

Momo put both hands behind her back.

Rem began showing her.

* * *

Mau got the timing wrong.

Only by enough that the thing he carried out first had to wait while the next pot finished. Fern took one look at the waiting bowls and asked why he had not told her he was taking them.

"Because I thought that meant I was doing something helpful." ᴹ

"You are. After the next thing is ready." ᶠᵉ

Frieren glanced up from the pieces she had finally finished.

"He does like to go first." ᶠʳ

Mau looked at her.

She gave him a very small, perfectly composed smile.

Fern laughed before she could turn it into a cough.

He put the bowls down and helped her with the next tray instead.

* * *

At dinner, Momo found a place already kept for her.

Not at the end where somebody would need to get up every time a bowl ran low. Beside Nazuna, who had watched her trying to leave the bench three times and had somehow made none of the remarks Momo had expected.

"How was your afternoon?" Nazuna asked. ᴺ

Momo took a bite before answering.

It was good.

Not exactly how she would have done it. The leaves had kept more of their own taste, and somebody had cut one of the roots a little too large.

She took another bite.

"Strange," she said. ᴹᵒ

Nazuna smiled.

"Have another one." ᴺ

Across the table, Mau moved to stand when someone called for a bowl. Yuka caught his sleeve, pointed toward the tray already coming and kept him beside her.

Rem set the next dish down without waiting for either of them to save dinner.

Momo ate while it was hot.
