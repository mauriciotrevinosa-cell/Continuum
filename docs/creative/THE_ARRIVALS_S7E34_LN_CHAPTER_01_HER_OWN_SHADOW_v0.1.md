*When: Day681. Yuka asks Mau to stand inside the mark and Gojo to stop counting as if he has already decided where she will fail.*

"I'm counting evenly," Gojo said. ᴳ

"You sound disappointed at four." ʸᵏ

Yuta looked away.

Mau stood where she had asked him to stand. The borrowed operations were quiet between them; he could feel the connection without either of them reaching through it for the work she meant to do herself.

She checked that once more.

Then she looked at the shadow beneath her feet.

* * *

The first time she had tried it, she had imagined a larger place to summon from.

The next, she had imagined a boundary so firmly that the thing inside it had nowhere left to move.

Mau's map had shown her where the joins could go. It had not told her what it felt like to stand in a place and decide that its shadows answered to her.

She knew more of that now.

The dog waiting on the near side. The rabbits crossing where she needed a gaze to turn. Nue finding a route above the one on the floor. Toad bringing something back, gently at last, because she had learned to ask it for the whole movement.

Not someone else's army.

Hers.

Yuka put her hands together.

"Domain Expansion." ʸᵏ

* * *

The boundary closed.

Mau felt the outside fall away before the surface beneath him finished darkening.

Not water. Not quite floor. A depth which accepted the shape of his feet without asking the ground beyond the barrier to disappear.

Yuka stood on the other side of it with her eyes open.

"Chimera Shadow Garden." ʸᵏ

Her voice sounded steadier than she felt.

She kept the next breath small.

* * *

The dog appeared where the light behind Mau should have left no useful shadow.

He turned toward it.

Too late for the small weight which had already touched the edge of his boot.

Not an attack. A contact at the place she had chosen, completed before his body finished correcting where he thought it would come from.

Yuka felt the cost of that choice.

The boundary wanted her attention again. So did the dark surface. So did the second place she had almost opened because the first had finally worked.

She let the second go.

Kept the first.

Then opened the boundary before it had to break.

* * *

The practice ground returned.

Mau was still looking at his boot.

Yuka could hear herself breathing.

Gojo had stopped counting at six.

Nobody spoke for a moment.

"Mine?" she asked, hating how small the question came out after the words she had just managed. ʸᵏ

Yuta stepped forward.

"Yours." ʸᵗ

She looked at Mau.

He was smiling in a way she could not mistake for being kind about a nearly successful attempt.

* * *

Her next call did not answer.

She knew enough now not to take that as something having been stolen from her.

The technique had worked harder than it had ever worked under her hands. For the moment, the place she reached toward was there and not ready to open again.

Gojo put the water down beside her.

"You don't chase that," he said. ᴳ

"I know." ʸᵏ

"You know it while you're pleased with yourself too?" ᴳ

She looked up at him.

He smiled.

She took the water.

* * *

They talked after she could hear them without counting the beats in her own temples.

Gojo asked about the boundary. Yuta asked why she had let the second opening go. Mau told her exactly when he had realized the first one was already beside him.

There was more to learn. Keeping a field long enough to fight in it. The targeting which still had to become reliable rather than merely surprising. What happened when another domain pushed against hers.

Those were next questions.

They did not take this answer away.

* * *

Later, the black dog came when she called.

She put her hands against its head and stayed like that long enough for Mau to sit down beside her without speaking.

"I wanted you inside because you'd know," she said. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"If it was really me doing it. If I was only repeating the part you could make happen." ʸᵏ

"I knew." ᴹ

She turned toward him.

"You were looking the wrong way." ʸᵏ

He laughed.

"I noticed." ᴹ

She kissed him, pleased enough not to ask the moment to become anything more useful.

The dog leaned its head between their shoulders.

Yuka laughed against Mau's mouth and let the interruption stay.
