He opened one eye.

Rimuru leaned over the edge with one hand over his mouth. Beyond the open door, Ori stood holding a ribbon which led upward instead of backward.

Milim floated at the other end of it.

Asleep. Entirely unconcerned by the way one foot had nearly met the doorframe.

Ori adjusted the ribbon with the seriousness of somebody bringing an important passenger through a narrow harbor.

"She wouldn't wake up," she explained. ᴼ

"So you brought her?" ᴹ

"Mum said breakfast soon. You said we'd go together." ᴼ

Milim opened one eye, looked at him and reached upward as if the noise had come from a useful pillow.

He withdrew from the edge.

"Not from there," he said. ᴿ
