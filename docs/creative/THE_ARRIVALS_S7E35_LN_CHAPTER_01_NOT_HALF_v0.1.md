*When: Day708. Ciel asks him why he keeps drawing the same line twice.*

Mau looked at the page.

*Because I keep thinking about what happens when both of us want to use it.*

The first small mark belonged to him. The second to Yuka. Between them was the support he already knew how to give, and the point where a thing they could share still did not mean two full things existed.

*She can already choose to use what we've built,* Ciel said.

*I know. I don't want to make that something she has to earn again.*

He moved the second mark farther from the first without breaking the line between them.

*I want this part to be hers without making it half of mine.*

* * *

Noise answered by showing him where he had put the problem in the wrong place.

Not a voice explaining a plan it had already finished without him. A difference in the pattern beneath his attention, the place where a second operation had been asked to begin by taking the first operation's room.

Mau followed it with Ciel.

The route could be built separately.

The supply would still come back to him.

*Both at the same time,* he said.

*Both demands,* Ciel answered. *Not one demand divided by two.*

He looked at the little marks again.

That was the thing he wanted.

It was also the thing he could not call a gift simply because he liked imagining her face when it worked.

* * *

He told Yuka after practice.

Before opening anything through the contract. Before pretending the fact he could make the first changes inside himself meant she did not need to hear what the changes were meant to ask of both of them.

She listened while he drew the two marks.

Then she put her finger on the line returning to him.

* * *

They left with a smaller first test than the one Mau had brought.

Two short pulls, each on its own safe target, with the drafts quiet before and after. Then the neutral operation. Reversal only when the supply and the exit were as well understood as the beginning.

Not half.

Not free.
