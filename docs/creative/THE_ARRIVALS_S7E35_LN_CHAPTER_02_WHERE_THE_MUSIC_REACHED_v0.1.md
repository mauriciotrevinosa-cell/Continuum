*When: Day714, evening. Bocchi discovers that the little covered place is a very poor place to hide from people who already know where the music will be.*

Nijika found her before she had finished deciding whether the stool was in the wrong position.

Kita moved it half a step and asked her to try the opening again.

Ryo listened, nodded and went to find the thing she had put down somewhere sensible enough that nobody had noticed it.

People arrived without becoming an audience all at once.

Marin sat on the bench with Wakana beside her. Coco brought her mother to see the place she had wanted people to be able to sit. Kaneki stood near the back until Ichika pulled him toward the gap Touka had kept.

No one needed to explain which part of the house they belonged to before choosing a chair.

* * *

Mau began at the side.

Yuka moved him when Stark arrived with Fern, making the space between the two benches large enough for a conversation instead of an apology every time somebody tried to sit down.

Stark told her about the new axe's grip before she asked.

Mau listened while Fern reached past him to make sure Frieren's book was not about to become the place somebody put a cup.

Ori had chosen the cushion beside Frieren. The compass hung where she could keep it out of the way of the small bundle of snacks Milim was attempting to negotiate.

Yuka stole one from Mau's hand and left him the less interesting piece.

He looked at it.

She smiled with absolutely no intention of returning what she had taken.

* * *

Bocchi found him before they began.

She did not leave her place. Only looked toward the side of the bench, the way she had looked up after the second ending in the quieter room.

He lifted his hand enough for her to see it.

She looked back at the others.

Nijika counted them in.

The first phrase reached the room and then went farther, under the new cover and out along the path where someone bringing the last tray stopped to hear it properly.

Mau recognized the ending before it arrived.

It belonged there.

* * *

Halfway through the next piece, his hand found the shape of the key inside his pocket.

He had not meant to check it.

The long wait remained long. The metal had not become warm. No voice came out of it to ask whether he was ready.

He could still remember another voice saying that readiness was something it had built into him.

*A vessel.*

The second beat struck cold beneath the music.

For a moment he heard the room in two ways. Here, where Ori was laughing at something Milim whispered. There, where a grey hand had known exactly where to reach before he understood why it was reaching.

Yuka touched his wrist.

He looked at her.

"Remembering," he said quietly. ᴹ

She nodded once.

Then she left her hand there, without asking him to prove he was back by becoming pleased quickly enough.

* * *

He heard the ending.

Not because the memory had gone politely when the song asked for the space. Because Yuka's fingers were warm against his and the musicians in front of him were playing something they had made with no reason to be afraid of the room it would enter.

He took his hand out of the pocket.

When the last note ended, he joined the others before Bocchi could begin wondering whether they had heard the part which mattered.

Her head went down.

Her smile stayed.

* * *

Afterward, everyone was suddenly very available for food.

Rem brought the first tray into the wider space. Momo sat long enough to finish what was on her plate before joining the people putting away the empty things. Fern gave Jinshi the page he needed and remained beside Stark when somebody offered a second helping.

Gojo tried to make Shoko admit his chosen seat had a better view.

She said she had a perfectly good view of him getting up to fetch more tea.

He went.

Yuka leaned close to Mau.

"Advanced lesson," she whispered. ʸᵏ

He laughed, surprised enough by the ordinary joke to feel how badly he had needed it.

* * *

They helped fold the cloths before going upstairs.

Yuka kept the one which belonged to the tray she had brought. Mau carried the last cups. They left neither a promise of a new room nor a regret about the old one for someone else to clear away after them.

At the corridor, she stopped him.

"Tomorrow. The two marks," she said. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"Together?" ᴹ

She smiled.

"That's the point." ʸᵏ

He kissed her good night and watched her reach her own door before turning toward the room where Rimuru would complain if he left his shoes across the way again.

Below them, someone was still humming the tune.
