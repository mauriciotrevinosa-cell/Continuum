*When: Day715. There are two blocks on the practice ground, and Yuka has made Gojo promise not to move either one to improve the joke.*

He had looked hurt.

Then he had moved his chair, which had occupied exactly the place she wanted left clear.

Mau stood on the other side of the two targets. The new pattern was not open yet. He could feel Ciel checking the separate beginnings while Noise kept the constructed routes from settling back into the more familiar one.

Yuka looked at him.

"Mine to the left." ʸᵏ

"Mine straight back." ᴹ

"Small first." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

Gojo stopped smiling when they began.

* * *

Both blocks moved toward Mau.

Not hard enough to become dangerous. Far enough to make Yuka release her pull before pretending the second beginning had been hers.

Mau closed his own at once.

The targets stopped.

For a moment they all looked at the two marks which now led in the same wrong direction.

Yuka pointed at hers.

"I didn't ask for that." ʸᵏ

"I know," Mau said. ᴹ

Gojo crouched beside the second track.

"Separate hands. Still borrowing the first place to stand." ᴳ

* * *

They took the correction slowly.

Ciel kept the first route quiet while Yuka found her own point. Noise held the second pattern apart from the familiar shape inside Mau, and he felt the strange sensation of something existing twice without needing to become half of itself in either place.

The second heart beat once against the attention he gave it.

Cold went through his hands.

He stopped before reaching for the next step.

Yuka let her hands fall.

Gojo looked up.

"Memory?" Yuka asked. ʸᵏ

Mau nodded.

She moved the nearer block back to its first mark while he sat down, giving him something which belonged to the present to look at without making a ceremony of waiting.

* * *

He did not tell her about the unfinished sentence in the grey room.

*When you are grown enough—*

He could not finish it for himself without choosing the answer he feared most and pretending that made it knowledge.

Ciel asked whether he wanted to end the session.

He looked toward Yuka. She was not preparing the next attempt. She was waiting to know whether there would be one.

*Not yet. Check the work first. If something is wrong, we stop. If I'm frightened of something I remember, I want to know the difference.*

She checked.

No new pattern moving without his direction. No other will crossing the route they had opened. The work had paused where he asked it to pause.

That was not an answer to everything.

It was an answer to this moment.

* * *

The next attempt was shorter.

Yuka waited until she could feel the beginning as her own. Mau kept his point separate and opened only when she said she was ready.

One block moved left.

The other came straight back.

Both stopped when their owners released them.

Nobody spoke immediately.

Then Yuka began smiling.

Mau looked at the two lines on the ground, clear enough that neither needed someone to explain which one belonged to whom.

Gojo nodded once.

"There." ᴳ

* * *

The cost had not split itself obligingly between them.

Mau could feel both demands coming home. Small demands today, and shorter than they wanted them eventually to be. Ciel kept that comparison beside the successful result instead of allowing success to erase it.

Yuka asked him before he had to decide how to make the answer sound kind.

"Both?" ʸᵏ

"Both." ᴹ

She looked at the tracks again.

"Then we make them cheaper." ʸᵏ

Gojo stood up with the expression of a man who had finally heard somebody else ask for the lesson he intended to give.

"Now you're being ambitious." ᴳ

* * *

They put the blocks away themselves.

Yuka took the one which had moved left. Mau took the other, and neither of them made the little accomplishment into a promise to be finished before they ate.

At the door, she caught his sleeve.

"I like that one," she said. ʸᵏ

He looked back toward the field.

"The pattern?" ᴹ

"The beginning where I know what I'm doing and you tell me when you need to stop." ʸᵏ

He was quiet for a moment.

Then he leaned down and kissed her.

The two blocks waited in their ordinary places for the next day.
