*When: Day746. Yuka puts the rope down, picks it up again and asks Toad to move the loose end before the real call begins.*

The tongue caught it.

Brought it back.

Stopped before throwing it at her.

Mau, beyond the mark with Yuta, looked disproportionately proud of that part.

Yuka pointed at him.

"I practised." ʸᵏ

"I saw." ᴹ

She dismissed Toad, checked the rest of the floor and made the sign for the serpent.

* * *

The body filled the space before its head finished turning.

Yuka had expected length. She had not quite expected what length became when it had somewhere to coil.

She moved away from the first strike and lost the route she had meant to take next.

The black dog crossed behind the lifted head.

She called it clear before the turn could close a loop around it, then followed the space the body had left instead of the place she had wanted to stand.

The first rope cast missed.

She let it fall.

The serpent struck at the moving end.

Yuka remembered that.

* * *

The rabbits crossed low.

The head turned toward them. The body did not follow immediately; its middle remained braced against the stone, holding enough weight for the next strike.

Yuka stopped trying to bring the head down by making the whole creature go where she wanted.

She made Toad reach for the rope.

Slow enough to catch.

Fast enough to take the end across the place she needed before the head could return.

The line lifted.

The serpent struck through the moving loop.

Yuka pulled from the side.

* * *

It was not enough.

The weight came toward her, and the floor ceased to offer anything useful beneath the sole she had planted too firmly.

She gave it the step.

Then another, along the turn rather than back against it.

The dog's next movement changed where the head could put its weight without losing the line it had taken. Toad held the far end low. Yuka let the serpent spend the force of its own correction before bringing her shoulder behind the rope.

Her arms shook.

She could feel the calls beginning to fight for more of her attention than the plan had allowed.

She dismissed the rabbits.

Kept the loop.

The great head came lower.

Yuka stayed where she had fought to put herself until the shadow beneath the body finally began to answer her instead of the struggle.

* * *

When it was gone, she looked down at the rope.

The line had left a mark across the gloves. Not through them.

She opened and closed her hands before looking toward Mau and Yuta.

"I kept that one," she said. ʸᵏ

Yuta came in with the water.

Mau brought the bag she had asked Toad to move earlier, and put it beside her as if its successful arrival had been as important as everything afterward.

She began laughing.

"Don't make it sentimental." ʸᵏ

"It's a very good bag." ᴹ

She leaned against him while she drank.

* * *

The later call needed room too.

Yuka let the serpent rise and turn before dismissing it again. Six families now, and each had a different reason for her not to become careless about the place she stood when she asked it to come.

She rested afterward.

Mau sat beside her with his hands empty and no part of the victory which required pretending he had secretly done it for her.

She took one of those hands.

"You look better today," she said. ʸᵏ

He watched the place where the serpent had vanished.

"Today is better." ᴹ

She accepted the difference.

They went home together.
