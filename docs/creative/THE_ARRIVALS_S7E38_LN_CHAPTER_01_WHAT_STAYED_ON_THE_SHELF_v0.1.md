*When: Day757. The first crystal buyer asks for everything on the shelf, and Jinshi takes the smaller page out instead.*

"This is what we can sell today," he said. ᴶ

The buyer read it twice.

Hollis waited beside him, having already explained that being introduced did not turn the receiving table into a route through the rest of the house.

Beyond the covered space, two soldiers carried a crate toward the workshop. Neither stopped to stare at the visitors.

The buyer watched them go.

"You have more than this." ᵇ

"We use them too," Jinshi said. ᴶ

* * *

Mau had helped count the lots before the visit.

Three entries after the first brigade trial. Day722, fifty-two new ordinary shadows. Day737, sixty-one. Day754, sixty-four. Igris and Beru had led the rooms while Mau remained with the route and Anko or Mikasa kept the human return from becoming an assumption.

The work had become familiar enough to repeat.

It had not become familiar enough to stop recording who returned, what had needed rebuilding and which passage had made a cart a worse idea than several smaller loads.

One hundred and seventy-seven new units.

Three hundred and sixty-four ordinary now. Sixteen named.

The Window had reached one hundred and fifteen, with one hundred and two points still held. Umaru had checked those entries herself and written *not spent* beneath the place Mau kept forgetting that a successful week did not oblige him to choose the rest of his body in a hurry.

He had liked watching the soldiers bring the last load home.

He had also felt the old cold when the number finished growing.

Both facts had survived being written beside each other.

* * *

The crystals needed a different count.

One hundred and seventy-four small pieces from the three operations, with the three larger rewards kept apart. Added to the old stock, enough to make someone standing on the visitor's side imagine the shelf could never empty.

Senku chose one small piece for the demonstration.

Not one already exhausted and polished to look convincing.

The buyer watched the familiar insert take the charge, the light hold and the comparison mark go down beside it. He asked how they knew it would not fade as quickly as the cheaper pieces he had bought elsewhere.

"That's why you watched this one," Senku said. "And why the batch stays identified." ˢᵉⁿ

Jinshi put the spent sample beside the test note, out of the stock being offered as usable fuel.

The buyer read the smaller page again.

* * *

He wanted to bargain on quantity.

Hollis let him.

Then he reminded him of the road, the packaging and the people who had to make sure the pieces inside were what the mark on the wrapping said they were. A sack was not a price merely because somebody could lift it.

The agreed forty stayed forty.

The one larger crystal stayed separate, with the price and the purpose of the purchase clear before anybody packed it against the smaller ones.

Jinshi checked the money where both men could see him count it.

Only then did the lots leave the shelf.

* * *

Mau came through after the sale, carrying a thing which had nothing to do with it.

A broken fastening from one of the household carriers. Wakana had asked for the old part, not an explanation of how Mau could probably use a shadow to carry the whole load without it.

He stopped long enough to meet the buyer.

The man looked at his hair, then his hands, then seemed to remember which part of the morning he had actually come to discuss.

"You'll have more?" he asked. ᵇ

Mau glanced at Jinshi's page before answering for work other people had just finished arranging.

"We can talk about the next lot. They need to know how the first one worked." ᴹ

"The roads are getting difficult." ᵇ

"I know." ᴹ

The buyer looked toward the soldiers again.

"People will come asking." ᵇ

Mau put the broken fastening in his other hand.

"Then tell them where to ask. Not that you bought the right to promise our next month for us." ᴹ

It came out calmly enough that Hollis did not need to soften it for him.

* * *

After the cart left, Jinshi put the retained stock beside the sale in the record.

Two hundred and fifty small usable crystals. Four older pylon pieces. Five larger rewards of their different sources.

Still things to make. Still things to keep. Still a home which could now buy ordinary supplies without pretending a good sale had removed the need to grow food, repair tools or make room for the people already there.

Mau took the fastening to Wakana.

Then he went to find Yuka, who had asked him not to agree to anything which would move their next afternoon together without coming to ask her first.

He had not.
