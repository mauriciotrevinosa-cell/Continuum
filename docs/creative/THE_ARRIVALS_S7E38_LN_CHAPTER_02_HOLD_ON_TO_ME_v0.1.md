"Back before dinner?" ᴹᵃ

"Tell him too." ᴹᵃ

Mau put a hand against his chest.

"I'm standing here." ᴹ

"Then it should be easy." ᴹᵃ

Yuka took his hand before he could argue with the point she had already won.

* * *

They began over grass.

Not the interesting part of the route. The first part, where setting two people back down could be an answer rather than a rescue.

Nue took the shared carrying loops and rose.

For a moment Mau felt his feet leave the ground. Then the wingbeat changed, Yuka tightened before finishing the order and one of his boots returned to the grass.

She asked the bird to land.

It did.

Mau waited until she had both feet beneath her before saying anything.

"That was a very nice patch of ground." ᴹ

She looked at him.

He smiled.

She laughed despite herself and checked the loop again.

* * *

The next lift worked.

Yuka could feel the extra weight in every part she had been doing thoughtlessly alone. The turn began earlier. The order to slow had to arrive before the ground seemed close enough to need it.

Mau remained behind her in the second loop, doing exactly the amount of nothing she had asked him to do.

No invisible hand taking his weight away. No other wing beneath theirs.

Only his arms around her waist when she told him to hold on.

The little protected meadow moved beneath them.

Yuka smiled so widely that her next word almost became a laugh instead of an order.

She finished the order properly.

* * *

They landed at the low rise she had chosen.

Nothing like the mountain he had carried her to. Nothing she needed to apologize for making smaller.

She dismissed Nue and sat down before excitement made her decide the return would cost whatever was left afterward.

Mau sat beside her.

For a while she looked at the patch of sky they had crossed rather than at him.

"You didn't help," she said. ʸᵏ

"You asked me not to." ᴹ

"I know. I could feel that it was all mine." ʸᵏ

He watched the pleasure move across her face.

Then she turned to catch him looking.

"What?" ʸᵏ

"Nothing. I like being taken somewhere." ᴹ

* * *

She had brought something to eat.

Not a feast. A little of the food she knew he would choose if he had not spent breakfast announcing that he was easy to feed, and something sweet she had kept away from Milim by putting it in a very uninteresting wrapping.

Mau looked at the wrapping.

"You planned that part." ᴹ

"She doesn't inspect everything that looks useful." ʸᵏ

"Umaru might." ᴹ

Yuka paused.

Then she began laughing.

"Eat it before they improve the method." ʸᵏ

* * *

He lay back after they had finished, and she put her head against his shoulder where she could see the sky without needing to keep something else in it.

The grass was grey.

Her fingers against his shirt were not.

He did not need to turn that difference into a question about what was wrong with his body in order to notice how much he loved the particular hand resting there.

Yuka moved closer.

"Next time a little farther," she said. ʸᵏ

"When you're ready." ᴹ

She looked at him.

"I meant us. Not only the bird." ʸᵏ

He propped himself up enough to meet the kiss she was already bringing him.

For a while neither proposed a more useful thing to do with the afternoon.

* * *

They came back the same short way.

This time she told him to hold on before Nue began lifting. His hands settled at her waist, familiar now without becoming something she had stopped noticing.

The landing was not elegant.

It was where she asked for it.

At the house, Maki looked at both of them, then at the sky which had not kept either one.

"Before dinner," she said. ᴹᵃ

Yuka smiled.

"I told you." ʸᵏ

Mau carried the empty wrapping to the kitchen. Yuka went with him, pleased enough that everyone they passed could see who had planned the afternoon.
