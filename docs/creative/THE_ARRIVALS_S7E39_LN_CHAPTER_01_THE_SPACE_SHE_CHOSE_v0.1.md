She lowered her hands.

The cloth settled in the grass beyond Mau.

He picked it up.

"It didn't touch her," he offered. ᴹ

Gojo looked at him.

"I appreciate loyalty. I also appreciate the difference between stopping a thing and launching it at the person holding the other end." ᴳ

Yuka laughed, mostly at herself, and asked to try again.

* * *

Mau watched the second beginning instead of the cloth.

The constructed eyes gave him more than the shape of an effect. With Mini Ciel holding the earlier flow beside it, he could find the place where the second pattern spent energy moving something which the neutral operation should never have needed to push.

Gojo touched the edge of the diagram.

"Here. You're still asking for the answer after you've already paid for a different question." ᴳ

Mau looked at Yuka.

She had felt it too, through the way the pattern had begun beneath her hands.

Ciel held the comparison. Noise changed the part they had actually found, rather than rebuilding everything which had worked yesterday because today's correction was interesting.

They began smaller.

* * *

The cloth came toward her wrist.

Slowed.

Kept coming in a way which did not bring it the last part of the distance.

Yuka stared.

Mau held the free end without pulling harder to make the result more impressive.

"There?" she asked. ʸᵏ

"There," Gojo said. ᴳ

She smiled.

Then the effect loosened before she meant it to and the cloth brushed her skin.

She looked down.

Gojo did not take the answer away.

"Now keep the beginning while you stop thinking about whether it worked." ᴳ

* * *

They used something lighter for the next part.

Not a blade. Not a test of how much trust love could prove by making one of them stand still while the other took a foolish risk.

A strip of paper, folded enough to hold its shape in Mau's hand.

Yuka let it come close, kept it from finishing the journey and released the field before reaching for the water.

The work returned through the support inside Mau, steadier and markedly smaller than the first attempt. Not gone. Less wasted before it had even become the thing she wanted.

Ciel gave him that distinction without needing to put every part of it into the room for the others.

He told Yuka what had changed.

She looked at the two lines on the page.

"Then that's the one we practise." ʸᵏ

* * *

Gojo left them with the warning before he left them with the achievement.

Air still had to arrive. So did light. Selecting a paper strip in front of her did not mean she could stop paying attention and let a new pattern decide the rest of the world perfectly.

Yuka listened without looking disappointed that a real beginning had not become every ending at once.

When he went, she put the paper down and asked Mau to hold out his hand.

He did.

The field slowed the movement near her fingers.

She considered it for a moment.

Then changed the part which applied to him.

His hand met hers.

* * *

She laughed softly, more pleased by that than by the cloth which had refused to arrive.

Mau stayed where he was while she tried the choice again. A short beginning. A pause. The place left open for the contact she actually wanted.

It was not effortless yet.

It was hers to practise.

"Come here," she said after the last attempt. ʸᵏ

"Are you keeping it up?" ᴹ

"Not this time." ʸᵏ

He came close enough for her to put both arms around his neck.

The paper waited on the grass beside the two water cups, doing nothing at all to improve the kiss.
