*When: Day773. Maomao stops beneath the new lamp because she can finally see the thing she dropped.*

It was a small folded label.

Not valuable enough to justify searching the dark with a second light, and irritating enough that she would have remembered the loss while trying to do something else.

She picked it up.

The lamp held its light above her without smoking against the cover of the walkway.

Jinshi stood a little farther along the path, waiting with a spare cloth wrapped around the part Senku had told him would remain warm.

"You put it here," she said. ᴹᵃᵒ

"Several people put it here. I asked about the place." ᴶ

"Why this place?" ᴹᵃᵒ

He looked at the route to the washing area, then at the label in her hand.

"People kept stopping here to find the thing they were carrying." ᴶ

* * *

The insert used the method the workshop already knew.

A small crystal. A measured demand. A housing which kept rain from turning an improvement into a new reason to swear beside the path.

One piece put into the test, not a light which had begun running on the idea that the house could afford lights now. Jinshi had taken it out of the usable count when Senku committed it to the first night's work.

Maomao checked the distance between the warm part and the wood.

Jinshi waited.

"Good," she said at last. ᴹᵃᵒ

He smiled.

"The lamp or the distance?" ᴶ

"You leaving enough space for somebody to check." ᴹᵃᵒ

* * *

They sat beneath it while the others tried the path.

Rem passed with an empty tray. Coco and her mother stopped to look at the light, then continued toward the common room without being asked to admire the people who had arranged it.

One soldier carried a crate along the darker side of the work area and paused when somebody showed it the better route.

Jinshi watched the movement rather than looking back at the window for the next page waiting on the table.

Maomao noticed.

"Nothing to copy?" she asked. ᴹᵃᵒ

"There is." ᴶ

"You haven't gone to find it." ᴹᵃᵒ

"You haven't gone back inside either." ᴶ

She looked at him.

Then she folded the recovered label more carefully and put it away.

* * *

The light dipped near the end of the test.

Jinshi wrote the time down instead of pretending it had lasted as long as the next version ought to last. Maomao waited while he removed the spent insert and put it where it would not be counted as something which could be sold tomorrow.

They took a smaller ordinary lamp for the walk back.

At the door, Jinshi reached to let her go first.

She stopped beside him.

"Same place for the next test," she said. ᴹᵃᵒ

"You think it's useful?" ᴶ

She glanced back at the bench.

"I found what I dropped." ᴹᵃᵒ

He went inside with her, leaving the next page on the table until they had both found something to eat.
