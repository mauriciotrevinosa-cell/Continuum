*When: Day794. Yuka checks the two loads before checking the marks on the ground.*

"The same?" she asked. ʸᵏ

Wakana showed her the way they had packed them.

Same material. Same wrapping. The same awkward handle she would not have chosen if carrying them by hand had been the point.

Mau waited while she finished looking.

Gojo looked pleased with her for asking the question before making the effect.

* * *

They began separately.

Yuka's pull moved the first load along the line they had marked. Mau's moved the other with the same setting and the same distance left to finish before release.

Ciel kept both beside the earlier attempts.

The lost work had become visible to him in a different way over the weeks: not a grand reserve waiting to be made larger, but all the little places where energy went somewhere which did not improve the thing he was asking it to do.

Gojo had made him find those places instead of merely admiring how clearly he could see them.

Now the beginnings were quieter.

The loads still moved.

* * *

Together was the question which mattered.

Yuka found her point. Mau found his. Neither borrowed the other's place to stand, and neither effect took half the first effect's room because a second hand had joined it.

Both loads rose.

Both followed their separate marks.

The work came back to Mau as two demands, cleaner than the first one had once been by itself.

He kept it steady while Yuka changed her direction without making his change with it.

Then they let the loads down.

Wakana checked the two resting places.

Gojo looked at Mau.

"Again at the same setting." ᴳ

Yuka began smiling before they did.

* * *

They repeated enough to make the first good result a result rather than a fortunate story about the morning.

The supply did not become two supplies. Ciel did not tell them it had stopped costing anything. She told Mau where the work was going and where it no longer had to go before the useful part could begin.

He repeated the answer for Yuka.

"Both still come back here," he said, touching his chest. "But they don't get smaller because we're doing them together." ᴹ

She nodded.

"And we haven't made the bill disappear." ʸᵏ

"No." ᴹ

"Good. I wanted that answer too." ʸᵏ

* * *

The neutral fields came afterward.

One around Mau. One around her. Two strips of paper held by people standing at different angles so neither could pretend the successful center had merely been wide enough to include them both.

The paper slowed near Yuka.

The other slowed near Mau.

She let her strip through while he kept his where it was.

Then he let his through too.

Gojo gathered the paper and gave them a moment before finding the next correction.

Mau looked at her across the small ordinary distance between them.

For once, it remained an ordinary distance instead of becoming the length of the place he might one day fail to reach in time.

* * *

They stopped for the day with the full comparison written down.

No point field opened to purchase the result after the fact. No new river of energy invented to make the old precision unimportant.

What existed twice could work twice. The person carrying it still needed to sleep, eat and tell the other person when something changed.

Yuka folded her copy beside his.

"Better than half," she said. ʸᵏ

Gojo smiled.

"Much better." ᴳ

Mau watched them agree, pleased enough that the old words stayed somewhere behind the voices in front of him.
