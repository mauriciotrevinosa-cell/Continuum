It was not meant to be a perfect map.

She had tried that first, then realized she would spend the whole time making the distances correct and leave herself nowhere to put the things he had actually been there to remember with her.

So the coast was too near the house. The little rise Nue had carried them to was much too large. Ilen's pots had room for a note beside them, although she had never seen a farm on a proper map marked by a man failing to leave a pot on a table.

Mai looked at the notes.

"Keep those." ᴹᵃⁱ

"The bad drawings?" ʸᵏ

"The parts you wrote before trying to sound like a person who makes maps." ᴹᵃⁱ

* * *

Frieren paused at the coast.

Yuka had put a small castle beside it, made of a few uncertain lines and a door which had been argued about more than the drawing could explain.

"Sand?" Frieren asked. ᶠʳ

Yuka nodded.

"He remembered another version. I was worried there wouldn't be anything left which was only happening to me." ʸᵏ

Fern looked up from the edge she had been helping keep straight.

"Was there?" ᶠᵉ

Yuka touched the little note beside the castle.

"Lots." ʸᵏ

Frieren looked at the note for a moment, then gave the page back without supplying a better thing for Yuka to have felt.

* * *

Shoko asked which parts Mau would be able to read easily.

Not because the old grey had returned yesterday. Because a present made to be looked at should remember the person who would look at it.

Yuka showed her the dark outlines, the little marks she had put beside the paths instead of relying on a change of color to tell him which one to follow.

Seiko suggested turning the whole thing around before deciding where it would fold.

Turbo, without looking particularly interested, pointed out that the last note would otherwise vanish inside the crease.

Mai moved the fold.

Yuka watched the paper become something she could actually give him.

* * *

"A year," Maki said quietly. ᴹᵃ

Yuka looked at her.

"Not yet. A few more days." ʸᵏ

"I know." ᴹᵃ

For a moment neither added anything.

Then Mai handed Yuka the pencil and told her to finish the mountain before anyone improved it until she could no longer claim it was hers.

Yuka bent over the page.

Beyond the room, somebody called Mau's name and received an answer from somewhere much nearer than they had expected.

She smiled without going to find him.

There would be time to show him what she had made.
