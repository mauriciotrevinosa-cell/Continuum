*When: Day802. Yuka reads the first page before trying the last test.*

She had asked to write some of it herself.

Not because Mau had made the words too difficult. Because she wanted the thing which would remain with her to include the parts she had needed to learn rather than only the parts he had known before they began.

There were marks beside the first two pulls. The paper. The place where she had stopped trying to make neutral space behave like a push. The warning about a prepared reversal not being a promise that her own body could heal somebody.

Gojo had corrected one drawing.

Yuta had made the stop instructions shorter.

Mau looked at the page in her hands and recognized the work they had actually done.

* * *

They began with Blue together.

Then the separate neutral fields, one allowed contact and the other not. Then a small Red from her side while his own operation remained his, both demands coming home without one becoming a half-hearted imitation of the other.

No new discovery was required to rescue the result.

They had already found the faults, changed them and repeated the work on days when a good beginning had not promised a good ending.

Today it held.

Ciel checked the end as carefully as the beginning.

Noise kept the two constructed patterns distinct when both went quiet.

* * *

Yuka sat beside the marker and read the next part.

The full pattern. The things it could support. The things having it did not mean she had already learned to place safely.

She looked up at Mau.

"Purple is not my birthday demonstration." ʸᵏ

"Please no." ᴹ

Gojo looked between them.

"I feel I haven't been trusted enough in these arrangements." ᴳ

Yuka pointed to the empty target line.

"You were allowed a chair." ʸᵏ

He conceded that with a small movement of the head.

* * *

They kept the other limits plain.

Her own domain remained her own work, not an extra room smuggled into the gift because the technique had one in someone else's life. His body remained the source sustaining both patterns. Sleep was something they had tested. Serious failure of that source was not something any of them had promised to turn into independence by smiling at it.

The stop signals existed to tell them what was happening, not to make her ask before defending herself.

Mau let her finish reading before asking whether she still wanted the permanent route made hers.

Yuka looked at the page, then at him.

"Yes. I wanted to know what the answer meant." ʸᵏ

* * *

The last part waited for the day they had chosen.

Not more hidden construction which would be performed while she slept. The settled pattern was ready. Anchoring the new access as hers belonged to the same person who would wake with it afterward.

The older loan remained where it had always been. Nothing she already had was put away to make a ceremony necessary.

Yuka kept the pages while Gojo took the markers back.

Mau followed her toward the house.

"You aren't disappointed that you already know what it is?" he asked. ᴹ

She looked at him as if the question had taken an unnecessarily long route to reach something obvious.

"I helped make it." ʸᵏ

He smiled.

She took his hand.

"I like knowing what I'm being given." ʸᵏ

* * *

At the door, Ciel spoke quietly to him.

*This part is ready.*

He felt the satisfaction before the older words came to find it.

*Grown enough.*

The sentence in his memory still had no ending.

He did not borrow the worst one and call it a prediction.

Yuka drew him into the common room, where someone had kept a place beside the cup she liked.

He went.
