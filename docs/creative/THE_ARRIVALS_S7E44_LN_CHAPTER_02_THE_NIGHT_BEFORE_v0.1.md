* * *

The meal for later was not another feast hidden from the people preparing the birthday.

Two portions from the things they had already agreed to make. A little of the stronger seasoning beside Yuka's, not mixed through the whole dish because Mau had decided romance required pretending he enjoyed every spice she could eat comfortably.

He had asked Momo before asking Rem to save it.

Momo had said yes and then told him which part would not improve by being kept warm for three hours.

He wrote that down.

Rem noticed.

"You don't need a domain to remember it," she said. ᴿᵉ

He laughed and checked the time again anyway.

* * *

Yuta was helping with the tables when Mau came outside.

Not an official gathering. Some could stay where they were; two needed moving if the weather did what the clouds were threatening. The smaller one was for the cake, which Ram had successfully kept out of every military plan submitted by the Fort.

Mau took one end.

Yuta took the other.

They carried it without making a test of who could have done it alone.

"Nervous?" Yuta asked. ʸᵗ

Mau looked at the table after they put it down.

"About which part?" ᴹ

Yuta considered him.

"The part you want to go well." ʸᵗ

Mau smiled a little.

"Yes." ᴹ

Yuta checked the leg which did not quite meet the floor and moved a small piece of wood beneath it.

"Start by keeping her cake level." ʸᵗ

Mau helped him check.

* * *

Mai brought the finished binding before dinner.

Only the binding. The pages belonged to the people who had made the notes and asked to keep them together. No hidden construction waiting to surprise somebody inside their own body.

Mau ran a thumb along the edge.

On the front he had written *Two Beginnings*.

Mai read it and looked toward the other side of the room, where Yuka was hastily moving a folded packet beneath her arm.

"Neither of you is subtle," she said. ᴹᵃⁱ

Mau looked at Yuka.

Yuka looked at the packet, then back at him.

"Tomorrow." ʸᵏ

He held up the book.

"Tomorrow." ᴹ

Mai took her spare thread away before either asked her to pretend she had not seen both expressions.

* * *

They had a few minutes after dinner.

Yuka sat beside Mau with the packet kept elsewhere, and he left his book wrapped instead of turning the wait into a lesson about what she would receive.

"A whole year," she said. ʸᵏ

"Nearly." ᴹ

"I'm allowed to practise saying it." ʸᵏ

He smiled.

She took his hand and turned it the way she had on the day from which they counted. Her own hand was the same shape and different now, more certain about where she wanted it to rest.

"Do you remember being frightened of this part?" she asked. ʸᵏ

"Holding your hand?" ᴹ

She nodded.

He looked down at them.

"I remember not wanting to get it wrong." ᴹ

Yuka leaned against him.

"You can stop rehearsing it now." ʸᵏ

* * *

Later, they went to their room together. Yuka left her packet wrapped and climbed into bed without asking to see his gift early.

The book waited on the chair, well away from the place he put his shoes.

Mau looked at it after the lamp went out.

He had made something which did not require the person receiving it to become a purpose he had chosen for her. She could use it. Learn it. Decide how much of her life it should occupy.

He wanted to believe that having a thing made inside him could leave him the same freedom.

The remembered sentence waited without an ending.

He did not finish it tonight.

*Rest.*

Noise's familiar, halting word came quietly through the work which had finally stopped asking for his attention.

Mau let his hands open on the blanket. Beside him, Yuka shifted in her sleep and settled against his arm.

Tomorrow was hers.

He meant to be there for it.
