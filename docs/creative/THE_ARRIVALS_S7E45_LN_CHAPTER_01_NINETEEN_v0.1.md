*When: Day808. Yuka wakes before anyone comes to tell her that she is nineteen.*

Mau had got up before her. For a while she stayed beneath the blanket on her side of their bed and listened.

Footsteps beyond the door. A voice being told to keep another voice down. Something light striking the wall and a whispered accusation which Milim delivered loudly enough to defeat the whole arrangement.

Yuka began smiling before the knock came.

"I'm awake," she called. ʸᵏ

There was a pause.

"We know," Ori answered. ᴼ

* * *

The first card had enough room for every mark Ori had wanted to put on it and very little room left for the words.

Yuka found them anyway.

Nineteen, written with great concentration. A bird whose wings occupied most of the page. A ribbon leading to a small floating shape Milim insisted was an excellent likeness because it looked very powerful.

Yuka sat on the edge of the bed and let them explain it.

Then she hugged both of them, which made the rest of the morning's plan considerably harder to keep secret.

* * *

Mau waited outside with no attempt to become the first person who had reached her door.

She found him while Milim was telling somebody the birthday girl had already seen the most important present.

He looked at her for a moment without speaking.

Yuka put her hands on her hips.

"You can say it." ʸᵏ

"I'm making sure you're ready to hear such advanced information." ᴹ

She came close enough to spoil the expression he was trying to hold.

"Happy birthday," he said. ᴹ

She kissed him.

Someone behind them made a small, deeply disapproving noise. When she looked, Tsurugi was attempting to eat the piece of breakfast he had been told not to take yet.

Maki took the rest out of his reach.

"Wait until she sits down." ᴹᵃ

He looked at Yuka.

She took the chair with as much dignity as a woman could maintain while her brother was accusing her of becoming slow with age.

* * *

Yuta's hand rested on her shoulder for a moment before he gave her the small wrapped notebook he and Maki had chosen.

"For the things you want to keep," he said. ʸᵗ

Yuka opened it carefully.

Empty pages. Not a plan somebody else had already written for what nineteen should make her do.

Mai leaned over to check that the binding had survived Tsurugi's contribution to wrapping it, then gave her the little pouch Marin had helped finish.

It had room for the things she kept losing at the bottom of the practice bag.

Yuka turned it over and found the small reinforcement where the rope would rub.

She looked up.

Mai smiled.

"You can use it. It isn't only for looking at." ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka hugged her before returning to the notebook.

Mau watched from the next chair with his own wrapped book still waiting beside his plate.

* * *

People found their own time to come near her.

Not a line of sixty speeches. Fern stopped to ask about the evening table and stayed long enough for the answer to become a conversation. Frieren let Ori show the card again before telling Yuka which part she had helped hold still while the ink dried.

Rimuru claimed he had heroically kept Gojo from making an announcement about the birthday woman's new defensive capabilities before breakfast.

Gojo said the house deserved accurate information.

Shoko passed Yuka the tea before explaining that accurate information could survive waiting until someone had swallowed.

Yuka laughed so hard that she put the cup down again.

* * *

The book came after breakfast.

Mau gave it to her without opening it for her.

She took off the wrapping and ran her fingers over the title.

*Two Beginnings.*

Inside were the pages she knew. Her marks, his corrections, the small parts Gojo had refused to let them misunderstand and Yuta had refused to let them make needlessly complicated.

The last page had been left for her.

She looked up at Mau.

"Now?" he asked. ᴹ

She nodded.

* * *

They went to the quiet edge of the practice ground.

Not to perform for the birthday guests. To finish the thing they had chosen together in a place where the space around it would remain ordinary if someone needed another moment.

Yuka held out her hand.

Ciel checked the scope one last time. Noise kept the two completed patterns distinct. Mau felt the familiar route open, then the new one settle into the place they had prepared for it without taking the first one's strength away.

He did not enter anything else.

Yuka felt the change arrive where she knew to look for it.

The draft no longer waited for the next supervised beginning to be made available again. The route was hers to start from, with the source and the responsibility they had already named remaining real beneath it.

Ciel's answer came quietly to Mau.

*It has stayed where she chose.*

He looked at Yuka.

She was smiling before he could repeat it.

* * *

She chose Blue first.

A small pull brought the book from the place he had set it to the hand she held ready. She released it before putting the fingers of her other hand between them.

The neutral operation formed at her own center.

Mau moved his hand toward hers.

The distance refused to finish.

She let him through.

Their fingers met.

For a moment she looked at the contact rather than the thing she had just made possible.

"No asking you each time," she said. ʸᵏ

"No asking me each time." ᴹ

"Both still come back to you." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"So we keep telling each other what we're doing." ʸᵏ

"Yes." ᴹ

She closed the effect herself and put her arms around him.

There was no field between them when she kissed him.

* * *

The cake waited until enough people had reached the tables without having to abandon what they were carrying.

Ram had kept it level. Rem had kept the right things beside it. Momo sat where she could see Yuka's face without needing to direct the room from the place she ate.

The band had chosen a small piece to play.

Bocchi began it after Nijika's count, and Yuka stayed beside Mau while the sound passed under the cover and out into the yard.

For once, the house looked large enough for all the reasons people had to be there without needing anyone to count those reasons aloud.

* * *

Before the cake was cut, Yuka looked round the table.

Her grandparents. Her brother. Mai, already reaching to stop a small elbow meeting the plate. The people she had learned to sit beside without first asking whether the chair was being kept for someone else.

There had been a time when a year had sounded like somebody else's future.

This one had reached her.

Mau's hand rested beside hers beneath the edge of the table.

She took it.

"What?" he asked quietly. ᴹ

She shook her head, smiling before the answer had found words.

Then Milim asked whether the birthday person was allowed a second piece before everyone else had a first, and Yuka laughed with the whole room instead of trying to save the feeling from the interruption.

* * *

Later, when the first plates were empty and people had begun moving into smaller conversations, Mau leaned close.

"I have one more thing. No technique." ᴹ

Yuka looked at him.

"Good," she said. "So do I." ʸᵏ

He smiled, and she went with him to help finish clearing before they kept the evening for the other reason they had both remembered.
