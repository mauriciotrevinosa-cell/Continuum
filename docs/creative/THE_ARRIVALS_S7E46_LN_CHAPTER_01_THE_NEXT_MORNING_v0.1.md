# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 40
## Chapter 1 — The Next Morning

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-26)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S7E46
**Continuity state:** Day809 morning–afternoon, after the nineteenth birthday and anniversary. Separate bedrooms and Rimuru's loft retained. Our Year belongs to Mau; Two Beginnings belongs to Yuka. Grey perception except Yuka persists. Permanent Limitless gift remains hers, source Mau, no permission per activation; no new mastery or allocations. Ordinary shared day, not immediate approach to Mau's birthday.

*When: Day809, morning. Yuka finds the anniversary map beneath a book Mau has been using to keep one corner flat.*

"You're flattening my mountains." ʸᵏ

Mau stopped trying to fold his sleeve over the stiff fingers of his left hand.

She stood in the open doorway with *Two Beginnings* against her hip. Behind her, someone was objecting to Milim's understanding of the word *leftovers*.

"Only the corner," he said. ᴹ

"That mountain took two attempts." ʸᵏ

"Then it should survive a book." ᴹ

Yuka came in to inspect the damage. He let her, although the corner had been entirely blank before he put anything on it. She lifted the book, pressed down the paper with her palm and looked pleased by the discovery that her work was still there.

The map had made the journey from the table to his room safely. He had checked it before sleeping. Once after waking, too.

She noticed where it lay, beside the things he reached for from his chair.

"You really did put it somewhere you use." ʸᵏ

"You gave me instructions." ᴹ

"Since when has that worked?" ʸᵏ

He finished his sleeve. She caught the cuff before he could push it back and folded the troublesome part once, leaving his hand free.

"There." ʸᵏ

He turned his wrist. Better.

From above them came the sound of movement in the loft, followed by Rimuru's voice.

"If breakfast is already happening, I was awake before you." ᴿ

Yuka looked up.

"We haven't gone down." ʸᵏ

"Then I was also awake before you." ᴿ

Mau took the map out from under the book and put it safely beside the chair. Yuka was still smiling when he reached for her hand.

* * *

There was cake left.

There was also breakfast, which Rem insisted was a separate category. Milim examined the distinction with the serious attention she usually gave rules which stood between her and a desired result.

Yuka took a piece of ordinary bread before anyone could decide birthday privileges lasted until the following breakfast. Mau sat beside her. Across the table, Tsurugi looked at the book she had brought down.

"More practice?" ᵀˢ

"Later." ʸᵏ

He reached for the bread. Maki moved the basket toward him before the movement could become a contest across three plates.

"What are you doing now?" he asked. ᵀˢ

Yuka looked at Mau.

They had not arranged anything for now. Yesterday had required plans, food, a quiet place and people kindly pretending not to watch him carry plates. This morning he had promised Kaneki help with a carrier that needed moving. Yuka had said something about finding where the practice rope had ended up.

"I don't know yet," she said. ʸᵏ

Tsurugi appeared disappointed by the lack of useful information and took another piece of bread.

Yuka leaned close enough for only Mau to hear.

"Apparently we need a better answer." ʸᵏ

"We could become mysterious." ᴹ

"You'd tell someone before lunch." ʸᵏ

He considered protesting. She gave him the look which said she was waiting for him to remember what he had done the last time he tried keeping a gift secret.

He ate his breakfast.

* * *

Kaneki had already cleared the route when Mau arrived.

The carrier was awkward rather than heavy for him. Its old fastening had been repaired; the handles still wanted two people unless one person intended to tip everything inside toward the other end.

Mau took the side Kaneki indicated. They lifted together and moved it slowly through the narrow part of the passage.

"I could have done the carrying," Mau said after they set it down. ᴹ

Kaneki checked that nothing had shifted inside.

"I know." ᴷ

"That sounded like you expected me to say it." ᴹ

"You usually do." ᴷ

Mau looked at the handles, then at the clean passage behind them. From the other side of the room came Ichika's voice calling for her father.

Kaneki straightened.

"Will you finish here?" ᴷ

"Go." ᴹ

He watched Kaneki leave before arranging the empty straps. One had slipped beneath the carrier. He could lift the whole thing, or he could crouch and pull the strap out.

He crouched.

By the time he finished, Yuka was outside with the rope over her shoulder and a cup for him.

"Your mysterious afternoon has become available," she said. ʸᵏ

* * *

They walked as far as the tree.

Someone had moved a chair into the shade, leaving the roots clear. Yuka sat down first, put the rope beside it and offered him the cup. He drank while she opened the book on her knees.

"I thought we'd try the next part tomorrow," she said. ʸᵏ

He looked at the page she meant. Her own note crossed one of his earlier arrows.

"The fold?" ᴹ

"The part where I keep trying to pull and turn at once, and Yuta makes that face." ʸᵏ

Mau knew the face.

"He'll come if you ask him." ᴹ

"I already did. After breakfast." ʸᵏ

"Then why are you asking me?" ᴹ

She closed the book on one finger.

"I'm asking if you're free. It's different." ʸᵏ

He had the sudden urge to kiss her, which was inconvenient while holding a cup. He put it down carefully.

She waited, watching him.

"I'm free," he said. ᴹ

"Good." ʸᵏ

Then she kissed him before he could make the answer longer.

The chair creaked as he leaned down. When they moved apart, she shifted sideways to leave room beside her and discovered there was not nearly as much room as she had intended.

Mau sat on the ground instead. She looked offended on his behalf.

"I was offering." ʸᵏ

"I noticed." ᴹ

She rested her hand in his hair. He leaned back against her knees, and the book opened above him with the soft sound of a page finding its place.

For a while they discussed the practice. Then she began telling him about a sentence Mai had insisted she remove from the map because it would not fit beneath the drawing.

He asked what it had said.

Yuka laughed and refused to tell him.

He stayed there long enough to try asking twice more.
