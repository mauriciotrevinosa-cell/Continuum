# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 40
## Chapter 2 — Where You Put It

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-26)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S7E46
**Continuity state:** Day810 morning. Low-output practical coordination of Yuka's existing Sky loan and Blue, alternating her techniques; Mau uses a separate small Blue in final drill. No new simultaneous-CT mastery in one body, domain, Purple, or stat growth. Shared supply demand observed locally with Ciel; Yuta coaches without hearing Ciel directly.

*When: Day810. Yuta puts the cloth bag on the ground instead of throwing it.*

Yuka lowered her hand.

"I thought you were going to throw it." ʸᵏ

"I was. Show me the movement first." ʸᵗ

Mau sat on the low practice marker with his feet on the ground. Yuka had asked him not to stand behind the target this time. He had agreed before remembering that the previous arrangement had allowed her to blame him when she looked in the wrong direction.

She opened the borrowed surface with the small movement they knew. The patch of space before her seemed to gather between her fingers.

Yuta watched her shoulders.

"Where do you want it to go?" ʸᵗ

She indicated the padded basket beside the marker.

"Show me that part." ʸᵗ

She turned the fold.

The bag remained on the ground. Somehow that made it easier to see that the turn had finished behind her intended line.

Mau put a finger beside the basket without moving it.

"There?" he asked. ᴹ

She let the surface go.

"I know where the basket is." ʸᵏ

Yuta bent to pick up the bag.

"Then stop following your hand after you place it." ʸᵗ

* * *

The first throw went through the space she had been trying to gather.

Yuka's movement came late. She reached farther, caught part of the surface and turned sharply enough to send the bag past the basket and directly toward Mau's lap.

He caught it.

There was a short silence.

"Excellent," he said. "Very threatening." ᴹ

She held out her hand for it. He returned it by an ordinary throw, which she caught with considerably more force than necessary.

Yuta hid a smile badly.

"Again. Smaller movement." ʸᵗ

This time she did not try to repair the miss halfway through. She let the bag fall, checked where her feet were and began from the start.

On the fourth attempt, it struck the side of the basket.

On the sixth, it went in.

Yuka turned to Mau before she looked at Yuta. He raised both hands, empty.

"Didn't help." ᴹ

She tried to keep the smile down and failed.

* * *

The trouble began when they added the retrieval.

Yuka released Sky before making the small Blue. The bag moved, but her attention stayed on the place where the fold had been. It struck the rim on the way back out.

The next one came too fast.

Mau caught that one as well, because she had sensibly chosen a stopping point with clear space behind it and he had less sensibly moved his knee into the clear space.

"You're becoming part of the equipment," she said. ʸᵏ

He rubbed his knee.

"I'd like that recorded as a training injury." ᴹ

"No." ʸᵏ

Yuta took the bag from him.

"You're watching whether the first thing worked while you're starting the second. Finish checking, or decide beforehand where the next one begins." ʸᵗ

She looked at the basket. Then she marked a stopping point nearer the ground with her heel.

Mau watched her perform the sequence without the throw: fold, release, look, pull.

It was slower.

It also ended where she meant it to.

* * *

They stopped for water before trying anything together.

Yuka sat beside Mau and pressed her wrist against his for a moment.

"Anything wrong?" she asked. ʸᵏ

Ciel had already given him the part that belonged to his body. He repeated it without turning her answer into a voice the others could hear.

"The short pulls were fine. I noticed when you tried to catch the bad fold with the next pull." ᴹ

Yuka grimaced.

"So did the basket." ʸᵏ

Yuta drank from his cup. He waited while she drew the two movements beside one another in her notebook.

"I keep thinking I should make it quick enough that there's no gap," she said. ʸᵏ

"You can make it quicker when it ends in the right place," he said. ʸᵗ

Mau leaned close to the page.

"We could put something in the gap." ᴹ

Yuka looked at him.

He pointed to himself, then to the first arrow.

* * *

His Blue stayed small and well away from them.

Yuta threw the bag along the marked line. Yuka caught the surface of its approach and turned it. Mau began the pull only when she gave the word.

The bag reached the basket too high.

It tipped over the far side and rolled along the ground.

"I said it too late," she said. ʸᵏ

"I started too hard," Mau said. ᴹ

Yuta walked to fetch it.

"Good. You can both be wrong." ʸᵗ

On the next attempt, Yuka spoke earlier. Mau did less. The bag touched the padding, rose a little at one corner and settled.

She kept her hand up until his operation closed, then released her own.

Both demands had reached him. He had felt the second one join the first, and Ciel had confirmed what he already knew from the difference. Nothing about that made the bag less satisfying to look at.

Yuka went over to pick it up herself.

"Again?" she asked. ʸᵏ

Yuta looked at the light, then at the number of marks she had already made on the ground.

"Once. After that, write down the part you got right." ʸᵗ

She looked back at Mau.

He smiled.

"I'll try to stay out of the basket." ᴹ

"Please do. I'm finally learning where it is." ʸᵏ
