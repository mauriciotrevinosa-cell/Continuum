# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 40
## Chapter 3 — The Difficult Part

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-26)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 3
**Source episode:** S7E47
**Continuity state:** Day811 afternoon. Bocchi/Mau chosen-sibling bond maintained through her own musical work. Kessoku remain authors and performers; Mau is a listener, not arranger or power solution. No new instruments, residents, magic, resources or romance changes.

*When: Day811. Bocchi asks him for seven minutes and immediately wishes she had asked for six.*

Seven sounded contractual.

Six could have been an accident. A person might have six minutes available without considering whether she intended to occupy the rest of his day.

Mau glanced at the empty chair beside her.

"Here?" ᴹ

She nodded too quickly.

He sat.

The others had gone to bring back something to drink before they worked on the next part. Bocchi had meant to ask while all of them were there. Instead she had waited until the last possible instant, when Ryo had already left and Kita was calling that they would be back soon.

Now she had him and no one to explain why she wanted him.

She adjusted the guitar on her knee.

"You don't have to say it's good." ᴮ

"I haven't heard it." ᴹ

"After." ᴮ

"All right." ᴹ

He settled into the chair. She played the beginning before she could invent another condition.

The first part came easily. They had used it enough that her fingers could travel ahead of her nerves. The change was the new part: a small space between one phrase and the next which had sounded empty alone and crowded once everyone else joined.

She reached it and filled it.

Stopped.

Mau waited.

"That isn't how it goes," she said. ᴮ

"Then play how it goes." ᴹ

She looked at him.

He pointed at the place where her fingers had stopped.

"I wasn't going anywhere." ᴹ

She tried again.

This time she let the gap stay. Her fingers wanted to apologize into it. She made them wait until the next phrase began.

When she finished, Mau was still looking at her hands.

"Did you think I'd forgotten?" she asked. ᴮ

"The part where you waited?" ᴹ

She nodded.

"I thought something else was going to come in." ᴹ

She lifted her head.

That was exactly what was supposed to happen.

"It is. With the others." ᴮ

"Then I want to hear that one." ᴹ

Her face warmed so quickly that she looked down to keep him from watching the process.

"It's unfinished." ᴮ

"You can tell me when." ᴹ

The door opened before she could turn that into another complicated arrangement. Kita came in with the cups. Nijika followed, noticed Mau and asked which version Bocchi had played.

Bocchi tried to answer.

Mau got up to help with the cups, which gave her just enough time to stop being embarrassed by the fact that he had stayed.

"The one with the space," she said. ᴮ

Ryo appeared behind Nijika.

"Good. We were going to fight her if she filled it again." ᴿʸ

Bocchi stared at her.

Mau put the last cup down.

"Can I stay for the fight?" ᴹ

"Sit down," Bocchi said, and only realized afterward that she had said it without apologizing. ᴮ

He did.

When they played it together, she heard the place she had managed to leave open become somebody else's part of the song.

Mau tapped once against his knee when it arrived.

She saw it without losing her place.
