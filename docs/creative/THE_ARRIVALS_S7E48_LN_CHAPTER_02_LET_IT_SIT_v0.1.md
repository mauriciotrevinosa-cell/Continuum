# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 40
## Chapter 6 — Let It Sit

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 6
**Source episode:** S7E48
**Continuity state:** Day814 late morning. Kitchen rotation shown with Rem/Ram/Mau/Fern, Momo has leisure. Frieren and Ori visit; Frieren remains teacher/friend to Mau and Mum to Ori. Fern retains role but takes time with Frieren. Ordinary existing ingredients only, no new harvest/greenhouse technology, crystals, recipe powers or private family disclosures.

*When: Day814. Ram takes the knife from Mau and replaces it with a spoon.*

"You've been demoted." ᴿᵃ

He looked at the uneven pieces on the board.

"Some of those were deliberate." ᴹ

"That's worse." ᴿᵃ

Rem moved the usable pieces into the bowl before either of them could continue. Fern was checking the other pot, with the sleeves of her clothes held clear of the work.

Mau took his new position seriously for almost a minute.

Then he began stirring too often.

"Let it sit," Rem said. ᴿᵉ

He stopped.

Ram looked at his hand as it started toward the spoon again.

He put it behind his back.

* * *

Ori came to the doorway to ask whether there would be enough time before they ate for Frieren to finish reading something to her.

Frieren stood behind her holding a book with a finger between the pages.

"There would have been time already," Fern said. ᶠ

"We found another part," Frieren said. ᶠʳ

Ori nodded with the confidence of someone whose explanation had been supported by a qualified adult.

Mau looked at the pot.

"I keep being told not to touch it. That probably means you have time." ᴹ

Ram gave him the spoon.

"Now." ᴿᵃ

He stirred while Frieren checked the distance to the nearest safe chair. Ori took her hand and led her to it before she could decide that leaning against the doorway counted as sitting.

The book opened. Ori leaned against her side.

Fern glanced toward them once, then went back to her pot.

* * *

Momo passed the doorway with a cup and no tray.

Mau raised his spoon in greeting. Ram made him lower it before he decorated the floor.

"Smells good," Momo said. ᴹᵒ

Rem thanked her and did not ask her to taste it, fetch anything or look at the thing they had not yet finished. Momo lingered long enough to hear Ori correct Frieren about where they had stopped reading.

Then she went on.

Mau watched her leave.

"Does she know we can still call her if I ruin this?" he asked. ᴹ

Ram looked at Rem.

"He needs another demotion." ᴿᵃ

Rem smiled and put a lid beside his hand.

* * *

When the food was ready, Frieren accepted the little bowl Fern offered her.

Ori waited for a verdict with a seriousness which made Fern turn slightly away.

Frieren tasted it. Took another spoonful.

"That's good," she said. ᶠʳ

Fern checked whether Rem had heard. She had, but was occupied with something on the far side of the table and seemed willing to remain occupied for a moment longer.

"It needed more time than I thought," Fern said. ᶠ

"You gave it more time." ᶠʳ

Ori decided that settled the matter and asked for some.

Fern put a little into a bowl to cool. Mau began clearing the board, leaving the carrying to Ram until she informed him he had recovered that much responsibility.

At the doorway, Frieren closed the book on the ribbon she had been using to keep her place.

"We haven't finished," Ori said. ᴼ

"After we eat. Fern hasn't heard that part." ᶠʳ

Fern looked up.

"I have something to finish." ᶠ

Jinshi passed beyond the doorway carrying the request book they had agreed to keep there.

Fern watched him go, then looked at Rem.

Rem took the cloth from her hand.

"After we eat," she agreed. ᴿᵉ

Mau set the cleaned board upright and carried the bowls out before anyone could return his knife.
