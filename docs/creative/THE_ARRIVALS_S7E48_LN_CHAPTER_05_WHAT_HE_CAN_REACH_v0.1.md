*When: Day816. Umaru writes three headings and tells Mau he is not allowed to answer all of them with the same word.*

Yuta looked over the page.

"Which word?" ʸᵗ

"Fine." ᵁ

Mau sat beside Yuka on the low practice bench and decided not to ask whether the rule applied to everybody.

Gojo read the headings upside down.

"Amount. Output. Wear." ᴳ

"You may suggest a better third word after he answers it." ᵁ

Frieren sat where she could keep the page of her book out of the breeze. Rimuru had come because Ciel could give him the other side of the explanation without Mau needing to repeat every sentence before anyone could ask a question.

No one had brought a target to destroy.

That improved Mau's opinion of the morning considerably.

* * *

They began with cursed energy.

Not a contest to see who could make the others step back. Each let the others examine a controlled, familiar release, then closed it again. Gojo made Mau repeat the transition so a bright first impression would not do all the work.

Yuta watched him finish.

"More than mine," he said. ʸᵗ

Mau looked up.

"Now?" ᴹ

"The supply you're actually reaching now. I'm not counting whatever you haven't opened." ʸᵗ

Umaru turned to Gojo.

"And you?" ᵁ

"Yuta has more than I do," he said. "I waste considerably less than people find polite." ᴳ

Yuta smiled.

"That part is true." ʸᵗ

* * *

Gojo put two small operations through the same place in quick succession.

They were clean enough for Mau to see the distinction before he explained it. A large supply did not make the untidy part of Mau's own release smaller.

"You can own more and spend it badly," Gojo said. "Or spend very little to keep doing something which exhausts someone with a larger amount. Don't let her turn that page into a tournament result." ᴳ

"I wasn't going to," Umaru said. ᵁ

She crossed out the little arrow she had begun beside Gojo's name.

Mau wisely looked at a different part of the page.

His larger current reserve had helped with Mai. His body had helped too. Neither fact had taught her how to make every possible object or made him Gojo at the thing Gojo had just done.

Umaru left all three headings separate.

* * *

Mana was another comparison.

Frieren closed her book only after making sure the mark would remain where she wanted it. She let the concealment change enough for Mau to examine the presence she chose to show them, then watched him offer his own controlled release.

He could feel the second heart behind the part he was using.

The familiar ways through his body did not grow wider because he thought about how much lay beyond them.

Frieren looked at him for a long moment.

"More than I can hold here," she said. ᶠʳ

Mau let the release settle.

"And that's not all of it," she added. ᶠʳ

It was not a question.

He shook his head.

* * *

Ciel had been following the distinction since Umaru asked him yesterday.

What she could inspect did not end neatly at the amount Mau could draw into a familiar channel. Beyond it lay work he had not awakened, patterns still being finished and the great source which had arrived in him before he understood why anyone wanted to give it to him.

*The Black Heart's source is not the same thing as your usable reserve,* she told him. *The source is inexhaustible. Your access isn't. Your body's ability to carry the flow isn't.*

He sat very still.

Rimuru listened too.

"So, infinite mana?" Umaru asked after Mau had repeated the part which belonged to her question. ᵁ

"Behind what I can reach," he said. "Not an infinite amount I can throw through myself today." ᴹ

Rimuru put a finger beside the first heading.

"Don't draw a line from that straight into every other box. His cursed energy hasn't become inexhaustible. The reserve which pays for his soldiers is still doing its own work." ᴿ

Umaru stopped the pencil where it was.

Then began the next line elsewhere.

* * *

Mau could feel the walls while they talked.

Some held unfinished work away from parts of him which already needed to function. Some routes were narrower than the things waiting behind them. Noise kept building; his body kept learning what it could bear. None of those things had completed itself because a page now contained the word *inexhaustible*.

"So it isn't that I keep running out of everything I own," he said. ᴹ

Rimuru looked at him.

"Sometimes you're reaching the end of what you can use. Sometimes you're reaching the end of what your body should carry. It matters which one you're trying to solve." ᴿ

Yuka leaned closer to read the three headings.

"And sometimes you're tired," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau smiled.

"And sometimes I'm tired." ᴹ

* * *

Umaru wanted to put Rimuru on the page too.

He looked at the place she had left.

"Put what we've actually compared. I haven't emptied myself for this, and his heart doesn't make the things from my world identical to Frieren's magic." ᴿ

Frieren nodded once.

"Different work can have different prices." ᶠʳ

Umaru considered the blank space, then wrote a question rather than a winner.

People who arrived later would get their own questions. Mau did not possess a useful measurement of everybody who might someday walk into the house.

Gojo looked pleased by the absence of a neat final ranking.

"An unusually sensible scoreboard." ᴳ

"It isn't a scoreboard," Umaru said. ᵁ

"Excellent. I'm winning." ᴳ

Yuta laughed.

* * *

Mai found them as the page was being turned over.

She waited for Mau to look up.

"Another hook. The pantry. Same shape as yesterday." ᴹᵃⁱ

He began opening his mouth.

She held up her hand.

"You will tell me if it's too much. I know." ᴹᵃⁱ

Then she made it.

Mau felt the little expense cross a body which had spent the morning learning the difference between having a very large future and being able to use all of it at once.

His hand remained steady.

Mai looked at it anyway.

"Fine?" she asked. ᴹᵃⁱ

Umaru raised her pencil.

Mau looked from one to the other and started laughing.

"Warm for a moment. No pain. I can still carry the cups." ᴹ

Mai nodded, satisfied by an answer she could actually use.

She went back to her cupboard.
