*When: Day817. Gojo moves the second target after Mau has carefully measured the first.*

"That one," he said. ᴳ

Mau looked at the stone now lying nearer the edge of the marked ground.

"You could have put it there before I started." ᴹ

"And lose the chance to watch you complain?" ᴳ

The pull gathered small and controlled. Mau brought the first weighted pad to its mark, released and reached for the next position before the pad had quite settled.

The second came in crooked.

It dragged along the last part of its path and stopped short.

Gojo waited.

"I saw it," Mau said. ᴹ

"Wonderful. Do it again." ᴳ

* * *

Seeing it was easy.

With Ciel keeping the flood of detail within what he had chosen to examine, he could follow where his attention stayed after an effect ended. He could find the little excess at the start of the next pull. Knowing exactly where it happened had not made him stop doing it.

He tried to shorten the interval. The second placement improved and the release worsened.

He slowed down. Both landed correctly, far too late for Gojo's approving silence to mean anything except that he was preparing another objection.

"You can tell me," Mau said. ᴹ

"You know this one." ᴳ

He did.

The next attempt was better.

* * *

Gojo took his turn without making the effect larger to announce the difference.

First placement. Release. Second placement.

The two pads settled with almost the same quiet scrape. He repeated it, then once more while Mau changed the second position.

Mau watched the release instead of the pull.

There.

"You decided before the first one stopped." ᴹ

"You keep waiting for permission from the thing you just moved." ᴳ

Mau looked at the pads.

That sounded annoyingly similar to something Yuta had told Yuka. Gojo noticed the expression before he could decide whether to admit it.

"Someone else already said it?" ᴳ

"To Yuka." ᴹ

Gojo smiled.

"You should listen when people teach your girlfriend. Very educational." ᴳ

Mau picked up the pads by hand.

"I'm moving these somewhere less convenient." ᴹ

"Now you're participating." ᴳ

* * *

They changed the test instead of increasing its size.

One near placement, one farther away. One directly ahead, one beside him. A sequence in which Gojo named the second only after Mau began the first, so preparation could not become memorizing the same two positions.

He missed the third change. Got the fourth and fifth.

On the sixth, an old phrase arrived with the feeling of stone behind his shoulders.

*Now you will grow.*

The pad stopped early.

Mau let the operation close and put his hands on his knees. The field remained the field. Gojo's feet were where they had been a moment ago. Ciel waited for an answer to the quiet question she had asked inside him.

*A memory,* he told her.

His mouth was dry. He took the water rather than making them watch him pretend it was not.

Gojo sat on the low marker.

"Want to tell me which part?" ᴳ

Mau shook his head once.

"Not right now." ᴹ

"Then tell me whether we're stopping." ᴳ

He looked at the two pads. Neither needed to be moved for the world to continue.

"A break." ᴹ

Gojo leaned back on his hands.

"Convenient. I was about to become unbearably impressive." ᴳ

Mau took another drink before he could laugh without spilling it.

* * *

Shoko found them arguing about the word *recovered*.

Gojo had objected to a note which made three clean repetitions sound like a judgment on everything he had once been able to do. Mau had objected that he was the one who insisted on being called impressive.

She read the page over Mau's shoulder.

"You could write what he did." ˢʰ

"We're experimenting with that radical idea," Mau said. ᴹ

Gojo held out a hand for the page. She moved it out of reach just long enough to finish reading.

"He did it three times," she said. ˢʰ

"Beautifully," Gojo said. ᴳ

She gave it back.

"Then do it four." ˢʰ

Mau began laughing properly this time.

Shoko looked at his face, then at the cup in his hand. She did not ask him a medical question in front of Gojo. She did stay long enough to see him get up without hurrying and move the target to the place he chose.

* * *

The last two sequences were clean.

The one before them had not been. He left all three in the note, with the pause between them.

Gojo checked the marks, made one small correction to the description of the release and handed the page back. His finger tapped the place where Mau had begun watching the next target too late.

"That costs you before you spend anything bigger," he said. ᴳ

Mau folded the page.

"Yuka will enjoy hearing that." ᴹ

"Yuka has excellent taste in explanations." ᴳ

Shoko made a sound behind them.

Gojo turned.

"I said explanations." ᴳ

"I heard you." ˢʰ

He went after her to find out what that was supposed to mean. Mau collected the pads, smiling to himself, and followed when he had put the field back the way they had found it.
