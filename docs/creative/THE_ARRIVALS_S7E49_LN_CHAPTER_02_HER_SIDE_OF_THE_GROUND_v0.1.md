## Chapter 10 — Her Side of the Ground

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S7E49
**Continuity state:** Day819 morning and evening. Yuka refines existing Chimera Shadow Garden over bounded placement/recall with Divine Dogs and Toad, not a new tame or effortless full domain. No simultaneous Sky/Limitless/native DE, new duration claim, CE cross-pool, regen or Blueprint rewrite. Mau is agreed external safety support; native cost remains hers. Couple communicates pause/trauma without exact vessel disclosure.

*When: Day819. Yuka puts Mau on the other side of the line and then moves the line farther away.*

"Was I looking too helpful?" he asked. ᴹ

"You were looking at the place I wanted to fix." ʸᵏ

He stepped back with both hands raised. Yuta waited beside the place where the water and her notebook had been set down.

The last time Yuka had needed this much concentration inside the boundary, an ox had been gathering enough speed to make a straight line feel personal. Today the ground was empty.

That should have made it easier.

Instead she kept looking at the far edge, thinking about how far she ought to be able to reach.

She rubbed her hands together once and made the sign.

"Domain Expansion." ʸᵏ

The familiar darkness gathered under her.

* * *

She began too far out.

One of the Dogs rose where she wanted it. The other position pulled her attention toward the edge until she was thinking about holding the boundary instead of the call.

She recalled before the position finished slipping.

The ground came back.

Yuta did not move toward the notebook immediately. She appreciated that enough to remain standing for another breath before sitting down.

"Too much at the end," she said. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

Mau held out the water when she looked for it. He had not crossed the line.

She drank, then pointed at a nearer mark.

"There. I want to do that part again." ʸᵏ

"After you can call normally without forcing it," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

She looked at her hands and put them flat on her knees.

* * *

They waited.

Mau sat on the ground outside the working area rather than hovering over it. He had brought his own note from the practice with Gojo. When she asked what he had been doing, he showed her the two placements.

She read the correction.

Then looked at him with an expression he had apparently been expecting.

"I know," he said. ᴹ

"You should listen when people explain things to me." ʸᵏ

He covered his face with one hand.

Yuta turned away, which failed to hide the way his shoulders moved.

Yuka laughed until her breathing had become easy again. When she tested a simple call later, the Dog emerged without the resistance which had told her to stop.

She dismissed it before getting up.

* * *

The second boundary was smaller.

She had to keep herself from trying to enlarge it once it settled. There was enough room for the thing she had chosen to do. Toad rose at the first position. One Dog moved from the second toward the third.

Yuka placed the opening it would return through nearer to her rather than chasing it with the edge of the field.

It disappeared cleanly.

Toad followed.

She opened the boundary before she had to fight it to let it go.

The ordinary floor returned. Her knees wanted her to sit down, and this time she did not pretend that was an objection to the result.

Mau waited while Yuta asked her to point to the place she had actually used.

The smaller mark.

She touched it with the toe of her boot.

"That part was mine," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau smiled at her from beyond the line.

* * *

She did not attempt a third domain.

They put the markers away, and she told Yuta which part she wanted to practise next without a boundary at all. They could make the movement familiar before putting another demand beneath it.

At the doorway, he asked Mau whether he was coming to lunch.

"In a minute," Yuka answered for both of them, then glanced at Mau to check. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

Yuta went on without making the minute into an event.

She took Mau's hand and pulled him down beside her on the bench.

"You stopped the other day," she said. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"In practice. Shoko said you took a break. She didn't say what for." ʸᵏ

He looked down at their hands. She let him have the time it took to answer.

"I remembered something I didn't want there." ᴹ

"And now?" ʸᵏ

"Now I'm here." ᴹ

She pressed her thumb against the side of his.

"I know. I was asking whether you want company later." ʸᵏ

He leaned his forehead against hers.

"Yes." ᴹ

"Then tell me when. I have very important plans to sit down for the rest of the morning." ʸᵏ

He laughed softly and stayed beside her until sitting stopped feeling like a task she had to complete before being allowed to rest.

That evening she found him with the map open beside his chair. She brought her own book, set it down unopened and asked whether he still wanted the company.

He moved over.

She took the place he had made.
