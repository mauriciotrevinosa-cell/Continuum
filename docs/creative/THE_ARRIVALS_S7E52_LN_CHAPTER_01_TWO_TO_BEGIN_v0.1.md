*When: Day838. Hollis arrives with the page they sent him and enough notes in the margin to prove that the other end of the road has read it.*

Wakana held one corner while Jinshi smoothed the other.

The first buyer had accepted the proposed grip. The second wanted the hand guard slightly changed and had drawn a shape which looked convincing until Wakana tried placing his own fingers where the drawing expected them to go.

Marin leaned closer.

"He wants space here," she said, pointing above the line. "He drew the space instead of the thing around it." ᴹᵃʳ

Wakana looked again.

"That makes more sense." ᵂ

Hollis gave a small, satisfied nod.

"I thought you might prefer his drawing to mine." ᴴᵒ

Mau, who had come carrying the cleared household fastening, set it beside Wakana instead of on the customer's paper.

"Did you draw one?" ᴹ

"Briefly. He decided to protect his investment." ᴴᵒ

* * *

The prices had come back accepted for two first pieces, with payment when the buyers could take and test the tools under the terms already written.

There was no deposit to spend before they finished. There was also no promise that the six possible later pieces would be made at the same speed or from material nobody had put aside.

Wakana checked the household list. The carrier was done. One fastening still needed work. Marin had asked for a small change to the next grip before they cut it, and he wanted to make both test handles first.

Fern waited until he finished counting the work in his own words.

"Can we say yes to these two?" she asked. ᶠ

"These two," he said. ᵂ

Jinshi put the accepted quantity on a clean page. Mau checked the material already set aside with them; Senku had kept the worked stock separate from the recovered plates still waiting to prove what they could do.

The answer went back into Hollis's bag as an agreement to make something specific, not a sack of money they had already earned.

* * *

The road letter needed less drawing and more careful reading.

It named the three posts within the road-keeper's responsibility, the place where notice should be sent and the people who would pass the message along. Visitors travelling under that notice were to be expected, not stopped by someone who had never been told they were coming.

It did not say what would happen beyond the third post.

Anko found that absence immediately.

"He didn't promise the next man's road for him," she said. ᴬ

Hollis nodded.

"The next man would have objected." ᴴᵒ

Mau read the first line again. Names rather than a description of a dangerous group someone had been warned to watch for.

He could imagine Yuka's name there. Wakana's. A person going to speak about a tool and coming home annoyed by the rain.

He handed it to Rimuru before imagination could become an agreement for the whole house.

Rimuru read to the end.

"We use it once and see whether the people at the posts actually got it," he said. ᴿ

"With notice," Anko added. ᴬ

Jinshi copied the contact onto the page where the old visit arrangements already lived.

* * *

Hollis asked for water before leaving.

Mau brought it and found him looking at the receiving space as though deciding whether to begin another conversation would make the next stop too late.

"There are more openings," Hollis said when he took the cup. ᴴᵒ

"On your route?" ᴹ

"Two reports beyond it. One from someone who had seen the place. One from someone who had seen someone running from it." ᴴᵒ

Mau sat on the end of the bench.

"Do they need help now?" ᴹ

"The nearer report says a party went in. I asked who would know whether they came back. I'm waiting for the answer." ᴴᵒ

Hollis drank. Mau let him finish before asking which place the first report had come through. They put that beside the second, with the difference between them still visible.

At the doorway, Yuka waited with a book under her arm.

Hollis noticed her and stood.

"I'm keeping you from something." ᴴᵒ

"You were giving us news," Mau said. ᴹ

"Then put it somewhere you can find it after you eat." ᴴᵒ

Mau took the empty cup.

Yuka held the door while Hollis gathered his things. When he was gone, she showed Mau the page she had marked.

It concerned what she wanted to take on their next exploration.

He went to find a place where they could spread it out.
