## Chapter 14 — What Frieren Packed

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S7E52
**Continuity state:** Day844 afternoon, planning ordinary settlement dungeon outing for845. Party Frieren/Fern/Stark/Yuka/Mau; Rimuru explicitly home with other defenders. Existing equipment/food, no gate upgrade/key revision, new tome or full restored powers. Frieren leads by choice, no B romance/royal-service promise imported from A. Supplies and withdrawal signals discussed before entry.

*When: Day844. Fern removes the third book from Frieren's bag.*

Frieren watched her place it on the table.

"That one is small." ᶠʳ

"Then it will be easy to put back on the shelf." ᶠ

Stark made the mistake of smiling while holding a bundle Fern had already weighed. She handed it to him and took the next one without looking away from Frieren.

Mau arrived with Yuka and stopped at the sight of the books on one side of the table and the food on the other.

"Are we choosing?" he asked. ᴹ

"Some of us already have," Fern said. ᶠ

Frieren picked up the smallest book and put it beside her water.

Yuka looked at the space left in the bag.

"What if we find another one?" ʸᵏ

Frieren looked at Fern.

"She makes a good point." ᶠʳ

* * *

The next day's party had come together because Frieren wanted to see the new arrangement of the ordinary dungeon rather than hear about it after everyone had decided the most useful part was something to sell.

Fern had agreed to go. Stark had agreed shortly afterward and had spent the time since asking questions about the floor and the things likely to fall off it.

Yuka wanted room to practise the choices they had been working on. Mau wanted to go with them and had already spoken with Rimuru about who would remain home.

They were not taking the whole household's defense through the same door. They were also not pretending that the five of them needed a personal escort to walk to a place they already used.

Mau put the route sheet on the table.

"You wanted the front," he said to Frieren. ᴹ

She moved one of the books off the corner of it.

"I still do." ᶠʳ

"Then tell us where you want the rest of us." ᴹ

She looked at the sheet before answering.

* * *

The practical part took longer than the books.

Where Stark would stand when a passage narrowed. How much clear space Fern wanted before she fired. What Yuka would call first, and how everyone would know whether a small pull came from her or Mau.

Yuka made a mark for the signal.

"This before the pull," she said. ʸᵏ

Mau nodded.

Frieren watched her make it once. Then she asked what would happen if the hand making the signal was occupied.

Yuka looked down at the rope she intended to carry.

They changed the signal to a word everyone could hear and kept the hand movement for when speaking would tell the wrong person what they were about to do.

Stark asked them to agree on a retreat word which sounded different from his name being shouted in alarm.

Fern looked at him.

"I don't always shout it in alarm." ᶠ

"Often enough that we should have another word." ˢᵗ

Mau put his head down over the paper. Yuka could see him trying not to laugh.

* * *

They checked the supplies with Rem before the bags closed.

Food for the intended outing and a reserve if something slowed the return. Water accessible without unpacking the whole load. Cloth and the ordinary things Shoko asked them to take even when somebody said they would probably be fine.

The point at which they turned back belonged to the plan too. They could look through another passage because Frieren wanted to see it; they would not turn a familiar dungeon into a promise that nothing inside could surprise them.

At the end, she still had the small book beside her water.

Fern looked at it.

Frieren waited.

"That one," Fern said. ᶠ

Frieren put it in the bag before the decision could be reviewed.

* * *

Mau found Yuka folding the rope into the place the new pouch kept clear.

"Excited?" he asked. ᴹ

She pulled one loop free and arranged it again.

"Yes. Are you going to try and catch all the things before anyone else sees them?" ʸᵏ

He looked offended.

"I don't catch all the things." ᴹ

She waited.

"I'll try not to," he said. ᴹ

She put a hand on his arm.

"Help when it needs helping. I just want to see what I can do before the whole room has been carried out in boxes." ʸᵏ

He leaned down and kissed her forehead.

Across the room, Frieren held out a newly empty part of her bag for Stark to admire.

"For what we find," she said. ᶠʳ

Stark looked at the books Fern had removed, then at the food he had agreed to carry.

"I hope we find something light." ˢᵗ
