Frieren stopped before the broken span and looked at the gap above it.

Fern waited for her to finish. Yuka's Dogs moved along the near side of the floor, keeping to the part she could see. Mau stood behind Stark rather than walking past him to examine everything first.

Something scraped within the wall.

Stark tightened his hand on the newer axe.

"Of course," he said. ˢᵗ

The first plated body came out beneath the fallen stone.

* * *

It was low enough that a cut aimed at a person's middle would have passed above it.

Stark changed his angle. The edge struck the joint behind the head and carried through before the shell could turn his wrist aside.

A second shape followed. Then two on the far side, crawling down where the wall had split.

Frieren marked their positions with her staff.

"Fern. Left." ᶠʳ

The shot struck the leading body before it dropped onto the passage. Stark moved across the place it would have landed. Yuka sent one Dog to hold the nearer turn and kept the second with her while she looked for the next opening.

Mau watched the remaining arch.

The attackers were using the fallen stone as cover. The arch itself was still deciding whether it wanted to be part of the same tactic.

* * *

They gave ground far enough to make the crawlers come out.

Frieren did not retreat past the turn she had chosen. Fern took the new line beside her. Stark let the next shell reach him where he could bring the axe down without striking the stone above his head.

Yuka recalled both Dogs and called the movement before making the pull. Stark held the passage while she changed operations.

One attacker slid off its line, struck the piece of fallen masonry she had chosen and turned its softer underside toward her. She released Blue and brought a Dog back into the opening, its bite reaching the exposed part before the shell could turn down again.

Mau stepped aside as another crawler came through the gap.

It had not noticed him in time to change direction. He caught its approach with a short physical strike, driving it away from Yuka's back and into the ground Stark was already turning to cover.

The axe finished the movement.

"That one needed helping," Mau said. ᴹ

"That one did," Yuka agreed, without taking her eyes off the gap. ʸᵏ

* * *

Twelve smaller bodies lay across the broken ground by the time the mouth of the passage cleared.

The larger creature had waited behind them.

Its shell scraped both sides of the opening. The front limbs unfolded, taking enough weight that the broken arch shifted above it.

Stark looked up.

"Behind me," he said. ˢᵗ

Yuka recalled the nearer Dog and moved. Toad caught the loop of rope when the loose stone under her boot slid, bringing her the last short distance without pulling her into Stark's swing. She recalled it as soon as her footing held.

Mau caught one falling block with the Ruler's grip, held it only long enough for Fern's line to clear and let it drop into empty ground.

The impact struck through the floor.

The creature lowered its head and pushed the rest of the opening apart.

* * *

Frieren's first shot struck where the shell covered the shoulder.

It damaged the plate. It did not stop the body beneath it.

She watched the limb move, then told Fern to wait.

Stark met the first reach with the axe haft braced to turn rather than hold the whole weight. His feet slid. Mau came in at the other side and struck the joint which had opened when the creature tried to follow Stark's movement.

The front of the body dipped.

Yuka pulled the nearer limb off its next foothold. She closed the borrowed operation before calling the Dog back into the place it left exposed.

The keeper turned toward the bite.

"Now," Frieren said. ᶠʳ

Fern's shot went beneath the lifted plate. The body convulsed. Stark stepped clear of the reaching limb and brought the axe into the damaged joint before it could put its weight there again.

Mau moved back rather than trying to make the finish his own.

Frieren sent the last shot through the opening Fern had made.

The keeper fell with a sound which made everyone look at the arch once more.

What remained of it stayed above them.

* * *

Yuka recalled her calls and kept one hand on the rope until she had finished checking the room with her own eyes.

Stark tested the axe edge in the light. The equipment had done its work. His arms still knew he had been the one holding it.

Fern came to the place where Frieren had stopped.

"You waited for it to turn," she said. ᶠ

Frieren nodded toward the shell.

"It couldn't keep that part covered while it looked for her." ᶠʳ

Yuka looked at the gap her Dog had occupied, then at the much larger body.

Mau waited beside her until she looked up at him.

He had blood from one of the smaller creatures on his sleeve. None of it came from a new wound in his arm.

"Did you see it?" she asked. ʸᵏ

"I saw you choose it." ᴹ

She smiled before turning back toward Frieren, who was already looking into the space beyond the broken wall.

There was another part of the room to investigate.

This time Stark went with her before anyone could ask him to.
