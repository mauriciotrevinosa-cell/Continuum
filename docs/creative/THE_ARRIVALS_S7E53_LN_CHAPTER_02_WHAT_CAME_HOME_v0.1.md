* * *

They counted before moving the load.

Thirty-seven smaller usable pieces, with the larger reward kept separate. Plates from the creatures which could be recovered without taking apart the whole passage. Material for the workshop to examine, not proof that it would behave like the stock they already knew.

Mau went back through the cleared room with the others still in reach.

He called the shadows from the defeated dungeon creatures after checking what remained. Twelve smaller forms answered, then the keeper. None carried a name he had lost; none needed one merely because the room was new.

They joined the ordinary ranks.

The change in the count settled at three hundred and seventy-seven ordinary, sixteen named. Three hundred and ninety-three, with his separate shikigami still separate.

He gave the keeper its first task: hold a load steady rather than reach for something living.

It did.

* * *

The Window had reached one hundred and sixteen after the clear.

Five new points. One hundred and seven held. Intelligence remained where Umaru's last page expected it to be.

Mau read the entries, told Ciel which part he intended to record and closed the display without spending them. The body's earlier changes remained under the movement of his hands.

Yuka watched the keeper settle beneath the load.

"How many?" she asked. ʸᵏ

Stark caught the edge of the load before it struck the wall.

Mau changed the arrangement and tried again.

Frieren, already waiting beyond the turn, held her book out of the way until they had passed.

* * *

They returned that day.

The ordinary portal remained ordinary. Rimuru was waiting close enough to see them come through, with Yuta farther back and no crowd assembled to hear another catastrophe had occurred.

Mau greeted him before beginning the inventory.

Yuka went to Maki, who took one look at the dust on her knees and asked about the rope. Fern carried the note concerning the room, and Stark took the axe to the place where he could clean it properly before putting it away.

Frieren walked past with the same small book she had taken inside.

"Did you find one?" ᴼ

Two hundred and eighty-five smaller usable pieces now. Four older pylon pieces unchanged. Six larger rewards, with the keeper's source marked so no one would compare it to the others by size alone.

The plates went to a different place, waiting for examination. They were not added to the worked material reserved for the first two cutters. The things already promised did not become an experiment because something new looked interesting.

Mau took the count to Umaru in the morning.

"Can the tradition take a turn helping here?" ᵀᵃ
