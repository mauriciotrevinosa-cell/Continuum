# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 40
## Chapter 20 — The Light She Drew

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 20
**Source episode:** S7E54
**Continuity state:** Day852 late afternoon. Coco/Qifrey/Richeh and Coco's mother, small existing glyph-light practice with measured existing ink. No discovery that all Witch Hat magic uses mana/crystals, public ink disclosure, imported family arrival or new permanent house grid. Mother's presence retained. Short side scene with its own purpose.

*When: Day852. Coco's mother holds the paper by the blank edge while Coco explains why she must not turn it yet.*

"Here?" her mother asked. ᶜᵐ

"Yes. Please." ᶜᵒ

Coco bent over the last part of the drawing. It was a small light, something to read by near a shelf rather than a replacement for every lamp in the house. Qifrey had asked her what it needed to illuminate before letting the size of the room make the decision for her.

She had chosen a page.

Beside her, Richeh finished checking the place where the paper would be supported. The drawing could be excellent and still be put somewhere a wet sleeve would ruin the whole exercise.

Coco closed the final line.

The light came softly enough that her mother did not move her hands.

"Now?" ᶜᵐ

Coco looked at Qifrey. He nodded.

"Now." ᶜᵒ

* * *

They put it beside the book, then moved it.

The first place lit the edge of the page and made the hand holding it cast a shadow over the words. Richeh tried another position. Coco's mother turned the page, waited and asked whether she was allowed to admit that the second place was easier.

"You have to," Richeh said. ᴿⁱ

Coco laughed.

Qifrey made a note of how much ink the exercise had used. Senku's crystal lamps had their own questions; this work still required their materials, their drawings and their decisions about what to show to whom.

No one would take the page outside to demonstrate how useful the house could be.

Coco's mother read another line, then looked up at her daughter.

"You made it for here." ᶜᵐ

Coco nodded.

She had imagined a much brighter result when she began. Something which would make everyone stop and look. Instead she had made a small patch of light which let her mother read without bending toward the window.

Her mother went back to the sentence she had been following.

That was better than stopping.

* * *

They put the work away before the table was needed for something else.

Qifrey checked the remaining ink. Richeh took the support apart so it could be used again without somebody having to invent a reason to store the whole arrangement forever.

Coco gathered the pages.

Her mother waited for her at the door.

"Were you going somewhere?" Coco asked. ᶜᵒ

"I thought we could walk before dinner." ᶜᵐ

Coco looked toward Qifrey.

He put the closed ink away.

"Your lesson is finished." ᑫ

She went quickly enough that her mother laughed when she caught up.

Behind them, Richeh turned the little support in her hands once more and found the place she would change next time.
