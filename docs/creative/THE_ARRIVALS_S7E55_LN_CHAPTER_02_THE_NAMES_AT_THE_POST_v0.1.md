## Chapter 22 — The Names at the Post

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 40

*When: Day862. The man at the first post asks their names, and Anko lets him finish finding the page before answering.*

Mau could see where he was looking. The letter had arrived. It had also been placed beneath two other things, one of which someone apparently expected to use again.

The man moved them, read the names and looked up.

Wakana shifted the wrapped tools against his side. Marin waited beside him. Nobody began untying the load as though being expected meant there would be no questions.

"This party?" the man asked. ˡ

Anko indicated the names in order.

He counted them, checked the route and stepped aside.

Mau had prepared three different answers to an objection which did not come.

He followed the others through.

* * *

At the next post they waited longer.

The person with the letter had gone a short distance to check a cart. His replacement knew they were coming and still wanted the written page. Anko asked where they should stand while it was found. Mau kept the wrapped load off the wet ground.

Marin looked at his face.

"Don't make that face," she whispered. ᴹᵃʳ

"Which face?" ᴹ

"The one where you're deciding whether everyone is doing this on purpose." ᴹᵃʳ

He breathed out.

The man came back with the page and an apology for the wait which sounded tired rather than frightened. He asked about the damaged road beyond the next turn.

Mau told him which side had held their weight on the way in.

They were through the third post before he realized he had spent the last part of the walk discussing mud.

* * *

The buyers tested their tools separately.

Wakana watched the hands rather than doing the cuts for them. Marin asked the second man to show her where the guard touched when his grip grew tired. He changed his wrist, then looked surprised that this was precisely the movement the new shape left room for.

The first man tried to increase the job halfway through the test.

Anko put a finger beside the agreed description.

"These are the terms for this one." ᴬ

He looked at the page, then at the thing he had brought to cut.

"And if I need a different one?" ˡ

"You ask for it. Before using this as the demonstration that the different one will work." ᴬ

Hollis, who had arrived with the other goods for the meeting, turned away to hide a smile.

The second buyer accepted first. The first accepted after another look at the fastening. They paid what had been agreed for two completed pieces, and the received column finally acquired a number.

* * *

Wakana kept the notes concerning the next changes without promising the rest of the original inquiry.

Mau helped put away the test board. Hollis came to stand beside him with news which did not fit on the sale page.

"The nearer party came back," he said. ᴴᵒ

Mau stopped lifting.

"All of them?" ᴹ

"All the ones who went in, according to the man who brought them out to the road. Two hurt. The opening's shut." ᴴᵒ

"You know who said that?" ᴹ

Hollis gave him the contact. Anko wrote it beside the first report rather than putting it among their own victories.

The second opening was still only the story of someone who had seen someone running. Hollis had no better answer for that one and said so.

Mau finished moving the board.

* * *

They brought the money home before deciding what it meant.

The work had used material and time. Part of the income belonged to replacing those things. Part could help with the next small household improvement. None of it made the road beyond the third post theirs.

Jinshi checked the receipt when Anko gave it to him. Fern read the passage report and put the practical delay beside the successful crossing rather than calling it either perfect or useless.

Wakana carried the remaining measurements back to his bench.

Marin took the empty wrapping from Mau and shook the dust from it before folding it away.

"Those two are someone else's problem to keep clean now," she said. ᴹᵃʳ

Mau looked at the empty space in his arms.

Then he went to find Yuka and tell her about the conversation he had not needed to have at the first post.
