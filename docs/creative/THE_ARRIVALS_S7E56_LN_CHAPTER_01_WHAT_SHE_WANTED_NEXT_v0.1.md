*When: Day866. Yuka puts the next page between them and keeps her hand on it while Mau reads.*

Round Deer.

He looked at the drawing, then at the notes she had made beside it. The parts concerned with movement had more corrections than the parts concerned with what she hoped it could do afterward.

"You want this one next," he said. ᴹ

"Yes." ʸᵏ

"For the healing?" ᴹ

"That too. But I want to understand this part before it surprises us during something else." ʸᵏ

She pointed to the place where the output could interfere with cursed energy being used around it.

Mau took his time. Ciel followed the accessible information with him, staying within the pattern work they had agreed to examine. The drawing did not give Yuka experience she had not earned. It gave them a better question to take into the room.

He looked up.

"Then we shouldn't build the plan around a borrowed pull holding it still." ᴹ

Yuka lifted her hand from the page.

"That's what I thought." ʸᵏ

* * *

The ordinary rope stayed on the table.

They had become good at treating it as a way to move, reach or be pulled out of the wrong place. Today Yuka wanted it as a physical tool whose usefulness would not depend on cursed energy continuing to flow through an object near the deer.

Mau tried the loop with her. She made him stand in the awkward place before explaining how she wanted him to turn.

"Here?" he asked. ᴹ

"If it goes that way. Not before. I need room for the ox." ʸᵏ

He looked at the line she had marked for the charge.

"Short turn into a longer run." ᴹ

"One I choose. I don't want to keep hurting something that repairs faster than I'm making a decision." ʸᵏ

He went quiet for a moment.

Yuka touched his sleeve.

"You don't have to do this one if it feels wrong." ʸᵏ

He looked down at her hand, then back at the drawing.

"I can do the part we've agreed. I was thinking about what not to repeat." ᴹ

She nodded and let him finish the movement before changing anything else.

* * *

Yuta checked the plan that afternoon.

Maki asked what the retreat would look like if the first turn failed. Yuka showed it without asking Mau to invent the answer for her.

His place inside the ritual was stated again: physical help, the same shared-helper arrangement they had already tested in this world's earlier subjugations. He would not call his own Mahoraga or turn a practice at her technique into a demonstration of everything he could summon.

Yuta would stay outside. Maki would watch the exit and the condition under which they would stop.

"And afterward," Maki said, "you wait before deciding success means you can repeat it all afternoon." ᴹᵃ

Yuka looked at her.

"I was going to." ʸᵏ

Maki looked at Mau.

"Then I was talking to him." ᴹᵃ

He raised both hands.

* * *

Shoko had one condition when they told her what they hoped to test after the subjugation.

"Nobody cuts themselves to give it something to fix." ˢʰ

Mau closed his mouth.

Yuka turned toward him slowly.

"I hadn't suggested it," he said. ᴹ

"Excellent. We can preserve that achievement," Shoko said. ˢʰ

She asked about the output, who would be providing the energy and what they actually knew it could affect. Yuka answered the native cost herself. The borrowed tools and the things which returned their demand to Mau were different work.

They did not turn a picture of healing into a promise about every illness, an old scar or a body nobody fully understood.

Shoko gave the page back.

"Get it under your control first. Then we find out what it does safely." ˢʰ

* * *

Yuka folded the rope while Mau put the last practice marker away.

She had packed the pouch herself. He could see where the notebook would fit, where the ordinary cloth would stay dry and which loop she wanted free first.

When he offered to take it upstairs, she let him.

At the foot of the stairs, she stopped him with a hand against his chest.

"You were listening," she said. ʸᵏ

"I usually try." ᴹ

"You didn't decide the answer before I finished." ʸᵏ

He looked at her for a moment.

Then leaned down when she reached for him.

She kissed him, took the pouch back and went up beside him, leaving the new page ready for the room where they would use it.
