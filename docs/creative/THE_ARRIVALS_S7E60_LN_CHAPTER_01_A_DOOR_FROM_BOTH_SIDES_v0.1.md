*When: Day887. The cup comes back through the doorway with the handle facing the wrong direction, and Ciel makes Mau repeat the test before he can declare the room ready.*

It had not broken.

It had also not stayed where he put it. Noise had corrected the surface beneath it while they were checking the other side, and the small movement was exactly the kind of thing which became important when the object was somebody trying to stand up.

Mau put the cup back.

*Again,* Ciel said.

*I'm doing it.*

The doorway held in the accessible shadow beside him. Beyond it lay a room small enough to understand at a glance: floor, clear walls, a place for a bed and a return point which did not depend on walking into the unfinished work behind it.

The other access remained closed.

* * *

They tested the empty room before bringing anyone into it.

What happened to a loose cloth when the opening changed. Where ordinary light reached. How air continued through the connection Noise had left rather than becoming a sealed box with a pleasant name. Whether the notification could reach Mau without carrying everything a person said while she thought she was alone.

The first call was too quiet.

The second brought the feeling of an alarm to the whole of his attention, which made him sit very still until Ciel took the excess out of it.

*A knock,* he told her. *She needs a knock.*

Noise changed the boundary of the signal.

The next attempt felt like someone waiting on the other side of a door.

Mau looked at the empty room and nodded.

*That.*

* * *

Rimuru volunteered for the first short visit after they had checked the results together.

Shoko remained outside with Mau. Yuka watched from the place where she could see the threshold without being in the way if someone needed to come back quickly.

Rimuru stepped through, turned and looked at the opening from inside.

"It's a room," he said. ᴿ

"We were hoping," Mau said. ᴹ

Rimuru put a hand on the return marker they had agreed to test. The opening remained accessible from his side. He came back before Mau had to do anything for him.

Then he went in again, used the notification and waited until Mau answered before returning.

He had the expression of someone resisting the temptation to try three unnecessary things because everyone was watching him be sensible.

"One at a time," Shoko said. ˢʰ

He looked at her.

"I hadn't done anything." ᴿ

"You're welcome." ˢʰ

* * *

Mau could feel the new construction being maintained.

It was a small, bounded demand within what they could support today. It was not the same operation as calling an existing soldier back to his shadow, and he did not pretend a free recall made every room in the world free to build and keep.

They kept the result with the scope they had tested. The doorway returned beside him; it did not give DIO a way to open a door at any place she imagined. It did not promise safe passage through every strange instance someone might build later.

If he travelled beyond the protected grounds, they would discuss whether she came or stayed in the ordinary house. Giving her shelter would not make every future journey a hidden transport of someone who had never agreed to go.

Rimuru listened and looked at the room again.

"You should tell her all of that before the comfortable part," he said. ᴿ

Mau nodded.

* * *

Yuka asked to see it when the first tests were finished.

He held the opening while she stepped just inside. She examined the floor, then the place where a table would fit. One hand stayed near the doorway until she had found how the return worked from her side.

"She gets to choose where the chair goes?" she asked. ʸᵏ

"Yes." ᴹ

"Good. You're terrible with doors and knees." ʸᵏ

He laughed.

She came back out and took his hand on the ordinary side of the threshold.

For a moment they both looked into the quiet space.

"It feels different when someone can leave," she said. ʸᵏ

He closed his fingers around hers.

* * *

The test while he slept waited until the following day.

They kept it short, with Rimuru inside by choice and Shoko outside. Mau lay down in the place he had agreed to use and found it difficult to fall asleep while thinking about whether he would.

Ciel told him he did not need to supervise unconsciousness.

Eventually he stopped trying.

When he woke, Rimuru was sitting outside with a cup. He had returned using the same route while Noise and Ciel maintained the construction. No one had needed to wake Mau to grant a personal permission.

Yuka looked pleased enough that he asked what had happened.

"You slept," she said. ʸᵏ

"An advanced technique." ᴹ

She leaned down and kissed his forehead.

"You're improving." ʸᵏ
