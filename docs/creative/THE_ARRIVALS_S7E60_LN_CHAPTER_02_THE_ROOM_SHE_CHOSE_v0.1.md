## Chapter 33 — The Room She Chose

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 40
**Reading order:** 33
**Source episode:** S7E60
**Continuity state:** Day889. DIO informed of tested scope, voluntary entry/exit, privacy, contact and travel limits, chooses bounded interior refuge. Physical whole guest, not shadow/absorbed soul/third heart; no authority, memory or power loan obtained by Mau. Second request/favor fulfilled as refuge without hidden Trial/new price; accountability remains. Ordinary furniture/linen supplied by household agreement, no new crystal/coin/inventory grant. Personal presence remains linked to life anchor without mobile protection.

*When: Day889. DIO asks to see the way out before she looks at the bed.*

Mau showed her.

She stood at the threshold with one hand against the ordinary wall of the room they were using for the visit. Shoko had allowed the walk, not a performance of how little help a goddess needed. Rimuru waited where he could hear the explanation; Yuka had brought the folded linen and set it down without putting it inside for her.

DIO looked at the return marker.

"I do this?" she asked. ᴰⁱᵒ

"You do it," Mau said. ᴹ

She tried from just inside. Came back. Looked at him without having waited for him to say she could.

Then she went in far enough to examine the bed.

* * *

He gave the limits before offering the quiet.

The room belonged to a construction Noise and Ciel were helping maintain. Its return opened beside Mau. It was not a way to choose an address anywhere outside. They had tested a brief stay, communication, air and a return while he slept; they had not tested every journey, every hostile space or a body which was not his behaving as though it were.

If he left the grounds, they would speak beforehand. DIO could stay in the physical house instead of being carried without agreement. New conditions meant new questions, not that accepting a refuge gave him leave to decide for her later.

She listened without interrupting.

"And if I want company?" ᴰⁱᵒ

"Knock. Or come out." ᴹ

"If I don't?" ᴰⁱᵒ

"We don't treat the room as a place to listen." ᴹ

* * *

They had agreed on a basic welfare signal and a call for help.

Nothing about those required Mau to receive her thoughts. Ciel would keep the work within its agreed scope; Noise would not take a guest as permission to begin examining everything she was. DIO would not reach into the guarded work or the hosts' memories because she was resting near it.

She looked toward the part of the construction which the room did not open onto.

"You kept things closed." ᴰⁱᵒ

"They were closed before you came." ᴹ

"Good." ᴰⁱᵒ

The answer was simple enough that Mau had no immediate reply to make.

She turned away first and put a hand against the edge of the bed.

* * *

The furnishings were ordinary.

A place to lie down. A little table. A chair she could move without asking him to rebuild the room around it. The household had agreed to lend what she needed until they knew what she would actually use.

DIO moved the chair closer to the table.

Yuka looked at Mau.

He looked at the opening and said nothing.

DIO noticed them trying not to laugh and paused with her hand still on the chair.

"Should I know something?" ᴰⁱᵒ

"Only that it's yours to move," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

She put the linen on the bed when DIO asked her to.

* * *

When the others had stepped back to leave her room to choose, DIO remained at the threshold.

Mau could see that she was tired. It did not make the question less important.

"Do you want this?" he asked. ᴹ

She looked behind her.

Then at the ordinary place outside, where a door remained a door and someone might knock because dinner was ready rather than because they wanted an answer about the world.

"Yes." ᴰⁱᵒ

"You can change your mind." ᴹ

"So can you about what you can safely offer. You tell me. We don't make the rest of the agreement out of silence." ᴰⁱᵒ

He nodded.

She stepped inside.

* * *

Nothing entered his chest.

No third beat arrived to explain what he owed. The room remained a room he could feel being held apart from the unfinished work, and DIO remained a person standing inside it.

At the doorway, she looked back.

"The second request," she said. "This is it." ᴰⁱᵒ

Mau let the words settle before answering.

"Then you have a place." ᴹ

She looked down for a moment, and when she raised her head the expression did not contain a lesson she intended to make him learn.

"Thank you." ᴰⁱᵒ

He stepped away when she asked for the quiet.

The contact closed to its resting state. Her way to call or return remained hers.

Yuka waited beside him.

After a little while she took his hand and asked whether he wanted to walk before they went back to the others.

He did.
