*When: Day891. Yuka finds Mau in the common room with the map open and enough space beside him for her to know he has been expecting her.*

"You started without me." ʸᵏ

"I found the page." ᴹ

She sat down and examined his work.

The new corner had acquired a chair with a knee drawn much too close to the door. Beside it, Mau had left room for whatever correction she intended to make.

Yuka put a hand over her mouth.

"That knee isn't mine." ʸᵏ

"Then the drawing is anonymous." ᴹ

She took the pencil.

* * *

The room carried on around them.

Bocchi came through with the others and asked whether the chair would be free later. Mau told her where he was putting the map when they went to eat, and she nodded before following Nijika toward the music room.

At the far table, Eren was listening while Mikasa changed a mark on the route. He asked a question about where the patrol could see the turn without standing in the road. She showed him. The conversation ended with the two of them putting the page away rather than inventing a reason to stay at work through the meal.

Umaru came looking for someone to settle a dispute before it became another rule. She found Yuka, accused her of being unavailable to the public and was offered a place on the other chair long enough to explain the case.

Mau watched Yuka take the accusation seriously for exactly as long as it deserved.

Then she laughed, and the color of her sleeve moved against the grey page beneath his hand.

* * *

They had not solved the world by selling two tools.

The next road report would still need somebody to ask who had seen what. The repaired handles would need to survive more work than the demonstration. There were relatives people still hoped to find, rooms they had not drawn and things inside Mau which being loved had not finished explaining.

He could leave the page open without demanding that it answer any of those questions tonight.

Yuka touched the drawing he had made.

"Here?" she asked. ʸᵏ

He looked at the small place beside the chair.

"Here." ᴹ

She added a cup.

* * *

Before the meal, Mau went to check whether DIO wanted to join them.

He used the signal they had agreed on and waited. The answer came after a short pause: yes, for a little while.

She returned through the opening herself. He gave her room to stand, then walked beside her at the pace Shoko had told her to use.

At the table, someone had left a place where the way out remained easy to reach. DIO sat without making the whole room stop to notice she had accepted a bowl.

Ori asked whether she had slept.

"Yes," DIO said. ᴰⁱᵒ

"Good," Ori said, and went back to the argument she had been having with Milim about whether flying over a boundary counted as crossing it. ᴼ

Frieren told them it depended which boundary.

Fern looked up immediately.

"Not that one." ᶠ

Milim looked offended by the speed with which her possible defense had been recognized.

Mau put his face behind his cup.

* * *

Afterward the tables emptied in the ordinary uneven way.

Some people stayed to talk. Some carried bowls. Momo sat down while Rem and Taihei took the first part of clearing, and Kaneki went to Touka when Ichika wanted him to see something before she became too sleepy to explain it.

Gojo said something to Shoko which made her laugh and then tell him to pick up what he had been standing beside for the last several minutes.

Mau took the tray before he could be included in the same accusation.

Yuka followed him as far as the washing, stayed to help with the last two cups and waited while he went to answer a question about tomorrow's short patrol.

When he returned, she had the map under her arm.

"I rescued it," she said. ʸᵏ

"From what?" ᴹ

"Your habit of agreeing to one more thing while holding three other things." ʸᵏ

He held out his empty hands.

She gave him one of hers.

* * *

They walked to the stairs slowly enough to be passed by people who knew where they were going.

DIO had already chosen to return to the room. Ciel had checked the small continuing demand and left the rest of the evening quiet. The room did not need Mau's constant attention to keep proving that helping someone had been real.

At the bottom step, Yuka gave him the map.

He put it under his arm before reaching for her.

She met him halfway, one hand at his shoulder, the other still holding his. When the kiss ended, she stayed close enough that he could feel her smile before he saw it.

"Tomorrow?" she asked. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"Yes." ᴹ

They went upstairs together. Rimuru called good night from the old room before retreating to the loft; Yuka answered while Mau opened the door which now belonged to both of them.

He put the map beside his chair. Yuka set her book down and moved his cup into reach, a small adjustment she no longer needed to ask about.

He opened the newest page once more.

The cup Yuka had drawn leaned a little toward the chair.

He left it that way.

Yuka had already settled on her side of the bed. He put out the lamp, found her hand beneath the blanket and stayed where she had made room for him.
