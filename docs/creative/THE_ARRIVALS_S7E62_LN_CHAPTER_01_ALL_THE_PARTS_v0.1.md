*When: Day906. Gojo stops the first demonstration himself and tells Senku to leave the failed attempt on the page.*

The pull had reached the far mark.

It had also gathered more of the ground than he meant to move. Mau could see the small difference in his expression before Gojo explained it. An effect could be large and still be wrong.

"Again," Gojo said. ᴳ

He made the same reach with less disturbance. Then he did it with the second mark moved and the first removed, so nobody could mistake a familiar arrangement for a recovered operation.

Shoko stood beside the notes. She did not congratulate him before he had finished deciding what counted.

When he took the page, he put his own reference beside the result.

Not Mau's. Not what someone thought the strongest person in a different story ought to do. The movement he remembered being able to make when he arrived.

* * *

Red waited until the next set of checks.

By Day918 they had moved the work into a cleared dungeon room with enough distance for a failed release to reach empty stone instead of somebody else's afternoon. Rimuru remained home. DIO had chosen the ordinary house for the visit and taken her book out before Mau left.

Gojo began small, then increased the operation under the conditions they had agreed. The familiar repulsion struck the far target and left the neighboring mark where it belonged.

He repeated the control after the larger output.

The next day, outside, he performed the bounded version again. Nobody described a temporary permission inside a room as proof that it had followed him out until they had watched it follow him out.

Mau stood beside him afterward.

"You look pleased," he said. ᴹ

"I've been very restrained about it." ᴳ

Shoko made a noise behind them.

Gojo did not turn around.

"Exceptionally restrained." ᴳ

* * *

There were other days between the tests.

Yuka took Mau to see how long she could hold the smaller shadow field without losing the return position. The result did not replace Gojo's work on the page. Yuta checked the operations which belonged to his own kit, and Maki asked that nobody write a cursed-energy target under her name merely because the table had a column for it.

Mau helped Kaneki carry a load, lost a game to Umaru and managed to hear Bocchi's new part without interrupting her to explain how much he liked the old one.

The days did not all become a test because one thing they wanted was getting closer.

* * *

Purple came after its components held.

The domain check came separately, with Gojo alone in the room they had cleared. He closed it, held the operation to the scope he had chosen and released it without making somebody else's brain the measuring device.

They checked the consequences, the recovery and the next operations afterward. Another test outside established what was still available there. His eyes, the neutral technique, the restored offensive work and the domain belonged together again without having to be excused as one lucky burst.

On Day938, he put the last note beside the earlier failures.

"That's me," he said. ᴳ

Senku looked at the full page.

"The version you came from." ˢᵉⁿ

"Yes. You can stop trying to make it sound unromantic." ᴳ

Shoko took the pencil from him before he could add anything decorative.

* * *

Mau's work looked less impressive from a distance.

Gojo left him a very small controlled operation to approach under the conditions they had agreed. Mau closed his own active technique before trying to carry the neutralizing layer through the place where contact would occur.

The first attempt broke when he became too interested in reaching the end.

The second reached, but he lost the layer as he changed his stance.

By the end of that afternoon, he could do the limited exercise twice without accidentally beginning his own technique under it. His strength did not disappear when the active operation stopped. That was the useful part of having a body which could carry its own side of an exchange.

Gojo watched his hands.

"Don't practise that by deciding you can ignore every other thing someone does to you," he said. ᴳ

Mau looked up.

"You sound like you have plans." ᴹ

"I have been waiting very politely." ᴳ

* * *

Ciel and Noise kept working on the repair route Mau already understood in pieces.

He had watched rapid healing. He had used smaller healing on himself. Neither fact meant the body would organize the stronger route correctly merely because he wanted it during a fight.

They worked with existing patterns, his own current tolerances and the parts Shoko could ask him to describe without making him injure himself for an answer. Old scars were not a test he promised to erase.

When Gojo asked about the rematch, Mau looked at the date they could use.

Then went to ask Yuka what she had already planned for that day.
