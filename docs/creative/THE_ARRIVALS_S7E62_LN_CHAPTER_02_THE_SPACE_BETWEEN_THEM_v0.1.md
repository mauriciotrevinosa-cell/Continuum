*When: Day949. Yuka tells Mau that winning does not excuse forgetting where he left his water.*

He took the cup from her, drank and handed it back before Gojo could offer an opinion.

The room had already been cleared. There were marks for the working space, observers outside it and an end signal both fighters had agreed to honor. No domains this time. No summoned army or live blades. Those were choices they had made for the fight they wanted to have, not missing pieces somebody would later pretend the other had forgotten to use.

Gojo rolled one shoulder and smiled at him.

"Ready?" ᴳ

Mau stepped onto the marked ground.

* * *

The first exchange taught him that watching Gojo teach was not the same as making Gojo want to touch him.

Blue changed the useful distance. A foot arrived where the invitation of the hands had made him look away from. Mau blocked late, turned with the impact and found the next space had already become less convenient.

He could meet the strength. The arrangement was the problem.

Gojo made it the problem again.

Mau stopped following the point of the attack and began watching the things Yuta had made him notice: the hand which was not asking for attention, the foot preparing the place the body would need next.

The next strike still reached him.

He was waiting for it this time.

* * *

Ciel kept the stream readable. Mini Ciel did not decide his next move for him.

Noise retained the movement he could actually observe; Mau began trying it where it belonged instead of reproducing a pose after its moment had passed. One turn worked. The next failed because Gojo recognized what he was attempting and changed the invitation.

Mau laughed through a breath he had not entirely recovered.

Gojo's smile sharpened.

"There you are." ᴳ

Red struck the ground beyond the line Mau had been trying to leave and turned the escape into pressure against his side.

He landed hard enough to lose the next word he meant to say.

Yuka moved at the edge of the room and stopped when he held up a hand. The agreed signal to end remained available. He had not used it.

He took a breath carefully.

His side answered with pain sharp enough that pretending it was only surprise would have been stupid.

* * *

*Fresh damage,* Ciel told him, and gave him the extent she could observe rather than a speech about courage.

The route was there, prepared and still more difficult to hold through pain than it had been to describe while sitting down.

He let the other active work close. Followed the repair without trying to make speed by pouring everything into the wrong place.

The breath after it hurt less.

He did it again within the limits Ciel gave him. The new damage steadied; the old stiff fingers remained old stiff fingers when he flexed his hand against the ground.

Gojo waited until he stood.

"That one worked," he said. ᴳ

Mau wiped his mouth with the back of his hand.

"I'd prefer to keep the sample size down." ᴹ

* * *

He went back in without making a larger technique the answer to every distance.

Blue forced a turn. He let it. Gojo's hand drew his attention high, but he saw the lower movement this time and put his own foot where the second exchange needed to begin.

His strike stopped at Infinity.

Gojo was already moving to punish the contact which had not happened.

Mau released his own active operation completely.

The neutralizing layer settled over the point of approach as he had practised, narrower and less forgiving than an invulnerable answer to the whole room.

His body kept its strength.

For an instant Gojo's eyes changed.

Mau reached him.

* * *

The first contact was not the finish.

Gojo turned with it, caught the shoulder and tried to send the next exchange back into a distance he controlled. Mau let the layer go before it broke under a movement he could not maintain, then rebuilt the limited contact on the next approach.

He had to give up the comfort of his own active defense to do it.

Gojo knew.

The return strike landed against the place Mau had left open. He accepted the cost he could take, moved through it and met the turn before Gojo could finish changing its direction.

His fist reached the agreed target cleanly.

Gojo's hand rose in the end signal before a friendly victory became an unnecessary second injury.

Mau stopped.

* * *

For a while they stood there breathing.

Then Gojo began laughing.

"You took your time." ᴳ

"You were making it inconvenient." ᴹ

"I was helping." ᴳ

Yuka reached them with the water. Shoko reached them immediately afterward, with an expression which made both men remember that there had been rules after the end signal too.

She checked the new repair before letting Mau tell the story of it.

"This doesn't mean the older damage should behave the same," she said. ˢʰ

"I know." ᴹ

Gojo looked at the hand Mau was holding open.

"And the contact doesn't mean you can run everything under it at once." ᴳ

"I noticed." ᴹ

Yuka held out the cup.

"And this means drink." ʸᵏ

He took it.

* * *

They went home with a result they could name.

Mau had found and held the contact. He had used a stronger repair on a fresh injury through the route they had prepared. He had begun learning a moving opponent's choices instead of collecting beautiful still images of how to fight.

Gojo had been there in the version of himself they had checked, laughing when the exchange ended rather than disappearing behind a limitation nobody mentioned until afterward.

At the door, he put a hand on Mau's shoulder.

"Next time we change the rules," he said. ᴳ

Mau looked at Yuka.

She looked at Shoko.

Shoko pointed both men toward the place where the food was waiting.
