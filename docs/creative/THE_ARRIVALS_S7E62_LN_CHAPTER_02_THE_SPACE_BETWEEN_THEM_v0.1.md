*When: Day949. Yuka tells Mau that winning does not excuse forgetting where he left his water.*

He took the cup from her, drank and gave it back before Gojo could offer an opinion.

The basin opened beneath the dungeon's empty sky. Above the ruined terraces, the light held the same unchanging angle they had seen on the inspection. No one lived in the ground they were about to ruin. The return corridor lay behind the spectators, well away from the line of the first exchange.

Gojo looked at the ordinary practice markers and then at Mau.

"Shall we pretend these will survive?" ᴳ

Mau stepped onto the ground between them.

"For the first minute." ᴹ

* * *

Blue made the minute considerably shorter.

The first pull did not try to drag Mau all the way to Gojo. It took the useful part of his balance while Gojo's foot arrived from the other side.

Mau blocked late, turned with the impact and found his next landing had already become inconvenient.

He grinned despite himself.

Gojo saw it.

The next invitation came higher, a hand which wanted to be watched. Mau followed the other shoulder instead. Yuta's slow wooden-sword lessons were suddenly useful in a space where the body behind the hand could change what a step meant.

He caught the strike.

Missed the next one.

Caught that turn anyway, and laughed when Gojo had to leave the place he had expected to keep.

* * *

Ciel held the stream of detail where Mau could use it. Mini Ciel sorted; it did not choose.

He began recognizing Gojo's decisions as movements still in progress. Noise retained what he actually saw, and Mau tried the useful part of a turn instead of wearing its outline after the moment had passed.

Gojo changed the invitation as soon as he noticed.

"That one was mine," he said. ᴳ

"You left it where I could see it." ᴹ

Red struck the ground beyond Mau's escape line.

The blast caught his side and sent him down the slope hard enough to make the next breath arrive in pieces.

Gojo let the distance remain while Mau raised a hand to say he was still choosing to continue.

* * *

The repair route had been prepared.

Making it work through pain was different from understanding why it should work. Mau closed his other active operation, followed the fresh damage Ciel could identify and stopped trying to hurry by making the wrong part larger.

His next breath hurt less.

He repeated the repair within the range they had practised. The new injury steadied. The old fingers remained stiff when he opened his hand against the stone.

Gojo waited until he stood.

"There it is." ᴳ

Mau wiped his mouth.

"You could have sent a smaller example." ᴹ

"You were enjoying the explanation." ᴳ

He was.

That was becoming difficult to hide.

* * *

Mau went back in on a different line.

He let Blue turn his approach rather than wasting the whole exchange arguing with it. His strike reached Infinity, and Gojo began moving toward the answer he expected to give it.

Mau released his own active technique.

Amplification settled across the limited place of contact. His body kept the strength already built into it. The neutralizing layer did not make the entire field disappear; it gave his hand a working path through the part it could meet.

Gojo's eyes sharpened.

The fist reached him.

He turned with it, caught Mau's shoulder and made the successful contact expensive before Mau could enjoy it too much.

Mau broke the layer, took the physical correction and rebuilt it on the next approach.

This time they both landed something.

* * *

They moved apart almost together.

Mau's cheek hurt. Gojo touched the mark along his jaw and looked at his own fingers.

Then started laughing.

Mau bent over with his hands on his knees and found he was laughing too. Not relief after surviving a thing he wished would stop. The simple, unreasonable pleasure of being met by somebody who kept having another answer.

Gojo straightened first.

The old practice markers lay scattered behind him.

He looked at the wide empty basin, at the terraces above it, at the person who had just reached him and wanted to try again.

"No limits." ᴳ

Mau lifted his head.

The smile on his face changed.

"No limits." ᴹ

* * *

They waited while the others moved.

The agreement was a lift on their arsenal and output, not a sudden decision that friends were disposable. Either could still end it. The people watching did not have to stand under a new idea to prove they trusted them.

Yuta took the checked corridor. Shoko went with him after making Gojo look at her long enough to remember she existed. Yuka stopped beside Mau before following.

She looked at his face and began smiling despite the worry.

"You look ridiculous," she said. ʸᵏ

"I'm having a very good day." ᴹ

"Try to keep it that way." ʸᵏ

He touched her hand once. She went, leaving her borrowed demand quiet by choice.

The corridor cleared.

Mau recalled the small neutralizing layer before opening another cursed technique. Behind him, his Monarch shadow spread across the stone, belonging to a different source and a different part of the work.

Gojo watched the first armored shape rise.

"Much better," he said. ᴳ
