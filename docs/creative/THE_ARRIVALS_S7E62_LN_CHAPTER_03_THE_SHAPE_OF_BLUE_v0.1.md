*When: Igris steps into the open. Gojo looks past the sword at the second shape rising behind it.*

"He's smiling," Beru said, with the offended fascination of someone who had expected the appearance of his king's soldiers to cause a more respectful difficulty. ᴮᵉ

"So am I," Mau said. ᴹ

He gave the orders before the moment could become a speech. Igris took one approach. Bellion another. The ordinary soldiers spread without pretending all twenty-four could hit one person at the same instant and remain useful.

Gojo's first Blue gathered between the two nearest lines.

Mau began moving to answer the familiar pull.

The field changed shape beneath his foot.

* * *

It did not pull equally toward a center.

The short crossing on one side became a long, dragging approach. The ground which should have given him a stable landing felt as though it sloped sideways without having moved. The soldier beside him went in faster while Mau's own next step stretched out of agreement with it.

Gojo crossed the narrow direction and met him at the end.

The hit sent Mau through a low wall.

He caught himself in the broken stone and stared back.

Gojo stood at the center of something which was no longer helpfully shaped like an orb.

"You've been doing homework," Mau said. ᴹ

"You were very rude about my three balls." ᴳ

* * *

Bellion made Gojo turn before he could follow the first hit.

The flexible reach of his weapon took away a convenient place to stand. Gojo changed the gradient and brought three soldiers into the same collision, leaving Bellion's strike to finish in empty air.

Igris came through the gap that opened.

Infinity held.

Gojo caught the angle with Blue and sent the knight sliding across the basin instead of granting him the same approach twice.

Mau did not call it a failed attack. Igris had made Gojo choose a direction. Bellion had shown him which field moved with that choice.

He used Ruler's grip on the detached block beside him and pulled his body around its changing weight, taking an ordinary physical route through the direction Gojo had made least convenient to watch.

For a breath he reached the flank.

Then Gojo's image moved without the body moving the way it should.

* * *

Mau struck the place the light had told him Gojo occupied.

The return blow arrived from closer to his left shoulder.

He staggered, furious for half a second with his old blurred edge before Ciel separated the things he was receiving.

*The path of the information changed. Your eye hasn't failed. The bright outline isn't the whole answer.*

He could see the cursed flow, but its route through the field had changed too. Seeing more did not automatically give him a map drawn from outside the distortion.

Noise began preserving the pattern.

Mau almost reached for the beginning of a copy.

*No,* Ciel said.

*I can see how—*

*You can see enough to begin another unfinished construction. He is already fighting with this one. Let Noise keep the observation. You need to use what you brought.*

* * *

Gojo came through the false edge again.

Mau met the impact with reinforcement, lost the step and let Beru take the next angle while he found the answer Ciel was asking him to choose.

*You have hands, surfaces, force and soldiers,* she said. *Stop trying to become the next thing he is. Make the things you already are arrive together.*

Mau looked at the broken blocks.

Then at the soldiers Gojo had moved.

He began laughing again.

*All right.*

* * *

The first answer was ugly.

Ruler's grip lifted three pieces of the broken wall. Mau sent them across different heights while the soldiers changed position beneath. He did not expect stone or a sword to pass Infinity. He wanted the world around Gojo to report what it did when it met the field.

One block turned. One slowed. The third gave him a clean view of the direction from which the pressure really came.

Igris moved at his signal.

Gojo turned to answer the knight and found Mau already letting his own neutral technique go so the familiar sky-grab could take its place.

Mau folded the surface of the approach. A falling block travelled around the visible image instead of toward it.

Gojo's true position became the place which had to answer.

He left it.

This time Mau followed the answer rather than the picture.

* * *

Their next exchange destroyed the remainder of the low terrace.

Gojo blocked the hand, shifted the gradient and made the knee arrive late. Mau let the sky-grab close. The thin layer of Amplification returned across his shoulder as he turned through the late step, giving up the other technique's protection to make contact instead. The hit was imperfect, but it was real. Gojo's elbow punished the choice before he could turn it into a second one.

They separated above a new cloud of dust.

Gojo looked pleased enough to make Mau suspicious.

"There you are," he said. ᴳ

The pressure around the dust began dividing into alternating bands.

Mau looked at the spaces between them and realized the next question was already being asked.
