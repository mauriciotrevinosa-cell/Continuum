*When: The dust hangs in strips. One strip falls, the next draws sideways, and the one between them remains nearly still.*

Gojo had two small seeds of Blue moving where the simple version would have put one.

The control between them was the new work. The cursed flow arrived with a relationship he could hold for a few breaths at a time, reinforcement in one place and a narrow cancellation beside it. Moving the pattern cost him attention. Keeping it useful while someone tried to strike his face cost more.

Mau tried the quiet strip.

Gojo shifted the relationship between the fields.

The quiet place moved.

So did the punch waiting at the end of Mau's decision.

* * *

He hit the next terrace hard enough for Beru to look briefly offended on behalf of its continued existence.

Mau got up before the shadow could offer a declaration.

"Don't chase the gap," he said. "Make him move it." ᴹ

Bellion changed the reach of his attack. Igris pressed from the other side. Neither could pretend Infinity had disappeared, but both made Gojo choose how much of the shifting field to devote to throwing them out of the way.

Mau sent a small Red through the opening that choice produced.

He released its direction with the launch. Closed the active work. Took the sky with the next movement rather than trying to keep two different cursed techniques in his hands at once.

The fold changed the route ahead of the already-moving shot.

Red came around the side Gojo had made look like open space.

* * *

Gojo saw it in time.

That did not make the answer free.

He gave up the interference pattern to take the stronger spatial turn which kept the repulsion off him. The narrow quiet strips vanished. Mau's soldiers found ordinary ground again, and the next attack came through it.

Gojo's grin flashed above the broken wall.

"You brought an arrangement." ᴳ

"I'm learning to listen." ᴹ

He let Sky close and used Shrine's familiar cuts across the ground behind Gojo's next step. Not an imaginary promise that a slash through air could simply ignore Infinity. The cuts removed footing and broke stone into the shapes his other authority could move.

Ruler's grip gave the fragments their next direction.

Gojo had to answer a new arrangement before rebuilding the one Mau had disrupted.

* * *

The answer gathered low and dark beneath Gojo's outstretched hand.

Blue became a well with a direction to every useful path around it. It was not a star being made in the basin, nor an event horizon granted by a convincing diagram. It was Gojo making a region of his technique behave like a demand the space could not stop answering.

The broken terrace began coming apart toward it.

Mau caught a larger block with the grip, used its motion to take his weight out of the direct pull and called Kaisel into the air above the collapse.

The dragon caught the new route beneath him.

Gojo looked up.

"Oh, we're going there." ᴳ

* * *

He met them above the basin.

Blue shortened his approach and Red forced the dragon's turn. Mau let Kaisel take the wide line while Kamish rose on the other side, filling the empty dungeon sky with a shape Gojo could not mistake for a piece of broken scenery.

"That one," Gojo said, almost delighted. ᴳ

Kamish answered with fire.

Infinity kept the burning reach from simply becoming contact. The field around it still had to take the position of the dragon into account when Gojo chose where to arrive next.

Mau used that choice.

They met between the two flying shapes, fists and cursed force punching a sudden clear place through the smoke.

* * *

The wheel came after the landing.

Mau closed the foreign technique in his active cursed channel before making the native call. Mahoraga rose from the stone with the weight of something which did not need a speech to be understood.

Gojo's smile thinned into concentration without leaving his face.

"Now you really are enjoying yourself." ᴳ

The first spatial pressure struck the great shikigami and forced it back. Mau let it take what it could survive, watching the state of the body rather than waiting sentimentally for a turn at any cost.

The wheel moved.

Gojo changed the next answer before the adaptation could finish teaching the whole exchange the same lesson.

Red. A new approach for Blue.

The beginning of Purple.

* * *

Mau recalled Mahoraga while it was still alive, before the new output could turn a useful observation into an irreversible loss.

The native call closed. His next technique became available only when he left that work behind.

Purple struck the empty ridge beyond them and took a vast piece of the unoccupied basin with it.

Gojo landed above the damage and looked toward the place the wheel had been.

"You put him away." ᴳ

"I wanted the answer. I don't need to lose him to prove I asked." ᴹ

Gojo laughed.

Then raised his hands in a sign Mau knew very well.
