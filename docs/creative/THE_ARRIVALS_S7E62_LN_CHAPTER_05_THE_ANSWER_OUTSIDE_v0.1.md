# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 41
## Chapter 5 — The Answer Outside

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 revised 2026-10-27
**Volume:** 41
**Reading order:** 5
**Source episode:** S7E62
**Continuity state:** SameDay949. Real Void clash, Gojo refinement wins; Mau's limited Void buys only a brief guard while an already-placed external Monarch anchor permits one costly ShadowExchange before the protection fails. Specific tested cross-system transit, not immunity to every sealed space. Exchange spent for remainder. Both CE channels burn out, Gojo uses one source-known risky brain repair; Mau does not copy. Monarch authority/forces remain distinct, no switching CE techniques to evade burnout. Blades used with their equipment bonuses, no free Infinity penetration. No permanent roster/equipment losses or levels.

*When: Gojo's hands come together. Mau knows which answer a body without a protected place inside it would receive.*

He made his own sign.

The basin vanished between two declarations.

For an instant, the space around Mau held the shape he had reconstructed and practised. A small, fiercely defended refusal to become the recipient of everything the other domain meant to give him.

Across it stood Gojo.

The smile had become very still.

Then Mau felt the difference between understanding the door and having lived in the house.

* * *

His boundary began losing its shape.

He fed it more of the reserve it could use. The edge steadied, then bent again. Gojo's refinement found the imperfect join without needing a lecture to identify it.

Mau could not keep the guard alive long enough to win this way.

He had not sent all his soldiers toward Gojo.

One waited beyond the place where the enclosing wall had risen, near the cut in the outer terrace. Mau had left it there before either sign, an anchor in the real basin rather than a hope that a door would appear when he wanted one.

*Now,* Ciel said.

He let the Monarch route take him while his own narrow protection still held.

* * *

Stone struck his knees.

The place he had left held one ordinary soldier for a fraction of the next moment. Gojo saw the substitution and dismissed the domain before keeping an empty victory became a further expense.

Mau's guard collapsed with the transit.

The sky returned around the dark curve of the vanishing barrier.

He stayed on one knee, breathing hard. His technique answered the next reflex with a raw absence. The attempted Blue did not form.

*Burnout,* Ciel told him. *Changing which cursed technique you select won't mend the channel.*

He looked at the Exchange route.

Spent. Not there for the next mistake.

Gojo landed on the far side of the newly bare ground.

"You put a door outside my room." ᴳ

Mau got up.

"Before you closed it." ᴹ

Gojo looked toward the anchor's former position. His eyes followed something Mau could not see all the way through.

"That detail is going to matter next time." ᴳ

Mau believed him.

* * *

For several breaths the space between them belonged to their bodies.

Mau drew the daggers.

Dragon's Wrath settled into his hands with the familiar change in speed and reach of the work his body could sustain. He had not become stronger because a sword looked impressive. These were the weapons he had paid for; the added demand and their advantage belonged to wearing them.

Gojo met the first rush without Infinity.

He turned the leading wrist, drove his knee into Mau's side and caught the second hand by the forearm before its shorter path could arrive. Mau used the grip instead of fighting to recover his original plan. He turned inside it and made Gojo release him to keep his balance.

Igris reached the flank.

Gojo ducked beneath the knight's cut. Bellion's blade changed its reach behind him; Gojo struck the ground, threw broken stone through the opening and came up against Mau again.

This time Mau recognized the turn late enough to be hit and early enough to keep the second dagger.

They separated with blood on both their mouths.

Gojo laughed through his next breath.

"You're much less polite with those." ᴳ

"You said no limits." ᴹ

"I did." ᴳ

* * *

The repair gathering in Gojo was not the one Mau had been practising on his ribs.

He saw where the operation went.

For a moment the old temptation returned: another answer, another useful piece, one more thing to begin building because the person in front of him already knew how.

Ciel stopped that reach before it became an instruction to Noise.

*Not your brain. Not in the middle of learning what your own recovery will tolerate.*

Mau pulled the thought back.

Across the broken ground, Gojo finished the dangerous repair once. Blood ran beneath his nose. He wiped it with the heel of his hand, and the air around his next step changed.

Infinity returned.

Mau's own cursed channel still had no answer.

* * *

He changed to the katana for its longer line and felt the lost advantage of the daggers as he put them away. The grip was solid. The reach did not promise to cut the space which stood between its edge and Gojo.

He used it to cut a hanging slab loose instead.

Ruler's grip turned the slab into cover. Beru came around it, taking a different height from Bellion. Mau kept moving behind the stone, forcing Gojo to distinguish his body from the shapes arriving beside it.

Blue took the cover apart.

Gojo came through the pieces.

Mau caught the first strike on the flat, felt the steel shudder and abandoned the block before Gojo could turn the whole weapon against his wrist. He kept the blade. Lost the ground.

Kaisel rose beneath him.

Red struck the space his body had occupied.

* * *

The dragon's wing vanished into black fragments.

Mau caught the harness as Kaisel broke shape beneath the damaged flight. Ruler's grip took the fall against a surviving terrace. His feet struck first, badly enough that pain climbed both legs.

Below him, the shadow gathered back toward its source.

Kaisel would return. The reserve paid for it.

The ordinary soldiers Gojo had scattered were asking for the same work. Mau felt their reconstruction as a separate expense from the cursed energy he could not yet deliver properly. Having two supplies had not made either one imaginary.

He recalled the outer ordinary line and kept the named fighters where they could make a difference.

"Don't keep giving him the same height," he told them. ᴹ

Bellion looked up.

Then smiled and changed the attack.

* * *

Gojo had learned the arrangement too.

He no longer committed the whole pull to the first soldier Mau offered. Blue bent along a narrow route, catching the second approach while the first stopped at Infinity. The false outline came apart where Kamish's fire crossed it. Gojo left the distortion before Mau could use that report to place him.

Mau watched his own old answer become insufficient.

He put the katana away and brought the daggers back. Switched their leading hands. Let Beru's sudden rush draw the line which had been waiting for him.

Then used the grip on a fragment below Gojo's heel instead of reaching for Gojo through Infinity.

The ground moved.

Gojo corrected with Blue.

Mau had wanted the correction.

He was already moving toward where it would have to finish.

* * *

He still took the blow.

It hurt less than being late by the whole decision.

The first usable response stirred in his cursed channel as he hit the terrace and rolled. He did not mistake its return for a fresh body or a full reserve. Ciel guided him through a small check; the familiar neutral work held.

Mau repaired enough of the new damage to stand without borrowing a lie from the next breath.

Above him, Gojo had stopped moving.

Blue waited at one hand. Red at the other.

The route between them curved through a field he had already begun arranging across the sky.

Mau looked up, daggers lowered.

"That's unpleasant." ᴹ

Gojo's smile came back, bright and entirely sincere.

"I've been working very hard." ᴳ
