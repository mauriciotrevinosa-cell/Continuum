*When: Purple takes the route Gojo has prepared for it. The place Mau chose to escape stops being outside its path.*

He saw the first bend in the dust before the output arrived.

The field laid across the basin had already made that journey possible. Gojo was not chasing him by wishing a projectile around every new corner. He had arranged a turn and given the attack a way to take it.

Mau had committed to the wrong side of that turn.

He put the daggers away.

Took the sky in both hands.

* * *

He did not catch Purple.

He caught the surface ahead of it while there was still a place his hands could reach. Folded the route out of line with his body. Pulled himself in the other direction with the grip, trusting the movement before his eyes could offer a comfortable picture of it.

The first fold tore.

He let it go and took the next piece farther ahead.

The edge of the passage caught his shoulder. Pain flashed through the arm as the transformed route broke past him and struck the empty height behind the terrace.

The ridge went away in a roar.

Gojo had already left the place Mau might have used for a counterattack.

* * *

Mau landed badly and kept the wounded arm close.

Blood made the old grey world darker along his sleeve.

Ciel gave him the damage she could identify. He closed Sky, put his attention into the fresh wound and repaired the part which would fail if he asked the hand for another hard movement immediately.

Not everything at once.

The effort left him breathing through his mouth. His old fingers still resisted when he opened them. The left edge of the basin remained blurred in the way he knew.

*You can stop,* Ciel said.

He looked across the space where Gojo stood with blood beneath his nose and dust ground into his clothes.

Gojo lifted his chin, asking.

Mau smiled back.

*One more arrangement.*

*Then make it yours.*

* * *

Kamish came low across the basin.

The dragon had learned not to offer Gojo the whole broad approach at the same height as before. Fire crossed the open ground while Bellion's blade made Gojo choose a different departure.

Blue caught the sword's changing reach.

Gojo shortened the route toward Mau instead.

Mau launched Red into the ground between them, gave up active steering and switched to Sky. The released blast came through a folded approach, arriving from the side Gojo's first adjustment had left open.

Gojo answered by shifting the receiving space rather than meeting the blast where Mau wanted it.

The old combination failed.

Mau had expected him to remember it.

* * *

Igris arrived in the departure lane.

Infinity held the sword. Gojo used the knight's commitment to begin pulling him out of the arrangement.

Mau closed Sky. The cuts of Shrine went through the terrace below them, releasing a shelf instead of making the impossible claim that every slash had become the one he had sacrificed.

Gojo's eyes followed the ground.

Ruler's grip moved the released stone upward.

The slab turned under Igris, changing the knight's path while Blue still acted upon it. Kamish's body crossed above. Bellion attacked from the place he had left empty the first time.

Gojo sent Red through the approaching shelf.

It burst into fragments.

Mau came through the fragments with his own technique already closed.

* * *

Amplification met Infinity.

Gojo met Mau.

The first blow glanced from the neutralizing layer. The second found his side as he changed the place he was protecting. Mau felt a rib give and kept the arm moving anyway, not because pain had stopped mattering but because the hand could still reach the next necessary place.

Gojo caught his wrist.

Mau turned with the catch, the movement Yuta had made him repeat until he hated how slowly the lesson went. It did not become faster than Gojo's experience. It stopped wasting the part of the motion he had already been given.

His forehead caught Gojo above the brow.

They broke apart bleeding.

Gojo laughed once, sharp with the effort of it.

"Very elegant." ᴳ

"I've been studying." ᴹ

* * *

The Blue gathering above them was smaller than the well which had eaten the terrace.

Its shape was worse.

Gojo had stopped asking the field to drag every direction toward the same answer. The narrow gradient ran through the place Mau needed for a second approach. One step would put him inside the pull. The other gave Gojo the angle for Red.

Mau let the layer fall.

Built the familiar convergence of his own Limitless instead.

Gojo saw it and rose, forcing the aim to travel away from the ground he had just prepared.

Mau followed the change with the ordinary geometry he already knew. No borrowed lens. No new field half built because it looked beautiful in someone else's hands.

Purple left him with a violent light he could only see as brightness.

Gojo's counter crossed it high above the ruined basin.

* * *

The collision took the remaining dust and made the air above them clear for a single terrible instant.

Kamish broke shape at the edge of the blast. Mau felt the cost of calling the dragon back toward repair and closed the demand before reflex could spend the rest on making him stand there again immediately.

The two fighters fell out of the torn approaches on different sides of the basin.

Gojo caught himself first.

Mau used the grip on a broken pillar to make the fall a hard landing rather than an ending. He got one foot under him. Then the other.

The arm would hold.

The reserve would not hold many more answers of that size.

Across the ground, Gojo wiped blood from his eye.

They looked at each other.

Both started forward.

* * *

The next fight was close enough for Mau to hear Gojo breathe.

He stopped asking the daggers to be useful against a defense which required his hands to do another job. Kept them stored. Let the neutralizing layer take its limited place at contact while his body and the grip did the movement.

Gojo shifted the distance.

Mau let Bellion's blade strike the slab behind his own heel. The fragments gave the grip a moving foothold, turning the short step Gojo had lengthened into a pivot around something else.

Gojo caught that change too.

His shoulder moved for the counter before Mau had finished the turn.

Mau saw it, abandoned the punch and let the wasted arm become the hand which took Gojo's wrist.

* * *

Infinity strained against the thin place Amplification could actually cover.

Mau could not neutralize the whole person. He kept contact with the wrist and paid for it when Gojo's free hand struck him hard enough to blur the good part of his vision.

Igris drove his sword into the stone at Gojo's retreating heel.

Not through Infinity. Into the ground.

Gojo chose the other direction.

Beru, who had endured an entire fight of being told where not to stand, finally reached the place Mau had been saving for him.

The insect did not try to cut Gojo through a defense which still worked. He braced his king's back as Mau changed the wrist grip into a turn of the whole body.

Mau's knee took the ground beyond Gojo's leg.

The next contact came at the shoulder, the layer moved with it, and both men hit the broken terrace.

* * *

Gojo started Red with the hand Mau had failed to catch.

Mau drove his own forearm across its line, keeping Amplification at the place where the operation would have to reach him. He could not do that and repair the rib. Could not do that and put up his own Infinity. He kept the choice he had made.

The first surge burned across his arm before the neutralizing layer steadied.

Gojo tried the hip turn.

Mau shifted with it.

Tried the shoulder.

Mau lost the angle for a breath, recovered it with Beru's support against the stone and felt exactly how little remained between having the position and losing it.

Gojo stopped gathering the next output.

His free hand opened.

"Your round." ᴳ

Then released the layer and fell sideways onto the stone beside him.

Beru reached down as though uncertain which part of victory ought to be lifted first.

Gojo lay on his back, looking at the space where the ridge had been.

"I liked the sky better when there was more of it in the way." ᴳ

Mau called the ordinary soldiers back. Counted the named answers, including the shapes waiting on repair. Checked the native call separately; Mahoraga remained alive, the brief progress retained without making him immune to an afternoon he had not finished experiencing.

His weapons were all where he had stored them.

There were no points for hitting his teacher. No corpse to raise. The basin had given them a very large bill in effort and nothing to sell.

"I need to work on the turn after the lens." ᴳ
