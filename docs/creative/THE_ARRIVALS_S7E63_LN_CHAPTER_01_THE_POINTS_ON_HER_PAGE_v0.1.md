He looked at the columns.

Then at her.

"We've been calculating for a while." ᴹ

"Good. That gives us something to check." ᵁ

Yuka sat on the other side of the table. Taihei brought the cups and stayed when Umaru made room without pretending she had not been expecting him.

Mau opened the part of the Window they had agreed to share.

One hundred and twenty-six. One hundred and fifty-seven points held.

For once the next line was not unfinished in the place they needed to understand.

* * *

The brigade work had continued between the larger days.

Six clears, recorded when the people came back rather than invented after somebody admired how many soldiers could stand in a courtyard. Mau had gone with alternating human companions; Anko and Mikasa had made sure the routes and returns were somebody's job. Igris and Beru had worked different rooms instead of both being described as the one who did everything.

Ninety-seven new ordinary units from the first. One hundred and twenty-one from the next. One hundred and eight, then one hundred and seventeen. Ninety-six. Sixty-four today.

Six hundred and three additions, not six hundred and three new people somebody had taken from the road.

Nine hundred and eighty ordinary soldiers now. Sixteen named. Mahoraga remained a different call.

Bellion had begun giving the hauling units tasks which did not require every capable fighter to carry the same crate. Muster's work mattered when a damaged shape needed rebuilding. None of that made a repaired soldier free to reconstruct or every soldier as strong as Beru.

* * *

The crystals had their own page.

Three hundred and eighteen smaller pieces added across the runs, with each larger reward kept identified. Six hundred and three small usable pieces in stock now, four older pylon pieces and twelve larger rewards of separate sources.

The piece spent on the lamp stayed spent.

Taihei checked that Umaru had not used the troop total in the column for fuel. She looked offended, then checked it herself.

Mau wisely drank from his cup.

* * *

Noise's completed work was less simple than changing the heading from one kind of energy to all of them.

The Window could now map the improvement to the separate channels they had tested: the shadow reserve, the capacity of his own mana and the handling of cursed energy. Each still had its own demands. A wider supply did not mean every route could deliver it at once, or that a new technique came with the understanding to use it.

Ciel confirmed the tests privately. Mau repeated what belonged to the decision.

Umaru turned the page.

Twenty for strength. Twenty for dexterity. Twenty for perception. Twenty for vitality. Twenty for intelligence.

One hundred deliberately spent, with fifty-seven kept because having a plan did not require making every future decision today.

Yuka looked at him before he confirmed.

"What do you want to check first?" she asked. ʸᵏ

He considered the answer rather than choosing the most impressive one.

"Movement. Then what feels different in the channels. No fight today." ᴹ

Umaru pushed the page into the place where he could reach it.

* * *

The change did not disappear when the Window closed.

He stood carefully. The floor remained beneath his feet, but the ordinary movement of rising had a different margin inside it. Intelligence read four hundred and sixty when he checked the figure again.

His stiff fingers did not become somebody else's hands. Yuka was still the part of the world which kept its color when she stood beside him.

They went outside to walk the marked distance before trying anything larger.

Taihei watched them go, then looked at Umaru's page.

"You kept him from spending everything." ᵀᵃ

"I have been preparing for this moment." ᵁ

Mau heard the answer through the open door and started laughing before Yuka could ask what he had noticed.
