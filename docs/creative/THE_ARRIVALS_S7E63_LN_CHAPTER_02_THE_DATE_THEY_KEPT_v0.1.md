*When: Eight days before Mau's chosen birthday. Fern puts the old calendar beside the new sheet, and Yuka recognizes the mark he has been pretending not to count.*

The date had belonged to the house before it belonged to any explanation of what Mau was.

It was the day they had chosen for him, carried from one calendar to the next. They did not need to recover his birth to keep it. This year's circle had become a useful place to put the plans which had once looked impossibly far away.

The key's opening followed it.

Yuka looked at Mau.

He touched the edge of the table rather than the key.

"I know." ᴹ

She sat beside him.

"Good. Then we can talk about both." ʸᵏ

* * *

The expedition had four names on it.

Mau. Yuka. Gojo. Yuta.

Rimuru stayed outside with Maki coordinating the house. Shoko would receive them; Frieren would help watch the ground and the route rather than leaving every useful person at the same doorway. They checked the roles before anyone began explaining why the strongest people should all be wherever the most exciting thing happened.

The original conditions still permitted companions.

They still permitted waiting.

Yuka read both lines herself. Gojo asked them to keep a copy of what they were seeing rather than trust that tomorrow's interface would remain identical because today's had been polite.

Mau looked at him too quickly.

Gojo noticed but only tapped the page.

"Keep what it actually says." ᴳ

* * *

Ciel found the shared administrative marker while they compared the key's status with the ordinary dungeon installation.

Mau and Rimuru brought that finding to the others. It was a connection between these local procedures, not a signature written over the whole world. It showed that something could speak to both places. It did not show what permissions it had or who would use them.

Senku wanted to observe it without changing the live installation first.

Fern wanted every person back outside before they tested anything involving access.

They agreed on both.

Mau kept his hands on the table until the discussion moved on.

* * *

DIO chose the ordinary house for the expedition days.

She had begun taking longer walks inside the protected grounds. Shoko still asked questions before she made any of them an argument about how much better she felt.

"You don't have to carry me everywhere because you offered a room," DIO told him. ᴰⁱᵒ

Mau nodded.

"I was going to ask." ᴹ

"You are asking. I am answering." ᴰⁱᵒ

The second small chamber had been built from the design they now knew, for a person who might need temporary shelter without occupying her private space. They tested the unassigned room with objects and the same return limits. No one was already living in it. No one called it a reason to collect a stranger who had not agreed to come.

DIO inspected the opening from outside and left her own chair where she liked it.

* * *

The birthday preparations were easier to hear than the gate preparations.

Milim asked a question too loudly. Umaru accused somebody of borrowing an idea from a previous war. Bocchi caught Mau looking toward the music room and shut the door with an apology which made the action considerably more suspicious.

Yuka was waiting for him by the new corner that evening.

She had the map open and a blank sheet beneath it.

"Not for you to read yet," she said when he looked. ʸᵏ

He sat down without reaching.

"I'm learning." ᴹ

"That's why I still let you sit here." ʸᵏ

She leaned against him while he told her what had happened with the scout routes that day. When he stopped speaking, she kept her hand in his until he remembered there was no need to put the silence to work.
