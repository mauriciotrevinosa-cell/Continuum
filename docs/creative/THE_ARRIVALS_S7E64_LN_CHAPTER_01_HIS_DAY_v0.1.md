## Chapter 9 — His Day

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)

**Reading order:** 9

**Continuity state:** B, existing chosen birthday morning. Birthday not biological age; household/date retained, no level/coin/armor gift or new power. DIO physicalhome by choice, rooms/relationships retained. Yuka finishing handmade scarf with household help, not delivered until V41#11. Original gate terms still wait/companions, opening tomorrow.

*When: The morning of Mau's chosen birthday. Rimuru tells him not to come downstairs yet and then immediately forgets the reason he was supposed to give.*

Mau looked toward the half-open door of their room. Yuka had got up before him to help downstairs. Rimuru stood against the doorframe with an expression which suggested he had been given a very simple task and was making it difficult.

"Is something wrong?" ᴹ

"No." ᴿ

"Then why can't I come downstairs?" ᴹ

There was a pause.

From beyond the door came Milim's voice telling somebody that secrecy worked better if they were quieter about it.

Rimuru glanced toward the stairs.

"I have been betrayed by the organization." ᴿ

Mau began laughing and sat on the edge of the bed to finish his sleeve.

* * *

They did let him out.

Ori reached him before the stairs and gave him a folded card which Milim had apparently interpreted as an opportunity to improve the size of every powerful creature on the page.

Mau found himself between several enormous shapes and a very small table.

"That's because you kept saying you were going to sit down," Ori explained. ᴼ

"Was I successful?" ᴹ

She pointed to the chair.

He thanked them properly, which included listening while Milim explained that the smallest dragon was still extremely dangerous despite the shortage of paper.

At the bottom of the stairs, Yuka waited with no visible gift in her hands.

She looked at him as if checking something she had wanted to see for several days.

"Happy birthday," she said. ʸᵏ

Then kissed him before he could turn the greeting into a joke about advanced information.

* * *

The table had a place for him which did not put every person within reach of his plate.

That was fortunate. There were things to taste, people to listen to and a considerable amount of discussion about whether the card's dragon anatomy had been unfairly limited by the artist's materials.

Yuta rested a hand on his shoulder as he passed. Maki moved a cup before somebody's generous gesture turned it into part of the floor. Mai caught the look on Yuka's face and said she could go finish the thing rather than guarding its absence all morning.

"After breakfast," Yuka said. ʸᵏ

Mau looked at her.

She looked back.

"No questions." ʸᵏ

He took another bite.

* * *

People found him during the morning without making a queue.

Bocchi asked if he would listen later, with the others, when the part he had heard unfinished finally arrived in its proper place. He told her he wanted to. Umaru informed him that all numerical planning was suspended until tomorrow unless the numbers concerned food or a game she was winning.

Frieren looked at the card and asked why he was so much smaller than the table.

"Artistic perspective," he said. ᴹ

"Milim perspective," she said. ᶠʳ

Ori laughed and took the card back for a moment to show her the part on the other side.

He watched them bend over it together. Frieren's hand kept the crease from closing over Ori's fingers.

The old ache could be there without taking this morning away from them.

* * *

For a little while the birthday was only the birthday.

He carried his own cup back, sat when someone made room and let Yuka leave to finish whatever she had been working on. She pressed his shoulder as she passed behind him, then leaned down.

"I want you here when I come back," she said. ʸᵏ

"I have several people prepared to enforce that." ᴹ

She smiled.

He watched her go with Mai and waited until the door had closed before asking Yuta whether he had a moment.

Yuta looked at the others.

Maki put down the thing she had been sorting. Mai had not gone far; he could hear her tell Yuka she would be back before the last seam needed checking.

Mau stood carefully enough that Maki noticed.
