*When: Mau's birthday, late morning. He begins by thanking them, which makes Yuta smile and Maki wait.*

They had found a place out of the busiest part of the common room. Mai checked that Yuka was still occupied before taking the last chair.

Mau had meant to begin naturally.

Now all three were looking at him.

"For helping her," he said. "With today. Her birthday. All the parts where she wanted something to be right and didn't want me to see her worrying about it." ᴹ

Mai's expression softened before she could decide whether he deserved to watch it happen.

"She cares whether you're happy," she said. ᴹᵃⁱ

"I know. I wanted to say I see what you do for her too." ᴹ

Yuta sat back a little.

For a moment, that was enough of a conversation to stay inside.

* * *

Then Mau said the other thing.

"I want to ask her to marry me." ᴹ

Mai stopped moving the thread she had brought with her.

Yuta looked at him, then at Maki, as if he wanted to check that she had heard the same words before trusting himself with an answer.

Maki did not look away from Mau.

"You want to ask her," she said. ᴹᵃ

He nodded.

"Not today. I haven't decided how. I wanted to talk to you first." ᴹ

"What have you talked about with her?" Yuta asked. ʸᵗ

Mau answered without giving them her private confidence. A future together. Where they might live. Things they wanted to be able to choose, with the other person there.

He did not say any of those things had already supplied her answer to the question he had not asked.

* * *

The questions came faster after that.

Was he waiting for an occasion or ready to invent one? Did he know what she would want from the day? Had he thought about whether the asking would be private? Did he intend to turn a celebration into a situation she could not refuse without disappointing everybody in the room?

"No audience deciding her answer," he said at once. ᴹ

Mai nodded.

Yuta asked another question and Maki put a hand against his arm before it could become the same question in a more complicated coat.

"Let him finish an answer." ᴹᵃ

Mau laughed, briefly relieved.

Then asked the thing he had not intended to make sound so bare.

"Would you be happy if I did? If she said yes?" ᴹ

* * *

Mai answered first.

"Yes." ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuta's face changed in a way Mau could not bear to look at for very long.

"Yes," he said. "I want her to have someone she can keep choosing. I think she found him." ʸᵗ

Mau put his hands together beneath the table.

Maki looked at them.

Then back at his face.

"I'm happy she loves you," she said. "I'm happy you love her. That doesn't mean we choose for her." ᴹᵃ

"I know." ᴹ

"And I want to know why you sound as though you're trying to leave the answer with us." ᴹᵃ

* * *

The relief did not disappear. It became harder to hold beside the thing he had hoped not to bring into the morning.

Mau looked toward the door.

"The marriage part is real," he said. ᴹ

"I didn't say it wasn't." ᴹᵃ

He nodded.

"There's something about tomorrow I should have talked about before. Not something that happened this morning. Something I don't know how to prove." ᴹ

Yuta leaned forward again.

Mau looked at him, then at Mai's thread.

"She's trying so hard with today. Can we finish today before I make it about the gate?" ᴹ

Maki stayed quiet long enough that he knew she was deciding what she had actually heard, not accepting whatever arrangement would make him look less frightened.

"Are you hurt? Something new?" ᴹᵃ

"No." ᴹ

"Does anybody need to act now?" ᴹᵃ

"I don't know of anything happening now." ᴹ

She watched him answer.

* * *

"Tonight," she said. "Before you go to bed. You find me, or I find you." ᴹᵃ

He nodded.

Yuta kept looking at him. Mau wanted to make the room easier for him, but the first reassuring sentence which came to mind would have been a promise he did not understand well enough to give.

He stayed seated until Yuta touched his shoulder.

"The first answer doesn't change because there's another conversation," Yuta said. ʸᵗ

Mau looked up.

Mai gathered the thread.

"And don't give her a ring that catches on the rope," she said. ᴹᵃⁱ

He laughed before he could stop the breath catching behind it.

"I might need help with that." ᴹ

"Obviously." ᴹᵃⁱ

She went back to Yuka, leaving him with a yes he had wanted and an appointment he could no longer pretend was vague.
