*When: Mau's birthday, afternoon. Yuka takes the wrapping back because he is trying too hard not to tear it.*

"You can open it," she said. ʸᵏ

"I'm opening it carefully." ᴹ

"You're negotiating with a knot." ʸᵏ

She undid it herself and put the bundle in his hands.

The scarf was soft and ordinary. One edge had the small difference which came from someone making the same movement many times and getting better after the first part was already done. Near the end, a raised line crossed the fabric where his thumb would find it.

He ran his fingers over it.

Yuka watched his hand.

"Marin helped. Mai fixed the part I thought wasn't going to hold." ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"You made this." ᴹ

She nodded, suddenly less certain than she had been while rescuing the wrapping.

* * *

He put it on.

Badly enough that she laughed and came close to arrange it. He let her. Her hands moved against his collar, tucking the fabric so it would sit instead of sliding onto the thing he was trying to carry.

"It isn't armor," she said. ʸᵏ

"I noticed." ᴹ

"Or a test." ʸᵏ

He looked down at her.

She checked the raised line with one finger, then lifted her eyes.

"I wanted you to have something you could reach for because you were cold. That's all." ʸᵏ

For a moment he could not make a joke small enough to belong to the answer.

He drew her into his arms instead.

* * *

The afternoon did not wait respectfully for the hug to end.

Milim wanted to know whether they could eat the next thing. Someone told her what time it was, and Umaru began explaining why time ought not to be used selectively against one side of an argument.

Mau laughed against Yuka's hair.

She stayed there for another breath, then took his hand to lead him back.

At the table, Mai inspected how the scarf sat. Marin looked pleased without trying to claim the crooked edge as her own professional work. Yuta asked whether he was warm enough now, and Maki said he was allowed to remove it while eating if he intended to keep it free of sauce.

Yuka looked at the food.

Then at the scarf.

"She's right," she said. ʸᵏ

He folded it over the back of his chair where he could still reach it.

* * *

Bocchi's part arrived exactly where he remembered waiting for it.

With the band around her, the space she had been brave enough to leave open became someone else's sound and then hers again. Mau saw her glance toward him and lifted a hand without making her stop being a musician to receive a brother's praise.

Afterward he told her the part had worked.

She argued for a little while about which part he meant. He let her explain it properly.

Yuka sat beside him and listened with them, her knee resting against his.

The day was still happening.

* * *

Near the end of the meal, he looked around the table and thought about a wedding without meaning to.

Not this table. A remembered room, a face waiting for him in a life which had been built to be lived inside a test.

He took a careful breath.

Yuka touched his hand under the edge of the table.

"Here?" she asked quietly. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"Here." ᴹ

She did not make him explain the memory before letting him stay.

He listened to the next story. Laughed when Taihei gave up trying to defend his version of it. Took his own plate back when the meal was done.

The scarf was waiting over the chair when he returned.

He put it on again.

Across the room, Maki saw him look toward the stairs and stood.
