## Chapter 8 — The Other Question

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 41
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S7E65
**Continuity state:** B late night. Maki follows up arrangedconversation, Mau distinguishes true marriage wish from fear of Architect measuring vessel. Identifies katana encounter from A disclosed tower, not intimate alliance/creatorDIOconfusion. CallsYuka/Rimuru for meeting; no detention or full reveal already resolved. No actual possession/prophecy proven.

*When: Late on Mau's birthday. Maki catches him before the first stair and asks whether he was coming to find her.*

He looked at her.

"I was going to." ᴹ

"Good. This saves us a journey." ᴹᵃ

She moved to the quieter side of the room. He followed without making her ask whether he intended to leave through another door.

Yuka was helping put away the last things from the table. When she looked over, he raised a hand to say he had seen her. Maki waited until he had stopped using the distance as something to watch.

"Tell me," she said. ᴹᵃ

* * *

He began with the easiest part to keep true.

"I want to marry her." ᴹ

Maki did not change expression.

"I believed you this morning." ᴹᵃ

He nodded, then rubbed one thumb across the other hand.

"I think tomorrow's gate might change the rules when we get there." ᴹ

"Because?" ᴹᵃ

"Because of the one who made the test. The way he was looking at what I could become." ᴹ

Maki's eyes sharpened.

"Which one?" ᴹᵃ

"The Architect. Not DIO. The one from the tower. The one I had the katana against before he put the heart in me." ᴹ

He stopped long enough to make sure that had not become a claim that he knew the man well.

"We weren't friends. I don't know how he thinks. I know what he was trying to measure." ᴹ

* * *

Maki had heard about the tower.

She had heard about the heart, the things that came back and the bodies which could make a familiar face look like a door something else had opened. None of that made the sentence he had just said sound like a detail everyone already possessed.

"What was he measuring?" she asked. ᴹᵃ

Mau looked down.

"Whether I could be a monarch. Or carry one. I don't know which part he expected to leave of me when it was done." ᴹ

Maki let one breath pass.

"Did he say that, or is that what you think he meant?" ᴹᵃ

He looked up at her.

The difference was so important that for a moment he could not make either version shorter.

"He called me a vessel." ᴹ

* * *

Maki turned toward the room.

"Yuka. Rimuru." ᴹᵃ

Both looked up.

Mau's hand tightened against the scarf before he noticed what he was doing.

Maki faced him again.

"You stay here. We're not doing the part where you decide how little of the answer will let everyone go to bed." ᴹᵃ

He took a breath.

Then nodded.

Yuka reached them first. Her eyes went from her grandmother to his face.

"What happened?" ʸᵏ

"He has something he needs to tell us before tomorrow," Maki said. "Stay with him. I'll get the others we need." ᴹᵃ

Yuka took his hand, not his wrist.

He did not pull away.

* * *

Rimuru moved a chair into the quiet part of the common room. Yuta came down before someone had finished asking for him. Shoko checked Mau's face, saw him shake his head at the immediate question and remained without making his terror a physical wound she could press until it explained itself.

Mau sat.

Yuka sat beside him.

He looked at her hand around his and knew how much easier it would have been to tell her when the fear was still small enough to fit inside an ordinary afternoon.

Maki returned to the table.

"Start with the words," she said. ᴹᵃ
