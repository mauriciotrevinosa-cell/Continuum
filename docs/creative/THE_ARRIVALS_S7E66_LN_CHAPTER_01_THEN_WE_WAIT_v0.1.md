*When: The night of Mau's birthday. Maki begins before anyone can make the gathering about tomorrow's packing.*

"He thinks the Architect was measuring whether he could carry a monarch," she said. "He hasn't told us why." ᴹᵃ

Yuka's fingers tightened around his.

Mau looked at the table. Then made himself look at the people sitting around it.

"He called me a vessel. For his monarch, who rests." ᴹ

The words sounded smaller in this room than they had in his head.

They did not hurt less.

* * *

He gave them the rest carefully.

The door the Architect said someone had left in him. The instruction to grow. The unfinished sentence about when he had grown enough.

He did not complete it for them as though fear had made the missing words a memory. He explained the dreams, the times a larger number had brought cold instead of satisfaction, the possibility he could not prove: that every improvement was making him more suitable for somebody else to use.

Rimuru put his cup down.

"Have you felt someone take control?" ᴿ

"No. I've been frightened it could happen. That's different." ᴹ

He could feel Ciel listening through the present, not taking the old scene from him without asking.

Yuta looked toward the place where the key had been set aside.

"Meet the Monarch. Does it mean you?" ʸᵗ

Mau shook his head.

"I don't think so. He said a vessel for his monarch. I don't know what he thinks happens to the person already there." ᴹ

* * *

Nobody asked him to repeat the whole tower.

They knew it had hurt him. They knew where the second heart came from. The new part was how long he had been hearing those sentences while they discussed what he would do next.

Shoko asked whether he could name a physical change that came with the worst moments. He answered what he knew and left the gaps empty. Gojo asked where the words ended and his inference began.

Mau told him.

Maki stayed quiet until the distinction was clear enough that nobody could turn his terror into a diagnosis or dismiss it because the diagnosis was missing.

Then Yuka spoke.

"Why didn't you tell me?" ʸᵏ

* * *

He had answers which had seemed careful while he was alone with them.

He could not prove it. There had been so many good days. She was working hard, learning, trusting him with the part of her future which had begun to feel possible again.

Under her eyes, the answers became what he had used to wait.

"I didn't want to give you a thing neither of us could fix," he said. ᴹ

"I was already there when you stopped hearing me. I just didn't know what you were hearing instead." ʸᵏ

He looked down at their hands.

"I'm sorry." ᴹ

She let the apology arrive without telling him it had made the last months easy.

"I don't need to know how to fix everything before you let me know you're frightened." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She waited until he looked up again.

"Then don't go tomorrow." ʸᵏ

* * *

The room held still around the question.

Mau could have begun with the reasons he had trained. The things the house used. The names of people who might be safer if he understood what he was becoming.

He looked at Yuka.

"Okay," he said. ᴹ

She did not move.

"Mau." ʸᵏ

"We can wait. It says we can wait." ᴹ

Rimuru looked from him to the key.

Mau went on before anybody could make the answer something offered only to quiet her.

"I want to stay. I want to find another way to understand it before I walk into his room again." ᴹ

Yuka's mouth trembled. She pressed it closed and nodded once.

* * *

They changed the plan.

Not a pause everyone pretended to accept while expecting him to disappear at dawn. The expedition packing stayed closed. Gojo moved his name from the entry line to the observation work. Rimuru asked which checks they could make without entering. Fern put the ordinary household work back where the assumed absence had displaced it.

Mau took part.

He asked Ciel what could be observed from outside. He suggested they isolate the key from the experiments on the installation rather than poke every connection at once. He told Yuka she did not have to remain awake holding his hand to make the choice stay true.

She held it anyway for another moment.

* * *

Later, when the urgent part had become a list small enough for different people to carry, they remained beside two empty cups.

Yuka touched the scarf where he had folded it away from the table.

"I didn't want this to be what you remembered about today," she said. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"I remember you making me warm." ᴹ

Her eyes closed briefly.

He stayed beside her until they were ready to stand, and the key remained where they had decided to leave it.
