# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 41
## Chapter 15 — What I Can Promise

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-27)
**Volume:** 41
**Reading order:** 15
**Source episode:** S7E67
**Continuity state:** B+1 morning. Deliberation compares staying/alternate infrastructure with reaching admin; Mau chooses under coercion, not obligation to buyfood. Yuka reluctantly accepts while still not wanting it, family future recalled, no false guaranteed return vow. DIO physicalhome, bothinnercells empty; no living passenger to bypass Operator-only. Outsidecrew retains roles; Yuka ownCTsource not revoked, consciously reserves demand.

*When: The morning after Mau's birthday. Yuka asks the question after the others have finished making the strongest version of both choices.*

"If we leave it there, can we live?" ʸᵏ

Fern answered first.

"Yes." ᶠ

Rimuru nodded.

"We can make that work. Harder isn't the same as impossible." ᴿ

Yuka looked at Mau.

He was grateful they had said it without making him say it for them.

"Then don't make this about saving us from being hungry," she said. ʸᵏ

"I won't." ᴹ

* * *

He told them why he still wanted to enter.

The first choice had been made while they believed the boundary of the problem was a frightening name and an uncertain memory. They now knew something answering to that procedure could act on the connected installation and change what it had said.

He wanted to reach it. Learn what it thought it had built in him. Look for a way to end that access rather than keep making a life around whatever it might interrupt next.

He could not promise the gate would provide those answers. Staying and investigating from outside remained a real option. Choosing to enter did not make the first choice cowardly or wrong.

Maki asked what he would do if the room began making new demands on his body.

"Treat it as the enemy's rule. Not something I owe it because it called me an operator." ᴹ

Gojo watched his face while he answered.

Then nodded once.

* * *

They prepared the part the new rule had not taken away.

People outside would record the time, watch both installations, keep the ground clear and decide on intervention from what they could observe. A rescue plan did not require pretending Gojo could open any space merely because he was himself again.

DIO stayed in the protected physical house. Both interior rooms were checked empty. Mau would not bring a hidden guest through a rule which named him alone.

Ciel would remain with him locally. They expected communication might fail across an unknown instance and would not turn silence into proof of death. The stored soldiers remained his; the question of whether they could manifest inside was one they would have to test there.

Yuka checked her own working pattern once more.

Then closed it.

"I'll keep the demand down unless we need it," she said. "That's my choice." ʸᵏ

Mau nodded.

* * *

They found a little space to speak before he went to the threshold.

Yuka took the scarf off his neck and folded it with hands which were not steady enough for the care she was trying to give it.

"I'll keep this here," she said. ʸᵏ

He let her.

For a moment she looked at the raised line near the edge rather than at him.

"I still don't want you to go." ʸᵏ

"I know." ᴹ

"Agreeing doesn't make me want it." ʸᵏ

He put his hands around hers, careful of the fabric between them.

"I know." ᴹ

* * *

She looked up.

"We talked about a family. You remember?" ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"I remember." ᴹ

"Then promise you'll come back." ʸᵏ

The answer he wanted to give hurt enough that he closed his eyes before it could become a lie.

"I can't promise a thing I don't control." ᴹ

Her face changed.

"Mau. Please." ʸᵏ

He leaned close enough for her to hear him without making the room hear too.

"I don't want to give you a beautiful answer and leave you with it if I couldn't make it true." ᴹ

She looked at him through tears she was still trying to stop.

He kept his hands where she could feel them.

"I will do everything in my power to come back to you. Every choice I can make. That's what I can promise." ᴹ

* * *

She kissed him before replying.

Hard enough that he could feel how much she had not managed to say. He held her until she moved back on her own.

"Then do all of it," she said. ʸᵏ

He nodded.

She wiped her face with the edge of her sleeve, handed the scarf to Maki and walked with him to the gate instead of letting the last thing he saw be her turning away.

Yuta put a hand on his shoulder. Gojo said something ordinary about making sure the man waiting inside understood how inconvenient he had become.

Mau laughed once, because they had left him room to.

Then he crossed.

Behind him, the message changed.

**Meet the Monarch — Trial started.**

**Participant: Operator.**

**Designation: Future Monarch.**
