*When: The instruments begin. The next warden moves before the broken hand of the first has stopped sliding across the floor.*

Mau ducked under a spear and found the shield behind it coming toward his face.

He turned. The edge grazed his shoulder hard enough to make the joint complain. His answer struck the shield where an ordinary piece of stone should have split.

It held.

Ciel followed the pulse travelling through the joined marks beneath it.

*The core is being supplied through the room. Break the supporting joint before you spend another strike on the whole shield.*

He stepped inside the return swing, drove his heel against the leg and hit the exposed anchor as the warden bent.

This time it broke.

* * *

The next three did not offer him the same arrangement.

One stayed back with reach. One cut the space before his landing. The third waited for the moment he would need to put weight on the hand he had already scraped.

Mau saw it soon enough to make the mistake smaller.

Not soon enough to avoid the impact entirely.

He hit the ground on his side, pain flashing through the place Gojo's exercise had taught him to respect. There would be no prepared repair until the field released it.

He rolled before the spear came down.

The weapon drove into the stone.

Mau caught its haft to change the wielder's balance, never taking it as his own blade. The low warden struck its ally where he had been. Two anchors came within reach in the same broken turn.

He made the opening count.

* * *

Outside, Yuka finished the first circuit of the clear ground and came back to the place where the time was being kept.

No message.

Gojo had checked the threshold without making the attempt an uncontrolled attack on the opening. Yuta remained beside him. Rimuru was receiving the ordinary perimeter reports so somebody still heard when nothing had changed elsewhere.

Yuka put her hands on the table until they stopped trying to close around something.

"The route still feels there," she said. ʸᵏ

Yuta looked at her.

"Then that's what we know. It isn't the whole answer." ʸᵗ

She nodded, hated him for a moment for being careful and loved him for not giving her a certainty he had invented.

Then went to check the people near the return space again.

* * *

Inside, the Architect spoke while the wardens kept Mau moving.

"He did not begin as a monarch. He served the one who made the war. He remained when the others raised their weapons against their maker." ᴬʳ

Mau drove a stone elbow away from his throat.

"Did you ask him to tell you that?" ᴹ

The voice continued as if the question had been a flaw in the room's acoustics.

A loyal servant left broken. A hidden power found at the edge of death. A return to a throne whose master was no longer there.

The story arrived as a justification for the room being used now. Mau heard the shape of what was missing even while the next warden tried to crush his hand against the floor.

Nobody in the story had asked the tired man what he wanted afterward.

* * *

Mau stopped chasing the bodies around the room.

He made the spear warden turn around its own lodged weapon. Made another step across the fractured ground where its weight would finish the break he had begun. The movements he had learned to read against Gojo belonged here too, without Gojo's mercy at the end of a clean exchange.

He could not afford to admire a result.

A fifth anchor. A sixth.

The seventh took skin from his knuckles when the protective surface failed under the strike. He changed the hand he led with and kept the other open until the next grip required it.

The remaining wardens pressed harder when the space began emptying.

He used the empty space.

* * *

The last warden went down near the foot of the central seat.

Mau stood above it with breath scraping against his injured side. Twelve walkers lay broken now. Their weapons had not become trophies in his hands.

The music continued.

Five anchors still fed the marks beneath his feet: the four seated figures and the enormous thing watching from the throne.

He looked up.

Its fixed smile had not changed.

The light beginning behind its eyes had.
