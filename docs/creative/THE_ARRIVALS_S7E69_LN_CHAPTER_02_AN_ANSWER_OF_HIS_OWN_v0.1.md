Mau explained the return, the way to call and the places the room did not open onto.

He explained the limit he could not make romantic: a refuge attached to him was not a promise that every dangerous place he might ever enter would know how to contain its guests safely. They would discuss travel. Ashborn would not become something carried without a choice.

"Something of the choice he failed to understand. You may see it when you are ready. It isn't an instruction to repeat it." ᴬˢ

The temple came back before he could decide what he would say to the man who had prepared it.
