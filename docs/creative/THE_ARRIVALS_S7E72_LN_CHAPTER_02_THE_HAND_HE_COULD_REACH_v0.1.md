*When: The fourth morning after his return. Mau asks for a little quiet and does not pretend it is because he has more work to do.*

Yuka asked whether he wanted her nearby.

He did.

She stayed in the common room with the book she had brought, far enough away that he did not have to perform his grief for her and close enough that the house did not disappear behind it.

He sat where his side could rest.

Inside, Ciel asked before joining him.

*I want to look at this part myself,* he told her. *You don't have to see it.*

*I'll be here when you come back to the room.*

He thanked her.

* * *

He did not ask Noise to make a bride.

He let himself remember.

The flowers had been white and blue. He knew the colors because he remembered making them, not because the grey world had suddenly decided to give him something back. The frost lay where it had lain. The people came up through the trees in the order his mind still knew.

For years inside that life, he had believed this was a day they could reach.

He could remember the happiness without declaring that the test had therefore been kind.

He could remember the woman he had loved without asking the woman downstairs to become her.

* * *

The seam opened where it always opened.

His breath stopped at the same place.

Mau put his hand against the chair and felt the wood beneath his palm. He had not lost the room he was sitting in. He could choose to stop.

For this much, he stayed.

The hand in the memory remained almost within reach. Infinity still refused to finish the small distance which mattered more than every grand thing he had learned to do.

He did not change the ending to make himself a better survivor of it.

He let the remembered scene reach the place where he usually tried to shut it away.

* * *

There was nobody new waiting for an answer inside the image.

Only the words and feelings he had carried out of a life made to hurt him. A history he could not protect by refusing every future which resembled its happy beginning.

"I'm sorry," he said quietly. ᴹ

The remembered face did not invent a reply.

He had brought love into that room. He had not been able to keep it from being used against everyone in it. He could grieve that without making the loss a contract which required him to stand here forever.

"It mattered," he said. "I won't pretend it didn't." ᴹ

His voice shook.

"But I can't keep living as if staying here is how I bring you back." ᴹ

* * *

He let the memory end.

Not erased. Not repaired until the terrible thing had secretly never happened. He knew it could return on another day, behind another ordinary word.

He had looked at it without making the world after it stop.

For a while he sat with his hand against the chair and cried quietly enough that he could still hear somebody turning a page across the room.

Ciel did not rush to fill the quiet with proof that his body was functioning.

The next breath arrived.

Then another.

* * *

When he opened his eyes fully, Yuka was looking at him.

She did not rise until he held out his hand.

Then she came to him, knelt beside the chair and put hers into it.

No unfinished distance.

No demand that he use the contact to prove the memory had become harmless.

He bent his head against hers and remained there until he could speak without making the words fight his breath.

"I'm here," he said. ᴹ

She pressed his fingers.

"I know." ʸᵏ

* * *

She brought him water afterward.

He drank before trying to tell her he was all right. The more honest answer took longer and turned out to be smaller.

"I'd like you to stay a little." ᴹ

She sat beside him.

The book remained open across her knees. He asked what part she had reached. She told him, and after a while began reading the part she liked aloud.

Mau listened, his hand around the one he could reach.
