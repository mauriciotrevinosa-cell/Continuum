*When: Yuka finds Mau in the corner they helped make, holding the scarf she gave him on his birthday.*

He had been folding it badly enough that she could have begun with the obvious correction.

Instead she sat down.

"Are you keeping something from me?" she asked. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

Then put the scarf beside the map instead of using it as work his hands needed to finish.

"Yes." ᴹ

She stayed still.

"Not a threat," he said. "Not another thing somebody said about what they want to do to me. The people who know are helping me with a surprise." ᴹ

Yuka's shoulders moved with a breath she had been holding too carefully.

"I asked Grandpa." ʸᵏ

"How did he survive?" ᴹ

She began smiling despite herself.

"Grandma found it very funny." ʸᵏ

* * *

He did not ask her to trust the word *surprise* forever.

"If you don't want to wait, tell me. I can explain what I'm trying to do without making the keeping of it another job for you." ᴹ

Yuka looked down at the scarf. Ran her thumb over the raised line, the one he kept finding when he wanted the thing she had made to be ordinary and close.

"I want a surprise," she said. "I just don't want another time where everyone knows I'm supposed to be frightened except me." ʸᵏ

He nodded.

"I understand." ᴹ

"Do you?" ʸᵏ

He thought before answering.

"Better than I did before I told you the words." ᴹ

She accepted that more readily than she would have accepted a promise that he had become impossible to misunderstand.

* * *

There were people moving through the house.

Ori passed with Frieren, trying to explain why Milim had been asleep before a game which required her to be awake. Bocchi's voice came from the music-room door, asking someone to listen to the end this time. Somewhere beyond them, Rem laughed at a thing Ram said without giving Mau enough information to defend whoever had caused it.

Yuka leaned against him.

He did not try to make the moment prove the memories had stopped hurting. His side still objected to one of the angles in which he liked to sit. He shifted, and she moved with him instead of making him pretend he had not needed to.

"Soon?" she asked. ʸᵏ

He looked at her.

"When I can do it properly. Not because I'm afraid I won't get another day." ᴹ

Her eyes shone before the smile arrived.

"That's a better reason to make me wait." ʸᵏ

* * *

He kissed her with the map resting safely beside them.

She put a hand on his cheek and kept him close when the first kiss ended, as if she had chosen another thing they were allowed to do without making it part of an argument against the world.

For a while they stayed in the corner other people had used before them and would use after them.

Then someone called them to eat.

Mau took the scarf. Yuka took the map so its edge would not catch against the chair.

They went to the table together.

* * *

He listened more than he spoke that evening.

Yuta managed to look at him without giving away the whole conversation. Mai was less concerned about his dignity and asked an apparently ordinary question about whether he had ever considered asking someone else to draw something before deciding a bridge belonged on a finger.

Yuka turned toward him.

Mau looked at Mai.

Maki put down her cup before laughing.

The sound caught him by surprise. Then Yuka laughed too, not because she had been excluded from the room but because he was there, being embarrassed in it where she could reach him.

Mau let the laughter take as long as it needed.

For once he was keeping something because he wanted to give it, and he had come home in time to choose how.
