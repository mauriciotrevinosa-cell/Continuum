He sat still until the first ache separated into places he could name.

Hip. Side. A strip across his back where Beru had caught him after Igris took away the turn. His forearm felt hot beneath the clean dressing.

Yuka opened one eye.

“Did the army come back while I was asleep?” ʸᵏ

“No.” ᴹ

“You look as if you're checking.” ʸᵏ

He flexed his right hand.

“I'm deciding which part of me wants breakfast first.” ᴹ

“The part that can carry you to Shoko.” ʸᵏ

She rolled onto her back and stretched, then reached out to touch the little section of hair above his ear which never lay quite where he thought it did.

“I'll be downstairs. Save me something if you get there first.” ʸᵏ

He bent and kissed her.

“What if Milim's already there?” ᴹ

Yuka considered the seriousness of the problem.

“Then save the plate.” ʸᵏ

* * *

Shoko was making a note when he arrived.

She made him sit before finishing it.

He answered the questions about the night, the repaired side and the arm. She removed the dressing far enough to inspect the skin and tested movement while he resisted the temptation to improve his answers by making the movement look easier than it felt.

“There,” she said when he stopped trying. ˢʰ

“There what?” ᴹ

“Now I can tell.” ˢʰ

She replaced the dressing with a lighter one.

The old stiffness in his fingers remained. She had him show her the difference between that and the new soreness, then wrote it down without suggesting one had excused the other.

“You can walk. You can look at your notes. You can let someone else enjoy being the person who knows what happened yesterday.” ˢʰ

“That's a very specific prescription.” ᴹ

“I know my patients.” ˢʰ

Gojo appeared in the doorway with two cups.

Shoko held out her hand for one before he could pretend he had brought both for someone else.

* * *

Yuta had drawn the first exchange on a piece of scrap paper.

The drawing was mostly arrows. One of them went straight through a shape labelled *Tank*, which gave Mau an immediate objection.

“I went over him.” ᴹ

“You went where he wanted you to go,” Yuta said. ʸᵗ

Gojo put his cup down.

“Which happened to be over him.” ᴳ

They sat on the clinic step in the sun. Shoko stayed inside with the door open, working through things that belonged to her own morning while the three men tried to agree on a moment which had lasted less time than the argument describing it.

Yuta stood to show him.

No weapons. He placed a foot on the edge of the step and turned his hip.

“You chose the landing before you knew whether it was still there.” ʸᵗ

Mau watched the angle.

“Because I wanted my hands free for Beru.” ᴹ

“Exactly.” ʸᵗ

“And then he didn't attack,” Gojo said. “He let you spend the answer.” ᴳ

Mau leaned forward.

They showed him the movement again. This time he saw the instant when he had committed two different parts of himself to the same assumption.

“I do that with Blue,” he said. ᴹ

Gojo looked at him.

Mau traced the movement on the page.

“I decide what the next bit of space is for. Then I keep trying to reach it after the other person has changed the question.” ᴹ

Gojo smiled.

“Now we're getting somewhere.” ᴳ

* * *

They stayed until the sunlight reached the opposite edge of the step.

The useful part was often embarrassingly small. A foot placed too close to a piece of rock. A hand opened before he had decided what would protect the space it left. He could remember the enormous attack at the end much more easily than the ordinary movements which had made the army work him so hard before it.

Yuta let him stand and try one adjustment slowly.

From inside, Shoko cleared her throat.

Mau sat back down.

Gojo looked very innocent for a man who had been about to suggest a second attempt.

“There is something else I'd like to ask,” Mau said. ᴹ

Yuta folded the page once, keeping the arrows on the inside.

“What?” ʸᵗ

“When you put a technique into a barrier, how much of the work is the technique and how much is the space you've taught it to use?” ᴹ

Gojo's expression sharpened with interest.

Shoko came to the doorway.

She looked at the three faces, then at the open space on the step beside them.

“This is going to need another cup,” she said. ˢʰ

* * *

By the time they went to eat, the question had acquired three possible answers and a very firm agreement not to try all three before lunch.

Yuka had saved him bread.

Milim had taken the plate.

“She said she was returning it,” Yuka explained. ʸᵏ

“To where?” ᴹ

“Herself.” ʸᵏ

Mau took the bread and sat beside her.

At the far end of the table, Kaneki and Taihei were discussing a cupboard shelf which needed lifting into place. Mau began to turn toward them.

Kaneki shook his head before he could offer.

“We've got it,” he said. “Go eat while it's still there.” ᴷ

Taihei grinned and took the shelf from the wall.

Mau looked at Yuka.

She moved her chair closer to his.

For once, there was nothing he needed to leave breakfast to do.
