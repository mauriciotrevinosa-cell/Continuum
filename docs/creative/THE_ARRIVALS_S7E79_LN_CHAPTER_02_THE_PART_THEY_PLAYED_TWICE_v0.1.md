## Chapter 14 — The Part They Played Twice

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S7 v0.1 (2026-10-28)
**Volume:** 42
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S7E79
**Continuity state:** B+8 evening, no week jump. Kessoku rehearsal and Mau/Bocchi sibling bond with Yuka/Umaru/Taihei present by choice. Band owns its music, no commissioned wedding concert/engagement announcement before proposal. Mai/Marin continue ring work separately, no new created object. Mau fresh spar recovery continues, old hand limitations reflected in ordinary tapping not cured by music.

*When: Bocchi asks them to listen, Umaru asks whether listening comes with snacks.*

“You brought snacks,” Taihei said. ᵀ

“For the listening part.” ᵁ

“That's what you just asked.” ᵀ

Umaru opened the bag with dignity.

They had found space along the side of the music room, out of the line where Nijika kept indicating that people would eventually learn not to put chairs. Yuka sat cross-legged beside Mau. Taihei took the back of the bench, and Umaru settled on the cushion she insisted she had borrowed rather than annexed.

Bocchi stood with her guitar and the careful expression of someone who had asked a simple thing and was now watching it become an event.

“It isn't finished,” she said. ᴮᶜ

“Neither are the snacks,” Ryo said. ᴿʸ

“Ryo.” ᴺⁱ

“A shared artistic condition.” ᴿʸ

Kita caught Bocchi's eye and smiled. They began before anybody could enlarge the audience further.

* * *

The first part went where Mau expected.

He had heard enough of their rehearsals to recognise the place where Nijika usually lifted the rhythm, where Kita's voice found its way over the guitar rather than trying to be louder than it. Then the phrase turned somewhere new.

His hand moved against his knee.

The old fingers missed part of the pattern. He changed hands without thinking, and Yuka rested two fingers lightly against the back of the first one while the song kept going.

They reached the new passage.

Bocchi's guitar answered Kita, then moved underneath her. The line almost resolved. Stopped short. Came back along a different route which made the next beat feel as if the room had taken a breath together.

Umaru stopped chewing.

When it ended, nobody spoke for half a second.

Then Mau pointed vaguely into the air.

“That.” ᴹ

Bocchi looked alarmed.

“Which that?” ᴮᶜ

“The part where I thought you were about to finish the line and you didn't.” ᴹ

“That's the part we're asking about,” Nijika said. ᴺⁱ

Mau looked at Yuka.

She nodded vigorously.

“Do it again,” she said. ʸᵏ

* * *

They played the passage twice more.

The second time Ryo changed something which Mau could hear but could not have described without waving his hands. Bocchi heard it immediately. She turned halfway toward the bass, answered with two notes and lost the next entrance because she was smiling.

Kita laughed into the line and had to stop.

“Keep that,” Umaru said at once. ᵁ

“The mistake?” Bocchi asked. ᴮᶜ

“The bit before the mistake.” ᵁ

“That's what I'm keeping,” Bocchi said, very quietly. ᴮᶜ

Mau leaned back against the wall.

His side still hurt. He could feel the bandage when he moved his shoulder. For several minutes, neither sensation had required a decision.

He liked the music more for having been asked to help with one small part of it rather than to explain what it was for.

* * *

Later, while the others argued about whether the new ending had arrived too early, Bocchi came to sit beside him.

She put the guitar carefully across her knees.

“I heard about yesterday,” she said. ᴮᶜ

“Everyone heard yesterday.” ᴹ

“The mountain part.” ᴮᶜ

He smiled.

“Basin.” ᴹ

“Ram said mountain.” ᴮᶜ

“She's having a good time with that.” ᴹ

Bocchi looked at his arm, then at her guitar.

“You were listening,” she said. ᴮᶜ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“I wasn't sure you'd want to.” ᴮᶜ

He turned toward her.

“Why?” ᴹ

She lifted one shoulder.

“People have been asking you difficult things.” ᴮᶜ

Mau considered the kindness in the answer and the little way she had left herself outside the category of somebody allowed to ask.

“You can ask me to hear a song,” he said. “I'd like you to.” ᴹ

Bocchi looked at him for a moment.

Then she nodded.

“Even the unfinished ones?” ᴮᶜ

“Especially if I'm allowed to request the good part twice.” ᴹ

She smiled down at the strings.

Across the room, Ryo had acquired the snack bag without a visible exchange of ownership. Umaru was explaining to Taihei that this constituted a diplomatic incident. Yuka came back with water and put a cup beside each of them.

Bocchi stood when Kita called her.

“We might change the ending again.” ᴮᶜ

Mau picked up his cup.

“I'll be here.” ᴹ

This time the promise was about the next few minutes, and he knew exactly how to keep it.
