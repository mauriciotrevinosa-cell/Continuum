*When: Mau finds the correction which worked yesterday, he tries to keep it while the second condition changes.*

The first opening was small.

Gojo had made him say what each part was for before beginning. The boundary. The interior distance. The surface which could carry his shadow work. No automatic attack. No audience inside.

They stood on the same empty ground as the day before. Shoko was close enough to see his face without crossing the marked line. Yuta watched the near edge. Yuka held the paper with the instruction written across the top.

Mau began.

The space deepened.

He held the boundary until Ciel confirmed the same route they had examined yesterday. He could feel its familiar effort. The ordinary burnout had passed during the night, and he had checked the single operation before beginning this one; there was no missing answer he had quietly repaired out of his own brain.

*First condition stable,* Ciel said.

He let the second begin.

* * *

Darkness gathered along the surface inside the circle.

It did not spread across the observers' feet. It belonged to the small space he had defined, deeper in one place than another where the distant edge still resisted agreement with the near one.

Mau noticed the flaw and changed the shape.

The surface came inward.

He corrected its depth.

The far boundary moved with it.

For a moment he had the sensation of holding two ends of a cord whose middle passed through a hand he could not see.

“They're sharing more than the drawing showed,” he said. ᴹ

Gojo leaned forward slightly.

“I can see the change. Bring the distance back before you adjust the surface again.” ᴳ

Mau tried.

The surface held.

The distance did not.

* * *

He could feel the useful shape just beyond the correction.

It was maddeningly close. Not a vast new power he would have to invent, not something waiting on the far side of a wall which had never opened. Two routes he could already touch, disagreeing at a point small enough that he thought he could place his attention around it and make the difference clear.

He narrowed his focus.

Mini Ciel brought the available changes into a cleaner order. The information made sense. His fingers moved with the correction before the rest of the execution had settled.

The boundary sharpened.

The surface lost its depth.

Mau reached for the part of the pattern which selected what an imposed operation belonged to.

For one instant the route he found was familiar in the wrong way.

A third line answered.

*That isn't the one—* Ciel began.

He recognised the cutting-pattern structure before there was any cut.

The instruction had reached farther than the work he meant to do.

He tried to close it.

* * *

Yuka saw the change in his eyes.

His face had been intent, faintly annoyed by the thing which would not quite agree. Then the expression emptied so suddenly that she started forward before she knew why.

“Mau?” ʸᵏ

He opened his mouth.

Nothing came out.

A thin line of blood reached his upper lip.

Gojo moved.

The little territory folded into a shape which no longer resembled any of the diagrams. He cut its support from the outside, precise enough that the released force went up through empty air instead of sideways into the people waiting beside it.

Yuta reached Mau as his knees gave way.

The paper fell out of Yuka's hands.

* * *

For Mau there was only the beginning of a pressure he could not locate.

He had the absurd thought that he needed to tell them what the third line was before somebody used the wrong word for it afterward.

He tried to turn toward Gojo.

The ground changed its relation to his body.

Then the sound was gone.

* * *

Yuta lowered him the last few inches with a hand behind his head.

Shoko was already beside them.

“Give me room.” ˢʰ

Yuka stopped so hard that her heel cut a little mark beside the boundary line.

Mau's hand lay open on the ground. She could see the ordinary dirt beneath his fingers, the old pale mark on one knuckle, the place the bandage had kept clean while they worked.

There was more blood at his nose now.

Shoko put a hand to his face and looked up at Gojo.

“What changed?” ˢʰ

“A third route tried to open,” he said. “I closed the boundary. The feedback had already reached him.” ᴳ

His voice had none of the afternoon's teasing in it.

Yuta shifted enough to let Shoko work without moving Mau's head.

Yuka crouched beside his empty hand.

“Mau,” she said again, much more quietly. ʸᵏ

This time she did not expect the answer before Shoko had finished trying to keep one possible.
