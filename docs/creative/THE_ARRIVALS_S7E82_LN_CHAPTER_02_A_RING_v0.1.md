They checked the standing connection before she began.

Mau felt a small weight cross the connection into him.

He stayed with it. No pain in the old place behind his eyes. No need to pour more into the route because the object mattered. It was a small thing made carefully, and his larger reserve and stronger body let him bear that small demand as such.

Mai finished the final edge and let her shoulders ease.
