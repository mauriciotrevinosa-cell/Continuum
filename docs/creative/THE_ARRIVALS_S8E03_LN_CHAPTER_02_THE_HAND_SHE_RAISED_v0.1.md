*When: she raises her hand, Mau moves before he knows which part of the threat it belongs to.*

He knew where Yuka was.

That was enough.

His fingers closed around Makima's wrist. He turned it away from Yuka, stepping close enough that if anything came out of the hand it would have to pass through the wall beside the bed instead of the place where she stood.

Behind him, Yuka moved too.

Maki caught the edge of the bed and drove it aside, opening a line. Yuta reached from the other direction. Rimuru had one hand around DIO's arm where she had just stepped clear of the dark opening near Mau's feet.

The opening to her room closed.

For one instant Mau felt the relief of that small completion.

Then Makima turned her wrist inside his grip.

The bandage pulled tight. Beneath its loosened edge, something flat and dark unfolded against her skin.

It did not point at Yuka.

It pointed at him.

*Mau. The contact—*

Ciel's voice stopped in the middle of the warning.

* * *

Yuta's hand missed his shoulder by the width of two fingers.

The floor between them had become a depth where no depth belonged. A dark edge rose around the two people standing closest together, folding the narrow space where Makima had made Mau catch her wrist.

Mau pulled back.

It was not the resistance of her body he met. The direction itself had changed. His heel found an upright surface where the clinic floor had been, and the arm he held was the only thing still within reach that had not changed its place relative to him.

He heard Yuka say his name.

He turned toward the sound.

Her hand was reaching. Yuta had caught her around the middle, holding her back from an edge that was already drawing shut.

Mau tried to reach with his free hand.

The last thing he saw was her face, full of colour, and the ring on the fingers which never reached his.

Then the clinic was gone.

* * *

He landed badly and corrected before his knee struck stone.

Makima's wrist was still in his hand.

He drove her back against a pillar, changed his hold and put his other hand around her throat. The impact shook dust out of a shallow seam above them.

“What did you do?” ᴹ

His voice came back from farther away than it should have.

He did not look toward the echo.

Makima had put one hand against his forearm. Her feet were on the ground. He knew exactly how much pressure it would take to change that and kept himself on this side of it.

“Where are they?” ᴹ

She watched him.

“At home.” ᴹᵏ

“Open it.” ᴹ

“This isn't my house.” ᴹᵏ

“You opened the gate.” ᴹ

“A door can be lent.” ᴹᵏ

He tightened his hold just enough to stop her shifting her chin.

“Then ask for it again.” ᴹ

She looked down at his hand with an interest he did not understand.

*Ciel.*

There was nothing.

He reached for the familiar place in his attention, where an answer could come while he was still forming the question.

*Rimuru. Can you hear me?*

No answer.

Behind him, something moved through his own shadow. He felt the readiness of his army and knew it was still there. That did not make the absent voice less absent.

Makima saw the moment he found out.

“You don't have to listen to so many people now.” ᴹᵏ

He put her back against the pillar when she tried to straighten.

“You threatened her.” ᴹ

“And you came.” ᴹᵏ

* * *

The room was enormous.

He took it in by pieces while watching her hands. Tall columns. An empty stretch of floor. Marks which did not belong to the clinic, travelling around the bases of the pillars and disappearing into the distance.

He had been in a place built to recognise him before.

The thought made his stomach turn.

“What do you want from me?” ᴹ

“I told you.” ᴹᵏ

“I'm not a pet.” ᴹ

“Not yet.” ᴹᵏ

The answer was soft enough that he could hear the small difficulty she still had speaking around his hand. He eased it a fraction, hating that she had made him notice.

“I helped you.” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴹᵏ

“You made all of that up.” ᴹ

“You wanted someone to know who you were.” ᴹᵏ

He could not stop the picture of the road coming back. His own care in lifting her. Asking where it hurt. Watching the ground because she had trusted him to carry her.

Behind the picture was Yuka's reaching hand.

He swallowed.

“Send me back.” ᴹ

“I would like you to become what they're afraid of,” Makima said. “All those people who know your name without ever seeing your face.” ᴹᵏ

“Why?” ᴹ

She smiled, and for the first time he could see no trace of the hurt stranger in it.

“Because I want you.” ᴹᵏ

She moved her fingers against his arm. A small touch, almost reassuring.

“And you should learn how much easier it is when someone else tells you what you're for.” ᴹᵏ

Mau pulled her hand off him.

“I am not yours.” ᴹ

“Then we'll have to work on that.” ᴹᵏ

She looked toward the place where the gate had been.

“Perhaps next time she can come too.” ᴹᵏ

He saw the raised hand again.

His own moved.

The cut crossed the space between them before he had finished deciding to make it.

Makima's head struck the stone.

Mau was still holding the body.
