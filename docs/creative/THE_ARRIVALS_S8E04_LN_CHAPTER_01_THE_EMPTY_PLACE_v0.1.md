There was a pale scrape across the boards from the bed Maki had moved.

Nothing else.

She put her hand over it, close enough to feel the floor's coolness without pressing down.

“Mau?” ʸᵏ

Behind her, DIO was trying to stand without Rimuru's arm.

“Stay,” he told her. ᴿ

“I can—” ᴰⁱᵒ

“Tell us from there.” ᴿ

She stopped struggling with the support. Shoko pulled the chair into place, waited until she was seated and looked toward Yuka.

“Step back enough that we can check it.” ˢʰ

Yuka did.

Her hand would not close properly. She looked at the ring, pressed it against her palm and let it go again.

“Rimuru.” ʸᵏ

He was listening to something she could not hear.

Then he looked at her.

“The connection broke at the crossing. Ciel can't reach him.” ᴿ

“Is he—” ʸᵏ

“We don't know what is happening inside.” ᴿ

He said it carefully enough that she knew he had refused an easier answer.

Yuka nodded once.

“Then tell me what we can try.” ʸᵏ

* * *

Gojo arrived while Maki was moving the last loose object clear of the scraped boards.

He asked where everyone had stood. Yuta showed him, using the positions of the bed and the pillar of the door instead of reaching into the place a second time.

DIO described the shape she had felt as she left Mau's room.

Not all of it. She said so herself.

“I was coming out. It folded after me.” ᴰⁱᵒ

Gojo went down on one knee.

For a while nobody asked him whether he had found it.

From the passage, Mai told someone to take the children to the other room. Frieren answered. Ori's voice followed, lower now, asking where Mau had gone.

Yuka did not turn toward it.

Gojo lifted his hand from above the boards.

“The opening isn't still here under a lid. Whatever made it has closed the local route.” ᴳ

“Can you reopen it?” Maki asked. ᴹᵃ

“I can damage the floor. That isn't the same thing.” ᴳ

Yuka swallowed.

“If I try through the connection we have?” ʸᵏ

Rimuru shook his head before she could make the attempt a demand.

“Let us check what responds first. I don't want to send something through him when he can't tell us what it's doing.” ᴿ

Mai had been standing with both hands empty.

“I'm not making anything,” she said. ᴹᵃⁱ

Shoko nodded to her.

* * *

They made a place to work instead of gathering closer around the absence.

Maki kept the passage open. Yuta stayed with Gojo. Rimuru asked Ciel to separate everything she had actually registered at the crossing from everything they feared it might mean.

Shoko set out clean cloth and water.

Yuka watched her for a moment.

“For when he comes back?” ʸᵏ

“For whatever he needs when he does.” ˢʰ

Yuka took the second basin from the shelf and brought it over.

Outside, the ordinary perimeter still needed watching. Rimuru sent the request without pretending he knew which soldiers Mau could reach now. A person would check the nearest stations and bring back an actual answer.

DIO asked for paper.

“I can show the part I felt.” ᴰⁱᵒ

Yuka found a sheet and put it in front of her.

Then she sat close enough to see where the first line went.

On the other side of the room, the small clock continued to move.
