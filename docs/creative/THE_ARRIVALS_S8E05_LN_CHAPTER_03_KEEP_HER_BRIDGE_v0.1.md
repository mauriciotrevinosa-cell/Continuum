## Chapter 14 — Keep Her Bridge

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S8 v0.1 (2026-10-30)
**Volume:** 43
**Reading order:** 14
**Source episode:** S8E05
**Continuity state:** B+19 household, several minutes after capture; no fixed interior/exterior time ratio. Frieren/Ori/Milim game room, quiet family support without forced PapaMau or false guarantee. Rem/Ram keep ordinary food available; short secondary perspective. DIO weakened adult helped earlier, not child's responsibility. Compass still to Frieren. No inside injury knowledge or magical return, no lunch/day elapsed outside inferred.

*When: Milim says she can break the door, Ori looks at the corridor.*

Frieren laid the pencil down.

“They would ask you if breaking it would help.” ᶠ

Milim folded her arms.

“They're taking too long.” ᴹⁱ

The small clock in the next room had barely been worth looking at. Ori had looked anyway. She understood that the hands had moved; she did not understand how little they seemed to have accomplished by doing it.

“Do they know where it went?” she asked. ᴼ

“Not yet.” ᶠ

“But they can find it.” ᴼ

Frieren reached for her hand.

“They're trying.” ᶠ

Ori looked down at the board on the step.

The new bridge was the best thing on it. Yuka had helped make the two ends meet instead of letting the path stop short of the chair. They had made it for the woman who lost twice and wanted to stay for another round.

“I told her to come out,” Ori said. ᴼ

Frieren moved closer.

“You asked someone to play.” ᶠ

“And then—” ᴼ

“Whatever that visitor did, it was her choice.” ᶠ

Ori's fingers tightened around hers.

Milim stopped looking toward the passage and sat on the floor beside the board.

For a while none of them tried to move a piece.

* * *

Ram brought a tray they had not asked for.

“Before someone decides waiting means nobody is allowed to eat,” she said. ᴿᵃ

Rem followed with the cups. She counted who was there, set down what they needed and took one cup back off the tray without explaining who it was for.

Ori watched her go.

“Yuka?” ᴼ

“I'll take it to her,” Rem said. ᴿᵉ

That helped. Yuka was in the room where Mau had disappeared, and somebody was taking something warm to her instead of waiting for her to remember she could ask.

Ori took the piece of bread Ram offered.

Milim did too, although she looked offended by the necessity.

“We should keep it,” Ori said after a while. ᴼ

Frieren followed her gaze.

“The bridge?” ᶠ

Ori nodded.

“So she can play when she's better.” ᴼ

They had all heard DIO trying to explain the strange opening from the other room. Her voice had sounded thinner than it did in the courtyard.

Frieren put the bridge beside the board, where nobody would step on it.

“We'll keep it.” ᶠ

Ori leaned against her. The compass swung between them, its needle settling toward the person who already had her arm around her.

Milim reached over and placed a small wooden guard at each end of the bridge.

Neither child explained the new rule.
