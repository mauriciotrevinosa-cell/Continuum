“Thank you.” ʸᵏ

Rem touched her shoulder before going back through the door.

DIO's drawing lay between them. The first fold was a line. The second turned through it at an angle which looked simple until Yuka tried to follow it in the direction Mau had gone.

It stopped being a path.

“This part?” she asked. ʸᵏ

“Where I came out,” DIO said. ᴰⁱᵒ

“And then his?” ʸᵏ

“I felt the beginning. Not where it finished.” ᴰⁱᵒ

Yuka put a small mark at the last place she could actually describe.

Across the room, Gojo was comparing it with the brief trace he had found above the floor. The two observations belonged to the same movement. That did not make either one a door they could open.

* * *

Yuta came to stand beside her.

For a while he said nothing. She was glad he did not ask whether she was all right before she had decided what the question could mean.

“You held me back,” she said. ʸᵏ

He looked at the place where the floor had opened.

“It was closing.” ʸᵗ

“I know.” ʸᵏ

She rubbed the edge of the cup with her thumb. It was still warm.

“I know why you did it.” ʸᵏ

“Yuka—” ʸᵗ

She looked up.

“Thank you.” ʸᵏ

He sat down beside her.

For a moment that was harder than if he had given her something else to do. She had to notice how frightened he was too.

She leaned against him, just long enough to feel his arm come around her shoulders.

* * *

Rimuru returned from the doorway with a report.

The nearest outer stations had been checked. Two of the soldiers could still be seen where they had been posted; neither had brought a new command or message from Mau. Igris was no longer beside the cart when the check reached it.

“He called him,” Yuka said. ʸᵏ

“Possibly. We didn't see the recall.” ᴿ

She nodded, making herself keep the difference.

“But we haven't found him broken.” ʸᵏ

“No.” ᴿ

It was something. She let it remain something instead of making it proof of everything else she wanted.

Rimuru took the drawing to Gojo. Yuta stayed beside her while she drank.

The water had not cooled yet.

She looked at the clock again and hated how little of its face she could ask to move.
