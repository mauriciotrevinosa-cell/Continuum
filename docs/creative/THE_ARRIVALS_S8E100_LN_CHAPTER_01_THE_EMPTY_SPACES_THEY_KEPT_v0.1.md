*When: Fern asks what should happen to the search page now that the homes are being used, Bocchi says, “Keep it.”*

Her answer is quiet.

It is also immediate.

Fern leaves the page where it is.

The empty room beside Bocchi's door has become a real place without becoming a promise that the people she wants are already on their way. Jinshi's reservation still belongs to him. Nazuna has not acquired an arrival merely because the house has made space for people it hopes to know.

No coin is put on the table this morning.

No one asks for one.

They agree where the private records will stay and how somebody can return to the conversation when there is news or a choice to make. Building a town and preparing a wedding have not erased the people they still want to find.

They have given the search somewhere to return to.

* * *

Anko has brought a letter received last night through the ordinary route.

Hinata has been back in Tempest long enough to finish the work she said was waiting. She will set out tomorrow morning with Testarossa and Souei, who have chosen the same journey for their own visits. If the road holds, the three expect to reach the house the following evening.

Rimuru reads the practical part after Anko chooses to share it.

“We have rooms now,” he says. ᴿ

Anko looks at him.

“I've inspected those too.” ᴬⁿ

He has learnt enough to avoid asking the obvious next question.

The little guest inn will receive all three. Their visit does not put Testarossa and Souei into a list of people who have permanently left Tempest. It gives them a place to see what the life between the two towns is actually becoming.

* * *

Mau sees Shoko after breakfast.

The new examination room has the same demanding questions as the old one and a much better place to put the things which used to compete for one small surface. His right arm has remained stable through the move. The faint line has not spread. The sensory patch no longer interferes with ordinary movement.

She hears Ciel's account through him, checks what he can feel and lets him return to familiar everyday control without turning the answer into permission for an unplanned experiment.

The old scars remain.

So do the fingers which have never quite moved freely and the world which gives its colour only to Yuka.

“Stable,” Shoko says. ˢᵒ

Mau looks at the hand she has finished examining.

“I like that word too.” ᴹ

“Keep helping me use it.” ˢᵒ

He nods.

* * *

Outside, the new paths have begun to collect ordinary evidence of being used.

A chair has moved toward the better shade. Somebody has found the place where a basket can wait without becoming a trip. The children know which turn leads to the music room and which door they should knock on instead of opening because they have something urgent to show a friend.

Mau meets Richeh and her brother coming back from the workroom.

He asks whether they are finding what they need.

Rili looks toward his sister.

“So far,” he says. ᴿˡ

She carries the small thing they have been discussing between both hands, safe enough that neither of them has to take it away from her to prove they care.
