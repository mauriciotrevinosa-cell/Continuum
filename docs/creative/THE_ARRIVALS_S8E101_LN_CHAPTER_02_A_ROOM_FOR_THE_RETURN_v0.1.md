*When: Hinata sees Anko waiting by the guest inn, she asks whether the inspection has concluded.*

“Not until the guest arrives.” ᴬⁿ

“Demanding.” ᴴⁱ

Anko takes the lighter bag, leaving Hinata the one she has already said she wants to carry herself.

The greeting is familiar enough now that neither needs to make it casual for the benefit of the people watching. Hinata leans close. Anko meets her halfway.

Behind them, Testarossa looks toward Rimuru with an expression which suggests she has noticed several interesting developments before crossing the first court.

Souei says nothing about it.

Mau considers that tactful and suspects the silence is not the same as missing anything.

* * *

The three have come by the road they announced.

They have rooms in the small inn, not beds taken from people who have only just found places for their own things. Testarossa and Souei expect to review the visit after four nights; their work between places has not become a permanent relocation by being welcomed here. Hinata names the same morning for checking what she needs next.

Jinshi shows them the common route before they put away the last bags.

“You won't get lost,” Mau says. ᴹ

Rimuru looks up at him.

“He may be exaggerating. Some of us still take the wrong turn toward the old room.” ᴿ

“Some of us are very recently moved.” ᴹ

Testarossa's smile becomes warmer.

“An encouraging problem.” ᵀˢ

* * *

Hinata finds the children after washing off the road.

All five show her something. The new rooms. The study place. The route which preserves the Fort's claim to be reachable whenever something interesting begins. Chloe has chosen a small thing to put by her bed and seems pleased to explain why it belongs there.

Hinata listens without turning the visit into another examination of whether they are grateful enough.

Shizue comes to the door while Kenya is still describing a rule he clearly hopes will sound more established if he says it confidently.

She catches the look Hinata gives her.

They both remember teaching children who try that.

For a moment, getting to remember together is enough.

* * *

Rimuru gives Testarossa the part of the outside conversation which can happen over food.

Some routes are working more reliably. Some buyers have learnt that refusing to deal with a place does not make the goods they need appear elsewhere. Others still need persuasion, and the accounts reaching her do not add up to a world which has made a single decision about what to trust.

“Then we keep finding out which conversation we're actually in,” Mau says. ᴹ

She looks toward him.

“That seems wise.” ᵀˢ

He remembers asking divine beings about mice.

“I've had practice.” ᴹ

Souei asks when they would like to discuss the less public questions. Rimuru suggests tomorrow, privately, with the people who actually know which parts they have seen. They do not turn a tired arrival into an interrogation merely because somebody capable has reached the door.

* * *

Anko walks Hinata back to the inn after the common room has begun to quiet.

They stop where the path divides.

“Your own room,” Anko says. ᴬⁿ

“So I've been told.” ᴴⁱ

“Close enough to somebody interesting.” ᴬⁿ

Hinata smiles.

“That part I've verified.” ᴴⁱ

She takes Anko's hand for the last stretch.

The door opens, the bag goes down and the town has done one of the things they built it to do: let someone return without displacing the life she has come to visit.
