*When: Yuka calls from the sitting room, Mau is looking for a shirt he has already put on.*

He looks down.

Then toward the open door.

“Coming.” ᴹ

Rimuru is waiting beside Shizue, who has decided the last small thing on her shelf can remain slightly crooked until tomorrow. Yuka has a dish to carry. Mau takes it before she can tell him she is perfectly capable, because he is standing nearest and the question does not need to become an argument about who must always help whom.

She lets him.

The four of them leave together.

At their door, Shizue checks that she has her key.

Mau waits while she finds it.

Then they walk toward the sound of the house.

* * *

They pass the doors which made the drawing difficult.

Yuta and Maki join from the short family route. Mai calls something after them and comes out with Tsurugi before they have finished answering. Rem is standing with Umaru and Suika; Taihei has gone ahead to help with the dish which apparently requires more hands than its maker admitted. Ram closes her door while Seika waits at the turn.

Nobody has to choose between being with the person beside them and belonging to the meal ahead.

They reach the common room in a loose group which becomes larger when the band comes down its own stair.

Bocchi has left her instrument behind.

Tonight, she is coming to eat.

* * *

The tables do not pretend everyone can sit at one enormous edge.

They make little groups with space to walk between them, close enough that a person can turn and speak to the next one without shouting across a hall. There is room under the covered part if somebody wants the air and room inside for those who prefer the familiar warmth.

The tree stands beyond it, with the ground around its roots still its own.

Mau stops long enough to look.

Yuka catches the edge of the dish before he tips it.

“After you put that down,” she says. ʸ

He laughs and obeys.

* * *

People have brought things they wanted to bring.

Momo is explaining one dish to Shuna while Okarun finds places for the cups. Ram and Rem disagree pleasantly about where the second bowl belongs. Touka points Kaneki toward the place Ichika has decided he must sit. Maomao catches Jinshi reaching for something before hearing the warning and tells him which one he actually wants.

Gojo arrives with Shoko, carrying the thing she would have told him he could manage himself a few months ago. She lets him carry it. He puts it down without waiting for an audience.

Marin calls Wakana over before he can go back to check something in the workshop.

He comes.

Senku has left a question with Byakuya and brings the argument rather than the equipment. Nano finds a place where Shizuka can put the book she chose to bring. Hakari and Karane make room for Kusuri before the chair becomes a dispute worth everybody's attention.

Echidna is already discussing tea. Sekhmet has found a comfortable place from which to object to the discussion's length. The others settle around them with the ease of people who do not have to become interesting before being given a seat.

* * *

Richeh brings her brother to the table where the girls have been waiting.

He sits with them, listens to Tetia's version of the first week and glances at his sister when the account reaches a point he suspects has acquired decoration. Agott corrects one detail. Coco's mother laughs before Coco can ask whether it really happened quite like that.

Olruggio puts his cup down beside Qifrey's.

Neither has brought work which must be saved from the dinner.

Across the room, Eren, Mikasa and Armin are finding a place with a view of the court. Jiji has made Vamola laugh at something Aira insists is not funny. Seiko gives them the look which means she is enjoying the noise despite its best efforts. Turbo Granny supplies an opinion no one asked for.

Mau hears it from two tables away and smiles.

There are familiar ways for a home to be loud.

* * *

Geld and the workers are guests at the meal, not the people expected to keep standing because they built the chairs.

Kurobe is persuaded to leave the discussion of one fitting until after eating. Bellion has already learnt which route keeps the serving dishes clear. Igris waits where somebody may want help, then accepts Mau's quiet reminder that being allowed to stay out does not mean having to earn every moment of it.

Beru is trying very hard not to look toward the covered thing left safely in the workshop.

Kamish's presence remains where it can be enjoyed without giving the new roof an unnecessary trial. Ear has found a place from which to watch several important people at once. Ranga appears satisfied with the resting space beside the open route.

Rimuru looks around the room.

Shion is sitting with Shuna and someone she has begun to know without requiring Rimuru to introduce the subject. Ramiris is explaining how difficult it is to make a proper challenge while Beretta enjoys having heard the explanation often enough to stop answering every complaint.

Hinata sits with Anko near enough to hear the children. Testarossa and Souei have found places as visitors who have been offered an evening, not recruited into a decision they did not make.

Rimuru turns toward Mau.

For once, neither idiot seems obliged to rescue the other from a title.

* * *

Rem calls into the Fort.

The answer comes immediately.

Umaru has already made her way to the table, but Ori appears with Chloe and the others follow behind them, carrying the argument they were apparently still finishing. Kenya says his version first. Alice corrects him. Ryota and Gale place the thing they brought where nobody will spill food on it.

Ori runs to Frieren before choosing her own place.

Frieren listens, gives the small answer required and waits while the child decides what to tell Shizue next. Fern and Stark make room. Himmel takes the offered chair without turning the choice into a question nobody has asked him to answer tonight.

Yuka watches the children settle.

Then looks toward Mau.

“They heard.” ʸ

He nods.

“First time.” ᴹ

* * *

There is no speech large enough to explain the meal without making it wait.

Geld thanks the people who helped him understand what they needed. Rimuru thanks those who have chosen to come without treating the people who remained in Tempest as if they chose less well. Someone raises a cup before the conversation has decided whose turn it is to be embarrassed.

Mau lifts his.

Yuka lifts hers beside it.

They drink, put the cups down and begin passing food.

* * *

Later, when the first hunger has become the pleasure of remaining at the table, Mau feels Ciel's attention settle near his own.

Not an alarm.

Not another thing he has missed.

*A good evening,* she says. ᶜⁱ

He looks around the room again.

*Yes.* ᴹ

Inside, his guests have the quiet they chose and the means to ask for the parts they want to share. Noise has left the unfinished work where it belongs. Nothing needs to be taken from tomorrow to prove tonight can last.

Yuka touches his hand beneath the table.

“What are you thinking?” she asks. ʸ

Mau looks at her, in colour among all the familiar shapes of the people they love.

“That I know where we go after this.” ᴹ

She smiles.

“Home.” ʸ

He turns his hand beneath hers and holds on.

The conversation continues around them.
