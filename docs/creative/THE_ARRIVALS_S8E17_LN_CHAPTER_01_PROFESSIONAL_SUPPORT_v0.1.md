* * *

They had spent the morning doing things which did not involve a wheel.

Yuka had helped Maki, then come to find Mau where Rem had put him to work carrying the small things he was finally learning not to grip as though they were protected enemies. He had managed a whole tray without breaking anything and had been offended by how pleased everyone looked.

Now they sat beneath the tree with no one requiring either of them to prove the improvement.

“I didn't think I could get there,” Yuka said. ʸᵏ

He looked at her hand where it rested on the bench.

“You kept having ideas.” ᴹ

“You kept helping.” ʸᵏ

“Professional defensive cheerleader.” ᴹ

She stared at him.

He nodded very seriously.

“A difficult position. Constant criticism. No uniform.” ᴹ

She pushed his shoulder.

“Idiot.” ʸᵏ

He caught her hand and kissed the knuckles before she could decide whether he had deserved it.

* * *

“This is the beginning,” he said after a while. “If you behave, perhaps I'll let you use more of mine to combine with it.” ᴹ

Yuka looked at the ring on the hand he was holding.

Then she looked at him.

“We're engaged.” ʸᵏ

“I remember.” ᴹ

“Your things are already my things.” ʸᵏ

“That doesn't sound like the agreement I read.” ᴹ

“You should have asked Mai to read it first.” ʸᵏ

He laughed, and she moved closer until the small space between their shoulders had gone.

“Then we practise together,” he said. “At the same time. Properly.” ᴹ

She nodded.

The possibility delighted her more than another thing he might have given her outright. Work they could do side by side, with answers she could bring instead of waiting for him to finish finding them.

* * *

Yuta found them later and heard enough of the conversation to mention Todo.

Gojo, passing behind him, added Yuji and then stopped when three people looked at him for the useful version of the suggestion.

They spoke about chosen memories, movements which could be shown without opening anybody's whole life and the difference between understanding a technique and making a version another body could actually carry.

Mau asked Rimuru to help when they were ready.

Not today. Not another experiment rushed into the remaining daylight because the idea was interesting.

Yuka wrote down what she wanted to try first.

Mau watched her finish the last line.

“What?” she asked. ʸᵏ

“Nothing.” ᴹ

“The useful kind of nothing?” ʸᵏ

He smiled.

“Very.” ᴹ

She closed the page, put it beside them and took his hand.

They stayed until somebody called them to help with dinner.
