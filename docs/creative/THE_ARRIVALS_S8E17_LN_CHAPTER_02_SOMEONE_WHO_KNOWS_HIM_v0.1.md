“What did they say?” ᴿ

The man repeated it. A name. A description. A woman carrying a sword which made the description of the sword rather longer than the description of the woman.

Mau looked at Rimuru.

He had never seen that particular hesitation on his friend's face before: hope reaching the answer before the rest of him knew whether to follow it.

“Shion?” Rimuru asked. ᴿ

The scout nodded.

* * *

They did not bring an unknown armed party straight through the perimeter.

Rimuru went to the open ground beyond it, where the approach could be seen from the house without making the house the place in which every question had to be answered.

He asked Mau to come.

Yuka was already putting her things down to join them.

“Not an expedition,” Rimuru said. ᴿ

“A walk,” Mau agreed. ᴹ

The slime looked at him, then at the two people who had turned a walk into a reason to hold hands.

He shook his head and went ahead.

* * *

The first thing Mau saw was the child.

She stood a little behind a woman with a straight, watchful posture and a man who appeared to be doing a very poor job of pretending he had waited patiently. A fourth figure was near the front, one hand against the enormous weapon resting beside her.

Rimuru stopped.

The woman looked up.

For a heartbeat her face had nothing in it but disbelief.

“Rimuru-sama?” ˢʰⁱ

Then she was moving.

The man laughed so loudly that Mau felt the sound through his ribs.

“There you are!” ⱽ

Rimuru answered both of them at once and did not quite manage either answer.

Yuka squeezed Mau's hand.

He could see why Rimuru had wanted someone beside him.

* * *

Shion reached for Rimuru with the certainty of someone who had been afraid long enough to forget the need to ask.

The sword moved with her.

Only a little. A shift of grip, the long edge coming away from its rest so she would not drag it across the ground.

“I knew you were here,” she said. “I heard—” ˢʰⁱ

Mau saw a raised hand in another room.

Someone who knew a name. Someone who had come looking. A weapon beginning to move beside the person she had claimed before he understood what she wanted.

His hand left Yuka's.

The next movement had already begun when Ciel called his name.

* * *

Veldora caught the strike before it reached Shion.

The impact drove the two of them across the clearing, his boots cutting trenches through the ground. He looked at Mau with surprise first, then an attention sharp enough to make the next movement quite deliberate.

“That was not a greeting.” ⱽ

Mau turned out of his hold.

The wheel took shape above him.

Rimuru's voice came from behind the first broken line of dust.

“Don't kill him! Stop him so I can talk!” ᴿ

Shion had lifted the sword into a guard.

Mau's eyes found it.

* * *

Hinata stepped into the line before he could close it.

Her blade met his first approach at an angle which refused the strength of the whole blow. She did not try to stand in front of it and win a contest of weight. She turned the movement, stepped clear and made him change the next strike before it could become the one he had already decided to throw.

He followed.

Veldora came across his shoulder, forcing him to use Infinity instead of the cut which had been forming.

The space between them compressed into a brief, ugly stillness.

Mau let the copied route go and put Blue against the dragon's supporting foot.

Veldora moved with it, laughing once at the discovery that the pull had teeth. The answer he gave came low enough to throw Mau through the edge of the ground before the next protection could settle.

Mau used his own flight to recover.

He did not look toward the house.

That was what frightened Yuka most.

* * *

She called the alarm instead of running into the first line between them.

Rimuru was already moving, trying to put his voice where Mau could hear it without making himself the next person struck in passing.

The child had brought a small protective field around Shion's flank. She watched Mau with a steadiness which did not make her body any larger. When one of the broken stones came toward her, the barrier turned it aside.

Mau saw the smaller figure in the defended lane and changed direction.

There was still enough of him choosing for that.

He went for the weapon again.

* * *

Hinata's second interception was a narrow light laid across the path he was taking toward Shion's guard.

An attempt to separate the attack, to make him withdraw from a line which could not be crossed safely.

Mau put his forearm through its edge.

The surface came away in a thin, terrible absence.

He recoiled, brought his own repair to the fresh damage and found Ciel's voice trying to reach the part of his attention which had not yet come back to the clearing.

The wheel turned.

Hinata pulled the line away from the others at once.

Mau followed it into empty ground.

* * *

Gojo reached the clearing first.

Yuta came behind him, Maki on the other side. Milim had needed no explanation beyond Rimuru's call and Yuka's face.

They saw the new visitors.

They saw the wheel.

Then Mau moved again, faster than the body which had left home that morning should have needed to move, and the question became how to get him to stop without making his fear right.
