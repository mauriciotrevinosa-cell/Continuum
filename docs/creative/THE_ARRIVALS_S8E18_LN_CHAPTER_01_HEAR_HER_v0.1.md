Infinity.

The familiar refusal of the last small distance. He had spent too long learning answers to it for his body to stop searching just because the answer had come from someone he trusted.

His hand changed.

Gojo saw the new preparation and moved the line away from Rimuru.

“Mau. Look at me.” ᴳ

The next strike stopped short of his chest.

For one instant the name almost reached him.

Then Shion's blade moved again beyond Gojo's shoulder, defending the place where Rimuru was trying to stand, and the clearing became the clinic for another terrible fraction of a second.

* * *

Hinata did not send another broad answer into that confusion.

She kept the dangerous edge in the empty lane she had already cleared, using her body and the blunt part of the weapon to turn Mau away from the others.

He caught the next approach with the same injured forearm.

This time the wheel had something it recognised.

The light took less from the place it touched. His repair caught the new loss before it spread into the work holding the rest together. On the next contact the response had narrowed further, answering this particular edge instead of asking his whole body to accept what it did.

Hinata saw the change.

She withdrew the line entirely.

It was not a contest she needed to finish by finding out whether he could survive a larger version.

Veldora took the space she left.

* * *

The dragon met him without a weapon.

They struck the ground together, two people trying to decide where the other's next movement would be allowed to happen. Veldora could have made a disaster of the whole clearing. Instead he took the direction of Mau's force and kept turning it away from the perimeter.

Mau answered the turn with the grip.

Blue followed, then vanished before the copied route changed again. For several seconds the different systems in him found a rhythm frightening precisely because it was no longer the frantic collection the house had once needed to help him hold.

He was better at this now.

That did not make the reason he was doing it right.

Yuta came to the side, blade held for interception. Maki took the opening beneath the raised arm and changed Mau's footing without trying to wrestle his full strength into obedience. Gojo closed the escape lane which would have put the next collision among the trees.

Milim waited through all of it with her hands ready.

Yuka stepped where she could be heard.

* * *

“Mau!” ʸᵏ

Not a warning through a corridor.

Not the sound left behind by a gate.

Her voice, here.

His head turned.

She was standing in the clearing. Both feet on the ground. Her hand empty except for the ring he had spent so long wanting to see again.

He looked at the weapon in Shion's hands.

Then the people between it and everyone else.

The order of things changed.

Milim caught his wrists.

* * *

“Hold still,” she said. ᴹⁱ

He looked at her.

Rimuru came into the space in front of him.

“It's me.” ᴿ

Mau's breathing was much too loud.

“They're mine,” Rimuru said. “They came looking for me. They haven't threatened her.” ᴿ

He looked toward Yuka.

She shook her head once.

“I'm all right.” ʸᵏ

The wheel went quiet.

Mau dismissed it.

* * *

Milim did not let go until he asked without trying to pull free.

When she released him, he looked down at his own hands. The fresh place on his arm was closing. Dirt and blood marked a clearing which had been whole a few minutes before.

Beyond Rimuru stood the woman with the sword.

Her face held none of the smile which had followed him into the dungeon.

He knew that now.

“I'm sorry,” he said. ᴹ

Shion did not answer immediately.

He made himself leave the silence for her instead of filling it with an explanation which might sound like a request that she forget what had happened.

“It's been a difficult time,” he added. “That doesn't make this your fault.” ᴹ

Hinata lowered the point of her blade.

“No,” she said. “It doesn't.” ᴴⁱ

He nodded.

* * *

Rimuru looked from one side of the clearing to the other.

“We're going to talk,” he said. “First we put the weapons away. All of us.” ᴿ

Mau stored the daggers he had called during the fight.

Yuta lowered his blade. Shion checked the girl behind her before resting the enormous sword against her shoulder. Hinata stepped clear of the lane in which her light had been.

Veldora rolled one shoulder, looked at the broken ground and seemed about to make a comment which Rimuru stopped by looking at him.

“Later.” ᴿ

“I had not said anything.” ⱽ

“Keep practising.” ᴿ

Yuka came to Mau's side and took his hand only after he had opened it.

He looked at her.

She stayed.

There was no more useful answer she could give him before the conversation began.
