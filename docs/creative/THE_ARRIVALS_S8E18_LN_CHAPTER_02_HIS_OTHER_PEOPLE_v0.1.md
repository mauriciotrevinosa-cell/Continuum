They talked outside until the first questions had stopped overlapping.

The city had not arrived as part of a whole familiar continent. Roads ended where other roads should have begun. Forest stood in places the party had expected to find something else. Tempest itself remained a city with people who needed food, work and someone who knew which part of its government had not vanished with its surroundings.

They had followed the rumour of a slime, then the rumour of a man who made a sun.

“So you brought an army?” Rimuru asked. ᴿ

“A modest precaution.” ⱽ
