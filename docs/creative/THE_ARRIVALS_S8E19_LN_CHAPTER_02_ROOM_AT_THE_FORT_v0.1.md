* * *

They gave her a job in the game before asking her to tell a story.

That helped.

She could hold the little wooden guard while Ori showed her the crossing. She could ask which rule had changed without making the question about herself. She could laugh when Milim insisted a defence had been entirely successful despite the complete absence of the thing it was supposed to defend.
