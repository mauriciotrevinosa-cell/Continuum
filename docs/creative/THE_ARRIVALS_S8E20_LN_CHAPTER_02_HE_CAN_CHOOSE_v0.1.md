*When: Veldora says a celebration should include a proper contest, Rimuru sets his cup down before the idea can finish growing.*

“Define proper.” ᴿ

Veldora spread his hands.

“A tournament.” ⱽ

Across the table, Gojo looked up.

Maki saw him look up and shut her eyes briefly, as if she had heard a door open somewhere very inconvenient.

Ramiris came closer to the map.

“A tournament needs a place.” ᴿᵃᵐ

“Exactly,” Veldora said, delighted to find expertise which appeared to agree with him. ⱽ

“And rules,” Hinata added. ᴴⁱ

“And people who can stop it,” Shoko said. ˢʰ

Mau rested his hands around his cup and tried not to look as interested as he was.

Yuka noticed immediately.

* * *

They had begun with the festival.

Food, introductions, somebody showing the household enough of the city that a name could acquire streets and people instead of remaining the place Rimuru had once led. There would be time for those who did not want to compete, for the children and the people who preferred to find a market or listen to music without a crater being made beside them.

The contest was meant to belong inside that invitation.

It took less than a minute to acquire a less reasonable purpose.

“Then whoever wins gets to keep Rimuru-sama,” Shion said. ˢʰⁱ

Diablo looked at her.

Veldora laughed with enough enthusiasm to make the suggestion sound like something already announced.

Rimuru stared at all three of them.

“No.” ᴿ

The table grew interested.

* * *

“I can choose where I live,” he said. ᴿ

Shion's face changed. The words had begun as a challenge to Diablo, a way to put her own certainty back into a room where the old answers kept arriving too small.

Rimuru did not make her regret the feeling behind them.

He still kept the answer.

“You can invite me. You can complain. You cannot make me the prize.” ᴿ

Diablo inclined his head.

“You have a Mau, Lord Rimuru. In the end, you will decide.” ᴰ

Mau looked up.

“A what?” ᴹ

Yuka began laughing before Diablo could explain.

* * *

Rimuru looked at his brother.

Mau was sitting with his cup safely between both hands, the man who had spent a year in a room which kept telling him what he was for and had come back wanting an ordinary day with the person beside him.

He was not a weapon Rimuru had placed behind the argument.

He was somebody who would understand why the choice mattered.

“He isn't an answer I get to throw at people,” Rimuru said. ᴿ

“Certainly not,” Diablo replied. “I mean that you are hardly without support when you give your own.” ᴰ

Mau raised his cup a little.

“That version I can do.” ᴹ

Rimuru shook his head, but he was smiling now.

“Then we are clear. A tournament for the festival. My address remains my decision.” ᴿ

Shion looked down, then back at him.

“Clear,” she said. ˢʰⁱ

It sounded like agreement this time, not a retreat she intended to reverse when he stopped looking.

* * *

Ramiris wanted paper.

Not names in little boxes yet. Space first. What the place could hold, what it would have to keep out of the spectators' reach, which safeguards belonged to a bounded arena and which people had merely heard stories about something similar working somewhere else.

Yuta leaned closer when she spoke about a controlled place to fight without turning the people watching into part of the danger. Shoko made it clear that nobody was being promised safe death by enthusiasm alone.

“We test it before anybody enters,” Rimuru said. ᴿ

Mau nodded with the others.

There were things he wanted to try. He could wait until trying them did not ask someone else to trust a promise nobody had checked.

* * *

Yuka leaned toward him while the first empty sheet was laid on the table.

“Would you enter?” she asked. ʸᵏ

He looked at the people around them. Gojo already arguing about the meaning of a limit. Veldora proposing a space large enough to make Hinata ask whether he intended to invite the whole horizon. Milim appearing beside Ramiris with immediate opinions of her own.

Then he looked at Yuka.

“If it's safe.” ᴹ

She smiled.

“Go easy on me.” ᴹ

“I haven't promised to fight you.” ʸᵏ

“I'm preparing.” ᴹ

She took his hand beneath the table.

“Then prepare properly.” ʸᵏ

Rimuru called them both back to the discussion before Mau could find another useful thing to say.

The paper in front of them was still blank.
