*When: Mau asks Noise for a conversation, the answer comes before he has found a comfortable place to sit.*

*Yes.*

He was halfway down the courtyard steps.

Mau stopped with one hand on the rail.

“You don't know what I'm going to say.” ᴹ

Somewhere beneath his attention, the small amusement he had learned to recognise moved through the unfinished work.

*You want something.*

“That doesn't narrow it down.” ᴹ

He went to the tree. Someone had left a folded cloth on the low seat where the roots gave the ground an inconvenient shape. He moved it, sat and waited until the first impulse to begin the conversation while standing up had passed.

There was no need to hide from the house. He wanted enough quiet to hear the answer properly.

*Ciel?*

*I am here.*

He let out the breath he had taken without noticing.

It still mattered every time.

* * *

“We're going to a place with a lot of things neither of us has seen.” ᴹ

Noise answered with interest rather than words. Mau felt it in the way several small pieces of his attention turned toward the idea at once.

“Yes. Exactly that.” ᴹ

He rested his hands on his knees.

“I want you to look. Read what you can. Keep the things you want to understand.” ᴹ

*And make.*

“Not while we're there.” ᴹ

The quiet became attentive in a different way.

Mau kept his hands still.

“No new constructions during the festival.” ᴹ

Noise did not answer.

“Tell me what you think.” ᴹ

*Small.*

He closed his eyes briefly.

“How small?” ᴹ

The answer arrived as a sense of space beneath the things already working. An unfinished edge, a place where a route might sit if it only had enough of a shape to be tried.

Mau recognised the feeling. It had preceded useful things. It had preceded other things too.

“No.” ᴹ

*Not use.*

“But build it and leave it there?” ᴹ

*Yes.*

He rubbed his face with the heel of one hand, thinking.

At the other side of the yard, somebody dropped a bucket. He heard Kaneki tell the person it was all right before he heard the water finish running between the stones.

“A door doesn't have to be open to become a problem if we've put it in the wrong wall.” ᴹ

Noise held the shape a moment longer.

Then let it go.

* * *

Ciel waited until Mau had opened his eyes.

*A distinction may help,* she said. *A record of a possible structure need not be connected to a live route.*

Mau nodded.

“Blueprints. As many as you want to keep, where we already keep the information. But nothing attached to my body, nothing changing an active technique and no new thing waiting behind a door because you thought it would be a good surprise.” ᴹ

*Good surprises.*

He laughed despite himself.

“You have made some very good surprises.” ᴹ

The small presence eased toward him.

“And I've spent a great deal of time not knowing which kind was happening. I don't want to do that while Yuka is trying to show me something or Rimuru is introducing somebody.” ᴹ

The name brought an image he had not requested: the ring on her hand, bright against the rest of the room.

Mau let it remain for a moment.

“I want to be there.” ᴹ

Noise was quiet.

Then, carefully:

*You will miss things.*

“I know.” ᴹ

*They will leave.*

“Some will. We can keep what we understood. Ask questions. Look again another time.” ᴹ

The answer cost him more than he had expected. A part of him could already imagine being in a room with something extraordinary and feeling the opportunity move out of reach.

He knew the temptation well enough not to pretend he was only explaining it to Noise.

“If I ask you to build something anyway, remind me of this conversation.” ᴹ

The amusement returned.

*You too?*

“Especially me.” ᴹ

* * *

They worked out what the promise did not include because neither of them wanted to discover the difference when something hurt.

Existing repair could continue. So could the quiet work which kept the things already part of Mau from coming apart. A soldier whose arm broke did not have to remain broken until the end of a holiday. Mai and Yuka did not lose their established connections because he had asked Noise to stop adding new ones.

He would still be able to use what he had.

That did not mean the work had permission to turn the first unfamiliar movement into a new limb merely because he had called it useful.

*No new structures,* Ciel said. *Maintenance of the existing ones is a separate task. We can record the boundary now.*

Mau looked at the ordinary window they had already used for notes. Ciel placed a short entry beside the list of work he had asked to review after returning.

He read it aloud, then asked Noise whether the words meant the same thing to it.

There was a correction.

*Not after festival.*

He waited.

*Home.*

“Until we come home,” he agreed. ᴹ

*Then talk.*

“Then we sit down and decide what to do next. I won't just leave you with a pile of plans and forget.” ᴹ

He felt the answer settle.

*I will remind you.*

Mau began laughing.

“I believe you.” ᴹ

* * *

The first small test was almost disappointingly ordinary.

Ciel opened an existing stored study which Mau had not finished reading. He could have followed the work inward. Instead he asked Noise to leave the study closed and keep its outline in the list for later.

It stayed there.

No new pressure. No request arriving in the wrong part of his body.

Mau looked out across the yard.

Fern was coming from the kitchen with a bowl. Stark had something tucked under one arm which seemed to require both of them to discuss where it belonged. The disagreement ended when Fern took the thing from him, looked at it properly and started laughing.

He watched without needing to turn what he saw into work.

*Like that?* Noise asked.

“Like that.” ᴹ

It was a small thing to have practised on purpose.

He stayed until the light moved beyond the bench.

* * *

Yuka found him there with two cups.

She offered one, then sat close enough that he had to move his knee to make room for hers.

“Are you working?” ʸᵏ

“We just finished.” ᴹ

“Something I should know?” ʸᵏ

“No new constructions until we're home.” ᴹ

She looked at him over the cup.

“You agreed to that?” ʸᵏ

“We negotiated.” ᴹ

“How long did that take?” ʸᵏ

He glanced toward the patch of shade where his feet had been when he sat down.

“There were counteroffers.” ᴹ

She smiled, but the smile softened before she drank.

“Thank you.” ʸᵏ

He turned toward her.

“You don't have to thank me for wanting a day with you.” ᴹ

“I know. I can still like that you made room for one.” ʸᵏ

He looked down at the cup, then rested his free hand beside hers. She moved her fingers through his without asking them to do more work than they could.

For a little while they listened to the house.

“What do you want to do when we get there?” he asked. ᴹ

“See what they have. Eat something you haven't chosen for its practical value.” ʸᵏ

“I have chosen some excellent practical food.” ᴹ

“You can tell me that while we eat something else.” ʸᵏ

“All right.” ᴹ

She leaned against his shoulder.

“And I want to go somewhere with you without either of us having to explain why we're going.” ʸᵏ

He turned his head and kissed her hair.

“Then that's what we'll do.” ᴹ

The quiet inside him remained quiet. Noise had left the list where it was, with more room beneath the first entry.

Mau did not open it again.
