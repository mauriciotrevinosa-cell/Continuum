He looked at the food.

Then at Rimuru.

His brother had chosen a seat from which he could watch the whole discovery without needing to pretend he had arrived late.

Yuka sat beside Mau. She had been to ask Shuna about the invitation before they came to dinner and had returned with the deeply unhelpful statement that he would be quite safe.

Shion waited.

“I made this part especially,” she said. ˢʰⁱ

Mau looked at the part.

It was difficult to establish exactly where it ended and the rest of the dish began. Something had been cut with tremendous confidence and then assembled with a confidence even greater than that.

He thought it might once have been intended to stand upright.

“Thank you,” he said. ᴹ

Rimuru put a hand over his mouth.

* * *

The meal had begun very pleasantly.

There were enough people at the tables for the house to have spread across several conversations. Somebody was trying to explain a game to Milim. Stark was answering a question from one of the visitors, and Fern was listening with an expression which suggested she had heard the part he had omitted.

Shuna had come to the table with Shion rather than letting her carry the whole thing alone.

Mau had appreciated that before he understood why he might appreciate anything else.

Now Shuna looked at the dish, then at him.

“I helped check the ingredients,” she said. ˢʰᵘ

Mau took a small piece.

He had eaten in places where the name of the thing mattered considerably less than whether it could be eaten at all. He had also learned that this was not a reassuring story to tell someone who had made dinner for him and was waiting to know whether he liked it.

He put it in his mouth.

Yuka watched his face.

Mau stopped moving for a moment.

Rimuru looked delighted.

Then Mau looked down at the plate again.

“That is very good.” ᴹ

Shion brightened.

“Really?” ˢʰⁱ

“Really.” ᴹ

“May I?” ʸᵏ

The conversation grew easier after the food had stopped being an examination.

Shion asked Yuka what she and Mai had gone to see. Yuka described the part which had interested her without turning it into a request for Shion to arrange a further visit.

Shion listened, then asked where it was.

“Would you like to come?” Yuka asked. ʸᵏ

“Something I should know?” ᴿ
