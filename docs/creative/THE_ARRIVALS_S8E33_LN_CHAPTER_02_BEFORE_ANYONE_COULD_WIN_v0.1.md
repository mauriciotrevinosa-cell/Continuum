* * *

They had not begun the morning with that test.

The first had been another physical return, this time with a registered participant bringing an ordinary carried object through the field. The second had checked whether Ramiris could remove a participant who was still resisting someone else's pull.

She could, provided the removal belonged to the rules she had established before the exchange began.

That distinction had become important when Gojo asked whether being prepared to interfere was the same as being able to interfere with anything at all.

Ramiris had told him no with sufficient force to end the possibility of anyone interpreting her confidence as a promise of omnipotence.

After that they had moved on to the exposure Yuta had offered to take.

It was brief enough to be a measured interruption, with Gojo and Yuta agreeing the release beforehand. Shoko had checked him before and after. Ramiris had compared what the return preserved with what it restored.

They repeated the small version only after Yuta said he wanted the second comparison.

Mau watched from outside the boundary.

He had not been asked to prove the room could survive killing him with people still taking refuge inside him.

They were making a contest he could actually imagine asking Yuka to enter.

* * *

The things they could not establish went on a different page.

Nothing which rewrote a person's allegiance. Nothing which destroyed the part of a person Ramiris needed to return. Nothing which continued attacking after the match had ended, or prevented the host from ending it at all.

They did not need to agree that those things were impossible.

They needed to agree they were not part of this game.

Guy read that page when it reached him.

“You wish to see what people can do and still send them home pleased with the invitation.” ᴳᵘ

* * *

The boundary took another discussion.

It would remain fixed. Nobody would win by moving the line under somebody else's feet. Beyond it meant beyond it whether a person had been thrown, flown, displaced or chosen a route through a shadow without returning inside before the count.

Flight was permitted above the fighting area, within the space Ramiris actually controlled. Trying to take the fight into the stands was not.

Weapons, bodies, magic and techniques would be checked against the same requirement: the host must be able to end the exchange, and what the people agreed to risk must remain within what they had tested.

There would be no new rule declaring a knee on the floor a loss halfway through somebody's bout. A surrender counted. Leaving the boundary counted. Being unable to continue counted, with Ramiris and the agreed officials stopping it before somebody mistook persistence for permission.

If the clock ended, the decision would belong to what had happened in the match: clean attacks, controlled positions and credible opportunities to finish. Standing behind a defence without doing anything else would not become a victory simply because a person had avoided being hit.

Mau looked at Gojo.

Gojo looked offended before anyone said his name.
