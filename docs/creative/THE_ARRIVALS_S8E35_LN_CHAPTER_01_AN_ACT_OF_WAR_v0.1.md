“HQ.” ᵁ

The attendant looked between them.

Umaru folded her arms.

* * *

They were standing beside one of the rooms being used to receive the new arrivals.

Gazel had stopped there to speak to someone about a sample. Luminous had just come out of the adjoining room. There were guards, attendants and several people whose work depended on noticing when the friendly part of a conversation was about to become expensive.

Umaru looked at the page again.

“This is an injustice.” ᵁ

Rimuru put his cup down.

“Is it?” ᴿ

“An act of war against the Fort.” ᵁ

One of the visiting officials went very still.

He looked toward the guards before looking back at the girl. The Fort had been mentioned several times since he arrived, always by people who seemed to consider its presence entirely ordinary. He had not yet established whether it was a household, a military position or a title whose owner happened to look younger than most of the people in the room.

In this company, none of those answers would have surprised him.

“I will contact the others,” Umaru continued, “so we can consider an appropriate punishment.” ᵁ

The official began to speak.

Gazel lifted one hand, gently enough to keep him from making the uncertainty into a declaration.

Rimuru laughed.

* * *

“Would the Fort consider reparations?” he asked. ᴿ

Umaru regarded him with the grave attention the word deserved.

“That depends.” ᵁ

“A large basket of Tempest sweets.” ᴿ

“How large?” ᵁ

Rimuru considered the company which would help Umaru empty it.

“Large enough to share.” ᴿ

“With the whole Fort?” ᵁ

“With the whole Fort.” ᴿ

She appeared to examine the proposal for hidden territorial demands.

Mau looked at Yuka.

She had pressed her lips together in a manner which suggested he should not rely upon her to negotiate anything at all for the next few moments.

“That will suffice,” Umaru said. “Thank you for your cooperation.” ᵁ

Rimuru inclined his head.

“A pleasure.” ᴿ

Umaru handed the page back to the attendant.

She had already turned toward the courtyard before remembering to look stern one last time.

“We will be expecting the delivery.” ᵁ

“Of course.” ᴿ
