*When: Souei asks whether she needs another moment, Yuka is checking the knot.*

“No.” ʸᵏ

He looked at the rope, then at her hands.

“You brought that deliberately.” ˢᵒ

“Yes.” ʸᵏ

Souei accepted the answer without asking her to explain it for his benefit.

Yuka tightened the knot one last time and put the free part where she could reach it. The length was an ordinary thing she had chosen to bring out of their packed belongings. Beretta had checked it with her equipment. It would break under forces which several people in the audience could apply without noticing.

She had no intention of asking it to hold one of those people by strength.

Across the barrier, Mau found her eyes.

He did not mouth an instruction.

He smiled.

* * *

She began with the Dog.

Souei's first movement was so slight that Yuka almost answered the place he had been standing instead of the change which followed it. The Dog cut toward his side. A second shape appeared where the first had just ceased to be useful.

Yuka moved before the blade reached her sleeve.

Steel thread caught the cloth.

She turned with the pull, using the movement to draw her own weapon without leaving the arm in the place he had chosen. The Dog came back across her retreat.

Souei let the thread go.

The second figure disappeared beneath the attack.

Yuka landed, saw the next thread lying lower than the first and stepped over it instead of giving him the same turn twice.

He smiled very slightly.

Then three figures moved.

* * *

In the seats, Maki stopped leaning back.

Yuta watched the hand nearest Yuka's weapon rather than the face above it.

Mau kept both hands on his knees.

The urge to say something useful was astonishingly strong. Not because she needed him to supply the idea, but because he could see the moment in which giving one would become very easy to excuse.

He did not.

Yuka crossed beneath the first reach, let a rabbit take the place where the second expected her ankle and turned into the third before the three attacks could agree on where she was.

Maki smiled.

Mau let out the breath he had been holding.

* * *

Souei had not expected the rabbits to obscure his vision for long.

They did not.

They gave her enough movement across the floor that the next thread had to be adjusted. Yuka saw the adjustment and used the small difference to find which of the figures had chosen it before the others followed.

She attacked that one.

The blade met his guard.

For the first time, the answer had weight all the way through it.

He was there.

She nearly smiled too soon.

Souei changed his grip, let her weapon slide past the place she had meant to hold and caught her wrist with the thread he had already brought around behind the rabbits.

Pain snapped across the joint.

She released the weapon rather than letting him use it to turn her whole arm.

It struck the floor beyond her reach.

The Dog came between them while she pulled free.

* * *

Her wrist hurt.

There was a line of blood where she had been fractionally too slow to give up the first position. Souei could see it. She did not insult him by pretending she had meant to be there.

She changed her breathing and recalled the native forms.

The rabbits thinned away. The Dog disappeared into the shadow at her feet.

Only then did she reach for the offered shape she had learned through the contract.

For a brief moment, Mau felt the familiar extra demand.

He recognised it without confusing it with an invitation to take over.

Yuka took the small surface ahead of the approaching line and turned it.

Souei's thread changed its apparent approach. The space it had crossed was no longer presented to her at the angle he had aimed for.

She stepped through the opening and reached her fallen weapon.

The borrowed operation shook at the edge of her control.

She closed it before it failed and rolled clear of the second attack.

* * *

Souei let her stand.

It was not mercy. He had seen which operation she had needed to release before reaching for the next. That small separation belonged to him now as much as the changed surface belonged to her.

Yuka saw him measuring it.

She could attempt to be faster than the understanding he had already gained, or she could make the understanding useful to her too.

She lowered the weapon.

The next shadow opened close to her feet.

Souei's gaze moved toward it, then to the corner of the floor where the second attack would ordinarily emerge.

Yuka called the Toad.

Its reach caught one of the false bodies and drew it across her front. The figure broke apart under the turn of her blade.

The real thread came from her left.

She let it find the rope.

* * *

For a breath, Souei thought he had caught her wrist again.

The movement answered him. The resistance arrived from the right height, with the right shape of hesitation before a person decided to sacrifice balance to save the joint.

Then the loop slipped free of her hand.

Yuka dropped beneath the pull.

The ordinary rope did not stop the thread. It travelled with it, carrying the false weight of a small object she had let sink into her shadow before the match began.

Souei's next attack was already moving toward the person he expected to rise at the end of that resistance.

She was not there.

The Garden spread beneath the smaller space she had chosen.

* * *

It was not a perfect domain.

The edge wavered. Yuka could feel exactly where she had asked it to hold longer than it liked. She did not try to cover the whole arena and call the failure courage.

She needed the place between Souei's real step and the position from which he meant to finish the exchange.

A dark duplicate rose into the attack he had already committed.

The blade cut through it.

Yuka came from the other side, breathing hard enough to feel the cost of each movement in her ribs.

Souei turned.

He was fast enough to make the first angle useless. Skilled enough to leave her with a weapon pointed at the place his throat had just occupied.

The Toad's tongue caught the arm he needed to recover the guard.

Yuka took one more step.

This time the point stopped beneath his jaw.

* * *

The whole thing held for less than the breath in which she understood it had worked.

Souei could feel the strain in the field. Another moment might have given him the opening he needed. A different sort of fight might have given him reasons to take an injury and continue through it.

This one had begun with an agreement about what a controlled finish meant.

He looked at the point, then at her.

“I yield.” ˢᵒ

Yuka withdrew the blade immediately.

The Garden broke around her feet.

She stood on ordinary floor, shaking in the hand which had held the edge steady.

For a moment the audience was too far away to matter.

She had heard him say it.

* * *

Souei waited while she recalled the Toad.

Then he bent to pick up the end of the rope she had dropped.

It had been cut nearly through. The knot was still there.

“I watched the wrong weight,” he said. ˢᵒ

“I was hoping you would.” ʸᵏ

“A little more than I intended.” ʸᵏ

“I would like another attempt sometime.” ˢᵒ

That part pleased her almost as much as hearing the result.
