* * *

Shion struck first.

The sword came down with a weight which made several people behind the barrier sit back before remembering what separated them from it.

Maki moved to the side.

The blade broke the stone where she had stood. Her own weapon reached Shion's ribs before the broken pieces had finished rising.

Shion turned enough to take the hit across her guard rather than the open side.

It still moved her.

Maki followed the displacement, keeping the point between them while Shion recovered the larger sword. The next cut came horizontally. Maki lowered herself beneath it, caught the shaft closer to the end and struck upward into the space beneath Shion's hands.

Shion had to let the intended return go.

She stepped back.

Maki grinned.

* * *

For the next few exchanges, strength did not solve the thing Shion wanted it to solve.

Maki was not where the full cut arrived. When Shion shortened it, she was near enough to make that shortening awkward. When she tried to catch the next approach with her free hand, Maki gave her the wrong part of the shaft and drove the butt into her shoulder.

The sound was unpleasantly solid.

Shion's expression changed.

Across the audience, Rimuru leaned forward.

Diablo looked delighted by something he had decided not to explain.

“Don't,” Rimuru said without looking at him. ᴿ

“I have said nothing.” ᴰⁱ

“Continue.” ᴿ

* * *

Maki saw it.

The next attack began with less of the broad motion Shion had offered first. There was still more than enough force behind it to be dangerous. What changed was where Shion intended the exchange to end.

Maki met the early part with the haft and moved inside the blade's best reach.

The pressure changed at contact.

She felt the difference through both hands before she could move far enough to make it somebody else's problem. The sword's force was not merely following the visible weight of the swing.

The polearm bent.

Maki released the lower hand and let the weapon turn with the demand instead of allowing the shaft to decide where her wrist should go.

She came away with a shallow cut across the outside of her arm.

Shion did not pursue the blood.

She pursued the space Maki needed to put both hands back where she preferred them.

* * *

“There,” Yuta said softly. ʸᵗ

“The result at the end of the cut.” ʸᵗ

Maki had only what she could read from the floor.

She was already changing the question.

* * *

She stopped offering the polearm as the place where the whole force would arrive.

The next three touches were brief. An angle, a turn, a weapon taken out of the way before contact could become a contest of who could spend more on it.

Shion's sword met air.

Maki's foot struck the outside of her knee.

Shion held her balance, but the hold cost her the immediate answer. Maki used it to bring the polearm across her arms, press close and force a position in which the larger blade could not complete its best cut without being withdrawn first.

For a moment, she had it.

Shion looked down at the bind.

Then she stepped toward Maki rather than away.

Maki felt the body behind the movement and changed her feet.

The boundary was nearer than it had been when she began the sequence.

* * *

She had enough room to turn out.

Doing it would give Shion the sword back at the best part of its reach. Remaining would keep the bind and require Maki to answer the body now coming through it.

She chose the bind.

Shion accepted the choice with a short, delighted laugh and drove upward through the trapped weapons.

The polearm cracked.

Maki was already letting go when Shion's other hand caught the lower part of it. The pull went through the remaining leverage instead of the place Maki expected a strike.

Her feet left the floor.

She turned in the air, using the broken shaft to change her angle before the sword could follow.

Shion did not throw it after her.

She only had to make the next landing inconvenient.

Maki crossed the boundary, struck the outside floor with one foot and pushed back toward the proper side before the second could settle.

Shion's reach held the route she needed.

The count ended.

* * *

Maki landed on both feet and looked at the line.
