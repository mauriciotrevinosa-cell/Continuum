* * *

The first spell formed before she had finished moving her hand.

There was no travelling fireball for the audience to follow. A compressed force took shape over the place Diablo had been offered as a starting position and struck downward hard enough to leave the floor answering in concentric breaks.

He was already beside it.

Carrera's second release followed the escape rather than the first point of impact.

Diablo caught the edge with a barrier so narrow Mau saw it only when the pressure split across it. One side struck the floor. The other passed into the host's containment above the empty part of the arena.

Ramiris watched the line and nodded once.

Diablo smiled up at Carrera.

“A warm greeting.” ᴰⁱ

She sent another.

* * *

It became obvious very quickly why the declarations at the table had taken time.

The things they were doing had edges. Places where a person had agreed to end the operation rather than carrying it into the sort of answer which would not leave an opponent available to congratulate afterward.

Inside those edges, neither was being gentle.

Diablo moved through a space Carrera had left apparently unoccupied and found the pressure waiting there. His sleeve tore. He put a barrier across the second part before it could take the arm with it and came out nearer her than he had been when she began.

His hand reached for the wrist preparing the next spell.

Carrera struck him across the body with the other.

The impact sent him through the broken stone.

Several people in the seats made an involuntary sound.

Diablo stood up laughing softly.

* * *

Gojo had stopped making comments.

He watched where the barriers appeared, how much of their effect belonged to the place they occupied and how much to the relationship between one prepared surface and the next.

Shoko looked at him.

“Interesting?” ˢ

“Very.” ᴳ

“Try not to look too pleased when somebody hits him.” ˢ

Gojo glanced at her.

“He looks pleased.” ᴳ

She looked back at the floor.

Diablo had indeed acquired the sort of smile which made the advice difficult to defend.

* * *

Carrera changed the shape of the next attack while Diablo was still deciding how to leave the last.

The pressure did not come from the direction she had made obvious. It gathered along a shorter line, struck the barrier he brought up and drove it back toward him instead of splitting harmlessly around it.

He gave ground.

She followed, taking away the interval he had been using to prepare the next arrangement.

For a moment, she had him defending one thing at a time.

That was the moment she enjoyed most.

It was also the one he had needed.

The barrier behind his left shoulder had not broken with the first impact.

He let her see the next one fail.

Then he moved.

* * *

Carrera turned with him rather than firing at the false position.

Her hand caught his forearm. The magic at her fingers burned through the end of the ruined sleeve.

Diablo did not pull the arm free.

He used the contact to keep her next turn attached to his own, stepped close enough to deny the best part of the release and let the remaining pressure strike the surface he had left behind them.

It rebounded at a changed angle.

Not back into her body. Across the route she needed to keep her balance.

Carrera felt the floor go away beneath her intended next step.

She corrected in the air.

Diablo was there to make her choose the longer correction.

For a fraction, the two of them stood almost politely close beside the boundary.

“After you,” he said. ᴰⁱ

Carrera tried to drive her elbow into his face.

He turned with it.

The last part of her body crossed the line before the return count ended.

* * *

She landed outside and stared at him.

Then she laughed.

“I had forgotten how irritating you are.” ᶜᵃ

Diablo bowed.

“I am delighted to have restored something familiar.” ᴰⁱ

The burned hand stayed close to his side until they reached the return room. Shoko saw it anyway and made him sit before he could explain how insignificant it was.

Rimuru found him there.

“Enjoyed yourself?” ᴿ

“Very much.” ᴰⁱ

“Good. You can both stop being so polite now.” ᴿ

Carrera laughed again from the next chair.

* * *

Yuta asked Geld about his shoulder before asking whether he was ready.

Geld moved it, checked the fit of his own gear and nodded.

They shook hands at the centre.

Nobody needed to tell them the rules a second time.

The first collision was still loud enough to make Milim look up from the explanation of the previous match she had been giving Ori.

Yuta's sword met the shield.

Geld did not move as far as the swordsman had expected.

* * *

He had chosen his position carefully.

There was room behind him, room to each side and no convenient line which let a faster opponent turn a first successful attack into a shove out of the contest. The shield gave him the space in which to make those choices matter.

Yuta tested the outside edge.

Geld turned with it.

The second attack came lower. He caught it against the angle he had preserved beneath the shield and answered with a strike of his own.

Yuta gave up the position before the impact could make him keep it.

His smile was small, rueful and entirely unperformed.

“You make that look easy.” ʸᵗ

“It took practice,” Geld said. ᴳᵉ

* * *

Yuta put his hand to the connection he had declared.

Rika arrived with enough presence to make the first row go quiet.

Geld looked up, took one measured step to change the angle and met the first reaching hand with his shield already set.

The impact drove his boots back.

He held.

Yuta came in from the other side.

For several breaths the fight became a succession of decisions Geld could not make by treating the two approaches as unrelated. The larger one demanded strength. The smaller one demanded he stop putting all his attention where the larger one wished it to be.

He answered both.

The effort showed in the next breath.

* * *

Maki watched Yuta stop smiling.

He was still enjoying himself. She could tell from the small correction he made after Geld caught the next angle with the edge of the shield.

He had simply decided the man deserved the part of his attention which would make the next exchange difficult.

Yuka noticed too.

“There he is,” she said. ʸᵏ

Maki smiled.

* * *

Yuta drew the sword back before the hit he could have completed.

Geld followed the withdrawal with his guard and found Rika's hand waiting for the place it left open. He stopped the first grip, turned the shield and struck the reaching shape hard enough to make its next movement fail.

Yuta was already beside him.

The throat markings appeared with the operation he had registered before the contest.

“Open your hand.” ʸᵗ

The command took only a brief hold.

Geld resisted it almost at once. His fingers began closing again before the weapon had fallen completely free.

Yuta coughed.

Rika caught the shield edge during the interruption and turned it outward.

The point of Yuta's sword reached the unprotected line beneath Geld's guard. His other foot had already closed the route which would have let the larger man drive forward through the position.

Geld felt the control, tested the smallest part of it and stopped.

“I yield.” ᴳᵉ

* * *

Yuta lowered the weapon before Rika released the shield.

He coughed again, more quietly, and accepted the moment he needed before speaking normally.

Geld retrieved his own weapon.

“The voice cost you.” ᴳᵉ

“You weren't an easy person to ask.” ʸᵗ

Then he smiled.

They walked out together while Rika's fuller presence withdrew at Yuta's choice, the connection still subject to its own limits rather than the audience's appetite for seeing it remain.
