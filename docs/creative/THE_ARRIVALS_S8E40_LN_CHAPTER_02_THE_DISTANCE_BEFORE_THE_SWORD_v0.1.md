*When: Benimaru changes his grip, Hinata has already noticed which foot he intends to move first.*

She did not move to answer it.

That would have told him too much.

She waited for the signal, let the first step become a commitment and met the sword far enough from her own body that the force could pass across the guard rather than through it.

Benimaru turned the follow-up before she could take the obvious opening.

The space between them filled with dark fire.

Hinata stepped through the part which had not yet closed.

Anko sat forward.

* * *

It did not look slow from the seats.

To Hinata, the first fractions had opened into enough detail to make the mistake of seeing more than she could use very easy.

The shoulder. The blade. The forming edge of the flame beyond the hand which would not hold the sword for the whole exchange.

She chose the sword.

Benimaru had expected her to.

He shortened the cut, took the parry nearer the hilt and drove his other hand toward the place she had planned to use for her next approach.

White light ran briefly along her guard.

The two forces met close enough to push them apart.

Neither followed the first retreat.

* * *

Benimaru began changing the rhythm.

The next flame was late. The sword was early. The one after it arrived with no flame at all, leaving the part of the floor she had been refusing suddenly available and therefore suspicious.

Hinata took it anyway.

He smiled.

The heat closed behind her.

She could have made the answer much larger than the question. There were names for operations neither of them had entered this contest to use on a friend of the same person.

She used the narrow one they had checked.

Light held for the breath she needed. Her sword caught his next attack at the outer edge, where his reach had committed more of the blade than he could immediately recover.

She turned beneath it.

The first clean touch struck his side.

* * *

Benimaru did not give her the second.

He drove forward through the small damage, made her defend the space nearest her own wrist and used his body to deny the easy path around him.

For three exchanges, Hinata had more to answer than the extra room in her perception could comfortably arrange.

One attack took the edge of her sleeve.

The next made her move a full step.

Benimaru followed, fire narrowing the routes she might otherwise choose to retreat along.

Hinata looked at the hand shaping it.

Then at the foot which needed to finish carrying him forward.

This time she showed him the answer she meant him to see.

* * *

He changed the sword to meet it.

She had already decided to accept the shallow cut that change would offer instead of moving the whole guard to prevent it.

The pain came across the outside of her arm.

Her own blade stayed on its route.

Benimaru caught it before the first point could reach him. The effort turned his guard across his body.

Hinata stepped close, pressed the bound weapons away from the line between their faces and put the short end of the guard beneath his jaw.

Her foot had reached the back of his lead ankle.

The fire at his other hand remained an unfinished answer.

He looked at the position.

Then he lowered that hand.

“Yours.” ᴮᵉ

Beretta confirmed it before the two could begin considering whether they enjoyed the argument enough to continue.

* * *

Nazuna had spent most of Leon's introduction examining the way the light fell inside the arena.

He waited until she looked back.

“A problem?” ᴸᵉ

“Not the room. I checked.” ᴺᵃ

His gaze went briefly toward the weapon she had chosen and then to her stance.

“And mine?” ᴸᵉ

“If you hit me with it, probably.” ᴺᵃ

That earned the smallest possible change in his expression.

The signal sounded.

Nazuna was in the air before the first beam reached the place she had left.

* * *

It was light, but not a borrowed sun and not an argument about what a vampire from another world ought to fear.

It would hurt because it was an attack.

Nazuna treated it that way.

She changed direction above the second release, came down at his flank and found the sword waiting there without the rest of him appearing to hurry.

Her hand struck his guard.

The impact moved both their feet.

She grinned.

“Good.” ᴺᵃ

Leon answered with a shorter spell.

She turned her shoulder away from the first part, took a narrow hit along the arm and felt the damaged flesh begin repairing as she moved.

Seika shifted forward in her seat.

Nazuna kept her attention on the person in front of her while he still required watching.

* * *

She reached Leon once more.

It cost a feint high enough to make him raise his guard, then a drop so close to the floor that the next beam passed above the turn she actually chose.

Her knee struck his defended side.

He moved with it.

For one satisfying instant, she had made the distance his problem.

Then the prepared light at his left closed the easy route out.

Nazuna saw the change and chose the other one.

Leon stepped where that choice would bring her.

The sword did not need to cut her to deny the landing. She twisted above it, came down outside the fixed boundary and heard the count finish before she could make the next take-off useful.

* * *

“Annoying rule.” ᴺᵃ

“You signed it.” ᴸᵉ

“Still annoying.” ᴺᵃ
