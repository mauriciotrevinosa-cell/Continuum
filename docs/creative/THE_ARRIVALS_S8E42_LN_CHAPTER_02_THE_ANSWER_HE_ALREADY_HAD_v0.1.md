*When: Mau steps forward again, Luminous notices that he has stopped trying to make every part of his defence say the same thing.*

The first approach still carried the refusal she had already examined. A direct spell would not simply cross the interval around his body because she wished it to.

The rest of the movement belonged to his hands and feet.

He let her put the barrier where it would be useful against the dagger. Turned the blade enough to make keeping the guard require another small choice. Used the separate pressure at the far side to move one loose fragment into the place where she had meant to step.

She avoided it.

His elbow reached her guard before she had finished changing the next one.

Luminous moved back, eyes narrowing.

* * *

He was not suddenly immune to the thing her hand had done.

He could still feel the cost in the body supplying him. It would have been a very foolish moment to ask that body to continue as though the existence of a great source behind it made every route equally ready to use.

He spent less on the next spell.

Blue gathered nearer the edge of her prepared position than the centre of her guard. The pull made the surrounding fragments move first.

Luminous answered the shape rather than the amount.

Mau had expected her to.

He let the operation go before it became something she could continue reading, took the sky with the next available movement and changed the surface along which the returning pieces would approach.

She cut through the nearer one.

He was already coming behind the farther.

* * *

The dagger reached her shoulder.

Not deep. He had turned the edge at the final part of the movement, letting the controlled contact say what the longer cut would have meant.

Luminous's answer struck his guarded side.

He took it badly enough to feel the breath leave him and well enough to remain in the position he had spent two separate operations obtaining.

The audience became louder.

For a few breaths, the two fighters stopped making the magic look separate from the bodies choosing where to put it.

Her hand reached for his wrist again.

Mau released the dagger rather than let the weapon decide which part of him must remain there.

The separate grip caught the falling metal outside the line between their bodies.

His now-empty hand turned beneath hers.

* * *

With one weapon no longer beside his wrist, the next turn had less steel to carry. His body and the equipment he had brought had not become a different set of numbers because he chose to open a hand.

He simply gave her a shorter attack, deliberately slower than the one she had just learned to expect.

For one instant, her answer arrived ahead of the movement he was actually making.

Mau stepped into that instant.

His palm struck the centre of the guard.

The force moved her a full pace.

The light at her hand scattered and gathered again before he could turn the opening into a finish.

She looked at him with something almost like delight.

“That one was deliberate.” ᴸᵘ

He was breathing too hard to waste the answer.

“Most of them are.” ᴹ

* * *

Ciel did not ask Noise to improve the route which had begun hurting when he tried to spend on it twice.

He was watching. Keeping the visible relationships she too had chosen to record. The way Luminous gathered one kind of force to defend and changed the same fine preparation to make contact costly.

There were differences still hidden behind the movements she had shown.

They remained hidden.

*We have enough to discuss later,* Ciel said.

*No building,* Noise answered.

Mau heard them both while catching the returning dagger.

*Later,* he agreed.

Then Luminous attacked, and the only thing which mattered was the part of her plan he could actually see.

* * *

She had stopped allowing his first choice to become the whole exchange.

The next light did not travel toward him at all. It forced the physical fragments he had placed behind her out of the positions which made them useful. Her body came after it, closing the short distance while he was deciding whether to maintain the pressure on objects which no longer deserved it.

He let them fall.

Her guard met his weapon.

The second hand touched his forearm before he could turn completely away.

He felt the draw begin.

He answered it with a movement she had already seen, fast enough to make her prepare for the same escape.

Then he took the other one.

* * *

He let the folded surface flatten completely before reaching for Limitless again.

The repulsion gathered close to his palm.

It was not a larger answer than the first Red he had used. It was a later one, placed after she had committed the hand which would have helped her turn it aside.

Luminous released him before the blast became contact.

She still had to spend the next part of the exchange leaving its path.

Mau followed the movement with his body.

One dagger took the guard. The other stopped short of her neck when the answering light reached the place his own unprotected flank would occupy if he finished the lunge.

For a moment, each could see exactly why the other had stopped.

Neither pretended it was a victory.

They disengaged together.

The clock sounded before either could make the next approach.

* * *

Mau lowered the daggers.

Luminous let the last light disappear from her hand.

The audience took longer to become quiet than the two of them had taken to stop.

Ramiris looked toward the other agreed officials. The discussion was brief, and not unanimous. Luminous's control through contact had been the strongest single answer either had found. Mau had taken more of the clean positions before and after it, forcing three guard changes into actual opportunities rather than merely remaining safe.

The decision went to him.

He breathed out slowly.

Luminous looked almost amused by the sound.

“Relieved?” ᴸᵘ

“I would have preferred another minute before needing to decide.” ᴹ

“You might have regretted receiving it.” ᴸᵘ

Mau smiled.

“I suspect my arm would have.” ᴹ

* * *

She examined the small cut at her shoulder before offering him her hand.

He took it carefully.

“You stopped treating the source as the same thing as what you could spend,” she said. ᴸᵘ

“I've had several unpleasant explanations of the difference.” ᴹ

“They appear to have been useful.” ᴸᵘ

There was no demand that he tell her who had given them.

He was grateful enough for that to say so without using the whole story.

“Thank you for the match.” ᴹ

“It answered some questions.” ᴸᵘ

Not all of them.

She made no pretence that it should have.

* * *

In the return room, she asked before touching the fresh cut again.

Mau looked toward Shoko. Shoko listened to what Luminous intended, asked one precise question and nodded when Mau said yes.

The warmth which followed was different from the contact on the floor.

It went toward the damage rather than taking from what the body was already spending. The wound closed under her hand. Nothing about the older scars changed. The blur remained where it had always remained, and the world beyond Yuka continued in the same shades.

Mau flexed the arm.

“That is considerably more pleasant.” ᴹ

“A useful distinction.” ᴸᵘ

Mau thought the second man was probably right.

He had not come here to promise a war.

Yuka asked what he wanted to drink, and for a while that was a much more interesting question.
