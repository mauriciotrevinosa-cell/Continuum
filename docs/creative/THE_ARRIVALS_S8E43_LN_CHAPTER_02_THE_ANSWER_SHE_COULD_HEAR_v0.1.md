They took their positions.

* * *

She reached him before the first broad movement of his sword could become useful.

Yuta shortened it into a guard. The contact came hard enough to make the first row hear the force and precisely enough that the sound did not explain the skill inside it.

Hinata's next step took the outside line.

He turned with her.

The blade touched his leg before the turn had finished.

He gave up the next attack to keep the wound shallow.

Across the room, Maki watched him stop smiling.

This time it happened before anybody else mistook politeness for the speed at which he could fight.

* * *

Hinata did not give him a comfortable moment in which to become two opponents.

She stayed close. Every attempt to open the distance required him to choose which part of the next attack he preferred to meet badly. Her perception supplied more of his beginning than most people could use, and she had enough practice to leave the unnecessary parts alone.

Yuta let a short hit strike his defended arm.

The cost bought the half-step he needed.

Rika appeared behind the next reaching hand.

Hinata moved before the larger presence had finished becoming the obvious thing to look at.

Her sword struck the opening beside Yuta's shoulder.

Rika's hand caught the defended angle before the blade could finish it.

For a moment, all three held a position none could continue using unchanged.

* * *

Yuta let the first defence go.

The sky surface turned beneath the line of Hinata's next attack. It did not make her forget where her sword was. It changed what the distance ahead of the sword permitted her to reach.

She shortened the strike, used the return to defend Rika's movement and gave ground before the altered surface could become a second attack at the same angle.

Yuta followed.

Hinata was already changing the next approach.

She had seen the distinction between a hand shaping the copied operation and a body still needing to stand somewhere useful to supply it.

Her blade found his guard.

The next blow reached the place the guard had just left.

* * *

Hinata began testing the larger hands as carefully as the sword.

Rika returned toward Yuta quickly when the approach became dangerous. The first time, that looked like an excellent reason not to attempt the same approach again.

The second time, Hinata made it the reason she did.

Her sword threatened Yuta's left. Rika crossed to deny it. Hinata shortened the strike into a touch against the guarding hand and used the turn to enter the space the larger body had just stopped covering.

Yuta had to answer without help.

He caught the first cut. The next struck nearer his ribs than he wanted, and he understood the question she was asking before he found a comfortable answer to it.

Rika had not become a second mind he could spend without considering where she chose to move.

Hinata was making them rearrange each other.

He stepped toward the place she had left open instead of allowing the next threat to pull Rika across him again. His sword met hers at an angle which required her to continue moving rather than wait for the gap to return.

Rika's hand came behind the new approach.

Hinata had to leave the better position.

For one breath, Yuta smiled at the larger presence beside him before looking back at his opponent.

“You noticed quickly,” he said. ʸᵗ

“You gave me two opportunities.” ᴴⁱ

“I should stop doing that.” ʸᵗ

Hinata's next attack made the advice immediately relevant.

* * *

He felt it across his ribs.

Fresh pain, familiar enough to know what required immediate attention and what could remain uncomfortable while he answered the person in front of him.

Rika forced space between them.

Yuta repaired the worst part at a cost he did not need an audience to count for him. He had not stopped being finite because a second reserve stood beside him.

Hinata saw the spending.

She attacked the transition.

He closed the surface turn before reaching for a different copied operation.

The markings appeared at his mouth.

Hinata's guard changed before he spoke.

* * *

The sound did not reach her in the way the command required.

She had declared and checked the small barrier with the organisers, just as Yuta had declared the brief motor interruption. It did not negate every curse another world might contain. It prevented her hearing the part of this one which needed to be heard.

The cost was other sounds as well.

She could see Rika.

She could see Yuta.

She could not use the little scrape beneath her own next step to tell how the angle behind her had changed.

Yuta did not speak.

He had wanted the guard.

* * *

The markings disappeared when he closed that operation.

Hinata understood the feint before he had finished the next movement. She released the sound barrier, turned toward Rika's approach and kept her sword between the larger hand and the part of her body it meant to hold.

Yuta took the surface beside that guard.

This turn was smaller than the first.

It changed the reach just enough.

Her blade missed the point where it should have stopped his shoulder. He came inside it before she could recover the better line, and Rika's hand caught the guarded arm she could no longer move away without opening the centre.

Hinata struck with the other hand.

Yuta caught the movement on the way in and placed his sword where the next continuation would be expensive for both of them.

They stopped.

* * *

For a moment Hinata tested the position without moving enough to begin another exchange.

The hold was real. So was the strain in the man maintaining the point. She had left him costs he would carry out of the arena even though the match had not lasted long enough to require the whole window he had declared.

He had still reached the better finish.

“I yield.” ᴴⁱ

Rika released her at once.

Yuta lowered the sword and let the last of the copied operation close.

He looked down at the leg which had begun hurting more clearly now that nobody was trying to strike it.

“That first one was very unpleasant.” ʸᵗ

“It was intended to be.” ᴴⁱ
