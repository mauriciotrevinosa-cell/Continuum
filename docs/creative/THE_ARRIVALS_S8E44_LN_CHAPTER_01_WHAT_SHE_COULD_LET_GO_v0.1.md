Shion did not strike the same refusal three times and wait for someone to tell her it was impressive.

She changed where she stood. Let the blade travel along the interval instead of trying to finish it. Watched the way Yuka's feet moved to keep the defence attached to the position she wanted to hold.

The next cut struck the floor beside them.

Water and stone rose together.

Yuka stopped the direct fragments. The breaking surface still required her to step, and Shion had chosen the side toward which the step would be easiest.

She was there when Yuka took it.

The second impact did not reach the body.

It reached the decision about where the body could go next.

Yuka gave up the neutral operation before holding it could become the only idea she had left.

The small sky turn caught the next approach obliquely.

She stumbled back.

Shion followed.

The next contact came at the end of the cut, with the late change in force Yuka had watched during Maki's match.

This time she did not try to catch it.

She moved the surface it would arrive along and let the last part pass where she had already decided not to stand.
