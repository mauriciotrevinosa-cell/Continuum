*When: Mau wakes, Yuka is sitting beside the bed with her feet tucked beneath her and a question she has apparently decided not to ask while he is asleep.*

“How long?” he asks. ᴹ

“Long enough that I was beginning to consider keeping you.” ʸᵏ

He turns his head toward her.

The room is quiet. Nobody has put a new set of notes beside his pillow. The repaired shoulder answers when he moves it, sore in the ordinary way of a body which has been asked to do something difficult and then allowed to stop.

Yuka watches the movement without asking him to perform it twice.

“Better?” ʸᵏ

“Much.” ᴹ

“Good.” ʸᵏ

She puts the question aside a little longer and comes to lie beside him.

* * *

They stay that way until being awake begins to feel less like another obligation.

Mau puts an arm around her. She settles with the easy familiarity which still sometimes catches him by surprise, not because it is new but because he is happy to find it waiting without needing to earn it again each morning.

“I want to learn what to do when the Garden loses,” she says eventually. ʸᵏ

He looks toward her.

“Before it loses,” she corrects. “Something I actually know how to hold. Not a different name I try because I don't like the first answer.” ʸᵏ

“You should ask them to teach you.” ᴹ

“I was going to.” ʸᵏ

Mau smiles.

“Then I should come and listen too.” ᴹ

She looks pleased by that in a way which matters more than if he had promised to put the whole answer in her hand.

* * *

By the time they go downstairs, several hours have passed since the last quarter.

Yuta has eaten, rested and waited for his own technique to answer again without trying to buy the return through the larger reserve beside him. Gojo has submitted to the last arm check without making it take longer through argument. Milim has visited the Fort and returned with a proposal which Ramiris immediately refuses to accept as a new spectator seating rule.

The four remaining competitors sit around a table.

It lasts very little time before someone suggests they should stop pretending they want to watch two of them wait while the others have an interesting fight.

Yuta looks at Gojo.

Gojo looks at Mau.

Milim grins.

“All four.” ᴹⁱˡ

* * *

Rimuru makes them ask the host before the enthusiasm becomes the plan.

Ramiris asks where they intend to put the first enormous mistake. Shoko asks whether any of them has confused resting for a few hours with becoming an entirely new person. Beretta asks how temporary alliances should be counted.

“Allowed,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“Until they're inconvenient,” Gojo adds. ᴳ

Mau smiles.

Milim looks delighted by the additional possibility of betrayal.

Rimuru considers the schedule with Jinshi.

“Five minutes,” he says. ᴿ

Veldora begins offering an opinion from the doorway.

“Not fifty,” Jinshi adds before the opinion can become substantial. ᴶ

* * *

They agree on it before anybody goes back to the seats.

No outside person enters the match. Declared partners remain declared partners. The fixed boundary, the return, the surrender and the host's right to stop an unsafe continuation remain exactly what they were.

The narrow information exposure does not become permission for a larger one because more people are present to enjoy it.

Mau listens to that part without looking away.

Shoko looks toward him.

“One operation,” she says. ˢ

“One,” he replies. ᴹ

The empty reconstruction he checks afterward is the familiar boundary he already knows how to make, without a mental payload. No third line begins opening behind the first. No attempt to make three unrelated techniques live inside one working brain because the audience might applaud.

He closes it and waits for the ordinary consequence to pass before agreeing the final check is complete.

The actual finale waits with him.

* * *

Yuka is there when he comes back.

“You asked to do this?” ʸᵏ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Because you want to?” ʸᵏ

He looks toward the other three, who have begun disagreeing about which one is most likely to make the first temporary alliance immediately inconvenient.

“Very much.” ᴹ

Yuka smiles.

“Then enjoy it. And remember I was promised a little more of this city afterward.” ʸᵏ

He leans down to kiss her.

“A very important appointment.” ᴹ

* * *

When the spectators return, the change is announced before the four enter.

There will be no semifinal result somebody has somehow missed. All four have asked to finish together, with the same host, the same checks and a little more time than an opening match to make each other regret a decision.

The applause begins before the explanation ends.

In the Fort seats, Umaru looks at Milim's place, then at the much larger floor they will use.

“We should have negotiated more sweets,” she tells Rem. ᵁ

Rem laughs.

Below them, the four competitors take their places.

For a moment Mau can see every person he is about to try to hit without remembering a single reason he ought to be afraid of coming home afterward.

Then Gojo smiles at him across the floor.

The host asks whether they are ready.
