*When: the signal sounds, Yuta attacks the person everyone expects him to leave until later.*

Gojo catches the blade against the defence around his body and laughs.

“Impatient?” ᴳ

“I've been waiting very politely.” ʸᵗ

Rika comes around the other side.

The first Blue changes the approach of the larger hand. The next movement changes Yuta's, making the two attacks arrive at a less comfortable distance from each other than the swordsman has chosen.

Yuta takes the sky surface beneath his blade and turns it.

Gojo has to move.

Across the floor, Milim grins at Mau.

“Your turn.” ᴹⁱˡ

* * *

Her first punch stops short of him.

She has watched enough of the morning not to be surprised by the interval. Instead of continuing to push a fist through the same impossible approach, she drops beneath it and strikes the floor.

The whole piece of ground Mau intended to stand on becomes an argument.

He goes up with Blue.

Milim is already following.

His fist meets her raised guard with enough force to make the air crack around them. Her answering kick takes the place where the rest of his body would have remained if he had expected her to wait politely for the next exchange.

He turns through it, feels the impact across his side and uses the separate grip on a loose fragment to change the landing before it becomes hers.

Milim laughs.

Mau finds himself laughing back.

* * *

The first alliance lasts approximately as long as Gojo needs to make Yuta wish somebody else would occupy him for a moment.

Mau sees the opening.

He closes his active copied operation, takes the narrow neutralising layer across his own forearm and enters the place where Infinity would otherwise finish the question before his fist arrived.

Gojo catches the strike on his guard.

The force moves him.

“You could have warned me.” ᴳ

“I thought you were observant.” ᴹ

Rika's hand comes from the other side.

Gojo moves before either man can make the temporary agreement seem permanent.

Yuta smiles at Mau.

Then he attacks him.

* * *

The sword takes the place where Mau would have brought his next guard if he had continued treating the previous three seconds as a promise.

Mau lets the neutralising layer go before gathering a small Red.

The repulsion changes Yuta's approach.

It does not finish it.

The released force meets a surface turned by the swordsman's own operation, comes off at a different angle and heads toward the part of the floor Milim has just occupied.

She leaves it with an offended shout which is half laughter.

Mau releases the copied work before taking his own sky fold. The next sword angle becomes inconvenient enough that Yuta has to withdraw the tip to preserve the better position.

Rika fills the space he leaves.

Mau gets out from beneath the larger hand a fraction before it would make him stay.

* * *

Yuka has never seen him look quite like this.

She has seen him happy. She has seen him reckless, frightened, absorbed in something difficult enough to make the rest of a room become less real than the problem he is trying to answer.

Here he is simply enjoying how inconvenient the people he loves can be when they have all been allowed to try.

Beside her, Mai makes a sound of disbelief as Milim catches the returning piece of broken stone and sends it back through the line Mau had just meant to use.

Maki laughs.

Yuka realises she is smiling so much her face hurts.

* * *

Gojo's next field takes all three of them seriously.

Two moving Blues make the floor offer stronger and weaker routes which refuse to remain in the same places. Holding them does not make him the owner of every possible movement inside the arena.

It makes each opponent decide how much attention he can afford to give the arrangement while the others continue trying to strike him.

Mau uses Shrine to break the ridge beside the lower field, then closes the cuts and moves the physical pieces with the separate grip.

Yuta turns one approach with Sky.

Milim goes through the gap the two operations leave between them before either man has time to decide whether he intended to cooperate.

Her punch reaches Gojo's defended position.

The direct contact stops.

The floor beneath his foot does not remain whole.

* * *

Gojo gives up one Blue to keep the landing.

Mau takes the interval with his own body, reaching far enough to make Gojo defend a physical approach rather than simply rebuild the more interesting pattern.

Yuta's voice comes from the side.

The markings are there.

Mau takes the instant to reinforce against the sound before the command can become something his body has to obey. It is the ordinary cursed-energy precaution he knows, not a new power hidden inside the quiet helper at the edge of his vision.

“Back.” ʸᵗ

The precaution costs him hearing at the wrong moment.

Rika's hand strikes his guard from behind the part of the scene he has been watching.

He lands on the broken floor with much less dignity than he intended.

Yuta withdraws before the next counter can make the success uncomfortable.

* * *

Mau gets up.

Gojo looks down at him from the small height he has kept above the worst ground.

“Your friend is very rude.” ᴳ

“Your student,” Mau replies. ᴹ

Yuta looks toward them both.

“You agreed on the rules.” ʸᵗ

Milim hits the ground between all three before anybody can turn the answer into a debate.

The four separate.

The audience rises with the movement and remembers the seats only when the host's barrier catches the first wall of dust.

* * *

The next part belongs to Milim.

She stops allowing the three clever men to decide where the exciting part of the fight will be. Her first turn makes Gojo move his field. The next forces Yuta to bring Rika between himself and the approach. The third finds Mau in the moment between giving up one copied operation and reaching for another.

He catches the hit badly enough to feel it through the repaired side.

He answers with his body before trying to buy a more elaborate solution.

For one breath, the two meet in the middle of the air and neither pretends the force has been made small.

Milim moves farther than she expects.

Mau has to repair what the reply does to his shoulder.

They part grinning.

* * *

Rimuru has stopped trying to remain in the chair.

Veldora stands beside him, apparently delighted that someone else has finally been given the burden of keeping the small dragonoid entertained.

Shoko watches the four bodies and the exit she can reach without crossing their floor.

Ramiris watches the containment.

The two women exchange one brief look.

Everything is still inside the part they agreed it could be inside.

For now, the four can keep playing.

* * *

Milim catches the edge of Rika's next reach and uses it to change her direction rather than trying to win an entire separate contest against the larger hand.

Yuta has to withdraw the approach.

Gojo narrows Blue to take the route Milim has chosen.

She takes the costlier one and makes Mau receive the end of it.

His guard holds.

His feet do not stay where he put them.

For a moment all three men are correcting something the same girl has made difficult.

They look toward one another through the moving dust.

Milim sees them do it.

Her grin widens.

“Now it's getting good.” ᴹⁱˡ
