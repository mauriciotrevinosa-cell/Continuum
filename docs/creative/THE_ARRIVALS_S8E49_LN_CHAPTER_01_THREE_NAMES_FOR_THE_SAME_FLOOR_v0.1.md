*When: Yuta reaches the next opening, Milim has already decided to make him regret it.*

Her hand catches the defended blade near the angle where he expected the guard to turn it aside. He lets the weapon move rather than force his wrist to remain attached to the first plan.

Rika takes the next approach.

Milim uses the larger body as the thing she can move around while Yuta is busy preserving his own.

Gojo's Red interrupts the turn.

She leaves its path, comes down near Mau and finds the dagger he has put between her guard and the next comfortable step.

For a moment the four make a tight arrangement of costs nobody can afford to forget.

Then Milim breaks the arrangement by taking the direct hit against her raised defence and continuing through it.

* * *

Mau goes backward.

The separate grip takes enough of the motion to keep him from leaving the line he has not quite reached. He lets the daggers move with his hands, closes the copied operation before choosing the next and finds Gojo already altering the space beside the place Milim will need to land.

Yuta comes from the other side.

This time Milim does not have one easy body to move around.

She still finds a way.

The first strike takes Yuta's guard. The second makes Gojo give up the better position. The third comes toward Mau before the narrow Blue he has begun shaping can make the approach safe.

He releases it.

His body meets the part the spell will no longer stop.

* * *

The impact hurts.

It does not tell him the afternoon has become a different room.

He can hear people cheering. He can see Yuka beyond the barrier. There is a real host standing close enough to end the exchange if the next choice stops being one they agreed to make.

Mau turns the pain into the repair it actually needs and looks toward the other two men.

Gojo has the expression he wears when the interesting answer requires someone else to understand it quickly.

Yuta is already moving closer.

Milim sees the change.

“Together?” she asks. ᴹⁱˡ

Gojo grins.

“Temporarily.” ᴳ

* * *

They do not become better at punching her simply because they have admitted the problem aloud.

They become less willing to leave each other the wrong moment in which to answer it.

Yuta's surface turn takes the edge of the next approach out of the route Mau needs. Mau's separate force moves the broken piece which would have made Gojo's landing late. Gojo's Blue narrows the place Milim can choose without having to take a real hit on the way through.

She takes one.

Then lands another.

The alliance nearly falls apart under the straightforward information that she remains very difficult to stop.

All three laugh at once, short and breathless.

Milim looks delighted.

* * *

The next approach brings them close enough to choose a different kind of answer.

Gojo reaches for Yuta's sleeve with his free hand. Mau puts his shoulder directly against the other side, close enough for the brief physical contact to be exactly what the native technique's exception requires.

Yuta understands.

Mau closes the last foreign operation completely.

*One,* Ciel says privately.

*One,* he agrees.

No new route opens behind it. No second unrelated technique tries to live inside the same working channel. What he has is the reconstructed boundary he already knows, deliberately empty of the mental burden he is not using.

Gojo's own prepared interval remains the one the host has checked.

Not a longer one because they are tired.

The information touches the one participant Gojo is not physically touching.

He ends the payload at the tested cap.

The three boundaries strike, strain and fail to settle, their brief collision closing around the place where the ordinary arena is already returning.

It is not one man combining three domains.

It is three men discovering how quickly three very expensive decisions can make the same floor impossible to keep.

* * *

Milim stops.

The raised hand remains halfway through the next attack. Her expression does not become frightened. It simply fails to reach the next part of itself as quickly as the rest of the room does.

Gojo lowers his hand.

Infinity is gone with the ordinary silence of the spent channel.

Yuta lets his own domain finish closing instead of trying to preserve an attack after the reason for it has ended.

Mau's copied route answers his next reflex with the same raw absence.

None of them tries to make a different technique's name repair it.

The host's stop signal sounds before anybody moves to strike again.

Mau reaches her first.

Not with a weapon.

He has put both daggers away before coming close enough that the next movement would matter.

Yuta comes beside him. Gojo is already looking toward Shoko, one hand lifted to show that the exposure has ended and no additional operation remains running.

Milim blinks once.

Her body still has not chosen the next coherent movement.

“Give her space,” Shoko calls. ˢ

Behind them, the floor remains still.

For the first time in five minutes, nobody is trying to win anything.
