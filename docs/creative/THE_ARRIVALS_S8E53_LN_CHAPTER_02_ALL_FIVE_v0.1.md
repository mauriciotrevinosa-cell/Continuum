*When: Kenya reaches the doorway, he stops so abruptly that Ryota almost walks into him.*

Shizue is sitting beside the window.

Rimuru has told them who is there. Told them enough that finding her alive should not require the shock of discovering the whole thing without warning.

It does not make the doorway simple.

Shizue looks up.

“Hello.” ˢᶻ

Kenya's mouth opens.

For once, he has no answer prepared.

* * *

Alice goes first.

Not because someone has told her she ought to be brave for the others. She has simply reached the point where continuing to stand farther away is harder than crossing the room.

Shizue opens her arms.

The girl reaches them before deciding whether she intends to cry.

Gale comes after her. Ryota takes one more moment, looks toward Rimuru as though checking he will not wake the room by believing it and follows when the slime smiles.

Kenya moves when the others have already made it impossible to pretend he will be the person who greets her very casually.

Chloe waits until there is room for her hand.

Shizue takes it.

* * *

They do not all fit comfortably in the first embrace.

They try anyway.

Rimuru moves the chair before it becomes the thing everybody trips over. Shizue laughs through the tears when someone begins explaining the part the person beside him has not yet finished explaining.

Kenya insists there are things she needs to know immediately.

Alice informs him that she also has things.

Gale tries to put the stories in order.

Ryota looks at her hand and asks whether she feels all right.

Shizue answers that one first.

“Yes.” ˢᶻ

He believes the answer a little more when she says it again while looking directly at him.

* * *

Chloe remains near the bed after the first rush has found places to sit.

There are heavy things she does not bring into this room. They remain where she and the other presence have agreed to keep them while she has a day which belongs to being here.

Shizue asks what she has been enjoying.

Chloe looks up.

Then begins telling her about the Fort.

The other four interrupt to improve the explanation. Shizue listens to the resulting disagreement with the increasingly astonished happiness of somebody being given a future in the form of things which would once have seemed too ordinary to save.

Rimuru stays near the door.

For a while, the wish he carried does not need anything from him except remaining where they can find him.

* * *

The first check has already established that the new body is doing the ordinary work of being alive without the spirit which once kept the old one going.

Shoko has asked before touching, explained what she is checking and left Shizue room to say when she has had enough new information.

There will be things to learn about this world.

They do not begin with making the first afternoon an examination of everything Shizue might be useful for.

When the children become hungry, she notices before they do.

“We should find you food.” ˢᶻ

“We can find you food,” Kenya says at once. ᴷᵉ

Alice agrees loudly enough to make the rest of the room laugh.

* * *

Mau and Yuka meet them in the courtyard.

Ori is beside Frieren. Umaru and Rem have kept space near the table while the visitors come through. Nobody begins by asking Shizue to tell the part of her life which makes other people look solemn.

Yuka introduces herself.

Shizue sees the hand in Mau's, the small silver band and the way the people at the table make room without turning the welcome into a ceremony.

She sits where she chooses.

Rimuru comes beside her only after asking which place she would like him to take.

Umaru begins explaining the Fort with the confidence of a person whose territory has recently negotiated a successful settlement with a much larger government.

Shizue listens very seriously.

* * *

The visit comes from Chloe first.

Not as a demand. She asks whether the Fort at home is different from the temporary place they have made in the guest courtyard.

Ori begins answering before the sentence is finished.

Umaru takes over to clarify the political distinctions.

Rem lets them explain enough to make the next question unavoidable.

“Could we see it?” Ryota asks. ᴿʸ

The table becomes quieter for a moment.

Mau looks toward Rimuru.

Rimuru looks at the household, not at the number of chairs as though that number can make the choice for them.

“We could invite them for a few days,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

* * *

The answer does not belong to one person.

Fern asks who wants to come and who will be told in Tempest. Maki asks what the children have been promised about returning. Shoko asks for an ordinary sleeping arrangement rather than a cheerful intention to discover one after everyone is tired.

Shizue listens before looking toward Rimuru.

“You were going back with them.” ˢᶻ

He nods.

“I'd like to come too,” she says. ˢᶻ

The room in the future drawing has not been built.

“I can bring the students back after the visit.” ᴴⁱ

Anko looks toward her.

Hinata meets the glance without pretending she has forgotten the other person she wants to see at the end of that road.

“If the arrangements suit everyone,” she adds. ᴴⁱ

All five children choose the visit. Nobody calls it a permanent move. The people remaining responsible in Tempest will have the route and the return plan before the group leaves.

Then toward Rimuru.

“I would like to see your home,” she says. ˢᶻ
