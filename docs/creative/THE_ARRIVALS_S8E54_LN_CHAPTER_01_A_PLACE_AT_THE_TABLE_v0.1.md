# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 47
## Chapter 17 — A Place at the Table

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S8 v0.1 (2026-10-30)
**Volume:** 47
**Reading order:** 17
**Source episode:** S8E54
**Continuity state:** B+45 afternoon. Tempest residents discuss choices without Mau/Rimuru present; prospective visits not compulsory migration. Actual temporary accommodation by residents' consent, subject to checking fit at home: five pupils/Fort three nights B47–49, Hinata return B50; Shizue lower bed in Rimuru/Diablo room by Diablo's voluntary loan, Rimuru keeps separate loft; Diablo screened travel bed in music room after practice for those nights, band permission; Hinata travel cot Anko/Nazuna room both agree, no implied bed-sharing. No empty guestwing/build/ArrivalHouse reopening. Ranga remains Tempest this trip, future visit chosen. Children and adults retain agency.

*When: Shion has written Rimuru's name at the top of the page three times before Shuna turns it over.*

“You can start with yours.” ˢʰᵘ

Shion looks at the clean side.

Across the table, Geld holds a pencil much too carefully for someone whose hands could carry the whole table. Kurobe has already found a fault in the chair beneath him. Benimaru is standing by the window, where he can see the people leaving the courtyard without having to explain why he is watching.

Rimuru is with Shizue and the children. Mau has gone to find the people who know what happens to the house when its occupants run out of beds.

There is nobody here to make a choice on their behalf.

Shion writes her own name.

Then, underneath it, she writes *visit*.

* * *

“I haven't decided how long.” ˢʰⁱ

“Then don't put a number yet.” ˢʰᵘ

“It looks unfinished.” ˢʰⁱ

Shuna gives her a patient look.

Shion frowns at the page, but leaves the space blank.

She wants to see the place where Diablo has somehow acquired arguments about laundry which do not involve their lord. She wants to know why Rimuru laughs differently when a small human girl threatens him with a declaration of war. She also wants to return to her own kitchen and find that Shuna has not moved everything while she was gone.

The last part comes out as an accusation.

“You moved the bowls.” ˢʰⁱ

“The bowls were beside the cleaning supplies.” ˢʰᵘ

“I knew where they were.” ˢʰⁱ

Benimaru laughs. Shion points the pencil at him until he stops.

Then she looks down again.

“I want to go,” she says. “I don't want to stop having things here.” ˢʰⁱ

Nobody tells her those are incompatible wishes.

* * *

Geld wants to see the ground before promising anybody a building.

Kurobe wants to see the tools they already use before bringing a cart of things they cannot store. Shuna would like to visit the workshop with Yuka and Mai; she would also like a date she can put on her own calendar instead of waiting to discover when everyone else has finished needing her.

Benimaru listens to them agree on the beginnings of a proposal.

“I'll stay here for now.” ᴮᵉ

Shion looks up quickly.

“For now,” he repeats. “I'm not being abandoned beside a stack of reports. We can decide who is responsible for what without pretending every person has to sleep in the same city.” ᴮᵉ

Rigurd will need the decisions in writing. There are people to consult, work already underway and people who have not been in any of these rooms.

Geld adds *after the household agrees on space* beside his own proposed visit.

Kurobe adds *measurements* beneath it.

Shion looks at both lines.

“You make going to see someone sound very difficult.” ˢʰⁱ

“Only if you promise to rebuild their home before seeing it,” Kurobe says. ᴷᵘ

She crosses out the small tower she has drawn beside Rimuru's name.

* * *

The bed problem begins with Diablo offering his.

Rimuru turns toward him so fast that his chair scrapes.

“Your actual bed?” ᴿ

“I am capable of identifying it.” ᴰⁱ

“I meant—” ᴿ

“I know what you meant.” ᴰⁱ

Shizue is sitting with them. He addresses the offer to her, explaining the lower space, the separate loft above it and the arrangement of the door. The room belongs to people who already live there. Nothing about the invitation makes sharing it compulsory.

“A curtain would help,” she says after listening. ˢᶻ

“We can arrange that,” Rimuru says. “Or look at something else if you don't like it when we arrive.” ᴿ

She glances at him.

“I'd like to try it.” ˢᶻ

Diablo inclines his head as though she has accepted a perfectly ordinary loan.

Fern asks where he intends to sleep.

His expression suggests he has anticipated the question and prepared something which will annoy Rimuru.

“I was about to ask the musicians.” ᴰⁱ

* * *

Seika hears him out with her arms folded.

“After practice.” ˢᵏ

“Naturally.” ᴰⁱ

“Packed away before they need the room.” ˢᵏ

“Yes.” ᴰⁱ

“You aren't going to decide the instruments should be somewhere else.” ˢᵏ

“I have grown attached to my continued welcome.” ᴰⁱ

Nijika starts laughing before Seika can ask the next question.

The screened travel bed will come out after the music is finished and go away in the morning. Three nights to begin with; then they will ask again rather than let a temporary arrangement quietly become someone's lost room.

Bocchi checks the place he means against the door and the equipment before nodding. Kita asks whether he wants the little lamp they keep near the wall.

Ryo wants to charge rent.

“No,” Seika says. ˢᵏ

Diablo looks almost disappointed that he will not get to negotiate.

* * *

The children are easier in one respect.

They have already formed an opinion about where they would like to sleep.

Umaru presents the Fort's offer with both hands on the table. Ori stands beside her, nodding at the parts they discussed and looking mildly offended by one which they did not.

“We didn't say they had to pass a test.” ᴼ

“A welcoming test.” ᵁ

“No.” ᴿᵉ

Rem does not even look up from the list of bedding.

The Fort can hold five visiting children without swallowing the passage beside it. The cold hearth stays cold; the kitchen route stays open. Frieren makes Ori show her where the extra cushions would go, and Chloe asks whether they are allowed to decide they would rather have a quieter place to rest.

“Of course,” Frieren says. ᶠ

Umaru considers whether a person can leave the Fort voluntarily without creating a political difficulty.

Then Ori says she sometimes does, and the argument ends.

* * *

They settle on three nights at the Inn, with the return journey beginning on the morning after the third. Hinata will accompany the students back; the adults looking after them in Tempest will have the route and the dates before anyone sets out.

Shizue wants time with them. She does not offer to become the only adult who can hear a child wake in the night.

Rem and Frieren discuss the first evening with her. Other people offer parts of the ordinary work without turning the visit into a shift someone must earn the right to enjoy.

Kenya asks whether the Fort has a roof.

“An excellent question,” Shoko says. ˢᵒ

“It has several defensive positions,” Umaru answers. ᵁ

“Does it have a roof?” ᴷᵉ

Ori takes his hand and begins explaining why the answer is complicated.

* * *

Hinata finds Anko near the courtyard door.

“I may be able to borrow a cot.” ᴴⁱ

Anko looks at her for a second, then toward Nazuna, who is listening with the air of somebody waiting to see whether she will have to make the obvious joke herself.

“There's floor space in our room,” Anko says. “If you're all right with it.” ᴬ

“Which of us?” ᴺ

“Both.” ᴬ

Nazuna lets the silence last just long enough.

“My curtain stays exactly where it is.” ᴺ

“Yes.” ᴬ

“And I still want to reach my bed.” ᴺ

Hinata smiles.

“So do I.” ᴴⁱ

They agree to check the actual placement when they arrive. A travel cot, its own screen, a clear door. Anko does not pretend that accepting the invitation says anything beyond wanting Hinata there for these days.

When their hands brush beside the table, however, neither of them is particularly interested in moving away.

* * *

Ranga will remain in Tempest on this journey.

He tells Rimuru himself, with a look toward the people he has not finished catching up with. He wants to visit the house later, when it will be a visit he has chosen rather than the shape his loyalty automatically takes whenever Rimuru turns toward a road.

Rimuru rests a hand against his fur.

“Tell me when.” ᴿ

Ranga's tail moves.

Across the courtyard, Shion has finally finished her page.

It contains her name, a visit she has not yet dated and a small tower with a very emphatic line through it.
