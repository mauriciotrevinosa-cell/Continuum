*When: Fern asks who has agreed to move, Geld looks at the page and says, “Nobody on this side of it.”*

Mau stops unfolding the plan.

Yuka looks down at the little room Rimuru has acquired through a deal he already regrets.

“We might need a different page,” she says. ʸ

“Several,” Fern answers. ᶠᵉ

There is room on the courtyard table now that dinner has been cleared. Rem has taken the children to find their scattered things. Shizue is with them because she wants to hear the rest of Alice's story, not because someone has already decided where she belongs whenever there is work to do.

Jinshi turns a clean sheet toward the people sitting opposite him.

“Let's start with what you actually want.” ᴶ

* * *

Geld's name goes under a visit to look at the ground.

A small group can come when the household is ready to receive them. He wants the boundary of the protected land, the old roots, the water and the paths people already use. He is less interested in the attractive building Mau has drawn than in what happens to the rain beneath it.

Mau looks at his page.

“We didn't draw rain.” ᴹ

“I noticed,” Geld says kindly. ᴳᵉ

Kurobe wants to see the workshop. Shuna wants to visit Mai and Yuka, and has no intention of treating that invitation as a promise to leave every other part of her life behind. Shion says she wants a visit whose length she can decide after talking to the people waiting for her here.

Fern writes what they say.

She does not put a job beside every name.

* * *

Testarossa asks which reports the household already receives.

Jinshi explains enough of the routes to keep her from promising a service somebody else has been providing for months. Hollis is still Hollis. Tamsin has not become unnecessary because a larger city owns better paper.

There are gaps between the routes which might benefit from someone speaking to both sides.

There are also people who will need to agree before anyone starts speaking in their names.

“We can send the questions first,” Testarossa says. ᵀᵉ

Jinshi nods.

Maomao, beside him, takes the second page and moves a proposed storage room away from the drinking water.

He watches her do it.

“You could have told me.” ᴶ

“You could have asked before putting it there.” ᴹᵐ

She leaves her hand on the page when his comes beside it.

Neither moves away immediately.

* * *

The missing people make the table quieter.

It happens when Yuka asks whether they should bring home a second list: people someone wants to find, rather than people who have already said they want to visit.

Mau leans back so he can see everybody.

Fern puts down her pencil.

Nobody can turn the wish into rooms and meals by writing names faster. Nobody has to give up the person they still want because another person at the table has been waiting too.

Jinshi rests a hand near the small pouch at his belt.

“Gaoshun,” he says. ᴶ

Maomao looks toward him.

“If something arrives which could be from our world, I want to know. I would like to look before deciding there is only one way to find him.” ᴶ

“We'll ask for the reports,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Ask,” Jinshi repeats. “Not promise you'll find him tomorrow.” ᴶ

Mau smiles faintly.

“Ask.” ᴹ

The choice remains Jinshi's. Nothing leaves the pouch.

* * *

Coco has been quiet while the adults speak.

When Qifrey turns the page, she touches its edge.

“We should remember the people we're looking for too.” ᶜᵒ

Richeh is beside her.

Mau sees the glance between them and waits instead of filling it.

Qifrey agrees that the list should travel home. Names they know, things which could identify a place, who should hear first if a report arrives. They do not decide the answer for the people who are missing.

“And we shouldn't bring everyone before we know where they can sleep,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

“Or tell them there is a home waiting when we mean a building we haven't drawn yet,” Kaneki adds. ᴷᵃ

He says it gently.

The names on the page are still people somebody wants to hold.

They deserve more than an enthusiastic mistake.

* * *

Hinata returns with the visit confirmed on the Tempest side.

Rigurd has the route and the planned return. Benimaru knows which children are travelling and with whom. Her own arrangements have been communicated rather than quietly abandoned for a pleasant journey.

“Three nights there,” she says. “Then I'll bring them back.” ᴴⁱ

Anko, who is leaning against the courtyard wall, looks at her.

“You make it sound as though we're going to keep them.” ᴬ

Hinata glances toward the doorway where Umaru is explaining a defensive position with a cake fork.

“I thought it was worth being specific.” ᴴⁱ

Fern makes the last note.

Then closes the book before anyone can find another reason she should remain seated beside it.

* * *

Shizue has been offered clothes which fit.

Shuna brings more than one possibility and does not look offended when the first is too elaborate for the journey Shizue has agreed to take. They talk about sleeves, shoes and what she would like to carry herself.

Rimuru stands in the doorway for a moment before she notices him.

“Am I taking too long?” she asks. ˢᶻ

“No.” ᴿ

She holds a sleeve against her wrist. This body is still new enough that its ordinary measurements occasionally surprise her.

“The children want to show me everything,” she says. ˢᶻ

“They've been practising on us.” ᴿ

“Do I need to know the rules of the Fort?” ˢᶻ

“Nobody knows all the rules of the Fort.” ᴿ

He says it with such feeling that she laughs.

He stays to listen while she chooses what to pack.

* * *

Outside, Himmel is trying to decide whether Rimuru's monument is more flattering from the other side.

Frieren has been waiting for him to admit that this is what he is doing.

“You're taking a very long time.” ᶠ

“It's an important part of the city.” ᴴᵐ

“You went around it twice.” ᶠ

He looks at her.

“You noticed.” ᴴᵐ

She is quiet for a moment, then turns toward the lamps beginning to come on beside the street.

“Are you coming?” ᶠ

He leaves the third view for another day.

* * *

Shion finds Rimuru when he is carrying the extra shoes Shizue has decided against.

She takes them from him automatically, then stops and looks down.

He waits.

“I can carry my shoes,” he says eventually. ᴿ

“They aren't yours.” ˢʰⁱ

“I can carry her shoes too.” ᴿ

Shion makes a small, unhappy sound which is too close to a laugh to hide inside her throat.

“Can I come even if there isn't anything you need me to do?” ˢʰⁱ

Rimuru's face changes.

He sets his hand over the pair she is still holding.

“Yes.” ᴿ

She looks up.

“I want you to come because you want to see us. You don't have to find a report to bring.” ᴿ

For a moment her fingers tighten around the shoes.

Then she gives them back.

“I do want to see it.” ˢʰⁱ

“Then we'll work out when.” ᴿ

She wipes at her face with an irritation he is kind enough not to mention.

“Shuna said she might change the kitchen while I'm gone.” ˢʰⁱ

“She already changed the kitchen.” ᴿ

Shion stares at him.

Rimuru looks at the shoes in his hands and realises he has made the farewell much more urgent than necessary.
