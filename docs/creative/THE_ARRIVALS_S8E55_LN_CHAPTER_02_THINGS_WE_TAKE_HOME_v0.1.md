Packing takes longer than arriving.

On the way here, everyone knew which things belonged to them. Now the guest courtyard contains cups from three different tables, a lamp someone lent Bocchi, two chairs brought in for a conversation nobody meant to make last so late and an impressive quantity of paper which Umaru insists has diplomatic value.

“Evidence.” ᵁ

“They're going in the rubbish.” ᴿᵉ

Kenya asks.

Rimuru says no with the experience of someone who has recently negotiated the same issue with a much more accomplished opponent.

Mau finds Ori's drawing beside the things Yuka has already packed.

“Inside the notes?” ʸ

The little things require their own care.

The road home is not made shorter by everyone wanting to show it to somebody new.

Hinata takes the first stretch near the children. Shizue walks until she wants the seat they have kept available with the luggage, then accepts it without having to prove she could have continued. The body is well. A long day on an unfamiliar road can still be a long day.

The old route and the stopping place have been checked. Scouts go ahead of the party; the same ordinary soldiers who have carried cases help with the heavier bags. None of them has to invent a new form to do it.

Mau walks beside Yuka until she gives him her bag.
