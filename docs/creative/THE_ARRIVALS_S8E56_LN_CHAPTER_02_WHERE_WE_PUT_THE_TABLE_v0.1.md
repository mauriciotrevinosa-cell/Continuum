*When: the map finally reaches the common table, somebody has to move the bread.*

Mau takes the plate. Rem catches the cup Yuka nearly puts an elbow into. Kaneki lifts one edge of the paper while Fern clears a place large enough to see what the drawing is trying to become.

It is a much less elegant beginning than the one Mau imagined in Tempest.

He likes it better almost immediately.

* * *

The first question is whether the tree is staying.

Ori asks it from beside Frieren, with Chloe close enough to see the circle on the page.

“Yes,” Mau says. ᴹ

“All of it?” ᴼ

“All the part the house has been careful to leave alone.” ᴹ

She studies the circle.

Then the question seems to have been answered well enough for her to care about the next thing.

“Can we still eat here?” ᴼ

Mau looks around the table.

At the bread in his hands. At the bowls which have not quite finished their journey from one end to the other. At people who are tired and interested and trying to make room on a surface which already has an excellent use.

“I want us to.” ᴹ

* * *

The drawing begins to change because someone finally asks what they want to keep.

Bocchi wants somewhere a person can practise without making every bedroom part of the audience. Momo wants the kitchen to stop acquiring people halfway through a doorway. Coco's mother would like a sensible place to leave the things used for cleaning, and she would like the path to it not to run through whoever is trying to rest.

“And somewhere you can sit without finding another thing to clean,” Coco says. ᶜᵒ

Her mother looks at her.

Then laughs and asks her to draw a chair.

Jinshi wants a place to talk to a visitor without making the rest of the house wait to eat. Maomao would prefer that future guests not mistake every shelf for something they are allowed to touch.

“That's going to need a very large sign,” Gojo says. ᴳ

Shoko looks at him.

He adds nothing further to the suggestion.

* * *

The old house does not have to disappear to make room for the new wishes.

There could be homes near it, paths between them and places where people can come together without being required to come together every time they are hungry.

Kaneki puts that question plainly.

“If someone wants dinner with their family?” ᴷᵃ

“They have dinner with their family,” Fern says. ᶠᵉ

“And if they want to come here?” ᴷᵃ

She looks toward the tables.

“Then we make sure this still works.” ᶠᵉ

Not one table growing until the last person at it cannot hear the first. Several places close enough to pass food, an outdoor space for the days the weather allows it, somewhere covered when it does not. A kitchen people can help in without making the same few people responsible for feeding every future arrival.

Ram asks where the washing will happen.

For a moment, the entire drawing looks less impressive.

Rem moves the bread back toward her.

“Eat before you make it worse.” ᴿᵉ

* * *

Mau puts the list from Tempest beside the plan.

It has fewer promises on it than the first excitement could have produced.

That is one of the things he is happiest to bring home.

Shion wants a visit. Shuna wants time at the workshop and people to see. Geld and a few volunteers can look at the ground when the house is ready to receive them. Kurobe wants measurements before tools. Testarossa can help them work out which reports are missing between the routes they already trust.

Benimaru and Rigurd still have a city full of people who know where to find them. Ranga has chosen to stay there for now and visit later. Ramiris has no intention of packing her dungeon into somebody's future garden.

Nobody on the page has been made a permanent resident by having their name written down.

“And HQ?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

Rimuru closes his eyes briefly.

“Apparently we're keeping it.” ᴿ

“Under review.” ᵁ

“Of course.” ᴿ

She is merciful enough not to ask for another basket tonight.

* * *

Shizue is beside the children, listening.

When someone asks how long she would like to stay, she looks toward Rimuru first, then back at the person who has asked.

“I'd like a little time to find out.” ˢᶻ

“You can have that,” Yuka says. ʸ

Diablo nods when Shizue glances toward him. They will revisit the loan of his bed after the first nights. The answer is not already a future room somebody has assigned her.

Hinata's return with the pupils is on the page where Fern has put the journey dates. Three nights here, then the road back. The children can look forward to another day without wondering whether wanting it will quietly become a decision never to go home.

Alice asks if there will be breakfast before they leave.

Rem looks at her very seriously.

“Yes.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru begins to offer suggestions for what it should contain.

“Three mornings away,” Fern reminds her. ᶠᵉ

“Planning.” ᵁ

* * *

They leave a page for people they still want to find.

Nobody asks Jinshi to empty his pouch. Nobody asks Kaneki to turn a person he misses into the next thing which will make a floor plan exciting. Qifrey keeps the names the girls have given him where they can be found again, including the one Richeh has been waiting to hear news of.

Mau can ask his scouts to watch for things people can actually recognise. The reports can travel through people who know what they are looking for. A coin can remain with someone who is not ready to choose.

When they do decide to invite more families, there will have to be somewhere to receive them.

Food, water, a door which closes. Room to arrive without becoming somebody else's permanent guest by accident.

Yuta sits back and looks at the growing collection of questions.

“We're going to need more paper.” ʸᵗ

Mai reaches for a clean sheet.

She does not reach for Mau's reserves to make something before they know what they need.

* * *

There are proposals for taller buildings.

There are proposals for keeping everything low enough to see the sky from the courtyard. Some families might want to share a building; others might want a little more distance and still come here for dinner.

Geld will need to see the actual ground. The roots and the protected boundary do not move because a pleasing line wants to pass through them. Water has to get where it is going. Materials have to exist before someone can build a room with them.

Hollis can help them work out what is available without promising the road will deliver everything at once.

The money gives them choices.

The choices give them more questions.

For once, nobody seems offended by that.

* * *

The children begin getting tired before the adults finish the drawing.

Shizue goes with the pupils to help find the things which have vanished into their bedding. Hinata stays long enough to answer a question from Kenya, then comes back to the table while Rem and Frieren settle the first part of the evening.

Anko pushes a chair out with her foot.

Hinata takes it.

Across from them, Seika asks Ram whether the tea they discussed has to wait for everyone to agree on a town.

Ram's mouth curves.

“I should hope not.” ᴿᵃᵐ

The drawing survives another cup being set beside it.

* * *

Mau and Yuka take their own page upstairs when the conversation has become something which can wait until morning.

In their room, he sets it beside the drawing Ori gave them.

The two chairs are still too close to the door. The window faces the way he wants. Rimuru's little mark remains beside the room Yuka has promised to consider, attached to a favour which will almost certainly make him complain.

Yuka comes to stand beside Mau.

“We're keeping the table,” she says. ʸ

“You mentioned.” ᴹ

“I'm making sure.” ʸ

He turns toward her.

Her hands find the front of his shirt. There is no audience to find an excuse for, no borrowed courtyard to return and no one waiting for them to decide the shape of their whole future before they can sit down.

“Did you like it?” she asks. ʸ

“The festival?” ᴹ

She nods.

“Yes.” ᴹ

Yuka waits.

He smiles and touches the silver band on her hand.

“I like this part too.” ᴹ

She pulls him toward the bed they already have.

Downstairs, someone laughs loudly enough for them both to hear.

Yuka begins laughing as well, and Mau closes the door.
