## Chapter 1 — The Blanket Came Home Too

**Volume:** 48
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S8E57
**Continuity state:** B+48 morning, directly after V47#22. MY in their existing shared room/former Yuka room, not future apartment. Engaged/abstinent, silver band left, family ring and flower bracelet distinct. Grey except Yuka and old sequelae unchanged. *When: Yuka wakes with both feet outside the blanket, Mau is sleeping very peacefully in the part he has stolen.*

She gives him a moment to discover this on his own.

He fails.

Yuka catches the edge beneath his shoulder and pulls. The blanket moves just far enough to convince her it is possible, then stops under the considerable weight of a man who has spent the journey insisting he can carry more luggage.

“Mau.” ʸ

He makes a quiet sound.

“Move.” ʸ

His arm comes around her.

That is agreeable. It is also very little help with the blanket.

“The other way.” ʸ

He opens one eye.

For a second he looks confused by the room. Then his gaze finds her, and the confusion becomes the small, unguarded smile she has learned he does not know how to hide immediately after waking.

“Morning.” ᴹ

“Give it back.” ʸ

He follows her glance, considers the evidence and lifts his shoulder.

She takes considerably more than her half.

“That's a lot of blanket.” ᴹ

“There are consequences.” ʸ

Mau settles onto the pillow again. His hand remains at her waist, warm through her shirt. He closes his eye as though he has found a tolerable arrangement with justice.

Yuka watches him for a little while before putting her cold feet against his leg.

Both eyes open.

“You could have warned me.” ᴹ

“I did.” ʸ

* * *

The room has acquired the smell of home during the night.

Their clean clothes. The wood beside the window. Something warm beginning downstairs, carried up by the draught beneath the door. The bags they have neglected still stand where Mau set them down, with one strap waiting to catch the first person who forgets it.

Yuka's hoodie hangs on its hook. His eyes pass over the shape of it before coming back to her, to the colour he can still see in the hair spread across the pillow and the small crease beside her mouth.

She is looking toward the table.

“We should put those away before someone steps on them.” ʸ

“The drawings?” ᴹ

“The bags.” ʸ

“I was going to be concerned about how someone reached the table.” ᴹ

She laughs and shifts closer.

The silver ring presses briefly against his wrist. She turns her hand, looking at it where it lies between them, and he touches the side of her finger with his thumb.

There are mornings when she catches him checking.

This one is gentler. He seems pleased that it is still an ordinary thing she can wear while stealing a blanket back from him.

“What?” she asks. ʸ

“You brought it home.” ᴹ

“I wasn't going to leave it in Tempest.” ʸ

“Would have made the next conversation with your grandfather difficult.” ᴹ

“For you.” ʸ

He nods gravely.

She leans over and kisses him before he can begin explaining how many different conversations her grandfather is capable of making difficult. His hand slides a little higher along her back. She rests against him, and for a while the bags and the drawings remain somebody else's idea of urgency.

Below them, someone calls for a bowl.

Another voice answers from much farther away than the bowl appears to have needed to travel.

Yuka lifts her head.

“We're home.” ʸ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

“You look happy about it.” ʸ

He brushes the hair away from her cheek with the hand that still moves more easily.

“I am.” ᴹ

She takes that hand and puts it back where she wants it.

* * *

Getting dressed takes longer because Yuka cannot find one of her socks.

Mau discovers it beneath the drawing of their prospective stairs. She asks him how it got there. He says she was the last person to move the page. She reminds him that he was the last person to move the bag.

They agree to leave the matter unresolved.

At the mirror she smooths her hair while he folds the blanket. He has almost finished when she sees him turning it over to inspect the edge.

“What are you doing?” ʸ

“Trying to find out which side was yours.” ᴹ

“The side with me under it.” ʸ

“That's useful. I'll remember.” ᴹ

She catches his sleeve as he passes, pulls him down for another kiss and lets him go before he can set the blanket on the floor.

Then she puts on the hoodie.

He looks at her.

“I brought that home too,” she says. ʸ

He opens the door for her.

* * *

Diablo is carrying a folded bed when they reach the foot of the stairs.

It is an impressive amount of dignity to maintain behind something with a loose strap. He stops before the strap catches a chair, adjusts it and continues toward the place where the musicians have agreed he can store it during the day.

Bocchi waits beside the music-room door with her guitar case.

“Was it all right?” she asks. ᴮ

“Quite comfortable. Thank you.” ᴰⁱ

“The wall makes a noise when the kitchen door closes.” ᴮ

“I became acquainted with it.” ᴰⁱ

She looks worried.

Diablo smiles.

“I have slept through considerably more ambitious attempts to disturb me.” ᴰⁱ

“Don't give them ideas,” Seika says from inside. ˢᵏ

Mau takes the little lamp from the top of the bedding before it slides off. Diablo thanks him, and Bocchi goes through the door she has been waiting to use.

The room is hers to practise in again before breakfast has finished being breakfast.

Yuka squeezes Mau's hand once before releasing it to go and help Rem with the cups.

* * *

Shizue has found an apron.

Rimuru sees this at almost the same moment Mau does. He stops in the kitchen entrance with such visible alarm that Momo turns to see what has happened.

“What?” ᴹᵒ

“Nothing. I was going to ask whether—” ᴿ

“I asked where the bowls went,” Shizue says. ˢᶻ

She lifts the one she is drying.

“I'm enjoying knowing.” ˢᶻ

Rimuru looks at her, then at the apron, then steps aside because Ram has arrived carrying something hot and has shown no inclination to ask twice.

“Excellent place for a reunion,” Ram tells him. ᴿᵐ

He moves all the way out of the doorway.

Mau takes two bowls from Shizue.

“You can put those on that table.” ᴹ

She looks where he points.

“And if somebody moves them?” ˢᶻ

“They'll tell you that was always where they wanted them.” ᴹ

Rimuru makes a small, wounded sound.

Shizue laughs, and hands him the next bowl.

* * *

The children come out of the Fort in an order which appears to have been negotiated at length.

Alice is first. Kenya claims to have offered her the place. Gale carries the blanket that somebody has brought farther than intended. Ryota is explaining that the argument would have finished sooner if everyone had stopped changing the rules.

Chloe walks with Ori, who has been waiting for an opportunity to show her something beneath the tree.

Frieren reaches out as they pass and smooths the back of Ori's hair.

“After breakfast.” ᶠ

“I know, Mum.” ᴼ

Ori takes her place, then looks toward Mau.

“Do you know how to make it float longer?” ᴼ

He glances at Frieren.

She lifts the paper boat from where she has kept it safe.

“The water has been accused of cheating.” ᶠ

“It goes too fast.” ᴼ

“We'll look after you've eaten,” Mau says. ᴹ

Ori considers the bowl being set in front of her and accepts the arrangement.

Beside him, Yuka is smiling into her cup.

* * *

They do not fit around the same table.

They did not fit last night either. This morning it feels easier to work out where to put the extra chairs. Stark moves one without being asked. Mai takes the seat somebody has left on the wrong side of a bag. Yuta waits until Yuka has passed him a bowl before asking whether they slept well.

“Very well,” Mau says. ᴹ

Yuka looks at him.

“There was a theft.” ʸ

“Resolved.” ᴹ

“I'm pleased to hear it,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

Mai looks from one to the other, decides she is hungry and reaches for the bread.

Mau catches the plate for her.

On his other side, Rem has sat down. Umaru immediately begins explaining what the five guests have misunderstood about the Fort. Rem listens, asks a question which makes the misunderstanding considerably worse and has to hide a smile when Umaru turns to her in outrage.

Ram sees it from across the table.

She stays where she is and eats while the food is hot.

* * *

Mau is halfway through his breakfast when the Noise returns to the subject of yesterday's promise.

*Today?* ᵀᴺ

He rests his spoon against the edge of the bowl.

*Today. After we've finished here.*

Ciel is quiet for a moment before asking whether Rimuru can join them.

Mau looks toward him. The slime has managed to sit beside Shizue and is now listening to a description of how the blanket was found in the passage.

*If he has time.*

*I will ask.*

The answer pleases him more than he expects.

Yuka nudges his knee beneath the table.

“Something?” ʸ

“The review we promised. Later this morning.” ᴹ

“Good.” ʸ

She passes him the bread before Umaru's explanation requires somebody to confiscate it as a visual aid.

Mau takes a piece.

There are things to do. People to find. A house which already has more wishes than the page can comfortably hold.

For now, Yuka's knee remains against his, and his breakfast is still warm.
