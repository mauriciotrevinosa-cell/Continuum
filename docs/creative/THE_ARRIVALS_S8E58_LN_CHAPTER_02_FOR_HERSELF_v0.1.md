*When: Ciel asks to see what he remembers, Mau looks toward the window before answering her.*

Yuka follows his glance.

There is a child outside carrying something with both hands. Another is explaining how the first ought to carry it. Beyond them, the tree makes its familiar shape above the edge of the roof.

Mau is still holding her hand.

*More than last time?* ᴹ

*Yes.* ᶜⁱ

He draws a slow breath.

*I remember where I stopped,* Ciel says. *I have not forgotten why.* ᶜⁱ

Yuka waits until his attention returns far enough for him to notice her.

“Do you want to sit?” she asks. ʸ

He nods.

She takes him upstairs before the passage acquires anybody else with a question.

* * *

Their room is still a little untidy.

It helps.

Yuka moves a folded shirt from the chair and sits sideways on the bed. Mau takes the chair, then discovers he has chosen the position farthest from her and gets up again.

She moves her feet so he can sit beside her.

“What did she ask?” ʸ

“To see more of before.” ᴹ

“The other history?” ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

He turns his hand over on his knee.

“The things I remember. Not just the part we tried.” ᴹ

Yuka knows about that part. She has known long enough for it to become one of the things she thinks of when he says something was difficult with the same expression he uses for a badly tied bag.

She looks at him now.

“What do you want to do?” ʸ

He begins answering, then stops.

Ciel does not fill the space for him.

* * *

*Why now?* he asks her. ᴹ

The question reaches her privately. He tells Yuka what he has asked before waiting for the answer.

*Because I would like to understand more of the person I am with,* Ciel says. *And because I have had time to decide what I want to ask for.* ᶜⁱ

He closes his eyes.

*You know what happened when we tried.* ᴹ

*I know what I heard. I also know I was allowed to stop.* ᶜⁱ

Her voice stays steady.

*I am asking whether we can choose to begin again. With more time. With someone beside you.* ᶜⁱ

Mau rubs his thumb over the side of his finger.

“She wants us to choose how,” he tells Yuka. ᴹ

“That sounds like her.” ʸ

He laughs once, very softly.

“Yes.” ᴹ

Yuka puts her hand over his.

* * *

There is an answer he could give which would make the next part easier for a few minutes.

He could say he is fine with it. Smile. Ask whether tonight is convenient and spend the afternoon discovering all the ways he has failed to be fine.

Yuka is watching his hands.

“I'm frightened,” he says. ᴹ

She shifts closer without pulling him against her.

“Of remembering?” ʸ

“That too.” ᴹ

He looks toward the drawings.

“I know some of it by where I try not to go. I can tell you it happened. I can remember telling someone it happened. Going back to the actual thing is different.” ᴹ

Yuka's fingers settle between his.

“Then you can say when you want to stop.” ʸ

“What if I don't notice?” ᴹ

She thinks before answering.

“We ask.” ʸ

He looks at her.

“You can get annoyed with me if I ask too much.” ʸ

“That hasn't worked very well for me before.” ᴹ

Her mouth moves into a small smile.

“I've noticed.” ʸ

He leans until his shoulder touches hers.

For a little while she lets that be the conversation.

* * *

Ciel asks whether she may include Rimuru in what they are deciding.

Mau agrees. Yuka nods when he tells her, and a few minutes later there is a knock at their door.

Rimuru has brought his own chair.

“I thought the other one might still belong to somebody.” ᴿ

Mau looks down and laughs despite himself.

Yuka gets up to make room.

Rimuru waits until she has moved the bag before setting the chair where the door will clear it. Then he sits, looking at both of them with the expression he uses when he has decided a joke can wait.

“Tell me.” ᴿ

*I would like to see more of Mau's memories,* Ciel says to her two hosts. *He is considering letting me. We would like someone with him.* ᶜⁱ

Rimuru's gaze moves to Mau.

“Do you want me here?” ᴿ

“Yes.” ᴹ

The answer is immediate enough to make something in Rimuru's face soften.

“Then I'm here.” ᴿ

* * *

They have to explain the difference between being there and receiving what Ciel sees.

Yuka listens while Mau finds the words. She knows she will be able to watch his face, hear what he chooses to say and ask him to look at her. The memories themselves will remain where they are unless he and Ciel choose to open a particular part to someone else. They are not choosing that today.

Rimuru can hear Ciel when she includes him.

He cannot see the image merely by wishing he could make it easier for his brother.

“I can tell you what I am able to tell you,” Mau says. ᴹ

“And you can leave the rest until you can,” Rimuru answers. ᴿ

Yuka watches Mau take that in.

Then she asks whether he would like Shoko to know they are trying.

His shoulders change.

“Does it need to become—” ᴹ

“An appointment with everyone standing around a bed? I wasn't asking for that.” ʸ

He waits.

“I want to be able to call someone who knows you if we need help.” ʸ

Mau looks toward Rimuru.

The slime leaves the answer with him.

“Yes,” Mau says. “I'd like that.” ᴹ

* * *

There are more decisions than he expected.

When to begin. What he thinks he can reach without forcing it. Whether Ciel wants words first, or the memory of a place he knows she has never stood in. What she wants him to do if she becomes quiet.

That question makes him look up.

*I would like you to ask whether I want to continue,* she says. ᶜⁱ

*All right.* ᴹ

*And I will answer. You do not have to guess for me.* ᶜⁱ

He nods.

Yuka has been watching the small movements of his face. He tells her this part, and she rests her head against his shoulder.

“That sounds fair.” ʸ

“It does.” ᴹ

*If you need to stop, I will stop too,* Ciel says. *You do not have to finish something because I asked to begin.* ᶜⁱ

Mau looks down at the hand in his.

The ring catches the light when Yuka moves her thumb.

“Yes,” he says, aloud this time. ᴹ

* * *

They choose after the evening meal.

There is time to tell Shoko. Time for Rimuru to finish what he has actually promised Shizue and the children. Time for Yuka and Mau to go downstairs without everyone needing to know why they have spent so long discussing an afternoon.

He will begin with something he wants Ciel to have seen.

That part matters to him enough to say twice.

“There's good in it,” he tells them. “A lot.” ᴹ

Rimuru smiles.

“You make that sound like a confession.” ᴿ

Mau lets out a breath that becomes a laugh.

“I suppose I do.” ᴹ

Yuka lifts his hand and kisses his knuckles before getting up.

“Then start there.” ʸ

* * *

Rimuru takes his chair back downstairs.

Yuka follows him far enough to check whether Mai is still waiting for the page. Mau remains at the window for a minute, listening to the ordinary house beyond the door.

*Are you still frightened?* Ciel asks. ᶜⁱ

*Yes.* ᴹ

*So am I.* ᶜⁱ

He turns a little toward the room, as though that might bring him nearer to her.

*You don't have to do it to prove anything.* ᴹ

*I know. Neither do you.* ᶜⁱ

He rests his forehead against the cool wood beside the window.

Below, somebody calls his name.

He hears Yuka answer that he is coming.

*Thank you for asking me,* he says. ᴹ

Ciel is quiet long enough for him to recognise that she is taking the words seriously.

*Thank you for considering my answer.* ᶜⁱ

Mau opens the door and goes downstairs.
