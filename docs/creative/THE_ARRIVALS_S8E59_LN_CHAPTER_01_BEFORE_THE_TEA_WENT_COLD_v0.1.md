Seika pours.

They have found two chairs beside the open back door. From here the light reaches the table without reaching the part where Nazuna will eventually complain about it. The kitchen is near enough for someone to ask where the cups went and be told to look at the table they have just passed.

Seika sets the pot down.

“There. A demonstration.” ˢᵏ

“Adequate.” ᴿᵐ

Seika watches her over the edge of her own cup.

“I'll try to live with that.” ˢᵏ

* * *

Nijika comes through with a cloth over her shoulder and stops when she sees them.

Seika knows that expression.

“What?” ˢᵏ

“Nothing.” ᴺⁱ

“You stopped walking for nothing.” ˢᵏ

Nijika smiles, asks whether either of them has seen the little case Ryo brought downstairs and accepts Ram's answer without finding another reason to remain.

Seika watches her go.

“She enjoys that.” ˢᵏ

“Your sister?” ᴿᵐ

“Finding out I also have afternoons.” ˢᵏ

Ram's mouth moves slightly.

“An alarming discovery.” ᴿᵐ

Seika looks toward her.

Then both women laugh.

* * *

The drawing comes up because they can see its corner beneath a bowl somebody has left on the next table.

“They've moved you closer,” Seika says. ˢᵏ

“On paper.” ᴿᵐ

“Does that help?” ˢᵏ

Ram considers the question while warming her fingers against the cup.

“Rem asked for what she wanted.” ᴿᵐ

Seika waits.

“I liked that.” ᴿᵐ

Through the other doorway they can see Rem sitting on the floor beside Umaru and Suika. Something small is being assembled from pieces which were apparently meant for more than one game. Umaru is explaining which ones are essential. Rem keeps handing her the piece she will ask for next.

“You asked too,” Seika says. ˢᵏ

Ram glances back at her.

“I did.” ᴿᵐ

“Good.” ˢᵏ

It comes out plainly enough that Ram looks down at her tea for a moment before drinking.

* * *

They speak about the journey home, and the ways a house can seem smaller after a city without becoming less pleasant to return to. Seika asks whether Ram slept well with an extra person in the room. Ram reminds her that Hinata is staying with Anko and Nazuna, then enjoys the expression which follows.

“There are a lot of cups to keep track of,” Seika says. ˢᵏ

“I shall have to label mine.” ᴿᵐ

“For me?” ˢᵏ

“It seems necessary.” ᴿᵐ

Seika shakes her head, smiling.

When Ram reaches for the pot again, she lets her.

The second cup is a little stronger.

Neither minds.

* * *

Rem looks up when they begin gathering the cups.

“Was it nice?” she asks. ᴿᵉ

Ram pauses.

Seika goes to return the pot, leaving her the answer.

“Yes.” ᴿᵐ

Rem's smile arrives slowly, with enough warmth to make Ram realise she has been watching rather more kindly than she intended to show.

“Good,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks between them, interested.

Ram takes the empty cups to the kitchen before the afternoon can acquire an investigation.
