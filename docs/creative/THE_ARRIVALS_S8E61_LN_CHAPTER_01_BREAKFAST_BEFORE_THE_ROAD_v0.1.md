*When: Alice asks whether there will be breakfast, Rem points at the bowl already waiting for her.*

The answer appears satisfactory.

Kenya eats quickly enough for Hinata to ask whether he intends to leave before the rest of them. Gale checks that the thing he has packed in the side of his bag is not somebody else's. Ryota discovers that he has put his cup away before using it.

Umaru watches the preparations with the expression of a person who has been informed that a successful visit has somehow made its own ending unavoidable.

“You could come back,” she tells them. ᵁ

“We know,” Chloe says. ᶜʰ

Umaru seems pleased to find that part of the agreement properly understood.

* * *

Shizue sits with them while they eat.

She has already helped find the small possessions which managed to travel farther inside the house than they had on the journey to it. Now she listens while Alice explains what she intends to tell somebody in Tempest first.

The story grows in the telling.

Shizue enjoys it enough to let the growth stand.

“You'll come too?” Kenya asks her. ᴷᵉ

She looks toward him.

“Not this morning.” ˢᶻ

“Later?” ᴷᵉ

“I'd like to.” ˢᶻ

He nods, then asks Hinata whether she has heard what Shizue said.

“I heard.” ᴴⁱ

She has heard several people promising future visits. She accepts the work of remembering without letting it turn this departure into something they have failed to prevent.

* * *

The question of Shizue's bed was settled before the children began carrying things toward the door.

She had asked Diablo herself whether staying a little longer would make his kindness inconvenient.

“The question is whether you want to stay,” he had answered. ᴰⁱ

“I do.” ˢᶻ

Then they had asked the people whose room he was borrowing at night.

Seika had come with Nijika and Bocchi to check the arrangement again. Three more nights, the travel bed packed away before anyone needed to practise, no instruments moved. If it stopped working for anyone, they would say so rather than wait for a person to guess.

Shizue had thanked them.

Diablo had put the folded bed back where it belonged for the day.

There was no new guest wing, but there was an answer they had actually asked one another for.

* * *

Mau carries one of the bags as far as the doorway.

He is quieter than yesterday. Yuka walks beside him, her fingers brushing the back of his hand once while they wait for the others to find the last strap.

Chloe stops beside Ori.

They have already said something to each other inside the Fort. Whatever it was does not need to be repeated for the adults.

Frieren smooths a folded blanket over her arm and waits with them.

“I'll show you if it works,” Ori says at last. ᴼ

“The boat?” ᶜʰ

Ori nods.

Chloe smiles.

“I want to see.” ᶜʰ

Mau looks toward the place beneath the tree where they have been testing it. He remembers the promise he made two mornings ago, the little time they found between other things to argue about the water.

“We'll keep practising,” he says. ᴹ

Ori looks up at him.

Then takes Chloe's hand for the last part of the walk to the road.

* * *

Rimuru hands Hinata the letter.

“No need to make an answer faster than the road,” he says. ᴿ

She puts it where it will stay dry.

“I'll tell them that you said so.” ᴴⁱ

He smiles.

Shizue embraces the children one at a time, with enough variation in how each wants to be held that it takes longer than Kenya has allowed in his estimate of when they ought to be leaving.

He stops estimating when it is his turn.

Anko waits beside the house while Hinata checks the group once more.

When she comes back, Anko takes her hand.

“Let us know you got there.” ᴬⁿ

“I will.” ᴴⁱ

Hinata leans nearer and kisses her cheek. Anko's smile arrives before she has decided whether to make a joke about being kept waiting.

“And about coming back,” Hinata adds. ᴴⁱ

“Good.” ᴬⁿ

They let go when the children are ready.

* * *

The road does not take them out of sight immediately.

There is time to wave twice. Time for Ryota to turn back because he thinks somebody has called him, and discover that Umaru is issuing a reminder about what belongs in the account of their visit.

Hinata waits while he answers.

Then they go on.

Shizue stands beside Rimuru until the turn has taken all five children from view.

When she looks back at the house, he does too.

“Tea?” he asks. ᴿ

She nods.

Behind them, the morning has left bowls on several tables and a little more room around each one. Rem is asking who wants to help clear them. Mai is rescuing a drawing from a damp cloth. Someone in the Fort has already found a different use for the place where the guests slept.

Shizue walks back inside with Rimuru.
