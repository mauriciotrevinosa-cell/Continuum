*When: Ciel recognises the dark beneath the wing, Mau asks whether she would like him to stay there.*

She knows what he means.

The memory is held at a point where she can hear something familiar without following it toward the sound that made her stop before.

*For a moment,* she says. ᶜⁱ

Mau keeps his hand open on the blanket.

Yuka is sitting beside him. Rimuru has brought the chair back after finishing the things the house actually needed from him this morning.

Outside the room, the afternoon continues without them.

Inside the memory, a voice is trying to find enough steadiness to call a name.

* * *

Ciel remembers hearing herself here.

That has remained difficult to explain even to the two people who know what she means. She had never lived this moment. Still, the shape of the voice was familiar enough that recognising it had hurt before she understood what it was trying to say.

Now she listens with permission to stop and the knowledge that Mau is holding something other than the cold stone.

The call reaches the edge of what he can remember clearly.

Rimuru's name.

The accusation which had broken inside it. The effort of remembering that the people coming toward him had not chosen to leave him there.

There is an answer somewhere beyond Mau's awareness.

He cannot offer words he never heard.

He leaves that edge where it is.

* * *

Rimuru watches him open his eyes.

“All right?” ᴿ

Mau takes time to answer.

“We're at the part she tried before.” ᴹ

Yuka shifts the cup a little closer.

“After Kamish?” ʸ

He nods.

Rimuru looks down at his hands.

Ciel chooses to include him in her next words.

*I understand more of what she was trying to tell you.* ᶜⁱ

He lifts his eyes.

*The other me.* ᶜⁱ

“Take your time,” he says. ᴿ

Mau hears him say it to her and feels something ease in his own chest.

* * *

They move back only when she asks.

There is no reason to pretend the sound will be easier because she has decided she wants to hear beyond it.

It is close. A small failure beneath something much louder. The next breath changes around it, and the man remembering on the bed has to open his eyes before he has finished recalling the effort of taking it.

Ciel stops the image herself.

Mau sits forward.

Yuka puts her hand against his back, waiting until he leans into it before moving closer.

“Breathe here,” she says quietly. ʸ

He looks at the floor between his feet.

It is wood.

The room smells of the clothes they have finally put away.

He takes another breath.

* * *

*You can stop,* Mau tells Ciel. ᴹ

Her answer is not quite steady.

*I have.* ᶜⁱ

He waits.

*I need to stay stopped for a little while.* ᶜⁱ

*All right.* ᴹ

Rimuru hears her choosing the words carefully. He has no image to put beside them, only the man bent forward on the bed and the voice of someone who has just come back from being closer to the memory than he can reach.

He begins telling Ciel about the chairs downstairs.

It is the first ordinary thing he finds.

One has been moved beside the open door. Someone keeps putting a folded cloth over the back of it, and someone else keeps borrowing the cloth because they believe it was left there for precisely that purpose.

“You don't have to answer,” he tells her. “I can keep talking.” ᴿ

*Please,* she says. ᶜⁱ

He does.

* * *

The next part is slower.

Mau describes what he intends to remember before holding the little stretch open. Sometimes Ciel chooses the words. Sometimes she wants to see enough to understand the distance between a description and the effort it took to remain conscious inside it.

Yuka does not ask him to speak while his attention is elsewhere. She watches for the movement of his hand and asks whether he wants the water when he is back.

There are several pauses.

During one, he rests his head against her shoulder and asks Rimuru whether somebody has finally reclaimed the damp cloth.

“Not yet.” ᴿ

“A difficult dispute.” ᴹ

“I'm trying not to let it become a national incident.” ᴿ

Mau laughs once.

Ciel hears it from here.

* * *

What frightens her most is how much he can still notice while hurting.

The position of a hand. The sound of someone trying to help. A very small thing which has to be done correctly while the rest of his body is demanding that everything stop.

She understands the concentration.

Now she can hear what it was holding back.

*Mau.* ᶜⁱ

He returns before she asks again.

Her voice breaks on his name.

He looks toward Rimuru, who has heard it too.

*I'm here,* Mau says. ᴹ

For a moment he has the terrible wish to apologise for letting her find it. Then he remembers what she asked him for, and the way she waited for his answer.

He stays where she can feel him present instead.

*I know,* she says at last. ᶜⁱ

* * *

They finish this part somewhere quiet.

After help has reached him. After there is more than one person left trying to decide what happens next. He offers only what he remembers from his own body: fragments of warmth, familiar people coming within reach, the peculiar relief of no longer having to be the person who makes the next decision correctly.

Ciel remains with him there.

Mau feels her attention change when she understands what he wants her to see.

He had wanted help.

He had been pleased when it arrived.

The memory does not end with the effort of enduring alone.

* * *

When he opens his eyes again, Yuka is looking at Rimuru.

The slime has one hand over his mouth.

Neither asks him to explain it immediately.

“I thought I understood more than I did,” he says finally. ᴿ

Mau looks down.

“I don't always know what I'm leaving out.” ᴹ

“I believe you.” ᴿ

Ciel's next words reach both of them.

*I would like to stop for today.* ᶜⁱ

“Then we stop,” Mau says. ᴹ

*And I would like to stay with you for a while.* ᶜⁱ

He has to swallow before replying.

“I'd like that.” ᴹ

* * *

They do not go downstairs immediately.

Rimuru moves his chair far enough for Mau to stretch his legs. Yuka asks him which of the papers he wants to keep on their table and which can go back to the growing collection below.

He chooses Ori's drawing first.

Then the page with the terrible stairs.

Yuka smiles and puts both where they can see them.

Ciel listens while they disagree about the best place to leave a cup.

After a while, she says, *Thank you for letting me choose.* ᶜⁱ

Mau rests his cheek against Yuka's hair.

*Thank you for coming back with me,* he answers. ᴹ
