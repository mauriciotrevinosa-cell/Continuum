*When: Shoko finds the second version of the drawing, the wall is still there.*

She sits down to look at it.

Gojo has added a table, two chairs and a sofa which seems to expect a very different standard of available floor space from the rest of the house.

The two bedroom doors remain separate.

“You kept it.” ˢᵒ

He looks up from the cup he has been pretending to inspect.

“You drew it.” ᴳ

Shoko runs her finger along the little space between the doors.

“That hasn't always stopped you improving things.” ˢᵒ

“I can recognise an excellent suggestion.” ᴳ

“Occasionally.” ˢᵒ

He smiles.

She stays in the chair.

* * *

For a little while they discuss the sofa.

Shoko points out that the person carrying it upstairs may want to be included in Gojo's ambitions. He offers several names. She rejects the first because Mau already has enough things to do, the second because he is unlikely to enjoy Milim demonstrating which wall would make the job easier and the third because he appears to be offering himself only after exhausting the entertainment.

“You could help,” she says. ˢᵒ

“I was intending to.” ᴳ

“Put that on the page too.” ˢᵒ

He writes something beneath the sofa.

She leans forward to read it.

“That's your name.” ˢᵒ

“A binding commitment.” ᴳ

She laughs before she can decide whether to reward him for it.

* * *

The sound leaves them quiet afterward.

Gojo looks at her with an attention she has been catching more often lately. It makes it unexpectedly difficult to reach for her cup without being aware of her own hand.

“What?” she asks. ˢᵒ

“You laughed.” ᴳ

“You've heard it before.” ˢᵒ

“I like hearing it.” ᴳ

Shoko looks down at the drawing.

He lets the words remain there without following them with a performance.

It is one of the things she is beginning to trust about the afternoons they have been finding together.

* * *

Someone comes down the stairs.

Shoko lifts her eyes automatically, then relaxes when she sees a person carrying empty cups at an ordinary pace.

Gojo notices.

“He asked you?” ᴳ

“To be available. Yes.” ˢᵒ

“How's he doing?” ᴳ

“He's letting people sit with him.” ˢᵒ

She thinks about the answer, then adds, “He asked before he needed it.” ˢᵒ

Gojo looks toward the stairs.

For once he does not try to make the small improvement sound more spectacular than it is.

“Good.” ᴳ

Shoko agrees.

* * *

She asks for the pencil.

He gives it to her immediately, then watches her put something beside one of the windows.

“A chair?” ᴳ

“Mine.” ˢᵒ

“I'm sensing a pattern.” ᴳ

“You can have the sofa.” ˢᵒ

“Very generous.” ᴳ

She puts the pencil down and looks at him.

“I'd like somewhere I can sit down without someone asking what I've brought in my bag.” ˢᵒ

Gojo's smile changes.

“I can do that.” ᴳ

“And if I shut my door—” ˢᵒ

“I'll knock.” ᴳ

She studies him for a moment.

“I know.” ˢᵒ

He looks absurdly pleased by how little there is left to add.

Shoko takes the page when she stands. At the doorway she pauses, turns back and asks whether he is coming to dinner.

“Are you saving me a chair?” ᴳ

“One that exists.” ˢᵒ

He gets up before she can leave without him.
