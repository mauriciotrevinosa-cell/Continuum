*When: Rimuru comes in, he stops where Mau can see him before moving the chair.*

Yuka is sitting in front of Mau with her hands open on the blanket.

She looks toward the doorway for a moment, and the relief on her face tells Rimuru how much effort it has taken to sound as steady as she has been trying to sound.

“Here?” he asks. ᴿ

She nods toward the space beside the bed.

He moves the chair there and sits.

Mau follows the movement with his eyes. His breathing changes before he has found anything he can say.

“I came,” Rimuru tells him. ᴿ

Mau nods once, too quickly.

“I know.” ᴹ

Then he starts crying harder.

* * *

Rimuru has no picture to put beside the words Ciel gave him on the way upstairs.

A memory had returned. Mau needed him. Yuka was with him.

He had asked whether Shoko should come too. Ciel had said help could be useful, and he had found someone to ask her before coming to the room himself.

Now he waits while Mau tries to get enough air to speak.

The first attempt is broken in the middle. The second becomes an apology before he has explained what he believes he is apologising for.

Yuka looks toward Rimuru.

He puts a hand beside Mau's, leaving him the choice of taking it.

“You don't have to make the sentence work quickly.” ᴿ

Mau shakes his head.

It matters that they hear it. They can both see that.

So they stay quiet enough for him to try again.

* * *

“After,” he says. ᴹ

Rimuru waits.

“In the Trial.” ᴹ

Yuka moves closer by a very small amount.

“The world. I told you about that.” ᴹ

“Yes,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

Mau looks between them. He seems to be searching for the moment when what he has to say will have become something they already know, so he will not have to finish carrying it into the room.

He cannot find it.

“There was still someone there.” ᴹ

Yuka feels her throat tighten.

Mau looks down at his own hands.

“Me.” ᴹ

* * *

The room is quiet when he finishes telling them.

He has used very few words. He cannot make himself look up to see what they have done.

It takes Yuka a moment to move.

She puts her hand beside his again, close enough that he can feel the warmth without having to be touched before he is ready.

“You remembered that just now?” she asks. ʸ

He nods.

“You didn't know you were going to?” ʸ

He shakes his head.

His next breath breaks before it has finished.

“I knew there was somewhere I wasn't looking. I didn't—” ᴹ

Yuka closes her eyes for a moment.

When she opens them, she is looking at him rather than trying to find the next question.

* * *

Rimuru moves his hand only when Mau reaches for it.

The grip is awkward. Mau is shaking too much to arrange it the way he intends, and the two fingers which never quite close catch against the blanket.

Rimuru turns his own hand to meet the one he is being offered.

“I'm staying,” he says. ᴿ

Mau looks at him.

“You asked me to come. I'm staying.” ᴿ

Yuka moves beside Mau instead of remaining in front of him. She asks whether he wants her arm around his back. He nods, and she lets him decide how much of his weight to put against her.

It is more than she expects.

She holds him.

* * *

Ciel has been trying to find words which will not make him feel he must take care of her before letting himself remain where he is.

He asks her something twice before she can answer.

*Are you there?* ᴹ

*Yes.* ᶜⁱ

He presses the hand Rimuru is not holding against the blanket.

*I'm sorry.* ᴹ

Her answer comes through tears.

*I'm here with you.* ᶜⁱ

*I didn't mean to make you—* ᴹ

*I know. Mau, I know.* ᶜⁱ

Rimuru hears her when she includes him, and looks at the man between them with a grief he cannot make useful by understanding it faster.

*I asked to know you,* she says. *I am glad you let me. I wish this hadn't happened to you.* ᶜⁱ

Mau closes his eyes.

*You don't have to make me glad about what happened in order to let me stay.* ᶜⁱ

He bends forward until Yuka has to move with him.

For a while none of them tries to finish the conversation.

* * *

Shoko knocks before she opens the door.

Rimuru tells her she can come in.

She takes one look at the room and leaves her bag beside the chair instead of making it the first thing Mau has to see.

“Can I sit here?” ˢᵒ

He nods.

She asks what is happening in his body now. He needs time to separate that from what he remembers, and she gives it to him, asking about one thing at a time.

The pain he expects is not the pain his back is making. His chest is tight. His hands will not settle. He knows whose room this is, and his body keeps trying to answer something which happened somewhere else.

Shoko listens.

She asks before checking him.

No new wound appears because he cannot stop remembering an old one. His hearts remain his own familiar complication. There is no new surge requiring everyone to clear the house.

He is frightened.

She treats that as something worth taking seriously without turning it into proof he is about to become a danger to the people beside him.

* * *

Mau eventually asks for water.

Rimuru gets it. Yuka holds the cup with him until he can put it down without looking at the edge of the table for several seconds first.

Shoko remains where she is.

“I thought I knew what I remembered,” he says. ᴹ

She looks toward him.

“I thought the part I couldn't talk about was just me being bad at talking about it.” ᴹ

Yuka rests her forehead against the side of his head.

“You couldn't tell me what you couldn't remember.” ʸ

He begins shaking his head.

“Some of it I could.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

She draws back enough for him to see her face.

“We can talk about that too. You don't have to solve both things before I can hold you.” ʸ

Mau looks at her for a long time.

Then his hand closes around hers again.

* * *

The room becomes easier in small ways.

He can notice the lamp without needing to work out which life it belongs to. He can hear footsteps beyond the door and recognise that they have continued down the passage. He can answer Shoko without using all his attention to keep the answer inside the present.

Rimuru does not ask whether that means he is finished.

Instead he asks whether the chair is in the way of the table Yuka refuses to give up.

She looks past him.

“A little.” ʸ

“I'm taking a considerable risk being here.” ᴿ

Mau makes a sound which is almost a laugh.

Rimuru moves the chair anyway.

* * *

Shoko stays until she and Mau have agreed what he will do if the fear returns too sharply for the room to remain easy to find.

He can call. He can wake someone. He can say he needs company without producing another account of why.

She asks him to tell her how the night went in the morning.

He agrees.

Rimuru waits a little longer after she leaves.

“Do you want this left here?” he asks, touching the back of the chair. ᴿ

Mau looks at it.

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Then it stays.” ᴿ

He gets up only when Mau is ready to let his hand go.

At the door he looks back.

“You can ask again.” ᴿ

Mau nods.

Yuka answers too.

“We will.” ʸ

* * *

Later, when the lamp has been moved out of his eyes, Mau lies with his head beside Yuka's shoulder.

She has not stopped being upset about the things she learned tonight. She is beginning to understand that the person who told her he was recovering has been trying to describe a place he was still finding his way out of.

He touches her sleeve.

“I don't want you carrying it instead of me.” ᴹ

Yuka looks down at him.

“You haven't given it away.” ʸ

He considers that.

“No.” ᴹ

“You let us be with you.” ʸ

His eyes close for a moment.

“I'm trying.” ᴹ

She brushes the hair away from his forehead.

“I can see.” ʸ

Inside, Ciel stays near without asking him to go anywhere else in the memory tonight.

When he finally begins to sleep, he is still holding Yuka's hand.
