Ciel had asked him how he was before he left the bed.

He had thought about the choice for long enough that Yuka opened her eyes and asked whether he was deciding something.

*I want to stay here today,* he had told Ciel. *With what's actually here.* ᴹ

“Downstairs.” ᴹ

Now Shoko takes her own cup when someone brings it past and remains beside them long enough to drink half of it.

She tells Mau where she will be later. Asks him to come and find her if the day becomes harder to manage than he expects. He agrees without making her extract a promise she knows he has begun wording very carefully.

When she gets up, Yuka thanks her.

Shoko touches her shoulder briefly on the way past.

Then goes to ask Gojo why he is examining a sofa somebody else is using.

“He'll move that,” he says. ᴹ
