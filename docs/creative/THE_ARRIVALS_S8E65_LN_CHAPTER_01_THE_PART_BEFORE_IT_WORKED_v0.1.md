*When: Bocchi asks if he can listen, Mau looks toward the chair before answering.*

“That one?” ᴹ

She nods.

He sits down before she has time to decide that the question was more inconvenient than she meant it to be.

The music room is clear. Diablo's folded bed is stored where they agreed it could remain during the day. Someone has put the little lamp back on its shelf, well away from the cable Ryo keeps insisting she knows how to avoid.

Kita is beside the door, listening while Nijika explains something from the other room.

Bocchi adjusts her hand.

“It's the part before the one you heard.” ᴮ

“All right.” ᴹ

She plays.

* * *

The first attempt stops before Mau expects it to.

Bocchi frowns at her hand and tries again. This time the phrase reaches the part he recognises, then ends in a sound which makes her look up at him with a deeply personal grievance against the instrument.

“I don't think it wanted to do that.” ᴹ

“It was me.” ᴮ

“You've checked?” ᴹ

She stares at him for a second.

Then laughs, a small sound he is pleased to have earned without needing her to explain the whole mistake first.

Kita smiles from the doorway and goes to answer Nijika.

Bocchi tries the phrase more slowly.

* * *

Mau can hear the difference by the fourth time.

He does not know enough to name everything she is changing. She seems happy to let him say which turn he liked and then work out for herself whether that is the one she intended him to notice.

When it works, she plays it once more before looking toward him.

“That?” ᴮ

He nods.

“That.” ᴹ

She lets the strings settle beneath her hand.

For a while they sit without making the quiet into the end of the visit.

* * *

The little drawing is tucked beneath the corner of her case.

Mau notices when she reaches for it, then stops herself.

“You can show me again.” ᴹ

“It's the same.” ᴮ

“I helped draw the other one. Same might be an improvement.” ᴹ

She gives him the page.

Her family's spaces are still there. The door she asked them to leave has acquired a clearer edge.

“I don't know how to explain this place,” she says. ᴮ

Mau looks at the drawing.

“To them?” ᴹ

She nods.

He takes a moment before answering. There are things she has to choose for herself, and things she might enjoy having someone help her imagine.

“What would you want to tell them first?” ᴹ

* * *

The answer changes while she finds it.

Her guitar. The band. Having people she can sit beside without rehearsing the whole conversation before leaving the room.

Then she looks at him.

“You'd be difficult to explain.” ᴮ

“That's been said.” ᴹ

“Not because of—” ᴮ

She lifts one hand, searching for a way to indicate enough of the other difficulties to leave them out of the question.

Mau waits.

“I have a brother here,” she says at last. ᴮ

The words reach him before he has prepared anything clever enough to put between himself and how much they mean.

“I'd like to meet them,” he says. ᴹ

She looks down at the page.

“When we can.” ᴮ

“When we can.” ᴹ

* * *

He offers to stand somewhere respectable when the explanation becomes necessary.

Bocchi looks at him for long enough that he regrets inviting an assessment.

“You could just be there.” ᴮ

He smiles.

“I can do that.” ᴹ

She takes the drawing back and puts it somewhere it will not be folded by the case.

Outside, Yuka is speaking to someone in the passage. Her voice gets nearer, then pauses when she sees the room is being used.

Bocchi lifts her hand.

Yuka comes in.

“Can I hear it?” she asks. ʸ

Bocchi looks toward Mau.

“He heard it four times.” ᴮ

“Five,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka takes the next chair.

“Then I'm behind.” ʸ

Bocchi puts her hand back on the strings.
