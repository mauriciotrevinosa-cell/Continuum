“Excellent. We have an operation.” ᵁ

* * *

The operation has several members and fewer agreed objectives than Umaru would prefer to admit.

Ori wants to know where people will sit. Coco would like to know whether the things they make have to survive rain. Suika points out that they do not yet know where the wedding will be, which makes the rain question more difficult to dismiss.

Milim volunteers for something large.

“We haven't chosen anything large,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“Then I can help choose.” ᴹⁱ

Maki pulls out the chair beside her.

“You can help listen first.” ᴹᵏ

Milim sits, apparently prepared to find listening impressive if given a fair opportunity.

Yuka looks at the page, then toward Mau.

“Do you have anything you want written down?” ʸ

He studies the collection of people waiting for the answer.

“That you get to enjoy it.” ᴹ

She narrows her eyes.

“Something for you.” ʸ

* * *

He takes longer with that.

There is the easy answer, and the one which feels oddly greedy after they have already given him so many places to sit.

“I'd like to have time with people,” he says. ᴹ

Yuta looks up from the next table.

“Before everything starts moving. I'd like to be here for it.” ᴹ

Mau looks toward Yuka again.

“And I'd like a little time with you when it isn't all moving.” ᴹ

She holds his gaze.

Then takes the pencil from Mai and writes something herself.

Umaru leans over.

“That's not a decoration.” ᵁ

“It stays,” Yuka says. ʸ

Maki reads it, smiles and leaves the page where everyone can see.

* * *

There are other small negotiations that morning.

Shizue asks about the bed again before the day can pass its agreed point without anyone noticing. Diablo finds Seika and the musicians, and they work out three more nights while a more lasting place is still a question they are trying to answer properly.

The room is checked. The bed will still be packed before practice. Someone who changes her mind can say so without having to make the objection enormous first.

Shizue looks less apologetic when the next review is written down.

“I'd like to help make the other arrangement possible,” she says. ˢᶻ

“We'll ask what you want in it,” Rimuru answers. ᴿ

She nods.

They leave the question on the page with the others, where it can become work instead of a kindness somebody has to guess when to stop accepting.

Mau sees Yuka only briefly before the operation moves to a table where more people can reach the paper.

“You don't have to disappear until we're finished.” ʸ

“How are you?” ʸ

The ordinary morning contains a cupboard door which has begun catching at the bottom.

Kaneki has taken the things off the lowest shelf so he can see what is happening. Mau kneels beside him and holds the door while he adjusts it. Neither has to demonstrate anything remarkable for the work to become easier with two people.

“Too far?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“A little back.” ᴷᵃ

The door settles into place.

Mau looks at Kaneki.

Kaneki looks at the jar.

“A difficult investigation,” Mau says. ᴹ
