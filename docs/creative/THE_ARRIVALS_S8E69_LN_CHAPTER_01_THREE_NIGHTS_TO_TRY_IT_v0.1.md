*When: Gojo offers his room, Shizue asks where he intends to sleep.*

He has prepared several excellent ways of making the offer sound effortless.

The question removes most of them.

Shoko is looking at him from beside the table.

Diablo has set the folded bed down exactly where the discussion cannot forget it exists. Seika and Nijika have come to find out what the next nights are supposed to do to their practice room.

“I have an idea,” Gojo says. ᴳ

Shoko lifts one eyebrow.

“Have you told the idea?” ˢᵒ

* * *

They have reached the morning they agreed to ask again.

Shizue would like to remain a little longer. Diablo would like his own familiar place to sleep. The musicians would like to stop making the last use of the room each evening depend on a polite person waiting outside with bedding.

None of these wishes becomes less reasonable because the others exist.

Gojo looks toward Shoko.

“Could we try it?” ᴳ

She glances at the folded bed.

Then back at him.

“A few nights.” ˢᵒ

The answer makes him stop smiling quite so deliberately.

“Yes.” ᴳ

“We'll check whether there's room before anybody moves.” ˢᵒ

* * *

Mau is carrying a cup when Yuka finds him watching the group work out how to take a folded bed upstairs without catching the loose strap on the turn.

“What happened?” she asks. ʸ

“A suggestion.” ᴹ

She looks at Gojo.

“His?” ʸ

“It survived.” ᴹ

Shoko hears him and turns.

“You can carry that end.” ˢᵒ

He puts his cup down.

Yuka takes it before he forgets where.

* * *

The room is not the apartment they drew.

Shoko says so before Gojo can begin imagining where a sofa would go. There are things she already keeps where she wants them, a path to the door which must remain usable and a place she would prefer not to discover blocked when she wakes.

They move a small piece of furniture with her agreement. The bed unfolds. Diablo checks the fastening before leaving it with them, then asks whether Gojo wants help arranging the screen he has been using.

“Yes,” Shoko says before Gojo can answer beautifully instead of practically. ˢᵒ

Gojo gives up on the beautiful answer and helps.

When they have finished, she walks to the door, opens it fully and returns by the route she will need in the dark.

Then sits on her own bed and looks at the other one.

“That works.” ˢᵒ

* * *

Gojo waits until the people carrying things have gone downstairs.

He is standing beside the folded screen with his hands resting on its edge.

“If you don't like it—” ᴳ

“I'll tell you.” ˢᵒ

Shoko looks at his face for a moment, the careful pleasure he has been trying to make less easy to see.

“You can tell me too.” ˢᵒ

“I think I might like it.” ᴳ

“You haven't slept here yet.” ˢᵒ

“A useful objection.” ᴳ

He looks toward the doorway.

“How strict are we being about needs?” ᴳ

“Extremely.” ˢᵒ

Shizue sees the offered room before deciding.

Gojo has cleared his own things. Nobody has put hers inside to make the choice feel already completed. She walks to the window, looks at the space beside the bed and asks where she can keep the clothes she has begun to recognise as her own.

He shows her.

“Three nights first,” she says. ˢᶻ
