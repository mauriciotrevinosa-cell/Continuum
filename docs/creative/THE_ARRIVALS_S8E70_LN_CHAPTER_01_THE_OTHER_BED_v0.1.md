*When: Shoko turns down the lamp, Gojo is still looking for somewhere to put his cup.*

“There.” ˢᵒ

He follows her finger.

“That's yours.” ᴳ

“It's a table.” ˢᵒ

He puts the cup down.

Shoko watches him do it with enough caution to make her begin laughing before she has settled against her own pillow.

“What?” ᴳ

“You've been much less polite about considerably more expensive things.” ˢᵒ

“Those people didn't know me as well.” ᴳ

“A reasonable concern.” ˢᵒ

He smiles in the lowered light.

* * *

The folded bed is less grand than the sofa he drew.

Gojo has discovered this in several positions before finding one he seems willing to keep. Shoko listens to the last small adjustment and waits for the comment which does not arrive.

That interests her enough to make her turn toward the screen.

“Comfortable?” ˢᵒ

“Yes.” ᴳ

She can hear how much of the answer is about the room rather than the bed.

For a little while she does not reply.

Then says, “Good.” ˢᵒ

* * *

The house makes its ordinary noises around them.

A door farther along. Someone trying to cross a passage quietly and finding the board which has never cooperated with the attempt. A laugh downstairs which arrives more clearly after another door opens.

Gojo turns his head toward it.

“It'll be strange when some of those are farther away.” ᴳ

“Some will be closer.” ˢᵒ

“You're thinking about the drawings?” ᴳ

Shoko looks at the shadow of the screen.

“I'm thinking about the noise you make when you can't find somewhere to put a cup.” ˢᵒ

He laughs.

She closes her eyes and enjoys having been there for it.

* * *

He says her name after the house has become quieter.

She opens her eyes again.

“What?” ˢᵒ

“Thank you.” ᴳ

It would be easy to ask him for what. To make him name the folding bed and the space beside it, so they could both pretend the answer would be smaller if it had furniture in it.

She leaves him the dignity of the words he has chosen.

“I wanted to try it.” ˢᵒ

“I know.” ᴳ

He sounds pleased to know.

Shoko shifts the blanket over her shoulder.

“If you start being unbearable, I'll tell you.” ˢᵒ

“You're very experienced at that.” ᴳ

“Go to sleep.” ˢᵒ

* * *

They are quiet long enough that she thinks he has obeyed.

Then he asks whether the lamp is where she wants it.

She opens her eyes, looks toward it and finds that the light is exactly where she left it.

“Yes.” ˢᵒ

“All right.” ᴳ

Shoko waits a moment.

“Are you?” ˢᵒ

His answer takes only a little longer.

“Yes.” ᴳ

She turns onto her side, facing the screen.

The space beyond it has stopped feeling like something she has rearranged for a guest.

For tonight, she lets herself enjoy that without deciding what the third morning will ask them to do about it.
