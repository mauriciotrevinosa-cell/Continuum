*When: Yuka finds him checking the bag, there is more paper in it than food.*

“You're meeting someone before lunch,” she says. ʸ

“There are quite a lot of questions.” ᴹ

“People with questions still get hungry.” ʸ

She puts the wrapped food where he can find it without emptying the rest of the bag.

Mau watches her arrange the strap, then looks toward the window.

The room has the same small untidiness it had yesterday. One corner of the page on their table has folded where a sleeve caught it. The hoodie is hanging where Yuka can reach it. The chair Rimuru left has remained because they have found uses for it besides the reason he brought it upstairs.

“What?” she asks. ʸ

He smiles a little.

“I'm remembering what I'm coming back to.” ᴹ

* * *

The answer reached them yesterday evening through the route Hollis had offered.

A place by the ford. A time. Enough practical detail to make the next conversation possible without requiring them to agree to a longer journey before having it.

Qifrey recognises another small turn of language. He still intends to see the person using it before deciding what else the letter can prove.

Rimuru has the location and the part of the day in which they expect to return.

If they decide the meeting needs more time, they will send word before the house has to begin guessing whether an ordinary delay has become something else. Ciel knows what has been agreed. Nobody has asked her to replace knowing where they intend to go.

Mau puts the last page in the bag.

Yuka catches the strap before he lifts it.

* * *

“How are you?” she asks. ʸ

He sits down because the question deserves more than the answer he can give while reaching for the door.

“A little nervous.” ᴹ

She sits beside him.

“About what he might say?” ʸ

“About wanting every question to have a useful answer.” ᴹ

Yuka thinks about that.

“You can come back with some of them still questions.” ʸ

He looks at her.

“I'd prefer that to you deciding you have to finish the whole thing because you already went outside.” ʸ

Mau nods slowly.

“I can do that.” ᴹ

She leans toward him and kisses him.

When she draws back, her hand stays against his shoulder.

“Good. Because I might need an opinion later.” ʸ

He smiles.

“I'll try to make it a good one.” ᴹ

* * *

Qifrey has brought fewer things than the number of questions would suggest.

The ordinary kit is familiar. The papers have been kept where rain will not turn their careful choices into a different message. Richeh's page is separate from the one they will show anyone who asks what they have come to discuss.

She waits beside him before they leave.

“You can give him that part,” she says. ᴿⁱ

Qifrey touches the edge she means.

“This part.” ᵠ

She nods.

Tetia has several suggestions about what they ought to look at if the meeting becomes longer. Agott tells her that visiting a place and agreeing to go everywhere in it are different decisions. Coco's mother thanks her for saying it before turning to Qifrey.

“Tell us what he actually says.” ᶜᵐ

“I will.” ᵠ

Mau adjusts the bag on his shoulder and waits while they finish.

* * *

Rimuru comes to the path carrying the last note from the visit arrangements.

The first survey group is to begin its journey this morning. Six people, equipment for their own camp, an arrival the house expects tomorrow evening rather than a promise the road cannot keep. There are things to prepare and people already deciding who wants to do which part.

“I'll be here,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“I know.” ᴹ

“Try to leave me a few problems I can solve with a measuring line.” ᴿ

Mau looks toward the house.

“I think they're going to manage that.” ᴹ

The slime grins.

Yuta takes the copy of the meeting place. Maki asks Qifrey whether he is satisfied with the route. Both questions have ordinary answers which do not make anyone invulnerable.

They finish checking what needs checking.

Then let the two men go.

* * *

Kaisel lowers enough for Qifrey to settle without having to turn the beginning of the journey into a demonstration.

Mau waits until he is comfortable before taking his own place.

“All right?” ᴹ

Qifrey looks toward the wing beside him, then the house behind it.

“It's a very different way to reach a conversation.” ᵠ

“You could have mentioned that before we brought the lunch.” ᴹ

Qifrey laughs.

Mau feels the sound make the departure easier.

* * *

They see the familiar road in a different shape from above.

The turns which had divided the journey into stages lie close enough together for Mau to recognise why he has underestimated how long other people take between them. He watches the route before looking farther, keeping the destination in the ordinary world rather than letting the view become another collection of things he might someday do.

Inside, Ciel asks whether he remembers what they agreed the meeting is for.

*To find out who's actually there. Listen. Decide the next part with people.* ᴹ

*And?* ᶜⁱ

He looks down at the bag Yuka has closed properly for him.

*Eat lunch.* ᴹ

There is unmistakable satisfaction in the silence which follows.

Qifrey looks toward him when he begins smiling.

“Useful advice?” ᵠ

“Very practical.” ᴹ

* * *

At the ford, they land with room between themselves and the place a traveller would reasonably choose to wait.

Mau lets Kaisel settle while Qifrey looks toward the road.

A figure is standing beside the low shelter beyond the water.

Qifrey knows the stance before the man turns.

Mau stays beside him, waiting for the person who asked for his help to decide how he wants to cross the last part of the distance.
