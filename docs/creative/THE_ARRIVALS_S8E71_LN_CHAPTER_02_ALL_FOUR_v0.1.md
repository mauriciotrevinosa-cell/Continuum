The answer from home had been quick.

Rimuru asked whether Qifrey was satisfied, heard the answer and went to tell the people who would care about it most. By the time Kaisel landed, Coco's mother had put a kettle on and Tetia had gone outside three times to look.

Now Olruggio stands in front of the woman the girl beside her has introduced.

For a moment he has nothing to say.

Coco begins filling the silence with an explanation, then stops when her mother takes her hand.

“I'm glad you found them,” she tells him. ᶜᵐ

“So am I.” ᴼˡ

“We've been doing so much.” ᶜᵒ

“Now I'd like your version.” ᴼˡ
