* * *

The visitors' sleeping space is still open ground when they arrive.

It has water nearby, a path to the place where they can wash, and the distance from the kitchen which Rem showed Mau before he stopped thinking that every useful thing ought to be moved closer to every other one.

Geld's two companions walk it first. One asks where the late noise is likely to come from. The other asks which way rain runs off the roof of the house.

Byakuya joins them with the drawing folded to the part he can already answer.

The tents go up where the visitors choose after that walk. Mau holds a pole steady while a worker tightens the line. Kurobe tests the ground beneath one corner with his heel, then moves the corner a little.

When they finish, Geld can stand inside without stooping.

He does so for a moment, looking around the space he will sleep in.

Then puts his bag down.
