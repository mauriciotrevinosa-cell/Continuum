*When: Geld asks Umaru to show him the quickest way to dinner, she takes his hand and starts walking.*

He has to shorten his stride twice.

Mau follows with Byakuya, carrying the measuring line and trying not to laugh at the solemnity with which Umaru explains the last turn.

“This one matters,” she says. ᵁ

“I can see that.” ᴳᵉ

She leads him through the Fort's door and out to the place where a call from the kitchen can reach them. Ori stays on the other side at her request. Rem calls from near the table.

“Dinner!” ᴿᵉ

Ori appears in the doorway immediately.

Geld looks down at Umaru.

“Like this.” ᴳᵉ

“Exactly like this.” ᵁ

He asks Byakuya to put the door on the drawing before either of them forgets that it already works.

* * *

They begin at the tree.

Kurobe walks the edge of the ground they have agreed to leave undisturbed. Qifrey shows him where the old house steps around the root circle. Geld asks what happens to water after heavy rain, and receives answers from three people who have had to carry things across it.

He listens to all three.

Mau holds one end of the line. Stark takes the other and waits while Kurobe checks the way a new roof would carry its load.

“We can make a large room,” Geld says. “A large room doesn't decide where all its weight should go.” ᴳᵉ

Mau looks up through the branches.

“So we keep the middle open.” ᴹ

“And keep the tree alive beneath it.” ᴳᵉ

He says it matter-of-factly, then turns to ask where they have already seen the first roots lifting a path.

Mau finds himself relieved by the question.

It has an answer somebody else knows.

* * *

The outside markers come next.

They use the places the house already relies on, walking the line of known protected ground rather than allowing an attractive curve on paper to settle it. Some of the space will need another look before Geld is willing to put a permanent home on it.

“We don't need to fill it,” Armin says. ᴬʳ

Geld looks toward him.

“We need to know what fits and leave a way to change what doesn't.” ᴬʳ

“Yes.” ᴳᵉ

The drawing gets smaller in one corner.

No one loses their room because of it. The room has not been built yet.

Jinshi asks where deliveries will stop, and moves the proposed store before its shortest route can become a passage through someone's bedroom court.

Mau draws a line from the store toward the kitchen.

Rem turns it away from the washing space.

“Those paths will be busy at the same time,” she says. ᴿᵉ

He changes it.

* * *

Bocchi brings her question outside with the band.

“If it's here,” she says, pointing to the music space, “is that too far?” ᴮ

Mau looks at the distance on the page, then at the actual door she means.

“Show me.” ᴹ

She glances at Nijika, who nods and walks with her.

They take the route from the proposed room to the common area. Geld follows at a distance which lets them speak to one another. When they return, Bocchi moves the mark closer by one section and points toward the bedrooms beyond it.

“But we'll keep them awake.” ᴮ

Kurobe crouches beside the page.

“The door doesn't have to face them. Neither does the place you put the instruments.” ᴷᵘ

Wakana kneels on the other side.

They spend several minutes working out where a wall can do something useful without turning into a reason for a friend to stop visiting.

Bocchi stays to listen.

When she adds a question about her family's future space, nobody asks her to choose which side of her life deserves the shorter walk.

* * *

Yuka finds Mau measuring a line with Yuta.

“This is where you're putting us?” she asks. ʸ

Mau points to the old common room, then to the part of the sketch beside it.

“Near it. I haven't stolen everybody's house.” ᴹ

“Yet,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

Yuka steps over the line and stands where the table in their proposed sitting room would go. She looks toward Maki, then at the places Mai and Tsurugi have been discussing.

“How much farther?” ʸ

Geld gives her the loose end.

She walks it.

Mau waits while she takes the route twice, once toward her family and once toward the common room. Rimuru arrives in time to hear her ask about the remaining bedroom.

“Mine?” ᴿ

She looks down at him.

“You haven't been removed.” ʸ

“I like to check.” ᴿ

“The favour is still mine.” ʸ

He sinks slightly into the grass.

Mau laughs and puts a small arrow on the side of the page where he wants the morning window.

* * *

By noon, the page contains several things Geld would not have learnt from a list of names.

Rem shows him why the walk to Umaru matters, and why Ram should not have to cross the whole place to find her. Seika joins the conversation without anyone deciding her home for her. Taihei asks about the corner where the two routes meet.

Kaneki has a quieter question about where Ichika can sleep when the common room is loud. Touka points out the place she would like to sit while still hearing her.

Frieren wants room for books and a sensible place to put a chair. Fern asks about light, then notices that she has started measuring where someone else might trip before choosing her own window.

Stark points to one.

She stays beside him while they look at it.

* * *

They eat outside with the drawing between the empty dishes.

The plan has become a cluster of homes opening onto short shared routes, with the old common space still at its heart. The outer homes can rise where the ground and the loads allow it, up to the four floors they have discussed. Nobody needs to stack the Fort over the kitchen to make room for a future family.

Geld tells them what he can begin checking next. Kurobe names the materials he will need to test before putting dates beside the work.

Mau looks at the paper.

There are still questions on it.

This time, he can also see the places where they will sit to answer them.
