*When: Qifrey reads the invitation a second time, Mau asks which part he wishes they hadn't written.*

“This.” ᵠ

He turns the page.

The line asks him to account for the apprentices in his care. Beneath it is another request: the names of people to whom he has disclosed the practice of magic.

Mau pulls out the chair beside him.

“Account for how?” ᴹ

“That depends on who believes the answer belongs to them.” ᵠ

He reads the words aloud when Coco's mother comes to the table. She listens with one hand on the back of the chair and asks whether they expect him to bring the girls.

“They haven't said so.” ᵠ

“Then don't let them pretend later that we agreed.” ᶜᵐ

Qifrey takes a fresh page.

“We won't.” ᵠ

* * *

Olruggio's note is tucked inside the formal one.

He will meet them at the shore before the appointed time tomorrow. The meeting itself is to take place at midday. He has asked for a room where the people who need to hear the answers can hear them together.

At the bottom, in much less formal writing, he has reminded Qifrey to bring something to eat.

Mau smiles when he sees it.

Qifrey folds that part toward himself.

“I used to manage before everyone started asking.” ᵠ

“Were you hungry?” ᴹ

“That's a different question.” ᵠ

“I'm beginning to understand him.” ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru comes with Yuka and the question Jinshi has been asking about every outside agreement: what are they actually promising?

They make the answer short enough to say without reading it.

Qifrey will explain his teaching. They will protect the secret of how the ink is made from public trade. People who already belong to the house, and the families who may join them, will not become prisoners because they know something useful.

“We can't promise that every person who will ever arrive has already been named,” Yuka says. ʸ

“No,” Qifrey answers. ᵠ

“Or hand them a list of where everybody sleeps.” ᴿ

Mau looks down at the formal request again.

“They can ask us how we keep dangerous things from getting passed around without asking where Ichika's bed is.” ᴹ

Coco's mother sits.

“They can also learn the difference between someone who lives here and someone who owns the people living here.” ᶜᵐ

Qifrey leaves the page in front of her long enough for her to choose the sentence she wants carried back.

* * *

Agott asks whether Mau intends to frighten them.

He takes the question seriously enough that she waits for his answer.

“If they threaten you, I don't intend to sound harmless.” ᴹ

“And if they only ask?” ᴬᵍ

“Then we answer.” ᴹ

She studies him.

“Qifrey answers,” he adds. “I might need some help.” ᴹ

Tetia smiles.

Richeh asks whether her message will go with them again. Qifrey tells her he will ask where it can be delivered, then returns the part Olruggio has already read so that she can decide if she wants to change anything.

Coco stays beside her mother.

“Will you tell them she's here?” she asks. ᶜᵒ

Qifrey looks toward the woman beside her.

“If she wants me to.” ᵠ

“I do,” her mother says. “And that I'm not letting someone else decide which part of being her mother is permitted.” ᶜᵐ

Mau nods once.

He will remember that sentence without needing a page.

* * *

Later, Yuka walks him through the courtyard.

The measuring marks have remained on the ground. One loose length of cord moves in the breeze where someone has left it tied safely clear of the path. She stops beside it and turns to face the old common room.

“You're looking very hard,” he says. ᴹ

“It's my wedding.” ʸ

He smiles before she turns back.

“Our wedding,” she corrects herself. “But I'm allowed to look hard.” ʸ

“Very allowed.” ᴹ

She takes his hand and leads him a few steps farther.

“I want us to walk through the place. Together. Before we get to everyone.” ʸ

He looks at the line of doors she is imagining along the edge of the court.

“Not just find you at the end?” ᴹ

“You already did that on the mountain.” ʸ

Mau laughs softly.

“I did.” ᴹ

She stops beneath the branches.

“I want this one to feel like we get to stay.” ʸ

His hand tightens around hers, gently enough that she can turn her fingers between his.

He looks toward the house again.

“Then I'd like to walk slowly.” ᴹ

Yuka rests against his side.

“I think we can arrange that.” ʸ

* * *

When they go inside, Geld has moved the working drawing away from the place where Rem is setting out cups.

It is such a small change that Mau almost misses it: the people who came to measure the house have begun to learn where the house needs room to go on living.

Rimuru asks him when they intend to leave tomorrow.

Qifrey answers from the other side of the table. They have time to reach the shore and meet Olruggio before the invitation requires them to be anywhere else. If the meeting runs late, the house will hear it from them.

“And you'll be here?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Rimuru looks toward the drawing, then at Geld's list.

“I seem to have acquired quite a lot of questions about drains.” ᴿ

“Important work.” ᴹ

“Enjoy the sea.” ᴿ

Yuka pulls Mau toward a cup before he can turn the answer into a competition.
