*When: Easthies asks where the other spectators should stand, Mau tells him to move them farther back.*

The answer irritates the man who asked for the demonstration.

Easthies moves them.

Qifrey stands with Olruggio outside the space Mau has chosen. There is room beyond both of them, and a clear way to the door. Mau looks at it once before turning back to his opponent.

“Stop means stop,” he says. ᴹ

“For both of us.” ᴱ

“Yes.” ᴹ

Easthies sets his feet.

Mau lets his hands hang loose at his sides.

* * *

*The same as Tsurugi?* he asks Ciel. ᴹ

*The purpose. Not the exposure.* ᶜⁱ

He watches Easthies' hand move toward his equipment.

*Less.* ᴹ

*Considerably. And you will still need to watch the result. I cannot promise a headache and nothing else because another person recovered.* ᶜⁱ

Mau acknowledges it.

No one has put the sword from that older fight in his hand. No one is threatening to take Yuka away if he fails. He has come here to make a short answer useful, and there is a difference between that and proving he can survive a long one.

He breathes out.

Then waits.

* * *

Easthies begins with a prepared mark.

Mau can see the movement which completes it. The spell reaches the margin around him and stops there, the space between what was sent and what it was meant to touch becoming impossible to finish crossing.

Easthies changes his angle.

Mau moves with him, keeping the other people outside the line. Another effect opens to his left. Qifrey says one word from beyond the edge, naming the part of the drawing Mau should watch.

He listens to it.

The next step takes him clear of the marked ground rather than trusting the space around his skin to explain everything it might do.

Easthies sees the change.

“You needed that warning.” ᴱ

“Yes.” ᴹ

The answer costs Mau nothing he wants to keep.

He lifts his hands.

* * *

The distance around him returns to ordinary space before the next thing opens.

Ciel keeps the interval they discussed. Mau gives the technique a boundary with one opponent inside it and the people watching beyond it.

“Domain Expansion.” ᴹ

The room disappears for a fraction of the time it would take someone to turn toward the voice.

Then it is a room again.

Easthies has stopped with his hand half raised.

Mau is beside him before his knee begins to give.

* * *

“It's over,” he says. ᴹ

Easthies does not answer at once.

Mau catches the weight of him, lowering him rather than making him discover the floor with his face. Qifrey is already moving when Mau calls him in. Olruggio stays at the edge long enough to make sure the spectators are not following both of them.

The pen Easthies held has remained between his fingers.

Qifrey takes it carefully and puts it where he can see it when he is able to look.

Mau hears Ciel count the returning responses. He feels the emptiness where the technique has just been, familiar enough that he does not try to repair his way through it by hurting a different part of himself.

He stays on one knee beside the man he chose to expose.

* * *

Easthies' first word is incomplete.

His second is Mau's name.

“Here,” Mau answers. ᴹ

The gaze finds him by degrees. Anger comes before the man has finished recovering the strength to make it useful.

Mau lets him have the time.

“What was that?” Easthies asks at last. ᴱ

“Enough information that you couldn't use what you were already doing.” ᴹ

Easthies shuts his eyes, then opens them again with visible care.

Qifrey asks a simple question. He gets a complete answer this time.

Olruggio bends to collect the piece which came loose from the demonstration. When he straightens, he looks toward the person who demanded it.

“Have you learnt what you needed?” ᴼˡ

No one asks Mau for a longer example.

* * *

The woman from the meeting comes forward only when Easthies tells her to.

She asks him whether he can stand. He says he can, and accepts the chair Qifrey brings instead of proving it immediately.

Then she looks at Mau.

“You could have done more.” ᴴ

“I said a short answer.” ᴹ

“And the agreement?” ᴴ

Mau looks toward Qifrey.

His friend has picked up the paper, keeping it clear of the equipment on the floor.

“Still about the people who live in the house,” Qifrey says. ᵠ

The woman nods slowly.

She asks for a clean table and water before they finish the final wording. Easthies remains in the chair while Olruggio watches him, and Mau stands with the ache of the closed technique settling behind his eyes.

The door opens to let an attendant through.
