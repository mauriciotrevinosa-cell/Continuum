*When: the attendant asks who needs the water, Mau turns toward Easthies.*

“He does.” ᴹ

The tray is still level.

The man carrying it looks past Mau at the papers in Qifrey's hand. His other wrist is hidden beneath the cloth laid over the cups.

Ciel catches the completed mark at the same moment Mau sees the wrist turn.

*Left.* ᶜⁱ

Mau moves.

The tray hits the floor after he has already caught the man's arm.

What comes out of the sleeve is black.

* * *

Later, he will remember how little time there was between recognising the colour as ink and feeling it strike his hand.

Most of it never reaches his face.

He turns the wrist away from Qifrey, breaks the narrow fitting beneath the sleeve and forces the man's arm down. The prepared spell has already spent its pressure. A thin spray catches his cheek. A smaller thread strikes the corner of his mouth as he opens it to warn the others.

He tastes it before he can spit.

The hand in his grip twists harder. The attacker is trying to drive the broken fitting toward his jaw.

Mau puts him on the floor.

Alive.

One knee beside the man's shoulder, one hand keeping the arm where it cannot complete another movement.

He has time to do that.

He has already swallowed.

* * *

Qifrey is beside him with the real water before Mau can ask.

“Spit. Don't drink it yet.” ᵠ

Mau obeys.

Olruggio takes the broken fitting from the floor and holds it clear of every cup. The cloth has fallen away from the attacker's other arm. Beneath it is a folded hat with a brim stiff enough to hold the curve someone tried to hide.

Easthies gets out of the chair too quickly and has to catch himself against its back.

“Stay there,” Olruggio snaps. ᴼˡ

For once, he is obeyed.

Mau rinses again when Qifrey tells him to. The pressure against the floor stays exact. The man beneath him is breathing hard and trying to laugh at the same time.

“No pen,” the man says. ᴮʳ

Mau stops rinsing long enough to look at him.

“What?” ᴹ

“Let's see what you draw without one.” ᴮʳ

* * *

It would be easy to make the next question hurt.

Mau knows that with an unpleasant clarity which has nothing to do with losing control. He can feel the fragile arrangement of the shoulder beneath his hand. He knows how much farther it would go.

Qifrey says his name.

Mau looks up.

His friend is holding out a clean cloth.

He takes it.

Olruggio gets the remaining equipment away. The guards who come through the door do so with Easthies telling them exactly whose hands to watch. Mau waits until the attacker is held securely before taking his weight off him.

The man keeps looking at Mau's mouth.

Mau wipes the last visible ink from it.

* * *

*Tell me,* he says to Ciel. ᴹ

*The external material is being removed. A small amount passed your mouth before you could clear it. I am tracing that separately.* ᶜⁱ

*Can we take it out?* ᴹ

*What remains loose, yes. Some has already made contact with tissue. I want Qifrey's account of the ink before either of us calls it ordinary contamination.* ᶜⁱ

Mau looks toward Qifrey.

“I swallowed some.” ᴹ

His friend's face changes.

“How much?” ᵠ

“Less than he wanted. More than none.” ᴹ

Qifrey holds the clean water steady.

Mau hears Noise move behind the places where Ciel is watching.

For a moment there is a bright, wordless attention inside him.

*Wait,* Mau tells it. ᴹ

The attention remains.

The movement stops.

* * *

The woman from the meeting kneels to look at the discarded tray without touching it.

Her face has gone very still.

“He wasn't assigned to this room,” she says. ᴴ

Olruggio looks toward the door through which the attacker was brought in.

“Find out how he got here.” ᴼˡ

Mau gets to his feet.

Qifrey rises with him.

“How do you feel?” he asks. ᵠ

Mau takes a moment to answer rather than giving the first one he would prefer to be true.

“Angry. I can breathe. Nothing hurts that wasn't already aching.” ᴹ

Qifrey looks toward his eyes, then the hand holding the cloth.

“Sit down while we finish checking.” ᵠ

Mau sits.

He calls Rimuru before anyone can decide this is the kind of detail he can leave out because he is still able to stand.
