*When: Rimuru asks whether they are leaving, Mau says, “Soon. Something happened first.”*

He tells him what.

Qifrey stays beside him while he explains the tray, the ink, the amount they have already removed and the part Ciel is still tracing. The breath at the other end of the connection changes before Rimuru speaks again.

*Are you hurt?* ᴿ

*Not from that. I used the short domain before it. I'm still coming out of the cost.* ᴹ

*Yuka's here. May I tell her?* ᴿ

Mau closes his eyes briefly.

*Yes. Tell her I'm coming home and that we'll go straight to Shoko.* ᴹ

He opens them to find Qifrey watching him.

“They know,” Mau says. ᴹ

His friend nods.

“Good.” ᵠ

* * *

They finish the agreement in a different room.

The woman who speaks for the Hall has called for two people to check what is brought through the door. Easthies remains seated. He can follow the conversation again, though he keeps the light to one side of his face.

The attack has made everyone less patient with claims that keeping the secret is the same as keeping people safe.

It has also given them a reason to write down exactly who is responsible for finding out what went wrong here.

Qifrey reads each line before signing.

He and Olruggio will account for the instruction they give. The private circle may include the house's residents and the families who join them. The means of making the ink will remain outside public trade. Changes to that instruction will be discussed with the people affected before information about them is sent anywhere.

The Hall's representatives undertake not to send people to remove children, alter memories or force entry under those claims. Concerns will be brought to the responsible teachers. Visits require an invitation.

“You can answer for the people you send,” Mau says. ᴹ

“For our own orders,” the woman answers. “I will not pretend every person who puts on a hat will obey me.” ᴴ

“Then don't pretend ours should have to surrender to every one of them.” ᴹ

She looks at the line she has agreed to sign.

Then signs it.

* * *

Mau does not ask to take the prisoner home.

He asks for the account of what was found in the man's equipment and a way to hear the result of the investigation. Qifrey keeps the closed sample container Olruggio has secured for them. They will not need to guess whether the material in Mau was the same as ordinary ink merely because it looked familiar.

Easthies watches the container.

“This changes nothing about why the prohibited work is dangerous,” he says. ᴱ

“Then help us find out who thought forcing it into someone would be useful,” Qifrey answers. ᵠ

Mau looks at his friend.

He has heard Qifrey sound courteous often enough to recognise when courtesy has become a very thin layer over something harder.

Easthies nods once.

It is an answer they can hold him to.

* * *

At the shore, Olruggio picks up his travelling bag.

“I'm coming with you.” ᴼˡ

Qifrey turns toward him.

“For how long?” ᵠ

“Tonight, to start with. I'd like to hear what the person examining him thinks before I decide the difficult part is finished.” ᴼˡ

Mau looks toward the sea, then back at them.

“I can ask about somewhere to sleep.” ᴹ

“I have a bedroll.” ᴼˡ

Qifrey adjusts the strap of his own bag.

“There's room in mine.” ᵠ

“We'll see what you've put on the floor before we call that an offer.” ᴼˡ

Mau smiles despite the taste he can still remember.

He tells the house they will be bringing one guest back for the night.

* * *

Yuka is waiting where Kaisel can land.

Mau sees her before he begins to climb down. She lets Qifrey and Olruggio get clear, then catches him by the front of his coat and looks at his face.

“How are you?” ʸ

“Still angry. Tired. It doesn't hurt.” ᴹ

Her hands move to his arms.

“Shoko's ready.” ʸ

“I said we'd go there first.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

She kisses his cheek, well away from the place he has been wiping at all afternoon, and takes his hand.

Mau lets her lead him inside.

* * *

Shoko makes the first part of the visit very ordinary.

Sit down. Open your mouth. Tell her what happened before telling her why he thinks it ought to be fine.

Qifrey explains the ink as he knows it. Olruggio puts the sample where she asks and names what was different about the delivery. Mau passes on Ciel's findings without turning them into a public voice in the room.

Shoko looks at him after each answer.

“Any change in breathing? Swallowing? Sensation?” ˢᵒ

He answers each separately.

She checks the two heart rhythms he already has, then his hands, the skin at his throat and the ordinary responses he is becoming very used to having examined.

“Tonight, you tell me if any of those answers changes,” she says. “Tomorrow morning I check again, even if you still think it doesn't hurt.” ˢᵒ

“Okay.” ᴹ

She looks at him a moment longer, as though pleased by the absence of the second half of that answer.

Then lets him stand.

* * *

There is food waiting after the examination.

Qifrey sits with the girls and their mother to explain the words they actually brought home. Olruggio takes a place beside him, and Richeh listens carefully to the part about where her message can go next.

Mau sits with Yuka and Rimuru.

He tells them about the demonstration, the cost and the moment the disguised attendant reached him. He tells them that the man is alive and held there, that they do not yet know his name.

Yuka listens without taking her hand out of his.

When he finishes, she puts his bowl closer.

“Now eat before you start deciding you need to explain it a fourth time.” ʸ

He looks at the food, then at her.

“People keep asking me to do that.” ᴹ

“They're very sensible.” ʸ

Mau picks up the spoon.

For the moment, nothing inside him moves that he has not asked to move.
