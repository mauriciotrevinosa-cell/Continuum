* * *

Shoko asks the same questions in a different order.

Mau answers them with rather less confidence that being bored by a question has anything to do with whether it matters. She listens, checks, and asks for the account of the ink again when Qifrey arrives with Olruggio.

The sample has not revealed a convenient different substance which will explain everything at a glance. Its behaviour on the test page is familiar to both teachers. The difficulty remains the place it reached.

“It isn't making everyone who touches it change shape,” Olruggio says. ᴼˡ

“No,” Qifrey answers. ᵠ

Shoko looks toward Mau.

“So whatever we watch for includes the way your body is using it.” ˢᵒ

Mau nods.

Inside, Ciel agrees that the trace has stopped moving as loose material. She has not finished accounting for every place it made contact.

He tells Shoko that too.

She lets him go with a request to stay within easy reach and a much firmer instruction to report a change while it is still small.

* * *

Breakfast has continued without him.

Ori has taken the place beside Frieren and is showing her a shape she folded badly enough that it now requires an explanation. Milim is trying to contribute a better explanation. Fern has finished her first cup and is listening to Stark describe where Geld thinks a covered path might go.

Mau sits with Yuka, and Rem puts down the bowl she saved.

“Still good?” she asks. ᴿᵉ

“Still being watched.” ᴹ

“Good,” she says. ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks up from the other side.

“You say that like it's a job.” ᵁ

“He's very good at giving us practice.” ᴿᵉ

Mau puts his spoon down long enough to hold both hands up.

“I'm sitting right here.” ᴹ

“See?” Umaru says. “Improvement.” ᵁ

Yuka laughs against her cup.

* * *

After the meal, Qifrey explains the agreement to the people who want to hear it.

Olruggio sits beside him. Coco's mother asks the question she cares about first and gets the words which answer it. Maomao asks where the line falls between using something finished and learning to make it. Jinshi asks whether a visitor who sees a light has thereby become somebody's problem.

“Seeing the object isn't the agreement to teach them its making,” Qifrey says. ᵠ

“Then that should be easy enough to keep separate,” Jinshi answers. ᴶ

It is one of the first questions of the morning to become simpler after someone asks it.

Mau enjoys that for a moment.

Richeh waits until the others have finished, then asks Olruggio when he is going back.

He looks at the page she has brought.

“I'm staying a little longer. We can send this through the route I used before.” ᴼˡ

She considers the answer.

“Can I finish it first?” ᴿⁱ

“Yes.” ᴼˡ

Qifrey watches his friend make room beside him at the table.

* * *

Outside, Geld and Byakuya have begun turning the walk into a plan they can build from.

The first material list is shorter than Mau expected and more demanding in the places where he thought quantity would solve it. Kurobe wants to test a joint before anyone promises how much load it will take. Water has to go somewhere after the new roofs collect it. The common room must still feed people while the work changes the ground around it.

“We can help carry,” Mau says. ᴹ

Geld looks toward his shadow, then back at him.

“When we know where it's going.” ᴳᵉ

Yuka catches Mau's expression.

“He means don't have Beru build a wall because he got excited.” ʸ

“I wasn't going to say Beru.” ᴹ

“You were thinking him.” ʸ

He looks down at the page.

“A little.” ᴹ

* * *

They help move the table into shade instead.

Mau takes the end nearest Yuta. He does it carefully, keeping the feet clear of the line Geld has just asked them not to disturb. When they set it down, Yuka reaches for a cup which has remained balanced improbably close to the edge.

He catches it before she has to.

For a moment his right wrist looks as though a strand of hair has caught across it.

He puts the cup down.

The strand is beneath the skin.

Yuka follows his gaze.

“Mau?” ʸ

He turns his arm. The line turns with it, dark and fine and much too straight.

*Ciel.* ᴹ

*I see it.* ᶜⁱ

“Let's go back to Shoko.” ᴹ
