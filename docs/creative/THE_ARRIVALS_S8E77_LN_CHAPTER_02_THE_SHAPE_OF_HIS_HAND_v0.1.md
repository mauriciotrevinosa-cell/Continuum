*When: Shoko takes his wrist, the line has reached the base of his thumb.*

Mau sits before she has to tell him.

Yuka takes the chair on his other side. Yuta goes to find Qifrey and Olruggio while Shoko looks at the mark beneath the skin, then at Mau's face.

“Sensation?” ˢᵒ

“Normal until here.” ᴹ

He points with his left hand, stopping short of the place where his right has begun to feel strangely distant. The two stiff fingers on the other hand still resist the movement they have resisted for a long time. It is almost comforting to find an old difficulty behaving like itself.

Shoko touches the right palm lightly.

He feels that.

She moves toward the thumb.

He feels pressure a moment after he sees her make it.

“Delayed,” he says. ᴹ

Her eyes lift to his.

“Keep telling me.” ˢᵒ

* * *

The next line appears while they are looking at the first.

It crosses the underside of his forearm and stops where a scar interrupts its course. For a moment it seems to be following the scar. Then the skin beside it draws inward, as though a thread has been pulled from somewhere too deep to reach.

Mau's fingers curl.

Yuka starts toward his hand and stops when he shakes his head.

“That hurts.” ᴹ

Shoko changes her grip at once.

“Where?” ˢᵒ

He tries to answer.

The skin between the two lines parts without opening like a cut.

Mau watches a narrow strip lose the shape his body gave it. There is no spray of blood. The absence makes the sight worse for a second: the surface of his arm has become something dark and glossy, pulling into filaments that do not quite settle where the rest of him says they should.

Yuka's chair scrapes the floor.

Mau looks at her instead of the arm.

“I'm here,” he says. ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

Her face has gone pale.

She stays beside him.

* * *

His first instinct is to repair it.

The answer comes back wrong.

The tissue gathers. The straight lines sharpen. A brief heat runs from the wrist toward the elbow, and the part he has just restored begins to pull apart again in exactly the same place.

*Stop feeding that loop,* Ciel says. ᶜⁱ

He stops.

It takes more effort than continuing would have done. The arm belongs to him. The pain belongs to him. The instruction not to fix it has to get past both facts before he can obey it.

Shoko sees the change in his face.

“What did she say?” ˢᵒ

“The repair is giving it another way to happen.” ᴹ

She does not ask him to try harder.

She supports the forearm so he no longer has to hold its weight himself, and tells Yuka where to put the folded cloth.

Mau lets them do it.

* * *

Qifrey arrives with Olruggio close behind him.

The sight on the bed stops both of them.

Then Olruggio puts down the closed ink sample while Qifrey comes to Mau's side.

“May I look?” ᵠ

“Please.” ᴹ

Qifrey bends close without touching the changing surface. His expression tightens as he follows one line to another.

“It resembles the beginning of a drawing,” he says. “It isn't a completed one I know.” ᵠ

“Is the ink doing that?” Shoko asks. ˢᵒ

“Not by itself.” ᵠ

Olruggio takes the test page they used this morning and lays it beside the sample. The ink on it has remained exactly where he put it.

“That material needs a working arrangement,” he says. “A surface. A shape. Something to let the mark become a spell. It doesn't turn an ordinary arm into one because someone spilled it.” ᴼˡ

Mau looks at his own arm.

He knows a place where incomplete arrangements are always being finished.

*Noise?* ᴹ

*Didn't,* comes the answer. ᴺ

He hears the fear inside the single word before he understands it.

*I know. I'm asking what you can see.* ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru arrives while Mau is listening.

He takes the place Yuta leaves clear near the door. Shoko tells him what has changed. He asks Ciel through the connection they already share, and waits when she tells him she needs another moment.

Mau hears Noise struggle to make the answer small enough for words.

*Material. Shape. Together.* ᴺ

There is a sensation of pieces which have lain near one another for a long time finding a way to touch. The old repairs in Mau's body have not been taught the difference between a foreign instruction and a new piece of the thing they are trying to keep whole.

Ink has supplied a material language to a body which is already trying to interpret everything it contains.

No deliberate new construction began.

The existing work has found something it does not know how to leave unfinished.

Mau repeats the useful parts aloud. His voice catches once when the filaments tighten at his wrist.

Yuka puts her hand against his shoulder.

He leans into it just enough to keep speaking.

* * *

“Then separate the instructions,” Shoko says. ˢᵒ

Mau looks toward her.

“I don't mean turn your body off. I mean stop asking the same repair to answer two different questions.” ˢᵒ

Qifrey turns the test page so Ciel can observe it through the access Mau is choosing to give her.

Olruggio draws a simple line beside the old one, then another which meets it without completing a seal. He names what has to happen before the marks become useful and what is still only ink sitting on a page.

Ciel asks Mau to stay with the sensation in his hand while she compares the ordinary example with the changing tissue.

He tries.

For a few seconds the pain makes every answer sound like the same answer.

Then he can distinguish the pull toward the unfinished mark from the deeper effort of keeping the arm his.

“There,” he says aloud. ᴹ

Shoko watches his face.

“Tell her which part.” ˢᵒ

He does.

* * *

The first useful pause is very small.

One dark filament settles against the others and remains there. The surface does not become skin again. It simply stops trying to move while Ciel holds the foreign pattern away from the reference his ordinary repair is using.

Mau breathes out too quickly.

Shoko looks toward his chest.

“Slowly.” ˢᵒ

He tries again.

The two rhythms inside him remain the two she checked this morning. The right forearm is still something she would not let him walk out of the room with. The distinction is enough for her to change the angle of the support instead of asking anyone to panic.

Yuka moves closer.

“Can I touch this side?” she asks, indicating his left shoulder. ʸ

Shoko nods.

Yuka puts an arm around him.

He lets his head rest against hers for one breath, then looks back at the arm.

“Okay,” he says. “What next?” ᴹ

* * *

They do not give him a grand answer.

Ciel needs to know whether the pause can hold when he stops actively watching it. Qifrey and Olruggio need to compare the material they have with what is happening in him. Shoko wants the pain and the sensation measured often enough that improvement does not become something they assume from a better-looking surface.

Rimuru asks which part he can help carry.

Mau almost says he has it.

He looks at the hand on the support and the people around it.

“Stay while we find out?” ᴹ

“Yes,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

The answer comes quickly enough that Mau smiles before the pain can make him forget he wanted to.

* * *

Outside the room, the house rearranges the day.

Yuta takes the immediate questions downstairs. Maki comes to the door, hears the practical account Shoko is willing to give and asks Yuka what she needs brought up. Nobody tells the children to stand and watch a frightening thing happen to someone they love.

Geld moves the measurement discussion away from the window. The work can wait for an answer without becoming a disaster of its own.

Inside, Mau follows Ciel's voice through the next attempt.

The line across his arm loosens a little.

It stays part of him.

He tells Shoko that he can feel her hand sooner this time.

She makes him say exactly where.
