*When: Ciel asks for permission to build a place to hold the unfinished part, Mau asks her to show him what she means.*

She does not make it into a room.

The image is closer to two lines which have been allowed to share an edge. One belongs to the repairs his body already knows how to make. The other carries the material understanding the ink has opened. Every time the first tries to close a gap, it completes part of the second.

He can feel the connection now that she has shown him where to look.

It is small enough to be absurd.

It has spent the afternoon making his hand unrecognisable.

*You want them to stop sharing the edge.* ᴹ

*Yes. Preserve what we can learn without allowing it to keep defining the tissue which is meant to carry it.* ᶜⁱ

Noise is very quiet beyond her.

Mau waits.

*Can do,* it says at last. ᴺ

*Only this part,* he answers. ᴹ

*This.* ᴺ

The certainty in the smaller answer matters more than an ambitious one would have done.

* * *

Mau tells Shoko what they propose.

He has to stop once while Ciel gives him a clearer word for the distinction, and again while Qifrey explains why an open drawing is not simply a failed completed one.

“Don't complete it for the sake of seeing whether it works,” Qifrey says. ᵠ

Olruggio turns the page with the unfinished mark toward Mau.

“You don't have to set every spell off to understand that it has somewhere dangerous to go.” ᴼˡ

Shoko looks at the dark surface supported on the cloth.

“And while they're doing that, what happens here?” ˢᵒ

Mau listens to Ciel's answer before passing it on.

“The hold stays. They separate the new material route from what was maintaining the arm yesterday. Then we let that repair resume in sections.” ᴹ

“Using yesterday's arm as the reference.” ˢᵒ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Not an imaginary perfect one.” ˢᵒ

He looks toward the old scars farther up.

“Mine.” ᴹ

She nods.

“Then tell me where you're starting.” ˢᵒ

* * *

They choose the smallest area which can give them a useful answer.

Mau stays conscious through it. He tells Shoko where the pressure changes and where it does not. Qifrey keeps the external example incomplete. Olruggio watches the contact between lines with an intensity which has made him forget he is still holding the same pen.

Ciel keeps the two processes apart.

Noise builds the separation they have agreed to, slowly enough that the word has a different meaning from the excited rush Mau remembers from old breaches.

The first part of the surface turns from glossy black into something paler.

Mau feels the cloth beneath it.

He draws a sharp breath.

“There.” ᴹ

Shoko touches another point.

He tells her he cannot feel that one properly yet.

“Good answer,” she says. ˢᵒ

He almost laughs.

* * *

Inside, DIO asks whether he needs anything from her.

Mau has not gone to the shared room. He can still hear the question through the arrangement which lets his guests reach him without claiming the body around them.

*Not a change,* he says. *Just wait with us.* ᴹ

*I can do that.* ᴰⁱ

Ashborn says nothing for a while.

Then asks whether the work is proceeding where Mau has chosen it to proceed.

*This part is,* Mau answers. ᴹ

The distinction leaves something still to talk about when his arm is no longer the urgent question in the room.

For now, Ashborn accepts it.

Mau returns his attention to Shoko's voice.

* * *

The next section takes longer.

It pulls at the wrist hard enough that Mau has to ask them to stop. They do. Yuka moves the cup toward his left hand while Shoko checks the hold and Ciel adjusts the part which tried to close too early.

Nobody tells him he was nearly finished as a reason to keep hurting.

When he is ready, he tells them.

This time the surface gathers from the edge instead of tightening toward the line. The shape of his thumb returns before the sensation does. He watches it with an expression Yuka has seen when he is afraid to believe the simple thing in front of him.

She puts her hand beside his on the bed.

“One thing at a time,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her fingers.

Then back at his own.

“One.” ᴹ

* * *

By the time the lamp needs tending, the arm looks enough like an arm that Olruggio sits down.

Shoko does not let that settle the examination. She tests the same places again, has Mau move the fingers he can move, and watches the wrist while he tries to turn it.

There is still numbness along one edge. The new lines are faint rather than gone. Ciel says the separation is holding without Mau having to stare at it every moment.

Noise offers one quiet word.

*Held.* ᴺ

Mau closes his eyes briefly.

*Thank you.* ᴹ

It is an answer to the work they actually did, not a promise to let everything else begin.

* * *

He stays in the examination room for the night.

The bed is less comfortable than theirs. He says so once, and Yuka tells him she will put it in the report. Shoko has arranged who she wants close enough to call and makes him repeat what he is to report instead of deciding it can wait until morning.

Olruggio takes the pages away to compare them somewhere that will let Qifrey's eyes rest. Rimuru comes back for a while, bringing news of the kitchen and a question about whether Mau wants the lamp lower.

Yuka chooses the chair beside the bed, then trades it for the better-supported one Maki brings when she sees it.

“Go to sleep if you can,” she tells him. ʸ

He looks down at the hand lying on the cloth.

Then at hers, resting safely on the other side of him.

“Will you tell me when you need to move?” ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

He believes her.

For the first time all afternoon, he stops watching his own skin long enough to close his eyes.
