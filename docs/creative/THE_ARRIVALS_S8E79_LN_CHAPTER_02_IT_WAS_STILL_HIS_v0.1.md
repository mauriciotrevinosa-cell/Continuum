*When: Mau wakes, the hand on the support has become a hand again.*

He does not move it at once.

The lines are still there, faint enough that he has to turn toward the light to follow one across his wrist. The scar above it has kept the shape it had before yesterday. When he tries the fingers, they move slowly, with an unpleasant delay along the outer edge.

*Morning,* Ciel says. ᶜⁱ

*You stayed.* ᴹ

*We arranged the work so I could remain without doing the same thing every second.* ᶜⁱ

He looks toward Yuka, asleep in the supported chair with a blanket over her legs.

*Good arrangement.* ᴹ

*I thought so.* ᶜⁱ

Mau smiles and waits for Shoko instead of turning the first encouraging sight into permission to leave.

* * *

The examination is longer than he would like.

He answers, moves, stops, and explains the difference between a place which feels numb and one which merely feels unfamiliar. Shoko asks him to close his eyes for part of it. When he opens them again, Yuka is awake and watching from beside the bed.

“Can I touch it?” she asks. ʸ

Shoko shows her where.

Mau sees her fingertip settle against his palm.

This time he feels it almost at once.

His face changes before he can decide what expression would be sensible.

Yuka leaves her hand there.

“There you are,” she says softly. ʸ

“Still here.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

Shoko lets them keep the moment before asking about the next finger.

* * *

He is allowed back to their room after lunch.

There are conditions, all of them more specific than simply being careful. No testing the new material route for a useful spell. No combat or strenuous work while the hold is still being watched. Another check tomorrow, and an earlier one if the sensation changes again.

Mau repeats them.

“Good,” Shoko says. “Now go somewhere comfortable before you decide this bed was a challenge.” ˢᵒ

He laughs.

Yuka brings the bag, though there is almost nothing in it, and walks beside him through the house.

The morning has left its evidence along the way. A page of measurements. A cup at the place where Jinshi likes to read a list before answering it. Something Ori has asked Frieren not to put away yet. People look up as they pass and ask how he is doing.

He has to practise answering without turning every one into the full story.

“Better. Still checking.” ᴹ

It is enough to let Rem smile and tell him she will save them food later.

* * *

Qifrey catches Olruggio outside the room before he carries the last pages away.

“You can stay,” he says. ᵠ

Olruggio looks at him.

“I have been.” ᴼˡ

“I mean after this part.” ᵠ

The answer takes longer.

Olruggio looks down at the papers, then toward the sound of Tetia asking somebody where she put the drawing she was certain she left on the table.

“If I do, I want a place to work where I can leave something without it becoming part of dinner.” ᴼˡ

Qifrey smiles.

“We can ask for that.” ᵠ

“And we discuss the teaching properly. Between us.” ᴼˡ

“Yes.” ᵠ

Olruggio shifts the pages to his other hand.

“Then ask.” ᴼˡ

Richeh comes past with the message she has finished. He tells her when it will go through the ordinary route, and lets her watch him put it safely with the other letters.

* * *

Upstairs, Yuka puts Mau's bag beside their chair.

He sits on the bed and looks toward the window. There is still a whole house outside the room. Tomorrow's plans have not disappeared because he failed to stand over them today.

She sits beside him.

“What?” ʸ

He turns his right hand over carefully.

“It feels strange.” ᴹ

“Stranger than yesterday?” ʸ

“Much less.” ᴹ

She smiles and puts her hand beside it, letting him choose the movement.

He closes his fingers around hers without pulling.

They stay that way while somebody calls from downstairs and a voice in the courtyard answers too loudly.

“When we move,” Yuka says, “I still want to hear that.” ʸ

Mau looks toward the door.

“We should put it on the drawing.” ᴹ

“I think we already did.” ʸ

He rests his head against hers.

For a while, neither goes to check.
