* * *

Olruggio has already found a problem with the proposed teaching space.

It shares a wall with somewhere children will be encouraged to run.

“I like children,” he tells Geld. “I also like knowing which noise means something has gone wrong.” ᴼˡ

Tetia looks at Qifrey.

“He does,” Qifrey says. ᵠ

“Which one?” ᵀᵉ

“Both.” ᵠ

Olruggio moves the mark for the supply cupboard. The door can face the quieter passage and still open toward the places people will use. He asks for a high surface where a child who is looking at something else will not catch a sleeve on an open bottle.

Coco's mother leans closer.

“And somewhere they can put the work they're proud of without having to clear it for the next meal.” ᶜᵐ

He looks at her.

Then marks that too.

* * *

Mau joins them carrying his own cup in his left hand.

“You're staying?” he asks Olruggio. ᴹ

“I'd like to.” ᴼˡ

Qifrey has gone quiet beside him.

Olruggio notices, and looks annoyed by the amount of feeling his friend has managed to put into merely listening.

“If we're doing this, we do it properly. A place for the work. Somewhere to sleep that isn't the same pile of papers. And you don't tell me three days afterwards when you've agreed to something difficult.” ᴼˡ

Mau looks at Qifrey.

“I feel strangely represented.” ᴹ

“You may listen too.” ᴼˡ

* * *

They bring the choice to the table before putting a permanent mark on the drawing.

Rimuru asks what Olruggio wants to keep of his work elsewhere. He answers carefully: the people he has already told where he is, the obligations he has taken on, the means of knowing if the agreement changes. Staying here does not require pretending the coast has stopped existing.

Qifrey asks about the teaching.

They decide which questions they will bring to one another before changing a lesson. Which equipment needs to be kept out of ordinary reach. How a person who wants to learn can ask without finding that curiosity has signed away all their evenings.

The girls are listening from the other side.

Agott asks whether this means they will have two different answers to every question.

“Only the ones worth asking,” Olruggio says. ᴼˡ

Qifrey laughs.

Richeh puts her chin in her hand, looking pleased in a way she would object to having explained aloud.

* * *

His place on the plan ends up near Qifrey, but not inside his bedroom.

Two private rooms with a work area they can reach from the teaching passage. The store between that and the general house can remain locked without locking anyone out of their own bed. Coco and her mother can reach the girls without walking through a lesson.

“I want to help with keeping the room usable,” her mother says. ᶜᵐ

Olruggio looks up.

“Because you want to?” ᴼˡ

“Yes. And I would like to learn the things I need to know if I'm doing it. I don't want to be handed a cloth and told afterwards which bottle I should never have touched.” ᶜᵐ

Qifrey gives her the same attention he gives an apprentice asking a serious question.

“Then we begin there.” ᵠ

Mau watches Coco lean against her mother's arm.

The page has room for that too.

* * *

Olruggio stays on the bedroll for now.

He says this himself when Tetia asks whether the new room is ready. Geld points out that the ground beneath it has barely finished being a decision. The answer makes her laugh, then go outside to look at the place she has been helping imagine.

Qifrey waits until she has gone.

“I'm glad,” he says quietly. ᵠ

Olruggio looks toward him.

For once, he leaves the irritation out of the answer.

“I know.” ᴼˡ

Then he asks where Qifrey has put the good pen, and their first argument as people choosing to remain begins over something small enough to enjoy.
