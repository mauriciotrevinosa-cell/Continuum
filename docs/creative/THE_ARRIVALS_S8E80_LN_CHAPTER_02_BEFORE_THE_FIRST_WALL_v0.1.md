*When: Geld puts the final working drawing on the table, Umaru looks for the Fort before looking for her own room.*

It is where she asked for it.

The old common space remains beside its door. A second way out leads toward the playing court, clear of the kitchen traffic. The path to the family rooms is short enough to walk when someone has already called dinner once and is preparing to call it less pleasantly.

She nods with the seriousness of a head of state.

“Acceptable.” ᵁ

Rimuru makes a small sound beside Mau.

“We've survived the difficult part.” ᴿ

Geld looks at the rest of the page.

“There are several difficult parts.” ᴳᵉ

* * *

They walk through them rather than voting on a picture nobody has learnt how to read.

The old house will keep the meals and the place people already know how to find. The new galleries will step around the roots instead of pressing the tree into a narrower circle. Homes face small shared courts, with paths between them and a way to go upstairs without entering another person's room.

Mau and Yuka's place is close to the centre, on the side where an east-facing window can actually see morning. Rimuru has his own bedroom and the third remains the guest room Shizue has been asked whether she wants. Yuta and Maki have an independent door nearby; Mai and Tsurugi each keep theirs.

“Nobody has to walk through us to find them?” Yuka asks. ʸ

Geld shows her the shared passage.

She follows it with her finger.

“Good.” ʸ

* * *

Rem and Ram choose the two-bedroom home with a small shared sitting place.

Rem looks toward Umaru while Ram asks how much noise will cross the landing from Seika's door. Seika says she would like to be close enough to find out, and neither looks at the other for a moment after that.

Umaru and Suika have their own space at the crossing, with Taihei on one short route and Mau on another. Bocchi and the band can get to the music room by their own stair. A small entrance lobby will keep an opened door from sending rehearsal straight into the sleeping court. The space set aside beside Bocchi's home is still a future family's space, not proof anyone has arrived.

Gojo asks about the two bedrooms he and Shoko chose.

Shoko asks about the room between them.

“A table,” she says. ˢᵒ

“A good one,” he adds. ᴳ

Geld writes it down without asking why both of them find it funny.

* * *

The quieter homes are still connected to the same centre.

Kaneki and Touka look at the room where Ichika can sleep. Eren, Mikasa and Armin choose places close enough to the paths they share without surrendering their own doors. Momo's group spends rather longer discussing the sitting space than Geld expected. Jiji offers a suggestion which Turbo Granny dismisses so promptly that he offers a worse one on purpose.

Maomao insists that a patient must not have to walk through their bedroom to reach a consultation. Jinshi points to the separate approach and then asks about the place she wants for plants.

Anko and Nazuna ask about dark sleeping rooms and a late route which will not wake every neighbour. Marin and Wakana keep home separate from the noisy work. Senku and Byakuya argue good-naturedly about which room ought to have the better light.

The five girls around Nano's carefully annotated page have already made two corrections. Nobody separates Kusuri from her friends merely because a laboratory needs a different door.

Mau listens while the seven witches discuss how a quiet tea court can still belong to the house.

Daphne wants a useful path toward the growing ground.

Sekhmet wants someone to stop putting that path where her chair will be.

Geld changes the chair.

* * *

The temporary buildings get names which describe what they are for.

A little inn for people who are visiting. Emergency rooms beside the clinical space, where help can arrive without entering someone's home. A separate reception and short-stay place near the agreed approach for people who have just reached them and do not yet know where they belong.

“Not Jinshi sitting in an empty house waiting for the world,” Maomao says. ᴹᵐ

Jinshi looks at her.

“Thank you.” ᴶ

“It was a poor use of your face.” ᴹᵐ

He spends the next part of the explanation looking far more pleased than the explanation requires.

Geld leaves room for future homes to rise where the ground will carry them, no more than the four floors they discussed. The first work will build what people have chosen, not every possible bed in every possible future.

* * *

The materials are the part they cannot draw into existence.

Kurobe has tested the joints they want to use. Some old stock will serve; some timber must come from Tempest, and some needs cutting and preparation before a soldier can carry it. Crystal income can pay for it without teaching wood to season faster. Glyphs can help with particular tasks, but they need people who know what they are doing.

Mau offers the hauling they can actually use.

“Eighty ordinary soldiers to start. You tell them where the loads go after the ground is marked. We can change the number when we know whether that's useful.” ᴹ

Geld looks toward Shoko before accepting the part which involves Mau.

“Existing commands, no physical work with that arm,” she says. “And you tell me if it costs you something different.” ˢᵒ

Mau nods.

The forty ordinary soldiers with Muster keep their separate home duty. No one has to empty the watch in order to carry a beam.

* * *

Rimuru will go to Tempest tomorrow.

Kurobe chooses to go with him to organise the tools and speak to the people he would like to invite to the work. Geld stays to mark the first ground with the people who live on it.

“Ask them,” Geld says. “Don't send them because I asked for help.” ᴳᵉ

“I will,” Rimuru answers. ᴿ

Mau looks around the table one more time.

There are doors on the page which have become decisions now. There are names beside them, and places left empty on purpose.

When Geld rolls it up to protect it from the next meal, the work has finally become something they can begin.
