“That was the lesson?” ᶜᵐ

“Part of it.” ᴼˡ

Coco smiles from the next chair.

Mau sits at the end nearest the open space, his right wrist resting on a folded cloth more from caution now than need. Shoko checked it before breakfast. The numb edge is smaller. The lines have faded enough that he can miss one if he turns his hand the wrong way.

He has not asked whether that makes them an invitation to try something ambitious.

He is trying very hard to deserve how pleased Ciel sounds about that.

* * *

The lesson begins with objects.

A closed bottle. An open one. A page on a safe surface and a page balanced where somebody will have to catch it. Olruggio moves between them while Qifrey asks the people at the table to tell him which situation they would rather discover after a child has run past.

Tetia is offended by how quickly everyone points to the balanced page.

“I don't knock everything over.” ᵀᵉ

“Exactly,” Agott says. “We'd like to preserve your success.” ᴬᵍ

Qifrey waits until the laughter has finished before setting down the pen.

He draws a line.

Nothing happens.

“This part matters,” he says. ᵠ

* * *

Mau understands the line differently now.

Before, he could see the shape and learn what Qifrey said about it. He could use a finished object and observe the result. The material itself had remained something outside the arrangement his body knew how to make.

Now there is a familiarity which makes the unfinished edge seem almost physical. He can feel why one part of the shape would meet another.

He can also remember what happened when something inside him tried to make that meeting without asking where it ought to end.

*I see it,* he tells Ciel. ᴹ

*And?* ᶜⁱ

*I'm leaving it on the paper.* ᴹ

Noise makes a small sound which might be approval.

Qifrey looks toward Mau when he asks his first question.

It is about where the line stops.

* * *

The people who want to draw get paper.

The people who want to understand the objects they use stay to watch. Maomao asks three questions about storage and one which makes Olruggio put the second bottle farther from the first. Jinshi listens long enough to know which part matters to the reception arrangements, then goes back to Geld without anyone deciding he has failed the lesson.

Coco's mother makes her first mark.

Coco leans closer, catches herself and waits until she is asked.

“Is that right?” her mother says. ᶜᵐ

Qifrey looks at the page.

“Tell me what you wanted it to do.” ᵠ

Mau watches the girl's face change as she hears her mother being given the same question she has learnt to answer.

* * *

Rimuru stops at the doorway before leaving.

Kurobe has put the material list in a case he appears to trust more than a folded page. Kaisel is waiting outside, already called for the journey the house agreed to yesterday. Geld remains with the first workers and the markings on the ground.

“Back this evening if everything stays as planned,” Rimuru tells Mau. ᴿ

“If not, tell us before dinner becomes a search party.” ᴹ

“I know.” ᴿ

“Listen to yourselves.” ʸ

* * *

After the lesson, Mau stays to ask one more question.

Qifrey lets him point to the part of the drawing he means. Olruggio stands beside them, listening while Mau explains what feels newly familiar and what he is refusing to finish inside himself.

“Then we study that here,” Qifrey says, touching the paper. ᵠ

“With actual ink,” Olruggio adds. “On something we can put down.” ᴼˡ

Mau looks from one to the other.

“I can agree to that.” ᴹ

They choose the small, external exercise Ciel can watch without moving the material route out of its hold. It gives Noise something permitted to compare and leaves every unfinished part of Mau's body where Shoko asked them to leave it.

When the mark is done, Qifrey takes the paper away before it can become the next experiment.

Mau looks up.

His friend smiles.

“Lunch.” ᵠ
