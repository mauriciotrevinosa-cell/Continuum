*When: Fern asks where everyone will sleep tomorrow, Rimuru puts the road list beside the drawing instead of on top of it.*

Twenty-two people are preparing to leave Tempest this morning.

Twelve workers. Kurobe with the tools. Ramiris, Beretta and Ranga. Five children whose names Fern makes him say rather than allowing the number to stand in for them. Hinata, who has agreed to bring them and remain while the first arrangements settle.

Geld looks at the list before looking at the space outside.

“The workers know what to bring,” he says. “Let's make sure we know what we're offering.” ᴳᵉ

* * *

The project camp grows along the safe clearing they already checked.

They leave room for each tent to open without a person stepping into a guy rope. The washing route stays clear. The place where the tools will be put is close enough to use and far enough from the sleeping area that Kurobe cannot wake twelve people merely by being unable to leave an interesting joint alone.

Mau carries the small things he has been allowed to carry. The ordinary soldiers take the heavy loads to Geld's marked positions.

Eighty dark figures are a remarkable amount of help once somebody has decided what they should do.

They are an equally remarkable amount of confusion if nobody has.

Geld stops the first pair before they put a stack exactly where the second pair will need to turn.

Mau changes the order.

“Better?” ᴹ

“Much,” Geld says. ᴳᵉ

They keep the separate home watch where it was.

* * *

The children's first nights are discussed with the people who will share them.

Umaru wants to know whether five visitors have remembered the correct rules of the Fort. Suika asks where their bags can go without blocking the door. Frieren points out that both questions can be answered before anybody arrives too tired to care.

They arrange temporary cots and screens in the Fort's usable sleeping space. The exit stays clear. Somebody can get out without climbing over another person. The new children's home on the drawing will still need to be built; nobody pretends a happy visit has finished it.

Shizue says which evenings she would like to spend with them. Frieren and Fern discuss lessons with Qifrey rather than waiting for Shizue to become every kind of teacher at once. Rem offers one part of the routine, then leaves the rest for other people to take.

Rimuru writes down who has actually agreed.

“And if one of them wants to go back?” Stark asks. ˢᵗ

“We arrange it,” he answers. ᴿ

The ease of the sentence lets them keep planning the welcome.

* * *

Hinata's cot has its own screened place in the visitors' camp.

Anko asks to look at it before anyone puts away the spare blankets. Nazuna watches her inspect the route with an expression which grows less innocent every moment.

“What?” Anko asks. ᴬⁿ

“Nothing.” ᴺᶻ

“You're looking very loudly.” ᴬⁿ

Nazuna grins and takes two of the blankets back inside.

Nobody has to lose a bed because somebody else's girlfriend is coming to visit.

* * *

By afternoon, Geld is marking the first work beside the existing common area.

This part will give them a protected work route and a covered place to gather without closing the old kitchen. The foundations avoid the root circle. Kurobe's test has settled which joint they can use, and the material they already have is enough to begin the first section while the carts are on the road.

Senku spends several minutes asking whether an alternative will save time.

Geld gives him a shovel and asks him to demonstrate on the part which has already been approved.

Byakuya turns away, smiling.

Mau watches the first dark soldier lower a marked load where it belongs.

Then another.

The page on the table has begun to acquire weight in the world.
