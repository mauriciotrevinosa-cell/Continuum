The cup in her hands stays warm for a long time.

Rem is outside with Umaru when Ram goes to find her.

They are looking at the first stretch of the gallery, which is still more frame than shelter. Umaru has an opinion about how wide the best place to sit ought to be. Rem asks whether it also has to hold the things she will leave beside her.

“Essential equipment,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“Snacks?” ᴿᵉ

“Essential.” ᵁ

Ram stops before reaching them, letting the sound finish.

When Rem turns, she asks about the room they have chosen together.

“Still happy?” ᴿᵃ

Rem looks from her sister to Umaru and back.

“Yes.” ᴿᵉ

The answer is simple enough to make Ram's expression soften before she has time to hide it.

“Then don't let her turn our sitting room into a store,” Ram says. ᴿᵃ

Umaru looks wounded.

“There are negotiations in progress.” ᵁ

* * *

They walk back together when the light begins to go.

Umaru takes Rem's hand to show her where the path will open toward their doors. This time Rem does not have to spend the whole movement wondering what her own response means.

She holds on.

Ram walks beside them, listening to the proposed shelf for the essential equipment and deciding which parts she can refuse with a straight face.

Inside, Seika has left the drawing open on the table.

Ram puts her cup down at the other side of it.
