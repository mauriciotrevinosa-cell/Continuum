* * *

Mau waits with Rimuru where the wheels can turn.

Kurobe climbs down and looks toward the frame which has risen since he left. He studies the first joint Geld points out before either of them says hello properly.

Mau smiles and lets the reunion happen in the language they chose.

The twelve workers hear Geld tell them where the camp is, then ask their own questions about it. They have brought what they were told to bring. There is somewhere to put it before anyone asks what else they can do.

Rimuru goes down the line of carts, welcoming people without trying to make their arrival into the beginning of a working shift.
