*When: Marin asks Yuka what Mau will notice first, Yuka says, “That I'm there.”*

Mai looks up.

Then away, very quickly.

Maki sees her do it and smiles.

Marin leaves the answer on the page instead of trying to improve it.

“Then let's make the rest something he can notice after.” ᴹᵃ

* * *

The sketches have begun to resemble one dress rather than several arguments.

Wakana has kept the movement Yuka liked. The sleeves no longer seem to belong to a person who plans to spend the whole evening standing perfectly still. Marin asks her to turn while holding a piece of trial fabric, and Maki points out the place it will catch if she reaches for Mau's hand.

Yuka stops.

“He said that.” ʸ

“He's spent enough time being grabbed by you to learn something,” Mai says. ᴹᵃⁱ

Yuka throws the smallest piece of cloth at her.

Mai catches it and looks pleased to have improved the lesson.

* * *

Bocchi comes because Yuka asks her to.

She arrives with Nijika, expecting a question about where the band will fit. Instead, Yuka shows her the route from the homes into the place beneath the tree.

“Would you want to help us choose what we hear?” she asks. ʸ

Bocchi looks at the page.

“Us playing?” ᴮ

“If you want. It can also be something you choose, or something we arrange together. I wanted it to sound like people we know.” ʸ

Nijika waits while Bocchi reads the shape of the request again.

“I could try something,” Bocchi says. ᴮ

Yuka smiles.

“I'd like to hear it when you're ready.” ʸ

No date is put beside the promise. Bocchi leaves with a place to begin and enough room to return if the first thing she makes is not the one she wants.

* * *

Ori asks whether the lights can be low enough to see the branches.

Wakana turns the drawing so she can point.

She marks the view from the common room, where the tree becomes a shape in front of a lit window. They discuss hanging nothing heavy over the roots, keeping the paths clear and using the actual new doors as the places where friends can join them.

It begins to look less like decorating a tree and more like letting the whole home participate in one short walk.

Yuka remembers the mountain.

How much Mau had prepared before she understood what he was asking. The way the people she loved had been there without turning the moment into a performance she could fail.

“I want him to recognise it,” she says. ʸ

“The mountain?” Maki asks. ᴹᵏ

“That feeling.” ʸ

Maki looks at the page for a moment.

Then moves one line so the people joining them will be beside the walk rather than at its end.

“Then we start here.” ᴹᵏ

* * *

Outside, Beru finally makes a hole in a piece of scrap wood without breaking the rest of it.

He stares at the result.

Igris leans closer.

Bellion asks whether it is meant to be there.

“Yes,” Beru says, with enormous dignity. ᴮ

Wakana returns in time to find all three of them looking at it as though it has survived a battle.

He praises the steadiness of the work, then asks if they would like to learn the next part.

Beru puts the scrap down with both hands.

Inside, Mau is helping Taihei decide how much food can be cut before someone admits the bowl is too small.

He hears none of it.
