*When: Mau comes back past the store, the mouse is still beneath the same board.*

He has returned the cloth to Wakana without catching it on anything.

That feels like enough success for one errand. He is ready to go inside and ask Yuka whether she has another small, supposedly easy task when the movement beneath the store catches his attention again.

The mouse is facing the common room.

Not the dropped grain near the step. Not the place where the workers have just moved a stack and exposed something worth investigating. Its nose turns toward a voice, then settles.

Mau slows without stopping.

*Ciel. Under the store.* ᴹ

*I see it.* ᶜⁱ

He waits for the obvious explanation.

For a moment, neither of them has one.

* * *

Rimuru is at the table with the next part of the material list.

Mau takes the empty place beside him and puts down the cup somebody has given him on the way through.

*Don't look toward the store yet,* he says through the connection. ᴹ

The slime keeps his attention on the page.

*What is it?* ᴿ

*Something may be watching the room through an animal. I don't know who. I don't even know how much of what I'm seeing belongs to it.* ᴹ

Rimuru turns the page.

Aloud, he asks whether the shorter covered route will make a difference to deliveries.

Mau answers the ordinary question while Ciel compares the faint foreign activity around the creature with the rest of the movement beneath the floor.

It comes and goes.

It is not the same as learning what the mouse thinks.

It is enough to make him want somebody who knows more about that distinction.

* * *

Ram arrives with something Rem asked her to put on the table.

Rimuru invites her into the quiet exchange before asking the real question. She looks at him, then Mau, and accepts without making the movement into a performance for the room.

*Keep doing what you're doing,* Mau says. *I don't want to make a frightened house out of one strange animal.* ᴹ

Ram sets down the object.

*Then tell me what is strange.* ᴿᵃ

He does.

Her expression does not change while he explains where it was, what it ignored and the impression of a connection which seems to wake when a voice gets louder.

*There are ways of looking through animals,* she answers. *That doesn't mean every animal has someone behind it. Or that I can ask one who its visitor is.* ᴿᵃ

*Can you watch where it goes without touching that connection?* ᴿ

She thinks about it.

*From somewhere else. Possibly. I would rather do that than reach into the thing we're trying not to warn.* ᴿᵃ

Mau lets his hand settle around the cup.

*Thank you.* ᴹ

* * *

He remembers another army.

Another version of the ground outside a house he has kept in his mind even though this one never had to stand through that day. A memory changed from outside. The sickening difference between what someone has lived and what they have been allowed to keep.

He does not let the memory decide the name of the person beneath the board.

Makima has used information they did not mean to give her. Others might want it too. One mouse cannot confess to all of them because Mau is afraid of what one of them can do.

*I'm thinking about the old line,* he tells Rimuru. ᴹ

*I know.* ᴿ

*That doesn't prove this is the same thing.* ᴹ

*No. We look at this one.* ᴿ

Ram listens without being given the private archive which made the words heavy.

* * *

The mouse leaves when the room becomes quieter.

Mau does not follow it out of his chair.

He finishes the conversation about deliveries, hears Geld's answer and takes the emptied cups where they need to go. Ram goes outside by the route she would reasonably use at this time of day.

Yuka finds him coming back.

“Everything all right?” she asks. ʸ

He looks at her, then offers the quiet connection rather than answering a sensitive question aloud in the middle of the room.

She accepts.

*Something small we need to check. Rimuru and Ram are helping. I don't know enough to tell you a name yet.* ᴹ

Her hand closes over his.

*Then tell me the part you do know.* ʸ

He does, while the house goes on sounding like a house.
