*When: the rain reaches the courtyard again, nobody has to move the drawing.*

The new roof holds above it.

Geld stands at the corner which leaked two days ago and waits. One of the workers waits with him. The water falls where they intended it to fall this time, away from the path which will stay in use while the next walls rise.

They look at one another.

Then go back to work.

Mau watches from the dry stretch beside the common room, where Yuka has brought a chair she says is not an invitation for him to supervise everybody.

He takes it as an invitation to sit beside her.

That seems to satisfy her.

* * *

Ram has an answer by morning, though it is not the answer they would prefer.

She has followed the little route from another vantage, careful not to use the suspicious connection itself. The animal went beyond the store and out toward the edge of the known ground. There are places there where her view could not remain with it.

No waiting person became visible.

No convenient name was spoken into the leaves.

*It may be only part of the way the observation travels,* she tells Mau and Rimuru. *Don't build the whole explanation from the last place I could see.* ᴿᵃ

Mau keeps his eyes on the sketch in front of them.

*Can you show me the place?* ᴹ

*Yes. Showing you where I lost it is all I can promise.* ᴿᵃ

*That's useful.* ᴹ

She looks at him for a moment, then puts her finger on the ordinary map they are pretending to discuss.

* * *

They make the small changes they can justify without accusing every person who came to help them build.

Sensitive conversations can wait for a private place or a chosen connection. The people responsible for visits and the stores are told to watch for repeated odd behaviour and report it without deciding every animal needs killing. Qifrey and Olruggio keep the teaching pages where they already agreed to keep them.

The work goes on.

Mau dislikes how ordinary that has to look.

He also knows that stopping every meal to stare into every shadow would give the watcher something terrible for free.

*I'm not going to let a mouse decide whether we have a home today,* he tells Yuka. ᴹ

She follows his gaze toward the Fort's door.

*Good. We worked hard on the door.* ʸ

He laughs quietly enough that nobody has to ask why.

* * *

Tomorrow he will inspect the point beyond the known protected ground.

Rimuru knows where. Ram has shown him what she could see. He will tell them before going and return within the part of the morning they agree, or send word when something changes. They are looking for a route, not promising to find an enemy conveniently standing at the end of it.

Yuka hears the plan before it becomes a departure.

“No heroic wandering,” she says when they are alone. ʸ

“A very short, unheroic inspection.” ᴹ

“You say that like you know how.” ʸ

“I'm practising.” ᴹ

She studies him, then rests her forehead against his.

“Come back for lunch.” ʸ

“That's the plan.” ᴹ

* * *

The afternoon gives them enough dry weather to see the first family walls from both sides.

They are still unfinished. There are gaps where windows will go, marks where cupboards will need to fit and stairs which nobody is allowed to use merely because they look almost complete. Shuna walks the space she chose and asks where the light will reach her work in the morning.

Shion stands in her own proposed room for a long time before calling anyone in to look.

Beretta asks Geld a question Ramiris has forgotten to ask because she is arguing with Umaru about whether the word headquarters acknowledges the Fort's older claim to importance.

Rimuru ends the dispute with a promise of sweets so precisely judged that both sides decide they have won.

Mau and Yuka go back beneath the new roof before the next shower reaches it.

* * *

At supper, Rem calls once.

Ori appears in the Fort's doorway with Chloe beside her. Kenya follows. Ryota and Gale bring the thing they were using, then put it down where somebody will not step on it. Alice asks whether they can finish after eating.

Frieren tells her yes.

The answer carries through the open doorway without anybody having to send a message across the tree.

Umaru looks toward Geld and gives a satisfied nod.

He returns it with the same seriousness he used when she first led him through the route.

Mau feels Yuka's knee touch his beneath the table.

They have not finished building.

They have already begun living in the part they wanted to keep.
