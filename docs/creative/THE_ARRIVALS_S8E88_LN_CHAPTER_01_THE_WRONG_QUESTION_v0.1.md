*When: Mau passes the last familiar marker, the ground behind him remains exactly where it was.*

He looks back once.

The house is hidden from here. He knows the direction of it, the route he took and the part of the morning Rimuru has written beside the place Ram could no longer follow. He has told Yuka before leaving. Ciel is with him, and the new material route in his arm has remained quiet through the walk.

For once, every practical part of a short inspection has survived becoming an actual departure.

He would like the rest to stay that way.

There is a track between two low roots. Too little by itself to mean anything. He crouches without touching it, looking for the next place a small animal could have gone.

The light changes before he finds it.

* * *

“We would speak with you.” ᴸ¹

Mau stands.

Three figures are waiting beyond the trees. He did not see them arrive. The space around the nearest one has the strange clarity of something which makes the ordinary morning look unfinished by comparison.

*Rimuru. Something's here. They want to talk.* ᴹ

He sends the impression Ciel has already fixed around their location, not a guess at a name.

The figure raises one hand.

“Then ask from there,” Mau says. ᴹ

The answer is already too late to keep the ground beneath his feet.

For an instant the trees are a shape moving past him. His body sets for a landing before he knows whether he is falling. The space catches him in another clearing, near enough that the air has the same smell and far enough that the old route has disappeared behind a fold of light.

Mau lands on his feet.

His right hand closes slowly.

*Still here,* Ciel says. *The transfer was local. I am telling Rimuru where you are.* ᶜⁱ

He lets one breath go before deciding what to do with the next.

* * *

“You didn't ask to move me,” he says. ᴹ

“We asked to speak away from the settlement.” ᴸ¹

“I was already away from it.” ᴹ

The three look at him. Two stand closer together. The third remains apart, watching the shape of Mau's shadow rather than his face.

He can feel how much that third figure dislikes what it sees.

“False Shadow Monarch,” it says.

Mau looks down briefly.

Then back up.

“That's a very long way to begin a conversation before giving me your names.” ᴹ

The nearer of the other two speaks. They identify themselves by the authority they now exercise over the arrivals, the interventions they can command and the part of the current control they claim to answer for. The word Rulers comes with enough weight that Mau no longer has to guess why the morning feels different around them.

He listens until the first has finished.

Then asks the question which has brought him out here in the first place.

“Why were you using the mice?” ᴹ

* * *

The nearest figure goes completely still.

The one studying his shadow finally looks at his face.

“What fucking mice?” ᴸ³

Mau blinks.

Inside, Ciel is very quiet for one memorable instant.

“The animals watching the house,” he says. ᴹ

“We have no need to look at you through vermin.” ᴸ³

He studies the answer instead of accepting the contempt as proof.

“Then I may have two conversations to finish.” ᴹ

The Ruler who first addressed him looks toward the other two.

“Apparently.” ᴸ¹

Mau almost laughs.

The relief in it is too small to mistake for safety and too real to suppress merely because the person across from him has made a terrible first impression.

*Rimuru,* he says privately. *The mouse problem may be separate.* ᴹ

*How separate?* ᴿ

*Their answer was fairly emphatic.* ᴹ

* * *

He does not call the whole army out.

The option remains with him. So does the awareness that making this clearing into a battle will not answer why these people waited beyond the protected ground rather than approaching the door.

“I want to go home when we've finished,” he says. ᴹ

“If you answer what we came to ask.” ᴸ¹

“You'll get better answers if that isn't a threat.” ᴹ

The first Ruler looks at him for a long moment.

Then lowers its hand.

The fold behind him opens far enough for the trees of the ordinary route to become visible again.

“You may return. We have not come to begin a war at that boundary.” ᴸ¹

Mau glances toward the way out, lets Ciel note it, then returns his attention to them.

“Good. Ask.” ᴹ

* * *

The first question is about Ashborn.

Mau hears the name and feels the quiet inside him deepen without turning into a command. The guest has not stepped into his mouth. The body remains his to stand in, and the answer remains his to choose.

“I know him,” he says. ᴹ

“You carry more than acquaintance.” ᴸ¹

Mau watches the light around the speaker.

Noise is watching too. Not touching the people across from him, not building anything in the arm Ciel has spent days keeping stable. Following the way the light holds itself together, the difference between a power named in an old memory and a working piece of the world directly in front of it.

Mau can feel the attention becoming enormous.

*Keep it as drawings,* he tells it. ᴹ

*Drawings,* Noise answers. ᴺ

The third Ruler's gaze sharpens.

“And something else is looking back.” ᴸ³

Mau keeps his hands where they can see them.

“It looks at things. That doesn't mean I'm attacking you.” ᴹ

“Does it mean you understand what you are?” ᴸ³

He thinks of how many people have asked a version of that question while standing close enough to decide they would rather use him than wait for the answer.

“No,” he says. “Does it mean you do?” ᴹ
