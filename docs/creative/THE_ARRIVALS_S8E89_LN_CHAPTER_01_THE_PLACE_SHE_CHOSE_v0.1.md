He turns back.

“Yes. If you want it.” ᴿ

She waits until he has put his attention properly beside her.

“I do,” she says. ˢᶻ

Yuka looks up from the other side of the table.

For a moment, the piece of paper between them stops being the place they put unfinished questions. It becomes the shape of a choice somebody has made.

“Then it stays yours,” she says. ʸ

* * *

Rimuru has heard from Ciel that Mau is still talking.

He knows where he is. The way back has been made visible. Mau has not asked the house to come out fighting, and Rimuru is resisting the temptation to make the absence into something louder than the message he actually received.

Yuka knows the same part.

She has one hand around her ring while she speaks about the third bedroom.

Shizue sees it.

“We can do this later,” she says. ˢᶻ

Yuka looks at her hand and lets it settle on the page.

“I'd rather know what we're coming home to.” ʸ

Rimuru is quiet for a moment.

Then asks Shizue which side of the room she wants the small desk on.

* * *

She wants a place to write to people without having to move her bedding first.

A shelf for the things the children make. A door which will close without cutting her off from the ordinary sound of the sitting room. She asks whether she can have her own way to the shared passage when she wants to go out early, rather than disturbing Mau and Yuka's morning.

Geld has already left the three bedrooms opening from the sitting space, with the apartment's shared door beyond them. Yuka shows her the route.

“We'll hear each other sometimes,” she says. ʸ

Shizue smiles.

“I'd like that. I'd also like to be able to ask for quiet.” ˢᶻ

“Please do,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

The answer makes her look at him a little longer.

He does not try to decide what the look means before she has.

* * *

They take the choice outside to Geld.

The home still has no finished floor to move into. That does not stop him asking useful questions. The desk should have enough light. The door should not swing into the place Rimuru wants to sit. The kitchen corner needs to work for people who may make themselves something small instead of joining the common meal.

Yuka asks about the space they have left for their own future.

Geld shows her what can be changed later without moving the main supports.

Shizue watches her listen.

“You don't need to make my room the answer to every future problem,” she says. ˢᶻ

Yuka turns toward her.

“We weren't going to.” ʸ

The firmness makes Shizue smile again.

Rimuru realises he has been waiting for a difficult feeling which has not arrived.

He is happy she wants to stay.

He lets himself begin there.

* * *

Inside, the children have found a way to turn a discussion of shelves into a debate about whose object should be the first to go on Shizue's.

She sits with them long enough to hear all five claims, then tells them a shelf can hold more than one thing.

Alice appears satisfied.

Kenya asks whether there is room for something larger.

Chloe watches Shizue laugh before joining the argument.

Yuka looks toward the path again.

Rimuru receives a short message, turns toward her and nods.

Still talking. Still able to come back.

She sits down beside the children while they wait.
