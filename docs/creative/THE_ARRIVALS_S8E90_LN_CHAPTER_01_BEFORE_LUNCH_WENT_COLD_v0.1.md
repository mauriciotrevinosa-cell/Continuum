*When: Mau reaches the courtyard, Yuka looks at his hands before looking at his face.*

“Nothing new,” he says. ᴹ

She comes close enough to check the answer for herself.

His right wrist has the same faint line it had when he left. His coat is dusty from the path rather than torn. The two familiar rhythms inside him are steady enough that Ciel has not asked for Shoko at the door.

Yuka puts her arms around him.

He holds her carefully for a moment, then more comfortably when she presses closer.

“You said short,” she murmurs. ʸ

“The inspection was very short.” ᴹ

“Mau.” ʸ

“The conversation had several difficult parts.” ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru has saved the place beside him.

The first question is whether the three who moved Mau knew about the watcher. He gives them the exact answer, with just enough of the voice to make Ram look up from her cup.

Gojo laughs first.

“Divine diplomacy.” ᴳ

“Apparently I had the wrong department.” ᴹ

Ram asks whether that is evidence the problem has gone away.

“No,” Mau answers. “It's evidence I asked somebody who objected to the method more than I expected.” ᴹ

Rimuru nods.

They leave the investigation where it belongs: unfinished, with a route to examine more carefully and a house which should keep its practical precautions.

Nobody begins congratulating themselves on having caught Makima merely because a powerful stranger denied owning a mouse.

* * *

He tells the people who want to hear the rest what he actually agreed.

DIO does not want to retake control. She will help the current controllers understand how to make the forced additions less destructive. The safety within the fixed protected ground remains distinct from travelling inside the refuge he offered. She cannot step out in her own body beyond the boundary and assume the same promise follows her.

Qifrey asks who has given the promise.

Mau answers that too.

Jinshi asks what they expect in return from the house.

“Not our family list,” Mau says. “Not the ink. Not a throne.” ᴹ

“Good,” Jinshi answers. ᴶ

“They called me false.” ᴹ

Gojo's face brightens.

“A new title.” ᴳ

Mau looks at him.

“I'm trying not to collect them.” ᴹ

“You're very bad at that.” ᴳ

Yuka moves Mau's bowl closer before the exchange can become another reason for him to forget it.

* * *

The distinction about the coins matters enough that Fern asks him to say it again.

He does, carefully: the invitation and its possible acceptance or refusal already belong to the balance. Whole regions arriving too quickly are another kind of event. Nobody has promised every person on their search page will come. Nobody has required them to give up the search because someone else has been moving land badly.

Bocchi listens from beside Nijika.

Richeh looks down at the reply she is carrying.

Qifrey reminds them that tomorrow's shore meeting is still arranged, with Olruggio and Kaisel's help. Mau asks whether they have everything they need to leave at the planned time.

Richeh answers him before either teacher can.

“Yes.” ᴿⁱ

He smiles.

“Good.” ᴹ

* * *

After lunch, Shoko gets the ordinary examination she says she would like to have after any conversation which involved the ground moving without its owner's permission.

The arm has remained stable. Mau tells her where the residual oddness is and how little it changed on the walk. She asks Ciel's account, hears it through him and lets him return to the people waiting without finding a new injury hidden behind the good news.

“That was a sensible use of your morning,” she says. ˢᵒ

“Which part?” ᴹ

“Telling someone where you were.” ˢᵒ

Then nods.

Yuka walks him through the unfinished gallery before the evening meal.

There are places where a wall is now solid enough to cast the shadow she has been imagining. There are still gaps she makes him step around instead of letting him test whether they can be ignored.

“The wedding is going to be here,” she says, indicating the route. ʸ

“I remember.” ᴹ

“You have to be here too.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

“I don't mean after making the entire world behave itself.” ʸ

“Show up. We'll deal with the rest with people. Not as the thing you have to finish before you're allowed to marry me.” ʸ
