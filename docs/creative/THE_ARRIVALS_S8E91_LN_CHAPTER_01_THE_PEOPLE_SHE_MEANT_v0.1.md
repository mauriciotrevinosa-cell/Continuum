* * *

His first longer conversation is with Qifrey and Olruggio about the visit.

He would like to stay through the next few days, if that remains comfortable for the people sharing the temporary space. He writes a short message for those waiting to hear from him on the coast. It goes with the announced afternoon letters through the route Hollis has been keeping open. They will review the visit on the third morning rather than allowing it to become a permanent move by accident.

Richeh listens.

She is happier with a day they have named than with a promise no one has thought about keeping.

Olruggio shows him where the travelling mat can go and where the teaching work must remain clear. Rili moves his bag away from the papers before being asked.

Qifrey smiles.

* * *

Later, Richeh takes him to see the work again.

This time there are friends around it. Tetia has something to add to the explanation. Agott corrects the part which belongs to a different attempt. Coco asks Rili a question which makes him look at the piece in his hand with renewed care.

He answers as someone who likes the work, not as someone inspecting how much of his sister's worth it proves.

She watches him pass it back to Coco.

For months she has imagined telling him that she has people here. The sentence always sounded too small beside how much she wanted him to understand.

Now she can let the table tell part of it.

* * *

At dinner she sits between her brother and Tetia.

He asks where the new room is supposed to go. She points him toward the unfinished stretch beyond the common space and explains which route leads back to the girls.

“You like it?” he asks. ᴿˡ

“Yes.” ᴿⁱ

“Then show me tomorrow.” ᴿˡ

She turns toward him.

The word tomorrow has become a small thing again.

She likes it much better that way.
