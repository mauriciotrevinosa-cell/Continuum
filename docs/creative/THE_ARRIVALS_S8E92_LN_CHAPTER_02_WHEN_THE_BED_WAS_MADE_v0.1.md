* * *

They talk before going downstairs.

Two beds here. Two private bedrooms in the home they have chosen on the drawing. Either of them can ask for a change before it becomes something difficult to say merely because the other has got used to the arrangement.

It is a smaller conversation than the first one and, to Gojo's surprise, a more serious one.

Shoko is still looking at him when he finishes putting the blanket down.

“What?” he asks. ᴳ

“You made it.” ˢᵒ

He looks at the bed.

“I've been doing that.” ᴳ

“I noticed.” ˢᵒ

For once, he lets himself enjoy being praised for something which cannot impress a room full of people.

She steps closer.

He has time to see the question in the movement and answer it by staying.

The kiss is brief. Her hand remains against his shoulder after it.

“Good morning,” she says. ˢᵒ

Gojo's smile returns slowly enough that she gets to see all of it.

Rimuru fails at the same courtesy.

Gojo looks at the slime.

“What?” ᴳ

“Nothing.” ᴿ
