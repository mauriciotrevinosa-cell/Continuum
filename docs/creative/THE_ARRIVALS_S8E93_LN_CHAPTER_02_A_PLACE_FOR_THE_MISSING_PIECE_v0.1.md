*When: Noise shows him the empty place, Mau understands why it has been so interested in the light.*

The drawing is not a complete answer.

It is a relationship between the bounded reference Ashborn has chosen to share and the structure Noise observed in the clearing outside. There is a place where the two explain something neither could explain alone.

Ciel holds it where Mau can examine the shape without trying to put it into his body.

“You think you can build that,” he says. ᴹ

Noise waits.

Then answers with unusual care.

“Think.” ᴺ

* * *

He asks what it would change.

They can name possibilities before they can promise results. The limit imposed by the system might cease to describe what the body can become. The incomplete inheritance might finally have the missing relation which kept it from functioning as a whole.

None of that tells them what Mau can safely carry on the first day.

He remembers winning fights by surviving damage he would never choose to call a stable level of power.

“Not because the number says I survived before,” he says. ᴹ

“No,” Ciel answers. ᶜⁱ

They leave the drawing a drawing.

No shard appears in his palm. Neither heart changes its rhythm. The familiar interface is still as inadequate in some places and as useful in others as it was when he woke.

Mau tells Noise to keep the work for the next conversation about scope and the body.

It does.

* * *

Rimuru has another unfinished answer waiting outside.

Ram has helped them watch the route again. One ordinary trap, placed where the animal can enter without being harmed, has caught the creature they have been following. The foreign activity ceased after the box closed.

That is the important part.

It is also the part which denies them the convenient chance to follow a live connection back to a name.

Mau looks at the small breathing creature.

“Don't hurt it,” he says. ᴹ

Ram gives him a dry look.

“That was not the plan.” ᴿᵃ

“I know.” ᴹ

They record what they actually saw. A pattern, a route, the point where something stopped. No confession. No face. No reason to cancel the sensible precautions merely because the most obvious listener is no longer beneath the floor.

* * *

The house has begun asking about moving days.

Some homes will be ready before others. Geld wants the stairs, water, doors and lights checked before enthusiasm puts beds in the way of work still needing to be done. The first finished sleeping spaces will make the rest easier by allowing old rooms to be changed without displacing their occupants into a corridor.

Mau gives his opinion where it helps.

Then stops talking when Touka points out something about laundry which neither of the men beside him had considered.

Yuka catches his eye across the table.

He smiles.

It is a very ordinary way to discover the world is larger than the next thing he might learn to carry.
