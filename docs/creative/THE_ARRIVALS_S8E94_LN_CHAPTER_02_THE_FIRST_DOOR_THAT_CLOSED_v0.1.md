*When: Hinata comes down with her bag, Anko is already outside.*

The road back to Tempest will take the same ordinary part of two days it took to bring her here. There are things waiting for her which she has not decided to abandon because the last week has given her reasons to stay.

The children know who will be with them. Rimuru has spoken through the arrangements with Shizue, Frieren and the people who have chosen their parts. Hinata has made sure they are real enough to leave in place.

Now Anko takes the bag long enough to check the strap, gives it back and looks at her.

“You can come back without bringing a reason in a cart.” ᴬⁿ

Hinata smiles.

“I was hoping.” ᴴⁱ

Anko kisses her before the answer can become too dignified to enjoy.

They stand together for a moment afterwards, then walk to the beginning of the road with Rimuru.

Nobody tells the house a door has closed merely because someone they love has gone out of it.

* * *

Rili has the page from his first visit arrangement beside him at breakfast.

It says they will ask again this morning.

Richeh sits with him rather than waiting somewhere she can pretend not to be waiting. Qifrey and Olruggio join them when he asks. Coco's mother brings an extra cup, then goes to find her own breakfast.

“I'd like to stay,” Rili says. ᴿˡ

His sister looks up.

He gives her time before continuing.

“As a home. Not only until I've seen what you meant in the letter.” ᴿˡ

She keeps her hands on the table.

“You don't have to because I'm here.” ᴿⁱ

“I know.” ᴿˡ

“I want you here.” ᴿⁱ

He smiles.

“I hoped so.” ᴿˡ

* * *

They discuss the practical parts after the choice has become his.

He has people to inform and a few things to have brought from the place he was staying. The first message is already travelling through the route which carried their earlier letters. He writes a second with the change clearly stated, rather than allowing his continued absence to become the only answer somebody gets.

The family space left beside Richeh's home can take his private room without moving her away from the girls. Geld checks the existing module before agreeing, then names what still has to be finished. Until then, Rili can keep the travelling space he has been using, with a room in the small guest inn available when that part is ready.

“You can still go back to visit,” Qifrey tells him. ᵠ

“I'd like to.” ᴿˡ

Richeh looks between them.

The choice has not become smaller because it still has a way out.

She takes her brother outside to show him which window she has been asking for.

* * *

The first home to pass its last inspection belongs to Gojo and Shoko.

Geld makes them walk the route themselves. The stair is secure. Water reaches the place it should. The door opens far enough to let a person carry something without turning the sitting room into an obstacle course. Both private bedrooms close.

Shoko tests hers twice.

Gojo waits rather than making a joke before she has decided what she thinks.

“Good?” he asks. ᴳ

She looks toward the window, then at the little shared room beyond the door.

“Good.” ˢᵒ

He goes to look at his own.

* * *

Mau and Yuka come when they are invited.

The place is not grand. It has enough room for the lives its two people have described, with a table where neither has to pretend a cup is being set down in somebody else's territory.

Gojo notices Mau looking at it.

“Very good table,” he says. ᴳ

Mau glances toward Shoko.

She is smiling.

“When?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Tomorrow,” Shoko answers. ˢᵒ

Gojo looks out along the gallery, toward the old house where their two beds are still waiting.

“Tomorrow,” he repeats. ᴳ

* * *

They leave the new door closed when they go back to supper.

It is the first one which can wait safely for its owners without someone standing behind it to hold up the rest of the room.

Mau turns once to look at it from the path.

Yuka catches his hand.

“Ours will do that too.” ʸ

He looks toward the unfinished place she means.

Then back at the door which has already learnt how to be a home.

“Yes,” he says. ᴹ
