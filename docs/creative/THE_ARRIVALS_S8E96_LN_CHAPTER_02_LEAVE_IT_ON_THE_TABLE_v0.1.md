Qifrey looks toward him.

“This one stays a work table,” Olruggio says. ᴼˡ

For a moment, his friend seems to have forgotten that was part of the reason they asked for the room.

“I'd like that.” ᵠ

* * *

The store is the first thing they put in order.

Closed bottles. Clear labels. A way to get at the finished fittings without opening the part which contains the materials for making them. Coco's mother knows enough now to ask about the shelf before someone puts a bottle where a sleeve will catch it.

Olruggio changes the shelf.

Qifrey gives her the key she has agreed to use and tells her which questions he still wants her to bring to them before trying something alone.

She accepts both parts.

“And if I don't want to learn the next thing?” she asks. ᶜᵐ

“Then we don't make it your job,” he answers. ᵠ

Coco, placing her own work on the lower table, smiles without looking up.

* * *

Richeh's room has the window she showed her brother.

His own is reached without going through it. The girls are still along the same short route. She can leave him to arrange his things and go to ask Agott a question without feeling she has exchanged one part of her life for another.

Rili puts the small piece she brought to the shore where he can see it.

“You kept that one out,” she says. ᴿⁱ

“I like it.” ᴿˡ

She looks toward the space he has left beside it.

“I have others.” ᴿⁱ

“I hoped you might.” ᴿˡ

Tetia calls from the passage before the answer can become anything more elaborate.

Richeh goes to find out what she needs.

Her brother stays to put the rest of his things away.

* * *

Mau is helping move the last light box when Mai asks for his attention through the arrangement they have kept since before the proposal.

One small fitting. She has said what she means to make before making it, although he has told her often enough that she does not have to announce every modest use as though he will collapse if she forgets.

He answers anyway.

“A hinge?” ᴹ

“A good hinge.” ᴹᵃⁱ

“I approve of good hinges.” ᴹ

She ignores the joke and checks how he feels after the familiar cost crosses to him. His reserves and body take the small work without the old damage it would have demanded from hers. The recent ink recovery has made her more careful about choosing the scale, not less.

“Fine,” he tells her. “Still fine.” ᴹ

“Then I'll tell you before the next one.” ᴹᵃⁱ

“You don't have—” ᴹ

She has already turned toward the fitting.

Yuka laughs behind him.

* * *

Maki and Yuta bring a box past the teachers' new door on their way to the home they are settling into near Yuka's future one.

Tsurugi carries the awkward part rather than admitting he could use help with the corner. Mai tells him exactly where he is going to hit it. He changes his grip a fraction before reaching the point she means.

Mau watches the whole family disappear along the short gallery Yuka walked twice before agreeing to it.

She catches him looking.

“Close enough?” he asks. ᴹ

“Yes.” ʸ

Her answer has the ease of something the ground has finally proved.

* * *

At the end of the afternoon, Olruggio leaves a difficult piece of work unfinished on the table.

Qifrey looks at it while reaching for his coat.

Then leaves his own page beside it.

Both private bedroom doors are waiting. The common meal is a short walk away. Nobody needs to put a cup over the wet corner merely to claim somewhere to eat.

Olruggio looks back once before closing the workroom.

“Tomorrow,” he says. ᴼˡ

Qifrey smiles.

They go to dinner.
