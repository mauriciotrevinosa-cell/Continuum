*When: Shuna sets the unfinished cloth beside her window, nobody asks when it will be ready for them.*

She looks at the light on it for a while.

Then moves it a little farther from the edge and goes to open the box she packed for herself.

Shion is in the next part of the gallery, trying to decide what belongs on the shelf where she had expected to put work. Rimuru finds her holding two things and looking faintly betrayed by the amount of choice the room permits.

“Either,” he says. ᴿ

“They won't both fit.” ˢʰⁱ

“Then change them when you want the other one.” ᴿ

She looks at him.

The answer is so ordinary that it takes her a moment to recognise how much permission she had been waiting to hear in it.

* * *

Ramiris has arranged her space twice before Beretta has finished unpacking once.

He lets her do it, then asks where the messages about the labyrinth are meant to go when she is busy objecting to somebody else's arrangement.

She points.

He puts the record there.

The labyrinth has remained in Tempest. People there know which work is theirs to continue and which questions must come back to her. Her visits and the means of carrying those questions have not disappeared because she has acquired a room she likes better. Beretta has kept his own part of the agreement beside hers, where she cannot generously offer him for something else and forget he already has a plan.

“This is very demanding,” she says. ᴿᵃᵐ

“You wanted to live in more than one place without abandoning either.” ᴮᵉ

She looks around her room.

“I did.” ᴿᵃᵐ

He lets the answer be the end of the argument.

* * *

Ranga chooses his resting place by lying down in it.

Mau is nearby with Ear when it happens. The wolf has a covered space close to the route he wants, room to move and people who know his presence does not make him an object to be kept at a post.

Ear approaches, considers the occupied patch and settles somewhere else with great deliberation.

Ranga looks at him.

“An agreeable neighbour.” ᴿᵍ

Mau has to hide a smile when Ear changes position so that he can still see the doorway.

“He's very particular.” ᴹ

“So am I.” ᴿᵍ

They appear to understand one another.

* * *

The children take longer.

They have liked the temporary nights in the Fort enough that moving into rooms of their own requires reassurance that the game is not being moved away with the bedding. Umaru walks the route with them, proving it with her own feet. Ori opens the direct door into the common space and waits while Chloe follows.

The new sleeping rooms have private places for things and a shared study space which does not need to be cleared for supper. Kenya tests where a bag can go before Gale points out somebody will walk into it. Ryota checks the lamp. Alice chooses the place for the object Shizue said could go on her shelf when she visits.

Chloe stands at her bed and looks toward the familiar route out.

“We can still come over?” she asks Frieren. ᶜʰ

“Yes.” ᶠ

Ori takes her hand.

The answer has somewhere to go.

* * *

Frieren's books are the last part of her own move.

Ori's bed is settled, the route to the Fort tried several times, and Milim has chosen a nearby room from which she can reach both her friends and Rimuru without treating anyone's roof as a shortcut.

Fern helps with the books until Stark asks whether she has unpacked her own things.

She stops.

Frieren looks up from the volume in her hands.

“We can finish these later.” ᶠ

Fern looks between them, then lets Stark take the small box she was supposed to be carrying home.

Himmel brings the last stack after they have gone.

Frieren thanks him and asks him to put it where she can reach it from the chair.

He stays long enough to ask about one title.

She begins explaining.

* * *

That evening, the Fort is full of people who no longer have beds blocking its next campaign.

The children run the short routes between the rooms and the games as though testing whether the promise will remain true on the third attempt. Rem calls them to eat. They arrive by the door they already know.

Mau sits with Yuka and watches the places beside them fill.

“Yes.” ᴹ
