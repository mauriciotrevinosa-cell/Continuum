*When: Ichika asks whether the little room is hers, Kaneki crouches so he can see the doorway from her height.*

“Yes.” ᴷᵃ

She goes in.

Comes back out.

Goes in again, this time carrying the thing she wants to put beside the bed.

Touka watches from the sitting room with a hand against the doorframe. Mau brings the box she asked for and leaves it where she points. The family home has a quiet sleeping place and a way for Ichika to reach the people she wants without passing through a workshop first.

“She's testing it,” Mau says. ᴹ

Touka looks toward the child appearing in the doorway again.

“So are we.” ᵀᵒ

* * *

Eren has put a chair where it makes the best use of the small view.

Mikasa moves it just enough to let someone get past. He looks at the changed angle, tries it and finds the view has survived.

Armin comes to their door with a question about the shared cupboard and remains after getting the answer. His own place is close enough that coming over does not need to become the evening's only journey.

They talk about what they will do tomorrow.

The subject is so plain that Eren almost misses the moment it stops being a promise to be defended and becomes a thing they are already using.

Mikasa looks at him when he falls quiet.

“What?” ᴹⁱᵏ

“Nothing.” ᴱʳ

He asks whether she wants the chair nearer the window.

* * *

Across the next court, Jiji has found something to say about every box Momo puts down.

She gives him one to carry.

He discovers a profound respect for silence until he reaches the wrong door and Seiko tells him where it belongs.

Vamola arrives with the lighter things. Aira has opinions about which place should remain clear. Turbo Granny dismisses two of them and finds a practical reason for the third which irritates Aira more than simply being contradicted would have done.

Okarun waits until Momo has finished deciding where the last box goes.

“Want to look outside?” he asks. ᴼᵏ

She looks toward the people still capable of putting things away without her.

“Yes.” ᴹᵒ

Seiko gives her the small nod of someone who is pleased without needing to turn it into a lesson.

* * *

Mau meets them in the court while going back to the common room.

He asks whether anything is still missing.

Jiji begins an answer which Momo tells him is not a useful contribution to civilisation. Vamola points out the actual missing thing: somewhere to leave the shoes which have begun migrating into the path.

Mau looks at the doorway.

Then at the small pile.

“We've had an important meeting about those,” he says. ᴹ

Momo laughs.

“Did you lose?” ᴹᵒ

“I believe the boots lost.” ᴹ

They find a temporary place which does not block anybody, then leave the permanent fitting for the people who know where the wall can take it.

* * *

At the common room, Kaneki catches up with him.

“I can help with the next part tomorrow,” he says. ᴷᵃ

Mau looks toward the court he has just left.

“After breakfast with them?” ᴹ

Kaneki smiles.

“After breakfast.” ᴷᵃ

They walk the rest of the way together.

The old house is still noisy ahead of them. Behind them, the new doors are beginning to make their own small evening sounds.
