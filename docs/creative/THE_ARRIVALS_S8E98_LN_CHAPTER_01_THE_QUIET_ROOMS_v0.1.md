*When: Jinshi asks which plants are allowed near the window, Maomao tells him he has asked the wrong question.*

“Which ones aren't?” he tries. ᴶ

“Which window.” ᴹᵐ

He turns to look at the three possible places and begins to understand why she has not unpacked the small pots yet.

The home is close enough to the new consultation space that Maomao can reach it without receiving every visitor through their bedroom. Jinshi has a place for papers which does not require moving her work into a box at the end of each day. The space he has kept for a future arrival remains a space, with Gaoshun's name on his private page and no one pretending a bed has become occupied because they wanted it.

Maomao points him toward the better light.

He carries the first pot there.

* * *

At the next door, Shizuka is deciding which shelf her favourite books deserve.

Nano offers a very precise account of the light. Karane objects that no one wants to calculate their way to finding a story before bed. Hakari listens to both, then suggests the shelf which lets Shizuka reach the books from the place she has already chosen to sit.

Shizuka smiles and puts the first volume there.

Kusuri has kept her own room among them. The work which needs a laboratory has a laboratory. She has not agreed to sleep beside it merely because she is good at the work.

When someone asks where she can be found in the evening, she points toward her friends.

The answer is accepted without a form.

* * *

Nazuna tests the dark in her new room by closing everything the way she will want it closed when she rests.

Anko waits in the shared part until she opens the door again.

“Good?” Anko asks. ᴬⁿ

“Very.” ᴺᶻ

They have a quiet way in from the gallery, private sleeping spaces and a route toward Seika's place which does not require waking the whole court. Anko has left Hinata's last letter beside the things she wants close enough to find without unpacking everything.

Nazuna notices.

“Another inspection?” she asks. ᴺᶻ

“You had your chance to be helpful.” ᴬⁿ

“I carried a blanket.” ᴺᶻ

Anko throws her a small cushion.

She catches it and puts it precisely where she wanted one.

* * *

Seika comes by after the doors have been tried.

She knocks, comes in when invited and asks Nazuna whether the route is as short as it looked on the page. They walk it together before the afternoon becomes evening. Anko stays to put away the rest of her things without being made to feel that having a visitor is a permanent duty.

Jinshi finds Maomao still choosing between two pots when he returns from the reception space.

“That one?” he asks. ᴶ

She looks up.

“Why?” ᴹᵐ

He gives her the reason she explained this morning, accurately enough that she stops looking at the plant.

For a moment he wonders whether he has misunderstood.

Then she takes his hand.

“Come and see the other window,” she says. ᴹᵐ

He follows her with no attempt to make the smile smaller.
