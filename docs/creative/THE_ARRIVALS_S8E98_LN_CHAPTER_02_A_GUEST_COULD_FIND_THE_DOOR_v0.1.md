*When: Hollis asks which door to use, Jinshi answers without leaving his own breakfast unfinished.*

The trade visit was announced.

There is a place to stop the load, a person who has agreed to receive it and a way to carry the crates to the store without walking them past a sleeping child. Hollis follows the route they give him, looks toward the new covered path and smiles.

“You've made it easier to arrive.” ᴴᵒ

“We've tried to make it easier to know you're arriving,” Jinshi answers. ᴶ

Mau helps with one small crate while the soldiers take the loads Geld has marked for them. No one has asked a merchant to walk through a private home to find the people doing business.

* * *

The little guest inn is ready for visitors who do not have a room of their own.

Its doors close. Its beds are not somebody else's bed with their belongings pushed under it. The path to the common place is easy to explain, and a guest can also find water without asking the whole house to wake up.

Beside the clinical work, the emergency rooms have their own approach. Shoko walks it with Maomao, then asks Stark to carry an empty support through the turn as though somebody might someday need more space than the page suggested.

They change where one cabinet will stand.

The short-stay reception is separate again, near the agreed entrance. Nobody is assigned to sit there all day hoping a lost person will appear. The house has names to call when an arrival is expected and a way to ask for help when someone is not.

Jinshi looks considerably happier with that arrangement.

Maomao notices and leaves him the pleasure of it.

* * *

Senku is waiting by the service hoist when Mau reaches it.

The test load is a crate. Not a person. Byakuya repeats that distinction before Senku can invite somebody interesting to improve the demonstration.

Ropes, balance and the parts Kurobe has checked do the work they are meant to do. The crate rises, stops where it should and comes down again without being caught by a dragon or saved by somebody pretending the failure was part of the plan.

“Good,” Byakuya says. ᴮʸ

Senku looks at the next adjustment.

“Better will be—” ˢᵉⁿ

“After we write down what worked.” ᴮʸ

Mau grins and leaves them to the argument.

Their own homes are close to the people they want, with the work kept somewhere they can choose to leave it. Suika does not have to sleep beside an experiment to remain near her family.

* * *

The tea court is quieter.

Daphne has the path to the growing ground she asked for. Sekhmet has the chair which is finally out of it. Minerva can reach the common room without crossing anyone else's private space. Carmilla has put a small thing beside her window, where she wants to see it rather than where it will be least noticed.

Pride, Satella and Echidna are discussing how to arrange the shared table when Mau comes past.

They ask his opinion, reject one part of it and keep another.

He finds that an excellent reason to be invited into a home.

At the workshop court, Marin and Wakana have finished carrying their own last box away from the workroom. Marin takes his hand and leads him toward the place they will sit when nothing needs to be pinned or cut.

Mau lets them go without asking when the next wedding fitting will be ready.

* * *

Diablo has chosen a room close to the future apartment Rimuru will share.

It is his own room, not a post outside another person's door. When Mau looks in, the place contains a chair arranged with the clear expectation that a conversation might take place in it.

“Visitors?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“When invited,” Diablo answers. ᴰ

Mau smiles.

“Very good door policy.” ᴹ

Diablo inclines his head with the satisfaction of someone who considers the compliment deserved.

* * *

By evening, the old room below Rimuru's loft is empty of Diablo's belongings.

Rimuru looks at the cleared space, then up toward the bed he has used for so long. His own move is close enough now that he has begun noticing which things he still reaches for without looking.

Mau finds him there and waits at the door.

“Ready?” he asks. ᴹ

Rimuru looks toward him.

“Ask me after we've found where all your things go.” ᴿ

Mau laughs.

Their new door is waiting to be tested tomorrow.
