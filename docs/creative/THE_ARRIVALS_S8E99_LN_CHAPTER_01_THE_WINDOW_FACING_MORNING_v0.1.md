*When: Mau opens the window, the first thing he says is, “East.”*

Yuka looks at him.

“We did spend quite a lot of time on that.” ʸ

He smiles and leaves it open while Geld checks the catch once more. The room has been measured, built and examined, but the light crossing it still seems like something the drawing could not promise until it arrived.

Their bed will go where they chose.

The room is not the first one they have shared. They have already learnt the little negotiations of waking, finding a sleeve beneath somebody else's shoulder and asking who moved the cup. This one is different because neither of them has merely made space in the other's old life.

They asked for it together.

Now Yuka stands beside the window, with colour in the room before anything else has been unpacked.

Mau turns toward her.

“Yes,” he says. “We did.” ᴹ

* * *

The last checks belong to the whole home.

Geld has Rimuru try his own bedroom door and Shizue hers. They walk the shared room without imagining the furniture smaller than it is. The kitchen corner has water and a place to prepare something modest when the common meal is not what somebody needs. The front door opens onto the gallery, not into another person's private room.

Yuka walks the route toward her family's doors one more time.

Mau goes with her as far as the turn.

“Still close?” he asks. ᴹ

Maki appears in her own doorway before Yuka can answer.

“Close enough to hear you asking again.” ᴹᵏ

Yuka begins laughing.

The answer has been tested.

* * *

Moving their things makes the old room look unexpectedly plain.

Mau finds the page which has remained beneath the table through several attempts at being tidier. Yuka retrieves the small thing she was certain had gone into a bag already. The chair has a mark on its arm where somebody rested a cup badly enough to leave evidence.

They look at it together.

“We keep that,” she says. ʸ

“The chair or the mark?” ᴹ

“Both.” ʸ

He carries the lighter pieces while the people helping them take the bed along the route Geld checked. Nothing goes through a window because a powerful person has become impatient with a turn.

At the old door, Mau waits for Yuka.

She looks back once.

Then closes it and takes his hand.

* * *

Rimuru brings the things from his loft in smaller journeys than anyone expected.

Some are practical. Some turn out to be objects he kept without deciding they had become possessions worth moving. The lower space has already been cleared by Diablo's move. By the time Rimuru takes the last thing down from his own bed, the old room is ready to become somebody else's useful space rather than a place half-occupied by everyone who once slept in it.

Shizue carries her personal things from the borrowed room Gojo left her.

She thanks him when he comes to help with the remaining box.

“You made it easy to stay,” she says. ˢᶻ

He gives her one of the easier smiles the house has been returning to him.

“I'm glad you did.” ᴳ

The bed remains in the old house for the use the household will choose next. Her new room has its own.

* * *

They meet over the first badly placed box in the sitting room.

Rimuru has chosen the spot where it will be in everybody's way precisely because he can get around it most easily. Yuka looks down at him.

“Move.” ʸ

“Me?” ᴿ

“The box.” ʸ

Mau laughs and helps him take it to the correct room.

Shizue puts the kettle where all four can find it. Yuka hands her the key she has chosen, then asks whether she wants help with the shelf or time to arrange it herself.

“Time first,” Shizue says. ˢᶻ

“Then call us.” ʸ

Her door closes without anyone taking the sound personally.

* * *

Ori visits with Frieren before the afternoon is gone.

She inspects the sitting room, asks which door is Rimuru's and looks out of the window in Mau and Yuka's room after Yuka invites her in. Frieren waits beside the table while Mau shows her the small shelf he kept separate from the books Yuka has already claimed space for.

“Can I come over?” Ori asks. ᴼ

“You can ask,” Yuka answers. ʸ

Ori looks toward Frieren.

“Mum?” ᴼ

Frieren nods.

“As long as you tell me where you're going.” ᶠ

Ori looks pleased with the arrangement and begins explaining which thing she would like to bring first.

Mau listens from the doorway.

The new room has not changed whose mother she runs to.

It has given her another place where people are glad she has arrived.

* * *

When the visitors have gone, Yuka shows Mau the space they have deliberately left simple.

The supports allow a later change without asking them to tear apart the whole home. For now, it is an ordinary empty corner with light on the floor and no demand to decide what year will fill it.

He looks at her.

She touches the ring on her left hand.

“Later,” she says. ʸ

He smiles.

“With you.” ᴹ

“With me.” ʸ

They go back to the sitting room, where Rimuru is insisting he had a very good reason for choosing the wrong box and Shizue has opened her door to ask if somebody would like tea.
