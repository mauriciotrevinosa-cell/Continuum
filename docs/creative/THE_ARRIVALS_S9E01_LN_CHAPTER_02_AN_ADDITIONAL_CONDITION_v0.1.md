# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 53
## Chapter 2 — An Additional Condition

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1 (2026-11-02)
**Volume:** 53
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S9E01
**Continuity state:** B+85 midday. New outside-ward meeting with Rimuru/Yuka/Gojo present, Ciel connected. Three earlier Rulers demand assessment as additional price for continued DIO consultation, acknowledge existing refuge/non-targeting pact remains. Trial nature/abuse withheld; no informed consent to torture. DIO objects privately; M does not accept yet.

*When: the first Ruler begins describing the agreement, Mau interrupts before the word unfinished can become part of it.*

“We finished it.” ᴹ

Rimuru stands beside him. Yuka is on his other side, her shoulder close enough that he could touch it without moving his feet. Gojo has stopped a little farther back, looking at the three figures as if politeness is an experiment he is willing to allow for another minute.

The marker of the protected ground remains behind them. Nobody has moved the earth to make the meeting easier.

The first Ruler regards Mau for a moment.

“The provisions concerning the woman remain recognised.” ᴸ¹

“Then begin there.” ᴿ

The second turns toward Rimuru. It is the one who asked the more practical questions about broken territories. Mau remembers because he had almost liked its willingness to hear the answer.

“What she has provided is insufficient for the next stage of correction,” it says. ᴸ²

Mau listens inward. DIO is listening too.

“She said she couldn't repair everything by explaining it,” he answers. “You agreed to start with slowing what you're doing.” ᴹ

“We have begun.” ᴸ²

“Good. What do you need?” ᴹ

The third Ruler looks at his shadow.

“To establish what accompanies her counsel.” ᴸ³

* * *

Gojo takes the last step forward.

“A fairly ordinary question would have achieved that.” ᴳ

The third does not answer him. Its gaze follows the edge of Mau's sleeve, the place where his skin disappears beneath it.

“You shelter an authority displaced from the control of this world and a sovereign who should have withdrawn from war. You observe what you encounter. You retain the means to reproduce it. You are asking us to accept guidance delivered through that accumulation.” ᴸ³

“DIO can choose what she tells you,” Mau says. “You don't need my abilities to hear an answer.” ᴹ

“We need to know what you will do with an answer.” ᴸ¹

Yuka moves before Mau can reply.

“Then you should have asked that when you made your agreement.” ʸ

All three look toward her.

She does not look away.

“He came home and told us what you promised. You don't get to add something and say he already agreed.” ʸ

The first Ruler lowers its chin slightly.

“He has not agreed to this.” ᴸ¹

“Keep remembering that.” ʸ

Mau glances at her. Her mouth is firm, the colour in her face stronger than it had been over the fruit bowl. He wants to kiss her for the sentence. Instead he lets his fingers brush hers once and turns back.

“What is this?” ᴹ

“An assessment.” ᴸ¹

* * *

They describe a bounded place which can present opposition without bringing it into the inhabited world. A body capable of improvement will enter at its present state. The environment will preserve that state while increasing the demands upon it.

Mau hears the shape of a dungeon and dislikes how carefully they avoid giving it that name.

“You want to see how hard I can hit something?” ᴹ

“We have seen you grow by surviving opposition,” the second answers. “That would tell us very little.” ᴸ²

“What would tell you something?” ᴿ

“The decisions he makes when survival ceases to offer advancement.” ᴸ²

Gojo smiles without humour.

“And who gets to decide that the answer is sufficient?” ᴳ

“The assessment has a completion condition,” the first says. “He will be shown it.” ᴸ¹

“Show him now.” ʸ

“The scenario is generated around the participant.” ᴸ¹

Yuka's fingers leave Mau's. Her arms fold across her chest.

“Convenient.” ʸ

*Do not accept,* DIO says. ᴰⁱ

Mau feels the urgency before the words have finished.

*I'm listening. I haven't accepted.* ᴹ

*Then keep doing that.* ᴰⁱ

* * *

Rimuru asks about observation.

The second Ruler raises a hand. A flat shape of light opens beside it, showing the same patch of empty ground from another angle. A small stone rests in the middle. Rimuru moves his foot; the view changes with it, without delay he can see.

“An exterior station will remain,” the Ruler says. “The participant's state can be monitored.” ᴸ²

“By us,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“You may attend.” ᴸ²

“And if we need him out?” ᴳ

The first answers.

“A recall can be requested through that station.” ᴸ¹

Gojo looks at the light, then back at its maker.

“Requested is doing quite a lot of work.” ᴳ

Mau hears Ciel recording the exact words. He is glad she does. He is also tired of being glad someone is keeping a record of people who have yet to give him an ordinary reason to trust them.

“Ciel stays with me?” he asks. ᴹ

“The participant's accompanying systems are not removed,” the second says. “Communication across the assessment boundary will be limited.” ᴸ²

“How limited?” ᴿ

“Ordinary exterior contact cannot dictate the participant's choices.” ᴸ²

Rimuru's expression changes.

“She's a person.” ᴿ

The Ruler is silent.

Mau steps closer to the light.

“Then write that part differently.” ᴹ

* * *

They spend longer on the conditions than the three seem to have expected.

The inhabited ground remains outside the assessment. Nobody from the house may be taken to supply its opposition. The guests inside Mau retain the private refuge they have chosen; nobody gets to search it, pull them out or require Ashborn to fight. The army and the shikigamis are his, not disposable materials the chamber may claim.

The first agrees to each boundary in turn.

When Mau asks whether the trial can kill him, it answers that the place can restore its participant between scenarios.

Yuka looks at him sharply.

He has gone still.

“Between,” Gojo repeats. ᴳ

“The participant returns to the entry state,” the Ruler says. ᴸ¹

Mau can hear the wind in the trees. For one second it is a different wind, in a different place, and his body remembers the instant before waking.

Yuka touches his wrist.

He looks down at her hand.

“I'm here,” he says, before she asks. ᴹ

She keeps holding it.

* * *

He does not give them an answer that afternoon.

The first Ruler asks when they should expect one. Mau says tomorrow, after the people who will have to live with the decision have heard what he has heard.

The third begins to speak.

Rimuru looks at it.

“You asked for an assessment of his choices. You're watching him make one.” ᴿ

For a moment, the clearing is wonderfully quiet.

They turn toward home together. Gojo stays a step behind, close enough that Mau hears the little breath he lets out when the protected ground is beneath their feet again.

“I don't like them,” Gojo says. ᴳ

“You hid it beautifully.” ᴹ

“Years of teaching.” ᴳ

Yuka stops before they reach the first covered turn. Mau stops with her.

“You heard what they called it?” she asks. ʸ

He nods.

She looks at his face until he gives her the actual answer.

“A trial.” ᴹ

Her grip tightens once.

“Then we talk about that before you start explaining why somebody needs you.” ʸ

He looks toward their home, beyond the common room and the tree.

“Okay,” he says. ᴹ
