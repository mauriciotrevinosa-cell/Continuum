# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 53
## Chapter 4 — Before He Said Yes

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1 (2026-11-02)
**Volume:** 53
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S9E02
**Continuity state:** B+85 evening–B+86 afternoon. House questions Rulers; broader recognition/control coordination offered as new exchange, old three-party pact intact. Conditional M assent after recall/status protocol/ownfamilyexcluded/guestsprivacy confirmed. No acceptance of hidden torture; house insists oversight. Kurobe+12 depart B86 as planned with goodbyes; Geld+2 remain untilB88. Assessment arranged B87 outside ward. DIO guests can remain inner safe; no trial entrance yet.

*When: Maki asks whether he has already decided, Mau says, “No,” and waits for her to believe the whole word.*

She looks at him a moment longer.

Then sits down.

Yuta has brought the account Ciel helped him copy. Rimuru has the same words on his side of the table. Gojo is standing beside Shoko, who has read far enough to turn the page back to the sentence about restoration.

Yuka puts a cup in front of Mau and takes the seat beside him.

“Start with what you want,” she says. ʸ

He had prepared to begin with what the Rulers wanted.

The change makes him think.

“I want the people arranging what arrives here to stop tearing the places apart,” he says at last. “I want DIO to stay where she chose to stay. I want to know whether those three can actually get anyone else to respect the agreement.” ᴹ

“And do you want a trial?” Mai asks. ᴹᵃ

“No.” ᴹ

There is no hesitation this time.

Shoko puts the page down.

“Good. Let's keep that clear.” ˢᵒ

* * *

They do not all have the same objection.

Fern wants to know who can enforce the recall. Yuta asks what happens if a technique used inside affects a contract outside. Maki objects to a completion condition nobody has been allowed to inspect. Frieren asks how an environment can decide what Mau is capable of without deciding what it wants him to become.

He looks at her when she says it.

She holds his gaze.

“That question keeps appearing around you,” she says. ᶠʳ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

“Notice before you enter.” ᶠʳ

Senku takes the sheet when Shoko passes it across. He reads two sentences, taps the one about preserved entry state and asks whether the chamber is holding Mau's body or merely recording it.

Nobody knows yet.

“Then that's a question,” he says. ˢᵉ

Mau listens while the list grows. For once, it does not feel like the room trying to stop him from doing the one useful thing he could do. It feels like the room refusing to let usefulness erase the other parts of him.

He writes the questions down himself.

* * *

The Rulers return to the outer meeting place the next afternoon.

Mau comes with the people who asked to attend. DIO speaks through him by choice when the first question concerns the next stage of the world's corrections.

The first Ruler answers more directly this time.

They can take her guidance to the others involved. A wider undertaking will require those authorities to recognise the refuge as something other than an unexamined source of hostile power. These three propose to place their assessment beside the proposal and answer for its result.

“You already answered for leaving her alone,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“Our undertaking remains,” the Ruler answers. “It does not compel every other hand which has acquired a place in the work.” ᴸ¹

“So you're offering to argue for her,” Mau says. ᴹ

“For the arrangement.” ᴸ¹

“And you want this in exchange.” ᴹ

“Yes.” ᴸ¹

He lets DIO hear the answer before giving his own.

*It is still a choice,* he tells her. ᴹ

*I know. I dislike what they have placed beside it.* ᴰⁱ

*So do I.* ᴹ

* * *

The second Ruler makes the exterior station again.

This time Ciel asks through Mau for a signal to be sent across it while she observes the return. Rimuru chooses the sequence. Gojo walks to the other side of the clearing and strikes a small mark in the dirt when the light asks him to.

The image returns the mark.

Rimuru sends a recall through the station. The little space it is displaying opens, allowing an empty cup to fall out onto the earth.

Gojo picks the cup up.

“And the same function applies to the occupied assessment?” ᴳ

“The station is its exterior interface,” the second answers. ᴸ²

“Answer the question,” Maki says. ᴹᵏ

The Ruler looks at her.

“Yes.” ᴸ²

Mau hears Ciel keep that answer too.

They establish a check and an outside interval. Rimuru will request a pause before the agreed interval has passed, whether the display looks healthy or not. Loss of meaningful communication will be reason to recall him. Nobody has permission to enter the house, borrow a resident, examine a guest's private mind or seize the forces stored with Mau.

“Does the assessment need me to believe any person from home is actually there?” he asks. ᴹ

“No one from your home will enter its scenarios,” the first says. ᴸ¹

Yuka hears the wording and asks whether the chamber can manufacture their appearances.

“It can present what the participant recognises.” ᴸ¹

Mau looks at the light.

The cup is still in Gojo's hand.

* * *

He asks for a few minutes.

They take them inside the protected ground, where the three do not follow.

Yuka turns toward him before anybody else speaks.

“I hate this.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

“You don't owe them the answer they want.” ʸ

He nods.

“I want to hear what I do when they stop making it about how much stronger I can get. And I want them to have to deal with an actual answer from me when they speak to the others.” ᴹ

“They could simply decide they don't like it,” Yuta says. ʸᵘ

“Then they don't like it. I come back.” ᴹ

Gojo rolls the cup between his hands.

“The second stops answering us, I go through the station.” ᴳ

“We request the recall first,” Rimuru says. “Then we stop asking.” ᴿ

Mau looks at Yuka.

She studies his face for a long time. Her anger has not gone anywhere. Neither has the hand which finds his.

“I want you to remember that being allowed to stop counts,” she says. ʸ

“I will.” ᴹ

He means it when he says it.

* * *

He says yes with the conditions spoken again.

The entry will be the following morning, outside the ward. They will bring their own observers. The way back is to remain subject to the station's recall, as demonstrated and confirmed. His body will remain his. His guests will remain out of reach.

DIO says that she objects.

Mau gives the Rulers her exact words.

The first acknowledges them without changing its offer.

Mau does not mistake that acknowledgement for kindness.

* * *

Kurobe and the twelve departing workers are loading the last of their travelling things when they return.

Mau helps carry one case as far as the cart. Kurobe takes it from him and asks whether the new fittings have behaved through a full household evening.

“Everybody found dinner,” Mau says. ᴹ

“Then start there.” ᴷᵘ

They say goodbye properly. Shuna passes on a message. Geld checks one fastening and leaves it alone when its owner tells him it is already checked. The cart goes along the ordinary road, with people waving until the turn takes it out of sight.

Yuka stays beside Mau.

When they walk home, he takes the route past the Fort. Ori calls to ask whether he can help move the little table back tomorrow.

“If I'm late, you can ask someone else,” he says. ᴹ

Ori thinks about it.

“You can still come when you're done.” ᴼ

He smiles at her.

“I would like that.” ᴹ
