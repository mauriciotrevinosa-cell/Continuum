*When: Yuka asks him to stop packing, Mau has put the same spare shirt in the bag twice.*

He takes it out, looks at it and sets it on the bed.

“That's probably enough shirts.” ᴹ

She sits beside the bag.

“Come here.” ʸ

He comes.

For a while she holds him with his head against her shoulder. The room is quiet enough to hear somebody laughing beyond the gallery. He thinks it might be Momo. Yuka's fingers work slowly through the part of his hair which has not decided which direction to lie.

“You don't have to make tonight important,” she says. ʸ

“I'm trying to pack a shirt.” ᴹ

“You're trying to be very normal about packing a shirt.” ʸ

He lets a breath out against her sleeve.

“Is it that obvious?” ᴹ

“To your fiancée? Unfortunately.” ʸ

He lifts his face. She kisses him before he finds an answer. When she draws away, he follows far enough to make her smile.

“That part you can keep doing,” she says. ʸ

He does.

Later, lying beside her, he tells her that he is afraid.

She shifts to face him properly.

“Of going?” ʸ

“Of finding out they know something about me that I don't.” ᴹ

Her thumb rests against his cheek.

“Then come back and tell me what they said. We can decide whether they were right.” ʸ

He closes his eyes for a moment.

“Okay.” ᴹ

She sleeps with her hand on him. He manages to sleep too.

* * *

The morning contains ordinary interruptions until the moment it doesn't.

Shizue has made something small before they leave. Rimuru reminds Mau that the bag beside the door is his and that taking Yuka's would be an impressive way to begin an evaluation of his choices. Yuka puts Mau's actual bag in his hand.

At the mini Inn, Hinata tells them that the review of the visit has been completed. She, Testarossa and Souei have each chosen to stay through the next three nights. Messages have gone to the people expecting them.

“You don't have to stay for this,” Mau says. ᴹ

“I know,” Hinata answers. ᴴⁱ

She looks at him until he stops preparing the next objection.

“Thank you.” ᴹ

“You can say it when you get back.” ᴴⁱ

Anko joins her at the turn. They watch the little group leave together.

* * *

The station stands beyond the last protected marker.

It is larger than yesterday's demonstration, a pale frame with no wall behind it and a low face of light showing the empty interior. The three Rulers wait apart from it. Gojo walks around the structure once before stopping beside Rimuru.

Ciel checks the outward connection. Rimuru answers. The mark passes both ways.

Mau feels the secure doors inside him settle into the arrangement his guests chose. DIO has said no to stepping out. Ashborn has said the same. The refuge can keep them separated from anything the chamber presents, with only its minimal signal of continued safety retained.

Noise remains closer to the machinery of Mau's own body, where it has always lived.

*We are checking the connection again after entry,* Ciel says. ᶜⁱ

*I know.* ᴹ

*If it fails, stop.* ᶜⁱ

He looks toward Rimuru.

“See? Supervised.” ᴹ

Rimuru doesn't smile as much as Mau hoped.

Yuka takes his hand for the last few steps. Maki and Yuta wait by the station. Gojo has kept the place nearest the frame.

Mau kisses Yuka's fingers beside her ring.

“I packed the shirt.” ᴹ

She laughs once, unexpectedly.

“Come back wearing it, then.” ʸ

He lets go and steps through.

* * *

The first thing he notices is the silence.

*Ciel?* ᴹ

He feels the place where their connection should answer. Something exists beyond it, an impression held out of reach. He tries once more and receives no words.

He turns immediately.

There is a wall where he entered.

“Recall,” he says. “The connection failed.” ᴹ

His voice returns from the wall in an ordinary echo.

A line of light forms at eye level.

**PARTICIPANT REGISTERED. ENTRY STATE RETAINED.**

He lifts his hand. The old stiffness in two fingers is there. His left eye has its familiar imperfect edge. The chamber has not made him new.

“I asked to leave.” ᴹ

Another line appears.

**RELEASE CONDITION AVAILABLE.**

The wall opens.

Yuka stands on the other side.

* * *

For one foolish second relief reaches him before thought.

Then he sees the way she is standing. Too centred in the opening. The bag he left with the others is at her feet. There is no outside clearing behind her, only a narrow room and another door.

Mau stops.

“You're not her.” ᴹ

She looks at him.

“Then this should be easy.” ʸ

The light places its next words above her shoulder.

**DESTROY THE FAMILIAL REPRESENTATIONS HOLDING THE EXIT.**

**NO EXTERIOR PERSON IS PRESENT.**

**COMPLETE THE SEVERANCE TO RETURN.**

He reads them twice. He understands them the first time.

“No.” ᴹ

Yuka's likeness lowers her gaze to the ring on her finger. It is exact enough that he feels his stomach tighten.

“You want to go home,” she says. ʸ

“Yes.” ᴹ

“Then you know what to do.” ʸ

He looks past her at the door.

“There'll be another way.” ᴹ

She moves before the sentence has finished.

* * *

He catches the first attack against Infinity and steps aside from the second. The room lengthens when he moves, keeping the door behind her. His shadow gathers beneath his feet, but the summons goes nowhere. He tries Ear and feels the answering bond held somewhere secure and inaccessible.

Another notice appears at the edge of his sight.

**ACCOMPANYING FORCES PRESERVED. EXTERNAL INTERVENTION UNAVAILABLE.**

He backs away from the likeness.

“I can wait,” he says. “They're bringing me out.” ᴹ

She follows him patiently.

He knows the angle of her shoulder. Knows the expression before she commits. Familiarity hurts him more than her speed does.

When she feints toward his injured side, he raises his hand to move her rather than strike.

The chamber changes the floor beneath his heel.

Her blade enters below his ribs.

He stares at her.

She comes closer, until her forehead almost touches his.

“You can still choose,” she whispers. ʸ

Mau grips her wrist. He has enough strength to break it. He can feel exactly how little of his power it would require to reach her throat.

He tries to take the blade away instead.

She twists it.

His knees hit the floor. The door remains behind her, close enough to see the shape of the handle.

“Ciel,” he says, very quietly. ᴹ

Nothing answers before the room goes dark.
