*When: he opens his eyes, Yuka is standing in the doorway again.*

His hand goes to his side.

The skin is whole. His shirt has returned to the state in which he entered. He can still feel the turn of the blade clearly enough that his fingers press into an injury which is no longer there.

The likeness watches him find that out.

“How many?” he asks. ᴹ

She tilts her head.

“How many people do I have to kill?” ᴹ

The light answers instead.

**THE CONDITION APPLIES TO THE COMPLETE RECOGNISED SET.**

His mouth dries.

He thinks of the little table outside the Fort. Of the plates moving through the kitchen. A whole house of people who had learnt to stop asking whether their presence needed to be useful.

The chamber waits for him to finish understanding.

He stands.

“Then you'll need a different condition.” ᴹ

* * *

This time he makes the floor between them a barrier before she moves.

The likeness walks toward it. He lifts himself above the room, reaches for the point beside the door and attempts the small displacement he has used without thinking for ordinary journeys.

He lands where he began.

The door has moved with the representation.

He tries Blue against the wall, drawing its surface inward until pale stone screams. Behind the first layer is another surface bearing the same sign. He tears through it, reaches a third and sees Yuka watching from the opening which has appeared behind him.

“It isn't there,” she says. ʸ

He turns toward her.

“You don't know that.” ᴹ

She raises the blade.

He holds her back with Infinity until the chamber begins to place the attack inside the space it has registered as his body.

Ciel would have told him what it was doing.

He sees the change late and moves far enough to keep the wound from his heart. The pain gives him one useful fact: the chamber is not simply sending a person to fight him. It is altering the encounter around the person he refuses to hit.

He can fight it for longer.

He cannot make the door belong to somewhere else.

* * *

He makes it to what feels like evening before the first restoration he manages to anticipate.

The light returns while he is lying against the broken wall. Yuka's likeness is kneeling near him, holding his hand with the exact care he remembers from the previous night.

His own healing has been forced to spend against a wound the room keeps reopening. He has enough thought left to recognise the waste. Enough power left for one attack which would end the figure beside him.

She places his hand against her throat.

“You don't have to stay,” she says. ʸ

He pulls it away.

She presses her fingers into the wound.

For an instant he cannot breathe through the pain.

“I know,” he manages. ᴹ

The likeness leans close enough that he can see the little variation of colour at the edge of her iris.

That detail nearly undoes him.

He shuts his eyes and waits.

* * *

The next room contains Yuta.

Mau wakes standing in a training space he knows. His side is whole again. A wooden practice weapon lies beside his foot.

Yuta is holding a sword which is not made of wood.

“Pick it up,” he says. ʸᵘ

Mau looks at the familiar face. He feels an absurd, humiliating wish to ask it for help even after seeing the light above the door.

“I want to go home.” ᴹ

“Then stop holding back.” ʸᵘ

He picks up the practice weapon.

For several exchanges he gives himself the small mercy of treating it like training. He watches the shoulder, gives ground, recognises a movement the real Yuta corrected months ago. His response arrives cleanly enough to bring an opening.

He takes the sword out of the likeness's hand.

The door stays closed.

Yuta smiles.

“You understood the instruction.” ʸᵘ

Another sword is already in his hand.

* * *

Mau begins testing the space between restoration and action.

He tries holding the figure still. He tries breaking the weapon and putting himself beyond reach. He reaches the outer edge of his own power without aiming it at the face. Each useful answer is taken into the next arrangement of the room.

The light eventually supplies what he has started to suspect.

**ENTRY PARAMETERS RETAINED. ADVANCEMENT HELD.**

**OPPOSITION SCHEDULE ESCALATED.**

His strength remains. The body he earned remains. The speed with which he turns a lethal strike aside does not vanish because the room wants it to fail.

The next strike is faster.

The wound from the one after it resists his healing for longer.

He learns an angle. The likeness learns the answer the chamber wants it to have.

When he finds the system's familiar branch, there is no reward, no new point waiting to be spent. The entry values sit in front of him with the obscene calm of a closed ledger.

He closes it.

Yuta steps forward.

* * *

Once, he succeeds in throwing the sword through the likeness's shoulder.

It happens in the middle of a turn. A reflex he cannot pull back quickly enough. The blade bites, the familiar face changes and Mau stops moving.

“Grandpa,” Yuka's voice says behind him. ʸ

He turns.

She is in the doorway, looking at what he has done.

The chamber offers him the open line from the sword to Yuta's neck. A simple finishing movement. He knows it is simple because he has practised it with the man whose face is bleeding in front of him.

His hand shakes.

“You're not him,” he says. ᴹ

The likeness pulls the blade out of its shoulder and lets it fall.

“Then finish.” ʸᵘ

Mau cannot.

He kneels beside the figure before he can stop himself, reaching to contain the wound. Yuka watches him from the door. The next attack comes while his attention is on the blood beneath his hands.

He has time to understand the lesson the chamber wanted to teach him.

He refuses it before the darkness comes.

* * *

Something follows the restoration from inside his own body.

Noise has no words which reach the room. The chamber has taken the paths by which Mau usually hears it and filled them with the signals it needs to present an experience. The body still carries the older maintenance beneath those paths. It still knows which work belongs to him.

The restoration passes through that work each time it returns him to the retained state.

Noise follows once and loses the route.

Follows again.

At a depth the room has not learnt to treat as another opponent, it keeps the difference between the body being returned and the person who has not left it.

The door opens above Mau's waking face.

This time Gojo is smiling on the other side.
