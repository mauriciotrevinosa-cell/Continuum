*When: the image loses Mau's face, Rimuru sends the recall before anyone has to ask him to.*

The station returns a pale line.

**REQUEST RECEIVED. PARTICIPANT STATE RETAINED.**

Yuka waits one breath.

Then another.

“Where is he?” ʸ

The low face of light shows the empty room from the demonstration. Mau's name remains beside it. A thin line rises and falls at a rate which might be his breathing.

Rimuru sends the signal again, adding the agreed failure condition.

The first Ruler raises its hand.

“The assessment has begun.” ᴸ¹

Gojo turns toward it.

“Then end it.” ᴳ

“The participant has been given the means to leave.” ᴸ¹

Yuka stares at the figure.

“We agreed that losing contact was enough.” ʸ

“A recall was requested.” ᴸ¹

Maki moves so quickly that her hand is already through the speaker's shoulder when its shape breaks into light.

The other two figures thin with it.

They have been standing beside projections since the entry closed.

“Don't finish that sentence,” Maki says to what is left. ᴹᵏ

* * *

Gojo reaches the frame before the third likeness disappears.

His hand meets something which has weight. He lets his technique go, surrounds himself with the quiet pressure of amplification and pushes.

The surface beside his fingers loses its brilliance. A small opening appears.

He sees the empty clearing on the other side.

He turns his hand, follows the edge and finds the same place from another angle. The chamber is not sitting behind the frame. What he can suppress by contact is the display which addresses it.

“It's an interface,” Rimuru says through his teeth. ᴿ

“I noticed.” ᴳ

Gojo pulls his hand back before he breaks the only piece of the route they still have.

Yuta keeps one palm on Yuka's shoulder while she tries the connection of her own contract. He can see the effort in her jaw. She reaches for something familiar, receives the proof that it still exists and finds no passage along which she can send a word.

“He's there,” she says. “I can feel the edge of it.” ʸ

Rimuru nods.

“Ciel can too.” ᴿ

“Then tell her to bring him back.” ʸ

The plea catches on the last word. She presses her lips together, furious with the sound.

Rimuru looks at her properly.

“She's working on it.” ᴿ

* * *

The station supplies a value when Ciel asks for the state of Mau's body.

It supplies the same value when she asks again.

Rimuru watches her lay the two impressions together. The line has repeated, including a tiny hesitation in the breath which no living person would reproduce at the same interval simply because someone asked politely.

“It isn't live,” he says. ᴿ

Gojo's face changes.

Yuka looks from one to the other.

“What does that mean?” ʸ

Rimuru makes himself answer without softening the useful part.

“It means we can't use that to tell whether he's all right.” ᴿ

She turns toward the empty place where the Rulers stood.

For a moment Maki looks as though she might try to cut that absence too.

Yuta catches her wrist.

“Find him first,” he says. ʸᵘ

Maki looks back at him. The anger in her face stays. Her hand lowers.

* * *

They send for help without bringing the whole house to the unprotected clearing.

Shoko arrives with what she would need if a body came through now. Senku comes to examine the station's repeated response. Frieren stands beside the frame long enough to understand which visible layers are only showing them somewhere else.

Each tries something the others have not.

None calls the failure a reason to stop.

Yuka goes inside the protected marker to speak to Hinata herself. She gives her the truth they have: contact failed, the organisers left a false display, they are working on getting Mau out. The children are to remain with the adults who are keeping the ordinary day going.

Hinata listens without interrupting.

“What do you need from me?” ᴴⁱ

Yuka looks toward the house.

“Please don't let them think everybody disappeared.” ʸ

Hinata nods once.

Anko takes Yuka's hand briefly before letting her go back.

* * *

Bellion waits where Mau asked the exterior guard to remain before entering.

Yuka calls Igris beside him and explains what has changed. Their attention goes toward the frame together. Beru emerges when she asks for him, already looking for the familiar person whose shadow is being held somewhere beyond reach.

There is no army she can order through a route which does not exist.

She gives them work which does.

Watch the ground. Report any place the relation to their king changes. Keep everyone clear if a route opens. Do not attack a person merely because they arrive with light around them.

Beru bends toward her.

“And my king?” ᴮ

She grips her own hand hard enough that the ring presses into the neighbouring finger.

“Tell me the second you can feel where he is.” ʸ

He bows.

* * *

By midday, Rimuru has a different line to watch.

It is faint, entirely unlike the clean display the station keeps offering. Ciel has found the old connection beneath the failed messages. She cannot hear Mau. She cannot make him hear her. Something at the far end still belongs to the same person.

*Hold the exterior reference,* she tells Rimuru. *Even if it never answers.* ᶜⁱ

He sits down beside the frame.

Yuka lowers herself next to him.

Gojo keeps standing.

When the station offers the familiar, false breath again, Rimuru turns its image away from them and holds the line which has not yet spoken.
