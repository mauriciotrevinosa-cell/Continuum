*When: the pain stops before he has finished asking it to, Mau keeps his eyes closed.*

He has learnt that opening them can begin the next part.

For a while he listens.

Something small touches a hard surface. A cup, set down carefully. A chair moving against a floor.

“You're taking longer than they expected.” ᴹᵏⁱ

He opens his eyes.

Makima sits across a table from him.

* * *

His body moves before the rest of him understands the room.

He is standing, the chair overturned behind him, one dagger in his hand. It has the familiar weight of the weapon he entered with. The chamber has returned it often enough that recognising it is no longer a comfort.

Makima looks at the blade.

Then up at him.

“Sit down, Mau.” ᴹᵏⁱ

He hears the way she says his name and remembers blood on her mouth outside the house. The weight of carrying her. His own voice telling the others that she might know who he had been.

“You're a copy.” ᴹ

“You keep finding ways to make what you see less important.” ᴹᵏⁱ

He does not sit.

She puts both hands around the cup, leaving them where he can see them.

“They wanted to know what you would choose. Their little place was quite useful.” ᴹᵏⁱ

Mau looks toward the wall.

The light above the door flickers.

For the first time since he entered, the orderly writing breaks into a shape he recognises from the instant Makima opened the gate beneath them.

* * *

He attacks the woman at the table.

It is the first uncomplicated movement he has made in so long that the relief of committing to it almost makes him sick.

The dagger enters her throat. The cup falls. He pulls the blade free and steps away before the body can touch him.

For a second the room contains nothing but his breathing.

Then Makima's head moves.

The wound closes with the same terrible ease he remembers.

“That still isn't the condition,” she says. ᴹᵏⁱ

He looks at the blood disappearing from the table.

“What did you do?” ᴹ

“Found something people were already willing to let happen.” ᴹᵏⁱ

Her voice is mild. It is almost worse than if she had enjoyed the words loudly enough to give him something obvious to hate.

He reaches for Ciel again.

The blank place answers.

Makima watches his face.

“They can't hear you.” ᴹᵏⁱ

* * *

She begins the speech from the market.

The first sentence is enough. Mau has heard the pattern before, watched fear turn ordinary people toward a story somebody wanted them to believe. He listens to the next sentence and knows the pause before the next word.

Makima gives it exactly.

Then stops.

“You remembered,” she says. ᴹᵏⁱ

He stares at her.

“You wrote it.” ᴹ

“You asked me that last time too.” ᴹᵏⁱ

He did. He remembers the room at home, the shift in her expression when Ori mentioned DIO, the raised hand moving toward Yuka. Remembers knowing so little about what the gesture might do that he had attacked before allowing himself another thought.

The room lets him remember that whole sequence.

It puts her gate's broken pattern beside the door while he does.

“The people outside aren't part of this,” he says. ᴹ

“They aren't here.” ᴹᵏⁱ

He grips the dagger harder.

“Leave them out of it.” ᴹ

Makima smiles as if he has asked for the part she was waiting to discuss.

* * *

Yuka enters through the door behind her.

Mau steps toward her and stops so abruptly that his heel skids on the floor. He knows. Knowing does not prevent the first movement.

The likeness wears the hoodie she left on their chair.

It takes the place beside Makima without looking at him.

“You see?” the woman says. “They make good reasons. You bring them with you.” ᴹᵏⁱ

“Don't talk about her.” ᴹ

“You would rather I let her speak?” ᴹᵏⁱ

He looks at Yuka's face. The chamber has given her the colour he cannot find anywhere else. His body reaches for the sight as if it is shelter, even now.

The likeness meets his eyes.

“You can go back,” she says. ʸ

He closes his own for a second.

“I know.” ᴹ

“Then why won't you?” ʸ

He has answered versions of that question until the answer feels worn thin from being held.

“Because I want to see you.” ᴹ

She looks confused.

He opens his eyes again.

“Her. I want to see her. I don't want the last thing I do before that to be this.” ᴹ

Makima sets the repaired cup down.

* * *

The room offers an easier version.

The blade is placed in his hand, Yuka already kneeling, the movement required so small that he could finish it before she has time to be afraid. The door behind her opens a fraction. He hears a sound from beyond it which resembles the common room at dinner.

He drops the weapon.

Makima watches it fall.

For the first time, she looks interested in a way which resembles surprise.

“You know she won't die outside.” ᴹᵏⁱ

He looks toward the half-open door.

He can hear a laugh. Someone setting a dish down. A voice calling for somebody who is late.

He wants to get up and run toward it so badly that his body begins shaking.

“Give me another way,” he says. ᴹ

Makima pushes the dagger closer with one finger.

* * *

Much later, after the table and the woman have gone, he keeps a different explanation beside the pain.

The Rulers began the place.

Makima found it.

She has the same gate. The same words. The same willingness to use a face he loves as the shortest route to something inside him.

He has no Ciel with whom to examine the thought. No Rimuru to ask whether knowing part of a story is enough. The next room is already opening.

The explanation stays.

* * *

Elsewhere, beyond the place in which Mau is being restored, the third Ruler watches the new pattern take hold.

“He still refuses,” the second says. ᴸ²

“Then the severance remains available.” ᴸ¹

The third closes the view of the woman at the table. Her likeness thins into the material of the assessment.

“And if he finds another passage?” it asks. ᴸ³

The first regards the trace left by her gate.

“He will have an account of what occurred.” ᴸ¹

The image returns to Mau.

No one opens the recall.
