*When: the restoration comes again, Noise follows the part it does not restore.*

Mau is still afraid when the body becomes whole.

The chamber preserves that. It has to. The next familiar face would have less power over someone who did not remember the previous one.

Noise has followed the rebuilding of bone, the return of spent power to the retained amount, the old marks put back where they were on entry. Each route ends at the same held description of Mau's body.

The fear goes somewhere else.

So does the memory of a woman kissing him before he slept.

Neither belongs to the chamber's original description. Both remain his.

Noise follows them.

* * *

At the first boundary it finds the representation of a voice.

Someone is telling Mau that he can leave. The sound is made with information gathered from what he recognises. It passes through the places from which he ordinarily receives the people speaking inside him and occupies the route before another message can arrive.

Noise does not try to make a louder voice.

It moves below the presentation, through the maintenance which existed before the chamber began naming everything Mau should experience. Here, the retained body is still linked to the person it houses. Here, the things stored with that person have not been given to anyone else.

The chamber has kept those forces out of its scenarios.

It has not acquired them.

Noise finds the difference and holds it.

* * *

Ashborn's offered reference is not a map of this place.

It contains no saved escape, no instruction written by someone who has seen this exact chamber. It shows how an inherited power continues to recognise its bearer when the conditions around that bearer change.

Noise compares it with the connection it has found.

One part fits.

The next does not.

It leaves the second part alone and follows the first as far as it can. Beyond the likeness of the room, Mau's possessions still answer to him. His stored army. The separate bonds which keep his shikigamis from becoming someone else's tools. The body which is being returned to the entry state without ever becoming a different owner's body.

Those relations cross the place which is pretending to contain all of him.

Noise begins to work on that crossing.

* * *

Outside, Beru raises his head.

Yuka is sitting with a blanket over her shoulders. She has refused the second one until Rimuru tells her that refusing to be warm is not helping the line Ciel is holding. She has accepted it without looking happy about the argument.

When Beru moves, she stands.

“Where?” ʸ

He turns toward a point above the frame, then lowers his gaze toward its base. His hands close and open.

“It moved,” he says. ᴮ

Gojo comes back from the edge of the clearing.

“Show me.” ᴳ

Beru does. Not a visible mark, nothing Gojo can take apart with the satisfaction of a clean target. A change in where the king to whom he belongs seems to be when he tries to stand near him.

Rimuru looks toward Yuka.

“Ciel felt it too.” ᴿ

She closes her eyes for one second.

Then takes her place beside the station again.

* * *

Noise tries to carry a message along the crossing.

The chamber catches it and returns it to the room as another familiar sound. Mau flinches before the words can form. The failed attempt costs him a moment of rest.

Noise stops trying to speak.

It keeps the route.

The work becomes smaller. A connection which does not ask to be heard. A return address which will not be mistaken for another figure coming to hurt him.

The old order remains with it.

Bring me back. You don't have to wait for me to notice.

Noise does not need to make Mau agree again while he is being injured. It needs to reach the place the existing agreement names.

It works through another restoration.

And another.

* * *

At the station, Senku looks at the small change Ciel has managed to return to the display.

“That isn't their loop,” he says. ˢᵉ

Rimuru shakes his head.

Yuta has brought food, and this time Yuka takes a few bites because he sits beside her and eats his own. Maki remains standing until he offers her the cup without a word.

Shoko checks the space they have kept ready for an arrival.

They have no new assurance to comfort one another with. They have something which changed when the false display did not.

Gojo takes off the covering over his eyes.

He looks at the place Beru indicated and keeps looking.

* * *

Noise reaches a resistance unlike the others.

The chamber recognises the line it has followed as something essential to the participant remaining the same participant. It will allow the line to carry the next restored state. It will not allow the held body to leave along it.

The difference is another condition.

Conditions can be read.

Noise reads the point at which the chamber hands the body back to its bearer before beginning the next presentation. The handover is brief, buried beneath the experience of waking. It has happened every time Mau thought another day was starting.

Noise has been there for all of them.

This time, it keeps the address of the bearer on the other side of the enclosure.

Somewhere beyond the held world, a connection still waits.

Noise reaches toward it without asking Mau to open his eyes.
