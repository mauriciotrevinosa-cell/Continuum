*When: Ciel feels the answer, Rimuru is already reaching for the place beside the frame.*

He has held the connection through the night without letting the station's false display replace it. Several times he has thought it changed. Each time Ciel has asked for another interval before naming what they could not yet prove.

This time she does not ask.

*Move everyone off the direct line.* ᶜⁱ

Rimuru stands.

“Back from the frame. Now.” ᴿ

Yuka is on her feet before he finishes. She looks toward Beru. The shadow has bent forward, claws open, his attention fixed on a point which keeps moving lower.

Gojo steps into the space the others have left.

“Where?” ᴳ

Rimuru points.

The air tears several paces to the side of the station.

* * *

Inside, Noise holds the handover open.

The chamber tries to deliver the retained state to the next room. Noise has found the older address beneath that delivery: the bearer who remains connected to what is waiting outside. It gives the body back along that relation instead of accepting the destination the presentation supplies.

The resistance is immediate.

Noise does not dismantle the chamber. It cannot make the world behind every surface stop existing. It keeps one return from becoming the next scenario.

The route buckles where it reaches the obstruction.

Something beyond it answers with the same identity Ciel has been holding all night.

Noise follows.

* * *

Mau comes through shoulder first.

For a moment he is simply a body falling out of light. His shirt is torn along one side. Blood has dried beneath the sleeve and begun again at an injury the interrupted restoration did not finish closing. His left arm hangs at an angle which makes Shoko move before she has thought about it.

He catches the ground with his good hand.

Gojo reaches him in the same instant.

The space between them throws Gojo back.

* * *

Mau sees faces.

Yuka. Rimuru. Maki beyond them. Someone in a white coat moving too quickly toward a place he has not yet learnt how to survive.

His body remembers what comes after they get close.

He drives the distance outward.

The first pulse takes loose earth off the ground. Gojo lands on his feet and raises one arm toward the others. Yuka has already caught herself against Yuta's shoulder. Nobody is bleeding, but the next step forward becomes something the air refuses to allow.

Mau gathers his knees beneath him.

He looks toward the familiar people and tries to make the interval last.

“Wait,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka's face changes.

He recognises that expression too. The room has used it often.

“Please.” ᴹ

* * *

The ordinary Infinity he knows has turned, under the pressure of the reflex, into a sustained refusal of approach. The red strain of the effect moves through the space around him, driven by the same technique he has not let go since his body decided the next person must be kept away.

It costs him enough that his injured arm begins shaking.

He keeps it anyway.

Rimuru crouches where the pressure will let him remain. He calls Mau's name in the voice he uses at home.

Mau looks toward him.

For a second Rimuru thinks the recognition is enough.

Then Mau asks whether he can have a minute first.

Yuka makes a sound beside Yuta which she cannot quite turn into a word.

Gojo looks at Mau's face.

The covering is still off his own eyes. He can see the technique's strain, the badly finished repair, the way Mau is preparing for the approach instead of recognising the person making it.

“Give him room,” he says. ᴳ

“He's asking for it,” Yuka answers, her voice breaking. ʸ

Gojo turns toward her just long enough to make sure she sees he heard.

“I know.” ᴳ

* * *

Ciel reaches the restored root and finds the last of the chamber's presentation still occupying Mau's expectation of a voice.

She has a connection again.

Reaching the person at the other end takes another moment.

Rimuru feels the effort in her silence. He keeps the place he has held instead of filling it with another demand for her to hurry.

*You have him,* he tells her. ᴿ

She stays with the route.

* * *

Noise stops pulling when the body is fully out.

The chamber attempts to recall the state it has lost. The route it used is no longer being supplied to it. The line collapses back into the folded place, taking the false doorway with it.

The remaining frame goes dim.

Mau is on the ordinary ground, beneath a sky which has been growing lighter for several minutes without his permission.

His army still belongs to him. The secure doors inside remain closed around the guests who chose them. No new power has been taken from either guest to pay for the passage.

Noise keeps the small portion of the work which can hold the return steady.

Everything else can wait.

* * *

Gojo lets his own technique fall away.

The air around his body acquires the smooth, close pressure of amplification. He takes one step toward Mau.

At the edge of his sleeve, the repulsion loses its hold.

He takes another step, making no sudden movement with his hands. Behind him, Yuta keeps the route clear. Shoko moves only far enough to see what she will have to treat when she can get there.

Mau lifts his eyes.

The familiar face is coming through the space he made to keep it away.

Gojo stops within speaking distance.

He has never seen Mau look at him like this.
