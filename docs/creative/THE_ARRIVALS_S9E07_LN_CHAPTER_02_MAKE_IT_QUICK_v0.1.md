*When: Gojo says his name, Mau tries to sit up straighter.*

It costs him enough that Gojo stops moving.

The injured arm slips against Mau's side. He catches it with the other hand, presses his shoulder back and looks up at the person standing over him.

“Can you wait?” he asks. ᴹ

Gojo lowers himself slowly.

“I can wait.” ᴳ

Mau looks past him at the people beyond the field.

Yuka is there. Her hand is over her mouth. Rimuru is closer to the ground, looking at Mau with an expression he would once have known how to answer.

Mau brings his eyes back to Gojo.

“Just a little,” he says. ᴹ

“All right.” ᴳ

For a few breaths they stay that way.

* * *

Gojo can feel where the amplification is doing its work.

The pressure presses against his shoulders and slides away in the thin space his own body occupies. Beyond him the field remains active. He cannot reach back for Yuka and bring her through it as if he has opened a doorway for everyone.

He keeps both hands visible.

Mau is watching them.

“I'm going to come closer,” Gojo says. ᴳ

Mau's breathing changes.

Gojo stops again.

“I can stay here for another moment.” ᴳ

There is a small nod. It might be thanks. It might simply be the point at which Mau cannot keep holding his head as high.

Gojo lets the silence last.

Behind him, nobody tries to make the moment useful by talking over it.

* * *

Mau tries to remember which version of Gojo this is.

The one who answers questions. The one who gives him space. The one who asks whether he is tired before deciding what to do with the answer.

The face does not tell him.

He has seen it be gentle before.

His arm hurts. The pain seems ordinary, which means almost nothing now. He looks at the ground and searches for the line of the room. There should be a place where the next door appears. Somewhere the instruction will return when he has had enough time to imagine something else.

Gojo speaks again.

“You don't have to hold that up.” ᴳ

Mau almost laughs.

He knows that invitation too.

“I know.” ᴹ

His voice is hoarse enough that Gojo leans a little nearer to hear it.

Mau looks back up.

He is so tired.

* * *

“Please,” he says. ᴹ

Gojo waits.

“This time, make it quick.” ᴹ

The clearing becomes quiet in a way it has not been through the entire night.

Gojo's hand stops halfway to his knee.

Mau sees the pause and tries to explain before the likeness can decide he asked badly.

“You did it once. I didn't have to—” ᴹ

The rest does not come.

He swallows and tries again.

“Please. I don't need another minute. Just don't make it take so long.” ᴹ

Gojo looks at the young man in front of him. At the careful way he has put his broken arm where it might be least inconvenient. At the face turned upward, waiting to discover whether asking for mercy has made the next part worse.

He has to take a breath before he can trust his voice.

“Mau. Look at me.” ᴳ

Mau obeys immediately.

That hurts too.

* * *

“I'm not going to do that.” ᴳ

The answer brings no relief to Mau's face.

Gojo understands, with a horror which leaves no room for anger yet, what the refusal may sound like to him.

“I'm going to stay here,” he says. “If you fall, I'm going to catch you.” ᴳ

Mau looks toward his hands again.

“I want to go home,” he says. ᴹ

“I know.” ᴳ

“I couldn't do it.” ᴹ

Gojo does not ask what.

“All right.” ᴳ

“I knew what they wanted. I just—” ᴹ

His breathing catches. The field around them trembles, pushing a little harder against the outside ground.

Gojo holds his place.

“You can tell me later.” ᴳ

Mau closes his eyes.

* * *

*Mau.* ᶜⁱ

He goes completely still.

The voice is close in the way the room never managed to be. It occupies the old relation between an answer and the person who chooses to give it.

*You are outside. Gojo is beside you. I am with Rimuru and with you.* ᶜⁱ

Mau's eyes open.

He looks at the man crouched in front of him.

At the place where the light breaks against his sleeve.

*Ciel?* ᴹ

*Yes.* ᶜⁱ

His mouth moves before any sound comes out.

*I couldn't hear you.* ᴹ

*I know. We kept the connection we could reach. Noise found the way to it.* ᶜⁱ

He tries to turn toward Rimuru. The movement hurts and he barely notices it.

*You're here.* ᴹ

*Yes.* ᶜⁱ

For the first time since the wall closed behind him, another voice reaches the place which answers inside his own body.

*Back,* Noise says. ᴺ

Mau draws a breath so sharply that Gojo lifts his hand without touching him.

* * *

“You're back,” Mau says aloud. ᴹ

The words break halfway through.

Ciel answers him again, patiently, with no demand attached to what he must do next.

He looks at Gojo's face and sees the fear in it.

The real fear.

Not the little opening the room gave him beside a throat.

“I'm back?” ᴹ

Gojo nods.

“You're back.” ᴳ

Mau tries to breathe. It becomes a sob before it becomes air.

“I'm back.” ᴹ

He lets the technique go.

* * *

The pressure ends around them.

Gojo releases amplification when there is nothing left for it to suppress. He is already close enough when Mau's body folds forward.

He catches him with one arm behind his shoulders and the other supporting the injured side, taking the weight before it reaches the ground. Mau grips the front of his clothes with a hand which will not stop shaking.

“I'm back,” he says again. ᴹ

Gojo holds him.

“Yes.” ᴳ

Yuka reaches them on her knees. She stops her hand a little short of Mau's face until he sees it. His eyes find her ring, then her eyes, then the colour the world has never given back anywhere else.

He begins crying harder.

“I didn't,” he says. “I didn't do it.” ᴹ

“You don't have to explain now,” she answers. ʸ

“I wanted to come home.” ᴹ

She touches his cheek.

“I know. You're here.” ʸ

He leans toward her hand. She stays where he can choose the last little distance himself.

* * *

Shoko comes beside Gojo and tells Mau before touching his arm.

His whole body tightens at her voice.

She stops.

Something passes over her face. She does not make him answer for it.

“I need to help the injury,” she says. “Gojo can stay. Yuka can stay. Tell me if you need me to wait.” ˢᵒ

Mau looks at her, then at the arm.

He nods once.

Rimuru settles where he can see him. Ciel remains close, giving Mau the next small thing to attend to while Shoko works. No one asks him how much stronger he became. No notice opens above his head to tell him he has earned a reward.

When Gojo lifts him, Mau catches at his sleeve.

Gojo waits for the request.

“Don't put me back.” ᴹ

Yuka stands beside them, still holding his good hand.

“We're taking you home.” ʸ

The protected marker is only a short walk away.

They cross it together.
