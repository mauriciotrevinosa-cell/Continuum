*When: Mau wakes, Shoko is waiting for him to notice her hand before she moves it toward his wrist.*

He looks at the hand.

Then her face.

“Can I?” she asks. ˢᵒ

His body has already tightened against the bed. He can feel himself doing it and cannot make the first response disappear quickly enough to spare her from seeing.

She keeps her hand where it is.

He looks around the room. Yuka is in the chair beside him, awake, her fingers wrapped around a cup she has forgotten. Rimuru is near the foot of the bed. Through the open door he can hear somebody asking whether a tray should stay outside.

*You are in the clinic,* Ciel says. *The injuries are being checked. You have been sleeping.* ᶜⁱ

He lets the breath out.

“Yes,” he tells Shoko. ᴹ

She takes his wrist.

The touch is ordinary.

He begins to cry anyway.

* * *

Shoko does not move closer to make the crying stop.

She finishes the thing she asked permission to do, tells him what she finds useful and releases his hand before taking the next part of the examination for granted.

His arm is no longer sitting at the terrible angle in which Gojo carried him. The wounds at his side have closed. He can feel the lingering tenderness, the effort his body has spent accepting its own shape after the chamber kept making that shape the beginning of another injury.

He tries to sit up.

Yuka puts the cup down, then waits for him to ask before helping.

That small pause hurts.

He holds out his good hand.

She takes it immediately.

* * *

“I know it's you,” he tells Shoko. ᴹ

She looks up from the sleeve she is moving carefully away from his arm.

“I believe you.” ˢᵒ

“It isn't because I think you'd—” ᴹ

The sentence stops. He looks down at the blanket.

Shoko waits.

He tries again.

“I know where I am. It's just faster than I am.” ᴹ

Her face softens without becoming the kind of pity he would want to escape.

“Then I'll give you time to catch up,” she says. ˢᵒ

He nods. Yuka's hand stays around his.

When Shoko asks him to move the arm, he does. The two old fingers resist in the familiar way. The chamber's return has left them as they were, and the care afterwards has not made their history disappear.

For once, he is glad of a familiar imperfection.

* * *

He asks how long he was gone.

Rimuru tells him when he entered and when they brought him across the marker again. One night outside. A morning on either side of it. Enough hours to keep the whole house worried and far too few to contain the distance Mau has brought back.

Mau looks at the light beyond the window.

“It was more.” ᴹ

“I know,” Rimuru says. ᴿ

“I stopped counting.” ᴹ

Nobody asks him to start again now.

He tries to find a number because it feels like the easiest way to make them understand. The old trial had one he could say, even when saying it made the room go still. This one has intervals he cannot put beside each other. A room in which his hands were gone. A morning with a bowl. Gojo waiting for an answer.

His breathing shortens.

Yuka's thumb moves once against his hand.

“You don't need a number for me to believe you,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her.

The attempt at counting loosens its hold.

* * *

“They said I could leave,” he tells them. ᴹ

Rimuru leans forward.

“What did they mean?” ᴿ

Mau's fingers tighten around Yuka's before he can stop them. He feels her return the pressure, then carefully eases his own.

“Copies. All of you. They said I had to kill them.” ᴹ

The room changes around the sentence.

Shoko puts down what she is holding.

Yuka keeps her eyes on his face.

“I knew,” he says. “They told me no one outside was there. I understood what they wanted. I kept thinking I could do something else.” ᴹ

He swallows.

“Then I kept thinking I could wait a little longer.” ᴹ

Rimuru looks down at his own hands for a moment. When he looks up again, his face has settled into an anger Mau does not have to defend himself against.

“You asked to leave before that?” ᴿ

“When Ciel didn't answer. Right away.” ᴹ

Rimuru nods once.

“We asked too.” ᴿ

* * *

Mau hears what they did outside in pieces small enough to keep.

The first recall. The repeated display. Gojo putting his hand through a route which showed nothing behind it. Ciel refusing to let the old connection go even when it did not carry a voice.

He looks toward Rimuru.

*You stayed?* ᴹ

*I stayed,* Ciel answers. ᶜⁱ

He wipes his face with the edge of the blanket. Shoko gives him something better without commenting on the choice.

“I thought you couldn't find me.” ᴹ

“We couldn't find a way to you,” Rimuru says. “We kept trying.” ᴿ

Mau nods until the difference begins to fit somewhere inside him.

He is grateful. He is furious. He is so tired that neither feeling stays separate for long.

Yuka moves the cup back into his sight.

“Water first.” ʸ

He accepts it with both hands. They shake. She steadies the bottom without taking it away.

* * *

When Shoko asks what he needs next, he almost gives the answer the chamber taught him to give.

A minute.

He stops before it reaches his mouth.

Nobody here is holding the next attack until he has rested enough to bear it.

“Can I go to our room?” he asks. ᴹ

Yuka's eyes fill before she smiles.

“When Shoko says you can.” ʸ

He looks toward the doctor.

She finishes considering him before answering.

“After you've kept some food down and we've seen you stand without trying to prove anything. Somebody stays near. We check again this evening.” ˢᵒ

“Okay.” ᴹ

It is a small list.

He can imagine doing the things on it.

Yuka lifts the spoon from the tray and offers it to him handle first.

He takes it. The first mouthful is warm, and this time nobody uses his hunger to ask him for anything.
