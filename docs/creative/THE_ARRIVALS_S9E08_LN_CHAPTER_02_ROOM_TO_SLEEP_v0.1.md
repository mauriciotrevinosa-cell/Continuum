*When: they reach the apartment, Mau stops beside the chair holding Yuka's hoodie.*

It is where she left it.

Black and orange, although only one small part of the room gives him colour. The rest remains the familiar range of grey he has been living with. He can recognise the hoodie by its shape, the fold she makes in the sleeve, the name she has refused to give back to its former owner.

Yuka sees what he is looking at.

“Still mine,” she says. ʸ

He laughs once.

The sound breaks a little. She lets it finish without trying to turn it into another question.

Shizue has put water within reach. Rimuru stays in the sitting room while Yuka takes Mau to their bed. The room looks too small to have contained one of the false mornings and exactly the right size to be theirs.

He sits down slowly.

Yuka takes his shoes off after asking. He is too tired to pretend he could do it more comfortably himself.

* * *

He wakes to a hand near his shoulder.

His body moves before he sees the rest of the room. He catches the wrist, stops the movement toward him and pulls his other arm across his chest as if a second attack is already there.

Yuka holds still.

“Mau.” ʸ

The name reaches him.

He lets go so abruptly that she has to catch her own hand against the mattress.

“Sorry.” ᴹ

She turns the wrist once. It is unharmed. He can see that and still cannot stop looking for the mark he might have left.

“I was fixing the blanket,” she says. “I should have woken you first.” ʸ

“You shouldn't have to ask to move in your own bed.” ᴹ

Her face changes.

He puts his hands on the blanket where he can see them.

“I know it's you.” ᴹ

“I know.” ʸ

The words help. They do not make the moment easy.

* * *

They eat in the sitting room because he asks to get out of the bedroom for a while.

Shizue talks about something one of the children made. Rimuru corrects a detail in a story Mau has heard before, and the disagreement is so ordinary that he follows it until he forgets to listen for a change in the room.

There is a knock at the shared door.

His hand closes around the spoon.

Rimuru looks toward him before going to answer.

Geld is leaving with the two workers who remained for the maintenance handover. The agreed checks are complete. He has come to say goodbye instead of allowing somebody to tell Mau later that he missed it.

Mau stands before he should.

Yuka's hand finds the back of the chair.

Geld steps inside far enough to spare him the walk.

“The people taking over know which work is theirs,” he says. “If something needs us later, you can send word.” ᴳᵉ

Mau nods.

“Thank you for our home.” ᴹ

Geld looks past him at the open doors, the cups, the people occupying what had been a plan beneath his hands.

“Use it,” he says. ᴳᵉ

They part with the ordinary promise to speak again. Mau sits down before his legs can make the decision for him.

* * *

By the second waking, he knows he needs to ask.

There has been no incident this time. He opened his eyes, saw Yuka beside him and understood where he was. His body still prepared for the moment when she would move.

He lies awake until she notices.

“You can say it,” she whispers. ʸ

He turns toward her.

The fear of hurting her with the request has kept him quiet longer than the request deserves.

“Could I have a little space tonight?” ᴹ

She goes still.

He makes himself continue while she can still hear the meaning from him.

“I want you here. I keep waking before I remember, and I don't want to spend the night trying to hide that from you. I think I need to sleep without someone close enough for my body to react first.” ᴹ

Yuka looks down at the blanket between them.

“One night?” ʸ

“We can see in the morning.” ᴹ

She is quiet long enough that he almost begins withdrawing the request.

Then she looks back at him.

“Okay.” ʸ

* * *

They discuss where before either gets out of bed.

Mau offers the sofa for himself. Yuka says that if sleeping alone is what he needs, moving him beside everyone passing through the sitting room would be a foolish way to get it. She can stay with Maki for the night, if Maki wants the company.

“It's your room too,” he says. ᴹ

“I'm aware. I'll keep my key.” ʸ

There is enough of her ordinary expression in the answer that he can breathe more easily.

“I'm not asking you to go away.” ᴹ

“Then tell me when you want me back.” ʸ

He thinks about the question.

“Breakfast?” ᴹ

She nods.

“Breakfast.” ʸ

* * *

Maki comes to the shared door after Yuka speaks to her.

She looks at Mau once, sees him preparing a defence of the request and raises a hand before he can begin.

“She told me.” ᴹᵏ

He nods.

“You can ask for things too,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Yuka takes only what she needs for one night. She kisses Mau's forehead after he leans toward her, gives Rimuru an instruction about making him drink the water and leaves with Maki by the short family route.

The door closes.

Mau sits on the bed and feels relief arrive beside missing her.

He lets both stay.

* * *

Rimuru looks in before going to his own room.

“Door?” ᴿ

“A little open.” ᴹ

He leaves it that way.

Shizue says goodnight from the sitting room and goes to her own bed. Nobody has surrendered a room or been made into a permanent watcher to give Mau one quiet night.

He lies down on the side which is his.

When he wakes later, there is no face beside him. He hears an ordinary movement beyond the open door and waits long enough to recognise it as Rimuru getting water.

*Here,* Ciel says softly. ᶜⁱ

He takes a slower breath.

Then, because nobody is coming to make rest the beginning of another test, he closes his eyes again.
