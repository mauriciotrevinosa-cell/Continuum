*When: Mai hears the date, she asks whether Mau has remembered what he offered Yuka at the engagement dinner.*

He looks toward Yuka.

She is already smiling.

“Of course I remember.” ᴹ

“The exact part,” Mai says. ᴹᵃ

“A new ring.” ᴹ

“For my wedding present,” Yuka adds. ʸ

Mai puts the page between them.

“Good. Then we can stop all three of you remembering different rings.” ᴹᵃ

Mau looks at the simple circle she has drawn. Marin has left space beside it for something more precise. Wakana is finishing another piece of work nearby and listening with the expression of someone who knows a small object can become a large conversation very quickly.

Yuka turns her engagement band with one finger.

“This one stays.” ʸ

“I know.” ᴹ

Her grandfather's ring rests on its cord beneath her collar. The flower bracelet is at her wrist. None of them needs to disappear to make room for the next thing.

* * *

Yuta arrives with two cups and the deeply unhelpful observation that a wedding ring ought to be less troublesome than raising grandchildren.

Mai takes one cup.

“Then you can explain how much you want him to put inside it.” ᴹᵃ

Yuta sits down.

Mau looks at him with sincere appeal.

“Something less than sixty years?” ᴹ

“I told you I wasn't working on it every day.” ʸᵘ

“Nobody's helping me with that distinction.” ᴹ

Yuka leans forward, laughing.

“I didn't ask you to put Rika in a new ring.” ʸ

Mau stops preparing the next objection.

“You said something like—” ᴹ

“I wanted something from you. Something we decided to make for us.” ʸ

She touches the line on the page.

“Tell me what you want it to do.” ʸ

* * *

He starts with the answer which frightens him least.

Useful. Comfortable. Something she can wear without thinking about whether she has just activated another demand on his body. Something she can keep through the ordinary work of a day.

Yuka waits until he has finished.

“That's what I want from the ring itself,” she says. “What do you want?” ʸ

He looks at her hand.

There is an answer with the shape of a promise to know whenever she is frightened. To find her wherever she goes. To put one more safeguard between her and every possible future.

He can hear how exhausting it would be to wear that promise.

“A way to ask,” he says finally. ᴹ

Yuta looks up from the page.

“For what?” ʸᵘ

“Attention. A moment. Come and sit with me when you can.” ᴹ

Yuka's smile becomes quieter.

“Both ways?” ʸ

“If you want.” ᴹ

She holds out his own hand beside hers.

“Then two rings.” ʸ

* * *

Ciel helps him describe what they already have before anybody begins naming something new.

They can reach each other through an existing connection. It has limits. They have lived through a place which could prevent a message from becoming a voice. A ring will not make that history false merely because he wants to give her something beautiful.

He says that aloud.

Yuka listens without withdrawing her hand.

“I wasn't asking it to beat every door in the world,” she says. ʸ

Mau breathes out.

“Good.” ᴹ

They discuss a small request along the connection when it is available. A touch, by choice. A reply, by choice. No view through the other person's eyes. No location secretly offered with the answer. The ring can remain an ordinary band when either wants it to.

“And if it doesn't work?” Mai asks. ᴹᵃ

Yuka looks down at the two circles.

“Then we still have wedding rings.” ʸ

Mau turns toward her.

She squeezes his hand beneath the table.

* * *

Wakana comes over when they begin deciding what the bands should feel like.

His questions are about their hands. What catches while working. Which edges become annoying after being touched all day. Marin adds a little change which makes the two rings look related without making them identical.

Mai listens, changes the drawing and asks Yuka to turn her hand again.

Mau watches her begin planning the precision she wants instead of the amount of suffering an object ought to cost.

“I'll tell you before I start,” she says to him. ᴹᵃ

“You can just—” ᴹ

Mai looks at him.

He stops.

“Yes. Please tell me.” ᴹ

Yuta starts smiling into his cup.

* * *

They leave the workshop with a drawing and work still to do.

Nobody has made a new technique by promising one. Mau has a precise, modest thing to discuss with Ciel and Noise, and two people who will tell him if the practical part is becoming uncomfortable to wear.

Yuka takes his arm on the way home.

“You looked relieved,” she says. ʸ

“I thought I'd promised you several decades of research.” ᴹ

“You did promise a lifetime.” ʸ

He looks down at her.

She has left enough amusement in the sentence that he can hear it properly.

“I intend to use the rest for other things too,” he says. ᴹ

Yuka stops walking, catches his sleeve and brings him close enough to kiss.

“Excellent,” she says afterwards. “I have plans.” ʸ

He goes home wanting to hear them.
