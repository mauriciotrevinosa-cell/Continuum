*When: Umaru announces that the Fort has prepared its terms, Yuka asks who surrendered.*

“That's what we're establishing.” ᵁ

Mau sits down beside her.

Ori has a page. Chloe has another. Milim has apparently been given responsibility for neither, which she considers an insult to her ability to hold paper.

“I can do something important,” she says. ᴹⁱ

“You can walk,” Yuka answers. ʸ

Milim looks toward the route outside.

“All the way?” ᴹⁱ

“All the way.” ʸ

She appears willing to consider it.

* * *

The terms concern where the children can stand, who carries the small things and whether the people getting married are allowed to disappear into an adult conversation before the Fort has eaten.

Seiko, listening from the next table, says that last one seems enforceable.

Hakari asks who is writing down the exceptions.

Umaru points at Ori's page.

Ori looks down at it.

“This is the drawing.” ᴼ

“An illustrated agreement,” Umaru says. ᵁ

Yuka leans across the table to see.

The drawing shows the tree, the people and a shape near Mau's foot with ears enormous enough to have acquired a tradition of their own.

Mau smiles.

“Ear has a place.” ᴹ

Ori nods seriously.

“He needs to see.” ᴼ

* * *

They choose the little jobs together.

Nobody must stand still through a whole ceremony because an adult thinks a row of children looks tidy. Somebody can carry the flowers already chosen. Somebody can help show a guest the correct route. If a person would rather stay beside Shizue or Frieren, that counts too.

Chloe wants to walk with Ori.

Suika wants to know whether she can sit after the walking.

Mau tells her he would consider sitting an excellent use of a place prepared for it.

Milim raises her hand.

“Can I carry you?” ᴹⁱ

Yuka turns toward Mau before he can answer.

“You're encouraging her.” ʸ

“I haven't said anything.” ᴹ

“Your face did.” ʸ

He laughs, and Milim spends the next minute arguing that the idea would improve the speed of the entire ceremony.

* * *

Rem comes to ask whether they want something to drink before the conversation becomes a second meal by accident.

Umaru stands to help her.

Rem reaches for her hand to draw her around the edge of the crowded table. It is a small movement, one they have made often enough that nobody nearby needs a reason for it.

Umaru looks down at the contact.

For a second she forgets what she was going to say.

Rem looks back.

“This way?” ᴿᵉ

“Yes,” Umaru answers, a little late. ᵁ

They go together.

Mau sees the pause and leaves it alone.

* * *

When the cups arrive, Yuka asks whether the terms have been met.

Umaru examines Ori's drawing, consults Chloe on a point nobody else heard and turns toward the couple.

“The Fort accepts the marriage.” ᵁ

“Very generous,” Yuka says. ʸ

“Pending dessert.” ᵁ

Mau lifts his cup.

“I thought there wasn't a toll.” ᴹ

“That was travel,” Umaru answers. “This is recognition.” ᵁ

Seiko begins laughing at the next table.

Yuka covers her face with one hand.

Mau looks toward the solemn little government assembled around them, finds Ori trying not to laugh because that might affect the dignity of the agreement, and accepts the terms.
