The house has been discussing a gift which will take the newly married couple out of the house.

Not forever. Not to rescue somebody or investigate a road which has become too dangerous for anyone else. Two weeks, or something close enough to two weeks that Mau will have to stop packing useful reasons to return early.

Shion has made a list of places people know how to reach. Shuna has asked which of those places would let them arrive without being expected to attend a dinner for somebody else's government.

Shizue has asked whether the gift contains any time in which the couple can choose nothing.

That question has removed several impressive suggestions.

Elmesia listens to the explanation and smiles.

“Five nights,” she says. ᴱˡ

“Where?” ᴿ

She tells him about the island hotel whose arrangements have survived the changed roads better than most. The harbour is working. Boats still make the short journeys around the sheltered coast. There are rooms which can look at the sea without requiring their occupants to discuss the sea's strategic value.

“You could learn from the last part,” she adds. ᴱˡ

They discuss dates rather than accepting a beautiful idea which cannot be reached.

The wedding comes first. Several days at home afterwards. The couple can travel by Kaisel along a route which has been checked, with stops chosen for the journey instead of one long flight intended to prove they can endure it.

The hotel has a period available within that plan.

Elmesia offers to arrange the stay as her gift once the two have said they want it. The meals and ordinary activities included in it will be set out clearly. There is no reception in which Mau must appear as the guardian of a new headquarters to repay the hospitality.
