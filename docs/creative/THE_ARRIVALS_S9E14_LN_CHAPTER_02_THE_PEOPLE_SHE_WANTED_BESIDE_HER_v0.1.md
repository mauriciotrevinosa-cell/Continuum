*When: Maki asks who Yuka wants beside her at the beginning of the walk, Yuka looks at all three of them.*

Mai raises an eyebrow.

“That's going to be crowded.” ᴹᵃ

“Then don't take up so much room.” ʸ

Yuta laughs before he can make the answer sound more diplomatic.

They are sitting in the place which has become easy to reach from Yuka's own home. She came to ask them, not to receive another answer about whether she may marry Mau. That answer belongs to her. This question belongs to the people she would like to have with her while she does.

“Grandpa,” she says. “Granny. Mai.” ʸ

Maki watches her for a moment.

“All right.” ᴹᵏ

Yuta looks down at his hands and smiles before looking up again.

“I'd like that.” ʸᵘ

Mai waits until the room has had its moment.

“I suppose someone should make sure you actually reach him.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka throws the small cloth beside her at Mai's shoulder.

* * *

The fitting begins with Yuka being told she can object.

She objects immediately to the idea that she might have forgotten how.

Marin laughs and asks her to turn.

The dress has come from the sketches Yuka chose, changed by the decisions she made while the village was still becoming a place they could live in. Wakana has left room for her to move. The sleeves have stopped doing the thing she disliked. There is a fastening she can reach herself.

She tries walking.

Stops.

Tries again, more quickly.

Maki watches the second attempt with a look which makes Yuka laugh before asking what it means.

“You look like yourself,” Maki says. ᴹᵏ

Yuka looks down at the dress.

That was what she asked for.

Hearing it from Maki makes it harder to answer.

* * *

Mai notices the change first.

She gives Marin a small look, reaches for the part of the fitting which still needs an ordinary decision and asks Yuka whether the edge is going to annoy her when she sits.

Yuka sits.

Finds the annoyance.

Begins explaining it with enough indignation to return to the room.

Wakana changes the pin.

Maki says nothing about the tears which did not quite arrive.

When Yuka stands again, she catches Mai looking at the finished line.

“What?” she asks. ʸ

“I was checking whether you can still walk between all your escorts.” ᴹᵃ

“You can walk behind us.” ʸ

“Now she tells me.” ᴹᵃ

Marin has to put down what she is holding before she laughs too hard to use it.

* * *

While Wakana checks the alteration, Yuka looks toward Maki.

“Does it ever stop sounding strange?” ʸ

Maki glances up.

“What?” ᴹᵏ

“Husband.” ʸ

Mai looks very interested in the answer.

Yuka feels herself smile before she can explain why she asked. The word has been waiting somewhere inside the planning, easy to overlook beside the date and the dress and the people who need an answer. Now she can picture using it to find him in an ordinary conversation.

My husband is outside.

My husband forgot where he put the cups.

She starts laughing at herself.

Maki watches her.

“You get used to saying it,” she answers. “It can still catch you.” ᴹᵏ

“Even after that long?” ʸ

Maki looks toward the closed door beyond which Yuta has been making a serious effort to be patient.

“Sometimes especially after that long.” ᴹᵏ

Yuka grows quiet for a moment.

“I want that,” she says. ʸ

Maki takes her hand, careful of the pin Wakana is moving, and squeezes it once.

“Then keep finding things you want to do together.” ᴹᵏ

Mai clears her throat.

“Preferably some which don't involve everyone else having to finish a dress.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka looks toward her.

“You wanted to be here.” ʸ

“I'm beginning to question the number of witnesses.” ᴹᵃ

The laugh which passes between them makes Yuka feel more like herself than standing perfectly still ever could.

* * *

Yuta waits outside the fitting room until Yuka asks him to come in.

He takes one step through the door and forgets whatever remark he had been preparing to make about being kept waiting.

Yuka looks toward him.

The expression on his face makes her smile before it makes her nervous.

“Grandpa?” ʸ

He blinks.

“Yes.” ʸᵘ

“You're staring.” ʸ

“I know.” ʸᵘ

Maki touches his sleeve.

He reaches for her hand without looking away from Yuka.

For a moment she sees the life the two of them have brought here together. The things they remember which their young faces do not explain. The place she had inside that life before finding another one she wanted to make for herself.

“I'm happy,” she tells them. ʸ

Yuta nods.

“I can see.” ʸᵘ

* * *

She leaves the dress with the people finishing it and goes home in Mau's hoodie.

Mai walks with her as far as the turn.

For a while neither speaks.

Then Yuka looks at her.

“Thank you.” ʸ

“For agreeing not to walk directly on top of you?” ᴹᵃ

“For earlier.” ʸ

Mai's expression softens almost invisibly.

“You were going to cry. They were going to cry. I wanted to finish before dinner.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka starts laughing.

Mai looks pleased with the success and lets her keep holding her arm for the rest of the short walk.

* * *

Mau is at the table when Yuka comes in.

He looks toward the door as if he has been trying not to do it too often.

“Good?” he asks. ᴹ

She puts her hands on his shoulders.

“Very.” ʸ

“Can I ask anything?” ᴹ

“You can ask whether I'm hungry.” ʸ

He looks at the plate he has kept covered for her.

“Are you hungry?” ᴹ

“Terribly.” ʸ

She kisses him, sits beside him and begins telling him everything about the afternoon which he is allowed to know.

He discovers he is happy to hear all of it.
