*When: Hinata asks whether the town has moved anything since her last visit, Anko says, “Only the difficult people.”*

Hinata looks past her.

Mau raises a hand from beside Rimuru.

“Still here.” ᴹ

“A failed policy,” Anko says. ᴬⁿ

Hinata laughs and comes close enough to kiss her before picking the bag up again. The route to the guest inn is familiar now. Nobody has to explain that the room belongs to her for the visit or that she can leave it to spend time with someone she came to see.

Behind her, Testarossa has already begun smiling at something Souei has chosen not to say.

* * *

Benimaru arrives with Rigurd and the answer from Elmesia.

She cannot leave her own work for the ceremony. She has sent her good wishes, confirmed the five nights the couple chose and added a line Rimuru reads twice before deciding whether he should allow Mau to see it.

Yuka takes the letter first.

Her face lights up.

“No official receptions,” she reads. ʸ

Mau looks toward Rimuru.

“Was that in doubt?” ᴹ

“Apparently enough to put in writing.” ᴿ

Rigurd begins explaining how well several things have continued without Rimuru standing over them every morning. Rimuru listens with a relief he tries unsuccessfully to hide inside sensible questions.

Mau leaves him to hear the whole account.

* * *

Veldora reaches the group after a conversation near the approach has become loud enough to announce him without assistance.

He looks from the tree toward the people waiting beneath the gallery, spots Milim and immediately begins explaining that the event has acquired proper distinction now he is here.

Milim tells him she has been here much longer.

The argument is proceeding admirably when Rimuru arrives.

“No fighting.” ᴿ

Both turn toward him.

He points at the ordinary ground beneath their feet.

“At the wedding. Before the wedding. While discussing how well you could fight at the wedding.” ᴿ

Mau begins laughing.

Rimuru turns the finger toward him without looking.

“You too.” ᴿ

“I haven't offered.” ᴹ

“An excellent beginning.” ᴿ

* * *

The arrivals have the rooms and dates they agreed before setting out.

Five nights, with the next review before leaving on the sixth morning. Those whose work requires a different journey can say so before the household invents a permanent bed for them. Tempest has people continuing its ordinary business; nobody has emptied the city to prove the wedding matters.

Shion meets Benimaru at the common turn and takes him to see a piece of the place she wants to show him herself. Shuna follows after finishing the conversation she was already in.

Diablo comes to greet the visitors with the ease of someone whose own room has become part of the house rather than a reason to stand outside Rimuru's door.

Rimuru notices.

He does not have to explain it before enjoying it.

* * *

Shoko catches Mau later, when the excitement of greeting people has begun to make him forget how long he has been on his feet.

She asks how the sleep has gone since the last check.

He tells her the part which improved and the part which still takes a moment when he wakes. The body has kept the ordinary movement he has been practising. The familiar old impairments remain; the fresh injuries have not reopened because a wedding acquired a date.

She makes him sit before continuing.

“I'm not withdrawing the wedding,” she says when she sees his face. ˢᵒ

He lets out the breath she correctly suspected he was holding.

“I didn't think you were.” ᴹ

“You have a very expressive lie.” ˢᵒ

He starts smiling.

She finishes the check and tells him to let the visitors carry their own interesting stories to a chair.

* * *

At dinner, Hinata asks Yuka how she feels.

Yuka considers the question with enough seriousness that Mau stops listening to Veldora's neighbouring account of something which may have become more impressive in the retelling.

“Happy,” she says. “A little impatient. I keep remembering things I wanted to ask somebody.” ʸ

“Ask,” Hinata answers. ᴴⁱ

Yuka turns toward Mau.

“Did you find your shoes?” ʸ

The table becomes interested immediately.

He looks from one expectant face to another.

“I know where they are.” ᴹ

Gojo leans back beside Shoko.

“He's ready.” ᴳ

Mau decides to eat before the witnesses become more helpful.
