*When: Gojo opens the door, Mau is already wearing the shoes.*

Gojo looks down.

Then up.

“Disappointing.” ᴳ

Mau steps inside.

“You had one responsibility.” ᴹ

“And you've robbed me of the achievement.” ᴳ

Shoko is at the table with a cup. She looks toward Mau, takes in the expression on his face and points to the food beside her.

“Eat before he starts helping.” ˢᵒ

Gojo closes the door with the dignity of a man whose household has failed to appreciate his contribution.

Mau sits down.

* * *

Yuta arrives while Mau is trying to decide how much breakfast is enough to prevent everyone asking whether he has had breakfast.

The answer appears to be slightly more than he would choose under observation.

Yuta gives him the smaller portion and tells Gojo that the groom is allowed to be nervous without receiving a lecture about circulation.

“I wasn't lecturing.” ᴳ

Shoko looks toward him.

Gojo changes the subject to the coat.

Mau is grateful enough to put the rest of the food in his mouth before anybody can ask him a question.

* * *

At home, Yuka has misplaced nothing except the sentence she thought she would say when Maki arrived.

It disappears the moment she sees her grandmother at the door.

Maki waits, smiling slightly.

Yuka goes to her and hugs her before the room can become busy.

Mai steps around both of them with the things Marin asked her to bring.

“An efficient beginning,” she says. ᴹᵃ

Yuka laughs into Maki's shoulder.

The room becomes easier to stand in.

* * *

Marin has them put the dress on before adding the last small piece which would otherwise catch somewhere inconvenient.

She asks Yuka to move, to sit, to lift her arms as though being married will not prevent her from reaching for something she wants. Each question returns Yuka to a body doing an ordinary thing rather than an image somebody else has prepared to admire.

Mai fixes the one edge which tries to argue.

Maki smooths Yuka's hair once, then lets her decide what she wants to change.

Yuka looks at herself.

She likes what she chose.

That makes the next breath harder and happier at the same time.

* * *

Mau has brought the wrong version of his page.

He discovers it after Gojo has finished arguing that the coat looks better left the way Wakana said it should be worn, an argument which Yuta considers an impressive way to claim credit for listening to somebody else.

The page in Mau's pocket is the long one.

He stares at it.

Gojo leans over his shoulder.

“We'll need more chairs.” ᴳ

Mau closes his eyes.

Yuta laughs once, then asks where the shorter one is.

“Beside our bed.” ᴹ

“Then ask.” ʸᵘ

Mau reaches for the familiar connection.

Yuka answers before he can finish apologising.

*I saw it. Mai has it with the rings. You can stop looking like you've lost the wedding.* ʸ

He looks toward the two men waiting for him to breathe.

*Thank you.* ᴹ

*You have to help me when I forget something too.* ʸ

He smiles before he replies.

*I will.* ᴹ

* * *

Rimuru comes by long enough to check what they actually need.

Gojo says better witnesses to his excellent work. Yuta says nothing which can be solved by encouraging him. Mau asks whether Yuka is all right and hears the answer from the person who just saw her.

“Happy. Busy. She told me to make sure nobody brings you over too early to stand outside your own door looking tragic.” ᴿ

Gojo considers that.

“We could give him something to hold.” ᴳ

“We are not accessorising him,” Yuta says. ʸᵘ

Mau starts laughing before he can keep the sound dignified.

Rimuru looks at him.

The expression which follows makes the laughter go quiet.

“Ready?” he asks. ᴿ

Mau takes a breath.

Looks at the people he asked to be with him.

“Yes.” ᴹ

* * *

Yuka stands at the bedroom door with her page in one hand.

Maki is beside her. Mai has the rings. Yuta will meet them after bringing Mau to the agreed turn. Nobody needs to hand her from one owner to another for the walk to mean that she has people willing to accompany her.

She hears the little household sounds beyond the door and thinks of all the times this route has simply taken them to dinner.

Today it will take her to her husband.

Marin asks whether she wants another moment.

Yuka looks down at the silver band already on her hand.

Then up at the people waiting.

“No,” she says, smiling. “I want to see him.” ʸ
