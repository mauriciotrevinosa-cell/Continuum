*When: Mau reaches their door, he forgets to check whether he has remembered everything.*

Yuka is standing on the other side of it.

For a moment the thought has no words beyond her name. He can see the line of her dress, the hand holding the little flowers, the way she has lifted her chin as if she has been preparing to be calm and would like him to assist with that effort.

He does not assist.

She sees his face and begins smiling before either of them has managed to speak.

“Hi,” she says. ʸ

He takes a breath.

It does nothing useful.

“Hi.” ᴹ

* * *

Gojo stops beside him.

Behind Yuka, Maki has one hand resting lightly against her back. Yuta moves to her other side. Mai looks from the bride to the groom and appears entirely satisfied with the amount of difficulty the afternoon has produced so far.

Yuka takes the smallest step forward.

Mau's attention follows it with an intensity which makes her blush.

“You can say something,” she tells him. ʸ

He nods.

Then has to try again.

“You look—” ᴹ

Yuka waits.

The word beautiful feels true and insufficient in a way he is suddenly willing to accept. He does not need to invent a new language before she can hear him.

“Beautiful,” he says. ᴹ

Her smile changes.

“Thank you.” ʸ

He can hear his own breathing. The music beginning farther along the route. Somebody whispering a correction to someone who was apparently about to stand in the wrong place.

He is here for all of it.

* * *

Mai hands him the folded page.

He looks down at it, then up at her.

“Thank you.” ᴹ

“Try this version,” she says. ᴹᵃ

Yuka begins laughing.

The sound breaks the little spell which had kept everyone standing at the door waiting for Mau to discover what to do with his hands. He offers one to her. She takes it, her fingers warm and familiar beneath the strangeness of the day.

Gojo touches Mau's shoulder once.

There is no joke in the gesture.

Mau turns toward him and finds the ordinary smile waiting, open enough that he can answer it without having to prove he is all right first.

“Let's go,” Gojo says. ᴳ

Mau looks back at Yuka.

She nods.

* * *

The walk begins at their own door, as she wanted.

Yuta and Maki remain beside her for the first part. Mai keeps close enough that Yuka can turn toward her without searching. Gojo walks beside Mau until the route widens and the family can make room for the next people joining from their own homes.

There is no rush to reach the end.

Bocchi's music carries the beginning quietly enough that they can hear someone laugh. Kita watches her, waits for the place they rehearsed and joins the sound. The piece makes space instead of filling every part of it.

Mau remembers telling Bocchi he could see them walking.

He had not imagined Yuka's hand tightening like this when she recognises the first notes.

He turns toward her.

She is already looking at him.

* * *

They pass Ori with Frieren.

Ori is standing exactly where she can see, with the serious effort of someone who has accepted a responsibility which ought not to be made smaller by excitement. The effort lasts until Yuka smiles at her.

She waves.

Frieren lets her.

Mau waves back with the hand he can spare, and Ori's happiness becomes large enough that he hears the little breath Yuka takes beside him.

Fern is near Frieren, Stark at her shoulder. Neither has been given so much work that they must watch the day from the place where something is being carried. Himmel smiles when Mau looks toward him.

The small circle around the tree has become a place people chose to stand, close enough that a face can remain a face.

Mau is grateful for every argument Yuka had about that part.

* * *

At the common turn, Umaru stands beside Rem.

Her other hand is occupied with making Milim remember how much space she is meant to leave. Milim remembers for three whole steps before seeing Yuka and becoming incapable of keeping the admiration inside her own body.

“She's so pretty,” she says, loudly enough to stop the sentence from being private. ᴹⁱ

Yuka laughs.

Mau looks at Milim.

“I know.” ᴹ

Rem smiles at him, then gently moves the delighted dragon far enough from the route that nobody has to negotiate an obstacle during the ceremony.

Umaru releases a very dignified breath.

The Fort has fulfilled its agreement.

* * *

Inside, Ciel takes the place DIO has offered.

The view of the ceremony rests beyond their table. Ashborn listens without being asked what a wedding means to someone who has lived as long as he has. Noise stands for part of the walk, then sits when it realises sitting will not make the people outside disappear.

“Friends,” it says quietly. ᴺ

DIO looks toward the gathered house.

“A great many.” ᴰⁱ

Ciel watches Mau turn toward Yuka again.

For once she does not have to calculate the distance he needs to survive before he can rest.

She lets herself simply see him happy.

* * *

Yuta slows when they reach the place beneath the branches.

Yuka turns toward him. For a moment she is looking at her grandfather instead of trying to hold the whole wedding in her attention.

He takes her free hand and kisses her forehead.

Maki embraces her next, carefully enough to spare the work in the dress and closely enough that the care does not become distance. Mai touches her arm, says something which makes Yuka laugh through the first threat of tears and goes to the place prepared for the rings.

Mau waits beside her until the little exchanges are finished.

When Yuta looks toward him, he does not receive a warning disguised as a blessing.

He receives a hand against his shoulder and a smile he has known through much less forgiving afternoons.

Mau holds the gaze long enough to answer it.

Then turns toward the woman beside him.

* * *

Rimuru stands where they asked him to stand.

The music comes to the ending Bocchi chose. Nobody fills the quiet immediately. Yuka has time to look around the little curve of family and friends, to see the people who made the day possible and the people whose presence was all they wanted to give.

She finds Shoko near Gojo.

Shizue beside the children.

The faces she wanted close enough to keep noticing.

Mau watches her see them.

When she looks back, he holds out both hands.

She puts hers in them.

Rimuru waits until they have found each other properly.

Then begins.
