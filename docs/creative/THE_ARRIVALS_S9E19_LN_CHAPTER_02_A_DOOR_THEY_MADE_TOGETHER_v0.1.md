*When: Beru tells them the gift is ready, Mau realises the cloth from the workshop has finally acquired permission to move.*

He looks toward Yuka.

She is already getting up.

“I knew you were making something,” she tells Beru. ʸ

The ant makes a sound of such wounded secrecy that Igris turns his head slightly away.

Bellion stands beside the covered object with the patient composure of someone who has finished the part which required everyone to agree what goes where. Kamish has chosen a place from which to enjoy being proved correct about the tree.

Wakana is nearby. Mai pretends she has merely stopped to see why everybody is standing.

Mau offers his hand to Yuka.

They go together.

* * *

The cloth comes away.

For a moment neither of them speaks.

It is a little home made of wood. Not their apartment copied so exactly that they would have to admire the accuracy instead of looking at it. A place with a tree at its heart, paths which lead back toward the same shared space and a small door set where someone approaching would know how to come in.

The door is open.

Mau leans closer.

He can see the small differences between pieces worked by different hands. The line which has been corrected without being disguised. A little place at the common centre where somebody has decided people ought to sit close enough to be heard.

Yuka touches the edge carefully.

“You made this?” she asks. ʸ

Beru straightens.

“Together, my queen.” ᴮ

* * *

Bellion explains the part which began as an argument.

Igris supplies one correction. Kamish supplies another with considerably less restraint. Beru objects that both are reducing the significance of the door, which has remained a central achievement through every stage of the work.

Yuka moves the tiny door gently with one finger.

It closes.

Opens again.

“It's a very good door,” she says. ʸ

Beru's expression becomes unbearable.

Mau has to put a hand over his mouth before the laugh reaches him too loudly.

Mai catches the movement.

“We checked the hinge more than once,” she says. ᴹᵃ

Wakana looks down, smiling.

* * *

Mau asks about the empty part beside one of the little routes.

Bellion looks at Yuka before answering.

“You have both spoken about wanting room for a future,” he says. “We didn't know its shape.” ᴮᵉˡ

Yuka's hand stops against the wood.

The little space does not contain a figure someone else has decided belongs to them. It waits beside the home they already chose, ordinary and unfinished enough to permit an answer later.

She looks toward Mau.

He is looking at the same place.

For a moment the great army which could have been asked to move the earth has given them something small enough to keep in their hands.

Mau swallows.

“Thank you,” he says. ᴹ

The words sound too small.

He says them again anyway.

* * *

Yuka embraces Beru before he has prepared a dignified response.

The response fails completely.

Igris steps closer out of habit, sees that the ant is managing the first impossible second of being hugged by the person he has spent so much time trying to guard, and leaves him to it.

Mau thanks Bellion, then Igris, then turns toward the dragon who is attempting to look as though he contributed nothing sentimental enough to be discussed.

“The tree?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Kamish lifts his head.

“It required correction.” ᴷᵐ

“I can see.” ᴹ

The dragon looks toward the miniature with deep satisfaction.

Yuka finally lets Beru go and asks whether they can put it in their sitting room, where everyone coming through will be able to see what they made.

The agreement is immediate.

* * *

They carry the gift home themselves.

Slowly. Yuka has insisted that holding the other side counts as helping and that Mau does not get to claim the entire object because he is standing nearest. He accepts the ruling with sufficient grace to make her suspicious.

Rimuru opens the shared door ahead of them.

Shizue moves one ordinary thing to make room, then waits while they choose where the little home belongs.

Yuka opens its tiny door again after putting it down.

Mau watches her.

“It can stay open,” she says. ʸ

He nods.

* * *

Inside, the view of the celebration has quietened.

DIO asks whether they should close it now. Ashborn has already turned toward his own space after giving a small, deliberate acknowledgement when Mau thanked him for listening. Ciel chooses to let her local shape go and returns to the familiar closeness of her voice.

Noise looks toward the impression of the gift one last time.

“Made,” it says. ᴺ

*They did,* Mau answers. ᴹ

“Friends.” ᴺ

*Yes.* ᴹ

The view closes by agreement.

Nothing private from the next room is offered in its place.

* * *

Mau finds Yuka standing before the little home when he returns his attention fully to the room.

She reaches for his hand without looking away.

“They left us somewhere to put what we haven't decided yet,” she says. ʸ

He studies the space beside the little path.

Then her face.

“We can take our time.” ᴹ

Yuka turns toward him, smiles and lifts his hand to her mouth.

“Yes,” she says. “We can.” ʸ
