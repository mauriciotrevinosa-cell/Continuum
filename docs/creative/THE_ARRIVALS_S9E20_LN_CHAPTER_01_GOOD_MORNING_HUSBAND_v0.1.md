*When: the last difficult fastening comes free, Yuka says she is grateful to have married someone with helpfully patient hands.*

Mau looks at his fingers.

The two old stiff ones have made the task slower than he wanted. She noticed and turned until he could manage the angle more comfortably, without making his hands a problem the evening had to stop and solve.

“An excellent reason,” he says. ᴹ

She glances over her shoulder.

“One of several.” ʸ

He smiles and steps back while she takes the ordinary clothes she left ready.

Outside their bedroom, Rimuru is talking quietly to Shizue. The little wooden home has its place in the sitting room. Someone has shut the shared door against the last of the celebration's noise without making the apartment feel empty.

Mau takes off the coat and hangs it where Yuka has already told him it belongs.

For once, he remembers the place.

* * *

When she comes back, he is sitting on the bed looking at the ring.

Yuka stops beside him.

“Still there?” she asks. ʸ

He turns toward her.

“It seems quite determined.” ᴹ

She takes his hand and looks at the plain silver beside the old marks on his skin. Then turns her own so the two new bands catch the same little change in the light.

He does not feel a signal simply because she is near.

He feels her fingers.

That is enough.

* * *

They talk about the day while they still remember which parts happened in which order.

Yuka tells him about Yuta seeing the dress. Mau tells her about Gojo's outrage at the shoes. She admits she almost lost her place when he said he wanted the little days. He tells her that he stopped understanding the page when she called him her husband.

“You answered the question,” she says. ʸ

“I was extremely well prepared for that one.” ᴹ

She leans close and kisses him.

He answers with a hand at her waist, draws her nearer when she wants it and lets the long happiness of the day settle somewhere quieter than applause.

For a while neither speaks.

When they draw apart, she rests her forehead against his.

“Tired?” she asks. ʸ

“Very.” ᴹ

“Me too.” ʸ

He brushes a loose piece of hair away from her face.

They smile at each other with the relief of people who do not have to make the night perform another ceremony before being allowed to sleep.

* * *

Yuka settles beside him in the familiar way.

There are things the two of them want to explore and conversations they will have when they are rested enough to give them the attention they deserve. Tonight, the warmth of her shoulder against him is its own choice. She kisses his hand, draws the blanket over them both and tells him he is not allowed to float away with it before she has had a proper night.

“A rule for married life?” ᴹ

“A deeply necessary one.” ʸ

He laughs quietly.

*Good night,* Ciel says when his attention turns inward. ᶜⁱ

He can hear the care in how little else she asks of him.

*Good night.* ᴹ

Yuka's breathing begins to quieten.

He closes his eyes beside her.

* * *

Morning arrives with Yuka watching him.

He opens his eyes, takes the moment he needs to recognise the room and finds her smiling rather than waiting anxiously for him to hurry through it.

“Good morning, husband.” ʸ

The word reaches him before the rest of the day.

He smiles.

“Good morning, wife.” ᴹ

Yuka puts her face against his shoulder for a second, laughing into the warmth there.

“It still sounds ridiculous.” ʸ

“You started.” ᴹ

She looks up.

“I intend to continue.” ʸ

* * *

They stay in bed long enough that Rimuru eventually knocks.

“Breakfast?” ᴿ

Yuka lifts her head.

“Is it still breakfast?” ʸ

There is a pause outside the door.

“The category is under discussion.” ᴿ

Mau begins laughing.

Shizue's voice reaches them from farther away.

“We saved you something.” ˢᶻ

Yuka looks toward Mau and lifts an eyebrow.

He accepts the invitation to surrender before another person has to come and inspect whether marriage has made them unable to find the shared room.

* * *

The little wooden door is open when they come out.

Rimuru has left room for both of them. Shizue has put the saved food where they can reach it without reorganising the table around their arrival. Through the shared door, the village is having a morning of its own.

Yuka sits beside Mau.

For a few minutes nobody asks them for the wedding's official conclusion. Rimuru tells them something Veldora said after they left which may have become less dignified in the retelling. Shizue corrects one detail and makes it worse.

Mau passes Yuka the thing she is reaching for.

She thanks him with the small touch she has always used when there is no need to turn the gesture into a conversation.

They eat breakfast in the home they chose, wearing the rings they gave each other yesterday.
