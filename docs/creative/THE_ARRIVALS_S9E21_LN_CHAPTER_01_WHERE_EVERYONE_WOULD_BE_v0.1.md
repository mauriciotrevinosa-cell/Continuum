Rimuru accepts the improvement.

DIO chooses where she would like to be after the visitors have gone.

Mau gives her the choice in the shared room inside, with the length of the journey and the way back made clear. She can remain in the refuge if she wants to travel. She can also spend the two weeks in the ordinary house, inside the protected ground, with people who know she is there.

She looks toward her private door.

“I think I'd like a window I can open myself,” she says. ᴰⁱ

“We can ask.” ᴹ

“This will do very well.” ᴰⁱ
