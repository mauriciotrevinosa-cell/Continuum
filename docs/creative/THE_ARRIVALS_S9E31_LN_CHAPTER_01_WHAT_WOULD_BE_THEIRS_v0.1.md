*When: B+123, afternoon. DIO has put a small plant by her guest-room window. Mau looks at it long enough that she begins defending the purchase before he has said anything.*

“It was very small,” she says. ᴰⁱ

“I can see that.” ᴹ

“And it needed a better place.” ᴰⁱ

Yuka leans past him to look.

“Are you keeping it?” ʸ

DIO glances toward the little pot.

Then back at them.

“If I can.” ᴰⁱ

* * *

She has liked the ordinary room.

Mau asks rather than inferring it from the plant. DIO tells them about the window, a conversation with Coco's mother and the inconvenience of wanting tea after everyone who knew which jar to open had gone somewhere else. She found the right jar eventually. It had apparently taken longer than she considers reasonable for someone with her former responsibilities.

Yuka smiles into the cup she has been given.

The room remains available for now. Jinshi has asked only that they tell him which arrangement she wants to keep. DIO chooses to stay a little longer, inside the protected ground, with the refuge still there if she wants the quieter place.

Mau agrees to pass the answer on.

He likes that there is a pot somebody will need to water when she changes her mind.

* * *

The question becomes harder to ask once he is holding tea.

Yuka notices him finding the words, puts her cup down and begins for both of them.

“We've been talking about a family.” ʸ

DIO looks at her, then at Mau.

He adds the important part before her expression can get any more interested.

“Later.” ᴹ

DIO leans back a little.

“All right.” ᴰⁱ

Mau looks down at his hands.

“I don't know what I'd give a child,” he says. “What belongs to the body. What belongs to the things Noise built. Whether any of it would know where to stop.” ᴹ

DIO listens without beginning an answer before he has finished. The first easy relief he feels comes from that: she has understood that he is asking about someone who may exist, not requesting another improvement to himself.

“Would they get all of this?” he asks at last. ᴹ

He has no useful gesture for the army, the rooms inside him, the hearts and the structures which are still unfinished. He touches his own chest instead.

Yuka takes his other hand.

* * *

DIO asks permission to look at the boundary of the constructed routes with Ciel's help.

Mau agrees to that part. Ciel comes to the edge of the conversation through him, names what they are checking and leaves the things which have nothing to do with the question alone. There is no child to examine. No new life waiting to be defined by a diagram. Only the present structure in a man who wants to understand whether he might accidentally turn someone else's beginning into an extension of his own.

DIO is quiet for a little while.

Then looks at him again.

“The things built into you do not have to become a child's starting point.” ᴰⁱ

Mau does not move.

“You can stop that?” ᴹ

“I can help keep the acquired routes with you. Make the separation deliberate before either of you chooses to try.” ᴰⁱ

Yuka's grip changes.

“The army?” she asks. ʸ

“His,” DIO says. “The things being constructed, his. That is something we can work on.” ᴰⁱ

Mau exhales slowly. He had not realised quite how tightly he was holding the idea of a child born already listening to doors it could not close.

DIO lets the relief reach him before explaining the rest.

“I can't tell you everything about a body whose beginning I don't know. And what Yuka was born with isn't something I should remove because one possibility frightens us.” ᴰⁱ

Yuka nods.

Her own cursed energy. The possibility of a technique. A child whose life would still be their own, including parts neither parent could choose in advance.

“I don't want somebody else instead,” Mau says quietly. “I just don't want them to have to carry me.” ᴹ

DIO reaches across the little table and rests her hand near his.

“Then that is the part we'll keep working on.” ᴰⁱ

* * *

They do not begin the work that afternoon.

They have asked one question and received an answer with edges they can understand. DIO can help with the acquired structures. Ciel will be there when they choose a careful review with Noise. Shoko will keep helping them understand what the actual body does. None of those things has turned their choice to wait into a countdown someone else owns.

Yuka asks whether DIO wants anything brought from the common room before they go.

DIO looks toward the plant, then at the cup.

“The name of the tea would be helpful.” ᴰⁱ

Mau begins laughing before she has finished explaining the jar problem.

* * *

Outside, Yuka keeps his hand in hers.

He is walking a little more quietly than usual. She gives him until the next turn before asking whether he is all right.

He nods.

“I was trying not to imagine someone being born with everything I can't sort out,” he says. ᴹ

She stops beside him.

“They'd have us,” she says. ʸ

He looks at her.

“And we'd have a whole house arguing about how to help.” ʸ

He laughs softly, the picture arriving with enough familiar voices to make it impossible not to.

She moves closer and puts both arms around him.

“But first I get you for a while.” ʸ

His hands settle against her back.

“A demanding marriage.” ᴹ

“You keep saying that.” ʸ

“I keep agreeing to it.” ᴹ

She kisses him there on the path, with the tea question still waiting and no reason to hurry toward a decision they have already made together.
