## Chapter 4 — A Better Reason to Ask

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1 (2026-11-03)
**Volume:** 57
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S9E31
**Continuity state:** B+123 late afternoon, shorter Rem/M familial window. Builds on actual B39 Shoko/Mau confidence and many chosen subsequent Rem/U hand/outing beats, NOT first discovery/reset. Rem wants deliberate just-two invitation and asks possible help finding food/place, not matchmaker assigning Umaru consent. M retains confidentiality/no telling U Rem feelings. No Subaru, age invented, forced pair/permission from brother or new household service duty. Own time wanted, not debt.

*When: Rem asks whether Mau remembers the game in Tempest, he says he remembers the game and appears to remember losing with unnecessary clarity.*

She laughs before he has finished defending himself.

They have found two places beside the common door while the afternoon finishes its ordinary work around them. Rem has a cup she brought for herself. Mau has the one Yuka gave him before going to help Mai with something she apparently cannot describe until he has seen the result.

Rem looks toward the landing where Umaru's voice has become briefly audible, then back at him.

“Do you remember the conversation afterward?” ᴿᵉ

He nods.

With Shoko. Rem trying to explain the simple fact of a hand which had stayed in her thoughts longer than the walk it belonged to. He does not repeat her words back at her as though she needs a record to prove she has been allowed to keep feeling them.

“I've liked the afternoons since,” she says. ᴿᵉ

“I noticed.” ᴹ

She smiles, looking down at the cup.

* * *

“I'd like to ask her to do something,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

“You have been.” ᴹ

“I know. This would be a little different.” ᴿᵉ

He waits.

Rem turns the cup once between her hands.

“Not because someone needs showing where to go. Not because the Fort has a plan and she wants me there to see it. Just us.” ᴿᵉ

Mau leans back against the doorpost. He has a very clear memory of several conversations in which somebody gave him enough space to finish the difficult part without mistaking his hesitation for a request to be rescued from it.

He leaves her that much room.

“I want her to know why I'm asking,” Rem says. “I don't want to hide the question inside another game forever.” ᴿᵉ

He nods.

“That sounds like a better reason to ask.” ᴹ

She looks up, relieved enough that the expression nearly becomes laughter.

“It feels much harder.” ᴿᵉ

“I remember that part too.” ᴹ

* * *

She has thought about a little walk near the garden and something to eat afterward.

Mau knows a place they could use without blocking somebody's afternoon route or making them appear to have escaped the household entirely. He can help find a basket and the kind of food which will survive a short walk. Rem considers the offer with a seriousness which makes it plain she has come for more than permission to carry lunch.

“If she wants to,” she says. ᴿᵉ

“If you both want to.” ᴹ

She nods.

He does not promise she will say yes. Rem has not asked him to borrow someone else's answer. She sits beside him long enough to finish the tea and tell him one funny thing Umaru did while he was away, with the pleased impatience of someone who had wanted to share the story since it happened.

Mau laughs at the right part.

When Rem stands, he asks whether she wants him to put anything aside yet.

“Not yet.” ᴿᵉ

“All right.” ᴹ

She takes her cup with her, pauses at the turn where she can hear Umaru again and goes toward the voice because she wants to, without needing an object to deliver first.
