*When: Umaru asks whether she can talk to both her brothers, Mau is holding a plate and Taihei has just told him he has put too much on it.*

Mau looks down.

“For sharing.” ᴹ

“With how many people?” ᵀᵃ

Umaru takes something from the edge before the question can be answered.

Both look at her.

“Evidence,” she says. ᵁ

Mau gives Taihei a deeply satisfied expression, receives one which suggests the argument has not been resolved and puts the plate down somewhere Umaru can reach without negotiating through his arm.

Then notices she has not taken a second piece.

“You wanted to talk?” ᴹ

She nods, suddenly less interested in the plate.

* * *

They find somewhere quiet before beginning.

Yuka has come to ask whether Mau is ready for the walk they mentioned at breakfast. He looks at Umaru first.

“Would you mind if she stayed?” ᴹ

Umaru glances toward Yuka, who is waiting far enough away to make saying no an actual possibility.

Then nods.

Yuka comes in only after he asks. They sit in the little shared space, where Rimuru and Shizue have both gone out to things of their own. Taihei takes the place beside his sister. Mau puts the rescued plate within reach of everybody.

Umaru looks at it.

“You brought the whole thing.” ᵁ

“Emotional support.” ᴹ

She begins laughing, which makes the first difficult silence easier to cross.

* * *

The story begins with Rem's hand.

Umaru explains that it keeps happening. A path she wants to show her. A place to sit. Something at the common table which can apparently only be appreciated if Rem takes Umaru with her to see it. The reasons are ordinary enough that Umaru had not thought much about them until she started noticing the part afterward.

“She doesn't let go straight away,” she says. ᵁ

Taihei listens without interrupting.

Mau can see Rem sitting beside him on the afternoon before, turning the cup once between her hands. He leaves that memory where Rem put it and listens to the person here.

“Do you want her to?” he asks. ᴹ

Umaru looks at him.

“Let go,” he clarifies. ᴹ

She looks down at her own fingers.

“No.” ᵁ

The answer surprises her a little by arriving so clearly.

Yuka smiles without taking the next sentence away from her.

* * *

Umaru has several questions which turn out to be one question she keeps approaching from different sides.

Whether enjoying it means something different now. Whether she would know if Rem meant something different too. Whether asking would make the comfortable parts awkward if one of them has misunderstood.

Taihei asks what she would like to do if she did not have to solve all three questions first.

Umaru considers it.

“Go somewhere. With her.” ᵁ

“You already do,” he says. ᵀᵃ

She gives him a look so similar to the one Rem gave Mau yesterday that Mau has to take a drink before his face betrays him.

Taihei catches the movement.

Mau becomes innocent immediately.

“On purpose,” Umaru says. “Where she knows why I asked.” ᵁ

Yuka leans forward a little.

“Then let her know that part.” ʸ

Umaru looks at her.

“Like a date?” ᵁ

“If that's what you want to ask.” ʸ

The word sits in the room for a moment, much bigger than the little outing she has apparently been imagining.

Mau lets her look at it without immediately covering it in a joke.

* * *

“What if she doesn't want it?” Umaru asks. ᵁ

“Then you'll have an answer you didn't invent for her,” Taihei says gently. ᵀᵃ

Umaru nods, though the answer is clearly less convenient than the one she hoped he might have hidden somewhere.

Mau rests his hand beside hers.

“You can ask something small. You don't have to bring a plan for the rest of your life.” ᴹ

She looks at Yuka.

Yuka gives him a thoughtful glance.

“Advice he has recently begun following.” ʸ

He accepts the correction with dignity.

Umaru laughs. Taihei touches the back of her head for a moment, the familiar gesture making her look younger and more annoyed with him at exactly the same time.

“You can tell us how it goes,” he says. “You don't have to.” ᵀᵃ

She leans against him briefly before pretending she has only moved to take something from the plate.

Mau believes the explanation with the appropriate lack of conviction.

* * *

Rem is near the garden when Umaru finds her later.

She turns at her name, smiles and comes close enough that Umaru has to decide whether to begin now or walk around with the question for another whole evening.

“Do you want to—” ᵁ

“Would you like—” ᴿᵉ

They stop.

Look at each other.

Rem laughs first, softly and with enough embarrassment that Umaru begins smiling too.

“You go,” Umaru says. ᵁ

Rem takes a breath.

“Would you like to go for a walk with me tomorrow? And eat somewhere, just the two of us?” ᴿᵉ

Umaru looks at the hand Rem has not yet offered.

Then back at her.

“As a date?” ᵁ

Rem's face warms.

“If you'd like it to be.” ᴿᵉ

Umaru takes the hand herself.

“I was going to ask you.” ᵁ

* * *

When they come to ask Mau about the little place by the garden, Yuka sees them before he does.

She touches his wrist beneath the edge of the table.

He looks up, sees the hands and understands only the part they have chosen to show him.

Rem asks whether his offer of a basket still stands.

He says it does.

Umaru immediately insists that this basket does not require a diplomatic account of where every biscuit has gone.

Mau looks at Yuka.

“A promising policy change.” ᴹ

Umaru refuses to be drawn into discussing it and asks for two of the good cups.
