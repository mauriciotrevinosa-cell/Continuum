*When: B+126, morning. Yuka tells Mau the aim is one clean touch, and he looks at her hand with considerably less confidence than the simplicity of the instruction deserves.*

“Anywhere?” ᴹ

“Shoulder.” ʸ

“A traditional target.” ᴹ

“You can stop talking at any time.” ʸ

Yuta, standing beyond the little cleared stretch, looks as though he is considering whether this is an instruction he should have given his friend years ago. He has agreed to watch the short session and stop it when the time they chose is up. That part is not negotiable simply because the two people practising happen to be enjoying themselves.

Mau sets the little pack down away from their feet.

Yuka rolls her shoulders once, looks at him and begins smiling.

He remembers how much he likes that expression before she starts moving.

* * *

Her first attempt is honest enough to be a trick.

She steps toward his left shoulder, making the place she wants so clear that Mau begins answering the movement before she has finished it. Her hand stops short. The other comes from the side he has just made easier to reach.

He turns inside it, feels her fingers brush the loose part of his sleeve and takes one step away.

Yuka stops.

Looks at his shoulder.

“Sleeve,” he says. ᴹ

“I noticed.” ʸ

“Very close.” ᴹ

She goes for him again while he is trying to sound helpful.

Yuta looks down at the page where he is keeping the time, apparently to conceal his amusement.

* * *

The body's strength has not politely left because Mau asked for a small session.

He keeps the movement short instead. Lets Yuka see where he intends to put his feet, then changes the answer when she uses the information. He does not accelerate until her best option has become being too slow. That would settle a different argument and teach them almost nothing about the one they chose.

Yuka learns the first rhythm in three attempts.

On the fourth, she takes his wrist, steps across the path he has been using and nearly reaches his shoulder with the back of her other hand. He lets a narrow Infinity guard form at the fingertip before contact, the limited defence they agreed she could make him spend attention on.

Her hand stops.

He sees her register the distance.

Then she releases his wrist and puts the smallest useful Blue at the place where his next step needs to land.

The shared draw reaches him as a familiar additional load. Hers to cast, his source carrying the cost they both know how to recognise. He shortens his own guard rather than pretending maintaining it everywhere has become free.

Yuka's fingers pass his collar.

He turns with them.

She laughs, breathless and delighted, because this time he actually had to choose.

* * *

They stop after the exchange.

Yuta asks what Mau felt first. Mau tells him where the extra draw reached him and when he released his own defence. Yuka describes the opening she thought she saw, then looks at her husband to find out whether she actually saw it or he kindly left it there.

“You made it,” he says. ᴹ

Her face brightens.

“I noticed the step late. I had to change.” ᴹ

Yuta nods. Offers one small correction about the hand she left behind when she moved.

She tries the shape without making a technique, then turns back toward Mau.

“Again.” ʸ

He looks toward Yuta.

There is time for one more.

* * *

Yuka changes almost nothing at first.

The same approach. The same wrist offered in the place where he knows she wants him to accept it. Mau gives it back with enough caution that she has to work for the turn, then feels the pull of Blue begin somewhere different.

Behind her own heel.

She is not moving him this time. She takes the small acceleration herself, lets it end before her hand reaches his guard and changes the last angle with ordinary movement he has seen a hundred times from her grandfather.

Mau shifts the defence.

Too late for the other hand.

Two fingers land on his shoulder, light enough that he can feel the absurd care she has taken not to turn the victory into an accidental shove.

Yuka stops moving.

He looks at the hand.

Then at her.

“There,” she says. ʸ

He starts laughing.

She keeps her hand exactly where it is, apparently requiring him to admire the evidence before anyone calls time.

* * *

Yuta calls time anyway.

The disappointment on both their faces makes him look so much like somebody's grandfather that Yuka immediately objects to his expression instead of the decision.

“You said one more,” he tells them. ʸᵗ

Mau lets the technique settle, takes a drink and answers the ordinary questions honestly. Tired, a little. No unfamiliar pressure or new pain. Enough room left to want another attempt, which is a far better place to stop than pretending the desire itself proves the body can keep going.

Yuka sits beside him on the low boundary of the clearing.

For a while they watch Yuta demonstrate the little hand correction once, without either of them turning it into another round. Then he takes the page back toward the house and leaves them to finish their water.

Yuka rests her head against Mau's arm.

“I got you.” ʸ

“You did.” ᴹ

“You can say it again.” ʸ

He turns toward her.

“My wife is very dangerous.” ᴹ

She closes her eyes with a satisfied little smile.

“Excellent husband.” ʸ
