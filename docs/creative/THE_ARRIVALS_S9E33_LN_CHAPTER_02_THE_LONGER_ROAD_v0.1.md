*When: Hollis says he has come by the longer road, Jinshi asks how much longer before asking what it cost.*

“Two days,” Hollis says. ᴴᵒ

Jinshi stops turning the page.

Mau has come to carry one of the light parcels to the place where Marin expects it. He stays after putting it down. Yuka, who has been examining something Rem wanted them to see before it goes into the store, looks up as the merchant takes a folded letter from inside his coat.

“This came through Tempest,” Hollis adds. “She asked that it reach Rimuru.” ᴴᵒ

Jinshi receives the letter, checks the outside and sends for the man it actually names. Nobody opens it simply because the story has become interesting.

Hollis puts his hands around the cup he has been offered.

“My goods are still here,” he says. “That was the better result.” ᴴᵒ

* * *

The obstruction was at a road which had been clear on his earlier journey.

Several armed people, a paper naming an authority Hollis had never paid and a request to examine everything carried from the new settlement. They had been sufficiently interested in the origin of the goods that he decided to turn aside before testing how much of the paper they actually believed.

“They let you leave?” Mau asks. ᴹ

“They weren't alone with me. There were three other carts waiting. I suggested we'd all save time going round.” ᴴᵒ

“And they agreed?” ᴶ

“The carts did.” ᴴᵒ

Jinshi nods, making a note which keeps the part Hollis saw separate from the name the paper claimed. Mau asks about the road itself. Hollis gives him the turn, the old marker beside it and the place where a traveller could still choose another way before finding himself inside the discussion.

The detour cost food, time and the sale Hollis would have reached yesterday. It did not become harmless because everybody arrived alive.

* * *

Rimuru reads Testarossa's letter before giving the group the part they need.

Other people have been asking where equipment from the settlement goes and which routes carry it. Not every question is hostile. Some are customers trying to find a seller. One recorded inquiry, however, names the same turn Hollis has just described and a fee nobody in the recognised local office has authorised.

That gives them something to check.

It does not give them a reason to announce that every unfamiliar official works for the same enemy.

Mau looks at the little map Jinshi has begun.

“We can look at the turn without making him take the road again.” ᴹ

Rimuru nods.

Hollis puts the cup down.

“And the other carts?” ᴴᵒ

Mau looks up.

“We ask whether they want company.” ᴹ

* * *

The plan grows around the people who actually use the route.

Jinshi wants the ordinary warning carried to the next buyers before they plan around a delivery date Hollis can no longer promise. Rimuru will answer Testarossa with the marker and the account the merchant has agreed may be shared. Nobody offers a public price for killing the obstruction or sends a crowd to discover whether the paper was worth drawing a sword over.

Mau asks Igris for twelve existing ordinary soldiers who can observe a road and withdraw if the useful task becomes bringing news home. The home watch keeps its people. The travelling soldiers will be reassigned from the stored companies, not counted as a new army because the task has a new description.

Hollis considers the offer of an escort for a future journey.

“No appearing in a customer's bedroom,” he says. ᴴᵒ

“No,” Mau agrees. ᴹ

“And if someone refuses them?” ᴴᵒ

“We find another arrangement. The warning still belongs to them.” ᴹ

Hollis nods. He will ask the other traders he knows. The first observation can begin tomorrow, before anybody has promised a protected passage which has not yet been tested.

* * *

Ram joins the quieter discussion after the ordinary accounts are settled.

Rimuru invites the small group into the familiar private connection. Yuka accepts beside Mau. The outward room remains a room where people are finishing tea, with no shouted announcement that the household should begin suspecting the next animal which crosses the path.

Ram has kept notes from the places she can observe without reaching into the foreign signal. Some quiet days. One brief repetition beside the store while the couple was away. The activity ended before she could follow more than the first part of its route.

*It didn't need you here to be interested,* she says. ᴿᵃ

Mau looks at the common door.

Not frightened people. A cloth being carried to the workshop. Rem laughing at something near the kitchen. The life he has no wish to turn into a performance designed for a listener.

*Then we keep finding out who wants to listen,* he answers. ᴹ

Rimuru puts a finger on the new road marker.

*One thing we can test. Then the next.* ᴿ

Outside the connection, Hollis asks whether somebody intends to tell him what Marin thought of the cloth.

Mau stands to fetch the woman who has an actual answer.
