*When: Bocchi asks whether Mau wants to hear the troublesome part, Ryo says he should be charged for the privilege.*

“She keeps playing it,” Ryo explains. ᴿʸ

Bocchi turns so quickly that the instrument nearly follows her.

“Because it keeps going wrong.” ᴮᵒ

“And we've become experts.” ᴿʸ

Kita puts one hand lightly against Bocchi's shoulder before the argument can make her apologise for having invited somebody. Nijika moves the spare chair with her foot, gives Mau a place which does not block the useful route and asks whether he has been fed.

“Recently,” he says. ᴹ

“Then you can survive the practice.” ᴺⁱ

He sits.

Bocchi looks at the troublesome part as though it has personally betrayed the confidence she placed in it before the invitation.

* * *

He hears the difficulty on the second attempt.

Not because he understands the music well enough to correct it. Because Bocchi reaches the place she expects to fail, changes the movement before the failure has happened and then looks exactly as annoyed with herself as Mau looks when he answers the wrong feint in training.

Kita catches the rhythm and gives it back to her.

Bocchi follows.

This time the phrase comes through.

She stops at once.

“Again,” Nijika says before she can decide success must have been an accident. ᴺⁱ

Mau sits still while they try it.

Ryo has another opinion. Kita laughs at the less useful part and answers the useful one. The next attempt works differently, then the one after that begins sounding like something they intended rather than something everybody has agreed to forgive.

Mau finds himself smiling before the phrase ends.

Bocchi sees him.

Ruins the next note.

Ryo announces a revised admission price.

* * *

They stop for food because the band chooses to stop.

There is no rehearsal quota to fill merely because Mau has come. Nijika and Ryo go to fetch something they wanted before the room became absorbed in the same difficult passage. Kita stays beside Bocchi, taking the instrument when she asks and putting it safely down before accepting a hand in exchange.

Mau waits through the comfortable quiet.

Then Bocchi asks whether he has put their honeymoon portrait up.

He tells her where.

“You can come see it,” he adds. ᴹ

She smiles and looks toward the wall beyond which the reserved space for her family has remained a real, empty possibility.

“I'd like them to see the room,” she says. ᴮᵒ

Kita's fingers settle around hers.

Mau nods.

“The music room?” ᴹ

“That too.” ᴮᵒ

* * *

She has names she has not stopped thinking about just because there were other things happening.

Her parents. Her sister. The dog, which makes the question of how many invitations she needs feel both embarrassingly practical and too important to leave vague forever.

Mau does not decide that an empty room has somehow solved the coin question. He asks which part she wants help asking first.

Bocchi looks relieved enough that he understands she has been worried he would turn wanting them into something larger and more urgent than she was ready to carry today.

“Whether he can come with her,” she says. “The dog.” ᴮᵒ

“Then we ask that before promising it.” ᴹ

She nods.

Kita asks whether Bocchi would like her there for the conversation.

The answer is immediate.

“Yes.” ᴮᵒ

Mau leaves the names and the choice with her. He can help arrange a conversation with DIO when she wants one; he does not need to take the page out of her hands to prove he has heard her.

* * *

Nijika returns with food just as Bocchi begins explaining which version of the portrait story Yuka told her.

It is apparently not Mau's version.

Ryo comes behind Nijika with an opinion about whether an artist ought to charge more for a subject who will not look in the correct direction. Kita agrees far too readily. Bocchi starts laughing, tries to apologise to Mau for the laughter and ends up laughing harder.

He accepts a piece of the food and allows himself to be outnumbered.

After a while, Bocchi finds the little troublesome rhythm against Kita's fingers on the table.

Kita answers it quietly.

This time Bocchi does not stop when somebody notices.
