*When: B+127, late afternoon. The thing Noise is proud of is small enough that Mau has to lean forward to see what it wants him to admire.*

It waits until he is properly looking.

Then moves the image toward Ciel with unmistakable satisfaction.

“This,” it says. ᴺ

Mau smiles before understanding any of it.

“Show me.” ᴹ

* * *

They have kept the ordinary part simple.

Shoko sees Mau move through the same short sequence he has already practised, checks how the body answers and makes him describe the effort before Ciel supplies a more exact account through him. No new technique. No attempt to turn the sequence into the beginning of a duel. Yuka sits near the edge with the page on which they are keeping what actually happens.

Mau finishes with enough strength left to be irritated by the smallness of it.

Shoko recognises the expression.

“An excellent place to stop,” she says. ˢᵒ

He looks at Yuka.

She is already writing.

He suspects a coalition.

* * *

The component stays apart from the permanent structures.

Ciel shows Mau its position in the familiar inner view while he remains seated in the ordinary room. Rimuru accepts the connection they agreed for the review. Noise has made the little passage they chose, with the point at which it can be disconnected plainly visible instead of concealed beneath the hopeful claim that everything should work.

A small amount of Mau's own mana goes into the isolated part.

He feels the draw because it is his. What comes back carries the same relation to him, the little beginning of a route which knows the difference between inheriting something and being replaced by the person who once held it.

Ciel lets the result settle before repeating the test.

Noise points toward the larger unfinished outline.

Mau looks at it, then back at the little passage.

“It can hold this much.” ᴹ

“This,” Noise agrees. ᴺ

The correction is gentle and utterly firm.

* * *

On the second attempt, they change only the part they meant to change.

The sample passes. The return remains his. The path closes when he asks it to close, leaving the rest of him where it was. There is no rush of strength from an unfinished heart, no helpful disappearance of the old number in the window. Two hundred remains two hundred. The body which earned it has not been rewritten by a successful model.

Ciel records the result.

Then the failure they have not attempted.

*The larger route is still incomplete,* she says. *A permanent connection would be a different operation. This result doesn't make that one safe.* ᶜⁱ

Mau watches Noise touch the unjoined part.

“What does it need?” ᴹ

The dark shape points at him.

Then at the part of the diagram which moves when his body answers a changing load.

Ciel explains slowly enough that he can follow the actual problem. The receiving structure has changed too much for Noise to finish it from the old model alone. They need to know how the living body redirects what it already carries, where the support holds and where extra demand would simply become damage. That knowledge can come from continued measured work; making the load larger before understanding the smaller answer would only give them a more dramatic mistake.

Rimuru asks which observations they can gather with the existing structure.

Noise brings another small drawing to the table.

Mau groans softly.

Ciel's amusement reaches him immediately.

*You did ask what it needed.* ᶜⁱ

* * *

Afterward, the ordinary body does the ordinary sequence again.

The same movement. The same brief known draw. Nothing improved simply because he now knows more about the work waiting inside him. Shoko compares what he says with what she can actually examine and asks whether he feels disappointed.

Mau considers lying for the very small amount of time required to remember whom he would be lying to.

“A little.” ᴹ

She nods.

Yuka puts the page down beside him.

“You came back from a test with all the same parts.” ʸ

“An unimaginative result.” ᴹ

“I'm fond of your parts.” ʸ

Shoko looks toward her.

Yuka goes red.

Mau begins laughing before either woman can decide how much dignity the room has left.

* * *

Noise stays with the unfinished model when Mau comes to thank it later.

He knows it wants to keep going. Can recognise the impatience because it looks uncomfortably like his own, right down to the way it keeps returning to the join which ought to fit if wanting it were enough.

Mau sits beside the table.

“We learned something,” he says. ᴹ

Noise looks up.

“Small.” ᴺ

“Still ours.” ᴹ

The dark shape considers him. Then moves the successful little piece closer, apparently deciding that if they are going to stop for the evening he should at least admire it properly.

Mau does.

When Yuka calls from the other side of the room, he asks whether it wants the view while they go to dinner. Noise looks toward Ciel, waits until she agrees to share the ordinary interval and settles beside the open part of the image with the satisfaction of somebody included.
