Yuta takes the page next.

Mau watches his expression change at the names below it.

“He wasn't joking about company,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“Neither was I,” Mau answers. ᴹ

* * *

The list is short enough to remember.

Uraume. Jogo. Hanami.

Sukuna's two declared Ten Shadows manifestations: Mahoraga, and Agito in the fused state he has reconstructed. The components committed to that totality will not also appear as an undeclared second collection of bodies. No new curse brought from outside after the field closes. No person in the audience becoming a fourth helper by claiming they had always intended to join.

His own body, his own learned techniques and their developed applications remain his. A change in his physical form will not be treated as another person entering, any more than Mau keeping a constructed body intact requires a fresh roster every time he heals.

Gojo rests a finger on the last condition.

“Don't assume he'll keep the shape you know.” ᴳ

Mau nods.

* * *

His own list is longer, but less generous than the number first suggests.

Forty ordinary soldiers and Muster stay home. The registered legion remains six thousand six hundred and fifteen; the field can receive six thousand five hundred and seventy-four of them. No shadow left guarding the house can become an emergency entry after the closure. The existing native shikigami, including Mahoraga and Agito, are declared separately. Ciel's assistance and Noise's work intrinsic to Mau are stated plainly. Neither is a slave awarded with his defeat.

His weapons are checked as weapons, with their actual equipment effects present. Taking the daggers off removes those effects. Nothing removes the strength his body has already gained. Mai's link and Yuka's borrowed routes will be isolated for the actual duel, with their agreement; he will not carry undeclared support out of another room or let an ordinary household task become interference by accident.

Mai looks offended that he thinks she would create a latch during this particular afternoon.

“I was going to warn you,” she says. ᴹᵃ

“I know.” ᴹ

She touches his arm once before going back to the page.

* * *

They meet the declared auxiliaries at the quarry inspection.

Jogo looks toward Gojo and becomes so still that nobody mistakes the recognition for friendliness. Hanami studies the damaged basin before looking at the people standing above it. Uraume's attention goes almost entirely to Sukuna, with one cold assessment of the rival whose army has apparently justified this much preparation.

They have come with the converged world, not out of Mau's memory. Their present bodies and current limits are their own. The clerk records each admission. Their permission ends if they yield at the marked exit; it cannot be handed to somebody else afterward.

Mau asks why they have agreed.

Jogo looks at him as though the answer should have been obvious.

“You built a place where curses are things to dispose of.” ᴶᵒ

“I built a place to live.” ᴹ

“People always have a kinder name for their side.” ᴶᵒ

Sukuna laughs softly behind him.

Hanami accepts the field boundary after requiring that the outer living ground remain outside the intended attacks. The concession costs none of them the basin's trees; there are none. It costs Sukuna the convenient excuse of following Mau beyond the marked volume with whatever he chooses to open.

Uraume signs without offering a reason which would make the arrangement easier for Mau to like.

* * *

The records are tested in front of both principals.

A harmless sample closes when its owner consciously yields. Another, holding no awareness, records the irreversible end of the simple pattern they put into it. The definitions agree on both copies. Nobody stops a person's heart for a demonstration. Shoko watches the test, makes one correction to the wording about *active repair* and has it read back before permitting her name to be used as a witness.

Mau tests his own declared route without assigning its ownership. He can feel the anchor's relation to his continuing shadow. No new power rushes through it. It does not resurrect anything. It is a place in which he has agreed his own awareness could be held, with Makima's claim waiting only on the valid result.

He stops before the last acceptance and looks toward Yuka.

She has read the same text. Has asked the questions he wanted to avoid. She does not make the decision for him with a nod which pretends it could be painless.

“I know what you're choosing,” she says. ʸ

He takes a breath.

Then accepts.

* * *

The matched articles ratify the field and rosters. The road restraint extends through noon on137, with the threatened orders withdrawn under the principal agreement. Sukuna accepts the reciprocal shadow permission with a smile which makes it clear he still considers the risk an entertaining price.

Makima accepts the separate observer rules.

The clerks receive the identical copies.

Nothing starts fighting yet.

Mau walks away with his own life still his, acutely aware that he has promised a way to lose it without being allowed the mercy of not knowing afterward.

At the edge of the approach Yuka takes his hand.

He has never been so grateful that she does not ask him to make the afternoon funny.
