* * *

The lesson starts with the mistake Mau already made once.

Several complete answers trying to become the same answer. Barriers built for different intentions. A body which could carry the information and still fail catastrophically at telling its own hands what to do with it.

He remembers waking up afterward. Does not need another demonstration of the bed.

This time there is one barrier. One cursed technique written into it. No automatic flood of Void information at the beginning. The shadow he extends across its floor belongs to his own monarch route, not a second innate technique forced through the same part of his brain. It has a boundary. Darkness outside it does not become his simply because he would find that convenient.

Gojo makes him separate those facts before trying anything interesting.

Mau creates the small territory.

Three seconds.

Inside, he can place the two already stored weapons at different edges of his own shade. He cannot make a hundred separate decisions at once because the room looks impressive. When he tries to turn the nearest route while also changing the barrier's conditions, Ciel catches the rising interference.

*End it.* ᶜⁱ

He ends it.

The brief burnout follows. His body stays his body. Yuta takes the page from him before the old stiff fingers can turn impatience into a dropped pencil.

“That part worked,” Yuta says. ʸᵗ

“The stopping?” ᴹ

“Especially the stopping.” ʸᵗ

* * *

They do not run the same attempt until success becomes exhaustion.

Gojo makes him work through the sequence on ordinary ground. A route. A weapon. One change to the way an opponent can approach it. Then an actual strike instead of another improvement to a room the enemy may refuse to let him finish building.

“You have enough ideas,” Gojo says. “Make them arrive where your feet are.” ᴳ

Mau changes the step.

This time Yuta has to turn the practice blade rather than simply watch the attempted combination lose its own opening.

Mau begins smiling.

*Don't spend the fight trying to become a newer copy of him,* Ciel says. *Use what you've already learned together.* ᶜⁱ

He looks toward Gojo.

*He'll be unbearable if I tell him that.* ᴹ

*Then don't. We have work to finish.* ᶜⁱ

* * *

The repair margin is Ciel's insistence, not a new source of power.

They mark a small part of Mau's own cursed energy which he will not spend on matching a spectacular attack. Enough to begin emergency repair of the control structures if they remain alive. Not enough to regrow a destroyed person from an empty pattern. Not an answer to having truly died under the agreement.

“I'll think I need it,” Mau says. ᴹ

*You will. That's why we decide now what it is for.* ᶜⁱ

He sits with that for a moment, then agrees.

Noise has brought the unfinished light structure again. The isolated passage holds. The permanent joining still will not settle under the safe, steady load they can reproduce here. It needs the relation between source and receiving body while that body is actually changing its answer. Forcing it closed in this room would be pretending the missing measurement does not matter.

Mau looks at the gap.

“We don't finish it just because tomorrow is getting closer.” ᴹ

Noise goes still.

Then touches the unjoined edge.

“Wait.” ᴺ

Mau nods.

* * *

At the common table, the other work has made visible progress.

Jinshi has return routes. Ram has marked a screened room at the quarry for anyone who needs to stop watching without leaving their family. Frieren will walk there with Ori and let her decide how much she wants to see. Milim has chosen the outer safety team with Diablo and the people actually maintaining those boundaries.

Umaru wants to go with Rem. Taihei will be there too. Yuka's family has decided among themselves instead of waiting for Mau to assign places to people he loves.

Shizue will stay with the children at the quarry. The home watch has its own assignments, including a clear return path to DIO and Ashborn's room. Rimuru will take the things he needs, then return to his own place beside Mau's people rather than turn their fear into another council.

Mau sits down as the food arrives.

Nobody asks him to practise one more thing before he eats.

Yuka takes the place beside him, looks at the meal and puts something on his plate because she likes it and wants him to try.

For the length of dinner, that is an excellent reason.
