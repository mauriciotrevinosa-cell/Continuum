*When: Sukuna takes three steps back, Mau does not follow the third.*

A black opening spreads beneath his heel.

Beru rises first, already turning toward the heat building on the right. Igris comes out facing left. Bellion looks beyond both of them and sees what their king has stopped to see: Uraume has not spent the opening exchange waiting for somebody to invite them into it.

The stone is becoming ice.

“Companies,” Bellion says. ᴮᵉˡ

The first ranks emerge in lanes which leave one another room to move.

* * *

From the ridge, the scale arrives differently than it did in the festival.

There are people here who saw the panels then, heard a number and laughed at the ant's theatrical death. There are people who came because they did not believe the number. Now the basin fills in disciplined pieces, each body finding a task instead of simply becoming another thing for the audience to count.

Yuuki sits forward.

Makima does not.

* * *

The first company goes toward the ice.

It breaks under them, rises in spears and takes legs from bodies which keep moving until there is no useful movement left. Uraume does not waste the second formation on the same attack. Frost spreads through the gaps and gives the stone a skin which makes every new step belong partly to somebody else.

Mau lets Bellion change the route.

He does not try to see through every soldier's eyes. His commanders already know what a broken advance looks like. He takes the one useful report Igris gives and puts Blue beneath an upper shelf of the quarry wall.

The shelf comes away.

It falls across the ice in a long, ugly break, making a road out of the thing which had been stopping his soldiers.

Uraume looks up.

Beru is already above them.

* * *

Jogo's fire meets Beru in the middle of the descent.

The ant turns, loses an arm to the crossing blast and lands with the other claws buried in the stone. The missing form begins breaking back into dark. Mau feels the restoration request and gives it enough mana to complete, not an excuse to rebuild every fallen body before the battlefield needs them again.

Beru's arm returns.

“Discourteous,” he says. ᴮᵉ

Jogo opens a bright mouth in the rock beneath him.

The ant moves before the eruption can finish finding his old position.

Igris takes the space Jogo has left uncovered, his sword crossing the first line of roots Hanami sends up to protect it. The roots splinter. More come behind them. Hanami is not trying to outnumber the army with wood. Each root gives Jogo a place to stand which Uraume's ice does not own.

Mau sees the coordination.

Sukuna hits him while he is appreciating it.

* * *

The blow comes through amplification, close enough that Infinity is a slowing problem to be met rather than a promise of safety.

Mau turns his head away from the worst of it. A knuckle opens his cheek. His right dagger goes for the inner elbow. Sukuna lets the shallow wound happen and drives Mau backward toward the root line.

Mau recognises the intended crossing.

He leaves a little Red behind him and steps to the side before releasing it.

Roots burst. Stone strikes Sukuna's back. Mau closes on the forced turn, lands one clean reinforced blow under the ribs and feels something give.

Then a cut opens along his own upper arm.

Sukuna's hands were striking.

The cut came from the root.

* * *

*Not the root,* Ciel says. *The cursed energy along it.* ᶜⁱ

Mau looks through the next fraction of movement with the filter opened a little further. Hanami's growth carries several traces which do not belong to Hanami. The little marks on the broken shelf. A thin seam in Jogo's cooling firestone.

Sukuna has spent attacks making routes.

He can send his own cutting through the charged contacts, extending the place from which a cut is applied. It is not a second technique he found because the field needed a surprise. It is a craftsman's alteration to where his first technique can begin.

Mau gives ground rather than crossing the next mark.

Sukuna sees him understand.

“There,” he says softly. ˢᵘ

Mau stores one dagger, reaches for the katana and cuts the charged root with a clean stroke which takes the conducting part away before he steps across it.

“I see it,” he answers. ᴹ

* * *

The fight changes its shape.

Bellion sends ordinary soldiers through routes which will cost bodies but expose the next hidden line. Igris works behind their advance, destroying the marked contacts rather than asking each new rank to survive discovering one. Kamish rises above the basin and does not release his breath yet. The dragon watches where the heat is gathering.

Mau moves where his soldiers have made an honest piece of ground.

Sukuna begins taking it apart again.

Neither army is decoration now. Every decision one principal makes about the ground gives the other a new way to reach him.

At the edge of the ice, Beru drives Uraume back two full steps.

Jogo sees it and begins making a room of fire.
