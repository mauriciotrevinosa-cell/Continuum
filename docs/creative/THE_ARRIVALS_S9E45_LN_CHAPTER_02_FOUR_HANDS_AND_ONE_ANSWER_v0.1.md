*When: the second pair of hands opens, Mau does not have time to admire the advantage.*

One catches his katana.

One drives a fist into his face.

The first pair meets beneath a mouth which does not need to stop breathing in order to speak.

Mau exchanges with the shadow behind Bellion before the lock completes. The stone where he stood divides, straight through the space in which the stronger body would still have been cut.

Sukuna turns toward the new position.

He has not gained another reserve by changing shape. He has gained the body which can spend the remaining one much more efficiently.

* * *

The renewed technique has a price.

Sukuna paid it while the transformation concealed part of the work: a dangerous repair of the exhausted control pathway, not an automatic cure supplied with extra arms. A little blood runs from his nose even as the new mouth finishes its first sentence.

Mau sees the sign.

Ciel sees the cost beneath it.

Neither mistakes that for permission to try the same reset indefinitely.

The next plane begins forming across Bellion's route.

Mau leaves the position before the marshal becomes the place the enemy is actually attacking. He takes an ordinary soldier's ready anchor instead, arrives low and sends a released Red past Sukuna's left side.

Sukuna has already made a fourth mark.

Only three will belong to the cut.

Mau chooses the wrong third point.

* * *

The line opens through his left side beneath the ribs.

He moves enough to keep the centre of his chest out of it. Not enough to keep the lung from becoming the reason the next breath does not arrive.

Sukuna is on him before he can exchange again.

Two hands take his weapon arm. A third catches the back of his head. The fourth opens toward the body which has no useful place left to turn.

Mau stores the katana.

His empty hand goes to Sukuna's wrist with amplification across his own skin. His innate technique goes quiet to let the learned barrier do its job.

At contact, the prepared cursed-energy route loses its conducting surface.

The new cut does not complete where Sukuna wanted it.

Mau takes a lesser blow through his shoulder instead and uses the shock to get his knee between their bodies.

* * *

The first fist is physical.

The delayed cursed energy follows after Sukuna has accepted the impact and begun his answer. It is not a new technique Mau found in a secret part of the window. It is the difference between two arrivals, practised until he can make an opponent prepare for the wrong one.

Sukuna's grip shifts.

Mau's other hand lands beneath it.

For one impossible fraction, intention, movement and energy meet without leaving the usual small argument between them.

Black Flash tears the air open around the blow.

Sukuna leaves the ground.

Mau follows because delight would be a terrible place to stop.

* * *

He reaches the falling body with a dagger drawn.

Sukuna's lower hand catches the blade across reinforced fingers. The upper one folds Mau's wrist back far enough to make his new arm stop feeling like an improvement. The other side of the body turns.

A fist finds Mau's abdomen.

Black fractures answer black fractures.

The impact drives him through a shelf of stone and breaks the useful shape out of his left leg.

On the ridge, nobody has to explain to Yuka why he has stopped moving.

He has not stopped being active.

The record gives her only that much to hold.

* * *

Mau gets his foot beneath him badly.

The new source passage can support the repair. It cannot make repairing and enduring the next strike the same task. Ciel makes him keep the little margin sealed while Noise maintains the damaged leg's structure long enough to accept weight.

His chest is still leaking breath where it should be holding it.

He takes the fraction he needs by changing his body to the single slime pattern he knows how to hold. His cursed casting stops with the change. The ordinary slash through the shape takes material but misses the gathered core, and he returns to the familiar body a little further down the broken slope.

Not unhurt. Not restored from a save point. The old body pattern comes back with its old stiff fingers and the fresh damage the manoeuvre has not paid to fix.

Sukuna's expression changes at the answer.

“The slime's influence.” ˢᵘ

Mau wipes blood from his mouth.

“Among other bad habits.” ᴹ

* * *

Three points form again.

Mau looks toward the first two and lets his shoulders promise the wrong escape. His remaining reinforcement holds the bad leg for a step it will not forgive afterward.

Sky Manipulation turns the surface he is standing against before Sukuna finishes assigning the plane. It moves Mau out of the selected space; it does not persuade an already completed world-targeting cut to become a harmless ribbon.

He releases Sky as he lands.

The katana returns.

Sukuna's lower arms catch the first angle. The second dagger comes out beneath them, too close to be treated as the continuation of the sword.

It enters below the second mouth.

Sukuna's breath catches.

Mau hears the little moment of genuine surprise and knows he will never get the same one again.

He takes it anyway.

* * *

They separate with blood on both sets of hands.

Around them the surviving officers hold positions instead of remaking the companies Sukuna can erase faster than a crowd can become useful. Kamish's wing is cut. Beru has gone quiet enough to make every word he does say worth hearing. Igris brings the katana's abandoned line back into reach, then leaves the principals the space their next choices require.

Mau can feel all the places he could still go.

Sukuna is learning how he chooses among them.

Winning another exchange will not be enough.

Mau looks at the hand he will have to put against the other man's skin.

*Ciel.* ᴹ

*I know,* she answers. ᶜⁱ

He begins making the opening.
