*When: Mau puts the katana away, Sukuna assumes he is about to try another body.*

The assumption lasts one hand.

Mau takes the next slash on a reinforced forearm rather than stepping where the new marks expect him to step. The wound opens to bone. He closes only what would keep the arm from working.

His palm finds Sukuna's wrist.

Mana opens the path Frieren taught him to begin.

Sukuna understands before it has finished.

“You—” ˢᵘ

* * *

The first thing Mau receives is the intention to kill him.

Not a philosophical explanation. A living movement, precise and immediate, travelling through a body which has another hand available where Mau's remembered body does not.

He nearly follows it.

Nearly becomes the man reaching for his own throat.

Ciel puts his name into the confusion.

*Mau. Your left foot. The ground under it.* ᶜⁱ

He holds the path instead of finishing the movement.

Noise opens the receiving structure.

* * *

It is not a lifetime which politely stops to tell itself in order.

Hands learning where cursed energy leaves a cut wasteful. A barrier made smaller without putting its entire inside into the same little space. The patience to notice that an opponent has begun enjoying one answer too much. Bodies which promised strength and failed to have anything interesting left after the first certainty broke.

Hunger.

Not an explanation which makes the people hurt by it less hurt. An appetite which has spent so long finding the next edge that the search has become its own reason to continue.

Mau sees Gojo through eyes which watched him fall.

For one instant his own memories try to answer with a hundred other streets.

*The ground,* Ciel says again. *You are not that man.* ᶜⁱ

Mau finds his left foot.

Keeps the path open.

* * *

The entire account enters the archive.

What Sukuna knows. The techniques he owns, the refinements he has actually built, his own experience of using them, the decisions beneath the movements Mau has spent the fight barely surviving. No imagined future skill arrives with it. No opponent's innate technique becomes Sukuna's merely because he once saw it.

Noise preserves the whole rather than forcing Mau to consciously live a thousand years between one heartbeat and the next.

Ciel begins sorting the urgent present from the immense remainder.

The route of the cut. The moment of assignment. What the extra mark conceals. The exact choice which would let Sukuna make another plane while Mau celebrates having avoided the first.

The transfer does not spare either mind.

Sukuna drives the hostile intention back through the contact, trying to make Mau accept a movement whose ending belongs to Sukuna alone.

Mau feels his fingers begin completing the wrong sign.

He ends the spell.

* * *

The return is worse than the lesson.

For a moment he has too many hands.

Too many positions from which the next cut should be possible. Too many reasons to turn his body toward a target which is his own chest.

He names the present things without speaking.

The ground. The pain in his leg. His own two hands.

Yuka beyond the boundary, not the person a remembered enemy intended to use as a lever.

His wife.

Mau comes back.

Sukuna's eyes are still finding the quarry.

* * *

Mau strikes the first conducting point before the other man can make the field belong to it.

His Dismantle is smaller than Sukuna's last attempt. It goes to a better place.

The cut breaks the charged surface instead of trying to hurt the body behind it. The second one takes the line Sukuna would have used to correct the first. By the third, Sukuna has enough of himself back to move, and Mau has enough of his own to know exactly where the correction must go.

Their fists meet.

Mau does not suddenly execute every operation in the archive perfectly. He uses the refinement of the operation already in his hand.

It is enough to make Sukuna's next breath arrive without the cut he meant to put inside it.

* * *

On the ridge, Gojo leans forward.

Yuta sees it a moment later.

Neither speaks a suggestion across the boundary. Neither could make Mau's bleeding brain do the work by wishing harder. What they can see is the difference between the boy who reaches for another answer and the man who makes the answer he already has arrive properly.

Sukuna takes a line across his upper arm.

Looks at the shallow result.

Then at Mau.

“You took the whole thing.” ˢᵘ

Mau is breathing hard enough that the answer nearly fails him.

“You weren't going to explain it kindly.” ᴹ

Sukuna laughs, sharp and delighted despite the blood.

* * *

The next exchange is the fastest either has made.

Sukuna changes a condition Mau has just learned. Mau sees the cost of the change and attacks the place the new advantage leaves uncovered. More information does not become prophecy; it makes the questions harder for both men.

The lower hands strike. The upper hands begin the sign.

Mau enters close enough that the completed plane would also divide the position Sukuna must stand in to keep it honest. Sukuna releases the attempt, changes to Cleave and opens Mau's shoulder.

Mau's dagger enters the arm responsible.

For one fraction they are almost holding each other up.

He knows now that there is a way to understand the hunger without letting it decide whose life must end in another man's hand.

He pulls free.

Calls the surviving officers to the positions he has actually practised.

The dark begins spreading beneath his own feet.
