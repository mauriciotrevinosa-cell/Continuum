# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 59
## Chapter 8 — A Place of His Own

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1 (2026-11-03)
**Volume:** 59
**Reading order:** 8
**Source episode:** S9E46
**Continuity state:** B136 continuous. Partial double Domain causes real neural injury and unreliable innate CE casting. No verdict. Both still fighting.

*When: Mau opens the territory, the first shadow which enters does not attack.*

Igris takes the position they practised. Bellion takes the second. Beru arrives at the third with one arm still unfinished.

Mau can feel the part they got right. The floor keeps its depth when the barrier closes. He can put his heel down on stone and call an officer out of the very same place. Nothing in the barrier needs to know how the shadow got there.

There is a difference between that and what he tries next.

“Three breaths,” Ciel tells him. “Count the first one.” ᶜ

He does. In that breath Sukuna crosses half the floor.

Mau wakes the first sure-hit. Sukuna closes two hands into the sign for Hollow Wicker Basket. Two others stay free. One checks Bellion's sword. The last catches Mau's wrist before the dagger can get between his ribs.

Mau leaves Limitless in the wall. He reaches for Shrine with his own body.

* * *

Gojo sees the edge of the barrier bite into the ground.

It should be smooth. Every time Mau practised this, the seam was smooth.

There is a line coming out of it now. A second curvature trying to close inside the first. Gojo can see exactly what Mau is asking it to do. He can see where it starts to answer.

“Let that one go,” he says. ᴳ

Yuta looks at him.

Gojo is speaking to himself. His hands have closed around the railing, and he is already pulling them away before anyone has to tell him the man below cannot hear him.

* * *

A strip of stone under Sukuna's heel opens in parallel cuts. He lifts his foot just in time to keep it, and Igris's blade goes through the hand that had been waiting for him there.

It worked on that strip. Mau has room to take one step and put the point of his dagger through Sukuna's side. For one stupid, exhilarating instant he can feel how a person could build an entire fight around that single step.

Then both edges ask him to decide what a target is.

He tells the second one to stop.

The first one tries to stop with it.

Something goes white behind his left eye. Not light. Light would have given him something to see.

Ciel cuts the connection. The nested line dies first. The barrier falls outward in dry pieces. The quarry comes back into his right eye and nowhere else.

Sukuna hits him in the throat.

Mau puts a hand on the stone because if he does not he will fall. The hand lands. He doesn't feel the landing until he is trying to lift it.

“Did it close?” ᴹ

“No.” ᶜ

He hears her say something else. He can tell from the shape of her voice that she has said it twice.

He lets go of Shrine.

* * *

Sukuna tries to open Mau's hand with a cut. Mau sees the fingers move and drops the dagger himself. It turns once. Bellion catches the handle, drives it across the ground with the flat of his sword, and puts it where Mau's other hand can reach.

The cut never arrives.

Sukuna looks down at his own palm. One of the mouths smiles. The other spits blood.

He has used the same hand for that cut a hundred times. This time all it has given him is enough heat to feel the skin part where Igris struck.

He closes it into a fist.

* * *

For a while Elmesia can only make sense of the fight by following the discarded things. A broken sword dragged away from a foot. A fallen piece of a shadow's armour. Two bodies moving around each other where there had been a domain large enough to hide them both.

Coco hears Qifrey set his cane down. She expects him to say something about how the barrier broke. Instead he puts his open hand beside hers. She can take it or leave it there.

She takes it.

In the protected room DIO reaches for the back of a chair she has not been using. Ashborn brings the chair the rest of the way. The shadow in the stands does not move. Neither of them speaks down the connection.

* * *

Mau attempts Blue once more.

He knows the shape. That is the terrible part. He can stand in the ruined quarry and explain the shape to himself down to the turn of his wrist. When he tries to make it, the pain drops his wrist before he has gathered enough force to pull a pebble.

Sukuna sees it.

He takes Mau by the front of his shirt and runs him backward until the stone stops them. Mau can't pull space out from behind his heels. He has one good leg and the knife in his left hand. He bends his knee, drags Sukuna down to it, and feels the knife go in.

Sukuna leans farther into it instead of pulling away.

Mau has to let go of the handle to keep the other man's thumbs out of his eyes.

His fingers close around Sukuna's wrist. The new hand obeys. The old one obeys a little later. He makes them hold anyway.
