It is the longest he has gone all day.

They come together again in the middle of the broken floor, and the next exchange is the fastest either of them has made. It's so fast it barely shows up. On the panels there is dust, and a small shock in the dust, and the shock is somewhere else. Ramiris's picture has started to fall behind. Hinata, who has made a career of following things like this, finds she is mostly following the rocks.

Sukuna changes a condition Mau has only just learned. Mau sees what the change cost him and goes for the place it left uncovered. More information hasn't become prophecy. It has only made the questions harder for both of them. The lower hands strike. The upper hands start a sign, and Mau steps inside it, so close that the plane would have to divide the spot Sukuna needs to stand in to keep it honest. Sukuna lets it go, changes to Cleave and opens his shoulder.

Mau's dagger goes through the arm that did it.

A thin line appears on Sukuna's ribs. Another on the side of his neck. Mau throws them almost absently, between the serious things, two neat little Dismantles no longer than a finger, hardly worth closing. Sukuna doesn't bother. He has a hole in his stomach and a bad shoulder. He has been cut by masters.

“You've gotten dainty,” he says. ˢᵘ

*Mau, the lower one.* ᶜⁱ

That's three.

There are the corners. There's the one that's a lie.

Mau could take a door. Five breaths, he's only on five.

He doesn't want a door.

There's the stone sliding, and the *Back!* that Ramiris's light carries as a tiny distant bark, and then nothing's there.

Gojo has very good eyes. He looks up at the empty air.

“Two what?” ᴳ

* * *

Mau can feel it in the bottom of his shadow. It's three notches of something he has no name for.

He can't call him again. He knows that without asking. He reached down there just now, and what answered had no more than a shape to it. Any more and the piece that's left won't be a piece. Three, then. It'll have to do. It's not a plan, it's more of a hope with a bad leg.

Sukuna's looking at the place where it went. He looks at the air above it. He has seen the wheel work from closer than anyone alive and he's not a stupid man. He's thinking it over, and he likes it very much, and he's on the point of saying so.

Mau hits him in the mouth.

He uses his head. He has been hit by that man for most of an afternoon and he has learned a good deal about the geography. He goes in low, under the upper arms, with his chin tucked. The top of his skull comes up into the wet place under Sukuna's ribs, right on the dagger hilt, and he drives it in. Something crunches. There are teeth in there; there are actually teeth. They come away against his forehead. They're quite long. The voice stops as if somebody has shut a door on it.

He hits it again.

“*Hghk,*” says the lower mouth. ˢᵘ

An elbow lands on his spine. He doesn't feel it. He's got his forehead right in there, and his teeth bared. He feels a hand take his hair and lift, and he goes up, right off the ground, a long way, kicking. He's looking into the upper face from about a foot away. It has blood all over it. It is absolutely radiant.

Mau lands on a slab. He's still got time to think, a little.

He has put marks in stone. Nobody's noticed. In the last two minutes, among all the rest, he has thrown three of those dainty small cuts at nothing in particular. One's in a shelf behind Sukuna's left shoulder. One's in a boulder, one's in the floor. He has been feeding them a thread of himself, each no more than a thought, and they're sitting there now just as those little door handles sat all morning. It's a good trick. He has it on excellent authority.

He gets up. The man comes on across the rubble with his lower jaw hanging, in no hurry, still enjoying himself.

*You can't,* Ciel says. ᶜⁱ

*I know how it goes.*

*You know how it goes for a man who's practised it for years with four hands. You have a burned brain and a filling in your heart. Knowing is not the same as your body doing it. We talked about this. Everyone's talked about this. It'll take a fifth of what's left. If you get the assignment wrong, it'll cut you. And you need three, and you can't lie.*

*I know.*

*You have to mean them.* ᶜⁱ

He looks at Sukuna.

He looks at him properly, for the first time. It's a strange thing. He hasn't really looked since the path closed. There's the whole terrible height of him, all that blood, and he's got a torn stomach and he's lost count of his own wounds, and he is walking across a field with his shoulders loose, because it hasn't occurred to him in a thousand years to walk any other way. He has been doing this since before there were any of the countries on that ridge. He has never once been sorry. He got here on his own. Nobody gave him anything. Everything he has he built, with his own hands, out of being bored.

It's not very hard, as it turns out. That's the awful thing.

His nose is pouring. There is a high thin noise behind his left eye. He has never been so frightened in his life. He looks up at the man who killed the sun, and he says the last of it, and it's the truest thing he knows.
