# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 59
## Chapter 9 — Close Enough to Hold

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1 (2026-11-03)
**Volume:** 59
**Reading order:** 9
**Source episode:** S9E47
**Continuity state:** B136 continuous. Both active. Wounds and finite reserves accumulate. No aid from stands or guests.

*When: Sukuna twists his wrist free, Mau finds that he has kept hold of two fingers.*

He uses them to pull.

Sukuna comes with the pull. His elbow catches Mau above the eye. Mau's knee gives on the next step and for a moment neither of them is standing; they are only heavier than the piece of stone the other man wants to get to first.

Sukuna gets there. He drives Mau's damaged leg against the edge.

The leg folds. Mau does not scream until he tries to put weight back on it. He can feel the Black Heart send more than enough mana toward the break. What he cannot do is get the bone to hold still long enough to set. Every time he gets the ends to touch, Sukuna makes him move again.

“Leave the knee,” Ciel says. “Brace it.” ᶜ

He gets a shadow under it. The brace is Igris's forearm.

Igris takes the weight without a word. Mau pushes off him and puts his forehead into Sukuna's lower mouth. Teeth come away against his skin.

Then Sukuna drives the dagger that is still in his own side deeper. Mau has barely understood why when the handle disappears beyond his reach and Sukuna's freed hand catches him by the throat.

* * *

Rem feels Umaru turn before she hears her say anything.

She pulls her close. Umaru puts both hands against Rem's ribs as if there is something she should push away, then leaves them there.

“I know he's still up,” Umaru says. ᵁ

“He is.” ᴿᵉ

She holds the back of Umaru's head and covers the gap between their faces and the field with her shoulder. Taihei sits down on the other side. The three of them stay that way through a sound none of them can pretend came from the stone.

A few seats over, Ori asks Frieren if she can go inside without making Mau think she left.

Frieren takes her hand.

“He knows you came.” ᶠʳ

Ori thinks about it. Then she asks if she can bring somebody. There is a girl in the aisle who has been trying to get past her own knees for several seconds. Ori knows that look. She gets up, goes to Agott and asks her if she wants to come find Shizue.

Agott says yes on the second try.

* * *

Mau tries to draw air through a grip that only tightens when he fights it.

Bellion comes in from Sukuna's blind side. Sukuna lets his own back take the point. It goes through enough flesh to stop before it can find Mau. He catches Bellion's sword under one elbow and brings his other fist down on the shadow's head. The armour loses shape. Mau feels the link sink into the floor. The next time he tries to bring Bellion back, he gets the movement of a hand and nothing attached to it.

He stops asking.

Sukuna hasn't let go.

Mau presses his free palm into the wound where the dagger went. His fingers find the handle.

He can't draw it out. Sukuna has already made sure of that. He can turn it.

The grip loosens for less than a second. Mau gets enough air for one small, animal noise and turns the knife again.

They hit the ground apart.

* * *

Shoko sees Mau roll onto the injured leg to get the other one under him. She had taught him how to get off the floor with a broken rib. She had never taught him what to do when the list of things he could safely put weight on had come down to one foot.

“I helped send him down there,” she says. ˢʰ

Gojo doesn't tell her the city would have been at risk either way. She knows what was at risk. She had counted that before she said he should go.

He puts his hand beside hers. She grips two fingers hard enough to hurt.

“Don't tell me you can see how this ends.” ˢʰ

“I can't.” ᴳ

She believes him. It is the first thing he has said in a long time that she wishes he had lied about.

* * *

Across the ridge a man who had come to see whether the settlement could defend his town asks Elmesia what happens if the one who gets up is Mau.

“He goes home to his family,” she says. ᴱˡ

The man keeps looking at the quarry. He had meant to ask something else. He doesn't have the nerve to say it now that she has answered him.

Makima hears him thank her anyway.

* * *

Sukuna gets his feet under him first.

Mau sees him lift a hand. Nothing gathers. Sukuna looks at the hand, and then at Mau, and makes a fist slowly enough that Mau can see the smallest finger fail to close.

Mau's right hand still closes all the way.

Sukuna has three more chances to make one fist than he does. Mau gets up before the man can choose which one to use.
