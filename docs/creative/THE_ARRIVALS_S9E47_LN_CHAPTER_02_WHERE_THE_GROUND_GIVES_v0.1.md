# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 59
## Chapter 16 — Where the Ground Gives

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.2 (2026-11-03)
**Volume:** 59
**Reading order:** 16
**Source episode:** S9E47
**Continuity state:** B+136 continuous. Both active. Black Flash in the clinch costs Mau the use of his right arm. Igris takes a second arm off Sukuna (two remain); Igris's and Beru's forms lost, not permanent. Mau's usable offensive CE spent. Margin held. Neither margin nor mana is an offensive CE refill.

*When: Sukuna comes for the good leg, Mau knows it is the only sensible thing left to do.*

He still can't stop him.

He gets a forearm down between the hand and his knee. The hand goes around it. Another takes his ankle. He has time to get one breath and look for the ground before Sukuna puts him on it.

The impact leaves a space in the noise. Into that space comes the sound of somebody stamping on the stone twice. It takes him a moment to know it is his own heel.

He gets his fingers into a seam the barrier left in the quarry. The stone comes loose. He takes a piece of it with him when Sukuna drags him back, and the edge goes into the hand holding his ankle. Sukuna changes hands. Mau hits that one too.

On the next change there is a moment with no hand on the ankle at all. Mau kicks him in the side where the knife went in.

The body above him contracts around the wound. Mau goes with it. He is close enough to hear the lower mouth trying to breathe through broken teeth when Sukuna punches him in the chest.

There is a flash between their bodies. For an instant the dust stands up in fine black lines.

Mau loses the use of his right arm. He knows this because he is giving it an instruction and it keeps hanging beside him. The new shoulder gives him a perfectly clear account of what has happened. The arm gives him nothing. It grew in a furnace, it has been the best thing that's happened to him all day, and now it's a thing he's carrying.

* * *

“Can he put it back?” Kita asks. ᴷⁱ

She means the arm. She can hear how little of the question she has managed to say.

Bocchi has both hands on the bench. She spent the morning rehearsing what she would shout for him. When the arm fell she made the noise she used to make in a room by herself when something was too loud and she couldn't find a way to turn it off.

Kita moves closer. Bocchi takes one hand off the bench and finds hers.

On the next row, Yuka has started to go down toward the rail. Mai puts a hand behind her elbow and helps her the rest of the way. There is no reason she should have to do it alone.

Yuta makes room. Maki stays on Yuka's other side. For a while the only thing any of them does is make sure she has somewhere to put her feet.

* * *

Igris can still stand. Beru can still move. Mau can feel the soldiers farther out, waiting on paths he can no longer hold open together. When he tries the first two at once, he loses track of which one he is standing on.

He leaves one open.

Igris comes up behind Sukuna. He has no sword, no armour to speak of, about two thirds of a body. He puts both arms around one of Sukuna's and leans back.

Mau reaches for the hilt in Sukuna's side with his left hand. Sukuna bites that hand. Mau has to use the heel of it instead of the fingers. He presses against the hilt. Sukuna bites down harder. Nothing in either man's body gives him a reason to be the one who lets go.

Beru has a clear line to the throat for the first time in a long while. One of Sukuna's feet comes off the ground. He turns with Igris still attached to him and drives the ant into the stone with the other foot.

Igris pulls again.

This time he comes away holding an arm.

He stands there with it, a knight who has not said one word all day, holding the arm of the king of curses against his chest, like something he has been asked to look after.

Two, then. Sukuna has two hands. It is the first time anybody has seen him with no more than anyone else.

Mau makes a fist of what is left of his grip. The strike lands where the upper and lower chests meet. Black spreads across his knuckles. The force runs through the body he is holding and comes back up his wrist.

He had forgotten what it was like to hit something as hard as he could and find out what it could do to him too.

Sukuna falls one step. He takes the last of Igris with him, one backhand, no looking. Mau feels the armour come apart in the dark. Beru doesn't get up. *My King,* something says at the bottom of his shadow, very faint, *I was—* and stops.

The last of the cursed energy Mau could put behind a blow goes with it. He can tell where Ciel has kept the rest. It is so small he could spend it trying to make another strike and still fail to move the hand.

“Leave it,” he says. ᴹ

“I will.” ᶜⁱ

* * *

Sukuna wipes his upper mouth with the back of a hand. There isn't much left to wipe.

“Are you waiting for a sword?” ˢᵘ

Mau looks at the place where Bellion's went down. It is farther than the other man is.

He shakes his head.

Sukuna comes the last step to him. He's close enough to bite. He stands there, breathing, with the whole quarry gone silent around them, and he looks Mau up and down, at the leg with nothing under it, and at the arm. He looks like someone going over a house he has already sold.

Then he smiles with half a face, and he takes a step back.

He takes another.

It's such an odd thing to see, after all this. He walks backward over the rubble, unhurried, three steps, five, ten. He doesn't take his eyes off Mau. He stops about twenty paces off, on a bare flat place in the rock. He sets his feet.

“You read it,” Sukuna says. ˢᵘ

It isn't a question.

Mau nods.

“Then you know I've never done this,” says the king of curses. He holds his two hands up in front of his chest, and they're perfectly steady, and they aren't empty at all. “I wanted you to know that.” ˢᵘ
