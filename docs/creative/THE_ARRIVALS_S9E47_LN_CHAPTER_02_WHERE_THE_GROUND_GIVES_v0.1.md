# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 59
## Chapter 10 — Where the Ground Gives

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1
**Volume:** 59
**Reading order:** 10
**Source episode:** S9E47
**Continuity state:** B+136 continuous. Both active. Final usable offensive CE spent. Neither clinical margin nor mana is an offensive CE refill.

*When: Sukuna comes for the good leg, Mau knows it is the only sensible thing left to do.*

He still can't stop him.

He gets a forearm down between the hand and his knee. The hand goes around it. Another takes his ankle. He has time to get one breath and look for the ground before Sukuna puts him on it.

The impact leaves a space in the noise. Into that space comes the sound of somebody stamping on the stone twice. It takes him a moment to know it is his own heel.

He gets his fingers into the seam the nested barrier left in the quarry. The stone comes loose. He takes a piece of it with him when Sukuna drags him back. The edge goes into the hand holding his ankle. Sukuna changes hands. Mau hits the second one too.

On the third change there is a moment with no hand on the ankle at all. Mau kicks him in the side where the knife went in.

The body above him contracts around the wound. Mau goes with it. He is close enough to hear the lower mouth trying to breathe through broken teeth when Sukuna punches him in the chest.

There is a flash between their bodies. For an instant the dust stands up in fine black lines.

Mau loses the use of his right arm. He knows this because he is giving it an instruction and it keeps hanging beside him. The fresh shoulder gives him a perfectly clear account of what has happened. The arm gives him nothing.

Sukuna tries to follow the flash with a cut.

He has enough left to open the front of Mau's shirt. The line underneath only goes as far as the ribs. Sukuna watches it stop.

Mau sees him understand.

* * *

“Can he put it back?” Kita asks. ᴷⁱ

She meant the arm. She can hear how little of the question she managed to say.

Bocchi has both hands on the bench. She had spent the morning rehearsing what she would shout for him. When the arm fell she had made the noise she used to make in a room by herself when something was too loud and she couldn't find a way to turn it off.

Kita moves closer. Bocchi moves one hand off the bench and finds hers.

On the next row, Yuka has started to stand. Mai puts a hand behind her elbow and helps her the rest of the way. There is no reason she should have to sit through it.

Yuta makes room at the rail. Maki stays on Yuka's other side. For a while the only thing any of them does is make sure she has somewhere to put her feet.

* * *

Igris can still stand. Beru can still move. Mau can feel the soldiers farther out waiting on paths he can no longer hold open together. When he tries the first two paths at once, he loses track of which one he is standing on.

He leaves one open.

Igris comes up behind Sukuna. The sword is gone. He puts both arms around two of Sukuna's and pulls.

Mau reaches for the hilt in Sukuna's side with his left hand. Sukuna bites that hand. Mau has to use the heel of it instead of the fingers. He presses against the hilt. Sukuna bites down harder. Nothing in either man's body gives him a reason to be the one who lets go.

Beru has a clear line to the throat for the first time in a long while. One of Sukuna's feet comes off the ground. He turns with Igris still attached to him and drives Beru into the stone with the other foot.

Igris pulls again.

This time he comes away holding an arm.

Mau makes a fist of what is left of his grip. The strike lands where the upper and lower chests meet. Black spreads across his knuckles. The force runs through the body he is holding and comes back up his wrist.

He had forgotten what it was like to hit something as hard as he could and find out what it could do to him too.

Sukuna falls one step. He takes the last of Igris with him. Mau feels the shadow's armour come apart in the dark.

The last of the curse energy Mau could put behind a blow goes with it. He can tell where Ciel has kept the rest. It is so small he could spend it trying to make another strike and still fail to move the hand.

“Leave it,” he says. ᴹ

“I will.” ᶜ

Sukuna wipes his upper mouth with the back of a hand.

“Are you waiting for a sword?” ˢᵘ

Mau looks at the place where Bellion's had gone down. It is farther than the other man is.

He shakes his head.

Sukuna comes the last step to him.
