# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 59
## Chapter 11 — The Weight of the Other Man

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1
**Volume:** 59
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S9E48
**Continuity state:** B+136 continuous. Physical clinch, no offensive casting. Both down, both records active. Mau spends finite medical CE with Ciel.

*When: Mau catches Sukuna's first punch on his shoulder, it is by accident.*

He had meant to go to the left. The knee that used to take him left goes straight down. The blow hits higher than Sukuna intended and takes them both off balance.

Mau goes first. He gets his head under Sukuna's chin and pushes until he feels the edge of the quarry step against the other man's heels.

He has just enough strength to push him over it.

Sukuna has just enough strength to take him along.

* * *

They fall less than the height of a man. It looks like nothing from the ridge.

Mau lands with the weight of Sukuna's remaining arms across his ribs. For a moment the world becomes exactly as wide as the place he cannot draw breath into.

His right arm still won't come up. He can move the elbow a little if he turns his whole body. So he turns. Sukuna gets one hand underneath him, then a second. The third goes searching for his throat in the small dark space between them.

Mau finds the handle of the dagger against his stomach.

This time he doesn't try to pull it free. He takes the hand looking for his throat and closes it around the handle with his own. Sukuna could let it go. Mau feels him make the decision to use the grip and wrench.

The blade comes out of Sukuna.

Mau rolls toward it. The point goes into him low. He spends everything the good leg has left driving himself farther along the blade, until the guard stops him and Sukuna can no longer bring the hand up.

Their foreheads touch.

Mau slips his free hand behind Sukuna's head and drives it against the bottom step.

Once.

Sukuna claws under his jaw.

Twice.

Mau's hand opens. He has to look at it to find out why. Then the two of them go down together.

* * *

Yuka hears the ridge begin to move. One person standing, then another, each waiting to see who will be the first to go down.

It is Milim who puts her hands out across the entrance. She puts them high, where even the people behind her can see what she is doing.

“Wait for them to tell us.” ᴹⁱ

Her voice shakes. The nearest guard stops anyway.

At the tables, both clerks look down. Gazel's clerk raises the slate. Testarossa does the same a breath later. Still active. The same for both.

Yuka looks back. From up here there is so little difference between two living men and two heaps of clothing that she has to find the back of Mau's head to recognize him.

She had kissed him beside a screen this morning. He had turned around because she asked him to. She wants to ask him again.

* * *

In the protected room Ashborn stands up.

He was standing before he decided to. He goes as far as the door, puts a hand on it and remembers that he would still be on this side if he opened it. The shadow they are watching through sits where Mau asked it to sit.

DIO draws the empty chair closer to hers.

“We can wait for him here.” ᴰ

Ashborn returns to the chair. He sits on the edge of it. Neither of them asks the shadow for a closer view.

* * *

The first thing Ciel can get Mau to do is swallow.

It takes three tries. She waits through all three.

There is a route she can keep warm at the base of his skull with the reserve he asked her to hold. She spends it there. The rest of his body can wait. If he loses that little route, nothing he has learned about putting himself back together will have anywhere to begin.

She tells him when she has spent it.

Mau doesn't answer. She can feel him trying to move his tongue again. She tells him to save it.

He tries the knee instead. This time nothing is driving it sideways. The brace takes. He can feel mana move through the bone and the rough line of the repair hold for one breath.

He gives it a second breath before he trusts it with anything more.

Beside him, Sukuna plants a hand.

It slides away on the first attempt.

On the second, the fingers find dry stone.

Mau's hand is under him too. He cannot remember putting it there. He bears down on it until the rest of the arm comes into view.

Two men on the ground begin to get up.
