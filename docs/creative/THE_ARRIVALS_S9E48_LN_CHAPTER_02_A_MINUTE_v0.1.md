# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 59
## Chapter 12 — A Minute

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1
**Volume:** 59
**Reading order:** 12
**Source episode:** S9E48
**Continuity state:** B+136 continuous. Sukuna dies irreversibly, records agree. Brief voluntary Arise and self-chosen return to abyss. Mau alive with major fresh injuries. No permanent roster addition.

*When: Mau puts his weight on the leg he has repaired, it takes him as far as one knee.*

Sukuna gets farther. He gets his feet under him, one after the other, and comes over the flat rock with his hand out.

Mau knows the reach of that arm now. He knows the last two fingers won't close. He lets the hand come down on the dagger's hilt and puts his palm across the wrist before Sukuna can pull.

Neither of them can draw it alone.

Mau pulls with him.

The knife comes out. Sukuna puts his other hand on Mau's shoulder. Mau has seen him use that grip enough times today to know where he will want to put the foot. He shoves his bad knee under it. The man stumbles against him.

Mau turns the point.

The knife goes back into the wound it made the first time.

Sukuna takes him down with a hand already closed on his throat. Mau tries to turn the blade, and the hand closes farther. For a while they lie there working at it. Sukuna can close his hand a little more. Mau can turn his wrist a little farther.

Then one of them can't.

Sukuna's grip opens a finger at a time. Mau keeps his hand on the knife after he feels the man stop pulling against it. There is nothing left in the wound trying to close around the steel.

He lets go.

* * *

The clerk raises the slate. Testarossa reads hers before she looks up. She holds it at the same height as his.

The word comes down from both sides.

Mau tries to stand. His body gets halfway and stops. He looks for Yuka and sees her colour coming down the steps, then the smaller movement of Shoko drawing level with her. Nobody is trying to stop them now.

“A minute,” he says, to the ground beside him. ᴹ

Yuka hears him. She puts one hand out toward Shoko so they both stop where he can see them.

“If you want it,” Mau says. ᴹ

He puts his left palm down in the shadow and says the word.

* * *

Sukuna rises far enough to sit beside him.

He looks at his hands. He turns one palm up as if he expects to find the marks in it from the stone. Mau watches him flex all five fingers.

“You could have asked for longer,” Sukuna says. ˢᵘ

Mau shifts his heel out of the blood.

“Would you have said yes?” ᴹ

Sukuna thinks about it. A smile comes back slowly to the mouth that had lost its teeth.

“I said yes to this.” ˢᵘ

For the length of a breath neither says anything. Mau can hear the people descending. Sukuna can too. He looks where they are coming from, and then over the edge at the stone where the two of them had stopped moving.

“Was it worth dying for?” Mau asks. ᴹ

Sukuna looks back at him.

“It was a worthy death.” ˢᵘ

He says it with the same attention he gave Mau's first step into the quarry. As if getting that part right matters even now.

The minute isn't quite gone when he puts both hands down and pushes himself upright. The shadow beneath him opens with the motion. Mau can feel the choice go through it. He keeps his hand flat on the ground until Sukuna has gone as far as he wishes to go.

The place beside him empties.

* * *

Yuka comes the rest of the way down. There is a place where she can kneel that will keep the weight off his leg. Shoko shows it to her with one hand while the other reaches for his neck.

Mau searches for something to say that will tell them how much of him is still there. The more he looks for it, the farther away it gets.

Yuka sets her palm down in front of him.

“You can just touch it.” ʸ

He moves his smallest finger across the stone.

She meets him where it stops.
