# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 60
## Chapter 1 — All the Way Up

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1
**Volume:** 60
**Reading order:** 1
**Source episode:** S9E49
**Continuity state:** B+136. Quarry triage and evacuation. Confirmed verdict stands. No additional shadow.

*When: Shoko asks Mau to look at her, he turns his whole head.*

She catches it before he can turn far. A look should cost him less than that.

“Just the eye that works.” ˢʰ

His right eye finds her. It slips once and comes back. She had let herself picture a great many things while he was on the ground. She had forgotten he might need to be told where to look.

Yuta opens the kit beside her. His hand goes straight to the roll she wants. He holds it within reach and waits for her to take it, then puts both hands where she shows him. They work until the terrible small effort it takes Mau to get air stops getting smaller.

Shoko lifts her head.

“Gojo. We need the board down here.” ˢʰ

She can see him take the first two steps too fast and make himself slow down. He brings the board the rest of the way at a walk.

* * *

“Can you tell me before you move him?” Yuka asks. ʸ

“Yes.” ˢʰ

Shoko tells her which way they will turn him and where the knife wound is. She speaks quietly. Yuka gets all of it. There is a point where Shoko could leave out what lifting him might do to the knee. She includes that too.

Yuka looks down at Mau.

“I'll go up on this side. You can look this way the whole time.” ʸ

Mau's mouth moves.

She leans in. He tries again.

“I thought you went up already.” ᴹ

“I haven't.” ʸ

Shoko sees the look Yuka gives her across the board. Mau knew where she was a moment ago. Now he doesn't. Shoko nods once and puts the new fact with everything else they will have to watch on the way home.

* * *

At the edge of the quarry the clerks leave the two copies on separate tables for inspection. A man wants to know whether the announcement meant the winner can hold anybody he kills after this. The clerk reads the line with Sukuna's name on it and waits until the man has finished reading it for himself.

Uraume comes to the outer mark and asks for the body. Testarossa hears the request there. She makes sure neither of them will have to take the route the family is using. The answer and the conditions are entered on both copies. No bargain changes hands.

Makima listens until the clerk has finished explaining.

“I wonder which part they'll tell their children,” she says. ᴹᵏ

Elmesia keeps a place clear by the rail. From where Makima is standing she might think the queen has failed to hear her. From the opposite bench the schoolgirls see Elmesia point out a path to a man who wants to leave and can't bring himself to step toward the field again.

* * *

They get Mau as far as the second landing before they have to put him down.

Shoko puts her hand flat against his chest. Yuta is already on his knees on the other side.

Yuka stops herself from calling his name. Shoko has asked him to breathe, and she wants that to be the only thing he has to listen to.

From below, someone calls that the return route is open. Gojo calls back that they need a minute. He says it in the same voice he would use to ask somebody to hold an elevator.

They get a minute.

When Shoko looks up this time, Mau is watching her before she asks.

“Are we going home?” ᴹ

“We are.” ˢʰ

She gives him the count before they lift again. At the top, the people on the path back up to let Yuka keep her place beside him. Nobody asks her to come away so somebody stronger can walk there.
