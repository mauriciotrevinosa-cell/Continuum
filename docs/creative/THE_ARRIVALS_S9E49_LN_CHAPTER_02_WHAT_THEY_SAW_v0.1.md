# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 60
## Chapter 4 — What They Saw

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1
**Volume:** 60
**Reading order:** 4
**Source episode:** S9E49
**Continuity state:** B+137. Medical observation continues. Moratorium through noon. Separate departures. Divergent witness accounts.

*When: Rem comes back from the clinic, Umaru asks her whether she managed to sleep.*

“Some,” Rem says. ᴿᵉ

Umaru nods. She had not meant Rem. It feels rude now to say that, so she turns the mug around between her palms until Rem puts a hand beside it.

“He did too.” ᴿᵉ

There is nowhere for the relief to go at first. Umaru gets up and takes the mug with her. She goes as far as the sink. Then she turns around and comes back.

“I couldn't see the end,” she says. ᵁ

“I know.” ᴿᵉ

“I wanted to.” ᵁ

Rem shifts her chair so Umaru can sit without moving her mug. For a while Umaru keeps both hands around it. Then she slips one under Rem's and lets it stay there.

Taihei comes through with a pan from the breakfast service. He asks how many eggs they want. Rem looks at Umaru instead of answering for her. Umaru says two. When he asks how she wants them she has to start over, because the first thing out of her mouth is a laugh.

* * *

Elmesia comes to sign the departure sheet after sunrise. The man from the ridge is waiting beside the clerk's table. He has two accounts folded into the same pocket.

“My brother lives on the road they were going to cut off,” he tells her. “I need to tell him why it's open.”

He takes out the top sheet and lets her see it. There is a description of teeth, and of a head against a stone step. No mention of a road. He folds it again.

“Did that happen?” he asks.

“Yes,” Elmesia says. ᴱˡ

He waits, perhaps for her to finish saying what happened. She gives him time. Finally he takes out the other sheet. It is the statement from the custodians. He reads the names at the bottom, slowly.

“Can I take both?”

“They're yours.” ᴱˡ

He tucks them away. Elmesia watches him pass a group who are going to carry the first account to a town farther east. She heard Makima ask one of them yesterday which of the two men he thought had looked more frightening at the end. The man had laughed when she asked. He wasn't laughing when he answered.

* * *

Qifrey finds Coco in the quiet courtyard with a blank sheet and a closed bottle. He sits beside her. When she makes room, she leaves the bottle between them.

“I thought I would want to draw something when I got home.” ᶜᵒ

“You still might.” ᑫ

“Not that.” ᶜᵒ

He hadn't asked her to. She needs to hear herself say it. He stays while she turns the paper over, and when she asks if they can go see Ori, he takes the paper with them.

* * *

At the clinic Yuka watches Mau close the fingers of his right hand. The first two move. The rest wait. He puts the hand back on the blanket.

“What happens if I try again?” ᴹ

“I'll ask you to stop again,” Shoko says. ˢʰ

He lets the hand lie there. It takes a while before the question he wanted to ask gets all the way to his mouth.

“How many times did I ask you that?” ᴹ

“Three.” ˢʰ

Yuka sees him try to make that funny. The smile begins and gives out. She moves her chair closer, where he can see her without having to keep turning his head.

The next time he asks, Shoko gives him the number again.
