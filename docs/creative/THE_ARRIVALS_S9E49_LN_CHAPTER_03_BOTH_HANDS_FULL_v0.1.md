# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 60
## Chapter 2 — Both Hands Full

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.2 (2026-11-03)
**Volume:** 60
**Reading order:** 2
**Source episode:** S9E49
**Continuity state:** B+136 night. Clinic at home. House rota for care and healing: Shoko and Yuta in shifts, Maomao and Jinshi run the list. Damage severe but recoverable with time. Yuka on the list like anyone else.

*When: they get him onto the clinic table, Maomao takes the pencil out of Jinshi's hand and writes the first name on the wall.*

She writes it on the wall because the paper keeps getting used to wipe things.

*Shoko. Now until she falls over.*

Under that, *Yuta*. Under that, a blank.

“He'll need somebody's energy going into him for about two days without a gap,” Maomao says, to the room in general. “Reverse output doesn't grow on trees. Two people can do it. So those two people are going to eat when I tell them, and sleep when I tell them, and everyone else in this building is going to carry things.” ᴹᵐ

The room has about nineteen people in it. Most of them have been standing against the walls for some time with their hands hanging, looking for something to hold.

“Carry what?” Stark asks. ˢᵗ

She looks at him as if he has finally said something useful.

“Water. A great deal of it. Clean cloth. Then the dirty cloth, the other way.” ᴹᵐ

He goes. Four others go with him. You can hear them on the stairs, relieved, arguing about who has the bigger bucket.

* * *

Shoko has her hands on his chest and has had them there for three hours.

It's slow work, which is a thing nobody ever believes about her. They see her close a cut in a kitchen with two fingers and they think that's what it is. This is building a wall one brick at a time, in the rain, while someone asks when it will be done. The lung first. Then the big vessel in the neck, which is holding on a strip of something that isn't quite flesh. The bone behind the knee. Somebody has shaved what was left of his hair, to get at the scalp, and it lies in a small burnt heap on the floor where nobody has had a hand free to sweep it.

“He's made of the strangest things,” she says. “Half his chest is new. I don't know whose it is. It's got no history.” ˢʰ

“Is that bad?” Yuta asks. ʸᵗ

“It heals like a baby's. It's the only part of him that's cooperating.” ˢʰ

Yuta has the other side. He hasn't done this much of it since a time he doesn't discuss. His forearms are shaking a little and he has moved his feet apart so that nobody will see. She sees. She nods toward the wall, where his name has acquired a time beside it.

“When it says stop, you stop. I am not treating two of you.” ˢʰ

“I know.” ʸᵗ

“I'm saying it now because in four hours I won't be polite.” ˢʰ

* * *

Yuka has the chair.

It's the good chair. Somebody brought it. She has been in it since they got here, with her knees against the side of the table, and she has one hand lying near his where he could find it if he were finding things. He isn't. He hasn't known anybody since the second landing.

There's dried blood up to her elbow. She doesn't remember how it got there.

Maki comes and stands beside her for some time without saying anything. Then she puts a bowl on Yuka's knee.

“I'm not hungry.” ʸ

“I didn't ask.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka looks at the bowl. She looks at the wall. Maomao has been adding to it. There's a long column of names now in that small mean handwriting: *Rem, Taihei, Mikasa. Rimuru, nights. Momo, kitchen. Kaneki, stairs.* It goes down nearly to the floor.

About halfway down it says *Yuka. Two hours. Then out.*

“She can't be serious,” Yuka says. ʸ

“She's never been anything else.” ᴹᵃ

“He's my husband.” ʸ

“Yes. And he's going to be lying on that table for a week, and then in a bed for another one. And then he'll be the worst patient anyone has ever had, and you'll want to have slept.” ᴹᵃ

Maki crouches. It takes some of the height out of it. She has a bruise on her jaw from something on the ridge; nobody's going to find out what.

“I watched somebody do it the other way,” she says. “It took about a month. By the end she was no use to him and he knew whose fault he thought it was. Eat your soup.” ᴹᵃ

Yuka eats some soup.

It goes down like gravel. Halfway through she starts to shake, and Maki stays exactly where she is and goes on looking at the wall as if she has found a spelling mistake. After a while Mai arrives on the other side, with a clean shirt and no comment.

When the two hours are up, Yuka stands. She looks at him for about as long as it takes to memorise something. Then she goes upstairs to their own bed, because it's on the wall, and lies down on top of the covers in the clean shirt with her boots on. She doesn't expect to sleep.

She sleeps nine hours. Nobody wakes her. She'll be furious about that, in the morning, and she'll eat breakfast anyway.
