# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 60
## Chapter 11 — Save Me a Place

**Status:** LN PRODUCTION DRAFT — S9 v0.1
**Volume:** 60
**Reading order:** 11
**Source episode:** S9E50
**Continuity state:** B+140. First short supported outing. Recovering, with weakness and neural follow-up; expected to return to prior health with time.

*When: Mau asks how far it is to dinner, Yuka says she will show him.*

She doesn't move from the doorway until he has his good foot under him. The cane is in his left hand. Shoko had wrapped the grip twice because the old fingers won't close right and the new ones still don't listen. In the clinic he'd said he could go out when they did. Shoko had asked who he was planning to let decide whether he could eat with his family.

He doesn't want her to know how long it took him to find an answer.

At the first step Yuka turns and walks backward. Her skirt moves at the edges of his sight.

“You can come closer,” he says. ᴹ

She does. She matches the pace of the cane.

* * *

Rimuru is in the common room moving a chair. Mau watches him move it another inch and move it back again.

“I can come back later if you want time alone with it.” ᴹ

Rimuru looks up. He starts to smile and has to make a second attempt at it.

“I thought you'd want the side that faces the door.” ᴿⁱ

Mau did. He hadn't said it. He stops beside the chair and gets his hand on the back of it.

“Will you sit on the other side?” ᴹ

Rimuru takes the chair he had been holding clear for somebody else. Across the room Rem sees him do it and brings a third bowl instead of carrying the two away to another table.

* * *

DIO comes through the interior passage with Ashborn just behind her. She stands where Mau can see her and lets him finish getting seated before she speaks.

“We brought your witness home.” ᴰ

The shadow from the ridge rises between their chairs. Its hands are empty. Mau had never thought what it would look like when the task was done and it had nowhere else it was expected to be.

“You can sit,” he tells it. ᴹ

It does. Ashborn takes the seat beside it. He reaches for the pitcher and fills two glasses, including the one Mau had forgotten he could ask for.

* * *

Umaru comes in late. She pauses when she sees him.

Mau tries to lift his right hand. It twitches on the table. He raises the other one instead.

“I saved you a place.” ᴹ

There are two places. Umaru looks back toward the door. Rem has just come in with another bowl, and Taihei follows with the bread he promised to warm. Umaru takes the nearest seat before anybody can make the choice harder.

She eats a little. Then she tells Mau he had better learn to eat with his left hand before the Fort finds out there is somebody it can legally defeat at cards.

“When was it illegal?” ᴹ

“You don't have clearance for that answer.” ᵁ

Rem puts a hand over her mouth. Taihei puts the bread down between them. Mau tries to laugh and stops when it pulls at his ribs, but the sound gets far enough to make Umaru laugh too.

* * *

After dinner Yuka takes him as far as the door to the courtyard.

“Here is good,” he says. ᴹ

She lets him stop there. Their chairs face the people still coming and going. There are plates left to clear and somebody is losing an argument about whether tea counts as another course. Mau listens to it until he can follow both voices again.

“Do you want me to tell you how the walk looked?” Yuka asks. ʸ

He considers it. This morning he would have wanted a number. Tomorrow he might want one again.

“Tell me how your day was first.” ᴹ

She puts her chair close enough that their knees touch. She starts with something that happened while he was asleep. A little thing. She hasn't told it to anyone else yet, and she takes her time with it.

Mau stays to hear the whole story.
