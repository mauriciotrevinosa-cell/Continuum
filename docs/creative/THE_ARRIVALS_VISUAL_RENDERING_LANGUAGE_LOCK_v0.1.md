# The Arrivals — Visual Rendering Language Lock v0.1

**Status:** CREATOR-APPROVED PRODUCTION LANGUAGE LOCK
**Scope:** visual development, calibration, manga pages, and color variants
**Spatial authority:** the latest creator-approved Spatial Bible and measured production plan always win.

## 1. Meaning of B&W

From this version onward, **B&W means native manga artwork**. It does not mean a color image converted to grayscale.

A valid B&W result must be authored for monochrome with:

- clear G-pen-like line hierarchy;
- deliberate solid-black masses and protected white space;
- screentone used as a designed value layer;
- selective hatching and texture;
- simplified, readable material detail;
- strong foreground / middle-ground / background separation.

Reject as a style failure when the result reads primarily as grayscale painting, desaturated color, charcoal, etched engraving, photographic monochrome, or uniform micro-detail without manga value design.

## 2. Meaning of color

From this version onward, **color means colored manga artwork**.

The black ink drawing remains the dominant construction. Color is applied beneath and around that drawing using:

- restrained flat local color;
- selective cel-shaded shadow shapes;
- controlled gradients only where they support depth or atmosphere;
- subtle paper or screentone texture;
- readable black linework and spot blacks after color is applied.

Reject as a style failure when the result becomes painterly concept art, loses the ink hierarchy, looks photorealistic, resembles a 3D render, or uses heavy HDR lighting.

## 3. Approved style sources and how to use them

Approved manga/anime material is used by **visual function**, never as a wholesale imitation and never as character identity.

| Approved source family | Permitted production role | Do not borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Witch Hat Atelier | Abandonment detail, overgrowth, moss and weathering, environmental texture, unused paths | Characters, costumes, props, architecture, or canon geometry |
| Call of the Night | Atmosphere, controlled decay, surrounding-environment mood, quiet depth without horror | Characters, signature lighting copied without scene justification, or setting geometry |
| Frieren | Spatial clarity, breathing room, restrained fantasy composition, hill/tree and distant vegetation readability | Character identity, wardrobe, proprietary props, or replacement building design |
| Dandadan | Vegetation energy, surface rhythm, selective finish; stronger use may be reserved for action | Characters, creatures, action effects, or uncontrolled distortion of space |

The combined house treatment may blend these functional lessons, but it must preserve the forest-enclosed geography and the approved inn design. The blend is subordinate to story continuity and measured space.

## 4. Reference-role contract

- **Structural/geography master:** controls shell, room layout, openings, camera plausibility, and major object placement only.
- **Autumn detail/material master:** controls timber, stone, plaster, foliage palette, and surface wear.
- **Atmosphere/shading master:** controls light direction, depth, air, and shadow hierarchy.
- **Blue-hour/night references:** used only for an explicitly requested time-of-day treatment.
- **Character references:** used only when those characters are actually present.
- **Style/technique references:** may guide marks, value structure, color handling, texture, and atmosphere; they may not provide character identity, wardrobe, canon geometry, lettering, or invented story objects.

References must be narrow and stage-appropriate. Restricted source excerpts must not be sent to a remote provider when Continuum policy forbids transmission. In that case use approved project-created derivatives and written technique summaries.

## 5. Spatial priority

No art-style reference may reinterpret approved geometry. For CAL-02 / CAL-03 specifically:

- the current measured room is the hard geometry authority;
- ruined and first-usable states use the same room and camera family;
- state changes may alter repair, cleanliness, supplies, and hearth activity, not room structure;
- rejected generated interiors have no spatial authority;
- the fireplace/chimney and stair must read as one directly adjacent architectural cluster from the approved entry camera; no window, full wall bay, or invented room may appear between them.

## 6. Evaluation shorthand

- **B&W / manga PASS:** designed ink, whites, screentone, line hierarchy, and selective texture.
- **B&W / manga FAIL:** grayscale or engraving logic dominates.
- **Color / colored manga PASS:** the same ink construction remains readable under restrained manga color.
- **Color / colored manga FAIL:** paint or rendering replaces the line-art construction.

Creator judgment remains the final promotion gate.
