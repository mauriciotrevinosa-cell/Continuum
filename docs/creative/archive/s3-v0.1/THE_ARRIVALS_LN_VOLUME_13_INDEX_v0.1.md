## Volume function

V13 turns informal hospitality into civic responsibility without treating residency as automatic. The second community presents a valid alternative survival model. Refuge pressure makes the unfinished settlement necessary before it is beautiful. Senku arrives only after work exists to criticize, with his reduced cognition visible to himself rather than hidden by the story.
