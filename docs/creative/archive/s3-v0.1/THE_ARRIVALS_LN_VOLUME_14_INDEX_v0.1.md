## Volume function

V14 makes the settlement physically and politically specific to Continuum. Imported systems change sanitation, maintenance and movement rather than only combat. Eren/Mikasa source-memory asymmetry becomes a freedom/agency problem without assigning guilt for unlived acts. Governance grows from actual cases, and the outside world begins attaching Mau's name to the settlement before its residents choose one.
