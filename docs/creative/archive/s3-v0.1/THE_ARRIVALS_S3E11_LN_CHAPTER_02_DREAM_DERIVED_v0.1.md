# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 11
## Chapter 14 — Dream-Derived

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 11  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S3E11 — `Reconstruction`

The dream-derived memories were harder because some dreams had predicted real things.

S2 had taught Mau the worst possible lesson:

dreams could be trustworthy.

So when a dream of the future site hardened into a memory of visiting it with Frieren, his mind had precedent.

They compared.

The remembered house did not exist.

The east window did.

Not physically.

As a choice.

Frieren had chosen it before walls.

Mau remembered waking there once.

Impossible.

He also remembered Frieren complaining that the morning light was too bright.

Plausible.

Future possibility wearing the emotional texture of past experience.

Rimuru wrote:

`DREAM-DERIVED / FUTURE-CONSTRUCTED`

Mau stared.

"So maybe this happens."

"Maybe," Rimuru said.

"Then it's not false."

Frieren answered.

"It didn't happen yet."

Mau looked at her.

"That's different."

"Yes."

He sat with that.

A memory could contain a future he still wanted.

He did not have to throw the want away because the memory was false.

That distinction saved more than the category deserved.
