# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 10 — The Letter

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 10  
**Source episode:** S3E14 — `Second Departure`

The letter was on the table.

That made it worse than the first disappearance.

The first time, Mau had been confused enough not to explain.

This time he had chosen words.

`I'm going to check something.`

`Ori is with me.`

`Please don't follow immediately.`

`I know how that sounds.`

Frieren stopped reading.

Momo read over her shoulder anyway.

"That's terrible."

Frieren continued.

`I don't think staying here while the memories keep changing is helping.`

`I know you would stop me because you love me.`

Frieren's hand shook.

`I love you too.`

That did not help.

`I need to know whether I can still choose something when I don't know what evidence belongs to me.`

`I will come back.`

No promise that he would be safe.

No destination.

No explanation of Ori's role.

Frieren folded the paper once.

Too neatly.

Ori's room was empty.

Frieren looked at the door.

The anger had two targets.

One she loved.

One she also loved now.

That made everything harder.

Rimuru read the letter.

"Immediate search."

No debate.

Mau had asked them not to follow immediately.

The household had learned enough not to obey that kind of request.
