# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 11 — Three Searches at Once

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Source episode:** S3E15 — `Second Search`

The second search was worse because it was organized.

They knew how to divide routes.

They knew how to preserve evidence.

They knew how to leave enough people behind.

They knew exactly what it cost.

Three efforts formed.

Frieren, Yuta and Himmel followed the route logic Mau was most likely to choose.

Rimuru, Diablo and Qifrey chased anomaly traces and memory-pressure reports.

Rem joined Mikasa, Kaneki and Aira on the southern road because travelers there had reported two strangers matching Mau and Ori's general description.

Okarun moved between groups.

Milim handled distance checks nobody else could reach quickly.

The witches sent information without joining.

Nobody trusted that arrangement.

Everybody used it.

At the future site, work continued with fewer hands.

Again.

A refugee family arrived.

Again.

One Searcher rumor had to wait.

Again.

This time nobody said the cost was unexpected.

They paid it anyway.

Agott stood over the planning board.

"At least we know why."

Coco looked at her.

"Do we?"

Agott hated that answer.

The search had better maps.

The people inside it did not have better certainty.
