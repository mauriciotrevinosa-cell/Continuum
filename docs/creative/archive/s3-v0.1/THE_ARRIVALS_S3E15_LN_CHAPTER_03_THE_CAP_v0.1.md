# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 13 — The Cap

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 13  
**Source episode:** S3E15 — `Second Search`

The cap reached Frieren before Mau did.

Okarun carried it between search lines.

He found Frieren near dusk.

She looked at the object in his hand.

Stopped.

Yuta recognized it a second later.

"No way."

Frieren took the cap.

Mud stained the brim.

Water had warped part of the shape.

The McLaren Triple Crown Edition cap Mau had brought from nowhere he remembered.

The one Momo had put on Bocchi.

The one Bocchi had worn into the mercantile town.

The one lost when blood mattered more than belongings.

Bocchi was not there to see it.

Frieren remembered anyway.

"Where?"

Okarun pointed south.

"Riverbank. Rem found it."

Yuta looked at the current.

"This doesn't prove Mau was there."

"I know."

The cap could have traveled for months.

Picked up.

Thrown away.

Washed through runoff.

Carried downstream.

A clue did not become magical because it was emotional.

Frieren held it tighter anyway.

"South."

They changed direction.

Not because the cap solved the search.

Because several weak clues had finally begun pointing the same way.
