# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 17 — Aira Doesn't Flinch

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S3E16 — `Body Noise`

The problem with rescue was movement.

Mau could not walk.

The organic structure reacted when anyone approached from behind.

Ori could touch him.

Kaneki could get close.

Rem could approach from the left without triggering the same response.

Nobody understood why.

Aira looked at the collapsing roof.

"We don't have time to understand why."

Correct.

The structure shuddered.

Aira stepped into range.

Mau shouted:

"Don't."

She did not stop.

"Great advice."

"Aira."

"I heard you."

The kagune-like limb snapped toward her.

Aira ducked badly.

It missed by less than it should have.

Mikasa swore.

Aira reached Mau's right side.

"Now what?"

Kaneki said:

"Keep talking."

"About what?"

"Anything."

Aira looked at Mau.

"You owe me new shoes."

Mau stared.

"What?"

"Mud."

"This is not my fault."

"You're in the mud."

"That's not causation."

"Good. Keep arguing."

They moved him three meters before the structure reacted again.

Aira flinched then.

Afterward.

When it no longer mattered.

Courage was not absence of fear.

It was terrible timing for fear.
