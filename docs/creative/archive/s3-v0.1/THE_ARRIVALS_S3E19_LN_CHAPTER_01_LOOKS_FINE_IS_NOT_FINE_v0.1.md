# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 12
## Chapter 23 — Looks Fine Is Not Fine

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 12  
**Reading order:** 23  
**Source episode:** S3E19 — `After Memory`

Mau looked fine.

So nobody used that sentence anymore.

They used questions.

"How many hours did you sleep?"

"Any memory mismatches today?"

"Any biological pain?"

"Any Noise terminology?"

"Do you want company?"

"Do you want none?"

The last question took longest to become normal.

Protocols appeared.

Not prison.

Signals.

If Mau said a memory felt wrong, nobody immediately argued.

If he asked `Do you remember it that way too?`, people answered only what they actually knew.

Raphael tracked thresholds.

Maomao tracked physical changes.

Kaneki tracked biological ones when relevant.

Ori tracked continuity.

Frieren tracked Mau.

Then corrected herself.

Frieren lived with Mau.

That was different.

At the future site, the second shelter became permanent storage by accident.

A new family arrived.

The mud path gained boards.

The old road remained wrong.

The city still did not exist.

But something had changed.

Mau stood beside the water plan.

Rimuru asked:

"Do you want to come on the northern search tomorrow?"

Mau looked at the route.

Once, he would have answered before the question finished.

"No."

Rimuru waited.

Mau looked toward the people who were going.

"They've got it."

Rimuru smiled.

"Yeah."

Mau stayed.

Not because he was sick.

Because other people could leave and come back.

The world did not end.

Neither did he.
