## Chapter 3 — Hospitality Is Not Citizenship

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2

**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E20 — `People Who Heard About Us`

The argument began because someone asked how long they could stay.

Nobody knew.

Rimuru said:

"As long as they're in danger."

Fern asked:

"Who decides when danger is over?"

Rimuru paused.

Good question.

Another resident asked whether people staying longer had to work.

Stark said:

"Everyone works."

Umaru shouted from behind him:

"FALSE."

Stark turned.

"YOU WORK."

"Under protest."

The room laughed.

The actual question remained.

Hospitality.

Temporary protection.

Permanent residence.

Alliance.

Guest.

Family.

Those words were not synonyms.

Mau listened from the side.

Months earlier he would have tried to solve the whole thing.

Now he asked one question.

"Does anyone need the answer today?"

Nano checked.

One family needed to know whether they could stay through the next two weeks.

That was answerable.

"Then answer that."

They did.

Two weeks guaranteed unless the family chose to leave earlier.

No promise beyond that.

No threat at the end.

A review.

Specific problem.

Specific rule.

Governance began as refusing to pretend they knew more than they did.
