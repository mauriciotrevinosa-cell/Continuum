# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 7 — Richeh

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 7  
**Source episode:** S3E22 — `Richeh`

The Searcher report said:

`young girl / witch hat style clothing / asking for Qifrey and Coco.`

Coco read the line three times.

Then ran.

Agott shouted after her.

Qifrey moved faster than both of them.

Mau saw three people leave the planning table without explanation.

He looked at Frieren.

"Should we—"

"No."

Good.

He stayed.

The reunion belonged to someone else.

Richeh was still on the road when Coco reached her.

For one second both stopped.

Recognition needed no system.

No contract.

No board.

Coco made a sound that was half name and half sob.

Then crossed the distance.

Richeh barely had time to brace.

Coco hit her hard enough that both nearly fell.

Words followed too fast.

Questions.

Apologies.

Proof of life.

Fragments of everything that had happened.

Richeh tried to answer.

Coco kept talking.

Eventually Richeh gave up and held on.

Qifrey arrived.

Then Agott.

The second wave was quieter.

Not less important.

By the time they walked toward the unfinished settlement, Coco had one hand locked around Richeh's sleeve as if Continuum might take people back when nobody watched.

It did not.

Not that day.
