# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 9 — Too Much Talking

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E22 — `Richeh`

Richeh's first dinner lasted too long.

Not because dinner took long.

Because Coco narrated Continuum.

Badly.

"This is Bocchi, she's Mau's sister but not actually, and this is Kita, they're dating, and that's Rimuru, he can become slime, and that's Milim, don't ask, and that's Diablo—"

"Why not ask?" Milim said.

Coco continued.

"—and that's Ori, she's from here, sort of, and—"

Ori raised a hand.

"Sort of?"

"Sorry."

"Accurate."

Richeh looked overwhelmed.

Qifrey finally intervened.

"She doesn't need everyone tonight."

Coco stopped.

Looked guilty.

Richeh took her hand.

"I want everyone."

Pause.

"Not all at once."

That became the rule.

For the next week, Richeh met the settlement in pieces.

A meal with Qifrey and Agott.

A walk with Coco.

A quiet conversation with Frieren about different magic assumptions.

A repair shift with Wakana.

No one assigned Mau a special introduction.

They met because Coco dragged Richeh to the water plan and Mau happened to be holding the wrong end of a measuring cord.

"You're Mau?"

He looked at Coco.

"What did you tell her?"

Coco smiled.

"Too much."

Richeh nodded.

"Way too much."

Good beginning.
