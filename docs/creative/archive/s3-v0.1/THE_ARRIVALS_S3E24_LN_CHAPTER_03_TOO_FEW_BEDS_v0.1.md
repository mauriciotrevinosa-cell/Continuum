# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 13
## Chapter 14 — Too Few Beds

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 13  
**Reading order:** 14  
**Source episode:** S3E24 — `Refuge`

They had forty-three sleeping spaces.

They needed fifty-one.

The number was wrong by breakfast.

Fifty-four.

Nano stopped updating the chalkboard for ten minutes out of spite.

Beds became politics.

Who needed privacy?

Who needed proximity to medical care?

Who could tolerate common rooms?

Who had children?

Who had trauma around closed doors?

Who snored badly enough to become a public issue?

Stark objected to the last category.

Everyone looked at him.

He withdrew the objection.

The old inn absorbed overflow again.

The future site absorbed more.

The distinction between old home and new refuge became logistical rather than emotional.

Mau moved between them and realized some new arrivals had never seen the inn at all.

To them, home was already the muddy place.

That surprised him.

A child asked where the town center was.

Mau pointed toward the tables.

"Currently?"

The child nodded.

Mau pointed again.

"There."

The child accepted that.

Adults were harder.
