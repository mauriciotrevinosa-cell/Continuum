They tore out the raised path first.

Three days of work.

Gone in an afternoon.

Momo complained the entire time.

Then carried boards.

Coco glared at Senku.

Then asked him why the new grade worked better.

Agott asked before Coco did.

That became a separate argument.

Senku did not redesign alone.

He tried once.

Rimuru stopped him.

"Explain."

Senku looked annoyed.

"Why?"

"So someone can repair it when you're not here."

That answer changed the meeting.

Qifrey added maintenance access.

Maomao separated clean and dirty service routes.

Wakana pointed out that workers needed places to actually stand while maintaining overhead elements.

Mikasa asked what happened during evacuation.

A native carpenter pointed out that Senku's first support assumption ignored local wood behavior.

Senku corrected it.

No ego crisis.

Good information.

By sunset the new plan belonged to more people than the old one.

Mau looked at the destroyed path.

"Feel bad?"

Coco asked.

"Yeah."

"Me too."

Senku heard.

"Good."

Coco glared.

He continued.

"If rebuilding doesn't hurt at all, you probably didn't build enough the first time."

Nobody found that comforting.

They rebuilt anyway.
