# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 8 — Emergency Means Temporary

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 8  
**Source episode:** S3E29 — `Who Decides?`

The first curfew lasted one night.

A hostile patrol had been seen two roads west.

Mikasa recommended no civilian travel after dark.

Reasonable.

Everyone agreed.

The second night, the patrol was gone.

Someone suggested keeping the curfew.

Mikasa said:

"No."

The room looked at her.

She continued.

"Emergency rule. Emergency ended."

That mattered.

A resident objected.

"What if they come back?"

"Then we reinstate it."

"Why remove something that makes us safer?"

Eren was not there yet.

The question arrived before him.

Mikasa looked toward the door.

"Because safety rules that never end stop being emergency rules."

Rimuru wrote the sentence down.

Every emergency measure gained:
- reason;
- start;
- review point;
- person or group responsible for ending it.

Not perfect.

Better.

Mau looked at the list.

After Sukuna, he understood why people accepted extreme rules when afraid.

After his own house arrest, he understood why ending them mattered too.
