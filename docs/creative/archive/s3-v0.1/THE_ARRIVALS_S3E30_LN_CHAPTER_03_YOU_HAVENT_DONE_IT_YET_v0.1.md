# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 14
## Chapter 11 — You Haven't Done It Yet

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 14  
**Reading order:** 11  
**Source episode:** S3E30 — `The Sea`

Eren learned enough on the road to become angry.

Not at Mikasa.

At the shape of the problem.

"You remember me doing things I haven't done."

"Yes."

"Things you hate."

Mikasa did not answer.

That was answer enough.

Eren kicked a stone off the path.

"So what am I supposed to do with that?"

Anko said:

"Nothing yet."

Eren looked at her.

She shrugged.

"You haven't done it."

Mikasa closed her eyes.

The sentence was brutal because it was obvious.

Eren looked back at her.

"Do you think I would?"

Mikasa hated the question.

Because source memory said yes.

Current person had not chosen.

"I don't know what you will do here."

Eren looked away.

That was the only fair answer.

At the settlement, no one arrested him.

No one disarmed him because of future acts he had not lived.

No one pretended the risk meant nothing either.

Mikasa explained what she knew to the small group that handled dangerous-source continuity.

Eren was present.

That mattered.

No secret trial about his own future.

The conclusion was narrow:

Current conduct matters.

Known capabilities matter.

Future-source information can inform caution.

It cannot become guilt.

Eren hated the distinction.

Then accepted it because the alternative was worse.
