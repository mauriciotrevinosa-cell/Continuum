# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 3 — Searcher Evidence

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E34 — `The Pattern`

Searchers brought stories.

That was normal.

Now the stories lined up too well.

A town warned that Otherworlders contaminated wells.

No evidence.

Another warned they replaced children.

No evidence.

A third claimed the settlement required new arrivals to surrender memories.

Mau stared at that one.

"That's creative."

Frieren did not laugh.

The rumor had appeared after the Memory Arc.

Details nobody outside should have known were twisted into something public.

Not accurate.

Close enough to be deliberate.

Anko marked spread order.

The rumor appeared east.

Then north.

Then west.

Each time after an official notice had passed nearby.

Not proof of one actor.

Enough to reject coincidence.

Diablo wanted to follow the administrative chain.

Anko wanted documents first.

Milim wanted to ask loudly.

Rimuru forbade `loudly`.

Searchers expanded their evidence role.

Not spies.

Not yet.

Witnesses.

Copies.

Dates.

Who said what first.

The settlement learned that surviving fear required recordkeeping as much as defense.
