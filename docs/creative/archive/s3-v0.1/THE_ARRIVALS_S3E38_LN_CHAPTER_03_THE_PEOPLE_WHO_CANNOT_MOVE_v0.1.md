# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 15
## Chapter 16 — The People Who Cannot Move

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 16  
**Source episode:** S3E38 — `Last Defense Front`

The inner refuge contained people who could not evacuate faster.

Injured.

Elderly.

Children.

One pregnant resident.

Two people whose powers made panic physically dangerous.

The collaborator under sanctuary from V14.

The resident who hated him.

Same shelter.

Nobody appreciated the irony.

A structural impact shook dust loose.

The harmed resident looked at the collaborator.

"If you run—"

"I can't."

"Good."

They held opposite sides of the same support when another tremor hit.

Not forgiveness.

Function.

Bocchi kept playing until her fingers hurt.

Rem moved water.

Aira managed frightened teenagers with the confidence of someone absolutely not frightened at all.

She was.

Kaneki stayed near the medical side where his senses mattered.

Ori counted doors.

Umaru counted people.

Fern counted everything.

The Last Defense Front did not wait for the strongest fighters to save it.

It kept itself alive.
