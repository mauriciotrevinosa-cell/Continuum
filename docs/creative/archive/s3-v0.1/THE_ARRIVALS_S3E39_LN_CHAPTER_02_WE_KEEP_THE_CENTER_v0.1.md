## Chapter 18 — We Keep the Center

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 15  
**Reading order:** 18  
**Source episode:** S3E39 — `Systems Break`

The west line fell back.

The north line stabilized.

Then the unknown signature returned.

Inside the settlement.

No visible intruder.

Sensors disagreed.

Human-like.

Anomaly.

Unknown.

Then nothing.

Mau felt the Noise react.

Not words.

Attention.

He stopped.

Frieren was too far away.

Rimuru was occupied.

Mau looked at the Last Defense map.

The old instinct said:

`Go find it.`

The new one asked:

`Who needs to know?`

He called it in.

Did not chase.

Diablo redirected.

Ori moved away from the possible anomaly zone.

Senku locked one infrastructure cluster.

The response happened without Mau becoming the response.

That choice lasted thirty seconds.

Then the unknown signal appeared under the central refuge.

Mau had no route that did not matter.

"Now?" he asked over relay.

Rimuru answered:

"Now."

Permission did not reduce courage.

It changed ownership.

Mau moved.
