# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 16
## Chapter 9 — Kaneki Knows This Part

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 16  
**Reading order:** 9  
**Source episode:** S3E43 — `Threshold`

The biological symptom came late.

Back pain.

Mau said it before anyone saw anything.

Kaneki moved closer.

"Same area?"

"Yes."

"Pressure or tearing?"

"Pressure."

Good distinction.

The kagune-like structure did not fully emerge.

A dark line appeared beneath the skin.

Kaneki watched.

No flinch.

Mau noticed.

"Still me?"

Kaneki looked annoyed.

"That's not the question I'm checking."

Mau smiled weakly.

Good answer.

Kaneki tracked:
- pain;
- location;
- movement;
- relation to stress;
- whether food affected it;
- whether Noise terminology changed before physical expression.

No claim that Mau had become a ghoul.

No claim that ghoul biology explained everything.

Only pattern recognition from someone whose body had once become a philosophical problem for everyone around him.

When the line receded, Kaneki wrote the time.

Mau asked:

"Are you okay?"

Kaneki stared.

"You're on the bed."

"That doesn't answer me."

Kaneki laughed.

Some habits survived protocols.
