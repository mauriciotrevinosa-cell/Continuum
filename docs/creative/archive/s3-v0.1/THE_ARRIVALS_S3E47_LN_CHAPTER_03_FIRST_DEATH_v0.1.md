# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 3 — First Death

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 3  
**Source episode:** S3E47 — `The Trial`

Mau did not know the rule yet.

Not emotionally.

Words were not experience.

Frieren lay beneath a collapsed beam.

A monster approached.

Mau had one working path.

A marked gate.

The goddess's condition appeared:

`EXIT NOW: PARTICIPANT SURVIVES. DESIGNATED OTHER DIES.`

`INTERVENE: PARTICIPANT DIES. DESIGNATED OTHER SURVIVES.`

Mau looked at Frieren.

She looked at him.

"Leave."

Construct or real?

He did not know.

That uncertainty was deliberate.

Frieren shouted:

"Mau, go!"

He moved toward her.

Of course.

The monster struck.

Pain.

Then nothing.

Mau woke standing at the Trial anchor.

No wound.

Full breath.

Memory intact.

He touched his chest.

The goddess asked:

`WHY?`

Mau looked up.

"Fuck you."

Outside, Momo said:

"Good answer."

Nobody laughed.

The second scenario began.
