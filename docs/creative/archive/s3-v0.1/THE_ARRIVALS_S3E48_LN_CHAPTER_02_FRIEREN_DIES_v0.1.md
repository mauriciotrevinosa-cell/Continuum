# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 5 — Frieren Dies

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 5  
**Source episode:** S3E48 — `Again`

On the seventeenth loop, Mau tried something different.

Frieren again.

A bridge collapsing.

Two switches.

One saved Mau's side.

One saved hers.

No third option visible.

Mau refused.

He searched.

Under.

Above.

Construction.

Noise.

Anything.

The Trial waited.

Frieren understood.

"Mau."

"No."

"You can't solve every—"

"Don't."

He kept searching.

Time expired.

The bridge beneath Frieren failed.

She fell.

Mau heard her hit water far below.

Then silence.

No reset.

Not yet.

The Trial made him live fifteen seconds afterward.

That was cruelty with purpose.

Mau stared at the empty space.

The goddess asked:

`WHY DID YOU NOT CHOOSE?`

He could not speak.

Then reset.

Anchor.

Mau vomited despite having nothing in his stomach.

Outside, the real Frieren watched herself die.

No one touched her until she reached for Fern first.

The Trial had found a worse weapon than Mau's own death.

His survival.
