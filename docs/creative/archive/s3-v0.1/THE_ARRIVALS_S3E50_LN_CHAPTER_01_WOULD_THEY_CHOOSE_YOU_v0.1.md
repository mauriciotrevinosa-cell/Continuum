# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 12 — Would They Choose You?

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 12  
**Source episode:** S3E50 — `The Offer`

The goddess gave him one image.

The settlement.

Real this time?

Maybe.

Everyone standing in the central court.

The Trial boundary still active.

Frieren.

Ori.

Rimuru.

Bocchi.

People Mau barely knew.

People who disliked decisions he had made.

People who had never spoken to him.

The goddess asked:

`WOULD THEY CHOOSE THIS PLACE IF THEY COULD LEAVE?`

Mau's answer came slowly.

"Some."

Good.

`WOULD FRIEREN?`

His throat tightened.

He wanted to say yes.

That was different from knowing.

The goddess pressed.

`WOULD SHE CHOOSE YOU IF HER WORLD WERE OPEN?`

Mau remembered Himmel.

Not as rival.

As history.

A whole world of people Frieren had lost.

Land.

Magic.

Time.

Things Mau could not recreate.

"I don't know."

Honest.

The goddess sounded almost gentle.

`THEN LET THEM ANSWER.`

The Trial boundary opened.

Not ended.

Opened enough that everyone heard the next words together.
