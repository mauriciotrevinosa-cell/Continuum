# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 17 — Some Already Know

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 17  
**Source episode:** S3E51 — `Questions`

Some people already knew.

They did not all share.

One resident packed immediately.

Not because departure was available yet.

Because knowing changed how they saw belongings.

Another threw away a half-written packing list.

A couple argued because one said `home` and meant source world while the other meant Continuum.

Someone from the second community said they would return if their terms allowed it.

No shame.

A young Arrival said:

"I think I'm staying."

Then cried.

No joy.

No grief.

Both.

Mau heard fragments.

Never enough to make a list.

Good.

Frieren gave no answer.

Neither did Ori.

Rimuru did not announce one.

Bocchi did not.

The story did not decide them for the reader before they decided themselves.

Mau told himself he was okay with that.

The lie was not complete.

He wanted Frieren to choose him.

That want embarrassed him.

Not because it was wrong.

Because he feared wanting could become pressure.

So he said nothing.

Frieren noticed anyway.

She said nothing too.

For now.
