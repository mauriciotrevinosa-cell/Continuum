# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 22 — The Biggest Slap of His Life

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 22  
**Source episode:** S3E52 — `Interception`

Frieren asked:

"How could you decide my life without me again?"

Mau answered too fast.

"Because you wouldn't have to live with losing this."

Silence.

Mau knew the sentence was bad.

He kept going.

"The return terms said Continuum could become dreamlike. If you went back—"

"Could."

"What?"

"Could become dreamlike."

Frieren stepped closer.

"You don't even know."

"I would know before agreeing."

"For me?"

Mau stopped.

Frieren's voice shook.

"You would know before agreeing for me?"

"No. I mean—"

"You mean you would carry the answer."

Mau tried again.

"If you believed this was a dream, then losing it wouldn't—"

Frieren hit him.

One slap.

Hard.

Not comedic in the moment.

Not a punishment anyone applauded.

Shock.

Grief.

A physical full stop because words had failed to stop him from explaining her own loss to her.

Mau stared.

Frieren's hand shook worse than his face hurt.

Tears finally came.

"Do not tell me what I am allowed to remember."

Mau's expression collapsed.

Frieren continued.

"Do not tell me which life will hurt less."

He swallowed.

"We came here against our will."

"Yes."

"You still owe me the chance to choose whether I leave."

Mau looked at her.

At everyone.

"I don't want you to stay because of me."

"That is also my choice."

"I don't want you to regret it."

Frieren laughed once through tears.

Cruel sound.

"You don't get to remove regret from my life by removing me from the decision."

Mau had no answer.

Good.

For several seconds nobody made the slap funny.

It needed to remain what it was:

a rupture.

Then Frieren touched his cheek.

Not apology yet.

Not forgiveness.

Proof she was still there.

That hurt more.
