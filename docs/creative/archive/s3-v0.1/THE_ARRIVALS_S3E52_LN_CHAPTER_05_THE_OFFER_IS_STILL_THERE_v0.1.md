# The Arrivals — Light Novel — Volume 17
## Chapter 25 — The Offer Is Still There

**Status:** SUPERSEDED BY S3 REBUILD v0.2  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Volume:** 17  
**Reading order:** 25  
**Source episode:** S3E52 — `Interception`

The next morning, nobody had solved home.

Good.

The goddess's offer remained.

Private terms had begun appearing only when individuals requested them.

No answers were public.

No scoreboard.

No pressure.

Arrival House handled appointments.

The coordinating table handled logistics.

Searchers still left.

Water still needed maintenance.

Someone complained about breakfast.

The city did not pause for existential freedom.

Mau remained under supervision.

Also good.

He sat at a table with Frieren.

Not the old inn table.

New settlement.

New table.

The old mug sat between them.

Mau had brought it months earlier.

Frieren had noticed and said nothing.

Two blank pieces of paper sat nearby.

No labels.

Mau looked at her.

"Do you know what you're going to do?"

Frieren thought.

Mau waited.

That was new.

She answered:

"I know some things."

He smiled slightly.

"Useful."

"Very."

"Are you going to tell me?"

"When I'm ready to tell you."

Mau nodded.

No argument.

Across the room, someone entered Arrival House and quietly asked to see their personal terms.

A door closed.

Another person left the building without asking anything.

Ori passed outside with Coco and Richeh.

Rimuru argued with Senku near the water court.

Eren and Mikasa walked in opposite directions after another disagreement, then stopped and waited for each other anyway.

Bocchi's music came from somewhere Mau could not see.

Life continued around unanswered choices.

Frieren reached across the table.

Took Mau's hand.

Not an answer.

A present-tense fact.

Mau held on without trying to turn it into the future.

Season Three ended before the first final choice was spoken.
