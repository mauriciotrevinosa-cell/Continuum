## Chapter 17 — Gone Before Breakfast

**Source episode:** S3E6 — `Someone Else's Certainty`

<!-- scene-id: s3e6-ln02-night -->

Mau came back inside.

That was why nobody treated the walk as a disappearance.

He ate.

Barely.

Sat with everyone.

Answered questions.

Went upstairs with Frieren.

Lay down.

Frieren stayed awake longer than he did.

At least Mau remembered that.

Sometime before dawn, he opened his eyes.

The room was empty.

No.

Frieren was there.

Sleeping.

Mau knew that.

He could see her.

He also remembered her leaving two hours earlier after saying:

`I need space from you.`

The two realities existed at once.

His chest hurt.

He touched the blanket near her hand.

Warm.

Present.

The memory insisted anyway.

<!-- scene-id: s3e6-ln02-direction -->

Then another certainty arrived.

A place north of the site.

Trees.

A cabin.

He had been told to go there if this happened.

By whom?

Rimuru.

No.

Qifrey.

Maybe.

A woman he could not see clearly.

The instruction felt older than the question.

Mau sat on the edge of the bed.

If he woke Frieren, she would stop him.

Because she loved him.

That thought should have been comforting.

Instead his mind supplied a second interpretation:

`She stops you because everyone has learned how much trouble you create.`

Mau closed his eyes.

Wrong.

Probably wrong.

He needed one thing he could verify without asking someone else who he was.

The place.

If the place existed, something was real.

That logic was terrible.

At five in the morning it felt clean.

<!-- scene-id: s3e6-ln02-leave -->

He dressed quietly.

Took water.

A coat.

Not enough food.

His watch.

No weapon worth calling a weapon.

He looked at Frieren.

She was still there.

Sleeping.

He remembered her not being there.

Mau left before one of those facts could win.

<!-- scene-id: s3e6-ln02-breakfast -->

Frieren woke to an empty side of the bed.

That was not immediately wrong.

Mau woke early sometimes.

She went downstairs.

No Mau.

Kitchen.

No Mau.

Storage.

No Mau.

Outside.

No Mau.

His boots were gone.

The water flask too.

Frieren stood in the doorway.

Behind her, breakfast continued for six more seconds.

Then Ori looked up.

"Where's Mau?"

Frieren did not answer.

Rimuru saw her face.

The future city stopped mattering for exactly one breath.

Then everything moved.
