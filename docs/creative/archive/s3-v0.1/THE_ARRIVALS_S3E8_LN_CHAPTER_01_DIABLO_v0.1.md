## Chapter 4 — Diablo

**Source episode:** S3E8 — `Bad Timing for a Reunion`

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln01-road -->

Rimuru met Diablo on a road he had no reason to be using.

For one second neither moved.

The search had reduced Rimuru's world to routes, traces and failures.

Then a familiar presence stood fifty meters ahead.

Black hair.

Dark clothing.

A smile that became too composed too quickly.

"Rimuru-sama."

Rimuru stopped breathing.

"Diablo?"

The name came out wrong.

Too small.

Diablo crossed the distance.

Whatever reunion he had imagined did not survive Rimuru grabbing him first.

Diablo froze.

Then smiled for real.

"My lord."

Rimuru pulled back.

There were questions.

Hundreds.

Where.

When.

How long.

What did Diablo remember.

Who else.

None came first.

"We're looking for someone."

Diablo's expression changed instantly.

"Then we look."

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln01-nointro -->

That was his introduction to Continuum's growing household.

Not dinner.

Not celebration.

A missing-person map held open on a wagon hood.

Rimuru pointed at routes.

"This is Mau."

Diablo looked at the rough sketch Anko had made.

"Important?"

Rimuru hesitated.

"Yes."

That was enough for Diablo.

Not because Mau had earned anything from him.

Because Rimuru had.

The distinction mattered.

<!-- scene-id: s3e8-ln01-sense -->

Diablo took the northern forest.

His perception reached differently from Frieren's.

Different framework.

Different blind spots.

He found traces others had missed.

Not Mau.

Discontinuities.

Places where the emotional impression of a path did not match the physical path cleanly.

He crouched near one.

Rimuru joined him.

"Can you tell what did it?"

"No."

Raphael supplied no answer either.

Diablo smiled without humor.

"Good."

Rimuru stared.

"Good?"

"If I had arrived and solved your problem in five minutes, I would have suspected the problem was too small."

"That's not comforting."

"It was not intended to be."

Diablo touched nothing.

No universal demon answer.

Only another partial witness.

They kept moving.
