# The Arrivals — Canon Digest, S1 to S4 V22 #15

What the Light Novel text actually says, read in order from S1 V1 #1 to S4 V22
#15 (606 chapters) on 2026-10-12. Written so a writer can check a past event,
a tenure, a room or a phrase without rereading the books.

- **Scope:** S1 (V1–V3), S2 (V4–V9), S3 (V10–V19), S4 V20, V21 and V22 #1–15.
  Everything after V22 #15 is not covered here. Use
  [CANON_TRAPS.md](./CANON_TRAPS.md) and the later chapters for that.
- **Authority:** this is a reading of the prose. A creator decision in
  [DECISION_HISTORY.md](./DECISION_HISTORY.md) or
  [STORY_CONSTITUTION.md](./STORY_CONSTITUTION.md) wins over anything here.
  Where the prose contradicts itself, §12 lists the slip. It does not pick a
  winner.
- **How to use it:** find the fact here, then open the chapter it names before
  you quote it. "V18 #4" means Volume 18, chapter 4. S4 dates are "Day N",
  counted from the first day of the ten-day window.

## 1. Mau

### Origin and memory

- He wakes in a **clearing** beside Frieren with no name, no home and no
  family. He knows facts without experiences ("I know what snow is. I don't
  know if I've seen it"). Frieren is the first thing he remembers.
- **His name** comes from the white football jersey in his backpack, printed
  "MAU T" #5. Frieren: "Maybe your name is Mau."
- **Species, origin and lifespan are unknown.** The containment showed his
  body holding every route open (elf, slime, ghoul, cursed-energy repair). He
  asked Ciel not to choose his species for him (V13 #21), so she restored him
  toward the "arrival state". Never write him as human.
- **Birthday:** chosen in S1E15 as "tomorrow" by the local calendar, not the
  day Frieren found him. It passed unnoticed during the memory crisis and was
  celebrated late in S3 (V15 #16–17), with a second circle drawn on the old
  inn's kitchen calendar.

### What he woke with (S1 V1 #2)

- A black-and-orange racing-team **hoodie**.
- A grey racing **watch** with orange accents.
- A backpack holding:
  - a navy knitted polo (the "dark blue knit", which is not the hoodie);
  - a red long-sleeved team shirt with a horse on it;
  - a white T-shirt;
  - the white #5 jersey;
  - a racing cap;
  - a festival bucket hat.
- **The hoodie** migrated to Frieren in S1 ("Because it belongs in my room."
  "...Our room?" "...Yes.").
- **The cap** was lost in a town street in S2 V4, the day a thrown stone cut
  Bocchi's forehead. In S3 V12 #14 Rem found it snagged on a river branch.
  Bocchi gave it back to him; it hangs on a hook in his room, with water damage
  on the brim.
- **The bucket hat** went to Bocchi after the stone, then to Frieren
  ("Originally." "Currently.").
- **The red shirt** is often worn by Frieren. She wore it for a week on a
  laundry day in the first year.
- **The backpack** stays under the bed. He packed it to leave in V20 #11.
- V21 #30–31: Rimuru recognised the hoodie and shirt as two teams of a racing
  sport from his own world. Mau does not remember the sport. His hands knew the
  cars were fast "from somewhere below remembering".

### The Noise, Construction and the seal

- **The Noise:** Mau's own name for an internal pattern that does four things:
  OBSERVE, ANALYZE, UNDERSTAND, CONSTRUCT (V13 #14).
  - His first Construction was in S2 V4: geometry lines in the air, a hurt
    hand, not reproducible.
  - In S2 he built **Hollow Purple** (Blue in the left hand, Red in the right)
    from Yuta's memory of Shinjuku.
  - His small Construction is still his own: "fixes axles and builds shelves".
- **The containment** (S3 V13 #14–18): Rimuru absorbed him temporarily. The
  pattern read Rimuru, Raphael's archive, Yuta's memories of Gojo and the
  Sukuna tether.
  - Shrine and Void collided inside Rimuru, and the wheel turned.
  - Raphael broke and rebuilt as **Ciel**.
  - Rimuru lost his left arm and slept as a bitten-looking slime until the
    evening of Day 18.
  - Rimuru sealed every construct with Infinite Imprisonment.
- **The 143 patterns** in the seal (V13 #20):

  | Finished but missing a requirement | 26 (Ten Shadows is one) |

  | Blueprint only | 67 |

  Sources: Rimuru, Raphael, Yuta, Gojo (via Yuta), Sukuna (the tether),
  Mahoraga, Frieren and Fern spells, Witch Hat glyphs, Okarun and Momo, the

- After the Trial, Analyze kept the shapes of people and things from the loops

- **The left eye** is the ghoul route kept as a valve: black sclera and red
  iris under pressure, with no change in diet (V13 #21). He wears his hair
  over it. Marin made him a dark-blue eye patch with a small star.
- **The shadow:**

### The guardrails (S3 V13 #20)

Qifrey wrote them; a copy is pinned inside Mau's door.

1. Nothing sealed is deleted.
2. The pattern may observe, analyze and understand, but not build beyond
   Mau's own Construction while the seal holds.
3. It may not read Ciel or Rimuru again. Mau later rewrote this rule (V13 #24):
   Ciel keeps her door shut and says so when something knocks; if it looks
   anyway, nobody calls it a betrayal.
4. Ciel watches only the guardrails unless Mau asks. This is **Ori's** rule.
   Mau added: "and Ciel tells Mau every time she looks."
5. Mau is told everything, not softened. This is **Frieren's** rule. In V21
   #2 Mau turns it both ways.
6. Nothing comes out unless Mau, Rimuru and Ciel all agree. Never while he is
   unstable. Never because someone needs a weapon. Qifrey underlined the last
   part.
7. Nobody outside learns. This is **Anko's** rule. It was replaced by the
   Rings in V17 #38.
8. Mau's own rule: "If I break one of these without meaning to, tell me.
   Don't hide it to be kind."

### The Sukuna wound and the bay night (S3 V18 #1–7)

- The pattern decided the seal was an injury. It tried to rebuild his body

### The Trial and 437 (S3 V19, S4 V20)

- The Goddess's Trial Domain named Mau as participant. If he refused, the
  settlement's protection would be withdrawn while monsters approached. He
  tested seven options, then chose to enter (V19 #4).
- **What the outside saw:** nothing. A frosted white wall as tall as the sky.
  Ciel's thread could reach him only at each reset, one word each time: the
  count. Senku timed the wall at **5 hours 41 minutes**.
- **The rules:** death returned him to the anchor, and he kept the memory of
  every loop. Every scenario contained a loss.
- **Key loops:**
  - Frieren under a beam (loop 1).
  - Rimuru's choice.
  - Strangers, a collaborator and a hostile fighter.
  - Frieren's bridge (no reset for 30 seconds).
  - Bocchi in a fire.
  - Ori's door.
  - The white room ("ONE LEAVES").
  - The inn loop where he destroyed everything.
  - The field with Maki ("Well, that's annoying").
  - A street of white light with a man whose eyes are covered: the only Gojo
    picture.
  - A room with no doors and a knife.
  - **The wedding** at the first clearing, elf ears, everyone present. The sky
    split, his own Infinity held him safe, and Frieren died fighting it.
- **The outside voice:** Frieren said "Mau" at the wall every hour. One time
  got through, at the end of the wedding loop. It was the only thing in 437
  that came from outside.
- **The number:** Ciel told him on the first night (V19 #25), "four hundred
  and thirty-seven". He asked her never to tell Rimuru; he would tell it
  himself. On the night of Day 5 (V20 #5–8) he told the house, which knows
  it: 437, every time, the loops. He never told the Gojo loops (CANON_TRAPS).
- **How long he was in there** (V20 #10):
  - Conservatively about a hundred years older than Frieren, which makes him
    the oldest person in the house.
  - Ciel's far end is "older than all of Chloe's resets combined". Rimuru
    folded that away; only Rimuru and Yuta know it (V20 #15). Mau has not been
    told.
- **Aftermath:**
  - Infinity leaked from grief; nobody could touch him.
  - He went alone for two days, upriver past the stepping stones to the falls,
    "somewhere I never died" (V20 #11–19).
  - Day 7 at dawn the deer drank at the pool. He wrote one line per death he
    could remember, 211 lines, and burned the pages.
  - A leaf landed on the back of his left hand and he felt it: he had told
    Infinity "over, real, here".
  - He came back at dusk on Day 7 and offered his left hand palm down. "It's
    down. I think. Try."

## 2. Frieren and Mau

- **S1:** a shared room "by accident". She cries for Fern at night and he
  holds her. The lake misunderstanding ("We're both dumb"). "You're the most
  important person to me."

  stepped in ("No."). Mau: "What's a kiss?"

  - The dreams forked: if he stepped into the geometry, she lived.
  - The Rimuru–Mau Contract held Rimuru to the barrier.
  - Mau stepped in front of her and Sukuna's hand went through his torso. He
    made Hollow Purple and Mahoraga was destroyed.
  - He was carried home under Rimuru's coat, apparently dead.
- **S2 V8, after Sukuna:** "Second Chance" was an anchor, not a resurrection.
  He woke to her first kiss: "I love you." / "I told you everything was gonna
  be okay." Then she was furious: "You decided I would survive losing you."
  They promised to hide nothing that large.
- **S2 V9 #12–15:** at the clearing, "Will you be my girlfriend?" "I thought
  I already was... Yes." She asked for the new window to face east.
- **S3:**
  - The memory crisis. She tells him "Then I'll remind you... As many as it
    takes."
  - **The date** (V13 #37): bad bread at a village stall, and blue thread he
    bought her. Ori later wove the thread into his birthday bracelet.
  - **The long-storm plan** (V16 #41–46): her "Super Duper Ultra
    Archi-Re-Que-Contra Mega Important Meeting" with every woman in the house.
    She raised Maomao's half dose herself; the label said "DO NOT ADD MORE".
    She wore the green coronation dress with twin coils and silver pins.
  - **Effects of that chocolate:** Mau followed her all afternoon and evening.
    He told her "The most beautiful person I've ever seen. Or place. Or
    thing... That green." It wore off overnight, and he woke after ten
    remembering everything.
  - She promised the tree: "I'm going to find a way to be there too."
  - "Say it again" (V18 #1). Her corrections: "Person. Then place. Then
    thing." / "THAT green."
- **"Both of us"** (V18 #7, after the bay): "If I live, and you live. Both
  of us. That's when I'm alive... A hundred years. The tree." It was renewed
  on Day 9 (V20 #23) and again under the grown tree on Day 70 (V22 #15):
  "Longer. Both of us. I promise." "Idiot."
- **Her birthday** (V18 #17–21):
  - Fern keeps it; Frieren had forgotten it for centuries.
  - The witches' book-keeper gave the morning spell, which dresses you in what
    you most love to wear. Its price was Mau's answer about waiting.
  - She woke in his hoodie, a day late.
- **The fort:** sister-in-law, codename AGENT MIMIC (V18 #14).
- **Her terms** (V20 #23): she read Return and Stay on day one with Fern as
  witness. Return would have made Continuum "dreamlike". She chose Stay on the
  first day, before his note. "Next time... you'll remember that I chose
  first."
- **The slap** (V19 #39), the biggest of his life, for trying to decide
  everyone's Return alone.
- **"Longer."** (V22 #15): she says the tree's early arrival broke the
  hundred-year promise, so he owes her a new one. They were the first ever to
  sit under the tree.

## 4. Season 1 (V1–V3, 57 chapters)

- **The old inn:**
  - An abandoned village: an inn with about ten upstairs rooms, five ruined
    houses, a well, a hill with a tree behind the inn, a lake trail and an old
    road.
  - Rebuilt from the ruins: kitchen, bath, music room (with a pull-string bell
    because the soundproofing works too well), warning bells.
- **The household grows** as in the table.
- **Rooms:** Fern and Bocchi became roommates. Frieren kicked out, took the
  hoodie and declared it "our room".
- **The suspicious deer:** a running gag ("It's planning something"). Mau
  told everyone about a deer on the north trail in the first month. In V20 #17
  it was "seen twice". Frieren: "Maybe it followed you home."
- **The town:**
  - Yuta found a mercantile town with a guild, market and herbalist.
  - "Otherworlders" was coined as new guild slang.
  - Guild jobs in pairs. Mau gave his whole share to Bocchi for a music item.
  - Anko marked the settlement on the map by hand.
- **The first chocolate** (S1E18): see §2.
- **S1 ending:** Sukuna (in Megumi's body) cast Malevolent Shrine on a
  regional city at midday, sensed Yuta, suppressed his energy and walked off.
  Nobody at the inn knows.

## 5. Season 2 (V4–V9, 99 chapters)

- **Rumours and fear** after the destroyed city. Guild jobs dried up. The
  first talk of a bigger home, with criteria: water, distance from roads,
  defensibility, soil, trade.
- **The Bocchi incident** (V4 #9–12): a thrown stone in town split her
  forehead; it left a faint mark. The cap was lost. Mau scrubbed her blood off
  the red shirt. It also brought his first Construction event.
- **G3 reaches the inn** (17 people):
  - Kita: "Bocchi-chan?"
  - Okarun does not remember Momo, and recovers her slowly (V6 #15).
  - Wakana became the repairer.
  - Umaru's orange hamster cloak.
- **Privacy rules and Rimuru's Contract** (V5 #7–8):
  - You may tell your own history, not another's weakness or trauma.
  - The Contract covers protected information. Okarun tested it and took
    lightning pain.
- **More arrivals:** Maki, Stark, G4, Agott and Himmel. The baseball game was
  played without powers.
- **The dreams:**
  - Mau's dreams came true: the cup, the noon rain.
  - The first warning dream showed prepared ground, blood, a wheel, Sukuna and

## 6. Season 3 (V10–V19, 374 chapters)

### Site, board and memory arc (V10–V13)

- **The site:** a lake "wide enough to hold sky", a river, a waterfall to the
  north, nineteen stakes. They keep the old inn.
- **The board:** PEOPLE OF CONCERN / POSSIBLE ARRIVALS, 32 cards. Mau has no
  name to write.
- **The memory arc:** false memories arrive "furnished". Nano's columns
  separate observed, remembered and interpreted.
  - **First disappearance:** a false instruction sent him to a cabin. The
    witches found him by a stream on Day 2 of the search. G5 arrived as
    "false positives" while the house searched.
  - **Second disappearance:** Ori went with him south and wrote the note. On
    Day 9 they killed a plated many-limbed creature together. A kagune-like
    structure appeared on his back.
  - Rem, Kaneki and Aira found them, and the search arrived on Day 12. Mau:
    "Who?" -> "Frieren?"
- **Containment** (V13 #14–20): see §1. **Ciel** was named on the evening of
  Day 18. The guardrails were written.
