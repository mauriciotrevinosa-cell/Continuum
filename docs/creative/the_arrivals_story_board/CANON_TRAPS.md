# The Arrivals — Canon Traps

Facts that have already been gotten wrong at least once while writing S4.
Read this before drafting any chapter. Grep the earlier volumes for anything
not on this list. Add a line every time the creator catches a new slip.

## Mau

- **Mau remembers nothing from before the clearing.** No world, no family, no
  places, no names. Frieren is not the first thing he remembers; she is the
  first thing (V22 "Look at Me"; V24 "Two": "No family. No world.").
- **Mau's species, origin and lifespan are unknown.** Never call him human or
  give him a human clock ("seventy years"). The elf-route trial was private:
  Mau, Frieren, Rimuru and Ciel; Maomao afterwards; Maki only when Frieren
  tells her (V22 "Look at Me"). See
  `S1_S4_CHARACTER_AND_SPACE_CONTINUITY_GATE_2026-10-12.md`.
- He does not know the F1 cars from memory. Rimuru drew them from *his own*
  memory (V21 "What We Make Together"); Yuta recognized the helmet ("My dad had
  a poster"). Mau knew only that they were fast, "from somewhere below
  remembering". He can tell what happened *in this world*: Day 45, the cloth,
  the pencil names (V21 "Your Hoodie's Team").
  Never name the models or years in his point of view: they are "the red
  and white one and the red one".
- **Mau's hoodie is black and orange**, with a racing team's name on it (S1;
  V21 "What We Make Together"). Never "orange and dark blue".
- The reset hour (V21 "Ugly Days"): his body wakes braced at the Trial anchor
  hour; Frieren puts her cold foot on his shin: "One. Still here." It is his
  anchor and her check. He does not have insomnia, and she cannot hide
  sleeplessness from him.
- **V23 Something Small's destruction dream is a Trial scenario**, not a
  recovered pre-clearing life. Demon Lord and God of Destruction are remembered
  titles inside that fragment, not current ranks or unlocked powers. Its
  unidentified deadlock is not the S5 Exchange war. Only Mau experiences the
  nightmare; the household does not acquire its contents (DH-078).
- **Inside the Trial he saw nothing of the outside.** He heard Frieren's voice
  once, at the very end (S3 "Everyone Came"). He never saw her at the wall.
- **The Gojo loop** (V20 "Breathe With Me"): a street of white light, a man
  with something over his eyes and a smile like a door left open. Keep later
  memories of it (V24 "Domain Clash") to that picture.
- **The house knows what happened in the Trial.** Mau told it on the night of
  Day 5 (V20 "Rule Five" to "The Last Two"): 437, every time, the loops. He
  never told the Gojo loops (V24 "Domain Clash"). Arrivals are not told
  (DH-071).
- **Don't date the thing in his shadow to the Sukuna night.** The creator
  isn't sure when it came; the first sign on the page is a night in the
  Muslin (recalled in V23 "Mimics Found: Three").
- **Mau first saw Fern behind the inn**, when Group 2 arrived (S1 "There You
  Are"). Frieren had lost Fern before she met Mau; he never saw them on the
  road together.
- **The first roof laugh was Mau's** (V21 "The Roof, Laughing", Day 26).
  Rimuru laughs often and always has; never write that he laughed "for the
  first time". The day off with Umaru's card was Diablo's (V21), and Rimuru
  laughed at him.

## Places

- **Rimuru's loft** is above the closet *inside* Mau and Frieren's room (the
  Muslin era too). He is never "down the hall".
- **The Muslin is boards, not cloth.** One floor; inside walls are boards
  slotted between posts; the roof is planks. "Muslin" is Wakana's sewing
  metaphor. Never "cloth walls", "a sketch of string and cloth" or "cloth
  over poles" (layout lock §10.1).
- **Rimuru's office is downstairs**; he sleeps in the loft. Nobody comes
  "down the stair from his office" at night.
- Frieren has no separate reading place in her room. She reads in **the
  reading chair Mau said no to** (S3 "A Small Library"; in the Muslin too).
  Never a cushion or a crate of books.
- **The pressed flowers:** four, in the old grimoire. Since moving day that
  grimoire sits on the shelf in the middle of the floor. The fifth went into
  the hoodie pocket (V24 "The Fifth Flower").
- **The east window:** cracked on Day 145; Senku's hurried pane went in on
  Day 146; Wakana's proper pane went in on Day 162.
- The queen's decree did **not** move the loft. Rimuru kept it and put a door
  on the ladder that latches from their side (V23 "By Decree of the Queen").
- **The roof:** before moving day they climbed out through the loft window
  of the unfinished east room (V22 "Yuta's Turn"). In the lived-in house it
  is the roof ladder and hatch at the end of the east corridor (V23 "Not
  Twice"). The loft ladder is inside the east room, never in the corridor.
- The crackers table (Seiko, Maki, Frieren, later Mai) meets in the
  **evening**, after dinner. It was founded on the way up from the river.

## Mechanics

- **Coins:** warm, no named metal (never "gold"). On use they go warm, warmer,
  then vanish, leaving the palm pink. The voice says "You are sure? [name]".
  That wording is intentional; do not "fix" it.
- **The ink** comes from a notch in the trunk of the ink tree, one bottle a
  week (V22 "Ink"). Never from the leaves.
- **The Trial:** nobody outside saw it. The 437 deaths were told in V20.
- **Coin arrivals don't know what the coins cost.** From Day 103 it is the
  house's quiet rule, never written in the ledger (V22 #31): tell them about
  the Trial and that the coins came out of it, never what happened inside.
  Seika has only Nijika's "Not all of it. Enough." (V22 #30). Frieren breaks
  it once, on purpose, for Gojo (V24 #6). The house itself knows; the rule is
  only about arrivals. Never let an arrival talk about the cost as if told
  (DH-071).
- **The wheel only adapts.** It turns at what hurts him and learns its shape;
  it does not listen, watch, attend, want or speak. Reading, turning toward
  things and the pull toward the gate are **The Noise** (Observe, Analyze,
  Understand, Construct). Creator correction, 2026-10-13 (DH-075; DH-073).
- **Mau cannot sign dungeon rules.** The Noise refuses only contracts that
  bind him inside the gate (V23 #4, Day 107: his name won't hold; The Noise
  said only "No"). Others sign; Fern notes he agrees. Every other contract
  he can sign normally. Writers' reason (creator): The Noise doesn't want to
  be deprived of anything in there. Nobody in the story knows that; never
  state it on the page or add words from The Noise (DH-074, DH-075).
- **Seiko's coin** was promised on Day 40 (V21 "The List") to Yuta's teacher,
  "for his doctor", repeated on Day 130 ("I don't say things twice") and
  given on Day 132. She never "doesn't know yet" who it is for.
- **The shop, in order:** the axe (Day 111), a box of rations, then Mikasa's
  blades (in use by Day 130). The blades are the third purchase, not the
  second.

## Calendar anchors

- The first devil: Day 119, killed by Mikasa and Eren; it already said "the
  one with the shadow".
- Yuta's Turn: Day 95. Not Twice: Day 116. The roof-night cycle lands on
  Day 130 (V23).
- Coco's mother is a few months after Ink (Day 71 to Day 195), not "years
  later".
- **The coins arrived on Day 10** (V20 "What It Bought"). Count from there.
- **The gate changes at dawn every seven days from Day 105:** 112, 119, 126,
  133, 140, 147, 154, 161, 168... (V23 shop panel: "changes in 6 days" on
  Day 106). Day 133 starts week five; Day 145 is in week six.
- **The first party went in four times before the gate changed:** Day 109,
  Day 110 twice, and Day 111 before noon. Ciel had seen it "four times" by
  Day 112.
- **The wheel's first click in S4 is Day 118** (V23 #15 "Out"). Yuta and Mau
  only; struck from behind; one turn outside the seal through a mended place;
  Yuta pulls him out before a second. Nobody went in Days 113 to 117. The
  Noise's leaning starts on the last night of the reign (Day 113) and is not
  the gate.
- **The west wing:** first bent Day 115, second Days 116 and 117, third Days
  120 and 121, fourth Days 125 to 128, last beam Day 129.
- **Seika arrived on Day 102; Ram before dawn on Day 124.** On Day 126 Seika
  came "three weeks ago", not "last week".
- Frieren put the mimic grimoire down only while she filled Ram twice a day
  (Days 124 to 129), not from the queen's second night.
- **Kessoku did not arrive together.** Bocchi in S1E2 (two years and a bit by
  V22), Kita at the end of S1 (with Okarun and Umaru), Nijika and Ryo in S3E8
  (about a year by V22). In their own world all four vanished within the same
  three days. Seika learns the gap on her first morning (V22 #29). Bocchi's
  family will come from the same day Seika left (DH-072). Never compute a
  ratio; every world keeps its own clock.
- **Kessoku were not in the Sukuna fight.** Mau stepped into Sukuna's line for
  Frieren (S2, V7 "Predicted Geometry"); Bocchi and Kita were at the inn and
  saw Yuta carry him home under Rimuru's coat (V7 "The Hand").
- **Roof nights and the crackers table fall on the same night**, every seven
  days: 81, 88, 95, 102, 109, 116, 123, 130... A chapter set on one of those
  nights must account for both.
- Look at Me's river is Day 72; the crackers table was founded on Day 81
  (nine days later).
- Frieren's cold foot outside the reset hour first happens in V23 "Not Twice"
  (Day 116). After that it is rare, not "never".
- **After the bell:** Rimuru slept as a slime in his office Days 145 to 147
  and was up in human shape by Day 148 (V24 "Everything Stops").
- **Hollis's route:** Ashby runs on Day 188, about 193, about 203, Day 209
  (eighteen chimneys, sold) and Day 214 (the fifth; the gate closes; three
  chimneys saved). Ashby's market is "every few days"; don't give it a fixed
  interval.
- **Cobb is "the Hannet boy".** Hannet's (his sister's) sheep: four taken in
  the winter.
- **Count durations from the scene that starts them.** A thought that began
  on Dating Day (Day 138) is two months old by Day 210, not one.
- **Check every "N days ago" against the *When:* lines.** V24 had "four days"
  for six, "a month" for six weeks, and "two days after" for one.
