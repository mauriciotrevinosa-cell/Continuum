# The Arrivals — Decision History

This file records **resolved changes of direction**. They are not contradictions unless a later audit finds that two unresolved authoritative versions still coexist.

## DH-001 — Season 1 count: 20 planning concept → 19 full episodes
Earlier planning briefly treated G3-first-days and Road Days / Sukuna finale as separate late-season units, producing a 20-episode shape.

Later creator decision fused/intercut that material into one larger S1E19.

**Current direction:** 19 full episodes. S1E19 contains G3, road days, safe-night contrast and Sukuna finale. This is resolved.

## DH-002 — S1E18 ending
Earlier brainstorm for the chocolate/day-in-life episode used a joke close in the spirit of “this never happened.”

During voice/story approval, the ending was changed.

**Current direction:** no kiss contact; Mau sincerely asks:
> What's a kiss?

This is resolved and is the Level-4 version.

## DH-003 — City-building timing
Earlier S2 planning expected the Otherworlder City to emerge materially in later S2 and later retained a first-move / first-construction beat near the season end.

As more S2 arcs accumulated, the story was rebalanced again.

**Current direction:** S2 owns social rupture, interim fortification, reconnaissance, strong-site discovery/verification, the final collective site choice and preliminary planning. **Meaningful construction begins in S3.**

The final S2 image remains at the inn: ordinary household life around an unfinished future-home site/foundation/layout plan.

This is a placement evolution, not a contradiction.

## DH-004 — CAL-17 compression vs canon pacing
CAL-17 intentionally stress-tested the Hollow Purple climax on one page.

Later visual/story review showed that the canon sequence likely needs much more room.

**Current direction:** keep CAL-17 as a one-page noncanon production stress test; audit the eventual canon page count separately, with a multi-page sequence currently favored.

## DH-005 — Sukuna/Mau physical connection
Some early visual prompting/renders drifted toward the idea of a severed Sukuna hand associated with Mau.

**Current direction:** Sukuna's arm/hand physically goes **through Mau** during the interception; the connection remains close and matters to the later inability to detach.

Resolved.

## DH-006 — Merging window placement
The climax was refined so the system event occurs immediately after Mau is impaled.

**Current direction:** wording approximately “merging began”; it causes Sukuna to partially black out briefly, creating the micro-window for Mau's goodbye and construction.

Exact wording/mechanics remain open, but placement/function are current.

## DH-007 — Recovery duration
Earlier future-board language used roughly two days of Yuta RCT/recovery.

The later consolidated working arc moved the current recovery timing to about **three days until Mau wakes**, with the first two days feeling hopeless.

Current working direction: ~3 days. Exact medical timing can still move during scripting.

## DH-008 — Frieren confession / Mau response
Earlier future seed had Frieren confessing and Mau being able to answer the same phrase honestly.

The later revival-specific direction was refined to preserve Mau's still-incomplete romantic language and the future Ori arc.

**Current direction:** Frieren initiates the kiss and says “I love you”; Mau reciprocates and reassures her but does **not** verbally mirror “I love you” in that awakening beat. They still do not have a formal relationship label.

## DH-009 — Confessor identity
A calibration draft briefly inverted the confessor.

This was corrected explicitly.

**Current direction:** Frieren is the confessor. Mau listens/reacts/reciprocates. Resolved.

## DH-010 — G3 composition
Early Season-arc brainstorming treated Group 3 membership as open and considered various combinations.

Later Group 3 work settled the current wave as:
Mikasa, Okarun, Umaru, Kita, Marin, Wakana Gojo, Qifrey, Coco.

Eren and Stark were deferred; Senku moved to a later phase.

This is a refinement from open roster → approved current roster.

## DH-011 — Sukuna finale information order
Some rough handoff language had Sukuna hearing local “Otherworlder” framing before attacking.

The final Level-4 S1E19 structure instead has:
sense Yuta
→ “So that brat is here.”
→ Malevolent Shrine
→ realization that the world/context is wrong
→ cursed-energy suppression.

Current S1E19 manga-ready material governs this.

## DH-012 — Ori placement and arrival timing
Earlier Story Board summaries still treated Ori as an arc whose season placement could remain S2, S3 or later.

The late-S2 development pass resolved that ambiguity at macro level.

**Current direction:** Ori is a major **late-S2** arc. Her first dreams begin relatively soon after the acute Sukuna aftermath, after several normal dreamless nights rather than an arbitrary multi-month gap.

The arrival connector is now:
early Ori dreams disclosed
→ household knows about the unknown girl
→ future-home scouts leave while Mau remains partly recovery-restricted
→ Mau receives a hyperreal locating dream
→ `I know where she is`
→ Frieren: `Let's go`
→ Mau/Frieren find Ori.

Exact episode number, dream imagery and scene dialogue remain later-layer work.

## DH-013 — Final-site discovery moves into the Ori era
An earlier late-S2 closure shape placed discovery of the future-home candidate after Ori's entire foundational arc and Mau/Frieren formalization.

That was refined so the world keeps moving while Ori receives the lived-space she needs.

**Current direction:** the scout/mixed team can discover the strong final-site candidate in parallel with Mau/Frieren finding Ori. Verification continues in the background throughout Ori's arc. After Ori's foundational chosen-family transition and Mau/Frieren formalization, only the **collective final site decision** needs to occur quickly before the finale.

This preserves:
- the scouting-system payoff;
- Mau learning to trust the household while he is absent/restricted;
- Ori's arc from feeling like a waiting room for the relocation plot;
- the compact post-Ori season closure.
