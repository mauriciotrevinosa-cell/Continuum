# The Arrivals — Decision History

This file records **resolved changes of direction**. They are not contradictions unless a later audit finds that two unresolved authoritative versions still coexist.

## DH-001 — Season 1 count: 20 planning concept → 19 full episodes
Earlier planning briefly treated G3-first-days and Road Days / Sukuna finale as separate late-season units, producing a 20-episode shape.

Later creator decision fused/intercut that material into one larger S1E19.

**Current direction:** 19 full episodes. S1E19 contains G3, road days, safe-night contrast and Sukuna finale. This is resolved.

## DH-002 — S1E18 ending
Earlier brainstorm for the chocolate/day-in-life episode used a joke close in the spirit of “this never happened.”

During voice/story approval, the ending was changed.

**Current direction:** no kiss contact; Mau sincerely asks:
> What's a kiss?

This is resolved and is the Level-4 version.

## DH-003 — City-building timing
Earlier S2 planning expected the Otherworlder City to emerge materially in later S2 and later retained a first-move / first-construction beat near the season end.

As more S2 arcs accumulated, the story was rebalanced again.

**Current direction:** S2 owns social rupture, interim fortification, reconnaissance, strong-site discovery/verification, the final collective site choice and preliminary planning. **Meaningful construction begins in S3.**

The final S2 image remains at the inn: ordinary household life around an unfinished future-home site/foundation/layout plan.

This is a placement evolution, not a contradiction.

**Current direction:** keep CAL-17 as a one-page noncanon production stress test; audit the eventual canon page count separately, with a multi-page sequence currently favored.

## DH-020 — Rimuru's power reference: arrival point vs. 100%

Recorded 2026-10-05 at the creator's request, because it is easy to lose across seasons.

**Current direction:**
- **Arrival point.** Rimuru enters Continuum in S1 from his Demon Lord era: he has **Raphael, not Ciel**. Everything he carries, can do and has observed at arrival belongs to that era. This is why the Tensura portion of the S3 sealed inventory reflects that era.
- **Ciel arrives early, for Continuum reasons.** In the LNs, Raphael's evolution into Ciel happens late and Rimuru names her. In this story it happens in **S3 V13**, forced by the Noise coupling. Rimuru still names her Ciel, the same name as in the LNs, without knowing why the name feels inevitable. He has no future knowledge of her.
- **Rimuru's 100% is his end-of-LN state.** When any later season asks what Rimuru can ultimately become, or where his ceiling is, the reference is the **final Tensura LN Rimuru**. It is not his arrival kit, and not an invented beyond-canon power level.
- **Reaching it is not automatic.** Continuum moves him along that path at its own pace and through its own causes, as Ciel's early arrival shows. A skill does not appear just because it exists later in the LNs; it needs an in-story cause.
- **Exact arrival point (creator, 2026-10-06).** The Rimuru who arrives is the anime's Rimuru at the point where the **Chloe arc concludes** (the creator places this at anime season 4). He therefore **knows about Chloe's time loops**: that he died many times across them and that she carried those resets. He is still Raphael-era, pre-Ciel, so this is consistent with the arrival rule above. It matters for S4: Rimuru knows what it is to be the one someone looped for (see `S4_EARLY_DIRECTION_2026-10-06.md`).
- **Mau's sealed copies are a snapshot.** What the Noise read on V13 Day 17 does not update as Rimuru grows. If Rimuru later reaches skills he did not have then, the seal does not contain them.
