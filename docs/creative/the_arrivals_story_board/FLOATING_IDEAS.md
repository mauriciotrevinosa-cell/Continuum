## Historical multi-romance / harem possibility
An earlier future seed intentionally left open whether multiple people could love Mau and whether a multi-partner outcome might ever be appropriate.

Current later material is much more specific:
- Mau/Frieren are the central intended romantic endpoint;
- Ori is explicitly not reciprocated romantically;
- the story values different forms of love and emotional boundaries.

No later explicit decision found in the preserved material cleanly converts the older broad multi-partner possibility into either “canon” or “forbidden.” Therefore it stays here as a **historical/open possibility**, not as a current active direction and not as a contradiction.
