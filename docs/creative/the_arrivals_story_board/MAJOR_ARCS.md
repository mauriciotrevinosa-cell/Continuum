# The Arrivals — Major Arcs

# Sukuna / Hollow Purple / Second Chance

**Standing:** strong creator-directed working arc; not episode-locked and not manga-ready.

## Setup
Mau begins seeing two recurring futures:
- if he does not intervene, Frieren dies;
- if he intervenes, Sukuna catastrophically wounds/impales Mau and Frieren lives.

Mau incorrectly thinks the visions come from The Noise / his fragmented internal system. They are actually part of a goddess/higher-entity challenge presenting a false binary.

Mau tells Yuta and Rimuru enough to understand the stakes. They search for a third option rather than endorsing his sacrifice.

## Hollow Purple rule
Mau does not simply “copy Limitless.”

He constructs a temporary one-use unstable analogue by understanding functional requirements. It may collapse or detonate at point-blank range. The construction is consumed after use and is not a permanent spammable unlock.

## Team roles
- Yuta: Sukuna knowledge, high-level counterplay, RCT emergency path.
- Rimuru/Raphael: analysis, battlefield communication, barrier logic.
- Okarun: rescue/repositioning.
- Qifrey: prepared field control; buys fractions of a second.
- Himmel: stable frontline/protection and emotional presence.
- Frieren: powerful active participant; never made incompetent just to enable Mau's sacrifice.
- Mau: adaptive analysis and hidden one-use catastrophe.

## Current climax order
1. Frieren believes she has a lethal opening.
2. Sukuna sees the counter-window.
3. Mau intercepts.
4. Sukuna's arm/hand goes physically through Mau's body.
5. Mau's system produces wording approximately like “merging began.”
6. The merging event partially blacks Sukuna out for a short moment.
7. Mau remains physically linked to Sukuna and gets a brief goodbye window.
8. Mau asks Himmel, approximately, to take care of Frieren if Mau dies.
9. Yuta extracts Frieren; Okarun extracts Rimuru.
10. Frieren is initially emotionally frozen by what just happened.
11. Mau begins constructing **Blue first**. It is visibly unstable and attached to/consuming one hand.
12. Barrier completes around the intended space.
13. **Red** forms from particles at/through the other hand.
14. Sukuna recovers, understands the danger and tries to detach/escape.
15. He discovers he cannot simply free himself from Mau. Exact mechanism remains open.
16. Because direct escape is failing, Sukuna invokes Mahoraga.
17. Mahoraga tries physically to separate Red and Blue.
18. It arrives too late; convergence is already irreversible.
19. Mahoraga is annihilated; the wheel falling remains a favored visual.
20. Very short Mau memory/flashback of Frieren smiling.
21. Hollow Purple emerges from the convergence as an extremely unstable phenomenon.
22. Detonation.
23. Sukuna survives near death.
24. Yuta could pursue/finish him.
25. Sukuna uses a costly binding vow/equivalent escape and points out that Mau may still be salvageable.
26. Yuta chooses Mau over pursuit.
27. Frieren returns to Mau; Yuta begins RCT.

CAL-17 remains a **one-page production stress test**, not the locked canon page count. Current direction is that this sequence probably needs multiple pages so farewell, Red/Blue formation, Mahoraga, blast and aftermath can breathe.

## Second Chance
Mau briefly encounters the goddess/higher entity while dying.

The core question is essentially: why did you do that?

Mau's answer is that Frieren is his first true memory/world and he accepts the result if she is safe.

Second Chance is limited:
- re-anchor Mau's soul;
- stabilize/repair only enough for survival to become possible;
- Yuta still does the actual brutal RCT work.

Mau does not remember the divine encounter afterward. Yuta and Mau therefore initially believe Yuta's RCT is the main explanation for survival.

## Recovery / revival
Current working timing: about **three days until consciousness**; first two feel hopeless.

Frieren refuses to leave Mau and eventually stops sleeping. Fern and Maomao get her to rest. Frieren finally sleeps beside/on Mau holding onto him; only then does Fern allow herself to break down.

Revival beat:
- Frieren wakes beside Mau;
- at first she does not process what she is seeing;
- Mau is already awake, looking at her and gently petting/touching her head;
- disbelief → relief → tears → embrace;
- Frieren initiates the kiss;
- Frieren says “I love you”;
- Mau does **not** repeat “I love you” verbally in this awakening beat;
- he reciprocates physically/emotionally and reassures her in the spirit of “I told you everything would be okay”;
- they are romantically changed, but still have no formal relationship label.

Frieren may be wearing Mau's 2021 McLaren Team Hoodie during recovery.

## Post-revival disagreement
Frieren's anger is not that Mau protected her. It is that he:
- accepted his own death as an acceptable cost;
- decided alone;
- kept a secret of that scale;
- failed to consider what his death would do to her.

The promise is about **no more unilateral sacrifice/secrets of that magnitude**, not “never risk yourself again.”

# Mau / Frieren / Himmel

Current intended emotional direction:
- Himmel remains one of the most important people Frieren has ever loved.
- Himmel taught Frieren what love means and why brief human lives matter.
- Mau is Frieren's first consciously recognized romantic love — the first person she knowingly chooses romantically and wants a future with.
- Mau does not “win” Frieren from Himmel.
- Himmel must retain dignity, generosity and agency.

The human-lifespan problem remains a later slow-burn consequence, potentially S3+.

# Ori — the goddess-created girl

**Standing:** preserved future arc; not episode-locked.

The goddess creates Ori partly out of fascination with Mau/anomaly and partly to understand love. Ori begins as an extension/child-like creation of the goddess but becomes increasingly individual through lived experience.

Core properties:
- no ordinary pre-creation memories;
- Mau becomes a first stable emotional reference;
- visible glitches/color shifts reflect identity instability;
- glitches near Mau may behave differently from ordinary instability;
- Ori likes/admires Frieren rather than becoming a simple hostile rival.

Key relationship engine:
- Mau/Frieren are emotionally romantic after revival but not formally labeled.
- A gossip-circle scene exposes that Mau never explicitly asked Frieren to be his girlfriend/partner.
- Someone asks Mau whether he loves Frieren romantically.
- Mau knows he loves Frieren deeply but still struggles with the category “romantically.”
- Ori overhears and sees a possible opening.

Ori then experiments with romance:
- one-on-one outings;
- sitting close;
- asking Mau to accompany her;
- creating reasons for intimacy;
- possibly asking to sleep near him because she is “scared,” with genuine fear mixed with desire for closeness.

Mau interprets care as care, not necessarily as courtship.

Frieren gets active POV:
- jealousy;
- hurt;
- uncertainty about what claim she can make without a formal label;
- fear that Mau may not choose her.

A favored turning beat is Frieren overhearing Ori learn that one of her plans “sounds romantic.”

Eventually Mau tells Ori, in substance:
> I love you, but as a friend.

Ori is heartbroken and leaves crying. She is not villainized. Mau and Frieren both help comfort her afterward.

The arc eventually helps Mau distinguish:
- family love;
- friendship;
- protectiveness;
- attachment;
- attraction;
- romantic love;
- choosing a future with one specific person.

Mau ultimately asks Frieren to define their relationship consciously. Exact label remains open.

The deeper theme is that love requires accepting loss of control: the goddess creates Ori to study love, but Ori becomes a person whose value lies in becoming more than the creator's intention.

# Otherworlder fear / relocation / future city

Sukuna's city destruction turns “Otherworlder” from a mostly neutral new descriptor into a feared social category.

Consequences can include:
- suspicion;
- discrimination;
- restrictions;
- refusal of service;
- harassment;
- political pressure.

The Bocchi incident turns abstract politics into a household safety problem.

Current relocation direction:
current base
→ social rupture
→ Bocchi injury
→ agreement that safer territory is necessary
→ fortify existing home as interim base
→ reconnaissance/cartography/site search
→ choose future location
→ late-S2 first move/first construction
→ S3 major settlement and city growth

City-building is necessity, not empire-building.

# Mau power secrecy / post-Sukuna progression

After Hollow Purple, the group with the clearest knowledge is Mau, Frieren, Yuta and Rimuru.

Current direction:
- do not publicize Mau's unknown mechanism;
- secrecy is risk containment, not vanity;
- Hollow Purple is consumed/unavailable after the Sukuna use;
- later Gojo interaction could allow a fresh reconstruction only through new understanding;
- Mau's durable toolkit should remain small;
- flower-related construction and eventual RCT/self-healing are possible long-term permanent pieces;
- spectacular temporary reconstructions do not automatically become permanent powers.
