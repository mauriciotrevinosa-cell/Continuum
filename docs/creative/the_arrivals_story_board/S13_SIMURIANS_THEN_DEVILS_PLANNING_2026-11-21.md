## Direction so far

- **Order (Claude's proposal; Mauri seems to agree):** Simurians first, then the Devils as the closing arc. Yoru's ninety-day contract runs until B+487 and simmers underneath.
- **No breather:** the Devils hit right on top of the Simurians, so Mau has no time to sort anything out with Yuka. The world shakes him while his personal life feels like it's collapsing.

## Claude's notes (proposals)

- **Where the jealousy comes from:** not "she likes someone else" but "he was there when she was dying, and I wasn't". In our story Yuka arrived already operated on (CANON_TRAPS), so Mau never saw that part of her life.
- **The collapse is internal, never a separation.** Mau and Yuka stay together and Yuka stays clingy (standing rules). Since Mau no longer hides things from her, what crumbles is his ability to name the insecurity while the world won't give him time, not the marriage. The danger is a relapse into "it's fine", and Yuka catches him.
- **Avoid misery fatigue:** small anchors of normality inside the pressure (the plate, Ori, the sailboat *Tomorrow* waiting) so the weight lands.

## Mauri's long reply (22 November 2026), structured

His full words are in the conversation of 22 November 2026; key quotes are kept here.

**Simurians**
- Subaru returns.
- **Dabura's history is altered.** He arrives with everything he learned and evolved fighting Mahoraga, but doesn't remember it was that fight: he only remembers honing his abilities. Possibly he follows Tsurugi.
- Most of the Simurians stay behind, as in the source. This time **Cross and Maru** are the ones who push for dialogue.
- **Tsurugi sells the idea that Mau is the most powerful, "the king"**, which makes Dabura even more interested.
- **Dabura is a close fight; he's a monster.** "Like Gojo: Mau can have more and better in his bag, but that doesn't make the others less, because they adapt, react, read and break everything." Abilities are not intelligence.

**Cross and Yuka (there is real romantic drama)**
- Cross invites her for a walk to talk; Mau gets more and more rabid.
- **The core:** Cross was there at one of the worst moments of her life.
- Yuka keeps justifying that how she gets close to Cross is fine. Mai, Maki or someone else tells her maybe she *is* doing something wrong; she digs in and ignores them.
- **Yuka may compare it to Makima, and that's unfair:** Makima was never romantic. It came from stopping one of the best strategists (if not the best) of her world, who only lost because of the plot. The house is who tells Yuka what everyone else is seeing.

**Mau collapses inside**
- Alien invasion, political negotiation, memories he thought were closed, and Yuka reconnecting with someone who was there at her lowest.
- **"It's fine, I can solve it, I'm not going alone, Makima is coming with me."**
- **The root insecurity is from S1.** When Fern first arrives, Mau assumes the best thing is to step away, and Frieren does the same; each believes it's best for the other. That insecurity matters more than the stored memories or the repressed feelings coming out. Candidate chapters to read before writing: S1E10–S1E13 ("There You Are" through "Space").
- **The Gojo rematch goes here:** Gojo sees his "celosito" and decides to distract him.
- **Dungeon with Makima** during the Cross stretch, to warm up and to "earn back what was lost in the great Hamster War". He's rich; it's a distraction.

**Bridge to the Devils (no breather)**
- When Yuka half-understands the other side of the Makima comparison and thinks Mau finally has a second to talk, **Yoru arrives** to warn him what will happen and how she expects the fight. Or, while that happens, something else does, and Mau has to go to a war alone.
- **Parallel:** he accepts help now, but Yoru has seen a point that differs from the memory and squeezes to see what breaks.
- When Yuka wants to talk, Mau and Makima have to go deal with Yoru's mess. **Denji, Reze, Power and others appear.**
- **Timing:** the ninety-day contract can't be broken. Yoru arrives announcing, and she can cross the protection because, under the contract, she can't harm anyone.

**Mau and Makima:** they start to feel like besties, not romantic, without losing her personality with everyone else, and with Mau's feelings that she receives.

**DIO and Elmesia (keep it as it is)**
- Elmesia asks to talk with Mau about what he thinks and how to help her know what DIO feels.
- DIO feels a little left out (Elmesia invited Mau, not her) without knowing why.
- Afterwards Mau says he'll bring DIO because she was sulking. He returns by Exchange, finds her down, and tells a small lie: he misread Elmesia's letter, and Elmesia assumed DIO would come inside him.
- DIO's face changes into Ori's face when she misunderstood something and has just heard what she wanted.
- Mau says sorry. DIO says he owes her something; Mau says okay, and tells her to bring her plant because Elmesia wanted to see it.

**The pause about a child:** it waits. First resolve the other thing. Yuka believes everything is fine while Mau has fallen back into "it's fine" and is fighting about twenty things inside and outside himself.
