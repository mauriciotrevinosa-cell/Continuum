## Direction so far

- **Order (Claude's proposal; Mauri seems to agree):** Simurians first, then the Devils as the closing arc. Yoru's ninety-day contract runs until B+487 and simmers underneath.
- **No breather:** the Devils hit right on top of the Simurians, so Mau has no time to sort anything out with Yuka. The world shakes him while his personal life feels like it's collapsing.
- **Dabura:** the 1v1 at the quarry; Mau wins and doesn't kill him (existing creator plan). Claude would like to write most of the fight.
- **Cross and Mau's jealousy:** depends on Cross and Yuka's dynamic in Modulo. They weren't a couple (Mauri). Fan summaries say:
  - Yuka confided her tumor and her six months in Cross and asked him to go to the doctor with her.
  - Thanks to her, Cross's heart began to waver.

  Verify before writing.
- **Mau's insecurity** tied back to something he did in S1: **pending, Mauri says which scene.**

## Claude's notes (proposals)

- **Where the jealousy comes from:** not "she likes someone else" but "he was there when she was dying, and I wasn't". In our story Yuka arrived already operated on (CANON_TRAPS), so Mau never saw that part of her life.
- **The collapse is internal, never a separation.** Mau and Yuka stay together and Yuka stays clingy (standing rules). Since Mau no longer hides things from her, what crumbles is his ability to name the insecurity while the world won't give him time, not the marriage. The danger is a relapse into "it's fine", and Yuka catches him.
- **Avoid misery fatigue:** small anchors of normality inside the pressure (the plate, Ori, the sailboat *Tomorrow* waiting) so the weight lands.
