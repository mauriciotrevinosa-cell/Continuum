# The Arrivals — S1E10 Fern / G2 Timing Correction v0.1

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE CREATOR CORRECTION — SURGICAL SUPERSESSION  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Branch:** `m3/critical-path`

## Correction

For **Fern**, the subjective time between:
- the E1 fight / separation from Frieren;
- and seeing Frieren again in E10/E11 convergence

is only **a couple of hours**.

Therefore the older E10 manga-first material that implies:
- an overnight road camp;
- sleeping before resuming travel;
- `morning, closer`;
- enough elapsed time for Fern/Anko/Maomao/Momo to feel like a formed temporary group

is **superseded on timing**.

## Current intended E10 movement

G2 can have a few hours of shared experience:
- initial confusion;
- first meetings;
- beam/lead;
- walking together;
- first practical coordination;
- early jokes/friction/chemistry;
- first impressions.

But on reaching the inn they should still feel like:

> **four strangers who happened to spend a few strange hours together.**

They are **not yet a mini-family**.

Their real integration begins after arrival at the inn.

## What remains valid from older E10 material

Preserve where compatible:
- beam as lead, not proof;
- Fern's urgency;
- Momo/Maomao/Anko personality contrast;
- practical first cooperation;
- Frieren senses Fern only near convergence;
- reunion itself still belongs to the following movement/E11.

## What is superseded

Do not carry forward as current canon:
- overnight camp as required event;
- first-light departure;
- road-days feeling;
- strong pre-inn group cohesion.

The old Draft 1 / manga panel script remains historical production evidence, but this correction governs future LN adaptation and continuity work.
