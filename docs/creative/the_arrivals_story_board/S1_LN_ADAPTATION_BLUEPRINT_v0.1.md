# The Arrivals — Season 1 Light Novel Adaptation Blueprint v0.1

**Status:** CREATOR-REVIEW BLUEPRINT — PROVISIONAL, NOT CHAPTER LOCK  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Branch:** `m3/critical-path`  
**Depends on:** `S1_FINAL_AUDIT_AND_LN_READINESS_v0.1.md`

## Purpose

Convert the approved manga-first S1 corpus into prose without treating manga episodes, manga chapters or page counts as LN quotas.

Core rule:

> **Adapt the story movement, not the panel count.**

One S1 episode may become:
- one long LN chapter;
- two or three chapters;
- occasionally more where POV/structure demands it.

Chapter length is allowed to vary substantially.

---

# 1. Recommended publication shape

Season 1 is too large to treat comfortably as one conventional LN volume.

Current recommended shape:

## Volume 1 — First Home
Approx. source coverage:
- E1–E6

Emotional spine:
```text
Frieren loses Fern
→ Frieren chooses Mau
→ two-person first home
→ Bocchi joins
→ Yuta joins
→ ordinary household forms
→ Rimuru arrives
→ Mau collapses
→ five people choose to remain together
```

Natural volume ending:
> Mau wakes after the Rimuru/Raphael incident and five people finally sit at the table.

## Volume 2 — Making It Ours
Approx. source coverage:
- E7–E14

Emotional spine:
```text
Mau's missing past hurts
→ reciprocal family care
→ trust / construction
→ inn becomes personal
→ G2 approaches
→ Fern returns
→ household expands
→ Mau/Frieren accidentally separate
→ mutual insecurity
→ lake reconciliation
```

Natural volume ending:
> Frieren/Mau restore the shared-room reality after finally understanding that neither wanted the distance.

## Volume 3 — The World Opens
Approx. source coverage:
- E15–E19

Emotional spine:
```text
birthday / chosen identity
→ native society opens
→ real work / economy / routine
→ household reaches late-season stability
→ G3 road threads emerge
→ safe final night
→ Sukuna destroys the assumption that Arrivals are only hopeful
```

Natural ending:
> Sukuna suppresses cursed energy and walks into a world that does not know what has arrived.

This three-volume shape is provisional.

Do not write toward fixed word counts.

---

# 2. Approximate chapter philosophy

A useful working range, **not a quota**:
- short/decompression chapter: ~1,500–3,000 words;
- normal chapter: ~3,000–5,500 words;
- large emotional/action chapter: ~5,500–8,000+ words when earned.

No requirement that neighboring chapters match.

Some chapters may end on:
- a quiet image;
- a question;
- a room/object;
- a reveal;
- an arrival;
- a hard cut.

Not every chapter needs a cliffhanger.

# 5. Adaptation method per chapter

For each LN chapter:

## Step A — source packet
Use:
1. approved Draft 1;
2. integrated cinematic overlay;
3. relevant spatial/continuity bible;
4. current audit;
5. later explicit creator decisions.

Manga panel script is evidence of staging and emphasis, not prose text to translate line-by-line.

## Step B — choose the scene lens
Before drafting, decide what best serves the scene:
- a specific close-third POV character;
- a brief external/cinematic lens;
- or a clearly marked switch at a scene break.

Do **not** default mechanically to Mau or Frieren.

The ensemble should gradually earn POV access where it improves the story.

For a close-third scene, track:
- emotional question;
- start state;
- end state;
- what that character notices;
- what that character does **not** understand yet.

## Step C — convert visual beats
For every major panel-only beat ask:
> What evidence can prose give without explaining the answer?

Use:
- gesture;
- sensory cue;
- object;
- pause;
- spatial distance;
- incomplete thought;
- action.

## Step D — dialogue preservation
Keep approved dialogue when it still works.

Only change when:
- prose context makes it redundant;
- new E2 expansion needs fresh lines;
- cadence becomes unnatural outside panel timing;
- later continuity supersedes wording.

## Step E — chapter QC
Check:
- voice;
- POV integrity;
- no accidental romance/power reveal;
- continuity;
- pacing;
- callback preservation;
- no manga-production language left in prose.

# 6. Recommended production cadence

Do **not** adapt all 19 episodes blindly in one uninterrupted generation pass.

Recommended:

### LN Voice Calibration before full-volume drafting

Use a compact Voice Check workflow similar to the manga process, but adapted to prose.

For each important POV/voice as it becomes relevant:
- provide **2 short canon-derived sample scenes**;
- one should test ordinary/comedic/social voice;
- one should test emotional/serious/interior voice;
- samples should be long enough to judge prose, but short enough that creator review is easy.

Creator reads the samples and gives:
- green light;
- targeted correction;
- or replacement direction.

Once green-lit, that character's LN voice becomes the working baseline and does **not** require full-chapter re-review just to reconfirm voice.

Start with:
1. Frieren;
2. Mau;
3. Bocchi;
4. Yuta;
5. Rimuru/Raphael;

then calibrate G2 and later ensemble characters as they enter.

This tests:
- dialogue expansion;
- interiority;
- narrator distance;
- comedy;
- emotional restraint;
- POV-specific observation.

### Then
Adapt **one volume at a time**, in multi-chapter batches.

Suggested rhythm:
- 2–4 chapters per writing/review batch during Volume 1;
- once consistency is proven, larger batches are acceptable;
- final pass across each completed volume for continuity and repeated prose habits.

This is faster than chapter-by-chapter micromanagement forever, but safer than writing all of S1 before the LN voice is proven.

---

# 7. What not to inherit from manga workflow

Do not carry over:
- page counts;
- panel counts;
- artificial chapter equality;
- visual exposition labels;
- camera instructions;
- `hold panel` language;
- page-turn logic when prose has a better pause.

Do inherit:
- scene purpose;
- emotional order;
- silence;
- spatial causality;
- object motifs;
- dialogue voice;
- visual callbacks translated into prose.
