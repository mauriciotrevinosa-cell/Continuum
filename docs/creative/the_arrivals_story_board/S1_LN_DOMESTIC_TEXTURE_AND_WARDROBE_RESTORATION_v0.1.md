# The Arrivals — S1 LN Domestic Texture + Wardrobe Restoration v0.1

**Status:** COMPLETED PRODUCTION PASS  
**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Scope:** S1 LN v0.1, with forward continuity into S2  
**Governing direction:** prefer meaningful lived-in expansion over compression

## Trigger

Creator review of the first LN illustration exposed a broader adaptation risk:

> facts that existed in Story continuity could survive in abstract form while losing the specific object/state that gave them emotional weight.

The pass therefore moved beyond visual hardening and restored recurring wardrobe/object continuity as prose-native story material.

## Governing rule

For `The Arrivals`, ordinary-life material does not need to advance macro plot to earn space.

A beat is doing story work when it:
- makes a relationship habitual;
- gives an object history;
- makes a room feel lived in;
- establishes a later absence;
- creates or strengthens a callback;
- distinguishes one character's routine from another;
- lets time and household life accumulate naturally.

When the choice is between compressing such a beat or allowing the LN to become longer, prefer the longer version.
