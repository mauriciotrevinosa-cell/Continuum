# The Arrivals — S1 LN Visual Continuity Hardening Audit v0.1

**Status:** COMPLETED PASS / S1 LN v0.1  
**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Scope:** all 57 Season 1 LN draft chapters, checked episode-by-episode against current Draft 1 sources and applicable later continuity/addenda already carried into the LN production pass.

## Why this audit exists

Illustration 01 exposed a production weakness that can exist even when prose is story-correct:

> a visual fact may be present somewhere in the chapter or source, but phrased too generically at the scene handoff, allowing downstream visual production to normalize it into the wrong default.

The audit therefore looked specifically for under-specification of:
- physical state;
- wardrobe state;
- story-critical props/accessories;
- injury state;
- room / object continuity;
- environment evolution;
- time/weather;
- visual relationship blocking.

This was **not** a story rewrite.

## Result

This audit found **three scenes that needed prose hardening**:
1. S1E1 final pre-Arrival collapse;
2. S1E15 opening shared-room continuity;
3. S1E17 first work-return visual state.

No broad S1 rewrite was required.

The more important root fix is now part of the authoritative source gate:

> story-critical visual states must be explicit enough at scene handoff that downstream visual production cannot safely substitute a contradictory generic default.

## Illustration-production consequence

Illustrations must still be audited against Story source and approved visual references.

The LN is not a substitute for:
- exact character model sheets;
- final wardrobe assets;
- exact design of unresolved source-canon props;
- creator approval.

For example, the prose may correctly say `Fern's hair ornament` while the exact ornament shape still comes from the approved visual/source reference rather than being invented from text.
