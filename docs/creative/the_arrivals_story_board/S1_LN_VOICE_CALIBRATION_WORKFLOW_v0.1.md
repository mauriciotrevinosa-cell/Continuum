# The Arrivals — S1 LN Voice Calibration Workflow v0.1

**Status:** WORKING METHOD — CREATOR-APPROVED DIRECTION  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Branch:** `m3/critical-path`

## Purpose

Adapt the manga Voice Check idea to light-novel prose without requiring full-chapter rereads just to validate voice.

The LN voice problem includes more than dialogue.

For each character, calibration checks:
- spoken cadence;
- internality;
- narrator distance;
- what they notice;
- what they avoid naming;
- comedy;
- emotional restraint;
- how visual acting becomes prose.

## Default calibration unit

When a character first becomes important as a prose lens, provide **two short canon-derived samples**.

### Sample A — ordinary register
Tests one or more of:
- daily life;
- comedy;
- casual conversation;
- social rhythm;
- practical behavior.

### Sample B — pressure register
Tests one or more of:
- grief;
- conflict;
- vulnerability;
- fear;
- serious decision;
- emotionally loaded silence.

The samples should come from real S1 material or direct adaptation of real S1 beats.

Do not create unrelated test scenes merely for voice.

## Creator review

Creator can respond with:
- **GREEN** — voice is trusted;
- **ADJUST** — specific issue to correct;
- **REDO** — sample fundamentally misses the character.

Once GREEN:
- continue drafting that character at speed;
- do not ask the creator to reconfirm voice chapter by chapter;
- reopen only if later prose enters a materially different register or drift appears.

## POV rule

Not every character must become a POV character.

Calibrate POV interiority only when the story actually benefits from entering that character's head.

A character may still receive dialogue/behavior calibration even if most scenes observe them externally.

POV should be chosen by story need, not cast quota.

## Initial sequence

Likely first calibration order:
1. Frieren;
2. Mau;
3. Bocchi;
4. Yuta;
5. Rimuru + Raphael.

Then:
- Fern;
- Momo;
- Maomao;
- Anko;

as G2 becomes relevant.

Later:
- G3 voices only when their S1E19/S2 material requires meaningful prose access.

## Batch rule

Do not make the creator review every character separately if several samples fit naturally in one compact packet.

A useful packet can contain:
- 2 Frieren samples;
- 2 Mau samples;

then the next packet:
- Bocchi;
- Yuta;

etc.

Keep each packet small enough to compare voice without turning into a chapter review.

## Relationship to existing manga Voice Checks

Existing manga Voice Checks remain evidence of:
- dialogue cadence;
- behavior;
- character logic.

LN Voice Calibration adds:
- prose interiority;
- narrator relationship to character;
- adaptation of silence;
- expanded dialogue where visual manga shorthand no longer carries enough information.

It does not erase prior approvals.

It extends them into the new medium.
