# The Arrivals — S1–S4 character and space continuity gate (2026-10-12)

**Status:** production cross-check, not a new story event or species reveal.  
**Purpose:** catch false classifications, knowledge leaks, location drift and disappearing residents before the next LN chapter. This focused pass compares the S1/S2 audit and S2 inventory, S3 v0.2 backtrace and production chapters, the S3 relationship/presence locks, the S3/S4 layout lock, and the S4 cast/presence ledger. It is not a claim that every chapter in 25 volumes has been line-edited.

## Authority and certainty

Current creator corrections and explicit dated decisions outrank older proposals. For S3, use v0.2 production and backtrace over older v0.1 drafts. For buildings, use the dated layout lock over a casual material word in prose. The S4 cast ledger tracks the current household but does not itself settle biological identity or private character knowledge. Keep `confirmed`, `character belief`, `possibility`, `private knowledge`, and `open` separate. Dialogue may express a mistaken belief **only if the narrative means it as a mistake**; it cannot silently become a fact.

## Space and residency at V22 #17

- **S1 / early S3:** abandoned inn is the lived home. S3's future site is not already a completed house (`S3_CONTINUITY_BACKTRACE_AND_REBUILD_v0.2.md` §3–4).
- **Muslin, late S3 into early S4:** a one-floor provisional house. Interior partitions are **boards slotted between posts**, movable by lifting them out (`S3_S4_FAMILY_HOUSE_MEGA_INN_LAYOUT_LOCK_2026-10-04.md` §10.1). The name comes from Wakana's sewing metaphor. The roof/ceiling and general walls are not literal fabric. Special curtains/screens exist (Tetia/Agott, Nazuna, a screen by the stove); describe them only where placed.
- **V22 #17:** Mau/Frieren's Muslin east-end room has library bay, reading chair, closet and Rimuru's loft. Diablo is outside its door. The real two-floor house is still being built and its rooms/doors cannot be imported back into the Muslin.
- **Real house:** two floors and its own detailed room map (§10.3); when the family moves, update the lived-base field. Momo/Vamola's shared room is explicit: V22 #27, `Moving Day, Round Two`, records Momo saying that Vamola has shared her room since arrival and should share it on the real-house plan too. Preserve that pre-move relationship as well as the new-house assignment.

## Pre-chapter gate (apply at every volume)

1. Record `TIME`, `LIVED BASE`, `PRESENT / AWAY`, `KNOWLEDGE STATE`, `MAU PHYSICAL / MEMORY STATE`, `SETTLEMENT STATE`, and after G5 `ARRIVAL / REFUGEE STATUS` (S3 backtrace §2).
2. For each named speaker, check whether they personally learned the fact they state. Keep a private-trial knowledge list: initial elf-route witnesses Mau/Frieren/Rimuru/Ciel; Maomao learns afterward; Maki learns privately in V22 #17. Do not imply general household knowledge without a scene.
3. Compare any species/lifespan assertion with the Mau table above. “Human-looking,” a conditional “if he is human,” and a worried guess are distinct from a confirmed human lifespan.
4. Compare room, wall, door and ceiling descriptions with the building and chapter date. A metaphor or one curtain cannot turn all partitions into cloth.
5. Scan the active residence roster and the last two movements for quiet co-presence. A zero-mention resident needs a natural beat, a stated absence, or an explicit deliberate choice; never erase a person because a plan omitted a row. Check Vamola, Mai, Nazuna and other flagged residents in `S4_PRESENCE_AND_RELATIONSHIP_AUDIT_2026-10-10.md`.
6. Update `CAST_LEDGER_S4.md` with actual changes in relationship, room, status or knowledge in the same pass. Record unresolved questions rather than filling them with inference.

**Audit limits / open items:** No exhaustive line-by-line read of all 25 volumes was performed here. The Muslin cloth-language drift found in V21 #9/#12, V22 #17 and V22 #26 was corrected in those passages; targeted search should be repeated as new chapters arrive. Mau's species, natural lifespan, eventual elf-route viability and the future reciprocal Frieren/Mau conversation stay open.
