# The Arrivals — S2 Final Audit Pass C: Time, Calendar and World Continuity v0.1

**Status:** PASS C COMPLETE — S2 MACRO STORY CLOSED  
**Date:** 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Pass C checks whether Season 2 can plausibly occupy roughly Month ~4 through Month ~13–14 without feeling like a chain of events that all happened in a few weeks.

It also checks whether the outside world, logistics, reputation and household growth remain continuous while the character arcs unfold.

## Verdict

> **PASS — the current S2 clock and world-state are coherent.**

No new blocking macro gap remains.

## Provisional planning clock

```text
S1 end / S2 opening                         ~Month 4
opening fear + Bocchi + G3                  ~Month 4–5
G3 integration / fortification              ~Month 5–6
G4 convergence spread across lived time     ~Month 6–8
G4 ordinary life / relationship runway      ~Month 8–10
fatal-dream escalation + ~one-week prep      ~Month 10–11
Sukuna battle + recovery                     ~Month 11-ish
post-revival life / Ori / formalization      ~Month 11.5–13
final site-search / long-term home choice    ~Month 13–14
```

These are planning ranges, not locked timestamps.

Episodeization may shift individual blocks while preserving the overall lived-time requirement.

## Calendar continuity requirements

Elapsed time should be visible through ordinary evidence rather than exposition alone:

- weather and daylight change;
- clothing changes with season;
- seasonal food / meals;
- lake/road/scouting conditions change;
- rooms/fortifications at the inn visibly evolve;
- injuries heal;
- routines change;
- characters refer back to events from weeks/months earlier;
- relationships have enough repeated ordinary contact to feel old rather than newly scripted.

## Year-turn / New Year-like festival at the inn — STRONG CANDIDATE

A strong episode/sequence candidate belongs after G4 is substantially integrated and before the fatal-dream/Sukuna phase fully takes over.

Shape:

```text
household learns local year-turn / New Year-like festival is approaching
→ public Otherworlder fear still makes a large visible trip into town unsafe/unwelcome
→ instead of disguising the entire family, they build a smaller festival at the inn
→ everyone contributes through food / decorations / games / traditions / comedy
→ relationship runway advances inside the celebration
→ the event marks that months have genuinely passed
```

Emotional function:

> **The outside world is not ready to celebrate with them, so they create a celebration for one another.**

This can support:
- G4 integration;
- Bocchi/Kita;
- Mau/Frieren;
- Himmel becoming ordinary household family rather than romantic rival;
- Maki/Yuta;
- larger ensemble interactions.

It is a strong episodeization candidate, not a locked episode number.

## Birthdays

Birthdays are useful continuity markers but do not each require full episodes.

Current rule:
- S1 already contains Mau's first Continuum birthday late in S1.
- Because the current S2 endpoint is ~Month 13–14, Mau's next birthday is **not guaranteed to occur before S2 ends**.
- Do not stretch S2 merely to force birthday #2.
- If episodeization naturally pushes the clock far enough, Mau birthday #2 can occur late S2.
- Otherwise it becomes a strong early-S3 marker.
- Frieren's birthday and other meaningful birthdays can be embedded inside other episodes/scenes if the final calendar supports them.
- A birthday may be a meal, gift, short subplot or emotional callback rather than an entire episode.

## Supply / household logistics — MACRO CONTINUITY RESOLVED

The Bocchi incident changes how the household interacts with the mercantile town.

Afterward:
- vulnerable household members do not continue making casual unsafe errands;
- direct town dependence decreases;
- the household diversifies supply through trusted merchants/intermediaries, scouting routes, hunting/fishing/foraging, safe guild/work income and other practical sources already supported by the setting;
- Rimuru's budgeting/logistics role grows in importance;
- G3/G4 contribute to work and supply rather than becoming passive mouths to feed;
- the inn is **not instantly self-sufficient**.

This pressure strengthens the future-home search:
- water matters;
- resource access matters;
- defensible routes matter;
- trade access matters;
- room for later food/infrastructure matters.

Exact economy, food quantities and job schedules are episode/worldbuilding detail, not macro blockers.

## Otherworlder reputation continuity

Sukuna's S1 city destruction changes the public meaning of `Otherworlder`.

That damage does not disappear simply because the protagonists later fight Sukuna.

Through S2:
- nearby hostility can remain acute;
- some individuals/merchants can still be sympathetic;
- rumors about the inn can simultaneously attract displaced Otherworlders and alarm locals;
- different regions/people can react differently;
- fear, curiosity, opportunism and sympathy may coexist.

After the S2 Sukuna battle:
- the outside world does not automatically know the full truth of what occurred;
- there is no instant reputation reset;
- the household may have saved lives without receiving public vindication.

This continuity helps justify why choosing a safer long-term home is still necessary even after Sukuna is temporarily removed from the immediate board.

## Scouting continuity

Scouting changes function over the season:

```text
survival / route discovery
→ fortification support
→ resource and trade-route mapping
→ future-home candidate comparison
→ search for isolated arrivals / anomalies
→ final site choice
```

Candidate-site search should appear intermittently through S2 rather than disappearing for months and suddenly producing the perfect location at the finale.

The final site therefore feels discovered through accumulated work.

## G3/G4 time continuity

G3 and G4 should not feel as though they arrived on consecutive weekends.

- G3 gets enough time to become "the people already here" before G4 is substantially integrated.
- G4 convergence can occur across a meaningful span of time.
- G4 then receives additional ordinary-life runway before the fatal-dream phase.

This also makes the growing rumor about the Otherworlder inn believable: enough travelers, merchants and time exist for the place to acquire a reputation.

## Post-Sukuna continuity

Recovery and post-revival life must occupy real time.

Do not compress:

```text
Mau wakes
→ Ori arrives immediately
→ Ori confesses
→ Mau asks Frieren out
→ new home found
```

Instead preserve:
- physical recovery;
- household emotional processing;
- Mau/Frieren ordinary couple-life;
- continued work/scouting;
- Ori search and integration;
- Ori closeness/confession/chosen-family resolution;
- buffer before Mau/Frieren formalization;
- continued site comparison;
- final future-home choice.
