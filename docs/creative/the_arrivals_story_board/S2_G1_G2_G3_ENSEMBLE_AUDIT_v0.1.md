# The Arrivals — S2 G1+G2+G3 Ensemble Audit v0.1

**Status:** WORKING ENSEMBLE AUDIT / STORY GENERATOR — NOT CANON LOCK  
**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Scope:** G3 integration era before G4 begins substantially converging.

## Purpose

Check whether the 17-person G1+G2+G3 household is becoming a real social network rather than:
- Mau + spokes;
- old groups remaining isolated;
- newcomers existing only for their source-world reunion;
- characters becoming job descriptions.

This is **not a screen-time quota**.

The test is:
> has each established character continued to feel alive, and do enough cross-group relationships exist that the household can plausibly become one community?

If someone later appears forgotten, apply the ensemble omission backtrace rule rather than compensating artificially in a later episode.

---

# 1. Overall diagnosis

The ensemble is structurally healthy.

There is no blocking relationship gap before G4.

The main creative opportunity is to deliberately exercise **cross-group relationships** during G3 integration so the story stops feeling like:

```text
G1 relationships
+ G2 relationships
+ G3 relationships
```

and starts feeling like:

```text
one household with overlapping friendships, work circles, sibling dynamics,
romances, study circles, patrol teams, jokes and habits
```

The characters at highest risk of becoming too functional or too tied to one relationship are:
- Anko;
- Wakana;
- Coco;
- Mikasa;
- Okarun;
- Umaru;
- Kita.

This is not because they lack story value. It is because their strongest current functions can easily narrow them:
- Anko → investigator/observer;
- Wakana → Marin + sewing;
- Coco → Qifrey + magic;
- Mikasa → patrol/security;
- Okarun → Momo memory problem;
- Umaru → Mau sibling attachment;
- Kita → Bocchi reunion/romance.

The G3 integration block is the ideal place to prevent that narrowing before it becomes a pattern.

# 3. Relationship lanes worth deliberately exercising

These are not fixed cliques. They are overlapping lanes that can generate stories.

### Home / family lane
Frieren, Mau, Fern, Bocchi, Rimuru, Umaru — with others constantly entering/leaving.

Useful for:
- room chaos;
- sleeping arrangements;
- meals;
- movie/game nights;
- sibling dynamics;
- Mau giving up his room.

### Girlies / moving-pack lane
Momo, Anko, Maomao, Marin, Kita, Bocchi, Fern, Mikasa, Umaru, Coco, with Frieren joining selectively.

Not all attend every time.

Useful for:
- outings;
- Maomao kidnapping gag;
- shopping/material runs;
- small adventures;
- social chemistry;
- preventing women from existing only through romance/protagonist relationships.

### Craft / magic / practical-work lane
Rimuru, Wakana, Qifrey, Coco, Frieren, Maomao, Mau, with Marin often entering through aesthetics/material choices.

Useful for:
- inn expansion;
- bedding/clothing;
- Witch Hat materials;
- lab/storage;
- magical-framework comparison;
- practical worldbuilding through work.

### Patrol / outdoors lane
Yuta, Mikasa, Okarun, Mau, Fern and rotating others.

Useful for:
- maps;
- perimeter;
- scouting;
- low-risk jobs;
- teaching newcomers routes;
- letting Mikasa/Yuta exist outside pure combat.

### Music / culture / entertainment lane
Bocchi, Kita, Marin, Momo, Umaru, Rimuru and whoever happens to join.

Useful for:
- music nights;
- movie nights;
- games;
- cultural exchange;
- comedy;
- ordinary evenings.

### Memory / privacy lane
Momo, Okarun, Anko, Yuta, Rimuru and eventually the wider household.

Useful for:
- confidentiality questions;
- who owns a personal history;
- how to help Okarun without treating him as a puzzle;
- Contract origin.

This lane should remain emotionally grounded and not become a detective case whose goal is to "solve Okarun" immediately.

# 4. Highest-value underused relationships

## Mau + Fern

This deserves deliberate exercise early in S2.

S1 established Fern as part of Frieren's world and Mau as Frieren's new-world anchor, but S2 benefits if Mau/Fern clearly become family **independent of Frieren**.

Good material:
- chores;
- casual table conversation;
- Fern correcting Mau about something practical;
- Mau asking Fern something about Frieren and receiving a completely mundane answer;
- Fern asking Mau for help rather than always going through Frieren.

This makes later Stark/Himmel arrivals richer because Fern can present Mau as someone important to *her* too.

## Frieren + Rimuru

S1 begins with rational distrust around Mau's collapse.

By G3 integration, small signs of comfort can show that this changed.

Possible:
- Frieren casually hands Rimuru something without explaining because she assumes he will understand;
- Rimuru complains about one of Frieren's impractical requests;
- Frieren asks him a real logistical question rather than treating him as an unknown system;
- slime-form physical comedy becomes normal.

No "friendship speech" needed.

## Anko + the household

Anko currently has the greatest omission risk.

She should not receive a forced arc merely to correct this.

Instead, exercise her through:
- ordinary conversations;
- writing/notes;
- dry observations;
- joining some outings;
- being amused by Momo/Maomao;
- participating in rumor discussions;
- noticing privacy boundaries around Okarun.

She can become connective tissue without becoming the household detective who narrates everyone.

## Wakana independent of Marin

Wakana needs at least some scenes where Marin is not the reason he is present.

The overcrowding/expansion phase is ideal:
- bedding;
- fabric;
- clothing repair;
- practical measurements;
- material quality;
- collaboration with Rimuru/Qifrey/Mau.

His competence should make people seek him out directly.

## Coco + Mau must become an actual sibling-like relationship

This is stronger than a generic "Coco should have peers" note.

Current intended direction:
- Coco gradually sees Mau as an older-brother-like / close sibling figure;
- Mau sees Coco as one of the younger people he naturally helps, learns beside and protects without infantilizing her;
- Qifrey can study/practice with **Mau and Coco together**, giving the trio recurring shared time;
- those sessions should include ordinary mistakes, curiosity, breaks, jokes and practical learning rather than existing only for lore;
- Mau/Coco should also receive small interactions outside Qifrey so their bond is not merely teacher-adjacent;
- Fern, Bocchi, Umaru and the girlies remain important additional relationships for Coco.

This matters before Agott arrives because Coco should already have a new-world family network rather than waiting for Witch Hat characters to reconstruct her entire social world.

## Okarun independent of Momo

His memory problem is emotionally central but should not consume his identity.

Mau is the strongest new friendship lane.

Okarun can also:
- join mundane jobs;
- become curious about Contracts;
- be awkward with Bocchi/Umaru;
- engage with impossible magic systems because he is already comfortable taking the bizarre seriously.

## Kita independent of Bocchi

Her route begins through Bocchi, but she should become a household member herself.

Marin/Momo provide easy social entry.

Mau becomes an independent friendship only after Kita observes how he treats Bocchi.

Music/culture gives her value that does not depend on romance.

# 5. Relationships that should NOT be over-engineered yet

Not every pair needs chemistry immediately.

Avoid manufacturing scenes solely because two names have not interacted.

Examples:
- Frieren does not need a special bond with every G3 girl;
- Yuta does not need to mentor every younger character;
- Mikasa does not need a war-trauma conversation with every serious character;
- Maomao does not need to scientifically interrogate every power system;
- Mau does not need a personalized welcome scene with all eight newcomers.

Some relationships can remain:
- polite;
- background;
- practical;
- undeveloped until later.

The network becomes believable through **uneven closeness**, not universal intimacy.

---

# 6. G3 integration ensemble guardrails

During this block:

- let old S1 pairings recur naturally so the season still feels continuous;
- add cross-group pairings without immediately making them "special relationships";
- allow scenes where Mau is absent;
- let G3 members interact with one another after arrival rather than dissolving their road bonds instantly;
- preserve G2 chemistry instead of treating G2 as finished once G3 arrives;
- use common-room meals and work as low-cost continuity surfaces;
- if a character seems forgotten, backtrace before compensating later.

The desired endpoint is not equal coverage.

It is:

> by the time G4 begins arriving, the audience can imagine what almost any established character is probably doing on an ordinary afternoon even when the camera is not on them.

---

# 7. Audit result

**No blocking ensemble gap.**

The G3 integration block should now be developed with special attention to:
- Mau/Fern;
- Frieren/Rimuru;
- Anko household presence;
- Wakana independent craft relationships;
- Coco independent peer relationships;
- Okarun outside Momo;
- Kita outside Bocchi;
- Mikasa outside patrol;
- Umaru branching outward from Mau.

These are **creative opportunities / continuity protections**, not mandatory episode assignments.

Next useful task:
> feed these relationships back into the G3 lived-space reservoir and discover additional small stories before moving toward the G3→G4 bridge.
