## Pacing principle

Normal episodes may cover:
- one continuous event;
- several ordinary days;
- parallel household lanes;
- an external interlude + home life.

Large episodes may become much longer than the average.

Especially:
- E05;
- E18–E23;
- E24–E30;
- E32.

Do not force equal chapter length.

## Season clock

Planning texture, not timestamp lock:

- S2 opening: around Month 4;
- fear/G3: Month 4–6;
- G4 convergence: Month 6–8;
- G4 lived life / relationship runway: Month 8–10;
- dreams/preparation/Sukuna: Month 10–11;
- recovery/Ori: Month 11.5–13;
- formalization/site choice/finale: Month 13–14.

Time should be felt through:
- seasons/weather;
- clothing;
- repairs and inn changes;
- injuries healing;
- repeated routines;
- relationships gaining shorthand;
- people referring naturally to weeks/months of shared life.

## Main LN craft targets

1. Preserve source-specific voices.
2. Give ensemble scenes lateral movement.
3. Let domestic actions carry subtext.
4. Keep romance undefined until characters actually define it.
5. Let grief and recovery occupy real prose time.
6. Make the inn physically change as population grows.
7. Make wardrobe progression visible and traceable.
8. Keep the outside world alive even during household-heavy arcs.
9. Do not make every emotional answer arrive in dialogue.
10. Let callbacks be ordinary objects before they become callbacks.

## Reader-review workflow

The creator may read many chapters without comment.

Silence means:
- no blocking reader issue surfaced.

Only flagged chapters require immediate revision.

Reader-facing references should use:
- `V4 C1`, etc.

not production scene IDs.
