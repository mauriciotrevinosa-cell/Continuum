## Expansion bias — stronger than S1 v0.1

S2 should be written with the lesson learned during the creator's S1 reader pass:

> preserve lived weight even when it adds substantial length.

A beat does **not** need to advance macro plot to earn prose space.

Prefer expansion when it makes any of these real:

- household routine;
- wardrobe use/borrowing/repair;
- room geography;
- object continuity;
- food and cleanup;
- shared sleeping arrangements;
- small work-return states;
- route familiarity;
- cross-group friendships;
- ordinary couple-life;
- jealousy that grows through repeated observation;
- someone learning the household's jokes;
- someone being absent from a chair/table/room where they used to be;
- the same item acquiring a different meaning months later;
- time passing through changed weather, clothes, habits and injuries rather than exposition.

Do not turn this into catalog prose.

The test is:
> if removing the beat would make a later joke, absence, reunion, grief or callback weaker, keep it.
