# The Arrivals — S2 Post-Sukuna Lived-Space Pass v0.1

**Status:** STRONG STORY PROPOSAL / CREATOR REVIEW — NOT EPISODE LOCK  
**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Scope:** Mau awake + family aftermath → trust repair / ordinary life → resumed scouting → Mau/Frieren couple-like life → short separation from predictive dreams → early Ori dreams disclosed → hyperreal locating dream / Mau+Frieren departure.

This pass begins after the immediate death/revival braid already established.

The goal is **not** to rush toward Ori.

The goal is to prove:
> Mau survived, but the household did not return to the exact version of itself that existed before Sukuna.

---

# 1. Entry state

At the start of this block:
- Mau is alive;
- Frieren has kissed him and said `I love you`;
- Mau has emotionally reciprocated but has not yet named his own feeling as romantic love;
- the household has had / is having its first large relief meal;
- the deeper anger and intervention are beginning;
- Mau/Yuta/Rimuru are carrying responsibility for the secrecy;
- several residents are temporarily clingier / more watchful;
- Mau is physically recovering and not yet back to full ordinary activity;
- Sukuna is gone from the immediate battlefield but remains alive elsewhere;
- public Otherworlder fear has **not** vanished.

The important emotional rule:

> relief and anger coexist.

Nobody has to choose one.

---

# 2. The first days after the relief meal

The meal is not the end of the aftermath.

The next morning can feel stranger.

People laughed the night before.

Now:
- dishes still exist;
- Mau is still hurt;
- everyone remembers what happened;
- some conversations avoided during dinner now have to happen.

This is where the intervention / responsibility conversations can occur over several days rather than as one tribunal.

Possible texture:
- Maomao checks Mau before breakfast and tells him he is not ready for whatever he was about to do;
- Fern is irritated before Mau has even said anything;
- Umaru is physically nearby more often than usual;
- Coco drifts into the same room under increasingly weak excuses;
- Frieren acts normal enough that outsiders might miss how closely she is tracking him;
- Rimuru becomes unusually quiet around Mau for periods;
- Yuta is tired enough that Maki starts forcing **him** to rest too.

The house is relieved.

The house is not healed.

---

# 3. The intervention should produce distributed consequences

The family eventually confronts Mau/Yuta/Rimuru.

This pass assumes the already-established core:
- different family members held different theories about Mau's sacrifice;
- both were partly true;
- Mau saw himself as the most expendable person **and** loved the family so much that he chose himself as the cost;
- the household rejects the idea that other relationships make Mau replaceable.

Do not make the aftermath only about Mau being scolded.

Different consequences can continue in separate relationships.

### Mau / Rimuru
The Contract remains a wound.

Rimuru may say in substance:
> do not ever put me in that position again.

Mau cannot answer:
> I promise I will never make a hard choice again.

A better promise:
> I will not hide something this large and decide for everyone again.

Rimuru may still be angry even after accepting that.

### Mau / Yuta
Yuta succeeded in creating the third outcome.

That does not make the week morally comfortable.

Possible post-crisis dynamic:
- Yuta becomes slightly overprotective / medically controlling;
- Mau notices;
- Maki notices too;
- eventually Yuta has to admit he was preparing to watch Mau die and then drag him back.

Their friendship can deepen through that admission.

### Mau / Anko
Anko can resume dry humor while still clearly angry.

This is valuable because:
> relationship continuity does not require emotional closure.

### Mau / Fern
Fern's anger is partly:
- secrecy;
- overexertion;
- the effect on Frieren.

She may become very strict about Mau's recovery.

### Mau / sisters
Bocchi / Umaru / Coco do not need the same reaction.

- Bocchi may need reassurance but also has Kita/Fern.
- Umaru may become physically clingy / homebody-protective.
- Coco may hover through study/work.
- all three can process at different speeds.

---

# 4. The first attempted errand should become a family joke with a wound underneath

Strong callback candidate.

Mau is finally cleared for something trivial.

He says he is going to:
- pick something up;
- check a nearby route;
- retrieve a tool;
- walk outside for ten minutes.

One person says they are coming.

Then another.

Then another.

Possible final absurdity:
- Frieren;
- Umaru;
- Coco;
- Fern;
- Bocchi;
- maybe Okarun / someone else who actually had a reason to go.

Mau:
> I was going five minutes away.

Nobody cares.

Surface:
- comedy.

Underneath:
- they watched him leave once and return apparently dead.

This can become the first point where Mau consciously changes his language:

Before:
> I'm going out.

After:
> I'm going to the east path with Okarun. We'll be back before dinner.

That is the lasting behavior.

Not asking permission.

Communicating because he belongs to people.

---

# 5. Mau/Frieren post-revival life has to become real before Ori

This is essential.

They are not yet formally boyfriend/girlfriend.

But emotionally and behaviorally:
> they are already living as a couple.

Possible ordinary texture:
- Frieren reaches for Mau's hand without thinking;
- Mau automatically makes room for her beside him;
- reading together;
- sleeping / resting near one another according to established household arrangement;
- Frieren checking Mau's injuries without making it dramatic;
- Mau bringing Frieren something she likes because he noticed it;
- small kisses / forehead contact / leaning;
- arguing about something mundane;
- Frieren becoming annoyed when Mau tries to hide pain;
- Mau learning that `I love you` changed nothing and everything at once.

Do not make every interaction romantic.

They should still:
- annoy each other;
- read;
- work;
- talk to Fern;
- exist in group scenes.

The relationship should feel **embedded inside family life**.

---

# 6. Frieren's anger can coexist with tenderness

Important guardrail.

Frieren should not be:
- angry for three episodes and physically cold;
or:
- affectionate immediately and therefore "over it."

She can:
- hold Mau's hand at dinner;
- sleep beside him;
- kiss him;
- still become furious when the topic of the secret comes up.

This is emotionally coherent.

Her love does not erase the boundary violation.

Her anger does not erase the love.

This helps establish a healthier future relationship:
> conflict is not abandonment.

We need at least several pieces of life that are **not about Mau's death**.

Possible story material:

### Common-room return
The first time Mau sits in the common room for a full evening again.

Nothing major happens.

That itself matters.

- Wakana sewing;
- Anko writing;
- Momo/Marin/Umaru noise;
- Bocchi/Kita music;
- Qifrey/Coco/Agott work;
- Rimuru slime somewhere inappropriate;
- Mikasa doing something practical;
- Yuta/Maki nearby;
- Stark/Himmel/Fern/Frieren occupying different parts of the room.

People occasionally look at Mau.

Eventually they stop.

### Cooking / chores
Mau tries to resume chores too early.

Everybody tells him no in different ways.

He eventually gets assigned something insultingly light.

He hates it.

### Movie / game night
A recurring tradition returns.

The emotional point:
> the household can do something stupid again.

### Outside outing
A small mixed outing with no major danger.

The world is still there.

Important:

# 9. Scouting / future-home work resumes gradually

The relocation problem still exists.

Sukuna does not solve it.

If anything, the battle reinforces:
- danger can find them;
- the inn is emotionally precious but strategically limited;
- the household is larger than ever.

Do not immediately send Mau on a dangerous expedition.

Possible progression:
1. scouts who were already active continue without him;
2. maps / candidate notes accumulate;
3. Mau participates in discussion from home;
4. later he joins a low-risk route/site check;
5. family overescorts him at first;
6. eventually scouting normalizes.

This keeps the future-home thread alive before Ori.

---

# 10. The outside world does not suddenly love them

Sukuna's defeat should not create a superhero reputation reset.

Most natives do not know:
- who fought;
- what exactly happened;
- whether Mau/Yuta/Rimuru saved anyone;
- whether the household caused / attracted the danger.

Post-Sukuna world texture can include:
- one merchant becoming more sympathetic;
- another becoming more afraid;
- rumors that the "Otherworlder inn" survived something terrible;
- exaggerations;
- people assuming the settlement is dangerous;
- isolated Otherworlders becoming more interested in finding them.

The household's internal victory and the world's external uncertainty can coexist.

---

# 11. Mau's body should remember the battle

Recovery cannot be only emotional.

Possible continuing effects:
- fatigue;
- pain;
- weakness;
- scar / healing tissue;
- CE/mana instability;
- Maomao/Yuta monitoring;
- Hollow Purple one-use aftermath;
- new tiny RCT behavior beginning only gradually if that direction remains.

Do not use recovery to hand Mau a sudden power upgrade.

If RCT begins emerging:
- tiny/passive;
- confusing;
- costly later;
- not resurrection proof.

The more useful story point is:
> Mau survived, but his body carries evidence that he did not walk away clean.

---

# 12. There should be a period of dream silence

This is a valuable separation between arcs.

After the goddess challenge ends:
- the predictive Sukuna dreams stop.

At first Mau may be relieved.

Then perhaps unsettled by how normal sleep feels.

This helps prevent Ori from reading like:
> prophecy arc 2 immediately starts.

Give Mau enough nights where:
- he sleeps;
- nothing prophetic happens;
- he wakes normally.

This does **not** require a long gap. Several normal nights / enough lived time to establish the old predictive system is over can be sufficient.

That silence is meaningful.

It tells the audience:
> the Sukuna dream system is over.

---

# 13. Mau should become more transparent when strange things begin again

This is one of the strongest possible behavioral payoffs to the entire Sukuna aftermath.

When Mau eventually begins dreaming about an unknown girl / Ori:

**he should not repeat the Sukuna mistake.**

He tells people.

Maybe not the entire inn at breakfast.

But he should tell:
- Frieren;
- Yuta / Rimuru if relevant;
- possibly Fern / others naturally present.

This immediately demonstrates:
> Mau learned something.

He does not know:
- who the girl is;
- whether she is real;
- whether the dream is predictive;
- whether it is another challenge.

But he refuses to carry it alone.

This can create a small emotional beat where Frieren notices the difference:
> this time, he told me.

No speech required.

---

# 14. The first Ori dreams should feel different from the Sukuna dreams

Do not simply reuse:
> exact future image → later event occurs.

Ori dreams can feel:
- persistent;
- spatial;
- intimate but unfamiliar;
- like glimpses of a person rather than a future event;
- emotionally specific without being fully interpretable.

Possible ingredients:
- unknown girl in an unfamiliar place;
- repeated environmental marker;
- same clothing/object;
- loneliness;
- a route / sound / landmark;
- Mau feeling that she is **somewhere**, not that something will happen.

This distinction supports the eventual conclusion:

> she may be a real person who can be found.

Exact imagery remains later-layer work.

---

# 15. Frieren's response to the Ori dreams matters

Because of the Sukuna secrecy wound, Frieren's first response should not be:
> absolutely not, ignore it.

Nor should she instantly trust the dream.

A more mature response:
- ask what Mau saw;
- help separate observation from assumption;
- insist he not go alone;
- participate in deciding what to do.

This is a direct payoff to:
> no more unilateral decisions.

Their relationship is already changing before Ori physically appears.

---

# 16. The Ori mystery is shared before the final locating dream

Mau should still become Ori's first anchor / actively find her.

The household knows about the recurring unknown girl **before** Mau knows exactly where she is.

Early phase:

```text
Ori dreams begin
→ Mau tells Frieren / trusted household
→ details are recorded / discussed
→ nobody yet has enough location certainty to launch a search
```

Then, while Mau remains at the inn under recovery restrictions and scouts are away:

```text
Mau naps
→ hyperreal Ori dream
→ location suddenly feels certain
→ Mau moves immediately
→ Frieren follows
→ "I know where she is" / "Let's go"
```

This is intentionally imperfect growth:
- Mau still reacts impulsively;
- but he is not hiding the phenomenon;
- Frieren receives the truth immediately and chooses to go;
- the household already understands why he moved.

Frieren can quickly leave word / tell someone nearby without turning the moment into a committee meeting.

The emotional rule is:
> Mau can still act fast without carrying the entire truth alone.

---

# 17. Social-mesh guardrail for the post-Sukuna block

Do not make every scene:
- Mau/Frieren;
- Mau/sister;
- Mau/Rimuru.

The household remains a network.

Every ordinary-life story should ask:
- who else naturally lives in this room?
- which relationships cross in the background?
- who has their own problem unrelated to Mau?
- who has moved forward while Mau was recovering?

The family should feel **bigger after surviving Sukuna**, not more centralized around Mau.

---

# 18. Suggested lived-space braid — NOT EPISODE ORDER

A current useful flow:

```text
relief meal
→ intervention / responsibility conversations begin
→ Mau physically overestimates his recovery
→ family clinginess / accidental escort comedy
→ Mau changes ordinary communication habits
→ common-room / movie / chore life returns
→ Mau/Frieren couple-like intimacy becomes normal
→ Yuta/Maki, Bocchi/Kita, Momo/Okarun, Witch Hat family, Stark/Fern/Himmel/etc. continue living
→ scouting/future-home work resumes without Mau first
→ Mau joins a low-risk outing later
→ external Otherworlder rumors remain mixed
→ Rimuru/Mau and Yuta/Mau trust repair continues
→ physical recovery visibly progresses
→ several normal dreamless nights establish that Sukuna predictive dreams are over
→ early Ori dreams begin relatively soon
→ Mau tells Frieren / trusted family rather than hiding them
→ scouting/future-home team leaves while Mau remains restricted at the inn
→ Mau naps / receives qualitatively hyperreal locating dream
→ Mau moves; Frieren follows
→ scouts can discover strong future-home candidate in parallel
→ Mau/Frieren find Ori
→ next block: Ori discovery / integration
```

Exact duration remains open.

The important pacing requirement:
> **there must be enough ordinary post-revival life that Ori enters a functioning relationship/family, not a hospital room.**

---

# 19. Handoff condition into Ori discovery

Do not begin the actual Ori discovery until:

- Mau is medically stable enough to participate in normal life;
- the family intervention has happened;
- anger remains but trust repair has begun;
- Mau has demonstrated at least one changed communication behavior;
- Mau/Frieren have had real ordinary couple-like life;
- the social mesh has resumed;
- scouting/future-home work is active again;
- the pre-Sukuna predictive dreams have been silent long enough to establish separation;
- Mau has told others about the early Ori dreams instead of hiding them;
- Mau is stable enough to leave with Frieren even if the family still considers him under partial recovery restrictions.

Then Ori can arrive into the story as:
> a new person entering a family that has already learned something from almost losing one of its own.
