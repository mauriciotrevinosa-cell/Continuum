# 5. Himmel as a "good omen" — PASS / SUBJECTIVE ONLY

Himmel joining unexpectedly is valuable because he is an unplanned helpful variable.

Mau may emotionally interpret this as:
> maybe the future is not completely closed.

Do **not** make it objective proof that the dreams are wrong.

The predictive challenge can omit secondary details and still remain accurate.

Therefore Himmel's role is:
- hope;
- evidence that other people make choices Mau cannot control;
- emotional support for Yuta/Rimuru's third-option insistence.

Not:
> scientific falsification of the dreams.

This distinction should remain clear in scripting.
