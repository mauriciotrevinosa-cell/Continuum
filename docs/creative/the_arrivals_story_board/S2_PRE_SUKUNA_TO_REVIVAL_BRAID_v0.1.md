# The Arrivals — S2 Pre-Sukuna to Revival Braid v0.1

**Status:** STRONG STORY PROPOSAL / CREATOR REVIEW — NOT EPISODE LOCK  
**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Scope:** early/settled G4 life → Himmel reaches the inn → predictive-dream escalation → fatal binary → secret preparation → Sukuna battle → Mau death / Second Chance → awakening / first communal return-to-life beat.

This document does **not** reopen the already-resolved Sukuna macro mechanics.

Battle mechanics remain governed by:
- `THE_ARRIVALS_S2_SUKUNA_MECHANICS_v0.1.md`
- `S2_COMPLETE_CONTINUITY_INVENTORY_v0.1.md`

The purpose here is to answer:
> what is the family actually living through while the Sukuna arc grows underneath their ordinary life?

---

# 1. Core dramatic shape

The strongest version of this stretch is not:

```text
romance arc
→ dream arc
→ training arc
→ battle
```

It is:

```text
the household becomes happier / denser / more interconnected
while Mau privately receives increasingly credible evidence
that this life may be about to break.
```

Every major Sukuna beat should therefore have ordinary life beside it.

The more complete the family becomes,
the more terrible Mau's eventual false binary feels.

---

# 2. Opening state — G4 is beginning to feel real

Current favored early sequence remains:

```text
Maki arrives first
→ Yuta/Maki receive private reunion time
→ Stark arrives second, potentially later the same day
→ Frieren/Fern/Stark receive their own reunion
→ hamburg steak dinner
→ following day broader household introductions begin
→ Maki starts acquiring mundane routines
→ Maki finds Hakari/Karane/Shizuka/Nano during one such routine
→ Agott continues approaching/searching

```

Important:
- Maki is not yet Frieren's confidante;
- Stark is not yet fully integrated;
- the four girls are not immediately household experts;
- Agott does not immediately trust everyone;
- Himmel has not yet reached the inn.

The house is expanding again,
but unlike G3 it now already knows how to absorb arrivals.

# 3. Ordinary G4 life must grow before the danger owns the season

Several developments should happen **in parallel**.

## 3.1 Yuta / Maki receive extra time rather than restarting from zero

They already lived a complete relationship.

Their Continuum problem is not:
> do we still like each other?

It is:
> what do we do with time we never expected to receive?

Useful lived beats:
- waking up together and having no crisis to solve;
- doing chores;
- Maki seeing how Yuta fits into the household;
- Yuta showing her places that became important to him;
- one of them automatically doing something based on decades-old habit;
- someone younger reacting with disbelief when they casually reference grandchildren/old age.

Do not make every Yuta/Maki scene philosophical.

The philosophy is stronger when it leaks out of ordinary life.

---

## 3.2 Maki becomes part of the social mesh

Before Frieren meaningfully opens up to her, Maki should:
- work with Mikasa/Yuta;
- talk casually with Fern;
- interact with Momo/Anko/Maomao;
- encounter Bocchi/Kita;
- see Mau/Frieren in normal life;

This lets Frieren trust **Maki**, not "Yuta's wife."

## 3.3 Stark becomes old-family + new-family at once

After Frieren/Fern/Stark reunion:

- he also has to learn the new household.

Useful friction/comedy:
- Stark realizes Frieren/Fern have routines he was not part of;
- he meets people who already know stories about him;
- Frieren/Fern are strangely proud of Mau;
- Mau/Stark remain slightly awkward at first;
- Stark gradually develops his own connections instead of existing only in the restored old party.

Stark should gain ordinary life **before** Himmel arrives.
