# The Arrivals — S2 Provisional Episode Roadmap v0.1

**Status:** CREATOR-REVIEW ROADMAP — COMPLETE FIRST EPISODEIZATION PASS, NOT LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Branch:** `m3/critical-path`  
**Source handoff:** `S2_EPISODEIZATION_MASTER_INPUT_v0.1.md`

## Purpose

This is the first complete Season 2 episodeization pass.

It deliberately follows the S1 workflow:
- build the whole provisional season;
- allow episode-sized ordinary-life material to emerge where the story needs it;
- let threads cross episode boundaries;
- then let the creator review the season as a whole;
- refine, merge, split, move or delete afterward.

This roadmap is **not** a target-count exercise.

The current first pass naturally lands at:

> **43 episode-sized story units**

That number is an output, not a quota.

Some units may later merge.
Some may split.
Some may expand substantially.
Some may disappear if a neighboring episode already accomplishes their purpose better.

Current hard/near-hard episode-scale anchors:
- the household festival is a full episode-sized unit;
- the Sukuna confrontation is one unusually large full episode-sized unit;
- Mau/Frieren formalization is the final major payoff;
- exactly one quiet final day follows it.

The core review question for every stretch remains:

> **What should the audience feel they have actually lived with these people before the next major thing happens?**

# MOVEMENT I — S1 CONTINUES, BUT THE WORLD HAS CHANGED
## Approx. Month 4 → Month 5

### S2E01 — The Threat Is Still Here

**Time:** roughly one day.

Season 2 opens as direct continuation rather than reset.

Yuta feels Sukuna and reacts with genuine terror.
The presence vanishes almost immediately when Sukuna suppresses himself.

Yuta warns the established household/core.

They know:
- Sukuna exists;
- Yuta is genuinely afraid;
- they do not know where Sukuna is;
- they do not have an actionable target.

So the strangest thing happens:

> life continues.

Breakfast, chores, work, guild/economic obligations and household rhythms continue under a threat nobody can locate.

G3 road/caravan life appears in parallel:
- travel fatigue;
- mediocre food;
- Kita/Marin social energy;
- Umaru suffering the lack of modern comfort;
- Wakana being quietly useful;
- Qifrey/Coco observing;
- Mikasa reading terrain;
- Okarun near-memory oddness.

The episode should make the audience feel:

> We are back in S1's home — but something awful is somewhere in the same world.

**Ending:** Yuta checks again and still cannot find Sukuna.

No cliffhanger attack.
The danger is that nothing happens.
