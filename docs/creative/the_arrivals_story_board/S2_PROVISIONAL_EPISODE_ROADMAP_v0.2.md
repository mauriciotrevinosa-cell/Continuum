## Purpose

This is the revised full-season episode pass after creator review.

It intentionally stays compact.

Each episode entry answers:
- what the episode mainly carries;
- what important threads move inside it;
- where it naturally hands off.

Detailed scene/choreography material is preserved in the master input and creator-review packet rather than duplicated here.

Current first-pass total:

> **38 provisional episode-sized units**

This is not a target or lock.

Some may still merge, split, expand or disappear.

Core methodological rule:

> **Breather does not mean time skip.**

Breathing comes from focus, lived interaction, ordinary life and consequence — not from forcing weeks/months to pass.

# MOVEMENT I — DIRECT S1 CONTINUATION / FEAR BECOMES PERSONAL

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E01** | **The Threat Is Still Here** | Direct S1 continuation. Yuta briefly senses a terrifyingly familiar presence, then it vanishes completely. He does **not** warn the household because he has no confirmation/location and barely trusts the instant himself. Home life continues. G3 remains on the road. This sensation becomes meaningful much later when Mau describes the Sukuna dreams. |
| **E02** | **Rumors Travel** | Broken reports of the destroyed city spread through survivors, merchants and guild chatter. No instant social flip: some people fear Otherworlders, some doubt the stories, some remain sympathetic. G3 hears distorted versions from the road. |
| **E03** | **People Start Looking Differently** | Fear produces real but uneven social consequences: awkward merchants, staring, shorter conversations, sympathetic pushback. The household begins discussing exposure, crowding and whether their presence may endanger locals. The relocation/disclosure problem **starts here**, before Bocchi. |
| **E04** | **Rainy Days** | Deliberate quiet episode. Rain keeps much of the household together; meals, repairs, music, conversation, boredom and small comedy dominate. False calm returns. A normal supply need emerges. Mau plans to go; Bocchi independently volunteers to accompany him. |
| **E05** | **Bocchi** | Starts as an ordinary work/supply day. Town tension escalates into Bocchi's bloody but non-life-threatening forehead injury. Mau gets her home; Maomao treats her; Fern stays close that night. No instant strategy council. The emotional shock is allowed to land. |
| **E06** | **What We Owe Each Other** | The concerns already growing since E03 finally crystallize after Bocchi: stay together, reduce exposure, fortify modestly, begin serious future-home search. G1/G2 controlled disclosure deepens because they are choosing to face danger together. Mau/Frieren's first important unexplained Construction event grows naturally from that disclosure. |
| **E07** | **First Measures** | Consequences become practical: basic alarms, safer routes, storage, supply changes, first scouting logic — not super-defenses. Meanwhile G3's merchant hears from other merchants that the city/town situation is tense and deliberately reroutes them away from it. |
| **E08** | **Eight More** | G3 reaches the inn. Arrival immediately becomes triage: food, rooms, baths, questions, beds. Kita finds Bocchi, then sees the bandage. Later Momo recognizes Okarun; he does not remember her. Favored close remains: `Okarun!` / `Who are you?` |

# MOVEMENT II — G3 BECOMES PART OF THE HOUSE

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E09** | **Too Many People, Not Enough House** | The inn proves physically finite. Existing room geography matters: Frieren/Mau's de facto room, Mau's nominal room, Bocchi/Fern, flexible spaces, baths, kitchen, improvised bedding. Rimuru begins expansion planning; Wakana finds immediate practical usefulness. Momo/Okarun and Kita/Bocchi continue without being solved. |
| **E10** | **What Are We Allowed to Tell Them?** | Newcomer questions expose a distinction G1/G2 did not need as sharply: knowing someone's history/power does not mean you may disclose it. Pressure from Umaru, Kita, Momo, Marin, Qifrey/Coco leads to the confidentiality Contract. Okarun tests it and learns enforcement is real. |
| **E11** | **New Combinations** | G3 begins mixing laterally rather than in obvious pairs. Qifrey/Coco/Mau study; Okarun/Mau friendship; Umaru branches outward; Anko/Mikasa-type unexpected conversations; Wakana/Marin household lanes; Bocchi/Kita ordinary reconnection. No one relationship owns the episode. |
| **E12** | **Nothing Day** | Multi-day quiet-life episode. Common-room laziness, cooking/music, Mau talking with Fern/Anko, Frieren reading against Mau, slime Rimuru, Wakana sewing, Mikasa participating instead of guarding. Coco joins Momo/Maomao's fake-detective nonsense. The point is simply: this is how the enlarged family lives. |
| **E13** | **Moving Pack** | Momo + Marin start an outing, drag Maomao out, invite Kita, who pulls Bocchi in; Fern and others join naturally. The outing has a real low-risk objective/adventure. It expands female social networks without making every woman attend or creating a symmetry-mandated "boys episode." |
| **E14** | **Wrong Place, Right People** | Mixed scouting/work group investigates an early future-home candidate and rejects it for practical reasons. Back at the inn, integration shows through dinner/work rather than declaration: everyone contributes, Mau helps across groups, Frieren reads instead of helping, Coco pesters her with questions, Qifrey finds his place. Bocchi/Kita's distributed romantic thread reaches explicit relationship around this stretch after Kita receives indirect help from Mau/others. |

# MOVEMENT III — G4 ARRIVES INTO A HOUSE THAT ALREADY WORKS

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E15** | **Maki** | Maki arrives after only a few days in Continuum, having followed a rumor about someone resembling Yuta. Her path can be explained through flashback rather than a long parallel road arc. Yuta/Maki reunion receives full emotional space; later introductions need not repeat the same exposition. |
| **E16** | **Hamburg Steak** | Stark converges in the same general period, potentially later the same day. Frieren/Fern/Stark reunion gets its own focus. Yuta/Maki and Frieren/Fern/Stark can have separate dinners occurring concurrently/intercut. Mau is genuinely happy someone from Frieren's life has returned; no rivalry framing. |
| **E17** | **Four New Faces** | After Maki/Stark begin routines, Maki encounters Hakari/Karane/Shizuka/Nano during an ordinary inspection/patrol rather than a heroic convergence. She brings them toward the inn. Small mundane déjà-vu/predictive-dream seeds may already appear in background. |
| **E18** | **First Days** | The four newcomers actually adapt: tech loss, rooms, food, culture shock, individual reactions. G3 now helps receive people. Small Mau predictions continue and at least one confirms later, making the phenomenon harder to dismiss. Agott's search continues outside. |
| **E19** | **Agott / Old Names** | Agott reaches Qifrey/Coco and must adjust to Coco's expanded life. Witch Hat relationships breathe. In the same episode, Momo/Okarun's thread can hit a meaningful recognition breakthrough — not necessarily full recovery, but enough that `Momo` begins to feel natural again. |
| **E20** | **Something in the Dream** | Mostly household/daily-life material with G4 increasingly normal. Mau's mundane predictions have accumulated evidence. During this lived episode, the first unmistakably Sukuna-related dream appears. Himmel's rumor trail is now closing in on Frieren. |
| **E21** | **Himmel** | Himmel reaches the inn. First comes genuine reunion: Frieren, Fern, Stark, disbelief, happiness. Mau welcomes someone deeply important from Frieren's old life. Himmel begins discovering the life Frieren built in Continuum rather than arriving as a romantic rival. |
| **E22** | **Ghosts and Choices** | Over several ordinary days, Himmel/Frieren confront what he meant to her; Frieren/Maki conversations deepen; Mau/Himmel become actual people to one another; G4 daily life continues. Mau's Sukuna dreams become more detailed in background. Frieren begins confronting a distinction the season will eventually make explicit: Himmel taught her love; Mau is her first romantic love. |

# MOVEMENT IV — THE FALSE FUTURE CLOSES IN

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E23** | **The Choice** | The Sukuna dreams progress from battle fragments to repeating fatal branches. Because earlier mundane dreams came true, Mau believes the binary. He spends roughly a day or two emotionally accepting what he thinks is coming and reaches a fixed conclusion: Frieren does not die, regardless of the cost to him. Episode ends with Mau going to Yuta/Rimuru because he knows he cannot do this alone. |
| **E24** | **Third Option** | Mau tells Yuta/Rimuru the fatal problem. They reject his self-sacrifice and search for a third outcome. Rimuru Contract/contingency logic enters. Yuta's Shinjuku memories are reviewed; Mau observes/analyzes Gojo/Sukuna information and discovers that Construction may be able to assemble a one-use Hollow Purple analogue if he can satisfy impossible requirements. Yuta also recognizes Mau's earlier dream description as matching the terrifying presence he sensed in E01. |
| **E25** | **One Week** | Preparation is braided into normal appearances. Mau trains with Yuta; Yuta thinks he is increasing Mau's survival odds while Mau is also satisfying Purple requirements. Yuta pushes RCT; Rimuru develops barriers; Qifrey receives role-specific field work and prepares the battlefield; Okarun trains All Out extraction. The household sees fragments but not the fatal truth. |
| **E26** | **Our Festival** | Full episode anchor, likely very near the confrontation. Mau/Yuta/Rimuru may be among those who push for the household festival knowing Mau may not return. For everyone else it is genuine joy: food, games, music, couples, old/new families mixing. For the trio it quietly functions as a possible farewell. |

# MOVEMENT V — SUKUNA / DEATH / RETURN

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E27** | **Sukuna** | **Unusually large episode.** The team leaves early under a low-risk-mission cover; Maki knows bounded truth; Himmel overhears enough danger to join. Mau waits as bait and manipulates Sukuna's arrogance into the accepted "any attack I choose" Contract wording, allowing the Rika ambush. Coordinated battle → Mahoraga enters → wheel/adaptation horror → battlefield split → Frieren/Maki emotional intercut → Frieren recognizes Mau as her **first romantic love**, trusts sensing and enters → Zoltraak/Mahoraga false opening → predicted geometry closes → Qifrey trap buys only enough time → Mau intercepts → Rimuru is Contract-blocked from saving him → Sukuna impales Mau → `merging began` buys farewell window → `Everything will be okay` / `Take care of her for me` → extraction → unstable Blue/Red → Mahoraga annihilated → Sukuna sacrifices future World Cutting Slash to survive Purple → Mau dies → Yuta abandons pursuit for RCT. Rimuru's coat becomes the covering over Mau. The episode continues to the inn: Qifrey, Okarun/Himmel, shattered Frieren/Rimuru, then Yuta carrying Mau. Someone asks where Mau is; his hand slips from under the coat. **End.** Full choreography lives in the creator-review packet. |
| **E28** | **The Aftermath** | Full grief/recovery episode. The entire household is damaged in different ways. Frieren and Yuta barely leave Mau; Fern/Maki force them to eat/rest; Umaru, Coco, Bocchi, Anko, Rimuru, Himmel, Stark and the rest process loss differently. Questions emerge about whether Mau knew how loved he was or considered himself expendable. Someone eventually suggests they may have to accept his death; Frieren refuses because he said everything would be okay. Fern finally gets Frieren to sleep beside Mau, then breaks herself. Yuta/Rika eventually collapse from repeated effort. |
| **E29** | **I Told You Everything Would Be Okay** | Near awakening, flash back to the post-Purple divine moment: `Accept fate completed`; goddess asks why he did not let Frieren die; Mau answers that he cannot imagine a world without her. Second Chance reanchors/stabilizes enough for Yuta's RCT to matter. Mau's system acquires the **RCT pattern/pathway, not mastery**. Return to morning: Mau wakes with Frieren asleep on him, strokes her head; she realizes he is awake, kisses him and says `I love you`; Mau embraces her: `I told you everything would be okay.` Yuta later finds them talking almost normally and collapses in relief; household reactions arrive in waves. |
| **E30** | **You Were One of Us Too** | First, joy: communal dinner; Mau/Frieren hold hands; Yuta/Rimuru notice and laugh. That night Frieren privately confronts Mau and establishes the no-major-secrets/unilateral-sacrifice promise. Then comes wider family accountability: Mau/Yuta/Rimuru are confronted; Rimuru tries to take Contract blame and Mau stops him; Mau's "everyone else had someone" arithmetic is rejected. Temporary clinginess begins — Umaru leg-cling, Coco questions, Frieren/Fern overprotectiveness, Anko anger — showing the cost of what happened. |

# MOVEMENT VI — THE HOUSE HEALS / ORI BECOMES REAL

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E31** | **Life After** | Intensity deliberately drops. Dates, double dates, movie nights, meals, small outings, recovery and ordinary work show the family healing. Mau/Frieren already behave as a couple without formal label. Clinginess slowly relaxes. Ori dreams begin inside this quieter stretch; unlike Sukuna, Mau tells Frieren/the household immediately. |
| **E32** | **The Girl in the Forest** | Mau is still under affectionate house arrest. A scouting group plans future-home reconnaissance; Mau asks to go and everyone says `No` at once. Later Mau naps on the sofa with Frieren beside him and receives the hyperreal Ori dream: location certainty. He tells Frieren; she goes with him. In parallel, the scout team finds the strongest future-home candidate yet. |
| **E33** | **Ori** | Mau/Frieren bring Ori home while the scout team brings back site notes/maps. The site thread progresses but stays background. Ori's first days focus on personhood, safety and practical adaptation. Mau is first anchor; Frieren is also one of the first people who chose to come find her. Romance is not yet the foreground. |
| **E34** | **Somewhere to Belong** | Ori begins functioning when Mau is not in the room and builds relationships with Frieren and the wider house. Site verification quietly continues. The episode ends in the late-night girlies conversation: Mau is dragged in, explains Frieren's kiss/`I love you`, admits he never said it back, then genuinely asks `What's a girlfriend?` Kita uses her own Bocchi formalization as the explanation. Ori hears formal ambiguity. |
| **E35** | **Trying to Understand Love** | Ori explores romantic possibility rather than confessing instantly. Mau/Ori closeness increases; Frieren becomes jealous and also fixates on the fact that Mau never verbally told her `I love you`. She can misread the gap as evidence that she loves Mau more than he loves her. Mau is genuinely clueless about why Frieren is upset. As Ori pushes, Mau begins understanding that he loves Ori and Frieren differently. |
| **E36** | **You Win** | Ori eventually confesses. Mau compassionately explains that he loves her deeply but not romantically — and can now say clearly that he loves Frieren romantically. Ori is genuinely hurt. During the early aftermath she passes/approaches Frieren and says `You win.` Frieren initially does not understand; when Mau enters/appears, she realizes what Ori meant. Frieren does not see a contest and begins helping Ori understand that romantic rejection did not erase belonging. Site choice can quietly mature in background. |
| **E37** | **Will You Be My Girlfriend?** | By now Ori's chosen-family direction has begun stabilizing and the site is effectively chosen. Mau asks the household for help. Rimuru gets Frieren out with grimoire money; family meeting explodes into detective-sibling operation, Wakana outfits, Fern management, advice panel, Marin/Momo decorations, Yuta/Maki grounding, Himmel/Stark/Qifrey practical help. Mau asks Frieren where his remembered life began: `I love you. I want to be with you. Will you be my girlfriend?` Frieren explicitly says yes. Major celebration dinner. Final major S2 payoff. |
| **E38** | **One More Day** | Exactly one quiet final day at the inn. Leftovers, cleanup, games, reading, sewing, study, light chores. Mau/Frieren are officially together and almost comically unchanged. Ori is still a little awkward but fully included. The unfinished future-home map sits on the table. Someone casually leaves a mug on top of it when called away to join the family/watch something together. The plan remains behind while they go to the people who already make the inn home. **END S2.** |

# FLEXIBLE STORY RESERVOIR

Nothing here is owed a full episode.

Use only where episodeization later discovers that the story needs more life, comedy, relationship texture or calendar/world continuity.

Material can be:
- a full S2 episode;
- half an episode;
- B/C lane;
- montage fragments;
- moved to S3 if unused.

|---|---|

| **Craft / clothing / bedding stories** | Wakana/Marin/Qifrey/Coco/Agott can support practical household episodes. |
| **Arrival-search / isolated anomaly outing** | Can deepen the emerging reconnaissance ethic and carry naturally into S3. |

Reservoir rule:

> If unused in S2, the idea is not "wasted."  
> Preserve it for S3 or later if the new settlement creates a better home for it.

# CURRENT REVIEW QUESTIONS FOR v0.2

The next creator overview should primarily test:

1. Does E01–E08 now feel like a natural escalation rather than a prewritten opening checklist?
2. Does G3 have enough life in E09–E14 without baseball being artificially forced?
3. Does Bocchi/Kita's explicit relationship have enough visible runway before the E34 callback?
4. Do Maki/Stark/four-girls/Agott feel like overlapping life rather than an arrival queue?
5. Is E20–E24 enough runway for dreams/Himmel/Frieren without artificial calendar padding?
6. Does Mau's E23 acceptance → E24 Third Option → E25 prep → E26 festival create the right emotional tightening before Sukuna?
7. Does E27 preserve every critical beat of the new battle without becoming structurally unreadable?
8. Does giving E28 entirely to grief make E29's awakening hit harder?
9. Does E31 provide enough genuine post-Sukuna ordinary life before Ori?
10. Does Ori have enough life as a person before romance becomes foreground?
11. Does Frieren's insecurity over Mau never saying `I love you` strengthen rather than cheapen the Ori tension?
12. Does E36 give Ori enough hurt/chosen-family motion before E37?
13. Does the final mug-on-map image feel earned by the site thread rather than decorative?
14. Which remaining episode still feels like a planning document rather than something we would be excited to write?

No episode should be protected merely because it exists in this roadmap.
