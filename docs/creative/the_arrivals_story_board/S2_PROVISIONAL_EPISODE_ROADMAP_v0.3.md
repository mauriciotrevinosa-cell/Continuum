## Purpose

v0.3 applies the creator's second structural review.

Main rule:

> **Merging episodes does not mean compressing story.**

A merged episode can simply be longer and carry one continuous emotional/story movement without artificial cuts.

This pass does **not** perform the dedicated slice-of-life/reservoir insertion pass yet.

Current provisional total:

> **32 episode-sized units**

This number is an output, not a target.

Several units are explicitly allowed to split later if scripting proves they are too dense.

# MOVEMENT I — DIRECT S1 CONTINUATION / FEAR BECOMES PERSONAL

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E01** | **The Threat Is Still Here** | Direct S1 continuation. Yuta briefly senses a terrifyingly familiar presence and then loses it completely. He says nothing because he has no confirmation/location and barely trusts the instant himself. G3 remains on the road. The sensation becomes meaningful only much later. |
| **E02** | **Rumors Travel** | Fragmented reports of the destroyed city spread gradually. Some people fear Otherworlders, some doubt the rumors, some remain sympathetic. G3 hears distorted versions from the road. |
| **E03** | **People Start Looking Differently** | Uneven social consequences appear: awkward merchants, staring, shorter conversations, sympathetic pushback. The household begins discussing exposure, crowding, safety, disclosure and whether they may eventually need another home. |
| **E04** | **Rainy Days** | Deliberate quiet/rainy-day episode. The family is largely stuck together: food, music, repairs, conversation, boredom, comedy. False calm returns. A normal supply need emerges; Mau plans to go and Bocchi independently chooses to accompany him. |
| **E05** | **Bocchi / What We Owe Each Other** | **Longer merged episode.** Starts completely ordinary: household routine → supply list → Mau/Bocchi trip → shopping → social confrontation → Bocchi's bloody but non-life-threatening forehead injury → Mau gets/carries her home → Maomao treatment → Fern stays close that night. After the emotional night breathes, the concerns building since E03 crystallize: stay together, reduce exposure, fortify modestly, begin real future-home search, deepen G1/G2 disclosure. Mau/Frieren's first important unexplained Construction event follows naturally from that disclosure. |
| **E06** | **First Measures** | G1/G2 adapt behavior to the new reality: fewer casual town trips, possible incognito/indirect errands, basic alarms, storage, safer routes, scouting and supply alternatives. The merchant carrying G3 hears from other merchants that the town is tense and reroutes them. **G3 may physically arrive at the very end.** |

# MOVEMENT II — G3 ARRIVES / THE HOUSE STRETCHES

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E07** | **Eight More** | First real G3 steps after arrival: Bocchi/Kita reunion and concern, Mau/Umaru, Momo/Okarun shock, Qifrey/Coco, Mikasa, Marin/Wakana, practical food/bath/bed questions. The arrival is social and messy rather than an introduction queue. |
| **E08** | **Too Many People, Not Enough House** | Starts the next day. The inn is physically overloaded: people collide in common spaces, temporary sleeping clusters form, some rooms hold far too many people, the bath/kitchen become bottlenecks. The surrounding abandoned houses have effectively been stripped/reclaimed until the inn is nearly the only major usable structure left. Privacy/disclosure questions begin naturally inside the chaos. |
| **E09** | **New Combinations** | Slice-heavy integration episode. Unexpected pairings and cross-group conversations matter as much as obvious source ties: Anko/Mikasa-type interactions, Okarun/Mau, Umaru branching outward, Wakana/Marin, Qifrey/Coco/Mau. The privacy problem from E08 becomes real enough that G1/G2 build the confidentiality Contract as one lane of the episode rather than a mechanics lecture; Okarun eventually tests enforcement. |
| **E10** | **Nothing Day** | Multi-day quiet-household episode. Common-room laziness, cooking/music, Mau/Fern/Anko, Frieren reading against Mau, slime Rimuru, Wakana sewing, Mikasa participating rather than guarding. Coco joins Momo/Maomao's fake-detective nonsense. Nothing needs to "advance." |
| **E11** | **Moving Pack** | First major girls outing. Momo+Marin start it, drag Maomao out, ask Kita, Kita pulls Bocchi in, Fern joins, and because this is the first one even Frieren/most current women may plausibly go. The group snowballs naturally and gets a real low-risk objective/adventure. |
| **E12** | **Wrong Place, Right People** | Mixed scouting/work examines an early future-home candidate and rejects it for practical reasons. Back home, G3 integration is shown indirectly through dinner/work: everyone contributes, Mau moves between people helping, Frieren reads instead of helping, Coco asks her questions, Qifrey finds where he fits. Bocchi/Kita's distributed romance thread reaches explicit relationship around this stretch after Kita receives indirect help from Mau/others. |

In parallel, Mau's Sukuna dreams progress from fragments to the fatal binary. # MOVEMENT V — THIRD OPTION / PREPARATION / LAST GOOD NIGHT

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E19** | **Third Option** | Full planning/exposition episode by design. Mau explains what he has seen, when he thinks it happens and the false binary. Yuta/Rimuru reject the sacrifice. Contract/contingency discussion, Shinjuku memory review, Plan C search and Construction analysis reveal a one-use Hollow Purple pathway. They decide exactly who to involve: Qifrey and Okarun, while keeping the wider house out. Someone may see the three talking from afar and assume it is ordinary serious conversation. Yuta pushes RCT, Rimuru develops barriers, Qifrey prepares field geometry/traps, Okarun trains All Out. The household continues meals, work and relationships as if nothing is fundamentally wrong. |
| **E21** | **Our Festival** | Full episode anchor, likely near the confrontation. Mau/Yuta/Rimuru may be among those who push for the celebration knowing Mau may not return. For everyone else it is real joy: food, games, music, couples, old/new family mixing. Near the end Frieren tells Mau they should do this again next year. Mau simply says yes. |

# MOVEMENT VI — SUKUNA / DEATH / GRIEF / RETURN

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E22** | **Sukuna** | **Unusually large episode.** Team leaves before most of the house wakes; everyone later assumes supplies/work. Maki knows bounded truth; Himmel overhears enough to join last-minute. Mau waits as bait → accepted "any attack I choose" Contract wording → Rika ambush → coordinated team fight → Mahoraga enters → adaptation reveal/telepathic panic → split battlefield → Frieren/Maki intercut → Frieren consciously recognizes **Mau as her first romantic love**, trusts sensing and reaches battle → Zoltraak/Mahoraga false opening → Qifrey trap → predicted geometry → Rimuru Contract prevents rescue → Mau impaled → `merging began` buys farewell window → `Everything will be okay` / `Take care of her for me` → extraction → unstable Blue/Red → Mahoraga annihilated → Purple detonates → Sukuna sacrifices future World Cutting Slash to survive → Mau dies → Yuta chooses Mau over pursuit and begins RCT. Rimuru's coat becomes the covering over Mau. Episode continues to the inn: returning fighters appear one by one; shattered Frieren arrives; Yuta carries Mau; someone asks where Mau is; his hand slips from under the coat. **End.** Full choreography remains in the detailed refinement packet. |
| **E23** | **The Aftermath** | Full grief episode. The entire household processes apparent death differently. Frieren/Yuta barely leave Mau; Fern/Maki force them to function; Umaru, Coco, Bocchi, Anko, Rimuru, Himmel, Stark and others break differently. At least one grieving resident lashes out at Rimuru — he was supposed to protect Mau — worsening Rimuru's existing guilt over the Contract. Questions emerge about whether Mau knew how loved he was or considered himself expendable. Someone suggests they may have to accept death; Frieren refuses because Mau said everything would be okay. Fern finally gets Frieren to sleep beside him, then breaks herself. Yuta/Rika eventually collapse from repeated effort. |
| **E24** | **I Told You Everything Would Be Okay** | Flash back near awakening to the divine moment: `Accept fate completed`; goddess asks why Mau did not let Frieren die; Second Chance reanchors/stabilizes his soul/body enough for Yuta's RCT to work. Mau's system acquires the **RCT pattern/pathway, not mastery**. Morning: Mau wakes with Frieren asleep on him and strokes her head. Frieren realizes he is awake → embrace → kiss → `I love you` → Mau: `I told you everything would be okay.` Yuta later finds them talking almost normally and breaks in relief; household reactions arrive in waves. Episode continues through the **communal celebration dinner**. Final scene: Frieren and Mau alone afterward, and now that he is alive she is furious. Their private no-major-secrets/unilateral-sacrifice promise closes the episode. |
| **E25** | **You Were One of Us Too** | Next day begins with the family intervention: Mau/Yuta/Rimuru accountability, distributed responsibility, Rimuru tries to take Contract blame and Mau stops him, the household rejects Mau's belief that everyone could recover from losing him. Immediate aftermath is overprotective/comedic: Mau can barely go anywhere alone. The same episode then begins showing healing — small outings, dates, movie nights, meals, people gradually loosening their grip. Early Ori dreams begin inside this normalization and Mau tells the household/Frieren immediately, demonstrating the change. |

# MOVEMENT VII — ORI / FUTURE HOME / LOVE CATEGORIES

| Ep | Provisional title | Core episode shape |
|---|---|---|
| **E26** | **The Girl in the Forest** | A future-home scouting group prepares to leave. Mau asks to go; everyone says `No` at once because house arrest is about fear of losing him, not mistrust. Later Mau naps on the sofa with Frieren beside him and receives the hyperreal Ori dream: location certainty. He tells Frieren; she goes with him. In parallel, scouts find the strongest future-home candidate yet. Mau/Frieren find Ori and return. Family reaction: `Why did you go alone? Who is she?` Frieren arrives behind him and makes clear he did not go alone / she knew. |
| **E27** | **Somewhere to Belong** | Ori's actual first-days/integration episode. Mau is first anchor, Frieren is also one of the first people who came for her, but Ori begins functioning when Mau is not in the room and builds relationships across the house. Site report/maps arrive and verification continues in background. Late-night girlies conversation closes the episode: Mau explains Frieren's kiss/`I love you`, admits he never said it back, asks `What's a girlfriend?`; Kita uses her own Bocchi formalization as the explanation. Ori hears formal ambiguity. |
| **E28** | **Trying to Understand Love** | Ori explores romantic possibility instead of confessing immediately. Mau/Ori closeness increases. Frieren becomes jealous and also fixates on the fact that Mau never verbally told her `I love you`, misreading the gap as possible evidence that she loves him more than he loves her. Mau is genuinely clueless about why she is upset. As Ori pushes, Mau starts recognizing that he loves Ori and Frieren differently. |
| **E29** | **You Win** | Ori eventually confesses. Mau compassionately explains that he does love her, but not romantically, and can now say clearly that he loves Frieren romantically. Ori is genuinely hurt. During the early aftermath she tells Frieren `You win.` Frieren initially does not understand; when Mau enters/appears, she realizes what Ori meant. Frieren does not see a contest and begins helping Ori understand that rejection did not erase family/belonging. Future-home choice quietly matures in background. |
| **E30** | **Still Here** | Short but important chosen-family stabilization unit if needed by scripting: Ori remains hurt/awkward across ordinary life, but Mau's care does not vanish, Frieren/Ori bond becomes independent, and the site is effectively chosen. **This is an explicit merge candidate with E29 if E29 already carries enough post-confession lived time.** |
| **E31** | **Will You Be My Girlfriend?** | Full formalization episode. Rimuru/grimoire distraction → family meeting → detective siblings Bocchi/Umaru/Coco → Wakana outfits → Fern timing/management → Hakari/Karane/Shizuka/Nano advice → Marin/Momo decorations → Yuta/Maki grounding → Himmel/Stark/Qifrey practical help. Mau asks Frieren where his remembered life began: `I love you. I want to be with you. Will you be my girlfriend?` Frieren explicitly says yes. Major celebration dinner. |
| **E32** | **One More Day** | Exactly one quiet final day at the inn. Leftovers, cleanup, games, reading, sewing, study, light chores. Mau/Frieren are officially together and almost comically unchanged. Ori is still mildly awkward but fully included. The unfinished future-home map sits on the table. Someone leaves a mug on top of it when called away to join the family/watch something together. The future house stays on paper while the person goes to the people who are already home. **END S2.** |
