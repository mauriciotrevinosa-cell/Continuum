# 1. Core methodology correction from creator review

The provisional v0.1 roadmap still overused elapsed time as a structural tool in a few places.

Current creator correction:

> **Breather does not mean time skip.**

Breathers exist to:
- change focus;
- let emotional consequences land;
- show ordinary household life;
- expand interactions;
- let arrivals become people;
- show relationships cross-pollinating;
- give the reader time with the family.

Do **not** manufacture weeks/months merely to prove that relationships are deep.

A small amount of in-world time can carry major emotional weight if enough lived interaction is actually shown.

Reference principle:
- a story can create profound bonds over months or less;
- emotional credibility comes from what the audience lives with the characters, not from a calendar quota.

The provisional Month ~8–10 G4 runway therefore remains a planning artifact, not a target that episodeization must force.
