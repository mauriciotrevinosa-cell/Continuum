# 11. E20–E23 — major restructuring direction

Creator feels v0.2 still pushes the false binary too mechanically and fragments one continuous movement into too many episode containers.

Key timing preference:
- Himmel arrives;
- roughly ~10 days later Sukuna confrontation can occur;
- no need for long artificial runway.

Possible lived progression:
1. Himmel arrives.
2. ~3 days of reunion/ordinary life.
3. During this time Frieren begins confronting what Himmel meant and what she feels now.
4. Mau's dream progression continues in parallel.
5. A clearly worse/fatal Sukuna dream appears after some lived days.
6. Mau spends ~2 days processing/accepting the false binary.
7. Then ~7 days of preparation follow.

These numbers are working rhythm, not locked math.

## Core structural principle

> **Combine connected story movements into longer episodes instead of splitting them merely because each beat has a label.**

Potential v0.3 shape:
- Himmel arrival/reunion + early daily life + dream escalation can overlap;
- Frieren/Himmel/Maki emotional thread and Mau dream thread should run simultaneously;
- "The Choice" should culminate Mau's acceptance and can end with him going to Yuta/Rimuru;
- next episode opens directly on the Mau/Yuta/Rimuru conversation.
