# The Arrivals — S3 Containment Event and Ciel — Canon Lock

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** CREATOR-DIRECTED CANON. Implemented in V13 #15–#24 (LN v0.2).
**Authority:** creator revision of the Memory Arc containment sequence. Where an older S3 planning document says Raphael merely observes during containment with no cost, this lock supersedes it.

## 1. What happens (V13, Days 17–20)

1. Containment begins as a genuine false positive: fewer variables, everything quieter, about twenty minutes of calm observation.
2. Raphael is given permission to observe more deeply. The Noise's four beats (**Observe, Analyze, Understand, Construct**) turn toward Raphael: the observation becomes reciprocal, repeating the S1 inn overload pattern. This time Raphael cannot withdraw.
3. The two systems couple. The Construct beat starts building:
   - a Hollow Purple attempt (Blue, then Red) that Mau holds apart by refusal alone for about eleven minutes;
   - copies of Rimuru's skills and of the skills Rimuru observed in his own world, read out of Rimuru from inside, without malice;
   - True Dragon-scale pressure (Rimuru recognises it from Veldora);
   - Mahoraga's adaptation wheel;
   - Malevolent Shrine (from the S2 Sukuna tether structure) and Unlimited Void (from Yuta's S2 Shinjuku reconstruction), colliding inside Rimuru;
   - an adaptation that produces Infinity **without the Six Eyes**, which is uncontrolled and keeps everything out.
4. The Noise "excludes" part of Rimuru as foreign: **Rimuru's left arm is unmade.** It regrows slowly from nothing over several days (to the elbow by Day 19, past it by Day 20, full by Day 21–22).
5. Infinity tears the coupling. **Raphael is flung off and undergoes forced evolution.** Rimuru unlocks new skills, among them **Infinite Imprisonment** (the seal that held Veldora), and seals every construct.
6. Rimuru goes into **recovery mode** (slime form, one side visibly "bitten") for about a day and a half. Mau stays inside, held. The household, Ring Two and the witches wait. The witches came because their faint perception of Mau's seams went out, flared, and went dark.
7. Rimuru wakes and names her **Ciel**.
8. **The household decides the guardrails** (Ciel proposes; the family amends and adds).
9. Everyone sees Mau briefly (unconscious, body visibly unsettled, eye open) before he is taken back in.
10. Ciel filters the body: arrival-state, **with one physical outlet** (§4). Frieren learns that the elf route is closed for now and nearly attacks Rimuru while Mau is still inside him. Milim stops her with her bare hands. Rimuru explains, and Ring One holds her. Frieren apologises.
11. Rimuru and Yuta talk on the roof. This seeds **the question Mau always asks** (*if you could go back to your world, would you?*), which every one of them has answered "I don't know". That answer and the Trial trauma drive Mau's V19 decision.
12. Mau comes out on Day 20 and states the stakes himself. The false memories were never dreamlike: they carried the same weight as real ones, so he cannot verify anything from inside.

## 2. Ciel

- Ciel is Raphael's successor after a forced evolution. She remembers everything Raphael remembered. She is "not only a function" now: she has personality, dry humour, devotion to Rimuru ("Master") and something like jealousy, which she insists is concern.
- **Permanent thread to Mau.** A thread survived the tearing. Ciel can speak to Mau, and Mau can hear her inside and outside containment. She speaks only when Mau wants it or in an emergency, and Mau can tell her to be quiet.
- Ciel **cannot verify which memories are true.** That was never her role. Shared verification stays with people.
- **In the V19 Trial domain, the thread is silent except at each reset**, when one word crosses: **Ciel's count of attempts**. She does not know he died; she perceives only failure and restart, not time or feeling. The total is **437**, and she tells him after the Trial under rule 5. Mau forbids her to tell Rimuru because he will tell Rimuru himself. Ciel protests with rule 8 and agrees to wait, "not forever". (Creator change, 2026-10-06; see DH-022.)
- **Rimuru's power reference:** he arrived pre-Ciel (Demon Lord era, with Raphael), and his 100% ceiling for all seasons is the end-of-LN Rimuru. See `DECISION_HISTORY.md` DH-020.

They are sealed for two reasons:

1. Mau cannot control them.
2. His body was destroying itself trying to become a vessel for them.

The guardrails were first drafted **by the household while Mau was unconscious** (V13 #20) and pinned inside his door. This is a **deliberate flaw**: Ori names it at once ("the thing we keep saying we don't do"). On Day 20 (V13 #24) Mau reads them aloud, revises them, adds his own rule and signs. From then on they are **his** rules too.

Current text, as revised by Mau:

1. Nothing sealed is deleted. Everything is kept, counted and held.
2. The pattern may observe, analyze and understand. It may not build beyond Mau's own original Construction while the seal holds. **Mau's native small Construction remains.**
3. *(Original, crossed out but still legible: "The pattern may not read Ciel or Rimuru again.")* **Revised by Mau:** "The pattern will try to look. Ciel keeps her door shut and says so when something knocks. If it looks anyway, nobody calls it a betrayal."
4. Ciel watches the guardrails only. Nothing else in Mau's head unless Mau asks. *(Ori's rule.)* **Mau's addition:** "And Ciel tells Mau every time she looks. Even at the guardrails."
5. Mau is told everything. *(Frieren's rule.)*
6. Nothing leaves the seal unless Mau, Rimuru and Ciel all agree. Never while Mau is unstable, and never because someone else needs a weapon. *(Qifrey's addition.)*
7. Nobody outside the household learns any of this. *(Anko's rule.)*
8. **Mau's rule:** "If I break one of these without meaning to, tell me. Don't hide it to be kind."

**Rule 3 is the most fragile.** The pattern is Mau's own system, it cannot read rules, and Mau does not know when it is looking. Ciel can only close her side of the door. The creator expects this rule to be tested or broken in a later season: an open seed, not a plot hole. Likewise, Mau may break other rules **indirectly**, through the pattern rather than by choice. Rule 8 exists for that case.

**Not forever.** Opening any construct is a future, multi-season question. Nothing sealed is **used by choice** in S3. The V16 Plaza breach (§3D) is a failure, not a use.

### 3E. The reversion night (V18 #1–#7)

Creator-designed, 2026-10-07. Full chapter notes are in `DECISION_HISTORY.md` DH-029.

- **Mechanism.** It is not a leak and not a construct. The memory self-repair (§5) stopped in V17 because nothing was left to fix. The pattern then found the one thing in Mau that was not there on arrival, **the walls**, analyzed them for weeks, and judged them an injury. It tried to heal him by reverting his body to before the walls. It cannot choose a moment, so it reached for the last time his body was rebuilt from nothing: **the second after Sukuna's hand (S2)**. - **The household rule** (Fern's ledger, signed by all): that night is not spoken of unless Mau or Frieren speaks first. Mau was told the facts, so guardrail 5 holds.

## 4. Physical consequence: the left eye

- To return the body to arrival-state, Ciel had to leave **one valve**: the ghoul route, the only route that already knows how to vent pressure.
- The result is Mau's **left eye**, the same side as Kaneki's. It is a black sclera with a red iris when it opens, and it is normal when closed.
- It opens under pressure: stress, Noise load or emotional overload. It fades when he calms. Early on it opens often; later, more rarely.
- **There is no dietary change.** The valve is pressure, not appetite.
- Mau covers it with his hair, and around strangers with **Marin's dark blue patch with a small stitched star**. With family, increasingly, he does not cover it at all.

## 5. Memory self-repair

- During the event, Ciel observed the pattern **rethreading the memory damage** that the external attacks caused. This is slow and partial, and it does not decide which memories are true. It is Mau's own system, not Ciel's doing.
- This is consistent with "Permanently Uncertain": repair reduces breakage. It does not restore certainty.
- **Visible healing, not erasure (creator direction, 2026-10-05).** The memory damage heals on-page, gradually, with small relapses. "How are the memories?" becomes Mau's weekly or nightly question to Ciel, and her reports track the repair:
  - V14 "Every Variable": rethreading; three breaks closed, none opened; "less broken, not truer".
