# 0. Why the rebuild exists

The first S3 LN production pass preserved many macro ideas but compressed or reordered enough transitional material that continuity broke at the lived-story level.

Observed failures:
- arrivals appear and then stop affecting occupancy / meals / sleeping / work;
- the old inn and the new site are sometimes treated as if they are the same lived base;
- construction advances, regresses, or becomes habitable without an explicit move;
- the first and second Memory disappearances do not consume enough lived time;
- Mau's exhaustion / unconscious states are skipped;
- agreed first-rescue witch terms disappear;
- Diablo's first S3 role loses the G5 mega-haul and eight-signature detection;
- Rem / Kaneki / Aira are introduced at the inn instead of organically finding Mau + Ori in the field;
- Mau's monster fight / head injury is omitted;
- Rimuru containment happens without the agreed Frieren-consent sequence;
- Rimuru / Raphael use `The Noise` terminology before produced S1/S2 continuity permits them to know it;
- public/community beats can appear for one chapter without creating later social consequences;
- post-Memory `The Move` was effectively skipped;
- later arcs therefore inherit an unstable answer to `where does everyone actually live?`.

This is not repaired with isolated inserts.

The rebuild restores a **stateful chronology**.

# 5. Memory Arc — corrected chronological spine

Keep the strong v0.1 opening material:

- remembered conversation that did not happen;
- impossible visit with real details;
- altered real Frieren/Himmel memory;
- Frieren becomes first decisive proof that external manipulation may be occurring.

Let ordinary work, relationship life and site work continue between symptoms.

Locked:
- Mau leaves alone under damaged epistemic logic;
- search is not resolved in the same day;
- search costs construction, route verification, defense and household attention;
- witches find Mau **collapsed / unconscious from exhaustion** before the household reaches him.

The witches:

- detect that something else is interfering with Mau;
- do not instantly cure him;
- keep him alive / stable enough to survive;
- have their own fascination / motives.

Locked prior direction:

```text
Mau missing
```

G5 composition remains intentionally not fully locked.

Population continuity rule:
> once the G5 cohort reaches the old inn / temporary camp, later chapters keep accounting for their beds, meals, questions, work and eventual residency/relocation status.

When the household reaches Mau:

The witches may escort / remain nearby enough to ensure the agreement is respected.

The bargain creates follow-up chapters.

- Frieren's first reaction includes relief / embrace;

# 6. Second escape — time must be lived

The second departure lasts **multiple days**.

Do not compress:
`leave -> fever -> rescue` into one night.

Required progression:

### Days on the road
- Mau and Ori travel;
- food / sleep / route decisions matter;
- Mau can function for stretches and then deteriorate;
- Ori's comparatively stable place in his memory becomes practically useful;
- they have ordinary conversations between symptoms so the escape feels lived.

### Monster encounter
Locked:
- a monster attacks;
- Mau protects Ori / fights;
- the monster badly injures Mau's head;
- Mau kills the monster;
- this is the physical explanation for the later head trauma that other characters must be told about;
- kagune / biological expression belongs to this second escape, not the first.

The kagune does not mean `Mau is a ghoul.`
It is cross-system biological reconstruction / expression.

### Collapse
After the fight:
- fever / fluctuations worsen;
- Mau keeps moving longer than he should;
- exhaustion + head injury + overload accumulate;
- he eventually passes out.

He remains unconscious approximately **1–2 days** in the existing approved range.

He may:
- wake briefly;
- speak / hallucinate / become confused;
- manifest kagune again;
- collapse again.

Ori has to keep him alive without pretending she can solve the problem.

# 9. Rimuru containment — consent and knowledge lock

Rimuru does **not** arrive already knowing:
> this is The Noise.

He can know:
- Mau has had unexplained overloads before;
- Raphael historically could not classify him;
- the current memory/biological crisis contains anomalous structure.

When ordinary stabilization is not enough, Rimuru proposes temporary absorption / containment analogous in principle to his internal containment relationship with Veldora.

Locked consent sequence:
1. Rimuru proposes it to Frieren.
2. Frieren initially refuses / resists.
3. The choice is not made over Mau like property.
4. Frieren gets private time with Mau.
5. She tells him she loves him and that she trusts the people who also love him / are trying to save him.
6. Mau's ability to give fully stable consent may be limited by his condition; do not fake certainty.
7. Frieren permits the containment because the alternative is worsening collapse, not because Rimuru wins an argument.
8. Rimuru absorbs / contains Mau.
9. Only **inside this containment phase** does Raphael gain enough access to observe an unknown endogenous structure more directly.
10. Raphael may ask permission to observe deeper.
11. The term / concept `The Noise` becomes usable only after this discovery / later comparison; it is not prior Rimuru knowledge.

Raphael:
- stabilizes;
- filters;
- separates some implanted/dream material enough to reduce cascade;
- cannot restore perfect memory;
- cannot identify Mau's origin;
- cannot declare a complete TRUE/FALSE history;
- cannot cure the structure.

Construction may have advanced during searches, but the old inn is still the lived household until this arc.

Required movement:


# 14. Unity message — must propagate

A previously agreed S3 direction is restored:

The main settlement, second Otherworlder community and willing native partners eventually produce a **joint public message / declaration** against collective punishment and fear.

It must not exist for one chapter.

Follow-up consequences must include several of:
- a native town opens / keeps a safe corridor;
- another authority quotes the message against them;
- hostile actors edit or distort it;
- frightened arrivals choose to seek refuge because they heard it;
- some members of the second community cooperate without surrendering independence;
- Searchers carry copies / testimony;
- the message changes negotiations;
- later attackers cite it as evidence of organized Otherworlder power;
- the first formal external partnership grows partly from people willing to stand behind it.

The message demonstrates unity **without declaring one nation or one ruler**.
