## 1. Creator problem that reopened S3

Reader review found that the strongest S3 material — especially the Memory Arc — was followed by a post-Memory drift:
- macro plot accelerated faster than domestic life;
- independence was sometimes written as distance from Mau;
- G5 / Ring Two blurred into temporary refuge status;
- source-world groups risked becoming separate household nuclei;
- established relationships such as Anko/Mau, Marin household energy, Maki/Frieren, Seiko family presence, Momo/Okarun and others became too intermittent;
- settlement prose sometimes sounded more physically complete than the creator's intended S3 stage.

The fix is additive. Do not return to the old compressed v0.2 architecture.

## 2. New immutable relationship rule

Mau may be the relational sun.

That is not the same as making every character a satellite with no independent life.

The family should behave like overlapping orbits:
- Mau/Frieren remain the central domestic pair;
- people form strong relationships that do not require Mau;
- those relationships make the household denser rather than replacing its roots;
- choosing proximity to Mau/Frieren is valid agency;
- Ring Two characters may learn why existing loved ones care about Mau and then form their own bond;
- Ring Three refugees/travelers do not need family status to deserve safety.

Full mapping lives in the Relationship & Household Canon Lock.

## 8. Later-volume review direction

Do not restart V16–V19 from scratch.

Existing strengths should be preserved:
- V16 already contains washing-court, culture, old-inn and dinner life;
- V17 already contains Everyone Home, Bathroom War, Music After Dinner and Old Inn Day;
- V18 Last Defense already distributes defense across combat / refuge / medical / infrastructure and gives many non-Mau characters material work;
- V19 already asks the correct late question: **can Mau accept other people's free choices, including choosing him?**

Further revision should be surgical:
- propagate the newly locked household topology;
- keep established faces environmentally present;
- make second-ring -> family growth visible where earned;
- ensure antagonist pressure also grows;
- preserve Trial / Return-Stay architecture;
- never compress the Memory Arc or strong late-S3 prose to pay for additions.

## 9. Construction ceiling into S4

S3 should end with:
- a functioning but unfinished settlement;
- first essential infrastructure;
- a provisional family core;
- a permanent main-house / mega-inn design that is still evolving from lived use;
- unfinished specialist spaces;
- enough evidence from real occupancy to plan S4 deliberately.

S4 can then materially expand:
- permanent mega-inn/family-house;
- final atelier/workshops/clinic;
- mature Arrival House;
- broader civic districts;
- stronger external political / military escalation.

The emotional rule remains:

> the settlement grows because the family needs room; the city does not replace the family.
