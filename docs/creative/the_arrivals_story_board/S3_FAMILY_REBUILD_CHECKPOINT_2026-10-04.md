## 1. Creator problem that reopened S3

Reader review found that the strongest S3 material — especially the Memory Arc — was followed by a post-Memory drift:
- macro plot accelerated faster than domestic life;
- independence was sometimes written as distance from Mau;
- G5 / Ring Two blurred into temporary refuge status;
- source-world groups risked becoming separate household nuclei;
- established relationships such as Anko/Mau, Marin household energy, Maki/Frieren, Seiko family presence, Momo/Okarun and others became too intermittent;
- settlement prose sometimes sounded more physically complete than the creator's intended S3 stage.

The fix is additive. Do not return to the old compressed v0.2 architecture.

## 2. New immutable relationship rule

Mau may be the relational sun.

That is not the same as making every character a satellite with no independent life.

The family should behave like overlapping orbits:
- Mau/Frieren remain the central domestic pair;
- people form strong relationships that do not require Mau;
- those relationships make the household denser rather than replacing its roots;
- choosing proximity to Mau/Frieren is valid agency;
- Ring Two characters may learn why existing loved ones care about Mau and then form their own bond;
- Ring Three refugees/travelers do not need family status to deserve safety.

Full mapping lives in the Relationship & Household Canon Lock.
