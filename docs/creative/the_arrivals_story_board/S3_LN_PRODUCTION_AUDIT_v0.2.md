# The Arrivals — Season 3 Continuity Audit v0.2

**Status:** POST-REBUILD AUDIT  
**Season:** 3  
**Active reader order:** 244 chapters / Volumes 10–19

# 1. Root defects from v0.1 and v0.2 resolution

## Location continuity
**Old defect:** old inn / worksite / settlement were treated interchangeably.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- V10–V13: old inn is default home.
- V14: explicit staged Move with both locations active.
- V14 ending onward: settlement becomes default home.
- old inn remains protected, inhabited when useful, and maintained as origin-home.

## Population continuity
**Old defect:** arrivals could enter and then disappear from sleeping/meals/work.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- G5 mega-haul receives explicit guest / temporary / traveling / departed statuses.
- Rem / Kaneki / Aira enter in field, then receive explicit post-rescue status choices.
- Arrival House foregrounds guest / temporary protection / residence / departure distinctions.
- second community remains independent and visible after introduction.
- old inn retains overflow function.

## Memory Arc compression
**Old defect:** searches, collapses and recovery occurred too quickly.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- first disappearance consumes a multi-day search;
- witches find Mau unconscious from exhaustion;
- first return uses a transfer bargain;
- 3 days of false stability are lived;
- second escape shows multiple travel days;
- monster attack/head injury occurs on page;
- Mau kills the monster;
- Ori leaves physical evidence;
- Mau spends roughly 1–2 days mostly unconscious;
- Rem/Kaneki/Aira find Mau+Ori in the field;
- return trip consumes additional days;
- containment occurs only after continuing deterioration;
- reconstruction/recovery occupy several chapters and days.

## The Noise knowledge
**Old defect:** Rimuru/Raphael behaved as if The Noise were already known.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- S1 limitation preserved: Raphael cannot externally identify Mau's unknown cause.
- through V12 and early V13, nobody diagnoses The Noise.
- Rimuru proposes containment based on observable deterioration only.
- Mau gives consent during a clear window.
- Frieren initially refuses; after private discussion and distributed trust, she accepts proceeding.
- only inside containment does Raphael partially observe an endogenous process.
- Mau supplies the working name / recognition.
- Memory manipulator and The Noise are explicitly not conflated.

## Witch continuity
**Old defect:** witches became a quick reveal and vanished.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- eight-signature route restored;
- witches find Mau first;
- Mau is unconscious/exhausted;
- family cannot simply take him;
- explicit bargain grants later question access with agency protections;
- multiple question sessions follow;
- witches contribute to reconstruction but do not cure or become ordinary residents.

## Diablo / G5 continuity
**Old defect:** Diablo/Milim appeared too quickly without agreed mega-haul/signatures chain.

**v0.2 resolution:**
```text
Mau missing
-> Diablo finds Rimuru
-> Rimuru asks help
-> Diablo gathers G5 mega-haul
-> G5 reaches old inn and remains population
-> Diablo detects eight dangerous signatures
-> reports instead of charging
-> sleep-deprived Frieren misreads / clashes with him
-> search converges on witch zone
```

## Rem / Kaneki / Aira entry
**Old defect:** they appeared at inn before rescue.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- Rem follows injured river trail and finds cap.
- Kaneki independently follows abnormal biological signal.
- Aira hears Ori.
- trio forms accidentally in field.
- they find Mau + Ori before knowing main household.
- they remain distinct people after rescue.

## Monster / head injury
**Old defect:** later injury lacked on-page cause.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- monster attack rendered directly;
- head impact rendered;
- Mau kills monster;
- Ori records events;
- search team later finds monster/body/blood evidence;
- field rescuers receive explicit injury history.

## Unity message
**Old defect:** unity message risked appearing once and disappearing.

**v0.2 resolution:**
- message negotiated across main settlement, second community and willing native partners;
- communities explicitly remain independent;
- message travels physically;
- hostile edit appears;
- correction appears;
- safe corridor results;
- refugees cite message;
- second community receives its own arrivals;
- bridge partnership follows;
- later propaganda uses the message as alleged proof of organized power.

# 2. Long-form continuity retained

- Mau/Frieren remain central romance without erasing Himmel.
- Frieren/Himmel altered memory is false alteration of real history, not retroactive romance rewrite.
- Ori remains independent person, not Goddess puppet.
- old inn survives.
- city remains unfinished and adaptive.
- imported systems change daily life, not only combat.
- Senku arrives after bad work exists.
- Senku's reduced cognition is acknowledged and adapts gradually.
- Eren is around first-sea memory state.
- Mikasa knows later history.
- no guilt assigned for unlived acts.
- defense is layered, not wall-copy.
- Last Defense distributes importance.
- later Noise overload differs from Memory Arc.
- Goddess authority is explicitly bounded to Trial domain.
- Return/Stay has no moral winner.
- full individual choice list remains S4 material.
- Mau's late flaw evolves from unilateral sacrifice to unilateral control of irreversible choice.
