## 0. Production rule

Season 3 is now entering **LN production**, not another compression/roadmap pass.

Authority order when continuity is in doubt:

1. already-produced S1/S2 LN chapters on `m3/critical-path`;
2. explicit later creator corrections committed after those chapters;
3. S1/S2 continuity inventories / audit passes;
4. `S3_BRAIDED_SEASON_ARCHITECTURE_v0.2.md`;
5. supporting future-arc / relationship / wardrobe source notes.

If a planned S3 beat appears to require a prior fact that is not supported by those sources, production must either:
- verify the earlier chapter directly; or
- phrase the S3 material without asserting the uncertain fact.

Do **not** silently convert an old planning note into "something that already happened."

## 1. Exact handoff from S2

S2 ends in `V9 C17 — One More Day`.

Locked handoff:
- Mau/Frieren are explicitly boyfriend/girlfriend;
- Ori remains chosen family and still carries some residual hurt without being excluded;
- the household spends one final deliberately ordinary day at the old inn;
- the future-home site has been chosen, but meaningful construction has **not** begun;

- a functioning city;

- a polished government.

Memory Arc receives full page-space.

- small wrongness;

- Frieren identifies a conversation Mau remembers that she does not;
- first disappearance;
- costly multi-network search;
- witches find Mau first;

## 8. Settlement / city rule

The city is not:
- a normal modern city;
- a normal medieval town;
- Tempest 2;
- a rectangular-grid settlement with magic paint.

Infrastructure must visibly synthesize systems.

Core triangle:
```text
Witch Hat maintained civic magic
+ Senku measurement/engineering
+ Rimuru/Continuum terrain-system knowledge
= Continuum-specific infrastructure
```

Maomao judges hygiene.
Qifrey/Coco/Agott judge spell geometry, safety and repairability.
Senku judges failure modes, measurements and maintenance.
Rimuru thinks at settlement scale.

Senku arrives after flawed work exists.
He improves/challenges; he does not erase prior labor.

Senku continuity:
- cognition begins above average but reduced from source-canon peak;
- he notices this himself;
- recovery/expansion is gradual;
- no instant civilization solve.

Stay:

S3 does **not** resolve the full individual stay/return list.

## 15. Mau's late-S3 failure

Mau's flaw evolves.

Old form:
> I decide to die for you.

New form:
> I decide your irreversible future so you never have to carry regret.

His private attempt to send everyone home is compassionate and wrong.

Required confrontation meaning:
- Frieren: how could you decide my life without me again?
- Mau: she would experience Continuum as dreamlike / avoid the loss;
- this makes Frieren angrier;
- slap/smack beat is allowed after emotional cause is clear;
- Mau admits he does not want anyone carrying lifelong doubt over choosing wrong;
- family rejects that logic;
- `Now let us decide.`

Mau Jail Part Two is valid after the confrontation.
