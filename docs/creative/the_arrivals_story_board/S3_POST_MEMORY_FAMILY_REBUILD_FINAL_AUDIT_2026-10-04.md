## 1. Why this audit exists

Reader review identified a real creative drift after the Memory Arc even though the prior 258-chapter mechanical audit had passed.

The problem was not missing macro plot.

It was that post-Memory S3 had begun to:
- move too quickly from event to event;
- confuse respecting agency with emotional distance;
- flatten G5 / Ring Two into administrative guest status;
- let source-world circles look like separate household nuclei;
- lose recurring relationships when those characters were not carrying the current plot;
- make the settlement feel more complete than the creator intended;
- let protagonist-side growth outpace antagonist-side growth.

The 2026-10-04 rebuild corrected those issues additively.

No strong Memory Arc sequence was compressed to pay for the revision.
