## 0. Core architectural rule

The settlement grows outward from a family home.

It does **not** replace the family home with a collection of franchise-specific houses.

Preferred long-term image:

> one increasingly ridiculous shared family-house / mega-inn  
> + attached or nearby specialist spaces  
> + broader public/refuge/civic settlement around it

Architecture bends around relationships.

Relationships are not reorganized to make an efficient site plan look clean.

## 2. Orientation lock

Cardinal orientation matters.

### East
Mau/Frieren's room keeps an **east-facing window**.

This survives the room-clustering conflict by changing the building plan, not by moving everyone who wants to be near them away.

Locked lived beat:
- Mau/Frieren tentatively choose a quiet corner;
- Ori, Coco, Umaru and others independently choose nearby rooms;
- Bocchi chooses privacy/acoustic buffering but explicitly does **not** want to be far;
- Mau realizes the quiet corner is disappearing;
- Mau/Frieren: in substance, **"I don't think we're winning this battle."**
- rather than separating the family, the unfinished residential wing is rotated / reoriented;
- Frieren keeps her eastern morning light;
- family proximity remains.

### Center
Shared common room + family courtyard are the emotional center.

The center should support:
- people passing through without invitation;
- lounging;
- meals in waves;
- movie nights;
- music spilling in;
- Fern being able to find people;
- Mau/Frieren being accessible without their bedroom becoming public space.

### Quieter residential edge
Bocchi's sleeping room sits on the quieter side of the family cluster.

Her music room / storage / acoustic buffer helps separate sound from sleep.

Rule:
> acoustic privacy is not emotional exile.

### Work / noise edge
Wakana's heavy workshop and Senku's louder work areas sit farther from sleeping rooms.

They remain easily reachable from the family core.

### Medical / chemical edge
Maomao/Kusuri medical and chemical storage stays close enough for emergencies but separated from:
- kitchen food storage;
- children's casual traffic;
- uncontrolled heat / workshop hazards.

### Witch Hat / atelier direction
The future atelier should be attached / near the family-house but maintain:
- safe spell geometry;
- maintenance access;
- enough separation for experiments;
- direct walkable connection to common family life.

### Arrival House direction
Arrival House is **not inside the intimate family bedroom core**.

It should sit toward a main approach / intake route:
- easy for newcomers to find;
- easy to leave;
- private enough for frightened arrivals;
- connected to medical / food / records;
- close enough for family help;
- clearly distinct from "you arrived, therefore you live with Mau."

This physical distinction supports Ring Three autonomy.

## 4. Mega-inn evolution

The long-term family-house should feel accidental even when later additions are planned.

S3:
- first cluster;
- people discover what they actually need.

Early S4:
- permanent family wing begins around real occupancy data;
- east window preserved;
- covered connectors improve;
- common room expands;
- more permanent bedrooms;
- sound / smell / privacy problems corrected.

Later S4+:
- workshop and atelier become mature attached / nearby structures;
- clinic becomes real;
- common room / dining may expand again;
- more courtyards;
- multiple entrances;
- guest rooms;
- family rooms added because people keep staying.

Running joke / emotional truth:

> "How did we build another inn?"

Because the old inn's **social logic** survived even when the building did not scale.

## 5. What not to do

Do not create:
- JJK house;
- Witch Hat house;
- Dandadan house;
- Kessoku house;
- Tensura house;
- 100 Girlfriends house.

Source-world relationships remain roots, not zoning categories.

Do not use architecture to prove character independence.

Do not isolate Ori, Bocchi, Umaru or Coco merely because Mau must respect agency.

Do not treat a private room as emotional distance.

Do not turn the family core into Arrival House.

Do not finish the whole settlement in S3.
