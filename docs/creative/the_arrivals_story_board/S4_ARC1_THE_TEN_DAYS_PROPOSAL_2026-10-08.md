# The Arrivals — S4 Arc 1: "The Ten Days" (proposal) + Volume 2: the peace volume

**Status:** PROPOSAL by Claude, 2026-10-08, at the creator's invitation ("si quieres tú finaliza la historia de este arco... después un volumen entero de paz... con señal a lo que viene"). The creator's own outline (Parts 1–3 opening) is canon-in-progress and lives in `S4_EARLY_DIRECTION_2026-10-06.md`. Everything marked **DECISION** below is the creator's to make before any prose is written.

**Reader warning:** this file references V19 in detail. The creator has not finished reading S3, so in chat he gets only spoiler-free summaries.

**Time budget:** S3 ends on the evening of Day 3 of the ten-day Return/Stay window, about Month 24. S4 covers at most six months. This arc takes about two weeks (Day 4 to the days after Day 10). The peace volume takes the rest of winter.

## Where S3 leaves things (facts the arc must honour)

- **The window.** Ten days, with seven left at S3's end.
  - Return: personal terms; Continuum may become dreamlike; a chooser may return into danger, or before a tragedy.
  - Stay: one bounded petition concerning a specific loved person. It cannot override that person's agency, is not guaranteed, and sometimes can concern the dead ("Some").
  - Terms can be reviewed privately, with a witness, and nothing irreversible is accepted before the terms are shown.
- **Open individual threads.** Coco whispered a name at "petition" (Richeh and Agott noticed). Rem asked about the dead. Kusuri asked about refusal. Kaneki stayed silent. Aira: "Ask me in a week." Frieren keeps her terms private. Mau told her what he wants ("Stay") without demanding it. Maki has "one small unfired thing" in her pocket.
- **Mau's plan.** Mau went alone to ask whether a *collective* Return existed, to "remove the burden of individual decision". He intended to come back before anything irreversible. It was not a death plan this time, but it had the same architecture ("secret decision").
- **The condition** (DH-023) was signed: "Not tonight." Ori's broken promise ("you left a note"). Umaru's "put it down" (V17). "Both of us" (V18). Ciel holds 437. Analyze is now carrying Trial material.
- **Ring Four was designed to disappear in S4** (DH-026). **The Plaza breach** (DH-024) was built as S4 setup. **The "let me out" voice** is unidentified. **Sukuna knows about the wheel.**

## Arc 1, as proposed

### Part 1: Anger (Days 4–5). Creator.
Proposed shape: Ring Three's conversation happens in public (the plaza and Arrival House) and Rings One and Two's happens in the house, cross-cut. Two touches to keep the family from reading as cold:
- Food still appears at his door. Ori's stone is still on the windowsill.
- Umaru is the first to break the silence with a *question* rather than a rule ("Did you think we'd be happier without you choosing for us, or without you?"). This brings back the S2 question of whether he felt loved.

### Part 2: The talk (night of Day 5). Creator.
Folded-in notes (see the early-direction doc): the "sometimes I died" of V19 becomes **"every time, 437"**. **Ciel confesses she looked** into his memories (guardrail 4), and Ori reacts. **Infinity** is what keeps Frieren out. **Maki** talks him down without touching him. **Ciel on the far end:** she offers it as "a possibility"; Rimuru keeps it to himself, assuming it is an example, and says the Milim comparison aloud. Milim may not know Chloe's cycles, depending on her arrival point.
- **Frieren speaks of the reversion night.** She is the only one besides Mau the ledger rule allows. When he asks her to sign the two-day contract: **"You said *both of us*. In that bed. With a hole in you."** It is the first time anyone in the room has heard it said aloud since the ledger. She does not sign. She also **does not follow**: the rules apply to everyone, and that is her growth. The two days mirror her two days outside the bay door, but this time she *chooses* to wait.

### Part 3: The two days (Days 6–7)

**Mau.**
- **Where:** the clearing where he woke in S1, "where it began". Not far, as his contract says. It is the first real memory he has, and the place he must re-learn is real.
- **One word a day.** Before leaving, he asks Ciel to send one word to Rimuru each evening: **"Here."** This inverts the Trial, where Ciel sent *him* one word per reset. The household gets two words in two days. Rimuru reads each one aloud at dinner.
- **Day 6.** He does the thing he never did in 437 loops: **nothing.** He sits and waits for the day to pass, and nothing dies. He counts the sunset. Nothing resets. He sleeps outside. When it rains, his body flinches at the first drops (Trial reflex) and then learns that rain is only rain.
- **Infinity.** He can't touch anything. Leaves stop a hair from his skin, and so does the rain. *This is why he left:* he could not let them touch him and could not bear their faces when they couldn't. On the second evening, after a day of trying, **a single leaf lands on the back of his hand.** He cries for the first time since the road.
- **The 437 letters he doesn't send.** In the backpack (S1) is a notebook. He writes *one* sentence to each person who died in front of him in the loops, real names on false deaths, and then **burns** them. Ciel watches the guardrails only and does not read them.
- **The deer** (S1, V16 "Frierennnnn", the V17 coast road) stands at the edge of the clearing on the second morning. It is not explained. It is not the Goddess. It does not need to be explained.
- **Hard rule:** he does not go near the Goddess's site. The condition holds, and the reader should *see* him choose not to. The road south is visible from the clearing.

**The house.**
- **Frieren** sleeps in the bed, on his side. On Day 7 she takes the grimoire with the four pressed flowers to the ink tree and reads beside it all day.
- Ori and Umaru take down the old note (V19) from wherever Ori kept it, and replace it with a new slip: "TELL ME FIRST. — he did, this time."
- Rimuru tells Yuta about Chloe on the roof, the full story for the first time: *she carried the loops alone, for me.* Yuta: "He carried them for us." This seeds the S4 Rimuru–Mau conversation.
- **Ring Three and Four.** The rumour mill runs: he has gone to the Goddess, the monster has left. Anko counters it with facts, not denials. The contract is posted.

### Part 4: He comes back (night of Day 7). Kept to the letter.
- He walks in at dusk, not morning, because he could not wait any longer. The whole fort is on the step again (Nano calculated the day).
- **Frieren walks into the Infinity, and it lets her through.** Not because she breaks it: because he lets it down for her first. Then for Ori. Then for everyone, one at a time, while Maomao checks his pulse and complains.
- He brings back nothing from the clearing except a leaf.

### Part 5: The conversations (Days 8–9). Mau finally talks. This is S4's root, answered once, not solved forever.
- **Ori:** the broken promise. He doesn't apologise with words. He gives her the first real "tell me first": *"I'm going to the Goddess tomorrow, with all of you, to ask for my own terms. I'm telling you first."*
- **Umaru:** "You promised you'd put it down." / "I'm putting it down. Can you carry the corner?" She carries his bag to the Arrival House the next day.
- **Rimuru:** 437 and Chloe. "Someone did that for me once. I swore nobody would do it alone again. And you did it for *hundreds of years*." Rimuru is angry at himself, not at Mau. The roof rule (V17) becomes nightly for a while.
- **Yuta:** Gojo, completed. "He stood in front alone and nobody stopped him. We stopped you on a road. Let us keep stopping you."
- **Frieren:** the most important and shortest. She tells him her terms, by her own choice, before Day 10. **DECISION:** Frieren's answer. Claude's lean is **Stay**, with her petition *unused*, or saved, or given away (see below). The creator has said S4 is the last season to strain them this hard, and this is where the strain resolves rather than repeats.

### Part 6: The last days (Days 9–10)
- Mau goes to the Goddess **with them**, as a chooser like everyone else. **DECISION:** Mau's own terms. Proposal: Return is meaningless for him, because his origin is blank, and that blankness is the point. His Stay petition is the most open question in the arc. Options:
  - Unused.
  - Given to someone whose petition the Goddess refused.
  - Concerning Frieren's lifespan. The elf route was closed in S3, and a petition "concerning a loved person" could ask for *her* to have the choice later. Big, and it touches the tree promise.
- **Individual answers. DECISION, and the creator's above all.** Claude's structural proposal:
  - **Ring Four largely Returns.** That is *how* Ring Four disappears, as designed (DH-026). The plaza feels emptier. "Monster town" has fewer neighbours.
  - **Ring Three splits.** A few Return. Ring Three stories are told in short bridge-side goodbyes.
  - **Rings One and Two: nobody Returns.** Every one of them is shown *choosing*, not defaulting. The Trial's lesson is that choice is real.
  - Alternatively, **one bittersweet Return** from Ring Two, to make the cost real. Candidates for the creator to weigh: Aira ("ask me in a week") or Mai.
- **Petitions. DECISION.** These are the best source of *new life* for the peace volume:
  - Coco's whispered name (her mother).
  - Rem's question about the dead. Rem's answer may be "not this time", and she stays anyway; that is the "realizes she has joined" stage completed.
  - Kaneki's silence (possibly Hide).
  - Kusuri's "if they refuse".
  - Maki's "unfired thing". Proposal: it was always her own petition request, written and never handed in.
  - **Petitions that succeed bring new arrivals into the peace volume.**
- **Day 10 sunset.** The window closes. The sky shows nothing. The plaza is quieter. The house is full.

## Volume 2 of S4: the peace volume (winter)

**Shape:** a whole volume of slice of life, goofy and purposeless days included (the creator's first rule). It is set in the first winter after the window. Marin's unclaimed winter coats (V19's last chapter) become a running thread: everyone gets one.

Proposed contents (a menu, not an order):
- **The real house gets built** (layout lock §10). Moving day, round two. Rooms finally get ink instead of pencil, except Rem's, which stays pencil because she asks again. The ink tree is *inside* the courtyard ring, as Wakana promised.
- **Petition arrivals settle in.** Each gets the household's lived integration: guest, then returns, then chooses space.
- **Fern and Stark's first winter as a couple.** Maomao's chocolate revenge lands (the aphrodisiac). Treat it with the same rule as the chocolate: it doesn't make anyone want what they didn't already want. Played for comedy, with Maomao smug.
- **The fort's winter campaign:** snow forts versus the toll booth, and the pack at full size.
- **Mau's roof nights with Rimuru and Yuta**, and the first time Mau laughs on the roof.
- **Frieren and Mau:** the room with the east window in snow. The "most beautiful" in a winter coat ("it doesn't work in a coat" / "it does").
- **A birthday or a festival** the household invents because nobody knows anyone's real date. This also supplies the locked S4 timeline table.
- **Ciel's daily "Looked. Quiet."** becomes household texture. One day she says "Looked. Busy." and refuses to elaborate for a chapter.

**Signals of what comes next** (each small, none resolved):
1. **Analyze is building.** Small inexplicable things in Mau's Construction, and once something that looks like *Copy* (Rika's infinity). Yuta notices first.
2. **People who haven't arrived yet.** Analyze carries patterns of a white-haired fighter. Yuta goes very still the first time Mau's hand moves the way *that* person's did. The arrival is not stated.
3. **The "let me out" voice** (DH-024). Once, on a quiet night, Ciel hears something knock that is not the wheel.
4. **Sukuna**, far away, hears that the window closed and that Mau stayed. He reacts.
5. **The hostile framing** (V17/V18) finds a new angle: "the Monster town people *chose* to stay with it."
6. **The Frieren-gets-hit seed** (`FLOATING_IDEAS.md`) is available as the volume's last chapter: the peace breaks on something small.
