It is still single use, and still not a "build a city" button, but it now does a little more. The creator added two extras partly because he doesn't want the Witch Hat group left without magic.
  1. **Re-arranging the land** (as agreed): for example a **dam for power** that barely alters the surroundings. The level sits between Dr. Stone and Tensura, nothing like the modern world, but enough electricity in the future for lights and climate control.

  2. **Accelerating the growth of a tree, and we know which one** (the ink tree, V16 "The Ink Budget"). **Frieren tells Mau they won't be able to keep their hundred-year promise, because the tree arrived before anyone expected.**
  3. **Building the house:** drainage, ideas, everything. It no longer has to be a small inn because of budget.
     - **A movie room.** In Tensura, Rimuru can put his memories on paper as manga; with Senku, they can make discs.
     - **A bath like Tensura's.**
     - **More modern architecture:** some want Japanese, someone Roman. Claude invents the details.
- **Rimuru's power.** We have "a tank of possibilities" with Rimuru. The creator doesn't want Mau to feel like the only one who can analyze. **Rimuru can analyze too.** The creator doesn't mind the current nerf, but wants this used.
- **Timing.** The house is built in the peace volume, or, if it runs long, about a month after everything. A good part of the season can be them **existing**, while the world turns more hostile and Arrival House stops receiving people. That bothers them, but it is "a problem for later": right now they want to rest. They are a family before anything.

## 3. The world changes: exclusion (creator)

- **Taken from AoT, without the island:** their city ends up cut off from the rest of the world by politics, and by monsters. After the Goddess, monsters appear far more often, until the traditional roads are no longer viable or safe.
- **Eren proposes it:** "For our safety and everyone else's, I think we'll have to do something I hate. We'll have to close ourselves off while the world keeps evolving, and work out what we really are to the world, and what we want."
- The point is not to alienate them. Changes planned since before S1 have never been introduced: **the Chainsaw Man devils were once central, and we have never seen one.**
- **The Goddess says, beforehand: "The world is evolving."** In hindsight, **the Goddess's protection was a warning about these changes.**
- **Threat geography:** the danger is concentrated around their region (as with Tempest), but appearances also happen elsewhere in the world.

## 4. Dungeons (creator)

- **The Goddess:** "As Mau cannot bring a loved one, I will give something that benefits him in the long run." A **dungeon** forms near the settlement.
- **Solo Leveling style**, with rules:
  - **A shop like Solo Leveling's**, usable only near the dungeon and **for the whole settlement**. Unlike Solo Leveling, **purchases can be kept and exist outside it** (potions and so on).
  - **Limits:** healing potions might exist only at about 10%-and-below strength, and only from **bosses**, so they are hard to get and break nothing.
  - **The dungeon evolves every week** instead of breaking: a new one each week (normal, double, and so on).
- **The Jin-Woo seed**, if he is brought in, must be **"nerfed to hell"**. He is the infinite-growth trope and can break everything, so we need great care.
- **Contracts combine with it.** Rimuru can use his **shadows for information and relay**, so they get a security system without inventing digital cameras or breaking the rules. Mau analyzes, and something good comes of it. The contracts are magic (paper for now; S2 showed what happens when one is broken, which is why Mau never technically breaks anything and is never penalized). They can evolve later.
- **The economy runs on loot, not on "let's build our world's tools and sell them".** Loot is used for trading. These monsters **cannot be destroyed with normal metal**; magic and cursed energy work (so nobody is too nerfed). As in Solo Leveling, they get crystals.

## 6. Arc: Coco's mother (creator, detailed)

The creator doesn't want it to be cruel. This is also how the mother is convinced not to take Coco away, since she could blame magic for what happened.

1. **She arrives crystallized.** Drama. Qifrey pulls Coco away to the fort, though Coco wants to touch the crystal. Qifrey knows that whoever touches it may crystallize too.
2. Mau starts to feel something. While others go to Coco, he stays looking at the mother. "Ciel, tell Rimuru and Yuta to come. Something is telling me it thinks it can help."
3. Rimuru and Yuta come. Frieren may be there as support, because this involves going back to something.
4. Mau, out loud: "Ciel, would anything happen to Rimuru if he absorbed Coco's mother?" Ciel: the crystal is no longer trying to crystallize anything else, so absorption is safe, "but I don't understand why."
5. **Mau's plan** is a very bad idea, but it needs him to be absorbed too, to contain everything and let Ciel analyze every molecule.
   - He will use **adapt**, letting himself crystallize and forcing the tool to adapt.
   - He must be joined to the mother when it happens, so that adapting means treating everything crystal as dangerous and undoing it.
   - Ciel gets **two reference points** and can create **a skill in Rimuru** to free the mother.
6. Rimuru wants to refuse. Yuta is there because he understood the roof rules, so he waits for Rimuru's opinion first, since it involves Rimuru's body.
   - Rimuru is about to get angry, because it's a ridiculous idea and very Mau.
   - **Ciel interrupts:** it is the best idea. It controls every variable and can create a skill; it is the safest way, with no harm to the mother, and all the risk... (she trails off) ...sits with "the two idiots who accepted". Rimuru: "Hey." Ciel: "Not my words. They had a ceremony." Rimuru laughs: "This is the most Mau idea I've ever heard." Ciel: but it's the best option.
7. Yuta: "Before you two idiots do anything" (they turn: "Ciel's words") "we have to ask one person's permission." Both: obviously, they just didn't want to give false hope.
8. **The three musketeers at the fort.** Coco has been holding Qifrey and hasn't stopped crying. She almost sends them away. Mau: "I think we can help. But before we move a finger, we want your opinion and your permission. If you say no, we won't do anything." They ask her to hear the whole plan before answering.
9. **Coco asks only two questions.**
   - Is there any risk to her mother during the process? All three: no. She is there to reduce variables and detect any change.
   - What is the risk? Mau tells the truth: for Yuta and Rimuru, none. He is the one with the problem. It's not a sacrifice: he has talked with Ciel, and if he gets trapped, Ciel removes the guardrails completely. "If I can hold a path for 36 minutes, I can hold 5." Coco: "Sure you can hold five?" Mau: "I can try." Coco accepts.
10. **Inside.** Mau and the mother go in. He touches the crystal and his hand begins to crystallize.
    - "Tell Coco it doesn't hurt to crystallize. It's like someone put a blanket on you. And five minutes." Rimuru relays it, and **Coco lets go of something she has held since the day her mother crystallized.**
    - The count starts. Ciel explains; Rimuru narrates.
    - Inside, **a wheel appears above Mau's head before he's crystallized**. The wheel crystallizes, and turns.
    - Ciel: problem detected. Through the thread to Mau she is receiving data. It's not yet a skill, but she is mapping it. "When I create it, I'll make it not just for him but the inverse, so it can un-crystallize someone else."
    - Minute one, another turn. The crystal stops advancing. Ciel: it has adapted to stop advancing; now waiting for regression.
    - Turns 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. **On the eighth the wheel completes**, and Mau's crystallization recedes. Mau comes back: it worked. Ciel has the map for the skill. Guardrails on.
11. Mau comes out first, exhausted. "I told you, I managed to adapt. Ciel has it, and she's changing it so it can reverse the process." He almost faints. Coco catches him, crying, saying thank you over and over.
12. **Ciel:** skill complete. She recommends bringing Coco's mother out now, so she doesn't panic in the void. The mother comes out: "Don't be scared." (No skill is used on Coco yet, so she isn't frightened.) Coco asks her mother to hold on a little, because she wants to have everything ready.
13. **That night** the mother is brought inside (Muslin or the new house). Coco arranges her room, they make food, and everyone contributes, except two.
    - Mau is helped to bed. He asks for Frieren: "In the shower."
    - He nods off. Rimuru, in the loft, sees an opportunity, jumps, and lands on Mau's stomach: "**You used me. I'm going to use you back.**"
    - Frieren comes in. Callback: "**Now who is cheating on who?**" They laugh. Frieren lies down, and the three sleep.
    - The next day is Claude's to write.

## Claude's notes (proposals, not decisions)

1. **Foundation never reached the LN text.** It exists only in the planning doc (`sources/THE_ARRIVALS_FUTURE_MACRO_ARCS_FOUNDATION_MEMORY_CRISIS_v0.1.md`). That doc's principle: "**Foundation creates the physical board. The people still build the city.**" It has a long setup, a real cost, and Rimuru hesitates. Proposals:
   - **Seed it backwards** with one or two lines in S3 (for example "Leadership After the Front" or "Cut the Real Cloth": Rimuru: "I have something saved. Not for this.").
   - **Keep the people building the house.** Foundation does what no one can: it raises the dam, grows the ink tree, and **digs the cellar and every tunnel in one breath**. That pays off V18 #43: "That's a lot of digging." "Years of it." The household still builds the rooms, the bath and the movie room by hand.
   - **The cost:** a single use, and Ciel spent weeks evolving it. Rimuru gives up the thing he has kept "for when it matters most".
2. **The ink tree and the promise.** The hundred-year promise is the reversion-night promise (V18 "Both of Us": "Both of us. A hundred years. The tree."). Growing the tree breaks it *by fulfilling it early*. Proposal: Frieren declares the promise "broken", so Mau owes her a new one, longer. This can **feed the queen chapter**: "your punishment for breaking your promise" is the tree arriving early. It isn't even his fault, and that is pure Frieren logic. The order would be Foundation, then the dungeon.
3. **The door, as canon has it** (containment lock §3A, V14 "What It Made"): the pattern's first invention. It opens onto the clearing on the morning Mau woke. Ciel's reading: the pattern seeks **arrival-state**, its first stable reference. Mau refused it then: "It's not before. It's just before you." **The creator's S4 beat is the perfect second refusal**: "before" calls with his whole body, and he chooses Frieren. Keep it uncanny and never a win condition. It may tie to his blank origin and Second Chance, which stays unknown in-world.
4. **The wheel.** Canon adaptations are the plaza wheel turning "not here yet" (V16/V19 Boundaries) and the uncontrolled Infinity. **The Coco's-mother arc is the third adaptation, and the first controlled one, with rotations counted (8).** That sets the rule the creator wants: adaptation takes multiple turns, each turn is partial progress, and it costs Mau exhaustion. Record it in the systems registry.
5. **"36 minutes" in Mau's line:** canon's controlled trial was **19 minutes** (V15), and the plaza seal was "nineteen minutes". No 36-minute hold exists in canon (checked 2026-10-10). Either change the line to 19 ("If I held a path for nineteen minutes, I can hold five"), or write a second controlled trial earlier in S4 that reaches 36 minutes. That second option is also visible proof that the seal and the wheel are progressing, which the creator wants.
6. **Threat order.** Proposal: the world-evolving announcement and monster frequency first (the roads close), then the dungeon, then **devils** as the first new class. Chainsaw devils are born from fear, and **the world's fear of Mau's city could literally spawn a devil**. The exclusion theme becomes a mechanic. **Frieren's demons** come later as the first *intelligent* threat (language as a weapon; Frieren's expertise). JJK curses overlap with devils (negative emotion), so hold them back or fold them in. Titans come with Eren's lifespan revelation.
7. **Jin-Woo.** The dungeon shop already *is* his System, and Mau's wheel is already the infinite-growth character. Two infinite-growth characters in one house is a problem. Proposal: the shadows idea lives in Rimuru's relay network, and Jin-Woo stays a seed for much later, or never comes. Creator decision.
8. **Mau's own coin.** In V20 Mau holds a coin too. The Goddess's line ("As Mau cannot bring a loved one...") can be what his coin becomes: the dungeon is what his coin buys.
9. **Coco's mother and the earlier plan:** she arrives as she was, still crystallized, and the cure costs something. The cost is Mau's adaptation and exhaustion. Consistent.
