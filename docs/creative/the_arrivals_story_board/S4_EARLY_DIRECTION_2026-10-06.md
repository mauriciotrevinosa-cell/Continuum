# The Arrivals — S4 Early Direction (creator, 2026-10-06)

**Status:** EARLY CREATOR DIRECTION, not a plan. The creator has more S4 ideas that he is **deliberately not sharing yet**. He wants to finish reading S3, feel its ending and see the reactions already written before building on them. Do not fill those gaps with invented S4 plot.

## What the creator has said

- **Opening.** S4 starts exactly where S3 ends, on the Trial aftermath. That aftermath needs time: consequences, reactions, suffering. Some of it may not be resolved until the end of S4, and that is acceptable **if continuity holds the whole way**.
- **Size is undecided.** The creator does not yet know whether S4 should be the same size as S1.
- **S4 is an emotional arc, not a torture arc.** The creator does not want the aftermath to read as "let's make Mau suffer and the family suffer again". Growth, fights, politics and insecurity will happen, but the spine is emotional.
- **The first part of S4 must hurt.** It is an accumulation of everything.
- **The root is Mau's silence.** Over the seasons Mau has conformed to other people's decisions and often does not say what he feels. He stays quiet. That accumulation is what breaks.
- **The family has carried different kinds of fault before.** S2's crisis was, in the creator's words, 100% Mau's fault. S3's was half his fault, and not through bad intent: he didn't know who he was, and the false memories felt real, so the family understood and endured. S4 must not repeat that pattern mechanically.
- **The Trial was meant as a breaking point.** The creator knows why he wanted the Trial to exist. The break is not meant to be "for the worse".
- **The creator expects S4 to be the last season that strains the Mau–Frieren relationship this hard.**

- **Escalation.** S4 escalates hard ("everything goes to 11"), and the creator plans to bring an "ugly reality" he has not described yet.
- **Ring Four** was designed to disappear in S4. This is the outer ring of people who come and go; it was called "Ring Three" before the V17 council (DH-026).
- **REMIND THE CREATOR** when S4 planning starts that the V16 Plaza breach and evacuation (DH-024) was built at his request as S4 setup.

Claude's notes (proposals, not decisions):
- **The weight is the point.** S4's first part "must hurt", and its root is Mau's silence. After the reversion arc every promise is on the table: "both of us", "I won't decide alone again" (S2), Ori's "tell me first" and Umaru's "put it down". Two days away will hurt for the right reasons.

Waiting without breaking would show her growth. Breaking anyway is also honest. Whether her panic attack recurs is the creator's call (DH-029).

## S4 opening: the creator's outline (shared 2026-10-08)

The creator decided to share this ("te lo ganaste"). It supersedes the 2026-10-07 two-day note above. **Creator canon unless revised.**

### Part 1: anger
- **Two conversations run in parallel: Ring Three, and Rings One and Two.**
- **Ring Three** heard *salvation* in the Return/Stay offer, all the more after the Last Defense deaths. (The creator remembered two dead. Canon is three of the settlement's people plus the named militia fighter, a count he approved earlier. Confirm.) Those deaths fuelled Mau's private question, *why didn't you stand in front?*, which led to the Trial.
- **Everyone is furious and offended with Mau, day after day.** It is not his decision. There are rules and promises. Some in Ring Three say: "I *would* go back, but it wasn't your choice to make."
- **The family does not ignore him.** It is not hate. The S2 questions come back: did he love them, and did he feel loved?

### Part 2: the talk (the condition from DH-023, fulfilled)
- Mau has said almost nothing since the Trial. People talk *at* him, trying to understand why he decided what he decided and what he saw. He hears rules about him, plans to move everything *with* him, and no way to help.
- **Rimuru:** "You made a promise to someone. You know who. You promised to tell a number." Mau asks someone to explain the count-everything rule, then asks, "What did Ciel tell you about that number?" Rimuru: "Only that it was the number of attempts you made." Mau: "Yes. I tried many times. Thousands of ways, thousands of days." **Ciel:** the number she has recorded "does not pass 500". Rimuru is furious. Mau: "Ciel doesn't know her number is something else." Ciel tells Rimuru to press. Rimuru: "If it isn't attempts, what is it? Why did you make Ciel hide it?"
- Mau looks at the count-everything rule. **"437."** Ciel: "Correct number." Rimuru relaxes. Mau: "Ciel thought they were attempts. The number is wrong. I think it's right. I lost count after two hundred."
- Mau asks Rimuru whether he knows what it is like to have every part of you cut, destroyed, vaporized or tortured for days, if not centuries. He doesn't remember how long he was there. **"437 times I died."** Nobody speaks.
- **What he describes:**
  - In some loops they killed everyone. In others Mau sacrificed himself and let some stand in front of him. "Every time I was in front, you lived. When I let you be in front, I watched you die, one by one."
  - He never knew why or for whom. He tried everything. Every day the only line was *the only way they live is if they go back.* He ignored it the whole time and told himself it was false, like the memories.
- **Mau's panic attack.** Frieren tries to take his hand and hug him, and he pulls away. He remembers a loop where he and Maki fought for a time he can't measure and neither survived. He remembers fighting someone white-haired and losing. He remembers someone kidnapping Frieren, with the price that he cut his own throat.
- **The last two loops.**
  1. Mau returns to the inn after searching alone for a solution, to test whether the problem was his presence. He finds most of them dead and someone screaming *where the hell were you?* He looks at Rimuru, Milim and Eren: "I think I beat you that time. I destroyed everything." He looks at Frieren and goes quiet. He remembers only hearing *Construct* begin to adapt and the wheel turning without stopping, faster and faster, everything evolving and being created.
  2. **The wedding.** He describes it in full. Something attacks, and his body reacts. He remembers Frieren in his arms. He does not want to repeat what he heard. **Frieren: "Tell me what that false version said."** He breaks, as if a weight has lifted, and tells her. Frieren: "No no no no." Mau: "I know. But then I heard a voice." That voice helped him tell the Trial from the real world. The reward was a way out of this world for them, or a way to bring the people they love here. He says it as if *he* is the problem, the reason everything goes to hell. "I saw an opportunity I didn't see in 437 deaths: a way to give you a life where you don't die."
- **Anko:** "You're thinking like you did with Sukuna again." Someone adds: "You forget that against Sukuna we found a third way." Mau breaks: "Sorry, sorry, sorry." Nobody judges him, because everyone finally understands something.
- **Analyze.** Ciel tells Rimuru that although it was a Trial, Analyze took the possible tools. Mau will no longer have only the sealed set (the creator said 148; canon is **143 sealed plus 4 uncounted fragments**), and the final number can't be known. Analyze doesn't know what it is analyzing. It understands that every memory held people who shouldn't be there, or *had not arrived yet*, and things Mau didn't know existed, and it is building them. **It finally analyzed Rika's infinity.** Mau may one day copy like Yuta, with no known what, how or when. **Limits:** Analyze is slow, every creation costs mana or cursed energy, there are new rules, and the walls hold.
- **Time.** Ciel analyzed memory discrepancies while she was silent and justified it as an emergency. Rimuru: "Give me the rough estimate." Ciel: **"At the short end, he is now about a hundred years older than Frieren."** And the far end? **"He could be older than all of Chloe's resets combined."** This terrifies Rimuru: it is what he promised would never happen again. He tells the room aloud. Mau: "And the non-conservative one?" Rimuru, softening: "Officially you'd be the oldest in the house." Milim, in tears: "Are you sure? I've lived a long time." Rimuru: if the far end is true, Mau has lived longer than all of Chloe's cycles. Milim knows Chloe ran roughly 2,000 years a cycle, thousands of times. She goes silent.
- **Frieren can't reach him.** She tries to comfort Mau and something won't let her. However hard she pushes, nothing gets near him.

### Part 3: the two days
- Mau stands: he needs space and goes to the room. Frieren wants to follow. **Fifteen minutes later** he comes back **with a backpack**. "The rules apply to everyone, right? So I can leave whenever I want?" Yes. Everyone fears the worst.
- He produces a **contract already signed by him**: *I'm leaving for two days. Nobody may follow or accompany me. I'll be back the night of the second day or the morning of the third. I'm not going far. I need to breathe, and to understand that I came back here.*
- He does not expect anyone to sign it. He asks **Frieren** to. She refuses: she can help. Mau: "I know. That's why I need to understand my mistake. However much time passed, I can't break my promises." Someone, wanting him to stop (nobody can touch him yet): "But you kept it." Mau: "Halfway. So, two days of punishment." Someone starts to answer. Mau: "Bad joke."
- Before he goes, someone asks whether they were with him the whole time. "No. When you died it was always at the start of a cycle. It never gave me time to reach you. And when I could, I was the one who died."
- He leaves. **The outline stops here.** The creator has ideas for escalation and reconciliation but nothing this concrete yet.
