# The Arrivals — S4 Early Direction (creator, 2026-10-06)

**Status:** EARLY CREATOR DIRECTION, not a plan. The creator has more S4 ideas that he is **deliberately not sharing yet**. He wants to finish reading S3, feel its ending and see the reactions already written before building on them. Do not fill those gaps with invented S4 plot.

## What the creator has said

- **Opening.** S4 starts exactly where S3 ends, on the Trial aftermath. That aftermath needs time: consequences, reactions, suffering. Some of it may not be resolved until the end of S4, and that is acceptable **if continuity holds the whole way**.
- **Size is undecided.** The creator does not yet know whether S4 should be the same size as S1.
- **S4 is an emotional arc, not a torture arc.** The creator does not want the aftermath to read as "let's make Mau suffer and the family suffer again". Growth, fights, politics and insecurity will happen, but the spine is emotional.
- **The first part of S4 must hurt.** It is an accumulation of everything.
- **The root is Mau's silence.** Over the seasons Mau has conformed to other people's decisions and often does not say what he feels. He stays quiet. That accumulation is what breaks.
- **The family has carried different kinds of fault before.** S2's crisis was, in the creator's words, 100% Mau's fault. S3's was half his fault, and not through bad intent: he didn't know who he was, and the false memories felt real, so the family understood and endured. S4 must not repeat that pattern mechanically.
- **The Trial was meant as a breaking point.** The creator knows why he wanted the Trial to exist. The break is not meant to be "for the worse".
- **The creator expects S4 to be the last season that strains the Mau–Frieren relationship this hard.**

- **Escalation.** S4 escalates hard ("everything goes to 11"), and the creator plans to bring an "ugly reality" he has not described yet.
- **Ring Four** was designed to disappear in S4. This is the outer ring of people who come and go; it was called "Ring Three" before the V17 council (DH-026).
- **REMIND THE CREATOR** when S4 planning starts that the V16 Plaza breach and evacuation (DH-024) was built at his request as S4 setup.
