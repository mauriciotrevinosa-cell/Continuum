The creator asked for this before V24: what is missing, who has been abandoned or hasn't appeared in a while and why, and which relationships have moved and which have stalled. **Scope:** all 109 S4 chapters (V20–V23).

**Method:**
- Each character was counted per volume.
- A **strong** chapter means the name appears at least four times, which roughly means the character has a scene. "Since" is how many chapters ago the last strong chapter was.
