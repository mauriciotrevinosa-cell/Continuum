# S4 season plan (Claude's plan at the creator's request, 2026-10-10)

**Status:** PLAN. The creator: "I want you to make a plan for all of them. I trust you." Volumes are not chapter-locked. The story dictates; don't compress.
**Time budget:** S4 runs at most **6 months** (about Month 24 to Month 30; `SERIES_TIMELINE_ESTIMATE_2026-10-07.md`).
**Sources:** `S4_CREATOR_IDEAS_2026-10-10.md` (rounds 1–5), `S4_COIN_SUMMONS_SLATE_2026-10-08.md` (with the arrival order), `SYSTEMS_REGISTRY_AND_LOOSE_THREADS_2026-10-10.md`, `S4_V21_PLAN_2026-10-10.md`.

## Shape
- **The first half is family:** peace, the house, the dungeon, the first arrivals.
- **The second half is the world changing:** devils, titans, the closing.
- **Arrivals come one at a time,** each with room to settle (guest, then returns, then chooses space).
- **The world's turn is gradual.** The Goddess's protection covers the settlement and not the roads, so inside stays safe and outside becomes dangerous. Isolation takes the shape of her protection.
