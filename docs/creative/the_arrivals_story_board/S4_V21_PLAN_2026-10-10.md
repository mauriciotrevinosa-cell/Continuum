### S4E11 — Ring Three
30. **A Knock on Their Door.** **Diablo goes to the cabin** that is bigger inside. "Mau is better. Before anything, he wants to know: would you rather talk in the settlement, or should he come to you?" The witches, who expected to be dismissed, have to choose. Treat it seriously, never as a joke, because they take it seriously.
31. **Seven at the Table.** The contract renegotiated.
    - **Greed:** her library, for the settlement.
    - **Daphne:** agriculture administrator (her source story is hunger).
    - The rest want to live there and share daily life.
    - **The quiet one, Envy, is the one the others follow.**
    - Their offer: the session becomes **one hour a week, a tea party instead of an interrogation, with all seven**. Mau agrees, as **a conversation, not an interrogation, because they're going to be family**.
32. **Envy.** She clings to Mau because she wants the love he gives, and tries the shape she once tried with Subaru. **Mau: he can give her a family's love. "And the whole world already thinks we're monsters for what we do, so they already envy us, don't they?"** It sounds like a dumb quip and isn't. Envy laughs, for the first time anyone has heard. The witches are named. Coco starts reading in Greed's library. **Coda:** that night, Ciel: "Looked. Done." Then, once, the Goddess: "*The world is evolving.*"
