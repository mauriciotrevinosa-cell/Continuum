# S4 Volume 21 — plan (proposal for the creator's go-ahead, 2026-10-10)

**Working title:** *Looked. Quiet.* (Ciel's daily phrase). Alternative: *What It Bought*.
**Span:** the first month after the window closes (winter). **Zero arrivals.** Nobody spends a coin.
**Shape:** slice of life first, with ugly days, healing days and fun days (creator rule), ending with Ring Three. Signals of what comes next appear, small and unresolved.
**Rules in force:** DH-041 (synchronisation), DH-046 (nobody saw the Trial), sealed S-01, the coin slate, the S4 ideas rounds 1–5.

## Changes against the older V21 menu (`S4_ARC1` proposal)

- **The real house moves to V22.** In V21, Foundation is *born* (Rimuru's builder story, Ciel's month). It is used and the house is built in V22.

## Chapters (24), in five episodes

### S4E06 — First Snow (ugly days)
1. **The Morning After the Window.** Day 11. Arrival House has no line. Coins sit on the long table. Marin hands out the unclaimed coats: everyone gets one.
2. **Ugly Days.** The body still resets: flinching, hands to the chest, bad nights. Rule five now runs both ways. Frieren.
3. **Looked. Quiet.** Ciel's daily check becomes house texture.
4. **The Washing Court Sings Badly.** Dessa's friend keeps her promise. Mau hears it. One look, nothing resolved (DH-042 echo).

### S4E07 — Healing Days
5. **Has Anyone Decided?** The coins are held.
   - Kaneki has told only Eren the name Hide.
   - Maki's petition stays in her pocket; Yuta's coin is in his sword case.
   - Coco: "until I find out". Kusuri is afraid of a refusal.
   - **Rem says her sister's name for the first time.**
6. **AGENT ____.** The codename gag: Fern earns hers.
7. **Temporary.** A month of Jinshi's "temporary" bed in Maomao's room. The clinic routine. Maomao's notebook, the first seed of the revenge.
8. **Trial Runs.** Fern and Stark. Ori and Milim (does Milim kick?).
9. **Top Bunk.** Eren and Mikasa. What Eren never says (the thirteen years), set up for the lifespan news later.
10. **The Roof, Laughing.** Mau, Rimuru and Yuta. The first time Mau laughs on the roof. **Yuta notices something like Copy** (Analyze is building).

### S4E08 — Fun Days
11. **The Winter Campaign.** Snow forts against the toll booth, the pack at full size, the Milim Beam against snow.
12. **Story Night: The Builder.** For the future movie room, Rimuru tells his life before the slime (Satoru Mikami, the contractor). Ciel: "A temporary skill has appeared. Give me a month." **Foundation is born.**
13. **Operation Sweet Revenge.** In flashback, Maomao's planning: two debts, one batch.
14. **Subjects.** The evening: Frieren and Fern, plus Mau and Stark "by accident". The inhibition effect is real.
15. **Exactly As They Already Wanted.** The reveal: the aphrodisiac was a placebo. Maomao, smug. Debts settled.
16. **Looked. Busy.** Ciel refuses to explain for a whole chapter (she is evolving Foundation).

### S4E09 — The List
17. **The List.** The coin conversation.
    - Forty at once is impossible (the infrastructure), so: who is worth bringing?
    - Yuta's Gojo, and with him a doctor named Shoko to take load off Maomao.
    - **Umaru offers her coin. Seiko's rolled paper. "My pain-in-the-neck granddaughter." Momo's hug.**
    - Armin is proposed, and the house decides to wait. Rem's Ram; Maki's Yuka.
    - Nobody spends a coin this month.
18. **Umaru's Coin.** Her dilemma: her brother, and Mau as her older brother here.
19. **What We Make Together.** In secret, the whole house builds the F1 models: Wakana's precision, Marin's paint, Senku's materials, Rimuru's and Yuta's memory.
20. **Your Hoodie's Team.** The 1988 McLaren and Schumacher's 2004 Ferrari. *You bought us something impossible.*

### S4E10 — Ring Three
21. **Tired of Coming.** The witches want to change the fortnightly contract.
22. **The Library.** **Greed** gives the settlement her library, and Coco starts reading there. One witch becomes **agriculture administrator** (proposal: **Daphne**, Gluttony, whose source story is about hunger; creator to confirm). Some ask only to live there and share daily life; **Envy** envied it.
23. **Names.** The new contract (no spells, no curses). The witches are named for the first time. Ring Three moves in.
24. **Coda: The World Is Evolving.** Night. Ciel: "Looked. Done." (Foundation is ready.) Then, once, the Goddess's voice: "*The world is evolving.*"
