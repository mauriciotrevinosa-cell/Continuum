## Creator decisions, round 7 (2026-10-10)
1. **The first witch is Pride and never gives a proper name.** At the last tea: *"You may call me Pride. The name was for someone smaller."* (This avoids the source's Typhon, a child.)
2. **The weekly tea is the seven witches and Mau.** Others may join occasionally, by invitation, and it is an event when they do. Mostly the house respects that the witches only want to talk with Mau. One name per tea; the first witch's comes last.
3. **Coco reads in Echidna's cabin for her mother:** the undoing of crystal. "Until I find out."
4. **Foundation gets several chapters,** in sections: Ciel's reveal; the table decides; Senku's proposal; Rimuru's architecture; Frieren and the tree; the Witch Hat ink; one breath; repeated failure.
5. **The house:**
   - Senku presents a proposal with concrete, rebar and everything else, having got carried away: "It'll take years to get it all."
   - Rimuru: use **Eastern architecture** (timber frame, joinery, insulation). It needs mostly wood.
   - Everyone puts a spoon in, and Claude cooks. Canon tie: Wakana's fort frame uses no nails, so joinery is his.
6. **Spending a coin:** you hold it, thinking of the person. The Goddess answers simply: *"You are sure? [name]."* If yes, the person arrives on her schedule: the next day, or right then (dealer's choice). **Summoned people appear at Arrival House.**
7. **Seika** arrives from **three days after Nijika's disappearance**: enough fright, without forcing heavy grief. **The Goddess chooses each arrival's moment; time between worlds is not synchronized.**
8. **Nokotan** is added to the franchise pool doc (#44, post-selector). The creator has her in his own library. The deer are a coincidence, and an exception.
