## What this volume must carry (all seconded)

**The coin and the dungeon**
- **Mau's coin and the dungeon.** Mau holds his coin, tries to think of someone from before, and nobody comes. The Goddess: "As you cannot bring a loved one, I will give something that benefits you in the long run." A gate forms near the settlement. **The coin stays warm in his hand.** He can give it or keep it (DH-050 §2).

**How the dungeon works** (creator §4, rounds 2–3)
- It is roughly from Jin-Woo's universe: a gate.
- It sits **just outside the protection line**, because the protection covers the settlement and not the land around it.
- **The shop** is usable only near the gate, by everyone in the settlement. Purchases **persist outside**.
- Healing potions exist only from bosses, at about 10% strength or less.
- **The dungeon changes every week** (normal, double, and so on). It is the only one that does, and the only one with the System.
- **Normal steel does nothing** to its monsters. Magic and cursed energy work, so nobody is left useless. Stark's first purchase is a weapon that works.
- **Crystals** are the currency and the trading good. Anko registers the loot and Fern rations the potions. Some weeks they come back with nothing, and some weeks they don't go, and that's fine.
- **Security:** contracts plus **Rimuru's shadow relay** at the gate, with Mau analyzing. No cameras, and nothing that breaks the world's rules.
- **Senku studies crystals as a power source.** Pushed too far, he gets "repeated failure".

**The queen chapter** (creator §4.1, beat for beat)
1. The dungeon run is Mau, Frieren, Stark and Fern. Frieren loves dungeons.
2. The mimic gag happens three times.
   - Frieren: "Never underestimate the knowledge of someone older than you."
   - Mau: "You forget I'm older than you now."
   - Fern wears her it's-always-like-this face. Stark already knows the show.
3. The fourth chest **is** a mimic. There was a grimoire. "Let's finish the dungeon first." She says nothing.
4. She jumps on Mau's back, piggyback. "You owe me... your punishment for breaking your promise" (the ink tree came early, DH-049). She is queen tonight and tomorrow.
5. Mau: "**If that is my punishment, I will happily oblige.**"
6. Afterwards she announces to everyone that she has a subject.
   - Frieren wears the hoodie. The crown gets made, despite complaints.
   - Umaru tells Eren not to bother the lovebirds.
   - Eren: "As long as she doesn't tell me what to do, I won't complain."
7. **The decrees.** Frieren to Rimuru: "Want to still have a loft? By decree of the queen, there shall be changes." Fern scolds her. Rimuru: "Not because you're queen, but you gave me an idea." The rooms expand.
   - Foundation was single-use, so the expansion is **by hand, in stages**: the west wing goes up.
   - Umaru demands a gigantic lounge and is stopped.
8. **Umaru asks for a temporary room for someone**: her brother, before she has decided. She wants the room to exist so the choice exists.

**Ram** (creator §7, DH-050 §3)
- She comes by Rem's coin, the second sister, a mirror of Seika. There is **nothing of Roswaal**.
- At first Frieren supplies her mana. Mau walks in on a session, and they just talk.
- Yuta's outward RCT repairs the horn partly, so mana goes from daily to weekly. It is **never fully repaired**.
- When Ram arrives, Rem is again the Rem from after the assassination attempts: kind, quick with a quip, 100% herself.
- **PENDING (creator):** which point in her story Ram comes from (see the question below).

**Threats and Ring Four**
- **The first devil** is born from the fear of Otherworlders. It is small, and **Mikasa and Eren kill it on the road**. Nobody thinks much of it.
- **The first Ring Four departure:** a refuge family leaves for the river towns after the road scare. The cottage stands empty, and the gap shows.

**Presence**
- **Seika settles in.** She builds a stage out of pallets in the music room, as she once did in a basement.
- **Echidna and the songbook:** at a weekly tea, Bocchi plays the dead person's love song from Seika's first night.
