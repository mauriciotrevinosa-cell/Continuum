## Source check: *Modulo* (read 2026-10-10, extracted to scratch on the creator's request, Vault unchanged)
- **Who she is.** Yuka Okkotsu is **Yuta and Maki's granddaughter**, with an older brother, Tsurugi. Maki died first, when Yuka was a child. Yuta died when Yuka was ten, and she was "Grandpa's little girl".
- **The ring.** When she was six, Yuta gave her his ring on a cord ("This ring will watch over you until you're a full-fledged sorcerer"). After his death, the family took it as a cursed object registered to the Gojo clan's warehouse and gave it to Tsurugi ("only Tsurugi is suited").
- **How she fights.** Martial arts (Keishoken) with cursed properties, and loud. She hid an illness, fought an alien alone in Tokyo, and accepted a duel.
- **The end.** She is **well** ("I'm so glad you're okay now, Yuka." / "In exchange, Dabura had to leave"). Her mother says "Family is important." The Simurians call humans family.
- **The creator's slate** (stands): she is the Yuka from the end, after the tumor; **she brings the ring** (a creator choice); Mau's Noise-blink to the ring; the elf's glare; "el oppa stoppa"; "Mau? Mau?"; she and Mau become siblings; Frieren is jealous; **the Ten Shadows** (creator, round 3).

He counts the embers in the hearth without meaning to. The house laughs the first time and not the third. Ciel detects something.
