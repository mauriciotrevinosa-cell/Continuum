## What V25 carries (all seconded)
- **Things that come back** (moved here from V24, DH-066).
  - The devil, back bigger (Anko's page four).
  - Devils start small and escalate, and the house only slowly learns that killing one is not the end (Chainsaw Man rules, discovered).
  - **Titans.** Rimuru analyzes them: not like Eren, nothing new behind them. The house assumes the Goddess brought them from Eren's world, and they stay a mystery.
  - **Eren can age:** there are no Paths here, so no thirteen-year clock. **Mikasa: the turn**, at their pace.
- **Mau and Rem, both ways** (creator, 2026-10-11).
  - The teapot reversal. "Why did you stay?" Rem is distrustful by nature (Re:Zero), which is exactly why her staying means something.
  - She recognizes the Trial in his face. The pencil stays.
- **The merchant route** (round 3).
  - The southern merchant who stayed partners with the house. A route for their goods, guarded because monsters are increasing. He hires warriors and shares the profits.
  - Not every bridge burned; everyone is more careful.
- **The mastermind shows a face** (round 2 §12; round 6 §5). One face, in a town to the east, saying the words. Later it proves to be a puppet; not here.
- **Coco's mother** (the creator's 13-step arc, §6), after real room (DH-064).
  - Coco decided "not until Fern says, not unless he wants to, find another way first" (V24 #36). She brings her mother anyway, without asking Mau, and it is Mau who feels the wheel volunteer.
  - The first controlled adaptation, 8 turns. "If I held a path for nineteen minutes, I can hold five" (19, not 36: round 2 §9).
  - A skill in Rimuru. "You used me. I'm going to use you back." / "Now who is cheating on who?"
  - The next day is Claude's to write.
- **Byakuya** (Senku's coin): helps Senku; nothing revolves around him.
- **Jiji** (S-01, sealed): cold, then soup, then nothing. Write from the sealed file only.
- **All the Years** (Day 212, DH-099): a breakfast remark leads Mau to choose an afternoon account for the coin arrivals. He tells them the Trial's conditions, years and cost himself; receiving a coin does not make them indebted to him.
- **Pressure builds, and Eren proposes closing the city.** That is V26's door.
- **Ring Four drains** (DH-048): one refuge family leaves after the titans. Never a crowd.
