## Placement (Claude, verified against the written timeline)

- **V29, Days 328–331.** This is the only free gap after the Sukuna truce (Day 286) and the first tower floors. Floor 10 is Day 327; the next chapter is the roof on Day 333. Before it the climb is slow (floors 4–10 over Days 295–327), and after it V30 opens with the festival and the first points session (Day 338), when the climb accelerates. The incident becomes the catalyst for going all out, not only "the dungeon says his name" (V28 "A Name He Didn't Want").
- It becomes E37 chapters 1–6. "Five on the Roof" and "The Last Firefly" follow, with small additions acknowledging what happened (inserting earlier is fine). V31's night-shift speech gains one line about it.

## Choices made under the creator's delegation ("te dejo que decidas")

- **"Let go", not stronger** (creator's clarification). It is the Day 145 kind of event ("Everything at Once", S4 V23): the walls open and what is in them comes out, aimed this time. There is no new power and no system stat. The left eye (the ghoul valve) goes black and red. Moving "without moving" is an unplanned leak jump (CANON_TRAPS: jumps are leaks, not earned teleportation).
- **The group:** four demons (Frieren's source kind, horned, speech as a lure; the house met two on Day 241–242) and one large devil at the gate, plus one devil hidden in the woods, the cutter.
- **The order** comes from "the new union of empires and otherworlders", against "the monsters of that settlement — the one who makes monsters, shadows, suns". Its identity, leaders and any tie to Sukuna, the hooded man or Pell stay OPEN (nations seed).
- **The cut** goes through Frieren's defensive barrier as if the barrier were not there. Behind it, a long stretch of forest falls in neat rows, the way the rumours said a city was once cut. Mau relaxed for one second because he saw her barrier go up; that second is his guilt. Nobody names what the cutter is. Shoko's healing can hold Frieren alive but cannot close the wound ("it's like the cut is still happening"). Mau's rough, borrowed reverse technique, steadied by Shoko, closes it, leaving a small scar on her chest.
- **Brutality:** completely one-sided. The cutter devil is cut apart, cut for cut. Three demons go to ash, one of them mid-word ("the elf was weak—"). The large devil is crushed. One demon is left alive with the message ("if anyone so much as raises an eyebrow at her again… everything they love, flat"), and it runs before he can change his mind.
- **The house** feels the pressure and the tremor the way it did on Day 145. Gojo and Yuta hold the lime by the old leak protocol. Rimuru goes because Ciel says Mau is not answering.
- **Frieren** is unconscious for about two days (Days 328–330). Mau does not move, sleep or eat; Ori tells him at least to sleep. She wakes on Day 330 at dawn with her hand on her chest and says she is all right. He breaks ("I'm sorry", over and over, his hand shaking). She says "I'm hungry", and he carries her down to breakfast.
