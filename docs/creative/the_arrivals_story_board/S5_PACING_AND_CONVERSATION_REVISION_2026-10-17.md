## Continuation standard

Shorter reading chapters are a reading preference, not a word ceiling, a five-chapter quota or permission to deliver an outline in dialogue. Keep a difficult conversation together until it has actually moved. Give information time to change behaviour before the next demand. Do not make everyone immediately produce a flawless supportive answer. Pronouns, implied subjects and cryptic replies must not hide who knows what or what question was answered. Let ordinary scenes last beyond stating that they occurred.
