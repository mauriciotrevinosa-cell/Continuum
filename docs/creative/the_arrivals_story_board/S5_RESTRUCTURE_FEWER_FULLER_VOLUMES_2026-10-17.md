# S5 restructure — fewer, fuller volumes with breathing chapters (creator + Claude, 2026-10-17)

**Status:** CREATOR-APPROVED DIRECTION ("va lo pensaste perfecto… tú eres mi co-escritora"). Claude leads the restructure. **Any session or tool writing S5 prose (including Codex): read this first and follow the new numbering.**

## The creator's intent
- S5 chapters stay **short on purpose**: about half an S4 chapter (~1,300–1,600 words). Splitting into "pt1 / pt2" is welcome.
- But the **story must breathe like S4**. S4 volumes had about 30 long chapters (~94–104k words). The creator expected an S5 volume to have **~50–60 short chapters**, not 25–35.
- **S5 is the whole summer** (about four months outside, with room to grow) and should be **a smaller season in number of volumes**: merge the thin volumes, then fill them with breathing chapters. No new plot is needed, only lived life.

## Mechanics
- Volume membership lives only in chapter headers (`# … Volume N`, `**Volume:** N`) and the volume index files. The project library ignores volumes. Episode codes (S5Exx) and filenames stay unchanged, except where a chapter is split or a breather is inserted.
- When merged, old V28 chapters continue V27's reading order (#30 onward), and so on.
- **Breathers** are new short chapters inserted at natural gaps. Use the same filename convention: digit-only indices, renumber within the episode, `_PART_2` slug for splits. Each opens with an italic *When:* line. A breather may be titled normally or as an interlude; it carries no new plot, power or canon decision.
- **Breather content:** what the rest of the house is doing; the 12 hours from other points of view; the minutes Mau is inside the door; longer recovery comedy; ordinary days of underused cast (Kessoku/Bocchi, Mai and Vamola, Kusuri's group, the witches, Marin and Wakana, Coco's mother, Gojo and Shoko, Hollis/Brenna/Cobb, Jinshi and the register girl). Follow the cast ledger and presence rules. Never place a character without being sure they belong there.
