## Direction captured (creator)

- **The tower is the Architect's trial for Mau, and the early floors make that clear.**
  - At first the companions feel safe and Mau barely fights.
  - At floor 10 the boss stops, ejects everyone, and a notice says the dungeon belongs to an *operator*.
  - Maki may still help most at 10, but the companions learn they cannot let Mau do nothing.
- **After the north road (Day 328):** Mau climbs almost solo to 30. There Stark, Maki and Kaneki step in to help because they see the change.
- **The breathers and outings are the house's response** to seeing him becoming "the strongest": the loneliness, which nobody wants. He is traumatised because what he always believed was the Trial nearly happened for real. At some point he stops going to Shoko before reporting.
- **Extraction at most guardians** (not all), aiming for Jin-Woo's re-evaluation scale of 100+ shadows.
  - Tusk is missing.
  - The Warden should be extracted, or reconsidered.
  - No guardian is a "simple boss".
  - Source scale matters: Kamish (a continent-coast catastrophe, stopped only by people lent the Rulers' power), Bellion (second in command of the Shadow Monarch's army), Beru (killed and maimed many S-ranks).
- **Beru needs the whole bag** and an extraordinary lock-in, for example removing the Six Eyes filters 100%. Mau comes out torn, as if something tried to shred him alive. The house sees him killing himself for something and cannot stop him, because he feels better.
- **Floor 100 is a Ruler.** Instead of a shadow, Mau gets a puzzle piece: a boost, e.g. the Ruler's Authority (Jin-Woo's invisible hand, distinct from Momo's).
- **Kamish:** the party size changes and the dungeon ejects everyone but Mau (it was the Architect; nobody knew then). Mau becomes a *true dragon*, which nearly kills him, and wins. The others find him on the floor bleeding, with Kamish as a shadow protecting him.
- **The Architect is gigantic.** He can switch off Mau's system buff; the Noise takes time to block it, so Mau fights without the body to bear it, on a timer before his body gives out.
- **"What happens after 101 is S6"**, where the creator plans to take things higher. Its scope is to be confirmed (see the questions in chat).
- **The creator invites larger choreography and longer sequences** so that each guardian is a true test with consequences, not a Solo Leveling boss "for his army".
