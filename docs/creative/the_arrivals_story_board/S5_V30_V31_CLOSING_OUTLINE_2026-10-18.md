## Decisions taken under the creator's delegation

1. **Kaneki's second coin comes from Mau.** It is Mau's warm Trial coin, kept since S4: "To keep. Or to give." (DH-050). The ledger seed reads "Mau's warm coin 'to give'". Precedent: Seiko gave her coin to Gojo, and it brought Shoko.
   - Kaneki holds the petition at the kilns, at eleven, on the night shift.
   - The voice says two names, *You are sure? Touka.* and *You are sure? Ichika.*, and pours the second cup at last.
   - They come from his world, some time after he vanished. Touka hits him first (the callback is "She hit me first").
   - Food uses Maomao's existing arrangement for Kaneki, "not elegant".
   - The half-built next room at the end of the west wing becomes the Kaneki family's room. Under Rimuru's rule, a new empty room is chalked again.
2. **Armin arrives early in V31** by **Mikasa's coin**; Eren keeps his own coin. He comes from his own world at a time after Eren and Mikasa were both gone from it, and he has been looking for them. His world's wider history is **not specified**. Nobody here asks him to be a soldier. His own thing is maps: Anko's board, Hollis's routes and the north field, with Senku and Fern. Fifth arrival: none.
3. **Tower structure.**
   - Transfer points sit every ten floors from 10: 20, 30 … 90. You can start from any one already secured.
   - Floors get larger, the materials get rarer, and each floor takes more work. Not every floor gets a scene: Anko's board summarises.
   - Named shadow minibosses are **copies**. The floor notice names each guardian, which is how Mau knows the names.

   | Floor | Shadow | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | 20 | Igris (red knight) | Speaks seldom; formal, few words |
   | 30 | Tank (great bear) | No speech |
   | 40 | Beru (ant king) | Speaks lavishly, devoted |
   | 50 | Kaisel (wyvern) | Mount; flight with load and duration limits |
   | 70 | Kamish (dragon) | Speaks; the hardest fight |
   | 90 | Bellion (the general, sword-whip) | Formal, commanding |

   - **Fang is not met in S5**: still a candidate, not erased.
   - The Red Gate dragon is **named Lantern by Ori** (V30). It is distinct from Kaisel and Kamish.
   - Common soldiers grow with reserve capacity.
4. **Mau's points.** He spends points for the first time on Day 338, with Umaru and Yuka, into Intelligence. That deepens the branch's shadow reserve, measured by Ciel. It is **not** mana, CE or a Black Heart. He keeps some points unspent.
   - Registration places stay at ten until the interface itself changes. It grows with level: 20 at floor 50, more at 100.
5. **Wedding trigger (R50–R57).**
   - **Day 345, at the long table.** Seiko makes the comment ("when you two marry"). Mau chokes and Frieren blushes; Himmel is present. The Trial wedding memory then hits Mau and he has a panic attack.
   - **Mau sends Frieren to Himmel.** She sees Gojo, Rimuru and Yuta, and they go with Mau.
   - **The tower trip.** The four go to the cleared garden terrace on floor 5, owner plus three. Diablo and Fern hold the house, and they announce it before crossing. Friends tell him: it was the Trial, she's alive, everyone is here, "it won't happen here" (offered as their conviction, not a guarantee).
   - **The private talk.** Frieren/Himmel talk in parallel. Later, in private, Frieren asks if he has thought about a wedding. Mau: not opposed, but he has to finish digesting the memory he kept putting away.
6. **Himmel's chosen distance.** He offers to stand Mikasa's north-end hour so that Eren and Mikasa can have their mornings, which uses Himmel without pausing his life. He returns visibly in V31 with tea and a pose (R53).
7. **The Beru gag** goes exactly per the creator: Beru wants Frieren's place beside Mau; Frieren takes the one free shot; Zoltraak; Mau remakes him; Beru apologises to "the queen of my monarch"; Igris and the others look at Mau. Mau: "She's the one in charge." Frieren: "Correct."
   - **Setting:** the north field at dusk, inside the lime, shot aimed at the sky.
8. **Jin-Woo fragments** come through the shadow, privately, days apart:
   - V30: "Hello." Then nothing.
   - V30, later: "Who are you?" Mau answers.
   - V31: "How did you get…", cut off.
   - Mau tells Ciel, Rimuru and Frieren only.
9. **Floor 100.** It gives the **components** of the divine potion, not a finished potion. No recipient yet; they go into Senku's NOT YET box under Maomao's eye.
   - A notice says The Noise's extraction eligibility has widened (otherworlders, monarchs). **Mau does not activate it.**
10. **The Architect (V31 end).**
   - **The fight.** After a rest, a hall beyond 100 opens: the statues test lessons the tower taught (near end first; stand where a teacher stands; leave a little in reserve).
   - **The Black Heart.** The Architect is cornered and delighted: "Yes. He will work." He forces the Black Heart in at a clear instant, which Mau does not consent to. It is a second structure whose use is still unknown, not infinite fuel and not Ashborn.
   - **The escape.** He escapes into the world.
   - **Afterwards.** That night Frieren hears two heartbeats, and nobody knows what they mean.
   - **The Sukuna scene (reader only).** Sukuna finds the Architect speaking aloud about returning his monarch: "To bring him back, you'd have to take everything from that boy. I can help with that."
   - S5 ends with Mau's shadows taking a first night shift, so that Eren/Mikasa and Anko/Nazuna have time, and with two heartbeats.
11. **Tobacco** keeps real pacing. In V31 (~Day 375) the lower leaves are primed and hung to dry ("one moon"), so Shoko's first smoke "from here" falls in S6. Her cigarette stays unlit.
