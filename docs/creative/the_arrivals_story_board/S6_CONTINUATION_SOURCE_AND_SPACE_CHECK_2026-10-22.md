## 4. Goddess contact and Red Gate — recommended bounded mechanism

Established:
- S6 *One Hour* / *Coats* restore Ciel inside the Red Gate because the thief's outside wall cannot reach there. Contact to Rimuru/outside remains cut. S5 first Red Gate used the same inside/outside distinction.
- After the return, Ciel can reach both hosts again; information is not automatically broadcast to everyone.
- Goddess has spoken privately to Mau in the exterior world before; in *A Really Good Cookie* others hear only his half and ask who he was addressing. Known Trial authorship does not make private conversations public.

**Do not give the Goddess unearned access to every Red Gate merely so she can quote his private gate speech.** The original seed's “I heard what you said in the Red Gate” needs a cause or an adjustment, not an omniscience assumption.

Recommended staging for parent to consider:
1. Put the first renewed contact in the ordinary exterior world before the final date/acceptance. Mau himself reports what he chose and what he feared in the gate, in a conversation with the Goddess. She can respond to what he voluntarily tells her now.
2. Her explanation becomes that she considered intervening but now recognises his wish to preserve his present bond—not proof she heard across the sealed boundary. If she refers to listening, make the object the exterior conversation actually written.
3. Final terms and the explicit loss of Yuka's confidencia come after the last-night disclosure, before acceptance. The initial contact establishes possibility, not a final cost already accepted without that knowledge.
4. No need to identify the third access as her channel, give Ciel an outside relay during the gate, give her general mind-reading, or undo the gate's barrier rules. The original seed is flexible about staging; if a verbatim gate-listening claim is retained, parent should seek an explicit creator mechanism rather than infer it.

This is a proposal, not a retroactive event or a statement that contact has already happened. It preserves the later Goddess help and cost while avoiding a new source of universal surveillance.
