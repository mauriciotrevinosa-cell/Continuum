## Practical floor for a next training scene

These are editorial inferences from the produced baseline, not new canon or a mandatory training prescription:

- There is enough precedent for a **smaller/cleaner/repeatable start and stop**, sensory filtering at the existing limit, an existing small Red/Blue task, or another bounded outer-access test. Better control need not mean higher maximum output or new stat spending.
- Keep57 held unless the scene actually chooses and records a new allocation. If it does spend, distinguish the new increments from unknown historical totals.
- Preserve old hand/eye/scar/hair changes. Measure a functional difference through an action rather than announce an invented recovery percentage.
- Keep resources distinct. Neither a partner's outlet nor the Black Heart grants another free reserve; no reason to deliberately overflow merely to demonstrate progress.
- For a truly new technique such as Ryu's beam, intimidation, Monarch's Domain or sustained own RCT, first establish what Noise has actually built, what can be opened, its fuel/body/control conditions and a bounded test. Neither source familiarity nor a blank System field supplies those steps.
- Do not silently convert a proposal in the October16 roster into current canon: that document explicitly identifies its proposed S5 endpoints and contains historical rows superseded by later prose (including old shadow standing-drain wording).
