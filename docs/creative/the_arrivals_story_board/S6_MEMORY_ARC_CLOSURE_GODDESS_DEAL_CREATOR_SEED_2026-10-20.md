## Captured (creator)

1. **The breaking point.** Tension climbs until the house splits: people hurt by mistake; some blame Yuka, some Mau, some everyone; others blame Frieren. It must actually explode.
2. **Before the deal,** alone with Mau, Yuka says she wishes she could go back to not feeling this. Back to saying "Oppa" without thinking about what she feels, because she only ever wanted him as someone to play with and lean on.
3. **After the explosion,** Yuka is alone with Mau, crying: "It's my fault, it's my fault." He holds her in silence.
4. **Only he hears the Goddess.** He pretends nothing is happening, but listens.
5. **Her offer: she can fix everything.** Not time travel. Tomorrow, people will remember things differently: not what happened between Mau and Yuka, but other versions of the same scenes. The Red Gate is remembered another way.
6. **Her price: two things.**
   - **First:** Mau will remember all of it, both Frieren and Yuka, and feel his love for both as intensely as he does now. Yuka won't know. He will have to live loving two people, and decide for himself what to do about it.
   - **The change happens** once everyone is asleep.
   - **Second:** "I don't know yet. Maybe another trial. Or as simple as a conversation."
7. **Mau accepts.** He looks at Yuka, still crying, kisses her once more, tells her not to worry, he'll fix everything, and to sleep for tonight.
8. **Everyone sleeps.** Even Nazuna on the stake: the Goddess put everyone to sleep except him. He hears: "This is going to be fun." Mau laughs: "For who?"
9. **Cut to breakfast.**
   - Anko scolds Nazuna for falling asleep on the stake. Nazuna says she doesn't remember, and the shadows were on watch anyway.
   - Yuka is talking with Ori about something.
   - Mau and Frieren hold hands.
   - Mau is quieter than usual, wondering what the second price will be. He can't tell anyone what happened.

## Claude's notes (seconded side by side, not decided)

**What makes it work:**
- **The cost lands on him,** which is the series' grammar: he takes the scars so nobody else has to. Here it is the scar nobody can see.
- **Continuity correction, creator DH-134 (2026-10-22):** the earlier editorial "who built the Trial?" hook was invalid: the house already knows the Goddess made it. V32 ch1 now revisits what she wanted Mau to learn. This proposed deal may resonate with that concern about her methods and intentions; it is not proof of the Trial's purpose or a Goddess/Architect alliance. The proposed deal and its second-price hook remain as captured below/above; no additional revelation is approved here.
- **It doesn't force an ending.** Mau loving two people, and deciding, stays open.

**Things to settle before writing:**
- **It breaks his oldest promise.** He decides alone, tells no one, and changes everyone's memories without their consent: the "tell first" rule the house built over seasons. That shouldn't be smoothed over. It's the hardest thing he has ever chosen alone, and it should cost him later (if anyone finds out, that's the scene). Writing it as a knowing relapse keeps it honest.
- **Who keeps the truth?** Ciel lives inside him; Rimuru heard everything (Volume 33 ch18); the shadows (Beru, Igris, Kamish) saw the gate; Daphne reads memory-eating and may notice a rewrite. Does the Goddess's change reach them? Recommendation: Ciel remembers, because she's part of him, so he isn't completely alone. The rest are the creator's call.
- **Does the deal restore what the thief took?** Presumably yes. She was angry ("He cheated"), and "you will remember everything" implies Frieren comes back to him. Worth stating on the page.
- **Yuka:** the rewrite also returns her feelings to "Oppa", which is her own wish (her one consent in the scene). Does any trace remain (the black dog, the Mahoraga, the book of rules)? They keep the powers; only the story around them changes.
- **The breaking point needs a mechanism.** How does the secret leak? (Rimuru breaking under it? Beru's tongue? The hand-holding seen? Yuka confessing?) Who gets hurt by mistake, and how? This is the next thing to plan.
- **Timing:** not fixed. It can sit wherever the arc earns it.
