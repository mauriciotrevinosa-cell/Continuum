## Critical distinctions

1. **Yuka's small technical book is not an established romantic diary.** S6E16 CH02 (*Nine*) explicitly shows ritual rules, tested exceptions and the two shikigami lists. S6E20 CH02 shows Yuta reading the ritual diagrams and making another drawing. No existing prose inspected establishes an entry about a kiss, sex, a date, cancer or her private guilt. Do not invent those pages as convenient evidence.
2. **There are two different bracelets on Yuka.** The System band is not the personal braided cord with the mirrored-lotus charm. The personal gift is ordinary jewellery: no emitted light, tracking, binding vow or built-in memory.
3. Memory changes do not by themselves prove objects, clinical measurements, receipts, shared skill results or bodily injuries changed. If the eventual creator-approved terms need to alter a specific physical record, establish that scope explicitly rather than silently deleting it.
4. A surviving object is not automatically proof of the secret romance. A birthday gift, a route drawing or a teammate's coat can remain compatible with a known sibling-like bond or an expedition. Only contents actually observed count.
5. Shadows preserve only what they witnessed or learned. A guard deliberately out of earshot cannot recite the couple's private future-family conversation. Ciel's private knowledge is real but is not automatically a public written archive.
