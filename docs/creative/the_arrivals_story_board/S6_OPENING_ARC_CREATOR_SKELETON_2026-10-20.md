## Captured skeleton (creator)

**0. S5 seed.** Beru can heal by more than eating. Keep it locked through S5; revisit in S6.

**1. Breather (short; don't stretch time).**
- A debrief of what the Architect explained, and of the second heart.
- One chapter: a day for Mau. The house understands he was tested again, through his trauma, with something pulling at him that gave his trauma what it wanted. Other things are exploiting him; the Goddess becomes a real question.

**2. The overload (a couple of chapters).** It shows the house now adapts to emergencies and new unions.
- A calm day of planning. Umaru asks about his page; he starts to explain and sees something syncing his CE and mana.
- Nothing too grave. Ciel detects it, talks to the Noise, asks for its help, and they move the overflow into the Black Heart.
- It ends calmly.

**3. A new system (people are tired of living in a jar).**
- **Shadow scouts on a rota.** Mau can now see through them. He sends a scout pack to check for more titans past the ridge. Nobody argues: they're free, and he only loses something if one is destroyed.
- **The first power-lending contract,** with Rimuru's help.
- **A map with Ciel:** Mau parks shadows in monsters' shadows, so the house always has their location.
- **The protection area and the emergency plan.** Creator's preferred version is that Mau shares the idea openly. A **Shadow Exchange, improved by the Noise,** sends the people near him to safety while he stays. If something corners them, Mau does the Exchange; when the cooldown ends he follows, or stays if he can handle it. They trust him to decide in extreme cases.
- **A week of calm,** while the world is about to attack.
- **The north road's message, told now:** how Mau attacked them and what words he left. When the army arrives and recites Mau's threat back, it lands.

**4. The union's attack (choreography).**
- **Betty gets through** because of her contract with Subaru: when one was pulled, the contract pulled the other. (Creator: "y el evil eye no". Meaning to confirm.)
- **Hybrids: the International Assassins arc.** A real danger, adjusted to this world: here the Gun Devil would be "more devil than an apple" because only two (devils) exist. Mods: Katana Man fits; add a new sorcerer hybrid.
- **The Goddess bet stands.** When Mau starts helping them, he puts away the wheel and everything.
- **The soldiers:** Mau repeats what he did with the demons ("nobody move"), to the soldiers, not the otherworlders. When one moves, Mau knocks him out without moving, Diablo-style.
- **Goddess:** she didn't know. She doesn't intervene. She can say "He cheated", meaning the memory thief, not Mau.
- **The union stays a mystery, worse than before:** they know something is gathering, and it has already attacked.

**5. After the theft.**
- Mau gets up; nobody holds him. He sees the cookies and remembers the deal with the Goddess.
- Frieren comes to hug him. He teleports and evades her, then asks who she is.
- He sees Yuka and teleports straight to her: "Did you see me fight? I won two cookies. One for you, because you love sweets."
- Ori tries to greet him; he answers her as "Yuka's friend". He does recognise people from the house, because he lived with them, but no longer holds them the way he did.

**6. Yuka's 18th birthday.**
- Her gift request to the house, made **before the war:** a day with her Oppa, sibling-style (like Frieren's day). Nobody objected. This is the first change.
- A group celebration, then she goes with Mau.
- **His changed memories changed his control:** some things got better, some worse. Flying now seems mastered. Before she can say anything he picks her up and flies off. She goes red.
- "Where do you want to go?" Without thinking: "The beach." They stop at a market for swimsuits. Mau gives her a lovely date that nobody calls a date. He brings her back at night.
- Maki and Yuta tear into him for not telling anyone.

**7. Meanwhile at the house.**
- The Subaru scenes (the witches, Rem and Ram) play out.
- Frieren wonders why he said he didn't know her, but with the birthday there has been no time to talk. Ori is hurt, with more questions than anger. Both think Mau is teasing them.
- **His false account:** he arrived after Yuka; the gate was there; the bond began in the Red Gate. He remembers waking, exploring, hearing stories, and walking to the settlement.

**8. The house realises.**
- He treats them like Taihei does: friendly and polite. Asked why he doesn't treat them as family: he has only known them a little while. **This is where it starts.**
- Mau flees Frieren, maybe tells her flat out to stay away, that he doesn't know her.

**9. Nights in Yuka's room.**
- He sleeps on the floor of Yuka's room, not with her. Casually, he takes stored blankets: "That's been our arrangement since the Red Gate. Am I taking too much floor?" No.
- They talk all night. She tells him her fears: what happens if her brother comes.
- At some point she has moved down to lie beside him, without remembering doing it. With him asleep, she feels something she may have kept for a long time: "Please, no. He's Oppa." He doesn't wake (he doesn't remember the Trial).
- The Oppa / Tsurugi / coin beat ("a bit more" around it).

**10. Into the second Red Gate.**
- At night Mau tells Yuta and Maki he agreed to an hour in the normal dungeon with Yuka: she heard there's a quiet garden and asked him along. They agree.
- It turns into a Red Gate. They look at each other and laugh: "What bad luck," at the same time.
- Mau pulls Yuka against his chest; she blushes. Before she can speak: "We have to move." He caught an ice arrow in his hand, aimed where her head was a second ago.
- **New notice:** inside this Red Gate he has an inventory and access to the shop for the whole period. The gate has multiple levels; it won't open until completed; each level requires beating its boss.

**11. Slice of life inside.**
- They buy things (coats, etc.) with the settlement's crystals, to be repaid. Scouting shadows go out.
- **Yuka's conflict:** she knows he feels something for her. She knows he loved Frieren before. She doesn't know if, because he doesn't remember Frieren, this is what could have been, and she hates that thought.

**12. The night under the stars.**
- The scouts are hunting the level boss. Yuka sits closer than usual. Mau looks at her; her heart is more mixed than anything.
- He kisses her. Before he can apologise she pulls him in and kisses him. Something neither of them had done takes over; she isn't thinking, she just wants him close.
- They don't stop until their shirts come off. **Then she feels all the wounds,** and knows he took them to protect them, because he couldn't bear to see any of them hurt.
- She cries. He stops and apologises in case he misread everything. She says no: it's his hand, and she remembered everything.
- He knows pieces are missing. "It's all right. If you want, we don't do anything. We just talk."
- She explains the tower. Cut. By now he knows he had a relationship with the elf, and it doesn't matter: he is with Yuka.
- (Supersedes the earlier version, where the jacket comes off and she stops at the ruined hand.)

**13. Training together.**
- Contracts and fighting together. Mau convinces Yuka to make a contract where he lends her an ability and his body carries the weight. They get good together, inseparable in a fight.
- **Taming shikigami, both.** Two mechanics:
  - Mau inside the summoning radius can destroy the shikigami without ending the ritual.
  - Only if something happens to Yuka can he step in or distract, without breaking the rules.
  - She coaches him; he lends her tools so she tames them cleanly.

**14. Mahoraga (the last floor, for the love of the game).**
- With all nine tamed, Mahoraga. By now they may be on the last floor, doing it for fun, with no one to hurt.
- Mau gives Yuka access to Igris, Beru and Kamish: Igris distracts, Beru protects, Kamish charges the fire that annihilates anything. Mau makes a Purple in the air as distraction and emergency.
- They beat Mahoraga twice, hers and his, in two minutes, and laugh.
- Yuka: they are the second and third in the whole history of the Ten Shadows to tame it, and without Sukuna the first with the technique. Mau: "You're the first. Mine is a copy." She laughs. He steals another kiss.
- **Mau:** "I know I had a past with someone, and I don't remember it. I don't know what would happen if I remembered. But I don't want to. I'm so happy with you I don't want to lose this."

**15. The wheel lesson (against the dungeon's boss).**
- Two ways: Mahoraga takes the hit and your wheel adapts, or you let him adapt.
- Recommended: summon and dismiss. Let him take the phenomenon's impact without being destroyed (even one bit left, he adapts). Always think about that before calling him. Once your wheel and his sync, put him away and use the others, or "my techniques if we have a contract".
- **Agito is tameable here, not a fusion.**
- **Yuka's second dog:** through the contract the Noise reached her without either noticing, leaving the option for the future.

**16. Clear and exit.**
- They beat the boss; clear. Behind them, Mau's shadows carry all the loot they can.
- They hold hands. Yuka: "Once we're back, we hide everything we did. We only talk about it when it's you and me."
- The waiting house sees a pack of shadows come out with a flood of crystals and bodies Mau hasn't arisen yet, then Mau and Yuka, talking about how they tamed the shikigami.
- Yuta, Maki and even Mai are worried; they were in there a good while. "You won't believe what we did."
- **South field, before the debrief:** a choreography they planned for a while, showing each shikigami. The dogs first (Maki sees Yuka has two), one by one to the second-to-last. Everyone thinks they've stopped. Then both summon, and two Mahoragas stand behind them, each with their own wheel. They look at each other ("it worked") and laugh. **The skeleton stops here.**

## Claude's notes (to reconcile when drafting)

- **Gojo's taming rule (S5 V31 "Three Doors"):** "If somebody helps, it doesn't count." The creator's mechanics (Mau in the radius, intervening only if Yuka is endangered, coaching, lending through a contract) need to be shown as a **discovered loophole**, so the rule bends instead of breaking. For example, a contract makes lent power hers. Or Gojo was right about solo help, and a contracted pair counts as one summoner.
- **Yuka's choice to hide it** goes past the moment she stopped. She keeps kissing him and asks to hide it, knowing about Frieren. That is a real failing for her to answer for later, not harmless. Writing it as her failing keeps the creator's rule intact (she never set out to win him; the pursuit came from Mau) and gives "Yuka tells" its weight later.
- **Yuka is the Ten Shadows** per the creator (Zen'in blood). She has the White Divine Dog (female). The "second dog" is the black one. Mau's Ear stays his and distinct.
- **The 134 points:** S5 ends with "DON'T TOUCH ANYTHING" until the Noise finishes. The overload completes the integration first, so spending in the gate is consistent.
- **The army recites Mau's threat:** this ties to DH-123 (the north road, one demon left alive with the message).
- **To confirm:** "el evil eye" (why Betty gets through and the evil eye doesn't); which two devils exist in this world; the sorcerer hybrid's name and concept.
