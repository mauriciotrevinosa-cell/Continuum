# S6 opening: plan and questions for the creator (2026-10-20)

**Purpose:** a single page that gathers everything decided for S6 so far, up to the second Red Gate's aftermath, plus the questions the creator answers before any prose. Sources: [integration seed](S6_SYSTEM_INTEGRATION_RETROACTIVE_OVERLOAD_CREATOR_SEED_2026-10-18.md), [union army and the Goddess's bet](S6_UNION_ARMY_AND_THE_GODDESS_BET_CREATOR_SEED_2026-10-19.md), [stolen memories](S6_STOLEN_MEMORIES_CREATOR_SEED_2026-10-19.md), DH-122 to DH-130.

Creator's plan (2026-10-20): "llego a kamish y de ahi armas lo de s6 … me encargo de ver bien por lo menos el arco de inicio entre mau y yuka acabamos con el after de la red gate y ya cuando tengas otra ves creditos acabamos s6".

## Skeleton (decided order)

1. **Opening breather** (series rule: seasons connect with a breather). It starts from the V31 facts log, Day 410 and after. Yuka's 18th birthday must land **inside** the memory arc, so it is not here.
2. **The overload (short).** The Noise integrates everything at once and the 440 Intelligence hits CE and mana retroactively. His body overloads like a battery. Ciel and the Noise repair it together, and Ciel pours all the excess into the Black Heart's empty bar.
3. **The union's attack.**
   - About 20,000 soldiers. The front group is otherworlders: Subaru and Betty (lied to: "Mau holds Rem and Ram"), Thomas Andre or similar, the hybrids (to be figured out). Behind them come devils, demons and CE monsters (Culling Game vibe, no Geto).
   - Before the fight: the house is moved to the ridge with a note ("If I can run, I run. The Exchange is cooling"), and Rimuru asks Mau not to kill.
   - The talk with the representative. The wheel turns; the Noise says "Maybe." The Goddess: "How interesting." The bet is two cookies, "not allowed to kill nobody, and you need to help them afterward".
   - A balanced fight (a tango): Mau analyses and learns, and every shadow has to restrain instead of kill.
4. **The theft.** While he is helping one of them under the contract, his memories are stolen. The attackers flee. The house finds him on the ground with two cookies. Most attackers go home; Subaru stays.
5. **Subaru's arc starts.**
   - He arrives cocky and tells Envy she'll do what he says because she loves him. **Pride** steps in.
   - The witches' talk follows. Rem tells him Mau died more times (437), counted them, and never asked for anything.
   - From cocky to serene, Eren-style.
6. **The stolen-memory arc (pillar of S6).**
   - **False history:** he arrived recently; the gate sought him; his bond with Yuka began in the Red Gate. Frieren is "the elf of the east room", Ori is "a kid with the fort".
   - **He is still Mau.** Frieren tries to hug him and he pulls away; it breaks her.
   - **Tells:** the forehead kiss, the coin he no longer has, his handwriting.
   - **Daphne** diagnoses it. The Noise saved Yuka because Frieren was taken first.
   - **The obstacle is Mau himself:** he resists being restored.
7. **The second Red Gate (only Yuka and Mau).**
   - Talking. He kisses her; she doesn't know what to do; she kisses back. It goes a little further (his jacket).
   - **She feels his ruined hand and stops.** Her conflict: she loved him as Oppa, wants more, and doesn't.
   - Yuka's 18th birthday falls in this stretch.
8. **The Red Gate's aftermath.** This is where the creator's planned stretch ends.
