[Yk] "You could have said."

[M] "You were talking."

She sat away from him enough for him to move. Waited while he stretched his arm, then began shifting the blanket so he would have somewhere to put his legs without sitting half across her knees.

The folded edge caught beneath her.

She pulled once. Nothing happened. Looked down and discovered the problem.

[Yk] "I can do this."

He kept his mouth shut.

[Yk] "You're allowed to help. Just don't say anything."

He lifted the corner she could not reach. She moved off it, freed the fold and spread the blanket across the bed again.

When she looked up, he was smiling.

[Yk] "That counts."

* * *

They lay down without making it graceful.

Yuka went first, drawing her knees toward the pillow and then straightening them beneath the blanket. Mau waited until she had found a place for her head before lowering himself beside her.

There was less room than he had expected.

[Yk] "Here."

[M] "You seem to have a plan."

[Yk] "I have a pillow. You're making it complicated."

Their clothes kept them from settling quite as neatly as they wanted. Her sleeve twisted beneath one elbow. He waited while she pulled it straight, then shifted with her until neither had a fastening pressed into the wrong place.

Afterward she sighed.

He could hear how tired she was without needing her to call it anything.

[M] "You told them you liked it."

[Yk] "Your shirt's terrible now."

[M] "Everywhere."

[Yk] "If it all comes back, this might feel different."

[M] "I don't know how it will feel."

[Yk] "I know."

[Yk] "If our story gets far away. The way the other one is now."

He stayed still.

[Yk] "You don't have to pretend you remember it the way I do."

[M] "I haven't asked her to take this from me."

[Yk] "I know. I'm not asking you to."

She held his hand a little more tightly.

[Yk] "But if you forget it, I'll remember for both of us."

Neither did he.

[Yk] "I'll still know what happened. You won't have to make something up so I don't get sad."

His throat tightened.

[M] "I love you."

Her face changed before she could prepare an answer for it.

[Yk] "I know."

He watched her blink, then move nearer.

[Yk] "I love you too."

* * *

She kept looking at him.

There was something else in her face now, a question which needed more of her than the small offer she had just made.

[Yk] "Can I ask you something before you get your answer?"

[M] "Ask."

Her hand tightened around his.

[Yk] "What we talked about. Having a family. I don't want that to turn into something we could only say when nobody else was there."

He stayed close enough for her to see him listening.

[Yk] "I want us to keep going. Even with all of this. You and me. I want to have children with you, Mau. A life we don't have to stop imagining every time we come home."

His breath moved against her forehead.

She did not lower her eyes.

[Yk] "Will we?"

He thought of the ordinary things they had begun putting into a future. A table. Her brother. The small people they had imagined before knowing how difficult it would be to sit together in the house which was already theirs.

He wanted that life.

[M] "Yes."

Her mouth trembled.

[Yk] "You don't have to say it because I'm crying."

[M] "I want a family with you. I want to have children with you. I mean it."

She watched his face for a long moment.

[Yk] "Even after everything?"

[M] "Yes."

She came closer, stopping with her forehead against his.

[Yk] "Promise me."

He could hear the question without mistaking it for a request to be comforted until morning.

[M] "I promise."

Yuka closed her eyes. Let the breath she had been holding leave her slowly, with both hands still keeping his near her.

When she looked at him again she was smiling through the tears.

[Yk] "Okay."

* * *

The kiss did not ask them to get up or go anywhere.

Yuka tilted her face. Mau met her, his right hand coming up beside her cheek when she moved close enough for it. She let go of the hand beneath the blanket to touch his shirt instead.

They kissed again after the first pause.

Then she rested with her forehead beside his mouth, breathing slowly while he kept his hand against the side of her face.

[Yk] "Stay until I'm asleep?"

[M] "Yes."

She settled lower on the pillow. Waited for him to move with her rather than pulling his arm where she wanted it this time.

He did.

The blanket had slipped from her shoulder again. He drew it up with his right hand, taking care not to pull the lower part out from beneath her feet.

She caught his hand before he could put it away.

[Yk] "You can leave that here."

He laid it on the bed between them. She put hers over it, then turned her face toward him and closed her eyes.

* * *

For some time she was not asleep.

He could tell by the small changes in her grip, the way she opened her eyes once when another door closed and looked at him before shutting them again.

He did not tell her to hurry.

Her breathing slowed. A finger moved against the edge of his palm and stopped. Her face lost the effort of deciding what to do with every feeling which crossed it.

When she spoke, the words were not quite together.

[Yk] "The table."

He looked down.

[M] "What?"

[Yk] "For dinner. Next time."

He brushed a strand away from the corner of her mouth.

[M] "I heard you."

She made a small satisfied sound. Her hand remained on his, warm through the place where their fingers met.

He waited while the next breath came, then the next. No unfamiliar stillness had entered the room. Only the ordinary tiredness of a long day finally reaching her where she had chosen to rest.

After a while he could have moved his hand without her knowing.

He left it there.

The coats hung beside the door. His boots were beneath the stool. Behind the closed window the night continued without giving him an answer merely because he had reached the end of the questions he could ask himself.

Ciel was still within reach.

[M] *I'm going to ask her now.*

[C] *I'm here.*

Mau kept Yuka's hand beneath the blanket and raised his eyes toward the window.

Remember being part of the life which let them speak over one another without being afraid of how he might answer.

Let the warmth in the room be real, even with everything he alone had brought into it.
