## Captured (creator)

- **Victim: Mau.**
- **Timing:** right after the S6 battle against the army. In that battle Mau uses an **evolved Exchange** to move everyone to a safe place that his shadows built in secret.
- **The theft is macabre, not hostile.** He doesn't see enemies. He keeps everyone, but in a **false history**:
  - he arrived at Arrival House not long ago, and the gate had sought him;
  - in the Red Gate he began a bond with **Yuka**;
  - Yuka is nervous about him going back into a gate in case it happens again.
  - This is **not a replacement for Frieren**. The Yuka bond is not romantic; she calls him Oppa.

- **Daphne** identifies the theft and helps restore him. Yuka's "trust, trust" and the forehead kiss before the dungeon are the anchor.
- **Not a micro-arc:** it should carry weight for a good while.

## Core principle (creator correction, 2026-10-19, verbatim)

> es que ahi esta tu error dices que mau no es mau pero en relaidad si es mau y ahi esta el problema

- **He is still Mau.** Same person: the same kindness and instincts, the same "nobody stands in the cold for me", the same all-in protectiveness. Only his history was stolen and rewritten. Never write him as "not himself" or a stranger. The horror is that everything good in him is intact and pointed at a false life. The house is fighting for someone who is fully present and fully wrong.

## Captured (creator)

## Claude's notes (seconded, not decided)

- It follows straight from the core principle: he is still Mau. Mau protects what he believes is his life with everything he has. To him, the restoration is not a cure. It erases the person he is now, and his bond with Yuka.
- **The house becomes "the ones trying to take something from him".** Daphne's work feels like the theft happening again. He may run, hide, refuse, or bargain ("leave me this").
- **It makes Yuka's balance unbearable.** Bringing back the real Mau means helping erase the version of him who chose her. She has to want him back more than she wants to be chosen.
- **The cracks become weapons both ways.** The forehead kiss, the coin he no longer has, his own handwriting are proof to the house and threats to him, and he may start hiding them.
- **Open:** does he resist passively (refusal, silence) or actively (running, fighting, using the shadows or the Exchange against the house)? Does the Noise side with the restoration or with the Mau who exists now?

## Something he never did with Frieren (creator, 2026-10-19, verbatim)

## Claude's notes (proposed in chat, awaiting the creator)

- "I love you" is already said between Mau and Frieren (S2–S3), so it can't be the line.
- **Push-back on anything sexual:** an altered Mau cannot really consent, and Yuka knows the truth. It would make her the exploiter, against the creator's own rule ("Yuka never treats it as a chance to win Mau"). If the beat leans that way, the version that lands is **Yuka stopping it** while wanting it: her balance at its breaking point.
- **Options that hurt more than a first time:**
  1. **He asks for help.** The real Mau's flaw was silence and deciding alone. With Yuka he says his fear out loud and asks her to come with him. Frieren sees he could have done that with her, and never did.
  2. **He promises her a lifetime.** He offers Yuka the thing Frieren most longed for (someone for her whole life who wouldn't bore her), said to someone else, with Frieren hearing it.
  3. **He gives Yuka a ritual that was Frieren's,** such as the reset hour or a hand on his chest.
