## Agreed design (seconded side by side)

- **Gap acknowledged.** S5 shows only the first stage: every spending session says mana, CE and the eyes are unchanged. The integration and the decision to stop spending were never on the page. Mau spent 440 into Intelligence, which was consistent while no integration had begun.
- **Seed at the end of S5 (V31, Day 407, "The Edge of the Window").** Ciel sees the Noise making room: faint, unfinished outlines beside the stats, two of them sitting where his cursed energy and his mana are. Mau chooses on his own to hold his 134 unspent points. Umaru crosses out her plan and starts a new one. Nobody yet knows what will happen to the points already spent. These outlines are **not** the Black Heart's empty nameless bar.
- **S6 opening arc: the integration lands, and it is retroactive.** The 440 Intelligence falls at once into CE and mana as well. His body is a battery built for the old charge, and it overloads: fever, CE leaking out of control, eyes burning, the walls shaking from the inside. It is his own power not fitting, not an attack.
- **Ciel and the Noise work together for the first time as a real team.** The Noise builds, Ciel calculates and Rimuru lends down the thread. The arc's emotional centre is that collaboration.
- **Outcome: part lost, part sealed.** Some excess is drained to save him, which is a real and permanent loss. The rest is sealed behind a cap and released gradually as his body can take it. The cap rises with Endurance, training and recovery of his base, which fits the S6 "reaching their 100%" stage. Exact amounts, the order (CE or mana first) and which stat raises the cap remain to be decided.
- **Spent points keep their shadow value.** The 15× cup stays. Retroactivity adds to CE and mana; it does not move points out of the shadow reserve.

## Open — creator's call

The overload is short, not a ten-volume arc. See [S6_UNION_ARMY_AND_THE_GODDESS_BET_CREATOR_SEED_2026-10-19.md](S6_UNION_ARMY_AND_THE_GODDESS_BET_CREATOR_SEED_2026-10-19.md).
