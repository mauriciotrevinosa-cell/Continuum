## Captured (creator)

Three things happen before the Yuka–Mau arc, in this order.

### 1. The overload (short)

- It does not last ten volumes. It happens when the Noise integrates everything at once and he rises.
- **Ciel redirects all the excess (energy, mana, etc.) into the Black Heart.** This settles the open question in [the integration seed](S6_SYSTEM_INTEGRATION_RETROACTIVE_OVERLOAD_CREATOR_SEED_2026-10-18.md).

### 2. The union's attack

- **Scale:** deliberately exaggerated, about **20,000 soldiers**, "just to feel the magnitude".
- **The front group is otherworlders**, chosen as **possible future additions to the house**:
  - **Subaru with Betty (Beatrice).** He was sold the story that Mau holds Rem and Ram hostage, and came to save them.
  - **Thomas Andre**, or someone like him, so that when Mau brings out Kamish, he smiles.
  - **Someone from a franchise we haven't seen yet:** maybe **Reze**, or **the hybrids group**. This opens another door in Mau.
- **Behind them, a group of devils, demons and other CE monsters.** Possibly the Culling Game players (creator wrote "uru kugarashi ryo": read as **Takako Uro, Kurourushi, Ryu Ishigori**; confirm). Enemies now, maybe changed later.
- **The house is moved** to the agreed place, with Mau's note: *I'm not taking you away. You're on the ridge, protected. If I can run, I run. The Exchange is cooling.*
- **Rimuru (or someone) asks Mau to promise not to kill anyone**, so that they see he is not the monster they believe.
- **Before the fight:**
  - Mau talks with the army's representative.
  - Someone asks him whether he really thinks he can take all of them.
  - Mau stretches while the wheel starts to turn. To the Noise: "You're going to have a lot of stuff after this, right?" For the first time the Noise sounds **enthusiastic**: "Maybe." Mau laughs.
- **The Goddess's bet:**
  - Out of nowhere, another voice: the Goddess. "How interesting."
  - Mau, still afraid but already thinking ahead: "Look who's watching. Want to make a bet?" Silence. Then: "Tell me."
  - "What would you give me if I beat the whole army?" Silence. The whole army waits, hearing one side of the conversation.
  - "A cookie." Mau laughs: "That's it?" "A really good cookie." He laughs and says make it two.
  - **The Goddess:** "Okay. But you are not allowed to kill nobody, and you need to help them afterward."
- **The fight is good.**

### 3. The aftermath and the theft

- After he beats them, he is helping them, **under contract**. As he goes to help one: **bam, the memories.**
- Everyone takes the chance to flee before he wakes.
- The house arrives. **Mau on the ground, with two cookies.**
- **Most of the attackers go home. Some stay.** Subaru stays because Rem and Ram tell him he's a complete idiot.

### Subaru's arc

- The witches' epic talk puts him in his place (see the approved seed in FLOATING_IDEAS, "Subaru and the witches", 2026-10-08).
- Subaru has died about 50 times and wears it like a medal. **Rem tells him Mau has died more times, counted them, and never once asked for anything in return.** (Rem heard the 437 at V25 "All the Years".)
- This ends Subaru the jerk. He goes into an Eren-style arc, from cocky to someone serene.

## Claude's notes (seconded side by side, not decided)

- **Thomas Andre and Kamish are already tied.** On floor 70 Kamish says five small ones stopped him "on the ash of the last city", and Mau thinks "five people with borrowed light stopped you". In Solo Leveling, Thomas Andre is one of the five national-level hunters who killed Kamish. His smile can be **recognition**, and Kamish can recognise him back ("You."). That is the strongest version of the creator's beat. The creator should confirm that our "five" are Solo Leveling's five.
- **The no-kill rule is what makes it a tango.** Two layers: Rimuru's request first (the house's, to prove he isn't a monster), then the Goddess's terms, which add "help them afterward". With 20,000 in the field and nobody allowed to die, every shadow has to restrain instead of finish: Tank holds, Bellion's whip pulls people off their feet, the Legion makes walls, and Kamish lies down as a barrier. That keeps the fight even without weakening Mau, and it fits the "balanced, analysing, learning" brief.
- **The help clause is the trap.** He is next to the thief only because the contract sent him to help. Whether the Goddess knew is the creator's call. Recommendation: keep it ambiguous for a long time. She paid anyway: the two cookies in his hand while his memories are gone is the image.
- **The hybrids can open the door through the second heart.** Chainsaw Man hybrids carry a devil heart with a pull-cord. Mau has a second heart, the cold one put in on 101. A hybrid could feel it ("you've got one too"). That could be the "other door". Reze alone keeps it personal; the whole group makes a bigger debut. The Chainsaw devils were planned as central since before S1 and have never been seen (S4_CREATOR_IDEAS), so this is their entrance.
- **The ridge already exists.** At the end of S5 the night shift puts Kamish lying along the north-field ridge every night. If the safe place the shadows built in secret (stolen-memory seed: "evolved Exchange… somewhere his shadows built in secret") is on or under that ridge, the night shift was the cover. Option, not canon.
- **The overload stays short but leaves the hook.** Ciel pours the excess into the Black Heart's empty bar, the bar Frieren asked him not to fill. Nobody can see what it does. That gives the Black Heart, which the creator says is "key" to the memory arc, its first movement.
- **Subaru stays human** (per the approved witches seed). The witches and Rem speak; the story doesn't declare him worthless. He leaves the scene hurt and freed.
