## Part A — The systems

### A1. Synchronisation (DH-041, S2 §38)
- **Rule:** Continuum nerfs every arrival. 0–100% is the world restoring each person toward the full version of themselves. **100% is that version's full state** (for Rimuru and Yuta, the end of their own stories).
- **Pace:** the world limits arrivals to give itself time to adapt, and **threat drives the pace**. Bocchi reached her 100% because Continuum never felt her as a threat.
- **Not a score:** percent is not a universal combat score. A low percent of an enormous being can exceed someone else's 100%.
- **100% is not the end:** it is 100% plus whatever is gained here.
- **Growth** depends on the source system, training, identity, complexity and behaviour. Yuta trains, so he is fast; Milim plays, so she is slow. Growth can be vertical (more of the same) or horizontal (new uses).
- **Proposed sub-rules for the registry** (creator to confirm):
  1. **Body-bound abilities sync fastest** (trained skill, a restriction body, Ackerman instinct, vampire or oni physiology). The world doesn't treat a body as a system to hold back.
  2. **Energy systems sync by threat** (mana, cursed energy, magicules, psychic power): slow, and in steps after crises.
  3. **Externally sourced power doesn't come at all** if its source doesn't exist here. Heiter's priestly magic comes from his world's Goddess, so his only power is "an indomitable liver".
  4. **Resource-bound systems are limited by resource, not by sync.** Witch Hat glyph magic works, but ink is finite (V16 "The Ink Budget").

### A3. The wheel: proposed rules (for the creator)
- **One adaptation takes several turns.** Each turn is partial progress (the first turn stops the harm advancing; later turns reverse it). The number of turns grows with how foreign the threat is.
- **Each turn costs Mau:** exhaustion first, then fever, then collapse (compare the 19-minute trial).
- **An adaptation, once complete, stays.** Ciel can **map** it and, with Rimuru, turn it into a skill (the Coco's-mother arc: 8 turns, then an un-crystallizing skill in Rimuru).
- **Count so far:** 2 uncontrolled. The Coco's-mother arc would be **the first controlled one, with turns counted on the page**.
