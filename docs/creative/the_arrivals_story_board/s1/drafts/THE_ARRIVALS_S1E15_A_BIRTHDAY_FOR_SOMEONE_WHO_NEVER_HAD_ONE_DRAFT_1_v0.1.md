# S1E15 — `A Birthday for Someone Who Never Had One`

## Scene 1 — The blank on the page

Fern is helping move a few remaining books and loose papers between Mau's nominal study room and the shared room he has resumed using with Frieren.

She finds one of Rimuru's household sheets: names, food notes, medicine notes, rough ages where useful, practical details.

Mau's line has several blanks.

Fern points at one.

**FERN**  
When is your birthday?

Mau looks up from a book.

**MAU**  
I don't know.

Fern waits.

**FERN**  
The day you were born.

**MAU**  
I know what the words mean.

He thinks.

**MAU**  
I don't remember it.

Fern looks at the empty line.

**FERN**  
Do you remember ever having one?

Mau searches his memory the way he sometimes does when someone asks about before.

Nothing arrives.

**MAU**  
No.

He says it neutrally.

That bothers Fern more than if he had sounded sad.

---

## Scene 2 — `Then we give him one.`

Fern finds Rimuru at the common table with accounts.

**FERN**  
Mau doesn't have a birthday.

Rimuru looks up.

**RIMURU**  
He doesn't know his birthday.

**FERN**  
He doesn't remember ever having one either.

Rimuru puts the paper down.

Fern has already decided what the solution is.

**FERN**  
Then we give him one.

Rimuru smiles.

**RIMURU**  
Okay.

Fern blinks.

**FERN**  
That's all?

**RIMURU**  
Did you expect an argument?

The idea spreads through the house almost instantly.

Momo agrees before Fern finishes explaining.

Bocchi agrees and immediately panics because birthdays require gifts and she does not know what Mau wants.

Yuta says yes without making it complicated.

Anko asks what date they are choosing and whether they should make the logic consistent.

Maomao asks what local ingredients can approximate cake.

Frieren listens from the side of the room.

She says nothing.

Rimuru looks at her.

**RIMURU**  
You're helping.

Frieren looks mildly offended by the idea that this was in question.

**FRIEREN**  
Obviously.

---

## Scene 3 — Choosing a date

They cannot recover Mau's real birthday, so the question becomes what a birthday means if nobody knows the original date.

Anko suggests using the day Frieren found him.

Fern considers it.

Momo points out that day is already tied to too much fear and confusion.

Yuta suggests choosing today because the idea exists today.

Bocchi says choosing today means they have almost no preparation time, then realizes that Rimuru can solve half of that problem unnervingly fast.

Rimuru checks the calendar they have built from local timekeeping.

**RIMURU**  
Tomorrow.

**FERN**  
Why tomorrow?

**RIMURU**  
Because today we prepare and tomorrow he has a birthday.

Anko accepts the operational logic.

Momo likes that the date belongs entirely to this world.

Frieren quietly does too.

They choose it.

No cosmic significance.

No recovered memory.

Just a date selected by people who want him to have one.

---

## Scene 4 — Mau walks in

The planning becomes chaotic enough that nobody hears Mau until he is already in the doorway.

He is carrying a bucket and a broken hinge.

Every conversation stops.

Rimuru hides the calendar sheet under another paper.

Bocchi hides a piece of ribbon behind her back badly.

Momo turns a bowl over on top of something.

Maomao casually moves a packet of ingredients behind her as if she has never concealed anything in her life.

Mau looks around.

**MAU**  
What are you doing?

Too many people answer at once.

**MOMO**  
Nothing.

**BOCCHI**  
Normal things.

**RIMURU**  
Planning.

Everyone looks at Rimuru.

Rimuru corrects.

**RIMURU**  
Not planning.

Mau looks at Frieren.

Frieren is the worst liar in the room because she does not try.

**FRIEREN**  
You should go somewhere else.

Mau blinks.

**MAU**  
...Okay.

He leaves.

The room stays silent until his footsteps are gone.

Bocchi looks horrified.

**BOCCHI**  
That sounded terrible.

Frieren looks at the door.

She realizes it did.

---

## Scene 5 — Mau's small disappointment

Mau does not think the household is plotting against him.

He knows them too well for that.

But he also knows they are sharing something he is not part of.

That feeling would not have mattered to him early in the season.

Now it does.

He sits outside with the broken hinge beside him and works on it longer than necessary.

Yuta passes and almost stops.

Mau looks up first.

**MAU**  
You know what they're doing?

Yuta is not good at lying either.

**YUTA**  
Yes.

Mau nods once.

**MAU**  
Okay.

Yuta studies him.

**YUTA**  
It's not bad.

Mau smiles a little.

**MAU**  
I figured.

That helps.

It does not erase the small sting of being outside the circle.

---

## Scene 6 — Frieren's distraction mission

Frieren finds Mau before dinner.

**FRIEREN**  
I need your help tomorrow.

Mau looks at her.

**MAU**  
With what?

**FRIEREN**  
A plant.

Mau narrows his eyes.

**MAU**  
Is this revenge for my plant?

**FRIEREN**  
No.

It sounds exactly like revenge.

She explains that there is an edible plant several hours away that might be useful in food.

Mau asks why Maomao is not going.

Frieren says Maomao is busy.

Mau asks why Momo is not going.

Frieren says Momo is busy.

Mau looks toward the inn.

**MAU**  
Everyone is busy.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Mau looks back at her.

**MAU**  
Okay.

He still trusts her enough to go without understanding.

---

## Scene 7 — The ordinary trip

They leave early the next morning.

The trip is intentionally uneventful.

They walk.

They argue about which trail is shorter.

Frieren is wrong and refuses to admit it.

Mau spots a bird with a ridiculous call and imitates it badly enough that Frieren tells him to stop.

He does it again.

They cross a shallow stream.

Mau nearly slips and grabs Frieren's shoulder.

Frieren says nothing about it, which is more generous than making fun of him.

They stop to eat.

Mau has packed too much bread and not enough of anything else.

Frieren eats some anyway.

The day feels like the kind of day they used to have before the house became crowded and before either started worrying about whether closeness had to be justified.

That is the real purpose of the trip.

The plant is almost incidental.

---

## Scene 8 — `What's a birthday?`

In the afternoon they sit under a tree while Frieren checks the plant she allegedly needed.

Mau is still thinking about yesterday.

**MAU**  
Frieren.

**FRIEREN**  
Hm?

**MAU**  
What's a birthday?

Frieren looks up.

Mau clarifies.

**MAU**  
I know it's the day someone was born. But why do people do things for it?

Frieren thinks about how to answer without giving away tomorrow—or today, from his perspective.

**FRIEREN**  
People celebrate that you were born.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Why?

Frieren looks at him.

The answer is simple enough that hiding it would be stranger.

**FRIEREN**  
Because they're glad you're here.

Mau absorbs that.

He looks out through the trees.

**MAU**  
That sounds nice.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
It is.

He still does not realize she is talking about him.

---

## Scene 9 — Preparation at home

While Mau and Frieren are away, the inn transforms in pieces.

Rimuru organizes.

Momo handles food and decorations with more confidence than anyone expected.

Maomao repeatedly has to be told which ingredients are for the cake and which are not experiments.

Fern makes something simple and useful rather than ornamental.

Yuta works quietly on a gift that reflects teaching/reading together.

Anko writes something in her notebook before turning it into a cleaner small card or note for Mau.

Bocchi struggles over a music-related gift and eventually chooses something small and personal rather than impressive.

Rimuru fabricates missing practical pieces where needed but refuses to let the entire celebration become something only his abilities could make.

The decorations are handmade, uneven and clearly the work of several people with different ideas.

That makes them better.

The cake is not perfect.

That also makes it better.

---

## Scene 10 — The return

Frieren times the walk home so they arrive after dark.

Mau is carrying the plant.

**MAU**  
We were gone a long time for this.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

**MAU**  
Was it worth it?

Frieren looks at the plant.

**FRIEREN**  
Probably not.

Mau laughs.

They reach the inn.

It is darker than usual from outside.

Mau notices.

**MAU**  
Did everyone go to sleep?

Frieren opens the door.

Light.

Voices.

Decorations.

Everyone there.

**MOMO**  
Happy birthday!

Mau stops in the doorway.

He looks behind himself as if someone else might be entering.

**MAU**  
...Whose?

Momo points at him.

**MOMO**  
Yours.

Mau looks at Frieren.

Then everyone else.

**MAU**  
I don't know when my birthday is.

Fern answers.

**FERN**  
Neither do we.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Then how is it my birthday?

Fern:

**FERN**  
We picked one.

Mau:

**MAU**  
You can do that?

Rimuru, from behind the table:

**RIMURU**  
We just did.

The room laughs.

Mau still looks confused.

Frieren steps beside him.

**FRIEREN**  
We're glad you're here.

The line connects to the conversation under the tree.

Mau understands.

---

## Scene 11 — Cake

Mau has never knowingly had a birthday cake.

He does not know what to do with the candles until Momo explains.

Bocchi gives conflicting instructions because she is overthinking the wish rule.

Anko tells her to stop changing the rules while explaining them.

Maomao asks whether inhaling smoke from the candles counts as a medicinal concern.

Momo tells her no.

Mau looks around the table while everyone talks over one another.

He closes his eyes and makes a wish he does not say aloud.

He blows out the candles.

Everyone cheers too loudly.

Mau laughs.

The cake is slightly uneven.

He thinks it is excellent.

Momo says he has no basis for comparison.

He says that means she cannot prove him wrong.

---

## Scene 12 — Gifts

The gifts come one at a time and feel like a map of the household.

Fern gives him something practical for reading/travel.

Yuta gives him something connected to their lessons.

Bocchi's gift carries a music callback and is awkwardly wrapped.

Anko gives him something small with a note that he reads twice.

Maomao's gift is useful and only mildly alarming.

Momo gives him something meant for everyday life in the inn.

Rimuru gives him something simple rather than extravagant, because the point is not abundance.

Frieren waits until later.

Mau becomes more animated with every gift, not because any one thing is expensive, but because every object proves someone thought about him while he was not there.

At one point he stops and looks at the growing pile.

**MAU**  
These are all mine?

**MOMO**  
Yes.

**MAU**  
Why?

Several people answer at once.

The answers overlap enough that none is individually important.

The room itself is the answer.

---

## Scene 13 — Frieren's gift

Later, after the loud part of the celebration, Frieren gives Mau her gift privately enough that the others do not crowd the moment.

It is modest: something chosen because she has watched him use, lose, borrow or need a version of it repeatedly.

Mau turns it over in his hands.

**MAU**  
You picked this?

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

**MAU**  
For me?

Frieren looks at him.

**FRIEREN**  
It is your birthday.

Mau smiles.

The answer means more than the object.

---

## Scene 14 — The shared room

Night.

The celebration is over.

The inn is quiet except for the last cleanup downstairs.

Mau and Frieren are in their shared room.

The gifts are gathered near Mau's side.

The real flower from E9 remains by the window.

The new objects make the room look even more inhabited than before.

Mau is still looking through things.

Then:

**MAU**  
Frieren.

**FRIEREN**  
Hm?

**MAU**  
Is today really my birthday now?

Frieren looks at him.

**FRIEREN**  
If you want it to be.

Mau considers that.

No recovered past appears to compete with the answer.

He does not need one.

**MAU**  
Okay.

A small smile.

**MAU**  
I like it.

Frieren looks toward the gifts, then toward him.

**FRIEREN**  
Me too.

The final image holds on the room: flower, gifts, two people who chose to share it, and a date that did not exist for Mau until the people around him decided he deserved one.

**END S1E15**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- The episode follows directly after E14 reconciliation; Mau/Frieren domestic rhythm is restored.
- The birthday is invented by the household, not discovered from lost memory.
- The date should belong to this world/current life rather than be tied to a traumatic arrival anniversary.
- No character gives a speech about found family; the preparation, gifts and noise do that work visually.
- Mau's positive overwhelm may stay mostly in attention, laughter and repeated questions rather than tears.
- E9 flower remains visible in the closing shared-room image.
- No romance label is added.
