# S1E18 — `Day in the Life / Maomao Chocolate`

## Scene 1 — Not everyone works today

The guild has work, but not enough interesting or necessary work for everyone to leave.

Some people go out.

Some stay.

The inn spends a morning behaving like a house rather than a staging area.

Momo is in the kitchen trying to manage too many things at once when she spots Mau passing.

**MOMO**  
Mau. I need you.

Mau turns immediately.

**MAU**  
Okay.

Momo blinks.

**MOMO**  
...You don't even know what for.

**MAU**  
You said you needed me.

Momo stares at him for a second, then points toward the kitchen.

**MOMO**  
Fine. Come here.

---

## Scene 2 — `A little`

Momo asks Mau to add a little of one ingredient while she works on something else.

Mau takes the phrase literally in the wrong direction.

He adds an amount so small it is nearly insulting.

Momo looks at the bowl.

Then at him.

**MOMO**  
A little more than that.

Mau adds one additional grain/pinch.

Momo closes her eyes.

**MOMO**  
I walked into that.

The kitchen sequence establishes how comfortable the household has become with small irritations that are not real conflict.

---

## Scene 3 — Maomao's experiment

In the workroom, Maomao is experimenting with cacao/chocolate and a local medicinal ingredient.

Her intended effect is mild: relaxation, lower social inhibition, easier expression of impulses someone would normally filter.

It does not create desires.

It is not a love potion.

It is not a truth serum.

The dose calibration is simply off.

Bocchi is nearby helping label containers.

Maomao breaks off a small piece.

**MAOMAO**  
Taste this.

Bocchi looks at it like a legal document she should not sign.

**BOCCHI**  
Is it poisonous?

**MAOMAO**  
No.

**BOCCHI**  
Will it make me sick?

Maomao considers.

**MAOMAO**  
Probably not.

Bocchi becomes less reassured.

She tastes a tiny amount anyway.

Nothing dramatic happens.

She says it tastes good.

Maomao writes that down as if Bocchi has contributed to science.

---

## Scene 4 — Yuta + Anko errand

Yuta and Anko take a small errand into town for supplies and guild paperwork.

Their conversation wanders naturally.

Anko eventually asks about Rika with the casual precision of someone who has waited until the question will not feel like an interrogation.

**ANKO**  
So Rika was a person before?

Yuta does not answer immediately.

**YUTA**  
Yes.

Anko lets him choose how much to say.

**ANKO**  
And she's still with you.

**YUTA**  
Yes.

Anko looks ahead.

**ANKO**  
Complicated.

Yuta almost laughs.

**YUTA**  
Yeah.

No exposition dump. No therapy scene. Just two people understanding there is more behind what they see.

---

## Scene 5 — Fern goes alone

Fern prepares for a safe mundane errand/job alone.

This time there is no buildup.

Frieren looks up from her book.

**FRIEREN**  
Before dark.

Fern:

**FERN**  
I will.

Fern leaves.

Frieren returns to reading.

The E17 progression has become normal behavior.

---

## Scene 6 — The unlabeled chocolate

Bocchi starts making a label for Maomao's chocolate.

`DO NOT—`

Momo calls for help from another room.

Bocchi sets the label down unfinished.

Maomao gets pulled away by a medicinal task.

The plate/container remains on the common table among ordinary food.

Nobody notices the problem because everybody assumes someone else knows what it is.

---

## Scene 7 — Frieren eats it

Frieren walks through the common room, sees the chocolate and takes a piece.

Mau sees her from across the table.

**MAU**  
Is that yours?

Frieren is already eating it.

**FRIEREN**  
It was on the table.

**MAU**  
That doesn't mean it's yours.

Frieren finishes the piece.

**FRIEREN**  
It does now.

Mau watches her.

Nothing happens.

For several minutes.

Then subtle things begin.

---

## Scene 8 — Slightly wrong Frieren

Mau sits to read.

Frieren sits beside him.

Closer than necessary.

Mau notices but does not object.

He gets up to move a cup.

Frieren gets up too.

He stops.

She stops.

**MAU**  
What are you doing?

**FRIEREN**  
Nothing.

Mau moves again.

Frieren follows.

**MAU**  
You're following me.

**FRIEREN**  
No, I'm not.

She is visibly following him.

Later she laughs at something Mau says more openly than usual.

Mau starts looking concerned rather than flattered because he does not understand the pattern.

---

## Scene 9 — Fern returns

Fern returns before dark.

She steps into the inn and immediately sees Frieren sitting closer to Mau than normal.

**FERN**  
Mistress Frieren?

Frieren looks up.

**FRIEREN**  
Welcome back.

Fern pauses.

**FERN**  
Are you feeling well?

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Mau, from beside her:

**MAU**  
She's been acting weird.

Frieren turns toward him.

**FRIEREN**  
No, I haven't.

Fern's eyes narrow.

Investigation begins.

---

## Scene 10 — Outside

Mau goes outside for air or to bring something in.

Frieren follows.

The evening is calm.

He catches her staring.

**MAU**  
What?

**FRIEREN**  
Nothing.

**MAU**  
You're staring.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
You stare at me.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Not like that.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
How do you know?

Mau opens his mouth.

He has no answer.

Frieren steps closer.

Mau does not understand what she is doing.

She leans toward him.

He stays still out of confusion, not invitation.

Before contact, Fern appears between them with decisive physical timing.

Frieren stops inches away.

**FRIEREN**  
...Fern.

**FERN**  
No.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Move.

Fern:

**FERN**  
No.

Mau looks between them.

**MAU**  
What was she doing?

Fern does not look at him.

**FERN**  
...Nothing.

Mau:

**MAU**  
That didn't look like nothing.

Fern:

**FERN**  
Later.

---

## Scene 11 — Fern finds the cause

Fern retraces Frieren's day.

The plate.

The unfinished Bocchi label.

Maomao's workroom.

She finds the notes.

Bocchi realizes what happened at exactly the same time.

**BOCCHI**  
Oh no.

Fern:

**FERN**  
What?

Bocchi points to the unfinished label.

**BOCCHI**  
I was going to finish that.

Fern closes her eyes briefly.

---

## Scene 12 — Maomao returns

Maomao returns to a room with the specific silence that means something happened while she was gone.

She sees Frieren.

Sees Fern.

Sees the plate.

Understands immediately.

Then starts laughing.

Fern is not amused.

**FERN**  
She tried to kiss Mau.

Maomao laughs harder.

From nearby:

**FRIEREN**  
I can still hear you.

Momo enters halfway through the laughter.

**MOMO**  
What happened?

Nobody answers quickly enough.

Momo becomes more suspicious.

---

## Scene 13 — What the chocolate actually does

Mau is still missing the central vocabulary.

Maomao explains the experiment.

**MAOMAO**  
It lowers inhibition.

Mau:

**MAU**  
What does that mean?

Maomao chooses simpler words.

**MAOMAO**  
It makes it easier to do things you'd normally stop yourself from doing.

Mau thinks.

**MAU**  
So it makes you want things?

Maomao:

**MAOMAO**  
No.

She becomes more precise because Fern is listening too closely.

**MAOMAO**  
It doesn't make you want things you don't already want.

Fern looks at Frieren.

Frieren looks away.

But the line is not a perfect translator of hidden truth. The effect can magnify affection, closeness, curiosity, playfulness or impulses someone has not categorized.

Maomao makes that limitation explicit enough to stop the room from turning the incident into a romantic verdict.

**MAOMAO**  
It also doesn't tell you what the impulse means.

That matters.

Mau still looks confused.

---

## Scene 14 — Consequences

Maomao gets consequences rather than moral condemnation.

Storage rules.

Labels.

Separate experimental food from common food.

Bocchi produces an enormous replacement label:

`DO NOT EAT`

Maomao says that is excessive.

Everyone else says it is not.

Momo finally gets the missing detail.

**MOMO**  
WAIT. SHE DID WHAT?

From upstairs or another room:

**FRIEREN**  
NOTHING HAPPENED.

Momo looks toward the ceiling/hall.

**MOMO**  
...I didn't say your name.

Silence.

The room collapses into laughter except Frieren, who has removed herself from the situation.

---

## Scene 15 — Shared room

Later.

The inn is quiet.

Mau and Frieren are in their shared room.

Frieren is already trying to read as if the day can be erased through concentration.

Mau has been thinking.

**MAU**  
Frieren.

Frieren does not look up.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I didn't ask anything.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
You were going to.

Mau hesitates.

Then sincerely:

**MAU**  
...What's a kiss?

Frieren's book lowers by one inch.

She stares at him.

Mau waits for the answer.

Cut before she finds one.

**END S1E18**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- Chocolate is a medicinal/recreational experiment with miscalibrated dose, not magic love potion.
- It does not create feelings, desires or personality.
- Frieren's behavior remains interpretable as affection/closeness/curiosity/unfiltered impulse without locking romance canon.
- Mau does not understand the kiss attempt or the concept of a kiss.
- Fern physically stops contact before it happens.
- E17 Fern independence persists: she completes a safe solo errand normally.
- Yuta/Anko conversation about Rika stays brief and non-expository.
- Maomao faces practical labelling/storage consequences.
