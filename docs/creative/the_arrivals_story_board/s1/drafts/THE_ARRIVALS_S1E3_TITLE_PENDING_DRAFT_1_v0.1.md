# S1E3 — `[TITLE PENDING]`

## Scene 1 — Bird

Cold.

That is the first thing Bocchi notices.

The second is weight on her chest.

She opens her eyes to a gray sky framed by bare branches.

For one second she does not move.

Something small shifts above her.

Bocchi slowly lowers her eyes.

A bird is standing on her coat.

It tilts its head.

Bocchi's entire body locks.

The bird hops once, closer to her face.

Bocchi inhales.

**BOCCHI**  
AAAAAAAH—!

The bird launches away in alarm.

Bocchi jerks upright, tangles one arm in the strap of her guitar case, loses balance, and falls sideways into the snow-dusted grass.

She lies there for a beat.

Her breathing is loud.

Then she notices the trees.

The road.

The ruined buildings beyond them.

None of it is familiar.

Bocchi stops breathing for an entirely different reason.

She sits up again.

**BOCCHI**  
...Where am I?

No answer.

She reaches for her phone.

It is with her.

No signal.

She stares at the screen.

Tries again as if the bars might appear because she wants them badly enough.

Nothing.

Her fingers begin to shake.

She looks at the settlement.

Then the forest.

Then the road.

A thought arrives fully formed:

*I have been kidnapped into the mountains and I'm going to die before I can explain why I missed rehearsal.*

Footsteps approach through the snow.

Bocchi hears them.

Her head snaps toward the sound.

This is somehow worse.

---

## Scene 2 — Two Strangers

Frieren appears first from between the buildings, staff in hand.

Mau is a step behind her.

They both stop when they see Bocchi.

Bocchi sees white hair, pointed ears, staff, unfamiliar clothes.

Then Mau, who at least looks human enough to make the situation more confusing rather than less.

Nobody speaks for a second.

Frieren lowers her staff slightly.

**FRIEREN**  
Are you hurt?

Bocchi opens her mouth.

Nothing usable comes out.

**BOCCHI**  
I— um— I— no— maybe—

She looks down at herself as if checking whether she is hurt for the first time.

Mau watches this process carefully.

**MAU**  
Can you stand?

Bocchi looks at him.

A direct question. Easier.

She tries to get up.

Her foot catches the guitar-case strap.

She almost goes down again.

Mau reaches out and steadies her by the forearm.

Bocchi freezes at being touched by a stranger.

Mau lets go immediately once she is balanced.

Frieren walks past them and picks up the bag Bocchi dropped during the scream.

Then the guitar case.

She looks at it, unfamiliar with the object but not alarmed by it.

Bocchi sees a stranger holding her guitar.

Her voice returns at once.

**BOCCHI**  
Ah— that's— that's mine.

Frieren hands it to her.

**FRIEREN**  
I assumed.

Bocchi hugs the case to herself.

Mau looks at her.

**MAU**  
What's your name?

Bocchi has answered this question thousands of times in school and still somehow feels unprepared.

**BOCCHI**  
Hitori Gotoh.

Beat.

**BOCCHI**  
People call me Bocchi.

Another beat.

**BOCCHI**  
Not everyone. Some people. It's complicated.

Mau nods as if this is ordinary useful information.

**MAU**  
I'm Mau.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Frieren.

Bocchi looks at Frieren's ears again.

She immediately looks away when Frieren notices.

---

## Scene 3 — Not From Here

They bring Bocchi toward the inn because standing in the road solving cosmology is not useful.

Bocchi walks between them but slightly behind, holding the guitar case with both hands.

Every few steps she looks back.

No city appears behind her.

No road sign she understands.

No explanation.

**BOCCHI**  
Is there... a station nearby?

Mau looks at Frieren.

Frieren looks at Mau.

**FRIEREN**  
What's a station?

Bocchi stops walking.

**BOCCHI**  
...Oh no.

They continue.

At the inn entrance, Bocchi looks up at the damaged roof and boarded window.

This does not reassure her.

Inside, however, there is a fire.

Food.

Blankets.

Signs that people have been sleeping here rather than waiting to murder someone in a picturesque ruin.

This helps slightly.

Frieren puts another piece of wood on the fire.

**BOCCHI**  
Where is this?

**FRIEREN**  
We don't know.

Bocchi stares.

Mau sits near the table.

**MAU**  
We're not from here either.

Bocchi looks at him.

**BOCCHI**  
You're from Japan?

**MAU**  
I don't know.

The answer knocks her off-balance again.

Frieren gives the shortest explanation possible: she woke in this world recently; Mau did too; neither recognizes the place; they found the abandoned settlement yesterday.

No map appears.

No authority is coming.

No one has a route home.

Bocchi lowers herself into a chair.

Her guitar case stays upright against her knees like a shield.

**BOCCHI**  
So... what am I supposed to do now?

Mau answers before Frieren does.

**MAU**  
I don't know.

Bocchi looks at him, horrified by the honesty.

A beat.

Mau continues, just as plainly.

**MAU**  
But I'll help you.

Bocchi says nothing.

The sentence is too simple to argue with.

Frieren looks at Mau.

For the briefest moment, E1 echoes in the way she watches him.

She does not comment.

---

## Scene 4 — A Room

Frieren shows Bocchi the upstairs corridor.

Mau carries one of Bocchi's bags because she is still clutching the guitar herself.

The inn looks worse when seen as a place she may actually have to live.

Several rooms are unusable.

One has no door.

Another smells damp.

Bocchi begins imagining every possible creature that could crawl through every gap.

Frieren opens one of the better rooms.

It has a bed frame, a small table, a window with cracked but mostly intact glass, and dust everywhere.

**FRIEREN**  
This one is usable.

Bocchi looks around.

**BOCCHI**  
Usable.

**FRIEREN**  
Mostly.

From behind them:

**MAU**  
That's her standard.

Frieren looks back at him.

Mau does not look guilty.

Bocchi makes a tiny involuntary sound that is almost a laugh.

It surprises her.

Mau puts her bag down by the wall.

**MAU**  
If you want another room, there are more.

Bocchi looks into the dark corridor full of unknown rooms.

**BOCCHI**  
This one is good.

Too fast.

**BOCCHI**  
I mean— fine. It's fine.

Frieren nods.

No one pressures her to be grateful or brave.

That helps more than reassurance would.

---

## Scene 5 — First Dinner

Dinner is awkward because there are now three people at the table and one of them has spent her whole life finding two-person conversations difficult.

Bocchi sits with shoulders slightly raised, eating carefully.

Mau asks questions when they occur to him.

Not interrogation. Genuine curiosity.

**MAU**  
What is the big case?

Bocchi almost says *nothing* out of reflex.

She stops.

**BOCCHI**  
A guitar.

Mau waits.

**MAU**  
What's a guitar?

Bocchi looks at Frieren.

Frieren keeps eating.

She is not going to rescue this conversation.

Bocchi looks back at Mau.

**BOCCHI**  
It's an instrument.

**MAU**  
What does it do?

Bocchi thinks about how to explain music to someone who asks what an instrument does.

**BOCCHI**  
It... makes music.

Mau nods.

**MAU**  
Can you show me?

Bocchi nearly chokes.

**BOCCHI**  
Now?

**MAU**  
If you want.

That last part saves her.

**BOCCHI**  
Maybe later.

**MAU**  
Okay.

He goes back to eating.

No disappointment.

No insistence.

Bocchi watches him for a second.

Frieren notices Bocchi watching but says nothing.

---

## Scene 6 — First Night Alone

Bocchi's room is colder than the common room.

She has enough blankets.

That is not the problem.

The problem is every sound.

Wood shifting.

Wind against glass.

Something outside moving through snow.

The entire building settling around her.

Bocchi lies rigid beneath the blanket, guitar case beside the bed.

She checks her phone again.

No signal.

Battery lower.

The screen shows pieces of a life that suddenly feels impossibly far away.

She turns it off to save power.

Darkness.

Immediately worse.

After several minutes she sees a faint orange light under her door from the corridor.

The common room fire.

Bocchi sits up.

She spends far too long deciding whether going downstairs will make her look needy.

A louder creak resolves the debate.

She takes a blanket around her shoulders and leaves the room.

---

## Scene 7 — Frieren

Halfway down the stairs, Bocchi hears a voice.

Frieren.

Not normal conversation.

Broken, quiet speech with long spaces between it.

Bocchi stops before she reaches the bottom.

From the stairwell she can see only part of the common room.

Mau is sitting near the fire.

Frieren is beside him.

Her face is turned down.

At first Bocchi thinks Frieren may be sick.

Then she sees the tears.

Frieren wipes them away once, almost annoyed by them.

More come.

**FRIEREN**  
I should've seen it sooner.

Mau does not answer immediately.

He does not tell her she could not have known.

He does not tell her everything happens for a reason.

He simply stays.

Frieren's hand closes around the edge of Mau's sleeve.

**FRIEREN**  
Fern would've listened if I'd been faster.

Mau looks at her.

**MAU**  
You tried to save her.

Frieren shakes her head once.

**FRIEREN**  
It wasn't enough.

Silence.

Mau does not argue with grief as if it is a debate.

Frieren leans toward him.

He lets her.

Eventually his arm comes around her shoulders.

It is not polished comfort. It is what he has learned to do because this has happened more than once since E1.

Bocchi watches from the stairs.

The two people who found her and seemed to know what to do are not okay either.

That realization does not make her less scared.

It makes the room feel more human.

Frieren's breathing gradually slows.

Her grip on Mau's sleeve remains.

Eventually she falls asleep against him.

Mau stays still so he does not wake her.

Only then does he notice Bocchi on the stairs.

Their eyes meet.

Bocchi freezes.

Mau does not call attention to her.

He lifts one finger to his lips.

Bocchi nods immediately.

---

## Scene 8 — Who's Helping You?

Bocchi reaches the bottom step carefully.

Mau cannot get up without moving Frieren.

He gestures toward a folded blanket near the fire.

Bocchi understands and takes it.

She sits on the floor at the other side of the hearth, still wrapped in the blanket she brought down.

For a while nobody speaks.

Fire fills the silence.

Bocchi looks at Frieren asleep against Mau.

**BOCCHI**  
Does she... do that a lot?

Mau glances down at Frieren.

**MAU**  
Sometimes.

Bocchi lowers her voice even more.

**BOCCHI**  
Because of Fern?

Mau nods.

Bocchi looks at him.

He has been awake through the whole thing.

He looks tired too.

The question leaves her before she has time to stop it.

**BOCCHI**  
Who's helping you?

Mau looks at her.

For once, a direct question does not have a direct answer.

He looks toward Frieren.

Then toward the fire.

**MAU**  
...I don't know.

Bocchi immediately regrets asking.

**BOCCHI**  
Sorry.

**MAU**  
Why?

Bocchi cannot explain why.

Mau shifts the blanket around Frieren slightly so it covers her better.

Bocchi watches.

No answer appears.

That is where the question stays.

A few minutes later Bocchi curls up beside the fire rather than returning upstairs.

**BOCCHI**  
Thank you.

Mau looks over.

**MAU**  
For the blanket?

Bocchi hesitates.

**BOCCHI**  
...Yeah.

It is easier than explaining the rest.

---

## Scene 9 — Morning After

Frieren wakes with her head against Mau.

She notices Bocchi asleep near the other side of the fire.

Then she notices Mau is awake.

**FRIEREN**  
How long have you been awake?

**MAU**  
I don't know.

Frieren studies him for a beat.

**FRIEREN**  
You say that a lot.

Mau looks at her.

Frieren almost smiles.

Bocchi wakes at exactly the wrong moment and immediately pretends she was not listening to anything.

Nobody mentions the previous night.

That mercy is mutual.

---

## Scene 10 — Day One With Bocchi

Bocchi does not suddenly become comfortable.

She becomes useful because being useful gives her something to do with her hands.

Frieren and Mau plan to search nearby buildings for salvage and food.

Bocchi looks toward the snowy lane.

Then toward the interior mess.

**BOCCHI**  
I can... stay here and clean.

She says it like an apology.

Frieren looks around the common room.

**FRIEREN**  
That would help.

Bocchi blinks.

No judgment.

No *you should come outside more*.

They leave her with the fire and enough wood.

Bocchi watches the door close.

The building immediately feels too quiet.

She almost follows them.

Instead she picks up a broom.

By the time they return, one corner of the common room is noticeably cleaner.

Not because Bocchi is naturally good at housekeeping.

Because anxiety made her clean the same area three times.

Mau notices.

**MAU**  
It looks better.

Bocchi tries not to look pleased.

She fails slightly.

---

## Scene 11 — Guitar

That evening, Mau sees the guitar case again.

He does not ask her to play this time.

He only looks at it.

Bocchi notices.

This creates a terrible social obligation entirely inside her own head.

After several minutes she opens the case.

Mau looks over.

Frieren is reading near the fire.

Bocchi sits with the guitar.

Her hands know what to do even when the rest of her does not.

She plays quietly at first.

The first notes change the room.

Mau stops what he is doing.

Frieren lowers the book slightly.

Bocchi immediately notices both of them listening and nearly stops.

Mau does not praise her loudly.

He simply keeps listening.

Frieren turns the page but does not raise the book all the way again.

Bocchi continues.

For the first time since waking in this world, something sounds exactly like itself.

---

## Scene 12 — Several Days

Time passes through repeated domestic images rather than title cards.

Morning.

Frieren and Mau leave with packs.

Bocchi stands in the doorway and watches them go.

Later they return with food, wood, seeds, and salvaged supplies.

Bocchi has swept another section and patched a small fabric tear badly but successfully.

Another morning.

Bocchi helps Mau sort usable nails from bent ones.

He does not know enough to pretend he is teaching her. They figure it out together.

Another afternoon.

Frieren works outside near the field.

Bocchi stands at the inn door.

Then one step outside.

Then several.

She stops when she realizes how far she has gone.

No one comments.

Another evening.

Mau carries water.

Bocchi holds the door open with her foot while carrying kindling.

Frieren brings in something edible and puts it on the table.

They move around each other with the beginning of routine.

Another night.

Bocchi plays guitar.

Mau sits nearby doing some small repair badly.

Frieren reads.

The boarded window from E2 rattles once in the wind and holds.

There are now three bowls on the table.

Three blankets near the fire.

Three sets of things in rooms upstairs.

The inn still looks broken.

It no longer looks empty.

---

## Scene 13 — Closing

Night.

Snow has stopped.

Exterior of the settlement.

Most buildings remain dark.

Only the inn has light.

From outside, Bocchi's guitar is faintly audible.

Inside, she finishes a song fragment and immediately looks down at the strings, uncertain whether she did too much.

Mau is the first to speak.

**MAU**  
Do another one.

Bocchi looks up.

Frieren, still reading:

**FRIEREN**  
If you want.

The exact phrase Mau used at dinner.

Bocchi notices.

She adjusts her fingers on the guitar.

**BOCCHI**  
...Okay.

She starts again.

Wide final image: the three of them in the damaged common room, small around the fire, with far more empty space than occupied space around them.

The house is not fixed.

None of them are fixed.

But the room is safe enough for music.

**END S1E3.**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- The episode working title `Bocchi` remains explicitly unapproved; production completion does not rename/lock it.
- Bocchi's E2 scream is revealed to be caused by an ordinary bird, preserving the intended non-monster gag.
- `Mau: But I'll help you.` is retained unmistakably for the E7 reciprocal callback.
- `Who's helping you?` is asked after Bocchi observes Mau supporting Frieren; the episode intentionally does not resolve it.
- Frieren's grief remains fragmented and embodied rather than a polished trauma monologue.
- Mau comforts Frieren through presence, limited truthful language, and physical steadiness rather than therapy-speech.
- Bocchi is anxious and occasionally funny but not a scream machine; after the opening she communicates through hesitation, observation, avoidance, and small choices.
- Bocchi begins contributing inside before gradually venturing outside, seeding later growth.
- No additional Arrival occurs in E3; the ending is routine/safety/music rather than another cliffhanger.
- The inn remains damaged and modest. The household changes visually from two to three people through objects, bowls, blankets, and use of space.
