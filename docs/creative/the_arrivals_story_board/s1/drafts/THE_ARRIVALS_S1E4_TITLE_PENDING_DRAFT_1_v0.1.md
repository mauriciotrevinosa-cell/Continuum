# S1E4 — `[TITLE PENDING]`

## Scene 1 — A Few Days Later

The inn has not transformed.

It has acquired habits.

Bocchi's guitar case now has a consistent place near the common-room wall. Three bowls dry upside down beside the kitchen area. A second window has been patched badly enough to be temporary and well enough to matter.

Morning.

Bocchi sweeps near the fireplace while Mau sorts a small pile of salvaged hardware into groups that make sense only after Frieren corrects two of them.

Frieren is studying the weather through the window.

The snow has thinned. The sky is clearer.

**FRIEREN**  
We should go to the lake.

Mau looks up.

**MAU**  
Why?

**FRIEREN**  
Fish.

Bocchi pauses mid-sweep.

She looks at the door.

Then Frieren.

Then the door again.

**BOCCHI**  
All of us?

Frieren looks at her.

**FRIEREN**  
If you want.

Bocchi considers the distance outside, then the fact that she has already managed the lane several times over the last few days.

**BOCCHI**  
...Okay.

Mau stands and immediately puts the wrong salvaged nails back into the wrong pile.

Frieren fixes them while passing.

Mau notices.

**MAU**  
I was going to do that.

Frieren keeps walking.

---

## Scene 2 — The Lake

The lake is farther than Bocchi expected and closer than she feared.

The path moves through forest and opens suddenly onto broad cold water framed by low hills and winter trees.

Bocchi stops when she sees it.

Mau keeps walking until he notices she did not.

**MAU**  
What?

**BOCCHI**  
Nothing.

She takes out her phone reflexively, then remembers there is no signal and no reason to photograph anything for anyone who can currently receive it.

She still takes the picture.

Mau watches.

**MAU**  
What does that do?

**BOCCHI**  
It saves pictures.

Mau leans slightly closer.

Bocchi immediately becomes aware of personal space and nearly drops the phone.

Frieren is already at the water.

She looks across the lake.

Her expression changes.

Mau notices first because he is watching her.

**MAU**  
What?

Frieren raises one hand slightly.

**FRIEREN**  
Stay here.

The ease leaves her voice.

Bocchi goes still.

Mau looks toward the far shore.

He sees nothing.

Frieren does.

Not with her eyes.

Something unfamiliar moves through the area.

Dense.

Structured.

Not mana.

But close enough to the idea of power that her instincts treat it seriously.

---

## Scene 3 — Yuta

On the opposite side of the lake, Yuta has already noticed them.

Or rather, Frieren.

The mana signature is enormous compared with anything he has encountered since arriving here.

He cannot identify the place.

He cannot identify the rules.

He has already woken in a body much younger than the one he remembers dying in.

Now something powerful has noticed him too.

His hand settles on his sword.

He does not draw immediately.

Frieren begins moving around the lake toward him.

Yuta watches.

The distance closes.

Bocchi and Mau remain behind at first, exactly as Frieren told them.

Bocchi whispers:

**BOCCHI**  
Is this normal?

Mau watches Frieren.

**MAU**  
I don't think so.

That answer is not comforting.

---

## Scene 4 — Misread

Frieren reaches a point where she can see Yuta clearly between the trees.

Young man.

Sword.

Power she cannot classify.

Yuta sees an elf-like mage carrying a staff and enough mana to erase any illusion that she is harmless.

Neither has enough context for the other.

Frieren stops.

Yuta does too.

**FRIEREN**  
Who are you?

**YUTA**  
I was going to ask you the same thing.

Not hostile.

Not friendly either.

Frieren studies his sword.

Yuta studies her staff.

A gust moves through the trees.

Yuta feels her mana shift.

He draws.

Frieren reacts to the draw.

Her staff comes up.

The mistake becomes mutual in less than a second.

Frieren fires a compact Zoltraak—not a city-breaking attack, but far too serious to ignore.

Yuta reinforces and moves.

The beam tears past as his sword catches enough of the edge to redirect his path rather than simply absorb it.

Ground cuts open behind him.

Yuta lands and immediately prepares to answer.

Frieren is already ready for that answer.

Then Mau arrives at the edge of the confrontation, breathing harder from catching up.

Bocchi is farther behind, having made the reasonable decision not to run directly toward two people firing supernatural attacks at each other.

Mau looks at Yuta.

Then Frieren.

Then the damaged ground.

**MAU**  
You're not from here either.

Both of them pause.

Yuta's weapon does not lower yet.

But the intention behind it changes.

**YUTA**  
...What?

Mau points vaguely around them.

**MAU**  
Neither are we.

Frieren looks at Mau.

Yuta looks at Frieren again.

The sentence creates a possibility neither had considered before the clash.

Yuta slowly lowers his sword.

Frieren lowers her staff a moment later.

Bocchi finally reaches them.

She sees the cut terrain.

Then the stranger with a sword.

**BOCCHI**  
I think I missed something.

No one answers immediately.

---

## Scene 5 — Four People by the Water

They do not become friends instantly.

They sit near the lake because nobody wants to walk all the way back while still unsure whether the stranger will attack again.

Yuta keeps his sword within reach but sheathed.

Frieren keeps her staff beside her.

Bocchi sits behind Mau enough that she can pretend it is accidental.

Mau starts with the simplest useful question.

**MAU**  
What's your name?

**YUTA**  
Yuta Okkotsu.

**MAU**  
I'm Mau.

Bocchi gives a small wave.

**BOCCHI**  
Hitori Gotoh.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Frieren.

Yuta looks at Frieren.

**YUTA**  
Sorry about the sword.

Frieren looks at the trench her spell created.

**FRIEREN**  
I fired first.

**YUTA**  
I drew first.

Mau looks between them.

**MAU**  
So you're both sorry.

Yuta lets out a short laugh before he can stop himself.

Frieren says nothing.

That is close enough to agreement.

---

## Scene 6 — Another World

Yuta's story does not fit theirs cleanly.

Japan means something to Bocchi.

It means nothing to Frieren.

Mau cannot tell whether it should mean something to him.

Yuta asks where they are.

Frieren gives the same answer she gave Bocchi.

**FRIEREN**  
We don't know.

Yuta looks across the lake.

**YUTA**  
Okay.

Bocchi is surprised by how calmly he takes it.

Yuta notices.

**YUTA**  
I've had stranger days.

Bocchi believes him immediately, which may not be rational.

The group starts back toward the settlement.

On the way, conversation comes in fragments rather than one stationary exposition block.

Yuta learns Frieren and Bocchi are from different places.

Mau has no accessible origin.

Yuta understands very quickly that Mau's `I don't know` is not evasive.

He does not interrogate him further.

That restraint registers with Mau.

---

## Scene 7 — The Body Problem

At the inn, Yuta stops in the doorway.

He looks at the damaged sign, boarded windows, and half-repaired common room.

**YUTA**  
You live here?

Mau:

**MAU**  
Mostly.

Frieren looks at him.

Mau:

**MAU**  
That's our standard now.

Bocchi looks down to hide a smile.

Inside, over food and warmth, Yuta finally explains the strangest part.

Not everything.

Enough.

**YUTA**  
I remember getting old.

Bocchi looks up.

Yuta touches his own face almost absently.

**YUTA**  
A lot older than this.

Mau studies him.

**MAU**  
How old?

Yuta pauses.

**YUTA**  
Old enough that this isn't the body I died in.

Silence.

Bocchi stops moving entirely.

Frieren's attention sharpens.

**BOCCHI**  
You died?

**YUTA**  
I think so.

He says it without drama because the drama happened somewhere else, a lifetime ago.

**YUTA**  
I remember the end. Then I woke up here like this.

Mau looks at his own hands.

**MAU**  
So this place changed you.

Yuta considers the wording.

**YUTA**  
Or rebuilt something. I don't know.

Frieren looks toward her travel case.

Her staff.

The ornament in her hair.

Things that should not have been with her when she fell asleep, yet arrived anyway.

The pattern is incomplete.

But it is a pattern.

---

## Scene 8 — Hope, Stopped

Frieren is quiet long enough that Bocchi notices.

Mau notices first.

Yuta keeps speaking softly with Bocchi about Japan, answering one or two practical questions.

Frieren looks at Yuta's young face.

Then at the ornament in her own hair.

**FRIEREN**  
Then maybe Fern can—

The words stop.

Nobody interrupts her.

Frieren looks down.

She knows what happens when possibility becomes the only thing she can think about.

Seven days of searching are still close enough to touch.

She changes the subject by standing.

**FRIEREN**  
We need more wood.

Bocchi looks at the existing pile.

There is enough wood for the night.

She does not say so.

Yuta also understands this is not about wood.

Mau watches Frieren reach for her cloak.

**MAU**  
I'll go.

Frieren looks at him.

He does not ask her to finish the sentence.

He simply goes with her.

Outside, they walk toward the nearest salvage pile.

For a while neither speaks.

Then Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
I shouldn't assume anything.

Mau picks up a piece of usable wood.

**MAU**  
Okay.

Frieren looks at him.

That is all he gives her.

It is enough.

---

## Scene 9 — Yuta Stays

Yuta does not ask for a permanent place.

He asks whether he can stay the night.

There are ten rooms and only three occupants.

The answer is obvious.

Frieren shows him the corridor.

He chooses a room with very little ceremony.

Bocchi watches him set his sword within immediate reach of the bed.

**BOCCHI**  
Do you sleep with that there?

**YUTA**  
Usually.

Bocchi looks at her guitar case in the neighboring room.

**BOCCHI**  
I sleep with mine there too.

Yuta glances at the guitar case.

**YUTA**  
Probably for different reasons.

Bocchi thinks.

**BOCCHI**  
...Mostly.

Yuta laughs softly.

The next morning he is still there.

No one formally asks whether he is joining them.

He simply sees Mau carrying a beam that is too awkward for one person and takes the other end.

Mau adjusts his grip.

**MAU**  
Thanks.

**YUTA**  
Where does it go?

Mau looks across the room.

**MAU**  
I was hoping you knew.

Yuta looks at him.

Then at the beam.

**YUTA**  
Okay. Put it down.

They do.

Frieren, passing behind them:

**FRIEREN**  
Storage.

Mau and Yuta look toward the storage area.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
We were close.

They were not.

---

## Scene 10 — Four

Later that day, Yuta goes out and returns with food far more efficiently than the others have managed.

Bocchi sees what he brought in.

**BOCCHI**  
You found all that today?

**YUTA**  
Yeah.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Can you teach me?

Yuta looks at him.

**YUTA**  
To hunt?

**MAU**  
To do that.

Mau gestures at the result, not the weapon.

Yuta understands.

**YUTA**  
Sure.

Frieren hears from across the room.

She looks over.

Not threatened.

Just noticing that Mau has asked someone else to teach him something.

That is new.

Bocchi takes one of the supplies toward the kitchen area.

Yuta reaches for another.

Mau follows.

Frieren returns to what she was doing.

The common room now has four people moving through it.

At dinner there are four bowls.

The inn remains mostly broken.

The table feels less empty.

**END S1E4.**
