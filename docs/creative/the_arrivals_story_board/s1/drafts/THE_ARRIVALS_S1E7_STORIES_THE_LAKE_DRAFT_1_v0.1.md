# S1E7 — `Stories / The Lake`

## Scene 1 — Rimuru's Probation

A couple of days have passed since Mau woke.

Rimuru is still at the inn.

That does not mean Frieren trusts him.

Morning.

Rimuru is trying to help repair a section of the common-room ceiling where water has stained the boards.

Frieren takes the ladder from him.

**RIMURU**  
I can do that.

**FRIEREN**  
I know.

She climbs instead.

Rimuru watches.

**RIMURU**  
That's somehow worse.

Yuta, carrying wood past them:

**YUTA**  
Give it time.

Frieren hears him.

**FRIEREN**  
I am.

Nobody argues.

Mau is nearby helping Bocchi untangle a string of salvaged wire from a pile of nails.

He looks normal again.

That is not enough to make Frieren stop checking him every few minutes.

Rimuru notices that too.

He also notices the shape of the group he entered: Yuta already knows where tools go, Bocchi knows which floorboard near the table squeaks, Mau moves between everyone without asking permission, Frieren's things are mixed with Mau's upstairs.

Rimuru has a chair.

He does not yet have a place in the rhythm.

---

## Scene 2 — A Normal Day Anyway

The day still requires work.

Yuta and Mau go check snares and gather food.

Bocchi works inside, then spends part of the afternoon outside the doorway repairing a torn sack because she has discovered she can be outdoors if she has something specific to look at.

Frieren checks the field and nearby forest.

Rimuru offers to come.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
Do you want me to stay here or just not come with you?

Frieren thinks.

**FRIEREN**  
Either is fine.

Rimuru looks at Bocchi.

Bocchi immediately looks away so she is not drafted into mediating.

Rimuru stays and fixes something else.

By evening everyone is tired enough that distrust becomes less important than food.

---

## Scene 3 — Dinner Starts Somewhere Else

Five people eat around the table.

Conversation does not begin with `tell us about your world.`

It begins because Rimuru tastes the stew, looks around the repaired ruin, and smiles at something only he can see.

**RIMURU**  
This reminds me of when my city was still basically a village.

Yuta looks up.

**YUTA**  
You had a city?

**RIMURU**  
Eventually.

Mau:

**MAU**  
How do you get a city?

Rimuru laughs.

**RIMURU**  
That's a long answer.

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
Did you build it?

Rimuru starts explaining, but the story immediately branches: monsters, people, allies, accidents, responsibilities, things that began small and became enormous.

He does not deliver a biography.

He remembers specific people while explaining specific problems.

That is what changes the room.

Yuta responds to one piece with a memory from his own life.

Bocchi recognizes the pattern and, after hesitating, mentions her band.

**BOCCHI**  
I was in a band.

Mau looks at her.

**MAU**  
The guitar people.

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
That's... one way to describe them.

Yuta smiles.

**YUTA**  
What were they called?

**BOCCHI**  
Kessoku Band.

The name changes Bocchi's face.

She tries to keep the sentence small.

It is not small to her.

She talks about Nijika, Ryo, Kita in fragments: rehearsal, live houses, food, things they said, embarrassing moments that are only embarrassing because she wants them back.

Nobody forces her to say she misses them.

It is obvious.

---

## Scene 4 — Frieren's Time

A comment from Yuta about how long ago something happened turns toward Frieren.

Rimuru asks how long she travelled with the hero party.

**FRIEREN**  
Ten years.

Rimuru waits.

**RIMURU**  
That's it?

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
It didn't feel like it.

The answer means something different to her than it does to everyone else.

Bocchi, who has learned not to fill every silence, lets this one remain.

Frieren mentions Himmel only because another part of the story makes him relevant.

Then Heiter.

Then Fern.

Not in an obituary.

In living fragments.

A spell somebody liked.

An argument.

Food.

A journey that took longer than expected.

Mau listens to all of it.

At first he is interested.

Then something starts changing.

Everyone's story produces another person.

Another place.

Another memory.

Even painful stories have shape.

Mau has nothing behind him to offer in return.

The more comfortable the table becomes, the more separate he feels inside it.

---

## Scene 5 — What About You?

Rimuru does not notice the change soon enough.

He is trying to make sure Mau is included.

That is exactly why he hurts him.

Conversation reaches a natural gap.

Rimuru looks toward Mau.

**RIMURU**  
What about you, Mau?

The room stills by degrees.

Bocchi realizes first.

Yuta second.

Frieren was already watching him.

Mau looks down at his bowl.

There is no anger.

No dramatic speech.

He simply stands.

The chair moves against the floor.

**MAU**  
I'm going outside.

Frieren rises immediately.

Mau is already through the door.

Frieren reaches for her cloak.

Yuta steps into her path—not forcefully.

**FRIEREN**  
Move.

**YUTA**  
I know where he's going.

Frieren's eyes narrow.

Yuta keeps his voice calm.

Unknown to her, Rika is already following Mau at a distance.

**YUTA**  
Let me go first.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Why?

Yuta glances toward the door Mau used.

**YUTA**  
Because he always comes to you.

Frieren says nothing.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Maybe he shouldn't have to this time.

That could sound accusatory from someone else.

From Yuta it is only an observation.

Frieren looks toward the door.

Then sits back down.

Not because she is less worried.

Because she is trying to trust someone else with it.

Yuta leaves.

---

## Scene 6 — The Lake Again

Mau is at the lake.

The same water where they first met Yuta.

He sits near the edge with his knees pulled up, looking at a reflection that tells him nothing.

Rika remains far enough away that Mau does not notice her clearly.

Yuta approaches without hiding his footsteps.

Mau does not turn.

Yuta sits a short distance away.

Silence.

A long one.

Finally:

**YUTA**  
Bocchi told me you don't remember anything.

Mau's eyes stay on the water.

**MAU**  
I remember things.

Yuta waits.

**MAU**  
I know what a tree is. I know what snow is. I know how to talk.

Beat.

**MAU**  
I don't remember me.

Yuta nods slowly.

**YUTA**  
I don't know what that feels like.

Mau looks at him for the first time.

The sentence surprises him because it refuses the easy lie.

Yuta picks up a small stone and turns it in his hand.

**YUTA**  
I remember too much, sometimes.

He does not compare the experiences as equal.

**YUTA**  
That doesn't mean I know what you should do with nothing.

Mau looks back at the lake.

**MAU**  
Everybody knows who they were.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Mostly.

Mau gives him a look.

Yuta smiles faintly.

The shared joke is small enough not to break the mood.

Then Yuta adds:

**YUTA**  
Remembering a whole life doesn't always tell you who you are now.

Mau thinks about that.

**MAU**  
Does it help?

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Sometimes.

No lesson follows.

Wind moves across the water.

After a while Yuta says:

**YUTA**  
When you were asleep, Frieren barely left the room.

Mau looks toward him.

**YUTA**  
Bocchi kept bringing her guitar upstairs because she thought you'd hear it.

Mau says nothing.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
You don't have a story to tell them yet.

Pause.

**YUTA**  
That doesn't mean you're not in theirs.

Yuta does not know if that helps.

He does not ask.

---

## Scene 7 — A Few Steps Back

Frieren and Bocchi eventually arrive.

Not immediately.

Frieren has waited longer than she wanted to.

Bocchi came because she wanted to, not because Frieren needed an escort.

Yuta sees them first.

Mau turns.

Frieren stops several steps away.

Every instinct tells her to close the distance.

She does not.

Bocchi continues past her.

She reaches Mau, visibly unsure what to do with her hands.

**BOCCHI**  
Um.

Mau waits.

Bocchi had a better version of this sentence in her head while walking.

It is gone now.

So she uses the simple one he gave her.

**BOCCHI**  
We're here to help you too.

Mau looks at her.

The callback lands without anyone explaining it.

Bocchi swallows.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Okay.

That is all.

Bocchi sits beside him.

Yuta shifts slightly to make room.

Frieren remains standing behind them for another beat.

Then Mau looks back at her.

Frieren comes closer.

Not first.

But not absent.

---

## Scene 8 — Back Home

They return after dark.

Rimuru has reheated food.

He apologizes to Mau before Mau can say anything.

**RIMURU**  
I wasn't thinking.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I know.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That doesn't make it better.

Mau considers.

**MAU**  
No.

Rimuru accepts that too.

Then Mau sits down and eats.

The conversation does not restart the story-sharing exercise.

Nobody asks him for memories.

They talk about the food instead.

The room recovers through ordinary things.

---

## Scene 9 — I'm Getting There

Night.

Frieren and Mau are in the room that has become functionally shared through repetition rather than declaration.

Mau is sitting near the window.

Frieren is putting away the book she did not read downstairs.

A long quiet.

**FRIEREN**  
Do you feel better?

Mau thinks.

Then looks at her.

**MAU**  
Do you?

Frieren pauses.

Fern's ornament is still in her hair.

**FRIEREN**  
I'm getting there.

Mau nods.

**MAU**  
Okay.

No promise that either of them is fixed.

Outside, the lake is invisible in the dark.

Inside, Mau is no longer dependent on one person for every kind of care.

Frieren knows that now too.

**END S1E7.**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- Frieren remains guarded around Rimuru after E6; trust is not resolved here.
- Dinner story-sharing emerges through associative conversation, not formal turns.
- Rimuru's `What about you, Mau?` is well-intentioned.
- Mau rupture is quiet; he does not deliver a speech before leaving.
- Yuta explicitly does **not** claim to understand amnesia.
- Yuta has Rika discreetly tracking Mau before stopping Frieren; Frieren need not know that detail yet.
- Frieren's growth is shown by allowing Yuta to go first and later staying a few steps back while Bocchi reaches Mau.
- Bocchi line `We're here to help you too.` is locked payoff to E3.
- Closing exchange remains exact in intent and wording.
- E7 expands Mau's support network; it does not cure the identity wound.
