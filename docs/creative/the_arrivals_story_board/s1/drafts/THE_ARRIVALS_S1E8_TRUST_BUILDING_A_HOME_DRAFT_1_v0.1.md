# S1E8 — `Trust / Building a Home`

## Scene 1 — A Reason to Go Together

The rain has replaced the snow for two days.

Water finds every weak point in an old building.

A new leak appears above storage. A section of timber near the rear wall has softened. One of the wells outside has debris in it that nobody wants to investigate by hand.

Rimuru studies the problems with the expression of someone who has spent too much time pretending not to notice them.

**RIMURU**  
We need better materials.

Frieren, reading at the table:

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

**RIMURU**  
There was stonework east of here. Maybe a cave or an old structure.

Frieren looks up.

Yuta is already out checking a route. Bocchi is repairing cloth near the fire. Mau is outside helping move salvaged wood.

Rimuru realizes Frieren is assessing whether he is trying to get her alone.

**RIMURU**  
You can bring someone else.

**FRIEREN**  
No.

Rimuru is unsure whether that means *no, I won't go* or *no, I don't need someone else.*

Frieren closes the book.

**FRIEREN**  
I'll go.

That answers it.

---

## Scene 2 — The Structure

The site is less a dungeon than a collapsed stone structure cut into a hillside.

Old supports. Natural cave behind worked stone. Enough intact material to be useful if they can determine what is safe to remove.

Frieren and Rimuru work without talking much at first.

Frieren checks magical residue and structural stability.

Rimuru checks the material itself and what can be moved without collapsing the rest.

They are effective together despite the tension.

That may be the first thing Frieren dislikes about the situation.

At one point Rimuru reaches toward a fractured support.

Frieren catches his wrist.

**FRIEREN**  
Not that one.

Rimuru looks up.

Hairline cracks continue into the ceiling.

**RIMURU**  
Right.

She lets go.

They move to another section.

Eventually the silence is no longer useful.

Frieren asks the question she has been holding since E6.

**FRIEREN**  
What did Mau say?

Rimuru stops.

He knows exactly which moment she means.

**RIMURU**  
Before he collapsed?

**FRIEREN**  
Yes.

Rimuru looks away.

For the first time since arriving, he seems less worried about Frieren's suspicion than about answering honestly.

Inside, Raphael speaks privately.

**RAPHAEL**  
Recommendation: answer truthfully.

Rimuru exhales.

**RIMURU**  
He said `Raphael.`

Frieren's expression changes by very little.

**FRIEREN**  
Who is Raphael?

---

## Scene 3 — The Private Thing

Rimuru sits on a fallen stone block.

He does not enjoy explaining this to someone who still partly suspects him.

**RIMURU**  
She talks to me.

Frieren waits.

Rimuru realizes that sentence was not enough.

**RIMURU**  
Inside my head. She thinks with me. Helps me understand things. Other people can't hear her.

Frieren studies him.

**FRIEREN**  
And Mau knew her name.

**RIMURU**  
Yes.

**FRIEREN**  
Because she was doing something to him.

Rimuru starts to answer.

Raphael corrects him first, privately and precisely.

No hostile action was performed. Ordinary passive analytical functions encountered a subject that did not classify normally. No detected mechanism explains the collapse.

Rimuru relays the correction instead of smoothing it into something more comforting.

**RIMURU**  
Not exactly. Raphael says she wasn't attacking him. She was doing what she does in the background—trying to understand what was around me.

Frieren's eyes narrow.

**FRIEREN**  
Trying to analyze him.

**RIMURU**  
Passively. But yes.

He does not hide the uncomfortable word.

**RIMURU**  
It didn't work.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Why?

Rimuru shakes his head.

**RIMURU**  
We don't know.

Frieren's attention sharpens at the plural.

Rimuru continues before she asks.

**RIMURU**  
She can't classify him properly. She doesn't know why he knew her name. She doesn't know why he collapsed.

Rimuru pauses.

**RIMURU**  
And we stopped.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
Stopped what?

**RIMURU**  
Anything that might trigger it again.

A beat.

**RIMURU**  
I'm curious. Raphael is curious. That doesn't matter more than Mau getting hurt.

Frieren watches him for a long moment.

There is still a gap between what Rimuru says and what she can verify.

**FRIEREN**  
I don't know if I believe you.

Rimuru accepts that without flinching.

Frieren adds:

**FRIEREN**  
But I'll try.

Rimuru looks at her.

That is more than he expected.

**RIMURU**  
Okay.

The conversation ends not because every question is answered, but because the remaining answers do not exist.

Raphael never mentions The Noise.

She cannot.

She does not know it is there.

---

## Scene 4 — Carrying Stone

The return trip is awkward for about ten minutes.

Then a large salvaged stone block becomes more urgent than emotional tension.

Rimuru offers to carry most of the load using abilities Frieren has not seen directly yet.

She watches him prepare.

**FRIEREN**  
What are you doing?

**RIMURU**  
Making this easier.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
That usually means something strange with you.

Rimuru laughs.

**RIMURU**  
You're learning.

He demonstrates only what is useful and bounded: storage/manipulation sufficient to move material, not a theatrical catalogue of power.

Frieren observes.

She does not look alarmed.

She looks annoyed that this would have been useful earlier.

**FRIEREN**  
You could've done that with the first load.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
You didn't trust me with the first load.

Frieren thinks.

**FRIEREN**  
Fair.

It is the first openly easy exchange between them.

---

## Scene 5 — The Question Rimuru Asks

Back at the inn, everyone notices the amount of usable material they returned with.

Mau circles the pile.

**MAU**  
That's a lot.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
And it's still not enough if we're going to keep patching one leak at a time forever.

Yuta has returned and looks at the ceiling.

A drip lands in the familiar bucket.

Bocchi looks at the bucket.

**BOCCHI**  
It has personality now.

Rimuru points at it.

**RIMURU**  
It has to go.

Mau:

**MAU**  
The bucket?

**RIMURU**  
The leak.

Mau nods.

Rimuru looks around the room.

Then at all of them.

**RIMURU**  
What do we actually need?

Frieren expects a list of structural necessities.

Rimuru means more than that.

Yuta says storage first.

Frieren says roof and insulation.

Mau says a place where the floor does not move when he steps on it.

Bocchi says nothing.

Rimuru looks at her.

She immediately regrets being visible.

**RIMURU**  
What about you?

Bocchi glances toward her guitar.

**BOCCHI**  
Maybe... somewhere I can play without bothering everyone?

Mau:

**MAU**  
You don't bother everyone.

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
That's not the point.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
She's right. A room would be good.

Rimuru begins mentally arranging the building.

**RIMURU**  
Okay. Music room.

Bocchi blinks.

**BOCCHI**  
A whole room?

**RIMURU**  
We have ten rooms and five people.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Mostly usable rooms.

Rimuru points at him.

**RIMURU**  
We're fixing that too.

---

## Scene 6 — The Bath Problem

The conversation spreads.

A kitchen that does not rely on one damaged counter.

Dry storage.

Better heating.

A bath.

That one stops everyone.

Bocchi looks at Rimuru as if he has offered civilization itself.

**BOCCHI**  
A bath?

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
Yes.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
A real one?

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
That was the idea.

Mau:

**MAU**  
What's wrong with the basin?

Everyone looks at him.

Mau looks back.

**MAU**  
What?

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
Nothing.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
We'll show you.

Frieren has already accepted the bath proposal as obviously useful.

Rimuru also suggests simple perimeter warnings and a better fence, not because an attack is imminent but because they sleep better if the settlement tells them when something enters it.

Threads. Bells. Sight lines. Nothing that turns the place into a fortress.

The list stops looking like emergency survival.

It starts looking like preferences.

---

## Scene 7 — Everyone Builds

Rimuru does not build Tempest around them.

He coordinates.

That distinction matters.

Yuta handles heavy work and perimeter posts.

Frieren uses practical magic where it saves labor but does not replace craft.

Mau learns to measure before cutting after wasting one board and being stared at by three people simultaneously.

Bocchi helps clear and insulate the room that will become music space.

Rimuru organizes supplies, transforms or processes materials where appropriate, and keeps changing the plan when reality disagrees with it.

The work stretches over days.

Hands become dirty.

Meals get eaten late.

One wall is opened and reveals more damage than expected.

**RIMURU**  
I hate old buildings.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
You wanted to fix it.

**RIMURU**  
I still hate it.

Mau, from inside the wall opening:

**MAU**  
There's another board broken in here.

Rimuru closes his eyes.

**RIMURU**  
Of course there is.

---

## Scene 8 — Bocchi's Room

The music room begins as one upstairs room with reinforced walls and improvised sound damping.

Bocchi stands in the doorway after they finish the first usable version.

It is not studio quality.

It is not even pretty.

It is hers to use.

Rimuru gestures.

**RIMURU**  
Try it.

Bocchi enters with guitar.

The others wait outside.

She plays one loud chord.

Everyone hears it.

But much less than before.

Bocchi opens the door again, eyes wide.

**BOCCHI**  
I can be louder.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
That's what you're excited about?

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
You don't understand.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I don't.

Bocchi closes the door in their faces and plays louder.

The group stands in the corridor listening to the muffled guitar.

Rimuru smiles.

For the first time, the thing he added is not necessary for survival at all.

That is why it matters.

---

## Scene 9 — Trust Without a Speech

Later, Rimuru is installing a simple alert line near the side entrance.

He needs a small metal piece held at a specific height while he ties it off.

He looks around.

Frieren is passing with a book under one arm.

**RIMURU**  
Can you hold this?

Without stopping to reconsider him, Frieren takes the piece.

Rimuru ties the line.

Several seconds pass before either notices what happened.

Rimuru looks at her hand holding the part.

Frieren follows his gaze.

**FRIEREN**  
What?

Rimuru smiles.

**RIMURU**  
Nothing.

Frieren looks suspicious of the answer but keeps holding the piece until he is done.

No one declares the trust issue solved.

It is not.

But something has changed.

---

## Scene 10 — Mau Learns From Someone Else

Mau and Rimuru are trying to fit a new hinge.

Mau asks how Rimuru knew the angle was wrong.

Rimuru shows him rather than explaining abstractly.

Frieren passes and hears Mau ask another question.

In E5 she noticed Mau learning from Yuta.

This time she does not pause.

She simply leaves a tool beside them that they are going to need in a minute.

Mau sees it.

**MAU**  
Thanks.

Frieren, already walking away:

**FRIEREN**  
Mm.

Rimuru looks between them.

**RIMURU**  
You two do that a lot.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Do what?

Rimuru decides not to explain.

**RIMURU**  
Nothing.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Everyone keeps saying that.

---

## Scene 11 — Building a Home

The improvements remain modest.

The roof no longer leaks into the common room.

The kitchen has a real work surface and better storage.

A simple bath works.

The rooms are drier and warmer.

Bocchi has somewhere to play.

The perimeter has a low fence and basic bells/threads at vulnerable approaches.

Nothing looks rich.

Everything looks chosen.

At dinner, the table has scratches from work and five bowls again.

Rimuru sits where the empty negative space used to isolate him.

Nobody assigned the seat.

That is simply where he sits now.

Mau gets up halfway through dinner because he hears one of the new bells outside.

Yuta starts to rise.

Frieren listens for a beat.

A small animal passes the window.

She keeps eating.

**FRIEREN**  
Not dangerous.

Mau sits back down.

Bocchi, from the table:

**BOCCHI**  
The deer?

Mau looks toward the window.

Yuta groans.

Rimuru has no idea why everyone is laughing.

The camera/page pulls wider.

The inn that E2 called `somewhere to stay` has changed without anyone announcing the change.

Five people made things here because they expect to use them tomorrow.

That is enough.

**END S1E8.**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- Frieren learns `Raphael` only in E8 from Rimuru.
- Raphael's disclosure is voluntary/private trust-building; she remains inaudible to others.
- Raphael corrects the `doing something hostile` framing and only claims passive analytical processing.
- Raphael cannot analyze Mau properly, cannot explain name recognition, and cannot detect The Noise at all.
- Rimuru/Raphael explicitly refuse further attempts that could risk Mau.
- Frieren trust line preserved exactly: `I don't know if I believe you. But I'll try.`
- Trust is subsequently shown visually through ordinary cooperation, especially Frieren automatically holding a piece for Rimuru.
- Home improvements are collective and modest; Rimuru coordinates rather than replacing everyone.
- Music room/bath/quality-of-life choices prove the group is moving beyond survival.
- E2 `somewhere to stay` is paid off visually without repeating the phrase as a speech.
