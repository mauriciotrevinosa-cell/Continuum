# S1E9 — `Making It Ours`

## Scene 1 — Shelves

The problem with improving a house is that improved houses create new problems.

Rimuru has added usable storage.

Frieren has filled it with books.

Mau stands in the common room holding three grimoires while staring at a shelf that is already full.

**MAU**  
There was space yesterday.

Frieren, reading at the table:

**FRIEREN**  
There was.

Mau looks at the books.

Then at Frieren.

**MAU**  
What happened?

Frieren turns a page.

**FRIEREN**  
Books.

Yuta passes behind Mau carrying supplies.

**YUTA**  
You walked into that one.

Mau follows him with the grimoires.

**MAU**  
I need another shelf.

Rimuru, from across the room:

**RIMURU**  
Everyone needs another shelf.

Bocchi opens the music-room door.

**BOCCHI**  
I don't.

Rimuru points toward the collection of cables, cloth, spare strings and small objects already accumulating in her room.

Bocchi closes the door again.

**RIMURU**  
You do.

---

## Scene 2 — Rooms That Say Something

The house has enough space now that people begin shaping it unconsciously.

Yuta's room is almost aggressively sparse.

Bed. Sword. A few practical items. Almost nothing decorative.

Mau stands in the doorway.

**MAU**  
You don't have anything.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
I have things.

Mau looks around.

**MAU**  
Where?

Yuta points at the sword.

Mau waits.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
I see your point.

Bocchi's music room is the opposite: not cluttered yet, but rapidly developing recognizable zones. Guitar. Case. Chair. Notes. Small pieces of fabric. Things she moved there because they made the room feel safer.

Rimuru's space becomes half bedroom, half planning station despite his attempts not to let that happen.

Frieren's room is where the continuity is clearest.

Her books.

Her travel case.

Her clothes.

And Mau's everyday things mixed among them without visible boundary.

Mau technically has another assigned room down the corridor.

It contains grimoires, spare books, things he studies, and objects with no better place to go.

It is not where he ends his day.

No one calls Frieren's room `their room` yet.

They simply both live there.

---

## Scene 3 — The Plant

Late morning.

Mau puts on his cloak.

Yuta is checking a strap near the door. Bocchi is sitting on the stair with guitar picks in her hand. Rimuru is measuring a section of wall.

Frieren looks up from a book.

**FRIEREN**  
Where are you going?

Mau:

**MAU**  
To check on my plant.

Frieren's expression softens into the smallest smile.

Yuta looks between them.

**YUTA**  
Your plant?

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
You have a plant?

Mau is already opening the door.

**MAU**  
Yes.

They both look toward Frieren for context.

Frieren has returned to her book.

**FRIEREN**  
Nothing.

Mau steps outside.

Frieren looks up again before the door closes.

**FRIEREN**  
Before dark.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Okay.

She lets him go alone.

That would have been unthinkable in E1.

---

## Scene 4 — The E1 Clearing

Mau remembers the route better than he expected.

The forest has changed with weather, but certain shapes remain.

The stream.

The slope.

The clearing.

The place where he woke.

Most of Frieren's flowers are gone now.

A few natural descendants or surviving plants remain around the area, ordinary and small.

Mau kneels where he remembers touching the first flower.

He does not know what he is trying to prove.

He raises one hand.

Nothing happens.

He tries to remember Frieren's movement.

The light.

The way the field appeared.

He has watched her use mundane magic many times since then: cleaning, heating, moving, fixing, gathering.

He imitates the intention more than the gesture.

Nothing.

A faint pressure scratches at the edge of thought.

A broken internal fragment—too incomplete to instruct him.

`...form...`

Mau winces.

Then silence.

He tries again.

No flower.

No spark.

No spell.

Only his hand over cold grass.

Mau lowers it, embarrassed despite being alone.

**MAU**  
...Okay.

He sits there for a while.

Before leaving, he finds one real flower growing near the edge of the clearing.

He carefully takes it with enough stem to keep it alive for the walk home.

That is the only flower he succeeds in bringing back.

---

## Scene 5 — Frieren Notices Watching

Earlier that week, Mau's attention around magic had already become more focused.

The pattern continues after he returns.

Frieren uses a small spell to dry damp cloth near the kitchen.

Mau watches her hand, the spell structure, the effect.

Too carefully.

Frieren notices without turning fully toward him.

**FRIEREN**  
What?

Mau looks away a little too fast.

**MAU**  
Nothing.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
You were staring.

Mau:

**MAU**  
I was looking.

Frieren finally looks at him.

Mau realizes that distinction did not help.

**MAU**  
It was interesting.

Frieren waits for him to explain why.

Mau does not know why.

**MAU**  
I don't know.

Frieren studies him.

Then lets it go.

She has begun noticing these little moments because she knows his routines better than anyone else.

She does not yet connect them to anything larger.

---

## Scene 6 — The Soundproof Room Works Too Well

Bocchi's room is now good enough that when the door is closed, the common room hears only a dull suggestion of guitar.

This creates a new problem.

Bocchi cannot hear people calling her either.

Rimuru has been standing outside the closed door for several seconds.

He knocks.

Nothing.

He knocks harder.

Nothing.

Yuta walks by.

**YUTA**  
She's playing.

**RIMURU**  
I know.

**YUTA**  
Then she can't hear you.

Rimuru looks at the wall he personally helped soundproof.

**RIMURU**  
I know.

Mau arrives carrying a board.

**MAU**  
Why are you both standing here?

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Rimuru succeeded.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
Too much.

Eventually Bocchi opens the door on her own and nearly walks into all three of them.

She freezes.

**BOCCHI**  
...Were you waiting for me?

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
We need a bell.

Bocchi slowly closes the door again.

---

## Scene 7 — Mau's Nominal Room

Mau finally gets his additional shelf.

It goes into his nominal room because Frieren's room has no sensible wall space left.

Rimuru helps level it.

**RIMURU**  
You actually use this room?

Mau looks around.

**MAU**  
Yes.

Rimuru waits.

Mau points at books.

**MAU**  
For these.

Rimuru looks at the bed frame with nothing on it.

Then the room's complete absence of everyday clothing or sleep things.

He understands enough not to comment.

**RIMURU**  
Another shelf on that wall later.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Good.

They continue working.

The room is real, but it is a study/overflow space—not Mau's lived center.

---

## Scene 8 — Dinner Without a Plot

Dinner is messy in the comfortable way E5 started and E8 accelerated.

Two conversations happen at once.

Bocchi complains about the proposed bell system for her music-room door.

Rimuru says the alternative is people opening the door while she plays.

Bocchi immediately accepts the bell.

Yuta asks Mau where he went.

Mau:

**MAU**  
My plant.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Right. The secret plant.

Frieren:

**FRIEREN**  
It's not secret.

Yuta:

**YUTA**  
Then what is it?

Frieren takes a bite.

**FRIEREN**  
A plant.

Mau looks pleased with that answer.

Bocchi:

**BOCCHI**  
You two are impossible.

Rimuru:

**RIMURU**  
I'm starting to understand that.

The conversation moves on before anyone notices Mau has not explained what he actually did there.

---

## Scene 9 — The Flower

Night.

Mau and Frieren go upstairs.

The room is unmistakably inhabited by both of them now.

Not through matching decorations.

Through overlap.

A Frieren book with Mau's small repair tool beside it.

His clothes folded near her travel case.

A cup he uses more often than she does.

A stack of grimoires he brought down from his nominal room because he was reading them in bed.

Mau takes the flower from where he kept it protected during dinner.

Frieren watches him place it in a small container with water near the window.

**FRIEREN**  
You brought one back.

Mau:

**MAU**  
Yes.

Frieren looks at the flower.

Then at him.

**FRIEREN**  
Why?

Mau considers telling her about the failed attempt.

He does not.

**MAU**  
I liked it.

Frieren accepts the answer.

Mau adjusts the container until it sits among their mixed belongings.

He has had very little opportunity to choose objects for himself.

This one he chose.

Frieren returns to her book.

Mau sits beside her.

The flower remains by the window.

No magic.

No success.

Just something real that he carried home.

**END S1E9.**

## Stage 3 continuity notes

- Mau technically owns/has an assigned room, but functionally lives with Frieren; everyday belongings are mixed in Frieren's room.
- Mau nominal room remains study/grimoires/overflow/storage and is not his emotional home base.
- `To check on my plant.` is preserved; Frieren understands and smiles, others do not.
- Frieren lets Mau go alone and only asks him to return before dark.
- Flower reconstruction attempt is private, fails completely, and is not disclosed to Frieren.
- The Noise is only an incomplete fragment (`...form...`) and gives no usable instruction.
- No training arc/power unlock begins.
- Frieren notices Mau watching mundane magic unusually closely, but Mau cannot explain why and evades.
- Mau brings back one real flower; it becomes his first deliberately chosen object placed in the functionally shared room.
