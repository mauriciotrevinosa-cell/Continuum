### Pacing note

E19 is intentionally much longer than the normal episode range because it is carrying a season-finale braid rather than a single domestic or mission spine. The G3 travel material needs elapsed-time texture so the world does not collapse geographically; the G1+G2 night needs enough quiet space to feel genuinely safe before the daylight Sukuna cut. The final Sukuna chapter should be sharp and comparatively fast once recognition occurs.
