# Stage 4 Production QC

- Reunion starts with Fern walking normally; Frieren crosses distance first.
- `Not for me.` must land after seven-day mismatch with visual space.
- Fern/Mau dynamic must not imply rivalry.
- Nine-person dinner should feel crowded/overlapping, not exposition circle.
- Overprotection is trauma behavior shown through action, not explained by speech.
- Shared-room continuity must match E9 exactly; missing Mau objects should be recognizable to reader.
- Mau retreat is considerate and unilateral, not a mutual decision or fight.
- Final `Stay.` has double emotional pressure and should not be over-narrated.
