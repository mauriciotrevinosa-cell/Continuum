## Production rule

This script adapts the approved E2 story into manga without adding new story. Dialogue may be reduced during rough production when expression, staging, silence, environment, or page rhythm communicates the same beat more effectively.

The 46-page count is provisional. Rough manga may naturally expand or contract individual pages if readability or acting requires it. Story remains the governing constraint.

## Recommended chapter structure

- **Chapter 1 — pp. 1–24:** morning after E1 through discovery/survey of the abandoned settlement and the decision to use the inn. End with first snow visible through the cracked upstairs window and Frieren's `Tonight.`
- **Chapter 2 — pp. 25–46:** making a small part of the inn usable, field/well survey, `home` conversation, first dinner/night, Fern conversation, and Bocchi's off-screen scream. End without revealing Bocchi.

# SCENE 1 — MORNING AFTER
**Pages 1–5**

### Page 1 — shared blanket
- Quiet opening page; gray dawn through trees.
- Fire collapsed to coals.
- Frieren and Mau asleep under the same blanket, most of it accidentally pulled over Mau.
- Frieren wakes first and studies the situation without expression.
- She pulls enough blanket back to cover herself.
- Mau's eyes open.

### Page 2 — leaving
- **MAU:** `Morning.`
- **FRIEREN:** `Morning.`
- Frieren begins packing immediately.
- Mau sits up, looking around at the same unknown world.
- **MAU:** `Are we leaving?`
- **FRIEREN:** `Yes.`

### Page 3 — no destination
- Mau watches Frieren close the travel case.
- **MAU:** `Where are we going?`
- **FRIEREN:** `I don't know.`
- Beat on Mau waiting for more.
- **MAU:** `Then how do you know we're going the right way?`

### Page 4 — Frieren logic
- Frieren looks toward the route ahead.
- **FRIEREN:** `That way is less steep.`
- Mau looks at the slope and accepts the logic.
- They finish packing.
- No extra explanation.

### Page 5 — flower callback
- Mau notices the remaining E1 flowers in the grass.
- Close on his hand almost reaching, then stopping.
- He leaves them where they are.
- Wide panel: Mau follows Frieren into the trees.
- No dialogue.

# SCENE 3 — THE SETTLEMENT
**Pages 10–15**

### Page 10 — first ruined house
- Reveal first damaged house.
- Wall partly collapsed; door open; weeds through step.
- Mau slows.
- No dialogue.

### Page 11 — settlement reveal
- Page opens wider: lane, damaged houses, shed, stone-bordered field, larger inn farther in.
- No smoke, no visible people.
- **MAU:** `There were people here.`
- **FRIEREN:** `Yes.`

### Page 12 — where did they go
- Empty windows and lane details.
- **MAU:** `Where did they go?`
- **FRIEREN:** `I don't know.`
- Mau gives her a look.
- **MAU:** `You say that a lot.`

### Page 13 — a lot unknown
- Frieren glances at him.
- **FRIEREN:** `There's a lot I don't know.`

### Page 14 — evidence
- Frieren inspects a cold chimney.
- Detail of road with no recent tracks.
- Rain barrel with old leaves/standing debris.
- Mau by a broken handcart.
- **MAU:** `How do you know nobody lives here?`

### Page 15 — not maintained
- Frieren points out the practical evidence rather than lecturing.
- **FRIEREN:** `Nobody's been taking care of it.`
- Mau looks back at the settlement with new understanding.
- End on the larger inn building.

# SCENE 4 — THE INN
**Pages 16–24**

### Page 16 — exterior
- Full readable exterior of the abandoned inn: timber/stone, damaged but standing, faded hanging sign on one chain.
- Mau tries the stuck entrance door.
- It does not move.

### Page 17 — door
- Mau pushes harder; old hinge groans.
- Frieren uses a small practical spell to shift the warped frame.
- Door opens.
- **MAU:** `You could've done that first.`
- **FRIEREN:** `You were already pushing.`

### Page 18 — common room
- First interior reveal: counter, tables, fireplace, broken glass, water damage.
- Use composition that can later be visually echoed in E17/E19 after the inn becomes lived-in.
- Mau walks into center and looks up.
- **MAU:** `This is bad.`

### Page 19 — mostly dry
- Frieren checks hearth/chimney/roof line.
- **FRIEREN:** `Mostly dry.`
- Mau turns.
- **MAU:** `That's your standard?`
- **FRIEREN:** `For now.`

### Page 20 — stairs
- Frieren heads upstairs without ceremony.
- Mau follows.
- Corridor reveal: roughly ten guest rooms; some doors missing, visible damage varies.
- No dialogue.

### Page 21 — room survey
- Frieren checks one room, then another.
- Mau watches methodology: roof, wall, window, floor.
- One room has intact structure but cracked window/usable bed frame.
- Establish geography for future room continuity.

### Page 22 — stay?
- Mau stands in the usable room.
- **MAU:** `Are we staying here?`
- Frieren looks through cracked window.
- First tiny snowflakes outside.
- Beat.

### Page 23 — snow beginning
- More snow drifting past window.
- Frieren's attention remains practical rather than sentimental.
- **FRIEREN:** `Tonight.`
- Mau looks from her to the room.
- He accepts it.

### Page 24 — CHAPTER 1 END
- Wide exterior of inn and tiny settlement as snow begins.
- Frieren and Mau small in an upstairs window / interior silhouette.
- No narration.
- **End Chapter 1.**

# CHAPTER 2 / SCENE 5 — MAKING ONE ROOM USABLE

### Page 25 — cleanup begins
- Broken furniture dragged aside.

- Mau tries to lift a damaged table incorrectly.
- **FRIEREN:** `Use both hands.`
- **MAU:** `I am.` after correcting grip.

### Page 26 — cleaning magic, not restoration
- Frieren uses a mundane cleaning spell on soot/dust.
- Visual distinction: dirt clears, but cracked boards/windows/roof remain damaged.
- Important production guardrail: magic does not turn inn pristine.

### Page 27 — glass lesson
- Mau carries broken glass outside.
- Begins placing it near natural walking line.
- **FRIEREN:** `Not there.`
- **MAU:** `Why?`
- Frieren points to where feet would pass.
- Mau relocates it safely without further dialogue.

### Page 28 — boarded window
- Mau holds salvaged board over broken common-room window.
- **MAU:** `Higher?`
- **FRIEREN:** `A little.`
- He goes too far.
- **FRIEREN:** `Less.`
- Dry physical comedy, not chibi-heavy.

### Page 29 — improvement
- Frieren fixes board with salvaged nails/hardware.
- Wind through gap decreases.

- **MAU:** `Still cold.`

### Page 30 — callback
- Mau gives her the same look as earlier.
- **MAU:** `That's also your standard?`
- Frieren actually considers.
- **FRIEREN:** `For now.`
- Tiny almost-smile possibility on Mau.

### Page 31 — first usable zone
- Wide common-room composition: fire functioning, debris consolidated, one window boarded, travel things organized, two bedroll areas beginning to exist.
- Still clearly a ruin.
- No dialogue.

# SCENE 7 — FIRST SNOW / HOME

## Environment

- Establish recurring visual anchors for later episodes: common-room fireplace, long table/counter, upstairs corridor, shared usable room, boarded window, field, well, lane, exterior sign.

- Do not label the shared room as emotionally significant yet.
