# Stage 4 Production QC

## Story / thematic chain
- Preserve visible E1→E3 care transfer: Frieren's E1 `But I'll help.` → Mau's E3 `But I'll help you.` → future Bocchi E7 `We're here to help you too.`
- `Who's helping you?` must remain unresolved here.
- Do not insert another Arrival cliffhanger.

## Character
- Bocchi's opening scream is situational; subsequent anxiety is primarily hesitation, catastrophizing, body language and avoidance.
- Do not make Bocchi a constant chibi/meme figure; emotional observation must remain credible.
- Mau is patient/direct, never a motivational-speaker archetype.
- Frieren's grief is sparse, repetitive and physically shown; do not expand into a clean explanatory monologue.

## Environment / continuity
- Reuse E2 inn geography exactly.
- Add Bocchi's room as one of the already-established usable rooms; do not change inn scale.
- Household occupancy change should be communicated through objects and use of space, not renovation.
- Common room remains visually sparse enough that later E8/E9 upgrades have room to register.

## Visual-first adaptation
- Bocchi's internal catastrophizing should be conveyed largely via expression/composition; avoid heavy internal-text boxes.
- The night scene should rely on stairwell framing, partial visibility, hands/sleeve/blanket and silence.
- The multi-day montage should not use day-number title cards.
