# The Arrivals — Future Ideas Board v0.1

**Status:** PROVISIONAL FUTURE IDEAS BOARD — NOT EPISODE-LOCKED  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Purpose:** keep important creator-directed ideas visible before they receive exact episode numbers, final placement, or full scene scripts.

This board is a **navigation / memory document**, not locked canon. It exists so strong ideas do not disappear just because they are not yet assigned to an episode. When a beat is promoted into an episode structure, draft, panel script, or other authoritative document, that later document becomes the production source of truth.

---

## How to read this board

- **Direction** means the creator currently wants to preserve the idea.
- **Placement open** means the exact episode / chapter / scene is intentionally undecided.
- **Dialogue approximate** means the emotional meaning is preserved but wording can still change.
- **Not canon yet** means this board must never silently override an approved episode document.

## 1. Sukuna / Second Chance major arc

**Standing:** strong S2 direction; placement open; major choreography still open.  
**Source provenance:** commit `59abee75b3277b4a150d1f930a70b602f46f5a78` (`story: seed S2 Sukuna second-chance major arc`).

Core direction:

- Sukuna arrives in his **Heian-era body** and is intended to have access to **Ten Shadows**; the exact arrival-state explanation can be solved later.
- The confrontation should happen only after the cast has enough emotional and tactical pieces in place, especially Himmel, Yuta, Maki, Rimuru, Okarun and Qifrey.
- Mau begins seeing two apparent futures: if he does not intervene, Frieren dies; if he intervenes, Mau is catastrophically wounded / apparently dies.
- Mau incorrectly believes the visions come from the Noise or his fragmented internal system. They are actually part of a **goddess / higher-entity challenge** presenting him with a false binary.
- Mau tells Yuta and Rimuru enough of the truth for them to understand that he has accepted the possibility of his own death. They keep searching for a third option rather than endorsing the sacrifice.
- Mau secretly prepares a **one-use Hollow Purple analogue** as a last resort. It is not a normal permanent copy of Limitless and may fail or detonate at point-blank range.
- During the decisive moment, Frieren misreads a tiny opening under extreme emotional and tactical pressure. Sukuna sees the opening.
- Mau gets between Sukuna and Frieren. Frieren remains physically unharmed; Sukuna's arm / hand goes through Mau's body.
- Mau's emotional truth is approximately: `It will be okay.` and `You are the most precious thing in this world. I couldn't let you get hurt.` Exact wording is not locked.
- Mau looks to Himmel and approximately asks him to take care of Frieren if Mau does not survive. This is **trust, not ownership transfer**.
- The team extracts the others; Mau holds the final window long enough to complete the Hollow Purple analogue.
- Mahoraga is too late to stop the completed interaction. Sukuna survives / escapes through a later-resolved mechanism rather than being casually erased.
- Yuta chooses to save Mau instead of chasing Sukuna.
- The goddess's **Second Chance** reanchors Mau's soul and repairs only enough for rescue to remain possible. Yuta still performs the brutal practical healing work with RCT over roughly two days.
- Mau does not retain a convenient memory of the divine conversation afterward.

The fight should feel like a coordinated survival problem, not a sequence of characters taking turns attacking Sukuna.

## 2. Frieren / Himmel / Mau relationship endpoint

**Standing:** creator-directed relationship endpoint; exact episode placement open.  
**Source provenance:** commits `59abee75b3277b4a150d1f930a70b602f46f5a78` and `f48c483fd0276c8e0e2fcbdb20f2d1b7d88a207d`.

Preserved distinction:

- **Himmel teaches Frieren what it means to love.** His role is formative, profound and permanent.
- **Mau is Frieren's first consciously recognized romantic love.** He is the first person Frieren knowingly identifies as the person she wants romantically and chooses a future with.
- Frieren continuing to love Himmel does not require her to want the same romantic future with him.
- The story must not frame Mau as having "won" against Himmel or Himmel as a discarded failed romance.
- Himmel should retain dignity, generosity and emotional importance.

A useful shorthand for internal guidance is:

> Himmel taught Frieren how to love. Mau is the first person Frieren knows she is in love with.

The exact sentence does not need to appear in dialogue.

## 3. Mau's awakening, Frieren's confession and first explicit romantic choice

**Standing:** intended emotional payoff of the Second Chance arc; scene wording / page count open.

Current direction:

- Frieren stays near Mau during the roughly two-day period in which Yuta is keeping him alive / repairing him.
- Her grief should feel like grief for a person she has finally realized she consciously loves, not simply generic concern for a teammate.
- Around the third day, Mau wakes and lightly pets / touches Frieren's hair while she is asleep beside him.
- A small Mau line may be approximately: `Someone likes to sleep a lot.`
- Frieren finally **confesses clearly** rather than continuing to treat her feelings as an abstract question.
- The confession leads to a kiss and Mau reciprocates.
- The scene should feel like recognition and relief after terror, not a sudden personality rewrite.
- Mau and Frieren are intended to end up together romantically.

The confession is not meant to erase the later consequences of loving a human with a much shorter lifespan. That mortality realization should remain a later slow-burn problem, potentially much later than S2.

## 4. Post-revival promise / relationship rule

**Standing:** small but important post-arc beat; placement shortly after recovery, exact scene open.  
**Source provenance:** commit `542d10aec2568b3fa6fae64219300e9c783c5e49` (`story: add Mau Frieren post-revival promise beat`).

After the immediate reunion, Mau and Frieren need a quieter repair scene.

Core conflict:

- Frieren is not angry because Mau wanted to protect her.
- She is angry because he decided alone that his life could be spent for hers and kept the scale of the danger secret.
- Mau's sacrifice should not be framed as pure heroic virtue without acknowledging what his death would have done to Frieren.

Approximate emotional spine:

- Frieren tells Mau he cannot make that kind of decision alone again.
- Major secrets of that scale are not acceptable between them going forward.
- Mau: `That was the first and last secret I'll ever keep from you.`
- Mau: `I couldn't imagine a world where you weren't there.`
- Frieren: `And you think I can live in a world without you?`
- Mau understands the part of the decision he failed to consider, apologizes sincerely, and they embrace.

A small Frieren-style comic line such as `If you die again, I'll kill you.` can survive if it does not weaken the real boundary underneath it.

The promise is **not** "never risk yourself again." It is about secrecy and unilateral self-sacrifice.

## 5. Mau's power secrecy after Sukuna

**Standing:** provisional power / continuity direction; exact mechanics open.  
**Source provenance:** commit `0e63669be836c3771f4a5e24af154fdfe71c889f` (`story: clarify Mau power secrecy and post-Sukuna progression`).

Current direction:

- Hollow Purple is the first time most witnesses see undeniable evidence that Mau's unknown system can do something radically beyond his apparent combat level.
- The event should **not** instantly turn Mau into a top-tier conventional fighter.
- Mau, Frieren, Yuta and Rimuru are the core early group who understand enough to agree that the mechanism should not yet be publicized.
- The secrecy is protective risk containment: they still do not know the system's true ceiling, triggers, costs or reproducibility.
- The Sukuna-era Hollow Purple construction is **consumed / unavailable** after use. It is not a permanent button.
- A later Gojo-focused period could allow a new reconstruction only through fresh understanding and rebuilt requirements.
- Mau's long-term permanent toolkit should stay small. Flower-related ability and eventual RCT / self-healing are possible durable pieces; spectacular temporary reconstructions do not automatically become permanent powers.
- Second Chance and Yuta's RCT remain separate: the goddess reanchors the soul / makes rescue possible; Yuta remains essential to repairing Mau's body.
- The secrecy itself can create future story pressure when other people notice that the public explanation of the Sukuna blast does not add up.

## 6. Otherworlder settlement / city direction

**Standing:** S2-scale direction; exact episode map open.

The abandoned village / inn area can evolve into a fortified early base before a true Otherworlder City exists. The city should emerge from necessity rather than instant nation-building ambition.

Useful long-term pressures include:

- more arrivals and civilians to protect;
- growing outside fear of Otherworlders;
- trade and diplomacy with native settlements;
- search / reconnaissance for new arrivals;
- specialized defense, healing, logistics, research and governance roles;
- the need to respond to dangerous arrivals without treating every Otherworlder as dangerous.

Rimuru may have a central governance role while still participating in field work. Roles should describe responsibilities, not rigid permanent social classes.

## 7. Placement status

These ideas are intentionally **not assigned exact episode numbers here**.

Current broad ordering logic is only:

1. S1 establishes the first groups, home, reunion hope and the possibility that dangerous arrivals exist too.
2. S2 grows the settlement and relationships before the Sukuna / Second Chance major confrontation.
3. Frieren / Himmel / Mau emotional clarity must be earned before the climax.
4. Mau's apparent death / Second Chance creates the explicit Mau/Frieren confession and the later post-revival promise.
5. Power secrecy, mortality, settlement politics and consequences continue after the immediate arc rather than all resolving at once.

When exact placement is decided, promote the beat into the appropriate episode structure / roadmap and leave this board as historical provenance rather than silently rewriting it.
