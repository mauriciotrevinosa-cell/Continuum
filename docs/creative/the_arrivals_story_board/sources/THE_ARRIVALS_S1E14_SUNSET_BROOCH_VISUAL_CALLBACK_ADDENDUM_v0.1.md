## Approved addition

The emotional lake payoff in E14 should take place as the day is falling into **sunset / late golden-hour light**.

When Frieren rests her head against Mau after:

> `I hope you know you're the most important person to me.`

her **hair ornament / brooch from the Fern reunion continuity must be clearly visible in the composition**.

This is a silent callback to E11, when Fern told Frieren to **keep it a little longer** instead of returning it immediately.

The image should therefore carry three layers without explanatory dialogue:

1. Frieren is physically choosing closeness with Mau again.
2. Fern remains present in Frieren's life through the ornament she allowed Frieren to keep.
3. Mau and Fern are not competing for the same place; both relationships can coexist.
