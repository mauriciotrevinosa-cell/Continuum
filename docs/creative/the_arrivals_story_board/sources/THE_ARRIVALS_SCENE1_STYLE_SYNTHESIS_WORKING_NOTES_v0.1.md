# The Arrivals — Scene 1 Style Synthesis Working Notes v0.1

**Status:** WORKING NOTES / PROVISIONAL / NOT CANON  
**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Project:** `The Arrivals`  
**Branch:** `m3/critical-path`

These notes capture current reactions to Scene 1 style tests only. They are intentionally non-final and must not override `THE_ARRIVALS_ABANDONED_VILLAGE_AND_INN_SPATIAL_BIBLE_v0.2.md`.

The approved spatial bible remains the source of truth for the village, inn, lake, hill/tree, well, cultivation plot, access paths, and interior logic.

---

# 1. Witch Hat Atelier

Current strengths:
- strongest overall environmental detail;
- best sense of long abandonment;
- convincing moss, overgrowth, weathering, and reclaimed surfaces;
- unused paths read clearly as paths that have not been traveled in a long time;
- construction designs are not objectionable on their own;
- strong local texture around houses and ground plane.

Current weaknesses:
- the distant background near the lake loses too much visual quality/detail;
- the lake-side depth feels weaker than the near/midground;
- architecture still does not reproduce the already-approved inn design closely enough.

Working takeaway:
- strongest current reference for abandonment, overgrowth, unused paths, and environmental texture.

---

# 2. Call of the Night

Current strengths:
- second strongest environmental detail after Witch Hat;
- particularly strong atmosphere around the settlement;
- gives the village a distinctive mood without making it horror-like or overtly threatening;
- slightly more destroyed/decayed interpretation works well;
- lake route is somewhat more legible;
- surrounding forest/environment feels especially successful.

Current weaknesses:
- still does not sufficiently reproduce the approved inn/building design;
- color and black-and-white interpretations diverge noticeably;
- that color/B&W mismatch is acceptable for exploration but may become a production consistency problem later.

Working takeaway:
- strongest current reference for atmosphere and environmental mood;
- useful for controlled destruction/decay without turning the location into horror.

---

# 3. Dandadan

Current strengths:
- good attention to detail;
- vegetation treatment is successful;
- final wash/finish works well;
- potentially more promising for later combat/action testing.

Current weaknesses:
- did not connect as strongly visually for this particular establishing/environment scene;
- not bad technically, but less aligned with the desired identity of this location than Witch Hat, Call of the Night, or Frieren.

Working takeaway:
- useful ingredient for vegetation, surface energy, and finish/wash;
- do not treat this Scene 1 result as evidence against the style for action scenes.

---

# 4. Frieren

Current strengths:
- good distribution of trees and environmental elements;
- gives individual elements enough breathing room;
- hill/tree treatment works well;
- small-line treatment in the far background gives a convincing suggestion of additional vegetation;
- overall composition remains clear and readable.

Current weaknesses:
- less visually compelling than the top environment-focused results for this scene;
- still does not solve the approved inn design.

Working takeaway:
- useful reference for spatial clarity, environmental spacing, hill/tree presentation, and lightweight distant vegetation detail.

---

# 5. Combined / Blend

Current strengths:
- visibly integrates elements from multiple style tests;
- nearby trees suggest Dandadan influence;
- houses carry some Frieren influence;
- atmosphere carries some Call of the Night influence;
- some Witch Hat-style attention to detail survives;
- proves that a blended visual language is possible.

Current weaknesses:
- something is lost when the influences are combined;
- the settlement becomes too open;
- instead of feeling like a hidden village inside a forest with a nearby lake, it begins to read as a village directly beside a lake;
- the result is not disliked, but its geographical/emotional framing is weaker than intended.

Working takeaway:
- blending works technically, but the blend must preserve the settlement's enclosed/hidden quality instead of averaging everything into an open lakeside village.

---

# 6. Current provisional synthesis

These are NOT final style weights.

Current useful ingredients:
- **Witch Hat Atelier:** abandonment, overgrowth, moss/weathering, unused paths, environmental detail.
- **Call of the Night:** atmosphere, surrounding environment, controlled decay, distinctive mood without horror.
- **Frieren:** composition clarity, spacing, hill/tree treatment, subtle distant vegetation.
- **Dandadan:** vegetation, surface energy, wash/finish.
- **Combined:** evidence that shared visual language is possible, but current blend opens the geography too much.

Current geographical/emotional target:
- the settlement should read as a **small abandoned village hidden/enclosed by forest with a nearby lake**;
- it should NOT read primarily as an **open lakeside village**;
- the lake may be visible/legible without becoming the dominant organizing feature of the settlement.

---

# 7. Important unresolved point

None of the Scene 1 style tests adequately captured the already-approved inn design.

This is not permission to redesign the inn.

Any future Scene 1/environment prompt or full chapter test should treat the approved spatial bible as the source of truth and use these style observations only for rendering language, atmosphere, texture, and finish.
