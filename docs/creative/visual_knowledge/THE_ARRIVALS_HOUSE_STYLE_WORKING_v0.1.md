# The Arrivals — House Style Working Direction v0.1

**Status:** working visual direction / deliberately evolvable  
**Not a fixed style formula.**

## Purpose

The Arrivals needs a recognizable visual language, but it should be discovered through production rather than frozen as a percentage mix of existing works.

The current four strongest discovery references are:

### Witch Hat Atelier
Useful current lessons:
- material detail;
- hand-crafted surfaces;
- environmental storytelling;
- convincing age/weathering;
- overgrowth;
- readable abandoned paths;
- architectural texture.

### Call of the Night
Useful current lessons:
- atmosphere;
- night/color language;
- environmental mood;
- luminous contrast;
- emotional lighting;
- controlled decay without turning every scene into horror.

### Frieren
Useful current lessons:
- restrained acting;
- compositional clarity;
- breathing room;
- quiet emotional panels;
- environmental spacing;
- distant vegetation/landscape economy.

### Dandadan
Useful current lessons:
- motion;
- extreme perspective;
- action camera;
- impact;
- surface energy;
- vegetation/finish in some contexts.

## These are discovery references, not permanent rules

Do not encode:
- fixed 25/25/25/25 weights;
- mandatory franchise names in every prompt;
- a rule that later references cannot influence the project.

A new manga panel, illustration, fan art piece, artist process or successful Continuum render may reveal a better solution.

The House Style should become less identifiable as a mixture and more identifiable as **The Arrivals** over time.

## House Style versioning

Use versions:

`House Style v0.1 -> production evidence -> v0.2 -> ...`

Each version records:
- visual principles retained;
- principles rejected;
- newly discovered techniques;
- example references by role;
- scene classes where a principle works or fails.

Do not version merely because a new reference was added.

## Scene treatments

House Style is the normal visual language. A specific scene may deliberately use another treatment.

Examples:
- magical revelation with oil-paint-like texture;
- dream with watercolor softness;
- divine encounter with luminous painterly treatment;
- Noise event with graphic/glitch abstraction;
- terror sequence with more aggressive ink;
- comedy with controlled chibi/super-deformation.

The temporary treatment must preserve:
- identity;
- story facts;
- wardrobe ownership;
- environment geometry unless the scene explicitly distorts perception;
- panel semantics;
- lettering authority.

## Current Scene 1 synthesis

For the abandoned settlement, the current useful direction remains:

- Witch Hat: abandonment/material detail;
- Call of the Night: atmosphere;
- Frieren: spacing/composition;
- Dandadan: surface energy/vegetation.

The target is a **small abandoned village enclosed by forest with a nearby lake**, not an open lakeside village.

The Spatial Bible remains authoritative for what the location physically is.
